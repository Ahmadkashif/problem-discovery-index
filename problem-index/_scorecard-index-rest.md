## Cases

**1. Product Compliance Regulatory Intelligence (51).** Compliance & Risks, Enhesa and 3E read product regulation worldwide and tell manufacturers which of their products a new rule touches and by when, against regulators' effective dates. It is the product-regulation branch of the archetype indexed at 55 (regulatory content publishers) and 51 (employment-law content). The unowned step is turning a regulatory change into a dated list of products needing retest, and which tests, before the effective date. *(batch 1)*

**2. Performing Rights Organisations & Collecting Societies (51).** ASCAP, BMI, SESAC, GMR and the 228 CISAC societies turn every reported use into a payment to the right member's share, working from the deepest works-and-usage record in music, and in most of the world under a statutory clock (the EU directive's nine-month distribution and three-year non-distributable rules). Members pay the commission for that determination and can move their rights. Scored on the global society market, matching the permissions agencies pocket (53) on Q1; on US societies alone it would be 45 (self-set calendars, four PROs). *(batch 3)*

**3. Sound-Recording & Performer Collecting Societies (51).** SoundExchange, PPL, GVL, SCPP/SPPF, SENA, Re:Sound and SCAPR's 61 performer societies match reported plays to recordings, owners and line-ups; members pay the deduction for that work, and under Directive 2014/26/EU Art. 13 money must be paid within nine months of the collection year or lost as non-distributable after three. The gap recording adds is session line-ups: the studio knew them on the day, and the society reconstructs them years later from claims. The neighbouring-rights twin of the composition-side collecting societies (51); scored on the global field, US-only would be 40. *(batch 3)*

**4. Nonclinical & IND Regulatory Strategy Consultancies (50).** Biologics Consulting, ProPharma, Certara and many boutiques invoice the answer to "what will FDA require?" against a deadline FDA sets on the work itself (the pre-IND package is due 30 days before the meeting), and FDA's preliminary responses grade the advice days later. Nobody has turned FDA's public pharmacology and toxicology reviews into searchable precedent. It qualifies at the threshold: the public record is open to rivals, and sponsor correspondence can be used only per engagement, so Q3 is 3 (its device twin scores 53 on a Q3 of 4). *(batch 2)*

## Near-misses and downgrades worth remembering

Pockets that scored 50+ on a builder's first pass and were cut on review, so the next batches calibrate against them:

- **Building & Fire Code Consultants** (`architecture-firms`, 51 → 49): Q4 cut to 4, because the clock is the owner's permit schedule, not a deadline any third party sets on the consultant.
- **Multi-Client Seismic Data Libraries** (`geophysical-survey-firms`, 52 → 48): Q2 cut to 3, because licensing an owned library earns without adding analysts (Pulse Seismic runs on 15 staff), and Q5 cut to 2 for a five-firm market.
- **Research Security & Foreign-Influence Due Diligence** (`contract-research-labs`, 51 → 49): Q5 cut to 3. The buyer is the provider's research head, and the providers are Strider plus a handful.

- **Drug Pipeline & Trial Intelligence** (`biotech-research-firms`, 51 → 49): Q5 cut to 2, because the market is three or four providers (Norstella, Clarivate, GlobalData, BioCentury).
- **PR Measurement & Media Analysis** (`public-relations-agencies`, 51 → 49): Q5 cut to 3, because the pocket is Cision, Onclusive and Meltwater plus a few small firms. Size was restated from whole-company to analyst counts.

- **Invalidity & PTAB Prior-Art Search Houses** (`legal-support-services`, 51 → kill switch at 49): searches commissioned through litigation counsel are client work product. Q5 was cut to 3 for a field of ~10–20 houses.
