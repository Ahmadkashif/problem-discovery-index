# Hospital Systems

**Layer:** Origin — a parent industry, not a prospect
**Primary Wave:** [[series/eras/wave-07-big-data|7 — Big Data]] *(statutory trigger)*
**Founding event:** HITECH Act enacted Feb 2009, within the American Recovery and Reinvestment Act; Meaningful Use incentive payments began 2011
**Children in this vault:** healthcare practice software, medical billing, telehealth platforms, urgent care, home health agencies, behavioral health clinics

## Profile

**What it is:** Hospitals and health systems — inpatient and outpatient care delivery organisations, from single community hospitals to multi-site regional networks, running on electronic health record (EHR) platforms that are now close to universal.

**Who pays:** Patients, employers and government payers pay for care. The federal government paid, separately and directly, for the *computing* — up to $44,000 per eligible professional over five years through Medicare, or up to $63,750 through Medicaid, plus base payments to hospitals scaling with discharge volume. No other origin in this vault was subsidised into existence by its own regulator.

**The economics:** Fee-for-service billing against thousands of CPT and ICD codes, adjudicated against hundreds of distinct payer contracts, on a cost base dominated by clinical labour. Digitisation did not simplify this. It made the same complexity machine-readable, which is not the same thing as making it simple.

## Why This Is an Origin

Every other origin in this vault was pulled into computing by competitive pressure — a rival was about to win on cost or speed. Hospital systems were pushed, by statute, with a deadline and a penalty attached. **This is the one origin where the trigger was not a market decision at all.**

That makes hospital systems the cleanest available case study in what a government-forced digitisation actually produces: an adoption curve compressed from decades into six years, and an integration debt that the mandate never touched, because the mandate measured adoption, not connection.

## The Contested Decision

> **When the penalty for not digitising arrives before the benefit of digitising does, what does an organisation actually optimise for?**

The honest answer, documented in this file's [[origins/hospital-systems/the-mechanism|mechanism]], is: the metric in the statute, not the capability the statute was written to produce.
