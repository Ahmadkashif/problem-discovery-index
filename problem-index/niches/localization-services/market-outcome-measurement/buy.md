# Creative Effectiveness From Advertising Measurement

**Industry:** [[industries/localization-services|Localization Services]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Advertising tests which words perform rather than which read best, and localization reviews the writing.
**Tags:** #causal-inference #hypothesis-testing #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #descriptive-statistics #revenue-impact #data-integration #word-embeddings
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show that a translation worked in the market it was made for, when the industry measures the artefact and the outcome sits in the client's systems — and whoever measures it takes the account.

## The Problem
Advertising and conversion optimisation settled a version of this argument decades ago: copy is judged by what it does rather than by how it reads. Variants are tested, performance is measured, and the copy that converts wins regardless of whether it would win a craft assessment. The practice is universal in performance marketing. Localisation produces copy for markets and judges it by linguistic review, which is the craft assessment.

## What Already Exists
Copy variant testing at scale; conversion and engagement measurement per variant; search term research driving wording; long-run brand effect measurement; and a norm that performance settles disagreements about copy.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to content whose variants are translations and whose markets are small. It requires: (1) testing across markets where traffic volumes are frequently too small for the variant testing advertising relies on, so the method must borrow strength across locales — this is the substantive difference; (2) content types beyond marketing copy, including documentation and support material, where the outcome is comprehension rather than conversion; (3) an agency that does not control the surface and cannot run the test itself; (4) linguistic acceptability as a constraint that cannot be traded away entirely, unlike in performance copywriting; and (5) a quality apparatus already in place that must be reconciled rather than replaced.

## Target Customer
Language service providers, enterprise localization and marketing leadership, conversion optimisation vendors, and market research providers.

## Impact If Solved
Advertising settled that copy is judged by what it does, and the practice is universal. Small per-market volumes and content whose outcome is comprehension rather than conversion are what the localisation version has to handle.
