**Parent Industry:** [[industries/localization-services|Localization Services]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to answer a linguist's question once rather than thirty times, and whoever routes and propagates those answers takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$0.8B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — email backlogs
**Target Buyer:** Operations
**Automation Potential:** Very high — deduplication, routing and propagation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Queries are the localisation pipeline's blocking mechanism. A linguist cannot proceed without an answer, the answer usually sits with a source author several steps away, and the same question arrives from many languages independently. The handling is manual: a coordinator collects, routes, waits, and distributes. It is high-volume, entirely mechanical, and it is where release delays originate more often than translation itself.

## Current Tools & Gaps
An email thread, a query spreadsheet and a coordinator. The gaps: no deduplication across languages; no routing to the right answerer; no propagation of an answer to everyone blocked by it; no searchable history of previous answers; and no measurement of query-driven delay.
