# Ticket Deduplication From Support Operations

**Niche:** [[niches/localization-services/query-management/profile|Query Management]]
**Industry:** [[industries/localization-services|Localization Services]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Support operations deduplicates, routes and publishes answers as standard, and localization queries run on email threads.
**Tags:** #automation #workflow-orchestration #word-embeddings #data-integration #evaluation-metrics #large-language-models #descriptive-statistics #k-means-clustering
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to answer a linguist's question once rather than thirty times, and whoever routes and propagates those answers takes the account.

## The Problem
Support operations solved the many-people-asking-the-same-thing problem comprehensively: incoming contacts are classified, duplicates are merged, questions are routed to the team that can answer, answers are published so the next person finds them, and the recurring questions are eliminated at source. The tooling is commodity and the practice is universal. Localisation queries — which have the same structure and block production rather than merely annoying a customer — run on email.

## What Already Exists
Automatic classification and deduplication; skills-based routing; knowledge base publication of answers; recurring-issue identification for elimination; and service level tracking on response.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to queries that block production and route outside the organisation. It requires: (1) the answerer frequently being in the client's organisation rather than the agency's, so routing crosses a company boundary with no shared system — this is the substantive difference; (2) deduplication keyed to a source segment across languages rather than to a topic, which is both easier and requires the segment identifier to travel with the query; (3) a blocked linguist rather than a waiting customer, so the cost of delay is production rather than satisfaction; (4) answers that must propagate to everyone blocked rather than only to the asker; and (5) query volume that is itself a signal about source content quality.

## Target Customer
Language service providers, enterprise localization teams, translation management vendors, and support and workflow tooling providers.

## Impact If Solved
Support operations made deduplication, routing and answer publication universal with commodity tooling. An answerer inside the client's organisation, and an answer that must reach everyone blocked, is what the localisation version adds.
