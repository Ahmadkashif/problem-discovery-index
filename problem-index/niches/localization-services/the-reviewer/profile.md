# The Reviewer

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/localization-services|Localization Services]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make the reviewer's judgement count for something, when their scores are the industry's quality currency and nothing validates them — and whoever grounds those scores takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.2B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — unvalidated scores
**Target Buyer:** Language leadership
**Automation Potential:** Medium — calibration and consequence

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Reviewers score translations against error typologies, and those scores govern vendor selection, linguist ratings and client quality reporting across the industry. Nothing validates them. Two reviewers score the same translation differently; a reviewer's preferences become errors; a stylistic disagreement is recorded as a defect. The reviewer knows this, has no way to calibrate, and their judgement carries consequences for other people's livelihoods that the process does not acknowledge.

## Current Tools & Gaps
An error typology, a scoring form and a sample. The gaps: no calibration between reviewers; no distinction between error and preference; no validation against any outcome; no feedback on their own consistency; and no acknowledgement of the consequences their scores carry.
