# Rater Calibration From Assessment Practice

**Niche:** [[niches/localization-services/the-reviewer/profile|The Reviewer]]
**Industry:** [[industries/localization-services|Localization Services]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Examination boards calibrate markers because scores with consequences must be reliable, and translation review does not.
**Tags:** #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #hypothesis-testing #descriptive-statistics #compliance #worker-facing #probability-distributions #cross-validation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make the reviewer's judgement count for something, when their scores are the industry's quality currency and nothing validates them — and whoever grounds those scores takes the account.

## The Problem
Educational assessment developed a complete apparatus for reliable human scoring, because scores that determine people's futures cannot vary with who marked them. Markers are trained on exemplars, calibrated against a standard, monitored for drift, double-marked on samples, and their severity is statistically adjusted. Inter-rater reliability is reported. Translation review scores determine livelihoods and account allocation and have none of it.

## What Already Exists
Marker training against exemplars; calibration and standardisation exercises; inter-rater reliability measurement; drift monitoring during marking; and statistical adjustment for marker severity.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a distributed freelance reviewer population working across language pairs. It requires: (1) reviewers spread across languages, so calibration cannot use a common exemplar set and must work within each language pair — this is the substantive difference and multiplies the calibration cost; (2) a scoring dimension that includes legitimate stylistic variation, where an examination has a mark scheme; (3) reviewers who are freelance and paid per review, so calibration time must be funded; (4) no examination board or equivalent authority to set the standard; and (5) commercial consequences rather than educational ones, which changes what an appeal process must look like.

## Target Customer
Language service providers, language leadership, translator associations, and assessment and quality tooling vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Assessment built calibration, reliability measurement and severity adjustment because consequential scores cannot vary with the marker. Calibration within each language pair, funded for freelance reviewers, is what the localisation version costs.
