# Estate & Trust Tax Research Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/estate-planning|Estate Planning]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Leimberg Information Services, Bloomberg Tax's estates and trusts portfolios, and the Thomson Reuters estate planning services publish the interpreted analysis of transfer tax law, state trust codes, and fiduciary rules that estate planning attorneys work from. Analysts and attorney-editors track legislation, rulings, and state law changes and maintain a corpus that a single sunset provision can invalidate across thousands of documents.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 attorney-editors, analysts, and researchers on the estates specialty
**Output:** Interpreted analysis, practice commentary, form and clause commentary — the subscription
**Proprietary data:** Decades of interpretive analysis across transfer tax and fifty state trust regimes
**Clock:** Legislative changes, scheduled sunsets, and state trust code amendments
**Buyer:** VP of Content / Managing Editor
