# Probate Genealogy & Heir Search Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/estate-planning|Estate Planning]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Forensic genealogy firms locate the rightful heirs of estates where no plan names them — reconstructing family trees from vital records, immigration files, and archives across countries, and proving kinship to a court's satisfaction. The research is the entire service and it is paid as a share of the recovered inheritance.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 forensic genealogists, researchers, and case managers
**Output:** Kinship reports, heir identification, court-admissible proof of relationship
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated research across jurisdictions and record types, and the record of what proof standard each court has accepted
**Clock:** Probate deadlines and escheat periods, after which an estate passes to the state
**Buyer:** Managing Director / Director of Research
