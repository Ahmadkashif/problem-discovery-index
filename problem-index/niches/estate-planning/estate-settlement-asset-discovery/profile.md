# Estate Settlement & Asset Discovery Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/estate-planning|Estate Planning]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Firms that locate and inventory a decedent's assets across financial institutions, insurers, and registries — the process the Pass 1 analysis describes as requiring coordination with institutions that have no interfaces and communicate by mail and fax — and manage the account closures and transfers that follow.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-200 case researchers, settlement specialists, and analysts
**Output:** Asset inventories, institution response management, settlement completion
**Proprietary data:** Institution-by-institution process knowledge — who to contact, what documentation each requires, how long each takes — accumulated across tens of thousands of settlements
**Clock:** Probate administration deadlines and estate tax filing dates
**Buyer:** VP of Operations / Head of Settlement Services
