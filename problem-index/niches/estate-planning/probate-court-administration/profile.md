# Probate Court Administration

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/estate-planning|Estate Planning]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Probate courts and their registrars supervise estate administration, review fiduciary accountings, adjudicate contests, and maintain the public record of every estate settled in the jurisdiction.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-80 court staff, examiners, and investigators per large jurisdiction
**Output:** Accounting reviews, guardianship investigations, adjudications
**Proprietary data:** The complete estate and fiduciary record for the jurisdiction, largely public but wholly unstructured
**Clock:** Statutory accounting and administration deadlines
**Buyer:** Court administrator — public procurement
