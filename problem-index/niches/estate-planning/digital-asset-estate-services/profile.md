# Digital Asset Estate Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/estate-planning|Estate Planning]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Small specialist firms handle the discovery, access, and transfer of digital assets in estates — cryptocurrency holdings, online accounts, and the access credentials that determine whether an asset is recoverable at all.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 3-15
**Output:** Digital asset discovery, access recovery, transfer facilitation
**Proprietary data:** Platform-by-platform process knowledge for estate access
**Clock:** Probate deadlines and platform inactivity policies
**Buyer:** Principal
