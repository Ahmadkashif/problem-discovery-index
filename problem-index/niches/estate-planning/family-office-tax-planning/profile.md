# Family Office Wealth Transfer Planning

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/estate-planning|Estate Planning]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The tax and wealth transfer groups inside multi-family offices design and maintain the structures — grantor trusts, valuation discounts, charitable vehicles — that move substantial estates efficiently, and monitor them against changing law for decades.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-80 tax attorneys, CPAs, and planning specialists per large office
**Output:** Structure design, ongoing plan monitoring, transfer tax analyses
**Proprietary data:** Structure performance and audit experience across a client base held for decades
**Clock:** Transfer tax filing deadlines, sunset provisions, and valuation date requirements
**Buyer:** Chief Wealth Strategist / Head of Tax
