# Corporate Trustee Fiduciary Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/estate-planning|Estate Planning]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The fiduciary, tax, and trust administration groups at the bank and independent trust companies administer trusts over decades — interpreting instruments, exercising discretion over distributions, preparing fiduciary accountings and tax returns, and documenting the reasoning that protects the trustee from surcharge.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-800 trust officers, fiduciary tax specialists, and administrators
**Output:** Distribution decisions and their documentation, fiduciary accountings, tax filings
**Proprietary data:** Decades of instrument interpretation and discretionary decisions across thousands of trusts — the accumulated record of how ambiguous language has been administered
**Clock:** Fiduciary accounting deadlines and tax filing dates, both statutory
**Buyer:** Chief Fiduciary Officer / Head of Trust Administration
