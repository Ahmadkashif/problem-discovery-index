# Estate Planning Platform Networks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/estate-planning|Estate Planning]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
WealthCounsel, ElderCounsel, and the document platform networks maintain the drafting systems, clause libraries, and state-specific logic their attorney members assemble plans from, plus the education and practice guidance that comes with membership. The content is the product; the software is the delivery mechanism.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-120 attorney-editors, drafting specialists, and education staff
**Output:** Drafting systems and clause libraries, state law configurations, practice education
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated drafting patterns across fifty state regimes and tax environments, plus the member support record showing where the templates fail
**Clock:** Tax law changes and state trust code amendments, which invalidate clauses on effective dates
**Buyer:** VP of Content / Chief Legal Editor
