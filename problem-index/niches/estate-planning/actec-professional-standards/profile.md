# Trust & Estate Professional Standards Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/estate-planning|Estate Planning]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
ACTEC and the estate planning councils develop professional standards and commentaries, produce the uniform act commentary the states adopt from, and run the education the specialty bar relies on.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-20 staff, with volunteer fellow committees
**Output:** Standards commentaries, uniform act analysis, professional education
**Proprietary data:** Committee deliberation records; the technical work is volunteer-supplied
**Clock:** Legislative sessions and annual meetings
**Buyer:** Executive Director
