# Fiduciary Litigation Support

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/estate-planning|Estate Planning]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Specialist practices analyze trust and estate disputes — surcharge claims against trustees, accounting challenges, undue influence and capacity contests — reconstructing fiduciary accountings, quantifying damages from investment or distribution decisions, and testifying. The written analysis is the invoice and it is tested in probate court.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 forensic accountants, fiduciary specialists, and analysts
**Output:** Fiduciary accountings, damages analyses, expert reports and testimony
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated case methodology and the record of which approaches probate courts accepted
**Clock:** Court schedules and statutory accounting deadlines
**Buyer:** Practice leader / Managing Director
