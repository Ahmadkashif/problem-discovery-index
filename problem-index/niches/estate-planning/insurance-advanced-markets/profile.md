# Insurance Advanced Markets Groups

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/estate-planning|Estate Planning]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The advanced markets groups at life insurers employ attorneys and CPAs who produce the estate and business succession planning technical content their agents and the independent advisors they serve use to structure cases. The analysis is genuine and it exists to sell policies.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 attorneys, CPAs, and technical writers per carrier
**Output:** Technical bulletins, case design support, planning concept materials
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated case design experience across advisor networks
**Clock:** Tax law changes and product launches
**Buyer:** VP of Advanced Markets
