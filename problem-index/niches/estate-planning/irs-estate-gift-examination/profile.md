# Federal Estate & Gift Tax Examination

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/estate-planning|Estate Planning]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The federal estate and gift tax examination function selects and examines transfer tax returns, challenges valuation discounts and structure substance, and produces the rulings and litigation positions that define what planning actually works.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 examiners, valuation engineers, and attorneys
**Output:** Examination determinations, valuation challenges, published guidance
**Proprietary data:** Complete transfer tax return and examination outcome data — the definitive record of which structures survive scrutiny
**Clock:** Statute of limitations and examination cycles
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer
