# Veterinary Licensure Examination Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/veterinary-practices|Veterinary Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Develop and administer the national licensure examination every veterinarian must pass, maintain the item banks and standards behind it, and coordinate licensure data across states.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-200 psychometricians, item developers, subject matter experts, and operations staff
**Output:** Licensure examinations and item banks, practice analyses, passing standards, licensure and discipline data exchange
**Proprietary data:** Item response data across the entire entering profession, plus practice analysis surveys
**Clock:** Examination windows and licence renewal cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Assessment Officer
