# Animal Health Clinical Development & Pharmacovigilance

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/veterinary-practices|Veterinary Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Run the clinical studies, regulatory submissions and post-market safety surveillance behind veterinary pharmaceuticals, vaccines and parasiticides — the products that generate a large share of practice revenue.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-1,500 veterinary clinicians, biostatisticians, regulatory scientists, and pharmacovigilance staff
**Output:** Clinical study design and results, regulatory submissions, label claims, adverse event surveillance and signal detection, real-world evidence studies
**Proprietary data:** Study datasets and adverse event reports across product portfolios, plus field usage data where practices share it
**Clock:** Regulatory review cycles and product launch programmes
**Buyer:** VP of Clinical Development / Chief Safety Officer
