The category, pricing and demand analytics functions inside the veterinary distributors that supply practices with pharmaceuticals, vaccines, consumables and equipment.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 category managers, pricing analysts, and demand planners
**Output:** Assortment and pricing decisions, demand forecasting and allocation, practice purchasing analytics, manufacturer programme administration
**Proprietary data:** Order-level purchasing across most US practices, joined to practice characteristics
**Clock:** Ordering cycles and manufacturer programme periods
**Buyer:** VP of Analytics / Chief Merchandising Officer
