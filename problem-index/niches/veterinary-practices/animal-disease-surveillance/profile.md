# Animal Disease Surveillance Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/veterinary-practices|Veterinary Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Monitor and respond to reportable animal diseases and zoonoses — surveillance, laboratory confirmation, outbreak investigation and movement control — across federal and state animal health programmes.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 epidemiologists, laboratory scientists, and field veterinarians
**Output:** Surveillance reporting, outbreak investigation and tracing, laboratory confirmation networks, movement and quarantine decisions, risk assessments
**Proprietary data:** Reportable disease and laboratory confirmation records nationally, plus animal movement data
**Clock:** Outbreak response and mandatory reporting timelines
**Buyer:** State veterinarian / federal programme director
