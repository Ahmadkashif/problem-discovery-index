# Veterinary Practice Consolidator Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/veterinary-practices|Veterinary Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator/rollup

## What They Do
Corporate analytics inside the corporate veterinary groups that now own a large share of US practices — pricing, protocol standardisation, doctor productivity, client retention and acquisition screening across thousands of hospitals.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 analysts, data scientists, and corporate development staff
**Output:** Pricing and fee schedule guidance, medical protocol standardisation and compliance measurement, doctor productivity and capacity analytics, client retention modelling, acquisition screening and integration
**Proprietary data:** Medical records, invoices, and outcomes across thousands of practices and tens of millions of patients — the largest clinical dataset in veterinary medicine outside the laboratories
**Clock:** Quarterly reporting and acquisition pipelines
**Buyer:** VP of Analytics / Chief Medical Officer
