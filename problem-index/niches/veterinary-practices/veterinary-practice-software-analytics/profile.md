# Veterinary Practice Information System Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/veterinary-practices|Veterinary Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The analytics functions inside the practice information systems veterinary hospitals run on, producing benchmark reports on visit volume, pricing, compliance and retention, and increasingly supplying the data layer for corporate groups and diagnostics partners.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 data engineers, analysts, and product researchers
**Output:** Industry benchmark reports, pricing and compliance analytics, reminder and retention modelling, clinical data extraction for partners
**Proprietary data:** Medical records, invoices, reminders and visit history across a very large share of US practices
**Clock:** Continuous visit flow with seasonal patterns
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Chief Product Officer
