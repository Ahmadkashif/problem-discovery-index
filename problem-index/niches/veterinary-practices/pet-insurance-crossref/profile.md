# Pet Insurance Underwriting & Claims Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/veterinary-practices|Veterinary Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Price pet health insurance and adjudicate the claims — the closest thing veterinary medicine has to a payer, and the only party holding what care actually costs across the country.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 actuaries, data scientists, claims analysts, and veterinary reviewers
**Output:** Premium rating by breed, age and region, claim adjudication and pre-existing condition determination, loss ratio and reserve analysis, cost trend reporting
**Proprietary data:** Millions of veterinary invoices with diagnosis, procedure and cost, joined to breed, age and geography
**Clock:** Annual renewals and continuous claim flow
**Buyer:** Chief Actuary / VP of Analytics
