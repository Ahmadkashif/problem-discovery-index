# Veterinary Economics & Workforce Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/veterinary-practices|Veterinary Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Study the profession's economics — practice revenue, veterinarian supply and demand, debt and compensation, and the workforce shortage that dominates the field's policy conversation.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 economists, statisticians, and researchers
**Output:** Economic state of the profession reports, workforce supply and demand models, compensation and debt studies, practice financial benchmarks
**Proprietary data:** Member surveys and licensure data, with modest response rates
**Clock:** Annual publication cycle
**Buyer:** Chief Economist
