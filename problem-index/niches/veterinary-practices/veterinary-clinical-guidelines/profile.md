# Veterinary Clinical Guideline Development

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/veterinary-practices|Veterinary Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Convene expert panels to write the clinical guidelines companion animal practice runs on — vaccination protocols, anaesthesia, pain management, life stage care, nutrition — which practices adopt as standards and corporate groups turn into mandated protocols.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 staff plus volunteer expert panels
**Output:** Clinical practice guidelines, protocol recommendations, accreditation standards, practitioner education
**Proprietary data:** Guideline development record and accreditation findings across member practices
**Clock:** Guideline revision cycles and accreditation reviews
**Buyer:** Chief Medical Officer / Director of Guidelines
