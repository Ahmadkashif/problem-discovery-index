# Veterinary Specialty & Emergency Referral Networks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/veterinary-practices|Veterinary Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator/rollup

## What They Do
Operate the specialty and emergency hospitals general practices refer to — surgery, oncology, internal medicine, criticalist care — and analyse case flow, outcomes and capacity across networks of referral centres.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 analysts, specialists, and clinical operations staff
**Output:** Referral pattern and case mix analytics, outcome and complication reporting, capacity and staffing models, pricing for specialty procedures
**Proprietary data:** Complex case records with diagnostics, procedures, complications and outcomes — the highest-acuity clinical data in the field
**Clock:** Emergency arrival patterns and specialist scheduling
**Buyer:** VP of Medical Operations / Chief Medical Officer
