# Veterinary Practice Finance & Valuation Advisory

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/veterinary-practices|Veterinary Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Value veterinary practices and advise on sales into a consolidation market, and underwrite the acquisition and start-up lending that funds it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 valuation analysts, bankers, and credit staff across the specialist advisers and lenders
**Output:** Practice valuations, transaction advisory, lending underwriting, market multiple analysis
**Proprietary data:** Transaction comparables and post-close performance in a market with no public pricing
**Clock:** Transaction timelines and loan decisioning
**Buyer:** Managing Director / Chief Credit Officer
