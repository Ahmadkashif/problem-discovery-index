# Veterinary Diagnostic Reference Laboratories

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/veterinary-practices|Veterinary Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Run the blood chemistry, haematology, urinalysis, cytology and molecular testing that companion animal medicine is diagnosed on — through reference laboratories and through the analysers placed inside practices — and supply the reference intervals, algorithmic flags and interpretive comments that tell a veterinarian what the numbers mean. Diagnostics is the largest single supplier relationship most practices have and a substantial share of what a practice charges for.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-3,000 clinical pathologists, veterinary scientists, assay developers, biostatisticians, and data scientists
**Output:** Test results with reference intervals and flags, interpretive comments, algorithmic disease indicators, new assay development and validation, practice-level diagnostic utilisation analytics
**Proprietary data:** Hundreds of millions of results across the national companion animal population, longitudinal at patient level for animals tested repeatedly, joined to breed, age, sex and practice — and carrying no privacy encumbrance of any kind, because animal health records sit outside every human medical privacy regime
**Clock:** Clinical turnaround expectations measured in hours, and the appointment the result has to reach
**Buyer:** Chief Medical Officer / VP of Research & Development
