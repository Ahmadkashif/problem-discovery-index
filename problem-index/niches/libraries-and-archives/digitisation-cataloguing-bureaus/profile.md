# Digitisation & Cataloguing Service Bureaus

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/libraries-and-archives|Libraries & Archives]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Imaging bureaus and cataloguing outsourcers — Backstage Library Works, Crowley, regional imaging vendors, scanning centres run by the Internet Archive — scan books, newspapers, microfilm and archival collections and create catalogue records or metadata for libraries, priced per page, per image or per record.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Production imaging technicians and cataloguers, often dozens to hundreds; no research function (estimate)
**Output:** Image files, OCR text, MARC records and metadata to the client's specification
**Proprietary data:** None of consequence; the content and records belong to the client
**Clock:** Grant end dates and client project schedules
**Buyer:** Head of digitisation or technical services at the client library
