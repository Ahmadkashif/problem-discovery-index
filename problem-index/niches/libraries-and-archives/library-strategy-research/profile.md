# Library Strategy & User Research Advisors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/libraries-and-archives|Libraries & Archives]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Ithaka S+R, the research and consulting arm of the nonprofit that runs JSTOR and Portico, runs recurring national surveys of faculty, students and library directors and sells local surveys, assessments and strategy projects to libraries and universities. Around it sit small library consultancies doing strategic plans, space studies and system selection, most with one to five consultants.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-40 researchers at Ithaka S+R; under 10 at nearly every other firm (estimate)
**Output:** National survey reports, local survey reports and benchmarks, strategy and assessment studies
**Proprietary data:** Longitudinal survey series on faculty and library practice, run for over two decades, plus local survey results benchmarked against them
**Clock:** Weak — accreditation self-studies and strategic-plan cycles are the client's own timetable
**Buyer:** Managing director of the research and advisory unit
