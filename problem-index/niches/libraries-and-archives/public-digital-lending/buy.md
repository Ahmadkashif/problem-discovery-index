# Vendor Dashboards That Only Grade Themselves

**Niche:** [[niches/libraries-and-archives/public-digital-lending/profile|Public Library Digital Lending]]
**Industry:** [[industries/libraries-and-archives|Libraries & Archives]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Each lending platform reports its own circulation and spend; nobody shows the library cost per loan across OverDrive, hoopla and cloudLibrary side by side.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #time-series-forecasting #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver the most patron loans per licence dollar under publishers' expiring and pay-per-use terms, and whoever proves cost per loan on the library's own data and helps the selector act on it takes the library's digital content budget.

## The Problem
A library running two or three platforms cannot compare what a loan costs on each: a metered OverDrive licence, a pay-per-use hoopla borrow capped per patron per month, a cloudLibrary title on a different term. Budget is split between platforms by habit.

## What Already Exists
OverDrive Marketplace reports and its curated purchase lists; hoopla's pay-per-use dashboards and spending caps; business intelligence tools (Power BI, Tableau) that libraries' analysts use when they have one.

## The Customization Gap
The library needs licence-aware cost per loan: purchase price amortised over loans to date and expected remaining loans before expiry, against pay-per-use prices and caps; title-level matching across platforms; and holds and turnaway signals normalised so demand can be compared. A generic BI tool needs all of that modelled before it says anything useful.

## Target Customer
Digital services librarians and finance staff at public library systems with more than one lending platform.

## Impact If Solved
Budget moved to the platform and licence model that delivers the most loans per dollar for each kind of title, with evidence a director can take to the board.
