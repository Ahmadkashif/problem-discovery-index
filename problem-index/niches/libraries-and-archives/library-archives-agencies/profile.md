# Federal & State Library and Archives Agencies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/libraries-and-archives|Libraries & Archives]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Institute of Museum and Library Services funds state library agencies and runs the national Public Libraries Survey; the National Archives sets records-management rules for federal agencies, appraises their records schedules and inspects compliance; state library agencies distribute federal and state aid and collect library statistics; state archives set retention schedules for local government records.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Dozens of appraisal archivists and records-management staff at NARA; a handful of research and statistics staff at IMLS, whose data collection was halted in 2025; one to three data staff at most state library agencies (estimate)
**Output:** Records schedules and appraisal decisions, compliance reports, national and state library statistics
**Proprietary data:** National library statistics series and federal records schedules and inventories
**Clock:** The agencies set clocks rather than meet them — M-23-07 required federal agencies to manage permanent records electronically from 30 June 2024
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — federal and state agencies
