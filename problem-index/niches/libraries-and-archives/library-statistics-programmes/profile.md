# Library Association Statistics & Benchmark Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/libraries-and-archives|Libraries & Archives]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Association of Research Libraries publishes annual statistics for its members; ACRL runs an academic library benchmarking service; the Public Library Association runs Project Outcome and benchmarking surveys; ALA's research office tracks challenges and library use. Members pay through dues or subscription.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Fewer than 10 per association programme (estimate)
**Output:** Annual statistics, benchmark dashboards, survey reports
**Proprietary data:** Member-submitted statistics series
**Clock:** Annual survey cycles set by the associations themselves
**Buyer:** Association director of research or assessment
