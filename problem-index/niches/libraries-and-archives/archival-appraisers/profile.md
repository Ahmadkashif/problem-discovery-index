# Manuscript & Archive Appraisers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/libraries-and-archives|Libraries & Archives]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Independent appraisers and rare-book dealers value personal papers, manuscripts and rare books when donors give them to university archives and libraries, producing the qualified appraisal the IRS requires for a charitable deduction above $5,000, and value collections for insurance.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 1-5 per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Signed appraisal reports
**Proprietary data:** Personal sales and auction records
**Clock:** The donor's tax filing deadline
**Buyer:** The donor or the receiving institution's development office
