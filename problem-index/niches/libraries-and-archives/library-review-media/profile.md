# Library Review & Selection Media

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/libraries-and-archives|Libraries & Archives]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Booklist (published by ALA), Library Journal and School Library Journal, Kirkus Reviews, and Choice (published by ACRL for academic libraries) review thousands of new titles a year before or around publication, so selectors can decide what to buy. Libraries subscribe; selection portals license the review text; Kirkus also sells paid reviews to self-published authors.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-25 review editors per outlet, with hundreds of freelance reviewers paid per review (estimate)
**Output:** Pre-publication reviews, starred reviews, best-of and core collection lists
**Proprietary data:** Decades of reviews and star decisions — paired with library holdings and circulation, an untested record of which reviews predicted demand
**Clock:** Publication dates set by publishers: a review that runs after the on-sale date has lost most of its value to selectors
**Buyer:** Editor-in-chief / publisher of the review outlet
