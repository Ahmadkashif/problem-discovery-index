# Library Platform Vendors' Analytics & Product Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/libraries-and-archives|Libraries & Archives]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The platform vendors — Clarivate's Ex Libris and Innovative, OCLC's management services, EBSCO's FOLIO services, SirsiDynix and the open-source hosts — run analytics modules, benchmarking dashboards and product research on top of the transaction data their multi-tenant cloud platforms hold for thousands of libraries.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-100 analytics, data and product research staff at the largest vendor, far fewer at the rest (estimate)
**Output:** Analytics modules and dashboards, cross-library benchmarks, product research
**Proprietary data:** Circulation, acquisitions, licensing and usage transactions across every hosted library
**Clock:** None external
**Buyer:** Vice president of analytics or product for the library business
