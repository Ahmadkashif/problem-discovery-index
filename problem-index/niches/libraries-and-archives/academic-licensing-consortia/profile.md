# Academic Library Consortia Licensing Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/libraries-and-archives|Libraries & Archives]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Library consortia — the California Digital Library for the University of California, Lyrasis, the Big Ten Academic Alliance, OhioLINK, state and regional consortia — negotiate journal packages, databases and ebook deals for their members, increasingly including open-access publishing terms. Their licensing staff analyse members' usage, price histories and publishing output to set negotiating positions and decide whether to walk away. Members fund the work through dues and cost-sharing.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically under 10 licensing and analysis staff, even at the largest: the California Digital Library's systemwide licensing unit is an assistant director, licensing analysts and a licensing services manager; no consortium with a 10+ analysis team was found (estimate)
**Output:** Negotiation analyses, cost-per-use and open-access output reports for members, licence terms
**Proprietary data:** Pooled usage, price and cancellation history across members, held under members' ownership and publishers' non-disclosure clauses
**Clock:** Contract expiry dates in multi-year publisher agreements, typically year-end
**Buyer:** Consortium executive director or director of licensing
