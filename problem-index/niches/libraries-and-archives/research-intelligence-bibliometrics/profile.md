# Research Intelligence & Bibliometrics Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/libraries-and-archives|Libraries & Archives]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Clarivate (Web of Science, Journal Citation Reports, InCites), Elsevier (Scopus, SciVal) and Digital Science (Dimensions) index the scholarly literature, link its citations and sell citation indexes, journal metrics and research-performance analytics. Academic libraries are the main subscribers to the indexes; research offices and university leaders buy the analytics for benchmarking, hiring and rankings submissions. Web of Science's journal selection is made by in-house editors, and its decisions move markets for journals: eLife was put on hold in October 2024.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~100-400 editors, bibliometric analysts and data scientists per vendor, excluding citation-linking production staff (estimate)
**Output:** Curated citation indexes, journal impact metrics, journal selection and suppression decisions, institutional and researcher benchmarking reports
**Proprietary data:** Citation indexes running back decades, journal evaluation records and suppression histories that no newcomer can rebuild
**Clock:** None external on the vendor; the annual metrics release is the vendor's own date, and rankings and national assessment timetables bind its clients rather than it
**Buyer:** Head of research analytics / editor-in-chief of the citation index
