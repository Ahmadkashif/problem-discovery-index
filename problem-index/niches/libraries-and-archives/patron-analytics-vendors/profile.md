# Patron Analytics & Library Marketing Data Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/libraries-and-archives|Libraries & Archives]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Small vendors segment a library's cardholders by geography and demographics, run email marketing and report on which neighbourhoods use the library, combining ILS extracts with census and commercial lifestyle data. Gale Analytics, OrangeBoy's Savannah and LibraryIQ are examples; public library systems pay subscriptions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analysts per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Patron segmentation, outreach campaigns, usage maps
**Proprietary data:** None of their own — patron records belong to the library and are protected by state confidentiality laws
**Clock:** Budget and board-reporting cycles set by clients
**Buyer:** Library marketing or community engagement director
