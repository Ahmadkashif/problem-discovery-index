# OCLC Cooperative Data & Collection Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/libraries-and-archives|Libraries & Archives]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
OCLC is the member cooperative behind WorldCat, the union catalogue of library holdings, and the main interlibrary loan network. Beyond cataloguing and resource-sharing subscriptions it sells collection evaluation and shared-print analysis (including the GreenGlass service), runs OCLC Research, and reported $262.7M of library services revenue in FY2025. It guards the data closely: its 2022 suit against Clarivate ended with Clarivate abandoning the MetaDoor record-exchange project.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-60 researchers, data scientists and collection analysts (OCLC Research took 8% of a $37.6M R&D budget in FY2025); metadata quality staff are production (estimate)
**Output:** Collection overlap and deselection analyses for libraries and consortia, shared-print retention recommendations, research reports
**Proprietary data:** WorldCat's holdings for tens of thousands of libraries and the interlibrary loan request history — the only cross-library view of what libraries hold and borrow
**Clock:** Weak — shared-print retention commitments and building projects set dates, but they are clients' schedules
**Buyer:** OCLC's vice president for research and data services
