# Digital Preservation & Repository Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/libraries-and-archives|Libraries & Archives]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Preservica, Portico (part of ITHAKA), APTrust, Lyrasis's hosted services, Archivematica service providers and LOCKSS networks store, check and migrate libraries' and archives' digital collections and the scholarly journals they license, so that files remain readable for decades. Libraries and publishers pay subscriptions or membership fees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Engineering, operations and support staff; no dedicated research or analysis function (estimate)
**Output:** Preservation storage, fixity checks, format migration, preservation reports
**Proprietary data:** Format and fixity records for the content they hold on others' behalf
**Clock:** None external
**Buyer:** Head of digital preservation at the client institution
