# SaaS Spend Management for Research Subscriptions

**Niche:** [[niches/libraries-and-archives/law-and-corporate-research-libraries/profile|Law Firm & Corporate Research Libraries]]
**Industry:** [[industries/libraries-and-archives|Libraries & Archives]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** SaaS management platforms already find unused licences across a company; research content has contract structures they do not understand.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #feature-engineering #data-integration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show, subscription by subscription and practice group by practice group, which licensed research content is used and recoverable before each renewal, and whoever makes the content budget defensible to the CFO takes the account.

## The Problem
IT tracks software licences in one tool; the library tracks research content in spreadsheets; finance pays both.

## What Already Exists
SaaS management and spend platforms (Zylo, Productiv, Vendr) that discover subscriptions through single sign-on and expense data and benchmark prices.

## The Customization Gap
Research content contracts have flat-rate plans with out-of-plan charges, practice-area modules, named-user versus concurrent seats, IP-authenticated access, pass-through terms for client billing, and individual journal and newsletter subscriptions bought on cards. The platforms need content-specific usage connectors and contract models before their recommendations mean anything here.

## Target Customer
Law firm and corporate library directors working with IT procurement.

## Impact If Solved
One view of software and content spend, with research-specific savings the generic tool would miss.
