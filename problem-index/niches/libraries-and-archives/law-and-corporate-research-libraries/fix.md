# Out-of-Plan Charges Found at Month End

**Niche:** [[niches/libraries-and-archives/law-and-corporate-research-libraries/profile|Law Firm & Corporate Research Libraries]]
**Industry:** [[industries/libraries-and-archives|Libraries & Archives]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A lawyer opens a database outside the firm's plan, the charge appears on next month's invoice, and the library spends days deciding who pays.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #quick-win #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show, subscription by subscription and practice group by practice group, which licensed research content is used and recoverable before each renewal, and whoever makes the content budget defensible to the CFO takes the account.

## The Problem
Flat-rate research plans exclude some content. Out-of-plan use triggers charges that may or may not be billable to a client, and the library learns of them from the invoice.

## Why It's Still Broken
The warning screens vendors show are clicked through, and the invoice arrives weeks after the work.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Daily ingestion of out-of-plan transactions, matched to the user's matter, with an alert to the library and the user the same day and an in-plan alternative suggested.

## Who Feels the Pain
Research librarians reconciling invoices; billing staff writing off charges.

## Impact If Fixed
Charges caught while they can still be recovered or avoided.
