# Chiropractic Academic Research Centres

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/chiropractic-practices|Chiropractic Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Palmer Center for Chiropractic Research and comparable college-based centres conduct the clinical trials and outcomes research that establish whether chiropractic care works for a given condition — the evidence base that guideline writers and payers cite when setting coverage.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-50 investigators, coordinators, and biostatisticians
**Output:** Published clinical research and grant-funded studies
**Proprietary data:** Trial data held under IRB protocols with restricted secondary use
**Clock:** Grant reporting and publication cycles
**Buyer:** Centre director — grant funded, not commercial
