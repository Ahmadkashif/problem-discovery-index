# Spinal Imaging Analysis Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/chiropractic-practices|Chiropractic Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors selling automated spinal measurement and postural analysis — vertebral angle measurement from radiographs, digital posture assessment, and range-of-motion capture — to chiropractors who need objective findings to justify care to payers. Their clinical and data teams build and validate the measurement algorithms and the normative comparisons behind them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 clinical scientists, imaging engineers, and validation staff
**Output:** Measurement algorithms, normative reference data, documentation-ready findings
**Proprietary data:** Annotated spinal imaging and postural measurement data accumulated across clinics
**Clock:** Product release cycles and payer documentation requirement changes
**Buyer:** VP of Clinical Affairs / Chief Technology Officer
