# Healthcare Cost Benchmark Data Organizations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/chiropractic-practices|Chiropractic Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
FAIR Health and comparable independent benchmark bodies hold enormous repositories of de-identified private healthcare claims and publish the cost benchmarks used to determine out-of-network reimbursement, including for chiropractic and other conservative care. Insurers, providers, regulators, researchers, and courts all rely on the benchmarks, which is why the organization was constituted as independent in the first place.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-200 data scientists, health economists, and research staff
**Output:** Benchmark databases, cost transparency products, published research studies
**Proprietary data:** Tens of billions of de-identified claim records contributed by payers under agreements that expressly permit benchmark publication
**Clock:** Data release cycles and regulatory reference obligations
**Buyer:** Chief Research Officer / VP of Data Products
