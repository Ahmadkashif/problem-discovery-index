# Chiropractic Malpractice Underwriting Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/chiropractic-practices|Chiropractic Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
NCMIC and the chiropractic malpractice carriers research claim experience by technique, patient presentation, and practice pattern to price coverage and to publish the risk management guidance the profession follows.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 underwriters, actuaries, and risk management staff
**Output:** Rating models, risk management guidance, claim trend analyses
**Proprietary data:** The profession's malpractice claim history — effectively complete, since one carrier dominates the line
**Clock:** Rate filings and renewal cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
