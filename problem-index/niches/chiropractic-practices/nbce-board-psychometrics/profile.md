# Chiropractic Board Examination & Practice Analysis

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/chiropractic-practices|Chiropractic Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The National Board of Chiropractic Examiners writes and administers the examinations every state licenses on, and conducts the periodic national practice analysis that defines what chiropractors actually do — the study that curricula, licensure, and payer policy all reference.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-80 test development staff, psychometricians, and research coordinators
**Output:** Board examinations, the national practice analysis, technical reports
**Proprietary data:** Decades of item performance and candidate outcome data, plus the only systematic survey of chiropractic practice patterns nationally
**Clock:** Examination administration windows and accreditation review cycles
**Buyer:** Director of Testing / VP of Research
