# State Chiropractic Licensing Boards

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/chiropractic-practices|Chiropractic Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State boards license chiropractors, define scope of practice, and adjudicate complaints and disciplinary actions. Staff investigate complaints and maintain the licensure and discipline record for the state.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 2-10 per state
**Output:** Licensure decisions, disciplinary findings, scope-of-practice determinations
**Proprietary data:** Complaint and discipline history within the state
**Clock:** Statutory adjudication timelines and licence renewal cycles
**Buyer:** Executive Director
