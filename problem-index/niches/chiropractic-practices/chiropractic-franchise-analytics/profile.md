# Chiropractic Franchise & DSO Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/chiropractic-practices|Chiropractic Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The development and analytics groups at The Joint Corp, HealthSource, and the chiropractic management organizations. They run site selection, model membership economics, and benchmark clinic performance across networks of several hundred to a thousand locations.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 analysts across development, franchise support, and operations
**Output:** Site selection models, membership and retention analytics, franchisee benchmark reporting
**Proprietary data:** Visit, membership, and retention data across a large clinic network — the only view of chiropractic consumer behaviour at scale
**Clock:** Franchise disclosure updates and development schedules
**Buyer:** VP of Analytics / Chief Development Officer
