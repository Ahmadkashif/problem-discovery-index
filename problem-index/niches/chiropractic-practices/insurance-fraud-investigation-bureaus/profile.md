# Insurance Fraud Investigation Bureaus

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/chiropractic-practices|Chiropractic Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The National Insurance Crime Bureau and insurer special investigation units analyze claim patterns to identify organized fraud, including the clinic and provider rings that periodically implicate chiropractic in staged-accident schemes. Analysts build cases, refer to law enforcement, and publish pattern alerts to members.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-400 analysts, investigators, and data scientists
**Output:** Fraud case referrals, pattern alerts, member intelligence products
**Proprietary data:** Member-contributed claim and investigation data joined to prosecution outcomes across the industry
**Clock:** Investigation and prosecution timelines
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — funded by member insurers
