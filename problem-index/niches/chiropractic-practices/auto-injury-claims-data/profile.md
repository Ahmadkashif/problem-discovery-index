# Auto Injury Claims Data & Medical Review Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/chiropractic-practices|Chiropractic Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Verisk's casualty solutions, ISO ClaimSearch, and Mitchell's auto casualty products hold the industry-wide claims database that soft-tissue injury claims are adjudicated against — the segment that carries much of chiropractic revenue. They license claim history, treatment pattern analytics, provider profiling, and reasonableness benchmarks to auto insurers, who use them to decide what a course of care is worth and whether a provider's billing pattern is an outlier.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-800 data scientists, actuaries, casualty analysts, and product staff
**Output:** Claims databases, provider and treatment pattern analytics, reasonableness benchmarks — the licensed product
**Proprietary data:** A contributed all-industry claims repository covering effectively the entire US property and casualty market, joined to settlement and litigation outcomes
**Clock:** Claim adjudication cycles and product release schedules
**Buyer:** Chief Product Officer / VP of Casualty Analytics
