# Independent Medical Examination Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/chiropractic-practices|Chiropractic Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
ExamWorks, MES, and the IME networks arrange examinations and peer reviews that determine whether a course of chiropractic or conservative care was medically necessary. Physicians examine or review records and produce a written opinion; insurers, employers, and defence counsel commission it and rely on it to deny, limit, or settle. The report is unambiguously the invoice, and it is routinely challenged in litigation.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-3,000 across coordination, medical review, and quality assurance
**Output:** IME and peer review reports — the billable artefact
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated exam findings and, more valuably, the record of which opinions survived challenge, deposition, and hearing, by examiner and jurisdiction
**Clock:** Litigation schedules, utilization review turnaround statutes, and claim deadlines
**Buyer:** Chief Medical Officer / VP of Clinical Quality
