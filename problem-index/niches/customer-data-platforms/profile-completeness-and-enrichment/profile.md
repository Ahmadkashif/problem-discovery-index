# Profile Completeness & Enrichment

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/customer-data-platforms|Customer Data Platforms]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to say what is missing from a profile and what can be inferred rather than bought — and whoever does that replaces a third-party data purchase with the organisation's own observations.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$350M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — buy data, do not infer it
**Target Buyer:** Data and marketing leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — inference from first-party behaviour is well-posed

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Profiles are incomplete in specific, patterned ways: attributes missing for identifiable reasons, present for some customers and not others, stale in predictable places. The standard response is to buy third-party data, which is expensive, of uncertain accuracy, increasingly constrained by privacy regimes, and frequently inferior to what the organisation could infer from its own observed behaviour. This is a distinct contest because it is a prediction problem over first-party data with a clear commercial alternative, and the alternative is what most organisations currently buy.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Third-party data appends, progressive profiling forms, occasional inference rules, and completeness percentages. The gaps: completeness measured against a schema rather than against what is needed; no inference from behaviour; purchased attributes unvalidated; staleness unmodelled; and no view of which gaps actually cost anything.
