# The Data Engineer

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/customer-data-platforms|Customer Data Platforms]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give the one person accountable for event consistency a way to enforce it on teams they do not control — and whoever does that resolves an accountability gap the whole architecture creates.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — finds out after it ships
**Target Buyer:** Data engineering leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — detection and enforcement are automatable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
One data engineer is accountable for the consistency of an event stream produced by a dozen product teams who have no reason to care, and finds out about every change after it ships. They have responsibility without authority — the defining condition of the role — and their tooling gives them detection at best and nothing at the point where a change is made. This is distinct from event governance as a systems problem: that builds the contract, this is about the person holding a line across an organisational boundary they cannot cross.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Validation warnings, event debugging tools, a tracking plan they maintain alone, and relationships with product teams. The gaps: no presence in the development workflow; notification after deployment; no visibility of upcoming changes; no way to make the cost of a break visible to the team causing it; and an escalation path that damages relationships they depend on.
