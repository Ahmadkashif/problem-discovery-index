# Identity Resolution Accuracy

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/customer-data-platforms|Customer Data Platforms]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to measure how often the identity graph merges two people or splits one — and whoever makes that measurable turns the category's central function from a threshold into an engineering discipline.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.3B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~26% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — probabilistic and unmeasured
**Target Buyer:** Platform and data leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the measurement is a well-posed problem

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every downstream use of customer data depends on deciding which records are the same person, that decision is probabilistic, and essentially no organisation has measured how often it is wrong or in which direction. An over-merge combines two people, so one person's history informs another's experience and in the worst case their data is shown to them. An under-merge splits one person, breaking suppression, double-counting customers and understating lifetime value. Neither appears in any dashboard. This is the largest concentration of contested value in the category because it is the foundation everything else stands on.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Deterministic matching on shared identifiers, probabilistic matching with configurable thresholds, vendor-reported match rates, and household-level heuristics. The gaps: no measured error rate in either direction; thresholds inherited from defaults; no way to choose the trade-off deliberately; no ground truth; and no visibility of a merge decision's downstream consequences.
