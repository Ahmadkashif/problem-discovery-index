# Corporate Immigration Practice Groups

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/immigration-law|Immigration Law Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup

## What They Do
The large corporate immigration firms handle employment-based immigration at industrial scale for multinational employers — hundreds of thousands of filings a year across dozens of countries, with knowledge management functions maintaining global rules and internal precedent.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,500 attorneys, paralegals, knowledge managers, and country researchers
**Output:** Filings and petitions, RFE responses, global mobility advice, employer compliance programmes
**Proprietary data:** Case outcomes across hundreds of thousands of filings — which arguments and evidence succeeded, with which service centre, on which petition type
**Clock:** Statutory filing windows, cap season, and RFE response deadlines
**Buyer:** Chief Knowledge Officer / Managing Partner
