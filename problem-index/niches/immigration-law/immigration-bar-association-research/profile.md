# Immigration Bar Association Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/immigration-law|Immigration Law Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The immigration bar association publishes practice advisories, maintains agency liaison relationships, and collects member reports of what is actually happening at service centres and consular posts — the nearest thing that exists to a shared record of adjudication behaviour.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-150 attorneys, practice advisory authors, and liaison staff
**Output:** Practice advisories, agency liaison minutes, member alerts, litigation support materials
**Proprietary data:** Member-reported adjudication experience across the country, aggregated from practitioners who see one office each
**Clock:** Policy changes and agency liaison meeting cycles
**Buyer:** Director of Practice Resources — member funded
