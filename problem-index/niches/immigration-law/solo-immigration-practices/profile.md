# Solo & Boutique Immigration Practices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/immigration-law|Immigration Law Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
The operator layer: 14,000 firms and attorneys, typically one attorney per 100-200 active cases with heavy paralegal leverage. Pass 1 identifies the asset precisely — experienced immigration attorneys develop highly specific pattern recognition about which arguments succeed with which field offices and adjudicators, and it lives in individual heads.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1-15 people, of whom the pattern recognition is one or two attorneys
**Output:** Petitions, RFE responses, hearing representation, client strategy
**Proprietary data:** A career of observed adjudication behaviour, entirely undocumented
**Clock:** Filing windows and RFE response deadlines
**Buyer:** The attorney
