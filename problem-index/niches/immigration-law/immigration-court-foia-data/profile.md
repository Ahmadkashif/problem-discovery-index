# Immigration Court FOIA Data Projects

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/immigration-law|Immigration Law Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Academic data projects obtain immigration court and enforcement records through freedom of information litigation and publish case-level statistics on outcomes by court, judge, and nationality — the only public window into adjudication variation.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 researchers, data staff, and FOIA specialists
**Output:** Case-level datasets, judge and court outcome statistics, enforcement trend reports
**Proprietary data:** Decades of records obtained through sustained FOIA litigation that nobody else has assembled
**Clock:** Data release cycles and litigation timelines
**Buyer:** Project director — grant funded
