**Parent Industry:** [[industries/immigration-law|Immigration Law Firms]]

## What They Do
INSZoom, Docketwise, and the case management platforms run deadline tracking, form assembly, and status logging for a large share of US immigration practices, and hold the filings and outcomes those firms record.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 product and data staff
**Output:** Deadline tracking, form automation, case status reporting
**Proprietary data:** Filings, RFEs, and outcomes across thousands of firms — the outcome corpus that no individual firm has enough of
**Clock:** Filing and RFE response deadlines
**Buyer:** Head of Product
