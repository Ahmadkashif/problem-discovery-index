# Country Conditions Expert Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/immigration-law|Immigration Law Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Country conditions experts research and testify to the situation an asylum applicant fears returning to — persecution patterns, state protection, regional variation — producing the report and testimony an immigration judge weighs.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 country researchers and expert witnesses per organization
**Output:** Country conditions reports, expert declarations, hearing testimony
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated country research and a record of which framings persuaded which courts
**Clock:** Hearing dates, which do not move
**Buyer:** Director / Principal
