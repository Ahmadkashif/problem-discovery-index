# EB-5 Economic Impact Analysis

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/immigration-law|Immigration Law Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Economists produce the job creation studies EB-5 regional centre projects must file, modelling the direct and indirect employment an investment generates under methodologies USCIS accepts.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 economists and analysts
**Output:** Economic impact reports, job creation models, methodology defence on RFEs
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated methodology precedent and the record of which modelling approaches USCIS has accepted or challenged
**Clock:** Filing deadlines and project financing timelines
**Buyer:** Principal / Managing Director
