# Immigration Legal Research Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/immigration-law|Immigration Law Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Specialist publishers maintain the corpus immigration practice actually runs on — USCIS policy manual provisions, agency memoranda, Administrative Appeals Office decisions, the Foreign Affairs Manual, and practice advisories on how each is being applied. Pass 1 names the gap precisely: Westlaw and Lexis cover case law and not the USCIS policy memo and AAO decision landscape that is most relevant to this practice.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 immigration attorneys, editors, and legal analysts
**Output:** Annotated practice treatises, policy alerts, practice advisories, form and filing guidance
**Proprietary data:** A curated and annotated agency policy corpus built over decades, with practitioner interpretation of provisions the agency itself does not explain
**Clock:** Policy memoranda and rule changes that take effect immediately and reshape live filings
**Buyer:** Editor-in-chief / VP of Content
