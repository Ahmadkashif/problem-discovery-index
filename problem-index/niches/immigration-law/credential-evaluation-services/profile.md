# Foreign Credential Evaluation Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/immigration-law|Immigration Law Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Credential evaluators determine whether a foreign degree is equivalent to a US bachelor's or higher — the finding that decides whether an H-1B beneficiary qualifies for a specialty occupation, whether a professional licence application proceeds, and whether a university admits. USCIS, licensing boards, and employers rely on the report; the applicant's eligibility turns on it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 credential analysts, comparative education researchers, and country specialists
**Output:** Equivalency reports, course-by-course evaluations, institutional recognition determinations
**Proprietary data:** Decades of institution-level recognition research and equivalency determinations covering thousands of foreign institutions and education systems, most of it unpublished anywhere
**Clock:** Petition filing deadlines and cap season, where a delayed evaluation loses a filing window entirely
**Buyer:** President / VP of Evaluation Services
