# Corporate Immigration Compliance Auditing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/immigration-law|Immigration Law Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Specialist firms audit employers' I-9 records, H-1B public access files, and export control screening, preparing them for the ICE and DOL inspections that carry per-violation penalties across an entire workforce.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 compliance specialists, auditors, and attorneys
**Output:** Audit findings, remediation plans, inspection response support, compliance programme design
**Proprietary data:** Error patterns across many employer populations and the enforcement outcomes that followed
**Clock:** Inspection notices with three-day response windows, and audit cycles
**Buyer:** Practice leader / Managing Director
