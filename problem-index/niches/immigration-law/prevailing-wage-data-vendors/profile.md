# Prevailing Wage & Labour Condition Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/immigration-law|Immigration Law Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Vendors package the Department of Labor's disclosure data — every labour condition application and PERM filing, with employer, job title, worksite, and wage — into the tools employers and counsel use to set wages and structure filings.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 data engineers and analysts
**Output:** Wage level determinations, filing comparables, employer and market benchmarking tools
**Proprietary data:** Structuring and normalization over a fully public disclosure corpus
**Clock:** Prevailing wage determination cycles and filing seasons
**Buyer:** Head of Product
