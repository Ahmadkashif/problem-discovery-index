# USCIS Adjudication & Administrative Appeals

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/immigration-law|Immigration Law Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Service centre adjudicators and the Administrative Appeals Office decide every petition, issue every Request for Evidence, and produce the decisions the entire practice is built around predicting.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 2,000-10,000 adjudicators, officers, and policy staff
**Output:** Adjudication decisions, Requests for Evidence, appeal decisions, policy manual updates
**Proprietary data:** Every petition filed, every RFE issued, every decision and its reasoning, by officer and service centre — the definitive answer to the question Pass 1 says experienced attorneys spend careers guessing at
**Clock:** Processing time targets and statutory windows
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — federal agency
