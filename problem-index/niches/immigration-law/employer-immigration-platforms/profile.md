# Employer Immigration Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/immigration-law|Immigration Law Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Technology-led providers manage employer immigration programmes end to end — intake, document collection, filing coordination through affiliated counsel, and status reporting — sitting between the employer paying and the legal work being done.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 case managers, analysts, and product staff
**Output:** Programme reporting, case status and forecasting, cost and timeline analytics for employers
**Proprietary data:** Case pipelines and processing outcomes across many employers, joined to filing characteristics
**Clock:** Cap season and filing windows
**Buyer:** Chief Product Officer / VP of Data
