# Salvage Auction Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/property-casualty-insurers|Property & Casualty Insurers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Copart and IAA, now part of RB Global (recalled), auction the vehicles insurers declare total losses, and handle towing, storage, title processing and sale. Insurers are the main sellers, and the auction proceeds are netted against the total-loss payment.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 pricing and data analysts beside thousands of yard and title staff (estimate)
**Output:** Salvage value estimates, auction results, cycle-time and title reporting to insurer clients
**Proprietary data:** Millions of salvage sales with damage, title and sale-price outcomes (estimate)
**Clock:** Storage-cost accrual and state title and salvage-branding deadlines
**Buyer:** Head of Insurance Services Analytics
