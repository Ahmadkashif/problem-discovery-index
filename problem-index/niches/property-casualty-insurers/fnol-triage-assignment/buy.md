# Contact-Centre AI Taught the Claims Taxonomy

**Niche:** [[niches/property-casualty-insurers/fnol-triage-assignment/profile|First Notice of Loss Triage & Claim Assignment]]
**Industry:** [[industries/property-casualty-insurers|Property & Casualty Insurers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Voice and chat AI platforms can take a first notice of loss; they need to ask the questions an experienced intake rep asks and capture the answers a claims system can route on.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #seq2seq #data-integration #automation #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to route each new claim to the right path — straight-through payment, desk, field, total loss or fraud referral — at first notice, and whoever cuts reassignments without paying a fraudulent or escalating claim on autopilot takes the account.

## The Problem
Many first notices still arrive by phone, often at night or during a catastrophe surge. Intake reps follow scripts, but the best ones listen for what matters: an injury, a third party at fault, a business use of the vehicle, a gap in coverage.

## What Already Exists
Contact-centre AI and conversational platforms, and digital FNOL forms in core claims systems.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: line-specific question flows that branch on coverage and loss type; extraction of injury mentions, third-party details and subrogation cues into structured fields; coverage verification against the policy during the call; catastrophe-surge modes with simplified flows; state-required disclosures and acknowledgement deadlines; and hand-off to a human whenever distress or injury is detected.

## Target Customer
Claims intake operations at carriers with large phone FNOL volumes and catastrophe exposure.

## Impact If Solved
Consistent, complete first notices at any hour or volume, with the routing-critical facts captured on the first call.
