# The Injury Mentioned on the First Call and Lost

**Niche:** [[niches/property-casualty-insurers/fnol-triage-assignment/profile|First Notice of Loss Triage & Claim Assignment]]
**Industry:** [[industries/property-casualty-insurers|Property & Casualty Insurers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The other driver said her back hurt, the intake rep typed it into a free-text note, and the claim went to a physical-damage desk for three weeks.
**Tags:** #bert #large-language-models #quick-win #automation #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to route each new claim to the right path — straight-through payment, desk, field, total loss or fraud referral — at first notice, and whoever cuts reassignments without paying a fraudulent or escalating claim on autopilot takes the account.

## The Problem
Early injury signals are the strongest predictor that a claim will escalate, and they are often captured only in free text at intake. The claim is routed on the structured fields, which say "property damage only".

## Why It's Still Broken
Intake forms have no reliable structured injury field for third parties, and nobody reads intake notes once the claim is assigned.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Scan intake notes and call transcripts for injury, treatment and attorney mentions as they are written. Set the structured flags, reroute the claim to the injury unit, and alert the assigned adjuster the same day.

## Who Feels the Pain
Injury adjusters inheriting claims weeks late, and carriers paying for the delay in severity.

## Impact If Fixed
Injury claims handled from day one, at the point where early contact most affects the outcome.
