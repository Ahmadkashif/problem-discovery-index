# First Notice of Loss Triage & Claim Assignment

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/property-casualty-insurers|Property & Casualty Insurers]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to route each new claim to the right path — straight-through payment, desk, field, total loss or fraud referral — at first notice, and whoever cuts reassignments without paying a fraudulent or escalating claim on autopilot takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$5B a year of claims intake, call-centre, digital FNOL, assignment and early-handling cost across personal and small commercial lines (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1.5% of US P&C carriers' underwriting and loss-adjustment expense (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium-High — digital and app FNOL are common at large carriers; assignment rules are static and reassignment is frequent
**Target Buyer:** VP Claims Operations / Head of Claims Intake
**Automation Potential:** Very High for intake, segmentation and straight-through handling of simple claims; low for the claims the triage sends to people

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every claim starts with the same decision: what kind of claim is this, and who should handle it? Personal auto alone wrote $371.0B of premium in 2025 (NAIC) and generates millions of claims a year (estimate). Most are simple glass, towing or minor physical damage claims. A few are total losses, injury claims that will escalate, or staged accidents. Assignment rules built on line, coverage and a severity guess send claims to the wrong desk, and every reassignment adds days and handling cost. The same intake also feeds fraud scoring, subrogation screening and the first reserve.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Digital FNOL in core claims systems, photo-estimating apps (the vault's auto-body-shops niche [[niches/auto-body-shops/photo-based-estimating/profile|⚡ Photo-Based Damage Estimating]]), fraud scoring at intake, and rules-based assignment. The systems vendors' own contest over first-notice routing is the insurtech sub-niche [[niches/insurtech-platforms/claims-core-systems/profile|🎯 Claims Core Systems]]; this niche is the carrier's operating contest on top of whichever system it runs. The gaps: static rules that do not learn from reassignments; triage blind to injury and litigation risk; straight-through thresholds set conservatively because nobody measures what they let through.
