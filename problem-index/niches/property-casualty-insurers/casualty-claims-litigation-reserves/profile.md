# Casualty Claims Litigation & Reserve Adequacy

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/property-casualty-insurers|Property & Casualty Insurers]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to spot, within days of first notice, the liability claims that will draw an attorney and run toward a verdict, and whoever moves those claims to early resolution or an adequate reserve before the demand letter arrives takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$40B a year of defence, cost-containment and adjusting expense on commercial auto, general liability and personal auto bodily injury claims, of which ~$3B is analytics, litigation-management and claims-technology spend (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of US P&C carriers' underwriting and loss-adjustment expense (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — claims systems are modern at large carriers, but the evidence that decides a claim's path is unstructured notes, letters and medical records
**Target Buyer:** Chief Claims Officer / VP Casualty Claims, with the Chief Actuary as co-buyer
**Automation Potential:** Medium — intake, extraction and triage automate; evaluation and negotiation stay with licensed adjusters

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is where social inflation lands. Nearly 200 corporate verdicts of $10M or more were returned in 2025, up 40.7% (Marathon Strategies). In the same year other liability occurrence and commercial auto liability still developed adversely by about $7.3B and $1.8B while personal lines released $15.9B (NAIC). Other liability and commercial auto together wrote $216.6B of direct premium in 2025 (NAIC). The claims department's early decisions determine whether a claim settles before an attorney is retained, and the reserving actuary inherits whatever those decisions leave behind.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Claims systems (Guidewire ClaimCenter, Duck Creek), claims-litigation analytics (CLARA Analytics and in-house models), verdict and settlement reporters, bill review, and reserving software (WTW ResQ, Milliman Arius). The systems vendors compete separately over reserving and routing functionality (the insurtech sub-niche [[niches/insurtech-platforms/claims-core-systems/profile|🎯 Claims Core Systems]]); this niche is the carrier's own contest to read and resolve escalating liability claims. The gaps: no structured record of the adjuster's early read; reserve changes made without recorded reasons; litigation analytics that score claims after suit rather than before representation.
