# Competitive Rate Intelligence Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/property-casualty-insurers|Property & Casualty Insurers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms that turn carriers' public rate filings into working rating engines and sell competitive premium comparisons. Quadrant Information Services has done this since 1991 and supports more than 20,000 carrier rate sets across all states and DC, refreshed monthly from filings received daily. S&P Global Market Intelligence sells rate filing data and analysis. Carriers' product managers buy rate-position studies, and comparison publishers buy quotes.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 rate analysts and custom-study staff per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Competitive premium comparisons by profile and territory, rate-change tracking, custom positioning studies
**Proprietary data:** Decades of encoded rating algorithms and historical rate sets; the inputs are public filings anyone can read
**Clock:** Competitors' rate effective dates and clients' own filing cycles; refresh cadence is self-set
**Buyer:** President / Head of Insurance Analytics
