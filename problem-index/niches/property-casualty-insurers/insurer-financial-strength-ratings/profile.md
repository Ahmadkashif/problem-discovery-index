# Insurer Financial Strength Rating Agencies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/property-casualty-insurers|Property & Casualty Insurers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Agencies that rate insurers' ability to pay claims. AM Best is the specialist incumbent across P&C. Demotech rates many Florida-domiciled homeowners carriers whose policies need an acceptable rating for mortgage lenders (recalled), and has been an SEC-registered NRSRO for insurance-company ratings since July 2022. KBRA and the large credit rating agencies rate others. Insurers pay for the rating because agents, commercial buyers, lenders and reinsurers require one. This is not a duplicate of [[niches/financial-data-vendors/credit-rating-agency-analytics/profile|🔍 Credit Rating Agency Analytics]], which covers only Moody's, S&P and Fitch and their issuer and instrument ratings.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-400 rating analysts at the largest specialist agency; tens at smaller ones (estimate)
**Output:** Financial strength and issuer credit ratings, capital adequacy assessments, rating reports and outlooks, catastrophe-stress reviews
**Proprietary data:** Decades of rating histories against insurer impairments, plus confidential submissions from rated companies — business plans, reinsurance programmes, capital model outputs
**Clock:** Annual rating reviews, post-catastrophe reviews, and mortgage-lender and reinsurer acceptance requirements that make a downgrade an immediate business event
**Buyer:** Chief Rating Officer

**Kill switches:** **data isn't theirs** — every rating rests on confidential submissions the rated insurer supplies under its rating agreement (business plans, reinsurance programmes, capital model outputs, management meetings), and that material may be used only for the rating, not pooled or reused. NRSRO oversight shapes the process but is not itself the kill switch. Scored consistently with Credit Rating Agency Analytics (47, kill switch); the life-side surveillance research on public filings is logged as [[niches/life-insurance-carriers/insurer-financial-surveillance-research/profile|🔍 Life Insurer Financial Surveillance & Carrier Due-Diligence Research]] (36).
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. A clean insight-as-invoice product with an outcome-labelled archive (ratings against later impairments), built on confidential submissions the rated insurers own, in a market of a few agencies.
