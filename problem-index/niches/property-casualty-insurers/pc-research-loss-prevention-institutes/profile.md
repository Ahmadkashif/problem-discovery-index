# P&C Research & Loss-Prevention Institutes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/property-casualty-insurers|Property & Casualty Insurers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Insurer-funded research bodies. The Insurance Institute for Business & Home Safety tests roofs, walls and vents against wind, hail and wildfire at full scale and runs the FORTIFIED programme (recalled). The Insurance Research Council publishes claims and litigation studies, and Triple-I and the Casualty Actuarial Society publish industry research. Members and sponsors fund the work and the output is mostly free.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-60 engineers, scientists and researchers at the largest; a handful at the others (estimate)
**Output:** Building-science test results, mitigation standards, closed-claim and litigation studies, public research reports
**Proprietary data:** Full-scale test results and closed-claim survey data no carrier collects alone
**Clock:** Self-set research calendars
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — funded by member insurers
