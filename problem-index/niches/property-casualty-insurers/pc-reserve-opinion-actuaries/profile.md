# P&C Reserve Opinion & Reserve Review Actuaries

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/property-casualty-insurers|Property & Casualty Insurers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
The P&C practices of Milliman, WTW, Oliver Wyman, the Big Four and specialist firms. They serve as appointed actuaries for carriers without one on staff, review reserves for management, auditors and boards, and price and value books in M&A and loss-portfolio transfers. Every P&C carrier files a Statement of Actuarial Opinion with its annual statement by 1 March, and the confidential Actuarial Opinion Summary goes to the domiciliary state by 15 March.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-800 P&C actuaries and analysts per large firm (estimate)
**Output:** Statements of Actuarial Opinion and actuarial reports, reserve reviews, loss-portfolio transfer pricing, reserve due diligence
**Proprietary data:** Benchmarks across many clients' development patterns; the claims and triangle data belong to each client
**Clock:** The 1 March opinion and 15 March summary, quarterly closes and audit timetables
**Buyer:** P&C Practice Leader
