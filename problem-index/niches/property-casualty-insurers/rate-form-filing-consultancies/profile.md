# Rate, Rule & Form Filing Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/property-casualty-insurers|Property & Casualty Insurers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Actuarial and regulatory consultancies that prepare rate, rule and form filings for carriers and MGAs, answer regulators' objection letters, and build state-by-state filing strategies for new products. Perr&Knight and a field of boutique actuarial practices are representative. Clients pay per filing or by retainer.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 actuaries and regulatory analysts per firm, beside filing-preparation staff (estimate)
**Output:** Actuarial memoranda, rate and rule exhibits, form filings, objection responses, product filing strategies
**Proprietary data:** Engagement files and a working memory of how each state's reviewers object; the experience data and the filings belong to clients, and closed filings become public record
**Clock:** Regulators' objection-response deadlines and prior-approval review periods, plus clients' product launch dates
**Buyer:** Managing Principal / Head of Regulatory Practice
