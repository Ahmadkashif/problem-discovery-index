# Cyber Catastrophe & Aggregation Modelling Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/property-casualty-insurers|Property & Casualty Insurers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms that model how a single cyber event — a cloud outage, a ransomware campaign through a common software supplier, a state-backed attack — would hit many insureds at once, and score individual companies' cyber exposure from outside-in scanning. CyberCube, Guidewire's Cyence, Moody's RMS and Kovrr are representative. Cyber writers, reinsurers and Lloyd's syndicates license the models to set aggregation limits, buy reinsurance and price accounts.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 security researchers, data scientists, actuaries and event-response analysts per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Accumulation scenarios and loss curves, portfolio aggregation reports, account-level exposure scores, event briefs within days of a major incident
**Proprietary data:** Outside-in scans of company technology footprints and dependencies, incident histories, and claims calibration contributed by insurer clients
**Clock:** 1 January and mid-year reinsurance renewals, Lloyd's scenario and exclusion requirements, and live incidents where clients want an exposure estimate within days
**Buyer:** Chief Research Officer / Head of Cyber Analytics
