# Insurance AI Governance & Unfair-Discrimination Testing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/property-casualty-insurers|Property & Casualty Insurers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Consultancies and software firms that test carriers' rating variables, external data and claims models for unfair discrimination, and build the AI governance programmes regulators now examine. Demand comes from the NAIC's December 2023 model bulletin, adopted by about half the states (estimate), and from Colorado's SB21-169 programme (recalled). Actuarial consultancies' model-risk groups, law firms' insurance regulatory practices and specialist governance software vendors compete for the work.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 statisticians, actuaries and governance specialists per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Disparate-impact test reports, model inventories and governance frameworks, regulator exam support
**Proprietary data:** Testing methods and engagement benchmarks; the policy, claims and model data belong to the carrier
**Clock:** State examinations and filing reviews; adoption dates vary by state
**Buyer:** Head of Insurance Model Risk Practice
