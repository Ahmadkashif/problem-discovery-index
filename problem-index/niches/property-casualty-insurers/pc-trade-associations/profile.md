# P&C Trade Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/property-casualty-insurers|Property & Casualty Insurers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The national trade associations of P&C carriers, the American Property Casualty Insurance Association and the National Association of Mutual Insurance Companies, and their state counterparts. They lobby on tort reform, rate regulation and catastrophe policy, and publish industry statistics and policy papers. Members pay dues.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Policy and research staffs of under ten dedicated analysts each (estimate)
**Output:** Policy papers, legislative testimony, industry fact sheets
**Proprietary data:** Member surveys; most statistics are drawn from public and bureau data
**Clock:** Legislative sessions and regulatory comment periods
**Buyer:** Chief Economist / Head of Policy Research
