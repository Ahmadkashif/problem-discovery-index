# Personal Auto Telematics & UBI Score Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/property-casualty-insurers|Property & Casualty Insurers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms that collect driving data from phones, plug-in devices and connected cars and turn it into driving scores for usage-based insurance. Cambridge Mobile Telematics, Arity (Allstate) and LexisNexis Risk Solutions are representative. Carriers pay per enrolled driver or per score to rate and discount policies.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 data scientists and actuaries per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Trip-level driving scores, crash detection, programme design and filing support for carriers' usage-based programmes
**Proprietary data:** Billions of trips with crash and claims outcomes, much of it collected through carriers' branded programmes under consumer consent (estimate)
**Clock:** Carriers' programme launches and filings; consent and consumer-reporting rules after the FTC's January 2026 order against GM
**Buyer:** Chief Science Officer / Head of Insurance Solutions

**Kill switches:** **data isn't theirs** — most driving data is collected for a specific carrier's programme under that carrier's contract and the driver's consent, which restrict reuse. The FTC's 2026 order barring GM from passing driver data to consumer reporting agencies for five years, after GM ended its data relationships with LexisNexis and Verisk, shows the regulatory weight behind that restriction.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. A score computed per trip rather than analysis produced by people, on data whose reuse is restricted.
