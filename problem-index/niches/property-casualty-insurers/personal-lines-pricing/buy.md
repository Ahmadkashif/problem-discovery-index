## The Problem
A modern rating plan is built on predictive models, tested on holdout data and deployed to the rating engine within weeks. Getting it filed takes months. Each state wants support in its own form: factor-by-factor exhibits, model documentation, data-source descriptions, and increasingly evidence that variables do not unfairly discriminate.

## What Already Exists
Pricing and optimisation platforms (Earnix, WTW Radar, Akur8) handle model building, rating-plan design and deployment. SERFF is the filing channel. Filing consultancies and in-house filing teams assemble the exhibits.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: state-specific exhibit templates generated from the model itself; plain-language explanations of each variable's effect drawn from the model's partial dependence; tracking of which variables each state has accepted or objected to before; disparate-impact test outputs packaged for states that follow the NAIC's 2023 AI bulletin; and versioning that ties each filed exhibit to the exact model and data used.

## Target Customer
Pricing actuaries and filing teams at carriers already running a pricing platform.

## Impact If Solved
The months between a finished rating plan and an approved one shrink, and the support behind each variable stays consistent across states.
