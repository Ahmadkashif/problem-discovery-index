# Driving Scores Refitted to the Carrier's Own Rating Plan

**Niche:** [[niches/property-casualty-insurers/personal-auto-segmentation-rating/profile|Personal Auto Segmentation & Telematics Rating]]
**Industry:** [[industries/property-casualty-insurers|Property & Casualty Insurers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Telematics vendors sell driving scores trained on pooled data; each carrier must make the score fit its own rating plan, its own losses and each state's filing rules.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #feature-engineering #transfer-learning #evaluation-metrics #compliance #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to rank drivers by expected loss more finely than the carrier quoting next to them and get that segmentation through state rate review, and whoever keeps its best risks after a rate change without a regulator disapproval takes the account.

## The Problem
A vendor score ranks drivers well on average. Inside a carrier's rating plan, much of what it measures is already captured by existing variables such as territory, vehicle and prior claims, so the incremental lift is smaller than the vendor's deck suggests. Some states limit how telematics may be used, and consent rules now constrain where driving data can come from.

## What Already Exists
Telematics platforms and scores from Cambridge Mobile Telematics, Arity and LexisNexis, mobile-app and connected-car data programmes, and the carriers' own usage-based programmes.

## The Customization Gap
The carrier needs: measurement of the score's lift net of existing rating variables on its own losses; refitting or blending with the carrier's own trip data where it has enough; state-by-state filing support, including the discount structures each state allows; a consent and provenance record for every data source in light of the FTC's 2026 GM order; and monitoring for the drift that comes when a programme's enrolled population changes.

## Target Customer
Usage-based insurance programme leads and pricing actuaries at personal auto writers.

## Impact If Solved
Telematics discounts priced on evidence of incremental lift rather than vendor averages, filed consistently, and defensible on consent.
