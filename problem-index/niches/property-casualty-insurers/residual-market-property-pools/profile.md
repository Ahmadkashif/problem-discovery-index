# Residual Market Plans & Public Property Pools

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/property-casualty-insurers|Property & Casualty Insurers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Insurers of last resort created by statute: Citizens Property Insurance Corporation in Florida, the California FAIR Plan, the Texas Windstorm Insurance Association, Louisiana Citizens and the beach and FAIR plans of other coastal states, plus auto insurance plans. FEMA's National Flood Insurance Program is the federal analogue. They file rates, buy reinsurance and risk transfer, and grow when private carriers retreat.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 actuaries, catastrophe modellers and analysts at the largest plans (estimate)
**Output:** Rate filings, exposure and depopulation analysis, risk-transfer programmes, reports to legislators
**Proprietary data:** The policies and claims private carriers declined, concentrated in the highest-hazard areas
**Clock:** Statutory rate filings, hurricane and wildfire seasons, risk-transfer renewals, legislative sessions
**Buyer:** Chief Risk Officer / Chief Actuary
