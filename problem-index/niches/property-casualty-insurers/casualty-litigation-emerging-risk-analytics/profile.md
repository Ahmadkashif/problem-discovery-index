# Casualty Litigation & Emerging-Liability Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/property-casualty-insurers|Property & Casualty Insurers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms that sell casualty carriers analytics on where liability costs are heading. Some mine court dockets for venue, judge and plaintiff-firm patterns; Trellis for state trial courts and Lex Machina for federal litigation are examples. Others model emerging mass-tort exposures, such as PFAS or microplastics, from the scientific literature; Praedicat is an example (recalled). Casualty underwriters, claims litigation managers and reserving teams buy them to price venues, set authority and anticipate the next class of claims.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 analysts, data scientists and legal or scientific researchers per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Venue and firm risk scores, litigation trend reports, emerging-liability scenarios and exposure screens
**Proprietary data:** Coded docket histories and literature-derived liability models; the underlying dockets and papers are public
**Clock:** Casualty reinsurance renewals and carriers' reserve reviews; trial dates bind the litigants, not this pocket
**Buyer:** Head of Casualty Analytics / Chief Product Officer
