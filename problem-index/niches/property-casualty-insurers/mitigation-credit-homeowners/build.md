# Mitigation Verified From the Homeowner's Own Evidence

**Niche:** [[niches/property-casualty-insurers/mitigation-credit-homeowners/profile|Homeowners Seeking Mitigation Credit in Wildfire & Hurricane Zones]]
**Industry:** [[industries/property-casualty-insurers|Property & Casualty Insurers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Carriers will not credit mitigation they cannot verify, and an inspection per home costs more than the discount is worth.
**Tags:** #cnns #object-detection #semantic-segmentation #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to verify a homeowner's mitigation — a fortified roof, defensible space, ember-resistant vents — at underwriting cost, and whoever gets verified mitigation credited in a filed rate and kept at renewal takes the account.

## The Problem
A homeowner in a wildfire zone clears vegetation within ~5 feet of the house, swaps vents and replaces a wood deck. Their carrier offers a small discount if they can prove it, then requires an inspection that may take months. Most homeowners never complete the process, and the carrier keeps rating the house as if nothing changed.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Imagery vendors score properties from above, where vents, ember-resistant eaves and the immediate zone around the house are often invisible. Inspection networks charge per visit. Mitigation credits were small until regulators started requiring them, so the verification market was thin.

## What to Build
A guided capture app that tells the homeowner exactly which photos to take, recognises the relevant features (vent mesh, roof covering, cleared zone, gutter guards, deck material), and checks consistency against aerial imagery and permits. It outputs a verified mitigation record mapped to the carrier's filed credits and to the modelled loss reduction, with field inspection only where evidence is ambiguous.

## Target Customer
Homeowners product heads at carriers writing in wildfire and hurricane states, and state mitigation programmes.

## Impact If Built
Credits that reach the homeowners who earned them, carriers that can keep writing mitigated homes, and a reason for homeowners to mitigate.
