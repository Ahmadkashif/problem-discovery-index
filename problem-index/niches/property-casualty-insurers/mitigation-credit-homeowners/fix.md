# The Fortified Roof Forgotten at Renewal

**Niche:** [[niches/property-casualty-insurers/mitigation-credit-homeowners/profile|Homeowners Seeking Mitigation Credit in Wildfire & Hurricane Zones]]
**Industry:** [[industries/property-casualty-insurers|Property & Casualty Insurers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A homeowner sends a fortified-roof certificate, gets the discount once, and loses it when the policy moves to a new system or a new carrier.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #quick-win #automation #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to verify a homeowner's mitigation — a fortified roof, defensible space, ember-resistant vents — at underwriting cost, and whoever gets verified mitigation credited in a filed rate and kept at renewal takes the account.

## The Problem
Mitigation designations and inspection forms arrive as PDFs and are keyed into a discount field. When the book is converted, the policy is rewritten or the homeowner changes carrier, the record is often lost and the homeowner must prove it again, if they notice.

## Why It's Still Broken
Designations are not stored as verified attributes of the property; they are stored as discounts on a policy.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Read certificates and inspection forms on arrival, verify them against the issuing programme's records where available, attach the designation to the property record with its expiry, and re-apply the credit automatically at renewal and on rewrites.

## Who Feels the Pain
Homeowners who invested in mitigation, agents fielding their complaints, and regulators sponsoring mitigation programmes.

## Impact If Fixed
Earned credits stick, and mitigation programmes keep the trust that makes homeowners invest.
