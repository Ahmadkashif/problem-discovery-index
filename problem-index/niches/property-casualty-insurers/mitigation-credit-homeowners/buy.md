# Property-Condition Scores Extended to Mitigation Features

**Niche:** [[niches/property-casualty-insurers/mitigation-credit-homeowners/profile|Homeowners Seeking Mitigation Credit in Wildfire & Hurricane Zones]]
**Industry:** [[industries/property-casualty-insurers|Property & Casualty Insurers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Imagery-based property scores already rate roofs and vegetation; they need to recognise and credit the specific mitigation features regulators now name.
**Tags:** #cnns #semantic-segmentation #transfer-learning #data-integration #compliance #evaluation-metrics
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to verify a homeowner's mitigation — a fortified roof, defensible space, ember-resistant vents — at underwriting cost, and whoever gets verified mitigation credited in a filed rate and kept at renewal takes the account.

## The Problem
Carriers buy roof-condition and wildfire-vulnerability scores for every property. Those scores capture some mitigation, such as roof covering and vegetation density, but not the feature list in a state's mitigation regulation, and not in a form a homeowner can understand or dispute.

## What Already Exists
Property condition and risk data providers (logged in the vault as [[niches/home-inspection/property-condition-risk-data-providers/profile|🔍 Property Condition & Risk Data Providers]]), catastrophe models with mitigation modifiers, and state inspection forms.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: feature-level outputs aligned to each state's mitigation credit list; a link from each feature to the modelled loss reduction the carrier filed; a homeowner-facing explanation and dispute path where regulators require one; ingestion of homeowner-submitted evidence to fill what imagery cannot see; and change detection so improvements are credited at the next renewal.

## Target Customer
Carriers already licensing property-condition and wildfire scores in high-hazard states.

## Impact If Solved
Mitigation credits applied consistently from data the carrier already buys, with a dispute path that satisfies regulators.
