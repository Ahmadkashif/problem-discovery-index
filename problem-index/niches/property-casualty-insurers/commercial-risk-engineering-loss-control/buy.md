# Field Inspection Apps That Speak Underwriting

**Niche:** [[niches/property-casualty-insurers/commercial-risk-engineering-loss-control/profile|Commercial Risk Engineering & Loss Control]]
**Industry:** [[industries/property-casualty-insurers|Property & Casualty Insurers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Mobile inspection and audit apps capture photos and checklists; they do not produce the coded hazard data and recommendation records an underwriting system can use.
**Tags:** #cnns #object-detection #large-language-models #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a field engineer's survey into recommendation-level risk data an underwriter will price on, and whoever can show that completed recommendations lower loss takes the account.

## The Problem
Engineers already use tablets and generic inspection apps. The output is a PDF assembled from checklists, photos and free text, and the underwriter reads a summary. Nothing structured flows into rating, terms or renewal decisions.

## What Already Exists
Generic field-inspection and audit apps, carrier-built survey tools, and report templates from survey vendors.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: a hazard and control taxonomy by occupancy (warehouse, woodworking, restaurant, fleet), aligned to the carrier's rating and appetite rules; image recognition for common findings such as blocked sprinkler heads, missing guards and stacked combustibles; recommendation records with due dates and insured responses; and a push of coded findings into the underwriting workbench and renewal workflow.

## Target Customer
Risk engineering departments and contracted survey firms serving commercial carriers.

## Impact If Solved
Surveys become underwriting data rather than reading material, and recommendation follow-up stops depending on an engineer's inbox.
