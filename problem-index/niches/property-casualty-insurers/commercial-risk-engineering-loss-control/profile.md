# Commercial Risk Engineering & Loss Control

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/property-casualty-insurers|Property & Casualty Insurers]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a field engineer's survey into recommendation-level risk data an underwriter will price on, and whoever can show that completed recommendations lower loss takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2B a year of carrier risk engineering staff, contracted survey firms, and loss control reporting and recommendation tracking for commercial property, liability, auto and workers' compensation (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.6% of US P&C carriers' underwriting and loss-adjustment expense (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — surveys are captured on tablets at best, reports are long documents and photos, and recommendation follow-up runs by email and spreadsheet
**Target Buyer:** Head of Risk Engineering / Chief Underwriting Officer, Commercial
**Automation Potential:** Medium — report drafting, data extraction and follow-up automate; the walk-through and the engineer's judgement do not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Commercial underwriters price risks they never see. The carrier's eyes are its loss control engineers and the survey firms it contracts, who walk a warehouse, plant or fleet yard and judge sprinkler adequacy, housekeeping, hot-work control, driver management and electrical hazards. Their output is a report and a list of recommendations. The value depends on two things the industry rarely measures: whether underwriters actually use the findings in price and terms, and whether insureds who complete recommendations go on to have fewer or smaller losses.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Survey apps and report builders, carrier underwriting workbenches, imagery and property data, and national inspection networks (logged in the vault as [[niches/home-inspection/insurance-property-inspection-networks/profile|🔍 Insurance Property Inspection Networks]]). The gaps: findings locked in prose; recommendation status tracked by hand; no join from recommendation to later loss.
