# Inter-Company Arbitration Forums

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/property-casualty-insurers|Property & Casualty Insurers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Member-owned forums where insurers settle subrogation disputes with each other through binding arbitration instead of court. Arbitration Forums is the main US example (recalled). Carriers pay filing fees and membership dues.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Arbitrators and case staff decide disputes; analytical staff are few (estimate)
**Output:** Arbitration decisions and case-handling services
**Proprietary data:** Decades of inter-company liability decisions
**Clock:** Filing and response windows in the forum's own rules
**Buyer:** President / Chief Operating Officer
