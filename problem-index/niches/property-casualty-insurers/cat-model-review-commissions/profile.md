# Catastrophe Model Review Commissions

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/property-casualty-insurers|Property & Casualty Insurers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Public bodies that review catastrophe models before carriers may use them in rates. Florida's Commission on Hurricane Loss Projection Methodology sets standards and accepts hurricane and flood models (recalled). California's Department of Insurance completed its first wildfire model review for ratemaking in July 2025.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Part-time commissioners plus a small staff and contracted professional teams (estimate)
**Output:** Model standards, acceptance decisions, review reports
**Proprietary data:** Model submissions and vendor disclosures held under confidentiality
**Clock:** Annual or biennial standards and submission cycles
**Buyer:** Commission chair / department model review lead
