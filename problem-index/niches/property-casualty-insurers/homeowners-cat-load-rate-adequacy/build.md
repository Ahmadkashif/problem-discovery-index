# A Catastrophe Load Filing Kit for High-Hazard States

**Industry:** [[industries/property-casualty-insurers|Property & Casualty Insurers]]

**One-liner:** A carrier that wants to price wildfire or hurricane risk with a model must assemble model evidence, reinsurance costs, mitigation credits and writing commitments into one filing, and today does it by hand for each state.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a modelled catastrophe load and the cost of reinsurance accepted in a rate filing for a high-hazard state, and whoever wins approval for an adequate rate without committing to write more than it can carry takes the account.

## The Problem
A modelled catastrophe load in a rate filing must show which model and version was used, how the exposure data was prepared, how average annual loss maps to territories and rating tiers, how mitigation credits were derived, and, where the state allows it, how the net cost of reinsurance was allocated. In California the filing must also tie to the carrier's commitment to write in distressed areas. Each element sits with a different team, and the regulator reads them together.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Cat modelling vendors stop at model output, filing consultancies work engagement by engagement, and reinsurance cost lives with the treasury and reinsurance teams. Until recently a filing kit for modelled loads had no market in the largest wildfire state.

## What to Build
A workflow that takes model runs and exposure data and produces state-ready exhibits: territory loads with uncertainty bands, mitigation credit derivations tied to modelled loss reductions, reinsurance cost allocation where permitted, a reconciliation to historical catastrophe experience, and a commitment tracker that shows writing in distressed areas against the pledge.

## Target Customer
Chief actuaries and catastrophe management heads at homeowners writers in California, Florida, Louisiana, Texas and other high-hazard states.

## Impact If Built
Adequate rates filed faster with consistent evidence, which is the alternative to non-renewal for carriers that want to stay in high-hazard markets.
