# Cat Model Output Turned Into Rate Exhibits

**Niche:** [[niches/property-casualty-insurers/homeowners-cat-load-rate-adequacy/profile|Homeowners Catastrophe Loads & Rate Adequacy]]
**Industry:** [[industries/property-casualty-insurers|Property & Casualty Insurers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Catastrophe modelling platforms produce event losses and exceedance curves; turning them into territory rates a regulator accepts is a separate job each carrier does itself.
**Tags:** #monte-carlo-methods #probability-distributions #descriptive-statistics #compliance #data-integration #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a modelled catastrophe load and the cost of reinsurance accepted in a rate filing for a high-hazard state, and whoever wins approval for an adequate rate without committing to write more than it can carry takes the account.

## The Problem
Model output arrives as event loss tables and average annual losses by location. Rates need loads by territory, construction, protection class and deductible, smoothed and credibility-weighted, with exhibits in each state's format and evidence that the result is reasonable against history.

## What Already Exists
Verisk's and Moody's RMS modelling platforms, KCC's models, and exposure-data tools. Pricing platforms handle the rest of the rating plan.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: aggregation of location-level model loss into rating cells with smoothing rules each state accepts; side-by-side comparison of model versions and vendors at territory level; derivation of deductible and mitigation relativities from the model; exhibits for each state's catastrophe-load rules (California's model review and distressed-area commitments, Florida's hurricane model requirements); and documentation linking every exhibit to a model version and exposure snapshot.

## Target Customer
Catastrophe pricing teams at regional and national homeowners writers.

## Impact If Solved
Model results reach approved rates without a bespoke spreadsheet build each cycle, and every load can be traced back to its source run.
