# Enterprise Pricing Tools Packaged for a Forty-Person Carrier

**Niche:** [[niches/property-casualty-insurers/small-regional-mutuals/profile|Small & Regional Mutual Insurers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/property-casualty-insurers|Property & Casualty Insurers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** The pricing, cat modelling and property data tools national carriers use exist; they are sold in configurations a small mutual can neither afford nor staff.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #linear-regression #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #compliance #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give a mutual with no data-science staff a pricing and catastrophe view its regulator and reinsurer will accept, and whoever turns bureau loss costs, cat models and the mutual's own thin experience into a filed rate change before the reinsurance renewal takes the account.

## The Problem
A small mutual wants roof-condition data, a hail score, a modern rating plan and a cat model view of its book. Each comes from a different vendor with an enterprise contract and an integration project.

## What Already Exists
Pricing platforms, property-condition data (logged in the vault as [[niches/home-inspection/property-condition-risk-data-providers/profile|🔍 Property Condition & Risk Data Providers]]), catastrophe models, and policy systems hosted for small carriers.

## The Customization Gap
The package needs: bundled pricing, property data and cat views at a small-carrier price; pre-built connectors to the policy systems mutuals actually run; default rating structures that start from bureau plans and add a few proven variables; state filing templates; and support delivered as a service, because there is no in-house team to configure it.

## Target Customer
Small and regional mutual and farm mutual carriers, possibly bought collectively through their associations or reinsurers.

## Impact If Solved
Small carriers price with the same signals as their national competitors and stop losing their best risks to them.
