# Carrier Pricing & Predictive Analytics Units

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/property-casualty-insurers|Property & Casualty Insurers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The pricing, product analytics and data science teams inside the large P&C carriers. They build rating plans, test new data sources, run competitive and retention analysis, and support every rate filing. Their output goes into the carrier's own premiums. No outside client pays for it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-500 actuaries, data scientists and analysts per top-25 writer; a handful at most small carriers (estimate)
**Output:** Rating plans, rate indications, filing support, new-variable evaluations, retention and competitive studies
**Proprietary data:** The carrier's own policy, quote, telematics and claims history: the deepest outcome-labelled insurance data anywhere, but only its own book
**Clock:** Rate filing calendars and objection deadlines in prior-approval states, quarterly results
**Buyer:** Chief Actuary / Chief Analytics Officer
