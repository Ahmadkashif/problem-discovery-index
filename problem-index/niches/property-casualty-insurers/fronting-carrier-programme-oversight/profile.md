# Fronting Carriers' Programme Oversight

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/property-casualty-insurers|Property & Casualty Insurers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Hybrid fronting carriers lend their licences and ratings to MGAs and cede most of the risk to reinsurers. State National (Markel) and Clear Blue are representative. Each runs dozens of programmes on its paper, and its oversight teams audit MGAs' underwriting, claims and data, monitor programme loss ratios and decide which programmes to keep. The carrier earns a fronting fee; the oversight analysis is not sold.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 programme auditors, actuaries and analysts per fronting carrier (estimate)
**Output:** Programme audits, loss-ratio monitoring, bordereaux validation, programme approval and termination recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Bordereaux and loss data across many MGA programmes, which no single MGA or reinsurer sees in full
**Clock:** Monthly and quarterly bordereaux, reinsurance renewals and rating-agency reviews
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer / Head of Programme Oversight
