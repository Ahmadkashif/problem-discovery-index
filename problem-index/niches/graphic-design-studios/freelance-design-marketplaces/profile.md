# Freelance Design Marketplaces

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/graphic-design-studios|Graphic Design Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Platforms that sit between clients and freelance designers and take a cut of each transaction: 99designs by Vista (contests and one-to-one projects, owned by Vista since 2020), Fiverr, Upwork and DesignCrowd. Their data science and trust-and-safety teams match briefs to designers, set pricing guidance and police fraud and plagiarism.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 data scientists and analysts working on the design categories (estimate)
**Output:** Matching, pricing guidance, ratings, fraud and originality enforcement — all internal
**Proprietary data:** Millions of briefs, contest entries, prices, ratings and outcomes
**Clock:** None external
**Buyer:** VP of data science
