# Unlimited Design Subscription Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/graphic-design-studios|Graphic Design Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Companies that sell graphic design as a flat monthly subscription — Superside, Design Pickle, ManyPixels and similar — staffed largely by remote designers, often offshore, working through a request queue. They aggregate demand that would otherwise go to freelancers and small studios.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Hundreds of production designers; no dedicated research or insight function (estimate)
**Output:** Design production against requests
**Proprietary data:** Request and turnaround histories
**Clock:** Service-level turnaround promises they set themselves
**Buyer:** Chief executive
