# Trademark Clearance & Design-Mark Search Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/graphic-design-studios|Graphic Design Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Search firms that tell a brand owner, before a new name or logo launches, which existing marks it might conflict with. Corsearch and Clarivate's CompuMark dominate full clearance searches, combining search tools across registers, business names, domains and common-law sources with human analyst review; both offer image-similarity search for figurative marks. Trademark counsel at law firms and in-house brand teams pay per search and subscribe to watch services that flag conflicting new filings. Every identity a studio designs should pass through one before launch.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 search analysts and researchers across the two leaders (estimate; neither publishes a count)
**Output:** Full clearance search reports with ranked conflict lists; design-mark searches; watch notices on new filings
**Proprietary data:** Curated multi-jurisdiction trademark and common-law databases (Corsearch cites 240 jurisdictions and 1,100+ databases) and the archive of every clearance report with the analyst's relevance calls — joinable to later refusals and oppositions, which nobody does
**Clock:** Launch dates, filing priority windows and opposition deadlines on watched marks
**Buyer:** VP of search operations / head of analyst services
