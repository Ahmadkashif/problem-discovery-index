# Stock & Generative Imagery Visual Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/graphic-design-studios|Graphic Design Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The creative-insights teams inside stock and generative imagery libraries — Getty Images' VisualGPS program and Shutterstock's trend reports are examples — that study which images people search for, license and respond to, and publish visual trend guidance to designers and marketers. The libraries themselves are analysed under [[industries/stock-media-marketplaces|Stock Media Marketplaces]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 researchers and creative-insight analysts (estimate)
**Output:** Visual trend reports, consumer visual research, curated collections
**Proprietary data:** Search, download and licensing behaviour across hundreds of millions of assets
**Clock:** None external; annual trend cycles they set themselves
**Buyer:** Head of creative insights
