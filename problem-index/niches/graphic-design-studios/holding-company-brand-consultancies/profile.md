# Holding-Company Brand Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/graphic-design-studios|Graphic Design Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The large brand and design consultancies owned by advertising holding groups — Landor, Design Bridge and Partners, Interbrand, Siegel+Gale and their peers — which consolidated many formerly independent identity and packaging studios. They sell identity and packaging programs to large companies with a strategy and research front end: brand audits, naming research, perception studies and the rationale that sells the new identity to a board.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 strategists and researchers per consultancy (estimate)
**Output:** Brand strategy and positioning research, naming studies, identity rationales, brand-strength frameworks
**Proprietary data:** Strategy archives and brand-strength frameworks; most underlying research is commissioned per client
**Clock:** Mergers, spin-offs and launch dates that force a rebrand
**Buyer:** Chief strategy officer
