# Design Software Vendors' Research & Trend Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/graphic-design-studios|Graphic Design Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Adobe, Canva and Figma sell the tools every studio works in — Adobe's all-apps plan became Creative Cloud Pro at $69.99 a month in June 2025; Canva reported ~265M monthly users at end-2025 and made Affinity free — and run user research, design-trend and product analytics teams that study how designers work.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 user researchers, analysts and trend teams per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Product decisions, trend reports, design-industry surveys used in marketing
**Proprietary data:** Usage telemetry and, in principle, the content of hundreds of millions of design files
**Clock:** Product release cycles they set themselves
**Buyer:** VP of product research
