# CAD-to-Illustration Tools Adapted for Outside Illustrators

**Niche:** [[niches/graphic-design-studios/technical-and-scientific-illustration/profile|Technical, Medical & Scientific Illustration]]
**Industry:** [[industries/graphic-design-studios|Graphic Design Studios]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** CAD-to-illustration software turns a 3D model into exploded views and line art for manufacturers' in-house publications teams; the outside illustrator receiving a STEP file redraws it by hand.
**Tags:** #cnns #transfer-learning #automation #data-integration #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn CAD models, specimens and source literature into figures that a subject expert — a technical publications manager, a clinician, a journal art editor — signs off without a correction round, and whoever gets expert sign-off first time at volume takes the account.

## The Problem
Assembly and service illustrators routinely receive CAD exports from clients. Without the manufacturer's CAD ecosystem they import what they can, trace views by hand, and redraw when the model changes, which is slow and introduces inaccuracies that an expert later catches.

## What Already Exists
SOLIDWORKS Composer, Creo Illustrate and similar tools generate exploded views, technical line art and callouts directly from native CAD models, priced and licensed for in-house technical publications teams.

## The Customization Gap
The outside-illustrator version needs: (1) robust import of neutral formats (STEP, IGES) without the native CAD system; (2) house-style output for service manuals — line weights, callout styles and parts-list tables to each client's standard, with S1000D or ATA iSpec 2200 conventions where required; (3) automatic view selection for each assembly or service step; (4) model-revision diffs that show which figures are affected; and (5) pricing per project or per seat for small studios.

## Target Customer
Technical illustration studios and freelance technical illustrators serving manufacturers.

## Impact If Solved
Figures are derived from the model rather than traced, accuracy is guaranteed by construction, and revisions take minutes instead of a redraw.
