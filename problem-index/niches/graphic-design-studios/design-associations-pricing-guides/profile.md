# Design Associations & Pricing Guides

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/graphic-design-studios|Graphic Design Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Professional bodies that publish the industry's few benchmarks: AIGA's Design Census (9,429 US respondents in 2019, covering pay, hours and roles) and the Graphic Artists Guild's *Handbook: Pricing & Ethical Guidelines*, the reference freelancers cite when quoting. Members and book buyers pay.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 people on research and publications at each body (estimate)
**Output:** Periodic surveys, a pricing handbook, advocacy material
**Proprietary data:** Survey responses and member data
**Clock:** Self-set publication cycles
**Buyer:** Executive director
