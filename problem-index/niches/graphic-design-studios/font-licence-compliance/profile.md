# Font Foundry Licence Compliance Operations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/graphic-design-studios|Graphic Design Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Type foundries and font platforms, led by Monotype, that license typefaces to studios and their clients and run compliance programs to find unlicensed use: scanning websites, apps and published material, auditing companies, and sending "continue, but pay" letters offering retroactive licences. Monotype also sells audit tooling to companies that want to check themselves.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 compliance analysts and licensing specialists (estimate)
**Output:** Usage audits, infringement findings, retroactive licence offers, enterprise licence proposals
**Proprietary data:** Font fingerprints across large libraries and records of licensed and detected use
**Clock:** Largely self-set; enterprise licence renewals
**Buyer:** Head of licence compliance
