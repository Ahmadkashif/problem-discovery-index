# Brand Valuation Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/graphic-design-studios|Graphic Design Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Firms that put a financial value on brands — for annual published rankings, purchase-price allocation after acquisitions, transfer pricing of brand royalties between group companies, licensing, litigation and the business case for a rebrand. Brand Finance (accredited to ISO 10668 and ISO 20671, valuing 6,000+ brands a year by royalty relief), Interbrand and Kantar BrandZ are the visible names; valuation arms of accounting firms do the transaction work. Identity studios cite their rankings when selling redesigns.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 valuation analysts and consumer researchers per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Brand valuation reports, published rankings, brand-strength scores, rebrand business cases
**Proprietary data:** Years of brand valuations and brand-strength research across thousands of brands
**Clock:** Transaction closings, financial reporting dates, tax filings and court schedules
**Buyer:** Head of valuation / Managing Director
