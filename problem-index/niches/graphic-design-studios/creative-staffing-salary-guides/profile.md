# Creative Staffing Salary Guides

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/graphic-design-studios|Graphic Design Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Creative staffing agencies such as Aquent and Robert Half's creative practice publish annual salary and rate guides for designers, art directors and production artists. Studios and in-house teams use them to set pay and freelance rates; the agencies use them to win placements.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 3-10 people compiling each guide from placement data (estimate)
**Output:** Annual salary and rate guides
**Proprietary data:** Placement and rate records from the agency's own desk
**Clock:** Annual, self-set
**Buyer:** Marketing director
