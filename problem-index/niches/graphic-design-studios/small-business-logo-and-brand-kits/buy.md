# Trademark Knockout Search Tuned for Logos

**Niche:** [[niches/graphic-design-studios/small-business-logo-and-brand-kits/profile|Small-Business Logo & Brand Kits]]
**Industry:** [[industries/graphic-design-studios|Graphic Design Studios]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Professional trademark clearance with image search exists, priced and worded for trademark counsel; a small business buying a $300 logo gets nothing.
**Tags:** #contrastive-learning #k-nearest-neighbors #compliance #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver a logo a small business can actually register and own — distinct from existing marks and free of unownable AI output — at a sub-$500 price, and whoever makes the mark clearable takes the customer.

## The Problem
Clearance is a professional service: a search across registers and common-law sources, reviewed by an analyst and interpreted by an attorney. At the low end of the logo market, nobody buys it, so owners file blind or never file at all.

## What Already Exists
Corsearch and Clarivate's CompuMark provide trademark clearance with analyst review and image-similarity design search across hundreds of databases; online filing services offer basic word-mark knockout searches.

## The Customization Gap
A logo-market version needs: (1) pay-per-logo pricing embedded in a platform's checkout; (2) automatic design-code assignment and image search, so the owner never has to learn the coding system; (3) results in plain language with a traffic-light risk and the conflicting marks shown side by side; (4) goods-and-services class selection guided by a description of the business; and (5) a clear boundary between the automated knockout and the legal opinion an attorney gives.

## Target Customer
Logo platforms and marketplaces that want a clearance step, and search providers seeking a high-volume, low-ticket channel.

## Impact If Solved
Clearance moves from a service almost no small business buys to a default step, cutting refused filings and later disputes.
