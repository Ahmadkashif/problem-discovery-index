# 3D Pack Mock-Ups Priced for Small Studios

**Niche:** [[niches/graphic-design-studios/packaging-design/profile|Packaging Design]]
**Industry:** [[industries/graphic-design-studios|Graphic Design Studios]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Accurate 3D packaging visualisation exists in the enterprise packaging stack; small studios still present flat artwork or generic stock mock-ups that don't match the real dieline.

Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Clients approve packs from renders. A small studio drops flat artwork onto a stock mock-up of a generic carton or pouch whose proportions, folds and finishes don't match the converter's dieline, so the client approves something that will look different on the shelf, and structural problems (copy across a fold, a logo on a gusset) surface late.

## What Already Exists
Esko Studio builds 3D mock-ups directly from dielines inside Illustrator, as part of the enterprise packaging suite. Online tools such as Pacdora generate mock-ups from dieline templates. Photoshop mock-up templates are sold by the thousand.

## The Customization Gap
A small-studio tool needs: (1) the converter's actual dieline imported and folded, not a template approximation; (2) substrate and finish rendering (kraft, foil, matte varnish, window film) good enough to judge contrast; (3) automatic flags where artwork crosses folds, flaps and glue areas; (4) shelf-context renders alongside competitor packs; and (5) pricing per project rather than per enterprise seat.

## Target Customer
Independent packaging studios and freelance packaging designers working for emerging consumer brands.

## Impact If Solved
Clients approve what will actually be printed, structural errors surface at concept stage, and small studios present at the standard of large agencies without the enterprise software bill.
