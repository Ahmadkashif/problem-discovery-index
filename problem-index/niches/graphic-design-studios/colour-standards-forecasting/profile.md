# Colour Standards & Forecasting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/graphic-design-studios|Graphic Design Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Colour-standard owners and colour forecasting services. The Pantone system is the shared language designers, brand owners and printers use to specify brand colours; its Color Institute sells trend forecasts, custom palette consulting and the widely publicised colour of the year. Brand owners, packaging studios and product companies pay for guides, digital libraries, forecasts and consulting.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 colour forecasters, colour scientists and consultants (estimate)
**Output:** Seasonal colour forecasts, custom brand palette studies, colour standards and libraries
**Proprietary data:** The colour standard itself and its physical and digital libraries, plus forecasting archives
**Clock:** Seasonal product and collection cycles
**Buyer:** Director of the colour institute / VP of colour services
