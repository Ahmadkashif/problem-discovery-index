# Creative Professional Liability Insurers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/graphic-design-studios|Graphic Design Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Insurers and programs that write professional liability and media liability cover for designers, studios and freelancers — the policies that respond when a client claims a logo infringes someone's trademark, a font was unlicensed or a print error caused loss. Studios pay premiums, often through small-business bundles.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Underwriting for designers sits inside broad small-business professional lines; no design-specific team of 10+ (estimate)
**Output:** Policies, claims handling
**Proprietary data:** Claims histories, pooled with other professions
**Clock:** Policy renewal dates
**Buyer:** Head of professional lines
