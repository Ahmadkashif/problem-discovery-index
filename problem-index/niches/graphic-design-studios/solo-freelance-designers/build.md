# Scope-Aware Feedback and Change-Order Assistant

**Niche:** [[niches/graphic-design-studios/solo-freelance-designers/profile|Solo Freelance Designers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/graphic-design-studios|Graphic Design Studios]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** No freelancer tool reads the client's feedback and tells the designer which requests are inside the quoted rounds and which are new, billable work.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #transformers #worker-facing #revenue-impact #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to hold the solo designer's scope and payment terms for them — counting revision rounds against the quote, holding source files until the invoice clears and transferring the licence only on payment — and whoever enforces the quoted job without the designer having the awkward conversation takes the designer.

## The Problem
Round-one feedback arrives across email, PDF comments, a proofing link, chat and a voice note, from several client stakeholders who disagree. The freelancer reconciles it alone, decides alone whether "let's explore a different icon" is a revision or a new direction, and usually absorbs extra rounds rather than risk the relationship.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Back-office suites stop at the signed contract and the invoice; proofing tools stop at the comment. Reading informal, contradictory, multi-channel feedback and comparing it with contract language was not feasible before capable language models, and freelancers are a price-sensitive, fragmented market few vendors design for.

## What to Build
An assistant connected to the designer's email, chat and proofing accounts that assembles each round's feedback into one numbered list attributed to each stakeholder, flags contradictions with a single clarifying question drafted, and tags every request against the contract as in-scope revision, out-of-scope change or new direction. When the round allowance is exceeded, it drafts the change order at the designer's rate, and it tracks the revision count visibly for both sides. The marketplace or payment platform that embeds this becomes the place the designer prefers to bring clients.

## Target Customer
Self-employed identity, packaging and layout designers with recurring direct clients, and marketplaces seeking to retain them.

## Impact If Built
One recovered billable round per job lifts a freelancer's effective rate by 15–30% (estimate) and saves hours of feedback reconciliation per round.
