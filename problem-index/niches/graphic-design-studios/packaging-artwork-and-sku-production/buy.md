# Artwork Lifecycle Management for a Studio With Thirty Brand Clients

**Niche:** [[niches/graphic-design-studios/packaging-artwork-and-sku-production/profile|Packaging Artwork & SKU Production]]
**Industry:** [[industries/graphic-design-studios|Graphic Design Studios]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Artwork management platforms are built for one brand owner managing many agencies; a studio needs the reverse — one agency serving many brand owners, each with its own system.
**Tags:** #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #compliance #automation #large-language-models
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to push every SKU's artwork through each copy or regulatory change to press-ready files right first time, and whoever turns a change around in the fewest days with zero label errors takes the account.

## The Problem
A mid-sized packaging studio may serve thirty brand clients. The large ones run Esko WebCenter, ManageArtworks or Kallik and expect the studio to work inside them; the small ones run email. The studio's own job queue, copy versions and approval states are split across all of these and a spreadsheet.

## What Already Exists
Esko WebCenter, Karomi ManageArtworks and Kallik Veraciti provide artwork lifecycle management — briefs, copy, approvals, versioning, compliance checks — configured by and for the brand owner. Generic proofing tools (Ziflow, Filestage) handle approvals for any file.

## The Customization Gap
The studio version needs: (1) connectors into each client's artwork system so tasks and approvals sync both ways; (2) a single studio-side queue across all clients with deadlines and print slots; (3) a lightweight equivalent of the enterprise workflow for clients who have none, with copy decks, approvals and verification records; and (4) pricing per active SKU rather than per brand-owner licence.

## Target Customer
Packaging studios of 10–60 people serving a mix of enterprise and emerging brands.

## Impact If Solved
One view of every SKU in production across every client, fewer missed approvals and print slots, and an enterprise-grade process offered to small clients as a service.
