# Packaging Artwork & SKU Production

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/graphic-design-studios|Graphic Design Studios]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to push every SKU's artwork through each copy or regulatory change to press-ready files right first time, and whoever turns a change around in the fewest days with zero label errors takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.8B US packaging artwork production, adaptation and prepress-ready file work billed by studios (estimate; SGS & Co, which agreed to absorb SGK Brand Solutions, operates at a larger, global scale)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~11% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — files are digital and enterprise operations run Esko WebCenter with GlobalVision inspection, but small studios verify by eye and track copy versions by email
**Target Buyer:** Packaging artwork or packaging development manager at a consumer-goods company; on the studio side, the production lead
**Automation Potential:** High — copy changes, SKU propagation and verification are rule-driven

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Once a pack design is approved, the work becomes production at volume: building and maintaining artwork for every SKU, size and market, and re-issuing it every time the copy deck changes for a reformulation, a new claim, a retailer requirement or a regulatory change such as sesame becoming a required allergen in January 2023. Success is binary and checkable — the files go to press right or they don't — and speed after a change is what clients compare. The buyer, clock and winner are different from the brand-design contest, which is why the parent niche splits here.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Illustrator with Esko DeskPack; Esko WebCenter and Automation Engine for workflow at brand owners and prepress houses; GlobalVision Verify for text, barcode and braille inspection; artwork-management platforms such as Karomi's ManageArtworks and Kallik on the brand side. Gaps: a small studio serving many brand owners must work inside each brand's system or none; copy decks still arrive as spreadsheets; and verification below enterprise scale is a proofreader with a ruler.
