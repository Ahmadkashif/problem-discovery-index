# Copy-Deck-Driven Artwork Regeneration

**Niche:** [[niches/graphic-design-studios/packaging-artwork-and-sku-production/profile|Packaging Artwork & SKU Production]]
**Industry:** [[industries/graphic-design-studios|Graphic Design Studios]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A one-line change to an ingredient statement is hand-edited into forty separate artwork files, when the artwork could be regenerated from the copy deck the way a report is regenerated from a database.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #optimization-fundamentals #automation #compliance #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to push every SKU's artwork through each copy or regulatory change to press-ready files right first time, and whoever turns a change around in the fewest days with zero label errors takes the account.

## The Problem
A reformulation changes the ingredient list, the allergen statement and three Nutrition Facts values across a flavour range. A production artist opens each SKU's file, edits the text, refits the panel (the new statement is two lines longer), checks it, and outlines the text again for the printer. Verification tools can catch the errors afterwards; nothing removes the hand editing that creates them.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Packaging artwork is built as free-form illustration files, not as templates bound to data, and the regulated panels have layout rules (type size minimums, panel order, fitting into irregular dieline areas) that generic data-merge tools can't handle. Brand-side artwork platforms manage approval of files; they don't generate them.

## What to Build
Bind each SKU's regulated panels — ingredients, allergen statement, Nutrition or Supplement Facts, net quantity, barcode, legal lines — to fields in a structured copy deck. When the deck changes, regenerate the affected panels in every SKU file, fitting text into the panel geometry under the minimum type size and format rules, and route only the panels that couldn't be fitted automatically to a designer. Each regeneration produces its own verification record.

## Target Customer
Packaging studios and in-house artwork teams maintaining ranges of 50 to 5,000 SKUs for food, supplement, personal-care and household brands.

## Impact If Built
Copy changes turn around in hours rather than days, the hand-editing step where errors originate disappears, and studios can price artwork maintenance as a predictable service.
