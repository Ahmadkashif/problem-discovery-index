# Trademark & Copyright Registration Offices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/graphic-design-studios|Graphic Design Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The USPTO examines trademark applications, including logos coded with six-digit design search codes derived from the Vienna Classification, and refuses marks likely to be confused with existing ones. The US Copyright Office registers creative works and, in its January 2025 report, set out when AI-assisted work can be protected. Between them they decide whether a studio's logo can be registered and what a client owns.

## Insight Function
**Size:** hundreds of trademark examining attorneys and registration specialists (estimate)
**Output:** Examination decisions, office actions, registration decisions, policy reports and guidance
**Proprietary data:** The complete record of applications, design codes, refusals and registrations
**Clock:** Statutory response periods and opposition windows
**Buyer:** Commissioner for Trademarks / Register of Copyrights
