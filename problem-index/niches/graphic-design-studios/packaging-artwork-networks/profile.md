# Packaging Artwork & Brand-Deployment Networks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/graphic-design-studios|Graphic Design Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Global groups that take approved packaging designs from brand owners and their design studios and turn them into press-ready artwork, prepress files, plates and cylinders across thousands of SKUs and markets. SGS & Co is the consolidator: Matthews International agreed to sell SGK Brand Solutions (formerly Schawk, described as having 6,500+ employees) to a new entity formed by SGS & Co's affiliates, for $350M upfront plus a 40% stake in the combined business. Brand owners pay on multi-year master service agreements, per SKU or per change.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 6,000+ staff in total; the regulatory, artwork-quality and brand-compliance analysts who could form an insight function number in the tens to low hundreds (estimate)
**Output:** Press-ready artwork, prepress files, plates, compliance checks, brand consistency audits
**Proprietary data:** Artwork, copy and print specifications for millions of SKUs, and the error and rework history behind them
**Clock:** Print slots, retailer resets and regulatory compliance dates
**Buyer:** Chief operating officer / head of artwork operations
