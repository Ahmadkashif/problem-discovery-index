# Trade Dress & Design Infringement Survey Experts

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/graphic-design-studios|Graphic Design Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Survey research consultancies and academic experts retained in trademark, trade dress and packaging look-alike disputes to measure consumer confusion, secondary meaning and genericness, and to testify about it. When a private-label pack imitates a brand's design, or a new logo is challenged, the case often turns on their survey. Litigation counsel pays, on court deadlines.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 survey methodologists, analysts and testifying experts per firm
**Output:** Confusion and secondary-meaning surveys, expert reports, rebuttals of the opposing expert, testimony
**Proprietary data:** Archive of past survey designs, stimuli and results, and of how courts treated them
**Clock:** Court-ordered expert disclosure and trial dates
**Buyer:** Practice leader / testifying expert
