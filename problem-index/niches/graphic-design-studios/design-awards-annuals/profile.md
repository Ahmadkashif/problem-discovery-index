# Design Award Programs & Annuals

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/graphic-design-studios|Graphic Design Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Award programs and annual publications — D&AD, the Communication Arts annuals, Pentawards for packaging, Graphis — that judge and publish the year's best work. Studios pay entry fees because awards win clients and recruits.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Small permanent staffs; judging is done by volunteer juries (estimate)
**Output:** Awards, annuals, exhibitions and talks
**Proprietary data:** Archives of entered and awarded work
**Clock:** Annual entry deadlines they set themselves
**Buyer:** Program director
