# Guidelines That Ship as a PDF Nobody Opens

**Niche:** [[niches/graphic-design-studios/brand-identity-programs/profile|Brand Identity Programs]]
**Industry:** [[industries/graphic-design-studios|Graphic Design Studios]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The guidelines are the last item in a fixed-fee project, written when the budget is already spent, and delivered as a 60-page PDF the client's staff never read.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #automation #quick-win #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a new identity used intact across every touchpoint after launch — through the templates, guidelines and rollout support the client's own staff and suppliers actually use — and whoever owns the rollout owns the retained relationship.

## The Problem
Guidelines take days to lay out by hand: clear-space diagrams, colour specs in four systems, type hierarchies, do-and-don't examples. They are produced at the end of the project, when hours are short, then emailed as a PDF. Client staff and suppliers ask the designer for "the logo" instead of opening it, and the do-and-don't page is the one nobody sees.

## Why It's Still Broken
The guidelines are treated as a deliverable rather than a tool, and the fixed fee pays for a document, not a maintained resource. Every value in them — colours, sizes, clear space — already exists in the master files, but is retyped by hand, so the document goes out of date as soon as anything changes.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Generate the guidelines from the master files: colour values, type styles, logo variants and minimum sizes read directly from the source, clear-space and misuse examples rendered automatically, and the result published as a versioned web page with the approved assets downloadable beside each rule. When the identity changes, the guidelines update with it.

## Who Feels the Pain
The designer laying out guidelines at the end of a budget; the client's marketing coordinator who can't find the right logo file; the printer guessing the colour.

## Impact If Fixed
Days of layout time come back on every identity project, the guidelines become something people actually use, and correct asset use stops depending on emailing the designer.
