# Brand Portals Sized for a Studio's Client List

**Niche:** [[niches/graphic-design-studios/brand-identity-programs/profile|Brand Identity Programs]]
**Industry:** [[industries/graphic-design-studios|Graphic Design Studios]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Brand portals are built for one corporate brand team managing one brand; an identity studio needs one account managing thirty clients' brands.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a new identity used intact across every touchpoint after launch — through the templates, guidelines and rollout support the client's own staff and suppliers actually use — and whoever owns the rollout owns the retained relationship.

## The Problem
The studio would happily host each client's living guidelines and approved assets — it is the natural guardian of the identity — but the tools are priced and structured for the client side. A small client won't pay enterprise brand-portal prices, so the guidelines ship as a PDF and the assets as a zip file.

## What Already Exists
Frontify, Bynder and Brandfolder provide brand guidelines sites, asset libraries and template tools, quote-priced for corporate brand teams. Canva for Teams offers brand kits and locked templates at low cost, and is where most small clients' staff actually produce material.

## The Customization Gap
The studio version needs: (1) one studio account holding many client brands, each with its own portal, permissions and studio branding; (2) guideline pages generated from the identity's master files rather than written from scratch; (3) template sync into the client's Canva and Figma so the rules travel to where staff work; (4) per-client pricing a ~$15,000 (estimate) identity project can absorb; and (5) usage analytics the studio can review when proposing the next phase of work.

## Target Customer
Identity studios and senior freelancers who launch several identities a year and want to stay attached to them.

## Impact If Solved
The studio keeps a live relationship with every identity it launches, the client gets a usable portal at small-business cost, and the rollout work flows back to the people who designed the system.
