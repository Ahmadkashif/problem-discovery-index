# Identity Drift Monitoring After Launch

**Niche:** [[niches/graphic-design-studios/brand-identity-programs/profile|Brand Identity Programs]]
**Industry:** [[industries/graphic-design-studios|Graphic Design Studios]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** An identity is judged by how intact it is a year after launch, and neither the studio nor the client's head of brand has any measure of it.
**Tags:** #cnns #contrastive-learning #object-detection #transformers #revenue-impact #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a new identity used intact across every touchpoint after launch — through the templates, guidelines and rollout support the client's own staff and suppliers actually use — and whoever owns the rollout owns the retained relationship.

## The Problem
Six months after launch, the logo appears stretched on a trade-show banner, the brand blue has drifted to three different blues across the website, social posts and a franchisee's flyer, and someone in sales has added a drop shadow. The head of brand notices some of it; the studio sees none of it until the client says the identity "isn't working" and starts talking to another studio. The drift is gradual, distributed across dozens of people and suppliers, and invisible in any single file.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Brand portals sell storage and distribution of approved assets, not monitoring of what gets made. Enterprise brand-compliance checks exist for regulated advertising review, not for identity integrity. Studios have no tooling and no data feed from the client's channels once the project closes.

## What to Build
A monitoring service the studio runs for its identity clients. It collects the client's public output — website pages, social posts, Canva exports, photos of printed collateral and signage submitted by staff — detects the identity's elements (logo, colours, type, clear space), and scores each piece against the identity rules learned from the master files and guidelines. A monthly report to the head of brand shows adherence by channel and supplier, with the worst offenders and suggested fixes. The studio sells it as part of a guardianship retainer.

## Target Customer
Identity studios of 5–30 people with ten or more launched identities, and their clients' heads of brand at multi-location or franchised businesses.

## Impact If Built
Turns a one-off identity fee into a recurring guardianship relationship, gives the client evidence of where the brand is breaking, and gives the studio the first measure of whether its identities survive contact with the client.
