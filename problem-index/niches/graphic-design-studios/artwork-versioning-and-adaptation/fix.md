# One Correction, Sixty Files

**Niche:** [[niches/graphic-design-studios/artwork-versioning-and-adaptation/profile|Artwork Versioning & Adaptation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/graphic-design-studios|Graphic Design Studios]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The client changes the price on variant 12, and the production artist opens the other fifty-nine files to make the same change by hand.
**Tags:** #automation #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #worker-facing #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce every size, language and channel variant from an approved master with no manual touch and no brand or legal-copy error, and whoever delivers the most compliant variants per hour takes the account.

## The Problem
A late correction — a price, a date, a legal line, a product shot — applies to every variant of a launch. Because each variant is an independent file, the change is repeated sixty times, re-exported sixty times and re-proofed sixty times, and the variant that was missed is the one the client finds.

## Why It's Still Broken
Variants are built by duplicating and editing files, so once created they have no link back to the master. Linked assets and smart objects help with images, but copy and layout changes still propagate by hand.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Variants generated from and linked to the master's content fields, so a correction to the price or legal line is made once and propagated to every variant, with any variant whose layout breaks under the new copy flagged for a designer and a report of exactly which files changed.

## Who Feels the Pain
Production artists repeating corrections overnight; proofreaders re-checking every variant; account leads explaining the one that slipped through.

## Impact If Fixed
Late corrections take minutes instead of hours and stop producing the single missed variant that undermines client confidence.
