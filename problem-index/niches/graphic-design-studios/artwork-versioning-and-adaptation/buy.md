# Creative Automation Platforms Re-Pointed at Studio Production

**Niche:** [[niches/graphic-design-studios/artwork-versioning-and-adaptation/profile|Artwork Versioning & Adaptation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/graphic-design-studios|Graphic Design Studios]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Creative automation platforms generate hundreds of ad variants from templates for brands' in-house teams; a studio adapting work for fifteen clients across print and digital can't use them economically.
**Tags:** #transformers #automation #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce every size, language and channel variant from an approved master with no manual touch and no brand or legal-copy error, and whoever delivers the most compliant variants per hour takes the account.

## The Problem
A studio's adaptation work arrives job by job, for different clients, mixing digital ad sizes with print specifications and point-of-sale. Enterprise creative automation assumes one brand, a long-lived template library and a digital ad focus.

## What Already Exists
Celtra, Bannerflow and Storyteq provide template-driven creative automation, variant generation and ad delivery for brand and agency in-house teams; Adobe and Canva offer bulk resizing within their own tools.

## The Customization Gap
A studio configuration needs: (1) per-job projects across many clients, with each client's brand rules attached; (2) masters imported from Illustrator, InDesign and Figma rather than rebuilt as platform templates; (3) print outputs with bleed, colour space and publication specs alongside digital; (4) translation handling with text-expansion rules; and (5) pricing per job or per variant that the studio can pass through.

## Target Customer
Production directors at studios and production houses with recurring adaptation work.

## Impact If Solved
Studios gain the throughput of in-house brand automation without abandoning their own file workflow or carrying an enterprise contract per client.
