# Wayfinding & Environmental Graphics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/graphic-design-studios|Graphic Design Studios]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver a sign message schedule and fabrication drawings that pass ADA review and go out to fabricator bid without RFIs, and whoever produces a schedule fabricators can price without questions takes the project.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600M US wayfinding, signage and environmental graphic design fees, excluding fabrication and installation (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~4% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — site surveys on paper and phone photos, sign locations marked on PDF floor plans, message schedules in Excel maintained by hand against the architect's changing drawings
**Target Buyer:** Environmental graphic design studio principal; on the client side, the architect or the owner's project manager for a hospital, campus, airport or mixed-use development
**Automation Potential:** Medium — schedules, location rules and ADA checks automate; the wayfinding strategy and the site judgement do not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Wayfinding is graphic design delivered into buildings. The studio designs a family of sign types, decides where every sign goes and what it says, and hands a sign location plan, a message schedule and design-intent drawings to a fabricator through a bid. Every interior room-identification sign must meet the 2010 ADA Standards §703: tactile characters 48 to 60 inches above the floor, uppercase sans serif between 5/8 and 2 inches high, braille, mounted on the latch side of the door. The work tracks the architect's drawing revisions for months. Trade-show exhibits and fabricated displays belong to specialty design studios, not here.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Excel for message schedules; Bluebeam Revu or Acrobat for marking sign locations on floor plans; Illustrator for sign-type drawings; occasionally Revit families when the architect requires them; specialised sign-planning software such as SignAgent at a few firms. Gaps: the schedule is disconnected from the floor plan and from the architect's model; room names and numbers change late; ADA compliance is checked by eye; and fabricators raise requests for information when the schedule and drawings disagree.
