# Packaging & Shelf-Impact Testing Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/graphic-design-studios|Graphic Design Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Consumer research firms that test packaging before launch: simulated-shelf findability, eye tracking, standout against competitors, purchase intent and communication of claims, scored against normative databases built from decades of past tests. Behaviorally (formerly PRS IN VIVO, founded 1972) is the specialist; Ipsos, Kantar and NIQ run packaging practices alongside concept and ad testing. Consumer-goods brand and insights teams pay per study, usually to choose between design routes from a studio.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 researchers, methodologists and panel and facility staff per specialist or practice
**Output:** Pack test reports with scores against category norms, eye-tracking heatmaps, shelf standout and findability metrics, route recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Normative databases of tested packs by category with shopper response and eye-tracking records — a pre-rebrand listing claimed 800+ custom studies a year for the specialist alone
**Clock:** Retailer shelf resets and line reviews, launch dates and print-plate deadlines
**Buyer:** Head of packaging research / Chief Research Officer

**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold by one point; scored the same way as the creative pre-testing firms logged under SMB marketing agencies, and for the same reason: the norms are rarely validated against what the tested pack later sold

The firm holds the most valuable dataset in this value chain — which design decisions make a pack easier to find — and grades its own predictions against in-market results almost nowhere.
