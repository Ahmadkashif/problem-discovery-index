# Niche Analysis — Online Tutoring Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/online-tutoring-platforms|Online Tutoring Platforms]]

## Niche Selection

A tutoring marketplace holds recordings of millions of one-to-one teaching sessions and uses them to compute a rebooking rate. The eight niches follow from that: the measurement nobody makes, the matching that runs without it, the two places where unpaid work accumulates, the two people the system governs, and the mechanical layers.

## Why These Niches

Effectiveness is the thing being sold and the thing nobody measures. Matching consumes whatever measurement exists, which is why it optimises satisfaction. Diagnosis and progress reporting are the two unpaid activities that distinguish good tutoring from likeable tutoring. The two underserved people are the tutor whose income depends on being liked, and the quality lead responsible for a standard they have no instrument for. Scheduling and vetting are mechanical, and the first is where the tutor's time is quietly taken.

## Filter Notes

Seven of the eight are terminal — each names one contest, and dividing further produces features rather than markets.

**Tutor effectiveness** is not, and its halves separate on where the evidence has to come from. Session instruction analysis stays entirely inside the product: the platform holds the recordings, and what a tutor does in a session — how much the student talks, whether questions are asked before explanations, how long the tutor waits after asking, whether misconceptions are diagnosed or corrected, whether the work is handed back to the student — is observable, gradeable against an established teaching literature, and immediately useful as feedback whether or not anyone ever measures the outcome. Learning gain attribution reaches outside it: whether the student actually learned requires assessments, grades or test scores the platform does not hold and must persuade families or schools to supply; the causal question requires a comparison to what would have happened without the tutoring; the horizon is months rather than sessions; and the finding may be that a well-liked, well-rated segment of the tutor base produces no measurable gain at all, which is not a result a marketplace wants attributable to individuals. A platform builds the in-session analysis for its own coaching and quality programme and defers the attribution indefinitely, which is why every platform in this category can tell you a tutor's rating and none can tell you whether their students learned.

Two adjacent candidates were rejected as belonging elsewhere: **in-person supplementary education delivered through physical centres** is the subject of [[industries/tutoring-centers|Tutoring Centers]], and **self-paced recorded course content** belongs to [[industries/online-course-platforms|Online Course Platforms]].
