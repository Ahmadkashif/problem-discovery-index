# Tutor Effectiveness & Learning Measurement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/online-tutoring-platforms|Online Tutoring Platforms]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Whether the platform can distinguish a tutor who helps students learn from one who is simply liked.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.24B — 28% of US online tutoring and homework help
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~28%
**Digital Adoption:** Near zero — rebooking rate is the proxy for everything
**Target Buyer:** Platform product leadership; schools and districts buying tutoring at scale
**Automation Potential:** High on the in-session half; genuinely hard on the outcome half

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche

The platform ranks tutors by rating and rebooking, takes a commission it sets unilaterally, and has no idea which of its tutors actually help students learn.

That is the central fact of this industry and it propagates into everything else. Matching optimises satisfaction because satisfaction is what is measurable. Rankings reward warmth. Commission is charged on a service whose quality the charging party cannot assess. And the unpaid preparation that most distinguishes an effective tutor from a pleasant one is systematically unrewarded, because the measurement cannot see it.

The niche is distinct because it is a measurement problem rather than a product problem. Everything else in this industry is downstream of it — the matching, the ranking, the pricing, the tutor's incentive to prepare — and none of those can be fixed without it.

## Current Tools & Gaps

Platforms collect post-session star ratings, track rebooking and retention, monitor cancellations and complaints, and in some cases sample session recordings for quality review. A few of the larger platforms and the district-contracted providers run pre- and post-assessments on their own curricula.

The gaps are both halves of the question. What happened in the session is recorded and unanalysed — the platform holds video of millions of hours of one-to-one teaching and computes nothing from it beyond duration. Whether the student learned is not measured at all in the consumer segment, because the assessments that would show it belong to a school the platform has no relationship with.
