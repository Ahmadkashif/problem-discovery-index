# Buy: Contact Centre Quality Management Adapted to Teaching

**Industry:** [[industries/online-tutoring-platforms|Online Tutoring Platforms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Contact centre quality management already scores every recorded interaction against a rubric and routes coaching from it; the rubric is about handling a call and the workforce is employed.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #workflow-orchestration #descriptive-statistics #automation #compliance
**Contested on:** Whether call centre quality infrastructure can be re-rubricked for teaching and applied to contractors.

## The Problem

Quality management for recorded conversations is a solved commercial problem, in contact centres. Speech analytics platforms transcribe every call, score it against a configurable rubric, surface outliers, route coaching, track agent improvement over time and report programme effectiveness to management. The category is mature, competitive and affordable.

The structural fit to tutoring is strong: recorded one-to-one conversations, a large distributed workforce, a quality rubric, coaching as the intervention. What does not fit is what the rubric measures and who the workforce is — call handling versus teaching, employees versus contractors.

## What Already Exists

NICE, Verint, CallMiner, Observe.AI and the speech analytics and quality management category. Automated scoring against configurable rubrics, coaching workflow, agent scorecards, calibration between human reviewers, and programme-level reporting. Transcription with diarisation. All production-grade and considerably ahead of anything in the education sector for this purpose.

## The Customization Gap

**The rubric is the entire adaptation.** Call quality rubrics score greeting, compliance statements, resolution, sentiment and handle time. A teaching rubric scores questioning, wait time, error diagnosis, cognitive load, scaffolding and release of responsibility — constructs from the education literature with no overlap. Building and validating it against expert raters is the substantive work and it is not a configuration exercise.

**The workforce is contractors, and the whole product assumes supervision.** Scorecards, coaching assignments, performance management workflow and targets all presume an employment relationship with a manager. Here the same mechanisms have to be reframed as optional development offered to independent professionals, which changes the product's defaults and its language everywhere.

**Handle time is not the efficiency metric — it is the product.** Contact centres optimise shorter interactions. Tutoring sells the hour. Every efficiency construct in these platforms is either irrelevant or actively wrong here, and leaving them enabled sends a disastrous signal to the workforce.

**Longitudinal student context matters.** Call quality is per-interaction. Teaching quality is partly about continuity — building on the last session, revisiting an earlier misconception. The analysis needs a per-student thread across sessions, which the interaction-centric data model does not have.

**Minors and recordings.** Contact centre compliance handles consent, PCI redaction and call recording law for adults. Here the recorded party is a child, consent comes from a parent, and the retention, access and analysis posture is substantially stricter and not something the vendors' compliance modules model.

## Target Customer

Speech analytics vendors, for whom online tutoring is an adjacent vertical with large session volume, an identical technical shape and no incumbent. Also tutoring platforms evaluating build-versus-buy for a quality programme, who will find 80% of the infrastructure already exists in a category they were not looking at.

## Impact If Solved

The transcription, scoring, coaching workflow, calibration and reporting infrastructure gets bought, and the teaching rubric, the contractor framing, the removal of efficiency constructs, the student thread and the minors' compliance get built. The practical result is a quality function with per-session coverage in a quarter rather than a year.
