# Lot Cataloguing & Online Listing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auctioneers|Auctioneers]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a photo table into a published, searchable catalogue, and whoever gets a sale listed in the fewest cataloguer-hours with the fewest "not as described" disputes takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600M–1B a year of U.S. cataloguing labour and cataloguing software spend at auction firms (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of auctioneer revenue spent on the function (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — photos and listings are digital; identification, lotting and description are typed by hand
**Target Buyer:** Operations manager or owner of an auction firm; product leads at bidding platforms and clerking vendors
**Automation Potential:** High — the same photograph, identify, describe and categorise loop repeats for every lot

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every auction firm, whatever it sells, must catalogue: ATG alone listed 26.8m lots in FY25 across its marketplaces. The work is the same shape everywhere (photograph, identify, lot, describe, categorise, publish) and the output is measurable in hours per lot and in disputes. Vendor claims put a 400–600-lot estate sale at 80–120 staff-hours of set-up. Restricted items (firearms, ivory, hazardous goods, titled vehicles) must be caught before listing. Condition grading for managed resale is analysed under [[niches/recommerce-platforms/condition-grading/profile|Condition Grading]] and attribute extraction under [[niches/recommerce-platforms/catalogue-attribute-automation/profile|Catalogue & Attribute Automation]]; the auction version differs in lotting, house style and multi-platform publishing. The marketplace seller's version of the same loop, turning an amateur's photos into a structured listing, is analysed as [[niches/online-marketplaces/listing-structuring/profile|Listing Structuring]] under Online Marketplaces, and the reference-price problem buyers and sellers face there as [[niches/online-marketplaces/pricing-and-reference-values/profile|Pricing & Reference Values]]. The difference here is that a professional cataloguer writes descriptions that become terms of sale, for lots that close on a fixed date.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Mobile cataloguing in Wavebid, AuctionMethod and Auction Flex; ATG's in-house category prediction; reseller listing assistants; vision-language models. Gaps: lotting suggestions, a model trained on the firm's own catalogues, restricted-item checks before publication, and one catalogue published to several platforms.
