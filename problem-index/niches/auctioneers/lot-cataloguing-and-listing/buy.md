# Multichannel Listing Tools Extended to Bidding Platform Feeds

**Niche:** [[niches/auctioneers/lot-cataloguing-and-listing/profile|Lot Cataloguing & Online Listing]]
**Industry:** [[industries/auctioneers|Auctioneers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Resellers already publish one inventory to many marketplaces with multichannel tools; auctioneers re-upload the same catalogue to each bidding platform by hand.
**Tags:** #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #large-language-models #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a photo table into a published, searchable catalogue, and whoever gets a sale listed in the fewest cataloguer-hours with the fewest "not as described" disputes takes the account.

## The Problem
A firm that sells on HiBid and Proxibid, or adds LiveAuctioneers for a better antique, maintains the catalogue in several places. Categories, required fields, image limits and upload formats differ, and late changes (a withdrawn lot, a corrected description) must be made everywhere.

## What Already Exists
Multichannel listing tools for resellers and retailers that map one product record to many marketplaces; platform upload templates; ATG's cross-listing between its own marketplaces (atgXL).

## The Customization Gap
Auction catalogues need: lot numbers and sale structure rather than SKUs; consignor codes that must survive every export for settlement; auction-specific fields (reserve, starting bid, increment table, closing order); category mapping across platform taxonomies; and synchronised withdrawals and corrections up to closing.

## Target Customer
Firms cross-listing sales on two or more bidding platforms.

## Impact If Solved
One catalogue maintained once, fewer listing errors, and freedom to add a platform for the lots that need it.
