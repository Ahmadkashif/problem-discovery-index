# State Auctioneer Licensing Boards

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auctioneers|Auctioneers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Roughly half the states license auctioneers (24–33 depending on the source), through boards such as Florida's Board of Auctioneers, which enforces written-agreement and escrow rules (s. 468.388) and runs an Auctioneer Recovery Fund (s. 468.392). Boards set education and exam requirements, investigate consignor complaints and discipline licensees. The appraiser counterpart is logged as [[niches/real-estate-appraisers/appraiser-licensing-boards/profile|State Appraiser Licensing Boards]] (31).

## Insight Function
**Size:** A handful of licensing, investigative and legal staff per state, usually inside a broader licensing department (estimate)
**Output:** Licences, investigations, disciplinary orders; nothing is sold
**Proprietary data:** Licence rolls and complaint files
**Clock:** Licence renewal cycles
**Buyer:** None commercial
