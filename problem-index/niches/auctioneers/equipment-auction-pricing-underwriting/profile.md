# Equipment Auction Majors' Pricing & Guarantee Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auctioneers|Auctioneers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The central pricing and underwriting teams at the national equipment auction firms (RB Global's Ritchie Bros. and IronPlanet, with $5.66B of construction and transportation GTV in 2025; Purple Wave; BigIron) set the guarantees, outright purchases and proposal numbers their territory managers offer consignors, and forecast lot prices for marketing and inventory decisions. Their output is used internally to win consignments; the firm is paid by commission and fees. RB Global's invoiced valuation products (Rouse) are profiled separately as [[niches/sales-finance-companies/equipment-valuation-guides/profile|Equipment Valuation & Residual Data Providers]] (47), and its IAA salvage business as [[niches/property-casualty-insurers/salvage-auction-platforms/profile|Salvage Auction Platforms]] (30).

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 pricing analysts and data scientists per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Internal price forecasts, guarantee and underwritten-contract pricing, territory proposal support
**Proprietary data:** The largest equipment auction record in North America. Sold prices are visible to bidders, but serial-level inspection reports, guarantee outcomes and bidding records are the firms' own
**Clock:** The firms' own sale calendars and consignors' decisions; no third-party deadline
**Buyer:** Head of Pricing / Chief Revenue Officer
