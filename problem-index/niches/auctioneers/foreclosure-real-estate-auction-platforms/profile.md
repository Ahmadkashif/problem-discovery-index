# Foreclosure & Distressed Real Estate Auction Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auctioneers|Auctioneers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Auction.com (held by Thomas H. Lee Partners funds when Ten-X was sold in 2020), Xome and similar platforms run foreclosure trustee sales and bank-owned (REO) auctions for mortgage servicers, while CoStar's Ten-X (bought for $190M in 2020) runs online commercial property auctions. Servicers pay fees to dispose of properties; buyers pay premiums. The platforms publish market research on foreclosure auction activity as marketing.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 research, pricing and data science staff per platform (estimate)
**Output:** Reserve and pricing recommendations to servicers, disposition analytics, published market reports
**Proprietary data:** Bidding histories, reserves and unsold outcomes are the platform's own; the sale prices themselves end up in public deed records; servicers' loan-level data is supplied for each asset
**Clock:** Statutory foreclosure sale dates set by courts and trustee sale notices; servicer disposition timelines under investor guidelines
**Buyer:** Head of Servicer Analytics / Chief Economist
