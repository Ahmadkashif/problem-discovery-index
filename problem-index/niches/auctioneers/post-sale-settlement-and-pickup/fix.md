# Settlement Statements Reconciled in a Weekend Spreadsheet

**Niche:** [[niches/auctioneers/post-sale-settlement-and-pickup/profile|Post-Sale Settlement & Pickup]]
**Industry:** [[industries/auctioneers|Auctioneers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The clerking report, the platform payout and the bank deposit never agree on the first try, so settlement waits for someone to reconcile them by hand.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #descriptive-statistics #data-integration #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to own the money flow after the hammer, and whoever collects from bidders, settles every consignor and reconciles the escrow account fastest without a licensing-board complaint takes the account.

## The Problem
After a sale, the clerking system shows hammer prices and buyer's premiums, the platform pays out net of its fees and card charges on its own schedule, some buyers paid by cheque or wire, and some lots are unpaid. The office reconciles these in a spreadsheet before cheques go to consignors, often over a weekend, and consignors call asking where their money is.

## Why It's Still Broken
Each system reports in its own format and timing, and no tool owns the three-way match between sale results, payment receipts and bank deposits.

## What a Fix Looks Like
An automatic match of lot-level results to platform payout lines and bank transactions, with exceptions listed (unpaid, short-paid, chargebacks), consignor statements generated once the match is clean, and a reconciliation report kept for the escrow record.

## Who Feels the Pain
Office managers and owners' spouses doing the books, and consignors waiting for payment.

## Impact If Fixed
Settlement in days rather than weeks and a day of clerical work per sale removed (estimate).
