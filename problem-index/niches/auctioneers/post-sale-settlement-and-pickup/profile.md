# Post-Sale Settlement & Pickup

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auctioneers|Auctioneers]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to own the money flow after the hammer, and whoever collects from bidders, settles every consignor and reconciles the escrow account fastest without a licensing-board complaint takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300–500M a year of U.S. office labour, payment processing margin and software spend on post-sale work at auction firms (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of auctioneer revenue spent on the function (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — payments are increasingly processed by the platforms; settlement, escrow reconciliation and pickup logistics are manual
**Target Buyer:** Office manager or owner of an auction firm; payments and product leads at bidding platforms
**Automation Potential:** High — invoicing, reminders, scheduling, settlement arithmetic and reconciliation repeat after every sale

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
After every sale the same sequence runs: invoices, payment collection and retries, unpaid-lot handling, pickup scheduling and shipping, buyer questions and disputes, consignor settlement under each contract, and escrow deposits and reconciliation under state rules. Florida, for example, requires unsettled proceeds in an escrow or trust account within two working days and monthly reconciliation. Platforms are moving into the money flow: ATG's atgPay processed 67% of U.S. LiveAuctioneers transaction value in FY25. Whoever runs this flow holds the firm's daily operations and its regulatory record.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Platform payment services (atgPay, HiBid payments), clerking-software settlement modules, QuickBooks, bank portals, texts and phone calls for pickup. Gaps: bidder default risk at registration, pickup and shipping automation, settlement that applies tiered contracts and reversals automatically, and an escrow reconciliation that flags late deposits.
