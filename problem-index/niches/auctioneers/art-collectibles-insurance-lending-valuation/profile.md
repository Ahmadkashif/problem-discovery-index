# Art & Collectibles Insurance and Lending Valuation Desks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auctioneers|Auctioneers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Private-client insurers that schedule fine art, jewellery and collections, and lenders that lend against art and collectibles (auction-house finance arms, specialist art lenders and private banks), employ valuation and collateral teams that set insured values, review appraisals, mark collateral and decide advance rates, drawing on auction results as their primary evidence.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 valuation and collateral specialists per carrier or lender (estimate)
**Output:** Internal insured-value reviews, collateral marks and advance-rate decisions
**Proprietary data:** Scheduled values, claims histories and loan collateral marks against realised sales
**Clock:** Policy renewals and loan covenant revaluations
**Buyer:** Head of Fine Art Underwriting / Head of Art Lending
