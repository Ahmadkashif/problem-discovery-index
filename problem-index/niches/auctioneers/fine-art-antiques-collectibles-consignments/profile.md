# Fine Art, Antiques & Collectibles Consignments

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auctioneers|Auctioneers]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to persuade a consignor that it will put a valuable piece in front of the bidders who will pay most for it, and whoever's attribution and estimate are confirmed by the hammer, rather than undercut by a passed lot or a post-sale re-attribution, takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$700M–1B a year of U.S. commission and buyer's premium at regional and national specialist houses below the two global majors (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of auctioneer commission and fee revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium-High — international online bidding through LiveAuctioneers and Invaluable is standard; attribution and estimates are specialist judgement
**Target Buyer:** Head of a specialist department or owner of a regional fine and decorative arts house
**Automation Potential:** Medium — research and condition reporting automate; attribution does not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A single painting, piece of silver, coin collection or sports card can be worth more than an entire house. The consignor, often advised by an executor, attorney or appraiser, shops it between houses on the estimate, the commission and the house's reach. Heritage Auctions sold $2.16B in 2025; Sotheby's and Christie's sold $5.7B and $4.7B at auction worldwide; below them sit dozens of regional specialist houses selling to international online bidders. The estimate is public in the catalogue and the hammer is public afterwards, so every miss is visible. Studies of the major houses found estimates for expensive paintings biased upward for decades (Mei and Moses, 2005). Dealers making outright offers and list-price marketplaces such as Chairish (bought by ATG in 2025) are the alternatives. Galleries pricing living artists' work belong to the queued art-galleries build.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Price databases (Artnet ~18M results, LiveAuctioneers ~29M free results, Invaluable, WorthPoint), catalogue raisonnés and specialist libraries, the house's own archive. Gaps: a graded record of each specialist's estimates and attributions, structured condition reports, and research time per lot.
