# Condition Reports Written After the Bidder Asks

**Niche:** [[niches/auctioneers/fine-art-antiques-collectibles-consignments/profile|Fine Art, Antiques & Collectibles Consignments]]
**Industry:** [[industries/auctioneers|Auctioneers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Remote bidders on valuable lots request condition reports by email in the days before the sale, and specialists write them one at a time under deadline.
**Tags:** #cnns #semantic-segmentation #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to persuade a consignor that it will put a valuable piece in front of the bidders who will pay most for it, and whoever's attribution and estimate are confirmed by the hammer, rather than undercut by a passed lot or a post-sale re-attribution, takes the account.

## The Problem
Catalogue descriptions say little about condition, and serious bidders who cannot attend the preview ask for reports: losses, restorations, retouching under UV light, repairs to porcelain, toning on coins. Requests cluster in the last days before the sale, when specialists are busiest, and answers vary in depth from one specialist to the next.

## Why It's Still Broken
Condition reporting is treated as a courtesy rather than part of cataloguing, so it happens reactively. There is no structured format, and the reports written are not reused when a piece is re-offered.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Structured condition capture at cataloguing for lots above a value threshold: a standard photo set including raking and UV light where relevant, a category-specific checklist, and a drafted report the specialist edits. Reports are published with the lot and kept with the piece's record.

## Who Feels the Pain
Specialists in the final week before a sale, remote bidders deciding how much to risk, and consignors whose lots draw cautious bids.

## Impact If Fixed
Fewer last-minute requests, more confident remote bidding, and fewer post-sale disputes over condition.
