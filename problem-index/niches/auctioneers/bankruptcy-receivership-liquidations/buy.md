# Trustee Case Software Linked to the Auction

**Niche:** [[niches/auctioneers/bankruptcy-receivership-liquidations/profile|Bankruptcy, Receivership & Court-Ordered Liquidations]]
**Industry:** [[industries/auctioneers|Auctioneers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Trustees run every case in case-management software; the auctioneer's inventory, sale results and fee request reach it as PDFs and spreadsheets.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to be the liquidator a trustee, receiver or secured lender retains, and whoever's up-front liquidation estimate is met at the sale, so the court-approved fee and any guarantee hold, takes the account.

## The Problem
For each case the trustee must track assets, sales, receipts and professional fees, and report to the court and the U.S. Trustee. The auctioneer's data (inventory, lot results, buyer payments, expenses) is re-keyed by the trustee's staff.

## What Already Exists
Bankruptcy trustee case-management systems and estate accounting tools, and the auctioneer's clerking software.

## The Customization Gap
The link needs: asset schedules mapped to lots; sale results and receipts posted as case transactions; expenses itemised as the court requires for reimbursement; drafted retention declarations, reports of sale and fee applications populated from sale data; and receiverships and assignments handled under each state's forms.

## Target Customer
Liquidation auctioneers who want to be the trustees' default choice, and the trustees' case administrators.

## Impact If Solved
Less re-keying for both sides and faster case closing, which trustees reward with the next retention.
