# Bankruptcy, Receivership & Court-Ordered Liquidations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auctioneers|Auctioneers]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to be the liquidator a trustee, receiver or secured lender retains, and whoever's up-front liquidation estimate is met at the sale, so the court-approved fee and any guarantee hold, takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300–600M a year of U.S. commission and fees on assets sold for bankruptcy trustees, receivers, assignees for the benefit of creditors and secured lenders (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of auctioneer commission and fee revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — sales run online, but inventories, retention papers, reports of sale and fee applications are assembled by hand from clerking printouts
**Target Buyer:** Owner of a regional industrial and commercial liquidation auction firm; the trustee or receiver as the client
**Automation Potential:** Medium — court paperwork and reports automate well; the liquidation estimate is judgement

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
When a restaurant, machine shop or distributor fails, its equipment and inventory are sold for creditors under a court's supervision. In bankruptcy the auctioneer is a professional retained with court approval and paid only as the court allows (Bankruptcy Code ss. 327 and 330, recalled); the sale itself is authorised by order, and a report of sale must be filed afterwards. Receivers and assignees work under state courts; secured lenders sell repossessed collateral, the work [[industries/repossession-services|Repossession Agencies]] hands over once equipment is recovered. Clocks are set by others: hearing dates, sale orders, lease rejections that force a premises to be emptied, and lenders' carrying costs. The largest cases go to national appraisal-liquidation firms, logged as a Pass 2 pocket; this niche is the regional firm handling the many smaller cases.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Online platforms (Proxibid, BidSpotter, HiBid), clerking software, trustees' case-management systems, value guides for equipment. Gaps: liquidation estimates with comparables a trustee can attach to a motion, reports of sale and fee applications generated from sale data, and a record of estimates against results.
