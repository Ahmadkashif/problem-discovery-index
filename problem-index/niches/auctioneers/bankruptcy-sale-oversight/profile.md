# Bankruptcy Trustees & U.S. Trustee Sale Oversight

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auctioneers|Auctioneers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Panel trustees retain auctioneers in Chapter 7 cases, bankruptcy courts approve retentions, sales and fees, and the U.S. Trustee Program reviews professionals' fee applications and trustees' reports. Together they decide which auctioneers get court work and on what terms.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Panel trustees are practices of a few people each; the U.S. Trustee Program's regional offices assign a few analysts and attorneys per case (estimate)
**Output:** Retention and sale orders, fee reviews, case reports; nothing is sold
**Proprietary data:** Case filings and reports of sale, largely public on court dockets
**Clock:** Statutory and court deadlines
**Buyer:** None commercial
