# Bidding Platform Bundles Repriced for a Seasonal Firm

**Niche:** [[niches/auctioneers/single-auctioneer-rural-firms/profile|Single-Auctioneer Rural Firms]]
**Industry:** [[industries/auctioneers|Auctioneers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** The platforms already bundle clerking, listing and payments; a one-auctioneer firm needs the bundle stripped to its sale-day essentials and priced per sale.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #automation #quick-win #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to become the one-auctioneer firm's entire back office, and whoever lets one licensed auctioneer and a family crew catalogue, run and settle a sale without hiring an office clerk takes the account.

## The Problem
A firm with a busy autumn and spring and an idle summer pays monthly subscriptions and per-lot or commission fees, and uses a fraction of the features, while doing settlement in a spreadsheet anyway.

## What Already Exists
Auction Flex 360 with HiBid (listed at $95 a month for one user), Proxibid and BidSpotter marketing packages, AuctionTime for farm equipment, and clerking apps from Wavebid and AuctionMethod.

## The Customization Gap
The small-firm edition needs: pricing per sale or per lot sold; an offline live-ring clerking mode for barns and fairgrounds without signal; consignor statements in plain language for farm families; a simple escrow ledger matching the state's rules; and printable sale bills and bidder cards for the in-person crowd that still comes.

## Target Customer
Single-auctioneer firms already listing on a national platform.

## Impact If Solved
Per-sale pricing would save a seasonal firm ~$500–1,500 a year of idle-month subscriptions (estimate, five slow months at the $95–295 monthly list prices), and one bundle would remove ~2–4 hours of re-keying per sale between clerking, listing and settlement (estimate).
