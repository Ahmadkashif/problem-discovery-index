# Estate & Personal Property Auctions

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auctioneers|Auctioneers]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor is fighting to net an estate more than the alternatives would, but that produces two contests with different winners: clearing a whole house by the executor's closing date, which the local firm with crews and an online timed sale wins against estate-sale companies and cleanouts, and placing the few valuable pieces in front of the specialist bidders who will pay most, which regional and national specialist houses win. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.4–2.0B a year of U.S. commission and buyer's premium on estate contents, antiques, art and collectibles sold by auction (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~30% of auctioneer commission and fee revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — bidding is online; inventory, valuation and executor reporting are manual
**Target Buyer:** Owner-auctioneer of an estate auction firm; executor or probate attorney as the consignor
**Automation Potential:** High for cataloguing and reporting, Medium for valuation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Estates are the classic auction consignment: a death, a move to care or a divorce leaves a household that has to become money by a date someone else sets, usually the sale or closing of the house. The executor answers to heirs and often to a probate court, and must report what was sold and for how much. Most of a house is low-value contents that only an efficient, fast sale can turn into money; a few pieces may be worth more than everything else together and need specialist marketing. Estate-sale companies running tag sales in the house, cleanout firms and donation pickups compete for the bulk; specialist houses compete for the best pieces; senior move managers, profiled under [[niches/moving-companies/senior-downsizing-specialists/profile|Senior Downsizing Specialists]], often decide who gets the call. Estate law and the executor's duties sit with [[industries/estate-planning|Estate Planning Law Firms]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Online timed auction platforms (HiBid, Proxibid, LiveAuctioneers), clerking software, sold-price research on WorthPoint and LiveAuctioneers price results. Gaps: a day-one triage of what goes where, executor-facing reporting that a court and heirs can follow, and estimates per item that survive lotting.
