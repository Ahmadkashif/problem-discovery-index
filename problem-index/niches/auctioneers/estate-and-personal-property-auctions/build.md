## The Problem
The auctioneer walks the house with the executor for an hour. In that hour the most consequential decisions are made: the dining set goes in the local sale, the painting over the fireplace should go to a specialist house, the books are a donation, the basement is a cleanout. A missed signed piece sells in a box lot for a few dollars; a piece routed to a specialist that the local market would have bought just as well costs the estate months and a higher commission.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The decision happens in a sales meeting, on foot, and relies on recognition that experienced auctioneers cannot explain. Comparable data exists for individual items but nothing assembles a house-level view during the walk.

## What to Build
A walkthrough capture that drafts a room-by-room inventory from video, estimates value ranges for recognised items, flags candidates for specialist review with the evidence, and proposes a routing for each group with the expected net under each route. The auctioneer's overrides are recorded and later graded against what each route delivered.

## Target Customer
Estate auction firms that compete with estate-sale companies and specialist houses for the same executors.

## Impact If Built
Fewer valuable pieces lost in box lots, better-argued proposals to executors, and a record that teaches junior staff what the founder sees.
