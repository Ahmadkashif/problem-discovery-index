## The Problem
An executor must inventory the estate, often with date-of-death values, and later account for every asset sold. Household contents are one line that becomes hundreds of lots, and the settlement statement from the auctioneer arrives as a printout or PDF the executor or attorney retypes.

## What Already Exists
Estate administration software for executors and law firms, probate practice-management tools, and the auctioneer's clerking software, which already produces consignor settlement statements.

## The Customization Gap
The link needs: the auctioneer's inventory and estimates exported as an estate inventory schedule; date-of-death value ranges where an appraisal is not separately commissioned, clearly labelled as auction estimates rather than USPAP appraisals; settlement data mapped to the accounting format the probate court expects; and handling of family-kept items, donations and withdrawn lots, so the executor's accounting reconciles to the auctioneer's records.

## Target Customer
Estate auction firms that want probate attorneys to refer them, and the attorneys and executors who receive their statements.

## Impact If Solved
Hours of retyping removed from every estate accounting, fewer reconciliation errors, and a reason for attorneys to keep sending estates to the firm whose data flows.
