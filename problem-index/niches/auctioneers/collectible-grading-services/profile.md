# Coin, Card & Collectible Grading Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auctioneers|Auctioneers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Third-party graders such as PSA (Collectors), PCGS, NGC, CGC and Beckett authenticate and grade coins, trading cards, comics and memorabilia for a fee per item, encapsulate them, and publish population reports and price guides that auction houses cite in catalogues. Graded items sell at auction on the grade.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Large grading floors of production graders; price-guide and research teams of under 10 per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Grades and certificates per item; population reports; price guides as by-products
**Proprietary data:** Population counts and grade histories per certified item
**Clock:** Customer turnaround tiers set by the grader itself
**Buyer:** Head of Grading / President
