# Online Auction Marketplace Operators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auctioneers|Auctioneers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Auction Technology Group (LiveAuctioneers, Proxibid, BidSpotter, the-saleroom and others; 26.8m lots listed and $12.1B of hammer value across its marketplaces in FY25), HiBid on Auction Flex software, Sandhills' AuctionTime and Equipmentfacts, Invaluable, and Liquidity Services' GovDeals and AllSurplus (~$1.57B GMV in FY2025) host independent auctioneers' sales online. Auctioneers pay commission on online sales, marketing packages and payment fees; ATG's take rate was 4.8%. Unlike the fixed-price marketplaces analysed under [[industries/online-marketplaces|Online Marketplaces]], where [[niches/online-marketplaces/listing-structuring/profile|Listing Structuring]] and [[niches/online-marketplaces/pricing-and-reference-values/profile|Pricing & Reference Values]] are the platform's own problems, these platforms host catalogues and estimates written by licensed auctioneers, and the price is set by a timed close.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 data science, pricing and marketplace analytics staff per platform (estimate)
**Output:** Search, recommendation and lot-category models; marketing targeting for auctioneers; free public price-results archives
**Proprietary data:** Bid-level histories and bidder behaviour across thousands of auctioneers are their own; the hammers themselves are published free (LiveAuctioneers' price-results database alone holds ~29M results back to 1999), so the price record is not a moat
**Clock:** None external; auctioneers' sale calendars
**Buyer:** Chief Product Officer / Head of Data
