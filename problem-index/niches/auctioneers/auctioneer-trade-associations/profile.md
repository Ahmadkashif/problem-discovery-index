# Auctioneer Trade Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auctioneers|Auctioneers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The National Auction Association (renamed from the National Auctioneers Association in July 2023; ~2,500 members) and the state auctioneer associations run conferences, designations such as CAI and AARE, education, lobbying on licensing and contests. The national body last published an industry-size survey for 2008.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 on research and benchmarking (estimate)
**Output:** Education, designations, occasional member surveys
**Proprietary data:** Member directories and survey responses
**Clock:** Annual conventions
**Buyer:** Chief Executive
