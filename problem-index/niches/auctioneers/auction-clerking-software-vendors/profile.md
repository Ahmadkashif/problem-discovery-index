# Auction Management & Clerking Software Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auctioneers|Auctioneers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Auction Flex (listed at $95 a month for one user on its 360 version), Wavebid, AuctionMethod, Maxanet, Bidpath and Auction Mobility sell cataloguing, clerking, invoicing, settlement and white-label bidding software to auction firms.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Small product and support teams; analytics staff under 10 per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Software subscriptions and transaction fees
**Proprietary data:** Clients' sale data held under service terms
**Clock:** None external
**Buyer:** Founder / Chief Executive
