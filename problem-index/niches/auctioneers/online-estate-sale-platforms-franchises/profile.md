# Online Estate Sale Platforms & Estate Franchise Systems

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auctioneers|Auctioneers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Online estate auction platforms such as MaxSold and Everything But The House, and franchise systems for estate sales and senior transitions, aggregate many local households into one brand, with central cataloguing standards, marketing and payment, and local operators or franchisees doing the house work.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Central pricing and analytics teams of under 10 per system (estimate)
**Output:** Platform fees and franchise royalties
**Proprietary data:** Household-level sale results across many markets
**Clock:** Sellers' move and closing dates
**Buyer:** Chief Executive / Head of Operations
