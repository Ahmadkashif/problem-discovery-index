# Personal Property Appraisal Practices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auctioneers|Auctioneers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Independent appraisers of household contents, fine and decorative art, jewellery and machinery write USPAP Standards 7 and 8 appraisals for estate tax returns, charitable donations above $5,000 on Form 8283 Section B, insurance schedules, divorce and equitable distribution. Many auctioneers hold appraiser credentials and sell appraisals alongside sales. Real property and business valuation for estates is logged as [[niches/real-estate-appraisers/estate-tax-valuation-advisory/profile|Estate, Tax & Litigation Valuation Advisory]] (40), and the manuscript and archive specialists as [[niches/libraries-and-archives/archival-appraisers/profile|Manuscript & Archive Appraisers]], which fails the gate for the same reason.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 1-5 appraisers per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Signed appraisal reports
**Proprietary data:** Personal files of appraisals and sales consulted
**Clock:** Estate tax return due dates, donation timing rules, court dates
**Buyer:** The appraiser-owner
