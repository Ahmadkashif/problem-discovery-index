# The Council Resolution That Parks the Truck for Months

**Niche:** [[niches/auctioneers/public-surplus-sellers/profile|Municipal & Public Surplus Sellers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/auctioneers|Auctioneers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Retired public assets wait for a surplus declaration on a meeting agenda, and value leaks away while they sit.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #compliance #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to become a public body's standing surplus-disposal contractor, and whoever returns the highest net proceeds with a disposal record that satisfies the auditor and the public-records request takes the account.

## The Problem
Many jurisdictions require the governing body to declare property surplus before it is sold. Items are batched for an agenda, the agenda slips, and vehicles and equipment sit in the yard for months, depreciating, sometimes vandalised or stripped for parts.

## Why It's Still Broken
Nobody owns the disposal calendar; the fleet manager waits on the clerk, who waits on enough items to make an agenda item worthwhile.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A standing surplus authority where the law allows it, or a rolling monthly surplus list drafted automatically from retired assets, with the auctioneer's listing prepared in parallel so items sell within days of approval.

## Who Feels the Pain
Fleet managers with full yards, finance directors whose proceeds shrink, and auctioneers whose sale calendars depend on public consignments.

## Impact If Fixed
Weeks to months cut from disposal time per asset (estimate), and higher proceeds.
