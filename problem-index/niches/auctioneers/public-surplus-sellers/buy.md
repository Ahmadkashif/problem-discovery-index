# Fleet Software Extended to Surplus Disposal

**Niche:** [[niches/auctioneers/public-surplus-sellers/profile|Municipal & Public Surplus Sellers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/auctioneers|Auctioneers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Public fleets already track assets in fleet management software; disposal falls out of it into email, council agendas and a marketplace upload.
**Tags:** #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #compliance #automation #large-language-models
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to become a public body's standing surplus-disposal contractor, and whoever returns the highest net proceeds with a disposal record that satisfies the auditor and the public-records request takes the account.

## The Problem
When an asset is retired, someone must take it out of service, find the title, get it declared surplus, list it, collect payment, transfer title, deposit proceeds to the right fund and close the record. Each step happens in a different place.

## What Already Exists
Fleet and asset management software used by public fleets, government auction marketplaces with listing tools, and the finance system.

## The Customization Gap
The extension needs: a disposal workflow keyed to the jurisdiction's surplus rules and approval thresholds; generation of the surplus list for the governing body's agenda; listing exported to the chosen marketplace or auctioneer with the asset's maintenance history; title transfer and buyer documentation; and proceeds posted to the correct fund with the record closed.

## Target Customer
Public bodies already running fleet software, and the auctioneers and marketplaces seeking standing contracts with them.

## Impact If Solved
Days removed from every disposal and a complete record for audit.
