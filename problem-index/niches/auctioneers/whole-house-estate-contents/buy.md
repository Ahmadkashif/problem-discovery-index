# Field-Service Scheduling Adapted to Estate Turnarounds

**Niche:** [[niches/auctioneers/whole-house-estate-contents/profile|Whole-House Estate Contents]]
**Industry:** [[industries/auctioneers|Auctioneers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Trades businesses already schedule crews with field-service software; an estate firm's schedule is set by closing dates, previews and pickup windows that those tools do not model.
**Tags:** #optimization-fundamentals #workflow-orchestration #automation #worker-facing #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to clear a whole house by the executor's closing date while netting more than an estate-sale company or a cleanout would, and whoever can lot, photograph and close a full house in the fewest crew-days without leaving value in the box lots takes the account.

## The Problem
An estate firm in spring may have five houses in progress, each at a different stage: walkthrough, sort and lot, photography, sale open, pickup, cleanout. Crews are shared, the owner auctioneer is the bottleneck, and the dates that matter are set by real estate agents and buyers' lenders.

## What Already Exists
Field-service management software used by contractors and cleaning firms (Jobber, Housecall Pro and similar) handles crew calendars, job stages and mobile check-ins.

## The Customization Gap
The estate version needs a backward schedule from the closing date through cleanout, pickup, sale close, listing and set-up; preview and pickup windows open to the public; dumpster, donation and hauling bookings; crew assignment by skill (cataloguer versus mover); and alerts when a house's lot count implies the sale date cannot hold.

## Target Customer
Estate auction firms with two or more crews.

## Impact If Solved
Fewer missed closings and double-booked crews, and an owner who stops scheduling by text message.
