# Farmland & Rural Real Estate Auctions

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auctioneers|Auctioneers]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to convince heirs or a retiring landowner that a public auction on a set date will beat a private listing, and whoever's price-per-acre call against soil productivity and recent county sales proves closest to the hammer takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300–500M a year of U.S. commission on farmland, ranch and rural property sold at auction (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of auctioneer commission and fee revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — many land auctions still run live at a community hall or fairground with phone bidders relayed by ringmen; simulcast and timed online land sales are growing
**Target Buyer:** Owner of a land auction and brokerage firm; licensed broker-auctioneer
**Automation Potential:** Medium — valuation inputs are data-rich, but the sale is a local event and the decision belongs to a family

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Farmland changes hands rarely and often at an estate settlement, when several heirs with different views must agree how to sell. The auction promises a transparent price on a known date, and multi-tract formats let neighbours bid on the pieces they want. The land auctioneer is usually also a licensed real estate broker. Census would class that brokerage side under NAICS 531210 (offices of real estate agents and brokers), and this niche includes it because the same firms run the auctions and the listings. The land auctioneer competes with brokers listing privately and with farm management companies that appraise and market land, profiled as [[niches/crop-farming/farm-management-companies/profile|Farm Management Companies]]. Price turns on tillable acres, soil productivity ratings, drainage, buildings, cash rents and who farms next door. The season is short, concentrated after harvest and before spring work (estimate), and buyers' 1031 exchange deadlines and lenders' closing timelines govern settlement.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Parcel and soil data and comparable sales from farmland data providers, profiled as [[niches/crop-farming/farmland-valuation-data/profile|Farmland Valuation Data Providers]]; county records; appraisers covered under [[niches/real-estate-appraisers/rural-and-agricultural-appraisers/profile|Rural & Agricultural Property Appraisers]]; simulcast bidding apps. Gaps: a price forecast for auction format specifically, phone and online bids recorded in one place, and a graded record of the firm's land calls.
