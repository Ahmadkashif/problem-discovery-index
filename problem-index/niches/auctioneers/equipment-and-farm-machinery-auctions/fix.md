# The Hours and Undercarriage Nobody Verified

**Niche:** [[niches/auctioneers/equipment-and-farm-machinery-auctions/profile|Equipment & Farm Machinery Auctions]]
**Industry:** [[industries/auctioneers|Auctioneers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Online equipment buyers bid on photos and an inspection report, and the report is written differently by every inspector, so bidders discount everything for the risk.
**Tags:** #cnns #object-detection #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to win a retiring farmer's or downsizing contractor's whole line-up against a promised net, and whoever's promised number holds up at the hammer, so the guarantee or no-reserve call does not lose money, takes the account.

## The Problem
A bidder three states away cannot start the engine or crawl under the dozer. They rely on the listing: meter hours, a few photos, and notes on leaks, tyres, undercarriage wear and known faults. Inspectors vary in thoroughness and vocabulary, meters get replaced, and photos skip the parts that matter. Disputes follow, and experienced bidders shade their bids on every lot from a firm whose reports they do not trust.

## Why It's Still Broken
Inspection is done by field staff under time pressure, often the same people setting up the sale. There is no shared condition scale for most equipment classes outside the largest firms' own programmes, and nothing checks a report against the photos before it goes live.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A guided inspection on a phone: a required photo set per equipment class, readings captured from the meter photo, wear measured where it can be, and a draft report in standard condition grades that the inspector confirms. Reports inconsistent with their photos are held for review.

## Who Feels the Pain
Remote bidders, inspectors blamed for disputes, and consignors whose machines sell at a discount for the firm's reputation.

## Impact If Fixed
Consistent reports narrow the discount remote bidders apply and cut post-sale disputes; the firm's history of graded reports also becomes training data for the proposal number.
