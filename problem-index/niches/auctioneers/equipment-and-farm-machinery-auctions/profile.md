# Equipment & Farm Machinery Auctions

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auctioneers|Auctioneers]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to win a retiring farmer's or downsizing contractor's whole line-up against a promised net, and whoever's promised number holds up at the hammer, so the guarantee or no-reserve call does not lose money, takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.5–2.5B a year of U.S. commission, buyer's fee and guarantee margin on construction, agricultural and transport equipment auctions (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~35% of auctioneer commission and fee revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High for bidding, Medium for valuation — timed online and simulcast sales are standard; the pre-sale number is still the auctioneer's call
**Target Buyer:** Owner or sales manager of a regional equipment and farm auction firm; territory manager at a national firm
**Automation Potential:** High for inspection reports and listings; Medium for the proposal number, which carries the firm's money when a guarantee is offered

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Equipment consignments are large, serialised and comparable. A farm retirement sale may put a few hundred lots and well over a million dollars (illustrative) through one auction, and the seller usually picks the firm on the promised net and the terms: commission, a guaranteed minimum, an outright purchase, or a no-reserve sale. RB Global (Ritchie Bros. and IronPlanet) reported $5.66B of construction and transportation GTV in 2025; Purple Wave ran 141 bi-weekly online auctions in 2025; BigIron fields hundreds of sales representatives; regional auctioneers sell through Proxibid, Equipmentfacts and AuctionTime. Prices move with commodity prices, interest rates and season, and machine-level detail (hours, undercarriage, emissions tier, options) drives value. The queued farm-equipment-dealers build covers the dealers whose trade-in offers are the alternative to auction.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Value guides and residual data (EquipmentWatch, Sandhills, Rouse within RB Global; profiled as [[niches/sales-finance-companies/equipment-valuation-guides/profile|Equipment Valuation & Residual Data Providers]]), auction platforms with past results, and the firms' own sale histories. Gaps: line-up-level forecasts with intervals, a record of each proposal number against the hammer, and inspection reports that are consistent from one inspector to the next.
