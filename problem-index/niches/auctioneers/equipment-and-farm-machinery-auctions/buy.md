# Value Guides Pointed at Whole Line-Up Proposals

**Niche:** [[niches/auctioneers/equipment-and-farm-machinery-auctions/profile|Equipment & Farm Machinery Auctions]]
**Industry:** [[industries/auctioneers|Auctioneers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Equipment value guides answer "what is this machine worth"; the auctioneer's question is "what will this line-up bring at my sale, in my region, in March".
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #feature-engineering #time-series-forecasting #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to win a retiring farmer's or downsizing contractor's whole line-up against a promised net, and whoever's promised number holds up at the hammer, so the guarantee or no-reserve call does not lose money, takes the account.

## The Problem
Auctioneers already pay for value data, and lenders and dealers use the same numbers. But the guide value for a single machine is not the price that machine brings in a particular sale, with a particular set of other lots, under a particular format and closing day, in a region with its own buyer base.

## What Already Exists
EquipmentWatch, Sandhills value products, RB Global's Rouse values and appraisals, and auction platforms' searchable past results. Lenders' appraisal products cover collateral value, as described under [[niches/sales-finance-companies/equipment-valuation-guides/profile|Equipment Valuation & Residual Data Providers]].

## The Customization Gap
The auctioneer needs: regional and seasonal adjustments learned from their own sales; format effects (no-reserve versus reserve, timed versus simulcast, lot order, closing hour); line-up effects, where a complete matched set of farm equipment brings more than the sum of its parts or crowds the same buyers; condition adjustments from inspection photos and hours; and an output a seller can read, a range with comparables rather than a single number.

## Target Customer
Regional equipment and farm auctioneers already licensing a value guide.

## Impact If Solved
Proposals backed by evidence rather than recall, and fewer lost consignments to firms that simply promised more.
