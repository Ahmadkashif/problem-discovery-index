# A Guarantee Desk That Knows Its Own Error

**Niche:** [[niches/auctioneers/equipment-and-farm-machinery-auctions/profile|Equipment & Farm Machinery Auctions]]
**Industry:** [[industries/auctioneers|Auctioneers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Equipment auctioneers price guarantees and no-reserve calls on a line-up from memory and value guides, and never measure how far their proposal numbers miss.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #monte-carlo-methods #confidence-intervals #time-series-forecasting #evaluation-metrics #tacit-knowledge-ml #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to win a retiring farmer's or downsizing contractor's whole line-up against a promised net, and whoever's promised number holds up at the hammer, so the guarantee or no-reserve call does not lose money, takes the account.

## The Problem
A farmer retiring after harvest invites three firms. Each walks the line, writes down models and hours, checks a value guide on a phone, and calls a number for the whole sale. The firm that wins often wins because it promised more or offered a guaranteed minimum. If commodity prices fall before the March sale, or the line-up's planter is a model the region no longer wants, the guarantee loses money. The firm learns this one sale at a time and never sees, across hundreds of sales, whether its proposal numbers run high, which territory managers estimate well, or which categories it misjudges.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Value guides sell machine-level values, not sale-level forecasts with intervals; platforms sell bidding, not underwriting. Regional firms have the history but no analyst, and the estimate given at the proposal is rarely recorded at lot level, so the join between proposal and hammer does not exist.

## What to Build
A proposal tool that captures each machine's proposed value at the walkthrough, forecasts each lot's hammer distribution from the firm's own results, public results and price indices projected to the sale date, and simulates the sale total with correlated market moves. It prices guarantees from the tail, not the mean, and reports every territory manager's proposal error by category and season after each sale.

## Target Customer
Owners and sales managers of regional equipment and farm auction firms that offer guarantees, and underwriting teams at the national firms.

## Impact If Built
Fewer losing guarantees in weak markets, proposals that win consignments without overpromising, and a measured record of who estimates well, worth a share of the margin on every guaranteed sale (estimate).
