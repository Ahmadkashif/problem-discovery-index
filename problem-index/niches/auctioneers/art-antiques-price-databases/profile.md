# Art & Antiques Price Databases

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auctioneers|Auctioneers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
The Artnet Price Database (~18M fine art, design and decorative art results), Artprice, MutualArt and askART compile auction results from houses worldwide. They sell subscriptions to specialists, dealers, appraisers, insurers, collectors and lenders, and publish market indices and reports on top. Broader collectibles price research by WorthPoint is already logged as [[niches/pawnshops/sold-price-aggregators/profile|Secondhand Sold-Price Aggregators]] (28).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Most staff enter results and edit records, which is production work. The analytics units that publish indices and reports, such as Artprice's econometrics department, give no headcount and are likely under 10 per firm (estimate).
**Output:** Searchable price records, artist indices and market reports
**Proprietary data:** Decades of compiled auction results with images. The inputs are published hammer prices, which the houses and free archives also display.
**Clock:** None external; the houses' sale calendars
**Buyer:** Head of Data / Editor of market analytics
