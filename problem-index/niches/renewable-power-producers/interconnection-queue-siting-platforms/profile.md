# Interconnection Queue & Siting Intelligence Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/renewable-power-producers|Renewable Power Producers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Platforms that help developers find sites and points of interconnection and track queue positions: Paces (siting, interconnection and permitting diligence with in-house transmission planning and power markets staff, Brooklyn), Interconnection.fyi (which led data collection for LBNL's Queued Up 2026), Grid Status, Nira Energy, LandGate and Transect (named from recall where not checked). Developers pay subscriptions and, at Paces, for diligence on specific projects.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 power engineers, analysts and data staff per platform (estimate; Paces lists transmission planning and power markets roles on its own board)
**Output:** Point-of-interconnection screens, queue trackers, capacity and congestion maps, siting and permitting reports
**Proprietary data:** Cleaned and linked public queue files, hosting and capacity estimates, land and permitting layers; little outcome data of their own
**Clock:** ISO cluster request windows (45 days under Order 2023), which bind the developers using the screens rather than the platforms
**Buyer:** Chief Product Officer or Head of Power Engineering
