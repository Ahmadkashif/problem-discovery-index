# Tax-Credit Qualification Records

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/renewable-power-producers|Renewable Power Producers]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to evidence every credit dollar that a buyer's diligence and the IRS will test, and whoever has the smallest share of credit value held back or excluded at transfer closing takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600M a year of prevailing wage and apprenticeship compliance, begin-construction and domestic-content advisory, and transfer diligence spend (estimate), protecting $47.5–49B of forecast 2026 transfers (Crux)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% of wind and solar energy revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — certified payrolls arrive as PDFs and scans from dozens of subcontractors; evidence is assembled in data rooms at sale
**Target Buyer:** Vice President of Tax or Head of Project Finance at an IPP or developer
**Automation Potential:** High for extraction and rule checks; judgement calls on classification and good-faith efforts stay with tax advisers

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Most wind, solar and storage credits are now sold rather than used by the owner, and a buyer pays only for credits it can defend. The evidence spans construction and operations: prevailing wages for every laborer and mechanic, apprentice labour-hour shares and ratios with good-faith request records, begin-construction proof under Notice 2025-42's physical work test with a four-year continuity window, domestic content cost shares if the bonus is claimed, and foreign-entity material assistance ratios for construction beginning from 2026. OBBBA's placed-in-service deadline of 31 December 2027 for wind and solar that began construction after 4 July 2026 makes the begin-construction file the most scrutinised document a project owns. Competitors are Big Four and boutique tax advisers, certified payroll platforms, transfer marketplaces' diligence teams (Crux, Reunion) and tax insurers.

## Current Tools & Gaps
LCPtracker-style certified payroll systems, data rooms, adviser checklists and spreadsheets. Davis-Bacon payroll compliance on federal jobs is already covered at [[niches/general-contractors/government-public-works/profile|🟠 Government & Public Works Contractors]] and the wage determinations themselves at [[niches/heavy-civil-contractors/davis-bacon-wage-determinations/profile|🔍 Davis-Bacon Wage Surveys & Determinations]] (19); what credit sales add is apprenticeship labour hours, begin-construction evidence and foreign-entity (FEOC) evidence, all tested at transfer diligence rather than by a contracting officer. Gaps: apprenticeship percentages are computed after construction instead of during it, begin-construction evidence is reconstructed from invoices at sale, and nothing ties each figure in the diligence binder to its source page.
