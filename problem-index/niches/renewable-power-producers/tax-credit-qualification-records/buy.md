# Davis-Bacon Payroll Compliance for Credit Buyers

**Niche:** [[niches/renewable-power-producers/tax-credit-qualification-records/profile|Tax-Credit Qualification Records]]
**Industry:** [[industries/renewable-power-producers|Renewable Power Producers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Certified payroll platforms already collect weekly payrolls and check wages on public works; renewable credit sales need the same collection extended to apprenticeship hours, post-construction repairs and a buyer's diligence format.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #descriptive-statistics #compliance #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to evidence every credit dollar that a buyer's diligence and the IRS will test, and whoever has the smallest share of credit value held back or excluded at transfer closing takes the account.

## The Problem
Developers adopt public-works payroll tools for prevailing wage and still assemble everything else by hand.

## What Already Exists
LCPtracker, Elations and similar platforms collect certified payrolls from contractors on federally funded projects, validate rates against Davis-Bacon wage determinations and produce compliance reports for the contracting agency. The public-works side of that workflow is profiled at [[niches/general-contractors/government-public-works/profile|🟠 Government & Public Works Contractors]], and the determinations are published by the Department of Labor, logged at [[niches/heavy-civil-contractors/davis-bacon-wage-determinations/profile|🔍 Davis-Bacon Wage Surveys & Determinations]].

## The Customization Gap
Prevailing wage is the part that carries over. The difference is apprenticeship, begin-construction and foreign-entity (FEOC) evidence that a public-works tool never collects, all tested at transfer diligence rather than by a contracting officer. The adaptation needs: apprentice labour-hour shares computed per facility across all contractors with ratio tests; good-faith request logs from registered programmes; correction and penalty calculations; payroll collection for alteration and repair work for years after placed-in-service; begin-construction and foreign-entity evidence alongside payroll; and output by credit tranche for the buyer, its counsel and its tax insurer rather than for a government agency.

## Target Customer
Developers already running a certified payroll platform on their construction contractors.

## Impact If Solved
Extending a tool contractors already use avoids a second payroll collection on ~40 subcontractors per project (estimate) and cuts ~6 weeks from transfer diligence.
