**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Credit evidence is assembled when the credits are sold, a year or more after the payrolls, letters and invoices it depends on were created, so gaps are found when they are most expensive to fix.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to evidence every credit dollar that a buyer's diligence and the IRS will test, and whoever has the smallest share of credit value held back or excluded at transfer closing takes the account.

## The Problem
A shortfall in apprentice hours or an underpaid craft found during construction costs a correction payment; found at sale, it costs an escrow, a price cut or an insurance exclusion. Most projects find it at sale.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Payroll tools serve contractors; tax advisers bill for reviews at sale; and the rules (begin-construction, foreign-entity ratios) changed twice in 2025, so nobody built for a moving target.

## What to Build
A ledger that ingests every subcontractor's weekly certified payroll as it arrives, extracts worker-level hours, rates and crafts, checks them against the wage determination and apprenticeship ratios, chases missing payrolls and good-faith request letters, tracks physical-work and continuity evidence and foreign-entity cost shares, and produces at any time a diligence binder in the buyer's format with every figure linked to its source.

## Target Customer
Tax and project finance heads at developers selling credits on 10+ projects a year.

## Impact If Built
Cutting escrow holdbacks from ~5% to ~1% of credit value (estimate) on $200M of credits sold a year releases ~$8M of cash at closing.
