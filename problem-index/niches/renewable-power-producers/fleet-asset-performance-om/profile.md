# Fleet Asset Performance & O&M

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/renewable-power-producers|Renewable Power Producers]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is "every serious competitor is fighting to recover the most lost energy per dollar of operations and maintenance", but wind and solar lose energy in different ways, to different vendors, on different data: wind is won on avoiding crane-dependent drivetrain failures, solar on clearing the many small inverter, tracker and string losses. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$9B a year of O&M spend across ~150–160 GW of wind and ~140–160 GW of utility-scale solar (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~26% of wind and solar energy revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High for monitoring — every plant streams SCADA — and Low for the maintenance decision, which runs on work orders, OEM reports and experience
**Target Buyer:** Vice President of Asset Performance or Head of Operations at an IPP
**Automation Potential:** Medium — detection and loss accounting automate; climbs, truck rolls and repairs stay physical

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
O&M is the largest controllable cost after a plant is built, and lost energy is the largest controllable revenue item. The fleet owner pays OEMs under long-term service agreements, independent service providers or its own technicians, and judges them by availability, which each contract defines differently. A remote operations centre watches everything; few owners can say, by cause, how many megawatt-hours each plant lost last month.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Asset performance platforms (Power Factors, Bazefield, Clir Renewables), OEM monitoring centres (GE Vernova, Vestas, Siemens Gamesa), condition monitoring (ONYX Insight), drone inspection (SkySpecs) and computerised maintenance systems (SAP, Maximo). Solar fleet monitoring as a vendor field is profiled at [[niches/solar-installers/solar-asset-performance-monitoring/profile|🔍 Solar Asset Management & Performance Monitoring]]. Gaps: lost energy is categorised differently by each OEM and contract, so fleet-wide loss accounting is rebuilt in spreadsheets, and the maintenance decision is not joined to its outcome.
