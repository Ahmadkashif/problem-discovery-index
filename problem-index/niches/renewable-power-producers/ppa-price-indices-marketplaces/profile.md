# PPA Price Indices & Offtake Marketplaces

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/renewable-power-producers|Renewable Power Producers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Platforms that run competitive PPA procurements between corporate buyers and developers and publish price indices from the offers they see. LevelTen Energy's marketplace and quarterly PPA Price Index are the reference: its Q2 2026 North American solar index fell 4.8% quarter on quarter to $61.40/MWh, and LevelTen tied the outlook to the 4 July 2026 tax-credit cutoff. Pexapark in Europe and procurement advisers that publish deal benchmarks work the same way. Buyers and developers pay transaction and subscription fees; the index is both a product and marketing.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 market analysts and data staff per platform (estimate)
**Output:** Quarterly PPA price indices by market and technology, procurement analytics, offer benchmarking for buyers and sellers
**Proprietary data:** Thousands of developer offers and buyer selections submitted through the marketplace over several years
**Clock:** The platform's own quarterly index calendar; buyers' procurement deadlines are client dates
**Buyer:** Head of Market Intelligence or Chief Product Officer
