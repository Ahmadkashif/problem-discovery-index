# Independent Wind & Solar O&M Service Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/renewable-power-producers|Renewable Power Producers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Independent service providers that run wind and solar plants for owners in place of, or after, the OEM: scheduled maintenance, fault response, major component exchanges, vegetation and module cleaning, and monthly reporting. National firms and many regional contractors compete (named from recall); owners pay fixed fees per turbine or per megawatt plus time and materials.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Technicians and site managers working site by site (production); performance engineering groups under 10 per provider (estimate)
**Output:** Maintenance performed, work orders, monthly availability and performance reports
**Proprietary data:** Work-order and parts histories across many owners' plants
**Clock:** Contract availability guarantees and maintenance schedules, client dates
**Buyer:** Vice President of Operations
