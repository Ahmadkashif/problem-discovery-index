# Power Price, Basis & Curtailment Forecast Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/renewable-power-producers|Renewable Power Producers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation firms

## What They Do
Firms that model wholesale power markets and sell long-term price curves, node-to-hub basis forecasts, curtailment estimates and project revenue reports to developers, IPPs, lenders and tax equity investors. Aurora Energy Research, Ascend Analytics, Wood Mackenzie, Enverus, Hitachi Energy's market analytics, ICF and smaller modelling shops are representative (named from recall). Lenders financing merchant, hedged or hybrid projects typically require a revenue report from a recognised curve provider before financial close, and IPPs buy curves for budgets and bids.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–120 market modellers and analysts per firm's North American power team (estimate)
**Output:** Quarterly price curves by hub and zone, nodal basis and curtailment forecasts, bespoke project revenue reports for financings
**Proprietary data:** Fundamental market models and the firm's archive of past curve vintages; the realised prices and curtailment that would grade them are public ISO data
**Clock:** Two halves. Curve subscriptions, the main product, run on each firm's own quarterly update calendar (Q4 2). Bespoke revenue reports are a condition precedent to financing closes and tax equity fundings, which are clients' deal dates that slip (Q4 4 at most, the load-forecaster analogue). Blended, Q4 3
**Buyer:** Head of North American Power Research
