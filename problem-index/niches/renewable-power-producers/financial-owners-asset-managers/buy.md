# Real Estate Fund Asset Reporting for Power Plants

**Niche:** [[niches/renewable-power-producers/financial-owners-asset-managers/profile|Financial Owners & Third-Party Asset Managers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/renewable-power-producers|Renewable Power Producers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Real estate funds already collect property managers' monthly operating data into one standard model and benchmark them; a fund owning power plants through several operators needs the same, with weather and power prices in place of tenants.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #feature-engineering #data-integration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to explain each month's production and revenue variance against the budget, and whoever leaves the smallest unexplained share of that variance takes the account.

## The Problem
Each operator reports in its own template, so the owner's analysts rekey operating data before they can compare anything.

## What Already Exists
Real estate investment management and investor reporting platforms collect property-level operating statements from third-party property managers in a standard chart of accounts, reconcile them, and benchmark properties and managers across a fund.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: a standard operating data model for generation (energy, availability, curtailment, prices, certificates) instead of rent rolls; resource normalisation against an independent weather benchmark; contract terms (PPAs, hedges, guarantees) as the equivalent of leases; and lender covenant tests such as debt service coverage computed from the same data.

## Target Customer
Energy asset management teams at funds already using real estate or infrastructure reporting software.

## Impact If Solved
Standardised monthly intake replaces ~3 analyst days of rekeying per operator per month (estimate), ~70 days a year for an owner with two operators.
