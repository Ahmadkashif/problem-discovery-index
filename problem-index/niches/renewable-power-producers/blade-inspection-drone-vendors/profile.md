# Blade Inspection Drone & Robotics Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/renewable-power-producers|Renewable Power Producers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Firms that fly drones or deploy crawling robots to photograph and inspect turbine blades, classify leading-edge erosion, cracks and lightning damage, and recommend repairs; SkySpecs and Aerones are representative (named from recall). Owners pay per turbine inspected or per campaign, usually before warranty expiry or after storms.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Image reviewers and blade engineers grading damage case by case, which the rubric counts as inspection production; analytics teams under 10 per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Per-blade damage reports with severity grades and repair recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Large libraries of graded blade images across makes and ages
**Clock:** End-of-warranty inspections and post-storm campaigns, set by owners' contracts
**Buyer:** Head of Inspection Services
