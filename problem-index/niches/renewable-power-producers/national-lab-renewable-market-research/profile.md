# National Laboratory Renewable Market & Queue Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/renewable-power-producers|Renewable Power Producers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory's Electricity Markets & Policy group publishes the Queued Up interconnection dataset (2026 edition with Interconnection.fyi), annual wind and utility-scale solar market reports and cost and price benchmarks, funded by the Department of Energy and released free with full data files. Other national laboratories publish resource data and cost models on the same basis.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~15–40 researchers across the relevant programmes (estimate)
**Output:** Free annual reports and datasets: queue status and timelines, project costs, performance and PPA prices
**Proprietary data:** Assembled queue histories from seven ISOs and dozens of non-ISO areas, partly filled with non-public data supplied by transmission providers
**Clock:** Self-set annual publication cycle
**Buyer:** Programme lead funded by DOE
