# Battery Storage Revenue Benchmarks & Market Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/renewable-power-producers|Renewable Power Producers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Research subscriptions that estimate what batteries actually earn, market by market and asset by asset, and forecast storage revenues. Modo Energy is the clearest example: its ERCOT battery index and monthly benchmark reports rank individual assets and optimisers by revenue and capture rate, and it reported 2025 ERCOT earnings to the end of November at ~$26/kW against $54.1/kW a year earlier (search summary). Owners, optimisers, lenders and investors subscribe.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 market analysts per firm's US team (estimate; Modo lists US market analyst roles in New York on its own board, 2026-10-05)
**Output:** Monthly revenue benchmarks by asset and optimiser, market research notes, revenue forecasts
**Proprietary data:** Asset-level revenue estimates reconstructed from grid operators' public disclosure data, and the benchmark history
**Clock:** The firm's own monthly publication calendar; grid operators release the disclosure data on their own schedules
**Buyer:** Head of Research
