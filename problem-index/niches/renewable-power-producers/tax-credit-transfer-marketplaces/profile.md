# Tax-Credit Transfer Marketplaces & Market Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/renewable-power-producers|Renewable Power Producers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Platforms that match sellers of clean energy credits with corporate buyers, run diligence and close transfers, and publish market reports on volumes and pricing. Crux reported $21B of transfers in the first half of 2026 and forecasts $47.5–49B for the year; Reunion and Basis Climate work alongside syndicators and advisers (named from recall). Fees are paid per transaction.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research staff per platform (estimate; Crux lists a Research & Intelligence business development role and one data engineer on its own board), with structurers and coverage bankers working deal by deal, which the rubric counts as production
**Output:** Closed transfers; semi-annual market intelligence reports
**Proprietary data:** Pricing and terms of deals run through the platform, plus market surveys
**Clock:** Buyers' tax payment dates and sellers' financing closes, both client dates
**Buyer:** Head of Research & Intelligence
