# Trading-Desk ETRM for a Two-Person Commercial Team

**Niche:** [[niches/renewable-power-producers/ppa-settlement-rec-delivery/profile|PPA Settlement, Curtailment Claims & Certificate Delivery]]
**Industry:** [[industries/renewable-power-producers|Renewable Power Producers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Energy trading and risk management systems already settle financial power contracts at scale; IPPs need them cut down to the PPA clauses, ISO resettlements and certificate deliveries a small commercial team actually handles.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to settle each contract month without a dispute, and whoever has the smallest share of invoiced settlement value disputed or restated takes the account.

## The Problem
IPPs that license an ETRM often still settle PPAs in Excel because the system's instrument model does not fit the contracts.

## What Already Exists
Allegro, ION's Endur and similar systems book, value and settle physical and financial power, gas and environmental trades for utilities and trading houses, with ISO interfaces and invoicing.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: templates for corporate virtual PPA structures (as-generated, shaped, hub or node settled); negative-price floors and curtailment clauses as configurable rules; each ISO's resettlement calendar; certificate delivery obligations linked to registry issuance; buyer-facing invoice formats; and configuration a commercial manager can do without a trading-systems team.

## Target Customer
IPP commercial teams too small for a full ETRM implementation but past 10 contracts.

## Impact If Solved
A configured template set could replace ~3 analyst days per contract per month (estimate), ~2 full-time staff for a 25-contract portfolio.
