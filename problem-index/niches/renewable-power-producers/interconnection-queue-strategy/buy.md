# Drug-Pipeline Risk-Adjusted Valuation for Queue Positions

**Niche:** [[niches/renewable-power-producers/interconnection-queue-strategy/profile|Interconnection Queue Strategy & Development Pipeline]]
**Industry:** [[industries/renewable-power-producers|Renewable Power Producers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Drug developers value every pipeline asset by its odds of passing each trial phase; a renewable developer's pipeline is the same staged bet, valued instead by headline megawatts.
**Tags:** #survival-analysis #bayesian-inference #monte-carlo-methods #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to put money only behind queue positions that will survive their network-upgrade assignments, and whoever has the lowest share of queued megawatts withdrawn after the first cluster study takes the account.

## The Problem
Development pipelines are reported to boards and buyers as gigawatts in the queue, which says nothing about how many will be built. Deposits rise at each study stage, so capital allocation needs a value per position that reflects its odds at each gate.

## What Already Exists
Pharmaceutical portfolio teams use risk-adjusted net present value: each asset's cash flows are weighted by phase-transition probabilities taken from industry benchmarks, and portfolio tools simulate the pipeline under those odds.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: stage gates defined by each ISO's cluster process (request, first study, restudy, facilities study, agreement) instead of trial phases; transition odds learned from queue history by grid operator, technology and substation crowding rather than from published trial benchmarks; deposits and withdrawal penalties as the cost at each gate; upgrade-cost reallocation when neighbours drop out; and tax-credit deadlines that make timing, not just survival, decide value.

## Target Customer
Chief investment officers and heads of development finance at IPPs, and buyers of development portfolios.

## Impact If Solved
Valuing a 10 GW pipeline by gate-weighted odds rather than headline megawatts could move ~$10–20M of annual deposits from positions with under 10% odds to positions over 40% (estimate).
