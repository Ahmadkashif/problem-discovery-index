# Interconnection Queue Strategy & Development Pipeline

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/renewable-power-producers|Renewable Power Producers]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to put money only behind queue positions that will survive their network-upgrade assignments, and whoever has the lowest share of queued megawatts withdrawn after the first cluster study takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2B a year of interconnection study deposits, transmission planning consultancy, queue data and development staff time spent on positions (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~6% of wind and solar energy revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — queue files and power-flow tools are digital, but the keep-or-drop decision runs on spreadsheets, PDFs and the interconnection lead's memory
**Target Buyer:** Vice President of Interconnection or Head of Development at an IPP or developer
**Automation Potential:** High for screening and study parsing; the deposit decision stays with a person

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is the generator side of interconnection. The utility's side, deciding what a feeder or substation can take, is profiled under [[niches/electric-utilities/interconnection-and-large-load-planning/profile|🔵 Interconnection & Large-Load Planning]] and [[niches/electric-utilities/der-interconnection-hosting-capacity/profile|🎯 DER Interconnection & Hosting Capacity]]; the studies themselves are [[niches/solar-installers/interconnection-grid-studies/profile|🔍 Interconnection & Grid Impact Studies]]. Here the buyer is the developer holding positions in ISO clusters: 2,061 GW were queued at the end of 2025, over 750 GW withdrew that year, and the median 2025 build took 61 months (LBNL). Since FERC Order No. 2023, each stage needs a larger deposit, so a position dropped late burns the most money.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Paces, Enverus's interconnection modelling (ex-Pearl Street), Nira Energy, Interconnection.fyi, Grid Status and LandGate screen points of interconnection and track queues; consultancies run power-flow studies; the LBNL dataset is free. The gaps: no tool scores a position's survival odds through its remaining studies, study results arrive as PDFs retyped by hand, and the developer's own estimate at each deposit is never recorded or graded.
