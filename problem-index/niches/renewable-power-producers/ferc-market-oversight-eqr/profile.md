# FERC Wholesale Market Oversight & Electric Quarterly Reports

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/renewable-power-producers|Renewable Power Producers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission grants independent power producers market-based rate authority, collects Electric Quarterly Reports of their wholesale sales, approves the interconnection rules in Order No. 2023, and runs market surveillance and enforcement through its Office of Enforcement. Generators also answer to the grid operators' market monitors, which belong to the queued Transmission & Grid Operators industry.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~30–100 analysts and economists in market oversight and surveillance (estimate)
**Output:** Market assessments, enforcement investigations, the public EQR dataset, rulemakings
**Proprietary data:** Non-public market and bid data received from grid operators for surveillance, plus the public EQR filings
**Clock:** FERC's own reporting calendar for EQRs (due 30 days after each quarter, recalled) and its own rulemaking schedule
**Buyer:** Director of the Office of Enforcement's analytics and surveillance division
