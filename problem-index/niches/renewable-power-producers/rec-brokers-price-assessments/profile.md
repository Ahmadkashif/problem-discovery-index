# REC & Environmental Attribute Brokers and Price Assessments

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/renewable-power-producers|Renewable Power Producers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Environmental commodity brokers that arrange trades in state compliance RECs, solar RECs and voluntary certificates between generators, utilities, retailers and corporate buyers, and the price reporting agencies' small desks that assess REC prices (Karbone, Evolution Markets and the environmental desks of S&P Global and Argus are representative; named from recall). Brokers earn commissions; agencies sell assessments.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Brokers working trade by trade (production) and assessment desks under 10 reporters per agency for RECs (estimate)
**Output:** Brokered trades, broker price sheets, daily or weekly REC assessments
**Proprietary data:** Broker blotters and agencies' assessment histories
**Clock:** State compliance-year true-ups for obligated buyers, client dates; assessment calendars are each agency's own
**Buyer:** Head of Environmental Markets
