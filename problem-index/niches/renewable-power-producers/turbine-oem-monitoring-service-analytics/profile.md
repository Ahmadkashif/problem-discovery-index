# Turbine OEM Remote Monitoring & Service Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/renewable-power-producers|Renewable Power Producers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The turbine makers' remote monitoring and performance engineering centres (GE Vernova, Vestas, Siemens Gamesa, Nordex; named from recall) watch tens of thousands of turbines under long-term service agreements, diagnose faults, plan component exchanges and sell software and upgrades that raise output. Owners pay through service agreements priced per turbine with availability guarantees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~50–300 performance engineers and data scientists per OEM's US service analytics (estimate); control-room operators are production and not counted
**Output:** Fault diagnostics, availability reporting under the service agreement, component exchange planning, performance upgrade proposals
**Proprietary data:** Design data and high-resolution operating history across each OEM's installed fleet
**Clock:** Availability measurement periods and guarantee true-ups in each service agreement, which are contract dates
**Buyer:** Head of Fleet Analytics or Service Engineering
