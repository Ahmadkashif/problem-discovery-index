# Tax Equity Investors, Credit Buyers & Project Lenders

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/renewable-power-producers|Renewable Power Producers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Banks that invest tax equity in partnership flips, corporations that buy transferred credits, and project finance lenders that fund construction and term loans for wind, solar and storage. Large US banks dominate tax equity; Japanese, European and US banks lead project debt (named from recall). They underwrite each project from the sponsor's data room, the independent engineer's report and a price curve.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Underwriting teams of deal bankers, credit officers and tax counsel working project by project (per-deal staff are production); portfolio research groups under 10 per institution (estimate)
**Output:** Investment and credit decisions per project
**Proprietary data:** Portfolio performance of financed projects
**Clock:** Financing closes and tax payment dates, client dates
**Buyer:** Head of Renewable Energy Finance
