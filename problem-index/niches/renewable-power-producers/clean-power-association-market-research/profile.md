# Clean Power Trade Association Market Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/renewable-power-producers|Renewable Power Producers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The American Clean Power Association's market intelligence team publishes quarterly and annual market reports on utility-scale wind, solar and storage and sells a project database subscription (CleanPowerIQ) to members and others; storage and state associations publish narrower reports (named from recall). The research supports advocacy and is a member benefit as much as a product.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–25 market analysts (estimate)
**Output:** Quarterly and annual market reports, project database subscriptions, policy briefs
**Proprietary data:** A project database compiled from public filings, interconnection data and press, plus project details reported by members
**Clock:** The association's own quarterly calendar and legislative sessions
**Buyer:** Head of Market Intelligence
