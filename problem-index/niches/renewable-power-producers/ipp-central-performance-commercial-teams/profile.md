# IPP & Yieldco Central Performance and Commercial Analytics Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/renewable-power-producers|Renewable Power Producers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The central analytics groups at the largest owners — NextEra Energy Resources, AES, Brookfield Renewable, Clearway, Invenergy, EDP Renewables, Ørsted and similar (named from recall) — forecast fleet output and revenue, benchmark plants and operators, price PPAs and acquisitions, and plan development portfolios. In wind, 28 firms with 500+ employees took ~85% of 2022 receipts (Census SUSB), so a few dozen such teams see most of the operating fleet.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–60 performance, energy-assessment and commercial analysts per large IPP (estimate)
**Output:** Internal budgets, reforecasts, acquisition and PPA pricing models, fleet benchmarking
**Proprietary data:** The owner's own interval data, outages, contracts and settlement history across hundreds of plants
**Clock:** Quarterly and annual results for listed owners and yieldcos (an issuer's own filing dates, capped at Q4 3), plus internal budget cycles
**Buyer:** Vice President of Asset Performance or Chief Commercial Officer
