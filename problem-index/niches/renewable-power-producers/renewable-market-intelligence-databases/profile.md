# Renewable Market Intelligence & Project Databases

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/renewable-power-producers|Renewable Power Producers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Research firms that track every utility-scale wind, solar and storage project from announcement to operation, benchmark capital costs, equipment prices and PPA activity, and sell market outlooks to developers, IPPs, OEMs, utilities and investors. Wood Mackenzie's power and renewables research, BloombergNEF, S&P Global Commodity Insights and Rystad Energy are representative (named from recall). Subscriptions and custom studies are the invoice.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~30–150 renewables analysts per firm's North American team (estimate)
**Output:** Project pipeline databases, quarterly market outlooks, cost and price benchmarks, supply-chain and OEM market share reports
**Proprietary data:** Project-level pipeline histories assembled from filings, press, interconnection data and interviews, plus cost surveys of developers and suppliers
**Clock:** Each firm's own quarterly and annual publication calendar
**Buyer:** Head of Power & Renewables Research
