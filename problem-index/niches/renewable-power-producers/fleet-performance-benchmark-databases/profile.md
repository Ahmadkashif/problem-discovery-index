# Wind & Solar Fleet Performance Benchmarking Databases

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/renewable-power-producers|Renewable Power Producers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms that pool operating data from many owners' plants to benchmark how wind and solar assets actually perform against their pre-construction estimates, and sell benchmarks, risk reports and, in some cases, production insurance built on them. kWh Analytics (solar generation data behind its insurance products and annual risk report) and reliability databases for wind such as Strategic Power Systems' ORAP are representative (named from recall).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 analysts and data scientists per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Performance benchmarks, annual risk and underperformance reports, insurance pricing inputs, component reliability statistics
**Proprietary data:** Interval generation and outage data contributed by owners and insureds across many plants
**Clock:** The firm's own report calendar; insurance renewals are clients' dates
**Buyer:** Head of Data Science or Chief Risk Officer

**Kill switches:** split by data regime. Participant reliability programmes (ORAP-style databases where owners contribute data under participation terms for benchmarking among participants): live, the contributed-data rule the vault applies to member pools. Vendor and insurer databases (kWh Analytics-style): none — the firm publishes risk reports and benchmarks built from the pooled data, which shows vendor product rights (terms unread). The vendor half is the larger commercial product and carries the verdict.
