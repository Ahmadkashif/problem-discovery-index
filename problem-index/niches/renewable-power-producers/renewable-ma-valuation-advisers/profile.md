**Parent Industry:** [[industries/renewable-power-producers|Renewable Power Producers]]

## What They Do
Boutique banks that sell development portfolios and operating plants (Marathon Capital, CohnReznick Capital and similar), valuation firms that prepare fair-market-value appraisals supporting a project's credit basis, and fairness and purchase-price allocation work (named from recall). Fees are success fees or per engagement.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Deal teams and appraisers working engagement by engagement (production); research staff under 10 per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Sale processes, valuation and basis appraisal reports
**Proprietary data:** Transaction comparables and appraisal files, built on clients' confidential information
**Clock:** Sale timetables and credit sale closings set by clients
**Buyer:** Managing director of the energy practice
