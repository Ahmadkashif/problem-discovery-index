# The Part Is Still in the Warehouse

**Niche:** [[niches/renewable-power-producers/the-wind-technician/profile|The Wind Technician]]
**Industry:** [[industries/renewable-power-producers|Renewable Power Producers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Crews load the truck from habit and the ticket's fault code, so the slip ring, fan or sensor the job needed is often back in the site warehouse when they reach the nacelle.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #naive-bayes #feature-engineering #worker-facing #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a stopped turbine running again on the first climb, and whoever has the highest first-time fix rate on technician callouts takes the account.

## The Problem
Each wasted trip means a climb down, a drive to the warehouse and usually another climb the next day, while the turbine stays down.

## Why It's Still Broken
Parts consumption by fault code is never analysed because work orders do not record which part fixed which code, and truck stock is set by experience.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A suggested kit for every ticket from the parts used on past tickets with the same code and model, plus a truck-stock list for the day's route that covers the most likely needs, checked off by the crew before leaving.

## Who Feels the Pain
Technicians making repeat climbs, site managers missing availability targets, and warehouse staff fielding urgent calls.

## Impact If Fixed
If ~1 in 5 of the ~3,000 repeat visits a year on a 1,000-turbine fleet is caused by a missing part (estimate), kits that prevent half of those remove ~300 repeat climbs a year.
