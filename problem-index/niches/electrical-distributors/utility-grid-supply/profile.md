# Utility & Grid Supply

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electrical-distributors|Electrical Distributors]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a utility's or co-op's transformers, cable, meters and line hardware on the shelf under an alliance agreement through storms and multi-year lead times, and whoever posts the highest fill rate when the storm hits takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$18B a year of distributor sales to investor-owned, cooperative and municipal utilities and their line contractors (estimate; Wesco's Utility & Broadband Solutions segment alone sold ~$5.45B in 2025, broadband included)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of 423610 receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — alliance contracts run on EDI and vendor-managed inventory at the big utilities; co-ops and municipals still order by phone and email
**Target Buyer:** VP of utility sales at the distributor; utility supply-chain and materials managers as the customer
**Automation Potential:** Medium — forecasting and replenishment automate; allocation during storms is judgement

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Utilities buy distribution transformers, conductor, poles hardware, meters, connectors and storm stock under multi-year alliance agreements that commit the distributor to fill rates, stocking locations and storm response. The contest is availability, not price: Wood Mackenzie estimated a 6% supply deficit for distribution transformers in August 2025, with unit costs up 78–95% since 2019 and pad-mount lead times still rising, and a hurricane or ice storm can consume months of stock in days. Wesco, Border States, Graybar and specialist utility distributors compete for the alliances. The utilities themselves are [[industries/electric-utilities|Electric Distribution Utilities]], whose storm side is [[niches/electric-utilities/storm-restoration-mutual-assistance/profile|Storm Restoration & Mutual Assistance]] and whose new-service work is [[niches/electric-utilities/new-service-connection-design/profile|New Service Connection Design & Estimating]]; line construction is [[industries/utility-contractors|Utility Contractors]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Utility ERPs and materials systems (SAP, Oracle), distributor vendor-managed inventory, EDI, storm-stock agreements and manual allocation during events. The gaps: storm demand forecast by intuition, stock split between utilities without a shared view, and long-lead transformer orders placed without a demand signal from the utility's own work plan.
