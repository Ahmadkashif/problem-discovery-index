# Small Independent Electrical Distributors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electrical-distributors|Electrical Distributors]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to cut a small independent's turnaround on contractors' project quote requests to the level of the chain branch across the street, without an IT project, and whoever delivers the shortest quote turnaround takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$23.4B in 2022 receipts at 5,881 firms with fewer than 20 employees in NAICS 423610 (Census SUSB 2022); AD's Independent Electrical Supply Division groups 700+ independents of all sizes with $44B+ in sales (search summary)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~11% of 423610 receipts and ~81% of firms (Census SUSB 2022)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — older or entry-level ERPs, price files loaded late, quotes in spreadsheets, SPA claims in a binder or not at all
**Target Buyer:** Owner-president; often the owner also runs purchasing and the biggest accounts
**Automation Potential:** Medium — the work automates, but the buyer has no one to run an implementation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Most electrical distributors are small: four in five firms in the code have fewer than 20 employees, against 192 enterprises with 500+ that take ~62% of receipts. The independents compete with branches of Graybar, Rexel, Sonepar companies, CED and Wesco on local relationships and service, but lack a quote desk, an SPA analyst, a pricing team and a content team. They buy through AD's IESD for programmes and rebates, and their manufacturer authorisations decide which lines they can sell. AI vendors price and staff for the mid-market and the chains; the independents are the customers they reach last. The industrial-side analogue is [[niches/industrial-distributors/small-independent-distributors/profile|Small Independent Industrial Distributors]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Entry-level and legacy distribution ERPs, IDEA's Industry Data Warehouse for price files, AD programmes, manufacturer portals and spreadsheets. The gaps: plan-and-spec quotes declined or answered late for lack of time, costs loaded weeks late, and nobody to run new software; quote turnaround is where the independent visibly loses to the chain branch.
