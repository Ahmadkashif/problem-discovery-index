# A Shared Quote Desk for Independents

**Niche:** [[niches/electrical-distributors/small-independent-electrical-distributors/profile|Small Independent Electrical Distributors]]
**Industry:** [[industries/electrical-distributors|Electrical Distributors]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to cut a small independent's turnaround on contractors' project quote requests to the level of the chain branch across the street, without an IT project, and whoever delivers the shortest quote turnaround takes the account.

## The Problem
Small independents live on counter and service business and lose project work they can't quote. When a good contractor sends a plan set, the owner either quotes it at night, quotes only the contractor's list, or passes, and the contractor's project business — and eventually its daily buying — drifts to a branch with a quote desk.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Quoting tools assume an in-house quoting team and an IT person to connect the ERP. Small independents have neither, and each is too small to justify a vendor's implementation effort.

## What to Build
A shared quoting service: the independent forwards the bid package, an AI-assisted desk (software plus a small pool of human quoters serving many independents) returns a priced package in the independent's catalogue with gaps flagged and SPA requests drafted, ready to load into the ERP. Pricing per quote, so a small firm pays only when it bids.

## Target Customer
Owners of one-to-ten-branch independents, reachable through AD's IESD.

## Impact If Built
Independents can bid project work again. Returning a plan-and-spec quote in ~1–2 days instead of passing (estimate) could recover ~10–20% of a small firm's lost project sales (estimate) and keep its contractors whole.
