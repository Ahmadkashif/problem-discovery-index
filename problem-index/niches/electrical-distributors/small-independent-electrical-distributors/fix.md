# The Plan Set That Waits for the Owner's Evening

**Niche:** [[niches/electrical-distributors/small-independent-electrical-distributors/profile|Small Independent Electrical Distributors]]
**Industry:** [[industries/electrical-distributors|Electrical Distributors]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** At a small independent every project quote request queues behind the owner, who prices it after closing time if there is time at all.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #gradient-boosting #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to cut a small independent's turnaround on contractors' project quote requests to the level of the chain branch across the street, without an IT project, and whoever delivers the shortest quote turnaround takes the account.

## The Problem
Bid requests arrive by email with plans, a spec and a contractor's list. Only the owner or one senior salesperson can price them, so they sit in an inbox until evening, and the ones due soonest or worth most are not handled first. Contractors learn which supplier answers late.

## Why It's Still Broken
Nobody triages the inbox, and every quote starts from a blank sheet because the contractor's list is never pre-loaded.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A triage step that reads each request, extracts the bid date, size and contractor, ranks the queue by deadline and value, and pre-loads the contractor's list into a draft quote at current cost, so the owner reviews and prices rather than types.

## Who Feels the Pain
Owners working evenings, contractors waiting on numbers, and the independent losing project work to the chain branch.

## Impact If Fixed
If triage and pre-loading cut quote turnaround from ~3–5 days to ~1 (estimate), the independent answers inside most contractors' bid windows and stops losing the jobs it never had time to price.
