# Buying-Group Data Turned Into a Member Quoting Service

**Niche:** [[niches/electrical-distributors/small-independent-electrical-distributors/profile|Small Independent Electrical Distributors]]
**Industry:** [[industries/electrical-distributors|Electrical Distributors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** The buying group already holds programme pricing and product data for 700+ independents (search summary); each member still builds every project quote by hand from scratch.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #k-nearest-neighbors #feature-engineering #data-integration #revenue-impact #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to cut a small independent's turnaround on contractors' project quote requests to the level of the chain branch across the street, without an IT project, and whoever delivers the shortest quote turnaround takes the account.

## The Problem
A small independent quoting a project has to find current costs, check which manufacturer programmes and group prices apply, and assemble the package in a spreadsheet or an old ERP. The data it needs mostly exists upstream, at the buying group and in IDEA's feeds, but reaches the member as PDFs and price files it has to load itself, so quotes take days.

## What Already Exists
AD's Independent Electrical Supply Division programmes and member services, IDEA's Industry Data Warehouse, distribution ERPs with basic quote modules, and quoting tools such as Parspec sold mainly to larger distributors. The buying groups as an insight pocket are logged at [[niches/industrial-distributors/industrial-buying-groups/profile|Industrial Buying & Marketing Groups]].

## The Customization Gap
A group-hosted quoting tool preloaded with each member's catalogue, group and programme pricing and current IDEA costs; templates for common project packages; SPA request drafting to the manufacturers members are authorised for; and export into each member's ERP, so a member opens a quote with costs already right instead of assembling them.

## Target Customer
Buying-group member-services leaders and the independents they serve.

## Impact If Solved
If preloaded costs and templates cut a member's quote preparation by about half (estimate), a two-branch independent can answer most contractor bid requests inside the contractor's deadline instead of passing on ~30–50% of them (estimate).
