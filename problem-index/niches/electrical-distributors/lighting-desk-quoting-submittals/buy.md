# Agency Quoting Tools Opened to the Distributor's Desk

**Niche:** [[niches/electrical-distributors/lighting-desk-quoting-submittals/profile|The Lighting Desk: Package Quoting & Submittals]]
**Industry:** [[industries/electrical-distributors|Electrical Distributors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Lighting agencies quote packages in their own systems and email the result; the distributor rekeys it into its quote and its ERP.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #k-nearest-neighbors #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a lighting fixture schedule into a priced package and a submittal the engineer approves on first review, and whoever gets the most fixture types approved without a resubmittal takes the account.

## The Problem
The distributor receives the agency's package quote as a PDF or spreadsheet, rekeys it, adds its margin, merges it into the electrical quote and later builds the submittal from the same lines. Every handoff is a chance for a catalogue string to change.

## What Already Exists
Agency quoting and sales-management software (Parspec released a sales-management product for lighting agents), manufacturer configurators, and distributor ERP quote modules.

## The Customization Gap
A shared package record that passes from agency to distributor with catalogue strings intact; automatic merge into the distributor's quote with margin rules; the same record driving the submittal and the purchase order; and change tracking when the agency revises the package after addenda.

## Target Customer
Distributors' lighting desks working with agencies that already use quoting software.

## Impact If Solved
One package record from agency quote to purchase order removes the rekeying of ~10–60 fixture types per job (estimate), where most lighting catalogue-string errors start.
