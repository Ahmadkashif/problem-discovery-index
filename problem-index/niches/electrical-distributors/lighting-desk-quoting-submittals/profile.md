# The Lighting Desk: Package Quoting & Submittals

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electrical-distributors|Electrical Distributors]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a lighting fixture schedule into a priced package and a submittal the engineer approves on first review, and whoever gets the most fixture types approved without a resubmittal takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$15B a year of commercial lighting and lighting-controls sales through US electrical distributors (estimate); the lighting desk's own labour is a small fraction of that
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of EW's ~$151.2B distributor sales estimate (search summary) (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — fixture schedules are read from PDFs, cut sheets are assembled by hand, and pricing comes from agencies by email
**Target Buyer:** Lighting manager or lighting specialist at the distributor; the VP of construction sales who owns the project book
**Automation Potential:** High for schedule extraction, cut-sheet assembly and option checking; low for agency negotiation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
On a commercial job the lighting package is quoted inside the electrical bid but works differently from the rest of it. Manufacturers' lighting agencies control pricing and specification in each territory and quote the package to the distributor, which resells it to the contractor; after award, the distributor's lighting specialist assembles the submittal: one cut sheet per fixture type, marked with the exact catalogue string, options, lamp, driver, controls and mounting, checked against the schedule and the energy code's controls requirements. Resubmittals cost weeks on fixtures with long lead times. Tools are built for the agencies and for contractors; the distributor's lighting desk, caught between them, is served by neither. Agencies themselves belong to the queued manufacturers' representatives build. The line with neighbouring niches runs three ways: schedule lines matched to SKUs for a straight resale quote go to [[niches/industrial-distributors/rfq-and-order-intake-automation/profile|RFQ & Order Intake Automation]]; agency-priced lighting packages and their submittals belong here; plan-and-spec takeoff goes to [[niches/electrical-distributors/bid-day-project-quoting/profile|Bid-Day Project Quoting & Takeoff]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Parspec (product matching, quoting and submittal packages; a vendor guide quotes Parspec's figure of 52% less submittal preparation time at Rexel USA, unverified), manufacturer configurators and spec sheets, agency quote emails, PDF tools and spreadsheets. The gaps: catalogue strings checked by eye, controls compatibility missed, and resubmittal reasons never recorded.
