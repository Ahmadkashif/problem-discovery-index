# Construction CRM Pointed at the Distributor's Project Pipeline

**Niche:** [[niches/electrical-distributors/contractor-project-supply/profile|Contractor Project Supply]]
**Industry:** [[industries/electrical-distributors|Electrical Distributors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** General CRMs and construction lead services track opportunities; a distributor's opportunity is one job quoted to several contractors at once.
**Tags:** #k-nearest-neighbors #large-language-models #feature-engineering #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to win and keep the electrical contractor's project business, but that splits into two contests with different winners: putting the complete, winning quote in the contractor's hands by bid day, and getting the long-lead gear to the jobsite on the date the schedule needs after award. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Distributors buy CRM and lead data to manage project pursuit, but the tools model an opportunity as one customer and one deal. In electrical distribution a single job is quoted to several bidding contractors, sometimes with different packages, and the distributor wins only if its contractor wins.

## What Already Exists
Salesforce, Microsoft Dynamics and distribution CRMs such as Proton.ai; construction lead and plan services such as Dodge and ConstructConnect, profiled for electrical contractors as [[niches/electrical-contractors/construction-project-lead-data/profile|Construction Project Lead & Plan Data]].

## The Customization Gap
A job-centred data model with many bidders per job; lead records joined to the distributor's quotes and to awards; win inferred from later orders; engineer and specifying-firm history attached to each job; SPA status per bidder; and alerts when a quoted job is awarded to a contractor the distributor didn't quote.

## Target Customer
Construction sales leaders at distributors already paying for a CRM and a lead service.

## Impact If Solved
Existing subscriptions start answering which jobs are live, who is bidding and which the distributor won; a desk quoting ~$200M of project work a year that pursues even a tenth of its untracked jobs to award could add ~$2–5M of won business (estimate).
