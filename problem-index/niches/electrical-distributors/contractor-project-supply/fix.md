# The Won Job Nobody Marked Won

**Niche:** [[niches/electrical-distributors/contractor-project-supply/profile|Contractor Project Supply]]
**Industry:** [[industries/electrical-distributors|Electrical Distributors]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Most project quotes end with no recorded outcome, so a distributor cannot tell its hit rate from its pipeline.
**Tags:** #logistic-regression #k-nearest-neighbors #feature-engineering #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to win and keep the electrical contractor's project business, but that splits into two contests with different winners: putting the complete, winning quote in the contractor's hands by bid day, and getting the long-lead gear to the jobsite on the date the schedule needs after award. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Quotes are sent and forgotten. When the contractor wins, orders start arriving under a job name, often weeks later, and nobody goes back to close the quote. When the contractor loses, nobody hears. Hit-rate reports are built on the few quotes someone remembered to update.

## Why It's Still Broken
Closing quotes is admin work with no immediate payoff, and the ERP rarely links a quote to the orders it produced.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A weekly job that matches new orders and releases to open quotes by contractor, ship-to, job name and product overlap, marks likely wins for one-click confirmation, and ages out quotes past the job's start date as likely losses. Licensed award data fills gaps where available.

## Who Feels the Pain
Sales managers setting quote priorities, quoters who never learn which prices won, and owners judging the project business on guesses.

## Impact If Fixed
A measured hit rate by contractor, market and quoter at almost no cost: order-to-quote matching can close out ~60–80% of quotes automatically (estimate), against the ~10–20% closed by hand today (estimate).
