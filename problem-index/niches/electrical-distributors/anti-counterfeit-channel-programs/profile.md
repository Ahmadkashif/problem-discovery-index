# Anti-Counterfeit & Authorised-Channel Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electrical-distributors|Electrical Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Brand-protection teams at electrical manufacturers, with NEMA's anti-counterfeiting work and customs enforcement, track counterfeit and grey-market circuit breakers, fuses and other safety products sold outside authorised distribution, and push buyers toward authorised distributors. Investigations and takedowns are often outsourced to the vendors mapped under [[industries/brand-protection-firms|Brand Protection Firms]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 per manufacturer, with investigations outsourced (estimate)
**Output:** Counterfeit alerts, authorised-distributor verification, enforcement referrals
**Proprietary data:** Seizure and complaint records and product-authentication data
**Clock:** Customs seizures and complaints as they arise
**Buyer:** Director of brand protection
