# Price Optimisation Rebuilt on a Copper Index

**Niche:** [[niches/electrical-distributors/wire-cable-copper-pricing/profile|Wire & Cable Pricing Against COMEX Copper]]
**Industry:** [[industries/electrical-distributors|Electrical Distributors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Distributors already license price optimisation; it sets wire prices as if copper stood still.
**Tags:** #linear-regression #time-series-forecasting #feature-engineering #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to price every wire and cable quote and order to the day's copper with hold terms the contractor accepts, and whoever keeps wire margin flat through a copper swing without losing the bid takes the account.

## The Problem
Price optimisation tools set customer-specific prices from segment, elasticity and margin targets, refreshed periodically. For wire, the right price is cost-plus on a moving copper reference, and a periodic refresh is always behind.

## What Already Exists
Zilliant, PROS, Vendavo and ERP matrix pricing; manufacturer price sheets; COMEX data feeds.

## The Customization Gap
A live copper reference with each manufacturer's adder and sheet timing; cost-plus rules per wire family and customer tier on top of the optimiser's elasticity; hold periods and escalation clauses as quote attributes; reel-length and cut pricing; tariff pass-through on imported cable; and a nightly sweep of open quotes and unreleased orders against the floor.

## Target Customer
Distributors already paying for a price optimisation platform.

## Impact If Solved
The pricing investment covers the most volatile category instead of exempting it; repricing open wire quotes nightly rather than monthly could cut below-floor wire releases by ~50% (estimate) and protect ~1 margin point on the category (estimate).
