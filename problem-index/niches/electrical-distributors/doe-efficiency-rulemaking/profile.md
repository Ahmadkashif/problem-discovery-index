# DOE Transformer & Lighting Efficiency Rulemaking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electrical-distributors|Electrical Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Department of Energy's appliance and equipment standards programme, supported by national-laboratory and consulting analysts, writes the efficiency standards for distribution transformers, lamps and other electrical equipment that distributors sell. The April 2024 distribution transformer rule (89 FR 29834) requires compliance from 23 April 2029, and a June 2026 request for information (91 FR 35903) reopened questions on supply chains and grain-oriented electrical steel. Each rule rests on a technical support document of engineering, market and economic analysis.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–20 DOE, national-laboratory and contractor analysts working the transformer and lighting rulemakings at any time (estimate): engineering, market, life-cycle-cost and national-impact analyses are separate teams on each rule
**Output:** Technical support documents, proposed and final rules, test procedures
**Proprietary data:** Teardown and engineering analysis, shipment models and confidential manufacturer interviews summarised into public documents
**Clock:** Statutory review deadlines under the Energy Policy and Conservation Act and any court-ordered schedules
**Buyer:** None in the commercial sense; contractors are paid by DOE
