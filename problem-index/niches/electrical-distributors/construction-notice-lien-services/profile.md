# Construction Notice & Lien Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electrical-distributors|Electrical Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Services that send preliminary notices, notices of intent and lien filings for suppliers on construction projects, so an electrical distributor selling to a contractor on account keeps its statutory right to be paid from the project if the contractor doesn't pay. Each state sets its own notice deadlines, which the services track. Procore's Levelset and specialist notice firms serve distributors' credit departments, paid per notice or by subscription.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Mostly notice processors and customer support; analysts, where they exist, number under 10 per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Preliminary notices, lien and bond-claim filings, deadline tracking
**Proprietary data:** Project and owner records and notice histories across clients
**Clock:** State statutory notice and lien deadlines
**Buyer:** Distributor credit manager
