# Gear Lead Times & Release Management

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electrical-distributors|Electrical Distributors]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get switchgear, transformers and other long-lead gear to the jobsite on the date the contractor's schedule needs, and whoever warns the contractor of a slip earliest takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$25B a year of switchgear, panelboards, transformers, busway, generators and other long-lead gear sold through US electrical distributors (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~17% of EW's ~$151.2B distributor sales estimate (search summary) (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — acknowledgements arrive as PDFs, status sits in manufacturer portals, and updates go to contractors by phone and email
**Target Buyer:** Project management or operations leader at the distributor; the contractor's project manager as the voice of the account
**Automation Potential:** High for status collection and reconciliation; medium for slip prediction; low for allocation and substitution decisions

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is the post-award contest. Once a contractor wins, the distributor has to deliver gear whose lead time it does not control: Wood Mackenzie put power transformers at an average of 128 weeks in Q2 2025 and pad-mount distribution transformers at ~75 weeks after order in mid-2024, still lengthening in 2025, and data center demand has stretched switchgear and busway lead times too (estimate), the same demand utilities face at [[niches/electric-utilities/large-load-data-centre-interconnection/profile|Large-Load & Data Centre Interconnection]]. IDEA's ProjectNexus page says promised ship dates keep moving per order and that projects need 18+ portal logins. The winner is the distributor whose dates the contractor can plan around, which depends on allocation from manufacturers, visibility across portals and the honesty of early warnings, not on quoting skill. On 13 May 2026 NAED selected an IDEA and Pull Logic joint venture to build ProjectNexus, an industry-owned order-status platform, with 298 branch locations in its pilot or beta.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Manufacturer order portals, EDI acknowledgements and ship notices, ERP purchase-order screens, spreadsheets kept by project managers, ProjectNexus as it rolls out, and AI lead-time prediction at Border States. The gaps: date revisions overwritten rather than kept, acknowledgements not reconciled with approved submittals, and no slip forecast.
