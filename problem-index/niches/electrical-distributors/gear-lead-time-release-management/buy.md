# Supply-Chain Visibility Software Built for Project Releases

**Niche:** [[niches/electrical-distributors/gear-lead-time-release-management/profile|Gear Lead Times & Release Management]]
**Industry:** [[industries/electrical-distributors|Electrical Distributors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Supply-chain visibility tools track shipments and purchase orders; a distributor's project manager tracks lines against a contractor's schedule and an approved submittal.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #survival-analysis #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get switchgear, transformers and other long-lead gear to the jobsite on the date the contractor's schedule needs, and whoever warns the contractor of a slip earliest takes the account.

## The Problem
General visibility platforms show where a purchase order or shipment is. They don't know which job it belongs to, which submittal it must match, which release the contractor asked for, or which milestone a slip will hit.

## What Already Exists
Supply-chain visibility and PO-tracking software, manufacturer portals, EDI acknowledgement and ship-notice feeds, and IDEA's ProjectNexus, which will share line status across manufacturers, reps, distributors and contractors.

## The Customization Gap
Job and release structure on top of purchase orders; reconciliation of acknowledged catalogue numbers and options with the approved submittal; contractor milestones from the schedule; staging and partial-release logic; drafted contractor updates; and a connector to ProjectNexus so the industry feed drives the board as it arrives.

## Target Customer
Distributors that want order visibility now and plan to join ProjectNexus.

## Impact If Solved
One board replaces the 18+ portal logins per project IDEA reports and cuts expediters' date-chasing from the ~40% of staff time IDEA cites toward ~10% (estimate), so the industry feed is useful on day one rather than one more screen.
