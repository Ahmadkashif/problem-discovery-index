# Electrical Manufacturers' Project Pricing & SPA Desks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electrical-distributors|Electrical Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The project pricing and channel teams at electrical manufacturers — Eaton, Schneider Electric (Square D), Siemens, ABB, Hubbell, Legrand, Acuity, Signify and peers — receive distributors' SPA requests on project quotes, often through rep agencies, decide the job price, issue the agreement and later adjudicate the ship-and-debit claims against it. Because every distributor quoting a job asks for pricing, the desk sees competing requests on the same project, which no distributor ever sees. Their technical counterparts are logged at [[niches/electrical-contractors/manufacturer-application-engineering/profile|Electrical Manufacturer Application Engineering]] (40); the manufacturers themselves are the queued electrical products and power equipment manufacturers builds.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~3–10 channel-analytics staff per large manufacturer who analyse SPA, claim and point-of-sale data for programme and pricing policy (estimate). The larger teams that price each job's SPA and adjudicate each claim are production headcount and are not counted.
**Output:** Channel programme and pricing-policy analysis drawn from SPA and claim history
**Proprietary data:** SPA request and approval history across all distributors and reps, with job, contractor and competing requests, plus ship-and-debit claim data
**Clock:** Internal programme and pricing cycles; bid dates govern the production desk, not the analytics
**Buyer:** VP of channel or pricing
