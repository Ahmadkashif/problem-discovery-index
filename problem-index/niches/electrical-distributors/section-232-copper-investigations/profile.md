# Section 232 Copper Investigations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electrical-distributors|Electrical Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Commerce Department's Bureau of Industry and Security investigates whether imports threaten national security under Section 232 and recommends action; the copper investigation produced Proclamation 10962, a 50% tariff on semi-finished copper and derivatives including wire, cable and connectors from 1 August 2025, extended to full value from 6 April 2026 by Proclamation 11021, with a process for adding derivative products. Distributors importing cable and fittings, and their customs brokers, must declare copper content and pay accordingly. Trade-remedy economics are logged at [[niches/customs-brokers/trade-remedy-economic-analysis/profile|Trade Remedy Economic Analysis]]; the bearing analogue is [[niches/industrial-distributors/bearing-trade-remedy-enforcement/profile|Bearing Trade-Remedy Enforcement]] (fails gate: a handful of analysts work the bearing orders).

## Insight Function
**Size:** A few analysts on the copper investigation and its derivative inclusions at any time (estimate); BIS's wider Section 232 staff serve steel, aluminium and other investigations and are not this pocket's function
**Output:** Investigation reports, recommendations, derivative-inclusion decisions
**Proprietary data:** Public comments plus confidential business information submitted by industry
**Clock:** The statutory 270-day deadline for the investigation report and the proclamation's inclusion windows
**Buyer:** None in the commercial sense
