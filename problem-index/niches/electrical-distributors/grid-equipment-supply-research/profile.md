# Grid Equipment Supply-Chain Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electrical-distributors|Electrical Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Energy and industrial research firms survey transformer, switchgear and other grid-equipment manufacturers and buyers on lead times, prices and capacity, and sell the results as subscription research and advisory to utilities, developers, manufacturers, distributors and investors. Wood Mackenzie is the most cited example: its Q2 2025 data put power transformers at an average of 128 weeks and generator step-up units at 143 weeks, an August 2025 analysis estimated supply deficits of 30% for power and 6% for distribution transformers, with distribution transformer unit costs up 78–95% since 2019, and a mid-2024 note put pad-mount distribution transformers at ~75 weeks after order. Its readers include [[industries/electric-utilities|Electric Distribution Utilities]], whose [[niches/electric-utilities/large-load-data-centre-interconnection/profile|Large-Load & Data Centre Interconnection]] and [[niches/electric-utilities/new-service-connection-design/profile|New Service Connection Design & Estimating]] niches wait on the same transformers, and the queued power equipment manufacturers build. It is logged here because transformer and switchgear lead times decide two of this industry's niches. Utility-side data platforms are logged at [[niches/electric-utilities/power-utility-data-platforms/profile|Power & Utility Data Platforms]] (38), and the transformer resale trade at [[niches/electric-utilities/transformer-brokers-remanufacturers/profile|Transformer Brokers & Remanufacturers]] (fails gate).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 analysts on grid equipment and supply chains per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Lead-time and price surveys, supply-demand outlooks, capacity trackers, advisory projects
**Proprietary data:** Survey panels of manufacturers and buyers, with the survey history
**Clock:** Quarterly and annual survey calendars the firms set
**Buyer:** Head of power and renewables research or practice lead, grid supply chain
