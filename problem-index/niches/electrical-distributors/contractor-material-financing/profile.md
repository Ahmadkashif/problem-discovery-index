# Contractor Material-Financing Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electrical-distributors|Electrical Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Fintech lenders that pay distributors upfront for a subcontractor's materials and collect from the subcontractor later. Billd is the best-known example: it pays the supplier in full and is repaid in up to 120 days, and claims more than $1B of material purchases financed. For the electrical distributor the platform replaces some of its own trade credit on project jobs; for the lender, the analysis is underwriting subcontractors and their projects. Distributors' own credit risk is mapped at [[niches/credit-bureaus/commercial-trade-credit/profile|Commercial Trade Credit Reporting]] and [[niches/independent-retailers/commercial-credit-bureaus/profile|Commercial Credit Bureaus]] (56); surety on the same contractors is [[niches/electrical-contractors/construction-surety-underwriting/profile|Construction Surety Underwriting]] (36).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 credit and risk analysts per platform (estimate)
**Output:** Credit decisions and limits on subcontractors and projects
**Proprietary data:** Repayment histories of subcontractors across projects and suppliers
**Clock:** The lender's own decision times; project pay applications
**Buyer:** Chief credit or risk officer
