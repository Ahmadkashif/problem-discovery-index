# Bid-Day Project Quoting & Takeoff

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electrical-distributors|Electrical Distributors]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to put a complete, spec-compliant, SPA-priced quote in the bidding contractor's hands before the bid closes, and whoever posts the highest hit rate on plan-and-spec quotes takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.5B a year of quotation-desk labour and quoting software at US electrical distributors (estimate: ~12,000–15,000 quotation and project sales specialists plus tooling)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1% of EW's ~$151.2B distributor sales estimate (search summary) in cost (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — drawings are read on screen, but takeoff gaps, substitutions and SPA requests are handled by hand
**Target Buyer:** Quotation or project sales manager; VP of construction sales
**Automation Potential:** High for schedule extraction and quote assembly; medium for substitution and pricing judgement

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is the pre-award contest. The input is a bid package — drawings, Division 26 spec, fixture schedule, addenda and usually the contractor's own partial list — and the output is a priced package that has to be complete, compliant and cheap enough to help the contractor win, delivered by a bid deadline the distributor doesn't control. The line with neighbouring niches runs three ways: schedule or list lines matched to SKUs for a straight resale quote go to [[niches/industrial-distributors/rfq-and-order-intake-automation/profile|RFQ & Order Intake Automation]], where the cross-distribution vendor field is profiled once; agency-priced lighting packages and submittals go to [[niches/electrical-distributors/lighting-desk-quoting-submittals/profile|The Lighting Desk]]; plan-and-spec takeoff, where the contest is what the list left out and what price wins, belongs here. Contractor-side estimating and takeoff are [[niches/electrical-contractors/estimating-job-costing/profile|Estimating & Job Costing]] and [[niches/electrical-contractors/electrical-estimating-services/profile|Electrical Estimating Service Bureaus]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Parspec (AI product matching, quoting and submittals; the company said in July 2025 that four of the five largest U.S. electrical distributors use it), ERP quote modules in Epicor Eclipse and SAP, PDF viewers and spreadsheets, manufacturer SPA portals, and contractor-side takeoff tools. The gaps: no record of the quoter's additions and substitutions, no measured hit rate, and SPA requests sent by email and tracked in memory.
