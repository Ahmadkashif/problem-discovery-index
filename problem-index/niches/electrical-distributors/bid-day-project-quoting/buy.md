# AI Takeoff Turned Toward the Distributor's Quote

**Niche:** [[niches/electrical-distributors/bid-day-project-quoting/profile|Bid-Day Project Quoting & Takeoff]]
**Industry:** [[industries/electrical-distributors|Electrical Distributors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** AI takeoff counts symbols on drawings for contractors; a distributor needs those counts turned into priced, stocked catalogue lines with SPA coverage.
**Tags:** #object-detection #cnns #large-language-models #k-nearest-neighbors #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to put a complete, spec-compliant, SPA-priced quote in the bidding contractor's hands before the bid closes, and whoever posts the highest hit rate on plan-and-spec quotes takes the account.

## The Problem
Distributors that try contractor takeoff tools get device counts and linear footage, which is the contractor's problem. The distributor's problem is a priced package in its own catalogue, from approved manufacturers, with SPA requests attached, in its ERP.

## What Already Exists
Contractor takeoff software (Trimble Accubid, ConEst, McCormick) and newer AI takeoff tools that detect symbols and measure runs; Parspec on the distributor side for matching and submittals.

## The Customization Gap
Mapping takeoff output to catalogue items using IDEA attributes and the spec's basis-of-design; manufacturer selection by engineer and spec; package structure by gear, lighting, wire and commodity; SPA request drafting per manufacturer and rep; and writing the quote into Eclipse or SAP with each line's source visible.

## Target Customer
Distributors with quote desks that already license a takeoff tool or receive contractor takeoffs.

## Impact If Solved
Takeoff becomes a priced quote rather than a count, cutting a plan-and-spec quote from ~8–16 hours of quoter time to ~2–4 (estimate).
