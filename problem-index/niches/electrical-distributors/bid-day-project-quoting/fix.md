# The SPA Request That Comes Back After the Bid

**Niche:** [[niches/electrical-distributors/bid-day-project-quoting/profile|Bid-Day Project Quoting & Takeoff]]
**Industry:** [[industries/electrical-distributors|Electrical Distributors]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The quote needs project pricing to win, and the manufacturer's answer arrives the day after the contractor submitted its bid.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #survival-analysis #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to put a complete, spec-compliant, SPA-priced quote in the bidding contractor's hands before the bid closes, and whoever posts the highest hit rate on plan-and-spec quotes takes the account.

## The Problem
SPA requests go to manufacturers or their reps by email or portal, late in the quote, with incomplete job details. Some come back in hours, some in days, some not at all, and the quoter either guesses a price or submits at list-minus.

## Why It's Still Broken
Each manufacturer has its own request format and turnaround, nobody tracks response times, and requests are sent only once the package is assembled.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Draft SPA requests as soon as the gear and lighting lines are identified, with job, contractor, engineer and competitor context filled in; track every request against the bid deadline; and learn each manufacturer's and rep's typical turnaround so late-risk requests are chased early.

## Who Feels the Pain
Quoters on deadline, contractors whose bids go in high, and manufacturers' reps who lose jobs they could have priced.

## Impact If Fixed
If ~20–40% of SPA requests come back after the bid today (estimate), drafting them as soon as the gear lines are identified puts project pricing into most of those quotes, worth several points of hit rate on gear-heavy jobs (estimate).
