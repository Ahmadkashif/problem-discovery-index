# Electrical Trade Press & Sales-Potential Estimates

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electrical-distributors|Electrical Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Trade titles that size and rank the electrical channel: Electrical Wholesaling's annual Market Planning Guide (~$151.2B of distributor sales for its 2026 edition, search summary) and Top 100 ranking, Electrical Marketing's quarterly sales-potential estimates by state and metro area, NAED's TED magazine and lighting-channel newsletters such as Inside Lighting. Manufacturers, reps and distributors use the estimates for territory planning. The industrial-channel equivalents are logged at [[niches/industrial-distributors/distribution-market-research-publishers/profile|Distribution Market Research Publishers]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 market analysts across the titles; most staff are editors and writers (estimate)
**Output:** Market-size estimates, rankings, sales-potential tables, surveys
**Proprietary data:** Employment-based estimation models and decades of rankings and survey responses
**Clock:** Self-set publication calendars
**Buyer:** Publisher or editorial director
