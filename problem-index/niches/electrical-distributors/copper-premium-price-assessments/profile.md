# Copper Premium & Scrap Price Assessments

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electrical-distributors|Electrical Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Price reporting agencies and metals research firms (Fastmarkets, Argus, S&P Global Commodity Insights and CRU are representative) assess the parts of the copper price that the exchanges don't publish: cathode and rod premiums over COMEX and LME, scrap discounts, and regional spreads, and sell them with market commentary to miners, smelters, rod and wire makers, traders and large buyers. Wire manufacturers' copper-based price sheets, which electrical distributors price from, sit downstream of the exchange price plus these premiums.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 base-metals reporters and analysts covering copper at each agency (estimate)
**Output:** Daily and weekly premium and scrap assessments, market commentary, short-term outlooks
**Proprietary data:** Reporter-collected transaction and bid-offer intelligence on premiums and scrap, with the assessment history behind each number
**Clock:** Daily and weekly publication calendars the agencies set; some supply contracts reference the assessments
**Buyer:** Head of metals pricing or editorial director, base metals
