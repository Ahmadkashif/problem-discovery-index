# Contractor Texting Turned Into Staged Will-Call

**Niche:** [[niches/electrical-distributors/branch-counter-sales/profile|Branch Counter Sales to Contractors]]
**Industry:** [[industries/electrical-distributors|Electrical Distributors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Distributors already take orders by text through shared inboxes; the texts still have to be read, matched and keyed before anyone picks them.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #k-nearest-neighbors #word-embeddings #automation #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get an electrician out of the branch with the right part during the early-morning rush, and whoever clears the 6:30 a.m. line with the fewest wrong-part return trips takes the account.

## The Problem
Electricians text a list or a photo the night before or from the truck. A counter salesperson reads it at 6 a.m., matches each item, keys the order and asks the warehouse to pull it, while the line forms.

## What Already Exists
Contractor messaging inboxes such as Prokeep, ERP counter and order screens, and the cross-distribution AI order-entry vendors profiled at [[niches/industrial-distributors/rfq-and-order-intake-automation/profile|RFQ & Order Intake Automation]], which target emailed POs more than counter texts.

## The Customization Gap
Matching electricians' shorthand ("20 1P QO", "100' 12/2 MC", "4 sq box deep") to catalogue items with account pricing; photo lines resolved through the counter identification model; orders drafted into the ERP as will-call with pick tickets printed before opening; and a text back with what's ready and what's substituted.

## Target Customer
Branches already using a contractor texting tool.

## Impact If Solved
Will-call staged before opening could take ~20–40% of walk-ins out of the morning line (estimate), leaving the counter free for the hard questions.
