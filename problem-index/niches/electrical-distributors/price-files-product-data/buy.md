# Product Information Management Fitted to IDEA Feeds

**Niche:** [[niches/electrical-distributors/price-files-product-data/profile|Manufacturer Price Files & Product Data]]
**Industry:** [[industries/electrical-distributors|Electrical Distributors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** PIM software manages product content well; electrical distributors need it to treat IDEA's feed, price changes and supersessions as the core workflow.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #k-nearest-neighbors #data-integration #automation #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to load each manufacturer's price file and product changes into the ERP, the open quotes and the webstore on the day they take effect, and whoever leaves the fewest lines quoted or sold on a superseded cost takes the account.

## The Problem
Distributors buy product information management for their webstores and find it built around marketing content, while their pain is prices, supersessions and ERP synchronisation.

## What Already Exists
General PIM platforms, IDEA's IDW and data services, ERP import utilities, and e-commerce platforms with catalogue modules.

## The Customization Gap
IDEA IDW ingestion as the primary source; manufacturer price-change and supersession handling; list and multiplier pricing structures; synchronisation to Epicor Eclipse or SAP item, contract and quote tables; and exception queues for items IDEA doesn't cover.

## Target Customer
Distributors with a PIM or e-commerce project that also need price data current in the ERP.

## Impact If Solved
One product data pipeline for the ERP and the webstore, current on the effective date, replacing ~1–3 FTEs of manual file loading at a mid-size distributor (estimate).
