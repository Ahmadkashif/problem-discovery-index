# NEMA Market Statistics Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electrical-distributors|Electrical Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The National Electrical Manufacturers Association runs statistical and business-conditions programmes for its members, collecting shipment and order data by product category and publishing indices and member-only market reports that manufacturers use to read demand in the electrical channel. NEMA also co-founded IDEA with NAED in 1998. Its standards work is a separate function, alongside the code bodies logged at [[niches/electrical-contractors/code-standards-development-bodies/profile|Electrical Code & Standards Development Bodies]]; NAED's distributor-side association role is noted in [[niches/industrial-distributors/industrial-distribution-associations/profile|Industrial Distribution Trade Associations]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–20 economists and statistics staff (estimate)
**Output:** Shipment statistics by product category, business-conditions indices, member market reports
**Proprietary data:** Member-submitted shipment and order data that no outside party holds
**Clock:** Monthly and quarterly reporting calendars the association sets
**Buyer:** Association president or chief economist
