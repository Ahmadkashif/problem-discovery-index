# Large Design Studios' In-House Research Labs

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landscape-architecture-firms|Landscape Architecture Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
The largest landscape architecture and planning studios (SWA, Sasaki, OLIN, Design Workshop, Mithun, MKSK and peers, each with 100–400 staff) run small research, data and performance groups that measure built projects, model heat, stormwater and ecology, and publish case studies. Their output supports design fees and marketing; occasionally it is sold as a planning-analytics service. This entry also records why the operator layer itself is not scored as an insight pocket here: a design studio's billable product is design, and its research function, where one exists, is small and supporting.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-25 researchers and data specialists at the few studios that maintain a lab (estimate)
**Output:** Performance studies, analytics embedded in design proposals, publications
**Proprietary data:** Built-project performance measurements and design archives
**Clock:** Competition and proposal deadlines; client reporting where performance targets are contractual
**Buyer:** Research or innovation director at the studio
