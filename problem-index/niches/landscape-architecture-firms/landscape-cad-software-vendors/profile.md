# Landscape CAD & Plant Database Software Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landscape-architecture-firms|Landscape Architecture Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors of landscape design software — Land F/X (Planting, Irrigation and Detail add-ons for AutoCAD, with Planting F/X at $695 a year), Vectorworks Landmark, and Autodesk's Civil 3D and AutoCAD — maintain plant databases, irrigation product libraries, manufacturer detail content and calculation tools. Content and product teams curate plant traits and manufacturer data; the software is the invoice.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 content, product and support staff curating plant and product data across the main vendors (estimate)
**Output:** Software subscriptions with embedded plant and product databases
**Proprietary data:** Curated plant and manufacturer libraries; usage data on which plants and products designers specify
**Clock:** Annual release cycles; none set by a third party
**Buyer:** Head of product or content
