# Plant Specifications Graded Against Survival

**Niche:** [[niches/landscape-architecture-firms/planting-design-specification/profile|Planting Design & Plant Specification]]
**Industry:** [[industries/landscape-architecture-firms|Landscape Architecture Firms]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Landscape architects specify thousands of plants a year and never learn which of those specifications were alive three years later.
**Tags:** #survival-analysis #gradient-boosting #random-forests #cnns #confidence-intervals #tacit-knowledge-ml #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to specify a plant palette that is available at bid and still alive at the end of the warranty, and whoever's palettes survive without substitution fights or replacement takes the account.

## The Problem
A senior planting designer "knows" that a particular oak struggles in parking-lot islands in clay, or that a popular grass declines after three summers in reflected heat. That knowledge came from being called back to failed sites, and it is partial, regional and unrecorded. Junior designers specify from the plant database and the last project's list. The outcome — replacement at the warranty walk, slow death in year two, removal by the maintenance crew in year four — is observed by owners and maintenance contractors, not by the designer.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The specification sits with the design firm and the outcome sits with others. The fee ends at closeout. Recording failures creates liability evidence, which makes firms wary. No single firm plants enough of any species in any one condition to learn from its own data alone.

## What to Build
A survival record per planting: species, cultivar, nursery size and source, planting date, location, exposure, soil, irrigation zone and maintenance contract — from the as-built schedule — joined to warranty replacements, maintenance work orders and periodic imagery checks. Survival models by species and condition, surfaced as a score beside each plant in the designer's schedule, with the expert's own annotations captured alongside so their reasons travel with the data.

## Target Customer
Planting design leads at firms with large public and campus portfolios; owners such as universities and park agencies who pay for replacements.

## Impact If Built
Palettes chosen on evidence, fewer replacements, and the senior designer's regional knowledge turned into a firm asset that survives their retirement.
