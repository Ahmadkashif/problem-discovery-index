# Park & Green Infrastructure Grant Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landscape-architecture-firms|Landscape Architecture Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The federal and state programmes that pay for much of the public landscape work: the Land and Water Conservation Fund's state-side grants, state park and trail grant rounds, federal transportation alternatives funding for trails and streetscapes, and state revolving funds and stormwater grants for green infrastructure. Programme staff write criteria, score applications, monitor compliance and approve reimbursements.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 grant managers and reviewers per programme at federal and large state level (estimate)
**Output:** Funding decisions and compliance determinations — not invoiced
**Proprietary data:** Application archives, scoring records and post-completion inspection reports
**Clock:** Appropriation cycles and statutory grant rounds
**Buyer:** Programme manager
