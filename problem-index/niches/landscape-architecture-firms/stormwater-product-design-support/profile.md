# Stormwater & Soil-Cell Product Manufacturers' Design Support

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landscape-architecture-firms|Landscape Architecture Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Manufacturers of proprietary stormwater treatment devices, underground detention chambers, permeable paving systems and suspended-pavement soil cells (Contech, ADS StormTech, Oldcastle Infrastructure, DeepRoot Silva Cell and others) employ engineers who size systems, draft layouts and prepare calculations for landscape architects and civil engineers at no charge, to get their product written into the specification.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 design-support engineers and technical staff per large manufacturer (estimate)
**Output:** Sizing calculations, layouts and specification text — given away to win the specification
**Proprietary data:** Laboratory and field performance testing, third-party verification results, and a record of thousands of sized projects
**Clock:** The designer's permit and bid deadlines
**Buyer:** Director of engineering or design services at the manufacturer
