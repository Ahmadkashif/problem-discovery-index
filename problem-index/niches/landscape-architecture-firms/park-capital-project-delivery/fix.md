# Grant Reimbursement Paperwork Assembled at Closeout

**Niche:** [[niches/landscape-architecture-firms/park-capital-project-delivery/profile|Park Capital Project Delivery]]
**Industry:** [[industries/landscape-architecture-firms|Landscape Architecture Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Grant-funded parks carry compliance requirements from application to closeout, and the evidence is hunted down after construction when the reimbursement request is due.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce park bid documents that come in within the construction budget at the first bid opening without missing a grant deadline, and whoever delivers parks that bid on budget takes the account.

## The Problem
Parks funded through the Land and Water Conservation Fund, state park grant programmes or federal transportation set-asides carry conditions: boundary maps, signage, environmental and cultural reviews, procurement rules, accessibility, wage and domestic-content provisions where federal money applies, and photo documentation. The agency asks the landscape architect to help assemble the reimbursement package after construction, and the evidence is scattered across submittals, field reports and photos.

## Why It's Still Broken
Grant conditions sit in an award agreement nobody on the design team reads closely; nothing maps each condition to the document that will satisfy it; and design firms are not paid separately for compliance support.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Read the grant agreement at award and generate a condition checklist mapped to project milestones, specification sections and submittals; collect the evidence during construction administration as it is produced; and assemble the reimbursement package automatically at closeout.

## Who Feels the Pain
Agency grant coordinators who risk losing reimbursement, project landscape architects pulled back onto finished projects, and small agencies without grant staff.

## Impact If Fixed
Reimbursements claimed on time and in full, and days of closeout scramble removed per grant-funded project.
