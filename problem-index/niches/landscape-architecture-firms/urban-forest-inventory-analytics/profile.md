# Urban Forest Inventory & Canopy Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landscape-architecture-firms|Landscape Architecture Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Consultancies that inventory municipal street and park trees, map tree canopy from imagery, and write urban forest management plans that landscape architects then design street-tree programmes against. Davey Resource Group is the largest — it ran StreetsLA's first inventory in decades, and inventoried ~18,000 public trees with a canopy assessment for Bloomington, Indiana for $125,250 — and is an industry co-operator in the USDA Forest Service's i-Tree tools; smaller firms such as PlanIT Geo compete.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 arborists, GIS analysts and urban forestry planners at the largest firm; 10-50 at the next tier (estimate)
**Output:** Tree inventories, canopy assessments, ecosystem-benefit valuations and urban forest management plans — the billable deliverable, often bundled with inventory software
**Proprietary data:** Methods and cross-city benchmarks; the inventories themselves are delivered to and owned by the municipalities and are generally public records
**Clock:** Grant application and reporting deadlines for urban forestry funding, canopy goals in climate plans, and storm or pest response
**Buyer:** Director of urban forestry consulting
