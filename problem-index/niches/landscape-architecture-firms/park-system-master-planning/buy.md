# Engagement Platforms Fitted to Park Needs Assessment

**Niche:** [[niches/landscape-architecture-firms/park-system-master-planning/profile|Park System Master Planning & Engagement]]
**Industry:** [[industries/landscape-architecture-firms|Landscape Architecture Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Civic engagement platforms collect park input well and know nothing about levels of service, unmet need or park benchmarks.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #hypothesis-testing #large-language-models #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a city's contested community input into a park plan the council adopts and the capital programme funds, and whoever produces adopted, defensible plans fastest takes the account.

## The Problem
Firms run engagement on one platform, the needs survey through a subcontractor, service-area analysis in GIS and benchmarking from NRPA data, then reconcile them manually into a priority ranking of facilities and programmes.

## What Already Exists
PublicInput, Social Pinpoint, Bang the Table (EngagementHQ), SurveyMonkey and Qualtrics for collection and reporting; ArcGIS for mapping; NRPA Park Metrics for agency benchmarks.

## The Customization Gap
The park-specific layer is: question templates that map to facility and programme categories planners use; an unmet-need and importance scoring method comparable to what survey subcontractors produce; joining responses to residents' park service areas so need can be mapped; side-by-side benchmarks against similar agencies; and weighting that keeps self-selected online input separate from the statistically valid sample.

## Target Customer
Park planning firms and agency planning staff running two or three system plans a year.

## Impact If Solved
A priority ranking assembled from one consistent dataset rather than four reconciled ones, with weeks taken out of each plan schedule.
