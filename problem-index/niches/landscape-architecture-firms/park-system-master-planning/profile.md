# Park System Master Planning & Engagement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landscape-architecture-firms|Landscape Architecture Firms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a city's contested community input into a park plan the council adopts and the capital programme funds, and whoever produces adopted, defensible plans fastest takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$350M a year in park system, comprehensive and site master planning fees (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~6% of fee revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — GIS and online surveys are standard; synthesis of engagement and level-of-service analysis are rebuilt by hand
**Target Buyer:** Planning studio leader at a park-focused firm; parks director on the client side
**Automation Potential:** Medium-High for engagement coding and service-area analysis; low for consensus-building

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A park system master plan or a site master plan ends in a vote, not a construction contract. The work is inventory, level-of-service and walk-access analysis, a statistically valid needs survey (often subcontracted to firms like ETC Institute), several rounds of public engagement, cost-phased recommendations and a document the council adopts. Agencies update these plans on five-to-ten-year cycles, often tied to accreditation, a bond measure or a grant programme requiring an adopted plan. The winners are firms whose plans get adopted without a backlash and get funded afterwards — a persuasion and evidence contest, unlike construction delivery.

## Current Tools & Gaps
ArcGIS for inventory and service areas; the Trust for Public Land's ParkServe for 10-minute-walk analysis; NRPA Park Metrics benchmarks; PublicInput, Social Pinpoint or SurveyMonkey for engagement; InDesign for the document. Gaps: engagement synthesis done by hand, needs-survey results not linked to the GIS analysis, and no tracking of which recommendations became funded projects.
