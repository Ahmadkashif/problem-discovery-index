# Engagement Evidence the Council Can Trace

**Niche:** [[niches/landscape-architecture-firms/park-system-master-planning/profile|Park System Master Planning & Engagement]]
**Industry:** [[industries/landscape-architecture-firms|Landscape Architecture Firms]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A master plan's priorities rest on hundreds of public comments that were skimmed, not coded, and the claim "residents asked for this" cannot be traced when a council member challenges it.
**Tags:** #bert #large-language-models #k-means-clustering #dbscan #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a city's contested community input into a park plan the council adopts and the capital programme funds, and whoever produces adopted, defensible plans fastest takes the account.

## The Problem
A park system plan collects input through a mailed needs survey, an online questionnaire, three or four public meetings, pop-up events and a pin map. The statistically valid survey is analysed properly by the subcontractor; everything else — often the material residents care most about — is summarised by the landscape architect under deadline. When the draft plan reaches council, opponents of a recommendation (a dog park, lighted fields, a parking lot removed) say the public never asked for it, and the firm has no traceable answer.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Engagement platforms collect input and count votes; they do not code open comments against the plan's recommendations. Research-grade qualitative coding tools exist for academics but assume a trained analyst and weeks of time. The plan's recommendations are written in a separate document with no link back.

## What to Build
A workspace that codes every comment from every channel into designer-defined themes, clusters map comments by location, weights channels separately from the statistically valid survey, and links each plan recommendation to the comments and survey results that support or oppose it. Every claim in the staff report becomes a click-through to the evidence, with minority-language comments reported separately.

## Target Customer
Planning studio leaders at park-focused firms and the in-house planning teams of large park agencies.

## Impact If Built
Plans adopted on the first hearing more often, fewer redesign cycles after public backlash, and roughly a week of synthesis saved per engagement round (estimate).
