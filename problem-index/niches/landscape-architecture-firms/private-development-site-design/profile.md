# Private Development Site Design

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landscape-architecture-firms|Landscape Architecture Firms]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to design landscapes that survive the developer's value-engineering round — drawn to the installed cost the pro forma assumed while still passing landscape review — and whoever avoids unpaid VE redesign takes the developer's next project.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.9B a year in fees from developers and builders of multifamily, mixed-use, commercial, industrial and master-planned residential projects (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~33% of fee revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — CAD production is digital; cost feedback reaches the designer late and by email
**Target Buyer:** Principal or studio director serving developer clients; development manager on the client side
**Automation Potential:** Medium-High — cost modelling, code tables and production coordination are automatable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Developer work is repeat-client, schedule-driven and cost-capped. The landscape architect is often a sub-consultant to the civil engineer or architect, the landscape is among the last scopes priced, and when the general contractor's estimate comes in over budget the landscape is the first thing cut. The firm then redesigns — fewer trees, smaller sizes, cheaper paving, amenity deck simplified — usually without additional fee, while still meeting the city's landscape code. ASLA's 2024 report found 61% of firms win new work mainly through repeat clients, and in this niche a developer's repeat business depends on whether the landscape came in at the number.

## Current Tools & Gaps
AutoCAD with Land F/X, Vectorworks and SketchUp for design; Excel estimates; the GC's estimate arrives as a PDF. Gaps: no cost model that updates with the design, no historical record of what the developer actually paid per unit for landscape, and VE decisions recorded only in email.
