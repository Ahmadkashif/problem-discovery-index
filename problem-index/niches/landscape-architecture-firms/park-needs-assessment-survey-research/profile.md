# Community & Park Needs-Assessment Survey Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landscape-architecture-firms|Landscape Architecture Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Survey research firms that run the statistically valid household surveys behind park system master plans, recreation needs assessments and community satisfaction studies. ETC Institute is the best-known, with household surveys in more than 400 communities in 49 states since 1998, and benchmarks each city's results against its national database; community-survey firms with their own benchmark databases compete for the same work. They are usually subcontracted by the landscape architecture or planning firm leading the plan, or hired directly by the parks department.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 survey researchers, analysts and fieldwork staff per leading firm (estimate)
**Output:** Needs-assessment survey reports with unmet-need and importance rankings, priority investment ratings and benchmark comparisons — the billable deliverable
**Proprietary data:** Normative databases of resident responses across hundreds of communities, which the firms describe as proprietary; individual city results become public records
**Clock:** Master plan adoption schedules, accreditation cycles, bond referendum dates and grant applications that require an adopted plan
**Buyer:** President or director of research at the survey firm
