# Sustainable Site Rating Systems

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landscape-architecture-firms|Landscape Architecture Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Rating and certification systems for sustainable landscapes and infrastructure — the Sustainable SITES Initiative administered by GBCI, the site credits of LEED, and Envision for infrastructure — publish criteria and review project documentation submitted by landscape architects and engineers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 3-10 staff dedicated to the landscape-specific system, with reviews spread across contracted reviewers (estimate)
**Output:** Certification decisions and rating-system updates
**Proprietary data:** Submitted project documentation
**Clock:** Applicant-driven review cycles
**Buyer:** Programme director
