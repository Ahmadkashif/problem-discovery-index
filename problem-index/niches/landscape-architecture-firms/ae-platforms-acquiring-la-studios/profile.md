# Multidisciplinary A/E Platforms Acquiring Landscape Studios

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landscape-architecture-firms|Landscape Architecture Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Large engineering and design firms and private-equity-backed A/E platforms buy landscape architecture practices to add site-design capability to civil, planning and architecture offerings. ASLA's 2024 report found one in four firms going through an ownership transition, and acquisition by a larger platform is a common exit. Corporate development teams screen targets and integration teams fold their staff and systems in.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 corporate development and integration staff at the larger acquirers, covering all disciplines (estimate)
**Output:** Acquisition decisions and integration plans — internal, not invoiced
**Proprietary data:** Target financials and utilisation data gathered in diligence; the acquirer's own project and billing history
**Clock:** Deal timetables set by sellers and lenders
**Buyer:** Chief development officer
