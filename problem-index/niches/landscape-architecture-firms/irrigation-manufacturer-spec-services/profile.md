# Irrigation Manufacturers' Specification & Design Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landscape-architecture-firms|Landscape Architecture Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Irrigation manufacturers (Rain Bird, Hunter, Toro and others) run specification teams and, in some cases, design services that produce irrigation plans, water-use calculations and specification sections for landscape architects, alongside water-efficiency content aimed at regulators and water agencies. Their aim is a specification written around their controllers, heads and drip products.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-80 specification managers, irrigation designers and water-efficiency staff per major manufacturer (estimate)
**Output:** Irrigation designs, specification sections and water-budget support — mostly free to the specifier, sometimes paid design services
**Proprietary data:** Product performance and distribution-uniformity testing; controller telemetry where smart controllers are connected
**Clock:** Designer permit and bid deadlines; water-efficiency rule changes
**Buyer:** Director of specification or design services
