# A Park Portfolio Record From Plan to Fifth Year

**Niche:** [[niches/landscape-architecture-firms/public-parks-open-space/profile|Public Parks & Open-Space Design]]
**Industry:** [[industries/landscape-architecture-firms|Landscape Architecture Firms]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A firm wins park work on its past parks and cannot show what any of them cost to build, how they bid, how they are used or what they cost to maintain.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #gradient-boosting #feature-engineering #data-integration #revenue-impact #large-language-models
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every competitor is fighting to win the next public RFQ on the strength of the last park — but planning a park system a council will adopt and delivering a capital project that bids within its budget are different contests with different winners. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Selection panels score firms on relevant experience, and every RFQ response shows the same thing: renderings and photos of completed parks. What panels increasingly ask for, and firms cannot produce, is evidence: did the project bid within the estimate, were change orders low, is the park used, did the planting establish, what does it cost the agency to maintain per acre. That information is scattered across the firm's project files, the agency's bid tabulations, its maintenance budgets and, for use, nowhere at all.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Design firms see a project as finished at closeout and are not paid to revisit. The data needed belongs to many parties — bid tabs to the agency's purchasing office, maintenance costs to the parks operations division, use counts to whoever installed counters or bought location data. No vendor sells to design firms on outcomes, and agencies do not think of their own records as a portfolio.

## What to Build
A portfolio record per park: design intent and programme from the plan, engineer's estimate against bid results and final cost, change orders by cause, planting establishment at the warranty walk, maintenance cost per acre from agency budgets where available, and use estimates from counters or anonymised mobility data. Assemble the bid and cost history automatically from public bid tabulations and council packets, and let the firm add its own files. The output is a project sheet with numbers, and a firm-level view of which design types perform.

## Target Customer
Principals and marketing directors at park-focused firms of 10–150 people that compete in qualifications-based selection several times a month.

## Impact If Built
Evidence-based qualifications differentiate a firm in a selection process where every competitor shows the same renderings, and the record tells the firm which of its own design habits cost agencies money.
