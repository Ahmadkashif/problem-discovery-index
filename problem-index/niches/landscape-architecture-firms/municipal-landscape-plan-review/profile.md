# Municipal Landscape Plan Review & Water-Efficiency Enforcement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landscape-architecture-firms|Landscape Architecture Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
City and county planning departments review landscape plans against local ordinances — parking-lot planting, buffers, tree preservation and mitigation — and in California local agencies and water suppliers enforce the Model Water Efficient Landscape Ordinance's documentation package and water budget.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 1-10 landscape reviewers per jurisdiction, often planners rather than landscape architects (estimate)
**Output:** Plan approvals, correction letters, inspections
**Proprietary data:** Permit files and correction letters
**Clock:** Statutory review periods
**Buyer:** Planning director
