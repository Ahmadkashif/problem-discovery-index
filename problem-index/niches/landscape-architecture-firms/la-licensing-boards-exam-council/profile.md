# Landscape Architecture Licensing Boards & Exam Council

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landscape-architecture-firms|Landscape Architecture Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State boards license landscape architects, and the Council of Landscape Architectural Registration Boards (CLARB) administers the four-section LARE that every state relies on. The exam is written by a volunteer Exam Committee of licensed landscape architects in four section sub-committees, led by contracted psychometricians; national pass rates in Spring 2026 ranged from 50% (grading, drainage and stormwater) to 80% (construction documentation and administration).

## Insight Function
**Size:** State boards typically 2-10 staff, often shared with architecture or engineering boards; CLARB's exam work runs through volunteers and contractors rather than a 10-person staff team (estimate)
**Output:** Licences, exam results, disciplinary decisions
**Proprietary data:** Exam item banks and candidate performance data
**Clock:** Exam administration windows
**Buyer:** Executive director
