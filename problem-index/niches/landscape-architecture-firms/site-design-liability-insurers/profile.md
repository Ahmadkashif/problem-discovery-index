# Design Professional Liability Insurers (Site Design Claims)

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landscape-architecture-firms|Landscape Architecture Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Carriers and specialist brokers that write professional liability for landscape architects, usually inside programmes built for architects and engineers. Their claims files hold the industry's best record of how site designs fail — water intrusion from grading toward buildings, ponding and ice on walks, trip-and-fall at grade changes, accessibility complaints, failed retaining walls and bioretention — and their risk-management staff turn it into underwriting rules and loss-prevention guidance. The same carriers are logged under [[niches/engineering-consultants/design-liability-insurers/profile|Design Professional Liability Insurers]]; this entry scores the site-design slice.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 underwriters, claims specialists and risk-management staff across the A/E programmes that cover landscape architects (estimate)
**Output:** Underwriting decisions and loss-prevention publications; the invoice is the premium, not the analysis
**Proprietary data:** Decades of claims files with causes, drawings, expert reports and settlements
**Clock:** Policy renewal dates and litigation schedules
**Buyer:** Head of A/E underwriting or risk management
