# Site Furnishing, Play & Hardscape Manufacturers' Specification Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landscape-architecture-firms|Landscape Architecture Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Manufacturers of site furnishings (Landscape Forms, Victor Stanley), play equipment (PlayCore, Landscape Structures) and segmental paving and walls (Belgard, Unilock) employ specification representatives and small design-assist teams who supply CAD details, specification text and layout help to landscape architects, plus design guides on topics such as inclusive play.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 specification and design-assist staff per large manufacturer, mostly field reps (estimate)
**Output:** Details, specification sections, layouts and design guides — free, in exchange for the specification
**Proprietary data:** Specification and sales records by project and region
**Clock:** Designer bid dates
**Buyer:** Director of specification or A&D marketing
