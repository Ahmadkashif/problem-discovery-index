# A Second Reviewer for the One-Person Firm

**Niche:** [[niches/landscape-architecture-firms/sole-practitioners-micro-firms/profile|Sole Practitioners & Micro-Firms]]
**Industry:** [[industries/landscape-architecture-firms|Landscape Architecture Firms]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A sole practitioner seals a grading plan that no second person has ever looked at, and carries the liability alone.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor serving this niche is fighting to give a one- to four-person practice the second reviewer and the production capacity of a ten-person firm on every stamped set, at a price a sole practitioner can carry, and whoever does takes the account.

## The Problem
In a larger firm, a principal redlines every grading plan, a second person checks the plant schedule against the plan, and someone reads the code tables. A sole practitioner does all of it alone, usually at the end of a long day, and the errors that slip through become a contractor's RFI, a homeowner's flooded patio or a code rejection — each costing more of a small fee than the project can bear.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Checking tools that exist are built into enterprise civil software or sold as consulting peer review. Sole practitioners are fragmented and price-sensitive, and vendors have not seen them as a market.

## What to Build
A low-cost, upload-and-check service: drop in the grading surface or the PDF set and receive a ranked review — drainage toward structures, accessible-route slopes, bed and walk relationships, tie-ins, plant schedule against plan, code table arithmetic for the jurisdiction — phrased the way a senior reviewer would phrase it, trained on captured senior redlines from larger firms.

## Target Customer
Self-employed and one- to four-person landscape architecture practices doing residential, small commercial and sub-consultant work; also small firms' newly licensed staff.

## Impact If Built
A second set of eyes on every stamped set for the cost of a software subscription, cutting the errors that cost small practices their margin and their sleep.
