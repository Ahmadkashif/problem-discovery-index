# Small-Firm Practice Software Fitted to Fixed-Fee Design

**Industry:** [[industries/landscape-architecture-firms|Landscape Architecture Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Practice management tools for design firms assume hourly billing and project managers; a sole landscape architect sells fixed-fee residential and small commercial packages.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #linear-regression #workflow-orchestration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor serving this niche is fighting to give a one- to four-person practice the second reviewer and the production capacity of a ten-person firm on every stamped set, at a price a sole practitioner can carry, and whoever does takes the account.

## The Problem
A sole practitioner quotes a fixed fee for a residential master plan or a small commercial landscape set, then loses track of hours as revisions multiply, permit comments arrive and the client asks for "one more option".

## What Already Exists
BQE Core, Monograph, Deltek Ajera and similar design-firm practice tools; QuickBooks and generic invoicing apps.

## The Customization Gap
The landscape micro-firm layer: fixed-fee package templates by project type with included revision counts; automatic tracking of hours against each package; change-of-scope prompts when a revision exceeds the package; permit status per jurisdiction; and a fee-history view showing which project types make money, so the next quote is grounded in the practitioner's own data.

## Target Customer
Self-employed landscape architects and micro-firms billing under ~$500K a year (estimate).

## Impact If Solved
Quotes based on evidence rather than habit, and scope creep caught when it happens rather than at the end of the job.
