# Professional Society & Foundation Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landscape-architecture-firms|Landscape Architecture Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The American Society of Landscape Architects publishes an industry report from member-firm surveys (the 2024 edition drew on more than 300 firm leaders) and salary and practice data; the Landscape Architecture Foundation publishes case-study briefs measuring the performance of built landscapes.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 3-8 staff on research and surveys across the two organisations (estimate)
**Output:** Member reports, salary surveys and case-study briefs — member benefits, not invoiced products
**Proprietary data:** Member survey responses and a library of measured case studies
**Clock:** Self-set publication schedules
**Buyer:** Chief executive
