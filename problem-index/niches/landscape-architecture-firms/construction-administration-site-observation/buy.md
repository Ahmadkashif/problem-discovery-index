# Construction Platforms Fitted to Landscape Observation

**Niche:** [[niches/landscape-architecture-firms/construction-administration-site-observation/profile|Construction Administration & Site Observation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/landscape-architecture-firms|Landscape Architecture Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Construction management platforms handle RFIs, submittals and punch lists for buildings; landscape observation needs plant counts, nursery standards and a warranty year they do not model.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to catch non-conforming work — the wrong plant, the wrong grade, the wrong paver — before it is buried or planted, with a record that holds up at closeout, and whoever closes out with the fewest disputes takes the account.

## The Problem
On larger projects the landscape architect works inside the GC's or owner's construction platform, where landscape items are forced into building-oriented forms; on smaller ones there is no platform at all.

## What Already Exists
Procore, Autodesk Construction Cloud, Newforma and Fieldwire for RFIs, submittals, observations and punch lists.

## The Customization Gap
The landscape layer needs: punch items tied to plant schedule entries and plan symbols rather than rooms; inspection checklists built from ANSI Z60.1 and the planting specification (caliper, root ball, branching, condition); nursery tagging records linked to the trees that arrive on site; soil and subgrade observation records with test results; and a warranty period that starts at substantial completion with replacement tracking through the one-year walk.

## Target Customer
Landscape architects administering construction on projects already using a GC or owner platform, and agencies standardising landscape CA across consultants.

## Impact If Solved
Landscape observations recorded in the same system as the rest of the project, in terms the planting contractor and the warranty can be held to.
