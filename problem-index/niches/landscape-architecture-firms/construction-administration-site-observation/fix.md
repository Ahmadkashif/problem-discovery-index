# The Warranty Walk Nobody Schedules

**Niche:** [[niches/landscape-architecture-firms/construction-administration-site-observation/profile|Construction Administration & Site Observation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/landscape-architecture-firms|Landscape Architecture Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Planting is warranted for a year after substantial completion, and the inspection that would enforce it is missed because nobody tracks the date.
**Tags:** #survival-analysis #descriptive-statistics #workflow-orchestration #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to catch non-conforming work — the wrong plant, the wrong grade, the wrong paver — before it is buried or planted, with a record that holds up at closeout, and whoever closes out with the fewest disputes takes the account.

## The Problem
Planting specifications commonly require the contractor to replace dead or unhealthy plants within a warranty period, often one year (estimate of common practice). A year after closeout the design team has moved on, the owner's facilities staff may not know the warranty exists, and the walk either never happens or happens after the period has lapsed. Owners pay for replacements the contractor owed.

## Why It's Still Broken
The warranty start date lives in a substantial completion certificate; no system turns it into a scheduled task, and the landscape architect's CA fee often ended at closeout.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Create the warranty record at substantial completion with the plant inventory from the as-built schedule, schedule reminders to owner and designer, provide a walk checklist on a phone that compares observed plants to the inventory, and issue the replacement list to the contractor with photos. The replacement data becomes the firm's first plant-survival record.

## Who Feels the Pain
Owners and park agencies paying for replacements they were owed; landscape architects whose design looks failed because nobody enforced the warranty.

## Impact If Fixed
Replacements recovered at no cost to owners, and a survival record the firm can learn from.
