# Field Observation That Writes Its Own Report

**Niche:** [[niches/landscape-architecture-firms/construction-administration-site-observation/profile|Construction Administration & Site Observation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/landscape-architecture-firms|Landscape Architecture Firms]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A landscape architect walks a site taking eighty photos and voice notes, then spends the evening turning them into a field report and a punch list nobody can map back to the plan.
**Tags:** #cnns #object-detection #large-language-models #transformers #worker-facing #automation #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to catch non-conforming work — the wrong plant, the wrong grade, the wrong paver — before it is buried or planted, with a record that holds up at closeout, and whoever closes out with the fewest disputes takes the account.

## The Problem
A site visit produces photographs, scribbled notes on a printed plan and comments spoken to the contractor's foreman. The report that follows — what was observed, what does not conform, what must be corrected by when — is written from memory hours later. Punch lists for planting can run to hundreds of items (wrong cultivar, undersized tree, girdling roots, mulch volcanoes, missing plants) and are typed by hand.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Construction field apps are built for GCs managing trades, not for a designer observing conformance; they do not know planting specifications or nursery standards. Landscape CA is a small fee line and nobody has targeted it.

## What to Build
A mobile capture tool that geolocates each photo and voice note to the plan sheet, transcribes notes, proposes the matching specification clause and plant schedule entry, recognises common planting defects in photos (root flare buried, staking left on, dead or missing plants against the plan count), and assembles the field report and punch list for review before sending. Items carry through to back-check visits and closeout.

## Target Customer
Project landscape architects at firms of 5–100 people doing several site visits a week; owner-side landscape architects at campuses and agencies.

## Impact If Built
A report that took an evening drafted before leaving the site, punch items that cannot be lost, and a geolocated record that settles closeout disputes.
