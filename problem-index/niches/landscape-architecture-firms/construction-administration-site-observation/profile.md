# Construction Administration & Site Observation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landscape-architecture-firms|Landscape Architecture Firms]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to catch non-conforming work — the wrong plant, the wrong grade, the wrong paver — before it is buried or planted, with a record that holds up at closeout, and whoever closes out with the fewest disputes takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$700M a year of fee hours spent on submittal review, RFIs, nursery tagging, site visits, field reports, punch lists and warranty walks (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of fee hours (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — field notes on paper or phone photos, reports written later in Word, submittals logged in spreadsheets at most small firms
**Target Buyer:** Project landscape architect or construction administration lead
**Automation Potential:** High for report writing, submittal checking and record keeping; low for field judgement

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Construction administration is the phase where design intent meets the contractor's material and the site's reality. The landscape architect reviews submittals and substitutions, visits the nursery to tag specimen trees, observes subgrade and soil installation, checks grades before paving, inspects planting for size and quality against ANSI Z60.1, issues field reports and punch lists, certifies substantial completion and returns for the one-year warranty walk. It is billed as a limited fee or hourly, it is where most disputes are born, and it runs on photographs and memory.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Procore, Newforma and Autodesk Construction Cloud on larger jobs (usually the GC's or owner's instance); Bluebeam for markups; phone cameras; Word templates for field reports. Gaps: observations not tied to plan locations, punch lists retyped from notes, no link from a field finding back to the specification clause, and warranty obligations tracked nowhere.
