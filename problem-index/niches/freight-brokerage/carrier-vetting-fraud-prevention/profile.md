# Carrier Vetting & Freight Fraud Prevention

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/freight-brokerage|Freight Brokerage]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Highway, Carrier Assure, and the vetting providers verify that a carrier accepting a load is who it claims to be — detecting identity theft, double brokering, and the organized rings that steal full truckloads by impersonating legitimate motor carriers. A broker tendering freight to a fraudulent carrier owns the loss, and the determination has to be made in the minutes before a load moves.

Distinct from the carrier safety scoring vendors logged under `charter-bus-operators`, which score compliance from public federal data; this pocket answers a different and newer question — whether the entity on the other end of the phone is real.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 fraud analysts, investigators, and data scientists
**Output:** Carrier identity verification, fraud risk determinations, network alerts
**Proprietary data:** Identity and behavioural signals joined to confirmed fraud outcomes across a broker network — a record no individual broker can assemble and that the public federal data does not contain
**Clock:** Loads tender and move within hours, and a fraudulent pickup is unrecoverable
**Buyer:** Chief Product Officer / VP of Risk
