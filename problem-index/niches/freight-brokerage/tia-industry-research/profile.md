# Brokerage Association Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/freight-brokerage|Freight Brokerage]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Transportation Intermediaries Association and the trucking associations produce operating benchmarks, economic outlooks, and the regulatory analysis behind advocacy on broker liability, fraud, and capacity policy.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 economists, researchers, and policy staff
**Output:** Operating benchmarks, economic outlooks, regulatory analysis
**Proprietary data:** Member-contributed operating and financial data
**Clock:** Publication schedules and legislative sessions
**Buyer:** Chief Economist / VP of Research
