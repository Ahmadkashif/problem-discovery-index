# Freight Market Forecasting Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/freight-brokerage|Freight Brokerage]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
FTR, ACT Research, and the transportation forecasting houses publish the capacity, rate, and freight volume outlooks that brokers, carriers, and equipment makers plan against — modelling truck orders, driver supply, and freight demand into forward quarters.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 economists, analysts, and researchers
**Output:** Capacity and rate forecasts, equipment order tracking, freight demand outlooks — the subscription
**Proprietary data:** Long-running equipment order and capacity series, plus the firm's own forecast archive
**Clock:** Publication cadence and client planning cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Economist / Director of Research
