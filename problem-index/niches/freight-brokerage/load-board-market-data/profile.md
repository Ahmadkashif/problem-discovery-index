# Load Board & Freight Rate Market Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/freight-brokerage|Freight Brokerage]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
DAT, Truckstop, and the load boards observe posted loads and available capacity continuously, derive spot rate benchmarks from transactions flowing through them, and sell the market intelligence brokers price against on every load.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 market analysts, data scientists, and rate operations staff
**Output:** Spot rate benchmarks, capacity and demand indices, lane analytics — the subscription
**Proprietary data:** Posted load and transaction data across a large share of the US spot market
**Clock:** Loads price and move within hours
**Buyer:** Chief Analytics Officer / Head of Market Intelligence
