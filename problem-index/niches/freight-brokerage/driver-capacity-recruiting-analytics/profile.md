# Driver Capacity & Recruiting Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/freight-brokerage|Freight Brokerage]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Recruiting platforms and driver market analytics vendors track pay rates, turnover, and applicant supply across the driver labour market, and sell the intelligence carriers use to set pay and brokers use to anticipate capacity.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 analysts, data scientists, and researchers
**Output:** Pay benchmarks, turnover and supply analytics, recruiting performance data
**Proprietary data:** Applicant flow and hiring outcome data across carriers, joined to advertised pay
**Clock:** Recruiting cycles and seasonal capacity swings
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Head of Market Research
