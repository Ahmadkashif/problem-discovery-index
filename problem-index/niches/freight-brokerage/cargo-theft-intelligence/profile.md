# Cargo Theft Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/freight-brokerage|Freight Brokerage]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
CargoNet, Verisk's cargo theft unit, and the industry intelligence services collect and analyze theft incidents, publish pattern alerts by commodity, lane, and method, and support law enforcement referrals in a loss category that has grown sharply with strategic theft and fictitious pickup.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 analysts and investigators
**Output:** Theft incident intelligence, pattern alerts, recovery support
**Proprietary data:** Contributed theft incident reports joined to method, location, and recovery outcomes across the industry
**Clock:** Active theft rings and recovery windows measured in hours
**Buyer:** Director of Intelligence / VP of Risk Products
