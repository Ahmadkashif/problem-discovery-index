# Freight Factoring Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/freight-brokerage|Freight Brokerage]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Freight factors advance carriers against their invoices and collect from brokers and shippers, underwriting both the carrier and the debtor. They see invoice-level detail across a very large share of small carriers and know which brokers pay and which do not.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 credit analysts, underwriters, and collections staff
**Output:** Advance underwriting, debtor credit assessment, portfolio analytics
**Proprietary data:** Invoice-level freight transactions joined to payment behaviour across thousands of carriers and their broker customers
**Clock:** Same-day funding decisions and invoice aging
**Buyer:** Chief Credit Officer / VP Analytics
