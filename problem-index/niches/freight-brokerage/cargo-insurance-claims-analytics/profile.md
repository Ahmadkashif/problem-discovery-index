# Cargo Insurance & Claims Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/freight-brokerage|Freight Brokerage]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Cargo underwriters and claims specialists price freight loss exposure, adjudicate damage and theft claims, and research which lanes, commodities, and handling arrangements actually produce losses.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 underwriters, claims analysts, and risk engineers
**Output:** Rating models, claim determinations, loss prevention guidance
**Proprietary data:** Cargo claim experience by commodity, lane, and carrier
**Clock:** Renewal cycles and claim adjustment deadlines
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
