# Supply Chain Visibility Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/freight-brokerage|Freight Brokerage]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
project44, FourKites, and the visibility platforms track shipments in transit across carriers and modes, predict arrival times, and publish the network congestion and transit performance benchmarks the industry watches.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 data scientists, network engineers, and analysts
**Output:** Arrival predictions, transit performance analytics, network congestion reporting
**Proprietary data:** Real-time shipment position and event data across carriers, shippers, and modes at a scale no individual participant sees
**Clock:** Shipments move continuously; predictions are consumed in real time
**Buyer:** VP of Data Science / Chief Product Officer
