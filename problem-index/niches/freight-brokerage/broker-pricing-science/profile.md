# Broker Pricing Science

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/freight-brokerage|Freight Brokerage]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The pricing and data science organizations at C.H. Robinson, RXO, and the large brokers quote loads in seconds against a market that reprices hourly, and manage the spread between what a shipper pays and what a carrier accepts — which is the entire business model.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 data scientists, pricing analysts, and engineers
**Output:** Quote pricing models, carrier cost prediction, margin management
**Proprietary data:** Quote, booking, and carrier acceptance data across millions of loads — the deepest observed record of what carriers actually take on any given lane at any given hour
**Clock:** Loads price and move within hours
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Pricing Science
