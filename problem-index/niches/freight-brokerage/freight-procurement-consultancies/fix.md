# Every Bid Is Designed From Scratch

**One-liner:** The firm has run hundreds of bids across the same lanes, seasons, and carrier sets, and each new engagement starts by rebuilding the lane package, the constraint set, and the carrier list from the client's data.

## The Problem
Bid design is where the expertise sits — how to package lanes so carriers can bid them efficiently, which constraints to impose, which carriers to invite for a given freight profile, how to structure volume commitments. Those choices materially affect the result, and they are made per engagement by whoever is leading it, from the client's data and their own experience. The firm has done this hundreds of times, frequently on overlapping lanes and carrier sets, and none of it is retrievable as design knowledge. Engagements are archived by client. So consultants re-derive packaging decisions colleagues have made, invite carrier sets built from personal familiarity, and the firm cannot say whether its own bid designs are consistent or which design choices are associated with better outcomes.

## Why It's Still Broken
Engagements are confidential and the instinct is to seal them entirely, when the transferable content — how a lane package was structured, which constraint patterns were used — is method rather than client data. The analysis lives in spreadsheets and platform configurations where the reasoning is implicit. And bid season is intensely deadline-driven, which makes structuring anything for reuse lose to shipping the current engagement.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A bid design record capturing method rather than client particulars: how lanes were packaged and why, the constraint structure, the carrier invitation logic, the award approach, and the rationale for each — recorded as the design is built. Indexed by freight profile and lane characteristics rather than by client, so a consultant starting an engagement sees how the firm has structured comparable bids before. Where post-award outcomes are captured, design choices join to results, which turns packaging and constraint decisions from craft into evidence — the firm can say which structures produced durable guides in which market conditions. Carrier invitation lists become an institutional asset rather than a personal network, which is the specific thing that limits how quickly a new consultant becomes productive. And consistency across the book becomes measurable, which matters because two clients in the same industry currently receive designs that differ for no recorded reason.

## Who Feels the Pain
Consultants rebuilding design decisions the firm has already made; practice leaders whose capacity is capped by senior consultant headcount during a compressed bid season; clients whose outcome depends on which consultant they drew; and the firm, whose most valuable method is undocumented and leaves with people.

## Impact If Fixed
Compresses design time in a business where the season is short and capacity-bound, and converts the differentiating expertise from personal to institutional. Joined to post-award outcomes, it also turns bid design into the only evidence-based practice in a field that currently runs entirely on experience.
