# Freight Procurement & Bid Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/freight-brokerage|Freight Brokerage]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Chainalytics, the managed transportation practices, and the specialist procurement firms design and run shippers' annual freight bid events — building the lane package, modelling award scenarios, analyzing carrier responses, and producing the routing guide the shipper operates under for the next year. The analysis is the deliverable and it sets what a shipper pays for transportation.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 procurement analysts, network modellers, and consultants
**Output:** Bid design and analysis, award optimization, routing guide construction
**Proprietary data:** Bid response and award outcomes across many shippers, lanes, and cycles — the record of what carriers actually accept, and at what price, under which award structures
**Clock:** Bid event calendars and contract start dates, both fixed
**Buyer:** Practice leader / Managing Director
