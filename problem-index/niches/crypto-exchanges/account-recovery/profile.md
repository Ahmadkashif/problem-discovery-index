# Account Recovery

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/crypto-exchanges|Crypto Exchanges]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a locked-out customer back to their funds without opening the door to the attacker running the same play — and whoever resolves the honest cases automatically without raising the takeover rate clears the queue that dominates support.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.0B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — manual review queues
**Target Buyer:** Support and risk leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — a risk decision with rich behavioural evidence

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The support queue is dominated by locked accounts and missing deposits. A customer lost their device, changed their number, failed a verification step, or triggered a security hold. Every one of those is also exactly what an account takeover looks like, so each is reviewed by a person against evidence that is thin and inconsistently weighed. The contest is a risk decision with asymmetric costs on both sides, made thousands of times, almost entirely by hand.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Support ticket queues, identity re-verification, cooling-off periods and manual review. The gaps: behavioural history not used as evidence; no risk scoring on the recovery request itself; recovery outcomes never measured against takeover rates; queue unprioritised; and the customer given no visibility or timeline.
