# Account Takeover Defence

**Niche:** [[niches/crypto-exchanges/account-recovery/profile|Account Recovery]]
**Industry:** [[industries/crypto-exchanges|Crypto Exchanges]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Account takeover defence is a mature field built around reversible losses and a bank that can claw money back, and crypto withdrawals are final.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #logistic-regression #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #compliance #automation #data-integration #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a locked-out customer back to their funds without opening the door to the attacker running the same play — and whoever resolves the honest cases automatically without raising the takeover rate clears the queue that dominates support.

## The Problem
Account takeover defence is well developed: device fingerprinting, behavioural biometrics, risk-based authentication, step-up challenges, and recovery flows tuned against measured takeover and friction rates. All of it evolved in contexts where a successful takeover produces a reversible transaction and an institution that can restore the funds. A crypto withdrawal cannot be reversed, which changes every threshold in the system.

## What Already Exists
Device and behavioural risk engines; risk-based authentication and step-up flows; recovery workflows with graduated verification; takeover rate measurement; and friction-versus-loss tuning methodologies.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to irreversible loss. It requires: (1) thresholds set against unrecoverable loss rather than against a reversible one, which shifts the entire tuning calculus and is the substantive adaptation; (2) delay as the primary mitigation, since a withdrawal hold is the only real second chance and the category treats friction as purely a cost; (3) the account's asset balance as a risk input, because exposure varies by orders of magnitude between accounts in a way retail banking's does not; (4) an attacker population that is sophisticated, well resourced and specifically targeting this asset class; and (5) recovery evidence that must satisfy a compliance obligation as well as a security one, which retail flows do not carry.

## Target Customer
Exchange support and risk leadership, identity and takeover vendors entering the asset class, and insurers pricing custody risk.

## Impact If Solved
Every threshold in the mature discipline assumes a reversible loss. Retuning recovery and step-up against irreversibility, with delay as the core mitigation, is the adaptation the category has not made.
