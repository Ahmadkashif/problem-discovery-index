# The Blockchain Analyst

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/crypto-exchanges|Crypto Exchanges]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make one analyst's hour of hop-by-hop tracing produce a defensible conclusion instead of a narrative — and whoever gives them the tooling to work at the case level rather than the hop level changes what the function can cover.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.3B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — vendor graph interfaces and manual narrative
**Target Buyer:** Compliance operations leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — tracing, assembly and drafting

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The analyst sits in a vendor's graph interface and traces funds backwards one hop at a time, deciding at each step which branch to follow, then writes a narrative for a report about a person they will never hear from and will never learn anything more about. The work is repetitive, cognitively heavy, unrewarded by feedback, and the entire compliance posture rests on it. This is a distinct audience with a distinct pain: skilled judgement applied through an interface that makes them do the mechanical part by hand.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Vendor graph explorers, case management, narrative templates, and a filing workflow. The gaps: tracing paths explored manually rather than enumerated; case evidence assembled by copy and paste; narratives written from scratch each time; no feedback on whether a judgement was right; and no measure of analyst throughput or consistency.
