# Investigation Tooling From Financial Crime

**Industry:** [[industries/crypto-exchanges|Crypto Exchanges]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Financial crime investigation tooling has case management, quality assurance and filing workflow that blockchain analytics never adopted.
**Tags:** #compliance #graph-theory #workflow-orchestration #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #large-language-models #descriptive-statistics #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make one analyst's hour of hop-by-hop tracing produce a defensible conclusion instead of a narrative — and whoever gives them the tooling to work at the case level rather than the hop level changes what the function can cover.

## The Problem
Bank financial crime functions have case management with structured evidence, quality assurance sampling with feedback to analysts, standardised narrative templates, filing workflow with review, and productivity and consistency measurement. Blockchain analytics built extraordinary graph tooling and none of the surrounding operational apparatus, so the analyst has the best tracing interface in the world sitting next to a spreadsheet and a document template.

## What Already Exists
Case management with structured evidence capture; quality assurance sampling and analyst feedback loops; narrative templates and filing workflow; investigation productivity measurement; and escalation and review structures.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to evidence that is a graph traversal rather than a set of transactions. It requires: (1) the traversal path itself as case evidence, since the conclusion depends on which branches were followed and no case management system has a shape for that — this is the substantive adaptation; (2) quality assurance on a judgement whose correctness is unknowable, so consistency between analysts substitutes for accuracy; (3) a counterparty who is an address rather than a person, changing what the narrative can assert; (4) public data that a reviewer can independently re-derive, which is an opportunity the banking apparatus never had; and (5) no customer contact, removing the clarification step that resolves many bank cases.

## Target Customer
Compliance operations leadership, analytics vendors whose products end at the graph, and financial crime tooling vendors for whom on-chain casework is unserved.

## Impact If Solved
The tracing interface is world-class and the operational apparatus around it does not exist. Treating the traversal path as case evidence is the piece both categories are missing.
