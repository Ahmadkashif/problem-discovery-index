# Token Listing Diligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/crypto-exchanges|Crypto Exchanges]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to assess an asset's code, distribution, team, legal characterisation and market integrity faster and more accurately than the venue that listed it first — and whoever does it without redoing the same analysis every other exchange already did takes the listing advantage.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.4B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — manual analysis per asset per venue
**Target Buyer:** Listings and legal leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — structured assessment over public artefacts

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Listing an asset means assessing its contract code, its token distribution and concentration, its team and their history, its legal characterisation, its liquidity and market integrity, and the operational work of supporting it. Every exchange does this independently for the same assets, mostly by hand, mostly from the same public artefacts, and mostly without a record of what the previous assessment concluded or whether it was right.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Legal memoranda, security audit reports, analyst research, and a listing committee. The gaps: no structured assessment framework; distribution concentration not computed systematically; delisting outcomes never fed back into listing criteria; the same public artefacts read repeatedly; and no post-listing monitoring of the assumptions the listing rested on.
