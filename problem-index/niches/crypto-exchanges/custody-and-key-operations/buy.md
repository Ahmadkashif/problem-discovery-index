**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Securities custody has a century of segregation rules, independent verification and reconciliation discipline, built around a custodian whose records are the asset.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to prove continuously that customer assets exist, are controlled, and are not encumbered — without revealing the operational detail that would help an attacker — and whoever proves it best takes the institutional accounts.

## The Problem
Securities custody solved asset safeguarding as an institutional discipline: client asset segregation with daily computation, independent custodians, depositories as a system of record, reconciliation between the custodian and the broker, rehypothecation limits and disclosure, and examination against all of it. Crypto exchanges custody assets with world-class cryptography and a governance model assembled ad hoc.

## What Already Exists
Client asset segregation and daily safeguarding computations; independent custodian structures; depository reconciliation; rehypothecation rules with disclosure; and examination programmes covering all of it.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to bearer assets where possession is control and the ledger is public. It requires: (1) proof by cryptographic demonstration rather than by a custodian's books, since the whole point of the asset class is that possession is provable and the discipline has no instrument for it — this is the substantive adaptation and inverts the usual reliance on records; (2) irreversibility, which means the segregation control must prevent rather than detect and reverse; (3) no depository as an independent system of record, removing the discipline's structural check; (4) key compromise as a loss mode with no equivalent in securities custody; and (5) public visibility of the custodian's own holdings, which enables verification the securities world never contemplated and leaks operational detail it never had to protect.

## Target Customer
Exchange custody leadership, institutional allocators applying custody standards, auditors bridging the two worlds, and custody technology vendors entering digital assets.

## Impact If Solved
The discipline relies on a custodian's records and the asset class makes possession directly provable. Rebuilding segregation and safeguarding around cryptographic demonstration rather than bookkeeping is the adaptation and no established framework has it.
