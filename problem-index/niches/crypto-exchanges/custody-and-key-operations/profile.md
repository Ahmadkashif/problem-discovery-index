# Custody & Key Operations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/crypto-exchanges|Crypto Exchanges]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to prove continuously that customer assets exist, are controlled, and are not encumbered — without revealing the operational detail that would help an attacker — and whoever proves it best takes the institutional accounts.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3.2B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~21% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** High — sophisticated key management, weak proof
**Target Buyer:** Exchange security and custody leadership
**Automation Potential:** Moderate — continuous attestation and anomaly detection

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Custody is where an exchange either survives or does not. Keys are held across hardware modules, multi-party computation schemes and cold storage, moved by signing ceremonies with human approvals, and the failure modes range from an insider to a supply chain compromise to a lost quorum. Layered on top is the demand the collapses of 2022 created: prove the assets are there. The contest is proof under adversarial conditions — demonstrating solvency and control continuously without publishing a map of the vault.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Hardware security modules, MPC signing, cold storage procedures, periodic proof-of-reserves attestations and third-party audits. The gaps: reserves proved at a point in time rather than continuously; liabilities attested by an auditor rather than proved cryptographically; encumbrance and rehypothecation invisible; key operations audited after the fact; and no detection of the signing pattern that precedes a compromise.
