# Tax Basis & Reporting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/crypto-exchanges|Crypto Exchanges]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to establish a defensible cost basis for assets that arrived from somewhere else with nothing attached — and whoever reconstructs basis most accurately files the fewest wrong forms under a regime that now makes it a legal obligation.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.7B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~11% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — spreadsheets and customer-supplied data
**Target Buyer:** Exchange tax and product leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — inference over transfer and market data

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A customer transfers coins in from a wallet, another exchange, a staking service, an airdrop or a friend. The exchange must eventually report the gain on disposal and has no idea what the customer paid or when. Under the 1099-DA regime this stopped being a customer inconvenience and became a filing obligation with penalties attached. The contest is reconstructing basis from what is knowable — transfer provenance, on-chain history, market prices, customer attestation — well enough to file.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Customer-supplied basis, third-party tax calculation tools, spreadsheets, and defaulting to zero basis where nothing is known. The gaps: no inference from on-chain provenance; transfers between an exchange's own customers not matched; basis quality not scored; customer attestation unstructured; and no reconciliation across exchanges where the same transfer is both an outflow and an inflow.
