# Screening Decision Quality

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/crypto-exchanges|Crypto Exchanges]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to measure and defend a freeze decision whose ground truth returns on a fraction of a percent of cases — and whoever can state a precision with a defensible interval sets the threshold everyone else guesses at.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.8B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — thresholds unmeasured
**Target Buyer:** Exchange compliance and model risk leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — evaluation under extreme label scarcity

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This contest is evaluation and adjudication. Given an attribution of whatever quality, the exchange must decide whether to freeze, how to justify it, how long to hold, how to let the customer contest it, and — the question nobody has answered — how well the decision performs. Ground truth arrives on a tiny fraction of cases, which makes this a measurement problem under extreme label scarcity rather than a graph inference problem. Different evidence, different methods, different owner.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Threshold rules, case management, analyst review and suspicious activity reporting. The gaps: precision never estimated; no deliberate labelling regime; no cost stated for a false freeze; no adjudication route for the customer; and no feedback from the rare resolved cases back into the threshold.
