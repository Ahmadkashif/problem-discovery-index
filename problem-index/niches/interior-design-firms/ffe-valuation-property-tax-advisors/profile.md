# Hospitality FF&E Valuation & Personal Property Tax Advisors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Valuation firms' machinery-and-equipment and personal property practices value the furniture, fixtures and equipment that interior designers specify: for business personal property tax renditions, for purchase price allocation when a hotel or office portfolio changes hands, and for insurance replacement cost. Hotel owners, REITs and acquirers pay a fee per engagement. The work sits next to the real-property appeal and hotel valuation pockets already logged under [[niches/commercial-real-estate/property-tax-appeal-consultancies/profile|🔍 Property Tax Appeal Consultancies]] and [[niches/hotels-boutique/hotel-valuation-feasibility-firms/profile|🔍 Hotel Valuation & Feasibility Firms]], but covers the FF&E layer those leave out.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 appraisers and analysts in the personal property practices of the larger valuation firms (estimate)
**Output:** FF&E appraisals, rendition support, purchase price allocation reports, insurable value reports
**Proprietary data:** Inventories and condition records from past engagements, plus depreciation and cost observations, though much of the cost basis comes from published cost services
**Clock:** Annual personal property rendition deadlines, transaction closings, insurance renewals
**Buyer:** Practice leader, personal property and FF&E valuation
