# Contract Furniture Dealer Design & Specification Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Dealers representing manufacturers such as Steelcase, MillerKnoll, Haworth and Teknion employ designers who lay out workstations and seating, produce bills of materials and SIF order files, and check layouts against accessibility and life-safety rules. The design work is given away to win the furniture order.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 designers and specification staff at large dealers (estimate)
**Output:** Furniture layouts, BOMs and orders; no billable analysis
**Proprietary data:** Layout and order histories by client and site
**Clock:** Move-in and lease commencement dates
**Buyer:** Dealer's director of design services
