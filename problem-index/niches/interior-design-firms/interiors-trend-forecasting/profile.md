# Interiors Trend & Colour Forecasting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Forecasting services that publish interiors, materials and colour trend research for manufacturers, retailers and design firms, sold by subscription; WGSN's interiors coverage and paint and colour companies' forecasting programmes are the main examples. Manufacturers use the forecasts to plan collections; designers use them for client presentations.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 forecasters and analysts on the interiors side of the larger services (estimate)
**Output:** Seasonal trend reports, colour forecasts, materials outlooks
**Proprietary data:** Archives of past forecasts, trade-show and retail observation, some consumer and search data
**Clock:** Trade calendars set by others — Milan Design Week, High Point Market in April and October, Maison&Objet — and manufacturers' collection cycles
**Buyer:** Head of interiors forecasting
