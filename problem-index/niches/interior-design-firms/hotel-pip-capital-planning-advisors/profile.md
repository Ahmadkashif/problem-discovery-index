# Hotel PIP & Capital Planning Advisors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Consultancies that negotiate property improvement plans with hotel brands on behalf of owners and acquirers, scope and price the renovation, and then retain and manage the design and purchasing firms that deliver it. The Litt Group is a representative example. Owners pay for PIP negotiation and capital planning, usually ahead of an acquisition or franchise renewal. The engineering side of the same due diligence is logged under [[niches/commercial-real-estate/property-condition-assessment-firms/profile|🔍 Property Condition Assessment Firms]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 in the larger hospitality capital-project consultancies; many are smaller (estimate; no firm's team size was found)
**Output:** PIP scope and cost estimates, negotiation positions with brands, capital plans
**Proprietary data:** Cost per key and scope outcomes from the renovations they have managed
**Clock:** Brand PIP deadlines, acquisition closings and franchise transfers
**Buyer:** Hotel owner's asset manager; within the consultancy, the founder or managing principal
