# A Verified Trade Product Graph

**Niche:** [[niches/interior-design-firms/product-spec-and-tearsheets/profile|Product Specification & Tearsheet Production]]
**Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Thousands of designers clip the same products from the same manufacturers every day, and each copy is incomplete in a slightly different way.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #graph-neural-networks #contrastive-learning #feature-engineering #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn any product page, catalogue PDF or showroom photo into a complete, accurate specification line — dimensions, finish options, trade price, lead time and performance data — in one click, and whoever has the most accurate product data takes the designer's clipping habit.

## The Problem
A product clipped from a manufacturer's website lacks the trade price, the finish and fabric codes, the COM yardage, and the lead time. The designer fills these in from a PDF price list or an email to the rep. The next designer clipping the same product repeats the work.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Manufacturers publish trade data in PDFs and gated portals; studio-software vendors keep clipped data inside each customer's account; and nobody has had the incentive to reconcile it into one record.

## What to Build
A product graph assembled from manufacturer sites, price lists, spec sheets and consented clipping activity: each product with its finish and fabric options as linked entities, dimensions, performance data, trade price by programme and observed lead times. Visual matching links duplicates across retailers and catalogues. Clippers in any studio tool resolve to the verified record.

## Target Customer
Studio-software vendors licensing the data, and manufacturers paying for accurate representation to designers.

## Impact If Built
Specification lines arrive complete in one click, cutting product-entry time substantially per project and removing the errors that become wrong orders.
