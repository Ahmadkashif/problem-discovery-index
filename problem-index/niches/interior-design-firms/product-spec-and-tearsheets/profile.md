# Product Specification & Tearsheet Production

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn any product page, catalogue PDF or showroom photo into a complete, accurate specification line — dimensions, finish options, trade price, lead time and performance data — in one click, and whoever has the most accurate product data takes the designer's clipping habit.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$250M US spend on product clipping, specification and product-data tools and services used by designers (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** touches nearly every firm; ~2% of revenue as tool and labour cost (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — browser clippers exist; the data they capture is incomplete
**Target Buyer:** Studio principal; on the supply side, manufacturers' marketing and trade-sales leads
**Automation Potential:** Very high

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every selection becomes a specification line and a tearsheet: image, dimensions, materials, finish and fabric options, price, lead time and, for commercial work, performance ratings. Designers build hundreds per project by clipping web pages and copying from PDFs; Material Bank's founder said designers spent up to 39% of their day searching for and ordering products. Studio-software clippers capture what is on the page; trade prices, finish codes and lead times usually are not on the page.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Browser clippers in Studio Designer, Houzz Pro, DesignFiles and Programa; Material Bank for samples; manufacturer trade portals; PDF price lists. Gaps: trade price and finish codes missing from clipped data; stale prices and lead times; no shared, verified product record across firms.
