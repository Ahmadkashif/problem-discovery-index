# Retail Product Information Adapted to Trade Specification

**Niche:** [[niches/interior-design-firms/product-spec-and-tearsheets/profile|Product Specification & Tearsheet Production]]
**Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Product information management systems already feed retailers clean product data; trade specification needs fields retail feeds never carry.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #feature-engineering #data-integration #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn any product page, catalogue PDF or showroom photo into a complete, accurate specification line — dimensions, finish options, trade price, lead time and performance data — in one click, and whoever has the most accurate product data takes the designer's clipping habit.

## The Problem
Manufacturers maintain product data for retail channels and leave trade data in PDFs.

## What Already Exists
Product information management platforms such as Akeneo and Salsify, and retail product feeds to marketplaces.

## The Customization Gap
Trade specification needs finish and fabric options as configurable attributes with codes; trade and net pricing by programme; COM and COL yardage; performance ratings (flammability, double rubs, cleanability); lead times by configuration; and a publishing channel into designers' clippers and Material Bank rather than retail marketplaces.

## Target Customer
To-the-trade furniture, lighting and textile manufacturers already running a retail PIM.

## Impact If Solved
Manufacturers publish trade-ready data once, and designers stop transcribing PDFs.
