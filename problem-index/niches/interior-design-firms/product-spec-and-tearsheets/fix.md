# The Tearsheet With Last Year's Price

**Niche:** [[niches/interior-design-firms/product-spec-and-tearsheets/profile|Product Specification & Tearsheet Production]]
**Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A product clipped months ago carries its old price and lead time into a proposal, and the studio eats the difference when the order is placed.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #feature-engineering #automation #revenue-impact #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn any product page, catalogue PDF or showroom photo into a complete, accurate specification line — dimensions, finish options, trade price, lead time and performance data — in one click, and whoever has the most accurate product data takes the designer's clipping habit.

## The Problem
Designers keep libraries of favourite products. Manufacturers raise prices, often more than once a year, and discontinue finishes. Libraries do not update, so proposals go out stale.

## Why It's Still Broken
A clipped record is a snapshot with no link back to a live source, and price increases arrive as PDF notices to a rep's mailing list.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Re-validate every library item and proposal line against current manufacturer data before a proposal is sent, flag price, lead-time and discontinuation changes, and read manufacturers' price-increase notices into the library automatically.

## Who Feels the Pain
Studio principals absorbing price differences; designers re-quoting clients.

## Impact If Fixed
Proposals go out at current prices, protecting margin on every line clipped more than a few months before.
