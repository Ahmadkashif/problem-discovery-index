# From Occupancy Data to a Setting Mix

**Niche:** [[niches/interior-design-firms/workplace-utilization-strategy/profile|Workplace Utilisation & Space Strategy]]
**Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Occupiers collect months of occupancy data and still decide how many desks and rooms to build by negotiation between departments.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #monte-carlo-methods #probability-distributions #gradient-boosting #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to prove, with measured occupancy rather than surveys and opinion, how much space and what mix of settings an occupier actually needs, and whoever produces the most defensible right-sizing recommendation wins the redesign.

## The Problem
Average utilisation is the wrong number for design. A floor at 40% average occupancy may be full every Tuesday. Meeting rooms booked for eight hold two people. Designers need peak-day demand by setting type and team, with a service level the occupier chooses.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Sensor vendors sell dashboards to facility managers; workplace strategists build bespoke spreadsheets per engagement; and joining badge, booking and sensor data at setting level takes data engineering a design firm does not have.

## What to Build
Ingest badge, booking and sensor data; model daily and team-level demand with seasonality and attendance-policy scenarios; simulate how many desks, focus rooms and meeting rooms of each size meet a chosen service level; and output a program the designer can lay out, with the evidence and the scenario assumptions visible to the occupier's leadership.

## Target Customer
Workplace strategy leads at interiors firms and heads of workplace at occupiers with 500+ employees per site.

## Impact If Built
Right-sizing decisions rest on demand curves rather than department negotiation, typically shrinking or reshaping space at renewal with confidence that peak days still work.
