# Occupancy Analytics Adapted to Design Decisions

**Niche:** [[niches/interior-design-firms/workplace-utilization-strategy/profile|Workplace Utilisation & Space Strategy]]
**Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Occupancy platforms tell a facility manager how busy the building was; a designer needs to know what to build next.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #descriptive-statistics #k-means-clustering #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to prove, with measured occupancy rather than surveys and opinion, how much space and what mix of settings an occupier actually needs, and whoever produces the most defensible right-sizing recommendation wins the redesign.

## The Problem
Occupiers already license sensors and analytics, and hand designers exported dashboards that answer operational questions.

## What Already Exists
Density, VergeSense, Basking and desk-booking systems with utilisation dashboards; workplace-experience surveys.

## The Customization Gap
The design adaptation needs setting-level peak demand rather than averages; team clustering by attendance pattern to inform neighbourhood planning; scenario controls for attendance policies; and exports that map directly to a space program and test fit.

## Target Customer
Interiors firms whose clients already run occupancy platforms, and the platforms themselves seeking design-firm partners.

## Impact If Solved
Data the occupier already paid for becomes design evidence without a bespoke analysis project.
