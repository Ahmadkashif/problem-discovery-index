# Freight Visibility Adapted to White-Glove Furniture

**Niche:** [[niches/interior-design-firms/trade-procurement-and-receiving/profile|To-the-Trade Procurement & Receiving]]
**Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Shipment-visibility platforms track pallets for shippers with integrations; a single custom sofa moving vendor to receiver to client home falls outside them.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #survival-analysis #data-integration #automation #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to know, without a phone call, the true status and landed date of every item on order and to catch a wrong acknowledgement before the item ships, and whoever closes the gap between vendor paperwork and the receiving dock takes the account.

## The Problem
Furniture moves by LTL and blanket-wrap carriers, often consolidated by the receiver, then delivered by a white-glove crew. Tracking is a set of PRO numbers on carrier websites.

## What Already Exists
Real-time transportation visibility platforms serving large shippers and carriers, analysed under [[industries/freight-brokerage|Freight Brokerage]].

## The Customization Gap
The design-trade version needs tracking keyed to the client's sidemark and PO line rather than shipment ID; ingestion of LTL and blanket-wrap carriers without integrations; receiver inspection events as a status stage; and delivery appointments coordinated with install dates.

## Target Customer
Receiving warehouses and white-glove delivery companies serving design firms.

## Impact If Solved
Item location becomes visible without calls, and installs are scheduled against real arrival dates.
