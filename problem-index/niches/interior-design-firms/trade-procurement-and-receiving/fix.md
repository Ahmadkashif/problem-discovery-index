# Damage Found at Install, Claim Window Closed

**Niche:** [[niches/interior-design-firms/trade-procurement-and-receiving/profile|To-the-Trade Procurement & Receiving]]
**Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Items are received, stored for weeks and unwrapped on install day, by which time concealed freight damage is too late to claim.
**Tags:** #cnns #object-detection #large-language-models #automation #quick-win #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to know, without a phone call, the true status and landed date of every item on order and to catch a wrong acknowledgement before the item ships, and whoever closes the gap between vendor paperwork and the receiving dock takes the account.

## The Problem
Receivers often inspect only the outside of a crate to keep storage moving. Concealed damage must be reported to the carrier within a short window (commonly days, estimate). When the item is unwrapped weeks later at the client's home, the studio absorbs the cost of the remake.

## Why It's Still Broken
Full inspection costs receiver labour that nobody prices, and the photographs that do exist are not reviewed systematically.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Require opened inspection for high-value and upholstered items, capture standard photographs at receipt, run automated damage detection on them, and file the claim with photographs and paperwork the same day a problem is found.

## Who Feels the Pain
Designers facing clients on install day; receivers in disputes; studios absorbing remake costs.

## Impact If Fixed
Damage is found while it is still the carrier's problem, recovering remake costs and protecting install dates.
