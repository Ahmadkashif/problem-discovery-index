# A Shared Status Network for Vendors, Receivers and Designers

**Niche:** [[niches/interior-design-firms/trade-procurement-and-receiving/profile|To-the-Trade Procurement & Receiving]]
**Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** The vendor, the carrier, the receiving warehouse and the designer each hold one piece of an item's status, and nothing connects them.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #survival-analysis #graph-theory #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to know, without a phone call, the true status and landed date of every item on order and to catch a wrong acknowledgement before the item ships, and whoever closes the gap between vendor paperwork and the receiving dock takes the account.

## The Problem
A designer with two hundred open items across ten projects learns status by asking. The vendor knows the production date; the carrier knows the truck; the receiver knows what arrived and in what condition. Each answers emails one at a time.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
To-the-trade vendors are thousands of small and mid-sized manufacturers without EDI; receivers are local businesses; and studio-software vendors each integrate only with their own customers.

## What to Build
A neutral status network keyed on the sidemark and PO line: vendors confirm production and ship dates by replying to an email the system reads; carriers are tracked automatically; receivers scan and photograph on arrival into the same record. Designers see one status per item, with predicted arrival from history, across every studio tool.

## Target Customer
Receiving warehouses and procurement services that coordinate for many studios, and mid-sized studios with high product volume.

## Impact If Built
Status calls largely disappear, and the receiver becomes the hub of a network rather than an inbox.
