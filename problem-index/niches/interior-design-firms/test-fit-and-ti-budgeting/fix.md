# The Free Test Fit That Never Converts

**Niche:** [[niches/interior-design-firms/test-fit-and-ti-budgeting/profile|Broker Test-Fits & TI Budgeting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Firms produce dozens of unpaid test fits a year for brokers and cannot say which brokers, building types or tenant sizes ever turn into paid work.
**Tags:** #logistic-regression #descriptive-statistics #feature-engineering #revenue-impact #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a candidate building's floor plate and a tenant's headcount into a test fit and a credible fit-out budget within 48 hours of a broker's request, and whoever is fastest and closest on cost wins the design commission when the lease is signed.

## The Problem
Test fits are a pitch. Some brokers send the firm a steady flow that converts; others use the firm as free drafting. Because test fits are rarely tracked as a pipeline, the firm keeps saying yes to all of them.

## Why It's Still Broken
Test fits are logged as drawings in a project folder, not as opportunities in a CRM, and the eventual commission, if it arrives, comes months later under a different project number.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Log every test-fit request as an opportunity with broker, tenant size, building and outcome; join commissions back when they arrive; and score new requests by likely conversion so the firm prioritises or charges for low-probability work.

## Who Feels the Pain
Workplace practice leaders and the junior designers who draw test fits at night.

## Impact If Fixed
Firms redirect unpaid effort toward brokers and tenants that convert, or begin charging for the rest.
