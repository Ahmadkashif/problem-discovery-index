# Automated Test Fits With a Cost Attached

**Niche:** [[niches/interior-design-firms/test-fit-and-ti-budgeting/profile|Broker Test-Fits & TI Budgeting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A test fit answers "does it fit"; the tenant's real question is "does it fit inside the allowance", and today the layout and the budget are produced by different people days apart.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #optimization-fundamentals #feature-engineering #regularization #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a candidate building's floor plate and a tenant's headcount into a test fit and a credible fit-out budget within 48 hours of a broker's request, and whoever is fastest and closest on cost wins the design commission when the lease is signed.

## The Problem
A tenant comparing four buildings needs four test fits and four budgets. The designer draws the layouts; the budget comes later from a cost consultant or a per-square-foot rule of thumb that ignores the floor's specific conditions — existing build-out, core position, window line, ceiling height. Tenants sign on budgets that are wrong by tens of dollars per square foot.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Generative layout tools are recent; cost data sits with contractors and cost consultants; and interior firms rarely keep their own completed-project costs in a usable form.

## What to Build
Generate layouts from the floor plate and program, then estimate the fit-out cost of each layout from its quantities (partitions, doors, enclosed rooms, millwork, power and data density) using a model trained on the firm's and its contractor partners' completed projects, adjusted for local market and existing conditions. Produce a one-page comparison per building against the landlord's allowance.

## Target Customer
Workplace practice leaders at commercial interiors firms and the tenant-rep brokers who commission test fits.

## Impact If Built
Test fits that take two or three days arrive in hours with a budget attached, and the firm that delivers them wins more of the design commissions that follow.
