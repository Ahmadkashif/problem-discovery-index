# Broker Test-Fits & TI Budgeting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a candidate building's floor plate and a tenant's headcount into a test fit and a credible fit-out budget within 48 hours of a broker's request, and whoever is fastest and closest on cost wins the design commission when the lease is signed.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M US spend on test-fit labour, pre-lease budgeting and space-planning tools (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~4% of interior design revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — layouts drawn by hand in AutoCAD or Revit; costs from broker guides and memory
**Target Buyer:** Workplace practice leader at a commercial interiors firm, selling through tenant-rep brokers
**Automation Potential:** High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
During a site search the tenant-rep broker asks interior designers for test fits — a quick layout showing how the tenant's headcount and program fit each candidate floor — and an order-of-magnitude fit-out budget to compare against each landlord's tenant improvement allowance. The work is fast, frequent, often unpaid or lightly paid, and done as a pitch for the eventual design commission. The clock is the broker's tour and the landlord's proposal deadline.

## Current Tools & Gaps
AutoCAD and Revit by hand; generative space-planning tools for layouts; broker and cost-consultant fit-out guides for budgets. Gaps: layouts and costs are produced separately; the firm's own history of actual fit-out costs is not used; and no one tracks which test fits converted.
