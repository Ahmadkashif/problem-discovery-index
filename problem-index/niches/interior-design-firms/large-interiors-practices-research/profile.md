# Large Interiors Practices' Research Groups

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The largest commercial and hospitality interior design firms employ hundreds of designers and run small research or insight groups that publish workplace and hospitality studies, mainly to win work.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 researchers working on interiors specifically. The same firms' whole research institutes (10-60 people) are logged under architecture as [[niches/architecture-firms/large-firm-design-research-institutes/profile|Large-Firm Design Research Institutes]], which pass the gate and stop at a client-data kill switch; only the interiors slice is counted here.
**Output:** Thought-leadership reports and pitch support
**Proprietary data:** Project archives
**Clock:** Self-set
**Buyer:** Design principal
