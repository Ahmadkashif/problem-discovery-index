# Hospitality FF&E Purchasing Agents

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary

## What They Do
Purchasing firms that buy furniture, fixtures, equipment and operating supplies on behalf of hotel owners once a hospitality designer has specified them: bidding the package, negotiating with manufacturers, managing logistics and warehousing, and installing. They are paid a fee, often a percentage of the procurement value.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 procurement analysts and estimators at the larger firms (estimate)
**Output:** Bid tabulations, value-engineering proposals, procurement budgets
**Proprietary data:** Purchase prices and vendor performance across thousands of hotel orders
**Clock:** Hotel opening and PIP completion dates
**Buyer:** Head of procurement or estimating
