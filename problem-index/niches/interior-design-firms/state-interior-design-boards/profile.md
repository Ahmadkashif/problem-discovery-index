# State Interior Design Registration Boards

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Twenty-nine states, Washington DC and Puerto Rico regulate interior design in some form, from title protection to practice acts that govern who may submit drawings for permit. Boards register designers, handle complaints and enforce the title or practice rules, often as part of a combined architecture board.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically a handful of staff per board
**Output:** Registrations, disciplinary actions
**Proprietary data:** Registrant and complaint records
**Clock:** Statutory renewal cycles
**Buyer:** Board executive director
