# Hotel Brand Design & Standards Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Franchisors' design and construction teams write brand standards and prototypes, issue PIPs, approve franchisees' designs and FF&E, and inspect for compliance. They decide what thousands of hotels must buy and when, and they are the reason hospitality interior design runs on a schedule.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-200 design, standards and technical staff at the largest franchisors (estimate)
**Output:** Brand standards, prototypes, PIPs and design approvals — internal outputs, not invoiced research
**Proprietary data:** Standards history, approvals and inspection results across every branded property
**Clock:** Self-set — the brand sets the PIP and standards deadlines others work to
**Buyer:** Head of global design or design and construction
