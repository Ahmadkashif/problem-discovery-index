# Interior Design Associations & Exam Body

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
ASID and IIDA represent interior designers and publish industry outlook research; CIDQ develops and administers the NCIDQ examination that most regulating jurisdictions rely on.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research staff at each; exam development relies on volunteer subject-matter experts and psychometric contractors
**Output:** Outlook reports, member surveys, the NCIDQ exam
**Proprietary data:** Member surveys and exam performance data
**Clock:** Annual reports and exam windows, self-set
**Buyer:** Executive director
