# Facility Asset Management Adapted to Interior Finishes

**Niche:** [[niches/interior-design-firms/healthcare-senior-living-interiors/profile|Healthcare & Senior Living Interiors]]
**Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Facility maintenance systems track equipment by serial number; furniture and finishes, which make up much of what residents and patients touch, are barely recorded.
**Tags:** #feature-engineering #descriptive-statistics #large-language-models #data-integration #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to prove that every finish and furnishing in a care setting meets infection-control, fire, accessibility and resident-safety requirements and survives the facility's cleaning regime, and whoever makes that proof fastest wins the specification.

## The Problem
When a chair fails, maintenance replaces it with whatever is available, and the facility drifts away from its specified, code-compliant palette.

## What Already Exists
Computerised maintenance management systems used by health systems and senior living operators for equipment, work orders and preventive maintenance.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs the designer's FF&E and finish schedule imported as assets by room; replacement work orders that propose the specified item or an approved compliant alternate; cleanability and disinfectant compatibility stored per asset; and failure coding by finish type that can feed back to the designer.

## Target Customer
Directors of facilities and design at multi-site senior living operators.

## Impact If Solved
Facilities keep their compliant palette through years of replacements, and failure data accumulates by product.
