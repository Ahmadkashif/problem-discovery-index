# Healthcare & Senior Living Interiors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to prove that every finish and furnishing in a care setting meets infection-control, fire, accessibility and resident-safety requirements and survives the facility's cleaning regime, and whoever makes that proof fastest wins the specification.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$700M US healthcare and senior living interiors design and specification fees (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of interior design revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — specifications in spreadsheets, performance data in manufacturer PDFs, facility outcomes in maintenance systems the designer never sees
**Target Buyer:** Principal of a healthcare or senior living interiors practice; on the client side the facility's director of design and construction or a senior living operator's VP of design
**Automation Potential:** Medium — specification checks are automatable; the balance between clinical and homelike design is judgement

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Care settings combine the strictest specification demands in interiors with clients whose residents and patients cannot speak for themselves. Fabrics must survive daily disinfection; flooring must reduce fall risk; seating must carry bariatric loads; wayfinding must work for people with dementia; and everything must pass fire and accessibility review. Senior living adds the commercial need to look residential, not clinical, because families choose communities on how they feel. Residential tools ignore the codes; commercial specification tools ignore the facility outcomes.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Excel spec books, manufacturer cleanability and disinfectant-compatibility statements, evidence-based design research, and facility maintenance systems on the client side. Gaps: no feedback from how finishes perform in use; disinfectant compatibility checked by hand; and maintenance data never returns to the designer.
