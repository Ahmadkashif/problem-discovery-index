# Finish Performance Evidence From Facility Outcomes

**Niche:** [[niches/interior-design-firms/healthcare-senior-living-interiors/profile|Healthcare & Senior Living Interiors]]
**Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Designers specify the same vinyl, carpet tile and upholstery across dozens of care facilities and never learn which ones failed early.
**Tags:** #survival-analysis #gradient-boosting #causal-inference #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #compliance #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to prove that every finish and furnishing in a care setting meets infection-control, fire, accessibility and resident-safety requirements and survives the facility's cleaning regime, and whoever makes that proof fastest wins the specification.

## The Problem
A healthcare interiors firm specifies finishes against manufacturer data and experience. After opening, the facility's maintenance team logs torn upholstery, stained flooring, delaminating surfaces and replacements in its own system. The designer, who will specify the next facility, never sees it.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Maintenance data belongs to the facility and is coded by asset, not by finish specification; designers are paid per project; and no party aggregates outcomes across facilities.

## What to Build
Join each facility's as-built finish schedule to its maintenance and replacement tickets, model time-to-failure by product, location, traffic and cleaning regime, and return a performance record per product the designer can use at specification and the operator can use at procurement.

## Target Customer
Senior living operators and health systems with multiple facilities, and the interiors firms that serve them.

## Impact If Built
Operators stop re-specifying finishes that fail early across their portfolio, and the designer can defend each choice with outcome evidence.
