# Workplace Experience Benchmarking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms that survey employees about how well their workplace supports their work and benchmark the result against a large comparable database, so occupiers and their designers can judge a fit-out before and after. The Leesman Index is the best known; HqO acquired it in June 2022, when its dataset held about a million office respondents and about 300,000 home-based respondents from 6,410 organisations. Occupiers pay per survey and benchmark.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 survey, analytics and consulting staff (estimate)
**Output:** Workplace experience scores, benchmark reports, pre- and post-occupancy comparisons
**Proprietary data:** One of the largest comparable workplace-experience survey datasets in existence
**Clock:** Mostly client-set — relocations, renewals and return-to-office decisions — with no third-party deadline
**Buyer:** Head of workplace research or product at the benchmark provider
