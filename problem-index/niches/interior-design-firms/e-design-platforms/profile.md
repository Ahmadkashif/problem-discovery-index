# Online & E-Design Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Platforms that sell flat-fee remote design packages delivered by networks of freelance designers and earn commissions on the furniture clients buy. Havenly is the largest surviving US example after several competitors closed or were absorbed.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 data and analytics staff (estimate)
**Output:** Style matching and product recommendation inside the platform; no invoiced research
**Proprietary data:** Style quizzes, client reactions to designs and purchase outcomes at scale — the closest thing in the industry to a taste dataset
**Clock:** None external
**Buyer:** Chief product or data officer
