# The Client Who Finds It Cheaper Online

**Niche:** [[niches/interior-design-firms/residential-full-service-studios/profile|Residential Full-Service Studios]]
**Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A client searches the model number on the proposal, finds it below the designer's price, and the studio's markup model becomes a trust problem.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #k-nearest-neighbors #feature-engineering #revenue-impact #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to become the single record from proposal to client invoice — selections, client deposits, purchase orders and markup — and whoever holds that record holds the studio's margin and its books.

## The Problem
Many trade vendors now also sell direct or through retailers, and the same item can appear online below the designer's marked-up price. Clients who discover this question every line on the proposal, ask for discounts, or buy the item themselves and leave the studio managing delivery of product it earns nothing on.

## Why It's Still Broken
The markup is embedded in the item price rather than stated as a fee for sourcing, procurement, receiving and installation, so the client compares a bundle against a bare product. Studios do not know in advance which items are exposed to retail comparison, so they discover it one awkward conversation at a time.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Check each proposal line against public retail availability before it goes out, and flag exposed items. For those, the studio can switch to a trade-exclusive or custom equivalent, price procurement as a visible per-item fee, or show the client what the studio's price includes (receiving, inspection, damage handling, installation). Policy is set once and applied consistently.

## Who Feels the Pain
Principals whose margin model depends on product; designers who handle the conversation; clients who feel misled.

## Impact If Fixed
Studios stop losing items, and goodwill, to retail comparison mid-project, and move toward a fee structure clients accept rather than resent.
