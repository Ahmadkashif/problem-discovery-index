**Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a brand's design standards and a property's PIP into a compliant, priced FF&E specification and bid package before the franchisor's deadline, and whoever does it fastest at the lowest defensible cost per key takes the renovation.

## The Problem
A buyer under contract for a hotel has weeks to understand the PIP the brand will impose, because it changes the price they should pay. The owner, the designer and the purchasing agent each read the same PDF and translate items such as "replace guestroom soft goods" or "upgrade lobby to current prototype" into quantities, specifications and costs. The result varies by who reads it.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
PIPs are brand-specific documents with inconsistent structure; cost data sits with purchasing agents and contractors; and design firms have seen this as pre-design work they do to win the job rather than a product.

## What to Build
Extract every PIP line, map it to brand-standard requirements and to FF&E and finish categories, compute quantities from the property's room mix and areas, and price the scope from the firm's completed projects and current vendor pricing. Output a scope matrix, a cost per key with a range, and the items where negotiation with the brand could save most.

## Target Customer
Hospitality design firms, and the owners and acquirers who commission them during due diligence.

## Impact If Built
A PIP scope and budget in a day rather than a week changes what an acquirer bids and lets the designer win the renovation at the deal stage.
