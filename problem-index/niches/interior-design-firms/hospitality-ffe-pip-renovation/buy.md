# Construction Estimating Adapted to FF&E

**Niche:** [[niches/interior-design-firms/hospitality-ffe-pip-renovation/profile|Hospitality FF&E & PIP Renovation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Estimating software prices partitions and drywall from drawings; hotel renovations are priced per key from furniture, fabric and finish packages it does not model.
**Tags:** #linear-regression #feature-engineering #descriptive-statistics #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a brand's design standards and a property's PIP into a compliant, priced FF&E specification and bid package before the franchisor's deadline, and whoever does it fastest at the lowest defensible cost per key takes the renovation.

## The Problem
Owners want a renovation budget per key, split between FF&E, OS&E and construction. Designers produce the FF&E part from spreadsheets and recent bids.

## What Already Exists
Construction estimating and take-off tools used by contractors and cost consultants, and published per-key guides such as the HVS/Nehmer Hotel Cost Estimating Guide.

## The Customization Gap
FF&E estimating needs item libraries for guestroom and public-space packages by brand tier; quantities driven by room mix rather than take-off; freight, warehousing, installation and purchasing-agent fees as standard lines; and price history from the firm's own procurement rather than national averages.

## Target Customer
Hospitality design firms and owners' capital projects teams.

## Impact If Solved
Budgets arrive faster and closer to the eventual bid, reducing the redesign cycles that follow a budget overrun.
