# Design Trade Media Rankings & Benchmarks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Trade publications survey the largest interior design firms each year and publish rankings, fee and billing trends, and salary benchmarks, funded by advertising, events and sponsorship.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 3-10 research and editorial staff on the surveys (estimate)
**Output:** Annual rankings and benchmark articles
**Proprietary data:** Firms' self-reported survey responses
**Clock:** Annual publication cycle, self-set
**Buyer:** Publisher or editor-in-chief
