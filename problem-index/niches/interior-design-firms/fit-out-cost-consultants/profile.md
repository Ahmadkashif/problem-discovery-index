# Commercial Fit-Out Cost Consultants

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Cost management and project management firms estimate and track the cost of office, retail and hospitality interiors for occupiers and owners, and publish annual fit-out cost guides by city (JLL put the US average at ~$280 per square foot in 2025; Cushman & Wakefield's guide covers 40 US cities). Occupiers and landlords pay for cost plans at test fit, design development and tender, and for cost tracking through construction. The same firms serve architecture and construction clients.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-500 cost managers and estimators in the interiors and fit-out practices of the larger firms (estimate)
**Output:** Cost plans, benchmark reports, tender analyses, published cost guides
**Proprietary data:** Historic cost data from thousands of completed fit-outs, by city, building class and specification level
**Clock:** Lease negotiations and tenant improvement allowance deadlines, budget approvals, tender dates
**Buyer:** Head of cost management or project and development services
