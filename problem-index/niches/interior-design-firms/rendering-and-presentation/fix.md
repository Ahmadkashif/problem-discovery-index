# The Rendering the Room Never Matched

**Niche:** [[niches/interior-design-firms/rendering-and-presentation/profile|Rendering & Client Presentation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A client approves a sunlit render with perfect proportions, then sees the finished room under north light with a sofa that feels larger, and blames the designer.
**Tags:** #cnns #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce a photoreal view of the proposed room with real, orderable trade products fast enough to show in the first meeting, and whoever ties the rendering to the SKUs takes the presentation.

## The Problem
Renders are tuned to persuade: idealised light, wide lenses, clean surfaces. Clients take them literally, and disputes after install often trace back to an expectation the render created.

## Why It's Still Broken
Render settings are chosen for beauty; nothing ties the light, lens and colour in the image to the room's actual orientation, windows and fixtures.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Render from site photographs with the room's real orientation and daylight by season, a lens close to human view, and colour-managed materials; show a second "as it will feel" view alongside the hero image.

## Who Feels the Pain
Designers handling post-install disappointment; clients who feel misled.

## Impact If Fixed
Fewer post-install disputes and remakes driven by expectations the presentation set.
