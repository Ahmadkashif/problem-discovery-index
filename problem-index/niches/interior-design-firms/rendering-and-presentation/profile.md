# Rendering & Client Presentation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce a photoreal view of the proposed room with real, orderable trade products fast enough to show in the first meeting, and whoever ties the rendering to the SKUs takes the presentation.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600M US spend by design firms on rendering software, outsourced visualisation and presentation tools (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of revenue as tool and outsourcing cost (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High — SketchUp, Enscape, V-Ray and generative rendering tools are widely used
**Target Buyer:** Studio principal or senior designer
**Automation Potential:** Very high for image production; low for the design judgement behind it

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Clients approve what they can see. Presentations range from mood boards to full photoreal renderings, and rendering is often outsourced to visualisation studios abroad at a per-view price and a turnaround of days. Generative image tools now produce convincing room images in seconds, but they invent furniture that does not exist. The contest is not image quality, which is becoming free, but whether every object in the image is a real product the studio can order.

## Current Tools & Gaps
SketchUp with Enscape or V-Ray, Chief Architect, Houzz Pro's 3D, outsourced visualisation studios, and consumer generative redesign apps. Gaps: generative images are not tied to SKUs; 3D models for trade products are scarce; and renders set expectations of light and scale the finished room does not meet.
