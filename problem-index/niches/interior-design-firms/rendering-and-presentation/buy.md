# Generative Image Tools Adapted to Trade Catalogues

**Niche:** [[niches/interior-design-firms/rendering-and-presentation/profile|Rendering & Client Presentation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Consumer room-redesign apps already restyle a photograph in seconds; designers need the same speed constrained to their trade sources and their client's room.
**Tags:** #diffusion-models #transfer-learning #contrastive-learning #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce a photoreal view of the proposed room with real, orderable trade products fast enough to show in the first meeting, and whoever ties the rendering to the SKUs takes the presentation.

## The Problem
Designers use consumer generative tools for inspiration and then cannot use the output with clients, because the products are invented and the room's dimensions are ignored.

## What Already Exists
General image generators and consumer room-redesign apps that restyle a photograph by prompt.

## The Customization Gap
The trade version needs generation restricted to a firm's chosen manufacturers and products; preservation of the room's actual geometry, windows and fixed elements; scale-correct placement from the floor plan; and export of the product list to the studio's proposal.

## Target Customer
Residential designers already experimenting with generative tools.

## Impact If Solved
Inspiration images become usable presentation material instead of a step that has to be redone.
