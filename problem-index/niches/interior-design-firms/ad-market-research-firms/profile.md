# A&D Market Research for Manufacturers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Research firms that study architects and interior designers on behalf of the manufacturers selling to them: how specifiers choose products, which brands they trust, how firms' project pipelines are moving. ThinkLab, SANDOW's research division built from the acquisition of Contract Consulting Group, is the clearest example and claims more than 10,000 industry interviews a year. Contract furniture, flooring and surfaces manufacturers pay through memberships and custom studies.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 researchers and analysts (estimate)
**Output:** Syndicated research, custom studies, sentiment trackers, events content
**Proprietary data:** Panels and interview archives of designers and architects
**Clock:** Largely self-set; trade events such as NeoCon and product-launch cycles give soft deadlines
**Buyer:** Head of research; on the client side, manufacturers' CMOs
