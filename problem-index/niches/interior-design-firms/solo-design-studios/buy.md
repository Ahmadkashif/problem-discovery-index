# Freelancer Business Suites Adapted to Product Resale

**Niche:** [[niches/interior-design-firms/solo-design-studios/profile|Solo & Two-Person Studios]]
**Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Freelancer suites handle proposals, contracts, scheduling and payments for creative businesses but have no concept of purchase orders, markup or deposits held for product.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #decision-trees #compliance #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let a one-person studio run procurement, billing and client communication without its first hire, and whoever replaces that first assistant takes the account.

## The Problem
Many solo designers start on a freelancer suite because it handles client onboarding and payments well, then hit a wall the first time they resell product.

## What Already Exists
HoneyBook and Dubsado for proposals, contracts, scheduling, invoicing and client portals across creative service businesses.

## The Customization Gap
Designers need product lines with trade cost, markup policy and sales tax by delivery state; purchase orders generated from approved items; client and vendor deposits tracked separately; and a resale-certificate register per vendor.

## Target Customer
Solo designers already on a freelancer suite who do not want to migrate to full studio software.

## Impact If Solved
The designer resells product correctly without changing platforms or adding a spreadsheet.
