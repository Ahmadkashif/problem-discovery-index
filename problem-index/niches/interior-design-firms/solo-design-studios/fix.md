# Undercharging Because Time Is Never Logged

**Niche:** [[niches/interior-design-firms/solo-design-studios/profile|Solo & Two-Person Studios]]
**Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Solo designers quote flat fees from instinct and never learn how far over them they run, because nobody logs the hours.
**Tags:** #linear-regression #feature-engineering #descriptive-statistics #revenue-impact #quick-win #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let a one-person studio run procurement, billing and client communication without its first hire, and whoever replaces that first assistant takes the account.

## The Problem
Time spent on site visits, showroom trips, sourcing and late-night client messages goes unrecorded. Fees for the next project are set by guesswork and by what competitors charge.

## Why It's Still Broken
Timers require discipline the owner does not have mid-day, and on flat fees there is no billing reason to log.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Passive time capture from calendar events, location at known addresses, studio-software activity and message threads, assembled into a weekly timesheet per project for one-tap confirmation, then compared against the fee at project close.

## Who Feels the Pain
The owner, who works more hours than they price and cannot see it.

## Impact If Fixed
The next fee quote is set from evidence; even a 10–15% fee correction on a few projects a year is material for a one-person firm (estimate).
