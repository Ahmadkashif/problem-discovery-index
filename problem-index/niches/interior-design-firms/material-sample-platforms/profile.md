# Material Specification & Sample Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Platforms that let designers search many manufacturers' materials at once and receive samples overnight, free to the designer and paid for by manufacturers. Material Bank is the leading example, listing 500,000+ products; its founder said designers had been spending up to 39% of their day searching for and ordering products.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 data and analytics staff (estimate)
**Output:** Platform analytics and demand insight for manufacturers; the core revenue is sample logistics and marketing
**Proprietary data:** The stream of sample requests by designer, project and product — the earliest observable signal of what is being specified
**Clock:** None set externally
**Buyer:** Chief data or product officer at the platform
