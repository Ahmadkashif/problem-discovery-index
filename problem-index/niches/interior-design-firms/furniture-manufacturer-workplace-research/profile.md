# Contract Furniture Manufacturer Workplace Research Groups

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/interior-design-firms|Interior Design Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Research groups inside the large contract furniture manufacturers study how people use offices and publish findings that inform product development and give dealers and designers material for client conversations.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 researchers per large manufacturer (estimate)
**Output:** Published workplace research, product insight, sales content
**Proprietary data:** Observational studies and occupancy data from client sites and labs
**Clock:** Product development cycles and trade events, self-set
**Buyer:** Head of research or insight
