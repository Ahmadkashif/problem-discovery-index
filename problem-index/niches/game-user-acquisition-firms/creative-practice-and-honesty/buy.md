**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to establish whether ads depicting mechanics the game does not have actually acquire anything of value after the churn they cause — and whoever measures that takes the account.

## The Problem
Advertising in regulated categories — financial services, pharmaceuticals, food, automotive — operates under substantiation requirements: a claim must be supportable before it runs, there is a pre-approval process, records are retained, and regulators act on breaches. Depicting a product feature that does not exist would be a straightforward violation. The compliance infrastructure is mature. Games advertising has drawn consumer protection attention for exactly this and has almost none of the apparatus.

## What Already Exists
Claim substantiation requirements and evidence files; pre-approval review workflows; retained records of what ran where; complaint and adjudication processes; and compliance monitoring across live creative.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to visual depiction of interactive gameplay rather than to verbal claims. It requires: (1) the claim being what a video appears to show rather than a stated assertion, so substantiation means comparing depicted gameplay against actual gameplay — this is the substantive difference and is a perception question rather than a factual one; (2) creative volume in the thousands per campaign, ruling out human pre-approval; (3) platform policy rather than statutory regulation as the main enforcement, with inconsistent application; (4) no established standard for what constitutes misleading in this medium; and (5) creative generated and iterated continuously rather than produced as a finished asset.

## Target Customer
UA teams and agencies, mobile publishers, platform policy teams, and advertising compliance vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Regulated advertising requires substantiation before a claim runs, with mature compliance infrastructure. A claim that is what a video appears to show, at thousands of creatives per campaign, is what has to be automated rather than reviewed.
