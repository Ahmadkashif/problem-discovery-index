# Bidding & Media Buying

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/game-user-acquisition-firms|Game User Acquisition Firms]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to allocate spend across networks whose own optimisers they cannot see, against a predicted value they do not trust — and whoever buys better takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$9B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~22% of category spend
**Digital Adoption:** Very high
**Target Buyer:** UA leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — allocation and bid optimisation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Media buying is where the money actually moves. Budget is allocated across networks, campaigns and geographies; bids are set against predicted value; and the networks' own optimisation systems sit between the buyer's instruction and the outcome in ways that are deliberately opaque. The buyer is optimising a portfolio through intermediaries who optimise for themselves, against a target number with substantial uncertainty, with feedback that arrives days late.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Network dashboards, a bid management layer, a payback target and a spreadsheet reconciling them. The gaps: no unified view across networks; no accounting for network-side optimisation; no portfolio allocation model; no handling of delayed and uncertain feedback; and no saturation modelling per source.
