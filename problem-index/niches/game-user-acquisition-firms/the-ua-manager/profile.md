# The UA Manager

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/game-user-acquisition-firms|Game User Acquisition Firms]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give the person held to cohort payback some defence when payback is determined mostly by what another team ships — and whoever supplies that evidence takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$4B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category spend
**Digital Adoption:** Low — no defence
**Target Buyer:** UA leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — attribution and evidence

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The UA manager is measured on cohort payback, and cohort payback is determined mostly by what the game team ships in the six months after the install. They control the sources, the bids and the creative; they do not control the content, the events, the monetisation or the balance, and those dominate the outcome. When payback misses, the conversation has no evidence in it, and the person with the least control over the number is the one answering for it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A payback dashboard, a target, and a monthly review. The gaps: no adjustment for content effects; no evidence to bring to the review; no shared accountability structure; no forecast that accounts for the roadmap; and no visibility of the content plan at bid time.
