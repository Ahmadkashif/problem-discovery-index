# Controllable Performance Measurement From Operations

**Industry:** [[industries/game-user-acquisition-firms|Game User Acquisition Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Management accounting separates controllable from uncontrollable variance so people are measured on what they influence, and UA is measured on everything.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give the person held to cohort payback some defence when payback is determined mostly by what another team ships — and whoever supplies that evidence takes the account.

## The Problem
Management accounting resolved this in principle a long time ago. Responsibility accounting holds managers accountable only for what they control; variance analysis separates a result into price, volume, efficiency and external components; and performance is assessed on the controllable portion. The principle is taught in every management accounting course and applied across operations, manufacturing and services. UA is measured on a number whose largest driver sits in another function.

## What Already Exists
Responsibility accounting and controllability principles; variance decomposition into component causes; flexible budgeting adjusted for volume and external factors; controllable margin reporting; and joint responsibility structures.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a variance whose components must be estimated rather than computed. It requires: (1) the uncontrollable component being a content effect that has to be inferred statistically rather than read off a rate or a volume — this is the substantive difference and is why the principle has not been applied; (2) a delay of months between the decision and the outcome; (3) two functions whose incentives conflict directly over the decomposition; (4) a prediction model in the middle, so the manager's own accuracy is part of what should be measured; and (5) no finance convention for this industry to lean on.

## Target Customer
UA and product leadership, mobile publishers, finance functions, and performance management vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Management accounting resolved controllability in principle long ago and it is taught everywhere. An uncontrollable component that must be inferred statistically rather than read off a rate is why it has never been applied here.
