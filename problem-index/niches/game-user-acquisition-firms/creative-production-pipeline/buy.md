**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce, format and ship thousands of creative variants a month without the production capacity becoming the ceiling on spend — and whoever automates that takes the account.

## The Problem
Programmatic advertising built dynamic creative optimisation to solve this: creative assembled from components at serve time, variants generated automatically across formats, performance fed back into which combinations to serve, and trafficking handled through a single interface. Retail and commerce advertisers run enormous variant counts this way. Games performance creative, which needs even more variants because fatigue is faster, is largely produced as finished files.

## What Already Exists
Component-based dynamic creative assembly; automatic multi-format rendering; performance feedback into component selection; centralised trafficking; and creative fatigue detection.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to video and playable creative where the content is gameplay. It requires: (1) creative built from gameplay footage and playable code rather than from product images and copy, so component assembly is far harder than swapping a headline over a photo — this is the substantive difference; (2) playable ads as a format with no programmatic equivalent; (3) networks whose own optimisation sits between the creative and the user, complicating feedback; (4) fatigue cycles measured in days rather than weeks; and (5) creative whose accuracy relative to the product is itself a live issue, which retail assembly never faces.

## Target Customer
UA teams and performance creative agencies, mobile publishers, ad networks, and creative automation vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Programmatic built component assembly and multi-format rendering at enormous scale. Creative built from gameplay footage and playable code, rather than images and copy, is what makes the assembly problem genuinely harder here.
