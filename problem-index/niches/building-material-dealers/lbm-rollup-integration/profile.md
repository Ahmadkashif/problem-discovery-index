# LBM Rollup & Platform Integration Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/building-material-dealers|Building Material Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The serial acquirers that are consolidating building-material dealing, and their corporate development and integration teams: Builders FirstSource ($15.2B of 2025 sales, ~585 locations), US LBM, QXO (Beacon, ~$11B, closed April 2025; Kodiak, ~$2.25B, completed April 2026), Home Depot's SRS (GMS, ~$5.5B, September 2025) and Lowe's (Foundation Building Materials, $8.8B, October 2025), plus private-equity platforms. The teams screen targets, model synergies, standardise pricing and estimating, and track post-close performance.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 corporate development, pricing and integration analysts per platform (estimate)
**Output:** Target screens, valuation and synergy models, integration scorecards, branch benchmarking
**Proprietary data:** Acquired dealers' customer, pricing, job and margin histories, and the platform's integration record
**Clock:** Deal timetables, lender covenants, sponsor holding periods and quarterly reporting
**Buyer:** Head of corporate development or the platform CFO
