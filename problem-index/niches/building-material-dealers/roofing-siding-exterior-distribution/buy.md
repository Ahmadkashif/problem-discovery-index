# Measurement Reports Turned Into Complete Roof Orders

**Niche:** [[niches/building-material-dealers/roofing-siding-exterior-distribution/profile|Roofing, Siding & Exterior Products Distribution]]
**Industry:** [[industries/building-material-dealers|Building Material Dealers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** A measurement report gives squares, pitches, ridges and valleys; turning it into a complete order for this manufacturer's system, this contractor's habits and this branch's stock still needs a counter salesperson.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #gradient-boosting #k-nearest-neighbors #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to land the complete roof or siding system a contractor ordered — matched to the job's measurement report and on the rooftop the morning of the tear-off — and whoever misses the fewest rooftop drops when a storm multiplies demand takes the account.

## The Problem
Contractors send a report and a shingle choice. The salesperson converts squares to bundles, adds starter, ridge, underlayment, ice and water shield by the code zone, flashing and vents, and checks stock.

## What Already Exists
Aerial and photo measurement reports, contractor-side estimating in roofing CRMs, and distributor ordering apps with product catalogues.

## The Customization Gap
System rules by manufacturer (the components a warranty requires), code-driven items by jurisdiction (ice barrier lines, drip edge), waste by roof complexity, the contractor's own accessory habits learned from past orders, substitutes from branch stock, and the delivery note for rooftop loading.

## Target Customer
Roofing distributors' counter and inside sales teams; contractors ordering directly.

## Impact If Solved
If complete-first-time orders rise from ~85% to ~95% of roofs (estimate), a busy branch avoids dozens of second trips a month in storm season.
