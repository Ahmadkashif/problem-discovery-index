# Roofing, Siding & Exterior Products Distribution

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/building-material-dealers|Building Material Dealers]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to land the complete roof or siding system a contractor ordered — matched to the job's measurement report and on the rooftop the morning of the tear-off — and whoever misses the fewest rooftop drops when a storm multiplies demand takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$75B in 2022 receipts in NAICS 423330, roofing, siding and insulation merchant wholesalers (Census SUSB 2022), of which 46 enterprises with 500+ employees took ~$63B
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~16% of the codes' receipts
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — the national distributors run contractor apps, online ordering and measurement-report integrations, while orders still change by phone the night before a tear-off
**Target Buyer:** Branch manager or regional VP at a roofing distributor; the contractor's owner or production manager as the customer
**Automation Potential:** Medium-High for quoting from measurement reports and storm-demand planning; low for rooftop delivery

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Roofing and siding distribution is the most concentrated corner of the trade, and its contest is different from lumber's. A reroof is sold from a measurement report (aerial or photo-based), quoted as a system — shingles, underlayment, ice and water shield, drip edge, ridge, vents, accessories — and delivered to the roof itself by conveyor or boom on the morning the crew tears off, because a stripped roof cannot wait. Storms multiply demand in a region overnight, and insurance-driven work pulls crews from other states. Consolidation has been rapid: QXO completed its ~$11B purchase of Beacon in April 2025, Home Depot's SRS bought GMS in September 2025, and ABC Supply remains the largest privately held roofing distributor (recalled). The contractor side is mapped at [[industries/roofing-contractors|Roofing Contractors]], including [[niches/roofing-contractors/storm-chasing-restoration/profile|Storm Chasing & Restoration]]; the measurement data is [[niches/roofing-contractors/aerial-roof-measurement/profile|Aerial Imagery & Roof Measurement]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Distributor contractor apps and online ordering, measurement reports from EagleView, Hover and others, roofing CRMs on the contractor side, and branch ERPs. The gaps: measurement-to-order conversion still checked by a counter salesperson, storm surge planned from experience, and rooftop drop failures recorded as anecdotes.
