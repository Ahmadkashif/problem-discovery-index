# Storm Surge Stock Positioned Before the Calls

**Niche:** [[niches/building-material-dealers/roofing-siding-exterior-distribution/profile|Roofing, Siding & Exterior Products Distribution]]
**Industry:** [[industries/building-material-dealers|Building Material Dealers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** After a hailstorm, the distributor with shingles in the right colours at the right branches wins a season of contractors; today that positioning is a regional manager's judgement call.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #gradient-boosting #survival-analysis #feature-engineering #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to land the complete roof or siding system a contractor ordered — matched to the job's measurement report and on the rooftop the morning of the tear-off — and whoever misses the fewest rooftop drops when a storm multiplies demand takes the account.

## The Problem
Hail and wind events create weeks of concentrated reroofing demand in a metro. Contractors flood in, and the branch that runs out of the popular colour or of ridge cap loses them to the competitor down the road.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Storm data, claims activity and contractor demand sit with different parties; distributors forecast from their own sales history, which lags the storm.

## What to Build
A post-event demand model joining storm swaths (hail size and wind reports from public weather data), housing stock and roof age, permit filings and early order signals, producing expected roofing squares by week, product line and colour for each branch, and transfer suggestions across the network. A two-week demo works on public storm reports and permit data for one past event.

## Target Customer
Regional supply chain and branch leaders at roofing distributors.

## Impact If Built
If a large hail event adds ~50,000–150,000 roofing squares of demand to a metro over a season (estimate) and the first two weeks decide which branches the incoming crews use (estimate), a branch in stock through those weeks can win ~$1–3M of extra sales (estimate, at ~$100–150 of material per square, estimate), and a planned transfer costs a fraction of an emergency one.
