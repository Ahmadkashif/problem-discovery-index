# LBM Buying Cooperatives

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/building-material-dealers|Building Material Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Member-owned cooperatives and buying groups through which independent lumberyards and pro dealers negotiate vendor programmes and rebates, buy commodities and get catalogues, takeoff and marketing services. LBM Advantage describes itself as one of the largest wholly member-owned buying cooperatives in lumber and building materials; Do it Best (which bought True Value in 2024, recalled) serves hardware stores and lumberyards from its own warehouses; LBMX and others run programmes for independents (recalled). Members pay dues and share rebates; vendors pay to be preferred.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 programme, category and member-analytics staff at the largest LBM group (estimate)
**Output:** Vendor programme performance, rebate calculations, member purchase benchmarks, commodity buying guidance, category reports
**Proprietary data:** Members' purchase and rebate-reporting data across hundreds of vendors, which no single dealer or manufacturer sees
**Clock:** Annual vendor programme negotiations, buying shows and rebate periods
**Buyer:** Cooperative CEO or head of member services
