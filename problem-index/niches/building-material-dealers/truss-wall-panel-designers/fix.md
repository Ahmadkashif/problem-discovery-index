# Plans Arriving Without Bearing Walls

**Industry:** [[industries/building-material-dealers|Building Material Dealers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Home designers' plans often omit bearing walls and point loads, so the truss designer guesses, emails, waits and guesses again.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #object-detection #quick-win #worker-facing #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a house plan and its revisions into approved, buildable truss and wall-panel layouts with fewer designer hours, and whoever gets layouts approved with the fewest field fixes per designer-hour takes the account.

## The Problem
A residential plan without a structural sheet leaves the truss designer to infer which walls bear and where girders land. Questions go to the builder by email and come back days later.

## Why It's Still Broken
Builders don't pay for structural drawings on simple houses, and the plant's question log lives in the designer's inbox.

## What a Fix Looks Like
On intake, propose bearing walls and girder locations from the plan and the plant's past designs for similar houses, generate the question list for the builder in one message, and track answers against the job.

## Who Feels the Pain
Designers waiting on answers, plants losing production slots, framers fixing guesses on site.

## Impact If Fixed
One round of questions instead of three, saving ~2–5 days of design lead time per house (estimate), and fewer layouts built on a wrong guess.
