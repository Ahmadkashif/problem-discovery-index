# Drawing Comparison Taught Structure

**Niche:** [[niches/building-material-dealers/truss-wall-panel-designers/profile|Truss & Wall-Panel Component Designers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/building-material-dealers|Building Material Dealers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** PDF overlay tools show that something moved; the designer needs to know whether it moved a load path.
**Tags:** #object-detection #cnns #large-language-models #data-integration #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a house plan and its revisions into approved, buildable truss and wall-panel layouts with fewer designer hours, and whoever gets layouts approved with the fewest field fixes per designer-hour takes the account.

## The Problem
Designers use generic overlay and markup tools to compare plan versions. They flag every changed line, including dimension text and notes, and leave the structural reading to the designer.

## What Already Exists
PDF comparison and overlay in markup software, construction document management platforms, and the plate suppliers' design tools.

## The Customization Gap
Filtering changes to those that matter structurally (bearing walls, spans, heights, roof pitches, openings in bearing walls), labelling them in component terms, and exporting them into the design software's job so the designer opens the affected trusses directly.

## Target Customer
Component plants and engineered-lumber design desks at dealers.

## Impact If Solved
Revision review in ~10–15 minutes rather than one to three hours (estimate), using tools the plant already pays for plus a structural filter.
