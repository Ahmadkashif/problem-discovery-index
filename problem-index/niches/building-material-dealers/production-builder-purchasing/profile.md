# Production Builder Purchasing & Lumber-Cost Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/building-material-dealers|Building Material Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The purchasing and estimating departments of the national and large regional home builders, which take off each plan once, bid framing, truss, window and millwork packages to dealers, negotiate per-plan prices and index terms, score suppliers and track lumber cost per house against the market. They are the dealers' largest customers and the party that researches dealers to price their packages.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–50 purchasing analysts and estimators at each national builder (estimate); estimators taking off plans are production
**Output:** Supplier bids and awards, per-plan cost models, supplier scorecards, lumber cost forecasts for margin guidance
**Proprietary data:** Competing dealers' bids on the same plans across divisions and years, and the builder's own cost per house
**Clock:** The builder's own bid cycles and quarterly reporting
**Buyer:** VP of purchasing at the builder
