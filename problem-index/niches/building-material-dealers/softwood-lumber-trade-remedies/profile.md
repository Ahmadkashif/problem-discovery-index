# Softwood Lumber Trade Remedies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/building-material-dealers|Building Material Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Commerce Department's Enforcement and Compliance teams that run the anti-dumping and countervailing duty orders on Canadian softwood lumber, reviewing each period's prices and provincial stumpage programmes and setting company rates, plus the Section 232 action on timber and lumber. Commerce announced its seventh-review final results on 14 October 2026 (All Others 10.64% AD plus 14.11% CVD, 24.75%, against 35.16% before); a 10% Section 232 tariff has applied since 14 October 2025. Dealers and wholesalers buying Canadian lumber pay the result through their suppliers' prices.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–40 case analysts and accountants across the softwood AD and CVD reviews at any time (estimate): each annual review covers several mandatory respondents in the AD case and federal and multiple provincial stumpage and subsidy programmes in the CVD case
**Output:** Preliminary and final review results, company-specific rates, issues and decision memoranda
**Proprietary data:** Respondents' sales, cost and stumpage submissions, much of it business proprietary
**Clock:** Statutory review deadlines for preliminary and final results, extendable within limits
**Buyer:** None in the commercial sense
