# Lumber & Panel Price Assessments

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/building-material-dealers|Building Material Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Price reporting services that assess where framing lumber, boards and structural panels actually trade, by species, grade, size, length and region, and sell the weekly and daily numbers with market commentary to sawmills, panel mills, wholesalers, treaters, dealers, builders and traders. Fastmarkets' forest products service, which carries the Random Lengths Weekly Report and the Framing Lumber (15 prices) and Structural Panel (11 items) composites, is the dominant one; Madison's Lumber Reporter is a smaller Canadian peer (recalled). Mills and wholesalers sell "on print" against the published numbers, and dealers use them to reprice quotes and builder programmes.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 forest-products reporters, editors and analysts at the main agency (estimate)
**Output:** Weekly and daily price assessments, composites, market commentary, short-term outlooks
**Proprietary data:** Reporter-collected trade intelligence from mills, wholesalers and buyers across hundreds of items, and the assessment history behind every number
**Clock:** Weekly publication calendars the agency sets; sales priced "on print" and some supply contracts reference the published figures
**Buyer:** Editorial director or head of forest products pricing
