# Variance POs Without a Cause

**Niche:** [[niches/building-material-dealers/production-builder-supply-programs/profile|Production Builder Supply Programmes]]
**Industry:** [[industries/building-material-dealers|Building Material Dealers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Variance POs and back-charges decide a dealer's standing with a builder, and they are coded so loosely that nobody can say which plans, sites or crews cause them.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #k-nearest-neighbors #data-integration #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to supply every start of a production builder's plans complete and on the builder's schedule at the per-plan price agreed for the period, and whoever runs the fewest short, late or variance-PO starts per hundred at that price takes the account.

## The Problem
Every extra the builder approves and every cost it claws back comes with a free-text reason. Over a year they add up to the number the builder's purchasing team brings to the renewal.

## Why It's Still Broken
Portals allow free text, dealers accept the builder's coding, and the outside salesperson negotiates disputes one at a time.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Classify every variance PO and back-charge by cause (plan error, takeoff miss, site damage, theft, builder change, late delivery) from its text and related tickets, report them by plan and superintendent, and contest the ones that belong to the builder.

## Who Feels the Pain
Outside salespeople defending the account, estimators blamed for builder changes, and the dealer's margin.

## Impact If Fixed
Contesting the ~20–30% of back-charges that belong to the builder (estimate) recovers real money on a large programme and turns the renewal from a complaint into a fix list.
