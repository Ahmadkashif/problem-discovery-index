# LBM Dealer & Lumber Wholesale Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/building-material-dealers|Building Material Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The trade associations of the channel: the National Lumber and Building Material Dealers Association and its regional federations (such as the Northeastern Retail Lumber Association), the North American Wholesale Lumber Association for two-step wholesalers, and the Structural Building Components Association for truss and panel plants (recalled). They lobby, run conferences and education, and publish benchmarks and surveys, funded by dues and events. NAW, which spans wholesale distribution, sits in [[niches/industrial-distributors/industrial-distribution-associations/profile|Industrial Distribution Trade Associations]]; contractor-side groups are [[niches/trade-associations/construction-trade-groups/profile|Construction Trade Groups]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research staff at each; benchmarks are commonly produced with outside firms (estimate)
**Output:** Operating benchmarks, wage and safety surveys, policy analysis, education
**Proprietary data:** Member survey responses
**Clock:** Annual conference and report calendars
**Buyer:** Association CEO
