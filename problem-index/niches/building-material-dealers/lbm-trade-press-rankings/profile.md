# LBM Trade Press & Dealer Rankings

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/building-material-dealers|Building Material Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Trade publications for lumber and building-material dealers — LBM Journal (with its annual LBM 100 dealer ranking), ProSales, HBSDealer and regional titles (recalled) — publishing news, dealer rankings by sales, technology surveys and market commentary, funded by advertising, events and sponsored content. The industrial and electrical counterparts are [[niches/industrial-distributors/distribution-market-research-publishers/profile|Distribution Market Research Publishers]] and [[niches/electrical-distributors/electrical-trade-press-estimates/profile|Electrical Trade Press & Sales-Potential Estimates]], both gate failures.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research staff per title; the larger staff is editorial (estimate)
**Output:** Rankings, surveys, news and sponsored reports
**Proprietary data:** Self-reported dealer sales and survey responses
**Clock:** Publication and event calendars the publishers set
**Buyer:** Publisher
