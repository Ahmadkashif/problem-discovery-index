# Lumber Trade Credit Ratings

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/building-material-dealers|Building Material Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Industry-specific credit and pay ratings on mills, wholesalers, dealers and traders in the lumber business, used by credit managers before selling on terms. Lumbermens supplied them from 1876 until it closed in late 2007; Lumber Blue Book filled the gap from 2009, and 526 Media Group bought it in February 2025, continuing it as Red Book Lumber Data (dates from trade-press reports, search summary). Subscribers pay for ratings, reports and trade-experience data. Supplier trade credit more broadly is [[niches/credit-bureaus/commercial-trade-credit/profile|Commercial Trade Credit Reporting]], and the large bureaus are [[niches/independent-retailers/commercial-credit-bureaus/profile|Commercial Credit Bureaus]] (56, indexed).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 credit analysts (estimate)
**Output:** Credit and pay ratings, company reports, trade-experience summaries
**Proprietary data:** Trade experience contributed by subscribers and rating histories on the lumber trade
**Clock:** None external; ratings update as experience arrives
**Buyer:** Publisher or head of ratings
