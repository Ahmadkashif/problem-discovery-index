# Quotes That Wait for the Owner

**Niche:** [[niches/building-material-dealers/small-independent-lumberyards/profile|Small Independent Lumberyards & Building Supply Dealers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/building-material-dealers|Building Material Dealers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Counter staff can't send a lumber quote because only the owner knows what the yard really pays this week, so quotes queue until evening.
**Tags:** #exponential-smoothing #time-series-forecasting #data-integration #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a small yard, where the owner still prices most quotes, to answer every customer request — a walk-in or phoned list, an emailed list or a plan set — on the day it arrives, and whoever lifts the yard's same-day quote rate highest takes the account.

## The Problem
In a small yard the point-of-sale prices for commodity lumber and panels are whatever the last truck cost, so staff know the shelf price is stale and hand anything sizeable to the owner.

## Why It's Still Broken
There is no commodity buyer, market reports cost time to read, and the system has no notion of replacement cost or margin floor, so delegation feels risky.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A weekly replacement-cost update for the yard's top commodity items from the co-op's buys or a market reference, with a margin floor per customer type, so counter staff can quote anything above the floor and route only exceptions to the owner.

## Who Feels the Pain
Owners pricing quotes at night, counter staff telling customers to wait, and customers who go elsewhere.

## Impact If Fixed
If staff can send ~70% of quotes without the owner (estimate), most requests are answered the same day, and on ~$3–5M of annual commodity sales (estimate) the yard also stops selling below replacement cost in a rising month.
