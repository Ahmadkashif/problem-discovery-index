# A Same-Day Quote Desk Priced for a Ten-Person Yard

**Niche:** [[niches/building-material-dealers/small-independent-lumberyards/profile|Small Independent Lumberyards & Building Supply Dealers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/building-material-dealers|Building Material Dealers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A small yard's quotes queue behind its owner; a quote desk that prices lists and plan sets from the yard's own file, as a service with no integration, gets most of them answered the day they arrive.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #object-detection #k-nearest-neighbors #tacit-knowledge-ml #revenue-impact #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a small yard, where the owner still prices most quotes, to answer every customer request — a walk-in or phoned list, an emailed list or a plan set — on the day it arrives, and whoever lifts the yard's same-day quote rate highest takes the account.

## The Problem
A remodeler leaves a list at the counter, a farmer phones in a pole-barn package, a builder emails a plan set. At a ten-person yard all three wait for the owner, who knows the costs, and the regional chain answers first.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Quoting and takeoff products assume an estimator or inside-sales team to operate them, and AI vendors' onboarding (ERP integration, data mapping) costs more than a small yard will pay. The cross-distribution intake vendors profiled at [[niches/industrial-distributors/rfq-and-order-intake-automation/profile|RFQ & Order Intake Automation]] sell to large inboxes, not to a counter with one phone.

## What to Build
A quote desk with no integration: the yard uploads its price file weekly (or the co-op supplies it), forwards every request — a photo of a counter list, a phoned order typed in by staff, an email, a plan set — and receives a priced quote in its own SKUs with margin floors applied, plus a board showing each request's age. Counter staff send quotes the owner would have priced; the owner's edits teach the desk the yard's habits. A two-week demo needs a sample price file, a few handwritten lists and one public plan set.

## Target Customer
Owners of yards with 5–30 employees, reached through co-ops and regional dealer associations.

## Impact If Built
If the same-day quote rate rises from ~40% to ~80% of requests (estimate) without hiring a ~$70,000-a-year estimator (estimate), the yard keeps the builders and remodelers who would otherwise go to the chain that answered first.
