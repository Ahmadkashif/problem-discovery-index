# Co-Op Catalogue Data Turned Into Quotes

**Niche:** [[niches/building-material-dealers/small-independent-lumberyards/profile|Small Independent Lumberyards & Building Supply Dealers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/building-material-dealers|Building Material Dealers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Co-ops already hold clean item data and programme prices for thousands of members; little of it reaches the member's quotes.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #k-nearest-neighbors #data-integration #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a small yard, where the owner still prices most quotes, to answer every customer request — a walk-in or phoned list, an emailed list or a plan set — on the day it arrives, and whoever lifts the yard's same-day quote rate highest takes the account.

## The Problem
The small yard's item file is thin and inconsistent, so every quote involves looking items up in co-op catalogues and vendor price sheets by hand.

## What Already Exists
Co-op catalogues and programme pricing, point-of-sale and ERP systems sold through co-ops, and general quoting tools.

## The Customization Gap
Mapping co-op item data to each member's item file, keeping programme costs and rebates current, and offering quote templates for common packages (decks, garages, pole barns, additions) priced from that data, inside the systems members already run.

## Target Customer
Co-ops serving independent yards, and the members themselves.

## Impact If Solved
Common packages quoted in an hour instead of half a day (estimate), and a stronger reason for members to stay in the co-op as consolidation accelerates.
