# The Job Account Opened After the First Load

**Niche:** [[niches/building-material-dealers/contractor-credit-lien-rights/profile|Contractor Credit, Job Accounts & Lien Rights]]
**Industry:** [[industries/building-material-dealers|Building Material Dealers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Outside salespeople ship the first load on the builder's general account and open the job account later, so the notice clock has been running unseen.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #k-nearest-neighbors #compliance #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a dealer's losses on contractor credit down while it keeps selling on account, and whoever delivers the lowest write-offs as a share of credit sales takes the account.

## The Problem
The builder needs the foundation package tomorrow; the job paperwork follows. By the time credit opens the job, a week or two of the notice window has gone.

## Why It's Still Broken
Order entry doesn't require a job record, and the credit department has no feed of new delivery addresses.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Flag any delivery to a new address on a builder account as a new job, open a provisional job record automatically, and send credit the address and first-delivery date the same day.

## Who Feels the Pain
Credit managers chasing deadlines they couldn't see, and owners writing off jobs whose lien rights lapsed.

## Impact If Fixed
Every job's notice clock starts on the right day; recovering one or two weeks of California's 20-day preliminary-notice window is the difference between full and partial lien rights on a job that goes bad.
