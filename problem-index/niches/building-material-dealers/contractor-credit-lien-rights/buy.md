# Receivables Software Taught Job Accounts

**Niche:** [[niches/building-material-dealers/contractor-credit-lien-rights/profile|Contractor Credit, Job Accounts & Lien Rights]]
**Industry:** [[industries/building-material-dealers|Building Material Dealers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Accounts-receivable automation chases invoices by customer; a dealer has to manage credit by job, with lien rights, waivers and joint checks attached to each.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #logistic-regression #data-integration #compliance #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a dealer's losses on contractor credit down while it keeps selling on account, and whoever delivers the lowest write-offs as a share of credit sales takes the account.

## The Problem
A builder may have twenty jobs open with one dealer. Payments arrive against some, waivers are requested on others, and a lender's joint check covers a third. Generic AR tools see one customer balance.

## What Already Exists
AR automation and collections platforms, credit-application tools such as Nuvo, commercial bureau scores, and notice services.

## The Customization Gap
Job-level ledgers with owner and lender attached, conditional and unconditional lien waiver tracking matched to payments, joint-check handling, state notice and lien deadlines per job, and collection scripts that know a lien is the dealer's leverage. Dunning by customer alone gets this wrong.

## Target Customer
Credit and collections teams at regional dealers.

## Impact If Solved
Cash applied to the right job and waivers never given ahead of payment; a collector handling ~200 active jobs (estimate) stops reconciling waivers by hand and spends the time on the ~10% of jobs at risk (estimate).
