# Building-Products Manufacturers' Channel & Pro Insight Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/building-material-dealers|Building Material Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The channel, pricing and pro-marketing intelligence teams at manufacturers that sell through dealers and two-step wholesalers — engineered wood and panels, siding and trim, roofing, windows and doors, decking (Weyerhaeuser, LP, Boise Cascade, James Hardie, Owens Corning, GAF, Andersen, Pella, Trex and peers, recalled). They analyse dealer sell-through and rebate claims, set dealer programmes, track pro preference and size end markets for their own sales and pricing decisions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–50 per manufacturer (estimate)
**Output:** Channel programme design, dealer performance and rebate analysis, pro-preference and share studies, market sizing
**Proprietary data:** Dealer and distributor sell-through, rebate and programme data across the manufacturer's channel
**Clock:** Annual programme cycles, price-increase announcements and buying shows
**Buyer:** VP of channel or pro marketing
