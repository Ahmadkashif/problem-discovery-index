# General AI Takeoff Taught the Yard's Framing Rules

**Niche:** [[niches/building-material-dealers/plan-set-takeoff-quoting/profile|Plan-Set Takeoff & Lumber Package Quoting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/building-material-dealers|Building Material Dealers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** General AI takeoff tools measure areas and count symbols; a lumber package needs those measurements turned into studs, plates, headers and sheets the way this yard and its framers build.
**Tags:** #object-detection #large-language-models #transfer-learning #data-integration #automation #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a builder's or remodeler's plan set into a complete, priced lumber package before the builder picks a supplier, and whoever wins the highest share of the plan sets it quotes takes the account.

## The Problem
AI takeoff tools sold to contractors and suppliers can measure wall lengths, count openings and compute roof area on a clean plan. The estimator still has to convert those quantities into a framing list, apply waste and price it.

## What Already Exists
General on-screen and AI takeoff tools (STACK and others, plus newer AI tools marketed to contractors and suppliers), Paradigm's takeoff service for lumberyards, and Higharc's distributor estimating built on its home-design model.

## The Customization Gap
A framing layer on top of measurement: wall framing rules by height and spacing, header and beam sizing by span and load path, corner and blocking conventions, sheathing layout and sheet counts, engineered-lumber calls, plate-length optimisation to stocked lengths, waste by category and builder, and mapping to the dealer's SKUs and price file in its ERP (BisTrack, Agility, Spruce). Regional code differences (wind and seismic zones, energy-code wall depth) change the rules.

## Target Customer
Dealers with an estimating team and some IT capacity that prefer to assemble the capability from general tools.

## Impact If Solved
Measurement automation only pays when it lands as a priced package in the ERP: turning measurements into a framing list is about half of a ~2-hour takeoff (estimate), so the framing layer is where the hours are.
