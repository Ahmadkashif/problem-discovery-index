# Plan-Set Takeoff & Lumber Package Quoting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/building-material-dealers|Building Material Dealers]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a builder's or remodeler's plan set into a complete, priced lumber package before the builder picks a supplier, and whoever wins the highest share of the plan sets it quotes takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.5B a year of estimating labour and takeoff tools at US dealers and component plants (estimate: ~12,000–18,000 estimators and takeoff specialists plus software and outsourced takeoff services)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.3% of the codes' receipts in cost (estimate); it decides the bids behind most of the ~$180B pro-dealer niche
**Digital Adoption:** Low — plans are read on screen, but quantities, assumptions and waste are judged by hand
**Target Buyer:** Estimating manager or general manager at a pro dealer
**Automation Potential:** High for drafting quantities from sheets; medium for the estimator's adjustments

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is the pre-award contest and the owner's MVP: a public plan set in, a priced lumber list out. The input is a set of drawings that rarely includes framing plans; the output must be complete, priced against the week's market and returned before two other yards answer. It differs from emailed list and PO intake, where the customer has already itemised the order and the contest is matching lines without retyping; that vendor field is profiled once at [[niches/industrial-distributors/rfq-and-order-intake-automation/profile|RFQ & Order Intake Automation]]. Here the contest is what the drawings leave out. Its electrical analogue is [[niches/electrical-distributors/bid-day-project-quoting/profile|Bid-Day Project Quoting & Takeoff]]; contractor-side estimating is [[niches/general-contractors/estimating-bidding/profile|Estimating & Bidding Operations]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Paradigm Estimate (a takeoff service owned by Builders FirstSource, returning SKU-mapped lists in as few as three days by its own account, search summary), STACK and other on-screen takeoff tools, and Higharc's AI Estimating, launched for distributors with US LBM in June 2026. Higharc had forward deployed and ML engineering roles open on 2026-10-24. The gaps: no measured hit rate (builders rarely say why a yard lost), no record of the estimator's assumptions, no grading against what the job consumed, and turnaround measured in days.
