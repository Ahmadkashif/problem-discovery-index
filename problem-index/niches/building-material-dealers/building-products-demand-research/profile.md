# Building-Products Channel & Housing Demand Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/building-material-dealers|Building Material Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Research and consulting firms that forecast housing starts and remodelling demand and study the building-products channel — which dealers, distributors and contractors buy which brands, how pros choose suppliers, where share is moving — for building-products manufacturers, dealers, builders and investors. Zonda and John Burns Research and Consulting work from builder and new-home surveys; Principia and The Farnsworth Group survey pros and the channel; the Home Improvement Research Institute pools remodelling research for its members (all recalled, not individually checked). Clients pay subscriptions and study fees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–60 analysts and consultants per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Housing and remodelling forecasts, channel share and pro-buyer studies, market sizing, custom studies
**Proprietary data:** Builder, contractor and dealer survey panels and new-home community data collected over years, alongside public Census starts and permits
**Clock:** Release calendars the firms set and clients' annual planning cycles
**Buyer:** Head of research or managing principal
