# Builder Portals Turned Into a Supplier Scorecard

**Niche:** [[niches/building-material-dealers/pro-dealer-contractor-supply/profile|Pro Dealer Supply to Builders & Remodelers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/building-material-dealers|Building Material Dealers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Builder-supplier portals already carry every PO, variance PO and schedule change; the dealer uses them to receive orders, not to measure itself the way the builder's purchasing team does.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #time-series-forecasting #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is "every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to be the builder's and remodeler's lumber and components supplier," but before award that fight is the fastest complete priced package from a plan set, won by estimating desks and takeoff tools, and after award it is every start delivered complete on the builder's schedule at the agreed price, won by scale, component capacity and logistics. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Production builders score suppliers on completeness, on-time delivery and variance POs, and decide renewals on it. The dealer often learns its score in the renewal meeting.

## What Already Exists
Builder-supplier platforms such as Hyphen's SupplyPro carry purchase orders, schedule releases and variance POs between builders and suppliers; BI tools and the dealer's ERP can report on them.

## The Customization Gap
A dealer-side scorecard built from the portal and ERP: per builder division and plan, starts supplied, short or late items, variance POs by cause, back-charges, and the forecast of next month's starts from the builder's own schedule releases. It needs plan-level price tracking against the lumber market so the dealer can see which plans lose money as costs move. Generic BI reports orders; the dealer needs the builder's view of the dealer.

## Target Customer
Outside sales managers and general managers serving production builders.

## Impact If Solved
Walking into a renewal with the builder's own numbers; if fixing the worst ~10% of plans and sites halves their variance POs (estimate), the dealer's score moves before the builder's purchasing team moves the account.
