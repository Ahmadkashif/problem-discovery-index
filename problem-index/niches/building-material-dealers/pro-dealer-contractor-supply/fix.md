## The Problem
Add-on orders are the most common and least examined event in pro dealing: a short package, a plan change, a damaged unit, a framer who used the material elsewhere. Each costs a truck run and an apology, and some are never billed.

## Why It's Still Broken
The call comes to the counter or the outside salesperson, who keys a new order under the job name. No field asks why, and the original package is in another ticket.

## What a Fix Looks Like
At order entry, link every add-on to its job's original package automatically (job name, address and builder matching), ask for a one-tap cause, and route takeoff-caused add-ons to the estimator weekly. Bill plan changes back to the builder by default.

## Who Feels the Pain
Drivers sent out for small loads, estimators blamed without evidence, outside salespeople absorbing the builder's frustration, and the dealer's margin.

## Impact If Fixed
If add-ons run ~5–15% of package value (estimate), tracing their causes is the cheapest way to cut them, and the cause codes are the label set for every later takeoff model.
