# Pro Dealer Supply to Builders & Remodelers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/building-material-dealers|Building Material Dealers]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is "every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to be the builder's and remodeler's lumber and components supplier," but before award that fight is the fastest complete priced package from a plan set, won by estimating desks and takeoff tools, and after award it is every start delivered complete on the builder's schedule at the agreed price, won by scale, component capacity and logistics. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$180B a year of dealer sales of lumber, panels, components, windows, doors and millwork to home builders, remodelers and framers (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~38% of the codes' ~$475B receipts (estimate; the receipts double-count two-step wholesale)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — national builders send POs through portals and the rollups run digital ordering, but custom and remodel work still starts with a PDF plan set and a phone call
**Target Buyer:** VP of sales or general manager at a pro dealer; the estimating manager
**Automation Potential:** High for takeoff drafting and order intake; medium for programme pricing and scheduling

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is the core of the pro dealer: selling the structure of a house, and increasingly its windows, doors, trim and installation, to builders who choose suppliers on price, completeness and reliability. Builders FirstSource sold $15.2B in 2025 across ~585 locations, nearly half of it value-added (manufactured components and windows, doors and millwork), and US LBM, QXO's Kodiak and hundreds of regional dealers compete with it. The niche splits by when the contest happens. Before award, a plan set has to become a priced package faster and more completely than the two other yards bidding it. After award, a production builder's starts have to be supplied plan by plan at the price agreed for the period, with every short-ship, variance PO and back-charge counted. Different people win each: the estimating desk and its tools, then the operation.

## Current Tools & Gaps
LBM ERPs (Epicor BisTrack, DMSi Agility, ECI Spruce), takeoff services and tools (Paradigm Estimate, STACK, Higharc's new AI Estimating), builder-supplier portals (Hyphen SupplyPro), and the dealers' own digital platforms (Builders FirstSource's myBLDR). The gaps: takeoffs never graded against job outcomes, and programme performance measured by complaint rather than by start.
