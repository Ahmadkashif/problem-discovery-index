# Failed Drops With No Cause Recorded

**Niche:** [[niches/building-material-dealers/job-site-delivery-dispatch/profile|Job-Site Delivery Dispatch & Yard Management]]
**Industry:** [[industries/building-material-dealers|Building Material Dealers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** When a load comes back, the ticket is reissued and the reason is lost, so the same builders and sites fail again.
**Tags:** #logistic-regression #large-language-models #quick-win #worker-facing #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get each load to a ready site, built in the right order and dropped in the right spot on the day promised, and whoever runs the fewest failed or second-truck deliveries per hundred drops takes the account.

## The Problem
Drivers know why a drop failed; the system records only that the ticket was re-delivered.

## Why It's Still Broken
Driver apps, where they exist, capture a signature and a photo, not a coded reason, and nobody reviews failures by cause.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A one-tap failure reason and photo at the site, rolled up weekly by builder, superintendent, branch and cause, with the worst repeat sites flagged to outside sales.

## Who Feels the Pain
Drivers blamed for site problems, yard crews rebuilding loads, and outside salespeople who never see the pattern.

## Impact If Fixed
If a few builders and sites cause most failures (estimate), coded causes let a dealer cut failed drops by about a third (estimate) with conversations rather than a new system, collected in seconds per drop.
