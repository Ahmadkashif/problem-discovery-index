# Truss Plate & Component Design-Software Suppliers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/building-material-dealers|Building Material Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The connector-plate manufacturers that supply truss and wall-panel plants with plates, design and layout software, plant automation and engineering review: MiTek (a Berkshire Hathaway company) and Alpine (ITW) are the two dominant ones (recalled). Plants buy plates and get the software and engineering seal service as part of the relationship. The dealer-side design contest is [[niches/building-material-dealers/truss-wall-panel-designers/profile|Truss & Wall-Panel Component Designers]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** Engineering teams that review and seal truss designs, and software teams that build the product; neither is an insight function, since sealing engineers and designers are production (estimate)
**Output:** Design software, sealed truss designs, plant equipment
**Proprietary data:** Millions of truss designs and plant production records across their customers
**Clock:** Builders' and plants' production schedules
**Buyer:** Head of engineering or software at the supplier
