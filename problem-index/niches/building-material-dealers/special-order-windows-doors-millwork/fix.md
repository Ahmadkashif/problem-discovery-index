# The Handing Nobody Checked

**Niche:** [[niches/building-material-dealers/special-order-windows-doors-millwork/profile|Special-Order Windows, Doors & Millwork]]
**Industry:** [[industries/building-material-dealers|Building Material Dealers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Door handing and swing are the classic special-order error, and the floor plan that settles them is never consulted at order entry.
**Tags:** #object-detection #large-language-models #quick-win #worker-facing #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get each special-order window, door and millwork unit configured, ordered and delivered right the first time against the house's openings, and whoever posts the lowest remake rate on special-order units takes the account.

## The Problem
Left-hand versus right-hand, in-swing versus out-swing: conventions differ by manufacturer and the builder's list often omits it. A wrong door arrives pre-hung and can't be flipped.

## Why It's Still Broken
The specialist configures from the schedule; the swing is drawn on the plan, and nobody cross-checks the two.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Read door swings from the floor plan, translate them into each manufacturer's handing convention, and require confirmation where the order disagrees.

## Who Feels the Pain
Window and door specialists, trim carpenters waiting on site, and the dealer paying for the remake.

## Impact If Fixed
If handing and swing errors are about a fifth of remakes (estimate), one check removes a fifth of the remake bill.
