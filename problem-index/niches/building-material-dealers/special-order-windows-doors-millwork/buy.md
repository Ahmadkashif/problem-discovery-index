# Quote Integration Extended to Acknowledgements

**Niche:** [[niches/building-material-dealers/special-order-windows-doors-millwork/profile|Special-Order Windows, Doors & Millwork]]
**Industry:** [[industries/building-material-dealers|Building Material Dealers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Integrators already move configured quotes into dealer ERPs; the acknowledgement that comes back from the factory is still a PDF nobody compares.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #k-nearest-neighbors #data-integration #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get each special-order window, door and millwork unit configured, ordered and delivered right the first time against the house's openings, and whoever posts the lowest remake rate on special-order units takes the account.

## The Problem
Factories confirm each order with an acknowledgement listing what they will build. Differences from the order — a size, a glass option, a colour — are the last chance to catch an error before production.

## What Already Exists
Quote-to-ERP integration (Saberis and similar), manufacturer dealer portals, and general document extraction.

## The Customization Gap
Parsing each manufacturer's acknowledgement format, matching lines to the dealer's order and configured quote by unit and attribute, flagging differences by severity, and opening a ticket with the manufacturer inside the acknowledgement window. It must handle mulled units, options codes and price changes.

## Target Customer
Dealers with several window and door brands and a high special-order volume.

## Impact If Solved
If acknowledgement checks catch half the remaining factory-side mismatches (estimate), a dealer ordering ~5,000 special-order units a year avoids ~25–50 remakes (estimate), with nothing new to learn for the specialist.
