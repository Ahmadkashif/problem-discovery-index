# Cloud Pricing Data Trackers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cloud-infrastructure-consultants|Cloud Infrastructure Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Small teams that track and normalize published cloud pricing across providers, regions, and instance families, and publish comparison tooling and price indices that architects use to reason about platform cost.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 3-15 data engineers and analysts
**Output:** Normalized pricing datasets, comparison tools, price movement indices
**Proprietary data:** Historical published-price series, normalized — genuinely useful and derived entirely from public list prices
**Clock:** Provider pricing announcements
**Buyer:** Head of Data
