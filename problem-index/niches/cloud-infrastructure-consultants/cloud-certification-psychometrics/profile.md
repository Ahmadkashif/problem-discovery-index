# Cloud Certification Programme Psychometrics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cloud-infrastructure-consultants|Cloud Infrastructure Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The certification programme teams at AWS, Microsoft, and Google, together with the testing providers who deliver their exams, define what a cloud architect is supposed to know. They run job task analyses, write and calibrate items, set cut scores, and manage exam security across millions of candidates — and consultancies hire and price against the resulting credentials.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 psychometricians, item writers, subject matter experts, and programme staff
**Output:** Certification examinations, blueprints, technical reports, credential frameworks
**Proprietary data:** Item response and candidate performance data across millions of exam administrations, plus the job task analyses that define the roles
**Clock:** Exam release cycles tied to platform service launches, and recertification windows
**Buyer:** Director of Certification / Head of Assessment
