# IT Sourcing & Price Benchmark Advisors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cloud-infrastructure-consultants|Cloud Infrastructure Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
ISG, NPI, UpperEdge, and comparable firms tell enterprises what they should be paying. Analysts maintain price benchmark databases built from thousands of negotiated technology and cloud contracts, then produce the written benchmark analysis a client takes into a renewal negotiation. The report is the invoice and it is used adversarially against the vendor across the table.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-800 benchmark analysts, sourcing advisors, and researchers
**Output:** Price benchmark analyses, contract term assessments, sourcing market indices
**Proprietary data:** Actual negotiated pricing and contract terms across thousands of deals — data neither vendors nor buyers publish and that no public source contains
**Clock:** Contract renewal and negotiation deadlines, which are fixed and expensive to miss
**Buyer:** Managing Director of Benchmarking / Head of Research
