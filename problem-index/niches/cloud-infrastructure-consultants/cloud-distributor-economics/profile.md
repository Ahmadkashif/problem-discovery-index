# Cloud Distributor & Marketplace Economics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cloud-infrastructure-consultants|Cloud Infrastructure Consultants]]

## What They Do
Pax8, Ingram Micro's cloud business, and TD Synnex intermediate cloud subscriptions to tens of thousands of resellers. Their analytics groups model attach rates, churn, and margin across the partner base and publish partner-facing benchmark reporting.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 analysts and data staff
**Output:** Partner benchmark reporting, attach and churn analytics, vendor performance analysis
**Proprietary data:** Subscription, attach, and churn data across tens of thousands of resellers and their end customers
**Clock:** Vendor programme cycles and quarterly reporting
**Buyer:** VP of Analytics / Chief Revenue Officer
