# Cloud Security Research Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cloud-infrastructure-consultants|Cloud Infrastructure Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Wiz Research, Orca's research group, Datadog Security Labs, and comparable teams discover cloud misconfiguration and vulnerability classes, publish threat research, and encode findings into the detection rules their platforms ship. The published research is a demand generation asset; the detection content is the product.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 security researchers, detection engineers, and analysts
**Output:** Threat research publications, detection rules, cloud risk benchmarks
**Proprietary data:** Configuration and exposure telemetry across thousands of customer cloud environments — the only cross-organization view of how cloud is actually misconfigured
**Clock:** Vulnerability disclosure timelines and incident response events
**Buyer:** VP of Research / Chief Technology Officer
