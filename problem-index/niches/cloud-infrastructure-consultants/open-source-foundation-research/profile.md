# Open Source Foundation Research Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cloud-infrastructure-consultants|Cloud Infrastructure Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The CNCF, the Linux Foundation's research arm, and comparable bodies run the adoption surveys and project health analyses that shape which cloud native technologies enterprises standardize on. Output is published free to drive ecosystem adoption.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 3-12 researchers
**Output:** Adoption surveys, landscape analyses, project health reporting
**Proprietary data:** Survey series and project contribution telemetry, most of it public by design
**Clock:** Conference and release calendars
**Buyer:** Director of Research
