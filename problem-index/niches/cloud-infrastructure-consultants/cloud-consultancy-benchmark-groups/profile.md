# Consultancy Financial Benchmark Groups

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cloud-infrastructure-consultants|Cloud Infrastructure Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Peer group facilitators and boutique advisors who benchmark MSP and consultancy financials — utilization, effective rate, recurring revenue mix, gross margin by service line — and publish the operating benchmarks the channel plans against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 2-10
**Output:** Financial benchmark reports, peer group facilitation, valuation guidance
**Proprietary data:** Financial data contributed by participating consultancies
**Clock:** Quarterly peer meetings and annual survey cycles
**Buyer:** Principal
