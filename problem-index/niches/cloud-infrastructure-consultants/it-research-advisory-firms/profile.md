# IT Research & Advisory Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cloud-infrastructure-consultants|Cloud Infrastructure Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Gartner, Forrester, IDC, and Info-Tech employ thousands of analysts who research technology markets, evaluate vendors, take client inquiry calls, and publish the ranked evaluations and market forecasts that enterprises buy technology against. Subscriptions are the revenue and the research is the entire product. Cloud consultancies sit downstream of it: their clients' architecture and vendor decisions are shaped by these publications before the consultant is engaged.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1,000-4,000 analysts, researchers, and data staff at the largest firms
**Output:** Vendor evaluations, market forecasts, inquiry responses, peer benchmarks — the subscription itself
**Proprietary data:** Millions of recorded client inquiry interactions revealing what enterprises are actually deciding, plus vendor briefing history and peer benchmarking data — a corpus no competitor can construct without the same installed base
**Clock:** Publication calendars, event schedules, and client contract renewal cycles
**Buyer:** Chief of Research / SVP Research Operations
