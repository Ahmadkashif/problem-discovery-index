## Why These Niches

Enterprise migration and cost optimization dominate cloud consulting revenue, making them the natural high-share segments. SMB onboarding and mainframe migration represent digitally neglected areas where consulting delivery still relies on manual discovery and tribal knowledge rather than standardized tooling. Regulated-industry cloud and startup DevOps are underserved because generic consulting frameworks don't account for compliance-heavy or resource-constrained environments respectively. Security auditing and IaC drift remediation are rule-heavy workflows ripe for automation — both involve scanning configurations against known-good baselines, a process currently done via spreadsheets and manual review cycles. Excluded: hyper-specific vendor certifications (e.g., Snowflake-only consulting) and training-only firms that don't deliver implementation.

## Why These Pockets

This industry contains the archetype. IT research and advisory firms are the largest and purest instance of the target shape found anywhere in the sweep so far — thousands of analysts, subscription revenue, published judgment as the entire product, and a proprietary corpus of client inquiry that no competitor can construct. The Pass 1 note observes that cloud consultants' highest-value work depends on senior architects whose pattern recognition is experiential and transfers slowly. Two positions above them, an industry exists that sells exactly that pattern recognition at scale and has the same problem in a more expensive form.

The gaps in both qualifiers are the same three, which is itself the finding. Neither scores its own output: advisory firms publish thousands of dated, checkable predictions a year and maintain no scorecard, and benchmark advisors tell clients what to pay and do not systematically capture what the client actually paid. Neither structures its judgment: an advisory firm's house position on a vendor is expressed across thousands of documents with no supersession model, and a benchmark advisor's comparable set — the entire analytical act — is made from memory and recorded only as a number. And both hold a demand signal they ignore: inquiry calls at one, engagement context at the other.

Eleven pockets logged without qualifying, and one is a near miss worth noting. FinOps vendors score 49 and hold what is arguably the best asset in the industry — cloud spend and utilization across thousands of organizations, the only cross-organization view of what cloud actually costs at real workloads — and sell it as a software subscription rather than as research. The largest insight workforce found here, at up to two thousand people, is hyperscaler solutions architecture, which scores 39 because all of it exists to sell platform consumption.
