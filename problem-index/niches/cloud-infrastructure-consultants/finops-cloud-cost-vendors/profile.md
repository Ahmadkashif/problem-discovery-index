# FinOps & Cloud Cost Management Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cloud-infrastructure-consultants|Cloud Infrastructure Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
CloudHealth, Apptio Cloudability, Flexera, Vantage, and ProsperOps ingest cloud billing data from thousands of organizations and turn it into waste identification, commitment optimization, and cost benchmarking. The analysis is the product, and it does much of the work senior cloud architects at consultancies are hired to do by eye.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 data scientists, cloud economists, and product analysts
**Output:** Optimization recommendations, cost benchmarks, commitment strategy analysis
**Proprietary data:** Cloud spend and utilization across thousands of organizations — the only cross-organization view of what cloud actually costs at real workloads
**Clock:** Monthly billing cycles and commitment expiry dates
**Buyer:** VP of Data Science / Chief Product Officer
