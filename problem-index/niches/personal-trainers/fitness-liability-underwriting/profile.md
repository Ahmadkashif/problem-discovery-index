# Fitness Professional Liability Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/personal-trainers|Personal Trainers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Underwrite the professional and general liability programmes independent trainers and studios buy, and analyse the injury claims that come out of them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 underwriters, claims analysts, and risk managers
**Output:** Programme pricing, risk selection, claims analysis, loss control guidance
**Proprietary data:** Injury claims from training sessions, joined to setting, modality and credential
**Clock:** Annual policy renewals
**Buyer:** Programme Underwriting Manager
