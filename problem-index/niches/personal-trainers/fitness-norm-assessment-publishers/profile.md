# Fitness Norm & Assessment Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/personal-trainers|Personal Trainers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Publish the fitness assessment batteries and normative reference tables that trainers, schools and clinicians score clients against, and run the longitudinal cohorts those norms were derived from.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 exercise scientists, epidemiologists, and statisticians
**Output:** Assessment protocols, normative percentile tables, health-related fitness standards, cohort research
**Proprietary data:** Multi-decade cohorts linking measured fitness to subsequent morbidity and mortality — a genuinely irreplaceable dataset
**Clock:** Annual school and programme assessment cycles
**Buyer:** Research Director
