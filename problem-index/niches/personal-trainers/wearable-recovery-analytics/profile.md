# Wearable Recovery & Training Load Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/personal-trainers|Personal Trainers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The physiology and data science organisations inside the recovery wearable companies, who turn continuous heart rate, variability, sleep and movement data into daily strain and readiness guidance sold as a subscription.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 physiologists, data scientists, and research staff
**Output:** Recovery and readiness scores, training load guidance, sleep analysis, published validation research
**Proprietary data:** Continuous multi-year physiological time series across millions of individuals, with self-reported behaviour attached
**Clock:** Daily, but internally generated — nothing outside the user requires the analysis on a date
**Buyer:** VP of Data Science / Chief Physiologist
