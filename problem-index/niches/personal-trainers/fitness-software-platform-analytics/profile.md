# Fitness Management Platform Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/personal-trainers|Personal Trainers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The analytics teams inside the scheduling, billing and programme delivery platforms trainers run their businesses on, producing industry benchmark reports and in-product performance metrics.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 analysts, data scientists, and product researchers
**Output:** Industry benchmark reports, retention and pricing analytics, in-product dashboards, demand forecasting
**Proprietary data:** Booking, attendance, cancellation and payment records across a large share of independent studios and trainers
**Clock:** Continuous booking flow
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Head of Insights
