# Fitness Certification & Credentialing Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/personal-trainers|Personal Trainers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Develop, deliver and defend the accredited certification examinations that define who may call themselves a personal trainer, plus the curricula and continuing education that maintain the credential. Pass 1 notes the industry has around 350,000 certified trainers; this layer certifies all of them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 psychometricians, item writers, subject matter experts, curriculum staff, and education designers
**Output:** Certification examinations and item banks, job task analyses, curricula, continuing education requirements, accreditation documentation
**Proprietary data:** Item response data across hundreds of thousands of candidates, plus job task analysis surveys of the working profession
**Clock:** Biennial recertification cycles, continuing education deadlines, and periodic accreditation review
**Buyer:** Chief Learning Officer / VP of Certification
