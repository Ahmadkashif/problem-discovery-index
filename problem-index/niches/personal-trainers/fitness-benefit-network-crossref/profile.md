# Fitness Benefit Network Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/personal-trainers|Personal Trainers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Sit between health plans and employers on one side and gyms, studios and trainers on the other, paying for member visits and reporting to the payer on whether the benefit changed anything.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 analysts, actuaries, and network managers
**Output:** Utilisation and engagement reporting, outcome and cost-offset analysis, network adequacy and reimbursement modelling
**Proprietary data:** Visit-level attendance for millions of members joined to eligibility and, for some populations, claims
**Clock:** Monthly reimbursement cycles and annual benefit renewals
**Buyer:** VP of Analytics / Chief Actuary
