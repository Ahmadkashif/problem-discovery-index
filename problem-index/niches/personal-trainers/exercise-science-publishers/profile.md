# Exercise Science Content & Textbook Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/personal-trainers|Personal Trainers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Publish the textbooks, journals and course content the exercise science and personal training curriculum is taught from, and commission the reviews that consolidate the field's evidence.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 editors, acquisitions staff, and content developers
**Output:** Textbooks, journals, course content, practitioner reference works
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated editorial catalogue; the underlying research is published elsewhere
**Clock:** Academic term and edition cycles
**Buyer:** Editorial Director
