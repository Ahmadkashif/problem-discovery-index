# Sports Science & Athlete Performance Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/personal-trainers|Personal Trainers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Supply the load monitoring, movement tracking and injury risk analytics that professional and collegiate teams run their athletes on, and the sports science staff who interpret it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 sports scientists, data scientists, and applied research staff
**Output:** Training load and readiness dashboards, injury risk models, return-to-play analytics, benchmarking against position norms
**Proprietary data:** Longitudinal athlete movement and load data joined to injury records across many teams
**Clock:** Competitive season schedules
**Buyer:** VP of Sports Science / Head of Research
