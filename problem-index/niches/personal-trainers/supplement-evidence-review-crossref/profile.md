# Sports Supplement Evidence Review Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/personal-trainers|Personal Trainers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Read the primary literature on supplements and ergogenic aids, grade the evidence, and publish structured monographs on efficacy, dosing, interactions and safety — the reference layer trainers, coaches and supplement brands rely on when the underlying research is contradictory and mostly small.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-200 researchers, reviewers, and editors
**Output:** Evidence monographs, effect size summaries, interaction and safety databases, evidence grading
**Proprietary data:** Structured extraction and grading of a large primary literature, maintained over years
**Clock:** Continuous publication flow and periodic monograph revision
**Buyer:** Editorial Director / Chief Scientific Officer
