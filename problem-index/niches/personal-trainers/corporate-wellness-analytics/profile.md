# Corporate Wellness Programme Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/personal-trainers|Personal Trainers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Design and measure employer wellness programmes — screenings, challenges, coaching, incentive design — and report to the employer on participation, health risk change, and claims impact.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 analysts, health economists, epidemiologists, and programme designers
**Output:** Programme evaluation and ROI analysis, health risk assessment reporting, incentive design, population risk stratification
**Proprietary data:** Health risk assessments, biometric screenings, and engagement data joined to claims for enrolled populations
**Clock:** Annual benefit plan cycles and renewal negotiations
**Buyer:** Chief Analytics Officer / VP of Outcomes

**Kill switches:** **HIPAA** — biometric, health risk and claims data sit inside the plan's protected perimeter.
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold and carries a live kill switch. The literature has repeatedly shown these programmes' reported savings to be an artefact of self-selection, and the vendors evaluating them are the parties selling them.
