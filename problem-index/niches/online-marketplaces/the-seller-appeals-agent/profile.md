# The Seller Appeals Agent

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/online-marketplaces|Online Marketplaces]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give the person handling an appeal the reason for the suspension and the authority to act on it — and whoever does that takes the account, because this conversation is where a marketplace's relationship with its sellers is decided.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$0.9B US in support operations and lost supply
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~4% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** None — explaining a decision they cannot see
**Target Buyer:** Seller support organisations and their leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — the reason exists and is not delivered

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A support agent handles an appeal from a seller whose income has stopped, cannot see why the account was suspended, and cannot explain a decision made by a system they have no visibility into. The seller is frequently in genuine distress; the suspension may have been correct or may have been a false positive; and the agent has a script, a template response and no access to the signals that triggered it. They absorb the anger, repeat a policy line, and escalate into a queue with a multi-day turnaround. It is the worst job in the marketplace, it is where the seller relationship is decided, and the information required to do it well exists two systems away.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Ticket queues, canned responses, escalation paths, and a suspension flag with a policy code. The gaps: no visibility into the detection signals; no explanation the agent can give or the seller can act on; no authority to reinstate; no measure of false positive rates, so nobody knows how often the seller is right; and no priority for cases where income has stopped.
