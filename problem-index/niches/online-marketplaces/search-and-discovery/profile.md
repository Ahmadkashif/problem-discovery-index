**Target Buyer:** Buyers of substitutes; separately, browsers of the unique
**Automation Potential:** Very High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Search and recommendation are the core technical investment at every serious marketplace and the surface on which buyers experience the whole business. It is the largest engineering line and the most visible competition.

It is **not terminal**, because the same surface serves two inventories whose contests are unrelated. Where many sellers offer the same identifiable product, the item is known, the attributes are structured, and the question is which seller's offer to rank first — a learned ranking problem over price, reliability, delivery and reviews, competing against another marketplace listing the same item. Where every listing is one of a kind, no catalogue exists, an amateur wrote the description, the photograph carries most of the information, and the buyer frequently cannot name what they want — the contest is representing the item well enough to surface it to somebody who was not looking for it, and the competitor is the buyer giving up. The data, the techniques, the buyer's intent and the definition of success have nothing in common. Filter Notes in the overview records the two rejected alternatives; the sub-niches below are the split.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Learned ranking, query understanding, recommendation, faceted navigation, and increasingly multimodal understanding of listing images. The gaps are specific to each inventory and are stated in the sub-niches.
