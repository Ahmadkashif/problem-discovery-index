# Unmet Demand Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/online-marketplaces|Online Marketplaces]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn every search that found nothing and every listing that expired into a map of where supply and demand fail to meet — and whoever does that takes the market, because that map is the operating instruction for a matching business.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.5B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** None — failures treated as absence
**Target Buyer:** The marketplaces themselves, and their supply acquisition functions
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the data is already logged

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Marketplaces hold the complete record of demand that went unmet: every search that returned nothing anyone wanted, every filter combination with no results, every listing that expired unsold, every buyer who browsed and left. That is a direct measurement of where supply and demand fail to meet — which is the single most actionable dataset for a business whose entire job is matching. Most marketplaces analyse conversion on the transactions that happened and treat the failures as absence rather than as signal. The corpus is already logged, the analysis is ordinary, and the finding tells supply acquisition exactly what to go and get.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Search analytics focused on top queries, conversion funnels, and category-level demand reporting. The gaps: zero-result and unsatisfying-result searches are not analysed for what was wanted; abandoned sessions carry no intent record; unsold listings are not joined to the demand that did not find them; no supply gap report reaches acquisition; and no measure of demand that left for a competitor.
