## What Makes This a Distinct Niche

Once a determination is made, someone must act on it. Every platform has its own notice process, its own evidence standard, its own response time and its own escalation path. There are also levers outside the platforms — registrar complaints, payment processor reports, hosting provider notices, customs recordation, and legal action.

The contest is lever choice. In practice the lever pulled is the one that is easiest on that platform: a marketplace notice form that removes a listing in two days is used far more than a payment processor report that might remove the operator's ability to take money at all. So enforcement optimises for the action with the shortest path rather than for the action with the greatest effect, and nobody has measured the difference.

The operations side is genuinely hard. Dozens of platforms, each with a different submission process and evidence requirement, several requiring a formal rights recordation first, some with volume limits, some with account relationships that must be maintained. Managing that at scale is real work and is most of what enforcement teams do.

## Current Tools & Gaps

Notice submission tooling with per-platform templates and API integrations where available. Rights recordation management. Escalation relationships with the larger platforms. Registrar and hosting abuse contacts. Payment processor reporting channels. Customs recordation.

The gaps are about strategy and evidence. Lever selection is by convenience and habit, and no firm has data on which lever produces the longest-lasting effect against which operator type. Evidence standards differ per platform and are met at the minimum, so a notice with weak evidence is filed where a stronger one would have supported a harder action. Payment and shipping levers are underused despite being the ones that touch the operator's economics. Cross-platform coordination is rare, so an operator is removed from one surface and continues on four. And response and reinstatement outcomes are tracked per notice rather than per operator.
