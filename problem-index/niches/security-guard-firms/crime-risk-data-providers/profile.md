# Crime Risk Data & Forecasting Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/security-guard-firms|Security Guard Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Produce address-level crime risk scores and forecasts that determine where security is deployed, how much, what a retailer or hotel pays for property insurance, and — critically — whether a crime was foreseeable when a negligent security lawsuit is filed afterwards.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-200 criminologists, statisticians, geographers, and data operations staff
**Output:** Address-level risk scores by crime type, forecast risk, site comparison analytics, expert reports for litigation
**Proprietary data:** Decades of reported crime normalised across thousands of reporting agencies with inconsistent definitions and coverage, joined to demographic and land use data at fine geography
**Clock:** Site selection decisions, insurance renewals, and litigation timelines
**Buyer:** Chief Analytics Officer / President
