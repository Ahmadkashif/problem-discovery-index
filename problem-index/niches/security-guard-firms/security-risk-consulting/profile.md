# Security Risk & Threat Assessment Consulting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/security-guard-firms|Security Guard Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Assess physical security risk at facilities, design protection programmes, and conduct behavioural threat assessments on individuals who have made a facility or a person unsafe — the analytical layer that decides what the guard force is actually for.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-800 security consultants, threat assessment professionals, analysts, and former investigators
**Output:** Risk and vulnerability assessments, security programme design, behavioural threat assessments and management plans, post-incident reviews
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated assessments and case histories, held per firm and per engagement
**Clock:** Incident response, litigation, and capital project schedules
**Buyer:** Managing Director / Practice Leader
