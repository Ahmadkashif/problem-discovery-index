# Executive Protection & Private Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/security-guard-firms|Security Guard Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Provide protective intelligence for principals at elevated risk — monitoring threats, assessing travel destinations, and investigating individuals who have expressed intent toward a client.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-40 analysts per firm, larger inside corporate security departments
**Output:** Protective intelligence assessments, travel risk briefings, threat monitoring, investigations
**Proprietary data:** Case and monitoring histories, held under absolute client confidentiality
**Clock:** Travel and event schedules, and live threats
**Buyer:** Director of Intelligence
