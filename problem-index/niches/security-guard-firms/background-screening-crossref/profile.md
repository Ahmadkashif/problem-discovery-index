# Background Screening Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/security-guard-firms|Security Guard Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Run the criminal, employment and licence checks every security officer must clear before being posted, at the volume an industry with very high turnover generates — screening is a statutory precondition of the job in most states, and a rehire is a rescreen.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-2,000 court researchers, data operations staff, compliance analysts, and data scientists
**Output:** Criminal and civil record reports, employment and licence verification, continuous monitoring, adjudication matrices
**Proprietary data:** Court record acquisition infrastructure across thousands of jurisdictions, plus accumulated adjudication decisions
**Clock:** Hiring turnaround expectations and licence renewal cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Compliance
