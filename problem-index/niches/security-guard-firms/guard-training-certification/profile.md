# Security Officer Training & Certification Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/security-guard-firms|Security Guard Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Deliver the pre-assignment, firearms and continuing training states require for licensure, and administer the certifications the industry's professional bodies award.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 curriculum developers, instructors, and certification staff
**Output:** State-approved training curricula, certification examinations, continuing education, compliance records
**Proprietary data:** Completion and examination records across large officer populations
**Clock:** Statutory pre-assignment deadlines and annual renewal requirements
**Buyer:** Director of Training
