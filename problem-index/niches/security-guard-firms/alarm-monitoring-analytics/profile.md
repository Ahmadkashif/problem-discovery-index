# Alarm Monitoring Central Station Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/security-guard-firms|Security Guard Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Operate the central stations that receive alarm signals and decide, within seconds, whether to dispatch — and analyse the enormous false alarm volume that decision produces.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 operations analysts, data scientists, and quality staff
**Output:** Dispatch decisioning and verification protocols, false alarm reduction analytics, operator performance monitoring, response time reporting
**Proprietary data:** Hundreds of millions of alarm events with verification outcome and dispatch disposition — one of the largest labelled event streams in physical security
**Clock:** Seconds-level response requirements and municipal false alarm ordinances
**Buyer:** VP of Monitoring Operations
