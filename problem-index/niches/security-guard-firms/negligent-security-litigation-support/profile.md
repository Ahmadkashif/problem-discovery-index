## What They Do
Analyse whether a property owner should have foreseen a crime and whether the security in place was adequate — the expert layer in premises liability litigation, which is the tail risk that shapes what every guard contract requires.

**Size:** 20-100 security experts, criminologists, and analysts across the specialist practices
**Output:** Foreseeability analyses, standard of care opinions, post-incident reconstruction, expert testimony
**Proprietary data:** Case files and verdict outcomes, held individually
**Clock:** Expert disclosure deadlines and trial dates
**Buyer:** Managing Principal
