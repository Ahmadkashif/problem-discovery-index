# Transfer Pricing Documentation Studios

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/accounting-firms-smb|SMB Accounting Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Economists and analysts producing transfer pricing documentation for multinational clients — functional analyses, comparability studies, benchmarking sets, and local files meeting OECD BEPS and country-specific requirements. Documentation must exist contemporaneously to avoid penalties, and it is examined by tax authorities in every jurisdiction the client operates in.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 economists, analysts, and tax specialists
**Output:** Master file, local files, benchmarking studies, functional analyses
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated comparable sets, tested-party selections, and the record of which benchmarks authorities accepted or rejected by jurisdiction
**Clock:** Statutory contemporaneous documentation deadlines across dozens of jurisdictions
**Buyer:** Transfer pricing practice leader or global documentation director
