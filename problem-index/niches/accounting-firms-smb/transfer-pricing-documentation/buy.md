# Benchmarking Databases Extended to Firm Selection Precedent
**Niche:** [[niches/accounting-firms-smb/transfer-pricing-documentation/profile|Transfer Pricing Documentation Studios]]
**Industry:** [[industries/accounting-firms-smb|SMB Accounting Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Orbis and Amadeus supply the company universe reliably; what no vendor supplies is which companies this firm has previously accepted as comparable, and which a given tax authority threw out.
**Tags:** #bert #transformers #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #automation #compliance

## The Problem
Benchmarking starts with a commercial database screen returning hundreds of candidate companies, which an analyst narrows through qualitative review — reading business descriptions to judge functional comparability. That review is the labour, it is repeated for every study, and it is applied inconsistently across analysts because the criteria are interpreted fresh each time.

## What Already Exists
Moody's Orbis, Bureau van Dijk Amadeus, and S&P Capital IQ provide broad, well-maintained financial and descriptive data with capable screening. Specialist tools layer on standard screening workflows and documentation output.

## The Customization Gap
Every product ranks candidates by screening criteria and stops. None carries the firm's own history — that this company was accepted as comparable in eleven prior studies in this industry, that this one was rejected twice for functional dissimilarity, that this one was struck by a specific authority on audit. Adding that layer turns qualitative review from a fresh judgment into a review of firm precedent, which is both faster and more consistent. The database subscription stays exactly as it is.

## Target Customer
Transfer pricing analysts performing benchmarking and the directors accountable for consistency across the documentation portfolio.

## Impact If Solved
Cuts qualitative review time substantially and makes comparability selection consistent across analysts and years, which is what authorities test on audit.
