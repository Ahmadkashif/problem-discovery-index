# State & Local Tax Research Units

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/accounting-firms-smb|SMB Accounting Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Dedicated state and local tax practices that determine nexus, taxability, sourcing, and apportionment for clients with multistate activity. Researchers read statute, regulation, and administrative guidance across fifty state regimes and 10,000+ local jurisdictions, and maintain client taxability matrices. Clients pay for nexus studies, taxability matrices, and exposure quantifications.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-100 researchers, analysts, and specialists
**Output:** Nexus study, taxability matrix, exposure quantification for voluntary disclosure
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated determinations across clients and jurisdictions, and the reasoning behind each position
**Clock:** Continuous state rule amendments; registration deadlines; audit cycles
**Buyer:** SALT practice leader; national director of indirect tax
