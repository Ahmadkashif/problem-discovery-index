# Specialty Credit Recovery Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/accounting-firms-smb|SMB Accounting Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Firms that identify and document niche tax credits and incentives — WOTC, disaster credits, employment and training incentives, state job-creation credits. Large analyst teams screen client payroll and operations data against credit eligibility rules and produce substantiation packages. Fees are usually contingent on the credit recovered.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-500 analysts and screeners
**Output:** Eligibility determination and substantiation package
**Proprietary data:** Screening histories across clients and jurisdictions; accumulated eligibility determinations
**Clock:** Credit claim windows and amended return statutes of limitation
**Buyer:** Practice leader or VP of credit services
