## Why These Niches

SMB accounting firms fragment along service mix (tax-heavy vs. bookkeeping-advisory), practice size (sole practitioner vs. multi-staff), client population (general vs. nonprofit vs. agricultural vs. Spanish-speaking), and operational function (payroll processing vs. document intake). These 8 niches cover the two largest revenue concentrations (seasonal tax prep and recurring bookkeeping/advisory), the two most digitally neglected segments (solo CPAs still running desktop software and nonprofit specialists using generic tools), two underserved client populations that existing software ignores (Spanish-language practices and rural ag accountants), and the two highest-ROI automation targets within firm operations (payroll processing and document intake). Excluded: large regional firms with 50+ employees (different buying behavior), forensic accounting (specialized niche), and government audit firms (distinct regulatory framework).

## Why These Pockets

Accounting's insight layer is unusually deep because tax law is a permanent, externally-driven research burden that the operator layer cannot absorb. Seven pockets qualified. The strongest sit where a written analytical artefact is itself the invoice and is later tested by an adversary — a study by the IRS, an opinion by opposing counsel, documentation by a foreign tax authority — which is what makes both the accumulated precedent and the challenge history genuinely proprietary. Tax research publishers are the purest instance: the entire company is an insight function, staffed larger than most CPA firms are in total, selling to the operator layer beneath it.

Eight pockets were logged without qualifying, and the reasons are the useful part of the map. Three fail the headcount gate outright despite scoring well on insight-as-invoice — practice valuation brokers, benchmark publishers, and peer review entities are all 3-10 person operations. Offshore delivery centers have the largest headcount in the industry by an order of magnitude and score lowest, because the work is transaction processing rather than research. Specialty credit recovery scores respectably but carries a live kill switch: the ERC moratorium demonstrated that a single IRS action can suspend the segment's revenue mid-engagement.
