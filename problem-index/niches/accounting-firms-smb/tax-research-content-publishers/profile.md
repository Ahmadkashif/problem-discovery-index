# Tax Research Content Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/accounting-firms-smb|SMB Accounting Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Publishers that sell tax research and explanatory content to accountants. Thomson Reuters Checkpoint, CCH AnswerConnect, Bloomberg Tax, Parker Tax. They employ credentialed tax analysts and editors who write and maintain code annotations, practice guides, client letters, decision trees, and state comparison charts. Accounting firms pay subscription fees for the answer being current and correct.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-400 analysts, editors, and attorneys
**Output:** Maintained research corpus — annotations, guides, charts, worked examples
**Proprietary data:** Decades of proprietary editorial content plus the mapping from primary authority to derived explanation
**Clock:** Legislative and judicial — acts passed in December, rulings released without notice, filing season
**Buyer:** VP of Content or Managing Editor of tax research
