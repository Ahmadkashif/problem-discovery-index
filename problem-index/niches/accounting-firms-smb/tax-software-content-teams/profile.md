# Tax Software Content & Compliance Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/accounting-firms-smb|SMB Accounting Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The teams inside tax software vendors (Intuit, Drake, CCH, Thomson Reuters) that keep forms, calculations, and e-file schemas current across federal and fifty state regimes. Every filing season they re-implement every changed form and rule before the season opens. Accounting firms buy the software on the implicit promise that the computation is right.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 content analysts, form specialists, and engineers
**Output:** Updated forms, calculation logic, and e-file schemas
**Proprietary data:** Historical form-to-logic mappings and the regression corpus of prior-year returns
**Clock:** Filing season — an immovable annual deadline set by taxing authorities
**Buyer:** VP of Tax Content or Director of Compliance Engineering
