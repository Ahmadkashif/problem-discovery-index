# Accounting Practice Benchmark Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/accounting-firms-smb|SMB Accounting Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Publishers of accounting industry benchmarking data — the Rosenberg Survey, AICPA PCPS surveys, CPA Trendlines. They collect and publish practice performance metrics that firms use to compare themselves against peers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 3-15 researchers and editors
**Output:** Annual benchmark reports and survey datasets
**Proprietary data:** Multi-decade longitudinal survey panel of firm economics
**Clock:** Annual publication cycle
**Buyer:** Publisher or research director
