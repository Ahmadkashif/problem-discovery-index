# Business Valuation Practices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/accounting-firms-smb|SMB Accounting Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Practices that value privately held businesses for estate and gift tax, litigation, ESOPs, buy-sell agreements, and transactions. Credentialed appraisers (ABV, ASA, CVA) build income, market, and asset approaches into a written opinion of value. The report is the product, and it is regularly challenged — by the IRS, by opposing counsel, or by a court.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-80 appraisers and analysts
**Output:** Written valuation opinion with supporting analysis
**Proprietary data:** Every prior valuation the firm has issued: comparable transactions, discount rates, marketability and minority discounts applied, and how each fared under challenge
**Clock:** Estate filing deadlines, court dates, transaction closings
**Buyer:** Valuation practice leader or national director of valuation services
