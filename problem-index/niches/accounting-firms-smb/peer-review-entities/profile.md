# Peer Review Administering Entities

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/accounting-firms-smb|SMB Accounting Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State CPA societies and the AICPA administering the mandatory peer review program that inspects firms' audit and attest quality. Reviewers examine engagement files against professional standards and issue reports that determine a firm's ability to practice.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 2-8 staff per administering entity
**Output:** Peer review reports and matters for further consideration
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated review findings across firms
**Clock:** Three-year mandatory review cycle
**Buyer:** Peer review director at a state society
