# CPA Rollup Platform Corporate Development

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/accounting-firms-smb|SMB Accounting Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Corporate development and integration teams at PE-backed accounting platforms consolidating the fragmented CPA market. They evaluate 100-300 acquisition targets a year, assessing client concentration, revenue quality, realization, and partner retention risk, then integrate closed firms onto a common operating model. Analysis is dense and repetitive, and the targets' data is unusually structured for M&A.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 analysts across corp dev and integration
**Output:** Diligence memo and post-close benchmark pack
**Proprietary data:** Every practice the platform has diligenced and integrated, plus post-close operating data
**Clock:** LOI-to-close timelines
**Buyer:** Head of Corporate Development or VP of Integration
