# CPA Practice Valuation & M&A Brokers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/accounting-firms-smb|SMB Accounting Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Brokers who value and broker the sale of accounting practices. Poe Group Advisors, Accounting Practice Sales and similar. They hold genuinely proprietary transaction comparables and the insight is the invoice — but the firms are tiny.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 3-10 brokers and analysts
**Output:** Practice valuation and sale memorandum
**Proprietary data:** Proprietary practice transaction comparables
**Clock:** Deal timelines
**Buyer:** Firm principal
