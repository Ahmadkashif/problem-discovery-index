# R&D Tax Credit Study Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/accounting-firms-smb|SMB Accounting Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Firms that produce IRC §41 research credit studies. alliantgroup, KBKG, Source Advisors, plus specialist practices inside mid-size CPA firms. Engineers and scientists interview a client's technical staff and write a substantiation package establishing that each claimed project meets the four-part test. The client pays a fee for the study, which must survive IRS examination years later.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-500 engineers, scientists, and tax specialists
**Output:** Substantiation package — technical narrative, time allocations, cost computation
**Proprietary data:** Thousands of prior studies mapping real engineering activities to accepted statutory framing, with examination outcomes
**Clock:** Filing deadlines up front, IRS examination two to four years later
**Buyer:** Managing director of the R&D credit practice; national technical director
