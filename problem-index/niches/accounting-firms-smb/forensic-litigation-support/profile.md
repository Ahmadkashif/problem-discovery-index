# Forensic Accounting & Litigation Support

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/accounting-firms-smb|SMB Accounting Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Practices that reconstruct financial records for disputes — fraud investigations, economic damages, marital dissolution, shareholder litigation. Accountants analyse transaction records and produce an expert report that is filed, deposed on, and cross-examined. Attorneys hire the firm because of the credibility of the report and the expert behind it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-100 forensic accountants and analysts
**Output:** Expert report and testimony exhibits
**Proprietary data:** Prior engagement workpapers, damages models, and the record of which methodologies survived Daubert challenge and cross-examination
**Clock:** Court schedules — discovery cutoffs, expert disclosure deadlines, trial dates
**Buyer:** Forensic practice leader or managing director of disputes and investigations
