# Unclaimed Property & Escheat Advisory

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/accounting-firms-smb|SMB Accounting Firms]]

## What They Do
Practices that determine a client's escheat exposure and manage voluntary disclosure agreements across state unclaimed property regimes. Analysts research fifty state dormancy periods, reporting rules, and exemptions, then reconstruct decades of client records to quantify liability. Clients pay for the exposure analysis and the negotiated settlement.

**Output:** Exposure quantification and voluntary disclosure filings
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated state positions, negotiated settlement outcomes, and dormancy determinations
**Clock:** State VDA windows and audit notices
**Buyer:** Unclaimed property practice leader
