# Cost Segregation Study Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/accounting-firms-smb|SMB Accounting Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Firms that produce engineering-based studies reclassifying building components from 39-year real property into shorter depreciation lives. KBKG, CSSI, Engineered Tax Services. Engineers inspect the property, take off quantities from construction drawings, and allocate cost across hundreds of components, defending each classification against IRS asset class precedent. The study is the billable artifact.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-200 engineers, estimators, and specialty tax staff
**Output:** Engineering study — component allocation with classification defence
**Proprietary data:** Thousands of prior studies with decided classifications by component and building type; hundreds of thousands of site photographs
**Clock:** Client filing deadlines; IRS examination years later
**Buyer:** Director of engineering or cost segregation practice leader
