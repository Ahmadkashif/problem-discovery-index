# Offshore Accounting Delivery Centers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/accounting-firms-smb|SMB Accounting Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Large offshore centers in India and the Philippines performing bookkeeping, tax return preparation, and audit support for US accounting firms. Headcount is enormous and the work is highly repeatable, but the output is processed transactions and prepared returns rather than research or insight.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-5,000 preparers and reviewers
**Output:** Prepared returns, reconciled books, audit workpapers
**Proprietary data:** Process and quality data; the client's books are client-owned
**Clock:** US filing season
**Buyer:** COO or Head of Delivery
