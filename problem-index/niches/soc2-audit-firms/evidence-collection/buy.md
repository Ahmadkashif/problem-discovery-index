# Buy: Request Management From Accounting Practice

**Niche:** Evidence Collection & Request Management
**Industry:** [[industries/soc2-audit-firms|SOC 2 & Attestation Audit Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Accounting practice management solved the client request cycle with structured lists, automated chasing and client portals, and attestation runs a spreadsheet and email.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #evaluation-metrics #compliance #automation #data-integration #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Whether evidence is pulled from connected systems or requested, chased and received as files.

## The Problem

Requesting documents from a client, tracking what has arrived, chasing what has not, and organising the result is the central operational problem of accounting practice, and the tooling for it is mature.

Practice management platforms in accounting handle the prepared-by-client list as a first-class object. Requests are structured items with owners, due dates and status. Clients see a portal with what is outstanding. Reminders are automatic and escalate. Documents are received into the right place. And the whole thing is templated, so the same engagement type produces the same list without anyone rebuilding it.

Attestation firms use some of this, unevenly. Larger firms have portals. Many run the request list in a spreadsheet, chase by email, and receive documents into a shared folder.

The accounting platforms also solved the recurrence problem — the same client, the same engagement, the same requests next year, pre-populated — which is exactly the situation here and is where most of the waste sits.

## What Already Exists

Accounting practice management: Karbon, Canopy, Practice Ignition and Jetpack Workflow, with structured client request lists, automated chasing, client portals and engagement templates.

Audit-specific: the request management features in audit platforms, which vary in quality and are strongest at the large firms.

Document collection: the general secure file request and collection tools used across professional services.

Client portals: the compliance platforms' own portals, which many clients already use and which the auditor frequently does not.

Attestation firms: portals at the larger firms, spreadsheets and email at many smaller ones.

## The Customization Gap

**Recurrence is not exploited.** Accounting platforms pre-populate this year's list from last year's and mark what changed. Attestation rebuilds the request list each period despite most items recurring.

**Requests go to the wrong person.** Accounting requests are routed to the client contact who holds the document. Attestation requests go to the compliance manager, who forwards them to the system owner, adding a hop and a delay to every item.

**Evidence is not verified on receipt.** Accounting platforms check that a document of the right type arrived. Attestation discovers the evidence is wrong when an associate opens it days later.

**The client's compliance platform is a better portal.** Many clients already run one, with the evidence in it, and the auditor operates a separate portal and asks them to upload things that are already there.

**Templates exist and are generic.** Standard programmes produce standard requests phrased in control language, where the useful version is specific to the client's systems.

**Chasing is manual at smaller firms.** Automated escalation is a solved feature and is not universally deployed in a market where a substantial share of firms are small.

## Target Customer

Smaller and mid-sized attestation firms, where the request cycle is run on spreadsheets and email and where adopting mature practice management would be an immediate improvement.

Accounting practice management vendors, for whom attestation engagements are an adjacent workflow with the same shape and frequently the same firms.

Compliance platform vendors, who already hold the evidence and the client relationship and could serve as the auditor's portal rather than being duplicated by one.

## Impact If Solved

A mature request management discipline exists in an adjacent practice, frequently inside the same firms, and has not been applied to attestation engagements.

Pre-populating the request list from the previous period is the single largest saving, because most requests recur and are currently rebuilt and re-answered from scratch every year.

And using the client's own compliance platform as the evidence portal, rather than operating a separate one, would remove an entire duplicated step from every engagement.
