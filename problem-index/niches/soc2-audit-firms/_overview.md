# Niche Analysis — SOC 2 & Attestation Audit Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/soc2-audit-firms|SOC 2 & Attestation Audit Firms]]

## Niche Selection

This is an assurance market with the classic structure: the party paying selects the auditor, the party relying cannot observe quality, and the output is binary. The eight niches below start with the testing that determines what the opinion is worth, then the delivery model, then the two places where the outcome is decided before testing begins, then the two people at either end, then the mechanical layers.

## Why These Niches

Testing determines what the opinion is actually worth, and the methodological compromise it rests on has largely dissolved. Fieldwork delivery is where the economics land — junior staff at high utilisation on fixed fees in concentrated periods. Scope and independence are the two places where the result is substantially decided before any testing happens: the boundary is drawn in the client's own document, and the client selects and pays the auditor. The two underserved people are the associate performing evidence checks under utilisation pressure and the enterprise customer reading a report they cannot evaluate. Evidence requests and report production are mechanical.

## Filter Notes

Seven of the eight are terminal — each names one contest every serious competitor is fighting over, and decomposing further would produce features rather than markets.

**Audit testing and evidence** is not, because doing the work better and being able to say so are different problems with different obstacles. Full-population testing is a methodological and technical question: compliance platforms now hold continuous, complete control populations for most clients, so the compromise that sampling exists to resolve has largely dissolved, and an auditor could examine every access review rather than twenty-five of them. It is answerable this year, it lowers cost per control once built, it is testable against the sample it replaces, and it requires nobody's permission. Rigour expression is the opposite in every respect: communicating how searching the work was, in a form the relying party can evaluate, requires the attestation report format to change, requires standards bodies to move, exposes competitors who would rather it did not, and delivers no benefit to the firm that does it alone — because the buyer of the audit does not want rigour expressed and the party who does is not in the transaction. A firm can test full populations and issue an identical binary report, and would, because the report format is fixed and nobody has asked.

Two adjacent candidates were rejected as belonging elsewhere: **the continuous evidence collection platform** is the subject of [[industries/grc-compliance-platforms|GRC & Compliance Platforms]], and **the security testing an audit references** belongs to [[industries/penetration-testing-firms|Penetration Testing Firms]].
