# Buy: Document Assembly and Controlled Distribution

**Niche:** Report Production & Distribution
**Industry:** [[industries/soc2-audit-firms|SOC 2 & Attestation Audit Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Financial reporting generates documents from structured data with full traceability, and secure document distribution is a mature category, and attestation reports are emailed PDFs assembled by hand.
**Tags:** #evaluation-metrics #compliance #data-integration #automation #workflow-orchestration #bert
**Contested on:** Whether the report is generated from the engagement record or assembled by hand from last year's document.

## The Problem

Two mature capabilities apply here and neither is used fully.

Financial reporting software generates statutory accounts from structured data, with every figure traceable to its source, automatic consistency checking across the document, and structured output formats that regulators and consumers ingest directly. The discipline exists because a financial statement assembled by hand contained errors and could not be checked.

Secure document distribution is its own category — controlled access, expiry, watermarking, revocation, access logging and supersession — used for confidential material in legal, financial and corporate transactions.

Attestation reports are assembled largely by hand from a template and distributed as email attachments. Both problems have solved answers in adjacent practices, frequently inside the same firms.

## What Already Exists

Financial reporting: disclosure management platforms generating statements from structured data, with traceability and consistency checking; and the structured reporting formats now standard in regulatory filing.

Document automation: the assembly platforms from legal and proposal domains, with conditional content and multi-variant rendering.

Secure distribution: virtual data rooms and document control platforms with access management, expiry, watermarking, revocation and access logging.

Structured financial reporting formats: machine-readable statement formats that regulators require, which are the precedent for a machine-readable attestation companion.

Attestation: templates, partial generation at larger firms, and email distribution.

## The Customization Gap

**No structured reporting format exists for attestation.** Financial statements have machine-readable formats because regulators required them. Attestation has none, and it is the single change that would most help the relying party.

**Traceability is manual.** Financial reporting traces every figure to its source automatically. An attestation report's control matrix is reconciled to the workpapers by a reviewer reading both.

**Distribution is uncontrolled for a confidential document.** Data room capability is mature and is used for transactions rather than for a document that circulates to hundreds of a client's customers under use restrictions.

**Supersession has no mechanism.** A reissued report or a bridge letter cannot reach the holders of the previous version, because nobody knows who they are.

**The client controls distribution and the firm carries the confidentiality.** The report is restricted in its use and the party who can enforce that is not the party distributing it.

**The reader would benefit most and buys nothing.** A structured format serves the relying party, which is why it has not been prioritised by anyone in the transaction.

## Target Customer

Standards bodies, who could define a machine-readable companion format — the highest-leverage change in this niche and one only they can make stick.

Audit firms, for whom document generation removes a reviewer reconciliation and reduces a real class of error, using capability that exists in adjacent practices.

Data room and document control vendors, for whom attestation report distribution is an obvious adjacent use of a mature product.

## Impact If Solved

A machine-readable companion format is the change with the largest downstream effect in this entire industry, because it converts an unreadable document into usable data for hundreds of relying parties per report.

Generation with traceability removes a manual reconciliation performed by senior reviewers and eliminates the class of error where the report and the testing diverge.

And controlled distribution with supersession would give a confidential, use-restricted document that circulates to hundreds of parties the handling that every comparable document already receives.
