# Garnishment & Wage Attachment

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/payroll-platforms|Payroll Platforms]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in garnishment processing is fighting to turn a court order into a correct deduction — right priority, right disposable income base, right exemption limit — without a person reading the document, and whoever automates that correctly takes the service line.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.3B US garnishment processing, wage attachment services and child support disbursement
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~4% of payroll revenue, with labour intensity far exceeding its share
**Digital Adoption:** Low — orders arrive as paper and are read by processors
**Target Buyer:** Garnishment operations leaders at payroll providers; the employees whose pay is attached have no voice in it
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the rules are published and the documents are structured enough to extract

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A garnishment order arrives as a court or agency document instructing an employer to withhold from an employee's wages. What must then be computed is genuinely intricate: the disposable income base differs by order type, federal and state exemption limits apply and the more protective governs, multiple concurrent orders have a priority sequence that differs by type and jurisdiction, some orders have a fixed amount and some a percentage, administrative fees may be permitted, and the aggregate cap must not be breached. Getting it wrong in one direction leaves the employer liable to the creditor for the shortfall; getting it wrong in the other takes money from someone's pay that the law protects, from a person who is already subject to a wage attachment and is least able to absorb it. The work is performed by processors reading documents, at scale, at every payroll provider.

## Current Tools & Gaps
The large providers operate garnishment service lines with trained processors, document intake and disbursement infrastructure; specialist garnishment outsourcers serve the middle market; child support orders increasingly arrive through standardised electronic formats, which is the one genuinely digitised slice. The gaps: order intake is manual document handling for everything except electronic child support; exemption and priority rules are applied from reference materials and processor knowledge rather than from executable content; the employee — whose pay is being attached — receives a notice and has no view of the calculation, the exemption applied or the remaining balance; and errors in the employee's favour are corrected slowly while errors against them may never be detected, because the party who would notice is the one with the least information.
