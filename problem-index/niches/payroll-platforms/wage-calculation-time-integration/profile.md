## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Between a clocked hour and a gross pay figure sits a set of rules and a data handoff, and both go wrong routinely. The rules determine overtime thresholds, the regular rate against which overtime is computed, shift differentials, meal and rest premiums, and how non-discretionary bonuses retroactively affect prior periods — all of which vary by jurisdiction and are configured by an employer into an engine that will apply whatever it is told. The handoff determines whether the hours arrive complete and approved before a fixed deadline, with exceptions resolved. A failure in the first is a legal exposure that accumulates silently across every affected employee; a failure in the second is a fire drill on a Thursday afternoon. They share a block of the market and nothing else, which is why the contested sentence cannot cover both.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Every payroll platform includes earnings rule configuration; time and attendance is a large adjacent category — UKG, Deputy, When I Work and the workforce management vendors — integrating with payroll through standard connectors. Both work. The gaps that span the block: nobody verifies that an employer's configured earnings rules match the law in the jurisdictions where its employees work, so a misconfiguration runs indefinitely; retroactive recalculation for non-discretionary bonuses is frequently not performed at all, which is a common and well-documented wage and hour failure; and the pre-run exception process surfaces problems at the last possible moment because the run is the trigger rather than the deadline being the trigger.
