# Nobody Publishes Payroll Tax Accuracy

**Niche:** [[niches/payroll-platforms/payroll-tax-content-filing/profile|Payroll Tax Content & Filing]]
**Industry:** [[industries/payroll-platforms|Payroll Platforms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Every payroll provider claims accuracy and guarantees to pay penalties, and none of them publishes an error rate — so an employer choosing a provider on the dimension that matters most has nothing to compare.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #evaluation-metrics #hypothesis-testing #confidence-intervals #compliance #quick-win #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in payroll is fighting to determine the correct taxing jurisdictions, rates and rules for every employee every period and verify them before the money moves — and whoever catches an error pre-disbursement rather than pre-notice takes the account.

## The Problem
An employer selects a payroll provider. Every candidate states that it handles all jurisdictions and guarantees penalty coverage. None states how many agency notices its clients received last year per thousand employees, what proportion were provider error versus client data error, how long errors took to detect, or how often an amended return was required. The provider knows every one of those numbers — notices arrive, are logged, are worked, and are closed. The buyer, who is selecting on the basis of not wanting any of this to happen, chooses on brand and price.

## Why It's Still Broken
Publishing an error rate means publishing a number above zero in a market where every competitor implies zero, so the first mover is punished — the same dynamic as visibility accuracy in freight and coverage in activity capture. The penalty guarantee is offered instead, which converts an accuracy question into a financial one and, in doing so, removes the buyer's need to ask — which suits everyone except the employees whose withholding was wrong, since a penalty guarantee compensates the employer and does nothing for the employee who owes tax they did not expect.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Define the measures and report them. Notices per thousand employees per year, by cause — provider content error, provider processing error, client data error, agency error — which is a classification the provider's own notice-handling operation already performs informally. Detection latency from the error occurring to it being identified, which is the number that distinguishes a provider catching its own errors from one waiting for the agency. Amendment rate. Employee-affecting error rate as a distinct measure, because an employer penalty and an employee under-withholding are different harms and only one of them is covered by the guarantee. Publish them, and let an employer verify against their own history. The provider that does this first takes a short-term competitive hit and gains the only defensible claim in a category where everyone asserts the same thing, and employers gain the ability to select on the property they actually care about.

## Who Feels the Pain
Employers choosing on brand because there is nothing else to choose on; employees under-withheld by an error that the penalty guarantee does not address; and the providers who are genuinely more accurate and have no way to demonstrate it.

## Impact If Fixed
The numbers already exist inside every provider's notice operation and require classification rather than measurement. Reporting the employee-affecting error rate separately is the element that matters most, because it names a harm the industry's standard remedy does not cover and that nobody currently counts.
