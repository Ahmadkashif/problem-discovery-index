# Decision Modelling

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/payment-fraud-vendors|Payment Fraud Vendors]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to build the best decision from device, behavioural, network and consortium signals against an adversary who adapts — and whoever models best on the labels that exist wins the transactions everybody else gets wrong.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.1B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~16% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** High — the category's strongest capability
**Target Buyer:** Data science and risk leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — an active modelling contest

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This contest is modelling. Given device fingerprints, behavioural biometrics, velocity, graph relationships, consortium history and merchant context, produce the best possible decision in milliseconds — against an adversary who probes the boundary continuously and changes when detected. It is the category's most developed capability and a genuine technical competition, and it is entirely separate from the question of where the labels come from.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Gradient-boosted ensembles, graph features, device intelligence, behavioural signals and real-time scoring infrastructure. The gaps: adversarial adaptation not modelled explicitly; concept drift handled by retraining schedules; decision cost asymmetry not encoded; calibration weak across merchant segments; and explanation for analysts and merchants thin.
