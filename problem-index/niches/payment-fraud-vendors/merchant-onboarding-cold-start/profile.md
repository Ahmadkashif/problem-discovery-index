# Merchant Onboarding & Cold Start

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/payment-fraud-vendors|Payment Fraud Vendors]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to know a new merchant's definition of normal before it has any history — and whoever transfers that from merchants already on the network wins the first ninety days the customer judges them on.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.0B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~14% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — learning from scratch each time
**Target Buyer:** Onboarding and data leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — transfer learning across merchants

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every new merchant is a new definition of normal: different basket sizes, different customer geography, different delivery patterns, different seasonality, different legitimate anomalies. The model spends its first months learning that, and the merchant judges the product on exactly those months. Meanwhile the vendor has hundreds of merchants whose patterns are already learned and structurally similar, and starts over each time.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Generic starting models, merchant-supplied configuration, a monitoring period and manual tuning. The gaps: no transfer from similar merchants; merchant similarity not modelled; early period not explicitly managed; expectations not set with the merchant; and no measure of time to acceptable performance.
