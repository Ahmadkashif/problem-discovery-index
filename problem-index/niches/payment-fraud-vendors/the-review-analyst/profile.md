# The Review Analyst

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/payment-fraud-vendors|Payment Fraud Vendors]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make forty seconds enough to decide correctly on exactly the cases the model could not — and whoever equips that decision changes the outcome of the transactions the product is worst at.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — a queue and a case screen
**Target Buyer:** Review operations leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — evidence assembly and calibration

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Manual review exists precisely where the model is least certain. Analysts make irreversible decisions about strangers' purchases in under a minute, on the hardest cases in the entire pipeline, and are graded on a chargeback rate that reflects only the half of their decisions that were approvals. It is the one place in the category where a human judgement is applied to the ambiguous region, and it is supported by a case screen and a target handle time.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Queue, case detail screen, external lookup tools and a handle time target. The gaps: evidence gathered manually per case; no similar-case retrieval; decline decisions never evaluated; analyst consistency unmeasured; and the queue ordered by age rather than by value.
