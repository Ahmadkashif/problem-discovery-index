# Consortium Signal Sharing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/payment-fraud-vendors|Payment Fraud Vendors]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make the network signal worth more than the sum of its members' own data — and whoever proves the consortium's marginal contribution can charge for it instead of asserting it.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~4% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — pooled but unmeasured
**Target Buyer:** Network and data leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — measurement and propagation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every vendor claims a network effect: their consortium sees an identity, device or card across many merchants, so they catch what a single merchant cannot. The claim is central to competitive positioning and is almost never quantified. Nobody reports how much of a decision rests on consortium signal, how quickly an attack detected at one merchant propagates to the rest, or what the marginal value of joining actually is.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Shared identity and device graphs, cross-merchant velocity, blocklists and reputation scores. The gaps: marginal contribution never measured; propagation latency unmeasured; contribution quality uneven and unincentivised; privacy basis inconsistently established; and stale negative signals never expiring.
