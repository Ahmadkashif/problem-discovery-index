# The Risk Strategist

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/payment-fraud-vendors|Payment Fraud Vendors]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let a strategist see both sides of a threshold move before they make it — and whoever gives them that turns a reactive craft into a measured discipline.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~6% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — dashboards and instinct
**Target Buyer:** Risk leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — simulation and impact estimation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The risk strategist moves thresholds and writes rules in response to a loss spike or a merchant complaint, and cannot measure the cost of either direction because half the outcomes do not exist. They are responsible for the commercial shape of the product — how much fraud, how much friction, how much revenue — with a dashboard showing approvals and chargebacks and nothing about what was turned away. The role is where the category's missing evidence is felt as a daily working condition.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Threshold controls, rule editors, performance dashboards and merchant conversations. The gaps: no simulation of a proposed change; no view of the declined population; changes not tracked against outcomes; no cost model for either error; and reactive rather than planned policy work.
