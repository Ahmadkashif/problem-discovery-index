# Good-Customer Recovery

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/payment-fraud-vendors|Payment Fraud Vendors]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give a wrongly declined customer a way through instead of a dead end — and whoever recovers them turns the category's largest invisible loss into revenue.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$200M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — a decline and nothing
**Target Buyer:** Merchant success leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — step-up and retry flows

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A legitimate customer is declined. They see a generic failure message, try once more, and leave — often permanently, and often telling other people. The transaction is lost, the relationship is lost, and the merchant never learns it happened. Everything needed to offer that customer another route — a step-up verification, a delayed review with a follow-up, a different payment method — exists, and the standard product response to uncertainty is a dead end.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Decline responses, generic error messages and occasional step-up authentication. The gaps: no recovery path after a decline; step-up used rarely and inconsistently; declined customers not followed up; no measurement of whether they returned; and no merchant-configurable treatment.
