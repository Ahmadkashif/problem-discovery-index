## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.4B US

**Target Buyer:** Dispute operations leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — retrieval, assembly and win prediction

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A chargeback arrives with a network reason code, a deadline and a required evidence set. Disputing it means finding the delivery confirmation, the authentication record, the terms acceptance, the communication history and the transaction detail, formatting them to the network's specification, and submitting before the clock runs out. Most of it is assembled by hand, for cases that are frequently unwinnable, by a team measured on a recovery rate nobody has decomposed.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Case queues, evidence templates, network portals and representment services. The gaps: evidence gathered manually from many systems; winnability not predicted; reason code requirements encoded inconsistently; outcomes not fed back into which cases to fight; and no measure of effort per dollar recovered.
