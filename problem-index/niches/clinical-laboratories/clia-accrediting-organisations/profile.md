# CLIA Accrediting Organisations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/clinical-laboratories|Clinical Laboratories]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Private organisations with CMS-approved deeming authority whose inspections stand in for state CLIA surveys. Per CMS (September 2026), the College of American Pathologists accredits 6,966 labs, COLA 5,639 and The Joint Commission 2,336, with AABB, ACHC, ASHI and A2LA accrediting a few hundred between them. Labs pay accreditation and inspection fees; the accreditor reports results to CMS.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~50-300 staff per large accreditor in standards, inspection management and quality (estimate); CAP's inspections are carried out largely by volunteer peer inspectors from other labs (recalled), and surveyors are production
**Output:** Inspection reports, deficiency citations, accreditation decisions, checklists and standards
**Proprietary data:** Deficiency findings across thousands of labs over many two-year cycles — which requirements labs fail, by lab type and size
**Clock:** The two-yearly inspection cycle and CMS's deeming oversight
**Buyer:** Chief executive or vice president of accreditation
