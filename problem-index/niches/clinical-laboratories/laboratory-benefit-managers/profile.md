# Laboratory Benefit Managers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/clinical-laboratories|Clinical Laboratories]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Firms that manage outpatient lab spending for health plans: they write lab coverage policies, run prior authorization for genetic and molecular tests, and apply automated claim edits to routine lab claims. Avalon Healthcare Solutions is the specialist (it claims 7–12% savings and, by one profile, over 39 million members; search summary), and Blue Cross Blue Shield of Oklahoma extended its lab benefit management programme to more commercial members from January 2026 (search summary). Large specialty benefit managers run genetic testing programmes (recalled). Plans pay per member per month or a share of savings.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-100 policy analysts, laboratory scientists and data analysts per firm (estimate); genetic counsellors and nurses reviewing individual requests are production
**Output:** Lab coverage policies, claim-edit rule libraries, utilisation and savings reports for plans
**Proprietary data:** Policy libraries are their own; the claims and authorization records belong to the client plans
**Clock:** Plan contract years and federal decision timeframes for authorizations, both set by or for the client
**Buyer:** Chief medical officer or head of policy at the benefit manager; plan VP of medical cost management
