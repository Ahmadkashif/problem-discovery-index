# Hospital Lab Outreach Valuation & Strategy Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/clinical-laboratories|Clinical Laboratories]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Consultancies that advise health systems on their labs: benchmark costs and staffing, value the outreach book, model sell, keep, joint-venture or lab-management options, and run the process when a system sells its outreach business to Quest, Labcorp or a regional lab. Accumen, Lighthouse Lab Services and diagnostics strategy firms such as Health Advances are examples (recalled). Health systems pay project fees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-60 consultants and analysts per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Lab benchmarking studies, outreach valuations, strategic option analyses, transaction support
**Proprietary data:** Benchmarks accumulated from past engagements; the client's own volumes, payer mix, contracts and costs drive each study
**Clock:** The client's board and transaction timetable
**Buyer:** Managing partner; health-system CFO or lab VP as client
