# MolDX Technical Assessment Programme

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/clinical-laboratories|Clinical Laboratories]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Medicare's molecular diagnostics programme, run by Palmetto GBA and adopted by Noridian, WPS and CGS across seven contractor jurisdictions covering 28 states (search summary). Its staff review labs' technical assessment dossiers — analytical and clinical validity, clinical utility — to decide whether a molecular test is covered, and assign the DEX Z-codes labs must put on claims. Coverage decisions follow the MolDX LCD (L35025). The work is paid for by CMS through the contractors; labs pay nothing to submit.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~15-40 medical directors, molecular scientists and analysts (estimate); distinct from the handful of medical directors per contractor who write other local coverage policy
**Output:** Technical assessment decisions, coverage articles, Z-code registry entries
**Proprietary data:** Years of submitted validation dossiers and assessment records for thousands of tests
**Clock:** CMS contract terms and the programme's own review timelines; CMS is the client
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer; CMS contract through the Medicare contractor
