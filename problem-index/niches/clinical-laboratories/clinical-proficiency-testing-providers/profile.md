# Clinical Proficiency Testing Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/clinical-laboratories|Clinical Laboratories]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
CMS-approved programmes that send identical specimens to thousands of clinical labs several times a year, collect the results, grade each lab against peers and report scores to the lab, its accreditor and CMS. CLIA requires labs to enrol for each specialty they are certified in, and approved programmes must run at least three testing events a year and report scores to CMS within 60 days of each event's reporting deadline (42 CFR 493, subparts H and I). The College of American Pathologists runs the largest scheme, alongside API, WSLH, AAB and ACP's MLE (recalled). Labs pay per programme per year.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~15-100 scientists, statisticians and programme staff per provider (estimate); specimen manufacturing is production
**Output:** Event reports grading each lab, peer-group statistics by method and instrument, regulatory score reports
**Proprietary data:** Years of every participating lab's results on identical challenges, by method and instrument — the only view of how clinical labs perform against each other
**Clock:** CLIA's required event frequency and score reporting to CMS and accreditors; labs need passing scores to keep testing
**Buyer:** Proficiency testing programme director at the provider; lab director as the customer
