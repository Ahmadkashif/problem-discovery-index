# Laboratory Professional Certification Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/clinical-laboratories|Clinical Laboratories]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Bodies that certify medical laboratory scientists, technicians, phlebotomists, histotechnicians and cytotechnologists by examination — the ASCP Board of Certification, American Medical Technologists and AAB (recalled) — and in ASCP's case also run workforce research such as the vacancy survey, whose 2024 edition drew 1,027 responses covering more than 18,626 employees. Candidates and employers pay exam and maintenance fees; several states require certification or licensure.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-80 psychometricians, exam developers and research staff per body (estimate)
**Output:** Certification exams, credential maintenance, workforce and vacancy surveys
**Proprietary data:** Item banks and candidate performance over decades; survey responses
**Clock:** State licensure rules and employers' hiring needs; exam windows are self-set
**Buyer:** Executive director of the certification board
