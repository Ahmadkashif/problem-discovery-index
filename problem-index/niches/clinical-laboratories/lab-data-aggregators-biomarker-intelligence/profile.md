# Lab Data Aggregators & Biomarker Testing Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/clinical-laboratories|Clinical Laboratories]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms that collect test results or testing-activity data from networks of labs they do not own and sell analysis to pharmaceutical companies: which patients are tested for a biomarker, where testing fails, and how that limits uptake of a targeted drug. Diaceutics (Belfast, AIM-listed) is the clearest case, with FY2025 revenue of £38.4M and work for 95 therapeutic brands (search summary). Claims-and-lab data platforms such as HealthVerity and Komodo sell lab data alongside other sources (recalled). Pharma brand and medical teams pay per brand engagement or subscription.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-150 analysts and data scientists per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Biomarker testing-rate analyses, lab-level testing gap reports, launch-readiness and commercial targeting for precision drugs
**Proprietary data:** Aggregated, de-identified lab testing data contributed by partner labs under data agreements, plus lab network relationships
**Clock:** The firm's delivery schedule; drug launch and label dates belong to the pharma client
**Buyer:** Chief commercial or data officer at the firm; precision medicine lead at the pharma client
