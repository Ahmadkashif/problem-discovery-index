# CMS Lab Fee Schedule & PAMA Rate-Setting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/clinical-laboratories|Clinical Laboratories]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The CMS staff and contractors who collect private payer rates from applicable labs under PAMA, compute weighted medians and set the Clinical Laboratory Fee Schedule. They also run the annual public meeting and advisory panel on new codes. The CY2027 cycle used data from 6,411 reporting NPIs, produced preliminary rates for 1,947 codes in September 2026 and promises final rates in November 2026.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Fewer than 10 CMS policy staff on the lab fee schedule, plus contractor support (estimate)
**Output:** CLFS rates, crosswalk and gapfill determinations, public data files
**Proprietary data:** Reported private payer rates by lab and code
**Clock:** Statutory reporting and rate cycles
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer
