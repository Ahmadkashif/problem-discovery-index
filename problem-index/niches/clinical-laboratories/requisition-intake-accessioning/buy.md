# Document Capture Trained on Lab Requisitions

**Niche:** [[niches/clinical-laboratories/requisition-intake-accessioning/profile|Requisition Intake & Accessioning]]
**Industry:** [[industries/clinical-laboratories|Clinical Laboratories]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Intelligent document processing already reads invoices and claims forms; lab requisitions mix checkboxes, handwriting, stickers and codes that generic models misread.
**Tags:** #transformers #large-language-models #data-integration #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to register each specimen and its requisition completely on first touch, and whoever accessions the highest share without a callback to the ordering office takes the account.

## The Problem
Labs that try generic document capture find it handles printed demographics but fails on test checkboxes across hundreds of requisition layouts, handwritten add-ons and test names in clinicians' shorthand.

## What Already Exists
Intelligent document processing platforms (ABBYY, Hyperscience, cloud document AI services) and healthcare intake tools used for faxed referrals.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs per-client requisition templates learned from examples, mapping to the lab's own test catalogue and order codes, and ICD-10 inference from written diagnoses with confidence. Tube-label-to-requisition matching rules from the lab's labelling policy have to be applied, and the output written straight into LIS order entry with an audit trail.

## Target Customer
Labs with high paper or fax volume that already license a document capture platform for billing or HR.

## Impact If Solved
Existing document tools become useful at accessioning. Moving even 60% of paper requisitions (estimate) to touchless entry cuts keying hours and transcription errors on the same volume.
