# Radiology Reading Worklists Adapted to Glass, Blocks and Stains

**Niche:** [[niches/clinical-laboratories/anatomic-pathology-digital/profile|Anatomic Pathology & the Digital Transition]]
**Industry:** [[industries/clinical-laboratories|Clinical Laboratories]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Radiology solved distributed reading with smart worklists a decade ago; pathology's worklist still assumes a physical tray on a desk.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #worker-facing #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to raise the cases each pathologist signs out per day, and whoever raises it with no rise in amended reports takes the account.

## The Problem
Pathology groups covering several hospitals and an outpatient lab balance work by moving glass and people. Even after digitising, they often lack worklist logic: subspecialty routing, workload balancing, turnaround targets by case type, and hand-offs between sites.

## What Already Exists
Radiology worklist and reading platforms that route studies by subspecialty, credentials, workload and urgency across sites (see [[industries/diagnostic-imaging-centers|Diagnostic Imaging Centers]]), plus digital pathology image management systems with basic case lists.

## The Customization Gap
Pathology adds what radiology lacks: cases made of many slides and blocks, recuts and stains that arrive in stages, frozen sections with surgeons waiting, and consults between pathologists. It also needs hybrid glass-and-digital operation during transition and AP LIS case state as the source of truth. Credentials and licensure follow the patient's state, as in teleradiology.

## Target Customer
Multi-site pathology groups and hospital AP departments that have begun scanning.

## Impact If Solved
Load balancing across sites and subspecialty routing borrowed from radiology. Raising cases per pathologist day ~5–10% (estimate) without hiring is the difference between meeting and missing turnaround in an understaffed specialty.
