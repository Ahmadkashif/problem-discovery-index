# Anatomic Pathology & the Digital Transition

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/clinical-laboratories|Clinical Laboratories]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to raise the cases each pathologist signs out per day, and whoever raises it with no rise in amended reports takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1B a year in slide scanners, image management, AP lab information systems, AI and workflow software sold to pathology labs (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% of industry revenue in tooling, against an anatomic pathology service line of perhaps a fifth of lab revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — most US cases are still read on glass under a microscope; scanning is routine in a minority of labs
**Target Buyer:** Pathology group president or lab medical director; AP operations director at a reference lab
**Automation Potential:** Medium-High — triage, stain prediction, measurement and report drafting; the diagnosis and signature remain the pathologist's

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Anatomic pathology turns tissue into a diagnosis: grossing, processing, embedding, cutting, staining, then a pathologist's read and sign-out. Every step is manual, and the read is the bottleneck. Anatomic pathology posts the highest vacancy rate in the lab, at 28.5% in ASCP's 2024 survey. Digital pathology moves the read to a screen and makes AI possible, but it adds scanning cost and time before any gain. Labcorp bought anatomic pathology assets from Incyte Diagnostics in 2025, and Tempus bought Paige and launched Paige Predict, which predicts 123 biomarkers from H&E slides. The ordering clinic's side of the biopsy loop is [[niches/healthcare-practice-software/dermatology-ehr-pathology-loop/profile|🎯 Dermatology EHR — the Pathology Loop]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Scanners from Leica, Philips, Hamamatsu and others; image management and AI from Proscia, PathAI, Tempus (Paige), Ibex and Aiforia; AP LIS from NovoPath, Epic Beaker AP, Clinisys and others; dictation from speech vendors and newcomers such as Voquill. Gaps: worklists in arrival order, priors fetched by hand, and AI tools bought one organ at a time without measured effect on sign-out volume.
