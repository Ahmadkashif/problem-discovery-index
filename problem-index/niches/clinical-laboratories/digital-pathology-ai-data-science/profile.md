# Digital Pathology AI Vendors' Clinical Data Science Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/clinical-laboratories|Clinical Laboratories]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The machine learning, clinical science and validation teams at digital pathology vendors: PathAI, Proscia, Tempus (which bought Paige and launched Paige Predict, predicting 123 biomarkers from H&E slides), Ibex, Aiforia, Modella AI and others. They train foundation and organ-specific models on slides from partner labs, run validation studies for FDA and for each lab's CLIA validation, and monitor deployed models. Labs pay software licences, not for the analysis.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-200 ML engineers, computational pathologists and clinical scientists per vendor (estimate); slide annotators are production
**Output:** Cleared and lab-validated algorithms, validation studies, deployment monitoring
**Proprietary data:** Training corpora of whole-slide images and diagnoses licensed from partner labs and hospitals, and deployed-model logs
**Clock:** FDA submissions on the vendor's own schedule; customers' go-live dates
**Buyer:** Chief scientific or chief medical officer at the vendor
