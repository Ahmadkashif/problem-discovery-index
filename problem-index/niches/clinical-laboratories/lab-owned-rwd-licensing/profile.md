# Lab-Owned Real-World Data Licensing Units

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/clinical-laboratories|Clinical Laboratories]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
The data businesses inside large and specialty labs that de-identify their own test results, often joined to clinical outcomes, and license them to drug developers, payers and researchers as datasets, cohort analyses and real-world evidence studies. Tempus is the clearest case: its Data and Applications segment took $316.4M in 2025, with Insights (data licensing) up 38%, and it was hiring real-world evidence data scientists in Chicago. Genomic and oncology labs such as Caris, Guardant Health, Foundation Medicine and NeoGenomics, and the national labs, run similar units (recalled). Pharma pays per dataset, per study or under multi-year agreements.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-150 data scientists, epidemiologists and RWE analysts per unit (estimate); clinical abstractors and de-identification operations are production
**Output:** Licensed de-identified cohorts, real-world evidence studies, biomarker-prevalence and treatment-pattern analyses
**Proprietary data:** The lab's own results history — genomic profiles, biomarkers, serial measurements — which no competitor can reproduce, plus outcomes abstracted from partner providers' records
**Clock:** The unit's own delivery schedule; clients' regulatory submissions and launch dates belong to the clients
**Buyer:** Head of data licensing or chief data officer at the lab; head of RWE at the pharma client
