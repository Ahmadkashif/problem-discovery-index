# Clinical Laboratory Trade & Professional Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/clinical-laboratories|Clinical Laboratories]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The American Clinical Laboratory Association, which with the Association for Molecular Pathology brought the suit that vacated FDA's LDT rule (search summary) and lobbies on PAMA, plus NILA for independent labs, ADLM for laboratory medicine and CLMA for lab managers (recalled). Members pay dues; most technical work is done by member volunteers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically under 10 research or policy analysts per association (estimate); journal editorial staff publish science, not commissioned analysis
**Output:** Advocacy, comment letters, member surveys, guidelines and journals
**Proprietary data:** Member surveys and committee records
**Clock:** Legislative and rulemaking calendars, such as the CLFS comment deadline
**Buyer:** Executive director or president
