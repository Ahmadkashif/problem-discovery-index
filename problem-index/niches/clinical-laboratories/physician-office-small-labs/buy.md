# Quality Management Software Re-Cut for a Two-Analyser Lab

**Niche:** [[niches/clinical-laboratories/physician-office-small-labs/profile|Physician Office & Small Independent Labs]]
**Industry:** [[industries/clinical-laboratories|Clinical Laboratories]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Laboratory quality management systems exist for hospitals and manufacturers; a two-analyser office lab needs one tenth of the features at one tenth of the price.
**Tags:** #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation #data-integration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a small moderate-complexity lab inspection-ready on a part-time director's hours, and whoever gets it through its two-yearly inspection with no condition-level deficiency takes the account.

## The Problem
Small labs either buy nothing and keep binders, or buy enterprise document control and QC software they cannot configure. Neither matches CLIA's requirements for a moderate-complexity lab run by two people.

## What Already Exists
Quality and document management systems (MasterControl, Qualio), lab QC data management from reagent vendors, and hospital lab quality modules. Testing labs' version of the same gap is analysed under [[industries/testing-laboratories|Testing Laboratories]].

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs CLIA- and accreditor-specific checklists pre-loaded and mapped to the lab's analytes, and competency assessment built around CLIA's six elements per tester per test system. It needs proficiency enrolment and result tracking, analyser-specific maintenance schedules, and pricing per lab per month rather than per seat.

## Target Customer
Physician office labs, urgent care and clinic labs, and small independents with one to ten testing staff.

## Impact If Solved
A small lab gets enterprise-grade compliance records for the price of a reagent order, about ~$200 a month (estimate), and a director can review the whole lab remotely in an hour.
