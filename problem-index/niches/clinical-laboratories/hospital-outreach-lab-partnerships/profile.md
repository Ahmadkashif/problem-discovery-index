# Hospital Outreach Programmes & Reference Lab Partnerships

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/clinical-laboratories|Clinical Laboratories]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to be the lab community practices send their routine orders to, and whoever keeps the largest share of each practice's routine order volume year on year takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$8B a year of routine testing for community physicians competed for by health-system outreach labs, national reference labs and regional independents (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15–20% of industry revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — interfaces carry most orders and results, but every new practice needs a build, and many still send paper
**Target Buyer:** Outreach director or system lab VP at a health system; sales and client-services leaders at reference and regional labs
**Automation Potential:** Medium — interface work, client onboarding and turnaround tracking are automatable; courier and draw coverage are not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Health systems run outreach programmes that test specimens from independent practices, nursing homes and clinics, using spare capacity in the hospital lab. They compete with Quest, Labcorp and regional independents for the same practices, and increasingly sell the book to them or hand them the lab to manage. Labcorp signed or closed 13 transactions with health systems and regional labs in 2025, including outreach businesses from Parkview Health and Community Health Systems. Quest began its largest lab-management contract, with Corewell Health, and plans a joint lab in Michigan in 2027. The practice chooses its lab on insurance networks and, among in-network options, on how fast and cleanly results land in its EHR, which is what decides how much of its volume a lab keeps from one year to the next. The hospital lab's inpatient work belongs to [[industries/general-hospitals|General Hospitals]]; this niche is the outreach business it sells to the community. The 2027 fee schedule cuts squeeze outreach margins and will push more sell-or-keep decisions.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Hospital LIS outreach modules (Epic Beaker Outreach, Oracle Health), interface engines, Quest's and Labcorp's own portals and EHR integrations, courier systems and outreach consultancies. Gaps: no end-to-end measure of requisition-to-result-in-EHR time per practice, slow interface onboarding, and outreach P&L that cannot be modelled against the new fee schedule.
