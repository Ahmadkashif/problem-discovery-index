# Remnant Specimen & Biobank Brokers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/clinical-laboratories|Clinical Laboratories]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Firms that source leftover clinical specimens and annotated samples from labs and hospitals, under consent and ethics approvals, and sell them with clinical data to diagnostics developers, drug companies and researchers. Discovery Life Sciences, iSpecimen, BioIVT and Precision for Medicine are examples (recalled). Buyers pay per specimen or per cohort; source labs receive fees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-50 scientists and data curators per firm (estimate); specimen handling and logistics are production
**Output:** Annotated specimen cohorts, matched clinical data, custom sourcing
**Proprietary data:** Specimen inventories and source-site relationships; the clinical annotations come from source sites
**Clock:** Clients' study and validation timelines
**Buyer:** Head of sourcing or chief scientific officer at the broker
