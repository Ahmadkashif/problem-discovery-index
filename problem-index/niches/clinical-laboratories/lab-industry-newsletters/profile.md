# Laboratory Industry Newsletters & Market Reports

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/clinical-laboratories|Clinical Laboratories]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Specialist publishers that cover the lab business — reimbursement, PAMA, deals, pricing and management practice — through subscription newsletters, webinars and occasional market reports (Dark Daily, 360Dx, Laboratory Economics and similar; recalled). Lab executives and vendors subscribe; sponsors pay for webinars.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Usually a handful of editors and analysts per publication (estimate); newsrooms are journalism, not research
**Output:** News, analysis, webinars, market estimates
**Proprietary data:** Subscriber surveys and editorial archives
**Clock:** Publication schedules set by the publisher
**Buyer:** Publisher or editor-in-chief
