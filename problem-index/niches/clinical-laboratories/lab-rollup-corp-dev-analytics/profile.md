# National Lab & Pathology Rollups' Corporate Development Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/clinical-laboratories|Clinical Laboratories]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The corporate development, integration and lab-management analytics teams at the national labs and the private-equity-backed pathology platforms that buy hospital outreach books, regional labs and pathology practices, or take over running hospital labs. Labcorp invested $582.0M in acquisitions in 2025 across 13 health-system and regional lab transactions. Quest's largest lab-management contract, with Corewell Health, began the same year. Their work is internal; nobody buys it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-100 analysts across corporate development, integration and lab-management performance per national lab (estimate); fewer at pathology platforms
**Output:** Target valuations, integration plans, lab-management performance reports for hospital partners
**Proprietary data:** Targets' volumes, payer mix and costs obtained in diligence, and the acquirer's own network data
**Clock:** Deal timetables set by the parties
**Buyer:** Chief strategy or corporate development officer
