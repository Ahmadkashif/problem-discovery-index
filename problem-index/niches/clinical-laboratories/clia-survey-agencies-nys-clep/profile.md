# State CLIA Survey Agencies & New York's Laboratory Evaluation Programme

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/clinical-laboratories|Clinical Laboratories]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State survey agencies inspect the 15,214 labs on CLIA certificates of compliance for CMS, mostly every two years. New York and Washington run their own CLIA-exempt programmes (7,235 and 8,106 labs, CMS September 2026). New York's Clinical Laboratory Evaluation Programme also reviews and approves laboratory-developed tests used on New York patients (recalled) — the main LDT review left since FDA's rule was vacated.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Surveyors and test reviewers, who are production; analytical staff are few (estimate)
**Output:** Survey reports and deficiency citations; LDT approvals
**Proprietary data:** Inspection findings; validation packages submitted by labs
**Clock:** Survey cycles and review queues
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer
