# Clinical Trial Central Laboratories

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/clinical-laboratories|Clinical Laboratories]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
CLIA-certified labs that test specimens from drug and device trials worldwide under one set of methods, manage kits and logistics to trial sites, and deliver cleaned laboratory datasets to sponsors. Labcorp's Central Labs revenue grew 11.1% in Q4 2025 within its $3.10B Biopharma Laboratory Services segment; Q2 Solutions, ICON, Eurofins, Medpace and Cerba Research compete (recalled). Sponsors pay per test and per study, plus data management fees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~50-300 data managers, scientific affairs and project staff per major central lab (estimate); bench and kit staff are production
**Output:** Validated trial lab datasets, data transfers to sponsors, safety-lab alerts, assay and specimen strategy for protocols
**Proprietary data:** Lab results from thousands of trials, but each study's data belongs to its sponsor
**Clock:** Sponsors' database locks, interim analyses and submission dates — the client's clock
**Buyer:** Head of central lab services or chief scientific officer at the central lab
