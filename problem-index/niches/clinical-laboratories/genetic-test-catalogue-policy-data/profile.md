# Genetic Test Catalogue & Coverage Policy Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/clinical-laboratories|Clinical Laboratories]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Vendors that maintain a structured catalogue of genetic and molecular tests on the market, with codes, components and prices, and track payers' coverage policies against them. Payers use the data to write policy and edit claims; labs and manufacturers use it to understand coverage. Concert Genetics is the best-known example (recalled; current ownership not confirmed). Payers and labs pay subscriptions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-60 genetic counsellors, policy analysts and data curators (estimate)
**Output:** Test catalogue and code mapping, payer policy comparisons, coverage and utilisation analytics
**Proprietary data:** A curated catalogue built over years from labs' public listings, plus structured readings of payer policies
**Clock:** The vendor's own update cadence; payers' policy cycles
**Buyer:** Head of product or data at the vendor; medical policy director at the payer client
