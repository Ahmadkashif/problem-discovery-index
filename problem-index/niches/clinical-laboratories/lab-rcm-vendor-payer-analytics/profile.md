# Lab Revenue Cycle Vendors' Denial & Payer Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/clinical-laboratories|Clinical Laboratories]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The analytics and payer-intelligence teams at lab-specialised billing vendors — XiFin, Telcor, Quadax and smaller firms (recalled except XiFin) — which process claims for many labs and publish benchmarks from them. XiFin's 2026 Payor Denial Impact Report analysed more than 30 million claims and says 73% of the largest labs use XiFin. Labs pay for the billing platform and services; the analysis comes bundled.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-100 payer-policy analysts and data analysts per vendor (estimate); claim processors, coders and appeals staff working individual claims are production
**Output:** Denial and appeal benchmarks, payer policy alerts, client performance reviews
**Proprietary data:** Claims, remittances and appeal outcomes for a large share of US lab volume across payers
**Clock:** The vendor's own reporting schedule; payers' timely-filing and appeal deadlines bind each claim, but that is the production work, not the analytics
**Buyer:** Chief product officer or head of client analytics at the vendor
