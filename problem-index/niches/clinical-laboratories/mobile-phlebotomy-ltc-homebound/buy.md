# Home Health Scheduling Re-Cut for Phlebotomy Routes

**Niche:** [[niches/clinical-laboratories/mobile-phlebotomy-ltc-homebound/profile|Mobile Phlebotomy for Nursing Homes & Home-Bound Patients]]
**Industry:** [[industries/clinical-laboratories|Clinical Laboratories]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Home health agencies already schedule clinicians to patients' homes with visit verification; a phlebotomy route is shorter, earlier and has a specimen clock attached.
**Tags:** #optimization-fundamentals #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to complete each draw ordered for a nursing-home resident or home-bound patient, and whoever loses the fewest ordered draws to missed visits and recollections takes the account.

## The Problem
Mobile phlebotomy services schedule with spreadsheets or with tools borrowed from home care, which assume hour-long visits booked days ahead rather than 10-minute draws ordered overnight and due by 7 a.m.

## What Already Exists
Home health scheduling and electronic visit verification platforms (WellSky, Axxess, HHAeXchange and others) and generic field-service scheduling software.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs lab order intake from facility EHRs and fax, and draw-time windows tied to medication passes and timed tests. It needs tube and handling requirements per order shown at the bedside, and specimen hand-off to the courier or lab with time stamps. Failed-draw reasons must go back to the facility immediately, with results routed into the facility's EHR rather than its fax machine.

## Target Customer
Mobile phlebotomy companies and the lab departments that run long-term care routes.

## Impact If Solved
A proven scheduling base with lab logic added. ~15% more draws per phlebotomist morning (estimate) from routes that follow timing constraints instead of habit.
