# Mobile Phlebotomy for Nursing Homes & Home-Bound Patients

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/clinical-laboratories|Clinical Laboratories]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to complete each draw ordered for a nursing-home resident or home-bound patient, and whoever loses the fewest ordered draws to missed visits and recollections takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$800M a year of testing and draw services for nursing-home residents and home-bound patients (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% of industry revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — orders arrive by fax and phone from facilities, routes are planned on paper, results go back by fax or a portal the facility rarely checks
**Target Buyer:** Owner or operations director of a regional lab or mobile phlebotomy service; director of nursing as the facility-side chooser
**Automation Potential:** Medium — order capture, route planning and result delivery automate; the draw at the bedside does not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Nursing-home residents and home-bound patients cannot go to a patient service centre, so phlebotomists go to them, usually before dawn so results are back for the morning medication pass and the physician's round. Medicare pays a specimen collection fee and a travel allowance for these draws (recalled), and a set of regional labs specialise in long-term care. The patients are frail, the veins are hard, orders change overnight, and every missed visit or recollection means a delayed anticoagulation dose adjustment or a missed infection. Facilities' own workflows are analysed under [[industries/skilled-nursing-facilities|Skilled Nursing Facilities]] and home-based care under [[industries/home-health-agencies|Home Health Agencies]]; neither covers the lab running the route.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Lab LIS order entry, fax and phone intake from facilities, generic route planning, and facility EHRs (PointClickCare and others) with lab interfaces only for the larger labs. Gaps: overnight order changes miss the route, draws that fail are not reported to the facility until it chases, and results land outside the facility's chart.
