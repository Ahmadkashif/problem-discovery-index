# Molecular & Genetic Test Coverage and Prior Authorization

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/clinical-laboratories|Clinical Laboratories]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get each high-dollar molecular or genetic test paid, through authorization, Z-code, coverage evidence and appeal, and whoever collects on the highest share of tests performed takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.5B a year in coverage, authorization, appeals and reimbursement staff and vendors at molecular, genetic and oncology labs (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of industry revenue, against a test category that took 43% of Medicare Part B lab spending in 2024 (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — portals and Z-code registries are online, but clinical evidence moves by fax and appeals are assembled by hand
**Target Buyer:** VP of reimbursement or market access at a molecular or genetic testing lab
**Automation Potential:** High for policy matching, submissions and appeal drafting; low for the evidence strategy behind coverage

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A single genetic or oncology test is paid hundreds to thousands of dollars, and whether it is paid depends on policy: the payer's or its lab benefit manager's criteria, Medicare's MolDX technical assessment and the DEX Z-code required on claims in MolDX states, and whether the ordering office obtained authorization at all. Medicare paid more than $3.6B for genetic tests in 2024 (HHS OIG). In XiFin's data, prior authorization made up 27.6% of molecular denials in 2025, against 13.1% in 2021. Labs often run the sample before the authorization exists, because clinicians and patients will not wait. The ordering practice's side of authorization is [[niches/physician-practices/ordering-practice-prior-authorization/profile|⚡ Prior Authorization at the Ordering Practice]], and the payer's decision is [[niches/health-insurance-carriers/outpatient-prior-authorization/profile|🎯 Outpatient Prior Authorization & Gold-Carding]]. This niche is the lab collecting after either one fails.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Lab RCM systems (XiFin and others), payer and benefit-manager portals, Availity, in-house reimbursement teams and patient-assistance programmes. Gaps: no prediction of coverage before the run, appeals built from scratch per case, and no closed loop between denials and the evidence a lab presents in its next coverage submission.
