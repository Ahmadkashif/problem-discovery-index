## The Problem
Financial coaching and workplace financial wellness programmes have spent years on the decisions that dominate outcomes below a certain balance: budgeting, emergency savings, debt prioritisation, match capture and benefit elections. They have curricula, behavioural programmes and measured engagement. They also have no ability to act — no accounts, no automation, no execution. The advice platform has all of the execution and none of the guidance.

## What Already Exists
Financial coaching curricula and programmes; workplace financial wellness platforms; debt prioritisation and budgeting tools; emergency savings programmes; and benefit election guidance.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is guidance joined to execution. It requires: (1) recommendations that can be acted on inside the same product, which is what coaching has always lacked and is the substantive combination; (2) the client's actual account and transaction data rather than self-reported budgets, making the guidance specific instead of generic; (3) delivery under an advisory regulatory frame, where coaching operates as education and the line matters; (4) a cost structure that supports no human coach, so the behavioural programme must work entirely through the product; and (5) integration with a workplace plan the platform does not run, which is where the match sits.

## Target Customer
Product and growth leadership, financial wellness vendors with no execution, workplace plan providers, and employers buying both separately today.

## Impact If Solved
Coaching knows what to say and cannot act; the platform can act and says nothing. Joining specific guidance to execution inside one product is the combination neither side has managed.
