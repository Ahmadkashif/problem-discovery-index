# Performance Attribution From Institutional Investing

**Industry:** [[industries/robo-advisors|Robo-Advisors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Institutional investors demand attribution against a stated benchmark as a condition of the relationship, and retail clients get a percentage.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell each client what the platform actually delivered for them, net of fees, taxes and their own behaviour, against a stated alternative — and whoever publishes that number first makes every competitor's marketing average look like what it is.

## The Problem
Institutional investing has rigorous attribution practice: returns decomposed against a policy benchmark into allocation, selection and interaction effects, reported net of fees, with standards governing presentation. It exists because institutional clients demanded it. Retail advice clients receive a return figure and a chart, and the attribution machinery has never been pointed at them.

## What Already Exists
Attribution methodologies decomposing return against a policy benchmark; performance presentation standards; net-of-fee reporting conventions; risk-adjusted measures; and the reporting infrastructure institutions require.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a client whose own behaviour is a return component. It requires: (1) client timing and cash flow decisions attributed explicitly as a component, which institutional attribution treats as exogenous and which is here the largest and most delicate term — this is the substantive adaptation; (2) tax as a first-order component, since after-tax outcome is the point of half the product and institutional attribution is typically pre-tax; (3) a counterfactual the client would recognise rather than a policy benchmark they never chose; (4) presentation to a non-professional audience, where the entire reporting apparatus assumes expertise; and (5) delivery at essentially zero marginal cost across millions of accounts.

## Target Customer
Investment and product leadership, clients evaluating the relationship, regulators considering value disclosure, and performance reporting vendors serving institutions.

## Impact If Solved
The attribution machinery is mature and was built because institutions demanded it. Pointing it at retail — with the client's own timing as an explicit component — is the adaptation, and the data is more complete here than it ever is institutionally.
