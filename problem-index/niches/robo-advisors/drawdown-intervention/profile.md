# Drawdown Intervention

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/robo-advisors|Robo-Advisors]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to find out what actually stops a client selling at the bottom — and whoever has run the experiments owns causal evidence nobody else in the industry has.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$550M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~14% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — untested campaigns
**Target Buyer:** Product and marketing leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — experimentation and targeted delivery

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This contest is causal. Knowing a client is likely to sell is useless without knowing what would change that. The candidate interventions are many — a message, a call from a licensed associate, a delay, a confirmation step, a projection, a reframing, doing nothing — and their effects are unknown, plausibly negative in some cases, and almost certainly different by client type. The only way to find out is to run experiments during market events, and the industry runs campaigns instead.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Market commentary emails, app banners, outbound calls to large accounts, and educational content. The gaps: interventions sent to everyone rather than targeted; no control groups; effects never estimated; heterogeneity never examined; and no record of what was tried in the last event.
