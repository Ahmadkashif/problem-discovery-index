# Client Understanding

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/robo-advisors|Robo-Advisors]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to know the client well enough to keep them invested — and the contest splits cleanly enough that it is not terminal.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.15B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~29% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — a questionnaire and an app
**Target Buyer:** Product and investment leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — inference and experimentation, both unexploited

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The platform's entire value proposition rests on the client staying invested through a drawdown, and its understanding of that client is a risk score assigned from six questions at signup. Meanwhile it observes every login, every allocation change, every panic sale and every funding pause — a continuous behavioural record of the exact thing that determines outcomes. This is the category's largest contest and its most conspicuous gap, but it is two contests: knowing what a client will do, and knowing what would change it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Signup questionnaires, risk scores, periodic reviews and market-event email campaigns. The gaps: the self-report never checked against behaviour; risk tolerance never updated; interventions sent without measurement; no experimental evidence for anything; and the behavioural record used for white papers rather than for client management.
