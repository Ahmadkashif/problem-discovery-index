# Transfers & Funding

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/robo-advisors|Robo-Advisors]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep money arriving — through an account transfer that stalls and a contribution that quietly stops — and whoever detects and recovers both without a human takes the growth the funnel currently leaks.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — automated flows, no recovery
**Target Buyer:** Operations and growth leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — detection and intervention over observable events

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
An account transfer from another custodian is where new clients are won or lost: it takes weeks, involves paperwork rejections and partial transfers, and the client cannot see what is happening. The recurring contribution is where existing clients are retained: it stops for a job change, a cash squeeze or an unnoticed bank failure, and a client whose funding stopped eleven months ago is the clearest attrition signal the platform has. Both are observable, mechanical and largely unmanaged.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Transfer initiation flows, operations queues, failed payment retries and reactivation emails. The gaps: transfer status not surfaced to the client; rejection causes not predicted or prevented; stopped contributions not detected as a signal; no distinction between a deliberate pause and a failure; and no recovery flow at all.
