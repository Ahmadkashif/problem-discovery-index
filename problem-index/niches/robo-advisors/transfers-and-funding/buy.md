# Payment Recovery and Dunning

**Niche:** [[niches/robo-advisors/transfers-and-funding/profile|Transfers & Funding]]
**Industry:** [[industries/robo-advisors|Robo-Advisors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Subscription businesses built an entire discipline around recovering failed payments, and investment platforms let a failed contribution simply not happen.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #evaluation-metrics #automation #revenue-impact #survival-analysis #confidence-intervals #workflow-orchestration #logistic-regression
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep money arriving — through an account transfer that stalls and a contribution that quietly stops — and whoever detects and recovers both without a human takes the growth the funnel currently leaks.

## The Problem
Subscription commerce developed involuntary churn recovery into a serious practice: retry timing optimised by failure code, account updater services, pre-dunning notifications, smart routing, and measured recovery rates. It exists because a failed payment is immediately visible as lost revenue. An investment platform's failed contribution costs the platform almost nothing this month, so none of the practice was ever adopted.

## What Already Exists
Retry optimisation by decline reason; card and account updater services; pre-dunning and payment method refresh flows; smart routing; and recovery rate measurement.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a payment the client benefits from rather than pays for. It requires: (1) framing recovery as the client's interest rather than the platform's, which changes the entire tone and is the substantive adaptation; (2) bank transfers rather than cards, where the failure modes, retry economics and updater infrastructure all differ; (3) voluntary cessation as the dominant case rather than involuntary failure, so classification precedes recovery; (4) a long tolerance window, since a missed month is not a churn event and over-aggressive recovery is harmful; and (5) an advisory relationship, which means outreach must be appropriate rather than promotional.

## Target Customer
Operations and growth leadership, clients whose funding failed silently, and payment recovery vendors for whom investment funding is unentered.

## Impact If Solved
The discipline exists because a failed subscription payment is visibly lost revenue and a missed contribution is not. Adapting it to a payment made in the client's own interest is what makes the outreach welcome rather than pushy.
