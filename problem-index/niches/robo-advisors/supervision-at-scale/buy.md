# Communications Supervision Practice

**Niche:** [[niches/robo-advisors/supervision-at-scale/profile|Supervision at Template Scale]]
**Industry:** [[industries/robo-advisors|Robo-Advisors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Communications supervision is a codified practice with lexicons, sampling and escalation, all designed around a human writing to a human.
**Tags:** #compliance #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #evaluation-metrics #descriptive-statistics #automation #confidence-intervals #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to supervise communications where one approved template reaches two hundred thousand people — and whoever redesigns supervision around the template rather than the message wins a function that currently samples the wrong thing entirely.

## The Problem
Communications supervision is well developed in brokerage and advisory: archiving, lexicon and risk-based sampling, escalation and disposition workflow, attestation, and examination against all of it. Every element assumes an individual composing a message to an individual, where the population of messages is heterogeneous and sampling is a reasonable inference. The digital platform's population is a handful of templates replicated at enormous scale.

## What Already Exists
Archiving platforms with retention; lexicon and risk-based sampling engines; review workflow with disposition and escalation; attestation and training programmes; and examination modules covering the whole process.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a population generated from templates. It requires: (1) the template and its generation logic as the supervised object, which existing platforms cannot represent at all — this is the substantive gap; (2) pre-send review rather than post-send sampling, since the scale makes after-the-fact discovery useless; (3) variant enumeration and testing, which is a software testing practice rather than a compliance one and does not exist in supervision tooling; (4) suitability assessed across the recipient population rather than per message, a concept the framework lacks; and (5) a regulatory conversation about control design, since the prescribed sampling can be satisfied while supervising almost nothing.

## Target Customer
Compliance and supervision leadership, reviewers, regulators examining digital advice, and supervision vendors whose model is the individual message.

## Impact If Solved
Every element of the practice assumes a human writing to a human. Representing the template and its generation logic as the supervised object is the missing capability, and it applies to every firm communicating at scale.
