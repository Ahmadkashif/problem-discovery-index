# Supervision at Template Scale

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/robo-advisors|Robo-Advisors]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to supervise communications where one approved template reaches two hundred thousand people — and whoever redesigns supervision around the template rather than the message wins a function that currently samples the wrong thing entirely.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — sampling rules from another era
**Target Buyer:** Compliance and supervision leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — testing and analysis before the send

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Supervision rules were written for a world of individual advisors sending individual communications, where sampling a percentage of messages was a sensible control. On a digital advice platform, most communication is a template sent simultaneously to hundreds of thousands of people, and personalised elements are generated by rules. Sampling ten percent of sends means reviewing the same template twenty thousand times while the one-off exception goes unexamined. The control is structurally misaligned with what it is supervising.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Communications archives, lexicon-based review, percentage sampling and periodic testing. The gaps: sampling by message rather than by template; personalisation logic unsupervised; no pre-send review of generated variants; suitability of a mass communication across segments never assessed; and reviewers spending their time on duplicates.
