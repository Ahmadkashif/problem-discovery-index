# Held-Away Assets

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/robo-advisors|Robo-Advisors]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to advise on the client's whole financial position when most of it is custodied somewhere else — and whoever makes the complete picture usable turns advice from account management into planning.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$500M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — aggregation shown, not used
**Target Buyer:** Product and advice leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — classification and allocation over aggregated positions

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The typical client's largest asset is a workplace retirement plan the platform does not custody. Around it sit an old employer rollover, a spouse's accounts, a brokerage account, and sometimes property. The platform advises on the slice it holds, which means the allocation it constructs so carefully may be irrelevant or actively wrong in the context of everything else. Aggregation exists as a display feature and almost never as an input to advice.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Account aggregation feeds, a net worth screen, and manual entry. The gaps: aggregated positions not classified into asset classes; the allocation not computed across the whole position; workplace plan options never modelled; advice not adjusted for what sits outside; and the aggregation connection allowed to break silently.
