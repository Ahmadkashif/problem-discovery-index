# Financial Planning Practice

**Niche:** [[niches/robo-advisors/held-away-assets/profile|Held-Away Assets]]
**Industry:** [[industries/robo-advisors|Robo-Advisors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Financial planning is a developed profession built around seeing everything, and digital advice sees one account and calls the result a plan.
**Tags:** #optimization-fundamentals #data-integration #compliance #evaluation-metrics #workflow-orchestration #confidence-intervals #automation #monte-carlo-methods
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to advise on the client's whole financial position when most of it is custodied somewhere else — and whoever makes the complete picture usable turns advice from account management into planning.

## The Problem
Financial planning has method: a complete data gathering process, goal quantification, cash flow modelling, asset location, insurance and tax coordination, scenario analysis and periodic review. Every element assumes a planner who sits with the client and collects the whole picture. Digital advice inherited the portfolio construction and none of the planning, because the data gathering step was replaced by a signup form.

## What Already Exists
Planning methodology and data gathering frameworks; goal-based planning with funded-status measurement; cash flow and scenario modelling tools; asset location practice; and planning software used by advisors.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to planning without a planner. It requires: (1) data gathered through aggregation and inference rather than through an interview, which is the substantive adaptation and determines whether anything else is possible; (2) goals captured and maintained in a product rather than discussed, so they decay unless the platform keeps them alive; (3) recommendations that must be actionable without a person to execute them, including inside plans the platform cannot trade; (4) a fee level that rules out professional time per client, which is the constraint the whole adaptation exists to satisfy; and (5) planning outputs that are supervised communications rather than conversations, changing how they can be expressed.

## Target Customer
Product and advice leadership, clients who believe they have a plan, planning software vendors serving advisors, and workplace plan providers.

## Impact If Solved
Planning's method assumes an interview that collects everything, and the signup form replaced it. Reconstructing the data gathering step from aggregation and inference is what would make the rest of the profession's method available at this price point.
