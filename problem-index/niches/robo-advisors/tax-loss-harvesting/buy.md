# Tax-Aware Portfolio Management

**Niche:** [[niches/robo-advisors/tax-loss-harvesting/profile|Tax-Loss Harvesting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/robo-advisors|Robo-Advisors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Private wealth management runs tax-aware portfolio management across a client's whole position with a tax professional in the loop, and robo harvesting optimises one account in isolation.

## The Problem
High-end private wealth management practises tax-aware investing properly: lot-level optimisation across all accounts, coordination with the client's tax position and their accountant, gain budgeting, asset location across account types, and multi-year planning. It works because the advisor sees everything and someone is thinking about the client's whole return. Robo harvesting is one excellent component of that, run in isolation on one account.

## What Already Exists
Tax-aware optimisation and gain budgeting; lot-level tax management across accounts; asset location methodology; coordination with tax preparation; and multi-year tax planning practice.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to scale without a human in the loop. It requires: (1) the client's tax position inferred or captured through a product rather than supplied by their accountant, which is the substantive adaptation and is the reason the practice has not scaled down; (2) optimisation across accounts the platform does not custody, connecting directly to held-away assets; (3) gain budgeting without a conversation, so the client's expected realisation must be modelled rather than discussed; (4) a fee level that cannot support professional judgement per client, which is the whole constraint; and (5) a client who may not know their own marginal rate, where private wealth simply asks the accountant.

## Target Customer
Investment and product leadership, tax professionals receiving the output, clients with positions outside the platform, and portfolio optimisation vendors serving the upper tier.

## Impact If Solved
The full practice exists and requires an advisor who sees everything. Capturing enough of the client's tax position through a product to run it without one is the adaptation, and it is what the fee model demands.
