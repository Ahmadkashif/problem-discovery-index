# Tax-Loss Harvesting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/robo-advisors|Robo-Advisors]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to prove what harvesting actually delivered for this specific client after wash-sale exposure they cannot see — and whoever can state a per-client realised number replaces an industry-wide average that applies to almost nobody.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$750M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~19% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** High — execution excellent, measurement absent
**Target Buyer:** Investment and product leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — per-client attribution and exposure detection

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Harvesting is the feature the category markets on. The execution is genuinely good. The value is quoted as an average benefit in basis points, derived from a simulation, that depends entirely on the client's marginal rate, holding period, contribution pattern, market path and whether they ever realise gains at all — which means it applies to almost no individual client. Underneath sits a substitute-security and wash-sale problem whose most dangerous exposure is in accounts the platform cannot see.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Automated harvesting engines, substitute security pairs, in-house wash-sale avoidance, and marketing averages. The gaps: no per-client realised value; no accounting for future gain realisation; wash-sale exposure from external accounts invisible; substitute pairs not evaluated for tracking cost; and no reporting a client could check.
