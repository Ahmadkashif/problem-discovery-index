# Contact Centre Practice for Advice

**Niche:** [[niches/robo-advisors/the-licensed-associate/profile|The Licensed Associate]]
**Industry:** [[industries/robo-advisors|Robo-Advisors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Contact centre practice optimises handle time and first contact resolution, and the call that matters here should probably take longer.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #worker-facing #evaluation-metrics #large-language-models #automation #descriptive-statistics #confidence-intervals #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to put a client's full history in front of a licensed associate before the call connects — and whoever does it turns the worst days of a decade from an endurance test into the moment the product proves itself.

## The Problem
Contact centre management is a mature operational discipline: forecasting and scheduling, skills-based routing, screen pop with customer context, quality monitoring, knowledge bases, and a metric set built around handling volume efficiently. Digital advice platforms have adopted it wholesale. The metric set is close to inverted for the interaction that matters, where a longer call that prevents a liquidation is worth far more than three short ones.

## What Already Exists
Workforce management and surge forecasting; skills-based routing; screen pop and customer context delivery; quality monitoring and call scoring; and knowledge base and agent assist tooling.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a call whose value is the financial outcome. It requires: (1) quality measured by the client's subsequent behaviour rather than by call handling, which is the substantive inversion and requires joining call records to account activity; (2) routing by financial risk rather than by skill or queue position, so the client about to liquidate is prioritised; (3) context that is a behavioural and planning history rather than an order status, which no screen pop implementation contains; (4) surge forecasting driven by market conditions rather than by seasonality, where the predictor is volatility and the correlation is strong; and (5) a licensed, supervised conversation, so agent assist must produce defensible advice rather than suggested phrasing.

## Target Customer
Client service and operations leadership, licensed associates, compliance functions supervising the calls, and contact centre vendors for whom advice is an unentered vertical.

## Impact If Solved
The whole metric set optimises for handling volume and the valuable call is the long one. Reorienting quality and routing around the client's subsequent financial behaviour is the adaptation, and the data to do it already exists on both sides.
