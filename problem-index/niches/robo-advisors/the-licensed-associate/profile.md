# The Licensed Associate

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/robo-advisors|Robo-Advisors]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to put a client's full history in front of a licensed associate before the call connects — and whoever does it turns the worst days of a decade from an endurance test into the moment the product proves itself.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$350M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — generic CRM, no context
**Target Buyer:** Client service leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — context assembly and triage

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
On the worst market days of a decade, a small licensed team becomes the entire company. Calls arrive from frightened clients, and the associate reads each client's history for the first time as the phone connects: what they hold, what they said their tolerance was, what they did in the last drawdown, how much they have contributed, whether they have called before. The product's whole value proposition is delivered or lost in those conversations, by people working with almost nothing in front of them.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A CRM record, the account view, and talking points circulated that morning. The gaps: no client history assembled before the call; no risk flag from behavioural signals; no record of what worked in previous calls; no routing by client need; and no measurement of whether the call changed anything.
