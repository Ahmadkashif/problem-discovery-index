# Niche Analysis — Robo-Advisors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/robo-advisors|Robo-Advisors]]

## Niche Selection

A digital advice platform rebalances, harvests and allocates flawlessly on top of a client understanding built from a six-question form. Its value depends almost entirely on a behaviour it does not manage — whether the client stays invested when markets fall — and it holds the cleanest behavioural finance dataset that has ever existed: millions of investors with a risk tolerance recorded before any market event, followed by a complete record of what they actually did in every drawdown since. It publishes occasional white papers from that and manages clients from the questionnaire. The eight niches below split the category by what competitors actually fight over.

## Why These Niches

Client understanding takes the largest share because it determines the allocation, the retention and the outcome, and is currently a form filled in once. Tax-loss harvesting is second because it is the category's headline feature, its value is quoted as an average that applies to almost nobody, and the exposure that could invalidate it sits in accounts the platform cannot see. The two low-digitized niches are the ones still done by hand or not at all: the client's actual financial position outside the platform, and supervision rules written for individual advisors applied to templates sent to two hundred thousand people. The two underserved audiences are the small licensed team that becomes the entire business on the worst days of a decade, and the client whose balance places them below any human attention. The two automatable niches are the funding and transfer flow that quietly stops, and the question of what the platform actually delivered.

## Filter Notes

Seven of the eight level-1 niches are terminal. Client Understanding is not: the label names a subject rather than a contest, and writing the contested statement produces two sentences with different winners. Risk tolerance measurement asks what a client's actual tolerance is, inferred from a behavioural record rather than taken from a self-report — a measurement and prediction problem where the winner is whoever predicts drawdown behaviour most accurately from what the platform already observes. Drawdown intervention asks what actually stops a client from selling at the bottom — a causal question answerable only by experimentation, where the winner is whoever has run the experiments and knows which message, delay or friction works for which client. One is inference about a person and the other is causal evidence about an action, and they are built by different people from different evidence. It therefore decomposes into **Risk Tolerance Measurement** and **Drawdown Intervention**.

Two candidates were considered and rejected. **The human advisory relationship** is the alternative these platforms were built against, and the contest over it belongs to [[industries/wealth-management-rias|Wealth Management RIAs]]. **Onboarding identity verification** is real work at account opening but is the contest of [[industries/identity-verification-vendors|Identity Verification Vendors]], where it is analysed on its own terms.
