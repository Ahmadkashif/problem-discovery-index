# Risk Tolerance Measurement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/robo-advisors|Robo-Advisors]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to predict how a client will actually behave in the next drawdown from the behaviour the platform already observes — and whoever predicts it best replaces the questionnaire as the input that sets the allocation.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — a self-report, never validated
**Target Buyer:** Investment and product leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — a prediction problem with labelled outcomes

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This contest is measurement. Given everything the platform observes — login frequency in falling markets, allocation changes, funding pauses, prior drawdown behaviour, goal edits, withdrawal patterns — what is this client's actual tolerance, and what will they do next time? It is a prediction problem with real labels, since every past drawdown produced an observed outcome for every client. It has nothing in common with determining what action would change that outcome.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A six-question instrument, a risk score, and an occasional review prompt. The gaps: the instrument never validated against behaviour; behaviour never used as a signal; no prediction of future behaviour; no confidence attached to the score; and no segmentation by anything that predicts anything.
