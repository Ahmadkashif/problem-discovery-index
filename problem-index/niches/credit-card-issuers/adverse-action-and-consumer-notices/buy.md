# Communications Platforms Fitted to Credit Notice Rules

**Niche:** [[niches/credit-card-issuers/adverse-action-and-consumer-notices/profile|Adverse Action & Regulatory Notices]]
**Industry:** [[industries/credit-card-issuers|Credit Card Issuers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Customer communications platforms generate millions of documents well; they do not know which credit event needs which notice by when.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #compliance #workflow-orchestration #evaluation-metrics #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn each model-driven decline, line decrease or rate increase into a notice whose stated reasons are the model's actual reasons and which goes out inside the Reg B and Reg Z clocks, and whoever makes reasons both faithful to the model and defensible to an examiner takes the account.

## The Problem
Notices are generated from templates triggered by system events, and the timing and content rules live in compliance memos.

## What Already Exists
Customer communications management platforms such as OpenText Exstream, Smart Communications and Quadient, used across banking and insurance.

## The Customization Gap
The card version needs an event-to-notice rulebook (decline, counteroffer, line decrease, rate increase, penalty APR, change in terms), each with its clock and required content; credit score disclosure fields; reason ranking from the decision record; delivery tracking proving when each notice went out; and versioning as rules change.

## Target Customer
Consumer compliance and card operations teams.

## Impact If Solved
Every required notice sent correctly and provably on time; at an issuer sending millions of notices a year, a 0.1% timing or content error rate is thousands of potential violations (estimate), so driving it towards zero closes a recurring examination finding.
