# Nonprofit Credit Counseling Agencies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-card-issuers|Credit Card Issuers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Nonprofit agencies, many affiliated with the National Foundation for Credit Counseling, that counsel indebted consumers and set up debt management plans in which card issuers agree to lower rates and fees; issuers fund part of the work through contributions on plan payments.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Analytical staff under 10 per agency; counsellors are production headcount
**Output:** Counselling sessions and debt management plans
**Proprietary data:** Client budgets and plan outcomes per agency
**Clock:** Issuers' plan acceptance terms
**Buyer:** Executive director
