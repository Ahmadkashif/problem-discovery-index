# Rewards & Loyalty Platform Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-card-issuers|Credit Card Issuers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors that run card rewards programmes for issuers — accrual, catalogues, travel booking, transfer-partner connections, offers — and sell programme analytics alongside: Kobie, Ascenda, Augeo and the networks' loyalty services are examples. Issuers pay platform and per-redemption fees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-60 programme analysts and data scientists per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Programme performance reporting, redemption and engagement analyses, programme-design advice
**Proprietary data:** Earn and redemption behaviour across client programmes, processed under each client's contract
**Clock:** Clients' programme launch and change dates
**Buyer:** Head of Analytics / Chief Product Officer
