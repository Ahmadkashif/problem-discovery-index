# Card Operations Benchmarking Roundtables

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-card-issuers|Credit Card Issuers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Peer roundtables that collect operating metrics from member issuers — chargebacks and disputes, fraud, collections and recoveries, compliance, co-brand partnerships — and turn them into benchmark reports and facilitated peer sessions. Auriemma Roundtables, the former Auriemma Consulting Group, is the established example: its card groups have run for decades, a 2021 session drew the top 20 card issuers among its members, and its data now underpins vendor benchmarks such as Quavo's 2026 dispute-management report. Members pay annual fees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~15-40 researchers, benchmark analysts and facilitators (estimate)
**Output:** Benchmark studies per roundtable, member surveys, peer-session findings
**Proprietary data:** Years of contributed operating metrics from most large US issuers, by function
**Clock:** The roundtable meeting calendar, set by the firm
**Buyer:** Managing director of the roundtables business
