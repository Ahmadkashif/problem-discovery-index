# Co-Brand & Card Portfolio Advisory

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-card-issuers|Credit Card Issuers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation firms

## What They Do
Payments-specialist advisers and the card practices of strategy consultancies run co-brand and private-label requests for proposal for airlines, hotel groups, retailers and technology companies, value portfolios for sale and purchase, and advise issuers on bids. The partner or seller pays a fee, sometimes with a success component.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-50 consultants and analysts per firm's card practice (estimate)
**Output:** Portfolio valuations, RFP design and bid evaluation, negotiation support
**Proprietary data:** A record of deal terms and portfolio economics across past processes, held under the confidentiality terms of each mandate
**Clock:** Contract expiry dates written into the partner's existing agreement, which set the client's process schedule
**Buyer:** Managing Director of the card practice
