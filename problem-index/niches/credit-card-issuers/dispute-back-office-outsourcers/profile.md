# Dispute & Fraud Back-Office Outsourcers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-card-issuers|Credit Card Issuers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Business-process outsourcers and processor service arms that work issuers' dispute, chargeback and fraud-alert queues — intake, evidence requests, network filings, cardholder calls — under service-level agreements, often offshore.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Large production teams; analytical staff under 10 per client programme
**Output:** Worked cases against service levels
**Proprietary data:** None of their own; case data belongs to each issuer
**Clock:** Reg Z and network deadlines on each case
**Buyer:** Operations director
