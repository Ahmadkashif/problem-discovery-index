# Marketing Uplift Tools Fitted to Credit Lines

**Niche:** [[niches/credit-card-issuers/credit-line-management/profile|Credit Line Management]]
**Industry:** [[industries/credit-card-issuers|Credit Card Issuers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Uplift modelling is mature in marketing, where the outcome is a purchase next week; a line increase's outcome includes a default eighteen months later.
**Tags:** #causal-inference #gradient-boosting #survival-analysis #evaluation-metrics #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to prove, on randomised holdout cells, that each line increase or decrease earns more incremental revolving balance and interchange than it adds in loss, and whoever measures that counterfactual best takes the account.

## The Problem
Issuers want to target line increases at accounts whose behaviour will change because of them, not at accounts that would have revolved anyway.

## What Already Exists
Uplift and experimentation tooling from marketing analytics, open-source causal libraries, and experimentation platforms.

## The Customization Gap
The credit version needs a net-revenue outcome that subtracts expected loss and funding cost; survival modelling of default with long, censored horizons; constraints from ability-to-pay and exposure limits; fair-lending checks on who receives increases; and documentation that passes model risk review.

## Target Customer
Credit strategy teams at mid-size issuers without in-house causal modelling.

## Impact If Solved
Line increases that create revenue rather than reward accounts that would have spent anyway.
