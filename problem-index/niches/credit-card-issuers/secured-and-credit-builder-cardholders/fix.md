# The Deposit Refund That Takes Two Statements

**Niche:** [[niches/credit-card-issuers/secured-and-credit-builder-cardholders/profile|Secured & Credit-Builder Cardholders]]
**Industry:** [[industries/credit-card-issuers|Credit Card Issuers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** When a secured cardholder graduates or closes the account, getting the security deposit back can take weeks, often for people who can least afford to wait.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #workflow-orchestration #compliance #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to graduate secured and starter cardholders to unsecured lines and increases at the moment their behaviour earns it — not on a fixed six- or twelve-month review — so that they stay as they become prime, and whoever reads early payment and cash-flow behaviour best takes the account.

## The Problem
Refunds wait for the balance to settle across statement cycles, for manual checks on pending transactions and disputes, and for a cheque to be cut.

## Why It's Still Broken
Deposit handling sits between the card processor and the deposit system, and refunds are a low-priority back-office queue.

## What a Fix Looks Like
An automated release rule that refunds the deposit less any unpaid balance as soon as pending activity clears, with electronic payment and a status the cardholder can see.

## Who Feels the Pain
Graduating cardholders, agents fielding "where is my deposit" calls, and complaint teams.

## Impact If Fixed
Deposits returned in ~5 business days rather than ~30–60 days (estimate), most "where is my deposit" calls gone, and a graduation that feels like a reward rather than a wait.
