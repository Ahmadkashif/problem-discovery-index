# Graduation on Behaviour, Not Tenure

**Niche:** [[niches/credit-card-issuers/secured-and-credit-builder-cardholders/profile|Secured & Credit-Builder Cardholders]]
**Industry:** [[industries/credit-card-issuers|Credit Card Issuers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Secured cardholders are reviewed for graduation on a fixed schedule, so the issuer loses the best of them to competitors and graduates some of the riskiest on the anniversary.
**Tags:** #survival-analysis #gradient-boosting #causal-inference #evaluation-metrics #feature-engineering #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to graduate secured and starter cardholders to unsecured lines and increases at the moment their behaviour earns it — not on a fixed six- or twelve-month review — so that they stay as they become prime, and whoever reads early payment and cash-flow behaviour best takes the account.

## The Problem
Graduation reviews at six, eight or twelve months use the bureau score and a payment count. Early behaviour — paying in full versus minimum, utilisation pattern, payment timing, linked deposit flows — carries most of the signal and is barely used.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Secured portfolios are small and low-margin, so they get the least analytics; and graduating early feels risky to a credit committee without evidence.

## What to Build
A monthly graduation score from the account's own behaviour plus consented cash-flow data, with a randomised test of earlier graduation for top-scoring cardholders, measuring loss, retention and later spend; and a line-sizing rule for the unsecured line that follows.

## Target Customer
Starter and secured product leaders at mainstream issuers and credit unions.

## Impact If Built
Better cardholders graduated when they are ready, kept as they become prime, with loss measured rather than feared.
