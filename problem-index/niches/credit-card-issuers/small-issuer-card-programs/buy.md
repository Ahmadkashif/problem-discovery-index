# Big-Issuer Decision Engines Packaged Small

**Niche:** [[niches/credit-card-issuers/small-issuer-card-programs/profile|Community Bank & Credit Union Card Programmes]]
**Industry:** [[industries/credit-card-issuers|Credit Card Issuers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Decision engines are priced and staffed for issuers with strategy teams; a small issuer needs the strategies, not the engine.
**Tags:** #decision-trees #gradient-boosting #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give an issuer with a few thousand to a hundred thousand card accounts underwriting, line and rewards strategies calibrated on pooled peer-portfolio performance rather than on its own thin history, and whoever's pooled performance data is deepest takes the account — whether the institution keeps the balances or rents its brand to an agent-bank programme.

## The Problem
Small issuers rely on the processor's built-in logic for approvals, lines and authorisations, with little ability to change it.

## What Already Exists
Decision engines from FICO, Experian, Provenir and Taktile, and ML underwriting models from vendors such as Zest AI.

## The Customization Gap
A small-issuer package needs pre-built, documented strategies for acquisition, line management and over-limit authorisation; adverse action reason mapping and fair-lending testing included; model-risk documentation an examiner of a small institution will accept; integration with the processor rather than a separate stack; and pricing per account rather than per seat.

## Target Customer
Card programme managers at community banks and credit unions running portfolios in house.

## Impact If Solved
Current strategies at ~$1–3 per account a year (estimate) — ~$20,000–60,000 for a 20,000-account portfolio — instead of an enterprise engine licence and a strategy analyst the portfolio cannot carry.
