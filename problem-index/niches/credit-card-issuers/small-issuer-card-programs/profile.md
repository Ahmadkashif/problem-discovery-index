# Community Bank & Credit Union Card Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-card-issuers|Credit Card Issuers]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give an issuer with a few thousand to a hundred thousand card accounts underwriting, line and rewards strategies calibrated on pooled peer-portfolio performance rather than on its own thin history, and whoever's pooled performance data is deepest takes the account — whether the institution keeps the balances or rents its brand to an agent-bank programme.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2B a year in card processing, programme management, decisioning and rewards services bought by small issuers (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of issuer spend on data, decisioning and operations (estimate); credit unions alone held $85.7B, ~7%, of card balances in 2024 (CFPB)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — processing is outsourced and digital; strategy is the processor's default
**Target Buyer:** Chief Lending Officer or Card Programme Manager at a community bank or credit union
**Automation Potential:** High — strategy, rewards and compliance can be packaged

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
About 3,700 institutions reported card balances in 2024, down from 4,700 a decade earlier (CFPB), and large issuers hold about 84% of balances. The rest run portfolios too small to support a credit strategy team, a rewards team or their own fraud models. They either issue through a processor or credit union service organisation and accept its default strategies, or hand the programme to an agent-bank provider such as Elan Financial Services or TCM Bank and keep only a revenue share. Both paths leave the institution's own member and customer data — deposits, tenure, relationship — out of the card decision. Their parent industries are [[industries/credit-unions|Credit Unions]] and [[industries/community-banks|Community Banks]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Processors (FIS Total Issuing Solutions, Fiserv, Velera), agent-bank programmes, ML underwriting vendors such as Zest AI, white-label rewards. Gaps: no pooled performance for strategy calibration, no use of deposit data in line decisions, and keep-or-rent decisions made on a quote.
