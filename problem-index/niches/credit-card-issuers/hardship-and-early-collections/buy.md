# Collections Voice Agents Fitted to the Issuer's Own Book

**Niche:** [[niches/credit-card-issuers/hardship-and-early-collections/profile|Hardship & Early-Stage Collections]]
**Industry:** [[industries/credit-card-issuers|Credit Card Issuers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** AI voice and messaging agents for debt collection were built for agencies working under Reg F; an issuer collecting its own accounts needs them to know its hardship programmes and its brand.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #compliance #workflow-orchestration #worker-facing #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to match each stretched or delinquent cardholder to the treatment — payment plan, APR reduction, re-age or settlement — that returns the account to paying rather than postponing its charge-off, and whoever predicts cure best takes the account.

## The Problem
Early-bucket collections is high-volume, repetitive contact with customers the issuer wants to keep.

## What Already Exists
AI voice and digital collections agents sold to collection agencies and lenders, with Reg F call-frequency and validation-notice logic.

## The Customization Gap
The issuer version needs: its own hardship programme eligibility and offer rules; CARD Act and state creditor rules rather than (or as well as) Reg F; Servicemembers Civil Relief Act rate caps; brand voice for a customer still holding the card; hand-off to a human for hardship disclosures; and outcome tracking by treatment, not only by promise to pay.

## Target Customer
Heads of collections at issuers with large early-bucket volumes.

## Impact If Solved
Routine contacts handled automatically, freeing experienced agents for hardship conversations.
