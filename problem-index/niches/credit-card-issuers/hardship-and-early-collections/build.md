# Outreach Before the First Missed Payment

**Niche:** [[niches/credit-card-issuers/hardship-and-early-collections/profile|Hardship & Early-Stage Collections]]
**Industry:** [[industries/credit-card-issuers|Credit Card Issuers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Card collections starts when a payment is missed, though the signs of stress — a falling payment, rising cash advances, a deposit that stopped arriving — show up weeks earlier.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #survival-analysis #change-point-detection #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to match each stretched or delinquent cardholder to the treatment — payment plan, APR reduction, re-age or settlement — that returns the account to paying rather than postponing its charge-off, and whoever predicts cure best takes the account.

## The Problem
By the time an account enters the first delinquency bucket, the cardholder has often already fallen behind elsewhere and the cheapest treatments are gone. Issuers see early signals in their own data and, where the cardholder banks with them, in deposits, but they rarely act on them.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Pre-delinquency outreach looks like a cost with no booked benefit, the signals sit in different systems, and contacting a current customer about hardship is delicate.

## What to Build
A stress model on payment amounts, utilisation, cash advances, bureau triggers and, with consent, deposit flows, that flags accounts at rising risk of first default and offers a low-cost, opt-in option — a payment-date move, a temporary plan — before the miss, measured on randomised holdouts.

## Target Customer
Collections strategy directors at issuers with deposit relationships or rich bureau trigger feeds.

## Impact If Built
Fewer accounts entering delinquency, cheaper cures, and cardholders helped before late fees and credit reporting.
