# Federal Reserve Card Data & Stress-Test Modelling

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-card-issuers|Credit Card Issuers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Federal Reserve collects monthly account-level card data from the largest bank holding companies on the FR Y-14M report, models card losses for its supervisory stress tests, and publishes aggregate analysis — the Philadelphia Fed's Consumer Finance Institute reports on large-bank card data are the public face. No one buys the output.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-50 economists, modellers and data staff working on card data across the Board and Reserve Banks (estimate)
**Output:** Supervisory loss projections, stress-test results, research reports and data briefs
**Proprietary data:** Account-level card data from the largest issuers, collected under supervisory authority
**Clock:** The annual stress-test cycle
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer
