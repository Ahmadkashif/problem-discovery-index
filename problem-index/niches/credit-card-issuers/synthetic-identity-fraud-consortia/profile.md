# Synthetic Identity & First-Party Fraud Consortia

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-card-issuers|Credit Card Issuers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors that score applications for synthetic identity and first-party fraud risk using pooled application and outcome data contributed by many lenders — SentiLink and Socure are representative, alongside the bureaus' own synthetic-identity products. Issuers pay per application scored. The wider identity market is covered under [[industries/identity-verification-vendors|Identity Verification Vendors]]; this pocket is its consortium-analytics layer as it serves card issuers, where TransUnion put synthetic-identity exposure across cards and other consumer credit at $3.3B at end-2024.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-80 data scientists and fraud analysts per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Application-level risk scores with reason codes, investigation support, fraud-typology research
**Proprietary data:** Applications across many lenders joined to confirmed fraud outcomes reported back by members
**Clock:** None external; issuers buy on their own planning cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Data Scientist / Head of Fraud Analytics
