# Card Network Issuer Consulting & Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-card-issuers|Credit Card Issuers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The consulting and analytics arms of the card networks — Visa Consulting & Analytics and Mastercard's data and services consulting — advise issuers on portfolio growth, activation, rewards design, authorisation performance, fraud and pricing, using network-wide transaction data. Engagements are sold for fees, but a large share is delivered as part of network agreements, where the advice helps win or keep an issuer's card volume.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 550+ payments consultants, data scientists and economists at Visa Consulting & Analytics (Visa FY2020 10-K; no newer count); a comparable unit at Mastercard (estimate)
**Output:** Portfolio diagnostics, benchmarking, campaign and rewards analyses, consulting engagements
**Proprietary data:** Network transaction data across every issuer and merchant on the network
**Clock:** Network agreement renewals and issuers' own campaign calendars
**Buyer:** Global head of consulting and analytics at the network
