# Card Portfolio Benchmarking Consortia

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-card-issuers|Credit Card Issuers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Consortia that collect account-level card performance from member issuers, normalise it, and sell back peer benchmarks — acquisition volumes and quality, line assignment, utilisation, revolve, losses, rewards and pricing — plus custom competitive analyses. Argus Information & Advisory Services, bought by TransUnion with Verisk Financial Services in April 2022 for $505.7M, is the defining example; Verisk Financial had $143M of revenue in 2021. Issuers' strategy, finance and marketing teams pay subscriptions to see how their portfolio compares with the market.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~100-300 analysts, data scientists and client consultants at the leading consortium (estimate; no public count)
**Output:** Monthly and quarterly peer benchmarks, segment and competitive studies, custom portfolio analyses for members
**Proprietary data:** Contributed account-level card and deposit performance from member issuers over many years — a cross-issuer view no single issuer, regulator or bureau assembles at this granularity
**Clock:** Monthly benchmark releases ahead of members' quarterly earnings and annual planning; no regulatory deadline on the consortium itself
**Buyer:** Head of the consortium business / Chief Analytics Officer
