# Card Issuer Trade Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-card-issuers|Credit Card Issuers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The American Bankers Association's card policy staff, the Consumer Bankers Association, the Electronic Payments Coalition and fintech lender associations lobby on rate caps, late fees, interchange and data rules, and publish occasional market monitors and surveys.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research staff on cards per association
**Output:** Policy papers, comment letters, periodic market monitors
**Proprietary data:** Member surveys
**Clock:** Rulemaking and legislative calendars
**Buyer:** Chief economist or head of policy
