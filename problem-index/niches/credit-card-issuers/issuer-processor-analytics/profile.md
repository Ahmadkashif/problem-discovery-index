# Issuer Processor Analytics Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-card-issuers|Credit Card Issuers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The analytics and consulting groups inside card processors — FIS Total Issuing Solutions (the former TSYS, acquired January 2026 at ~$13.5B enterprise value), Fiserv's card platforms, and newer processors such as Galileo, Marqeta and i2c — that sell portfolio reporting, benchmarking and strategy support to the issuers whose accounts they process.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-150 analysts and consultants per processor (estimate)
**Output:** Portfolio dashboards, peer comparisons, strategy and fraud-rule consulting
**Proprietary data:** Account and transaction records of every client issuer on the platform, held as processor for each client
**Clock:** Clients' contract renewals and conversion dates
**Buyer:** Head of Analytics Services
