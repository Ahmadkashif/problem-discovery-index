# Contract Obligations Nobody Can Find at Renewal

**Niche:** [[niches/credit-card-issuers/co-brand-partnership-bidding/profile|Co-Brand Partnership Bidding & Renewal]]
**Industry:** [[industries/credit-card-issuers|Credit Card Issuers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** When a co-brand renewal opens, the issuer's team spends weeks reconstructing what the current contract and its amendments actually commit both sides to.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #compliance #workflow-orchestration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to price a partner's portfolio — remuneration per mile or point, the spend and revolve the partner's customers will generate, acquisition through the partner's own channels, and the balances that move at contract expiry — closer to its true lifetime value than the rival bidder, and whoever prices it best takes the account.

## The Problem
Exclusivity, marketing spend commitments, service levels, data-sharing rights and — most important — the portfolio purchase terms at termination are spread across a master agreement and years of amendments.

## Why It's Still Broken
Contracts are negotiated by deal teams and filed by legal; nobody maintains a live obligations register.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Extract every obligation, rate and termination term into a register with the clause linked, apply amendments by effective date, and track performance against each commitment monthly.

## Who Feels the Pain
Partnership teams preparing a renewal, finance teams reconciling payments, and legal teams asked what happens to the portfolio if the partner leaves.

## Impact If Fixed
Renewals start from a known position instead of a document hunt.
