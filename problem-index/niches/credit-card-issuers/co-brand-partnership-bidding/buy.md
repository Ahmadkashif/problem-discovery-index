# Revenue-Share Engines Fitted to Co-Brand Contracts

**Niche:** [[niches/credit-card-issuers/co-brand-partnership-bidding/profile|Co-Brand Partnership Bidding & Renewal]]
**Industry:** [[industries/credit-card-issuers|Credit Card Issuers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Partner settlement software handles rebates and revenue shares; co-brand contracts stack miles purchases, bounties, marketing commitments and minimum guarantees that change with each amendment.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #evaluation-metrics #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to price a partner's portfolio — remuneration per mile or point, the spend and revolve the partner's customers will generate, acquisition through the partner's own channels, and the balances that move at contract expiry — closer to its true lifetime value than the rival bidder, and whoever prices it best takes the account.

## The Problem
Every month the issuer pays its partner for miles or points earned, bounties for new accounts, a share of revenue and marketing contributions, and true-ups against minimum guarantees, all under a contract amended several times.

## What Already Exists
Revenue-share, rebate and partner-incentive management software (Vistex, Varicent, SAP and Oracle modules) used in consumer goods and distribution.

## The Customization Gap
The card version needs: rates that differ by card product and transaction type; miles purchased at one price for spend and another for bonuses; bounties that depend on activation and spend thresholds; minimum guarantees with carry-forward rules; amendment history applied by effective date; and reconciliation against the partner's loyalty ledger, which is the partner's system, not the issuer's.

## Target Customer
Partnership finance teams at co-brand issuers.

## Impact If Solved
Monthly settlements that match the contract without a spreadsheet, and disputes with partners resolved from a record both sides trust.
