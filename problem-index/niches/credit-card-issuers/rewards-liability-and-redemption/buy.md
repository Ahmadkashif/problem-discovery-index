# Insurance Loss-Reserving Methods for Points

**Niche:** [[niches/credit-card-issuers/rewards-liability-and-redemption/profile|Rewards Liability & Redemption Cost]]
**Industry:** [[industries/credit-card-issuers|Credit Card Issuers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Insurers have estimated, for a century, what claims incurred today will cost as they develop over years — the same shape as points earned now and redeemed later.
**Tags:** #bayesian-inference #survival-analysis #time-series-forecasting #evaluation-metrics #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to know what each issued point will actually cost — by redemption channel, transfer partner and breakage — and to change earn and burn rules without triggering a redemption run, attrition or a regulator's attention, and whoever forecasts cost per point best takes the account.

## The Problem
Rewards liability is reported quarterly and trued up later, and the estimate is usually a single breakage rate per product.

## What Already Exists
Actuarial reserving software and methods — development triangles, chain-ladder and Bornhuetter-Ferguson estimates, stochastic reserving — used by property-casualty insurers and their auditors.

## The Customization Gap
Points need: development triangles by earn cohort and redemption channel rather than by accident year; cost per point that varies by channel and partner contract; adjustments for promotions and announced changes that shift development; cardholder-level covariates such as revolving status and tenure; and documentation that satisfies the issuer's auditors on the accounting estimate.

## Target Customer
Rewards finance and accounting-policy teams at issuers with proprietary currencies.

## Impact If Solved
A liability estimate with a known error band and a method auditors already recognise.
