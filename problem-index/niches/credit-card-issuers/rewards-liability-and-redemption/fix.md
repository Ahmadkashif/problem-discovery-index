# Points Forfeited at Product Change

**Niche:** [[niches/credit-card-issuers/rewards-liability-and-redemption/profile|Rewards Liability & Redemption Cost]]
**Industry:** [[industries/credit-card-issuers|Credit Card Issuers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A cardholder who downgrades, converts or closes a card can lose a points balance through a system default nobody explained, and the complaint arrives months later.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #compliance #workflow-orchestration #descriptive-statistics #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to know what each issued point will actually cost — by redemption channel, transfer partner and breakage — and to change earn and burn rules without triggering a redemption run, attrition or a regulator's attention, and whoever forecasts cost per point best takes the account.

## The Problem
Forfeiture rules differ by product, transition path and delinquency status, and they are applied by the rewards engine when an account event fires. Agents processing a product change often cannot see what will happen to the balance.

## Why It's Still Broken
The rules were written into systems over years of product launches, and the conduct risk sits with compliance while the configuration sits with technology.

## What a Fix Looks Like
An inventory of every forfeiture rule against the disclosures that promise it, a pre-event check that warns the agent and the cardholder before a balance is lost, and monitoring of forfeited points by event type.

## Who Feels the Pain
Cardholders who lose points, agents handling the complaint, and compliance teams answering the regulator.

## Impact If Fixed
Fewer forfeiture complaints and a programme whose rules match its disclosures.
