# Large-Issuer Credit Risk & Decision Science Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-card-issuers|Credit Card Issuers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The credit risk, decision science and portfolio analytics organisations inside the largest issuers — the group that holds about 84% of US card balances (CFPB). They build acquisition, line, fraud, collections and loss-forecasting models, run thousands of tests a year, and produce the loss forecasts behind the allowance and the stress-test submissions. Their output is used inside the issuer, not sold.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Several hundred to a few thousand analysts and data scientists per top-ten issuer (estimate)
**Output:** Credit and fraud models, strategy tests, loss forecasts, allowance and stress-test inputs
**Proprietary data:** Decades of account-level performance and test-cell history on tens of millions of accounts
**Clock:** Quarter-end allowance and earnings dates, and the annual stress-test submission for the largest bank holding companies
**Buyer:** Chief Risk Officer / Head of Decision Sciences

**Kill switches:** live — data isn't theirs to use freely and compliance gates: the data is cardholder information under GLBA and FCRA, every model passes model risk governance and fair-lending review, and the largest issuers build in house.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. The industry's largest insight functions, monetised as interest and interchange rather than invoiced, behind governance that makes outside tooling a multi-quarter procurement.
