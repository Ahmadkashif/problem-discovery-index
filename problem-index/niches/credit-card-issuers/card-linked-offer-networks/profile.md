# Card-Linked Offer Networks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-card-issuers|Credit Card Issuers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Platforms that place merchant offers inside issuers' apps and online banking and pay cardholders cash back when they buy — Cardlytics is the largest in the US, and some issuers run their own offer programmes. Merchants pay for campaigns measured on actual card purchases; issuers share the revenue and fund part of the rewards from it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~30-100 analysts, data scientists and measurement staff (estimate)
**Output:** Campaign targeting and measurement, purchase-based audience and category insights for merchants
**Proprietary data:** Purchase data across partner issuers' cardholders, licensed under each issuer agreement
**Clock:** Merchants' campaign calendars
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / Head of Measurement
