# Workers' Compensation Bill Review & Utilization Review Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/acupuncture-practices|Acupuncture Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Vendors that reprice and authorize the acupuncture delivered under workers' compensation and auto injury claims — Mitchell, Genex, Sedgwick, CorVel. Nurse reviewers and bill analysts apply state fee schedules and treatment guidelines to every submitted bill and treatment request, and bill the carrier a percentage of the savings achieved.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-1,000 nurse reviewers, bill analysts, and clinical staff
**Output:** Repriced bills, authorization determinations, savings reports
**Proprietary data:** Repriced claim history across states, providers, and treatment patterns, joined to appeal and independent review outcomes
**Clock:** Statutory bill review and utilization review turnaround windows, measured in days
**Buyer:** VP of Clinical Operations / SVP Medical Management
