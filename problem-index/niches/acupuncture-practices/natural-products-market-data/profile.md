# Natural Products Channel Data Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/acupuncture-practices|Acupuncture Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms whose product is data about the herbal and supplement channel that acupuncture dispensaries buy from — SPINS, NielsenIQ's natural channel practice, Nutrition Business Journal. They ingest point-of-sale feeds from natural retailers, normalize every SKU against a proprietary ingredient and attribute taxonomy, and sell category reports, share tracking, and trend forecasts to herb manufacturers, distributors, and retail buyers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 data analysts, taxonomists, and category insight staff
**Output:** Category reports, share and trend analyses, custom insight studies
**Proprietary data:** Retailer POS feeds under exclusive supply agreements plus a hand-built ingredient and claims taxonomy covering hundreds of thousands of SKUs
**Clock:** Retailer category review calendars and brand planning cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Insights / Head of Category Analytics
