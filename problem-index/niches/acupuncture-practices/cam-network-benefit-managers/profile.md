# CAM Benefit Network Managers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/acupuncture-practices|Acupuncture Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Specialty network managers that health plans delegate complementary care to — American Specialty Health, Optum Physical Health, Landmark. They credential acupuncturists, own the fee schedules, adjudicate the claims, and run the clinical policy unit that decides what acupuncture is covered for and how many visits are allowed. Their revenue is capitation and administrative fees; the clinical policy work supports the contract rather than being sold on its own.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 in clinical policy, analytics, and outcomes research
**Output:** Medical policy documents, network adequacy analyses, plan-level utilization reporting
**Proprietary data:** Adjudicated claims across millions of CAM visits joined to credentialing and outcome data — a corpus no academic group or payer holds
**Clock:** Plan year policy refresh, state mandate effective dates, NCQA accreditation cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Medical Officer / VP Clinical Policy
