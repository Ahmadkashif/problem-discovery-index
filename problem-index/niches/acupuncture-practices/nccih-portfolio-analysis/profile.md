# Federal Complementary Health Research Portfolio Staff

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/acupuncture-practices|Acupuncture Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The National Center for Complementary and Integrative Health and its counterparts fund and synthesize the research base that determines whether acupuncture is treated as evidence-backed medicine. Program officers and analysts manage grant portfolios, run portfolio analyses to find evidence gaps, and produce the public evidence summaries that payers and guideline writers cite downstream.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-60 program officers, portfolio analysts, and science writers
**Output:** Portfolio analyses, strategic plans, public evidence summaries
**Proprietary data:** The full grant and outcome record for federally funded complementary health research
**Clock:** Appropriations cycles, council meetings, strategic plan periods
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — a federal center director
