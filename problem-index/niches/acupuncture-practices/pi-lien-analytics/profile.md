# Personal Injury Lien & Medical Funding Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/acupuncture-practices|Acupuncture Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Firms that fund and later collect on treatment provided to personal injury plaintiffs, a substantial share of acupuncture volume in several states. Underwriters assess whether a case will settle for enough to cover the lien, buy the receivable at a discount, and manage recovery. Their analysts research settlement patterns by injury type, jurisdiction, attorney, and carrier.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 underwriters, case analysts, and recovery staff
**Output:** Funding decisions and lien valuations
**Proprietary data:** Settlement outcomes by attorney, venue, injury profile, and carrier — data no public source assembles
**Clock:** Statute of limitations and settlement timelines, but largely case-driven rather than fixed
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer / VP Portfolio Analytics
