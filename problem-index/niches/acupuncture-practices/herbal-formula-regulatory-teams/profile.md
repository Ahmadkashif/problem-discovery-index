# TCM Herbal Formula Manufacturer Regulatory Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/acupuncture-practices|Acupuncture Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The regulatory affairs and technical content groups inside the herbal formula manufacturers that supply acupuncture dispensaries — Evergreen Herbs, KPC, Golden Flower, Blue Poppy. They maintain structure-function claim substantiation, new dietary ingredient filings, monograph documentation, and the clinical content that accompanies each formula.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 3-10 per manufacturer
**Output:** Claim substantiation files, regulatory filings, practitioner-facing formula monographs
**Proprietary data:** Formula composition and sourcing history, adverse event reports, practitioner feedback
**Clock:** FDA cGMP inspections, NDI notification windows, product launch calendars
**Buyer:** VP Regulatory Affairs
