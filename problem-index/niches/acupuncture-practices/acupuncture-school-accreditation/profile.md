# Acupuncture Program Accreditation Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/acupuncture-practices|Acupuncture Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
ACAHM accredits the master's and doctoral programs that produce licensed acupuncturists. Staff and volunteer site-visit teams review self-studies, verify outcomes data — graduation rates, board pass rates, job placement — and issue accreditation decisions that control a school's access to federal financial aid.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-15 core staff plus volunteer reviewers
**Output:** Accreditation decisions, site visit reports, annual outcomes data reports
**Proprietary data:** Longitudinal program outcomes across every accredited institution
**Clock:** Accreditation review cycles and USDE recognition renewal
**Buyer:** Executive Director
