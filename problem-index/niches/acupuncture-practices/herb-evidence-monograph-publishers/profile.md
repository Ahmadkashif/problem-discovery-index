# Herb & Supplement Evidence Monograph Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/acupuncture-practices|Acupuncture Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Publishers that maintain graded evidence monographs on botanicals, Chinese herbal formulas, and complementary therapies — TRC Healthcare's Natural Medicines, the American Botanical Council's HerbalGram and HerbMedPro, Examine.com. Credentialed researchers read every new trial, systematic review, case report, and regulatory action on a given herb and maintain a single monograph carrying an efficacy grade, safety rating, dosing, and a herb-drug interaction matrix. Acupuncturists running herbal dispensaries, pharmacists, payers, and supplement retailers subscribe.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-200 pharmacists, PhD researchers, and medical editors
**Output:** The graded monograph and interaction matrix — the subscription product itself
**Proprietary data:** Two decades of structured evidence extraction and grading decisions across thousands of botanicals, plus the accumulated adverse-event and interaction record
**Clock:** New trial publication, FDA warning letters, recalls, and Chinese pharmacopoeia revisions — subscribers expect the monograph current within weeks
**Buyer:** VP of Content / Editorial Director
