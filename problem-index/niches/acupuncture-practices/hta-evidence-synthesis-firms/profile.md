# Health Technology Assessment Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/acupuncture-practices|Acupuncture Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Firms that produce the systematic reviews payers rely on when deciding whether to cover acupuncture for a given indication — Hayes, ECRI, and the AHRQ-designated Evidence-based Practice Centers. Methodologists and clinical researchers screen the literature against a protocol, extract and grade every eligible study, and issue a rated report. Health plans, hospital systems, and government agencies subscribe or commission reports directly.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 methodologists, clinical researchers, and medical librarians
**Output:** The rated evidence report — the commissioned deliverable
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated structured extractions and rating rationales across thousands of technology assessments, including how prior ratings aged
**Clock:** Client contract deadlines and coverage committee meeting dates
**Buyer:** VP of Research / Director of Evidence Services
