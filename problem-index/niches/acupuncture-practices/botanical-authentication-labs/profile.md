# Botanical Authentication & GMP Testing Labs

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/acupuncture-practices|Acupuncture Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Analytical labs that verify the identity, potency, and contamination status of raw herbs and finished formulas before they can be sold — Alkemist Labs, Eurofins Botanicals, ChromaDex. Chemists run HPTLC, HPLC, DNA barcoding, and heavy metal panels against reference material and issue a certificate of analysis. Herbal formula manufacturers supplying acupuncture dispensaries cannot release product without one.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 analytical chemists and lab technicians
**Output:** Certificates of analysis and adulteration findings
**Proprietary data:** A reference spectral and genetic library of authenticated botanical material built specimen by specimen over decades — genuinely irreplaceable and largely unexploited
**Clock:** Product release testing gates every shipment; FDA cGMP inspection cycles
**Buyer:** Laboratory Director / VP Quality
