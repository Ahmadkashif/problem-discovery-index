# Conservative-Care Treatment Guideline Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/acupuncture-practices|Acupuncture Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Publishers of the evidence-based treatment guidelines that decide whether an acupuncture course gets authorized — MCG Health, Official Disability Guidelines, InterQual, and the medical policy units at Carelon and EviCore. Physicians, nurses, and literature analysts maintain per-condition care pathways specifying which conservative treatments are indicated, for how many visits, and with what documentation. Health plans, workers' compensation systems, and independent review organizations license the guideline and apply it to every authorization request.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-400 physicians, nurse reviewers, and medical writers
**Output:** The licensed guideline edition and its supporting evidence dossier
**Proprietary data:** The full chain from literature grading to guideline language to authorization outcomes and appeal reversals — no academic body holds the downstream half
**Clock:** Annual edition release, state workers' compensation adoption deadlines, and utilization review turnaround statutes
**Buyer:** Chief Medical Officer / VP of Clinical Content
