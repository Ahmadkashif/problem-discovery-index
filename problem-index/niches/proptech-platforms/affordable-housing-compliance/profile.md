**Market Size:** ~$680M US software spend across affordable housing owners, agents and compliance service providers
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~6% of proptech revenue

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Affordable housing operates under a compliance regime that has no counterpart in market-rate property management. A Low-Income Housing Tax Credit property must certify every household's income at move-in and recertify on a schedule, using income calculation rules that are specific, unforgiving and frequently counterintuitive — what counts as income, how to annualise irregular earnings, how to treat assets, how to handle a household member's part-time job with variable hours. Getting it wrong risks credit recapture, which is a financial event of a scale that makes the compliance function existential rather than administrative. Properties layered with multiple programmes — tax credits plus project-based rental assistance plus a state or local programme — must satisfy several sets of rules at once, and where they conflict somebody has to decide. The work is performed by compliance specialists who are scarce, expensive and ageing out of the workforce, and it is performed with paper files and checklists.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Yardi, RealPage and MRI offer affordable housing modules that handle the ledger, rent calculation and required reporting formats; specialist compliance software and outsourced compliance services fill the rest. The tooling produces the forms and the reports. What it does not do is the work: income calculation from source documents is manual, file assembly for a compliance review is manual, and the determination of whether a file is complete and correct before an auditor sees it is a specialist reading it. First-pass audit findings are common and each one means a file reconstructed under deadline. Nothing extracts income from the pay stubs, benefit letters and self-employment records that households provide, nothing applies the calculation rules mechanically with the reasoning shown, and nothing pre-audits a file against the criteria a reviewer will apply.
