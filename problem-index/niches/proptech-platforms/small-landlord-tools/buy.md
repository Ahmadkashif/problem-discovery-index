**Contested on:** Every serious competitor selling to small landlords is fighting to get a person with four units from an empty unit to a compliant lease, a working ledger and a filed tax return without hiring anyone — and whoever makes that path unassisted takes the segment.

## The Problem
A landlord with four units has rent coming into a bank account, mortgage payments going out, repairs on a credit card, a new water heater that is a capital improvement rather than an expense, mileage driven to the properties, and insurance and tax bills. In March they assemble a rental schedule from statements and memory. They miss deductions, misclassify capital improvements as repairs, and cannot substantiate what they claim. The accounting is not hard; it is simply never done until it must be.

## What Already Exists
Bank and card feed aggregation is a commodity service. Transaction categorisation with machine learning is standard in every small business accounting product. Receipt capture and extraction from a photograph works well. Mileage tracking is a solved consumer app category. Tax form generation is available through established providers. Every component is purchasable and cheap.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to the rental schedule's specific structure, which general bookkeeping does not model. It requires: (1) categorisation into the rental tax schedule's own categories with per-property allocation, since a landlord with four properties needs four schedules and general products give them one business; (2) the repair-versus-capital-improvement distinction applied at categorisation time with the reasoning shown, because it is the most consequential and most commonly wrong judgement in rental accounting and it determines depreciation for decades; (3) depreciation schedules maintained automatically per property and per improvement, which is where landlords most often lose money and which no consumer product tracks; (4) mixed personal and rental accounts handled gracefully, since most small landlords do not have clean separation and a product that demands it will be abandoned; and (5) year-round categorisation with monthly prompts rather than an annual reckoning, because the substantiation problem is entirely a timing problem.

## Target Customer
Independent landlords, the landlord platform vendors already holding rent payment data, and the tax preparation providers serving this population.

## Impact If Solved
Continuous categorisation converts a March reconstruction into a filing, and the repair-versus-improvement and depreciation handling recover real money that small landlords routinely leave on the table. Every component is bought; the value is entirely in the rental-specific structure the general products do not model.
