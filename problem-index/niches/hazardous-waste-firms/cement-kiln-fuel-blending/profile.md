# Fuel Blending for Cement Kilns

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hazardous-waste-firms|Hazardous Waste Firms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn generators' high-heat-value wastes into fuel that meets a cement kiln's permit and contract specification, and whoever delivers the largest share of blended loads inside the kiln's specification takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1B in US hazardous waste fuel-blending and kiln-fuel revenue (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — blend plans are built in spreadsheets from profile data and fingerprint results; kilns report back by phone and certificate
**Target Buyer:** Fuel-blending plant manager, and the alternative-fuels manager at the cement kiln that buys the fuel
**Automation Potential:** High for blend planning against specification limits; sampling and acceptance stay manual

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Fuel blenders take spent solvents, paint wastes and other high-heat-value streams from many generators, bulk and blend them to a fuel specification, and ship the fuel to the small number of cement kilns permitted to burn hazardous waste-derived fuel (~a dozen, estimate), which pay less than an incinerator charges or even pay for good fuel. Systech Environmental and Giant Resource Recovery are kiln-linked blenders, and the consolidators run blending plants of their own (estimate). The kiln, not the generator, is the decisive customer: it accepts fuel only inside its permit limits and contract specification for heat value, chlorine, metals, water and solids. The contest is spec conformance across a variable intake, which is a different capability from guaranteeing burn capacity.

## Current Tools & Gaps
TSDF ERPs and LIMS hold intake profiles and fingerprint results; blend plans are spreadsheets; kilns send acceptance certificates and complaints by email. Gaps: no model predicts the blended fuel's parameters from intake records before tanks are filled; off-spec loads are found at the kiln; and intake is not ranked by its value to the blend.
