# Refinery Blend Optimisation for Waste-Derived Fuel

**Niche:** [[niches/hazardous-waste-firms/cement-kiln-fuel-blending/profile|Fuel Blending for Cement Kilns]]
**Industry:** [[industries/hazardous-waste-firms|Hazardous Waste Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Refineries blend gasoline and diesel to specification with mature optimisation software; hazardous waste fuel blenders do the same job on spreadsheets with far noisier inputs.
**Tags:** #optimization-fundamentals #bayesian-inference #data-integration #evaluation-metrics #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn generators' high-heat-value wastes into fuel that meets a cement kiln's permit and contract specification, and whoever delivers the largest share of blended loads inside the kiln's specification takes the account.

## The Problem
Blending to specification is a solved optimisation problem when the components are well characterised. Waste-derived fuel components are characterised by a profile and a fingerprint, change load to load, and arrive whether or not the tank needs them.

## What Already Exists
Refinery product-blending optimisers (AspenTech's and Honeywell's blending applications) compute recipes that meet product specifications at lowest cost from component qualities and inventories.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: component qualities as uncertain estimates updated by each fingerprint; intake that cannot be refused once accepted under a profile; storage-time limits under the permit; kiln-specific specifications and acceptance history; and a compliance record of which generators' waste went into which fuel batch, because the batch is still hazardous waste until the kiln burns it.

## Target Customer
Operations managers at fuel-blending plants with more than one kiln customer.

## Impact If Solved
A 1–2% improvement in the share of on-spec batches (estimate) keeps a blender inside its kiln contracts and is worth ~$0.3–0.6M a year at a mid-sized plant (estimate).
