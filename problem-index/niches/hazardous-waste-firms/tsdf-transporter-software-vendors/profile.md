# TSDF & Transporter Software Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hazardous-waste-firms|Hazardous Waste Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors of the ERPs that run hazardous waste firms' profiles, orders, receiving, manifests and billing: Terralink (TDX and the TDXConnect generator portal), WASTELINQ, Wastebits and EnviroWare (search summary). TSDFs, transporters and brokers pay licence and subscription fees; the consolidators largely build their own.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analysts per vendor (estimate); staff are developers, implementers and support
**Output:** Software; customers' own reports
**Proprietary data:** Customers' profiles, orders and manifests held in the vendors' systems
**Clock:** Customers' regulatory changes (e-Manifest rules) and release cycles
**Buyer:** Founder or product head
