# SKU & Product Hazard Classification Data Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hazardous-waste-firms|Hazardous Waste Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Vendors that keep a regulatory classification for every product a retailer, distributor or manufacturer handles, built from its safety data sheet: transport class and shipping description, storage rules and, for retailers, whether the item is hazardous or universal waste when damaged, returned or expired, and in which states. 3E and UL's WERCS are the large players. Retailers and their hazardous waste contractors pay subscriptions per SKU or per programme, and the classification drives store disposal programmes and the contractor's profiles. The same firms' regulatory-change products are profiled as [[niches/testing-laboratories/product-compliance-regulatory-intelligence/profile|🔍 Product Compliance Regulatory Intelligence]] (51, indexed); this pocket is the SKU classification line, not the regulatory-change line.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–50 regulatory-rule analysts per vendor who maintain the classification logic by jurisdiction (estimate); the classifiers who process each SKU are production and not counted
**Output:** SKU-level transport, storage and waste classifications; state overlays; programme reports for retailers
**Proprietary data:** Large safety data sheet libraries and years of SKU classifications with the reasoning behind them, plus the record of how ambiguous products were classified
**Clock:** State and federal rule changes (client clocks, Q4 at most 4) and retailers' enforcement exposure, which carries no fixed deadline; per-SKU turnaround binds production, not analysis
**Buyer:** Head of regulatory content or chief product officer at the vendor

**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. The classification is the invoice, but the analysts whose work grows with volume are SKU classifiers (production), the rule team is small, no third party sets a hard deadline, and two or three vendors make a thin market. Scored lower than the indexed regulatory-change pocket on Q2, Q4 and Q5 for those reasons.
