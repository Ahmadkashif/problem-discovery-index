# Recertification by Copy and Paste

**Niche:** [[niches/hazardous-waste-firms/waste-profiling-classification/profile|Waste Profiling & Waste-Code Classification]]
**Industry:** [[industries/hazardous-waste-firms|Hazardous Waste Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Every year the generator re-signs last year's profile unchanged, including for streams whose process changed months ago.
**Tags:** #change-point-detection #large-language-models #compliance #workflow-orchestration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a generator's safety data sheets, process notes and lab results into an approvable waste profile, and whoever gets the highest share of profiles approved on first submission takes the account.

## The Problem
Annual recertification asks the generator to confirm the profile is still accurate. It almost always says yes. Process changes, new raw materials and different suppliers surface later as receiving discrepancies.

## Why It's Still Broken
The recertification form shows the old profile, not the evidence that something changed, and the TSDF holds that evidence in its receiving results.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Send each recertification with the year's receiving history attached: fingerprint drift, discrepancies and volume changes under the profile, with specific questions where the record suggests change. Require a reason when evidence of drift is waved through.

## Who Feels the Pain
Approval chemists, receiving staff and generators' EHS coordinators who sign without the facts.

## Impact If Fixed
Catching drift at recertification on ~5% of profiles (estimate) prevents the discrepancies those streams would have produced, ~2–3 held loads per drifted profile a year (estimate).
