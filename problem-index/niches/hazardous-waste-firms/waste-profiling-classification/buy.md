# Document AI Adapted to Waste Profiles

**Niche:** [[niches/hazardous-waste-firms/waste-profiling-classification/profile|Waste Profiling & Waste-Code Classification]]
**Industry:** [[industries/hazardous-waste-firms|Hazardous Waste Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Insurers and banks already extract fields from millions of forms with document AI; TSDFs still key safety data sheets and lab reports into profiles by hand.
**Tags:** #transformers #large-language-models #data-integration #evaluation-metrics #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a generator's safety data sheets, process notes and lab results into an approvable waste profile, and whoever gets the highest share of profiles approved on first submission takes the account.

## The Problem
Approval chemists read attachments (safety data sheets, analytical reports, old profiles) and copy values into the TSDF's system to check them against permit limits.

## What Already Exists
Document AI services (Google Document AI, Azure AI Document Intelligence, ABBYY) extract fields and tables from forms, invoices and claims with confidence scores.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs extractors for safety data sheet sections and analytical report tables across formats and languages; unit and range normalisation; mapping of extracted constituents to the TSDF's acceptance parameters; a check that claimed waste codes follow from the extracted data; and a reviewer screen that shows every value next to its source.

## Target Customer
TSDF technical-services teams and the ERP vendors that serve them.

## Impact If Solved
Cutting chemist keying from ~30 minutes to ~10 per profile (estimate) on ~10,000 profiles a year saves ~3,300 chemist hours at a large network.
