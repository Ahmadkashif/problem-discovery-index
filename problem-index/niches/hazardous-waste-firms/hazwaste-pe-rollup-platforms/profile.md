# Private-Equity Hazardous Waste Service Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hazardous-waste-firms|Hazardous Waste Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Investor-owned platforms buying regional collectors, lab-pack operators, field-service firms and small TSDFs, such as Heritage-Crystal Clean, taken private by J.F. Lehman for ~$1.2B in 2023 (search summary), alongside other sponsor-backed environmental services groups (estimate). Their corporate-development teams find, price and integrate targets, and exit by selling to consolidators such as Veolia, whose $3B Clean Earth purchase set the recent benchmark.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~2–8 corporate-development and integration staff per platform (estimate)
**Output:** Acquisition models, integration plans, board reporting
**Proprietary data:** Targets' customer lists, pricing and permits obtained in diligence under NDA
**Clock:** Deal timetables and fund cycles set by the sponsors
**Buyer:** Platform CEO or the sponsor's operating partner
