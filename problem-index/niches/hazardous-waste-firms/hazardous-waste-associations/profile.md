# Hazardous Waste & Hazmat Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hazardous-waste-firms|Hazardous Waste Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Environmental Technology Council represents commercial TSDFs in Washington; NAHMMA represents household hazardous waste programme managers; the Institute of Hazardous Materials Management awards the CHMM credential; the Dangerous Goods Advisory Council works on transport rules (estimate). Members pay dues; the work is advocacy, training, conferences and credentials.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research or policy staff each (estimate)
**Output:** Comment letters, policy papers, training, credential exams
**Proprietary data:** Member surveys and directories
**Clock:** Rulemaking comment periods and conference calendars
**Buyer:** Executive director
