# Quotes Built on a Walk-Through Guess

**Niche:** [[niches/hazardous-waste-firms/lab-pack-hhw-collection/profile|Lab Pack & Household Hazardous Waste Collection]]
**Industry:** [[industries/hazardous-waste-firms|Hazardous Waste Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A lab-pack job is quoted from a half-hour walk through the stockroom, and the invoice that follows is often far from the quote.
**Tags:** #linear-regression #gradient-boosting #descriptive-statistics #revenue-impact #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to clear a laboratory's or a collection event's chemical inventory into compliant shipments, and whoever ships each inventory in the fewest drums takes the account.

## The Problem
The sales rep or chemist estimates drums and labour by eye. Unknowns, reactive items and a hidden second cabinet push the job over budget, the customer disputes the invoice, and the operator writes off the difference or loses the account.

## Why It's Still Broken
Past jobs are not stored in a form that links what was seen on the walk-through to what was packed, so estimators cannot learn from their own history.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Store each job's walk-through notes and photos with its final drum count, labour and unknowns; fit a simple estimator from shelf counts, lab type and photos; and quote a range with the drivers stated.

## Who Feels the Pain
Lab-pack sales reps and field chemists; university and school EHS managers with fixed budgets.

## Impact If Fixed
Narrowing quote error from ~30% to ~10% (estimate) removes most invoice disputes and the write-offs that follow, worth ~2–3% of lab-pack revenue (estimate).
