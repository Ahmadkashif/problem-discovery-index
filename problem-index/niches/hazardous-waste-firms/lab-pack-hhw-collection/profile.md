# Lab Pack & Household Hazardous Waste Collection

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hazardous-waste-firms|Hazardous Waste Firms]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to clear a laboratory's or a collection event's chemical inventory into compliant shipments, and whoever ships each inventory in the fewest drums takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$700M in US lab-pack, chemical clean-out and household hazardous waste programme revenue (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~6% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — inventories are handwritten or photographed and retyped; quotes are built from a walk-through
**Target Buyer:** University or hospital EHS director; county or city household hazardous waste programme manager
**Automation Potential:** Medium — label capture, classification drafts and drum planning automate; handling and packing stay manual

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Lab packing turns thousands of small, mixed, often poorly labelled containers into compliant drums. Schools, universities, hospitals, research labs and retailers' returns centres buy clean-outs; counties buy household hazardous waste (HHW) collection days and permanent depots. Field chemists do the work on site, under 10–14 hour days in some job ads (search summary). Clean Harbors brands it CleanPack, and Veolia, Republic's environmental solutions unit, Triumvirate and regional firms compete. Disposal is priced by the drum, and drum count depends on how the chemist groups containers under DOT and waste-code rules, so efficient packing is the customer's cost and the operator's margin.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Paper inventory sheets and phone photos; the operator's ERP for orders and manifests; safety data sheet lookups by hand. Gaps: no drum-planning tool that proposes groupings under the operator's own segregation rules; quotes built on a walk-through guess; HHW event data (participants, volumes by category) kept in spreadsheets for the county's annual report.
