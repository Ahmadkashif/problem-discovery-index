# Consolidator Waste Acceptance, Pricing & Capacity Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hazardous-waste-firms|Hazardous Waste Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Central technical-services, pricing and network-planning teams at the consolidators that own most commercial incinerators and hazardous landfills: Clean Harbors (ten incinerators, $6.03B of 2025 revenue, 9% of full-time staff at a Global Capabilities Center in India), Veolia after Clean Earth, Republic's environmental solutions unit (24 TSDFs, six hazardous landfills) and WM. They set disposal pricing, allocate incinerator capacity, route streams across facilities and run approval standards. Nobody outside the company pays for their output.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–100 pricing, network-planning and technical-standards analysts per consolidator (estimate); approval chemists, receiving staff and profile processors are production and not counted
**Output:** Internal pricing guidance, capacity plans, routing rules, acceptance standards
**Proprietary data:** Every profile, fingerprint result, discrepancy and treatment outcome across the network — the deepest record in the industry
**Clock:** Internal budget and capacity cycles; per-load and per-manifest deadlines bind production work, not this analysis
**Buyer:** SVP of disposal operations or chief commercial officer at the consolidator
