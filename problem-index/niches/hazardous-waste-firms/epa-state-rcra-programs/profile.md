# EPA RCRA, e-Manifest & State Hazardous Waste Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hazardous-waste-firms|Hazardous Waste Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
EPA's Office of Resource Conservation and Recovery writes the RCRA rules, runs RCRAInfo and the e-Manifest system (15 fee-funded staff, ~$20M of fees expected in FY2027), and publishes Biennial Report and manifest data. Authorised states, such as California's Department of Toxic Substances Control (state environmental agencies are not built as an industry), permit and inspect TSDFs, generators and transporters and bring most enforcement. Taxpayers and fee payers fund them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Hundreds of permit writers, inspectors and data staff across EPA and the larger states (estimate); permit writers and inspectors work case by case and are production; data and programme analysts number in the tens per agency (estimate)
**Output:** Rules, permits, inspection and enforcement decisions, national data releases, e-Manifest fee schedules
**Proprietary data:** RCRAInfo, e-Manifest and Biennial Report records, which are published; inspection files
**Clock:** The agency's own cycles (Biennial Report collection in even years, fee schedules revised every two years (search summary)), which it sets and administers itself, so they score as self-set
**Buyer:** Office director or state programme manager
