# Hazardous Waste Flow & Manifest Data Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hazardous-waste-firms|Hazardous Waste Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
The position for firms that turn public e-Manifest and RCRAInfo data into products: who generates which waste, where it goes, which facilities are gaining or losing volume, and which generators' status has changed. EPA has received ~13.5 million manifests from over 575,000 sites since 2018, and manifest data is public 90 days after receipt (search summary). Today the data is used mainly by environmental records vendors for site histories and by consolidators' sales teams for prospecting; no firm sells flow analytics at scale.
