# Generator TSDF Audit Consortia & Vendor Audit Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hazardous-waste-firms|Hazardous Waste Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Because a generator can share Superfund liability for any facility its waste reaches, large generators audit the TSDFs they use before approving them. Member consortia such as CHWMEG pool those audits so each facility is audited once and the report is shared among members, and environmental consultancies run custom vendor audits for individual generators (estimate). Generators pay membership dues or audit fees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 consortium staff (estimate); audits are performed by contracted auditors per facility, who are production
**Output:** TSDF audit reports, facility summaries, member databases
**Proprietary data:** Years of facility audit reports, findings and corrective actions shared among members
**Clock:** Members' vendor-approval cycles and annual audit schedules (client clocks)
**Buyer:** Executive director of the consortium
