# Three Vendors, Three Portals, No Single Stream List

**Niche:** [[niches/hazardous-waste-firms/industrial-generator-programs/profile|Industrial Generator Waste Programs]]
**Industry:** [[industries/hazardous-waste-firms|Hazardous Waste Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A generator using two or three waste vendors downloads three reports in three formats every quarter and still cannot produce one list of its streams.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #descriptive-statistics #compliance #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to take a large generator's long-term disposal liability off its books, and whoever diverts the largest share of the generator's tons away from land disposal takes the account.

## The Problem
Every vendor portal names streams differently, reports in different units and uses its own profile numbers. The EHS analyst merges them in a spreadsheet each quarter, and the merged list never quite matches e-Manifest.

## Why It's Still Broken
Vendors have no reason to standardise, and the generator's EHS platform imports only what someone keys in.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Use the generator's own e-Manifest records, which every vendor's shipments now reach, as the spine; map each vendor's report onto it by manifest number; and keep one stream list with stable identifiers across vendors.

## Who Feels the Pain
EHS analysts at multi-site generators; corporate sustainability teams reporting landfill diversion; auditors.

## Impact If Fixed
Cutting a ~40-hour quarterly merge to a few hours (estimate) saves ~150 analyst hours a year per generator and gives the diversion figure a source an auditor can follow.
