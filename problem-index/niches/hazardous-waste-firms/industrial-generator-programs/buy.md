# Procurement Spend Analytics Rebuilt Around Waste Streams

**Niche:** [[niches/hazardous-waste-firms/industrial-generator-programs/profile|Industrial Generator Waste Programs]]
**Industry:** [[industries/hazardous-waste-firms|Hazardous Waste Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Spend analytics already classify every invoice a company pays; for hazardous waste they see a vendor and a dollar figure, never the stream, the method or the outlet.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #descriptive-statistics #data-integration #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to take a large generator's long-term disposal liability off its books, and whoever diverts the largest share of the generator's tons away from land disposal takes the account.

## The Problem
Procurement runs the waste RFP on price per drum and per ton because that is what the spend system can see. The EHS team cares about method, outlet and audit status. The two never meet in one record.

## What Already Exists
Procurement and spend-analytics suites (Coupa, SAP Ariba, Sievo) classify invoice lines, benchmark prices and track contract compliance.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs invoice lines linked to profile numbers and manifests; a taxonomy of streams, management methods and outlets rather than commodity codes; vendor-audit and permit status per outlet; landfill-avoidance and CERCLA exposure as evaluation criteria next to price; and RFP scoring that rewards diversion.

## Target Customer
Indirect-procurement leads and EHS directors at large generators running national waste RFPs.

## Impact If Solved
Joining spend to streams lets an RFP trade a ~5–10% price premium (estimate) for a measurable diversion gain instead of awarding on unit price alone, and cuts RFP preparation from ~3 months to ~1 (estimate).
