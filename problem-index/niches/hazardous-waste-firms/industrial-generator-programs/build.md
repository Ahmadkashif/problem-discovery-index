# A Liability-Weighted Map of Every Stream's Outlet

**Niche:** [[niches/hazardous-waste-firms/industrial-generator-programs/profile|Industrial Generator Waste Programs]]
**Industry:** [[industries/hazardous-waste-firms|Hazardous Waste Firms]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A generator with landfill-avoidance goals cannot see, stream by stream, which outlets its waste reaches, what each method leaves behind in liability, and what moving each stream would cost.
**Tags:** #optimization-fundamentals #descriptive-statistics #large-language-models #data-integration #compliance #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to take a large generator's long-term disposal liability off its books, and whoever diverts the largest share of the generator's tons away from land disposal takes the account.

## The Problem
A multi-site generator may run 300–1,000 approved streams (estimate) across several vendors and dozens of outlets. Its board has a landfill-avoidance target; its EHS director cannot say which streams still go to land disposal, why, and what the alternatives would cost.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Each vendor sees only its own share of the generator's waste, and vendors are paid by the ton, so none has an incentive to show the full picture. Method codes and outlets sit on manifests and Biennial Report entries that nobody has joined to cost.

## What to Build
A stream map built from the generator's manifests in e-Manifest, its Biennial Report filings and vendor invoices: every stream, outlet, management method, cost per ton and vendor-audit status. For each landfilled stream it lists permitted destruction and recycling outlets, estimated cost and freight, and the liability profile of each method, and it tracks the target over time.

## Target Customer
Corporate EHS and sustainability directors at generators with 10+ large-generator sites, and the national account teams of vendors that want to win on diversion.

## Impact If Built
Moving ~10% of a 50,000-ton generator's landfilled tons to destruction or recycling (estimate) advances its target by ~5 points a year, and the vendor that proposes the plan holds a contract worth ~$20–40M a year (estimate).
