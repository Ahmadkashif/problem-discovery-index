# RCRA Closure Financial Assurance Sureties & Insurers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hazardous-waste-firms|Hazardous Waste Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Every permitted TSDF must show it can pay for closure and, for land disposal units, post-closure care, which Clean Harbors says typically runs 30 years, and must carry liability coverage for accidental releases (estimate). Operators meet this with surety bonds, insurance policies, letters of credit or a financial test. Commercial sureties and a few environmental insurers write the bonds and closure policies; they price the operator's credit and the closure cost estimate in its permit file.

## Insight Function
**Size:** A handful of underwriters per carrier for this line (estimate), inside larger commercial surety and environmental teams
**Output:** Bonds, closure and post-closure policies, liability policies
**Proprietary data:** Operators' financial statements and closure cost estimates; claims history when an operator fails
**Clock:** Annual cost-estimate inflation adjustments and permit renewals — clients' regulatory dates
**Buyer:** Head of commercial surety or environmental underwriting
