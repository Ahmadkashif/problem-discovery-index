# CERCLA Allocation & Waste-In Consultants

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hazardous-waste-firms|Hazardous Waste Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
When a former disposal site becomes a Superfund site, the generators that sent waste there share cleanup costs. Allocation consultants and site-group managers reconstruct each party's waste-in volume and toxicity from old manifests, invoices and facility records, run allocation models and support negotiations and litigation. PFAS, with PFOA and PFOS designated under CERCLA since 2024 (search summary), adds new sites. Potentially responsible party groups pay, usually through their counsel.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Firms and practices of ~5–30 people working site by site (estimate); allocation analysts work per engagement and are production
**Output:** Waste-in databases, allocation reports, expert testimony
**Proprietary data:** Engagement files of historical manifests and party records, compiled for clients
**Clock:** Agency orders, consent decree and court schedules
**Buyer:** Managing principal
