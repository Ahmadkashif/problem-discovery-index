# Field Service Software Rebuilt for Accumulation Clocks

**Niche:** [[niches/hazardous-waste-firms/small-quantity-generators/profile|Small & Very Small Quantity Generators]]
**Industry:** [[industries/hazardous-waste-firms|Hazardous Waste Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Field service platforms schedule recurring visits for HVAC and pest control companies; a small hazardous waste collector needs the same scheduling driven by each customer's regulatory clock.
**Tags:** #optimization-fundamentals #time-series-forecasting #data-integration #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a small generator compliant on a scheduled pickup it never has to think about, and whoever keeps the most customer sites free of accumulation-limit citations takes the account.

## The Problem
Regional collectors with a few dozen trucks run recurring stops in generic field-service software or spreadsheets. Visits are scheduled by habit and route density, and paperwork is filled in on paper.

## What Already Exists
Field service management platforms (ServiceTitan, Jobber, FieldEdge, Salesforce Field Service) handle recurring visits, routing, mobile forms and invoicing.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: each customer's generator status and accumulation limits as scheduling constraints; manifest creation and e-Manifest submission from the driver's tablet; hazmat shipping descriptions and placarding by load; consolidation rules for very small generators where states allow it; and a customer compliance record that updates with each stop.

## Target Customer
Owners and operations managers of regional hazardous waste collectors with 5–50 trucks (estimate).

## Impact If Solved
Moving manifests onto the tablet saves ~10 minutes a stop (estimate), ~2,500 hours a year for a 20-truck collector making ~15,000 stops, and cuts the per-manifest fee from $25 to $5 for image uploads replaced by electronic manifests.
