# Small & Very Small Quantity Generators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hazardous-waste-firms|Hazardous Waste Firms]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a small generator compliant on a scheduled pickup it never has to think about, and whoever keeps the most customer sites free of accumulation-limit citations takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.5B in US scheduled collection for small and very small quantity generators (auto and fleet shops, dental and medical offices, print shops, dry cleaners, small labs) (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — the owner keeps the manifests in a drawer; the route driver keeps the schedule
**Target Buyer:** Owner or office manager of a small generator; branch or route-service manager at the collector
**Automation Potential:** Medium — status tracking, reminders and route frequency automate; the pickup is physical

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Most of the over 575,000 sites that have shipped under e-Manifest since 2018 (EPA) are likely small generators (estimate). A small quantity generator (100–1,000 kg a month) has accumulation-time and quantity limits, contingency and training duties, and re-notified its status by 1 September 2025 (search summary); a very small quantity generator needs no EPA ID number at all (search summary). They have no EHS staff and no technical-services rep. They are served by branch route businesses: Safety-Kleen inside Clean Harbors, Heritage-Crystal Clean (taken private by J.F. Lehman in 2023 (search summary)) and regional collectors running milk runs with parts washers, drums and used oil. The owner wants never to think about it; the risk is a pickup that comes after an accumulation limit has passed.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Route software and the collector's ERP; generic field-service apps at smaller collectors; paper manifests in the customer's file. Gaps: nobody computes the customer's monthly status or accumulation clock; route frequency is set by route density and revenue, not by the customer's limit; and re-notification and training reminders are left to the owner.
