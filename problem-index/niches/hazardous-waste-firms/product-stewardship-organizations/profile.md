# Paint & Battery Stewardship Organisations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hazardous-waste-firms|Hazardous Waste Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Producer-funded nonprofits that run state stewardship programmes for leftover paint, batteries and similar household hazardous products: PaintCare in about a dozen states plus DC and Call2Recycle for batteries (estimate). They collect fees from producers through product prices, fund drop-off sites and household hazardous waste events, and pay hazardous waste firms to transport, recycle and dispose of what is collected. For lab-pack and HHW contractors they are a payer alongside counties.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 programme, data and reporting staff per organisation (estimate)
**Output:** Programme plans, annual reports to state agencies, collection-site networks, contractor reimbursement rules
**Proprietary data:** Producers' sales reports by product and state, collection volumes by site and contractor invoices
**Clock:** Statutory programme-plan and annual-report dates set by state law — regulatory compliance dates, scored as at most Q4 4
**Buyer:** Executive director or programme director
