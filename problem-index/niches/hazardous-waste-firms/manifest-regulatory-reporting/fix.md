# Paper Manifests Still Uploaded as Pictures

**Niche:** [[niches/hazardous-waste-firms/manifest-regulatory-reporting/profile|e-Manifest & Generator Regulatory Reporting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/hazardous-waste-firms|Hazardous Waste Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Receiving facilities pay $25 to upload a scanned paper manifest that would cost $5 as an electronic one, and the image carries no usable data.
**Tags:** #transformers #large-language-models #data-integration #compliance #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce a generator's regulatory filings straight from its manifest record, and whoever files Biennial and exception reports that match e-Manifest with the fewest state corrections takes the account.

## The Problem
Many generators and transporters still sign paper. The receiving facility must submit the manifest to e-Manifest and pays by type: $5 electronic or hybrid, $7 for data plus image, $25 for a scanned image (EPA, from October 2025). Images are cheapest in staff time and most expensive in fees, and leave the national record without data.

## Why It's Still Broken
Converting a signed paper manifest to data means keying it, so facilities upload the picture. Generators lack accounts or habits for electronic signing, though the third rule now requires small and large generators to register.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Read each scanned manifest into data automatically, have a clerk confirm the few low-confidence fields, and submit as data plus image at $7 instead of $25; then use the generator registrations to move repeat shippers to electronic signing at $5.

## Who Feels the Pain
Receiving facilities paying fees; clerks keying data; generators and states using incomplete records.

## Impact If Fixed
Moving 100,000 image uploads a year at a large network (estimate) to data plus image saves $1.8M in fees, and moving them to electronic saves $2M.
