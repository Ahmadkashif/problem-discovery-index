# EHS Platform Reporting Modules Wired to e-Manifest

**Niche:** [[niches/hazardous-waste-firms/manifest-regulatory-reporting/profile|e-Manifest & Generator Regulatory Reporting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/hazardous-waste-firms|Hazardous Waste Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Large generators already pay for EHS platforms with waste modules; the modules hold whatever was keyed in, not the federal record of what actually shipped.
**Tags:** #data-integration #descriptive-statistics #large-language-models #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce a generator's regulatory filings straight from its manifest record, and whoever files Biennial and exception reports that match e-Manifest with the fewest state corrections takes the account.

## The Problem
EHS teams maintain waste data in Enablon, Cority, Sphera or VelocityEHS for internal metrics and filings. The data is entered from vendor reports and drifts from e-Manifest.

## What Already Exists
Enterprise EHS platforms with waste-tracking, regulatory calendar and report-template modules.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs a scheduled pull from the e-Manifest system for each registered site; matching to internal stream identifiers; monthly generator-status calculation per site; exception-report alerts when final signed copies do not arrive in time; Biennial Report code fields; and state formats and state-only codes.

## Target Customer
EHS platform administrators at generators already licensing a waste module, and the platform vendors' product teams.

## Impact If Solved
Removing re-keying of ~5,000 manifest lines a year at a large generator (estimate) saves ~400 hours and gives the platform's waste metrics a federal source of truth.
