# e-Manifest & Generator Regulatory Reporting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hazardous-waste-firms|Hazardous Waste Firms]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce a generator's regulatory filings straight from its manifest record, and whoever files Biennial and exception reports that match e-Manifest with the fewest state corrections takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$200M in manifest processing, e-Manifest fees, reporting software and consultant time (estimate; EPA expects ~$20M of e-Manifest fees in FY2027)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium-Low — e-Manifest is national, but hybrid and paper manifests persist and generators rebuild reports from spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** EHS reporting manager at a multi-site generator; compliance manager at a TSDF or transporter
**Automation Potential:** High — the filings are structured and the source records are in one federal system

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
EPA's e-Manifest system has received ~13.5 million manifests from over 575,000 sites since 2018 and tracks ~2 million shipments a year. The third e-Manifest rule moved discrepancy, exception and unmanifested-waste reports into it, required small and large generators to register, and brought export manifests in from 1 December 2025. Fees reward electronic manifests ($5) over scanned paper ($25), and EPA plans rules to sunset paper. Large generators' Biennial Reports (due 1 March of even years; next 2028) and small generators' re-notifications (2025, then 2029) describe the same shipments again (search summary). The record exists; the reports are still assembled by hand. Competitors are EHS platforms (Enablon, Cority, Sphera, VelocityEHS), TSDF ERPs with e-Manifest interfaces and environmental consultants (see [[niches/environmental-consultants/permit-compliance-tracking/profile|⚡ Permit Compliance Tracking]]).

## Current Tools & Gaps
RCRAInfo and myRCRAid for filings; EHS platforms storing waste data; vendor portals. Gaps: Biennial Reports not compiled from e-Manifest; no reconciliation before filing; paper manifests still uploaded as images.
