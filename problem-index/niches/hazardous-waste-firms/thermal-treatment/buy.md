## The Problem
An incinerator's feed plan must keep heat input, chlorine, metals and ash feed rates inside the operating limits written into its permit while burning what arrived, honouring customer commitments and leaving room for bulk deliveries. Schedulers do this in spreadsheets, shift by shift.

## What Already Exists
Refinery and chemical production scheduling and planning tools (AspenTech's planning and scheduling suite, Honeywell's) optimise feedstock slates against unit constraints and inventories, using assays to characterise each crude.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: waste profiles and fingerprint results standing in for crude assays, with far wider uncertainty; permit operating parameter limits and feed-rate limits as hard constraints; drum, tote and bulk inventory with storage-time limits under the permit; customer commitments and contract priority; outage and automatic waste feed cut-off history as capacity loss; and a record of the plan the permit file can show.

## Target Customer
Plant managers and feed schedulers at commercial incinerators, and operations directors at networks of them.

## Impact If Solved
A 1–2 point gain in utilization of practical capacity (estimate) is ~6,000–12,000 tons a year across a 631,721-ton network, ~$5–10M of revenue at ~$800 a ton (estimate), with fewer feed-limit excursions.
