# Warehouse Dock Receiving Adapted to Manifests

**Niche:** [[niches/hazardous-waste-firms/tsdf-receiving-discrepancies/profile|TSDF Receiving & Discrepancy Resolution]]
**Industry:** [[industries/hazardous-waste-firms|Hazardous Waste Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Warehouses match every inbound truck to an advance ship notice and a purchase order automatically; TSDFs match every truck to a profile and a manifest by hand.
**Tags:** #data-integration #optimization-fundamentals #time-series-forecasting #compliance #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to match each arriving load to its approved profile and manifest at the gate, and whoever leaves the fewest loads in unresolved discrepancy takes the account.

## The Problem
The gate is a dock: appointments, inbound documents, quality checks, put-away and exceptions. TSDFs run it without the appointment, matching and exception tools any distribution centre takes for granted.

## What Already Exists
Warehouse management and dock scheduling systems (Manhattan, Blue Yonder, Körber, Opendock) handle appointments, advance ship notice matching, quality holds and exception workflows.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs the approved profile in place of the purchase order and the e-Manifest record in place of the ship notice; the Waste Analysis Plan's sampling rules as the quality step; discrepancy categories and the regulatory resolution window; storage-time limits under the permit for held loads; and signatures flowing back to e-Manifest.

## Target Customer
TSDF operators running high-volume receiving, especially consolidators standardising across many facilities.

## Impact If Solved
Appointment slots and automatic matching can cut average truck dwell from ~2 hours to under 1 (estimate), which at ~40,000 loads a year is ~40,000 driver and bay hours returned.
