# One Screen From Gate to Lab Result

**Industry:** [[industries/hazardous-waste-firms|Hazardous Waste Firms]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A receiving clerk checks a load across the ERP, the scale ticket, the LIMS and e-Manifest in four windows, and the discrepancies that matter are found late because nothing joins them.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #gradient-boosting #data-integration #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to match each arriving load to its approved profile and manifest at the gate, and whoever leaves the fewest loads in unresolved discrepancy takes the account.

## The Problem
Quantity and type discrepancies are common: wrong container counts, weights outside tolerance, a waste code on the manifest that is not on the profile, a fingerprint outside the profile's range. Each must be resolved with the generator or reported, and loads held for resolution fill bays and storage.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The four records come from four systems, often from four vendors, and receiving has been treated as a cost centre staffed by clerks rather than as a data problem.

## What to Build
A receiving workspace that pulls the e-Manifest record or reads the paper copy, matches every line to the approved profile, order and scale weight, shows fingerprint results as they post, highlights each mismatch with the rule it breaks, and opens a structured discrepancy case with the generator contact and draft report attached.

## Target Customer
Receiving managers at commercial TSDFs and operations IT at consolidators replacing legacy systems.

## Impact If Built
Cutting time to resolve each discrepancy by half on ~1,200 discrepant loads a year at a large facility (estimate) frees ~1,500 staff hours and clears held loads from bays days earlier.
