# TSDF Receiving & Discrepancy Resolution

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hazardous-waste-firms|Hazardous Waste Firms]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to match each arriving load to its approved profile and manifest at the gate, and whoever leaves the fewest loads in unresolved discrepancy takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M in receiving, gate and discrepancy-handling staff time and software across US TSDFs (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2–3% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — clipboards and paper or hybrid manifests at the gate; LIMS results not linked to the ERP order; discrepancies worked by phone
**Target Buyer:** Receiving manager or plant manager at a TSDF; operations IT lead at a consolidator
**Automation Potential:** High for matching manifests, weights and profiles; sampling and the acceptance decision stay with people under the Waste Analysis Plan

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every one of the more than 1,000 facilities GAO counts runs the same gate routine: check the manifest against the approved profile, weigh, sample and fingerprint under the Waste Analysis Plan, accept, reject or hold, sign the manifest and, when quantities or types differ, resolve the discrepancy with the generator or report it, now through e-Manifest under the third rule. Handlers ship ~2 million manifested loads a year (EPA). The work is mechanical matching wrapped around a lab test, and a deviation from the Waste Analysis Plan can be a permit violation (search summary). Competitors are TSDF ERP vendors (Terralink's TDX, WASTELINQ, EnviroWare (search summary)), LIMS vendors and the consolidators' in-house systems.

## Current Tools & Gaps
ERPs hold orders and profiles; LIMS hold results; e-Manifest holds the signed record; scales print tickets. Gaps: no single screen joins them per load; discrepancy calls are not recorded as structured cases; paper and image manifests are keyed by hand.
