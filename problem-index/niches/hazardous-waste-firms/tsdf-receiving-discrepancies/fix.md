# Discrepancies Settled by Phone and Recorded Nowhere

**Niche:** [[niches/hazardous-waste-firms/tsdf-receiving-discrepancies/profile|TSDF Receiving & Discrepancy Resolution]]
**Industry:** [[industries/hazardous-waste-firms|Hazardous Waste Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A discrepancy is resolved in a phone call between a receiving clerk and a generator's EHS coordinator, and the next time the same generator sends the same problem nobody remembers.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #descriptive-statistics #compliance #workflow-orchestration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to match each arriving load to its approved profile and manifest at the gate, and whoever leaves the fewest loads in unresolved discrepancy takes the account.

## The Problem
Clerks call, email and wait for generators to explain or correct a load. What was agreed lives in a mailbox. Unresolved cases must be reported, and repeat offenders are not flagged to the approval desk.

## Why It's Still Broken
The third e-Manifest rule moved the discrepancy report into the system but not the resolution work that precedes it, and receiving systems have no case record.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Treat every discrepancy as a case with a type, the profile and generator, the communication thread and the outcome; draft the e-Manifest report when the window runs short; and feed repeat patterns to the approval chemists who recertify that generator's profiles.

## Who Feels the Pain
Receiving clerks, approval chemists, generators' EHS coordinators and the drivers waiting at the gate.

## Impact If Fixed
Recording cases makes repeat offenders visible; cutting repeat discrepancies by a quarter at a facility with ~1,200 a year (estimate) avoids ~300 held loads.
