# PHMSA Hazmat Safety Data & Enforcement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hazardous-waste-firms|Hazardous Waste Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Pipeline and Hazardous Materials Safety Administration's Office of Hazardous Materials Safety writes the hazardous materials regulations that govern every hazardous waste shipment (classification, packaging, marking, placarding, shipping papers, training, security plans), registers hazmat shippers and carriers, investigates incidents and publishes incident data from carriers' Form 5800.1 reports. FMCSA enforces on the road. Hazardous waste transporters are among the regulated carriers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Tens of data, risk and investigation analysts (estimate); inspectors and investigators work case by case and are production
**Output:** Rules, interpretations, incident statistics, enforcement cases, special permits
**Proprietary data:** Incident reports, registrations and inspection records, largely published
**Clock:** Rulemaking and harmonisation cycles set internally; no hard third-party deadline on the analysis
**Buyer:** Associate administrator for hazardous materials safety
