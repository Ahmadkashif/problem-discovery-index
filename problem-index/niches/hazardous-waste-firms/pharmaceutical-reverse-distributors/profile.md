# Pharmaceutical Reverse Distributors' Returns Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hazardous-waste-firms|Hazardous Waste Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Reverse distributors collect expired and unsaleable drugs from pharmacies, hospitals and wholesalers, process them for manufacturer credit, and send what cannot be credited to destruction, much of it as hazardous waste pharmaceuticals to incinerators under Subpart P (in force in 37 states (search summary)). Inmar is the largest; regional firms serve independents (estimate). Pharmacies pay a fee, usually a share of credit recovered; manufacturers pay for returns processing and reports.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 returns-analytics and manufacturer-reporting staff at the largest firm (estimate); sorters, credit processors and warehouse staff are production and not counted
**Output:** Credit estimates and statements for pharmacies; returns and destruction reports for manufacturers
**Proprietary data:** Item-level returns across many pharmacies and manufacturers: NDC, lot, quantity, reason, credit and disposition
**Clock:** Manufacturers' return-policy windows and pharmacies' credit cycles, both set by the parties to the firm's contracts; per-shipment destruction deadlines bind production
**Buyer:** VP of analytics or general manager of the returns business

**Kill switches:** **data isn't theirs** — the returns record that carries the scored work is pharmacies' and manufacturers' transaction data held under service agreements, and no checked terms grant aggregate product rights.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. The invoice is a processing fee, the analytics team is small once processors are set aside, and the cross-pharmacy returns archive belongs to clients.
