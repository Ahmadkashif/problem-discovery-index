# Hazardous Waste Brokers & Outlet Desks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hazardous-waste-firms|Hazardous Waste Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Firms that manage generators' hazardous waste without owning treatment capacity: they profile streams, arrange transport, choose outlets among competing TSDFs, pay the TSDFs and invoice the generator, often bundling compliance help. Many are regional companies of a few dozen people (estimate). Their value is knowing which facility will take which stream at what price and when, especially when incineration is tight, as in 2023's 50,000–60,000 ton backlog (search summary).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 outlet, pricing and compliance analysts per firm (estimate); project managers and field staff who handle each account are production
**Output:** Outlet selection, consolidated invoices, generator reports
**Proprietary data:** The prices the broker itself pays TSDFs across many facilities and streams; generators' profiles and shipment data held under service agreements
**Clock:** Generators' accumulation limits and project schedules (client clocks)
**Buyer:** Owner or president
