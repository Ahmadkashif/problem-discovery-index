**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a portfolio of hundreds of advertisers into calibrated priors on how much each platform overstates — and whoever does that is selling the only thing in this market that cannot be copied from a platform's documentation.

## The Problem
Combining many small studies into a reliable estimate is a formalised discipline. Meta-analysis has established methods for pooling effect sizes, modelling between-study heterogeneity, detecting publication bias, and producing a defensible summary with its uncertainty. Evidence-based medicine rests on it. Performance agencies run dozens of small experiments a year across a client portfolio, each individually underpowered, and combine none of them — which is precisely the situation meta-analysis was invented for.

## What Already Exists
Fixed and random effects meta-analytic pooling; heterogeneity modelling and subgroup analysis; publication and selection bias detection; effect size standardisation across studies; and prospective meta-analytic registries.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is from published studies to a private portfolio of commercial experiments. It requires: (1) heterogeneity that is the finding rather than a nuisance, since how overstatement varies by vertical and purchase cycle is exactly what a new client needs to know — subgroup structure is the product here, where medicine treats it cautiously; (2) selection bias from experiments run only where someone suspected a problem, which is a real distortion and must be modelled rather than assumed away; (3) effect sizes standardised across platforms, verticals and outcome definitions, which needs a common scale nobody has defined; (4) confidentiality requiring abstraction before pooling, which medicine does not face and which shapes what can be retained; and (5) a continuously updating estimate rather than a periodic review, since the platforms change and a prior from three years ago may be stale.

## Target Customer
Agency data leadership, in-house measurement teams, and measurement vendors who could operate a cross-advertiser registry.

## Impact If Solved
Meta-analysis was invented for exactly this situation and agencies pool nothing. Heterogeneity by vertical and cycle is the product rather than a nuisance, and selection bias from testing only where trouble was suspected is the distortion that must be modelled.
