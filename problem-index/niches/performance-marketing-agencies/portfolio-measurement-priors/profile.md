# Portfolio Measurement Priors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/performance-marketing-agencies|Performance Marketing Agencies]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a portfolio of hundreds of advertisers into calibrated priors on how much each platform overstates — and whoever does that is selling the only thing in this market that cannot be copied from a platform's documentation.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of category fees
**Digital Adoption:** Low — the corpus is discarded per engagement
**Target Buyer:** Agency data and executive leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — pooling is the entire method

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Performance agencies sit on the one vantage point nobody else has: the same measurement question asked across hundreds of advertisers, dozens of verticals and every major platform, with the client's actual revenue visible on the other side of the engagement. That is the dataset from which cross-platform incrementality could actually be learned — priors on how much each platform overstates, by vertical and purchase cycle, calibrated against geo experiments run across a whole client base. Instead the corpus is discarded at the end of each engagement and the next client's plan starts from the platform's own numbers again.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Per-client measurement performed and forgotten, occasional case studies, and practitioner intuition about which platforms overstate. The gaps: no pooling across clients; no experimental record retained; no priors by vertical or cycle; nothing transferred to a new engagement; and no asset that survives a client leaving.
