# Creative Production Throughput

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/performance-marketing-agencies|Performance Marketing Agencies]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce assets at the rate the platforms consume them, at a cost that survives the fee — and whoever wins on cost per asset and cycle time takes the volume work the whole category depends on.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.6B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category fees
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — generative tools, manual pipeline
**Target Buyer:** Agency creative operations leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — production is a pipeline problem

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is an industrial contest with an unambiguous scoreboard: assets delivered per week, cost per asset, and time from brief to live. It is won by whoever builds the better pipeline — generation, templating, versioning, localisation, review, trafficking and tagging — and it requires no prediction, no measurement science and no strategic judgement. Production shops compete here and win or lose on throughput economics, which makes it a different business entirely from deciding what to make.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Generative image and video tools, templating and resizing systems, project trackers, review threads, and manual trafficking. The gaps: pipelines assembled from disconnected tools; review as the dominant bottleneck; localisation done manually; tagging skipped; trafficking as a separate manual step; and no measurement of the pipeline's own throughput.
