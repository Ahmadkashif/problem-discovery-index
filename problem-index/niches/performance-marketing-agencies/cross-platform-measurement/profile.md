# Cross-Platform Measurement & Allocation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/performance-marketing-agencies|Performance Marketing Agencies]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce one allocation decision from platform numbers that double-count and cannot be added — and whoever does that credibly is selling the only judgement a platform cannot supply about itself.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$7B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~28% of category fees
**Digital Adoption:** Low — self-graded numbers, summed
**Target Buyer:** Agency measurement leadership and client finance
**Automation Potential:** Very High — reconciliation and modelling are both automatable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The allocation decision an agency is paid to make is made against numbers each platform reports about itself, which double-count, use incompatible windows, and cannot be added together. Summed across platforms, reported conversions routinely exceed the orders the client actually took — a discrepancy every practitioner knows about and every weekly report reproduces. This is the largest concentration of contested value in the category because allocation is the deliverable, and it is the one thing a client cannot get from the platforms directly.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Platform-reported conversions, blended return calculations, occasional marketing mix modelling, and spreadsheets reconciling the difference. The gaps: no deduplication across platforms; incompatible attribution windows; no incrementality input; no reconciliation against actual orders; and a weekly report that presents a sum of incomparable numbers.
