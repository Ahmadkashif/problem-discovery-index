# The Fee Model

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/performance-marketing-agencies|Performance Marketing Agencies]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get paid for the finding that a channel is not incremental — and whoever builds a commercial model that rewards that is selling something no percentage-of-spend competitor can match.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of category fees
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — a percentage in a contract
**Target Buyer:** Agency and client leadership
**Automation Potential:** Medium — measurement automatable, the model is commercial

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Most agencies charge a share of media spend, which pays them more for recommending more spend and pays them nothing for the finding that a channel is not incremental. Clients have noticed. This is a distinct contest because it is the commercial structure rather than the work — an agency can be excellent at measurement and creative and still be structurally unable to act on its own best findings, and the firm that solves the contract is competing on a different axis from the firm that solves the analysis.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Percentage of spend, retainers, hybrid arrangements, and occasional performance bonuses with weak definitions. The gaps: incentives that reward spend growth; no mechanism to pay for a spend reduction; performance bonuses defined against platform-reported metrics the agency influences; no shared measurement both parties trust; and no way to price the finding that is most valuable to the client.
