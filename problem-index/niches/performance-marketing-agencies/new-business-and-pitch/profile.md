# New Business & Pitch Economics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/performance-marketing-agencies|Performance Marketing Agencies]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to win work without giving away the analysis for free — and whoever changes that economics releases the capacity the whole category burns on unpaid pitching.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.5B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category fees
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — decks and unpaid audits
**Target Buyer:** Agency leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — the audit work is largely automatable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Retention cycles have shortened, pitches are frequent and expensive, and each one requires the agency's best people to produce an audit and a strategy for a prospect who may take it and stay where they are. A substantial share of a firm's senior capacity goes into work that is never paid for, and the shortened retention cycle means it recurs constantly. This is a distinct contest from the fee model — that is about how paid work is priced, this is about the unpaid work required to get it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Manual account audits using platform access or estimates, bespoke strategy decks, credentials presentations, and reference calls. The gaps: audits rebuilt by hand each time; no reusable analytical asset; no protection of the work given away; no measure of pitch cost against win rate; and no way to demonstrate capability without doing the work free.
