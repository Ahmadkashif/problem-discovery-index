# Bid Management Practice

**Niche:** [[niches/performance-marketing-agencies/new-business-and-pitch/profile|New Business & Pitch Economics]]
**Industry:** [[industries/performance-marketing-agencies|Performance Marketing Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Construction and professional services run disciplined bid-no-bid processes with tracked win rates and reusable content libraries, and agencies pitch on enthusiasm.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #gradient-boosting #evaluation-metrics #descriptive-statistics #automation #confidence-intervals #revenue-impact #logistic-regression
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to win work without giving away the analysis for free — and whoever changes that economics releases the capacity the whole category burns on unpaid pitching.

## The Problem
Firms that bid for work as a core activity manage it as a discipline. Construction, engineering and professional services run formal bid-no-bid decisions against scored criteria, maintain proposal content libraries so nothing is written twice, track win rates by client type and competitor, and calculate cost of sale per opportunity. They do it because bidding is expensive and unmanaged bidding destroys margin. Agencies, whose pitch costs are comparable relative to revenue, mostly decide to pitch because someone senior is enthusiastic.

## What Already Exists
Bid-no-bid decision frameworks with scored qualification; proposal content libraries and reuse; win-loss tracking and analysis; cost-of-sale measurement per opportunity; and competitor intelligence in bidding.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a pitch whose deliverable is original analysis of the prospect's own business. It requires: (1) the pitch product being a bespoke diagnosis rather than a priced proposal, so content reuse helps less and the automation must target the analysis itself — this is where the adaptation is substantive rather than procedural; (2) the analysis being valuable to the prospect independently of the decision, which creates a give-away problem construction bidding does not have; (3) very short cycles measured in weeks, against bid processes designed for months; (4) win-loss feedback that is informal and often untruthful, so the learning loop needs inference from outcomes rather than reported reasons; and (5) firms whose senior capacity is simultaneously the delivery resource and the bid resource, making the opportunity cost immediate rather than budgeted.

## Target Customer
Agency leadership and new business functions, and bid management vendors for whom analysis-led pitching is an unserved case.

## Impact If Solved
Bidding disciplines exist because unmanaged bidding destroys margin, and agencies decide on enthusiasm. Automating the analysis rather than reusing proposal content is the substantive adaptation, since the pitch product is a bespoke diagnosis.
