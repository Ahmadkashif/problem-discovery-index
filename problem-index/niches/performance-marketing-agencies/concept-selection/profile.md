# Concept Selection & Prediction

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/performance-marketing-agencies|Performance Marketing Agencies]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to know which concepts are worth producing before the money is spent — and whoever builds that prediction from a portfolio of creative and its measured performance owns the one advantage a platform cannot supply.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.4B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category fees
**Digital Adoption:** Low — taste and a test budget
**Target Buyer:** Agency strategy and creative leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — a prediction problem with a corpus

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This contest is won by whoever can say, before production, which ideas are worth making. It requires a corpus of creative with its measured performance, a representation of what a concept is, and the statistical care to separate the concept's effect from budget, audience and timing. It requires nothing about production capacity, cost per asset or pipeline design. An agency can be excellent at this and outsource all production, which is precisely the split the category is showing between strategy shops and production shops.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Creative testing inside platforms, historical performance by asset, practitioner intuition, and competitor observation. The gaps: testing that requires volume most accounts lack; no concept representation; no cross-client learning; effects confounded with budget and audience; and no prediction before spend.
