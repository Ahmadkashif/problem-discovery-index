# The Account Director

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/performance-marketing-agencies|Performance Marketing Agencies]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to take the deck-building off the account director so the thinking happens — and whoever does that changes what an agency is actually able to deliver.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.5B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category fees
**Digital Adoption:** Low — a week of slides
**Target Buyer:** Agency operations leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — assembly and narration are automatable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Thursday is for building six weekly decks, Friday is for the calls about them, and the thinking that would actually help the client happens in gaps that keep not appearing. The account director is where the agency's value is supposed to be delivered — judgement, strategy, the argument for what to do next — and their week is consumed by the ritual of reporting what already happened. Their capacity is the constraint on how much of the agency's actual expertise reaches a client.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Reporting pipelines feeding dashboards, slide templates, spreadsheets for the numbers the dashboard misses, and calendar full of client calls. The gaps: decks assembled manually despite the data being automated; narrative written from scratch weekly; no prioritisation of which accounts need attention; no record of advice given; and no protected time for analysis.
