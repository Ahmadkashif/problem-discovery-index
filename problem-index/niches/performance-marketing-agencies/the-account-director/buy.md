# Client Service Practice

**Industry:** [[industries/performance-marketing-agencies|Performance Marketing Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Professional services firms manage client portfolios with health scoring, structured reviews and utilisation discipline, and agencies allocate attention by whoever emailed most recently.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to take the deck-building off the account director so the thinking happens — and whoever does that changes what an agency is actually able to deliver.

## The Problem
Professional services firms manage client portfolios deliberately: relationship health assessed and reviewed, engagement risk tracked, utilisation and capacity planned, structured review cadences, and formal knowledge capture from each engagement. Law and consulting firms run this because their entire asset is people's time and judgement. Agencies, whose economics are identical, allocate attention by inbox order and capture knowledge in decks nobody reopens.

## What Already Exists
Client health and relationship scoring; engagement risk and escalation processes; utilisation and capacity planning; structured review cadences; and knowledge management capturing engagement learning.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a portfolio where performance data is continuous and objective. It requires: (1) health signals drawn from account performance and measurement quality rather than from relationship assessment, which is a stronger and more automatable signal than professional services has ever had — the data advantage is real and unused; (2) a weekly cadence against the monthly or quarterly reviews the practice assumes, which changes what can be manual; (3) the deliverable being an analysis rather than an engagement outcome, so knowledge capture must record recommendations and results rather than project closure notes; (4) churn that is frequently caused by measurable performance decline, making prediction genuinely feasible where professional services relies on relationship judgement; and (5) small teams with no dedicated operations function, so anything requiring administration will not be adopted.

## Target Customer
Agency operations and leadership, account directors, and professional services automation vendors for whom agency portfolios are an adjacent market.

## Impact If Solved
Professional services scores relationship health on judgement and agencies have continuous objective performance data they do not use. Weekly cadence and measurable churn drivers make prediction feasible where the established practice relies on instinct.
