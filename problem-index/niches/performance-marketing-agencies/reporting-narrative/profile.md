# Reporting Narrative

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/performance-marketing-agencies|Performance Marketing Agencies]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to write what happened rather than to move data into a chart — and whoever automates the part that consumes the week takes the value the pipeline vendors left on the table.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~4% of category fees
**Digital Adoption:** Low — pipelines solved, meaning not
**Target Buyer:** Agency operations and account teams
**Automation Potential:** Very High — narration from data is now tractable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Pipeline vendors solved getting the data into a dashboard, and left untouched the part that consumes the week — agreeing what the numbers mean and writing what happened. The connectors, the warehouse and the visualisation are commodity; the explanation is manual, is rewritten weekly for every client, and is where the hours go. This is a distinct niche because the contest is over generating the interpretation, which is a different capability from moving data and is the part the incumbent vendors declared out of scope.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Data connectors and pipelines, dashboards, slide templates, and a person writing commentary. The gaps: narrative written from scratch each week; no detection of what is worth commenting on; no diagnosis behind the description; inconsistent interpretation between team members; and no record of what was said last time.
