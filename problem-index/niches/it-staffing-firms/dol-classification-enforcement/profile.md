# Wage, Hour & Classification Enforcement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/it-staffing-firms|IT Staffing Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Federal and state labour enforcement agencies investigate worker classification, overtime, and wage payment across the contingent workforce, and their determinations set the boundary every staffing model operates inside.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,500 investigators, analysts, and attorneys per agency
**Output:** Investigation findings, back wage determinations, opinion letters, enforcement statistics
**Proprietary data:** Investigation and settlement records across employers and industries
**Clock:** Statutory limitations and investigation timelines
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — federal or state agency
