# Contingent Workforce MSP & VMS Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/it-staffing-firms|IT Staffing Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Managed service providers and vendor management systems run enterprises' entire contingent labour programmes — distributing requisitions to a panel of staffing suppliers, setting and enforcing rate cards, scoring supplier performance, and reporting programme savings back to the client. Every IT staffing firm placing into a large enterprise is submitting through one of them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,500 programme analysts, rate and market researchers, and data scientists
**Output:** Rate card benchmarking, supplier scorecards, programme savings and compliance analysis, demand and supply forecasting
**Proprietary data:** Requisitions, submissions, rates, fills, and tenure across thousands of suppliers and millions of contingent engagements — the only view of the whole market, since each staffing firm sees only its own submissions and each client only its own programme
**Clock:** Requisition submission windows, contract expiry, and tenure limits
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / SVP of Programme Analytics
