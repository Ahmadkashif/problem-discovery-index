# Technical Assessment Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/it-staffing-firms|IT Staffing Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
HackerRank, Codility and their peers score candidates on coding assessments — the automated answer to what Pass 1 says recruiters spend 60-70% of their day on, which is judging whether a candidate can actually perform at the level their resume claims.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-200 psychometricians, content engineers, and data scientists
**Output:** Assessment content, candidate scores and percentiles, benchmark reports, interview tooling
**Proprietary data:** Millions of scored assessments with item-level response and behaviour data across skills and seniorities
**Clock:** Hiring process timelines
**Buyer:** Chief Science Officer / VP of Data
