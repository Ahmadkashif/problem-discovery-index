# Labour Market Analytics Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/it-staffing-firms|IT Staffing Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Lightcast and its peers collect job postings and profile data at national scale, normalize them against a maintained skills taxonomy, and sell the resulting view of demand by skill, occupation, and geography to staffing firms, employers, and educators.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 data scientists, taxonomists, labour economists, and researchers
**Output:** Skills taxonomies, demand and supply analytics, compensation and posting trend data, talent market reports
**Proprietary data:** A deduplicated historical posting corpus and the skills ontology built over it; the underlying postings are publicly visible, the normalization is not
**Clock:** Monthly data cycles and planning calendars
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Research
