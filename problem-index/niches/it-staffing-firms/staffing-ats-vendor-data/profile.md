# Staffing ATS Vendor Data Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/it-staffing-firms|IT Staffing Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The data groups at Bullhorn, JobDiva and the staffing platforms run applicant tracking and CRM for most of the industry, holding candidate records, submissions, interviews, placements, and redeployment history across tens of thousands of agencies.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-200 data and product staff
**Output:** In-product analytics, matching and sourcing features, industry benchmark publications
**Proprietary data:** Submission-to-placement outcomes and consultant redeployment across the industry — including the bench cycling Pass 1 identifies as a core economic problem
**Clock:** Requisition and contract renewal cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Product Officer / VP of Data
