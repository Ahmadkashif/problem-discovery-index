# Staffing Industry Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/it-staffing-firms|IT Staffing Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
The staffing industry research firms size the contingent labour market, track bill rates and pay rates by skill and geography, and publish the rankings and benchmarks the sector plans against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 analysts and researchers
**Output:** Market sizing and forecasts, bill and pay rate benchmarks, buyer surveys, industry rankings
**Proprietary data:** Long-running rate series and buyer survey panels in a market with no public pricing
**Clock:** Annual and quarterly publication cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Research / Managing Director
