# Worker Classification Advisory

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/it-staffing-firms|IT Staffing Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Specialist practices determine whether a worker is properly an employee or an independent contractor under federal and state tests that differ materially by jurisdiction, and defend the position when a state agency or the Department of Labor challenges it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 employment attorneys, analysts, and compliance specialists
**Output:** Classification determinations, risk assessments, remediation programmes, audit and litigation defence
**Proprietary data:** Determinations and enforcement outcomes across many engagements and jurisdictions
**Clock:** Audit notices and statutory response windows
**Buyer:** Practice leader / Managing Director
