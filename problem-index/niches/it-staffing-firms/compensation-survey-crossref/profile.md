# Compensation Survey Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/it-staffing-firms|IT Staffing Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
The compensation survey publishers set the market rates staffing firms price permanent placements against and that clients use to challenge a proposed salary.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 compensation analysts, job matchers, and statisticians
**Output:** Survey publications, market rate percentiles, job architecture frameworks
**Proprietary data:** Employer-contributed pay data matched to a proprietary job taxonomy
**Clock:** Annual survey and merit cycles
**Buyer:** Global head of surveys
