# Background Screening Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/it-staffing-firms|IT Staffing Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Screening providers run the criminal, employment, and education verification every contract placement requires, on a turnaround that determines whether a candidate is still available when the client is ready.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-2,000 researchers, verifiers, adjudicators, and analysts
**Output:** Consumer reports, adjudication recommendations, dispute resolution
**Proprietary data:** Assembled court and verification records plus the firm's own adjudication and dispute history
**Clock:** Placement turnaround — a slow report loses the candidate
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer
