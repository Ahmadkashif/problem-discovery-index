# Employer of Record & Payrolling Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/it-staffing-firms|IT Staffing Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Employer of record and payrolling providers take on the employment relationship for contingent workers a client has already sourced, absorbing classification, payroll tax, and benefits compliance across jurisdictions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 compliance analysts, payroll specialists, and researchers
**Output:** Jurisdictional compliance determinations, classification assessments, payroll and benefits administration, risk reporting
**Proprietary data:** Employment compliance rules across many jurisdictions, and worker engagement data across clients
**Clock:** Payroll cycles and regulatory effective dates
**Buyer:** VP of Compliance / Chief Data Officer
