# Floorplan Lenders' Dealer Risk & Curtailment Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/powersports-and-boat-dealers|Powersports & Boat Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The credit and portfolio teams at the lenders that finance dealer inventory: Wells Fargo Commercial Distribution Finance and the Brunswick and Polaris joint ventures it backs, Northpoint Commercial Finance, Huntington Distribution Finance, Bank of America and Harley-Davidson Financial Services (10-Ks; Northpoint and Huntington from search summary and recall). They set credit lines and advance rates, design curtailment schedules, read field-audit results and dealer financial statements, and manage OEM repurchase agreements. The insight is priced into the credit, not sold.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 portfolio-risk and analytics staff per lender on the recreational book (estimate); the credit officers reviewing each dealer's line are production, like underwriters, and are not counted
**Output:** Curtailment policy, aging and audit-exception reports, portfolio loss and concentration analysis
**Proprietary data:** Unit-level floorplan histories (floor date, aging, curtailments, payoffs), audit findings and dealer financials across thousands of dealers
**Clock:** The lender's own audit, review and curtailment calendar
**Buyer:** Chief Credit Officer / Head of Portfolio Risk
