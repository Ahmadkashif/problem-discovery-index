# Model-Year Stocking & Floorplan Curtailment

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/powersports-and-boat-dealers|Powersports & Boat Dealers]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to set a dealer's model-year stocking order and aged-unit plan so that inventory sells before the floorplan's free period ends, and whoever leaves the fewest units past their first curtailment at model-year end takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.5–2.5B a year in floorplan interest, curtailment-driven discounts and inventory tools across the industry's new-unit inventory (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** New units are roughly half to two-thirds of dealer receipts (estimate; OneWater's non-boat sales were 18.7% of revenue in fiscal 2025)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — lender portals and DMS inventory reports are digital; the stocking order is set at the OEM dealer meeting from memory and a spreadsheet
**Target Buyer:** Dealer principal / General Manager
**Automation Potential:** Medium — forecasting and curtailment tracking automate; the order and the discount decision stay with the manager

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A car dealer orders continuously against an allocation. A boat or powersports dealer commits to most of a year's new inventory in one or two stocking orders, months before the season, and floors it through Brunswick Acceptance, Polaris Acceptance, HDFS, Northpoint or a bank. OEMs pay the interest for a period (MasterCraft "generally ranging up to nine months"), then the dealer pays interest and curtailments that "reduce the allowable advance rate as our inventory ages" (OneWater 10-K). A unit that misses its season becomes a non-current model year. That clock, and the once-a-year commitment, separate it from the car twin [[niches/franchised-car-dealers/new-vehicle-allocation-turn/profile|New-Vehicle Allocation & Inventory Turn]] and from RV seasonal stocking ([[niches/rv-dealerships/seasonal-inventory-management/profile|Seasonal Inventory Management]]).

## Current Tools & Gaps
OEM dealer-meeting order tools and retail programme bulletins; lender portals; DMS inventory aging reports; Lightspeed's AI Command Center (March 2026) flags aging inventory; 20 Group composites for benchmarks; registration data from Statistical Surveys or Info-Link. Gaps: no tool scores a proposed stocking order against local demand and the dealer's own sell-through, and none ranks floored units by the cost of carrying them past the next curtailment.
