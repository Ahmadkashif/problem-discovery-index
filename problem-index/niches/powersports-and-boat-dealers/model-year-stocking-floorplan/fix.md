# The Aged Unit Found at the Curtailment Notice

**Niche:** [[niches/powersports-and-boat-dealers/model-year-stocking-floorplan/profile|Model-Year Stocking & Floorplan Curtailment]]
**Industry:** [[industries/powersports-and-boat-dealers|Powersports & Boat Dealers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The general manager learns a boat has aged past its free period when the lender's curtailment notice arrives, too late to use the OEM programme that would have moved it.
**Tags:** #survival-analysis #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to set a dealer's model-year stocking order and aged-unit plan so that inventory sells before the floorplan's free period ends, and whoever leaves the fewest units past their first curtailment at model-year end takes the account.

## The Problem
Floored units age quietly. The OEM's interest-free period ends on one date, the lender's first curtailment falls on another, and this month's OEM retail programme expires on a third. The general manager sees aging on a monthly report sorted by days in stock, not by the next deadline or by the money available to move each unit.

## Why It's Still Broken
The dates live in three places: lender statements, OEM programme bulletins and the DMS. Nobody owns the join, and the office manager reconciling statements isn't the person who decides discounts.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A weekly list, one line per floored unit, sorted by the next cash event: free period ending, curtailment due, programme expiring. Each line shows the carrying cost of keeping the unit another 30 days, the OEM money available now, and a suggested action (feature it, move it at the boat show, dealer-trade it, wholesale it), with the decision recorded so next year's stocking order can learn from it.

## Who Feels the Pain
General managers and sales managers deciding discounts; office managers paying curtailments; dealer principals watching floorplan cash in the off-season.

## Impact If Fixed
Acting 30–60 days earlier on the 10–15% of units that age (estimate) saves a few hundred dollars of interest per boat and keeps more units inside OEM programme windows, worth tens of thousands of dollars a year at a 200-unit store.
