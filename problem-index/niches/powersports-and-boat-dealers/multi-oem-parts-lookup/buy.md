# Industrial Interchange Search for a Parts Counter

**Niche:** [[niches/powersports-and-boat-dealers/multi-oem-parts-lookup/profile|Parts Lookup & Special Orders Across OEM Catalogs]]
**Industry:** [[industries/powersports-and-boat-dealers|Powersports & Boat Dealers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Industrial distributors already match a worn or obsolete part to its current equivalent across manufacturers; a powersports parts counter does the same job with no interchange tool.
**Tags:** #contrastive-learning #k-nearest-neighbors #transformers #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a customer's description, photo or old part into the right current part number across several OEM and aftermarket catalogs, and whoever has the lowest wrong-part return rate takes the account.

## The Problem
The same impeller, filter, belt or gasket appears under different numbers in OEM and aftermarket catalogs, and old numbers are superseded. Counter staff carry the cross-references in their heads.

## What Already Exists
Industrial distributors use interchange and cross-reference search to match a breakdown part to equivalents across brands, with attributes and images ([[niches/industrial-distributors/breakdown-replacement-interchange/profile|Breakdown Replacement & Interchange]]).

## The Customization Gap
Needed: OEM diagram references and serial-break logic, not just attribute matching; marine and powersports supersession chains; aftermarket equivalents from powersports and marine distributors with fitment by model and year; customer-facing photo capture at the counter and on the dealer website; and returns data to show where cross-references fail.

## Target Customer
Parts managers at multi-line stores; dealer groups centralising parts.

## Impact If Solved
Selling an aftermarket equivalent from stock instead of special-ordering an OEM part on ~1 lookup in 10 (estimate) saves ~3–7 days of customer wait on each of those orders, roughly 300–600 orders a season at a busy counter (estimate).
