# Electronic Parts Catalog Publishers for Powersports & Marine

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/powersports-and-boat-dealers|Powersports & Boat Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Publishers such as Dealer Spike's PartSmart license OEM parts diagrams and part lists, convert them into searchable electronic catalogs with supersessions and serial breaks, and sell lookup to dealer parts counters and dealer websites; OEMs also publish their own catalogs. Aftermarket distributors maintain fitment for their own lines.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 non-production staff per publisher doing cross-catalog analysis (supersession mapping across lines, lookup and return analytics) (estimate); the ~10–30 catalog editors converting OEM files are production and are not counted
**Output:** Licensed electronic catalogs and lookup software; website parts sections
**Proprietary data:** Converted OEM diagram libraries and supersession histories; dealer lookup and order logs
**Clock:** OEM model-year releases and supersession bulletins
**Buyer:** VP of Content / Product
