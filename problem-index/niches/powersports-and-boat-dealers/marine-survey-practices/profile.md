# Marine Survey Practices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/powersports-and-boat-dealers|Powersports & Boat Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Accredited marine surveyors (SAMS and NAMS members, recalled) inspect a vessel for a buyer, lender or insurer: condition and valuation surveys before purchase or financing, insurance surveys, and damage surveys after a loss. Fees run ~$20–30 per foot (estimate). Dealers meet them on used-boat sales and on trades their lenders want surveyed.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1–5 surveyors per practice; each report is per-vessel production work
**Output:** Condition and valuation survey reports
**Proprietary data:** Each practice's past reports, owned by the commissioning client
**Clock:** The buyer's closing or the insurer's renewal
**Buyer:** Boat buyer, lender or insurer
