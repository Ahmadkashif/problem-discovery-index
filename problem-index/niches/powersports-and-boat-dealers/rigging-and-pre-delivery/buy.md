# Manufacturing Work Instructions for the Rigging Bay

**Niche:** [[niches/powersports-and-boat-dealers/rigging-and-pre-delivery/profile|New-Unit Rigging & Pre-Delivery Inspection]]
**Industry:** [[industries/powersports-and-boat-dealers|Powersports & Boat Dealers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Factories already run digital work instructions with photo verification at each step; a dealer's rigging bay is a low-volume, high-mix assembly line without them.
**Tags:** #cnns #object-detection #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver every new boat and machine rigged and set up right the first time, and whoever has the fewest comebacks in the first 30 days after delivery takes the account.

## The Problem
Rigging is assembly: a known configuration, a sequence of steps, torque values and checks. Dealers do it from experience and a paper checklist.

## What Already Exists
Connected-worker and digital work-instruction platforms for manufacturing guide operators step by step, capture photos and measurements, and flag deviations, with quality records per serial number.

## The Customization Gap
Needed: instructions assembled per unit from two or more OEMs' rigging guides (hull and engine), not one product's routing; low volume with high mix, so no time study per configuration; outdoor and on-water steps (water test, engine trim and RPM at wide-open throttle); records keyed to HIN and engine serial for warranty; and integration with the DMS deal and parts kit.

## Target Customer
Marine dealer groups standardising rigging across stores.

## Impact If Solved
A group of 20 stores that cuts rigging comebacks by a third (estimate) avoids several hundred repeat visits a year and the claim disputes they generate.
