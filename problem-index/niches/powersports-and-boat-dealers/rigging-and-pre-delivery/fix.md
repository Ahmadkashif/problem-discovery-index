# The Missing Part Found on Delivery Day

**Niche:** [[niches/powersports-and-boat-dealers/rigging-and-pre-delivery/profile|New-Unit Rigging & Pre-Delivery Inspection]]
**Industry:** [[industries/powersports-and-boat-dealers|Powersports & Boat Dealers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The rigger discovers the boat needs a different control box or prop the morning the customer arrives, because nobody built the parts kit from the deal.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #quick-win #automation #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver every new boat and machine rigged and set up right the first time, and whoever has the fewest comebacks in the first 30 days after delivery takes the account.

## The Problem
A deal specifies hull, engine and options, but the rigging parts — controls, harnesses, gauges, prop, battery cables, accessory brackets — are pulled from stock when the rigger starts. A missing part means a substitute, a delay or a delivery with a promise to come back.

## Why It's Still Broken
Deals are written in the sales module; parts are managed in the parts module; the kit list exists in the rigger's head and the OEM rigging guide.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Generate the rigging kit from the deal's configuration when the deal is written, check stock, order shortfalls with the delivery date in view, and stage the kit before the boat enters the bay.

## Who Feels the Pain
Riggers, parts staff, salespeople facing an unhappy buyer, and the buyer.

## Impact If Fixed
Kitting from the deal (estimate: shortfalls cut from ~20% of deals to ~5%) removes most delivery-day substitutions and the come-back visits they create.
