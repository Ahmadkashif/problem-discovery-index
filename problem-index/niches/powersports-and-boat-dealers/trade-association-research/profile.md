# Marine & Powersports Trade Association Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/powersports-and-boat-dealers|Powersports & Boat Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
NMMA publishes monthly and annual boating statistics (215,237 new boats retailed in 2025, down 8.8%) and runs boat shows; the Motorcycle Industry Council publishes retail sales reports to members (motorcycles and scooters down 7.6%, ATVs down 3.5% in 2025); MRAA runs dealer education, certification and Dealer Week; SVIA sets ATV safety training standards (recalled).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research staff per association (estimate)
**Output:** Statistical summaries, member reports, market outlooks
**Proprietary data:** OEM-reported sales at MIC; licensed registration data at NMMA
**Clock:** Self-set monthly and annual releases
**Buyer:** Member OEMs and dealers through dues
