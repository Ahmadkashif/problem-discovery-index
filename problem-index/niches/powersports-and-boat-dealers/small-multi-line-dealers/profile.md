# Small Multi-Line Dealers Without an F&I Office

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/powersports-and-boat-dealers|Powersports & Boat Dealers]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to earn finance and protection-product income on deals at stores with no F&I manager, and whoever books the most back-end gross per retail unit at those stores takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M a year in finance reserve and protection-product income earned or forgone at small dealers, plus FSP fees (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** Firms with fewer than 20 employees took ~$25B of the industry's 2022 receipts, about 38% (Census SUSB 2022)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — lender portals are digital; deals are structured by the owner or a salesperson at the counter, or handed to an outside F&I provider
**Target Buyer:** Dealer Principal / Owner
**Automation Potential:** High for lender routing, menus and paperwork; the conversation with the buyer stays human

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Most firms in the industry are small: 3,141 of 3,549 boat dealer firms and 4,834 of 5,965 motorcycle and other dealer firms had fewer than 20 employees in 2022 (SUSB). A store that size often carries three to six OEM lines and no dedicated F&I manager. Medallion Bank, a large recreation lender, receives "approximately half" of its loan volume through financial service providers that "provide finance and insurance, or F&I, services to small dealers that do not have the desire or ability to provide F&I services themselves" (Medallion 10-K). Recreation loans are large and long: about $29,000 at origination over about 15 years on Medallion's 2025 book. F&I tools and menus are built for stores with an F&I office and a manager trained on them. The lenders themselves are profiled under [[industries/sales-finance-companies|Sales Finance Companies]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Lender portals and instant-decision programmes (Octane's Roadrunner Financial, Sheffield, Synchrony, Medallion, OEM captives); outside F&I service providers; DMS F&I modules; Ekho's digital retail and paperwork. Gaps: no routing of a deal to the lender most likely to approve it at the best terms, no compliant product presentation a salesperson can run, and paperwork split across lenders and states.
