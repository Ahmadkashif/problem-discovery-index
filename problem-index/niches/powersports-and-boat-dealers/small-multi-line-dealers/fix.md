# The Credit App Sent to One Lender at a Time

**Niche:** [[niches/powersports-and-boat-dealers/small-multi-line-dealers/profile|Small Multi-Line Dealers Without an F&I Office]]
**Industry:** [[industries/powersports-and-boat-dealers|Powersports & Boat Dealers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A small store re-keys the same credit application into one lender portal, waits, gets a decline, and re-keys it into the next.
**Tags:** #logistic-regression #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to earn finance and protection-product income on deals at stores with no F&I manager, and whoever books the most back-end gross per retail unit at those stores takes the account.

## The Problem
Each lender and captive has its own portal. A near-prime buyer can take three or four tries; some buyers leave before the third.

## Why It's Still Broken
Lender portals are built by lenders for their own programmes; multi-lender networks reach car dealers more than small recreational stores.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Enter the application once; submit it to the right lenders in order of likely approval for this unit and buyer, with each lender's programme rules checked first; show offers side by side.

## Who Feels the Pain
Owners and salespeople doing F&I at the counter; buyers waiting at the desk.

## Impact If Fixed
Saving 30–60 minutes of re-keying per financed deal and recovering a few deals a month lost to declines (estimate) is worth several thousand dollars a month at a small store.
