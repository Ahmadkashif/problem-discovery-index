# Car-Dealer Digital Retailing for the Ten-Person Store

**Niche:** [[niches/powersports-and-boat-dealers/small-multi-line-dealers/profile|Small Multi-Line Dealers Without an F&I Office]]
**Industry:** [[industries/powersports-and-boat-dealers|Powersports & Boat Dealers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Franchised car dealers buy digital-retailing platforms that structure the whole deal online; a small powersports store needs the same thing priced and built for a counter, not a showroom.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #compliance #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to earn finance and protection-product income on deals at stores with no F&I manager, and whoever books the most back-end gross per retail unit at those stores takes the account.

## The Problem
Small dealers lose back-end income because deal structuring depends on a trained person they don't have.

## What Already Exists
Automotive digital-retailing platforms let a buyer or salesperson build payments, apply for credit, choose protection products and sign online, connected to lender networks and the DMS; they are standard at [[industries/franchised-car-dealers|Franchised Car Dealers]].

## The Customization Gap
Needed: recreational lenders and OEM captive programmes (Octane/Roadrunner, Sheffield, Synchrony, Medallion, HDFS, OEM promotional rates) instead of auto lender networks; unit types that mix boat, motor and trailer on one contract; protection products for marine and powersports (engine, hull, tyre and wheel, theft); integration with recreational DMSs (Lightspeed, DX1, Blackpurl, Dockmaster); and pricing a single store can pay. Ekho, which sells digital powersports retail and paperwork, is building in this direction.

## Target Customer
Single-store owners and small groups; FSPs serving many small dealers.

## Impact If Solved
A store that captures product income on one more deal in five (estimate) earns tens of thousands of dollars a year at 250 retail units.
