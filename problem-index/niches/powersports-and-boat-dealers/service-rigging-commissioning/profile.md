# Service, Rigging & Commissioning

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/powersports-and-boat-dealers|Powersports & Boat Dealers]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn technician hours into billed, right-first-time work, but the spring queue of customers' boats and machines and the rigging bay that prepares new inventory for delivery are won on different measures (billed hours per technician across the year; comebacks in the first 30 days after delivery) by different vendors. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$6B a year in dealer service labour, rigging and set-up (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of receipts and a far larger share of gross profit (estimate; OneWater's non-boat sales were 41.7% of gross profit in fiscal 2025)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — repair orders are in the DMS; scheduling, rigging checklists and water-test logs are on whiteboards and paper
**Target Buyer:** Service Manager / Fixed Operations Director
**Automation Potential:** Medium — scheduling, status and documentation automate; the work is hands-on

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The shop does two different jobs in the same bays. One is customer work: spring commissioning, autumn winterization and repairs on boats and machines the store didn't necessarily sell, compressed into a few weeks, by technicians the industry can't hire fast enough (62% of powersports dealers short-staffed in 2023, 71% for technicians, Powersports Business). The other is the dealer's own inventory: rigging a hull with an outboard, controls, gauges, electronics and a trailer, or uncrating and setting up a side-by-side, before the customer takes delivery. Car-dealer service drives ([[niches/franchised-car-dealers/service-drive-fixed-ops/profile|Service Drive & Fixed Ops Revenue]]) have neither the seasonal wall nor the rigging bay.

## Current Tools & Gaps
DMS service modules (Lightspeed, DX1, Blackpurl, Dockmaster for marine); texting tools (Kenect, BoatBot); OEM service manuals and diagnostic software per brand. Gaps: no capacity planning across the season, and no structured rigging record linking what was installed to what came back.
