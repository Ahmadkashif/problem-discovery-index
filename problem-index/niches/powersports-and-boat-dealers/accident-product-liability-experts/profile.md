# Boating & Off-Road Accident Reconstruction & Product Liability Experts

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/powersports-and-boat-dealers|Powersports & Boat Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Engineering and reconstruction firms retained by counsel in boating, side-by-side and motorcycle accident and product-liability cases, analysing hull and engine failures, rollovers, propeller strikes and dealer rigging or service work. Dealers appear as defendants when a rigging or repair error is alleged.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1–10 experts per firm
**Output:** Expert reports and testimony
**Proprietary data:** Case files held for the retaining counsel
**Clock:** Court scheduling orders
**Buyer:** Litigation counsel
