# Boat & Powersports Registration Data Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/powersports-and-boat-dealers|Powersports & Boat Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Statistical Surveys (Grand Rapids, Michigan) compiles state registration records into monthly market-share reports for marine, RV, powersports, trailer and manufactured-housing makers and dealers; its monthly marine releases are preliminary on the 31–33 states reporting at the time (search summary). Info-Link Technologies (Miami) holds records on every registered US boat with new and used transactions going back about 20 years (search summary). OEMs buy share by segment, county and dealer territory for product planning, dealer development and incentive design; dealers buy their local share; associations buy the industry totals they publish.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 analysts and data staff per firm (estimate; record keying and state-file cleaning are production)
**Output:** Monthly and annual registration-based share reports by segment, brand, model, county and dealer territory; custom territory studies
**Proprietary data:** Decades of cleaned, model-coded state registration history, including used-boat transfers at Info-Link
**Clock:** The firms' own monthly release calendar, timed to state file availability
**Buyer:** OEM VP of Marketing or Dealer Development; dealer principal for local reports
