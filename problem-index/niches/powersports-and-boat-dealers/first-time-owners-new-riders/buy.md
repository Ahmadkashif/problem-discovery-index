# Subscription Onboarding for a Boat Owner

**Niche:** [[niches/powersports-and-boat-dealers/first-time-owners-new-riders/profile|First-Time Boat Owners & New Riders]]
**Industry:** [[industries/powersports-and-boat-dealers|Powersports & Boat Dealers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Software companies run customer-success onboarding with health scores and staged nudges; a dealer handing a first-timer a ~$40,000 boat (estimate) runs none.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #survival-analysis #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a first-time boater or rider using the unit past the second season, and whoever has the highest share of first-time buyers back in the store for service or a trade within 24 months takes the account.

## The Problem
The first months of ownership decide whether a new owner becomes a repeat customer. Dealers treat delivery as the end of the sale.

## What Already Exists
Customer-success platforms for subscription businesses track onboarding milestones, score account health from usage, and trigger staged messages and human outreach.

## The Customization Gap
Needed: milestones from boating and riding (first launch, first trailer trip, first service at hours, winterization) instead of software logins; usage signals from engine hours where connected platforms expose them, service visits and accessory purchases; content specific to the model; a seasonal calendar so outreach fits the riding season; and integration with the DMS and the service scheduler.

## Target Customer
Dealer groups with marketing staff and customer databases large enough to run programmes.

## Impact If Solved
A group retaining 10% more first-time buyers into a second service visit (estimate) adds thousands of repair orders a year across 50 stores.
