# The Delivery Walk-Through Forgotten by the Lake

**Niche:** [[niches/powersports-and-boat-dealers/first-time-owners-new-riders/profile|First-Time Boat Owners & New Riders]]
**Industry:** [[industries/powersports-and-boat-dealers|Powersports & Boat Dealers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A new owner gets an hour of instructions at delivery and remembers none of it at the ramp a week later.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #worker-facing #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a first-time boater or rider using the unit past the second season, and whoever has the highest share of first-time buyers back in the store for service or a trade within 24 months takes the account.

## The Problem
The delivery walk-through covers the battery switch, fuel, bilge, kill cord, trim, trailer straps and the controls. At the ramp, the owner calls the store, or doesn't and has a bad day.

## Why It's Still Broken
The walk-through is spoken, not recorded, and the owner's manual covers every option the model could have, not the boat as rigged.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Record the walk-through on the salesperson's phone, cut it into short clips by topic, and attach them with the rigging record to the owner's account; add an assistant that answers "why won't it start" from that unit's configuration and escalates to the service department.

## Who Feels the Pain
First-time owners; salespeople and service writers taking ramp calls on weekends.

## Impact If Fixed
If ~25% of weekend ramp calls are walk-through questions (estimate), recorded clips and a unit-aware assistant cut them by ~50% and save first-time buyers a bad first outing.
