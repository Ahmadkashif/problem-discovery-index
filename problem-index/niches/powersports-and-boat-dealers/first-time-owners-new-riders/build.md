# A First Season Plan Tied to the Unit

**Niche:** [[niches/powersports-and-boat-dealers/first-time-owners-new-riders/profile|First-Time Boat Owners & New Riders]]
**Industry:** [[industries/powersports-and-boat-dealers|Powersports & Boat Dealers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A dealer knows which buyers are new to boating or riding and does nothing different for them after delivery.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #survival-analysis #large-language-models #worker-facing #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a first-time boater or rider using the unit past the second season, and whoever has the highest share of first-time buyers back in the store for service or a trade within 24 months takes the account.

## The Problem
First-time buyers who struggle with launching, trailering or a first breakdown use the unit less, skip the first service and drift away. The dealer sees it only as a missing service visit or a private sale listing.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
CRMs track leads, not ownership. OEM programmes are brand-wide, not tied to the dealer's service department. And dealers rarely flag first-time buyers in the DMS.

## What to Build
Flag first-time buyers at the deal. Give each a first-season plan for their specific unit: short videos on launching, trailering and pre-ride checks for that model; a booked on-water or riding lesson; reminders tied to hours and calendar; a quick-answer assistant for "it won't start" moments that knows the unit; and a first service booked at delivery. A churn model scores who is drifting (no service, no app use, no hours logged) so a person calls them.

## Target Customer
Dealer principals and service managers at stores selling entry pontoons, fishing boats, PWCs and entry side-by-sides and motorcycles.

## Impact If Built
Raising the share of first-time buyers who return within 24 months from, say, 40% to 55% (estimate) adds several hundred service visits and dozens of trade-ups a year at a store selling 300 entry units.
