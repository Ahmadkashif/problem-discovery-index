# First-Time Boat Owners & New Riders

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/powersports-and-boat-dealers|Powersports & Boat Dealers]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a first-time boater or rider using the unit past the second season, and whoever has the highest share of first-time buyers back in the store for service or a trade within 24 months takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1B a year in service, parts, accessory and trade-up gross from first-time buyers across the industry (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** First-time buyers are an estimated quarter to a third of new-unit buyers in entry segments such as pontoons, small fishing boats and youth and entry side-by-sides (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — buyers research online; ownership support after delivery is a handshake and an owner's manual
**Target Buyer:** Dealer Principal / Sales and Service Managers
**Automation Potential:** Medium — onboarding, reminders and training content automate; the delivery walk-through and first service stay in person

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A first-time boat owner has to learn launching, docking, trailering, fuel, winterization and the rules of the water; a new rider has to learn the machine and get licensed. NMMA's CEO said 2025's weakness hit entry-level buyers hardest, "where financing costs have the greatest impact" (search summary of NMMA coverage). Coast Guard figures attribute about 70% of 2024 boating deaths to boats whose operators had no safety instruction (search summary). Buyers who stop using the unit sell it privately, skip service and never trade up. Boat clubs such as Freedom Boat Club, whose franchisees Brunswick Acceptance also finances (Brunswick 10-K), compete for the same customer by removing ownership altogether. Tools aimed at dealers sell the first unit; few serve the owner afterwards. The RV twin is [[niches/rv-dealerships/first-time-rv-buyers/profile|First-Time RV Buyers]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
OEM owner apps and connected-vehicle programmes, Discover Boating and rider-training courses, dealer CRMs and texting tools. Gaps: no structured first-season programme tied to the unit and the buyer's experience, and no measure of whether first-time buyers come back.
