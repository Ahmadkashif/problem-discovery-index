# OEM Warranty & Recall Claim Filing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/powersports-and-boat-dealers|Powersports & Boat Dealers]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get warranty and recall claims paid across every OEM line a dealer carries, and whoever has the highest share of claim dollars paid on first submission and kept through audit takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$800M a year in warranty and recall labour and parts paid to powersports and marine dealers, plus the administrator time to claim it (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1–1.5% of receipts, concentrated in the service department's gross (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — claims are filed online, but by re-keying repair orders into each OEM's portal
**Target Buyer:** Service Manager / Warranty Administrator
**Automation Potential:** High — claim drafting, coding, photo checklists and tracking are rule-bound

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A franchised car store files against one OEM's policy manual ([[niches/franchised-car-dealers/warranty-claims-chargebacks/profile|Warranty Claims & Chargeback Defense]]). A multi-line powersports store files against several, and a marine store files against the hull OEM and the engine OEM on the same boat, through portals such as the Polaris dealer extranet, BRP's BOSSWeb and Mercury's MercNET (recalled), each with its own labour-time tables, failure codes, photo and parts-return rules and filing windows. Recalls arrive from three regulators: CPSC for off-road vehicles, NHTSA for on-road motorcycles and the Coast Guard for boats and engines (recalled), and units in stock must often be fixed before sale. The work is mechanical, recurring and under-staffed.

## Current Tools & Gaps
DMS warranty modules (Lightspeed, DX1, Blackpurl, IDS, Dockmaster); OEM portals; outside warranty administrators for larger stores. Gaps: no single drafting step across OEMs, no rejection-risk check before submission, no tracking of parts returns and filing windows across lines, and no record of why claims were rejected.
