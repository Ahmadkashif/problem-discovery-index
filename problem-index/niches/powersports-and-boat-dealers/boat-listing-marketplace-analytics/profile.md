# Boat Listing Marketplace & Broker MLS Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/powersports-and-boat-dealers|Powersports & Boat Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Boats Group runs Boat Trader, YachtWorld and boats.com (search summary) and the broker listing service behind YachtWorld, where yacht brokers share listings and report sold boats. Its data teams turn listing, price-change, enquiry and sold records into pricing guidance, market reports, lead scoring and dealer performance dashboards. Dealers and brokers pay for listings and leads; the analytics support that product.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 analysts and data scientists (estimate)
**Output:** Price guidance, days-to-sell and demand reports, lead scoring, dealer dashboards, published market reports
**Proprietary data:** Asking prices, price changes, time on market and enquiries across most US boats listed for sale; broker-reported sold prices
**Clock:** Seasonal listing cycles and the firm's own release schedule
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Head of Insights
