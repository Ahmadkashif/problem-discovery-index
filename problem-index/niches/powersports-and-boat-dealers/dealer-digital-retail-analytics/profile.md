# Dealer Website, Lead & Digital Retail Analytics Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/powersports-and-boat-dealers|Powersports & Boat Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors that run dealer websites, lead handling, texting and digital retail for powersports and marine stores — Dealer Spike (claims 6,800+ dealers), Ekho, Kenect and BoatBot among them — and sell reporting on traffic, leads, conversion and campaign results. Dealer Spike's open roles in October 2026 included a Senior Director of Revenue Analytics (remote US) and engineers for web analytics and enterprise reporting (remote Mexico).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–20 analytics and reporting staff at the largest vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Dealer dashboards, lead and conversion reports, OEM programme reports, benchmark comparisons
**Proprietary data:** Website sessions, inventory views, leads and message threads across thousands of dealer sites
**Clock:** None external; monthly reporting set by the vendor
**Buyer:** VP of Product / Head of Analytics
