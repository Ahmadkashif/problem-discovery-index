# Spring Commissioning & Seasonal Service Capacity

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/powersports-and-boat-dealers|Powersports & Boat Dealers]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to fill the service department's technician hours through the spring commissioning peak and the winter trough, and whoever bills the most technician hours per technician across the year takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$4B a year in customer-pay and warranty service labour at powersports and marine dealers (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~6–7% of receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — repair orders are digital; the season is planned on a whiteboard and by phone
**Target Buyer:** Service Manager
**Automation Potential:** Medium-High for booking, status and forecasting

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The work is customer units — boats out of storage, bikes and side-by-sides out of garages — arriving in the same ~8–10 weeks (estimate), then a winterization wave in autumn and a trough in between. Technicians are scarce (71% of powersports dealers short of technicians in 2023, Powersports Business) and mostly paid flat rate. The contest is filling the year, not a day. Status calls are a large share of the interruption load, which is the same pain the car dealer's service drive has ([[niches/franchised-car-dealers/service-appointment-capture/profile|Service Appointment Capture]]), but here the binding constraint is seasonal capacity, not phone answering. Slip and dry-stack storage operations belong to the queued Marinas industry; this niche is the dealer's own service department, including winterization and storage contracts the dealer sells.

## Current Tools & Gaps
DMS service schedulers; texting and AI answering (Kenect, BoatBot); pick-up and delivery by phone. Gaps: no demand forecast for the season, no pre-booking engine that moves commissioning into February and March, and no automated status updates tied to the repair order.
