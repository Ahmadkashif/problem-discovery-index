# Specialist Boat & Powersports Value Guides

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/powersports-and-boat-dealers|Powersports & Boat Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Category-specific used-value guides outside the J.D. Power and Black Book family: BUC values for boats, and National Powersport Auctions' Value Guide, built on its own dealer-auction results and sold to dealers through a membership (search summary: over 75,000 transactions a year across 26,000+ models). Dealers, lenders and surveyors look them up on trades, loans and insurance values.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analytical staff per guide (estimate); the guides are by-products of an auction or listing business
**Output:** Used-value lookups and reports
**Proprietary data:** NPA's own auction results; BUC's compiled sales reports
**Clock:** Self-set publication cadence
**Buyer:** Dealer used-unit manager
