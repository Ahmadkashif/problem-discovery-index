# Marine & Powersports Dealer Group Corporate Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/powersports-and-boat-dealers|Powersports & Boat Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The corporate finance, inventory and data teams at consolidated dealer groups: OneWater Marine (95 dealerships in 17 states), MarineMax (over 70 retail dealership locations and 65+ marinas and storage sites) and the larger powersports groups (10-Ks for OneWater and MarineMax). They plan stocking orders across stores, move aged units between stores, price used inventory, track floorplan and integrate acquisitions. The output serves the group's own decisions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 analysts per group (estimate)
**Output:** Inventory allocation and aging plans, used-unit pricing, store performance and acquisition models, lender and investor reporting
**Proprietary data:** Appraisal, reconditioning and realised-price histories and floorplan aging across dozens of stores
**Clock:** The group's own planning calendar; quarterly results for the public groups
**Buyer:** CFO / VP of Inventory or Pre-Owned
