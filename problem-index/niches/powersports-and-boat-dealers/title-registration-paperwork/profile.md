# Title, Registration & Multi-State Deal Paperwork

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/powersports-and-boat-dealers|Powersports & Boat Dealers]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to file every title, registration and tax form a recreational deal needs inside each state's deadline, and whoever has the fewest filings rejected or penalised by the state per hundred deals takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M a year in title-clerk time, filing and runner fees, penalties and paperwork software across the industry (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** <1% of receipts, touching every unit sold (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — paper manufacturer's statements of origin, wet signatures, mailed or walked-in packets to several agencies
**Target Buyer:** Office Manager / Title Clerk
**Automation Potential:** High for form preparation and tracking; agency acceptance stays manual in many states

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A car deal needs one title and one registration at one agency. A boat deal can need a hull title and registration, often through a state natural-resources or wildlife agency rather than the DMV; a separate trailer title at the DMV; an outboard title in a few states; Coast Guard documentation instead of a state title for larger vessels; and sales tax handling for buyers from out of state, common at boat shows. Powersports deals add on-road titles for motorcycles and OHV or snowmobile registrations for off-road units (all recalled). Each agency has its own forms, fees and deadline, commonly 20–30 days (estimate). The independent car-dealer version, [[niches/auto-dealers-independent/title-registration-processing/profile|Title & Registration Processing]], deals with one vehicle record per deal.

## Current Tools & Gaps
DMS deal modules print state forms; electronic registration in some states for on-road units; Ekho sells DMV paperwork as part of digital powersports retail; title services and runners. Gaps: no single tracker of every filing a deal needs across agencies, no completeness check before submission, and no alert before a deadline.
