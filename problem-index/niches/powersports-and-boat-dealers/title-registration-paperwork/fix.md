# The Out-of-State Boat Show Buyer's Packet

**Niche:** [[niches/powersports-and-boat-dealers/title-registration-paperwork/profile|Title, Registration & Multi-State Deal Paperwork]]
**Industry:** [[industries/powersports-and-boat-dealers|Powersports & Boat Dealers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A buyer from another state signs at the boat show, and the clerk spends days working out which state's forms, taxes and agencies apply.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #compliance #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to file every title, registration and tax form a recreational deal needs inside each state's deadline, and whoever has the fewest filings rejected or penalised by the state per hundred deals takes the account.

## The Problem
Boat shows draw buyers from neighbouring states (recalled). Their deals need the home state's registration and title forms, a decision on where sales tax is collected, and sometimes a temporary permit to use the boat before registration arrives.

## Why It's Still Broken
Each out-of-state deal is rare enough at any one store that the clerk relearns the rules each time, from agency websites and phone calls.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A deal-time checklist for the buyer's state: forms pre-filled, tax treatment stated with its source, temporary-use options, the agency address or portal, and the deadline, generated when the deal is written at the show.

## Who Feels the Pain
Title clerks after every show; sales managers whose show deals stall; buyers who can't put the boat in the water.

## Impact If Fixed
Turning a two-to-three-day research job into a 20-minute checklist (estimate) for each of the 20–40 out-of-state show deals a busy store writes saves weeks of clerk time a year.
