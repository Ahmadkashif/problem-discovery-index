# Coast Guard Boating Safety & Recreational Product Recall Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/powersports-and-boat-dealers|Powersports & Boat Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Coast Guard's recreational boating safety office sets manufacturer standards, runs factory compliance visits and defect notification and recall oversight for boats and associated equipment, and publishes the annual Recreational Boating Statistics from state accident reports (its 2024 report counted 556 deaths and 3,887 incidents, search summary). CPSC plays the recall role for ATVs and side-by-sides and NHTSA for on-road motorcycles (recalled). Dealers feel these bodies as recall campaigns and stop-sales on stock.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 statistics and analysis staff (estimate); factory-compliance inspectors and recall case officers are production and are not counted
**Output:** Annual statistics report, compliance findings, recall determinations, standards
**Proprietary data:** National accident database compiled from state reports; factory compliance and defect files
**Clock:** Statutory annual reporting and recall notification obligations
**Buyer:** Office chief (appropriated budget)
