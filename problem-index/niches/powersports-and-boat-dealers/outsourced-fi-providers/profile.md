# Outsourced F&I Providers for Small Dealers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/powersports-and-boat-dealers|Powersports & Boat Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Financial service providers that run finance and insurance for small powersports, marine and RV dealers without their own F&I staff: they take applications, place loans with lenders, sell protection products and prepare paperwork, earning a share of reserve and product income. Medallion Bank receives about half of its recreation loan volume through such providers (Medallion 10-K).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Production F&I processors; analysis, where it exists, is a few people per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Funded loans and sold protection products
**Proprietary data:** Deal and credit files held for lenders and dealers
**Clock:** Lender funding deadlines on each deal
**Buyer:** Dealer principal
