# Auto Auction Condition Reports for Hulls and Engines

**Niche:** [[niches/powersports-and-boat-dealers/trade-in-appraisal-condition/profile|Trade-In Appraisal & Condition Assessment]]
**Industry:** [[industries/powersports-and-boat-dealers|Powersports & Boat Dealers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Wholesale car auctions grade every vehicle to a standard condition report that buyers trust sight unseen; boat and powersports trades get no standard report at all.
**Tags:** #cnns #object-detection #gradient-boosting #feature-engineering #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to price a used boat or machine at the moment of trade, and whoever has the smallest gap between the trade allowance plus reconditioning and the price the unit actually brings takes the account.

## The Problem
Dealers trading used boats and machines between stores, to wholesalers or to remote retail buyers rely on phone calls and photos, because there is no condition standard to price against.

## What Already Exists
Auto auction condition-report operations grade each vehicle with standardised damage codes and photos, increasingly from camera tunnels and vision models, and arbitration later tests the report ([[niches/auto-dealers-independent/auction-condition-report-ops/profile|Wholesale Auction Condition Report Operations]]).

## The Customization Gap
Needed: a condition schema for hulls (deck and transom firmness, moisture by zone, gelcoat, stringers, through-hulls), engines (hours by RPM band, compression, fault history, lower-unit oil), trailers, and powersports drivetrains and frames; capture designed for a trade desk, not a camera tunnel; marine-surveyor terminology so lenders and insurers recognise it; and grades calibrated against realised prices rather than arbitration claims.

## Target Customer
Dealer groups moving used units between stores; wholesale buyers and remarketing desks at lenders.

## Impact If Solved
A shared condition grade lets a group price inter-store transfers without a second inspection, saving ~1 day and one trip per unit on ~200–400 transfers a year at a 20-store group (estimate).
