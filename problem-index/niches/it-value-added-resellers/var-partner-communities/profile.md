# Distributor-Run VAR Partner Communities

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/it-value-added-resellers|IT Distributors & Value-Added Resellers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Communities that distributors run for small and mid-sized resellers — TD SYNNEX's Varnex and Ingram Micro's Trust X Alliance — offering pooled vendor programmes, events, peer benchmarking and marketing support in exchange for purchase commitments. They are the IT channel's nearest equivalent to buying groups, but run inside the distributor rather than owned by members.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 staff on member analytics and benchmarking per community (estimate)
**Output:** Member benchmarks, pooled programme terms, events
**Proprietary data:** Members' purchase and performance data with the distributor
**Clock:** Annual distributor programme years
**Buyer:** Community or partner-programme head at the distributor
