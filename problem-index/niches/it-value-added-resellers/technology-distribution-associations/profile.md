# Technology Distribution Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/it-value-added-resellers|IT Distributors & Value-Added Resellers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Industry bodies for IT distributors, chiefly the Global Technology Distribution Council, which represents the large distributors to vendors and publishes aggregate distribution sales trends from members' data. The solution-provider association, CompTIA, is logged at [[niches/it-managed-services/it-channel-association-research/profile|IT Channel Association Research]] (37) and not repeated.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research staff (estimate)
**Output:** Aggregate distribution sales trends, advocacy and events
**Proprietary data:** Members' submitted sales data
**Clock:** Self-set publication calendar
**Buyer:** Association chief executive
