# The End Customer Spelt Three Ways

**Niche:** [[niches/it-value-added-resellers/partner-pos-sellout-reporting/profile|Partner POS & Sell-Out Reporting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/it-value-added-resellers|IT Distributors & Value-Added Resellers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The same hospital appears as three different end customers across the quote, the registration and the POS line, and the vendor rejects the credit because they don't match.
**Tags:** #word-embeddings #k-nearest-neighbors #data-integration #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to file each vendor's point-of-sale and sell-out report in that vendor's format with the end-customer and serial detail it requires, and whoever gets the highest share of reported lines accepted on first submission takes the account.

## The Problem
Reps type end-customer names freehand at each step. Vendors match POS lines to registrations and their account master by name and address, and mismatches break the link that pays the reseller.

## Why It's Still Broken
No one owns a clean end-customer master across CRM, quote, distributor order and POS file, and each vendor's master spells customers its own way.

## What a Fix Looks Like
One end-customer identity per account, matched to each vendor's account identifiers where available, and stamped onto every registration, order and POS line automatically.

## Who Feels the Pain
Distributor reporting teams, reseller finance, and reps whose credit goes missing.

## Impact If Fixed
Resolving end-customer identity once could remove a third or more of rejected POS lines (estimate) for a few weeks of data-cleaning work.
