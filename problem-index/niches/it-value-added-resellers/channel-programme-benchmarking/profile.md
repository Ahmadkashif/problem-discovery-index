# Channel Programme Benchmarking & Consulting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/it-value-added-resellers|IT Distributors & Value-Added Resellers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Research and advisory teams that benchmark IT vendors' partner programmes and advise channel chiefs on programme design, incentives and MDF: Canalys's channels practice (now within Omdia), The Channel Company's IPED consulting arm alongside CRN's Partner Program Guide, and smaller channel consultancies. Vendors pay for benchmarks, partner-sentiment research and programme redesigns, often before resets such as Cisco 360. The broader technology research firms (Gartner, Forrester, IDC) are logged at [[niches/cloud-infrastructure-consultants/it-research-advisory-firms/profile|IT Research & Advisory Firms]] (54, indexed) and not repeated.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 channel analysts and consultants at the largest providers (estimate)
**Output:** Partner-programme benchmarks, partner surveys and sentiment indices, incentive and MDF design recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Partner survey panels and programme benchmark histories (theirs); vendor programme and partner performance data shared in consulting engagements (the client's)
**Clock:** Own research calendar; vendors' fiscal-year programme launches are the clients' dates
**Buyer:** Head of channels research or practice lead
