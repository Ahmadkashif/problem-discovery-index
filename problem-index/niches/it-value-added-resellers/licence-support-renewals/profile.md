# Licence & Support Contract Renewals

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/it-value-added-resellers|IT Distributors & Value-Added Resellers]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to renew each customer's expiring software licences, cloud subscriptions and hardware support contracts before the vendor's renewal date, and whoever renews the largest share of expiring contract value on time takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$60B a year of software licence, cloud subscription and support-contract renewals through US resellers and distributors (estimate); much of it is booked net, so it carries a larger share of resellers' gross profit than of revenue
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of receipts at gross value (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — subscriptions sit in Partner Center and distributor platforms; renewal calendars, co-terms and interpretation of programme changes live in spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** VP of software or renewals at the reseller; cloud business unit head at the distributor
**Automation Potential:** High — expiry tracking, eligibility checks and renewal quotes are rule-bound once the rules are current

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Renewals are the recurring core of a reseller's software and support business, and the vendors keep moving the ground under them. Microsoft's Microsoft 365 price increase applies at each renewal after 1 July 2026, and Microsoft says timely renewals recapture 20% more revenue than late ones (its own figure); its growth margins from October 2026 depend on per-tenant eligibility locked at the transaction. Broadcom's VMware renewals carry a 72-core minimum and a 20% late penalty, according to Arrow's notice to partners. Cisco support contracts must co-term. The clock is the vendor's renewal date, the measure is renewed value on time, and the licensing resellers (SoftwareOne, Crayon), large VARs and cloud distributors (Pax8, TD SYNNEX, Ingram Micro) all compete on it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Microsoft Partner Center and distributor subscription platforms, PSA and CRM renewal fields, vendor install-base and contract reports, licence-optimisation tools sold to end customers (Flexera, CoreView). The gaps: expiries spread across portals, programme changes interpreted by one specialist, eligibility checked by hand, and no ranking of which renewals will arrive late.
