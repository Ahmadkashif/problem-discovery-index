# IT Equipment Lessors' Residual-Value Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/it-value-added-resellers|IT Distributors & Value-Added Resellers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Captive and independent lessors that finance IT equipment sold through resellers — the vendors' financing arms (Dell Financial Services, HPE Financial Services, Cisco Capital) and independent lessors — price leases on a forecast of what servers, storage, network gear and laptops will be worth at return. Their asset-management teams forecast residuals, manage returns and remarket used equipment. Resellers use the financing to close deals; the residual forecast stays inside the lessor's pricing.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–50 residual-value, remarketing and asset-management analysts per large lessor (estimate)
**Output:** Residual forecasts by product family and term; return and remarketing decisions
**Proprietary data:** Returned-asset condition records and remarketing prices across many lease cohorts
**Clock:** Lease maturities in the lessor's own book
**Buyer:** Head of asset management or residual risk
