# The MDF Claim Rejected for Missing Proof

**Niche:** [[niches/it-value-added-resellers/vendor-incentive-mdf-claims/profile|Vendor Rebates, Incentives & MDF Claims]]
**Industry:** [[industries/it-value-added-resellers|IT Distributors & Value-Added Resellers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A reseller runs the event or campaign the vendor approved, then loses the reimbursement because the invoices, attendee lists and screenshots weren't filed in time.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to claim every rebate, back-end incentive and market development fund reimbursement the reseller earned under each vendor's programme terms before the claim window closes, and whoever recovers the largest share of earned incentive dollars takes the account.

## The Problem
MDF requires pre-approval, the activity, and proof of performance — invoices, attendee lists, leads, photos, screenshots — submitted within a window. Marketing staff run the activity and move on; the evidence scatters across inboxes.

## Why It's Still Broken
Each vendor's proof requirements differ and live in its programme guide; nobody at a small reseller owns claims as a job.

## What a Fix Looks Like
At approval, generate the vendor's proof checklist and deadline; collect evidence as it is created (invoices from accounting, registrations from the event tool, leads from the CRM); assemble and file the claim before the window closes, flagging gaps early.

## Who Feels the Pain
Reseller marketing managers, finance, and account managers who promised the vendor the campaign.

## Impact If Fixed
If even a quarter of the unused MDF the ZINFI survey describes is lost to filing rather than to unspent budget, recovering it is worth ~$20–60K a year to a reseller with $200K of annual MDF allocations (estimate).
