# Rebate Platforms Rebuilt for Partner Tiers and Specialisations

**Niche:** [[niches/it-value-added-resellers/vendor-incentive-mdf-claims/profile|Vendor Rebates, Incentives & MDF Claims]]
**Industry:** [[industries/it-value-added-resellers|IT Distributors & Value-Added Resellers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Rebate and incentive platforms already compute volume rebates; IT programmes pay on conditions they don't model.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #feature-engineering #data-integration #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to claim every rebate, back-end incentive and market development fund reimbursement the reseller earned under each vendor's programme terms before the claim window closes, and whoever recovers the largest share of earned incentive dollars takes the account.

## The Problem
An IT vendor's incentive depends on the reseller's tier, certified headcount, specialisations, accepted POS reporting and, now, per-customer growth. A volume-rebate engine sees only spend.

## What Already Exists
Enable and Vistex for rebate administration, mainly on the vendor side; Model N for high-tech channel incentives; PRM platforms with MDF modules (Impartner, Zift, ChannelScaler).

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: tier and certification status as rule inputs; eligible-offer lists that change at each reset; POS acceptance as a precondition; Microsoft-style per-tenant growth tests checked before the quote; and the reseller's view across many vendors rather than one vendor's view across many partners.

## Target Customer
Finance and vendor-programme teams at mid-sized and large resellers.

## Impact If Solved
One reseller-side incentive engine replaces a spreadsheet per vendor and could cut monthly reconciliation time by ~half (estimate).
