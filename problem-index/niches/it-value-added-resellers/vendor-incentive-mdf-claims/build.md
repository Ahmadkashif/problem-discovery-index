# An Earned-Versus-Claimed Ledger for Every Vendor Programme

**Niche:** [[niches/it-value-added-resellers/vendor-incentive-mdf-claims/profile|Vendor Rebates, Incentives & MDF Claims]]
**Industry:** [[industries/it-value-added-resellers|IT Distributors & Value-Added Resellers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A reseller knows what vendors paid it, not what it earned, so unclaimed incentives are invisible until the window has closed.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #random-forests #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #compliance #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to claim every rebate, back-end incentive and market development fund reimbursement the reseller earned under each vendor's programme terms before the claim window closes, and whoever recovers the largest share of earned incentive dollars takes the account.

## The Problem
Each vendor's programme pays on different products, tiers, growth tests and reporting conditions, and resets every year or two. The finance team reconciles what arrived against a spreadsheet estimate, so incentives that were earned but never claimed — because a POS line was rejected, a registration number was missing, or an eligible product was not on last year's list — never show up.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Rebate platforms were built for vendors and manufacturers administering programmes, not for a reseller holding twenty of them; programme rules are prose, not data.

## What to Build
Programme guides parsed into rules (eligible offers, rates, tier and specialisation conditions, claim windows) with source text for review; invoiced lines and POS acceptances applied to them; a ledger of earned, claimed, paid and at-risk incentive by vendor and programme, with deadline alerts; and a model that flags lines likely to be rejected before submission.

## Target Customer
CFOs and controllers at resellers with $50M+ of vendor-incentive-bearing sales.

## Impact If Built
Recovering even 5% more of earned incentives is worth ~$100–300K a year for a reseller earning $2–6M in incentives (estimate).
