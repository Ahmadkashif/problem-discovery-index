# Vendor Rebates, Incentives & MDF Claims

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/it-value-added-resellers|IT Distributors & Value-Added Resellers]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to claim every rebate, back-end incentive and market development fund reimbursement the reseller earned under each vendor's programme terms before the claim window closes, and whoever recovers the largest share of earned incentive dollars takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$8–15B a year of rebates, back-end programme incentives and MDF earned by US IT resellers and distributors (estimate); TD SYNNEX alone carried $972.7M of receivables from vendors at the end of FY2025. Labour and software spent claiming it ~$400M (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2–3% of receipts in earned incentives (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — programmes are PDFs and portals, accruals are spreadsheets, MDF proof of performance is screenshots and invoices
**Target Buyer:** CFO or controller at the reseller; vendor programme manager
**Automation Potential:** High — programme terms, POS matching and claim assembly are mechanical once rules are current

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
For many resellers, vendor incentives are a large share of net profit on thin hardware margins. IT programmes differ from the industrial and electrical claim work mapped at [[niches/industrial-distributors/rebates-and-spa-claims/profile|Supplier Rebates & SPA Claims]] and [[niches/electrical-distributors/spa-ship-and-debit-claims/profile|Manufacturer SPAs & Ship-and-Debit Claims]]: payment depends on partner tier, certifications and specialisations, accepted POS reporting, product eligibility lists and, increasingly, per-customer growth tests. Cisco consolidated its rebates into one Cisco Partner Incentive with an Eligible Offers list from January 2026; Microsoft replaced a Business Premium security rebate with a 7.5% promotional price from October 2026. MDF is the other half: a vendor survey by ZINFI found 60% of MDF goes unused each quarter (search summary). Third-party claim administration is logged once, at [[niches/industrial-distributors/rebate-spa-claim-administration/profile|Rebate & SPA Claim Administration Services]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Vendor programme portals and PRM platforms, distributor rebate statements, ERP accruals, spreadsheets. The gaps: programme rules keyed by hand after each reset, no earned-versus-claimed ledger, POS rejections discovered after the window, and MDF claims assembled from scattered evidence.
