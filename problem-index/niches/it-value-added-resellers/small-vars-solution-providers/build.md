# A Win-Loss Memory for the Five-Person Reseller

**Niche:** [[niches/it-value-added-resellers/small-vars-solution-providers/profile|Small VARs & IT Solution Providers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/it-value-added-resellers|IT Distributors & Value-Added Resellers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A small reseller sends hundreds of quotes a year and never learns which ones it lost, to whom, or by how much.
**Tags:** #logistic-regression #gradient-boosting #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to be the system a five-person reseller builds its quotes in, and whoever gives those resellers the highest quote win rate takes the account.

## The Problem
The owner quotes from instinct about price, margin and which distributor to source from. Lost quotes simply go quiet. Without outcomes, the owner can't tell whether it lost on price, product, timing or relationship.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Quoting tools record quotes, not outcomes; distributors see orders, not losses; a small reseller has too little volume for its own model.

## What to Build
Outcome capture in the quoting tool — a one-tap won, lost, to whom, why — plus inbox reading that infers outcomes from customer replies; then pooled win-probability models across many small resellers on the same platform, giving each owner a suggested margin and the likely win rate at it, with its own history weighted most.

## Target Customer
Owners of small resellers on a shared quoting or distributor platform, sold by the platform.

## Impact If Built
Pricing on evidence rather than instinct could lift a small reseller's win rate by a few points or add ~1 point of margin (estimate) — ~$30–60K a year on $3–6M of sales.
