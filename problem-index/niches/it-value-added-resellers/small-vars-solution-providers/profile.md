# Small VARs & IT Solution Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/it-value-added-resellers|IT Distributors & Value-Added Resellers]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to be the system a five-person reseller builds its quotes in, and whoever gives those resellers the highest quote win rate takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$13.6B in 2022 receipts across 4,062 firms with fewer than 20 employees in 423430 (Census SUSB 2022), plus thousands of small resellers filed as computer systems design or managed services firms (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of the code's receipts from ~80% of its firms
**Digital Adoption:** Low to medium — distributor portals and a PSA or accounting package; quotes in a spreadsheet or a low-cost quoting tool
**Target Buyer:** Owner-president, who is also the lead salesperson and often the engineer
**Automation Potential:** High — quote assembly, registration and renewals are the same mechanical work at smaller volume

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Four in five firms in the code have fewer than 20 employees and share 3% of receipts. The owner of a small reseller does every job the large reseller splits across specialists: requirement, configuration, pricing, registration, licensing and renewals. It has no deal-desk relationships, little leverage on special pricing, and depends on its distributor for credit, pricing tools and partner-community programmes (TD SYNNEX's Varnex, Ingram Micro's Trust X Alliance). Its contest is narrower than the large reseller's: winning the quotes it sends against bigger competitors and the vendors' own direct and marketplace channels. Many small resellers are also MSPs; their managed-service contracts belong to [[industries/it-managed-services|IT Managed Services]], their product resale here.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Distributor platforms (Ingram Micro Xvantage, TD SYNNEX, Pax8 and AppDirect for cloud), low-cost quoting tools (QuoteWerks, ScalePad's Quoter, Salesbuildr, Zomentum, Quote.ly), PSAs (ConnectWise, Kaseya's Autotask, HaloPSA). The gaps: no win-loss record, quoting tools that price but don't advise, registration and renewals tracked in the owner's head, and AI vendors' integration work priced for larger firms.
