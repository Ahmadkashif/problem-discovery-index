# The Owner Who Quotes After Hours

**Niche:** [[niches/it-value-added-resellers/small-vars-solution-providers/profile|Small VARs & IT Solution Providers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/it-value-added-resellers|IT Distributors & Value-Added Resellers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The owner of a small reseller spends the day with customers and builds quotes at night, so quotes go out late and get the least checking.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #worker-facing #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to be the system a five-person reseller builds its quotes in, and whoever gives those resellers the highest quote win rate takes the account.

## The Problem
Every quote means pulling distributor prices from two portals, copying lines, checking registrations and writing the proposal. The owner does it after hours, a day or two after the customer asked.

## Why It's Still Broken
Low-cost quoting tools still need the lines typed or pasted; the owner can't justify an integration project; distributors' tools stop at their own catalogue.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Forward the customer's email or the distributor quote to an assistant that drafts the priced quote from both distributors, applies the reseller's usual margin and registrations, and returns it for a five-minute review.

## Who Feels the Pain
Owners and their one or two salespeople; customers waiting for a price.

## Impact If Fixed
Cutting quote preparation from ~45 to ~10 minutes (estimate) gives an owner back ~5 hours a week and gets quotes out the same day.
