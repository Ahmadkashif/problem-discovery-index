# Channel Data Management Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/it-value-added-resellers|IT Distributors & Value-Added Resellers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms that collect, cleanse and report partners' point-of-sale and inventory data on behalf of IT vendors: E2open (which acquired Zyme), Model N, Vistex, CMR with ChannelPOS and PartnerAvenue, and PRM platforms with sell-out modules. They receive files from distributors and resellers in every format, match end customers and products, and deliver sell-out reporting, incentive calculations and channel inventory views to the vendor that hired them. The reporting work on the partner side is the level-1 niche [[niches/it-value-added-resellers/partner-pos-sellout-reporting/profile|Partner POS & Sell-Out Reporting]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 channel analytics consultants at the largest provider (estimate); data-operations staff who cleanse and match POS lines are production and are not counted
**Output:** Cleansed sell-out data, channel inventory and performance reports, incentive calculations for the vendor client
**Proprietary data:** POS and inventory reports from thousands of partners, processed for each vendor client under its service agreement
**Clock:** Vendor clients' quarterly programme and claim cycles — clients' clocks
**Buyer:** Head of channel data or analytics services at the provider

**Kill switches:** **data isn't theirs** — live. The partner POS they process is furnished to each IT vendor under that vendor's programme terms and handled on its behalf under a service agreement; no checked terms give the vendor client, let alone the processor, product rights over it. Unlike the subscription half of [[niches/consumer-products-makers/pos-data-harmonisation-platforms/profile|Retail POS Data Harmonisation Platforms]] (34, terms undetermined), the collection here is a service run for one client at a time.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. A data-processing business with a small analytical layer, a handful of providers and other people's data. None was confirmed hiring engineers on its own page on 2026-10-24.
