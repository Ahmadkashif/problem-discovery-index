# A Contract-Aware Catalogue Behind Every Public Quote

**Niche:** [[niches/it-value-added-resellers/public-sector-contract-vehicles/profile|Public-Sector Contract Vehicles]]
**Industry:** [[industries/it-value-added-resellers|IT Distributors & Value-Added Resellers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A reseller with five vehicles keeps five price lists by hand, and no tool tells the rep which vehicle a quote line is legal on today.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #word-embeddings #k-nearest-neighbors #compliance #data-integration #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to quote every public-sector order from the right contract vehicle at an allowed contract price, and whoever has the lowest share of orders rejected or modified for off-contract items or prices takes the account.

## The Problem
Each vehicle's awarded SKU list and ceiling prices change by modification. Quotes are checked against them by a contracts administrator, late and by hand, and errors surface as rejected orders or reporting findings.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Catalogue vendors price from distributor feeds; government portals publish their own catalogues; nobody joins the vendor's SKU churn to each reseller's contract lines.

## What to Build
A catalogue that holds every awarded line per vehicle with its ceiling and modification date; matches each vendor SKU to its contract line, including replacements and successors; checks every public quote line for vehicle, eligibility and ceiling before it is sent; drafts the modification request when a needed SKU is missing; and assembles each vehicle's sales report from invoiced lines.

## Target Customer
Contracts directors at resellers holding three or more federal, state and cooperative vehicles.

## Impact If Built
Cutting off-contract rejections from ~3–8% of public orders to under 1% (estimate) keeps a few million dollars a year of a $100M public book from slipping a quarter, and halves the contracts desk's upkeep.
