# The End-of-Sale SKU Still on the Contract

**Niche:** [[niches/it-value-added-resellers/public-sector-contract-vehicles/profile|Public-Sector Contract Vehicles]]
**Industry:** [[industries/it-value-added-resellers|IT Distributors & Value-Added Resellers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The vendor ends sale of a model, its successor isn't on the vehicle yet, and every agency order for that product stalls until a modification clears.
**Tags:** #word-embeddings #k-nearest-neighbors #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to quote every public-sector order from the right contract vehicle at an allowed contract price, and whoever has the lowest share of orders rejected or modified for off-contract items or prices takes the account.

## The Problem
Vendors publish end-of-sale dates and successor SKUs months ahead; contract modifications to add successors take weeks; contracts teams notice when an order fails.

## Why It's Still Broken
End-of-sale notices go to product managers and sales, not to the contracts desk, and nobody maps them to the vehicles' awarded lines.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Watch vendor end-of-sale notices and price-list changes, map each affected SKU to the vehicles where it is awarded, propose the successor match, and file modification requests ahead of the end-of-sale date.

## Who Feels the Pain
Contracts administrators, public-sector reps, and agency buyers who can't order.

## Impact If Fixed
Filing successor modifications before the end-of-sale date avoids weeks of stalled orders per product transition (estimate: several transitions a year per vehicle).
