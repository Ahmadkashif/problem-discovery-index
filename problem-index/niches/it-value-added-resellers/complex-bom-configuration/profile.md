# Complex BOM Configuration & Site Fit

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/it-value-added-resellers|IT Distributors & Value-Added Resellers]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to send solution quotes whose bills of materials install as quoted at the customer's site, and whoever ships the lowest share of quoted lines needing a post-order change order or return takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.5B a year of pre-sales engineering time spent building and reviewing solution BOMs at US resellers, against ~$2–5B a year of post-order change orders, returns and repeat site visits on solution deals (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.4% of receipts in engineering time (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — configurators are digital, the fit review is a senior engineer's eye and a chat thread
**Target Buyer:** VP of pre-sales or solutions architecture at the reseller
**Automation Potential:** Medium — retrieval of similar past configurations is near-term; trusted fit flags need a labelled history

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The vendor's configurator answers "are these parts valid together?" The reseller has to answer "will this install at this customer?" — optics coded for the customer's switches, power cords for its PDUs, rails for its cabinets, licence tiers for the features it asked for, support that co-terms with what it already runs. The answer is tacit knowledge from installs, held by a few senior engineers, and the outcome is measured weeks later on site. The winner is whoever's quotes need the fewest corrections after the order, whether that is a reseller with deep benches or a tool that gives a thin bench the same memory.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Vendor configurators and compatibility matrices, channel CPQ, the customer's asset inventory where one exists, and vendor install-base reports. The gaps: expert corrections unrecorded, install outcomes never joined to quote lines, and the customer's site context kept in people's heads.
