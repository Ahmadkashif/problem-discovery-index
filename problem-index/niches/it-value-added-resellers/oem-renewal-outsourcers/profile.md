# OEM Renewal Outsourcers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/it-value-added-resellers|IT Distributors & Value-Added Resellers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Firms that run support-contract and subscription renewals for IT vendors on commission or fee: managing the install base, quoting renewals, chasing resellers and customers before contracts lapse. ServiceSource, now part of Concentrix, is the best-known; distributors and business-process outsourcers run similar renewal desks. The vendor pays for renewal bookings, not for the analysis. The reseller-side renewal contest is the level-1 niche [[niches/it-value-added-resellers/licence-support-renewals/profile|Licence & Support Contract Renewals]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 renewal analytics and install-base data staff at the largest provider (estimate); renewal sales reps working each contract are production and are not counted
**Output:** Renewal forecasts, install-base cleansing, at-risk lists for the vendor client
**Proprietary data:** Install-base, contract and renewal history across vendor clients, held under each client's service agreement
**Clock:** Contract expiry dates of the vendor client's customers — clients' clocks
**Buyer:** Head of renewal analytics or client services at the outsourcer
