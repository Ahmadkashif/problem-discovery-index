# Federal IT Contract Administration & Category Management

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/it-value-added-resellers|IT Distributors & Value-Added Resellers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The federal offices that set the terms resellers sell under: GSA's Federal Acquisition Service IT category, which runs the Multiple Award Schedule, analyses vendors' transactional price data and negotiates OneGov agreements directly with OEMs; NASA's SEWP programme office, which awarded SEWP VI to 2,115 winners with ordering from 1 November 2026; and the inspectors general who audit schedule pricing. Their analysis shapes ceiling prices, vehicle terms and, under OneGov, who holds the prime contract.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~30–100 category-management, pricing and data analysts across GSA's IT category (estimate); contracting officers negotiating each schedule and order are production and are not counted
**Output:** Pricing analyses and benchmarks, category strategies, negotiated government-wide agreements
**Proprietary data:** Transaction-level price data reported by schedule holders, award and order history across agencies
**Clock:** Federal fiscal-year and contract-period calendars set by the agencies themselves
**Buyer:** Assistant commissioner for the IT category

**Kill switches:** none — government procurement is not a switch (lesson n). The transactional price data schedule holders report is confidential commercial information, protected from disclosure (the Trade Secrets Act and FOIA exemption 4), so statutory confidentiality applies to sharing it outside government; it does not apply to GSA's own analysis, because the data is reported to GSA for exactly this use. The judgement is that the switch is not live for this pocket; were it judged live, the score would still be 25 and the pocket would stay out of the index.
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. One buyer, no invoice, and calendars it sets for itself. Its pricing data is the most complete view of what the government pays resellers, which is why OneGov can bypass them.
