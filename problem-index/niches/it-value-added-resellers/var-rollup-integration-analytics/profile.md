# VAR & Solution-Provider Rollup Integration Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/it-value-added-resellers|IT Distributors & Value-Added Resellers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Private-equity-backed resellers that grow by buying regional VARs and integrators, and the corporate development and integration teams inside them. They screen targets, model vendor-programme tiers and incentive economics after a merger, and track synergy in sourcing, distributor terms and services attach. The analysis serves the platform's own decisions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 corporate development, pricing and integration analysts per platform (estimate)
**Output:** Target screens, post-merger programme-tier and incentive models, synergy tracking
**Proprietary data:** Combined transaction, margin and vendor-programme history across the acquired resellers
**Clock:** Deal timetables, lender covenants and sponsor holding periods
**Buyer:** Head of corporate development or chief strategy officer
