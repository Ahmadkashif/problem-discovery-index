# IT Channel Media Research & Rankings

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/it-value-added-resellers|IT Distributors & Value-Added Resellers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Trade publishers covering the IT channel — CRN and its Solution Provider 500 from The Channel Company, Channel Futures and its MSP 501, and smaller outlets — publish rankings, survey reports and news, paid for by vendor advertising, events and sponsored research. Their consulting arms are covered in [[niches/it-value-added-resellers/channel-programme-benchmarking/profile|Channel Programme Benchmarking & Consulting]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research analysts per publisher (estimate); editorial staff are writers, not analysts
**Output:** Rankings, survey reports, news
**Proprietary data:** Ranking submissions and reader surveys
**Clock:** Self-set ranking and event calendar
**Buyer:** Publisher or head of research
