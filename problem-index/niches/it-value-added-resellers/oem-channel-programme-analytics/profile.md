# IT Vendors' Channel & Partner-Programme Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/it-value-added-resellers|IT Distributors & Value-Added Resellers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The channel, partner-programme and incentive analytics teams inside the large IT vendors — Cisco, HPE, Dell, Lenovo, Microsoft's partner organisation, the security and storage vendors. They analyse partners' point-of-sale reports, deal registrations and special-pricing history to set programme tiers, incentive rates and eligible-offer lists, and to judge which resellers grow the business. Their decisions arrive at resellers as programme resets such as Cisco 360 (January 2026) and Microsoft's growth margins (October 2026).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–80 channel and programme analysts per large IT vendor (estimate); deal-desk analysts who price each special-pricing request and adjudicate claims are production and are not counted
**Output:** Programme and incentive design, partner segmentation, sell-out and registration analysis
**Proprietary data:** Partner POS and sell-out reports, registrations and special-pricing requests across every reseller in the vendor's channel, including competing requests on the same deal
**Clock:** Internal quarterly programme and pricing cycles
**Buyer:** Chief partner officer or VP of channel operations
