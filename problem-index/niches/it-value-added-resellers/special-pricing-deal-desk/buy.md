# PRM Turned Around to Face the Reseller

**Niche:** [[niches/it-value-added-resellers/special-pricing-deal-desk/profile|Deal Registration & Special-Pricing Bids]]
**Industry:** [[industries/it-value-added-resellers|IT Distributors & Value-Added Resellers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Partner relationship management platforms run registration for each vendor; the reseller dealing with twenty vendors has no equivalent on its side.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #word-embeddings #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get each registered deal and special-pricing request approved by the vendor at a price that beats the competing resellers, and whoever wins the highest share of its registered opportunities takes the account.

## The Problem
Every vendor's registration form, rules and expiry differ. The reseller re-enters the same customer and opportunity in each, tracks status by email, and finds lapses late.

## What Already Exists
Impartner, Zift, ChannelScaler (formerly Allbound and Channel Mechanics), Introw and Vartopia, sold to vendors to run their programmes; CRM opportunity records at the reseller.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: one opportunity mapped to each vendor's fields and product taxonomy; each programme's rules (first to register, incumbency, size floors, tier eligibility, extension limits) checked before filing; customer entity matching across portals; status and expiry pulled back into the CRM; and the approved number carried to the distributor order line.

## Target Customer
Inside sales and alliance operations teams at resellers registering with ten or more vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Filing once and tracking centrally saves ~2–4 hours per rep per week (estimate) and stops registrations lapsing unseen.
