# The Registration That Lapsed Before the PO

**Niche:** [[niches/it-value-added-resellers/special-pricing-deal-desk/profile|Deal Registration & Special-Pricing Bids]]
**Industry:** [[industries/it-value-added-resellers|IT Distributors & Value-Added Resellers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A registration expires while the customer deliberates, and the reseller learns it when the distributor's price comes back without the protected discount.
**Tags:** #survival-analysis #workflow-orchestration #automation #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get each registered deal and special-pricing request approved by the vendor at a price that beats the competing resellers, and whoever wins the highest share of its registered opportunities takes the account.

## The Problem
Registrations last a fixed period (~60–90 days, estimate) and public-sector and enterprise deals often take longer. Extensions must be requested before expiry, and each portal reminds differently or not at all.

## Why It's Still Broken
The expiry lives in the vendor's portal and the deal's timeline in the reseller's CRM; nobody owns the join, and the rep is working on the next deal.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Pull every registration's expiry into the CRM opportunity, estimate from the reseller's history how long each deal type takes to close, and file extensions automatically where the deal is still alive. Flag any quote whose lines rely on a registration that expires before the expected PO date.

## Who Feels the Pain
Inside reps who requote at a worse price; account managers who lose deals to rivals who registered later; finance, which sees margin vanish.

## Impact If Fixed
Catching lapses before the PO could protect ~1% of hardware deals that now ship without the registered discount (estimate), worth ~$50–75K a year on a $50M hardware book.
