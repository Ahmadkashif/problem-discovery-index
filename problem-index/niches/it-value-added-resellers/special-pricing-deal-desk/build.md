# Approval Odds Before the Request Is Filed

**Niche:** [[niches/it-value-added-resellers/special-pricing-deal-desk/profile|Deal Registration & Special-Pricing Bids]]
**Industry:** [[industries/it-value-added-resellers|IT Distributors & Value-Added Resellers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Account managers ask deal desks for discounts by instinct, with no record of what each vendor approved on similar deals at similar points in its quarter.
**Tags:** #logistic-regression #gradient-boosting #causal-inference #confidence-intervals #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get each registered deal and special-pricing request approved by the vendor at a price that beats the competing resellers, and whoever wins the highest share of its registered opportunities takes the account.

## The Problem
Ask for too little and the reseller leaves margin on the table or loses to a rival who asked for more; ask for too much and the request stalls or is cut. The experienced account manager's sense of what a vendor's deal desk will approve — by product family, deal size, competitor and week of the vendor's quarter — is the reseller's best pricing asset and is never written down.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Approvals sit in vendor portals and email. Vendors see all resellers' requests but have no reason to tell any one of them its odds; resellers never assembled their own history.

## What to Build
A reseller-side ledger of every registration and special-pricing request with requested and approved discount, timing and outcome, and a calibrated model of approval probability by discount level and of win probability by customer price, retrained each quarter. The account manager sees the odds before filing and the margin at each price.

## Target Customer
CROs and alliance leaders at resellers filing hundreds of special-pricing requests a year with their top vendors.

## Impact If Built
Pricing requests on evidence could lift win rates on contested deals by a few points or add ~0.5–1 point of margin at the same win rate (estimate) — ~$0.5–1M a year per $100M of solution sales.
