# Deal Registration & Special-Pricing Bids

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/it-value-added-resellers|IT Distributors & Value-Added Resellers]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get each registered deal and special-pricing request approved by the vendor at a price that beats the competing resellers, and whoever wins the highest share of its registered opportunities takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$90B of US solution sales priced under registered or special-pricing discounts, carrying ~$5–10B a year of registered and non-standard discount (estimate); the labour spent filing and negotiating it is ~$400M (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1–2% of receipts in discount value (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — each vendor's portal is digital, the reseller's side is spreadsheets, email and memory of what the deal desk approved last time
**Target Buyer:** Chief revenue officer or VP of vendor alliances at the reseller
**Automation Potential:** High for filing and tracking; medium for predicting approval and winning price

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Registration protects a reseller's price on an opportunity it found; special pricing (a non-standard discount from the vendor's deal desk) is how it competes on large or contested deals. Both are decided by the vendor, against rules that differ by programme and change at resets such as Cisco 360 in January 2026, and both are scored the day the customer awards. The counterpart is the vendor's deal desk and the competing resellers, not the customer's site, which is why this contest has a different winner from configuration fit.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Vendor partner portals, PRM platforms (Impartner, Zift, ChannelScaler), Introw and Vartopia on the vendor side, the reseller's CRM. The gaps: one opportunity re-entered into many portals, expiries tracked by hand, registration numbers lost before the distributor order, and no reseller-side history of approvals by vendor, product and quarter-week.
