# Distributor Quote Tools Opened to Every Vendor's Export

**Niche:** [[niches/it-value-added-resellers/configurator-quote-assembly/profile|Quote Assembly From Configurator Exports & Distributor Quotes]]
**Industry:** [[industries/it-value-added-resellers|IT Distributors & Value-Added Resellers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Distributors already convert configurator files into their own quotes; the output stops at the distributor's catalogue and doesn't reach the reseller's quote.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #word-embeddings #data-integration #automation #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn vendor configurator exports, distributor quotes and price files into a priced customer quote in the reseller's quoting tool without retyping a line, and whoever posts the highest no-touch line rate on the reseller's own files takes the account.

## The Problem
Resellers send configurator exports to the distributor, receive a distributor quote, then rebuild it in their own CPQ with their services and margin, sometimes against a second distributor for comparison.

## What Already Exists
Distributor platforms (Ingram Micro Xvantage, TD SYNNEX ION and StreamOne) with configuration and quote tools, and distributor solution-design desks.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: distributor quotes delivered as structured lines into any CPQ the reseller uses; vendor SKU, distributor part number and reseller catalogue item all carried on each line; side-by-side sourcing across distributors; and the reseller's services and registration numbers merged in.

## Target Customer
Distributors competing for reseller loyalty, and the resellers who buy from two of them.

## Impact If Solved
Structured distributor quotes delivered straight into the reseller's CPQ would remove one full rekey per solution quote (estimate: ~15–25 minutes) and make dual sourcing cheap.
