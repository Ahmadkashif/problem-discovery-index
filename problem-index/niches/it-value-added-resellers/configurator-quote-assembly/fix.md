# The Superseded SKU in Last Year's Template

**Niche:** [[niches/it-value-added-resellers/configurator-quote-assembly/profile|Quote Assembly From Configurator Exports & Distributor Quotes]]
**Industry:** [[industries/it-value-added-resellers|IT Distributors & Value-Added Resellers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Reps build new quotes by copying old ones, and the copied lines carry SKUs the vendor has since replaced or renamed.
**Tags:** #word-embeddings #k-nearest-neighbors #data-integration #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn vendor configurator exports, distributor quotes and price files into a priced customer quote in the reseller's quoting tool without retyping a line, and whoever posts the highest no-touch line rate on the reseller's own files takes the account.

## The Problem
Copying a past quote is the fastest way to build a new one. Vendors rename offers (Microsoft renamed its Copilot add-on offers from 16 October 2026 and told partners to update catalogue mappings) and end models, so copied lines price wrong or fail at the distributor.

## Why It's Still Broken
Catalogue updates arrive as price-list files and announcements; the reseller's saved templates and past quotes are never re-checked against them.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Re-validate every line copied from a template or past quote against current vendor and distributor catalogues at the moment of copying, propose the successor SKU, and re-check saved templates whenever a price list publishes.

## Who Feels the Pain
Inside reps whose orders bounce; licensing specialists who field the questions.

## Impact If Fixed
Catching superseded SKUs at copy time removes a recurring class of order rejections (estimate: a few percent of copied quotes) at almost no cost.
