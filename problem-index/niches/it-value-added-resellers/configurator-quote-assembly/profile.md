# Quote Assembly From Configurator Exports & Distributor Quotes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/it-value-added-resellers|IT Distributors & Value-Added Resellers]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn vendor configurator exports, distributor quotes and price files into a priced customer quote in the reseller's quoting tool without retyping a line, and whoever posts the highest no-touch line rate on the reseller's own files takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1–1.5B a year of inside-sales and quoting-specialist time at US resellers spent rekeying and repricing lines (estimate: ~227,000 QCEW employees, ~15% in quoting roles, ~$70,000 loaded cost, ~40–50% of their time on assembly); software spend on automating it ~$100M (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.3% of receipts in labour (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — every input is a digital file, and most are still copied line by line
**Target Buyer:** VP of operations or inside sales manager at the reseller
**Automation Potential:** Very high — extraction is largely solved; matching vendor SKUs, distributor part numbers and catalogue items is the remaining work

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A reseller's quote is assembled from other people's documents: the vendor configurator's export (CSV, XLSX or PDF), one or two distributor quotes with cost and stock, price files, and the reseller's own services. Reps copy, paste and reprice. It is the reseller-side counterpart of the order intake that distributors automate for industrial customers and for [[industries/electrical-distributors|Electrical Distributors]], [[industries/building-material-dealers|Building Material Dealers]] and [[industries/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors|Plumbing & HVAC Distributors]], profiled at [[niches/industrial-distributors/rfq-and-order-intake-automation/profile|RFQ & Order Intake Automation]]; the distributor-side version for IT — resellers' purchase orders keyed into TD SYNNEX's or Ingram Micro's systems — is the same workflow as that niche and is not profiled again. This niche is about transcription and matching; judging whether the configuration fits the customer's site belongs to [[niches/it-value-added-resellers/complex-bom-configuration/profile|Complex BOM Configuration & Site Fit]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Channel CPQ with distributor feeds (ConnectWise CPQ, QuoteWerks, Kaseya Quote Manager, ScalePad's Quoter), Quote.ly's file extraction for IT resellers, distributor quote-to-order tools. Checked on 2026-10-24: none of the AI quoting vendors in the distributor field (Avent, Mercura, Comena, Hexa, Ventura and peers) mentions IT resellers or distributors on its page. The gaps: vendor-specific export formats, renamed and superseded SKUs, distributor part numbers that differ from vendor SKUs, and no measured no-touch rate on any reseller's own files.
