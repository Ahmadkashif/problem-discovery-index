# E-rate Consultants

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/it-value-added-resellers|IT Distributors & Value-Added Resellers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Firms that prepare and defend schools' and libraries' E-rate applications — Funds For Learning, CSM and many smaller practices — filing Forms 470 and 471, answering USAC reviews and appeals, and advising on eligibility and budgets. Some publish annual trend reports from USAC's open data and their own applicant base. Schools pay fees; the resellers serving them are covered in [[niches/it-value-added-resellers/erate-schools-libraries/profile|K-12 Schools & Libraries Buying Through E-rate]], and USAC's own integrity work at [[niches/telecom-resellers/usac-program-integrity/profile|USAC Contribution Audits & Lifeline Program Integrity]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analysts behind trend and benchmark work at the largest firm (estimate); consultants preparing each applicant's forms are production
**Output:** Filed applications and appeals; annual trend reports
**Proprietary data:** Applicants' pre-filing workpapers; filed forms are public USAC data
**Clock:** FCC Form 471 window (21 January – 1 April 2026), on per-applicant work
**Buyer:** Firm principal
