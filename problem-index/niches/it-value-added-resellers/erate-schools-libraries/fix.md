# The Invoice Reduced for an Ineligible Licence

**Niche:** [[niches/it-value-added-resellers/erate-schools-libraries/profile|K-12 Schools & Libraries Buying Through E-rate]]
**Industry:** [[industries/it-value-added-resellers|IT Distributors & Value-Added Resellers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A firewall or wireless licence bundled with security features E-rate doesn't fund is invoiced at full cost and reduced on review.
**Tags:** #bert #large-language-models #compliance #quick-win #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver E-rate category two equipment and services that USAC reimburses in full, and whoever has the lowest share of invoiced dollars reduced or denied takes the account.

## The Problem
Vendors bundle eligible functions with ineligible ones in a single licence SKU. Without a cost allocation, the invoice claims the whole amount, and USAC reduces it, sometimes after the funding year has closed.

## Why It's Still Broken
Allocations need vendor documentation of what each bundle contains, which changes with each product release, and the reseller's quoting tool has no field for it.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A maintained list of bundled SKUs with their eligible share and supporting vendor documentation, applied automatically on E-rate quotes, with the allocation carried through to the Form 471 and the invoice.

## Who Feels the Pain
District technology directors who lose funding, resellers who absorb the difference, E-rate specialists who rebuild allocations each year.

## Impact If Fixed
Allocating bundled licences correctly up front avoids reductions that can reach a fifth or more of a licence line's value (estimate) on the affected SKUs.
