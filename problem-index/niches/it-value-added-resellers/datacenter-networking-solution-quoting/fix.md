# The Quarter-End Deal Nobody Reviewed

**Niche:** [[niches/it-value-added-resellers/datacenter-networking-solution-quoting/profile|Data-Centre & Networking Solution Quoting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/it-value-added-resellers|IT Distributors & Value-Added Resellers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The deals booked in the last week of a vendor's quarter are the largest and the least reviewed, because the senior engineers are the bottleneck exactly then.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #feature-engineering #worker-facing #workflow-orchestration #quick-win
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a customer's infrastructure requirement into a priced vendor bill of materials that wins, but that splits into two contests with different winners: a configuration that installs as quoted at the customer's site, scored after delivery in change orders and returns, and a price the vendor's deal desk approves at a level that beats the competing reseller, scored at award. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Vendors discount hardest at quarter-end, so customers and resellers push large deals into the final days. The two or three engineers who review solution BOMs face a queue of dozens; the quotes that go out unreviewed are the ones most likely to need a change order later.

## Why It's Still Broken
Review is first come, first served, and nothing ranks quotes by risk. The engineers' time is fixed, and quarter-end is set by the vendor, not the reseller.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Rank the review queue by risk — deal size, number of vendors, new customer site, configurations similar to past change orders, unusual optics or licence mixes — so senior review goes to the riskiest third and the rest get an automated checklist. Log which quotes skipped review and track their outcomes.

## Who Feels the Pain
Pre-sales engineers working nights at quarter-end; field engineers who find the gaps on site; account managers who absorb the change order.

## Impact If Fixed
Pointing scarce review at the riskiest third of quarter-end quotes could catch most of the costly errors (estimate: ~60–70% of change-order value) without adding staff.
