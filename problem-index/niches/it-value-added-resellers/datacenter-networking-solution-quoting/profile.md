# Data-Centre & Networking Solution Quoting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/it-value-added-resellers|IT Distributors & Value-Added Resellers]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a customer's infrastructure requirement into a priced vendor bill of materials that wins, but that splits into two contests with different winners: a configuration that installs as quoted at the customer's site, scored after delivery in change orders and returns, and a price the vendor's deal desk approves at a level that beats the competing reseller, scored at award. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$90B a year of data-centre, networking and security solution sales (hardware, software tiers, support and attached services) through US resellers and distributors (estimate, ~20% of the code's $416B receipts plus resellers filed under other codes)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~20% of receipts and a larger share of gross profit (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — vendor configurators and distributor feeds are digital; the reseller's review of fit and price is people, chat and spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** VP of Solutions or pre-sales at the reseller; chief revenue officer when the case is margin
**Automation Potential:** Medium — high for assembling and pricing the quote, low for the final fit and price judgement without a labelled history

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is where a reseller's margin is made or lost. A solution deal is a long bill of materials across several vendors, built in each vendor's configurator, priced with distributor cost plus registered and special-pricing discounts, and delivered with the reseller's installation services. The commodity end of the business — laptops, monitors, licences ordered by SKU — is decided by price and stock; this end is decided by two judgements that different people make against different counterparts. The pre-sales engineer judges whether the configuration fits the customer's site; the account manager and deal desk judge what the vendor will approve and what wins. Different clocks, inputs and measures, so the niche is decomposed.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Vendor configurators (Cisco Commerce Workspace, server vendors' tools), channel CPQ (ConnectWise CPQ, QuoteWerks, Kaseya Quote Manager), distributor platforms (Ingram Micro Xvantage, TD SYNNEX ION and StreamOne), vendor partner portals for registration and special pricing. The gaps: no link from the quote to the install outcome, no record of the expert's corrections, and no reseller-side history of what deal desks approve.
