# Channel CPQ Extended Past the Purchase Order

**Niche:** [[niches/it-value-added-resellers/datacenter-networking-solution-quoting/profile|Data-Centre & Networking Solution Quoting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/it-value-added-resellers|IT Distributors & Value-Added Resellers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Channel CPQ tools already hold the reseller's quotes; they stop at the purchase order, so nothing they record learns from what happened at install.
**Tags:** #feature-engineering #k-nearest-neighbors #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a customer's infrastructure requirement into a priced vendor bill of materials that wins, but that splits into two contests with different winners: a configuration that installs as quoted at the customer's site, scored after delivery in change orders and returns, and a price the vendor's deal desk approves at a level that beats the competing reseller, scored at award. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Resellers already pay for a quoting tool that stores every quote, its lines and its margin. When the deal turns into a project, the record moves to the PSA or ERP and the quote is frozen as a PDF.

## What Already Exists
ConnectWise CPQ, Kaseya Quote Manager, QuoteWerks and ScalePad's Quoter, with distributor price feeds and PSA integrations; Salesforce-based CPQ at the largest resellers.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: change orders, returns and install tickets written back to the originating quote lines; configurator export versions kept so the expert's edits are visible; registration and special-pricing numbers attached per line; and a "similar past deals" panel at quote time showing what was added later on comparable configurations for the same customer type.

## Target Customer
Operations and solutions leaders at mid-sized resellers already standardised on a channel CPQ.

## Impact If Solved
Closing the loop inside the existing tool avoids a new platform and, by surfacing what was added later on similar deals, could cut post-order change orders by ~a quarter (estimate) in the first year.
