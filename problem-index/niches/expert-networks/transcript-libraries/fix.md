# The Transcript Nobody Can Find

**Niche:** [[niches/expert-networks/transcript-libraries/profile|Transcript Libraries]]
**Industry:** [[industries/expert-networks|Expert Networks]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A transcript discussing a private company's pricing is tagged only to the public competitor named in the request, and the subscriber researching the private company never sees it.
**Tags:** #bert #transformers #graph-theory #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to hold a compliant, searchable transcript on the specific company or private competitor a subscriber is researching before they search for it — and whoever covers the long tail first takes the research seat.

## The Problem
Tagging follows the call's original subject. Everything else discussed — suppliers, competitors, private challengers, products — is tagged inconsistently or not at all, and informal references ("the Texas player") are missed entirely.

## Why It's Still Broken
Tagging is done at publication by editors working to volume targets, against an identifier database that covers public companies well and private ones poorly.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Entity linking over every mention against a company graph including private companies and subsidiaries, with informal references resolved from context, and retroactive re-tagging of the back catalogue.

## Who Feels the Pain
Subscribers who pay for a library and commission a call that already exists in it; libraries whose long-tail coverage is invisible.

## Impact If Fixed
The back catalogue's long-tail value is unrealised until it is findable; re-tagging it is a one-time project with permanent return.
