# Commissioning Calls From Failed Searches

**Niche:** [[niches/expert-networks/transcript-libraries/profile|Transcript Libraries]]
**Industry:** [[industries/expert-networks|Expert Networks]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Every zero-result search in a transcript library is a subscriber telling the library which call to commission next, and it is logged and ignored.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #large-language-models #word-embeddings #k-means-clustering #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to hold a compliant, searchable transcript on the specific company or private competitor a subscriber is researching before they search for it — and whoever covers the long tail first takes the research seat.

## The Problem
Libraries decide which calls to host through editorial calendars: earnings seasons, sector themes, analyst instinct. Meanwhile subscribers search for companies and topics the library does not cover, find nothing, and go to a network for a bespoke call.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Search logs are owned by product, commissioning by content, and the two teams measure different things — engagement and transcripts published.

## What to Build
Cluster failed and low-engagement searches into coverage gaps by company and topic, forecast demand ahead of earnings and event calendars, and rank commissioning candidates by expected subscriber reads per call cost. Close the loop by measuring reads of commissioned transcripts against the forecast.

## Target Customer
Heads of content and research at transcript library platforms.

## Impact If Built
Each hosted call is the library's unit cost. Commissioning against revealed demand raises reads per call, which is the library's core economics.
