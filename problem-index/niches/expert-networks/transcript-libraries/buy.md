# Newsroom Editorial Planning

**Niche:** [[niches/expert-networks/transcript-libraries/profile|Transcript Libraries]]
**Industry:** [[industries/expert-networks|Expert Networks]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Digital newsrooms plan coverage from audience demand signals and a library still commissions calls from an editorial calendar.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #descriptive-statistics #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to hold a compliant, searchable transcript on the specific company or private competitor a subscriber is researching before they search for it — and whoever covers the long tail first takes the research seat.

## The Problem
The library's in-house analysts each host several calls a week, chosen from a mix of editorial priority and subscriber request. There is no shared view of what is under-covered relative to demand.

## What Already Exists
Newsroom planning tools combine search and social demand signals, coverage calendars and assignment workflow, and measure each piece's audience after publication.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: (1) entity-level demand by ticker and private company rather than by topic; (2) expert availability as a constraint, since a call needs a findable, compliant expert; (3) the earnings calendar as the primary demand driver; and (4) subscriber segment weighting, since a call read by large funds is worth more than one read by trial users.

## Target Customer
Content operations leads at transcript libraries.

## Impact If Solved
Moves commissioning from instinct to demand and makes analyst capacity allocation measurable.
