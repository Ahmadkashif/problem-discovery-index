# Expert-Sourced Data Products

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/expert-networks|Expert Networks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Networks and specialist vendors that turn recurring surveys or structured expert inputs into data products — prescribing trackers, IT spending-intent indices, procurement sentiment series — sold by subscription to investors.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 analysts and data staff per product line
**Output:** Recurring indices and datasets derived from expert panels
**Proprietary data:** Repeated panel responses over time from verified practitioners
**Clock:** Monthly and quarterly release schedules tied to earnings
**Buyer:** Head of Data Products
