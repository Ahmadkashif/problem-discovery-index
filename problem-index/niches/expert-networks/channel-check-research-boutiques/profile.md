# Channel-Check & Primary-Research Boutiques

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/expert-networks|Expert Networks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Independent research firms that interview suppliers, distributors, resellers and customers of listed companies and sell the findings to institutional investors ahead of results.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-100 analysts per firm across a fragmented field
**Output:** Recurring channel-check reports and surveys on listed companies, sold by subscription or commission
**Proprietary data:** Long-standing contact networks inside supply chains and repeated-survey histories
**Clock:** Quarterly earnings dates
**Buyer:** Director of Research
