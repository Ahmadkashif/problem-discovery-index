# Feedback on Every Forward

**Niche:** [[niches/expert-networks/the-network-associate/profile|The Network Associate]]
**Industry:** [[industries/expert-networks|Expert Networks]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** An associate forwards thousands of profiles a year and almost never learns which of them produced a good call.
**Tags:** #tacit-knowledge-ml #k-nearest-neighbors #gradient-boosting #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make a first-year associate forward experts as well as a third-year project manager — and whoever compresses that learning curve keeps both the matching quality and the people.

## The Problem
The call outcome reaches client service and billing. The associate who chose the expert sees revenue credited, not the rating or the dispute.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Associate metrics were designed for volume, and feedback on quality was assumed to arrive through managers.

## What to Build
A personal outcome feed: each forwarded profile, whether chosen, how the call rated, and which screener answers predicted the good calls; plus nearest-neighbour comparison with the senior project managers' forwards on similar requests.

## Target Customer
Heads of operations and training at networks.

## Impact If Built
Turns a quota job into a learnable skill, which is the cheapest retention and quality lever the network has.
