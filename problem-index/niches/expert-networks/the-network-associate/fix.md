# The Screener Written From Scratch at Midnight

**Niche:** [[niches/expert-networks/the-network-associate/profile|The Network Associate]]
**Industry:** [[industries/expert-networks|Expert Networks]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Screener questions decide what evidence of fit the network collects, and they are drafted ad hoc by the least experienced person on the project.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #logistic-regression #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make a first-year associate forward experts as well as a third-year project manager — and whoever compresses that learning curve keeps both the matching quality and the people.

## The Problem
Weak screeners ask "are you familiar with X?" and get yes from everyone. Strong ones ask for a specific decision, date or number only a real participant would know.

## Why It's Still Broken
Good screener design is tacit and passed on informally; past screeners are not searchable by topic or graded by outcome.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A library of past screeners graded by how well their answers predicted good calls, with drafted questions for new requests that ask for decision-specific evidence.

## Who Feels the Pain
Associates; clients reading generic screener answers; experts asked vague questions.

## Impact If Fixed
Better screeners improve every downstream match at no extra sourcing cost.
