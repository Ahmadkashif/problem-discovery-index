# Deal-Sprint Diligence Calls

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/expert-networks|Expert Networks]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver dozens of relevant customers, former employees and competitors of an often private target inside a deal team's two-to-three-week diligence clock — and whoever fills the call plan fastest with people who actually used or sold the product wins the deal team's next sprint.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$700M global
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~25% of industry revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — heavy portal use, manual call-plan management
**Target Buyer:** Private equity deal teams, commercial due diligence consultancies; network PE client-service heads
**Automation Potential:** High — call plans are structured and deadline-bound

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A private equity firm or the consultancy it hires has a fixed bid deadline and a question list about a target that is often private: what do customers think, why do they churn, how does the sales team really sell. The call plan is twenty to sixty calls in two or three weeks, weighted toward current customers of the target, which are the hardest people to find and the most valuable. Compliance is lighter than public equity on MNPI but heavier on confidentiality, because the deal itself is confidential. The contest is speed and breadth of relevant experts against a deal clock.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Network portals with project dashboards, bundled call-and-survey packages, transcription and per-call summaries. The gaps: call plans tracked in spreadsheets against the question list; no coverage view of which segments have been heard from; customer sourcing done by guesswork about who buys from the target; and findings reconciled by hand into the diligence report.
