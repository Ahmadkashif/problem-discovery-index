# Finding the Target's Customers

**Niche:** [[niches/expert-networks/deal-sprint-diligence-calls/profile|Deal-Sprint Diligence Calls]]
**Industry:** [[industries/expert-networks|Expert Networks]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** The most valuable diligence call is with a current customer of a private target, and networks find them by guessing who might buy from the company.
**Tags:** #graph-neural-networks #graph-theory #large-language-models #k-nearest-neighbors #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver dozens of relevant customers, former employees and competitors of an often private target inside a deal team's two-to-three-week diligence clock — and whoever fills the call plan fastest with people who actually used or sold the product wins the deal team's next sprint.

## The Problem
A deal team wants fifteen current customers of a private software vendor. Nobody publishes the customer list. Associates search for job titles that use that category of software, ask in screeners whether the candidate uses the vendor, and discard most respondents.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The evidence of who uses what is scattered — case studies, job postings mentioning tools, conference talks, the network's own past screener answers — and networks treat each request as new rather than as a query against accumulated knowledge.

## What to Build
A vendor–customer graph built from the network's own historical screener answers (which record which tools respondents use), public case studies and job postings, used to predict likely customers of a target and rank candidates who work at them. Each confirmed or rejected screener answer updates the graph.

## Target Customer
Heads of private equity client service at networks; commercial due diligence practices that source their own calls.

## Impact If Built
Customer calls are the scarcest input in a diligence sprint. A network that can find them in a day rather than a week wins the sprint and the next one.
