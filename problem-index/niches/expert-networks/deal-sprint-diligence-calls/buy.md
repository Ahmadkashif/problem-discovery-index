# Project Portfolio Coverage Tracking

**Niche:** [[niches/expert-networks/deal-sprint-diligence-calls/profile|Deal-Sprint Diligence Calls]]
**Industry:** [[industries/expert-networks|Expert Networks]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Survey research has quota management for sample coverage, and diligence call plans are tracked in a spreadsheet nobody updates after day four.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver dozens of relevant customers, former employees and competitors of an often private target inside a deal team's two-to-three-week diligence clock — and whoever fills the call plan fastest with people who actually used or sold the product wins the deal team's next sprint.

## The Problem
The call plan specifies segments — customer size, geography, churned versus retained, competitor users — and the question list. As calls happen, nobody maintains a live view of which cells are filled and which questions are answered.

## What Already Exists
Survey platforms manage quotas by segment in real time and stop fielding when cells fill; project management tools track tasks against deadlines.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: (1) segment assignment from a call transcript or screener rather than a form field; (2) question coverage measured from what was actually discussed, not what was planned; (3) multiple networks feeding one plan; and (4) deadline-aware recommendations — which cells to fill with the days left.

## Target Customer
Private equity deal teams and diligence consultancies; network project managers running large sprints.

## Impact If Solved
Coverage gaps in a diligence sprint are discovered when the report is drafted, after the deadline has passed. A live coverage view catches them while a call can still be booked.
