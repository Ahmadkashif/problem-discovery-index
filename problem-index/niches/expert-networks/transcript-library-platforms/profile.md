# Transcript Library Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/expert-networks|Expert Networks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Commission, host, transcribe, anonymise, compliance-review and publish expert calls as a searchable subscription library — AlphaSense (including Tegus), Third Bridge Forum, In Practise, and the libraries run by the large networks. Subscribers are hedge funds, long-only managers, private equity firms and consultancies; they pay an annual licence for access to the corpus.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-800 in-house call hosts and content analysts, transcript editors, compliance reviewers and taxonomy staff across the major platforms
**Output:** Compliance-reviewed expert-call transcripts tagged to public and private companies, plus AI search and summaries over the corpus
**Proprietary data:** Tens to hundreds of thousands of timestamped first-hand expert transcripts (Tegus alone reported over 100,000 at acquisition), with subscriber search and read logs — a corpus no competitor can reconstruct
**Clock:** Earnings seasons and deal timetables set subscriber demand; coverage must exist before the quarter's results or the bid deadline
**Buyer:** Chief Content Officer / Head of Research
