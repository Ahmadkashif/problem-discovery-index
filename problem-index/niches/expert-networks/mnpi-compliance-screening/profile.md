# MNPI & Compliance Screening

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/expert-networks|Expert Networks]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give a fund's chief compliance officer full-population evidence that no expert call carried material non-public information, in a form an examiner accepts — and whoever does that cheaply enough to run on every call becomes the network a regulated fund can approve.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$200M global (compliance cost carried by networks and funds)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of industry cost base
**Digital Adoption:** Low — attestation forms, policy PDFs and sampled chaperoning
**Target Buyer:** Chief compliance officers at networks and at buy-side clients
**Automation Potential:** High — rules are written down and transcripts are text

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Compliance is what makes expert calls purchasable by regulated investors. The insider-trading prosecutions that ran through expert-network relationships in 2010–2013 shaped a standard apparatus: employment checks, expert attestations, client-specific restrictions, chaperoned calls and transcript review. It is applied per call, partly by the network and partly by the client, by people reading documents. The contest is evidence quality at a cost that scales.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Network attestation workflows, client compliance approval tools, call recording, sampled chaperoning, and human transcript review. The gaps: client policies applied by reading rather than by rule; employment status checked once at onboarding; review of a sample rather than every call; and no shared vocabulary between network and fund for what was checked.
