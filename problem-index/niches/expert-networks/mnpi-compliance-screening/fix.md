# Fifty Client Policies in Fifty PDFs

**Niche:** [[niches/expert-networks/mnpi-compliance-screening/profile|MNPI & Compliance Screening]]
**Industry:** [[industries/expert-networks|Expert Networks]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Each client fund imposes its own expert-call policy, the network's compliance analysts apply them by memory, and violations are caught by the client after the call is booked.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #evaluation-metrics #compliance #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give a fund's chief compliance officer full-population evidence that no expert call carried material non-public information, in a form an examiner accepts — and whoever does that cheaply enough to run on every call becomes the network a regulated fund can approve.

## The Problem
One fund bars current employees of any public company; another requires six months since departure; another bans calls with anyone at a list of companies. The network's analysts check profiles against these from documents and memory.

## Why It's Still Broken
Policies arrive as prose and change without notice, and the network has never treated them as data.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Extract each client's policy into versioned rules, apply them automatically at profile forwarding with the rule cited, and flag policy documents that changed.

## Who Feels the Pain
Network compliance analysts; client compliance teams rejecting experts after the analyst has scheduled; associates whose sourcing is wasted.

## Impact If Fixed
Catching a policy conflict at forwarding rather than after scheduling removes a recurring source of rework on both sides.
