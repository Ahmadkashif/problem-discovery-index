# Communications Surveillance

**Niche:** [[niches/expert-networks/mnpi-compliance-screening/profile|MNPI & Compliance Screening]]
**Industry:** [[industries/expert-networks|Expert Networks]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Banks run model-based surveillance over traders' communications, and expert-call review is a narrower problem that still runs on human listening.
**Tags:** #bert #transformers #naive-bayes #evaluation-metrics #compliance #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give a fund's chief compliance officer full-population evidence that no expert call carried material non-public information, in a form an examiner accepts — and whoever does that cheaply enough to run on every call becomes the network a regulated fund can approve.

## The Problem
Compliance staff listen to or read expert calls looking for a narrow class of statement: non-public, company-specific, material.

## What Already Exists
Trade and communications surveillance platforms used by banks and brokers scan email, chat and voice for misconduct with lexicons and increasingly with language models, and route alerts to reviewers.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: (1) a different target — disclosure by a third party about a named company, not misconduct by an employee; (2) the expert's employment relationship to each company mentioned as a feature; (3) client-specific restricted topics layered on top; and (4) output acceptable to two compliance functions at once, the network's and the fund's.

## Target Customer
Compliance technology leads at networks and funds; surveillance vendors looking for adjacent use.

## Impact If Solved
The surveillance industry has built the pipeline; the expert-call variant is a re-targeted classifier and a different alert workflow.
