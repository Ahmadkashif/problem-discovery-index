# Cross-Call Synthesis

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/expert-networks|Expert Networks]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to be the layer that turns all of a client's calls and library transcripts into a faithful, cited synthesis — and whoever is trusted not to invent a quote wins the research workflow above the calls.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$150M global (emerging AI synthesis products across networks, libraries and research platforms, estimated)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% and growing
**Digital Adoption:** Low across calls; high per call
**Target Buyer:** Product heads at networks and libraries; research operations at funds
**Automation Potential:** Very High — text in, structured synthesis out

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Distinct from the client analyst niche, this is the vendor-side product contest: networks, libraries and research platforms are all adding AI synthesis over calls, and the one that becomes the place a research team assembles evidence captures the workflow. The deciding property is faithfulness — a synthesis that misattributes a statement is worse than none.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Per-call AI summaries from networks; library-wide AI search and summaries; general LLM assistants. The gaps: synthesis confined to one vendor's transcripts; no faithfulness guarantees; and no source weighting.
