# Custom Call Brokerage

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/expert-networks|Expert Networks]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** This niche is not terminal — finding an expert for a fund that covers the same tickers every quarter and finding forty experts on a private target inside a two-week deal clock are different contests with different winners, and they are stated separately in the sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.8B global
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~60% of industry revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — modern client portals over a manual sourcing engine
**Target Buyer:** Network COO and heads of client service
**Automation Potential:** High — sourcing, screening and matching are repetitive and outcome-labelled

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The one-hour bespoke call, sourced to order and billed per hour or per credit, is the industry's core product and the bulk of its revenue. A client asks for a kind of person, the network finds candidates, screens them, clears them for compliance, schedules the call and bills it. Every large network sells it and clients routinely send the same request to three networks and take whichever profiles arrive first and look best.

It is **not terminal**. The public-equity investor covering a stable set of tickers needs experts who add something the library and last quarter's calls did not, under the strictest MNPI posture, and buys on non-redundancy and compliance. The private equity deal team or consultancy in diligence needs dozens of customers, former employees and competitors of an often private target within days, and buys on volume of relevant experts inside the deal clock. Different demand shape, different compliance profile, visibly different winners.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Proprietary expert databases and CRMs, LinkedIn search and messaging, screener forms, scheduling and recording tools, and client portals. The gaps: matching quality is never measured against call outcomes; the senior project manager's judgement is not captured; clients multi-source the same request and the losing networks' work is wasted; and refund and dispute data is treated as billing rather than as labels.
