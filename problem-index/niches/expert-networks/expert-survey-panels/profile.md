# Expert Survey Panels

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/expert-networks|Expert Networks]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to field a quantitative survey to a verified population of practitioners — physicians, IT buyers, procurement heads — fast enough for an investment decision, with respondents who are who they say they are — and whoever proves respondent quality wins the quantitative half of the research budget.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M global (network-run B2B surveys for investors and consultancies, estimated)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of industry revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — survey platforms used over the network's expert database
**Target Buyer:** Heads of survey and quantitative products at networks; fund and diligence teams buying surveys
**Automation Potential:** Very High — questionnaire design, fielding, quality control and analysis

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Networks increasingly sell surveys of their vetted experts — a hundred IT decision-makers on cloud spend, two hundred physicians on prescribing intent — as the quantitative complement to calls. The network's advantage over general panels is verified identity and role. The contest is respondent quality at speed.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Commercial survey platforms, the network's expert database for sampling, manual quality checks. The gaps: respondent fraud and inattentive answering; weighting to an unknown population; questionnaire design repeated per project; and survey results never compared with what later happened.
