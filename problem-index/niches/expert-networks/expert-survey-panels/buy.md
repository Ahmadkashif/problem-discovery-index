# Consumer Panel Quality Control

**Niche:** [[niches/expert-networks/expert-survey-panels/profile|Expert Survey Panels]]
**Industry:** [[industries/expert-networks|Expert Networks]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Consumer panel providers have built fraud and quality systems over a decade, and B2B expert surveys apply attention checks.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #logistic-regression #dbscan #evaluation-metrics #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to field a quantitative survey to a verified population of practitioners — physicians, IT buyers, procurement heads — fast enough for an investment decision, with respondents who are who they say they are — and whoever proves respondent quality wins the quantitative half of the research budget.

## The Problem
Expert survey quality control is lighter than consumer panel practice despite higher incentives per respondent.

## What Already Exists
Consumer panel quality systems: device fingerprinting, duplicate detection, speeder and straight-liner detection, open-end scoring.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: (1) role verification against employment history, which consumer panels do not need; (2) small populations, so each respondent matters more; (3) linkage to call history as a trust signal; and (4) quota cells defined by professional attributes.

## Target Customer
Survey operations at networks.

## Impact If Solved
Brings mature practice to a premium product.
