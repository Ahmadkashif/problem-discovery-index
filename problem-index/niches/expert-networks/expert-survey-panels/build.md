# Proving the Respondent Is Real

**Niche:** [[niches/expert-networks/expert-survey-panels/profile|Expert Survey Panels]]
**Industry:** [[industries/expert-networks|Expert Networks]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Survey incentives attract people who claim to be IT buyers and are not, and the network's verified-expert claim is only as good as its fraud detection.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #logistic-regression #large-language-models #dbscan #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to field a quantitative survey to a verified population of practitioners — physicians, IT buyers, procurement heads — fast enough for an investment decision, with respondents who are who they say they are — and whoever proves respondent quality wins the quantitative half of the research budget.

## The Problem
Paid B2B surveys attract professional respondents and fabricated identities. Inattentive or inconsistent responses inflate sample size without adding information.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Quality control relies on attention checks and manual review of open-ends, which skilled fraudsters pass.

## What to Build
Respondent quality scoring from consistency with the expert's verified profile and call history, response-timing patterns, open-end language quality, and clustering of near-duplicate response patterns, with quality reported per survey.

## Target Customer
Heads of survey products at networks.

## Impact If Built
Verified respondents are the network's only defensible edge over general panels; measuring it makes the edge sellable.
