# Rereading Thirty Transcripts for One Quote

**Niche:** [[niches/expert-networks/the-client-research-analyst/profile|The Client Research Analyst]]
**Industry:** [[industries/expert-networks|Expert Networks]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The partner asks who said the churn was about the implementation, and the analyst spends an hour searching transcripts for a sentence they half-remember.
**Tags:** #word-embeddings #transformers #k-nearest-neighbors #worker-facing #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a pile of expert calls into a traceable synthesis an investment committee can check, before the committee meets — and whoever owns that step owns the analyst's workflow rather than one of its inputs.

## The Problem
Transcripts from different networks arrive in different formats and are searched by keyword, which misses paraphrase.

## Why It's Still Broken
No tool spans the project's transcripts from every source.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Semantic search over every transcript in a project, returning passages with expert metadata.

## Who Feels the Pain
Analysts and associates in the last days before a committee.

## Impact If Fixed
A small tool that removes a recurring hour of searching per question.
