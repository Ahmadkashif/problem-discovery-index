# The Memo With Its Evidence Attached

**Niche:** [[niches/expert-networks/the-client-research-analyst/profile|The Client Research Analyst]]
**Industry:** [[industries/expert-networks|Expert Networks]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** The investment memo says "customers are price-sensitive" and nobody reading it can see that four experts said so, two disagreed, and the two who disagreed were the current customers.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #word-embeddings #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a pile of expert calls into a traceable synthesis an investment committee can check, before the committee meets — and whoever owns that step owns the analyst's workflow rather than one of its inputs.

## The Problem
Expert evidence reaches the committee as the analyst's prose. The count, source mix and disagreement behind each claim are lost.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Networks sell calls and libraries sell transcripts; neither has owned the step after.

## What to Build
Claim extraction across a project's calls, clustered by question, with stance and source type, feeding a drafted memo section in which every sentence carries citations to transcript lines.

## Target Customer
Directors of research at funds; private equity deal team leads.

## Impact If Built
Makes primary-research evidence inspectable at the point of decision, and gives analysts back the nights spent reconciling.
