# Qualitative Research Coding Tools

**Niche:** [[niches/expert-networks/the-client-research-analyst/profile|The Client Research Analyst]]
**Industry:** [[industries/expert-networks|Expert Networks]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Academic and UX researchers code interview transcripts into themes with mature tools, and investment analysts do the same task in a notes document.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #k-means-clustering #word-embeddings #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a pile of expert calls into a traceable synthesis an investment committee can check, before the committee meets — and whoever owns that step owns the analyst's workflow rather than one of its inputs.

## The Problem
Thematic synthesis of interviews is a well-studied research method with dedicated software, unused in finance.

## What Already Exists
Qualitative analysis and research repository tools code transcripts to themes, count support and link quotes, increasingly with model assistance.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: (1) source metadata specific to experts — relationship to company, tenure, recency; (2) claims with numbers and dates rather than themes alone; (3) ingestion from several networks and libraries; (4) compliance metadata retained; and (5) sprint-length timelines rather than study-length ones.

## Target Customer
Research operations leads at funds and consultancies.

## Impact If Solved
Imports a mature method and tool category into the most manual step of investment research.
