# Healthcare Primary Market Research Agencies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/expert-networks|Expert Networks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Market research agencies that run physician, payer and patient interviews and surveys for pharmaceutical and medical-device companies and for investors in them, frequently recruiting through expert networks and healthcare panels.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-1,000 researchers and analysts at larger agencies
**Output:** Brand tracking, launch forecasting inputs, message testing, KOL mapping, investor-commissioned physician surveys
**Proprietary data:** Physician and payer panels and accumulated survey norms
**Clock:** Product launch plans, brand planning cycles and investor deadlines
**Buyer:** Practice or Managing Director
