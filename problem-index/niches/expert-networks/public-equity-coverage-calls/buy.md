# Research Management Systems

**Niche:** [[niches/expert-networks/public-equity-coverage-calls/profile|Public-Equity Coverage Calls]]
**Industry:** [[industries/expert-networks|Expert Networks]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Funds already store notes and documents per ticker in research management systems, and expert calls arrive in them as attachments nobody can query.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #word-embeddings #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to put an expert in front of a fund analyst who adds something the analyst's last calls and the transcript library did not already say, cleared under the fund's MNPI policy — and whoever does that most reliably becomes the fund's first call.

## The Problem
Expert-call transcripts land as files in the analyst's inbox or a shared drive and are filed by ticker if at all. Next quarter the analyst cannot find what a given expert said about a specific question.

## What Already Exists
Buy-side research management systems store notes, models and documents by security, with search and tagging; market-intelligence platforms provide semantic search across broker research, filings and transcripts.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: (1) expert metadata as first-class fields — role, employer, tenure, departure date, relationship to the company — so a claim can be weighted by source; (2) compliance metadata carried with the transcript, including which network, which approval and any flagged passages; (3) question-level indexing rather than document-level, since analysts revisit questions not calls; and (4) ingestion from several networks and libraries in their different formats.

## Target Customer
Research operations and technology leads at funds; research management system vendors.

## Impact If Solved
The fund's own call history is its most specific primary research and is effectively unsearchable. Making it queryable by question and source turns past spend into a reusable asset.
