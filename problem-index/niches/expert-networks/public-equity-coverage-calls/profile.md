# Public-Equity Coverage Calls

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/expert-networks|Expert Networks]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to put an expert in front of a fund analyst who adds something the analyst's last calls and the transcript library did not already say, cleared under the fund's MNPI policy — and whoever does that most reliably becomes the fund's first call.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$900M global
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~30% of industry revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — portals, libraries and compliance workflows are mature
**Target Buyer:** Hedge fund and long-only research heads; network client-service leads
**Automation Potential:** High — repeat coverage makes redundancy measurable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A public-equity analyst covers twenty to fifty companies and returns to the same questions every quarter. They have access to transcript libraries and to their own prior calls. The marginal call is valuable only if it says something new, and it is risky in a way deal calls are not: the subject is a listed company, the analyst is trading on the view, and the fund's compliance policy is at its strictest. The contest is non-redundancy under compliance.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Network portals, transcript libraries, fund-side compliance approval tools and research management systems. The gaps: nothing checks a proposed call against what the analyst's prior calls and the library already cover; client compliance rules are applied by hand; and repeat experts are re-booked on habit rather than on evidence they add information.
