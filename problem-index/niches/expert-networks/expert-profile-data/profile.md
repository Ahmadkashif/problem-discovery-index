# Expert Profile & Restriction Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/expert-networks|Expert Networks]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to know an expert's current employer and restriction status before the call rather than after it — and whoever keeps a large database current without re-asking every expert wins both compliance and speed.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$150M global (database maintenance, onboarding and payments operations)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of industry cost base
**Digital Adoption:** Low — self-reported profiles updated when the expert next takes a call
**Target Buyer:** Heads of operations and compliance at networks
**Automation Potential:** High — change detection on public signals is routine

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A network's database holds hundreds of thousands to millions of experts, each with an employment history, attestations, tax and payment details and restrictions. Most are inactive at any time and their records decay: people change jobs, join public companies, enter government. A stale current-employer field is both a compliance risk and a sourcing failure. The contest is currency of the record.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Expert self-service portals, onboarding and payment workflows, periodic re-attestation. The gaps: updates only at the next call; no detection of job changes between calls; duplicates across sourcing channels; and payment and tax operations handled manually across many countries.
