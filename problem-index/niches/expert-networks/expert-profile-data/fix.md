# The Expert Who Joined the Subject Company

**Niche:** [[niches/expert-networks/expert-profile-data/profile|Expert Profile & Restriction Data]]
**Industry:** [[industries/expert-networks|Expert Networks]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A former employee of a competitor joined the subject company last quarter, the database still says competitor, and the call is booked.
**Tags:** #change-point-detection #logistic-regression #evaluation-metrics #compliance #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to know an expert's current employer and restriction status before the call rather than after it — and whoever keeps a large database current without re-asking every expert wins both compliance and speed.

## The Problem
The pre-call attestation is supposed to catch it, and usually does, but only after sourcing, screening and scheduling, and only if the expert reads the form honestly and carefully.

## Why It's Still Broken
The check is placed at the end of the workflow because that is where the attestation sits, not where the risk enters.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Check likely current employer against the project's subject companies at forwarding, using the staleness score, and require re-confirmation before a profile reaches the client.

## Who Feels the Pain
Network compliance; clients whose compliance teams reject late; experts placed in a compromising position.

## Impact If Fixed
Moves the highest-risk check to the front of the workflow at almost no cost.
