# Commercial Due Diligence Advisors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/expert-networks|Expert Networks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Strategy consultancies and specialist commercial due diligence firms hired by private equity buyers and lenders to test a target's market, customers and plan, typically on a two-to-four-week clock. Expert calls and expert surveys are among their largest research inputs.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-2,000 consultants across specialist CDD practices and strategy firms' private equity groups
**Output:** Commercial due diligence reports, market models, customer-interview syntheses, often with reliance letters for lenders
**Proprietary data:** Archives of prior diligence on the same sectors and companies, interview notes and survey data
**Clock:** Bid deadlines and lender credit committees
**Buyer:** Partner leading the private equity practice
