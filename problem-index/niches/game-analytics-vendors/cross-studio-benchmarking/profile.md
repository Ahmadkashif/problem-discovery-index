# Cross-Studio Benchmarking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/game-analytics-vendors|Game Analytics Vendors]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell a studio whether its numbers are good, which requires seeing comparable games — and whoever turns that corpus into a product takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$150M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — corpus unused
**Target Buyer:** Analytics vendor leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — aggregation with contractual design

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A studio cannot tell whether its day-one retention is good, because good is defined relative to comparable games and only the vendor can see them. These vendors hold event data from thousands of titles — retention by genre, monetisation model, platform and season — and sell each studio a view of its own funnel. This half is a data product built on a cross-customer position, with its own contractual structure and its own commercial logic, and it is not something a studio can build for itself at any price.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A dashboard of the studio's own numbers, and occasional published industry averages of unknown provenance. The gaps: no comparable-set definition; no benchmark distributions; no seasonal baselines; no market movement signal; and no contractual basis for using cross-customer data.
