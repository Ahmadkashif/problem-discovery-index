# Data Trust & Metric Definitions

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/game-analytics-vendors|Game Analytics Vendors]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make one number mean one thing across every tool and every team, and whoever establishes that takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$120M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — inconsistent
**Target Buyer:** Analytics leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — definition management and reconciliation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The same metric name means different things in the analytics platform, the internal warehouse, the publisher's report and the finance model. Day-one retention is computed from different day boundaries; an active user is defined differently by each tool; revenue includes or excludes platform fees depending on who is asking. Meetings spend their first ten minutes reconciling numbers, and decisions are made on whichever figure the person presenting happened to use.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Several tools, each with its own definitions, and a document somebody wrote once. The gaps: no authoritative definition anywhere; no reconciliation between sources; no lineage from number to source events; no change log for definitions; and no way to tell which figure is right.
