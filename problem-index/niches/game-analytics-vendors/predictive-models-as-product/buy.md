**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to ship churn and lifetime value scores that are actually true of a given studio's players, when the models are fitted generically across a whole customer base — and whoever makes them trustworthy takes the account.

## The Problem
Credit risk modelling developed a complete governance discipline because models drive expensive decisions and silently stop working. Independent validation before deployment, ongoing performance monitoring, population stability testing, calibration checks, documented model limitations, and defined retirement criteria are all standard and in many places mandated. Game analytics ships predictive scores into product interfaces with none of it and customers act on them daily.

## What Already Exists
Independent model validation; ongoing performance and calibration monitoring; population stability indices; documented model limitations and appropriate use; and model retirement criteria.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a model shipped as a product feature to many customers rather than used internally by one institution. It requires: (1) one model serving thousands of heterogeneous games, so validation must be per customer rather than per model — this is the substantive difference and multiplies the monitoring problem; (2) populations that shift with every content update, far faster than a lending book; (3) outcomes observed in days rather than over a loan term, which makes continuous validation genuinely feasible; (4) no regulator, so the governance must be a commercial differentiator rather than a requirement; and (5) an end user who is a product manager rather than a risk professional, so the limitations must be expressed in the interface.

## Target Customer
Game analytics vendors, studio data teams, publishers, and model governance and monitoring vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Credit risk built validation and monitoring because unchecked models make expensive decisions and fail silently. One model serving thousands of heterogeneous games means validation has to be per customer, which is the multiplication to solve.
