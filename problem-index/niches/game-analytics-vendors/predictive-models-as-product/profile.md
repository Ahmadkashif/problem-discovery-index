# Predictive Models as Product

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/game-analytics-vendors|Game Analytics Vendors]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to ship churn and lifetime value scores that are actually true of a given studio's players, when the models are fitted generically across a whole customer base — and whoever makes them trustworthy takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$220M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~18% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium, generic
**Target Buyer:** Analytics product leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — per-customer fitting and calibration

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Churn scores and lifetime value estimates ship as standard features fitted generically across a customer base, and studios treat them as facts about their own players. A score produced by a model trained on a different mix of games, with different mechanics and monetisation, presented in a product interface with no uncertainty attached, will be acted on as though it were a measurement. The gap between what these numbers are and how they are used is the category's quietest liability.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A churn probability column, a predicted lifetime value figure and a segment builder that uses both. The gaps: no per-customer fitting or calibration; no uncertainty on any score; no validation against realised outcomes; no statement of what the model was trained on; and no guidance on appropriate use.
