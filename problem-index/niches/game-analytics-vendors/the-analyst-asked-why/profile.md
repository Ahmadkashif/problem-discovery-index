# The Analyst Asked Why by Thursday

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/game-analytics-vendors|Game Analytics Vendors]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let the person asked why a metric moved answer it properly within the deadline they were given — and whoever equips them takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$130M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~11% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — no tooling
**Target Buyer:** Analytics leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — investigation tooling

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every week a metric moves, the analyst is asked why by Thursday, and the answer is assembled from partial data by forming hypotheses and checking whether the numbers contradict them. The work is genuinely skilled and structurally impossible: the question is causal, the data is descriptive, the deadline is short, and the answer will be acted on as though it were established. The analyst is the organisation's shock absorber for a gap in its instrumentation, and nothing supports the role.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A query interface, a dashboard, four other systems and two days. The gaps: no investigation workflow; no standing change timeline; no reusable analysis for recurring questions; no way to record or reuse past investigations; and no mechanism for saying the data cannot answer it.
