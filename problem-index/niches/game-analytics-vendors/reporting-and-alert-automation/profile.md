## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$40M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — partial
**Target Buyer:** Analytics operations
**Automation Potential:** Very high — scheduled reporting and alerting

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A meaningful share of an analytics team's time goes on producing recurring reports: the weekly performance summary, the publisher's monthly pack, the post-release readout, the executive dashboard someone screenshots into a deck. Alongside it sit alerts — thresholds set once, firing constantly or never. Both are mechanical, both consume the team's most skilled people, and both are largely built by hand every period.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A dashboard, a scheduled export, a set of alert thresholds somebody guessed, and a person making slides. The gaps: no automated narrative generation; no alert thresholds derived from actual behaviour; no routing to the right audience; no suppression of noise; and no measurement of which reports are read.
