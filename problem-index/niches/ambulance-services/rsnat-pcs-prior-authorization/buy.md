# Dialysis Referral Platforms Extended to Ambulance PCS

**Niche:** [[niches/ambulance-services/rsnat-pcs-prior-authorization/profile|PCS & RSNAT Prior Authorization for Scheduled Transports]]
**Industry:** [[industries/ambulance-services|Ambulance Services]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Dialysis organizations already exchange referrals and documents electronically with hospitals and nephrologists; the ambulance company that carries the patient is left to fax.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep each repetitive patient's Medicare transports paid without prepayment review, and whoever has the fewest RSNAT claim lines denied per 1,000 scheduled round trips takes the account.

## The Problem
Most RSNAT patients ride to dialysis, and the documents the MAC wants (the nephrologist's PCS and notes on why the patient cannot travel by other means) sit with the dialysis unit and the physician. The ambulance supplier asks for them by fax every 60 days.

## What Already Exists
Referral and document-exchange platforms used by dialysis organizations and hospitals (e-referral networks, health information exchanges, Direct messaging), and general prior-auth tools at ordering practices, profiled at [[niches/physician-practices/ordering-practice-prior-authorization/profile|⚡ Prior Authorization at the Ordering Practice]].

## The Customization Gap
The exchange must treat the ambulance supplier as a recipient with a narrow, recurring need. It needs a PCS template carrying Medicare's required fields and a renewal schedule tied to the 60-day window. Requests must be routed to the attending physician rather than the unit's front desk, and the notes have to address bed confinement and why other transport is unsafe. Signed documents then flow straight into the supplier's packet.

## Target Customer
Operators with large dialysis transport volumes, and dialysis organizations that want their patients' transport to stop failing.

## Impact If Solved
Replacing six fax rounds a year per patient with one electronic request and five automatic renewals could save the PCS specialist ~2 hours per patient a year (an estimate), ~600 hours for 300 patients.
