# Air Ambulance IDR & Out-of-Network Negotiation Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ambulance-services|Ambulance Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Firms that negotiate and dispute out-of-network air ambulance claims under the No Surprises Act, on either side. In Q2 2025 the provider-side representatives among the top ten air ambulance initiators included No Surprise Bill (374 disputes), HSI Health (198) and Quick Med Claims (193). On the plan side, Claritev (1,046) and ClearHealth Strategies (422) were among the top ten non-initiating parties (CMS supplemental tables). They prepare open-negotiation letters, IDR submissions and offer briefs, and are paid per dispute or as a share of the award or savings.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Staff preparing disputes scale with volume but are production; analysts building reusable offer models are under 10 per firm on air ambulance (estimate)
**Output:** Filed disputes, offers and briefs; awards or savings
**Proprietary data:** Clients' claims, remittances and determinations
**Clock:** The open-negotiation and IDR windows on each dispute (production)
**Buyer:** Air operator revenue-cycle leader, or plan payment-integrity leader
