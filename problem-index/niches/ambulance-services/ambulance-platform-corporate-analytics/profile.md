# Ambulance Platform Corporate Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ambulance-services|Ambulance Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The private platforms that own many ambulance services run central finance, revenue cycle, deployment and contract analytics across their markets. Global Medical Response (AMR on the ground in 37 states and DC, plus Air Evac Lifeteam, REACH, Guardian Flight and Med-Trans in the air) cares for over five million patients a year, and in May 2024 closed a refinancing that extended its maturities to October 2028. DocGo ran 296,014 transportation trips for $200.8M of revenue in 2025 (10-K). Priority Ambulance and Falck's US operations are named from recall. Their analysts price 911 bids, track payer mix and collections by contract, monitor response-time compliance, and report to boards, lenders and, for DocGo, public investors.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–60 analysts and data staff at the largest platforms, fewer below them (estimate)
**Output:** Contract performance reviews, bid models, payer-mix and collection analysis, lender and investor reporting
**Proprietary data:** Patient-level ePCR, CAD and claims records across hundreds of contracts, the largest private ambulance corpus outside the payers
**Clock:** DocGo's 10-Q and 10-K dates, lenders' reporting covenants, county contract renewals and RFPs (client clocks)
**Buyer:** Platform chief financial officer or chief operating officer
