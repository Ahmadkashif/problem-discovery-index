# Ambulance Reimbursement & Compliance Counsel

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ambulance-services|Ambulance Services]]

## What They Do
Law and consulting firms that specialise in ambulance billing, Medicare audits, compliance programmes and transactions. Page, Wolfberg & Wirth (Mechanicsburg, Pennsylvania, founded 2000) advises EMS and ambulance agencies on revenue cycle, compliance and HIPAA. It runs billing and compliance assessments, handles Medicare audits, OIG investigations and Corporate Integrity Agreements, and sells a flat-fee advice line (pwwemslaw.com). Clients pay by the hour, by engagement or by subscription.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Attorneys and consultants under 20 at the leading firm, working matter by matter (estimate)
**Output:** Legal advice, compliance assessments, audit defence, training
**Proprietary data:** Client matters, held under privilege
**Clock:** Audit, appeal and investigation deadlines on each matter
**Buyer:** Operator chief executive or compliance officer
