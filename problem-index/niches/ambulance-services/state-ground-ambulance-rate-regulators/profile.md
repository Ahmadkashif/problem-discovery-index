# State Ground-Ambulance Rate & Balance-Billing Regulators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ambulance-services|Ambulance Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State insurance departments that administer the ground ambulance surprise-billing laws now in force in about 22 states (Commonwealth Fund, search summary). They set or approve payment standards for out-of-network ground transports in state-regulated plans, collect rate filings from ambulance services and handle complaints. Oregon's Division of Financial Regulation, for example, adopted rules under HB 3243 on standardized payment and rate reporting, effective 29 June 2026 (Oregon rule order, search summary). Federal law still leaves ground ambulance outside the No Surprises Act, and a federal advisory committee recommended protections in its report of 28 August 2024 (CMS). Taxpayers fund the work.

## Insight Function
**Size:** A few analysts per state on ambulance rates (estimate)
**Output:** Payment standards, rate-filing reviews, complaint decisions, reports to legislatures
**Proprietary data:** Rate filings and complaint files submitted to the state
**Clock:** Statutory and legislative calendars, including sunset dates such as Texas's 1 September 2027 (search summary)
**Buyer:** State insurance commissioner
