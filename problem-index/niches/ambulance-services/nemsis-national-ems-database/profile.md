# NEMSIS & the National EMS Database

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ambulance-services|Ambulance Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
NEMSIS is the national standard for EMS patient care data and the national database built from it, a program of NHTSA's Office of EMS hosted at the University of Utah. Every state submits its agencies' ePCR records in the NEMSIS v3.5 format. The NEMSIS Technical Assistance Center maintains the standard, validates submissions, publishes reports, and releases an annual public research dataset. The 2025 release, announced on 6 May 2026, holds 63,635,893 activations from 14,801 agencies in 54 states and territories (NEMSIS). Researchers request it free; the federal grant pays.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–25 staff across data management, analysis and standards work (estimate)
**Output:** The NEMSIS standard and schematron, the annual public-release research dataset, national reports and data requests
**Proprietary data:** The National EMS Database of state submissions; the public release is a de-identified subset
**Clock:** Its own annual release and standard-version calendar
**Buyer:** NHTSA Office of EMS (grant), with researchers as free users
