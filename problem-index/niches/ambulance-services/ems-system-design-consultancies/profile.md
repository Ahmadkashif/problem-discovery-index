# EMS System Design & Response-Time Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ambulance-services|Ambulance Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation firms

## What They Do
Consultancies that counties and cities hire to assess their EMS system, set response-time standards, design deployment and write or evaluate 911 ambulance RFPs. Fitch & Associates is one of the best-known: Greene County, New York engaged it in December 2023 to assess the county's EMS resources and develop options for a sustainable system (county page, search summary), and county studies by the firm appear in many public procurement records. Fees are per engagement.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Consultants scale with engagements and work one county at a time (per-engagement production); research staff building reusable products are under 10 per firm (estimate)
**Output:** System assessments, deployment studies, response-time standards, RFP documents
**Proprietary data:** Clients' CAD and ePCR extracts used under engagement agreements, plus each firm's accumulated benchmarks
**Clock:** The client's RFP and contract dates
**Buyer:** County EMS administrator or city manager
