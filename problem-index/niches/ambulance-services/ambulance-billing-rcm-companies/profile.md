# Ambulance Billing & Revenue Cycle Companies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ambulance-services|Ambulance Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Specialist firms code, bill and collect ambulance claims for operators and municipalities, usually for a percentage of collections. Digitech (Chappaqua, New York, a Sarnova company) claims "500+ organizations"; Ambulance Information Management (AIM) sells billing software and outsourced billing; Intermedix (R1 RCM), Emergicon and 911 Billing Services also appear in vendor lists (search summaries). They handle level-of-service coding, PCS and RSNAT paperwork, face sheets, denials and appeals.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Coders, billers and PCS staff scale with client volume but are production; analysts producing reusable insight are under 10 per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Paid claims, collection reports, denial and payer-mix reporting to clients
**Proprietary data:** Client claims and ePCRs under business associate agreements
**Clock:** Timely filing limits, RSNAT windows and appeal deadlines on each claim (production)
**Buyer:** Operator chief financial officer or municipal EMS chief
