# Ground Ambulance Cost Study & Rate Analysis Contractors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ambulance-services|Ambulance Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation firms

## What They Do
Research contractors that analyse ambulance costs for regulators. RAND Health Care's Payment, Cost, and Coverage Program analysed the Medicare Ground Ambulance Data Collection System for CMS, with a Year 1–2 cohort report and a Year 1–4 appendix covering data submitted through 15 May 2025; MITRE designed the sampling and instrument in 2019 (CMS). MedPAC's own report followed in June 2026. States setting ground ambulance payment rates under their new surprise-billing laws commission similar studies. Government agencies pay by contract.

## Insight Function
**Size:** A few analysts per contractor on ambulance work (estimate)
**Output:** Cost reports, sampling designs, rate studies
**Proprietary data:** Organisation-level GADCS submissions and state cost filings, held for the government client
**Clock:** The regulator's own reporting and rulemaking calendar
**Buyer:** CMS or a state insurance or health department
