# ePCR Vendor Research & Benchmark Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ambulance-services|Ambulance Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
The ePCR vendors that hold most agencies' charts publish benchmarks from them. ESO's Research and Performance Improvement team produces the annual ESO EMS Index (17.31 million 911 encounters in the 2026 edition), a Fire Index and a Trauma Index. Its research dataset comes from the ESO Data Collaborative, which ESO describes as anonymised records from thousands of agencies pooled for research and year-over-year benchmarking; the 2024 public release holds 14,831,650 encounters from 3,342 agencies (ESO). The 2026 Fire Service Index names its research authors: Remle Crowe, Senior Director of Research and Data Enablement, a principal research scientist and a research program lead, with two executives (ESO). ImageTrend sells analytics to agencies and states. The indices are free marketing; agencies pay for the software.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research staff at the largest vendor (estimate; three researchers and two executives sign the 2026 Fire Service Index, and an external Research Leadership Group of eight reviews data requests)
**Output:** Annual indices, research datasets on request, agency benchmarking dashboards
**Proprietary data:** De-identified records from contributing agencies; the full customer ePCR base behind them
**Clock:** Their own annual release calendars
**Buyer:** Vendor chief product or marketing officer
