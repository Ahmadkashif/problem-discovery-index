# EMS & Ambulance Liability, Fleet and Aviation Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ambulance-services|Ambulance Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Specialty insurance programmes price auto, professional liability, workers' compensation and accident cover for ambulance services, and aviation underwriters cover air medical fleets. VFIS, part of the Glatfelter group, insures fire departments, EMS agencies and 911 centres and, in some states, commercial emergency and non-emergency ambulance operations through its Glatfelter Commercial Ambulance programme. It sells through regional agencies and reports more than 15,000 clients (VFIS and agency pages, search summary). Operators pay premiums through brokers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Underwriters, loss-control staff and claims handlers per programme, all production; actuarial and research analysts on EMS under 10 per carrier (estimate)
**Output:** Quotes and policies, loss-control programmes, driver training
**Proprietary data:** Loss experience by service type, vehicle and driver
**Clock:** Annual renewals set by each account (client clocks)
**Buyer:** Programme chief underwriting officer
