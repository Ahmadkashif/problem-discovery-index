# Patient Transport Scheduling Software Taught Ambulance Levels

**Niche:** [[niches/ambulance-services/interfacility-transfer-intake/profile|Interfacility Transfer Intake & Level-of-Care Matching]]
**Industry:** [[industries/ambulance-services|Ambulance Services]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Hospital transport-ordering portals book rides; they do not ask the questions that decide whether a BLS, ALS or critical care crew should go.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a hospital's or nursing facility's transport request into the right crew at the door, and whoever has the lowest share of interfacility transports billed at a different level of service from the one dispatched takes the account.

## The Problem
Hospitals increasingly book discharges and transfers through portals and case-management software, but the transport order is a free-text field and a "stretcher" checkbox.

## What Already Exists
Hospital transfer-centre platforms, case-management and discharge-planning software, ride-booking portals built for NEMT and rideshare, and the web booking modules some ambulance CAD vendors offer.

## The Customization Gap
The ordering form must ask Medicare's level questions in a case manager's language: active infusions and their drugs, cardiac monitoring, airway support, isolation, weight and bariatric needs, behavioral holds, and whether a nurse must accompany. It must check PCS status for non-emergency Medicare runs and the RSNAT affirmation for repetitive ones. The result has to land in the operator's CAD as a structured job, not as an email.

## Target Customer
Hospital transfer centres and case-management departments, and operators that want hospitals to book through a structured channel.

## Impact If Solved
Moving half of a hospital's 30 daily transport requests (an estimate) from phone to a structured form saves ~1.5 hours a day of case-manager and call-taker time at each end.
