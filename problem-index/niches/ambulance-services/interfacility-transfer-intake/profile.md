# Interfacility Transfer Intake & Level-of-Care Matching

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ambulance-services|Ambulance Services]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a hospital's or nursing facility's transport request into the right crew at the door, and whoever has the lowest share of interfacility transports billed at a different level of service from the one dispatched takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$0.4B a year of call-taking and scheduling staff, transfer-centre coordination and booking software for interfacility and discharge transports (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% of 621910 receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — requests arrive by phone and fax from case managers and transfer centres, and the level of care is set from a verbal description
**Target Buyer:** Operator director of communications or hospital contracts manager; hospital transfer-centre director on the other side
**Automation Potential:** High for intake and booking; the clinical level-of-care call stays with a clinician for edge cases

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Interfacility work moves patients between hospitals, from hospitals to nursing facilities and home, and to scans and treatments. It is booked by people who are not clinicians in the ambulance company's sense: case managers, unit clerks and transfer-centre nurses. They describe the patient on the phone, and the operator picks BLS, ALS or specialty care. Too low, and the crew arrives to find a patient on a drip it cannot manage, so the run is upgraded or cancelled. Too high, and the claim is downcoded because the documentation does not support the level sent. Either way the level billed differs from the level dispatched. Competitors are operators' communications centres, hospital transfer centres and the software both use. Hospitals' discharge planning is profiled at [[niches/general-hospitals/case-management-post-acute-placement/profile|🟠 Case Management & Post-Acute Placement]]. A psychiatric hospital's intake of ED referrals, which often ends in an ambulance transfer, is [[niches/psychiatric-hospitals/intake-ed-referrals-medical-clearance/profile|🟠 Psychiatric Intake, ED Referrals & Medical Clearance]]. Wheelchair and stretcher-van trips under Medicaid NEMT brokers belong to [[industries/non-emergency-medical-transport|Non-Emergency Medical Transport]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Phone, fax, CAD call-taking screens and a few web booking portals. Gaps: no structured level-of-care questionnaire tied to Medicare definitions, no record of the level requested against the level found at pickup, and no feedback to the facilities that request the wrong level repeatedly.
