# Hospital Insurance Discovery Tools Taught Ambulance Demographics

**Niche:** [[niches/ambulance-services/face-sheet-insurance-discovery/profile|Face-Sheet Retrieval & Insurance Discovery]]
**Industry:** [[industries/ambulance-services|Ambulance Services]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Insurance discovery services find coverage for hospital self-pay accounts that carry a full registration record; an ambulance run may carry only a name spelled by ear and an approximate age.
**Tags:** #k-nearest-neighbors #logistic-regression #data-integration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to find billable coverage for patients carried with no insurance details, and whoever converts the highest share of transports opened as self-pay into paid insured claims takes the account.

## The Problem
Billing firms buy hospital-grade insurance discovery and get poor match rates on ambulance runs, because the demographics are thinner and noisier.

## What Already Exists
Insurance discovery and eligibility search services sold to hospitals, clearinghouse eligibility transactions, and Medicaid eligibility portals.

## The Customization Gap
The ambulance version must match on noisy demographics (phonetic names, approximate ages, scene addresses that differ from home addresses) and use the receiving hospital and date of transport as anchors to find the hospital encounter. It needs retroactive Medicaid checks timed to state application windows, and it must respect state ground-ambulance balance-billing rules when deciding what to bill to whom.

## Target Customer
Billing firms and operators already paying for insurance discovery on hospital terms.

## Impact If Solved
Raising match rates on self-pay runs from, say, 30% to 45% (estimates) would add 15 insured claims per 100 self-pay runs worked.
