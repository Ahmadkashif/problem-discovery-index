# Face-Sheet Retrieval & Insurance Discovery

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ambulance-services|Ambulance Services]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to find billable coverage for patients carried with no insurance details, and whoever converts the highest share of transports opened as self-pay into paid insured claims takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$0.2B a year of face-sheet retrieval, eligibility searching, insurance discovery services and self-pay follow-up for ambulance claims (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1% of 621910 receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — eligibility checks are electronic, but face sheets are requested from hospital registration by fax, portal or phone, one patient at a time
**Target Buyer:** Billing firm operations director or operator revenue-cycle director
**Automation Potential:** High — hospital face-sheet exchange, eligibility searches and Medicaid retro-eligibility checks are mechanical

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A 911 crew carries patients who are unconscious, confused, injured or simply without their cards. The ePCR records a name, a date of birth and perhaps an address. Insurance is found later: from the receiving hospital's registration face sheet, from eligibility searches across payers, or from a Medicaid application filed after the hospital stay. Runs opened as self-pay that stay self-pay are mostly written off or sent to collections, and in the 22 states with ground ambulance surprise-billing protections (Commonwealth Fund, search summary), what can be billed to an insured patient is limited too. Competitors are billing firms' own face-sheet teams, insurance discovery vendors selling to hospitals, and the hospital data-exchange feeds some ePCR vendors offer. Hospital-side eligibility and estimates are profiled at [[niches/general-hospitals/patient-access-auth-estimates/profile|⚡ Patient Access: Prior Authorization, Eligibility & Estimates]], and medical debt collection at [[niches/collections-agencies/medical-debt-collectors/profile|🔵 Medical Debt Collectors]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Hospital portals and fax requests for face sheets, clearinghouse eligibility (270/271) searches, insurance discovery services and collection agencies. Gaps: no automatic face-sheet exchange with every receiving hospital, eligibility searches run on incomplete demographics, and no tracking of which self-pay runs later became insured.
