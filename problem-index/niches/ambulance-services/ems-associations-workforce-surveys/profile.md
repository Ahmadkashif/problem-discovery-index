# EMS Associations & Workforce Surveys

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ambulance-services|Ambulance Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Trade associations for ambulance services lobby on Medicare rates and publish member surveys. The American Ambulance Association runs an annual EMS employee turnover survey with Newton 360, managed by John Carroll University's Center for Applied Talent Analytics. The 2025 edition covered 171 organizations and more than 28,488 employees and sells to non-members for $500. The 2022 edition put full-time EMT turnover at 36% and paramedic turnover at 27% (AAA pages, search summary). Air medical services have their own association. Members pay dues.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Association staff under 10 on research; the survey analysis is done by a university centre (estimate)
**Output:** Turnover and cost surveys, policy briefs, comments on CMS rules
**Proprietary data:** Survey responses contributed by member organizations
**Clock:** CMS rulemaking and congressional calendars; its own survey cycle
**Buyer:** Association executive director
