# Monitor-Defibrillator Makers' Clinical Data Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ambulance-services|Ambulance Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The makers of the monitor-defibrillators, CPR devices and transport ventilators in every ambulance, chiefly ZOLL and Stryker's Physio-Control line (the latter named from recall), also sell software that brings each device's case file of ECG, CPR quality, capnography and shocks into agency review. ZOLL's RescueNet CaseReview links case files from its defibrillators and AutoPulse device to the chart, and its data business extends to ePCR, dispatch and billing through emsCharts and RescueNet (ZOLL Data Systems). Their clinical and resuscitation science teams analyse those files to support product claims, publish research and help agencies run cardiac arrest quality programmes. Agencies pay for devices and software; the insight is a sales tool.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 clinical scientists and data analysts per major maker (estimate)
**Output:** Published resuscitation research, CPR-quality benchmarks for customers, product evidence
**Proprietary data:** Device case files uploaded by customer agencies, the largest record of what happens during prehospital cardiac arrests
**Clock:** Their own product and publication calendars; FDA submissions when a claim needs one
**Buyer:** Maker's chief medical officer or head of clinical affairs
