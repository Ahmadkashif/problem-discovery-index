# Air Medical Membership Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ambulance-services|Ambulance Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Air operators sell household memberships that waive the patient's balance after insurance pays, when a participating provider flies. AirMedCare Network, whose providers are GMR's Air Evac Lifeteam, Guardian Flight, Med-Trans and REACH, reports over 3 million members, 320+ locations in 38 states and a standard membership of $99 a year. It states that membership is not insurance and does not decide who is dispatched (AirMedCare Network pages, search summary). Households and some employers and municipalities pay.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Membership marketing and service staff; pricing and analytics under 10 (estimate)
**Output:** Memberships sold and renewed; balance waivers on covered flights
**Proprietary data:** Member rolls and the flights members took
**Clock:** Annual renewal cycles and state regulators' views on whether memberships are insurance
**Buyer:** Air operator chief revenue officer
