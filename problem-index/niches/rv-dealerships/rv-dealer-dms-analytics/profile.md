# RV Dealer Management System Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/rv-dealerships|RV Dealerships]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The analytics functions inside the dealer management systems Pass 1 names, which hold sales, finance, service and parts records across the dealer base — and which Pass 1 says lack RV-specific intelligence.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 product, implementation, and analytics staff
**Output:** Inventory and sales reporting, service scheduling, parts management, dealer benchmark reports
**Proprietary data:** Transaction and repair order records across many dealerships, held per installation
**Clock:** Daily operations, seasonal service peaks
**Buyer:** VP of Product
