# RV Service Information & Technical Content

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/rv-dealerships|RV Dealerships]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Publish the service manuals, wiring diagrams, appliance procedures and diagnostic content RV technicians work from across the automotive, plumbing, electrical, LP gas and appliance systems a single unit combines.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 technical writers, editors, and content specialists
**Output:** Service procedures and manuals, wiring and plumbing schematics, appliance and component documentation, training content
**Proprietary data:** Assembled technical content across hundreds of component manufacturers whose documentation exists nowhere in one place
**Clock:** Model year releases and component changes
**Buyer:** Editorial Director
