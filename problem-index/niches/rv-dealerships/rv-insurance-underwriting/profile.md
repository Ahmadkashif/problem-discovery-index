# Recreational Vehicle Insurance Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/rv-dealerships|RV Dealerships]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Underwrite the specialty policies covering RVs as both vehicles and dwellings, and adjudicate the claims — including the water intrusion losses that dominate the category.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 underwriters, actuaries, and claims analysts
**Output:** Rating and eligibility, claims adjudication, total loss valuation, loss frequency and severity analysis
**Proprietary data:** Claim histories by unit type, use pattern and storage arrangement
**Clock:** Annual renewals and seasonal claim patterns
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
