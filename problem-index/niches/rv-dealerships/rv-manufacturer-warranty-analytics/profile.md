# RV Manufacturer Warranty & Quality Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/rv-dealerships|RV Dealerships]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The warranty, quality and reliability functions inside the RV manufacturers, analysing dealer warranty claims to drive design changes, supplier quality actions and warranty reserves.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 quality engineers, warranty analysts, and supplier quality staff
**Output:** Failure mode analysis, warranty reserve models, supplier quality actions, service bulletins, design feedback
**Proprietary data:** Warranty claims joined to build records, floor plan, supplier and production date across the entire installed base
**Clock:** Warranty reporting cycles and model year programmes
**Buyer:** Director of Quality
