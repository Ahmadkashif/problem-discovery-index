# Recreational Lending Underwriting Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/rv-dealerships|RV Dealerships]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Underwrite the long-term consumer loans that finance RV purchases — twenty-year terms on a depreciating asset in a discretionary category — and manage the resulting portfolio and repossession exposure.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 credit analysts, modellers, and portfolio managers
**Output:** Credit decisioning and pricing, loan-to-value limits, collateral valuation policy, loss forecasting, repossession and remarketing analytics
**Proprietary data:** Loan performance joined to unit type, use pattern and resale outcome across long horizons
**Clock:** Application decisioning expectations and quarterly portfolio review
**Buyer:** Chief Credit Officer / VP of Analytics
