# RV Dealer Group Corporate Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/rv-dealerships|RV Dealerships]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator/rollup

## What They Do
Corporate analytics inside the national RV dealer groups — inventory ageing and pricing, trade-in appraisal policy, service throughput, and acquisition screening across hundreds of locations.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 analysts, data scientists, and corporate staff
**Output:** Inventory pricing and ageing analytics, appraisal guidance, service capacity planning, store performance reporting, acquisition modelling
**Proprietary data:** Appraisal values against eventual sale price across very large trade volumes — the largest record of RV valuation error in existence
**Clock:** Seasonal selling and service cycles, quarterly reporting
**Buyer:** VP of Analytics
