# Vehicle History & Title Data Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/rv-dealerships|RV Dealerships]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Assemble title, branding, damage, odometer and service records into the history reports buyers and dealers check before a unit changes hands — the closest thing to a disclosure record for a used recreational vehicle.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-800 data acquisition staff, analysts, and engineers
**Output:** History reports, title brand and salvage records, damage and loss events, valuation adjustments for history
**Proprietary data:** Records assembled from thousands of contributing sources under agreements built over decades
**Clock:** Transaction-time report requests
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Product
