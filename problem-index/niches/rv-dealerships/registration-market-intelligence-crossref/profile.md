# Registration & Market Intelligence Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/rv-dealerships|RV Dealerships]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Assemble state registration records into market share, model mix and territory penetration analytics, sold to manufacturers and dealer groups who otherwise cannot see what sold where.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-800 data operations staff, analysts, and researchers
**Output:** Registration-based share and mix reporting, territory analysis, competitive tracking, market forecasts
**Proprietary data:** Normalised registration records assembled state by state under licences and conventions that differ everywhere
**Clock:** Monthly registration data releases against manufacturer planning cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Chief Analytics Officer
