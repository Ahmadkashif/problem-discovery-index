# RV Extended Service Contract Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/rv-dealerships|RV Dealerships]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Price and administer the extended service contracts sold in the RV finance and insurance office — coverage across an asset that is simultaneously a vehicle, a house and an appliance suite — and adjudicate the claims that follow.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 actuaries, claims adjudicators, technical reviewers, and analysts
**Output:** Contract pricing and coverage design, claim adjudication, component failure frequency models, reserve setting, dealer programme profitability analysis
**Proprietary data:** Component-level failure and repair cost histories across hundreds of thousands of covered units, by make, floor plan, component and usage — the only structured record of what actually breaks on an RV and when
**Clock:** Annual programme renewals and continuous claim flow
**Buyer:** Chief Actuary / VP of Product
