# RV Inspection & Technician Certification Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/rv-dealerships|RV Dealerships]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Certify independent RV inspectors and service technicians, define inspection standards, and administer the training that addresses the multi-system competency problem Pass 1 describes.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-25 standards, curriculum, and certification staff
**Output:** Inspection standards, technician certification and curricula, examination content, inspector directories
**Proprietary data:** Certification records and inspection report formats; the inspection findings themselves belong to the inspectors
**Clock:** Certification and renewal cycles
**Buyer:** Executive Director
