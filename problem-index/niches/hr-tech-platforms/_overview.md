## Why These Niches

Voluntary attrition is the most expensive recurring event in a company, its structural drivers are visible in the data months ahead — pay compression against new hires, managers with unmanageable spans, roles with no internal mobility path, promotion velocity stalled for a cohort — and the platforms holding every one of those inputs produce a turnover percentage after the fact. That gap defines the category's most valuable unclaimed capability, and it carries an unusually sharp ethical boundary: the same data supports organisational diagnosis and individual surveillance, and which one gets built is a design decision rather than a policy statement.

Compliance and benefits is the larger block by spend and **failed the filter as one niche**. Employment compliance is a content maintenance problem — leave, sick time, pay transparency and classification rules now differ by state and increasingly by city, and the contest is detecting a change before an employer is configured wrongly. Benefits administration is a data integration problem — enrolment is configured once and carrier feeds diverge silently until a claim is denied at a pharmacy counter. Different disciplines, different failure modes, different vendors. Decomposed below.

Frontline workforces and contingent labour are the two underdigitised populations. Hourly workers are served by systems built for salaried employees, and contingent workers are frequently not in the HR system at all, which is why many large employers cannot state how many people work for them. Employees and people on leave are the two underserved constituencies — the employee is the subject of every record and the audience for almost none of them, and a leave or accommodation request is an entitlement with a statutory clock that is usually managed as an email thread.

The automation niches are the category's two permanent reconstruction burdens: job architecture rebuilt from spreadsheets on every migration, and pay decisions checked for equity and market position after they are made rather than before.

## Filter Notes

**Niche 2 failed the filter as stated and was decomposed.** "Compliance and benefits administration" is an HR operations department rather than a contest. Employment compliance is a regulatory content problem of the same shape as court rules and tenant screening ordinances elsewhere in this vault: thousands of jurisdictions, continuous change, published inconsistently, and the contested capability is detection latency. Benefits carrier connectivity is an integration and reconciliation problem: eligibility files that diverge from the system of record and are discovered when an employee is turned away at a counter, where the contested capability is detecting divergence before the employee does. Different teams, different vendors, different failures. Both sub-niches are terminal.

**Niches 1, 3–8 are terminal as stated.** Each names something testable: lead time on an attrition diagnosis and whether it names a condition rather than a person, whether an hourly worker can prove their own hours and pay, whether an employer can state its total headcount including contingent labour, whether an employee can answer a question about their own employment unaided, whether a leave entitlement is tracked against its statutory clock, whether job architecture can be inferred rather than rebuilt, and whether a pay decision is checked before it is made.
