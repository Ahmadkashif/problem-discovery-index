# Case Management Infrastructure From Adjacent Regulated Domains

**Niche:** [[niches/hr-tech-platforms/leave-and-accommodation/profile|Leave & Accommodation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/hr-tech-platforms|HR Tech Platforms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Case management with statutory deadlines, document requirements, restricted-access records and audit trails is mature software in legal, clinical and benefits administration, and leave and accommodation cases are run in Outlook.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #compliance #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #data-integration #automation #descriptive-statistics #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in leave and accommodation software is fighting to run an individual's case as an entitlement with a statutory clock rather than as an email thread — and whoever makes the interactive process trackable takes the account.

## The Problem
Everything a leave or accommodation case needs — a case record with states, deadline tracking with escalation, document requests with receipt tracking, restricted access to sensitive material, a complete audit trail, and template correspondence with variable substitution — is standard in legal case management, in clinical care coordination and in disability claims administration. Leave administrators build the equivalent from an inbox, a spreadsheet, a folder of letter templates and a calendar reminder, and the capability gap is entirely one of which departments bought software.

## What Already Exists
Case management platforms are mature across legal, healthcare, insurance claims and public sector casework, with deadline engines, document management, restricted access models and audit trails as standard. Disability carriers run sophisticated claim administration for exactly analogous processes. Workflow and low-code platforms make building a case model straightforward. Document generation with variable substitution is commodity. Nothing needs inventing.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to employment's specific entitlements and to the sensitivity of the material. It requires: (1) an entitlement engine that computes concurrent federal, state and company leave correctly, which is where manual administration most often errs and which is deterministic once the jurisdictional content exists; (2) medical information handled under a stricter access model than the general HR record, ideally in a separate store with a defined and audited access list, since a manager approving leave does not need and should not have the diagnosis; (3) clocks driven by the correct statutory triggers rather than by a generic due date, because notice, certification and determination periods run from different events and getting the trigger wrong is a common error; (4) manager-facing views that expose what a manager legitimately needs — dates, restrictions, adjustments — and nothing more, which is both a legal requirement and an obvious decency; and (5) employee-facing status, since every adjacent domain's case management shows the subject where their case stands and HR uniquely does not.

## Target Customer
Absence management and HCM vendors, third-party leave administrators, and employers running accommodation processes without a system.

## Impact If Solved
Case management is bought and proven infrastructure, and the entitlement engine is the only genuinely domain-specific component. The restricted access model for medical information is the element most often absent in current practice and is the one with the clearest obligation behind it — a manager who can see an employee's diagnosis in an HR system is a failure that case management from any adjacent domain would have prevented.
