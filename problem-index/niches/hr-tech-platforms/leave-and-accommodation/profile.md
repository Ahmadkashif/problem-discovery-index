# Leave & Accommodation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hr-tech-platforms|HR Tech Platforms]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in leave and accommodation software is fighting to run an individual's case as an entitlement with a statutory clock rather than as an email thread — and whoever makes the interactive process trackable takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.1B US spend across leave administration, absence management and accommodation case management
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of HR technology revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — accrual engines are digital and case handling is correspondence
**Target Buyer:** Leave administrators, HR compliance and disability management functions; employees are the subjects
**Automation Potential:** High — entitlement calculation, deadline tracking and documentation are mechanical

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
An employee who needs leave or an accommodation enters a process with statutory structure: eligibility tests, notice requirements, medical certification with deadlines, an interactive process that the employer is obliged to engage in and document, intermittent leave that must be tracked against an entitlement measured in hours, job protection, benefits continuation, and a return-to-work sequence. Getting any of it wrong has consequences for the employee immediately and for the employer eventually. The process is run, in most organisations, by a leave administrator using an email inbox, a spreadsheet and a set of letter templates, for an employee who is simultaneously dealing with a birth, a serious illness or a disability. It is the HR process with the highest stakes for the individual, the most statutory structure, and the least system support — and the distinction from the compliance sub-niche matters: that one is about knowing the rules, this one is about running a case.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Absence management vendors and third-party leave administrators serve larger employers; the HCM platforms track leave balances and some offer leave case management; disability carriers administer some leave types. The gaps: intermittent leave, which is the hardest to track and the most disputed, is reconciled manually against timesheets; the interactive process for accommodations is an email thread with no structure, no clock and no record of what was considered — which is precisely what an agency or a court will ask to see; certification deadlines and their consequences are tracked in a diary; concurrent entitlements across federal, state and company policy are computed by hand and frequently wrong; and the employee, who is managing a medical situation, has no view of their own case status or what is required of them next.
