# Salvage & Auto Auction Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/towing-companies|Towing Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Operate the salvage and wholesale auctions damaged and abandoned vehicles are disposed through, and sell the pricing and demand analytics derived from them — the market that sets what a lien sale or a totalled vehicle is actually worth.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 analysts, data scientists, and pricing staff
**Output:** Salvage and wholesale price benchmarks, demand and buyer analytics, condition grading, disposal channel recommendations, total loss decision support
**Proprietary data:** Every salvage transaction with damage description, condition grade, buyer type and realised price, across millions of vehicles
**Clock:** Auction cycles and insurer disposal timelines
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Analytics
