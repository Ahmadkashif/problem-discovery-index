# Towing & Recovery Association Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/towing-companies|Towing Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Produce safety standards, training and certification for recovery operators, advocate on move-over laws, and publish occasional rate and operating surveys.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-20 staff
**Output:** Recovery training and certification, safety standards, legislative advocacy, operating surveys, roadside fatality tracking
**Proprietary data:** Member surveys and an informal record of operators killed at the roadside
**Clock:** Annual convention and legislative sessions
**Buyer:** Executive Director
