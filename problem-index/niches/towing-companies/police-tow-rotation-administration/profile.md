# Police Tow Rotation & Municipal Administration

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/towing-companies|Towing Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Administer the police rotation lists that allocate accident and enforcement tows among operators, set rate schedules, inspect equipment, and investigate complaints — the mechanism that determines a large share of an operator's revenue.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 per jurisdiction — programme administrators, inspectors, and complaint staff
**Output:** Rotation list administration, rate schedules, equipment and facility inspections, complaint investigations, suspensions
**Proprietary data:** Rotation performance, response time and complaint records, held municipally with no aggregation
**Clock:** Rotation cycles and annual permit renewals
**Buyer:** Programme administrator
