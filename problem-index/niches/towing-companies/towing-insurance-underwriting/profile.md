# Towing & Recovery Insurance Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/towing-companies|Towing Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Underwrite the specialty coverages towing operators must carry — on-hook cargo, garagekeepers, and commercial auto liability on a class with severe loss experience — and adjudicate the damage claims that follow.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 underwriters, actuaries, claims analysts, and loss control staff
**Output:** Programme pricing and eligibility, claims adjudication, loss control requirements, operator risk profiling
**Proprietary data:** Claims joined to operator, equipment type, recovery type and driver history
**Clock:** Annual renewals
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
