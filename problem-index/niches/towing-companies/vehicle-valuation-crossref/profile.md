# Vehicle Valuation Guide Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/towing-companies|Towing Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Publish the values that decide whether a towed vehicle is worth redeeming, what a lien sale realises, and what an insurer pays when a vehicle is totalled after a recovery.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 analysts and data operations staff
**Output:** Wholesale and retail value guides, condition adjustments, total loss valuations, salvage value estimates
**Proprietary data:** Auction and retail transaction histories tied to configuration and condition detail no public source carries
**Clock:** Monthly guide publication and continuous auction feeds
**Buyer:** VP of Valuation Services
