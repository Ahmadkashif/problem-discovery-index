# Consumer Towing Regulation & Enforcement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/towing-companies|Towing Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Regulate non-consensual towing from private property — rate caps, signage and notice requirements, release procedures — and investigate the complaints that make this one of the most complained-about consumer service categories.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 per jurisdiction — investigators, analysts, and licensing staff
**Output:** Rate rules, licensing, complaint investigations, enforcement actions, consumer guidance
**Proprietary data:** Complaint records showing which operators and which properties generate disputes, held per jurisdiction
**Clock:** Complaint intake and licence renewals
**Buyer:** Consumer protection director
