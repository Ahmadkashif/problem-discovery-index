# Commercial Fleet Breakdown Networks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/towing-companies|Towing Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Manage roadside breakdowns for commercial fleets — dispatching heavy recovery and mobile repair, authorising work, and controlling the cost of a truck stopped on a shoulder with freight on it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 case managers, technical advisers, network analysts, and data staff
**Output:** Event triage and repair authorisation, provider dispatch and rate control, downtime and cost analytics, failure pattern reporting to fleets
**Proprietary data:** Breakdown events joined to component failure, repair performed, downtime and cost across large fleet populations
**Clock:** Downtime measured in hours against freight commitments and hours-of-service limits
**Buyer:** VP of Analytics / Chief Operating Officer
