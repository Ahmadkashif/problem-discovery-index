# Roadside Assistance Network Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/towing-companies|Towing Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Sit between the parties who promise roadside assistance — insurers, vehicle manufacturers, motor clubs, fleet operators — and the tow operators who actually perform it. They take the call, diagnose what is wrong, decide which provider gets dispatched, quote the customer an arrival time, and pay the claim. Almost all of an independent tow operator's non-police, non-private-property work arrives through this layer, at a rate it sets.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-1,500 data scientists, dispatch operations analysts, network managers, and claims analysts across the major networks
**Output:** Provider selection and dispatch routing, arrival time prediction and customer promises, service-level and provider scorecards, claim adjudication and rate setting, network coverage and capacity planning
**Proprietary data:** Tens of millions of roadside events with location, weather, vehicle, reported problem, dispatched provider, promised and actual arrival, service performed and customer outcome — the only record of this industry's demand and performance that exists anywhere
**Clock:** Contractual service level agreements measured in minutes, against a customer stranded at the roadside and a client counting breaches
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Data Science
