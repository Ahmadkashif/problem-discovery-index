# Impound Lien Processing & Title Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/towing-companies|Towing Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Run the statutory process that converts an unclaimed impounded vehicle into a saleable title — owner and lienholder identification, notice service, waiting periods and title application — across fifty states with different rules and unforgiving deadlines.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 lien processors, title specialists, and compliance analysts
**Output:** Owner and lienholder searches, statutory notices, lien sale documentation, title applications, compliance auditing
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated processing history showing which state agencies accept what, and where notices fail
**Clock:** Statutory notice and waiting periods that forfeit the lien entirely if missed
**Buyer:** Director of Title Services
