# OEM Connected Vehicle Roadside Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/towing-companies|Towing Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The connected vehicle teams inside manufacturers, whose telematics detect a fault or a crash and trigger roadside assistance before the driver calls — and whose diagnostic data could say what is actually wrong before anyone is dispatched.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 connected services engineers, data scientists, and diagnostics staff
**Output:** Fault detection and automatic incident notification, remote diagnostics, service dispatch triggers, warranty and reliability analytics
**Proprietary data:** Vehicle-generated fault codes and telemetry from the entire connected fleet, joined to service and warranty outcomes
**Clock:** Real-time incident response
**Buyer:** VP of Connected Services
