# Tire & Parts Programme Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/fleet-managers|Fleet Managers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The fleet programme groups at tire and parts manufacturers analyze wear, failure, and cost-per-mile across managed fleet accounts, and produce the specification and maintenance guidance that determines what fleets buy.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 engineers, analysts, and programme staff
**Output:** Cost-per-mile analyses, specification guidance, failure investigation
**Proprietary data:** Wear and failure data across managed fleet accounts and duty cycles
**Clock:** Programme review and contract cycles
**Buyer:** Director of Fleet Programmes / Technical Director
