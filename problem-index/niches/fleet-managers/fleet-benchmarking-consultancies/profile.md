# Fleet Benchmarking Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/fleet-managers|Fleet Managers]]

## What They Do
Mercury Associates, Utilimarc, and the fleet advisory firms benchmark fleet cost, utilization, and replacement practice against peer organizations, and produce the studies that justify fleet budgets and replacement capital to boards and councils.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 fleet analysts and consultants
**Output:** Benchmark studies, replacement analyses, fleet optimization recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Contributed fleet cost and utilization data across public and private fleets
**Clock:** Client budget cycles and capital planning calendars
**Buyer:** Principal / Director of Analytics
