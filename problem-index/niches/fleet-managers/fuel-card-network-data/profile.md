# Fuel Card Network Data Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/fleet-managers|Fleet Managers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The data and analytics groups at WEX, Comdata, and the fleet payment networks process every fuel and maintenance transaction across millions of commercial vehicles, build fraud and spend controls, and publish the fuel price and consumption benchmarks the industry watches.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-400 data scientists, fraud analysts, and product staff
**Output:** Spend analytics, fraud detection, fuel price and consumption benchmarking
**Proprietary data:** Transaction-level fuel and maintenance purchasing across a large share of the US commercial fleet — a near-real-time economic dataset nobody else holds
**Clock:** Continuous; reporting cadence self-set
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Analytics
