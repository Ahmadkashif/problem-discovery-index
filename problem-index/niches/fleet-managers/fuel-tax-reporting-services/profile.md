# Fuel Tax & Registration Reporting Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/fleet-managers|Fleet Managers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Specialist firms prepare the interstate fuel tax and apportioned registration filings commercial fleets owe — reconciling mileage by jurisdiction against fuel purchases, computing the tax due to each state, and filing quarterly. Errors produce audits and assessments, and the filing is the deliverable.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 tax analysts, auditors, and compliance specialists
**Output:** Quarterly fuel tax filings, apportioned registration, audit defence
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated jurisdictional rule interpretation and the record of which mileage and fuel documentation approaches survived state audit
**Clock:** Quarterly statutory filing deadlines and audit response windows
**Buyer:** VP of Compliance Services / Practice Leader
