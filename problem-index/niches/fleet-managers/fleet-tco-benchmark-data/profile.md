# Fleet Cost-of-Ownership Benchmark Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/fleet-managers|Fleet Managers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Vincentric and comparable providers model total cost of ownership by vehicle configuration, duty cycle, and geography — depreciation, fuel, maintenance, insurance, financing — and license the result to fleets making acquisition decisions and to manufacturers who use favourable findings in marketing.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 cost analysts, statisticians, and automotive researchers
**Output:** Cost-of-ownership models and comparative analyses — the licensed product
**Proprietary data:** Modelled cost components validated against fleet-reported experience across vehicle configurations and operating conditions
**Clock:** Model year introductions and fleet acquisition planning cycles
**Buyer:** Director of Research / Chief Analytics Officer
