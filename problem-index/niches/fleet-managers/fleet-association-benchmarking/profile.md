# Fleet Association Benchmarking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/fleet-managers|Fleet Managers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
NAFA and the private fleet councils survey members on cost, staffing, and practice, publish the benchmark reports fleet managers use in budget defence, and run the certification programmes the profession hires against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-25 researchers and programme staff
**Output:** Benchmark surveys, certification, practice guidance
**Proprietary data:** Member-contributed fleet cost and practice data
**Clock:** Annual survey and publication cycles
**Buyer:** Director of Research
