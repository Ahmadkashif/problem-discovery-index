# Vocational Upfit Engineering

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/fleet-managers|Fleet Managers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The engineering groups at upfitters and body builders spec and integrate the equipment that turns a chassis into a working vehicle — weight distribution, power take-off, body mounting — and produce the documentation that makes the finished vehicle compliant and warrantable.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-80 engineers and technical staff
**Output:** Upfit specifications, weight and compliance calculations, integration documentation
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated chassis and equipment integration knowledge across configurations
**Clock:** Chassis model year changes and fleet order cycles
**Buyer:** Director of Engineering
