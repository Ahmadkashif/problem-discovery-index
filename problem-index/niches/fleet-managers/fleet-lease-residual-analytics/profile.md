# Fleet Lease Residual & Remarketing Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/fleet-managers|Fleet Managers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The residual setting and remarketing analytics groups at Element, Holman, Wheels, and Enterprise Fleet decide what a leased vehicle will be worth at term end — committing the lessor's capital to that forecast — and manage disposal to realize it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 analysts, statisticians, and remarketing specialists
**Output:** Residual forecasts, lease pricing, disposal strategy and channel analytics
**Proprietary data:** Lease-end condition and realized resale outcomes across millions of vehicles, joined to the operating history that produced them — a link no valuation guide has
**Clock:** Lease-end schedules and quarterly reserving
**Buyer:** Chief Analytics Officer / VP Remarketing
