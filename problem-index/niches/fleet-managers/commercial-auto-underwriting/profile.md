# Commercial Auto & Fleet Risk Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/fleet-managers|Fleet Managers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Commercial auto underwriters and fleet risk engineers price a line that has been persistently unprofitable industry-wide, researching how driver behaviour, fleet composition, and safety programmes translate into loss experience, and setting the requirements fleets must meet to be insurable.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 underwriters, actuaries, and risk engineers
**Output:** Rating models, risk engineering recommendations, loss trend analyses
**Proprietary data:** Fleet loss experience joined to telematics and safety programme data across insured fleets
**Clock:** Rate filings and renewal cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer / Head of Risk Engineering
