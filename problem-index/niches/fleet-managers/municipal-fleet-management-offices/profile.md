# Municipal Fleet Management Offices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/fleet-managers|Fleet Managers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
City and county fleet offices manage public vehicle fleets, set replacement policy, and produce the utilization and cost analyses that justify capital requests to councils. Several of the largest publish their analyses openly and effectively set public sector practice.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-40 analysts and fleet engineers per large jurisdiction
**Output:** Replacement analyses, utilization studies, capital justification
**Proprietary data:** Vehicle cost and utilization data across a public fleet, with unusually complete records
**Clock:** Budget cycles and council approval calendars
**Buyer:** Fleet Director — public procurement
