# Fleet Electrification Transition Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/fleet-managers|Fleet Managers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Consultancies and specialist analytics firms assess whether a fleet's duty cycles can be met by electric vehicles, model charging infrastructure requirements and total cost, and produce the transition plans regulatory mandates and corporate commitments now require.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 engineers, analysts, and modellers
**Output:** Duty cycle feasibility analyses, infrastructure plans, transition cost models
**Proprietary data:** Telematics-derived duty cycle characterization across fleet types, joined to observed electric vehicle performance
**Clock:** Regulatory fleet mandates and capital planning cycles
**Buyer:** Practice leader / Chief Analytics Officer
