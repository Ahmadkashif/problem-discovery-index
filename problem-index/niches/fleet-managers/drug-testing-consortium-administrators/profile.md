# Drug & Alcohol Testing Consortium Administrators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/fleet-managers|Fleet Managers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Consortium and third-party administrators manage the federally mandated random testing pools, coordinate collections and medical review, and file the clearinghouse reports that determine whether a driver may operate. A programme failure is an out-of-service order.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-400 programme administrators, compliance analysts, and coordinators
**Output:** Random selection administration, testing coordination, clearinghouse reporting, audit defence
**Proprietary data:** Programme compliance and violation history across employer clients
**Clock:** Statutory random testing rates and clearinghouse query and reporting deadlines
**Buyer:** VP of Compliance / Programme Director
