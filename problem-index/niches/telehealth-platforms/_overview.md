# Niche Analysis — Telehealth Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/telehealth-platforms|Telehealth Platforms]]

## Niche Selection

These platforms hold a complete digital record of every interaction and measure visit volume with it. The eight niches follow from that: the decision at the centre of the visit, the outcome nobody checks, the two places continuity breaks, the two people the system runs on, and the mechanical layers.

## Why These Niches

The prescribing decision is where the commercial structure and the professional obligation meet, and it is the most scrutinised thing these businesses do. Outcome measurement is the absent foundation everything else would rest on. Continuity and referral closure are where episodic care fails patients. The two underserved people are the contract clinician working to throughput with no feedback, and the coordinator reconstructing care from systems that do not connect. Licensure matching and documentation are mechanical and both consume more than they should.

## Filter Notes

Seven of the eight are terminal — each names a single contest and dividing further yields features rather than markets.

**Prescribing and clinical decision-making** is not, and its halves separate on whether the same data is pointed at helping the clinician or at auditing the business. Point-of-care decision support puts history, interactions, risk factors and the relevant guideline in front of the clinician at the moment they decide. It serves the clinician, who wants it; it serves the platform, which gets better care and fewer adverse events; it is buildable from the platform's own record plus purchased clinical content; and nobody in the organisation opposes it. Prescribing pattern accountability points the same data at the platform: what share of visits for this presentation end in a prescription, how that compares to clinical norms and to in-person practice, which clinicians are far from the distribution, and whether the incentive structure is producing prescriptions the evidence does not support. That is measurement aimed at the business model itself, in a category where direct-to-consumer prescribing has already drawn federal scrutiny and enforcement; it names individual clinicians; and the finding, once written down, is discoverable. A platform builds the support half readily and defers the accountability half until a regulator asks, which is the observed history of the entire direct-to-consumer prescribing segment.

Two adjacent candidates were rejected as belonging elsewhere: **practice management and EHR software for in-person clinics** is the subject of [[industries/healthcare-practice-software|Healthcare Practice Software]], and **the operations of physical behavioural health providers** belongs to [[industries/behavioral-health-clinics|Behavioral Health Clinics]].
