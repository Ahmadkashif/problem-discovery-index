# The Care Coordinator

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/telehealth-platforms|Telehealth Platforms]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Whether the person holding a patient's care together across four disconnected systems has any tool that spans them.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.8B — 6% of US virtual care delivery
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~6%
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — a phone, a fax line and four browser tabs
**Target Buyer:** Platform operations leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very high — most of the day is retrieval, status chasing and transcription

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche

A coordinator is on the phone reconstructing a patient's care from four systems that do not connect. The referral was made, the specialist needs records, the pharmacy needs a prior authorisation, and every one of those lives in a different place with a different login and no shared view of the patient.

The role is the connective tissue of virtual care and it is the least equipped function in the industry. A coordinator's day is spent on hold with payers, faxing records to specialist offices, calling pharmacies, re-entering the same patient details into the fourth portal, and holding in their head which of forty patients has something outstanding.

The niche is distinct from care coordination as a process because the subject is the person doing it. Even a fully closed-loop referral system would leave the coordinator with the residue — the exceptions, the phone calls, the things no integration covers — and that residue is most of the job.

## Current Tools & Gaps

A ticketing or task system, the platform's clinical record, payer portals, pharmacy phone lines, fax, and a spreadsheet. Some platforms have a coordination module; most have a queue of tasks created by clinicians in free text.

The gaps are consolidation and memory. There is no single patient view spanning the systems. There is no record of what was attempted, so a coordinator picking up a case cannot see that the specialist office was called twice and did not answer. Payer portal work is entirely manual. And the coordinator's accumulated knowledge — which specialist offices respond, which payer lines are fastest, which pharmacies stock what — exists only in their head.
