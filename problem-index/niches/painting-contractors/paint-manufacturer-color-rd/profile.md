# Paint Manufacturer Colour Science & Formulation R&D

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/painting-contractors|Painting Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The colour science, formulation, and durability testing groups inside the coatings manufacturers, who develop products, run accelerated weathering programmes, and publish the annual colour forecasts the residential market follows.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-1,500 chemists, colour scientists, and testing staff across the major manufacturers
**Output:** Product formulations, durability and weathering data, colour matching systems, colour trend forecasts
**Proprietary data:** Formulation and accelerated-weathering libraries built over a century, plus spectrophotometric colour matching histories
**Clock:** Product development cycles and annual colour launches
**Buyer:** VP of Research & Development
