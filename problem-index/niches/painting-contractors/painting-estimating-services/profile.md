# Painting Takeoff & Estimating Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/painting-contractors|Painting Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Produce quantity takeoffs and bid estimates for painting contractors who cannot staff an estimator, working from drawings on a per-bid fee.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 3-15 estimators per firm
**Output:** Quantity takeoffs, priced bid estimates
**Proprietary data:** Bid and award histories, rarely retained
**Clock:** Bid due dates
**Buyer:** Owner
