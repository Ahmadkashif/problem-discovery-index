# Construction Cost Data Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/painting-contractors|Painting Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Publish the unit cost databases painting and finishes work is estimated against — cost per square foot by surface, preparation level, coating system, height and region — used by estimators, owners, and insurers who need a defensible number without a contractor's bid.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-400 cost engineers, data collectors, and analysts
**Output:** Unit cost databases, productivity rates, regional cost indices, annual cost books
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated crew productivity and material pricing collected by survey over decades
**Clock:** Annual publication with quarterly updates
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Chief Product Officer
