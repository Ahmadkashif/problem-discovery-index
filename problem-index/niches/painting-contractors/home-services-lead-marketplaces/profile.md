# Home Services Lead Marketplaces

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/painting-contractors|Painting Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator/rollup

## What They Do
Match homeowners to painting contractors and sell the leads, running the matching, pricing and quality analytics over a very large volume of residential project requests.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 data scientists, marketplace analysts, and trust and safety staff
**Output:** Lead matching and pricing models, contractor quality scoring, demand forecasting, project cost guides
**Proprietary data:** Millions of project requests joined to quotes, hires, and reviews
**Clock:** Continuous marketplace flow with seasonal demand cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Data Science
