# Architectural Specification & Technical Service Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/painting-contractors|Painting Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Manufacturer representatives who write coating specifications for architects and facility owners, survey existing substrates, and troubleshoot application problems in the field — technically real work given away to win the specification.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 specification representatives and technical service engineers
**Output:** Project specifications, substrate surveys, field troubleshooting reports, warranty determinations
**Proprietary data:** Field failure and complaint histories tied to product, substrate, and applicator
**Clock:** Design and construction schedules
**Buyer:** Director of Architectural Services
