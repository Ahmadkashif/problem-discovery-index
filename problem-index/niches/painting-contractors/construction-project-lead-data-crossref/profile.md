# Construction Project Lead & Plan Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/painting-contractors|Painting Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Track construction projects from planning through bidding and sell the leads, plans and specifications to the subcontractors who need to find work — the channel through which a painting contractor learns a job exists in time to bid it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-1,500 project reporters, plan room staff, and data operations
**Output:** Project leads and stages, plans and specifications, bidder lists, market forecasts
**Proprietary data:** Reported project pipelines assembled from permits, plan rooms, and direct reporting relationships
**Clock:** Continuous project reporting against bid dates
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Content
