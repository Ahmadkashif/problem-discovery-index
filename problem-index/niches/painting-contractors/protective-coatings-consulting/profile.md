# Protective Coatings Consulting & Failure Analysis

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/painting-contractors|Painting Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Assess the condition of coating systems on bridges, water tanks, ships, refineries and industrial structures, write the specifications the recoating work is bid against, analyse coatings that failed early, and inspect the application. The client is an asset owner deciding whether to spend millions recoating a structure now or in five years, and the answer arrives as a report.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-400 coatings engineers, corrosion specialists, laboratory staff, and certified inspectors at the larger consultancies
**Output:** Coating condition surveys and remaining-life assessments, recoating specifications, failure analysis reports, laboratory testing, inspection documentation
**Proprietary data:** Decades of field condition observations across asset types, coating systems, and exposure environments, joined to what those systems actually did — the only long-run field performance record of coatings that exists anywhere
**Clock:** Asset inspection and capital maintenance budget cycles, plus failure investigations that arrive unscheduled
**Buyer:** President / Director of Consulting Services
