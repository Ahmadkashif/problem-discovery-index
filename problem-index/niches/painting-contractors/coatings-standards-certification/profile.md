# Coatings Standards & Inspector Certification Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/painting-contractors|Painting Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Write the surface preparation and coating application standards that industrial painting specifications are built on, and certify the inspectors who enforce them on site.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-400 standards staff, technical committees, certification and training personnel
**Output:** Consensus standards, certification programmes and examinations, technical training, guidance documents
**Proprietary data:** Standards development record and certification examination performance across the inspector population
**Clock:** Standards revision cycles and certification renewal periods
**Buyer:** Chief Technical Officer / VP of Standards
