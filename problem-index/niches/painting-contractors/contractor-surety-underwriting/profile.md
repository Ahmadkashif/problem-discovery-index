# Contractor Surety & Trade Credit Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/painting-contractors|Painting Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Underwrite the bonds and trade credit lines that let a painting contractor take commercial and public work, assessing whether a small trade contractor can finish what it is about to be awarded.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 surety underwriters, financial analysts, and claims staff
**Output:** Bond capacity decisions, credit limits, contractor financial analysis, claims and completion analysis
**Proprietary data:** Financial statements and completion outcomes across thousands of small trade contractors, including which ones failed mid-contract
**Clock:** Annual bond facility renewals and per-project bond requests
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
