# Lead Paint Assessment & Environmental Testing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/painting-contractors|Painting Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Test buildings for lead-based paint, asbestos in coatings and joint compound, and other hazards before renovation, and design and clear the abatement work. Federal renovation rules make the assessment a precondition of legally disturbing painted surfaces in pre-1978 housing.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-200 risk assessors, industrial hygienists, and laboratory staff at the larger firms
**Output:** Risk assessment and inspection reports, abatement specifications, clearance testing
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated testing results by building age, region, and construction type
**Clock:** Renovation project schedules and statutory clearance requirements
**Buyer:** Director of Environmental Services
