# Lender Plan & Cost Review

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/architecture-firms|Architecture Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Before a construction loan closes, the lender hires a consultant to review the architect's drawings and specifications for completeness, check the budget, schedule and contracts, and flag risks. The same firms often go on to inspect monthly draws, the work logged as [[niches/general-contractors/construction-fund-control-inspection/profile|Construction Fund Control & Draw Inspection]]. Lenders pay because a thin drawing set at closing becomes change orders the loan did not budget for.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-300 reviewers per national firm (estimate)
**Output:** Pre-closing plan and cost review reports
**Proprietary data:** Document-completeness findings across thousands of financed projects, joinable — at firms that also inspect draws — to which projects later overran
**Clock:** Loan closing dates
**Buyer:** Head of construction risk services
