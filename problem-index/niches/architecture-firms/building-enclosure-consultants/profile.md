# Building Enclosure Consultants

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/architecture-firms|Architecture Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Building science and enclosure consultants peer-review architects' wall, roof, window and waterproofing details during design, run building enclosure commissioning through construction, and field-test mock-ups and installed assemblies for air and water leakage. They are the outside version of the principal's red pen on the envelope, and many of the same firms also do forensic investigation of failures.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-300 enclosure engineers, architects and field testers per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Design peer-review reports, commissioning reports, field test results
**Proprietary data:** Review comments and field test outcomes across thousands of assemblies — which details fail water and air tests, by system and installer
**Clock:** Design milestones and mock-up testing dates set by the project schedule; commissioning requirements in owner standards and rating systems
**Buyer:** Practice leader, building enclosure group
