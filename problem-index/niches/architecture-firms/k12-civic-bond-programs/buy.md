# Facility Condition Software Feeding Design

**Niche:** [[niches/architecture-firms/k12-civic-bond-programs/profile|Public K-12 & Civic Bond Programmes]]
**Industry:** [[industries/architecture-firms|Architecture Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Facility condition assessment software scores every building system for the bond; the design team starting the renovation measures it all again.
**Tags:** #feature-engineering #survival-analysis #data-integration #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver a voter-approved bond programme on its promised budget and schedule through state agency review (the Division of the State Architect in California), and whoever can evidence on-budget delivery wins the next qualifications-based selection.

## The Problem
Before a bond, architects or consultants walk every school and rate roofs, mechanical systems, windows and finishes to build a facility condition index and a needs list. After the bond passes, the renovation design team re-surveys the same buildings because the assessment data is in a format design can't use.

## What Already Exists
Facility condition assessment and capital planning software (Gordian's offerings, VFA by Accruent, and others) used by districts and consultants, plus Revit for the design itself.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: assessment records tied to building and room locations that can seed an existing-conditions model; system ages and remaining-life estimates carried into the renovation scope; photos and deficiency notes linked to the sheets that will address them; and condition-to-scope traceability so the district can show voters which promised repairs were made.

## Target Customer
Architects running assessment-to-design work for districts, and district capital planning staff.

## Impact If Solved
Re-survey hours removed at the start of design and a traceable line from bond promise to completed work.
