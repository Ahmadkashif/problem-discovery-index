# The Consultant Reissue Nobody Diffed

**Niche:** [[niches/architecture-firms/cd-production-sheet-coordination/profile|Construction Document Production & Sheet Coordination]]
**Industry:** [[industries/architecture-firms|Architecture Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The structural engineer reissues backgrounds the week before deadline, and the architect discovers a moved column in the RFI log.
**Tags:** #cnns #object-detection #change-point-detection #data-integration #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to cut the hours between a design-development model and an issued, internally consistent construction set, and whoever eliminates schedule, tag and cross-reference errors without adding a new authoring tool takes the account.

## The Problem
Structural, mechanical, electrical and plumbing consultants reissue their models and sheets repeatedly in the CD phase. The architect's team links the new model and hopes, or overlays sheets in Bluebeam and scans for red and green. Changes that affect the architecture — a deeper beam, a new shaft, a relocated column — are missed under deadline.

## Why It's Still Broken
Consultants don't send change lists, overlay comparison is visual and slow, and no one owns the comparison during crunch weeks.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Automatic comparison of each consultant reissue against the last, producing a list of element-level changes that touch architectural elements (clearances, shafts, ceilings, openings), assigned to the right team member before the set goes out.

## Who Feels the Pain
Job captains and project architects in crunch weeks; contractors who find the conflicts; owners who pay for them.

## Impact If Fixed
Fewer coordination RFIs and change orders from late consultant changes, at the cost of minutes per reissue.
