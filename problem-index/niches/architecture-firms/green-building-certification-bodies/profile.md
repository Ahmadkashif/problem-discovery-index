# Green Building Certification Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/architecture-firms|Architecture Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The bodies that run building rating systems — the Green Business Certification Inc. review arm behind LEED, and the operators of WELL, Green Globes and Living Building Challenge — publish the standards, review each project's documentation and award the certification. Architects prepare most of the documentation; owners pay the fees because certification is required by some public owners, used in financing and leasing, and reported in ESG disclosures. A standard LEED review takes about 20–25 days per round.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 reviewers, technical staff and standards developers per body (estimate; GBCI uses staff and contracted review teams)
**Output:** Certification reviews and decisions, rating system versions, credit interpretations
**Proprietary data:** Documentation and achieved credits for every certified project — on the order of 190,000+ LEED-certified projects cited — including energy models and design data
**Clock:** Owner occupancy, financing and leasing dates; rating-system version sunsets; public-owner mandates
**Buyer:** Chief technical officer / Head of certification
