# Architectural Licensure Examination Body

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/architecture-firms|Architecture Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
NCARB develops and administers the Architect Registration Examination, runs the Architectural Experience Program, keeps the record each candidate's licence and reciprocity rest on, and publishes annual data on the profession — 116,005 licensed architects and more than 39,000 candidates in 2024, with a 55% exam pass rate. Candidates pay exam and record fees; member boards rely on its exam.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-120 exam development, psychometric, experience and research staff (estimate)
**Output:** Licensure examinations, practice analyses, experience programme standards, annual statistics
**Proprietary data:** Item responses and experience records for every candidate who has sought a US licence
**Clock:** Exam administration, jurisdiction licensure requirements, exam-version transitions
**Buyer:** Chief assessment officer / Director of examination
