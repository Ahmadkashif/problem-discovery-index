**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor is fighting to win qualifications-based selections from institutional owners on the strength of past delivery — but health systems select on clinical planning and a record of passing state health-facility review, while school districts and civic owners select on delivering bond-funded programmes on budget, and the winners differ. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Every institutional selection asks for relevant experience: comparable projects, final cost against budget, schedule performance, references, key staff's roles. Marketing coordinators rebuild these from scratch per submission, because project descriptions in the library are written as marketing copy and the actual outcomes sit in accounting, CA records and principals' memories.

## What Already Exists
Deltek Vantagepoint's CRM and proposal module, OpenAsset for imagery and project records, Unanet CRM (formerly Cosential) for AEC pursuits, and general proposal automation tools such as Loopio and Responsive.

## The Customization Gap
The architecture-specific layer needs: project records that carry verified outcome fields — construction cost at bid and at completion against the owner's budget, change-order rate, schedule variance, agency review rounds; staff-role histories mapped to each project; automatic matching of an RFQ's evaluation criteria to the strongest evidence; and drafting tailored to the owner type, since a school board and a hospital capital committee score different things.

## Target Customer
Marketing directors and principals at institutional practices of 30 to 500 staff responding to dozens of public RFQs a year.

## Impact If Solved
Hours per submission cut substantially, and submissions that lead with verified delivery evidence rather than adjectives — which is what selection panels score.
