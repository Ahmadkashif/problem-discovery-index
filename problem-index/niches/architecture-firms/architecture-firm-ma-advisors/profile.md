# Architecture Firm Ownership Transition & M&A Advisors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/architecture-firms|Architecture Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Boutique advisors value architecture firms, plan internal ownership transitions and broker sales to larger design firms, in an industry where large firms' share of billings has grown fast and many founders are approaching retirement.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 2-10 per advisory firm
**Output:** Valuations, transition plans, transaction advice
**Proprietary data:** Deal terms and firm financials from past engagements
**Clock:** Founder retirement and transaction timelines
**Buyer:** Managing principal
