# The Field Report Typed at Night

**Niche:** [[niches/architecture-firms/construction-administration/profile|Construction Administration Services]]
**Industry:** [[industries/architecture-firms|Architecture Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The architect walks the site in the afternoon and writes the observation report from photos and scribbles after hours, days later.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #cnns #object-detection #worker-facing #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn around submittals and RFIs inside the contractual response period without taking on the contractor's means-and-methods liability, and whoever answers fastest with a defensible record takes the account.

## The Problem
Site observation visits produce dozens of photos and quick notes. The report — observed progress, nonconforming work, items for the contractor's attention — is a contract record, but it is written late, often thinly, because the architect drives straight to the next meeting.

## Why It's Still Broken
Field reports are unbilled overhead inside a fixed CA fee, and nothing in the workflow turns photos into a draft.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Photos and voice notes captured on a phone, located against the plan, and drafted into a report that groups observations by area and spec section, cites the governing detail, and carries open items forward from the last visit for the architect to edit and issue.

## Who Feels the Pain
Construction administrators; owners who need timely records; firms whose defence in a later dispute depends on what the field report says.

## Impact If Fixed
Reports issued the same day, with fuller records, at a fraction of the evening hours.
