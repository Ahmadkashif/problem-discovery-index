# Construction Administration Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/architecture-firms|Architecture Firms]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn around submittals and RFIs inside the contractual response period without taking on the contractor's means-and-methods liability, and whoever answers fastest with a defensible record takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$20B in construction administration fees (estimate, at ~20% of billings)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~20% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — logs and routing are digital on the contractor's platform; the review itself is reading PDFs side by side
**Target Buyer:** Director of construction administration; principal responsible for CA
**Automation Potential:** High for first-pass review and drafting; low for site judgement

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Under AIA B101 and A201 the architect reviews submittals, answers RFIs, observes construction, certifies payment applications and processes change orders. Every response has a contractual clock and a liability edge: the architect reviews for general conformance with the design intent and must not appear to approve the contractor's dimensions, quantities or methods. It is under-fee'd, labour-heavy and the place where drawing errors cost the firm.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Procore, Autodesk Construction Cloud and Newforma for logs; Bluebeam for markups; specifications in Deltek Specpoint or SpecLink. Gaps: no requirement-by-requirement checking; RFI responses written without searching prior answers; site observation reports typed from notes.
