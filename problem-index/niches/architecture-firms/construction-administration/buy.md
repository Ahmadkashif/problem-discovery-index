# Submittal Logs That Read the Spec

**Niche:** [[niches/architecture-firms/construction-administration/profile|Construction Administration Services]]
**Industry:** [[industries/architecture-firms|Architecture Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Newforma and Procore know every submittal is due Friday; they don't know whether the curtain wall data sheet meets the spec's air infiltration rating.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #seq2seq #compliance #automation #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn around submittals and RFIs inside the contractual response period without taking on the contractor's means-and-methods liability, and whoever answers fastest with a defensible record takes the account.

## The Problem
Submittal review is a document comparison done by a person: product data against Part 2 of a spec section, shop drawings against details and schedules. Hundreds of packages per project, each on a clock.

## What Already Exists
Newforma's submittal exchange, Procore and ACC submittal modules, Bluebeam Studio for markup, and specification-generated submittal registers.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: extraction of each spec section's requirements into a checklist; extraction of submitted values from manufacturer sheets in their varied units and test standards; requirement-by-requirement comparison; recognition of approved substitutions; and draft stamp comments in language that preserves the architect's limited-review position — inside the platform the contractor already uses.

## Target Customer
Firms whose CA teams work in clients' and contractors' Procore or ACC projects and in their own Newforma.

## Impact If Solved
Routine product-data review falls to minutes, and every review shows what was checked.
