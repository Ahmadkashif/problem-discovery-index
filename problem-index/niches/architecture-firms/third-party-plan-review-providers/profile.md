# Third-Party Plan Review & Private Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/architecture-firms|Architecture Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Private firms that review architects' permit drawings for code compliance on behalf of building departments that outsource plan review, or — in states such as Florida that allow private providers — on behalf of the applicant. They issue the correction comments and approvals that decide when a building can start. Florida's 2024 building-regulation law (HB 267, effective January 2025) tightened the timeframes officials have to act on private-provider applications and deems some approved if they miss them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-500 plan reviewers and inspectors per large provider (estimate)
**Output:** Plan review comment letters, approvals, inspection reports
**Proprietary data:** Correction comments across many jurisdictions and building types — a cross-jurisdiction map of what architects get wrong
**Clock:** Statutory and contractual plan review deadlines
**Buyer:** VP of building code services / Director of plan review
