# Embodied Carbon & LCA Data Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/architecture-firms|Architecture Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
One Click LCA, the nonprofit EC3 database and similar tools supply the material carbon data and calculation engines architects use for whole-building life-cycle assessments and product selection. Demand moved from voluntary to code when California's CALGreen embodied carbon requirements took effect in July 2024 for large nonresidential buildings, with the threshold falling to 50,000 sq ft in 2026.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-200 data, LCA and EPD specialists per vendor (estimate; One Click LCA's whole team is cited at 180 to 500)
**Output:** Curated LCA and EPD datasets, compliance reports, benchmarks
**Proprietary data:** Hundreds of thousands of curated LCA datasets, plus aggregate results of the project assessments run on the platform
**Clock:** CALGreen and local embodied carbon codes, Buy Clean procurement rules, rating-system requirements
**Buyer:** Head of data / Chief product officer
