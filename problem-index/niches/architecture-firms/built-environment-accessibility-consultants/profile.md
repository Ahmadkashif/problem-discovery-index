# Built-Environment Accessibility Consultants

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/architecture-firms|Architecture Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Firms that review architects' drawings and completed buildings against the ADA Standards for Accessible Design, Fair Housing Act design requirements and state accessibility codes, and survey existing facilities for barrier-removal plans. Much of their work is driven by settlement agreements and litigation risk, where noncompliant multifamily and public buildings must be retrofitted.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-80 reviewers and surveyors per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Plan review reports, facility accessibility surveys, transition plans, compliance certifications
**Proprietary data:** Findings from thousands of plan reviews and site surveys — which elements architects and builders most often get wrong
**Clock:** Permit submissions, settlement compliance deadlines, public-entity transition plan dates
**Buyer:** Principal / Director of accessibility services
