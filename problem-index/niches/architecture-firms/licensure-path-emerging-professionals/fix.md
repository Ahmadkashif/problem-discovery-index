# The Supervisor Who Signs Hours Blind

**Niche:** [[niches/architecture-firms/licensure-path-emerging-professionals/profile|Licensure-Path Emerging Professionals]]
**Industry:** [[industries/architecture-firms|Architecture Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** An AXP supervisor approves months of a candidate's hours in one sitting with no record to check them against.
**Tags:** #feature-engineering #large-language-models #compliance #worker-facing #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to cut time to licensure by converting a candidate's daily project work into logged AXP hours and passed ARE divisions, and whoever ties exam preparation to the work the candidate is actually doing takes the account.

## The Problem
Candidates submit experience reports in batches; supervisors — busy licensed architects — approve them from memory. Errors go both ways: hours over-claimed in areas the candidate barely touched, or real experience under-reported because it wasn't remembered.

## Why It's Still Broken
The supervisor has no view of what the candidate did except recollection, and approving hours is unpaid administrative work.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Each experience report pre-filled from timesheet and project activity, with the evidence listed per area, so the supervisor reviews and approves in minutes with something to check against.

## Who Feels the Pain
Supervisors signing for work they can't verify; candidates whose hours are delayed or questioned.

## Impact If Fixed
Faster, more accurate experience reporting and a defensible record for both parties.
