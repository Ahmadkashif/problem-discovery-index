# Adaptive Exam Prep Built on Firm Projects

**Niche:** [[niches/architecture-firms/licensure-path-emerging-professionals/profile|Licensure-Path Emerging Professionals]]
**Industry:** [[industries/architecture-firms|Architecture Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** ARE prep courses teach from generic case studies; the candidate's own firm has a dozen real projects that would teach the same objectives better.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #k-nearest-neighbors #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to cut time to licensure by converting a candidate's daily project work into logged AXP hours and passed ARE divisions, and whoever ties exam preparation to the work the candidate is actually doing takes the account.

## The Problem
Candidates prepare for exam divisions on contracts, project management and construction evaluation using generic videos and question banks, often having never seen the corresponding work. Pass rates are low and retakes cost months.

## What Already Exists
ARE prep platforms (Amber Book, Black Spectacles, PPI and others) with videos, practice exams and community; generic adaptive learning platforms.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: practice scenarios generated from the firm's own closed projects — a real change order, submittal log or fee proposal — mapped to ARE objectives; adaptive sequencing on the candidate's weak objectives; and firm-licensed delivery so the employer funds it and supervisors can see progress.

## Target Customer
Firms that pay for staff exam prep and want higher first-time pass rates.

## Impact If Solved
Higher pass rates per attempt and prep that also teaches the firm's own practice.
