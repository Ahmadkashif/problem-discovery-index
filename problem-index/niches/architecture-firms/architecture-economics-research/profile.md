# Architecture Industry Economics Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/architecture-firms|Architecture Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The AIA Economics & Market Research Group produces the monthly Architecture Billings Index from its Work-on-the-Boards panel — a reading that leads nonresidential construction by about 9–12 months and stayed below 50 through 2025 — plus the biennial Firm Survey Report, the compensation report and a consensus construction forecast. Members and the trade press use the free index; the detailed reports are sold.

## Insight Function
**Size:** under 10 economists and survey staff (estimate; no published headcount)
**Output:** Architecture Billings Index, firm survey and compensation reports, construction forecasts
**Proprietary data:** A monthly panel of firm billings, inquiries and design contracts, and biennial firm financial data
**Clock:** Self-set monthly and biennial calendars
**Buyer:** Chief economist
