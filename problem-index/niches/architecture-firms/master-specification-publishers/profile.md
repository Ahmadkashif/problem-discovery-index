# Master Specification Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/architecture-firms|Architecture Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Publishers of master guide specifications — AIA MasterSpec, now powered by Deltek Specpoint after Deltek bought Avitru (formerly ARCOM) in 2019, along with SpecLink and others — maintain hundreds of specification sections that architects edit into each project manual. Architects pay subscriptions for the content and the editing software.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 specification writers, architects and editors per publisher (estimate)
**Output:** Master specification sections, quarterly updates, evaluations and coordination notes
**Proprietary data:** The master text and, increasingly, subscriber editing behaviour — which paragraphs firms keep, delete or rewrite
**Clock:** Code and referenced-standard revision cycles; publisher's own update calendar
**Buyer:** Content director / Head of specification products
