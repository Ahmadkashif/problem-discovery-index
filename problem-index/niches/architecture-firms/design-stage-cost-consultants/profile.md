# Design-Stage Cost Consultants

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/architecture-firms|Architecture Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Independent cost consultants — the quantity surveying firms and US cost-management specialists — price the architect's design at each stage for the owner: a cost plan at schematic design, updated estimates at design development and construction documents, and reconciliation with contractors' pricing. Several publish construction cost reports from their own data. Owners pay them to keep the design within the budget the architect is contractually bound to design to.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-500 estimators and cost managers per large firm; many boutiques of under 20
**Output:** Stage cost plans, estimates, value-engineering studies, published cost indices
**Proprietary data:** Priced estimates and outturn costs across thousands of projects by building type, region and system
**Clock:** Owner design-stage approvals, board and funding deadlines, bid dates
**Buyer:** Head of cost research / Managing principal
