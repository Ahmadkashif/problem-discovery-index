# Large-Firm Design Research Institutes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/architecture-firms|Architecture Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The largest architecture firms run in-house research groups that study workplace performance, healthcare outcomes, learning environments and materials, publish surveys and reports, and feed findings into design practice. The research supports business development and design quality; clients pay for the design, not the research.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 researchers per large firm (estimate)
**Output:** Published research reports, workplace and occupant surveys, post-occupancy evaluations
**Proprietary data:** Post-occupancy and occupant survey data from client buildings, and the firm's own project records
**Clock:** Self-set publication calendars
**Buyer:** Director of research
