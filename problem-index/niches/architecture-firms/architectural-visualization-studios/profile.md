# Architectural Visualization Studios

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/architecture-firms|Architecture Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Studios, many offshore, that produce renderings, animations and virtual tours of unbuilt projects for architects and developers, priced per image or per package.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Production artists, sometimes in the hundreds; analytical staff typically under 10
**Output:** Renderings, animations, interactive tours
**Proprietary data:** Asset libraries and past imagery; no analytical corpus
**Clock:** Client marketing and entitlement presentation dates
**Buyer:** Studio founder
