# State Architecture Registration Boards

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/architecture-firms|Architecture Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Each of the 55 US jurisdictions has a board that licenses architects, sets practice and seal rules, and investigates complaints, usually relying on NCARB's exam and experience record.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 2-15 staff per board, often shared with other professions
**Output:** Licensure decisions, disciplinary orders, rules
**Proprietary data:** Licensee and complaint records for the jurisdiction
**Clock:** Licence renewal cycles and statutory complaint timelines
**Buyer:** Executive director
