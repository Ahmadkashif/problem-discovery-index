# Retrieval & Synthesis

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ux-research-agencies|UX Research Agencies]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a usable answer out of years of transcripts and tagged findings when the question is a design question rather than a keyword — and whoever answers it takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$0.4B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — keyword search
**Target Buyer:** Research operations
**Automation Potential:** Very high — retrieval and synthesis over existing material

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This half works on the corpus as it is. Whatever was contributed, however inconsistently tagged, however variable in quality — the question is whether a useful answer can be extracted from it. It is a retrieval and synthesis problem over material the team already holds, deployable without changing anyone's habits, and valuable immediately even if no further material is ever contributed properly. That independence from the curation problem is what makes it a separate purchase.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A search box matching words in transcripts and tags. The gaps: no semantic retrieval; no synthesis across studies; no evidence weighting; no contradiction surfacing; and no handling of inconsistent tagging.
