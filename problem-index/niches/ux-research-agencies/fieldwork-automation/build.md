# From Approved to In-Field in a Day

**Industry:** [[industries/ux-research-agencies|UX Research Agencies]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a study from approved to in-field without a week of setup, and whoever automates that takes the account.

## The Problem
Study setup is repetitive work done from scratch each time. The screener is rebuilt, the discussion guide is adapted from the last one, the recording and consent flow is configured again, the recruitment brief is rewritten, and the observer arrangements are made by email. Each study differs a little and is set up entirely. The week it consumes is a week the study is not running, and it falls on the same people who are running three other studies.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Tooling in this space is point solutions — a screener tool, a scheduling tool, a recording tool — with nothing joining them. Study templates are assumed to constrain method. Each agency's process is its own. And the setup is billable or absorbed.

## What to Build
Template the study and generate what follows from a definition. Define a study once — objective, profile, method, stimulus — and generate the screener, recruitment brief, schedule and consent flow from it, which is the core and collapses the week. Maintain reusable templates per study type so a usability test, a concept test and a diary study each start configured. Generate a first-draft screener from a participant profile definition rather than writing questions each time. Set up recording, consent and observer access automatically as part of scheduling, which is where most of the fiddly failure happens. Reuse and adapt discussion guides with the previous study's version as a starting point. Brief observers automatically with the guide, the schedule and the conventions, since that currently happens by email and inconsistently. Prepare the stimulus and its variants where the method requires it. Check the setup against a readiness list before the first session, which catches the recurring omissions. Keep everything for the study in one place rather than across five tools. And make the templates editable by researchers, since a rigid template will be abandoned.

## Target Customer
UX research agencies and in-house teams, research operations, research platform vendors, and workflow automation providers.

## Impact If Built
A week of repetitive setup stands between a decision and the first session, and most of it was done last month. Generating the screener, schedule and consent from a single study definition collapses it.
