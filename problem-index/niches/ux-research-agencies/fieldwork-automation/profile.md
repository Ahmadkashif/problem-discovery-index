# Fieldwork Automation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ux-research-agencies|UX Research Agencies]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a study from approved to in-field without a week of setup, and whoever automates that takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$0.2B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — partial
**Target Buyer:** Research operations
**Automation Potential:** Very high — setup, capture and logistics

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Between a study being approved and the first session sits a week of setup: building the screener, configuring the recruitment, writing the discussion guide from the last one, setting up recording and consent, preparing the stimulus, briefing observers and booking the room or the link. Almost all of it is repeated from the previous study with small variations, almost all of it is manual, and it is the difference between a study running this month and next.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A screener tool, a scheduling link, a recording setup and last study's documents copied into a new folder. The gaps: no reusable study templates; no generated screeners from a profile definition; no automated recording and consent setup; no observer briefing flow; and no reuse of discussion guides.
