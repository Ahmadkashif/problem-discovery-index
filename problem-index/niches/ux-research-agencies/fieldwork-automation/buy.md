# Study Templates From Survey Platforms

**Niche:** [[niches/ux-research-agencies/fieldwork-automation/profile|Fieldwork Automation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/ux-research-agencies|UX Research Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Survey platforms made study setup a template-and-configure exercise, and qualitative fieldwork is assembled from scratch.
**Tags:** #automation #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #evaluation-metrics #compliance #quick-win #descriptive-statistics #large-language-models
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a study from approved to in-field without a week of setup, and whoever automates that takes the account.

## The Problem
Survey platforms reduced study setup to selecting a template, configuring a sample and launching. Validated question libraries, quota configuration, routing logic, panel integration and automatic fielding are all built in, and a competent researcher can be in field the same day. Qualitative research, which needs fewer participants and less complex instrumentation, takes a week to set up because the tooling is fragmented.

## What Already Exists
Study templates by research type; validated question and screener libraries; quota and routing configuration; integrated panel fielding; and one-click launch with monitoring.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to moderated sessions with humans, stimulus and consent rather than a self-serve instrument. It requires: (1) scheduling live sessions across participant, moderator and observer availability, which no survey platform models — this is the substantive difference and is the hardest logistical piece; (2) consent and non-disclosure over unreleased products with records attached to each session; (3) stimulus preparation — prototypes, builds, environments — as part of setup; (4) a discussion guide rather than a question set, which is a document rather than a configuration; and (5) observers who must be briefed and given access.

## Target Customer
UX research agencies and in-house teams, research operations, research platform vendors, and survey platform vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Survey platforms made setup a same-day template exercise with validated libraries and integrated fielding. Live session scheduling across three parties, with consent and stimulus, is what the qualitative version has to add.
