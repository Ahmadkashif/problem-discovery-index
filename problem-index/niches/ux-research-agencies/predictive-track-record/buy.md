**Industry:** [[industries/ux-research-agencies|UX Research Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Forecasting research built scoring rules and calibration measurement because unscored predictions teach nobody anything, and research findings are never scored.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show that its predictions about user behaviour were correct, in a discipline that has never recorded one — and whoever builds that record takes the account.

## The Problem
Decision and forecasting research established that expert predictions improve only when they are recorded, resolved and scored, and built the apparatus: explicit probabilistic forecasts, proper scoring rules, calibration curves, resolution criteria agreed in advance, and track records maintained over time. The finding that matters most is that unscored expertise does not improve and unscored confidence is systematically miscalibrated. Research findings are expert predictions that are never scored.

## What Already Exists
Explicit probabilistic forecasting formats; proper scoring rules; calibration curves and reliability diagrams; resolution criteria agreed at forecast time; and maintained individual track records.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to qualitative findings about mechanisms rather than probabilities about events. It requires: (1) predictions that are mostly about what will happen and why rather than about whether a discrete event occurs, so the resolution criteria must be constructed rather than stated — this is the substantive difference; (2) resolution depending on whether the client acted, which confounds the score; (3) outcomes observable only in the client's systems; (4) practitioners who would resist probabilistic framing as alien to qualitative method, so the format must respect the discipline; and (5) small numbers of predictions per researcher, limiting what calibration can be established.

## Target Customer
UX research agencies, in-house research functions, professional bodies, and forecasting and measurement vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Forecasting research established that unscored expertise does not improve and unscored confidence is miscalibrated. Predictions about mechanisms rather than events, resolved only if the client acted, is what has to be constructed here.
