# Predictive Track Record

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ux-research-agencies|UX Research Agencies]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show that its predictions about user behaviour were correct, in a discipline that has never recorded one — and whoever builds that record takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$0.9B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~22% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — never checked
**Target Buyer:** Agency leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — prediction capture and verification

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Research makes testable predictions about what users will do, hands them over in a report, and the discipline has no record of how often those predictions were correct. The consequence is not only intellectual: research is the first budget line cut in a downturn, and the stated reason is always an inability to demonstrate return — which cannot be settled, because settling it needs exactly the outcome data nobody collects. The corpus that would fix it is a few years of studies matched to subsequent outcomes.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A findings report and a testimonial. The gaps: predictions not stated in a testable form; no record of what was predicted; no access to post-release outcomes; no verification of any past prediction; and no accuracy figure anywhere in the discipline.
