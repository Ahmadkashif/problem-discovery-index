## The Problem
Between fieldwork and a readout sits the analysis: transcribing, reading, marking what matters, grouping across participants, identifying patterns, deciding what is a finding, and building slides. Most of that is mechanical and the rest is the entire value of the discipline. The mechanical part consumes the available time, so the judgement happens at the end, tired, against a deadline — which is where the quality of a finding is actually determined.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Qualitative software was built for academic timescales and adopted thinly in commercial practice. Researchers are rightly protective of interpretation and read automation as a threat to it. Every study's coding framework is bespoke. And the analysis time is billable.

## What to Build
Take the transcript work and the grouping out, and leave the interpretation. Transcribe, align and index everything automatically as fieldwork completes rather than afterwards, which is the core and returns most of the analysis window to analysis. Suggest candidate codes against the study's framework for the researcher to confirm rather than assigning them, which keeps interpretation where it belongs. Cluster similar statements across participants, since a finding is almost always a repeated pattern and finding it is what the reading is for. Surface disconfirming instances explicitly, because a rushed synthesis finds what it expects. Count how many participants support each candidate finding automatically, which is the figure that should appear in the report and usually does not. Reuse coding frameworks across studies so the agency's method accumulates. Link every finding to the exact moments supporting it, which makes it checkable and quotable. Report coverage — how much of the material informed the findings — which is the honest quality signal nobody has. Support two researchers coding independently with agreement measured, which is standard method and rarely affordable. And generate the readout's skeleton from the coded material, which is a meaningful share of the remaining time.

## Target Customer
UX research agencies and consultancies, in-house research teams, research repository vendors, and qualitative analysis software providers.

## Impact If Built
The mechanical part consumes the window and the judgement happens tired, at the end, which is where finding quality is determined. Automatic transcription, indexing and clustering returns the time to the thinking.
