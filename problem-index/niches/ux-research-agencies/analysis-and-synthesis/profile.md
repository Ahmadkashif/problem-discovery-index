# Analysis & Synthesis

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ux-research-agencies|UX Research Agencies]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn twelve hours of interviews into findings without the thinking getting whatever time is left after the transcript reading — and whoever automates the mechanical layer takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~25% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — manual
**Target Buyer:** Research leads
**Automation Potential:** Very high — transcription, coding and clustering support

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Twelve hours of interviews become a findings deck through a weekend of transcript reading, sticky-note clustering and slide building, and the analysis that needed thinking gets whatever time is left. It is the largest cost in a study, the part most compressed when a deadline moves, and the part that determines whether the findings are any good. The material is rich and structured enough to support substantial automation of everything except the judgement.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A transcript, a spreadsheet or a virtual whiteboard, and a slide template. The gaps: transcription and coding done manually; no clustering support; no cross-participant pattern surfacing; no reuse of coding frameworks; and no record of how much of the material actually informed each finding.
