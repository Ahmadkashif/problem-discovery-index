# The Research Operations Person

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ux-research-agencies|UX Research Agencies]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stop an entire agency's research pipeline depending on one person's spreadsheets — and whoever removes that dependency takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$0.35B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — one person
**Target Buyer:** Operations leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very high — pipeline, consent and payment automation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
One person keeps the participant pipeline, the consent paperwork, the incentive payments and the calendar working for every study in the agency, and everything upstream stops when any of it breaks. They are the single point of failure for the whole firm's output, they are typically the most junior function in it, and the work is invisible until it is missing. Nothing in the tooling market is built for them specifically, and the role has the highest turnover in the agency.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A scheduling tool, a panel account, an email inbox, a payments process and several spreadsheets. The gaps: no unified pipeline view; consent and agreement handling manual; incentive payment slow and manual; no capacity view across concurrent studies; and no handover if the person is unavailable.
