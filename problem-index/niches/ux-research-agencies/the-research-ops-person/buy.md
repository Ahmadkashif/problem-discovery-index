# Coordination Tooling From Clinical Site Operations

**Niche:** [[niches/ux-research-agencies/the-research-ops-person/profile|The Research Operations Person]]
**Industry:** [[industries/ux-research-agencies|UX Research Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Clinical site coordination is a defined role with purpose-built systems, and research operations is one person with spreadsheets.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #automation #compliance #data-integration #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #descriptive-statistics #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stop an entire agency's research pipeline depending on one person's spreadsheets — and whoever removes that dependency takes the account.

## The Problem
Clinical research coordination is a recognised role supported by real systems: participant screening and enrolment, electronic consent with audit trails, visit scheduling, retention contact, payment administration and complete records. The role is defined, the systems are mature, and the whole point is that a site does not stop because one coordinator is away. Research operations does structurally similar work with none of it.

## What Already Exists
Participant screening and enrolment workflows; electronic consent with audit trails; visit scheduling across constraints; retention and reminder programmes; and participant payment administration.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a light, fast, commercially priced version. It requires: (1) tooling affordable to a twenty-person agency rather than to a clinical site, which rules out the incumbent systems entirely and is the substantive difference; (2) non-disclosure over unreleased products as the compliance concern rather than patient safety; (3) recruitment in days and studies in weeks rather than enrolment over months; (4) device, software and context requirements as scheduling constraints; and (5) a single operations person rather than a coordinator role with cover, meaning the system must itself provide the continuity.

## Target Customer
UX research agencies and in-house teams, research operations specialists, panels, and research operations vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Clinical coordination is a defined role with mature systems, designed so a site does not stop when one person is away. Tooling priced for a twenty-person agency is what has to change for any of it to transfer.
