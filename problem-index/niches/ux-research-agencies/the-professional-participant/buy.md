# Reputation and Matching From Labour Marketplaces

**Industry:** [[industries/ux-research-agencies|UX Research Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Labour marketplaces built reputation, matching and prompt payment because supply quality depends on it, and research panels built a screener.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make research participation work for the people who do it repeatedly for income, because their behaviour is what determines whether any study's sample is real — and whoever serves them takes the account.

## The Problem
Online labour marketplaces learned that supply quality depends on how the supply side is treated, and built accordingly: reputation systems that reward accurate self-description, matching that routes work to people who fit, transparent pay rates, prompt payment, and dispute routes. The lesson is that a marketplace which pays only for outcomes and punishes honesty gets dishonesty. Research panels pay for qualification and get participants who qualify.

## What Already Exists
Two-sided reputation systems; matching on verified rather than claimed attributes; transparent rate and opportunity visibility; prompt and guaranteed payment; and dispute and appeal routes.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a marketplace where the buyer needs a specific rare profile and the worker is paid for an hour, not an outcome. It requires: (1) matching on demographic and behavioural attributes that the worker has no portfolio to demonstrate, so reputation must be built on accuracy of self-description rather than on work quality — this is the substantive difference; (2) studies that need a specific profile rather than a general skill, so the matching is a rarity problem; (3) participation frequency needing a cap for validity reasons, which no labour marketplace imposes; (4) client confidentiality preventing the usual transparency about what the work is for; and (5) panels whose business model rewards throughput rather than accuracy.

## Target Customer
Recruitment panels and participant marketplaces, research agencies, participants, and marketplace platform vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Labour marketplaces learned that paying only for outcomes and punishing honesty gets dishonesty, and built reputation and matching in response. Reputation based on accuracy of self-description, with a frequency cap, is what the research version needs.
