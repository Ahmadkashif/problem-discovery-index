# The Professional Participant

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ux-research-agencies|UX Research Agencies]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make research participation work for the people who do it repeatedly for income, because their behaviour is what determines whether any study's sample is real — and whoever serves them takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$0.25B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~6% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — nothing exists
**Target Buyer:** Panels and participants
**Automation Potential:** Medium — matching, payment and reputation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A population of people take part in research repeatedly, as a meaningful part of their income. They are screened aggressively, paid modestly and often late, told nothing about what their session contributed to, and have every incentive to shade their answers to qualify. They are simultaneously the supply side of the entire industry and the single largest threat to its validity, and nobody has built anything for them — which is precisely why the incentives point the way they do.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A panel profile, a screener, an incentive paid eventually, and no relationship. The gaps: no honest matching to studies they genuinely fit; no prompt or reliable payment; no reputation that rewards honesty; no feedback on what their participation produced; and no route to raise a problem.
