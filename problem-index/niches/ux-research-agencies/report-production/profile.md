# Report & Deliverable Production

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ux-research-agencies|UX Research Agencies]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce the deliverable without a day of slide building, and whoever automates it takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$0.1B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — manual
**Target Buyer:** Research leads
**Automation Potential:** Very high — generation from the analysis

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The deliverable — a findings deck, a highlight reel, a summary for executives — is built by hand at the end of every study, from material that already exists in the analysis. Slides are laid out, clips are cut, quotes are copied, and the same structure is rebuilt each time. It is a day or more per study, it happens when time is shortest, and it is the least valuable use of a researcher's remaining hours.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A slide template, a video editor and the analysis in another tool. The gaps: no generation of the deck from the coded analysis; no automatic clip extraction; no audience-specific versions; no reuse of structure; and no consistency across the agency's output.
