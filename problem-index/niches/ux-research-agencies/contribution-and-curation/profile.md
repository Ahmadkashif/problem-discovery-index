# Contribution & Curation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ux-research-agencies|UX Research Agencies]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get research written up in a form worth retrieving, when doing it properly competes with the next study and benefits somebody else later — and whoever solves that takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$0.3B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — goodwill
**Target Buyer:** Research leadership
**Automation Potential:** Medium — workflow and incentive design

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This half is about what goes in and who maintains it. Writing a study up so that it is retrievable later costs the researcher time at the end of a project, when the next one has started, for a benefit that accrues to a colleague months from now. No amount of search technology fixes a corpus that was never curated, and no mandate survives contact with a delivery schedule. It is a practice, incentive and workflow problem sold to leadership rather than a product sold to operations.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A repository with an upload button and an expectation. The gaps: no contribution built into project close; no standard for what a contributed finding looks like; no curation or retirement of stale material; nobody owning quality; and no visibility of who contributes or who benefits.
