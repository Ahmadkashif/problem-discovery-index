# Getting the Work In

**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get research written up in a form worth retrieving, when doing it properly competes with the next study and benefits somebody else later — and whoever solves that takes the account.

## The Problem
Contribution to a repository is an unfunded act of generosity performed at the worst possible moment. The study is finished, the readout has happened, the next project has started, and the researcher is asked to write findings up in a structured form for the benefit of someone they may never meet. Predictably, it is skipped, or done minimally, and the resulting corpus is patchy, inconsistently structured and full of material nobody can assess.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The incentive is entirely misaligned and no tooling changes that. Mandates lose to delivery schedules. Nobody owns curation in teams too small to staff it. And the cost of a patchy corpus is borne later by people who do not know what they are missing.

## What to Build
Make contribution a by-product rather than a task, and put curation somewhere. Generate the structured contribution from the study's own artefacts — the coded material, the readout, the notes — so contributing costs minutes rather than hours, which is the core and is the only version that survives a schedule. Define what a contributed finding must contain: a claim, its evidence, its sample, its date and its product context, since material without those cannot be assessed later. Build contribution into project close as a gate rather than as an aspiration. Curate automatically where possible — deduplicate, flag stale findings, group related ones — as no small team will staff a curator. Retire or mark findings whose product has changed, because a confidently retrieved obsolete finding is worse than nothing. Show researchers when their contributions were used, which is the only reliable motivator available. Report contribution and use together, so the corpus's health is visible. Assign ownership per practice area rather than centrally, which scales to small teams. Make the structured form the deliverable rather than an extra artefact, which removes the duplication entirely. And keep the standard low enough to be met, since a high standard produces an empty repository.

## Target Customer
UX research agencies and in-house research teams, research leadership, repository vendors, and knowledge management providers.

## Impact If Built
Contribution is an unfunded act of generosity at the worst possible moment, which is why the corpus is patchy. Generating it from the study's own artefacts makes it a by-product rather than a task.
