# Research Repository

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ux-research-agencies|UX Research Agencies]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make years of accumulated research answerable to the question somebody is asking today — and whoever does takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$0.7B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~18% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium, unused
**Target Buyer:** Research operations
**Automation Potential:** High — retrieval plus curation practice

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every research team now has a repository holding years of transcripts and tagged findings, and the most common thing anyone says about it is that nobody searches it. The material represents an enormous amount of paid-for work about real users, and the same questions are researched again because retrieving the previous answer is harder than running a new study. The tooling exists and the outcome is unchanged, which is itself the clue about where the problem is.

### Contested sub-niches
This niche is not terminal. It names an artefact, and the failure has two independent causes:
- [[niches/ux-research-agencies/retrieval-and-synthesis/profile|🎯 Retrieval & Synthesis]] — getting an answer out of what is there
- [[niches/ux-research-agencies/contribution-and-curation/profile|🎯 Contribution & Curation]] — getting material in that is worth retrieving

## Current Tools & Gaps
A repository product, a tagging taxonomy and an intention to keep it current. The gaps: search that matches words rather than questions; material contributed inconsistently; no synthesis across studies; no curation or retirement; and no measure of whether anyone uses it.
