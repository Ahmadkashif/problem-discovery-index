# Outside Special Processing & Sub-Tier Expediting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/aerospace-parts-suppliers|Aerospace Parts Suppliers]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give production control a trusted completion date for every lot at an outside heat treater, plater or NDT house, and whoever's predicted dates hold best against processors' real turn times takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$500M a year in expediting and production-control labour spent on outside processing, plus visibility tools, at US aerospace suppliers (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.6% of the two codes' receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — status by phone and email; processors' systems don't talk to suppliers'
**Target Buyer:** Production control manager or VP Operations
**Automation Potential:** High for status capture and date prediction

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Special processes (heat treatment, chemical processing and plating, NDT, shot peening, coatings) must be done by processors with Nadcap accreditation that the customer has approved. Nadcap lists 4,500+ accredited suppliers across 24 critical processes (PRI's own page), and its most audited categories are reported to be chemical processing, heat treating and NDT (search summary). A detail part may leave the shop two or three times, and each trip is a black box of queue and furnace time that the shop does not control. As Boeing's 737 rate moved from 42 to 47 in 2026 and Airbus worked around a Pratt & Whitney engine shortage, these processors became the hidden constraint behind many delinquent lines (practitioner view, not a measured share).

## Current Tools & Gaps
ERP outside-processing operations, purchase orders and packing slips; the primes' supplier portals for delinquency lists; newer platforms such as First Resonance's ION; sub-tier risk mapping from Exiger and others, the archetype indexed as [[niches/contract-manufacturing/supply-chain-risk-intelligence/profile|Multi-Tier Supply Chain Risk Intelligence]]. The gaps: no lot-level status from processors, lead times fixed in the ERP, and no learning from processors' real turn times.
