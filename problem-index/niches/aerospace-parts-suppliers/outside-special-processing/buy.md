# Supplier Portals Extended to the Sub-Tier

**Niche:** [[niches/aerospace-parts-suppliers/outside-special-processing/profile|Outside Special Processing & Sub-Tier Expediting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/aerospace-parts-suppliers|Aerospace Parts Suppliers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Primes already push delinquency lists to suppliers through portals; the same visibility stops one tier down, at the processors that cause much of the delay.
**Tags:** #data-integration #time-series-forecasting #workflow-orchestration #automation #large-language-models #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give production control a trusted completion date for every lot at an outside heat treater, plater or NDT house, and whoever's predicted dates hold best against processors' real turn times takes the account.

## The Problem
A tier-2 supplier receives a daily delinquency list from its prime's portal and must answer with recovery dates. Its own processors have no portal. The supplier ends up being the human API between two systems.

## What Already Exists
Supplier collaboration portals (Exostar and the primes' own), factory platforms with supplier modules such as First Resonance's ION, and multi-tier risk tools such as Exiger.

## The Customization Gap
A light processor-side layer (email and phone capture for the small processors, simple portal for the larger) that records receipt, start and ship per lot. Status flows up into the supplier's ERP and out to the prime's delinquency response with Nadcap and approval status attached. Processors must see value too, for example fewer status calls and visibility of incoming work.

## Target Customer
Suppliers answering daily delinquency lists from two or more primes.

## Impact If Solved
One status source from processor to prime removes the double handling and cuts the daily delinquency response from ~1–2 hours to ~15 minutes per prime (estimate).
