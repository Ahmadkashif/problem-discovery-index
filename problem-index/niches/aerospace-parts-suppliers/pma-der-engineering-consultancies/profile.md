# PMA & DER Engineering Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/aerospace-parts-suppliers|Aerospace Parts Suppliers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Engineering firms staffed with FAA Designated Engineering Representatives, or holding organisation designation authorisations, that prepare and find compliance on PMA substantiation packages, repairs and alterations for parts makers and operators. A PMA applicant without in-house designees pays them to turn its reverse-engineering data into an approvable test-and-computation package.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-80 engineers and designees per larger firm (estimate)
**Output:** Substantiation reports, compliance findings, test plans and analyses
**Proprietary data:** Experience of which equivalence arguments the FAA accepts for which part types; the packages themselves belong to clients
**Clock:** FAA review cycles and the applicant's programme dates
**Buyer:** Principal engineer or managing director
