# Aviation Products Liability Underwriters

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/aerospace-parts-suppliers|Aerospace Parts Suppliers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The specialist aviation insurers and pools that write products liability and grounding cover for parts makers, among them Global Aerospace, AIG, Allianz, Starr, Chubb and Lloyd's syndicates (recalled). Underwriters assess each supplier's parts, programmes, quality record and claims history to price a policy.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 aviation underwriters, engineers and claims specialists per carrier (estimate)
**Output:** Underwriting assessments and prices; claims investigations
**Proprietary data:** Claims and loss history across insured parts makers
**Clock:** Policy renewal dates
**Buyer:** Head of aviation underwriting
