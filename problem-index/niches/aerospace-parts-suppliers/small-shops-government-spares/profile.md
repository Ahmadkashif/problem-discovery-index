# Small Shops Bidding Government Spare Parts

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/aerospace-parts-suppliers|Aerospace Parts Suppliers]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to put a small AS9100 shop's quotes on the government solicitations it can actually win, and whoever lifts the shop's awards per quote submitted takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$150M a year in bid triage, quoting labour and bid-tool subscriptions at small suppliers and distributors quoting aviation spares to DLA and the services (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** small in receipts; the audience spans the ~913 firm counts under 500 employees in the two codes plus machine shops and distributors coded elsewhere (Census SUSB 2022)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-medium — DIBBS and SAM.gov are digital; triage and technical-data checks are manual
**Target Buyer:** Owner, general manager or contracts manager at a 10-to-100-person supplier
**Automation Potential:** High for screening and drafting; pricing judgement stays with the owner

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
DLA Aviation manages more than 1.8 million national stock numbers for 2,341 weapon systems (search summary of a DLA flyer), and much of its demand arrives as small, short-fuse RFQs on DIBBS, many set aside for small business. The work rewards shops that can screen fast: approved-source restrictions, critical safety items, source approval requests, technical data access through cFolders and JCP certification, first article test requirements and specialty-metals clauses (recalled DLA practice). Government contracting tools are built for primes chasing large programmes, and quoting tools for commercial RFQs. The small shop on DIBBS is served by neither, which is why bid tools such as SalesPatriot have appeared.

## Current Tools & Gaps
DIBBS, SAM.gov and cFolders themselves; bid-finding and award-history tools (SalesPatriot, SAM Search, BidPulsar, Starbridge, Cleat.ai, HigherGov); the federal opportunity intelligence archetype indexed as [[niches/contract-research-labs/federal-opportunity-intelligence/profile|Federal R&D Opportunity Intelligence]]. The gaps: eligibility checks on technical data and approved sources, price-to-win from NSN award history, and feedback from losses.
