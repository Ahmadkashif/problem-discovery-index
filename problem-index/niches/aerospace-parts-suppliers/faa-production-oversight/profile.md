# FAA Production Oversight & Suspected Unapproved Parts

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/aerospace-parts-suppliers|Aerospace Parts Suppliers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The FAA's Aircraft Certification Service oversees production approval holders (production certificate and PMA holders) through manufacturing inspection offices and runs the Suspected Unapproved Parts programme. The 2024 investigation into titanium with falsified records in Boeing and Airbus structures is the kind of case it handles (programme detail recalled; the investigation via search summary of AP and FAA statements).

## Insight Function
**Size:** manufacturing inspectors are production headcount; staff dedicated to analysing supplier production oversight and SUP reports are plausibly under 10 (estimate)
**Output:** Surveillance findings, enforcement actions, unapproved-parts notifications
**Proprietary data:** Production-approval audit findings and SUP reports across holders
**Clock:** The FAA's own surveillance plans and investigation timelines
**Buyer:** FAA leadership; a single buyer
