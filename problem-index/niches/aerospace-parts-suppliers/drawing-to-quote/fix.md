# The Lost Quote Nobody Records

**Niche:** [[niches/aerospace-parts-suppliers/drawing-to-quote/profile|Drawing-to-Quote for Aerospace RFQs]]
**Industry:** [[industries/aerospace-parts-suppliers|Aerospace Parts Suppliers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Shops keep the quotes they win and forget the ones they lose, so they never learn where their prices sit against the market.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #logistic-regression #revenue-impact #quick-win #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn an RFQ drawing package into a priced, routed quote inside the customer's window, and whoever's estimates land closest to the shop's own job actuals takes the account.

## The Problem
A lost RFQ disappears from the system. Sometimes the buyer says why ("you were 30% high"); usually not. Without lost quotes, the shop cannot see which part families or customers it overprices, or whether it is losing on price, lead time or capability.

## Why It's Still Broken
Recording a loss feels like admin with no payoff, and quoting tools close the opportunity without asking.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Require a loss reason and, where given, the winning price or gap when a quote closes, and review win rates by part family, customer and price band monthly.

## Who Feels the Pain
Owners and sales leaders who cannot explain a falling win rate; estimators who are told they are "too high" without evidence.

## Impact If Fixed
A few months of loss data typically shows where prices sit ~10–20% off the market on specific part families (estimate), the cheapest pricing insight a shop can get.
