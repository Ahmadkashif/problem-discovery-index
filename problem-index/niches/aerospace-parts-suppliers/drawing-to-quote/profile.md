# Drawing-to-Quote for Aerospace RFQs

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/aerospace-parts-suppliers|Aerospace Parts Suppliers]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn an RFQ drawing package into a priced, routed quote inside the customer's window, and whoever's estimates land closest to the shop's own job actuals takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$700M a year in estimating labour and quoting software at US aerospace parts suppliers (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.9% of the two codes' receipts, plus machine shops coded elsewhere (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — quoting tools are common; estimates still lean on an estimator's memory of similar parts
**Target Buyer:** Estimating manager, VP Sales or owner
**Automation Potential:** High for feature extraction, routing proposals and pricing; medium for the cost drivers that need experience

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
As tier-1s and primes re-source detail parts during rate ramps, commercial RFQs arrive in packages of tens to hundreds of part numbers with short windows. Aerospace quotes carry cost drivers generic machining quotes miss: Nadcap special processes and their outside cost, FAI and source inspection, titanium and nickel alloy machinability, tight positional tolerances, material traceability and minimum buys. The contest is estimation accuracy against what the job really costs. Government spares bidding is a separate niche because its winner is decided by eligibility and award history, not estimation.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Paperless Parts, ERP estimating modules, CADDi for drawing search, and newer AI quote-to-order entrants such as Korso; custom-parts networks such as Vendra route work to shops. The general-fabrication archetype is [[niches/metal-fabrication/estimating-and-quoting-automation/profile|Estimating & Quoting Automation]], and the large-CM version is the Pass 2 pocket [[niches/contract-manufacturing/cm-quoting-cost-engineering/profile|🔍 Contract Manufacturer Quoting & Cost Engineering]]. The gaps: aerospace cost drivers as structured features, and a quote-versus-actual record by part family.
