## What They Do
Accredited registrars (BSI, NSF-ISR, PRI Registrar, DNV, Intertek and others, recalled) that audit suppliers' quality systems against AS9100 under the IAQG scheme and issue the certificates primes require.

## Insight Function
**Size:** auditors are production headcount; technical review and scheme staff per body are a handful in aerospace (estimate)
**Output:** Audit reports and certificates, uploaded to OASIS
**Proprietary data:** Audit findings across certified sites, shared through OASIS
**Clock:** Surveillance and recertification cycles
**Buyer:** Aerospace programme manager at the registrar
