# Aerospace Fleet & Production Forecast Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/aerospace-parts-suppliers|Aerospace Parts Suppliers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms that maintain aircraft-by-aircraft fleet databases and forecast airframe and engine production, deliveries and aftermarket demand: Cirium, Aviation Week Network's fleet and forecast products, Forecast International and Teal Group are representative. Parts suppliers, their investors and lenders buy the forecasts to size rate ramps, spares demand and programme risk. With IATA reporting a backlog above 17,000 aircraft in December 2025 (and above 18,000 by its June 2026 AGM, search summary) and Boeing planning 737 increases beyond 47 a month, demand for credible rate forecasts is high.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-100 forecasting and market analysts per larger provider; the fleet-record researchers who keep the database current are production and not counted (estimate)
**Output:** Fleet and order databases, production and delivery forecasts by programme, engine and aftermarket demand forecasts
**Proprietary data:** Decades of aircraft-level fleet, order, delivery and retirement history and every past forecast; but OEM orders and deliveries are published and aircraft registries are public, so the outcome record is not exclusive
**Clock:** Largely self-set refresh and forecast cycles; OEM rate announcements and air-show order cycles shape timing but are not deadlines on the vendor
**Buyer:** Head of forecasting or chief analyst at the provider

**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. The forecast archive is a genuine, ungraded asset: every production forecast resolves against actual deliveries, and few vendors publish how their past rate calls held up. It falls short on the clock (the vendor's own refresh cycle, per the batch P1 ruling on data vendors), on a buyer market of a handful of firms, on Q3 (forecasts are judged against public delivery and registry records, the same forecast-against-public-actuals shape that holds [[niches/electric-utilities/load-forecasting-resource-planning-consultancies/profile|🔍 Load Forecasting & Resource Planning Consultancies]] at Q3 3; Q3 4 here credits the curated fleet history), and on Q2 (the data staff maintaining fleet records are production).
