# Aerospace Quality Standards & Supplier Registry

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/aerospace-parts-suppliers|Aerospace Parts Suppliers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The International Aerospace Quality Group and its regional sectors write the 9100-series standards (AS9100, AS9102 first article inspection, AS9145 and others), with SAE International publishing them in the Americas. IAQG also runs the certification scheme that oversees the bodies auditing suppliers and the OASIS database that lists every certified supplier and its audit results. The successor standard, IA9100, is targeting late-2026 publication with transition timing still unsettled.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 staff across the IAQG secretariat, sector bodies and SAE's aerospace standards staff, with technical work done by volunteer committees (estimate)
**Output:** Standards, scheme rules, OASIS supplier records, oversight of certification bodies
**Proprietary data:** OASIS holds two tiers: public certificate listings (Tier 1) and private audit results, nonconformances, corrective actions and suspensions (Tier 2) that each certified supplier must make available to its aerospace customers and authorities on request but may withhold for competition or confidentiality reasons; plus the committee record behind each standard
**Clock:** Self-set revision and transition cycles (IA9100 timing is IAQG's own); primes adopt the standards contractually rather than regulators by reference
**Buyer:** IAQG scheme or standards leadership; SAE's aerospace standards director
