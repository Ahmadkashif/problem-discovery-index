# DoD Spare-Parts Pricing Oversight

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/aerospace-parts-suppliers|Aerospace Parts Suppliers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Government teams that test whether sole-source and commercial-item spare-parts prices are fair: DCMA's cost and pricing and commercial item specialists, DLA's pricing analysts, and the DoD Inspector General, whose audits of aviation spare-parts pricing at sole-source suppliers have drawn congressional attention (recalled). Their work feeds negotiations and audit reports rather than invoices.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-30 auditors per DoD Inspector General spare-parts pricing audit team (estimate); DCMA and DLA analysts pricing each individual buy are production and are not counted
**Output:** Audit reports on spare-parts pricing and the price-reasonableness and commercial-item determinations behind them
**Proprietary data:** Contractor cost and pricing data, sole-source price histories and negotiation records
**Clock:** Audit plans and congressional requests; contract award dates bind the per-buy analysts, not the audit team
**Buyer:** Agency leadership; there is effectively one buyer
