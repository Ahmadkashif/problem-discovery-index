# Prime & Engine-OEM Supplier Performance Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/aerospace-parts-suppliers|Aerospace Parts Suppliers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The supplier management, supplier quality and supply-risk teams at airframers and engine OEMs (Boeing, Airbus, GE Aerospace, RTX's Pratt & Whitney and Collins, Rolls-Royce, Safran) that score suppliers on delivery and quality, run rate-readiness reviews before rate breaks, and decide where to dual-source. After Boeing completed its acquisition of Spirit AeroSystems on 8 December 2025 and began moving the 737 to 47 a month in 2026 (Boeing 10-Q), this work grew.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-200 supplier quality, supply-chain analytics and risk staff per prime (estimate)
**Output:** Supplier scorecards, rate-readiness assessments, sourcing decisions
**Proprietary data:** Delivery, quality escape and corrective-action history across the whole supply base
**Clock:** The prime's own rate breaks and programme milestones (self-set)
**Buyer:** VP supply chain or supplier management at the prime
