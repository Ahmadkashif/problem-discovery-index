# Drawing Ballooning & Characteristic Accountability

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/aerospace-parts-suppliers|Aerospace Parts Suppliers]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to extract every requirement on a drawing or model, notes included, into a ballooned Form 3, and whoever posts the highest characteristic recall on the shop's own scanned and model-based drawings takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$250M a year of the first article market: ballooning and characteristic-list labour plus software (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.3% of the two codes' receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-medium — auto-ballooning works on vector PDFs; scans and MBD are done by hand
**Target Buyer:** Quality manager or quality engineering lead
**Automation Potential:** High; the residual is ambiguous notes that need an engineer's reading

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Characteristic accountability is the part of AS9102 that a customer checks first, and a single missed requirement, often one buried in a general note, gets the report rejected. The inputs are drawings and models, the skill is reading engineering notation (GD&T, thread and hole callouts, flag notes, finish symbols), and the winner is whoever misses least on the hardest drawings a shop actually receives: scanned government technical data from decades ago, hand-revised prints, and model-based definitions with no drawing at all.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Net-Inspect, DISCUS, High QA, InspectionXpert and GroundControl balloon vector PDFs and read dimension text. The gaps: raster scans with poor contrast and hand revisions, notes that carry requirements without a dimension, PMI in STEP models, and no measurement of recall on a shop's own drawing mix.
