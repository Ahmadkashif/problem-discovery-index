# Aerospace Supply Chain & M&A Advisory

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/aerospace-parts-suppliers|Aerospace Parts Suppliers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Boutique and practice-level consultancies and advisers that assess aerospace suppliers' capacity, cost and programme exposure for primes, investors and acquirers: aerospace practices at the strategy firms, specialist boutiques such as AeroDynamic Advisory and Alton Aviation Consultancy, and sector M&A advisers. Primes pay for supply-base assessments during rate ramps; private equity pays for commercial due diligence on supplier acquisitions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 consultants and analysts per firm or practice (estimate)
**Output:** Supply-base and capacity assessments, commercial due diligence reports, market studies
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated supplier assessments, site visits and interview notes across programmes
**Clock:** Deal timetables set by sell-side bankers and primes' sourcing events
**Buyer:** Practice leader or managing partner
