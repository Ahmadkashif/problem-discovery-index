# Aerospace Component Rollups

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/aerospace-parts-suppliers|Aerospace Parts Suppliers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Acquirers that buy proprietary aerospace component businesses and run them for aftermarket pricing power: TransDigm is the archetype, with HEICO's acquisition programme and private-equity platforms such as Arcline's (which bought Kaman) and AE Industrial's following variants of the model (recalled). Their corporate development and pricing teams screen targets and set aftermarket prices across the portfolio.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 corporate development, pricing and integration analysts per platform (estimate)
**Output:** Acquisition screens and models, portfolio pricing analyses, integration plans
**Proprietary data:** Aftermarket price and volume history across acquired businesses, and target files
**Clock:** Deal timetables and annual price-list cycles
**Buyer:** Chief development officer or head of pricing
