# Superalloy & Titanium Machining Applications Engineering

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/aerospace-parts-suppliers|Aerospace Parts Suppliers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The application engineering and test centres of cutting-tool makers (Sandvik Coromant, Kennametal, Seco, Iscar, Walter) that develop machining strategies and cutting data for titanium and nickel superalloy parts and run trials with aerospace suppliers (recalled). The output wins tool sales; it is not invoiced separately.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 application engineers per large tool maker, a share of them aerospace specialists (estimate)
**Output:** Cutting-data recommendations, machining strategies, trial reports
**Proprietary data:** Test-centre cutting data and tool-life results across alloys and strategies
**Clock:** Customers' new-programme launches and re-sourcing; mostly self-set
**Buyer:** Head of aerospace segment or application engineering
