# Supplier Quality Data Networks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/aerospace-parts-suppliers|Aerospace Parts Suppliers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Software networks that host suppliers' first article reports, inspection data and nonconformances for their customers, such as Net-Inspect, which some primes use as a supplier FAI portal, and the primes' own supplier quality portals.

## Insight Function
**Size:** under 10 analytics staff per vendor; the rest are product, support and implementation (estimate)
**Output:** Software subscriptions and hosted records
**Proprietary data:** Suppliers' and primes' inspection data, held on their behalf
**Clock:** Customers' FAI and audit dates
**Buyer:** Vendor CEO or product head
