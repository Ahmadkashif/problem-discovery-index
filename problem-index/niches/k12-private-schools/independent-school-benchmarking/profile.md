# Independent School Benchmarking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/k12-private-schools|K-12 Private Schools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
The independent school association collects tuition, enrolment, salary, and financial data from member schools and publishes the benchmarking database heads of school and business officers plan against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 researchers and data staff
**Output:** Tuition and financial benchmarks, salary surveys, enrolment trend reporting, peer group comparison tools
**Proprietary data:** Member-contributed financial and enrolment data across thousands of schools, over decades
**Clock:** Annual data collection and budget season
**Buyer:** Director of Research — member funded
