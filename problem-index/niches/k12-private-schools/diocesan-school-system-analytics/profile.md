# School System Central Office Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/k12-private-schools|K-12 Private Schools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup

## What They Do
Diocesan and network central offices oversee dozens or hundreds of schools, tracking enrolment, finances, and viability across the system and deciding which schools consolidate or close.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 analysts and administrators per system
**Output:** Enrolment and financial monitoring, viability assessments, consolidation planning
**Proprietary data:** Enrolment, tuition, and financial records across many schools in a region over decades
**Clock:** Budget and enrolment cycles
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — system administration
