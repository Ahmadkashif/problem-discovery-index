# Donor Wealth Screening Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/k12-private-schools|K-12 Private Schools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Wealth screening providers score a school's parent and alumni base for giving capacity, driving the advancement work that funds financial aid and capital projects.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 data analysts, research staff, and data scientists
**Output:** Capacity and propensity scores, prospect profiles, portfolio segmentation
**Proprietary data:** Assembled wealth indicators joined to observed giving across the client base
**Clock:** Campaign calendars and fiscal year end
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Research
