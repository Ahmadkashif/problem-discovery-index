# Financial Aid Need Assessment Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/k12-private-schools|K-12 Private Schools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
FACTS, Clarity, and the school and student service organizations collect a family's full financial picture — income, assets, debts, household composition, business ownership — and compute what that family can be expected to contribute. The school makes its aid award on that number, which decides whether a child attends.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 need analysts, methodology staff, verification specialists, and data engineers
**Output:** Expected family contribution calculations, verification findings, aid recommendation reports, school-level aid modelling
**Proprietary data:** Detailed household financial submissions from hundreds of thousands of families annually, joined to the awards schools made and the enrolment decisions that followed
**Clock:** The compressed admission and award season Pass 1 puts at January to March, against contract deadlines that do not move
**Buyer:** Chief Product Officer / VP of Data
