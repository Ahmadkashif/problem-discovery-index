# Independent Educational Consultants

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/k12-private-schools|K-12 Private Schools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Independent educational consultants advise families on school selection and application strategy, drawing on a personal knowledge of which schools suit which children and which admission offices respond to what.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1-5 consultants, usually one
**Output:** School recommendations, application strategy, family counselling
**Proprietary data:** A career of observed admission outcomes and school knowledge, entirely undocumented
**Clock:** The admission cycle
**Buyer:** The consultant
