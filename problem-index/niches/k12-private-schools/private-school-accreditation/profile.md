# Private School Accreditation Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/k12-private-schools|K-12 Private Schools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Regional and association accrediting bodies evaluate private schools against standards on a multi-year cycle, running self-study and visiting team processes whose outcome affects a school's standing with families, colleges, and in some states its legal operation.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 standards staff, visit coordinators, and analysts per accreditor
**Output:** Accreditation decisions, visiting team reports, standards development, school improvement recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Self-study and visit findings across thousands of schools over multiple cycles — an organizational quality record nobody else holds
**Clock:** Multi-year accreditation cycles with fixed visit dates
**Buyer:** Executive director / Director of Accreditation
