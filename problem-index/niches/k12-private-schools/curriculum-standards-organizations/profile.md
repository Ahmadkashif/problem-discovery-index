# Curriculum & Programme Standards Organizations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/k12-private-schools|K-12 Private Schools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Curriculum programme organizations define the frameworks private schools adopt to differentiate themselves — international, classical, and specialized programmes — and authorize and review schools against them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 curriculum specialists, assessment staff, and school services personnel
**Output:** Curriculum frameworks, programme authorization, examination and moderation, teacher training
**Proprietary data:** Programme implementation and student outcome data across authorized schools
**Clock:** Authorization cycles and examination sessions
**Buyer:** Director of Programme Development
