# Tuition Refund Insurance Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/k12-private-schools|K-12 Private Schools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Specialty insurers underwrite tuition refund plans that cover a family's obligation when a student withdraws mid-year, pricing the risk that a given school's families will leave.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 underwriters, actuaries, and analysts
**Output:** Rating and eligibility decisions, withdrawal claim analysis, plan design
**Proprietary data:** Mid-year withdrawal experience by school type, region, and family characteristic — a direct measurement of the attrition schools cannot predict
**Clock:** Annual enrolment contract cycle
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
