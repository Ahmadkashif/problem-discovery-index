# Admission Testing Organizations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/k12-private-schools|K-12 Private Schools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
The independent school entrance testing bodies write, administer, and score the admission examinations private schools use to compare applicants from hundreds of different sending schools. The score report is one of the few standardized inputs in an admission process Pass 1 says otherwise runs on gut feel and committee.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-250 psychometricians, item writers, research staff, and score reporting analysts
**Output:** Test forms and items, scaled scores and percentiles, score reports and interpretive guidance, validity research
**Proprietary data:** Item response data across decades of test-takers, plus the item bank itself and its calibration history
**Clock:** Testing windows and admission deadlines, both fixed and compressed
**Buyer:** Chief Research Officer / VP of Assessment
