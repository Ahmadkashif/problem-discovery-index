# Adaptive Assessment Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/k12-private-schools|K-12 Private Schools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The adaptive assessment publishers run the interim tests schools administer several times a year to measure where each student is and how much they have grown. The score and growth report is the product, and it drives instructional grouping, intervention decisions, and the school's account of its own effectiveness. Their volume is predominantly public districts; private schools are a substantial minority of the same product.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 150-700 psychometricians, research scientists, content developers, and data engineers
**Output:** Adaptive tests, achievement and growth scores, norms, instructional reports, efficacy research
**Proprietary data:** Longitudinal achievement and growth records on tens of millions of students, with item-level response data — the largest learning measurement corpus in existence
**Clock:** Fall, winter, and spring testing windows
**Buyer:** Chief Research Officer / VP of Psychometrics
