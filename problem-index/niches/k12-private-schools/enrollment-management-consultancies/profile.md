# Enrolment Management Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/k12-private-schools|K-12 Private Schools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Consultancies advise private schools on the enrolment problem Pass 1 calls existential — tuition pricing, discount strategy, yield management, and retention — modelling how a change in aid policy moves net tuition revenue.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-70 consultants and analysts
**Output:** Pricing and discount strategy, yield and attrition modelling, enrolment forecasts, market studies
**Proprietary data:** Pricing, discount rate, yield, and retention outcomes across many client schools over years
**Clock:** The admission cycle and budget adoption dates
**Buyer:** Principal / Managing Director
