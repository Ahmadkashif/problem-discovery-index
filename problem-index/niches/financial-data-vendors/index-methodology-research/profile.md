# Index Methodology & Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/financial-data-vendors|Financial Data Vendors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
MSCI, S&P Dow Jones Indices and FTSE Russell design, maintain and rebalance benchmark indices, publishing methodologies, consultations and rebalance announcements that asset managers license and track.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 index researchers, methodologists and operations analysts
**Output:** Index methodologies, rebalance lists, constituent and weight files, consultation papers
**Proprietary data:** Index histories, licensed constituent decisions and classification schemes
**Clock:** Scheduled rebalance and reconstitution dates that move flows
**Buyer:** Head of Index Research / Methodology
