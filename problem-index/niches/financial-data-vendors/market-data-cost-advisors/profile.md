# Market Data Cost & Inventory Advisors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/financial-data-vendors|Financial Data Vendors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation firms

## What They Do
Consultancies and inventory platforms — TRG Screen, Calero-MDSL, and research-pricing benchmarkers such as Substantive Research — help financial institutions track, benchmark and negotiate market data and research spend.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-80 analysts per firm
**Output:** Spend benchmarks, contract reviews, inventory and usage reports
**Proprietary data:** Pricing and contract data across many clients' vendor agreements
**Clock:** Renewal dates and budget cycles
**Buyer:** Head of Market Data / Procurement
