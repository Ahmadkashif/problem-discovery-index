# Fixed-Income Evaluated Pricing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/financial-data-vendors|Financial Data Vendors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to price illiquid bonds defensibly every day and survive the client's price challenges — and whoever's evaluations hold up under challenge keeps the fund administrator's NAV process.

## What They Do
Evaluated pricing services — ICE Data Services, Bloomberg BVAL, LSEG's pricing service, S&P Global's evaluated pricing and others — produce daily prices for millions of bonds and other instruments that rarely trade. Funds, administrators and custodians use them to strike net asset values and support fair-value determinations under SEC Rule 2a-5. Evaluators combine trades, dealer quotes, comparables and models, and they field price challenges from clients.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 evaluators, quantitative analysts and challenge-resolution staff per provider
**Output:** Daily evaluated prices with inputs and transparency metrics; challenge responses
**Proprietary data:** Contributed dealer quotes and evaluator decision histories over years, plus the complete record of challenges and their resolution against subsequent trades
**Clock:** Daily NAV strike deadlines, typically end of US trading day
**Buyer:** Head of Evaluated Pricing / Head of Valuation Services
