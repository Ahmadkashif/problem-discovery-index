# AI Research Platforms on Licensed Content

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/financial-data-vendors|Financial Data Vendors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Search and AI research platforms such as AlphaSense (which acquired Tegus in 2024) aggregate filings, transcripts, broker research and expert-call libraries and sell search, summarisation and model-ready data to investment professionals.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 content, data and research staff
**Output:** Search, summaries, extracted KPIs, expert-call transcripts
**Proprietary data:** Aggregated licensed content and a proprietary expert-call library
**Clock:** Earnings season and client deadlines, indirectly
**Buyer:** Chief Content Officer
