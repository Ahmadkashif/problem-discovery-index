# Survey Platforms That Check the Answer

**Industry:** [[industries/financial-data-vendors|Financial Data Vendors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Survey tools collect what a GP types; a private-markets submission needs validating against last quarter, other LPs' reports and the fund's own arithmetic before a researcher ever sees it.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #autoencoders #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor's private-markets research team is fighting to grow coverage faster than the private universe grows, from sources that must be asked rather than parsed — and whoever automates the chase while keeping the GP and LP relationships intact wins the coverage race.

## The Problem
GP and company submissions arrive through portals and spreadsheets with errors: cumulative figures that decrease, NAVs inconsistent with cash flows, a fund reported under a sibling vehicle's name. Researchers find them by inspection, sometimes after publication.

## What Already Exists
Survey and form platforms (Qualtrics, vendors' own portals), and generic data validation rules.

## The Customization Gap
Validation at submission time using fund arithmetic (cumulative monotonicity, NAV and cash-flow consistency), cross-source checks against pension-disclosed figures for the same fund, entity checks against the vendor's graph, and learned anomaly scoring on submission patterns — returned to the submitter immediately, while the GP's operations person is still at the screen.

## Target Customer
Private-markets research operations leaders.

## Impact If Solved
Errors corrected by the source at submission cost nothing; errors found by a client after publication cost credibility.
