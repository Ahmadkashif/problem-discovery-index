**Contested on:** Every serious competitor's private-markets research team is fighting to grow coverage faster than the private universe grows, from sources that must be asked rather than parsed — and whoever automates the chase while keeping the GP and LP relationships intact wins the coverage race.

## The Problem
Coverage claims are counts of records held. What clients actually need to know — what fraction of last year's Series A rounds in a sector, or of a vintage's buyout funds, the database contains — is unknown, because the denominator is private. Research teams allocate effort by client escalation and competitive comparison.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The question sounds unanswerable. It is not: ecologists estimate unseen populations from overlapping samples, and a vendor observes the same universe through several partly independent sources — filings, news, surveys, pension disclosures, client-submitted corrections.

## What to Build
Capture–recapture estimation of the unobserved universe by segment, from overlap between sources with source dependence modelled; coverage reported with intervals per sector, geography, stage and vintage; research effort allocated to the segments with the largest estimated gap per hour of research cost; and a standing coverage report that becomes a research product in its own right.

## Target Customer
Heads of private-markets research and product at private-markets data vendors.

## Impact If Built
Converts a coverage race fought on anecdote into one fought on measured completeness, and points hundreds of researchers at the gaps that matter.
