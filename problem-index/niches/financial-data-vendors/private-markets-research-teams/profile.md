# Private Markets Research & Collection Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/financial-data-vendors|Financial Data Vendors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor's private-markets research team is fighting to grow coverage faster than the private universe grows, from sources that must be asked rather than parsed — and whoever automates the chase while keeping the GP and LP relationships intact wins the coverage race.

## What They Do
The research organisations at PitchBook (Morningstar), Preqin (BlackRock), MSCI's private capital business, Dealroom, Crunchbase and the private-markets units of S&P and LSEG. They survey GPs and companies, file public-records requests to pension funds, monitor filings and news, confirm rounds by phone and email, and publish company, deal, fund and performance data plus research reports that deal teams, LPs, consultants and bankers license.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-3,000 researchers and analysts per large vendor
**Output:** Company and deal records, fund profiles and cash-flow performance, benchmarks, league tables, quarterly research reports
**Proprietary data:** GP- and company-submitted data, years of FOIA-obtained pension disclosures, confirmed-by-phone records, and the entity graph linking firms, funds, companies and investors
**Clock:** Quarterly LP reporting and fundraising cycles; no hard statutory deadline
**Buyer:** Head of Private Markets Research / Chief Research Officer
