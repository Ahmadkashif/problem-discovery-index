# Financial Data Standards Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/financial-data-vendors|Financial Data Vendors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
FISD (the financial information industry association within SIIA), XBRL US and GLEIF maintain standards, identifiers and industry forums that vendors and consumers rely on.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Small staffs; research functions well under 10 people
**Output:** Standards, identifier registries, industry guidance
**Proprietary data:** Member input and registry data
**Clock:** Standards and regulatory timetables
**Buyer:** Executive Director
