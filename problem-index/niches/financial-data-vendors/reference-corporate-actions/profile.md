# Reference & Corporate Actions Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/financial-data-vendors|Financial Data Vendors]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce a golden copy of corporate actions and security identifiers that downstream systems can process without manual scrubbing — and whoever's events are right first time removes the client's reconciliation team.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2B
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of the segment
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — feeds are digital, announcement parsing is not
**Target Buyer:** Head of reference data
**Automation Potential:** Very High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Security masters, identifiers (CUSIP — operated by CUSIP Global Services, owned by FactSet since 2022 — ISIN, FIGI, LEI) and corporate actions (dividends, splits, mergers, elections) feed every trading, custody and accounting system. Bloomberg, LSEG, ICE, S&P, SIX and others compete on timeliness and accuracy; clients still buy several feeds and scrub them into a golden copy.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Vendor feeds, client-side EDM platforms, ISO 15022/20022 messages. Gaps: announcements parsed manually from issuer and agent notices; vendor disagreements reconciled by client staff; no confidence on events.
