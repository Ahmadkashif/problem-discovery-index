# Three Feeds, Three Ex-Dates

**Niche:** [[niches/financial-data-vendors/reference-corporate-actions/profile|Reference & Corporate Actions Data]]
**Industry:** [[industries/financial-data-vendors|Financial Data Vendors]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Clients pay for multiple corporate-actions feeds because no single one is trusted, then pay staff to reconcile them.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #quick-win #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce a golden copy of corporate actions and security identifiers that downstream systems can process without manual scrubbing — and whoever's events are right first time removes the client's reconciliation team.

## The Problem
Vendor accuracy by market and event type is unmeasured, so redundancy is the only defence.

## Why It's Still Broken
Vendors do not publish accuracy; clients do not pool their reconciliation outcomes.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Vendors measure and publish first-time-right rates by market and event type, using clients' corrections as ground truth.

## Who Feels the Pain
Client operations teams and the portfolios that mis-process events.

## Impact If Fixed
Lets clients buy fewer feeds and trust the one they keep.
