# Linking What Was Said to What Was Filed

**Niche:** [[niches/financial-data-vendors/filings-transcript-ingestion/profile|Filings & Transcript Ingestion]]
**Industry:** [[industries/financial-data-vendors|Financial Data Vendors]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Guidance given on the call and the numbers later filed live in different products, and nobody links a statement to the figure that tested it.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #bert #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a filing or a call into structured, cited data within minutes rather than hours — and whoever is first and right on earnings night becomes the feed quant and fundamental clients wire into their models.

## The Problem
Clients' research workflow is to compare what management said with what happened. Vendors hold both and link neither.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Transcripts and fundamentals are separate product teams and data models.

## What to Build
Extract quantitative guidance and KPI statements from transcripts, link them to the template fields and periods they concern, and track realisation when the numbers arrive.

## Target Customer
Content technology and product leadership.

## Impact If Built
A new product from existing content: a management credibility record per issuer.
