# Filings & Transcript Ingestion

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/financial-data-vendors|Financial Data Vendors]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a filing or a call into structured, cited data within minutes rather than hours — and whoever is first and right on earnings night becomes the feed quant and fundamental clients wire into their models.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.2B
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of the segment
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — automated capture, human editing
**Target Buyer:** Head of content technology
**Automation Potential:** Very High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every issuer event — a 10-K, 10-Q or 8-K on EDGAR, a SEDAR+ or ESEF filing, an earnings release, a call — must be captured, parsed, transcribed, segmented and linked within minutes. The pipeline is shared infrastructure for fundamentals, estimates and transcripts products, and it is where document AI applies most directly. Generic web collection is out of scope ([[industries/web-data-extraction-firms|Web Data Extraction Firms]]).

## Current Tools & Gaps
EDGAR feeds, XBRL processors, ASR and editors, NLP taggers. Gaps: unstructured sections (MD&A, risk factors, notes) under-extracted; transcript editing load; weak cross-linking between call statements and filed numbers.
