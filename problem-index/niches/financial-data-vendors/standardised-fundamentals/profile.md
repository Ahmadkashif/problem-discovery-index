# Standardised Fundamentals

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/financial-data-vendors|Financial Data Vendors]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to standardise a new filing correctly within hours and to show how each derived number was produced — and whoever does that best becomes the source every client's comp table and backtest silently trusts.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.3B
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% of the segment
**Digital Adoption:** High at input (XBRL), low at judgement
**Target Buyer:** Head of fundamentals content
**Automation Potential:** High — the judgement is learnable from the vendor's own history

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The raw filings are public and XBRL-tagged, so any competitor can read them; the standardised template, and the thousands of mapping decisions that populate it, cannot be copied. S&P Capital IQ, FactSet, LSEG and Bloomberg each maintain their own standardisation, and smaller entrants (Calcbench, Daloopa and others) compete on as-reported fidelity or model-ready detail. The fight is speed after filing, correctness of judgement on ambiguous items, and — increasingly — the ability to show the derivation when a client asks.

## Current Tools & Gaps
XBRL pre-population, keying tools, validation rules, sample QA, offshore collection teams. Gaps: the mapping judgement is tacit and unrecorded; issuer extension tags are mapped by hand every quarter; no consistency measurement; no lineage exposed to clients.
