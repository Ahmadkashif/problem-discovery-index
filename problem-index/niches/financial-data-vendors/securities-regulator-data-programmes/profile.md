# Securities Regulator Structured Data Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/financial-data-vendors|Financial Data Vendors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The SEC (including its Division of Economic and Risk Analysis and the EDGAR and XBRL programmes), ESMA (ESEF) and their peers define and police the structured disclosure the vendors ingest; the US Financial Data Transparency Act of 2022 extends machine-readable requirements.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 economists, data and structured-disclosure staff
**Output:** Taxonomies, structured-data rules, risk analytics for examinations
**Proprietary data:** Filings and non-public supervisory data
**Clock:** Rulemaking and statutory deadlines
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / Director of economic analysis
