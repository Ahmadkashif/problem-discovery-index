# Private Markets Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/financial-data-vendors|Financial Data Vendors]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Not terminal as stated — competitors are fighting either to find and resolve every private financing round and valuation first, or to hold accurate cash-flow-level performance for the most private funds; different sources, buyers and winners, stated separately in the sub-niches.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.8B
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of the segment, growing faster than the rest
**Digital Adoption:** Low — collection runs on surveys, phone calls, FOIA requests and PDFs
**Target Buyer:** Head of private-markets research
**Automation Potential:** High — document chase and extraction dominate the labour

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
There is no filing regime for private markets comparable to EDGAR. PitchBook (Morningstar), Preqin (BlackRock), MSCI's Burgiss business, Dealroom, Crunchbase and the private-markets arms of S&P and LSEG build coverage from press releases, Form D filings, GP and company surveys, FOIA requests to public pension funds and LP-administrator data. Research teams of hundreds do the chase. The contest is coverage and accuracy where the information is deliberately private.

It is **not terminal**: company and deal data and fund performance data are sourced differently, bought by different people and won by different vendors.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Research CRMs, survey portals, news monitoring, manual PDF extraction, entity databases. Gaps: FOIA and survey chase run by hand; extraction from inconsistent PDFs; entity resolution across renamed firms, feeder vehicles and continuation funds; no confidence attached to individual records.
