# The Quartile That Depends on Who Reported

**Niche:** [[niches/financial-data-vendors/private-fund-performance-data/profile|Private Fund Performance & LP Data]]
**Industry:** [[industries/financial-data-vendors|Financial Data Vendors]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Every GP claims a top-quartile fund, partly because quartile boundaries differ by vendor and by which funds happen to report.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #confidence-intervals #hypothesis-testing #evaluation-metrics #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to hold accurate, cash-flow-level performance for the most private funds — and whoever has the cleanest cash flows sets the benchmarks LPs and consultants measure managers against.

## The Problem
Quartile benchmarks are computed on non-random samples; boundaries move with coverage and NAV timing, and the uncertainty is never shown.

## Why It's Still Broken
Point quartiles are what fundraising materials quote.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Publish quartile boundaries with intervals, coverage statistics per vintage and strategy, and sensitivity to reporting lag.

## Who Feels the Pain
LPs and consultants comparing managers; honest GPs crowded out by selective claims.

## Impact If Fixed
Makes the benchmark an honest measurement rather than a marketing input.
