# Private Fund Performance & LP Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/financial-data-vendors|Financial Data Vendors]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to hold accurate, cash-flow-level performance for the most private funds — and whoever has the cleanest cash flows sets the benchmarks LPs and consultants measure managers against.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$800M
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~4% of the segment
**Digital Adoption:** Low — FOIA responses and quarterly PDFs
**Target Buyer:** Head of fund performance research
**Automation Potential:** High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
LPs, consultants and GPs raising capital benchmark funds by IRR, TVPI and public-market-equivalent measures. MSCI (Burgiss), Preqin, PitchBook and Cambridge Associates build performance datasets from LP-administrator data, GP submissions and public pension disclosures obtained under public-records laws. The contest is cash-flow accuracy and coverage, which determines whose benchmark quartiles are believed.

## Current Tools & Gaps
FOIA programmes, GP survey portals, LP data partnerships, manual extraction. Gaps: inconsistent reporting dates and NAV lags; cash flows reconstructed from cumulative figures; quartile boundaries sensitive to which funds report.
