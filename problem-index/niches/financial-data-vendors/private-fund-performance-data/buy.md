# Document AI for Pension Board Packets

**Niche:** [[niches/financial-data-vendors/private-fund-performance-data/profile|Private Fund Performance & LP Data]]
**Industry:** [[industries/financial-data-vendors|Financial Data Vendors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Document extraction services read invoices and forms; a pension board packet has a different private-equity table layout per system per year.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #cnns #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to hold accurate, cash-flow-level performance for the most private funds — and whoever has the cleanest cash flows sets the benchmarks LPs and consultants measure managers against.

## The Problem
Hundreds of public pension systems publish or release portfolio reports in PDF formats that change, often scanned.

## What Already Exists
General document AI and OCR services.

## The Customization Gap
Layout learning per pension system across years; fund-name resolution to the vendor's graph; unit and date normalisation; reconciliation checks against prior quarters before acceptance.

## Target Customer
Fund data vendors and large consultants maintaining performance databases.

## Impact If Solved
Removes the largest manual step in public-source performance collection.
