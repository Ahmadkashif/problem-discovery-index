# The Client Data Specialist

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/financial-data-vendors|Financial Data Vendors]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to answer "why does your number differ" faster and more convincingly than the competing terminal — and whoever can show the derivation wins the argument that decides which vendor the client trusts at renewal.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$900M (client service and data-specialist cost, estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of the segment's cost base
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — ticketing and chat, little lineage
**Target Buyer:** Head of client service
**Automation Potential:** High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every terminal vendor runs a large client support and specialist organisation — Bloomberg's help desk, FactSet's consultants, the S&P and LSEG specialist desks. A large share of the questions are about data provenance and discrepancies, asked hours before the client's own deadline. Answering requires lineage the vendor did not keep.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Ticketing and chat systems, methodology documents, audit logs. Gaps: no exposed lineage, no precedent search over resolved tickets, slow path to content correction.
