# Credit Rating Agency Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/financial-data-vendors|Financial Data Vendors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Moody's, S&P Global Ratings and Fitch rate issuers and instruments; their analysts consume vendor fundamentals and produce ratings, research and surveillance that issuers pay for and investors rely on.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1,000-5,000 rating analysts and surveillance staff
**Output:** Credit ratings, rating actions, sector research and surveillance reports
**Proprietary data:** Decades of rating histories with default and transition outcomes, plus issuer-provided confidential information
**Clock:** Issuance calendars, surveillance cycles and regulatory requirements for NRSROs
**Buyer:** Chief Credit Officer / Head of Analytics
