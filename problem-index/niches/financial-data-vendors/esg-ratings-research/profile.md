# ESG & Sustainability Ratings Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/financial-data-vendors|Financial Data Vendors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
MSCI ESG Research, Morningstar Sustainalytics, ISS ESG and S&P Global's sustainability scores assess companies on environmental, social and governance criteria from disclosures, questionnaires and controversies monitoring, and sell ratings and underlying data to asset managers and index users.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-2,000 analysts and data researchers
**Output:** Company ESG ratings and scores, controversy assessments, carbon and climate datasets, regulatory-disclosure data
**Proprietary data:** Questionnaire responses, analyst assessment histories and controversy records per company over many years
**Clock:** Annual company review cycles, client regulatory reporting (EU SFDR), and the EU ESG Ratings Regulation applying from mid-2026
**Buyer:** Head of ESG Research
