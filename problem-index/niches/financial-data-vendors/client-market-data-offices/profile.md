# Client Market Data Management Offices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/financial-data-vendors|Financial Data Vendors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Banks, asset managers and hedge funds run market data offices that buy, inventory and cut vendor seats and feeds.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Procurement and inventory administration; not a research function
**Output:** Inventory and spend reports for internal use
**Proprietary data:** Internal usage and contract records
**Clock:** Renewal and budget cycles
**Buyer:** Head of Market Data
