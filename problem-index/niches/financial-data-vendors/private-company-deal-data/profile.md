# Private Company & Deal Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/financial-data-vendors|Financial Data Vendors]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to find every private financing round and valuation before anyone else and resolve it onto the right company — and whoever has the earliest, most complete coverage wins the deal teams who source from it.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1B
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~6% of the segment
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — web and filing monitoring plus manual confirmation
**Target Buyer:** Head of company and deal research
**Automation Potential:** High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Venture and growth investors, corporate development teams and bankers source targets and comparables from private company databases. PitchBook, Crunchbase, Dealroom, CB Insights and S&P's private-company data compete on finding rounds, valuations and investors early. Sources are Form D filings, press releases, company websites, registries and direct confirmation. Speed and resolution are the whole contest.

## Current Tools & Gaps
News and filing monitoring, web collection, researcher confirmation. Gaps: post-money valuations mostly inferred; rounds discovered late when undisclosed; company identity ambiguous across rebrands and subsidiaries.
