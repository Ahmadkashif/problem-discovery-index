# Company Registries as a Structured Source

**Niche:** [[niches/financial-data-vendors/private-company-deal-data/profile|Private Company & Deal Data]]
**Industry:** [[industries/financial-data-vendors|Financial Data Vendors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Generic registry-data providers deliver company filings; turning share allotments and articles changes into round size and price per share is domain work.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to find every private financing round and valuation before anyone else and resolve it onto the right company — and whoever has the earliest, most complete coverage wins the deal teams who source from it.

## The Problem
In jurisdictions such as the UK, registry filings disclose share allotments and capital changes that reveal rounds and sometimes prices. Extraction is manual and per-jurisdiction.

## What Already Exists
Registry APIs and company-data aggregators delivering filings and basic fields.

## The Customization Gap
Parsing allotment filings into share classes, counts and prices; reconstructing capitalisation tables over time; inferring post-money valuations with stated uncertainty; per-jurisdiction rules.

## Target Customer
Private-company data vendors expanding outside the US.

## Impact If Solved
Converts registries from a lookup into a valuation source.
