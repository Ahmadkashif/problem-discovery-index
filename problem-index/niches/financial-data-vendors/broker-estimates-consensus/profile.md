# Broker Estimates & Consensus

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/financial-data-vendors|Financial Data Vendors]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to hold the broadest set of contributed broker estimates at line-item detail and to clean them into a consensus clients trust — and whoever gets both the contributions and the hygiene right owns the number earnings surprises are measured against.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.2B
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of the segment
**Digital Adoption:** High — contributions are electronic, hygiene is manual
**Target Buyer:** Head of estimates content
**Automation Potential:** High — exclusion decisions are repeated thousands of times per season

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Consensus is built from estimates that sell-side brokers choose to contribute. LSEG's I/B/E/S, FactSet Estimates, S&P's estimates and Visible Alpha (acquired by S&P Global in 2024) compete on breadth of contributors and, increasingly, on detail — segment and KPI-level forecasts extracted from broker models. Once contributions are in, specialists exclude stale, basis-inconsistent and erroneous estimates before the consensus is published. The two skills — contributor relationships and hygiene — are what this contest is about, and neither resembles reading a filing.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Contribution feeds and portals, model-extraction pipelines, rule-based staleness flags, specialist review. Gaps: staleness defined by fixed windows rather than by events; basis alignment manual; exclusion reasoning recorded as codes; no measurement of whether hygiene improved consensus accuracy.
