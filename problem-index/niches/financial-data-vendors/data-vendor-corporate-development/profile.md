# Data Vendor Corporate Development

## What They Do
The industry is consolidating — S&P Global with IHS Markit and Visible Alpha, LSEG with Refinitiv, Morningstar with PitchBook, MSCI with Burgiss, BlackRock with Preqin, FactSet with CUSIP Global Services — and each acquirer runs corporate development and integration analytics.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 corp dev and integration analysts
**Output:** Acquisition theses, content-overlap and integration analyses
**Proprietary data:** Internal product usage and content-coverage data
**Clock:** Deal timetables
**Buyer:** Head of Corporate Development
