**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #time-series-forecasting #optimization-fundamentals #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor's content organisation is fighting to add issuers, fields and history per analyst-hour without losing accuracy — and whoever turns collection judgement into a reusable asset first gets coverage growth that no longer scales with headcount.

## The Problem
Coverage expansion — another thousand small caps, a new KPI set for a sector, ten more years of history for a market — is decided by sales escalation and competitive anxiety. The cost per issuer-field varies enormously by market, language and filing format, and the demand varies by client segment, yet neither is estimated before the decision.

## What Already Exists
Product management and roadmap tools (Productboard, Aha!), demand forecasting, and operations-research models for resource allocation.

## The Customization Gap
Estimating demand from client usage of adjacent coverage and lost-deal reasons; estimating collection cost from historical effort per filing type, language and issuer complexity; weighting by competitive gap measured against competitor coverage; and solving for the allocation of a fixed analyst budget across candidate expansions, revisited each quarter.

## Target Customer
Chief Content Officer and content strategy leads.

## Impact If Solved
Coverage is the vendor's largest investment decision after headcount itself; pricing it in demand and cost turns it from escalation-driven to measurable.
