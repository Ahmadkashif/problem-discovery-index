# Fundamentals & Estimates Content Operations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/financial-data-vendors|Financial Data Vendors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor's content organisation is fighting to add issuers, fields and history per analyst-hour without losing accuracy — and whoever turns collection judgement into a reusable asset first gets coverage growth that no longer scales with headcount.

## What They Do
The content organisations inside S&P Global Market Intelligence, FactSet, LSEG, Bloomberg, Morningstar and the smaller fundamentals and estimates specialists. They read every filing, release and broker contribution, standardise it under a house methodology, maintain point-in-time history, and answer for its accuracy. Their output is the data clients license; the seat and the feed are just how it is delivered.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 2,000-10,000 collection analysts, QA reviewers, methodologists and estimates specialists per large vendor
**Output:** Standardised and as-reported fundamentals, segment and KPI data, broker estimates and consensus, point-in-time history, methodology documents
**Proprietary data:** Decades of mapping and exclusion decisions over public filings and contributed estimates, the house templates, and the client error-ticket history — none reproducible from the public filings themselves
**Clock:** Issuer filing and earnings dates, with contractual timeliness commitments to clients
**Buyer:** Chief Content Officer / Head of Content Operations
