# Usage Metering With Purpose

**Niche:** [[niches/financial-data-vendors/entitlements-exchange-reporting/profile|Entitlements & Exchange Reporting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/financial-data-vendors|Financial Data Vendors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Usage-based billing platforms meter calls and seats; exchange policy prices the purpose of use, which no meter records.
**Tags:** #feature-engineering #gradient-boosting #evaluation-metrics #compliance #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to prove, per user and per use, exactly what each client was entitled to and declared — and whoever makes the exchange audit an uneventful lookup keeps both the exchanges and the clients' market data managers on side.

## The Problem
Whether data feeds a human display, an algorithm or a calculated product determines the fee, and clients declare it themselves.

## What Already Exists
Usage-based billing and metering platforms, API gateways.

## The Customization Gap
Classifying usage patterns by likely purpose (machine-paced access, derived-output signatures), checking them against client declarations, and flagging mismatches before an exchange does.

## Target Customer
Vendors' entitlements teams and clients' market data offices.

## Impact If Solved
Finds under-declaration before it becomes back-billing.
