# The Audit That Reaches Back Three Years

**Niche:** [[niches/financial-data-vendors/entitlements-exchange-reporting/profile|Entitlements & Exchange Reporting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/financial-data-vendors|Financial Data Vendors]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** An exchange audit asks for usage evidence under policies that have since changed, and the team reconstructs it from archived spreadsheets.
**Tags:** #data-integration #compliance #evaluation-metrics #workflow-orchestration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to prove, per user and per use, exactly what each client was entitled to and declared — and whoever makes the exchange audit an uneventful lookup keeps both the exchanges and the clients' market data managers on side.

## The Problem
Audit look-back periods span multiple policy versions; evidence is scattered.

## Why It's Still Broken
Declarations are produced, filed and forgotten; nothing stores the computation.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Store each declaration with its inputs and the policy version; make reproduction a query.

## Who Feels the Pain
Market data compliance staff and the finance teams paying back-bills.

## Impact If Fixed
Shortens audits and reduces disputed back-billing.
