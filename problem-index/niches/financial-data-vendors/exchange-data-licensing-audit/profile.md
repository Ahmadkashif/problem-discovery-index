# Exchange Market-Data Licensing & Audit

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/financial-data-vendors|Financial Data Vendors]]

**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Exchange data businesses (NYSE/ICE, Nasdaq, Cboe, LSEG, Deutsche Börse and others) license feeds to vendors and audit their usage declarations.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Licensing and audit teams; no dedicated research or insight function producing a billable analytical artefact
**Output:** Policies, fee schedules, audit findings
**Proprietary data:** Declarations and usage records of licensees
**Clock:** Annual policy cycles and audit schedules
**Buyer:** Head of Market Data Licensing
