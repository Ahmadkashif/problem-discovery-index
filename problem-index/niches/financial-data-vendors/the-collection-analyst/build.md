# Precedent on the Screen

**Niche:** [[niches/financial-data-vendors/the-collection-analyst/profile|The Collection Analyst]]
**Industry:** [[industries/financial-data-vendors|Financial Data Vendors]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** The analyst resolving a hard footnote at 2 a.m. needs the issuer's prior treatment, peer precedents and the policy paragraph, and today has a wiki search.
**Tags:** #tacit-knowledge-ml #large-language-models #k-nearest-neighbors #word-embeddings #worker-facing #evaluation-metrics
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to retain the analysts who carry issuer judgement through earnings season — and whoever makes that judgement capturable and the job survivable gets accuracy that compounds instead of walking out with attrition.

## The Problem
Judgement items are resolved alone under deadline, from memory and scattered references.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Tools are owned by throughput-focused operations; precedent data is unstructured.

## What to Build
Retrieval over the issuer's history, peers' similar items and the policy library, shown in the keying screen, with one-click escalation carrying the context to a senior.

## Target Customer
Content operations leadership.

## Impact If Built
Shorter resolution time and faster ramp-up for new analysts.
