# The Scorecard That Punishes Judgement

**Niche:** [[niches/financial-data-vendors/the-collection-analyst/profile|The Collection Analyst]]
**Industry:** [[industries/financial-data-vendors|Financial Data Vendors]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Following a wrong XBRL tag is the issuer's error; overriding it wrongly is the analyst's, so analysts learn not to override.
**Tags:** #evaluation-metrics #hypothesis-testing #worker-facing #tacit-knowledge-ml #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to retain the analysts who carry issuer judgement through earnings season — and whoever makes that judgement capturable and the job survivable gets accuracy that compounds instead of walking out with attrition.

## The Problem
QA error counting is asymmetric and discourages the judgement the product depends on.

## Why It's Still Broken
The scorecard is simple and comparable; nobody measures what it suppresses.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Score evidenced overrides separately, adjudicate ambiguous cases rather than charging errors, and measure override precision as a positive skill.

## Who Feels the Pain
Experienced analysts, and clients receiving tag-faithful wrong numbers.

## Impact If Fixed
Restores the incentive to exercise judgement.
