# Waste Industry Associations & Research Foundations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/waste-haulers|Waste Haulers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The National Waste & Recycling Association (private haulers' trade body; its 2023 driver-shortage paper counts more than 130,000 private collection trucks), SWANA and its Applied Research Foundation (public and private solid waste professionals; driver-shortage and safety studies), and the Environmental Research & Education Foundation (EREF), which publishes landfill tip-fee analyses (an unweighted national average of $54.03 a ton in 2021, search summary) and studies of fires at facilities and in collection vehicles. Members, sponsors and grants pay.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research staff at each organisation (estimate)
**Output:** Benchmark surveys, safety statistics, research reports, policy papers
**Proprietary data:** Member and facility surveys, incident compilations
**Clock:** Self-set publication and conference calendars
**Buyer:** Executive director or director of research
