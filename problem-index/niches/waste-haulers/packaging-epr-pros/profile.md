# Packaging EPR Producer Responsibility Organisations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/waste-haulers|Waste Haulers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Producer-funded nonprofits that run state packaging EPR programmes: they collect producers' supply reports and fees, write programme plans and needs assessments for state regulators, and reimburse local governments and service providers for recycling collection and processing. Seven states have passed packaging EPR laws (California, Colorado, Maine, Maryland, Minnesota, Oregon and Washington). Circular Action Alliance is the PRO in most of them; Oregon producers were first invoiced on 1 July 2025 and Colorado's on 1 January 2026. For haulers the PRO is a new payer: collection costs that cities used to fund through rates can now be reimbursed under the programme.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 programme, fee-modelling and data analysts (estimate); needs assessments are often contracted to consultancies
**Output:** Programme plans, eco-modulated fee schedules, needs assessments, reimbursement rules and annual reports to state agencies
**Proprietary data:** Producers' supply reports of packaging by material and state, plus reimbursement claims from local governments and service providers
**Clock:** Statutory programme-plan, reporting and invoicing dates set by state law and agencies — regulatory compliance dates, scored as at most Q4 4
**Buyer:** Executive director or chief programme officer of the PRO
