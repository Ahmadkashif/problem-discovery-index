# Coaching That Only Reaches the Driver's Seat

**Niche:** [[niches/waste-haulers/route-drivers-helpers/profile|Route Drivers & Helpers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/waste-haulers|Waste Haulers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Safety coaching is built around the driver's camera events, so helpers, who are often temporary and work outside the cab, get a toolbox talk and nothing else.
**Tags:** #worker-facing #compliance #quick-win #large-language-models #cnns
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep collection crews out of the injury and collision statistics, and whoever brings the crews' OSHA recordable incident rate lowest takes the account.

## The Problem
Event recorders face the driver and the road. Helpers' unsafe steps — riding while reversing, crossing behind the truck, lifting overloaded carts — appear only on rear cameras nobody reviews for coaching, and many helpers come through staffing agencies for weeks at a time.

## Why It's Still Broken
Coaching workflows are tied to the driver's ID and the vendor's driver score; helpers have no profile in the system.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Rear-camera clips of helper behaviour classified and queued for short, same-week coaching in the helper's language, with agency-supplied helpers included and a simple record that follows them between assignments.

## Who Feels the Pain
Helpers, who carry the struck-by risk; supervisors; staffing agencies supplying temporary crew.

## Impact If Fixed
Bringing helpers into coaching covers the half of a rear-load crew now outside the programme; if helper injuries fall by a fifth (estimate), a 100-crew operation avoids several recordable injuries a year.
