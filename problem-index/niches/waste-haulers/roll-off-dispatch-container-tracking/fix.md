# Rental Days Nobody Bills

**Niche:** [[niches/waste-haulers/roll-off-dispatch-container-tracking/profile|Roll-Off Dispatch & Container Tracking]]
**Industry:** [[industries/waste-haulers|Waste Haulers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Roll-off prices include a rental period, and days beyond it are chargeable, but the overrun is only billed when someone remembers to check the box's drop date.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #automation #quick-win #revenue-impact #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep every roll-off box earning, and whoever runs the most billable hauls per container per month takes the account.

## The Problem
A haul typically includes a set number of days on site (estimate: 7–14), then a daily rental charge. Boxes sit for weeks; the charge is skipped because dispatch never flagged it, or waived because the customer complains and nobody can show the dates.

## Why It's Still Broken
Drop and pull dates live in the dispatcher's notes, not as events in billing; billing runs on hauls.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Drop and pull dates recorded automatically from the driver's tablet or the box tracker, with rental overruns calculated nightly, posted to the invoice, and a reminder texted to the customer before charges start.

## Who Feels the Pain
Owners losing revenue and box availability; dispatchers blamed for empty yards.

## Impact If Fixed
Billing overrun days on 500 boxes averaging ~5 chargeable days a month at ~$5–10 a day (estimate) recovers ~$150–300K a year, and the reminder text gets boxes pulled sooner.
