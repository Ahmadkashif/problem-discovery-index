# Equipment Rental Fleet Software for Roll-Off Boxes

**Niche:** [[niches/waste-haulers/roll-off-dispatch-container-tracking/profile|Roll-Off Dispatch & Container Tracking]]
**Industry:** [[industries/waste-haulers|Waste Haulers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Equipment rental companies already manage utilisation, dwell and off-rent calls for fleets of machines on job sites; a roll-off box is a rental asset that also needs a truck and a landfill trip every time it moves.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #optimization-fundamentals #automation #revenue-impact #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep every roll-off box earning, and whoever runs the most billable hauls per container per month takes the account.

## The Problem
Roll-off operators manage boxes the way rental yards managed machines before telematics: by phone and memory.

## What Already Exists
Equipment rental software tracks each asset's status, location and time on rent, prompts off-rent calls, bills rental periods automatically and reports time and dollar utilisation by asset class.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: a haul (drop, swap, pull) as the billable event alongside rental days; tonnage from scale tickets with overweight and prohibited-material charges; landfill and transfer-station hours and waits in dispatch; municipal right-of-way permits for street placements; and contractor order habits by text and phone.

## Target Customer
Roll-off operators outgrowing a whiteboard or a billing-only hauler package.

## Impact If Solved
Rental yards track time utilisation as their core metric; lifting box utilisation by ~10 points (estimate) on 500 boxes is equivalent to ~50 boxes of avoided capital.
