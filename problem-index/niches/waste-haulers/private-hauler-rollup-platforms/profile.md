# Private Hauler Rollup Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/waste-haulers|Waste Haulers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Private equity-backed regional platforms that buy independent haulers for route density and disposal access, integrate their billing and routes, and often sell to a public consolidator; GFL's Q3 2025 deals included a private equity-backed Oklahoma hauler, and GFL itself bought WCA Waste (37 collection operations, more than 1,000 collection vehicles) for ~$1.21B (search summary). Corporate development and integration teams model each tuck-in's route overlap, price headroom and disposal internalisation. Owners and investors pay through returns on exit.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Corporate development and integration teams of ~2–8 per platform (estimate)
**Output:** Acquisition models, integration plans, route-density and pricing analyses
**Proprietary data:** Acquired companies' customer and route books once owned; targets' data under NDA during diligence
**Clock:** Deal timelines and fund exit horizons
**Buyer:** Chief executive or head of corporate development
