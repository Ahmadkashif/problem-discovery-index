# Collection Truck Body & Chassis Makers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/waste-haulers|Waste Haulers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Makers of refuse bodies (automated side-loaders, front-loaders, rear-loaders, roll-off hoists) and the cab-over chassis they ride on. Terex bought Dover's Environmental Solutions Group — Heil, Marathon, Curotto-Can, Bayne, plus 3rd Eye cameras and Soft-Pak software — for $2.0B in October 2024 (search summary). Oshkosh's McNeilus, Labrie and New Way build bodies; Mack, Peterbilt and Autocar build chassis. Haulers buy trucks and parts; Republic alone runs ~17,800 collection vehicles.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Engineering, product and dealer teams; market research and competitive intelligence under 10 people per maker (estimate)
**Output:** Truck designs, safety options, body telematics, market forecasts for internal planning
**Proprietary data:** Body controller and telematics data from customers' trucks, warranty and parts records
**Clock:** Model-year launches and emissions rules
**Buyer:** VP of product or strategy
