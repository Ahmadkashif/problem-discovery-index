# Waste Fleet Auto Liability & Workers' Comp Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/waste-haulers|Waste Haulers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Specialty insurance programmes, run by carriers, MGAs and brokers, that write auto liability, physical damage and workers' compensation for collection fleets — a hard class, given the fourth-highest occupational fatality rate and the frequency of backing and intersection claims. The consolidators self-insure large retentions (Republic's 10-K notes rising deductible retentions); independents buy from the programmes. Haulers pay premiums; underwriters and loss-control staff price and inspect.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 underwriters and loss-control specialists per programme (estimate)
**Output:** Pricing and risk-selection decisions, loss-control surveys, renewal terms
**Proprietary data:** Loss histories across insured haulers, telematics and video data supplied by insureds
**Clock:** Policy renewals and claim notice periods
**Buyer:** Programme manager or class underwriter
