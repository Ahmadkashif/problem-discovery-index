# Telecom Revenue Assurance for the Container Book

**Niche:** [[niches/waste-haulers/commercial-container-service/profile|Commercial Front-Load Container Service]]
**Industry:** [[industries/waste-haulers|Waste Haulers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Telecom carriers have long run revenue-assurance software that reconciles what was delivered against what was billed; a hauler's commercial book has the same leak with containers instead of minutes.

Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Commercial books leak. Containers are upsized on the street without the rate changing, extra pickups are run as favours, accounts sit on promotional rates years after the promotion, and fees are waived and never restored. Nobody reconciles what the truck does against what the invoice says.

## What Already Exists
Telecom and utility revenue-assurance platforms reconcile network usage records against billing records, flag leakage by rule, and track recovery. Hauler billing systems (Soft-Pak, Routeware, AMCS) can report on rates but not against service records.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: service records from lift sensors and cameras instead of call records; container size observed versus contracted; pickup frequency observed versus billed; franchise rules that cap or forbid certain charges; and a recovery workflow that goes through a sales rep, because correcting a long-standing rate is a customer conversation, not a back-office adjustment.

## Target Customer
CFOs and revenue managers at haulers with large open-market commercial books and mixed acquired billing systems.

## Impact If Solved
Revenue-assurance programmes in other utilities commonly recover 1–2% of billed revenue (estimate); on a $50M commercial book that is ~$0.5–1M a year.
