# Residential Franchise & Municipal Contract Collection

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/waste-haulers|Waste Haulers]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to price a multi-year franchise bid against routes it has never run, and whoever's bid lands closest to the realised cost per home over the contract term takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$15B in US residential collection revenue under franchise, contract and subscription (estimate; WM $3.51B and Republic $3.01B in 2025)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~20% (estimate; 18.1% of Republic's revenue)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — automated trucks, RFID carts and tablets at the majors; bid costing in spreadsheets everywhere
**Target Buyer:** Director of municipal marketing or bid manager at a regional hauler
**Automation Potential:** Medium — cost modelling and proposal assembly automate; the city relationship does not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Residential collection is won by competitive bid. A city issues an RFP for exclusive service to its homes, often for five to ten years with renewal options; Republic's residential contracts "usually range in duration from one to five years", with some franchises much longer. The hauler bids a monthly rate per home for routes it has never run, with service standards, cart deliveries, SB 1383 obligations and liquidated damages such as $100–150 per missed collection (search summary). Rates then move only by the contract's adjustment formula. Majors, regionals and the incumbent compete; the city's consultant shapes the RFP. A bid that underestimates cost loses money for the whole term, and one that overestimates loses the award, so cost-forecast accuracy decides the account.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Route modelling in RouteSmart, Routeware and AMCS; parcel data from counties; bid workbooks owned by one analyst; public bid tabulations scattered across council minutes, though general procurement-intelligence vendors such as [[niches/it-value-added-resellers/public-sector-procurement-intelligence/profile|🔍 Public-Sector IT Procurement Intelligence]] track the solicitations. Gaps: no system compares bid assumptions with realised cost on won contracts, and losing bids are rarely analysed.
