# Construction Estimating Feedback Loops for Franchise Bids

**Niche:** [[niches/waste-haulers/residential-franchise-collection/profile|Residential Franchise & Municipal Contract Collection]]
**Industry:** [[industries/waste-haulers|Waste Haulers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Contractors' estimating platforms already compare bid quantities and unit costs with job-cost actuals; franchise bids need the same loop with routes in place of line items.
**Tags:** #linear-regression #gradient-boosting #data-integration #revenue-impact #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to price a multi-year franchise bid against routes it has never run, and whoever's bid lands closest to the realised cost per home over the contract term takes the account.

## The Problem
Haulers estimate franchise bids in spreadsheets with no link to the operating data that would grade them.

## What Already Exists
Construction estimating and job-costing suites keep a cost database of unit rates, estimate jobs from takeoffs, and update the database from completed jobs' actual costs.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: a route-cost database (homes per hour by density, set-out rates, tons per home) in place of unit rates; parcel and street data in place of drawings; multi-year rate-adjustment formulas tied to CPI, fuel and disposal indices; liquidated-damage and service-standard costs priced into the bid; and actuals drawn from truck telemetry and scale tickets rather than timesheets.

## Target Customer
Regional haulers with a dedicated bid team and several franchises up for renewal each year.

## Impact If Solved
Reusing graded unit costs across bids could cut bid preparation from several weeks of analyst time to days (estimate: ~60–100 hours saved per bid).
