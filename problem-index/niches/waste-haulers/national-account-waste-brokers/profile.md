# National Account Waste Brokers & Invoice Auditors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/waste-haulers|Waste Haulers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Brokers that manage waste and recycling service for multi-site businesses (retail chains, restaurant groups, property portfolios, manufacturers) without owning trucks: they negotiate with local haulers, pay the haulers' invoices, bill the client one consolidated invoice, audit charges and report diversion. Quest Resource Holding (FY2025 revenue $250.2M, 17.0% gross margin) and RoadRunner Recycling, which bought the in-container camera firm Compology in 2022 (search summary), are representative, alongside Rubicon's marketplace business and private brokers. Clients pay a managed-service margin; haulers see the broker as a customer and a competitor for the end account.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 pricing, procurement-analytics and sustainability-reporting analysts at the largest broker (estimate); invoice auditors and site coordinators who process each location's bills are production and not counted
**Output:** Client spend and diversion reports, hauler rate benchmarks used in negotiation, savings analyses in proposals
**Proprietary data:** The prices the broker itself pays local haulers for comparable service across thousands of sites and many markets; client site volumes and spend held under service agreements
**Clock:** Hauler contract renewals and clients' sustainability-reporting calendars, both set by the parties to the broker's own contracts
**Buyer:** Chief operating officer or head of procurement analytics at the broker

**Kill switches:** none on the half that carries the scored work — the broker contracts with haulers in its own name, so the purchase prices behind its benchmarks are its own records. The other half, client site volumes, spend and diversion data held under service agreements, would be a live "data isn't theirs" switch for any cross-client product, and no checked terms grant aggregate rights.
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. The cross-hauler price archive is unusual and unused beyond negotiation, but the invoice is a service margin rather than analysis, the analytical team is small once auditors are set aside, and no third party sets the clock.
