# Parcel Network Planning for Fixed Weekly Routes

**Niche:** [[niches/waste-haulers/residential-route-planning/profile|Residential Route Planning & Rebalancing]]
**Industry:** [[industries/waste-haulers|Waste Haulers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Parcel carriers' territory planning tools design stable delivery areas balanced on observed workload; residential collection is a fixed weekly territory problem with heavier, slower stops.
**Tags:** #dynamic-programming #optimization-fundamentals #k-means-clustering #automation #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to build residential routes that drivers finish as planned, and whoever leaves the smallest gap between planned and actual route hours takes the account.

## The Problem
Collection routes must be stable (residents learn their day) yet rebalanced as homes are added, which generic vehicle-routing solvers handle poorly.

## What Already Exists
Parcel and postal territory planning software balances fixed delivery areas on historical stop counts and dwell times, keeps territories stable across re-plans and models growth.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: collection day assignment that changes as few households as possible; truck capacity by weight and volume with mid-route disposal trips; separate trash, recycling and organics routes sharing carts and days; side-of-street service for automated arms; and franchise boundaries that a route may not cross for reporting.

## Target Customer
Haulers starting a new franchise or converting a city to automated or three-stream collection.

## Impact If Solved
A rebalance that moves ~5% of households instead of ~20% (estimate) avoids thousands of missed-pickup calls in the first weeks of a new schedule.
