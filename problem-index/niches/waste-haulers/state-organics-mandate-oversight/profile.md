# State Organics & Recycling Mandate Oversight

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/waste-haulers|Waste Haulers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State agencies that set and oversee collection mandates that haulers carry out, with CalRecycle's SB 1383 programme the largest: it requires jurisdictions to provide organics collection, review every hauler route for contamination each year, keep records and file an Electronic Annual Report, against a statewide target of 75% less organic waste to landfill by 2025. Other states and large cities run smaller organics and recycling mandates. The wider permitting and enforcement work of state environmental agencies (not built as an industry) is outside this pocket, which covers only collection-mandate oversight.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–60 policy, data and programme analysts on organics and jurisdiction compliance (estimate); staff reviewing individual jurisdictions' filings are production
**Output:** Regulations and guidance, jurisdiction compliance reviews, statewide disposal and diversion analysis, reports to the legislature
**Proprietary data:** Annual reports from every California jurisdiction, disposal reporting data and commissioned waste characterisation studies — largely public records
**Clock:** Statutory targets set by the legislature; the agency's review and reporting cycles are its own, so self-set
**Buyer:** Deputy director for the organics or local assistance programme
