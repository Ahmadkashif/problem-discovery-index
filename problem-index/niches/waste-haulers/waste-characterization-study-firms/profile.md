# Waste Characterisation & Diversion Study Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/waste-haulers|Waste Haulers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Firms that sort and weigh samples of collected waste to estimate its composition, and model diversion and collection programmes for state agencies and cities — Cascadia Consulting Group, SCS Engineers, Tetra Tech and MSW Consultants are representative. Statewide characterisation studies inform mandates such as SB 1383 and EPR needs assessments; city studies inform franchise design. Agencies and cities pay per study.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Field sort crews and per-study analysts; no firm found with 10+ analysts working outside individual engagements (estimate)
**Output:** Composition studies, diversion potential analyses, programme design reports
**Proprietary data:** Sort data and study archives, commissioned and owned by the clients, often published in summary
**Clock:** Clients' study schedules and regulatory programme timelines
**Buyer:** Practice lead for solid waste planning
