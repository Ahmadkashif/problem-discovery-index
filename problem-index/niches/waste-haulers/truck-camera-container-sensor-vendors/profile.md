# Truck Camera & Container Sensor Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/waste-haulers|Waste Haulers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Firms that put cameras and sensors on collection trucks and in containers and sell detection of overfilled, contaminated or missed containers: AMCS Vision AI, Prairie Robotics, Safe Fleet's WasteVision AI, Terex's 3rd Eye and Compology, now part of RoadRunner Recycling (search summary). WM builds its own Smart Truck system. East Lansing's Prairie Robotics programme cut contamination ~25%. Haulers and jurisdictions pay for hardware and subscriptions; some vendors also sell human review of flagged images.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analysts per vendor (estimate); image reviewers who confirm detections are production
**Output:** Detection feeds, evidence images, contamination and overflow reports for customers
**Proprietary data:** Millions of labelled container images captured on customers' trucks and in customers' containers
**Clock:** Customers' billing cycles and SB 1383 review schedules
**Buyer:** Chief technology officer or head of product
