# Franchise Tonnage, Diversion & Compliance Reporting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/waste-haulers|Waste Haulers]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce franchise and state reports straight from scale tickets and stop data, and whoever's reported tonnages reconcile to disposal-site scale records with the smallest variance takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$150M in analyst time and reporting software spent by private haulers on franchise, municipal and state waste reporting (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.2% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium-low — the source data is digital, the reports are assembled in spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** Municipal contract manager or director of compliance at a regional hauler
**Automation Potential:** High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every franchise asks for periodic reports of tons by stream, customer counts, diversion, complaints, service failures and franchise fees calculated on gross receipts. California adds SB 1383 records, and EPR programmes in Oregon and Colorado have begun reimbursing recycling collection costs, which brings producer responsibility organisations into the reporting chain. The work is mechanical once each stop and load is tagged to a jurisdiction. The contest is whether the reported tons reconcile with the scale records the city, county or state can check. Hauler platforms' report modules (Routeware with its Recyclist acquisition, AMCS, Soft-Pak), consultants hired by cities to audit franchises, and in-house analysts compete for the work.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Scale-house systems, billing exports, Re-TRAC Connect and state portals, BI dashboards and spreadsheets. Gaps: shared loads crossing city lines need a documented allocation; diversion definitions differ by contract; franchise-fee calculations are disputed in audits; and each city's template is rebuilt by hand. Consultants who audit franchises for cities are logged in Pass 2 below. The disposal-site end of the same reconciliation, where the tickets are written, is [[niches/landfill-operators/scale-house-ticketing-fees/profile|⚡ Scale-House Ticketing, Tonnage & Fee Reporting]]. Contamination measured downstream, in materials recovery facility bales and compost feedstock, stays with the queued recycling-facilities and compost-producers builds; this niche reports tons, not quality.
