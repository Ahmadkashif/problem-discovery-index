# Utility Regulatory Reporting Engines for Franchise Exhibits

**Niche:** [[niches/waste-haulers/franchise-tonnage-reporting/profile|Franchise Tonnage, Diversion & Compliance Reporting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/waste-haulers|Waste Haulers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Regulated utilities already generate dozens of regulator-specific filings from one data model; a hauler's franchise reports are many small regulators asking similar questions in different templates.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #compliance #automation #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce franchise and state reports straight from scale tickets and stop data, and whoever's reported tonnages reconcile to disposal-site scale records with the smallest variance takes the account.

## The Problem
Haulers rebuild each franchise's report from exports every period.

## What Already Exists
Utility regulatory reporting tools map operational and financial data to each commission's forms, keep the calculation logic auditable and version-controlled, and produce filings on schedule.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: per-contract definitions of diversion, gross receipts and service failures; tonnage allocation across jurisdictions; SB 1383 route-review records and EAR counts; EPR reimbursement claims to the producer responsibility organisation; and LLM-assisted mapping of each new contract's reporting exhibit into the data model.

## Target Customer
Haulers with 10+ municipal contracts and a central compliance team.

## Impact If Solved
Onboarding a new contract's reports in days rather than weeks, and cutting each recurring report from ~2 days to a few hours (estimate), frees most of a compliance analyst per 15–20 contracts.
