# Solid Waste Procurement & Rate Consultants

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/waste-haulers|Waste Haulers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Consultancies that cities and counties hire to design and run collection franchise procurements, evaluate haulers' proposals, review rate-adjustment requests and audit franchise compliance and fee remittances — HF&H Consultants, R3 Consulting Group, Gershman, Brickner & Bratton, MSW Consultants and NewGen Strategies & Solutions are representative. Jurisdictions pay by engagement. They shape the RFPs haulers bid on and the audits haulers face.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Firms of ~20–60 staff (estimate), nearly all working engagement by engagement; no firm found with a 10+ research function separate from its consultants
**Output:** RFP documents, proposal evaluations, rate-review reports, franchise audits
**Proprietary data:** Engagement files across many franchises, including haulers' cost submissions in rate reviews
**Clock:** Each client's procurement and council calendar
**Buyer:** Principal or managing partner
