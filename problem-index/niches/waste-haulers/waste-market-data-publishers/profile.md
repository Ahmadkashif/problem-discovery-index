# Waste Industry Market & M&A Data Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/waste-haulers|Waste Haulers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Small publishers that track the size, structure and deal flow of the waste industry — Waste Business Journal and Environmental Business International are representative — selling market reports, company directories and consolidation data to haulers, investors, lenders and suppliers. Trade outlets such as Waste Dive compile public filings (for example, the five public haulers' quarterly acquisition spend) but sell advertising and events rather than data.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analysts per publisher (estimate)
**Output:** Annual market reports, facility and company databases, M&A summaries
**Proprietary data:** Facility and company directories built from surveys and public records
**Clock:** Self-set annual publication
**Buyer:** Publisher or founder
