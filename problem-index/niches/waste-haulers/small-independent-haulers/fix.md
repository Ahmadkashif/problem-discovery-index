# Service That Never Stops for Non-Payment

**Niche:** [[niches/waste-haulers/small-independent-haulers/profile|Small Independent Haulers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/waste-haulers|Waste Haulers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Small haulers keep collecting from customers who stopped paying months ago, because suspending a stop means the owner remembering to tell the driver.
**Tags:** #automation #quick-win #revenue-impact #workflow-orchestration #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to become the one system a 2–20 truck hauler bills, routes and collects payments on, and whoever gets the owner's days sales outstanding lowest takes the account.

## The Problem
Billing and routing are separate: the invoice says 90 days past due, the route sheet still lists the stop, and the driver empties the cart.

## Why It's Still Broken
Most small-hauler billing packages export a route list rather than drive it, and owners avoid confrontations with neighbours.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A suspend rule in billing (for example, 60 days past due after two notices) that removes the stop from the driver's route, prints a cart tag, and restores service automatically when payment posts.

## Who Feels the Pain
Owners carrying bad debt; drivers servicing stops that pay nothing; paying customers subsidising the rest.

## Impact If Fixed
If ~3% of a 3,000-household book is 90+ days past due at ~$30 a month (estimate), enforcing suspension recovers or removes ~$30K a year of unpaid service for a hauler of that size.
