# Small Independent Haulers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/waste-haulers|Waste Haulers]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to run a 2–20 truck hauler's billing and payment collection, and whoever gets the owner's days sales outstanding lowest takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$6.9B in receipts at the 5,796 solid waste collection firms with fewer than 20 employees (Census SUSB 2022), plus most of the 1,300 small "other waste collection" firms (~$1.0B); billing and payments software spend ~$100M (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of receipts, ~85% of firms
**Digital Adoption:** Low — QuickBooks, paper invoices, cheques and a hauler package used for invoicing only
**Target Buyer:** Owner-operator, often with a spouse or one office manager running billing
**Automation Potential:** High for invoicing, payments and dunning; the owner still drives

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A small hauler runs a few rear-loaders or front-loaders and some roll-off trucks, serves subscription households in unfranchised areas and local businesses, and competes with consolidators that may make it an acquisition offer at any time. The binding constraint is cash: service runs before it is billed, extras such as bulky items and extra bags are billed weeks later from paper notes, customers pay late, and an owner who is also a driver chases payments in the evening. The vendors fighting for this owner's billing are Hauler Hero ($16M Series A in February 2026), Cairn (bought by Solen in December 2025, >350 customers, search summary), CurbWaste, Terex's Soft-Pak and Routeware, alongside general recurring-billing and payment processors. Several sell routing in the same bundle, but the contest that decides the account is billing and collections, measured by how fast invoiced revenue turns into cash. Small-hauler routing is the same optimisation contest as residential route planning.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Billing-first hauler packages, QuickBooks, card and ACH processors, and spreadsheets. Gaps: autopay enrolment is low and manual; nothing ranks which overdue accounts to chase first; extras are billed late or not at all; and suspend-for-nonpayment depends on the owner remembering to tell the driver.
