# Utility Prepay and Autopay Billing for Subscription Trash

**Niche:** [[niches/waste-haulers/small-independent-haulers/profile|Small Independent Haulers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/waste-haulers|Waste Haulers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Small utilities and propane dealers already run quarterly prepay, autopay and dunning for households; subscription trash is the same recurring household bill with a truck behind it.
**Tags:** #logistic-regression #automation #revenue-impact #workflow-orchestration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to run a 2–20 truck hauler's billing and payment collection, and whoever gets the owner's days sales outstanding lowest takes the account.

## The Problem
Subscription haulers bill quarterly in advance or monthly in arrears by paper invoice, take cheques, and chase late payers by phone.

## What Already Exists
Utility and recurring-billing platforms handle autopay enrolment, prepay plans, card updates, dunning sequences, late fees and service suspension for unpaid accounts.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: service suspension that removes a stop from the next day's route and tells the driver; cart retrieval after cancellation; seasonal accounts (summer homes); bulky-item and extra-bag charges added from the driver's tablet; and price-increase notices that follow state rules for consumer services.

## Target Customer
Owners of subscription-heavy haulers in rural and unfranchised areas.

## Impact If Solved
Moving most households to autopay typically cuts days sales outstanding by weeks (estimate: from ~45 to ~15 days), freeing a month of revenue in cash for an owner who finances trucks.
