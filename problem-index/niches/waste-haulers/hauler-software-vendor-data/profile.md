# Hauler Software Vendor Data Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/waste-haulers|Waste Haulers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors of hauler operating software — Routeware (more than 1,500 clients after buying RubiconSmartCity and RubiconPro in 2024), AMCS, Terex's Soft-Pak (search summary), Hauler Hero ($16M Series A in February 2026) and Solen's Cairn (search summary) — whose billing, routing, in-cab and camera products hold service, route and billing records for thousands of haulers and municipal fleets. Their data and AI teams build features such as route optimisation, overflow detection and customer-service agents. Haulers pay software subscriptions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 data, analytics and AI staff at the largest vendor (estimate); implementation and support staff are production
**Output:** Product features (optimisation, detection, forecasting), occasional benchmark reports for customers
**Proprietary data:** Customers' routes, lifts, billing and camera events across many haulers, held under each customer's subscription terms
**Clock:** Self-set product releases; customers' franchise and SB 1383 reporting dates belong to the customers
**Buyer:** Chief product officer or head of data at the vendor
