# The Notice That Never Reaches the Repeat Offender

**Niche:** [[niches/waste-haulers/contamination-route-reviews/profile|Contamination Route Reviews & Organics Compliance]]
**Industry:** [[industries/waste-haulers|Waste Haulers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A cart tag blows away and a postcard goes to the landlord, so the household that contaminates the organics cart every week never learns what it did wrong.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #compliance #worker-facing #quick-win #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to find and notice contaminated carts on every route the regulation requires reviewed, and whoever brings the measured contamination rate on reviewed routes down furthest takes the account.

## The Problem
SB 1383 requires the generator to be notified and educated. In practice notices go to the account holder (often a property manager), arrive days later, and carry no photo of the actual cart.

## Why It's Still Broken
Billing records hold the account holder, not the resident; camera images are kept by the vendor; and notices are generated in batches.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A notice sent the same day by text, app or door hanger at the service address with the photo of the contaminated cart, in the household's language, and a follow-up check of that cart on the next two collections.

## Who Feels the Pain
Route reviewers whose tags change nothing; drivers dumping contaminated organics loads; MRF and compost operators downstream.

## Impact If Fixed
WM reports up to 20% lower contamination when cameras are paired with targeted outreach; reaching the actual household with photo evidence is the step that makes the outreach targeted.
