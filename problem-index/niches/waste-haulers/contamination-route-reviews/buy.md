# Food Safety Inspection Software for Lid-Flip Audits

**Niche:** [[niches/waste-haulers/contamination-route-reviews/profile|Contamination Route Reviews & Organics Compliance]]
**Industry:** [[industries/waste-haulers|Waste Haulers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Health departments' inspection apps already schedule field inspections, record findings with photos, issue notices and track repeat violators; contamination route reviews are the same workflow at the curb.
**Tags:** #cnns #compliance #workflow-orchestration #worker-facing #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to find and notice contaminated carts on every route the regulation requires reviewed, and whoever brings the measured contamination rate on reviewed routes down furthest takes the account.

## The Problem
Route reviewers record findings on paper tags and spreadsheets, then rebuild the records the regulation requires.

## What Already Exists
Environmental health inspection software schedules inspections by risk, captures findings and photos on a tablet, generates notices in the right language, escalates repeat violations and reports to the state.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: routes and collection days instead of establishments; three-stream contamination categories under 14 CCR 18984.5; notices translated into languages the jurisdiction must cover; camera detections as an input alongside human findings; the 18984.6 record fields and EAR counts; and the hauler-city split of responsibility in a franchise.

## Target Customer
Jurisdictions and haulers running SB 1383 route reviews with in-house or contracted auditors.

## Impact If Solved
Cutting the post-review data entry from ~30 minutes per route to minutes (estimate) saves hundreds of hours a year for a jurisdiction with a few hundred routes.
