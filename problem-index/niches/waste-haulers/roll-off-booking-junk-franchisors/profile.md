# Roll-Off Booking Networks & Junk-Removal Franchisors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/waste-haulers|Waste Haulers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
National brands that sell roll-off rentals and junk removal to households and small contractors online and by phone, then fulfil through local haulers or franchisees: dumpster booking networks such as Budget Dumpster and Dumpsters.com, and junk-removal franchisors such as College Hunks Hauling Junk and JunkLuggers. They earn a margin on subcontracted hauls or franchise royalties and compete with local haulers for the same customers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Pricing, marketing-analytics and franchise-performance staff under 10 per brand (estimate)
**Output:** Price quotes by market, lead routing, franchisee benchmarks
**Proprietary data:** National booking and pricing histories; franchisees' sales and job data reported under franchise agreements
**Clock:** Self-set marketing and pricing cycles
**Buyer:** Chief marketing officer or head of franchise operations
