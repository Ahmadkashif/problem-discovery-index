# Parking Enforcement Evidence Workflows for Overflow Events

**Niche:** [[niches/waste-haulers/overage-extra-service-capture/profile|Overage & Extra-Service Charge Capture]]
**Industry:** [[industries/waste-haulers|Waste Haulers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Camera-based parking and traffic enforcement already turns an image into a notice, a payment and an appeal at scale; overflow charges need the same chain of evidence.
**Tags:** #cnns #object-detection #workflow-orchestration #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn what the truck camera saw into overage and extra-service charges that stick, and whoever has the lowest share of those charges credited back after dispute takes the account.

## The Problem
An overage charge is a penalty notice in all but name, and customers contest it the way drivers contest tickets.

## What Already Exists
Automated parking and traffic enforcement platforms capture an image, match it to a registered owner, issue a notice with evidence, handle payment and run an appeal process with review queues and statutory timelines.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: matching an image to a container and account instead of a plate; contract and franchise rules instead of a traffic code; a first-warning-per-year rule and seven-day waits like those in the Nevada settlement; an option to resolve by upsizing service instead of paying; and an appeal handled by a customer service rep inside the hauler's CRM rather than by a hearing officer.

## Target Customer
Haulers building or replacing their overage workflow after adding cameras.

## Impact If Solved
A structured appeal process with evidence typically cuts reversals; halving the share of overages credited back on a book billing ~$1M of overages a year retains ~$100–200K (estimate).
