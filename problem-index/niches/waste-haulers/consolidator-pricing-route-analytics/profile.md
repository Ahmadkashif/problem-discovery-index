# Consolidator Pricing & Route Analytics Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/waste-haulers|Waste Haulers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The central pricing, route-optimisation and corporate-development teams of the public consolidators (WM, Republic Services, Waste Connections, GFL and Casella), which together spent ~$1.5B, ~$659M and ~$545.7M on acquisitions in the first three quarters of 2025. They set annual price increases (Republic's Capture tool; average yield +4.1% in 2025), design routes for ~17,800 Republic collection vehicles and their peers' fleets, and model tuck-in acquisitions for route density and disposal internalisation. Their analysis is consumed internally and paid for through price and margin.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–100 pricing, route and acquisition analysts per consolidator (estimate; no team sizes published)
**Output:** Price-increase programmes, route plans and rebalances, acquisition models and integration plans
**Proprietary data:** Account-level price and cancellation histories and stop-level service telemetry across tens of thousands of routes, the deepest operating record in the industry
**Clock:** Self-set annual pricing cycles and deal timelines; quarterly earnings dates govern disclosure of yield, not the analysis behind it
**Buyer:** Chief financial officer or SVP of pricing at the consolidator
