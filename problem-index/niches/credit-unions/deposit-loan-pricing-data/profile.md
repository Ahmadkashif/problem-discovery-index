# Deposit & Loan Pricing Benchmark Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-unions|Credit Unions]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Curinos and comparable providers collect actual deposit and loan pricing, balance movement, and product terms contributed directly by banks and credit unions, normalize them by product and market, and sell the benchmarks institutions use to set rates. A credit union deciding what to pay on a money market account or charge on an auto loan is deciding against these numbers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 analysts, economists, data scientists, and product staff
**Output:** Rate and balance benchmarks, elasticity analyses, market share and pricing studies — the subscription
**Proprietary data:** Contributed account-level pricing and balance flow data that no institution publishes and no regulator collects at this granularity
**Clock:** Rate-setting cycles, which compress sharply whenever the policy rate moves
**Buyer:** Chief Analytics Officer / Head of Research
