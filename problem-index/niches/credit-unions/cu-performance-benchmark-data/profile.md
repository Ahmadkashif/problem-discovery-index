# Credit Union Performance Analytics Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-unions|Credit Unions]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Callahan & Associates and comparable providers turn quarterly regulatory call report filings into peer benchmarking, strategic analysis, and industry research that credit union executives and boards use to evaluate their own performance and set strategy.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-120 analysts, researchers, and data staff
**Output:** Peer benchmarks, performance analytics, industry trend research — the subscription
**Proprietary data:** Peer group construction methodology and longitudinal analysis over a call report dataset that is itself public
**Clock:** Quarterly call report release cycle, fixed and industry-wide
**Buyer:** VP of Research / Chief Analytics Officer
