# Asset-Liability & Interest Rate Risk Modelling Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-unions|Credit Unions]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
ZM Financial, Empyrean, QRM, and comparable vendors model how an institution's balance sheet behaves under rate scenarios — deposit decay, prepayment, and repricing — and produce the interest rate risk analysis examiners require and boards act on. The modelling assumptions are the substance and the examiner reviews them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 financial engineers, quantitative analysts, and consultants
**Output:** Interest rate risk models, deposit behaviour studies, board and examiner reporting
**Proprietary data:** Behavioural assumption libraries derived from client balance sheets across hundreds of institutions and multiple rate cycles
**Clock:** Regulatory reporting deadlines and board meeting cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Risk Officer / Head of Quantitative Services
