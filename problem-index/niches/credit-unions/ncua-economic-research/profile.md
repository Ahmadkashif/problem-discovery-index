# Federal Credit Union Regulator Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-unions|Credit Unions]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The regulator's economics and examination analytics functions monitor industry condition, build the risk models that direct examination resources, and publish the aggregate statistics the industry plans against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-120 economists, analysts, and examination staff
**Output:** Industry condition reports, examination risk models, aggregate statistics
**Proprietary data:** Complete call report and examination findings across every insured institution
**Clock:** Quarterly reporting and examination cycles
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer
