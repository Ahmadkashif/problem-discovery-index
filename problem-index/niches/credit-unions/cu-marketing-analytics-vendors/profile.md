## What They Do
Vendors serving credit union marketing build propensity and next-best-product models on member data, run campaign measurement, and publish cross-institution benchmarks on acquisition and wallet share.

**Output:** Propensity models, campaign measurement, growth benchmarks
**Proprietary data:** Member behaviour and campaign response across many institutions
**Clock:** Campaign calendars, self-set
**Buyer:** VP of Analytics / Chief Marketing Technologist
