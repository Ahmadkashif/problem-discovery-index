# Credit Union Research Institutes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-unions|Credit Unions]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Filene Research Institute and the trade association economics groups fund and publish applied research on cooperative finance, member behaviour, and industry strategy, and produce the economic analysis behind the sector's regulatory advocacy.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-60 researchers, economists, and programme staff
**Output:** Applied research reports, industry forecasts, member behaviour studies
**Proprietary data:** Commissioned research archive and member-contributed survey series
**Clock:** Publication calendars and legislative sessions
**Buyer:** Chief Research Officer / Chief Economist
