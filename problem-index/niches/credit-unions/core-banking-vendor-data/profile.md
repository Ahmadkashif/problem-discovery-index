# Core Banking Platform Data Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-unions|Credit Unions]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The data and analytics groups at Fiserv, Jack Henry, and Symitar build the reporting, benchmarking, and increasingly the member analytics that run on transaction and account data flowing through their core platforms across thousands of institutions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 data engineers, analysts, and product staff
**Output:** In-product analytics, peer benchmarking, derived data products
**Proprietary data:** Account and transaction data across thousands of institutions and tens of millions of members
**Clock:** Software release cycles, internal
**Buyer:** VP of Data Products / Chief Technology Officer
