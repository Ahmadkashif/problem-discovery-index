# CDFI Certification & Impact Reporting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-unions|Credit Unions]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The federal community development financial institution programme certifies institutions serving underserved markets and requires impact reporting as a condition of certification and grant eligibility. Consultants and internal teams produce the target market analyses and impact reports that certification and funding depend on.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 across programme administration and the consultancies supporting applicants
**Output:** Target market analyses, certification applications, impact reports
**Proprietary data:** Lending and impact data across certified institutions
**Clock:** Certification renewal and grant application deadlines
**Buyer:** Programme Director / Practice Leader
