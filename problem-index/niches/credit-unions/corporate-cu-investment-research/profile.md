# Corporate Credit Union Investment Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-unions|Credit Unions]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Corporate credit unions provide liquidity, settlement, and investment services to natural person credit unions, and their investment and economics desks produce the rate outlook, portfolio strategy, and balance sheet advisory that member institutions use.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 investment analysts, economists, and advisory staff
**Output:** Rate outlooks, portfolio strategy, balance sheet advisory
**Proprietary data:** Aggregate liquidity and investment behaviour across the member base
**Clock:** Rate cycles and member reporting calendars
**Buyer:** Chief Investment Officer / Head of Advisory Services
