# Credit Union Merger & Valuation Advisory

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-unions|Credit Unions]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Advisory firms guide credit union mergers and bank acquisitions by credit unions — valuing the target, modelling combined performance, and producing the analysis boards and regulators require before approving a combination.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 analysts and advisors
**Output:** Valuation analyses, merger models, regulatory application support
**Proprietary data:** Completed transaction terms in a market where most deals are not publicly priced
**Clock:** Regulatory approval timelines and board decision calendars
**Buyer:** Managing Director
