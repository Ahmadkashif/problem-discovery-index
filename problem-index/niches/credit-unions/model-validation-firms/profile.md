# Model Validation Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-unions|Credit Unions]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Independent firms validate the credit loss, interest rate risk, and financial crime models institutions rely on — reviewing conceptual soundness, testing outcomes, and issuing the validation report examiners expect to see. The report is the invoice and its absence is an examination finding.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 quantitative analysts and validators
**Output:** Model validation reports and findings
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated validation findings across institutions and the record of which approaches examiners accepted — held under client confidentiality
**Clock:** Examination cycles and annual validation requirements
**Buyer:** Practice leader / Managing Director
