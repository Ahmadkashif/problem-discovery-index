# CUSO Lending Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-unions|Credit Unions]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Credit union service organizations pool lending capacity across member institutions — participations, indirect lending, and commercial programmes — and small analytics teams underwrite, allocate, and monitor the resulting portfolios.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 3-12
**Output:** Underwriting analysis, participation allocation, portfolio monitoring
**Proprietary data:** Loan performance across participating institutions
**Clock:** Participation cycles and member reporting
**Buyer:** Chief Credit Officer
