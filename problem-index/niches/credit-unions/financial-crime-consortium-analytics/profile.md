# Financial Crime Consortium Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-unions|Credit Unions]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Verafin, NICE Actimize, and Feedzai run fraud and anti-money-laundering detection across large consortia of financial institutions, and their financial crime research teams analyze patterns that no single institution can see — a mule network moving between twelve credit unions, an elder fraud scheme repeating across a region. Detection and the investigative intelligence that supports it are what the institution actually buys.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 financial crime analysts, data scientists, and investigators
**Output:** Detection models, cross-institution alerts, typology research and investigative intelligence
**Proprietary data:** Transaction and case data pooled across hundreds of institutions under consortium agreements — the only view of criminal activity that moves between institutions rather than within one
**Clock:** Suspicious activity reporting deadlines, which are statutory and unforgiving
**Buyer:** VP of Financial Crime Research / Chief Data Scientist
