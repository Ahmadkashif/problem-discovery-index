# Employment Practices Liability Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hr-consultants|HR Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Insurers underwrite employment practices liability — discrimination, harassment, wrongful termination — pricing an employer's exposure from industry, size, jurisdiction, turnover, and HR practice.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 underwriters, actuaries, and claims analysts
**Output:** Rating and eligibility decisions, loss control guidance, claims analysis
**Proprietary data:** Employment claim frequency and severity by employer characteristic and jurisdiction — the empirical record of which HR practices actually precede a claim
**Clock:** Annual renewal
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
