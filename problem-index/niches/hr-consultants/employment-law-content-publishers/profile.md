# Employment Law Compliance Content Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hr-consultants|HR Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Mineral, SixFifty, Brightmine, and their peers track employment law across federal, fifty state, and hundreds of local jurisdictions, and turn it into the handbook policies, compliance alerts, and guidance HR consultants and small employers rely on. Pass 1 names them directly and names the gap: they supply legal update feeds and do not map changes to any specific client's configuration.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 employment attorneys, regulatory analysts, and content editors
**Output:** Jurisdiction-specific policy language, compliance alerts, handbook content, guidance libraries
**Proprietary data:** A maintained multi-jurisdiction interpretation of employment law built over years, with the reasoning behind each position held informally
**Clock:** Legislative effective dates, which are fixed and non-negotiable
**Buyer:** Chief Content Officer / VP of Legal Content
