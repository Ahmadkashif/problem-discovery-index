# Unemployment Claims Management

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hr-consultants|HR Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Specialist firms respond to and contest unemployment insurance claims on behalf of employers across all fifty state systems, representing them at hearings and managing the tax rate consequences. They work at enormous volume, usually on contingency or per-claim fees, against statutory response windows measured in days.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,500 claims analysts, hearing representatives, and tax specialists
**Output:** Claim responses, hearing representation, protest decisions, tax rate impact analysis
**Proprietary data:** Claim facts joined to determinations and appeal outcomes across fifty states and thousands of employers — the only assembled record of what actually wins a UI contest and where
**Clock:** State response deadlines, typically 7-14 days, with the right to contest lost on default
**Buyer:** SVP of Operations / General Manager
