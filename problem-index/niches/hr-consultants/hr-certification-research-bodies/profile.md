# HR Certification & Research Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hr-consultants|HR Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The HR professional bodies define the competencies the profession is certified against, run the credentialing examinations, and publish the workforce research and benchmarking their several hundred thousand members cite.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-150 researchers, psychometricians, and content staff
**Output:** Certification examinations, competency models, workforce research, member benchmarking surveys
**Proprietary data:** Examination performance across hundreds of thousands of candidates, plus long-running member survey series
**Clock:** Examination windows and recertification cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Knowledge Officer / VP of Research
