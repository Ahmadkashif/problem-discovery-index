# Compensation Survey Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hr-consultants|HR Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Mercer, WTW, Payscale, Salary.com, and ERI collect pay data from thousands of employers, match it to a common job architecture, and publish the market rates that set salary ranges across the economy. Pay transparency laws in a growing number of states now require employers to post ranges, which made the underlying benchmark a legal exposure rather than a planning input.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 compensation analysts, job matchers, statisticians, and survey operations staff
**Output:** Survey publications, market rate percentiles, job architecture and levelling frameworks, custom analyses
**Proprietary data:** Employer-contributed pay data under reciprocity, matched to a proprietary job taxonomy maintained over decades — a contributor network no new entrant can assemble
**Clock:** Annual survey and merit cycles, plus statutory pay transparency effective dates
**Buyer:** Global head of surveys / Chief Data Officer
