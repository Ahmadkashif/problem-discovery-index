# Background Screening Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hr-consultants|HR Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Sterling, HireRight, Checkr, and their peers assemble criminal, employment, education, and licence histories on job candidates and return the report an employer hires or declines on. They are consumer reporting agencies: the report is a regulated product, and its accuracy is the company's entire liability surface.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-2,000 researchers, verifiers, adjudicators, compliance analysts, and data scientists
**Output:** Consumer reports, adjudication recommendations against employer criteria, dispute resolutions
**Proprietary data:** Assembled court and verification records at national scale, plus a decade of their own adjudication decisions, disputes, and corrections — the raw records are public, the assembly and the outcome history are not
**Clock:** Hiring turnaround — a report that takes a week loses the candidate
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Operations
