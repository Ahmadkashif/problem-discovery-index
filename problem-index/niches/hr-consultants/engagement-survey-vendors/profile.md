# Employee Engagement Survey Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hr-consultants|HR Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Engagement and listening platforms run employee surveys, benchmark the results against normative databases, and sell employers the analysis of what their workforce thinks.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 organizational psychologists, analysts, and data scientists
**Output:** Engagement scores, driver analysis, benchmark comparisons, action recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Normative databases built from millions of survey responses across employers
**Clock:** Annual and pulse survey cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Science Officer / VP of People Analytics
