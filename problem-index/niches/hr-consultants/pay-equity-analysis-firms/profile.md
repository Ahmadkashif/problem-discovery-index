# Pay Equity Analysis Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hr-consultants|HR Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Specialist firms run the statistical pay equity analyses employers commission under pay transparency and reporting regimes — building comparator groups, fitting regression models, and identifying pay gaps that survive legitimate explanatory factors.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-80 labour economists, statisticians, and consultants
**Output:** Pay equity studies, remediation recommendations, regulatory filings, litigation support
**Proprietary data:** Methodology and comparator construction precedent; the employee data belongs to the client
**Clock:** Reporting deadlines and merit cycles
**Buyer:** Practice leader / Managing Director
