# PEO Compliance & Workforce Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hr-consultants|HR Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup

## What They Do
Professional employer organizations — ADP TotalSource, TriNet, Insperity — become the co-employer for hundreds of thousands of SMB worksite employees, and run internal compliance research groups that must get employment law right across every jurisdiction their clients operate in, at their own liability.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 compliance analysts, employment counsel, and workforce researchers
**Output:** Compliance positions, policy templates, client advisories, risk assessments
**Proprietary data:** Employment, wage, turnover, and claims records across hundreds of thousands of worksite employees at small employers — the population nobody else measures directly
**Clock:** Payroll cycles and regulatory effective dates
**Buyer:** VP of Compliance — internal cost centre
