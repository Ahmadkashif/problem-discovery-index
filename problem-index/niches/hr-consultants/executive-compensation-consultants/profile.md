# Executive Compensation Consultants

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hr-consultants|HR Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Independent compensation consultants advise boards on executive pay — peer group construction, pay-for-performance analysis, plan design, and the disclosure that goes into the proxy. The compensation committee retains them directly, and their analysis is what the committee's decisions are defended with.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-200 consultants and analysts
**Output:** Peer group recommendations, competitive pay analyses, plan design, proxy support, say-on-pay defence
**Proprietary data:** Proprietary executive survey data layered over public proxy disclosure, plus accumulated peer group and plan design precedent
**Clock:** Proxy season and compensation committee calendars, immovable
**Buyer:** Practice leader / Managing Partner
