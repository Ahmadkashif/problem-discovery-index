# Employee Benefits Brokerage Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hr-consultants|HR Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The analytics and actuarial groups inside benefits brokerages model plan design, project claims cost, and build the renewal recommendations employers accept or reject each year — the benefits benchmarking Pass 1 says consultants currently do by manual re-entry into carrier quoting tools.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-400 actuaries, underwriters, and benefits analysts
**Output:** Renewal projections, plan design modelling, benchmarking against book peers, stop-loss analysis
**Proprietary data:** Plan design, contribution, and claims experience across a large book of employer clients
**Clock:** Renewal and open enrolment calendars, fixed annually
**Buyer:** Chief Actuary / National Practice Leader
