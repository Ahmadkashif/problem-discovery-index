# Payroll Platform Research Institutes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hr-consultants|HR Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The research arms of the large payroll processors analyze their own payroll records — covering tens of millions of US workers — and publish employment, wage, and turnover research that markets, economists, and policymakers read.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 economists, data scientists, and researchers
**Output:** Employment and pay reports, wage growth series, workforce research, client-facing benchmarks
**Proprietary data:** Payroll records for a very large share of US private employment, weekly, at individual level — the largest private employment dataset in existence
**Clock:** Monthly and quarterly release calendars
**Buyer:** Head of Research / Chief Economist
