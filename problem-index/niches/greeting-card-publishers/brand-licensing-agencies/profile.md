# Brand Licensing Agencies & Extension Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/greeting-card-publishers|Greeting Card Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Licensing agencies represent brand and character owners, find licensees across product categories (cards, gift wrap, party goods, apparel, toys), negotiate deals and manage approvals and royalty collection, earning a commission on royalties. Larger agencies also sell brand extension strategy. Card publishers meet them as the counterparty offering properties and setting minimum guarantees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 strategy, insight and royalty analysts at the larger agencies (estimate)
**Output:** Licensing programme strategies, category plans, licensee recommendations, royalty reporting to clients
**Proprietary data:** Deal terms and licensee royalty reports across the brands they represent
**Clock:** Licensing show calendars, licence renewal dates and licensee reporting quarters
**Buyer:** Agency principal or Head of Strategy
