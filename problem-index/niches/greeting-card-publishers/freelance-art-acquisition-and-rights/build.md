# Matching Pocket Briefs to Artist Portfolios

**Niche:** [[niches/greeting-card-publishers/freelance-art-acquisition-and-rights/profile|Freelance Art Acquisition & Rights]]
**Industry:** [[industries/greeting-card-publishers|Greeting Card Publishers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** An art director with sixty open pocket briefs and a few thousand artists' portfolios on file matches them from memory, and never learns which artists' work actually sold.
**Tags:** #contrastive-learning #transformers #k-nearest-neighbors #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to bring in enough fresh, on-trend freelance art for each season's pocket briefs, with rights cleared for every format it will appear in, and whoever builds the deepest pipeline of artists who submit to it first wins the line review.

## The Problem
Each season the line plan produces briefs: a watercolour floral for Sympathy — General, a bold hand-lettered design for Birthday — Friend — Humor. The art director sends briefs to artists she remembers or browses agent catalogues. Good artists who submitted once years ago are forgotten, and the publisher has no record linking artists to the sell-through of their designs.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Portfolios arrive as PDFs, links and email attachments, never indexed. Sales history is in a different system from the art archive. Agents keep their own catalogues and want to control the matching.

## What to Build
Index every artist's portfolio and past designs with image embeddings fine-tuned on card art; join each published design to its pocket sell-through; and for each open brief, return the artists whose style fits and whose past work sold in similar pockets, with their usual terms and turnaround. Track response times and acceptance so the publisher can see its own decision speed, a reason artists choose rivals.

## Target Customer
Art directors and creative sourcing managers at mid-size card publishers that rely on freelance art.

## Impact If Built
More briefs answered by proven artists, faster sourcing in the weeks before a line review, and a pipeline that grows with use rather than living in one person's memory.
