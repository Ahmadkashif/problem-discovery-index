# Consumer-Products Licensing Royalty Auditors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/greeting-card-publishers|Greeting Card Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Accounting and licensing-compliance practices that audit licensees' royalty reports for brand and character licensors. Card publishers holding entertainment licences are among the licensees audited: the auditor tests reported sales against shipments and scans, royalty bases against contract definitions (gross or net, treatment of returns, shrink and allowances), minimum guarantees and unreported products, then presents a claim. Licensors pay fees or contingency on recoveries.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 auditors per leading consumer-products licensing practice, across perhaps 10-20 firms (estimate)
**Output:** Royalty audit reports, claims and settlement support
**Proprietary data:** A cross-licensee archive of findings: which licensee categories misreport which terms and by how much
**Clock:** Contractual audit rights and look-back periods (commonly a few years, estimate) and licence renewal dates
**Buyer:** Partner leading the licensing compliance practice
