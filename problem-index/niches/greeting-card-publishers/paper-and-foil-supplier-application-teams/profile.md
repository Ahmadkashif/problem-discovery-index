# Specialty Paper & Foil Suppliers' Application Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/greeting-card-publishers|Greeting Card Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Makers of specialty papers and boards and of hot-stamping foils and special-effect materials sell into card publishers and their printers. Their application and technical service teams test how stocks, foils and coatings behave, publish swatch books and trend palettes, and help production teams solve finishing problems.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 application, colour and technical-service staff per major supplier (estimate)
**Output:** Technical data, application guidance, swatch and trend collections, problem-solving support
**Proprietary data:** Test results on their own materials across presses and stocks
**Clock:** Customers' seasonal print schedules
**Buyer:** Head of Technical Services or Marketing
