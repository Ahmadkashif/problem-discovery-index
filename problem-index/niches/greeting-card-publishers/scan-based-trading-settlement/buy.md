# Deduction Management Rebuilt Around Units, Not Invoices

**Niche:** [[niches/greeting-card-publishers/scan-based-trading-settlement/profile|Scan-Based Trading Settlement & Shrink]]
**Industry:** [[industries/greeting-card-publishers|Greeting Card Publishers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Consumer-goods suppliers already buy software to dispute retailer deductions; scan-based trading turns the dispute from invoice lines into unit flows per store.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #hypothesis-testing #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (deduction-recovery and reconciliation vendors, EDI analytics providers and publishers' own settlement teams) is fighting to pin each store's missing cards under scan-based trading to a cause the retailer will accept in settlement, and whoever recovers the most disputed shrink per store takes the publisher's settlement work.

## The Problem
Publishers settle monthly with each scan-based account and dispute differences by email and spreadsheet.

## What Already Exists
Deduction and claims management tools for consumer-goods suppliers (for example HighRadius) match retailer deductions to invoices, promotions and proofs of delivery and route disputes; EDI providers such as SPS Commerce and TrueCommerce deliver the underlying documents.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: the unit of dispute changed from invoice line to store-and-UPC unit flow; scan reports (EDI 852) and shipment notices joined to merchandiser counts and seasonal return records; the negotiated shrink allowance applied per account; evidence packs in each retailer's settlement format; and seasonal take-back and destroy credits reconciled against what was shipped for that season.

## Target Customer
Retail finance teams at card publishers and other scan-based suppliers (magazines, books, seasonal goods), and the recovery vendors competing to run their settlements.

## Impact If Solved
Settlement becomes a monthly routine with evidence attached rather than a quarterly argument.
