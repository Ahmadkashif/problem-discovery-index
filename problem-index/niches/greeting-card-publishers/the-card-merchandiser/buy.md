# Retail Execution Apps That Recognise Individual Cards

**Niche:** [[niches/greeting-card-publishers/the-card-merchandiser/profile|The Card Department Merchandiser]]
**Industry:** [[industries/greeting-card-publishers|Greeting Card Publishers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Retail execution apps already schedule visits, collect photos and run audits; they cannot tell one birthday card from another.
**Tags:** #cnns #transfer-learning #object-detection #data-integration #worker-facing #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep every pocket full, correctly filed and on season with the fewest paid merchandiser hours per store, and whoever gives the merchandiser the fastest way to see and fix what is wrong with a fixture keeps the service contract.

## The Problem
Publishers and merchandising services need visit scheduling, task lists, photo capture and reporting, and they already buy apps for it. The card-specific checks are done by eye.

## What Already Exists
Retail execution platforms such as Repsly and GoSpotCheck handle routes, visits, forms, photos and reporting for field teams; shelf image recognition vendors add product detection for packaged goods.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: a card design catalogue as the recognition target, refreshed every season; detection of partially visible card faces and pocket tags; planogram matching by pocket rather than by facings; seasonal set and pull tasks generated from the publisher's calendar; reorder and credit drafts sent to the publisher's order system; and visit pay or scoring tied to fixture condition measured from photos.

## Target Customer
Field operations leaders at publishers already using a retail execution app.

## Impact If Solved
The existing app becomes the fixture-checking tool instead of a visit log, without a new rollout to thousands of merchandisers.
