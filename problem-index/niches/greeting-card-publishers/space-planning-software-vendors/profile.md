# Retail Space Planning Software Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/greeting-card-publishers|Greeting Card Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors such as Blue Yonder and NielsenIQ (Spaceman) sell planogram and space planning software to retailers and to suppliers that manage categories for them, including card publishers drawing card department planograms. Their product is software and implementation services, not analysis.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Fewer than 10 analysts per vendor work on space-planning research or benchmarks; most headcount is engineering, implementation and support, which does not count for the gate (estimate)
**Output:** Planogram software, optimisation engines, implementation services
**Proprietary data:** Fixture libraries and clients' planograms and sales held in hosted systems
**Clock:** Clients' reset calendars
**Buyer:** Product leader for space planning
