# One Rights Ledger for Owned, Licensed and Character Art

**Industry:** [[industries/greeting-card-publishers|Greeting Card Publishers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A publisher's art rights sit in three places (purchase records for owned art, artist licences, property licences), and no single record says what any one design may be used for.

## The Problem
A product manager planning a gift bag line wants to reuse twenty card designs. Some were bought outright years ago, some are licensed from freelance artists for cards only, and two carry a character licence that covers cards in North America but not bags. Finding out takes the licensing team days, and the safe answer is often "no", which leaves owned art unused.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Agreements span decades and template versions, many on paper. Licensing platforms are designed for the licensor's side, and contract tools store documents without a design-level model of rights. The card market is too small for vendors to model its rights structure.

## What to Build
A ledger keyed by design, populated by language-model extraction from every agreement and amendment with human review of uncertain fields: ownership or licence, permitted products and formats, territories, term and renewal, exclusivity, royalty basis, and approval obligations for licensed properties. It answers use questions instantly with the clause cited, blocks planning of designs into products or seasons they cannot legally ship in, and lists owned art eligible for reuse.

## Target Customer
Directors of licensing and creative operations at card and social stationery publishers holding thousands of agreements.

## Impact If Built
Owned art gets reused across formats instead of sitting idle, legal exposure from out-of-scope use falls, and the licensing team stops working as a manual lookup service.
