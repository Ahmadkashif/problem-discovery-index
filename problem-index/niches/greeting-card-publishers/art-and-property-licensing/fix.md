## The Problem
Product, licensing and sales teams ask format, territory and term questions about specific designs several times a day. The coordinator locates the agreement (often a scan), finds the clause, checks for amendments in email, and replies. The answer is not recorded, so the same question about the same design is asked again next season.

## Why It's Still Broken
There is no structured rights field per design to update, and the backlog of old agreements is too large to abstract by hand. Each question is small, so nobody funds the fix.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Extract format, territory and term fields from agreements as they are retrieved, design by design, and store each answer with its clause. Within months the most-asked designs are covered and questions answer themselves; the backlog is then processed in batches.

## Who Feels the Pain
Licensing coordinators; product managers waiting on answers; artists whose art is left unused because nobody was sure.

## Impact If Fixed
Hours a week returned to the licensing team, and faster product development on owned art.
