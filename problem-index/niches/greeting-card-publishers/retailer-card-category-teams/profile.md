# Retailer Card & Seasonal Category Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/greeting-card-publishers|Greeting Card Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The buyers and category managers at mass merchants, drug chains and grocers who own the card department (often alongside gift wrap, party and seasonal), award it to a publisher, run line reviews and negotiate supply agreements, incentives and shrink allowances.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 1-5 people per retailer for cards and social expression, relying on the supplying publisher's category team for analysis (estimate)
**Output:** Line-review decisions and supply agreements
**Proprietary data:** The retailer's own scan data for the department
**Clock:** Contract renewals and reset calendars
**Buyer:** Divisional merchandise manager
