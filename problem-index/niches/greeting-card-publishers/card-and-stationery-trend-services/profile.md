# Card & Stationery Trend Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/greeting-card-publishers|Greeting Card Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Small trend consultancies, trade-magazine trend features and independent forecasters that publish colour, motif and lettering trends aimed at card, gift and stationery designers, sold as reports, seminars and trade-show presentations. The large forecasting services cover stationery inside broader lifestyle and interiors products and are scored elsewhere (see the Pass 2 prose in the overview).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 1-8 per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Seasonal trend reports and presentations
**Proprietary data:** Image collections and the forecasters' own archives
**Clock:** Trade-show and design-season calendars
**Buyer:** Founder
