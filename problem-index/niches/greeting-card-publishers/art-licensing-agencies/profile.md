# Art Licensing Agencies & Artist Representatives

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/greeting-card-publishers|Greeting Card Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Agencies that represent illustrators and surface designers, place their art with card, gift wrap and home-goods publishers on flat-fee or royalty terms, exhibit at licensing trade shows such as Surtex, and take a commission on fees and royalties.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 2-15 staff per agency, almost all in sales, artist management and contracts (estimate)
**Output:** Art placements and licence agreements
**Proprietary data:** Artist portfolios and placement histories
**Clock:** Publishers' seasonal brief and line-review calendars
**Buyer:** Agency owner
