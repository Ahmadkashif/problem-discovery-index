# Licensing Industry Association Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/greeting-card-publishers|Greeting Card Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The brand licensing trade association runs the main licensing trade shows and publishes an annual study of global licensed merchandise sales by property type and product category, including stationery and paper goods. Members (licensors, agents, licensees such as card publishers) use it for benchmarks and pitches.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research staff, with fieldwork largely contracted out (estimate)
**Output:** Annual licensing industry study, show content
**Proprietary data:** Survey responses from members
**Clock:** Annual study and show calendar
**Buyer:** Association president
