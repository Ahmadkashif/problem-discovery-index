# Publisher Category Management & Consumer Insight Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/greeting-card-publishers|Greeting Card Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The largest card publishers, Hallmark and American Greetings (majority-owned by Elliott since February 2025, with CD&R and the Weiss family as minority owners), run category management and consumer insight teams. They analyse scan data from the departments they supply, study card buyers and occasions, and build the line-review and renewal cases that win or keep retailers' card departments. Their analysis is given to retailers as part of the service, not sold.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 category managers, analysts and consumer researchers per major publisher (estimate)
**Output:** Department reviews, planogram recommendations, renewal cases, shopper research
**Proprietary data:** Pocket-level scan histories across the departments each publisher supplies, plus its own design archive
**Clock:** Retailers' line reviews, reset dates and contract renewals, which are the client's own schedule rather than a regulatory or court deadline
**Buyer:** Chief Customer Officer or VP of Category Management
