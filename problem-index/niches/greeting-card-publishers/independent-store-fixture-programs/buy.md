# Rep Order Apps That Know Pocket Numbers

**Niche:** [[niches/greeting-card-publishers/independent-store-fixture-programs/profile|Independent Store Fixture Programs]]
**Industry:** [[industries/greeting-card-publishers|Greeting Card Publishers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Wholesale order-writing apps and marketplaces take an order for a product; a card fixture reorder is a set of empty pocket numbers that each map to whatever design currently lives in that pocket in that store.
**Tags:** #cnns #object-detection #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep its own fixture in each independent store stocked with sellers, rotating slow designs out on its exchange terms with the fewest rep visits, and whoever turns pocket counts into automatic replenishment and rotation keeps the fixture when the owner reconsiders the space.

## The Problem
Independent stores reorder cards by walking the fixture and noting empty pockets. The rep or the store owner then writes stock numbers on a form or types them into an app. The design in a pocket changes with every exchange, so the stock number list drifts from what is actually on the fixture.

## What Already Exists
Rep order-writing apps used by gift and stationery rep groups, publisher B2B web portals, and wholesale marketplaces such as Faire, which handle catalogues, minimums, terms and payment for independent retail.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: a per-store fixture map that records which design sits in each pocket after every exchange; reorder by photo or by pocket position rather than by catalogue search; automatic substitution when a design is discontinued; exchange credits netted against reorders; and order history kept at pocket level so the publisher can see sell-through by design and store type. Marketplaces can host the transaction, but the fixture map is the publisher's.

## Target Customer
Specialty sales and customer service teams at card publishers with fixture programs; independent rep groups carrying several card lines.

## Impact If Solved
Reorders become faster and correct for the store, and every reorder becomes usable sell-through data for the publisher.
