# Independent Store Fixture Programs

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/greeting-card-publishers|Greeting Card Publishers]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep its own fixture in each independent store stocked with sellers, rotating slow designs out on its exchange terms with the fewest rep visits, and whoever turns pocket counts into automatic replenishment and rotation keeps the fixture when the owner reconsiders the space.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M a year of US publisher revenue through independent card, gift, book, garden, hospital and museum shops (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of publisher revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — reorders run on rep visits, paper reorder forms keyed to pocket stock numbers, phone and email, with wholesale marketplaces taking a growing share of first orders
**Target Buyer:** VP of Sales, Specialty Channel, at a mid-size card publisher
**Automation Potential:** High for replenishment and rotation; the store relationship stays with the rep

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Outside the mass channel, mid-size and small publishers (humour, art, letterpress, faith and regional lines) place their own spinner or wall fixture in an independent store and keep it filled through independent rep groups or their own reps. Each pocket carries a stock number; the owner or rep counts empty pockets and reorders by number. Publishers often offer exchange privileges, swapping slow designs for new ones, to keep the fixture productive. The store owner judges the fixture by how it sells per square foot against every other vendor competing for that wall. This niche covers only the publisher's own fixture program; the store's reordering across all its vendors belongs to [[niches/independent-retailers/vendor-reorder-automation/profile|Vendor Reorder Automation]] and the brand-side sell-through gap to [[niches/retail-pos-platforms/wholesale-brand-sell-through/profile|Wholesale Brand Sell-Through]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Rep order-writing apps, Faire and other wholesale marketplaces, publisher web portals, and paper reorder forms. The gaps: no pocket-level sell-through for the publisher; exchange decisions made by a rep's memory; and no way to tell which designs sell in which kinds of stores.
