# Card & Gift Sales Rep Groups

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/greeting-card-publishers|Greeting Card Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Independent sales agencies that carry several card, stationery and gift lines into independent stores in a territory, write orders on visits and at regional gift markets, service fixtures and earn commission on what they sell.

## Insight Function
**Size:** None dedicated; agencies of a few to a few dozen reps, with no analysts (estimate)
**Output:** Orders and account service
**Proprietary data:** Account relationships and order histories across the lines they carry
**Clock:** Gift market and seasonal ordering calendars
**Buyer:** Agency principal
