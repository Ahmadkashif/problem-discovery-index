# One Spanish-Language Block for Every Store in a Tier

**Niche:** [[niches/greeting-card-publishers/multicultural-faith-and-language-lines/profile|Multicultural, Faith & Spanish-Language Lines]]
**Industry:** [[industries/greeting-card-publishers|Greeting Card Publishers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Stores in the same tier get the same culturally specific block, whatever mix of occasions their shoppers actually buy for.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #hypothesis-testing #revenue-impact #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to match each store's card assortment to the languages, faiths and communities of the shoppers it actually serves, and whoever places the right Spanish-language, faith and culturally specific designs in the right stores sells them through instead of taking them back.

## The Problem
A Spanish-language block typically has a fixed set of pockets (birthday, Mother's Day, sympathy, religious occasions) repeated in every store of a tier. In one store the sympathy pockets empty weekly while birthday cards sit; in another the reverse.

## Why It's Still Broken
Small lines are planned as a unit, and pocket-level differences inside them are too small to show in department reports.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Within each line's existing pocket count, re-weight occasions per store cluster from the line's own sales, keeping the block's size fixed so no retailer negotiation is needed. Review at each reset.

## Who Feels the Pain
Shoppers who find the occasion they need missing; line managers whose small lines look weak on averages.

## Impact If Fixed
A quick gain in sell-through without asking the retailer for more space.
