# Packaging Prepress Automation Adapted to Card Finishes

**Niche:** [[niches/greeting-card-publishers/embellishment-prepress/profile|Embellishment & Production Art Prepress]]
**Industry:** [[industries/greeting-card-publishers|Greeting Card Publishers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Packaging prepress suites automate trapping, step-and-repeat and preflight; card finishes need effect separation and variant generation they were never designed for.
**Tags:** #semantic-segmentation #transfer-learning #workflow-orchestration #automation #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (outsourced prepress houses, specialty printers' prepress departments and publishers' in-house production art teams) is fighting to turn approved card designs into foil, emboss, die-cut, glitter and spot-gloss separations that pass the printer's proof first time, and whoever delivers the most designs right first time inside the seasonal window takes the publisher's production art work.

## The Problem
Card production teams use the same prepress tools as packaging printers and get the generic benefits (preflight, trapping) without help on the effects that make their products distinctive.

## What Already Exists
Esko's packaging prepress and workflow tools automate preflight, trapping, step-and-repeat and approval routing; Adobe Illustrator scripting and actions handle repeatable steps.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: effect layers (foil, emboss, glitter, spot gloss, flocking) as first-class objects with stock-specific rules; automatic variant generation from a master card to other sizes, boxed packs and matching gift bags, carrying effects across; die-line libraries for standard card formats with fold and safe-zone checks; and a link to the printer's proof rejection codes so rules tighten over time.

## Target Customer
Prepress managers at card publishers and outsourced prepress houses already running an Esko or Adobe-scripted workflow.

## Impact If Solved
The existing prepress investment covers the card-specific half of the work, with less hand-building per variant.
