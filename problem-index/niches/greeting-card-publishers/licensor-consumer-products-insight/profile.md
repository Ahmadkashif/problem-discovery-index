# Entertainment Licensors' Consumer Products Insight

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/greeting-card-publishers|Greeting Card Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Film studios, toy companies and character owners run consumer products divisions that license properties to card, gift wrap and party publishers among many other categories. Their insight teams track property popularity, plan which categories and retailers to license into, and read licensee royalty reports to steer the programme. The insight supports the royalty stream and is not sold.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 consumer insight, category and royalty analytics staff at the largest licensors (estimate)
**Output:** Property and category plans, style guide and trend direction for licensees, internal sales analysis
**Proprietary data:** Royalty and sales reports from every licensee across categories, plus audience data on the property
**Clock:** Mostly self-set: the licensor's own release slates and licensing-show calendar, plus licensee reporting periods it writes into its own contracts
**Buyer:** SVP of Consumer Products Insights
