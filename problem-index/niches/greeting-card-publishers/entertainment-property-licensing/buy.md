**Industry:** [[industries/greeting-card-publishers|Greeting Card Publishers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Licensors' approval portals track submissions; the licensee still learns about off-model characters and banned phrases only when a design comes back rejected.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #cnns #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to bid minimum guarantees on the film, TV, game and character properties that will still be selling when the cards reach the shelf a year later, and to get licensor approvals through inside the print window, and whoever picks and clears properties best takes the licensed pockets.

## The Problem
Each licensed design goes through the licensor's approval stages, and each rejection costs days or weeks. Many rejections are predictable: a character drawn off-model, a colour outside the palette, a logo too small, a caption the licensor's guidelines forbid.

## What Already Exists
Licensors run approval workflows on licensing platforms such as Flowhaven, with style guides, asset libraries and comment threads.

## The Customization Gap
The licensee side needs: a pre-check that compares each design against the style guide and the licensor's past rejection comments (character proportions, palette, logo rules, caption rules); submission packs built to each licensor's format; and stage tracking linked to the publisher's print schedule so designs at risk are escalated. The platforms hold the comments history the pre-check would learn from, but the licensee rarely has a structured copy.

## Target Customer
Licensing coordinators at card publishers holding several entertainment licences.

## Impact If Solved
Fewer rejection rounds and fewer licensed designs dropped for missing the print slot.
