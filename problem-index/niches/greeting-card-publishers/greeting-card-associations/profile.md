# Greeting Card Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/greeting-card-publishers|Greeting Card Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Greeting Card Association in the US and its UK counterpart represent publishers, run awards, publish guidance for new companies and artists (including typical fee and royalty ranges), and issue consumer statistics such as the long-cited ~6.5 billion US cards a year. The US association has also represented card senders' interests on postage rates.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 staff, with statistics drawn from members and commissioned surveys (estimate)
**Output:** Industry statistics, guidance, awards, advocacy
**Proprietary data:** Member-reported figures, not refreshed regularly in public
**Clock:** Annual meetings, awards and occasional postal rate proceedings
**Buyer:** Executive director
