## Why These Niches

Agriculture instrumented itself thoroughly and learned surprisingly little. Every pass across a field is recorded at sub-metre resolution and the question underneath the whole industry — did this input actually change the outcome on this ground, or did the weather — is answered by anecdote and by a retailer's trial plot. The platforms hold what is in principle the largest agricultural experiment ever run and use it to draw maps. That makes on-farm causal measurement the contested capability of the largest block.

Digital agronomy is the other large block and **failed the filter as one niche**. A retail agronomist writes prescriptions for inputs their employer sells, which makes their contested problem the credibility of a recommendation with a structural conflict attached. An independent consultant sells time and judgement, which makes their contested problem how many acres one person can cover well. Different economics, different products, different customers. Decomposed below.

Specialty crops and grain marketing are the two underdigitised areas, each for a specific reason: permanent crops need block- and tree-level management that row-crop software does not model, and grain marketing tools were built for merchandisers rather than for the growers on the other side of the trade. Small diversified farms and farm labour are the two underserved constituencies — every product in the category assumes scale and a single commodity, and the workforce that performs the most labour-intensive agriculture in the country is recorded by systems built for the employer.

The automation niches are the industry's two long-running data burdens: machine data from different equipment brands that will not reconcile, and a growing pile of sustainability programmes each wanting different evidence in a different format.

## Filter Notes

**Niche 2 failed the filter as stated and was decomposed.** "Digital agronomy" describes two businesses with opposite economics. Retail agronomy is free advice attached to input sales, so the contested capability is making a recommendation credible when the adviser's employer profits from the product — which is a measurement and transparency problem before it is a modelling one. Independent consulting is billed by the acre, so the contested capability is how many acres one consultant can cover at quality — a scouting, reporting and prioritisation problem. Both sub-niches are terminal and are written in full.

**Niches 1, 3–8 are terminal as stated.** Each names something testable: an estimated input effect with an interval on the grower's own ground, block-level management for permanent crops, a grower's own position and basis exposure across contracts, recordkeeping for a farm with twenty crops and no agronomist, provable pay and hours for the worker as well as the regulator, a coherent field record across equipment brands, and one evidence set satisfying multiple programmes.
