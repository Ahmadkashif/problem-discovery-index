# Variety Performance That Lives in the Manager's Memory

**Niche:** [[niches/agtech-platforms/specialty-permanent-crops/profile|Specialty & Permanent Crops]]
**Industry:** [[industries/agtech-platforms|Agtech Platforms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Which variety on which rootstock performs on which part of this property is the most valuable knowledge a permanent crop operation generates, it takes decades to accumulate, and it is held in the head of whoever has been there longest.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #survival-analysis #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #hypothesis-testing #tacit-knowledge-ml #worker-facing #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in specialty crop software is fighting to manage a permanent planting at the block and the tree rather than at the field — and whoever makes per-block economics and harvest labour legible takes the operation.

## The Problem
A block planted in 2003 to a particular variety on a particular rootstock has performed in a specific way on that soil, in that frost pocket, with that irrigation. The manager who planted it knows how it established, which years it suffered, how it responded to a hard prune in 2014 and why the northern rows always lagged. None of it is written down. When that manager retires, the operation's accumulated understanding of its own ground goes with them, and the next replant decision is made without thirty years of relevant observation that the operation itself produced.

## Why It's Still Broken
The knowledge is long-horizon and tacit, accumulated as experience rather than as records, and recording it requires someone to write things down every year about blocks that mostly behave normally. Nothing forces it — there is no annual return that requires a block history — and the payoff is decades away, which is the classic structure of things that do not get done. The records that do exist, such as they are, are yield and spray records kept for compliance rather than for learning.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Make the block's history a standing record that accumulates as a by-product of normal work. Planting details — variety, rootstock, spacing, date, nursery source — recorded once at establishment, which almost no operation does systematically and which is the foundation for everything. Annual yield, quality grade-out and harvest labour per block, which the operation already generates and does not attribute. Significant events — frost, hail, a hard prune, a replant of failed trees, an irrigation change — recorded in a sentence when they happen, which is a minute a few times a year. Observations during the season attached to the block rather than to a general note. Over ten years that is an unmatched record of the operation's own ground; across a platform's customer base it is the variety and rootstock performance evidence that nurseries and extension services currently produce from small trials. And the succession value is immediate rather than deferred: a new manager arriving at an operation with a block history starts years ahead of one who starts with a map.

## Who Feels the Pain
Operations whose institutional knowledge is one person; new managers rebuilding thirty years of observation; and the replant decisions made without the evidence the property itself produced.

## Impact If Fixed
This is the cheapest and longest-horizon fix in the industry: a few minutes a year per block, producing a record whose value compounds for decades and which currently ceases to exist when a person leaves. The cross-operation aggregation is the additional prize, since variety performance by soil and climate is exactly the question every permanent crop grower asks and no current source answers well.
