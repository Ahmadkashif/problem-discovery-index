# Medicare Physician Payment & MIPS Measure Contractors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/physician-practices|Physician Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
CMS contracts research firms to build and maintain the analytics behind physician payment policy. They develop and field-test MIPS cost and quality measures, study practice-expense and valuation questions for the fee schedule, and run scoring and feedback for the Quality Payment Program. Acumen, RAND and Mathematica are representative (named from recall). The hospital-side measure contractors are logged as [[niches/general-hospitals/cms-quality-measure-contractors/profile|🔍 CMS Hospital Quality Measure Contractors]] (36).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~30-150 economists, statisticians and clinical analysts per contractor's CMS physician-payment portfolio (estimate)
**Output:** Measure specifications, field-test reports, cost-measure scoring, technical reports cited in rulemaking
**Proprietary data:** None owned. They work on Medicare claims and QPP submissions supplied by CMS
**Clock:** The annual fee schedule rulemaking calendar (proposed rule in summer, final rule by November) and contract deliverable dates
**Buyer:** Project director at the contractor; CMS is the client

Scored consistently with the hospital measure contractors: the analysis is what CMS pays for, and CMS is the only buyer. Q4 is 4, not 5, because the rulemaking calendar and contract dates are set by CMS, the client itself (the same change is being made to the hospital pocket).
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. Hard federal clocks, but the corpus belongs to CMS and the market is one agency.
