# Chronic & Advanced Primary Care Management Programs

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/physician-practices|Physician Practices]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep enrolled Medicare patients in monthly care management the practice can bill, and whoever keeps the most patients billable every month with documentation that survives audit takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.5B a year of care-management staff, outsourced care-management vendors and remote monitoring programmes billed by physician practices (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.25% of office revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — the billing codes are electronic, but the work is monthly phone calls, consent forms and minutes logged by hand
**Target Buyer:** Practice owner or director of care management
**Automation Potential:** Medium — tracking, outreach scheduling and documentation automate; the monthly human contact is what Medicare pays for

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Medicare pays primary care practices monthly for managing patients between visits. Chronic Care Management (99490 and its add-ons) needs 20 minutes of clinical staff time a month. Principal Care Management covers a single complex condition, and remote patient monitoring covers device readings. Since 2025 the Advanced Primary Care Management codes (G0556–G0558, created in the CY2025 fee schedule per CMS-1832-F) pay a monthly amount tiered by condition count and QMB status, with no time threshold (tier details from ACP and AAFP summaries, search summary). The work is low-tech: a nurse or MA calls each enrolled patient, updates the care plan and logs the minutes. Practices run it themselves or hand it to vendors who take a share.

## Current Tools & Gaps
EHR care-management modules, outsourced vendors (ThoroughCare, CareHarmony and others), RPM device companies, spreadsheets of minutes. Gaps: engagement decays silently until a month goes unbilled; APCM's capability requirements are tracked by hand; time logs are reconstructed for audits.
