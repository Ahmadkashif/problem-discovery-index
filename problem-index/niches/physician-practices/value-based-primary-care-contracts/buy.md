# Population Health Platforms Tuned to MSSP Benchmark Rules

**Niche:** [[niches/physician-practices/value-based-primary-care-contracts/profile|Primary Care in Value-Based Contracts (ACOs & MA Risk)]]
**Industry:** [[industries/physician-practices|Physician Practices]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Population health platforms show cost and gaps; a physician-led ACO needs them computed the way CMS will settle the year, with benchmark, risk-cap and regional adjustments applied.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #linear-regression #data-integration #compliance #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep an attributed population's cost under its benchmark, with quality and risk scores complete before the performance year closes, and whoever books the most shared savings per attributed patient with no benchmark or RADV clawback takes the account.

## The Problem
A dashboard says cost per beneficiary is down 3%. CMS's reconciliation applies regional trend, risk-score growth caps, the quality score and the track's sharing rate, and the ACO learns a year later whether that 3% was savings.

## What Already Exists
Innovaccer, Arcadia, Lightbeam, Navina and EHR population health modules ingest CMS claim feeds and produce cost, utilisation and gap reports. Enablers such as Aledade and Pearl run their own analytics.

## The Customization Gap
The ACO-specific layer needs:

- a running estimate of the year's settlement under its own track (BASIC level, ENHANCED) and agreement period;
- risk-score growth caps and regional adjustments applied as CMS applies them;
- quality reporting through the APM Performance Pathway, with the measures the ACO is actually scored on;
- explanation of each month's movement down to patients and practices.

## Target Customer
Physician-led ACOs without a large actuarial team; 296 ACOs were in the ENHANCED track in 2026 (CMS Fast Facts).

## Impact If Solved
A settlement-faithful estimate lets the ACO change course in the second half of the year rather than learn the result after it. A ~1% swing in cost against benchmark is worth ~$1M a year for a 10,000-beneficiary ACO (estimate).
