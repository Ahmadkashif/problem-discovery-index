# Primary Care in Value-Based Contracts (ACOs & MA Risk)

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/physician-practices|Physician Practices]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep an attributed population's cost under its benchmark, with quality and risk scores complete before the performance year closes, and whoever books the most shared savings per attributed patient with no benchmark or RADV clawback takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3B a year of enablement fees, population health software and care-team staff for practices in ACO and MA risk contracts, excluding the capitation itself (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.5% of office revenue, with far more revenue at stake in the contracts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — claims feeds and population health dashboards exist, but gap closure still runs on printed lists and phone outreach
**Target Buyer:** ACO executive director or medical group CMO
**Automation Potential:** Medium-High — attribution, gap lists and risk suspects are computable; changing what happens in the visit is not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The Shared Savings Program has 511 ACOs and 12.6M assigned beneficiaries in 2026, and 76% of ACOs sit in two-sided tracks (CMS Fast Facts). Medicare Advantage risk contracts add capitation for many primary care groups. The practice earns its share of savings only if three things hold: the patients attributed to it stay attributed, their cost stays under the benchmark, and their quality and risk scores are complete and defensible. All three are settled by a calendar year that closes on 31 December. The work belongs to the practice and its enabler, not the plan.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Enablers: Aledade, Privia, agilon, Pearl Health, Astrana. Software: Innovaccer, Arcadia, Navina, Lightbeam, EHR population health modules. Gaps: attribution churn surprises the group after the year starts; gap lists from the ACO, each plan and the EHR disagree; risk suspects arrive without chart evidence.
