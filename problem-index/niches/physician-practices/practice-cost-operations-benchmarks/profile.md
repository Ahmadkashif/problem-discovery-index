# Medical Practice Cost & Operations Benchmarks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/physician-practices|Physician Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Medical group associations survey member practices on cost, revenue, staffing, collections and productivity, and sell the benchmarks back as subscriptions and reports. MGMA's DataDive cost, revenue and practice operations modules and AMGA's operating benchmarks are representative (named from recall). Practice administrators, consultants, lenders and acquirers use them. The physician compensation half of the same surveys is logged as [[niches/hr-consultants/compensation-survey-publishers/profile|🔍 Compensation Survey Publishers]] (54) and is not re-scored here.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-60 survey researchers, analysts and data staff per association (estimate)
**Output:** Annual cost and revenue survey reports, benchmark subscriptions, custom cuts for consultants and acquirers
**Proprietary data:** Member-contributed practice financial and staffing data across thousands of groups over decades; no patient data
**Clock:** The associations set their own annual survey and release cycles
**Buyer:** Vice President of data solutions at the association
