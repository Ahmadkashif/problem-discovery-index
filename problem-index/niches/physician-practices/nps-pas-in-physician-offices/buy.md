# Credentialing Platforms Extended to State Collaboration Rules

**Niche:** [[niches/physician-practices/nps-pas-in-physician-offices/profile|Nurse Practitioners & Physician Assistants in Physician Offices]]
**Industry:** [[industries/physician-practices|Physician Practices]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Credentialing platforms track licences and payer enrolments; groups with NPs and PAs across several states also need each collaboration agreement's terms tracked and enforced.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to bill each NP and PA visit under the right rule (incident-to under the physician or the clinician's own NPI), with the state's supervision or collaboration terms met, and whoever captures the most compliant revenue per advanced practice clinician takes the account.

## The Problem
A multi-state group may employ NPs in a full-practice-authority state and in states that require a collaborating physician, a set chart-review percentage, quarterly meetings or limits on how many NPs one physician may collaborate with. PAs have their own supervision or collaboration rules. These terms live in signed PDFs and a spreadsheet.

## What Already Exists
Credentialing and enrolment platforms (CAQH, Medallion, CertifyOS, symplr) track licences, DEA registrations and payer enrolment. Credentialing as an operation is profiled in [[niches/behavioral-health-clinics/credentialing-operations/profile|⚡ Credentialing & Payer Enrollment Operations]].

## The Customization Gap
The advanced-practice layer needs:

- extraction of each collaboration or supervision agreement's obligations into tracked tasks;
- each state's ratio limits checked when a physician leaves or an NP moves clinic;
- chart co-signature sampling generated and tracked at the rate the state or agreement requires;
- prescribing limits for controlled substances by state and clinician type.

## Target Customer
Medical groups and MSOs with NPs and PAs in three or more states.

## Impact If Solved
Avoiding a lapse in a required collaboration agreement prevents billing that a board or payer could treat as unlicensed practice. Compliance staff time per clinician could fall by ~50% (estimate).
