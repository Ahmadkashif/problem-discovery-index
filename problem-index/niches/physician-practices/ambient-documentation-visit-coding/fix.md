# Level Creep Nobody Is Watching After Ambient Go-Live

**Niche:** [[niches/physician-practices/ambient-documentation-visit-coding/profile|Ambient Documentation & Office Visit Coding]]
**Industry:** [[industries/physician-practices|Physician Practices]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** After an ambient scribe goes live, a group's E/M distribution and HCC capture shift within weeks, and no one owns the question of whether the shift is documentation catching up or exposure building.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #change-point-detection #hypothesis-testing #compliance #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn the office visit into a signed note that supports the billed E/M level and risk-adjustment codes before the physician leaves for the day, and whoever closes the most notes the same day with no downcoding on payer or RADV audit takes the account.

## The Problem
The ambient pilot is sold on minutes saved and burnout. Revenue cycle notices the higher levels as good news. Compliance notices only when a payer flags the group as an outlier against specialty peers, or when an MA plan's RADV review finds unsupported diagnoses.

## Why It's Still Broken
Ownership is split. IT and the CMIO run the ambient rollout, finance books the revenue, and compliance audits a ten-chart sample per physician once a year. Nobody compares each physician's coding distribution before and after go-live, against peers, with the transcripts available to explain the change.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A monitoring report that detects change points in each physician's E/M mix, G2211 use and HCC capture rate after go-live. It compares them with specialty benchmarks and samples the notes behind each shift for coder review. The answer for each physician is "supported", "needs coaching" or "stop and audit", reviewed monthly in the first six months.

## Who Feels the Pain
Compliance officers who sign off on the programme; physicians who face a takeback months later; revenue cycle leaders who booked revenue that may be reversed.

## Impact If Fixed
Catching unsupported drift in the first quarter rather than at the first audit could avoid extrapolated takebacks. On a typical audit sample these run to ~$50,000–$500,000 per group (estimate). Supported gains are kept with documentation ready.
