# Vaccines Paid Below What They Cost

**Niche:** [[niches/physician-practices/independent-pediatric-practices/profile|Independent Pediatric Practices]]
**Industry:** [[industries/physician-practices|Physician Practices]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Commercial payers reimburse some vaccines below the practice's acquisition cost plus handling, and practices find out only by comparing invoices with remittances by hand.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #data-integration #linear-regression #revenue-impact #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a pediatric practice's vaccine stock reconciled across VFC and private inventory, with every dose billed under the right administration codes, and whoever leaves the fewest doses wasted, borrowed or unbilled takes the account.

## The Problem
A practice pays the manufacturer or distributor for privately bought vaccines months before it is paid. Each payer reimburses the product code at its own rate, and some rates sit below cost once storage, wastage and the capital tied up in the fridge are counted. Administration codes are often underpaid or bundled.

## Why It's Still Broken
Purchase invoices sit in accounting, remittances in the billing system, and fee schedules in contracts. Pediatric practices are too small to have a contract analyst, and payers update vaccine rates on their own schedules.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A per-vaccine, per-payer margin report built from purchase invoices and 835 remittances. It flags product codes paid below cost and administration codes underpaid against contract, and produces the evidence for a rate request or for the practice's purchasing group.

## Who Feels the Pain
Pediatric practice owners; administrators negotiating payer contracts; the families whose access shrinks when practices stop stocking unprofitable vaccines.

## Impact If Fixed
Identifying below-cost vaccine and administration rates gives the practice a documented case for rate increases. A ~5% margin gain on private vaccine revenue is worth tens of thousands of dollars a year to a mid-sized practice (estimate).
