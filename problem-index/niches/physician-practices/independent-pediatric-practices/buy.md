# Inventory Systems Adapted to VFC Eligibility and Borrowing Rules

**Niche:** [[niches/physician-practices/independent-pediatric-practices/profile|Independent Pediatric Practices]]
**Industry:** [[industries/physician-practices|Physician Practices]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** General medical inventory tools track stock and expiry; a pediatric practice needs them to pick the right fridge for each child, at the moment of administration, by VFC eligibility.
**Tags:** #data-integration #decision-trees #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a pediatric practice's vaccine stock reconciled across VFC and private inventory, with every dose billed under the right administration codes, and whoever leaves the fewest doses wasted, borrowed or unbilled takes the account.

## The Problem
The MA drawing up a vaccine must take it from the VFC or private stock according to the child's eligibility that day, screened at every visit. Coverage changes month to month for Medicaid families. The wrong pick creates a borrowing event to document and repay.

## What Already Exists
Medical supply inventory systems, barcode scanning, smart fridges with temperature monitoring, and pediatric-focused inventory services such as VaxCare. EHR immunisation modules record the lot.

## The Customization Gap
The pediatric layer needs:

- an eligibility screen at check-in that sets the dose source for the visit;
- a barcode scan at the fridge that blocks the wrong-inventory pick;
- automatic borrowing logs and replacement tracking in the state's format;
- ordering suggestions for each inventory, timed to the state programme's ordering cycle.

## Target Customer
Pediatric practices enrolled in VFC with mixed Medicaid and commercial panels.

## Impact If Solved
Cutting wrong-inventory picks and paperwork could save the vaccine coordinator ~5 hours a week (estimate) and protect the practice's VFC enrolment through state site visits.
