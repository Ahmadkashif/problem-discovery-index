# Shared Savings ACO Enablement Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/physician-practices|Physician Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Enablement companies form and run Medicare Shared Savings Program ACOs for independent primary care practices. They supply the analytics, care-management staff and capital, and take a share of the savings. Aledade, Pearl Health and Caravan Health (now part of Signify Health and CVS) are representative (named from recall). Their analytics teams build attribution, cost and quality forecasts, care-gap lists and risk suspects for each participating practice. The MA risk-bearing version is logged as [[niches/health-insurance-carriers/ma-risk-bearing-primary-care-platforms/profile|🔍 Medicare Advantage Risk-Bearing Primary Care Platforms]] (35).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~30-300 analysts, actuaries and data scientists per enabler (estimate)
**Output:** Attribution and benchmark forecasts, practice performance reports, care-gap and high-risk lists; paid for through the savings share, not as insight
**Proprietary data:** CMS claim feeds for assigned beneficiaries, practice EHR extracts and outcomes across many ACOs
**Clock:** CMS performance year, quality reporting window and annual reconciliation
**Buyer:** Chief analytics officer at the enabler

**Kill switches:** **HIPAA**, and **data isn't theirs**. CMS supplies the claim feeds under a data use agreement limited to ACO operations (recalled), and practice records belong to the participating practices.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. Analytics drives the savings, but it is never invoiced, and the claims belong to CMS. Aledade was hiring AI platform and data engineers on 24 October 2026, which makes it a placement lead even though it fails the rubric.
