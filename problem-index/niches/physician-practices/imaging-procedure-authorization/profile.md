# Imaging & Procedure Authorization

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/physician-practices|Physician Practices]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get each ordered scan or procedure authorized before its scheduled date without a peer-to-peer, and whoever clears the most orders on first submission, or proves none was needed, takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3B a year of practice staff, outsourced authorization services and automation for advanced imaging, cardiology testing, sleep studies, surgery and other procedures (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.5% of office revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — radiology benefit manager portals are electronic, but their question trees are answered by staff reading the chart
**Target Buyer:** Director of revenue cycle or authorization manager at a cardiology, orthopaedic, neurology, gastroenterology or oncology group
**Automation Potential:** Very High — criteria are published, and most requests a reviewer would approve match them on the facts in the chart

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Medical-service requests are decided against written criteria: the payer's medical policy or a radiology benefit manager's guideline (EviCore, Carelon). The decision turns on whether the chart shows the facts the criteria ask for, such as six weeks of conservative therapy, a specific exam finding or a prior test result. A request missing one fact is pended, denied or sent to a peer-to-peer call that takes a physician out of clinic. The contest is first-pass approval: assemble the facts so the reviewer, increasingly an algorithm, approves on sight. Medicare Advantage denied 7.7% of 52.8M requests in 2024 and 80.7% of appealed denials were overturned (KFF), which shows how many were winnable from the start. Obtaining the authorization is usually the ordering practice's job, and it stays here. When the scan or procedure is done at a hospital, the hospital's scheduling-side check belongs to [[niches/general-hospitals/patient-access-auth-estimates/profile|⚡ Patient Access: Prior Authorization, Eligibility & Estimates]]. A freestanding imaging centre's check belongs to Diagnostic Imaging Centers, which is still in the build queue.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Payer and RBM portals, Availity, EHR authorization worklists, automation from Commure (Athelas), Cair Health and SuperDial, and outsourced authorization teams. Gaps: criteria not parsed into checklists; evidence not cited from the chart; "no auth required" answers given from memory. Physical therapy's version, with visit-count reviews, is [[niches/physical-therapy/prior-authorization/profile|⚡ Prior Authorization & Concurrent Review]].
