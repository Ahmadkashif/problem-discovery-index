# Independent Physician Associations & Clinically Integrated Networks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/physician-practices|Physician Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Independent physician associations and clinically integrated networks let independent practices contract jointly with payers and share quality programmes without merging. Most are regional and physician-governed, and pay a management company or enabler for analytics.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically under 10 analysts per network (estimate); the large ones buy analytics from enablers
**Output:** Network quality reports, payer contract performance summaries, member practice dashboards
**Proprietary data:** Members' contract and quality data, held under participation agreements
**Clock:** Payer contract and quality reporting cycles
**Buyer:** Network executive director
