# Drug Coverage Policy & Prior-Authorization Criteria Databases

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/physician-practices|Physician Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
These firms collect every payer's formulary and medical-benefit drug policies, including prior authorization criteria, step-therapy rules and site-of-care restrictions. They structure them into searchable databases with history. MMIT, Policy Reporter and Clarivate's formulary products are representative (named from recall). Pharmaceutical market-access and field reimbursement teams pay for them to track access to their drugs. Some products are also sold to hubs and providers. MMIT's health-plan market data arm is logged separately as [[niches/health-insurance-carriers/health-plan-market-data-publishers/profile|🔍 Health Plan Market, Enrollment & Financial Data Publishers]] (37).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~30-150 policy analysts, clinical pharmacists and data scientists per vendor, excluding document collectors (estimate)
**Output:** Coverage and criteria databases, access scorecards by drug and payer, policy-change alerts, market-access analytics
**Proprietary data:** Years of structured policy versions with effective dates. The policies themselves are public, but the clean history is costly to rebuild
**Clock:** Payers' policy effective dates and P&T cycles; manufacturers' launch and contracting dates are the clients' own
**Buyer:** Head of data products or chief analytics officer at the vendor
