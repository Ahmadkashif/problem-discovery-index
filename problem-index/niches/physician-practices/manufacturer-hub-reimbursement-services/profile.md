# Manufacturer Hub & Field Reimbursement Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/physician-practices|Physician Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Drug makers pay hub operators to run patient-access services for their specialty drugs: benefits investigation, prior authorization support, copay programme enrolment and appeals help for the prescribing practice. Lash Group (Cencora), CareMetx and AssistRx are representative (named from recall). Their analytics teams report access trends, time to therapy and payer behaviour back to the manufacturer's market-access team. The practices' side of this work is [[niches/physician-practices/medical-benefit-drug-access/profile|🎯 Medical-Benefit Drug Access (Buy-and-Bill & Infusions)]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-100 access analysts and data scientists per hub operator, excluding case managers, who are production (estimate)
**Output:** Programme performance and payer access reports for manufacturers; per-case benefit verifications for practices
**Proprietary data:** Outcomes of millions of benefit investigations and authorizations across payers and drugs
**Clock:** Payer decision clocks on each case; manufacturers' quarterly business reviews
**Buyer:** Head of analytics for patient services at the hub operator
