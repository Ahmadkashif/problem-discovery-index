# AMA RUC & Specialty Society RVU Surveys

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/physician-practices|Physician Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The AMA/Specialty Society RVS Update Committee (RUC) recommends relative values for new and revised CPT codes to CMS. Specialty societies survey their members on physician time and work for each code. The AMA's staff runs the process, and the societies' health policy staff prepare the presentations (named from recall). CMS accepts or modifies the recommendations in each year's fee schedule rule. The CPT code set itself sits in [[niches/medical-billing/medical-coding-content-publishers/profile|🔍 Medical Coding Content Publishers]] (54).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-25 AMA staff, plus a few health policy staff at each specialty society (estimate)
**Output:** RVU recommendations, survey summaries and practice-expense inputs submitted to CMS
**Proprietary data:** Decades of code-level survey responses and committee rationales
**Clock:** CMS's annual rulemaking calendar; the committee meets several times a year to feed the next proposed rule (recalled)
**Buyer:** AMA director of physician payment policy
