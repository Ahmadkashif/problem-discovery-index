# Prior Authorization at the Ordering Practice

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/physician-practices|Physician Practices]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every competitor here is fighting to get the ordered service approved before it is due. But scans and procedures are won by first-pass approval on payer and radiology benefit manager criteria, while infused and injected drugs are won by benefits investigation, step-therapy evidence and funding before the first dose. Staff, vendors and win conditions differ. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$5B a year of practice staff time, outsourced authorization services and automation spent on prior authorization for medical services and medical-benefit drugs (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.8% of office revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — portals and ePA exist, but the evidence is still assembled by people reading charts and policies
**Target Buyer:** Practice administrator or director of revenue cycle at a specialty group or MSO
**Automation Potential:** Very High — requirement checks, evidence matching and status tracking are mechanical, and federal API mandates arrive in 2027

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The ordering physician's office does the work that payers' utilisation management decides on. Practices report 39 requests per physician per week at ~13 hours (AMA, 2024). CMS-0057-F decision clocks of 72 hours and seven days apply from 2026, payer APIs from January 2027, and the MIPS electronic prior authorization attestation from the 2027 performance period. The payer side is [[niches/health-insurance-carriers/outpatient-prior-authorization/profile|🎯 Outpatient Prior Authorization & Gold-Carding]]. Hospitals' scheduled services are [[niches/general-hospitals/patient-access-auth-estimates/profile|⚡ Patient Access: Prior Authorization, Eligibility & Estimates]], and pharmacy-benefit drugs are [[niches/pharmacy-independents/prior-authorization-automation/profile|⚡ Prior Authorization Automation]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
CoverMyMeds and Surescripts (pharmacy ePA), payer portals and Availity, EviCore and Carelon portals, EHR authorization worklists, and automation from Commure (Athelas), Cair Health, Infinitus and SuperDial. Gaps: gold-card status not tracked by the practice; authorizations not tied to the scheduled date and units; criteria-to-evidence matching done by hand.
