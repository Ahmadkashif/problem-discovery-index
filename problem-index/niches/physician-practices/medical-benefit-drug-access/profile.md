# Medical-Benefit Drug Access (Buy-and-Bill & Infusions)

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/physician-practices|Physician Practices]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get each infused or injected drug approved, funded and paid before the first dose, and whoever starts the most patients on therapy with no denied or unfunded doses takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2B a year of benefits-investigation, authorization and patient-assistance staff and services at oncology, rheumatology, ophthalmology, gastroenterology, neurology and allergy practices (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.3% of office revenue, on drug revenue several times larger (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — benefits are checked by phone and portal, and copay assistance is applied for form by form
**Target Buyer:** Director of infusion or reimbursement manager at a specialty group
**Automation Potential:** High — benefit checks, step-therapy evidence and foundation applications follow rules, but each payer and manufacturer has its own

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Practices that buy drugs, store them and bill the payer under a J-code carry the drug's cost until they are paid. Before the first dose they must confirm the drug is covered under the medical benefit rather than the pharmacy benefit, and obtain authorization with step-therapy and diagnostic evidence. They must also confirm whether the payer requires a specific site of care or a specialty pharmacy ("white bagging"), find the patient's out-of-pocket cost, and enrol the patient in manufacturer or foundation assistance. A dose given without all of this can mean a five-figure write-off. Drug access is won by benefits investigation and funding, not by the criteria match that wins a scan. Pharmacy-benefit drug authorization is covered at [[niches/pharmacy-independents/prior-authorization-automation/profile|⚡ Prior Authorization Automation]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Manufacturer hub services, specialty-practice reimbursement staff, infusion EHR modules, automation vendors (SamaCare, Mandolin, Infinitus), and copay foundation portals. Gaps: benefits investigation repeated per drug and per reauthorization; assistance enrolment tracked in spreadsheets; authorized units not matched to billed units.
