# Hub-Style Benefits Investigation Brought In-House

**Niche:** [[niches/physician-practices/medical-benefit-drug-access/profile|Medical-Benefit Drug Access (Buy-and-Bill & Infusions)]]
**Industry:** [[industries/physician-practices|Physician Practices]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Manufacturer hubs run benefits investigations well for their own drugs; a practice needs the same capability across every drug it gives, tied to its own schedule and inventory.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get each infused or injected drug approved, funded and paid before the first dose, and whoever starts the most patients on therapy with no denied or unfunded doses takes the account.

## The Problem
Practices use dozens of manufacturer hubs, each with its own forms, portal and turnaround. Staff re-key the same patient into each one and still call the payer to confirm. Reauthorizations come due on different cycles for each drug.

## What Already Exists
Manufacturer hub services (Lash Group, CareMetx, AssistRx and others), voice agents that call payers for benefit verification (Infinitus), eligibility transactions, and infusion-practice software.

## The Customization Gap
The practice layer needs:

- one benefits-investigation record per patient and drug, whichever hub or payer supplied each piece;
- reauthorization dates tied to the infusion schedule, so no dose falls outside an approval;
- buy-and-bill margin checked against the payer's drug rate before the first dose;
- assistance enrolment and renewal tracked across manufacturers and foundations.

## Target Customer
Reimbursement managers at specialty groups with 50 or more infusion or injection patients a day.

## Impact If Solved
Removing duplicate entry across hubs could save each reimbursement specialist ~1–2 hours a day (estimate). Tying reauthorizations to the schedule prevents doses given outside an approval.
