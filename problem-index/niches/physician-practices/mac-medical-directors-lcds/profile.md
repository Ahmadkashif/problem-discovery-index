# MAC Medical Directors & Local Coverage Policy

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/physician-practices|Physician Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Medicare Administrative Contractors process Part B claims for physician practices, and their contractor medical directors write Local Coverage Determinations. These set when a test or procedure is covered in each jurisdiction, after evidence review, public comment and notice periods. Practices code to these policies, and payer criteria often copy them. Medical review, recovery audit and improper payment work by the same contractors is logged as [[niches/medical-billing/cms-program-integrity/profile|🔍 Federal Programme Integrity Contractors]] (49).

## Insight Function
**Size:** A handful of contractor medical directors and policy staff per MAC jurisdiction (estimate)
**Output:** LCDs and billing articles, evidence summaries, responses to comments
**Proprietary data:** Jurisdiction claims and comment records
**Clock:** The LCD process's comment and notice periods
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer; CMS contract
