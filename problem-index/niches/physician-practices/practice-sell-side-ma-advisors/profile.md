# Physician Practice Sell-Side M&A Advisors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/physician-practices|Physician Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation firms

## What They Do
Boutique investment banks and brokers represent physician owners selling to private-equity platforms, hospitals and insurers. They value the practice, normalise earnings, run the auction and negotiate the post-sale compensation model and rollover equity. Provident Healthcare Partners is representative (named from recall). Buy-side earnings testing is logged as [[niches/private-equity-firms/transaction-advisory-qoe-practices/profile|🔍 Transaction Advisory & Quality of Earnings Practices]] (54, kill switch), and fair market value opinions for hospital–physician deals as [[niches/general-hospitals/physician-fmv-valuation/profile|🔍 Physician Compensation & Fair Market Value Advisory]] (46, kill switch).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-40 bankers and analysts per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Valuations, marketing books, buyer analyses, negotiated term sheets
**Proprietary data:** Deal comparables and buyer behaviour seen across their own mandates, built on client financials under NDA
**Clock:** Deal timelines set by the parties; state transaction notice periods apply to some deals
**Buyer:** Managing partner
