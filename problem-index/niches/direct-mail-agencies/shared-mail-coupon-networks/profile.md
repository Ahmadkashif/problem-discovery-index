# Shared Mail & Coupon Clearing Networks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/direct-mail-agencies|Direct Mail Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Shared-mail programmes bundle many advertisers' circulars and coupons into one weekly package delivered to most households in a market; the best known, formerly Valassis's RedPlum and now the Save package, passed to RRD with Vericast's print marketing and coupon clearing businesses in July 2024. Coupon clearing processes manufacturers' coupon redemptions from retailers, which yields redemption data by offer and geography.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 analytics staff across shared mail planning and coupon analytics (estimate)
**Output:** Zone and household targeting for shared-mail inserts, redemption analytics for advertisers
**Proprietary data:** Weekly distribution histories by delivery zone and coupon redemption records — the latter processed on behalf of manufacturers
**Clock:** Weekly in-home dates and retailers' ad calendars, set by the advertisers
**Buyer:** SVP of Analytics at the shared-mail operator
