# Firm-Offer Declines That Waste the Wave

**Niche:** [[niches/direct-mail-agencies/financial-services-prescreen-mail/profile|Financial Services Prescreen & Acquisition Mail]]
**Industry:** [[industries/direct-mail-agencies|Direct Mail Agencies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A prescreened consumer applies and is declined because the criteria used at the bureau weeks earlier no longer match underwriting, and nobody traces the decline back to the selection.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #change-point-detection #evaluation-metrics #compliance #quick-win #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to lower the cost per booked, activated account across prescreen waves — mailing names the issuer's credit policy will approve and that will actually respond — and whoever closes the loop from mailed name to funded account fastest takes the account.

## The Problem
Prescreen criteria are set when the list is pulled; the consumer applies weeks later. In between, their credit file changes and the issuer may tighten underwriting. Responders who are then declined cost postage, application processing and an adverse action notice, and they come away with a poor view of the brand.

## Why It's Still Broken
Selection criteria are owned by marketing and the bureau, underwriting rules by credit risk, and the decline reason codes by operations. Declines on prescreened applications are reported in aggregate, so no one sees which criteria or segments drift from underwriting until the next quarterly review.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Join each prescreened application's decline reason to its selection cell and the criteria used, flag cells whose decline rate is rising, and propose criteria adjustments for the next wave before it is pulled. A weekly report gives marketing and credit risk the same view.

## Who Feels the Pain
Acquisition managers whose cost per account rises without explanation; credit risk teams processing avoidable declines; consumers who were told they were pre-selected.

## Impact If Fixed
Catching criteria drift within a wave rather than a quarter removes avoidable declines from every subsequent wave and puts marketing and credit on one set of numbers.
