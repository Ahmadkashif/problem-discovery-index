# Experimentation Platforms Adapted to Prescreen Waves

**Niche:** [[niches/direct-mail-agencies/financial-services-prescreen-mail/profile|Financial Services Prescreen & Acquisition Mail]]
**Industry:** [[industries/direct-mail-agencies|Direct Mail Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Digital experimentation platforms already run champion-challenger tests at scale; they assume instant outcomes, unconstrained randomisation and no firm-offer rules.
**Tags:** #hypothesis-testing #confidence-intervals #causal-inference #evaluation-metrics #compliance #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to lower the cost per booked, activated account across prescreen waves — mailing names the issuer's credit policy will approve and that will actually respond — and whoever closes the loop from mailed name to funded account fastest takes the account.

## The Problem
Prescreen programmes are among the most tested marketing in the economy — offer, APR, bonus, envelope, model version — but test design and reading live in analysts' spreadsheets, with cells sized by habit and results read before activation data matures.

## What Already Exists
Web and product experimentation platforms (feature-flag and A/B testing suites) with sequential testing, power calculations and results dashboards; marketing-mix tools; and issuers' internal champion-challenger frameworks for credit strategy.

## The Customization Gap
Prescreen testing needs cells defined on bureau criteria and model deciles rather than web sessions; randomisation that respects the firm-offer obligation (every selected consumer must receive a qualifying offer, so "no offer" holdouts are drawn before selection); outcomes that mature over months, read with survival methods rather than conversion rates; cost per piece that varies with postage and format; and a record of each test for compliance and fair-lending review.

## Target Customer
Analytics leads in card and lending acquisition teams, and the agency analysts who design and read their waves.

## Impact If Solved
Properly sized, correctly read tests stop issuers rolling out challengers on immature data, and shorten the time from test to rollout by a wave or two each cycle.
