# Mailer & Postal Policy Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/direct-mail-agencies|Direct Mail Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Trade groups for commercial and nonprofit mailers — the Association for Postal Commerce, the Alliance of Nonprofit Mailers and similar bodies — represent members before the Postal Service and the regulator on prices, service and preparation rules, and brief members on changes. The broader marketing associations that publish response-rate research are logged under SMB marketing agencies as [[niches/marketing-agencies-smb/marketing-association-research/profile|🔍 Marketing Association Research]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically under 10 staff, mostly policy and advocacy
**Output:** Regulatory filings, member briefings, conference programmes
**Proprietary data:** Member surveys and filing archives
**Clock:** Regulatory dockets
**Buyer:** Executive Director
