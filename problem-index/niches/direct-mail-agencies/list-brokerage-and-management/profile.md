# List Brokerage & List Management Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/direct-mail-agencies|Direct Mail Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
List brokers recommend rental, exchange and co-op sources to mailers for each campaign and place the orders; list managers represent list owners, marketing their files and handling rentals and exchanges. Brokers are typically paid a commission on the rental by the list owner, so the mailer receives the recommendation without a separate fee. The digital dataset exchange that resembles this trade is profiled under [[industries/data-marketplace-brokers|Data Marketplace Brokers]]; compiling the lists themselves belongs to the queued directory and list publishers industry.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 brokers and list research staff at the larger firms (estimate)
**Output:** List recommendations and circulation plans per campaign; usage and performance histories
**Proprietary data:** Order and continuation history across many mailers — which lists each mailer re-ordered after testing, the strongest available signal of which lists work
**Clock:** List due dates tied to clients' mail dates; year-end fundraising and political cycles add seasonal pressure
**Buyer:** President / Managing Director of the brokerage
