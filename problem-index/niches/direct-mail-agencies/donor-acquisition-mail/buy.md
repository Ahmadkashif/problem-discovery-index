# Co-op Models Retrained on Second Gifts

**Niche:** [[niches/direct-mail-agencies/donor-acquisition-mail/profile|Donor Acquisition Mail]]
**Industry:** [[industries/direct-mail-agencies|Direct Mail Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Data cooperatives already model which names will give; their models answer "who responds" because that is what members ask them to answer.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #evaluation-metrics #feature-engineering #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to find first-time donors who go on to give a second gift, at an acquisition cost the client can recover from that later giving — and whoever predicts second-gift donors rather than first-gift responders takes the account.

## The Problem
Co-op selections are a staple of nonprofit acquisition. The nonprofit sends its donor file; the co-op builds a model of who resembles those donors across its pooled data and returns ranked names for the next mailing.

## What Already Exists
Cooperative databases such as Wiland and Epsilon Abacus, which pool transaction and giving histories from member organisations and build custom response models per campaign.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: a training target of retained donors (second gift within 12 months) rather than first-gift responders; the nonprofit's own retention history supplied as labels each season; reporting of model deciles on second-gift rate alongside response; and scoring of exchange and rental lists on the same basis so all sources compete on one measure.

## Target Customer
Acquisition managers at nonprofits already buying co-op selections, and the co-ops' nonprofit account teams.

## Impact If Solved
The cheapest route to second-gift selection uses the co-op data nonprofits already license, changing only the question it is asked.
