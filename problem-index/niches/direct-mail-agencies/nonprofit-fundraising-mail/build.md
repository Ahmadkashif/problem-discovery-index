# One Donor Ledger From Acquisition Source to Upgrade

**Niche:** [[niches/direct-mail-agencies/nonprofit-fundraising-mail/profile|Nonprofit Fundraising Mail]]
**Industry:** [[industries/direct-mail-agencies|Direct Mail Agencies]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A nonprofit knows which list each new donor came from and what that donor gave last month, but not the lifetime value of each acquisition source — the number that should set acquisition budgets.

Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Acquisition sources are judged on first-gift response rate and cost per donor; housefile segments are judged on renewal revenue. A donor acquired from a co-op model in 2022 who later becomes a monthly donor or a mid-level giver is counted as a housefile success, and the source that found them gets no credit. Budgets for the next acquisition season are set without the evidence.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Acquisition and housefile programmes are often run by different teams or even different agencies, the CRM stores the original source code inconsistently, and meaningful lifetime value takes three to five years of giving to observe. Agencies are paid per campaign, not on lifetime outcomes.

## What to Build
A ledger that keeps every donor's acquisition source, package and offer alongside every subsequent gift, upgrade, monthly-giving conversion and lapse, with survival models projecting lifetime value for recent cohorts. The output is a source-level scorecard — expected five-year net value per donor by list, co-op model, exchange partner and package — feeding the next acquisition plan.

## Target Customer
Directors of direct response and their agencies at nonprofits mailing acquisition programmes of hundreds of thousands of pieces or more a year.

## Impact If Built
Budgets move toward sources that produce donors who stay, and the acquisition programme can be defended to a board on projected lifetime value rather than on a first-year loss.
