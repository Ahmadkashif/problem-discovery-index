# Nonprofit CRM Reporting Adapted to Mail Programmes

**Niche:** [[niches/direct-mail-agencies/nonprofit-fundraising-mail/profile|Nonprofit Fundraising Mail]]
**Industry:** [[industries/direct-mail-agencies|Direct Mail Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Nonprofit CRMs hold every gift; their reporting was built for gift processing and major-gift officers, not for appeal-by-package-by-segment mail results.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #compliance
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is "every serious competitor is fighting to raise net revenue per donor from mail," but acquisition programmes and housefile programmes split that into two contests with different inputs, clocks and winners. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Agencies export gifts from the client's CRM every week, rebuild campaign results in their own spreadsheets by appeal code, package and segment, and send them back as PDFs. The nonprofit's own staff see a different number in the CRM's dashboards.

## What Already Exists
Blackbaud Raiser's Edge NXT, Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud and similar CRMs, with appeal and campaign codes, gift batches and standard fundraising reports; agency reporting templates.

## The Customization Gap
Mail programmes need: keycode parsing to source, package, segment and ask-string version; cost-per-piece and postage loaded by drop so net revenue per thousand can be computed; response curves by days since in-home; cross-channel credit when a mail recipient gives online; and documentation that supports the USPS cooperative mail rule's fundraising exemption, which depends on the nonprofit receiving each donor's details.

## Target Customer
Development operations managers and agency account teams at nonprofits running monthly appeal calendars.

## Impact If Solved
One reporting view shared by agency and client removes weekly reconciliation, and puts net revenue by package in front of the people choosing the next appeal.
