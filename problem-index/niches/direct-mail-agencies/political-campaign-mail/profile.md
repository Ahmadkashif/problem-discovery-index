# Political Campaign Mail

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/direct-mail-agencies|Direct Mail Agencies]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to land an approved, disclaimer-compliant piece in target voters' homes before their ballots arrive — and whoever turns an approved script into in-home mail fastest in the final weeks takes the campaign.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.5B in a presidential cycle for candidate, party, PAC and ballot-measure mail, including postage and production (estimate, from USPS's 3.37 billion pieces of political and election mail in 2024)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~4% of combined direct mail and door-to-door spend in an election year, far less in odd years (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-medium — voter files and model scores are digital; approvals, script changes and drop decisions run by text message and phone at the last minute
**Target Buyer:** Principal of the political mail consulting firm; campaign manager or general consultant who approves the programme
**Automation Potential:** Medium — targeting, sequencing and compliance checks, not the message

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The clock is set by law, not by the client: Election Day, state ballot-mailing dates, early-voting windows and ballot-return deadlines. A piece that lands after a voter has returned their ballot is wasted postage. Mail is targeted from voter files with vote history and support and turnout scores, sequenced into programmes of five to fifteen pieces per household, and changed in the final weeks as polling moves. Every piece needs the correct "paid for by" disclaimer under FEC rules for federal races or state rules otherwise, and independent-expenditure mail carries coordination restrictions. Campaigns are short-lived buyers who start from scratch each cycle, so the consulting firm and mail house carry the memory.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Voter files and models from partisan data vendors, campaign CRMs and voter-contact platforms, political mail houses and union and party print shops, USPS Political Mail tracking. Gaps: ballot-return files arrive daily but mail files freeze days before the drop; approvals and disclaimer checks run by text; little evaluation of which pieces moved turnout or support.
