# Proofing Workflow Tools Adapted to Campaign Approvals

**Niche:** [[niches/direct-mail-agencies/political-campaign-mail/profile|Political Campaign Mail]]
**Industry:** [[industries/direct-mail-agencies|Direct Mail Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Creative proofing software already routes artwork through reviewers; campaign mail needs that routing in hours, with a legal check on the disclaimer built in.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to land an approved, disclaimer-compliant piece in target voters' homes before their ballots arrive — and whoever turns an approved script into in-home mail fastest in the final weeks takes the campaign.

## The Problem
A political mail piece may need sign-off from the candidate, campaign manager, general consultant, counsel and, for a coalition or union-funded piece, the sponsor. In October that happens by screenshot and text message, with the lettershop holding press time.

## What Already Exists
Online proofing and creative review platforms used by agencies and brand teams, with versioned proofs, annotation and approval routing.

## The Customization Gap
Campaign use needs: approval chains defined per committee and per piece type, with timeouts that escalate rather than wait; automatic checks that the "paid for by" statement, committee name and any required authorisation language match the paying committee and the jurisdiction's rules; independent-expenditure flags that keep coordinated parties out of the loop; mobile-first approval; and a hand-off to the mail house the moment the last approval lands.

## Target Customer
Political mail consulting firms producing dozens of pieces a week across many campaigns in the final month.

## Impact If Solved
Shaving a day from approval to press in the final weeks is a day more of voters who receive the piece before voting.
