# National Door-to-Door Distribution Networks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/direct-mail-agencies|Direct Mail Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Firms such as FlyerNation USA and GPS Flyers sell door-hanger and flyer distribution nationally to franchise systems, telecom providers, utilities and campaigns, and subcontract the walking to local crews, adding GPS tracking and reporting on top.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically under 10 in operations and reporting; no research function
**Output:** Distribution campaigns and proof-of-delivery reports
**Proprietary data:** Route histories and GPS traces from subcontracted crews
**Clock:** Clients' campaign dates and, for political work, election calendars
**Buyer:** Founder / Operations Director
