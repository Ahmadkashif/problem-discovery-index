# Response Modelling & Database Marketing Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/direct-mail-agencies|Direct Mail Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation firms

## What They Do
Independent analytics firms build response, uplift and lifetime-value models on mailers' customer and donor files, design and read tests, and advise on circulation strategy, for catalogers, insurers, nonprofits and the agencies that serve them. They are hired per model or on retainer.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 statisticians and analysts at the larger consultancies (estimate)
**Output:** Selection models, segmentation, test reads and circulation recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Little of their own — models are built on each client's house file and campaign history
**Clock:** Clients' mail dates and planning cycles
**Buyer:** Principal / Managing Partner
