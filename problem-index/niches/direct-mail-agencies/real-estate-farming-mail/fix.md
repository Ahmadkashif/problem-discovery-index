# Farms Chosen Without Looking at Turnover

**Niche:** [[niches/direct-mail-agencies/real-estate-farming-mail/profile|Real Estate Agent Farming Mail]]
**Industry:** [[industries/direct-mail-agencies|Direct Mail Agencies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Agents farm the neighbourhood they live in or like, not the one where enough homes sell, and spend years mailing an area that produces two listings a year for everyone combined.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show an agent that a farm area's monthly mail produced listing appointments — and whoever matches MLS listings and the agent's CRM back to the farm's mailing history keeps the agent subscribed.

## The Problem
A farm's ceiling is set by how many homes sell in it each year and how many competitors already dominate it. Agents pick farms by familiarity, and mailing services sell whatever area the agent draws on the map.

## Why It's Still Broken
Turnover and competitor share are computable from MLS history, but the mailing vendor has no incentive to tell an agent their chosen farm is a poor bet.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A farm selection check before the first order: annual turnover rate, median price and resulting commission pool, the top three agents' current share, and the number of mailings needed to reach a plausible share — so the agent chooses the farm with the best odds rather than the nearest one.

## Who Feels the Pain
New agents spending scarce money on a farm that can't pay back; teams allocating farms to agents without data.

## Impact If Fixed
A ten-minute check at the start avoids years of mailing to an area whose numbers were never going to work.
