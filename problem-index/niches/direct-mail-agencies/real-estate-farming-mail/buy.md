# Agent CRM Lead Sources Adapted to Farms

**Niche:** [[niches/direct-mail-agencies/real-estate-farming-mail/profile|Real Estate Agent Farming Mail]]
**Industry:** [[industries/direct-mail-agencies|Direct Mail Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Agent CRMs already track where each lead came from; a farm lead arrives as a phone call from someone who saw twelve postcards, and the CRM files it under "sphere" or "call-in".
**Tags:** #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show an agent that a farm area's monthly mail produced listing appointments — and whoever matches MLS listings and the agent's CRM back to the farm's mailing history keeps the agent subscribed.

## The Problem
An agent's CRM shows leads by source, but farm mail rarely gets credit because callers don't cite a postcard and agents don't tag the lead.

## What Already Exists
Agent CRMs such as Follow Up Boss and brokerage platforms, with lead-source fields, pipelines from lead to appointment to listing, and integrations with lead portals.

## The Customization Gap
Farm attribution needs: the farm's address list loaded as a geography, so any lead whose property address falls in the farm is tagged automatically; mailing history attached to each farm household; listing appointments and signed listings tied back to the farm; and a farm-level report of leads, appointments and listings against mailing spend.

## Target Customer
Agents and team leads already running a CRM who farm one or more neighbourhoods.

## Impact If Solved
Automatic farm tagging gives agents a true count of what their farm produced, using a system they already pay for.
