# Real Estate Agent Farming Mail

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/direct-mail-agencies|Direct Mail Agencies]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show an agent that a farm area's monthly mail produced listing appointments — and whoever matches MLS listings and the agent's CRM back to the farm's mailing history keeps the agent subscribed.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1B a year in "just listed", "just sold" and market-update mail sent by individual agents and teams to geographic farms (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% of combined direct mail and door-to-door spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — ordering and templates are online; measurement of what the mail produced is absent
**Target Buyer:** The individual agent or team lead, paying from their own commission
**Automation Potential:** High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Farming is a years-long bet on a fixed set of ~500 to 2,000 homes (estimate): the agent mails the same households every month so that when a homeowner decides to sell, the agent is the name they remember. The outcome — listings in the farm, and the agent's share of them — is public in the MLS but arrives slowly and at a low base rate, since only a small share of homes sell each year. The buyer is a solo professional with no analyst, mailing through farming postcard services (such as ProspectsPLUS! and Corefact) or a local printer, and deciding each quarter whether to keep paying.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Farming postcard services with templates, farm-area list pulls and subscription mailing; agent CRMs (Follow Up Boss and brokerage-provided systems) tracking lead source; MLS data on every listing and sale. Gaps: no farm-level view of listings won versus listings lost to competitors over time, farm choice by affinity rather than turnover, and lead source recorded by the agent's memory.
