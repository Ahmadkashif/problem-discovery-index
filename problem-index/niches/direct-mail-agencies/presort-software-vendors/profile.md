# Mail Preparation & Presort Software Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/direct-mail-agencies|Direct Mail Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
BCC Software (Mail Manager, and the Bulk Mailer line it took over from Satori), AccuZIP, Window Book and a few others sell the USPS-certified software that standardises addresses, runs presort, generates Intelligent Mail barcodes and produces Mail.dat files for PostalOne!. Their postal affairs and product teams track every USPS rule and price change and ship certified updates before each effective date.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 postal affairs, certification and product research staff per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Certified software releases, rate tables, postal rule interpretations and customer guidance
**Proprietary data:** Rule and rate libraries and certification histories; customers' mailing files stay with the customers
**Clock:** USPS price-change effective dates and certification cycles — hard deadlines the software must meet on the day
**Buyer:** VP of Product / Director of Postal Affairs
