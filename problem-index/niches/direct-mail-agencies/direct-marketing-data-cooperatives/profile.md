# Direct Marketing Data Cooperatives

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/direct-mail-agencies|Direct Mail Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Cooperative databases pool transaction-level purchase and giving histories contributed by member catalogers, retailers and nonprofits, and sell modelled prospect selections back to members for each mailing. Epsilon's Abacus, Wiland, Path2Response and Alliant are the national players; Abacus self-reports more than 3,000 contributing brands. A mailer sends its buyer or donor file and a target quantity, the co-op builds a response model across its pooled data, and returns ranked names priced per thousand.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 data scientists, modelling analysts and client analytics staff per large co-op (estimate)
**Output:** Custom response models and modelled name selections per campaign, plus post-campaign performance analyses
**Proprietary data:** Pooled multi-brand transaction histories that no single mailer or compiler holds, joined to which modelled names later bought from or gave to the member
**Clock:** Members' merge/purge and mail dates — set by the client's campaign calendar (holiday catalogues, year-end appeals), not by a third party
**Buyer:** Chief Analytics Officer / SVP of Data Science
