# CDP Identity Resolution Adapted to Mail Matchback

**Niche:** [[niches/direct-mail-agencies/response-matchback-reporting/profile|Response Matchback & Campaign Reporting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/direct-mail-agencies|Direct Mail Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Customer data platforms already resolve a customer's emails, devices and orders into one profile; they treat a mailing address as an attribute, not as the key a mail campaign is matched on.
**Tags:** #k-nearest-neighbors #logistic-regression #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to credit each response to the right list, segment and version within the client's planning cycle, in numbers the client's finance team and the list owners both accept — and whoever delivers a reproducible matchback before the next circulation plan takes the account.

## The Problem
Mailers with a CDP have the buyer side of matchback in one place, but the agency still exports orders and rebuilds the match against the mail file in its own scripts.

## What Already Exists
Customer data platforms with deterministic and probabilistic identity resolution, profile unification and audience exports, analysed under [[industries/customer-data-platforms|Customer Data Platforms]].

## The Customization Gap
Mail matchback needs: CASS-standardised address matching at individual and household level with NCOA-aware handling of movers; import of mail files with keycodes, source lists and in-home dates as campaign objects; response windows from in-home date; credit rules aligned with merge/purge priority; prospect records that never became customers retained for comparison; and holdout flags carried through.

## Target Customer
Mailers with an established CDP and the agencies that report their mail results.

## Impact If Solved
Matchback becomes a standard job inside the client's own data platform, and agency and client read the same numbers.
