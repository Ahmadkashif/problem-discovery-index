# Competitive Mail Intelligence Panels

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/direct-mail-agencies|Direct Mail Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Panels of households forward every piece of mail they receive to a vendor that scans, codes and archives it — offer terms, APRs and bonuses, creative format, mailer, and estimated volume and spend. Mintel Comperemedia and Competiscan focus on financial services, insurance and telecoms; Who's Mailing What! keeps a searchable creative archive across thousands of mailers. Card issuers, banks, insurers and their agencies subscribe to benchmark rival offers and plan their own.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-200 coders, analysts and panel operations staff across the three main providers (estimate)
**Output:** Coded offer databases, volume and spend estimates, competitive reports and alerts
**Proprietary data:** Decades of panel-collected mailpieces with coded offer terms — observed rather than owned, since the pieces belong to the mailers, but assembled nowhere else
**Clock:** Weekly publication and clients' planning cycles; no third-party deadline
**Buyer:** Head of Research / General Manager of the competitive intelligence product
