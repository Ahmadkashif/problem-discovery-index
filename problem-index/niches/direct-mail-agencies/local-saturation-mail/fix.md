# "How Did You Hear About Us?" as the Only Measurement

**Niche:** [[niches/direct-mail-agencies/local-saturation-mail/profile|Local Service Businesses on Saturation Mail]]
**Industry:** [[industries/direct-mail-agencies|Direct Mail Agencies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A local owner judges thousands of dollars of mail a month by what callers remember when the dispatcher asks, and callers mostly say "Google".
**Tags:** #evaluation-metrics #hypothesis-testing #causal-inference #quick-win #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show a plumber, roofer or pizzeria which carrier routes produced booked jobs or orders — and whoever ties route-level drops to the owner's own job records keeps the owner mailing every month.

## The Problem
Customers who received a postcard often search the business name before calling, and tell the dispatcher they found it online. Mail looks weak and search looks strong, and owners cut the channel that started the call.

## Why It's Still Broken
Tracked phone numbers and promo codes are printed on some pieces but not used consistently, and nobody compares calls from mailed and unmailed areas.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A tracked number per route group on every piece, call records matched to caller address where available, and a simple monthly report of new-customer calls and jobs from mailed areas versus comparable unmailed ones — set up once by the agency or printer and left running.

## Who Feels the Pain
Owners deciding whether to renew; dispatchers asked to log a question nobody answers accurately; agencies losing customers who stopped mailing for the wrong reason.

## Impact If Fixed
A cheap, consistent measure keeps owners from cutting a channel that works and shows them which routes don't.
