# Local Service Businesses on Saturation Mail

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/direct-mail-agencies|Direct Mail Agencies]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show a plumber, roofer or pizzeria which carrier routes produced booked jobs or orders — and whoever ties route-level drops to the owner's own job records keeps the owner mailing every month.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$4B a year in saturation and EDDM postcards, shared-mail inserts and route-targeted mail bought by local service businesses and franchisees (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of combined direct mail and door-to-door spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — routes picked by eye on the USPS EDDM map, results judged by "how did you hear about us?"
**Target Buyer:** Owner or office manager of a home-service business, restaurant or clinic; local marketing manager at franchise systems
**Automation Potential:** High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
These buyers mail by geography, not by name. Every Door Direct Mail and saturation pricing let a business pick carrier routes and mail every household without a list, so there is no Move Update work and no list rental — and also no name to match a customer back to. The buyer is an owner without a marketing team who decides each month whether to keep mailing based on whether the phone rang. Agencies and online EDDM printers serve them with templates and route maps; the large agencies' analytics never reach this tier, and the owner's own records — jobs in a field-service system, orders in a POS — are never joined to the routes.

## Current Tools & Gaps
USPS EDDM Online for route selection and counts; online printers and local agencies offering EDDM packages; shared-mail programmes such as RRD's Save package; field-service platforms (ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber) and restaurant POS systems that hold every customer address. Gaps: no route-to-job join, no unmailed comparison routes, route choice by habit.
