# Field-Service Marketing Modules Adapted to Saturation Mail

**Niche:** [[niches/direct-mail-agencies/local-saturation-mail/profile|Local Service Businesses on Saturation Mail]]
**Industry:** [[industries/direct-mail-agencies|Direct Mail Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Field-service platforms already send postcards to past customers from their own records; they don't plan or measure route-level prospecting mail to people who have never called.
**Tags:** #evaluation-metrics #causal-inference #data-integration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show a plumber, roofer or pizzeria which carrier routes produced booked jobs or orders — and whoever ties route-level drops to the owner's own job records keeps the owner mailing every month.

## The Problem
Owners use one system to run jobs and a separate printer or website to buy EDDM, so prospecting mail sits outside the system where results are recorded.

## What Already Exists
Marketing add-ons inside field-service platforms (ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber) that send reminder and reactivation postcards and emails to existing customers, with campaign tracking by customer record.

## The Customization Gap
Saturation prospecting needs: carrier-route geometry and household counts from USPS; EDDM and saturation price thresholds and piece-size rules; route selection informed by job density and housing data; drop calendars tied to seasonal demand (furnace season, storm season); holdout routes for comparison; and attribution of first-time customers to routes rather than to a campaign record that never existed.

## Target Customer
Field-service platform product teams and the larger home-service operators using them.

## Impact If Solved
Putting prospecting mail inside the system of record lets owners see mail's cost per booked job next to every other channel without a separate tool.
