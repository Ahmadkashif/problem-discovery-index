# Integrated Mail Platform Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/direct-mail-agencies|Direct Mail Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The largest mail producers — RRD (which added Vericast's shared mail and coupon businesses in 2024), Quad, IWCO Direct and Taylor — combine data services, print, lettershop and postal logistics for national mailers, and run marketing analytics and data science teams that model audiences, plan circulation and report results as part of the production relationship.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 analytics and data science staff per platform, excluding production and data processing headcount (estimate)
**Output:** Audience models, circulation recommendations, campaign reporting — bundled into print, postage and data services
**Proprietary data:** Production and postal data across many clients' campaigns; the audience and response data belong to the clients
**Clock:** Clients' in-home dates, USPS price changes and induction schedules
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / SVP of Marketing Analytics
