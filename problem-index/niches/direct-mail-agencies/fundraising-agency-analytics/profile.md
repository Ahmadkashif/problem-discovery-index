# Fundraising Agency Analytics Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/direct-mail-agencies|Direct Mail Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation firms

## What They Do
The larger nonprofit direct-response agencies run analytics departments that segment client donor files, build renewal and upgrade models, design tests, and report campaign and lifetime results across dozens of nonprofit clients. Their analysis is part of what wins and keeps accounts, billed inside agency fees alongside creative and production management.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 analysts and data scientists at the larger agencies (estimate)
**Output:** Segmentation and selection models, test designs and reads, campaign and donor lifetime reporting
**Proprietary data:** Cross-client results and test archives — held under each client's data agreement rather than owned
**Clock:** The appeal calendar peaks against the 31 December tax-year deadline, a fixed external date; the rest of the year follows client schedules
**Buyer:** Chief Analytics Officer / VP of Analytics at the agency
