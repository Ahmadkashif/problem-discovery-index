# Mail Tracking & Delivery Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/direct-mail-agencies|Direct Mail Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors such as GrayHair Software take USPS Informed Visibility scan data on mailers' behalf and turn it into tracking dashboards, in-home reports, delivery performance analysis and postal advisory — telling a mailer when its pieces reached homes and where the network was slow.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 postal analysts and data staff at the larger vendors (estimate)
**Output:** In-home and delivery performance reports, mail quality analysis, postal consulting
**Proprietary data:** Aggregated scan histories across many mailers' campaigns — but each mailer's scan feed is provided under its own Mailer ID and USPS terms
**Clock:** Clients' in-home windows, USPS price and promotion dates, election mail windows
**Buyer:** VP of Postal Affairs / President of the tracking vendor
