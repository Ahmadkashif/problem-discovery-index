# USPS Mail Acceptance & Marketing Mail Pricing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/direct-mail-agencies|Direct Mail Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Postal Service sets and enforces the preparation rules every commercial mailing must meet to earn its price: Move Update, Intelligent Mail barcodes, eDoc, and Seamless Acceptance scorecards that turn preparation errors into postage assessments. It also prices Marketing Mail within the regulator's rules and runs the promotions mailers plan around. The Postal Regulatory Commission's review of those prices is already profiled as [[niches/last-mile-delivery/postal-regulatory-analysis/profile|🔍 Postal Regulatory Analysis]] and is not repeated here.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Hundreds across business mail acceptance analytics, pricing and product management (estimate)
**Output:** Mailer scorecards and assessments, preparation standards, price changes and promotion rules
**Proprietary data:** eDoc and scan data for every Full-Service mailing — the complete record of how commercial mail is prepared and moves
**Clock:** Price-change filings under the PRC's rules (the latest effective 12 July 2026) and annual promotion calendars
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — an independent federal establishment
