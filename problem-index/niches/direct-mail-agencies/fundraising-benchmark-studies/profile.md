# Fundraising Benchmark Studies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/direct-mail-agencies|Direct Mail Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Annual benchmark studies of nonprofit fundraising — M+R Benchmarks and the Fundraising Effectiveness Project among them — pool participating organisations' results to publish response rates, revenue per piece, acquisition returns and donor retention. They are produced by small teams inside agencies or associations, usually free to read and funded as marketing or member service.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically under 10 people per study
**Output:** Annual benchmark reports
**Proprietary data:** Participating nonprofits' contributed programme results
**Clock:** Self-set annual publication
**Buyer:** Agency principal or association research lead
