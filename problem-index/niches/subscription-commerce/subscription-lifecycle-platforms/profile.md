# Subscription Lifecycle Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/subscription-commerce|Subscription Commerce]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Not terminal — the contest differs by whether the customer chose the contents, and the decomposition is recorded below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.6B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~27% of category operating spend
**Digital Adoption:** High
**Target Buyer:** Replenishment operators; separately, curation operators
**Automation Potential:** High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is the machinery every operator runs on: plan management, recurring orders, billing, skip and swap, and the customer account. It is the largest software line in the category and where the platform vendors compete.

It is **not terminal**, because the two business models running on that machinery fail for opposite reasons and need opposite capabilities. A replenishment subscription delivers something the customer chose; it fails when the timing is wrong, and its contest is predicting consumption so the next delivery arrives as the last one runs out — a prediction problem with a checkable answer. A curated subscription delivers something the customer did not choose; it fails when the contents disappoint, and its contest is selection under taste uncertainty with no checkable answer and a much harder feedback problem. The cancellation reasons, the data that matters, the techniques and the operational consequences are unrelated. Filter Notes in the overview records the two rejected alternatives; the sub-niches below are the split.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Recurring order management, plan and cadence configuration, skip and swap, dunning, and customer portals. The gaps are specific to each model and are stated in the sub-niches.
