## The Problem
Analysing repeated measurements on the same individuals over time is a whole statistical field, with mixed-effects models, growth curve analysis and panel methods that separate within-person change from between-person difference. Consumer research firms spend heavily to recruit and maintain panels with far less detail than a subscription business gets automatically. The methods are taught, implemented and freely available, and the category uses a retention curve.

## What Already Exists
Mixed-effects and hierarchical models for repeated measures; growth curve and trajectory analysis; panel data methods separating within and between variation; latent class and trajectory clustering; and the consumer panel research tradition of longitudinal measurement.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a panel where the company controls the treatment. It requires: (1) treating each delivery as an intervention with an observable response, since the company chooses what to send and when and can therefore run genuine experiments on its own panel — this is a stronger position than any research panel and is entirely unused; (2) trajectory clustering to find the subscriber types, which is more informative than the demographic segmentation the category uses and is directly available; (3) within-subscriber comparison as the primary design, since each subscriber is their own control and that removes the between-person confounding that ruins cross-sectional analysis; (4) attrition handled as informative rather than as missing at random, because the subscribers who leave are exactly the ones whose trajectory carries the finding; and (5) delivery to a business with no statistician, which means the methods must arrive as a product rather than as an analysis.

## Target Customer
Subscription operators, platform vendors who could embed this, and the longitudinal methods community for whom this is a freely generated panel of unusual quality.

## Impact If Solved
Research firms pay heavily for worse panels than this category generates automatically. The company controls the treatment, which makes genuine within-panel experimentation available in a way no research panel allows, and each subscriber serves as their own control.
