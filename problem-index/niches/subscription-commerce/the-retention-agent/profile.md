# The Retention Agent

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/subscription-commerce|Subscription Commerce]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to move the retention conversation earlier than the cancel page — and whoever does that takes the saves, because by the time the agent is involved the decision has been made and a discount is the only tool left.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$420M US in retention operations
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of category operating spend
**Digital Adoption:** None — handed a decision already made
**Target Buyer:** Customer experience organisations and their leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — earlier detection and better tooling are both buildable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Retention agents are handed customers at the moment they have already decided to leave, armed with discounts, and measured on saves they mostly cannot make. The structural problem is timing: the decision was made before the conversation began, frequently weeks before, in response to a specific problem the agent cannot see and cannot fix from a script of offers. The agent absorbs the frustration of a customer who wants to leave, applies a discount that addresses a price objection the customer probably did not have, and is measured on an outcome largely determined before they were involved. It is a role designed around the last moment of a process whose failure happened much earlier.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Cancel flow scripts, discount offers, pause options, and a save rate target. The gaps: the agent cannot see the subscriber's delivery history, skips or complaints; no diagnosis of the actual problem is presented; the offers are financial where the problems are operational; no route to fix the underlying issue rather than discount it; and the save rate is measured without distinguishing genuine saves from deferred cancellations.
