# Payoff Statement Procurement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/title-and-settlement-offices|Title & Settlement Offices]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to obtain an accurate, good-through payoff statement from every lienholder — servicers, HELOC lenders, private noteholders, judgment creditors — before the Closing Disclosure is final, and whoever gets the right number first with no post-funding shortage takes the agency's payoff desk.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$250M annual staff time and outsourced payoff-ordering services (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% of industry receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — large servicers run payoff portals; everyone else answers by fax and phone
**Target Buyer:** Escrow manager
**Automation Potential:** High for requesting and checking; medium for private and disputed payoffs

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The payoff is a pre-closing number with a deadline: it must be in hand, good through the funding date, and correct before the Closing Disclosure is balanced. The counterparty is the servicer's payoff department, and the failure modes are specific — a statement that omits a late fee or escrow advance and leaves a shortage after funding, a per-diem calculated to the wrong date, a HELOC that keeps accepting draws after the payoff, a private noteholder who will not respond, a judgment creditor who wants more than the docket shows. The winner is whoever gets the right figure first, so the CD balances once.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Servicer payoff portals, authorisation forms, fax, and payoff-ordering services offered by some vendors. Gaps: no routing directory spanning servicers and private lenders; payoff statements read and keyed by hand; shortage risk not checked against the loan's history.
