# Document Automation for Servicer Payoff Portals

**Niche:** [[niches/title-and-settlement-offices/payoff-statement-procurement/profile|Payoff Statement Procurement]]
**Industry:** [[industries/title-and-settlement-offices|Title & Settlement Offices]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Robotic process automation and document extraction tools already work web portals and read invoices; payoff ordering is portal work plus statement reading.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #automation #data-integration #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to obtain an accurate, good-through payoff statement from every lienholder — servicers, HELOC lenders, private noteholders, judgment creditors — before the Closing Disclosure is final, and whoever gets the right number first with no post-funding shortage takes the agency's payoff desk.

## The Problem
Large servicers' payoff portals each demand their own login, form and authorisation upload, and return statements in their own layout.

## What Already Exists
Robotic process automation platforms and intelligent document processing tools used for accounts payable and claims intake.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs connectors per servicer portal, mapping from the production system's lien and borrower data to each portal's fields, extraction tuned to payoff statement layouts, per-diem logic, good-through monitoring with automatic re-request, and secure handling of the borrower's nonpublic personal information under the office's privacy controls.

## Target Customer
Agencies whose payoff volume is concentrated in a few dozen large servicers.

## Impact If Solved
Portal ordering and statement keying disappear for the majority of payoffs, leaving processors the private and disputed ones.
