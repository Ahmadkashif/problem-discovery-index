# Underwriter Agency Audit & Agent Risk Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/title-and-settlement-offices|Title & Settlement Offices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Each title underwriter's agency department audits its appointed agents' escrow accounts, policy issuance and premium remittance, scores agent risk, and handles closing protection letter claims. Underwriting rules and title claims themselves belong to the underwriters' own industry, `title-insurance-carriers`, still in the build queue; the claims corpus is logged under Land Surveyors as [[niches/land-surveyors/title-underwriting-claims-research/profile|Title Underwriting & Claims Research]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 auditors and agency risk analysts per national underwriter (estimate)
**Output:** Agent audits, risk ratings and remittance reviews, all internal
**Proprietary data:** Escrow reconciliations, policy registers and claim history by agent across thousands of agents
**Clock:** Audit cycles and statutory reporting
**Buyer:** No external buyer — SVP of agency operations
