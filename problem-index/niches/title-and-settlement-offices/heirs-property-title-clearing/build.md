# An Heirship Tree With Evidence for Every Link

**Niche:** [[niches/title-and-settlement-offices/heirs-property-title-clearing/profile|Heirs' Property & Estate Title Clearing]]
**Industry:** [[industries/title-and-settlement-offices|Title & Settlement Offices]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Clearing heirs' property means proving who every heir is, through three or four generations, and that proof is rebuilt by hand on every file.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #graph-neural-networks #k-nearest-neighbors #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to assemble a path to insurable title that the underwriter will accept — affidavit of heirship, probate, quiet title or a partition under the heirs' property act — for a family with no will and many heirs, and whoever locates every heir and gathers the signatures fastest closes the sale.

## The Problem
The record owner died in 1971 without a will; two of the six children have since died, one with a will and one without; the grandchildren live in four states. The title office needs a family tree that an underwriter will accept, with each death, marriage and birth supported, and current addresses for every living heir.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Few files of this kind reach any one office, the evidence is spread across vital records, obituaries, probate dockets and family memory, and the families are poor customers for software.

## What to Build
An heirship workspace that starts from the record owner, proposes the family tree from probate filings, obituaries and public records, links each relationship to its evidence, computes each heir's fractional interest under the state's intestacy rules at each death, flags the gaps an underwriter will ask about, and finds current contact details for living heirs. It outputs the affidavit of heirship or the probate or partition filing the chosen path requires.

## Target Customer
Title offices and closing attorneys in the Southeast and Appalachia; heirs' property legal-aid centres; underwriters' curative departments.

## Impact If Built
Weeks of genealogy per file become days, more family land can be sold, refinanced or protected, and the office can quote the path and timeline at the first meeting.
