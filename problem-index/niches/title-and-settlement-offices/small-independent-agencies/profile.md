# Small Independent Title Agencies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/title-and-settlement-offices|Title & Settlement Offices]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a one-to-four-person agency on its underwriters' and lenders' approved lists — Best Practices evidence, daily escrow reconciliation, cyber and E&O cover, vetting questionnaires — without a compliance hire, and whoever turns that evidence into a by-product of daily work takes the shop.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.2B in receipts at the 4,072 title firms with fewer than five employees (Census SUSB 2022); the compliance tooling they buy is a small fraction of that (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of industry receipts, 60% of firms (Census SUSB 2022)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — they run production software, but compliance evidence lives in binders and inboxes
**Target Buyer:** Agency owner, who is usually also the examiner or escrow officer
**Automation Potential:** High for evidence collection and reconciliation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Most title firms are tiny: 60% have fewer than five employees and 71% of ALTA's 2024 survey respondents had revenue under $1M. The owner examines, closes, reconciles escrow and answers the underwriter's audit. The same compliance load falls on them as on a national agency — ALTA Best Practices (version 5.0 published 8 October 2026), underwriter audits of escrow accounts and policy remittance, lender vetting through services such as Secure Insight, cyber-insurance questionnaires — with no one whose job it is. Losing an underwriter appointment or a lender's approved-agent status can end the business.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Production systems with escrow accounting; reconciliation services; Best Practices assessment providers; underwriter agent portals. Gaps: evidence assembled by hand before each assessment; every lender's vetting questionnaire answered separately; no continuous view of whether the agency is compliant today.
