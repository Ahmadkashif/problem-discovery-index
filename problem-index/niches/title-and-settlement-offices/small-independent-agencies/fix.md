# Forty Lenders, Forty Vetting Questionnaires

**Niche:** [[niches/title-and-settlement-offices/small-independent-agencies/profile|Small Independent Title Agencies]]
**Industry:** [[industries/title-and-settlement-offices|Title & Settlement Offices]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Every lender an agency closes for sends its own settlement-agent questionnaire asking for the same licences, policies, insurance certificates and bank letters.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #compliance #data-integration #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a one-to-four-person agency on its underwriters' and lenders' approved lists — Best Practices evidence, daily escrow reconciliation, cyber and E&O cover, vetting questionnaires — without a compliance hire, and whoever turns that evidence into a by-product of daily work takes the shop.

## The Problem
Lenders must manage third-party risk, so each vets its settlement agents: licences, E&O and fidelity certificates, closing protection letters, escrow account verification, Best Practices status. A small agency fills in the same answers in each lender's format, and renews them each year.

## Why It's Still Broken
Vetting services pool some of it, but lenders still send their own forms, and the agency keeps no structured master copy.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A maintained agency profile with every document and answer, versioned and dated, from which any lender's questionnaire is pre-filled for the owner to approve, with expiry reminders for each certificate.

## Who Feels the Pain
Agency owners and office managers.

## Impact If Fixed
At an estimated 1–2 hours per questionnaire across 20–40 lender relationships a year, a pre-filled profile returns roughly 20–80 owner hours a year (estimate).
