# Security Compliance Automation Mapped to Best Practices

**Niche:** [[niches/title-and-settlement-offices/small-independent-agencies/profile|Small Independent Title Agencies]]
**Industry:** [[industries/title-and-settlement-offices|Title & Settlement Offices]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Compliance automation platforms already collect SOC 2 evidence from cloud systems; title agencies need the same, mapped to a different framework.
**Tags:** #compliance #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a one-to-four-person agency on its underwriters' and lenders' approved lists — Best Practices evidence, daily escrow reconciliation, cyber and E&O cover, vetting questionnaires — without a compliance hire, and whoever turns that evidence into a by-product of daily work takes the shop.

## The Problem
Small agencies face security and privacy questions from underwriters, lenders and cyber insurers that look like SOC 2 questions, without the staff to answer them.

## What Already Exists
Compliance automation platforms that connect to cloud services, collect evidence continuously and map it to SOC 2, ISO 27001 and similar frameworks.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs a Best Practices control library (escrow trust accounting, NPI protection, settlement procedures, policy production and remittance, insurance coverage, complaints), connectors to title production systems and escrow banks rather than only cloud infrastructure, and pricing for a three-person office.

## Target Customer
Small agencies preparing for their first or next Best Practices assessment.

## Impact If Solved
A proven category of tooling reaches the 60% of title firms it currently ignores.
