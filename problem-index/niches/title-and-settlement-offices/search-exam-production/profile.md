# Title Search & Examination Production

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/title-and-settlement-offices|Title & Settlement Offices]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a clean chain into an underwriter-acceptable commitment in minutes and send only the genuinely defective chains to an examiner, and whoever draws that line most accurately takes the lender channel's refinance and purchase volume.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.8B annual searcher and examiner payroll, plant access and outsourced search and exam spent by title agencies (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of industry receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High in plant counties, low elsewhere
**Target Buyer:** Agency production manager; head of title operations at a multi-state agency
**Automation Potential:** High for clean files; low for the difficult third

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Search and examination is the factory floor: BLS counts 48,580 title examiners, abstractors and searchers, 19,260 of them in legal services. Most files are clean — a recent prior policy, a mortgage to pay off, taxes current — and the commitment is a near-template. ALTA puts difficult files at 36%, and those are where examiner judgement matters. The money in this niche is in throughput on clean files, especially for refinances, where lenders route orders to whichever agent turns commitments fastest. The organisations that own the title plants these searches run against are profiled in Pass 2 under Land Surveyors as [[niches/land-surveyors/title-plant-search-operations/profile|Title Plant & Search Operations]] (55, indexed; under review: counts production headcount); this niche is the agent's own production line.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Title plants and automated search products (DataTrace TitleIQ, underwriter plants), production-system commitment builders, and since 2026 underwriter AI review — First American's AgentNet Assist: Title Intelligence, Qualia Clear, RecordsOnline's CommitmentAI. Gaps: none of them knows which files this agency's examiners would treat as clean; confidence is not calibrated by county; and commitments carry boilerplate requirements copied from the last file.
