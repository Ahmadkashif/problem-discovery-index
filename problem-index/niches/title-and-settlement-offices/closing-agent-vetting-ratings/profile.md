# Closing-Agent Vetting & Settlement Risk Ratings

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/title-and-settlement-offices|Title & Settlement Offices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Vendors that vet and monitor title agents, escrow officers and closing attorneys on behalf of mortgage lenders — licences, E&O and fidelity coverage, closing protection letters, trust-account verification, complaints and incidents — and sell the resulting ratings into lenders' vendor-management workflows. Secure Insight is the best-known example and self-reports vetting more than 95,000 settlement professionals and verifying more than 125,000 trust accounts since 2012. Lenders pay per agent or per loan; the agents themselves are the subject, not the customer.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 vetting analysts and investigators (estimate)
**Output:** Settlement-agent risk and performance ratings, trust-account verifications, monitoring alerts
**Proprietary data:** A shared database of vetted settlement professionals, their trust accounts and incident history, pooled across many lenders
**Clock:** The lender's funding date — an agent must be approved before the loan closes through it — plus annual re-vetting
**Buyer:** Head of vetting operations or chief product officer at the vetting vendor — a handful of such firms, selling to lenders' vendor-management teams

**Kill switches:** none — licensing, insurance and incident records are compiled for this purpose, and trust-account confirmations are collected with the agent's consent.
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. The rating is a real product with a pooled database behind it, but it is bought as a compliance utility, the labour grows with the number of agents rather than with analysis, the clock is the lender's onboarding rather than a hard external deadline, and the vendors are a handful of firms.
