# Offshore Title Search & Examination Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/title-and-settlement-offices|Title & Settlement Offices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Business-process outsourcing firms, mostly in India and the Philippines, that run searches on US county systems, type commitments, prepare exam reports and work post-closing queues for US agencies and underwriters at per-file prices.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Hundreds to thousands of production staff; analytical core under 10
**Output:** Searches, exam reports, typed commitments, post-closing work
**Proprietary data:** None of their own — the files belong to the clients
**Clock:** Client turn-time agreements
**Buyer:** Delivery head at the vendor
