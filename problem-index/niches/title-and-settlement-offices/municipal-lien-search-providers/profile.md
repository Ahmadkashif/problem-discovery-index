# Municipal Lien & Open-Permit Search Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/title-and-settlement-offices|Title & Settlement Offices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Outsourced research firms that contact municipalities, utilities and code-enforcement offices for the items a search of recorded documents misses: unrecorded utility balances, special assessments, open or expired permits and code violations. PropLogix, Rexera and reQuire sell these reports per file to title agents, who pass the cost to the transaction; Florida is the deepest market. HOA estoppels and resale certificates are a separate pocket, profiled under HOA Management as [[niches/hoa-management/resale-certificate-document-services/profile|Resale Certificate & Association Document Services]] (47).

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-200 researchers and quality reviewers at the larger providers (estimate); analytical core under 10
**Output:** Municipal lien search reports per parcel, with payoff figures for open items
**Proprietary data:** Per-municipality process, contact and turnaround knowledge, and an archive of past search results by parcel
**Clock:** The closing date — the report must arrive before clear-to-close
**Buyer:** Founder or VP of operations
