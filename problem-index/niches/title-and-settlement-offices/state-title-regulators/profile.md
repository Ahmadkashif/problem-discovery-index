# State Insurance Department Title Divisions

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/title-and-settlement-offices|Title & Settlement Offices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State insurance departments license title agents and, in some states, escrow officers; audit agents' escrow accounts; and in promulgated-rate states such as Texas collect agent-level statistical data for rate proceedings. Texas data show ~$2.50B of gross title premium in 2024, ~84% of it retained by agents.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 examiners, auditors and actuarial staff per large state (estimate)
**Output:** Licensing, escrow audits, rate orders, statistical reports
**Proprietary data:** Agent-level premium, expense and audit data in promulgated states
**Clock:** Statutory rate proceedings and audit cycles
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — state government
