# Lender Closing Portals With Two-Way Fee Sync

**Niche:** [[niches/title-and-settlement-offices/closing-disclosure-balancing/profile|Closing Disclosure Balancing & Settlement Statements]]
**Industry:** [[industries/title-and-settlement-offices|Title & Settlement Offices]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Lenders already offer settlement agents a portal to enter fees for the CD; the agent's production system already holds those fees, and the two do not talk.
**Tags:** #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #compliance #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make the lender's Closing Disclosure and the settlement agent's statement agree to the cent in one round inside TRID's three-business-day window, and whoever eliminates the redisclosure round gets onto the lender's preferred settlement-agent list.

## The Problem
An escrow officer working with ten lenders rekeys fees into ten portals, then re-checks the CD each lender generates.

## What Already Exists
Lender closing platforms and origination systems with settlement-agent collaboration features; production-system fee exports.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs a two-way sync that maps the production system's charge codes to each lender's CD fields and tolerance buckets, pushes changes in either direction with the source recorded, applies the title premium disclosure calculation consistently, and alerts both parties when a change crosses a redisclosure threshold.

## Target Customer
Agencies and lenders that already exchange high volumes through a common closing platform.

## Impact If Solved
Ending rekeying saves an estimated 15–30 minutes of escrow time per financed file — roughly 40–75 hours a month for an office closing 150 financed files (estimate) — and both sides work from one set of figures.
