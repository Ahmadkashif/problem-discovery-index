# Land Title Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/title-and-settlement-offices|Title & Settlement Offices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The American Land Title Association and the state land title associations: advocacy, education, the Best Practices framework (version 5.0 published 8 October 2026), quarterly market share analysis from underwriters' statutory filings, and critical-issue studies such as the 2024 curative-time study and the 2026 seller impersonation study.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research staff; the critical-issue studies are run with an outside firm, ndp | analytics
**Output:** Market share reports, member surveys, policy research
**Proprietary data:** Member survey responses
**Clock:** Quarterly market share releases and legislative calendars
**Buyer:** Chief executive — member funded
