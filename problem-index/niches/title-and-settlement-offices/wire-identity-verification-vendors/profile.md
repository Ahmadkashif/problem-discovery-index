# Closing Wire & Seller Identity Verification Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/title-and-settlement-offices|Title & Settlement Offices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors that verify payee bank accounts, buyer and seller identity and payout instructions on each closing, often backed by a loss guarantee, and publish fraud-trend reports to the industry; CertifID is the best-known example. Title and escrow offices pay per transaction. This is the vault's profile for these vendors; signer identity proofing at the notarial act is covered under [[industries/notary-signing-services|Notary & Signing Services]]. Demand is rising with the threat: the FBI's IC3 recorded $275.1M of real estate fraud losses in 2025, and ALTA's 2026 study found 59% of title firms saw a seller impersonation attempt in 2025.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 fraud operations analysts and investigators (estimate)
**Output:** Verification results, fraud alerts, loss-backed verification certificates, published fraud reports
**Proprietary data:** Verification outcomes and attempted frauds across many offices' closings
**Clock:** Disbursement on closing day and bank wire cut-offs
**Buyer:** Head of fraud operations at the verification vendor — a handful of such firms, selling to agency owners and underwriters' risk teams
