# Which Endorsement Is Available in Which State

**Niche:** [[niches/title-and-settlement-offices/commercial-title-escrow/profile|Commercial Title & Escrow]]
**Industry:** [[industries/title-and-settlement-offices|Title & Settlement Offices]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Lender's counsel sends a national endorsement list; the officer finds out state by state which endorsements are filed, at what charge, and what substitute exists.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #compliance #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver a marked pro forma policy, with every exception reconciled to the ALTA/NSPS survey and the endorsements lender's counsel demands, before the loan's closing deadline, and whoever negotiates exceptions and endorsements fastest across states takes the national accounts.

## The Problem
On a multi-state portfolio deal, counsel asks for the same ALTA endorsements on every parcel. Some states promulgate rates and forms, some do not allow certain endorsements, and some offer a state-specific substitute. The officer and the local agents answer from memory and rate manuals.

## Why It's Still Broken
Rate manuals are per underwriter and per state, filed as PDFs, and change on their own schedules. Nobody maintains a cross-state matrix as a living document.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A maintained matrix of endorsement availability, charge basis and accepted substitute by state and underwriter, extracted from filed rate manuals and checked by underwriting counsel, answerable inside the pro forma workflow: for each requested endorsement on each parcel, available or not, the state form to use instead, and the charge.

## Who Feels the Pain
Commercial title officers and their local agents; lender's counsel waiting on answers.

## Impact If Fixed
Endorsement questions are answered in minutes rather than across a day of calls, and quotes stop changing after the pro forma goes out.
