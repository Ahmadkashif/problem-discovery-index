# Transaction-Management Portals Wired to the Title File

**Niche:** [[niches/title-and-settlement-offices/residential-purchase-title-escrow/profile|Residential Purchase Title & Escrow]]
**Industry:** [[industries/title-and-settlement-offices|Title & Settlement Offices]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Real estate agents already live in transaction-management platforms that track contract milestones; the title file's curative status is the milestone those platforms cannot see.
**Tags:** #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #large-language-models #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to take a purchase file from open order to clear-to-close with every Schedule B-I requirement met before the contract closing date, and whoever scopes and clears curative fastest without a post-closing surprise wins the listing and buyer's agents who choose the title company.

## The Problem
Agents run each deal in a transaction-management platform — contract, inspection, appraisal, financing contingency, closing date. The title commitment, the open requirements and the escrow balance live in the title office's production system. The agent sees "title ordered" and nothing else until the officer calls.

## What Already Exists
Brokerage transaction-management platforms with milestone tracking and document storage; title-company client portals that show documents and signing appointments; production-system APIs that expose order status.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is a title-aware milestone feed: each open B-I requirement translated into plain language ("seller's 2004 second mortgage needs a release"), its owner and predicted clearance date pushed into the agent's platform; changes in the closing-date risk flagged to the agent before the call; and privacy rules applied so the buyer's agent sees what the buyer's agent should see and nothing from the seller's side.

## Target Customer
Title agencies whose purchase volume comes from a concentrated set of brokerages and agents.

## Impact If Solved
Agents see curative progress in the tool they already use, status calls fall, and the agency's responsiveness becomes visible to the people who choose it.
