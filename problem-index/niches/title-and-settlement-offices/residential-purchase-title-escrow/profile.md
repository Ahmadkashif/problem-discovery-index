# Residential Purchase Title & Escrow

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/title-and-settlement-offices|Title & Settlement Offices]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to take a purchase file from open order to clear-to-close with every Schedule B-I requirement met before the contract closing date, and whoever scopes and clears curative fastest without a post-closing surprise wins the listing and buyer's agents who choose the title company.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3.5B annual staff time, software and outsourced services spent by title offices on residential purchase files (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~28% — the size figure as a share of the industry's ~$12.5B receipts; purchase files account for a larger share of revenue, ~55–60% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — orders, documents and settlement statements are digital; curative and status run on email and phone
**Target Buyer:** Agency owner or escrow manager
**Automation Potential:** High for curative tracking and status, low for the examiner's and officer's final calls

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A purchase file is the industry's core product and its sales channel at once. The real estate agent, not the buyer, usually chooses the title company, and agents choose on one thing they can observe: whether the file closes on the contract date without a last-week surprise. That makes curative the competitive variable. ALTA's 2024 time study found 36% of files are difficult and take about 45 hours of work against 22 for a standard file, with payoffs, property taxes and HOA dues the most common curative actions. A refinance has no seller, no commission, no HOA estoppel and a lender who picked the agent; a purchase has all of them, plus a contract date that moves only by amendment. In attorney-closing states the same file is run by [[niches/small-law-firms/real-estate-closing-attorneys/profile|Real Estate Closing Attorneys]] under Small Law Firms; this niche is the title agent's version.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Production systems (SoftPro, Qualia, RamQuest) run the order, documents and settlement statement; real estate transaction-management platforms run the agents' side; title plants and underwriter portals run search and policy issuance. Signings are increasingly handled by mobile loan signing agents, covered under [[industries/notary-signing-services|Notary & Signing Services]]. The gaps: no forecast at order open of which requirements a file will need and how long they will take; curative status lives in the processor's notes; and agents get status by calling.
