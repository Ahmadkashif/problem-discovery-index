# Closing Cost & Title Fee Data Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/title-and-settlement-offices|Title & Settlement Offices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Vendors that maintain title rates, settlement fees, recording fees and transfer taxes for every county and many agents, and sell fee quotes to lenders through origination systems so that Loan Estimates land within TRID tolerance. ClosingCorp's SmartFees self-reports drawing on 70,000+ rate cards; LodeStar sells guaranteed closing cost estimates in all 50 states. Lenders pay per quote or by subscription; title agents are the subject of the data and sometimes pay to be listed.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 rate researchers and data analysts (estimate)
**Output:** Fee quotes by API, closing-cost benchmarks
**Proprietary data:** Assembled rate cards and fee schedules, and quote-to-actual history where lenders return final figures
**Clock:** TRID's Loan Estimate deadline, three business days after application (recalled rule) — a hard regulatory clock on the lender that the quote serves
**Buyer:** Head of data or product at the fee-data vendor — a handful of such firms, selling to lenders' compliance and origination teams
