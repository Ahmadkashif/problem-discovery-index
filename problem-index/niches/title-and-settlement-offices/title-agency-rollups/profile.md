# Title Agency Rollups & Affiliated-Business Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/title-and-settlement-offices|Title & Settlement Offices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Private-equity-backed agency platforms and the title agencies owned by real estate brokerages, homebuilders and lenders under RESPA affiliated-business arrangements. Corporate development and operations teams buy agencies and measure office performance and capture rates.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 corporate development and analytics staff (estimate)
**Output:** Acquisition models, office performance and capture-rate reporting, all internal
**Proprietary data:** Order, fee and cycle-time data across acquired offices and affiliated brokerages
**Clock:** Acquisition pipeline and month-end close
**Buyer:** No external buyer — internal cost centre
