# Title Statutory & Market Share Data Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/title-and-settlement-offices|Title & Settlement Offices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Insurance statutory-data vendors and trade publishers that compile title premiums, losses and market shares from underwriters' annual statements and sell or publish them alongside their wider insurance data.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1-5 people on title within much larger insurance-data businesses
**Output:** Market share tables, financial ratios, trade news
**Proprietary data:** None unique — statutory filings are public
**Clock:** Statutory filing calendar
**Buyer:** Head of the insurance data product
