# Escrow Reconciliation & Trust Account Monitoring Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/title-and-settlement-offices|Title & Settlement Offices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Services that pull bank and production-system data daily, perform three-way reconciliation of an agent's escrow trust accounts, and flag shortages, stale items and suspicious disbursements to the agent and, with permission, its underwriter.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analytical staff; the product is software with a support team
**Output:** Daily reconciliations and exception alerts
**Proprietary data:** Agents' trust-account activity, held for the agent
**Clock:** Daily reconciliation and month-end
**Buyer:** Founder
