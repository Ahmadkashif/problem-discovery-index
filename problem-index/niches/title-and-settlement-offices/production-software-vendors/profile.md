# Title & Escrow Production Software Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/title-and-settlement-offices|Title & Settlement Offices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
SoftPro, Qualia and RamQuest (now owned by Qualia) run orders, commitments, settlement statements and escrow ledgers for most agencies. Their product and data teams see order volume, fees, requirements and cycle times across thousands of offices, and are adding AI features such as Qualia Clear. Agencies pay licence and per-file fees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 product analysts, data scientists and data engineers (estimate)
**Output:** In-product analytics and benchmarking, AI review and drafting features
**Proprietary data:** Every order, requirement, fee and disbursement their customers process — the widest operational view of the industry
**Clock:** Customers' closing dates and the vendor's release cycle
**Buyer:** Chief product officer

**Kill switches:** **client data ownership** — the files belong to the agencies, carry consumers' nonpublic personal information, and cannot be pooled for analysis without consent; scored consistently with [[niches/real-estate-appraisers/appraisal-software-analytics/profile|Appraisal Software Analytics]] and [[niches/mortgage-brokers/los-vendor-data-teams/profile|Loan Origination System Data Teams]].
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. The vendors hold the operational record that every Pass 1 opportunity in this industry needs, and sell software, not insight.
