# FinCEN & CFPB Real Estate Settlement Oversight

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/title-and-settlement-offices|Title & Settlement Offices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The CFPB writes and enforces RESPA and TRID, which govern settlement charges, referral payments and the Closing Disclosure. FinCEN's geographic targeting orders and its Residential Real Estate Rule made settlement agents the reporting persons for cash transfers to entities and trusts; a federal court vacated the rule on 19 March 2026 in Flowers Title Companies, LLC v. Bessent.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 staff working on real estate settlement (estimate)
**Output:** Rules, guidance, enforcement actions, Bank Secrecy Act analysis
**Proprietary data:** Targeting-order filings and consumer complaints
**Clock:** Rulemaking and litigation calendars
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — federal government
