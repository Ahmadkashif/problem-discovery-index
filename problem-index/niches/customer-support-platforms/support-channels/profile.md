# Support Channels

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/customer-support-platforms|Customer Support Platforms]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every competitor is fighting to resolve a customer's issue in whichever channel they chose — but a live call and an asynchronous message are different products with different stacks and opposite constraints. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$4.8B US spend across contact channel infrastructure, ticketing and conversation management
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~34% of support software revenue
**Digital Adoption:** High — every channel is instrumented and the experience across them is not
**Target Buyer:** Support operations leaders and contact centre technology owners
**Automation Potential:** High and different on each side of the split

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Support arrives through whichever door the customer chooses, and the organisation runs a stack behind each one. The channels share a ticket record and share almost nothing else. Voice is synchronous, expensive per contact, staffed to a forecast, and technologically distinct — telephony, interactive response, speech recognition, real-time assistance — with a vendor set that overlaps only partially with the digital platforms. Messaging and asynchronous support has no session: a customer writes, leaves, and returns hours later expecting continuity, and the contest is whether the issue closes without them needing to return at all. Treating both as one product produces a ticket system with a phone attached, which is what most organisations run and why the experience differs so sharply between them.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Zendesk, Salesforce Service Cloud, Intercom, Freshdesk and Gorgias anchor the digital side; the contact centre platforms — Genesys, NICE, Five9, Amazon Connect and the telephony providers — anchor voice; several vendors span both with varying depth. The gap that spans the block is continuity: a customer who calls after messaging starts over, the agent does not see the prior conversation in a usable form, and the organisation counts two contacts. Channel switching is the most common cause of customer frustration in support and is a data joining problem rather than a technology one. What the two sides need to do about it differs, which is the substance of the decomposition below.
