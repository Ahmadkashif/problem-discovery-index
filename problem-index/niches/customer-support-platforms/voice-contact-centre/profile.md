# Voice Contact Centre

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/customer-support-platforms|Customer Support Platforms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in voice support is fighting to resolve a caller's issue without a human while they are on the line, and to make the agent faster when a human is needed — and whoever raises containment without raising repeat calls takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.9B US contact centre voice technology and real-time agent assistance
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~21% of support software revenue
**Digital Adoption:** High in infrastructure, uneven in intelligence — the least disrupted stack in the category
**Target Buyer:** Contact centre operations leaders and technology owners
**Automation Potential:** Very High — speech, intent and real-time assistance are all mature and unevenly deployed

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Voice is synchronous and expensive, and everything about it follows from those two facts. The customer is on the line, which means the system has seconds rather than minutes, no opportunity to retrieve and consider, and no tolerance for a wrong turn — a customer routed into an unhelpful automated branch will press zero, and the containment was worse than useless because it added time. Cost per contact is several times that of a digital channel, so containment is the dominant economic lever and is pursued aggressively, frequently at the expense of the customer's experience. The stack is distinct: telephony, interactive voice response, speech recognition, real-time transcription and agent assistance, staffed to a forecast by workforce management. The vendors overlap only partly with the digital support platforms. And the population that uses voice skews toward customers who could not or would not use another channel, which makes aggressive containment a fairness question as well as an economic one.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Genesys, NICE, Five9, Amazon Connect and Talkdesk anchor the platform layer; real-time transcription and agent assist products have matured quickly; workforce management is a developed adjacent category. The gaps: containment is measured as calls not reaching an agent, which counts an abandoned caller as a success — the same measurement failure as digital deflection, with a customer who was on hold; real-time agent assistance retrieves knowledge and rarely uses the call's own context; after-call work, which is a substantial share of handle time, is manual summarisation; routing is skills-based on a static skill matrix rather than on demonstrated outcome; and the interactive response menu is designed by committee and evaluated by nobody.
