# The Recovered Dispute the Rep Never Gets Back

**Niche:** [[niches/wireless-retail-agents/rep-pay-chargeback-netting/profile|Rep Pay & Chargeback Netting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/wireless-retail-agents|Wireless Retail Agents]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** When the dealer wins back a chargeback from the carrier, the rep's deduction for the same line usually stays.
**Tags:** #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #worker-facing #compliance #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (incentive-compensation platforms such as Xactly, Spiff and QCommission, payroll providers and in-house spreadsheets) is fighting to net each carrier chargeback against the right rep's advanced commission under a written plan with an audit trail that holds up under state wage law, and whoever makes every clawback explainable to the rep takes the chain's commission payroll.

## The Problem
Finance disputes a chargeback and wins it weeks later. Payroll took the deduction from the rep in the meantime. Nobody connects the two, so the dealer keeps the recovered money and the rep's pay stays reduced.

## Why It's Still Broken
Disputes and rep pay are run by different teams in different systems, and nothing in either process flags a reversal.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Tag every rep deduction with the carrier line it came from and every dispute with the same key. When a dispute closes in the dealer's favour, generate a reinstatement on the next pay run automatically and show it on the rep's statement.

## Who Feels the Pain
Reps, who lose pay they earned; payroll and HR, who face the complaint; and the chain, which is exposed if the deduction can't be justified.

## Impact If Fixed
A simple data key that restores pay owed, removes a standing grievance and closes a wage-claim exposure.
