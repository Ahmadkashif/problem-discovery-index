# Chargebacks Explained Line by Line to the Rep

**Niche:** [[niches/wireless-retail-agents/rep-pay-chargeback-netting/profile|Rep Pay & Chargeback Netting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/wireless-retail-agents|Wireless Retail Agents]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A rep sees a deduction on a paycheck and has no way to find the customer, line or reason behind it.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #survival-analysis #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #compliance #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (incentive-compensation platforms such as Xactly, Spiff and QCommission, payroll providers and in-house spreadsheets) is fighting to net each carrier chargeback against the right rep's advanced commission under a written plan with an audit trail that holds up under state wage law, and whoever makes every clawback explainable to the rep takes the chain's commission payroll.

## The Problem
Reps keep personal spreadsheets of every sale to audit their pay, and argue clawbacks with managers who can't see the carrier file either. Disputes eat manager time, and unexplained deductions drive the turnover chains complain about.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Rep statements in incentive-compensation tools show what the plan calculated, not the carrier event that triggered it, because the carrier line is never loaded. Dealers haven't demanded it, because explaining clawbacks has felt like inviting disputes.

## What to Build
A rep portal fed by the activation ledger. It shows every sale's commission status (pending, earned, at risk, charged back), the window end date, and for each chargeback the line, date, carrier reason in plain words and plan clause. It forecasts next paycheck's net with expected clawbacks, and includes a one-tap dispute when the rep believes the clawback is wrong, routed with the evidence.

## Target Customer
HR and sales-compensation leads at chains with high rep turnover, where every departure costs hiring and training.

## Impact If Built
Fewer pay disputes, a defensible record for every deduction, and reps who learn which of their deals come back.
