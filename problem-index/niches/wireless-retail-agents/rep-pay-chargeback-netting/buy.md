# Incentive-Compensation Clawback Rules Fitted to Carrier Windows

**Niche:** [[niches/wireless-retail-agents/rep-pay-chargeback-netting/profile|Rep Pay & Chargeback Netting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/wireless-retail-agents|Wireless Retail Agents]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Incentive-compensation platforms support clawbacks for software and insurance sales; wireless needs clawbacks that follow each carrier's window and reverse when the dealer wins a dispute.
**Tags:** #data-integration #evaluation-metrics #compliance #workflow-orchestration #worker-facing #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (incentive-compensation platforms such as Xactly, Spiff and QCommission, payroll providers and in-house spreadsheets) is fighting to net each carrier chargeback against the right rep's advanced commission under a written plan with an audit trail that holds up under state wage law, and whoever makes every clawback explainable to the rep takes the chain's commission payroll.

## The Problem
Generic clawback rules assume a single cancellation event and a fixed period. A wireless line can be charged back for different reasons on different windows, partially, by carrier, and then reinstated.

## What Already Exists
Xactly, Spiff, CaptivateIQ and QCommission, which offer clawback and holdback logic, rep statements and plan documents with acknowledgement.

## The Customization Gap
Needed: earning rules keyed to each carrier's and product's chargeback window; partial clawbacks for feature removal or plan downgrades; automatic reinstatement when a carrier dispute succeeds; state wage-law guardrails (written plans, acknowledgement, deduction limits and final-pay timing that vary by state); and monthly plan versioning tied to carrier plan changes.

## Target Customer
Sales-compensation managers at chains of 100+ stores already using an incentive-compensation platform.

## Impact If Solved
Clawbacks that track the carrier's actual events and the law, with less manual adjustment and less wage-claim exposure.
