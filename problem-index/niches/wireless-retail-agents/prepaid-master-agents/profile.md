# Prepaid Master Agents & Dealer Aggregators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wireless-retail-agents|Wireless Retail Agents]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Master agents hold the brand agreements and sign up independent dealers under them. They distribute devices and SIMs, pay out commissions and spiffs net of chargebacks, and handle disputes with the brand. Small dealers usually deal with the master agent, not the carrier. NWIDA began admitting master-agent members in 2025.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically under 10 analysts per master agent (estimate); the rest is payout, onboarding and logistics staff
**Output:** Dealer payouts and statements, dealer onboarding, inventory distribution
**Proprietary data:** Activation and chargeback records across their dealer base
**Clock:** Brand statement and payout cycles
**Buyer:** Founder or COO
