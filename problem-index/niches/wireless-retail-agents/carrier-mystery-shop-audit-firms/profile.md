# Carrier-Commissioned Mystery Shop & Store Audit Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wireless-retail-agents|Wireless Retail Agents]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation firms

## What They Do
Customer-experience measurement firms such as Ipsos, Intouch Insight, BARE International and Market Force run mystery-shop and store-audit programmes. Carriers commission them to visit corporate and authorised-dealer stores, check sales practices, disclosures, authentication, brand standards and wait times, and report store-level scores. The carrier pays; the results feed the dealer's scorecard and audit findings.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-100 programme managers, analysts and reporting staff on telecom retail programmes at the larger firms (estimate), above a field force of independent shoppers
**Output:** Store and rep-level shop scores, compliance findings, trend reports for the carrier's channel teams
**Proprietary data:** Years of standardised observations of wireless store behaviour across carriers and dealers, though each programme's data belongs to the carrier that commissioned it
**Clock:** The carrier's programme calendar (monthly or quarterly waves); no regulatory deadline
**Buyer:** Head of channel performance research at the measurement firm
