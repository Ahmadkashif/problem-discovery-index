# Authorised Retailer Chain Analytics & Corporate Development

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wireless-retail-agents|Wireless Retail Agents]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The BI, commission analytics and corporate-development teams at the largest exclusive chains: Victra (1,800+ Verizon stores, close to 7,000 staff), Prime Communications on AT&T, Wireless Vision on T-Mobile (backed by Gemspring Capital) and their peers. They forecast scorecard tiers, model chargebacks, plan staffing, and value the store portfolios being bought from carriers or closed. Verizon's sale of 274 corporate stores to about six operators in 2026 is exactly this team's work.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-40 analysts at the three or four largest chains (estimate); under 10 at most others
**Output:** Internal scorecard projections, chargeback and door-level P&L models, acquisition and closure cases
**Proprietary data:** Years of activation, commission, chargeback, traffic and staffing records across hundreds or thousands of stores
**Clock:** Carrier month-end, statement and dispute deadlines; carrier-set store transfer dates
**Buyer:** CFO or Chief Strategy Officer at the chain
