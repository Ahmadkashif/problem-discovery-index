# Retail Execution Apps Pointed at Carrier Audits

**Niche:** [[niches/wireless-retail-agents/store-compliance-sales-practice-audits/profile|Store Compliance & Sales-Practice Audits]]
**Industry:** [[industries/wireless-retail-agents|Wireless Retail Agents]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Restaurant and convenience chains already run store audits on retail-execution apps; wireless dealers need the same tool to cover the transactions, not just the shelves.
**Tags:** #cnns #object-detection #evaluation-metrics #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (carrier audit programmes, retail-execution apps such as Zenput and the chains' own loss-prevention teams) is fighting to produce, for every line added, feature sold and SIM changed, evidence that the customer authorised it and was authenticated, and whoever makes that evidence automatic takes the dealer's compliance budget before the carrier's next audit or complaint review.

## The Problem
District managers walk stores with paper or generic checklists for signage, device displays, cash handling and staffing. The carrier's audit also asks about sales practices and authentication, which a walk-through can't see.

## What Already Exists
Zenput (Crunchtime) and FORM run task lists, photo audits and corrective actions for multi-unit retailers; some add image recognition for display compliance.

## The Customization Gap
Needed: carrier-specific brand-standards templates, updated as carrier programmes change; photo checks for device displays and demo units by planogram; transaction-level audit tasks drawn from POS exceptions (unusual add-on rates, high post-sale removals, after-hours activations, inventory transfers between stores); and an audit-ready file per store that mirrors the carrier's audit headings.

## Target Customer
Directors of operations and loss prevention at chains of 50+ stores.

## Impact If Solved
Audit preparation becomes continuous rather than a scramble, and district managers spend visits on the stores the data flags.
