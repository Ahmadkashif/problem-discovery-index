# Consent Evidence Captured With Every Account Change

**Niche:** [[niches/wireless-retail-agents/store-compliance-sales-practice-audits/profile|Store Compliance & Sales-Practice Audits]]
**Industry:** [[industries/wireless-retail-agents|Wireless Retail Agents]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** When a customer says "I never agreed to that", the store has a signature on a long receipt and the rep's word.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #descriptive-statistics #evaluation-metrics #compliance #workflow-orchestration #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (carrier audit programmes, retail-execution apps such as Zenput and the chains' own loss-prevention teams) is fighting to produce, for every line added, feature sold and SIM changed, evidence that the customer authorised it and was authenticated, and whoever makes that evidence automatic takes the dealer's compliance budget before the carrier's next audit or complaint review.

## The Problem
Every add-on, new line, financed device and SIM change is a later dispute waiting to happen. The dealer bears the chargeback and the carrier's audit finding, and legitimate sales are lost in the argument along with crammed ones.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Activation happens in carrier systems the dealer doesn't control. Consent capture has been treated as the carrier's job, and dealers have treated it as friction.

## What to Build
At the end of each transaction, a short itemised summary in plain language on the customer-facing screen or by text, listing each line, feature, device payment and monthly total change, confirmed item by item. The confirmation is linked to the POS record with a timestamp and the rep's ID. A monitor flags reps whose add-on rates, post-sale removals or complaints are outliers against peers.

## Target Customer
Loss-prevention and compliance directors at exclusive chains; carriers' indirect-channel teams as co-sponsors.

## Impact If Built
Disputes resolved from a record rather than recollection, cramming detected in weeks rather than after the complaints pile up, and honest reps protected.
