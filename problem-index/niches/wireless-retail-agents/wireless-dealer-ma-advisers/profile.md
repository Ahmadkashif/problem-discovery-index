# Wireless Dealer M&A Advisers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wireless-retail-agents|Wireless Retail Agents]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation firms

## What They Do
Boutique investment banks, business brokers and valuation firms that sell dealer store portfolios, value dealer agreements, and advise on carrier-driven store transfers and closures. The work is episodic and spikes in years like 2026, when Verizon moved 274 corporate stores to authorised retailers and T-Mobile closed dealer doors.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 2-10 per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Sale mandates, valuation reports, fairness opinions
**Proprietary data:** Deal comparables from past transactions
**Clock:** Deal timetables and carrier approval of transfers
**Buyer:** Managing partner
