# The Spiff That Ended Last Week

**Niche:** [[niches/wireless-retail-agents/multi-carrier-prepaid-dealers/profile|Multi-Carrier Prepaid Dealers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/wireless-retail-agents|Wireless Retail Agents]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A dealer keeps pushing a brand for a spiff that was withdrawn on a week's notice and finds out when the statement arrives.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #quick-win #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (master agents and multi-carrier activation and bill-pay platforms such as T-CETRA) is fighting to put a dozen brand portals, spiff sheets and commission statements behind one counter screen, and whoever shows the owner which brand actually pays most per activation after chargebacks takes the store's activations.

## The Problem
Spiffs are announced by flyer, text, portal banner or a master agent's email, and dealer-side explainers note they "can vanish with a week of notice". A store running several brands can't keep track, and the counter keeps recommending on stale incentives.

## Why It's Still Broken
There's no common feed. Each brand and agent announces changes its own way, and the owner is also the cashier.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A shared inbox for the store's spiff and commission notices, from forwarded email, texts and photographed flyers. A language model extracts brand, product, amount, start and end dates into one current table, flags expiring spiffs, and posts the table to the counter screen.

## Who Feels the Pain
Owners and counter staff in multi-carrier stores.

## Impact If Fixed
No more selling on incentives that no longer exist, for the cost of forwarding messages to one address.
