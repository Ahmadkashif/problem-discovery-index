# Multi-Carrier Prepaid Dealers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wireless-retail-agents|Wireless Retail Agents]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (master agents and multi-carrier activation and bill-pay platforms such as T-CETRA) is fighting to put a dozen brand portals, spiff sheets and commission statements behind one counter screen, and whoever shows the owner which brand actually pays most per activation after chargebacks takes the store's activations.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$90M a year in activation platforms, bill-pay processing and back-office services across 7,000+ multi-carrier prepaid stores (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.4% of industry receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — a counter with several brand portals open, paper spiff flyers and a cash drawer
**Target Buyer:** Store owner, often running one to five stores
**Automation Potential:** Medium-High for the commission picture; low for the counter relationship

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Wave7 Research estimates more than 7,000 multi-carrier prepaid dealer stores. These are independent phone shops that sell several prepaid brands, often MVNOs such as Ultra Mobile, Simple Mobile and Lyca, along with bill payments, international top-ups, accessories and sometimes repairs. Many serve immigrant and cash-paying neighbourhoods. Wave7's quarterly survey of 30 such dealers shows how fast brand preference moves, and one dealer complained a brand "has held back commissions". The owner chooses which brand to recommend to each customer, and that choice depends on commission, spiffs and chargeback rules the owner tracks from memory. These dealers aren't exclusive and aren't franchisees, so they can be dropped quickly, as when Metro ended non-exclusive dealers in 2020 (NWIDA estimated 250–750 stores were affected). The brands are covered under [[industries/telecom-resellers|Telecom Resellers]], and their customers as [[niches/telecom-resellers/international-prepaid-subscribers/profile|🟣 International-Calling & Cash-Paying Prepaid Subscribers]]; this niche is the shop.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Multi-carrier activation and payment platforms (T-CETRA among them), master agents' portals, brand dealer portals, cash registers and spreadsheets. The gaps: no comparison of what each brand nets per activation after chargebacks and spiffs; spiff changes missed; commission statements unreconciled.
