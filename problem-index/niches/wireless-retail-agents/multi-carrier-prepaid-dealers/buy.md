# Multi-Carrier POS With a Commission Brain

**Niche:** [[niches/wireless-retail-agents/multi-carrier-prepaid-dealers/profile|Multi-Carrier Prepaid Dealers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/wireless-retail-agents|Wireless Retail Agents]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Multi-carrier activation and bill-pay platforms already sit on the dealer's counter; they process transactions but don't tell the owner what each one earned.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #evaluation-metrics #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (master agents and multi-carrier activation and bill-pay platforms such as T-CETRA) is fighting to put a dozen brand portals, spiff sheets and commission statements behind one counter screen, and whoever shows the owner which brand actually pays most per activation after chargebacks takes the store's activations.

## The Problem
The platform records every activation, top-up and bill payment across brands. The commission side (rates, spiffs, chargebacks, residuals) lives in master-agent statements and flyers.

## What Already Exists
Multi-carrier prepaid platforms such as T-CETRA, which handle activations, refills and payments for many brands from one interface, and general retail POS systems.

## The Customization Gap
Needed: commission and spiff schedules per brand and master agent with effective dates; statement import and matching against the platform's own transaction log; chargeback and residual tracking per activation; prepaid 911 fee and sales-tax liability by state; and a simple "which plan pays the store most for this customer" view, shown at the counter, that respects the customer's need first.

## Target Customer
Multi-carrier dealers already on an activation platform; the platform vendor as the buyer of the module.

## Impact If Solved
The commission picture appears on the screen the owner already uses, with no new vendor relationship.
