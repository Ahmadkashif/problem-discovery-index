# Brand Profitability After Chargebacks, Per Store

**Niche:** [[niches/wireless-retail-agents/multi-carrier-prepaid-dealers/profile|Multi-Carrier Prepaid Dealers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/wireless-retail-agents|Wireless Retail Agents]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A multi-carrier dealer steers each customer to a brand on headline commission and this week's spiff, without knowing which brand actually nets the most once chargebacks and residuals are counted.
**Tags:** #survival-analysis #gradient-boosting #large-language-models #data-integration #revenue-impact #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (master agents and multi-carrier activation and bill-pay platforms such as T-CETRA) is fighting to put a dozen brand portals, spiff sheets and commission statements behind one counter screen, and whoever shows the owner which brand actually pays most per activation after chargebacks takes the store's activations.

## The Problem
Brand A pays more up front but its customers rarely recharge, so it charges back. Brand B pays less but its customers stay and pay their bills in the store. The owner senses this from experience but can't quantify it, and spiffs shift the answer weekly.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Each master agent and platform sees only its own brands, statements are in different formats, and no single vendor has an interest in showing the owner the cross-brand comparison.

## What to Build
A store-level ledger across every brand and platform the dealer uses. It parses each statement, applies the current commission and spiff schedules, models the probability that each activation survives its chargeback window and keeps paying bill-pay fees, and shows expected net per activation by brand and plan, updated as spiffs change.

## Target Customer
Owners of one to ten multi-carrier stores, reached through dealer associations or a multi-carrier platform as a feature.

## Impact If Built
The owner's brand recommendations follow real economics, and the store sees which brands' chargebacks are eroding its margin.
