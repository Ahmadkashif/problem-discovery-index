# Carrier Commission Recovery

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wireless-retail-agents|Wireless Retail Agents]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (iQmetrix ReconcilePro, outsourced reconciliation services and spreadsheet-run back offices) is fighting to match every carrier statement line to the activation that earned it and file the dispute before the carrier's window closes, and whoever recovers the most short-paid dollars per month-end takes the dealer's reconciliation.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$150M a year in reconciliation staff time, outsourced services and software (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.7% of industry receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — matching tools exist, but PDF statements, plan changes and dispute filing are still manual
**Target Buyer:** Controller or Director of Commissions
**Automation Potential:** High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is the dealer facing the carrier. The receivable is the commission each activation should have earned under the plan in force that month, the evidence is the POS record, and the clock is the carrier's dispute window. A short payment not disputed in time is lost. iQmetrix says ReconcilePro can reach a 98% reconciliation rate when its Carrier Activation is used consistently (vendor claim), and third-party services say most POS reconciliation is incomplete. The winner is the tool or service that covers the most lines, carriers and statement formats, and files on time.

## Current Tools & Gaps
ReconcilePro inside iQmetrix RQ, Modero, Commission Tracker (whose PDF statements are keyed by hand), outsourced reconciliation shops, and Excel. The gaps: expected commission computed from hand-keyed plan rules; identifiers broken by exchanges and ports; and dispute deadlines tracked in calendars.
