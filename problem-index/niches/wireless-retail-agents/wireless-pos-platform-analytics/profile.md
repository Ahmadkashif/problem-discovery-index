# Wireless Retail POS & Activation Platform Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wireless-retail-agents|Wireless Retail Agents]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The vendors whose point-of-sale, activation and reconciliation software runs wireless stores. The largest is iQmetrix, whose RQ platform powered over 20,000 US and Canadian wireless stores by 2019 and, by its own count, $15.5B of wireless sales in 2020. Multi-carrier prepaid platforms such as T-CETRA serve the independent end. Their data and product teams build reporting, reconciliation matching and benchmark features from the transactions passing through their systems.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-40 data, analytics and reporting product staff at the largest vendor (estimate); fewer at the rest
**Output:** In-product dashboards, reconciliation matching rules, occasional industry content
**Proprietary data:** Item-level sales, activation and inventory records across thousands of dealer and carrier stores
**Clock:** Carrier month-end statement cycles that the reconciliation features must meet
**Buyer:** VP Product or Head of Data at the platform vendor
