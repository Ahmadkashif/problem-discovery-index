# Daily Net-Commission Ledger for the Prepaid Owner

**Niche:** [[niches/wireless-retail-agents/prepaid-brand-exclusive-dealers/profile|Prepaid Brand-Exclusive Dealers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/wireless-retail-agents|Wireless Retail Agents]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A prepaid dealer learns what last month really earned only when the master agent's statement and the chargebacks arrive, weeks after the rent and payroll were paid.
**Tags:** #survival-analysis #gradient-boosting #large-language-models #data-integration #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (master agents, the brands' dealer portals and prepaid POS vendors) is fighting to tell an owner-operator, store by store and day by day, what the month's activation commissions, bill-pay fees and first-recharge chargebacks will net to, and whoever makes that cash figure trustworthy takes the dealer's stores.

## The Problem
An owner with eight Cricket or Metro stores sees activations and cash daily. Commission, spiff and bill-pay fee income lands later, net of chargebacks for customers who never recharged or cancelled inside the window. Cash-flow decisions such as hiring, opening a ninth store or ordering phones get made on gross numbers that shrink weeks later.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Prepaid owners are small buyers, statement formats vary by brand and master agent, and the vendors closest to the data (master agents and portals) have little reason to show a dealer projected chargebacks.

## What to Build
A ledger that records each activation and bill payment as it happens, applies the current commission schedule, and holds back an expected chargeback reserve per activation from a recharge-survival model trained on the dealer's own history (plan, price point, payment method, port-in, store). The ledger is reconciled automatically when statements arrive. The owner sees daily expected net by store and the month's projected cheque, with the accrued 911-fee liability set aside.

## Target Customer
Owner-operators and small multi-store groups under exclusive prepaid agreements, reached through prepaid POS vendors or master agents as a white-labelled feature.

## Impact If Built
An owner who knows the month's net by the 10th rather than the 40th avoids the cash crunches that close small stores, and can see which stores and plans actually make money after chargebacks.
