# Small-Business Bookkeeping Taught Brand Statements

**Niche:** [[niches/wireless-retail-agents/prepaid-brand-exclusive-dealers/profile|Prepaid Brand-Exclusive Dealers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/wireless-retail-agents|Wireless Retail Agents]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Prepaid dealers already keep books in QuickBooks or Xero; the books show deposits, not which activation, fee or chargeback produced them.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #evaluation-metrics #automation #compliance #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (master agents, the brands' dealer portals and prepaid POS vendors) is fighting to tell an owner-operator, store by store and day by day, what the month's activation commissions, bill-pay fees and first-recharge chargebacks will net to, and whoever makes that cash figure trustworthy takes the dealer's stores.

## The Problem
The accountant books a master-agent payout as one deposit. Commissions, spiffs, bill-pay fees, residuals and chargebacks are netted inside it, along with store-level detail the owner needs and the cash-handling and 911-fee liabilities the state cares about.

## What Already Exists
QuickBooks Online and Xero with bank feeds and receipt capture, add-on revenue-recognition apps, and bookkeeping services that serve convenience-store and franchise owners.

## The Customization Gap
Needed: parsers for each brand's and master agent's statement layout that split a payout into commissions, spiffs, fees and chargebacks by store; an accrual for expected chargebacks; separate liability accounts for prepaid 911 fees and sales tax by state, with filing reminders; cash-drawer reconciliation for bill payments taken in cash; and store-level P&L classes set up for wireless dealers.

## Target Customer
Owner-operators with 2–30 stores and the small bookkeeping firms that serve them.

## Impact If Solved
Books that explain the business instead of recording deposits, and tax and fee liabilities that are always set aside, without changing the accounting system the owner and their bookkeeper already use.
