# Training Driven by Each Rep's Own Chargebacks

**Niche:** [[niches/wireless-retail-agents/the-wireless-sales-consultant/profile|The Wireless Sales Consultant]]
**Industry:** [[industries/wireless-retail-agents|Wireless Retail Agents]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Every rep gets the same training module while their own chargebacks show precisely what they keep getting wrong.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #revenue-impact #tacit-knowledge-ml
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (carrier learning portals, frontline training apps such as Axonify and the chains' own trainers) is fighting to get a rep selling this month's plans, promotions and authentication rules correctly within days of a change, and whoever cuts the errors that turn into chargebacks and complaints takes the chain's training budget.

## The Problem
One rep's deals come back because of promotion misunderstandings, another's because of feature removals, a third's because of fraud they didn't spot. Training assigns all three the same compliance module.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Chargeback causes sit in finance and training sits in HR or operations. Training vendors don't see the carrier files, and nobody has turned them into a per-rep error profile.

## What to Build
A coaching engine that turns each rep's chargebacks, removals and complaints into a short list of patterns ("customers on this promotion cancel at day 95 when the credit structure wasn't explained"). It assigns two-minute scenario drills built from the veteran managers' own explanations, and measures whether the pattern fades over the next cohort of sales.

## Target Customer
Training leads at chains with high rep churn, where shortening the time to competence pays for itself.

## Impact If Built
New reps reach a veteran's chargeback rate faster, and the chain learns which coaching actually changes outcomes.
