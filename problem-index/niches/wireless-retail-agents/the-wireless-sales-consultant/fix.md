# The Promotion Explained Wrong for Three Weeks

**Niche:** [[niches/wireless-retail-agents/the-wireless-sales-consultant/profile|The Wireless Sales Consultant]]
**Industry:** [[industries/wireless-retail-agents|Wireless Retail Agents]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** One rep's misreading of how a promotion's credits post spreads through a store's huddle, and the cancellations arrive a quarter later.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #descriptive-statistics #worker-facing #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (carrier learning portals, frontline training apps such as Axonify and the chains' own trainers) is fighting to get a rep selling this month's plans, promotions and authentication rules correctly within days of a change, and whoever cuts the errors that turn into chargebacks and complaints takes the chain's training budget.

## The Problem
Promotional device credits typically post monthly over ~24–36 months (estimate) and stop if the line is cancelled or changed. A customer who believed they got a "free phone" finds otherwise on the second bill, returns angry, cancels, and the commission charges back. The misunderstanding usually started at the counter.

## Why It's Still Broken
Promotion mechanics are dense, the store's knowledge is passed on verbally, and the feedback (cancellations) arrives months later, so nobody connects it to the explanation.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A one-paragraph, plain-language "how this promotion pays out" card generated from the promotion terms when it launches, read to or texted to the customer at sale and acknowledged. Chargebacks and complaints are then tracked by promotion to show which explanations fail.

## Who Feels the Pain
Customers surprised by their bills, reps who lose the commission, and stores that take the complaint.

## Impact If Fixed
Fewer post-promotion cancellations and complaints, from a single standard explanation per promotion.
