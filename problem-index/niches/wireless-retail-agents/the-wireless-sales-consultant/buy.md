# Microlearning Platforms Fed the Monthly Plan Change

**Niche:** [[niches/wireless-retail-agents/the-wireless-sales-consultant/profile|The Wireless Sales Consultant]]
**Industry:** [[industries/wireless-retail-agents|Wireless Retail Agents]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Frontline microlearning apps already push short daily lessons to retail staff; nobody writes the lessons from each month's carrier plan and promotion changes fast enough.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #seq2seq #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #automation #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (carrier learning portals, frontline training apps such as Axonify and the chains' own trainers) is fighting to get a rep selling this month's plans, promotions and authentication rules correctly within days of a change, and whoever cuts the errors that turn into chargebacks and complaints takes the chain's training budget.

## The Problem
Plans, promotions and eligibility rules change monthly, sometimes mid-month. Chains write their own summaries and huddle scripts by hand and reach reps days or weeks late.

## What Already Exists
Axonify and similar frontline learning platforms deliver daily questions and short lessons on phones and measure knowledge by topic; carriers run their own certification portals.

## The Customization Gap
Needed: lesson generation from the carrier's plan, promotion and policy documents on the day they arrive, with a trainer's review before release; scenario questions using real eligibility edge cases; knowledge tracking per rep linked to their chargeback causes; and an at-the-counter question box that answers from the current documents, citing the paragraph.

## Target Customer
Training and enablement leads at exclusive chains already paying for a learning platform.

## Impact If Solved
A plan change reaches every rep within days, in a form they'll actually read, without the trainer writing it from scratch.
