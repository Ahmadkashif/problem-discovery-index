# Commission Reconciliation & Chargebacks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wireless-retail-agents|Wireless Retail Agents]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor here is fighting to make each activation's commission end up where it belongs. Toward the carrier, that means recovering short-paid lines before dispute windows close, a contest won by statement-matching coverage. Toward the rep, it means netting chargebacks into pay with an audit trail that survives wage law, a contest won by payroll-grade explainability. These are two contests with different winners. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$250M a year in back-office reconciliation labour, outsourced services and commission software across the industry (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1.2% of industry receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — statements arrive as files and PDFs, matching is spreadsheet work, and rep clawbacks are calculated by hand at many chains
**Target Buyer:** CFO or Controller; Director of Commissions at larger chains
**Automation Potential:** High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
In this industry commission is revenue. Every activation creates a receivable that may be paid in full, short-paid, paid late or reversed months later, and every reversal flows on to a rep. The month-end runs on two ledgers that rarely meet: what the carrier paid against what the dealer sold, and what the dealer advanced to reps against what it has since lost. GameStop's Spring Mobile carried the first as an allowance "for chargebacks from AT&T for estimated customer cancellations", and Verizon's own store plans in the 2012 Deleon case show the second, with commissions unearned for 120 days to a year.

## Current Tools & Gaps
iQmetrix ReconcilePro, Modero, Commission Tracker, QCommission, Xactly, Spiff, outsourced reconciliation shops, and Excel. The gaps: no one ledger from carrier line to rep paycheck; comp-plan rules re-keyed by hand each month; and chargeback reasons that never reach the rep.
