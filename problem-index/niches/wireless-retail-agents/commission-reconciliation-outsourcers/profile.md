# Commission Audit & Reconciliation Outsourcers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wireless-retail-agents|Wireless Retail Agents]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation firms

## What They Do
Service firms that reconcile carrier commission statements and chargebacks for dealers who lack a back office, matching lines on phone number, IMEI, ESN or SIM and disputing invalid chargebacks. Wireless Retail Solution (RK Infotec) is one that publishes its method; most work offshore or as small teams.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Reconciliation clerks doing production work; analysts in the sense of the gate typically under 10 (estimate)
**Output:** Monthly reconciliation reports and dispute files per client
**Proprietary data:** None of their own; each client's statements and POS data
**Clock:** Carrier dispute windows
**Buyer:** Owner
