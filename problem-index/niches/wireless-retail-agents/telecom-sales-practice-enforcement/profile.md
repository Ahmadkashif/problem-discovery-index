# Telecom Sales-Practice & Account-Security Enforcement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wireless-retail-agents|Wireless Retail Agents]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The FCC's Consumer and Governmental Affairs and Enforcement bureaus, state attorneys general and state consumer-protection offices. They take complaints about wireless sales (cramming, unauthorised lines, misrepresented promotions) and bring cases, and prosecutors handle the criminal end, such as the Manhattan SIM-swap indictment that named AT&T and T-Mobile retail employees. The FCC's November 2023 SIM-swap and port-out order (WC Docket 21-341) requires carriers to authenticate customers before a SIM change or port-out and to notify them immediately, but a July 2024 bureau order (DA 24-649) deferred compliance with all of its rules until OMB approval and a Federal Register compliance-date notice, and no such notice had appeared by October 2026.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 staff per body on wireless retail sales practices (estimate): an FCC division handles all telecom consumer matters, and a state AG's consumer unit assigns a few attorneys and investigators to a telecom case when one arises
**Output:** Complaint data, investigations, consent decrees and enforcement actions
**Proprietary data:** Consumer complaint records and investigation files
**Clock:** Statutes of limitations and case schedules the agencies largely set for themselves
**Buyer:** Bureau chief or division head
