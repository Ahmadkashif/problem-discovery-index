# State Prepaid 911 Fee & Telecom Tax Administrators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wireless-retail-agents|Wireless Retail Agents]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State revenue departments and 911 boards that set, collect and audit the prepaid wireless 911 charge sellers collect per retail transaction ($0.55 in Virginia, $1.95 in Pennsylvania, a percentage of the sale in Kansas, 2% of gross receipts in South Dakota), and the sales-tax auditors who apply rules such as California's Regulation 1585 to discounted phones.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analysts per state on these fees (estimate), within larger audit divisions
**Output:** Rate notices, filing guidance, audits and assessments
**Proprietary data:** Retailer filings and audit files
**Clock:** Statutory filing dates the agencies set
**Buyer:** Division administrator
