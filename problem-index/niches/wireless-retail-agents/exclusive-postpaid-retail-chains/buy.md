# Retail Workforce Analytics Taught Carrier Scorecards

**Niche:** [[niches/wireless-retail-agents/exclusive-postpaid-retail-chains/profile|Exclusive Postpaid Retail Chains]]
**Industry:** [[industries/wireless-retail-agents|Wireless Retail Agents]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Retail analytics and workforce platforms already forecast traffic and grade stores; they grade on sales per hour, not on a carrier's tier.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #gradient-boosting #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (iQmetrix, the chains' own BI teams and incentive-compensation vendors such as Xactly) is fighting to project each store's month-end carrier scorecard and net commission from live activation, attach and early-cancellation data, and whoever gives district managers a projection accurate enough to act on by mid-month takes the chain's analytics budget.

## The Problem
Chains already pay for store analytics and labour scheduling. Those tools optimise conversion and sales per labour hour, which are not what the chain is paid on. A store can have a strong sales month and drop a tier because protection attach slipped or early cancellations spiked.

## What Already Exists
Workforce management and retail analytics from UKG, Legion and Zebra's Reflexis, traffic counting from ShopperTrak-style vendors, and BI dashboards on Tableau or Power BI fed from iQmetrix RQ.

## The Customization Gap
Needed: a scorecard metric layer with versioned definitions per carrier and month; chargeback-adjusted activation counts using expected early cancellations; traffic split by visit intent, so service visits don't depress conversion; staffing recommendations that weigh the tier impact of each hour, not just sales; and carrier calendar events (launches, promotions, plan changes) as forecast drivers.

## Target Customer
Directors of retail analytics or operations at chains already licensing a workforce or BI platform.

## Impact If Solved
The chain gets scorecard-aware scheduling and reporting from tools it already owns, without a new vendor, and stops staffing for sales the carrier doesn't pay for.
