# Exclusive Postpaid Retail Chains

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wireless-retail-agents|Wireless Retail Agents]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (iQmetrix, the chains' own BI teams and incentive-compensation vendors such as Xactly) is fighting to project each store's month-end carrier scorecard and net commission from live activation, attach and early-cancellation data, and whoever gives district managers a projection accurate enough to act on by mid-month takes the chain's analytics budget.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$450M a year in store systems, analytics, back-office and workforce tooling across ~8,000–10,000 exclusive postpaid dealer doors (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% of industry receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium-High — POS, traffic counters and carrier portals are digital; the scorecard itself arrives after the month and is projected in spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** COO or VP Retail Operations at a multi-hundred-store chain
**Automation Potential:** High for projection and staffing; low for what happens on the floor

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
These are the carrier-branded stores most customers can't tell from corporate ones: Victra with 1,800+ Verizon stores, Cellular Sales, Russell Cellular and Round Room on Verizon, Prime Communications on AT&T (which bought Spring Mobile's ~1,284 stores in 2019), and Wireless Vision, Arch Telecom and GP Mobile on T-Mobile. Each is exclusive to one carrier, and its revenue is that carrier's comp plan multiplied by its scorecard tier. The chain's whole management system (district managers, bonuses, which stores to open or close) runs on a scorecard it can only see after the month ends. In 2026 the stakes rose: Verizon is moving 274 corporate stores to about six authorised retailers, and T-Mobile is closing dealer doors.

## Current Tools & Gaps
iQmetrix RQ or similar POS, carrier activation portals, Tableau or Power BI dashboards built by small in-house teams, workforce management from UKG or Legion, and incentive-compensation software for rep pay. The gaps: no tool projects the carrier's scorecard and the chargeback-adjusted commission mid-month; scorecard definitions change with the plan; and door-level economics are reconstructed by hand when a store sale or closure is on the table.
