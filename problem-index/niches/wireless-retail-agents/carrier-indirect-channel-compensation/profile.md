# Carrier Indirect-Channel & Dealer Compensation Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wireless-retail-agents|Wireless Retail Agents]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The teams inside Verizon, AT&T, T-Mobile and the prepaid brands that run the authorised-dealer channel. They design comp plans and spiffs, set scorecards and tiers, approve and close doors, audit stores, and measure dealers' chargebacks and fraud. They are the industry's payer: every dollar of dealer commission is set here. In 2026 these teams were moving doors in opposite directions, with T-Mobile closing dealer stores (a $108M second-quarter charge) and Verizon handing 274 corporate stores to authorised retailers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-100 channel analysts, compensation designers and dealer-performance staff per national carrier (estimate)
**Output:** Comp plans, scorecards, dealer performance and fraud analytics, door-portfolio decisions
**Proprietary data:** Every activation, chargeback, survey and audit result across the carrier's whole dealer channel
**Clock:** The carrier's own monthly plan cycle and quarterly earnings; no external deadline on the analysis
**Buyer:** VP Indirect Channel or Head of Dealer Compensation
