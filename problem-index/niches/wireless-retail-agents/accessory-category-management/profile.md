# Accessory Suppliers' Carrier-Channel Category Management

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wireless-retail-agents|Wireless Retail Agents]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Case, screen-protector, charger and audio brands such as OtterBox and ZAGG, and the distributors that supply carrier and dealer stores, run category-management teams. These teams recommend assortments and planograms for each device launch, analyse sell-through by store, and argue for wall space. Accessories are one of the few products where the dealer keeps a merchandise margin, and accessory attach is a common scorecard metric (estimate).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-30 category, pricing and sales analysts per larger accessory brand or distributor (estimate)
**Output:** Launch assortments, planogram recommendations, sell-through and attach analyses for carrier and dealer buyers
**Proprietary data:** Sell-in history by device model, and sell-through data shared by some carrier and dealer accounts
**Clock:** Handset makers' launch dates, which the accessories must meet on shelf
**Buyer:** VP Category Management or Head of Channel Insights at the supplier

## Scorecard
| Criterion | Weight | Score |
|---|---|---|

| Q2 Labor mass + repeatable | ×2 | 3 |

| **Weighted total** | | **35/60** |

**Kill switches:** none — sell-in data is the supplier's own; account-shared sell-through is used under the account's terms, which limits but doesn't block the work.
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. Launch dates set a real external clock, but the analysis supports selling accessories rather than being sold, and much of the best data belongs to the retail accounts. Compare [[niches/greeting-card-publishers/publisher-category-insight-teams/profile|🔍 Publisher Category Management & Consumer Insight Teams]] (34, kill switch), whose work rests on two or three retailers' scan data; here the core dataset is the supplier's own sell-in across many carrier and dealer accounts, so the retail terms limit the work without blocking it.
