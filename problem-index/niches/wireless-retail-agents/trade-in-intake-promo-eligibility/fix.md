# The Promised Credit That Shrinks on the Bill

**Niche:** [[niches/wireless-retail-agents/trade-in-intake-promo-eligibility/profile|Trade-In Intake & Promo Credit Eligibility]]
**Industry:** [[industries/wireless-retail-agents|Wireless Retail Agents]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A customer is told their old phone is worth the full promotional credit, the processing centre grades it down, and the store takes the blame.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #cnns #worker-facing #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (trade-in administrators' in-store tools and device diagnostic vendors such as Phonecheck) is fighting to grade a traded-in phone at the counter the same way the processing centre will grade it weeks later, and whoever closes the gap between promised and paid credit takes the dealer's trade-in workflow.

## The Problem
The store has no record of the phone's condition when it was handed over, so it can't challenge a downgrade or show the customer what was found. Downgrades become complaints, and some become cancellations.

## Why It's Still Broken
Taking photos is extra work at a busy counter, and nothing in the flow asks for it. The downgrade notice goes to the customer, not the store.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Four standard photos per traded-in phone, taken in a fixed spot and attached to the transaction, with the customer's acknowledgement of visible defects. A monthly report of downgrades by store and rep, matched to the photos, shows where grading habits slip.

## Who Feels the Pain
Customers who expected the full credit; reps who absorb the anger; stores that lose lines to the dispute.

## Impact If Fixed
Disputes resolved from evidence, honest expectations set at the counter, and a small but real reduction in trade-in-driven cancellations.
