# Trade-In Intake & Promo Credit Eligibility

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wireless-retail-agents|Wireless Retail Agents]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (trade-in administrators' in-store tools and device diagnostic vendors such as Phonecheck) is fighting to grade a traded-in phone at the counter the same way the processing centre will grade it weeks later, and whoever closes the gap between promised and paid credit takes the dealer's trade-in workflow.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$80M a year in counter time on trade-in intake, diagnostics tools and dispute handling (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.4% of industry receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — IMEI checks and promotion rules are digital; condition grading is a rep looking at the phone
**Target Buyer:** VP Retail Operations
**Automation Potential:** High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Most flagship promotions now hinge on a trade-in: the customer's credit depends on the model, its condition and whether the account and plan qualify. The rep checks eligibility, inspects the phone, wipes it and ships it. Weeks later the processing centre run by the carrier's trade-in administrator grades it. If the grade is lower (a hairline crack, a locked account, a different model), the credit shrinks on the customer's bill and the customer comes back to the store, not the carrier. The administrators themselves are analysed as [[niches/wireless-carriers/device-protection-trade-in/profile|🔍 Device Protection & Trade-In Programme Administrators]] (42) under wireless carriers. Warehouse-side grading for resale is [[niches/recommerce-platforms/condition-grading/profile|🎯 Condition Grading]] under recommerce platforms, and repair belongs to the queued electronics-repair-shops industry. This niche is the dealer's intake step.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Carrier trade-in tools in the activation flow, IMEI blacklist and lock checks, device diagnostic apps from vendors such as Phonecheck, and data-erasure tools. The gaps: condition graded by eye against definitions the rep half-remembers; no photo record; promotion eligibility rules that change monthly; and no feedback from the final grade to the rep.
