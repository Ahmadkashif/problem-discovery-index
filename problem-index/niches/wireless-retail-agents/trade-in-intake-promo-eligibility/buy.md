# Device Diagnostics Apps Tuned to Carrier Programme Grades

**Niche:** [[niches/wireless-retail-agents/trade-in-intake-promo-eligibility/profile|Trade-In Intake & Promo Credit Eligibility]]
**Industry:** [[industries/wireless-retail-agents|Wireless Retail Agents]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Device diagnostics apps already test a phone's functions and erase it for resale; they report a generic condition, not the grade a specific carrier promotion pays on.
**Tags:** #cnns #decision-trees #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (trade-in administrators' in-store tools and device diagnostic vendors such as Phonecheck) is fighting to grade a traded-in phone at the counter the same way the processing centre will grade it weeks later, and whoever closes the gap between promised and paid credit takes the dealer's trade-in workflow.

## The Problem
Dealers that use diagnostics apps get a functional test and an erasure certificate, but still grade the cosmetic condition and check promotion eligibility by hand.

## What Already Exists
Device diagnostic and certification tools from vendors such as Phonecheck and Blancco, used across refurbishers, retailers and carriers, plus IMEI blacklist and activation-lock checks.

## The Customization Gap
Needed: mapping of diagnostic results and photos to each carrier programme's grade definitions; promotion eligibility rules (model, account standing, plan, line type) updated monthly; a single result that feeds the activation flow; and a record kept for later disputes with the administrator.

## Target Customer
Chains already licensing a diagnostics tool for trade-ins and returns.

## Impact If Solved
One intake step that tests the device, grades it the way the programme will, and confirms eligibility, using a tool already on the counter.
