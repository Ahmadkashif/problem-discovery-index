# Bank-Grade Identity Verification at the Wireless Counter

**Niche:** [[niches/wireless-retail-agents/activation-and-port-in-processing/profile|Activation & Port-In Processing]]
**Industry:** [[industries/wireless-retail-agents|Wireless Retail Agents]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Banks and fintechs verify identities in seconds with document scans and selfie checks; wireless counters still rely on a rep eyeballing a licence before handing over thousands of dollars of financed phones.
**Tags:** #cnns #object-detection #gradient-boosting #evaluation-metrics #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (the carriers' own activation tools, iQmetrix's multi-carrier activation and device-setup vendors) is fighting to take a new line from ID check to a working, ported phone with its data moved in ~20 minutes without raising fraud, and whoever cuts the most counter minutes per activation takes the dealer's counter.

## The Problem
New-line fraud with stolen or synthetic identities leaves the dealer with a chargeback and a lost device, and the rep's visual check is both slow and unreliable. Slowing every customer down to catch the few is not acceptable either.

## What Already Exists
Document verification and liveness checks from identity vendors such as Socure and Jumio, used in bank and fintech onboarding, plus the ID scanners some stores already have.

## The Customization Gap
Needed: verification that runs within the counter flow in seconds on a store tablet; risk-tiered step-up, so most customers see nothing extra; deal-level signals (device count, financing, port-in tenure) combined with the identity result; outputs that match the carrier's own ID requirements and are recorded for chargeback disputes; and privacy handling that respects the carrier's control of customer data.

## Target Customer
Loss-prevention directors at chains with rising fraud chargebacks.

## Impact If Solved
Fewer fraud chargebacks and lost devices without adding minutes for genuine customers.
