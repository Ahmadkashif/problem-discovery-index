# One Guided Flow From ID Scan to Working Phone

**Industry:** [[industries/wireless-retail-agents|Wireless Retail Agents]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A rep types the same customer details into several screens and waits on each one, while the next customer leaves.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #time-series-forecasting #evaluation-metrics #automation #workflow-orchestration #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (the carriers' own activation tools, iQmetrix's multi-carrier activation and device-setup vendors) is fighting to take a new line from ID check to a working, ported phone with its data moved in ~20 minutes without raising fraud, and whoever cuts the most counter minutes per activation takes the dealer's counter.

## The Problem
Activation time is the counter's binding constraint on launch days, weekends and the tax-refund season. Much of it is re-entry and waiting: the same name and address in the POS, the carrier portal and the financing form, and a port request submitted only after the plan is chosen.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Carrier systems are separate and access-controlled, carriers change their flows, and the dealer can automate only around them, not inside them. Each carrier's dealer channel is a small market for an integrator.

## What to Build
A guided counter flow that captures the customer's details once (ID scan and a short form on the customer's own phone before they reach the counter), pre-validates port-out details with the customer while they wait, pre-fills each carrier and financing screen where the carrier permits it, runs device setup and data transfer in parallel with the paperwork, and times every step so the dealer can see where minutes go.

## Target Customer
Operations leaders at chains with long weekend queues; carriers' indirect-channel teams, who gain from faster dealer activations.

## Impact If Built
If activation drops from ~60 to ~35 minutes (estimate), each counter handles roughly 70% more activations at peak, with less customer abandonment.
