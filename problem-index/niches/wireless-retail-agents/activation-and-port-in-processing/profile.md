# Activation & Port-In Processing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wireless-retail-agents|Wireless Retail Agents]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (the carriers' own activation tools, iQmetrix's multi-carrier activation and device-setup vendors) is fighting to take a new line from ID check to a working, ported phone with its data moved in ~20 minutes without raising fraud, and whoever cuts the most counter minutes per activation takes the dealer's counter.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$350M a year in counter labour spent on mechanical activation steps plus activation and setup tooling (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1.7% of industry receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — every step is digital, but spread across separate carrier, financing, porting and setup screens
**Target Buyer:** VP Retail Operations
**Automation Potential:** Very high for the mechanical steps; identity judgement stays with a person

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A new postpaid line with a port-in, a financed device, a trade-in and a data transfer can take ~45–90 minutes at the counter (estimate). That means scanning ID, running the carrier credit check, choosing plan and promotion, signing device-payment agreements, entering the port-out account number and PIN from the old carrier, activating the SIM or eSIM, moving photos and apps, and setting up protection. Each step is mechanical and repeated thousands of times a day, and every minute costs a rep a customer waiting behind. Port and SIM-change security is the carrier's job, analysed as [[niches/wireless-carriers/porting-sim-account-security/profile|⚡ Number Porting, SIM Changes & Account Takeover Defence]] under wireless carriers, and resellers' porting back offices as [[niches/telecom-resellers/number-porting-provisioning/profile|🟠 Number Porting & Provisioning Desk]] under telecom resellers. This niche is the dealer's counter flow around them, where speed matters and where the carrier's authentication steps have to fit in.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Carrier activation portals, iQmetrix's Carrier Activation within RQ, ID scanners, eSIM transfer and device-setup tools from handset makers and carriers, and paper checklists. The gaps: re-keying between screens; port-ins that fail after the customer has left because the account number or PIN was wrong; and no measurement of where the minutes go.
