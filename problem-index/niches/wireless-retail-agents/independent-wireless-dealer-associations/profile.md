# Independent Wireless Dealer Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wireless-retail-agents|Wireless Retail Agents]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The National Wireless Independent Dealer Association (NWIDA), which merged with WiBOC in 2019, says it supports 40,000+ independent wireless sales and repair businesses. It sells member discounts on devices, parts, payroll and benefits, lobbies on right to repair, and spoke for Metro dealers when T-Mobile ended non-exclusive dealers in 2020. It publishes little research.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 staff (estimate)
**Output:** Member programmes, advocacy statements, occasional estimates
**Proprietary data:** Member lists and informal surveys
**Clock:** Self-set; legislative sessions for advocacy
**Buyer:** Executive director
