# University Research Development Offices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/grant-writers|Grant Writers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
The research development and sponsored programmes offices at research universities help faculty identify opportunities, structure proposals, assemble large multi-investigator submissions, and satisfy the compliance requirements attached to federal awards. They are the largest concentration of grant expertise anywhere.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-200 research development specialists, proposal managers, and compliance staff per major research university
**Output:** Opportunity identification, proposal development support, submission and compliance management
**Proprietary data:** Complete proposal and award history for the institution — submitted, funded, declined, with reviewer summaries where returned
**Clock:** Federal submission deadlines, which are absolute
**Buyer:** Vice President for Research — institutional budget
