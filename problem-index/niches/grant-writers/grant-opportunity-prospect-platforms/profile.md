# Grant Opportunity & Funder Research Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/grant-writers|Grant Writers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Instrumentl, GrantStation, and the funder research platforms assemble and enrich funder profiles — giving history, priorities, typical award size, board relationships, deadlines — and match them to a nonprofit's programmes. The Pass 1 analysis states the problem exactly: a federal proposal takes 40-80 hours at a 10-25% win rate, and most firms have no systematic way to assess which opportunities are worth pursuing before investing weeks.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 research analysts, funder researchers, and data staff
**Output:** Funder profiles, opportunity matching, deadline and eligibility intelligence — the subscription
**Proprietary data:** Enriched funder profiles built on public filings plus researcher-added intelligence on priorities, process, and relationships that appears in no filing
**Clock:** Application deadlines, which are absolute and non-negotiable
**Buyer:** VP of Research / Chief Product Officer
