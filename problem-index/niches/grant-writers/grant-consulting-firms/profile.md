# Grant Consulting Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/grant-writers|Grant Writers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
The operator layer itself: firms of two to ten writers producing proposals for nonprofits, universities, and agencies. The written proposal is unambiguously the invoice, the deadline is absolute, and the win rate is 10-30% — the shape this sweep looks for, at a scale that cannot support a research function.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 2-10 writers, of whom the research is done by whoever is on the proposal
**Output:** Grant proposals, opportunity assessments, funder research
**Proprietary data:** The firm's own submission and outcome history, and its accumulated sense of what each funder responds to — held in individual writers' heads
**Clock:** Submission deadlines, absolute
**Buyer:** Principal
