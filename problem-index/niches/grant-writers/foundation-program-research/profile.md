# Foundation Programme Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/grant-writers|Grant Writers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Large foundations employ programme officers and strategy staff who research fields, set funding priorities, evaluate proposals, and assess what their grantmaking accomplished — the decisions every grant proposal in the sector is written toward.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-200 programme officers, researchers, and evaluation staff at the largest foundations
**Output:** Funding strategies, proposal assessments, portfolio evaluations
**Proprietary data:** Complete proposal and outcome history across a portfolio, with the reasoning behind every declination
**Clock:** Board meeting and docket cycles
**Buyer:** Vice President of Programmes — endowment funded
