# Federal Award Data Enrichment

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/grant-writers|Grant Writers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Providers take the federal spending and award disclosure data and make it usable — resolving recipients into organizations, classifying programmes, and building the competitive intelligence that shows who has won what from which agency.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 data engineers, analysts, and researchers
**Output:** Structured award data, recipient resolution, competitive award analysis
**Proprietary data:** Entity resolution and classification over a public award corpus
**Clock:** Data release cycles and proposal deadlines
**Buyer:** Head of Data Products
