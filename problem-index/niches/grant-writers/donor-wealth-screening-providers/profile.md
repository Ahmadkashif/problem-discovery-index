# Donor Wealth Screening Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/grant-writers|Grant Writers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
DonorSearch, iWave, and WealthEngine assemble wealth and philanthropic capacity indicators — property, securities filings, giving history, board affiliations — and score a nonprofit's constituent file for major gift potential. Development teams prioritize their entire outreach against the result.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 data analysts, research staff, and data scientists
**Output:** Capacity and propensity scores, prospect profiles, portfolio segmentation
**Proprietary data:** Assembled wealth indicators joined to observed giving across the client base, plus matching logic across sparse identifiers
**Clock:** Campaign calendars and fiscal year end
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Research
