# Federal Peer Review Administration

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/grant-writers|Grant Writers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Federal agency review offices recruit reviewers, assign proposals, run the panels that score them, and administer the process that decides how hundreds of billions in research and programme funding is allocated.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,500 scientific review officers and programme staff per large agency
**Output:** Review panel administration, scoring and summary statements, funding recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Every proposal submitted, every reviewer score, and every summary statement — the definitive record of what gets funded and why, confidential by policy
**Clock:** Council and review cycles with fixed dates
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer
