# Single Audit & Uniform Guidance Compliance

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/grant-writers|Grant Writers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Accounting firms perform the federal award audits organizations spending above a threshold must obtain, testing compliance with the uniform guidance requirements attached to every federal grant. Findings restrict future funding, and the report is filed federally on a statutory deadline.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-500 auditors and compliance specialists in the larger practices
**Output:** Single audit reports, compliance findings, corrective action recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated finding patterns across grantees and programmes — held under client confidentiality, though the filed reports themselves are public
**Clock:** Statutory submission deadlines with funding consequences for late filing
**Buyer:** Practice leader / National Director of Government Services
