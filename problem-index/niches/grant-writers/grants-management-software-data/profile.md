# Grants Management Software Data Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/grant-writers|Grant Writers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The data groups at Fluxx, Submittable, and the grantmaking platforms process applications and awards on behalf of foundations, and see the application-to-decision pipeline across many funders and thousands of applicants.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 data and product staff
**Output:** In-product analytics, funder benchmarking, application pipeline reporting
**Proprietary data:** Applications joined to funding decisions across many funders — the only view of what actually gets funded and what does not
**Clock:** Grant cycle deadlines
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Chief Product Officer
