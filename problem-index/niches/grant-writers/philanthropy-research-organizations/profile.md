# Philanthropy Research Organizations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/grant-writers|Grant Writers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The philanthropy research bodies track giving levels, funder priorities, and sector trends, publishing the annual studies that describe where charitable money is going and to whom.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 researchers, economists, and analysts
**Output:** Giving estimates, funder trend research, sector reports
**Proprietary data:** Long-running giving estimation series and funder survey data
**Clock:** Annual publication cycles
**Buyer:** Director of Research — philanthropically funded
