# Grant Professional Certification Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/grant-writers|Grant Writers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The grant professional associations define the competencies the field is certified against, run the credentialing examinations, and publish the ethical and practice standards the profession works to.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-25 certification and standards staff
**Output:** Competency frameworks, certification examinations, practice standards
**Proprietary data:** Candidate performance data and the job analyses defining the role
**Clock:** Examination and recertification cycles
**Buyer:** Director of Certification
