# Market Integrity

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/virtual-economy-operators|Virtual Economy Operators]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to police a market with real monetary value using rules written for stolen credit cards — and whoever brings real surveillance to it takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.5B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~21% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — reactive
**Target Buyer:** Trust and compliance leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — surveillance and controls

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Item markets with real value attract exactly what financial markets attract: wash trading, coordinated price manipulation, cornering of limited supply, and the use of trades to move value between parties for reasons unconnected to the game. The operator polices this with fraud rules built for payment abuse, reacting to reports rather than surveilling the market. The complete order and trade record sits in its systems and is used for chargeback response.

### Contested sub-niches
This niche is not terminal. It names a domain, and two regimes with different law and different evidence sit inside it:
- [[niches/virtual-economy-operators/manipulation-detection/profile|🎯 Manipulation Detection]] — are the trades genuine
- [[niches/virtual-economy-operators/value-transfer-controls/profile|🎯 Value Transfer Controls]] — is the economy being used as a payment rail

## Current Tools & Gaps
Payment fraud rules, a reports queue, trade holds and account bans. The gaps: no market surveillance; no wash trade detection; no manipulation patterns; no identity or threshold framework; and no regulatory position on what the operator is actually running.
