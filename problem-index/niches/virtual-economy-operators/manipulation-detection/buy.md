**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell a genuine trade from a manufactured one across accounts that are pseudonymous and plentiful — and whoever builds that surveillance takes the account.

## The Problem
Market surveillance vendors sell tested libraries of manipulation detectors — wash trading, layering, spoofing, marking the close, ramping, cornering, front running — refined over decades of regulatory enforcement and calibrated against confirmed cases. The taxonomy is public, the detection logic is documented in regulatory guidance, and the false-positive behaviour is understood. Virtual item markets face substantially the same abuses and have never even catalogued them.

## What Already Exists
Manipulation pattern taxonomies and detector libraries; calibration against confirmed enforcement cases; alert scoring and triage; case management with evidence retention; and regulatory-grade reporting.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a market with no order book in the traditional sense and no identity behind accounts. It requires: (1) pseudonymous participants with unlimited accounts, so linkage analysis must run before any pattern detector means anything — this is the substantive difference and it inverts the usual order of work; (2) trading mechanics that are often direct peer-to-peer swaps rather than a central limit order book, which several standard detectors assume; (3) items rather than fungible instruments, so each item is a thin market of its own; (4) no confirmed-case corpus to calibrate against, since nobody has enforced; and (5) participants who include minors and casual players, changing what an appropriate response looks like.

## Target Customer
Virtual economy operators, third-party marketplaces, market operations teams, and surveillance vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Exchange surveillance ships tested detector libraries refined against decades of enforcement. Pseudonymous accounts mean linkage analysis has to run first, and item-level thin markets are what the standard detectors do not assume.
