# Manipulation Detection

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/virtual-economy-operators|Virtual Economy Operators]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell a genuine trade from a manufactured one across accounts that are pseudonymous and plentiful — and whoever builds that surveillance takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.4B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — nothing runs
**Target Buyer:** Market operations and trust leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very high — graph and sequence analysis over the trade record

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This half is answerable from data the operator already holds. Every order, every trade, every price and every counterparty is recorded; the question is whether the activity is genuine. It is a surveillance and graph analysis problem, deployable without any change in the operator's legal posture, and valuable purely for protecting the price data other participants rely on. An operator can run it entirely as market operations and never touch the question of what the economy is being used for.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A payment fraud engine and a reports queue. The gaps: no trade graph analysis; no wash trading detection; no ramping or cornering patterns; no account linkage; and no exclusion of manipulated trades from published prices.
