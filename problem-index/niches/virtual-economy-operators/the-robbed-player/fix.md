# The Agent Can See Exactly What Happened

**Niche:** [[niches/virtual-economy-operators/the-robbed-player/profile|The Robbed Player]]
**Industry:** [[industries/virtual-economy-operators|Virtual Economy Operators]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The support agent is looking at the login from a new country, the eleven trades in four minutes, and a policy that says there is nothing they can do.
**Tags:** #worker-facing #quick-win #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation #evaluation-metrics #descriptive-statistics #confidence-intervals
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give a player whose inventory was stolen something other than a policy saying nothing can be returned — and whoever builds that recovery takes the account.

## The Problem
The support agent has the full picture on screen: an unfamiliar device, a login from elsewhere, the password change, and every item transferred out in a four-minute window. It is unambiguous. The policy permits nothing. The agent sends a message about account security to a person who has lost a large amount of money, and does this repeatedly, and it is the worst part of the job.

## Why It's Still Broken
The policy was written for simplicity rather than for cases — a blanket rule spares the operator every difficult judgement and forces the agent to deliver a refusal they can see is wrong. Nobody scoped a limited exception. The reversal mechanism does not exist. And there is no route for the agent to escalate a clear case.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Carve out the unambiguous cases and give the agent a path. Define a narrow clear-compromise exception with explicit evidence criteria, which is the fix and covers the cases that are actually unambiguous. Give agents an escalation route for cases meeting it, since the absence of any path is what makes the role intolerable. Freeze the destination accounts immediately on a credible report, which preserves options and costs nothing if the report is wrong. Automate the evidence assembly — device, location, session, trade sequence — so the review is minutes rather than hours. Set a fast decision window, because recovery probability falls sharply with time. Act on the receiving accounts consistently, which is where the deterrent effect comes from. Track how many cases meet the clear-compromise criteria, as that number will be smaller than feared and will make the exception affordable. Tell the player what was decided and why rather than quoting a policy. Support the agent explicitly in a role where they deliver bad news they disagree with. And publish the exception so players know it exists, which also deters false claims by making the criteria explicit.

## Who Feels the Pain
Players who lost thousands and were told no; support agents delivering refusals they can see are wrong; operators whose reputation absorbs each case; and the thieves, who face nothing.

## Impact If Fixed
A blanket rule spares the operator every difficult judgement and forces the agent to deliver a refusal they can see is wrong. A narrow clear-compromise exception with automated evidence assembly covers the cases that were never ambiguous.
