**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to set drop rates, sinks and limited releases without a forecast of what they do to prices people pay real money at — and whoever supplies that forecast takes the account.

## The Problem
Monetary authorities built the apparatus for exactly this: forecasting models linking policy instruments to price outcomes, transmission mechanism analysis, scenario projection with fan charts, and deliberate communication because expectations themselves move markets. The methods are public and extensively documented. A virtual economy operator with a complete transaction record and direct control of issuance has a far cleaner version of the same problem and none of the apparatus.

## What Already Exists
Policy transmission modelling; inflation forecasting with uncertainty fans; scenario analysis under alternative policy paths; distributional impact analysis; and forward guidance and communication practice.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to an authority that also controls supply, demand and the rules simultaneously. It requires: (1) instruments that are drop rates and sinks rather than interest rates, acting directly on quantity rather than through a transmission mechanism — this is the substantive difference and makes the effects faster and more direct; (2) complete observation of every transaction, removing the estimation and sampling machinery that dominates real central banking; (3) an objective that is player experience and revenue rather than price stability; (4) a population that enters and exits continuously, so the distributional question is about new entrants versus incumbents; and (5) an operator who has strong commercial reasons not to publish a policy it would then be held to.

## Target Customer
Virtual economy operators, platform economy designers, secondary marketplaces, and economic modelling consultancies.

## Impact If Solved
Central banking built transmission modelling and forecasting because supply decisions move prices. Instruments acting directly on quantity, with complete observation, make the virtual version cleaner — and the reluctance is commercial rather than technical.
