# Monetary Policy & Supply Design

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/virtual-economy-operators|Virtual Economy Operators]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to set drop rates, sinks and limited releases without a forecast of what they do to prices people pay real money at — and whoever supplies that forecast takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3.5B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~29% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — instinct
**Target Buyer:** Economy design leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — supply modelling and price forecasting

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Drop rates are money supply. Sinks are withdrawal. Limited releases are issuance. A rarity change is a revaluation. Prices in the secondary market respond to all of it, and the people setting those parameters are designers optimising engagement with no forecast of the price effects and frequently no visibility of the secondary market at all. The operator is conducting monetary policy and does not describe it that way, which is precisely why nobody has built the instruments for it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A drop table, an engagement dashboard and a revenue report. The gaps: no model linking supply decisions to secondary prices; no price index; no visibility of the secondary market at design time; no forecast before a rarity change; and no record of past decisions against their price consequences.
