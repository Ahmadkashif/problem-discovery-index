## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$700M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~6% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — game-grade
**Target Buyer:** Security engineering
**Automation Potential:** Very high — authentication, holds and anomaly response

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Inventories worth real money are protected by account security designed for game logins. The account holds assets with genuine value, and the protections around it — optional two-factor, a password reset flow, no transaction-level authentication, no holds on high-value transfers — are the standards of a consumer entertainment product rather than of a custodian. Every control that would fix this exists off the shelf, and the reason they are not applied is friction rather than difficulty.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Optional two-factor, email password reset, a support recovery path and a device list. The gaps: no transaction-level authentication for high-value trades; no holds on transfers after a credential change; no anomaly-triggered lock; no protection defaults scaled to inventory value; and account recovery that is itself an attack vector.
