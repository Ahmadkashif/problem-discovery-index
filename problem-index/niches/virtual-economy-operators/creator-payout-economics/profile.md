# Creator Payout Economics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/virtual-economy-operators|Virtual Economy Operators]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make a creator economy legible to the people earning in it, when the platform issues the currency, sets the rate, sizes the pool and controls discovery — and whoever makes it legible takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~17% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — opaque
**Target Buyer:** Creator relations leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — forecasting, transparency and planning

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A creator earning inside a platform economy is paid in a currency the platform issues, at a rate the platform sets, from a pool the platform sizes, with discovery controlled by an algorithm they cannot see. Every variable in their business belongs to one counterparty. They cannot forecast income, cannot diagnose a decline, cannot plan, and have no recourse. The platform's own interest is in a supply of creators who keep producing, which this arrangement steadily undermines.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A creator dashboard showing earnings to date, a payout schedule and a help centre. The gaps: no forward visibility of the pool or the rate; no explanation of earnings changes; no discovery diagnostics; no notice of rate or algorithm changes; and no comparable benchmarks.
