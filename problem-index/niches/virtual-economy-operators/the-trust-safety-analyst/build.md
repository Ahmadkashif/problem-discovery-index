# Case Tooling for Financial Decisions

**Niche:** [[niches/virtual-economy-operators/the-trust-safety-analyst/profile|The Trust & Safety Analyst]]
**Industry:** [[industries/virtual-economy-operators|Virtual Economy Operators]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** The person confiscating thousands of dollars of goods is working in a moderation queue.
**Tags:** #worker-facing #graph-theory #compliance #workflow-orchestration #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #automation #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to equip the person making financial-crime-grade decisions about accounts with tools built for game abuse — and whoever re-equips them takes the account.

## The Problem
An analyst opens a case. The decision in front of them may remove an inventory worth several thousand dollars, end an account someone has held for a decade, or conclude that a trade pattern is laundering. The interface shows a report, an account and a ban button. There is no trade graph, no valuation, no linkage view, no decision framework, and no record of how comparable cases were decided. The consequences are financial; the instrument is a moderation tool.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Trust and safety tooling was built for content and conduct, and the economy cases were absorbed into it without re-scoping. The analyst population is small. The cost of a wrong decision falls on the user. And nobody has classified these as financial decisions requiring a different standard.

## What to Build
Give the analyst the evidence, the value and the framework. Put the trade graph and account linkage in the case view rather than requiring a separate query, which is the core — a decision about a trading pattern made without seeing the pattern is a guess. Show the monetary value of what a decision would confiscate, since an analyst acting without knowing the magnitude cannot apply proportionality. Provide a decision framework with defined thresholds and required evidence per outcome, which is what makes decisions consistent and defensible. Retain the evidence and reasoning for every decision, as these are disputed and the operator's position must survive scrutiny. Surface comparable past cases and their outcomes, which is how calibration happens in practice. Support graduated outcomes — hold, restrict, partially reverse — rather than a binary ban, because the binary forces overreach or inaction. Measure consistency across analysts and feed it back, which no team currently does. Build a genuine appeals path with a different reviewer, since the absence of one is what makes errors permanent. Flag cases where the value crosses a threshold requiring a second reviewer. And train for the financial nature of the work rather than for content moderation, which is what the role has quietly become.

## Target Customer
Virtual economy operators, trust and safety leadership, third-party marketplaces, and investigation and case management vendors.

## Impact If Built
A decision about a trading pattern made without seeing the pattern is a guess, and a confiscation made without knowing the value cannot be proportionate. Putting the graph and the valuation in the case view is the minimum this work requires.
