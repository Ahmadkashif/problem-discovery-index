# Age and Harm Controls From Regulated Gambling

**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Licensed gambling runs age verification, self-exclusion and harm monitoring as a condition of operating, and the item economies feeding it run none.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to hold a defensible position on third-party sites that accept its items as stakes, when the items, the trading infrastructure and the audience are all the operator's — and whoever builds that position takes the account.

## The Problem
Regulated gambling has a mature protective apparatus: age verification before participation, self-exclusion registers, deposit and loss limits, behavioural markers of harm, mandated intervention and audited reporting. It exists because the harms are well documented and the licence depends on addressing them. The item economies that feed the unregulated adjacent ecosystem have none of it, and their user base is substantially younger than any licensed operator's.

## What Already Exists
Age and identity verification; self-exclusion infrastructure; deposit and loss limits; behavioural harm markers and intervention; and audited compliance reporting.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to an operator who supplies the stakes rather than running the game. It requires: (1) controls applied to trading rather than to wagering, since the operator's surface is the item transfer and not the bet — this is the substantive difference and it means the intervention point is upstream of the harm; (2) a user base with a large minor population, where licensed operators exclude minors entirely; (3) no licence and therefore no obligation, so the controls must be chosen rather than required; (4) the actual gambling occurring on a third party the operator does not control, limiting what any intervention can achieve; and (5) a trading feature used overwhelmingly for legitimate purposes, so controls must be targeted rather than blanket.

## Target Customer
Virtual economy operators, legal and policy leadership, regulators and consumer protection bodies, and responsible gambling technology vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Licensed gambling built age verification and harm monitoring because the licence required it. Applying those controls to item transfers rather than to wagering puts the intervention upstream of the harm, which is the only place this operator can act.
