# Price Discovery & Market Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/virtual-economy-operators|Virtual Economy Operators]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to publish a reference price people can rely on for goods they buy with real money — and whoever publishes it credibly takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — none published
**Target Buyer:** Marketplace product
**Automation Potential:** Very high — aggregation and index publication

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
People trade goods worth real money at prices established by scattered, inconsistent and frequently manipulated data. Some operators publish a recent sale price; most publish nothing. Third-party sites fill the gap with varying methodology and no accountability. The operator holds the complete trade record and could publish an honest reference price, volume and depth for every item — which is mechanical work, would materially improve every participant's position, and nobody has done properly.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A last-sale price on some items, a third-party price tracker, and community spreadsheets. The gaps: no volume-weighted reference price; no depth or liquidity measure; no exclusion of manipulated trades; no historical series; and no published methodology.
