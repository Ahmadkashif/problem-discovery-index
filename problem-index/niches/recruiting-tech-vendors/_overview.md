# Niche Analysis — Recruiting Tech Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/recruiting-tech-vendors|Recruiting Tech Vendors]]

## Niche Selection

Recruiting technology holds the largest record of hiring decisions ever assembled and has no mechanism for establishing whether any of them were correct. The eight niches follow from that: the screening decision itself, the sourcing that feeds it, the two coordination failures, the two people, and the mechanical layers.

## Why These Niches

Screening is where the decision happens and where nobody knows if it was right. Sourcing determines who enters the pipeline at all. Manager feedback and scheduling are the two coordination failures that consume a recruiter's week. The two underserved people are the candidate who hears nothing and the recruiter measured on speed. Volume and compliance are mechanical, and the first is being reshaped faster than anything else in the category.

## Filter Notes

Seven of the eight are terminal — each names one contest and dividing further produces features rather than markets.

**Screening and matching** is not, and its halves separate on re-examining the decision versus running the experiment that would settle it. Rejection audit takes a sample of rejected applications and has them re-reviewed blind, by different people, against the same requirements — measuring how often the rejection was wrong by the employer's own standard. It needs nobody's permission, uses records already held, costs a few reviewer hours a month, produces a false-rejection rate within weeks, and every party wants the answer: the employer is losing candidates it wanted, the recruiter is being asked to screen faster than the evidence supports, and the vendor gets a quality signal. Counterfactual evidence is the other thing entirely: generating the outcome data that does not exist, by advancing some candidates the screen would have rejected — randomised at the margin of the threshold — and observing how they perform. It is the only design that produces genuine evidence about screening validity, and it requires an employer to deliberately hire people its process rejected, to wait years for a usable sample, and to accept the ethical and legal complexity of randomising employment prospects. So the audit is buildable and the experiment is not run — which is why, as the analysis states, every ranking feature in this category remains a model of past recruiter behaviour, and the field already knows what that produces.

Two adjacent candidates were rejected as belonging elsewhere: **the instruments used to test candidates** are the subject of [[industries/talent-assessment-platforms|Talent Assessment Platforms]], and **agency placement of contingent and permanent workers** belongs to [[industries/staffing-agencies|Staffing Agencies]].
