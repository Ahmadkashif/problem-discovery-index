# Sourcing & Candidate Discovery

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/recruiting-tech-vendors|Recruiting Tech Vendors]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Whether sourcing finds the people who could do the job or the people whose profiles are most legible to a search.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.16B — 18% of US recruiting technology spend
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~18%
**Digital Adoption:** High — search and outreach at scale
**Target Buyer:** Sourcing teams; sourcing platform vendors
**Automation Potential:** Very high — and the automation has changed both sides

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche

Sourcing determines who enters the pipeline at all, which makes it upstream of every other decision in the process. A candidate never found cannot be rejected, cannot appear in an audit, and cannot be in any fairness analysis.

Search runs over profile data — titles, companies, skills listed, keywords — which rewards people whose careers are legible in that vocabulary. Someone whose relevant experience is real but described differently, who worked at a company nobody recognises, whose title was idiosyncratic, or who is not on the platform at all is invisible, consistently and by construction.

The niche is distinct because the bias is one of visibility rather than judgement, which makes it invisible to every downstream measurement. A perfectly fair screening process applied to a systematically narrow pipeline produces a narrow outcome, and nothing in the pipeline's metrics shows it.

## Current Tools & Gaps

Boolean and semantic search across profile databases. Aggregated profile data from public sources. Outreach automation with sequencing and personalisation. Talent pool and CRM management. Diversity sourcing filters of varying legality and usefulness. Response rate analytics.

The gaps are coverage and capability inference. Nobody measures who the search does not reach — the profiles absent from the database, the careers described in the wrong vocabulary, the people whose experience is equivalent but unsearchable. Outreach response rates are measured and the population that never received outreach is not. And search matches vocabulary rather than capability, which is now the largest practical improvement available.
