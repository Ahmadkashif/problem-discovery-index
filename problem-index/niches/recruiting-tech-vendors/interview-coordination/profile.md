**Target Buyer:** Coordinators; talent operations leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very high — this is a constrained optimisation problem

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche

Arranging a five-person panel across three time zones takes a coordinator two days of messages, and one cancellation restarts it.

Single-interview scheduling is solved — a link, a calendar, a booking. Panels are a different problem: multiple interviewers with partial availability, required and optional attendees, ordering constraints, interviewer load limits, room or tooling requirements, a candidate with their own constraints and a current job, and time zones. It is a constrained scheduling problem and it is solved by a person sending messages.

The niche is distinct because the failure is operational and expensive in a specific way: coordination delay is the largest controllable component of time to hire, and candidates are lost in the gaps.

## Current Tools & Gaps

Calendar integration and self-scheduling links, which work well for one interviewer. Panel scheduling features in the better ATS products and in dedicated coordination tools, of varying capability. Interviewer availability preferences. Rescheduling workflows. Automated reminders.

The gaps are the optimisation and the recovery. Panel assembly is treated as a search over interviewer calendars rather than as a constrained optimisation with interchangeable interviewers, so a slot that would work with a different qualified interviewer is never found. Cancellation triggers a manual restart rather than a re-solve. And interviewer load is unmanaged, so a small number of people carry most of the burden until they stop responding.
