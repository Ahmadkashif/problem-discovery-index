**One-liner:** Every field that makes consequential human judgements at volume measures inter-rater reliability; recruiting screening is the one that does not.

**Contested on:** Whether reliability methodology from other judgement-heavy fields transfers to a screening decision.

## The Problem

Measuring agreement between human judges is a solved methodological problem with implementations everywhere. Clinical diagnosis, content moderation, grant review, academic peer review, annotation, quality assurance in contact centres and medical coding all run inter-rater reliability analysis, with established statistics, calibration practice and remediation workflows.

Recruiting screening makes more consequential judgements at higher volume than several of these and measures none of it. The methodology is not missing; the practice is absent.

## What Already Exists

Reliability statistics — kappa, alpha, ICC — implemented in every statistical package. Calibration session practice from peer review and annotation. Double-review workflow patterns from clinical and moderation settings. Blinding methodology from peer review. Annotation platforms with adjudication queues. Content moderation quality tooling with exactly this shape.

## The Customization Gap

**The decision is a rejection with no appeal and no feedback.** Annotation disagreement produces a label; screening disagreement produces a person not hired, who is never told and never returns. That asymmetry argues for a higher sampling rate on rejections than on advances, which is the opposite of how quality sampling usually works.

**The reviewers are the people being audited and have no slack.** Recruiters screening four hundred applications cannot also re-review. The audit needs its own reviewer capacity — a separate pool, a rotation, or generative pre-screening with human confirmation — and designing it to be affordable is the operational problem.

**Blinding requires substantial redaction.** Peer review blinds an author's name. Here a resume is full of identifying information, and effective blinding means removing names, institutions, dates, locations and phrasing markers while keeping the substance. That redaction is itself a capability and doing it badly invalidates the audit.

**The criterion is the employer's own stated requirements, which are frequently vague.** Reliability analysis assumes a rubric. Here the rubric is a job description written for advertising, and low agreement may reflect an unusable rubric rather than unreliable reviewers — a finding that is itself the most valuable output and needs to be distinguished.

**The results are legally sensitive.** A documented false rejection rate, with breakdowns by group, is discoverable. Privilege, retention and reporting need deliberate handling — which is a reason to structure the programme carefully rather than a reason not to run it.

## Target Customer

Employers' talent acquisition quality functions, where they exist, and ATS vendors building an audit module. Also the annotation and moderation quality vendors, whose tooling is the closest existing fit and who could serve this market with modest adaptation.

## Impact If Solved

The reliability statistics, blinding practice, double-review workflow and adjudication tooling get reused, and the rejection-weighted sampling, separate reviewer capacity, resume redaction, vague-rubric diagnosis and legal handling get built. Concretely: a reliability programme for the highest-volume consequential judgement in the organisation, using methodology every adjacent field already runs.
