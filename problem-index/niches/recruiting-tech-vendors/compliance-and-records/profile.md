# Compliance, Records & Audit Trail

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/recruiting-tech-vendors|Recruiting Tech Vendors]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Whether the record shows why a decision was made, or only that it was.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600M — 5% of US recruiting technology spend
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5%
**Digital Adoption:** Moderate — records are retained, reasons are not
**Target Buyer:** Legal and compliance functions; HR technology teams
**Automation Potential:** Very high — capturing the reason is a field

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche

Applicant tracking systems were built partly as compliance infrastructure. They retain applications for statutory periods, record demographic self-identification where collected, log status changes and support the reporting obligations employers carry.

What they do not record is the basis for any decision. A rejection is a status with a timestamp and frequently a dropdown value selected from six generic options. Why this application was rejected — which requirement was unmet, what the reviewer concluded, what the automated screen scored and on what — is either not captured or captured in a free-text note nobody structures.

The niche is distinct because the regulatory direction has changed. Automated employment decision tools are increasingly subject to audit, disclosure and record-keeping obligations, and those obligations ask about reasons rather than about retention.

## Current Tools & Gaps

Retention policies and statutory record keeping. Demographic self-identification collection. EEO reporting. Status change logs. Rejection reason dropdowns. Audit trail features of variable depth. Bias audit support arriving in response to new requirements.

The gaps are the decision basis and the model record. Automated screening decisions are frequently not logged with the score, the threshold, the model version and the features that drove them — so an employer asked to explain a rejection eighteen months later cannot reconstruct it. Configuration changes to filters and thresholds are unversioned. And the reason fields that exist are too coarse to be evidence of anything.
