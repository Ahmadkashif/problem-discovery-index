# Analyst Rankings & Accuracy Scoring

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Organisations that rank sell-side analysts — survey-based rankings such as Extel (formerly Institutional Investor Research) and Coalition Greenwich broker studies, and accuracy-based scores such as LSEG StarMine and TipRanks. Brokers, investors and corporates pay for the results and the underlying feedback.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-200 survey researchers and quantitative analysts
**Output:** Annual analyst and broker rankings, accuracy scores, client feedback reports
**Proprietary data:** Decades of investor vote and survey responses; accuracy scoring built on estimate histories
**Clock:** Annual survey cycles; earnings for accuracy scores
**Buyer:** Head of Research Rankings
