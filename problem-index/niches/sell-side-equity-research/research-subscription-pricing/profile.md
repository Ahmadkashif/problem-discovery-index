# Research Subscription Pricing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to set a per-client price for unbundled research that the client's research budget committee will accept and that tracks what the client actually consumes — and whoever does that best keeps the account through each annual budget review.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$700M global (estimated; concentrated in EU and UK clients)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7%
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — tiered price lists, entitlement systems
**Target Buyer:** Research COO; head of research sales
**Automation Potential:** Medium-High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Unbundled clients buy research as a priced service from a research budget, reviewed annually by a committee that compares brokers' prices and consumption. The contest is packaging, tiering and entitlement against measured consumption — a pricing problem with a different buyer than the portfolio manager who votes.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Tiered subscription lists, bespoke negotiation, entitlement platforms, client-side consumption reports, and benchmarking from consultancies such as Substantive Research. The gap: prices anchored to negotiation history, not to consumption or value.
