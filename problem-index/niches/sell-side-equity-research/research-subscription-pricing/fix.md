# The Client's Consumption Report Disagrees With Ours

**Niche:** [[niches/sell-side-equity-research/research-subscription-pricing/profile|Research Subscription Pricing]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** At renewal the client presents its own tally of calls and reads, and the broker cannot reconcile it.
**Tags:** #data-integration #descriptive-statistics #evaluation-metrics #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to set a per-client price for unbundled research that the client's research budget committee will accept and that tracks what the client actually consumes — and whoever does that best keeps the account through each annual budget review.

## The Problem
Clients track research consumption for MiFID II record-keeping; the broker's own records are partial. Renewals are negotiated on the client's numbers.

## Why It's Still Broken
Interaction logging is incomplete and readership feeds are fragmented across platforms.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A reconciled consumption statement per client per period, produced before the renewal meeting.

## Who Feels the Pain
Research sales at renewal time.

## Impact If Fixed
Negotiations on shared numbers rather than the client's alone.
