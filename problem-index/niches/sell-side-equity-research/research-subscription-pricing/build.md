# Pricing Research Against Consumption

**Niche:** [[niches/sell-side-equity-research/research-subscription-pricing/profile|Research Subscription Pricing]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Research price lists are set by negotiation history, while the client's budget committee arrives with its own consumption data.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #survival-analysis #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to set a per-client price for unbundled research that the client's research budget committee will accept and that tracks what the client actually consumes — and whoever does that best keeps the account through each annual budget review.

## The Problem
The client's research budget committee compares price against what its staff read, called and attended. The broker often has less visibility than the client into its own consumption.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Unbundled pricing is only since 2018, regimes keep shifting (EU Listing Act, FCA joint payments from 2024), and many brokers still treat research as a cost centre.

## What to Build
A client-level consumption ledger, price-per-consumption benchmarks across the client base, renewal-risk models, and tier recommendations tied to actual usage patterns.

## Target Customer
Research COOs at brokers with material EU and UK client revenue; independent research providers.

## Impact If Built
Prices that survive the budget review, and early warning on clients likely to cut.
