# Tiered Subscription Packaging

**Niche:** [[niches/sell-side-equity-research/research-subscription-pricing/profile|Research Subscription Pricing]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Information businesses have mature tiering and willingness-to-pay methods that research departments adopted only partially.
**Tags:** #k-means-clustering #feature-engineering #descriptive-statistics #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to set a per-client price for unbundled research that the client's research budget committee will accept and that tracks what the client actually consumes — and whoever does that best keeps the account through each annual budget review.

## The Problem
Tiers were drawn up quickly in 2017–18 and rarely revisited.

## What Already Exists
Subscription pricing practice from data and media businesses: usage segmentation, willingness-to-pay studies, good-better-best tiering.

## The Customization Gap
Analyst access (calls, bespoke work, corporate access) is the expensive, scarce component and cannot be priced like content; inducement rules constrain bundling of corporate access; and clients need tiers they can defend to their own clients.

## Target Customer
Research COO; research sales management.

## Impact If Solved
Tiers matched to how clients actually use research, with the scarce analyst time priced explicitly.
