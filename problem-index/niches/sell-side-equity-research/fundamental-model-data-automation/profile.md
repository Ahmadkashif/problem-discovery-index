# Fundamental Model Data Automation Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors that extract reported financials and KPIs from filings, releases and presentations into source-linked datasets and model templates for analysts — Daloopa and Canalyst (now within AlphaSense via Tegus). Brokers and buy-side firms pay per seat or per coverage universe.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 data analysts and engineers
**Output:** Source-linked historical fundamentals and KPIs, updated within hours of each release
**Proprietary data:** Tagged KPI history across thousands of companies — built from public documents but expensive to reproduce
**Clock:** Earnings releases: data must land within hours
**Buyer:** Head of Data Content
