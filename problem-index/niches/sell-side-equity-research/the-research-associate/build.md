# An Apprenticeship Layer on the Model

**Niche:** [[niches/sell-side-equity-research/the-research-associate/profile|The Research Associate]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Associates see the analyst's revised numbers but never the reasoning, so judgment is learned slowly if at all.
**Tags:** #tacit-knowledge-ml #large-language-models #k-nearest-neighbors #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn the associate's reporting-season hours from transcription into supervised judgment — and whoever does that best trains the department's next analysts instead of losing them to the buy side.

## The Problem
The associate types in the analyst's revised estimates without the reasons. The tacit pattern — how this management team guides, what this stock trades on — is transmitted only by proximity.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Revision rationales are not captured anywhere structured, and training is informal.

## What to Build
A side panel on the model that shows, for each revised cell, the analyst's prior revisions on the same line, the guidance history, how those revisions scored against reported results, and the note passage that explained them — plus the associate's own forecast scored privately against the analyst's and the outcome.

## Target Customer
Directors of research concerned with retention and succession.

## Impact If Built
Faster development of associates into analysts, and a measured view of who is ready.
