**Tags:** #automation #descriptive-statistics #worker-facing #quick-win #evaluation-metrics
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn the associate's reporting-season hours from transcription into supervised judgment — and whoever does that best trains the department's next analysts instead of losing them to the buy side.

## The Problem
Charts are rebuilt, restyled and pasted into the authoring tool, then rebuilt again when numbers change.

## Why It's Still Broken
Exhibits are linked to personal model layouts and templates do not connect to them.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Exhibit definitions bound to model ranges, regenerated in house style whenever the model changes.

## Who Feels the Pain
Associates on report mornings.

## Impact If Fixed
Removes a repetitive, error-prone step from every published note.
