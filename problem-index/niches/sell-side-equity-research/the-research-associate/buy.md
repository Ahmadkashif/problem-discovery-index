# Spreadsheet Automation for the Fill

**Niche:** [[niches/sell-side-equity-research/the-research-associate/profile|The Research Associate]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Spreadsheet automation tools exist; none know a research model's layout or the analyst's adjustments.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #feature-engineering #data-integration #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn the associate's reporting-season hours from transcription into supervised judgment — and whoever does that best trains the department's next analysts instead of losing them to the buy side.

## The Problem
The quarterly fill is a repetitive copy from source documents to fixed cells.

## What Already Exists
Excel automation, AI spreadsheet assistants, vendor add-ins that pull standardised financials.

## The Customization Gap
Mapping from source line items to each analyst's custom rows, preserving historical segment splits, applying adjustments, and leaving a sourced audit trail for compliance.

## Target Customer
Research departments; associate-heavy sector teams.

## Impact If Solved
Hours removed per company per quarter.
