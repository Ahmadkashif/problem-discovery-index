# Entitled Readers Who Never Pay

**Niche:** [[niches/sell-side-equity-research/research-monetisation/profile|Research Monetisation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Research remains entitled to readers at clients who have stopped paying, because nobody reconciles the entitlement list with the payment list.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #compliance #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** This niche is not terminal — earning commission share through the broker vote in bundled markets and pricing research as a priced product to unbundled clients are different contests with different winners, and they are stated separately in the sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Entitlements on Bloomberg, FactSet, LSEG and AlphaSense are granted generously and removed slowly. Under MiFID II, providing research free to an unbundled client can be an inducement problem; everywhere, it is unpaid service.

## Why It's Still Broken
Entitlements are administered by research operations on platform portals; payments are tracked by sales and finance; the two lists use different client identifiers.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A reconciliation that matches entitled readers to paying clients, flags unpaid consumption by volume, and routes each case to sales for conversion or removal — run monthly.

## Who Feels the Pain
Research operations, compliance and the sales team negotiating with clients who are already reading for free.

## Impact If Fixed
Closes an inducement exposure and gives sales a list of heavy unpaid readers to convert.
