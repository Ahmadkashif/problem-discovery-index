**Contested on:** This niche is not terminal — earning commission share through the broker vote in bundled markets and pricing research as a priced product to unbundled clients are different contests with different winners, and they are stated separately in the sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Research contracts renew annually or by vote period, and the department rarely knows in advance which clients will cut. Usage signals that predict churn and expansion exist in readership and CRM logs and are not used.

## What Already Exists
SaaS revenue intelligence and churn-prediction tooling; product analytics; customer health scoring.

## The Customization Gap
Research consumption is multi-channel and largely offline (calls, meetings); the payer is a committee distinct from the readers; payment is lumpy and regime-dependent; and regulatory rules on inducements constrain what can be offered free. Health scores must be built per client across analysts and channels and mapped to the client's budget calendar.

## Target Customer
Research sales management; research COO.

## Impact If Solved
Early warning on budget cuts and evidence for renewals, adapted from a mature practice.
