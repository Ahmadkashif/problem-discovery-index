# The Interaction-to-Payment Join

**Contested on:** This niche is not terminal — earning commission share through the broker vote in bundled markets and pricing research as a priced product to unbundled clients are different contests with different winners, and they are stated separately in the sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Readership comes from platform feeds, calls and meetings from CRM, events from corporate access, and payment from vote spreadsheets, commission reports or RPA invoices. Each is reviewed separately. Nobody can say what a client consumed per dollar paid, or which interaction types predict payment.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The data is held by different teams with different identifiers for the same client and person. Readership feeds are platform-specific. Vote formats vary by client. And measuring value per interaction invites uncomfortable conclusions about popular but unpaid products.

## What to Build
Entity resolution of clients and individual readers across platforms, CRM and payment records; a client-period panel of consumption and payment; models of payment on consumption with selection adjustment; and two products from the same join — vote attribution where research is bundled and price evidence where it is not, which the sub-niches develop separately.

## Target Customer
Head of research sales and the research COO at brokers of every size.

## Impact If Built
Turns payment from a twice-yearly surprise into a running estimate, and gives the department evidence for both its vote campaigns and its price list.
