# Research Monetisation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** This niche is not terminal — earning commission share through the broker vote in bundled markets and pricing research as a priced product to unbundled clients are different contests with different winners, and they are stated separately in the sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.0B global (estimated research-sales, vote-management and pricing effort tied to how research is paid for)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~20% — touches every research dollar
**Digital Adoption:** Low to Medium — readership logs exist, votes arrive as spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** Head of research sales; director of research; COO of the research division
**Automation Potential:** High — the data is mostly present and unjoined

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Research has three payment regimes at once. In the US it is still mostly paid through commissions allocated by the broker vote; in the EU it has been unbundled under MiFID II since 2018 and paid from research payment accounts or the manager's own P&L, with partial relief under the 2024 Listing Act amendments; in the UK the FCA has allowed joint payments again since August 2024 under conditions. The department must win votes in one regime and defend a price list in another.

It is **not terminal**. Vote attribution is a measurement contest over interaction data and wins in bundled markets; subscription pricing is a packaging and entitlement contest and wins in unbundled ones. Different data, different buyers on the client side (portfolio managers voting versus a research budget committee), different winners.

## Current Tools & Gaps
CRM, readership feeds from aggregation platforms, vote spreadsheets, commission-management reports, RPA budget statements, and entitlement systems. The gaps: no join between interaction and payment; price lists set by negotiation history; entitlements that over-serve unpaid readers.
