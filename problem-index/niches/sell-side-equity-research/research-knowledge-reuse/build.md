# A Forecast-Level Index of the Archive

**Niche:** [[niches/sell-side-equity-research/research-knowledge-reuse/profile|Research Knowledge Reuse]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Every claim and forecast the department ever published is in its archive and none of them is indexed as a claim with an outcome.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #word-embeddings #evaluation-metrics #tacit-knowledge-ml #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to retrieve what the department has already said, forecast and been right or wrong about, at the moment a client or a successor asks — and whoever does that best turns the research archive into a product instead of a library.

## The Problem
Searching the archive returns documents, not the department's stated views, and never says whether those views turned out right.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Claim extraction from prose was unreliable until recently, and outcome-joining was nobody's job.

## What to Build
Extract each forecast, rating, price target and directional claim with date and analyst; join to subsequent outcomes; serve as retrieval for analysts, sales and successors, and as a track-record report for the director of research.

## Target Customer
Directors of research; research sales.

## Impact If Built
The archive becomes the department's evidence of value and the successor's starting point.
