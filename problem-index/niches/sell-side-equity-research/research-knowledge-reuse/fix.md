# The Client Question Answered From Memory

**Niche:** [[niches/sell-side-equity-research/research-knowledge-reuse/profile|Research Knowledge Reuse]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A salesperson asks the analyst what the firm said about a company two years ago, and the analyst digs through folders.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #word-embeddings #automation #worker-facing #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to retrieve what the department has already said, forecast and been right or wrong about, at the moment a client or a successor asks — and whoever does that best turns the research archive into a product instead of a library.

## The Problem
Client questions about past views interrupt analysts many times a week.

## Why It's Still Broken
The research portal searches by keyword and date, and sales does not trust it.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A sales-facing assistant answering from published research only, with citations to the note and date.

## Who Feels the Pain
Analysts interrupted, sales waiting, clients waiting.

## Impact If Fixed
Fewer interruptions and faster, cited answers.
