# Research Knowledge Reuse

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to retrieve what the department has already said, forecast and been right or wrong about, at the moment a client or a successor asks — and whoever does that best turns the research archive into a product instead of a library.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$700M global (estimated)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7%
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — archives are searchable by keyword, not by claim or forecast
**Target Buyer:** Director of research; research operations
**Automation Potential:** Very High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A department has published tens of thousands of notes, each with estimates, ratings, price targets and claims. Clients ask "what did you say about this in 2022", successors need the history, and the department needs to know which of its calls worked. The archive is the department's proprietary corpus, and it is used as a document store.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Internal research portals, platform search on Bloomberg, FactSet and AlphaSense, and the analyst's own folders. The gaps: no claim-level or forecast-level index; no link from a published view to its outcome.
