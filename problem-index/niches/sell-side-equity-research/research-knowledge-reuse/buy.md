# Enterprise Search Over Published Research

**Niche:** [[niches/sell-side-equity-research/research-knowledge-reuse/profile|Research Knowledge Reuse]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Enterprise semantic search is mature; research archives need entitlement-aware, time-aware retrieval of views rather than documents.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #word-embeddings #k-nearest-neighbors #evaluation-metrics #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to retrieve what the department has already said, forecast and been right or wrong about, at the moment a client or a successor asks — and whoever does that best turns the research archive into a product instead of a library.

## The Problem
Generic search over research returns the most similar document, not the department's latest view as at a date.

## What Already Exists
Enterprise semantic search and retrieval platforms; AlphaSense for externally aggregated research.

## The Customization Gap
Point-in-time correctness (what was the view on that date), entitlement controls by client, superseded-view handling, and compliance on reusing unpublished drafts.

## Target Customer
Research operations and sales.

## Impact If Solved
Answers grounded in the department's own published record.
