# Buy-Side Research Budget & Broker Review Desks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Teams inside asset managers that run the broker vote, set research budgets, manage research payment accounts and value research consumption, sometimes supported by consultancies such as Substantive Research.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 2-8 people per asset manager
**Output:** Broker votes, research budgets, consumption valuations
**Proprietary data:** Their own consumption and vote records
**Clock:** Annual budget and semi-annual vote cycles
**Buyer:** Head of Research Procurement / COO

## Scorecard
Not scored — fails the gate (no dedicated research/insight function of 10+ people).

**Kill switches:** not assessed — fails gate
**Verdict:** Logged — fails gate. The function is small at nearly every manager; the consultancies supporting it are boutique.
