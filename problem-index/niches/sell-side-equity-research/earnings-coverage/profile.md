# Earnings Coverage

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a correct read of the print — which number the stock will trade on and whether the beat or miss is real — to clients before the open, and whoever does that best owns the morning call and the vote.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.6B global (estimated share of broker research spend attributable to company-level earnings coverage)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~25–30% of department effort and client consumption
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — authoring and distribution are digital, the analytical work is Excel and memory
**Target Buyer:** Director of research; sector team heads
**Automation Potential:** High for the mechanical half, low for the read itself

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The quarterly cycle — preview, print, first take, call, model roll, revised estimates and price target — is the most consumed and most time-critical product a research department makes. It is judged in minutes on report mornings and repeated four times a year for every covered company. The contest is not producing the note; every broker produces one. It is being first with the right emphasis: the line item that matters, the quality of the beat, the credibility of the guide.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Release feeds and terminals, the analyst's Excel model, consensus from LSEG I/B/E/S, FactSet and Bloomberg, and authoring templates in BlueMatrix or in-house systems. The gaps: no structured memory of how each management team guides or what each stock trades on; preview notes written from scratch each quarter; and no measurement of whether the department's first-take emphasis was the one the market agreed with.
