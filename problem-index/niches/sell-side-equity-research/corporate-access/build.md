# Matching Investors to Management Teams

**Niche:** [[niches/sell-side-equity-research/corporate-access/profile|Corporate Access]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Who gets a slot with a CEO is decided from requests and memory, without data on which investors use the meeting and which merely attend.
**Tags:** #k-nearest-neighbors #gradient-boosting #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to put the right investors in front of the right management teams and to prove afterwards that the meeting was worth the vote — and whoever does that best keeps both the issuers and the investors coming back.

## The Problem
Management time is scarce, investor requests exceed it, and issuers judge brokers on whether the meetings were with relevant, long-term holders. Allocation is negotiated by the corporate access team from memory.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Outcomes — subsequent ownership changes from public filings, vote changes, repeat requests — sit outside the scheduling system and are never joined.

## What to Build
An investor–issuer relevance model using holdings data, style, past meeting history and subsequent position changes, generating ranked allocation proposals and post-event evidence for both issuer and investor.

## Target Customer
Heads of corporate access at brokers of all sizes.

## Impact If Built
Better meetings for issuers, evidence of value for investors, and a measurable link to the vote.
