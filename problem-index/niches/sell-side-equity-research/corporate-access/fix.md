# The Roadshow Feedback Report Assembled by Hand

**Niche:** [[niches/sell-side-equity-research/corporate-access/profile|Corporate Access]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** After each roadshow the issuer expects a summary of investor feedback, typed up from sales notes.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #automation #worker-facing #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to put the right investors in front of the right management teams and to prove afterwards that the meeting was worth the vote — and whoever does that best keeps both the issuers and the investors coming back.

## The Problem
Investor feedback is scattered across sales notes and emails and compiled into a document for the issuer's IR team.

## Why It's Still Broken
The notes are unstructured and the report is low priority once the roadshow ends.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Structured capture after each meeting and an auto-drafted feedback summary by theme, reviewed before sending.

## Who Feels the Pain
Corporate access staff and the sales team.

## Impact If Fixed
Faster, more consistent feedback that keeps issuers returning.
