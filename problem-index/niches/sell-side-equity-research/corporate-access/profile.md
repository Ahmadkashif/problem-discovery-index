# Corporate Access

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to put the right investors in front of the right management teams and to prove afterwards that the meeting was worth the vote — and whoever does that best keeps both the issuers and the investors coming back.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.2B global (estimated value attributed to corporate access in research payments)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12%
**Digital Adoption:** Low — CRM, spreadsheets and email
**Target Buyer:** Head of corporate access; director of research
**Automation Potential:** Medium-High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Brokers arrange non-deal roadshows, conferences, field trips and one-to-one meetings between issuers and investors. Investors consistently rate access among the most valued broker services, and under MiFID II it is treated as a non-research minor benefit or a priced service depending on how it is provided. The contest is matching — which investors for which management team — and evidencing the value.

## Current Tools & Gaps
CRM, conference scheduling software, spreadsheets, and email. The gaps: requests matched by memory; no record of which meetings converted into positions, votes or repeat attendance; issuer feedback compiled by hand.
