**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to put the right investors in front of the right management teams and to prove afterwards that the meeting was worth the vote — and whoever does that best keeps both the issuers and the investors coming back.

## The Problem
A broker conference hosts dozens of companies and hundreds of investors in one-to-ones and small groups, scheduled by hand.

## What Already Exists
Generic meeting-matching and event scheduling software; assignment and scheduling solvers.

## The Customization Gap
Priority weights reflect commission and vote value, issuer preferences on holders, compliance constraints on who may meet whom around offerings, and last-minute changes on the day.

## Target Customer
Corporate access and conference teams.

## Impact If Solved
Days of manual scheduling removed per conference and better-matched meetings.
