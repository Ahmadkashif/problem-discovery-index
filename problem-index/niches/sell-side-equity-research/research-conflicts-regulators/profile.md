# Research Conflicts Regulators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
FINRA (Rule 2241), the SEC (Reg AC), the FCA and ESMA (MiFID II research payment rules) oversee research conflicts, payments and disclosures, examine broker-dealers and set the rules on unbundling.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 examiners and policy staff relevant to research
**Output:** Rules, examination findings, enforcement actions, policy reviews such as the FCA's 2023–24 investment research review
**Proprietary data:** Examination findings across all regulated research departments
**Clock:** Examination and rulemaking cycles
**Buyer:** Programme office
