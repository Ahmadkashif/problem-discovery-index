# Analyst Estimates & Consensus Data Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Collect every published sell-side estimate, rating and price target — and, increasingly, full line-item broker models — normalise them, and sell consensus, estimate history and derived signals to the buy side, corporates and quants. LSEG I/B/E/S and StarMine, FactSet Estimates, S&P Global's Visible Alpha and Bloomberg are the main providers. Investors, IR teams and quant funds pay subscriptions for the consensus and the history.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-1,500 estimates collectors, content analysts, model-standardisation analysts and quantitative researchers
**Output:** Consensus estimates by line item, estimate revision history, broker-model aggregates, surprise and revision signals, analyst accuracy scores
**Proprietary data:** Decades of point-in-time, analyst-attributed estimate history across tens of thousands of companies, joined to reported actuals — the only complete record of what the sell side forecast and when
**Clock:** Earnings calendars: consensus must be right before every release and actuals posted minutes after
**Buyer:** Head of Estimates Content / Chief Product Officer, Estimates

**Kill switches:** none live — estimates are published research contributed under licence and contain no material non-public information; model-level data (Visible Alpha) is entitlement-gated by contributing broker, which may limit reuse of model-level data in derived products — a contract term to confirm per contributor, not a live kill switch on the estimate-level history the opportunity rests on.
**Verdict:** Qualified — indexed. Q5 is the weak point: the buyer is a handful of large vendors rather than a fragmented market, so one reference does not unlock many more. Everything else is at the ceiling — the forecast archive is complete, attributed, point-in-time and graded by public results four times a year.
