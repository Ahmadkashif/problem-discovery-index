# Research Outsourcing & Knowledge Process Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Offshore and nearshore teams that support sell-side analysts with model maintenance, earnings notes, data work and editing — Acuity Knowledge Partners, Evalueserve and CRISIL's research services among them. Brokers pay by FTE or by output.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1,000-6,000 analysts across the sector
**Output:** Model updates, draft notes, exhibits, data sets for the client's analysts
**Proprietary data:** None of their own — work is performed inside the client's models and research
**Clock:** The client's earnings calendar
**Buyer:** Head of Research Operations (client side)
