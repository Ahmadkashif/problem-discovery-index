# Company-Compiled Consensus & IR Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Services that compile consensus on behalf of a listed company's investor relations team directly from the analysts covering it — Vara Research and Visible Alpha's corporate offering among them — plus IR platforms that track coverage and perception.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-80 analysts per firm
**Output:** Company-compiled consensus by line item, pre-close investor briefings, perception studies
**Proprietary data:** Line-item consensus collected directly from covering analysts on behalf of issuers
**Clock:** The issuer's pre-close and reporting calendar
**Buyer:** Head of Investor Relations (client); Head of Consensus Services (vendor)
