# Coverage Leverage for the One-Analyst Sector

**Niche:** [[niches/sell-side-equity-research/small-mid-cap-coverage/profile|Small & Mid-Cap Coverage at Regional Brokers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** An analyst covering twenty-five small caps has no associate, no automation and the same report-morning clock as a bulge-bracket team.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #automation #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to cover more small and mid-cap names per analyst after MiFID II without losing the differentiated view that justifies payment — and whoever does that best becomes the default coverage for an under-researched market segment.

## The Problem
Small companies report less consistently, have fewer KPIs in standard datasets and are less covered by extraction vendors. The analyst does everything personally.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Vendors prioritise large caps and large departments; small-cap filings are messier; the budget per seat is smaller.

## What to Build
A bundle for small departments: extraction tuned to small-cap releases, per-model mapping, preview and first-take drafting in the analyst's voice, and the disclosure pre-check, priced per analyst.

## Target Customer
Directors of research at regional and mid-tier brokers; independent and issuer-sponsored research providers.

## Impact If Built
More names covered per analyst at maintained quality, and fewer orphaned listed companies.
