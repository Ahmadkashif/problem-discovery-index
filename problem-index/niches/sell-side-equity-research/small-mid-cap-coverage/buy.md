**Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]

**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #evaluation-metrics #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to cover more small and mid-cap names per analyst after MiFID II without losing the differentiated view that justifies payment — and whoever does that best becomes the default coverage for an under-researched market segment.

## The Problem
Large brokers have internal data science teams building research automation; regional brokers do not.

## What Already Exists
Extraction datasets (Daloopa, Canalyst), AI research platforms (AlphaSense), authoring platforms (BlueMatrix).

## The Customization Gap
Coverage of small-cap and non-US filings, pricing for small teams, pre-integrated compliance for small compliance staffs, and minimal configuration.

## Target Customer
Regional broker research and compliance heads.

## Impact If Solved
Puts large-department leverage within reach of the brokers carrying most small-cap coverage.
