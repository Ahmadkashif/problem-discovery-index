# Small & Mid-Cap Coverage at Regional Brokers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to cover more small and mid-cap names per analyst after MiFID II without losing the differentiated view that justifies payment — and whoever does that best becomes the default coverage for an under-researched market segment.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$900M global (estimated)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9%
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium
**Target Buyer:** Director of research at a mid-tier or regional broker
**Automation Potential:** High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Research coverage of small and mid-cap companies fell after MiFID II unbundling, leaving many listed companies with one analyst or none. Mid-tier and regional brokers carry this coverage with thin teams — one analyst for twenty-five or more names, often without an associate. They are underserved by tooling priced and designed for bulge-bracket departments.

## Current Tools & Gaps
The same terminals and authoring tools as large brokers, often at reduced scope; models maintained by the analyst personally. The gaps: no leverage on the mechanical work and no economic case for adding headcount.
