# The Orphaned Name After the Analyst Leaves

**Niche:** [[niches/sell-side-equity-research/small-mid-cap-coverage/profile|Small & Mid-Cap Coverage at Regional Brokers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** When a regional broker's only analyst on a sector leaves, coverage is suspended and the companies go dark.
**Tags:** #tacit-knowledge-ml #large-language-models #k-nearest-neighbors #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to cover more small and mid-cap names per analyst after MiFID II without losing the differentiated view that justifies payment — and whoever does that best becomes the default coverage for an under-researched market segment.

## The Problem
With one analyst per sector, a departure means suspended coverage, a formal notice to clients and months before a reinitiation.

## Why It's Still Broken
Everything the analyst knew is in their model and memory; the department has no structured record to hand over.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A continuously maintained coverage file per company — estimate history, guidance-bias profile, key debates, prior notes indexed — so a successor can reinitiate in weeks.

## Who Feels the Pain
Research directors, issuers losing coverage, and clients losing the view.

## Impact If Fixed
Shorter coverage gaps and lower franchise loss on departures.
