# Research Authoring & Distribution Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Platforms that brokers use to author, compliance-check, entitle and distribute research and to receive readership data — BlueMatrix is the best known independent provider, alongside in-house systems.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 product and analytics staff
**Output:** Authoring, disclosure automation, distribution, readership analytics
**Proprietary data:** Readership and distribution logs across many brokers — held on the brokers' behalf
**Clock:** Report release times
**Buyer:** Chief Product Officer
