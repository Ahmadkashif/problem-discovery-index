# Model Maintenance

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get reported numbers into each analyst's own model layout — correct, sourced and with presentation changes flagged — within minutes of the release.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$800M global (estimated, including extraction datasets bought for sell-side models)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8%
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — datasets exist, the last mile is manual
**Target Buyer:** Director of research; research operations
**Automation Potential:** Very High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The model is the analyst's core instrument and the source of every published estimate. Keeping it current — fills, restatements, re-segmentations, share counts, guidance — is repetitive, high-volume and deadline-bound, which is why vendors such as Daloopa and Canalyst exist. The unsolved part is the analyst-specific mapping.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Extraction datasets with source links, standardised financials from terminals, Excel add-ins. The gaps: mapping into idiosyncratic layouts, adjustments that differ by analyst, and silent breaks when a company changes presentation.
