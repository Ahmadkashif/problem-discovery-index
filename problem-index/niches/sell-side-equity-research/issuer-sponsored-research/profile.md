# Issuer-Sponsored Research Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation firms

## What They Do
Firms paid by listed companies — typically small caps that lost broker coverage — to publish research on them, such as Edison Group, Hardman & Co and Zacks Small-Cap Research. The issuer pays a fixed fee; the research is distributed free to investors.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 analysts per firm
**Output:** Initiations, earnings updates and models on sponsoring issuers
**Proprietary data:** Mostly public filings; a history of the firm's own estimates on sponsored names
**Clock:** Issuer reporting calendar
**Buyer:** Head of Research
