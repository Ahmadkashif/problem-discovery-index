# Independent Research Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation firms

## What They Do
Research firms with no investment banking or trading arm that sell equity and thematic research by subscription — CFRA, Argus Research, Morningstar's equity research and New Street Research among them. Clients are asset managers, wealth platforms and brokers that redistribute the research.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-300 analysts per firm
**Output:** Company and sector research, ratings, fair-value estimates, models
**Proprietary data:** Their own archive of ratings and fair-value estimates with history
**Clock:** Earnings calendars and client budget cycles
**Buyer:** Director of Research / Head of Product
