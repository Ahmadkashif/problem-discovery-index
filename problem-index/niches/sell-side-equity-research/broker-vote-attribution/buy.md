# Marketing Attribution for the Vote

**Niche:** [[niches/sell-side-equity-research/broker-vote-attribution/profile|Broker Vote Attribution]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** B2B marketing has multi-touch attribution for long sales cycles; research sales has a vote and a spreadsheet.
**Tags:** #causal-inference #linear-regression #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show, client by client, which research interactions earned the vote — and whoever measures that best directs analyst effort and defends commission share where research is still paid through execution.

## The Problem
The broker vote is a long-cycle, multi-touch B2B purchase decision, structurally similar to enterprise deal attribution.

## What Already Exists
Multi-touch attribution and marketing-mix modelling in B2B marketing analytics; account-based engagement scoring.

## The Customization Gap
The "purchase" is a points allocation by many individual voters inside one client, aggregated by a trading desk; touches include offline calls and conference meetings; and voters weight analysts, not only the firm. Attribution must work at the voter-analyst level and reconcile to commission dollars.

## Target Customer
Research sales management.

## Impact If Solved
A mature attribution practice adapted to the vote, replacing anecdote.
