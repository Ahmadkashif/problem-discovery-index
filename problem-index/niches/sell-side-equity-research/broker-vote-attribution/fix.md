# The Call Nobody Logged

**Niche:** [[niches/sell-side-equity-research/broker-vote-attribution/profile|Broker Vote Attribution]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The interaction most associated with a high vote — the one-to-one analyst call — is the one most often missing from CRM.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #evaluation-metrics #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show, client by client, which research interactions earned the vote — and whoever measures that best directs analyst effort and defends commission share where research is still paid through execution.

## The Problem
Analysts take dozens of client calls a week and log few of them. Sales logs what sales arranged. When the vote comes, the evidence of service is incomplete and the client's own service log is the only record.

## Why It's Still Broken
Logging is manual, low priority on busy days, and benefits the department rather than the analyst.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Interaction capture from calendar and recorded-line metadata already retained for compliance, auto-matched to client and topic, confirmed in one click by the analyst.

## Who Feels the Pain
Analysts at vote time; research sales arguing for commission share with incomplete evidence.

## Impact If Fixed
A complete interaction record, which is the prerequisite for any attribution and a direct input to client service reviews.
