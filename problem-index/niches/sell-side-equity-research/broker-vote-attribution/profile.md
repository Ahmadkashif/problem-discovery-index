# Broker Vote Attribution

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show, client by client, which research interactions earned the vote — and whoever measures that best directs analyst effort and defends commission share where research is still paid through execution.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$900M global (estimated)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9%
**Digital Adoption:** Low — votes arrive as spreadsheets, interactions logged inconsistently
**Target Buyer:** Head of research sales; director of research
**Automation Potential:** High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Where research is paid through execution commissions, clients run a broker vote: portfolio managers and analysts allocate points to brokers and analysts, and the trading desk directs commission accordingly. The broker's research revenue is that vote. Winning it means knowing which calls, meetings, models and notes moved it — a measurement problem over interaction data, with portfolio managers as the voters.

## Current Tools & Gaps
CRM logging by sales, readership feeds, vote spreadsheets, Extel and Coalition Greenwich surveys as external benchmarks. The gap: no client-level model of what moved the vote, and analysts managing effort by anecdote.
