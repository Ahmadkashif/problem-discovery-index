# The Peer Company Mentioned Without a Disclosure

**Niche:** [[niches/sell-side-equity-research/research-compliance-review/profile|Research Compliance Review]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A sector note discusses fifteen companies and the disclosure block covers the five in the title.
**Tags:** #bert #large-language-models #compliance #evaluation-metrics #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to clear every research report for release inside the earnings-morning window with zero disclosure or quiet-period errors — and whoever does that best lets the department publish first without a FINRA finding.

## The Problem
Industry and thematic reports mention many issuers in passing. Each mentioned subject company may trigger disclosure obligations, and the authoring system only knows the tagged tickers.

## Why It's Still Broken
Issuer tagging is manual, done by the analyst at submission, and skipped under time pressure.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Automatic recognition of every issuer named in text and tables, matched to identifiers and to the disclosure database, with missing disclosures added for reviewer confirmation.

## Who Feels the Pain
Supervisory analysts, compliance and analysts whose reports bounce back on report mornings.

## Impact If Fixed
Removes one of the most common, purely mechanical sources of release delay and disclosure error.
