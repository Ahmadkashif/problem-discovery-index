# Contract Review Practice for Research

**Niche:** [[niches/sell-side-equity-research/research-compliance-review/profile|Research Compliance Review]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Legal teams use clause-level AI review against playbooks; research compliance reviews reports against a rulebook without one.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #evaluation-metrics #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to clear every research report for release inside the earnings-morning window with zero disclosure or quiet-period errors — and whoever does that best lets the department publish first without a FINRA finding.

## The Problem
Clause-level review against a playbook is the same shape as report review against Rule 2241 and house procedures.

## What Already Exists
AI contract review platforms with playbooks, deviation flags and reviewer workflows.

## The Customization Gap
The playbook must encode Rule 2241, Reg AC and firm procedures; must reconcile against live lists that change intraday; must operate within information-barrier controls on unpublished research; and must handle release-time pressure measured in minutes.

## Target Customer
Research compliance at broker-dealers.

## Impact If Solved
A proven review pattern adapted to the rulebook research is actually governed by.
