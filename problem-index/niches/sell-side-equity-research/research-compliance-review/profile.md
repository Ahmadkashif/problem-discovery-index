# Research Compliance Review

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to clear every research report for release inside the earnings-morning window with zero disclosure or quiet-period errors — and whoever does that best lets the department publish first without a FINRA finding.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$700M global (estimated supervisory analyst, research compliance and control-room effort on research)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7%
**Digital Adoption:** Low — disclosure blocks are automated, the review itself is reading
**Target Buyer:** Head of research compliance; chief compliance officer
**Automation Potential:** High for checklist items, medium for judgment

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every report passes supervisory analyst and compliance review against FINRA Rule 2241, Reg AC, the firm's written supervisory procedures and live restricted, watch and quiet-period lists. Release speed is a competitive variable on report mornings, and an error is a regulatory finding. No other niche in the department carries both pressures at once.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Disclosure modules in BlueMatrix and in-house authoring systems, control-room list systems, and communication surveillance from Smarsh, Global Relay and NICE Actimize. The gaps: no automated reading of the report body for price-target basis, unmentioned-issuer disclosures or rating-change language; and no audit trail at the level of the individual check.
