# Appraiser Errors & Omissions Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/real-estate-appraisers|Real Estate Appraisers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Underwrite the professional liability programmes appraisers carry, and analyse the claims — nearly all of which allege a valuation was wrong.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 underwriters, claims analysts, and risk managers
**Output:** Programme pricing and eligibility, claims analysis, loss control guidance, risk profiling by practice type
**Proprietary data:** Claims alleging valuation error, joined to the appraisal, the appraiser and the eventual outcome
**Clock:** Annual policy renewals
**Buyer:** Programme Underwriting Manager
