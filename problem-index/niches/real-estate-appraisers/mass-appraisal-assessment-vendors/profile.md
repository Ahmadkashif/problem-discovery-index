# Mass Appraisal & Assessment Systems

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/real-estate-appraisers|Real Estate Appraisers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Supply the computer-assisted mass appraisal systems county assessors value entire jurisdictions with, and perform the revaluation projects that reset assessed values for every parcel.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 valuation modellers, data collectors, and project staff across the vendors
**Output:** Mass appraisal models, revaluation projects, assessment rolls, ratio study support, appeal defence
**Proprietary data:** Parcel-level characteristic and sale data across many jurisdictions, owned by each assessor
**Clock:** Statutory revaluation cycles and assessment roll certification dates
**Buyer:** VP of Appraisal Services
