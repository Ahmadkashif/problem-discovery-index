# Estate, Tax & Litigation Valuation Advisory

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/real-estate-appraisers|Real Estate Appraisers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Produce appraisals for purposes where the number will be adversarially examined — estate settlement, gift tax, divorce, eminent domain, casualty loss and litigation — and defend them under cross-examination or IRS review.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 appraisers and analysts across the specialist practices
**Output:** Valuation reports for tax and litigation, retrospective valuations, expert testimony, review appraisals
**Proprietary data:** A record of which valuation approaches survived challenge, held individually
**Clock:** Filing deadlines, court dates, and statutory valuation dates
**Buyer:** Managing Principal
