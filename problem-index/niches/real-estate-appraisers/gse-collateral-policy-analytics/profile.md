# Secondary Market Collateral Policy & Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/real-estate-appraisers|Real Estate Appraisers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Set the collateral rules the entire US mortgage market follows — what an acceptable appraisal is, when one is required at all, and what the automated review flags — and run the analytics behind appraisal modernisation. Pass 1 identifies this layer as the source of the structural pressure reshaping the profession.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-1,500 economists, statisticians, valuation modellers, and policy analysts
**Output:** Collateral policy and eligibility rules, automated appraisal review scoring, appraisal waiver decisioning, market and property value trend research, modernisation pilots
**Proprietary data:** Essentially every appraisal submitted with a conforming loan for over a decade, in structured form, joined to loan performance — the largest appraisal corpus in existence and one nobody else can approach
**Clock:** Loan delivery requirements and policy release cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Collateral Policy / Chief Economist
