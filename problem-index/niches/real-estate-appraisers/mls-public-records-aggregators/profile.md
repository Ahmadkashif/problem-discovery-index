# MLS & Public Records Data Aggregators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/real-estate-appraisers|Real Estate Appraisers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Assemble listing data from hundreds of separate multiple listing services and recorded transactions from thousands of county recorders into normalised national datasets — the comparable sales every appraisal and every valuation model is built on. Pass 1 names MLS as the comparable sale source in every market.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-1,500 data operations staff, normalisation analysts, and engineers
**Output:** Normalised listing and transaction datasets, property characteristic files, sales history, market statistics
**Proprietary data:** Licensing relationships with hundreds of MLSs that publish under different schemas and rules, plus decades of recorded document extraction
**Clock:** Daily listing feeds and continuous recording
**Buyer:** VP of Data Operations
