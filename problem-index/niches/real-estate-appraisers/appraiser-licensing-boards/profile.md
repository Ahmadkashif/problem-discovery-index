# State Appraiser Licensing Boards

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/real-estate-appraisers|Real Estate Appraisers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
License and discipline appraisers, investigate complaints, and enforce compliance with uniform standards within each state.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 per state — investigators, reviewers, and licensing staff
**Output:** Licensure, complaint investigations, disciplinary actions, compliance reviews
**Proprietary data:** Complaint and discipline histories held state by state
**Clock:** Licence renewal cycles and complaint timelines
**Buyer:** Board executive director
