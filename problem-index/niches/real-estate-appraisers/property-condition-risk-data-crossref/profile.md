# Property Condition & Risk Data Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/real-estate-appraisers|Real Estate Appraisers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Assemble property characteristic, condition and hazard exposure data at address level and sell it to insurers, lenders and investors who need to understand a specific structure without visiting it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-2,000 data operations staff, analysts, and modellers
**Output:** Property attribute and condition data, hazard exposure scoring, replacement cost estimation, risk indicators
**Proprietary data:** National assembly of assessor, permit, imagery-derived and inspection-derived attributes maintained over decades
**Clock:** Underwriting and renewal cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Property Data
