# Hybrid Appraisal Inspection & Data Collection Networks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/real-estate-appraisers|Real Estate Appraisers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Field the networks of non-appraiser data collectors who inspect and photograph properties to a defined standard so a licensed appraiser can value them remotely — the operational half of the hybrid and desktop appraisal products Pass 1 identifies as reshaping the profession's fee income.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 quality analysts, network managers, standards staff, and computer vision engineers
**Output:** Standardised property data collection, floor plans and photographs, condition and quality ratings, collector quality scoring
**Proprietary data:** Standardised interior and exterior property captures at volume, with condition ratings attached
**Clock:** Loan turn time requirements
**Buyer:** VP of Valuation Products
