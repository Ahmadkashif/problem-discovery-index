# Appraisal Standards & Qualification Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/real-estate-appraisers|Real Estate Appraisers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Write the uniform standards every appraisal in the country must comply with, and set the education, experience and examination criteria for licensure. Pass 1 notes the profession is regulated at state level under these standards.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-120 standards staff, board members, and qualification analysts
**Output:** Uniform standards and revisions, qualification criteria, guidance and advisory opinions, examination content standards
**Proprietary data:** The standards development record and accumulated interpretive guidance
**Clock:** Biennial standards revision cycle and continuing education requirements
**Buyer:** Chief Standards Officer
