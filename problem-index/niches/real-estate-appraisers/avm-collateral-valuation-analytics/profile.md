# Automated Valuation Models & Collateral Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/real-estate-appraisers|Real Estate Appraisers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Produce a value for a residential property from data alone, at a cost of a few dollars and a latency of seconds, and sell it to the lenders, servicers, insurers, investors and secondary market participants who need to know what a house is worth. Pass 1 describes the effect on the operator layer directly: automated valuation is progressively absorbing routine low-complexity properties and reducing appraiser fee income, while desktop and hybrid products restructure what remains.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-1,500 data scientists, valuation modellers, property data operations staff, statisticians, and model risk analysts
**Output:** Point value estimates with confidence scores, forecast standard deviations, valuation ranges, collateral risk scoring, portfolio revaluation, model governance documentation
**Proprietary data:** Assembled national coverage of deed and mortgage records, assessor files, listing histories, and property characteristics, joined and maintained over decades across thousands of recording jurisdictions that publish inconsistently
**Clock:** Loan origination and servicing decisions expecting same-second response, secondary market delivery deadlines, and periodic portfolio revaluation cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / Head of Valuation Science
