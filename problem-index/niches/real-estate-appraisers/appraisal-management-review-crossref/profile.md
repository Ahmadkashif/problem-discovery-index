# Appraisal Management & Review Operations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/real-estate-appraisers|Real Estate Appraisers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Sit between lenders and appraisers — assigning orders, enforcing independence requirements, and reviewing completed reports for quality and compliance before they support a loan. The layer that determines which appraisers get work and at what fee.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 review appraisers, quality analysts, vendor managers, and compliance staff
**Output:** Order assignment and panel management, appraisal quality review, revision requests, turn time and quality scorecards
**Proprietary data:** Review findings across thousands of appraisers, including which adjustments and comparables get challenged and by whom
**Clock:** Loan closing dates and lender turn time requirements
**Buyer:** VP of Valuation Operations
