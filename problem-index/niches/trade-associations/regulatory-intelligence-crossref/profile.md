# Regulatory Intelligence Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/trade-associations|Trade Associations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Track and interpret regulatory activity across agencies and jurisdictions and publish the analysis that tells a regulated industry what is coming and what it means — the intelligence an association's government affairs function either buys or duplicates.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 regulatory analysts, attorneys, and content specialists
**Output:** Regulatory tracking and alerting, impact analysis, comparative jurisdiction analysis, compliance guidance, comment period intelligence
**Proprietary data:** Continuously maintained interpretation across agencies that publish inconsistently, accumulated over decades
**Clock:** Comment deadlines, effective dates, and enforcement actions
**Buyer:** Chief Content Officer / VP of Regulatory Analysis
