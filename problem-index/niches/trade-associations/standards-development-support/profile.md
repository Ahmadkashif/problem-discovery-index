# Standards Development Support Organizations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/trade-associations|Trade Associations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Run the consensus process behind voluntary standards — committee administration, balloting, comment resolution and accreditation compliance — for the associations whose standards get adopted into codes, contracts and regulation.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 standards staff, committee secretariats, editors, and accreditation specialists
**Output:** Consensus standards and revisions, ballot and comment resolution records, interpretations, adoption tracking, accreditation documentation
**Proprietary data:** The standards development record — proposals, negative ballots, technical objections and their resolution — accumulated across decades and committees
**Clock:** Ballot periods, revision cycles, and accreditation maintenance deadlines
**Buyer:** VP of Standards / Chief Technical Officer
