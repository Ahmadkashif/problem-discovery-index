# Grassroots Advocacy Platform Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/trade-associations|Trade Associations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Supply the platforms associations mobilise members through — matching members to their legislators, generating contacts, and reporting on campaign reach — plus the analytics on what actually moved a legislator.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 data engineers, analysts, and political researchers
**Output:** District matching and member mobilisation, contact delivery and tracking, campaign performance reporting, legislator engagement analytics
**Proprietary data:** Millions of constituent contacts joined to legislator, issue, timing and subsequent vote — the only large record of whether advocacy campaigns do anything
**Clock:** Legislative calendars and vote timing
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Head of Analytics
