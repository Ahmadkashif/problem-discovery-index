# Association Sector Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/trade-associations|Trade Associations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Study the association sector itself — membership trends, compensation, governance practice and revenue mix — for the associations that are themselves the members.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-20 researchers
**Output:** Membership and revenue benchmarking, compensation surveys, governance studies, sector forecasts
**Proprietary data:** Member submissions at moderate participation
**Clock:** Annual survey cycle
**Buyer:** VP of Research
