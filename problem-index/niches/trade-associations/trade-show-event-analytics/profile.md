# Trade Show & Event Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/trade-associations|Trade Associations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator/rollup

## What They Do
Run and measure the exhibitions and conferences that generate the majority of many associations' revenue — attendance auditing, exhibitor return measurement, floor traffic analysis, and pricing of space and sponsorship.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 audit staff, research analysts, and event data specialists
**Output:** Audited attendance and demographics, exhibitor lead and return reporting, floor traffic analytics, sponsorship valuation, pricing models
**Proprietary data:** Registration, attendance and exhibitor performance across many events and years
**Clock:** Fixed event dates that cannot move
**Buyer:** VP of Event Analytics / Chief Data Officer
