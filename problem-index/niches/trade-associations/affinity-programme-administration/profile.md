# Affinity & Member Insurance Programme Administration

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/trade-associations|Trade Associations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Design and administer the insurance and benefit programmes associations offer members — often the association's largest non-dues revenue line — and underwrite populations defined by occupation rather than by employer.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 underwriters, actuaries, and programme managers
**Output:** Programme design and pricing, eligibility rules, participation and loss reporting, royalty and revenue analysis
**Proprietary data:** Loss experience for occupationally defined populations that appear in no standard actuarial classification
**Clock:** Annual programme renewals
**Buyer:** VP of Programme Underwriting
