# Certification Programme Administration

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/trade-associations|Trade Associations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Build and run the professional certification programmes associations put their name on — job task analyses, item development, psychometric scoring, standard setting and accreditation maintenance — for bodies that own the credential and cannot staff the science behind it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 psychometricians, test developers, item bank managers, and accreditation specialists across the specialist firms
**Output:** Job task analyses, item banks and examinations, scoring and equating, passing standard studies, accreditation documentation, examination security
**Proprietary data:** Item response data across many credentialing programmes and populations, plus accumulated job analysis surveys of dozens of professions
**Clock:** Examination administration windows, recertification cycles, and accreditation review dates
**Buyer:** Chief Assessment Officer / VP of Credentialing
