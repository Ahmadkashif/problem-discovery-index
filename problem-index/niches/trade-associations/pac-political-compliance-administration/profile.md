# PAC & Political Compliance Administration

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/trade-associations|Trade Associations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Administer association political action committees and lobbying disclosure — contribution eligibility, receipt and disbursement compliance, and the federal and state filings that carry personal liability for a treasurer who gets them wrong.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 compliance analysts, treasurers, and political law specialists
**Output:** Contribution and disbursement compliance, federal and state disclosure filings, lobbying registration and reporting, gift and ethics rule guidance, audit response
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated filing and enforcement history across jurisdictions with materially different rules
**Clock:** Statutory filing deadlines — monthly, quarterly and pre-election — with penalties and personal liability attached
**Buyer:** Director of Political Compliance
