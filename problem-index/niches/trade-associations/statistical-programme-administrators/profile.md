# Antitrust-Safe Statistical Programme Administrators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/trade-associations|Trade Associations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Collect confidential operating and pricing data from competing member companies and publish aggregated benchmarks — work that exists because competitors legally cannot exchange this information among themselves and require an independent intermediary with strict aggregation rules to do it safely.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 survey statisticians, data operations staff, and programme managers
**Output:** Aggregated industry statistics, cost and pricing benchmarks, capacity and shipment series, participation reporting, methodology documentation
**Proprietary data:** Decades of confidential member submissions in industries where no public data exists — often the only measurement of a sector that exists anywhere
**Clock:** Monthly, quarterly and annual publication schedules that members plan against
**Buyer:** Managing Director / Director of Statistical Services
