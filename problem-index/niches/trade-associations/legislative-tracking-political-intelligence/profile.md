# Legislative Tracking & Political Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/trade-associations|Trade Associations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Track every bill in fifty state legislatures and Congress, map them to the issues a client cares about, follow committee movement and vote history, and profile the legislators and staff who decide outcomes — the core operating tool of every association government affairs department.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 legislative analysts, data engineers, taxonomists, and political researchers
**Output:** Bill tracking and issue tagging, committee and floor movement alerts, legislator and staff profiles, vote and sponsorship analytics, lobbying disclosure data, passage likelihood indicators
**Proprietary data:** Normalised bill text and status across fifty-two legislative bodies that publish in incompatible formats, plus decades of vote, sponsorship and disclosure records
**Clock:** Legislative sessions, crossover deadlines, committee calendars and adjournment dates
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Product
