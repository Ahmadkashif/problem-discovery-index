# Association Management Companies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/trade-associations|Trade Associations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator/rollup

## What They Do
Operate smaller associations under contract — staffing the executive function, running membership, events and finance across dozens of client organisations simultaneously.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 across the larger firms, though the analytical function within that is small
**Output:** Membership and financial reporting, benchmarking across managed clients, governance support, programme administration
**Proprietary data:** Operating and membership data across dozens of associations — a comparative view no individual association has
**Clock:** Board meeting and fiscal year cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Operating Officer
