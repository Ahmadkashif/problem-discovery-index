# Exempt Organisation Tax & Compliance Advisory

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/trade-associations|Trade Associations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Advise associations on the tax rules unique to exempt organisations — unrelated business income, lobbying expenditure limits, non-deductible dues disclosure, private benefit and intermediate sanctions — and prepare the annual returns that are public documents.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 tax specialists, exempt organisation attorneys, and preparers
**Output:** Annual information return preparation, unrelated business income analysis, lobbying proxy tax calculations, structural and governance advice, examination defence
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated positions and examination outcomes across many exempt organisations
**Clock:** Annual return deadlines and examination timelines
**Buyer:** Partner / Practice Leader
