**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to engage the disabled experts who can actually say whether a site works as specialists rather than as research participants — and whoever builds that practice takes the account.

## The Problem
Expert witness practice solved the general problem of engaging specialist judgement: credentials are established and stated, the expert is instructed rather than observed, the opinion is signed and carries the expert's standing, fees reflect the expertise, and the opinion can be tested. It exists across many technical fields and is why specialist judgement is treated as evidence. Accessibility assessment depends on comparable expertise and routes it through research recruitment.

## What Already Exists
Credential establishment and declaration; instruction rather than observation; signed opinions carrying professional standing; fee structures reflecting expertise; and mechanisms for testing an opinion.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a technical assessment delivered at volume rather than a one-off opinion. It requires: (1) assessments delivered repeatedly at commercial cadence rather than as occasional opinions, so the engagement model must be lighter than expert witness practice while keeping the professional standing — this is the substantive difference; (2) expertise grounded in daily use of a technology rather than in formal qualification, so credentialing must recognise practice; (3) a field with no professional body to award standing; (4) assessments that feed a product backlog as well as, sometimes, a legal process; and (5) accessibility of the working tools themselves as a precondition, which expert practice never has to consider.

## Target Customer
Accessibility firms, assistive technology specialists, professional and advocacy bodies, and client accessibility functions.

## Impact If Solved
Expert witness practice established how specialist judgement is credentialed, instructed and paid for. Volume assessment on a commercial cadence, credentialed on practice rather than qualification, is what has to be lighter here.
