# Blocker Attribution & Evidence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/digital-accessibility-firms|Digital Accessibility Firms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to say which specific defect blocked which task, how much that matters, and in a form a product team will act on and a legal process will respect — and whoever states that takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$180M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — lists of criteria
**Target Buyer:** Client leadership and counsel
**Automation Potential:** Medium — attribution, ranking and documentation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This half turns a test result into something usable. A flow failed — which of the twelve defects encountered actually stopped it, how severe is that relative to everything else in the backlog, what does it mean for how many users, and how should it be stated so that a product team prioritises it and a legal process can rely on it. It is attribution, ranking and documentation sold to the client and increasingly to their counsel, and it is what separates an outcome statement from an observation.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A findings list ordered by criterion, with severity ratings assigned by convention. The gaps: no attribution of a completion failure to a specific defect; no severity based on task impact; no estimate of affected population; no defensible evidence format; and no statement a product team can prioritise against.
