# The Manual Auditor

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/digital-accessibility-firms|Digital Accessibility Firms]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to support an auditor working a site keyboard-only and then twice with screen readers for weeks, documenting each failure against a criterion — and whoever supports that work takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$120M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — no support
**Target Buyer:** Audit leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — capture, documentation and reuse

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
An expert works through a site keyboard-only, then with a screen reader, then again with a second screen reader, documenting each failure against a criterion, for weeks. The work is exacting, repetitive and entirely manual: navigate, observe, judge, locate, classify, write up, screenshot, repeat. The judgement is skilled and irreplaceable; everything around it is clerical, consumes most of the time, and is done in a spreadsheet.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A browser, a screen reader, a criteria checklist and a spreadsheet. The gaps: no capture of what was observed; no assistance with criterion classification; no reuse of judgements on unchanged components; no location capture; and no support for maintaining concentration across weeks of exacting work.
