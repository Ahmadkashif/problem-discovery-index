# Overlay & Quick-Fix Claims

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/digital-accessibility-firms|Digital Accessibility Firms]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting over what a conformance claim is worth when it rests on a script, and whoever can establish what a remediation actually achieved takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$160M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — contested claims
**Target Buyer:** Legal and procurement
**Automation Potential:** Medium — verification and claim substantiation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Overlay widgets grew directly out of the litigation incentive — products promising rapid conformance through a script — and are contested, with disability advocacy organisations including the National Federation of the Blind having objected publicly and litigation continuing against sites using them. Underneath the controversy is a measurement question that applies to any remediation: what did it actually change for users, and what claim does that support. Nobody supplies an independent answer, so buyers choose between competing assertions.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A vendor's claim, a badge on a site and a legal opinion. The gaps: no independent verification of what any remediation achieved; no before-and-after measurement with assistive technology; no substantiation standard for a conformance claim; no disclosure of what a script can and cannot affect; and no route for users to contest a claim.
