# Assistive Technology Testing at Scale

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/digital-accessibility-firms|Digital Accessibility Firms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to test whether flows complete across the screen reader, browser and operating system combinations users actually have, at a cost that permits doing it repeatedly — and whoever does takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$220M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — expert time
**Target Buyer:** Audit and delivery leadership
**Automation Potential:** Medium — coverage, tooling and partial automation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This half is about the cost and coverage of establishing the fact. A flow may complete with one screen reader and fail with another, on one browser and not the next, and each combination requires a person with real expertise working through it. The combinatorics make comprehensive testing unaffordable, so it is done once a year on a sample, and the finding goes stale immediately. Reducing the cost per combination is a delivery problem sold into the firm's own operation, independent of what is done with the results.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Expert testers, a set of devices, and a schedule that permits one pass. The gaps: no automation of the repeatable parts of a flow walk; no prioritisation of which combinations matter; no reuse of test definitions across releases; no remote or distributed testing capability; and no measurement of coverage achieved.
