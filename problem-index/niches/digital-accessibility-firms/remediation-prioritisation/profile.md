# Remediation Prioritisation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/digital-accessibility-firms|Digital Accessibility Firms]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get four thousand findings into a backlog in an order somebody can work through, and whoever makes that list actionable takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$220M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — unranked lists
**Target Buyer:** Engineering leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — grouping, routing and ranking

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The audit delivers four thousand findings into a backlog that already has everything else in it, with no statement of which ones actually stop anyone doing anything. The engineering team cannot triage a list of that size against commercial work, so it is worked through by whoever is available, in whatever order is easiest, or not at all. The remediation phase is where accessibility programmes stall, and the cause is the shape of the deliverable rather than a lack of will.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A findings spreadsheet, an import into the issue tracker and a severity column. The gaps: no grouping by root cause; no routing to the responsible team; no ranking by user impact; no estimate of effort; and no tracking of what the remediation achieved.
