# Document & Media Accessibility

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/digital-accessibility-firms|Digital Accessibility Firms]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make tens of thousands of documents, videos and images usable without remediating each one by hand, and whoever automates the bulk takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$50M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — partial
**Target Buyer:** Content operations
**Automation Potential:** Very high — generation, tagging and verification

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Organisations hold enormous document and media estates: policy documents, forms, reports, recorded sessions, product imagery. Each needs structure, alternative text, captions or a transcript. The work is high-volume, largely mechanical and currently done by hand or not at all, which is why most such estates are inaccessible in bulk. It is separable from interface accessibility in tooling, skills and buyer, and it is the part of the problem most amenable to automation.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A remediation service priced per document, a captioning vendor and an alternative text field somebody fills in. The gaps: no prioritisation by what is actually accessed; no bulk processing pipeline; no quality verification of generated output; no prevention at the point of authoring; and no handling of the back catalogue at all.
