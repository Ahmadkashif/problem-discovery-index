# The Budget Case Rebuilt Every Year

**Niche:** [[niches/seo-tooling-vendors/outcome-connection/profile|Outcome Connection]]
**Industry:** [[industries/seo-tooling-vendors|SEO Tooling Vendors]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Every budget cycle the SEO spends two weeks assembling the same spreadsheet from the same four sources to prove the channel is worth funding.
**Tags:** #data-integration #descriptive-statistics #evaluation-metrics #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #worker-facing #revenue-impact #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to connect a position change to a revenue change inside the customer's own systems — and whoever does that solves the hardest recurring task in the customer's job.

## The Problem
Budget season arrives. The SEO exports rankings, exports analytics, exports conversion data, exports the content production log, and spends two weeks joining them in a spreadsheet with a set of assumptions they invent each year because last year's file is gone or was built by someone who left. The resulting case is presented, questioned on its assumptions, and survives or does not. Next year the same two weeks. The underlying data has not changed, the join has not changed, and the work is redone from scratch annually because nothing persists it.

## Why It's Still Broken
The business case is treated as a document produced for a meeting rather than as a model that should exist continuously — that framing is why it is rebuilt rather than maintained. The join spans systems owned by different teams and nobody owns the combination. Assumptions are invented ad hoc, so they cannot be inherited or defended. And the work is invisible until it is needed urgently.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Make the case a standing artefact. Build the join once and keep it current, which is the fix and converts two weeks a year into a dashboard that is always ready. Record the assumptions explicitly with their sources, so they are inherited, inspectable and improvable rather than reinvented — the argument that loses a budget is usually an assumption that cannot be defended. Version the model so last year's case is retrievable and this year's is comparable, which also survives staff turnover. Track predicted against realised value, which is the strongest possible evidence at the next budget conversation and which almost nobody accumulates. Produce the case in the finance function's own format and vocabulary, since the audience is finance and the translation is currently done badly under time pressure. Include the counterfactual of not investing, because that is the decision being made and a case that only shows upside answers a different question. Separate maintenance from growth investment, as they have different returns and conflating them weakens both arguments. Surface the case continuously rather than annually, so the channel's value is visible when decisions are made outside budget season. Make it exportable and presentable without rework. And measure how long it takes to answer what is SEO worth, because in most organisations the answer is two weeks and it should be a click.

## Who Feels the Pain
SEOs spending weeks annually rebuilding an argument; finance functions receiving cases they cannot evaluate; and channels that lose budget because their case was assembled under pressure.

## Impact If Fixed
The case is treated as a document for a meeting rather than a model that should persist, so it is rebuilt annually from unchanged data. A maintained join with recorded assumptions and a predicted-versus-realised track record turns two weeks into a click.
