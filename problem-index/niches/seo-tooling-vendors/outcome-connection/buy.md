## The Problem
Marketing attribution is a developed practice with substantial tooling: multi-touch models, marketing mix modelling, incrementality testing and unified measurement platforms that connect channel activity to revenue. Paid channels are instrumented thoroughly because spend is visible and must be justified. Organic search, which frequently delivers more sessions than any paid channel, is connected to revenue by a session count and a conversion rate, because the channel has no spend line and therefore no procurement pressure to prove itself.

## What Already Exists
Multi-touch attribution platforms; marketing mix modelling; incrementality experimentation; unified measurement frameworks; and customer journey stitching across touchpoints.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a channel with no spend, no impression log and a partially withheld data source. It requires: (1) the input being a position and a visibility state rather than an impression and a cost, so the standard attribution model has nothing to attribute from — reconstructing an exposure measure is the substantive work; (2) query-level data that is aggregated and partially withheld by the search engines, which caps the achievable granularity and means the honest unit is a topic cluster rather than a keyword; (3) a long tail of low-volume entry points where individual attribution is meaningless and aggregation is essential; (4) no spend variable, which breaks marketing mix models that regress outcomes on budget and requires a visibility index in its place; and (5) incrementality testing that cannot be run by withholding spend, so the experiment must use geographic or page-level variation instead.

## Target Customer
In-house marketing measurement teams, SEO tooling vendors, and attribution vendors for whom organic search is an unmodelled channel.

## Impact If Solved
Paid channels are instrumented because spend forces the question and organic has no spend line to force it. Reconstructing an exposure measure from visibility, and replacing the budget variable with a visibility index, is what lets mature attribution methods apply at all.
