# The Small Site Owner

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/seo-tooling-vendors|SEO Tooling Vendors]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell a small business the two things to do this month without teaching them a discipline — and whoever does that reaches the enormous audience the category's tooling structurally excludes.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$250M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~6% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — mostly nothing at all
**Target Buyer:** Vendor growth teams and small businesses
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the advice can be fully generated

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The tooling in this category is built for people whose job is SEO. A restaurant owner, a plumber, a local retailer or a two-person software company has a website, gets some of their customers through search, and cannot use any of it — the vocabulary, the dashboards, the metrics and the price all assume a practitioner. They either buy an agency they cannot evaluate, or do nothing. This is a distinct contest because the product required is not a simplified version of the professional one; it is a different product that produces decisions rather than data.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Professional tools with simplified tiers, website builder plugins offering checklists, agency retainers, and directory listing services. The gaps: interfaces requiring domain expertise; metrics that are meaningless without training; advice that is generic; no prioritisation by what matters for this business; and no way for the owner to tell whether an agency is doing anything.
