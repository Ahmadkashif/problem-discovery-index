## The Problem
Estimating a population quantity from a sample is among the best-developed areas of applied statistics: frame construction, stratification, weighting, variance estimation, design effects, non-response adjustment and the reporting conventions that go with them. Official statistics and market research run on it. A new measurement category is being constructed right now — generative answer visibility — by engineers who have never needed sampling, using hand-picked prompt lists and unweighted counts, and the numbers are already appearing in board reports.

## What Already Exists
Probability sampling design with stratification; weighting and post-stratification; variance and design-effect estimation; repeated-measures tracking design; and established reporting conventions for estimates.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is from sampling humans to sampling a stochastic system with an unbounded question space. It requires: (1) a frame over natural-language questions rather than over a listed population, which has no precedent and must be constructed from demand signals — this construction is the substantive contribution and where a defensible method is made or lost; (2) two distinct variance sources, the sampling of questions and the system's own non-determinism, which must be separated rather than conflated and which classical designs do not distinguish; (3) sampling cost near zero, inverting the usual economics and moving the entire design problem to representativeness; (4) a population that shifts as the underlying system is updated, which makes tracking design rather than one-off estimation the right frame; and (5) an audience with no statistical training, so intervals must be presented in a way that informs rather than confuses a marketer.

## Target Customer
Visibility measurement vendors, enterprise measurement teams, and survey research firms for whom generative surfaces are an unclaimed application.

## Impact If Solved
A new measurement category is being built by people with no sampling tradition while the numbers already reach board reports. Constructing the question frame and separating question-sampling variance from system non-determinism is where a defensible method is made.
