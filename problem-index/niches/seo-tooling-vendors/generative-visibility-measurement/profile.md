# Generative Visibility Measurement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/seo-tooling-vendors|SEO Tooling Vendors]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce a defensible estimate of how often a brand appears in generated answers across a surface that is personalised, non-deterministic and closed — and whoever establishes that method sets the category's next currency.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$700M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~18% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — a handful of prompts, no method
**Target Buyer:** Vendor data leadership and enterprise measurement
**Automation Potential:** Very High — a sampling and estimation problem

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is a measurement contest, won by whoever builds a defensible method for estimating presence on a surface that cannot be enumerated. The answer varies by phrasing, by user, by session and by time; there is no interface that returns it; and the question the user asked is not a keyword. Everyone currently ships a figure derived from running a small set of prompts, with no sampling frame, no weighting and no uncertainty. The first vendor to publish a method that survives scrutiny will define how the category is measured, which is a position with unusual durability.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Prompt sets run periodically, screenshot capture, share-of-voice figures with unstated derivation, and manual checking. The gaps: no sampling frame over the space of questions; no weighting to real demand; no separation of system variance from genuine change; no confidence intervals; and no coverage of the several answer surfaces that exist.
