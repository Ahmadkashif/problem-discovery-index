# Entity & Citation Optimisation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/seo-tooling-vendors|SEO Tooling Vendors]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to work out what makes a generated answer cite one source rather than another — and whoever establishes that gets to sell the practice that replaces a twenty-year-old one.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$500M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — speculation and folklore
**Target Buyer:** Content and brand teams, and the agencies advising them
**Automation Potential:** High — the corpus of observed citations is the evidence

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Getting cited is a different problem from getting ranked and nobody yet knows how it works. There is no published signal, no interface to query, no position to optimise toward, and the mechanism combines what the underlying model knows about an entity, what it retrieves at answer time, and how a source is structured. The practice being sold today is largely folklore reasoning by analogy from ranking. The contest is to replace that with something evidenced, and whoever does it owns the practice layer of a twenty-year-old industry's successor.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Structured data recommendations carried over from search, advice to build authority, speculation about content formatting, and pattern-matching on observed citations. The gaps: no evidence base linking source characteristics to citation; no separation of model knowledge from retrieval; no treatment of entity representation across the web; no testing method; and advice indistinguishable from a decade of ranking guidance.
