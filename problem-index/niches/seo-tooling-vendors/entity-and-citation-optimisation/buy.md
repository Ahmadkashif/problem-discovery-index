## The Problem
Representing things and their relationships, resolving references to them across sources, and reasoning about what is known is a developed field with real infrastructure: knowledge graphs, entity linking, ontologies, provenance tracking and consistency checking. Organisations use it for master data, for search and for analytics. SEO adopted the word entity a decade ago, used it to justify structured data markup, and continued optimising individual pages against keyword lists.

## What Already Exists
Knowledge graph construction and entity linking; ontology and schema standards; provenance and source tracking; entity resolution across heterogeneous sources; and consistency and conflict detection.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to influencing an external model's representation of an entity you do not control. It requires: (1) the target graph being inside a system you cannot inspect or edit, so the intervention is indirect through the web corpus — which is the entire game and has no analogue in enterprise knowledge management; (2) the objective being citation and correct description rather than internal data quality, which changes what a good representation means; (3) working across the whole public web rather than within an organisation's sources, since what third parties say about an entity may matter more than what the brand's own site says; (4) verification by observing generated outputs rather than by querying the graph, which is an inference problem the field has not needed; and (5) an audience of marketers rather than data engineers, so the practice must be expressible without ontology vocabulary.

## Target Customer
Brand and content teams, SEO agencies building a successor practice, visibility tooling vendors, and knowledge graph vendors for whom external-model influence is unserved.

## Impact If Solved
The field has the representation methods and SEO used the word without the substance. The target graph is inside a system you cannot inspect, so the intervention runs through the public web corpus and verification runs through observed outputs.
