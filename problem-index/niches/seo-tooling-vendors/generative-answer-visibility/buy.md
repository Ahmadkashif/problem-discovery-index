## The Problem
Measuring an unobservable population through sampling is what survey research does. Brand tracking studies estimate awareness, consideration and message association from samples, report confidence intervals, weight for representativeness, and manage the trade-off between cost and precision explicitly. The discipline is old, rigorous and well understood. SEO tooling never needed it, because the search results page was enumerable — you could simply look. Now it is not, and the category has no sampling tradition at all.

## What Already Exists
Survey sampling design with stratification and weighting; confidence interval reporting as standard; tracking study methodology for trend measurement; message association and share-of-voice measurement; and cost-precision trade-off management.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is from sampling people to sampling a generative system. It requires: (1) the population being the space of possible phrasings and contexts rather than a population of humans, which needs a sampling frame nobody has constructed — building that frame is the substantive work and is where a defensible methodology would be established; (2) responses that vary by personalisation, session and time, so the design must separate genuine change from system variance, which is exactly what tracking methodology is built for and has never been applied here; (3) enormous achievable sample sizes at low cost, which makes precision cheap and shifts the design problem entirely toward representativeness; (4) an output a marketer will act on weekly rather than a quarterly study, changing cadence and presentation; and (5) no panel provider or established norms, so the vendor must both define the method and convince the market of it.

## Target Customer
SEO and brand visibility vendors, enterprise marketing measurement teams, and market research firms for whom generative surfaces are an unclaimed measurement problem.

## Impact If Solved
The results page was enumerable so the category never needed sampling, and now it is not. Constructing a sampling frame over phrasings and contexts is the substantive work, and cheap sample sizes make representativeness rather than precision the design problem.
