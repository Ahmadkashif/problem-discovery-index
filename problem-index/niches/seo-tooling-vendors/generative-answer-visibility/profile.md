# Generative Answer Visibility

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/seo-tooling-vendors|SEO Tooling Vendors]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** This niche is not terminal — measuring whether a brand appears in a generated answer and getting it cited there are different contests with different winners, and they are stated separately in the sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.2B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~30% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — a tab added beside the rank tracker
**Target Buyer:** Vendor product leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — both halves are model-driven

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The entire measurement stack reports position in a list of links, at a moment when the answer sits above the list, is generated fresh each time, and cites whoever it cites. This is where the category's premise broke in public and it is the largest concentration of contested value in it: every vendor's product assumes a ranked list of ten results and a click-through relationship that has been eroding for a decade.

It is also **not terminal**. The label names a surface rather than a contest, and writing the contested statement produces two sentences describing different winners. One fight is measurement: establishing whether a brand appears, how often, and in what framing, across answers that are personalised, non-deterministic, unavailable through any interface, and generated for questions nobody phrases the same way twice — a sampling and estimation problem. The other is influence: becoming the source a generated answer cites, which is a question of entity representation, authority and how content is structured for retrieval, with no ranking signal to optimise against. The skills barely overlap. The two contests are stated in the sub-niches.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Rank trackers reporting position, early generative-visibility tabs sampling a handful of prompts, manual spot checks, and share-of-voice estimates with no stated method. The gaps: no principled sampling of a non-deterministic surface; no uncertainty on any figure; no model of what causes citation; no connection between the two halves; and a product architecture built around a ranked list.
