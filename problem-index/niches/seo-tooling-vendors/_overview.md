# Niche Analysis — SEO Tooling Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/seo-tooling-vendors|SEO Tooling Vendors]]

## Niche Selection

This is a category whose measurement instrument is being deprecated by the thing it measures. Rank tracking is still accurate and no longer means what customers buy it for; the numbers surrounding it are estimates presented as integers; and the outcome the customer actually cares about sits on the other side of a wall in their own analytics. Underneath all of it the vendors hold a longitudinal record of what changed on millions of sites and what happened next — a natural-experiment corpus used to draw a line chart. The eight niches below split the category by what competitors actually fight over.

## Why These Niches

Generative visibility takes the largest share because it is where the category's premise broke in public, and because a vendor that cannot say whether a brand appears in a generated answer is selling a measurement of a surface that is shrinking. Demand estimation is second because search volume and difficulty are the numbers every content budget is built on and they are extrapolations displayed as integers. The two low-digitized niches are the ones where tooling produces output nobody can act on well: an issue list prioritised by a generic severity label, and a brief that manufactures the interchangeable page everyone complains about. The two underserved audiences are the in-house SEO with a day to explain a traffic drop and the small site owner who cannot use any of this. The two automatable niches are the corpus nobody exploits — a decade of natural experiments across millions of sites — and the wall between a ranking and a revenue figure.

## Filter Notes

Seven of the eight level-1 niches are terminal. Generative Answer Visibility is not: the label names a surface rather than a contest, and writing the contested statement produces two sentences describing different winners. One fight is measurement — establishing whether and how often a brand appears in answers that are personalised, non-deterministic, unavailable through any interface and generated fresh for questions nobody phrases the same way twice, which is a sampling and estimation problem. The other is influence — getting cited, which is an entity, authority and source-structure problem with no ranking signal to optimise against. A competitor can lead decisively on either while being ordinary at the other, and the skills barely overlap. It therefore decomposes into **Generative Visibility Measurement** and **Entity & Citation Optimisation**.

Two candidates were considered and rejected. **Web crawling infrastructure** is a genuine strength of these vendors by the industry's own account and is not where the contest is; the fight over crawling at scale belongs to [[industries/web-data-extraction-firms|Web Data Extraction Firms]]. **Paid search management** shares a buyer but is the contest of [[industries/performance-marketing-agencies|Performance Marketing Agencies]] and [[industries/programmatic-ad-platforms|Programmatic Ad Platforms]].
