# The In-House SEO

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/seo-tooling-vendors|SEO Tooling Vendors]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give the in-house SEO an answer when traffic drops rather than four charts that correlate — and whoever does that changes whether the role can defend itself inside a business.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$350M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — correlation and a deadline
**Target Buyer:** Marketing and SEO leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — the diagnosis is inferable from data already held

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Organic traffic falls, the algorithm changed, a competitor moved, the site shipped a release, and the SEO has a day to explain which — with tools that show correlation and nothing else. The in-house SEO occupies an unusual position: accountable for a channel they do not control, using tooling that describes rather than explains, in an organisation that will attribute any decline to them. Their recurring crisis is a diagnostic one, and it is distinct from the causal modelling work because this is about the person, the deadline and what they can defend in a meeting.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Rank trackers, analytics, search console data, algorithm update trackers, and a spreadsheet to line them up. The gaps: no attribution of a change to a cause; no separation of site, competitor and algorithm effects; no record of what the site itself changed; no confidence in any explanation; and a deadline that arrives before any analysis could be done properly.
