# Site Audit & Impact Prioritisation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/seo-tooling-vendors|SEO Tooling Vendors]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to rank a site's issues by what fixing them would do for that site — and whoever does that turns an eleven-thousand-row export into a ticket an engineering team will actually take.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — crawling solved, prioritisation absent
**Target Buyer:** Technical SEO and engineering leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — impact estimation is learnable from the corpus

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every crawler produces a prioritised list of site issues, prioritised by a severity label the vendor assigned in general, not by what fixing it would do for this site. The crawling is excellent and the output is a spreadsheet of eleven thousand rows sorted by a word — critical, high, medium — that was decided once for all sites. The SEO must then convince an engineering team to spend sprint capacity on items they cannot attach a number to. This is distinct from the causal work: that explains what happened, this decides what to do next.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Site crawlers with issue detection, generic severity labels, issue count trends, and exports to spreadsheets. The gaps: severity assigned universally rather than per site; no estimate of the traffic or revenue effect of a fix; no accounting for engineering cost; no grouping of rows into actionable work; and no evidence when the fix is done.
