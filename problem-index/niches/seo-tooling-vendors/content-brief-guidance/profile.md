# Content Brief & Production Guidance

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/seo-tooling-vendors|SEO Tooling Vendors]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to guide a writer toward something worth reading rather than toward a statistical average of whoever currently ranks — and whoever does that stops the tooling from manufacturing the page everyone complains about.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$350M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — widely used, actively harmful
**Target Buyer:** Content teams and editors
**Automation Potential:** High — the guidance is generated either way

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Content briefs generated by tooling tell a writer to use a term nine times, hit 1,800 words and cover twelve subheadings taken from whoever currently ranks, which produces the interchangeable page everybody complains about. The tooling works by averaging the top results and instructing the writer to reproduce the average, which is a mechanism that guarantees convergence — and the convergence is the thing the search engines and the audience are both reacting against. This is a distinct contest because the product actively produces the outcome the category complains about.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Brief generators computing term frequency and structure from top-ranking pages, content scores measuring conformance to that average, and word count targets. The gaps: guidance derived from the current average rather than from what works; scores rewarding conformance; no notion of what a page adds; no editorial judgement; and a mechanism that converges everything toward sameness.
