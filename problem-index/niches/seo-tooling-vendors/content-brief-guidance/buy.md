## The Problem
Editorial commissioning is a real craft with real method: identify what is not being said, decide what a piece must add, brief a writer on angle and audience and evidence, and edit against whether it delivers. Newsrooms and magazines have practised it for a very long time and it reliably produces work people choose to read. SEO content production replaced that brief with a checklist derived from competitors, and then wondered why the output is indistinguishable.

## What Already Exists
Editorial commissioning practice with angle and audience definition; gap analysis against existing coverage; sourcing and evidence standards; structural editing against contribution; and audience-first content strategy.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is from a commissioning editor to software briefing writers at volume. It requires: (1) gap identification automated across the existing corpus rather than performed by an editor who has read everything, which is now tractable and is the substantive build; (2) volume of hundreds of briefs where an editorial process handles a handful, so the judgement must be encoded rather than exercised per piece; (3) a retrieval objective alongside a reader objective, since the piece must be findable as well as worth reading and the editorial tradition ignores the first; (4) writers who are frequently not journalists and need more explicit specification than an experienced commissioning brief provides; and (5) measurement against traffic and citation rather than against editorial reception, which gives this version a feedback loop that traditional editorial never had and should exploit.

## Target Customer
Content teams and editors, SEO tooling vendors, content agencies, and publishing tooling vendors for whom search-driven commissioning is unserved.

## Impact If Solved
Editorial practice reliably produces work people choose to read and SEO replaced its brief with a competitor checklist. Automating gap identification across the corpus is the substantive build, and the traffic feedback loop is something traditional editorial never had.
