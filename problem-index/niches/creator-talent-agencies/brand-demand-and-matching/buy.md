**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get the whole roster in front of the right brands rather than the same twenty brands in front of the same forty creators — and whoever widens the demand side monetises the roster the relationships never reach.

## The Problem
Business-to-business sales solved systematic demand generation: territory and account coverage so nothing is unworked, intent and trigger signals to time outreach, account scoring for prioritisation, pipeline measurement, and a distinction between relationship selling and coverage. Agencies sell a bespoke high-value product to a definable set of buyers and run on relationships and inbound, which is how sales organisations operated before any of that existed.

## What Already Exists
Territory and account coverage models; intent and trigger signal monitoring; account scoring and prioritisation; pipeline and conversion measurement; and outbound sequencing practice.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a product that is a specific person's audience. It requires: (1) the match between a creator and a brand being the substance of the pitch, so account scoring must be per creator-brand pair rather than per account — this is the substantive difference and multiplies the problem; (2) a relationship-led culture that treats systematic outbound as beneath the business; (3) inventory that is people with preferences about who they work with; (4) a small buyer universe where over-contacting damages the relationships the agency depends on; and (5) managers who own relationships and will resist a system touching them.

## Target Customer
Partnership and commercial leadership, managers, creators, and sales technology vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Sales organisations industrialised coverage and agencies still run on address books. Scoring per creator-brand pair rather than per account is the adaptation, and it is what makes the long tail of the roster reachable.
