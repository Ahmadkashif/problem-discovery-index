# Half the Roster Was Never Pitched

**Niche:** [[niches/creator-talent-agencies/brand-demand-and-matching/profile|Brand Demand & Matching]]
**Industry:** [[industries/creator-talent-agencies|Creator Talent Agencies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Nobody has put this creator in front of a brand in five months and nobody has noticed.
**Tags:** #quick-win #descriptive-statistics #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact #workflow-orchestration #automation #worker-facing #confidence-intervals
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get the whole roster in front of the right brands rather than the same twenty brands in front of the same forty creators — and whoever widens the demand side monetises the roster the relationships never reach.

## The Problem
Attention within an agency follows revenue, so the creators who are already earning receive the pitches and the ones who are not receive fewer, which is a loop. A creator can go months without being put in front of anyone, conclude the agency is not working for them, and leave — and the agency will not have noticed, because nothing reports pitch activity per creator. The signed representation agreement is a commitment the agency is quietly not honouring.

## Why It's Still Broken
Activity is tracked by deal rather than by creator, so a creator with no deals generates no records and is therefore invisible — a system that logs outcomes cannot show the absence of attempts. Managers prioritise rationally toward revenue. Nobody owns roster coverage. And the departing creator gives a different reason on the way out.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Report pitches per creator, not deals. Track pitch activity per creator and report who has not been pitched in thirty, sixty and ninety days, which is the fix and produces a list nobody currently has. Set a coverage expectation, since an unmeasured obligation is unmet by default. Review the list weekly, as it is short and actionable. Ask why a creator is not being pitched, because the answer is sometimes that they are hard to place and sometimes that nobody got to it. Package the uncovered creators for brands the agency already knows, which is the cheapest available action. Report conversion by creator, so the difference between unpitched and unsellable is visible. Tell the creator what has been pitched on their behalf, since the absence of communication is what makes them leave. Rebalance manager loads if one manager's creators are systematically uncovered. Track departures against pitch activity, which will show the relationship immediately. And treat an uncovered creator as an operational failure rather than as a market outcome.

## Who Feels the Pain
Creators who signed and are waiting; managers whose attention is rationally elsewhere; agencies losing roster members for reasons they never identify; and firms with signed representation obligations they are not meeting.

## Impact If Fixed
A system that logs outcomes cannot show the absence of attempts, so an unpitched creator is invisible. Reporting pitches per creator produces the list of people the agency is quietly failing and it is short enough to act on weekly.
