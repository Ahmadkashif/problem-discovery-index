# Beyond the Address Book

**Industry:** [[industries/creator-talent-agencies|Creator Talent Agencies]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** The agency's commercial reach is the union of its managers' contact lists, and the roster is larger than that.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get the whole roster in front of the right brands rather than the same twenty brands in front of the same forty creators — and whoever widens the demand side monetises the roster the relationships never reach.

## The Problem
Deals happen where relationships exist. A manager knows a set of brands and pitches their creators to them; another manager knows a different set. Creators outside those intersections wait for inbound. A brand whose audience overlaps exactly with a creator in the roster will never hear about them if nobody at the agency knows anyone there. The constraint is not the roster's quality or the brands' budgets — it is that introductions only travel along existing relationships.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Deal flow is a relationship asset, so widening it systematically threatens the thing managers are valued for — a business built on who people know does not build a system that makes knowing people less necessary. Fit is treated as a judgement rather than a measurable property. Outbound to brands without a relationship feels like a downgrade. And nobody measures which creators are never pitched.

## What to Build
Model fit and pitch systematically. Model audience and brand fit from creator audience composition, content themes, past deal performance and brand category, which is the core and identifies matches no relationship would produce. Monitor brand demand signals — campaign launches, category spend, competitor activity, hiring — so outreach is timed rather than cold. Report which creators have not been pitched in a quarter, since the long tail of the roster is where the unmonetised value sits and nobody looks. Support outbound to brands nobody knows, as the relationship constraint is the binding one and can be relaxed with evidence of fit. Use past deal outcomes to sharpen the fit model, because performance against a brand category is the strongest signal and is recorded. Package the roster by audience rather than by manager, which is how a brand thinks and not how the agency is organised. Route inbound enquiries to the best fit rather than to whoever received them. Measure pitch-to-deal conversion by fit score, so the model is validated commercially. Preserve the manager's relationship advantage where it exists rather than replacing it, since adoption depends on that. And report roster coverage as a metric, because the unpitched creator is a signed liability.

## Target Customer
Partnership and commercial leadership, managers, creators waiting for deals, and influencer marketing platforms on the brand side.

## Impact If Built
A business built on who people know does not build a system that makes knowing people less necessary, so the roster's reach is a set of address books. Modelling fit and monitoring brand demand reaches the creators relationships never will.
