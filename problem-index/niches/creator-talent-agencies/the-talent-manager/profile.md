# The Talent Manager

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/creator-talent-agencies|Creator Talent Agencies]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make a role survivable when the relationship is the product and being unavailable is a professional failure — and whoever does it keeps the people the agency's revenue is attached to.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$200M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — a phone and a CRM
**Target Buyer:** Talent leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — administration, escalation and boundaries

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A talent manager carries a handful of creators whose businesses, anxieties and crises arrive at any hour, and the relationship is the product, so being unavailable is a professional failure. They are negotiator, administrator, strategist, therapist and crisis responder, for people whose careers are volatile and whose livelihoods feel precarious. The agency's revenue is attached to these relationships and the role has no boundaries, no tooling and a predictable burnout.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A phone, a CRM, a calendar and a contract folder. The gaps: administrative load unmeasured and unsupported; no escalation for the crises; no boundary mechanism; relationship continuity absent if the manager leaves; and no view of where their time goes.
