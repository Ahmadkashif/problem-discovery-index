# Deal Pricing & Market Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/creator-talent-agencies|Creator Talent Agencies]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a year of signed deals into a pricing model rather than a folder of PDFs — and whoever structures it negotiates from evidence while everyone else negotiates from memory.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$440M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~22% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — PDFs and recollection
**Target Buyer:** Commercial leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — extraction and modelling of existing deals

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Across a roster and a year of deals, an agency knows what brands pay for what deliverables, which rights they actually exercise, and which of them pay late. None of it is structured. It lives in signed PDFs and a manager's memory, which is why the same negotiation is conducted from scratch every time, why junior managers underprice, and why a departing manager takes the pricing knowledge with them. The agency holds the market's pricing data and uses it to argue one deal at a time.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Signed contracts in a drive, a CRM with deal values and a manager's recollection. The gaps: terms never extracted into structured data; no pricing model; rights and usage not priced separately; brand behaviour not tracked; and the knowledge leaving with individuals.
