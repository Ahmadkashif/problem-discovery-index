# Nobody Reviews the Signings

**Niche:** [[niches/creator-talent-agencies/trajectory-forecasting/profile|Trajectory Forecasting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/creator-talent-agencies|Creator Talent Agencies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The agency has signed sixty creators over four years and has never looked at how those decisions turned out.
**Tags:** #quick-win #descriptive-statistics #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #revenue-impact #hypothesis-testing #worker-facing #survival-analysis
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to predict whether an individual creator's career will sustain, from signals available before the peak — and whoever forecasts durability better than a follower count signs the people everyone else misses and declines the ones everyone else overpays for.

## The Problem
Signings are celebrated and outcomes are absorbed. A creator who sustained is a success story; one who did not is a departure nobody analyses. Nobody has assembled the list of everyone signed, what was believed about them at the time, and what happened — which means the agency has four years of expensive experience and no accumulated knowledge from it. The same reasoning is applied to the next signing.

## Why It's Still Broken
Signing is a decision with no scorecard, so the outcome dissolves into the general record of the business — a decision nobody grades cannot be improved, and grading it would grade the people who made it. Outcomes arrive over years. Departures are attributed to fit or to the creator. And nobody owns the review.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Assemble the history and read it. List every signing with its date, the reach at signing and the outcome, which is the fix and is an afternoon's work with a large return. Record what was believed at the time, so future reviews have the reasoning and not only the result. Compare the signings that sustained with those that did not on the available signals, since a small sample will still show the obvious patterns. Include the declines where they can be tracked, as the creator you did not sign and who succeeded is the most informative case. Review as a group rather than individually, which makes it a learning exercise rather than an appraisal. Track the terms too, because overpaying is a distinct failure from misjudging durability. Repeat annually, since the market is changing and last year's pattern may not hold. Share the findings across managers, as the knowledge currently lives with whoever made the decision. Use it to shape the criteria rather than to grade people, which is what makes it survivable politically. And keep the record going forward, since the value compounds and starting is the only hard part.

## Who Feels the Pain
Agencies repeating expensive mistakes; managers with no feedback on their judgement; creators signed on criteria nobody validated; and firms whose knowledge leaves with the person who acquired it.

## Impact If Fixed
A decision nobody grades cannot be improved, and grading it would grade the people who made it. Assembling the signing history with outcomes is an afternoon's work and is the only way the firm's experience becomes knowledge rather than anecdote.
