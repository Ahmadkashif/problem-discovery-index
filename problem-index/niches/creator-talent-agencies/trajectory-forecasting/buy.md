# Survival Modelling From Other Talent Markets

**Niche:** [[niches/creator-talent-agencies/trajectory-forecasting/profile|Trajectory Forecasting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/creator-talent-agencies|Creator Talent Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Sport and music built analytics for predicting whose career lasts, and creator representation uses a follower count.
**Tags:** #survival-analysis #gradient-boosting #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #cross-validation #time-series-forecasting #hypothesis-testing #descriptive-statistics
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to predict whether an individual creator's career will sustain, from signals available before the peak — and whoever forecasts durability better than a follower count signs the people everyone else misses and declines the ones everyone else overpays for.

## The Problem
Professional sport went through this transition in public: scouting on visible performance was replaced, not without resistance, by models predicting durability, development curves and injury risk, and the clubs that moved first gained a real advantage. Music analytics went partway. Creator representation is at the beginning of the same road, with better data than either — public, continuous, granular and available for the whole market rather than for a league.

## What Already Exists
Player development curve modelling; durability and career length prediction; scouting analytics integrated with judgement; valuation models for contracts; and the documented history of how these were adopted against resistance.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a career whose main risk is external and shared. It requires: (1) a dominant risk factor that is a platform's distribution decision rather than the individual's ability, which sport has no analogue for and which changes what is predictable about a person; (2) a career measured in a few years rather than a decade, so the forecast horizon is short and the data per subject thin; (3) public data for the whole population rather than a scouted subset, which is a considerable advantage; (4) no league structure, so there is no standard performance measure and the equivalent must be constructed; and (5) an agent who is also the developer of the career, making the outcome partly endogenous.

## Target Customer
Signing and agency leadership, investors, creators, and sports and music analytics vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Sport made this exact transition and documented the resistance. The new factor here is that the dominant risk is a platform's decision rather than the person's ability, which changes what a forecast about the individual can and cannot claim.
