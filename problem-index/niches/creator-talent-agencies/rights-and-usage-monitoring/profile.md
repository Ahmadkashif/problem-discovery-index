# Rights & Usage Monitoring

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/creator-talent-agencies|Creator Talent Agencies]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to know whether a brand is still running a creator's content after the licence expired — and whoever monitors it turns an unbilled overrun into either a renewal or a fee.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$50M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — nobody is watching
**Target Buyer:** Business affairs leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — detection against a rights register

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Usage rights are time-bounded, platform-bounded and territory-bounded, and enforcement depends on somebody noticing. An expired usage right still running as a paid advertisement is a routine occurrence, it is worth real money, and nobody is looking — because looking means watching a brand's paid media across platforms and territories and comparing it against a date in a contract nobody extracted.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A date in a spreadsheet and occasional incidental discovery. The gaps: no monitoring of where content is running; rights registers not structured; overruns discovered by chance; whitelisting spend not tracked against caps; and the value of unbilled usage never quantified.
