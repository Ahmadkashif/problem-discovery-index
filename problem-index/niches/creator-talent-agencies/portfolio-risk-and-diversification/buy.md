# Concentration Risk From Financial Institutions

**Industry:** [[industries/creator-talent-agencies|Creator Talent Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Financial institutions measure, limit and report concentration risk as a matter of course, and an agency whose entire book depends on one platform reports nothing.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stop a roster being one undiversified bet on a distribution algorithm — and whoever measures and manages the correlated exposure runs an agency that survives a platform change rather than one that discovers it.

## The Problem
Concentration risk management is standard practice: exposures measured against defined factors, limits set and enforced, stress scenarios run, and the results reported to boards and regulators. It exists because institutions repeatedly discovered that a set of positions they thought were independent moved together. Creator agencies have a textbook version of the same problem and none of the practice.

## What Already Exists
Exposure measurement against risk factors; concentration limits with escalation; stress and scenario testing; correlation estimation; and board-level risk reporting.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to positions that are people and a factor that is a private algorithm. It requires: (1) a risk factor owned by a private company that does not announce its changes, so the exposure cannot be hedged and the scenario cannot be dated — this is the substantive difference; (2) positions that cannot be reduced, since an agency cannot sell a representation agreement, leaving only selection and creator-side diversification; (3) correlation estimated from a handful of historical platform changes rather than from market data; (4) a firm with no risk function and no regulator requiring one; and (5) relationships that make a risk-driven decision to decline feel like a personal judgement.

## Target Customer
Agency leadership and investors, finance and operations staff, creators, and risk analytics vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Institutions measure concentration because they repeatedly discovered independent positions moving together, which is exactly this. The unhedgeable, unsellable nature of the positions means selection and creator diversification carry the entire mitigation.
