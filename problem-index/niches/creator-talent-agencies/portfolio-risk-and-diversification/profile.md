# Portfolio Risk & Diversification

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/creator-talent-agencies|Creator Talent Agencies]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stop a roster being one undiversified bet on a distribution algorithm — and whoever measures and manages the correlated exposure runs an agency that survives a platform change rather than one that discovers it.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$260M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — never measured
**Target Buyer:** Agency leadership and investors
**Automation Potential:** High — exposure measurement and construction

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This contest is about the roster as a whole. Forty creators on the same platform, in the same format, serving the same audience, are one position with forty names on it — and a single ranking change moves all of them together. The exposure is measurable, the mitigation is selection and helping creators diversify their own dependence, and the decision belongs to agency leadership rather than to the manager signing an individual. It is portfolio construction, not prediction about a person.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A roster list and revenue by creator. The gaps: platform, format and category concentration unmeasured; correlated risk never quantified; diversification accidental; no stress view of a platform change; and no reporting of roster exposure to leadership or investors.
