# Forty Names, One Position

**Niche:** [[niches/creator-talent-agencies/portfolio-risk-and-diversification/profile|Portfolio Risk & Diversification]]
**Industry:** [[industries/creator-talent-agencies|Creator Talent Agencies]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** The roster's primary risk factor is shared across every creator on it and is therefore not diversified at all.
**Tags:** #optimization-fundamentals #confidence-intervals #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact #descriptive-statistics #monte-carlo-methods #hypothesis-testing #survival-analysis
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stop a roster being one undiversified bet on a distribution algorithm — and whoever measures and manages the correlated exposure runs an agency that survives a platform change rather than one that discovers it.

## The Problem
An agency's revenue is commission on creator income. Creator income depends on platform distribution. If most of the roster depends on the same platform, in similar formats, reaching similar audiences, then a ranking change is not a risk to one creator — it is a simultaneous revenue event across the whole business. Agencies know this happens and experience it periodically, and no agency measures its exposure, sets any limit, or constructs a roster with it in mind.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Signing decisions are made one at a time by individual managers, so nobody occupies the position where the aggregate is visible — a portfolio built by independent decisions has no portfolio manager. The exposure only manifests occasionally. Diversifying means declining creators who look good individually, which is an unpopular discipline. And no investor has demanded the reporting.

## What to Build
Measure the exposure and manage it deliberately. Compute roster concentration by platform, format, category and audience, which is the core and is the number that does not exist anywhere in the industry. Model the correlated revenue effect of a platform change, since a stress view is what makes an abstract risk actionable. Set exposure limits or at least targets, as an unlimited concentration is a decision nobody is making deliberately. Weigh diversification in signing decisions, which means sometimes declining a strong individual candidate and is the discipline the business lacks. Help creators diversify their own platform dependence, because that is the only mitigation available for positions that cannot be sold and it serves the creator too. Report exposure to leadership and investors as a standing measure, which changes the conversation from anecdote to management. Track the correlation empirically from past platform changes, since the agency has lived through several and never measured the effect. Distinguish the creator whose audience is portable from one whose reach is entirely algorithmic, as that distinction is the real diversification. Model the revenue concentration across creators too, because a roster where three creators are most of the revenue has a second, compounding concentration. And revisit as the roster changes, since exposure drifts with every signing.

## Target Customer
Agency leadership and investors, signing managers, creators whose own concentration is the agency's, and portfolio analytics vendors.

## Impact If Built
A portfolio built by independent decisions has no portfolio manager, so nobody sees the aggregate. Measuring platform, format and audience concentration across the roster is the number that turns a periodic shock into a managed exposure.
