# Roster Strategy

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/creator-talent-agencies|Creator Talent Agencies]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to build a roster that survives the platform changes that will hit all of it at once — and the contest splits cleanly enough that it is not terminal.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$560M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~28% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — signed on follower count
**Target Buyer:** Agency leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — prediction and portfolio construction

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The commercial model requires picking people whose careers will last, and signing decisions are made on current reach — the same metric the brands use and the same mistake. Creator careers are short and volatile in a way traditional representation never dealt with, the dominant risk is a distribution algorithm that changes without notice, and that risk is shared across the whole roster. Nobody forecasts a trajectory and nobody models the portfolio, which makes this the largest contest in the category and two problems rather than one.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Follower counts, engagement rates, a manager's instinct and a term sheet. The gaps: no trajectory forecast; no measure of career durability; platform concentration across the roster unmeasured; correlated risk unmanaged; and signing outcomes never reviewed.
