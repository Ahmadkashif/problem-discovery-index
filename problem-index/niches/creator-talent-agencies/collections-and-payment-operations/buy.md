**Industry:** [[industries/creator-talent-agencies|Creator Talent Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Receivables management is a mature discipline with dunning, ageing and credit control, and agencies chase by email when someone has time.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to collect money already earned without a person spending their month on it — and whoever automates the chase pays creators on time, which is the thing they most notice.

## The Problem
Accounts receivable management is well developed: ageing analysis, automated dunning sequences, credit control with terms set by counterparty risk, dispute handling, collections escalation and days sales outstanding as a managed metric. Every business of scale runs it. Agencies have the same exposure to large slow-paying counterparties and run it as an email task.

## What Already Exists
Ageing and dunning automation; credit control and terms setting; dispute and query workflow; collections escalation paths; and days sales outstanding measurement and targets.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a counterparty the agency must keep selling to. It requires: (1) a debtor who is also the customer the agency wants more business from, so collection pressure trades directly against commercial relationship — this is the substantive constraint and is why escalation is avoided; (2) money that is mostly the creator's rather than the agency's, which makes the agency an intermediary with an obligation rather than a creditor; (3) a small firm with no credit control function; (4) invoices tied to bespoke deliverables whose completion is itself sometimes disputed; and (5) no realistic legal escalation, since litigating a brand ends the relationship.

## Target Customer
Finance leadership, administrators, creators, and receivables automation vendors.

## Impact If Solved
The receivables discipline is mature and assumes a creditor willing to escalate. Here the debtor is the customer and the money is the creator's, which makes automation and brand-level behaviour tracking the levers rather than escalation.
