# Collections & Payment Operations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/creator-talent-agencies|Creator Talent Agencies]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to collect money already earned without a person spending their month on it — and whoever automates the chase pays creators on time, which is the thing they most notice.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$90M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — email and a spreadsheet
**Target Buyer:** Finance leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — a standard receivables problem

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Somebody at the agency spends their month chasing brands for money already earned, reconciling payments against deals whose terms live in PDFs, and telling creators their payment is still coming. Invoices run ninety days past due as a matter of routine, the creator experiences it as the agency failing them, and the chasing is done by email, one brand at a time, by a person with no leverage and no system.

## Current Tools & Gaps
An accounting package, an email account and a spreadsheet of who owes what. The gaps: no dunning process; payment terms not tracked per brand; late payers not scored or escalated; creator payment timing not managed; and the cost of late payment never quantified.
