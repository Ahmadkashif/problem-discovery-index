**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stop one person reconciling hundreds of deals whose terms live in PDFs — and whoever gives them structured data turns an impossible job into a manageable one.

## The Problem
Asking a simple question — has this deal been fully delivered, what is still owed, when does the usage right expire — requires opening a contract, checking a spreadsheet, searching email and sometimes asking the manager. Multiply by hundreds of live deals and a constant stream of questions from managers, creators, brands and finance. The administrator is the agency's only source of operational truth and holds it across four systems and their own memory.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The agency's systems were adopted separately for separate purposes, so the deal exists in fragments and the administrator is the integration — a person who successfully holds a process together removes the pressure to build the system that would. Contracts are documents. Agency software is thin and the category is small. And the role's load is invisible until they leave.

## What to Build
Make the deal a record rather than a reconstruction. Create a single deal record holding terms, deliverables, approvals, obligations and payments, which is the core and is what the administrator is currently assembling by hand each time. Populate it by extracting terms from the signed contract, since retyping is the barrier and extraction is now feasible. Link deliverables to payment triggers, so the state of one determines the state of the other automatically. Connect to the accounting system rather than requiring re-entry, as double entry is where the errors come from. Show the deal's full state on one screen, which answers most of the questions the administrator currently researches. Let managers and creators self-serve the routine questions, because answering them is most of the interruption. Flag exceptions rather than requiring a review of everything, so attention goes to what is wrong. Keep an audit trail, since disputes about what was agreed are common. Report the administrator's load by task, which nobody has measured. And design for one person doing all of it, because that is the reality in almost every agency.

## Target Customer
Operations leadership and administrators, managers and creators asking the questions, finance teams, and agency management software vendors.

## Impact If Built
A person who successfully holds a process together removes the pressure to build the system that would, so the deal lives in four places and one head. A single deal record populated by extraction turns a reconstruction into a lookup.
