**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to track hundreds of live obligations written in prose across a roster — and whoever turns bespoke deal terms into monitored commitments stops the breaches that are currently the ordinary state.

## The Problem
Contract lifecycle management is a real category: clause libraries, obligation extraction, milestone and renewal tracking, approval workflow and repository search. It is built for enterprise legal and procurement teams handling supplier agreements, and it is priced, configured and shaped accordingly. An agency with a handful of business affairs staff and hundreds of short bespoke deals does not adopt it.

## What Already Exists
Obligation extraction from contracts; clause libraries and deviation detection; milestone and renewal tracking; approval workflow; and repository search.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to short, bespoke, high-volume deals with creative deliverables. It requires: (1) deliverables that are creative work with approval rounds rather than goods or services with acceptance criteria, which no procurement model represents — this is the substantive difference; (2) usage rights and exclusivity as first-class tracked objects, where enterprise contracts treat them as clauses; (3) hundreds of small deals rather than dozens of large ones, changing the economics of per-contract processing; (4) an agency with no legal department, so everything must run without a contract manager; and (5) obligations that fall on the creator as well as the agency and the brand, which is a three-party structure procurement tools do not model.

## Target Customer
Business affairs leadership, agency operations, brands, and contract lifecycle vendors.

## Impact If Solved
The extraction and tracking technology exists and is shaped for enterprise legal teams. Representing creative deliverables with approval rounds, and usage rights as tracked objects, is what the creator version needs and no procurement model has.
