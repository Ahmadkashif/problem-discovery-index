# Prose Into Monitored Commitments

**Niche:** [[niches/creator-talent-agencies/contract-and-obligation-administration/profile|Contract & Obligation Administration]]
**Industry:** [[industries/creator-talent-agencies|Creator Talent Agencies]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Hundreds of live obligations, each written in a paragraph, tracked by a person who has read all of them once.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #automation #compliance #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #bert
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to track hundreds of live obligations written in prose across a roster — and whoever turns bespoke deal terms into monitored commitments stops the breaches that are currently the ordinary state.

## The Problem
A single deal might require three posts within a window, one story within a week of each, usage rights for ninety days across paid social in two territories, exclusivity in a product category for six months, whitelisting permission with a spend cap, three approval rounds and payment thirty days after final delivery. Multiply by hundreds of live deals. Each obligation has a date, a condition and a party responsible, and the system of record is a spreadsheet someone updates when they remember.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Contracts are prose because they are negotiated documents, so the obligations were never structured — a commitment expressed in a sentence cannot be tracked by a system that was never given the sentence. Contract lifecycle tools are built for enterprise procurement and do not fit. Business affairs staff absorb the tracking. And breaches are usually small enough individually to be settled quietly.

## What to Build
Extract the obligations and monitor them. Extract every obligation from the signed contract into structured commitments with dates, parties and conditions, which is the core and turns prose into something a system can watch. Track deliverable due dates and chase automatically, since chasing is most of the administrative load and requires persistence rather than judgement. Monitor usage windows and alert before expiry, because a right that expires while a campaign is still running is the most common and most expensive breach. Detect exclusivity conflicts across the roster before a deal is signed, as that check is currently a manager's memory and the failure is a contractual breach. Track approval rounds against what was agreed, since scope creep in approvals is a real and unbilled cost. Surface the obligations due this week as a working list rather than a document store. Flag terms that differ from the agency's standard, which is where the risk concentrates. Link obligations to payment triggers, connecting to collections. Report breach and near-miss rates, which nobody measures and which would justify the whole investment. And keep the structured record as the system of record rather than the PDF, because that is what makes everything else possible.

## Target Customer
Business affairs leadership and administrators, managers, brands on the other side of the obligations, and contract management vendors.

## Impact If Built
A commitment expressed in a sentence cannot be tracked by a system that was never given the sentence. Extracting obligations into dated, monitored commitments converts a spreadsheet of remembered promises into a working list.
