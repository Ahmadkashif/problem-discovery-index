# Contract & Obligation Administration

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/creator-talent-agencies|Creator Talent Agencies]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to track hundreds of live obligations written in prose across a roster — and whoever turns bespoke deal terms into monitored commitments stops the breaches that are currently the ordinary state.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — a spreadsheet with a date column
**Target Buyer:** Business affairs leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — extraction and obligation tracking

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every deal is a bespoke set of deliverables, usage windows, exclusivity terms, whitelisting permissions, approval rounds and payment terms, written in prose. A roster of forty creators generates hundreds of live obligations, and the failures — an expired usage right still running as a paid advertisement, a deliverable nobody chased, an exclusivity breach nobody noticed — are the ordinary state rather than the exception. The tracking mechanism is a spreadsheet with a date column.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Signed PDFs, a shared spreadsheet, calendar reminders and a manager's attention. The gaps: obligations not extracted from contracts; no automated tracking of windows and permissions; exclusivity conflicts undetected; approval rounds untracked; and breaches discovered by the counterparty.
