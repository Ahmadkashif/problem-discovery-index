# Regulatory Change Platforms Adapted to Product Scope

**Niche:** [[niches/testing-laboratories/product-compliance-regulatory-intelligence/profile|Product Compliance Regulatory Intelligence]]
**Industry:** [[industries/testing-laboratories|Testing Laboratories]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Regulatory change management platforms map rules to obligations and controls for banks and insurers; product regulation needs them mapped to product categories, substances and standard editions instead.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #transformers #data-integration #compliance #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this pocket is fighting to tell a manufacturer, before the effective date, exactly which of its products a new or changed regulation applies to and what evidence it needs — and whoever maps regulatory change to product scope most accurately and earliest keeps the compliance team's subscription.

## The Problem
Product-compliance analysts read regulations from dozens of jurisdictions, classify them and write summaries, with much of the tagging done by hand against internal taxonomies. Volume rises every year as product rules multiply, and analysts' throughput sets how many jurisdictions the vendor can cover.

## What Already Exists
Regulatory change management and legal content platforms from Wolters Kluwer, Thomson Reuters and LexisNexis, built around obligation libraries and control mapping for financial services and corporate compliance.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: taxonomies of product categories and harmonised tariff codes rather than business processes; extraction of substance identifiers, thresholds and test methods from regulatory text; linkage to the standards each regulation references, by edition; multilingual ingestion of national gazettes; and mapping to product attributes rather than controls.

## Target Customer
Content operations leaders at product-compliance intelligence vendors and at testing groups running regulatory update services.

## Impact If Solved
Analysts spend their time on interpretation rather than tagging, coverage widens without proportional hiring, and the classified record becomes queryable by product rather than by document.
