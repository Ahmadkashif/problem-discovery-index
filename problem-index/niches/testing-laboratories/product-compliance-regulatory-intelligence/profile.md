# Product Compliance Regulatory Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/testing-laboratories|Testing Laboratories]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this pocket is fighting to tell a manufacturer, before the effective date, exactly which of its products a new or changed regulation applies to and what evidence it needs — and whoever maps regulatory change to product scope most accurately and earliest keeps the compliance team's subscription.

## What They Do
Subscription services that track product regulations and standards worldwide — chemical and substance restrictions, electrical safety, EMC and radio rules, labelling, packaging and PFAS restrictions — and map each change to the product categories, substances and standards it touches, so manufacturers know what applies to which products and by when. Compliance & Risks, Enhesa and 3E are examples, and the large testing groups run regulatory update services of their own. Manufacturers' compliance and regulatory teams pay; testing labs consume the same output to scope test programmes. This is the product-regulation branch of an archetype already indexed twice: [[niches/compliance-consulting/regulatory-content-publishers/profile|🔍 Regulatory Intelligence Publishers]] (55) and [[niches/hr-consultants/employment-law-content-publishers/profile|🔍 Employment Law Compliance Content Publishers]] (51).

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 regulatory analysts, chemists, engineers and content specialists per larger vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Curated, translated and classified regulatory content, change alerts, and applicability mappings from each regulation to product attributes, substances and referenced standards
**Proprietary data:** Years of maintained interpretation — which product scopes, thresholds and standard editions each regulation covers, how ambiguous definitions have been read, and what subscribers asked — built on public law but held nowhere else in that structured form
**Clock:** Legislative and regulatory effective dates set by governments, such as CPSC's certificate eFiling requirement from 8 July 2026 and state and federal PFAS deadlines; a late alert means a client ships a non-compliant product
**Buyer:** Head of regulatory content or chief product officer
