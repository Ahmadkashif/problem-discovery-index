# Insurer-Owned Testing & Approval Laboratories

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/testing-laboratories|Testing Laboratories]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Property insurers that run their own test labs to approve the fire protection and building products their policyholders install — FM Approvals in the US, VdS in Germany and LPCB in the UK. Manufacturers pay for approval testing; the insurer benefits through lower losses.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 test engineers and researchers per organisation (estimate)
**Output:** Approval standards, test reports and approval guides
**Proprietary data:** Large-scale fire and hazard test results joined to the insurer's own loss experience — a combination nobody else holds
**Clock:** Largely self-set; product launch dates for manufacturers
**Buyer:** Head of approvals or research
