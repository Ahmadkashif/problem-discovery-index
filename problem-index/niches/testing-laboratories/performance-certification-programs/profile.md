# Industry Performance Certification Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/testing-laboratories|Testing Laboratories]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Trade-association and independent programmes that certify the rated performance of products — AHRI for heating, cooling and water-heating equipment, NFRC for windows, HVI for ventilation fans, and the certification bodies behind ENERGY STAR. They select products for verification testing at independent labs, publish directories of certified ratings and penalise failures. Manufacturers pay programme fees; utilities, regulators and contractors rely on the directories.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-120 certification engineers and programme staff per programme (estimate)
**Output:** Certified rating directories, verification test results and programme rules
**Proprietary data:** Years of rated-versus-tested performance by manufacturer and product line, including challenge-test failures
**Clock:** Federal efficiency standard effective dates and annual verification cycles
**Buyer:** Vice president of certification
