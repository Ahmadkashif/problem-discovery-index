# Federal Laboratory Recognition Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/testing-laboratories|Testing Laboratories]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The federal programmes that decide which labs' results count: FDA's LAAF programme for certain food testing, CPSC's acceptance of third-party labs for children's products, OSHA's Nationally Recognized Testing Laboratory programme, EPA-delegated drinking water lab certification, and FCC lab recognition. They recognise accreditation bodies or labs, monitor them and withdraw recognition.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-100 programme staff per agency programme (estimate)
**Output:** Recognition decisions, lab lists and enforcement actions
**Proprietary data:** Submitted results and audit records across recognised labs — for LAAF, results are sent directly to FDA
**Clock:** Statutory and rulemaking deadlines
**Buyer:** Programme director
