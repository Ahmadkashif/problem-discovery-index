# Forensic Engineering & Failure Analysis Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/testing-laboratories|Testing Laboratories]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Engineering and scientific firms that test failed products, materials and structures to determine cause — for manufacturers, insurers and lawyers — and testify about it. Exponent reported $582M in FY2025 revenue across 17 practices with more than 900 consulting staff; Rimkus, SEA, EFI Global and Envista are other examples, several of them running high volumes of insurance claim investigations alongside bespoke product work. The same firms are indexed at 51 with no kill switch as [[niches/engineering-consultants/forensic-engineering-firms/profile|🔍 Forensic Engineering Firms]], and the metal-fabrication view is logged as [[niches/metal-fabrication/materials-testing-failure-analysis/profile|🔍 Materials Testing & Failure Analysis]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-900+ engineers and scientists at the larger firms
**Output:** Failure analysis reports, root-cause opinions and expert testimony
**Proprietary data:** Investigation files across thousands of failures and the record of which methods and opinions survived challenge — much of it created at counsel's or an insurer's direction
**Clock:** Court schedules, expert disclosure deadlines, claim deadlines and recall decisions
**Buyer:** Practice director

**Kill switches:** attorney work product and client confidentiality. This deliberately disagrees with the engineering-consultants entry, which holds that a substantial non-litigation product-safety practice sits outside privilege. Seen from the testing-lab side, failure analysis of a product or material is usually commissioned once a dispute, claim or recall is in prospect, by counsel or an insurer anticipating subrogation; the part of the archive that would make an insight product valuable — opinions joined to how they fared under challenge — is exactly the part created in anticipation of litigation. A tool that pools it would face privilege review before procurement could start.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. The rubric score is harmonised with the indexed entry at 51; the only disagreement is whether the privilege kill switch is live, and here it is judged live
