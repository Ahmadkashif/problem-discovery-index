# Manufacturer QMS Adapted to ISO/IEC 17025

**Niche:** [[niches/testing-laboratories/laboratory-quality-manager/profile|The Laboratory Quality Manager]]
**Industry:** [[industries/testing-laboratories|Testing Laboratories]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Quality management software handles documents, training and corrective actions well for factories; it has no concept of a scope of accreditation, a proficiency round or a measurement uncertainty budget.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a lab continuously assessment-ready — corrective actions closed, uncertainty budgets current, every proficiency result answered — without the quality manager rebuilding the evidence before each visit, and whoever makes readiness continuous wins the quality budget.

## The Problem
Labs buy a QMS for document control and corrective actions, then keep separate spreadsheets for everything specific to a lab: which methods are on the scope, which analysts are authorised for which methods, which proficiency rounds are due and how each was answered, equipment traceability chains, and impartiality risks.

## What Already Exists
MasterControl, Qualio, ETQ and similar QMS platforms built for regulated manufacturers, with document control, training, CAPA, audits and supplier management.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: the scope of accreditation as a first-class object linked to methods, analysts, equipment and proficiency; a proficiency calendar with results imported from providers and unsatisfactory results opening corrective actions automatically; authorisation of analysts per method from training and demonstration-of-capability records; equipment calibration chains to national standards; and impartiality risk registers as ISO/IEC 17025 requires.

## Target Customer
Quality managers at mid-size labs that already own a manufacturer QMS.

## Impact If Solved
One system for the whole quality record instead of a QMS plus a drawer of spreadsheets.
