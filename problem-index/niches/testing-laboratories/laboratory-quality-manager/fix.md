# The Evidence Hunt in Assessment Week

**Niche:** [[niches/testing-laboratories/laboratory-quality-manager/profile|The Laboratory Quality Manager]]
**Industry:** [[industries/testing-laboratories|Testing Laboratories]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Before every assessment the quality manager spends days pulling together records that exist but are filed in five places.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #compliance #workflow-orchestration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a lab continuously assessment-ready — corrective actions closed, uncertainty budgets current, every proficiency result answered — without the quality manager rebuilding the evidence before each visit, and whoever makes readiness continuous wins the quality budget.

## The Problem
Assessors sample records across the standard: an analyst's training for a method, the calibration of the balance used, the validation file, the last proficiency result and its follow-up, a corrective action's effectiveness check. The quality manager knows the records exist and spends assessment week finding them.

## Why It's Still Broken
Records are created in the LIMS, the QMS, instrument software and shared drives, and nothing maps them to the clauses and scope items they evidence.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Index every record as it is created against the clause and scope item it supports, show readiness gaps continuously, and produce on demand the evidence trail for any method an assessor picks.

## Who Feels the Pain
Quality managers, lab directors facing findings, and analysts pulled off the bench to find paperwork.

## Impact If Fixed
Assessment preparation shrinks from days to hours, and gaps surface months before an assessor finds them.
