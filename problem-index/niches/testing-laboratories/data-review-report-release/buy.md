# Report Writers Adapted to Accredited Certificate Rules

**Niche:** [[niches/testing-laboratories/data-review-report-release/profile|Analytical Data Review & Report Release]]
**Industry:** [[industries/testing-laboratories|Testing Laboratories]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Report designers can lay out any document; an accredited test report must follow rules about what it contains, which results carry the accreditation mark, and how statements of conformity are made.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #compliance #automation #data-integration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to move a finished batch through second-person review onto a signed certificate the same day without opening a defensibility gap, and whoever releases fastest with a record an assessor accepts sets the turnaround the rest of the market is priced against.

## The Problem
Every lab maintains report templates per client and test type. ISO/IEC 17025 and accreditation bodies' rules specify required content, how non-accredited results are distinguished, when the accreditation symbol may appear, how opinions and interpretations are marked, and how a pass/fail statement accounts for uncertainty. Templates drift from these rules; assessors find the drift.

## What Already Exists
LIMS report designers, general reporting tools and document generation platforms.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: report content driven by the lab's scope of accreditation, so non-accredited results are flagged automatically; accreditation-symbol rules applied per result; decision rules and uncertainty applied to conformity statements; required elements validated before release; and amendment handling built in.

## Target Customer
Quality managers and LIMS administrators at accredited labs with many report formats.

## Impact If Solved
Reports compliant by construction, fewer assessment findings, and less programmer time spent on templates.
