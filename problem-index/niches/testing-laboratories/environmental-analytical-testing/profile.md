# Environmental Analytical Testing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/testing-laboratories|Testing Laboratories]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to return a defensible, accreditation-compliant result inside each method's holding time at a per-sample price consultants and utilities will accept, and whoever runs the densest automated lines — PFAS above all — takes the monitoring contracts.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$4.5B US environmental analytical testing revenue (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~16% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — instruments, LIMS and electronic data deliverables are digital; chain-of-custody forms, case narratives and much client communication are not
**Target Buyer:** VP of operations or laboratory director at a regional or national environmental laboratory network
**Automation Potential:** High for login, review, narratives and deliverables; low for sample preparation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Environmental labs analyse soil, groundwater, drinking water, wastewater, air and waste for consultants, utilities, industrial dischargers, the Department of Defense and state agencies, using EPA methods under state certification built on the TNI standard and, for defence work, the DoD quality systems manual. The work is bid per sample and differentiated on turnaround and data quality, and every method carries an absolute holding time. PFAS has become the capacity race: EPA's drinking water rule sets 4 ppt limits for PFOA and PFOS and requires initial monitoring by April 2027, and PFAS methods are among the slowest and most contamination-prone in the lab. The consultants who buy this data are covered in [[industries/environmental-consultants|Environmental Consultants]]; this niche is the lab's side of the same transaction.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Enterprise LIMS from LabWare, STARLIMS and environmental specialists; chromatography data systems from Waters, Thermo and Agilent; deliverables loaded into EarthSoft EQuIS and state databases. The gaps: no prediction of which incoming samples will need dilution or cleanup before they foul an instrument; dozens of client- and state-specific deliverable formats maintained by hand; and the case narrative that accompanies every data package written from scratch by an analyst.
