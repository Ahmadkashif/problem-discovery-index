# LIMS & Laboratory Informatics Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/testing-laboratories|Testing Laboratories]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
LabWare, STARLIMS, LabVantage, Thermo Fisher's SampleManager and cloud vendors such as QBench and CloudLIMS sell the systems labs run on, along with chromatography data system vendors. Labs pay licences and services.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-100 product managers and market researchers per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Software and implementation services, not research
**Proprietary data:** Configuration knowledge across customers; the data inside the systems belongs to the labs
**Clock:** Customers' accreditation and regulatory change dates
**Buyer:** Chief product officer
