# TIC Rollup & Laboratory Network Integration Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/testing-laboratories|Testing Laboratories]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The consolidators — Eurofins, SGS, Bureau Veritas, Intertek, UL Solutions, Element, Pace, Montrose and private-equity-backed lab platforms — buy independent labs and integrate them. Corporate development, pricing and operations analytics teams benchmark acquired sites on turnaround, utilisation, price per test and margin.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-100 corporate development, pricing and operations analysts per platform (estimate)
**Output:** Internal acquisition models, site benchmarks and integration plans
**Proprietary data:** Operating data across dozens to hundreds of acquired labs — a cross-site view no independent has
**Clock:** Deal timetables and integration milestones
**Buyer:** Chief operating officer or head of corporate development
