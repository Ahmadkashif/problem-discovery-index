# Field Forms Apps Adapted to ASTM Field Tests

**Niche:** [[niches/testing-laboratories/construction-materials-testing/profile|Construction Materials Testing & Special Inspection]]
**Industry:** [[industries/testing-laboratories|Testing Laboratories]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Mobile forms apps replaced clipboards in many trades; a materials technician needs a form that knows the mix design, the specification and the arithmetic of every ASTM field test.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #feature-engineering #worker-facing #automation #quick-win #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to have a certified technician on every pour and compaction test the schedule demands and a signed result with the engineer of record before the next placement, and whoever staffs the field reliably and reports the same day wins the contractor's and owner's next project.

## The Problem
Field technicians record truck tickets, slump, air, temperatures, cylinder sets, nuclear gauge readings and compaction calculations on paper or generic forms, and someone retypes them for the report. Out-of-specification results are sometimes noticed only at the office.

## What Already Exists
Generic mobile forms and inspection apps (GoCanvas, Fulcrum and similar) and the field modules of construction materials lab software.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: forms pre-loaded with the project's mix designs, specification limits and proctor values; built-in calculations for percent compaction and air and unit weight; batch ticket capture from a photo; immediate flags when a reading is out of specification, with the limit shown for the superintendent; cylinder IDs generated at casting; and offline operation on sites without signal.

## Target Customer
Construction services managers at mid-size testing firms not running a full lab platform.

## Impact If Solved
Same-day reports without evening retyping, fewer calculation errors, and a documented on-site call when a load is rejected.
