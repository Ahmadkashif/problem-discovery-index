# Laboratory Trade Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/testing-laboratories|Testing Laboratories]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Associations that represent testing labs and set consensus standards — ACIL in the US, the TIC Council internationally, and The NELAC Institute (TNI), which writes the environmental lab accreditation standard. Members pay dues; volunteers do most of the technical work.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically under 10 research staff; the standards work is done by member volunteers
**Output:** Advocacy, member surveys and consensus standards
**Proprietary data:** Member surveys and committee records
**Clock:** Conference and standards cycles
**Buyer:** Executive director
