# Retailer & Marketplace Product Compliance Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/testing-laboratories|Testing Laboratories]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Large retailers and online marketplaces set test protocols for their suppliers and sellers, approve the labs that may test, and review test reports and certificates before products are listed. Since July 2026, CPSC certificate data filed at import must name the testing lab. These teams buy no insight; they consume lab output and decide on listings.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-200 product safety and compliance staff at the largest retailers and marketplaces (estimate)
**Output:** Internal protocols, lab approvals and listing decisions
**Proprietary data:** Test reports across all their suppliers and sellers, plus returns and complaint data — a cross-supplier failure view
**Clock:** Listing deadlines, seasonal resets and regulatory effective dates
**Buyer:** Vice president of product safety and compliance
