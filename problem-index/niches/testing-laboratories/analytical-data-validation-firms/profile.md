# Third-Party Analytical Data Validation Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/testing-laboratories|Testing Laboratories]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Independent chemists who review laboratory data packages against EPA national functional guidelines, DoD requirements or project quality plans, and assign qualifiers that decide whether each result can be used. Consultants, federal agencies and responsible parties pay them per sample or per package. The consultant-side workflow is analysed in [[niches/environmental-consultants/lab-data-qaqc-review/profile|Lab Data QA/QC Review]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-80 validation chemists at the larger firms (estimate)
**Output:** Validation reports and qualified datasets
**Proprietary data:** Findings across thousands of data packages from many labs — which labs, methods and analytes generate which qualifiers
**Clock:** Project and regulatory deadlines — remedial decisions, permit reports, federal programme milestones
**Buyer:** Firm principal or validation practice lead
