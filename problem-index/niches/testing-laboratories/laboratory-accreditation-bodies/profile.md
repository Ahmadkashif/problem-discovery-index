# Laboratory Accreditation Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/testing-laboratories|Testing Laboratories]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Accreditation bodies assess labs against ISO/IEC 17025 and sector requirements and grant the accreditation that regulators and clients rely on — A2LA, ANAB, AASHTO re:source, IAS, PJLA and NIST's NVLAP in the US. They are not government regulators, but FDA, CPSC, EPA programmes and the FCC recognise them, which gives them a quasi-regulatory role. Labs pay assessment and annual fees. A2LA held 4,350 accreditation certificates in 2024, assessed by 300 contracted assessors. The bodies that accredit certification bodies rather than labs, ANAB among them, are logged as [[niches/compliance-consulting/certification-accreditation-bodies/profile|🔍 Accreditation Bodies for Certification Bodies]] (42).

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-400 staff and contracted assessors at the larger bodies
**Output:** Assessment reports, nonconformity findings and accreditation decisions
**Proprietary data:** Nonconformity findings across thousands of labs over many assessment cycles — which clauses, sectors and lab types fail, and how findings predict later problems
**Clock:** Reassessment and surveillance cycles, plus regulators' recognition requirements
**Buyer:** Chief executive or vice president of accreditation
