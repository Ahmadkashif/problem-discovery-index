# Cloud LIMS Preconfigured for One Accreditation Scope

**Niche:** [[niches/testing-laboratories/small-independent-labs/profile|Small Independent Accredited Labs]]
**Industry:** [[industries/testing-laboratories|Testing Laboratories]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Cloud LIMS are now affordable for small labs; configuring one for a specific accredited scope still takes months of the owner's time.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #compliance #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give a five- to twenty-five-person accredited lab a system it can run without an IT person or LIMS administrator, and whoever gets a small lab live and assessment-ready in weeks rather than a year wins a long tail the enterprise vendors ignore.

## The Problem
A twelve-person food micro lab buys a cloud LIMS, then spends months defining tests, specifications, sample types, report templates and instrument connections. Go-live slips; the old spreadsheets stay in use.

## What Already Exists
Cloud LIMS such as QBench and CloudLIMS, with configurable workflows, client portals and some quality features; vendor professional services at extra cost.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: starter configurations per scope (food micro, drinking water, cannabis, construction materials, EMC) with methods, QC rules, holding times, report formats and proficiency schedules already defined; import of the lab's existing test list and client history from spreadsheets; and a guided configuration assistant that asks the owner questions rather than presenting settings.

## Target Customer
Owners and lab managers of small accredited labs choosing or rescuing a LIMS rollout.

## Impact If Solved
Go-live in weeks with a configuration an assessor will recognise, and LIMS revenue from a segment vendors find expensive to serve.
