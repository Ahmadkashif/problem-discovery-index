# The Presumptive Positive Phoned In at Midnight

**Niche:** [[niches/testing-laboratories/food-pathogen-emp-testing/profile|Food Pathogen & Environmental Monitoring Testing]]
**Industry:** [[industries/testing-laboratories|Testing Laboratories]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A rapid screen flags a presumptive pathogen overnight, confirmation takes days, and the plant decides whether to hold product based on a phone call.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #compliance #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to return results on a food plant's pathogen and environmental monitoring samples fast enough to release held product on schedule, without a false negative, and whoever shortens time-to-result most reliably wins the plant's standing order.

## The Problem
Presumptive positives arrive at odd hours. A technician calls the plant contact, leaves a message, emails a spreadsheet. The plant must decide on holds and intensified sanitation while confirmation runs, often without a clear record of what was communicated when.

## Why It's Still Broken
LIMS report final results; presumptive status, confirmation progress and the communication trail sit outside it.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Structured result states (presumptive, confirmation in progress, confirmed, negative on confirmation) pushed instantly to named plant contacts with escalation if unacknowledged, linked lot and site information, and a timestamped communication log for audits.

## Who Feels the Pain
Night-shift technicians, plant food safety managers woken at midnight, and auditors reconstructing the timeline.

## Impact If Fixed
Faster, documented hold decisions and fewer disputes about who knew what when.
