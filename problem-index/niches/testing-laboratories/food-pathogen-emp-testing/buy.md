# LIMS Sample Plans Adapted to Monitoring Zones

**Niche:** [[niches/testing-laboratories/food-pathogen-emp-testing/profile|Food Pathogen & Environmental Monitoring Testing]]
**Industry:** [[industries/testing-laboratories|Testing Laboratories]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** LIMS manage recurring sample schedules for any client; an environmental monitoring programme needs site maps, zones and swab rules that change automatically after a positive.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to return results on a food plant's pathogen and environmental monitoring samples fast enough to release held product on schedule, without a false negative, and whoever shortens time-to-result most reliably wins the plant's standing order.

## The Problem
Plants run monitoring plans with sites by zone, rotation schedules, and intensified "vector" swabbing around any positive. Labs receive swabs labelled by site code and must map them back to a plan they don't hold.

## What Already Exists
LIMS recurring-sample scheduling and client portals; standalone environmental monitoring software sold to plants.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: the plant's site map with zone and adjacency held in the lab's system; swab kits pre-labelled per scheduled site; automatic generation of follow-up swab plans when a positive occurs; results shown on the map for the plant; and records formatted for customer and regulatory audits.

## Target Customer
Food division managers at lab networks; plant quality managers who outsource their environmental monitoring logistics.

## Impact If Solved
Fewer mislabelled swabs, faster and complete follow-up after positives, and a stickier service.
