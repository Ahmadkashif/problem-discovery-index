# Chamber Scheduling on Generic Resource Booking

**Niche:** [[niches/testing-laboratories/emc-wireless-compliance-testing/profile|EMC & Wireless Compliance Testing]]
**Industry:** [[industries/testing-laboratories|Testing Laboratories]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Booking tools schedule rooms and people well; an EMC chamber's schedule is wrecked by retests nobody can predict and products that arrive late or not ready.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #survival-analysis #optimization-fundamentals #workflow-orchestration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to get a product through emissions, immunity and radio testing on the first chamber booking, and whoever turns a failing scan into a diagnosed fix fastest — in a lab the FCC still recognises — wins the launch.

## The Problem
Chambers, open-area test sites and radio benches are booked weeks ahead. Products arrive without firmware in test mode, fail and need retests, or overrun. Labs either leave slack and lose revenue or overbook and push clients back.

## What Already Exists
Resource scheduling and booking software, ERP project modules, and shared calendars.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: test plans converted to chamber and engineer durations by test type; predicted first-pass failure and overrun probabilities by product category and client history; engineer qualification constraints; client readiness checks (test-mode firmware, accessories, documentation) before the booking; and automatic backfill from a standby list when a slot opens.

## Target Customer
Operations managers at EMC and wireless labs.

## Impact If Solved
Higher chamber utilisation without longer client waits — the two numbers an EMC lab is run on.
