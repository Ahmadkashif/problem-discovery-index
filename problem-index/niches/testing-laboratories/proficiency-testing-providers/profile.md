# Proficiency Testing Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/testing-laboratories|Testing Laboratories]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Proficiency testing providers send the same characterised sample to hundreds or thousands of labs, collect their results, assign a reference value and score every lab, usually as a z-score. Accreditation bodies and state programmes require labs to take part: TNI-based programmes ask for two studies a year per field of testing, and AASHTO-accredited construction materials labs must take part in every applicable programme and score within ±2. LGC AXIO serves more than 13,000 labs in over 160 countries and runs 2,700+ tests a year; AASHTO re:source ships 23,000+ samples a year to about 3,000 labs; Waters ERA, Fapas, Bipea and ASTM's programmes are other examples. Labs pay per scheme and round.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-150 scheme scientists, statisticians and material production staff at the larger providers (estimate)
**Output:** Round reports scoring each participant, with assigned values, peer-group statistics and method breakdowns
**Proprietary data:** Every participant's results on identical samples across years, by method, instrument and matrix — the only dataset that shows how labs actually perform against each other over time, reported as round snapshots and rarely analysed longitudinally
**Clock:** Accreditation and state programme participation frequencies; labs need results before reassessments and must answer unsatisfactory scores with corrective action
**Buyer:** Proficiency testing programme director or technical director

**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold; the strongest near-miss without a kill switch. The scored report is what labs buy, and the archive is unique and under-used, but labs choose a scheme for accreditation acceptance and price rather than for its analysis, and labour grows with schemes and rounds rather than with participants
