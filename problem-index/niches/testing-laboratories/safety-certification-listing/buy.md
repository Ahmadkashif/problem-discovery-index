# Supplier Audit Software Adapted to Listing Conditions

**Niche:** [[niches/testing-laboratories/safety-certification-listing/profile|Safety Certification & Listing]]
**Industry:** [[industries/testing-laboratories|Testing Laboratories]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Inspection and audit apps already run millions of checklists a year; none of them load a listing's critical-component list and construction file as the checklist.
**Tags:** #object-detection #cnns #large-language-models #compliance #automation #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to have its mark be the one retailers, insurers and inspectors ask for while running each listing's factory follow-up at the lowest cost to the manufacturer, and whoever's mark is demanded with the least disruptive follow-up keeps the product for its whole life.

## The Problem
A field representative at the factory compares production units against the listing: the right fuse rating, the approved plastic, the marked power cord, the labelled transformer. The reference is a long construction description; the checklist is generic; photos are filed separately.

## What Already Exists
Mobile inspection and audit platforms (SafetyCulture and enterprise quality suites) handle checklists, photos, scoring and corrective actions for supplier audits.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: checklists generated from each listing's critical-component list and construction details; photo capture that reads component markings and compares them with approved parts; automatic drafting of a variation notice when a mismatch is found; and synchronisation back to the certifier's listing database.

## Target Customer
Field services managers at certification bodies; manufacturers' quality teams preparing for follow-up visits.

## Impact If Solved
Shorter visits, fewer missed mismatches, and evidence-rich findings that close faster.
