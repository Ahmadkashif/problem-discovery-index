# The Variation Notice Written on Paper at the Factory

**Niche:** [[niches/testing-laboratories/safety-certification-listing/profile|Safety Certification & Listing]]
**Industry:** [[industries/testing-laboratories|Testing Laboratories]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** When a field representative finds a non-conforming part, the notice that holds the product travels by paper and email while the factory keeps shipping.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #compliance #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to have its mark be the one retailers, insurers and inspectors ask for while running each listing's factory follow-up at the lowest cost to the manufacturer, and whoever's mark is demanded with the least disruptive follow-up keeps the product for its whole life.

## The Problem
A variation notice tells the manufacturer that production does not match the listing and the mark must not be applied until it is resolved. It is often handwritten or typed after the visit, sent by email, and resolved through back-and-forth with engineering, while product in the channel may already carry the mark.

## Why It's Still Broken
The notice is a legal hinge between certifier and manufacturer, so it has stayed a signed document; the field system and the engineering review sit in different tools.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A structured notice raised on site with photos and the listing clause, sent instantly to the manufacturer's portal, with the resolution path (correct production, request evaluation of the change) tracked to closure and timed.

## Who Feels the Pain
Field representatives, manufacturers' quality managers, and the certifier's engineers who must evaluate changes quickly.

## Impact If Fixed
Faster closure, fewer marked non-conforming units in the channel, and a clean record of every finding.
