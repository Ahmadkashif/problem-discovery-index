# Accreditation-Readiness & Lab Quality Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/testing-laboratories|Testing Laboratories]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Consultants and retired assessors who help labs write quality manuals, validate methods, estimate uncertainty and prepare for their first ISO/IEC 17025 assessment or a scope extension. Labs pay by the project or the day.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 1-10 per firm
**Output:** Quality manuals, validation packages, gap assessments and training
**Proprietary data:** Templates and personal experience
**Clock:** The client's assessment date
**Buyer:** Lab owner or quality manager
