# Building Product Evaluation Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/testing-laboratories|Testing Laboratories]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Evaluation services such as ICC-ES and IAPMO Uniform ES review test reports from accredited labs against published acceptance criteria and issue evaluation reports that building officials accept as evidence that a product complies with the code. Manufacturers pay for the evaluation and its periodic renewal.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 staff engineers and technical reviewers per service (estimate)
**Output:** Evaluation reports, acceptance criteria and listings
**Proprietary data:** The acceptance criteria development record and every evaluation's review history; the underlying test data belongs to manufacturers
**Clock:** Code editions on a three-year cycle, report renewal dates and product launches
**Buyer:** Director of evaluation services
