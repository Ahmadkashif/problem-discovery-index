# Independent Consumer Product Testing Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/testing-laboratories|Testing Laboratories]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Publishers that buy products anonymously, test them in their own labs and sell the ratings to consumers — Consumer Reports in the US, Which? in the UK, Stiftung Warentest in Germany, and online reviewers that run lab protocols. Members and subscribers pay for the ratings; the organisations refuse manufacturer money.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 engineers, technicians, statisticians and survey researchers per organisation (estimate)
**Output:** Product ratings, reliability predictions and buying guides
**Proprietary data:** Decades of standardised test results plus member surveys on reliability and satisfaction that no manufacturer or lab holds
**Clock:** Largely self-set — model-year and buying-season calendars
**Buyer:** Chief content officer or head of testing
