# Standards Development Organisations & Method Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/testing-laboratories|Testing Laboratories]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Organisations that write and sell the test methods and product standards labs work to — ASTM International, AOAC International for food and agricultural methods, UL Standards & Engagement, and national bodies adopting ISO and IEC standards. Volunteer committees write the documents; staff manage the process, run interlaboratory studies for precision statements and sell the standards, which are their principal revenue. Codes and regulations incorporate them by reference — building codes cite ASTM test methods, and CPSC mandates the toy safety standard ASTM F963 — so revisions are timed to adoption cycles. The design-standards branch of the same archetype is logged as [[niches/engineering-consultants/engineering-standards-publishers/profile|🔍 Engineering Standards Publishers]] (45); this entry is harmonised with it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-400 staff engineers, standards developers and editors, with the technical work done by volunteer committees (estimate)
**Output:** Published standards and methods, precision statements and method validation programmes
**Proprietary data:** Interlaboratory study data behind precision statements and the ballot and comment record behind each standard, accumulated across editions
**Clock:** Revision cycles aligned to code editions and regulatory incorporation by reference
**Buyer:** Vice president of standards or technical operations
