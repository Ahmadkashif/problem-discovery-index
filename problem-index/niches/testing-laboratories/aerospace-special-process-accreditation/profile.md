# Aerospace Special-Process Accreditation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/testing-laboratories|Testing Laboratories]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Nadcap programme, administered by the Performance Review Institute, audits aerospace suppliers' special processes — materials testing labs, nondestructive testing, heat treating, chemical processing, and in electronics soldering and harness assembly — on behalf of the prime manufacturers, who require accreditation before they buy. Suppliers pay for audits. The same body is scored from the electronics side as [[niches/electronics-contract-mfg/nadcap-aerospace-quality-audits/profile|🔍 Aerospace & Defence Special Process Auditing]] (48, kill switch); this entry is harmonised with it rather than scored a second way.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 staff auditors, technical staff and task-group coordinators (estimate)
**Output:** Audit findings, nonconformance records, accreditation decisions and the special-process audit criteria
**Proprietary data:** Audit findings across thousands of supplier sites and process types over decades, with recurrence and corrective-action outcomes
**Clock:** Accreditation renewal cycles and primes' programme qualification deadlines — a lapsed accreditation stops a supplier shipping
**Buyer:** VP of accreditation or technical director
