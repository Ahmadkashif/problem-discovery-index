# Telecommunication Certification Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/testing-laboratories|Testing Laboratories]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Private organisations recognised by the FCC to review radio test reports and issue equipment authorisations on its behalf, often alongside equivalent roles for Canada and other markets. Manufacturers pay per certification. The FCC's 2025 order bars bodies and labs owned or controlled by foreign adversaries, and a 2026 item proposes limiting recognition further.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-80 reviewing engineers per body (estimate)
**Output:** Equipment authorisation grants and review findings
**Proprietary data:** Review history across many manufacturers' submissions; the test data itself belongs to the applicant and grants are public
**Clock:** Product launch dates and FCC rule changes
**Buyer:** Certification manager or lab director
