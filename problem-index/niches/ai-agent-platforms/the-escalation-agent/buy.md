**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to hand a human the full account of what the agent did and a way to put it right — and whoever does that takes the account, because this handoff is where every agent failure becomes a customer's experience.

## The Problem
Handing a case from one person to another without losing what matters is a studied problem with real answers. Contact centres built warm transfer and screen-pop so the receiving agent has the context before they speak. Clinical handover developed structured formats precisely because ad hoc handover was killing people, and the structure — situation, background, assessment, recommendation — is deliberately designed to carry judgement rather than only facts. Agent-to-human handoff passes a transcript.

## What Already Exists
Warm transfer and screen-pop in contact centre platforms; structured clinical handover formats carrying assessment and recommendation; case management systems with full interaction history; incident handover practice from operations; and knowledge-centred service linking cases to resolutions.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a handoff from software that cannot be asked a follow-up question. It requires: (1) the handoff carrying the agent's assessment and uncertainty rather than only its actions, which is exactly the clinical insight — the receiving party needs to know what the handing-over party thought and how confident they were, and an agent can state both; (2) system effects as a first-class section, since a human handing over says what they did and an agent's actions touched systems the receiving agent cannot see; (3) a recommendation, because the agent frequently knows what should happen next and currently says nothing; (4) the handoff triggered before the failure completes where possible, which is a warm transfer and is far better than handing over after the damage; and (5) two-way structure, so the human's resolution returns to the agent's learning loop, which has no analogue in human-to-human handover and is where the compounding value is.

## Target Customer
Support organisations, agent platforms, contact centre software vendors, and the human factors community whose handover work applies directly.

## Impact If Solved
Clinical handover exists because ad hoc handover was dangerous, and its structure carries assessment and uncertainty rather than only facts. An agent can state both and currently states neither, and a warm transfer before the failure completes beats any handoff afterwards.
