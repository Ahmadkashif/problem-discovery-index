# The Escalation Agent

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ai-agent-platforms|AI Agent Platforms]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to hand a human the full account of what the agent did and a way to put it right — and whoever does that takes the account, because this handoff is where every agent failure becomes a customer's experience.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$220M US in escalation handling cost and churn
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of category revenue equivalent
**Digital Adoption:** None — inherits an action they cannot explain
**Target Buyer:** Support organisations and their leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — the trajectory record exists and is not delivered

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
When an agent does something wrong on a customer's behalf, a human support agent inherits an angry customer, an action they did not take and cannot explain, and no clear path to undo it. They have a transcript at best, frequently not even that, and no view of what tools were called with what arguments and what actually changed in which system. They are apologising for a decision they cannot account for, to someone who is angry, with no authority to reverse what happened. This is where every agent failure lands, it is the moment that determines whether the customer forgives the deployment, and the person handling it is given less information than the agent had.

## Current Tools & Gaps
The conversation transcript, the ticketing system, and whatever the agent wrote into it. The gaps: no trajectory view for the human; no record of effects in downstream systems; no undo path; no explanation of why the agent did what it did; no priority for escalations arising from agent errors; and no feedback route from the escalation back into the agent's improvement.
