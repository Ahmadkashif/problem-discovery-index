# Agent Platforms & Products

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ai-agent-platforms|AI Agent Platforms]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Not terminal — the contest differs by whether the buyer is building the agent or buying its output, and the decomposition is recorded below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$800M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~32% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** High
**Target Buyer:** Engineering teams; separately, business functions
**Automation Potential:** High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is what the category sells: the orchestration, state, tool calling and deployment machinery that turns a model into something that completes tasks, and the products built on it. It is the largest revenue line and where the visible competition happens.

It is **not terminal**, because it covers two businesses that share a technology stack and nothing else. A framework is infrastructure bought by an engineering team that will build the agent themselves; it competes with other frameworks and with building it in-house, is priced as tooling or is free, and is judged on whether the team can control and reason about what the agent does. A vertical outcome product is a service bought by a business owner who wants tickets resolved or code shipped; it competes with an outsourcer or with headcount, is priced per outcome, and is judged on resolution rate in one bounded domain — the buyer never sees a graph. The evidence that wins each deal is unrelated. Filter Notes in the overview records the two rejected alternatives; the sub-niches below are the split.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Graph-based orchestration with explicit state, tool calling, connector standards, deployment and monitoring, and vertical products in bounded domains. The gaps are specific to each side and are stated in the sub-niches.
