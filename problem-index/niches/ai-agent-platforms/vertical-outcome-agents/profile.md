# Vertical Outcome Agents

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ai-agent-platforms|AI Agent Platforms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to resolve a higher share of one bounded domain's tasks without a human than an outsourcer would at the same cost — and whoever does that takes the account, because the buyer is comparing against a labour contract, not against a framework.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$490M US and the category's fastest-growing line
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~20% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Moderate — traction where tasks are bounded and failures recoverable
**Target Buyer:** Business function owners: support, operations, engineering leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — within the domain, most of the work is repetitive

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A vertical agent sells a resolved ticket, a merged pull request or a completed operational task. The buyer runs a support organisation or an operations team, compares against an outsourcing contract and a headcount number, and judges on resolution rate, cost per resolution and what happens when it is wrong. They do not evaluate an orchestration graph and will never see one. Traction has come where the task is bounded and the failure is recoverable, which is a real and honest limitation. The contest is domain depth: knowing the workflows, the systems, the exceptions and the escalation rules of one function well enough to resolve the awkward cases, which is where the resolution rate is actually decided.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Domain-tuned agents, integrations into the function's systems, escalation to human staff, and resolution reporting. The gaps: resolution rate is reported without a quality dimension, so a deflection and a resolution count the same; no comparison against the human baseline on the same tickets; the long tail of exceptions is handled by escalation rather than by depth; deployment quality depends on a forward-deployed engineer; and the effect on the human team that remains is not measured.
