# The Forward-Deployed Engineer

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ai-agent-platforms|AI Agent Platforms]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make a deployment work without a person living inside it — and whoever does that takes the market, because the delivery model is currently a headcount line that grows with revenue.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$280M US in loaded delivery cost
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~11% of category revenue equivalent
**Digital Adoption:** None — the delivery model is a person
**Target Buyer:** Vendor delivery organisations and their leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — most of the work repeats across deployments

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Forward-deployed engineers are the category's actual delivery mechanism. They sit inside customer deployments for weeks or months, building integrations, discovering edge cases, tuning prompts and workflows, and translating between what the customer's business does and what the agent can handle. It works, and it is a headcount line that scales linearly with revenue in a business whose valuation assumes it does not. The knowledge they accumulate — which edge cases matter, which prompts work for which task shapes, which integration patterns recur — is the company's real intellectual property and it lives in individuals who are permanently on site and permanently too busy to write anything down.

## Current Tools & Gaps
The platform itself, notebooks, customer-specific configuration, and the engineer's accumulated experience. The gaps: no reusable deployment template per vertical; no library of edge cases by domain; no capture of the tuning decisions made and why; no measurement of where deployment time actually goes; and no path from a deployment's learnings back into the product.
