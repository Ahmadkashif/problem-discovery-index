# Lodging Tax Authorities

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hotels-boutique|Boutique Hotels]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
City and county revenue departments administer transient occupancy taxes, audit remittances, and — increasingly — chase short-term rental operators who never registered.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-40 revenue analysts and auditors per jurisdiction
**Output:** Tax assessments, audit findings, compliance actions
**Proprietary data:** Remitted revenue by property — the only complete record of what a market's lodging supply actually earns
**Clock:** Monthly and quarterly remittance deadlines
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — municipal budget
