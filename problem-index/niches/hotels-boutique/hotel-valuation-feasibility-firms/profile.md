# Hotel Valuation & Feasibility Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hotels-boutique|Boutique Hotels]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Specialist hospitality consultancies value hotels and write the feasibility studies lenders and developers require before a hotel is financed or built — projecting occupancy, rate, and operating performance a decade out for a property that may not exist yet.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 valuers, analysts, and market researchers
**Output:** Appraisals, feasibility studies, market studies, impact analyses
**Proprietary data:** Hotel transaction comparables and operating statements collected over decades, in a market where neither is public
**Clock:** Financing and transaction deadlines
**Buyer:** Practice leader / President
