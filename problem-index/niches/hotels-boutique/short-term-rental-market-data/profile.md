# Short-Term Rental Market Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hotels-boutique|Boutique Hotels]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
AirDNA, Key Data, and their peers measure the short-term rental supply that competes directly with boutique hotels — listings, occupancy, rate, and revenue by market — and sell it to investors, operators, municipalities, and increasingly to hotels trying to understand what they are losing bookings to.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 data engineers, analysts, and researchers
**Output:** Market reports, listing-level performance estimates, investment screening tools
**Proprietary data:** Assembled listing histories and modelled occupancy over a supply base that publishes nothing; the underlying listings are observed rather than owned
**Clock:** Monthly reporting and seasonal investment cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Chief Product Officer
