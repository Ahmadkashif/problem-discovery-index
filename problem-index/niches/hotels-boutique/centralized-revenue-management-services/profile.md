# Centralized Revenue Management Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hotels-boutique|Boutique Hotels]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup

## What They Do
Third-party hotel management companies operate centralized revenue teams that price hundreds or thousands of properties from one location — the twelve-person revenue function Pass 1 says a boutique GM is competing against with a spreadsheet. Many sell it as a standalone service to hotels they do not otherwise manage.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 revenue managers, analysts, and data staff
**Output:** Daily rate and inventory decisions, forecasts, channel mix strategy, owner reporting
**Proprietary data:** Booking pace, rate, and channel performance across hundreds of properties in many markets — held as agent for the owners, not owned
**Clock:** Daily pricing decisions against a perishable inventory
**Buyer:** SVP of Revenue Management / Chief Commercial Officer
