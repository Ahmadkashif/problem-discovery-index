# Guest Reputation & Experience Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hotels-boutique|Boutique Hotels]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Revinate, TrustYou, and their peers aggregate guest reviews and survey responses across platforms, classify what guests complain about, and sell hotels the analysis of their own reputation.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 analysts, data scientists, and linguists
**Output:** Reputation scores, sentiment and topic analysis, competitive comparison, response workflows
**Proprietary data:** Historical review corpora and classification models; the reviews themselves are public
**Clock:** Continuous review flow; no hard deadline
**Buyer:** VP of Product / Head of Data
