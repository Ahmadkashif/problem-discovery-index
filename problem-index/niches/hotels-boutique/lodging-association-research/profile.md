# Lodging Association Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hotels-boutique|Boutique Hotels]]

## What They Do
The lodging association's research arm publishes the industry's economic impact and employment studies and analyzes policy affecting hotels — labour rules, short-term rental regulation, travel demand.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-20 researchers and policy analysts
**Output:** Economic impact studies, employment research, policy analysis
**Proprietary data:** Member surveys layered over licensed industry data
**Clock:** Legislative sessions and annual publication
**Buyer:** VP of Research — member funded
