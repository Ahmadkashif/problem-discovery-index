# OTA Partner Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hotels-boutique|Boutique Hotels]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The online travel agencies that Pass 1 says capture 60-70% of independent bookings run analytics groups producing the market intelligence and rate guidance they give partner hotels — demand outlook, compression dates, competitive rate position.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-800 data scientists, economists, and market analysts
**Output:** Partner-facing market insight tools, demand outlooks, rate guidance
**Proprietary data:** Global search, shopping, and booking behaviour — the deepest forward demand signal in travel, seeing intent before any hotel does
**Clock:** Daily pricing and continuous demand shifts
**Buyer:** VP of Analytics / Head of Partner Products
