# PMS & Channel Manager Data Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hotels-boutique|Boutique Hotels]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Cloudbeds, Mews, SiteMinder, and Little Hotelier run reservations, billing, and channel distribution for a large share of independent hotels, and see every booking, cancellation, and rate change those properties make.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 data and analytics staff
**Output:** In-product reporting, rate suggestions, channel performance analytics
**Proprietary data:** Booking, rate, and channel mix across tens of thousands of independent properties — the segment the enterprise revenue systems do not serve
**Clock:** Daily booking flow
**Buyer:** Chief Product Officer / VP of Data
