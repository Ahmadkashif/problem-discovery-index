# Short-Term Rental Compliance Enforcement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hotels-boutique|Boutique Hotels]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Vendors identify unregistered short-term rentals for municipalities by matching listings to addresses and registration records, and administer the registration and tax collection that follows — enforcement that materially affects the supply competing with boutique hotels.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 data analysts, compliance staff, and investigators
**Output:** Identified non-compliant listings, address matching, registration and tax administration
**Proprietary data:** Listing-to-address resolution across many jurisdictions, plus registration and enforcement outcomes
**Clock:** Municipal enforcement and tax remittance cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Product — municipal end customers
