# Revenue Management System Data Science

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hotels-boutique|Boutique Hotels]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
IDeaS, Duetto, and their peers build the demand forecasting and pricing engines that set rates for large hotel portfolios. Pass 1 notes these systems are priced and designed for chains of 50+ properties, leaving independents without access.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-200 data scientists, pricing researchers, and analysts
**Output:** Demand forecasts, rate recommendations, inventory controls
**Proprietary data:** Booking curves and price response across large client portfolios — the record of what happened when a rate moved
**Clock:** Daily and intraday repricing
**Buyer:** Chief Science Officer / VP of Data Science
