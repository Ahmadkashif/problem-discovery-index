# Independent Revenue Management Consultants

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hotels-boutique|Boutique Hotels]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Individual consultants and tiny firms provide outsourced revenue management to boutique hotels that cannot justify a full-time revenue manager and cannot afford an enterprise system — the workaround for exactly the gap Pass 1 identifies.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1-5 people, often one former chain revenue manager
**Output:** Rate strategy, daily pricing decisions, channel mix recommendations
**Proprietary data:** A career of observed market behaviour, held in one person's head
**Clock:** Daily pricing decisions
**Buyer:** The consultant
