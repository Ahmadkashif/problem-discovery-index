# Hotel Performance Benchmarking & Demand Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hotels-boutique|Boutique Hotels]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
STR — now inside CoStar — collects daily occupancy, rate, and revenue from a very large share of global hotel supply and returns each property its performance against a competitive set. Kalibri Labs does the adjacent thing with transaction-level data, measuring what a booking actually nets after acquisition cost. Every hotel in the world, chain and independent alike, manages to these reports; RevPAR index is the number owners, lenders, and management contracts are written around.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 analysts, data engineers, economists, and client researchers
**Output:** Daily and monthly benchmarking reports, competitive set analysis, market forecasts, pipeline and supply research
**Proprietary data:** Voluntarily contributed daily property-level performance from most of the world's branded and much of its independent supply, assembled over four decades — a reciprocity network no competitor can buy or rebuild
**Clock:** Daily submission and weekly reporting, against a business that reprices every day
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / SVP of Analytics
