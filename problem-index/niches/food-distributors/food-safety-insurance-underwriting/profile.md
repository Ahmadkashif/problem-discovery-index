# Product Contamination Insurance Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/food-distributors|Food Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Specialty underwriters price product recall and contamination coverage, assessing supply chain structure, traceability capability, and food safety programme maturity to determine what a recall would actually cost the insured.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 underwriters, food safety engineers, and claims analysts
**Output:** Underwriting assessments, recall cost models, risk improvement recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Realized recall cost experience against pre-loss assessments
**Clock:** Renewal cycles and rate filings
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
