# Route & Warehouse Software Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/food-distributors|Food Distributors]]

## What They Do
The optimization science groups at route planning and warehouse management vendors build the algorithms that sequence multi-temperature deliveries into tight windows and slot perishable inventory for rotation, and benchmark performance across their installed base.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 operations researchers, engineers, and analysts
**Output:** Routing and slotting algorithms, performance benchmarking, configuration advisory
**Proprietary data:** Execution data across distributor deployments — planned versus actual delivery and picking performance at scale
**Clock:** Release cycles, internal
**Buyer:** VP of Science / Chief Product Officer
