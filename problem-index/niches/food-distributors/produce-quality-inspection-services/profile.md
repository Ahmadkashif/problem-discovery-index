# Produce Quality Inspection Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/food-distributors|Food Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Third-party inspectors and the federal inspection service grade arriving produce loads against specification, and their finding determines whether a load is accepted, discounted, or rejected — a determination worth tens of thousands of dollars per truck and contested by whichever side it disadvantages.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,500 inspectors, graders, and quality specialists
**Output:** Inspection certificates, condition findings, arrival quality determinations
**Proprietary data:** Arrival condition findings by shipper, commodity, origin, and lane — a direct record of who ships product that holds up
**Clock:** Arrival inspection windows; a load waits on the finding
**Buyer:** VP of Inspection Services / Chief Quality Officer
