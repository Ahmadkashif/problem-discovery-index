# Food Supply Chain Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/food-distributors|Food Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer

## What They Do
Specialist consultancies design distribution network footprints, warehouse operations, and route structures for food distributors, producing the analyses that justify eight-figure facility and fleet investments.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 industrial engineers, network modellers, and consultants
**Output:** Network design studies, warehouse and route analyses, investment business cases
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated network modelling and benchmark data across engagements, largely client-owned
**Clock:** Capital approval cycles and facility programme schedules
**Buyer:** Principal / Managing Director
