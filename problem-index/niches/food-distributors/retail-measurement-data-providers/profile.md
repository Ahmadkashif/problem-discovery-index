# Retail Measurement Data Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/food-distributors|Food Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Circana and NielsenIQ collect point-of-sale data from retailers under exclusive agreements, normalize every item against a proprietary product taxonomy, project it to market totals, and sell share, pricing, and promotion analytics to the manufacturers and distributors who plan against it. This is the same archetype as the natural channel data vendors indexed under `acupuncture-practices` and the e-commerce share measurement providers under `ecommerce-sellers` — three distinct companies, three distinct channel datasets, one recurring business.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1,000-5,000 data analysts, taxonomists, category insight staff, and statisticians
**Output:** Share and pricing analytics, promotion effectiveness, category reports, custom studies — the subscription
**Proprietary data:** Retailer point-of-sale feeds under exclusive supply agreements, plus a hand-built product taxonomy over millions of items and decades of projection methodology
**Clock:** Retailer category review calendars and manufacturer planning cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Analytics Officer / VP of Insights
