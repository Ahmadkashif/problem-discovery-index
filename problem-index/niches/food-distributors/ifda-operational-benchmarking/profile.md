# Distribution Association Operational Benchmarking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/food-distributors|Food Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The foodservice distributor and grocery industry associations collect operational and financial data from members and publish the productivity, cost-per-case, and profitability benchmarks distributors run their operations against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 researchers and analysts
**Output:** Operational benchmark reports, financial profiles, industry outlooks
**Proprietary data:** Member-contributed operating data across distributors, longitudinally
**Clock:** Annual survey and publication cycle
**Buyer:** VP of Research
