# Food Traceability Compliance Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/food-distributors|Food Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Federal traceability rules require that critical tracking events and key data elements be recorded and producible within a short window for a defined list of higher-risk foods. Specialist providers map a distributor's product flows against the requirement, establish the data capture, and maintain the records that must be produced to a regulator on demand during an outbreak investigation.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 food safety specialists, regulatory analysts, and data staff
**Output:** Traceability programme design, data element mapping, records production and gap analyses
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated interpretation of which product flows and transformations fall in scope, and the record of what regulators actually accepted during investigations
**Clock:** Statutory compliance dates and, once an outbreak begins, a records production window measured in hours
**Buyer:** VP of Regulatory Services / Chief Food Safety Officer
