# Deduction & Trade Promotion Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/food-distributors|Food Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Specialist firms and software vendors resolve the thousands of weekly invoice discrepancies the Pass 1 analysis describes — catch-weight variances, grade adjustments, short-ship credits, promotional allowances — determining which deductions are valid, recovering those that are not, and analyzing why they recur.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 deduction analysts, dispute specialists, and data staff
**Output:** Deduction validation and recovery, root cause analysis, trade spend reporting
**Proprietary data:** Deduction reason and outcome history across customers and trading partners — a direct record of where trading relationships systematically leak money
**Clock:** Deduction dispute windows and month-end close
**Buyer:** VP of Analytics / Chief Financial Officer
