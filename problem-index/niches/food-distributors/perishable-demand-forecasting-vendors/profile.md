# Perishable Demand Forecasting Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/food-distributors|Food Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors selling demand forecasting and replenishment for perishables model the weather, promotion, menu, and substitution effects that drive the two-to-five percent spoilage the Pass 1 analysis identifies as the industry's central loss.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 data scientists, supply chain analysts, and product staff
**Output:** Demand forecasts, order recommendations, waste analytics
**Proprietary data:** Forecast-versus-actual and spoilage outcomes across distributor deployments and product categories
**Clock:** Daily order cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Data Science / Chief Product Officer
