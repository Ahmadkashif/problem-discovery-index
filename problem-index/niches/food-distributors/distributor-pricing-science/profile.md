# Distributor Pricing Science

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/food-distributors|Food Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The pricing and margin analytics groups inside the large distributors set customer-specific pricing across thousands of SKUs — cost-plus structures, deviated pricing, catch-weight handling, and rebate mechanics — which is where a distributor's margin is actually made or lost.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-200 pricing analysts, data scientists, and revenue managers
**Output:** Pricing models, margin analytics, customer profitability analysis
**Proprietary data:** Item-level cost, price, and volume across hundreds of thousands of operator customers, joined to retention outcomes
**Clock:** Contract cycles and weekly price file releases
**Buyer:** VP of Pricing / Chief Commercial Officer
