# Shelf Life & Food Science Laboratories

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/food-distributors|Food Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Food science laboratories run the shelf life studies, challenge tests, and microbiological validations that establish how long a product remains safe and saleable — the numbers that determine date codes, distribution range, and whether a product can be sold at all.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 food scientists, microbiologists, and technicians
**Output:** Shelf life determinations, challenge study reports, validation documentation
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated shelf life and challenge study results across formulations, packaging, and storage conditions
**Clock:** Product launch dates and regulatory validation requirements
**Buyer:** Laboratory Director / VP of Scientific Services
