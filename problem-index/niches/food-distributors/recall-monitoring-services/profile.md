# Recall Monitoring & Response Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/food-distributors|Food Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Providers monitor federal and state recall and withdrawal notices continuously, determine whether a distributor carries the affected lots, and manage the notification and reconciliation that a recall requires — work that must complete before the product moves further down the chain.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 regulatory analysts, food safety specialists, and response coordinators
**Output:** Recall matching and impact determination, customer notification, effectiveness check reporting
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated recall matching across product identifiers and lot conventions, plus response outcome history
**Clock:** Recall notification and effectiveness check deadlines, measured in hours and days
**Buyer:** VP of Food Safety / Director of Regulatory Services
