# Apparel Technical Design & Spec Houses

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/alterations-tailoring|Alterations & Tailoring]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Independent studios that produce the technical package a factory sews from — graded specs, construction callouts, bill of materials, tolerances, and fit comment sets. Brands without in-house technical design outsource to them, and the tech pack is what they invoice for. Errors here are the direct upstream cause of garments that need altering.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 technical designers, patternmakers, and fit specialists
**Output:** The tech pack and fit comment history — the billable deliverable
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated construction solutions and fit corrections across brands and factories, plus which callouts factories actually execute correctly
**Clock:** Product development calendars and factory sampling windows
**Buyer:** Studio principal / Director of Technical Design
