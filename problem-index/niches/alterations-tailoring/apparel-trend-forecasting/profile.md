# Apparel Trend Forecasting Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/alterations-tailoring|Alterations & Tailoring]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Subscription services that tell brands what to make eighteen months out — WGSN, Fashion Snoops, Trendalytics, Edited. Analysts, designers, and data staff synthesize runway, street, retail assortment scrapes, social signal, and macro-cultural research into directional forecasts: silhouettes, colour palettes, fabrications, and detail-level calls. Brands, retailers, and mills subscribe and build ranges against it. This sits two positions above the alterations shop rather than one — it is the apparel chain's insight layer, and no closer home exists in the vault.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-400 trend analysts, designers, editors, and data scientists
**Output:** Seasonal forecasts, colour and silhouette direction, retail assortment analytics — the subscription itself
**Proprietary data:** Decades of published forecasts plus continuous retail assortment and pricing scrapes; the forecast archive is unique, though sell-through outcomes are not held
**Clock:** Brand range-building calendars — forecasts must land ahead of seasonal range reviews
**Buyer:** Head of Content / Chief Product Officer
