# Apparel Fit & Returns Analytics Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/alterations-tailoring|Alterations & Tailoring]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms whose product is a model of how garments fit real bodies — True Fit, Fit Analytics, Bold Metrics, 3DLOOK. They ingest garment specification data from hundreds of brands, normalize it against a common measurement ontology, join it to purchase and return outcomes across retailers, and sell size recommendation and fit intelligence back to those retailers. Every fit-related return the alterations trade exists to prevent is a data point in their corpus.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-200 data scientists, fit analysts, and garment ontologists
**Output:** The fit model and the brand-level fit intelligence reports sold alongside it
**Proprietary data:** Cross-brand garment specification data joined to purchase, return, and return-reason outcomes — a join no individual brand or retailer can assemble
**Clock:** Retailer season launch and range review calendars
**Buyer:** VP of Data Science / Chief Product Officer
