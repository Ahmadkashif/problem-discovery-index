# Apparel Tariff Classification & Trade Advisory

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/alterations-tailoring|Alterations & Tailoring]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Trade practices at law firms and customs consultancies that classify garments for duty purposes. Apparel is the most granular chapter in the tariff schedule — duty turns on fibre content by weight, knit versus woven, gender, and construction details — so a single garment can face rates differing by twenty points depending on how it is characterized. Specialists write classification opinions, prepare binding ruling requests, and defend classifications under audit.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 trade specialists, licensed brokers, and customs attorneys
**Output:** Classification opinions, binding ruling requests, first sale and FTA qualification analyses
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated classification decisions and ruling outcomes joined to product construction detail
**Clock:** Entry filing deadlines, CBP ruling windows, audit and protest periods
**Buyer:** Partner, customs and trade practice / VP Global Trade
