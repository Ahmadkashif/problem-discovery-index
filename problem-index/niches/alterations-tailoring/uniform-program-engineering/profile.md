# Uniform Program Sizing & Industrial Engineering

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/alterations-tailoring|Alterations & Tailoring]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The analytics and industrial engineering groups inside uniform rental and workwear programs — Cintas, UniFirst, Aramark, Vestis. They run the largest continuous alterations and garment-fitting operation in the country by volume, sizing and re-sizing millions of employees across client sites, and analyze wear life, size distribution, and route economics to set inventory and pricing.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 industrial engineers, analysts, and sizing program staff
**Output:** Sizing and inventory models, wear-life analysis, program pricing studies
**Proprietary data:** Longitudinal size distribution and garment wear-life data across millions of wearers and hundreds of industries
**Clock:** Client contract renewal cycles and new-site rollout dates
**Buyer:** VP of Operations / Director of Industrial Engineering
