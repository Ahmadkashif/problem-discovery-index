# Body Scan & Fit Standard Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/alterations-tailoring|Alterations & Tailoring]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Firms that define what a brand's sizes actually mean — Alvanon, [TC]², Human Solutions. They run large-scale 3D body scanning programs, analyze the resulting anthropometric data by market and demographic, and sell brands a fit standard: a size chart, a set of graded base blocks, and physical or digital fit forms built to the brand's declared target body. Every downstream alteration a consumer pays for is a consequence of a decision made here.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-200 anthropometrists, technical designers, and data analysts
**Output:** The fit standard — size charts, block libraries, body data reports, fit form specifications
**Proprietary data:** One of the few large-scale 3D body scan corpora in existence, accumulated market by market over decades and effectively impossible to reproduce
**Clock:** Brand range-building calendars and seasonal block sign-off dates
**Buyer:** VP of Technical Design / Head of Product Development
