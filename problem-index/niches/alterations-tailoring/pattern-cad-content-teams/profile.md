# Apparel CAD & Digital Asset Content Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/alterations-tailoring|Alterations & Tailoring]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The content groups inside apparel CAD and 3D simulation vendors — Browzwear, CLO, Lectra, Optitex, Gerber. They build and maintain the digital assets the software is worthless without: base block libraries, graded size sets, physically characterized fabric libraries, avatar sets, and trim catalogues. The assets ship as part of the subscription rather than being sold separately.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 patternmakers, textile physicists, and asset librarians
**Output:** Block libraries, fabric physics parameter sets, avatar libraries
**Proprietary data:** Measured fabric drape and mechanical parameters across thousands of materials — expensive to produce and held by very few
**Clock:** Software release cycles, set internally
**Buyer:** VP of Product / Director of Content
