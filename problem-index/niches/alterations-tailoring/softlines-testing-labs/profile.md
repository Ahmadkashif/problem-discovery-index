# Softlines Testing & Quality Assurance Labs

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/alterations-tailoring|Alterations & Tailoring]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The apparel testing arms of SGS, Bureau Veritas, Intertek, and UL. Technicians run colourfastness, dimensional stability, seam strength, flammability, and restricted-substance panels on garments and fabrics against brand and regulatory protocols, and issue pass/fail reports that gate shipment. Brands cannot ship without them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-2,000 lab technicians, chemists, and technical reviewers per softlines division
**Output:** Test reports and non-compliance findings
**Proprietary data:** Failure history by supplier, factory, fabric construction, and test method across thousands of brands — an unmatched view of who actually makes reliable garments
**Clock:** Shipment release gates and regulatory enforcement windows
**Buyer:** VP of Softlines / Global Technical Director
