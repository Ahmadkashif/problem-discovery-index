# Made-to-Measure Platform Fit Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/alterations-tailoring|Alterations & Tailoring]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The fit algorithm groups inside made-to-measure clothing platforms — Indochino, Proper Cloth, Hockerty, Suitsupply's MTM operation. They convert customer measurements or scans into pattern adjustments, then absorb the consequence directly: every garment that comes back for remake or in-store alteration is a labelled failure of their own model. That closed loop is rare and almost entirely unexploited.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 fit engineers, patternmakers, and data analysts
**Output:** Pattern adjustment rules and remake-rate analysis — internal, not sold
**Proprietary data:** Customer measurements joined to remake and alteration outcomes at garment level, across hundreds of thousands of orders
**Clock:** Delivery promise windows, which are short and self-imposed
**Buyer:** VP of Product / Head of Fit
