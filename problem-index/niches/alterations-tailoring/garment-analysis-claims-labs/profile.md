# Garment Analysis & Damage Claim Labs

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/alterations-tailoring|Alterations & Tailoring]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Independent analysis labs — principally the Drycleaning & Laundry Institute's — that adjudicate who is at fault when a garment is damaged. A cleaner, retailer, or consumer submits the garment; analysts examine construction, fibre, dye, and damage morphology and issue a written finding attributing cause to the manufacturer, the cleaner, or the consumer. The finding settles the claim. The insight genuinely is the invoice; the operation is simply tiny.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 3-10 analysts
**Output:** Written fault-attribution findings
**Proprietary data:** Decades of adjudicated damage cases linked to garment construction and care labelling
**Clock:** Claim resolution windows
**Buyer:** Director of the analysis laboratory
