# Apparel Sizing Standards Committees

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/alterations-tailoring|Alterations & Tailoring]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
ASTM Committee D13 on Textiles and the ISO/TC 133 sizing groups write the voluntary standards that define body measurement tables and size designation systems. Committee work is done by volunteers seconded from brands, labs, and consultancies; the standards body itself provides secretariat staff rather than researchers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 2-6 staff per committee, with volunteer technical contributors
**Output:** Published standards and measurement tables
**Proprietary data:** None held by the body — the underlying anthropometric data belongs to the consultancies that contribute it
**Clock:** Standard revision ballot cycles
**Buyer:** Standards staff manager
