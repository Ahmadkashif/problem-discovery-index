# On-Demand Alterations Platform Operations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/alterations-tailoring|Alterations & Tailoring]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Venture-backed marketplaces routing alteration jobs to a network of shops and in-store tailors — Hemster, Alterations Now, and the in-house tailoring programs at large apparel retailers. Small operations teams price jobs, forecast turnaround, and match jobs to capacity.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 3-10 operations and data staff
**Output:** Pricing models, turnaround forecasts, capacity matching
**Proprietary data:** Job-level pricing and turnaround data across a shop network — small but genuinely unique, since no other party sees alteration jobs at scale
**Clock:** Customer promise dates, self-imposed
**Buyer:** Head of Operations
