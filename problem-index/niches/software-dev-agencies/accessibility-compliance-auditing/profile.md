# Digital Accessibility Compliance Auditing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/software-dev-agencies|Software Development Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Audit websites and applications against accessibility standards, produce conformance reports, and defend or settle the accessibility litigation and regulatory actions that follow — a market created almost entirely by lawsuits.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 accessibility specialists, assistive technology testers, and remediation engineers
**Output:** Conformance audits and reports, remediation guidance, accessibility conformance statements, expert testimony, ongoing monitoring
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated audit findings and settlement outcomes — a record of which defects actually attract claims
**Clock:** Litigation deadlines, settlement remediation schedules, and regulatory compliance dates
**Buyer:** Managing Director / VP of Accessibility Services
