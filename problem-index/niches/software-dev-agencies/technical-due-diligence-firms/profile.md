# Technical Due Diligence Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/software-dev-agencies|Software Development Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Assess a software asset before an investor buys it — code quality, architecture, technical debt, security posture, team capability and the cost to fix what is wrong — and deliver the report that adjusts the price or kills the deal.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-400 engineers, architects, and analysts across the specialist firms
**Output:** Technical diligence reports, code quality and debt assessments, architecture and scalability review, remediation cost estimates, post-close technology plans
**Proprietary data:** Hundreds of assessed codebases with the investment thesis, the price paid, and — for the firms that stay engaged — what actually happened afterwards
**Clock:** Deal timelines, typically two to four weeks and immovable
**Buyer:** Managing Director / Head of Technology Diligence

**Kill switches:** **client confidentiality** — each assessment belongs to the sponsor and cannot be pooled, which is what stops the assessments becoming a benchmark.
