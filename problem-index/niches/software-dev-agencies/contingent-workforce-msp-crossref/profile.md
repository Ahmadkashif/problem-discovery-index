# Contingent Workforce MSP & VMS Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/software-dev-agencies|Software Development Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Run the programmes through which enterprises buy contract technical talent and project services — supplier panels, rate cards, requisition distribution and performance measurement — the gate a development agency must pass to sell into a large client.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,500 programme analysts, rate benchmarking staff, supplier managers, and data scientists
**Output:** Rate card design and benchmarking, supplier scorecards, requisition and fill analytics, programme savings reporting, worker classification compliance
**Proprietary data:** Requisition, submission, fill and rate data across many suppliers and clients — the only comparative view of technical contract labour pricing
**Clock:** Requisition cycle times and programme reporting periods
**Buyer:** VP of Programme Analytics / Chief Data Officer
