# Engineering Productivity Analytics Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/software-dev-agencies|Software Development Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Instrument version control, issue tracking and deployment systems to report on delivery throughput, cycle time and engineering capacity — the metrics an agency reports to a client and an executive uses to judge a team.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 data engineers, analysts, and researchers
**Output:** Delivery metrics and benchmarks, cycle time and throughput analysis, capacity and allocation reporting, published industry state-of-delivery research
**Proprietary data:** Repository and workflow telemetry across many engineering organisations
**Clock:** Sprint and reporting cadences, internally set
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Head of Research
