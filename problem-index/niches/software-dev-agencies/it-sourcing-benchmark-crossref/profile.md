# IT Sourcing & Price Benchmark Advisors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/software-dev-agencies|Software Development Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Tell enterprise buyers what a rate or a contract should cost, benchmarked against confidential deals the advisor has seen, and run the sourcing processes that select development and services suppliers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-800 sourcing advisors, benchmark analysts, and contract specialists
**Output:** Rate and contract benchmarks, sourcing process management, supplier selection analysis, contract renegotiation support
**Proprietary data:** Confidential deal terms and rates observed across many client engagements in a market with no published pricing
**Clock:** Contract renewal and sourcing event timelines
**Buyer:** Managing Partner / Practice Leader
