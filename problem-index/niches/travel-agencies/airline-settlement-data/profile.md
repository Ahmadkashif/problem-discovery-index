# Airline Settlement & Agency Sales Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/travel-agencies|Travel Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
ARC, the airline-owned settlement corporation, accredits US agencies and settles every ticket they sell — $100.4B in 2025 from 9,978 agency locations by its December count. It sells the resulting transaction data, managed in partnership with IATA and combined with airlines' direct-channel sales, through APIs, data sharing and dashboards to airlines, agencies, destinations and airports. Its sale of ticket records to federal agencies drew press criticism in 2025.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 data product and analytics staff (estimate)
**Output:** Monthly agency sales statistics, airline and agency business intelligence, data feeds
**Proprietary data:** Settled transactions for essentially every US agency air ticket, including refunds and exchanges
**Clock:** Weekly settlement and monthly data releases on ARC's own calendar
**Buyer:** Head of data and analytics
