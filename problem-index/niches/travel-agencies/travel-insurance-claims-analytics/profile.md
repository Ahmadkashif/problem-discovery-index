# Travel Insurance Underwriting & Claims Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/travel-agencies|Travel Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Travel insurers and their administrators (Allianz Partners, AIG's Travel Guard, Generali, Seven Corners and others) price trip-cancellation and travel-medical cover that agencies sell on commission, and analyse claims by destination, supplier and reason. Their pricing and claims analytics are internal; the product sold is the policy.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 actuaries, pricing analysts and claims analysts per large carrier or administrator (estimate)
**Output:** Rate filings, product pricing, claims-cause and supplier-failure analysis
**Proprietary data:** Policy and claims histories, including supplier defaults and trip disruptions, across every insured trip
**Clock:** State rate-filing cycles and claims handling timelines
**Buyer:** Chief actuary or head of pricing
