## The Problem
NDC made up 21.2% of ARC-settled transactions by December 2025. A traveller who books an NDC fare in the corporate tool gets content the GDS may not carry; when they call to change it, the agent often cannot service it from the same screen, has to go to an airline portal or aggregator, and the change may not flow back into the client's reporting.

## What Already Exists
GDS NDC programmes from Sabre, Amadeus and Travelport, NDC aggregators, airline agency portals, and TMC mid-office tools built around the GDS PNR and ticket.

## The Customization Gap
TMC servicing needs: one queue for GDS tickets and NDC orders; exchange and refund flows that apply the client's policy and the airline's order rules; automatic sync of changes into the client's data feed and duty-of-care tracking; and agent scripts that do not require knowing each airline's portal. It also needs reporting that reconciles NDC orders with ARC settlement, since order and ticket records do not line up one to one.

## Target Customer
Operations directors at regional and national TMCs that cannot build their own NDC servicing layer.

## Impact If Solved
Fewer calls bounced between systems, servicing time per NDC change closer to that of a GDS ticket, and complete client reporting — the condition for steering travellers to NDC fares at all.
