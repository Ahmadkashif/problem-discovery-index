# The Hotel Nights the Programme Never Sees

**Niche:** [[niches/travel-agencies/corporate-travel-management/profile|Corporate Travel Management]]
**Industry:** [[industries/travel-agencies|Travel Agencies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A large share of corporate hotel nights are booked outside the programme, so negotiated rates go unused, volume commitments are missed and the safety team doesn't know where people are sleeping.

## The Problem
Travellers book flights in the corporate tool and hotels on brand apps or consumer sites, for loyalty points or a cheaper visible rate. The TMC sees the flight and not the hotel. The programme's negotiated hotel volume falls short, the hotel withdraws the rate next year, and the security team's traveller tracking shows an empty bed.

## Why It's Still Broken
The evidence of the stay is in the expense report and the card feed, which the TMC doesn't own, and matching a card transaction to an air trip is fiddly. Leakage is measured once a year, if at all, as a percentage in a review deck rather than as a list of stays.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Match card and expense hotel transactions to air itineraries within days of the stay, flag the trips with no programme hotel, and feed the stays into duty-of-care tracking and hotel-volume reporting. Prompt the traveller at the moment of air booking with the programme rate and the loyalty points it still earns.

## Who Feels the Pain
Travel managers missing hotel volume commitments; security teams with incomplete traveller locations; TMCs whose reporting understates the programme.

## Impact If Fixed
Recovering even a quarter of leaked nights (estimate) restores negotiated-rate leverage in the next sourcing cycle and closes the largest blind spot in traveller tracking.
