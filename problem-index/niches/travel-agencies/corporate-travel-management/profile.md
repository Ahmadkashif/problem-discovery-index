# Corporate Travel Management

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/travel-agencies|Travel Agencies]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is "every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a company's travel inside its managed programme", but that splits into two contests with different winners: getting a growing company's employees to book in the tool without calling an agent, and accounting for every traveller within minutes of an incident. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$5B US travel management company transaction fees, management fees and booking-tool revenue (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of agency receipts (estimate), on a much larger share of air sales through corporate agency locations
**Digital Adoption:** High — online booking tools, GDS and NDC connections, card feeds and policy engines are standard, though servicing still runs through agents
**Target Buyer:** Travel manager, procurement director
**Automation Potential:** High for booking and routine changes, medium for disruption and complex itineraries

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A TMC is paid by the corporate client, not mainly by suppliers: per-transaction fees for online and agent-assisted bookings, or a management fee, plus incentives on airline and hotel volume. The client buys a programme — policy enforced at the point of booking, negotiated fares and hotel rates loaded and used, data on every trip, 24/7 service, and traveller safety. The market is consolidating at the top (Amex GBT completed its ~$540M purchase of CWT in September 2025 after the Justice Department dropped its suit) and being attacked from software (Navan listed on Nasdaq in October 2025), while hundreds of regional TMCs serve mid-size companies.

## Current Tools & Gaps
SAP Concur, Navan, Spotnana-powered TMC platforms and the TMCs' own booking tools; GDS and NDC content; duty-of-care feeds from International SOS and Crisis24; data consolidation and reporting from TMCs and specialist vendors; expense and card platforms covered under [[industries/spend-management-platforms|Spend Management Platforms]]. Gaps: NDC content that is bookable but hard to service; leakage of hotel bookings outside the programme (~20-40% of nights, estimate); disruption handled one traveller at a time; and policy that is enforced by rules but never tested against outcomes.
