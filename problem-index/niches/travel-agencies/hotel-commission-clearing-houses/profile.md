# Hotel Commission Clearing Houses

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/travel-agencies|Travel Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Payment intermediaries such as Onyx CenterSource collect commission owed by thousands of hotels and pay it to agencies in consolidated batches with remittance detail. Hotels pay for the service; agencies receive the money and the statements their back offices reconcile.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 people in analytics; the business is payment processing and support (estimate)
**Output:** Consolidated commission payments and remittance files
**Proprietary data:** Commission payment records across hotels and agencies, held on behalf of hotel clients
**Clock:** Payment cycles agreed with hotel clients
**Buyer:** Head of product
