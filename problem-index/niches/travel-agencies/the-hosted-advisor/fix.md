# Ten Supplier Logins Before the First Quote

**Niche:** [[niches/travel-agencies/the-hosted-advisor/profile|The Independent Hosted Advisor]]
**Industry:** [[industries/travel-agencies|Travel Agencies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A new advisor spends their first weeks registering with cruise lines, tour operators and hotel programmes under the host's numbers, one portal at a time, before they can quote a client.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #automation #worker-facing #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a new advisor to steady paid commissions before they give up, and whoever gets recruits through their first year of earnings fastest takes the account.

## The Problem
Each supplier needs its own agent registration tied to the host's accreditation number, often with approval by the host and a wait. Logins multiply, some suppliers reject the registration for a mismatched detail, and the advisor's first client inquiry arrives before half the portals work.

## Why It's Still Broken
Suppliers each own their trade portal and verification process, and the host's onboarding sends a checklist rather than doing the registrations.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A registration assistant that pre-fills each supplier's form from the host's data and the advisor's profile, submits in bulk, tracks approvals, chases rejections with the reason, and stores credentials in one vault; the host approves the batch once.

## Who Feels the Pain
New advisors losing momentum in week two; host support staff answering the same registration questions; clients waiting for a first quote.

## Impact If Fixed
A new advisor can quote across the main suppliers within days rather than weeks (estimate), at the moment their motivation and their friends' inquiries are highest.
