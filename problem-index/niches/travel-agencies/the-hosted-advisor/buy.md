# Learning Platforms Tied to What Advisors Sell

**Niche:** [[niches/travel-agencies/the-hosted-advisor/profile|The Independent Hosted Advisor]]
**Industry:** [[industries/travel-agencies|Travel Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Hosts run training on generic learning platforms that know which videos an advisor watched but not whether they then booked anything.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #k-nearest-neighbors #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a new advisor to steady paid commissions before they give up, and whoever gets recruits through their first year of earnings fastest takes the account.

## The Problem
Onboarding courses cover booking systems, supplier products, sales and compliance. Advisors also take dozens of supplier specialist courses. None of it is connected to what they sell, so a new advisor learns about river cruises while their inquiries are for Disney.

## What Already Exists
Learning management and enablement platforms (TalentLMS, Docebo, Thinkific and sales-enablement tools) with courses, quizzes, certificates and completion reports.

## The Customization Gap
The host version needs: course recommendations driven by the advisor's actual inquiries and bookings; supplier academy completions imported and linked to commission levels; a library of real proposals and client conversations from successful advisors (with consent) searchable by trip type; and reporting that relates training to time to first commission rather than to completion rates.

## Target Customer
Training and advisor-success leads at host agencies and franchise networks.

## Impact If Solved
Training time spent on what the advisor is about to sell, and evidence of which programmes produce productive advisors.
