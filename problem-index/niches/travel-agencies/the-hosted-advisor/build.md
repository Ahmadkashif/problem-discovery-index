# A First Year Measured in Commissions

**Niche:** [[niches/travel-agencies/the-hosted-advisor/profile|The Independent Hosted Advisor]]
**Industry:** [[industries/travel-agencies|Travel Agencies]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Hosts recruit hundreds of new advisors a year and learn which ones will make it only when they quit or don't.
**Tags:** #survival-analysis #gradient-boosting #causal-inference #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a new advisor to steady paid commissions before they give up, and whoever gets recruits through their first year of earnings fastest takes the account.

## The Problem
A new advisor joins a host, completes onboarding, books a few trips for friends and family, and waits months for the first commission. Many stop before it arrives (~half within 12-18 months, estimate). The host sees logins, course completions and bookings, but has no early warning of who is drifting and no evidence of which support — mentoring, a niche focus, a supplier academy, a lead programme — actually changes the outcome.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Hosts are small businesses themselves, their data is spread across portals and back offices, and the outcome — a sustainable income — takes a year or more to observe.

## What to Build
An advisor-progress model: activity, training, inquiries, proposals, bookings and commission timing for every cohort, with survival modelling of time to a sustainable monthly commission and of attrition, and causal estimates of which interventions moved recruits forward. The advisor sees their own path ("advisors who reached ~$1,000 a month by month nine had sent this many proposals by month three", an illustrative estimate); the host's success team sees who to call this week.

## Target Customer
Heads of advisor success and owners at host agencies with several hundred or more advisors.

## Impact If Built
Support aimed at the recruits who can be saved, fewer people leaving just before their commissions arrive, and recruiting spent on profiles that succeed.
