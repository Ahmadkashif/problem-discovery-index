# TMC Programme Analytics & Reporting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/travel-agencies|Travel Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The analytics and account-management groups inside the large TMCs — Amex GBT (now including CWT), BCD, CTM, FCM and the larger regional firms — consolidate a client's bookings, card and expense data, measure policy compliance, leakage and savings against negotiated rates, and present it at quarterly reviews and contract renewals. The reporting is part of the service the client pays transaction or management fees for.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 analysts, data engineers and programme managers per large TMC (estimate)
**Output:** Programme dashboards, savings and compliance reports, quarterly business reviews, benchmarks across clients
**Proprietary data:** Booking and servicing records across many corporate programmes, combined with client card and expense feeds
**Clock:** Client quarterly reviews and renewal dates
**Buyer:** Head of client analytics or global account management
