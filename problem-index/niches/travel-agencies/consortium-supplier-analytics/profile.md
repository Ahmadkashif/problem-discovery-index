# Consortium & Host Network Supplier Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/travel-agencies|Travel Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Consortia (Virtuoso, Signature, Internova's Travel Leaders Network, Ensemble) and the largest host agencies analyse their members' sales by supplier to negotiate preferred-partner terms, set marketing fees and report production back to suppliers and members. Virtuoso alone reports ~$35B in normalised annual sales across over 1,200 agency locations. Suppliers pay marketing and partnership fees partly for access to that sales view.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 supplier-relations analysts and data staff per large network (estimate)
**Output:** Supplier production reports, preferred-partner scorecards, member benchmarking
**Proprietary data:** Bookings reported by member agencies across thousands of suppliers
**Clock:** Annual preferred-partner renewals and the network's own event calendar
**Buyer:** Head of supplier relations or chief operating officer
