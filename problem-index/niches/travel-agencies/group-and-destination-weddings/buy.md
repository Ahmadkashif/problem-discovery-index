# Event Registration That Knows It Is Filling a Block

**Niche:** [[niches/travel-agencies/group-and-destination-weddings/profile|Group & Destination Wedding Travel]]
**Industry:** [[industries/travel-agencies|Travel Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Registration and payment tools collect guests and money well, but have no idea that each guest is drawing down a supplier block with its own deadlines.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #automation #descriptive-statistics #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to fill a contracted room or cabin block without paying attrition, and whoever forecasts pickup early enough to release or extend space before the supplier's cut-off takes the account.

## The Problem
Advisors set up a wedding page with a form and a payment link, then copy every registration into the supplier's portal and a spreadsheet, and reconcile guest payments against the supplier's deposit schedule by hand.

## What Already Exists
Event registration platforms (Eventbrite, Cvent, Jotform with Stripe) with forms, payment plans, reminders and attendee dashboards.

## The Customization Gap
The group-travel version needs: the block as inventory (room or cabin categories, occupancy rules, rates); per-guest payment plans that roll up to the supplier's deposit and final-payment dates; name and passport collection in each supplier's format; push of bookings into supplier group portals; automatic reminders keyed to supplier deadlines; and a group-leader view that shows pickup without exposing guests' payment details.

## Target Customer
Destination-wedding and group-cruise specialists, and hosts that offer group tools to their advisors.

## Impact If Solved
One system of record for each group, hours a week of double entry removed, and deadlines that drive reminders automatically.
