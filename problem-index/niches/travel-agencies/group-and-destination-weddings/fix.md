# The Final-Payment Chase by Text Message

**Niche:** [[niches/travel-agencies/group-and-destination-weddings/profile|Group & Destination Wedding Travel]]
**Industry:** [[industries/travel-agencies|Travel Agencies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Weeks before final payment, the advisor is personally texting dozens of wedding guests who haven't paid, because the supplier will cancel their rooms if they don't.
**Tags:** #logistic-regression #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #automation #worker-facing #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to fill a contracted room or cabin block without paying attrition, and whoever forecasts pickup early enough to release or extend space before the supplier's cut-off takes the account.

## The Problem
Supplier final-payment dates are firm, and unpaid guests are cancelled and their rooms returned to the block or released. The advisor spends the last fortnight chasing late payers, fielding questions about insurance and room changes, and updating the couple on who is still outstanding.

## Why It's Still Broken
Guest payment status lives in supplier portals that do not send reminders on the advisor's behalf, and the advisor's tools do not know the supplier's dates.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Automatic, deadline-aware reminders by text and email with a payment link; a model that flags guests likely to miss the date so the advisor calls those few personally; an assistant that answers routine questions about payment plans and insurance; and a daily summary for the couple.

## Who Feels the Pain
Group-specialist advisors in peak season; couples embarrassed by chasing their own guests; guests who lose rooms they meant to keep.

## Impact If Fixed
Peak-season chasing shrinks from days to an hour or two of targeted calls (estimate), and fewer guests are cancelled at final payment.
