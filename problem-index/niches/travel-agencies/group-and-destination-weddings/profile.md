# Group & Destination Wedding Travel

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/travel-agencies|Travel Agencies]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to fill a contracted room or cabin block without paying attrition, and whoever forecasts pickup early enough to release or extend space before the supplier's cut-off takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.5B commissions and group incentives on agency-booked group hotel, resort and cruise space (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of agency receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — block contracts are PDFs, guest tracking is a spreadsheet, chasing is by text
**Target Buyer:** Group-specialist advisor or agency owner
**Automation Potential:** High for tracking and reminders, medium for release decisions

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Destination weddings, family reunions, church and alumni trips and group cruises run on supplier-held blocks: the agency contracts space with deposit, name-in and release dates, and each guest books and pays individually over months. The advisor earns group commission plus tour-conductor credits, and carries attrition risk if the block underfills. Unlike a tour operator (see [[industries/tour-operators|Tour Operators]]), the agency does not package or own the inventory; unlike corporate meetings, which belong to [[industries/event-planning|Event Planning]], the guests are consumers paying their own way.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Supplier group portals (cruise lines, all-inclusive resort groups), wedding-travel microsites, event registration tools with payment links, and agency CRMs. Gaps: no system holds the block contract, guest payments and supplier deadlines together; pickup is judged by feel; reminders are sent by hand; and the couple or group leader is updated by screenshot.
