# Mass Notification With Itineraries Behind It

**Niche:** [[niches/travel-agencies/traveller-tracking-duty-of-care/profile|Traveller Tracking & Duty of Care]]
**Industry:** [[industries/travel-agencies|Travel Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Enterprise mass-notification platforms reach employees fast but know where they work, not where they are travelling.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell a company within minutes of an incident which of its people are in the affected area, including those who booked outside the programme, and whoever accounts for every traveller fastest takes the account.

## The Problem
Many large companies already run a critical-event notification platform for offices and facilities. Travellers are handled separately through the TMC's or the assistance provider's tool, so an incident means two systems, two lists and two sets of responses.

## What Already Exists
Critical event management and mass-notification platforms (Everbridge, AlertMedia and others) with multi-channel messaging, response tracking and geofenced alerts for employee home and office locations.

## The Customization Gap
The travel adaptation needs: live ingestion of TMC itinerary feeds and NDC order changes; time-aware location (in the air, connecting, at the hotel); inclusion of out-of-programme stays from card data; messaging rules that respect time zones and flight status; and response handling that hands an unaccounted traveller to the assistance provider rather than a facilities manager.

## Target Customer
Corporate security teams that own a notification platform and want one incident workflow for offices and travellers.

## Impact If Solved
One list and one response workflow per incident, with travellers handled as carefully as people in a building.
