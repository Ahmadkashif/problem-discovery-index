# Corporate Travel Data Consolidation & Rate Audit

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/travel-agencies|Travel Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Specialist vendors that consolidate a corporate client's travel data across TMCs, cards and expense systems, audit whether negotiated hotel rates were loaded and offered, and re-shop booked hotel reservations for lower rates before the cancellation deadline. Corporate travel managers pay per audit, per booking or by subscription, often on a savings basis.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 analysts and data staff per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Consolidated programme data, rate-loading and availability audits, re-shop savings reports
**Proprietary data:** Rate-audit and re-shop histories across clients, built on client booking data
**Clock:** Each re-shop must land before the hotel's cancellation deadline; audits run ahead of the client's programme year
**Buyer:** Head of analytics or chief executive at the vendor
