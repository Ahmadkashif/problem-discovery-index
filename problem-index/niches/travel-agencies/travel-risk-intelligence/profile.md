# Travel Risk Intelligence Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/travel-agencies|Travel Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
International SOS, Crisis24 and smaller providers such as Riskline publish country and city risk ratings, real-time security and medical alerts, and analyst assessments for corporate travel programmes, often delivered through the TMC's tracking feed. Crisis24 says it rates 200+ countries, 800+ provinces and 400+ cities across 27 threat categories. Corporate security, HR and travel managers pay subscriptions, usually bundled with tracking and assistance.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-400 intelligence analysts and regional specialists per large provider (estimate; providers do not publish headcount)
**Output:** Risk ratings, travel advisories, incident alerts, pre-trip briefings, special reports
**Proprietary data:** Years of the provider's own ratings and incident assessments, plus ground networks and assistance case histories; most incident inputs are open sources
**Clock:** The events themselves — an attack, outbreak or storm — rather than a deadline set on the work
**Buyer:** Head of intelligence or chief security officer at the provider
