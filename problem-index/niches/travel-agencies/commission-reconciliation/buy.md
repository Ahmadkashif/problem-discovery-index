# Accounts Receivable Automation Adapted to Supplier Commissions

**Niche:** [[niches/travel-agencies/commission-reconciliation/profile|Supplier Commission Reconciliation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/travel-agencies|Travel Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** AR automation platforms match remittances to invoices and chase late payers for B2B sellers; an agency's commissions are receivables without invoices.
**Tags:** #k-nearest-neighbors #large-language-models #data-integration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to match every expected commission to a payment and chase the rest before the supplier stops answering, and whoever recovers the highest share of earned commission takes the account.

## The Problem
Commission statements arrive as PDFs, CSVs, emails and clearing-house files, each with its own layout, and the agency must turn them into matched receipts and a list of what is missing.

## What Already Exists
Accounts receivable automation platforms (HighRadius, Billtrust, Versapay and similar) that capture remittances, apply cash to open invoices with machine matching, and run collections workflows.

## The Customization Gap
The commission version needs: open items generated from bookings rather than issued invoices; expected amounts that change with cancellations, date changes and non-commissionable components; matching on guest name, confirmation number and stay dates; the host-advisor split and advisor statements; and collection routes specific to travel — the property, the brand's agency desk, industry reporting tools and contingency recovery.

## Target Customer
Finance leads at host agencies and larger leisure agencies already using an AR tool for other revenue.

## Impact If Solved
Statement processing becomes largely automatic and the commission team spends its time on the claims that need a person.
