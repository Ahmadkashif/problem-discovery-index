# Supplier Trade-Channel Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/travel-agencies|Travel Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Cruise lines, hotel groups and tour operators run trade-sales teams that analyse agency production to set commission tiers, overrides, preferred-agency status and co-op marketing. Advisors book a large share of cruises (a CLIA-reported survey says 63% of cruisers used one), so these teams decide much of an agency's income.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 channel and sales analysts per large supplier (estimate)
**Output:** Agency production rankings, tier and override decisions, campaign analysis
**Proprietary data:** The supplier's own bookings by agency, advisor and consortium
**Clock:** Annual tier resets and the supplier's own campaign calendar
**Buyer:** Vice president of trade sales
