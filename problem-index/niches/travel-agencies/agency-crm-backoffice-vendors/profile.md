# Agency CRM & Back-Office Software Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/travel-agencies|Travel Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Trams and ClientBase (back office and CRM), and newer itinerary and CRM tools such as Travefy, Axus, Tern and TravelJoy, sell subscriptions to agencies, hosts and independent advisors. Their analytics are product features — sales reports, commission tracking — rather than a research product.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 data and analytics staff per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** In-product reports and benchmarks
**Proprietary data:** Agencies' client and booking records held under subscription terms
**Clock:** Product release cycles
**Buyer:** Chief product officer
