# DOT Aviation Consumer Protection

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/travel-agencies|Travel Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Department of Transportation's aviation consumer protection staff write and enforce rules that bind ticket agents as well as airlines — refunds under 14 CFR 260 (finalised April 2024), fare advertising and disclosure — and analyse consumer complaints. Their output is rules, guidance, complaint statistics and enforcement actions, funded by appropriation.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 analysts and investigators working on complaints and enforcement analysis (estimate)
**Output:** Complaint statistics, enforcement orders, rulemaking analyses
**Proprietary data:** Consumer complaint records and enforcement files
**Clock:** Statutory rulemaking deadlines and complaint response cycles
**Buyer:** Office director, publicly funded
