# GDS Marketing Information Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/travel-agencies|Travel Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Sabre, Amadeus and Travelport, the global distribution systems agencies book through, package their booking records as marketing information data (MIDT) and related analytics, sold mainly to airlines for network planning, sales and revenue management. The EU code of conduct for reservation systems, as retained in UK law, lets them sell marketing, booking and sales data to all carriers on equal terms, provided it does not identify the subscribing agency unless the agency agrees. Whether GDSs belong in this code or in [[industries/ticketing-agencies|Ticketing & Reservation Agencies]] varies by source; this profile covers only the agency-booking data product.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 data product, analytics and client staff per GDS (estimate)
**Output:** MIDT files, market-share and demand analytics, agency-level production reports where agencies consent
**Proprietary data:** Every booking made through the GDS by every subscribing agency
**Clock:** Monthly and daily data deliveries on the vendor's own schedule
**Buyer:** Head of data products at the GDS
