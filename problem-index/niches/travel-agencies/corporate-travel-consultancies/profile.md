# Corporate Travel Consultancies & Sourcing Advisors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/travel-agencies|Travel Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Advito, BCD Travel's consulting arm, the consulting groups inside the large TMCs and independent travel consultancies design corporate travel programmes: TMC and airline RFPs, annual hotel sourcing, policy reviews, savings benchmarks and price forecasts. BCD reported $22.9B in sales for 2024; Advito publishes an annual industry price forecast that buyers use to budget and negotiate. The client's procurement or travel function pays by project or retainer.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-200 consultants and analysts per large consultancy (estimate)
**Output:** Programme assessments, sourcing recommendations, negotiated-rate analyses, benchmarks, annual price forecasts
**Proprietary data:** Benchmarks accumulated across client programmes — booking, card and hotel data supplied by each client and its TMC under engagement terms
**Clock:** The annual hotel sourcing season and contract renewal dates, set by the client's programme calendar
**Buyer:** Head of consulting or managing director
