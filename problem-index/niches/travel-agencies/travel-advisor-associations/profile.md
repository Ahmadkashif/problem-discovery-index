# Travel Advisor Associations & Certification Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/travel-agencies|Travel Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
ASTA represents US travel advisors, lobbies on rules such as DOT's refund regulation and runs tools like its hotel commission watch list; GBTA serves corporate travel buyers and publishes its Business Travel Index with partners; The Travel Institute certifies advisors. Members, sponsors and course fees fund them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 dedicated research staff per body; major studies are produced with outside partners (estimate)
**Output:** Member surveys, policy papers, the GBTA index (global spending forecast ~$1.57T for 2025), certification exams
**Proprietary data:** Member surveys and certification records
**Clock:** Annual conventions and regulatory comment deadlines
**Buyer:** Chief executive or head of research
