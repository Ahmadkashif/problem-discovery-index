# Travel Market Research Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/travel-agencies|Travel Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Phocuswright, Skift Research and a few smaller houses size travel markets by segment and channel, survey travellers and advisors, and publish reports, data sheets and conference content. Phocuswright's U.S. Travel Market Report series (June 2025) includes a dedicated online travel agency report. Suppliers, agencies, technology vendors and investors buy subscriptions and single reports.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 analysts per publisher (estimate)
**Output:** Market-sizing reports, channel-share estimates, consumer and advisor surveys, forecasts
**Proprietary data:** Long-running market models, consumer survey panels and executive interviews; much of the underlying data is public filings and supplier disclosures
**Clock:** The publisher's own report and conference calendar
**Buyer:** Head of research
