# Airline Distribution & NDC Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/travel-agencies|Travel Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Small consultancies and independent analysts advise airlines, agencies, TMCs and investors on distribution economics: GDS contracts, NDC strategy, surcharges on GDS bookings, and agency technology choices. NDC reached 21.2% of ARC-settled transactions by December 2025, which keeps the work in demand. Clients pay by project or retainer.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 1-10 consultants per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Strategy reports, distribution cost studies, vendor selections, expert commentary
**Proprietary data:** Interview notes and engagement files, largely client-confidential
**Clock:** Client contract renewals and airline distribution changes
**Buyer:** Founder or principal
