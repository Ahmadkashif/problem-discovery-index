# Travel Agency Brokers & Valuation Advisers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/travel-agencies|Travel Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Business brokers and small advisory firms value and sell travel agencies and TMCs, mostly owner-retirement sales to larger agencies, consolidators and host networks. Sellers pay a success fee.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 1-10 people per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Valuations, offering memoranda, negotiated sales
**Proprietary data:** Deal files and comparable transactions, held privately
**Clock:** The seller's own timetable
**Buyer:** Founder or principal
