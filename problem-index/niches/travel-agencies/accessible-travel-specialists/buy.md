# Crowdsourced Accessibility Mapping Pointed at Travel Inventory

**Niche:** [[niches/travel-agencies/accessible-travel-specialists/profile|Accessible Travel Specialists]]
**Industry:** [[industries/travel-agencies|Travel Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Accessibility mapping apps already crowdsource reviews of restaurants, shops and streets; travel needs the same idea applied to specific room types, cabins and transfers.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #k-nearest-neighbors #data-integration #worker-facing #evaluation-metrics
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to verify that a room, cabin, transfer or excursion is actually usable for a specific traveller's disability before departure, and whoever's verification holds up on arrival most often takes the account.

## The Problem
Specialist advisors and disabled travellers each hold fragments of verified information. There is no shared place to put them that maps to bookable inventory.

## What Already Exists
Crowdsourced accessibility apps and maps for public venues (AccessNow, Wheelmap and similar), with ratings, photos and community moderation.

## The Customization Gap
The travel version needs: records keyed to bookable units (hotel room type, ship and cabin number, transfer company and vehicle) rather than to a place; measurement fields rather than star ratings; dated evidence and expiry when properties renovate; links to supplier booking codes so the verified room can be requested specifically; and contribution incentives for advisors, whose verification work is their competitive edge.

## Target Customer
Accessible-travel specialists and the hosts and consortia that support them.

## Impact If Solved
Verified information compounds across the community instead of staying in one advisor's files.
