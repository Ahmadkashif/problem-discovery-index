# State Seller of Travel Regulators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/travel-agencies|Travel Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
California's Attorney General, Florida's Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services, and Washington and Hawaii regulators register sellers of travel and enforce trust-account, bond and disclosure rules. California also runs the Travel Consumer Restitution Fund, which repays registered sellers' customers up to $15,000 per passenger when a seller fails.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analysts per state programme (estimate)
**Output:** Registrations, complaint handling, enforcement actions, restitution decisions
**Proprietary data:** Registration filings, complaints and restitution claims
**Clock:** Annual registration renewals and claim deadlines
**Buyer:** Programme manager, publicly funded
