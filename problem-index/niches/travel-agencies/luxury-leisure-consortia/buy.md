# Partner Relationship Software for Preferred-Supplier Programmes

**Niche:** [[niches/travel-agencies/luxury-leisure-consortia/profile|Luxury Leisure Advisors & Consortia]]
**Industry:** [[industries/travel-agencies|Travel Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Consortia manage thousands of preferred-partner agreements, production targets and marketing funds in spreadsheets and their own portals, while partner relationship software exists for exactly this shape of programme.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #time-series-forecasting #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get the client something on arrival that a direct booker would not get — the upgrade, the credit, the recognition — and whoever has its amenities honoured at the front desk most reliably takes the account.

## The Problem
Each preferred partner has an agreement: amenities owed, marketing fees paid, production expected from members, events attended. The supplier-relations team tracks them across contracts, sales reports from members and the partner's own figures, and reconciles them at renewal.

## What Already Exists
Partner relationship management platforms (Salesforce PRM, Impartner and similar) built for technology and manufacturing channel programmes: tiering, deal registration, market development funds, scorecards.

## The Customization Gap
The travel version needs: amenity terms as structured objects linked to bookings; production measured from member sales by partner, brand and property; marketing-fund accruals tied to campaigns and events; amenity-delivery outcomes on the partner scorecard; and member-facing views that show advisors what each partner owes their clients.

## Target Customer
Supplier-relations and finance teams at consortia and at hosts running preferred programmes.

## Impact If Solved
Renewals based on a complete scorecard rather than a spreadsheet reconciliation, and amenity terms that advisors can see and enforce.
