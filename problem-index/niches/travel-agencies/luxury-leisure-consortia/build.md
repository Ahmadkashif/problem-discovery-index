# Amenities Nobody Checks

**Niche:** [[niches/travel-agencies/luxury-leisure-consortia/profile|Luxury Leisure Advisors & Consortia]]
**Industry:** [[industries/travel-agencies|Travel Agencies]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Consortia promise amenities through thousands of preferred partners and never measure, property by property, whether the client actually received them.
**Tags:** #tacit-knowledge-ml #bayesian-inference #large-language-models #hypothesis-testing #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get the client something on arrival that a direct booker would not get — the upgrade, the credit, the recognition — and whoever has its amenities honoured at the front desk most reliably takes the account.

## The Problem
Every preferred-partner listing is a promise: book through a member advisor and the client gets breakfast, a credit and an upgrade subject to availability. Whether that happens depends on the property's staff reading the booking, the general manager's attitude to agency business, and occupancy that night. Advisors learn which properties deliver through their own clients' complaints and keep the knowledge to themselves. The consortium renews partners on sales volume.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Outcome data sits with clients and advisors, arrives as texts and anecdotes, and is never structured. Consortia fear antagonising the partners who pay marketing fees, and individual advisors gain from the private advantage of knowing which properties to avoid.

## What to Build
An amenity-delivery record built from structured outcome signals: a one-tap post-stay check by the advisor or client, supplier folio data where available, complaint and recovery records, and LLM extraction of advisor messages. A hierarchical model estimates delivery rates by property, brand and season with honest uncertainty for thinly booked properties, surfaces them to advisors at the point of recommendation, and gives the supplier-relations team evidence for renewals. This captures the senior advisor's private supplier ledger and makes it the network's.

## Target Customer
Consortium heads of supplier relations and member services; large luxury agencies and hosts with their own preferred programmes.

## Impact If Built
The network can show members, and partners, which amenities are actually delivered; advisors stop sending clients to properties that ignore agency bookings; and renewals reward delivery, not just volume.
