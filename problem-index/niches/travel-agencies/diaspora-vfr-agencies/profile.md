# Diaspora & VFR Fare Agencies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/travel-agencies|Travel Agencies]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to change or refund a family's net-fare ticket the day their plans change without triggering a debit memo, and whoever reissues changes fastest under the consolidator's rules takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1B agency margin and service fees on consolidator and net fares sold to diaspora communities (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of agency receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — quotes by phone and WhatsApp, payment by cash, card or Zelle at the counter, tickets issued through a consolidator
**Target Buyer:** Storefront agency owner
**Automation Potential:** Medium — fare search and reissue logic automate, the relationship stays personal

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Storefront and home-based agencies in immigrant neighbourhoods sell visiting-friends-and-relatives travel to South Asia, West Africa, the Middle East, Latin America and the Caribbean. Their customers fly long-haul, often multi-generational, with heavy baggage and plans that change around weddings, funerals and visas. The agencies buy net fares from consolidators below published levels and add their margin; the customer stays loyal because the agent speaks their language, takes their call at night and gets the ticket changed when the funeral date moves. Online travel agencies serve this traffic badly because the cheapest fares are unpublished and the changes are complex.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Consolidator booking sites and their GDS-based ticketing, WhatsApp, paper ledgers, and the owner's memory of each family. Gaps: net-fare rules that differ by consolidator and airline; reissues calculated by hand; debit memos that wipe out a month's margin; and no record of customer history beyond the agent's phone.
