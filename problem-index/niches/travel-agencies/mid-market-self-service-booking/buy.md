# Consumer Hotel Search With Policy Underneath

**Niche:** [[niches/travel-agencies/mid-market-self-service-booking/profile|Mid-Market Self-Service Booking Adoption]]
**Industry:** [[industries/travel-agencies|Travel Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Travellers leave the corporate tool because consumer hotel search shows better rates and loyalty options; the fix is to put the consumer experience inside the programme rather than fight it.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #k-nearest-neighbors #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a growing company's employees to book and change their own trips in the tool without calling an agent, and whoever keeps the highest share of trips booked and serviced touchless takes the account.

## The Problem
An employee compares the corporate tool's hotel results with a brand app and a consumer site, finds a lower visible rate or a member rate, and books outside. The company loses the booking data and the safety record; the TMC loses the transaction.

## What Already Exists
Consumer hotel search and ranking from online travel agencies and metasearch, with mature personalisation and rate display; hotel content aggregators and bed banks that supply rates to corporate tools.

## The Customization Gap
The corporate version needs: ranking that blends traveller preference with distance to the meeting, policy cap, negotiated rate and the company's volume commitments; display of loyalty earning on programme rates; rate parity checks that show when an outside rate is genuinely lower and allow it with the booking captured; and reporting that tells the finance buyer which searches ended in a booking outside.

## Target Customer
Booking-tool product managers at TMCs and travel platforms serving companies without a travel manager.

## Impact If Solved
Higher hotel attachment to air bookings, complete traveller location data, and a measurable reason for the finance buyer to keep the tool.
