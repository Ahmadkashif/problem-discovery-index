# Account Reconciliation Software for Weekly ARC Settlement

**Niche:** [[niches/travel-agencies/ticketing-exchanges-settlement/profile|Air Ticketing, Exchanges & ARC Settlement]]
**Industry:** [[industries/travel-agencies|Travel Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Every week an agency must reconcile what it ticketed against what ARC will settle, and finance teams do it with exports and spreadsheets while reconciliation software exists for exactly this shape of work.
**Tags:** #k-nearest-neighbors #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #automation #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to process exchanges, refunds and voids without manual rework, and whoever generates the fewest agency debit memos per thousand tickets takes the account.

## The Problem
Before each weekly settlement the agency reviews its sales report, voids, refunds, exchanges and memos, matches them to its own back office and client invoices, and corrects errors before the deadline. Mismatches — a void that didn't take, an exchange recorded twice, an NDC order missing from the back office — become cash problems.

## What Already Exists
Account reconciliation and close-management platforms (BlackLine, Trintech, Numeric and similar) with rule-based and learned matching, exception queues and audit trails.

## The Customization Gap
The ARC version needs: ingestion of ARC sales and memo reports and GDS ticketing records; matching rules for exchanges, residual values, voids and refunds; NDC order-to-settlement mapping; the weekly settlement calendar as the close deadline; and posting back to agency back offices such as Trams.

## Target Customer
Controllers and accounting managers at mid-size TMCs, consolidators and leisure agencies.

## Impact If Solved
Settlement errors caught before the deadline instead of after, and a weekly close measured in hours rather than days.
