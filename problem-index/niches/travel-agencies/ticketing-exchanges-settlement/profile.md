# Air Ticketing, Exchanges & ARC Settlement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/travel-agencies|Travel Agencies]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to process exchanges, refunds and voids without manual rework, and whoever generates the fewest agency debit memos per thousand tickets takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.2B agency mid- and back-office labour and tooling spent on air ticketing, servicing and settlement (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~4% of agency receipts (estimate), on $100.4B of ARC-settled air sales in 2025
**Digital Adoption:** High — every transaction is electronic, but exceptions are worked by hand in GDS terminals and airline portals
**Target Buyer:** Director of operations or ticketing manager at a TMC, consolidator or large leisure agency
**Automation Potential:** Very high for rule application and reconciliation, medium for disputes

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every ticket an accredited US agency issues is reported and settled through ARC on a weekly cycle; airlines audit the transactions and issue agency debit memos when an exchange, refund or fare breaks a rule, settled through the same system. Since DOT's 2024 rule (14 CFR 260), an agency that is merchant of record must refund cancelled or significantly changed tickets within 7 business days on card purchases. NDC adds orders that settle differently from tickets, and 1,139 agencies were reporting NDC transactions by December 2025. The work is mechanical and unforgiving: the right answer exists, and the cost of a wrong one arrives weeks later.

## Current Tools & Gaps
GDS ticketing and automated exchange tools, ARC's reporting and memo-management systems, robotic queue-processing tools, NDC aggregators and agency back offices. Gaps: automated exchanges that fail on complex fares and fall back to manual work; refunds tracked in spreadsheets against the DOT clock; NDC orders that do not reconcile cleanly with ARC settlement; and debit memos disputed late or not at all.
