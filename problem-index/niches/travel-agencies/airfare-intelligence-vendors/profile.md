# Airfare Intelligence Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/travel-agencies|Travel Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms that shop and archive published airfares across channels and sell competitor fare data and analytics, mainly to airline pricing and revenue-management teams and secondarily to online agencies and TMCs benchmarking fares. Infare, used by more than 165 airlines and airports, was acquired by OAG in 2023 in a deal valuing the combined company at over $500M.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 data analysts, data scientists and client analysts per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Competitor fare feeds, fare-position dashboards, pricing analytics
**Proprietary data:** Years of shopped fares by route, channel and booking date — an archive nobody else can reconstruct after the fact
**Clock:** Competitors' fare changes, continuously; no deadline set on the vendor's work
**Buyer:** Chief product officer or head of data at the vendor
