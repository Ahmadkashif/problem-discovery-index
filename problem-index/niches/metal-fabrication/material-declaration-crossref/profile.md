**Parent Industry:** [[industries/metal-fabrication|Metal Fabrication]]
**Category:** Insight Layer

## What They Do
Compliance data providers assemble the substance, conflict mineral, and material declaration data manufacturers must collect from their supply chains to sell into regulated markets.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 compliance analysts, chemists, and data engineers
**Output:** Substance declarations, compliance determinations, supply chain data collection, regulatory updates
**Proprietary data:** Assembled supplier declarations and substance data across enormous component populations
**Clock:** Regulatory deadlines and customer declaration requirements
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Compliance Content
