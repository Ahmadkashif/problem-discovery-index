# Metals Price Reporting Agencies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/metal-fabrication|Metal Fabrication]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Metals price reporting agencies assess and publish the steel, aluminium, and scrap prices that mill contracts, service centre agreements, and fabrication quotes are indexed to. In a market where most transactions are private and negotiated, the published assessment is what buyers and sellers agree to settle against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 market reporters, analysts, economists, and methodology staff
**Output:** Daily and weekly price assessments, forward curves, cost models, market analysis, index licensing
**Proprietary data:** Reporter-collected transaction, bid, and offer data across a market with no exchange for most grades, plus decades of continuous series and the assessment methodology built around them
**Clock:** Daily and weekly publication that settles physical contracts and index-linked supply agreements
**Buyer:** Editorial director / Chief Data Officer
