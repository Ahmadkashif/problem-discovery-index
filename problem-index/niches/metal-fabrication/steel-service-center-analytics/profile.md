# Steel Service Centre Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/metal-fabrication|Metal Fabrication]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Metals service centres buy from mills, process to size, and sell to fabricators, and their commercial analytics groups manage inventory position and pricing in a market where the value of the inventory moves faster than it turns.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-200 commercial analysts, pricing staff, and demand planners
**Output:** Inventory and hedging position, pricing and margin management, demand forecasting, customer segmentation
**Proprietary data:** Order and shipment data at fabricator level across regions — the earliest read on what is actually being built
**Clock:** Mill order cycles and price movements that reprice inventory weekly
**Buyer:** Chief Commercial Officer / VP of Analytics
