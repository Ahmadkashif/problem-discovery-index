## What They Do
The acquisitive fabrication platforms run corporate operations analytics across many shops, standardizing estimating, tracking machine utilization, and underwriting acquisitions.

## Insight Function

**Output:** Acquisition models, estimating standardization, utilization and margin analysis, capacity balancing
**Proprietary data:** Quoted versus actual job cost across many shops and job types
**Clock:** Acquisition pipeline and quarterly reporting
**Buyer:** No external buyer — internal cost centre
