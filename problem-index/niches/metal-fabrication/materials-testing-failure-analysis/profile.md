# Materials Testing & Failure Analysis

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/metal-fabrication|Metal Fabrication]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Independent laboratories test material properties, verify heat treatment and coating, and determine why a fabricated component failed — findings that decide warranty liability, contract acceptance, and litigation.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-250 metallurgists, materials engineers, and laboratory technicians
**Output:** Material certification and verification, failure analysis reports, root cause determinations, expert testimony
**Proprietary data:** Failure modes correlated to material, process, and application across thousands of investigations
**Clock:** Production hold decisions and litigation schedules
**Buyer:** Laboratory director / Practice leader
