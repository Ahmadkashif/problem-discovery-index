# Fabrication Estimating Benchmarks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/metal-fabrication|Metal Fabrication]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Small consultancies and associations publish the shop rate, cycle time, and productivity benchmarks fabricators quote against, in a market where every quote is a bet on labour hours nobody measures well.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-20 analysts and consultants
**Output:** Shop rate and productivity benchmarks, estimating standards, cost model guidance
**Proprietary data:** Contributed operating data from participating fabricators
**Clock:** Annual benchmarking cycles
**Buyer:** Principal — partly member funded
