## What They Do
The programming and nesting software vendors turn part geometry into machine instructions and material layouts, determining how much of a sheet becomes product and how much becomes scrap.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-200 software engineers, applications engineers, and operations researchers
**Output:** Nesting and toolpath generation, material utilization optimization, machine integration
**Proprietary data:** Part geometry, nesting outcomes, and machine performance across many shops
**Clock:** Production scheduling
**Buyer:** VP of Product / Chief Technology Officer
