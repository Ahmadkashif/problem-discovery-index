# Workplace Safety Enforcement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/metal-fabrication|Metal Fabrication]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Federal and state workplace safety agencies inspect fabrication shops, cite hazards, and set the standards governing machine guarding, hexavalent chromium exposure, and confined space work — the rules a shop's operations are built around.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,500 compliance officers, industrial hygienists, and analysts
**Output:** Inspection findings and citations, standards development, injury and exposure data, enforcement statistics
**Proprietary data:** Inspection, citation, and injury records across the manufacturing establishment population
**Clock:** Inspection and abatement timelines
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — federal or state agency
