# Nondestructive Testing Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/metal-fabrication|Metal Fabrication]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
NDT providers inspect welds and fabricated assemblies by radiography, ultrasonics, and other methods, and their findings determine whether a fabrication is accepted, reworked, or rejected.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-800 certified technicians, level III examiners, and analysts
**Output:** Inspection reports and acceptance determinations, defect characterization, procedure qualification
**Proprietary data:** Defect findings by weld type, process, fabricator, and material across enormous inspection volumes
**Clock:** Production hold points and project schedules
**Buyer:** VP of Operations / Technical Director
