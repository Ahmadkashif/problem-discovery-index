# Welding Certification & Procedure Qualification

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/metal-fabrication|Metal Fabrication]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The welding society writes the codes fabricated structures must be built to, qualifies welding procedures, and certifies the welders and inspectors whose stamps make a fabrication acceptable. Codes are adopted by reference into building and pressure vessel regulation.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-250 standards engineers, certification staff, and technical committee support
**Output:** Welding codes and standards, procedure qualification, welder and inspector certification, technical interpretations
**Proprietary data:** The code corpus with its full development history, plus certification performance across the welding workforce
**Clock:** Code revision cycles and certification renewal periods
**Buyer:** Executive director / VP of Certification
