# Industrial Distributor Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/metal-fabrication|Metal Fabrication]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The industrial distributors supplying consumables, tooling, and abrasives to fabrication shops run category analytics across millions of transactions, and their purchase data is a leading indicator of shop activity.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 category, pricing, and supply chain analysts
**Output:** Assortment and pricing analysis, customer segmentation, demand planning, vendor programme management
**Proprietary data:** Consumable purchase histories at shop level across the manufacturing base
**Clock:** Replenishment cycles and quarterly vendor programmes
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Category Management
