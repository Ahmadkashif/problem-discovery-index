# Fitness Industry Benchmark Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/gyms-independent|Independent Gyms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
The industry association research units publish the annual operating and consumer participation reports gym owners use to compare their revenue per member, attrition, and payroll ratio against peers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 3-12 researchers
**Output:** Annual industry reports, operating benchmarks, consumer participation surveys
**Proprietary data:** Member-contributed operating data and long-running participation series
**Clock:** Annual publication
**Buyer:** Research director — association funded
