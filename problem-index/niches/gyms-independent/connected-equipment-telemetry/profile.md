# Commercial Equipment Telemetry Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/gyms-independent|Independent Gyms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Life Fitness, Technogym, Precor, and Matrix ship connected cardio and strength equipment that reports usage and fault codes back to the manufacturer. The teams behind that telemetry support service contracts and product development.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 reliability engineers, service analysts, and data staff
**Output:** Service scheduling, fault diagnosis, product reliability analysis
**Proprietary data:** Usage hours and fault histories across a large installed base — the raw material for the preventive maintenance Pass 1 says gyms do not do
**Clock:** Service contract obligations and warranty periods
**Buyer:** VP of Service / Director of Connected Products
