# Gym Management Software Data Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/gyms-independent|Independent Gyms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The data groups at Mindbody, ABC Fitness, Zen Planner, and Pike13 process membership, billing, scheduling, and check-in for tens of thousands of facilities. Pass 1 records that their retention reporting is backward-looking and requires manual CSV export for anything beyond a canned dashboard.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-120 data and analytics staff
**Output:** In-product reporting, industry benchmark publications, billing and dunning analytics
**Proprietary data:** Complete member lifecycle — join, attend, lapse, cancel — across a large share of the independent gym population
**Clock:** Monthly billing cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Chief Product Officer
