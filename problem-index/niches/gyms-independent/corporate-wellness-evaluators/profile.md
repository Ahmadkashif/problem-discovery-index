# Corporate Wellness Programme Evaluators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/gyms-independent|Independent Gyms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Consulting firms measure whether an employer's wellness spending changed anything — participation, health risk, claims cost — and produce the analysis that decides whether the programme is renewed, redesigned, or cut.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-70 analysts, health economists, and actuaries
**Output:** Programme evaluations, ROI and value-on-investment studies, benchmarking against peer employers
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated evaluation methodology and peer benchmarks; the underlying claims and participation data belongs to the employer and its carrier
**Clock:** The benefit plan year and open enrolment
**Buyer:** Practice leader / Managing Director
