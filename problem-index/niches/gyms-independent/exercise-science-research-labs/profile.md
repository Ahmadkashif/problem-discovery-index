# Exercise Science Research Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/gyms-independent|Independent Gyms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
University human performance laboratories and association research programmes produce the exercise physiology evidence that certification bodies turn into guidelines and the industry turns into programming.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 researchers and graduate staff per programme
**Output:** Peer-reviewed studies, position stands, exercise guidelines
**Proprietary data:** Laboratory protocols and study data, mostly published
**Clock:** Grant and publication cycles
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — grant and institutional funding
