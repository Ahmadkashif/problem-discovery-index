# Health Club Liability Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/gyms-independent|Independent Gyms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Specialty insurers underwrite general and professional liability for fitness facilities, pricing the risk of injury claims against facility type, equipment mix, supervision model, and waiver practice.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 underwriters, actuaries, and loss control specialists
**Output:** Rating models, loss control recommendations, claims analysis
**Proprietary data:** Injury claims by facility characteristic and equipment type across a book of thousands of clubs
**Clock:** Annual policy renewal
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
