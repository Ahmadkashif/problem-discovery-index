# Wearable & Fitness Device Data Science

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/gyms-independent|Independent Gyms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
The data science groups at the wearable makers turn continuous physiological signal into the readiness, recovery, and training-load guidance their subscribers act on daily.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 data scientists, physiologists, and algorithm engineers
**Output:** Recovery and strain scores, training recommendations, population research publications
**Proprietary data:** Continuous longitudinal physiology at population scale — heart rate, sleep, and activity across millions of people over years
**Clock:** Product release cycles, internal
**Buyer:** VP of Data Science
