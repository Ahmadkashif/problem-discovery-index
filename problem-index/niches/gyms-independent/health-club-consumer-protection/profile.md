# Health Club Contract Regulation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/gyms-independent|Independent Gyms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State consumer protection units administer the health club registration, bonding, and contract cancellation rules that govern membership agreements, and handle the complaints that follow.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 3-15 analysts and investigators per state programme
**Output:** Registration decisions, complaint findings, enforcement actions
**Proprietary data:** Complaint records by operator
**Clock:** Registration renewal and complaint timelines
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer
