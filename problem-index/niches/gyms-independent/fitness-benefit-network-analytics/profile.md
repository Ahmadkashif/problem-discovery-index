# Fitness Benefit Network Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/gyms-independent|Independent Gyms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Tivity Health, Wellhub, Active&Fit, and ClassPass sit between the people paying for fitness — health plans, Medicare Advantage insurers, employers — and the forty thousand gyms and studios that deliver it. They pay facilities per visit and are paid by the plan per member. The analytics teams inside them produce the utilization and outcomes reporting that determines whether a health plan renews the programme.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 analysts, actuaries, and data scientists
**Output:** Utilization reporting, engagement analysis, outcomes and cost-impact studies presented to plan clients
**Proprietary data:** Individual check-in histories across thousands of facilities, joined to plan eligibility and, under agreement, to claims — the only view of who actually goes to the gym and what happens to them
**Clock:** Health plan bid and renewal cycles, annual and fixed
**Buyer:** VP of Analytics / Chief Data Officer
