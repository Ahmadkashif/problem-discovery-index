# Fitness Certification Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/gyms-independent|Independent Gyms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
ACSM, NASM, ACE, and their peers define what a personal trainer or group instructor must know, write and administer the certification examinations, and publish the exercise guidelines the profession practises against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 exercise scientists, psychometricians, and content staff
**Output:** Certification examinations, competency frameworks, exercise guidelines, continuing education
**Proprietary data:** Candidate performance across hundreds of thousands of examinations, plus the job analyses that define the role
**Clock:** Examination and recertification cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Science Officer / VP of Certification
