# Fitness Franchise Development & Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/gyms-independent|Independent Gyms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup

## What They Do
The franchise systems — Anytime Fitness, Planet Fitness, the boutique studio platforms — run development groups that select sites, model trade areas, and analyze franchisee performance across thousands of locations to decide where the next unit goes and which operators are failing.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 site selection analysts, franchise business consultants, and data staff
**Output:** Trade area models, site recommendations, franchisee performance reviews
**Proprietary data:** Unit economics and member behaviour across thousands of comparable locations — the largest matched-facility dataset in fitness
**Clock:** Development pipeline and franchisee review cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Development / Head of Analytics
