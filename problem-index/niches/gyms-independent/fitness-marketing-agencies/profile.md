# Fitness Lead Generation Agencies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/gyms-independent|Independent Gyms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Specialist agencies run acquisition campaigns for gyms and studios — paid social, offers, trial funnels — and manage the trial-to-member conversion Pass 1 puts at 20-30%.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 media buyers, analysts, and campaign strategists
**Output:** Campaign performance reporting, offer testing, funnel diagnostics
**Proprietary data:** Creative, offer, and conversion results across hundreds of gyms and markets
**Clock:** January and September acquisition seasons
**Buyer:** Agency principal / Head of Growth
