# Gym Brokerage & Valuation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/gyms-independent|Independent Gyms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Business brokers specializing in fitness value and sell independent gyms and studios, pricing them on membership base, contract quality, lease terms, and equipment condition.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 2-8 brokers and analysts
**Output:** Valuations, offering memoranda, buyer matching
**Proprietary data:** Completed transaction comparables in a segment with no public price data
**Clock:** Transaction timelines
**Buyer:** Principal
