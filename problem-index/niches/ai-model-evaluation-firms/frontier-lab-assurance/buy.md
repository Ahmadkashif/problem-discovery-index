# Independent Assurance and Audit Practice

**Niche:** [[niches/ai-model-evaluation-firms/frontier-lab-assurance/profile|Frontier Lab Assurance]]
**Industry:** [[industries/ai-model-evaluation-firms|AI Model Evaluation Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Financial audit, clinical trial monitoring and safety certification all solved how a paid party produces a trustworthy opinion about the party paying them, and this industry has adopted none of it.
**Tags:** #compliance #evaluation-metrics #hypothesis-testing #confidence-intervals #descriptive-statistics #worker-facing #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to find capability or risk that the lab's own evaluation team did not — and whoever does that takes the contract, because a lab with a large internal team buys external evaluation for exactly one reason.

## The Problem
The structural problem of this business — an external party paid by the subject to produce an independent opinion — is one that several professions spent a century building institutions around. Auditing has independence rules, mandatory rotation, partner sign-off, working paper standards, peer review of the firm itself, and a professional body that can remove the right to practise. Clinical trials have independent monitoring boards and pre-registration. AI evaluation has a contract and a report.

## What Already Exists
Auditing standards with independence requirements, engagement quality review and documentation norms; mandatory rotation and non-audit service restrictions; professional bodies with disciplinary powers; clinical trial pre-registration and independent data monitoring committees; notified bodies and conformity assessment in product safety; and working paper standards that make an opinion reconstructable by a third party.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a subject that changes weekly and a field with no professional body. It requires: (1) pre-registration of the evaluation plan before the model is seen, borrowed directly from clinical trials, which prevents the plan from being shaped around what the model turns out to do and is cheap to adopt unilaterally; (2) working papers sufficient for another firm to reconstruct the conclusion, since a report whose basis is not reconstructable is an opinion rather than an assurance; (3) independence rules adapted to a market with a handful of customers, where the audit profession's rotation model is unworkable and the honest alternative is disclosure of concentration rather than a pretence of independence; (4) a scope statement that survives publication, because these reports are quoted selectively and a scope written to be quotable is the defence; and (5) staged disclosure of findings, since the safety-relevant ones cannot always be published immediately and the profession needs a convention for that rather than case-by-case negotiation.

## Target Customer
Assurance firms, frontier labs, regulators designing oversight regimes, and the audit and conformity assessment professions for whom this is an emerging application.

## Impact If Solved
A century of institutional design addresses exactly this conflict and the field operates on a contract and a report. Pre-registering the evaluation plan before seeing the model is adoptable unilaterally, today, by any firm willing to.
