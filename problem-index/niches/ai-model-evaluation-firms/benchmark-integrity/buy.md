**One-liner:** Educational assessment and competitive machine learning both solved the problem of testing against material the candidate must not have seen, and this industry is rediscovering it from scratch.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce a score that survives the question "was this in the training data?" — and whoever does that takes the account, because every number the industry currently sells is an upper bound of unknown looseness.

## The Problem
Keeping test material secure from candidates who would benefit from seeing it is the entire operational discipline of high-stakes assessment. Item banks are rotated, exposure is tracked per item, items are retired on a schedule, equating procedures keep scores comparable across forms, and the whole apparatus is run by an independent body rather than by the candidate. Competitive machine learning solved the adjacent problem with held-out test sets, submission limits and private leaderboards. This industry publishes its test set and hopes.

## What Already Exists
Item response theory with item banking, calibration and equating across forms; item exposure control methods from computerised adaptive testing; secure test administration and item retirement practice; independent assessment and certification bodies as an institutional model; and competition platforms with private leaderboards, submission limits and staged reveals.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a candidate that reads the entire public internet. It requires: (1) exposure control where the leak channel is a web crawl rather than a candidate's memory, which means an item is compromised the moment it is published anywhere and the retirement schedule must be driven by publication rather than by administration count; (2) equating across forms so that scores from different private item sets are comparable, which is exactly what assessment solved with item response theory and what this field currently handles by hoping the sets are similar; (3) submission-limited access to a private set, borrowed from competition practice, which prevents the slower leak in which repeated evaluation against a held-out set tunes a model to it; (4) an independent custodian, since the assessment world learned that the party being measured cannot hold the items and this field has not internalised that yet; and (5) procedural item generation calibrated to a known difficulty, which combines the generation approach with the assessment machinery for keeping generated items comparable.

## Target Customer
Evaluation firms, benchmark maintainers, standards and certification bodies, and the assessment industry for whom this is a direct and unclaimed application.

## Impact If Solved
High-stakes assessment solved secure item custody decades ago and this field publishes its tests. Equating across private forms is the specific piece that makes independent held-out evaluation practical rather than incomparable.
