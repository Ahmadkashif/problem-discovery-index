**Automation Potential:** High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is the largest revenue line: the harnesses, runners, graders and reporting that turn a set of test cases into a number. Every firm in the category sells some version of it.

It is **not terminal**, because "evaluation platform" describes the mechanism rather than the contest, and the two businesses that use that mechanism are almost unrelated. A firm contracted privately by a frontier lab is competing to find capability or risk the lab's own evaluation team missed, on unreleased models, under confidentiality, where the finding is the deliverable and a single non-obvious one justifies the engagement. A platform sold to a product team building on top of models is competing to tell them whether last Tuesday's prompt change made their application better on their own task, inside a build pipeline, at a price that fits a tooling budget. The buyers number in the handful and the thousands respectively, the cadence is quarterly against per-commit, the alternatives are an internal research team against a spreadsheet of test prompts, and a good result means something different in each. Filter Notes in the overview records the two rejected alternatives; the sub-niches below are the split.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Harness frameworks, task runners, tracing, grader integrations, regression reporting and dashboards. The gaps are specific to each side and are stated in the sub-niches.
