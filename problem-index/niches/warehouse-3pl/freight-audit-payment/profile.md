# Freight Audit & Payment

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/warehouse-3pl|Warehousing & 3PL]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Receive, audit, code and pay freight invoices on behalf of shippers and 3PLs across every mode, and produce the transportation spend reporting the client's finance and supply chain functions run on.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 audit analysts, data engineers, and business intelligence staff
**Output:** Invoice audit and payment, general ledger coding and allocation, spend and lane analytics, carrier performance reporting, accrual estimation
**Proprietary data:** Invoice-level freight spend across many shippers, modes and carriers — the most complete picture of what transportation actually costs that exists outside the carriers themselves
**Clock:** Payment terms and month-end close
**Buyer:** VP of Analytics / Chief Product Officer
