# Cargo & Warehouse Liability Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/warehouse-3pl|Warehousing & 3PL]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Underwrite the cargo, warehouse legal liability and contingent coverages 3PLs carry, and adjudicate the loss, damage and theft claims that arise from goods in someone else's custody.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 underwriters, actuaries, claims adjusters, and risk engineers
**Output:** Programme pricing and eligibility, claims adjudication and subrogation, cargo theft pattern analysis, facility risk assessment
**Proprietary data:** Loss and theft claims joined to commodity, facility, lane and security posture — the closest thing to a cargo crime dataset that exists
**Clock:** Annual renewals and claim reporting windows
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
