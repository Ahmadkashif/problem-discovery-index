# Parcel & Freight Audit and Contract Negotiation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/warehouse-3pl|Warehousing & 3PL]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Audit carrier invoices line by line against contract terms, recover overcharges and service failures, and use the resulting cross-client rate visibility to renegotiate agreements — the layer that knows what shipping actually costs.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 audit analysts, data engineers, and negotiation consultants
**Output:** Invoice audit and recovery, accessorial analysis, contract benchmarking, negotiation modelling, spend reporting
**Proprietary data:** Invoice-level shipping costs across many shippers and carriers — the only comparative view of discount structures in a market where every agreement is confidential
**Clock:** Invoice payment terms and refund claim windows
**Buyer:** Chief Analytics Officer / VP of Client Solutions
