# Warehouse & Supply Chain Software Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/warehouse-3pl|Warehousing & 3PL]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The data organisations inside the warehouse management, transportation management and supply chain execution platforms, running slotting, labour, wave planning and forecasting logic over the operations of a large share of the industry.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-1,500 data scientists, industrial engineers, and product researchers
**Output:** Slotting and picking optimisation, labour standards and planning, wave and order release logic, inventory positioning, benchmark publications
**Proprietary data:** Task-level execution records — every pick, putaway, cycle count and travel path — across thousands of facilities
**Clock:** Daily wave cycles and peak season
**Buyer:** VP of Data Science / Chief Product Officer
