# Multi-Tier Supply Chain Risk Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/warehouse-3pl|Warehousing & 3PL]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Map supply chains beyond the first tier and monitor the disruption risk running through them — supplier failure, facility events, geographic concentration, and the port and inland bottlenecks a 3PL absorbs.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 analysts, data scientists, and risk researchers
**Output:** Supplier and facility mapping, disruption alerting, concentration and single-point-of-failure analysis, event impact assessment
**Proprietary data:** Assembled multi-tier relationship graphs built from filings, shipping records and direct research
**Clock:** Continuous monitoring against live disruption events
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / Head of Risk Analytics
