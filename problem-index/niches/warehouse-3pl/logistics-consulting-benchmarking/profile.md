# Logistics Network Consulting & Benchmarking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/warehouse-3pl|Warehousing & 3PL]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Design distribution networks and run the tenders that award 3PL contracts — modelling where facilities should sit, what they should cost, and which provider should win, on the analysis a shipper cannot perform in-house.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 network modellers, industrial engineers, and sourcing consultants
**Output:** Network optimisation studies, facility siting and sizing, 3PL tender design and evaluation, cost-to-serve modelling, operational benchmarking
**Proprietary data:** Cost and productivity benchmarks assembled across many client engagements and provider bids — the only comparative view of what warehousing actually costs to operate
**Clock:** Tender timelines and contract expiry dates
**Buyer:** Partner / Practice Leader
