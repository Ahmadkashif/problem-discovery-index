# Engineered Labour Standards & Productivity Consulting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/warehouse-3pl|Warehousing & 3PL]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Establish how long each warehouse task should take — measured, timed, adjusted for conditions — and build the standards that pay, staff and price warehouse labour, which is the dominant cost in a 3PL contract.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 industrial engineers, time study analysts, and implementation consultants
**Output:** Engineered labour standards, incentive pay design, staffing models, productivity reporting, cost-to-serve rates
**Proprietary data:** Time study libraries across facility types, equipment and task variants, accumulated over decades
**Clock:** Contract pricing and peak staffing cycles
**Buyer:** Practice Leader / Director of Industrial Engineering
