**Size:** 100-500 analysts, industrial engineers, and corporate development staff
**Output:** Contract margin and cost-to-serve analytics, facility performance reporting, labour and capacity planning, bid pricing support, acquisition screening
**Proprietary data:** Cost, productivity and margin outcomes across many facilities and contract structures, joined to what was bid
**Clock:** Quarterly reporting, contract renewals, and peak season
**Buyer:** VP of Analytics
