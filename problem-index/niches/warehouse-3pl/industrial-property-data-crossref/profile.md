# Industrial Property Data & Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/warehouse-3pl|Warehousing & 3PL]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Track industrial and warehouse property — inventory, availability, asking and effective rents, absorption and construction pipeline — and sell the benchmarks 3PLs sign leases against and investors underwrite on.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-2,000 researchers, data collectors, economists, and analysts
**Output:** Availability and rent benchmarks by submarket, absorption and supply pipeline tracking, comparable lease analytics, market forecasts
**Proprietary data:** Property-level research assembled by direct survey over decades, in a market with no public transaction record
**Clock:** Quarterly market reporting against leasing and underwriting cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / Chief Economist
