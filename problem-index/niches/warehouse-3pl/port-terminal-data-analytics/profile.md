# Port, Terminal & Container Flow Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/warehouse-3pl|Warehousing & 3PL]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Track vessel movements, container flows, terminal dwell and inland transfer, and sell the visibility products shippers and 3PLs plan inbound receiving against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 data engineers, maritime analysts, and data scientists
**Output:** Vessel and container tracking, arrival prediction, terminal congestion and dwell analytics, trade flow reporting
**Proprietary data:** Vessel position feeds, carrier and terminal data integrations, and customs filings assembled into a continuous flow picture
**Clock:** Vessel schedules and receiving appointments
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Chief Product Officer
