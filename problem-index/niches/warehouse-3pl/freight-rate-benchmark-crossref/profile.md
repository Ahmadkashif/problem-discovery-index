# Freight Rate Benchmark Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/warehouse-3pl|Warehousing & 3PL]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Publish the lane-level rate benchmarks transportation contracts are priced and audited against — the reference a 3PL negotiates its own buy and sell rates from.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 economists, data scientists, and market analysts
**Output:** Lane rate benchmarks and indices, rate forecasts, market condition reporting, contract versus spot analysis
**Proprietary data:** Transactional rate data contributed by shippers, brokers and carriers in a market with no exchange
**Clock:** Weekly rate publication against bid and procurement cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Analytics
