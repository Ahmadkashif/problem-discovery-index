# Warehouse Automation Vendor Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/warehouse-3pl|Warehousing & 3PL]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Model, sell and tune automated storage, robotics and sortation systems — sizing throughput, simulating configurations, and analysing performance once installed.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 simulation engineers, data scientists, and applications engineers
**Output:** Throughput simulation and system sizing, configuration recommendations, performance monitoring and tuning, return on investment modelling
**Proprietary data:** Simulated designs joined to realised throughput across installed systems — a record of how often the promised numbers were met
**Clock:** Project delivery milestones and peak readiness dates
**Buyer:** VP of Solutions Engineering
