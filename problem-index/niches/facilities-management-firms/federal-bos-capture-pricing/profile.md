# Federal BOS Capture & Pricing Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/facilities-management-firms|Facilities Management Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The capture, pricing and cost-volume teams at the large base-operations contractors (V2X, Amentum, KBR, Fluor and the federal arms of commercial IFM providers) track recompetes years ahead, build price-to-win and cost models for installation contracts, and assemble the past-performance evidence that decides awards. Opportunity tracking itself is bought from intelligence vendors profiled at [[niches/contract-research-labs/federal-opportunity-intelligence/profile|🔍 Federal R&D Opportunity Intelligence]]; the internal pricing analysis mirrors the commercial bid teams at [[niches/cleaning-companies/facility-services-consolidator-analytics/profile|🔍 Facility Services Consolidator Bid Analytics]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–80 capture, pricing and cost analysts per large contractor, excluding proposal writers (estimate)
**Output:** Price-to-win analyses, cost volumes, incumbent and competitor assessments, past-performance volumes
**Proprietary data:** Delivered labour and cost history on installations the firm has run, joined to its own past bids and their outcomes, plus its CPARS record
**Clock:** Solicitation release and proposal due dates set by contracting officers
**Buyer:** VP of Capture / Director of Pricing
