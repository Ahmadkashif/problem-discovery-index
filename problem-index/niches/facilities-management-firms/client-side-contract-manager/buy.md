# Spend Analytics Rebuilt as a Pass-Through Audit

**Niche:** [[niches/facilities-management-firms/client-side-contract-manager/profile|The Client-Side IFM Contract Manager]]
**Industry:** [[industries/facilities-management-firms|Facilities Management Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Procurement teams already classify and audit company spend; the facilities pass-through arrives as one line on a provider invoice and escapes it.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #k-means-clustering #large-language-models #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give the occupier an independent read of whether its IFM contract is delivering what it was priced to deliver, and whoever can reconcile the provider's own work orders and pass-through invoices to the SLA schedule without the provider's help takes the account.

## The Problem
Under an IFM contract, subcontracted work reaches the client as pass-through cost plus markup on the provider's invoice. The client's procurement tools see one supplier and one invoice; the hundreds of subcontractors and thousands of jobs underneath are invisible.

## What Already Exists
Spend analytics and recovery-audit tools (Sievo, Coupa spend analysis and recovery-audit firms) classify spend by category and supplier, detect duplicates and price variance, and recover overpayments.

## The Customization Gap
The IFM version needs: access to the provider's subcontractor invoices and work orders behind each pass-through line; classification by trade, asset and work type; tests against the contract's markup caps, approval thresholds and rate cards; detection of work billed as pass-through that the fixed price already covered; and benchmarks of job cost by trade and region drawn from the client's own history and, with consent, from peers.

## Target Customer
Client-side FM contract managers and procurement category managers for facilities.

## Impact If Solved
The largest and least visible part of the facilities spend becomes auditable with tools procurement already understands.
