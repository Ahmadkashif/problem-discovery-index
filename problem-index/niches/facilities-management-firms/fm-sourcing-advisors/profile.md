# FM Outsourcing & Sourcing Advisors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/facilities-management-firms|Facilities Management Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Independent FM consultancies (Facility Engineering Associates is an example) and the facilities practices of procurement advisory firms help occupiers decide whether to outsource, write the IFM specification and SLA schedule, run the RFP, benchmark the bids and negotiate the contract. Their analysis of what an IFM portfolio should cost is what the client takes to the table. The janitorial-only version is a gate failure at [[niches/cleaning-companies/cleaning-bid-spec-consultancies/profile|🔍 Janitorial Bid & Specification Consultancies]]; the IT equivalent qualified at [[niches/cloud-infrastructure-consultants/it-sourcing-price-benchmark-advisors/profile|🔍 IT Sourcing & Price Benchmark Advisors]] (52).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–50 sourcing advisors, benchmark analysts and FM engineers per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Outsourcing business cases, specifications and SLA schedules, bid evaluations and price benchmarks
**Proprietary data:** Bid pricing and contract terms from the IFM procurements the firm has run, anonymised for benchmarking
**Clock:** Contract expiry and renewal dates
**Buyer:** Managing Director, Workplace & FM Advisory
