# Institutional Facility Condition Assessment Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/facilities-management-firms|Facilities Management Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Engineering and capital-planning consultancies walk universities, school districts, hospitals, municipalities and federal campuses on three- to five-year cycles, rating every building system's condition, estimating remaining life and replacement cost, and producing the facility condition index and capital renewal plan that the owner's FM team and board budget against. Bureau Veritas, ISES and Gordian's assessment services are examples; defence installations run their own condition assessments under the Sustainment Management System, which GAO found years behind schedule and inconsistently applied. The work sits upstream of every FM contract's capital and deferred-maintenance scope.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~30–300 assessors, cost analysts and capital planners per national firm (estimate; assessors count only where they also analyse, not as field inspectors)
**Output:** Condition assessment databases, facility condition indexes, capital renewal forecasts and funding scenarios
**Proprietary data:** Component-level condition, age and cost observations across hundreds of campuses, with repeat assessments that could test past remaining-life calls
**Clock:** Owners' capital budget cycles, bond programmes and board calendars, which are client schedules
**Buyer:** Practice Leader, Asset Management / Facility Assessment Services

**Kill switches:** data isn't theirs — institutional owners increasingly require assessment data to be collected in the owner's own capital-planning software and refuse proprietary systems (a Penn State reassessment RFP says so explicitly), so the condition corpus sits in each client's system rather than the assessor's. This is what separates the pocket from lender property condition assessments, where the assessor keeps its own archive across lenders: see [[niches/home-inspection/commercial-property-condition-assessment/profile|🔍 Commercial Property Condition Assessment Firms]] (51) and [[niches/commercial-real-estate/property-condition-assessment-firms/profile|🔍 Property Condition Assessment Firms]] (45). It is consistent with [[niches/municipal-services/infrastructure-asset-management-consulting/profile|🔍 Infrastructure Asset Management Consulting]], where municipal ownership of asset data was the issue.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch
