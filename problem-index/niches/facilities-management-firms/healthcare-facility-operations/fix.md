# Interim Life Safety Measures Nobody Wrote Down

**Niche:** [[niches/facilities-management-firms/healthcare-facility-operations/profile|Healthcare Facility Operations]]
**Industry:** [[industries/facilities-management-firms|Facilities Management Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A renovation impairs a fire barrier or an exit route for three weeks, the right precautions are taken on the floor, and the assessment that justified them is missing when the surveyor asks.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #compliance #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a hospital survey-ready on any given day, with every life-safety test, inspection and deficiency documented the way a surveyor reads it, and whoever walks out of an unannounced survey with no Environment of Care or Life Safety findings takes the account.

## The Problem
Hospitals renovate constantly while occupied. Each project that compromises life-safety features requires an assessment of whether interim life safety measures apply, which measures (extra fire watches, temporary barriers, additional drills), and documentation that they were carried out. The work is coordinated between the FM team, contractors and infection prevention, and the paperwork falls between them.

## Why It's Still Broken
No single party owns the record. Contractors document their own work, infection prevention documents its risk assessment and the facilities team documents PMs. The ILSM assessment is a form filled in at project start, if at all, and never updated as the project changes.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Tie the ILSM assessment to each project record in the CMMS. Prompt it whenever a work order or permit touches a barrier or egress element, capture the measures and their daily checks on the phones of whoever performs them, and close it automatically when the impairment ends. Make it searchable by area for the surveyor's tracer.

## Who Feels the Pain
Hospital facilities directors and their FM providers, project managers on occupied-hospital renovations, and the patients whose safety the measures exist to protect.

## Impact If Fixed
One of the most predictable documentation findings becomes a by-product of the work rather than a reconstruction.
