# Healthcare Facility Operations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/facilities-management-firms|Facilities Management Firms]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a hospital survey-ready on any given day, with every life-safety test, inspection and deficiency documented the way a surveyor reads it, and whoever walks out of an unannounced survey with no Environment of Care or Life Safety findings takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$6B a year of outsourced hospital and health-system plant operations and facility management (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of US integrated facility services revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — work orders are digital, but survey evidence lives in binders, shared drives and the plant director's memory
**Target Buyer:** Hospital VP of facilities or support services; regional directors at healthcare FM providers
**Automation Potential:** Medium — evidence assembly and gap detection are automatable; the testing and judgement are hands-on

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Hospitals outsource plant operations, clinical engineering and environmental services to providers such as Medxcel, Crothall, Sodexo, HHS and ABM's healthcare business, or run them in-house with the same obligations. The building is regulated as part of patient safety: Joint Commission (or DNV, or ACHC) surveys under CMS deemed status arrive unannounced, and Life Safety and Environment of Care standards made up five of the ten most-cited hospital standards in 2018, the fire-extinguishing-system standard non-compliant in 88.9% of surveys. Utility systems, medical gas, generators, fire barriers, interim life safety measures during construction and infection control risk assessments all require documented testing on fixed frequencies. A finding can put accreditation and Medicare billing at risk, so the contest is daily readiness, not cost.

## Current Tools & Gaps
CMMS for PMs and work orders, life-safety drawings, fire-protection vendors' inspection reports, construction ILSM checklists and survey-readiness binders. The gaps: no live view of which elements of performance are evidenced and which are not; deficiencies from vendor reports tracked by hand; and ILSM decisions during renovations recorded inconsistently.
