# Clinical Accreditation Software Pointed at the Building

**Niche:** [[niches/facilities-management-firms/healthcare-facility-operations/profile|Healthcare Facility Operations]]
**Industry:** [[industries/facilities-management-firms|Facilities Management Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Hospitals already buy accreditation-readiness software for clinical standards; the Environment of Care and Life Safety chapters run on different evidence that those tools do not ingest.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #data-integration #compliance #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a hospital survey-ready on any given day, with every life-safety test, inspection and deficiency documented the way a surveyor reads it, and whoever walks out of an unannounced survey with no Environment of Care or Life Safety findings takes the account.

## The Problem
Quality departments run tracer audits, policy management and standards crosswalks in accreditation software. Facilities teams sit outside it, preparing their chapters in binders.

## What Already Exists
Accreditation and quality management platforms used by hospital quality departments: standards libraries, tracer and mock-survey tools, policy management and corrective action tracking.

## The Customization Gap
The building chapters need: evidence from the CMMS, BAS logs, generator load tests and fire-protection vendor reports rather than from clinical records; NFPA frequency logic referenced by the standards; deficiency-to-repair tracking with the allowed correction windows; interim life safety measure and infection-control risk assessments tied to active construction projects; and drawings of fire and smoke barriers linked to the penetrations found in them.

## Target Customer
Hospital quality and facilities leaders who want one readiness view, and healthcare FM providers who report into the hospital's quality structure.

## Impact If Solved
Facilities readiness moves inside the hospital's existing accreditation programme, visible to the people accountable for the survey outcome.
