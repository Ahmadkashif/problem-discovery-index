# Technical Facility Services Rollup Corporate Development

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/facilities-management-firms|Facilities Management Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Private-equity-backed platforms and the large providers buy regional mechanical, electrical, fire-protection and facility-services firms to build density (CBRE's late-2025 acquisition of Pearce Services is one example). Small corporate development teams screen targets, model synergies and run integrations.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 3–10 per platform (estimate)
**Output:** Target screens, acquisition models, integration plans
**Proprietary data:** Diligence files on targets reviewed
**Clock:** Deal timetables
**Buyer:** Head of corporate development
