# FM Software & Outsourcing Analyst Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/facilities-management-firms|Facilities Management Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Research and advisory firms such as Verdantix and Everest Group publish market sizing, vendor assessments and buyer surveys on IWMS, CMMS, smart-building software and facility-services outsourcing. Software vendors and IFM providers pay for subscriptions, inclusion briefings and the right to cite a favourable rating in sales; occupiers buy the reports when choosing a platform or provider.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 analysts covering real estate, facilities and smart buildings per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Vendor and provider assessments, market forecasts, buyer surveys, advisory calls
**Proprietary data:** Survey panels, vendor briefing notes and years of published assessments; most inputs come from the vendors themselves
**Clock:** Self-set publication calendars
**Buyer:** Research Director for real estate and facilities
