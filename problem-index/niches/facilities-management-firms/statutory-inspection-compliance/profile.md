# Statutory Inspection & Life-Safety Compliance

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/facilities-management-firms|Facilities Management Firms]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to know, site by site, which code-required inspections are due, done and passed, and whoever closes each inspector's deficiency into a completed repair before the fire marshal, the insurer or the client's auditor asks takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$5B a year of code-required inspection, testing and deficiency-repair spend run through US FM accounts (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of US integrated facility services revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — inspectors use apps, but what reaches the FM firm is a PDF per visit, tracked in a spreadsheet
**Target Buyer:** Compliance or EHS managers and regional directors at FM providers; client-side risk managers
**Automation Potential:** High for scheduling, extraction and tracking; the inspections themselves are hands-on

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Outside hospitals, an FM firm governing a multi-site corporate, retail, education or industrial portfolio carries every building's statutory calendar: sprinkler and standpipe (NFPA 25), fire alarm (NFPA 72), fire doors (NFPA 80), emergency generators (NFPA 110), emergency lighting, elevators, boilers and pressure vessels, backflow and kitchen hoods. Since 1 January 2026 refrigeration and comfort-cooling equipment holding 15 lb or more of HFC has also carried EPA leak-repair obligations. Most inspections are subcontracted to specialist firms, each reporting in its own format, and frequencies vary by adopted code edition and jurisdiction. The contest is not doing the inspections; it is the governance loop around them.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Inspection software used by fire-protection contractors (Inspect Point, BuildingReports), checklist apps such as SafetyCulture, CMMS recurring PMs and AHJ third-party reporting portals in some cities. The gaps: no portfolio frequency library by jurisdiction, deficiencies left in PDFs, and repair quotes stalled awaiting client approval with no clock on them.
