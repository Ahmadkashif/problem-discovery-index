# Impairments Left Open Because Nobody Owns the Quote

**Niche:** [[niches/facilities-management-firms/statutory-inspection-compliance/profile|Statutory Inspection & Life-Safety Compliance]]
**Industry:** [[industries/facilities-management-firms|Facilities Management Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** An inspector finds a deficiency, a vendor quotes the repair, and the quote waits weeks for a client approval nobody is chasing.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #compliance #descriptive-statistics #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to know, site by site, which code-required inspections are due, done and passed, and whoever closes each inspector's deficiency into a completed repair before the fire marshal, the insurer or the client's auditor asks takes the account.

## The Problem
Most deficiency repairs are above the FM contract's included threshold, so they need a quote and client approval. The quote goes by email to a client contact who may not understand it. Meanwhile the deficiency stays open, sometimes an impaired system, and the FM firm carries the risk of a loss or citation for a repair it was not authorised to make.

## Why It's Still Broken
Approval sits between the FM firm, the vendor and the client, and no party's system tracks it end to end. Quotes are PDFs, approvals are emails, and nothing escalates by severity.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Every deficiency carries its severity class and an allowed time to repair; the quote request, the quote, the approval and the completed repair are tracked against that clock, with automatic escalation to the client's risk manager when an impairment ages. A monthly report shows open deficiencies by age and by who is holding them up.

## Who Feels the Pain
Compliance coordinators, account facility managers who own the risk, and clients who discover after a loss that the repair was quoted months earlier.

## Impact If Fixed
Impairments close in days, not weeks, and the record shows who decided what and when.
