# Fire-Protection Inspection Software Turned Toward the Portfolio

**Niche:** [[niches/facilities-management-firms/statutory-inspection-compliance/profile|Statutory Inspection & Life-Safety Compliance]]
**Industry:** [[industries/facilities-management-firms|Facilities Management Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Fire-protection contractors already run structured digital inspections; the FM firm that hires them receives a PDF.
**Tags:** #data-integration #descriptive-statistics #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to know, site by site, which code-required inspections are due, done and passed, and whoever closes each inspector's deficiency into a completed repair before the fire marshal, the insurer or the client's auditor asks takes the account.

## The Problem
The inspection data is structured at source, with device-level results, NFPA references and photos, then flattened into a report for the customer. The FM firm governs many such vendors and receives the flattened version from all of them.

## What Already Exists
Inspection and service platforms for fire and life-safety contractors (Inspect Point, BuildingReports and similar) that schedule inspections, record device-level results against NFPA forms and generate customer reports and AHJ submissions.

## The Customization Gap
The FM-side version needs: a customer portal that aggregates results across all inspection vendors and trades, not one contractor's jobs; data feeds instead of PDFs; a frequency library by site, system and jurisdiction independent of any vendor's schedule; deficiency-to-work-order handoff into the FM firm's CMMS, including quotes and client approvals; and coverage of non-fire statutory items (elevators, boilers, backflow, refrigerant leak inspections) that the fire tools do not handle.

## Target Customer
FM providers and large in-house facilities teams that require their inspection vendors to report into one platform.

## Impact If Solved
Vendor data arrives structured, the portfolio view assembles itself, and the FM firm can switch inspection vendors without losing the history.
