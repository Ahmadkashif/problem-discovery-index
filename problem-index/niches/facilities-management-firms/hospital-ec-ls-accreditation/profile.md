# Hospital Life Safety & Environment-of-Care Accreditation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/facilities-management-firms|Facilities Management Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Joint Commission, DNV and ACHC accredit hospitals under CMS deemed status, and their Life Safety and Environment of Care surveys judge the work hospital FM teams and providers do: fire protection, utility systems, medical gas, egress, barriers and interim life safety measures. Those chapters supplied five of the ten most-cited hospital standards in 2018. This pocket is the building-specific slice of an organisation family already profiled for clinical lines at [[niches/home-health-agencies/home-health-accreditation-organizations/profile|🔍 Home Health Accreditation Organizations]] (48) and [[niches/behavioral-health-clinics/behavioral-accreditation-bodies/profile|🔍 Behavioral Health Accreditation Bodies]] (44).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 standards, interpretation and data staff on the building chapters per accreditor (estimate); the life safety code surveyors are examiners and are excluded
**Output:** Standards interpretations for the Life Safety and Environment of Care chapters; aggregate citation data
**Proprietary data:** Building-level survey findings across thousands of hospitals over repeated cycles
**Clock:** CMS-linked survey cycles, roughly every 36 months (estimate), and standards revision dates
**Buyer:** Director of Engineering at an accreditor
