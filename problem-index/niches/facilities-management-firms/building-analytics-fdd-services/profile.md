# Building Analytics & Fault-Detection Service Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/facilities-management-firms|Facilities Management Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Fault detection and diagnostics providers (SkySpark-based integrators, Clockworks Analytics, CopperTree, Cimetrics and the controls manufacturers' remote operations centres) connect to a building's automation system, run rules and models over its trend data, and send the FM team a prioritised list of faults with estimated energy and comfort impact. Many sell it as a managed service in which their engineers review findings monthly with the site team. Equipment-level telemetry from manufacturers is profiled at [[niches/hvac-contractors/oem-connected-equipment-data/profile|🔍 HVAC OEM Connected Equipment Data]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–60 analytics engineers and building-performance analysts per provider (estimate)
**Output:** Monthly fault reports, prioritised findings and savings verification for each building
**Proprietary data:** Fault histories and fixes across thousands of buildings' trend data
**Clock:** None external; monthly service cycles, with building performance standards as an indirect driver
**Buyer:** Head of Analytics Services / Chief Technology Officer
