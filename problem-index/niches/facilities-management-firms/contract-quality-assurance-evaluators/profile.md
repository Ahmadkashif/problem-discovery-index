# Federal Contract Quality Assurance Evaluators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/facilities-management-firms|Facilities Management Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Government quality assurance evaluators and contracting officer's representatives inspect base-operations and facility contractors' work against the surveillance plan, record discrepancies and feed the evaluations that become CPARS ratings.

## Insight Function
**Size:** One to a handful per contract (estimate)
**Output:** Surveillance checklists, discrepancy reports, inputs to CPARS
**Proprietary data:** Sampled inspection records per contract
**Clock:** Monthly surveillance and annual evaluations
**Buyer:** Contracting officer
