# Equipment Breakdown Insurers & Jurisdictional Inspection

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/facilities-management-firms|Facilities Management Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Equipment breakdown insurers, with HSB the best-known specialist, insure boilers, chillers, electrical gear and production machinery against sudden failure. Many also act as authorised inspectors performing the jurisdictional boiler and pressure-vessel inspections that state laws require before an operating certificate is renewed. Their engineering and actuarial teams study why building equipment fails in order to price the cover and set the maintenance recommendations FM teams then carry out. Wider commercial risk engineering is profiled as a niche at [[niches/property-casualty-insurers/commercial-risk-engineering-loss-control/profile|🟠 Commercial Risk Engineering & Loss Control]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~30–200 engineers, actuaries and data scientists per specialist carrier, not counting field inspectors (estimate)
**Output:** Equipment breakdown pricing, loss-prevention recommendations, engineering guidance and failure studies
**Proprietary data:** Claims and inspection records on millions of insured objects, joined to the equipment's age, type and maintenance findings
**Clock:** Policy renewals, a contract date; the state certificate-inspection dates bind the field inspectors and the owners, not this team
**Buyer:** Chief Engineering Officer / Head of Underwriting Analytics
