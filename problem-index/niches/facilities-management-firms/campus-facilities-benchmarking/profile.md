# Campus Facilities Benchmarking Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/facilities-management-firms|Facilities Management Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Gordian's Sightlines business benchmarks colleges' facilities spending, staffing, space and capital reinvestment against peer campuses, and publishes the annual State of Facilities in Higher Education report. Its 13th edition put deferred capital renewal at $156 per gross square foot in 2025, up 8%, with spending on existing buildings at 73.5% of need. Member institutions pay for an annual benchmarking analysis and presentation to their leadership, and the facilities teams and their FM providers use it to argue for budget.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–80 analysts and client-facing benchmarking staff (estimate)
**Output:** Annual campus benchmarking reports and board presentations; the published state-of-facilities report
**Proprietary data:** Member-contributed facilities spending, staffing, condition and space data across hundreds of campuses and tens of thousands of buildings (an earlier edition cited 52,000+ facilities on 325 campuses), contributed for benchmarking use
**Clock:** Institutional fiscal years and board budget cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Benchmarking Services
