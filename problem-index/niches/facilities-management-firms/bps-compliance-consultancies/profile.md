# Building Performance Standard Compliance Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/facilities-management-firms|Facilities Management Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Energy engineering and building-performance consultancies prepare the annual benchmarking and emissions filings that building performance standards require, have them certified by a registered design professional, and sell the compliance-path plan that tells an owner which retrofits will keep the building under its limits as they tighten. New York's Local Law 97 is the largest: most buildings over 25,000 sq ft, a first report on calendar-2024 emissions due 1 May 2025, and a $268 penalty per metric ton over the limit. Around 16 US jurisdictions had adopted a standard by one count. Building owners pay, directly or through the IFM provider that runs the building. Independent firms (Bright Power and Steven Winter Associates are examples) compete with engineering firms' energy practices, the IFM providers' own sustainability services and ESCOs. These are largely the same firms that perform the ten-yearly energy audits profiled at [[niches/energy-auditors/commercial-ashrae-auditors/profile|🔵 Commercial ASHRAE Auditors]]; this pocket scores only the annual filing and the compliance-path forecast attached to it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~15–80 energy engineers, analysts and registered design professionals per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Certified annual compliance filings, and compliance-path plans with projected emissions by year and by measure
**Proprietary data:** The firm's own compliance-path projections for each building in its book, joined year after year to what the building later filed. Both sides of the record are largely open to rivals: the city publishes Local Law 87 audit results, including energy conservation measure detail, for 2012–2024, and Local Law 84 benchmarking outcomes are public.
**Clock:** The annual filing date (1 May in New York), which DOB extended to 31 December 2025 for the first report on request and a filing fee; the compliance-path forecast itself runs on the owner's capital-planning schedule
**Buyer:** Principal / Director of Building Performance Services
