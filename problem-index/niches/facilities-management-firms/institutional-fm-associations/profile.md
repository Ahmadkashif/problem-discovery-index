# Institutional Facilities Association Benchmarking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/facilities-management-firms|Facilities Management Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
APPA, the association of higher-education facilities officers, runs its Facilities Performance Indicators survey of campus facilities costs and staffing; ASHE, the healthcare engineering society, publishes operations and maintenance benchmarks for hospital plant teams. Members contribute data and receive the comparisons. The commercial-building equivalents, BOMA's Experience Exchange Report and IFMA's O&M benchmarks, are already profiled at [[niches/cleaning-companies/boma-operating-cost-benchmarks/profile|🔍 Building Operating Cost Benchmark Programmes]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically under 10 staff on benchmarking per association (estimate)
**Output:** Annual benchmark surveys and member reports
**Proprietary data:** Member-contributed cost and staffing data
**Clock:** Annual survey cycle
**Buyer:** Executive director or research lead
