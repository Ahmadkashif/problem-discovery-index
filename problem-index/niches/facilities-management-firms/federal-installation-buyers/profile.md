# Military Installation & Federal Facility Buyers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/facilities-management-firms|Facilities Management Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The commands and agencies that buy base operations and facility support (NAVFAC, Army installation management, the Air Force civil engineer centre, GSA's Public Buildings Service) run condition assessments, set maintenance priorities, write performance work statements and surveillance plans, and rate contractors in CPARS. GAO estimated a $285B DOD maintenance backlog for FY2025, found the services funding about 80% of maintenance needs against a 90% goal, and found that DOD does not consistently track work orders. Its 13 recommendations are aimed at these organisations.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–100 asset managers, condition-assessment analysts and facility planners per command (estimate); contract specialists, who administer awards, are excluded
**Output:** Condition data, maintenance and restoration priorities, budget requests, contract requirements and performance evaluations
**Proprietary data:** Condition assessments and work-order records across the federal estate, which GAO judged generally unreliable for measuring maintenance effectiveness
**Clock:** Federal budget cycle and congressionally mandated reporting
**Buyer:** Director of Asset Management at a command
