# Construction Estimating Rebuilt for Recurring Service

**Niche:** [[niches/facilities-management-firms/ifm-bid-solutioning/profile|IFM Bid Solutioning & Baseline Pricing]]
**Industry:** [[industries/facilities-management-firms|Facilities Management Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Contractors estimate one-off construction jobs with mature software and cost databases; IFM bid teams price five years of recurring work in workbooks.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #linear-regression #monte-carlo-methods #feature-engineering #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to price a five-year, multi-site contract from an asset list and spend history it knows to be wrong, and whoever comes nearest the cost the portfolio actually turns out to have, without pricing itself off the shortlist, takes the account.

## The Problem
An IFM price combines PM task hours per asset, reactive allowances, subcontract quotes, wages under local labour rules, materials, management overhead and risk, across many sites and years. Bid teams rebuild that structure for each opportunity in spreadsheets, and the logic varies by who built it.

## What Already Exists
Construction estimating platforms (Sage Estimating, ProEst, Trimble and similar), backed by unit-cost databases such as RSMeans, handle quantity take-off, assemblies, crew rates, location factors and bid-day adjustments for one-off projects.

## The Customization Gap
Recurring service needs: assemblies defined as PM task sets per asset class with frequencies; reactive allowances as distributions rather than quantities; multi-year escalation with wage-determination and union-rate steps; self-delivery versus subcontract options per service line; site sampling with extrapolation to unvisited buildings; and a transition reconciliation that compares the verified asset list with the priced one and feeds the gap back into the next estimate.

## Target Customer
Solution-design and pricing teams at IFM providers, and at mid-size regional FM firms bidding against them.

## Impact If Solved
Consistent, auditable bids built in days instead of weeks, and a pricing method that improves with every transition rather than resetting per opportunity.
