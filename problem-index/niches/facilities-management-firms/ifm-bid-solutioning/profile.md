# IFM Bid Solutioning & Baseline Pricing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/facilities-management-firms|Facilities Management Firms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to price a five-year, multi-site contract from an asset list and spend history it knows to be wrong, and whoever comes nearest the cost the portfolio actually turns out to have, without pricing itself off the shortlist, takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$500M a year of solution-design, pricing, site-survey and transition-planning effort on US IFM bids (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1% of US integrated facility services revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — bid models are workbooks built per opportunity from the provider's rate cards and the RFP's data
**Target Buyer:** VP of Solutions / Head of Pricing at IFM providers
**Automation Potential:** Medium-High for baseline estimation; the commercial judgement stays human

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A bid team has weeks to price a portfolio it has barely seen. The inputs are the client's asset list (often years out of date), the incumbent's spend where the client shares it, site walks of a sample of buildings, and the provider's own productivity standards. The price is then fixed for years. Winning on an underestimate loses money for five years; losing on an overestimate loses the account. The cost is revealed only in transition, when the provider verifies assets and sees real work-order volume. The winner is whoever estimates closest to that number before it is visible.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Excel pricing models, PM task libraries, the provider's labour and subcontract rate cards, and published unit-cost references from the publishers profiled at [[niches/cleaning-companies/facility-cost-data-publishers/profile|🔍 Facility & Construction Cost Data Publishers]]. The gap is the provider's own history: delivered cost on the accounts it has already run is never joined back to the bid-time data it had for them.
