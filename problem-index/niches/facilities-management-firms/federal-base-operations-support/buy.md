# Commercial SLA Reporting Rebuilt for the QASP

**Niche:** [[niches/facilities-management-firms/federal-base-operations-support/profile|Federal Base Operations Support Contracts]]
**Industry:** [[industries/facilities-management-firms|Facilities Management Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Commercial IFM providers already report SLA performance monthly to corporate clients; federal contracts rate the same work on a different structure with a statutory response window.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #compliance #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to carry an Exceptional or Very Good CPARS record from the option years into the recompete, and whoever documents performance against the surveillance plan well enough that the government's evaluation cannot drift to Satisfactory takes the account.

## The Problem
BOS contractors that also run commercial accounts own KPI reporting platforms built for monthly client reviews. On federal work the same data has to answer a different question: whether each performance standard met its acceptable quality level under the government's surveillance method, and what the contractor says in response to an evaluation.

## What Already Exists
IFM providers' commercial KPI dashboards and SLA reporting built on their CMMS, plus business intelligence tools.

## The Customization Gap
Federal adaptation needs: KPIs defined from the PWS and QASP rather than from a commercial SLA schedule; the government's sampling method reproduced, so the contractor sees what the evaluator will see; corrective action requests tracked to closure with root cause; CPARS rating elements (quality, schedule, cost control, management, small business, regulatory compliance) as the reporting frame; a 14-day response workflow; and controls on controlled unclassified information.

## Target Customer
Federal business units of commercial IFM providers, and mid-size BOS contractors moving from small-business set-asides to full-and-open competition.

## Impact If Solved
Federal programs reuse reporting the provider already owns, adapted to the rating structure that decides recompetes, rather than building it by hand per contract.
