# Commercial CMMS & Work-Order Network Data Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/facilities-management-firms|Facilities Management Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The data and analytics groups at the commercial facilities-maintenance platforms (ServiceChannel, Corrigo, Eptura, FM:Systems, Planon, Facilio) see work orders, invoices and contractor performance across many clients and sites. ServiceChannel alone is described as connecting roughly 70,000 providers across 330,000+ locations. They build in-product benchmarking, contractor scorecards and spend analytics that clients and IFM providers use to manage subcontractors. The platform contest itself is profiled at [[niches/field-service-software/multi-site-facilities-service/profile|🟠 Multi-Site Facilities Service]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–150 data scientists, analysts and product data staff per platform (estimate)
**Output:** In-product benchmarks, contractor performance scores, spend and invoice analytics
**Proprietary data:** Work-order, invoice and contractor-performance records across thousands of clients and hundreds of thousands of locations
**Clock:** None external to the platform; clients' budget cycles at most
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Product Analytics
