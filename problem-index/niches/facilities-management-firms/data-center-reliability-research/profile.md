# Data Centre Reliability Certification & Outage Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/facilities-management-firms|Facilities Management Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Uptime Institute certifies data-centre designs, constructed facilities and operations (its Management & Operations assessment grades the facility-operations teams that FM providers run), and sells research on outages and operating practice. It reports over 4,300 awards in more than 120 countries. Its seventh Annual Outage Analysis (2025) found power the dominant cause, at 54% of impactful outages. Data-centre services were the fastest-growing part of CBRE's facilities management business in late 2025.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–80 consultants, assessors and research analysts (estimate)
**Output:** Tier and operations certifications, outage analyses, member research and surveys
**Proprietary data:** Certification records across thousands of facilities and incident information supplied by members, plus a database of public outages
**Clock:** Clients' commissioning and certification schedules; self-set publication cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Research Officer / Head of Certification
