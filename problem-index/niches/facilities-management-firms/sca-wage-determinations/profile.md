# Service Contract Act Wage Determination Programme

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/facilities-management-firms|Facilities Management Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Department of Labor's Wage and Hour Division issues the Service Contract Act wage determinations that set minimum wages and fringe benefits by occupation and locality on federal service contracts, including every base-operations and federal facility contract. Under FAR 52.222-43 the determination current at each option year applies, and the contractor must notify the contracting officer within 30 days of a new determination to recover the required increase. Wage, hour and classification enforcement more broadly is profiled at [[niches/it-staffing-firms/dol-classification-enforcement/profile|🔍 Wage, Hour & Classification Enforcement]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 wage analysts and economists (estimate)
**Output:** Area wage determinations by occupation and locality, revised periodically
**Proprietary data:** Determinations built largely on published federal wage surveys and collective bargaining agreements
**Clock:** Contract option dates and periodic revision cycles
**Buyer:** Branch chief for service contract wage determinations
