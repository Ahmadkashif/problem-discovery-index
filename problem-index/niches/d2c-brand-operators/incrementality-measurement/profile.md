# Incrementality Measurement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/d2c-brand-operators|D2C Brand Operators]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to establish what a marketing dollar actually caused rather than what each channel claims — and whoever does that takes the account, because every budget decision in the category currently rests on numbers the people making them do not believe.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$6.2B US attributable to the measurement problem
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~22% of category operating spend
**Digital Adoption:** None — everyone knows the numbers are wrong
**Target Buyer:** Every brand allocating marketing budget, and the investors behind them
**Automation Potential:** High — experimentation and modelling are both mechanisable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every channel claims the same conversions, the platform-reported numbers exceed the brand's actual orders, and the analytics platform disagrees with both. The discrepancy is structural rather than a configuration error: the platforms report what they can claim, using modelled conversions and their own attribution windows, and they have every incentive to claim generously. The brand knows what actually happened. Establishing causation between the two — what would have sold anyway, what each channel actually caused, what the marginal dollar does — is the central analytical problem of the industry and the basis for its largest recurring decision. The attribution vendors that emerged to fill the gap mostly read platform claims back with better presentation.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Platform-reported conversions, last-click analytics, multi-touch attribution tools, media mix modelling and server-side conversion APIs. The gaps: no routine experimentation, which is the only method that establishes causation directly; modelled attribution is fitted to platform-claimed outcomes rather than to the brand's own orders; baseline demand is not estimated, so organic sales are attributed to whichever channel touched them; and the marginal return of the next dollar — the actual decision — is not produced by any of it.
