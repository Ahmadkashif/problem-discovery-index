## The Problem
Establishing what an intervention caused, when you cannot observe the counterfactual, is the founding problem of econometrics and of the experimentation discipline. Randomised design, difference-in-differences, synthetic control, regression discontinuity and instrumental variables are all mature, taught and implemented. Geographic experimentation for advertising specifically has a published literature. Direct-to-consumer marketing largely uses last-click attribution, which is a rule about which touch to credit rather than a method for estimating an effect.

## What Already Exists
Randomised controlled experimentation with power analysis; difference-in-differences and synthetic control for market-level interventions; geographic experimentation methodology developed for advertising; Bayesian media mix modelling with priors and uncertainty; and the panel data methods for estimating effects from observational variation.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a small brand with limited spend and impatient decisions. It requires: (1) designs that work at small scale, since the published geographic methods assume national budgets and most brands in this category cannot run a twenty-market experiment — small-scale and sequential designs are the adaptation that makes causal measurement available to the category at all; (2) priors from a cross-brand corpus, since a small brand's own history cannot identify a media mix model and a well-chosen prior from comparable brands can; (3) results that arrive at the cadence of a budget meeting, which means sequential analysis rather than waiting for a fixed horizon; (4) the platforms' modelled conversions treated as a covariate rather than as an outcome, which is the specific methodological correction and is not how any attribution vendor sets it up; and (5) costs stated plainly, since a holdout costs revenue and the case for running it has to be made in those terms rather than as a methodological preference.

## Target Customer
Brands and their growth functions, attribution and modelling vendors, and the applied econometrics community for whom this is a large market using none of its methods.

## Impact If Solved
A century of causal method exists and this industry allocates on last-click. Small-scale sequential designs are what make causal measurement available to brands below national scale, and treating platform-modelled conversions as a covariate rather than an outcome is the correction no vendor makes.
