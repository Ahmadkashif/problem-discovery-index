# The Growth Marketer

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/d2c-brand-operators|D2C Brand Operators]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stop a skilled marketer spending their week assembling a report they do not believe — and whoever does that takes the account, because that week is the largest recurring waste in the function.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.9B US in loaded marketer time
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of category operating spend
**Digital Adoption:** None — a week spent reconciling disbelief
**Target Buyer:** Growth organisations and their leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the assembly is entirely mechanical

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A growth marketer spends a large share of every week reconciling numbers from platforms that disagree, producing a report whose central figures they do not believe, to inform decisions they will make on intuition anyway. The work is pure assembly: export from four platforms, paste into a spreadsheet, apply last week's adjustments, reconcile against the order data, build the chart, write the commentary. It is the most expensive person in the function doing data entry to produce an artefact nobody trusts, and it recurs every week in every brand in the category. Neither the assembly nor the disbelief is addressed by any product in the stack.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Platform dashboards, spreadsheet exports, dashboard tools, and attribution products. The gaps: no automated assembly against the brand's own order data; the same reconciliation is rebuilt weekly rather than once; the discrepancies are re-explained each time rather than tracked; no record of which decisions were made on which numbers; and no measure of how much time the function spends on this.
