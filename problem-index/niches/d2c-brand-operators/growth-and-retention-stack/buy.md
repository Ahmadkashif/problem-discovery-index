## The Problem
Allocating a fixed budget across activities with uncertain, non-linear and time-shifted returns is a capital allocation problem, and finance has spent decades on it: expected return against variance, marginal return equalisation, real options for irreversible commitments, and explicit discounting of cash that arrives later. Marketing budgets in this category are set as a percentage of revenue, split by last year's proportions, and adjusted by whoever argued most persuasively.

## What Already Exists
Portfolio optimisation under uncertainty; marginal return equalisation as an allocation principle; discounted cash flow for time-shifted returns; real options analysis for irreversible commitments; Bayesian decision analysis with explicit priors; and sequential allocation methods including bandit approaches.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to returns that are poorly measured and interdependent. It requires: (1) allocation under measurement uncertainty rather than under outcome uncertainty, since the deeper problem here is that the returns are badly estimated rather than merely random — which argues for allocating partly to reduce uncertainty, a framing the bandit literature supplies and nobody applies; (2) interdependence between the options, since acquisition changes the audience retention operates on and portfolio methods assume separable returns; (3) explicit discounting of lifetime value, because a return arriving over three years and one arriving next month are routinely compared undiscounted in this category; (4) cash constraints as a hard limit, since these brands are frequently cash-constrained and an allocation that is optimal on paper can be fatal; and (5) results at a monthly cadence, since the auction and the audience both move faster than a planning cycle.

## Target Customer
Brand leadership and finance functions, growth teams, and the decision analysis practitioners for whom marketing allocation is an under-served application.

## Impact If Solved
Capital allocation under uncertainty is a mature discipline and marketing uses last year's split. Allocating partly to reduce measurement uncertainty is the framing this category needs, since its deeper problem is estimation rather than randomness.
