# Growth & Retention Stack

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/d2c-brand-operators|D2C Brand Operators]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Not terminal — the contest differs by whether the customer has bought before, and the decomposition is recorded below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$8.4B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~30% of category operating spend
**Digital Adoption:** High
**Target Buyer:** Performance marketers; separately, retention marketers
**Automation Potential:** High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is where the money goes: acquiring customers and getting them to buy again. It is the largest operating line in a direct-to-consumer brand and the function the whole category is organised around.

It is **not terminal**, because the two halves run on opposite economics and are executed by different people. Paid acquisition buys attention in a real-time auction at a price that rises structurally as more brands bid, is measured in cost per new customer, and competes against every other advertiser for the same impression — the constraint is price. Owned retention sends messages to people who already bought at a marginal cost close to zero, is measured in repeat purchase rate and lifetime value, and competes against nothing external — the constraint is audience fatigue and the risk of burning a list that took years to build. A brand's central strategic question is how to split between them, and that is only a question because they are two different contests. Filter Notes in the overview records the two rejected alternatives; the sub-niches below are the split.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Auction platforms with automated bidding, creative testing, email and messaging platforms with segmentation and flows, loyalty programmes and subscription tooling. The gaps are specific to each half and are stated in the sub-niches.
