# The Owner Who Is the Credit Policy

**Niche:** [[niches/sales-finance-companies/small-independent-finance-companies/profile|Small Independent Finance Companies]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sales-finance-companies|Sales Finance Companies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** In a small finance company the owner approves the marginal deals from memory, so the credit policy is unwritten, unexaminable and unsellable.
**Tags:** #tacit-knowledge-ml #decision-trees #large-language-models #compliance #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give a five-to-fifty-person finance company bank-grade decisioning, servicing and collateral reporting at its scale, and whoever lets it pass its warehouse lender's audit without hiring a data team takes the account.

## The Problem
The owner knows which dealers to trust and which borrowers will pay. When the bank asks for the credit policy, there is a three-page document nobody follows; when the owner is away, decisions stop; when the owner wants to sell, the buyer discounts the book.

## Why It's Still Broken
Writing policy down takes time the owner spends deciding deals, and the owner's rules are learned exceptions, not a grid.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Record each decision with a one-line reason, then derive the de facto policy from a year of decisions — the rules the owner actually applies — as a readable document and a simple scorecard the staff can follow and the bank can review.

## Who Feels the Pain
Owners, their staff, their warehouse lenders and examiners, and any acquirer.

## Impact If Fixed
A written, defensible policy and a business that can decide deals without the owner in the room.
