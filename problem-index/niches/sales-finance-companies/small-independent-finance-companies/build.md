# A Warehouse-Ready Data Room as a Service

**Niche:** [[niches/sales-finance-companies/small-independent-finance-companies/profile|Small Independent Finance Companies]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sales-finance-companies|Sales Finance Companies]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A small finance company's credit line depends on reports and evidence it produces by hand once a month and scrambles to assemble at every field exam.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #descriptive-statistics #data-integration #automation #compliance #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give a five-to-fifty-person finance company bank-grade decisioning, servicing and collateral reporting at its scale, and whoever lets it pass its warehouse lender's audit without hiring a data team takes the account.

## The Problem
The bank wants a monthly borrowing base, static pool data by vintage, delinquency and charge-off roll rates, eligibility exceptions, and on request a sample of contracts with titles. The owner's controller builds it from servicing exports. A field exam sample that turns up missing titles or mis-aged accounts can shrink the line.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Large lenders build this in-house; vendors sell platforms that small firms cannot configure; and each warehouse facility defines eligibility differently.

## What to Build
A managed service that connects to the servicing system and the document store, maintains a reconciled loan-level dataset, generates each facility's borrowing base and static pools from configured definitions, links every loan to its contract and title image, and keeps a standing data room the lender can sample at any time.

## Target Customer
Owners and controllers of finance companies with $20M–$500M receivables (estimate) on one or two facilities.

## Impact If Built
Cleaner field exams, faster facility renewals and the loan-level evidence lenders now require, without a data hire.
