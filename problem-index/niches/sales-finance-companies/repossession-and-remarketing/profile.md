# Repossession, Remarketing & Deficiency

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sales-finance-companies|Sales Finance Companies]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a defaulted contract into the highest net recovery through a repossession and sale that survive each state's notice rules, and whoever recovers the most per charged-off dollar without a wrongful-repossession or notice defect takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3B a year in repossession fees, transport, storage, auction and reconditioning costs and recovery staff across sales finance companies (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of sales finance companies' non-interest operating spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — assignments move through vendor portals, but condition reports, personal property inventories, state notices and deficiency calculations run on PDFs, letters and spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** Director of Recovery & Remarketing
**Automation Potential:** Medium — notices, refunds and channel choice are rule-bound; the recovery itself is physical and outsourced

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is the lender's side of recovery, distinct from the agents who tow the vehicle (the queued Repossession Agencies industry) and the agencies that later chase deficiencies ([[niches/collections-agencies/auto-loan-deficiency/profile|Auto Loan Deficiency Collectors]]). With Cox Automotive estimating ~1.73 million repossessions in 2024, the most since 2009, the lender decides when to assign, which agent, how to handle personal property and redemption, which notices each state requires before and after sale, which channel to sell through and what deficiency, if any, it can lawfully pursue. The competitors are in-house recovery departments, repossession forwarders working through the RDN network, remarketing services at Manheim, ACV and OPENLANE, and recovery-management software.

## Current Tools & Gaps
RDN (which carries an estimated ~90% of assignments) and Clearplan for assignment management; DRN's licence-plate network for location; wholesale auctions and their condition reports ([[niches/auto-dealers-independent/auction-condition-report-ops/profile|Wholesale Auction Condition Report Operations]]); servicing-system letter templates. Gaps: state notice logic kept by paralegals; channel choice by habit; deficiency figures that do not survive a challenge.
