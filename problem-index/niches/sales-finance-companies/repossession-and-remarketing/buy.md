# Legal Calendaring Adapted to State Repossession Notices

**Niche:** [[niches/sales-finance-companies/repossession-and-remarketing/profile|Repossession, Remarketing & Deficiency]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sales-finance-companies|Sales Finance Companies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Law firms run rules-based docketing that computes every deadline from a triggering event; recovery teams track state notice deadlines in spreadsheets.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #decision-trees #workflow-orchestration #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a defaulted contract into the highest net recovery through a repossession and sale that survive each state's notice rules, and whoever recovers the most per charged-off dollar without a wrongful-repossession or notice defect takes the account.

## The Problem
Each state sets its own sequence: right-to-cure notices before repossession in some, notices of intent to sell with specific content and timing, redemption and reinstatement periods, personal property handling, and post-sale explanations before a deficiency can be pursued. A missed or defective notice can bar the deficiency and invite statutory damages.

## What Already Exists
Court-rules docketing software used by litigation departments, which computes deadlines from triggering events under jurisdiction-specific rule sets.

## The Customization Gap
Recovery needs rule sets for consumer collateral under each state's UCC Article 9 enactment and retail installment sales act; triggers from servicing and repossession events (assignment, recovery, sale scheduled, sale completed); notice generation with state-required content; certified-mail tracking; and links to add-on refunds and deficiency calculation.

## Target Customer
Recovery operations and compliance at lenders repossessing across many states.

## Impact If Solved
Notices sent on time with the right content on every file, protecting deficiency rights and avoiding the class actions defective notices attract.
