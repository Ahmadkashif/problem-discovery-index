# Insurance Policy Administration Adapted to Financed Add-Ons

**Niche:** [[niches/sales-finance-companies/ancillary-product-refunds/profile|Ancillary Product Cancellations & Refunds]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sales-finance-companies|Sales Finance Companies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Insurers' policy administration systems compute return premiums on cancellation automatically; lenders financing similar products do it by hand.
**Tags:** #decision-trees #data-integration #automation #compliance #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to cancel and refund every add-on product on every early-terminated contract by that product's own terms and the state's rule, automatically and provably, and whoever closes the refund gap with an audit trail an examiner accepts takes the account.

## The Problem
Lenders hold the financed premium or price for products they do not administer and owe refunds they do not calculate.

## What Already Exists
Policy administration platforms that handle mid-term cancellation, return premium methods (pro rata, short-rate) and state rules for insurance products.

## The Customization Gap
The lender's case adds: products that are not insurance (GAP waivers, service contracts) with contract-specific methods; the administrator, the dealer and the lender each holding part of the money; refunds credited to the loan balance or paid to the borrower depending on termination type; and state rules specific to motor vehicle retail installment sales.

## Target Customer
Servicing and compliance teams at captives and large independents.

## Impact If Solved
A proven calculation engine pointed at the lender's version of the problem rather than a bespoke build.
