# Product Terms Captured at Funding, Refunds Triggered at Termination

**Niche:** [[niches/sales-finance-companies/ancillary-product-refunds/profile|Ancillary Product Cancellations & Refunds]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sales-finance-companies|Sales Finance Companies]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** The information needed to refund an add-on correctly is on the product contract at funding and is never captured, so every refund starts with someone reading an image years later.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #decision-trees #automation #compliance #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to cancel and refund every add-on product on every early-terminated contract by that product's own terms and the state's rule, automatically and provably, and whoever closes the refund gap with an audit trail an examiner accepts takes the account.

## The Problem
Servicing systems record that $2,400 of add-ons were financed, not that they were a 60-month GAP waiver from one administrator with a pro rata refund and a $50 fee, and a service contract from another with a time-and-mileage method. At termination the information has to be rediscovered.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Funding teams are measured on speed, not on structuring product terms; administrators' contracts vary endlessly; and refunds were treated as a back-office nuisance until regulators made them a finding.

## What to Build
Read product contracts at funding and store structured terms; subscribe to termination events from servicing; compute refunds by contract method and state rule; file cancellations with administrators through their portals or APIs; reconcile receipts and dealer chargebacks; and keep a per-refund audit record.

## Target Customer
Servicing directors at indirect lenders terminating thousands of contracts a month.

## Impact If Built
Every refund calculated and paid without a human finding the product, closing a standing compliance exposure.
