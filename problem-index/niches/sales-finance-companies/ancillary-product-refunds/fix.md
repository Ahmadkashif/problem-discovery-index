# The Refund Owed After Repossession

**Niche:** [[niches/sales-finance-companies/ancillary-product-refunds/profile|Ancillary Product Cancellations & Refunds]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sales-finance-companies|Sales Finance Companies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Payoffs trigger refunds because the borrower asks; repossessions often do not, because nobody on the recovery side is looking for add-ons.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #compliance #automation #quick-win #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to cancel and refund every add-on product on every early-terminated contract by that product's own terms and the state's rule, automatically and provably, and whoever closes the refund gap with an audit trail an examiner accepts takes the account.

## The Problem
After a repossession sale, unearned GAP and service contract fees should be cancelled and credited before the deficiency is calculated. When recovery and servicing are separate teams, the cancellation is missed, the deficiency is overstated and the borrower is pursued for money that should have been credited.

## Why It's Still Broken
The recovery workflow is built around the vehicle; the add-ons are invisible unless someone opens the contract.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Make add-on cancellation a mandatory step in the recovery workflow, triggered by repossession sale, with the credit posted before any deficiency letter can be generated.

## Who Feels the Pain
Borrowers, recovery and compliance staff, and the collection agencies that inherit overstated balances.

## Impact If Fixed
Correct deficiencies and removal of one of the most commonly cited servicing failures in auto finance.
