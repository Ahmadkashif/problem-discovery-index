# The Instant Approval That Becomes a Three-Day Stipulation

**Niche:** [[niches/sales-finance-companies/prime-auto-decisioning/profile|Prime Auto-Decisioning]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sales-finance-companies|Sales Finance Companies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The engine approves in seconds with conditions attached, and the conditions are discovered at funding, after the customer has driven away.
**Tags:** #decision-trees #evaluation-metrics #workflow-orchestration #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to auto-decision the largest share of a dealer's prime and near-prime applications in seconds without loss drift, and whoever holds the highest straight-through rate at a stable loss rate takes the account.

## The Problem
Automated approvals often carry stipulations — proof of income, residence, insurance — written in generic text the F&I manager skims. The dealer delivers the car, the funder finds the missing stipulation, and the deal sits.

## Why It's Still Broken
Stipulation rules accumulate in policy over years, are applied broadly to be safe, and are never measured for whether they prevent losses.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Score each stipulation rule by how often it fires, how long it delays funding and whether deals that satisfied it performed differently from those that would have been waived. Retire the ones that add delay without information, and present the survivors to the dealer in plain, specific terms at approval.

## Who Feels the Pain
F&I managers, funders, dealer relationship managers fielding complaints, and borrowers asked for documents days after purchase.

## Impact If Fixed
Shorter time-to-fund on the lender's highest-volume paper, with stipulations kept only where they earn it.
