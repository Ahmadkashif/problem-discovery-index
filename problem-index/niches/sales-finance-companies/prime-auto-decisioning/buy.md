# Bank-Account Income Verification Inside the Decision

**Niche:** [[niches/sales-finance-companies/prime-auto-decisioning/profile|Prime Auto-Decisioning]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sales-finance-companies|Sales Finance Companies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Consumer-permissioned bank data already verifies income for personal loans in seconds; auto lenders still ask dealers for pay stubs after the approval.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #automation #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to auto-decision the largest share of a dealer's prime and near-prime applications in seconds without loss drift, and whoever holds the highest straight-through rate at a stable loss rate takes the account.

## The Problem
Income is the most common stipulation and the most common misrepresentation. Verifying it after approval turns an instant decision into a multi-day wait at funding.

## What Already Exists
Open-banking income and cash-flow products (Plaid, Finicity and similar) and payroll-connection services used by personal loan and mortgage lenders.

## The Customization Gap
In indirect lending the consumer is at the dealer, not on the lender's app, so consent must be captured in the F&I office through the dealer networks' flows; the verification must return inside the decision window; cash-flow attributes must be mapped into auto credit policy (payment-to-income on a 72-month contract, not a 36-month loan); and adverse-action reasons must cover the new data.

## Target Customer
Prime and near-prime indirect lenders with high income-stipulation rates.

## Impact If Solved
Fewer conditional approvals, faster funding, and less room for the stated-income inflation that drives first-party fraud.
