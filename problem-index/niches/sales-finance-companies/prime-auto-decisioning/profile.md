# Prime Auto-Decisioning

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sales-finance-companies|Sales Finance Companies]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to auto-decision the largest share of a dealer's prime and near-prime applications in seconds without loss drift, and whoever holds the highest straight-through rate at a stable loss rate takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.5B a year in decision engines, bureau data, verification and review staff for prime and near-prime indirect paper (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of sales finance companies' non-interest operating spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Very High — most prime applications never reach a human
**Target Buyer:** Head of Credit Strategy at a captive or prime independent
**Automation Potential:** Very high; the contest is how far up the risk curve automation can reach

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Prime paper is a speed and coverage contest. The borrower qualifies almost everywhere; the dealer books with whichever source answers first with a usable approval, and captives add subvened rates tied to the manufacturer's incentive calendar. The work is pushing the automated boundary outward — into thin files, high loan-to-value and self-employed income — without letting early defaults or fraud rise. People matter only at the margin of the engine. The competitors are the captives, bank auto lenders and the decision-engine vendors that supply independents.

## Current Tools & Gaps
In-house engines at captives; defi SOLUTIONS, MeridianLink and Experian decisioning at independents; auto scores from the bureaus; income and identity verification from Informed.IQ and the bureaus. Gaps: automated approvals that later need manual stipulations (which defeats the speed), slow detection when a dealer learns to game the automated boundary, and no measure of the lost deals the engine declined that competitors booked and that performed.
