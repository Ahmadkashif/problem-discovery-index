# Identity Checks That Assume a Social Security Number

**Niche:** [[niches/sales-finance-companies/itin-and-thin-file-borrowers/profile|ITIN & Thin-File Borrowers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sales-finance-companies|Sales Finance Companies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Identity and fraud tools key on the SSN, so ITIN applicants either fail them automatically or pass with no real check at all.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #k-nearest-neighbors #evaluation-metrics #compliance #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to predict repayment for borrowers with no usable bureau file from verifiable non-bureau evidence such as job tenure, bank cash flow, rent and remittance history, and whoever approves the most of them at a loss rate its funders accept, with a fair lending record that survives examination, takes the account.

## The Problem
A lender serving ITIN borrowers turns off checks that would fail every applicant, leaving consular IDs, foreign passports and ITIN letters reviewed by eye — the gap through which synthetic and straw-borrower fraud passes.

## Why It's Still Broken
Identity vendors build for the majority market; specialist lenders are too small to demand alternatives.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Verification paths designed for ITIN applicants: document authentication for consular and foreign IDs, ITIN validation against IRS letters, device and phone tenure, and linkage checks across the lender's own applications to catch reused identities.

## Who Feels the Pain
Fraud teams and credit analysts at specialist lenders; honest borrowers subjected to blanket suspicion; warehouse lenders exposed to the fraud.

## Impact If Fixed
Real identity assurance for a population now checked by eye, closing one of the main fraud channels in this segment.
