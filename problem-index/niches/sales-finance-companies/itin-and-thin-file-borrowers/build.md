# A Verified Non-Bureau Credit File at the Dealer

**Niche:** [[niches/sales-finance-companies/itin-and-thin-file-borrowers/profile|ITIN & Thin-File Borrowers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sales-finance-companies|Sales Finance Companies]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A borrower with nine years of steady work and on-time rent arrives at a dealer with no credit file, and the lender has nothing verified to decide on.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #feature-engineering #transfer-learning #evaluation-metrics #compliance #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to predict repayment for borrowers with no usable bureau file from verifiable non-bureau evidence such as job tenure, bank cash flow, rent and remittance history, and whoever approves the most of them at a loss rate its funders accept, with a fair lending record that survives examination, takes the account.

## The Problem
Lenders willing to serve thin-file borrowers decide on stated information backed by paper, which invites fraud and forces high rates on good borrowers to cover it.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Alternative data is fragmented, consent flows at the dealer are clumsy, performance history on this population is held by a few specialist lenders, and the fair lending stakes deter large institutions.

## What to Build
A consented file assembled at the dealer: bank-account cash flow, payroll verification, rent and utility payment history, tax filing evidence under an ITIN, with each item verified at source. A model calibrated on specialist lenders' performance data, transferred carefully to new lenders, with fair lending testing documented for every release.

## Target Customer
Specialist non-prime auto lenders and credit unions serving immigrant communities.

## Impact If Built
Approvals based on verified evidence instead of paper, lower fraud, and pricing that reflects the borrower rather than the absence of a file.
