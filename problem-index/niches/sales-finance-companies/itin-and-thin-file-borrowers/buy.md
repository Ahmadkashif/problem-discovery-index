# Cash-Flow Scores Built for Personal Loans, Recalibrated for Auto

**Niche:** [[niches/sales-finance-companies/itin-and-thin-file-borrowers/profile|ITIN & Thin-File Borrowers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sales-finance-companies|Sales Finance Companies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Cash-flow underwriting scores exist for short personal loans; a six-year secured auto contract on a depreciating car behaves differently.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #survival-analysis #transfer-learning #evaluation-metrics #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to predict repayment for borrowers with no usable bureau file from verifiable non-bureau evidence such as job tenure, bank cash flow, rent and remittance history, and whoever approves the most of them at a loss rate its funders accept, with a fair lending record that survives examination, takes the account.

## The Problem
Lenders try cash-flow scores on thin-file auto applicants and find them miscalibrated for long terms and collateral.

## What Already Exists
Cash-flow scores and attributes from open-banking data vendors and the bureaus, trained mostly on personal loans and cards.

## The Customization Gap
Auto needs survival over 60–84 months rather than 12–36; the collateral's effect on willingness to pay (borrowers protect the car they drive to work); payment-to-income limits on cash-flow income; remittance outflows treated as obligations or as evidence of stability; and adverse-action reasons for every attribute used.

## Target Customer
Credit strategy teams at specialist lenders with a few years of thin-file performance data.

## Impact If Solved
Usable scores for a population bureaus cannot score, calibrated on the product the lender actually sells.
