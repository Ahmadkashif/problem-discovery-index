# Equipment Valuation & Residual Data Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sales-finance-companies|Sales Finance Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
EquipmentWatch, Sandhills Global's value products and RB Global's Rouse Services (bought by Ritchie Bros. in 2020) publish used values and residual forecasts for construction, agricultural and transport equipment and run appraisals of fleets pledged as collateral. Equipment finance companies and captives set lease residuals, advance rates and charge-off reserves against them; asset-based lenders require the appraisals for borrowing bases. The equipment counterpart of [[niches/auto-dealers-independent/vehicle-valuation-guides/profile|🔍 Vehicle Valuation Guide Publishers]]. Rouse's rental-rate and utilisation benchmarking for rental fleets is left to the queued Equipment Rental Companies industry; this pocket is the value and residual work lenders buy.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 valuation analysts, appraisers and data scientists per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Value guides and residual forecasts by make, model, year and hours; desktop and field appraisals; market reports
**Proprietary data:** Auction results (RB Global holds the largest equipment auction record), listing and dealer transaction data, and contributed rental and sales data from fleet owners
**Clock:** Lender-required appraisal cycles in credit agreements, lease-end residual settings and monthly value updates — mostly set by clients' lenders rather than by a regulator
**Buyer:** Head of Valuation Products / Chief Appraiser
