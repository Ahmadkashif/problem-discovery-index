# The Resubmission Nobody Compares

**Niche:** [[niches/sales-finance-companies/non-prime-deal-structuring/profile|Non-Prime Deal Structuring]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sales-finance-companies|Sales Finance Companies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** When a dealer resubmits a countered deal, the analyst sees a new application rather than what changed, which is exactly where packed income and swapped vehicles hide.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #compliance #quick-win #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to return a counteroffer on advance, term, down payment and stipulations that the dealer can close and the borrower can pay, and whoever structures the most closable deals that still perform takes the account.

## The Problem
The lender counters at a lower advance. Forty minutes later the deal returns with a higher stated income, a larger "cash down" and a different trim level. On screen it is a fresh application that now fits the grid.

## Why It's Still Broken
Origination systems key applications by submission; linking the resubmission to the original is left to the analyst's memory, and a busy queue means a different analyst often sees it.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Link resubmissions automatically by applicant, dealer and vehicle; show a diff of what changed since the counter; flag changes that are implausible (income up 30% in an hour) and route them to a senior analyst.

## Who Feels the Pain
Analysts, fraud teams and dealer-risk managers; and the honest dealers whose good deals are slowed by blanket stipulations used to compensate.

## Impact If Fixed
A cheap linkage closes one of the most common doors to first-party and dealer fraud at the point of decision.
