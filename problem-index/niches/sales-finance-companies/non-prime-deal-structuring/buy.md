# Pricing Optimisation Turned to Structure

**Niche:** [[niches/sales-finance-companies/non-prime-deal-structuring/profile|Non-Prime Deal Structuring]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sales-finance-companies|Sales Finance Companies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Insurers and personal lenders optimise price against take-up and risk; non-prime auto competes on structure, which the same tools can optimise only if they model the dealer in the middle.
**Tags:** #optimization-fundamentals #gradient-boosting #logistic-regression #evaluation-metrics #compliance #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to return a counteroffer on advance, term, down payment and stipulations that the dealer can close and the borrower can pay, and whoever structures the most closable deals that still perform takes the account.

## The Problem
Non-prime lenders tune program grids — maximum advance by tier, term caps, minimum down — by committee, and dealers learn the grids within weeks.

## What Already Exists
Price optimisation and demand-modelling platforms used in insurance and consumer lending (Earnix and similar), which fit conversion and risk models and solve for the best offer under constraints.

## The Customization Gap
Auto needs the dealer as the decision-maker on acceptance (it compares counters from several lenders); structure variables rather than price alone, since rates are often capped by state; the dealer's reserve and acquisition fee economics; collateral depreciation by vehicle; and fair lending constraints on how structure can vary.

## Target Customer
Credit strategy teams at non-prime lenders with enough volume to fit acceptance models by dealer segment.

## Impact If Solved
Program grids set by measured acceptance and loss rather than by committee, refreshed faster than dealers can game them.
