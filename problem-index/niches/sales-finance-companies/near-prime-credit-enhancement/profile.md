# Near-Prime Credit Enhancement Programs

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sales-finance-companies|Sales Finance Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Open Lending's Lenders Protection program prices near- and non-prime auto loans for credit unions, banks and finance companies with its own risk model and backs them with default insurance written by partner insurers. The lender pays program fees for the decision plus the insurance, which lets it book loans it would otherwise decline.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-40 credit modellers and analysts (estimate)
**Output:** Risk-based pricing decisions and insured approvals at application; portfolio performance reporting to lenders and insurers
**Proprietary data:** Two decades of near-prime auto loan performance and insurance claims across participating lenders
**Clock:** Decisions while the dealer waits; no external deadline on the analytics
**Buyer:** Chief Risk Officer / Head of Analytics
