# The Same Delinquency Counted Three Ways

**Niche:** [[niches/sales-finance-companies/warehouse-and-abs-reporting/profile|Warehouse & ABS Collateral Reporting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sales-finance-companies|Sales Finance Companies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The servicer report, the borrowing base and the asset-level file each age accounts by their own rule, and the differences surface as investor questions.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #data-integration #compliance #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce borrowing base, servicer and asset-level reports from one reconciled loan-level dataset with evidence that each contract exists and is pledged once, and whoever makes the finance company's numbers verifiable by its banks and investors takes the account.

## The Problem
An account on a payment extension is current in one report, 30 days late in another and excluded from a third. Each is defensible under its own definition; together they look like an error.

## Why It's Still Broken
Definitions live in formulas written by different analysts at different times, and nothing checks reports against each other before they go out.

## What a Fix Looks Like
One table of loan states with every definition applied as a labelled column, and an automated reconciliation that explains every difference between reports before submission.

## Who Feels the Pain
Securitization analysts, treasurers, trustees, rating agency surveillance analysts and investors.

## Impact If Fixed
Fewer investor queries and no apparent inconsistencies to explain at the moment funding is most sensitive.
