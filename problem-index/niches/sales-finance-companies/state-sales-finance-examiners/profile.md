# State Sales Finance Licensing & Examination Units

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sales-finance-companies|Sales Finance Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State financial regulators license sales finance companies and examine them for compliance with retail installment sales, rate, fee, repossession and refund laws; state attorneys general bring enforcement actions. In most states the same banking or financial-institutions department also licenses mortgage lenders and money transmitters, profiled as [[niches/mortgage-lenders/state-mortgage-regulators/profile|🔍 State Mortgage Regulators & Multistate Examination]] and [[niches/money-service-businesses/state-money-transmitter-regulators/profile|🔍 State Money Transmitter Regulators & Multistate Exams]], and scored the same way here. Federal supervision of the largest nonbank auto lenders sits with the CFPB, already profiled as [[niches/collections-agencies/cfpb-consumer-research/profile|🔍 Federal Consumer Finance Research]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** Examiners are production staff; research or data analytics units dedicated to sales finance are under 10 per state (estimate)
**Output:** Licences, examination reports, consent orders
**Proprietary data:** Examination findings and complaint files
**Clock:** Statutory examination cycles and licence renewals
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer
