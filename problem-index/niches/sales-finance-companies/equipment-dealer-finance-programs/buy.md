# Small-Business Cash-Flow Underwriting for Seasonal Borrowers

**Niche:** [[niches/sales-finance-companies/equipment-dealer-finance-programs/profile|Equipment Dealer & Vendor Finance Programs]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sales-finance-companies|Sales Finance Companies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Small-business lenders already underwrite from bank-account cash flow in minutes; equipment captives still ask a farmer or contractor for two years of tax returns.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #time-series-forecasting #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to approve a small-business buyer at the dealer's counter on thin financials while setting a residual the used-equipment market will honour, and whoever decides fastest with credit and residual losses on plan takes the account.

## The Problem
Mid-ticket equipment deals stall at the dealer while the buyer finds tax returns and an analyst spreads them. Seasonal businesses look weak in the wrong month.

## What Already Exists
Cash-flow underwriting platforms used by online small-business lenders, reading permissioned bank transactions and accounting-software data.

## The Customization Gap
Equipment needs seasonality models by trade (harvest, construction season, snow removal); payment schedules matched to cash flow (skip payments, seasonal structures); the equipment's own contribution to revenue (a second excavator for a contractor already running one); and integration with the dealer portal so the answer arrives while the buyer is at the counter.

## Target Customer
Credit heads at captives and independent equipment finance companies with mid-ticket dealer programs.

## Impact If Solved
Faster mid-ticket approvals on borrowers that tax returns misjudge, with payment structures that fit their cash flow.
