# Dealer-Level Funding Trust Scores

**Niche:** [[niches/sales-finance-companies/contract-funding-verification/profile|Contract Funding & Stipulation Verification]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sales-finance-companies|Sales Finance Companies]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Every package gets the same review whether the dealer has sent five hundred clean contracts or keeps sending packed ones, so funders spend equal time on unequal risk.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #bayesian-inference #evaluation-metrics #workflow-orchestration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to fund a clean contract the same day without funding a defective or fraudulent one, and whoever cuts days-to-fund while catching every defect before the money moves takes the account.

## The Problem
Funding review depth is set by policy, not by evidence about the dealer. A reliable dealer's clean deal waits in the same queue as a dealer whose stipulations have been fabricated twice this quarter.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Defect history sits in kickback notes and post-funding audit files, not in a structured table, and dealer relationship teams resist anything that looks like a public grade of their dealers.

## What to Build
A per-dealer, per-F&I-manager trust score built from kickback rates by defect type, post-funding audit findings, stipulation failures, early payment defaults and buybacks, updated continuously with Bayesian shrinkage for low-volume dealers. Route packages to review depth by score: light-touch for trusted dealers, full review and call-backs for drifting ones.

## Target Customer
Funding and dealer-risk heads at indirect lenders funding hundreds of contracts a day.

## Impact If Built
Faster funding where it is safe, attention concentrated where defects and fraud concentrate, and an evidence base for dealer conversations.
