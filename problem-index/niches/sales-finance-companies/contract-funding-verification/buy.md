# Mortgage Loan-File Review Adapted to Retail Installment Contracts

**Niche:** [[niches/sales-finance-companies/contract-funding-verification/profile|Contract Funding & Stipulation Verification]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sales-finance-companies|Sales Finance Companies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Mortgage has mature automated loan-file review against investor guidelines; auto funding checks a smaller file against fifty states' contract rules with nothing comparable.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #cnns #evaluation-metrics #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to fund a clean contract the same day without funding a defective or fraudulent one, and whoever cuts days-to-fund while catching every defect before the money moves takes the account.

## The Problem
Funders read every package against rules that change by state and by dealer agreement, with defects found late and inconsistently.

## What Already Exists
Mortgage document-review and quality-control platforms that classify loan files, extract data and test it against agency and investor guidelines, with exception workflows.

## The Customization Gap
Auto needs a library of state retail installment contract forms and versions; truth-in-lending recomputation for simple-interest auto contracts; state caps on documentation fees, add-on prices and rates; the lender's dealer agreement (maximum reserve, approved add-ons, advance limits); title and lien rules per state; stipulation consistency checks against the application; and new obligations such as California's CARS Act from October 1, 2026.

## Target Customer
Funding operations at lenders with multi-state dealer networks.

## Impact If Solved
Consistent, rule-cited review on every package, with funders resolving exceptions rather than reading pages.
