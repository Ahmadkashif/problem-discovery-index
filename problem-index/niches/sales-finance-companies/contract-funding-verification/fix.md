# Kickbacks Returned One Defect at a Time

**Niche:** [[niches/sales-finance-companies/contract-funding-verification/profile|Contract Funding & Stipulation Verification]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sales-finance-companies|Sales Finance Companies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A funder stops at the first defect and sends the package back; the dealer fixes it; the next funder finds the second defect, and the money waits a week.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #automation #quick-win #evaluation-metrics
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to fund a clean contract the same day without funding a defective or fraudulent one, and whoever cuts days-to-fund while catching every defect before the money moves takes the account.

## The Problem
Dealers complain less about strict lenders than about slow ones. Serial kickbacks — a missing initial on Monday, an unsupported income on Wednesday, a title issue on Friday — are the single biggest source of funding delay and dealer frustration.

## Why It's Still Broken
Funders are measured on packages touched, so stopping at the first defect is rational; checklists are long; and the defect notice is free text the dealer's back office misreads.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Run the full checklist on every package, automatically where possible, and return one consolidated defect notice with each item specified and the evidence attached. Track cycles per package by dealer and publish the improvement to dealers.

## Who Feels the Pain
Dealer office managers, F&I managers waiting for cash, funders fielding calls, and dealer relationship managers losing share.

## Impact If Fixed
Fewer funding cycles per contract and days off the dealer's cash cycle, with no change in risk tolerance.
