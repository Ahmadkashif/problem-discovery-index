# Consumer & Equipment Finance Association Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sales-finance-companies|Sales Finance Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The American Financial Services Association, the NAF Association for non-prime auto finance and the Equipment Leasing and Finance Association publish member surveys and benchmarks — ELFA's annual Survey of Equipment Finance Activity (100 member companies; $146.9B of 2024 new business) and monthly index, AFSA and NAF industry surveys — and lobby on behalf of finance companies.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research staff per association, with outside firms engaged for major studies (estimate)
**Output:** Member surveys, benchmark reports, monthly activity indices
**Proprietary data:** Members' confidential survey submissions
**Clock:** Self-set publication calendars
**Buyer:** Association President / Research Director
