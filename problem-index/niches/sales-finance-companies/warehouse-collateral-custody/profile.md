# Warehouse Collateral Custody, Verification & Backup Servicing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sales-finance-companies|Sales Finance Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Trust banks and specialist firms hold contract files and titles as custodian for warehouse lenders and ABS trustees, verify collateral files against tapes, and stand ready as backup servicer if the finance company fails. Tricolor's 2025 collapse, with collateral allegedly pledged to more than one lender, has put their verification work under scrutiny.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Custodial and verification clerks; no dedicated research function of 10+ (estimate)
**Output:** Custody receipts, file exception reports, backup servicing readiness
**Proprietary data:** Custodial records for the pools they hold
**Clock:** Funding dates and monthly reporting set by facility agreements
**Buyer:** Head of Corporate Trust / Custody Services
