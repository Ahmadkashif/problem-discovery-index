# Indirect Origination Network Data Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sales-finance-companies|Sales Finance Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The data and analytics groups at the dealer-to-lender networks and origination platforms — Dealertrack within Cox Automotive (whose network passed 900 financing sources by 2010), RouteOne (owned by captive lenders) and defi SOLUTIONS — see every application, decision and eContract that passes through them and sell credit-availability indices, look-to-book benchmarking and platform analytics to lenders and dealers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 analysts and data scientists per platform (estimate)
**Output:** Credit availability indices, lender benchmarking, dealer and lender platform analytics
**Proprietary data:** Application, decision and contract flow across thousands of dealers and hundreds of lenders
**Clock:** None external; the platforms run on transaction volume
**Buyer:** VP of Data & Analytics
