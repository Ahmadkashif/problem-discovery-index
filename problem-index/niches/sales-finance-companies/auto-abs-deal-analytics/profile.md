# Auto ABS Deal Modelling & Surveillance Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sales-finance-companies|Sales Finance Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Intex, Moody's Analytics' structured finance business, dv01 (now within Fitch Solutions), Yield Book, Finsight and similar vendors model the cash-flow waterfall of securitisations, normalise monthly servicer reports and loan-level data, and sell the deal models, performance analytics and pool comparisons that ABS and MBS investors, underwriters and issuers price and monitor against. This is the vault's single cross-asset profile for that layer: auto loan and lease ABS (a record ~$127B of US auto loan issuance in 2025, S&P), credit card master trusts, and agency and non-agency mortgage deal and prepayment modelling are the same vendors, the same coded-waterfall product and the same buyers. The card and mortgage builds link here rather than profiling it again.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-100 deal modellers, data analysts and structured-finance researchers per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Deal cash-flow models, normalised loan-level and pool performance data, issuer and vintage comparisons — the subscription
**Proprietary data:** A library of coded waterfall models across public and many private auto, card and mortgage deals, and years of normalised performance across issuers; the raw inputs are public filings (Form ABS-EE carries 72 fields per auto loan) or issuer tapes licensed for private deals
**Clock:** Monthly distribution and reporting dates fixed in each deal's documents, and new-issue pricing windows set by issuers and underwriters
**Buyer:** Head of Structured Finance Data / Chief Product Officer
