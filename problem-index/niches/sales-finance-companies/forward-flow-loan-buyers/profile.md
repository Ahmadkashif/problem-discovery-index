# Private Credit Forward-Flow & Whole-Loan Buyers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sales-finance-companies|Sales Finance Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Asset-based finance teams at private credit managers buy pools of auto, powersports and equipment loans and commit to buy future originations from finance companies. The October 2025 sale of ~$4.06B of Harley-Davidson Credit motorcycle loans to KKR and a PIMCO-linked trust, with a five-year forward flow for about two-thirds of future originations, is the template. Their analysts diligence loan tapes, model pool returns and monitor the flow.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 asset-based finance analysts per manager (estimate)
**Output:** Pool valuations, bid prices, forward-flow pricing and monitoring — used for the manager's own investments
**Proprietary data:** Loan-level tapes and performance across the originators they buy from, held under each originator's confidentiality terms
**Clock:** Bid deadlines in loan-sale processes and monthly purchase settlements
**Buyer:** Head of Asset-Based Finance
