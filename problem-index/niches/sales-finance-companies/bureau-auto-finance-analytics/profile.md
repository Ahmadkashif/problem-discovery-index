# Credit Bureau Automotive Finance Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sales-finance-companies|Sales Finance Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
The automotive finance analytics units at Experian, TransUnion and Equifax turn the auto tradelines lenders furnish into market share by lender type, origination and delinquency trends, competitive benchmarking and prospect lists. Experian's quarterly State of the Automotive Finance Market — the source of the captives' 43.4% share of new-vehicle loans in Q1 2026 — is the public face of a paid benchmarking business.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 analysts and consultants per bureau's automotive unit (estimate)
**Output:** Lender market share and benchmark reports, portfolio and competitive analytics, custom studies
**Proprietary data:** Auto tradelines covering essentially every financed vehicle, furnished monthly by lenders
**Clock:** Self-set quarterly publication and clients' planning cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Automotive Analytics
