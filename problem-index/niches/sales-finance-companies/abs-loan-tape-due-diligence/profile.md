# ABS Agreed-Upon Procedures & Loan-Tape Due Diligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sales-finance-companies|Sales Finance Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Accounting firms' securitisation groups and due diligence providers compare a sample of loans on an issuer's data tape against the underlying contracts, titles and servicing records before each securitisation, and the findings are disclosed to investors on Form ABS-15G ahead of the first sale. Warehouse lenders commission similar sample testing at field exams.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Large testing teams, but the work is production sample testing; analysts designing the procedures number under 10 per engagement team (estimate)
**Output:** Agreed-upon procedures reports listing exceptions found in the sample
**Proprietary data:** None of its own — the tape, contracts and records are the issuer's
**Clock:** Third-party due diligence findings must be disclosed before the first sale of each deal
**Buyer:** Partner, Securitisation Services
