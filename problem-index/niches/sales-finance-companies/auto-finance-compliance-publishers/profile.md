# Auto Finance Compliance Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sales-finance-companies|Sales Finance Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Law-firm knowledge operations and specialist publishers — Hudson Cook's CounselorLibrary is the best known in auto finance — turn federal rules and fifty states' retail installment sales acts, licensing laws, repossession and refund statutes into the compliance manuals, state charts, form guidance and change alerts that finance companies and dealers subscribe to.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-30 attorneys, editors and analysts per publisher (estimate)
**Output:** State law surveys and charts, compliance manuals, regulatory change alerts, model forms
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated state-by-state interpretation, maintained over years, plus a view of which questions subscribers ask
**Clock:** Statutory and regulatory effective dates — California's CARS Act became operative on October 1, 2026 — and enforcement actions
**Buyer:** Editorial Director / Practice Leader
