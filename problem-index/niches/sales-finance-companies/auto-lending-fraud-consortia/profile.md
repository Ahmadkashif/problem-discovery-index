# Auto Lending Fraud Consortium & Verification Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sales-finance-companies|Sales Finance Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Point Predictive runs a consortium of auto lenders' applications and fraud outcomes and sells fraud scores and alerts at decision time; Informed.IQ verifies income, identity and stipulation documents for lenders; the bureaus and LexisNexis sell auto fraud products alongside. Point Predictive's annual report — $10.4B of estimated auto lending fraud exposure in 2026, 69% first-party — is the industry's reference figure.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 data scientists and fraud analysts per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Application fraud scores, dealer and ring alerts, verification results, an annual fraud study
**Proprietary data:** Applications and confirmed fraud and early-default outcomes pooled across participating lenders — a cross-lender view no lender has alone
**Clock:** Decisions are needed while the customer is in the F&I office; no regulator or court sets a deadline on the vendor's work
**Buyer:** Chief Analytics Officer / Head of Fraud Research
