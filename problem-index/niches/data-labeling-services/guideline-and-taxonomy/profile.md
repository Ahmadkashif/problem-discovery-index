# Guideline & Taxonomy Iteration

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/data-labeling-services|Data Labeling Services]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor here is fighting to find the ambiguity in a guideline before it produces a batch of inconsistent data — and whoever does that takes project delivery, because guideline ambiguity is the single most common cause of the disagreement everyone attributes to annotators.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$220M US attributable to taxonomy and guideline development
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~6% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** None — guidelines change and the effect is never measured
**Target Buyer:** Taxonomy authors, project leads and the customers who specify them
**Automation Potential:** Very High — ambiguity is detectable from the disagreement pattern

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The guideline is the specification, and it is written once by somebody who has not annotated the data, revised reactively when questions accumulate, and never evaluated. Its ambiguities produce disagreement that is attributed to annotators, its revisions produce inconsistency between the batches annotated before and after, and its version is frequently not recorded against the items it governed. This matters more than its low profile suggests: guideline ambiguity is probably the largest single controllable cause of poor annotation quality, it is detectable from the disagreement pattern — disagreement concentrated on a category or a boundary is a specification problem — and nothing in the category performs that detection. The contest is treating the guideline as an artefact to be tested and measured rather than as documentation.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Guideline documents, example sets, annotator question channels, and revision by accumulation. The gaps: ambiguity is discovered by annotators asking questions, which reaches only the conscientious ones and only after they have annotated; disagreement is not analysed for structure, so a category boundary nobody can apply is indistinguishable from careless work; revisions are not versioned against items, which makes the resulting inconsistency undetectable and unfixable; a revision's effect is never measured, so nobody knows whether a change helped; and the examples are chosen by the author rather than drawn from the items that actually caused disagreement.
