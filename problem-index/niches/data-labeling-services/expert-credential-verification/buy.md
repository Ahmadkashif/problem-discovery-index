**Contested on:** Every serious competitor here is fighting to establish that a contributor can actually do the work, at the speed a contract demands — and whoever does that takes the expert tier, because identity verification is commodity and capability verification is the constraint.

## The Problem
Medical boards, legal bar examinations and professional certification bodies have spent a century answering exactly this question — can this person do this work — and have developed the apparatus: item banking, item response calibration, adaptive administration, validation against job performance, and security practice against item exposure. The annotation industry needs the same answer in days rather than in years, and produces a twenty-question test with no validation and no item security.

## What Already Exists
Item response theory with item banking and calibration; computerised adaptive testing, which obtains more information in less time; criterion validation methodology relating test performance to job performance; item exposure control and security practice; and the work sample testing literature, which finds that samples of the actual work predict performance better than knowledge tests.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a timescale of days and a validation criterion that is available. It requires: (1) work samples rather than knowledge items, since the literature is clear that a sample of the actual task predicts performance best and this industry uniquely has the actual task available — which is both the easiest instrument to build and the strongest; (2) validation against delivery outcomes, which is available here and is unavailable to most licensing bodies, since the vendor observes exactly how each contributor subsequently performed — this is a substantial advantage over the discipline the methods come from and is unused; (3) rapid item calibration, because a new domain's items have no history and the calibration must accumulate during the project rather than before it; (4) item exposure control, since assessments circulate among a connected contributor population far faster than in a licensing context and an uncontrolled item bank is compromised within weeks; and (5) an honest false-negative posture, because rejecting a capable expert is a cost too and the instrument's error rate in both directions should be known.

## Target Customer
Expert marketplaces and delivery organisations, the assessment and psychometric vendors for whom this is an adjacent commercial market, and the labs setting expert requirements.

## Impact If Solved
A mature assessment discipline addresses this exact question and the industry improvises. Work samples are both the strongest instrument and the easiest to build here, and outcome validation is available in a way the parent discipline rarely has.
