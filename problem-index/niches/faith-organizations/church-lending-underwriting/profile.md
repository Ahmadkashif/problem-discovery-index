# Congregational Lending Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/faith-organizations|Faith Organizations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Denominational loan funds and specialist church lenders underwrite building and expansion loans to congregations, assessing giving trends, attendance trajectory, and leadership stability in borrowers who file no public financials.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 credit analysts and portfolio staff
**Output:** Underwriting decisions, portfolio monitoring, default analysis
**Proprietary data:** Congregational financial and attendance data collected in underwriting, joined to repayment outcomes — one of the few places congregational finances are systematically recorded
**Clock:** Loan closing and covenant reporting cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Credit Officer
