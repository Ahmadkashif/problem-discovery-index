# Abuse Prevention Risk Accreditation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/faith-organizations|Faith Organizations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Praesidium, MinistrySafe, and comparable bodies assess organizations serving children and vulnerable people against abuse risk standards, deliver the screening and training programmes, and issue the accreditation that insurers increasingly require before writing coverage. The written risk assessment determines whether an organization is insurable.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 risk analysts, investigators, and training specialists
**Output:** Risk assessments, accreditation decisions, screening and training programmes
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated abuse risk findings and case analysis across thousands of organizations — a body of evidence about how abuse occurs in institutional settings that no individual organization or researcher holds
**Clock:** Insurance renewal requirements and accreditation cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Risk Services / Chief Research Officer
