# Nonprofit Data & Ratings Organizations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/faith-organizations|Faith Organizations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Candid and the nonprofit ratings organizations assemble, structure, and enrich the federal information return corpus for the entire nonprofit sector, adding programme classification, financial analysis, and grant flow data, and sell it to funders, researchers, and the organizations themselves. Congregations are largely exempt from filing, which is precisely why the faith sector is the blind spot in the dataset.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-400 data analysts, classifiers, and researchers
**Output:** Structured nonprofit financial and programme data, ratings, grant flow analytics — the subscription
**Proprietary data:** Decades of structured and classified filings plus grant flow data contributed by funders; the underlying returns are public and the structuring layer is not
**Clock:** Filing seasons and funder reporting cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Research
