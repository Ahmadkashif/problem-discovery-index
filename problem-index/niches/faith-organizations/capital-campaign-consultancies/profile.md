# Capital Campaign Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/faith-organizations|Faith Organizations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Generis, RSI, and the campaign firms run feasibility studies — interviewing members, testing giving capacity, and forecasting what a congregation can actually raise — then manage the campaign itself. The feasibility study is the analytical product and it determines whether a building programme proceeds.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 campaign consultants, analysts, and researchers
**Output:** Feasibility studies, giving capacity analyses, campaign management
**Proprietary data:** Campaign results across thousands of congregations joined to the pre-campaign study findings — a direct record of what actually predicts a successful campaign
**Clock:** Campaign timelines and building programme schedules
**Buyer:** President / Director of Research
