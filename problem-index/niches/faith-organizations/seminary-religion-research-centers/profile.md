# Academic Religion Research Centres

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/faith-organizations|Faith Organizations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
University and seminary-based centres run the national congregational surveys and maintain the religion data archives that most published research on American religious life draws from, funded by grants and philanthropy.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 researchers and data staff
**Output:** National congregational surveys, data archives, published research
**Proprietary data:** Long-running survey series and the archived datasets the field depends on
**Clock:** Grant cycles and publication schedules
**Buyer:** Centre director — grant funded
