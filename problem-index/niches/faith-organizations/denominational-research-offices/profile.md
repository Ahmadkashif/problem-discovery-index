# Denominational Research Offices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/faith-organizations|Faith Organizations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Denominational national offices maintain congregational statistics, track membership and attendance trends across their affiliated bodies, and produce the demographic analysis that informs church planting and resource allocation decisions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 3-20 researchers per denomination
**Output:** Congregational statistics, trend analyses, planting and closure research
**Proprietary data:** Annual congregational reports across affiliated bodies, in some denominations spanning a century
**Clock:** Annual reporting cycles and assembly calendars
**Buyer:** Director of Research — denominationally funded
