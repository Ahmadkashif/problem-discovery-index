# Translation & Field Linguistics Organizations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/faith-organizations|Faith Organizations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
SIL International, Wycliffe, and the translation organizations employ field linguists who document minority languages — phonology, grammar, lexicon — as the necessary precondition for translation work. The linguistic output is published freely and constitutes the largest body of minority language documentation in existence.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-3,000 field linguists, translators, and language technologists
**Output:** Language documentation, grammars and lexicons, translations, the global language catalogue
**Proprietary data:** Field documentation across thousands of languages, much of it the only record that exists — genuinely irreplaceable and given away by design
**Clock:** Project timelines set internally
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — donor funded
