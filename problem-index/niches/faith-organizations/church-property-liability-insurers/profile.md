# Congregational Property & Liability Insurers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/faith-organizations|Faith Organizations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Church Mutual, GuideOne, and Brotherhood Mutual specialize in insuring congregations, researching the property, liability, and abuse exposures specific to religious organizations and producing the risk management guidance the sector follows.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 underwriters, actuaries, and risk control specialists
**Output:** Rating models, risk control guidance, claim trend research
**Proprietary data:** Claim experience across hundreds of thousands of congregations — the only substantial dataset on congregational risk in existence
**Clock:** Rate filings and renewal cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer / VP Risk Control
