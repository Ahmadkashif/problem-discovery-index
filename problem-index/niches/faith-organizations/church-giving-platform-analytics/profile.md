# Church Management Platform Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/faith-organizations|Faith Organizations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The data teams at Pushpay, Tithely, Ministry Brands, and Planning Center analyze the giving, attendance, and engagement data flowing through tens of thousands of congregations, build in-product analytics, and publish the giving benchmark reports the sector cites.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 data analysts and product staff
**Output:** In-product engagement analytics, giving benchmarks, published sector reporting
**Proprietary data:** Giving and attendance data across tens of thousands of congregations — effectively the only real dataset on an industry whose participants are exempt from financial filing
**Clock:** Release cycles, internal
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Chief Product Officer
