# Religious Research Organizations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/faith-organizations|Faith Organizations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Barna Group, Lifeway Research, and the religion research centres run tracking studies on belief, practice, attendance, and generational change, and sell the resulting reports and custom studies to congregations, denominations, publishers, and ministries planning against a shifting religious landscape.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 survey researchers, analysts, and writers
**Output:** Tracking studies, custom research, published reports — the product
**Proprietary data:** Long-running religious behaviour survey series with decades of comparable measurement
**Clock:** Publication calendars and client study deadlines
**Buyer:** President / Director of Research
