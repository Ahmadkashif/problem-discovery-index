**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let the support agent explain a risk decision to the person it affects — and whoever makes automated holds explainable turns the category's worst customer interaction into an ordinary one.

## The Problem
Consumer lending has been legally obliged for decades to tell a declined applicant the principal reasons for the decision. That obligation forced an entire practice into existence: reason code generation from scoring models, models constrained so that explanations are possible at all, disclosure templates, and dispute and correction processes. It is not perfect and it works. Platform risk systems make decisions of comparable consequence to a person's income with no equivalent, because nobody has required it.

## What Already Exists
Adverse action reason code generation; model explainability constrained to permit it; standardised disclosure and notification templates; dispute, correction and reconsideration workflows; and fair lending and disparate impact auditing.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is from a one-time credit decision to an ongoing account relationship with continuous income. It requires: (1) explanations for holds and interruptions rather than for a single approve-or-decline, since the decision recurs and the affected person keeps trading through it — the lending framework has no model for an ongoing relationship; (2) an expected duration, which lending never has to state and which is the creator's most urgent question; (3) a resolution path the person can act on immediately rather than a reapplication in six months, since the money exists and is being withheld; (4) explanation that does not enable evasion in a genuinely adversarial minority, which lending largely avoids by using mostly public inputs and which here requires a deliberate disclosure design; and (5) prioritisation by dependence, since income interruption harms are wildly unequal and lending decisions do not have this property.

## Target Customer
Platform risk and support operations, creator marketplaces, and risk tooling vendors for whom explainable income interruption is unserved.

## Impact If Solved
Lending was compelled to explain and built the practice; platforms were not and did not. The ongoing relationship, the duration question and the immediate resolution path are all things the lending framework has no model for.
