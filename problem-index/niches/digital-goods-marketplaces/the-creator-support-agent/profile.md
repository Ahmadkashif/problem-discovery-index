# The Creator Support Agent

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/digital-goods-marketplaces|Digital Goods Marketplaces]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let the support agent explain a risk decision to the person it affects — and whoever makes automated holds explainable turns the category's worst customer interaction into an ordinary one.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$500M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~6% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — scripts and opacity
**Target Buyer:** Support operations leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — explanation and triage are automatable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A support agent explains to a creator that their earnings are held, cannot say why because the risk system does not tell them, and cannot say when. This is the category's defining support interaction and it is structurally unwinnable: the agent is the human face of a decision they have no access to, speaking to someone whose rent depends on it. It is a distinct niche because the problem is the boundary between an automated risk system and a human conversation, which is a design question rather than a fraud question or a support staffing question.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Ticketing systems, canned responses, an escalation path to a risk team that rarely responds in time, and a policy page. The gaps: no visibility into why a hold was placed; no expected duration; no route for the creator to supply what would resolve it; no prioritisation by the creator's actual dependence on the funds; and no feedback on how often holds turn out to be wrong.
