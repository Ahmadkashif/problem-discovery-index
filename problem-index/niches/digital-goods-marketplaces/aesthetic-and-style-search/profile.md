# Aesthetic & Style Search

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/digital-goods-marketplaces|Digital Goods Marketplaces]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to match a buyer to a feeling they cannot put into words — and whoever builds the interaction that extracts an aesthetic intent wins the searches that keyword and similarity search both fail.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.0B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~11% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** High — embeddings deployed, intent unaddressed
**Target Buyer:** Platform discovery engineering
**Automation Potential:** Very High — representation and interaction are both model-driven

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The aesthetic query has no ground truth and cannot be typed. A buyer knows a template is wrong within half a second of seeing it and cannot say in advance what right would look like. Every retrieval system in use assumes the query can be stated — in words, or by an example the buyer already has — and this buyer has neither. The contest is over representation and interaction together: learning a style space separate from subject, and designing a loop that converges on an intent the buyer can only recognise. It is entirely separable from whether the asset is technically usable, which is the sibling contest.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Keyword search over creator tags, visual similarity from a selected example, curated collections, and browsing by category. The gaps: style entangled with subject in every general embedding; no way to start a search with no example; no refinement that captures why a result was rejected; no representation of mood, restraint, density or era; and no learning from the long unsuccessful sessions that dominate the category.
