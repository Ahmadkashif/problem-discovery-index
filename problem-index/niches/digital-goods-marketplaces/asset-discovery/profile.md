# Asset Discovery

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/digital-goods-marketplaces|Digital Goods Marketplaces]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** This niche is not terminal — matching an aesthetic intent and establishing technical fit are different contests with different winners, and they are stated separately in the sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.9B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~21% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** High — invested in, still failing
**Target Buyer:** Platform discovery engineering
**Automation Potential:** Very High — both halves are model-driven

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A creator whose work is not found does not earn, which makes discovery a livelihood question in this category rather than a convenience one. Visual embedding search is deployed and genuinely better than keyword matching, and buyers still cannot find what they want, because what they want is a style, a mood and a compatibility requirement rather than a subject. Discovery is therefore the second-largest concentration of contested value: it determines which creators have incomes and which do not.

It is also **not terminal**. Writing the contested statement produces two sentences describing different companies winning. One fight is over aesthetic intent — the buyer wants something that feels a particular way and cannot express it in words, which is a perceptual representation and interaction problem with no ground truth. The other is over technical fit — whether this asset works with the buyer's engine version, software release, file format and licence situation, which is a metadata completeness and constraint-satisfaction problem with an unambiguous right answer. A competitor can lead decisively on either while being ordinary at the other. The two contests are stated in the sub-niches.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Creator-supplied tags, keyword search, category browsing, visual embedding similarity, and bestseller ranking. The gaps: no way for a buyer to express an aesthetic intent; no style representation distinct from subject; no compatibility metadata worth filtering on; no notion of what an asset works with; and ranking that concentrates on established sellers.
