# Niche Analysis — Digital Goods Marketplaces

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/digital-goods-marketplaces|Digital Goods Marketplaces]]

## Niche Selection

A digital good copies perfectly at zero cost the moment it is sold, which means every contest in this category is ultimately about defending economics that the product's own nature does not defend. Delivery, payment and entitlement are solved and commoditised; what remains unsolved is who may use the thing, who else has it, whether it can be found, what it was made from, and who bears the cost of finding out. The eight niches below split the category by what competitors actually fight over.

## Why These Niches

Redistribution takes the largest share because it is a direct substitution for sales rather than a leak, and because the cost of responding to it has been pushed onto the individual creator who is least able to bear it. Discovery is second because a creator whose work is not found does not earn, which makes ranking a livelihood question rather than a convenience one. The two low-digitized niches are the layers with no machine representation at all: what a licence permits, and what an asset was made from — the second of which generative tools have made urgent and unresolved. The two underserved audiences are the reviewer adjudicating originality between two files and two assertions, and the support agent explaining an opaque hold to someone whose income has stopped. The two automatable niches are where the platform holds the whole corpus and computes little from it: what creators should make next, and where their work has already gone.

## Filter Notes

Seven of the eight level-1 niches are terminal. Asset Discovery is not: the label names a function rather than a contest, and attempting the contested statement produces two sentences describing different winners. One fight is over matching an aesthetic intent — a style, a mood, a feeling — to a corpus that cannot be described lexically, which is a perceptual representation problem where the buyer cannot state the query. The other is over technical fit: whether this asset works with the buyer's engine version, software release, file format and licence situation, which is a metadata completeness and constraint-checking problem with a right answer. A competitor can win either decisively while being ordinary at the other, which is the test. It therefore decomposes into **Aesthetic & Style Search** and **Compatibility & Technical Fit**.

Two candidates were considered and rejected. **Payment, entitlement and file delivery** is commoditised by the industry's own account, and the contest that exists over it belongs to [[industries/payment-processors|Payment Processors]]. **Course and educational content production** is a genuine contest but it is the contest of [[industries/online-course-platforms|Online Course Platforms]] as an industry, and siting it here would duplicate that analysis.
