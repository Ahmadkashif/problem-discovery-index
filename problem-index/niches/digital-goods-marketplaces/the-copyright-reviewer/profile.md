# The Copyright Reviewer

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/digital-goods-marketplaces|Digital Goods Marketplaces]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give the copyright reviewer evidence about which file came first and what was derived from what — and whoever supplies that turns an unanswerable adjudication into a determinable one.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — two files and two assertions
**Target Buyer:** Trust and safety operations
**Automation Potential:** Medium — evidence gathering automatable, judgement assisted

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A reviewer decides copyright and originality questions at volume, between two creators who both claim the work, on evidence consisting of two files and two assertions. The decision removes one person's listing and their income, and in a category built on originality it is the closest thing to a court. The reviewer has no priority record, no derivation analysis, no upload history across marketplaces, and a policy written for clearer cases. This is distinct from provenance, which asks what an asset is; here two parties both claim the same thing and someone must decide.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Ticket queues, side-by-side file comparison, upload timestamps within the platform, statutory counter-notice processes, and a policy document. The gaps: no cross-platform priority evidence; no structural derivation analysis; no record of a creator's own development history; no precedent; and no feedback on whether decisions held.
