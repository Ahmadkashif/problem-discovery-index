# Creator Opportunity Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/digital-goods-marketplaces|Digital Goods Marketplaces]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell a creator what to make next from the demand the platform already observes — and whoever does that controls the supply side of the marketplace.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.0B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~11% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — the data is held, not used
**Target Buyer:** Platform creator success leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — demand and supply are both fully observed

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The platform observes every search, every rejected result, every abandoned session and every completed purchase, across the whole catalogue, continuously. That is a direct measurement of unmet demand — the thing buyers looked for and could not find — and creators, who spend weeks making things speculatively, are told nothing about it. They guess, make something, and find out months later whether anyone wanted it. This is the category's own corpus problem: the record of exactly what its supply side most needs to know, rendered as a bestsellers page.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Bestseller lists, category trending pages, creator sales dashboards, and community speculation. The gaps: no unmet demand reporting; no saturation measurement; no revenue forecast for a candidate; no view of what buyers searched for and did not buy; and no feedback on why an asset did not sell.
