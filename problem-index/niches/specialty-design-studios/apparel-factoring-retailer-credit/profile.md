# Apparel Factoring & Retailer Credit

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/specialty-design-studios|Specialty Design Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Factors such as CIT Commercial Services, Rosenthal & Rosenthal and Milberg Factors buy apparel and accessory brands' receivables, approve credit on each retailer order before it ships, and carry the risk that the retailer does not pay. Their credit departments research the department stores, specialty chains and boutiques that buy from the brands studios design for. Brands pay a factoring commission and interest.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 credit analysts and collection specialists per large factor (estimate)
**Output:** Order-by-order credit approvals and limits on retailers
**Proprietary data:** Payment behaviour, deductions and chargebacks of thousands of retailers across hundreds of brand clients — a view no single brand has
**Clock:** Order approval before shipment; retailer payment terms
**Buyer:** Chief Credit Officer
