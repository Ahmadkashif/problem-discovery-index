# Exhibition Industry Research & Exhibit Trade Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/specialty-design-studios|Specialty Design Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Center for Exhibition Industry Research (CEIR) publishes exhibitor spending and attendance research — its late-2025 survey found exhibit space takes 40.5% of exhibitor spend — and the Exhibit Designers & Producers Association (EDPA) serves exhibit houses with education and standards.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Fewer than 10 research staff; surveys largely contracted out (estimate)
**Output:** Exhibitor spending and industry index reports for members
**Proprietary data:** Periodic exhibitor and organiser surveys
**Clock:** Self-set publication schedules
**Buyer:** Association leadership
