# Colour Systems & Forecast Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/specialty-design-studios|Specialty Design Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Publishers of colour systems and seasonal colour forecasts used by fashion, textile and product designers to specify colour: Pantone's fashion and home colour system and its Color Institute, Coloro (developed with WGSN), and similar systems. Designers and brands buy guides, physical swatches, digital libraries and forecast subscriptions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 colour forecasters, colour scientists and content staff (estimate)
**Output:** Colour systems, physical standards, seasonal palettes and forecasts
**Proprietary data:** Spectral definitions of thousands of colour standards and an archive of seasonal palettes
**Clock:** Seasonal palette releases ahead of brand range-building
**Buyer:** Head of Colour at the brand; Product Director at the publisher
