# Sourcing Agents & Buying Offices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/specialty-design-studios|Specialty Design Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Sourcing agents and buying offices — Li & Fung is the historical giant, with many Hong Kong, Shanghai, Dhaka and Istanbul agents beside it — place brands' orders with factories, run product development and technical design teams that interpret studios' tech packs, follow up samples and production, and inspect. They earn a commission on the free-on-board value of orders.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-500 merchandisers, technical designers and QC staff per large agent (estimate)
**Output:** Factory placement, costing and development follow-up; the insight is embedded in the service
**Proprietary data:** Factory capability, cost and delivery histories across many brands and categories
**Clock:** Brand calendars and factory production slots
**Buyer:** Head of Sourcing at the brand
