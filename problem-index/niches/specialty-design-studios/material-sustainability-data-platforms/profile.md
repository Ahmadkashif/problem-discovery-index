# Material Sustainability Data Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/specialty-design-studios|Specialty Design Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Platforms and standards bodies that hold environmental and social data on materials, mills and factories for apparel and footwear: Worldly (which runs the Higg facility and product modules developed with Cascale, formerly the Sustainable Apparel Coalition) and Textile Exchange, which publishes material market reports and runs recycled and organic content standards. Brands pay subscriptions or membership to score materials, report emissions and substantiate claims.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-200 analysts, methodologists and data operations staff (estimate)
**Output:** Facility and material impact scores, benchmark reports, material market reports
**Proprietary data:** Years of self-assessed and verified facility modules and material impact data across thousands of suppliers
**Clock:** Brand sustainability reporting cycles and incoming EU product and disclosure rules
**Buyer:** Chief Sustainability Officer at the brand; Head of Data at the platform
