# Fabric Digitization & 3D Material Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/specialty-design-studios|Specialty Design Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Services and hardware vendors that turn physical fabrics into digital materials for 3D garment simulation — scanning appearance and measuring bending, stretch and drape so a fabric behaves correctly in CLO 3D, Browzwear or Style3D. Vizoo and Swatchbook are representative; 3D software vendors run their own fabric kits and libraries. Brands and mills pay per material or by subscription.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 material technicians and digitisation staff (estimate)
**Output:** Digitised material files with physics parameters
**Proprietary data:** Measured physical parameters across thousands of fabrics
**Clock:** Brand digital line-review calendars
**Buyer:** Head of 3D / Digital Product Creation at the brand
