# The Size Run Nobody Fit-Tested

**Niche:** [[niches/specialty-design-studios/footwear-and-leather-goods-design/profile|Footwear & Leather Goods Design]]
**Industry:** [[industries/specialty-design-studios|Specialty Design Studios]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Samples are developed and approved in one sample size, and fit problems in the smallest and largest sizes appear only after the production order ships.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a shoe or bag from sketch to a confirmed last, pattern and wearable sample in the fewest factory rounds, and whoever controls the last and the fit across the size run takes the account.

## The Problem
Development samples are made in a women's 7 or men's 9. Grading to the full run scales the last and pattern by rules that do not hold at the extremes, and a heel that fits in size 7 slips in size 11.

## Why It's Still Broken
Full size-run samples cost money and time that small brands skip, and the grading rules are the factory's, not the studio's.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A size-run risk check at sample approval that flags styles and constructions whose past grading produced fit complaints at the extremes, and recommends which sizes to sample before bulk.

## Who Feels the Pain
Brands absorbing returns in edge sizes; designers blamed for fit problems they never saw; customers outside the sample size.

## Impact If Fixed
Edge-size fit problems caught with two extra samples instead of a season of returns.
