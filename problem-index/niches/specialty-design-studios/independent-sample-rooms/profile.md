# Independent Sample Rooms & Development Centres

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/specialty-design-studios|Specialty Design Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Independent sample rooms in New York, Los Angeles and other garment districts cut patterns and sew prototypes and small runs for studios, founders and brands, charging per sample or per pattern.

## Insight Function
**Size:** No research function; shops of 5-30 patternmakers and sewers (estimate)
**Output:** Physical samples and patterns
**Proprietary data:** Pattern files and sample histories, mostly client-owned
**Clock:** Client sampling deadlines
**Buyer:** Shop owner
