# Brand Licensing Design Direction Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/specialty-design-studios|Specialty Design Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Brand management companies such as Authentic Brands Group and WHP Global own apparel and lifestyle brands and license them to manufacturers and retailers. Their in-house design and creative teams issue seasonal brand books, trend and design direction, and approve licensees' samples; licensees' own designers and outside studios then produce the tech packs.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 design directors, brand managers and approval staff (estimate)
**Output:** Seasonal design direction, brand standards and sample approvals for licensees
**Proprietary data:** Licensee sales and royalty reporting by category, with approval histories
**Clock:** Licensee line reviews and royalty reporting periods
**Buyer:** Chief Brand Officer at the holding company
