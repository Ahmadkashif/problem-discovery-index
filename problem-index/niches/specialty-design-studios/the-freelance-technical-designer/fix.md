# Seven Client Logins Before Breakfast

**Niche:** [[niches/specialty-design-studios/the-freelance-technical-designer/profile|The Freelance Technical Designer]]
**Industry:** [[industries/specialty-design-studios|Specialty Design Studios]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A freelancer starts the day by logging into each client's PLM, email and chat to find out what happened overnight, because nothing aggregates them.
**Tags:** #data-integration #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #worker-facing #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor serving this person is fighting to let one technical designer carry many brands' specs, fit histories and factory threads without rebuilding them per client, and whoever becomes the freelancer's working memory takes the seat.

## The Problem
Factory questions, brand comments and sample updates arrive across many systems and time zones. The freelancer checks each one in turn and copies key items into a personal to-do list, and something is always missed.

## Why It's Still Broken
Each client chooses its own tools, and none is built to share a feed with another client's system.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A personal inbox that pulls notifications from each client's PLM, email and chat channels, groups them by client, style and round, summarises what needs action, and links back to the source system without copying client files.

## Who Feels the Pain
Freelancers spending the first hour of every day on triage; factories waiting; brands assuming their message was seen.

## Impact If Fixed
An hour a day returned, and fewer missed questions that cost a factory day.
