# Fiber Promotion Research Organisations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/specialty-design-studios|Specialty Design Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Fibre promotion bodies such as Cotton Incorporated and The Woolmark Company, funded by grower levies, run consumer research, trend and fabric development services, and technical support for designers and mills, mostly free to the industry to grow demand for their fibre.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-200 researchers, trend analysts, fabric developers and technical staff (estimate)
**Output:** Consumer surveys, trend and fabric direction, technical bulletins — given away to designers and brands
**Proprietary data:** Long-running consumer surveys and fabric development libraries
**Clock:** Seasonal trend cycles, self-set
**Buyer:** Vice President of Research at the promotion body
