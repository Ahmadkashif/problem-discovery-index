# Print Repeat & Colourway Production

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/specialty-design-studios|Specialty Design Studios]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn an approved motif into mill-ready repeats, separations and colourways that strike off right first time, and whoever cuts strike-off rounds takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$200M US fees for textile print and surface design production work — repeats, colour reduction, separations and colourways (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~4% of specialty design fees (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — fully digital artwork, mostly hand-operated in image editors
**Target Buyer:** Print Studio Director; brand Print Lead
**Automation Potential:** High — repeat building, colour reduction and recolouring are rule-bound

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Textile and print designers create motifs and sell them to brands — at trade shows, by studio subscription, or by commission, often around $400 and up per design for exclusive use (practitioner report). Turning a motif into production artwork is a separate, mechanical stage: building a seamless repeat to the mill's repeat size, reducing colours to the screens or channels available, separating colours, generating colourways to the client's palette, and preparing strike-off requests against colour standards. Each strike-off round at the mill adds cost and weeks.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Adobe Photoshop and Illustrator; NedGraphics and Pointcarré for textile-specific repeat, colour reduction and colourways; mill specifications by email. Gaps: colourways recoloured by hand; predicted print colour on the substrate unknown until strike-off; repeat and colour-count errors caught by the mill.
