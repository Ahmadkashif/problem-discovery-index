# Image Editors Adapted to Mill-Ready Separations

**Niche:** [[niches/specialty-design-studios/print-repeat-and-colorway-production/profile|Print Repeat & Colourway Production]]
**Industry:** [[industries/specialty-design-studios|Specialty Design Studios]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Photoshop can do everything a mill needs, by hand; the textile-specific steps — repeat checking, colour reduction to a screen count, separations — are where the hours go.
**Tags:** #k-means-clustering #dimensionality-reduction #cnns #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn an approved motif into mill-ready repeats, separations and colourways that strike off right first time, and whoever cuts strike-off rounds takes the account.

## The Problem
A painted or photographic motif has thousands of colours; a rotary screen print may allow eight to twelve. Reducing colours while keeping the artwork's character, cleaning the separations, and checking the repeat for visible seams takes hours per design.

## What Already Exists
Adobe Photoshop and Illustrator; textile CAD such as NedGraphics and Pointcarré, which include repeat and colour reduction tools operated manually.

## The Customization Gap
Mill-ready output needs: colour reduction that clusters to a target count while preserving edges and motif detail; automatic seam and tile-artefact detection in the repeat; separations formatted to the mill's specification; and validation against repeat size, print width and colour count before files leave the studio.

## Target Customer
Print studios and freelance textile designers delivering production artwork to mills and brands.

## Impact If Solved
Hours per design removed from production artwork, and mill rejections for technical file errors eliminated.
