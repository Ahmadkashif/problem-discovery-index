# Forty Colourways Recoloured by Hand

**Niche:** [[niches/specialty-design-studios/print-repeat-and-colorway-production/profile|Print Repeat & Colourway Production]]
**Industry:** [[industries/specialty-design-studios|Specialty Design Studios]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A buyer asks for every print in five more colourways against the new season palette, and a designer spends days recolouring layers one by one.
**Tags:** #k-means-clustering #diffusion-models #automation #worker-facing #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn an approved motif into mill-ready repeats, separations and colourways that strike off right first time, and whoever cuts strike-off rounds takes the account.

## The Problem
Colourways are recoloured by selecting each separated colour and substituting a palette colour, then judging balance by eye and repeating. Most proposed colourways are rejected at review, so most of the effort is thrown away.

## Why It's Still Broken
Recolouring tools exist but are manual; nothing proposes balanced colourways from a palette and the motif's structure.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Automatic generation of a set of candidate colourways from the separated artwork and the season palette, balanced for contrast and dominant-colour ratios, for the designer to curate and refine.

## Who Feels the Pain
Print designers doing repetitive recolouring at deadline; buyers waiting on boards.

## Impact If Fixed
Days of recolouring reduced to an hour of curation per print family.
