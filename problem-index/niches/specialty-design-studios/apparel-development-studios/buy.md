# 3D Sampling Pointed at Fit Approval, Not Visuals

**Niche:** [[niches/specialty-design-studios/apparel-development-studios/profile|Apparel Design & Development Studios]]
**Industry:** [[industries/specialty-design-studios|Specialty Design Studios]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** 3D garment software can already replace a physical round, and most studios use it for line-review visuals because the fabric, the avatar and the approval workflow are not set up for fit decisions.
**Tags:** #gaussian-processes #transfer-learning #evaluation-metrics #automation #workflow-orchestration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a brand's styles to an approved production sample in the fewest sampling rounds before line review, and whoever converges fastest at a predictable fee takes the account.

## The Problem
Vendors claim 50–80% fewer sample rounds with production-accurate 3D, yet studios still ship physical protos for fit. The simulation looks right but cannot be trusted where it matters — drag lines, ease, how a stretch fabric relaxes — so the fit session still waits for the courier.

## What Already Exists
CLO 3D, Browzwear VStitcher and Style3D simulate garments from patterns with fabric physics; fabric testers and digitisation services measure parameters; avatars come in standard size sets.

## The Customization Gap
Fit approval needs: fabric parameters measured or calibrated for the actual production fabric rather than a library lookalike; an avatar built to the client's own fit model, posture included; a review mode that shows strain and ease maps against the client's tolerances; a record of which 3D fit decisions were later confirmed or overturned by the physical sample, so trust is earned category by category; and corrections exported directly into the factory comment sheet.

## Target Customer
Heads of technical design at studios already licensing CLO or Browzwear who want one physical round removed per style.

## Impact If Solved
Moving the first fit round into 3D on categories where it has proved reliable saves roughly a month per style and a round of sample and courier cost, without asking the client to trust the simulation blindly.
