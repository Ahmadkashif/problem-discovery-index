# The Fit Comment the Factory Never Received

**Niche:** [[niches/specialty-design-studios/apparel-development-studios/profile|Apparel Design & Development Studios]]
**Industry:** [[industries/specialty-design-studios|Specialty Design Studios]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A sample comes back with last round's fault still in it because the correction was in an email the factory's patternmaker never saw.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #automation #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a brand's styles to an approved production sample in the fewest sampling rounds before line review, and whoever converges fastest at a predictable fee takes the account.

## The Problem
Fit comments travel by PLM comment, email attachment, chat message and voice note. The factory's merchandiser receives them, passes some to the patternmaker, and replies "noted." The next sample arrives with one correction made, one ignored and one applied to the wrong size. Prior-round comments not passed on in structured form are a recognised top cause of re-samples.

## Why It's Still Broken
There is no single versioned list of open corrections per style that both sides acknowledge item by item. Each channel is convenient for someone, and nobody reconciles them before the next sample is cut.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A per-style correction ledger: every comment from any channel is extracted into a numbered item, translated, and sent to the factory for item-level acknowledgement before cutting. When the sample arrives, the technical designer gets the open list and checks each item off against the measured sample.

## Who Feels the Pain
Technical designers who discover the miss in the fit room; brands that lose a month; factories blamed for comments they never received.

## Impact If Fixed
Closing the communication gap removes the cheapest-to-prevent class of extra round entirely, at the cost of a few minutes per style per round.
