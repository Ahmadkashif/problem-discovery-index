# Social-Image Trend Prediction Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/specialty-design-studios|Specialty Design Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Vendors that forecast garment and accessory trend adoption quantitatively by running image recognition over social media. Heuritech, founded in Paris in 2013, is representative: it analyses around three million images and videos a day across 2,000+ garment attributes and sells forecasts up to a year ahead by subscription to brands such as Louis Vuitton, Dior and Adidas. Unlike editorial trend forecasting, the output is a numeric adoption curve per attribute.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 data scientists, fashion analysts and client teams (estimate)
**Output:** Attribute-level trend adoption forecasts by audience and region
**Proprietary data:** A labelled attribute taxonomy and years of image-derived trend curves; the source images themselves are public social media
**Clock:** Brand range-building and buying calendars
**Buyer:** Chief Merchandising Officer / Head of Design at the brand
