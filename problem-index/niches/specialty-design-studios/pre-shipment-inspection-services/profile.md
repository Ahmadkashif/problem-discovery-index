# Pre-Shipment Inspection & Factory QC Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/specialty-design-studios|Specialty Design Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Inspection companies such as QIMA, V-Trust and the consumer-products divisions of the large testing groups send inspectors into factories to check garments, footwear and accessories against the tech pack — measurements, workmanship, labelling, packaging — using AQL sampling before shipment, and audit factories for capability and compliance. Brands and importers pay per inspection-day.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-2,000 inspectors, auditors and report reviewers per large provider (estimate)
**Output:** Inspection reports with pass/fail verdicts, defect classifications and photographs; factory audit reports
**Proprietary data:** Defect and measurement histories by factory, category, construction and tech pack across thousands of brands
**Clock:** Ship dates and letters of credit — a failed inspection holds the shipment
**Buyer:** VP of Quality / Director of Sourcing at the brand
