Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Designers win work with renderings. A detailer then rebuilds the design as shop drawings: panel dimensions, joinery, materials, finishes, hardware, graphic templates, crate plans. Dimensions are re-keyed, materials are re-specified, and the graphic panel sizes in the print files drift from the drawings during revision. A detailing team can spend more hours on this than the designer spent on the concept.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
General CAD vendors serve architecture and manufacturing, where component libraries and standard joinery are mature. Exhibit construction mixes custom millwork, aluminium extrusion systems, fabric graphics, lighting and AV, and every house has its own construction standards, so there has been no common library to automate from.

## What to Build
A detailing assistant trained on a house's own past shop drawings: it reads the concept model, proposes construction for each element from the house's standard details and extrusion systems, generates dimensioned shop drawings, a bill of materials, graphic templates with bleeds, and a crate and pack plan, and flags elements with no precedent for a detailer.

## Target Customer
Design and engineering directors at exhibit houses and museum exhibit design firms with in-house detailing teams.

## Impact If Built
Detailing hours drop sharply on repeat construction types, and drawings, materials and graphics stay synchronised because they come from one model.
