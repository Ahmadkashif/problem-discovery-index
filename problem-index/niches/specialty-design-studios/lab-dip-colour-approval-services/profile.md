# Lab-Dip & Colour Approval Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/specialty-design-studios|Specialty Design Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Third-party colour services that create and distribute physical and digital colour standards for retailers and brands and assess the lab dips and strike-offs mills submit against them, approving or rejecting each with comments. Color Solutions International, a DyStar division, is representative; large retailers run equivalent in-house colour departments. Brands pay per standard and per assessment, and the approval gates bulk dyeing and, ultimately, shipment.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-200 colour technicians, assessors and colour scientists (estimate)
**Output:** Colour standards and approve/reject decisions with corrective comments on every submission
**Proprietary data:** Paired spectrophotometer readings and visual decisions across thousands of submissions, mills, substrates and dye classes — the record of where instrumental tolerances and trained eyes disagree
**Clock:** Brand development and bulk production calendars; a late approval holds dyeing and risks the ship date
**Buyer:** VP of Colour / Head of Colour Services

**Kill switches:** none — the colour standards are the brand's, but the assessment record is generated by the service; client confidentiality limits pooling across brands without being a procurement gate.
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold; the strongest near-miss in this industry. The approve/reject call is a genuine expert judgement made at high volume against shipment gates, but it is invoiced as a per-submission service fee rather than as an insight product, and the buyer pool is a few dozen large retail colour departments.
