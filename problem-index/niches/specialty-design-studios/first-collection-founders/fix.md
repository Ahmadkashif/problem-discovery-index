# The Sample Approved From a Factory Photo

**Niche:** [[niches/specialty-design-studios/first-collection-founders/profile|First-Collection Brand Founders]]
**Industry:** [[industries/specialty-design-studios|Specialty Design Studios]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** To save courier time, a founder approves a pre-production sample from photos the factory sends, and the bulk order arrives with measurements out of tolerance.
**Tags:** #cnns #large-language-models #quick-win #automation #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a founder with no product development experience from a mood board to a first production run at factory minimums they can afford, and whoever makes that first order arrive right takes the account and the founder's next collections.

## The Problem
Factory photos show a garment on a hanger or a table, flattering and unmeasured. Founders without a fit model or a tape measure approve what looks right. The faults — short body length, narrow sleeve opening, mismatched colour — surface in bulk.

## Why It's Still Broken
The founder does not know what to ask for, and the factory has no reason to volunteer measurements.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A remote approval checklist sent to the factory: measured photos of each point of measure against a ruler, flat-lay views in standard positions, colour under daylight next to the approved swatch, and an automatic comparison against the tech pack's tolerances before the founder can approve.

## Who Feels the Pain
Founders holding unsellable inventory; factories blamed for unclear instructions; studios called in to rescue the second order.

## Impact If Fixed
A wrong bulk order — often the founder's entire first-season inventory — avoided with a ten-minute checklist.
