# Event General Contractor Data Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/specialty-design-studios|Specialty Design Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The general service contractors that run trade show floors for organisers — Freeman and GES are the largest in the US — supply installation labour, drayage, furnishings, rigging and electrical to every exhibitor, and increasingly sell event data and strategy services to organisers and exhibitors. Their exhibitor service desks see every booth's orders, labour and freight.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 analysts and strategists in data and insight practices (estimate)
**Output:** Event performance and attendee analytics for organisers; exhibitor benchmarks
**Proprietary data:** Labour, drayage and order histories across thousands of exhibitors and shows; attendee and engagement data
**Clock:** Show dates, fixed months in advance
**Buyer:** Show organiser's Chief Experience Officer; contractor's Head of Data
