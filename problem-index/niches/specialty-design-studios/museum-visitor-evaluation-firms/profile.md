# Museum Visitor Research & Exhibit Evaluation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/specialty-design-studios|Specialty Design Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Research firms that study how visitors use exhibits — front-end studies before design, formative testing of prototypes, and summative evaluation after opening — for museums, science centres and the designers who build their galleries. RK&A and Knology are representative. Funders such as federal science and museum grant programmes often require evaluation, and the evaluation report is the deliverable.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 researchers and evaluators per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Front-end, formative and summative evaluation reports with recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Visitor observation, tracking and interview data across many exhibitions — though reports are usually client-owned and frequently published by grant requirement
**Clock:** Grant reporting deadlines and exhibition opening dates
**Buyer:** Director of Exhibitions or Director of Evaluation at the museum
