# Apparel & Children's Product Safety Regulators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/specialty-design-studios|Specialty Design Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Consumer Product Safety Commission (children's product certification, tracking labels, flammability and drawstring rules), the Federal Trade Commission (fiber content, origin, RN and care labelling), and Customs and Border Protection (textile classification and forced-labour import enforcement) set and enforce the rules every tech pack's labelling and construction must meet.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Hundreds of analysts, inspectors and compliance staff across the agencies (estimate)
**Output:** Rules, guidance, recalls, enforcement actions and detentions
**Proprietary data:** Incident reports, recall files, import and detention records
**Clock:** Statutory and enforcement timelines
**Buyer:** Agency division leadership
