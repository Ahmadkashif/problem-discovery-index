# A Show-Season Total Cost Forecaster

**Niche:** [[niches/specialty-design-studios/trade-show-exhibit-programs/profile|Trade Show Exhibit Programmes]]
**Industry:** [[industries/specialty-design-studios|Specialty Design Studios]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** An exhibitor approves a booth design without a forecast of what it will cost to ship, install, dismantle and store at each of the twenty shows it will travel to.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #monte-carlo-methods #confidence-intervals #feature-engineering #revenue-impact #evaluation-metrics
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver a client's whole show calendar at the lowest total cost per show — refurbishment, shipping, drayage, storage and install labour — while the exhibit still looks new, and whoever predicts and controls those floor costs best keeps the programme.

## The Problem
The build cost of a custom exhibit is quoted precisely; its operating cost is not. Every show adds outbound and return freight, drayage priced by weight, installation and dismantle labour at the venue's rates and jurisdiction rules, rigging, electrical, storage and refurbishment. A design with more components, heavier crates or complex connections can cost far more over its life than a slightly pricier design that installs in half the time — and nobody computes this at design review.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The cost data is spread across the exhibit house's invoices, the general service contractor's bills to the exhibitor, freight carriers and the warehouse. Each party sees one slice, and labour rates and rules vary by venue and city.

## What to Build
A forecaster trained on the house's past programmes: from a design's component list, crate plan and the client's show calendar, predict labour hours, drayage weight, freight and storage per show, with intervals from Monte Carlo over venue rates and crew performance, and compare design alternatives and rent-versus-own options over the exhibit's life.

## Target Customer
Account directors at exhibit houses selling multi-year programmes, and corporate exhibit managers defending budgets.

## Impact If Built
Design decisions made on total cost of ownership, with fewer budget overruns surfacing on the show floor and a measurable reason for a client to renew the programme.
