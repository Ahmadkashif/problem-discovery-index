# Asset Tracking Adapted to Exhibit Property

**Niche:** [[niches/specialty-design-studios/trade-show-exhibit-programs/profile|Trade Show Exhibit Programmes]]
**Industry:** [[industries/specialty-design-studios|Specialty Design Studios]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Generic warehouse and asset-tracking software knows where a crate is; an exhibit programme needs to know what is in it, what condition it is in and which booth configuration it belongs to.
**Tags:** #object-detection #cnns #data-integration #compliance #automation #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver a client's whole show calendar at the lowest total cost per show — refurbishment, shipping, drayage, storage and install labour — while the exhibit still looks new, and whoever predicts and controls those floor costs best keeps the programme.

## The Problem
An exhibit property is hundreds of components — panels, extrusions, counters, lightboxes, graphics, flooring, hardware — packed into crates and reconfigured for 10x10, 20x20 and island spaces. Parts go missing, get damaged in transit, or get pulled for another client's show, and the gap is found at the booth space.

## What Already Exists
Barcode and RFID asset-tracking systems and warehouse management software track items in, out and by location.

## The Customization Gap
Exhibit property needs: kit definitions per booth configuration so a pull sheet is generated from the drawing; condition photographs on return with damage detected and refurbishment costed; show history per component; flame-retardancy certificates attached to fabrics and materials for the fire marshal; and reconciliation of what was packed against what came back.

## Target Customer
Warehouse and account managers at exhibit houses holding property for dozens of corporate clients.

## Impact If Solved
Missing and damaged components caught at the warehouse rather than on the show floor, and refurbishment priced from evidence.
