# Trade Show Exhibit Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/specialty-design-studios|Specialty Design Studios]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver a client's whole show calendar at the lowest total cost per show — refurbishment, shipping, drayage, storage and install labour — while the exhibit still looks new, and whoever predicts and controls those floor costs best keeps the programme.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$800M US design and programme management fees for corporate trade show exhibits (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~17% of specialty design fees (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — models are digital; labour, drayage and property history are spreadsheets and invoices
**Target Buyer:** Corporate Exhibit Manager / Trade Show Manager
**Automation Potential:** High for estimating and rules checking

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A corporate exhibitor runs a calendar of shows a year with one or two reusable exhibit properties, reconfigured for different booth sizes. CEIR's latest survey puts exhibit space at 40.5% of exhibitor spend, and 52% of exhibitors plan to reuse their booths as they are. The design job is therefore less a one-off creation than a programme: which components go to which show, what needs refurbishing, what to rent, and how to keep installation labour and drayage — billed by the hour and by weight on each show floor — inside budget.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Vectorworks or SketchUp for design; exhibit-house job costing; general service contractors' order portals; warehouse inventory by spreadsheet or barcode. Gaps: no forecast of per-show total cost before the season; no link between design choices and the labour they cause.
