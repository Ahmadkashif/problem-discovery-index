# Billing Company Benchmarking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/medical-billing|Medical Billing Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Consultancies and associations collect operating data from third-party billing companies and publish the benchmarks — collection rate, days in accounts receivable, cost to collect — the industry sells against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-25 analysts and consultants
**Output:** Operating benchmarks, cost-to-collect studies, pricing guidance, peer comparison
**Proprietary data:** Contributed operating data from participating billing companies
**Clock:** Annual reporting cycles
**Buyer:** Principal — partly member funded
