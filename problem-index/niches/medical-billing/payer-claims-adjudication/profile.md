# Payer Claims Adjudication & Policy

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/medical-billing|Medical Billing Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Health plan payment integrity and medical policy organizations write the coverage and edit rules that determine which claims pay, and adjudicate the ones that do not — the entity whose behaviour the entire billing industry exists to predict.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-2,000 medical policy staff, payment integrity analysts, and clinical reviewers
**Output:** Coverage and medical policy, claim edit rules, payment integrity findings, appeal determinations
**Proprietary data:** Complete claim, edit, denial, and appeal history across the plan's entire membership
**Clock:** Prompt payment statutes and appeal timelines
**Buyer:** VP of Payment Integrity / Chief Medical Officer
