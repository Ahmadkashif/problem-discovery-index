## What They Do
Federal contractors run medical review, improper payment measurement, and recovery audit across Medicare and Medicaid, setting the enforcement standard every billing practice calibrates against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-2,000 clinical reviewers, statisticians, and data analysts
**Output:** Improper payment rate measurement, medical review determinations, recovery findings, provider education
**Proprietary data:** National claims with review determinations and improper payment findings
**Clock:** Statutory review and appeal timelines
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — federal contract
