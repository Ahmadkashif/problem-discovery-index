# Healthcare Price Transparency Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/medical-billing|Medical Billing Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
New data vendors assemble the negotiated rate files payers and hospitals are now required to publish, normalize them across incompatible formats, and sell the resulting picture of what each payer actually pays each provider for each service — information that was confidential until the transparency rules.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-250 data engineers, healthcare economists, and analysts
**Output:** Normalized negotiated rate data, provider and payer rate benchmarking, contract modelling, market analysis
**Proprietary data:** Assembly and normalization over enormous published files that are public in principle and unusable in practice
**Clock:** Monthly file publication cycles and payer contract negotiation seasons
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / Head of Product
