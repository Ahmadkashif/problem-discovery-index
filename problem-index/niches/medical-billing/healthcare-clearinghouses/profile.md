# Healthcare Claims Clearinghouses

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/medical-billing|Medical Billing Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Clearinghouses route claims from providers to payers and remittances back, editing claims against payer rules before submission. Pass 1 identifies them as the infrastructure the industry runs on.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,500 edit content staff, payer connectivity engineers, and data analysts
**Output:** Claim edits and scrubbing rules, rejection analytics, payer connectivity, eligibility and status transactions
**Proprietary data:** Claim and remittance flow across most US payers and providers — the only view of how the whole adjudication market behaves
**Clock:** Daily submission cycles and payer processing windows
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Network
