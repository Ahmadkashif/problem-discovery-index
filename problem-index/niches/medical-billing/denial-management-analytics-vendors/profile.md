# Denial Management Analytics Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/medical-billing|Medical Billing Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Specialist vendors sell the denial prediction and appeal automation tools billing companies use to attack what Pass 1 identifies as the industry's heaviest manual burden — denial management and accounts receivable follow-up.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 data scientists, denial content specialists, and analysts
**Output:** Denial prediction, appeal letter generation, payer rule content, worklist prioritization
**Proprietary data:** Denial reasons and appeal outcomes across client books, plus maintained payer rule content
**Clock:** Timely filing and appeal deadlines
**Buyer:** Chief Product Officer / VP of Data Science
