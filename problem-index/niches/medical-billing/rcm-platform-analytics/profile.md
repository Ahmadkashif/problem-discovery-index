# Revenue Cycle Platform Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/medical-billing|Medical Billing Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer

## What They Do
The large revenue cycle management platforms bill and collect for hospitals and physician groups at national scale, and their analytics organizations produce the denial, yield, and collection analysis clients renew on.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-2,000 data scientists, denial analysts, and revenue integrity staff
**Output:** Denial root cause analysis, payer behaviour reporting, yield and collection forecasting, coding and documentation improvement
**Proprietary data:** Claims, remittances, denials, appeals, and outcomes across hundreds of providers and every major payer
**Clock:** Timely filing limits and appeal deadlines
**Buyer:** Chief Analytics Officer
