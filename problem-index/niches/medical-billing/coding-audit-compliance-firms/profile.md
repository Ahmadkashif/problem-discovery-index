# Coding Audit & Compliance Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/medical-billing|Medical Billing Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Independent auditors review provider coding and documentation against the record, quantifying under- and over-coding exposure and defending providers in payer and government audits.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-250 certified coders, auditors, and compliance specialists
**Output:** Coding audit findings, documentation improvement recommendations, audit defence, compliance programme design
**Proprietary data:** Coding error patterns across many providers and specialties, joined to payer audit outcomes
**Clock:** Audit response deadlines and annual compliance cycles
**Buyer:** Practice leader
