# Early Value Prediction

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/app-marketing-firms|App Marketing Firms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to predict six-month value from a delayed, coarsened and partly suppressed early signal — and whoever does that accurately bids on better information than everyone else in the auction.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$900M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of category fees and salaries
**Digital Adoption:** High — sophisticated and unvalidated
**Target Buyer:** UA data science leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — a modelling contest

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Given whatever the schema encodes, the modelling contest is to predict eventual value from it under delay, censoring and suppression, quickly enough to bid. This is a continuously refit statistical problem with a clear objective, it is owned by data scientists rather than by whoever configured the integration, and a team can be excellent at it while working with a poorly designed schema — or the reverse. That separation is what makes it a distinct fight from the schema design it depends on.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Predictive lifetime value models fitted to early signals, network-side optimisation with its own models, cohort extrapolation, and vendor-supplied prediction products. The gaps: models validated on fit rather than on realised value; delay treated as latency rather than as structure; suppressed cohorts excluded rather than modelled; no uncertainty carried into the bid; and no measurement of prediction error over time.
