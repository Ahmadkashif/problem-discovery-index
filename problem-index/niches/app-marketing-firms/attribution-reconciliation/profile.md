# Attribution Reconciliation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/app-marketing-firms|App Marketing Firms]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce one allocation from four sources that each report a different number — and whoever does that credibly replaces a weekly judgement call on a seven-figure budget.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.1B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~18% of category fees and salaries
**Digital Adoption:** Low — four numbers and a Monday
**Target Buyer:** UA management and finance
**Automation Potential:** Very High — reconciliation is computable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every network reports its own installs, the measurement partner reports different ones, the aggregated platform signal reports something late, finance reports the revenue, and the user acquisition manager allocates a seven-figure budget across the disagreement every Monday. The disagreement has knowable causes — self-attribution rules, claim windows, view-through policies, aggregation lag — and nobody computes them. This is distinct from the prediction niche: that estimates what a user is worth, this decides which source to believe about where the users came from.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Network dashboards, measurement partner reporting, aggregated platform postbacks, finance records, and a weekly spreadsheet. The gaps: self-attributing networks double-claiming; incompatible claim windows; no deduplication; aggregation arriving after the decision; and a reconciliation performed by judgement under a deadline.
