# The Lift Study the Network Ran

**Niche:** [[niches/app-marketing-firms/incrementality-verification/profile|Incrementality Verification]]
**Industry:** [[industries/app-marketing-firms|App Marketing Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The network offers a free lift study, designs it, selects the holdout, analyses it and reports a strong result, and the budget increases.
**Tags:** #hypothesis-testing #causal-inference #confidence-intervals #evaluation-metrics #compliance #quick-win #descriptive-statistics #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to establish how much of a reported return survives an experiment — and whoever does that in a channel where attribution is aggregated supplies the only number anyone can actually trust.

## The Problem
The network's account team offers to run an incrementality study at no cost. They define the holdout, run it on their own infrastructure, choose the analysis window, decide which conversions count, and present the result. It shows meaningful lift. The advertiser increases the budget. Nobody outside the network saw the assignment, the raw data or the analysis, and nobody knows whether other studies were run and not reported. This is the standard way incrementality is measured in this channel, and it is a measurement produced entirely by the party being measured, about its own performance, for the purpose of a budget decision.

## Why It's Still Broken
Free is compelling and independent testing costs real suppressed spend, so the conflicted study wins on price — the cheaper measurement drives out the credible one. Advertisers lack the design capability to run their own. The methodology is described as proprietary, which forecloses inspection. And a study showing no lift is simply not presented, so the visible record is selected.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Separate the measurer from the seller. Hold the randomisation independently, or at minimum verify the assignment, which is the fix and is the structural change without which nothing else matters. Pre-register the design, metric and analysis before the study runs, since flexibility afterwards is how an honest procedure produces a favourable result. Require the raw assignment and outcome data so the analysis can be reproduced, which is the minimum an advertiser should demand and rarely does. Count every study run, including unreported ones, because selective presentation is the largest distortion and is invisible by design. Use the advertiser's own outcome data rather than the network's conversion definition. Apply a consistent method across networks, since comparing one network's lift methodology to another's compares nothing. Refuse to act on underpowered results, which would disqualify many of these studies immediately. Sanity-check against the aggregate, because a set of network lift figures that sums beyond the business's total growth is arithmetically impossible and this check is almost never run. Use the network study as a hypothesis and test the important ones independently, which is a proportionate use of a limited budget. And publish an industry standard for lift study conduct, because every advertiser negotiating this alone is how the current practice became normal.

## Who Feels the Pain
Advertisers increasing budgets on studies they cannot inspect; networks with honest measurement losing to flattering measurement; and a channel whose only causal evidence is produced by the sellers.

## Impact If Fixed
The cheaper measurement drives out the credible one, and the seller designs, runs, analyses and selectively reports its own evidence. Independent randomisation with a pre-registered design is the structural change, and summing network lift against total business growth is an arithmetic check nobody runs.
