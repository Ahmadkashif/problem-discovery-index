# The Creative Producer

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/app-marketing-firms|App Marketing Firms]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell the person making the work which of it succeeded and why — and whoever does that turns a production treadmill into a craft that improves.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$450M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of category fees and salaries
**Digital Adoption:** Low — output measured, learning absent
**Target Buyer:** Creative and growth leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — the feedback loop is buildable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A motion designer produces dozens of ad variants a week, sends them into a system that tells them which one spent, and never learns which one worked or why. They are producing the input to the only remaining lever the platforms have not automated, at industrial volume, with no feedback. This is distinct from creative measurement as a systems problem: that builds the attribution, this is about the person whose craft cannot develop because nothing comes back.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Production tooling, asset delivery into network interfaces, spend reports by asset identifier, and occasional creative reviews. The gaps: spend reported as if it were performance; no concept-level attribution reaching the designer; no explanation of why something worked; feedback arriving weeks later if at all; and a craft that cannot compound.
