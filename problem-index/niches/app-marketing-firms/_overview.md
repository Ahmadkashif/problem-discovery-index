# Niche Analysis — App Marketing Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/app-marketing-firms|App Marketing Firms]]

## Niche Selection

This is the one corner of marketing where the measurement constraint was imposed deliberately by a platform owner rather than emerging from neglect. The response has been genuinely inventive — conversion value schemas, predictive lifetime value from the first hours, aggregated modelling — and what has not followed is rigour about what those inventions cost. The discipline optimises hard against numbers it has not validated, expressed by unusually numerate people under an unusually severe information constraint. The eight niches below split the category by what competitors actually fight over.

## Why These Niches

Predictive value takes the largest share because the whole economics of the job is a payback question and the signal needed to answer it now arrives as a handful of bits with a random delay. Attribution reconciliation is second because a seven-figure budget is allocated every Monday across four sources that disagree. The two low-digitized niches are the ones with real money and almost no method: the store listing that converts every click, and the incrementality testing that would say whether any of the reported return is real. The two underserved audiences are the manager allocating across a disagreement and the designer who never learns which of their work succeeded. The two automatable niches are the creative measurement the aggregation broke and the monetisation model that determines what a user is worth.

## Filter Notes

Seven of the eight level-1 niches are terminal. Predictive Value Under Aggregation is not: the label names a problem area rather than a contest, and writing the contested statement produces two sentences describing different winners. Designing the conversion value schema is an information design problem — a handful of bits must encode whatever the team most needs to know about an early user, and the choice determines what every downstream model can ever learn. Predicting six-month value from those bits is a modelling problem under delay, coarsening and suppression. One is decided once and constrains everything; the other is refit continuously within the constraint. They require different skills, are owned by different people, and a team can be excellent at one while being poor at the other. It therefore decomposes into **Conversion Value Schema Design** and **Early Value Prediction**.

Two candidates were considered and rejected. **The mobile measurement platform itself** is a real contest but belongs to [[industries/marketing-attribution-vendors|Marketing Attribution Vendors]], where the measurement question is analysed in general. **In-app monetisation and ad mediation** shares personnel but is the contest of [[industries/mobile-game-publishers|Mobile Game Publishers]], where the economics of the app rather than its acquisition are decided.
