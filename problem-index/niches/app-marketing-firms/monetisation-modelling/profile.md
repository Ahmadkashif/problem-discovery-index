# Monetisation Modelling

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/app-marketing-firms|App Marketing Firms]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to establish what a user is actually worth over their life, across purchases, subscriptions and advertising revenue — and whoever does that correctly sets the number every acquisition decision divides by.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$350M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~6% of category fees and salaries
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — modelled, rarely validated
**Target Buyer:** Product and monetisation leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — a well-posed forecasting problem

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Everything in acquisition divides by a lifetime value figure. That figure combines in-app purchases with a heavy tail, subscription renewals with their own retention dynamics, and advertising revenue that depends on a mediation stack and fluctuating rates — three quite different processes summed into one number and used as the denominator of every decision. It is owned by product or monetisation rather than by acquisition, is frequently computed by a different method from the one acquisition assumes, and is validated about as often as everything else in this category.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Cohort curve extrapolation, payback tables, analytics platform lifetime value features, and separate treatment of advertising revenue. The gaps: curve extrapolation without uncertainty; the revenue tail underestimated by averages; subscription and purchase dynamics modelled identically; advertising revenue attributed by rough allocation; and a figure that acquisition and finance compute differently.
