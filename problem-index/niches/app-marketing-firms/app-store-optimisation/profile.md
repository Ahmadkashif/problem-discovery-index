# App Store Optimisation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/app-marketing-firms|App Marketing Firms]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to improve the one page that converts every paid click and every organic browse — and whoever tests it properly gets a multiplier on all acquisition spend rather than an improvement to one channel.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$800M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% of category fees and salaries
**Digital Adoption:** Low — keyword tools and quarterly tests
**Target Buyer:** Growth and product leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — testing and content generation are automatable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The store listing converts every paid click and every organic browse, is optimised with keyword tools built on estimated volumes, and is tested with store experiments most teams run once a quarter. It is the single highest-leverage surface in the discipline because it multiplies every acquisition channel simultaneously, and it receives a fraction of the attention that campaign optimisation does. This is distinct from the measurement niches because the store's own experimentation gives clean causal answers that the rest of the discipline cannot obtain.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Keyword research tools on estimated volumes, store experiment features used occasionally, competitor monitoring, and creative assets produced for campaigns and reused. The gaps: keyword volumes that are estimates presented as facts; experiments run rarely and often underpowered; localisation treated as translation; the relationship between paid traffic and listing conversion unexamined; and no systematic testing programme.
