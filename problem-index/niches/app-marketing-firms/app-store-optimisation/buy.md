# Conversion Optimisation Practice

**Niche:** [[niches/app-marketing-firms/app-store-optimisation/profile|App Store Optimisation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/app-marketing-firms|App Marketing Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Web conversion optimisation is a mature discipline with continuous testing programmes, and the app store listing gets a quarterly screenshot swap.
**Tags:** #hypothesis-testing #confidence-intervals #evaluation-metrics #monte-carlo-methods #descriptive-statistics #automation #revenue-impact #causal-inference
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to improve the one page that converts every paid click and every organic browse — and whoever tests it properly gets a multiplier on all acquisition spend rather than an improvement to one channel.

## The Problem
Conversion rate optimisation on the web is a developed practice: a hypothesis backlog prioritised by expected impact, continuous experimentation with proper statistics, qualitative research feeding quantitative tests, and a measured programme-level return. Teams run it continuously because the compounding effect is well established. The app store listing is the equivalent surface with higher leverage — it converts every channel at once — and is optimised sporadically with campaign leftovers.

## What Already Exists
Hypothesis backlogs with impact prioritisation; continuous experimentation programmes; sequential and Bayesian testing methods; qualitative research feeding test design; and programme-level return measurement.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a page the team does not host and cannot instrument. It requires: (1) experimentation through the store's own limited tooling, with fewer variants, longer cycles and less control than a web test — the practice must be adapted to a constrained instrument rather than assuming a testing platform; (2) no analytics on the page itself, so the funnel is a black box between impression and install and the diagnostic layer web optimisation relies on is absent; (3) traffic composition that is a mix of paid and organic with different intent, where the test result is an average over populations that should be separated; (4) approval and review processes between a change and its appearance, which slows the cycle beyond anything web practice contends with; and (5) a downstream quality consequence, since a listing can raise installs and lower cohort value, which web conversion practice rarely has to weigh.

## Target Customer
Growth and product teams, app marketing agencies, and conversion optimisation practitioners for whom the store listing is an unserved surface.

## Impact If Solved
Web practice runs continuous programmes because compounding is established, and the higher-leverage store surface gets quarterly attention. A constrained testing instrument with no on-page analytics is what the practice must be adapted to rather than assumed away.
