# The UA Manager

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/app-marketing-firms|App Marketing Firms]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give the manager a defensible allocation instead of a weekly judgement across four disagreeing sources — and whoever does that changes how a large share of mobile advertising spend is decided.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category fees and salaries
**Digital Adoption:** Low — experience and a spreadsheet
**Target Buyer:** Growth leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — the allocation is an optimisation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The user acquisition manager allocates a seven-figure budget every week across sources that disagree, using predictions nobody has scored, under a measurement regime that removed most of their visibility, with full accountability for the payback. They are among the most quantitatively capable people in marketing and are working with the weakest information of anyone in it. Their judgement is the actual allocation mechanism for a large share of a ninety-billion-dollar market, and it exists only in their head.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Network dashboards, measurement partner reporting, spreadsheets, predictive models of varying maturity, and accumulated experience. The gaps: no consolidated allocation tooling; adjustments applied by instinct with no record; no record of past decisions against outcomes; no support for the weekly decision; and expertise that leaves with the person.
