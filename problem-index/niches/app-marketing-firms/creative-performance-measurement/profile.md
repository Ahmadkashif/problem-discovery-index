# Creative Performance Measurement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/app-marketing-firms|App Marketing Firms]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to establish which creative actually worked when attribution arrives aggregated — and whoever does that recovers the measurement the privacy regime removed from the one lever still worth pulling.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$500M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of category fees and salaries
**Digital Adoption:** Low — spend as a proxy
**Target Buyer:** UA and creative leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — a measurement and modelling contest

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Networks consume dozens of video and playable variants a week and report which one spent, which under aggregated attribution is not the same as which one worked. Creative is the lever the platforms have not automated away and the one whose measurement the privacy regime damaged most, because creative-level breakdowns are exactly what aggregation removes. The contest is to recover a usable creative-level signal from what remains, which is a measurement problem with a clear objective and is separable from who makes the creative.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Spend-based creative reporting, network-side creative optimisation, occasional controlled tests, and creative tagging of varying quality. The gaps: no creative dimension in aggregated attribution; network allocation substituted for outcome; variants too numerous and individually too small to measure; no concept-level aggregation; and no controlled testing practice.
