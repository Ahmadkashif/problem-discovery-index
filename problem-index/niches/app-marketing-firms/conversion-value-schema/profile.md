# Conversion Value Schema Design

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/app-marketing-firms|App Marketing Firms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to decide what a handful of bits should encode about an early user — and whoever designs that against measured information content sets the ceiling on everything the team can ever learn.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$700M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of category fees and salaries
**Digital Adoption:** Low — chosen once at launch
**Target Buyer:** UA and data leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — an information design problem

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The conversion value is a design problem handed to the marketer. A small number of bits must encode whatever the team most needs to know about an early user, and the choice — revenue bucket, event sequence, retention proxy — determines what every downstream model can ever learn. Most teams picked a schema at launch and have not revisited it. This is an information design contest with a measurable objective and no modelling in it, decided once and constraining everything after, which makes it entirely separable from the prediction problem it feeds.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Schema templates from measurement vendors, revenue bucketing conventions, event-based encodings, and a configuration set at launch. The gaps: no measurement of a schema's information content; no comparison between candidate schemas; buckets chosen by convention; no revision as the app's economics change; and a decision made once by whoever was configuring the integration.
