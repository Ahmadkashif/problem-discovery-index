# Independent Crop Consultants

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/crop-farming|Crop Farming]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Independent certified crop advisers scout fields and write recommendations for a fee rather than for a commission on inputs, which makes them the purest instance of agronomic insight sold as insight. They are also almost always one to five people.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1-8 per practice
**Output:** Scouting reports, agronomic recommendations, prescription maps
**Proprietary data:** Multi-year field history for a defined client base, held by the individual consultant
**Clock:** The growing season and the intervention windows within it
**Buyer:** The consultant, who is usually the whole firm
