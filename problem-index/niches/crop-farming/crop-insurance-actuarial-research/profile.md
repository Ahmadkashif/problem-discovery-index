# Crop Insurance Actuarial & Rating Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/crop-farming|Crop Farming]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The actuarial teams supporting the federal crop insurance programme — at the rating agency contractors and the approved insurance providers — set premium rates by county, crop, and practice, model yield distributions, and analyze loss experience across the entire programme. Their rates determine what a farmer pays for the risk transfer that makes lending possible.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 actuaries, statisticians, and agricultural economists
**Output:** Rate methodology, yield distribution models, loss experience analyses
**Proprietary data:** Unit-level yield and indemnity history across every insured acre in the country, spanning decades
**Clock:** Statutory rate filing and programme deadlines tied to the crop year
**Buyer:** Chief Actuary / Director of Programme Research
