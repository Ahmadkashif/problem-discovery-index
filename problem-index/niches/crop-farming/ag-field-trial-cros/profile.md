# Agricultural Field Trial Contract Research Organizations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/crop-farming|Crop Farming]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Eurofins Agroscience, Smithers, and the specialist field trial CROs run the good laboratory practice studies that support crop protection registration — efficacy, residue, environmental fate, and worker exposure trials across multiple sites and seasons. The study report is the deliverable and no product reaches a farmer's field without one.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,500 field scientists, study directors, analysts, and quality staff
**Output:** GLP study reports supporting registration submissions
**Proprietary data:** Trial execution and analytical data across crops, geographies, and seasons — sponsor-owned under study contracts
**Clock:** Registration submission deadlines and the growing season, which cannot be rescheduled
**Buyer:** VP of Scientific Services / Managing Director
