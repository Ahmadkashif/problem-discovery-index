# Farm Management Companies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/crop-farming|Crop Farming]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Farmers National Company and comparable firms manage farmland on behalf of absentee owners — selecting and monitoring operators, setting lease structures, approving agronomic programmes, and producing the annual reporting owners make decisions on. They also appraise and market farmland.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-200 farm managers, appraisers, and analysts
**Output:** Management reports, lease and operator analyses, farmland appraisals
**Proprietary data:** Multi-year field performance under known operators and lease structures across a large managed portfolio — the only dataset linking management practice to realized return
**Clock:** Crop year, lease renewal dates, and land transaction timelines
**Buyer:** President / Director of Farm Management
