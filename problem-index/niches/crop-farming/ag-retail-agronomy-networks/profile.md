# Agricultural Retail Agronomy Networks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/crop-farming|Crop Farming]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Nutrien Ag Solutions, GROWMARK, and the large agricultural retailers employ thousands of agronomists who scout fields, write prescriptions, and advise growers on what to apply and when. The agronomy is the reason growers buy inputs from them rather than from a cheaper channel, and it is delivered as a service attached to product sales.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1,000-5,000 agronomists and crop advisers, with 50-200 in agronomic research and analytics
**Output:** Field prescriptions, scouting reports, trial results, agronomic recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Field-level application, scouting, and yield outcome data across millions of acres and years, plus a proprietary trial network
**Clock:** The growing season, which is absolute
**Buyer:** VP of Agronomy / Director of Agronomic Research
