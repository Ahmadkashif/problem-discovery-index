# Commodity Checkoff Research Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/crop-farming|Crop Farming]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Checkoff-funded bodies such as the soybean, corn, and cotton boards commission and conduct agronomic, market development, and utilization research funded by a statutory levy on production. Cotton Incorporated in particular runs a substantial in-house research operation spanning agriculture and end-use.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-200 researchers, agronomists, and programme staff at the larger boards
**Output:** Agronomic research, market development studies, utilization research — published to the industry
**Proprietary data:** Commissioned research archives and long-running production and consumer studies
**Clock:** Programme funding cycles and annual research calendars
**Buyer:** Director of Research — levy-funded rather than commercial
