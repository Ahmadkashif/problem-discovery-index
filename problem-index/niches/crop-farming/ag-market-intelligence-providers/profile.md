# Agricultural Market Intelligence Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/crop-farming|Crop Farming]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
DTN, S&P Global's agriculture desk, StoneX research, and comparable providers collect cash grain bids from thousands of elevators, run proprietary weather and crop models, and publish the price analysis, basis data, and yield forecasts that farmers, elevators, and processors trade on. The Pass 1 analysis identifies grain marketing as the financial decision farmers make with the least adequate data; this is who sells that data.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 market analysts, meteorologists, crop modellers, and data staff
**Output:** Cash bid and basis data, price forecasts, crop condition and yield analysis — the subscription
**Proprietary data:** An elevator-level cash bid network built over decades, proprietary weather modelling, and a long record of the firm's own crop estimates against realized outcomes
**Clock:** Market open, government report release times, and harvest and planting windows
**Buyer:** Chief Analytics Officer / Head of Market Research
