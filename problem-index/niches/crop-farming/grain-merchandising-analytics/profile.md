# Grain Merchandising Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/crop-farming|Crop Farming]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The merchandising desks at elevators, co-ops, and grain trading firms model basis, freight, and storage economics to set the cash bids farmers sell into, and manage the position risk that results.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 merchandisers and analysts per firm
**Output:** Bid setting, basis and freight models, position risk analysis
**Proprietary data:** Local origination flow and basis behaviour, visible before it appears in any published series
**Clock:** Daily bid posting and delivery windows
**Buyer:** Head of Merchandising / Chief Risk Officer
