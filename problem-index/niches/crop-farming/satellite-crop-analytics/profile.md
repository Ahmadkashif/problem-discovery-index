# Satellite & Field-Level Crop Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/crop-farming|Crop Farming]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Climate FieldView, Arva, Descartes Labs, and comparable platforms combine satellite imagery, machine telemetry, soil maps, and weather to produce field-level crop health signals and variable-rate prescriptions — attempting to automate the early stress detection the Pass 1 analysis identifies as the highest-stakes judgment in farming.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 remote sensing scientists, agronomists, and data scientists
**Output:** Field health analytics, prescription maps, yield forecasts
**Proprietary data:** Imagery joined to machine-recorded planting, application, and yield data across millions of acres — the join is proprietary even though the imagery mostly is not
**Clock:** The growing season, and the narrow windows in which an intervention still helps
**Buyer:** Chief Science Officer / VP of Data Science
