# Crop Protection Regulatory Science

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/crop-farming|Crop Farming]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The regulatory science and field development organizations at Bayer, Corteva, Syngenta, and BASF generate the dossiers that get a crop protection product registered and keep it registered — efficacy across geographies and seasons, residue and environmental fate, resistance monitoring, and the re-registration reviews that can remove a product from the market.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-3,000 regulatory scientists, field development staff, and toxicologists per major company
**Output:** Registration dossiers, label support data, resistance monitoring, re-registration submissions
**Proprietary data:** Multi-year, multi-location trial data across the global portfolio, and resistance surveillance nobody else conducts at scale
**Clock:** Registration and re-registration deadlines set by regulators, which determine whether a product can be sold
**Buyer:** Head of Regulatory Science / VP Field Development
