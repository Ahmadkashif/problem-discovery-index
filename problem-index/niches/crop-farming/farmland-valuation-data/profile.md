# Farmland Valuation Data Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/crop-farming|Crop Farming]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
AcreValue and comparable providers assemble parcel-level farmland characteristics, sale records, and soil productivity into valuation estimates and comparable sale data that lenders, investors, and landowners use in a market with limited public price disclosure.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 data scientists, agricultural economists, and analysts
**Output:** Parcel valuations, comparable sale data, land market analytics
**Proprietary data:** Assembled parcel, soil, and sale data with valuation models tuned on observed transactions
**Clock:** Transaction and lending cycles
**Buyer:** Head of Data Products / Chief Analytics Officer
