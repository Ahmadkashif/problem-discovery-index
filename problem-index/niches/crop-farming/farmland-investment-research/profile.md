# Farmland Investment Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/crop-farming|Crop Farming]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The research groups at farmland investment managers underwrite acquisitions, model crop mix and water risk, and produce the return attribution and outlook reporting institutional investors require of an alternative asset class.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 agricultural economists, analysts, and underwriters
**Output:** Acquisition underwriting, portfolio return attribution, sector outlooks
**Proprietary data:** Parcel-level operating results across a managed portfolio, joined to lease structures and water access
**Clock:** Acquisition timelines and investor reporting cycles
**Buyer:** Head of Research / Chief Investment Officer
