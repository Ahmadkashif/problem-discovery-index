# Federal Agricultural Statistics Service

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/crop-farming|Crop Farming]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The national agricultural statistics service surveys farmers, conducts objective yield measurement in fields, and publishes the crop production and stocks reports that move commodity markets at a fixed minute on a fixed day. Its estimates are the benchmark every private forecaster is measured against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-2,000 statisticians, survey methodologists, and field enumerators
**Output:** Crop production estimates, acreage and stocks reports, the agricultural census
**Proprietary data:** Farmer-reported survey responses and in-field objective yield measurements across the country, confidential by statute
**Clock:** Report release times among the most rigidly fixed in the economy
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer
