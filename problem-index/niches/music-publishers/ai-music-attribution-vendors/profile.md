# AI Music Attribution & Training-Licensing Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/music-publishers|Music Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Young data firms detect AI-generated music, estimate which works a model trained on or imitates, and administer training licences for rights owners. They supply the plumbing behind deals such as NMPA's licence with Udio and Kobalt's with ElevenLabs. Rights owners and AI developers pay for attribution reports and licence administration.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Attribution and detection reports, licence usage reporting, training-set audits
**Proprietary data:** Models and reference sets built on catalogues licensed from rights owners, and attribution histories
**Clock:** Licence reporting periods, and regulatory transparency obligations on model developers (estimate)
**Buyer:** Chief technology officer or head of rights
