# Broadcast & Usage Monitoring Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/music-publishers|Music Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms such as BMAT, Soundmouse, Mediabase and WARM fingerprint and detect music played on radio, TV, online video and in venues. They sell detections and usage reports to collecting societies for distribution, to broadcasters for cue sheets and reporting, and to publishers checking their income. Producers prepare the cue sheets themselves as a delivery item, which is analysed under postproduction as [[niches/postproduction-studios/delivery-schedules-and-cue-sheets/profile|🟠 Delivery Schedules, Cue Sheets & Paper Deliverables]]. Monitoring is how those sheets are checked against what was actually broadcast.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-200 data, operations and analyst staff per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Detection logs, usage reports matched to works, cue-sheet data
**Proprietary data:** Fingerprint reference libraries and the detection history the vendor generates itself
**Clock:** Society distribution cycles and broadcasters' reporting deadlines, both set by the clients
**Buyer:** Head of data products or chief operating officer
