# UGC & Content ID Claiming Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/music-publishers|Music Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Services such as Orfium and Pex find publishers' compositions in user-generated video on YouTube and other platforms. They file claims, answer disputes and collect the publisher's share, and are paid a commission on the revenue they recover.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 claims and dispute analysts per service; the matching itself is automated (estimate)
**Output:** Claims, dispute responses and recovered-revenue reports
**Proprietary data:** Claim and dispute histories by channel and use type
**Clock:** Platform dispute response windows
**Buyer:** Chief executive or head of operations
