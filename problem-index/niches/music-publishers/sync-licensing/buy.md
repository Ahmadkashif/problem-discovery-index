# A Sales CRM Rebuilt Around the Brief

**Niche:** [[niches/music-publishers/sync-licensing/profile|Sync Licensing]]
**Industry:** [[industries/music-publishers|Music Publishers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Sales CRMs track pipelines well, but a sync pipeline is briefs, songs and co-owners rather than accounts and opportunities.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that competitors fight to turn a brief into a licensed song, but it splits into two contests with different winners: putting the right song in front of the supervisor within hours, which is won by catalogue knowledge and a record of what each buyer licenses, and clearing and quoting a song whose copyright is split among several publishers, which is won by control of the shares and speed of approval. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Sync teams need to know who asked for what, what they were sent, what is pending approval and what closed at what fee. They keep this in email and spreadsheets because their pitch tools do not cover the whole pipeline.

## What Already Exists
Salesforce and HubSpot pipelines, with email capture, stages and forecasting; Synchtank's built-in CRM for licences.

## The Customization Gap
A sync pipeline's unit is the brief, attached to many songs, each with its own co-owners, restrictions and fee. Stages include quote requested, approvals pending by party, most-favoured-nation matching with the master side, and licence issued. Forecasting has to weight by approval risk, not just by deal stage. Email parsing needs to recognise briefs, shortlists and quote requests in supervisors' informal language.

## Target Customer
Sync and licensing teams of five to fifty people at publishers and combined publisher-label groups.

## Impact If Solved
One view of every open brief and approval, and a sync forecast leadership can rely on, built from tools the company already licenses.
