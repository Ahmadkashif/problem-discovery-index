# Music Publisher & Songwriter Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/music-publishers|Music Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
In the US these are NMPA, AIMP, NSAI and the Songwriters Guild of America; internationally, CISAC and ICMP. They publish the industry's headline statistics, such as NMPA's annual US revenue figure ($7.29B for 2025) and CISAC's Global Collections Report. They also lobby, litigate and represent members in rate proceedings such as Phonorecords V.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research staff per association; economic analysis is bought from outside experts (estimate)
**Output:** Annual statistics, policy positions and rate-proceeding filings
**Proprietary data:** Member-reported revenue and society collections data
**Clock:** Annual meetings and report cycles; Copyright Royalty Board schedules
**Buyer:** Chief executive or general counsel
