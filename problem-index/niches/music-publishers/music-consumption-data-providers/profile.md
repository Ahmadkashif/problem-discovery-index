# Music Consumption Data & Chart Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/music-publishers|Music Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Luminate, whose data sits behind the Billboard charts, along with Chartmetric, Soundcharts and similar firms, compiles streaming, sales, airplay and social data into charts, dashboards and market reports. Labels, publishers, catalogue buyers and media pay by subscription.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 data, analytics and research staff at the largest vendor, far fewer at the rest (estimate)
**Output:** Weekly charts, consumption datasets, catalogue and artist analytics, year-end market reports
**Proprietary data:** Compiled consumption history built from streaming, retail and airplay feeds licensed from their sources. Luminate's archive runs back through the SoundScan sales tracking that began in 1991 (estimate), while the smaller vendors aggregate data that platforms publish.
**Clock:** Weekly chart cut-offs and year-end reporting, set by the vendor itself
**Buyer:** Head of data or insights
