# Multi-Party Approval Workflows With Fee Matching

**Niche:** [[niches/music-publishers/split-copyright-sync-clearance/profile|Split-Copyright Sync Clearance & Quoting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/music-publishers|Music Publishers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Approval routing and e-signature tools already gather sign-offs from several parties; they know nothing about shares, fee matching or restrictions.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #automation #large-language-models #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to quote and clear a song whose composition is split among several publishers, and whose master sits with a label, before the edit locks, and whoever can give a cleared answer fastest wins the placement over equally good songs.

## The Problem
Collecting approvals from four co-publishers and a label runs as parallel email threads, with fees recalculated by hand whenever one party counters.

## What Already Exists
DocuSign and Adobe Acrobat Sign for multi-party signature, approval workflow tools in project management suites, and Synchtank's licence generation.

## The Customization Gap
The music version needs a share-weighted fee calculation, so each party's quote reflects its percentage. It needs most-favoured-nation logic that re-prices every party when one fee changes. It must check each party's standing restriction list before routing, enforce the rule that silence is not consent with reminders and expiry, and keep an audit trail that ties the issued licence to every approval.

## Target Customer
Licensing coordinators and business affairs teams at independent and major publishers.

## Impact If Solved
Approval cycles shrink from days to hours using tools the company already understands, with fewer fee errors and no licence issued before every share has said yes.
