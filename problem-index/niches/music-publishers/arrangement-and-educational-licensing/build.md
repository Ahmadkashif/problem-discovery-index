# Rule-Based Instant Arrangement Licences

**Niche:** [[niches/music-publishers/arrangement-and-educational-licensing/profile|Custom Arrangement & Educational Print Licensing]]
**Industry:** [[industries/music-publishers|Music Publishers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A high-school band director waits weeks for permission to arrange a pop song for a ten-minute halftime show that could be licensed by rule in a minute.
**Tags:** #decision-trees #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #automation #revenue-impact #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to grant a band director or arranger a custom-arrangement or print licence in days, at a price set by rule rather than by negotiation, and whoever does that becomes the default route before directors give up and perform unlicensed.

## The Problem
Most requests are standard: a school ensemble, a non-commercial performance, a term of one season to three years, a handful of copies. Each is still reviewed and priced as if it were unique, and owners who cannot be found or who do not reply stall the arranger's whole season.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Each request is worth little, so publishers have not invested, and multi-publisher songs need every owner to agree to the same rules.

## What to Build
A licensing engine in which participating owners set rules by ensemble type, use (performance, competition, recording, streaming), term and copy count, with prices to match. Standard requests are licensed instantly and exceptions are routed, and the engine identifies the print-rights holder and co-owners from registration data.

## Target Customer
Print and educational publishers and permissions intermediaries.

## Impact If Built
More licences issued, less unlicensed performance, and directors who can plan their shows on time.
