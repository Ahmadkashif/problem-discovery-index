# Self-Serve Licence Checkout From Stock Media

**Niche:** [[niches/music-publishers/arrangement-and-educational-licensing/profile|Custom Arrangement & Educational Print Licensing]]
**Industry:** [[industries/music-publishers|Music Publishers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Stock media marketplaces already sell standard licences at checkout; arrangement licensing still runs on request forms.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #automation #data-integration #revenue-impact #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to grant a band director or arranger a custom-arrangement or print licence in days, at a price set by rule rather than by negotiation, and whoever does that becomes the default route before directors give up and perform unlicensed.

## The Problem
Educators are used to buying digital sheet music instantly, yet the permission to arrange still takes weeks.

## What Already Exists
Stock photo, video and audio marketplaces with tiered licences sold at checkout, e-commerce platforms with licence templates, and digital sheet music storefronts.

## The Customization Gap
Arrangement licensing needs a rights lookup that separates the print-rights holder from the copyright owner. It needs co-owner consent for each song, licence terms by ensemble, use and season, exclusions for commercial recordings and broadcast, and reporting back to every owner by share.

## Target Customer
Print publishers already selling digital sheet music online.

## Impact If Solved
Standard licences sold at the moment of need through a storefront the publisher already runs.
