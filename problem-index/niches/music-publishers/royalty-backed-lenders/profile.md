# Royalty-Backed Lenders & Advance Funds

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/music-publishers|Music Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Lenders and funds such as Sound Royalties, Duetti and beatBread advance cash to songwriters, artists and small publishers against future royalties, or buy royalty streams outright. They underwrite each deal from the borrower's statements and are paid through the advance or the stream, not through fees for analysis.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 underwriting and data staff per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Underwriting decisions, pricing and portfolio monitoring
**Proprietary data:** Royalty performance across hundreds or thousands of financed catalogues, plus borrowers' statements received under confidentiality
**Clock:** Borrower-driven; covenant and recoupment tests
**Buyer:** Chief credit officer
