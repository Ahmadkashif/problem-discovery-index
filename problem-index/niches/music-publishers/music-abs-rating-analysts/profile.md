# Music Royalty ABS Rating Analysts

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/music-publishers|Music Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Rating agency analysts rate music royalty asset-backed securities by reviewing catalogue valuations, cash-flow models and surveillance reports. KBRA has assigned 64 ratings on music royalty deals across 15 issuers since 2020, about $8B of issuance, and issuers pay for the ratings.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analysts dedicated to music at any one agency (estimate)
**Output:** Ratings, presale reports and surveillance reports
**Proprietary data:** Issuer cash-flow and performance data across every rated music deal
**Clock:** Issuance dates and surveillance cycles
**Buyer:** Head of the asset-backed securities group
