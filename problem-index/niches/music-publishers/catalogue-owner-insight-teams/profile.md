# Catalogue Funds & Major Publisher Insight Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/music-publishers|Music Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
These are the in-house data, insight and acquisition analytics teams at the major publishers (Sony Music Publishing, UMPG, Warner Chappell) and at catalogue owners such as Concord, Primary Wave, Reservoir, BMG and Chord. They forecast catalogue earnings, support acquisitions, track sync and streaming, and report to investors and lenders.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 per owner (estimate)
**Output:** Internal forecasts, acquisition models, investor and asset-backed security reporting
**Proprietary data:** Decades of song-level earnings across owned catalogues, the deepest outcome data in the industry
**Clock:** Earnings dates for listed owners, asset-backed security reporting dates and fund valuation dates
**Buyer:** Head of data or chief financial officer
