# Copyright Royalty Board

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/music-publishers|Music Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Three Copyright Royalty Judges and their staff set statutory royalty rates, including the mechanical rates in the Phonorecords proceedings. Phonorecords V will set rates for 2028–2032. A settlement for physical and download rates was filed in June 2026, and streaming rate proposals followed in October 2026.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Three judges and under 10 analytical staff (attorney-advisors), inside a small office (estimate)
**Output:** Rate determinations
**Proprietary data:** The evidentiary record of each proceeding, much of it under protective order
**Clock:** Statutory proceeding schedules
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — federal body
