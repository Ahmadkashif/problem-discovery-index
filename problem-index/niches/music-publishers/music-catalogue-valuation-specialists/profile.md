# Music Catalogue Valuation Specialists

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/music-publishers|Music Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Specialist valuers price song and master catalogues for acquisitions, refinancings, asset-backed securities, fund reporting and estates. Citrin Cooperman's music and entertainment valuation group, built on Massarsky Consulting (bought in 2022), priced 566 catalogues worth nearly $13B in 2025, up from 557 worth $10.7B in 2024. It publishes average multiples from that work: around 15 times earnings for older publishing catalogues. A few boutiques and the music teams of larger valuation firms do similar work. Buyers, sellers, lenders, fund boards and rating agencies pay a fee per valuation, and the Hipgnosis dispute, in which a sale of non-core songs came in 14.2% below the valuer's mark and the valuer resigned, shows what rides on it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-30 analysts per specialist practice (estimate)
**Output:** Catalogue valuation reports (song-level earnings forecasts, decay assumptions, discount rates, implied multiples) and periodic fair-value marks for funds and asset-backed securities
**Proprietary data:** Song-level earnings histories from hundreds of catalogues a year seen in valuation work, and the firm's own decay curves and multiples built from them. The statements arrive under engagement confidentiality, but the assumption library is the firm's work product.
**Clock:** Fund and listed-vehicle reporting dates, asset-backed security issuance and periodic valuation updates; acquisition valuations run to the buyer's deal timeline
**Buyer:** Head of the music valuation practice

**Kill switches:** none live. Client statements arrive under engagement confidentiality, but aggregate use is established practice (Citrin publishes catalogue counts, deal sizes and multiples from its engagements). The data also comes from the valuer's own client, unlike the opposing-party books that keep [[niches/music-publishers/music-royalty-audit-practices/profile|🔍 Music Royalty Audit Practices]] behind a live switch. That matches how the vault treats [[niches/private-equity-firms/portfolio-valuation-advisory/profile|Portfolio Valuation Advisory]] (55) and [[niches/investment-banking-boutiques/financial-reporting-valuation/profile|Financial Reporting Valuation]] (50).
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. The work is a repeatable, invoiced forecast with a fast-growing archive, but the specialist field is a handful of practices, and most acquisition valuations run to a deal timeline rather than a filing date. Those two limits separate it from the generalist valuation pockets indexed under private equity and investment banking.
