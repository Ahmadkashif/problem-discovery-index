# Prepayment and Cash-Flow Models From Structured Finance

**Niche:** [[niches/music-publishers/catalogue-acquisition-and-underwriting/profile|Catalogue Acquisition & Underwriting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/music-publishers|Music Publishers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Mortgage and asset-backed security analytics already forecast decaying cash flows by cohort with uncertainty; music catalogues are priced without them.
**Tags:** #survival-analysis #time-series-forecasting #monte-carlo-methods #bayesian-inference #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to forecast a catalogue's next ten years of earnings, song by song, more accurately than the rival bidders, and whoever forecasts best wins the catalogues worth winning and walks away from the ones that would be overpaid.

## The Problem
A catalogue's income is a pool of decaying cash flows, much like a pool of mortgages that prepay. Yet buyers model it with one decay curve and a multiple.

## What Already Exists
Structured-finance cash-flow and prepayment modelling, such as the cohort, hazard and scenario models used for mortgage and consumer asset-backed securities, which rating agencies and investors already run. Music royalty securitisations borrow the legal structure but not the analytics.

## The Customization Gap
A music version needs several additions. Income types decay differently: performance, mechanical and sync do not move together. Rate determinations, such as Phonorecords decisions and PRO settlements, are regime shifts rather than noise. Revival events like a sync, a cover or a viral moment are jumps rather than smooth decay. Concentration matters, since a handful of songs often carry the catalogue. And termination windows can remove a song outright. The cohort is the song's age and genre rather than a loan vintage.

## Target Customer
Catalogue fund analysts, royalty asset-backed security arrangers and their rating agencies.

## Impact If Solved
Catalogue pricing and royalty asset-backed security surveillance on the same cash-flow discipline that the rest of structured finance uses, with scenario ranges instead of a single mark.
