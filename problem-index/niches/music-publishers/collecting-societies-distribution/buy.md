**Industry:** [[industries/music-publishers|Music Publishers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)

**Tags:** #k-nearest-neighbors #random-forests #data-integration #automation #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this pocket is fighting to identify every use of every work and pay the right share to the right member inside the distribution window, before unidentified money becomes non-distributable, and whoever does that most accurately and fastest keeps the writers and publishers whose repertoire makes its licence worth buying.

## The Problem
A society's works database holds millions of works, many registered more than once under variant titles, with writers known by several names and shares that conflict with other societies' records. Every duplicate or conflict becomes unidentified or suspended money at distribution.

## What Already Exists
Master data management and entity resolution platforms used in banking, retail and government to match, merge and govern records about people and organisations, with survivorship rules and human review queues.

## The Customization Gap
A music version needs the industry's identifiers (IPI for parties, ISWC for works, ISRC for recordings) and their known failure modes. It needs CWR ingestion and output, share arithmetic that must total 100% for each territory and right type, and merge rules that respect reciprocal agreements and other societies' authority over their own members. Its audit trail must let a member see why a work was merged or split.

## Target Customer
Data and documentation heads at collecting societies.

## Impact If Solved
A cleaner works database before distribution, giving fewer unidentified lines and conflicts, built on tooling proven in other industries.
