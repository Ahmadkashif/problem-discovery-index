# Music Supervision Companies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/music-publishers|Music Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Music supervisors and small supervision firms choose and license music for films, series, games and ads on behalf of productions. They send the briefs publishers answer, negotiate the fees and prepare cue sheets, which close out the production's delivery schedule (see [[niches/postproduction-studios/delivery-schedules-and-cue-sheets/profile|🟠 Delivery Schedules, Cue Sheets & Paper Deliverables]]).

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1-10 per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Music selections, clearances, licence negotiations and cue sheets
**Proprietary data:** Personal stashes of songs and their own deal histories
**Clock:** Production and delivery schedules
**Buyer:** The supervisor who owns the firm
