# A Termination Calendar Computed From the Agreements Themselves

**Niche:** [[niches/music-publishers/termination-and-reversion-management/profile|Termination & Reversion Management]]
**Industry:** [[industries/music-publishers|Music Publishers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Nobody can list every terminable song in a catalogue with its window, deadlines and the earnings at stake without weeks of a lawyer's reading.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #seq2seq #evaluation-metrics #compliance #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to know, for every grant in a catalogue, when it can be terminated or will revert, and to act inside the statutory window, and whoever finds the window first either keeps the song or gets it back.

## The Problem
A catalogue of 20,000 songs may rest on a few thousand agreements. Which grants were executed by the author? Which works were published when? Which are works made for hire? Which windows are open, and which notices could arrive next year? The answer exists only if someone reads everything.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The rules are legal, the documents are old and scanned, and each publisher's archive is private. Music lawyers do the work client by client, for a fee.

## What to Build
Extraction of grant date, parties, author execution, work-for-hire language, works covered, territory, term, reversion and retention clauses, each cited to its page. The extracted facts are joined to publication dates from release data and Copyright Office records, Section 203 and 304 windows and service and recordation deadlines are computed for every work, and the calendar is ranked by earnings.

## Target Customer
Business affairs heads at publishers and catalogue funds; estate counsel acting for writers.

## Impact If Built
Windows found years ahead rather than by notice, renegotiation from a position of knowledge, and diligence reports in days.
