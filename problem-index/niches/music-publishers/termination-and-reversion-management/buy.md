# Contract Lifecycle Management With Copyright Law Added

**Niche:** [[niches/music-publishers/termination-and-reversion-management/profile|Termination & Reversion Management]]
**Industry:** [[industries/music-publishers|Music Publishers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Contract management software already extracts dates and sends alerts; it does not know when the Copyright Act lets an author take a song back.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #compliance #automation #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to know, for every grant in a catalogue, when it can be terminated or will revert, and to act inside the statutory window, and whoever finds the window first either keeps the song or gets it back.

## The Problem
Publishers already pay for document storage and clause extraction, but the termination calculation still happens in a lawyer's spreadsheet.

## What Already Exists
Contract lifecycle management platforms such as Ironclad, DocuSign CLM and Agiloft, with clause libraries, extraction models and renewal alerts.

## The Customization Gap
The publishing adaptation needs a copyright clause library (grant, assignment, work for hire, reversion, retention, controlled composition) and logic that decides which termination regime applies. It has to calculate per work, not per contract, since publication dates differ song by song. It needs Copyright Office recordation tracking and a link to each song's earnings so alerts are ranked by money.

## Target Customer
Legal and business affairs teams at publishers already running a contract management platform.

## Impact If Solved
The statutory calendar sits inside the system the legal team already uses, with no separate tool and no spreadsheet.
