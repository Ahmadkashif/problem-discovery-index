# Chargeback Representment Tools for Society Disputes

**Niche:** [[niches/music-publishers/share-conflict-resolution/profile|Share Conflict & Overclaim Resolution]]
**Industry:** [[industries/music-publishers|Music Publishers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Payments companies already assemble evidence packs for card-network disputes by rule and deadline; publishers assemble them for society disputes by hand.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #random-forests #workflow-orchestration #automation #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to resolve share conflicts with documentary proof faster than the counterparty and the society queue, because disputed money stays in suspense until someone proves their share, and whoever clears conflicts fastest with the least analyst time wins the administration mandate.

## The Problem
Every conflict needs the same work: classify it, gather the documents, format them for the forum and respond in time.

## What Already Exists
Chargeback management and representment software used by merchants and payment processors. It classifies disputes by reason code, compiles evidence to each card network's rules, tracks deadlines and learns win rates by reason.

## The Customization Gap
The publishing version needs society-specific dispute rules and formats (the MLC's dispute policy, the PROs' processes, foreign societies, Content ID). The evidence is copyright documents, not transactions. Cases are identified by work rather than by transaction, and outcomes change registrations across every society, not one payment.

## Target Customer
Administration teams with high conflict volumes.

## Impact If Solved
A mature dispute-handling pattern applied to rights conflicts, with deadline tracking and win-rate learning included.
