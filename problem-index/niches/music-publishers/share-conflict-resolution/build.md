**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to resolve share conflicts with documentary proof faster than the counterparty and the society queue, because disputed money stays in suspense until someone proves their share, and whoever clears conflicts fastest with the least analyst time wins the administration mandate.

## The Problem
A mid-size publisher can have thousands of open conflicts across the MLC, PROs, foreign societies and YouTube (estimate). Each is researched from scratch, and nobody knows which ones hold the money.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Each society exposes conflicts differently, the evidence sits in the publisher's private archive, and past outcomes are rarely recorded as data.

## What to Build
One queue fed from every society and platform, ranked by the money in suspense and the deadline. For each case the system classifies the likely cause, retrieves the governing agreement clauses and documents, shows similar past cases and their outcomes, and drafts the evidence pack and message in each society's format. It recommends prevail or concede, with high precision required on concede.

## Target Customer
Copyright administration heads at independent publishers and administrators.

## Impact If Built
Several times the conflicts cleared per coordinator, with suspended money released sooner and the largest cases handled first.
