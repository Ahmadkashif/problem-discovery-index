# The Same Duplicate Registration Disputed Every Quarter

**Niche:** [[niches/music-publishers/share-conflict-resolution/profile|Share Conflict & Overclaim Resolution]]
**Industry:** [[industries/music-publishers|Music Publishers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A conflict resolved at one society reappears at three others, because the fix never reaches the registrations that caused it.
**Tags:** #k-nearest-neighbors #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to resolve share conflicts with documentary proof faster than the counterparty and the society queue, because disputed money stays in suspense until someone proves their share, and whoever clears conflicts fastest with the least analyst time wins the administration mandate.

## The Problem
A duplicate work registration, or an expired administration claim, is cleaned up at the MLC after weeks of correspondence. The same stale registration still sits at a foreign society and a PRO, and the conflict comes back there next quarter.

## Why It's Still Broken
Resolution is handled as a ticket at one society, not as a correction to the publisher's master record pushed everywhere.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Treat every resolved conflict as a correction to the master ledger. Push it by CWR and portal update to every society holding the work, and check that each one took effect.

## Who Feels the Pain
Coordinators re-working the same cases, writers whose money is frozen again and administrators whose clients see recurring holds.

## Impact If Fixed
Each conflict is solved once, which shrinks the queue at its source.
