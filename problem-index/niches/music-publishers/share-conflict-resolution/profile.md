# Share Conflict & Overclaim Resolution

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/music-publishers|Music Publishers]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to resolve share conflicts with documentary proof faster than the counterparty and the society queue, because disputed money stays in suspense until someone proves their share, and whoever clears conflicts fastest with the least analyst time wins the administration mandate.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$200M a year of US royalties passing through suspense over share disputes, plus ~$60M of administration labour spent resolving them (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of US publishing revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — society portals show conflicts, but evidence is assembled by hand
**Target Buyer:** VP of copyright administration at a publisher or administrator
**Automation Potential:** High — most conflicts repeat a few causes and settle on documents

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
When claims on a work exceed 100%, or a counterclaim contradicts a registration, the society holds the disputed share. The MLC's dispute policy holds only the disputed shares in suspense and leaves resolution to the claimants. HFA escalates unresolved conflicts to the MLC after two weeks, and publisher help pages put resolution at weeks to months. The causes repeat: duplicate registrations, administration deals that expired, shares assigned to successor companies, wrong splits and the occasional bad-faith claim. Proof sits in agreements and letters of direction. This is a different contest from matching recordings to compositions, which is the distributor's problem ([[niches/music-distribution-platforms/recording-to-composition-matching/profile|🎯 Recording-to-Composition Matching]]): here both parties already know the work and disagree about who owns it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
The MLC's Overclaims Tool, PRO and foreign society dispute processes, YouTube Content ID disputes, and email with counterparties. The gaps: no single queue across societies, no ranking by money in suspense, evidence pulled from archives case by case, and outcomes not recorded for reuse.
