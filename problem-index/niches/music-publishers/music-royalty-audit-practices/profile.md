# Music Royalty Audit Practices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/music-publishers|Music Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Accounting practices audit publishers', labels' and collecting societies' royalty statements on behalf of songwriters, artists, estates and catalogue owners, and audit licensees on behalf of rights owners. They test splits, rates, foreign income, deductions, recoupment and unreported uses against the contract, then present and negotiate a claim. Clients pay hourly or on contingency against amounts recovered.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 auditors per leading practice, across perhaps a dozen specialist firms (estimate)
**Output:** Audit reports and claims, settlement negotiation support, and expert testimony when claims are litigated
**Proprietary data:** A cross-counterparty archive of findings: which publishers, labels and societies misapply which terms, by how much, and how claims settled. No single writer or publisher sees this.
**Clock:** Contractual objection periods after which statements become binding (commonly two to three years, estimate), and court or arbitration dates where claims escalate
**Buyer:** Managing partner of the royalty audit practice

**Kill switches:** two are judged separately.
- *Data isn't theirs to use:* live. The publisher's, label's or society's statements and books are opened to the auditor under the contract's audit clause, for that audit only, and settlements usually carry confidentiality terms. This is why the ruling differs from [[niches/music-publishers/music-catalogue-valuation-specialists/profile|🔍 Music Catalogue Valuation Specialists]] (47, no live switch). A valuer's data comes from its own client under engagement confidentiality, and publishing aggregate multiples from that data is established practice. An auditor's data comes from the opposing party under a use-limited audit right, and no comparable aggregate publication exists. The ruling matches the film sibling [[niches/film-distributors/participation-royalty-auditors/profile|🔍 Profit Participation & Royalty Auditors]] (46).
- *Compliance gate (privilege and work product):* live for the litigated share of the work. When a claim escalates, expert reports, testimony and litigation consulting are retained through counsel as attorney work product, which follows the vault's majority rule for litigation work done through counsel. The contractual audit itself sits outside privilege, so this switch alone would narrow the product rather than stall it.

**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. A repeatable audit with a contractual deadline and a cross-counterparty record nobody else holds. The record is locked behind the use limits that make the audits possible, and the litigation work runs through counsel.
