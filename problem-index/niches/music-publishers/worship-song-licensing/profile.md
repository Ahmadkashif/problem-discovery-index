# Worship Song Licensing & Usage Reporting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/music-publishers|Music Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
CCLI and smaller rivals sell churches annual blanket licences to project, print, record and stream worship songs. They collect usage reports from churches and distribute royalties to the publishers. CCLI's licence covers more than 600,000 songs and is held by over 24,000 UK churches, and an older interview put US licence holders at ~145,000. US fees ran from $59 to $5,266 a year by church size in 2017.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 reporting, data and distribution staff (estimate)
**Output:** Distributions to publishers and song-usage charts such as the CCLI Top 100
**Proprietary data:** Decades of congregational song-usage reports that nobody else holds
**Clock:** Annual licence renewals and distribution periods, set by the licensor
**Buyer:** Head of operations at the licensor
