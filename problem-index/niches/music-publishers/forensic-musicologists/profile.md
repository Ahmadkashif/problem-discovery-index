# Forensic Musicologists

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/music-publishers|Music Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Musicologists compare melody, harmony, rhythm and lyrics for infringement claims and pre-release clearance, mostly as expert witnesses retained by counsel for publishers, writers and labels.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1-5 per practice (estimate)
**Output:** Expert reports, testimony and clearance opinions
**Proprietary data:** Case analyses and comparison files
**Clock:** Court and discovery schedules
**Buyer:** Retaining counsel
