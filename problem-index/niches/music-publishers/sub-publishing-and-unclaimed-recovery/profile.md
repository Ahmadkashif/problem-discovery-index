# Sub-Publishing & Unclaimed Royalty Recovery

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/music-publishers|Music Publishers]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to claim a song's earnings in every territory before the collecting society's identification period runs out and the money is shared out by market share, and whoever claims most completely and earliest takes the administration and sub-publishing mandates.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1B a year of foreign and unclaimed income owed to US catalogues (estimate; ASCAP alone made $455M from performances abroad available for distribution in 2025)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~14% of US publishing revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — sub-publisher statements arrive as PDFs and spreadsheets; claims are filed by portal and email
**Target Buyer:** Head of international administration or sub-publishing at a publisher or administrator
**Automation Potential:** High for gap detection and claim preparation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Abroad, US songs are collected by local societies and by sub-publishers that the US publisher appoints for a share, commonly 10–25% (estimate). Money that cannot be matched to a registered owner is held for a period and then shared out among the society's members by market share. The EU's collective management directive treats sums unclaimed three years after the collection year as non-distributable. At home, the MLC will begin market-share distributions of unclaimed money in April 2027, starting with January 2021 usage and a database snapshot around 10 February 2027. Administration deals are won on the claim "we collect more", and this niche is where that claim is tested.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Sub-publisher statements, society portals, CWR registrations, the MLC's Matching Tool and administrators' international teams. The gaps: no expected-income baseline per territory, statements arriving one to two years after use (estimate) and in inconsistent layouts, and claims prioritised by habit rather than by money and deadline.
