# Revenue Assurance From Telecoms

**Niche:** [[niches/music-publishers/sub-publishing-and-unclaimed-recovery/profile|Sub-Publishing & Unclaimed Royalty Recovery]]
**Industry:** [[industries/music-publishers|Music Publishers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Telecom carriers have long used revenue-assurance systems to compare usage with billed revenue and find leakage; publishers compare usage with royalties by hand.
**Tags:** #hypothesis-testing #feature-engineering #descriptive-statistics #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to claim a song's earnings in every territory before the collecting society's identification period runs out and the money is shared out by market share, and whoever claims most completely and earliest takes the administration and sub-publishing mandates.

## The Problem
Leakage between usage and payment is a known, measurable problem in other industries, yet in publishing it is handled as ad hoc investigation.

## What Already Exists
Telecom revenue-assurance and leakage-detection platforms that reconcile network usage records against billing, flag gaps by rule and model, and track recovery.

## The Customization Gap
In publishing, the "usage record" comes from third parties (DSP country reports, broadcast monitoring), and the "bill" is many societies' statements with different lags and currencies. Shares and deal terms change by territory. The deadlines differ by society, and the MLC's market-share date is one of them. And recovery is a claim, not a re-bill.

## Target Customer
Administrators and publishers with large foreign income and an operations analytics team.

## Impact If Solved
A proven reconciliation discipline applied to an industry that still finds missing money by anecdote.
