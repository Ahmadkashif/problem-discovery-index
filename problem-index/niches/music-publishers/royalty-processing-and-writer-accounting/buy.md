# Accounts-Payable Document Automation for Statements

**Niche:** [[niches/music-publishers/royalty-processing-and-writer-accounting/profile|Royalty Processing & Writer Accounting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/music-publishers|Music Publishers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Invoice-capture tools already read thousands of supplier layouts and post them to the ledger; royalty statements are a harder version of the same problem.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #feature-engineering #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn every incoming statement line into the right writer's account by the contractual statement date, with each figure traceable to its source so that a writer's audit finds nothing, and whoever does that at volume becomes the royalty department's system of record.

## The Problem
Royalty teams face layout variety that finance teams solved for invoices years ago.

## What Already Exists
Accounts-payable document automation platforms such as Rossum and ABBYY that extract fields from any invoice layout, learn from corrections and post to ERP systems.

## The Customization Gap
A royalty statement has thousands of lines, not dozens. The key field is a musical work matched by title variants and writer names, not a vendor ID. Amounts come in mixed currencies and periods. And posting requires splits by share and deal terms rather than a single ledger account.

## Target Customer
Royalty departments at publishers that already use document automation in finance.

## Impact If Solved
Proven layout-learning technology applied to the publisher's highest-volume document.
