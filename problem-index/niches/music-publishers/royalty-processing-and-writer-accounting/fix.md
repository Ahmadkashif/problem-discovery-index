# The Suspense Report Worked in a Spreadsheet Before Every Deadline

**Niche:** [[niches/music-publishers/royalty-processing-and-writer-accounting/profile|Royalty Processing & Writer Accounting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/music-publishers|Music Publishers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Unmatched lines are exported, worked by hand and re-imported each period, and the same titles come back next time.
**Tags:** #k-nearest-neighbors #descriptive-statistics #automation #quick-win #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn every incoming statement line into the right writer's account by the contractual statement date, with each figure traceable to its source so that a writer's audit finds nothing, and whoever does that at volume becomes the royalty department's system of record.

## The Problem
Before each writer statement run, analysts export the suspense report, look up each unmatched title, and re-import the matches. Next period, the same society sends the same unmatched titles.

## Why It's Still Broken
Manual matches are saved as one-off postings, not as aliases the importer will reuse.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Save every manual match as a source-specific alias, suggest matches from past decisions, and report suspense by source, so the noisiest statements get fixed at the format level.

## Who Feels the Pain
Royalty analysts at period end, and writers whose lines sit in suspense.

## Impact If Fixed
A suspense report that shrinks each period instead of recurring.
