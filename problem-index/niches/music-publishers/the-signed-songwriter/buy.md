# Artist Earnings Dashboards From Distribution

**Niche:** [[niches/music-publishers/the-signed-songwriter/profile|The Signed Songwriter]]
**Industry:** [[industries/music-publishers|Music Publishers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Distributors already show artists their recording earnings almost in real time; composition income needs splits, society lags and recoupment that those dashboards lack.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #descriptive-statistics #data-integration #worker-facing #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show a signed writer what their songs are earning and when the advance will recoup, sooner and more believably than rival publishers, and whoever does that keeps the writer when the option comes up.

## The Problem
Writers compare the instant recording dashboards their artist friends use with the twice-yearly publishing statement and conclude that publishing is opaque.

## What Already Exists
Distributor and label artist dashboards (streams, earnings, payouts by DSP and country), and streaming services' artist analytics.

## The Customization Gap
A writer version must split each song among co-writers and co-publishers, apply the writer's deal (advance balance, recoupment rules, administration fees), model each society's lag and conversion separately for performance and mechanical income, show sync fees by use, and label what is estimated and what has been paid.

## Target Customer
Publishers with in-house product teams or a portal vendor.

## Impact If Solved
A writer-facing view built on a proven pattern, so the publisher's service matches what writers already see for recordings.
