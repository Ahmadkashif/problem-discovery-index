# The Signed Songwriter

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/music-publishers|Music Publishers]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show a signed writer what their songs are earning and when the advance will recoup, sooner and more believably than rival publishers, and whoever does that keeps the writer when the option comes up.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$500M a year in advances to US signed writers, plus writer-services and portal spend (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of US publishing revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — some publishers offer portals, most send semiannual statements
**Target Buyer:** Head of creative or writer relations at a publisher
**Automation Potential:** High for estimates and explanations

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The writer is the industry's core labour and is served by none of its tools. Signed writers live on advances, learn their earnings up to a year after the fact, and cannot verify the statement. Publishers compete hard for writers at signing and at option time. Advance size is part of that contest, but so is the promise of transparency and service, which made Kobalt's writer portal a selling point. The niche is distinct from the DIY artist whose distributor offers publishing administration ([[niches/music-distribution-platforms/publishing-administration/profile|🟠 Publishing Administration]]): the signed writer has a publisher, an advance and a deal whose terms decide every number.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Semiannual statements, portals at some publishers showing past earnings, PRO member portals, and streaming dashboards for the recordings. The gaps: no forward estimate, no recoupment projection, deal terms not applied to live usage, and statements nobody explains.
