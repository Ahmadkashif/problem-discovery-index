# The Mechanical Licensing Collective

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/music-publishers|Music Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The MLC is the statutory collective designated under the Music Modernization Act to administer the blanket mechanical licence for US digital services from January 2021. It matches digital service usage to works, distributes monthly, runs the public works database and its matching and overclaims tools, and holds unmatched royalties. It has distributed more than $4.2B, held ~$273.8M of unmatched blanket royalties at the end of 2024, and plans to begin market-share distributions of unclaimed money in April 2027. The Copyright Office continued its designation in 2026.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-150 data, matching and distribution staff (estimate)
**Output:** Monthly distributions, matching and reprocessing, the public works database, and market-share distributions from April 2027
**Proprietary data:** Digital service usage reports and the works database. The database is public by statute.
**Clock:** Statutory and regulatory: monthly distribution obligations, the April 2027 market-share start, Copyright Office designation reviews and audits of digital services
**Buyer:** Chief executive and board, funded by an administrative assessment on digital services

**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. It has a hard statutory clock and the most complete US digital usage record, but it is a single statutory organisation with no market behind it, and its output is a distribution rather than an invoiced analysis. Its unmatched pool is the high-impact problem of [[industries/music-distribution-platforms|Music Distribution Platforms]].
