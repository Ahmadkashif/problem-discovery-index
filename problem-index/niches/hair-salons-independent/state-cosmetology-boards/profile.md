# State Boards of Cosmetology

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hair-salons-independent|Hair Salons (Independent)]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State boards license cosmetologists and salons, inspect establishments for sanitation and practice violations, and discipline licensees. A salon cannot operate and a stylist cannot work without them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 licensing analysts and inspectors per state board
**Output:** Licensing decisions, inspection findings, disciplinary actions
**Proprietary data:** Licensee records and inspection histories by establishment
**Clock:** License renewal cycles and inspection schedules
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — fee-funded state agency
