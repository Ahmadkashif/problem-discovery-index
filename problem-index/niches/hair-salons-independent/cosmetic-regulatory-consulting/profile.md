# Cosmetic Regulatory & Safety Substantiation Consulting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hair-salons-independent|Hair Salons (Independent)]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Regulatory consultancies prepare the safety substantiation dossiers, facility registrations, product listings, and adverse event procedures that cosmetics manufacturers — including every professional haircare and colour brand selling into salons — now require. The Modernization of Cosmetics Regulation Act imposed the first substantive federal obligations on the category since 1938, and an industry that operated for eighty years without a substantiation requirement acquired one on a statutory schedule.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-120 toxicologists, regulatory scientists, and technical writers
**Output:** Safety substantiation dossiers, ingredient assessments, registration and listing filings, adverse event evaluation
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated ingredient safety assessments, prior submissions, and the agency's responses to them — a record of what has been accepted and what has been questioned
**Clock:** Statutory MoCRA deadlines, plus international registration schedules with their own fixed dates
**Buyer:** Practice leader / Managing Director
