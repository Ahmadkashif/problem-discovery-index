# Professional Beauty Distributor Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hair-salons-independent|Hair Salons (Independent)]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The professional distributors — SalonCentric, State, the regional houses — supply colour and care products to salons through stores and field consultants, and hold purchase histories at individual salon level across the channel.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 category and sales analysts
**Output:** Assortment planning, promotional analysis, field consultant targeting
**Proprietary data:** Salon-level purchase histories across a channel manufacturers cannot see into directly
**Clock:** Promotional and launch calendars
**Buyer:** VP of Category Management
