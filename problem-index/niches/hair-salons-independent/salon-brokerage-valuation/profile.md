# Salon Brokerage & Valuation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hair-salons-independent|Hair Salons (Independent)]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Business brokers value and sell independent salons, pricing them on clientele, stylist retention, lease terms, and chair mix — where the central valuation question is how much of the book follows the stylists rather than the address.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1-6 brokers
**Output:** Valuations, offering memoranda, buyer matching
**Proprietary data:** Completed transaction comparables in a segment with no public price data
**Clock:** Transaction timelines
**Buyer:** Principal
