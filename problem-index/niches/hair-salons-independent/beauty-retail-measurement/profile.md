# Beauty Retail Measurement Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hair-salons-independent|Hair Salons (Independent)]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
The syndicated retail trackers measure beauty sell-through at the point of sale across mass, prestige, and specialty retail, and sell brand share and category movement back to manufacturers. In beauty this is the reference dataset for everything except the professional salon channel, which sits outside retail measurement entirely.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-1,000+ analysts, data engineers, and client service staff
**Output:** Share and volume reporting, category movement, new item tracking
**Proprietary data:** Retailer point-of-sale feeds under long-term contracts, plus panel and modelling layers
**Clock:** Weekly and monthly reporting cadence
**Buyer:** VP of Data Products
