# Salon Business Coaching & Benchmarking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hair-salons-independent|Hair Salons (Independent)]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Coaching organizations work with thousands of salons on the operating numbers — chair utilization, retail attachment, pre-book rate, stylist productivity, commission structure — and hold the only meaningful financial benchmark set for a segment where almost nobody publishes anything.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-120 coaches, analysts, and educators
**Output:** Operating diagnostics, benchmark comparisons, compensation and pricing recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Salon P&Ls and productivity metrics across thousands of independent operations, collected continuously over years
**Clock:** Coaching cadence and annual planning; no hard external deadline
**Buyer:** Founder / President
