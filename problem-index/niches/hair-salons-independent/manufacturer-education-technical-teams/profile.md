# Professional Brand Education & Technical Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hair-salons-independent|Hair Salons (Independent)]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
L'Oréal Professionnel, Wella, Redken, and their peers run large education organizations that teach stylists colour formulation, chemistry, and technique. The education is how the brand keeps a salon; the product is close to substitutable without it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 educators, technical artists, and colour chemists per major brand
**Output:** Formulation systems, education curricula, technical support, colour correction guidance
**Proprietary data:** Formulation chemistry and decades of technical support cases — though what happened at the chair after the formula was mixed is almost never recorded
**Clock:** Collection launches and education calendars
**Buyer:** VP of Education / Technical Director
