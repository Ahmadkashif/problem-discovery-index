# Salon Suite Franchise Development

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hair-salons-independent|Hair Salons (Independent)]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup

## What They Do
Sola, Phenix, and the salon suite operators lease individual studios to stylists who have left commission salons. Their development teams select sites, model trade areas, and track studio occupancy across thousands of locations.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-50 development analysts and franchise consultants
**Output:** Site selection, trade area models, occupancy and franchisee performance analysis
**Proprietary data:** Studio occupancy and tenant tenure across thousands of locations — a direct read on independent stylist economics
**Clock:** Lease and development pipelines
**Buyer:** VP of Development
