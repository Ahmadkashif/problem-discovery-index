# Booking Marketplace Data Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hair-salons-independent|Hair Salons (Independent)]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Booksy, StyleSeat, Vagaro, and Fresha process appointments and payments for hundreds of thousands of beauty professionals, and their data teams see the client-stylist relationship at a resolution no salon owner has.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-120 data and analytics staff
**Output:** In-product analytics, demand and no-show reporting, marketplace matching
**Proprietary data:** Booking, rebooking, and lapse behaviour at client-stylist level across the industry — including what happens when a stylist changes salons, which is the industry's central retention question
**Clock:** The 4-8 week rebooking cycle
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Chief Product Officer
