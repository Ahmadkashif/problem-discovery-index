# Professional Beauty Market Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hair-salons-independent|Hair Salons (Independent)]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Specialist research firms size and track the professional salon products market — what salons buy, what they retail, which brands are gaining, how the distributor channel is shifting — and sell the result to the manufacturers who cannot see past their own distributors.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 analysts and researchers
**Output:** Market sizing, brand share estimates, channel and distributor analysis, subscription reports
**Proprietary data:** Long-running salon panels and distributor sell-through series covering a channel with no public data at all
**Clock:** Annual and semi-annual publication cycles
**Buyer:** Practice head / Managing Director
