# Cosmetology School Outcomes & Compliance

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hair-salons-independent|Hair Salons (Independent)]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The compliance and institutional research functions at cosmetology school groups track licensure pass rates, completion, and employment outcomes, and file the federal and accreditor reporting that determines whether the school keeps its Title IV eligibility.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 institutional research and compliance staff per school group
**Output:** Outcomes reporting, accreditation self-studies, federal disclosure filings
**Proprietary data:** Student progression, licensure results, and employment outcomes across many campuses and cohorts
**Clock:** Federal reporting and accreditation deadlines, with the institution's funding attached
**Buyer:** VP of Compliance / Director of Institutional Research
