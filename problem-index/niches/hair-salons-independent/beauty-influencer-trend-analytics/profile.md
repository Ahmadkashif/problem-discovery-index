# Beauty Trend & Social Listening Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hair-salons-independent|Hair Salons (Independent)]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Social intelligence firms track what is emerging in beauty across social platforms — colour trends, ingredient interest, technique virality — and sell the read to brands deciding what to launch and how to market it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 analysts, data scientists, and trend researchers
**Output:** Trend reports, emerging ingredient and colour signals, influencer and campaign analysis
**Proprietary data:** Historical social corpora and the classification models built over them; the underlying platform content is licensed rather than owned
**Clock:** Product development and campaign calendars
**Buyer:** VP of Insights / Head of Data
