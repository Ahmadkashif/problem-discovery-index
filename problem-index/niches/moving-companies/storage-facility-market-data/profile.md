# Self-Storage Market Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/moving-companies|Moving Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Storage market data providers track facility supply, occupancy, and street rates across markets, selling the analysis to operators and investors — and to the moving companies whose storage-in-transit business depends on it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 analysts, data engineers, and researchers
**Output:** Supply and occupancy tracking, street rate indices, market feasibility studies, investment analysis
**Proprietary data:** Scraped and surveyed rate and occupancy data across facility populations
**Clock:** Monthly rate movements and seasonal demand
**Buyer:** Head of Research / VP of Data
