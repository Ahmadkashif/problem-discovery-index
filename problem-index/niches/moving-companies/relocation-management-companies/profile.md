# Relocation Management Companies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/moving-companies|Moving Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Relocation management companies run corporate employee relocation programmes end to end — selecting and managing movers, administering benefits and home sale programmes, auditing invoices, and reporting cost per move back to the employer paying for it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 programme analysts, supplier managers, and cost auditors
**Output:** Programme cost reporting, supplier performance scorecards, policy benchmarking, invoice audit findings
**Proprietary data:** Move-level cost and supplier performance across many corporate programmes and lanes — the only cross-employer view of what relocation actually costs
**Clock:** Employee start dates, which do not move
**Buyer:** VP of Analytics / Chief Client Officer
