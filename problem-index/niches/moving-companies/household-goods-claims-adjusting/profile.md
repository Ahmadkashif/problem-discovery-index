# Household Goods Claims Adjusting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/moving-companies|Moving Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Specialist adjusters handle damage and loss claims on household goods shipments, valuing damaged items, determining liability under released or full value protection, and settling with the customer.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-200 adjusters, valuation specialists, and analysts
**Output:** Claim determinations, item valuations, liability findings, carrier performance reporting
**Proprietary data:** Damage patterns by item type, packing method, crew, and lane across many carriers
**Clock:** Statutory claim filing and settlement periods
**Buyer:** VP of Claims
