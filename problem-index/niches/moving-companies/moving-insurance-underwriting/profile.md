# Moving Company Insurance Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/moving-companies|Moving Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Specialty insurers underwrite cargo, auto liability, and workers' compensation for moving companies, pricing an industry with heavy seasonal labour, high crew turnover, and severe injury exposure.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 underwriters, actuaries, and loss control specialists
**Output:** Rating and eligibility decisions, loss control programmes, claims analysis
**Proprietary data:** Loss experience by carrier, lane, crew composition, and season
**Clock:** Annual renewal, timed ahead of peak season
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
