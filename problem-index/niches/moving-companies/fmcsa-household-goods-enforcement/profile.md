# Household Goods Carrier Enforcement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/moving-companies|Moving Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Federal regulators license interstate household goods carriers, enforce the consumer protection rules governing estimates and hostage-load practices, and publish the complaint and safety records consumers are directed to check.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 investigators, analysts, and enforcement staff
**Output:** Operating authority decisions, complaint investigations, enforcement actions, safety and complaint data publication
**Proprietary data:** Complaint and enforcement records by carrier across the interstate population
**Clock:** Investigation and licensing timelines
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — federal agency
