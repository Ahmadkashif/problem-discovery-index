# Van Line Corporate Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/moving-companies|Moving Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup

## What They Do
The national van lines set the tariffs their agent networks quote from, allocate shipments across agents and drivers, and analyse claims and service performance across hundreds of independent agencies.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 pricing analysts, dispatch planners, and claims analysts
**Output:** Tariff and pricing structures, load allocation, agent and driver performance scoring, claims analysis
**Proprietary data:** Shipment weight, distance, cost, damage claims, and service outcomes across hundreds of agents and decades
**Clock:** The summer peak, which concentrates most of the year's volume into weeks
**Buyer:** VP of Pricing / Chief Analytics Officer
