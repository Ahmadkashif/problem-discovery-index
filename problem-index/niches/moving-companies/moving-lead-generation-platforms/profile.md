# Moving Lead Generation Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/moving-companies|Moving Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Lead marketplaces capture consumers searching for movers and sell the enquiries to operators, mediating most of the customer acquisition in a fragmented industry.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 data scientists, marketplace analysts, and quality staff
**Output:** Lead scoring and routing, pricing, conversion reporting, fraud and duplicate detection
**Proprietary data:** Consumer enquiry behaviour and conversion outcomes across lanes, seasons, and operators
**Clock:** Move dates and the summer peak
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Chief Product Officer
