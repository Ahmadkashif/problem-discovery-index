# Movers Association Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/moving-companies|Moving Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The moving and storage association publishes operating benchmarks and tariff guidance for member carriers and advocates on the consumer protection and licensing rules governing interstate moves.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-25 researchers and policy staff
**Output:** Operating benchmarks, tariff and rate guidance, regulatory analysis, certification programmes
**Proprietary data:** Member operating surveys and long-running rate series
**Clock:** Annual reporting and rulemaking cycles
**Buyer:** Director of Research — member funded
