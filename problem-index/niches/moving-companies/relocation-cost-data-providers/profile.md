# Relocation Cost Data Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/moving-companies|Moving Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Cost-of-living and relocation data providers supply the housing, goods, and services cost differentials employers use to set relocation allowances and assignment pay.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-70 economists, price collectors, and analysts
**Output:** Cost-of-living indices, housing differentials, allowance calculators, assignment cost models
**Proprietary data:** Priced market baskets collected across locations over decades
**Clock:** Quarterly index updates and annual compensation cycles
**Buyer:** Head of Research
