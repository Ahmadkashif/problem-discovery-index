# Moving Software Vendor Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/moving-companies|Moving Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The moving management platforms run estimating, dispatch, and billing for the minority of operators who use them. Pass 1 records that most independent movers run on spreadsheets, whiteboards, and phone calls.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 product and data staff
**Output:** Estimating tools, dispatch and crew scheduling, job costing, customer communication
**Proprietary data:** Estimated versus actual weight, hours, and cost across participating operators — the exact gap the industry prices blind on
**Clock:** Daily dispatch and the summer peak
**Buyer:** Head of Product
