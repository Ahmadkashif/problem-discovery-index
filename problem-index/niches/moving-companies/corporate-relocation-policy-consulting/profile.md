# Corporate Relocation Policy Consulting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/moving-companies|Moving Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Consultancies design employer relocation policies — what benefits to offer, at what cost, benchmarked against peer employers — and run the supplier selection processes that award relocation programmes.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-70 consultants and analysts
**Output:** Policy design and benchmarking, cost modelling, supplier selection, programme audits
**Proprietary data:** Policy provisions and cost benchmarks across many employers
**Clock:** Annual policy review and programme renewal cycles
**Buyer:** Practice leader
