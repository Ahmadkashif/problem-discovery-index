## The Problem
US-flag operators must crew with US mariners holding current USCG credentials, STCW endorsements and medical certificates. Operators in protected trades must also prove the US citizenship of their ownership chain, and MSP and cargo-preference eligibility add their own conditions. Mariners rotate through union halls and multiple operators; credentials expire on different dates; ownership changes ripple into eligibility. Records live in crewing spreadsheets, union dispatch systems and legal files.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The market is a few dozen operators. Crewing software is built for international ship managers with global crew pools, not US union dispatch and citizenship rules.

## What to Build
One compliance record per ship and per mariner: credentials and endorsements read from documents, expiry forecasts against rotation plans, and eligibility status for each programme the ship depends on. Alerts reach the operator weeks before a credential lapse or an ownership change puts a ship's trading rights at risk.

## Target Customer
Marine personnel and compliance managers at US-flag operators and their ship managers.

## Impact If Built
Avoiding one day's delay at the ~$50,000–80,000 a day it costs to run a US-flag ship (estimate) covers a year of the tool for a small operator (estimate). Programme eligibility can then be shown with evidence on demand.
