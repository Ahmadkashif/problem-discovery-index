# Third-Party Ship Managers' Fleet Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ocean-shipping-carriers|Ocean Shipping Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
V.Group, Anglo-Eastern, Synergy Marine, Bernhard Schulte, Wilhelmsen and Fleet Management Ltd (recalled) crew, maintain and operate ships for owners for a management fee. Each manages hundreds of ships, and each runs central performance, safety and cost analytics across them. They are the natural rollup of the operator layer and the employers of many of the officers in [[niches/ocean-shipping-carriers/ships-officers-engineers/profile|🟣 Ships' Officers & Engineers]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–50 fleet performance and analytics staff per large manager (estimate)
**Output:** Fleet performance and cost benchmarking, safety analytics, owner reports
**Proprietary data:** Operating, maintenance, crew and incident data across managed fleets, belonging to the owners
**Clock:** Owners' reporting cycles, vetting and inspection schedules
**Buyer:** Chief Operating Officer / Head of Fleet Performance
