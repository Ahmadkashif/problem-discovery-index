# Rate Evidence Rebuilt for Every Tender

**Niche:** [[niches/ocean-shipping-carriers/us-flag-preference-cargo/profile|US-Flag Preference Cargo Carriers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/ocean-shipping-carriers|Ocean Shipping Carriers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Each preference-cargo bid needs cost evidence to show the rate is fair and reasonable, and the evidence is reassembled from scratch each time.
**Tags:** #feature-engineering #compliance #quick-win #automation #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to have a cargo-preference-eligible US-flag ship available where government-impelled cargo loads, and whoever has a compliant ship inside the most tenders' loading windows takes the account.

## The Problem
Vessel operating costs, crew costs, port charges and voyage days are pulled from accounts and spreadsheets for each bid, under a short tender deadline.

## Why It's Still Broken
Cost data sits in finance systems and is never kept in the shape the rate review asks for.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Maintain a current per-ship, per-route cost model in the review format, so each bid's rate evidence is generated in minutes and stays consistent between bids.

## Who Feels the Pain
Commercial and finance staff at US-flag carriers bidding against tight deadlines.

## Impact If Fixed
Rate evidence prepared in minutes rather than ~1–2 days per bid (estimate) across ~50–150 bids a year per carrier (estimate), consistent from one bid to the next.
