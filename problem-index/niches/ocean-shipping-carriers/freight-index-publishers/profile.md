# Shipping Freight Index & Assessment Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ocean-shipping-carriers|Ocean Shipping Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
The Baltic Exchange publishes daily dry bulk, tanker and container route assessments from panels of member shipbrokers, and forward freight agreements settle against them (recalled). The Shanghai Shipping Exchange publishes the weekly SCFI and CCFI container indices (recalled), and Drewry's World Container Index comes out every Thursday ($4,585 per 40ft box on 22 October 2026). Carriers, charterers, traders and index-linked contracts use them as the market price. Container contract-rate benchmarks built from shipper contributions are already profiled at [[niches/cold-chain-logistics/freight-rate-benchmark-platforms/profile|🔍 Freight Rate Benchmark Platforms]] (54, Xeneta and Freightos), and the energy agencies' freight desks at [[niches/oil-gas-field-services/energy-price-reporting-crossref/profile|🔍 Energy Price Reporting Agencies]] (53); neither is profiled again.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 index, methodology and data staff at the Baltic Exchange, similar at the others (estimate); panel assessors are member brokers, not the publishers' staff
**Output:** Route assessments, indices, settlement prices, historical data licences
**Proprietary data:** Panel assessment histories and index methodologies
**Clock:** The publishers' own daily or weekly calendars
**Buyer:** Head of Indices / Chief Executive
