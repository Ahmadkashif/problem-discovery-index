# Ship Valuation & Sale-and-Purchase Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ocean-shipping-carriers|Ocean Shipping Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
VesselsValue (bought by Veson Nautical in 2023), Clarksons Valuations, MSI and the valuation desks of shipbrokers such as Braemar and BRS (recalled) value ships for loan covenants, financing, sale and purchase, fund reporting and impairment tests. Lenders commonly ask for two or three independent valuations at each loan-to-value test (estimate), and the model-based providers sell daily values for whole fleets.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 valuation modellers and analysts at the largest providers (estimate); broker valuers issuing per-ship certificates are production under this run's per-job appraiser ruling
**Output:** Valuation certificates, model-based daily values, residual and demolition forecasts, fleet value reports for lenders
**Proprietary data:** Sale-and-purchase transaction records, valuation histories and broker market reads; AIS-derived trading and condition data at VesselsValue
**Clock:** Lenders' loan-to-value covenant test dates and owners' reporting dates — contract dates — plus year-end audits for listed owners
**Buyer:** Head of Valuations / Chief Product Officer
