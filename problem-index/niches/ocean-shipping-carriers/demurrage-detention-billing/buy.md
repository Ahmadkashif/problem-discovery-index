# Receivables Dispute Software Adapted to 46 CFR 541

**Niche:** [[niches/ocean-shipping-carriers/demurrage-detention-billing/profile|Demurrage & Detention Billing and Disputes]]
**Industry:** [[industries/ocean-shipping-carriers|Ocean Shipping Carriers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Accounts-receivable and dispute-management tools track cases and deadlines; the FMC rule adds invoice content, certification and timing rules they do not know.
**Tags:** #bert #large-language-models #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to bill every chargeable container day inside the FMC's 30-day window on an invoice that survives dispute, and whoever collects the highest share of billed demurrage and detention without waiver takes the account.

## The Problem
Carriers run D&D disputes through generic receivables tools or email, with deadlines tracked by hand.

## What Already Exists
AR automation and dispute platforms (HighRadius, Billtrust, SAP and Oracle modules) with case workflows, aging and collections.

## The Customization Gap
The rule's mandatory invoice elements, the 30-day issuance and resolution clocks, the NVOCC pass-through extension, certifications and the URL for dispute documentation. Dispute reasons specific to containers (closed terminal, no appointment, vessel delay, wrong party) must be classified and matched to milestone evidence.

## Target Customer
Finance and billing teams at carriers already on an AR platform.

## Impact If Solved
Moves ~5,000–10,000 disputes a year at a large carrier (estimate) onto tracked 30-day clocks, building compliance into the receivables process rather than checking it after the fact.
