# Classification Societies' Rules & Advisory Units

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ocean-shipping-carriers|Ocean Shipping Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The IACS member societies — DNV, ABS, Lloyd's Register, ClassNK, Bureau Veritas, KR, CCS, RINA and others (recalled) — write technical rules and class ships through design approval and periodic surveys. They issue statutory certificates on behalf of flag states and sell advisory work on fuels, emissions, performance and digital systems. Their published fuel and decarbonisation outlooks are widely cited.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Hundreds of rule developers and advisory consultants at the largest societies (estimate); surveyors and plan approvers are production
**Output:** Class rules, design approvals, statutory certificates, advisory studies and outlooks
**Proprietary data:** Survey, damage and condition records across each society's classed fleet
**Clock:** Advisory work runs to client project timetables; statutory survey windows and certificate expiry govern the surveys, which are production
**Buyer:** Director of Maritime Advisory / Head of Rules
