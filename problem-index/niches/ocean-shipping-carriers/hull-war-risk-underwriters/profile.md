# Hull, Machinery & War Risk Underwriters

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ocean-shipping-carriers|Ocean Shipping Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Nordic mutual hull clubs (Norwegian Hull Club, the Swedish Club, Gard's hull book), Lloyd's marine syndicates and company markets insure ocean-going ships against physical loss and damage. War-risk underwriters charge additional premiums for voyages into areas listed by the Lloyd's Market Association's Joint War Committee, including the Red Sea since 2023 (recalled). The marine-construction fleet book is profiled separately at [[niches/heavy-civil-contractors/marine-contractor-hull-pi-underwriting/profile|🔍 Marine Contractors' Hull, P&I & Jones Act Underwriting]] (32), and cargo underwriting at [[niches/freight-brokerage/cargo-insurance-claims-analytics/profile|🔍 Cargo Insurance & Claims Analytics]] (34).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Pricing and actuarial staff dedicated to ocean hull plausibly under 10 per market (estimate); underwriters pricing each risk and claims analysts handling each case are production and not counted
**Output:** Hull and war-risk pricing, survey requirements, claims analysis
**Proprietary data:** Hull claims histories by ship type, age and operator; war-risk voyage records
**Clock:** Annual renewals (contract dates) and listed-area changes set by the Joint War Committee
**Buyer:** Head of Marine Hull or War Underwriting
