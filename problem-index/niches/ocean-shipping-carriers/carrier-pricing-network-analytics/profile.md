# Container Lines' In-House Pricing & Network Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ocean-shipping-carriers|Ocean Shipping Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Pricing, revenue-management and network-planning teams inside the top container lines and their alliances decide rates, allocations, port rotations and capacity deployment. Concentration is high: MSC holds about 21.5% of global fleet capacity, Maersk 13.8% and CMA CGM 12.8% (Alphaliner, search summary). The decision gap inside these teams is the industry's high-impact problem, [[problems/ocean-shipping-carriers/high-impact|The Allocation Call Nobody Records]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~50–300 per top carrier across pricing, revenue management and network design (estimate)
**Output:** Rates, allocations and network designs, consumed internally
**Proprietary data:** Complete booking, rate and load histories for the carrier's own network
**Clock:** Annual contract seasons and alliance network changes, both set by the carriers
**Buyer:** Chief Commercial Officer
