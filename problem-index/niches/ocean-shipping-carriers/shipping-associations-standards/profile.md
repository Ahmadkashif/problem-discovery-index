# Shipping Associations & Standards Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ocean-shipping-carriers|Ocean Shipping Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The World Shipping Council speaks for container lines on policy. BIMCO publishes standard contracts (GENCON, NYPE, SHIPMAN) and market commentary. The International Chamber of Shipping represents national shipowner associations, and the Chamber of Shipping of America represents US owners. DCSA writes the digital standards for booking, track-and-trace and the electronic bill of lading that its member carriers pledged to adopt fully by 2030 (DCSA). Other names are recalled.
