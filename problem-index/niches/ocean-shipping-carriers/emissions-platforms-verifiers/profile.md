# Maritime Emissions Compliance Platforms & Verifiers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ocean-shipping-carriers|Ocean Shipping Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Platforms such as ZeroNorth, DNV's emissions tools, Siglar Carbon and Marasoft compile ships' EU MRV, EU ETS, FuelEU Maritime and IMO DCS reports from operators' noon and fuel data. Accredited verifiers, the class societies and independents such as Verifavia, check them (recalled names). Operators pay per ship per year for the platforms and per report for verification. The operator-side work is the niche [[niches/ocean-shipping-carriers/emissions-monitoring-reporting/profile|🟠 Emissions Monitoring, Reporting & Verification]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–50 regulatory and data analysts per larger platform (estimate), the only non-production function here; verifiers' per-ship examiners are production
**Output:** Verified emissions reports, allowance and FuelEU balance calculations, monitoring plans
**Proprietary data:** Operators' monitoring data under approved plans; whether the platforms may reuse it across clients depends on SaaS terms this build did not read
**Clock:** Regulator deadlines on the deliverable itself — verified EU reports by 31 March and allowances by 30 September (European Commission); FuelEU report by 31 January and Document of Compliance by 30 June (search summary)
**Buyer:** Head of Regulatory Products / Head of Verification

**Kill switches:** split by regime. The verifier half is live: operators' data is examined in an accredited verification engagement and held under accreditation confidentiality. The platform half is undetermined (terms unread): the platforms process operators' monitoring data under SaaS agreements whose reuse terms this build did not read.
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. The verdict follows the platform half, because the verifiers' examiners are production and the platforms' analysts are the only insight function counted. This is the one pocket in the sweep with a hard regulatory clock on its own deliverable (Q4 5). As buyers, the platforms and accredited verifiers number a few dozen, the largest of them class societies (Q5 3). Q3 is 2 while reuse rights are unknown. Even if the terms granted product rights, Q3 would rise to 3 or 4 and the total to 40–43, still below threshold.
