# Island Arrival Forecasts Shippers Can Plan Shelves Around

**Niche:** [[niches/ocean-shipping-carriers/jones-act-noncontiguous-trades/profile|Jones Act Noncontiguous Liner Trades]]
**Industry:** [[industries/ocean-shipping-carriers|Ocean Shipping Carriers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Island retailers need to know days ahead whether Wednesday's ship will be Wednesday's ship, and today they learn when it is late.
**Tags:** #temporal-fusion-transformers #time-series-forecasting #gradient-boosting #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver an island's weekly freight on the day the island plans around, and whoever lands the most containers on the scheduled arrival day takes the account.

## The Problem
A grocery distributor in Honolulu or Anchorage schedules receiving crews, trucks and promotions around the weekly arrival. When weather on the crossing, a mainland port delay or a terminal problem moves the ship, the customer finds out from a schedule notice late in the voyage.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Arrival-prediction vendors focus on international trades with many carriers and ports. Noncontiguous trades are few lanes with a few customers each, too small for general visibility vendors to tune.

## What to Build
A per-voyage arrival and container-availability forecast with an honest range, using the ship's position and speed, weather routing on the crossing, mainland departure delays and terminal performance at the island port. It updates daily and pushes each customer's expected availability with alerts when the probability of missing the planned day crosses a threshold.

## Target Customer
Noncontiguous carriers' customer service and operations heads, for their largest retail and grocery accounts.

## Impact If Built
Raising on-day arrivals from ~80% to ~90% (estimate) on a weekly service gives every customer on it five more on-time arrivals a year. The carrier's on-day record, which is what customers buy, becomes visible and defensible.
