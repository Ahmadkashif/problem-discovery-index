# Weather Routing & Voyage Performance Analysts

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ocean-shipping-carriers|Ocean Shipping Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Weathernews, StormGeo and DTN (recalled) route ships around weather for owners and charterers and produce post-voyage performance evaluations. Those evaluations are the evidence in charter party speed and consumption claims. Newer entrants such as Sofar Ocean's Wayfinder (recalled) sell model-based routing, and the same speed–fuel models feed emissions forecasting ([[problems/ocean-shipping-carriers/ml-opportunity|ML opportunity 3]]).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–80 meteorologists, route analysts and performance modellers per firm (estimate); per-voyage claim analysts are production
**Output:** Route advisories, post-voyage performance reports, claim evidence
**Proprietary data:** Archives of routed voyages and their performance outcomes; weather inputs are licensed public model output, and vessel data comes from clients' noon reports
**Clock:** Charter party claim time bars — contract terms
**Buyer:** Head of Marine Services

**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. The routing model is the firm's own, but its inputs are public weather and clients' reports (Q3 3), and the claim clock is contractual (Q4 3). Routing is model-generated with analysts checking it, so headcount does not grow with voyages (Q2 2), and a handful of firms make up the field (Q5 2). That matches [[niches/broadcast-stations/tv-weather-data-providers/profile|🔍 TV Weather Data & Graphics Providers]] (35), where DTN also appears.
