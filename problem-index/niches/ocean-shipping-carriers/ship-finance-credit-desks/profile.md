# Ship Finance Lenders' Credit & Climate-Alignment Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ocean-shipping-carriers|Ocean Shipping Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Shipping desks at banks such as DNB, ABN AMRO, BNP Paribas, Crédit Agricole and KfW IPEX, and Chinese leasing houses (recalled), lend against ships. They analyse owners' credit, monitor loan-to-value covenants with broker valuations and compute portfolio climate alignment for Poseidon Principles disclosure (recalled). Vessel operating lessors belong to the queued aircraft-and-railcar-lessors industry.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 portfolio, risk-modelling and research analysts per major lender (estimate); credit analysts working each deal are production and not counted
**Output:** Credit approvals, covenant monitoring, portfolio climate-alignment scores
**Proprietary data:** Borrowers' financials, fleet data and fuel-consumption data supplied under loan agreements
**Clock:** Covenant test dates and the annual alignment disclosure
**Buyer:** Global Head of Shipping Finance
