# Maritime Security & Threat Intelligence Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ocean-shipping-carriers|Ocean Shipping Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Ambrey, Dryad Global and Risk Intelligence (recalled) sell threat assessments, voyage risk reports and incident alerts for the Red Sea, the Gulf of Guinea and other high-risk areas. Their buyers are carriers, charterers, insurers and governments, and some of these firms also supply armed guards. The Red Sea return in late 2026, with 63% of Far East–Europe capacity back on Suez routings by mid-October (Alphaliner), puts every routing decision through this kind of assessment. Vessel-behaviour risk scoring from satellite data is profiled at [[niches/satellite-operators/maritime-domain-awareness-analytics/profile|🔍 Satellite Maritime Domain Awareness Analytics]] (40) and is not profiled again.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~15–50 intelligence analysts per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Threat assessments, voyage risk reports, incident alerts, advisory
**Proprietary data:** Incident databases and analyst source networks; inputs are largely open-source
**Clock:** Sailing dates are the client's timetable, and events set the pace
**Buyer:** Head of Intelligence
