# Demurrage, Detention & Laytime Claims Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ocean-shipping-carriers|Ocean Shipping Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Outsourced desks calculate and negotiate tanker and bulk laytime and demurrage claims for owners and charterers. Marcura, for example, lists Senior Laytime Analyst roles in Taguig on its own board (checked 2026-10-24). Freight-audit firms check liner demurrage and detention invoices for importers against free-time terms and the FMC billing rule.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Per-claim laytime analysts and invoice auditors, who are production under this run's ruling; the staff behind the method are plausibly under 10 per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Laytime calculations, claim submissions, invoice audit findings
**Proprietary data:** Clients' charter parties, statements of facts and invoices
**Clock:** Charter party claim time bars and the FMC's 30-day dispute window — per case
**Buyer:** Head of Claims / Operations Director
