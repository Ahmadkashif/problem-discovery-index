# Bunker Brokers, Suppliers & Fuel-Testing Labs

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ocean-shipping-carriers|Ocean Shipping Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Bunker traders and brokers sell marine fuel to carriers at ports worldwide: Bunker Holding (with KPI OceanConnect and Minerva), Integr8 Fuels and Monjasa (recalled). Fuel-testing laboratories ([[industries/testing-laboratories|Testing Laboratories]]) such as VPS and LR's FOBAS test delivered samples against ISO 8217 and the MARPOL sulphur limits (recalled). Fuel is the largest voyage cost, and it now also carries EU ETS and FuelEU charges.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Traders working per deal and lab analysts per sample, who are production; market-research teams publishing bunker reports are a handful per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Fuel supply, price quotes, test reports, market commentary
**Proprietary data:** Transaction records and sample-test histories
**Clock:** Delivery dates and supplier claim time limits — per deal
**Buyer:** Head of Trading / Laboratory Director
