# US-Flag Programme Administration (MARAD and Sealift Contracting)

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ocean-shipping-carriers|Ocean Shipping Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Maritime Administration runs the Maritime Security Program, which pays $6.5M per enrolled ship in fiscal 2025–26 under 46 U.S.C. 53106, monitors cargo-preference compliance and keeps US-flag fleet data. USTRANSCOM and Military Sealift Command contract commercial sealift (recalled). Their rules shape the sub-niche [[niches/ocean-shipping-carriers/us-flag-preference-cargo/profile|🎯 US-Flag Preference Cargo Carriers]].

**Output:** Operating agreements, payments, compliance determinations, sealift contracts
**Proprietary data:** Operators' programme filings
**Clock:** Appropriations and fiscal-year cycles
**Buyer:** Associate Administrator
