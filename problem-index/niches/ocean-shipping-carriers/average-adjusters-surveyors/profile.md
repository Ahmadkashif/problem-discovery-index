# Average Adjusters, Marine Surveyors & Casualty Experts

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ocean-shipping-carriers|Ocean Shipping Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Average adjusters, such as Richards Hogg Lindley and its peers (recalled), apportion general-average sacrifices between ship and cargo after a casualty. Marine surveyors and casualty experts inspect damage, establish cause and give evidence in disputes. Cargo-loss expert witnesses are logged at [[niches/logistics-consultants/logistics-expert-witness-claims/profile|🔍 Logistics Expert Witness & Cargo Claims Consultants]] (fails gate).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Per-case adjusters and surveyors, who are production; research staff are a handful per firm (estimate)
**Output:** General-average adjustments, survey reports, expert evidence
**Proprietary data:** Case files belonging to the casualty's parties
**Clock:** Case and court timetables
**Buyer:** Senior partner
