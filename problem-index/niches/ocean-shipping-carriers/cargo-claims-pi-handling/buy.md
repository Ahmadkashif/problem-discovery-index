# Insurance Claims Platforms Adapted to Hague-Visby Claims

**Niche:** [[niches/ocean-shipping-carriers/cargo-claims-pi-handling/profile|Cargo Claims & P&I Handling]]
**Industry:** [[industries/ocean-shipping-carriers|Ocean Shipping Carriers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Insurers' claims platforms run intake, reserves and settlement workflows well; a carrier defending cargo claims needs maritime liability rules and its own operational evidence attached.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #evaluation-metrics #compliance #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to resolve each cargo claim inside the one-year Hague-Visby time bar for the least money, and whoever settles claims at the lowest paid-to-claimed ratio takes the account.

## The Problem
Carrier claims teams track cases in spreadsheets or a thin module of the booking system while insurers on the other side use mature claims platforms.

## What Already Exists
Claims platforms built for [[industries/property-casualty-insurers|Property & Casualty Insurers]] (Guidewire ClaimCenter, Duck Creek, Sapiens) handle intake, reserves, correspondence and settlement workflows.

## The Customization Gap
A carrier-side claim is a defence, not an indemnity. The platform needs the Hague-Visby and Hamburg time bars and limitation formulas, the US COGSA equivalents, B/L clause libraries, and the carrier's exceptions (perils of the sea, inherent vice, insufficient packing). It also needs links to reefer, stowage and voyage data and to the P&I club's deductible and notification rules. The customisation is a maritime liability layer and operational data feeds.

## Target Customer
Insurance and claims managers at mid-sized carriers that want a supported platform rather than an internal build.

## Impact If Solved
Built-in time bars that prevent even one defaulted ~$100,000 claim a year (estimate) pay for the platform. Pulling evidence automatically cuts handler time per claim by an estimated ~20–30% (estimate).
