# Shipbroker Research Desks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ocean-shipping-carriers|Ocean Shipping Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Research teams inside shipbrokers — Clarksons' broking research, Braemar, SSY, Gibson, Fearnleys, BRS and Poten & Partners (recalled) — write weekly market reports, annual reviews and client briefs. The research supports the brokers' commission business on charters and sale-and-purchase deals; it is rarely sold on its own. The subscription research arms are profiled at [[niches/ocean-shipping-carriers/maritime-research-houses/profile|🔍 Maritime Research & Market Intelligence Houses]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 analysts per larger house (estimate)
**Output:** Weekly market reports, fixture lists, annual reviews, bespoke analysis for clients
**Proprietary data:** The house's own fixture and negotiation records
**Clock:** Self-set weekly reports
**Buyer:** Head of Research
