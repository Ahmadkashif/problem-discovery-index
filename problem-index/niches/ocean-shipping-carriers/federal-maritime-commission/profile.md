# Federal Maritime Commission

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ocean-shipping-carriers|Ocean Shipping Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The FMC regulates ocean common carriers, NVOCCs and marine terminal operators in US trades. It reviews and monitors carrier agreements, including the vessel-sharing alliances, enforces OSRA 2022 and the 2024 demurrage and detention billing rule (46 CFR 541), and hears charge complaints (recalled except the rule).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–50 economists and analysts in trade analysis and enforcement (estimate)
**Output:** Agreement monitoring, competition analysis, enforcement actions and rulemaking
**Proprietary data:** Carriers' confidential agreement minutes, monitoring reports and filings
**Clock:** Statutory review periods for agreements and complaint deadlines (recalled)
**Buyer:** Director, Bureau of Trade Analysis
