# P&I Clubs' Loss Prevention & Claims Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ocean-shipping-carriers|Ocean Shipping Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The twelve International Group P&I clubs insure about 87% of the world's ocean-going tonnage against third-party liabilities: cargo, crew injury, collision, pollution and wreck removal. Each club retains claims up to $10M and shares larger ones through the Pooling Agreement (IG P&I). Loss-prevention departments publish circulars and casualty analyses, and underwriting and claims teams analyse members' records to set premiums at the annual renewal. Cargo claims that carriers pass to their club are the other side of [[niches/ocean-shipping-carriers/cargo-claims-pi-handling/profile|🟠 Cargo Claims & P&I Handling]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–20 loss-prevention, actuarial and analytics staff per large club (estimate); underwriters pricing each member and claims handlers working each case are production and not counted
**Output:** Premium setting, loss-prevention guidance, casualty studies, claims handling for members
**Proprietary data:** Decades of members' claims, casualty and survey records, the deepest liability dataset in shipping
**Clock:** The clubs' common annual renewal date (20 February, recalled), which they set themselves
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer / Head of Loss Prevention
