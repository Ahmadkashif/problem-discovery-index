# Electronic Title, Lien & Title-Processing Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wholesale-auto-auctions|Wholesale Auto Auctions]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Providers that connect lenders to state electronic lien and title (ELT) systems, hold and release titles for lienholders, and process title work for auctions, fleets and dealers. Examples are ELT service providers such as DDI Technology and the dealer-software vendors' title services (named from recall). They are paid per title or per transaction.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Title processors and clerks are production; no research team (estimate)
**Output:** Lien perfection and release, title transfers, custody reports
**Proprietary data:** Title and lien status records held for lender, fleet and auction clients
**Clock:** State title processing times and the auction's title windows (client clocks)
**Buyer:** VP of operations
