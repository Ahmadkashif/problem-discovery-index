# Auction Management Systems & Independent Sale Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wholesale-auto-auctions|Wholesale Auto Auctions]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Auction Edge sells the management system, simulcast and the EDGE Pipeline listing network that most independent auctions run on (~206 auctions, a third-party count; search summary). It also sells the Pipeline Market Report, built from sale data at 175+ of those auctions and licensed into OPENLANE's autoniq buyer tool (search summary). Revenue is software and listing fees, with the market report on top.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under ~10 analysts behind the market report (estimate); support and implementation staff are production
**Output:** Auction software; the Pipeline Market Report data feed
**Proprietary data:** Sale results from the independent auctions on its platform
**Clock:** Client sale days
**Buyer:** CEO / head of product
