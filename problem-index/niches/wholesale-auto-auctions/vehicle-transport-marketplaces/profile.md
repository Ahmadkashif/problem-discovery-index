# Vehicle Transport Marketplaces & Load Boards

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wholesale-auto-auctions|Wholesale Auto Auctions]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Platforms that match cars needing transport with car haulers: Cox's Central Dispatch load board, dispatch and booking platforms such as Super Dispatch and RunBuggy (named from recall), and the auctions' own transport desks (ACV Transportation, OPENLANE's carrier services). Shippers post loads, carriers book them, and the platform earns subscriptions or a margin per move.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Rate and pricing analytics under ~10 per firm (estimate); dispatchers and carrier-support staff are production
**Output:** Rate quotes, load matching, occasional market rate commentary
**Proprietary data:** Posted loads, booked rates and carrier performance on the platform
**Clock:** Pickup and delivery dates set by shippers (client clocks)
**Buyer:** Head of product / VP of operations
