# The Run List Posted the Night Before Without Photos

**Niche:** [[niches/wholesale-auto-auctions/independent-auctions/profile|Independent Auctions]]
**Industry:** [[industries/wholesale-auto-auctions|Wholesale Auto Auctions]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Dealers plan their buying from run lists, and an independent's list goes up late, half-photographed and without condition notes, so remote buyers skip it.
**Tags:** #cnns #transfer-learning #large-language-models #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep an independent auction's dealer consignors from moving to the national chains, and whoever keeps the largest share of its dealer consignors' wholesale units year on year takes the account.

## The Problem
Consignments arrive until the day before the sale. The independent's small staff check them in, take a few photos and post the run list the evening before, often with gaps: no undercarriage, no interior, no notes. National chains publish fuller listings earlier. ACV's run list opens up to 24 hours before a sale, with full condition reports. Remote dealers prepare their buying from the fuller lists.

## Why It's Still Broken
The bottleneck is labour at check-in: one or two people photograph and describe dozens of cars against a deadline. Independents cannot run an inspector network.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A guided phone capture at check-in: a fixed shot list with automatic quality checks, VIN decode through NHTSA vPIC to fill equipment, and an AI draft of the condition notes for the clerk to confirm. Each car is posted as soon as it is captured rather than in one batch the night before.

## Who Feels the Pain
Independent auction staff working late before sale day; remote dealers bidding blind or not at all; consignors whose cars run to thin rooms.

## Impact If Fixed
Posting complete listings a day earlier could lift online views per unit by a third (estimate). If that adds even half a bidder per car, conversion rises ~1 point, about 250 more sales a year at an auction offering 25,000 units. Dealers who see their units listed fully and early are the ones who keep consigning (estimate).
