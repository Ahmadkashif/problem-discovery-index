# Arbitration & Post-Sale Claims

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wholesale-auto-auctions|Wholesale Auto Auctions]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to settle buyers' post-sale claims under the NAAA guidelines without taking the car back, and whoever settles the largest share of granted claims by price adjustment rather than unwinding the sale takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M a year in arbitration staff, post-sale inspections, case handling and claim tooling across US auctions (estimate), separate from the ~$150–300M of adjustments and unwinds themselves (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5–6% of auction, service and floorplan revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — claims arrive by portal, phone and email; evidence is photos, videos and repair-estimate PDFs gathered by hand; post-sale inspections are physical
**Target Buyer:** Director of arbitration or VP of customer assurance at an auction; owner or GM at an independent
**Automation Potential:** Medium — case assembly, threshold checks and adjustment proposals are automatable; the borderline call stays with a person

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Arbitration is what makes buying sight-unseen possible, and the NAAA rewrote its rules on 1 June 2026. The $800 per-defect threshold stays below $50,000, with 2% of the price at $50,000 and above. Generic "No Arb" announcements no longer protect sellers, warning-light announcements no longer cover the cause, and ADAS components are arbitrable. Windows are short: Manheim gives digital buyers 10 calendar days and asks for evidence within 5. A granted claim ends in one of two ways. The buyer keeps the car with a price adjustment, or the sale is unwound and the car comes back to be relisted. An unwind costs far more. The competitors are the auctions' in-house desks, which compete for buyers and sellers on how claims end, and the assurance products that move claim cost around: ACV's Go Green seller assurance (part of $81.6M of customer assurance revenue in 2025) and Cox's DealShield returns. Pre-sale disclosure, the cause of most claims, is [[niches/wholesale-auto-auctions/announcement-disclosure-checks/profile|Announcement & Disclosure Checks]]. Whether the report was right is the data question at [[niches/auto-dealers-independent/auction-condition-report-ops/profile|Wholesale Auction Condition Report Operations]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Auction case-management modules, arbitration portals, post-sale inspection lanes, email and phone. ACV is hiring a Software Engineer IV, Arbitrations in Chennai (2026-08-27), and OPENLANE a claim-resolutions specialist (2026-10-08). Gaps: no adjustment estimate the buyer trusts; cases assembled by hand from four systems; and the June rule changes held in memos.
