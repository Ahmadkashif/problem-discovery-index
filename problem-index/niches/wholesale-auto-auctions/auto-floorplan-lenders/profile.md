# Independent Auto Floorplan Lenders

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wholesale-auto-auctions|Wholesale Auto Auctions]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The lenders that finance independent dealers' wholesale purchases: OPENLANE's AFC (~11,600 active dealers, average line ~$379,000, ~1.7 million floorplan transactions in 2025), Cox's NextGear Capital, ACV Capital, and independents such as Westlake Flooring, Kinetic Advantage and Car Financial (named from recall). They set lines and curtailment terms, fund purchases at the auction and audit lots. The insight is priced into the credit, not sold.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Per-dealer credit and risk specialists are production; portfolio analytics is under ~10 per lender (estimate)
**Output:** Credit lines, curtailment schedules, audit follow-ups
**Proprietary data:** Unit-level floorplan, payoff and audit histories
**Clock:** Curtailment and payoff dates on each line (client clocks)
**Buyer:** Chief credit officer
