# Wholesale Price Index & Market Insights Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wholesale-auto-auctions|Wholesale Auto Auctions]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
The economists and analysts who publish the wholesale market's public numbers. Cox Automotive's industry insights team publishes the Manheim Used Vehicle Value Index (205.9 in September 2026, down 0.6% year on year), with a mid-month reading and an official figure on the fifth business day. It also publishes wholesale supply, conversion, segment indices and forecasts such as ~11.8 million wholesale units in 2026. The national auctions and value-guide publishers issue market reports of their own. The index is free. It sells the auction, its MMR and its advisory relationships, and lenders, analysts and the press use it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–25 economists and analysts at the largest publisher (estimate); smaller teams elsewhere
**Output:** Monthly price index, mid-month flash, quarterly calls, annual forecasts, market commentary
**Proprietary data:** The publisher's own auction transaction record, the same corpus behind its licensed valuations
**Clock:** The publisher's own release calendar (fifth business day; next release 6 November 2026)
**Buyer:** Chief economist / head of industry insights
