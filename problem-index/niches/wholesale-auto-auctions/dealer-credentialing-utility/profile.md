# Dealer Credentialing Utility (AuctionACCESS)

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wholesale-auto-auctions|Wholesale Auto Auctions]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
AuctionACCESS issues the credential that admits licensed dealers and their representatives to dealer-only auctions. It is accepted at more than 400 marketplaces in the US and Canada, for $103 a year per individual (search summary). It verifies licences and identities so each auction does not have to.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Verification and support staff are production; no research team (estimate)
**Output:** Dealer credentials and verification status
**Proprietary data:** Dealer licence, bond and representative records
**Clock:** State dealer-licence renewal dates
**Buyer:** General manager
