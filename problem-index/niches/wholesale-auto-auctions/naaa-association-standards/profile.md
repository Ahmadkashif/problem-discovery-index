# National Auto Auction Association Standards & Survey

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wholesale-auto-auctions|Wholesale Auto Auctions]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The NAAA writes the Arbitration Guidelines most US auctions adopt. The version effective 1 June 2026 kept the $800 threshold, added the 2% rule for cars at $50,000 and up, banned generic "No Arb" announcements and added ADAS. The association also publishes the annual Market Intelligence Report: 12.3 million offered, more than 8 million sold, 58.5% conversion and gross sales above $100B in 2025. It runs training and certification for auction staff. Members pay dues.

## Insight Function
**Size:** A small staff with volunteer committees; research is under ~10 people (estimate)
**Output:** Arbitration Guidelines and position statements; the annual member survey report
**Proprietary data:** Member auctions' survey responses
**Clock:** The association's own guideline and report calendar
**Buyer:** Executive director
