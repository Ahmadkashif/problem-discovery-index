# Captive Finance Lease-End Remarketing Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wholesale-auto-auctions|Wholesale Auto Auctions]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The remarketing and residual analytics teams at OEM captive finance companies and bank lessors decide what happens to each lease return. They set how long the grounding dealer's option runs, the upstream private-label price and the release date to open auction, and they forecast what the returns will bring. They are the auctions' largest commercial consignors. Cox Automotive expects off-lease volume to rise 23% in 2026, with EVs going from ~10% to ~16% of returns. Their analysis prices their own residual risk and steers their own sales. It is not sold.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 remarketing and residual analysts per large captive (estimate)
**Output:** Residual forecasts, grounding and upstream pricing rules, channel and timing decisions, loss reserves on lease portfolios
**Proprietary data:** Lease-end condition, grounding decisions and realised prices across every channel for the captive's own portfolio. Only the captive holds the grounding choices
**Clock:** Lease maturities (contract dates on the captive's own book) and quarterly residual reserve reviews tied to 10-Q and 10-K reporting
**Buyer:** VP of remarketing / head of residual risk
