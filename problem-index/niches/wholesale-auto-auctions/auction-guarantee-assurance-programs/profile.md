# Auction Return-Guarantee & Seller-Assurance Programs

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wholesale-auto-auctions|Wholesale Auto Auctions]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The teams that price and run the risk products wrapped around a wholesale sale. Cox Automotive's DealShield lets subscribing buyers return eligible purchases within 21 days and 360 miles, or 30 days and 500 miles, at more than 175 auction locations (search summary). ACV's Go Green covers sellers against defects its condition report missed. That was part of $81.6M of customer assurance revenue in 2025, alongside price guarantees that pay the seller the shortfall below a guaranteed price. The fee or margin on each product is priced from claim and return history.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–25 pricing, actuarial and assurance analysts per programme (estimate). ACV's Analytics Engineer III, Assurance posting (2026-08-19) "partners with the Assurance Business to surface insights on arbitration risk"
**Output:** Guarantee and assurance pricing, eligibility rules, loss reporting
**Proprietary data:** Returns, assurance claims and price-guarantee shortfalls joined to condition reports and sale prices
**Clock:** The programmes' own return and claim windows; no third-party deadline
**Buyer:** Head of assurance products / VP of customer assurance
