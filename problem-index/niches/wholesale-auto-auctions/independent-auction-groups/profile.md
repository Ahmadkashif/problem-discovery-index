# Regional Auction Groups & Independent Alliances

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wholesale-auto-auctions|Wholesale Auto Auctions]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Multi-site groups below the national chains, such as America's Auto Auction, and alliances of independent auctions that share marketing, commercial accounts and standards, such as ServNet (both named from recall). They win regional commercial consignors and buy or affiliate single-site auctions. The national chains themselves are now three or four groups: Cox's Manheim, Carvana's ADESA, OPENLANE, and ACV inside Copart since 16 October 2026.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Corporate development and commercial analytics under ~10 per group (estimate)
**Output:** Acquisition and consignor pricing analysis for internal use
**Proprietary data:** Group sale results across member sites
**Clock:** Consignors' contract and lease-maturity dates (client clocks)
**Buyer:** CEO / chief commercial officer
