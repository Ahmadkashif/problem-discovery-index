# "Battery: Not Tested"

**Niche:** [[niches/wholesale-auto-auctions/used-ev-wholesale/profile|Used EVs at Wholesale]]
**Industry:** [[industries/wholesale-auto-auctions|Wholesale Auto Auctions]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The one fact a buyer needs about a used EV is the one line the condition report leaves blank.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #automation #quick-win #data-integration #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to put a battery figure on a used EV's condition report that buyers will bid on and not dispute, and whoever has the lowest battery-related arbitration rate on EV units sold takes the account.

## The Problem
Inspectors trained on combustion cars complete the standard report. For EVs the battery line is often "not tested", or a dashboard range copied in. Buyers either pass or bid low, and EVs no-sell or sell at a discount the battery may not deserve.

## Why It's Still Broken
There is no standard procedure in the inspection app, inspectors lack the reader or the training, and the extra minutes are unpaid in a per-car pay model.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Make the battery read a required step for EVs in the inspection app, with a supplied OBD reader and a short procedure per make that pulls whatever the BMS exposes. Pay inspectors for the extra step. Where no reading is possible, record why, so buyers see "BMS not readable on this model" rather than a blank.

## Who Feels the Pain
Captives watching EV off-lease returns sell below residual; independent dealers who will not touch EVs they cannot judge; inspectors with no procedure.

## Impact If Fixed
If missing battery data costs a $500 discount on half the EVs sold (estimate), filling the line on 10,000 EV units a year at one national auction recovers ~$2.5M for consignors.
