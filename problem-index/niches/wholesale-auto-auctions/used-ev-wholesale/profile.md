# Used EVs at Wholesale

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wholesale-auto-auctions|Wholesale Auto Auctions]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to put a battery figure on a used EV's condition report that buyers will bid on and not dispute, and whoever has the lowest battery-related arbitration rate on EV units sold takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$150M a year in battery diagnostics, EV-trained inspection, high-voltage handling, charging on lots and EV remarketing services at wholesale (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% of auction, service and floorplan revenue today, rising with off-lease EV returns (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — battery state of health is often missing from condition reports or shown in incompatible formats; many reports say the battery was not tested
**Target Buyer:** The auction's VP of inspection services; remarketing directors at captives with large EV lease books; the independent dealers who must decide whether to retail an EV at all
**Automation Potential:** High for battery data capture and estimation; the dealer's trust in the figure is the slow part

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
EVs are arriving at wholesale faster than the tools to grade them. Cox Automotive expects EVs to rise from ~10% of off-lease supply in the first half of 2026 to ~16% in the second, and possibly 19% by summer 2027. The Manheim EV index was up 4.3% year on year in September 2026 while non-EVs fell 1.6%. The value of a used EV turns on its battery, which an inspector cannot see, hear or meter. The audience is underserved twice over. Captives need a grade buyers accept, and independent dealers, the usual buyers of older units, have no way to judge a battery themselves. In the UK, Copart added a free battery health report built with Aviloo in July 2026, and Shoreham ran 1,000 checks with Generational (search summary). No US auction battery grade was confirmed for this note. The competitors are the national auctions' inspection teams, battery-diagnostics vendors and OEM captives' own certification. The fleet operator's view of the same batteries is [[niches/fleet-managers/mixed-fuel-ev-fleets/profile|Mixed Fuel & EV Transition Fleets]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
OEM dashboards and range estimates, OBD and BMS readers, battery-diagnostics vendors (Recurrent in the US, Aviloo and Generational in the UK), and the auctions' condition-report apps. Gaps: no common battery grade across auctions; tests too slow for lane throughput; and no record of how battery grades relate to later claims or retail prices.
