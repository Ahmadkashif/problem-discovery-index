# Private-Label Remarketing Platform Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wholesale-auto-auctions|Wholesale Auto Auctions]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The analytics and consulting teams behind the private-label remarketing sites that OEM captives and lessors use to sell lease returns to their own dealers before open auction. OPENLANE powers more than 40 of them, representing most North American manufacturers and captives, and reported $257.1M of SaaS and other revenue in 2025. The analysts advise each consignor on grounding windows, upstream floors and release timing, and report each programme's performance.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~15–40 analysts and consultants (estimate)
**Output:** Programme performance reports, pricing and release-timing recommendations, benchmark comparisons for each consignor
**Proprietary data:** Grounding, upstream bidding and sale records across most captives' programmes, a cross-consignor view no captive has
**Clock:** Consignors' lease maturities and monthly programme reviews (client clocks)
**Buyer:** Head of commercial analytics at the platform; on the client side, the captive's VP of remarketing

The grounding decisions, upstream bids and private-label sale records are each captive's own, held on a SaaS platform whose client terms were not seen; under the batch rulings unread SaaS terms are undetermined, and the switch would be live if the terms reserve the data to each captive. Sales that fall through to the open marketplace are the operator's own records and carry no switch.
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold (35). The cross-captive view is the richest remarketing dataset in the market, but the analysis is sold as part of a platform fee, the clocks are clients' lease maturities, and the buyer is effectively one platform (Q5 1).
