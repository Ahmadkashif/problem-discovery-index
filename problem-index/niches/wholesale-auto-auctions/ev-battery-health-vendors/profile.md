# EV Battery Health Certification Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wholesale-auto-auctions|Wholesale Auto Auctions]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms that grade the battery of a used EV for buyers, sellers and auctions. Recurrent works in the US (named from recall). In the UK, Aviloo supplies Copart's free battery health report (July 2026) and Generational runs Shoreham Vehicle Auctions' checks, 1,000 cars with a median state of health of 95.6% (search summary). They sell per-vehicle reports or data subscriptions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Data science teams under ~10 at the US vendors (estimate); test technicians are production
**Output:** Battery health reports and grades; degradation research
**Proprietary data:** Battery readings by model and age, partly from consumer-consented connected cars and partly from scans done for clients
**Clock:** None beyond client sale days
**Buyer:** CEO / head of data
