# Title & Payment Settlement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wholesale-auto-auctions|Wholesale Auto Auctions]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to close each sale into a negotiable title and cleared funds. But titles are won by whoever controls the paper chain through sellers, lienholders and state title offices, against the auction's title window. Funds are won by whoever carries the buyer's credit, against the floorplan lender's payoff terms. Different players win on different clocks, so the niche is decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.2B a year in title processing, cashiering, payment clearing, floorplan servicing tied to auction purchases and settlement write-offs (estimate), excluding floorplan interest income
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of auction, service and floorplan revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low for titles (paper titles mailed between parties in most states, with electronic titling only where states run it), Medium for funds (floorplan and ACH are electronic, but drafts, checks and exceptions are handled by hand)
**Target Buyer:** Controller or VP of operations at an auction; title department manager; floorplan lender's auction-relations head
**Automation Potential:** Medium — tracking, chasing and exposure checks are automatable; state title offices and lienholders set the pace

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A wholesale sale is not finished when the hammer falls. The seller must deliver a negotiable title within the auction's window, or the buyer may return the car, and the NAAA framework lets the title guarantee lapse at 90 days (search summary). The buyer must pay, usually by drawing on a floorplan line. OPENLANE's AFC alone serviced ~1.7 million floorplan transactions in 2025 for ~11,600 active dealers. The auction often pays the seller or releases the car before either is settled. OPENLANE's 10-K says that in those cases "we bear settlement risk". The work is back-office and paper-heavy. ACV lists title clerks in Texas, and OPENLANE holds most US titles in a central location.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Auction title and cashier modules, state ELT systems where they exist, floorplan lenders' portals (NextGear Capital, AFC, ACV Capital and others), bank ACH and draft processing, and spreadsheets for exceptions. Gaps: title status and payment status live in different systems, so a gate pass can print while either is open; and title delays are worked in sale-date order, not by risk.
