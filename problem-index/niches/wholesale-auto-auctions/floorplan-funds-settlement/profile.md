# Floorplan & Funds Settlement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wholesale-auto-auctions|Wholesale Auto Auctions]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to pay sellers and collect from buyers on every sale without the auction carrying an uncollected receivable, and whoever has the lowest settlement write-offs per dollar of gross sales takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$700M a year in cashiering, payment processing, floorplan servicing tied to auction purchases, collections and settlement write-offs (estimate), excluding floorplan interest income
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of auction, service and floorplan revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — floorplan draws and ACH are electronic; drafts, checks, returned payments and credit exceptions are handled by people
**Target Buyer:** Auction controller or treasurer; floorplan lender's head of auction relations
**Automation Potential:** High for exposure checks and payment-risk scoring; low for collections on a dealer that has gone dark

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Most dealers buy wholesale on floorplan lines. OPENLANE's AFC had ~11,600 active dealers at the end of 2025, with average lines of ~$379,000 and ~16 vehicles outstanding per dealer at ~$12,800 each, and it serviced ~1.7 million floorplan transactions. Cox's NextGear Capital and ACV Capital do the same for their own marketplaces, and independent lenders such as Westlake Flooring, Kinetic Advantage and Car Financial (named from recall) serve the rest. The auction sits between buyer and seller. OPENLANE's 10-K says that when it releases a car or pays a seller before the buyer's money arrives, "we bear settlement risk". The contest is credit and cash, not paper, and it is won by whoever owns the lender relationship and the payment rails. The lender's own credit book is the Pass 2 pocket [[niches/wholesale-auto-auctions/auto-floorplan-lenders/profile|Independent Auto Floorplan Lenders]], and the lot audits behind it are [[niches/franchised-car-dealers/floorplan-audit-services/profile|Floorplan Inventory Audit Services]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Auction cashier systems, floorplan lender portals and APIs, bank ACH and draft clearing, and collections teams. Gaps: no live view of a buyer's exposure across open bids, unpaid purchases and remaining floorplan headroom at the moment of bidding; and returned payments found days after the car has left.
