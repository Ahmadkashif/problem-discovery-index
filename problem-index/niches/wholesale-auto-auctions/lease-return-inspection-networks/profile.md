# Lease-Return & Remarketing Inspection Networks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wholesale-auto-auctions|Wholesale Auto Auctions]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Field inspection networks that grade lease returns at the lessee's home or the grounding dealer for captives and lessors. They record excess wear and use for lease-end billing and produce the condition report that follows the car into remarketing. Third-party networks such as AiM (named from recall) do this work. The auctions sell it too: OPENLANE's 10-K lists condition reporting, inventory verification auditing and programme compliance auditing for non-marketplace customers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Field inspectors and report reviewers are production; no research team (estimate)
**Output:** Lease-end condition reports and excess wear-and-use assessments
**Proprietary data:** Inspection reports owned by the lessor clients
**Clock:** Lease maturities and turn-in appointments (client clocks)
**Buyer:** VP of operations
