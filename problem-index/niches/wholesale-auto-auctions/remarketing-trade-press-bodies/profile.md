# Remarketing Trade Press & Professional Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wholesale-auto-auctions|Wholesale Auto Auctions]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The trade journals and professional groups around remarketing: Auto Remarketing, which reports the NAAA survey and the auctions' results, and its Used Car Week conference; the International Automotive Remarketers Alliance for consignors' remarketing managers; and wholesale-focused conferences (named from recall). They earn advertising, sponsorship, event fees and dues.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Editorial teams of a few reporters and event staff (estimate)
**Output:** News, interviews, conference programmes, occasional surveys
**Proprietary data:** Reader and attendee lists; member surveys
**Clock:** Publication and event calendars (their own)
**Buyer:** Publisher / executive director
