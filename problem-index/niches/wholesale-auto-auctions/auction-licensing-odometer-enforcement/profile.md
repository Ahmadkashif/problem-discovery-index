# Auction Licensing & Odometer-Fraud Enforcement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wholesale-auto-auctions|Wholesale Auto Auctions]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State motor vehicle agencies license auto auctions and the dealers who buy at them, audit their records and investigate title and odometer fraud. Federally, NHTSA enforces the odometer disclosure rules, which allow electronic disclosure since 2021 (recalled), and investigates rollback rings that often launder cars through wholesale channels. State DMVs and transportation departments are not built as an industry in this vault; the owner blocked that row as government-only.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Licensing staff and investigators are production; no analysis is sold (estimate)
**Output:** Licences, audits, enforcement actions
**Proprietary data:** Licence files and investigative records
**Clock:** Statutory licence renewal and investigation timelines
**Buyer:** Agency director
