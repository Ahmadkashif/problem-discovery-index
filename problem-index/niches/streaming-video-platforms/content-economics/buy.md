## The Problem
Managing a multi-billion-dollar portfolio of risky assets is a solved discipline: valuation methodology, performance attribution separating skill from market, risk budgeting across the portfolio, and measurement of individual decision-makers over time. Content slates are portfolios of exactly this scale and risk profile, and are managed without valuation, without attribution, and without any assessment of whether individual commissioners are good at it.

## What Already Exists
Asset valuation methodologies; performance attribution separating skill from beta; portfolio risk budgeting; decision-maker track record measurement; and capital allocation frameworks.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to assets whose returns are subscriber behaviour. It requires: (1) a return measured in retention and acquisition rather than in cash flow, so the valuation must be built from the causal work — this is the dependency that makes everything else possible; (2) assets that cannot be traded or marked to market, so there is no price discovery at all; (3) returns that depend on the platform's own promotion, meaning the asset and its distribution are entangled in a way securities are not; (4) creative and strategic reasons for holding assets that a pure return framework does not capture; and (5) commissioners whose track record would become measurable, which is the political obstacle rather than a technical one.

## Target Customer
Content and finance leadership, boards and investors, studios and licensors, and portfolio analytics vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Investment management values portfolios of this size and measures the people running them. The adaptation depends entirely on the causal valuation, and the obstacle it creates — measurable commissioner track records — is why it has not happened.
