# Discovery & Personalisation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/streaming-video-platforms|Streaming Video Platforms]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get each subscriber to the title that will keep them subscribed rather than the one they will watch tonight — and whoever optimises recommendation for retention rather than for engagement changes what the catalogue is worth.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$12B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~20% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very High — the category's strongest capability
**Target Buyer:** Product and data leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — an active modelling contest

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Recommendation determines what any subscriber sees of a catalogue that is far larger than they will ever browse. It is the platform's most developed technical capability and it is optimised for engagement — watching, completing, continuing — rather than for the outcome the business is measured on, which is whether the subscriber renews. It also decides which titles get exposure, which means it silently determines the performance of the content the business is trying to value.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Collaborative and content-based models, artwork personalisation, row ranking and extensive testing. The gaps: optimised for engagement rather than retention; the recommender's own effect on title performance unaccounted for in content valuation; catalogue exploration weak; new and narrow titles disadvantaged; and cold start on both sides.
