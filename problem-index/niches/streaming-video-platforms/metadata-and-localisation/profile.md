# Metadata & Localisation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/streaming-video-platforms|Streaming Video Platforms]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce descriptions, tags, artwork, subtitles, dubs and audio description for every title in dozens of languages without the cost scaling with the catalogue — and whoever breaks that relationship can carry a catalogue everyone else cannot afford.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$8B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — produced by hand at enormous volume
**Target Buyer:** Content operations leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — generation with quality control

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every title needs descriptions, genres, tags, artwork in many aspect ratios, subtitles, dubs and audio description across dozens of languages, and most of it is produced by hand. The volume is enormous, the quality requirement is real — bad subtitles and wrong tags directly damage the product — and the cost scales linearly with catalogue size and territory count. It is the largest purely operational cost in the business and it gates international expansion.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Localisation vendors, subtitle and dubbing pipelines, artwork studios and metadata teams. The gaps: generation used tentatively and reviewed fully; tagging quality not measured against its downstream effect; artwork variants produced manually; accessibility treated as compliance; and no link between metadata quality and discovery performance.
