# Commissioning Decision Support

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/streaming-video-platforms|Streaming Video Platforms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to predict what an unmade title will be worth from a script, a cast, a comparable set and a gap in the catalogue — and whoever forecasts better than instinct changes which nine-figure commitments get made.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$8B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — comparables and relationships
**Target Buyer:** Content leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — forecasting from sparse pre-production signal

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This contest is prediction before anything exists. There is no viewing data, no audience, no completed work — only a script or a pitch, attached talent, a budget, a set of comparable titles and the platform's knowledge of its own audience segments and catalogue gaps. It is a forecasting problem under severe data scarcity, and it is used by an executive making a commitment, which is a completely different activity from measuring a title that has already run.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Comparables, executive judgement, relationships and a greenlight meeting. The gaps: no forecast from pre-production attributes; segment demand and catalogue gaps not modelled; comparables chosen by hand and unvalidated; past greenlight outcomes not tracked; and portfolio composition unmanaged.
