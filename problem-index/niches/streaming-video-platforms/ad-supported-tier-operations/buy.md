**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to forecast, fill and frequency-cap inventory across a supply chain that makes capping structurally difficult — and whoever solves the repetition viewers notice keeps the tier that is supposed to be the growth engine.

## The Problem
Broadcast television developed serious inventory management: audience forecasting per daypart, inventory allocation between guaranteed and scatter, traffic and scheduling with competitive separation rules, make-good management against guarantees, and pod construction designed around the viewer's experience. Streaming ad tiers inherited the terminology and rebuilt the operation on programmatic plumbing that does not support several of those disciplines.

## What Already Exists
Audience forecasting by daypart and programme; guaranteed and scatter inventory allocation; traffic and scheduling with separation rules; make-good management; and pod construction practice.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to individually addressed, programmatically filled breaks. It requires: (1) a pod assembled per viewer in real time from multiple demand sources rather than scheduled in advance, which removes the scheduler's control over separation and repetition — this is the fundamental difference and is the cause of the experience problem; (2) audience forecasting at individual rather than panel level, which is more accurate and far more volatile; (3) demand arriving through paths that obscure advertiser identity, which broadcast never faced because it sold directly; (4) a viewer who can cancel rather than change channel, so the experience cost is a churn cost; and (5) measurement that is nominally superior and practically weaker than broadcast's panel currency.

## Target Customer
Advertising and product leadership, advertisers and agencies, measurement providers, and ad technology vendors serving connected television.

## Impact If Solved
Broadcast's disciplines assume a scheduled pod and a directly sold advertiser, and streaming assembles the pod per viewer from obscured demand. Restoring separation and repetition control inside a programmatic pod is the adaptation and is what the viewer is complaining about.
