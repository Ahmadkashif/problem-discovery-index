# Churn & Lifecycle

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/streaming-video-platforms|Streaming Video Platforms]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to manage a subscriber who signs up for one title and leaves when it ends — and whoever converts that pattern into a durable relationship stops paying acquisition costs for the same person repeatedly.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — churn measured, not managed
**Target Buyer:** Subscription leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — prediction and intervention

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Serial subscription is the category's defining consumer behaviour: sign up for one series, watch it, cancel, return when the next one arrives. It is entirely visible in the platform's own data, it makes acquisition cost recur for the same individual, and it is treated as a market condition rather than as something to be managed. The interventions that would change it — what to recommend as the series ends, when to signal what is coming, how to price a relationship rather than a month — are barely exercised.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Churn reporting, a cancellation flow and a win-back email. The gaps: churn predicted late if at all; the end-of-series moment unmanaged; win-back untargeted; pricing and plan structure not used as a retention instrument; and the serial subscriber not recognised as a distinct pattern.
