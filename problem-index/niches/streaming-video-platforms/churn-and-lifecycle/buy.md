**Industry:** [[industries/streaming-video-platforms|Streaming Video Platforms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Telecoms built churn science into an operating discipline with prediction, save desks and contract design, and streaming reports a monthly rate.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to manage a subscriber who signs up for one title and leaves when it ends — and whoever converts that pattern into a durable relationship stops paying acquisition costs for the same person repeatedly.

## The Problem
Telecommunications and pay television spent decades on churn: prediction models operating at individual level, save desks with offer authority, contract and bundle design as retention instruments, win-back programmes with measured economics, and lifetime value as the governing metric. The discipline is mature and effective. Streaming platforms have far better behavioural data, a far cheaper intervention channel, and a fraction of the practice.

## What Already Exists
Individual churn prediction models; save desk operations with offer hierarchies; contract and bundle design for retention; win-back programme economics; and lifetime value management.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a subscriber whose reason for staying is a content calendar. It requires: (1) retention driven by what is arriving rather than by price or contract, so the primary instrument is the release schedule and not an offer — this is the substantive difference and means retention is a content decision; (2) a cancellation that is one click with no negotiation, removing the save desk entirely; (3) deliberate serial subscription that is rational consumer behaviour rather than dissatisfaction, which changes what an intervention should even try to do; (4) a far richer behavioural signal than telecoms ever had, making prediction easier; and (5) an intervention channel that costs almost nothing, which changes the economics of acting on a prediction.

## Target Customer
Subscription and product leadership, content leadership whose calendar is the retention instrument, and churn and retention vendors from telecoms.

## Impact If Solved
Telecoms built the discipline with worse data and an expensive intervention channel. Here the instrument is the content calendar rather than an offer, which makes retention a content decision and explains why the subscription team cannot solve it alone.
