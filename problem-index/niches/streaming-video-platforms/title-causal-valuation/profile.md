# Title Causal Valuation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/streaming-video-platforms|Streaming Video Platforms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to attribute subscribers acquired and retained to the titles that caused them — and whoever produces a number a chief financial officer will defend changes what the industry spends billions on.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$10B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~17% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — substituted by consumption metrics
**Target Buyer:** Data and finance leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — causal inference over exceptional data

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This contest is measurement after the fact. The title exists, it has been watched by millions of identified subscribers over a known period, and the question is what it contributed. The data is exceptional and the experimental design is poor, which makes this a causal inference problem of unusual quality and unusual difficulty. It has nothing in common with deciding whether to greenlight a script.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Hours viewed, completion rates, and a top-ten list. The gaps: no causal estimate; natural experiments unanalysed; deliberate experiments not run; retention contribution over tenure not modelled; and segment-level value invisible.
