# The Commissioning Executive

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/streaming-video-platforms|Streaming Video Platforms]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give the person committing nine figures something better than comparables and instinct — and whoever supports that decision without insulting the judgement it depends on changes how the money is spent.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$4B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — a deck and a meeting
**Target Buyer:** Content leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — evidence assembly and portfolio view

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The commissioning executive makes the largest individual financial commitments in the business, on the least evidence available to anyone in it, in a meeting, from a deck. They carry relationships with creators that are genuine assets, they are judged on outcomes they cannot measure, and their track record is unknown to themselves and everyone else. The role sits at the exact point where the industry's measurement gap becomes a person's professional risk.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A pitch deck, comparables, relationships, a budget and a greenlight meeting. The gaps: no evidence pack; comparables selected by advocates; no view of catalogue gaps or segment demand; no record of their own track record; and no portfolio view of the slate.
