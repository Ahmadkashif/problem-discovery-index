**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give the person committing nine figures something better than comparables and instinct — and whoever supports that decision without insulting the judgement it depends on changes how the money is spent.

## The Problem
Investment committees make decisions on highly uncertain propositions all the time and developed practice for it: a structured memo, an explicit thesis and the conditions that would falsify it, base rates for the category, a recorded rationale, and a post-mortem process that compares outcomes to the thesis. None of it eliminates judgement; all of it improves the decision and creates a record. Greenlight meetings have none of that structure.

## What Already Exists
Structured investment memos with explicit theses; base rate and comparable discipline; decision rationale recording; portfolio construction and risk budgeting; and post-mortem and attribution practice.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a decision about creative work with relationships attached. It requires: (1) an outcome measured in subscriber behaviour rather than in a financial return, which depends on the causal valuation and is the binding dependency; (2) relationships with creators that have option value beyond the individual project, which no investment framework captures and which is a genuine reason to commission; (3) a much longer feedback delay than most investment categories, since a series takes years to produce and then years to assess; (4) a culture where structured decision process reads as bureaucracy, which is the adoption obstacle; and (5) commitments that are announced publicly, so a recorded rationale carries a reputational risk a private memo does not.

## Target Customer
Content and finance leadership, boards, producers, and decision process advisors serving media.

## Impact If Solved
Investment committees improved uncertain decisions with memos, base rates and recorded rationales without eliminating judgement. The adaptation must accommodate relationship option value and a very long feedback delay, and the cultural reception is the real obstacle.
