# The Controller

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/spend-management-platforms|Spend Management Platforms]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give the controller back the three weeks a month they spend approving what they were always going to approve — and whoever does it sells to the person who actually chooses the platform.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$800M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — tooling for the process, not the person
**Target Buyer:** Customer and product leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — triage, automation and close preparation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The controller is the buyer, the administrator and the person whose month the product shapes. Three weeks go to approvals they always grant and receipts they chase, and the actual accounting — reconciliation, accruals, review, reporting — compresses into the final week. They are simultaneously the platform's champion and the person it burdens most, and the product is designed around transactions rather than around their month.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Approval queues, receipt dashboards, export tools and a close checklist if they built one themselves. The gaps: no view organised around the close; exceptions and receipts not triaged; no early warning of what will break at month end; no visibility into their own time; and no benchmark against peers.
