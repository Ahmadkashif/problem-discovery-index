# Documentation & Receipts

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/spend-management-platforms|Spend Management Platforms]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to collect only the documentation that is actually needed and to get it without nagging anybody — and whoever asks the auditors what they require first stops chasing most of it.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$900M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — automated nagging
**Target Buyer:** Product and accounting leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the requirement itself is unexamined

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The receipt requirement is an audit artefact. Its collection has been automated into a nagging loop between software and employees: reminders, escalations, card freezes, weekly digests. What nobody has done is ask which receipts are actually needed — by the auditor, by the tax treatment, by the expense category, by the amount — and stop requiring the rest. The contest is scoping the requirement correctly and then satisfying it from data the platform already has.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Receipt capture apps, email forwarding, OCR extraction, reminder sequences and card suspension. The gaps: requirement not scoped to what auditors ask for; merchant data not used to substitute for receipts; reminders sent uniformly regardless of need; no measurement of which receipts are ever examined; and the employee's time never counted.
