# Exception Judgement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/spend-management-platforms|Spend Management Platforms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn thousands of recorded approve-or-deny judgements a month into automated judgement that a controller trusts — and whoever does it takes the approval queue out of the product entirely.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.3B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~14% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — corpus untouched
**Target Buyer:** Product and data leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — supervised learning on clean labels

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This contest is learning from decisions. Each exception carries a transaction, its context, an approver and a verdict — a labelled example of whether this spend, by this person, in this situation, was acceptable. Across a customer base that is millions of examples. The problem is a conventional supervised one with unusually clean labels, and its difficulty is trust and calibration rather than data. It has nothing in common with evaluating whether the policy that generated the exception should exist.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Approval workflows, audit logs, delegation rules and reminder emails. The gaps: decisions never modelled; approver notes unread; no confidence scoring; nothing auto-approved; and no measurement of decision consistency between approvers.
