# GL Coding & ERP Integration

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/spend-management-platforms|Spend Management Platforms]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to code transactions correctly into a chart of accounts unique to every customer without rebuilding the model from scratch each time — and whoever transfers learning across customers makes implementation weeks instead of months.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.4B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~16% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — per-customer rules built by hand
**Target Buyer:** Implementation leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — transfer learning across charts of accounts

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every transaction must land in the right account, department, class and project. Each customer's chart of accounts is unique, built by their accountants over years, with idiosyncratic names and conventions. The platform builds the coding rules per customer during implementation, largely by hand, and the knowledge acquired does not transfer to the next customer even though the underlying economics — this merchant is software, this one is travel — are the same everywhere.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Merchant category mapping, per-customer rules, implementation questionnaires and controller corrections. The gaps: no learning across customers; corrections not used as training signal; chart of accounts semantics not modelled; recoding at month end unmeasured; and integration behaviour differing silently per ERP.
