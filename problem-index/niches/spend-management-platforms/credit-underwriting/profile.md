# Credit Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/spend-management-platforms|Spend Management Platforms]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to set a corporate card limit for a company with twelve months of history and no credit file — and whoever joins the limit decision to what happened afterwards underwrites a book everyone else is guessing at.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.0B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~22% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — fast decisions, absent feedback
**Target Buyer:** Credit and risk leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the signals are continuous and already held

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
These platforms extend real credit — corporate card limits — underwritten on bank balances and cashflow rather than on a traditional credit file, in minutes, to companies that may be a year old. The economics depend on getting it right. The outcome arrives months later in collections, in a different system owned by a different team, and does not find its way back to the model that set the limit. Meanwhile the platform watches the customer's spending and balances every day, which is the strongest early warning available to any lender anywhere.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Bank data aggregation, cashflow scoring, limit rules and collections workflow. The gaps: outcomes not joined to the underwriting decision; spending behaviour not used as ongoing risk signal; limits set once and rarely revisited; distress not detected early; and no measurement of underwriting performance by segment.
