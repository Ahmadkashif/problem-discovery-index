## The Problem
Commercial lending has credit workstations: financial statement spreading, ratio analysis, peer benchmarking, credit memo generation, approval routing and portfolio review. The apparatus is mature and assumes audited statements, a relationship manager, and a decision timeline measured in weeks. The spend platform analyst has bank transaction data, minutes rather than weeks, and a borrower with no statements at all.

## What Already Exists
Credit workstation platforms with spreading and ratio analysis; peer benchmarking libraries; credit memo templates and generation; approval routing and delegated authority frameworks; and portfolio review tooling.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to bank data, speed and a young borrower. It requires: (1) analysis built from transaction-level bank data rather than from statements, which is the substantive adaptation and is a richer and more current input than the original; (2) a decision in minutes, so the workstation must front-load rather than support extended analysis; (3) peer benchmarking drawn from the platform's own portfolio rather than from published industry data, which is both more relevant and entirely absent; (4) business failure as the dominant outcome rather than covenant breach, which reshapes what the memo should address; and (5) an analyst covering high volume rather than a relationship manager covering a book.

## Target Customer
Credit leadership, credit analysts, and commercial lending platform vendors for whom this borrower profile is unserved.

## Impact If Solved
The workstation assumes statements and weeks, and has neither here. Rebuilding it around transaction data, minute-scale decisions and the platform's own portfolio as the benchmark is the adaptation.
