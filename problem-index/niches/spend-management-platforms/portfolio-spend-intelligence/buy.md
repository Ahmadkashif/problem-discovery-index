**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn line-item purchasing across thousands of companies joined to their bank balances into answers nobody else can produce — and whoever organises it holds a real-time read on how businesses spend and how that relates to their survival.

## The Problem
Consumer transaction panels — card and bank data aggregated and sold as market intelligence — are an established industry with mature products: company revenue nowcasts, category share tracking, cohort analysis, and the consent, anonymisation and methodology practice that makes them saleable. Business spend has no equivalent panel of comparable quality, and these platforms hold exactly the raw material.

## What Already Exists
Consumer transaction panel products and methodologies; panel representativeness and weighting practice; anonymisation and aggregation standards; nowcasting methods; and the commercial and legal frameworks around selling derived transaction data.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to business rather than consumer spending. It requires: (1) line-item and vendor-level detail rather than merchant aggregates, which is richer than consumer panels achieve and is the substantive advantage; (2) representativeness that skews heavily to technology-sector and venture-backed companies, which must be corrected or disclosed honestly; (3) the bank balance join, which consumer panels do not have and which is what enables the survival question; (4) business customer consent obtained under a software agreement rather than a data-sharing one, so the governance must be established deliberately; and (5) a customer base who are also the subjects, which consumer panels never face and which changes the whole design.

## Target Customer
Data and commercial leadership, software and services vendors, investors and researchers, and panel data vendors with no business-spend equivalent.

## Impact If Solved
Consumer panels proved the model and built the governance. The business version has better granularity and a bank-balance join no consumer panel has, and the representativeness and consent questions are the real work.
