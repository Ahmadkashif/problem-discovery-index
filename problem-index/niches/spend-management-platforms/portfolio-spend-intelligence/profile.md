# Portfolio Spend Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/spend-management-platforms|Spend Management Platforms]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn line-item purchasing across thousands of companies joined to their bank balances into answers nobody else can produce — and whoever organises it holds a real-time read on how businesses spend and how that relates to their survival.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$650M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — asset unexploited
**Target Buyer:** Data and commercial leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — modelling over data already held

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The platform holds line-item purchasing behaviour across thousands of companies, joined to each company's bank balances, revenue and payment history. That is a real-time view of how businesses actually spend and how spending relates to financial condition — a dataset with genuine macroeconomic content and immediate practical value: vendor benchmarking, category spend comparison, early distress signals, and a picture of software and services adoption that vendors themselves cannot see.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Per-customer dashboards, occasional marketing reports and internal analysis. The gaps: no cross-customer benchmarks; vendor-level spend intelligence unbuilt; spending patterns not linked to survival; the macroeconomic signal unused; and governance for the asset never established.
