# Policy Design

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/spend-management-platforms|Spend Management Platforms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to say which spend policies actually produce better outcomes rather than merely more exceptions — and whoever can answer that across thousands of companies sells policy instead of a rule builder.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.1B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — policies never evaluated
**Target Buyer:** Product and customer success leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — cross-company comparison and experimentation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This contest is causal and comparative. Thousands of companies run different policy configurations on the same platform: different limits, different approval structures, different receipt thresholds, different category rules. Their spend outcomes are observable. That is the only setting in which the question "does this policy actually do anything" can be answered, and answering it means comparison and experimentation rather than learning from individual decisions. Different evidence, different methods, different buyer.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Rule builders, configuration templates from implementation, and best-practice documentation. The gaps: policy effects never measured; no comparison across the customer base; templates based on convention rather than evidence; no experimentation; and no definition of what a good spend outcome is.
