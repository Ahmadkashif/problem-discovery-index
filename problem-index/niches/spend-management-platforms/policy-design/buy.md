# Control Design From Internal Audit

**Niche:** [[niches/spend-management-platforms/policy-design/profile|Policy Design]]
**Industry:** [[industries/spend-management-platforms|Spend Management Platforms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Internal audit has a whole discipline for assessing whether a control is designed and operating effectively, and spend policy is assessed by whether it exists.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to say which spend policies actually produce better outcomes rather than merely more exceptions — and whoever can answer that across thousands of companies sells policy instead of a rule builder.

## The Problem
Internal audit and controls frameworks distinguish design effectiveness from operating effectiveness, test controls against defined risks, assess whether a control is proportionate to the risk it addresses, and identify controls that are redundant or ineffective. The discipline exists precisely because organisations accumulate controls that cost more than the risk they mitigate. Spend policy is assessed by whether a rule exists and whether exceptions were approved.

## What Already Exists
Control design and operating effectiveness testing; risk and control matrices; control rationalisation methodology; sampling and evidence standards; and continuous controls monitoring.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to controls implemented in software across thousands of organisations. It requires: (1) effectiveness assessed empirically from outcome data rather than through sampling and interviews, which is a genuine upgrade the discipline has never had access to and is the substantive contribution; (2) comparison across organisations, where audit assesses one entity at a time and has no benchmark; (3) control cost quantified in employee friction, which audit acknowledges and rarely measures; (4) the platform rather than an auditor as the assessor, which changes the commercial framing entirely; and (5) continuous rather than annual assessment, since the data arrives every day.

## Target Customer
Product leadership, customer internal audit and finance functions, external auditors assessing spend controls, and GRC vendors whose continuous monitoring stops at exception counts.

## Impact If Solved
Audit assesses one entity at a time using sampling and interviews. Assessing control effectiveness empirically across thousands of organisations is something the discipline has wanted and never been able to do.
