# Card Misuse Detection

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/spend-management-platforms|Spend Management Platforms]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to find the spend that is within policy and still wrong — and whoever detects insider misuse without accusing honest employees takes the risk the rule engine was never built to see.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — external fraud handled, internal not
**Target Buyer:** Card operations leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — behavioural detection over rich data

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
External card fraud is handled well: networks and issuers detect stolen-card patterns competently. What is not handled is the employee whose spending is entirely within policy and is nonetheless personal, inflated, duplicated, or routed through a vendor they control. That spend passes every rule because the rules were designed for limits and categories rather than for intent, and it is detectable only from behavioural patterns the platform observes and does not model.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Network fraud scoring, limit enforcement, duplicate detection on exact matches and controller intuition. The gaps: no behavioural model of misuse; peer comparison unused; vendor relationships not examined; investigation unsupported; and no measurement of what is being missed.
