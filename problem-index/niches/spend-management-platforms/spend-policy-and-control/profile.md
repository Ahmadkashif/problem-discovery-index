# Spend Policy & Control

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/spend-management-platforms|Spend Management Platforms]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make control mean something better than a rule engine that generates exceptions humans rubber-stamp — and the contest splits cleanly enough that it is not terminal.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.4B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~27% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — static rules, human exceptions
**Target Buyer:** Product and risk leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — a labelled corpus and an untested policy set

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Control is what the category sells. It is implemented as category restrictions, transaction and monthly limits, receipt thresholds and approval routing — a static rule engine. Every rule produces exceptions, every exception goes to a manager or controller, and the overwhelming majority are approved. The approvals are a labelled judgement about whether spend was acceptable, made thousands of times a month, stored as audit trail, and used for nothing. This is the category's largest contest and it is two: learning from the decisions, and evaluating the rules that produced them.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Rule builders, approval routing, limit management and audit logs. The gaps: approvals never used as training data; policies never evaluated for effect; exception volume treated as inevitable; no comparison of policy configurations across companies; and no measure of whether control is achieving anything.
