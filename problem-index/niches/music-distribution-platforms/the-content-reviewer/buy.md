**Industry:** [[industries/music-distribution-platforms|Music Distribution Platforms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Large platforms built trust and safety into a discipline with policy, tiering, appeals and quality measurement, and distributors run a review queue.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let a reviewer decide in seconds whether a release is legitimate, at a volume that only grows — and whoever gives them evidence instead of a filename changes both the accuracy and the survivability of the role.

## The Problem
Trust and safety at scale is a developed discipline: written policy with worked examples, automated triage by severity, reviewer training and calibration, quality assurance sampling, appeals with independent review, and transparency reporting. Music distributors face a smaller but structurally identical problem and typically run a queue, a policy document and an escalation email.

## What Already Exists
Policy frameworks with enforcement tiers; automated triage and severity routing; reviewer calibration and quality assurance; appeals processes with independent review; and transparency reporting.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to content that is a commercial asset with downstream rights consequences. It requires: (1) a decision that determines whether a release reaches a launch date, so a wrong rejection has a hard commercial cost rather than a content availability one — this changes the balance between the two errors; (2) infringement judgements about music, where fingerprinting helps and sampling, covers and interpolations make it genuinely ambiguous; (3) enforcement consequences arriving from the streaming services afterwards, so the distributor is both enforcer and enforced-against; (4) an uploader who is a paying customer rather than a platform user, which changes the appeal expectation; and (5) far lower volumes than a major platform, so the tooling must be affordable at that scale.

## Target Customer
Trust and safety leadership, reviewers, artists, streaming services, and trust and safety platform vendors.

## Impact If Solved
The discipline is mature at platform scale and distributors have the same problem with a queue. Rebalancing for a wrong rejection that costs a launch date, at volumes that cannot fund a platform-scale operation, is the adaptation.
