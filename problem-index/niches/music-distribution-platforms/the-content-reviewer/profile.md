# The Content Reviewer

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/music-distribution-platforms|Music Distribution Platforms]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let a reviewer decide in seconds whether a release is legitimate, at a volume that only grows — and whoever gives them evidence instead of a filename changes both the accuracy and the survivability of the role.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$105M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — a queue and a judgement
**Target Buyer:** Trust and safety leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — detection with evidence assembly

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Reviewers check uploads for impersonation, infringement and metadata defects at a volume that only grows, deciding in seconds whether a release is legitimate. A wrong approval puts infringing or impersonating content onto every major service under the distributor's name; a wrong rejection blocks a legitimate artist's release. The decision is made from a filename, some metadata and a short listen, with no assembled evidence and no feedback on whether previous decisions were right.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A review queue, fingerprint matching for known recordings, a policy document and an escalation path. The gaps: evidence not assembled before review; artist identity and history not surfaced; impersonation patterns not modelled; decision outcomes never returned; and reviewer consistency unmeasured.
