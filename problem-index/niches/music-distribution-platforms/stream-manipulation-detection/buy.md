# Invalid Traffic Detection From Advertising

**Niche:** [[niches/music-distribution-platforms/stream-manipulation-detection/profile|Stream Manipulation Detection]]
**Industry:** [[industries/music-distribution-platforms|Music Distribution Platforms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Advertising built invalid traffic detection with standards, accreditation and defined remedies, and streaming manipulation is handled by penalty and silence.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to identify artificial streams before the penalty lands and to tell a wrongly accused artist why — and whoever detects accurately and explains defensibly stops the enforcement falling on the innocent.

## The Problem
Advertising faced the identical problem — automated activity generating payable events — and built a substantial response: invalid traffic detection with defined categories, independent accreditation of detection vendors, standardised reporting of what was filtered, and credit mechanisms when invalid activity is billed. The practice is imperfect and it is a practice. Streaming manipulation is detected privately, enforced unilaterally, explained minimally and appealed rarely.

## What Already Exists
General and sophisticated invalid traffic categorisation; accredited detection methodologies; standardised filtered-activity reporting; credit and remediation mechanisms; and industry bodies setting the standards.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a fixed pool where fraud is a transfer between participants. It requires: (1) a zero-sum royalty pool, so undetected fraud takes money from identifiable other artists rather than from an advertiser — this changes who the victim is and makes detection a duty to the artist community; (2) the penalised party often being an innocent buyer of a fraudulent service, which advertising handles as a vendor problem and streaming handles as artist misconduct; (3) no accreditation of the detection, so an artist has no basis to challenge it; (4) enforcement that removes a release rather than withholding payment for specific events; and (5) a distributor in the middle with no visibility into either side.

## Target Customer
Trust and safety leadership, artists, streaming services, industry bodies, and invalid traffic vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Advertising built categories, accreditation and remedies for the same problem. The distinctive feature here is a fixed pool, which makes the victim another artist and makes silent unexplained enforcement much harder to justify.
