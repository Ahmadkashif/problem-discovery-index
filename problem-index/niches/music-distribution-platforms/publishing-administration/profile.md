# Publishing Administration

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/music-distribution-platforms|Music Distribution Platforms]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to register a composition correctly in every territory and collect what it earns from societies that each work differently — and whoever makes that work for an artist with four songs opens a revenue stream most of them never see.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$150M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — forms, societies and follow-up
**Target Buyer:** Rights administration leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — registration, tracking and collection

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The composition side is administered by performing rights organisations, mechanical licensing bodies and publishers, territory by territory, each with its own registration process, identifiers, deadlines and statement formats. An independent artist who wrote their own songs is entitled to this money and mostly does not receive it, because collecting it requires registrations they do not know to make and follow-up nobody performs. It is a separate operation from distributing the recording and is frequently not offered at all.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Publishing administration deals, society registration forms and periodic statements. The gaps: registration not automated from the release; territory coverage incomplete; earnings not tracked against what should have been earned; statements from societies unreconciled; and the artist unaware the money exists.
