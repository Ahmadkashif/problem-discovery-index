**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Insurance and benefits claims administration industrialised chasing entitlements across many payers, and publishing collection is done by email.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #automation #data-integration #compliance #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact #descriptive-statistics #confidence-intervals
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to register a composition correctly in every territory and collect what it earns from societies that each work differently — and whoever makes that work for an artist with four songs opens a revenue stream most of them never see.

## The Problem
Chasing entitlements owed by many payers with different rules, formats and timelines is exactly what claims administration does. Medical billing, benefits administration and insurance recovery all built the same apparatus: eligibility checking, structured submission per payer, status tracking, denial management with resubmission, and reconciliation of what was paid against what was owed. Publishing collection has the identical structure and almost none of the apparatus.

## What Already Exists
Payer-specific submission formats and rules; eligibility verification; claim status tracking and follow-up; denial management and resubmission; and payment posting with reconciliation against expected amounts.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a payee who is an individual artist. It requires: (1) per-claim amounts that are very small, so the entire operation must run without human touch where claims administration tolerates a person per exception — this is the binding economic constraint; (2) payers who are collecting societies across many countries with divergent processes and languages; (3) an entitlement that depends on registration made in advance rather than on an event, so the equivalent of eligibility is something the artist must have done years earlier; (4) statements that report earnings rather than adjudicate a claim, which means the denial concept is replaced by silent underpayment; and (5) an artist who does not know the entitlement exists, where a patient knows they were treated.

## Target Customer
Rights administration leadership, independent songwriters, collecting societies, and claims administration vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Claims administration industrialised multi-payer entitlement chasing and assumes a person per exception. Doing it with no human touch at a few dollars per claim is the adaptation, and silent underpayment replaces the denial it was built around.
