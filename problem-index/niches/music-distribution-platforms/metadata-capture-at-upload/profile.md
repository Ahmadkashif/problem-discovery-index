# Metadata Capture at Upload

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/music-distribution-platforms|Music Distribution Platforms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get correct rights data out of an artist who does not know what a publisher is — and whoever designs that capture well prevents the matching problem instead of repairing it downstream.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$180M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — a form and a hope
**Target Buyer:** Product leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — guided capture and validation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This contest is capture design at the point of origin. An artist uploads a track and is asked for writers, splits, publishers, roles and identifiers — concepts many of them have never encountered, in a form, at the moment they are excited about releasing music. What they enter determines whether money finds them for as long as the recording exists. It is a product and validation problem about extracting accurate data from a non-expert, and it has nothing in common with inferring matches across corpora afterwards.

## Current Tools & Gaps
An upload form, required fields, a help article and a validation check. The gaps: terminology assumed rather than explained; splits entered without understanding; no validation against what is knowable; the cost of an error never shown to the uploader; and no correction path once the release is live.
