# The Artist Support Agent

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/music-distribution-platforms|Music Distribution Platforms]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let an agent answer a royalty question with an actual answer rather than a description of a process — and whoever gives them visibility into the accounting changes what support can be.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$135M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — a ticket queue and a statement
**Target Buyer:** Support leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — traceability and case assembly

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Agents explain royalty accounting to people whose income depends on it, using a statement they cannot fully trace and a system they cannot see into. The questions are specific — why is this number lower than last month, where did the money from this territory go, why was my release taken down — and the tools available answer none of them. The agent absorbs the frustration of an artist whose livelihood is at stake and has nothing to offer but a description of a process.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A ticket queue, the artist's statement, a knowledge base and an escalation path. The gaps: no line-level traceability; no view into royalty processing; takedown and enforcement reasons unavailable; recurring question patterns uncatalogued; and no way to resolve rather than explain.
