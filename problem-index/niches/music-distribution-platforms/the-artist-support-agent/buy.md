# Case Handling From Financial Services

**Niche:** [[niches/music-distribution-platforms/the-artist-support-agent/profile|The Artist Support Agent]]
**Industry:** [[industries/music-distribution-platforms|Music Distribution Platforms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Banks give front-line staff a full transaction view because a customer asking where their money went deserves an answer, and music support has a statement.
**Tags:** #worker-facing #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #compliance #evaluation-metrics #automation #descriptive-statistics #confidence-intervals
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let an agent answer a royalty question with an actual answer rather than a description of a process — and whoever gives them visibility into the accounting changes what support can be.

## The Problem
Financial services front-line support has a complete transaction view, the ability to trace a balance to its components, defined complaint handling with timelines, and escalation paths with authority attached. It exists because a customer asking about their money is entitled to an answer and regulators require one. Artists are asking exactly the same question about money they earned, and the person answering has a summary document.

## What Already Exists
Full transaction visibility for front-line staff; balance decomposition and transaction tracing; complaint handling frameworks with timelines; escalation with defined authority; and quality monitoring on money-related conversations.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to income rather than a balance. It requires: (1) tracing a payment back through a pro-rata pool computed by a third party, which has no banking analogue — a transaction has a counterparty and a royalty has a share of a pool; (2) money arriving from many services with different lags, so "where is my money" has several simultaneous answers; (3) an artist rather than a banking customer, with no statutory complaint regime behind them; (4) amounts that are small individually and existential in aggregate for the person asking; and (5) enforcement actions and takedowns arriving in the same queue, mixing an accounting question with a policy one.

## Target Customer
Support and operations leadership, agents, artists, and support platform vendors serving financial services.

## Impact If Solved
Banks give front-line staff full transaction visibility because a money question deserves an answer. The adaptation is tracing a share of a pro-rata pool rather than a transaction, which is harder and is exactly what the artist is asking about.
