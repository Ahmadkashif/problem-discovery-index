# DSP Delivery & Ingestion

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/music-distribution-platforms|Music Distribution Platforms]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a release accepted by every streaming service first time, against rules that sit on top of a shared standard and change without notice — and whoever stops releases missing their date on a specification detail wins the artists who have one shot at a launch.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$180M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — automated delivery, manual rules
**Target Buyer:** Integration leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — rule modelling and pre-flight validation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every streaming service has its own ingestion rules on top of a shared standard: artwork requirements, title formatting conventions, contributor role vocabularies, explicit content handling, release date and territory logic. A release rejected on a specification detail misses its date, which for an artist who has coordinated a campaign around it is the whole point. The rules are documented inconsistently, change without notice, and are known to integration teams through experience.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Delivery pipelines, format specifications, rejection handling and a support queue. The gaps: rules encoded per service by hand and going stale; rejections not predicted before delivery; rejection reasons cryptic and undiagnosed; no rehearsal before a release date; and no measurement of first-time acceptance.
