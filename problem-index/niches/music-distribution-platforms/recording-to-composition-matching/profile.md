# Recording-to-Composition Matching

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/music-distribution-platforms|Music Distribution Platforms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to determine which composition a recording embodies and who wrote it, across corpora with different identifiers and no shared key — and whoever matches most accurately at scale controls where the composition money goes.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$240M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~16% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — manual claims and partial registries
**Target Buyer:** Data and rights leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — entity resolution with audio evidence

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This contest is inference across corpora. Given a recording, determine the composition it embodies and the writers and splits attached to it, using registries that were never designed to join, metadata of variable quality, and the acoustic and lyrical content of the recording itself. It is entity resolution with an unusually strong additional signal, and it operates on material that already exists — which is a different problem from getting the data right at upload.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Registry lookups, manual claims, and matching on title and artist name. The gaps: audio and lyric evidence unused for matching; the distributor's matched corpus not used as training data; no confidence expressed; cover versions and alternate titles handled poorly; and no feedback of matches to registries.
