# Royalty Accounting & Reconciliation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/music-distribution-platforms|Music Distribution Platforms]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn dozens of incompatible statement formats into a payment an artist can trace back to a stream — and whoever makes a royalty statement explainable takes the trust the whole category runs on.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$330M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~22% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — reconciliation by pipeline and spreadsheet
**Target Buyer:** Royalty operations leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — ingestion, reconciliation and explanation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Royalty statements arrive from every service in different formats, on different schedules, with different territory, rate and deduction structures, and must be reconciled against the distributor's own catalogue and then split among artists and rights holders. The artist receives a number. Whether that number is right is not checkable by them and is often not fully traceable by the distributor either, which makes the industry's most fundamental promise an assertion.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Ingestion pipelines, royalty engines, spreadsheets and a statement generator. The gaps: statements not traceable to source lines; discrepancies against expected revenue unreconciled; format changes breaking ingestion silently; currency, tax and deduction handling opaque; and no artist-facing explanation.
