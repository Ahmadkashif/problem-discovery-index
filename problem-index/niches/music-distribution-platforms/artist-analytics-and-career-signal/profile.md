# Artist Analytics & Career Signal

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/music-distribution-platforms|Music Distribution Platforms]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn the earliest visible evidence of an artist's trajectory into something the artist can act on — and whoever surfaces it first holds the signal the whole industry pays to discover late.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$60M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~4% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — dashboards without direction
**Target Buyer:** Artist services leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — modelling over data already held

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A distributor sees an artist's streams across every service, by territory, by track, by playlist and by period, from their first release onward. That is the earliest and most complete view of an emerging career that exists anywhere — earlier than a label's, earlier than a publication's, earlier than the artist's own sense of it. It is rendered as a dashboard of counts, and the decisions it could inform — where to tour, which track to push, when something is breaking — are made without it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A streaming dashboard, territory and playlist breakdowns, and a follower count. The gaps: no trajectory modelling; breakout not detected early; territory data not connected to touring or marketing decisions; playlist effect unmeasured; and the aggregate view across artists unexploited.
