**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Markets and retail built the practice of reading early signals to act before a trend is obvious, and music analytics reports what already happened.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn the earliest visible evidence of an artist's trajectory into something the artist can act on — and whoever surfaces it first holds the signal the whole industry pays to discover late.

## The Problem
Reading early signals to act before a trend is generally visible is a practised discipline in markets, retail demand planning and epidemiology: leading indicator construction, change point detection, cohort trajectory analysis and explicit distinction between noise and signal. Music analytics products, including the distributors' own, report counts and trends after the fact and leave the inference entirely to the reader.

## What Already Exists
Leading indicator construction and validation; change point and early warning detection; cohort trajectory comparison; signal-versus-noise discipline; and forecast communication with uncertainty.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a career rather than a series. It requires: (1) a trajectory that is a person's career with irreversible decisions attached, so a false positive costs an artist real money on a tour that should not have been booked — this raises the precision requirement considerably; (2) very short histories, since most artists have a handful of releases and the methods assume longer series; (3) an outcome that is not a single variable but a set of career possibilities, which complicates what is even being forecast; (4) a reader with no analytical training, so the output must be a statement rather than a chart; and (5) an aggregate view across thousands of artists that no market participant has of its own instruments.

## Target Customer
Artist services leadership, artists and managers, labels and investors in music rights, and analytics vendors.

## Impact If Solved
The early-signal disciplines are mature and music analytics reports history. The constraint is precision, because a false breakout signal sends an artist on a tour they cannot afford.
