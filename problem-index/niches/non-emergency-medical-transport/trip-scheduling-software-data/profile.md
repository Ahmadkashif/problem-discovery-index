## What They Do
The dispatch and scheduling platforms run trip assignment, routing, and GPS verification for operators — though Pass 1 records that most still fall back on manual scheduling boards and paper trip logs.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-70 product and data staff
**Output:** Scheduling and routing, trip verification and GPS logging, billing export, no-show tracking
**Proprietary data:** Scheduled versus completed trips, no-show patterns, and route performance across many operators
**Clock:** Appointment times and daily dispatch
**Buyer:** Head of Product
