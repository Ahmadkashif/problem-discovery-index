# Rideshare Healthcare Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/non-emergency-medical-transport|Non-Emergency Medical Transport]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The rideshare platforms' healthcare divisions supply ambulatory medical trips to health plans and brokers, competing directly with traditional providers on the lower-acuity share of the benefit.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-200 data scientists, operations analysts, and health programme staff
**Output:** Trip matching and pricing, completion and reliability reporting, health outcome studies with plan partners
**Proprietary data:** Ride supply, timing, and completion at national scale, joined for healthcare clients to appointment adherence
**Clock:** Appointment times and real-time dispatch
**Buyer:** VP of Healthcare / Head of Data Science
