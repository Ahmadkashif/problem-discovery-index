# NEMT Broker Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/non-emergency-medical-transport|Non-Emergency Medical Transport]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Transportation brokers hold statewide Medicaid contracts, take capitated payment, and assign every covered trip to a network of local providers — which makes them the entity that decides what a trip is worth and which operator gets it. Their analytics organizations must demonstrate utilization management and cost control to the state that pays them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-800 utilization analysts, network managers, actuaries, and data scientists
**Output:** Utilization and cost reporting to state clients, trip assignment and rate setting, provider network performance, eligibility and fraud analysis
**Proprietary data:** Trip-level data across entire state Medicaid populations — origin, destination, mode, cost, no-show, and the member's care pattern behind it
**Clock:** Appointment times, which cannot be missed, and contractual state reporting deadlines
**Buyer:** Chief Analytics Officer / VP of Operations
