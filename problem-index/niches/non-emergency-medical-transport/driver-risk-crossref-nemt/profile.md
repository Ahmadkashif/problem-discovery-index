# Commercial Driver Risk Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/non-emergency-medical-transport|Non-Emergency Medical Transport]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Driver risk data providers score drivers and fleets from violation, crash, and telematics records — the screening that decides who may transport Medicaid members and what an operator pays to insure them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 data analysts, actuaries, and researchers
**Output:** Driver and fleet risk scores, continuous monitoring, underwriting data
**Proprietary data:** Assembled violation and crash records joined to observed loss outcomes
**Clock:** Hiring decisions and continuous monitoring requirements
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer
