**Parent Industry:** [[industries/non-emergency-medical-transport|Non-Emergency Medical Transport]]

## What They Do
Billing operations reconcile trip logs against Medicaid claims and pursue the denials Pass 1 identifies as a manual, paper-driven burden — trip verification, mileage disputes, and eligibility mismatches at high volume and low margin.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-400 billers, claims analysts, and reconciliation staff
**Output:** Claim submission and reconciliation, denial analysis and appeals, trip verification, collection reporting
**Proprietary data:** Denial reasons and appeal outcomes across many operators and state programmes
**Clock:** Timely filing limits and broker payment cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Operations
