# Medicaid Programme Integrity

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/non-emergency-medical-transport|Non-Emergency Medical Transport]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Programme integrity units investigate transportation billing fraud — trips billed that never happened, mileage inflation, and ineligible members — in a benefit that is a persistent target because verification is weak.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-200 investigators, data analysts, and auditors per state
**Output:** Fraud investigations and referrals, recovery actions, provider exclusions, risk analytics
**Proprietary data:** Claims and trip data joined to investigation outcomes across the state programme
**Clock:** Investigation and recovery timelines
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — state agency
