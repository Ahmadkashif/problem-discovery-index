# Transportation Benefit Actuarial Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/non-emergency-medical-transport|Non-Emergency Medical Transport]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Consulting actuaries set the capitation rates states pay transportation brokers, projecting trip volume and cost per member — the number that determines whether the benefit is adequately funded and whether providers can be paid enough to serve it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 actuaries and analysts
**Output:** Capitation rate development, utilization projections, rate certification, programme cost analysis
**Proprietary data:** Utilization and cost experience across many state transportation programmes
**Clock:** Contract year rate certification deadlines
**Buyer:** Practice leader
