# NEMT Fleet Insurance Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/non-emergency-medical-transport|Non-Emergency Medical Transport]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Specialty insurers underwrite auto liability and passenger injury exposure for medical transport fleets, pricing a book that carries frail passengers, mixed employment models, and heavy urban mileage.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 underwriters, actuaries, and loss control staff
**Output:** Rating and eligibility decisions, driver screening requirements, loss control programmes, claims analysis
**Proprietary data:** Loss experience by fleet, service type, and driver employment model
**Clock:** Annual renewal and broker contract requirements
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
