# Mobility Management Consulting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/non-emergency-medical-transport|Non-Emergency Medical Transport]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Transportation consultancies design coordinated mobility programmes for regions — combining Medicaid transport, transit, and human services rides — and study how to move people who cannot drive to the appointments they need.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-70 planners, analysts, and transportation engineers
**Output:** Programme design and coordination plans, cost and demand modelling, service evaluations, procurement support
**Proprietary data:** Programme cost and utilization comparisons across regions
**Clock:** Planning and grant cycles
**Buyer:** Practice leader
