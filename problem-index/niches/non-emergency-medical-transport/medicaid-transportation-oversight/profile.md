# Medicaid Transportation Oversight

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/non-emergency-medical-transport|Non-Emergency Medical Transport]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State Medicaid agencies design the transportation benefit, procure and oversee the brokers, and audit whether members who needed a ride actually got one — the decisions that set this industry's economics entirely.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 programme analysts, contract managers, and auditors per state
**Output:** Benefit design and procurement, broker performance oversight, access monitoring, rate setting
**Proprietary data:** Trip, claim, and complaint data for the whole state programme, joined to member eligibility and care utilization
**Clock:** Procurement cycles and federal reporting requirements
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — state agency
