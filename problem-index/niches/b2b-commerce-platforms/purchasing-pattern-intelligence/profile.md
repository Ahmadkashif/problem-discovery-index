# Purchasing Pattern Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/b2b-commerce-platforms|B2B Commerce Platforms]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to predict what a business customer will order next from the cleanest repeat-purchase record in commerce — and whoever does it accurately enough to act on owns the reorder before the customer initiates it.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.1B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — data held, rarely modelled
**Target Buyer:** Ecommerce leadership and category management
**Automation Potential:** Very High — prediction from a clean transaction record

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
B2B commerce holds the best-posed prediction problem in commerce and does almost nothing with it: the buyer is a repeat purchaser with a known entitlement, buying known parts on a predictable cycle, under a negotiated agreement. Consumer commerce has spent two decades building recommendation on sparse, anonymous, one-off behaviour. B2B has dense, identified, recurring transactions with stable baskets and visible cycles — and ships the storefront with a bestsellers carousel. This is the industry's own corpus problem: it holds the record of exactly what its customers need predicted, and it does not model it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Order history reporting, reorder lists, simple bestseller and recently-viewed modules, and occasional consumer recommendation engines imported wholesale. The gaps: no cycle modelling per customer and part; no companion-line prediction from job context; no attrition detection when a repeat order stops; no substitution intelligence; and no push of the prediction to the rep or the customer at the right moment.
