# Reorder & Account Purchasing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/b2b-commerce-platforms|B2B Commerce Platforms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to make a repeat order faster and more certain than phoning the rep — and whoever does that takes the volume, because the buyer knows what they want and is choosing between channels rather than between products.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.1B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Moderate — the capability exists and is slower than a phone call
**Target Buyer:** Repeat account purchasers and their organisations
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the purchasing pattern is highly predictable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The B2B buyer is a repeat purchaser with a known entitlement, buying known parts on a predictable cycle, under a negotiated agreement. Almost nothing about consumer discovery applies. What they need is to reorder accurately, at the right price, through whatever approval their organisation requires, with delivery to the right location on a date that fits their operation — and to be confident it is correct, because an error costs a production line rather than a return. The competitor is not another supplier's website; it is their own rep, who knows their account and gets it right, and their own procurement system, which is where their budget controls live. The storefront wins this volume only by being genuinely better than a phone call.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Order history, reorder buttons, requisition lists and saved carts. The gaps: reordering requires the buyer to remember what they need rather than being told; approval workflow is rudimentary and frequently drives the order back to email; delivery scheduling for a production requirement is not supported; substitutions and discontinuations are discovered at order time; and the buyer's own part numbers and cost centres are not first-class.
