# The Inside Sales Rep

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/b2b-commerce-platforms|B2B Commerce Platforms]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give the inside sales rep a working surface rather than three systems and a memory — and whoever does that gets the rep to keep the platform open, which is the only path to the data that every other feature needs.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.0B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — reps work around the platform
**Target Buyer:** Sales management, though the user is the rep
**Automation Potential:** High — assembly and lookup dominate the day

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Inside sales reps assemble quotes by hand from a resource planning system, a spreadsheet and their memory. They are the highest-volume users of the commerce platform's underlying data and almost never its users, because every tool in the category was designed for the customer buying or for the administrator configuring, and nobody designed for the person in the middle who does the work. The rep knows which customer buys what, which substitutions are acceptable, which prices move and which do not, and none of that is in any system — it is in their head, and it leaves when they do.

## Current Tools & Gaps
The resource planning system, a spreadsheet, email, a phone, and the storefront used as a lookup tool. The gaps: no unified surface showing the customer, their entitlement, their history and open items together; no assembly aid, so every quote is retyped; no capture of the rep's substitution and preference knowledge; no visibility of what the customer did on the storefront; and no handoff when the rep leaves.
