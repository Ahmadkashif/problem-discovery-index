# Quote to Cash

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/b2b-commerce-platforms|B2B Commerce Platforms]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to bring the quote inside the commerce platform rather than leaving it in a resource planning system and an inbox — and whoever does that captures the highest-value orders, which currently never touch the storefront.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.4B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — outside the platform entirely
**Target Buyer:** Sales operations and finance
**Automation Potential:** High — pricing, approval and conversion are all mechanisable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Quote-to-cash workflow is frequently outside the commerce platform entirely, sitting in the resource planning system or in email. That means the highest-value transactions — the large, negotiated, configured or project-based orders — are the ones the digital channel does not touch, and the platform's reported share of business systematically excludes the business that matters most. The quote is where the price is negotiated, where the margin is decided, where the approval happens and where the order originates, and it is conducted in a spreadsheet attached to an email thread. Nothing about the resulting order is analysable, comparable or repeatable.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Resource planning quoting modules, spreadsheets, email, and configure-price-quote tools at larger firms. The gaps: quotes are not created from or converted into storefront orders; negotiated prices do not flow back into the customer's pricing; margin approval is a manual chain; quote outcomes are not tracked, so nobody knows the win rate or what was lost on; and the customer cannot see or act on their quote digitally.
