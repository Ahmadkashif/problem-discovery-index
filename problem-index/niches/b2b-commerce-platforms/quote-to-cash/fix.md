# The Quote Nobody Followed Up

**Niche:** [[niches/b2b-commerce-platforms/quote-to-cash/profile|Quote to Cash]]
**Industry:** [[industries/b2b-commerce-platforms|B2B Commerce Platforms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Quotes are sent by email and their outcome is recorded nowhere, so nobody knows the win rate, what was lost, to whom, or on what — which is the most basic commercial information a distributor could have.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact #confidence-intervals #hypothesis-testing #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #gradient-boosting
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to bring the quote inside the commerce platform rather than leaving it in a resource planning system and an inbox — and whoever does that captures the highest-value orders, which currently never touch the storefront.

## The Problem
A rep sends forty quotes a week. Some convert into orders, which appear in the order system with no link to the quote. Some are lost to a competitor. Some are never decided. Nobody knows which is which, because a quote is a document and its outcome is a conversation. The distributor cannot say what their win rate is, which products or price levels lose, which customers quote and do not buy, or whether the discount a rep gave made any difference. The single most informative dataset a sales organisation could have is generated forty times a week per rep and evaporates.

## Why It's Still Broken
The quote lives in a spreadsheet or an email and has no record in a system that could hold an outcome. Following up is a rep's personal discipline rather than a process. Linking a converted quote to its order requires a reference nobody carries through. And the win rate has never been reported, so its absence is not felt as a gap.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Record the quote and close the loop on it. Create every quote as a record with an identifier that carries onto the resulting order, which is the minimum and makes conversion measurable — this is the fix and it requires a field rather than a project. Track outcomes explicitly, including lost and why, with a short structured reason from the rep who will usually know. Prompt follow-up on open quotes automatically at an interval that reflects the buying cycle, since a substantial share are simply never chased. Report win rate by product, customer, rep, price level and discount given, which is the analysis nobody can currently run and which frequently shows that the discounting is not buying the business it was given for. Compare quoted to accepted prices, so the organisation learns where its pricing is above the market and where it is leaving margin. Let the customer accept digitally, which both speeds conversion and records the outcome without asking the rep. Alert when a customer who quotes regularly stops converting, which is an account signal currently invisible. And feed the loss reasons into pricing and product decisions, since they are the market's direct feedback and are currently absorbed in conversations.

## Who Feels the Pain
Sales leadership with no win rate; reps whose discounting is unguided by any evidence; and distributors giving away margin on quotes that would have converted anyway.

## Impact If Fixed
The most informative dataset a sales organisation could have is generated forty times a week per rep and evaporates. A quote identifier carried onto the order makes conversion measurable with a field rather than a project, and win rate by discount level usually shows the discounting is not buying the business.
