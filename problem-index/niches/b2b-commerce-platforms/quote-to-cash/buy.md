# Configure-Price-Quote Practice

**Niche:** [[niches/b2b-commerce-platforms/quote-to-cash/profile|Quote to Cash]]
**Industry:** [[industries/b2b-commerce-platforms|B2B Commerce Platforms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Configure-price-quote is a mature enterprise software category with approval workflow, guided selling and margin control, and most distributors quote in a spreadsheet.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #convex-optimization #revenue-impact #evaluation-metrics #compliance #automation #confidence-intervals #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to bring the quote inside the commerce platform rather than leaving it in a resource planning system and an inbox — and whoever does that captures the highest-value orders, which currently never touch the storefront.

## The Problem
Producing a correct, approved, profitable quote quickly is what configure-price-quote software does, and the category is mature: guided configuration with validity constraints, pricing with discount governance, approval routing by margin threshold, quote documents generated automatically, and analytics on win rates and discounting. It is deployed widely in manufacturing and technology sales. Distribution, whose quote volume is far higher and whose margins are thinner, largely uses spreadsheets because the enterprise products are scoped and priced for a different buyer.

## What Already Exists
Configure-price-quote platforms with constraint-based configuration; discount governance and approval matrices; quote document generation and versioning; win-loss and discount analytics; guided selling with recommended options; and renewal and repeat quoting workflows.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to high-volume, low-value-per-quote distribution rather than to complex enterprise sales. It requires: (1) speed over sophistication, since a distribution rep produces many quotes a day and an enterprise tool that takes twenty minutes per quote is unusable — the design constraint is the opposite of the enterprise category's and is why the products have not transferred; (2) quoting from an existing entitlement and price resolution rather than from a list price and a discount, which is the reverse of the enterprise model and is where the pricing work connects; (3) most quotes requiring no configuration at all, which means configuration must be optional rather than central; (4) integration with the storefront so a customer can self-serve the simple ones and only the complex ones reach a rep; and (5) a price point that a distributor will pay, which is the practical barrier and is why the category has left this segment alone.

## Target Customer
Distributors and manufacturers below enterprise scale, sales operations, and the configure-price-quote vendors for whom high-volume distribution is an unserved segment.

## Impact If Solved
The category is mature and designed for a slow, complex, high-value quote, which is the opposite of the distribution case. Quoting from an existing entitlement rather than from list-minus-discount is the reverse of the enterprise model and is where it connects to the commerce platform.
