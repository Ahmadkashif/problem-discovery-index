# Procurement Integration

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/b2b-commerce-platforms|B2B Commerce Platforms]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to connect to a large customer's purchasing system in hours rather than weeks — and whoever does that takes the account, because the connection is the condition of the relationship and its cost decides which customers are worth having.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.6B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~11% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — standards exist, connection is a project
**Target Buyer:** Integration teams on both sides, and the specialists between them
**Automation Potential:** High — most of the variation is configuration

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Punchout and electronic ordering standards have existed for decades and are supported by every platform, and connecting to each large customer's procurement system remains a per-customer project measured in weeks. The standards are real; what varies is each procurement platform's interpretation, each customer's configuration, the catalogue format they want, the fields they require, the identifiers they use, their tax and unit-of-measure conventions, and their approval and confirmation expectations. That variation turns a standard into a bespoke integration, which sets a floor on the size of customer worth connecting and leaves the mid-market unserved. It is the clearest example in this industry of a solved standard whose implementation cost was never addressed.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Punchout and electronic data interchange support in the platform, specialist integration providers, and per-customer projects. The gaps: no library of pre-built configurations per procurement platform; the customer's required catalogue format is produced by hand each time; testing requires the customer's cooperation and is the schedule's critical path; changes on either side break the connection silently; and no smaller customer is served because the cost floor excludes them.
