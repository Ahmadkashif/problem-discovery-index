# The Clip Request Answered by Scrubbing

**Niche:** [[niches/sports-teams/game-video-tagging/profile|Game Video Tagging & Opponent Breakdown]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sports-teams|Sports Teams & Clubs]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** An assistant coach asks for "every time they doubled the post in the fourth quarter", and the coordinator finds them by scrubbing film.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #worker-facing #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn every game's film into a breakdown tagged in the coaching staff's own terms, and whoever's clubs need the fewest video-staff hours per game to reach a coach-ready breakdown takes the account.

## The Problem
Clip requests arrive by text in plain language, often late at night. If the tags do not cover the question exactly, the coordinator watches the games again.

## Why It's Still Broken
Tag panels are designed before the season, and coaches' questions change weekly.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Natural-language search over the tagged library plus the tracking and event data, returning candidate clips with timestamps for the coordinator to check, and remembering each new question as a tag for the future.

## Who Feels the Pain
Video coordinators and the assistant coaches waiting on clips before a game.

## Impact If Fixed
Answering a typical request in ~10 minutes instead of an hour, for ~6–8 requests a week (estimate), frees ~5–7 hours a week, or ~150 hours over a ~26-week season.
