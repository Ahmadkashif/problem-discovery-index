# Vendor Auto-Tagging With a Custom Vocabulary

**Niche:** [[niches/sports-teams/game-video-tagging/profile|Game Video Tagging & Opponent Breakdown]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sports-teams|Sports Teams & Clubs]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Clubs already pay for a video platform with automatic tagging; it needs to map its labels to the staff's terms and learn from corrections.
**Tags:** #transfer-learning #k-nearest-neighbors #data-integration #worker-facing #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn every game's film into a breakdown tagged in the coaching staff's own terms, and whoever's clubs need the fewest video-staff hours per game to reach a coach-ready breakdown takes the account.

## The Problem
The platform's automatic tags cover generic events. The staff's custom tag panel is filled in by hand, so the two never meet.

## What Already Exists
Hudl Sportscode, Catapult's video suite, Teamworks' football and hockey products, and league event feeds with tracking-derived labels.

## The Customization Gap
A mapping layer from vendor and league labels to the staff's panel, learned from past games (which vendor labels and tracking patterns precede each staff tag). It needs a correction loop that updates the mapping within the season and an export the coaching staff's existing workflow accepts. This can live as a plug-in to the platform the club already uses.

## Target Customer
Clubs locked into a video platform contract who want fewer manual tags without switching vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Even a 30% cut in manual tags (estimate) gives a coordinator back one to two hours per game, ~80–160 hours a season on an 82-game schedule.
