# Sponsorship Exposure Measurement & Valuation Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sports-teams|Sports Teams & Clubs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms that measure how much exposure a sponsor's logo and mentions got across broadcasts, streams and social media, and turn it into media value and sponsorship valuations. Relo Metrics (formerly GumGum Sports, partnered with Meltwater in 2025, search summary), Nielsen Sports, and KORE's Hookit (KORE bought Hookit in 2022, search summary; Two Circles bought KORE in 2024) are the main names. Rights holders pay to prove value to partners, and brands pay to evaluate what they bought.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–80 measurement analysts, computer-vision and data staff per firm (estimate); account staff who assemble per-client reports are counted only where they analyse
**Output:** Exposure and media-value reports per partner and season, sponsorship valuations, benchmark studies
**Proprietary data:** Years of logo-detection and social measurements across properties; the broadcasts and posts measured are other parties' content
**Clock:** Season-end recaps and partners' renewal dates (client clocks)
**Buyer:** Head of measurement or chief product officer at the firm
