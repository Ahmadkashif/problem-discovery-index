# College Sports Commission & NIL Go Clearinghouse

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sports-teams|Sports Teams & Clubs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The body created under the House settlement to enforce the revenue-sharing cap and review third-party NIL deals. Deloitte runs the NIL Go clearinghouse, where athletes report deals over $600. The commission had 29 full-time employees in September 2026, 12 of them on NIL Go, and cleared more than $227M of deals in July and August 2026 at over 200 deals a day (AP). It is funded by the conferences.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 29 staff in total; deal evaluators are production, and policy and analytics staff are under ~10 (estimate)
**Output:** Deal clearances and denials, investigations, guidance to schools
**Proprietary data:** Every reported NIL deal and its outcome, held in confidence
**Clock:** Per-deal review targets (68% resolved within seven days in July–August 2026)
**Buyer:** Chief executive of the commission
