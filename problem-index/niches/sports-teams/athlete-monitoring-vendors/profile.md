# Athlete Monitoring & Injury-Risk Analytics Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sports-teams|Sports Teams & Clubs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors of wearables, athlete management systems and load and injury-risk analytics sold to clubs' performance and medical staffs. Catapult reported US$140.7M of revenue for the year to March 2026, with annualised contract value of ~US$133.8M and Pro Team retention above 96% (search summary). Kinexon, Teamworks (AMS and Sports EMR), Kitman Labs and others compete (estimate). Clubs pay hardware and software subscriptions; the analysis is bundled into the product.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–80 sports scientists and data scientists at the largest vendor (estimate), fewer elsewhere
**Output:** Load and readiness dashboards, injury-risk flags, return-to-play tracking, benchmark reports across a vendor's clients
**Proprietary data:** Sensor and wellness data captured on each client's athletes; the vendor's own data is its models and methods
**Clock:** Season and training calendars set by clubs; no outside deadline
**Buyer:** Chief product or science officer at the vendor

**Kill switches:** undetermined (terms unread). Athlete biometric and health data is captured under the vendors' client agreements and under the leagues' collective bargaining agreements, whose wearable clauses were not read, so whether a vendor may pool it is not known.
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold (32). Real science teams on a large, growing installed base, but the insight is bundled into hardware and software, no outside clock governs it, the field is a handful of vendors, and the data rights are unresolved.
