# Regional Sports Networks' Research & Ad Sales Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sports-teams|Sports Teams & Clubs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The networks that buy clubs' local game rights and resell them through cable carriage, streaming and advertising. Main Street Sports Group, which emerged from Diamond Sports' 2023 bankruptcy and runs FanDuel Sports Network, held 15 networks with rights to 20 NBA and NHL teams in February 2026 after several MLB clubs left over missed payments (AP via WTOP). Team-owned networks and league-run media are the alternatives. Revenue is carriage fees, subscriptions and advertising.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Audience research and ad-sales analytics under ~10 per network (estimate)
**Output:** Ratings and audience reports for advertisers, carriage negotiation support
**Proprietary data:** Subscriber and viewing data from distributors and measurement providers, licensed for internal use
**Clock:** Carriage renewals and the advertising market's upfront and scatter cycles
**Buyer:** Network general manager
