# The Suite Contract Nobody Can Find

**Niche:** [[niches/sports-teams/premium-seating-suites/profile|Premium Seating, Suites & Club Memberships]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sports-teams|Sports Teams & Clubs]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Suite terms, escalators, food credits and renewal options sit in signed PDFs, and the inventory sheet does not match them.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #data-integration #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a building's suites, loge boxes and club seats sold on multi-year contracts, and whoever's clubs open each season with the highest share of premium inventory already under contract takes the account.

## The Problem
A five-year suite contract has an annual escalator, an included food-and-beverage credit, ticket allocations for non-season events, a right of first refusal and a renewal notice date. Each deal differs. The premium team's spreadsheet holds the price and the expiry year, and the rest is in the PDF.

## Why It's Still Broken
Contracts were negotiated one by one by sellers who have since moved on, and nobody had time to abstract them.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Extract every premium contract's terms with an LLM into a structured register, reconcile it to the ticketing system's suite inventory and the billing records, and flag mismatches and upcoming notice dates.

## Who Feels the Pain
Premium service managers answering holders' questions, finance teams billing escalators, and sellers who miss a renewal notice window.

## Impact If Fixed
Catching missed escalators and notice dates on ~50–150 premium contracts (estimate) recovers revenue and gives the renewal team a calendar it can trust.
