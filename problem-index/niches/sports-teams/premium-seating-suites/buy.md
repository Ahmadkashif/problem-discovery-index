# Account-Based Selling for Corporate Suites

**Niche:** [[niches/sports-teams/premium-seating-suites/profile|Premium Seating, Suites & Club Memberships]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sports-teams|Sports Teams & Clubs]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** B2B account-based selling tools find companies likely to buy software; a premium seller needs companies likely to buy a suite in this market this year.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #k-nearest-neighbors #feature-engineering #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a building's suites, loge boxes and club seats sold on multi-year contracts, and whoever's clubs open each season with the highest share of premium inventory already under contract takes the account.

## The Problem
New buildings and renovations add premium inventory that has to be sold to companies within a few seasons. Sellers work from personal networks, chamber of commerce lists and past buyers.

## What Already Exists
B2B intent and account-based marketing platforms, firmographic databases, and the club's CRM.

## The Customization Gap
Sports-specific signals and fit: local headquarters and office expansions, client-entertainment-heavy sectors (law, banking, construction, healthcare), companies whose executives already hold season tickets, past single-game suite rentals, and sponsorship relationships. Scoring must also weigh fit to a specific inventory class (a ten-person suite versus four club seats) and term length.

## Target Customer
Premium sales teams at clubs opening or renovating buildings.

## Impact If Solved
Focusing a team of ~6–10 premium sellers (estimate) on the most likely buyers can shorten the sell-out of new premium inventory by a season, worth millions of dollars of revenue brought forward.
