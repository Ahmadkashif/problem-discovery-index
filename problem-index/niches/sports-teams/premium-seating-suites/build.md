# Suite Usage as an Early Renewal Signal

**Niche:** [[niches/sports-teams/premium-seating-suites/profile|Premium Seating, Suites & Club Memberships]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sports-teams|Sports Teams & Clubs]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A corporate suite that sits half-empty in year two of a five-year contract will not renew, and the club usually learns that when the controller's letter arrives.
**Tags:** #survival-analysis #gradient-boosting #logistic-regression #feature-engineering #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a building's suites, loge boxes and club seats sold on multi-year contracts, and whoever's clubs open each season with the highest share of premium inventory already under contract takes the account.

## The Problem
Suite holders pay for a season of access whether they use it or not. Use is the leading indicator: scanned guests per game, games where tickets were transferred to clients, nights resold, food orders, the executive sponsor's own attendance. A premium account rep sees some of this and remembers less. Renewal conversations start a year before expiry, often too late to change the pattern.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Usage data is spread across ticketing scans, transfer logs, resale and the hospitality system, and the club's premium team is a handful of sellers rather than analysts. Contracts are long, so a club sees few renewals a year to learn from.

## What to Build
A usage ledger per premium account, joined from scans, transfers, resale and catering orders. A time-to-non-renewal model is pooled across clubs through a vendor, because one club has too few events. The rep sees an early-warning list with the reasons and suggested interventions (client-entertainment events, sharing the suite with another company, a smaller product).

## Target Customer
Premium sales and service leaders at big-league clubs and Power Four departments, and the outsourcing firms that sell premium inventory for new buildings.

## Impact If Built
Saving two suites a year at ~$200–500K each (estimate) pays for the system many times over.
