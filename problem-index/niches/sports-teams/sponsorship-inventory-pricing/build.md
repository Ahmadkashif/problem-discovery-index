# Valuations Graded Against Signed Deals

**Niche:** [[niches/sports-teams/sponsorship-inventory-pricing/profile|Sponsorship Inventory Pricing & Deal Valuation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sports-teams|Sports Teams & Clubs]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Sponsorship valuations are quoted with confidence and almost never checked against what the deal actually signed for.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #bayesian-inference #confidence-intervals #evaluation-metrics #feature-engineering #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to price a club's unsold inventory (patches, naming rights, signage, digital, hospitality) from what comparable deals actually signed for, and whoever's clubs earn the most contracted partnership revenue per available inventory asset takes the account.

## The Problem
A club pricing a new jersey patch or arena naming right commissions a valuation or pulls comparables from a deal database. The number that comes back is a point estimate. Months later the deal signs, sometimes at that number, often well below after a long negotiation, sometimes not at all. That outcome is the label, and it is rarely fed back to the valuer or the club's own pricing.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Signed fees are confidential and often reported only as ranges in trade press. Valuers sell their estimate, not a track record, and a public error rate would be uncomfortable. Clubs see only their own deals.

## What to Build
A club-side pricing model with calibrated ranges. It is trained on the club's own signed deals with the bundle allocated to each asset, on licensed comparables, and on the club's audience and exposure measures. Every quote is logged and scored against the signed fee or no-sale outcome, so sellers see which ranges clear. Across a multimedia-rights holder's 50–100 schools the same model gets enough deals to learn.

## Target Customer
Partnership strategy leads at big-league clubs and college multimedia-rights holders.

## Impact If Built
Raising contracted revenue per available asset by 10% on ~$10–30M of open inventory at a big-league club (estimate) is ~$1–3M a year.
