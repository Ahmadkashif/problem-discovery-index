# Players Associations' Research & Survey Units

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sports-teams|Sports Teams & Clubs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The unions representing players in each league research collective bargaining economics, salary data and working conditions for their members. The NFL Players Association surveyed 1,759 players for its 2026 team report cards; an arbitrator found the cards breached the CBA and barred their release, and they leaked in February 2026 (search summary). The MLBPA, NBPA and NHLPA run comparable research (estimate). Members fund the work through dues.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Research and economics staff under ~10 per union (estimate)
**Output:** Bargaining analysis, salary and arbitration data for members and agents, member surveys
**Proprietary data:** Member contracts, survey responses and grievance records
**Clock:** CBA expiries and salary-arbitration calendars set in the agreements
**Buyer:** Executive director
