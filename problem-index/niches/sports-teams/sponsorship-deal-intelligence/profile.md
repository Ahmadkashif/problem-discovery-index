# Sponsorship Deal Intelligence Databases

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sports-teams|Sports Teams & Clubs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms whose product is a database of who sponsors whom, with what assets and at what estimated price. SponsorUnited is the clear leader. It says its platform covers 10K venues audited by 3,000 scouts and 500K devices scraped, trains deal estimates on $45B of transacted deals, and has 11K users across brands, rights holders, agencies and media companies. Clubs use it to prospect and price; brands use its proposal evaluator to judge what they are offered.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–60 research, data and analyst staff (estimate); the 3,000 venue scouts are production and are not counted
**Output:** Deal and asset database subscriptions, estimated deal values, market reports, an AI analyst over the data
**Proprietary data:** Audited and scraped records of sponsorships across leagues, venues and media, plus deal-value estimates; the transacted-deal data used to train estimates comes from sources not described
**Clock:** The vendor's own release cycle and its clients' selling and renewal seasons (client clocks)
**Buyer:** Chief product officer or head of research at the vendor

**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold (39). The data is the product and nobody else has a comparable sponsorship census. But it grows with scouts and scrapers rather than analysts, no outside deadline governs it, and it is essentially a one-leader market (Q5 2).
