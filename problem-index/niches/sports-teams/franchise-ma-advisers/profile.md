# Franchise M&A, Valuation & Ownership Advisers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sports-teams|Sports Teams & Clubs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Investment banks and boutiques that advise on buying and selling clubs and stakes, value franchises and run ownership processes through league approval. Inner Circle Sports, founded in 2002, agreed in 2026 to be acquired by William Blair (search summary); larger banks and a few other boutiques compete (estimate). Sellers and buyers pay success fees on transactions. Published franchise valuations from business media are free lists, not a pocket.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Bankers staffed deal by deal (production); any standing research team is under ~10 (estimate)
**Output:** Valuations, buyer lists, offering memoranda, fairness opinions
**Proprietary data:** Deal files containing clubs' confidential financials
**Clock:** League approval votes and transaction closings (client clocks)
**Buyer:** Managing partner
