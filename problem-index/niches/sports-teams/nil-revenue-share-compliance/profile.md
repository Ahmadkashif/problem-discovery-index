# College Revenue-Share & NIL Compliance Platforms and Advisers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sports-teams|Sports Teams & Clubs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Software vendors and advisory firms helping athletic departments run revenue sharing and third-party NIL under the House settlement. Teamworks lists Compliance, GM, Influencer and Wallet (athlete payment) products. Learfield runs NIL programmes for many of its schools (search summary). Smaller advisory boutiques and law firms advise on cap allocation, Title IX and NIL Go submissions (estimate). Departments pay subscriptions, retainers and project fees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–20 cross-client analysts and product researchers at the larger firms (estimate); per-school advisers and compliance staff who prepare individual deal submissions are production and are not counted
**Output:** Cap ledgers and allocation advice, NIL deal preparation and fair-value support, compliance reporting
**Proprietary data:** Methods and benchmarks across client schools; the payment, contract and athlete records belong to each school
**Clock:** The cap year (1 July to 30 June) and per-deal NIL Go reviews; per-deal deadlines bind production work
**Buyer:** Chief executive or head of college product at the vendor or advisory firm
