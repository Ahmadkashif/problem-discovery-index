# League Club-Business Consulting & Media Units

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sports-teams|Sports Teams & Clubs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
League-office groups that pool data from every club and advise clubs on ticketing, sponsorship, marketing and media. The NBA's Team Marketing & Business Operations group is an in-house consultancy serving NBA, WNBA and G League teams; its president described it as building best practices and "industry-leading analytics on behalf of 85 teams in four leagues" (search summary). MLB's media operation produced local broadcasts for at least 13 clubs in 2026 after the RSN collapse. The NFL and NHL run comparable club-business groups (estimate). Clubs pay through league economics, not invoices.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–60 analysts, data scientists and consultants per league group (estimate)
**Output:** Benchmarks across clubs, ticketing and sponsorship playbooks, club-level consulting, league-run local media
**Proprietary data:** Pooled ticketing, sponsorship, attendance and fan data from every club in the league, held under league rules
**Clock:** Season and league-meeting calendars the league sets for itself
**Buyer:** League executive vice president for club business

**Kill switches:** live (membership data: club data furnished under league constitutions). Clubs furnish their ticketing, sponsorship and fan records to the league as members, under constitutions and data-sharing rules that were not read, and no checked term lets the league turn them into a product.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch (35). The deepest cross-club business data in the industry, used well, but it is the members' data, the work is funded by the clubs as members rather than invoiced, it runs on the league's own calendar, and four or five leagues buy it. The ticketer-side equivalent is the already-profiled [[niches/ticketing-agencies/primary-ticketing-data-science/profile|Primary Ticketing Platform Data Science]] (37, kill switch).
