# Amateur & International Scouting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sports-teams|Sports Teams & Clubs]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn scouts' reports, video and tracking into draft and signing decisions, and whoever's clubs get the most major-league value per signing-bonus dollar takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600M a year of amateur and international scouting staff, travel, scouting software, amateur tracking and video spent by MLB, NFL, NBA and NHL clubs (estimate), deciding ~$300–450M of MLB draft and international bonuses alone (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1–2% of club spending (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — grades and notes are typed into a scouting system after the game, and the most valuable read is still the scout's memory
**Target Buyer:** Scouting director, assistant general manager for amateur and international scouting, or vice president of player personnel
**Automation Potential:** Medium — report capture, retrieval and calibration automate; the look and the relationship with the player's family do not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Big-league player evaluation already runs on tracking data and R&D groups. Amateur and international scouting is where it still runs on people in the stands: area scouts covering high schools, junior colleges and colleges, international scouts following 14- and 15-year-olds in the Dominican Republic and Venezuela before they can sign, cross-checkers and a director who decide between them. Outcomes arrive four to seven years later. The contest is won on value per bonus dollar, which no vendor reports and which every club tries to estimate privately.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Club scouting systems (in-house or from Teamworks, which bought PFF's enterprise business, Zelus Analytics and Telemetry Sports); amateur tracking from TrackMan, Hawk-Eye installations and portable units; video from Synergy and Hudl; public draft and minor-league data. Gaps: reports not linked to the clips and pitches they describe, grades on drifting scales, no grading of each scout's projections, and nothing kept when a scout leaves.
