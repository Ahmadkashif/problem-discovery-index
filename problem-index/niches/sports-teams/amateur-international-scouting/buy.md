# Voice Capture for Scouts at the Park

**Niche:** [[niches/sports-teams/amateur-international-scouting/profile|Amateur & International Scouting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sports-teams|Sports Teams & Clubs]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Speech-to-text note apps exist everywhere; a scout needs one that writes a report in his club's grading format and links it to the pitch he was watching.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #data-integration #worker-facing #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn scouts' reports, video and tracking into draft and signing decisions, and whoever's clubs get the most major-league value per signing-bonus dollar takes the account.

## The Problem
Scouts type reports at night in a hotel, hours after the look, into a form with dozens of fields. Detail is lost, and the report is written to the form rather than to what was seen.

## What Already Exists
General voice-note and transcription apps; the club's scouting system's mobile form; video and tracking apps used at the park.

## The Customization Gap
A capture tool that maps spoken observations to the club's tool grades and fields ("fastball 92–94, touched 95, arm works, will add strength" becomes present and future grades with the phrase kept). It timestamps each note so it can be linked to the pitch-tracking row and video clip. It also works offline in rural ballparks, and queues the report for the scout to confirm in a minute.

## Target Customer
Amateur and international scouting departments at MLB, NFL and college programmes.

## Impact If Solved
Saving ~30–60 minutes of report writing per look (estimate) across ~150 looks a spring returns ~100 hours per scout. More importantly, it captures the detail the grading model in the build note needs.
