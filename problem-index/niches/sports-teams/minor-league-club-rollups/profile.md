# Minor-League Club Ownership Groups

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sports-teams|Sports Teams & Clubs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Companies that own and operate many minor-league clubs. Diamond Baseball Holdings, formed under Endeavor in 2021 and sold to Silver Lake in 2022, owns ~42–49 of the 120 affiliated MiLB clubs (search summary; counts conflict). It runs shared ticketing, sponsorship, purchasing and finance services across its clubs. Revenue is the clubs' own tickets, sponsorship and concessions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Central revenue and analytics staff under ~10 (estimate); club front offices are operators
**Output:** Shared pricing, sponsorship and purchasing playbooks; portfolio reporting to the owner
**Proprietary data:** Ticketing and sponsorship records of the owned clubs
**Clock:** Seasons and the owner's own reporting calendar
**Buyer:** Chief operating or revenue officer of the group
