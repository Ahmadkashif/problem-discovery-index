# The Recap Deck Built in December

**Niche:** [[niches/sports-teams/sponsor-renewal-proof/profile|Sponsor Outcome Proof & Renewal]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sports-teams|Sports Teams & Clubs]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The season's proof for each partner is assembled after the season ends, too late to fix anything it reveals.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #automation #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to show a partner what its sponsorship produced in the partner's own business before the renewal date, and whoever's clubs keep the highest net partnership-dollar retention at renewal takes the account.

## The Problem
Activation staff collect photos, screenshots and vendor reports through the season and build each partner's recap deck after the last game. Shortfalls (a board that ran short in August, posts that never went out) are discovered in the deck, when the only remedy left is a discount at renewal.

## Why It's Still Broken
During the season the team is running game days. Recaps are seen as a year-end deliverable, not a running check, and nobody has a weekly view of delivery against each contract.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A weekly delivery report per partner, generated from board logs, scripts, posts and measured exposure against the contract's entitlements, with shortfalls flagged for a makegood within the season. The year-end recap becomes an export of the running record.

## Who Feels the Pain
Activation managers working weekends in December, partnership sellers walking into renewals with surprises, and partners who learn of missed deliverables late.

## Impact If Fixed
Catching shortfalls in-season turns discounts into makegoods. On a $50M book, avoiding a 1% renewal concession is ~$500K a year (estimate).
