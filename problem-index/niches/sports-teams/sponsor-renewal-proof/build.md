# A Lift Study Written Into the Contract

**Niche:** [[niches/sports-teams/sponsor-renewal-proof/profile|Sponsor Outcome Proof & Renewal]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sports-teams|Sports Teams & Clubs]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Partners ask at renewal whether the sponsorship moved their business, and the club has no outcome data because nobody asked for it at signing.
**Tags:** #causal-inference #hypothesis-testing #confidence-intervals #bayesian-inference #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to show a partner what its sponsorship produced in the partner's own business before the renewal date, and whoever's clubs keep the highest net partnership-dollar retention at renewal takes the account.

## The Problem
A regional bank, a grocery chain or a health system signs a three-year deal. In year three its marketing lead has to justify the renewal to a finance team that discounts media value. The club can show impressions and photos. It cannot show account openings, store visits or patient volumes in the home market against a comparable market, because it never had that data.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Partners are reluctant to share outcome data with a property that is also negotiating price with them. Clubs have not had the measurement staff to run a credible study. Measurement vendors sell exposure, which they can measure alone.

## What to Build
A data-sharing clause and a lift product built in at signing. The partner shares a small set of outcome series by market or postcode under an agreement. The club or its vendor sets up geo-holdouts or a synthetic control around activations (theme nights, giveaways, a naming launch) and reports lift with intervals each season. Results go to both sides' finance teams before the renewal window.

## Target Customer
Partnership VPs at big-league clubs and Power Four departments with regional partners (banks, grocers, health systems, utilities) whose outcomes are local and measurable.

## Impact If Built
If credible lift studies raise net partnership-dollar retention by 10 points on the ~30% of a book up for renewal each year (estimate), a club with a ~$50M book keeps ~$1.5M more a year.
