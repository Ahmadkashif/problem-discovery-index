# Exposure Measurement Joined to Entitlements

**Niche:** [[niches/sports-teams/sponsor-renewal-proof/profile|Sponsor Outcome Proof & Renewal]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sports-teams|Sports Teams & Clubs]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Clubs already buy logo-exposure measurement; it reports media value per logo, not delivery against each contract line.
**Tags:** #cnns #object-detection #large-language-models #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to show a partner what its sponsorship produced in the partner's own business before the renewal date, and whoever's clubs keep the highest net partnership-dollar retention at renewal takes the account.

## The Problem
Exposure vendors detect logos in broadcasts and social posts and assign a media value. The partner's contract, however, promises specific things: board minutes per game, a named placement, posts per month. The vendor's report and the contract use different units, and someone reconciles them by hand.

## What Already Exists
Computer-vision exposure measurement from Relo Metrics (formerly GumGum Sports), Nielsen Sports and KORE's Hookit, delivered as dashboards and season reports.

## The Customization Gap
Map each measured exposure to the contract line it fulfils, using entitlements extracted from contracts with an LLM and the club's board and PA logs. Count shortfalls per line and per game, and price makegoods. Keep media value as one column rather than the headline. Feed the delivered-exposure series into lift studies as the treatment variable.

## Target Customer
Clubs already licensing an exposure-measurement vendor.

## Impact If Solved
Turns a vendor report into a contract-line recap with no extra measurement spend, removing most of the ~10–20 hours per partner (estimate) of manual reconciliation.
