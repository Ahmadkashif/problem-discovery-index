# Institutional Sports Investors & Lenders

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sports-teams|Sports Teams & Clubs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Private-equity funds and lenders that buy minority stakes in clubs or lend against them. On 2024-08-27 NFL owners approved private-equity stakes of up to 10% for Arctos, Ares, Sixth Street and a consortium of Blackstone, Carlyle, CVC, Dynasty Equity and Ludis, each investing at least 3% and holding for six years (search summary). MLB, NBA, NHL and MLS allow similar funds under their own rules (estimate). The funds earn returns on the stakes; the clubs and leagues get capital.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 investment, research and data staff at the largest dedicated sports fund (estimate)
**Output:** Investment cases, franchise valuations, portfolio monitoring
**Proprietary data:** Club financials supplied under NDA and league confidentiality rules; the fund's own deal history
**Clock:** League approval votes and the fund's holding periods (client and league clocks)
**Buyer:** Managing partner or head of research at the fund

**Kill switches:** live — data isn't theirs. Club financial information is supplied under non-disclosure agreements and league confidentiality rules and cannot be reused as a product.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch (26). The research is monetised through investment returns, not invoiced; the inputs are confidential club data; and a handful of league-approved funds make up the market. The general shape is the private-equity pattern already profiled under [[industries/private-equity-firms|Private Equity Firms]].
