# Stadium Feasibility & Economic Impact Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sports-teams|Sports Teams & Clubs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Consultancies that study whether a market can support a new stadium, arena or ballpark, what it would cost, how it could be funded and what economic impact it would have. CSL International is a long-running example: its studies for Louisville's soccer stadium (2016) and Des Moines' soccer park expansion (2019) were priced at ~$73–75K each (search summary). Cities, counties and clubs commission them, usually ahead of a public financing vote.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Consultants staffed per study (production); any standing research team is under ~10 (estimate)
**Output:** Market, feasibility, funding and economic-impact reports
**Proprietary data:** Past study files and comparable-venue data, largely built on clients' and venues' figures
**Clock:** Council and legislature schedules and lease negotiations (client clocks)
**Buyer:** Firm principal
