# In-Market Reach and Revenue Across Every Screen

**Niche:** [[niches/sports-teams/local-media-rights/profile|Local Media Rights & Team Streaming]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sports-teams|Sports Teams & Clubs]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A club now sells its games through cable, over-the-air partners and its own stream, and nobody can tell it how many in-market households it reaches or what each one is worth.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #survival-analysis #bayesian-inference #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to replace the rights fee a collapsing regional sports network used to pay, through streaming subscriptions, over-the-air reach and local advertising, and whoever delivers a club the most local media revenue per in-market household takes the account.

## The Problem
A club that left its RSN now has viewers on a local broadcast partner, on its own or the league's streaming product, and on any cable carriage that survived. Each distributor reports differently: streaming sign-ups by day, over-the-air ratings from a measurement panel, cable subscriber counts in carriage statements. The club's president has to set next season's stream price and decide whether to keep the over-the-air deal, without a single measure of households reached or revenue per household.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Until 2023 the RSN held this problem and paid a fixed fee. Clubs have no media analytics staff. League-run media sees many clubs but reports to the league.

## What to Build
A club-side media ledger joining streaming subscriptions and churn, over-the-air ratings, remaining carriage, advertising sold per game and sponsorship tied to broadcasts. On top of it, forecast in-market households reached per game and revenue per household by channel, and model the effect of price, blackout and schedule changes.

## Target Customer
Presidents and heads of media at the MLB, NBA and NHL clubs that left or lost an RSN deal, and the league media operations producing for them.

## Impact If Built
Each 1% of in-market households converted to paying subscribers at ~$15–20 a month (estimate) is worth several million dollars a year in a large market.
