# Payroll and Cap Software for a Roster

**Niche:** [[niches/sports-teams/college-athletics-revenue-sharing/profile|College Athletics Departments Under Revenue Sharing]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sports-teams|Sports Teams & Clubs]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Payroll and compensation-planning software exists for every employer; a department needs one that understands a cap shared across sports and a clearinghouse for outside deals.
**Tags:** #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #compliance #automation #large-language-models
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to help an athletic department spend its revenue-sharing cap on athletes who will stay through their agreements, and whoever's schools lose the fewest revenue-share dollars to athletes who leave before their agreement ends takes the account.

## The Problem
Business offices track revenue-share payments in the university's payroll or accounts-payable systems and in spreadsheets kept by the general manager. Neither knows the cap, the per-sport allocation, or which payments count toward it.

## What Already Exists
University ERP and payroll systems, compensation-planning tools, and sports operations platforms such as Teamworks with its Compliance, GM and Wallet products.

## The Customization Gap
A cap ledger that counts each payment the way the settlement counts it, across sports and the July–June cap year. It must track roster limits and scholarship equivalents, record each agreement's term, payment schedule and departure provisions, show how much of the cap is exposed to an athlete leaving mid-agreement, and produce the reports the conference and the commission ask for. It also has to sit beside, not replace, the university's payroll.

## Target Customer
Business offices and general managers at departments paying revenue share.

## Impact If Solved
Replacing spreadsheet cap tracking shows, on a ~$20M pool (estimate), how many dollars are already paid to athletes who could still leave, and saves the business office ~3–4 weeks of reconciliation a year (estimate).
