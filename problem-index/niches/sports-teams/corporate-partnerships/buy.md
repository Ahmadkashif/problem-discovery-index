# Sponsorship CRM Extended to Contract Lines

**Niche:** [[niches/sports-teams/corporate-partnerships/profile|Corporate Partnerships & Sponsorship]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sports-teams|Sports Teams & Clubs]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Clubs already pay for a sponsorship CRM; it tracks the partner and the pipeline, not the contract's individual promises.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to grow a club's partnership revenue, but that hides two contests with different winners: pricing unsold inventory from what comparable deals close for, won on the most contracted partnership revenue per available inventory asset, and proving to existing partners what their money produced, won on net partnership-dollar retention at renewal. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Partnership teams run their pipeline in a CRM and their fulfilment in spreadsheets beside it. The CRM knows the partner, the contract value and the renewal date. It does not know that the contract owes 30 seconds of LED rotation per half-inning, two PA reads a game and four suite nights.

## What Already Exists
KORE (Two Circles) partnership management on Salesforce or Dynamics, which 118 of 154 big-five-league US teams used when Two Circles bought it; generic Salesforce with custom objects; contract-management tools.

## The Customization Gap
Sports-specific extensions: LLM extraction of entitlements from contract language into countable, dated obligations; a home-schedule calendar that handles rainouts, doubleheaders and playoff dates; connectors to the video board, PA script and social tools to count delivery; makegood records; and recap generation that shows delivery against each contract line.

## Target Customer
Clubs already licensing a sponsorship CRM who want the fulfilment and recap work inside it.

## Impact If Solved
An activation team of 5–10 at a big-league club (estimate) gets back ~1,000 hours a season of recap and tracking work, and missed deliverables are caught while a makegood is still possible.
