# A Partnership Ledger From Pitch to Renewal

**Niche:** [[niches/sports-teams/corporate-partnerships/profile|Corporate Partnerships & Sponsorship]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sports-teams|Sports Teams & Clubs]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A partnership's price, promises, delivery and renewal outcome live in four systems, so a club cannot learn which kinds of deals keep or grow their partnership dollars at renewal.

**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to grow a club's partnership revenue, but that hides two contests with different winners: pricing unsold inventory from what comparable deals close for, won on the most contracted partnership revenue per available inventory asset, and proving to existing partners what their money produced, won on net partnership-dollar retention at renewal. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
A partnership deal starts as a proposal priced off a rate card and a seller's judgement, becomes a contract with dozens of entitlements, is delivered across a season by the activation team, and ends in a renewal negotiation where the partner asks what it got. Each stage is recorded somewhere different: the proposal in a deck, the contract in a PDF, the delivery in spreadsheets and board logs, the renewal in the CRM. Nobody can ask "which categories, inventory bundles and activation types keep or grow their dollars at renewal, and which churn after one term?"

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Vendors entered at one stage each: CRMs at the pipeline, SponsorUnited at deal data, measurement firms at exposure. Clubs treat each deal as a relationship, and senior sellers keep their own history. The renewal outcome comes three to five years after the pitch.

## What to Build
One ledger per partnership: the pitch (inventory, price, comparables used), the contract as a list of dated obligations extracted by an LLM, delivery against each obligation, exposure measured, partner feedback and the renewal fee. On a few seasons of ledgers, model time-to-churn and renewal uplift by category, inventory mix and delivery rate. The ledger also feeds the two sub-niches: comparables for pricing, proof for renewal.

## Target Customer
Chief revenue officers and partnership VPs at big-league clubs and Power Four athletic departments, and multimedia-rights holders such as Learfield and Playfly that sell partnerships on schools' behalf.

## Impact If Built
On a partnership book of ~$30–100M a year at a big-league club (estimate), moving renewal retention by 5 points is ~$1.5–5M a year of revenue kept.
