# Corporate Partnerships & Sponsorship

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sports-teams|Sports Teams & Clubs]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to grow a club's partnership revenue, but that hides two contests with different winners: pricing unsold inventory from what comparable deals close for, won on the most contracted partnership revenue per available inventory asset, and proving to existing partners what their money produced, won on net partnership-dollar retention at renewal. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.2B a year of partnership-department staff, sponsorship software, deal data, valuation and measurement services at North American clubs and college athletic departments (estimate), on ~$8–10B of team sponsorship revenue (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15–20% of club revenue comes from sponsorship (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — pipelines live in a CRM and exposure is measured by vendors, but contracts are PDFs, entitlements are spreadsheets and recaps are slide decks
**Target Buyer:** Chief revenue officer or senior vice president of corporate partnerships
**Automation Potential:** Medium — pricing comparables, fulfilment tracking and recaps automate; the relationship and the renewal conversation do not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Sponsorship is the largest revenue line a club fully controls. Media rights are set by leagues and networks, and tickets are capped by seats. Jersey patches (now sold in the NBA, NHL, MLB and MLS), naming rights, LED and signage, digital and social inventory, hospitality and category exclusivities are priced, sold and renewed by the club's own partnership team. The niche spans the deal's whole life. That is why the contested sentence splits: the work before signing and the work before renewal use different data and are won by different vendors.

## Current Tools & Gaps
KORE (part of Two Circles since October 2024) for partner records and fulfilment; SponsorUnited for deal intelligence and a proposal evaluator; Relo Metrics, Nielsen Sports and KORE's Hookit for exposure and media value; Salesforce or Dynamics for pipeline. Gaps: inventory valued from a rate card rather than comparable signed deals, entitlements not reconciled to delivery, and no outcome data from the partner's side at renewal.
