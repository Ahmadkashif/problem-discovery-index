# College Multimedia Rights Holders

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/sports-teams|Sports Teams & Clubs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Companies that pay college athletic departments a guaranteed fee for their sponsorship, radio and signage rights and then sell those rights to brands through on-campus sales teams. Learfield works with 100+ colleges, conferences and venues, has ~$1.2B of annual revenue and agreed to be bought by TPG for ~$1.8–2.0B in 2026 (search summary). Playfly says it serves 65+ athletic departments and sells the NCAA's local event sponsorship (search summary). Revenue comes from the brands; the schools receive the guarantee and a revenue share.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 research, analytics and sponsorship-insight staff at the larger firm (estimate); on-campus sellers are production
**Output:** Sponsorship proposals and recaps for brands, audience research, valuations of school inventory, NIL programme reporting
**Proprietary data:** Sales and fulfilment records across 100+ schools' sponsorship programmes, held as the rights holder
**Clock:** Rights renewals with schools and brands' renewal dates (client clocks)
**Buyer:** Chief revenue or data officer at the rights holder

**Kill switches:** none — the rights holder buys the rights and holds its own sales and fulfilment records; a marketplace's or rights holder's own transaction records carry no switch.
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold (31). The widest view of college sponsorship pricing in the country, but it monetises the view by selling inventory rather than analysis, and two or three firms make up the market. Its data is the natural training set for [[niches/sports-teams/sponsorship-inventory-pricing/profile|Sponsorship Inventory Pricing & Deal Valuation]] in college.
