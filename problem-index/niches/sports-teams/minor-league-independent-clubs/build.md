# One Revenue Plan for Seventy Home Dates

**Niche:** [[niches/sports-teams/minor-league-independent-clubs/profile|Minor-League & Independent Clubs]]
**Industry:** [[industries/sports-teams|Sports Teams & Clubs]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A small club decides price, promotion, group targets and sponsor nights for each home date in separate spreadsheets, with no view of what the date will earn.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #bayesian-inference #time-series-forecasting #ai-agent #workflow-orchestration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to run a small club's ticketing, sponsorship, promotions and fan data from one system that a front office of twenty to forty people will actually use, and whoever lifts a club's total revenue per home date the most takes the account.

## The Problem
In winter the front office lays out the season: which dates get fireworks, which get a giveaway, which are sold to a sponsor as a theme night, which are pushed to schools and churches. Ticket prices are set once. Each decision is made by a different person in a different file, and nobody forecasts what a date will earn across tickets, sponsorship and merchandise.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The clubs are small buyers, each vendor sells one slice (ticketing, CRM, sponsorship), and the shared demand model this needs is only possible by pooling data across clubs.

## What to Build
A date-by-date revenue plan for the season. A pooled demand model (schedules and attendance from public data, weather, promotions, school calendars, resale signals) forecasts each date. The plan suggests where to put promotions, theme nights and group pushes, and it updates weekly in season. It extends the per-game pricing MVP in [[niches/sports-teams/per-game-ticket-pricing/profile|Per-Game Ticket Pricing & Demand Forecasting]] into the club's whole revenue calendar.

## Target Customer
Presidents and general managers of affiliated and independent clubs, and multi-club owners such as Diamond Baseball Holdings that can buy centrally.

## Impact If Built
Lifting revenue per home date by ~5% at a club earning ~$8–15M a year (estimate) is ~$400–750K, more than the cost of a front-office department.
