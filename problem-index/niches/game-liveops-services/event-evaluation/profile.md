# Event Evaluation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/game-liveops-services|Game LiveOps Services]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to measure what an event cost over the following months rather than what it earned in the week it ran — and whoever measures the cost takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~20% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium, short-horizon
**Target Buyer:** Live operations leads
**Automation Potential:** High — long-horizon causal measurement

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every live game runs events, and every event is judged on the revenue it produced in the week it ran. The cost — players who found it exhausting, unfair or manipulative and quietly reduced their engagement — arrives over the following months and is attributed to nothing. The reporting structure therefore favours the more aggressive event every single time, and the accumulated effect is a game that earns well for two years and then does not, with no single decision to point at.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Event revenue reports, participation rates, and an A/B framework measuring a week. The gaps: no post-event retention measurement; no attribution of later churn to earlier events; no fatigue accounting; no comparison across event designs on a long horizon; and no cost side to the ledger at all.
