# Player Support & Community Triage

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/game-liveops-services|Game LiveOps Services]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a continuous stream of player reports and community noise into the handful of signals the live team must act on today — and whoever does the triage takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$150M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — outsourced
**Target Buyer:** Support and community leads
**Automation Potential:** Very high — classification, routing and resolution

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A live game generates a permanent stream of support tickets, bug reports, refund requests, exploit reports and community discussion across several channels in several languages. Most of it is repetitive and mechanically resolvable. Buried in it are the few items the live team must see today — an exploit spreading, a progression blocker, a payment failure affecting a region. The triage that separates the two is done by outsourced teams working from a macro list, and the signal reaches the live team late or not at all.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A ticketing system, a macro library, an outsourced support vendor and several community channels nobody aggregates. The gaps: no automated classification; no account-state lookup at the point of response; no cross-channel aggregation; no detection of emerging issues from volume; and no feedback path from support into the live team.
