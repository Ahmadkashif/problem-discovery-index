# The Live Operations On-Call

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/game-liveops-services|Game LiveOps Services]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make one person responsible for a game running in every timezone on a holiday weekend into something survivable — and whoever does it takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — one person
**Target Buyer:** Live operations managers
**Automation Potential:** High — runbooks, automation and routing

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The game runs continuously in every timezone. The calendar has no gaps. One person is responsible for what happens when something goes wrong at three in the morning on a holiday weekend — and unlike an infrastructure on-call, the failures are as often design or economy incidents as technical ones, which means the runbook is judgement rather than a procedure. The role is under-tooled, under-staffed and a reliable source of attrition in a category that depends on continuity.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A pager, a config console, a chat channel and a list of who to wake. The gaps: no runbooks for economy and event incidents; no automated mitigation; no decision authority defined for out-of-hours changes; no follow-the-sun coverage in most teams; and no record of what previous incidents required.
