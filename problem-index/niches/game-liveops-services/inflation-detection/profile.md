# Inflation Detection

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/game-liveops-services|Game LiveOps Services]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make a slow compounding economic drift visible while it is still cheap to correct — and whoever builds the instrument that shows it takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — invisible in dashboards
**Target Buyer:** Live operations and analytics leads
**Automation Potential:** Very high — index construction and monitoring

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The contest here is retrospective and immediate: what is the economy doing right now, and has it already drifted. It requires no behavioural model and asks nobody to trust a forecast — it is index construction, aggregation and alerting over data the game already writes. That makes it a fundamentally easier sale than simulation, bought by an operations team rather than a design lead, and it is the instrument panel that any team would install before it would run a laboratory.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Revenue and engagement dashboards, and a designer's sense that something feels off. The gaps: no price index; no currency stock series; no measure of reward value erosion; no alerting on economic drift; and no attribution of drift to the change that caused it.
