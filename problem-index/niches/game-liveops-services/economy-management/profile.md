# Economy Management

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/game-liveops-services|Game LiveOps Services]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a currency economy with millions of participants in balance when it is tuned by hand and the drift that ruins it compounds invisibly for a quarter — and whoever makes the economy legible takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$750M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~25% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** Economy design leads
**Automation Potential:** High — simulation and monitoring

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A live game economy has faucets and sinks. Currency enters through events, rewards and purchases and leaves through crafting, upgrades and consumption. If the balance drifts the economy inflates: rewards stop meaning anything, purchases lose value, and the progression that structures the whole experience flattens. It is tuned in a spreadsheet against a system with millions of participants, and the failure takes a quarter to become visible in dashboards that were never built to show it.

### Contested sub-niches
This niche is not terminal. It names a domain, and beneath it are two separable businesses:
- [[niches/game-liveops-services/economy-simulation/profile|🎯 Economy Simulation]] — what the economy will do under a proposed change
- [[niches/game-liveops-services/inflation-detection/profile|🎯 Inflation Detection]] — what the running economy is doing now

## Current Tools & Gaps
A designer's spreadsheet, a remote config console, a revenue dashboard and an engagement chart. The gaps: no model of the economy as a system; no currency stock or price level reported anywhere; no forecast of a change's effect; no distributional view across the player base; and no alarm when the drift begins.
