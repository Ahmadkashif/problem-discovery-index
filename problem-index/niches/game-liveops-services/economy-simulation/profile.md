# Economy Simulation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/game-liveops-services|Game LiveOps Services]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell a designer what the economy will look like in three months if they ship a proposed change — and whoever makes that forecast trustworthy takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$450M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — nothing exists
**Target Buyer:** Economy design leads
**Automation Potential:** High — agent-based and stock-flow simulation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The contest here is prospective. A designer proposes a change — a new reward, a repriced sink, an event's currency grant — and wants to know what the currency stock, price level and progression distribution will look like in three months. Every input needed is in the telemetry and the simulation is feasible. It is sold before the decision ships, its value is entirely in avoiding a mistake, and it requires the designer to trust a model — which is a different product and a different sale from an instrument that reports what is already happening.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A spreadsheet projecting a few weeks linearly, and experience. The gaps: no behavioural response modelled; no population heterogeneity; no feedback between price and behaviour; no uncertainty on the projection; and no validation of past forecasts against what happened.
