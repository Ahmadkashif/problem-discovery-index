# Section 608 Technician Certification Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors|Plumbing & HVAC Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
EPA-approved organisations that train and test HVACR technicians for Clean Air Act Section 608 certification, which a buyer must show to purchase regulated refrigerant at a distributor's counter — ESCO Institute and Mainstream Engineering among them (recalled). Many distributors host proctored exams in their branches and check certification cards before each refrigerant sale. Technicians or their employers pay per exam; the bodies also sell training and A2L safe-handling courses.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Exam development and administration staff; psychometric or analytical work is a few people per body (estimate)
**Output:** Exams, certifications, certification look-up services, training materials
**Proprietary data:** Records of certified technicians and exam results
**Clock:** EPA's certification requirements and refrigerant transition training needs
**Buyer:** Technicians, contractor owners and distributor training managers
