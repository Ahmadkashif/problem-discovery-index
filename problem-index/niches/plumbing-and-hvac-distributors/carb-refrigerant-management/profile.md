# California Refrigerant Management Programme

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors|Plumbing & HVAC Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The California Air Resources Board's refrigerant staff write and enforce the state's HFC rules: GWP limits on new equipment that ran ahead of EPA's (a 750-GWP limit for new residential and light-commercial air conditioning from 1 January 2025, recalled), the Refrigerant Management Program's leak inspection, repair and reporting duties for large systems, and incentive programmes for low-GWP refrigeration. California distributors and their customers sell and service under both CARB's and EPA's rules.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 engineers, analysts and programme staff on the refrigerant rules (estimate)
**Output:** Regulations and their technical support documents, compliance determinations, leak-reporting data, enforcement
**Proprietary data:** Facility leak and service reports filed under the Refrigerant Management Program, plus market and emissions inventories
**Clock:** Statutory targets (California's 40% HFC reduction below 2013 levels by 2030, recalled) and the regulation's own effective dates
**Buyer:** None in the commercial sense; the programme is funded by the state
