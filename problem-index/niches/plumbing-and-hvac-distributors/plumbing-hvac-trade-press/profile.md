# Plumbing & HVAC Trade Press & Distributor Rankings

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors|Plumbing & HVAC Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Trade titles that cover plumbing and HVAC distribution and publish annual distributor rankings and market commentary — Supply House Times, The Wholesaler, ACHR News and Contracting Business (recalled), and Modern Distribution Management's HVACR top-distributor list, on which Johnstone Supply ranked second in 2026 (search summary). Advertising and events fund them; rankings rest on companies' self-reported revenue.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Editorial teams; research and ranking work is a few people per title (estimate)
**Output:** News, rankings, surveys and event content
**Proprietary data:** Self-reported distributor revenue and survey responses
**Clock:** Editorial and event calendars the publishers set
**Buyer:** Publisher or editorial director
