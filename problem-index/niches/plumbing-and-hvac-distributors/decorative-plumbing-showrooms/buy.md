# Showroom Selection Software Taught the Rough-In

**Niche:** [[niches/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors/decorative-plumbing-showrooms/profile|Decorative Plumbing & Kitchen-Bath Showrooms]]
**Industry:** [[industries/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors|Plumbing & HVAC Distributors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Selection and quoting tools present products beautifully; they don't know that this trim needs that valve.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #k-nearest-neighbors #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a homeowner's or designer's showroom selections into a complete, compatible order — rough valves matched to trims, finishes matched across brands, lead times known — that reaches the plumber's rough-in without a wrong or missing piece, and whoever delivers the most selection packages complete and compatible takes the account.

## The Problem
Showrooms that bought selection software use it to present and quote, then re-key selections into the ERP and check compatibility by hand from spec sheets.

## What Already Exists
Showroom quoting and selection tools, ERP quote modules, manufacturers' dealer portals and configurators, and general CRM for homeowner follow-up.

## The Customization Gap
Plumbing logic inside the selection: required rough-in parts added automatically, valve type (pressure-balance versus thermostatic) checked against trims, finishes matched across brands, lead times and discontinuations pulled live, the rough-in package split from the finish package and scheduled to the plumber's dates, and the homeowner's sign-off attached to each change. The ERP order has to be generated from the selection, not re-keyed.

## Target Customer
Distributors with showrooms that want to keep their current presentation tool.

## Impact If Solved
If re-keying and compatibility checks take a consultant ~1–2 hours per project (estimate), automating them lets each consultant carry ~20–30% more projects and cuts order errors at the source.
