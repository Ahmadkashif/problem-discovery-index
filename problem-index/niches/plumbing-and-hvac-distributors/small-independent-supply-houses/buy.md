# Buying-Group Commerce Fed With the Member's Own Stock

**Niche:** [[niches/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors/small-independent-supply-houses/profile|Small Independent Plumbing & HVAC Supply Houses]]
**Industry:** [[industries/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors|Plumbing & HVAC Distributors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Buying groups offer members a catalogue and a webstore template; without the member's live stock and contractor prices, contractors don't use it.
**Tags:** #data-integration #large-language-models #k-nearest-neighbors #automation #workflow-orchestration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stop a one- to three-branch supply house's contractors moving their daily orders to a national chain's app, and whoever keeps the largest share of those daily orders at the house takes the account.

## The Problem
Many small houses have a webstore from their buying group or ERP vendor that shows products and list prices. It doesn't show what's on the shelf today or what this plumber pays, so contractors keep calling or switch to an app that does.

## What Already Exists
Buying-group e-commerce programmes, ERP-vendor storefronts, B2B commerce SaaS and texting platforms such as Prokeep.

## The Customization Gap
A connector that syncs the member's stock, contract prices and order status from its ERP into the group's storefront and text channel nightly or hourly, maps the member's item codes to the group's catalogue, and handles the trade's units (feet of pipe, boxes of fittings, cylinders of refrigerant with certification checks). It must install without the member's IT department, because there isn't one.

## Target Customer
Buying groups serving plumbing and HVAC independents, and their members.

## Impact If Solved
If live stock and prices lift a member's online order share from a few percent toward ~15–25% (estimate), the owner and counter staff answer fewer routine calls and contractors have less reason to try a chain's app.
