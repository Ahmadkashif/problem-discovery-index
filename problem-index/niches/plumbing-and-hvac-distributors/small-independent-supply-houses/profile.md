# Small Independent Plumbing & HVAC Supply Houses

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors|Plumbing & HVAC Distributors]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stop a one- to three-branch supply house's contractors moving their daily orders to a national chain's app, and whoever keeps the largest share of those daily orders at the house takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$14.7B in 2022 receipts at 3,849 firm counts with fewer than 20 employees across the three codes (Census SUSB 2022)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7.6% of the three codes' ~$192.7B receipts (derived)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — an older ERP or DDI-type system, phone and text orders, a buying group's catalogue, and the owner's memory
**Target Buyer:** Owner-president, often also the purchasing manager and the best counter person
**Automation Potential:** Medium — order intake, lookups and status messages automate well; the owner's contractor relationships are the asset being defended

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Nearly three in four firms in the three codes have fewer than 20 employees, yet together they take under 8% of receipts. They survive on service and relationships with local plumbers and HVAC dealers, many through buying groups such as Affiliated Distributors or as Winsupply local companies (recalled). Their contractors now carry Ferguson's and Watsco's apps — Watsco's e-commerce reached 37% of its sales in the 12 months to June 2026 — and expect stock, price and order status at 6 a.m. without calling. AI vendors and integrators price for mid-market houses and reach these last. The contest is narrowed to one measure the owner can see, the share of contractors' daily orders kept; quoting and pricing tools compete for the same owner's attention, but a five-person house adopts one thing at a time. Larger independents' commercial desks are in [[niches/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors/plan-and-spec-equipment-selection/profile|Plan-and-Spec Equipment & Fixture Selection]]; the electrical and industrial counterparts are [[niches/electrical-distributors/small-independent-electrical-distributors/profile|Small Independent Electrical Distributors]] and [[niches/industrial-distributors/small-independent-distributors/profile|Small Independent Industrial Distributors]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Small-distributor ERPs (DDI System, Eclipse for some, older systems), buying-group e-commerce templates, Prokeep and plain texting, and Bluon's network for HVAC parts visibility. The gaps: no affordable contractor app on the house's own data, no integration budget, and no one to run it.
