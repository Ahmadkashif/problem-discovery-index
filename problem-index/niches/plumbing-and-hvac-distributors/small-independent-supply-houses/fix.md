# The Owner Who Answers Every Stock Question Himself

**Niche:** [[niches/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors/small-independent-supply-houses/profile|Small Independent Plumbing & HVAC Supply Houses]]
**Industry:** [[industries/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors|Plumbing & HVAC Distributors]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** At a small supply house the owner's cell phone is the inventory system, the price list and the order tracker.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #automation #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stop a one- to three-branch supply house's contractors moving their daily orders to a national chain's app, and whoever keeps the largest share of those daily orders at the house takes the account.

## The Problem
Plumbers text the owner at 6 a.m. and on Saturdays: is it in stock, what's my price, is my order on the truck. He answers from memory or drives to the branch to look. When he is on vacation, the contractors call someone else.

## Why It's Still Broken
The answers are in the ERP, but the ERP has no customer-facing channel, and texts arrive on a personal phone where nothing is logged.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Route the house's text line through an assistant that answers stock, price and order-status questions from the ERP, drafts orders for the counter to confirm, and passes anything unusual to the owner with the context attached. Log every question so the owner sees what contractors ask.

## Who Feels the Pain
Owners who never stop working, counter staff interrupted by routine questions, and contractors waiting on an answer.

## Impact If Fixed
If routine stock and status questions take ~1–2 hours of the owner's day (estimate), automating them returns most of that time and keeps the house answering at 6 a.m. when he can't.
