# A Contractor App That Runs on a Small House's Existing ERP

**Niche:** [[niches/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors/small-independent-supply-houses/profile|Small Independent Plumbing & HVAC Supply Houses]]
**Industry:** [[industries/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors|Plumbing & HVAC Distributors]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** The national chains spent hundreds of millions on contractor apps; a five-person supply house needs the same answers from its own ERP for a few hundred dollars a month.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #k-nearest-neighbors #data-integration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stop a one- to three-branch supply house's contractors moving their daily orders to a national chain's app, and whoever keeps the largest share of those daily orders at the house takes the account.

## The Problem
Watsco says it has invested more than $250M in technology over five years. A local plumbing supply house with one branch competes for the same plumbers with a phone, a text line and an ERP that has no customer-facing interface.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Small-distributor ERPs have thin or no APIs, product data is incomplete, and vendors can't afford integration projects for accounts this size.

## What to Build
A light app that reads the house's ERP (by API, database view or nightly export), answers contractors' questions by text or chat in plain language — "do you have a 50-gallon power-vent in stock," "what's my price on 3/4 copper," "is my order ready" — places orders into the ERP as drafts, and uses the buying group's catalogue data to fill product gaps. A demo can run on a sample DDI-style export.

## Target Customer
Owners of one- to three-branch plumbing and HVAC supply houses, sold through buying groups.

## Impact If Built
If a small house loses ~5–10% of its contractors' daily orders a year to chain apps (estimate), keeping half of that is worth ~$100,000+ a year at a $5M house, from software that needs no new staff.
