# PVF Package Pricing & Price Holds

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors|Plumbing & HVAC Distributors]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to hold a firm price on a mechanical contractor's pipe, valve and fitting package across a release schedule that runs for months while copper and PVC move, and whoever quotes the lowest price held firm through the job's last release takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$20B a year of pipe, valves and fittings sold to commercial plumbing and mechanical contractors on project terms (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of the three codes' ~$192.7B receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — price sheets arrive as files and feeds, but hold terms, release tracking and repricing decisions live in spreadsheets and the PVF manager's head
**Target Buyer:** PVF or commodity pricing manager; VP of commercial sales; CFO when margin leaks
**Automation Potential:** High — repricing to manufacturers' sheets and simulating margin over the release window are mechanical; negotiating price protection with manufacturers is not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The PVF half of a commercial package — copper tube and fittings, cast-iron soil pipe, PVC and CPVC, steel pipe, valves and hangers — is a commodity sale decided on price and terms. Contractors bid a job once and buy over twelve months, so they want the supply house to hold its price across releases. The house's costs move with copper (Section 232 tariffs of 50% on copper pipes, tubes and fittings from 1 August 2025, on full value from 6 April 2026) and with PVC pipe sheets, whose pricing is the subject of In re: PVC Pipe Antitrust Litigation over OPIS's "PVC & Pipe Weekly"; Atkore agreed in April 2026 to pay $72.5M to direct purchasers and $64M to non-converter sellers, subject to court approval. The electrical analogue is [[niches/electrical-distributors/wire-cable-copper-pricing/profile|Wire & Cable Pricing Against COMEX Copper]]; here the hold spans a whole job's releases and two commodities with different references. Waterworks pipe sold to utilities is a separate segment (see Filter Notes).

## Current Tools & Gaps
ERP price-sheet loads, manufacturers' price announcements, pricing tools such as Zilliant and Vendavo (recalled), COMEX data and spreadsheets. The gaps: hold terms are rarely recorded, released lines aren't priced against the hold, and no one simulates margin at release before agreeing to hold.
