# Price Optimisation Software Given a Release Schedule

**Niche:** [[niches/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors/pvf-package-price-holds/profile|PVF Package Pricing & Price Holds]]
**Industry:** [[industries/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors|Plumbing & HVAC Distributors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** B2B pricing tools set customer prices well for stock orders; they don't know a commercial PVF quote is a promise that runs for months.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #monte-carlo-methods #linear-regression #data-integration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to hold a firm price on a mechanical contractor's pipe, valve and fitting package across a release schedule that runs for months while copper and PVC move, and whoever quotes the lowest price held firm through the job's last release takes the account.

## The Problem
Distributors that bought price optimisation use it for counter and account pricing. Project PVF quotes are priced outside it, in spreadsheets, because the tool has no notion of a hold, a release schedule or a manufacturer's price-protection terms.

## What Already Exists
B2B price optimisation (Zilliant, Vendavo, PROS, Pricefx; recalled), ERP matrix pricing, manufacturers' price-sheet feeds.

## The Customization Gap
Project terms as objects: hold start and end, quantities by release, the commodities each line tracks (copper, PVC, iron, steel), manufacturers' price-protection windows on the house's own purchases, and the expected price-sheet changes over the hold. The tool should recommend a price and hold together, then reprice unreleased lines against the hold as sheets change and alert when a job's remaining releases go underwater.

## Target Customer
Distributors already running a pricing tool for stock business who want project PVF inside it.

## Impact If Solved
If project PVF is ~10% of a house's sales and runs ~1–2 points below its potential margin (estimate), bringing it into the pricing tool recovers that on every job without changing how contractors buy.
