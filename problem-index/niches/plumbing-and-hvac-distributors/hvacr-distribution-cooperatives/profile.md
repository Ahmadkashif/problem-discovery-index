# HVACR Distribution Cooperatives

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors|Plumbing & HVAC Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Member-owned distribution groups in which independent owners run local HVACR stores under a shared brand, buying through the group's central purchasing and distribution centres and sharing its catalogue, e-commerce and vendor programmes. Johnstone Supply, a member cooperative since 1981, is the model: more than $4B in combined revenue across its group, ranked #2 among HVACR distributors on MDM's 2026 list with ~470 locations, ~350 of them independently owned (search summary). Members pay through their purchases and dues; vendors pay to be preferred.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 category, vendor-programme and member-analytics staff at the central organisation (estimate)
**Output:** Vendor programme performance, member sales and margin benchmarks, assortment and replenishment guidance, rebate calculations
**Proprietary data:** Central purchasing and distribution-centre transactions to member stores, plus members' sell-through reports, across the whole group
**Clock:** Annual vendor negotiations, buying shows and rebate periods
**Buyer:** Cooperative CEO or head of member services
