# Refrigerant Reclaimers & Wholesalers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors|Plumbing & HVAC Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Companies that buy back recovered refrigerant, reclaim it to purity standards and sell reclaimed and virgin refrigerant to HVACR distributors and large end users, such as Hudson Technologies and A-Gas (recalled). Distributors run recovery-cylinder take-back for them at the counter. EPA's rule requiring reclaimed HFCs for servicing certain equipment from 1 January 2029 (search summary) and the AIM Act's falling allowances raise their importance.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Commercial, trading and plant staff; market analysis is done by a few people per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Refrigerant supply and pricing decisions, buy-back offers, compliance documentation for reclaimed product
**Proprietary data:** Recovered-refrigerant volumes and purity by source, and their own sales
**Clock:** AIM Act allowance step-downs and EPA's reclamation dates
**Buyer:** Head of refrigerant sales or trading
