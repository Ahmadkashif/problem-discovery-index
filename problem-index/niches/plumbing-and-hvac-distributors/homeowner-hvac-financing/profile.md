# Homeowner HVAC Financing Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors|Plumbing & HVAC Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Consumer lenders that fund homeowners' HVAC replacements at the kitchen table through dealer programmes, often promoted by manufacturers and distributors: Synchrony's home specialty cards, Service Finance Company (Truist), GoodLeap and Foundation Finance among them (recalled). They underwrite the homeowner, pay the dealer, and price promotional rates and dealer fees; distributors push the programmes because financed systems sell at higher efficiency tiers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 credit-risk, pricing and portfolio analysts on the home-improvement or HVAC programme at each lender (estimate)
**Output:** Underwriting models, dealer-fee and promotional-rate pricing, dealer and channel risk monitoring
**Proprietary data:** Their own loan books: applications, approvals, repayments and defaults by dealer, region and equipment value
**Clock:** Internal pricing and funding cycles; promotional calendars tied to manufacturers' seasons
**Buyer:** Head of home-improvement lending or chief credit officer for the programme

**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. A risk-bearer prices its insight into rates and dealer fees rather than invoicing it (Q1 2), and a handful of lenders dominate HVAC programmes (Q5 2). The contractor-side financing of material purchases, a different product, is [[niches/electrical-distributors/contractor-material-financing/profile|Contractor Material-Financing Platforms]] (26). The end of the federal 25C credit for systems placed in service after 31 December 2025 makes financing a larger part of the efficiency-tier sale, which raises the lenders' volume but not their score.
