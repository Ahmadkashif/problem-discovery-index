# Plan-and-Spec Equipment & Fixture Selection

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors|Plumbing & HVAC Distributors]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn an engineer's equipment and fixture schedules into a priced package of the supply house's own lines that helps the contractor win the bid and clears submittal, and whoever posts the most awarded packages approved on the first submittal takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1B a year of applications, quotation-desk labour and quoting software at US plumbing and HVAC distributors (estimate: ~8,000–12,000 commercial quotation and applications specialists plus tooling)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.5% of the three codes' receipts in cost (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — schedules are read on screen and selections run in OEM software, but the selection reasoning, accessories and submittal outcomes are kept by hand or not at all
**Target Buyer:** Commercial quotation or applications manager; VP of commercial sales
**Automation Potential:** High for schedule extraction and first-pass selection; medium for accessory and approval judgement

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is the pre-award contest for the equipment and fixture half of a commercial package, and it is this industry's own analogue of [[niches/electrical-distributors/bid-day-project-quoting/profile|Bid-Day Project Quoting & Takeoff]] and [[niches/building-material-dealers/plan-set-takeoff-quoting/profile|Plan-Set Takeoff & Lumber Package Quoting]]. What makes it different is cross-brand re-selection: the schedule names a competitor's model with performance at design conditions, and the house must find its own equivalent that meets sensible capacity, airflow, static pressure, electrical and efficiency, or a fixture equal that meets rim height, flush rate, carrier and lead-free rules, and then win the engineer's approval. The line with the shared vendor field follows the three distributor builds: schedule or list lines already matched to stocked SKUs for a straight resale quote go to [[niches/industrial-distributors/rfq-and-order-intake-automation/profile|RFQ & Order Intake Automation]]; re-selection against performance requirements from plans and specs belongs here. Applied equipment such as chillers is largely quoted by manufacturers' representatives (estimate), the queued manufacturers' representatives build.

## Current Tools & Gaps
OEM selection programs, fixture manufacturers' spec sheets, Eclipse quote modules, spreadsheets for comparison sheets, and Rebar's computer-vision HVAC quoting ($14M Series A, March 2026; plumbing next; hiring applied-AI engineers in New York). The gaps: selection runs are discarded, accessories depend on memory, and submittal responses never return to the distributor's records.
