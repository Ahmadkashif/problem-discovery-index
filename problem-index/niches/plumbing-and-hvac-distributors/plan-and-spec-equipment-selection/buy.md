# Blueprint AI Turned Toward the House's Own Lines

**Niche:** [[niches/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors/plan-and-spec-equipment-selection/profile|Plan-and-Spec Equipment & Fixture Selection]]
**Industry:** [[industries/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors|Plumbing & HVAC Distributors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** AI takeoff tools read drawings and count equipment; a supply house needs them to answer in its own brands, at its own prices, with the comparison sheet the engineer will read.
**Tags:** #cnns #object-detection #large-language-models #transformers #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn an engineer's equipment and fixture schedules into a priced package of the supply house's own lines that helps the contractor win the bid and clears submittal, and whoever posts the most awarded packages approved on the first submittal takes the account.

## The Problem
Computer-vision takeoff and blueprint AI can now find every mechanical schedule and count fixtures and units on a plan set. What a distributor receives is a list of the engineer's basis-of-design items; the specialist still re-selects, prices and builds the submittal.

## What Already Exists
Rebar's AI quoting for commercial HVAC suppliers (computer vision on blueprints, 40 clients at its March 2026 Series A), contractor-side takeoff tools, Parspec on the electrical and lighting side, and OEM selection programs.

## The Customization Gap
Distributor logic on top of reading: the house's carried brands and their performance data, cross-brand equivalence at design conditions, fixture equals under ADA, flow-rate and lead-free rules, accessory inference, project pricing requests to manufacturers, and comparison sheets in the format each engineer expects. It must also log every override, because the specialist's overrides are the house's selection knowledge.

## Target Customer
Distributors with commercial desks that would rather configure a vertical tool than build one.

## Impact If Solved
If a package drops from ~4–8 specialist hours to ~1–2 (estimate), a desk answers ~2–3 times the bids with the same staff, and the house keeps its override data rather than leaving it in a vendor's shared model.
