# AHRI Shipment Statistics Programme

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors|Plumbing & HVAC Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Air-Conditioning, Heating, and Refrigeration Institute collects monthly shipment data from member manufacturers for central air conditioners and heat pumps, furnaces, water heaters, boilers and other equipment, publishes headline releases and gives members detailed category data. Distributors, analysts and investors read the releases to judge the season; HARDI's separate monthly TRENDS survey of distributor sales is compiled for it by ITR Economics from voluntary member responses (search summary) and is too small to pass the gate on its own. AHRI's certification directory is a different function, profiled at [[niches/hvac-contractors/equipment-performance-certification/profile|HVAC Equipment Performance Certification]] (54, indexed).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–15 statistics, economics and data staff across the shipment programmes (estimate)
**Output:** Monthly shipment releases, member-only category statistics, historical series
**Proprietary data:** Member-submitted shipment data by product category that no outside party holds
**Clock:** Monthly reporting calendars the association sets
**Buyer:** Association president or head of statistics
