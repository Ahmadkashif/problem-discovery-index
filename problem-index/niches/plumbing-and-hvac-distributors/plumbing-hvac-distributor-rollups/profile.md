# Plumbing & HVAC Distributor Rollup Integration Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors|Plumbing & HVAC Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Serial acquirers that buy regional plumbing and HVAC distributors and integrate them. Watsco's "buy and build" strategy has added businesses such as Jackson Supply (~$230M of annualised sales across 25 Sunbelt locations, acquired in Q2 2026), and Ferguson spent $0.3B on eight acquisitions in 2025; private-equity platforms do the same in regional plumbing and PVF (recalled). Their corporate development and integration teams screen targets, model synergies and track performance after close.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 corporate development, pricing and integration analysts per acquirer (estimate)
**Output:** Target screens, valuation and synergy models, integration scorecards
**Proprietary data:** Acquired companies' customer, pricing and margin histories, plus the acquirer's integration track record across dozens of deals
**Clock:** Deal timetables, lender covenants and sponsor holding periods
**Buyer:** Head of corporate development or the acquirer's CFO
