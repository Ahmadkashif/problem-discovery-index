# PVC Resin & Pipe Price Assessments

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors|Plumbing & HVAC Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Price reporting services that assess PVC resin contract and spot prices and PVC pipe market conditions, and sell weekly reports and commentary to resin producers, pipe makers, distributors and large buyers. OPIS's "PVC & Pipe Weekly" is the best-known pipe-market report; OPIS is mainly a fuel and energy price reporter, part of Dow Jones's professional information business since News Corp bought it from S&P Global and IHS Markit for $1.15B in February 2022. ICIS, CDI and S&P Global assess resin (recalled). Plumbing distributors price PVC and CPVC off manufacturers' sheets that move with these markets.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–25 petrochemical and plastics reporters and analysts covering vinyls at each agency (estimate)
**Output:** Weekly resin and pipe-market assessments, commentary, short-term outlooks
**Proprietary data:** Reporter-collected market intelligence from producers, converters and buyers, with the assessment history behind each number
**Clock:** Weekly publication calendars the agencies set; some resin contracts reference the assessments
**Buyer:** Head of plastics or petrochemicals pricing, editorial director

**Kill switches:** none — the assessments are the agencies' own work product from voluntary market contributions. The antitrust exposure is a business risk, not one of the three kill-switch types: In re: PVC Pipe Antitrust Litigation (N.D. Ill. 24-cv-07639) alleges pipe makers shared confidential information by contributing to and reading OPIS's "PVC & Pipe Weekly", names OPIS as a defendant, and Atkore agreed in April 2026 to pay $72.5M and $64M to two purchaser classes, subject to court approval.
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. This tests the PVC price-volatility angle and is scored the same as [[niches/building-material-dealers/lumber-panel-price-assessments/profile|Lumber & Panel Price Assessments]] (41): no exchange prices PVC pipe or resin, so Q3 is 4, a point above [[niches/electrical-distributors/copper-premium-price-assessments/profile|Copper Premium & Scrap Price Assessments]] (38), whose base price is COMEX's. Q4 is 2 because the calendar is the agency's own, and Q5 is 2 because a handful of agencies make the market. OPIS belongs to the energy price-reporting family logged at [[niches/oil-gas-field-services/energy-price-reporting-crossref/profile|Energy Price Reporting Agencies]] (53) and indexed for metals at [[niches/metal-fabrication/metals-price-reporting-agencies/profile|Metals Price Reporting Agencies]] (51). Both carry Q4 5 from before the ruling that an agency's own publication calendar earns 2, which is logged for Stage E rather than copied here. Against the indexed 51, the gap is Q4 (2 against 5, six points), Q2 (3 against 4, since a vinyls desk is a small team inside the agency while those pockets count 100–500 staff) and Q5 (2 against 3). The litigation shows how much weight the pipe market put on one weekly report, which is also the reason the report's future form is uncertain.
