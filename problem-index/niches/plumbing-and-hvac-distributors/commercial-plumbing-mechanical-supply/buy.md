# Construction CRM Pointed at the Supply House's Bid Board

**Niche:** [[niches/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors/commercial-plumbing-mechanical-supply/profile|Commercial Plumbing & Mechanical Project Supply]]
**Industry:** [[industries/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors|Plumbing & HVAC Distributors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Construction CRMs and lead services track jobs well for contractors; a supply house needs the same board organised around which contractors are bidding and which half of the package it is quoting to each.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #k-nearest-neighbors #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to win the mechanical or plumbing contractor's material package on a commercial job, but contractors routinely split that package, and the two halves are won differently: the equipment and fixtures go to whoever re-selects the engineer's schedule in its own lines and gets the substitutions approved, and the pipe, valves and fittings go to whoever holds the lowest firm price across a release schedule that runs for months while copper and PVC move. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Outside and inside sales at a supply house track bids on whiteboards and spreadsheets: which jobs are out, which contractors are bidding them, who has the fixtures and who the PVF. Lead data shows the job and later the award, but not the supplier side.

## What Already Exists
Construction lead services and plan rooms (the vendors profiled at [[niches/electrical-contractors/construction-project-lead-data/profile|Construction Project Lead & Plan Data]]), general CRMs (Salesforce, HubSpot, Dynamics) and ERP opportunity modules.

## The Customization Gap
A distributor's bid board needs one job to many contractors to many quotes, split by package half (equipment, fixtures, PVF), with engineer and basis-of-design brands, the house's competing lines, bid date, submittal status, price-hold expiry and inferred award, fed from lead data and the house's own email. It also has to stay usable at 7 a.m. by a salesperson who will not type into it.

## Target Customer
Commercial sales managers at regional plumbing and HVAC houses without in-house CRM teams.

## Impact If Solved
If the board shows each salesperson the ~10–20 jobs a week worth quoting rather than everything on the plan room (estimate), quote desks spend their hours where the house can win, and managers see package-level hit rates for the first time.
