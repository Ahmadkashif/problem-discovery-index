# HVAC & Plumbing Manufacturers' Channel & Allocation Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors|Plumbing & HVAC Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The channel, sales-operations and allocation teams at HVAC and plumbing manufacturers — Carrier, Trane, Lennox, Daikin, Rheem, Bosch, Mitsubishi on the HVAC side and water-heater, fixture and valve makers on the plumbing side (recalled) — that analyse distributors' sell-through and inventory, set dealer and distributor programmes, and decide who gets scarce product. They decided allocation through the 2025 A2L launch, when Watsco alone converted more than $1B of inventory, and through the R-454B cylinder shortage. Their work serves the manufacturer's own sales and pricing; the manufacturers themselves are the queued HVAC equipment manufacturers and plumbing products makers builds.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–50 channel, pricing and supply-planning analysts per large manufacturer (estimate)
**Output:** Distributor allocation and fill decisions, programme and rebate design, sell-through and share analysis
**Proprietary data:** Distributors' point-of-sale and inventory reports under programme terms, warranty registrations and the manufacturer's own shipments
**Clock:** Internal programme, pricing and supply-planning cycles; regulatory transitions (the AIM Act's dates, DOE efficiency effective dates) and the cooling and heating seasons shape demand but set no due date on this team's work
**Buyer:** VP of distribution or channel sales
