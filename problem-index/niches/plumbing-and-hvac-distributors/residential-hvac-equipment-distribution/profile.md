# Residential HVAC Equipment Distribution to Dealers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors|Plumbing & HVAC Distributors]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to have the dealer's brand of complete, AHRI-matched system — now in R-454B or R-32 — on the branch shelf on the day a no-cool call becomes a replacement, and whoever keeps the highest in-stock rate on complete matched systems through the peak weeks takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$45B a year of residential and light-commercial unitary equipment, coils, furnaces and related supplies sold through HVAC distribution (estimate, from 423730 receipts of $80.6B in 2022 and Watsco's mix of 67% equipment)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~23% of the three codes' ~$192.7B receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — dealer apps and e-commerce are mature at the leaders (Watsco's e-commerce was 37% of sales in the 12 months to June 2026), but allocation, transfers and pre-season buys run on spreadsheets and judgement
**Target Buyer:** VP of sales or operations at an OEM-aligned distributor; branch and regional managers
**Automation Potential:** Medium — high for forecasting, transfers and matched-system checks; low for dealer relationships and allocation negotiation with the OEM

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is where most of the industry's HVAC money moves. Residential AC replacement is ~80–90% of unit sales (Watsco 10-K), and a dealer replaces a failed system the day the homeowner agrees, from whichever branch of its brand's distributor has the condenser, coil and furnace that make a certified match. Brands are sold through exclusive territories (Watsco's Carrier joint ventures were 53% of its 2025 revenue) or OEM-owned branches, so the contest is between brand channels for the dealer's loyalty, and the refrigerant transition made availability the deciding factor: the A2L launch touched ~55% of Watsco's products in 2025, R-454B service cylinders ran short in spring 2025 (search summary), and EPA lifted the R-410A installation deadline in May 2026. Seasonal buying and branch stocking sit inside this niche, because the stock is how the contest is won. The parts counter is [[niches/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors/hvac-parts-counter-will-call/profile|HVAC Repair Parts Counter & Will-Call]], the proposal step is [[niches/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors/matched-system-quoting-compliance/profile|Matched-System Quoting & Compliance Lookup]], and the dealer side is [[niches/hvac-contractors/residential-service-repair/profile|Residential Service & Repair]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
ERPs (Eclipse, Infor CSD), Watsco's HVAC Pro+ and OnCallAir, OEM dealer portals, AHRI's directory and inventory planning tools. The gaps: no measure of matched-system availability on peak days, no record of sales lost to a stock-out, and allocation decided by phone between OEM and distributor.
