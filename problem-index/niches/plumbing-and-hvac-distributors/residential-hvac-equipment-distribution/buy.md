# Dealer Portals Taught to Sell the Whole System

**Niche:** [[niches/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors/residential-hvac-equipment-distribution/profile|Residential HVAC Equipment Distribution to Dealers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors|Plumbing & HVAC Distributors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** B2B e-commerce platforms show stock by SKU; a dealer needs to know, in one screen, whether a complete, certified, region-compliant system is available today and where.
**Tags:** #k-nearest-neighbors #large-language-models #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to have the dealer's brand of complete, AHRI-matched system — now in R-454B or R-32 — on the branch shelf on the day a no-cool call becomes a replacement, and whoever keeps the highest in-stock rate on complete matched systems through the peak weeks takes the account.

## The Problem
Dealers order more and more from apps (Watsco's e-commerce reached 37% of sales), but most distributor storefronts are generic B2B catalogues. A dealer searching for a 3-ton heat pump sees condensers, then has to find the matching coil and air handler, check that the combination is certified at the efficiency the region requires and that the coil is rated for the refrigerant, then check stock at three branches.

## What Already Exists
B2B commerce platforms (Optimizely, Salesforce Commerce, Shopify B2B and ERP storefronts), OEM dealer portals, Watsco's own HVAC Pro+ apps and the AHRI directory.

## The Customization Gap
System-first search: AHRI matched combinations as the unit of sale, regional efficiency rules applied by ship-to, refrigerant compatibility, availability computed for the whole system across nearby branches, and substitutions offered within the same certified family when one component is out. It also needs the dealer's price, rebates and pre-season terms on the system, not the pieces.

## Target Customer
E-commerce and digital leaders at HVAC distributors without Watsco's in-house team.

## Impact If Solved
If dealers place ~20–30% more system orders online when the system is the unit of search (estimate), counter and phone load falls in the peak weeks and fewer orders ship with a missing component that brings the dealer back.
