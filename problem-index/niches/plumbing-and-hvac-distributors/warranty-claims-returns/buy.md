# Document AI Taught to File in Twelve Portals

**Niche:** [[niches/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors/warranty-claims-returns/profile|Warranty Claims & Defective-Part Returns]]
**Industry:** [[industries/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors|Plumbing & HVAC Distributors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Generic document AI reads a nameplate and a counter ticket; it doesn't know each manufacturer's portal, serial format or labour table.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #cnns #data-integration #automation #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn each warranty replacement handed across the counter into a claim the manufacturer pays on first submission, and whoever posts the highest first-pass paid-claim rate takes the account.

## The Problem
Distributors can buy extraction cheaply. What they need is the claim filed: the right fields in the right portal, with the right defect code and evidence, for a dozen manufacturers whose portals change each year.

## What Already Exists
Intelligent document processing platforms, robotic process automation, ERP return modules and the manufacturers' own portals.

## The Customization Gap
Per-manufacturer claim templates and serial decoders; registration lookups; labour allowance tables by model family; evidence requirements (nameplate photo, failed-part tag, invoice); portal automation where no API exists; and tracking of return-or-scrap instructions and credit ageing per claim.

## Target Customer
Distributors with in-house operations teams that prefer configuring automation tools to buying a niche product.

## Impact If Solved
If keying a claim takes ~10–15 minutes across portals (estimate), automated filing returns most of a warranty clerk's peak-season week and shortens the time to credit.
