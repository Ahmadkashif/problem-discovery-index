# HVAC Repair Parts Counter & Will-Call

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors|Plumbing & HVAC Distributors]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to hand a service technician the right replacement part — OEM or approved universal — from a model and serial number, a nameplate photo or a description while the homeowner waits, and whoever sends the most technicians back to the job with the right part on the first trip takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$25B a year of repair parts, controls, refrigerant and supplies sold over HVAC and refrigeration counters and will-call (estimate; Watsco's "other HVAC products" were 29% of its 2025 sales)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% of the three codes' ~$192.7B receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — apps take a growing share of orders, but identification still runs on manufacturers' parts sites, cross-reference books, texted photos and the counter veteran's memory
**Target Buyer:** Branch manager; VP of operations; parts and supplies category manager
**Automation Potential:** High for model lookup, cross-reference and will-call picking; medium for diagnosing what the technician actually needs

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Service technicians fix more than they replace, and every repair starts at a counter or a phone. The question is identification: a capacitor or motor for a unit whose nameplate is half gone, a board for a furnace whose model was superseded twice, a coil that matches a condenser installed before the refrigerant changed. The counter veteran answers from years of patterns; the alternatives are now on the technician's phone — Bluon's PartsConnect (April 2026) scans a model number against 30M+ model records from 240+ manufacturers and shows compatible parts at local distributors, and online sellers ship universal parts overnight. It is the HVAC counterpart of [[niches/electrical-distributors/branch-counter-sales/profile|Branch Counter Sales to Contractors]] and [[niches/industrial-distributors/breakdown-replacement-interchange/profile|Breakdown Replacement & Interchange]]: same shape, different catalogue and a refrigerant rulebook. The technician side is [[niches/hvac-contractors/diagnostic-troubleshooting/profile|Diagnostic Troubleshooting]], which finds the failed component, and [[niches/hvac-contractors/residential-service-repair/profile|Residential Service & Repair]], which runs the service call; this niche starts where they end, with the part handed across the counter, so they are not duplicates.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Manufacturers' parts sites and bills of material, universal cross-reference tools, Bluon, Prokeep text ordering, Watsco's HVAC Pro+ app, and Eclipse or Infor CSD at the point of sale. The gaps: no record of the request as asked, the photo, the alternatives considered or whether the part fixed the unit; and every new model year breaks the cross-references.
