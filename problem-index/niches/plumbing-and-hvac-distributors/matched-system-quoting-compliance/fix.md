# The Rebate Denied for a Non-Matching Coil

**Niche:** [[niches/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors/matched-system-quoting-compliance/profile|Matched-System Quoting & Compliance Lookup]]
**Industry:** [[industries/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors|Plumbing & HVAC Distributors]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A dealer installs a condenser with a coil that isn't in its certified match, and the homeowner's rebate is denied weeks later.
**Tags:** #k-nearest-neighbors #decision-trees #data-integration #compliance #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a dealer's replacement call into a priced proposal for a certified, compliant matched system — the right efficiency for the region, an A2L-rated coil and furnace, rebates checked — in one step, and whoever returns the most dealer proposals that need no correction takes the account.

## The Problem
The coil the dealer wanted was out, so the counter sold a near-equivalent. The system works, but the combination isn't in the AHRI directory at the claimed efficiency, the utility's rebate is rejected and the dealer eats the difference or argues with the homeowner.

## Why It's Still Broken
Counter substitutions are made on fit and availability; the match check happens, if at all, when the rebate paperwork is filed.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Check every system sale at the point of sale: when a dealer's order contains outdoor and indoor units, verify the combination against AHRI's directory and the job's rebate programme, and propose a certified alternative in stock before the order is released.

## Who Feels the Pain
Dealers absorbing denied rebates, homeowners waiting for money they were promised, and counter staff blamed for substitutions made to keep the dealer moving.

## Impact If Fixed
If a few hundred dollars to over a thousand rides on each rebate (estimate) and ~2–5% of rebated systems hit a match problem (estimate), a point-of-sale check saves dealers real money and makes the distributor the safe place to buy.
