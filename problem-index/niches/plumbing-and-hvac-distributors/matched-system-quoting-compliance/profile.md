# Matched-System Quoting & Compliance Lookup

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors|Plumbing & HVAC Distributors]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a dealer's replacement call into a priced proposal for a certified, compliant matched system — the right efficiency for the region, an A2L-rated coil and furnace, rebates checked — in one step, and whoever returns the most dealer proposals that need no correction takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$500M a year of inside-sales and dealer-support labour spent assembling and checking residential system quotes, plus proposal software (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.3% of the three codes' ~$192.7B receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — the AHRI directory, OEM dealer tools and Watsco's OnCallAir exist, but most quotes are still checked by an inside salesperson against lookups and rule sheets
**Target Buyer:** VP of dealer development or digital at an HVAC distributor; the dealer's owner as the end user
**Automation Potential:** Very High — every check is a lookup against published tables: AHRI matches, DOE regional efficiency rules, refrigerant compatibility, rebate and programme lists

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A residential replacement is sold as a system, and every system has to be a certified match whose rating the homeowner's rebate, the inspector and the regional efficiency rule will accept. Since 2023, split-system ACs sold for installation in the South and Southwest have had to meet higher SEER2 minimums than in the North (recalled); since 2025, A2L systems need coils and furnaces rated for the refrigerant; the federal 25C credit of up to $2,000 ended for systems placed in service after 31 December 2025, while state and utility programmes continue. The work is mechanical and repetitive, which is why it automates: Watsco added more than 10,000 A2L SKUs with "regulatory match-ups" to its platform in 2025, and SetSale raised a $2M seed in March 2026 to bring live pricing, incentives and financing into dealers' proposals. The certification directory itself is the indexed [[niches/hvac-contractors/equipment-performance-certification/profile|HVAC Equipment Performance Certification]] pocket; the dealer's sales process is [[niches/hvac-contractors/residential-service-repair/profile|Residential Service & Repair]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
AHRI's directory, OEM dealer quoting tools, Watsco's OnCallAir and HVAC Pro+, field-service pricebooks (ServiceTitan, Profit Rhino), utility rebate lists. The gaps: checks run one at a time by people, regional and refrigerant rules are applied from memory, and rebate eligibility is confirmed after the sale.
