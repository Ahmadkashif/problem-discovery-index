# Proposal Software Fed With the Distributor's Matches and Rules

**Niche:** [[niches/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors/matched-system-quoting-compliance/profile|Matched-System Quoting & Compliance Lookup]]
**Industry:** [[industries/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors|Plumbing & HVAC Distributors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Dealer proposal tools present options to homeowners well; their system data is only as good as the matches, prices and rules fed into them.
**Tags:** #data-integration #large-language-models #k-nearest-neighbors #automation #compliance #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a dealer's replacement call into a priced proposal for a certified, compliant matched system — the right efficiency for the region, an A2L-rated coil and furnace, rebates checked — in one step, and whoever returns the most dealer proposals that need no correction takes the account.

## The Problem
Dealers present replacements on tablets through proposal and pricebook tools. Those tools carry the dealer's prices, but their equipment lists are loaded by hand or from one brand, so a proposal can show a combination that isn't certified, isn't compliant in the region or isn't in stock.

## What Already Exists
Watsco's OnCallAir, field-service pricebooks and proposal modules (ServiceTitan, Profit Rhino), SetSale's AI sales tool, OEM dealer tools.

## The Customization Gap
A distributor feed into those tools: the house's carried AHRI matches with current stock and the dealer's cost, regional efficiency and refrigerant rules applied by job ZIP, state and utility rebate eligibility by match, and automatic updates when catalogues turn over, as they did across ~55% of Watsco's products in the A2L year.

## Target Customer
Distributors without Watsco's in-house platform, and proposal-tool vendors seeking distributor data.

## Impact If Solved
If ~5–10% of dealer proposals now carry a combination that later has to be changed (estimate), a live feed removes most of those corrections and the call-backs and denied rebates they cause.
