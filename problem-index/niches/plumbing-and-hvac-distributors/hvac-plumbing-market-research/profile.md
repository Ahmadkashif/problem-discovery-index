# HVAC & Plumbing Market Research Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/plumbing-and-hvac-distributors|Plumbing & HVAC Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Research firms that size and forecast North American markets for HVAC equipment, water heaters, boilers, controls and plumbing products, with competitor shares and route-to-market analysis, and sell the studies to manufacturers, distributors and investors. BRG Building Solutions publishes studies such as North America Commercial HVAC and North America Commercial Boilers and Water Heaters (~$20,500–$21,000 a single-user licence, search summary); BSRIA and Interact Analysis cover HVAC globally (recalled).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 analysts per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Market studies with volume forecasts, competitor shares and distribution-channel analysis; custom studies
**Proprietary data:** Interview programmes with manufacturers, distributors and contractors over years, alongside public AHRI shipment releases and Census data
**Clock:** Annual study calendars the firms set and clients' planning cycles; regulatory transitions create demand but don't set deadlines
**Buyer:** Managing director or head of research
