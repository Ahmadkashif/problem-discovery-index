# Concussion Assessment & Return-to-Play Tools

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/youth-sports-orgs|Youth Sports Organizations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Supply the baseline and post-injury cognitive assessments and return-to-play protocols schools and clubs use to decide when a concussed child may play again — a decision now governed by statute in every state.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 neuropsychologists, psychometricians, clinical researchers, and product staff
**Output:** Baseline and post-injury assessments, normative comparisons, return-to-play protocol support, validity research, symptom tracking
**Proprietary data:** Millions of paired baseline and post-injury assessments across a youth population, with recovery trajectories
**Clock:** Statutory removal-from-play and clearance requirements
**Buyer:** Chief Science Officer / VP of Clinical Research
