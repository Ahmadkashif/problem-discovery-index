# Recruiting & Athlete Exposure Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/youth-sports-orgs|Youth Sports Organizations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator/rollup

## What They Do
Host athlete profiles, highlight video and performance data, and connect families to college programmes — selling the promise of exposure to a population whose realistic recruitment odds are very low and rarely stated.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 data scientists, video engineers, recruiting analysts, and product staff
**Output:** Athlete profiles and video hosting, performance and measurables databases, recruiting match and interest analytics, college programme intelligence
**Proprietary data:** Millions of athletes with measurables, video, performance history and — crucially — where they actually ended up
**Clock:** Recruiting calendars and signing periods
**Buyer:** VP of Data Science / Chief Product Officer
