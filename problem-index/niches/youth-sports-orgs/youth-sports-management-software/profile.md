# Youth Sports Management Platform Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/youth-sports-orgs|Youth Sports Organizations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Supply the registration, scheduling, payment, communication and roster systems leagues and clubs run on, and analyse the resulting participation and payment activity.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 data engineers, analysts, and product researchers
**Output:** Registration and payment processing analytics, participation trend reporting, scheduling optimisation, retention and churn analysis, published participation studies
**Proprietary data:** Registration and participation records for millions of children across leagues, sports and years, with fees paid and years played
**Clock:** Season registration windows
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Head of Insights
