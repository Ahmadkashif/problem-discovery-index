# National Governing Bodies & Sanctioning Organizations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/youth-sports-orgs|Youth Sports Organizations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Sanction competition, write playing rules and age and eligibility standards, certify coaches and officials, and set the safety requirements affiliated clubs must follow.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 rules, coaching education, and membership staff per body
**Output:** Playing rules and rule change decisions, coach and official certification, age and eligibility standards, safety requirements, membership reporting
**Proprietary data:** Membership and sanctioning records, plus rule change deliberation history
**Clock:** Annual rule cycles and season sanctioning
**Buyer:** Executive Director / Director of Sport Development
