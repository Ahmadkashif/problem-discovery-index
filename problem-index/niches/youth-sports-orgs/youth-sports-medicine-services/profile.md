# Youth Sports Medicine & Athletic Training Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/youth-sports-orgs|Youth Sports Organizations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Provide athletic training coverage, injury assessment and rehabilitation to schools, clubs and events — the clinical layer that sees youth sports injuries as they happen.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 athletic trainers, sports medicine physicians, and outcomes staff across the hospital and provider networks
**Output:** Sideline assessment and injury management, return-to-play clearance, rehabilitation programmes, injury prevention screening, outreach reporting to schools
**Proprietary data:** Injury encounters joined to sport, mechanism, treatment and return-to-play outcome
**Clock:** Season schedules and clearance requirements
**Buyer:** Director of Sports Medicine Outreach
