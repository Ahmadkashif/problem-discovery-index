# Athletic Equipment Safety Standards & Certification

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/youth-sports-orgs|Youth Sports Organizations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Write the performance standards protective equipment must meet, certify products against them, and administer the reconditioning and recertification regimes that determine whether a helmet may still be worn.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 test engineers, biomechanists, standards staff, and certification reviewers
**Output:** Performance standards, product certification, test protocols, reconditioning standards, research on impact and injury mechanisms
**Proprietary data:** Decades of laboratory impact test results across products and generations
**Clock:** Certification cycles and reconditioning seasons
**Buyer:** Executive Director / Technical Director
