# Youth Sports Insurance & Abuse Liability Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/youth-sports-orgs|Youth Sports Organizations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Underwrite the accident, general liability and sexual abuse and molestation coverages leagues, clubs and facilities must carry, and defend the claims — a market that has been repriced dramatically by abuse litigation and by the collapse of capacity for that coverage.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 underwriters, actuaries, claims counsel, and risk consultants
**Output:** Programme pricing and eligibility, screening and supervision requirements as conditions of cover, claims defence, loss control guidance, risk transfer structuring
**Proprietary data:** Injury and abuse claims joined to sport, age group, supervision policy, screening practice and facility — the only cross-organisation record of what actually goes wrong in youth sport
**Clock:** Annual renewals aligned to season start
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
