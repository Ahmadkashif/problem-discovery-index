# Athlete Safety & Abuse Prevention Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/youth-sports-orgs|Youth Sports Organizations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Set and enforce the athlete safety policies national governing bodies and their affiliated clubs must follow — training requirements, one-on-one contact rules, reporting obligations — investigate allegations, and maintain the ineligibility database that bars a banned individual from coaching elsewhere.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 investigators, policy staff, trainers, and case analysts
**Output:** Policy standards and audits, investigations and sanction decisions, centralised ineligibility database, mandatory training curricula, prevalence research
**Proprietary data:** Investigation records and sanction outcomes across sports — the only centralised record of substantiated abuse in organised youth sport
**Clock:** Statutory reporting obligations and case timelines
**Buyer:** Chief Executive / Director of Investigations

**Kill switches:** **federal statutory mandate and appropriation** — the body exists by statute and depends on congressionally directed funding, and its jurisdiction covers only the governing body system, not the wider club and recreational sector where most children play.
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold and kill-switched. Holds the only centralised record of substantiated abuse in organised youth sport, covering a minority of the participation it would need to cover to matter.
