# Background Screening Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/youth-sports-orgs|Youth Sports Organizations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Screen the coaches, officials and volunteers who will be alone with children — criminal history, sex offender registries, identity verification and continuous monitoring — at the volume a largely volunteer, annually turning-over workforce generates.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-2,000 court researchers, verification specialists, compliance analysts, and data scientists
**Output:** Criminal record reports, registry checks, identity verification, continuous monitoring and re-screening, adjudication guidance
**Proprietary data:** Court record acquisition infrastructure across thousands of jurisdictions, plus accumulated adjudication decisions
**Clock:** Season start dates and annual registration deadlines
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Compliance
