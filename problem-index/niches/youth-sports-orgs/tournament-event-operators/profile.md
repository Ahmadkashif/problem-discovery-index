# Tournament Operators & Sports Tourism Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/youth-sports-orgs|Youth Sports Organizations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator/rollup

## What They Do
Run the travel tournament circuits that now dominate competitive youth sport, and produce the economic impact analyses cities use to justify building complexes for them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 event analysts, economists, and operations staff
**Output:** Event scheduling and capacity planning, team and participation forecasting, economic impact studies, facility utilisation analysis, destination reporting
**Proprietary data:** Team registration, travel and spend data across circuits, joined to destination and facility
**Clock:** Season calendars and facility booking cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Analytics / Director of Research
