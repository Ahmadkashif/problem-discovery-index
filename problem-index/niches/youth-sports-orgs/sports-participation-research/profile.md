# Sports Participation Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/youth-sports-orgs|Youth Sports Organizations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Survey households on youth sports participation, cost, drop-out and access, and publish the statistics the field's policy and philanthropic conversation is conducted in.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 researchers and analysts
**Output:** Participation rate reporting by sport, age and income, cost of play studies, drop-out and access research, policy recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Consistently fielded household survey series over many years
**Clock:** Annual publication cycle
**Buyer:** Research Director
