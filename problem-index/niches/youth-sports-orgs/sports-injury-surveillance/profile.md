# Sports Injury Surveillance Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/youth-sports-orgs|Youth Sports Organizations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Collect injury reports from athletic trainers and programmes into surveillance datasets, and publish the injury rates by sport, position and mechanism that drive rule changes and safety policy.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 epidemiologists, athletic trainers, and data staff
**Output:** Injury rate reporting by sport and mechanism, exposure-adjusted incidence, rule change evaluation, safety recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Injury reports with exposure denominators — the rare surveillance system that counts participation hours as well as injuries
**Clock:** Season cycles and annual reporting
**Buyer:** Research Director
