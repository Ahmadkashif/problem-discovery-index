# Transit Project Management Oversight Contractors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/heavy-civil-contractors|Heavy Civil Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Under 49 U.S.C. 5327, the Federal Transit Administration hires project management oversight contractors (PMOCs) to oversee grantees' major capital projects: new rail and bus rapid transit lines and extensions, many built by heavy civil contractors. PMOCs review grantees' cost estimates, schedules and risk (FTA Oversight Procedure 40 sets the risk and contingency review), sit in on progress and report to FTA. A 2025 FTA proposal would raise the oversight threshold to $300M total cost with $100M federal, and estimated that oversight would end on 49 of 88 current projects (search summary; final status not confirmed).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–50 cost, schedule and risk specialists per PMOC firm on FTA work (estimate), but each team is assigned to one grantee's project and writes that project's reviews, so the work is per-project production; no cross-project research function of 10+ was found
**Output:** One project's risk and contingency reviews, cost and schedule assessments and monthly oversight reports
**Proprietary data:** Project records owned by the grantees; reports owned by FTA
**Clock:** FTA's Capital Investment Grant milestones and the client agency's review calendar
**Buyer:** PMOC programme manager at the consultancy; FTA's oversight office on the client side
