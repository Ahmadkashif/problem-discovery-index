# Large Heavy Civil Contractors' Estimating & Data Groups

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/heavy-civil-contractors|Heavy Civil Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The 59 enterprises with 500+ employees in the code, which took ~$14.4B of its 2022 receipts (Census SUSB), include national heavy civil and marine firms. Several run central estimating, cost-engineering and data groups across districts (Kiewit, which also owns the InEight software business, is the best-known example; recalled). Acquisitive marine firms are consolidating too: Saltchuk bought Great Lakes Dredge & Dock in April 2026, and Orion closed its J.E. McAmis acquisition in February 2026 (both via search summary). These groups pool production rates, bid histories and equipment costs across hundreds of jobs and use them to bid.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 cost engineers, data analysts and estimating-systems staff at the largest firms, excluding project estimators (estimate)
**Output:** Production-rate libraries, bid reviews, risk and contingency models, acquisition screening
**Proprietary data:** Bid items paired with field actuals across many districts and years, the industry's richest production record
**Clock:** Owners' bid dates and quarterly reporting
**Buyer:** Chief estimator or VP of estimating and operations analytics

**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. They hold exactly the bid-versus-actual record the high-impact problem describes, and use it to win work rather than sell it. Scored against [[niches/utility-contractors/utility-contractor-rollup-analytics/profile|Utility Contractor Rollup Analytics]] (40): the same shape, with Q2 one lower because project estimators are production headcount and the analytical core is small. The general-contractor analogue, [[niches/general-contractors/gc-preconstruction-estimating/profile|General Contractor Preconstruction & Estimating]] (48, no switch), scores higher on three points that don't carry over. On Q1 (4 there, 2 here), GC preconstruction is often sold to owners as a fee-based service before a GMP; heavy civil estimating is a cost of bidding on low-bid public work. On Q4 (5 there, 3 here), the owner's bid date binds the project estimators who submit the bid, not the central data group whose rate libraries and reviews run on the firm's own calendar. On size (50–500 there), that pocket counts estimators, which is production headcount under the later ruling; that 48 is logged as pre-ruling.
