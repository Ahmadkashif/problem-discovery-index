# Lenders' Technical Advisers on Infrastructure P3s

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/heavy-civil-contractors|Heavy Civil Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Engineering and cost consultancies hired by lenders, and sometimes by public owners, on public-private partnerships and privately financed infrastructure: ports, rail, transit, water. They review the design-build contractor's price, schedule and risk before financial close, then certify progress each month so construction draws can be released (recalled; no firm list checked). Their reports are what the lenders pay for, and the contractor's schedule and cost data is what they analyse.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 technical advisers per firm on infrastructure finance work (estimate), but each team certifies one project's monthly draw, so the work is per-project production; no cross-project research function of 10+ was found
**Output:** One project's due-diligence report, monthly monitoring reports and drawdown certificates
**Proprietary data:** Project cost and schedule records, owned by the project company and confidential to the lenders
**Clock:** Monthly drawdowns and milestone tests set in the client's credit agreement
**Buyer:** Head of infrastructure advisory
