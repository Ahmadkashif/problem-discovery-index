# Marine Contractors' Hull, P&I & Jones Act Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/heavy-civil-contractors|Heavy Civil Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Specialist marine insurers, syndicates and mutual clubs underwrite the cover that dredging and marine construction contractors must carry on dredges, crane barges, tugs and scows: hull and machinery, protection and indemnity, and Jones Act crew liability (recalled; no market list checked). They price fleets on loss history, crew practice and the work's exposure. The ocean-going versions of the same functions are profiled at [[niches/ocean-shipping-carriers/hull-war-risk-underwriters/profile|Hull, Machinery & War Risk Underwriters]] (fails gate) and [[niches/ocean-shipping-carriers/pi-club-loss-prevention/profile|P&I Clubs' Loss Prevention & Claims Analytics]] (35, kill switch). Contractor surety, which every federal job above $150,000 also requires under the Miller Act, is profiled at [[niches/utility-contractors/utility-contractor-surety-underwriting/profile|Utility Contractor Surety & Liability Underwriting]] (39) and [[niches/painting-contractors/contractor-surety-underwriting/profile|Contractor Surety & Trade Credit Underwriting]] (44). It is the same underwriting function and is not profiled a third time.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Pricing and actuarial staff dedicated to brown-water and marine-construction fleets plausibly under 10 per market (estimate); underwriters pricing each fleet and claims analysts handling each case are production and not counted
**Output:** Fleet pricing, survey requirements, claims analysis, loss-control guidance
**Proprietary data:** Loss histories on marine construction fleets and crew injury claims
**Clock:** Annual renewals and vessel survey cycles
**Buyer:** Head of marine underwriting
