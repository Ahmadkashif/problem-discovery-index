# Public Bid Tabulation Data Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/heavy-civil-contractors|Heavy Civil Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms that collect owners' published bid tabulations and sell them as searchable unit-price histories. Oman Systems (Nashville, since 1992) builds Bid Tabs Professional on DOT bid data, refreshed every two weeks, used by WSDOT, INDOT and MoDOT and sold to contractors and consultants (search summary). Edgevanta pitches a bid-tab agent over 20+ states (startup directory, search summary). Both sit mainly on DOT data, the queued highway contractors build's territory. Pre-bid project and plan data, which also carries bid results, is the larger and already-profiled business at [[niches/electrical-contractors/construction-project-lead-data/profile|Construction Project Lead & Plan Data]] (53, indexed).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analysts per firm; the rest is data collection and software (estimate)
**Output:** Bid-tab databases, item price reports, estimating add-ins
**Proprietary data:** Cleaned and coded copies of public tabs
**Clock:** Owners' letting calendars
**Buyer:** Founder or product head
