# Dredging & Navigation Channel Contracts

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/heavy-civil-contractors|Heavy Civil Contractors]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to have a DQM-certified dredge of the right type mobilised near the work when the Corps advertises, at a price under 125% of the government estimate, and whoever books the most working dredge-days a year at awardable prices takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3B a year of U.S. dredging (estimate: the federal bid market averaged ~$2.28B a year in 2023–2025 per the Great Lakes Dredge & Dock 10-K, ~$586M capital, ~$605M coastal protection and ~$1,086M maintenance, plus port, state and private work; DCA counted nearly $2.1B of Corps awards in FY2023, search summary)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium-High on the dredge (DQM sensors report daily to the Corps' Mobile District office, search summary), Low in bidding and pay reconciliation
**Target Buyer:** VP of estimating or chief operating officer at a dredging contractor
**Automation Potential:** High for bid analytics, pay-volume reconciliation and production advice; low for operating the plant

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Dredging is a fleet business sold one letting at a time. The Corps advertises maintenance, capital deepening and beach-nourishment contracts through its districts. The low responsive bid wins, but only if it is no more than 125% of the Corps' estimate (33 U.S.C. 624(a)(2)), and DCA reports that 126 of 135 comparable FY2023 projects (93%) were won below the Government Estimate Awardable Range (search summary). Hopper, cutter suction and mechanical dredges suit different material and distance, and moving a dredge from the Gulf to the Atlantic costs weeks and millions (estimate). Foreign fleets are barred. Fifty-six companies won Corps dredging work in FY2023 (21 large, 34 small, per DCA via search summary), but three hold ~56% of the market (GLDD 10-K). Pay comes from before-and-after surveys with allowable overdepth (UFGS-35 20 23). Saltchuk took the largest dredger, Great Lakes Dredge & Dock, private in April 2026 (search summary).

## Current Tools & Gaps
Hydrographic survey software, DQM monitoring systems, estimating spreadsheets built around the contractor's own dredge cost models, and the Corps' Navigation Data Center reports (advertising schedule, five-year contracted schedule, bid abstracts, past contracts awarded). The Corps estimates with CEDEP, which is not released. The gaps: no modelled view of where the government estimate will land, fleet scheduling across lettings done in heads, and pay volume reconciled by hand.
