# Heavy Civil Estimating & Field Software Data Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/heavy-civil-contractors|Heavy Civil Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The vendors whose systems hold heavy civil estimates and field cost codes: HCSS (HeavyBid, HeavyJob; "thousands of contractors", 400+ staff, per its careers page), Trimble (B2W since 2022, search summary) and InEight. Their product and data teams see production assumptions and actuals across their customer bases and use them in product features and benchmarks.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 product analytics and data staff per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** In-product analytics, benchmark reports, AI features
**Proprietary data:** Estimate items, crew and equipment assumptions, and cost-code actuals across thousands of contractors
**Clock:** Release cycles the vendors set
**Buyer:** VP of product or chief technology officer
