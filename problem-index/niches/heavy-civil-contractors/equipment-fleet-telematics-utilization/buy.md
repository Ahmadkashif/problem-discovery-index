# Fleet Platforms Taught Floating Plant

**Niche:** [[niches/heavy-civil-contractors/equipment-fleet-telematics-utilization/profile|Equipment Fleet Telematics & Utilisation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/heavy-civil-contractors|Heavy Civil Contractors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Mixed-fleet platforms track yellow iron; marine contractors also run dredges, crane barges, tugs and scows whose hours, fuel and inspections live in vessel logs.
**Tags:** #data-integration #time-series-forecasting #survival-analysis #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn mixed-OEM telematics into equipment hours posted to job cost codes and hourly rates estimators bid with, and the vendor whose posted hours and rates match audited job cost most closely takes the account.

## The Problem
A marine contractor's most expensive assets are floating, with Coast Guard inspection and documentation cycles, and none report through AEMP feeds.

## What Already Exists
Construction fleet platforms, OEM portals, vessel maintenance software, and the Corps' DQM data on certified dredges.

## The Customization Gap
Vessel engine and generator hours from onboard systems, DQM feeds for dredges, Coast Guard inspection and drydock schedules, and the same cost-code posting and rate logic as the land fleet.

## Target Customer
Equipment and marine operations managers at marine and dredging contractors.

## Impact If Solved
If floating plant joins the same hour and rate system (estimate: the ~30–50% of fleet value that is floating at marine firms), the largest assets finally get real utilisation and cost numbers.
