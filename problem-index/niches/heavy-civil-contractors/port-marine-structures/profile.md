# Port, Wharf & Marine Structures

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/heavy-civil-contractors|Heavy Civil Contractors]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to drive, cap and deck wharves, piers and bulkheads while the port keeps ships working at the neighbouring berths, and whoever hands back each berth with the fewest days of closure takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$5B a year of wharf, pier, bulkhead, breakwater, jetty and terminal civil work by 237990 contractors (estimate; Orion Group's Marine segment alone reported ~$545M of 2025 revenue and a $19.4B marine opportunity pipeline, per its earnings call via search summary)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — design is in BIM and pile records are digital at large ports, but production, pile acceptance and berth handover are tracked on paper and spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** VP of marine operations or chief estimator at a marine contractor
**Automation Potential:** Medium for pile-driving records, sequencing and handover tracking; low for the work

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Port work is built around an operating terminal. The owner is a port authority or terminal operator whose revenue comes from vessels alongside, so the contract is phased berth by berth with liquidated damages tied to handover. The spreads are crane barges, pile-driving rigs and dive teams. The risks are driving refusal and pile damage in variable soils, tide-limited concrete work, and ship movements. Delivery is often design-build or CM-at-risk, and funding often includes the $2.25B Port Infrastructure Development Program created under the IIJA (search summary). The contest is berth availability, not price per cubic yard, which separates it from dredging.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Pile-driving analysers and dynamic testing (PDA-type records), BIM coordination at large ports, P6 schedules, field apps. The gaps: no link from pile logs and installation data to the schedule and berth-handover plan, and no learning from driving records across jobs.
