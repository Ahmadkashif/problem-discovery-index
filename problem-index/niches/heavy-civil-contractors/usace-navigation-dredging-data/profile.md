# USACE Navigation, Dredging & DQM Data Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/heavy-civil-contractors|Heavy Civil Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Corps of Engineers runs the data programmes that dredging and waterway contractors work against. The Navigation and Civil Works Decision Support Center keeps the Dredging Information System and publishes the dredging advertising schedule, five-year contracted schedule, bid abstracts and past contracts awarded, alongside waterborne commerce statistics. The Dredging Quality Management office at Mobile District receives daily sensor data from every DQM-certified pipeline, hopper and scow plant on Corps contracts, which has been required on private pipeline dredge contracts since 2018 (search summary). Corps cost engineers estimate dredging with CEDEP, which is not released outside government.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Tens of statisticians, analysts and DQM specialists across the programmes (estimate)
**Output:** Dredging and navigation statistics, bid and award reports, DQM compliance monitoring, cost-estimating support to districts
**Proprietary data:** The full lifecycle record of Corps dredging (DIS) and continuous sensor data from certified dredges nationwide
**Clock:** The agency's own reporting and advertising calendars and its contract-set daily DQM feeds; no outside deadline governs the work
**Buyer:** None external; programme managers inside the Corps
