**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #compliance #workflow-orchestration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to close a complete set of conforming subcontractor and supplier quotes, with the owner's DBE or small-business participation documented, before the bid deadline, and whoever has the lowest conforming quote plugged on every scope when the deadline hits takes the account.

## The Problem
Under USDOT's interim final rule, recipients may not set contract goals or count DBE participation until their certification programme has re-evaluated every DBE. A contractor bidding in five states faces five timetables and changing directories.

## What Already Exists
Certified-payroll and DBE compliance platforms, state DBE directories, and the owner's bid documents.

## The Customization Gap
A per-owner status of whether contract goals are in force, live certification status per firm from each state's directory, the bid-form requirements per owner, and a Corps small-business subcontracting plan track for federal work.

## Target Customer
DBE and compliance officers at contractors bidding USDOT-funded transit, rail and port work in several states.

## Impact If Solved
If no bid is rejected for a participation defect (estimate: one or two at risk a year at a multi-state firm), the contractor protects its most expensive bids.
