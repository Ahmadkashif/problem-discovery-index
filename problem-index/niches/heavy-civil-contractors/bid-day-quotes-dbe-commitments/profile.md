# Bid-Day Subcontractor Quotes & DBE Commitments

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/heavy-civil-contractors|Heavy Civil Contractors]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to close a complete set of conforming subcontractor and supplier quotes, with the owner's DBE or small-business participation documented, before the bid deadline, and whoever has the lowest conforming quote plugged on every scope when the deadline hits takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M a year of estimating and compliance labour spent assembling quotes and participation paperwork (estimate), governing ~$12B of subcontract and material spend in the code's bids (estimate: ~30–35% of receipts)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1% in cost (estimate); decides the price of about a third of every bid
**Digital Adoption:** Low — quotes arrive by email, PDF, phone and text in the final hours; the quote sheet is a spreadsheet or the estimating system's quote module filled by hand
**Target Buyer:** Chief estimator; DBE or small-business compliance officer at larger firms
**Automation Potential:** High for quote intake, scope levelling and participation paperwork; low for the final plug decision

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A heavy civil bid prices the contractor's own crews from production rates and everything else from quotes: rebar, ready-mix, stone, steel piles, trucking, surveying, environmental controls, specialty subs. Most quotes arrive on bid day, many with exclusions, and the estimator must level them by scope and plug the lowest conforming one in time. On USDOT-funded transit, rail and port work the bid must also meet the DBE contract goal or document good-faith efforts. USDOT's interim final rule of 3 October 2025 removed the race and sex presumptions, required every DBE to be re-evaluated, and barred recipients from setting contract goals or counting participation until their state's certification programme finished. So the rules changed mid-stream state by state. On Corps work the parallel duty is the large-business subcontracting plan. The production-rate side of the estimate is the high-impact problem; this niche is the quote side.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Estimating systems' quote modules (HeavyBid, B2W), bid-invitation platforms, state DBE directories, LCPtracker-type compliance tools, and Trunk Tools' TrunkBid for levelling (beta since March 2026, search summary). GC-side DBE tracking after award is at [[niches/general-contractors/government-public-works/profile|Government & Public Works Contractors]]. The gaps: quotes re-keyed under deadline, exclusions missed, and DBE status checked against directories that are being rebuilt.
