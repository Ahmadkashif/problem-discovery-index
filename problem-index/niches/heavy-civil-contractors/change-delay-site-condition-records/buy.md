**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn each field event into a notice inside the contract's clock and a time impact analysis the owner accepts, and whoever gets the most time and money granted at the change-order stage, before it becomes a claim, takes the account.

## The Problem
The Corps' UFGS-01 32 01.00 10 wants AACE 52R-06 prospective analyses on the last approved schedule, activities of 20 workdays or less, float owned by the project, and SDEF or .xer files. Other owners want different methods.

## What Already Exists
Oracle Primavera P6, schedule-quality checkers, and scheduling consultants.

## The Customization Gap
Each spec parsed into machine-checkable rules; fragnet templates per event type (high water, environmental window, track outage, owner-furnished item); automatic copy of the right baseline; and output in the owner's format with the narrative drafted. The general-contractor scheduling function is at [[niches/general-contractors/project-scheduling-management/profile|Project Scheduling & Management]].

## Target Customer
Schedulers and project-controls managers at firms working for several public owners.

## Impact If Solved
If a TIA takes ~1 day instead of ~3–5 (estimate), a scheduler covering four jobs keeps up with every event inside the notice clock.
