# Heavy Civil Trade Associations & Standards Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/heavy-civil-contractors|Heavy Civil Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Dredging Contractors of America (which commissions an annual U.S. Dredge Report; FY2023 counted 56 firms winning nearly $2.1B of Corps dredging, via search summary), the American Railway Engineering and Maintenance-of-Way Association (publisher of the railway engineering manual), the Association of State Dam Safety Officials, the Deep Foundations Institute and the transportation builders' associations (recalled, except DCA's report). They lobby, publish standards and run conferences. Construction-wide association economics is at [[niches/general-contractors/agc-industry-economics/profile|Construction Association Economics]] (38).

## Insight Function

**Output:** Market reports, manuals and standards, conference programmes
**Proprietary data:** Member surveys and committee work
**Clock:** Annual reports and conference calendars the associations set
**Buyer:** Executive director
