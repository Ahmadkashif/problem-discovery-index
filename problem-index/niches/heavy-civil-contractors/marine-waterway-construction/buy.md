# Weather and Water Windows Built Into the Schedule

**Niche:** [[niches/heavy-civil-contractors/marine-waterway-construction/profile|Marine, Port & Waterway Construction]]
**Industry:** [[industries/heavy-civil-contractors|Heavy Civil Contractors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** CPM scheduling software treats a workday as a workday; marine work needs schedules that know tides, wave heights, river stages and environmental dredging windows.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #monte-carlo-methods #probability-distributions #optimization-fundamentals #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is "every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver work in and over water inside the owner's navigation and environmental windows," but that fight splits three ways. Dredging is won by having a certified dredge near the work and pricing under the Corps' 125%-of-estimate ceiling. Port and marine structures are won by crane-barge and pile-driving spreads that keep berths working. Locks, dams and levees are won by pricing in-the-wet cofferdam and river-stage risk on federal megaprojects. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
A breakwater job can only place stone below a wave height threshold, a beach nourishment only outside turtle season, and a lock cofferdam only below a river stage. Schedulers model this with calendars typed by hand and contingency added by feel.

## What Already Exists
Primavera P6 and its peers, schedule risk tools that run Monte Carlo on durations, and public NOAA and USGS wave, tide and river-stage data.

## The Customization Gap
Calendars generated from historical metocean and river-stage records for the site, activity-level operability thresholds by spread, environmental windows from the permit, and a risk run that samples real weather sequences rather than triangular durations. Output goes in the owner's schedule format (P6 .xer or the Corps' SDEF).

## Target Customer
Schedulers and project managers at marine and waterway contractors bidding exposed-water work.

## Impact If Solved
If weather contingency is set from site data rather than by feel (estimate: ~5–10 days on a season-long job), bids get tighter where the water is kind and safer where it isn't. Weather-day claims also come with evidence attached.
