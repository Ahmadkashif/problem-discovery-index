# DOT Bid-Tab Tools Taught Corps, Port and Transit Items

**Niche:** [[niches/heavy-civil-contractors/public-bid-tab-unit-price-intelligence/profile|Public Bid Tab & Unit-Price Intelligence]]
**Industry:** [[industries/heavy-civil-contractors|Heavy Civil Contractors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** DOT bid-tab products cover highway items well; a marine, dredging or rail contractor needs Corps districts, port authorities and transit agencies mapped into the same history.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #word-embeddings #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn the bid results of Corps districts, port authorities, transit agencies and railroad owners, plus DOT marine, structures and rail items, into a clean, item-mapped unit-price history the estimator trusts on bid day, and whoever holds the largest correctly mapped item history across those owners takes the account.

## The Problem
Oman Systems' products are built on DOT data, refreshed every couple of weeks (search summary). A dredging or port contractor's comparables are Corps abstracts and authority bid results, which no product maps (none found).

## What Already Exists
Bid Tabs Professional and similar DOT bid-tab software, the Corps' Navigation Data Center dredging reports, SAM.gov awards.

## The Customization Gap
Collectors for Corps district bid abstracts and NDC reports, port and transit bid results; item mapping for dredging (by dredge type, material and placement), marine structures and trackwork; and the Corps' 125% awardability rule built into the analysis.

## Target Customer
Estimators at marine, dredging and rail contractors.

## Impact If Solved
If assembling comparables for a non-highway bid drops from ~1–2 estimator-days (estimate) to under an hour, a firm bidding ~50 such jobs a year (estimate) saves ~50–100 estimator-days and prices from evidence on the bids where it currently guesses.
