# Takeoff Taught Field and Course Sections

**Niche:** [[niches/heavy-civil-contractors/sports-field-golf-park-builders/profile|Sports Field, Golf Course & Park Builders]]
**Industry:** [[industries/heavy-civil-contractors|Heavy Civil Contractors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Earthwork takeoff tools compute cut and fill; a field builder also needs drainage trench lengths, layered stone and root-zone volumes, and turf and infill quantities from the same plans.
**Tags:** #object-detection #cnns #large-language-models #numerical-methods #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to hand over a laser-graded, drained field or course that passes the owner's grade, drainage and playability tests before a season opener the owner won't move, and whoever opens on the promised date takes the account.

## The Problem
Field bids come in packages of plans with typical sections for drainage, base and surfacing. The estimator measures each layer by hand.

## What Already Exists
On-screen takeoff and earthwork software, machine-control surface models.

## The Customization Gap
Section templates for natural turf, synthetic turf, tracks and golf greens; layer quantities from surfaces and sections; drainage network lengths and slopes; and a check against the owner's performance specs.

## Target Customer
Estimators at sports-field and golf-course builders.

## Impact If Solved
If a field takeoff takes ~2 hours instead of ~1–2 days (estimate), a small firm bids more of the season's lettings.
