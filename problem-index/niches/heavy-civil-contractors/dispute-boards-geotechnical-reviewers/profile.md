# Dispute Boards & Geotechnical Baseline Reviewers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/heavy-civil-contractors|Heavy Civil Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Standing dispute review boards, usually three experienced engineers or contractors appointed jointly by owner and contractor on large tunnel, dam and transit jobs, hear disputes during construction and issue recommendations. Geotechnical consultants write and review the geotechnical baseline reports that allocate subsurface risk on those jobs (recalled). Formal delay and quantum analysis for claims is the privileged practice at [[niches/general-contractors/construction-claims-forensics/profile|Construction Claims & Schedule Forensics]] (51, kill switch).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Three-person boards and individual reviewers; no standing team of 10 (estimate)
**Output:** Board recommendations, baseline reports and reviews
**Proprietary data:** None beyond members' experience
**Clock:** Board meeting schedules and dispute referrals
**Buyer:** Board chair
