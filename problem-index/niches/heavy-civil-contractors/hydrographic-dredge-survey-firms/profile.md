# Hydrographic & Dredge Survey Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/heavy-civil-contractors|Heavy Civil Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Survey firms run multibeam and single-beam sounding boats for dredging contractors, port authorities and the Corps' districts. They take the before, progress and after surveys that set pay volumes under UFGS-35 20 23, and the condition surveys behind maintenance dredging (recalled). Construction staking on land is at [[niches/land-surveyors/construction-staking/profile|Construction Staking]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** Survey crews and processors; analysts who interpret rather than produce surveys number under 10 per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Processed surveys, volume computations, charts
**Proprietary data:** Survey archives owned by the clients who commissioned them
**Clock:** Contract survey windows and dredging schedules
**Buyer:** Survey firm principal
