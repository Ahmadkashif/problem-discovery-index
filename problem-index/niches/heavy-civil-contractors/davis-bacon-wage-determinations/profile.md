# Davis-Bacon Wage Surveys & Determinations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/heavy-civil-contractors|Heavy Civil Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Department of Labor's Wage and Hour Division surveys contractors' wages by county and construction type and publishes the general wage determinations on SAM.gov that set minimum pay on federal and federally assisted construction (recalled). There are four types: building, residential, highway and heavy. Dredging is treated as heavy work (a California determination lists "Heavy (Heavy and Dredging)"; search summary), and a project spanning types may carry several determinations. Heavy civil contractors build their certified payroll and bid labour rates on them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Tens of wage analysts and survey staff (estimate)
**Output:** General and project wage determinations, survey results, conformance rulings
**Proprietary data:** Survey submissions of contractor wage rates by craft, county and construction type
**Clock:** Survey cycles and modification schedules WHD sets itself
**Buyer:** None external
