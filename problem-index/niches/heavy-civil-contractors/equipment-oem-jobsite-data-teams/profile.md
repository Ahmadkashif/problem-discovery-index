# Equipment OEM Connected-Fleet & Jobsite Data Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/heavy-civil-contractors|Heavy Civil Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The connected-fleet and jobsite-technology groups at construction equipment makers and machine-control suppliers (Caterpillar, Komatsu, Deere, Volvo, Trimble, Topcon and peers; recalled) analyse telematics, machine-control and productivity data from customers' machines. They sell fleet portals, productivity services and parts and service programmes, and publish AEMP 2.0 feeds for mixed-fleet tools. Their analysis supports machine sales, dealer service and subscriptions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–100 data scientists and solution analysts per large OEM (estimate)
**Output:** Fleet and productivity dashboards, predictive-maintenance alerts, jobsite productivity services
**Proprietary data:** Engine, hydraulic, location and machine-control data from their installed base
**Clock:** Product release cycles and dealer service programmes, all internal
**Buyer:** VP of digital or connected solutions
