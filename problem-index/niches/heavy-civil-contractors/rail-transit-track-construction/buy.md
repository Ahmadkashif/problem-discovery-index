# Field Apps Taught Roadway-Worker Protection

**Niche:** [[niches/heavy-civil-contractors/rail-transit-track-construction/profile|Railroad & Transit Track Construction]]
**Industry:** [[industries/heavy-civil-contractors|Heavy Civil Contractors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Construction safety and daily-report apps capture toolbox talks and crews; rail work also needs on-track safety briefings, track authorities and flagging records each railroad audits.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #compliance #worker-facing #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to finish new track, turnouts and yard work inside the outage windows the host railroad or transit agency grants, and whoever returns track to service on time in the largest share of granted windows takes the account.

## The Problem
Each railroad has its own contractor safety rules, qualification cards and briefing forms. Crews fill them on paper and the contractor's safety manager collects them weekly.

## What Already Exists
Construction safety apps, daily-report tools (mapped at [[niches/construction-tech-platforms/daily-report-automation/profile|Daily Report & Field Capture]]), and railroads' own contractor-orientation programmes.

## The Customization Gap
Railroad-specific briefing templates, track-authority and flagging details per work group, worker qualification checks against each railroad's list, and an audit-ready record per window.

## Target Customer
Safety managers and rail superintendents at contractors working for several railroads.

## Impact If Solved
If briefing and authority records are complete for every window (estimate: from ~80% to near 100%), the contractor protects its railroad qualification, which is the licence to bid there at all.
