# Dam Safety Inspection & Risk Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/heavy-civil-contractors|Heavy Civil Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Engineering firms' dam safety practices act as FERC-approved independent consultants for hydropower licensees and as dam safety engineers for Corps, Bureau of Reclamation, state and private owners (recalled; firm names not checked). Under 18 CFR Part 12 Subpart D, each covered development's periodic inspection or comprehensive assessment must be completed and filed within five years of the due date of the previous Part 12D report, and comprehensive assessments recur within ten years (18 CFR 12.40). The January 2022 rule set up that alternating cycle, FERC approves each consultant team, and a firm may not serve for more than two consecutive Part 12D inspections of the same project (both via search summary). Their findings drive the rehabilitation work that heavy civil contractors then bid, mapped at [[niches/heavy-civil-contractors/locks-dams-levees/profile|Locks, Dams & Levee Construction]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** Dam safety engineers at the larger practices number ~10–60 per firm (estimate), but each team inspects one dam and writes that dam's report, so the work is per-dam production; no cross-dam research or risk function of 10+ was found
**Output:** One Part 12D report, failure-mode analysis or risk analysis per dam
**Proprietary data:** Inspection and risk-analysis histories, owned dam by dam by the licensees
**Clock:** The licensee's Part 12D filing, due within five years of the previous report's due date, inside a window the licensee schedules
**Buyer:** Dam safety practice leader
