# Federal Behavioral Health Statistics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/behavioral-health-clinics|Behavioral Health Clinics]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
SAMHSA's Center for Behavioral Health Statistics and Quality runs the National Survey on Drug Use and Health and the treatment episode datasets that underpin nearly all national behavioral health policy. Statisticians and survey methodologists design instruments, manage a very large field operation, and publish the results freely.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-150 statisticians, methodologists, and analysts, plus contractors
**Output:** National survey estimates, treatment episode data, published reports
**Proprietary data:** The national survey series itself, with decades of comparable measurement
**Clock:** Congressionally mandated reporting and annual publication schedules
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — a federal centre director
