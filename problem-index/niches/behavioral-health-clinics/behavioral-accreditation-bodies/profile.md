# Behavioral Health Accreditation Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/behavioral-health-clinics|Behavioral Health Clinics]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
CARF International and the Joint Commission's behavioral health programme write the standards that behavioral organizations are surveyed against, conduct the surveys, and issue accreditation that payers and states require for participation. Standards development and survey findings analysis are continuous research functions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-120 standards staff, surveyors, and quality analysts
**Output:** Accreditation standards, survey findings, aggregate quality reports
**Proprietary data:** Survey findings across thousands of behavioral programmes nationally, refreshed on a three-year cycle
**Clock:** Three-year accreditation cycles and standards revision schedules
**Buyer:** VP of Standards / Chief Accreditation Officer
