# Psychological Assessment Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/behavioral-health-clinics|Behavioral Health Clinics]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Pearson Clinical Assessment, PAR, MHS, and WPS develop, norm, and publish the instruments behavioral health runs on — the Wechsler scales, the MMPI, the BASC, the Conners, and hundreds of narrower measures. Psychometricians design items, run national standardization samples, establish reliability and validity, and build the norm tables. Clinicians pay per administration, and a test without current norms is clinically indefensible.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 psychometricians, research scientists, and clinical content staff
**Output:** The instrument, its norms, and the technical manual — the product in full
**Proprietary data:** National standardization samples accumulated across decades, each costing millions to collect and impossible to reconstruct retrospectively
**Clock:** Restandardization cycles, and diagnostic nomenclature revisions that force instrument updates
**Buyer:** VP of Research / Chief Psychometrician
