# Behavioral Health EHR Clinical Content Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/behavioral-health-clinics|Behavioral Health Clinics]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The clinical content groups at Netsmart, Qualifacts, SimplePractice, and Valant build the note templates, assessment libraries, treatment plan content, and state reporting logic that behavioral clinics work inside. Content is bundled into the subscription.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 clinical content specialists, regulatory analysts, and product staff
**Output:** Note templates, assessment libraries, state reporting configurations
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated state-by-state reporting requirement knowledge and template usage patterns across thousands of clinics
**Clock:** State reporting rule changes and software release cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Clinical Content / Chief Product Officer
