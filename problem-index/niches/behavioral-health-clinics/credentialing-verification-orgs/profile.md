# Credentialing Verification Organizations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/behavioral-health-clinics|Behavioral Health Clinics]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Medallion, Verifiable, symplr, and the traditional CVOs perform primary source verification of clinician licences, education, malpractice history, and sanctions, and assemble the credentialing file payers require before a clinician can bill. Behavioral health is the segment where this hurts most, because clinician turnover is high and every payer enrollment restarts the clock.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-400 verification specialists, analysts, and credentialing staff
**Output:** Verified credentialing files and enrollment submissions
**Proprietary data:** Verification history across hundreds of thousands of clinicians and every payer's idiosyncratic requirements, plus the record of what causes rejections
**Clock:** Payer enrollment queues, NCQA recredentialing cycles, and clinician start dates
**Buyer:** VP of Credentialing Operations / Chief Operating Officer
