# Behavioral Health Actuarial & Disparity Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/behavioral-health-clinics|Behavioral Health Clinics]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Milliman's healthcare practice, RTI, and comparable firms produce the commissioned studies that shape behavioral health policy and payment — network adequacy analyses, out-of-network utilization disparity research, and rate adequacy studies. Payers, foundations, and government agencies commission the work, and the report is the deliverable.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 actuaries, health economists, and research analysts
**Output:** Commissioned research reports and actuarial analyses
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated methodology and prior analyses; the underlying claims data is licensed or client-supplied rather than owned
**Clock:** Client deadlines, regulatory filing dates, and legislative sessions
**Buyer:** Principal / Practice Leader
