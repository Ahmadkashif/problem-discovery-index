# Mental Health Parity Compliance Analysis

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/behavioral-health-clinics|Behavioral Health Clinics]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Federal parity law requires health plans to produce written comparative analyses demonstrating that non-quantitative treatment limitations on behavioral benefits are no more restrictive than those on medical benefits. Consultancies and internal compliance teams assemble these documents — a substantial written analytical artefact that regulators demand on request and reject frequently.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 compliance analysts, clinical policy staff, and attorneys
**Output:** The NQTL comparative analysis — a document produced on statutory demand
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated analyses across plans plus the record of which framings regulators accepted or rejected
**Clock:** Statutory — regulators can demand the analysis and require a response within a fixed window
**Buyer:** Chief Compliance Officer / VP Regulatory Affairs
