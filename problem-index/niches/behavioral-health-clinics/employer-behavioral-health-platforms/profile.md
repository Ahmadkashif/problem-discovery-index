# Employer Behavioral Health Platform Outcomes Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/behavioral-health-clinics|Behavioral Health Clinics]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Spring Health, Lyra, Headway, and Alma contract with employers and health plans to deliver behavioral care through curated clinician networks. They compete on demonstrated outcomes, so their clinical outcomes and health economics teams are central rather than peripheral: the annual outcomes and ROI report is what the employer renews against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 outcomes researchers, health economists, and data scientists
**Output:** Outcomes and ROI reports, published effectiveness research, network quality analytics
**Proprietary data:** Measurement-based care trajectories — repeated symptom scale scores across hundreds of thousands of episodes — joined to claims, utilization, and clinician-level outcomes
**Clock:** Employer plan year renewals and RFP cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Medical Officer / VP Clinical Outcomes
