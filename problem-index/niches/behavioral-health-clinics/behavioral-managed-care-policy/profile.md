# Behavioral Managed Care Clinical Policy Units

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/behavioral-health-clinics|Behavioral Health Clinics]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The clinical policy and analytics groups at Carelon Behavioral Health, Magellan, and Optum Behavioral Health. They write the medical necessity criteria applied to authorizations, run network adequacy and access analytics, and produce the parity and quality reporting that regulators and health plan clients require.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 clinical policy staff, analysts, and outcomes researchers
**Output:** Medical necessity criteria, network and access analyses, regulatory reporting
**Proprietary data:** Behavioral claims and authorization data across tens of millions of covered lives, joined to network and outcome measures
**Clock:** Parity reporting obligations, state contract deliverables, NCQA accreditation cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Medical Officer / VP Clinical Policy
