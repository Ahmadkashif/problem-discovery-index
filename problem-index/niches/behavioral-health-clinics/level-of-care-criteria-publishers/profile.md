# Level-of-Care Criteria Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/behavioral-health-clinics|Behavioral Health Clinics]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The ASAM Criteria for addiction treatment and the LOCUS/CALOCUS family define what level of care a patient qualifies for. Several states mandate their use, so the criteria determine reimbursement for a large share of behavioral treatment. Small editorial and research teams maintain them and license them to payers and providers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 clinical editors, researchers, and implementation staff
**Output:** The criteria publication and its licensed implementation tooling
**Proprietary data:** Criteria development history and the accumulated record of implementation and inter-rater reliability studies
**Clock:** Edition revision cycles and state adoption mandates
**Buyer:** Director of Criteria Development / Chief Clinical Officer
