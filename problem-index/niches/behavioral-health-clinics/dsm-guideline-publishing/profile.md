# Diagnostic Nomenclature & Practice Guideline Publishing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/behavioral-health-clinics|Behavioral Health Clinics]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The American Psychiatric Association publishes the DSM and the practice guidelines that define diagnosis and treatment standards across the field. Staff coordinate expert workgroups, run field trials, manage evidence review, and produce publications that every clinician, payer, and researcher in behavioral health depends on.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 staff coordinating large volunteer expert workgroups
**Output:** Diagnostic manuals and clinical practice guidelines — genuine publishing revenue
**Proprietary data:** Field trial data, revision deliberation records, and decades of nomenclature development history
**Clock:** Revision cycles measured in years, largely self-determined
**Buyer:** Director of Publishing / Chief of Practice Guidelines
