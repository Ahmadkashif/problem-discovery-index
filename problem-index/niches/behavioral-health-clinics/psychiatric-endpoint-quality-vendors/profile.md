# Psychiatric Trial Endpoint Quality Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/behavioral-health-clinics|Behavioral Health Clinics]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Signant Health, WCG, and Cronos train and monitor the clinicians who administer psychiatric rating scales in drug trials, and analyze the resulting scores for rater drift, scoring anomalies, and inflated baselines. Because CNS trials succeed or fail on rating quality rather than on drug effect alone, sponsors buy endpoint reliability as a distinct service. This sits on the pharmaceutical research side of psychiatry rather than in the clinic, and is logged here because the vault has no closer home for it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 clinical scientists, rater trainers, psychometricians, and surveillance analysts
**Output:** Rater certification, scoring surveillance reports, endpoint reliability analyses — the billable deliverable
**Proprietary data:** Rater performance and scoring behaviour across thousands of trials and tens of thousands of raters, which no sponsor or CRO can assemble across programmes
**Clock:** Database lock and interim analysis deadlines, among the hardest in any industry
**Buyer:** VP of Scientific Services / Chief Medical Officer
