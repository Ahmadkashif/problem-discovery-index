# HACCP & Food Safety Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/food-manufacturing|Food Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Consultancies build and validate the hazard analysis plans, environmental monitoring programmes, and preventive controls documentation that federal food safety rules require, and prepare plants for regulatory and customer audits. The written plan is the deliverable and the plant operates under it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 food safety scientists and consultants
**Output:** Hazard analyses, preventive controls plans, environmental monitoring programmes, audit preparation
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated plan design and the record of which approaches auditors and regulators accepted — held under client confidentiality
**Clock:** Regulatory compliance dates and customer audit schedules
**Buyer:** Practice leader / Managing Director
