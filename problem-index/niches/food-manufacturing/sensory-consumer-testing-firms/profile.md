# Sensory & Consumer Product Testing Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/food-manufacturing|Food Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Curion, Sensory Spectrum, and the product testing practices run trained descriptive panels and consumer tests that determine whether a reformulation is detectable, whether a product beats its benchmark, and whether a cost reduction will be noticed. The test result decides whether a launch or a formulation change proceeds.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 sensory scientists, panel managers, and research analysts
**Output:** Descriptive panel results, consumer test findings, benchmark comparisons
**Proprietary data:** Trained panel calibration maintained over years, plus normative sensory and consumer data across product categories — expensive to build and impossible to acquire
**Clock:** Product launch gates and formulation decision deadlines
**Buyer:** Chief Science Officer / VP of Sensory Research
