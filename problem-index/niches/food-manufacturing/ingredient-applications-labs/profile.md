# Ingredient Applications Laboratories

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/food-manufacturing|Food Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The applications and flavour creation laboratories at Givaudan, IFF, Kerry, and ADM develop the formulations manufacturers actually launch — matching a target profile, solving a stability or processing problem, replacing a removed ingredient. The development work is the reason a manufacturer buys from one supplier over another, and it is delivered attached to ingredient sales.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-3,000 flavourists, food scientists, and applications technologists at the largest houses
**Output:** Formulations, prototypes, applications guidance, sensory support
**Proprietary data:** Flavour and formulation libraries built over decades, and the record of which formulations succeeded in market — among the most guarded assets in the food industry
**Clock:** Customer launch calendars and reformulation deadlines
**Buyer:** Chief Science Officer / VP of Applications
