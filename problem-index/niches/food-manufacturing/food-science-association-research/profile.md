# Food Science Association Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/food-manufacturing|Food Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Institute of Food Technologists and the industry science bodies publish the journals and technical guidance the profession works from, convene the expert panels that produce consensus positions on emerging safety and labelling questions, and run the certification the field hires against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 technical staff, editors, and programme managers
**Output:** Journals and technical guidance, expert consensus reports, certification
**Proprietary data:** Publication archive and expert panel deliberation records
**Clock:** Publication and conference calendars
**Buyer:** Chief Science Officer / VP of Science and Policy
