# Thermal Process Authorities

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/food-manufacturing|Food Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Federal rules require that thermal processes for low-acid canned and acidified foods be established by a recognized process authority. A small set of organizations and consultants run the heat penetration studies and issue the scheduled process letter — a document without which the product cannot legally be produced, and which is filed with the agency.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 thermal process engineers, microbiologists, and study staff per organization
**Output:** Scheduled process determinations, heat penetration study reports, deviation evaluations
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated thermal process studies across product formulations, container geometries, and equipment types — decades of validated processes no individual manufacturer holds
**Clock:** A product cannot be produced without the letter, and a process deviation stops the line pending evaluation
**Buyer:** Director of Process Authority Services
