# Retailer Private Label Development

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/food-manufacturing|Food Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Retailer own-brand teams specify products, qualify co-manufacturers, and run the benchmarking and cutting sessions that decide whether a private label item matches or beats the national brand it competes with.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 product developers, quality specialists, and sourcing staff per large retailer
**Output:** Product specifications, supplier qualification, benchmark and cutting evaluations
**Proprietary data:** Comparative product evaluations against national brands across categories, plus supplier capability and performance history
**Clock:** Range review and launch calendars
**Buyer:** VP of Own Brand / Director of Product Development
