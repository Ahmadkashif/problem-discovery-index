# Ingredient Regulatory & Specification Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/food-manufacturing|Food Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Providers maintain the ingredient databases food developers work from — regulatory status by jurisdiction, permitted use levels, allergen and claim implications, functional properties, and supplier specifications — normalized across sources that publish inconsistently.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 food scientists, regulatory analysts, and data staff
**Output:** Ingredient regulatory status, use level and claim data, specification normalization
**Proprietary data:** Curated regulatory status across jurisdictions and ingredient functional data assembled from sources that do not agree
**Clock:** Regulatory changes and product development cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Content / Director of Regulatory Data
