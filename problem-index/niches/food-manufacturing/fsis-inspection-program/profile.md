# Federal Meat & Poultry Inspection

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/food-manufacturing|Food Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Federal inspectors are present in every meat and poultry establishment in the country, verifying process control continuously and issuing the noncompliance records and enforcement actions that determine whether a plant keeps operating.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5,000-9,000 inspectors, with several hundred in data analysis and policy
**Output:** Inspection findings, noncompliance records, sampling results, enforcement actions
**Proprietary data:** Continuous inspection and sampling data across every regulated establishment — the most complete food safety dataset in existence
**Clock:** Continuous inspection presence and statutory reporting
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer
