# Food Machine Vision Inspection Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/food-manufacturing|Food Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Key Technology, TOMRA, and the food vision vendors build the optical sorting and inspection systems that grade product at line speed — attacking directly the defining bottleneck the Pass 1 analysis describes, where human inspectors evaluate hundreds of units per minute and accuracy degrades measurably over a shift.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 vision engineers, food technologists, and applications staff
**Output:** Inspection and sorting systems, defect classification models, applications configuration
**Proprietary data:** Defect image and classification data across product types and production lines — the labelled visual dataset of food defects, accumulated at customer sites
**Clock:** Production continuous; system release cycles internal
**Buyer:** VP of Technology / Chief Applications Officer
