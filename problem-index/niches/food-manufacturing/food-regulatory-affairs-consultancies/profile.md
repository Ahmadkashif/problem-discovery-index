# Food Regulatory Affairs & Label Compliance

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/food-manufacturing|Food Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Registrar Corp, EAS Consulting, and the food regulatory practices review labels against federal and state requirements, handle facility registration and import prior notice, prepare ingredient submissions, and respond to agency findings and import refusals. A label that fails review cannot ship, and a detained import accrues cost daily.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 regulatory specialists, food scientists, and attorneys
**Output:** Label reviews and compliance opinions, registrations and submissions, refusal and warning letter responses
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated label review findings joined to agency correspondence and import refusal outcomes — the record of what regulators actually enforce, which the published rules do not state
**Clock:** Compliance effective dates, import entry, and agency response windows
**Buyer:** VP of Regulatory Services / Practice Leader
