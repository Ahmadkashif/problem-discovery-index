# Plant Productivity Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/food-manufacturing|Food Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Small operational consultancies run line efficiency and yield improvement programmes in food plants, analyzing downtime, changeover, and giveaway to recover the margin lost to variability.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 3-15 industrial engineers and food process specialists
**Output:** Line efficiency analyses, yield improvement programmes, changeover optimization
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated plant performance findings, client-owned
**Clock:** Client improvement programme milestones
**Buyer:** Principal
