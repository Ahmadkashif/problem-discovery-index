# Food Manufacturer R&D and Quality Organizations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/food-manufacturing|Food Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The research, product development, and quality organizations inside the large food companies formulate products, run the process trials that establish operating windows, and manage the batch-to-batch yield variability the Pass 1 analysis identifies as a three-to-eight percent margin swing.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-2,000 food scientists, process engineers, and quality staff per large manufacturer
**Output:** Formulations, process specifications, quality standards, yield improvement work
**Proprietary data:** Formulation, process parameter, and yield data across every product and plant — including the operator adjustments that mitigate variability and are, per Pass 1, not fully articulable
**Clock:** Launch calendars and production schedules
**Buyer:** Chief Science Officer / VP of Quality
