# Packaging Testing & Migration Laboratories

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/food-manufacturing|Food Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Laboratories test food contact materials for chemical migration, barrier performance, and seal integrity, and issue the compliance documentation and shelf life support that lets a package be used with a given food.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 chemists, packaging engineers, and technicians
**Output:** Migration test reports, compliance declarations, barrier and integrity findings
**Proprietary data:** Migration and performance data across material and food combinations, accumulated over decades
**Clock:** Launch dates and regulatory compliance deadlines
**Buyer:** Laboratory Director / Technical Director
