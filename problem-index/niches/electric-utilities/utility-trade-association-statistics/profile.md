# Utility Trade Association Statistics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electric-utilities|Electric Distribution Utilities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Edison Electric Institute, the American Public Power Association and NRECA publish statistics, benchmarks and member surveys alongside their advocacy. APPA's statistical report, for example, counts 1,998 public power utilities.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under ~10 dedicated statistical and benchmarking staff per association (estimate)
**Output:** Statistical reports, member surveys, benchmarking
**Proprietary data:** Member survey responses
**Clock:** Self-set annual publications
**Buyer:** Director of Research
