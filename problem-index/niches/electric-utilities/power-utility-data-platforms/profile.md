# Power & Utility Data Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electric-utilities|Electric Distribution Utilities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Subscription platforms that compile utility and grid data from FERC, EIA, grid operators and commissions into linked datasets and analytics. Hitachi Energy's Velocity Suite, S&P Global Market Intelligence's power data, Enverus (which absorbed Pearl Street Technologies' interconnection modelling) and Grid Status are representative (named from recall). Yes Energy listed data and full-stack engineering roles on its own board on 2026-10-24.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~50-300 data operations staff and analysts per large platform (estimate)
**Output:** Utility, plant, transmission and market datasets; analytics, maps and alerts
**Proprietary data:** Cleaned, linked history of public filings (FERC Form 1 and 714, EIA-860, 861 and 923, grid-operator queues). Valuable work, but built from sources anyone can re-assemble
**Clock:** Agency release schedules (EIA-861 2025 final data released 2026-10-07) and daily market data, which the platform follows; no third party imposes a deadline on its own deliverables
**Buyer:** Head of Power Data Products
