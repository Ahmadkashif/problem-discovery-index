# Utility R&D Consortium

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electric-utilities|Electric Distribution Utilities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Electric Power Research Institute runs collaborative research programmes funded by member utilities' annual contributions. Its work covers distribution planning (the DRIVE hosting-capacity method and the OpenDSS simulator), asset performance, vegetation, DER integration and more. EPRI says it works with more than 450 companies in 45 countries (as quoted in search results). Smaller consortia exist alongside it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Hundreds of researchers within ~1,000+ staff (third-party estimates range from 700 to ~2,200)
**Output:** Research reports, methods, software tools and member databases
**Proprietary data:** Laboratory test data and member-contributed field and equipment-failure databases
**Clock:** Annual programme cycles the institute sets itself
**Buyer:** Vice President of a research sector
