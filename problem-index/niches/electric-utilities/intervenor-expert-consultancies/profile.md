# Intervenor & Consumer-Advocate Expert Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electric-utilities|Electric Distribution Utilities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Firms that analyse utility filings and testify for state consumer advocates, commission staff, industrial customer groups and environmental organisations. Synapse Energy Economics, GDS Associates and Exeter Associates are representative (named from recall). They review revenue requirements, cost of capital, load forecasts and resource plans, and propose adjustments that become disallowances.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~15-80 analysts and expert witnesses per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Intervenor testimony, discovery strategy, alternative forecasts and revenue adjustments
**Proprietary data:** Cross-jurisdiction knowledge of which arguments commissions accepted; the inputs are utility filings and discovery responses
**Clock:** Testimony due dates in the commission's procedural schedule
**Buyer:** Principal / Managing Consultant

**Kill switches:** **live** — engagements are usually retained through counsel for the advocate or customer group, and the most valuable inputs, confidential discovery responses, may be used only in that proceeding under protective orders. The vault treats counsel-commissioned work on another party's data as "data isn't theirs".
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. Insight-as-invoice on a hard procedural clock, with a fragmented buyer base of fifty advocates and many customer groups, blocked by how the work is commissioned and what the data is.
