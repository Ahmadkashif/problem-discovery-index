# Storm Outage Prediction Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electric-utilities|Electric Distribution Utilities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Weather-analytics vendors and university-utility research centres that forecast outages and damage before storms so utilities can size and stage crews. DTN's utility products and the University of Connecticut's outage prediction model built with Eversource are examples (recalled). Major events caused 80% of US outage hours in 2024 (EIA), which is the demand behind them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-40 meteorologists and data scientists per vendor's utility practice (estimate)
**Output:** Pre-storm outage and damage forecasts, crew-need estimates, event briefings
**Proprietary data:** Weather model archives and forecast skill records; the outage histories they train on belong to each utility, with ORNL's public county-level EAGLE-I data the exception
**Clock:** The storm's arrival
**Buyer:** Head of Utility Meteorology
