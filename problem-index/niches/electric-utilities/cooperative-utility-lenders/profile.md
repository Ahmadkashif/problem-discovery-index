# Cooperative & Public Power Lenders

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electric-utilities|Electric Distribution Utilities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Lenders specialising in cooperatives and public power: the National Rural Utilities Cooperative Finance Corporation (CFC), CoBank and USDA's Rural Utilities Service (recalled). They underwrite construction and refinancing loans, and CFC publishes a Key Ratio Trend Analysis benchmarking co-ops (recalled).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-60 credit analysts per lender's electric portfolio (estimate)
**Output:** Credit decisions, loan monitoring and member benchmarking reports
**Proprietary data:** Borrowers' financial and operating reports over decades
**Clock:** Loan cycles and borrower reporting dates
**Buyer:** Chief Credit Officer
