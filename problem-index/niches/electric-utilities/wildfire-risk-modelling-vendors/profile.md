# Wildfire Risk Modelling for Utility Mitigation Plans

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electric-utilities|Electric Distribution Utilities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Firms that model ignition probability and fire-spread consequence across utility service territories for wildfire mitigation plans and public safety power shutoff decisions. Technosylva and Reax Engineering are representative (recalled). Rhizome lists wildfire risk mitigation and customers including National Grid, Seattle City Light and Vermont Electric Cooperative on its site. Overstory sells vegetation-driven wildfire risk and listed staff wildfire machine learning and data science roles on its own board on 2026-10-24.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-100 fire scientists, modellers and data scientists per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Risk maps by circuit segment, daily fire-weather consequence forecasts, plan-ready risk models
**Proprietary data:** Fire-spread models and fuel layers the vendor owns; ignition, asset and outage data supplied by each utility
**Clock:** Wildfire Mitigation Plan filing and review cycles (Energy Safety in California approves or denies each base plan after public comment) and daily shutoff decisions in fire season
**Buyer:** Chief Science Officer

**Kill switches:** **live for half the pocket.** The utility-specific half, ignition-probability and consequence models trained on each utility's asset, ignition and outage records under contract, is walled per client, the same switch recorded for [[niches/utility-contractors/utility-vegetation-management-analytics/profile|🔍 Utility Vegetation Management Analytics]] (46). The other half is vendor-owned and clean: Technosylva-style fuel layers and fire-spread models, and Overstory and AiDash satellite vegetation layers. Scored alone, that half lands at ~39–42 (estimate) with no switch, so it is below threshold either way.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. The verdict follows the utility-trained risk models, which is what the mitigation plans buy. The vendor-owned fuel, fire-spread and satellite layers are clean but below threshold (~39–42, estimate) because a handful of firms sell them on clocks set by their clients.
