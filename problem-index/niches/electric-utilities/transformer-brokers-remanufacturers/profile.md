# Transformer Brokers & Remanufacturers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electric-utilities|Electric Distribution Utilities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Firms that broker new and surplus distribution and power transformers, refurbish used units, and find inventory for utilities facing long lead times and supply deficits (Wood Mackenzie puts power transformers at 128 weeks in Q2 2025). The supply-side story is covered under [[niches/electrical-distributors/utility-grid-supply/profile|🔵 Utility & Grid Supply]] and [[niches/electrical-distributors/grid-equipment-supply-research/profile|🔍 Grid Equipment Supply-Chain Research]] (40). The manufacturers themselves are the queued Power Equipment Manufacturers row.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 2-15 sales and engineering staff, no research function (estimate)
**Output:** Equipment sales and refurbishment
**Proprietary data:** Inventory lists and buyer contacts
**Clock:** Utility outage and project dates
**Buyer:** Owner
