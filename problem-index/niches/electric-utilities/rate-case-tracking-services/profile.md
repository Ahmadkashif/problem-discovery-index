# Rate Case Tracking & Regulatory Research Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electric-utilities|Electric Distribution Utilities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Services that track every utility rate case and commission decision and rank state regulatory climates for investors. S&P Global's Regulatory Research Associates is the dominant service (it counted a record ~$22B of electric and gas rate requests in 2025, per a search summary); sell-side utility analysts and a few boutiques cover parts of the same ground. Buyers are utility equity and credit investors, rating agencies and utilities benchmarking their own outcomes.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~15-40 analysts at the leading service (estimate)
**Output:** Rate case summaries and calendars, authorised-return databases, state regulatory climate rankings
**Proprietary data:** Decades of structured case outcomes and the firm's own rankings; the inputs are public dockets
**Clock:** Commission decision dates and procedural schedules, which the service follows rather than meets; its publication timing is its own
**Buyer:** Head of Regulatory Research
