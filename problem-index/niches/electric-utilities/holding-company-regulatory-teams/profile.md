# Utility Holding Company Regulatory & Rate Case Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electric-utilities|Electric Distribution Utilities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Investor-owned holding companies own most of the ~170 investor-owned operating utilities (count derived from APPA's shares) and run central regulatory affairs, rates and revenue-requirement groups. These groups prepare rate cases, riders and resource-plan filings for each state jurisdiction, answer discovery and supply witnesses. They are the best-funded analysis function in the industry, and their output sets what customers pay.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~30-150 regulatory, rates and revenue-requirement analysts per holding company (estimate)
**Output:** Rate case filings, cost-of-service and rate design studies, rider filings, data request responses
**Proprietary data:** Each operating company's ledgers, plant records, load research and every past filing and order; summaries are public in FERC Form 1, but the workpapers are the utility's own
**Clock:** Commission procedural schedules and statutory suspension periods
**Buyer:** VP of Regulatory Affairs
