# Legal Discovery Tools Rebuilt Around the Data Request

**Niche:** [[niches/electric-utilities/rate-cases-revenue-requirement/profile|Rate Cases & Revenue Requirement]]
**Industry:** [[industries/electric-utilities|Electric Distribution Utilities]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** E-discovery software manages documents produced in litigation; rate case discovery is questions answered by witnesses on a ten-day clock.
**Tags:** #bert #large-language-models #word-embeddings #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a revenue requirement through a contested case with the fewest disallowances, and whoever builds the evidentiary record that survives discovery and intervenor cross-examination takes the account.

## The Problem
Regulatory teams borrow tools from litigation, such as matter management, document review and deadline calendars, but the work is different. Each data request is a question needing a drafted answer, a sponsoring witness, attachments and a confidentiality designation, served within the procedural schedule.

## What Already Exists
Relativity and Everlaw for review; legal matter and deadline management; enterprise search over Microsoft 365; commission e-filing portals for service.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation: the data request as the unit of work, with routing by FERC account, schedule and witness; retrieval of prior answers from the utility's archive and public dockets; drafting grounded in workpapers; consistency checks against pre-filed testimony and earlier responses in the case; protective-order tiers; and service tracking by intervenor. See [[problems/electric-utilities/low-impact-1|Rate Case Discovery Run as a Document Hunt]].

## Target Customer
Regulatory affairs departments and the outside counsel who run discovery for mid-sized utilities.

## Impact If Solved
Repeat questions, perhaps ~30%–50% of a case's data requests (estimate), answered from the archive. Deadlines are met without weekend surges and contradictions are caught before intervenors find them.
