# Rate Cases & Revenue Requirement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electric-utilities|Electric Distribution Utilities]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a revenue requirement through a contested case with the fewest disallowances, and whoever builds the evidentiary record that survives discovery and intervenor cross-examination takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.5B a year of regulatory consulting, outside counsel, expert-witness and in-house regulatory staff cost tied to US electric rate proceedings (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.3% of retail electricity revenue, which sets the price of the other 99.7% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — revenue requirement models are large Excel workbooks; discovery is email and shared drives
**Target Buyer:** VP of Regulatory Affairs / Director of Rates & Revenue Requirement
**Automation Potential:** Medium — discovery, schedules and consistency checks are automatable; witness testimony and settlement are not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
An investor-owned utility earns what its commission allows, and the rate case is where that is decided. It covers a test-year cost of service, rate base, depreciation, cost of capital, a class cost-of-service study and rate design. All of it is filed with testimony, then tested through data requests, intervenor testimony, hearings and often settlement. Requests reached a record in 2025, nearly $31B for electric and gas (PowerLines), and affordability politics have made disallowances more likely. Public power and co-ops set rates through their boards or councils with lighter process, which is covered under [[niches/municipal-services/utility-rate-consultants/profile|🔍 Municipal Utility Rate Consultants]]. The commission's side is the queued Utility Commissions row.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Excel revenue requirement and cost-of-service models (sometimes vendor templates); depreciation study software; outside consultancies for cost of capital, depreciation and cost of service ([[niches/utility-contractors/utility-asset-rate-case-consulting/profile|🔍 Utility Asset Management & Rate Case Consulting]]); e-discovery and docket tools from legal practice. Gaps: no link from each adjustment to its workpaper and source ledger; discovery archives unsearchable; no record of which arguments commissions accepted.
