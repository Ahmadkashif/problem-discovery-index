# Event Logistics Software Adapted to Mutual Assistance

**Niche:** [[niches/electric-utilities/storm-restoration-mutual-assistance/profile|Storm Restoration & Mutual Assistance]]
**Industry:** [[industries/electric-utilities|Electric Distribution Utilities]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Emergency management and event logistics platforms track people, rooms and meals; they don't know a crew's mutual-aid terms or how restoration cost is recovered.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #descriptive-statistics #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #automation #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to restore the last customer sooner with fewer crew-days, and whoever stages the right number of crews in the right places before the storm arrives takes the account.

## The Problem
During a big storm a utility hosts thousands of outside workers. Each crew has to be received, checked in, assigned, lodged, fed, fuelled and eventually released. Then the invoices arrive in dozens of formats and have to be matched to what each crew actually did.

## What Already Exists
Emergency management platforms (WebEOC, Juvare), workforce check-in apps, hotel and catering booking services, accounts-payable automation (Coupa, SAP Concur), and FEMA's grants portal for public entities.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation: rosters keyed to mutual-aid requests from the regional group, with each crew's home utility, rates and terms; check-in and release timestamps tied to assignments in the outage system; daily cost accrual per crew; invoice matching against the roster and agreed rates (see [[problems/electric-utilities/low-impact-2|Storm Cost Recovery From a Shoebox of Crew Invoices]]); and output formatted for the commission's storm-cost proceeding or FEMA Public Assistance categories.

## Target Customer
Storm logistics and storm accounting teams at mid-sized IOUs, public power systems and co-ops.

## Impact If Solved
One record from request to reimbursement, crews released ~0.5–1 day sooner, and storm cost recovery filed ~2–4 months earlier (estimate).
