# G&T Cooperatives & Joint Action Agencies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electric-utilities|Electric Distribution Utilities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Generation-and-transmission cooperatives and public power joint action agencies supply wholesale power to dozens of member distribution utilities. They also run shared planning, load forecasting, rates and member engineering services for those members.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-60 planning, forecasting and rates staff per organisation (estimate)
**Output:** Member load forecasts (also used for lender requirements, recalled), wholesale rate studies, member engineering support
**Proprietary data:** Members' wholesale metering and, in many cases, aggregated AMI data
**Clock:** Wholesale rate filings and lender planning requirements; partly self-set
**Buyer:** VP of Member Services / Planning

**Kill switches:** **live** — the corpus that matters, members' aggregated AMI and load data, is furnished by member utilities under membership and wholesale terms. That is the contributed-data switch the vault applies to member-trust pools. The G&T's own wholesale metering at delivery points is clean, but on its own it is too coarse for the planning work the pocket sells.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. The verdict follows the member-data half, because member AMI is what makes the planning service possible. The insight is also bundled into wholesale power rather than invoiced, so it would be below threshold even without the switch. These organisations matter more as a sales channel to small utilities (see [[niches/electric-utilities/small-coops-and-munis/profile|🟣 Small Cooperatives & Municipal Utilities]]).
