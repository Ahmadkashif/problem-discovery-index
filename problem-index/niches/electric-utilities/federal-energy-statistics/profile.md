# Federal Electricity Statistics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electric-utilities|Electric Distribution Utilities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The US Energy Information Administration collects Form EIA-861, a census of utilities covering sales, revenue, customers, reliability, distributed generation, AMI and demand response. It also collects EIA-860 on generators and related surveys, and publishes them free. FERC collects Form 1 financial reports from investor-owned utilities. Most third-party utility analytics start from these datasets. They are utility-level (EIA-861, Form 1) or plant-level for generators of 1 MW and up (EIA-860), so they give territory context for a capacity screen but no feeder limits or study outcomes.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~50-100 statisticians and analysts in EIA's electricity programmes (estimate)
**Output:** Public datasets and reports (EIA-861 2025 final data released 2026-10-07)
**Proprietary data:** Respondent-level submissions, some held confidential
**Clock:** Self-set release calendar
**Buyer:** Director of Electricity Statistics
