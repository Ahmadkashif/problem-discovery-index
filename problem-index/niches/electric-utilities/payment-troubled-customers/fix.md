# The Assistance Payment That Arrives After the Disconnection

**Niche:** [[niches/electric-utilities/payment-troubled-customers/profile|Payment-Troubled Residential Customers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/electric-utilities|Electric Distribution Utilities]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A household applies for energy assistance, the agency approves it, and the pledge reaches the utility days after the household was disconnected.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #compliance #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a payment-troubled household connected and paying, and whoever matches each household to the right arrangement or assistance programme before its disconnection notice takes the account.

## The Problem
LIHEAP and similar programmes are run by state agencies and community action agencies with their own intake and approval timelines. Pledges reach utilities by file transfer, portal or fax on varying schedules. The disconnection process does not know an application is pending, so households in the middle of applying get cut off, then reconnected with fees once the pledge posts.

## Why It's Still Broken
The utility and the agencies have no shared status, and data-sharing agreements and privacy rules slow integration. Each agency sends pledges differently.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A pending-assistance flag the utility can set from agency feeds or a customer's self-declaration with proof of application. It holds disconnection for a defined window, chases pledge status automatically, and gives the agency a simple status view of its applicants' accounts.

## Who Feels the Pain
Households in hardship; representatives handling reconnection calls; agencies whose funds arrive too late to prevent harm; commissions fielding complaints.

## Impact If Fixed
Disconnections of households with a pending assistance application, perhaps ~5%–10% of winter disconnections (estimate), avoided along with their reconnection fees, at almost no cost.
