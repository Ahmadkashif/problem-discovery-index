# Interconnection & Large-Load Planning

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electric-utilities|Electric Distribution Utilities]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is: every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell an applicant quickly what capacity exists and what connecting will cost. But for small distributed generation, storage and chargers the contest is clearing thousands of applications against a hosting number the study later confirms. For data centres and factories it is answering a few huge, often speculative requests with a megawatt figure, date and cost that hold until energisation. Different volumes, buyers and winners, so it is decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.5B a year of distribution planning, interconnection study, software and consulting spend at US utilities (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.5% of retail electricity revenue, but it decides where a large share of the ~$208B 2025 IOU capital budget goes (estimate; capex figure from EEI)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — load-flow software is standard; intake, screening and study tracking run on portals, spreadsheets and PDFs
**Target Buyer:** Director of Distribution Planning / Manager of Interconnection
**Automation Potential:** High for screening and intake; low for final study sign-off

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is where load growth meets the grid. Every new connection runs through a small planning group that must decide whether the feeder, transformer bank and protection can take it, and who pays if not: a rooftop array, a battery, a fleet depot, a 100 MW data-centre campus. The workload has risen sharply as data-centre demand heads toward 6.7%–12% of US electricity by 2028 (LBNL for DOE) while DER applications keep arriving. The bulk-system interconnection queues and generator studies run by ISOs and transmission owners belong to the queued Transmission & Grid Operators row and to [[niches/solar-installers/interconnection-grid-studies/profile|🔍 Interconnection & Grid Impact Studies]]. This niche is the distribution utility's own screening and study work.

## Current Tools & Gaps
CYME, Synergi Electric, PSS SINCAL and OpenDSS for load flow; PowerClerk and GridUnity for application workflows; Gridsight, Kevala and envelio for capacity analytics; hosting-capacity maps in 26 states by May 2024 (DOE). Gaps: the study archive is PDFs; quick reads by senior planners are never recorded; hosting maps are stale; nothing distinguishes real large-load requests from placeholders.
