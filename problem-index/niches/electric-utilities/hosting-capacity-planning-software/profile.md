# Distribution Planning & Hosting-Capacity Software Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electric-utilities|Electric Distribution Utilities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors of planning engines and capacity analytics sold to utilities. The incumbents are Eaton CYME, DNV Synergi Electric and Siemens PSS SINCAL. Newer analytics firms include Gridsight, Kevala and envelio, and GridUnity sells interconnection workflow (named from recall and company sites). Gridsight (a Founding Forward Deployed Engineer) and GridUnity (full-stack and staff engineers) were hiring on their own boards on 2026-10-24.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-60 power systems engineers and data scientists per vendor's analytics team (estimate)
**Output:** Software licences, capacity analytics and hosting-map services
**Proprietary data:** Algorithms and product; the network models and loading data are each utility's property
**Clock:** State orders on hosting-map updates and interconnection timelines; partly set by clients
**Buyer:** Chief Product Officer
