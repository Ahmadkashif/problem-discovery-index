# Utility Customer Satisfaction & Operational Benchmarking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electric-utilities|Electric Distribution Utilities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Survey and benchmarking studies sold to utilities: J.D. Power's electric utility residential and business customer satisfaction studies, Escalent's Cogent Syndicated utility research, and operational benchmarking consortia such as First Quartile Consulting (named from recall). Utilities buy the diagnostic detail, cite rankings, and in some cases tie incentive pay to them. Consumer-experience benchmarking in another industry is logged as [[niches/wired-broadband-providers/isp-customer-experience-benchmarks/profile|🔍 ISP Customer Experience Benchmarks]] (41).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-30 researchers and analysts per firm's utility practice (estimate)
**Output:** Annual syndicated studies, utility-level diagnostics, benchmarking reports, award licences
**Proprietary data:** Years of respondent-level survey data and consortium benchmark submissions
**Clock:** Self-set annual study calendars
**Buyer:** Utility practice lead

**Kill switches:** split. The survey half (J.D. Power, Escalent) is clean: the firm collects the responses itself with respondents' consent. The consortium half (First Quartile-style operational benchmarking) carries a **live** switch, because its submissions are membership data contributed by utilities for benchmarking and reported back only to the same members.
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. The verdict and scorecard follow the clean survey half: the study is the product, but the calendar is the firm's own and the market is two or three firms. The consortium half would be logged as a kill switch.
