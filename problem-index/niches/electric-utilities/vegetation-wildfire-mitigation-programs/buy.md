# Regulatory Compliance Software for Mitigation Plan Data

**Niche:** [[niches/electric-utilities/vegetation-wildfire-mitigation-programs/profile|Vegetation Management & Wildfire Mitigation Programmes]]
**Industry:** [[industries/electric-utilities|Electric Distribution Utilities]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Governance, risk and compliance platforms track obligations and evidence; mitigation plans need spatial, initiative-level progress data in the regulator's own schema.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #compliance #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show regulators that each trimming and hardening dollar removed measurable ignition and outage risk, and whoever ties completed work to risk reduced, span by span, takes the account.

## The Problem
Wildfire mitigation plans commit a utility to dozens of initiatives, each with targets in miles, structures or inspections. Regulators expect periodic progress reporting with spatial data. Teams assemble those submissions from work management, GIS and contractor spreadsheets by hand.

## What Already Exists
GRC platforms (ServiceNow GRC, Archer, AuditBoard); work management and GIS systems; data warehouses; consultancies that prepare plan filings.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation: initiative structures and metrics that match each state regulator's plan guidelines; spatial reconciliation of completed work against committed circuits and risk tiers; automated assembly of the regulator's data tables with validation before submission; evidence trails from each reported mile to the work order and inspection photo; and variance explanations drafted for initiatives behind target.

## Target Customer
Wildfire mitigation plan managers and regulatory compliance staff at utilities in states with plan requirements.

## Impact If Solved
Reporting cycles shortened from ~2–3 weeks to a few days per submission, and fewer data deficiencies flagged by reviewers (estimate), with progress visible internally before the regulator sees it.
