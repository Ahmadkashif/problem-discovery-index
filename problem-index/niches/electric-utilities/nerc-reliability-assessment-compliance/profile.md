# NERC Reliability Assessment & Compliance Monitoring

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electric-utilities|Electric Distribution Utilities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
NERC and its six regional entities set and enforce reliability standards, including the CIP cybersecurity standards that apply to utilities registered for the relevant functions. They also publish long-term, summer and winter reliability assessments. Bulk-system planning and operation belong mostly to the queued Transmission & Grid Operators row; distribution utilities meet NERC through registration and audits.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-60 reliability assessment and event analysis staff (estimate); compliance auditors across the regional entities are production headcount and not counted
**Output:** Reliability assessments, event analyses, standards, audit findings
**Proprietary data:** Registered entities' compliance evidence, event reports and confidential infrastructure data
**Clock:** Audit cycles and standards effective dates (FERC-approved); the assessment publication calendar is NERC's own
**Buyer:** Director of Reliability Assessment
