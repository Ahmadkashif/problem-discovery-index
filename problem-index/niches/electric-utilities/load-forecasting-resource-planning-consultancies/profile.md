# Load Forecasting & Resource Planning Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electric-utilities|Electric Distribution Utilities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Consultancies that build the load forecasts and integrated resource plans utilities file with commissions. Itron's forecasting practice, E3, Ascend Analytics, Charles River Associates, Guidehouse, Black & Veatch and 1898 & Co. are representative (named from recall). They model peak and energy demand, large-load pipelines, DER and electrification adoption and capacity expansion (PLEXOS, Aurora, EnCompass), then defend the result in testimony. Data-centre growth, projected by LBNL for DOE at 6.7%–12% of US electricity by 2028, has made these forecasts the most contested numbers in resource-plan proceedings.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-150 forecasters, planners and modellers per firm's utility practice (estimate)
**Output:** Load forecasts, integrated resource plans, capacity expansion studies, testimony
**Proprietary data:** Archives of the firm's own forecast vintages across clients, plus its end-use and econometric model libraries; the actuals those forecasts would be graded against are public (FERC Form 714, EIA-861)
**Clock:** Resource-plan and rate case filing dates. Commissions set resource-plan schedules but can extend them, and the utility chooses when to file a rate case, so the deadline sits on the client's filing rather than on the forecast itself
**Buyer:** Practice Leader, Load Forecasting & Resource Planning
