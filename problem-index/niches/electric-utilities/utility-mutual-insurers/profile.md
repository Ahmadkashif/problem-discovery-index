# Utility Mutual Insurers & Wildfire Liability Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electric-utilities|Electric Distribution Utilities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Industry mutuals such as AEGIS and Energy Insurance Mutual write liability and property cover for utilities, including the wildfire liability that has become the hardest utility risk to place in the West (recalled). Their underwriters and risk engineers review members' mitigation programmes to price cover.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-80 underwriters, risk engineers and actuaries per mutual (estimate)
**Output:** Underwriting decisions and pricing; risk engineering reviews
**Proprietary data:** Members' loss histories and submitted mitigation information
**Clock:** Policy renewal dates
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
