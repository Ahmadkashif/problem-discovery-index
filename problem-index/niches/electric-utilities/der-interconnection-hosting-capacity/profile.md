# DER Interconnection & Hosting Capacity

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electric-utilities|Electric Distribution Utilities]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to clear rooftop, storage and EV-charger applications against a hosting-capacity number that later studies confirm, and whoever sends the fewest applications to full study without a later violation takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1B a year of DER interconnection processing, hosting-capacity analysis and supplemental-review spend at US utilities (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.2% of retail electricity revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — online application portals are common; screens and supplemental reviews are partly manual
**Target Buyer:** Manager of DER Interconnection
**Automation Potential:** High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is a volume business. Thousands of small applications for solar, batteries, EV chargers and, increasingly, flexible loads arrive each year at a large utility. Each is checked against the state interconnection rules' screens (penetration thresholds, short-circuit contribution, voltage) and either passes fast-track or goes to supplemental review. Hosting-capacity maps, mandated in several states and published by 58 utilities and agencies by May 2024 (DOE), are supposed to let applicants self-screen, but they are refreshed infrequently and often disagree with later reviews. The installer's side of this workflow is covered in [[niches/solar-installers/permitting-interconnection/profile|⚡ Permitting & Interconnection]]; this is the utility's side.

## Current Tools & Gaps
PowerClerk and GridUnity portals; CYME and Synergi hosting-capacity modules; EPRI DRIVE; envelio's connection-request automation. Gaps: hosting values computed under hidden assumptions and not reconciled with supplemental-review results; flexible-interconnection (export-limited) options handled case by case; no feedback from actual feeder performance after interconnection.
