# Customs & Trade Law Practices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/customs-brokers|Customs Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Trade practices at law firms and the specialist consultancies produce classification and valuation opinions, prepare binding ruling requests, structure first sale and duty planning programmes, and defend importers under focused assessment and penalty proceedings. The written opinion is the invoice and it is tested by the customs administration.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-200 trade attorneys, licensed brokers, and analysts at the larger practices
**Output:** Classification and valuation opinions, ruling requests, audit defence, penalty mitigation
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated ruling requests and outcomes, and the record of which framings the administration accepted
**Clock:** Entry deadlines, ruling response windows, protest and audit periods
**Buyer:** Practice leader / Partner
