# Forwarder Trade Compliance Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/customs-brokers|Customs Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The trade compliance and advisory groups inside Expeditors, DB Schenker, Flexport, and the large forwarders classify goods, manage licensing, and advise clients on duty planning at a scale no independent broker reaches. Compliance quality is what keeps large importer accounts.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-800 licensed brokers, classification specialists, and compliance analysts
**Output:** Classifications, entry filings, compliance advisory, duty planning analyses
**Proprietary data:** Classification decisions and customs outcomes across millions of entries and thousands of importers
**Clock:** Entry filing deadlines on every shipment
**Buyer:** VP of Global Trade Compliance / Chief Compliance Officer
