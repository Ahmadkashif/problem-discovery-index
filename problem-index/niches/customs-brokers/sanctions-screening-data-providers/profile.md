# Sanctions & Restricted Party Screening Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/customs-brokers|Customs Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Dow Jones Risk & Compliance, LexisNexis WorldCompliance, and LSEG World-Check maintain the restricted party, sanctions, and adverse media data that every trade transaction is screened against. Analysts monitor designations from dozens of authorities, resolve entities across aliases and scripts, map ownership and control relationships, and maintain profiles. Screening is a mandatory step before any shipment moves, and a missed match is a criminal exposure.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-1,500 research analysts, entity resolution specialists, and regional researchers
**Output:** Screening lists, entity profiles, ownership and control mapping, adverse media research — the licensed product
**Proprietary data:** Entity resolution and ownership research across millions of profiles in dozens of languages, and the aliases, relationships, and adverse media linkages that no authority's published list contains
**Clock:** Sanctions designations effective on announcement, with screening required before every transaction
**Buyer:** VP of Content / Head of Research Operations
