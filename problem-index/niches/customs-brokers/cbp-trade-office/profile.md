# Customs Administration Trade Offices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/customs-brokers|Customs Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The customs administration's trade office employs national import specialists who issue binding classification rulings, targeting analysts who select shipments for examination, and the policy staff who write the regulations the whole industry works to.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-2,000 import specialists, targeting analysts, and trade policy staff
**Output:** Binding rulings, targeting models, regulatory guidance, enforcement actions
**Proprietary data:** Every entry filed into the country, joined to examination findings and enforcement outcomes
**Clock:** Statutory ruling response windows and entry processing
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer
