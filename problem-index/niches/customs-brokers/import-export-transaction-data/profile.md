# Import & Export Transaction Data Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/customs-brokers|Customs Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Panjiva, ImportGenius, and Descartes Datamyne assemble bill of lading and customs manifest records into searchable trade transaction data, resolve shippers and consignees into companies, and sell supply chain visibility and competitive intelligence to importers, brokers, and investors.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 data operations staff, entity resolution specialists, and analysts
**Output:** Transaction search, supplier and buyer intelligence, trade flow analytics — the subscription
**Proprietary data:** Entity resolution across manifests where shipper and consignee names are inconsistent, incomplete, and frequently deliberately obscured
**Clock:** Continuous; no third-party deadline governs the work
**Buyer:** Head of Data Operations / Chief Product Officer
