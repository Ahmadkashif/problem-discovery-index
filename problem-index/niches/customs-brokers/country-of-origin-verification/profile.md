# Country of Origin & Forced Labour Verification

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/customs-brokers|Customs Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Firms and programmes that verify where goods actually originated and whether any input in the chain implicates forced labour — tracing supply chains, testing isotopic and material signatures, and assembling the documentary package an importer must produce when a shipment is detained. The finding determines whether goods enter the country.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-200 supply chain investigators, scientists, and trade analysts
**Output:** Origin determinations, traceability packages, detention response documentation
**Proprietary data:** Supply chain tracing records and, in the analytical firms, isotopic and material reference libraries built specimen by specimen
**Clock:** Detention response deadlines, which run while goods sit at the border accruing cost
**Buyer:** VP of Verification Services / Practice Leader
