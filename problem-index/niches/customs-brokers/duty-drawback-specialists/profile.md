# Duty Drawback Specialists

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/customs-brokers|Customs Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Specialist firms recover duties paid on imported goods that are subsequently exported or destroyed — reconstructing the transaction trail, matching imports to exports under statutory substitution rules, and filing claims. The recovery analysis is the entire service and firms are typically paid a share of what they recover.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 drawback specialists, analysts, and licensed brokers
**Output:** Drawback claims and the supporting reconciliation analyses
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated claim methodology and the record of which substitution and documentation approaches survived customs review
**Clock:** Statutory filing windows — a missed deadline forfeits the recovery permanently
**Buyer:** Managing Director / Practice Leader
