# Global Trade Content Databases

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/customs-brokers|Customs Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Descartes CustomsInfo, Thomson Reuters ONESOURCE Global Trade, and E2open maintain the trade content every entry is filed against — tariff schedules and duty rates for hundreds of countries, rules of origin for every trade agreement, import and export licensing requirements, and the regulatory conditions attached to each classification. Analysts track changes across every customs administration on earth and keep the content current. Brokers and importers subscribe because tracking it themselves is impossible at their scale.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-800 trade content analysts, customs specialists, and researchers
**Output:** Tariff and regulatory content, rules of origin logic, duty calculation data — the subscription itself
**Proprietary data:** Decades of normalized, cross-referenced trade content covering hundreds of jurisdictions, plus the interpretive layer that makes national schedules comparable
**Clock:** Tariff changes effective on announced dates, trade agreement entry into force, and the entry filing deadlines every subscriber works to
**Buyer:** VP of Content / Director of Global Trade Research
