# Customs Bond Surety Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/customs-brokers|Customs Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Surety underwriters price the customs bonds every importer must post, assessing the risk that duties, taxes, and penalties will go unpaid — a risk that moved sharply when antidumping duties began arriving years after entry.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 underwriters and analysts
**Output:** Bond underwriting decisions, exposure analyses, collateral requirements
**Proprietary data:** Claim and default experience by importer profile and commodity
**Clock:** Bond renewal cycles and duty assessment timelines
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
