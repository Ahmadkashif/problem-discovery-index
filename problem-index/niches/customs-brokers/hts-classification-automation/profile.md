# Tariff Classification Automation Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/customs-brokers|Customs Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors selling automated tariff classification build the models and rule sets that propose a code from a product description, trained on classification decisions and rulings. Brokers use them to accelerate the licensed broker's review rather than to replace it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-80 trade specialists, data scientists, and content staff
**Output:** Classification suggestions, confidence scoring, ruling retrieval
**Proprietary data:** Classification training data assembled from published rulings and customer decisions
**Clock:** Entry deadlines, inherited from customers
**Buyer:** VP of Product / Head of Trade Content
