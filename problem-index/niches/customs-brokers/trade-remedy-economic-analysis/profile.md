# Trade Remedy Economic Analysis

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/customs-brokers|Customs Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Economic consultancies and law firm trade practices produce the injury, dumping margin, and subsidy analyses that antidumping and countervailing duty proceedings are decided on. The analysis determines whether an entire product category faces punitive duties, which reshapes sourcing for every importer and broker downstream.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 economists, accountants, and trade analysts
**Output:** Injury analyses, margin calculations, questionnaire responses, hearing testimony
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated case methodology and the record of which analytical approaches the agencies accepted
**Clock:** Statutory investigation deadlines, which are among the most rigid in administrative law
**Buyer:** Practice leader / Managing Director
