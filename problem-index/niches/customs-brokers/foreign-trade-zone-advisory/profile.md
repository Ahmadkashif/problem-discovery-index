# Foreign Trade Zone Advisory

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/customs-brokers|Customs Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Consultancies that assess whether a foreign trade zone or bonded structure would save an importer duty, prepare the applications, and administer the compliance obligations that follow. The feasibility analysis is the deliverable and the savings case is what sells it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 zone specialists, analysts, and compliance staff
**Output:** Feasibility analyses, zone applications, ongoing compliance administration
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated savings modelling and application outcomes across zones and industries
**Clock:** Application review timelines and annual reporting obligations
**Buyer:** Practice leader
