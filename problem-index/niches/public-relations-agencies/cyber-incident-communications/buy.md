# Disclosure Management Software Extended to Media Lines

**Niche:** [[niches/public-relations-agencies/cyber-incident-communications/profile|Cyber Incident & Disclosure Communications]]
**Industry:** [[industries/public-relations-agencies|Public Relations Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Finance teams already link every number in a filing to one source; incident communications need the same linkage across filings, notices and press statements.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation #evaluation-metrics
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep the media statement, customer and regulator notices and any Form 8-K saying the same thing inside the four-business-day and state-notification clocks, under counsel's privilege, and whoever keeps disclosure consistent and on time wins the insurer- and law-firm-panel slot.

## The Problem
The 8-K under Item 1.05 must describe the nature, scope and timing of the incident and its material impact, and later amendments follow new information. The press statement and customer notices must not contradict it. Today the reconciliation is done by reading.

## What Already Exists
Disclosure management platforms such as Workiva link figures and text across 10-Ks, 10-Qs and 8-Ks to a single source with review and approval workflows. Breach-notification vendors maintain state-by-state notice templates.

## The Customization Gap
Incident use needs: qualitative facts (characterisations, not just numbers) as linked sources; audience documents outside the SEC filing set (press statement, customer FAQ, employee memo, regulator letters) in the same graph; privileged access controls run by outside counsel; amendment tracking when forensics changes the scope; and a timeline view of the four-business-day and state clocks. The platforms know filings; they do not know media lines or privilege.

## Target Customer
Listed companies already licensing a disclosure platform, and the law firms and PR firms they engage for incidents.

## Impact If Solved
Incident disclosures with the same single-source discipline as financial reporting, using software the client's finance team already trusts.
