# Cyber Incident & Disclosure Communications

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-relations-agencies|Public Relations Agencies]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep the media statement, customer and regulator notices and any Form 8-K saying the same thing inside the four-business-day and state-notification clocks, under counsel's privilege, and whoever keeps disclosure consistent and on time wins the insurer- and law-firm-panel slot.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M in US agency fees for cyber and data-incident communications (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% of US PR agency receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — forensic and legal work is tooled; the communications strand runs on drafts circulated by counsel
**Target Buyer:** Breach coach (outside counsel) and the client's general counsel; agency crisis practice lead
**Automation Potential:** Medium-high for consistency checking and notice drafting; low for the disclosure judgement

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A material cyber incident at a listed company starts a statutory clock: Form 8-K Item 1.05 within four business days of the materiality determination, plus state breach-notification deadlines and sector rules. The communications firm is usually engaged through outside counsel so its work stays privileged, and often appears on cyber insurers' approved vendor panels. The contest is not reading the public mood; it is saying the same, defensible thing to every audience on time while facts change hourly. The forensic side of the same incident, getting the notification decision-maker what they need, is mapped under [[niches/digital-forensics-firms/the-client-in-crisis/profile|Digital Forensics Firms: The Client During the Incident]]; this niche starts where that decision becomes words for the public.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Breach coaches run the response; forensic firms investigate; notification vendors mail letters and run call centres; the PR firm drafts statements, FAQs and media lines in Word. The gaps: no system holds the current fact set and checks the 8-K, customer notice, regulator notices, employee memo and media statement against it; no record of which statement version each audience received; and no reuse of language from past incidents that survived regulatory and litigation scrutiny.
