# Strategic & Financial Communications Research Units

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-relations-agencies|Public Relations Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
The research, polling and intelligence teams inside the strategic and financial communications firms (FGS Global, Brunswick, Teneo, Kekst CNC, Joele Frank, Sard Verbinnen and peers) that advise on mergers, shareholder activism, restructurings, litigation and executive transitions. They survey investors, employees and policymakers, map stakeholder positions and model how an announcement will land, mostly inside advisory engagements billed alongside counsel.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 researchers and analysts per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Stakeholder and investor perception studies, activism vulnerability assessments, announcement-reaction analysis, polling
**Proprietary data:** Engagement files and perception studies across many deals and contests, mostly commissioned by and confidential to clients
**Clock:** Deal announcements, shareholder meeting and nomination deadlines, earnings dates and court dates set by others
**Buyer:** Head of research and insights at the firm
