# The List Cleaned by Hand Before Every Send

**Niche:** [[niches/public-relations-agencies/media-list-outreach-operations/profile|Media List Building & Outreach Operations]]
**Industry:** [[industries/public-relations-agencies|Public Relations Agencies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Agencies pay for a maintained database and still spend hours per campaign checking whether each reporter is still there, because what they learn is never sent back.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #quick-win #worker-facing #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep every pitch list current to the week as reporters change beats, outlets and status, and whoever holds the freshest verified map of who covers what wins the database renewal.

## The Problem
Before a send, an account executive opens each reporter's recent articles to confirm they still cover the beat, removes bounced addresses from last time, and fixes titles. The next agency using the same database does the same work on the same records.

## Why It's Still Broken
Agencies' corrections stay in their saved lists or spreadsheets. Vendors offer a "suggest an edit" button that nobody has time to use, and there is no credit for contributing.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Capture corrections automatically from agency behaviour (bounces, removals, "no longer covers" tags, replies saying "I've moved") and feed them to the vendor's research queue with the evidence, crediting contributing customers with fresher data in return.

## Who Feels the Pain
Account executives repeating checks; reporters receiving pitches for beats they left; vendors whose research teams rediscover what customers already know.

## Impact If Fixed
Hours per campaign returned to every agency on the platform, from signals already generated.
