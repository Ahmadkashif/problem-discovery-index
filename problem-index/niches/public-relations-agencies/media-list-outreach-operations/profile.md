# Media List Building & Outreach Operations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-relations-agencies|Public Relations Agencies]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep every pitch list current to the week as reporters change beats, outlets and status, and whoever holds the freshest verified map of who covers what wins the database renewal.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$900M in US spend on media databases, outreach tools and the staff time spent building and cleaning lists (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~6% of agency staff time and software spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High — every agency licenses a database; the cleaning is manual
**Target Buyer:** Agency operations or media relations director; product and data leads at database vendors
**Automation Potential:** High — beat tracking, move detection, de-duplication and follow-up sequencing are mechanical

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Newsroom layoffs, outlet closures and the move of reporters to newsletters and independent platforms mean a media list decays quickly. Cision claims about 1.4 million contacts and Muck Rack and Meltwater several hundred thousand each, maintained by research teams that verify records by hand. Agencies pay median contracts in the low tens of thousands of dollars a year (Vendr data) and still clean lists themselves before each send. The contest is freshness: whose record of a reporter's current beat and outlet is right this week. The judgement about which reporter fits which story is a separate problem, treated in the industry's high-impact note.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Cision, Muck Rack, Meltwater, Prowly and Prezly databases and send tools; bounce handling in email; agency spreadsheets of "verified" lists. The gaps: last-verified dates hidden or stale, beat labels coarse, moves to newsletters and freelancing missed, and agencies' own bounce and reply signals never fed back to the vendor.
