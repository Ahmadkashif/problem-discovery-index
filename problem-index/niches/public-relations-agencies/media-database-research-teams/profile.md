# Media Contact Database Research Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-relations-agencies|Public Relations Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The research operations inside media database vendors (Cision, Muck Rack, Meltwater, Prowly and regional players) that find, verify and profile journalists, editors, producers and creators: current outlet, beat, contact details and pitch preferences. Agencies and in-house teams pay annual subscriptions, with reported median contracts in the low tens of thousands of dollars, largely for the accuracy of these records.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 media researchers and data staff per large vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Verified contact and beat records, outlet profiles, pitching preferences
**Proprietary data:** Contact histories and verification records for hundreds of thousands to ~1.4 million contacts (Cision's claim), plus, for outreach platforms, pitch send, open and reply data across many agencies
**Clock:** Continuous newsroom churn; no external deadline
**Buyer:** VP of data or content operations

**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. Classic labour-scaled verification with a real cross-agency pitch-outcome corpus nobody uses to grade relevance, but no outside clock governs the work and a handful of vendors make a thin buyer market.
