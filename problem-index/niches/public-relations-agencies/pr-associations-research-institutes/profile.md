# PR Professional Associations & Research Institutes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-relations-agencies|Public Relations Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Public Relations Society of America, the Institute for Public Relations, AMEC (the measurement association that publishes the Barcelona Principles, revised to version 4.0 in June 2025), the Page Society and agency trade bodies publish research, standards and benchmarks for the profession, much of it produced by volunteer commissions and academic partners.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically under 10 dedicated research staff per body (estimate), supplemented by volunteer commissions
**Output:** Standards and frameworks, member surveys, research papers, awards
**Proprietary data:** Member surveys and commission outputs
**Clock:** Annual conference and publication calendars
**Buyer:** Executive director
