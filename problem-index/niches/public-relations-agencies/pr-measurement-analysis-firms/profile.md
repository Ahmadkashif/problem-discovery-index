# PR Measurement & Media Analysis Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-relations-agencies|Public Relations Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Specialist firms and divisions that read and code a client's media coverage by hand and by machine and sell the analysis: message pull-through, tonality, spokesperson share, share of voice against competitors, and quarterly or annual reputation reports. Cision's insights business (which absorbed PRIME Research, described in Cision's filings as a leading global provider of media measurement and analysis with 700+ staff when acquired in 2018), Onclusive (1,100+ people, with a "Coding Operations" team of media analysts), Fullintel, Carma, Commetric and similar firms are representative. Corporate communications departments and agencies pay retainers for the reports, which go to chief communications officers and boards.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~50-300 media analysts and measurement consultants at the largest firms (estimate; PRIME Research's 700+ in 2018 and Onclusive's 1,100+ are whole-company counts, much of it coding production)
**Output:** Coded coverage datasets and analyst-written measurement reports per client, monthly to annually
**Proprietary data:** Years of human-coded coverage per client and sector against client message frameworks, with coder decisions and coding manuals; the underlying articles are licensed or public, but the coded layer exists nowhere else
**Clock:** Client reporting cycles (monthly, quarterly, board and budget reviews), plus earnings and annual-meeting calendars for listed clients; no regulator sets the deadline
**Buyer:** Head of measurement or chief research officer at the firm
