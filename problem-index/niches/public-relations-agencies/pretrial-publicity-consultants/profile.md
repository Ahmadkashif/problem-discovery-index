# Pretrial Publicity & Litigation Communications Consultants

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-relations-agencies|Public Relations Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Litigation communications specialists and the media-analysis practices of jury consulting firms that analyse coverage of high-profile cases, run community-attitude surveys for change-of-venue motions, and advise trial teams on public statements. Law firms pay them, usually under privilege.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 3-15 people doing media and venue analysis per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Media content analyses and venue surveys filed with motions, communications advice to trial teams
**Proprietary data:** Case files and surveys commissioned for specific matters
**Clock:** Court filing deadlines and trial dates
**Buyer:** Trial counsel
