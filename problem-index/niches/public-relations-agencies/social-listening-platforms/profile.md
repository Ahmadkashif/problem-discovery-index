# Social Listening & Consumer Intelligence Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-relations-agencies|Public Relations Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Platforms such as Brandwatch (owned by Cision), Talkwalker, Sprinklr and Meltwater's social products that collect public social media and forum posts and sell dashboards, alerts and analyst-built reports on brand conversation, sentiment and trends. PR agencies use them for issues monitoring, crisis response and campaign reporting.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 analysts, research consultants and data scientists per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Listening dashboards, alerts, trend and audience reports, custom analyst studies
**Proprietary data:** Historical archives of collected social posts and the vendor's classifiers; the posts are obtained under platform API licences
**Clock:** Client campaigns and crises; no external deadline
**Buyer:** Head of research services or product
