# AI Answer-Engine Visibility Trackers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-relations-agencies|Public Relations Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
A new category of vendors, including Muck Rack's Generative Pulse and a wave of start-ups, that query AI assistants with panels of buyer questions, record how brands appear and which sources are cited, and sell visibility reports to PR agencies, SEO teams and brands. Muck Rack's tracking finds more than 80% of AI-cited links are earned media, which is why PR firms now buy these reports.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-80 analysts and data staff at the larger vendors (estimate)
**Output:** Brand visibility and citation-share reports by question, assistant and competitor
**Proprietary data:** Longitudinal archives of AI answers and citations to fixed question panels, which cannot be reconstructed after the fact
**Clock:** Model and product releases by AI companies; no deadline on the work itself
**Buyer:** Head of research or product
