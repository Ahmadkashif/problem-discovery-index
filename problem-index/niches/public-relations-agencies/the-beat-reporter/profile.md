# The Beat Reporter

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-relations-agencies|Public Relations Agencies]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor serving the beat reporter is fighting to become the channel through which reporters choose to receive pitches and find sources, and whoever filters the inbox well enough to earn reporters' trust controls access to them for every PR firm.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$150M in spend on journalist-facing source-request, pitch-filtering and expert-sourcing services, mostly paid by the PR side (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1% of US PR agency receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High on the PR side, low on the reporter's side — reporters manage pitches with inbox rules and deletion
**Target Buyer:** Product heads at outreach and source-request platforms; paid by agencies and in-house teams
**Automation Potential:** High — filtering, matching and source vetting are well suited to models

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The entire PR industry is organised around a person its tools do not serve. Journalists receive the pitches, choose the sources and decide what runs, yet every media database, outreach tool and newswire is built for the sender. In Muck Rack's 2025 survey 55% of journalists got more than five pitches a day and only 3% said pitches always matched their beat. Source-request services (HARO, which Cision shut down as Connectively in December 2024 before Featured.com relaunched the brand in 2025, plus Qwoted, ProfNet and others) are the closest thing to a reporter-side product, and they now fight AI-generated source responses.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Muck Rack's journalist profiles and pitch preferences, source-request platforms, newsroom inbox rules, and personal lists of trusted sources. The gaps: no filter that ranks incoming pitches by fit to what the reporter is working on; no reliable vetting of whether a responding "expert" is real and qualified; and no feedback channel that tells senders why a pitch failed.
