# A Pitch Inbox That Ranks by What the Reporter Is Writing

**Niche:** [[niches/public-relations-agencies/the-beat-reporter/profile|The Beat Reporter]]
**Industry:** [[industries/public-relations-agencies|Public Relations Agencies]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Reporters delete most pitches unread because nothing sorts them by fit to the stories they are actually working on.
**Tags:** #transformers #word-embeddings #logistic-regression #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor serving the beat reporter is fighting to become the channel through which reporters choose to receive pitches and find sources, and whoever filters the inbox well enough to earn reporters' trust controls access to them for every PR firm.

## The Problem
A reporter covering climate technology gets dozens of pitches a day: most about unrelated sectors, some relevant to a piece she filed last month, a few useful for the story she is drafting now. She scans subject lines and deletes. The useful pitch is lost among the rest.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The paying customer in PR software is the sender. A reporter-side filter would expose how many pitches are irrelevant and could reduce the value of send-volume tools. Newsrooms rarely buy software for individual reporters.

## What to Build
An inbox layer, free to reporters, that ranks incoming pitches by similarity to the reporter's recent work and declared current topics, groups duplicates of the same news, and surfaces only embargoed or exclusive offers that fit. The reporter's open and reply actions train the ranking. Senders who opt in see an anonymised relevance score for their pitches, which becomes a quality signal the platform can sell to agencies.

## Target Customer
Paid by agencies and outreach platforms for the relevance signal; used free by reporters and newsrooms.

## Impact If Built
Reporters find the useful pitch in seconds, and senders learn which pitches land, which raises the relevance of the whole channel.
