# FARA Unit & Congressional Lobbying Disclosure Offices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-relations-agencies|Public Relations Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Justice Department's FARA Unit, which registers agents of foreign principals (many of them PR and public affairs firms), receives their informational materials within 48 hours of distribution and pursues non-compliance, and the House and Senate offices that receive Lobbying Disclosure Act registrations and reports. Their output is public disclosure and enforcement, not a paid product.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analysts (estimate); the 10-40 staff across the FARA Unit and the House and Senate disclosure offices (estimate) mostly process, review and publish filings, and three separate offices do not make one function
**Output:** Registrations, public filing databases, advisory opinions, enforcement referrals
**Proprietary data:** Complete filing histories of registrants, foreign principals, informational materials and lobbying contacts, published but poorly structured
**Clock:** Statutory filing deadlines (FARA's 48-hour rule for informational materials; quarterly lobbying reports)
**Buyer:** Unit chief
