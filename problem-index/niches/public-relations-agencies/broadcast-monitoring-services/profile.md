# Broadcast Monitoring Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-relations-agencies|Public Relations Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Services that record television, radio and podcast output, transcribe it and let PR teams search, alert on and clip mentions: TVEyes, Critical Mention (now part of Onclusive) and the broadcast arms of the large monitoring vendors. Agencies use them to prove broadcast coverage and to follow crises on air.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analysts per vendor (estimate); the 20-150 staff (estimate) are mostly transcription, clipping and QA production, and broadcast analysis is bought from the measurement firms in pocket 9
**Output:** Searchable transcripts, alerts, clips and broadcast coverage reports
**Proprietary data:** Archives of recorded broadcasts and transcripts across many markets; the content itself belongs to broadcasters
**Clock:** Broadcast schedules and clients' reporting cycles
**Buyer:** Head of product or operations

**Kill switches:** none live for analysis. In Fox News v. TVEyes (2d Cir., 2018) the court held that letting clients watch unaltered clips was not fair use, while the search function was not appealed; redistribution of content is constrained, analysis of transcripts is not.
**Verdict:** Logged — fails gate (staff are transcription and QA production rather than analysts; the product is access to clips and transcripts, and the analysis sits with the measurement firms).
