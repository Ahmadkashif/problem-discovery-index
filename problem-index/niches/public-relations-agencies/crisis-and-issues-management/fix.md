# The Crisis Plan Nobody Opens

## The Problem
Crisis plans contain the decision rights, contact trees, pre-approved holding statements and scenario playbooks a team needs. They are written once, refreshed rarely, and stored as PDFs. When a crisis starts, people call the counsellor they trust instead, and the pre-approved language is rewritten from scratch.

## Why It's Still Broken
The plan is a deliverable billed at the start of the retainer, not a working tool. Contact details and approvers go stale within months, and simulations test the people, not the document.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Turn the plan into a live checklist: the scenario is identified in the first minutes, the right playbook, approvers and holding statement appear on a phone, contacts are verified quarterly, and every simulation updates the plan with what failed.

## Who Feels the Pain
Client communications leads in the first hour of a crisis; agency counsellors redoing work that was already paid for; general counsel approving language under time pressure.

## Impact If Fixed
The first hour of a crisis starts from pre-approved positions and current contacts instead of a blank page.
