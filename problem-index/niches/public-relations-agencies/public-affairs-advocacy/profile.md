# Public Affairs & Issue Advocacy

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-relations-agencies|Public Relations Agencies]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show legislators and agencies verifiable support from real constituents and credible third parties before a vote or comment deadline, and whoever produces support that survives scrutiny for astroturfing takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2B in US fees for public affairs, coalition building and grassroots advocacy run by firms classified in NAICS 541820 (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% of US PR agency receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — advocacy platforms send letters, but coalition recruitment, third-party validators and legislator relationships run on phone calls, meetings and memory
**Target Buyer:** Head of government affairs or public policy at a corporate client; public affairs practice lead at the firm
**Automation Potential:** Low-medium — tracking, targeting and mobilisation logistics automate; credibility does not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
NAICS 541820 explicitly includes lobbying and political consulting establishments. Public affairs firms are hired by companies and coalitions to move a bill, a rule or a permit: they recruit allies, place op-eds, organise local business owners and patients to contact legislators, and brief staff. The clocks are external (committee votes, comment periods, session deadlines), and the audience is small and sceptical: legislative staff discount form letters and spot manufactured campaigns. Firms representing foreign principals also face FARA registration and its 48-hour filing rule for informational materials. Candidate campaigns and PACs are a separate queued industry; this niche is the hired firm working an issue for a corporate or coalition client.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Bill and regulatory tracking from FiscalNote, Quorum and Bloomberg Government (the tracking vendors are analysed under [[niches/trade-associations/legislative-tracking-political-intelligence/profile|Trade Associations: Legislative Tracking & Political Intelligence]]); advocacy action platforms such as VoterVoice; polling vendors for message testing. The gaps: no record of which validators and which constituent contacts actually moved which offices, and no check that a mobilisation will read as authentic to the staffer receiving it.
