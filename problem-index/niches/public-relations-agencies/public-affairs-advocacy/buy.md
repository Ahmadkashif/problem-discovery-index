# Fundraising CRMs Rebuilt for Coalitions

**Niche:** [[niches/public-relations-agencies/public-affairs-advocacy/profile|Public Affairs & Issue Advocacy]]
**Industry:** [[industries/public-relations-agencies|Public Relations Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Nonprofit and campaign CRMs already track supporters, asks and actions; coalition campaigns run on spreadsheets of member organisations and validators.
**Tags:** #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #k-nearest-neighbors #evaluation-metrics #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show legislators and agencies verifiable support from real constituents and credible third parties before a vote or comment deadline, and whoever produces support that survives scrutiny for astroturfing takes the account.

## The Problem
A coalition campaign has dozens of member organisations, hundreds of validators (local business owners, clinicians, former officials), and thousands of supporters, each with a home district, a relationship to particular offices, and a history of what they agreed to do. The firm tracks this in spreadsheets per campaign.

## What Already Exists
Supporter CRMs and action platforms used by nonprofits and campaigns (Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud, EveryAction, NationBuilder) track contacts, asks, actions and donations, with district matching.

## The Customization Gap
Public affairs needs: organisations and individuals in one graph with their district and office relationships; validator credibility and consent records (what each agreed to sign or say, and where); action provenance for authenticity (each contact traceable to a real constituent, defending against astroturf accusations); client confidentiality between coalitions; and FARA and lobbying-disclosure flags where a foreign principal or reportable contact is involved. Campaign CRMs assume one organisation mobilising its own supporters.

## Target Customer
Public affairs firms running multi-member coalitions for corporate clients.

## Impact If Solved
Coalition work that is traceable and reusable across campaigns, built on CRMs the field already knows.
