# The Volume Alert That Cried Wolf

**Niche:** [[niches/public-relations-agencies/social-flashpoint-response/profile|Social Media Flashpoint Response]]
**Industry:** [[industries/public-relations-agencies|Public Relations Agencies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Listening alerts set on mention volume fire so often that teams mute them, and the one that mattered arrives muted.
**Tags:** #change-point-detection #evaluation-metrics #descriptive-statistics #quick-win #worker-facing #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to call, within the first hours, whether an online flare-up will reach mainstream media and whether responding will feed it or kill it, and whoever calls escalation right most often keeps the client's crisis retainer.

## The Problem
An agency sets a spike alert for each client at a round number of mentions per hour. Campaign launches, sports sponsorships and unrelated namesakes trip it every week. After a month the alerts go to a folder nobody reads.

## Why It's Still Broken
Nobody records which alerts were real and which were noise, so thresholds are never tuned, and nobody owns the alert configuration once the account team that set it moves on.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Ask the watcher to mark each alert "real issue / noise / campaign" in one tap, use those labels to tune thresholds and exclusions per client monthly, and report alert precision to the account lead so it has an owner.

## Who Feels the Pain
Overnight watchers drowning in alerts; senior counsellors woken for nothing; clients whose real issue was missed.

## Impact If Fixed
Alert precision that rises month on month from labels the team already has in its head, at almost no cost.
