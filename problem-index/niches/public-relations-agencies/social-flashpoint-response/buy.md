**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to call, within the first hours, whether an online flare-up will reach mainstream media and whether responding will feed it or kill it, and whoever calls escalation right most often keeps the client's crisis retainer.

## The Problem
An issue's mention count rises. The watcher must decide whether this is a genuine departure from the brand's normal background or the daily noise of a large brand. Thresholds set by hand are either too sensitive (alerts every night) or too slow.

## What Already Exists
Syndromic surveillance systems in public health use change-point and aberration detection (CUSUM, EARS-style methods, Bayesian outbreak models) and growth-rate estimates to flag outbreaks from early counts against seasonal baselines. Listening vendors provide basic spike alerts.

## The Customization Gap
Reputation surveillance needs: brand-specific baselines with weekly and campaign seasonality; separation of owned-campaign volume from organic complaint; multi-stream signals (social, news, search, review sites) combined; amplifier-weighted counts rather than raw posts; and alert thresholds tuned to the counsellor's tolerance for false alarms. The epidemiological methods are proven; the inputs and baselines are new.

## Target Customer
Agency social and crisis teams, and listening vendors seeking better alert precision.

## Impact If Solved
Alerts that fire on real departures from a brand's normal, using methods with decades of validation in a field that faced the same problem.
