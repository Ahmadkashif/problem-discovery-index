# Reach Evidence for Unmeasured In-Language Outlets

**Niche:** [[niches/public-relations-agencies/multicultural-in-language-media/profile|Multicultural & In-Language Media Relations]]
**Industry:** [[industries/public-relations-agencies|Public Relations Agencies]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Multicultural campaigns are funded on the promise of reaching specific language communities, and agencies cannot show who heard the message because the outlets that carry it have no audited audiences.
**Tags:** #bayesian-inference #causal-inference #transformers #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to prove to a public-health, utility or government client that its message was actually picked up and heard in in-language communities where outlets have no audited audiences and commercial monitoring sees little, and whoever can evidence that reach credibly takes the account.

## The Problem
A state health department funds a vaccine campaign in Vietnamese, Haitian Creole and Spanish media across three metro areas, and its grant report must say how many people in each community were reached. The agency knows the radio segments ran, the community papers printed the story and the messaging-app newsletters reposted it. It has no audience figures for most of those outlets, so the report multiplies guesses, or falls back on the few Spanish-language broadcasters that are measured and understates everything else.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Audience measurement firms do not panel small in-language outlets; monitoring vendors do not capture most of them; and the outcomes that would show impact (hotline calls, clinic visits, programme sign-ups by preferred language) sit in the client's systems, rarely joined to media timing.

## What to Build
A reach-evidence model per campaign: confirmed pickup assembled from transcribed air checks, print scans and reposts matched to the pitch log; outlet-level reach estimated from proxies (broadcast signal coverage, follower geography and language, circulation statements, community survey recall), with intervals rather than point figures; and, where the client shares it, language-coded outcome series tested against pickup timing with placebo checks. The output is a defensible reach-and-response statement per community that a grant officer or auditor can follow.

## Target Customer
Multicultural agencies and the multicultural practices of general agencies serving public health departments, utilities and government programmes.

## Impact If Built
Campaign reports that state reach per community with stated uncertainty, which protects renewals and grant funding that now rest on figures nobody can defend.
