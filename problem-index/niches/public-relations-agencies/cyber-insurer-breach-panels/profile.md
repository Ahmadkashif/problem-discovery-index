# Cyber Insurers' Breach Response Panels

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-relations-agencies|Public Relations Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Cyber insurers maintain panels of approved incident-response vendors, including crisis communications firms, and pay for their work under breach-response coverage, usually engaged through a breach coach law firm. Their claims and panel teams analyse incident costs and vendor performance to price policies and manage panels.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 claims analysts and panel managers per large cyber insurer (estimate)
**Output:** Internal claims analytics, panel vendor selection and rate negotiation, pricing inputs
**Proprietary data:** Incident and claims records across thousands of policyholders, including communications spend and outcomes
**Clock:** Incident timelines and statutory notification deadlines; renewal cycles
**Buyer:** Head of cyber claims
