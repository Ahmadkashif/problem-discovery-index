# Public Affairs Polling & Message-Testing Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-relations-agencies|Public Relations Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Opinion research firms that poll voters, stakeholders and elite audiences for corporate public affairs campaigns, coalitions and PR firms, and test which messages persuade: who supports a bill, which arguments move them, which messengers they trust. Many also work for candidates and parties, which sits with the separately queued political campaigns industry.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 researchers and analysts per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Polls, message-testing studies, focus groups, legislative-district opinion profiles
**Proprietary data:** Archives of question wordings, message tests and results over many issues and cycles; most fieldwork is bought from panel operators and most studies are owned by the commissioning client
**Clock:** Votes, comment deadlines and election dates that the poll must precede
**Buyer:** Founding partner or head of research
