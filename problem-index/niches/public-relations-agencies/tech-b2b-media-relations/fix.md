# The Funding Announcement Pitched to Three Hundred Reporters

**Niche:** [[niches/public-relations-agencies/tech-b2b-media-relations/profile|Technology & B2B Media Relations]]
**Industry:** [[industries/public-relations-agencies|Public Relations Agencies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Startup clients equate list size with effort, so funding news goes to hundreds of reporters who never cover rounds of that size, and the agency's next pitch to them is deleted unread.
**Tags:** #logistic-regression #evaluation-metrics #descriptive-statistics #quick-win #worker-facing #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn client news that reporters treat as interchangeable (another AI launch, another funding round, another survey) into a data- or customer-led story that a named beat reporter will run, and whoever matches angle to reporter best takes the account.

## The Problem
A seed or Series A announcement is the most common tech PR job. The client wants to see a big list; the database returns everyone tagged "venture capital" or "startups"; the pitch goes out under embargo to hundreds. Most of those reporters cover rounds above a certain size, a specific sector or a region, and say so through what they publish. Muck Rack's 2025 survey found 86% of journalists disregard irrelevant pitches.

## Why It's Still Broken
List size is visible to the client and relevance is not. Nobody shows the client, before sending, how many of the listed reporters have covered a comparable round in the past year, and nobody records afterwards which reporters ignored the agency for good.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Before any send, score each listed reporter on whether they covered comparable news (round size, stage, sector, region) in the past 12 months, cut the list to those who did plus deliberate long shots, and show the client the trimmed list with the evidence. Record ignore and unsubscribe outcomes per reporter so the next client's list starts cleaner.

## Who Feels the Pain
Account executives sending the volume; reporters receiving it; and the agency's future clients, whose pitches arrive from an address reporters have learned to ignore.

## Impact If Fixed
Shorter lists with equal or better coverage, and a measurable relevance record the agency can show clients instead of list length.
