# Sales Intent Data Pointed at Newsrooms

**Niche:** [[niches/public-relations-agencies/tech-b2b-media-relations/profile|Technology & B2B Media Relations]]
**Industry:** [[industries/public-relations-agencies|Public Relations Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** B2B sales teams already buy intent and timing signals that say which prospect is in market this week; PR teams pitch reporters with no equivalent signal of who is working on what.
**Tags:** #word-embeddings #k-nearest-neighbors #time-series-forecasting #data-integration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn client news that reporters treat as interchangeable (another AI launch, another funding round, another survey) into a data- or customer-led story that a named beat reporter will run, and whoever matches angle to reporter best takes the account.

## The Problem
Timing decides tech coverage. A reporter who published a piece on AI security budgets yesterday is unlikely to take another this week but may be building a follow-up; a reporter whose recent bylines show a shift from consumer apps to enterprise infrastructure is newly relevant. PR teams find this out by reading feeds by hand, if at all.

## What Already Exists
Sales intent platforms (Bombora, 6sense, G2 buyer intent) and sales engagement tools (Outreach, Salesloft) score accounts by recent research activity and sequence outreach accordingly. Muck Rack and Cision provide reporter alerts on new articles.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: intent defined from bylines rather than web research (topic acceleration, beat drift, follow-up patterns, social posts asking for sources); entity resolution for reporters across outlets and newsletters; embargo-aware sequencing rather than sales cadences; and suppression rules that protect the relationship (no more than one pitch per reporter per client per fortnight, no pitch within days of a hostile piece). Sales tools optimise for volume; PR must optimise for not burning the contact.

## Target Customer
Tech agency media teams and in-house tech PR leads running weekly announcements.

## Impact If Solved
Pitches timed to when a reporter is working the topic, with fewer sends and fewer unsubscribes, borrowed from tooling B2B sales teams already trust.
