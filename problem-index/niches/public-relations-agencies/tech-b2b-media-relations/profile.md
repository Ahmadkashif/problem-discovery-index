# Technology & B2B Media Relations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-relations-agencies|Public Relations Agencies]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn client news that reporters treat as interchangeable (another AI launch, another funding round, another survey) into a data- or customer-led story that a named beat reporter will run, and whoever matches angle to reporter best takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$4B in US agency fees from technology, software and B2B practices (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~25% of US PR agency receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High — outreach, monitoring and analyst-relations tools are standard, but story-to-reporter matching is still done from memory
**Target Buyer:** Head of media relations or practice lead at a tech-focused agency
**Automation Potential:** Medium — list building and drafting automate; the angle and the reporter relationship do not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Technology and B2B clients announce often (product releases, funding rounds, partnerships, customer wins, research reports) and most of that news looks the same to the small pool of reporters who cover it. Startups judge agencies within a quarter on whether they land a tier-one business or trade story around a launch. The craft that wins is finding the angle (proprietary data, a named customer, a contrarian executive view) that turns a commodity announcement into something one specific reporter wants this week, and protecting the agency's credibility with that reporter for the next client.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Muck Rack, Cision and Meltwater for lists, outreach and monitoring; analyst-relations tools for Gartner and Forrester briefings; survey vendors for data stories; newswires for formal releases. The gaps: no tool ranks reporters by fit to a specific angle using what they actually wrote and answered; the agency's own pitch-and-response history is not joined to coverage; and nobody tells the account team which "data story" formats a reporter has run versus ignored.
