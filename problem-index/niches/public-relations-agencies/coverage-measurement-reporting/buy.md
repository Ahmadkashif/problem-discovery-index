# Marketing Analytics Dashboards Re-Cut for Earned Media

**Niche:** [[niches/public-relations-agencies/coverage-measurement-reporting/profile|Coverage Monitoring & Measurement Reporting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/public-relations-agencies|Public Relations Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Paid media teams already report from automated dashboards that join spend to outcomes; PR reporting starts each month from an export and a blank deck.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #causal-inference #time-series-forecasting #data-integration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce a de-duplicated, message-coded coverage report that a chief communications officer will defend to the CFO without advertising value equivalents, and whoever does it with the least analyst time per client keeps the measurement line.

## The Problem
The client's marketing team sees paid search, social and email results in a live dashboard joined to site visits and leads. The PR agency sends a monthly PDF of clips and reach. Leadership compares the two and PR looks unaccountable.

## What Already Exists
Marketing reporting platforms such as AgencyAnalytics, Whatagraph, Supermetrics with Looker Studio, and Tableau connect to ad platforms and web analytics, automate monthly reports and add AI-written commentary. Client reporting for paid agencies is analysed under [[niches/marketing-agencies-smb/client-reporting-attribution/profile|SMB Marketing Agencies: Client Reporting and ROI Attribution]].

## The Customization Gap
Earned media reporting needs: monitoring connectors with syndication collapse and false-match removal; outlet tiering against the client's priority list; message pull-through and spokesperson fields; Barcelona Principles 4.0-style outcome metrics rather than AVEs; coverage-window effects on site visits and branded search; and AI-answer citation tracking. The dashboard tools have the connectors and the automation; they lack the earned-media data model.

## Target Customer
Agencies whose clients already run paid-media dashboards and want PR on the same screen.

## Impact If Solved
PR reported alongside paid channels in the client's own tools, on comparable outcome measures.
