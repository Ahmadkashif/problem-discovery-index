# Agency Research & Analytics Units

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-relations-agencies|Public Relations Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The in-house research, data and analytics units of the largest PR firms and holding-company PR groups: Edelman's Data & Intelligence (DXI) unit, which runs the annual Trust Barometer (33,938 respondents in 28 countries for 2026), and the research and analytics arms of FleishmanHillard, Weber Shandwick, Burson and their peers. They run surveys, audience analysis and measurement for client engagements and produce thought-leadership studies that market the firm.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-400 researchers, analysts and data scientists per large firm or group (estimate)
**Output:** Client-commissioned audience and opinion research, campaign analytics, flagship studies such as the Trust Barometer
**Proprietary data:** Flagship longitudinal studies the firm owns, plus a much larger body of client-commissioned research it does not
**Clock:** Client campaign and planning cycles; flagship launches timed to self-chosen events
**Buyer:** Chief research or data officer at the firm
