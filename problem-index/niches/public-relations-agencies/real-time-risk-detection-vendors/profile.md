# Real-Time Issues & Risk Detection Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-relations-agencies|Public Relations Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors such as Dataminr, Signal AI and Factal that scan news, social media and other public sources and alert corporate communications, security and risk teams to emerging events and issues affecting a company, often minutes after they surface. Agencies use them on crisis retainers; corporate teams buy them directly.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 analysts, domain experts and data scientists per large vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Real-time alerts, issue summaries, risk dashboards
**Proprietary data:** Event and alert histories across many clients, with detection models; the source content is public or licensed from platforms and publishers
**Clock:** The events themselves; no deadline set on the vendor's work
**Buyer:** Chief product officer or head of analysis
