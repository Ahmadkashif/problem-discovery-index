# Newswire Editorial & Compliance Desks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-relations-agencies|Public Relations Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The editorial operations at PR Newswire, Business Wire, GlobeNewswire and smaller wires that review, format and time-release press releases, including earnings and other market-moving news for listed companies, and report pickup to the sender. Companies and agencies pay per release or by subscription for distribution and disclosure-grade timing.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analysts per wire (estimate); the 50-300 staff on the desks (estimate) are editors and compliance reviewers formatting and time-releasing releases, which is production, not an insight function
**Output:** Distributed releases with verified timing; pickup and engagement reports
**Proprietary data:** Complete archives of releases with exact release times and downstream pickup across outlets and sites
**Clock:** Market open and close, issuers' earnings dates and exchange notification rules for material news
**Buyer:** Head of editorial operations or product
