# Corporate Reputation Tracking Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-relations-agencies|Public Relations Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms that survey the public and stakeholders continuously about large companies' reputations and sell trackers, rankings and driver analysis to corporate communications teams and their agencies. The RepTrak Company is the best-known specialist; brand and reputation trackers from YouGov, Morning Consult and Ipsos compete for the same budgets. Chief communications officers use the scores to set targets, and agencies are increasingly judged against them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 researchers, methodologists and client analysts per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Reputation scores and rankings, driver models (which attributes move trust and support), stakeholder studies
**Proprietary data:** Long-running reputation tracker histories across thousands of companies and countries; much fieldwork runs on panels rented from survey operators
**Clock:** Annual ranking publications and clients' quarterly and annual reviews; nothing set by a regulator
**Buyer:** Chief research officer
