# Insurance Loss Research Institutes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-body-shops|Auto Body Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Insurance Institute for Highway Safety and its affiliated Highway Loss Data Institute conduct crash testing, publish safety ratings, and analyze insurance loss experience by vehicle model. Insurers fund the work; the output is published freely and shapes vehicle design, consumer purchasing, and repair procedure requirements across the industry.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-200 researchers, engineers, and statisticians
**Output:** Crash test ratings, loss experience reports, published research
**Proprietary data:** Insurance loss experience pooled across nearly every major US auto insurer, joined to vehicle configuration — a dataset no individual insurer can assemble
**Clock:** Model year test programs and publication cycles, largely self-set
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — funded by member insurers
