## What They Do
The Society of Collision Repair Specialists and state associations research reimbursement practices, insurer behaviour, and regulatory developments to support advocacy and member education. Output is member communication and legislative testimony rather than a sold product.

**Output:** Position papers, member surveys, legislative testimony
**Proprietary data:** Member survey responses on reimbursement and insurer practices
**Clock:** Legislative sessions and regulatory comment periods
