# Collision Estimating Data Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-body-shops|Auto Body Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
CCC Intelligent Solutions, Mitchell (Enlyte), and Audatex (Solera) publish the databases every collision estimate in the country is written against — labor times by operation and vehicle, parts identification and pricing, refinish formulas, and repair-versus-replace logic. Research staff build and maintain those entries vehicle by vehicle, and insurers and shops license the result. The number a shop and an insurer argue over is a number one of these firms published.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-1,500 data researchers, estimators, and analysts
**Output:** The estimating database itself — labor times, parts data, procedure logic — plus industry trend research
**Proprietary data:** Decades of written estimates, supplements, and claim outcomes flowing through a platform that carries a large share of US collision claims; nobody else observes the gap between estimated and actual repair at this scale
**Clock:** Model year introductions, OEM parts and procedure updates, monthly data releases
**Buyer:** Chief Product Officer / VP of Data & Analytics
