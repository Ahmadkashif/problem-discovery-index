# Aftermarket Parts Certification Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-body-shops|Auto Body Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
CAPA and comparable certifiers test aftermarket collision parts against OEM equivalents — fit, material, coating, corrosion resistance — and certify those that pass. Insurers specify certified parts in repair agreements, so the certification decision directly determines which parts get used on millions of repairs.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-30 engineers, test coordinators, and inspectors
**Output:** Certification decisions and test reports
**Proprietary data:** Comparative test results across aftermarket manufacturers and part types, accumulated over decades
**Clock:** Certification renewal cycles and factory audit schedules
**Buyer:** Technical Director / Executive Director
