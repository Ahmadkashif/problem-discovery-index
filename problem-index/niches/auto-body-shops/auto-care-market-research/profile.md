# Aftermarket Association Market Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-body-shops|Auto Body Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Auto Care Association and comparable bodies produce the aftermarket's market sizing, vehicle population forecasts, and channel demand studies, and maintain the ACES and PIES data standards that let parts data move between trading partners. Members receive research as a benefit; non-members buy reports.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-30 researchers, data standards staff, and analysts
**Output:** Market forecasts, demand studies, data standards documentation
**Proprietary data:** Member-contributed sales and channel data plus long-run vehicle population modelling
**Clock:** Annual research calendar and standards release cycles, mostly self-set
**Buyer:** VP of Research / Chief Technology Officer
