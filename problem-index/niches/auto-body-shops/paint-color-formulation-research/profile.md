# Automotive Paint Colour Formulation Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-body-shops|Auto Body Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The colour science groups at Axalta, PPG, BASF, and Sherwin-Williams Automotive. They obtain every OEM colour, measure it spectrophotometrically, and publish refinish formulas — plus the variant formulas that account for how the same factory colour drifts across plants, model years, and weathering. Body shops match paint against these formulas; the research ships with the paint rather than being invoiced separately.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-300 colour scientists, formulators, and technicians globally
**Output:** Colour formula databases, variant libraries, colour tools
**Proprietary data:** Millions of spectrophotometer readings returned from shops in the field, mapping how each OEM colour actually varies in the wild — a dataset no OEM or competitor holds
**Clock:** Model year colour launches and refinish system releases
**Buyer:** Director of Colour Technology / VP Refinish
