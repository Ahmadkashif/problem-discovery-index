# Collision Repair Training & Repairability Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-body-shops|Auto Body Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
I-CAR develops the training curriculum and technical standards the collision industry certifies against, and runs a Repairability Technical Support function that researches OEM procedures, resolves technician inquiries, and publishes bulletins on new vehicle construction and repair methods. Shops buy training and subscriptions; insurers use I-CAR credentials as a network requirement.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-300 curriculum developers, technical researchers, and instructors
**Output:** Training curriculum, technical bulletins, repairability inquiry responses
**Proprietary data:** The accumulated inquiry record — years of specific questions technicians could not resolve from OEM documentation, which is a direct map of where repair information fails
**Clock:** New vehicle introductions and OEM procedure changes
**Buyer:** VP of Technical Programs / Director of Curriculum
