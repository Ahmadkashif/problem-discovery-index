# Total Loss Valuation Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-body-shops|Auto Body Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
When damage exceeds a threshold the vehicle is totalled and someone must state what it was worth. CCC, Mitchell, and J.D. Power Valuation Services produce the valuation report that settles the claim — comparable vehicle selection, condition adjustments, options and mileage adjustments, and a defended market value. The report is the deliverable, it is regulated in several states, and it is routinely challenged by policyholders and their attorneys.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 valuation analysts, data researchers, and statisticians
**Output:** The valuation report and its comparable set — the billable artefact
**Proprietary data:** Vehicle transaction and listing history joined to settlement outcomes and challenge results, built over decades
**Clock:** Claim settlement deadlines and state prompt-payment statutes
**Buyer:** VP of Valuation Products / Chief Analytics Officer
