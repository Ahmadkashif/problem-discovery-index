# OEM Certification Program Administrators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-body-shops|Auto Body Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Assured Performance, VeriFacts, and the OEM network administrators audit body shops against manufacturer repair standards — equipment, training, procedure adherence — and issue the certification that lets a shop appear on an OEM's approved repairer list. The audit finding is the invoice, and it controls the shop's access to a referral stream.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-60 auditors, technical reviewers, and program analysts
**Output:** Audit findings and certification decisions
**Proprietary data:** Standardized capability and compliance data across thousands of shops nationally, refreshed annually — the only such view that exists
**Clock:** Annual recertification cycles and OEM program requirement updates
**Buyer:** VP of Programs / Director of Certification
