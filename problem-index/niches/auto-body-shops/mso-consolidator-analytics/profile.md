# Collision MSO Consolidator Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-body-shops|Auto Body Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The corporate development and operations analytics groups inside the large multi-shop operators — Caliber Collision, Gerber, Crash Champions, Classic Collision. They screen and model acquisitions, benchmark performance across hundreds or thousands of locations, negotiate insurer agreements on the strength of network data, and run cycle time and severity analytics.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-100 analysts across corp dev, pricing, and operations
**Output:** Acquisition models, network performance analytics, insurer negotiation analyses
**Proprietary data:** Repair-level data across a thousand-plus location network — technician productivity, cycle time, supplement rates, parts sourcing — at a scale no independent shop or insurer sees from the inside
**Clock:** Deal timelines and insurer agreement renewal cycles
**Buyer:** SVP Corporate Development / VP Business Analytics
