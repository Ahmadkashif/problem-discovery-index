# Diminished Value & Independent Appraisal Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-body-shops|Auto Body Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Independent appraisers who quantify the value a repaired vehicle lost by virtue of having been damaged, and who serve as umpires in appraisal clause disputes between insurers and policyholders. The written appraisal is the product and is used in negotiation and litigation.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1-5 per firm
**Output:** Diminished value appraisals and umpire decisions
**Proprietary data:** The appraiser's own case history — small, personal, and unshared
**Clock:** Claim and litigation deadlines
**Buyer:** The appraiser, who is usually the whole firm
