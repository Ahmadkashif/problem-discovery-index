# Education Market Research & District Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tutoring-centers|Tutoring Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Track district and school spending, programme adoption, enrolment and staffing, and sell it to the providers trying to sell into education — the market map behind every district contract a tutoring provider chases.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 researchers, data operations staff, and analysts
**Output:** District and school profiles, adoption and spending tracking, market sizing and forecasts, purchaser surveys, contact data
**Proprietary data:** District-level programme and spending records assembled from budgets, board minutes, procurement filings and surveys across thousands of districts
**Clock:** Budget and adoption cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Research
