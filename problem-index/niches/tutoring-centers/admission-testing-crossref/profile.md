# Admission Testing Organizations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tutoring-centers|Tutoring Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Develop and administer the admission examinations a large part of the tutoring industry exists to prepare students for, and set the scales and score reports admission decisions are made against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-250 psychometricians, item writers, research scientists, and operations staff
**Output:** Examinations and item banks, scoring and equating, score reports, validity and fairness research, concordance studies
**Proprietary data:** Item response data across large candidate cohorts, joined in some programmes to subsequent academic performance
**Clock:** Fixed test administration dates and admission cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Assessment Officer / VP of Research
