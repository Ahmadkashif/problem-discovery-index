# Adaptive Assessment Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tutoring-centers|Tutoring Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Build the adaptive diagnostic assessments schools and tutoring providers place students with — measuring where a learner actually is, on a vertical scale, and generating the growth expectations progress is judged against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 150-700 psychometricians, item developers, research scientists, and content specialists
**Output:** Adaptive item banks and scales, placement and growth reporting, norms and growth projections, instructional recommendations, validity research
**Proprietary data:** Item response data across tens of millions of test events, plus longitudinal growth trajectories on a stable vertical scale
**Clock:** School assessment windows, three or more times a year
**Buyer:** Chief Research Officer / VP of Psychometrics
