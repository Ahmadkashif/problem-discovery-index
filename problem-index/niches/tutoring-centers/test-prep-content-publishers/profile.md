# Test Preparation Content Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tutoring-centers|Tutoring Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Produce the practice materials, item banks and strategy content the test preparation half of tutoring runs on, reverse-engineering examination specifications from published frameworks and released items.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 content developers, item writers, and editors
**Output:** Practice tests and item banks, strategy content, score prediction from practice performance, curriculum aligned to examination frameworks
**Proprietary data:** Practice item performance data joined, for some providers, to reported official scores
**Clock:** Test administration dates and examination redesigns
**Buyer:** VP of Content
