# State Education Agency Programme Administration

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tutoring-centers|Tutoring Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Administer the state assessment systems and the federal programme funding that pays for a large share of school-contracted tutoring, and evaluate whether the programmes worked.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 across the larger state agencies — assessment staff, programme administrators, and research analysts
**Output:** State assessment administration and reporting, programme eligibility and monitoring, accountability determinations, evaluation studies
**Proprietary data:** Statewide longitudinal student data linking assessment, enrolment, programme participation and outcomes
**Clock:** Assessment windows, federal reporting deadlines, and funding cycles
**Buyer:** Assessment or programme director

**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold and doubly fenced. Statewide longitudinal data systems link every student's assessment history to programme participation, which is the only place tutoring's effect could actually be measured at scale, behind the strongest access wall in education.
