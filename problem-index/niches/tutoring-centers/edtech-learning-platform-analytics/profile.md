# Learning Platform Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tutoring-centers|Tutoring Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The data science functions inside the adaptive learning and practice platforms tutoring centres assign work through, modelling mastery, sequencing content and reporting progress.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 learning scientists, data scientists, and content analysts
**Output:** Knowledge state and mastery estimation, content sequencing, progress and growth reporting, engagement analytics, efficacy claims
**Proprietary data:** Billions of item responses with response times and hint usage, at fine granularity across long student trajectories
**Clock:** School year and assignment cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Learning Science / Head of Data
