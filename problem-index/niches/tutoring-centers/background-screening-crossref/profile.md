# Background Screening Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tutoring-centers|Tutoring Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Screen the adults who will be alone with children — criminal history, sex offender registries, identity and credential verification — at the volume a high-turnover, largely part-time tutoring workforce generates.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-2,000 court researchers, verification specialists, and compliance analysts
**Output:** Criminal record reports, registry checks, identity and credential verification, continuous monitoring, adjudication guidance
**Proprietary data:** Court record acquisition infrastructure across thousands of jurisdictions, plus accumulated adjudication decisions
**Clock:** Hiring and school-year start dates
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Compliance
