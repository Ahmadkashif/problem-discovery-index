# Education Efficacy & Evidence Review

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tutoring-centers|Tutoring Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Review and rate the evidence behind instructional interventions — including tutoring — against methodological standards, and publish the ratings that determine whether a programme can be funded from certain public sources.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 researchers, methodologists, and reviewers
**Output:** Evidence reviews and effectiveness ratings, methodological standards, practice guides, meta-analyses
**Proprietary data:** Structured extraction and quality rating of a large intervention literature
**Clock:** Review cycles and funding eligibility determinations
**Buyer:** Director of Research
