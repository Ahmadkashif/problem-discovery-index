# Curriculum & Instructional Materials Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tutoring-centers|Tutoring Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Develop the instructional programmes and intervention curricula tutoring providers deliver — scope and sequence, item and task design, teacher guidance — and generate the efficacy evidence districts and franchisors require before adopting them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 curriculum designers, learning scientists, editors, and efficacy researchers
**Output:** Instructional programmes and intervention sequences, placement and progress monitoring tools, efficacy studies, implementation guidance
**Proprietary data:** Item and task banks with usage and performance data, plus efficacy study results across implementations
**Clock:** Adoption cycles and school year calendars
**Buyer:** Chief Academic Officer / VP of Learning Science
