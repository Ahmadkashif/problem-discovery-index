# Tutoring Franchise Corporate Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tutoring-centers|Tutoring Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator/rollup

## What They Do
Corporate analytics inside the tutoring franchise systems — enrolment funnel, retention, centre economics, instructor productivity and territory planning across hundreds of centres.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 analysts and franchise business consultants
**Output:** Enrolment and retention analytics, centre performance benchmarks, pricing guidance, territory and site selection, curriculum utilisation reporting
**Proprietary data:** Student enrolment, attendance, assessment and retention records across the franchise base
**Clock:** School year enrolment cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Franchise Performance
