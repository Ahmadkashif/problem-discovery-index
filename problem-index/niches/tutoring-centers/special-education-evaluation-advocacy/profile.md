# Educational Evaluation & Special Education Advocacy

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tutoring-centers|Tutoring Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Conduct independent psychoeducational evaluations and advocate for services in school placement disputes — the assessments that determine whether a child receives support and what kind, and that are argued over at hearings.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 educational psychologists, evaluators, and advocates across the practices
**Output:** Psychoeducational evaluations, diagnostic determinations, service recommendations, hearing testimony, programme placement analysis
**Proprietary data:** Evaluation results joined to placement outcomes, held per matter
**Clock:** Statutory evaluation and hearing timelines
**Buyer:** Practice Director
