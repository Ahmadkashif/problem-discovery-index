# Online Tutoring Marketplace Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tutoring-centers|Tutoring Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator/rollup

## What They Do
Match students to tutors, deliver sessions in their own platform, and run the matching, quality and pricing analytics over millions of recorded tutoring hours.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 data scientists, learning analysts, and trust and safety staff
**Output:** Tutor matching and ranking, session quality scoring, pricing and demand forecasting, engagement and outcome reporting
**Proprietary data:** Millions of recorded sessions with transcripts, tutor and student attributes, and continuation and satisfaction outcomes — the largest corpus of one-to-one instruction that has ever existed
**Clock:** Academic calendars and examination dates
**Buyer:** VP of Data Science
