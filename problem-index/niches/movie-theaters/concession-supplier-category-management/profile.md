# Concession Supplier Category Management

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/movie-theaters|Movie Theaters]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The beverage and snack majors and the foodservice distributors that supply theatre concessions run small cinema-channel account and category teams. They negotiate pouring rights and volume rebates with circuits (Cinemark says it negotiates prices and rebates directly with suppliers), recommend assortments, combos and promotions tied to releases, and track sell-in by circuit.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 category and channel analysts per supplier on the cinema channel (estimate); account managers who sell the contract are not counted
**Output:** Category reviews, assortment and combo recommendations, promotional plans tied to the release calendar
**Proprietary data:** Sell-in and rebate data by circuit and site; sell-through sits in the exhibitors' point-of-sale systems
**Clock:** Annual supply contract cycles and promotional calendars around tentpole releases
**Buyer:** Head of cinema or entertainment channel at the supplier
