# Online Ticketing Aggregators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/movie-theaters|Movie Theaters]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Fandango and Atom Tickets sell movie tickets across circuits for a convenience fee and sell advertising and release marketing to distributors. Their presale data by title and market is watched as an early demand signal. The large circuits increasingly sell direct: about 87% of AMC's online tickets went through AMC's own channels in 2025. Fandango also runs ClubBuy, the purchasing programme behind the Independent Cinema Alliance's Marketplace.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~15-40 analysts and data scientists at Fandango, fewer at Atom (estimate); ad sales and studio partnership staff are not counted
**Output:** Presale and demand analytics for distributor clients, moviegoer surveys, media and marketing performance reports
**Proprietary data:** Cross-circuit presale transactions by title, theatre and showtime, and fan behaviour on the app and site
**Clock:** The distributors' release dates and presale openings
**Buyer:** Distributor marketing and distribution executives
