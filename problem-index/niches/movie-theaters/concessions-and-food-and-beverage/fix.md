# The Line Nobody Measures

**Niche:** [[niches/movie-theaters/concessions-and-food-and-beverage/profile|Concessions & Food and Beverage]]
**Industry:** [[industries/movie-theaters|Movie Theaters]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Theatres track per-caps to the cent and never measure how many patrons looked at the line and walked into the movie without buying.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #evaluation-metrics #feature-engineering #quick-win #revenue-impact #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to raise food and beverage spend per patron in the few minutes between ticket scan and trailers without letting the line run past showtime, and whoever sells the most per head at the counter, kiosk, app or seat while holding service time takes the account.

## The Problem
Per-capita spend averages every patron, including those who never bought because the line was too long. Managers staff the counter by habit, and the cost of an under-staffed peak never appears in any report.

## Why It's Still Broken
Ticket scans and concession sales sit in different screens, and nobody joins them by show and arrival time to count the patrons who scanned in with no purchase while the queue was long.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A weekly report by show: patrons scanned, share with a concession purchase, and the counter wait estimated from transaction timing, flagged where purchase rates fall at peak starts. It uses data the point of sale already holds.

## Who Feels the Pain
General managers judged on per-caps; counter crews facing angry queues; patrons who miss the trailers or skip the popcorn.

## Impact If Fixed
Seeing the lost sales by peak lets managers add a cashier for the 30 minutes that matter, at the cost of a report.
