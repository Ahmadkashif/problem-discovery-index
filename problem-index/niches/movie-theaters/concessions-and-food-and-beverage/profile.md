# Concessions & Food and Beverage

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/movie-theaters|Movie Theaters]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to raise food and beverage spend per patron in the few minutes between ticket scan and trailers without letting the line run past showtime, and whoever sells the most per head at the counter, kiosk, app or seat while holding service time takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2B a year in concession supplies, counter and kitchen labour, equipment and F&B systems, around ~$5–6B of concession revenue (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~35–40% of receipts are concessions; ~15% of receipts are spend on running them (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — point of sale is digital everywhere; ordering, prep and par levels are by feel
**Target Buyer:** VP of food and beverage at a circuit; general manager at an independent
**Automation Potential:** Medium-High — forecasting, ordering and mobile ordering automate; service is hands-on

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Concessions carry the exhibitor's margin. Supplies cost ~20% of concession revenue at AMC and Cinemark, and Cinemark's US patrons spent $8.30 each in 2025 against a $10.52 ticket, so a dollar more per head adds about as much revenue as a ten percent ticket price rise, and none of it is shared with the distributor. The window to sell is short and fixed: the minutes between arrival and the trailers. Dine-in circuits extend it to the seat. FDA menu labelling applies to chains of 20 or more locations. Menu-trend research is bought from the foodservice intelligence firms profiled as [[niches/catering-companies/foodservice-market-intelligence/profile|🔍 Foodservice Market Intelligence Firms]], not rebuilt here.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Vista, Veezi, RTS and Omniterm point of sale; self-serve kiosks and in-app ordering at the large circuits; restaurant inventory tools at some sites. Gaps: no link from the film slate to item demand, no measure of walk-aways from the line, and combo and pricing tests run without controls.
