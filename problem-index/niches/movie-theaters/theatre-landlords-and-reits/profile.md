# Theatre Landlords & Entertainment REITs

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/movie-theaters|Movie Theaters]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Landlords who own theatre buildings and underwrite operators' leases. EPR Properties is the largest specialist: theatres were 154 properties with 17 operators and ~38% of its annualised adjusted EBITDAre in early 2025, AMC was 13.7% of its Q3 2025 revenue, and percentage rent tied to theatre sales rose to $7.0M that quarter. Shopping-centre owners hold most other theatre leases.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 people on theatre underwriting and asset management at the specialist REIT (estimate)
**Output:** Lease underwriting, tenant coverage monitoring, percentage-rent forecasts
**Proprietary data:** Tenant sales reports by site under lease terms
**Clock:** Lease renewals and quarterly earnings
**Buyer:** Chief investment officer
