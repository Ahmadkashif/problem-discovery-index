# Circuit Programming & Revenue Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/movie-theaters|Movie Theaters]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The head-office film, pricing and insights teams of the large circuits (AMC, Regal, Cinemark, Marcus, Cinépolis USA, Harkins and a few others) analyse attendance, concession spend, loyalty behaviour and film performance across hundreds of theatres. They support the film buyers' weekly calls, set prices and subscription terms, and plan premium-format and recliner investments. Their output is used inside the circuit; nobody outside pays for it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 analysts per large circuit across film, pricing, loyalty and finance analytics (estimate)
**Output:** Weekly programming and performance reports, price and promotion analyses, capital-investment cases, loyalty analytics
**Proprietary data:** The circuit's complete theatre-by-show admissions and concession history, plus loyalty member histories (AMC Stubs members are ~51% of AMC's US attendance)
**Clock:** The distributors' Friday release dates and the weekly booking cycle; quarterly earnings for the public circuits
**Buyer:** Chief content officer or VP of film; chief financial officer
