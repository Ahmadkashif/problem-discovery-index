# Cinema Equipment Service & Remote Monitoring

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/movie-theaters|Movie Theaters]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Integrators and projector makers install, service and remotely monitor projectors, media servers and sound systems for exhibitors, often from a network operations centre that watches thousands of screens. Barco, Christie, NEC and Sony make the projectors; Cinemark alone reports ~22% of its auditoriums moved to Barco laser.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Network operations technicians and field engineers in the tens to low hundreds; analytics staff under 10 (estimate)
**Output:** Service visits, remote fixes, uptime reports
**Proprietary data:** Device telemetry and service tickets across client screens
**Clock:** Showtimes and service-level agreements
**Buyer:** Head of service operations
