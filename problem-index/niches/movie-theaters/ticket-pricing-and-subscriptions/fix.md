# Subscriber Visits Counted as Wins

**Niche:** [[niches/movie-theaters/ticket-pricing-and-subscriptions/profile|Ticket Pricing & Subscriptions]]
**Industry:** [[industries/movie-theaters|Movie Theaters]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Subscription dashboards report member visits and attendance share, which rise whether the programme makes money or not.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #causal-inference #evaluation-metrics #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to price each showtime, seat type and subscription so that discount nights and passes fill empty seats without cannibalising full-price weekend sales, and whoever measures that cannibalisation honestly takes the account.

## The Problem
Programme reports celebrate that members drive ~30% of box office or half of attendance. Those shares say nothing about whether members visit more, or the same amount at a lower price, or crowd out paying patrons on sold-out weekends.

## Why It's Still Broken
Visit counts are easy to report and match how programmes are pitched; incremental measures need pre-join histories and control groups nobody set up.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Report, alongside visits, each cohort's visits and spend in the twelve months before joining versus after, and the share of member visits in sold-out shows. It is a query on loyalty data the circuit already has.

## Who Feels the Pain
Finance teams judging subscription economics; pricing teams setting the next fee; distributors whose share of a pass visit is negotiated on uncertain numbers.

## Impact If Fixed
A before-and-after cohort view shows within a quarter whether a programme is growing visits or only discounting them.
