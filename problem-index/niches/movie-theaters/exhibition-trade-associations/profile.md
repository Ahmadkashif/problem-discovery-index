# Exhibition Trade Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/movie-theaters|Movie Theaters]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Cinema United (the National Association of Theatre Owners until March 2025, whose members run more than 32,000 US screens), the Independent Cinema Alliance, the United Drive-in Theatre Owners Association and Art House Convergence represent exhibitors, run conventions such as CinemaCon, lobby on windows and mergers, and publish occasional research. Art House Convergence's 2026 National Audience Survey reached 27,000+ moviegoers at 50+ cinemas.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research staff across the associations, with surveys run with outside partners (estimate)
**Output:** Audience surveys, screen counts, policy papers, convention programming
**Proprietary data:** Member rosters and survey responses
**Clock:** Annual conventions and policy deadlines
**Buyer:** President or executive director
