# Royalty Audit Software Pointed at Film Rent

**Niche:** [[niches/movie-theaters/exhibitor-film-rent-and-settlement/profile|Exhibitor Film Rent & Settlement]]
**Industry:** [[industries/movie-theaters|Movie Theaters]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Licensees in music and publishing already compute and audit percentage royalties against contract terms; cinemas pay the largest percentage licence in retail and audit it by eye.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #feature-engineering #automation #compliance #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to hold an engagement-level record of the terms the theatre actually agreed, so that accruals are right every week and each distributor's invoice is checked before it is paid, and whoever catches the most overcharges and knows each sales office's settlement habits takes the account.

## The Problem
Film rent is a royalty: a percentage of gross that varies by period, with minimums and allowances. Exhibitors handle it with spreadsheets and the general ledger.

## What Already Exists
Royalty and licensing platforms (Rightsline, Exactuals RAPID, Flowhaven and Vistex) model contract terms, compute royalties from sales data, and support audits for licensors and licensees.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: weekly sliding scales keyed to week of run rather than sales tiers; theatre-level engagements numbering in the hundreds per film; firm terms and post-run renegotiation as normal events; reconciliation to the grosses reported to Comscore; daily accrual updates; and a price point a regional circuit can pay, since the platforms are priced for studios and labels.

## Target Customer
Regional circuits and outsourced cinema accounting firms.

## Impact If Solved
A proven terms engine, configured for sliding scales, gives exhibitors the audit capability licensors take for granted.
