# ADA & Public-Assembly Enforcement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/movie-theaters|Movie Theaters]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Department of Justice and US Attorneys enforce the ADA movie rule (28 CFR 36.303(g)) on captioning and audio description devices, as in the 2020 AMC Tyler Galleria settlement; state and local fire marshals enforce occupancy and egress codes; and state health departments enforce food codes and, at chains of 20 or more, federal menu labelling.
