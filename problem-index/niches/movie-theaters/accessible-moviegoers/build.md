# Device Readiness Proven Show by Show

**Niche:** [[niches/movie-theaters/accessible-moviegoers/profile|Accessible Moviegoers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/movie-theaters|Movie Theaters]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** The ADA rule counts "fully operational" devices, and no theatre can show, for any given show, that its devices were.
**Tags:** #survival-analysis #change-point-detection #evaluation-metrics #compliance #worker-facing #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make the captioning or description device a deaf, hard-of-hearing or blind patron picks up work for the whole show, every show, and whoever can prove device readiness show by show takes the account.

## The Problem
Devices age, batteries fade, pairing drifts and units go missing. Staff learn a device is dead when a patron returns it mid-film. When a complaint reaches the DOJ or a lawyer, the theatre has a check-out sheet and recollections.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Device makers sell hardware and stop at the charging rack; ticketing vendors don't track assistive devices; and theatres treat accessibility as a one-time compliance purchase.

## What to Build
A readiness log built on device check-in and check-out at the counter (a scan per unit per show), the charge level and fault codes the units report where available, and a patron's one-tap rating on return. A battery and failure model flags units to retire before they fail. Patrons can see and reserve a device with the ticket. Every show produces a record a compliance officer can produce on request.

## Target Customer
ADA compliance and operations leaders at circuits; device makers seeking a service layer.

## Impact If Built
Fewer failed devices in use, documented readiness that answers complaints, and a reason for deaf and blind patrons to trust the theatre again.
