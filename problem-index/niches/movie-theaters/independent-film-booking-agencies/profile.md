# Independent Film Booking Agencies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/movie-theaters|Movie Theaters]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Booking agencies and freelance film buyers negotiate engagements with distributors on behalf of independent and small-town theatres that can't staff a buyer, for a fee per screen or a share of gross. A Montana independent described working through one to secure films from the majors.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 2-10 bookers per agency (estimate)
**Output:** Weekly bookings and holdover decisions for client theatres; negotiated terms
**Proprietary data:** Grosses and terms history across their client theatres
**Clock:** The weekly booking cycle and release dates
**Buyer:** Agency owner
