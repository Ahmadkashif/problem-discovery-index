# A Weather-Aware Night Planner for Drive-Ins

**Niche:** [[niches/movie-theaters/drive-in-theaters/profile|Drive-In Theaters]]
**Industry:** [[industries/movie-theaters|Movie Theaters]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A drive-in's season turns on twenty-odd nights, and the owner decides whether to open, how much food to prep and how many staff to call from a phone weather app at 3 p.m.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #logistic-regression #time-series-forecasting #feature-engineering #worker-facing #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to sell each weekend night's field in advance and staff the snack bar to it while the weather call is still open, and whoever lets a two-person owner sell, cancel and refund a night cleanly takes the account.

## The Problem
An open night with a half-empty field after an afternoon storm wastes prep, labour and film rent minimums. Cancelling a night that turns clear loses the season's best gross. Owners make the call on instinct, with no record of how attendance responded to forecast rain, temperature, sunset time and the films on the bill in past seasons.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
There are under 300 drive-ins, most run by families with no software budget, and ticketing vendors build for indoor cinemas that never cancel for weather.

## What to Build
A planner that combines hourly forecasts, sunset time, presales and past nights' carloads by film type to predict tonight's field, recommends open or cancel by a set hour, sets snack bar prep and staff call-ins, and triggers the rain-check refunds and social posts if the owner cancels.

## Target Customer
Drive-in owners, reached through UDITOA and through the cloud ticketing vendors some already use.

## Impact If Built
Better open-cancel calls and prep on a ~25-night season could add several percent to a drive-in's annual margin (estimate), which for many is the difference between reopening next spring or not.
