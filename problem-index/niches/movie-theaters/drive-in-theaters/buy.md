# Event Ticketing With Rain Checks Built In

**Niche:** [[niches/movie-theaters/drive-in-theaters/profile|Drive-In Theaters]]
**Industry:** [[industries/movie-theaters|Movie Theaters]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Outdoor event ticketing already handles weather cancellations and rain dates; drive-ins sell by the car on cinema software that assumes a roof.
**Tags:** #logistic-regression #time-series-forecasting #automation #workflow-orchestration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to sell each weekend night's field in advance and staff the snack bar to it while the weather call is still open, and whoever lets a two-person owner sell, cancel and refund a night cleanly takes the account.

## The Problem
Drive-ins need advance sales by the vehicle or the head, capacity limits by the field rather than seats, and a clean way to cancel a night and issue rain checks or refunds to every buyer at once.

## What Already Exists
Outdoor-event ticketing platforms (Eventbrite, Ticket Tailor, and the systems fairs and outdoor concerts use) handle general-admission capacity, weather cancellations, bulk refunds and rain-date transfers.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: per-car pricing with occupants, double-feature bills from a film schedule, reporting of grosses per film for settlement and for Comscore, FM frequency and arrival instructions on the ticket, snack bar pre-orders, and a cancel-by deadline tied to the night's weather forecast.

## Target Customer
Drive-in owners currently using cash or a basic web plug-in.

## Impact If Solved
A configured event platform gives a drive-in clean advance sales and one-tap weather cancellation without building cinema software from scratch.
