# Carloads Counted on a Clipboard

**Niche:** [[niches/movie-theaters/drive-in-theaters/profile|Drive-In Theaters]]
**Industry:** [[industries/movie-theaters|Movie Theaters]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Gate cash and hand counts mean a drive-in owner settles film rent from a clipboard and has no history to plan next season.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #object-detection #cnns #automation #quick-win #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to sell each weekend night's field in advance and staff the snack bar to it while the weather call is still open, and whoever lets a two-person owner sell, cancel and refund a night cleanly takes the account.

## The Problem
At the gate a family member takes cash per car or per head and keeps a tally. That tally becomes the gross reported to distributors for film rent and the only record of the night. Miscounts and undercounts create disputes with distributors and leave the owner with no reliable attendance history.

## Why It's Still Broken
Gate lines move fast at dusk, connectivity is often poor in a field, and card readers alone don't count occupants or link the count to the film schedule.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A tablet gate app that works offline, records car and head counts against the night's double feature, takes cards and cash, and syncs when connected. Optionally, a camera at the entrance counts vehicles to cross-check the tally.

## Who Feels the Pain
Owners settling with distributors; distributors' sales offices auditing small engagements; the next season's planning.

## Impact If Fixed
Accurate counts settle film rent without dispute and build the attendance history every other improvement depends on.
