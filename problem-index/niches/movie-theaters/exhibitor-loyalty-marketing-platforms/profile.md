# Exhibitor Loyalty & Marketing Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/movie-theaters|Movie Theaters]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Platforms that run loyalty programmes, moviegoer segmentation and targeted marketing for exhibitors, led by Movio, part of Vista Group (FY2025 revenue $164.3M, NZD). They hold member and transaction data for many circuits, model who will see which film, and sell campaign tools to exhibitors and audience-targeting products to distributors promoting releases.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~15-30 data scientists and analysts at Movio on the moviegoer-data side (estimate); customer-success and sales staff are not counted
**Output:** Member segments, campaign targeting and attribution reports, film-audience propensity scores, marketing analytics dashboards
**Proprietary data:** Loyalty and transaction histories across many exhibitors' members, which no single circuit sees
**Clock:** The distributors' release dates and campaign launch windows
**Buyer:** Head of marketing or loyalty at a circuit; distributor marketing executives
