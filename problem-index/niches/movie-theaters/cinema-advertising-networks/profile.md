# Cinema Advertising Networks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/movie-theaters|Movie Theaters]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
National CineMedia and Screenvision lease the pre-show screen time from exhibitors and sell it to national and local advertisers. NCM's network covered 17,621 screens in 1,371 theatres at the start of 2026, under exhibitor agreements with a weighted remaining term of ~15.6 years, and it bought Spotlight Cinema Network (art house and dine-in, 1,200+ screens) in November 2025 for $8.2M. Small research teams package attendance into audience estimates and commission post-campaign brand studies to support the sales force.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 dedicated research and audience analysts at NCM, fewer at Screenvision (estimate); sales and ad-operations staff are not counted
**Output:** Audience delivery estimates by film and theatre, post-campaign lift studies, upfront sales research
**Proprietary data:** Campaign delivery logs across the network and years of ad effectiveness studies; attendance originates with exhibitors under the agreements
**Clock:** Advertisers' upfront and campaign flight dates
**Buyer:** Chief revenue officer; head of research
