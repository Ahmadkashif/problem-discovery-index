# Digital Cinema Delivery & Key Management

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/movie-theaters|Movie Theaters]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Service providers that deliver Digital Cinema Packages to theatres by satellite, network or drive, and generate and send the KDM keys that unlock them, mostly on contract to distributors. Exhibitors deal with them when content or keys are late, wrong or missing.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Delivery operations and key-desk staff; no analytics function of note (estimate)
**Output:** Content deliveries and KDMs per screen and engagement
**Proprietary data:** Delivery and key logs by theatre and title
**Clock:** Release dates and showtimes
**Buyer:** Distributor head of servicing
