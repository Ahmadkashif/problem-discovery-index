# IT Monitoring Retargeted at Projection Booths

**Niche:** [[niches/movie-theaters/booth-content-and-projection/profile|Booth Operations: Content, Keys & Projection]]
**Industry:** [[industries/movie-theaters|Movie Theaters]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** IT teams monitor thousands of servers with alerting, certificate-expiry tracking and runbooks; a booth is a rack of servers and projectors watched by a manager on a busy floor.
**Tags:** #autoencoders #change-point-detection #evaluation-metrics #automation #workflow-orchestration #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to have every scheduled show find its content ingested and its key valid on the right server, with the projector healthy, before the doors open, and whoever drives dark-screen incidents closest to zero takes the account.

## The Problem
Booth equipment produces logs and status, and some integrators watch it remotely, but alerts are technical, certificate expiries surprise theatres, and fixes depend on who answers the phone.

## What Already Exists
IT infrastructure monitoring and observability tools (Datadog, PRTG, Zabbix) and certificate lifecycle managers track device health, expiring certificates and incidents, with on-call routing and runbooks.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: connectors for projector and media server protocols and logs; KDM windows and server certificates treated as expiring credentials tied to scheduled shows; alerts ranked by the next affected showtime; plain-language runbooks a shift manager can follow; and automatic tickets to the integrator with the log excerpt attached.

## Target Customer
Technical operations at circuits and integrators running remote monitoring for exhibitors.

## Impact If Solved
A proven monitoring stack, taught cinema protocols and show-linked priorities, gives every booth a watcher that never sleeps.
