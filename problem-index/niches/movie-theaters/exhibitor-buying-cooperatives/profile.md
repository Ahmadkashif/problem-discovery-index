# Exhibitor Buying Cooperatives

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/movie-theaters|Movie Theaters]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Purchasing programmes that pool independents' buying power for concessions, equipment and services. NATO's Cinema Buying Group once counted 400+ independent members and organised digital conversion for 3,000+ screens through Cinedigm. The Independent Cinema Alliance took it over as the Cinema Buying Alliance and now runs a Marketplace with Fandango ClubBuy for ~180 member companies and ~5,000 screens.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 staff on purchasing and vendor programmes (estimate)
**Output:** Negotiated vendor programmes and member pricing
**Proprietary data:** Member purchase volumes by vendor
**Clock:** Vendor contract renewals
**Buyer:** Executive director
