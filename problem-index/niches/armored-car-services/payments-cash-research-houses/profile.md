# Payments, ATM & Cash-Cycle Research Houses

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/armored-car-services|Armored Car Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Independent research and advisory firms that publish market data and forecasts on ATMs, cash automation and the cash cycle — RBR in London and Datos Insights in the US are representative — sold as subscription reports, datasets and consulting to ATM makers, carriers, banks and investors. RBR's reports rank suppliers of retail cash automation and count ATM fleets by country; Datos Insights tracks ATM estates and deployer behaviour, and reported that US deployers removed ~6,000 ATMs in 2025.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–25 analysts on ATMs, cash automation and self-service at the largest such firm (estimate); cash is a slice of broader banking and payments coverage elsewhere
**Output:** Market sizing and forecast reports, ATM and cash-device census datasets, vendor rankings, advisory projects
**Proprietary data:** Survey panels of banks, deployers and carriers and long-running ATM and device census series built from them
**Clock:** Self-set annual publication cycles; clients' strategy and budget seasons
**Buyer:** Managing director or head of research

**Kill switches:** none — the surveys and census series are the firms' own.
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. The research is the invoice, but output is a few annual reports and projects that do not grow with analyst headcount, no third party sets the deadline, and only a handful of firms do this work. Their census numbers disagree with each other (ATMIA estimated 520,000–540,000 US ATMs in 2023; RBR counted 427,500 for 2021 on a different method), which is the same unmeasured-accuracy gap the vault's indexed data vendors share, but at too small a scale to qualify.
