# Cash Forecasting Software Tuned to the Carrier's Constraints

**Niche:** [[niches/armored-car-services/atm-cash-forecasting-replenishment/profile|ATM Cash Forecasting & Replenishment]]
**Industry:** [[industries/armored-car-services|Armored Car Services]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** ATM cash forecasting products exist and claim large savings; their output stops at an order and leaves the carrier to discover it cannot be delivered.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #gradient-boosting #optimization-fundamentals #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep each ATM between a cash-out and idle cash with the fewest unscheduled visits, and whoever holds the least cash in machines at the contracted cash-out rate takes the account.

## The Problem
An owner licenses forecasting software, sends orders to its carrier, and the carrier adjusts them to fit its routes and limits, often without telling the owner. The forecast vendor is then blamed for cash-outs the adjustment caused.

## What Already Exists
Morphis's forecasting product, OptiCash (marketed by Printec), and ATM makers' modules such as Hyosung's predict machine demand and recommend orders.

## The Customization Gap
The software needs the carrier as an input: route days per site, vault order cut-offs, truck value limits, cassette configurations and the emergency visit price, ideally from a live feed. Orders should be generated feasible rather than corrected later, and adjustments logged so owner, vendor and carrier can see who changed what.

## Target Customer
Banks and deployers already licensing forecasting software; forecasting vendors seeking a carrier-integration feature.

## Impact If Solved
Orders that the carrier can serve as written remove the silent adjustments behind many cash-outs and make vendor savings claims testable.
