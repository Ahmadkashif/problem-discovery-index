# Large-Bank Currency Operations & Cash Forecasting Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/armored-car-services|Armored Car Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The currency operations groups at the largest US banks, which buy armored services, forecast branch and ATM cash, place Federal Reserve orders and deposits under Operating Circular 2, manage carrier contracts and run commercial cash-vault services for business clients. They are the industry's largest customers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 cash forecasting and currency operations analysts at each of the largest banks (estimate), counted per bank
**Output:** Branch, ATM and vault cash plans; Fed orders; carrier performance reviews
**Proprietary data:** The bank's own branch, ATM and vault cash histories and carrier invoices
**Clock:** Fed order and deposit cut-offs, recirculation fee weeks, holidays and paydays, carrier contract renewals
**Buyer:** Head of currency operations
