# The Trade-Show Run Booked by Email

**Niche:** [[niches/armored-car-services/valuables-precious-metals/profile|High-Value Valuables & Precious Metals Transport]]
**Industry:** [[industries/armored-car-services|Armored Car Services]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Jewellery trade shows concentrate hundreds of exhibitors' inventory into a few days of pickups and deliveries, coordinated by email threads and phone calls.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #automation #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to move a declared-value consignment of bullion, jewellery or banknotes door to door, across borders where needed, with the declaration, customs entry and insurance cover right first time, and whoever delivers with no customs hold and no gap between declared and insured value takes the account.

## The Problem
Before a major show, exhibitors book pickups from their offices, deliveries to the venue's secure area and returns afterwards. Item lists, declared values, insurance confirmations and venue access details arrive in different formats. Plans change at the last minute, and the busiest days are exactly when inventory concentrated in one place is most attractive to thieves.

## Why It's Still Broken
Show runs are seasonal peaks, so desks absorb them with overtime rather than systems. Exhibitors are small businesses used to booking by phone, and each show's logistics rules differ.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A booking portal per show where exhibitors enter or upload item lists and declared values, confirm insurance, choose windows and receive confirmations, with language-model extraction from whatever documents they send. The desk sees the full schedule and value per vehicle and window, and can spread exposure before the week starts.

## Who Feels the Pain
Valuables desk staff working the show weeks; exhibitors chasing confirmations; crews handling last-minute changes at venues.

## Impact If Fixed
A structured booking for each show cuts the email load in the peak weeks and gives security a value-by-window picture in advance, when it can still change the plan.
