# Freight Forwarding Software Adapted to Valuables

**Niche:** [[niches/armored-car-services/valuables-precious-metals/profile|High-Value Valuables & Precious Metals Transport]]
**Industry:** [[industries/armored-car-services|Armored Car Services]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Freight forwarders run bookings, customs and handoffs on mature platforms; valuables desks do the same job with spreadsheets because the platforms ignore value limits and custody.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to move a declared-value consignment of bullion, jewellery or banknotes door to door, across borders where needed, with the declaration, customs entry and insurance cover right first time, and whoever delivers with no customs hold and no gap between declared and insured value takes the account.

## The Problem
A valuables shipment is a freight shipment with three extra constraints: value limits on every vehicle, vault and flight leg; unbroken custody with named handoffs; and insurance attached to the consignment. Desks juggle these by hand, splitting consignments and chasing documents by email.

## What Already Exists
Forwarding and customs platforms such as CargoWise and Descartes handle bookings, routing, customs filings and documents for ordinary freight.

## The Customization Gap
The valuables version needs: value-aware routing that keeps every leg under its vehicle, vault and airline limit; custody events for every handoff, with the names of the people releasing and receiving; specie insurance certificates generated per consignment and checked against declared value; precious-metal assay and bar lists; temporary-import documents for jewellery travelling to trade shows and back; and language-model extraction of item lists from shippers' invoices into customs and insurance documents.

## Target Customer
Global services and valuables operations at national carriers; specialist valuables logistics firms.

## Impact If Solved
Fewer customs holds, no leg over its limit and every consignment documented the same way for customs, insurer and customer.
