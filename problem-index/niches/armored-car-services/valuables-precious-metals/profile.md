# High-Value Valuables & Precious Metals Transport

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/armored-car-services|Armored Car Services]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to move a declared-value consignment of bullion, jewellery or banknotes door to door, across borders where needed, with the declaration, customs entry and insurance cover right first time, and whoever delivers with no customs hold and no gap between declared and insured value takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M of US revenue from secure transport and storage of jewellery, precious metals, banknotes and other valuables (estimate; Loomis's US International line was SEK 561M, ~$56M, in 2025, and Brink's Global Services is not disclosed separately)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — bookings by phone and email, item lists and declarations on paper or spreadsheets, customs and insurance documents assembled per shipment
**Target Buyer:** Logistics or operations manager at a jewellery manufacturer, bullion dealer, refiner or central-bank counterparty
**Automation Potential:** Medium — documents and checks automate; custody and handoffs stay physical

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Cash is fungible and counted; valuables are declared. The shipper states a value on the manifest, pays a fee based on it, and the carrier's liability is generally capped at it. In July 2022 thieves took 22 bags from a Brink's tractor-trailer at a Lebec, California truck stop; Brink's said the manifests declared ~$8.7M, while 14 jewellers sued claiming ~$100M. Under-declaration to save fees is the industry's open secret, and it surfaces only after a loss. Demand is also volatile: Loomis said strong demand for cross-border movement and storage of precious metals drove its International line in 2025. The work crosses customs, airline handoffs and specie insurance, with no equivalent in daily cash routes.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Carrier booking desks (Brink's Global Services, Loomis International, Malca-Amit and similar specialists), customs brokers, freight-forwarding systems, specie insurance certificates per consignment. Gaps: declared values unchecked at booking, consignments split by email to stay under vehicle limits, trade-show runs coordinated by phone, and no record joining declaration, customs and insurance per item.
