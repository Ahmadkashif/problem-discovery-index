# Retail Replenishment Forecasting Applied to Branch Cash Orders

**Niche:** [[niches/armored-car-services/bank-branch-cash-logistics/profile|Bank & Credit Union Branch Cash Logistics]]
**Industry:** [[industries/armored-car-services|Armored Car Services]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Retailers forecast and reorder thousands of SKUs per store automatically; a branch ordering five denominations of cash still does it by habit.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #gradient-boosting #exponential-smoothing #data-integration #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to fill a bank's branch and vault cash orders on the next scheduled stop across its whole footprint, and whoever has a vault close enough to every branch to make same-day emergency deliveries takes the account.

## The Problem
A branch manager orders currency and coin for the next delivery from experience: last month's usage, a feel for upcoming paydays, a margin for safety. Over-ordering ties up cash and raises insurance exposure in the branch; under-ordering creates an emergency run.

## What Already Exists
Retail replenishment platforms such as Blue Yonder and RELEX forecast demand and generate orders per store and SKU, with seasonality, promotions and lead times. Cash-specific forecasting from vendors such as Morphis exists but is often bought only for ATMs.

## The Customization Gap
Treat denominations as SKUs and the adaptation is still substantial: orders in the carrier's and the Fed's standard units (straps, bundles, coin boxes) rather than single items; deposits as negative demand that can be recycled within the branch; teller cash recyclers that change how much cash a branch needs; delivery only on contracted route days with carrier cut-offs; insurance limits on cash held in the branch; and the Fed recirculation fee, which makes ordering fit $10s and $20s that the same bank deposited that week a cost rather than a neutral move.

## Target Customer
Branch operations managers at community and regional banks and credit unions that already run a core system with teller-cash data.

## Impact If Solved
Orders generated automatically from branch data cut emergency runs and branch cash levels at once, and give the bank a forecast it can hand its carrier.
