# Cash Forecasting & ATM Optimisation Software Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/armored-car-services|Armored Car Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Software firms that forecast cash demand for ATMs, branches and vaults and recommend orders: Morphis (cloud cash forecasting for banks and credit unions), OptiCash (marketed by Printec), and forecasting modules from ATM makers such as Hyosung. Banks, credit unions and deployers license the software; carriers sometimes run it inside managed services.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 forecasting analysts and data scientists per vendor (estimate); implementation, support and engineering staff are not counted
**Output:** Forecasting software, cash order recommendations, savings reports
**Proprietary data:** Forecast models and benchmarks; the transaction data they run on belongs to clients
**Clock:** Clients' order cut-offs and holiday peaks
**Buyer:** Chief product officer
