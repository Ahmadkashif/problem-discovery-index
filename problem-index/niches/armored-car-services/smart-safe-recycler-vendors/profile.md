# Smart Safe & Retail Cash Recycler Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/armored-car-services|Armored Car Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Makers and providers of smart safes and retail cash recyclers that validate deposits and feed provisional credit: Tidel (owned since 2022 by GardaWorld's Sesami), FireKing, Glory and smaller providers such as Cash Depot that let retailers pick their own carrier. They sell or lease devices and software subscriptions to carriers, banks and retailers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analysts per firm (estimate); no evidence that safe makers such as Tidel or FireKing employ a dedicated analysis team, and engineering, production and service staff are not counted
**Output:** Devices, device software and dashboards; some cash-usage analytics for retail clients
**Proprietary data:** Device telemetry and deposit records from thousands of installed safes
**Clock:** Daily provisional credit cut-offs; retail contract renewals
**Buyer:** Head of product or general manager
