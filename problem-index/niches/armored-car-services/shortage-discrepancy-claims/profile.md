# Shortage, Discrepancy & Loss Claims

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/armored-car-services|Armored Car Services]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to establish where a shortage arose — the customer's count, the truck or the counting room — with evidence before the customer's claim window closes, and whoever settles the most discrepancies without a write-off or an insurance claim takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$150M a year of carrier investigation staff, claims handling and discrepancy write-offs (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~4% of receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — scans are digital, but evidence is pulled by hand from separate systems, seal numbers are often handwritten and claims run by email
**Target Buyer:** Director of vault operations or VP of security at a carrier; claims manager
**Automation Potential:** Medium — evidence assembly and attribution automate; deciding that an employee stole stays human

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A carrier signs for value it cannot see inside a sealed bag. Its liability is set by contract and declared value, and every difference between a customer's declaration, a safe's validated count, an ATM's counters and the vault's count becomes a claim, a write-off or an investigation. Most are customer miscounts or counting errors (estimate); a few are theft, by store staff, crews or counting-room employees. The work has its own clocks — customers' contractual claim windows, banks' adjustment deadlines, insurance notification periods under specie policies — and its own legal frame: the Employee Polygraph Protection Act lets an employer ask an employee with access to take a polygraph during a loss investigation. No other part of the business mixes customer service, forensic evidence and labour relations this way.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Vault count systems, handheld scan logs, camera systems with retention schedules, email and spreadsheets for claims; general case-management or claims software where any is used. Gaps: no joined evidence trail per bag, no model of which customers and stations produce discrepancies, video overwritten before a claim arrives, and crews left without a timeline.
