# Insurance Claims Case Management Adapted to Custody Disputes

**Industry:** [[industries/armored-car-services|Armored Car Services]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Insurers run claims through mature case-management systems with evidence, reserves and deadlines; carriers run custody claims through inboxes.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to establish where a shortage arose — the customer's count, the truck or the counting room — with evidence before the customer's claim window closes, and whoever settles the most discrepancies without a write-off or an insurance claim takes the account.

## The Problem
Customer claims, internal investigations and specie insurance notifications follow different rules and clocks, and a carrier handles them in different places. Deadlines are missed, evidence is lost when camera retention expires, and the same loss is described three ways to the customer, the insurer and the employee.

## What Already Exists
Claims and case-management platforms used by insurers and adjusters (Guidewire ClaimCenter, Duck Creek, and general case tools such as ServiceNow), with document handling, deadlines, reserves and audit trails.

## The Customization Gap
Custody disputes need: a case keyed by bag, seal and business day; automatic evidence holds that stop video and device logs being overwritten once a discrepancy opens; contract liability rules (declared value, guarantee terms for validated safe cash, excluded drop types); specie policy deductibles and notification periods; investigation steps that comply with polygraph and employment law; and language-model drafting of the customer response, the insurer notice and the crew update from one evidence set.

## Target Customer
Claims and security teams at national carriers; risk managers at regional independents whose specie insurers ask for better claims files.

## Impact If Solved
No missed claim windows, no lost video, and one consistent record per loss — which matters most at the specie renewal, where the carrier's loss history sets its price.
