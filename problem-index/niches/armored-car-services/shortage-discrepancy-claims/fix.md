# The Seal Number Written by Hand

**Niche:** [[niches/armored-car-services/shortage-discrepancy-claims/profile|Shortage, Discrepancy & Loss Claims]]
**Industry:** [[industries/armored-car-services|Armored Car Services]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A transposed digit on a manifest makes an intact bag look tampered with, and starts an investigation of the crew who carried it.
**Tags:** #cnns #transfer-learning #evaluation-metrics #quick-win #automation #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to establish where a shortage arose — the customer's count, the truck or the counting room — with evidence before the customer's claim window closes, and whoever settles the most discrepancies without a write-off or an insurance claim takes the account.

## The Problem
Customers write seal numbers and declared amounts on deposit slips and manifests; crews copy or scan them; the vault compares. Barcodes help where they exist, but many bags, envelopes and older customers still rely on handwriting. A misread digit becomes a "seal mismatch" exception, the most serious category, because it suggests the bag was opened.

## Why It's Still Broken
Scanning was rolled out for the carrier's own labels, not for what customers write. Crews are judged on stop time, so nobody photographs the bag at pickup. The vault discovers the problem days later, when only the paperwork survives.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A photo of every bag and seal at pickup on the crew's handheld, with on-device reading of the seal number and declared amount and an immediate prompt if they disagree with the manifest. The photo travels with the bag record, so the vault can see whether the seal it received is the seal that left the store.

## Who Feels the Pain
Messengers flagged for seal mismatches they did not cause; vault exception clerks; customers asked to explain paperwork weeks later.

## Impact If Fixed
Most seal mismatches are clerical (estimate); catching them at the door removes them from the investigation queue entirely and leaves investigators with the cases that matter.
