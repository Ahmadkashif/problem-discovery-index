# Armored Car Licensing & BSA Enforcement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/armored-car-services|Armored Car Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State agencies license armored car operators and armed crews — California's Highway Patrol licenses operators under 13 CCR 1111 — while the Armored Car Industry Reciprocity Act makes a crew member's home-state weapons licence valid across states, and federal motor carrier rules govern the fleets. FinCEN enforces the Bank Secrecy Act where carriers act as money transmitters: in February 2025 it fined Brink's Global Services USA $37M, its first enforcement action against an armored car company.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 staff dedicated to armored carriers at any one agency (estimate); armored car licensing sits inside broader private security or vehicle programmes
**Output:** Licences, inspections, enforcement actions
**Proprietary data:** Licence, inspection and examination records
**Clock:** Statutory licensing and examination cycles
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer
