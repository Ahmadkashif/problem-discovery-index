# Specie Loss Adjusters & CIT Risk Surveyors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/armored-car-services|Armored Car Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Loss adjusters who investigate and quantify cash-in-transit, vault and valuables claims for specie insurers, and risk surveyors who inspect carriers' vaults, vehicles and procedures before cover is written. Large thefts such as the 2024 Sylmar burglary (~$30M) and the 2022 Lebec jewellery theft show the work: establishing what was really taken, how and under which policy terms.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 specialists per firm on cash-in-transit and specie (estimate)
**Output:** Adjustment reports, survey reports with risk-improvement requirements
**Proprietary data:** Case files per loss and survey, held for the instructing insurer
**Clock:** Claims and policy deadlines set by the insurer
**Buyer:** Specie claims manager at the insurer
