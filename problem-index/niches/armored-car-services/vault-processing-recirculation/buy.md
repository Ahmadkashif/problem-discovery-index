# Warehouse Management Adapted to Currency Inventory

**Niche:** [[niches/armored-car-services/vault-processing-recirculation/profile|Vault Counting, Fitness Sorting & Local Recirculation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/armored-car-services|Armored Car Services]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Warehouse management systems track ownership, location and condition of millions of units; a currency vault needs the same and usually runs on a narrower vault system and spreadsheets.
**Tags:** #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #compliance #descriptive-statistics #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to sort deposited notes to Federal Reserve fitness standards and send them back out to the bank's own branches and ATMs instead of round-tripping them through the Fed, and whoever recirculates the most fit notes locally takes the account.

## The Problem
A vault holds inventory for many owners, in many denominations and conditions, moving in and out every day under dual control. Mistakes in ownership or counts become disputes with banks; inventory that is invisible becomes funding cost.

## What Already Exists
Warehouse management systems such as Manhattan Associates and SAP Extended Warehouse Management handle multi-owner inventory, locations, lots and conditions, cycle counts and audit trails.

## The Customization Gap
Currency needs: denominations and fitness grades as lot attributes fed directly from sorters; custodial ownership per bank with separate ledgers; Fed standard order and deposit units; dual-control steps recorded for every movement; reconciliation to the banks' general ledgers and the Fed's statements; and holds for discrepancies under investigation.

## Target Customer
Vault managers at carriers expanding vault services; banks running their own cash centres.

## Impact If Solved
A single, audited inventory per owner reduces disputes and gives planners the visibility the recirculation contest depends on.
