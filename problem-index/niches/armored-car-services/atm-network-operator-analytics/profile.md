# ATM Network Operator Placement & Cash Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/armored-car-services|Armored Car Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The network, site and cash analytics teams at the large ATM operators that own and run machines in retail locations and sell ATM-as-a-service to banks. NCR Atleos is the largest: 2025 revenue of $4.35B, ~78,000 owned and operated ATMs within an installed base of ~600,000, and an ATM-as-a-service business with $288M of annualised recurring revenue. Brink's agreed in February 2026 to acquire it. These teams decide where machines go, which to pull, and how much cash and service each gets.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–80 analysts in network, placement and cash analytics at the largest operator (estimate)
**Output:** Site selection and removal decisions, surcharge and interchange analysis, cash and service plans
**Proprietary data:** Transaction and dispense data from the operator's own machines across retail sites
**Clock:** Retail host contract renewals and the operator's own planning cycle
**Buyer:** Head of network strategy
