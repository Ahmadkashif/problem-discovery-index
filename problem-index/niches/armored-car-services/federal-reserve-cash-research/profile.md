# Federal Reserve Cash Research & Currency Operations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/armored-car-services|Armored Car Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Federal Reserve System's Cash Product Office at the San Francisco Fed, with the Atlanta, Boston and Richmond Reserve Banks, runs the Diary of Consumer Payment Choice and other cash research; the Reserve Banks' cash operations receive and pay out currency to depository institutions under Operating Circular 2; and the Board sets the annual currency print order (3.8–5.1 billion notes for 2026). The Diary found cash at 14% of US consumer payments in 2024, steady at about seven payments a month for four years.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 economists and analysts across the Diary and cash product research (estimate)
**Output:** Public research reports and datasets, currency demand forecasts, print order inputs
**Proprietary data:** The Diary panel and the Reserve Banks' records of every depository institution's cash orders and deposits
**Clock:** The Fed's own annual research and print-order cycles
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer
