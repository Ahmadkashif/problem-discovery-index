# Courier Dispatch Software Adapted to Armored Work

**Niche:** [[niches/armored-car-services/regional-independent-carriers/profile|Regional Independent Armored Carriers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/armored-car-services|Armored Car Services]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Courier dispatch apps already optimise routes and capture proof of delivery cheaply; they minimise distance and produce exactly the predictable routes an armored carrier must avoid.
**Tags:** #optimization-fundamentals #monte-carlo-methods #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep the bank and retail accounts that the national carriers bid with smart-safe credit and ATM bundles, and whoever can offer next-day provisional credit through the customer's own bank takes the account.

## The Problem
A regional carrier with twenty or thirty trucks (estimate) plans routes on a whiteboard or a courier app, records custody on paper or a basic handheld, and reconciles at the vault by hand. National carriers have in-house systems built for the job.

## What Already Exists
Last-mile and courier dispatch platforms (Onfleet, Route4Me and similar) with route optimisation, driver apps, proof-of-delivery photos and customer notifications.

## The Customization Gap
Armored work needs: per-truck and per-route value limits from the insurance programme; route and time variation as a constraint rather than an inefficiency; crew assignment by weapons licence and state; bag-level custody scans with seal capture at every handoff; vault check-in and check-out reconciliation; customer windows tied to bank and Fed cut-offs; and no public tracking links that reveal a truck's position.

## Target Customer
Owners and operations managers of regional carriers with roughly 10–100 vehicles (estimate).

## Impact If Solved
Affordable software that encodes the armored rules gives independents the custody record and route discipline their bank customers and underwriters increasingly ask for.
