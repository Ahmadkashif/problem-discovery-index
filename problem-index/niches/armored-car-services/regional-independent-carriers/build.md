# A Bank-Neutral Provisional Credit Network for Independents

**Niche:** [[niches/armored-car-services/regional-independent-carriers/profile|Regional Independent Armored Carriers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/armored-car-services|Armored Car Services]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** National carriers win retail accounts with next-day credit through partnerships with many banks; an independent with one safe vendor and two banks cannot offer it.
**Tags:** #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #compliance #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep the bank and retail accounts that the national carriers bid with smart-safe credit and ATM bundles, and whoever can offer next-day provisional credit through the customer's own bank takes the account.

## The Problem
Provisional credit needs a bank willing to post credit on the carrier's word about a safe's contents, a data feed in the bank's format, and contractual terms on who bears loss. Loomis cites partnerships with more than 165 financial institutions. A regional independent typically has relationships with a handful (estimate), so a retail prospect banking elsewhere cannot get next-day credit through it.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Each national carrier built its bank network for itself as a competitive moat. Safe makers provide device data but not bank agreements. Banks see little reason to integrate with dozens of small carriers one by one.

## What to Build
A shared credit network for independent carriers: one standard data feed from any safe make, one set of bank agreements and risk terms, carrier-level guarantee and insurance verification, and settlement reconciliation between safe totals, vault counts and bank credits. Banks integrate once and accept validated totals from any participating carrier; independents offer the retailer's own bank on day one.

## Target Customer
Owners of regional carriers, the independent carriers' association as a sponsor, and banks that want to keep commercial cash clients without national carrier lock-in.

## Impact If Built
Independents could bid smart-safe accounts on equal terms with the nationals, which is the difference between growing and selling out in the segment where the industry's growth now sits.
