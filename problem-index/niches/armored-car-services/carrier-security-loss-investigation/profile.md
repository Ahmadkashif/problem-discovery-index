# Carrier Corporate Security & Loss Investigation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/armored-car-services|Armored Car Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The corporate security, risk and investigations departments of the national carriers: they analyse attacks, near-misses and route risk, investigate internal and customer-side losses, set crew and vault procedures, and prepare the loss and control record presented to specie underwriters. Their directors meet through the National Armored Car Association's security directors meeting. Brink's names security expertise, risk management and high-quality insurance coverage among its competitive advantages.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analysts per national carrier (estimate); the larger headcount is security directors, branch security managers and investigators who run procedures and cases rather than analysis
**Output:** Incident analyses, route and site risk assessments, investigation findings, procedures, insurance submissions
**Proprietary data:** Incident, near-miss, discrepancy and investigation records across the carrier's whole network, joined to routes, sites and personnel
**Clock:** Specie insurance renewals and claims notification periods; prosecutions and civil claims after an attack
**Buyer:** Chief security officer
