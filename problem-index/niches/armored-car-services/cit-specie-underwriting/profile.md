# Cash-in-Transit & Specie Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/armored-car-services|Armored Car Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Specie underwriters, mostly in the London market, insure armored carriers' cash and valuables in transit and in vaults, along with ATM and fidelity perils. Beazley's cash-in-transit product offers line limits up to US$100M; Marsh puts London specie capacity at ~US$2.5B; older reporting had Lloyd's syndicates writing up to ~85% of cash-in-transit cover worldwide. Brink's says its own loss experience, and to a limited extent other carriers', drives its premium rates.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically under 10 specie underwriters, surveyors and claims specialists per syndicate or broker practice (estimate); no firm found with a 10+ cash-in-transit analysis function
**Output:** Risk assessments, vault and route surveys, pricing and claims decisions
**Proprietary data:** Loss histories across carriers and countries; carriers' survey and control disclosures given under confidentiality
**Clock:** Annual renewals and claims notification periods
**Buyer:** Class underwriter for specie
