# Armored Car, ATM & Cash Advocacy Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/armored-car-services|Armored Car Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The National Armored Car Association (Reston, Virginia), sponsored by the three largest carriers, monitors state and federal regulation and runs annual and security directors' meetings. The Independent Armored Car Operators Association, founded in 1971, serves privately owned carriers with an annual conference of ~120 members. The ATM Industry Association publishes ATM population estimates (520,000–540,000 US machines in 2023) and best-practice guidance, and cash advocacy groups publish material on cash access.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 staff on research at any one association (estimate)
**Output:** Regulatory monitoring, conferences, occasional estimates and guidance
**Proprietary data:** Member surveys and incident sharing among members
**Clock:** Legislative sessions and conference calendars
**Buyer:** Executive director
