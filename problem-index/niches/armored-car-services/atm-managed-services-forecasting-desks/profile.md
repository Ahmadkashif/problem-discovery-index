# ATM Managed Services Cash Forecasting Desks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/armored-car-services|Armored Car Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The cash forecasting and optimisation teams inside the national carriers' ATM managed services businesses, which plan loads and visits for fleets of bank- and deployer-owned machines. Brink's lists cash forecasting and cash optimisation among its AMS services, and its digital retail and ATM services together produced ~$1.5B in 2025; Loomis's US ATM line was SEK 3.3B. The forecast is bundled into the managed-service fee rather than sold on its own.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–50 forecasters and cash analysts per national carrier's US AMS desk (estimate), counted per carrier
**Output:** Replenishment plans and cash orders, cash-out and idle-cash reports to owners
**Proprietary data:** Dispense, deposit and cassette histories for thousands of machines across many owners, joined to the carrier's visit records
**Clock:** Route days, vault and Fed order cut-offs, holidays and paydays; contractual cash-out rates reviewed at renewal
**Buyer:** Head of ATM managed services at the carrier
