# Field Service Management Adapted to ATM Fleets

**Niche:** [[niches/armored-car-services/atm-managed-services/profile|ATM Managed Services]]
**Industry:** [[industries/armored-car-services|Armored Car Services]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Field-service platforms schedule technicians for every kind of equipment; an ATM visit is different because some visits need an armed crew carrying cash and some need a technician who must not.
**Tags:** #optimization-fundamentals #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #worker-facing
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor is fighting to keep each ATM in a bank's or deployer's fleet available to withdraw from, but writing it produces two contests with different winners: holding the least cash in machines at the contracted cash-out rate, which forecasting vendors, carriers' forecasting desks and banks' cash teams compete on; and restoring faulted machines fastest without sending technicians into staged jams, which carriers' service arms, ATM makers' service businesses and independent service organisations compete on. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
An ATM fleet generates two streams of work: replenishment by armored crews on route days, and fault calls for technicians on demand. Scheduling them in one plan saves visits, but the skills, vehicles, security rules and liabilities differ.

## What Already Exists
Field-service platforms such as Salesforce Field Service and ServiceNow Field Service Management schedule technicians by skill, location and service-level agreement, and track parts and work orders.

## The Customization Gap
ATM fleets need: two worker types with different licences and vehicles; visits that combine a cash load with a first-line fix when an armored crew is already going; safe-access rules (who may open the vault versus the top hat); host-site hours; security risk by site and time; cassette and denomination data attached to each work order; and service-level agreements that count cash-outs and faults differently.

## Target Customer
Service delivery managers in carriers' ATM managed services; operations heads at independent deployers who subcontract cash and service separately.

## Impact If Solved
One plan for both streams removes duplicate visits and gives owners a single service record per machine.
