# Grocery Wholesaler & Cooperative Retail-Services Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/grocery-stores|Grocery Stores]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The wholesalers and retailer-owned cooperatives that supply independent supermarkets also sell them retail services: category management and planograms, retail pricing, weekly ad planning, private-label programmes, store analytics and sometimes accounting. UNFI, C&S (which completed its purchase of SpartanNash in September 2025 and serves close to 10,000 independent locations, search summary), Associated Wholesale Grocers, Associated Food Stores and Wakefern are the main players (recalled). Retailers pay through wholesale upcharges and service fees, and the wholesalers also sell their shipment data and promotional access to CPG suppliers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~50-300 category managers, pricing analysts, ad planners and retail-services analysts per large wholesaler (estimate)
**Output:** Category plans and planograms, retail price files, weekly ad programmes, store performance reviews, supplier data products
**Proprietary data:** Two regimes. The wholesaler's own shipment and invoice data across thousands of stores is theirs. Member and customer stores' point-of-sale movement is held under supply and service agreements.
**Clock:** Weekly ad cycles and vendor deal windows (client clocks)
**Buyer:** SVP of retail services or retail analytics at the wholesaler
