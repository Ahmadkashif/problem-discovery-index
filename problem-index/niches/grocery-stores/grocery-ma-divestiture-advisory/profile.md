# Grocery M&A & Store Divestiture Advisory

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/grocery-stores|Grocery Stores]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Investment banks and boutiques with grocery teams advise on supermarket and wholesaler deals and on store packages divested to win antitrust clearance. Solomon Partners advised C&S on its purchase of SpartanNash (search summary). The failed Kroger–Albertsons merger (blocked in December 2024, recalled) shows how much of the work is building and defending divestiture packages. Sellers and buyers pay success fees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 grocery-focused bankers per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Valuations, buyer lists, divestiture packages, fairness opinions
**Proprietary data:** Deal files and client store data under confidentiality agreements
**Clock:** Deal timetables and antitrust waiting periods
**Buyer:** Head of the grocery or consumer retail group
