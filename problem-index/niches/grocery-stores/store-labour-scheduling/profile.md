# Store Labour Scheduling & Workload Forecasting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/grocery-stores|Grocery Stores]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to match each store's scheduled hours to its 15-minute workload of customers, trucks and online orders, and whoever delivers the fewest understaffed intervals at the same payroll takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.2B a year of US spend on workforce management software, labour standards and scheduling services at supermarkets (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.15% of grocery sales, set against store labour of ~12–14% of sales (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium-High at chains, which run workforce management suites; Low at independents, where the manager builds the schedule in a spreadsheet
**Target Buyer:** VP of store operations or labour; director of workforce management
**Automation Potential:** High for the forecast and the first draft of the schedule; medium for the final schedule, which carries availability, seniority and union rules

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Labour is the largest cost a store manager controls on a 1.7% net margin (FMI). The workload has more drivers than in most retail. Front-end traffic peaks after work. DSD and warehouse trucks arrive on their own schedules. Fresh production needs early-morning bakers, cutters and deli staff. Online orders add picking hours that move with promotions. Fair Workweek laws require schedules posted well ahead: Seattle's Secure Scheduling Ordinance, which covers retail and food-service employers with 500+ employees worldwide, requires 14 days' notice and predictability pay for later changes (Seattle Office of Labor Standards). The forecast must therefore be right two weeks out, at 15-minute grain, by department.

## Current Tools & Gaps
UKG, Legion (hiring engineers in India, Romania and Mexico in October 2026), Logile (engineered standards and grocery workforce management), Zebra Workcloud (Reflexis) and Blue Yonder workforce management. Gaps: online-order picking hours are forecast separately from store work, truck arrivals are not fed in live, and engineered standards are years old.
