# Fresh Perishables Ordering & Markdown

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/grocery-stores|Grocery Stores]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is "every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to cut fresh-department shrink without emptying the shelves". But that contest splits in two. Ordering vendors win before the truck arrives, by cutting the most shrink at an unchanged in-stock rate. Markdown, surplus and dynamic-pricing vendors win after the product is aging, on margin recovered per lot. The buyers, data and winners differ. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.5B a year of US spend on fresh ordering, forecasting, markdown, surplus and electronic-label pricing tools and services at supermarkets (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.2% of grocery sales, against fresh sales of ~42% of the store (FMI) (estimate for the tooling share)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — large chains run forecasting and replenishment, but fresh orders are still adjusted by department managers and culls are rarely logged by reason
**Target Buyer:** VP of fresh merchandising; VP of replenishment or supply chain at a regional or national chain
**Automation Potential:** High for the forecast and markdown schedule; low for the condition judgement on the floor until it is captured

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Fresh is where a supermarket's money and its losses sit together. Produce, meat, seafood, bakery and deli made up 42% of sales in 2024 (FMI), and their shrink runs several times the store average because the product dies on the shelf (estimate). The work runs on a daily clock, with orders cut for deliveries two or three days out. The decision is made by department managers whose reads of ripeness, weather and promotion lift are tacit and whose turnover is high. Centre-store replenishment has been automated for years; fresh was left to people because demand is volatile, units are often random-weight, and the product changes condition between receipt and sale.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Afresh, RELEX, Crisp (with Shelf Engine), Upshop, Invafresh and Blue Yonder sell fresh forecasting and ordering. Wasteless, electronic shelf labels (Vusion, Hanshow), Flashfood and Too Good To Go handle markdowns and surplus. Gaps: culls and overrides carry no reason, condition at receipt is never captured, and ordering and markdown tools rarely share a record of the same lot.
