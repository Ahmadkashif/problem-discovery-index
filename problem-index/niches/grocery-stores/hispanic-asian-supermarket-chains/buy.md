# Seasonal Retail Planning Adapted to Lunar and Religious Calendars

**Niche:** [[niches/grocery-stores/hispanic-asian-supermarket-chains/profile|Hispanic & Asian Supermarket Chains]]
**Industry:** [[industries/grocery-stores|Grocery Stores]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** General merchandise retailers already plan seasonal buys months ahead with one shot at the order; multicultural grocers need that planning on festival calendars that move each year.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #bayesian-inference #feature-engineering #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to size the direct-import and festival-season orders that drive these chains' sales, and whoever leaves the lowest unsold seasonal stock at an unchanged in-stock rate takes the account.

## The Problem
The buying problem is the same as a toy or Halloween buy: one order, placed long before the season, with no reorder. Seasonal planning tools exist, but for the wrong calendar.

## What Already Exists
Seasonal and assortment planning tools used by general merchandise retailers (Oracle Retail planning, Blue Yonder, RELEX seasonal planning), which plan pre-season buys and in-season allocation around fixed holidays.

## The Customization Gap
Season definitions must follow lunar and religious calendars, with date shifts year to year. Store clustering must be by community mix rather than climate. Item data for imported goods without GS1 records must be built, and import lead times by origin port and supplier modelled. Allocation must run across stores from a single container, and leftover markdowns must respect cultural timing: festival sweets lose most of their value the day after.

## Target Customer
Merchandising leaders at multicultural supermarket chains already running a grocery ERP.

## Impact If Solved
Using an existing planning tool for festival buys could cut pre-season planning from ~6 buyer-weeks per festival to ~2 (estimate), across four or five major festivals a year.
