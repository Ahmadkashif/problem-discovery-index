# Food Retail Association Research (FMI, NGA)

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/grocery-stores|Grocery Stores]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
FMI – The Food Industry Association and the National Grocers Association publish the industry's benchmark studies. FMI's include *The Food Retailing Industry Speaks*, which put food retail profit margins at 1.7% in 2025, and *The State of Fresh Foods*. NGA commissioned the 2026 economic impact study that credited independents with $353.5B of sales (Grocery Dive). Members pay dues; some reports are sold to non-members, and many are produced with research partners.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~5-10 in-house research staff per association (estimate); fieldwork and analysis are often done by partner firms
**Output:** Annual benchmark and trend reports, member surveys, economic impact studies
**Proprietary data:** Member survey responses (membership data) and partner-supplied market data
**Clock:** Self-set annual publication calendars, often timed to conferences
**Buyer:** Head of research at the association
