# Store-Pick Online Order Fulfilment

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/grocery-stores|Grocery Stores]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to pick online orders from the store's own shelves, and whoever delivers the lowest labour cost per order at an unchanged fill rate takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2B a year of US spend on store-pick software, picking labour tools, in-store fulfilment hardware and platform fees tied to picking at supermarkets (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** Online orders are ~10–15% of grocery sales (estimate), and 9.3% of fresh sales were digital in 2024 (FMI)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium-High — picking runs on handheld apps, but the inventory and location data under them is often wrong
**Target Buyer:** VP of e-commerce operations at a chain
**Automation Potential:** High for batching, pick paths and inventory truth; the physical pick is still a person with a cart

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Grocery tried to take picking out of the store and partly retreated. Takeoff Technologies, which built micro-fulfilment centres in the back of stores, filed for Chapter 11 in May 2024 (search summary). In November 2025 Kroger said it would close three Ocado-powered robotic centres, take about $2.6B of charges, refocus e-commerce on its stores and pilot "capital-light, store-based automation" (Grocery Dive). The work that remains is picking from shelves that shoppers also use, against inventory counts that drift, under substitution and temperature clocks. The contest is cost per order without losing fill rate: every minute of searching or walking is margin on an order that often already loses money.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Instacart (marketplace, Storefront, Carrot white-label tools, Caper carts and FoodStorm), Ocado's store-pick software, Mercatus, and in-house apps at the largest chains. DoorDash and Uber handle delivery. Gaps: location and on-hand data are not corrected from pickers' scans, batches ignore aisle congestion and temperature, and pick cost is measured as a rate rather than per order. Marketplace substitution is [[niches/gig-delivery-platforms/substitution-and-accuracy/profile|🟠 Substitution & Order Accuracy]], and delivery routing is [[niches/last-mile-delivery/grocery-same-day/profile|🔵 Grocery & Same-Day Delivery]].
