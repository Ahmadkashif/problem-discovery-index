# Store Food-Safety & Traceability Records

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/grocery-stores|Grocery Stores]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a store's temperature, sanitation and traceability records complete without clipboards, and whoever produces the highest share of complete key-data-element records for Food Traceability List receipts takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$500M a year of US spend on temperature monitoring, digital food-safety checklists, traceability capture and third-party food-safety audits at supermarkets (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.06% of grocery sales (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — wireless cooler sensors are common at chains, but department checklists, cooling logs and receiving records are often paper, and lot codes are rarely captured
**Target Buyer:** Director of food safety and quality assurance at a chain
**Automation Potential:** High for capture and monitoring; the corrective judgement stays with the department lead

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A supermarket runs a dozen small food businesses under one roof: a deli that cooks and cools, a bakery, a seafood counter, a sushi bar, a meat room that grinds, and a produce department that cuts fruit. Each operates under the state's adopted FDA Food Code and local health inspections. Since the Food Traceability Rule, any non-small supermarket that receives listed foods must also keep lot-level receiving records and produce them within 24 hours of an FDA request. Enforcement has been deferred to 20 July 2028 (FDA). That is a client compliance date: it sets when chains must be ready, not when this niche's vendors are paid.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Wireless temperature monitoring (SmartSense by Digi, Testo, ComplianceMate), digital checklists (Zest, Jolt), traceability networks (ReposiTrak, iFoodDS, Trustwell), and third-party audits (Ecolab's EcoSure, Steritech). Gaps: checklist, sensor and receiving data sit in separate tools, store transformation of listed foods is not linked to its input lots, and no one tests whether the records would come out in 24 hours.
