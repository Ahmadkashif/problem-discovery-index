# WIC Vendor Management & EBT/eWIC Processors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/grocery-stores|Grocery Stores]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
State WIC agencies authorise grocery stores as WIC vendors, set price peer groups and maximum reimbursements, publish the Authorized Product List and monitor vendors for overcharging. Processors such as Conduent and FIS run the EBT and eWIC card systems that settle SNAP and WIC purchases with stores (recalled). Stores are paid through these systems; states pay the processors.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 vendor-management analysts per state WIC agency (estimate); processors' staff are payments production
**Output:** Vendor authorisations, peer-group price limits, APL files, high-risk vendor reviews; card settlement
**Proprietary data:** WIC redemptions by store and item per state; EBT settlement records
**Clock:** State authorisation cycles and federal reporting
**Buyer:** State WIC vendor-management director; processor programme head
