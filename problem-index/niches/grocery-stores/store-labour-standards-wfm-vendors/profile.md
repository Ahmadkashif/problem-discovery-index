# Grocery Labour Standards & Workforce Forecasting Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/grocery-stores|Grocery Stores]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Workforce management vendors that sell supermarkets engineered labour standards (seconds per item scanned, minutes per case stocked, minutes per order picked), workload forecasts at 15-minute grain and schedule optimisation. Logile is the grocery specialist. Legion, UKG and Zebra Workcloud (Reflexis) sell across retail (recalled). Their industrial engineers run time studies in client stores, and their forecasting teams tune models per chain. Chains pay software subscriptions plus engineering and implementation services.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-150 industrial engineers, forecasting analysts and labour consultants per grocery-focused vendor (estimate); software engineers are excluded
**Output:** Labour standards libraries, workload forecasts, schedule recommendations, labour-model reviews
**Proprietary data:** Task-time studies across many grocers' stores, plus forecast-versus-actual histories per client
**Clock:** Weekly schedule posting, with Fair Workweek posting deadlines in some cities binding the grocer's own scheduling (client and production clocks)
**Buyer:** Head of labour science or professional services at the vendor
