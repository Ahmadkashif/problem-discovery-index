# Household & Receipt Panel Data Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/grocery-stores|Grocery Stores]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms that recruit consumer panels to record what households buy across every grocer, by scanning receipts in an app or scanning items at home, and sell the resulting shopper data to CPG makers and retailers. Numerator and Fetch are receipt-based (recalled); Circana and NielsenIQ run household panels alongside their point-of-sale measurement. Grocers buy panel data to see where their shoppers spend the rest of their grocery budget, which no loyalty programme shows; CPG makers buy it to see brand switching across retailers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~100-500 analysts, panel methodologists and insight consultants per large panel business (estimate); receipt-processing and data-operations staff are production
**Output:** Subscription shopper dashboards, leakage and switching analyses, custom studies and survey add-ons
**Proprietary data:** Purchase records from consented panellists across all retailers and channels, with demographics; not available to any single retailer
**Clock:** The vendor's own monthly and quarterly release calendar; client planning cycles
**Buyer:** Chief data or insights officer at the panel vendor
