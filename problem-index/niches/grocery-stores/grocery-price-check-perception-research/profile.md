# Grocery Price-Check & Price-Perception Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/grocery-stores|Grocery Stores]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Vendors that collect competitors' shelf and online prices item by item and sell price-gap reports to supermarket pricing teams. Some also survey shoppers on which store they believe is cheapest and tie that perception to the prices actually charged. Datasembly-style web price collection, field price-check services and price-image research sit here (recalled). Price-optimisation software vendors (Revionics, Clear Demand) consume these feeds but sell software. Chains pay subscriptions for weekly price checks against named competitors, plus studies of their price image.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-100 analysts and data staff per firm (estimate); field price checkers and scraping engineers are production
**Output:** Weekly competitive price-gap reports by item and zone, price-index tracking, price-perception studies
**Proprietary data:** Histories of collected shelf and online prices by store and item, and shopper perception surveys; online prices can be collected by any competitor, in-store checks less easily
**Clock:** The vendor's own weekly delivery cadence; clients reprice on their own ad cycles
**Buyer:** Chief executive or head of data at the vendor
