# Supermarket Site Selection & Trade-Area Forecasting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/grocery-stores|Grocery Stores]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Analytics firms and consultancies that forecast a proposed supermarket's sales before the chain signs a lease or buys land. They model how much it would take from the chain's own stores and competitors, and size the trade area. Location analytics firms such as Buxton and Placer.ai, and boutique grocery site analysts, sell these studies (recalled), and the largest chains run in-house real-estate research teams that do the same. Chains, grocery-anchored developers and lenders pay per study or by subscription.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-20 modelling and data-science staff maintaining cross-client forecast models and data layers at the largest firms, under 10 at most others (estimate). Analysts who prepare one site study at a time for one client are per-engagement production and are not counted. The gate passes on the largest firms only.
**Output:** Site sales forecasts, cannibalisation estimates, trade-area and competitor studies, portfolio and closure reviews
**Proprietary data:** Archives of past forecasts and analog-store performance, plus licensed mobility, demographic and competitor-location data
**Clock:** Lease negotiation windows and the chain's real-estate committee dates (client clocks)
**Buyer:** Head of research or managing director at the firm
