# Meat & Seafood Cutting Room

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/grocery-stores|Grocery Stores]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn each delivery of subprimals and fish into the cuts, grinds and trays that sell before their date, and whoever raises the department's realised margin per pound received takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M a year of US spend on meat and seafood department production planning, scale and label systems, yield tracking and grind-log compliance at supermarkets (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.05% of grocery sales, against meat at ~11% of store sales (FMI) (estimate for the tooling share)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — scales print labels and record sales by PLU, but cutting tests, production plans and grinding logs are mostly paper or spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** VP or director of meat and seafood merchandising at a chain
**Automation Potential:** Medium — production quantities and yield tracking automate well; the cutting itself and the read of a carcass do not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Meat made up 11% of store sales in 2024 (FMI), and its margin is decided in the back room. A cutter takes a box of beef chuck or pork loin and decides how much becomes steaks, roasts, stew meat or grind, based on what is selling, what is on ad, and how the subprimal looks. The yield from each box, and the price spread between a ribeye and ground beef, set the department's margin. Retailers that grind raw beef must keep grinding logs that let USDA trace a grind back to its source lots (recalled). Seafood adds species substitution risk and shelf lives measured in days. Case-ready product from packers has replaced in-store cutting at many chains. Where cutting stays, it is a craft whose yield knowledge sits in the cutter's hands.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Scale and label systems (Hobart, Mettler Toledo, Bizerba), meat production modules in store operations suites (Upshop, Invafresh), and cutting-test spreadsheets from packer sales reps. Gaps: cutting tests are run once and filed, production is planned from yesterday's sales, and grind logs are handwritten. Specialty butchers are [[niches/specialty-food-retail/craft-butcher-shops/profile|🔵 Craft Butcher & Charcuterie Shops]], and packers are [[niches/food-manufacturing/meat-and-poultry-processors/profile|🔵 Meat & Poultry Processors]].
