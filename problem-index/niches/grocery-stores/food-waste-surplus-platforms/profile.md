# Food Waste Data & Surplus Recovery Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/grocery-stores|Grocery Stores]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Organisations that measure supermarket food waste or move surplus food before it is thrown away. Flashfood and Too Good To Go sell grocers' short-dated food to consumers through apps. Spoiler Alert sells surplus-inventory software to food makers. ReFED, a nonprofit, publishes food-waste data and estimates for the US food system (recalled). Grocers pay marketplace commissions; ReFED is funded by philanthropy and sponsors.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analysts each (estimate); Too Good To Go's machine-learning and data roles (Copenhagen) serve its own marketplace
**Output:** Surplus marketplace sales, waste reports to retail partners, published food-waste estimates
**Proprietary data:** Marketplace transactions with grocer partners; ReFED's modelled estimates
**Clock:** Self-set; partners' sustainability reporting dates
**Buyer:** Chief executive or head of data
