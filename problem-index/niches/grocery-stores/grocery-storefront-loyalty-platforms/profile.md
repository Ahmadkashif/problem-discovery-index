# Grocery E-Commerce Storefront & Loyalty Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/grocery-stores|Grocery Stores]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Platforms that run a grocer's own website, app, loyalty programme and digital coupons under the grocer's brand: Instacart's Storefront and Carrot tools, Swiftly, Eagle Eye, Birdzi and Mercatus (recalled). Most also run a retail media network on the grocer's behalf and share the ad revenue. Their analytics teams produce customer segments, offer targeting and campaign reports for each grocer and its CPG advertisers. Grocers pay platform fees, a share of media revenue, or both. Swiftly's model charges no software fee and earns from media (search summary).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~30-200 analysts and data scientists serving grocer clients per platform (estimate); marketplace and ads engineering at Instacart is far larger and serves its own business
**Output:** Loyalty segments, personalised offers, campaign and media reports, basket analyses, delivered as dashboards inside the platform
**Proprietary data:** Grocer clients' customer, order and loyalty transactions processed by the platform, pooled across clients only where contracts allow
**Clock:** Clients' weekly ad and offer cycles (client clocks)
**Buyer:** Head of data science or retailer analytics at the platform
