# Apparel Markdown Optimisation Adapted to Days, Not Weeks

**Niche:** [[niches/grocery-stores/fresh-markdown-expiry-pricing/profile|Fresh Markdown & Expiry Pricing]]
**Industry:** [[industries/grocery-stores|Grocery Stores]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Fashion retail has run markdown optimisation for decades; fresh food needs the same mathematics on a clock of days and per lot rather than weeks and per style.
**Tags:** #bayesian-inference #dynamic-programming #causal-inference #gradient-boosting #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to price or divert short-dated fresh product before it is culled, and whoever recovers the most margin per marked-down lot net of cannibalised full-price sales takes the account.

## The Problem
Supermarkets mark down fresh on fixed rules because nothing else fits the clock. Markdown optimisation exists, but it was built for seasonal merchandise.

## What Already Exists
Markdown and lifecycle pricing tools sold to apparel and general merchandise retailers (Revionics, Blue Yonder markdown, Oracle Retail Offer Optimization, and similar), which plan markdown cadences across a season by style and store.

## The Customization Gap
Fresh needs per-lot decisions, because two packs of the same item can carry different dates. Its horizon is one to five days and it recalculates daily or hourly. Elasticity depends on days-to-expiry as well as price. It needs a link to electronic shelf labels, or printed labels where there are none. It must include non-price endings such as deli transfer, surplus apps and donation. Cannibalisation of the same item at full price beside the markdown is immediate and must be measured.

## Target Customer
Pricing teams at chains that already license a markdown or price-optimisation tool for general merchandise.

## Impact If Solved
Applying an existing tool to fresh instead of buying a new one could save ~$200,000 a year in licences and integration at a 100-store chain (estimate), while moving fresh markdowns off fixed rules.
