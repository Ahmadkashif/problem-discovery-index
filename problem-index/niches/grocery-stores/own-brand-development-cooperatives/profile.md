# Own-Brand Development & Procurement Cooperatives

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/grocery-stores|Grocery Stores]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Organisations that develop and source supermarkets' own brands. Topco Associates is a member-owned cooperative that runs procurement and private-brand programmes for grocers. Daymon, part of Advantage Solutions, designs and manages private-brand portfolios for retailers (both recalled). Their category, quality and consumer-insight staff decide which own-brand items to launch, benchmark them against national brands, write specifications, run supplier bids and audit quality. Members pay through fees and procurement margins, and Daymon's clients pay service fees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~30-150 category strategists, quality and sensory staff and insight analysts per organisation (estimate)
**Output:** Own-brand assortment strategies, item specifications, supplier bid evaluations, quality benchmarks, consumer research
**Proprietary data:** Cross-member own-brand cost, specification and quality-audit records, plus member or client sales data
**Clock:** Retailers' line reviews and supplier bid calendars (client clocks)
**Buyer:** Head of brand strategy or insights at the cooperative or firm
