# Grocery Strategy & Online-Grocery Research Boutiques

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/grocery-stores|Grocery Stores]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Small consultancies and research shops that specialise in supermarkets: store strategy, format and fresh-department advice, and online grocery tracking. Brick Meets Click publishes a monthly online grocery sales survey, and McMillanDoolittle and Willard Bishop (now part of Inmar) advise chains (recalled). Chains and suppliers pay for projects, speeches and sponsored studies.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analysts each (estimate)
**Output:** Strategy projects, monthly online-grocery survey releases, sponsored white papers
**Proprietary data:** Their own shopper surveys and project files
**Clock:** Self-set publication schedules; client project dates
**Buyer:** Founder or managing partner
