# Outplacement & Career Transition Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/document-preparation-services|Document Preparation Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Employers making layoffs pay outplacement firms, such as LHH (Adecco), Right Management (ManpowerGroup), RiseSmart (Randstad), Careerminds and many regional boutiques, to help departing employees find new work. The package almost always includes resume and LinkedIn rewriting by in-house or contracted writers, coaching and job-search tools. That makes these firms the largest institutional buyers and producers of career documents. Some publish labour-market and layoff reports as marketing, and most report "time to landing" outcomes to the employer that paid.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-30 analytics and research staff at the largest firms (estimate)
**Output:** Programme outcome reports to employer clients (landing rates, time to landing, salary recovery); occasional published research
**Proprietary data:** Outcome records for large numbers of displaced workers by occupation, level and region, plus the documents and coaching each received — the closest thing to an outcome dataset for career documents
**Clock:** The employer's layoff dates and WARN notices set programme starts, but no third party sets a deadline on the firm's own analysis
**Buyer:** Head of analytics or programme quality at the outplacement firm

**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. These firms hold the best evidence of which career documents and coaching help people land, and sell transition services, not that evidence. Outcome reporting is a retention feature of the contract.
