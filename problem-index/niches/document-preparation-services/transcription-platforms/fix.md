# The 24-Hour Promise and the Unclaimed Hard File

**Niche:** [[niches/document-preparation-services/transcription-platforms/profile|General Transcription Platforms (Per-Minute, Hybrid)]]
**Industry:** [[industries/document-preparation-services|Document Preparation Services]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Experienced contractors skip the crosstalk-heavy file at the flat rate, so the file the customer most needed a human for is the one that misses its deadline.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #feature-engineering #worker-facing #workflow-orchestration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to justify a human-verified tier priced near eight times the machine transcript, file by file, as recognisers improve, and whoever predicts which minutes actually need a human, and prices and routes them that way, takes the account.

## The Problem
Freelancers can see a file's preview and length before claiming it. They pass over the poor-audio, multi-speaker or heavily technical ones, which sit until a coordinator adds a bonus or assigns them near the deadline to whoever is online, often a less experienced worker. The result is the worst transcripts on the hardest files, delivered late.

## Why It's Still Broken
Pay is flat by design, and bonuses are manual and reactive. The platform doesn't flag difficulty at upload, so nobody acts until the clock is nearly out.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Score every file for difficulty at upload, post the hard ones with a difficulty premium immediately, and route them first to workers whose QA history shows they handle that audio type. Add a deadline watch that escalates unclaimed files while there is still time to do them properly.

## Who Feels the Pain
Customers who paid for a human transcript of their hardest recording; coordinators firefighting bonuses; skilled contractors who would take hard files at a fair rate.

## Impact If Fixed
Hard files claimed early by the right people, fewer late or poor deliveries, and a price signal that tells skilled workers their skill is wanted.
