# Contact-Centre Quality Scoring for Resume Drafts

**Industry:** [[industries/document-preparation-services|Document Preparation Services]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Contact centres score every call automatically against a rubric; resume platforms still hand-review a sample of drafts after clients have complained.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #evaluation-metrics #automation #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a questionnaire and an old resume into a keyword-matched, ATS-readable resume in the fewest paid writer-hours, and whoever holds revision and refund rates down at a flat per-order writer fee takes the account.

## The Problem
Draft quality varies across hundreds of contractor writers. Editors review a sample, and clients find the rest: invented metrics, keyword stuffing, a summary that could describe anyone, formatting that a parser will mangle.

## What Already Exists
Conversation-analytics and quality-management tools from contact centres score every interaction against a rubric, flag outliers and coach agents. General writing tools check grammar and tone.

## The Customization Gap
The rubric must be resume-specific. It checks that each accomplishment has action, scope and result, flags metrics that don't appear in the client's material (a fabrication risk), measures keyword coverage of the target postings without stuffing, runs a parse test against common applicant-tracking parsers, and checks tone and length by seniority. Scores need to feed writer coaching and the matching model, and to flag a draft before the client sees it.

## Target Customer
Quality and writer-development leads at resume platforms and multi-writer boutiques.

## Impact If Solved
Every draft checked before delivery, problems caught internally, and coaching aimed at each writer's specific weaknesses.
