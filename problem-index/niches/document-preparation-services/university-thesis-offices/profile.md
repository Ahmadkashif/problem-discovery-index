# University Thesis & Dissertation Offices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/document-preparation-services|Document Preparation Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Graduate schools' thesis and dissertation offices write the format manuals, review every deposit for conformance, run similarity checks such as iThenticate, set the term deposit deadlines and send accepted theses to the institutional repository and ProQuest. For dissertation editors and formatters they are the rule-setter and the inspector. Repository abstracting is logged under Libraries & Archives as [[niches/libraries-and-archives/scholarly-abstracting-indexing-databases/profile|🔍 Scholarly Abstracting & Indexing Databases]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** A handful of format reviewers per graduate school (estimate)
**Output:** Format manuals, review decisions and correction lists, deposit approvals
**Proprietary data:** Correction histories for every deposit
**Clock:** Term deposit deadlines they set themselves
**Buyer:** Associate dean of the graduate school
