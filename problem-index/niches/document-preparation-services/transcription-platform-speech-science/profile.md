# Transcription Platforms' Speech & Data Science Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/document-preparation-services|Document Preparation Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The per-minute platforms (Rev, Verbit, 3Play Media, GoTranscript, TranscribeMe) aggregate transcription demand that small shops once served, and run speech and data science teams that build recognisers from the human transcripts their workforces produce. Rev's Reverb was trained on 200,000 hours of English transcribed by humans, and Rev sells it as an API alongside its human tier. Verbit has bought captioning and transcription firms, including VITAC, to add volume (see [[niches/postproduction-studios/captions-subtitles-and-accessibility/profile|Captions, Subtitles & Accessibility Files]] for the broadcast side).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-60 speech scientists, ML engineers and data scientists per platform (estimate)
**Output:** Recognition and diarisation models, routing and QA analytics used internally; API access sold per minute
**Proprietary data:** Large corpora of audio with human-verified transcripts in verbatim and clean styles, plus editor-effort logs
**Clock:** Customers' turnaround promises and universities' accessibility dates are customers' clocks, not deadlines on the team's analysis
**Buyer:** VP of AI or Head of Speech at the platform

**Kill switches:** none — Rev's terms permit training its recognition models on self-serve customer content (enterprise and Unlimited customers can opt out), so the leading corpus is the platform's to use; enterprise opt-outs narrow it rather than close it.
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. The corpus is a real moat, but what the platform invoices is transcripts and API minutes, not insight. The research team does not grow with volume, and a handful of platforms make up the market.
