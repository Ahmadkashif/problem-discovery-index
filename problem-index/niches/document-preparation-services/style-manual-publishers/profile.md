# Style Manual Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/document-preparation-services|Document Preparation Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The editorial teams behind the style manuals editors and formatters apply: the APA Publication Manual and APA Style team, the University of Chicago Press's Chicago Manual of Style, the AMA Manual of Style from the JAMA Network, and the MLA Handbook. They revise the rules in new editions, answer questions online and sell print and subscription access.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Small editorial teams per manual, typically under 10 (estimate)
**Output:** Manual editions, online subscriptions, style guidance and Q&A
**Proprietary data:** Their own rulebooks and reader questions
**Clock:** Self-set edition cycles of several years
**Buyer:** Director of the style programme or press
