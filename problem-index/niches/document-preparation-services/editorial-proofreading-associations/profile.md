# Editorial & Proofreading Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/document-preparation-services|Document Preparation Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Editorial Freelancers Association (which publishes rate guidance for editors), ACES: The Society for Editing, and the Board of Editors in the Life Sciences (which certifies manuscript editors) support the freelance and staff editors who do much of this code's proofreading and copy-editing work, through training, job boards, conferences and credentials.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 staff each (estimate), largely volunteer-run
**Output:** Rate surveys, training, conferences, certification exams, member directories
**Proprietary data:** Member rate surveys and directories
**Clock:** Self-set
**Buyer:** Executive director or board
