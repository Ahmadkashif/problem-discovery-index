# Proofreading & Style-Guide Conformance

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/document-preparation-services|Document Preparation Services]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to bring a document into line with a named style guide while changing nothing the guide doesn't require, and whoever returns a change log the client can accept without rereading the document takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M US spend on contracted proofreading, copy-editing to house style and technical editing for businesses, law firms, nonprofits, agencies and self-publishing authors (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~11% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — work in Word and PDF with tracked changes; style rules applied from memory and checklists
**Target Buyer:** Owner of an editing and proofreading firm; publications or communications manager buying editing
**Automation Potential:** High for consistency and style rules; medium for judgement calls on clarity and meaning

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Census puts editing, proofreading and technical editing in this code. Clients name a standard: APA, Chicago, AMA for medical writing, AP for communications, or a house style guide for an annual report, a grant application or a product manual. They pay per word, per page or per hour. The value is consistency and correctness without unwanted changes: the client wants to accept the edits, not re-read the document. Freelance marketplaces and generic grammar tools have pushed simple proofreading toward commodity pricing, so firms compete on style-guide mastery, technical subject matter and the reliability of their change record.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Word tracked changes and macros, PerfectIt for consistency checking, Grammarly and similar tools, style manuals online, and house style sheets kept as Word files. The gaps: house styles not machine-readable, consistency checked by eye across long documents, and change logs that mix required corrections with editor preference.
