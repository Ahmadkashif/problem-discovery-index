# Academic Author-Services Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/document-preparation-services|Document Preparation Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Cactus Communications (Editage, Paperpal), AJE (now owned by MPS Limited after Springer Nature's ownership), Enago (Crimson Interactive, which also makes Trinka) and Scribendi aggregate academic editing across large networks of subject-specialist editors. They sell English-language editing, formatting, translation and submission support to researchers worldwide, many of them non-native English speakers, and increasingly sell AI writing tools and manuscript-screening services to journals and publishers. They are the largest operators in the academic half of this code.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-50 analytics, research-integrity and data staff per firm (estimate); editors themselves are production
**Output:** Edited manuscripts and formatted submissions; AI writing tools; publisher-facing integrity checks and researcher surveys
**Proprietary data:** Millions of pre-submission manuscripts before and after editing, with journal targets and sometimes outcomes
**Clock:** Authors' submission and grant deadlines, which are the clients' own schedules
**Buyer:** Chief technology or data officer at the author-services firm

**Kill switches:** live — unpublished manuscripts are the authors' confidential work supplied for editing, with reuse restricted by the service terms and research confidentiality (data isn't theirs to use).
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. Editing is the invoice, the analytics team is small beside the editor network, and the richest corpus belongs to the authors.
