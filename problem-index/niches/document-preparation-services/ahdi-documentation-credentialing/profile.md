# AHDI & Healthcare Documentation Credentialing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/document-preparation-services|Document Preparation Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Association for Healthcare Documentation Integrity sets the profession's standards for medical transcriptionists, now called healthcare documentation specialists, and for scribes. It publishes the quality assessment best practices revised in May 2025 (98.0 minimum, critical errors at minus three) and back-end speech-recognition guidance, and awards the RHDS and CHDS credentials. Volunteer task forces of vendor and health-system quality leads write much of the guidance.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 staff (estimate), with volunteer task forces
**Output:** Best-practice toolkits, credential exams, continuing education, a style and nomenclature guide
**Proprietary data:** Credential exam results and member surveys
**Clock:** Self-set revision cycles
**Buyer:** Executive director
