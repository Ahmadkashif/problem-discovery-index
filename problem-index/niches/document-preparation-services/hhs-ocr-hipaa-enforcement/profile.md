# HHS Office for Civil Rights HIPAA Enforcement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/document-preparation-services|Document Preparation Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Office for Civil Rights at the Department of Health and Human Services enforces the HIPAA Privacy, Security and Breach Notification Rules, including against business associates such as medical transcription and documentation vendors. It investigates complaints and breach reports, publishes the breach portal and settles cases with corrective action plans.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Investigators and attorneys working cases; no dedicated research function on documentation vendors (estimate)
**Output:** Investigations, resolution agreements, guidance
**Proprietary data:** Complaint and breach-report files
**Clock:** Statutory breach-notification timelines bind the regulated entities, not OCR's analysis
**Buyer:** OCR director
