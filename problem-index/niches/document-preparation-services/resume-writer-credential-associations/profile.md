# Resume Writing & Career Credential Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/document-preparation-services|Document Preparation Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Professional Association of Résumé Writers & Career Coaches (CPRW credential), the National Résumé Writers' Association (NCRW, renewed every three years with continuing education), Career Directors International and similar groups run credentials, conferences, training and directories for independent resume writers and career coaches.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 staff each (estimate), often run by a founder or a volunteer board
**Output:** Credential exams, training, conferences, member directories, occasional member surveys
**Proprietary data:** Exam submissions and member lists
**Clock:** Self-set event and certification calendars
**Buyer:** Executive director or founder
