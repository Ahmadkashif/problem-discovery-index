# Resume Parsing & Job-Matching Engine Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/document-preparation-services|Document Preparation Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Textkernel (which absorbed Sovren), Daxtra, HireAbility and the parsing teams inside applicant tracking systems build the engines that read resumes into structured fields and match them to jobs. Every resume writer's "ATS-friendly" claim is a bet on how these engines behave. Consumer tools such as Jobscan sell writers and job seekers a simulation of them. The engines' buyers are ATS vendors, staffing firms and employers, not the writers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-80 NLP, taxonomy and data staff per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Parsing and matching software licensed per document or per seat; skills taxonomies
**Proprietary data:** Parsed resumes and match outcomes processed for customers, plus taxonomy work
**Clock:** None set by a third party on the analysis
**Buyer:** Chief product or data officer at the parsing vendor
