# The Evidence Drive in the Mail

**Niche:** [[niches/document-preparation-services/evidentiary-transcription/profile|Evidentiary & Investigative Transcription]]
**Industry:** [[industries/document-preparation-services|Document Preparation Services]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Recordings still arrive on thumb drives, discs and ad hoc file shares, and the custody record is a set of emails nobody could reconstruct under oath.
**Tags:** #data-integration #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn poor evidentiary audio into certified verbatim transcripts, speakers attributed and uncertainty marked, that hold up under cross-examination and arrive before the discovery or trial date, and whoever delivers the hardest recordings defensibly on that clock takes the account.

## The Problem
A small transcription firm receives evidence the way each agency sends it: a courier with a drive, a disc burned from an interview room, a link to an evidence portal that expires, an email attachment. Files are copied to a transcriptionist's home computer, sometimes in several formats. When a transcript is challenged, the firm must show which file it worked from, who handled it and that it wasn't altered, and the answer lives in inboxes.

## Why It's Still Broken
Each agency has its own system and its own rules, and the firm is too small to integrate with them. Criminal-justice data rules (the FBI CJIS Security Policy for many agencies) make firms cautious about any new tool.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A single intake portal that hashes every file on receipt, records who accessed it and when, streams audio to transcriptionists without local copies, and prints a custody summary with each certified transcript. Connectors for the common agency evidence portals replace couriers where agencies allow it.

## Who Feels the Pain
Transcription firm owners answering custody questions; transcriptionists juggling file formats; prosecutors and defence counsel whose transcript is challenged.

## Impact If Fixed
A custody record that takes seconds to produce, fewer lost or duplicated files, and a firm able to meet agencies' security requirements without heavy IT.
