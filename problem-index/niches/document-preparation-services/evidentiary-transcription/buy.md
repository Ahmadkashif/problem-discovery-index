# eDiscovery Transcription Adapted to Certified Evidence

**Niche:** [[niches/document-preparation-services/evidentiary-transcription/profile|Evidentiary & Investigative Transcription]]
**Industry:** [[industries/document-preparation-services|Document Preparation Services]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** eDiscovery platforms already transcribe audio for searching; an evidentiary transcript needs verbatim conventions, speaker attribution and a certification those transcripts don't carry.
**Tags:** #transformers #large-language-models #data-integration #compliance #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn poor evidentiary audio into certified verbatim transcripts, speakers attributed and uncertainty marked, that hold up under cross-examination and arrive before the discovery or trial date, and whoever delivers the hardest recordings defensibly on that clock takes the account.

## The Problem
Prosecutors and defence teams review terabytes of video and audio, and the platforms they use generate machine transcripts so reviewers can search and jump to moments. When a recording matters, someone still commissions a certified human transcript, often from a different vendor, and the two never meet.

## What Already Exists
eDiscovery and digital-evidence platforms (Relativity, Everlaw, and the evidence systems police agencies use) with automatic transcription, search and clip tools; generic recognisers; and transcription firms working in Word.

## The Customization Gap
The evidentiary layer needs agency and court transcript conventions (verbatim marks, speaker labels by role, time-coding to the original media, overlapping-speech notation); a handoff that sends a selected segment from the review platform to a certified transcriptionist with the custody record attached; bilingual handling with translation marked separately from transcription; and a returned transcript linked back to the media timestamps in the review platform, so the search transcript and the certified one are reconciled.

## Target Customer
Litigation-support and evidence managers in prosecutors' and public defenders' offices; transcription firms serving them.

## Impact If Solved
Certified transcripts commissioned in minutes from the moment a reviewer flags a clip, with custody and timestamps intact, rather than by email and file transfer days later.
