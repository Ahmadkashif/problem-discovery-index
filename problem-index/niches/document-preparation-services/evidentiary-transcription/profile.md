# Evidentiary & Investigative Transcription

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/document-preparation-services|Document Preparation Services]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn poor evidentiary audio into certified verbatim transcripts, speakers attributed and uncertainty marked, that hold up under cross-examination and arrive before the discovery or trial date, and whoever delivers the hardest recordings defensibly on that clock takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$250M US spend on transcription of law-enforcement, investigative, insurance and non-court legal recordings (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — evidence arrives on drives, discs and agency portals, and transcripts are formatted by hand to each agency's conventions
**Target Buyer:** Owner of a legal and law-enforcement transcription firm; records or evidence manager at a police department or prosecutor's office; insurer special-investigations manager
**Automation Potential:** Medium — recognition helps on clear speech, but the defensible transcript of poor audio is human work

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Body-worn camera audio, suspect and witness interviews, jail calls, wiretaps, 911 recordings and insurance recorded statements have to be written down verbatim, with every speaker attributed and every inaudible passage marked. Prosecutors, defence lawyers, insurers and courts rely on them. Spanish-English audio often needs translation and transcription together, the territory of [[industries/localization-services|Localization Services]]. Transcripts are frequently certified, chain of custody matters, and since January 2026 California's SB 524 requires police reports drafted with generative AI, such as Axon's Draft One working from bodycam audio, to carry a disclosure and keep the first draft. Recordings of court proceedings, depositions and examinations under oath taken by stenographers are a different code and belong to [[industries/court-reporting-firms|Court Reporting Firms]]. Defence teams' review side is [[niches/legal-practice-software/criminal-defense-discovery/profile|Criminal Defense & Digital Discovery]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Foot pedals and Express Scribe, eDiscovery platforms with built-in transcription, agency evidence systems, generic recognisers and Word templates per agency. The gaps: no calibrated marking of uncertain words, no consistent speaker attribution on chaotic scenes, custody logged by email, and traumatic content assigned blind.
