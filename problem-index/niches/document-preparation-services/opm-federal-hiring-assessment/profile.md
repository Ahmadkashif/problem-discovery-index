# OPM Federal Hiring Policy & Assessment Units

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/document-preparation-services|Document Preparation Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Office of Personnel Management sets the rules federal resume writers write to: qualification standards by series and grade, the two-page resume limit in force since 27 September 2025, and the Merit Hiring Plan's essay questions. Its assessment psychologists design and run hiring assessments that agencies use to rank applicants. In effect it is the regulator, and the designer of the screen, for the federal-resume niche.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~30-100 personnel research psychologists, policy analysts and assessment staff (estimate)
**Output:** Qualification standards, hiring policy and guidance, validated assessments and the ranking results agencies receive
**Proprietary data:** Applicant assessment and selection records across agencies, the largest body of evidence on how federal applicants are screened
**Clock:** Policy and executive-order deadlines and agencies' hiring announcements
**Buyer:** Associate director for hiring policy or assessment at OPM
