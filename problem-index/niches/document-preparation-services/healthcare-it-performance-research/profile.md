# Healthcare IT Performance Research Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/document-preparation-services|Document Preparation Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
KLAS Research interviews provider organisations about the software and services they use and publishes vendor performance ratings, market reports and the annual Best in KLAS rankings. Its coverage includes the speech recognition and ambient documentation tools that are absorbing medical transcription (a 2025 Best in KLAS ambient speech segment; a September 2025 report from interviews with 35 Epic customers on Epic's native ambient tool) and the transcription and documentation services vendors themselves. Vendors pay for reports, data and advisory; provider organisations get access by participating. A few smaller firms (Black Book and the healthcare practices of general IT analyst houses) work the same ground.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~100-300 researchers, interviewers and analysts at the largest firm (estimate)
**Output:** Vendor performance reports, segment rankings, emerging-technology studies, custom research for vendors and providers
**Proprietary data:** Years of structured provider interviews scoring named products on outcomes, support and loyalty — a voice-of-the-customer corpus no competitor has at the same depth
**Clock:** Self-set: annual Best in KLAS cycle and report calendar; vendors' sales cycles are not a third-party deadline
**Buyer:** Head of research or analytics at the research firm
