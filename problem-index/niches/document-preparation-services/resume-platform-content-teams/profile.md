# Resume Platform Groups' Content & Research Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/document-preparation-services|Document Preparation Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The groups that roll up resume services and builders (Talent Inc. with TopResume, TopCV and Resume.io; Bold with LiveCareer, Zety and MyPerfectResume; and others) publish "career studies", hiring-manager surveys and resume statistics to win search traffic and press coverage for their consumer brands.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically under 10 people producing research content per group (estimate); the rest is marketing, product analytics and writer operations
**Output:** Survey-based reports and statistics used as marketing content
**Proprietary data:** Order and builder usage data, rarely joined to job-search outcomes
**Clock:** Self-set publishing calendars
**Buyer:** Head of content or marketing
