# Resume Writing & Career Documents

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/document-preparation-services|Document Preparation Services]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor is fighting to turn a client's career history into documents that win interviews, but volume platforms and senior-career boutiques win that fight on different terms: writer-hours and refund rates at a flat fee on one side, positioning that reaches retained recruiters and boards on the other. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$700M US spend on professionally written resumes, cover letters, LinkedIn profiles and executive bios (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~20% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — a questionnaire, a phone interview, a Word file and email revisions
**Target Buyer:** Owner of a resume writing practice; operations head at a resume platform
**Automation Potential:** Medium — drafting automates; the interview that finds the accomplishment and the judgement about positioning do not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Resume writing is sold per document or package, by thousands of solo practitioners, small boutiques and a few volume platforms. Talent Inc. (TopResume, TopCV, Resume.io) built the largest by acquisition, with writers working as independent contractors for a flat fee per order. Credentials are voluntary (PARWCC's CPRW, NRWA's NCRW, which must be renewed every three years). Two forces press on every writer: consumer AI builders that produce a passable resume for free, and applicant tracking systems whose parsing and keyword screens decide whether a human ever reads the document. No one measures outcomes, so writers sell on testimonials.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Word and Google Docs templates, questionnaires in Typeform or Jotform, HoneyBook or Dubsado for orders, Jobscan-style keyword matchers, and recording tools for the intake call. The gaps: interview notes turned into accomplishments by hand, nothing tracking whether documents won interviews, and revision rounds nobody prices.
