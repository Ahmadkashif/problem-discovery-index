## The Problem
Writers send a questionnaire of many open questions: targets, achievements, metrics, reasons for leaving, gaps. Clients answer the easy ones, skip the hard ones, and the order stalls for days. Writers then chase by email or start drafting with holes.

## Why It's Still Broken
The questionnaire is a static form designed for completeness, not for the client's patience. Writers fear that shortening it loses the detail the call depends on.

## What a Fix Looks Like
An adaptive intake that starts from the uploaded resume, asks only what is missing for each role (scope, metric, result) and accepts voice answers. It summarises what it already knows so the client corrects rather than writes, and hands the writer a gap list for the call.

## Who Feels the Pain
Writers losing days per order to chasing; clients overwhelmed by a blank form at a stressful moment in their career.

## Impact If Fixed
Orders start faster, intake calls go deeper because the basics are done, and drafts need fewer revision rounds.
