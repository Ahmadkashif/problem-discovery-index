# The Outcome Record the Trade Never Kept

**Niche:** [[niches/document-preparation-services/resume-career-documents/profile|Resume Writing & Career Documents]]
**Industry:** [[industries/document-preparation-services|Document Preparation Services]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Resume writers sell interviews and never learn whether their documents won any, so neither they nor their clients can tell good practice from habit.
**Tags:** #survival-analysis #causal-inference #logistic-regression #feature-engineering #revenue-impact #worker-facing
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor is fighting to turn a client's career history into documents that win interviews, but volume platforms and senior-career boutiques win that fight on different terms: writer-hours and refund rates at a flat fee on one side, positioning that reaches retained recruiters and boards on the other. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
A writer delivers a resume and the client vanishes into a job search. Some come back for an update years later, some send a thank-you note, most say nothing. Practices that every writer argues about (one page or two, a summary or not, how many metrics, which format survives parsing) are settled by opinion and association conference sessions, not evidence.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Clients have no reason to report outcomes after they are hired. Solo writers see too few cases to learn from even if they did. Applicant tracking systems hold the callback data, and they belong to employers.

## What to Build
A client-side application tracker the writer hands over with the documents. It logs applications, callbacks and interviews with almost no effort, and it is worth using to the client because it organises the search. Outcomes then pool across participating writers (with consent and without client identities) into time-to-interview models by document feature, adjusted for field, seniority and market. Each writer gets a private dashboard of their own results against the pool.

## Target Customer
Independent writers and boutiques, the resume associations that could sponsor a shared tracker, and platforms with enough volume to learn alone.

## Impact If Built
For the first time writers could sell on evidence and drop practices that don't help. They would also have an answer when a client asks why a human beats a free AI builder.
