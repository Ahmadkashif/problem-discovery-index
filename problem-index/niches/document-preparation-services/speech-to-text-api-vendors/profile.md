# Speech-to-Text API Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/document-preparation-services|Document Preparation Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Deepgram, AssemblyAI, Speechmatics, the hyperscalers' speech services and OpenAI's Whisper-family models sell or release the recognisers that transcription shops, platforms and their customers build on. They price per audio hour, publish accuracy benchmarks, and compete to make the human transcription pass unnecessary.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-200 speech research and ML staff per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Recognition, diarisation and audio-intelligence APIs and models; public benchmark claims
**Proprietary data:** Licensed and customer-contributed training audio, evaluation sets
**Clock:** None set by a third party
**Buyer:** Head of research or product at the API vendor

**Kill switches:** live — customers' audio reaches these vendors under API agreements that commonly bar retention or training without the customer's opt-in; without checked terms permitting aggregate use, the customer corpus is not theirs to use.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. Model research that is sold as minutes of compute, not insight; the human-verified corpora that matter sit with the transcription platforms.
