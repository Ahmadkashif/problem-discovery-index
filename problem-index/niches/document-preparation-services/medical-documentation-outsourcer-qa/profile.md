# Healthcare Documentation Outsourcers' QA Departments

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/document-preparation-services|Document Preparation Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The surviving medical transcription and documentation vendors, among them DeliverHealth (which bought Nuance's transcription business and eScription platform in 2021), AQuity Solutions and iMedX, aggregate health systems' dictation across onshore and offshore editing teams. Each runs a quality department that audits editors' reports against AHDI's 98.0 standard (AQuity adds a 0.25-point minor-error tier) and reports accuracy and turnaround to clients.

## Insight Function
**Size:** QA editors and auditors, sometimes dozens per vendor, all scoring production work
**Output:** Audit scores, editor feedback, client accuracy reports
**Proprietary data:** Audit sheets and draft–final pairs for client accounts
**Clock:** Contract turnaround and monthly client reporting
**Buyer:** Director of Quality
