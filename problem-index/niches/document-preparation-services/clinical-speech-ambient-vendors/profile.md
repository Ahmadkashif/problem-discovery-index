# Clinical Speech Recognition & Ambient Documentation Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/document-preparation-services|Document Preparation Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Microsoft (Dragon Copilot, which since March 2025 combines Dragon Medical One dictation with DAX ambient listening), Solventum (Fluency Direct and Fluency for Transcription), Abridge, Ambience and Suki sell the front-end recognition and ambient documentation that replace transcription. Some also supply the back-end engines documentation vendors edit on. They are suppliers and substitutes at once, and their clinical AI teams decide how much dictation is left for the industry.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~50-500 clinical NLP, speech and evaluation staff per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Models and software subscriptions; evaluation studies of documentation quality and clinician time saved
**Proprietary data:** Encounter audio paired with clinician-edited notes across their customer bases
**Clock:** Health systems' go-live and contract dates; no third party sets a deadline on the analysis
**Buyer:** Chief medical information officer or head of clinical AI at the vendor
