# The Claim a Recruiter Can't Verify

**Niche:** [[niches/document-preparation-services/executive-resume-boutiques/profile|Executive & Senior-Career Resume Boutiques]]
**Industry:** [[industries/document-preparation-services|Document Preparation Services]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** "Grew revenue 40%" at an unnamed division with no date is the sentence that makes a search researcher move on.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #evaluation-metrics #quick-win #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a senior leader's record into a resume, LinkedIn profile and bio that retained recruiters and boards shortlist, and whoever converts a long discovery interview into quantified, verifiable leadership evidence fastest takes the account.

## The Problem
Executive resumes are full of percentages without baselines, divisions without names and results without dates, often because the client is protecting confidential figures or misremembering them. Researchers read these as either inflation or a candidate who can't be checked, and both hurt.

## Why It's Still Broken
Writers work under client confidentiality concerns and tight timelines, and a checklist for verifiability is rarely applied claim by claim.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A pass over every quantified claim that asks: baseline stated, period stated, unit named, and can it be expressed without breaching confidentiality, for example as a range, a rank or a public figure? The writer gets a short list of rewrites and questions for the client before delivery.

## Who Feels the Pain
Senior candidates screened out silently; writers whose work is judged on outcomes they never see.

## Impact If Fixed
Claims that read as credible on first pass, and fewer candidacies lost to a sentence.
