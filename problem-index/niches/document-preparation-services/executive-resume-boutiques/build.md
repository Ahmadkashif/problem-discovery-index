# Leadership Evidence Checked Against the Public Record

**Niche:** [[niches/document-preparation-services/executive-resume-boutiques/profile|Executive & Senior-Career Resume Boutiques]]
**Industry:** [[industries/document-preparation-services|Document Preparation Services]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** An executive resume stands or falls on claims of scope and results that search researchers will check, and boutiques assemble them from memory and interview notes without checking first.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #feature-engineering #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a senior leader's record into a resume, LinkedIn profile and bio that retained recruiters and boards shortlist, and whoever converts a long discovery interview into quantified, verifiable leadership evidence fastest takes the account.

## The Problem
A boutique writer spends ~2–3 hours (estimate) interviewing an executive and more hours turning the transcript into a narrative: the business they ran, its size, what changed under them. Executives misremember dates, inflate scope and use internal names nobody outside recognises. Recruiters and board committees cross-check against filings, press releases and profile databases, and a mismatch ends a candidacy quietly.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Boutiques are tiny and craft-proud, and general AI tools write fluent claims without evidence. The useful public sources (filings, press, deal announcements, board disclosures) are scattered.

## What to Build
A research assistant that extracts every claim from the interview and draft (role, scope, revenue, headcount, outcomes, dates) and looks for corroborating public evidence. For public companies that means filings and earnings materials, plus press releases, deal records and board appointments. Each claim is marked as corroborated, consistent but unverifiable, or contradicted, so the writer resolves discrepancies with the client before a recruiter finds them. It also drafts the bio and board CV from the same verified claims.

## Target Customer
Executive resume and branding boutiques; career coaches working with senior leaders.

## Impact If Built
Senior candidates whose documents hold up under diligence, and writers who spend their hours on positioning rather than fact-finding.
