**Tags:** #large-language-models #k-nearest-neighbors #feature-engineering #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a senior leader's record into a resume, LinkedIn profile and bio that retained recruiters and boards shortlist, and whoever converts a long discovery interview into quantified, verifiable leadership evidence fastest takes the account.

## The Problem
Retained search researchers build longlists from profile databases, LinkedIn searches and their own files, using titles, company tiers, scope signals and keywords. An executive whose profile uses internal titles or omits the signals researchers search on never reaches the longlist, whatever their resume says.

## What Already Exists
Search-firm research and CRM tools, executive profile databases and recruiter search products used on the search side, plus public company data and simple keyword matchers on the candidate side.

## The Customization Gap
The candidate-side adaptation simulates the researcher's search. It infers the queries a search for the client's target role would run (titles, sectors, company sizes, functional scope), tests how the client's LinkedIn profile and bio would rank against comparable executives, and suggests the external-facing titles and scope statements that close the gap, without the stuffing researchers recognise.

## Target Customer
Executive resume boutiques and senior-career coaches.

## Impact If Solved
Clients who appear on the longlists their experience warrants, and a measurable before-and-after the boutique can show.
