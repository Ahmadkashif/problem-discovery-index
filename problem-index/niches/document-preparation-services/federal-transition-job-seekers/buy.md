# Keyword Matchers Retargeted at Qualification Standards

**Niche:** [[niches/document-preparation-services/federal-transition-job-seekers/profile|Federal & Military-Transition Job Seekers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/document-preparation-services|Document Preparation Services]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Resume-to-posting matchers score keyword overlap for corporate applicant tracking systems; a federal screen is a person applying a qualification standard to evidence.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #k-nearest-neighbors #feature-engineering #automation #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get an applicant rated qualified on an announcement's specialized-experience screen inside OPM's two-page limit, and whoever translates a career, often a military one, into the grade's qualification language in two pages takes the account.

## The Problem
Federal applicants and their writers use commercial matchers built for private-sector screens. They reward repeating the posting's words and ignore what a federal specialist checks: level of responsibility, duration and grade equivalence.

## What Already Exists
Jobscan-style resume-to-posting matchers, AI resume builders, and military skills translators that map occupational codes to civilian titles.

## The Customization Gap
The federal version reads announcements in their standard structure (qualifications, specialized experience, education substitution, time-in-grade). It checks duration and grade evidence, not just keywords, and handles the two-page limit by ranking content on its value for the specific announcement. It supports the Merit Hiring Plan's essay questions as separate drafts, and draws on OPM's published qualification standards for the series.

## Target Customer
Federal resume writers; federal employees seeking promotion or transfer; transition programme staff.

## Impact If Solved
Applications built for the screen they will actually face, and a faster cut from a long career history to two pages.
