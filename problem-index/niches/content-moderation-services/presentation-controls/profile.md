# Presentation & Dosimetry

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/content-moderation-services|Content Moderation Services]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Whether the intensity of each unavoidable exposure, and its accumulation across a shift and a career, is deliberately controlled or left to a full-fidelity default nobody chose.

## Profile

**Market Size:** ~$1.0B
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8%
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — optional toggles, unmeasured
**Target Buyer:** Vendor operations and clinical staff, vendor general counsel
**Automation Potential:** High for the controls, low for the clinical calibration

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche

Some material genuinely requires human judgement. This is the half of exposure management that governs what happens then: at what fidelity it is shown, which portion of it is shown, for how long, and how the accumulation is accounted for across a shift, a month and a career.

It is a different discipline from its sibling. [[niches/content-moderation-services/exposure-triage/profile|🎯 Exposure Triage]] is classification and routing — reduce the count, measurable in weeks, and it lowers cost as a side effect, which is why any vendor would build it first. Dosimetry is interface design and occupational health. Its evidence is clinical and accrues over months, it lowers no cost at all, and it requires defining a unit of psychological exposure that does not yet exist. A vendor can build triage and stop, and almost all would.

That is exactly why the durable position is here. Triage reduces how often people are harmed; dosimetry is the half that would let a vendor say how much harm its operation causes and demonstrate that it is bounded — which is the claim that matters in front of a regulator, a court, or a client under scrutiny.

The contest is over whether full fidelity is a decision or a default. In most operations nobody chose it; the review tool renders what was uploaded, a quality auditor later grades against what was uploaded, and the reviewer who reduces fidelity to protect themselves risks being marked wrong.

## Current Tools & Gaps

Review interfaces at the larger platforms offer some viewer-side controls — greyscale, blur, audio muting, frame-stepping instead of playback. They are opt-in, individually toggled, unrecorded, and frequently reset between items. Rotation between severe and ordinary queues is practised informally at some vendors. Breaks and counselling are contractual in better agreements.

The gaps are the whole discipline. There is no unit of exposure, no dose-response evidence base and no threshold, so there is nothing to accumulate against. Fidelity defaults to maximum because that is what the auditor will grade. Segment isolation — showing the eleven seconds at issue rather than the twenty-minute file — is technically trivial and rarely implemented. Nothing tracks cumulative severe exposure, so nothing can cap it. Recovery is discretionary, which means it is taken least by the people struggling most. And clinical surveillance, where it exists, is voluntary and disconnected from any exposure record, so no vendor can relate what a reviewer saw to how they are doing.
