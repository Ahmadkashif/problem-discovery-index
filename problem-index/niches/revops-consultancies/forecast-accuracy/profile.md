# Forecast Accuracy

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/revops-consultancies|RevOps Consultancies]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to improve a forecast whose accuracy nobody can measure, because the series required to score it is destroyed every week — and whoever makes it scoreable takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.6B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~27% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — unscored
**Target Buyer:** Revenue leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — capture plus scoring

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every revenue organisation forecasts weekly, almost none retain the forecasts, and the discipline that exists to make revenue predictable cannot say whether its own predictions are getting better. Forecast accuracy is the single most requested improvement in this field, and the number that would establish whether any change helped is overwritten by the process that produces it. It is a remarkable state for a quantitative function inside organisations that measure everything else.

### Contested sub-niches
This niche is not terminal. It names a property, and obtaining it requires two separately bought things:
- [[niches/revops-consultancies/snapshot-retention/profile|🎯 Forecast Snapshot Retention]] — creating the series at all
- [[niches/revops-consultancies/methodology-and-scoring/profile|🎯 Forecast Methodology & Scoring]] — using it to grade and improve

## Current Tools & Gaps
A CRM forecast field, a weekly spreadsheet, a commit call and a number that gets overwritten. The gaps: no retained history of forecasts; no scoring of any past forecast; no measured bias by representative, stage or segment; no comparison between methods; and no evidence that any methodology change helped.
