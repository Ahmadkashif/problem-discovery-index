# Forecast Snapshot Retention

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/revops-consultancies|RevOps Consultancies]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to capture every forecast at every level before it is overwritten, because nothing downstream is possible without that series — and whoever captures it takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$0.85B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~14% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — nothing retained
**Target Buyer:** Revenue operations
**Automation Potential:** Very high — scheduled capture

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This half requires no statistics and no methodology change. It is capture: snapshot the forecast fields, the pipeline, the stage distribution and the commit positions on a schedule, store them immutably, and keep them. It is a week of engineering, it can be bought and implemented without anyone agreeing on a forecasting method, and it is the precondition for every claim about improvement that the discipline makes. Its separateness is what makes it the correct first purchase.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A CRM field updated in place and a spreadsheet overwritten weekly. The gaps: no scheduled capture; no history table; no capture at representative and manager level; no pipeline snapshot alongside; and no recovery of whatever history exists in archives.
