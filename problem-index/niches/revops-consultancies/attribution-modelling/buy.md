# Incrementality Testing From Performance Marketing

**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Performance marketing learned that attribution and incrementality disagree and built the tests, and business-to-business attribution runs on the model alone.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to say which activities produced revenue, using a model whose output nobody has ever checked against a controlled test — and whoever validates it takes the account.

## The Problem
Consumer performance marketing learned the hard way that attributed conversions and incremental conversions are different quantities, often by a large factor, and built the response: geographic holdouts, audience-level tests, and media mix models validated against experiments. The practice is standard among large advertisers and the lesson — that attribution without validation misallocates budget systematically — is well established. Business-to-business revenue attribution has the same problem with longer cycles and no tests.

## What Already Exists
Geographic and audience holdout designs; incrementality measurement as routine practice; media mix modelling calibrated against experiments; test-and-learn budget allocation; and published methodology for validating attribution.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to long, multi-person, high-value sales cycles. It requires: (1) purchase decisions made by buying groups over quarters rather than individuals in sessions, so the unit of measurement is an account over time rather than a user in a funnel — this is the substantive difference; (2) deal counts small enough that experimental power is a genuine constraint; (3) touches that include human sales activity as well as marketing, which consumer measurement does not have to attribute; (4) holdouts that are hard to construct when the addressable market is a named account list; and (5) revenue recognised long after the activity, stretching every measurement window.

## Target Customer
Marketing and revenue leadership, RevOps consultancies, attribution vendors, and marketing measurement providers.

## Impact If Solved
Performance marketing established that attributed and incremental conversions differ by large factors and built the tests. Buying groups deciding over quarters, with small deal counts, is what makes the experimental design genuinely hard here.
