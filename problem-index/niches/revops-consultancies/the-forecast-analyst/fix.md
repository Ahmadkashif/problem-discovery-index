# Chasing Updates That Should Be in the System

**Niche:** [[niches/revops-consultancies/the-forecast-analyst/profile|The Weekly Forecast Analyst]]
**Industry:** [[industries/revops-consultancies|RevOps Consultancies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The analyst spends Tuesday sending reminder messages to eleven representatives about deals they were supposed to update on Monday.
**Tags:** #worker-facing #quick-win #automation #workflow-orchestration #evaluation-metrics #descriptive-statistics #data-integration #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stop one analyst spending every week assembling a pack from numbers that disagree for reasons everyone has stopped questioning — and whoever automates that takes the account.

## The Problem
A meaningful share of the analyst's week is chasing. Deals have not been updated, stages are stale, close dates are in the past, and the forecast cannot be assembled until the representatives fix them. The analyst sends messages, waits, sends more, and escalates to managers. It is social work performed by someone with no authority, it recurs identically every week, and both the analyst and the representatives resent it.

## Why It's Still Broken
The requirement is not enforced anywhere — a deadline that exists only as an expectation is met by whoever feels like it, and the person chasing has no standing to change that. The system does not block. Managers do not follow up consistently. And the analyst absorbing it means nobody senior experiences the problem.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Put the enforcement in the system and take it off the analyst. Automate the reminders so no person is sending them, which is the fix and removes the social cost immediately. Escalate to the manager automatically on a schedule rather than via the analyst, since that is where the authority is. Report update compliance by representative and manager, which makes the pattern visible to the people who can act on it. Flag stale deals in the representative's own view rather than in the analyst's report. Block or flag the forecast submission where required fields are not current, which is a system control rather than a request. Reduce what must be updated weekly to the minimum that matters, since much of the requirement is habit. Make updating fast on a phone, as that is where the representative is. Show representatives why the update matters rather than only that it is required. Measure the chasing time so the cost is visible. And make the analyst's role assembling insight rather than collecting inputs, which is the point of the whole fix.

## Who Feels the Pain
Analysts doing social enforcement with no authority; representatives receiving reminders from someone who cannot help them; managers unaware of the compliance pattern; and the forecast, assembled from whatever arrived in time.

## Impact If Fixed
A deadline that exists only as an expectation is met by whoever feels like it, and the person chasing has no standing to change that. Automated reminders with manager escalation removes the social cost and makes the pattern visible.
