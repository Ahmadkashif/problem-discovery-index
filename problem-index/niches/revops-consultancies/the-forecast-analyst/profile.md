# The Weekly Forecast Analyst

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/revops-consultancies|RevOps Consultancies]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stop one analyst spending every week assembling a pack from numbers that disagree for reasons everyone has stopped questioning — and whoever automates that takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$0.6B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — manual
**Target Buyer:** RevOps leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very high — assembly, chasing and reconciliation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every week an analyst assembles the forecast pack, chases representatives for updates that should already be in the system, and reconciles numbers that disagree for reasons everyone has stopped questioning. It is the same work every week, it consumes a skilled person entirely, and the output is a deck that is discussed and discarded. The role exists because the systems do not agree and the process does not enforce itself, and it absorbs both failures invisibly.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A CRM report, a spreadsheet, a slide template and a sequence of reminder messages. The gaps: no automated pack assembly; no enforcement of update deadlines; no reconciliation between systems; no explanation of recurring discrepancies; and no measurement of how much of the week this consumes.
