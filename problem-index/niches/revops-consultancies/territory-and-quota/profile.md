# Territory & Quota Design

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/revops-consultancies|RevOps Consultancies]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to set territories and quotas that are actually attainable across a sales team, when the method is splitting last year's number in a spreadsheet — and whoever models it takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$0.7B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** Sales leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — optimisation and equity modelling

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Territories and quotas are set annually in a spreadsheet by splitting last year's number, and the resulting inequity is the single largest driver of sales attrition nobody models. Some representatives are given a territory that cannot produce the number; others are given one where the number arrives without effort. Both outcomes damage the organisation, both are visible in the data used to set the quotas, and neither is examined because the exercise is treated as arithmetic rather than as design.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A spreadsheet, last year's attainment and a growth target. The gaps: no measurement of territory potential; no equity analysis across the team; no attainment distribution modelling; no link between quota design and attrition; and no evaluation of last year's design against what happened.
