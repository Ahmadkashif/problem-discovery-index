# Quotas That Can Actually Be Hit

**Niche:** [[niches/revops-consultancies/territory-and-quota/profile|Territory & Quota Design]]
**Industry:** [[industries/revops-consultancies|RevOps Consultancies]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** The largest driver of sales attrition is set in a spreadsheet by division.
**Tags:** #optimization-fundamentals #confidence-intervals #evaluation-metrics #gradient-boosting #descriptive-statistics #revenue-impact #hypothesis-testing #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to set territories and quotas that are actually attainable across a sales team, when the method is splitting last year's number in a spreadsheet — and whoever models it takes the account.

## The Problem
Territory and quota setting decides, for each salesperson, whether the year is achievable. It is done by taking the company target, dividing by the headcount with adjustments for seniority and last year's attainment, and assigning accounts by geography or industry. Territory potential is not measured. The resulting distribution of attainment is wildly uneven, the people in poor territories leave, and the cost of replacing them exceeds anything the exercise saved.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Territory potential is genuinely hard to measure and nobody has tried. The exercise happens once a year under time pressure. Attrition is attributed to individuals rather than to territory design. And the spreadsheet method produces a number that sums correctly, which is the only test applied.

## What to Build
Measure territory potential and design for an attainable distribution. Estimate each territory's realistic potential from account characteristics, historical performance and market data rather than from last year's result, which is the core and is the input the whole exercise lacks. Model the resulting attainment distribution before the year starts, since a design that puts a quarter of the team in an unattainable position is knowable in advance. Optimise the assignment for balanced potential rather than for equal headcount splits, which is a solvable assignment problem. Separate representative performance from territory quality in the historical data, as attributing a territory's result to the person is the error that propagates each year. Model the attrition cost of an inequitable design explicitly, which is the number that justifies doing this properly. Evaluate last year's design against what happened, which nobody does and which would improve each subsequent year. Handle mid-year changes, since territories move and quotas rarely follow. Show representatives the basis for their quota, which is the largest available improvement in perceived fairness. Test a proposed design against historical performance before committing to it. And treat the exercise as design with an objective rather than as arithmetic that must sum.

## Target Customer
Sales and revenue leadership, RevOps consultancies, sales performance management vendors, and compensation planning providers.

## Impact If Built
A design that puts a quarter of the team in an unattainable position is knowable before the year starts, and nobody looks. Measuring territory potential and modelling the attainment distribution turns arithmetic into design.
