# Templates and Blueprints From Platform Ecosystems

**Niche:** [[niches/revops-consultancies/funnel-model-reuse/profile|Funnel Model & Reporting Reuse]]
**Industry:** [[industries/revops-consultancies|RevOps Consultancies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Cloud platforms ship reference architectures and deployable blueprints, and revenue operations rebuilds the funnel model by hand.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #automation #data-integration #evaluation-metrics #sets-and-logic #descriptive-statistics #compliance #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stop rebuilding the same funnel model, stage definitions and dashboard set for the twentieth time, and whoever packages it takes the account.

## The Problem
Cloud and software platforms learned that a reference architecture nobody can deploy is a document, and shipped blueprints instead: parameterised, deployable, versioned templates that stand up a known-good configuration in minutes and can then be adapted. The pattern turned architectural knowledge into an artefact. Revenue operations has equally well-understood reference designs and distributes them as screenshots and prose.

## What Already Exists
Deployable parameterised blueprints; reference architectures with working implementations; versioning and update paths; validation against best practice; and marketplaces distributing them.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to business process configuration in a CRM rather than infrastructure. It requires: (1) the deployable unit being stage definitions, fields, reports and dashboards inside a SaaS platform rather than infrastructure resources, so deployment goes through platform-specific mechanisms with their own limits — this is the substantive difference; (2) parameterisation across business vocabulary rather than technical settings, since clients' stage names and segments carry meaning; (3) deployment into a live system with existing data rather than into an empty environment; (4) several unrelated platforms to support; and (5) adaptation after deployment being the norm, so the template must survive divergence.

## Target Customer
RevOps consultancies, in-house revenue operations, CRM and analytics vendors, and template and marketplace providers.

## Impact If Solved
Platforms replaced reference documents with deployable blueprints and turned architecture into an artefact. Deployment into a live CRM with existing data, parameterised on business vocabulary, is what the revenue version requires.
