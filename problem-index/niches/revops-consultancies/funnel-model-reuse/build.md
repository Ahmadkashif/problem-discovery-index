# The Twentieth Funnel Model, Deployed Rather Than Built

**Niche:** [[niches/revops-consultancies/funnel-model-reuse/profile|Funnel Model & Reporting Reuse]]
**Industry:** [[industries/revops-consultancies|RevOps Consultancies]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** The structure is the same every time and the build is from scratch every time.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #automation #data-integration #evaluation-metrics #descriptive-statistics #sets-and-logic #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stop rebuilding the same funnel model, stage definitions and dashboard set for the twentieth time, and whoever packages it takes the account.

## The Problem
Every engagement produces a funnel model: stages with entry and exit criteria, conversion reporting between them, velocity and ageing measures, cohort views, a dashboard set for representatives, managers and leadership. The definitions differ between clients in wording and segmentation and almost never in structure. Each is built by hand in the client's platform, tested, and then left behind when the engagement ends.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The artefacts live in clients' systems and are assumed unreusable. Consultancies sell days rather than assets. Platform differences make a universal template feel impossible. And rebuilding is billable.

## What to Build
Package the structure and parameterise the variation. Build deployable funnel model templates parameterised by stage names, segments and reporting emphasis, which is the core and captures the reuse without pretending clients are identical. Provide standard stage definitions with entry and exit criteria that a client adapts rather than invents, since that debate consumes days on every engagement and converges to the same place. Ship dashboard packs per audience — representative, manager, leadership — rather than building each. Include the conversion, velocity and ageing measures as standard rather than as bespoke analysis. Deploy into the client's platform through tooling rather than by manual configuration. Maintain the templates against platform releases, which is what stops them rotting. Cover the several major platforms rather than one, since consultancies work across them. Record which templates were used and how they were adapted, so the parameterisation improves. Include the documentation and training material in the package, which is the part most often skipped. And measure how much of an engagement the template covers, which is the number that justifies the investment.

## Target Customer
RevOps consultancies and their delivery teams, in-house revenue operations, CRM and analytics platform vendors, and template and asset vendors.

## Impact If Built
The structure is identical across engagements and the build is manual every time because the artefacts stay in clients' systems. Parameterised deployable templates capture the reuse without pretending clients are the same.
