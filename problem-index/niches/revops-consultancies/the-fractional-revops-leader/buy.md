# Multi-Client Practice From Fractional Finance

**Industry:** [[industries/revops-consultancies|RevOps Consultancies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Outsourced finance built multi-client tooling, standard chart structures and practice management, and fractional RevOps has none.
**Tags:** #worker-facing #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #automation #compliance #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact #descriptive-statistics
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let one person run an entire revenue operations function part-time across several companies — and whoever equips them takes the account.

## The Problem
Outsourced and fractional finance solved this shape of practice. Accounting platforms support a practitioner working across many client entities from one interface, standard chart structures make each client's setup familiar, workflow tooling tracks what is due for whom, and practice management handles the commercial side. A bookkeeper serving twenty clients does so with purpose-built tooling. A fractional RevOps leader serving three logs into three separate stacks.

## What Already Exists
Multi-entity practitioner interfaces; standardised structures across clients; practice workflow and deadline management; client reporting generated from templates; and practice management for scoping and billing.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a stack of several unrelated SaaS products per client rather than one accounting platform. It requires: (1) a practitioner working across CRM, marketing automation and reporting tools at each client, none of which support multi-tenant practitioner access — this is the substantive difference and is why no equivalent exists; (2) configuration that is bespoke per client rather than standardised by regulation; (3) work that is advisory and design rather than recurring compliance, so templates must be adaptable rather than uniform; (4) no professional body or standard practice to anchor on; and (5) client systems whose vendors price per seat per company.

## Target Customer
Fractional practitioners, companies using them, consultancies with fractional offerings, and practice management vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Outsourced finance built multi-entity tooling and standard structures so one practitioner can serve many clients well. Several unrelated SaaS products per client, none supporting practitioner access, is why the equivalent does not exist here.
