# The Fractional RevOps Leader

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/revops-consultancies|RevOps Consultancies]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let one person run an entire revenue operations function part-time across several companies — and whoever equips them takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$0.5B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — alone
**Target Buyer:** Fractional practitioners
**Automation Potential:** High — multi-client tooling and templates

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Fractional RevOps leadership is now a substantial part of this market: one experienced person running the whole function for two or three companies, a few days a month each. They carry the systems, the reporting, the process design and the relationship with sales leadership, with no team, no peer review and no continuity between visits. Tooling assumes a full-time person inside one company, so the practitioners work across several stacks with nothing designed for that at all.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Several clients' systems, a personal set of templates and a calendar. The gaps: no multi-client working environment; no reusable templates or playbooks; no continuity of context between visits; no peer network; and no way to demonstrate results across clients.
