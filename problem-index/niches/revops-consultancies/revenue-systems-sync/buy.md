# Reconciliation From Financial Systems Integration

**Niche:** [[niches/revops-consultancies/revenue-systems-sync/profile|Revenue Systems Sync]]
**Industry:** [[industries/revops-consultancies|RevOps Consultancies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Financial systems reconcile between ledgers as a control, and revenue systems reconcile when somebody notices.
**Tags:** #data-integration #compliance #automation #workflow-orchestration #evaluation-metrics #descriptive-statistics #sets-and-logic #change-point-detection
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep CRM, marketing automation, quoting and finance in agreement about the same accounts and deals, and whoever automates that reconciliation takes the account.

## The Problem
Financial systems integration treats reconciliation as a control rather than as a chore. Automated matching between ledgers, exception-only review, ageing of unresolved items, defined ownership for each break, and reporting on reconciliation status are standard because the consequences of ledgers disagreeing are unacceptable. Revenue systems disagree continuously and reconcile when somebody assembling a report notices.

## What Already Exists
Automated matching between systems; exception-only review workflows; break ageing and ownership; reconciliation status reporting; and controls requiring resolution before close.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to entities that are accounts and opportunities rather than transactions. It requires: (1) records that are mutable business objects edited by many people rather than immutable transactions, so a difference may be a legitimate update rather than a break — this is the substantive difference and makes classification the central task; (2) no natural matching key across systems, unlike a transaction reference; (3) a weekly rhythm with no hard close obligation; (4) legitimate definitional differences between systems, since finance and sales genuinely mean different things by an amount; and (5) no control requirement forcing resolution, so the practice must be justified operationally.

## Target Customer
Revenue operations teams, RevOps consultancies, integration platform vendors, and reconciliation and control vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Financial reconciliation is a control with exception review, break ageing and ownership because disagreeing ledgers are unacceptable. Mutable business objects with no natural matching key is what makes classification, rather than matching, the central task.
