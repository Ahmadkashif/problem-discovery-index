# CRM Data Distortion

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/revops-consultancies|RevOps Consultancies]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to quantify the specific ways CRM data is distorted by the people entering it, because every model built on it inherits those distortions silently — and whoever measures them takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$0.9B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — known, unmeasured
**Target Buyer:** RevOps leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — distortion detection from behavioural patterns

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Everyone agrees CRM data is bad, every vendor sells enrichment and validation, and almost nobody measures the specific distortions that actually break the analysis built on top of it. Stages advanced to satisfy a manager rather than to describe reality. Close dates pushed by a fortnight indefinitely. Opportunities created to hit an activity target. These are not data quality problems in the enrichment sense — they are behavioural artefacts, they are detectable in the record, and every forecast and attribution model inherits them.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Enrichment and validation tooling, required fields and a data hygiene dashboard. The gaps: no detection of behavioural distortion patterns; no quantification of their effect on downstream models; no distinction between missing data and misleading data; no feedback to the people creating it; and no adjustment in the models that consume it.
