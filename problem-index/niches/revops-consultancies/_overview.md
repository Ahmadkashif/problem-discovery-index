# Niche Analysis — RevOps Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/revops-consultancies|RevOps Consultancies]]

## Niche Selection

Revenue operations exists to make revenue predictable, and its two central artefacts — the forecast and the attribution model — are produced by methods nobody grades against what happened. Underneath both sits CRM data whose distortions everyone knows about and nobody measures. The eight niches below follow that: the forecast, the attribution, the data they rest on, the territory design nobody models, and the people producing it weekly.

## Why These Niches

The forecast and the attribution model carry the money and the discipline's credibility: both are the thing clients judge the work by, and neither is scored anywhere. CRM data distortion and territory design are the two places where everyone knows the practice is broken and nobody has measured it — stages advanced to satisfy a manager, and quotas set by splitting last year's number in a spreadsheet. The two underserved audiences are the analyst assembling a weekly pack from numbers that disagree, and the fractional leader running a whole revenue operations function part-time across several companies with no team. The last two are mechanical: rebuilding the same funnel model for the twentieth time, and keeping four systems in agreement.

## Filter Notes

Seven of the eight are terminal — each names one contest that every serious competitor is fighting over, and decomposing further would produce features rather than markets.

**Forecast accuracy** is not. It names a property, and getting it requires two things that are bought separately and fail for different reasons. Snapshot retention asks how to create the historical series at all — capturing every weekly forecast at every level before it is overwritten, which is a week of engineering, requires no statistics, and is the precondition for everything else. Methodology and scoring asks what to do with that series once it exists — which method predicts best, where the bias sits, which representatives and stages are systematically optimistic, whether a new approach beats the old one — a modelling and measurement problem sold to revenue leadership on the back of a record that must already exist. A client can implement retention this quarter and score nothing for a year, and it is still the right first purchase; a consultancy selling methodology into an organisation with no retained history is selling a claim it cannot test. The order is forced and the two are separately priced.

Two adjacent candidates were rejected as belonging elsewhere: **implementing the CRM platform itself** belongs to [[industries/saas-implementation-partners|SaaS Implementation Partners]], and **building the data warehouse underneath the revenue stack** sits with [[industries/data-platform-integrators|Data Platform Integrators]].
