# Forecast Methodology & Scoring

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/revops-consultancies|RevOps Consultancies]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to establish which forecasting method actually predicts best, and where the bias sits, using a retained history — and whoever scores it takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$0.75B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — conviction
**Target Buyer:** Revenue leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — scoring and model comparison

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This half consumes the series and produces judgements: which method is more accurate, at what horizon the forecast becomes meaningful, which representatives and stages are systematically optimistic, whether the methodology change last year helped. It is statistical work sold to revenue leadership as an answer rather than an instrument, it cannot begin without retained history, and it is where the discipline's competing methodologies finally become comparable.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A methodology adopted on conviction and a quarterly variance to plan. The gaps: no scoring against the retained series; no bias decomposition by level or segment; no comparison between methods; no calibration reporting; and no adjustment of the forecast using the measured bias.
