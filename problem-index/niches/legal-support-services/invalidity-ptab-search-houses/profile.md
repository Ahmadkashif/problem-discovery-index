# Invalidity & PTAB Prior-Art Search Houses

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/legal-support-services|Legal Support Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Search houses find prior art against issued patents and chart it claim element by claim element. Litigation counsel and in-house IP teams buy the report to decide whether to petition for inter partes review or request ex parte reexamination, to build district-court invalidity contentions, and to price settlements. Vendor pages from Sagacious IP, GreyB, Evalueserve, Parola Analytics, Lumenci, Effectual Services and the US firm TPR International all list invalidity searching; Unified Patents' PATROLL bounties, which pay $2,000–$28,000 for art against NPE-asserted patents, show the same product sold openly. This is the top tier of the industry's own patent-search segment: the few firms large enough to have a dedicated research function.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~15–150 invalidity and litigation-search analysts per house, in a field of ~10–20 houses with ten or more search analysts, mostly India-based (estimate)
**Output:** Invalidity search reports with ranked references and element-level claim charts, in IPR-petition, contention and in-house formats
**Proprietary data:** Beyond the public patent and literature record, only the archive of searches delivered to clients — which references, passages and ratings each analyst produced against which claims. Some of it can later be compared with public outcomes: IPR petitions, institution and final written decisions, and reexamination requests are public; invalidity contentions are served on the other side rather than docketed, often under protective order
**Clock:** The one-year bar on IPR petitions from service of the infringement complaint (35 U.S.C. §315(b)) and court-ordered invalidity-contention deadlines; the house works to the litigator's internal date inside those windows
**Buyer:** Head of search operations / VP of litigation search at the house

**Kill switches:** **Data isn't theirs to use** — invalidity searches are commissioned through litigation counsel as client work product, and the delivered archive, the house's only corpus beyond the public record, belongs to the client matter it was produced for. Any engine that learns from it depends on reusing client searches across engagements.
