# Patent Office PCT Search Contractors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/legal-support-services|Legal Support Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Private search firms sell search capacity to the patent office itself. The USPTO, acting as an International Searching Authority under the Patent Cooperation Treaty, contracts them to draft international search reports and written opinions for applications in assigned technology areas. In March 2023 it awarded two five-year contracts, to CPA Global (Clarivate) with a ~$267.4M ceiling and to Cardinal Intellectual Property with a ~$271.3M ceiling, following a commercial search pilot that began in 2005. The agency pays per search; the contractor's searchers produce the report the applicant and every later examiner see.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~100–500 contract searchers and reviewers across the two awardees (estimate)
**Output:** PCT international search reports with X, Y and A categorised citations, and written opinions on patentability
**Proprietary data:** None owned — applications are confidential until publication and the reports belong to the Office; the contractor keeps its working methods
**Clock:** The PCT time limit for the search report (three months from receipt of the search copy, or nine months from priority if later), contractually enforced
**Buyer:** USPTO contracting officer; inside the firm, the programme director
