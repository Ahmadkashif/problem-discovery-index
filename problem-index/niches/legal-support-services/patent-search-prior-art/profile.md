# Patent Search & Prior-Art Analysis

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/legal-support-services|Legal Support Services]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to find the closest prior art — including non-patent and foreign-language literature — before the examiner, the PTAB petitioner or the opposing expert does, and whoever's searches are least often beaten by art someone else finds later takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.0B a year in patentability, invalidity, state-of-the-art and landscape searches bought by US clients, including offshore search houses (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High — the work is done entirely in patent and literature databases, with AI semantic search now standard
**Target Buyer:** Head of search operations at a search house; patent prosecution or litigation partner; in-house IP counsel at a corporate filer
**Automation Potential:** Medium-High — retrieval and first-pass charting automate; the relevance call on the closest art does not yet

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Census puts patent searching and independent patent agents in this code, and the search house is the purest research business in the industry: its product is a report saying which documents disclose which claim elements. Patentability searches run before filing, against the Paris Convention priority year and the client's disclosure dates; invalidity searches run against the one-year bar for inter partes review that starts when the accused infringer is served (35 U.S.C. §315(b)) and against court-ordered contention deadlines. Much of the volume is done by India-based houses whose analysts specialise by technology (Sagacious IP, for one, runs separate engineering and ICT search teams). Patent figure preparation, a different trade, sits with [[niches/drafting-services/patent-drawing-bureaus/profile|Patent Drawing Bureaus]], and trademark clearance with [[niches/graphic-design-studios/trademark-clearance-search-providers/profile|Trademark Clearance & Design-Mark Search Providers]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Derwent Innovation, Questel Orbit, PatSnap, LexisNexis TotalPatent, free sources (Espacenet, Google Patents, USPTO PPUBS) and AI search tools. Gaps: searches are not recorded as structured data, so a house cannot see which of its searches were later beaten; non-patent literature coverage is uneven; and claim charts are assembled by hand after the search.
