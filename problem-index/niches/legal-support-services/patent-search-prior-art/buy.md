# Systematic-Review Tools for Prior-Art Search

**Niche:** [[niches/legal-support-services/patent-search-prior-art/profile|Patent Search & Prior-Art Analysis]]
**Industry:** [[industries/legal-support-services|Legal Support Services]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Medical systematic reviewers already run documented, reproducible literature searches with screening workflows and audit trails; patent searchers do the same job with less discipline and no equivalent tool.
**Tags:** #transformers #bert #evaluation-metrics #hypothesis-testing #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to find the closest prior art — including non-patent and foreign-language literature — before the examiner, the PTAB petitioner or the opposing expert does, and whoever's searches are least often beaten by art someone else finds later takes the account.

## The Problem
A defensible search needs a record of what was searched, how, and what was screened out and why — especially when an invalidity search must be explained in a deposition or a patentability search is challenged by a client.

## What Already Exists
Systematic-review platforms (Covidence, DistillerSR, Rayyan) manage reproducible search strategies, dual screening, conflict resolution and PRISMA-style reporting, with machine-learning screening prioritisation.

## The Customization Gap
Patent search needs: classification-code strategies (CPC, IPC, FI/F-terms) alongside keywords; family de-duplication across offices; claim-element-level rather than study-level screening; machine translation with certified-translation tracking; X/Y/A-style categorisation; and output into claim charts and IPR or contention formats.

## Target Customer
Search houses selling invalidity and freedom-to-operate work, where defensibility of method matters to litigators.

## Impact If Solved
Reproducible, auditable searches that a house can defend and reuse, with screening prioritisation cutting reading time on large result sets by an estimated third (estimate).
