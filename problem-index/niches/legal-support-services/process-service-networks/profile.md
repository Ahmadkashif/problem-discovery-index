# National Process Service Networks & Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/legal-support-services|Legal Support Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
ABC Legal, Proof and similar networks take serves from law firms, volume filers and agencies through web portals and APIs and dispatch them to thousands of independent servers, returning photo- and GPS-stamped attempt records and affidavits. Proof raised a $7M Series A in 2022 and reports more than 3,500 law-firm and agency users. Filers pay per serve.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analysts doing anything resembling research (estimate); headcount is servers, dispatch, support and engineering
**Output:** Completed serves and affidavits
**Proprietary data:** Millions of geotagged, photographed attempts with outcomes — the richest attempt dataset in the industry, held as an operational record
**Clock:** Statutory service windows on each case
**Buyer:** Chief operating officer
