# Pre-Flight Checking From Print Production

**Niche:** [[niches/legal-support-services/court-filing-efiling-services/profile|Court Filing & E-Filing Services]]
**Industry:** [[industries/legal-support-services|Legal Support Services]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Printers have checked PDFs against technical specifications for decades; courts reject PDFs for the same kinds of defects and nobody runs the check first.
**Tags:** #decision-trees #large-language-models #compliance #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a filing accepted by the clerk on the first submission, before the deadline, and whoever predicts and prevents clerk rejections best takes the account.

## The Problem
Page size, embedded fonts, file size limits, text searchability, bookmarks, signatures and redaction of personal identifiers all cause rejections, and differ by court.

## What Already Exists
PDF pre-flight tools from print production (Adobe Acrobat Preflight, Enfocus PitStop) check documents against configurable profiles and fix many defects automatically.

## The Customization Gap
Court use needs: per-court profiles maintained as rules change; checks for unredacted Social Security numbers, account numbers and minors' names; signature-block and /s/ conventions; document-type checks (lead document versus attachment); and integration into the e-filing envelope step rather than a separate desktop tool.

## Target Customer
E-filing providers and attorney-service firms embedding checks for their filers.

## Impact If Solved
Technical rejections largely eliminated, leaving clerks to judge the filings they are meant to judge.
