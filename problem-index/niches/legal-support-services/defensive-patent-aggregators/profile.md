# Defensive Patent Aggregators & Risk Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/legal-support-services|Legal Support Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
RPX, Unified Patents, the LOT Network and Allied Security Trust sell operating companies protection against patent assertion by non-practising entities: buying or licensing patents before they are asserted, filing patent office challenges against NPE patents (Unified funds IPRs and runs PATROLL prior-art bounties for members by technology zone), and supplying litigation and patent-market data. Members pay annual subscriptions; the analysis of which patents threaten them is a large part of what they buy.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–150 patent analysts, prior-art researchers and data staff per organisation (estimate)
**Output:** Threat assessments, prior-art sets and IPR or reexamination filings against NPE patents, litigation and market data
**Proprietary data:** Litigation and NPE campaign databases, acquisition-market records, crowdsourced prior-art submissions
**Clock:** IPR bars and litigation schedules on the patents they challenge; acquisition windows in the patent market
**Buyer:** Chief executive or head of analytics
