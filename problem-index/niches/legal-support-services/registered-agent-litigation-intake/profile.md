# Registered Agent Litigation Intake

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/legal-support-services|Legal Support Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
CT Corporation, CSC, Cogency Global and other registered agents receive service of process for the companies they represent in every state, scan it and forward it, usually within a day. Client companies pay annual fees per entity and jurisdiction.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Large intake and processing teams, but under 10 people producing analysis (estimate)
**Output:** Forwarded documents, intake logs and notifications
**Proprietary data:** A national stream of every suit, subpoena and garnishment served on client entities — often before it is visible on any docket
**Clock:** Court answer deadlines on every document received
**Buyer:** Head of service-of-process operations
