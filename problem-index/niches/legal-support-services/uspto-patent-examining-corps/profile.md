# USPTO Patent Examining Corps

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/legal-support-services|Legal Support Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Patent examiners search the prior art for every application and decide whether the claims are new and non-obvious; the PTAB reviews issued patents in IPR and post-grant proceedings. Their searches and citations are the yardstick against which every private search in this industry is eventually measured, and they regulate the registered patent agents the code contains through the Office of Enrollment and Discipline.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Thousands of examiners, plus PTAB judges and search support staff (estimate)
**Output:** Office actions with cited art, search notes, allowances and rejections; PTAB institution and final written decisions
**Proprietary data:** The complete record of searches, citations and outcomes for every application, including unpublished ones
**Clock:** Statutory and regulatory pendency and response periods; patent term adjustment accrues against the Office when it is late
**Buyer:** Commissioner for Patents / Chief Information Officer; federal procurement
