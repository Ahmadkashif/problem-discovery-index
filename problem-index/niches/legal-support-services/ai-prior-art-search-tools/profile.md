# AI Prior-Art Search Tool Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/legal-support-services|Legal Support Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Newer vendors (IPRally, Amplified, XLSCOUT and the AI layers inside PatSnap and Questel, among others) sell semantic and graph-based prior-art search, automated claim charting and novelty scoring to search houses, law firms and corporate IP teams by subscription. Their relevance and evaluation teams build training data from patent office citations and customer feedback.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–60 patent analysts and relevance evaluators per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Search software and model releases; benchmark and evaluation reports used in sales
**Proprietary data:** Training sets built from public citations, customer relevance feedback under licence terms
**Clock:** Product release cycles; no third-party deadline on the vendor
**Buyer:** Head of product or chief technology officer
