# Patent Information Databases & Abstracting Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/legal-support-services|Legal Support Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Clarivate's Derwent World Patents Index, LexisNexis IP, Questel, PatSnap, IFI Claims and Minesoft collect the world's patent documents, normalise families and legal status, and in Derwent's case have technically trained editors rewrite each family's abstract and assign proprietary manual codes. Search houses, law firms and corporate IP departments subscribe; every searcher in this industry works inside one or more of them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~300–900 editorial and indexing staff at the largest abstracting service (Derwent reported ~900 editorial staff in 2016), far fewer at most peers (estimate)
**Output:** Enhanced abstracts, manual codes and indexing, family and legal-status data, search platforms
**Proprietary data:** Decades of expert-rewritten abstracts and codes for ~2.5M new records a year (2016 figure), plus normalisation and correction histories
**Clock:** Patent offices' weekly publication cycles set the intake pace; there is no external deadline on the output
**Buyer:** Head of patent content or editorial operations
