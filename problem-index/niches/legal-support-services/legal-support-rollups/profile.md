# Legal Support Services Rollups

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/legal-support-services|Legal Support Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Private-equity-backed platforms assemble court reporting, record retrieval, process serving, filing and litigation support firms into national providers, buying local operators and integrating their scheduling and billing. Court reporting is usually the core business and belongs to the queued court-reporting-firms industry; process serving and filing are add-ons here.

## Insight Function
**Size:** A handful of corporate development and pricing staff per platform; integration headcount (systems, billing, scheduling) is operations, not research (estimate)
**Output:** Acquisition models and pricing analyses, used internally
**Proprietary data:** Combined job and billing histories of acquired firms, tied to their law-firm clients' matters
**Clock:** Deal timelines set internally and by sponsors
**Buyer:** Chief financial officer or head of corporate development
