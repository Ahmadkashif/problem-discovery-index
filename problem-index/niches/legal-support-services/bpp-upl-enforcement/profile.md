# Petition Preparer & Unauthorised-Practice Enforcement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/legal-support-services|Legal Support Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The U.S. Trustee Program and bankruptcy courts police bankruptcy petition preparers under 11 U.S.C. §110 — fee caps, disclosures, disgorgement and injunctions — while state bars' unauthorised-practice committees and court licensing offices (Arizona's legal document preparer programme, California county registration) police non-lawyer document preparers serving the public.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 people dedicated to preparer enforcement in any one office; the work is a sideline of trial attorneys and bar counsel (estimate)
**Output:** Investigations, disgorgement orders, injunctions and certification decisions
**Proprietary data:** Case files, complaints and certification records
**Clock:** Court schedules in enforcement matters
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer; agency or bar leadership
