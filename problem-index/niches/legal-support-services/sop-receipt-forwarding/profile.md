# Service-of-Process Receipt & Forwarding

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/legal-support-services|Legal Support Services]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to read every served document correctly — entity, court, case type, answer date — and put it in front of the right lawyer the same day, and whoever never lets a served suit become a default takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$500M a year in the service-of-process handling share of US registered agent fees, plus in-house litigation intake staff time (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — documents are scanned and forwarded electronically within a day, but classification and deadline entry are largely done by people
**Target Buyer:** Head of service-of-process operations at a registered agent; litigation intake or legal operations manager at a large company
**Automation Potential:** High — classification, entity matching and answer-date computation are bounded, high-volume tasks

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is the receiving end of process serving. Every company registered to do business in a state must keep a registered agent there to accept service, and large registered agents such as CT Corporation and CSC receive summonses, complaints, subpoenas and garnishments for vast numbers of client entities, scan them and forward them, CSC within 24 hours. The value is in not missing anything: a summons routed to the wrong subsidiary or logged with the wrong answer date becomes a default judgment, and agents carry the reputational and contractual risk. The work is sorting at volume against hard court deadlines.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Registered agents' own intake systems, online logs and e-mail alerts; corporate matter-management and e-billing systems on the receiving side. Gaps: answer dates computed by staff from varied state rules; garnishments and agency notices misclassified; and no structured hand-off into the client's matter system.
