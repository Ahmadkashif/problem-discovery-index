# An Attorney's Redlines Turned Into a Style Model

**Niche:** [[niches/legal-support-services/freelance-contract-paralegals/profile|Freelance & Contract Paralegal Services]]
**Industry:** [[industries/legal-support-services|Legal Support Services]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Every correction a supervising attorney makes to a freelancer's draft is a lesson about what that attorney wants, and it is lost in a tracked-changes file nobody revisits.
**Tags:** #tacit-knowledge-ml #large-language-models #transformers #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a supervising attorney's two-line instruction into a filing-ready draft in that court's form and that attorney's style, and whoever's drafts need the fewest minutes of attorney review takes the account.

## The Problem
One attorney wants objections stated in full, another wants general objections cut; one cites the local rule by number, another never does; one hates passive voice in a declaration. A freelancer serving a dozen attorneys learns these by being corrected, and forgets some. The attorney spends the review time the freelancer was hired to save.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Drafting assistants are sold to firms and learn the firm's documents, not one outside paralegal's portfolio of supervisors. Redlines are scattered across email attachments. And the freelancer is a one-person buyer.

## What to Build
A drafting assistant owned by the freelancer that ingests each attorney's past redlines and final filed versions, learns per-attorney preferences and per-court forms, and pre-applies them to new drafts with each applied preference visible. Review time per draft, tracked automatically, measures whether it works.

## Target Customer
Freelance paralegals and small paralegal service firms; paralegal associations as a distribution channel.

## Impact If Built
Drafts that come back with fewer corrections, an estimated 20–30% less attorney review time per task (estimate), and a freelancer who can take more clients without lowering quality.
