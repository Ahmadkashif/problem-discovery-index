# The Conflict Check Kept in a Spreadsheet

**Niche:** [[niches/legal-support-services/freelance-contract-paralegals/profile|Freelance & Contract Paralegal Services]]
**Industry:** [[industries/legal-support-services|Legal Support Services]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A freelancer who drafted for the plaintiff's lawyer last year must not draft for the defence this year, and the only safeguard is a spreadsheet of party names they update when they remember.
**Tags:** #k-nearest-neighbors #large-language-models #compliance #quick-win #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a supervising attorney's two-line instruction into a filing-ready draft in that court's form and that attorney's style, and whoever's drafts need the fewest minutes of attorney review takes the account.

## The Problem
Conflicts follow the paralegal, and the attorneys who hire them inherit the risk. In small legal markets the same parties and counsel recur constantly, and party names are spelled differently from matter to matter.

## Why It's Still Broken
Conflict tools are built into firm practice-management systems; the freelancer works inside each client's system and has none of their own.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A personal matter register with fuzzy matching on parties, related entities and opposing counsel, checked automatically when a new assignment arrives, with the result recorded for the hiring attorney.

## Who Feels the Pain
Freelance paralegals; the attorneys who would face a disqualification motion.

## Impact If Fixed
A conflict screen in seconds per assignment and a record that protects freelancer and attorney alike.
