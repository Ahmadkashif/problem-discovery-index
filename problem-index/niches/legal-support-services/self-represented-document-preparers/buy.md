# Attorney Form Software Re-Scoped for Preparers

**Niche:** [[niches/legal-support-services/self-represented-document-preparers/profile|Self-Represented Litigants & Legal Document Preparers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/legal-support-services|Legal Support Services]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Bankruptcy, family-law and eviction form software already produces court-ready packets for attorneys; preparers need the same packets with the legal-judgement features taken out and the compliance features put in.
**Tags:** #decision-trees #large-language-models #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a self-represented customer's papers accepted and the case moving without crossing into legal advice, and whoever gets the most filings through first time while staying on the right side of the unauthorised-practice line takes the customer.

## The Problem
Preparers use attorney-grade form software awkwardly or not at all, because licences, workflows and built-in "recommendations" assume a lawyer is deciding.

## What Already Exists
Bankruptcy petition software (Best Case, used by consumer bankruptcy attorneys), family-law form packages, and court-hosted guided interviews built on document-assembly platforms.

## The Customization Gap
Preparer use needs: removal or neutral framing of features that recommend exemptions, chapters or claims; mandatory disclosures (a bankruptcy petition preparer's notice and fee disclosures under §110, California's registration number on every document); district fee-cap checks; customer-directed selection logs; and pricing for one-office storefronts.

## Target Customer
Bankruptcy petition preparers and multi-practice document-preparation offices.

## Impact If Solved
Professional-grade packets at preparer prices, with the disclosures regulators check produced automatically.
