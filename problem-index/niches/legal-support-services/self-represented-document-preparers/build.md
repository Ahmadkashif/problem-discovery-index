# An Interview That Stops at the Advice Line

**Niche:** [[niches/legal-support-services/self-represented-document-preparers/profile|Self-Represented Litigants & Legal Document Preparers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/legal-support-services|Legal Support Services]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A preparer must fill in a customer's forms without choosing for them, and nothing records where information ended and advice would have begun.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #decision-trees #evaluation-metrics #compliance #ai-agent
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a self-represented customer's papers accepted and the case moving without crossing into legal advice, and whoever gets the most filings through first time while staying on the right side of the unauthorised-practice line takes the customer.

## The Problem
"Which form do I need?" and "should I claim this exemption?" are the questions customers ask first, and the ones a preparer may not answer as advice. Preparers handle this by habit and scripts; when a bar regulator or U.S. Trustee investigates, the preparer has no record of what was said.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Consumer legal tools avoid the line by keeping humans out; attorney tools assume a lawyer makes the call. The preparer market is small and fragmented, and unauthorised-practice exposure scares vendors off.

## What to Build
A guided interview the customer completes with the preparer, in plain language and the customer's own language, that presents court-published information and options neutrally, records the customer's selection at each decision point, fills the forms from the answers, and flags questions that need a lawyer with referral to legal aid. A transcript of every session forms the preparer's compliance file.

## Target Customer
Legal document preparers and petition preparers; court self-help centres as partners.

## Impact If Built
Faster, more accurate form sets, customers who understand what they filed, and preparers who can show a regulator exactly where they stayed.
