# Self-Represented Litigants & Legal Document Preparers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/legal-support-services|Legal Support Services]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a self-represented customer's papers accepted and the case moving without crossing into legal advice, and whoever gets the most filings through first time while staying on the right side of the unauthorised-practice line takes the customer.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M a year paid to registered legal document assistants, certified legal document preparers, unlawful detainer assistants and bankruptcy petition preparers (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — storefront offices, paper intake, court forms filled in PDF or by hand, filings carried to the clerk
**Target Buyer:** Owner of a document-preparation office; court self-help centre managers as a public-sector channel
**Automation Potential:** Medium-High for form completion and checking; low for the conversation that decides which forms apply, which is where the advice line sits

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The person served here is the self-represented party, present on at least one side of about three-quarters of state civil cases — tenants, debtors, divorcing spouses, small-estate heirs. They cannot afford a lawyer and the law forbids the preparer to act as one. California registers legal document assistants and unlawful detainer assistants with the county clerk under a $25,000 bond and continuing education; Arizona certifies legal document preparers by exam under Supreme Court rules; federal law caps and polices bankruptcy petition preparers (11 U.S.C. §110), with fee limits around $125–150 a case in several districts and disgorgement when they overcharge. Tools are built either for lawyers or for consumers going it alone; nothing is built for the preparer in the middle.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Court self-help sites and fillable forms, consumer document sites, general PDF editors and bankruptcy form software built for attorneys. Gaps: no intake that keeps a record of the customer directing each choice; no check of local filing requirements before the customer stands at the clerk's window; and no follow-through on hearing dates the customer must attend alone.
