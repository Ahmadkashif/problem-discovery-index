# A Vacated-Default Rate Every Filer Can See

**Niche:** [[niches/legal-support-services/volume-creditor-eviction-service/profile|Volume Creditor & Eviction Service]]
**Industry:** [[industries/legal-support-services|Legal Support Services]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Volume filers choose service networks on price and turnaround because the one number that measures quality — how often defaults built on their affidavits are later vacated — is scattered across court dockets nobody joins back to the serve.
**Tags:** #survival-analysis #logistic-regression #hypothesis-testing #data-integration #compliance #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make every affidavit at volume provably true — GPS, photo and time evidence that survives a traverse hearing and a regulator's audit — and whoever can show a volume filer the lowest rate of vacated defaults per thousand serves takes the filer's docket.

## The Problem
A creditor-rights firm sends thousands of serves a month (illustrative) to two or three networks. It knows each network's price, completion rate and turnaround. It does not know how many of the resulting default judgments were later vacated for defective service, challenged at a traverse hearing or swept into a class action, because those events surface months or years later in court records under the case, not the serve.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The join is laborious: court dockets by county, motions to vacate in free text, traverse-hearing outcomes rarely coded. Networks have no reason to publish a number that could hurt them, and filers have historically treated vacatur as a cost of doing business.

## What to Build
A service-quality ledger that links each serve (network, server, method, attempt trail) to the downstream docket events for that case — answer, default, motion to vacate, traverse hearing, outcome — and reports vacated-default and challenge rates by network, server, method and county, with survival curves for time-to-challenge. Plausibility scores on the attempt trail add a leading indicator before the court record catches up.

## Target Customer
Litigation operations leads at creditor-rights and landlord firms; compliance officers at debt buyers who answer to regulators for their vendors.

## Impact If Built
Filers can buy service on measured defensibility, rewarding networks whose affidavits hold up, and regulators gain a way to find bad serving before thousands of defaults are entered.
