# The Traverse Hearing Fought From a Missing Record

**Niche:** [[niches/legal-support-services/volume-creditor-eviction-service/profile|Volume Creditor & Eviction Service]]
**Industry:** [[industries/legal-support-services|Legal Support Services]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** When a defendant contests service months after a default, the server must testify about one serve among thousands, and the GPS points and photos that would prove it sit in a network's system the server cannot reach and the filer never asked for.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #compliance #quick-win #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make every affidavit at volume provably true — GPS, photo and time evidence that survives a traverse hearing and a regulator's audit — and whoever can show a volume filer the lowest rate of vacated defaults per thousand serves takes the filer's docket.

## The Problem
A traverse hearing, or a motion to vacate a default for improper service, can come a year or more after the serve. In New York City the licensed agency must report a scheduled traverse hearing to the city within 10 days of notice, and the server is expected to appear with records. The attempt evidence was uploaded to whichever network dispatched the job, the affidavit was filed by the law firm, and the server's own notes are on an old phone. Pulling the record together takes days, and an incomplete record loses the hearing even when the serve was real.

## Why It's Still Broken
Each network keeps attempt evidence in its own system, keyed to its job number rather than the court's index number. Filers store the affidavit, not the evidence behind it. Nobody owns retrieval after the job is paid.

## What a Fix Looks Like
An evidence pack generated and archived at filing for every serve — affidavit, GPS points, photos, attempt times, server licence — keyed to the court case number and retrievable by the server, the agency and the filer. A traverse notice entered against the case number pulls the pack, drafts the city's traverse report and alerts the server.

## Who Feels the Pain
Servers called to testify about a serve they barely remember; agencies facing the city's 10-day reporting rule; filers whose judgments are vacated for lack of proof.

## Impact If Fixed
Hearings argued from the original record instead of memory, and the city's reporting deadline met without a scramble.
