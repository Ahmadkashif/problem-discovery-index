# Consumer Research Reports Applied to Prior Art

**Niche:** [[niches/legal-support-services/independent-inventors-startups/profile|Independent Inventors & Early-Stage Startups]]
**Industry:** [[industries/legal-support-services|Legal Support Services]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Consumer-facing report products already turn complex data — credit files, home inspections, vehicle histories — into a scored summary a layperson trusts; prior-art searches have never had that treatment.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give a first-time inventor a trustworthy answer to "is there anything left to patent, and is it worth the cost?" for a few hundred dollars, and whoever's answer most often matches what the examiner later finds takes the inventor.

## The Problem
Inventors cannot read a search report and cannot compare providers, which leaves the market to whoever markets hardest.

## What Already Exists
Consumer report formats — vehicle history reports, home inspection report software, credit-score explanations — that standardise sections, scores and plain-language summaries, plus AI patent search tools that already rank references.

## The Customization Gap
Prior-art reporting for inventors needs: a feature-by-feature novelty table rather than a reference list; figures from references set beside the inventor's drawing; cost-and-timeline guidance for filing paths (provisional, utility, design); clear statements of what a search cannot establish; and the disclosures that keep providers apart from invention-promotion practices.

## Target Customer
Low-cost search providers and agent practices with inventor volume.

## Impact If Solved
A report an inventor understands in ten minutes, and a standard that lets good providers compete on quality.
