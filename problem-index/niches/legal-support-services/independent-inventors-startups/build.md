# A Patentability Answer an Inventor Can Read

**Industry:** [[industries/legal-support-services|Legal Support Services]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** An inventor deciding whether to spend thousands on an application needs the probable claim scope left after the prior art, in plain words, and gets a list of patent numbers.
**Tags:** #transformers #bert #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give a first-time inventor a trustworthy answer to "is there anything left to patent, and is it worth the cost?" for a few hundred dollars, and whoever's answer most often matches what the examiner later finds takes the inventor.

## The Problem
The decision the inventor faces is economic: is there a claim worth paying for? A conventional search report answers a different question — which documents are closest — and leaves the inventor to infer the rest.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Professionals are wary of predicting claim scope in writing, the individual-inventor market is small per head, and quality cannot be shown without tracking outcomes for years.

## What to Build
A service, delivered through a registered agent or search firm, that takes the inventor's description and drawings, runs a semantic and classification search, maps the invention's features against the closest references, and returns a plain-language report: features already disclosed, features apparently new, a likely-narrowest claim worth filing, and a confidence band. Each report is later checked against the examiner's first action when the inventor files, building a public calibration record.

## Target Customer
Independent patent agents and small search firms serving inventors; university and startup incubators buying on founders' behalf.

## Impact If Built
Inventors decide with evidence, fewer doomed applications are filed, and honest providers can prove they are honest.
