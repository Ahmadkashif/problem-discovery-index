# Online Legal Document Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/legal-support-services|Legal Support Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
LegalZoom, Rocket Lawyer and similar platforms sell consumers and small businesses self-help legal documents — formations, wills, leases, trademark filings — by interview, subscription or flat fee, with optional attorney access through networks. They aggregate the document-preparation demand that storefront preparers serve one customer at a time, and sit on the same unauthorised-practice line.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~30–150 legal content, product analytics and data staff per large platform (estimate)
**Output:** Document templates and interviews maintained across states; conversion and product analytics
**Proprietary data:** Millions of customer interview answers and document choices, under the platforms' own privacy terms
**Clock:** State filing deadlines for some products (annual reports, trademark responses); mostly self-set
**Buyer:** Chief product officer or head of legal content
