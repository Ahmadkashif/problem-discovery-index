# Patentability & Freedom-to-Operate Search Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/legal-support-services|Legal Support Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
The same search houses, plus independent searchers and patent agents, run patentability searches on inventions before filing and freedom-to-operate searches on products before launch. Prosecution counsel, in-house IP teams and inventors pay per search. Patentability is the highest-volume search product in the industry; freedom-to-operate is the most consequential, because it decides whether a product ships.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–400 search analysts per house across patentability and FTO work (estimate)
**Output:** Patentability reports with closest references; FTO reports mapping live claims of in-force patents to the client's product features
**Proprietary data:** Search archives keyed to clients' invention disclosures (confidential until the application publishes at 18 months, and permanently if it is abandoned unpublished) and to clients' confidential product descriptions; FTO targets are public patents, but the product mapped against them is not
**Clock:** Mostly the client's filing or launch schedule; statutory dates (the Paris Convention priority year, the one-year grace period after an inventor's own disclosure) bind some patentability work
**Buyer:** Head of search operations at the house — the same ~10–20 houses with ten or more search analysts as the invalidity pocket (estimate)

**Kill switches:** **Data isn't theirs to use** — the same rule as [[niches/legal-support-services/invalidity-ptab-search-houses/profile|Invalidity & PTAB Prior-Art Search Houses]]: the deliverables are client-owned. Patentability searches are performed on a client's invention disclosure, confidential until 18-month publication; FTO searches map public patents against a client's confidential product and are routinely commissioned through counsel to keep them privileged because they bear on wilful infringement. The delivered archive, the only corpus the house has beyond the public record, cannot be reused across clients.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch
