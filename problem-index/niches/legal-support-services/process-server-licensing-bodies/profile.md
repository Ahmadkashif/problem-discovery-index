# Process Server Licensing & Enforcement Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/legal-support-services|Legal Support Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
New York City's Department of Consumer and Worker Protection licenses servers and agencies, requires GPS devices and tamper-proof electronic logs, and audits records; it suspended or declined to renew 242 licences between 2019 and fall 2024. California county clerks register servers under a $2,000 bond; other states certify servers through court bodies or leave service largely unregulated.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 staff per body dedicated to process-server oversight (estimate)
**Output:** Licences, audits, fines and suspensions
**Proprietary data:** Licensing files, monthly agency recordkeeping reports and traverse-hearing notices (NYC)
**Clock:** Licence renewal cycles and complaint investigations
**Buyer:** Commissioner or county clerk
