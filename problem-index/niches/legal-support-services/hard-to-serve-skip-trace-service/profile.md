# Hard-to-Serve & Skip-Trace Service

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/legal-support-services|Legal Support Services]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to locate and serve the defendant who does not want to be found inside the service window, and whoever converts the most dead-address and evasive files into valid service before the court's deadline takes the litigator's account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600M a year in rush, stakeout, skip-trace and evasive-defendant service (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — skip-trace databases are digital; the stakeout, the neighbour conversation and the decision to keep trying run on experience
**Target Buyer:** Litigation partner or paralegal manager at a firm with a defendant who is avoiding service; family-law attorneys; corporate counsel serving executives
**Automation Potential:** Low for the serve, medium for locating and diligence documentation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Here one serve can carry a whole case. A business defendant ducking a fraud suit, a parent avoiding custody papers, an executive behind a gatehouse, a respondent to a restraining order. The plaintiff has 90 days in federal court (FRCP 4(m)) and often less in state proceedings; missing the window means a motion to extend or dismissal. Buyers pay hourly or per stakeout and choose servers by reputation for finding people. Skip tracing for service overlaps with the debtor location work analysed under [[niches/collections-agencies/skip-tracing-operations/profile|Skip Tracing Operations]], but the deliverable here is a valid serve on a named person, documented well enough to win substituted service if the person cannot be reached.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Skip-trace data from identity-resolution vendors; ServeManager; social media and property records searched by hand; private-investigator partners. Gaps: no model of when a located subject can be served; diligence documentation assembled after the fact; and no record of which tactics worked for which kinds of subject.
