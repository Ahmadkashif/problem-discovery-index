# Field-Service Dispatch Taught Service-of-Process Law

**Niche:** [[niches/legal-support-services/process-serving/profile|Process Serving]]
**Industry:** [[industries/legal-support-services|Legal Support Services]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Field-service platforms already route technicians, capture GPS and photos, and close jobs with signed forms; they do not know that a "job" here ends in a sworn affidavit governed by fifty states' rules.
**Tags:** #optimization-fundamentals #k-means-clustering #feature-engineering #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to prove valid service of process — but the volume creditor and eviction market and the hard-to-serve litigation market reward different winners on different evidence, so it is decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Multi-server firms dispatch hundreds of jobs a week across a metro area with deadlines measured in days. Dispatch is done by a person with a map and a spreadsheet, or inside server software with basic routing.

## What Already Exists
Field-service management platforms (ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro, Salesforce Field Service) handle routing, technician tracking, photo capture, job forms and customer notifications for trades.

## The Customization Gap
Service of process needs: attempt windows rather than appointment slots, because the "customer" does not want the visit; a diligence counter per job tied to each state's substituted-service threshold; affidavit generation with court-specific elements; tamper-evident logs that satisfy New York City's recordkeeping rules; per-serve pay tracking for subcontracted servers; and deadline escalation to the attorney when service inside the statutory window looks unlikely.

## Target Customer
Owners and dispatchers at multi-server firms with ten or more servers.

## Impact If Solved
An estimated 10–20% more completed serves per server-day from better routing (estimate), with compliance records produced as a by-product of the dispatch system rather than a separate chore.
