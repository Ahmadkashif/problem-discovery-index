# Paralegal & Process Server Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/legal-support-services|Legal Support Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
National and state associations of paralegals run voluntary certification exams and continuing education; the national and state process server associations run directories, training and lobbying. Members pay dues and exam fees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 staff on research, exam development or benchmarking per association, with psychometrics outsourced (estimate)
**Output:** Certification exams, member surveys, directories, training
**Proprietary data:** Member and exam records; occasional compensation surveys
**Clock:** Exam windows and annual conferences, self-set
**Buyer:** Executive director
