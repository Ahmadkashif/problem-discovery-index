# Restoration Equipment Manufacturers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/insurance-restoration|Insurance Restoration]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Equipment makers supply the dehumidifiers, air movers, and moisture meters restoration runs on, and publish the drying calculations and equipment-sizing guidance contractors follow.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 engineers, applied scientists, and technical trainers
**Output:** Equipment specifications, drying calculation methods, technical training and certification content
**Proprietary data:** Laboratory drying performance data and field application research
**Clock:** Product cycles and training calendars
**Buyer:** VP of Engineering / Director of Technical Services
