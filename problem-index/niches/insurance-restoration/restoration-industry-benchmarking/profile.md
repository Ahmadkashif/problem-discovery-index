**Parent Industry:** [[industries/insurance-restoration|Insurance Restoration]]

## What They Do
Small consultancies publish the operating benchmarks restoration companies compare themselves against — gross margin by job type, equipment utilization, cycle time, and carrier programme performance.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 3-10 consultants and analysts
**Output:** Operating benchmark studies, peer group comparisons, profitability analysis
**Proprietary data:** Contributed financial data from participating restoration firms
**Clock:** Annual benchmarking cycles
**Buyer:** Principal
