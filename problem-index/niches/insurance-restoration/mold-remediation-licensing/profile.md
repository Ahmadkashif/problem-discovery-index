# Mould Remediation Licensing & Enforcement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/insurance-restoration|Insurance Restoration]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State programmes license mould assessors and remediators, require independent clearance testing, and investigate complaints in the states that regulate the work.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-25 licensing analysts and investigators per state
**Output:** Licensing decisions, clearance requirements, complaint findings, enforcement actions
**Proprietary data:** Licensee and complaint records by contractor
**Clock:** Licence renewal and complaint timelines
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer
