# Restoration Certification & Standards Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/insurance-restoration|Insurance Restoration]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The certification body writes the water, fire, and mould remediation standards the industry works to and certifies the technicians Pass 1 identifies by their credentials. Carriers write those standards into programme requirements, which gives them force.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 standards developers, certification staff, and technical committee support
**Output:** Consensus remediation standards, technician certification examinations, technical guidance
**Proprietary data:** Standards development history and certification performance across the technician population
**Clock:** Standard revision cycles and recertification periods
**Buyer:** Executive director
