# Restoration Job Management Platform Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/insurance-restoration|Insurance Restoration]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The job management platforms run workflow, documentation, and equipment tracking for restoration contractors, holding moisture readings, drying logs, photographs, and equipment placement across a large share of US jobs.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 product and data staff
**Output:** Workflow tooling, documentation packages for carrier compliance, equipment tracking
**Proprietary data:** Drying logs, moisture readings, and equipment deployment against realized dry times across hundreds of thousands of jobs — the empirical answer to how long a structure actually takes to dry
**Clock:** Carrier documentation requirements and daily drying cycles
**Buyer:** Head of Product
