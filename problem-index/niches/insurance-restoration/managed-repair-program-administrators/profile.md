# Managed Repair Programme Administrators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/insurance-restoration|Insurance Restoration]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Programme administrators run the preferred contractor networks carriers assign claims through — recruiting and scoring contractors, assigning jobs, auditing estimates line by line, and tracking the response time and satisfaction metrics Pass 1 says determine whether a restoration company keeps status worth 60-80% of its revenue.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,500 estimate auditors, network analysts, and performance staff
**Output:** Estimate audits and adjustments, contractor scorecards, assignment decisions, network performance reporting
**Proprietary data:** Estimates, audit findings, cycle times, reopen rates, and satisfaction scores across thousands of contractors and millions of claims — the only record of what a given job type should actually cost and who delivers it well
**Clock:** Carrier service level agreements measured in hours from first notice of loss
**Buyer:** Chief Operating Officer / VP of Network Performance
