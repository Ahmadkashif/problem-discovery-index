## Why These Niches

Water damage and fire restoration are the two largest revenue segments, together representing over 40% of the industry. Mold remediation and storm response are surprisingly under-digitized — mold protocols rely on manual moisture readings and paper inspection forms, while storm response shares the same offline-deployment challenges as public adjusting. Multifamily properties and commercial accounts are underserved because most restoration companies optimize for single-family residential work and lack the project management infrastructure for multi-unit or large-commercial jobs. Xactimate supplement negotiation and moisture documentation are high-frequency, rule-intensive workflows where automation would have immediate ROI.

## Why These Pockets

Pass 1 frames the operator's central risk as a single number: underscope and the contractor absorbs the cost, overscope and the carrier audits and penalizes. Both sides of that are determined by parties above the contractor, and both qualified.

The estimating data publishers set the unit costs every property claim in America settles on — material, labour, and equipment for every repair task, by postal code, updated monthly. It is one of the strongest positions found anywhere in this sweep: 58, tied for the top of the index. Its defect is that the prices are produced by market survey and never checked against outcomes, while essentially every property estimate in the country is written in the company's own software, revised through supplements, and settled at a final amount. The divergence between published price and settled cost is computable line by line and is not computed — so nobody knows where the list is wrong or by how much, while the entire industry argues about exactly that on every claim.

Managed repair administrators run the preferred networks that carry most of a restoration company's revenue, and their two core functions are both allocated badly. Estimate audit capacity is spent on files chosen by dollar threshold and rotation, over a corpus of millions of estimates joined to audit findings that would say precisely which ones to open. And the contractor scorecard — which determines assignment volume and therefore revenue — ranks companies on cycle time and satisfaction with no case-mix adjustment, so it substantially measures which jobs a contractor was assigned, in a loop where assignment is driven by the score.

The rest of the industry holds decisive data attached to other invoices. Job management platforms hold drying logs and moisture readings against realized dry times across hundreds of thousands of jobs — the empirical answer to the moisture migration Pass 1 says technicians predict from experience — and sell workflow software. Public adjusters hold the only systematic measurement of how much carriers underscope, by carrier and loss type, and use it one claim at a time.
