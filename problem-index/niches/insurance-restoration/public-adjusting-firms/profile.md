# Public Adjusting Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/insurance-restoration|Insurance Restoration]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Public adjusters represent policyholders against carriers, re-scoping losses the carrier's adjuster underscoped and negotiating the difference — usually on a percentage of the additional recovery.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-100 adjusters, estimators, and analysts per larger firm
**Output:** Independent scopes and estimates, supplement claims, appraisal and negotiation support
**Proprietary data:** Original carrier scope against final settlement across many claims — a direct measurement of how much carriers underscope, by carrier and loss type
**Clock:** Policy proof-of-loss deadlines and appraisal timelines
**Buyer:** Principal / Managing Partner
