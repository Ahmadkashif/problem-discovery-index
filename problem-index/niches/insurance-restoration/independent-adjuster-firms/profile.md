# Independent Adjuster Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/insurance-restoration|Insurance Restoration]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Independent adjusting firms investigate and scope property claims on carriers' behalf, deploying at enormous scale after catastrophes when carrier staff cannot cover the volume. The adjuster's scope is what the claim is paid on.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-3,000 adjusters, reviewers, and quality staff, surging far higher after a catastrophe
**Output:** Damage scopes and estimates, coverage recommendations, claim reports
**Proprietary data:** Scope and settlement outcomes across many carriers and events; the files themselves belong to the carrier
**Clock:** Catastrophe deployment and carrier cycle time requirements
**Buyer:** COO / VP of Claims Operations
