# Cause & Origin Forensic Engineering

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/insurance-restoration|Insurance Restoration]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Forensic engineering firms determine what caused a fire, a water loss, or a structural failure, and their finding decides coverage, subrogation, and litigation exposure on the largest property claims.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-800 forensic engineers, fire investigators, and technicians
**Output:** Cause and origin reports, failure analyses, subrogation support, expert testimony
**Proprietary data:** Failure patterns by construction, product, and era across tens of thousands of investigations
**Clock:** Evidence preservation windows and litigation schedules
**Buyer:** Practice leader / Chief Engineer
