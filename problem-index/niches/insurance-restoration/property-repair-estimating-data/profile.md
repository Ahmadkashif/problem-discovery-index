# Property Repair Estimating Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/insurance-restoration|Insurance Restoration]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Xactware and CoreLogic publish the line-item unit costs — material, labour, and equipment for every repair task, priced by postal code and updated monthly — that essentially every US property insurance claim is settled on. Pass 1 records the consequence for the operator layer: Xactimate line-item accuracy is one of the criteria that determines whether a restoration company keeps the preferred vendor status representing 60-80% of its revenue.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 400-1,500 cost researchers, pricing analysts, data engineers, and content staff
**Output:** Monthly price lists by geography, line-item definitions, estimating methodology, claims analytics
**Proprietary data:** Continuous market cost research across thousands of local markets, plus the estimate corpus — effectively every property repair estimate written in the country passes through their software
**Clock:** Monthly price list releases; a claim is settled on whichever list was current
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Chief Product Officer
