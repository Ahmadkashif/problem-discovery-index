# Weather Verification Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/insurance-restoration|Insurance Restoration]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Weather forensics providers reconstruct what happened at a specific address on a specific date — hail size, wind speed, rainfall — from radar and station data, producing the report that supports or refutes a claim's stated cause of loss.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 meteorologists, data scientists, and analysts
**Output:** Address-level weather verification reports, hail and wind swath data, catastrophe footprints
**Proprietary data:** Reprocessed historical radar and observation archives with proprietary interpolation to address level
**Clock:** Claim investigation windows and catastrophe response
**Buyer:** Chief Meteorologist / VP of Product
