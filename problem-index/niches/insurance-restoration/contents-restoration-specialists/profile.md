# Contents Restoration Specialists

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/insurance-restoration|Insurance Restoration]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Contents specialists decide what personal property can be restored and what must be replaced, inventorying and valuing household goods after a fire or water loss — a judgment that moves the claim by tens of thousands of dollars.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 2-10 contents specialists per operation
**Output:** Contents inventories, restorability determinations, valuations
**Proprietary data:** Restorability outcomes by item type and damage exposure, held informally
**Clock:** Claim cycle time
**Buyer:** Owner-operator
