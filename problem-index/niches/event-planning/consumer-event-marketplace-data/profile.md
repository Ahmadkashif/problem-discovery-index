# Consumer Event Marketplace Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/event-planning|Event Planning]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
The Knot Worldwide and the consumer event marketplaces observe most US weddings from engagement through vendor booking, and publish the annual spend studies the entire consumer events industry references. The research is genuine and the revenue is vendor advertising.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 researchers, data scientists, and analysts
**Output:** Spend and behaviour studies, vendor category benchmarks, regional pricing research
**Proprietary data:** Booking, budget, and vendor selection behaviour across a large share of US weddings, longitudinally
**Clock:** Annual study publication; wedding season cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Insights / Chief Marketing Officer
