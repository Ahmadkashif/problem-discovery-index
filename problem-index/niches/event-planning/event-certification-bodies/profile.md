# Events Industry Certification Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/event-planning|Event Planning]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Events Industry Council and the professional bodies run the certification programmes the industry hires against, conduct the practice analyses that define competence, and maintain the standards planners are assessed on.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 certification staff, psychometricians, and standards developers
**Output:** Certification examinations, practice analyses, competency standards
**Proprietary data:** Item performance and candidate data across the certified population
**Clock:** Examination cycles and recertification periods
**Buyer:** Director of Certification
