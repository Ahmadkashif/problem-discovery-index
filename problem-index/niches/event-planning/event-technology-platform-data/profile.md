# Event Technology Platform Data Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/event-planning|Event Planning]]

## What They Do
The data groups at Cvent, Bizzabo, and the event platforms analyze the sourcing requests, contracts, registrations, and attendee behaviour flowing through their systems, and publish the market pace and rate benchmarks the industry plans against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 data scientists, analysts, and research staff
**Output:** Market pace and rate benchmarks, supplier performance analytics, attendee behaviour research
**Proprietary data:** Sourcing requests and contracted outcomes across a large share of US corporate meetings, visible before any market reporting
**Clock:** Continuous; publication cadence self-set
**Buyer:** VP of Data Science / Head of Research
