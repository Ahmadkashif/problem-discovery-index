# Meetings & Events Intelligence Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/event-planning|Event Planning]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Knowland and comparable providers employ research staff who collect what meetings actually took place — reading hotel reader boards, working property contacts, and monitoring event signals — and build a database of which organizations met where, at what size, in what season. Hotel and venue sales teams subscribe because there is no other way to know who books group business, and no participant publishes it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-400 field researchers, data analysts, and account intelligence staff
**Output:** Meeting activity records, account and group profiles, market share and pace analytics — the subscription
**Proprietary data:** Observed meeting activity assembled by human collection across thousands of properties over years, in a market where neither the venue nor the organizer discloses anything
**Clock:** Continuous collection; no external deadline governs publication
**Buyer:** VP of Research / Chief Data Officer
