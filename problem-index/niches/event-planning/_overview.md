## Why These Niches

Corporate events and weddings are the two dominant revenue segments — together they represent over 60% of industry revenue with fundamentally different client dynamics (corporate procurement vs. emotional consumer purchase). Nonprofit and festival production are digitally neglected despite complex logistics because their budgets don't justify enterprise event platforms. Hybrid events and cultural community events are underserved audiences — hybrid demands production capabilities that most planners lack, while cultural events need multilingual, community-accessible tools that don't exist. Vendor coordination and budget reconciliation represent the highest automation ROI — event planners spend 40-60% of their time on vendor communication and financial tracking that follows predictable patterns.

## Why These Pockets

One qualifier, in an industry whose insight layer is otherwise entirely funded by somebody's commission or advertising.

The qualifier is a human collection business. Meetings intelligence providers employ field researchers who establish what meetings actually took place — reading hotel reader boards, working property contacts, monitoring signals — in a market where neither the venue nor the organizer discloses anything. Hotel sales teams subscribe because there is no alternative source. Its gaps follow from the collection being inference. Coverage is unmeasured and certainly uneven, so a subscriber cannot tell whether a gap in an account's history means the account did not meet or the collection did not see it — opposite conclusions from the same blank. Entity resolution turns a reader board fragment into a pursuable account, done by researcher judgment, with fragmentation and false merges both damaging and neither measured. And the evidence behind each inferred record is discarded, so a firmly sourced entry and a thin guess look identical, and a departing researcher takes a market's data quality with them.

Three near misses at 46 are worth stating because they share one shape. Venue sourcing intermediaries hold the negotiated group rates that neither hotels nor organizers publish anywhere, and earn a commission from the party being negotiated against. Event platform data teams see sourcing requests and contracted outcomes across most US corporate meetings before any market reporting, and publish it free as marketing. Consumer marketplaces observe most US weddings from engagement through booking — data that would settle the industry's defining problem, the gap between what a client expects to spend and what the event costs — and are funded by vendor advertising.
