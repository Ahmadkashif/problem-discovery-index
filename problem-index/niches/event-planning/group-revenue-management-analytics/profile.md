# Group Revenue Management Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/event-planning|Event Planning]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The revenue management science groups at hotel companies and their software vendors price group business — deciding what a block of rooms plus meeting space is worth against the transient demand it displaces, which is the number every event planner negotiates against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 revenue scientists, analysts, and product staff
**Output:** Group pricing models, displacement analysis, forecasting
**Proprietary data:** Group inquiry, quote, and conversion data joined to realized transient demand across a portfolio
**Clock:** Booking windows and forecast cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Revenue Management / Chief Data Scientist
