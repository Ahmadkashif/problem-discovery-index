# Corporate Meetings Agency Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/event-planning|Event Planning]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The strategic meetings management and analytics groups at Maritz, BCD Meetings & Events, and CWT consolidate a corporate client's entire meetings spend, enforce policy, and produce the savings and compliance reporting the client's procurement function renews the contract against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 analysts, sourcing specialists, and programme managers
**Output:** Spend consolidation and savings reporting, policy compliance analytics, supplier performance
**Proprietary data:** Meetings spend and supplier performance across many corporate programmes
**Clock:** Client reporting cycles and annual budget planning
**Buyer:** VP of Analytics / Head of Strategic Meetings Management
