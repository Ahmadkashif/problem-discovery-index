# Event Safety & Security Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/event-planning|Event Planning]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Specialist firms produce the crowd management plans, risk assessments, and emergency operations documents that venues, municipalities, and insurers require before a large event proceeds. The written plan is the deliverable and it is examined after any incident.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-80 crowd scientists, security planners, and risk analysts
**Output:** Crowd management plans, risk assessments, emergency operations documentation
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated incident and near-miss findings across events and venue types
**Clock:** Event dates and permit approval deadlines
**Buyer:** Principal / Director of Planning
