# Venue Sourcing Intermediaries

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/event-planning|Event Planning]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
HelmsBriscoe, ConferenceDirect, and the sourcing networks place meetings into hotels and venues on behalf of organizers, negotiate the group rate and concessions, and earn a commission from the property. Their associates hold the only broad view of what group business actually transacts at.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 sourcing analysts, contract specialists, and market researchers
**Output:** Venue recommendations, negotiated rate and concession outcomes, market condition guidance
**Proprietary data:** Negotiated group rates, concession packages, and attrition terms across thousands of placements — data neither hotels nor organizers publish anywhere
**Clock:** Booking windows and event dates
**Buyer:** VP of Client Solutions / Head of Market Intelligence
