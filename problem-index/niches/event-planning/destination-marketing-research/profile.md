# Destination Marketing Organization Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/event-planning|Event Planning]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Convention and visitors bureaux research meeting demand for their destinations, produce the economic impact studies that justify public convention centre investment, and supply market intelligence to planners considering the city.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-30 research staff per large bureau
**Output:** Economic impact studies, demand research, destination market intelligence
**Proprietary data:** Booking pace and lead data for the destination, plus event impact history
**Clock:** Public funding cycles and annual reporting
**Buyer:** VP of Research — publicly funded
