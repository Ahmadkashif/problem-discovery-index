# Event Measurement & ROI Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/event-planning|Event Planning]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Small practices design measurement frameworks for corporate events — attribution to pipeline, attendee behaviour analysis, and the studies marketing leaders use to defend event budgets. The study is the invoice and it exists because event spend is chronically hard to justify.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 measurement specialists, analysts, and researchers
**Output:** Measurement frameworks, attribution studies, event impact analyses
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated measurement methodology; the underlying pipeline data belongs to clients
**Clock:** Client budget cycles and post-event reporting windows
**Buyer:** Managing Principal
