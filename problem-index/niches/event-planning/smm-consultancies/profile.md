# Strategic Meetings Management Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/event-planning|Event Planning]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Boutique consultancies design corporate meetings programmes — policy, supplier strategy, technology selection, and the business case that gets the programme funded. The written assessment and roadmap is the deliverable.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 consultants and analysts
**Output:** Programme assessments, policy design, supplier strategy, savings business cases
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated programme benchmarks across clients, largely client-owned
**Clock:** Client budget cycles and programme launch dates
**Buyer:** Principal / Managing Director
