# Buy: Identity Lifecycle, Already Solved

**Niche:** Programme Operations & Compliance Reporting
**Industry:** [[industries/security-awareness-training|Security Awareness Training]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Identity governance already handles joiners, leavers, role changes, contractors and shared accounts with a maturity awareness enrolment does not approach.
**Tags:** #compliance #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #evaluation-metrics #graph-theory
**Contested on:** Whether the administrative machinery runs itself, or consumes the awareness manager's week on enrolment, exceptions and evidence.

## The Problem

Managing who exists, who has access, what changes when they move roles, how contractors are handled, how shared accounts are governed and how leavers are removed is the identity governance problem, and it is thoroughly solved.

Identity governance platforms handle joiner-mover-leaver flows, integrate with HR and procurement systems, manage non-employee populations, govern shared and service accounts, certify access periodically and produce audit evidence continuously. The exceptions that consume an awareness manager's week are the ordinary daily work of an identity team.

Awareness platforms integrate with the HR system and treat everything else as an exception. The organisation frequently has an identity governance platform, run by a team down the corridor, that already holds a complete and current picture of the population the awareness programme is trying to enrol.

## What Already Exists

Identity governance: SailPoint, Saviynt, Okta Identity Governance and the category generally, with lifecycle management, non-employee identity handling, access certification and audit reporting.

Identity providers: the authoritative record of who can log in to what, maintained continuously.

Non-employee identity management: a distinct product area for contractors, vendors and partners, which is the population awareness enrolment misses most.

HR systems: the source most awareness platforms integrate with, which covers employees and not the rest.

Awareness platforms: HR-based enrolment with manual exception handling.

## The Customization Gap

**Enrolment uses the wrong source.** HR knows employees. Identity knows everyone who can log in, which is the population that matters for security training and includes the contractors, shared accounts and third parties HR does not.

**Non-employee lifecycle is solved and unused.** The specific product capability for managing contractor and vendor identities exists, is deployed at many organisations, and is not connected to awareness enrolment.

**Role change is handled there and not here.** Identity governance reassigns access on role change as a core function. Awareness assignment mostly does not respond to role changes at all.

**Shared account governance has an established practice.** Identity treats shared accounts as a governed object with named users and periodic certification, which is exactly the structure awareness training needs and does not have.

**Access certification is the model for coverage review.** Periodic certification that access is still appropriate is a mature practice, and its equivalent — periodic review that training assignment is still appropriate — does not exist.

**The teams do not talk.** Identity and awareness sit in the same security function, address overlapping populations, and integrate with different systems.

## Target Customer

Awareness platform vendors, for whom identity-based enrolment is a modest integration change producing much better coverage than HR-based enrolment.

Identity governance vendors, for whom training assignment is an adjacent entitlement — training is a thing a person should have, governed like any other — and a new use for data they already maintain.

Security leadership, who own both functions and would recognise immediately that one of them already knows the answer the other is struggling to assemble.

## Impact If Solved

A mature lifecycle capability in an adjacent product, run by an adjacent team in the same function, would resolve the exception handling that consumes the awareness manager's administrative time.

Enrolling from the identity provider rather than from HR would extend the programme to the contractors, third parties and shared accounts it currently misses, several of which are higher exposure than the average employee.

And treating training assignment as a governed entitlement, reviewed periodically like access, would keep assignment current as people move — which is a mature practice next door and an absent one here.
