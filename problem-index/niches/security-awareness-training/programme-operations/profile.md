# Programme Operations & Compliance Reporting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/security-awareness-training|Security Awareness Training]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Whether the administrative machinery runs itself, or consumes the awareness manager's week on enrolment, exceptions and evidence.

## Profile

**Market Size:** ~$60M
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2%
**Digital Adoption:** High — largely solved
**Target Buyer:** Compliance, programme operations, HR systems owners
**Automation Potential:** Very high — it is enrolment, tracking and evidence

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche

Underneath the campaigns and the content is the administration: enrolling new joiners, removing leavers, maintaining group membership as people change roles, handling exceptions and exclusions, chasing completions, producing the evidence package the auditor wants, and managing the language and accessibility variants.

Most of it works. Platforms integrate with HR systems, assign automatically, remind, escalate and report. It is the most operationally mature part of the category.

The contest is the residue, which is consistently the same set of problems. Joiner and leaver synchronisation is imperfect, so people are missed or chased after departure. Group membership goes stale as people change roles, so assignment reflects last year's organisation. Exceptions accumulate — the contractor, the shared account, the person on leave, the acquired subsidiary on a different HR system — and each becomes a manual case. And the auditor's evidence request is assembled by hand every year.

It is the smallest niche in the industry and the one where the remaining work is most obviously mechanical.

## Current Tools & Gaps

HR system integration for enrolment and deprovisioning. Automatic assignment by group. Reminder and escalation workflow. Completion reporting and evidence export. Multi-language delivery. Exception handling, usually manual.

The gaps are the exceptions and the joins. Contractors, shared accounts, field staff without individual mailboxes, acquired entities on separate systems and people on extended leave are each handled case by case and together are a substantial share of the manager's administrative time. Role changes do not reassign, so a person who moved into a high-exposure role keeps their old assignment. Coverage is not reported, so nobody knows what proportion of the workforce the programme actually reaches. And the audit evidence package is reassembled annually rather than maintained.
