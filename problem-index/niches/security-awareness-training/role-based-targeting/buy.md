# Buy: Adaptive Learning and Access Data

**Niche:** Role-Based Risk Targeting
**Industry:** [[industries/security-awareness-training|Security Awareness Training]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Adaptive learning platforms assess prior knowledge and skip what a learner already knows, and identity governance already computes exactly who holds which risky access.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #bayesian-inference #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #data-integration #automation #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Whether the programme reflects what a person is actually exposed to and what they already know.

## The Problem

Two mature capabilities would resolve most of this and neither is connected.

Adaptive learning platforms assess what a learner already knows and deliver only what they do not. They are standard in educational technology and in professional certification, they use well-established knowledge modelling, and they routinely cut course time substantially for learners with prior knowledge.

Identity governance platforms compute, continuously, exactly who has which access, which entitlements are privileged, which combinations are risky, and who can authorise what. They exist to manage access risk and they hold the clearest available exposure signal in the organisation.

Security awareness assigns the same content to everyone regardless of what they know, and groups them by department rather than by what they can actually do.

The pieces are in the building, in two other product categories, serving two other teams.

## What Already Exists

Adaptive learning: knowledge tracing and adaptive sequencing in educational platforms, professional certification systems that test out of known material, and the underlying models with open implementations.

Identity governance: SailPoint, Saviynt, Okta and the identity governance category, with entitlement inventories, privilege classification and segregation-of-duties analysis.

Data access governance: the platforms that map who can reach which data, with sensitivity classification.

Corporate learning platforms: the LMS category, with skills frameworks and competency-based assignment, described in [[industries/corporate-training|Corporate Training]].

Security awareness: uniform assignment with departmental grouping.

## The Customization Gap

**No integration with identity governance exists.** The system that knows exactly who holds risky access and the system that assigns security training have no connection, in most organisations run by teams that sit next to each other.

**Adaptive assessment is absent.** Awareness training has no diagnostic. Everyone starts at the beginning, which is the practice adaptive learning replaced.

**Knowledge modelling would need building.** Adaptive platforms model knowledge in structured domains with well-defined prerequisites. Security awareness content is not organised that way, and would need a competency model before adaptation could work.

**Exposure and knowledge are different dimensions.** Adaptive learning targets what you know; this needs to target what you know and what you are exposed to. Combining them is the adaptation and neither source category does it.

**Compliance requires universal completion.** Frameworks mandate training for everyone, which conflicts with skipping content — resolvable by recording a diagnostic pass as completion, which nobody does.

**The buyers are different teams.** Identity governance is bought by IAM, adaptive learning by L&D, awareness by security. Nobody is positioned to connect them.

## Target Customer

Awareness platform vendors, for whom identity governance integration is a modest build producing a genuinely differentiated targeting capability.

Identity governance vendors, for whom exposure-based training assignment is an adjacent use of the entitlement data they already compute, and a new buyer inside existing accounts.

Corporate learning platforms, whose adaptive and competency-based capabilities are more developed than anything in the security awareness category and who already serve the same organisations.

## Impact If Solved

Two mature capabilities in adjacent categories would resolve most of this, and the gap is integration rather than invention.

Identity governance entitlement data is the clearest exposure signal in any organisation and is computed continuously for another purpose entirely.

And a diagnostic that lets people skip material they demonstrably know would shorten the programme substantially for a large share of the workforce, which is both better learning and the single change most likely to improve how the programme is regarded.
