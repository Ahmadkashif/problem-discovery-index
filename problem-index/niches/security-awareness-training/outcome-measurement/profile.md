# Programme Outcome Measurement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/security-awareness-training|Security Awareness Training]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Whether simulated performance predicts resistance to real attacks, which every large customer holds the data to test.

## Profile

**Market Size:** ~$450M
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15%
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — the metric is the test
**Target Buyer:** Security leadership, insurers, auditors, boards
**Automation Potential:** High — the data is already collected

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche

The category has the cleanest available path to an outcome measure of any assurance business in this cluster, and does not take it.

Every customer runs an email security gateway that records the real phishing attempts reaching the organisation, including which were delivered and which were interacted with. Every customer has an incident record showing actual compromises and their initial access vectors. Every customer has a reporting stream from employees flagging real suspicious messages. And every customer has the simulation results alongside all of it.

So the question — does performance on simulations predict resistance to real attacks — is estimable inside any large customer, and across customers by a vendor with a large installed base. Whether people who click simulations are the ones who fall for real phishing is a join between two datasets the organisation already holds.

Nobody has done it. The stated reason is usually that real incidents are rare, which is true and is why the cross-customer version matters. The unstated reason is that the current metric improves reliably and the real one might not.

## Current Tools & Gaps

Simulation platforms reporting click rates and trends. Email gateways reporting blocked and delivered phishing volumes. Incident records with initial access vectors. Employee reporting streams. Published vendor benchmarks. Insurance applications asking whether awareness training is conducted.

The gaps are the whole niche. Nothing joins simulation results to gateway data, so the real threat level an organisation faces is not related to its programme. Nothing joins simulation results to incidents, so individual or organisational susceptibility has never been validated against a real outcome. Reporting streams are not used as an outcome measure despite being the behaviour that matters. No vendor has published outcome research from its own installed base. And insurers accept the programme as a control without any evidence relating it to loss.
