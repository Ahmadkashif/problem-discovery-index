# Medical Device Quality System Auditors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-manufacturing|Contract Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Notified bodies and accredited auditors assess contract manufacturers producing medical devices against quality system regulations and international standards, and issue the certifications without which the manufacturer cannot supply regulated product.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-400 auditors, technical reviewers, and certification staff
**Output:** Audit reports, non-conformance findings, certification decisions
**Proprietary data:** Findings across hundreds of manufacturing sites and the record of which corrective actions regulators accepted
**Clock:** Certification cycles and regulatory transition deadlines
**Buyer:** VP of Certification / Technical Director
