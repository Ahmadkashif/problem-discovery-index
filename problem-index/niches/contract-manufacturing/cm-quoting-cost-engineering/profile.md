# Contract Manufacturer Quoting & Cost Engineering

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-manufacturing|Contract Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The cost engineering and quoting organizations inside Jabil, Flex, Benchmark, and the large contract manufacturers price programmes that will run for years at margins of five to twelve percent, absorbing every yield loss and material overrun themselves. Getting the quote wrong is how contract manufacturers lose money.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 cost engineers, industrial engineers, and quoting analysts
**Output:** Programme quotes, should-cost analyses, yield and capacity models
**Proprietary data:** Actual cost, yield, and rework outcomes across thousands of past programmes, joined to what was quoted — the direct measure of quoting accuracy
**Clock:** RFQ submission deadlines, which are fixed and frequent
**Buyer:** VP of Cost Engineering / Chief Operating Officer
