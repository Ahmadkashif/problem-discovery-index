# Materials Property Databases

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-manufacturing|Contract Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Granta, Total Materia, and MatWeb curate materials property data — mechanical, thermal, electrical, and processing characteristics — from standards, supplier datasheets, and test literature, normalized so that designers and manufacturing engineers can compare and substitute materials. Selection decisions made against these databases determine manufacturability downstream.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 materials scientists, data curators, and engineers
**Output:** Curated property databases, comparison and selection tooling, standards cross-references
**Proprietary data:** Normalized property data across hundreds of thousands of materials, reconciled across standards regimes and test methods that do not agree
**Clock:** Standards revisions and supplier datasheet updates
**Buyer:** VP of Content / Director of Materials Data
