**Size:** 10-40 economists and researchers
**Output:** Economic forecasts, capital spending surveys, policy analysis
**Proprietary data:** Long-running member survey series
**Clock:** Quarterly publication and legislative sessions
**Buyer:** Chief Economist / VP of Research
