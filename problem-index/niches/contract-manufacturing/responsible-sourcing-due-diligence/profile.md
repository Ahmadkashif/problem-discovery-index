# Responsible Sourcing Due Diligence Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-manufacturing|Contract Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Responsible Minerals Initiative and comparable programmes audit smelters and refiners, maintain conformant facility lists, and supply the due diligence framework manufacturers use to satisfy conflict minerals and forced labour requirements. A facility falling off the conformant list propagates through thousands of supply chains.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 assessors, programme staff, and researchers
**Output:** Facility assessments, conformant lists, due diligence guidance, reporting templates
**Proprietary data:** Assessment findings across smelters and refiners globally, and the mapping from facilities to the supply chains that use them
**Clock:** Annual securities filing deadlines and import enforcement actions
**Buyer:** Programme Director / VP of Responsible Sourcing
