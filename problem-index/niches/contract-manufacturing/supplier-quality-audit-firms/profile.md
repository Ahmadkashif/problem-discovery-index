# Supplier Quality Audit Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-manufacturing|Contract Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Firms performing second-party supplier audits on behalf of OEMs — assessing a contract manufacturer's process control, traceability, and quality system before award and periodically after. The audit report determines whether a manufacturer gets the programme.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-200 auditors, quality engineers, and reviewers
**Output:** Supplier audit reports, capability assessments, corrective action verification
**Proprietary data:** Capability and non-conformance findings across hundreds of manufacturing sites
**Clock:** Award decisions and periodic audit cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Supplier Quality / Practice Leader
