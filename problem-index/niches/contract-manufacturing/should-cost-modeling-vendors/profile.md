# Should-Cost Modelling Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-manufacturing|Contract Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
aPriori, Facton, and the cost engineering consultancies model what a part ought to cost to make — decomposing a design into process steps, cycle times, machine rates, material, and regional labour — and sell that estimate to OEMs who use it to negotiate with contract manufacturers. The whole product is a defensible number to put across the table.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 cost engineers, manufacturing researchers, and data analysts
**Output:** Should-cost models, regional cost libraries, negotiation analyses
**Proprietary data:** Process time and machine rate data by region and technology, built through observation and study over decades and maintained continuously
**Clock:** Sourcing events and negotiation cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Cost Engineering / Chief Product Officer
