# Electronic Component Data Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-manufacturing|Contract Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
SiliconExpert, Z2Data, S&P Global's component database, and Nexar maintain structured data on millions of electronic components — parametric specifications, lifecycle and obsolescence status, product change notices, regulatory compliance declarations, and multi-source alternatives. Contract manufacturers and their OEM customers subscribe because a part going end-of-life mid-programme is a redesign, and a non-compliant part is a shipment stopped at a border.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-1,500 component analysts, data researchers, and compliance specialists
**Output:** The component database, obsolescence forecasts, compliance declarations, alternates and risk scoring — the subscription itself
**Proprietary data:** Curated parametric, lifecycle, and compliance data across millions of parts, assembled from manufacturer datasheets, change notices, and direct enquiry over decades — the manufacturers publish it inconsistently and nobody else normalizes it
**Clock:** Product change notices and end-of-life announcements, plus regulatory declaration deadlines that stop shipments
**Buyer:** VP of Content / Chief Data Officer
