# Export Control Classification & Trade Compliance

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-manufacturing|Contract Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Trade compliance groups and specialist consultancies classify products and technology against export control lists, determine licensing requirements, screen parties, and produce the written determinations that let a manufacturer ship. A wrong classification on a controlled item is a criminal exposure rather than a commercial one.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 trade compliance specialists, engineers, and attorneys
**Output:** Classification determinations, licensing analyses, screening and jurisdiction opinions
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated classification decisions joined to agency rulings and advisory opinions
**Clock:** Shipment dates, licence processing timelines, and enforcement actions
**Buyer:** VP of Global Trade Compliance / Practice Leader
