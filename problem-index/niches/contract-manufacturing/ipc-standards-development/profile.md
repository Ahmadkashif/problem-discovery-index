# Electronics Industry Standards Development

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-manufacturing|Contract Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
IPC writes the standards that define acceptable electronics assembly worldwide — solder joint criteria, design rules, cleanliness requirements — runs the certification programme that qualifies inspectors and operators against them, and publishes industry market research. Every contract manufacturer's quality system references these documents.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 80-250 standards developers, technical staff, certification and research personnel
**Output:** Assembly and design standards, certification programmes, industry market research
**Proprietary data:** Standards development history, certification performance across tens of thousands of individuals, and member-contributed market data
**Clock:** Standards revision cycles and certification renewal
**Buyer:** VP of Standards / Chief Technology Officer
