# Multi-Tier Supply Chain Risk Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-manufacturing|Contract Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Resilinc, Everstream, Interos, and Exiger map supply chains past the first tier — which sub-tier suppliers make which parts at which sites — and monitor those sites for disruption, financial distress, and regulatory exposure. OEMs and contract manufacturers subscribe because their own visibility stops at their direct suppliers and their risk does not.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 supply chain analysts, researchers, risk specialists, and data scientists
**Output:** Multi-tier supply maps, disruption alerts, site and supplier risk assessments — the subscription
**Proprietary data:** Sub-tier supplier and site relationships assembled from voluntary supplier disclosure, trade records, and analyst research over years — a map nobody, including the manufacturers themselves, otherwise holds
**Clock:** Disruption events, plus forced labour and sanctions compliance deadlines that stop shipments at the border
**Buyer:** Chief Research Officer / VP of Supply Chain Intelligence
