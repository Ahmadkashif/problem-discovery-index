# Sewer Inspection Defect Coding Standards Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/plumbing-contractors|Plumbing Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Define and certify the defect coding scheme sewer camera inspections are recorded in — the standardised vocabulary for root intrusion, offsets, cracking, bellies and deposits — and certify the operators who apply it. Pass 1 identifies reading sewer camera footage as a specific, high-value instance of the industry's tacit knowledge, where experienced technicians identify defects junior ones miss or misclassify.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 standards staff, trainers, and certification personnel
**Output:** Defect coding standards, operator certification, training curricula, data exchange specifications
**Proprietary data:** Certification examination performance and the coding standard's revision history; the coded inspection data itself belongs to the utilities
**Clock:** Certification renewal and standard revision cycles
**Buyer:** Executive Director
