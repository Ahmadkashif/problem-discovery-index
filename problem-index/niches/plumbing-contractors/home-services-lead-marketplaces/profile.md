Match homeowners with emergency and planned plumbing needs to contractors, and run the matching, pricing and quality analytics over a very large volume of urgent service requests.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 data scientists, marketplace analysts, and trust and safety staff
**Output:** Lead matching and pricing, demand forecasting, contractor quality scoring, published cost guides
**Proprietary data:** Millions of described problems joined to quotes, hires and reviews
**Clock:** Continuous, with sharp weather-driven emergency spikes
**Buyer:** VP of Data Science
