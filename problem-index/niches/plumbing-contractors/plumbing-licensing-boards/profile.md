# State Plumbing Licensing & Inspection Authorities

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/plumbing-contractors|Plumbing Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
License plumbers and contractors, administer journeyman and master examinations, and inspect installed work against the adopted code.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 per jurisdiction — examination, licensing, and inspection staff
**Output:** Licensure, examinations, inspection results and corrections, disciplinary actions
**Proprietary data:** Inspection failure records at address level, held municipally and almost never analysed
**Clock:** Licence renewal cycles and permit inspection schedules
**Buyer:** Board executive / Chief Building Official
