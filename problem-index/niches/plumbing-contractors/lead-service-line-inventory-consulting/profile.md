# Lead Service Line Inventory & Replacement Consulting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/plumbing-contractors|Plumbing Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Tell every water system in the United States what material each of its service lines is made of, and in what order to replace the lead ones. Federal rules require a complete service line inventory and set a replacement schedule, and the records that would answer the question — tap cards, permit files, meter installation records, plumbing codes in force at the time of construction — are incomplete, inconsistent, and largely on paper. The consultancy's deliverable is a defensible determination for every address in a system, plus the plan to dig them up.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-800 engineers, data analysts, records researchers, GIS staff, and statisticians across the specialist firms and the water practices of the large consultancies
**Output:** Service line material inventories, predictive material determinations, statistically designed verification sampling plans, replacement prioritisation and programme plans, regulatory submissions and public notification support
**Proprietary data:** Verified material determinations at address level across hundreds of systems, joined to the historical records and construction-era attributes that predicted them — the only accumulating evidence base about what is actually buried where
**Clock:** Hard federal deadlines — inventory submission, annual updates, mandatory replacement rates, and public notification obligations that attach to every unknown line
**Buyer:** VP of Water Practice / Chief Engineer
