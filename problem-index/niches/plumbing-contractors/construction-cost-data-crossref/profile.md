# Mechanical & Plumbing Cost Data Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/plumbing-contractors|Plumbing Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Publish the labour unit and material cost databases plumbing and mechanical work is estimated against — installation hours per fixture, per foot of pipe by material and diameter, adjusted for building type, height and region.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-400 cost engineers, data collectors, and analysts
**Output:** Labour unit databases, material cost feeds, regional cost indices, annual cost books
**Proprietary data:** Crew productivity observations collected over decades, against a task taxonomy nobody else maintains
**Clock:** Annual publication with quarterly updates
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Chief Product Officer
