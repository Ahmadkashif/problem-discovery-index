# Buried Water Infrastructure Condition Assessment

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/plumbing-contractors|Plumbing Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Inspect buried water and wastewater mains without digging them up — acoustic leak detection, electromagnetic wall thickness, CCTV and sonar survey — and report which segments are failing and in what order they should be replaced.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 engineers, inspection technicians, data analysts, and asset management specialists
**Output:** Condition assessments, remaining life estimates, leak location, renewal prioritisation, capital planning input
**Proprietary data:** Decades of inspection records on buried pipe by material, diameter, age and soil, some of it joined to subsequent breaks
**Clock:** Capital planning cycles; no statutory driver for the assessment itself
**Buyer:** VP of Asset Management
