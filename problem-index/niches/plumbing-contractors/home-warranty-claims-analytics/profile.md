# Home Warranty & Service Contract Claims Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/plumbing-contractors|Plumbing Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Price and administer home service contracts covering plumbing systems and water heaters, decide claim by claim what is covered, and manage the contractor networks that perform the work.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 actuaries, claims analysts, network managers, and data scientists
**Output:** Contract pricing, claim adjudication rules, failure frequency models, contractor cost and quality analytics
**Proprietary data:** Component failure and repair cost histories across millions of covered homes, joined to home age, system type and region
**Clock:** Annual contract renewals and continuous claim flow
**Buyer:** Chief Actuary / VP of Analytics
