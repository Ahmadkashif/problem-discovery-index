# Plumbing Product Certification & Listing Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/plumbing-contractors|Plumbing Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Test and certify plumbing products against the standards codes require — pressure, flow, material safety, and the drinking water contact certification no product can be sold without — and maintain the listing directories inspectors check against in the field.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 test engineers, certification reviewers, standards staff, and auditors
**Output:** Product certifications and listings, test reports, standards development, factory audit findings
**Proprietary data:** Decades of test results across products and manufacturers, plus the record of what failed and why
**Clock:** Certification renewal cycles, standard revisions, and factory audit schedules
**Buyer:** VP of Certification Services
