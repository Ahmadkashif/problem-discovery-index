# Plumbing Fixture & Fitting Manufacturer R&D

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/plumbing-contractors|Plumbing Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Product development, materials engineering and reliability testing inside the fixture, valve, water heater and piping manufacturers, plus the technical service groups that diagnose field failures.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-1,500 engineers, materials scientists, and reliability and technical service staff
**Output:** Product designs, accelerated life testing, warranty failure analysis, installation specifications, technical support
**Proprietary data:** Warranty and field failure records joined to build and materials data across the entire installed base of their products
**Clock:** Product development programmes and warranty reporting
**Buyer:** VP of Engineering
