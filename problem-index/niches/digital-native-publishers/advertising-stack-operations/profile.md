# Advertising Stack Operations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/digital-native-publishers|Digital Native Publishers]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to run a deep, leaky ad stack that funds the newsroom without destroying the page experience that retains readers — and whoever manages that trade with evidence rather than instinct keeps both revenue and audience.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~21% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** High — sophisticated and unmeasured at the margin
**Target Buyer:** Revenue operations leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — yield, latency and experience optimisation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The ad stack is deep: header bidding, multiple demand partners, direct campaigns, identity solutions and a constant stream of configuration decisions. It funds the journalism and it degrades the page — latency, layout shift, intrusive formats and the cumulative effect on whether a reader comes back. Both sides of that trade are measurable and the industry manages one of them.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Header bidding wrappers, ad servers, demand partner dashboards and page speed tools. The gaps: the retention cost of ad experience never measured; partner value not evaluated net of latency; configuration changes made without testing; and yield optimised without reference to audience effects.
