# Supply Chain Transparency Practice

**Niche:** [[niches/digital-native-publishers/revenue-attribution-per-page/profile|Revenue Attribution per Page]]
**Industry:** [[industries/digital-native-publishers|Digital Native Publishers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Advertisers ran supply chain transparency studies and found the leakage, and publishers on the other end of the same chain never ran the equivalent.
**Tags:** #data-integration #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact #descriptive-statistics #confidence-intervals #compliance #automation #graph-theory
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to say what a page actually earns after a chain of intermediaries has taken undisclosed margin — and whoever can follow the money gives publishers the first true unit economics they have ever had.

## The Problem
Advertiser-side transparency studies traced spend through the programmatic chain, matched buyer logs to publisher logs, and quantified how much never reached a publisher at all. The method is established: obtain both ends of the chain, match at the impression level, and report the gap. Advertisers did it because they were paying. Publishers, who are receiving, have largely not done the mirror-image analysis on their own inventory.

## What Already Exists
Log-level matching methodologies; supply path optimisation practice; transparency study designs; contractual audit rights; and industry frameworks for fee disclosure.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to the sell side. It requires: (1) the publisher as the party seeking transparency rather than the advertiser, so the leverage is weaker and the data access harder — this is the substantive difference and determines what is obtainable; (2) matching at the impression level against intermediaries who have no obligation to cooperate, making inference from discrepancies necessary; (3) attribution down to the page rather than to a campaign, which no transparency study attempts; (4) non-advertising revenue included, which the method has no concept of; and (5) small publishers without audit rights or legal budget, so the analysis must work from what is already received.

## Target Customer
Revenue and finance leadership, publisher trade bodies, advertisers, and supply path and transparency vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Advertisers proved the method and found the leakage because they were paying. Running it from the receiving end, down to the page, is the mirror image nobody has built and is where the publisher's leverage would come from.
