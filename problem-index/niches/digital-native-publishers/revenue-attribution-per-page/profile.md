# Revenue Attribution per Page

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/digital-native-publishers|Digital Native Publishers]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to say what a page actually earns after a chain of intermediaries has taken undisclosed margin — and whoever can follow the money gives publishers the first true unit economics they have ever had.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.8B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — revenue reported in aggregate
**Target Buyer:** Revenue and finance leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — reconciliation across logs and reports

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This contest is accounting through a supply chain. Between an impression and the money sit exchanges, networks, intermediaries and platforms, each taking a cut that is not fully disclosed, reporting different numbers from each other and from the publisher's own logs. Adding subscription contribution, licensing and commerce earnings per page gives a complete unit economics that no publisher currently has. It is reconciliation and counterparty work, not causal inference about readers.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Ad server reports, exchange dashboards, aggregate revenue statements and a spreadsheet. The gaps: revenue not attributed to pages; intermediary take not measured; discrepancies between reports unreconciled; non-advertising revenue never allocated; and cost per page never computed at all.
