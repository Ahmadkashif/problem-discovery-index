# Content Value Measurement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/digital-native-publishers|Digital Native Publishers]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to say what a piece of journalism is actually worth to the business — and the contest splits cleanly enough that it is not terminal.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$4B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~29% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — pageviews measured precisely
**Target Buyer:** Data and editorial leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the record exists and is unread

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The industry ranks its journalism by pageviews while its revenue has shifted to subscriptions, licensing and commerce. The question that now determines survival — which articles create durable readers and what each page actually earns — is answerable from data publishers already hold, and the stack does not address it because analytics vendors ship what the advertising model rewarded for twenty years. This is the category's largest contest and it is two: causation about readers and accounting about money.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Web analytics, content dashboards, ad revenue reports and subscription counts. The gaps: content not linked to subscription outcomes; revenue not attributed to pages; the causal question never posed; editorial ranked on traffic; and the behavioural record unused.
