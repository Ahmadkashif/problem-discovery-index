# The Reporter

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/digital-native-publishers|Digital Native Publishers]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stop measuring reporters on a metric the business has already abandoned — and whoever replaces it with something that reflects contribution changes what the newsroom produces.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.4B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — traffic targets and a CMS
**Target Buyer:** Editorial leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — measurement, research support and workflow

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Reporters are measured on traffic and asked to produce volume against a referral supply that keeps shrinking, by a business that already knows traffic is the wrong measure. The demoralising part is not the workload but the incoherence: everyone involved understands that the target does not correspond to what the company needs, and it remains the target because nothing has replaced it. Around that sit the ordinary costs of the job — research, sourcing, records, transcription — that consume hours and produce nothing visible.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A content management system, a traffic dashboard, a transcription tool if they are lucky, and a target. The gaps: measured on the wrong outcome; no view of their own contribution to subscriptions; research and sourcing unsupported; the publication's own archive unusable; and volume expectations set against a declining supply.
