**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to decide who sees what for free, when, and at what price — and whoever measures the readers a paywall turns away as well as the subscribers it converts optimises the whole thing rather than half of it.

## The Problem
Revenue management is a mature science: segment demand, price differentially, allocate inventory between segments, and optimise total revenue rather than any single conversion rate. Airlines and hotels run it continuously with dedicated teams. A publisher faces a structurally similar problem — heterogeneous willingness to pay, a decision about who gets access on what terms, and an outcome that should be total contribution — and answers it with one rule.

## What Already Exists
Demand segmentation and willingness-to-pay estimation; differential and dynamic pricing; inventory allocation between fare classes; overbooking and no-show modelling; and total revenue optimisation practice.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a good that is not scarce. It requires: (1) no capacity constraint, since serving another reader costs nothing — which removes the allocation problem revenue management was built around and replaces it with a habit-versus-revenue trade; (2) a free reader who has real value as advertising inventory and as a future subscriber, so the excluded segment is not worthless as an empty seat is; (3) fairness and editorial mission considerations that constrain differential pricing in ways airlines do not face; (4) a purchase that is a recurring relationship rather than a one-off transaction, so retention enters the objective; and (5) public sensitivity to differential pricing, which makes personalisation a reputational question.

## Target Customer
Subscription and revenue leadership, paywall vendors, publisher executives, and pricing science vendors with no media presence.

## Impact If Solved
Revenue management optimises allocation of a scarce good and the good here is not scarce. Replacing the capacity constraint with a habit-versus-revenue trade, where the excluded reader still has value, is the adaptation.
