# Commerce & Affiliate

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/digital-native-publishers|Digital Native Publishers]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to run a commerce line that earns without costing the trust the journalism depends on — and whoever measures both sides of that trade runs it as a business rather than as a side project.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$500M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~4% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — links inserted, little managed
**Target Buyer:** Commerce leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — link management, attribution and inventory

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Commerce and affiliate revenue grew because it worked and is run as a side project: links inserted by writers, commission reported by networks, product recommendations refreshed when someone remembers. It is one of the revenue lines growing while advertising contracts, it trades directly against reader trust, and almost nothing about it is measured — not link health, not conversion by placement, not the reputational cost, and not whether the recommendation was any good.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Affiliate network dashboards, a link plugin and a spreadsheet of commission rates. The gaps: broken and outdated links unmonitored; performance by placement and format unmeasured; product recommendations not refreshed; commission not reconciled; and the trust cost never examined.
