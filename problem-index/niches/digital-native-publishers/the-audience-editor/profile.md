# The Audience Editor

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/digital-native-publishers|Digital Native Publishers]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make someone accountable for traffic from platforms that change without notice and are actively reducing supply — and whoever gives that person evidence instead of folklore changes what the role can do.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — dashboards and speculation
**Target Buyer:** Audience leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — detection, attribution and forecasting

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Audience editors are responsible for traffic from platforms that change without notice, do not explain, and are deliberately reducing the supply. They are held to targets set against a declining base, diagnose ranking changes from folklore and forum posts, and are the first people blamed when a platform decides something. The role exists at the exact point where the publisher's dependency on systems it does not control becomes a person's job.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Analytics, search console data, social dashboards and industry chatter. The gaps: platform changes detected late and diagnosed by rumour; traffic loss not attributed to cause; targets set from a declining base; no forecast; and no distinction between a platform change and the publisher's own doing.
