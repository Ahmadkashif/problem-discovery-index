# Royalty Checks Nobody Reconciles

**Niche:** [[niches/trust-companies/special-asset-administration/profile|Special Asset Administration]]
**Industry:** [[industries/trust-companies|Trust Companies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Oil and gas royalties held in trusts arrive as checks and stubs that are deposited and posted, and almost nobody compares them with what the wells produced.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #change-point-detection #time-series-forecasting #data-integration #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to value, manage and report the farms, buildings, family companies and mineral interests held in trusts with evidence an examiner and a remainder beneficiary will accept, and whoever produces defensible special-asset reviews without a specialist visiting every asset every year takes the account.

## The Problem
A trust may hold small royalty interests in dozens of wells operated by several companies. Each operator sends statements in its own format showing volumes, prices, deductions and the trust's decimal interest. Payments stop when an operator changes or a division order lapses; deductions creep up; decimal interests are wrong for years. The trust accounting system posts the cash and sees nothing unusual.

## Why It's Still Broken
Statement formats vary by operator, volumes are small per trust, and few trust officers know how to read a check stub. Mineral management firms do this work for a fee, but many departments hold too few interests to hire one.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Automated reading of operator statements into a standard record, comparison against the trust's decimal interest and against expected production and prices, and alerts when payments stop, deductions jump or volumes break from trend — with a drafted inquiry to the operator.

## Who Feels the Pain
Trust officers responsible for mineral interests they did not choose; beneficiaries whose income quietly falls; and trustees exposed if underpayment is later discovered.

## Impact If Fixed
Catching stopped or underpaid royalties within a quarter rather than years recovers income the trust is owed and documents the oversight a prudent trustee is expected to show.
