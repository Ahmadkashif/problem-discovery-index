# Property Management Software Fitted to a Fiduciary

**Niche:** [[niches/trust-companies/special-asset-administration/profile|Special Asset Administration]]
**Industry:** [[industries/trust-companies|Trust Companies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Property management software tracks leases, rents and maintenance for landlords; a trustee also needs income-and-principal allocation, impartiality between beneficiaries and a review record an examiner reads.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #feature-engineering #data-integration #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to value, manage and report the farms, buildings, family companies and mineral interests held in trusts with evidence an examiner and a remainder beneficiary will accept, and whoever produces defensible special-asset reviews without a specialist visiting every asset every year takes the account.

## The Problem
Trust-held real estate is managed through outside property managers or directly by the officer, with rents deposited into the trust and expenses paid from it. The trust accounting system sees the cash; the lease terms, tenant history and condition live elsewhere or nowhere.

## What Already Exists
Property management platforms such as AppFolio and Yardi for residential and commercial landlords, and farm lease and record tools used by farm managers.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: allocation of rents, capital improvements, depreciation and mineral receipts between income and principal under the applicable state principal and income act; a view of how each asset serves income versus remainder beneficiaries; a feed into the trust accounting ledger rather than a separate general ledger; special-asset review templates and committee packets; and handling of the asset types landlords never see, such as farm crop-share leases, royalty interests and minority business stakes.

## Target Customer
Special-asset officers at trust companies managing tens to hundreds of trust-held properties.

## Impact If Solved
One record per asset tied to the trust ledger removes duplicate entry and allocation errors and turns the annual review into a check rather than a reconstruction.
