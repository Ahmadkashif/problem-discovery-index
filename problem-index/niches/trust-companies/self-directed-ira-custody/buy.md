# Alternative-Asset Data Platforms Fitted to IRA Custody

**Niche:** [[niches/trust-companies/self-directed-ira-custody/profile|Self-Directed IRA Alternative-Asset Custody]]
**Industry:** [[industries/trust-companies|Trust Companies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Alternative-investment platforms collect valuations and statements from fund sponsors for wealth managers; IRA custodians need the same for thousands of tiny sponsors, single properties and private notes.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #feature-engineering #data-integration #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to hold real estate, private notes and LLC interests for IRA owners while catching prohibited transactions and reporting a defensible value every year without giving advice, and whoever screens and values alternative assets with the least manual review takes the account.

## The Problem
Every year the custodian needs a value for every asset, and for alternative assets that value comes from a sponsor statement, an appraisal or the owner's own letter.

## What Already Exists
Alternative-investment platforms and fund-data aggregation services used by wealth managers to collect capital account statements and valuations from established fund sponsors.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: coverage of the long tail — small private placements, single-property LLCs, promissory notes, real estate — where no sponsor portal exists; mapping of each holding to the Form 5498 box 15 codes; evidence standards for what counts as a qualified valuation for each asset type; stale-value detection; and owner-facing requests that do not stray into advice.

## Target Customer
Self-directed IRA custodians with tens of thousands of alternative holdings.

## Impact If Solved
A defensible value on file for each asset before the reporting deadline, with less manual chasing and fewer values carried forward unchanged for years.
