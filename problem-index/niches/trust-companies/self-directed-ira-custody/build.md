# Prohibited-Transaction and Sponsor Screening From the Documents

**Niche:** [[niches/trust-companies/self-directed-ira-custody/profile|Self-Directed IRA Alternative-Asset Custody]]
**Industry:** [[industries/trust-companies|Trust Companies]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A self-directed IRA custodian processes deeds, notes and operating agreements it does not read for substance, so a tenant who is the owner's son or a sponsor recruiting hundreds of IRA investors goes unnoticed.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #graph-theory #k-means-clustering #evaluation-metrics #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to hold real estate, private notes and LLC interests for IRA owners while catching prohibited transactions and reporting a defensible value every year without giving advice, and whoever screens and values alternative assets with the least manual review takes the account.

## The Problem
Owners direct their IRAs into assets with names, addresses and parties in the documents: a lease, a promissory note, an LLC's member list. Transactions with disqualified persons can disqualify the whole IRA, and fraudulent sponsors have used self-directed IRAs to raise money from retirees. The custodian does not advise, but it holds the documents and the payment flows that would reveal both.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Custodians position themselves as passive to limit liability, documents are unstructured and varied, and each custodian is too small to fund the tooling alone.

## What to Build
Document reading that extracts parties, addresses, terms and payment instructions from each asset's paperwork and compares them with the owner's known relationships and addresses; a graph across accounts that shows clusters of owners investing in the same sponsor, entity or address, with growth and payment-pattern flags; and a review queue that documents what was checked without turning the custodian into an adviser.

## Target Customer
Operations and compliance heads at self-directed IRA custodians.

## Impact If Built
Earlier detection of self-dealing and of sponsor concentrations that look like fraud, protecting owners' tax status and the custodian's reputation, with a screening record regulators can see.
