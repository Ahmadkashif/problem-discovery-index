# Self-Directed IRA Alternative-Asset Custody

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/trust-companies|Trust Companies]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to hold real estate, private notes and LLC interests for IRA owners while catching prohibited transactions and reporting a defensible value every year without giving advice, and whoever screens and values alternative assets with the least manual review takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$100M a year in technology, valuation and outsourced services bought by self-directed IRA custodians (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** under 1% of fiduciary income (estimate); IRA custody overall is far larger, with ~$2.9T of non-managed IRA assets in ~25M accounts at insured institutions alone at the end of 2025, almost all of it in conventional securities
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — online account opening, but deeds, promissory notes, subscription documents and valuation letters move as PDFs and paper
**Target Buyer:** COO / Head of Operations at a self-directed IRA custodian
**Automation Potential:** High for document intake and valuation collection, medium for screening

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Self-directed IRA custodians, mostly state-chartered trust companies, hold assets mainstream custodians refuse: rental houses, private loans, LLC and limited partnership interests, private placements, precious metals. The account owner directs every investment; the custodian is passive and gives no advice, yet it must report each asset's year-end fair market value on Form 5498 (with box 15 codes for non-traded stock, debt, LLC interests and real estate), process the asset's cash flows, and avoid facilitating transactions with disqualified persons. The audience the tools ignore is the owner holding a single property or note in an IRA, and the custodian's small operations team serving tens of thousands of such owners. Competitors include Equity Trust, Inspira Financial (formerly Millennium Trust), Kingdom Trust, Provident Trust Group and Entrust.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Custodial recordkeeping systems built for securities, plus document imaging and manual valuation requests. The gaps: no reading of the asset documents for disqualified-person red flags, valuation letters chased by mail, stale values carried forward, and no cross-account view when many owners invest in the same sponsor.
