# The Year-End Valuation Letter Chased by Mail

**Niche:** [[niches/trust-companies/self-directed-ira-custody/profile|Self-Directed IRA Alternative-Asset Custody]]
**Industry:** [[industries/trust-companies|Trust Companies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Each winter the custodian mails valuation requests for every alternative asset, waits, re-mails, and reports last year's value for whatever never came back.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #descriptive-statistics #workflow-orchestration #compliance #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to hold real estate, private notes and LLC interests for IRA owners while catching prohibited transactions and reporting a defensible value every year without giving advice, and whoever screens and values alternative assets with the least manual review takes the account.

## The Problem
Owners and sponsors respond late or not at all; responses arrive as letters, spreadsheets and appraisals in every format; operations staff key them in. Values that never arrive are carried forward, so an IRA may report the same number for a property for years.

## Why It's Still Broken
The custodian depends on parties it does not control, the work is seasonal, and nothing measures how stale the reported values have become.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Automated requests by email and portal with reminders scheduled backwards from the reporting deadline, ingestion and extraction of whatever comes back, plausibility checks against prior values and asset type, and a dashboard of stale and missing values that escalates the oldest.

## Who Feels the Pain
Operations staff during valuation season; owners who receive inaccurate statements; custodians exposed when an asset reported at par turns out to be worthless.

## Impact If Fixed
More current values with less keying, and a measured stale-value rate the custodian can manage down.
