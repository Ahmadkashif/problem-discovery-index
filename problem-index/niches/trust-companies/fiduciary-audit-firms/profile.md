# Outsourced Fiduciary Audit & Controls Reporting Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/trust-companies|Trust Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
The financial institutions practices of regional CPA and consulting firms audit trust departments' fiduciary activities for their audit committees, test account administration against instruments and policy, and issue controls reports on trust operations for the trust companies that service other institutions' accounts.

## Insight Function
**Size:** The ~10-50 auditors per firm (estimate) test sampled accounts at volume and are production staff, not counted for the gate; the methodology and analytics layer above them is under ~10 per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Fiduciary audit reports, findings on sampled accounts, controls reports
**Proprietary data:** None of its own; the accounts, instruments and findings belong to the client institution
**Clock:** The client's audit cycle and its trust examination schedule
**Buyer:** Practice leader, financial institutions assurance
