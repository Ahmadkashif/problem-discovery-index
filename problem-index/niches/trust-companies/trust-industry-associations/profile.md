# Trust Industry Associations & Credentialing Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/trust-companies|Trust Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The American Bankers Association's wealth management and trust programmes and the Institute of Certified Bankers, which awards the CTFA credential that trust officer postings prefer; trust training firms such as Cannon Financial Institute; state trust associations; and fiduciary risk management groups. They run conferences, schools and credentialing, and occasionally publish surveys.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under ~10 research staff at any one body (estimate)
**Output:** Training, credential exams, conference programmes, occasional member surveys
**Proprietary data:** Member and candidate records; small survey panels
**Clock:** Conference and exam calendars
**Buyer:** Executive director / head of programmes
