# Corporate Trust & Indenture Administration

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/trust-companies|Trust Companies]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that corporate trustees compete to administer indentures without taking on liability they did not price, but in performing structured deals that means computing every payment-date waterfall and report correctly on a fixed monthly clock, while in defaulted issues it means acting only on proper holder direction with indemnity and notice. Different teams, clocks and winners. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$500M a year in corporate trust technology, data and outsourced services (estimate), against ~$3.6B of gross corporate trust and agency fiduciary income at FDIC-insured institutions in 2025
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~6% of FDIC-reported fiduciary income ($3.6B of $55.6B)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — payments and registers are digital; indentures are read by hand and many duties are tracked in calendars
**Target Buyer:** Head of Corporate Trust
**Automation Potential:** High for reporting and obligations tracking, low for default judgement

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Insured trustees acted on ~424,000 corporate and municipal trusteeships with ~$30.5T outstanding at the end of 2025, concentrated in U.S. Bank, BNY, Computershare (which bought Wells Fargo's corporate trust business in 2021) and Citi. The trustee, paying agent, registrar, escrow agent or collateral administrator has duties that are mostly ministerial while an issue performs and become fiduciary after a default. Fees are thin per issue; liability is the risk. Unlike personal trust there are no beneficiaries to counsel, only documents to obey, which is why the work splits cleanly by deal state.

## Current Tools & Gaps
In-house trust systems for payments and registers, deal-by-deal waterfall code or spreadsheets, depository notice channels and outside counsel. The gaps: no structured map of each indenture's duties, notices and holder thresholds across the book; waterfall logic re-coded per deal; and successor-trustee handovers that arrive incomplete.
