## The Problem
Trusteeships change hands when trustees resign in defaults, when conflicts arise, and in portfolio sales such as Wells Fargo's corporate trust business moving to Computershare in 2021. Each handover should include the indenture and every supplement, the register, payment history, open notices and pending matters. Files arrive partial, in inconsistent formats, and the successor discovers the gaps when a duty falls due.

## Why It's Still Broken
The outgoing trustee has little incentive to curate files for a competitor, and no standard defines a complete handover package.

## What a Fix Looks Like
An intake checklist generated from the documents actually received: extract obligations from what arrived, list which supplements and records are referenced but missing, compare payment history with the schedule, and drive a chase list to the predecessor with deadlines before the first duty falls due.

## Who Feels the Pain
Successor-trustee relationship managers and the risk teams that accepted the appointment.

## Impact If Fixed
Gaps found in the first week rather than at the first missed notice, and a documented handover that protects the successor if a predecessor's error surfaces later.
