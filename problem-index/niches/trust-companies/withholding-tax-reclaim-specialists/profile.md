# Withholding Tax Reclaim Specialists

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/trust-companies|Trust Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Cross-border investors suffer dividend and interest withholding above treaty rates and must reclaim the excess from each foreign tax authority. Custody banks reclaim for clients as part of asset servicing and outsource the harder markets and back years to specialists such as Goal Group and TaxTec, which maintain jurisdiction rules and documentation requirements and file reclaims for a contingency fee. TaxTec's 2025 study, a vendor estimate, put unclaimed withholding at $16.4B globally.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-60 tax-rule researchers and reclaim analysts per firm (estimate); filing operations are production
**Output:** Reclaim filings and recoveries, jurisdiction rule libraries, unclaimed-reclaim analyses for custodians and asset owners
**Proprietary data:** Jurisdiction rules, forms and authority behaviour accumulated over years (owned), applied to clients' dividend and holding records (client-owned)
**Clock:** Each tax authority's limitation period for reclaims — third-party, but rolling over years rather than a single dated deadline
**Buyer:** Head of tax services
