# Custody Network & Depository Risk Assessors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/trust-companies|Trust Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms such as Thomas Murray assess and rate central securities depositories, sub-custodians and other market infrastructure that custody banks rely on in each market, scoring them on risk components such as asset safety, asset servicing, liquidity, counterparty, operational and governance risk. Custody banks' network management teams, broker-dealers, fund boards and asset owners buy the ratings and underlying assessments to choose and monitor their agents and to meet rules that leave depository risk with the investor — under Rule 17f-7 the primary custodian must give a fund an initial risk analysis of depository arrangements and monitor them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-80 analysts and assessors at the largest specialist provider (estimate)
**Output:** Depository and sub-custodian risk ratings, assessment reports, market profiles and due-diligence data
**Proprietary data:** Years of assessments of the same infrastructures, built from questionnaires, site visits and analyst judgement — owned by the assessor
**Clock:** Annual network reviews and fund-board reporting under Rule 17f-7 and comparable depositary rules abroad; recurring, but not a dated third-party deadline on the assessor
**Buyer:** Head of Network Management at custody banks and brokers; for the pocket itself, the head of the ratings business
