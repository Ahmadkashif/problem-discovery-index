# Fiduciary Tax Compliance

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/trust-companies|Trust Companies]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to file every trust's 1041 and K-1s correctly by the April deadline despite late and corrected tax information, and whoever finishes the most returns without an amendment takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600M a year in fiduciary tax software, outsourced preparation and tax information services bought by trust companies (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1% of FDIC-reported fiduciary income spent on tax tooling and outsourcing (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — ledgers export to tax software, but tie-outs, elections and corrections are worked by hand
**Target Buyer:** Head of Fiduciary Tax
**Automation Potential:** High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every non-grantor trust files Form 1041 with a K-1 for each beneficiary, by April 15 for calendar-year trusts or within the 5½-month extension; grantor trusts need tax information letters or the optional filing methods; state fiduciary returns follow. The work is the same shape thousands of times: compute distributable net income from the ledger, allocate income and deductions to beneficiaries, apply the instrument and state law on capital gains, decide elections, and absorb corrected tax information from the holdings. Competitors are the tax software vendors' fiduciary modules (CCH and Thomson Reuters), the accounting firms that prepare returns for smaller departments, the trust accounting vendors' tax interfaces, and in-house units. The research content that tells preparers what the rules are is profiled under estate planning as [[niches/estate-planning/estate-tax-research-publishers/profile|🔍 Estate & Trust Tax Research Publishers]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Ledger exports into fiduciary tax software; spreadsheets for tie-outs, elections and correction tracking. The gaps: no forecast of which holdings will issue corrections, no automatic identification of every trust a correction touches, and amended K-1s explained by phone.
