# Default & Bondholder Direction Administration

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/trust-companies|Trust Companies]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to act in a default only on documented holder direction, with indemnity, notices and timing the indenture and the court will uphold, and whoever runs default administration without taking on exposure it did not price takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$80M a year in default administration tooling, information-agent and data services bought by trustees (estimate), on 5,203 defaulted issues with ~$310B outstanding at insured trustees at the end of 2025
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of corporate trust tooling spend (estimate), and a much larger share of corporate trust liability
**Digital Adoption:** Low — direction letters, indemnity negotiations and notices run through email and outside counsel
**Target Buyer:** Head of Default Administration / Corporate Trust Risk
**Automation Potential:** Medium for notices and tracking, low for the decision to act

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
After an event of default, a trustee's duties stop being ministerial: it must act as a prudent person would, yet it can usually act only when holders of a stated percentage direct it and offer indemnity satisfactory to it. The work is reading the indenture, identifying who the holders actually are behind the depository, sending notices on the document's timetable, weighing conflicting directions, filing proofs of claim in bankruptcy, and documenting the authority for every step. Competitors are the default groups of the large trustees, successor-trustee specialists that take resignations in distress, and the outside counsel and information agents they hire (the agents are logged below as a Pass 2 pocket).

## Current Tools & Gaps
Matter files with counsel, depository notice channels, spreadsheets of holder positions and directions. The gaps: holder positions that cannot be verified on the day a direction matters, no single ledger of directions, indemnities and actions per issue, and notice deadlines tracked by individuals.
