# Holder Positions Nobody Can Verify on the Day of the Vote

**Niche:** [[niches/trust-companies/default-and-bondholder-direction/profile|Default & Bondholder Direction Administration]]
**Industry:** [[industries/trust-companies|Trust Companies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Bonds are held through the depository and its participants, so a trustee receiving a direction letter often cannot confirm that the signers hold the percentage they claim.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #compliance #evaluation-metrics #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to act in a default only on documented holder direction, with indemnity, notices and timing the indenture and the court will uphold, and whoever runs default administration without taking on exposure it did not price takes the account.

## The Problem
Directions arrive from funds claiming positions; positions change daily; groups overlap; and the trustee relies on self-certification plus participant position reports obtained at a point in time. Acting on an invalid direction, or refusing a valid one, is the trustee's liability.

## Why It's Still Broken
Beneficial ownership sits below the depository and its participants, and the tools to identify holders are sold to issuers and their information agents rather than to trustees.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A standard verification workflow per direction: record the claimed positions, request participant-level confirmation or medallion-backed certifications, reconcile against the latest position reports and prior directions, and store the verification with the direction so its validity is documented on the date the trustee acted.

## Who Feels the Pain
Default administrators facing competing holder groups; trustee counsel defending actions; holders delayed while validity is argued.

## Impact If Fixed
Validity decided in hours with a documented basis, removing a common ground for litigation against the trustee.
