# A Direction and Indemnity Ledger for Every Defaulted Issue

## The Problem
A defaulted issue may draw competing direction letters from holder groups, requests to accelerate or to forbear, indemnity offers of varying quality, and a bankruptcy timetable. The trustee must know at every moment which directions are valid under the indenture's thresholds, what it has agreed to do, and what notices are due. Lawsuits against trustees turn on exactly this record.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Default volumes are cyclical and each matter is handled with outside counsel, so trustees never invested in tooling; legal teams keep matter files, not structured ledgers.

## What to Build
A per-issue ledger that ingests direction letters, holder certifications, indemnity agreements and counsel advice; checks each direction against the indenture's thresholds and verified positions; tracks notice deadlines from the obligations map; drafts notices to holders; and logs every trustee action with the authority relied on. A portfolio view shows defaulted issues by stage and open exposure.

## Target Customer
Default administration heads and corporate trust risk officers at large trustees and successor-trustee specialists.

## Impact If Built
Every action defensible on its face, fewer missed notices, and faster decisions on directions because validity is checked in minutes rather than by a round of counsel emails.
