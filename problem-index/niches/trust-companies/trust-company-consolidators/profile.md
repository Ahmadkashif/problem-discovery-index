# Trust Company Consolidator Corporate Development

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/trust-companies|Trust Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Acquisitive trust companies, bank wealth divisions and private-equity-backed wealth platforms buy independent trust companies and the trust books of banks exiting the business, then convert the accounts onto their own platform.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~2-8 people per acquirer in corporate development and integration (estimate)
**Output:** Target screens, valuations of trust books, integration plans
**Proprietary data:** Their own deal history and retention after past conversions
**Clock:** Deal timetables and regulatory approvals for charter changes
**Buyer:** Head of corporate development
