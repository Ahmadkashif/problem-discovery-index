# Securities Lending Data & Benchmark Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/trust-companies|Trust Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Custody banks run large securities lending programmes as agent lenders for their clients' portfolios. Data vendors — DataLend within the EquiLend group and S&P Global's securities finance data business — collect loan-level transaction data from agent lenders, prime brokers and beneficial owners and sell back rates, utilisation, benchmarks and programme performance reports. DataLend runs a give-to-get model: every client contributes its transaction data and receives the anonymised aggregate.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-100 data analysts and researchers per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Daily lending rates and utilisation, performance benchmarks, client performance reports for agent lenders and beneficial owners
**Proprietary data:** Contributed securities finance transactions across most of the market; DataLend's give-to-get terms permit aggregate use, while S&P Global's contributor terms have not been checked
**Clock:** Daily publication (self-set) and beneficial owners' programme reviews; SEC Rule 10c-1a moves loan reporting to FINRA from September 28, 2028 and public dissemination from March 29, 2029, a market-structure event that will make part of the product public rather than a deliverable deadline
**Buyer:** Head of Securities Finance Data / product head
