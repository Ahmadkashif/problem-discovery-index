# Custody Search & Fee Benchmarking Consultants

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/trust-companies|Trust Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Pension funds, endowments and insurers changing custodian hire investment consultants' custody search practices or specialist advisers to run the RFP, compare service models and benchmark the fee proposals, then to review the incumbent's fees and service every few years.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under ~10 dedicated custody specialists per firm (estimate), inside larger consulting practices
**Output:** Custodian search reports, fee benchmarks, service reviews
**Proprietary data:** Fee proposals and service terms gathered across past searches, supplied by custodians for each client's search
**Clock:** The client's own search timetable and contract renewal dates
**Buyer:** Head of custody search / consulting practice leader
