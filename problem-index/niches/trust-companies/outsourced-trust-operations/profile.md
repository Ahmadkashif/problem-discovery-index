# Outsourced Trust Operations Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/trust-companies|Trust Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Platform vendors and back-office specialists — SEI and FIS among them — run trust accounting, income collection, corporate actions, statements and sometimes tax preparation for banks and trust companies that keep the client relationship and fiduciary decisions but not the processing.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Hundreds of operations staff across providers; analytics on top of the processing under ~10 per provider (estimate)
**Output:** Processed transactions, statements, reconciliations, operational reporting
**Proprietary data:** Operational records for many client institutions, held under processing agreements
**Clock:** Daily processing cycles and clients' statement and tax dates
**Buyer:** Head of outsourcing services
