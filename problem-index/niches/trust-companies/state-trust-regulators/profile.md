# State Trust Company Regulators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/trust-companies|Trust Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State banking departments charter and examine non-depository trust companies — South Dakota's Division of Banking (115 chartered trust companies holding nearly $815B in 2024, 41 examined that year), Nevada's Financial Institutions Division, and the banking regulators of New Hampshire, Wyoming and Delaware among them — rating each company and approving new charters.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Trust examination staffs in the tens at most per state (estimate), with no separate research unit
**Output:** Examination reports and ratings, charter decisions, supervisory guidance
**Proprietary data:** Confidential supervisory information on each chartered company
**Clock:** Statutory examination cycles and application timelines
**Buyer:** Director of banking / chief trust examiner
