# Bank Compliance Software Extended to Fiduciary Rules

**Niche:** [[niches/trust-companies/community-bank-trust-departments/profile|Community Bank Trust Departments]]
**Industry:** [[industries/trust-companies|Trust Companies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Community banks already license compliance management software for vendor management, policies and audits; it knows Regulation Z and BSA, not fiduciary account reviews and trust committees.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #compliance #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give a trust department of three to ten people the account review, fiduciary audit and tax capacity examiners expect of a large trust company, and whoever makes a sub-$1B department pass its examination without adding staff takes the account.

## The Problem
The bank's compliance officer runs a compliance platform; the trust department runs on spreadsheets beside it, so the board sees two incompatible pictures of risk.

## What Already Exists
Bank compliance and risk management platforms such as Ncontracts and similar tools, with policy libraries, audit tracking, vendor management and exam management modules built around lending, deposit and BSA rules.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: a fiduciary rule library (the OCC's Part 9 for national banks and the state equivalents), account-level review and acceptance workflows fed from the trust accounting system, trust committee packet and minute templates, fiduciary audit programmes, conflict-of-interest and self-dealing checks for fiduciary investments, and exam request lists for trust examinations.

## Target Customer
Community banks with trust departments that already license a compliance platform for the rest of the bank.

## Impact If Solved
One compliance record for the whole bank, with the trust department's obligations inside it, and a board report that finally includes fiduciary risk.
