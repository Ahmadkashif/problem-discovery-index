# An Examination-Ready Trust Department in a Box

**Niche:** [[niches/trust-companies/community-bank-trust-departments/profile|Community Bank Trust Departments]]
**Industry:** [[industries/trust-companies|Trust Companies]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A five-person trust department must produce the same reviews, committee records and audit evidence as a large trust company, and assembles them by hand before every examination.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #gradient-boosting #evaluation-metrics #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give a trust department of three to ten people the account review, fiduciary audit and tax capacity examiners expect of a large trust company, and whoever makes a sub-$1B department pass its examination without adding staff takes the account.

## The Problem
Examiners rate the trust department on management, operations, compliance and asset management. A small department's evidence is scattered: reviews in spreadsheets, acceptance memos in folders, committee minutes typed after the meeting, audit findings in a binder. Preparing for an exam takes weeks out of a team that has no slack.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Trust platforms sell processing, not governance. Bank compliance vendors target lending and deposit rules, where most banks have obligations; the trust department is a minority use case inside a small customer.

## What to Build
A governance layer sitting on the department's trust accounting feed: account acceptance and annual review workflows with exception ranking, drafted review memos, committee packets and minutes generated from the decisions recorded, a fiduciary audit evidence library mapped to the examination's components, and an examination request responder that pulls the evidence automatically. Built to be operated by the existing staff, not a new hire.

## Target Customer
Heads of trust departments at banks with under $1B of fiduciary assets, and the bank CEOs deciding whether to keep the business.

## Impact If Built
Exam preparation shrinks from weeks to days (estimate), the department's record no longer depends on one officer's memory, and a bank can keep a profitable trust line instead of selling it when that officer retires.
