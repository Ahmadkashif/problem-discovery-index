# The Trust Committee Minutes Written From Memory

**Niche:** [[niches/trust-companies/community-bank-trust-departments/profile|Community Bank Trust Departments]]
**Industry:** [[industries/trust-companies|Trust Companies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A small department's trust committee approves acceptances, reviews and discretionary decisions in a monthly meeting, and the minutes that examiners read are written afterwards from notes.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #compliance #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give a trust department of three to ten people the account review, fiduciary audit and tax capacity examiners expect of a large trust company, and whoever makes a sub-$1B department pass its examination without adding staff takes the account.

## The Problem
Examiners expect the committee's minutes to show what was reviewed, what was decided and why. In a small department the senior officer prepares the packet, presents it, and writes the minutes later; details are lost and decisions are recorded without reasons.

## Why It's Still Broken
There is no staff to take minutes, and the packet and minutes live in separate documents with nothing connecting them to the account records.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Build the packet from the accounts and decisions in the queue, record each decision against its item during the meeting, and generate draft minutes with the reasons attached for the chair's approval the same day, filed against each account.

## Who Feels the Pain
Senior trust officers doing the work of a committee secretary; directors signing minutes they cannot verify; examiners writing findings about documentation.

## Impact If Fixed
Accurate minutes the same day, an account-level decision record, and one of the most common documentation findings removed.
