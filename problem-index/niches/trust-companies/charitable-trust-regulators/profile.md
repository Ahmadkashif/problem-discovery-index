# State Charitable Trust Regulators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/trust-companies|Trust Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State attorneys general supervise charitable trusts, including charitable remainder and lead trusts and private foundations administered by trust companies: they collect registrations and annual reports, review trustee conduct, and bring enforcement actions when charitable assets are misused.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Attorneys, auditors and analysts in the tens at the largest offices, with any analytics layer under ~10 (estimate)
**Output:** Registration reviews, investigations, enforcement actions and settlements
**Proprietary data:** Registration filings and annual reports, much of it public, plus confidential investigation files
**Clock:** Filing deadlines the office sets for registrants; enforcement timelines set by courts
**Buyer:** Chief of the charities unit
