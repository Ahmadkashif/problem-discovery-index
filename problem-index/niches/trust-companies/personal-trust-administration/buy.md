# Wealth CRM Pointed at the Trust Relationship

**Niche:** [[niches/trust-companies/personal-trust-administration/profile|Personal Trust Administration]]
**Industry:** [[industries/trust-companies|Trust Companies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Wealth CRMs already hold households, contacts and notes; they model a client who owns the money, not a trust with a grantor, a trustee, current beneficiaries and remaindermen whose interests conflict.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #compliance #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make each discretionary decision on an old instrument consistent, documented and defensible at a caseload no officer can hold in memory, and whoever makes the senior officer's judgement repeatable and reviewable takes the account.

## The Problem
Trust officers keep family context in CRM notes, email and memory. The CRM shows a household and a list of calls. It cannot show who is entitled to what under which standard, which beneficiary is in conflict with which, or what the committee decided last time and why.

## What Already Exists
Wealth CRMs such as Salesforce Financial Services Cloud and the CRM modules bundled with trust accounting platforms, with household views, activity logging and task management.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: a trust object with roles (grantor, trustees, protector, directed advisers, current and remainder beneficiaries) rather than a household; the governing standards from the abstracted instrument shown on every beneficiary record; request intake as a case linked to the instrument and the distribution history; a committee workflow with packets, decisions and reasons; language-model drafting of memos and beneficiary replies grounded in the instrument; and a consistency view across officers. It also needs the restriction model fiduciary files require, so remainder beneficiaries never see current beneficiaries' requests.

## Target Customer
Heads of personal trust at institutions already licensing a wealth CRM who want the decision workflow inside it rather than in another system.

## Impact If Solved
One place to see a family's entitlements, history and decisions cuts preparation for each call and memo by ~15–30 minutes (estimate) and makes every decision traceable without new software for officers to learn.
