# Bondholder Information, Tabulation & Restructuring Agents

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/trust-companies|Trust Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
When an issuer seeks bondholder consents, runs an exchange offer or solicits votes on a Chapter 11 plan, it hires an information, tabulation or claims agent — D.F. King, Kroll's issuer and restructuring administration business, Epiq and Stretto are examples — to identify holders behind the depository, distribute documents, tabulate responses and maintain claims registers. Corporate trustees in default rely on the same agents to reach holders and verify positions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-30 holder-identification researchers at the largest firms (estimate), who trace beneficial holders behind depository participants and intermediaries; tabulation, noticing and call-centre staff are production and not counted
**Output:** Holder identification reports, tabulations and voting results, claims registers
**Proprietary data:** Little of their own: holder positions come from depository participant reports and intermediaries, and engagement records belong to the issuer or debtor
**Clock:** Consent expiration dates and court-set voting and claims bar dates — hard and third-party
**Buyer:** Managing director, issuer or restructuring services
