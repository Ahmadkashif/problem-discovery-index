# Print Colour Management Standards & Certification

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/printing-shops|Printing Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Define the process control specifications printers calibrate to, and certify shops and experts against them — the framework that is supposed to make a file print the same way in two different plants.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 standards staff, technical committee members, trainers, and auditors
**Output:** Process control specifications, certification programmes, training curricula, characterisation datasets
**Proprietary data:** Certification audit measurements across certified facilities
**Clock:** Certification renewal and specification revision cycles
**Buyer:** Executive Director / Chief Technology Officer
