# Ink & Consumables Technical Service

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/printing-shops|Printing Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Formulate inks, coatings and pressroom chemistry, and troubleshoot runnability and colour problems on customers' presses — technical work given away to hold the consumables account.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-400 chemists, technical service representatives, and colour matchers
**Output:** Formulations and colour matches, press-side troubleshooting, substrate compatibility guidance
**Proprietary data:** Formulation libraries and field problem histories tied to substrate, press and condition
**Clock:** Customer production schedules
**Buyer:** Director of Technical Service
