# Print Estimating Service Bureaus

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/printing-shops|Printing Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Produce estimates for printing companies without an in-house estimator, working from specifications on a per-quote fee.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 3-15 estimators per firm
**Output:** Job estimates and quotations
**Proprietary data:** Quote and award histories, rarely retained
**Clock:** Quote turnaround expectations
**Buyer:** Owner
