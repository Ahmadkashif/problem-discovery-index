# Print Procurement & Managed Print Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/printing-shops|Printing Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Buy print on behalf of large enterprises, running competitive sourcing across supplier bases, benchmarking prices and consolidating spend — sitting between the buyer and the printer and taking a margin on the difference.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 sourcing analysts, estimators, and category managers
**Output:** Price benchmarking, supplier selection, specification optimisation, spend analytics, print sourcing programmes
**Proprietary data:** Quoted and awarded prices across many suppliers and specifications — the only comparative view of print pricing that exists
**Clock:** Sourcing event and contract cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Sourcing Analytics
