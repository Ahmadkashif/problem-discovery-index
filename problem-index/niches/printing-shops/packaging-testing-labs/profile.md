# Packaging Structural Testing & Design Laboratories

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/printing-shops|Printing Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Test packaging for distribution performance — drop, compression, vibration, climate — and design structures that survive the supply chain, producing the test reports that qualify a pack for shipment and for retailer acceptance.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 packaging engineers, test technicians, and design staff across the laboratories
**Output:** Distribution test reports, structural designs and dielines, material specifications, failure analysis, retailer compliance certification
**Proprietary data:** Test results across pack designs, materials and distribution modes, occasionally joined to real damage rates in the field
**Clock:** Product launch schedules and retailer qualification deadlines
**Buyer:** Director of Packaging Engineering
