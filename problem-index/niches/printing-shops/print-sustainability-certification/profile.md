# Print & Paper Sustainability Certification

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/printing-shops|Printing Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Certify chain of custody for paper fibre and sustainable operating practice for printing facilities, and audit the claims brands make about the environmental provenance of printed material.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 standards staff, auditors, and certification reviewers
**Output:** Chain of custody certification, facility sustainability certification, audit findings, claim substantiation guidance
**Proprietary data:** Audit findings and non-conformance histories across certified operations
**Clock:** Annual audit and certification renewal cycles
**Buyer:** Director of Certification
