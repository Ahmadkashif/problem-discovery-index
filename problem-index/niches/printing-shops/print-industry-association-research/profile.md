# Printing Industry Association Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/printing-shops|Printing Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Produce the industry's financial ratio studies, wage and benefit surveys, and capacity and technology trend reports for member printing companies.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-30 economists, researchers, and analysts
**Output:** Financial ratio and operating benchmark studies, wage surveys, technology adoption research, economic forecasts
**Proprietary data:** Member financial submissions collected annually over decades, at declining participation
**Clock:** Annual survey and publication cycle
**Buyer:** Chief Economist / VP of Research
