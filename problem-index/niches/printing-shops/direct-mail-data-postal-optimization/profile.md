# Direct Mail Data & Postal Optimization Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/printing-shops|Printing Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Clean, standardise and update the address lists direct mail is printed against, apply change-of-address processing, and engineer the presort and commingling that determines what postage a mailing costs. Postage typically exceeds the cost of printing, so this layer decides the economics of the job.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 data processing staff, postal analysts, list specialists, and software engineers
**Output:** Address standardisation and validation, move update processing, deliverability scoring, presort and commingle optimisation, postal qualification documentation
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated deliverability outcomes and address histories beyond the licensed postal files, plus commingling pool behaviour across many mailers
**Clock:** Postal rate changes, statutory move update requirements, and mail induction deadlines
**Buyer:** VP of Data Services / Chief Postal Officer
