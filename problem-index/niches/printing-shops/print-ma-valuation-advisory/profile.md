# Printing Industry M&A & Valuation Advisory

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/printing-shops|Printing Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Advise on the sale and consolidation of printing companies in a long-consolidating industry, valuing businesses whose equipment is depreciating faster than their customer relationships.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 3-12 bankers and analysts per boutique
**Output:** Valuation opinions, transaction advisory, equipment and customer base diligence
**Proprietary data:** Private transaction comparables
**Clock:** Transaction timelines
**Buyer:** Managing Director
