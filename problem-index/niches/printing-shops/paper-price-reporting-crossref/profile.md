# Pulp & Paper Price Reporting Agencies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/printing-shops|Printing Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Publish the assessed prices for pulp, paper grades and packaging board that supply contracts across the printing and packaging industry are settled against, built from confidential transaction reporting and trader interviews rather than an exchange.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 price reporters, editors, market analysts, and economists
**Output:** Daily and weekly price assessments, indices contracts reference, supply-demand analysis, capacity and cost curve research
**Proprietary data:** Confidential transaction submissions and market intelligence accumulated over decades, plus the methodology and reporter judgment that turns inconsistent submissions into a published number
**Clock:** Fixed publication windows against which real contracts settle
**Buyer:** Editorial Director / Head of Price Reporting
