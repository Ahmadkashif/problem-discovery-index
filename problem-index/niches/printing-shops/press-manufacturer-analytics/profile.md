# Press Manufacturer Performance Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/printing-shops|Printing Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The performance and reliability analytics groups inside the press manufacturers, who instrument installed presses, model uptime and consumable usage, and run the service programmes shops buy alongside the equipment.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 engineers, data scientists, and service analytics staff
**Output:** Press performance benchmarking, predictive maintenance, makeready and waste analytics, consumable optimisation, service programme design
**Proprietary data:** Continuous telemetry from thousands of installed presses — makeready duration, waste sheets, colour convergence, stops and faults — joined to service history
**Clock:** Service contract cycles and product programmes
**Buyer:** VP of Digital Services
