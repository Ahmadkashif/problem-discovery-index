# Colour Standard & Specification Licensing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/printing-shops|Printing Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Own and license the colour reference systems brands specify in and printers match to, and sell the measurement instruments and software that verify a match. Pass 1 names matching a swatch on press as the highest-skill task on the press floor; this layer defines what the swatch means.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 colour scientists, standards staff, software engineers, and technical support
**Output:** Colour reference systems and libraries, spectral definitions, colour management software, instrument calibration standards, brand colour specifications
**Proprietary data:** The reference system itself, plus spectral measurement data and the accumulated mapping between named colours and achievable output across substrates and processes
**Clock:** Product and library release cycles; nothing external requires the work on a date
**Buyer:** Chief Technology Officer / VP of Colour Science
