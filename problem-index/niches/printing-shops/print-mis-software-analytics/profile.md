# Print MIS & Estimating Software Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/printing-shops|Printing Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The analytics functions inside the print management information systems, which hold estimates, actual job costs, scheduling and shop floor data for thousands of printing businesses.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 analysts, product managers, and implementation consultants
**Output:** Estimating standards libraries, scheduling optimisation, job costing analysis, benchmark reporting
**Proprietary data:** Estimated versus actual job costs across thousands of shops — the direct evidence of where estimating goes wrong
**Clock:** Continuous job flow
**Buyer:** VP of Product
