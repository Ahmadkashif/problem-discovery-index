# Environmental Permitting & NEPA Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/environmental-consultants|Environmental Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Specialist practices produce the environmental impact statements, wetland delineations, protected species surveys, and permit applications that infrastructure and development projects cannot proceed without. The document is the deliverable, it is reviewed by agencies and litigated by opponents, and a project waits on it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-500 biologists, planners, and regulatory specialists
**Output:** Impact statements, delineations and surveys, permit applications, agency response packages
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated survey findings and the record of which mitigation approaches agencies accepted in each region
**Clock:** Permit review timelines and project schedules that stall without approval
**Buyer:** Practice leader / Director of Permitting
