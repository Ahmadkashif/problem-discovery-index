# Environmental Records Database Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/environmental-consultants|Environmental Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
EDR, ERIS, and Banks Environmental assemble, geocode, and maintain every federal and state environmental regulatory record — spill reports, underground tanks, hazardous waste generators, landfills, enforcement actions — alongside historical fire insurance maps, city directories, topographic sheets, and aerial photography going back a century. Every Phase I Environmental Site Assessment in the country buys a radius report from them, because the Pass 1 analysis is exactly right that searching fifteen-plus databases manually is the alternative.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-800 data researchers, geocoders, historical records specialists, and analysts
**Output:** Radius search reports, historical property records, risk screening — the report is the product and it is purchased per site
**Proprietary data:** Decades of assembled, geocoded, and quality-controlled regulatory records plus a historical imagery and directory archive that cannot be reconstructed — the source agencies publish inconsistently, retroactively, and without geocoding
**Clock:** The due diligence standard requires records current within a defined window, and the report gates a property closing
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Content Operations
