# Environmental Data Management Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/environmental-consultants|Environmental Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Locus, EarthSoft, and comparable vendors ingest laboratory electronic data deliverables, normalize them across labs and methods, and compare results against the regulatory screening levels that vary by state, medium, and land use — automating precisely the manual comparison the Pass 1 analysis identifies as a chronic burden.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 environmental scientists, data engineers, and regulatory content staff
**Output:** Normalized analytical datasets, screening level comparisons, compliance reporting
**Proprietary data:** A maintained library of regulatory screening levels across states, media, and land uses, plus lab deliverable format handling accumulated across hundreds of laboratories
**Clock:** Regulatory reporting deadlines and permit monitoring schedules
**Buyer:** VP of Product / Director of Regulatory Content
