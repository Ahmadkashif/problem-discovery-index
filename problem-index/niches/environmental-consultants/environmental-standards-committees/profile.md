# Environmental Due Diligence Standards Committees

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/environmental-consultants|Environmental Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The ASTM committee that maintains the Phase I standard defines what a due diligence investigation must include for a purchaser to claim liability protection. The standard governs the industry's primary revenue product, and the committee is staffed by volunteers with secretariat support.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 2-8 staff, with volunteer technical committees
**Output:** The due diligence standard and its revisions
**Proprietary data:** None held by the body — the practice knowledge sits with the consultants who volunteer
**Clock:** Standard revision ballots and federal rule recognition
**Buyer:** Standards staff manager
