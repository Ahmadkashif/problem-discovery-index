# Environmental Insurance Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/environmental-consultants|Environmental Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Pollution legal liability and cost cap underwriters assess contaminated site risk, price coverage, and analyze the claim experience that reveals which site conditions and remedial approaches actually produce cost overruns.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 underwriters, environmental engineers, and claims analysts
**Output:** Underwriting assessments, coverage terms, claim and cost overrun analyses
**Proprietary data:** Remediation cost outcomes against original estimates across insured sites — the only dataset that grades environmental cost estimates against reality
**Clock:** Transaction timelines and policy renewal cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
