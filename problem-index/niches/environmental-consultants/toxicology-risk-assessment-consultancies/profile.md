# Toxicology & Risk Assessment Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/environmental-consultants|Environmental Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
ToxStrategies, Gradient, and the health sciences practices produce the human health and ecological risk assessments that determine how clean a site must be, and the expert opinions on causation in contamination and product liability disputes. The written assessment sets remediation obligations worth tens of millions and is contested by regulators and opposing experts.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-200 toxicologists, epidemiologists, and risk assessors
**Output:** Risk assessments, exposure analyses, causation opinions, expert testimony
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated assessment methodology and the record of which approaches regulators and courts accepted
**Clock:** Regulatory submission deadlines and litigation schedules
**Buyer:** Practice leader / Principal scientist
