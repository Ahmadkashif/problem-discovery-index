# Federal Toxicity Assessment Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/environmental-consultants|Environmental Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The federal integrated risk information programme and its counterparts develop the toxicity values — reference doses and cancer slope factors — that every human health risk assessment in the country uses. A single value change can alter cleanup obligations at thousands of sites.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-200 toxicologists, risk assessors, and reviewers
**Output:** Toxicity assessments, hazard characterizations, published values
**Proprietary data:** The assessment development record and systematic review evidence base
**Clock:** Assessment development schedules and interagency review
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer
