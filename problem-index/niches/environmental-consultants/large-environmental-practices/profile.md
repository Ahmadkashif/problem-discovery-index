# Large Environmental Consulting Practices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/environmental-consultants|Environmental Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The environmental practices at Tetra Tech, Arcadis, Jacobs, and AECOM produce site assessments, remedial investigations, feasibility studies, and compliance reports at national scale. The written report is unambiguously the invoice, it is submitted to regulators, and it is produced against statutory deadlines — the shape this sweep looks for, at thousands of people.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 2,000-15,000 scientists, engineers, and analysts per large practice
**Output:** Site assessments, remedial investigation reports, feasibility studies, compliance submissions
**Proprietary data:** Site characterization and remediation performance across thousands of sites — owned by clients under contracts written for liability management, with reuse restricted
**Clock:** Regulatory submission deadlines, agency review cycles, and transaction closings
**Buyer:** Practice leader / Chief Technical Officer
