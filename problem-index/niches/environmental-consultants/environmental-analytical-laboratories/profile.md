# Environmental Analytical Laboratories

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/environmental-consultants|Environmental Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Pace, Eurofins Environment, and ALS analyze the soil, water, and air samples consultants collect, running accredited methods against regulatory detection limits and issuing the data packages that every site decision rests on. The Pass 1 analysis notes those results arrive in inconsistent formats and must be manually compared against screening levels — the labs produce the first half of that problem.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-3,000 chemists, technicians, and quality staff per major network
**Output:** Analytical data packages, quality control narratives, method development
**Proprietary data:** Method performance and quality control history across millions of samples; the analytical results themselves belong to the client
**Clock:** Sample holding times, which are absolute — a sample not analyzed within the method window is invalid and cannot be recollected
**Buyer:** VP of Technical Services / Laboratory Director
