# Environmental Liability Transfer Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/environmental-consultants|Environmental Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Firms that acquire contaminated properties and assume the cleanup obligation price the liability, execute the remediation, and monetize the redeveloped site. Their diligence teams estimate remediation cost and duration with the firm's own capital at stake.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 environmental engineers, geologists, and analysts
**Output:** Liability valuations, remediation cost models, closure strategy
**Proprietary data:** Estimated-versus-actual remediation cost and duration across an owned portfolio
**Clock:** Transaction timelines and regulatory milestones
**Buyer:** Chief Investment Officer / VP Technical
