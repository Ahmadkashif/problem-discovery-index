# Creator Risk & Brand Suitability

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/influencer-marketing-platforms|Influencer Marketing Platforms]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell a brand whether this specific creator is a risk for this specific brand — and whoever answers that replaces a generic authenticity score with a judgement somebody can act on.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.2B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of category spend
**Digital Adoption:** Low — a score and a manual scroll
**Target Buyer:** Brand safety, legal and partnership teams
**Automation Potential:** High — retrospective review is automatable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every platform has a searchable creator database and an authenticity score, and none of them can tell a brand whether this specific creator is a risk for this specific brand. Risk in this category is not a property of a creator in the abstract — a creator who is perfectly suitable for one brand is disqualifying for another, depending on category, values, regulatory exposure and what the brand has publicly committed to. The contest is over producing a brand-specific judgement from a creator's entire public history, which is a different problem from measuring whether their followers are real.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Generic authenticity and brand safety scores, keyword scans of recent posts, manual review of a creator's feed, and social listening. The gaps: scores that are universal rather than brand-specific; review limited to recent and text content; no coverage of video, audio or comments; no monitoring after selection; and no record of what was checked when a problem later emerges.
