# The Creator

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/influencer-marketing-platforms|Influencer Marketing Platforms]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give a creator the market data and the settlement terms a supplier in any other industry would take for granted — and whoever does that changes the balance of every negotiation in the category.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.0B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category spend
**Digital Adoption:** Low — negotiation by instinct, payment by chasing
**Target Buyer:** Creators and creator business vendors
**Automation Potential:** High — benchmarks and settlement are both mechanical

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A creator quoting for a brand deal has no market data, no idea what the same brand paid someone comparable last month, and will wait three months to be paid whatever they agreed. They are the supply side of a ten-billion-dollar market and are the only participant with no pricing information and no payment terms worth the name. This is distinct from the manager's problem: that is about coordination load, this is about a party to a commercial negotiation operating without any of the information the other side has.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Rate guidance from peers and community spreadsheets, invoicing by email, ninety-day payment terms, and platform dashboards showing campaign deliverables. The gaps: no rate benchmarks by tier, category or deliverable; no visibility of what comparable creators were paid; no leverage from their own performance history; payment terms set by the brand's finance function; and no recourse on late payment.
