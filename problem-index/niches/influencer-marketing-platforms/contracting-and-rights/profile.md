# Contracting & Rights

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/influencer-marketing-platforms|Influencer Marketing Platforms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn hundreds of bespoke partnership agreements into terms a system can read and enforce — and whoever does that stops brands from running assets they no longer have the right to use.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.1B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~11% of category spend
**Digital Adoption:** Low — prose agreements and calendar reminders
**Target Buyer:** Brand legal and partnership operations
**Automation Potential:** High — the terms are enumerable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A partnership agreement contains deliverables, timings, usage rights with a defined term and territory, exclusivity windows, whitelisting and boosting permissions, approval rights and payment terms. All of it is prose in a document. Nothing in the brand's advertising stack, asset library or media buying tools knows what any of it says, which means rights expire while assets keep running and exclusivity is breached by a different team. This is a structured-obligation contest with real legal consequences, and it is separable from the creative collaboration next door.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Contract templates, electronic signature, document storage in the platform, and spreadsheet trackers with expiry dates. The gaps: terms unreadable by any downstream system; no enforcement at the point of use; expiry tracked by a reminder if at all; exclusivity invisible across teams and campaigns; and renewal handled as a new negotiation.
