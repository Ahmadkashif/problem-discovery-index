# Content Review & Approval

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/influencer-marketing-platforms|Influencer Marketing Platforms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make a hundred simultaneous creative conversations with people outside the company tractable — and whoever does that decides how many partnerships one team can actually run.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$900M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of category spend
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — approval modules that nobody uses
**Target Buyer:** Brand partnership and creative leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — routing, chasing and versioning are mechanical

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A hundred creators are drafting content simultaneously. Each draft needs a brief the creator will actually read, a review by people who are busy, feedback that is specific enough to act on, one or more revision rounds, a final approval, and a go-live on a date. The conversations happen in direct messages and email because that is where creators are, and the approval module in the platform is used to record a decision that was already made elsewhere. This is a multi-party creative workflow contest with no legal component, which makes it a different build and a different buyer from the rights fight next door.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Approval modules with comment threads, brief templates, direct messages and email in practice, and shared drives for assets. The gaps: briefs that are documents rather than usable guidance; feedback consolidated by the manager by hand; no version history when review happens off-platform; no visibility of where each of a hundred drafts stands; and chasing done by message.
