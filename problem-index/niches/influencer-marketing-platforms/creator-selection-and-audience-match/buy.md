# Audience Overlap Practice

**Niche:** [[niches/influencer-marketing-platforms/creator-selection-and-audience-match/profile|Creator Selection & Audience Match]]
**Industry:** [[industries/influencer-marketing-platforms|Influencer Marketing Platforms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Media planning has measured audience duplication and incremental reach for fifty years, and influencer selection counts followers.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #matrix-decompositions #confidence-intervals #evaluation-metrics #hypothesis-testing #dimensionality-reduction #revenue-impact #bayesian-inference
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to predict whether a creator's audience contains buyers this brand does not already have — and whoever answers that replaces follower count as the currency of the category.

## The Problem
Media planning solved the question of whether a second buy adds anyone new. Reach and frequency modelling, audience duplication analysis, incremental reach curves and optimal schedule construction have been standard practice since television planning, refined continuously, and implemented in planning tools every large advertiser uses. Influencer selection asks the same question — will these forty creators reach different people, and are any of them people the brand does not already have — and answers it with a sum of follower counts.

## What Already Exists
Reach and frequency modelling with duplication; incremental reach curves; audience overlap estimation from panels and probabilistic methods; optimal schedule construction under budget; and cross-media reach reconciliation.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is from measured panels to inferred creator audiences. It requires: (1) audience composition estimated rather than measured, since there is no panel and the follower base must be characterised from sparse public signals — the entire planning apparatus assumes a measured audience and here it must be inferred, which is the central adaptation; (2) overlap between creators estimated without identity, which is a statistical problem the planning tools solve with panel data that does not exist here; (3) overlap with the brand's own customer base as the key output, which media planning rarely computes and which is the most valuable single number in this category; (4) very small schedules of tens of placements, where the continuous reach curves of media planning are the wrong approximation and discrete portfolio selection is the right one; and (5) outcome validation through sales rather than through reach, since reach was always the deliverable in planning and here it is a proxy for something measurable.

## Target Customer
Brand partnership and media planning teams, influencer platforms, and media planning vendors for whom creator audiences are unmodelled.

## Impact If Solved
Fifty years of duplication and incremental reach practice assumes a measured panel, and creator audiences must be inferred instead. Overlap with the brand's own customer base is the output planning rarely computes and the most valuable number here.
