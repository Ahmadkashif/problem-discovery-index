# Creator Selection & Audience Match

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/influencer-marketing-platforms|Influencer Marketing Platforms]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to predict whether a creator's audience contains buyers this brand does not already have — and whoever answers that replaces follower count as the currency of the category.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.8B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~28% of category spend
**Digital Adoption:** Low — search filters over gameable proxies
**Target Buyer:** Brand partnership and performance leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — a well-posed prediction with a corpus behind it

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The decision that determines whether a campaign works is made on follower count and engagement rate, when the question is whether this creator's audience contains buyers the brand does not already have. Follower count is purchasable, engagement rate is inflated by pods and anti-correlated with account size in ways that make cross-tier comparison meaningless, and neither describes the match at all. This is the largest concentration of contested value in the category because selection determines the outcome, and the platforms hold a corpus of thousands of past partnerships that would answer it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Searchable creator databases, follower and engagement filters, authenticity scores, audience demographic estimates, and category tags. The gaps: metrics that describe the creator rather than the match; no measure of audience overlap with the brand's existing customers; no prediction of purchase behaviour; no use of past partnership outcomes; and tier comparisons that are not comparable.
