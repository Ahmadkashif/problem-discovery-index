# The Influencer Marketing Manager

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/influencer-marketing-platforms|Influencer Marketing Platforms]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to answer the status question without a person asking it — and whoever does that converts a week of chasing into the selection and relationship work the role was created for.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$900M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of category spend
**Digital Adoption:** Low — three messaging apps and a spreadsheet
**Target Buyer:** Brand partnership leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — status and chasing are mechanical

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
An influencer marketing manager's week is a hundred parallel conversations about whether the draft was approved, when the post goes live, and whether the brand can still run that asset. Every one of those questions has an answer that exists somewhere in a system, and every one is answered by a person reading messages. The manager is the integration layer between the creators, the brand's stakeholders, legal, finance and the platform, and their capacity is the constraint on how many partnerships a brand runs.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A platform for contracts and payments, two or three messaging apps where the work happens, email, a spreadsheet, and a calendar. The gaps: status that must be assembled by asking; chasing done manually; stakeholder updates rebuilt by hand; no prioritisation of what needs attention; and the context living in one person's message history.
