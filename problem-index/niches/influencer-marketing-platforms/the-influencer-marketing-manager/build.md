# A Hundred Parallel Conversations

**Niche:** [[niches/influencer-marketing-platforms/the-influencer-marketing-manager/profile|The Influencer Marketing Manager]]
**Industry:** [[industries/influencer-marketing-platforms|Influencer Marketing Platforms]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** An influencer marketing manager's week is a hundred parallel conversations about whether the draft was approved, when the post goes live, and whether the brand can still run that asset.
**Tags:** #worker-facing #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #automation #evaluation-metrics #descriptive-statistics #quick-win #transformers
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to answer the status question without a person asking it — and whoever does that converts a week of chasing into the selection and relationship work the role was created for.

## The Problem
The manager opens their day to forty messages. Where is my contract. Has the draft been approved. Can I post Thursday instead. The legal team wants to know if that claim was removed. The brand director wants a status update. Finance is asking about an invoice. Every one of these is a question about a state that exists somewhere — in the platform, in a thread, in a document — and every one is answered by this person going and looking. They spend the week as a human query interface, and the work they were hired for, which is choosing the right creators and building relationships with them, is what happens if there is time.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The platform holds contracts and payments and the work happens in messaging apps the platform cannot see, so the state is genuinely fragmented and no single product owns it. Creators will not use the brand's tool, which puts the conversation permanently outside. Chasing is invisible labour that never becomes a requirement. And the manager's capacity has never been framed as the constraint it is.

## What to Build
Answer the questions instead of relaying them. Aggregate state from every source — platform, messages, email, published content — into one derived status per partnership, which is the foundation and is what makes everything else possible; the state exists, it is simply scattered. Give creators a way to get answers without asking, since a large share of inbound traffic is a question the system could answer and the creator has no other route to it. Chase automatically on the brand's behalf at sensible intervals, which is mechanical and is a substantial share of the week. Generate the stakeholder update rather than having the manager assemble it, because that report is rebuilt from scratch every time it is requested. Surface what needs attention today, ranked by consequence, since a hundred partnerships cannot each be checked and the current triage is whoever messaged most recently. Draft replies to the recurring questions for the manager to send, which keeps the relationship human while removing the typing. Capture the context of every conversation against the partnership, so it is not held in one person's inbox — which is the fix note's subject. Handle the scheduling and go-live coordination, which is a calendar problem dressed as a relationship problem. Escalate only genuine exceptions, so the manager's attention goes to disputes and decisions. And measure time spent on coordination against time spent on selection and relationships, because that ratio is the state of the role.

## Target Customer
Brand partnership teams and the managers themselves, agencies running campaigns at volume, and platform vendors whose product does not see where the work happens.

## Impact If Built
The state exists in the platform, the threads and the documents, and a person walks between them all week answering questions. Deriving one status per partnership and giving creators a way to self-serve removes most of the inbound and returns the week to selection and relationships.
