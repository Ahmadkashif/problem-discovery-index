## The Problem
Modelling and executing configurable business processes is a solved discipline with decades of practice: process definition, state machines, role-based task assignment, conditional routing, service-level monitoring and exception handling. Case management systems handle exactly the pattern here — a long-running matter with variable steps, multiple parties and documents attached. Influencer platforms implement a fixed pipeline with a few toggles.

## What Already Exists
Business process and workflow engines with configurable definitions; case management for variable-path matters; role and approval routing; service-level monitoring with escalation; and exception and ad hoc task handling.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a process whose main participant is outside the organisation. It requires: (1) the creator as a full participant rather than as a record, since they must see, act and respond, and no internal workflow engine is designed for a counterparty with their own interests — this is the central difference; (2) creative content as the object flowing through the process, which needs review, annotation and versioning rather than a document attachment; (3) hundreds of parallel instances started simultaneously, which is a campaign rather than a caseload and changes the interface entirely; (4) configurability that a marketing manager can operate, since a process engine requiring an implementation consultant will not be adopted by the buyer here; and (5) obligations extending past the campaign, as usage rights and exclusivity persist long after the process completes and most workflow engines end at closure.

## Target Customer
Brand partnership teams, influencer platform vendors, and workflow and case management vendors for whom external-participant creative processes are unserved.

## Impact If Solved
Case management already handles variable-path matters with parties and documents, and influencer platforms hard-code a pipeline instead. The creator as a full participant with their own interests, and obligations that outlive the process, are what the engines do not model.
