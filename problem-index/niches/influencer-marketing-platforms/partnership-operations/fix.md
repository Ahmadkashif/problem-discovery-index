# The Campaign That Lives in a Spreadsheet Beside the Platform

**Niche:** [[niches/influencer-marketing-platforms/partnership-operations/profile|Partnership Operations]]
**Industry:** [[industries/influencer-marketing-platforms|Influencer Marketing Platforms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The platform holds the contracts and the payments, the spreadsheet holds what is actually happening, and only the spreadsheet is current.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #automation #descriptive-statistics #quick-win #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #compliance

## The Problem
Open any influencer marketing team's campaign and there is a spreadsheet. It has a row per creator and columns the platform does not have: who is chasing whom, what was verbally agreed, which draft is with legal, why this one is delayed, who said what in a direct message. The platform has the contract, the brief and the payment. The spreadsheet has the state of the world. New team members are trained on the spreadsheet. Reporting comes from the spreadsheet. The platform, which was bought to be the system of record, is a repository the team updates when they remember.

## Why It's Still Broken
The gap between what the platform models and what the work involves is filled with a spreadsheet because a spreadsheet is infinitely flexible, and every unmodelled fact goes there — which means the shadow system grows in exact proportion to the platform's rigidity. Nobody reports the workaround as a problem, because it works. Platform adoption is measured by logins rather than by whether the platform holds the truth. And each missing field seems too small to raise.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Find out what the spreadsheet holds and absorb it. Audit the columns customers actually maintain alongside the platform, which is the fix's starting point and is a research exercise almost no vendor runs — the answer is a specification for the product the customer needed. Support free-form notes and custom fields properly, since a large share of the shadow content is facts with nowhere to go and the absence of a text field is why an entire parallel system exists. Capture the communication, because the direct messages and emails contain the state and are the single largest missing input. Make status derivable rather than manually maintained, so the platform can answer the question the spreadsheet is kept to answer. Let teams define their own views and columns, which is most of what the spreadsheet provides. Import the existing spreadsheet, which is both a migration path and a diagnosis. Surface the exceptions the spreadsheet tracks — the delayed, the disputed, the special case — since those are why it exists. Report on platform completeness, so a team can see how much of the truth is outside it. Treat every export to a spreadsheet as a signal and instrument it, because the export is the moment the product failed. And measure the share of campaign state held in the platform, since that is the only honest adoption metric in a category full of shelfware.

## Who Feels the Pain
Managers maintaining two systems; teams whose reporting depends on a file one person owns; and brands whose partnership record disappears when someone leaves.

## Impact If Fixed
The shadow spreadsheet grows in exact proportion to the platform's rigidity, because every unmodelled fact goes there. Auditing the columns customers actually keep is a research exercise almost nobody runs and is a specification for the product they needed.
