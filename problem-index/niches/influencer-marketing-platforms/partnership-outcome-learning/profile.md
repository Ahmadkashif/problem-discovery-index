# Partnership Outcome Learning

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/influencer-marketing-platforms|Influencer Marketing Platforms]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a corpus of thousands of past partnerships into the answer to the only question the category is asked — and whoever closes that loop makes every subsequent selection better than the last.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$900M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of category spend
**Digital Adoption:** Low — executed well, learned from not at all
**Target Buyer:** Platform data leadership and brand measurement
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the corpus exists and the decisions are few

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
These platforms hold the largest record of creator partnerships ever assembled — which creators worked with which brands, with what content, at what price, against what response — and use it to power a search filter. The category has built excellent tooling for executing campaigns and almost none for learning from them. What makes this unusually tractable is the scale of the decision: the unit is a person rather than an impression, the number of decisions is small enough to reason about individually, and a modest amount of returned outcome data would go a very long way.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Campaign reports with reach and engagement, discount code redemptions, link clicks, and occasional brand lift studies weeks later. The gaps: outcomes that never return at usable granularity; no learning across campaigns; no cross-brand transfer; no counterfactual for what a different creator would have produced; and reporting that describes rather than informs the next decision.
