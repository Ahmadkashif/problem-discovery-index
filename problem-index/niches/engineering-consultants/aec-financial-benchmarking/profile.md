# Design Firm Financial Benchmarking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/engineering-consultants|Engineering Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Zweig Group, PSMJ, and the AEC advisory publishers survey design firms on fees, utilization, overhead, compensation, and transaction multiples, and sell the benchmark reports and valuation guidance the industry runs its businesses against. In a sector of sixty thousand mostly small firms, there is no other source.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 researchers, analysts, and advisors
**Output:** Financial and operating benchmarks, compensation surveys, valuation and M&A guidance
**Proprietary data:** Contributed firm financial data and completed transaction terms across a fragmented private market
**Clock:** Annual survey and publication cycles
**Buyer:** Director of Research / Principal
