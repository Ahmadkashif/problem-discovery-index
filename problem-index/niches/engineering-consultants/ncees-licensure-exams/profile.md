# Engineering Licensure Examination Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/engineering-consultants|Engineering Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
NCEES develops the fundamentals and professional engineering examinations that every state licenses on, conducts the practice analyses defining competence in each discipline, and administers the credential evaluation and comity systems that let engineers practise across states.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 80-200 exam development staff, psychometricians, and subject matter coordinators
**Output:** Licensure examinations, practice analyses, technical reports on exam performance
**Proprietary data:** Decades of item response and candidate outcome data across every licensed engineer in the country, by discipline
**Clock:** Examination administration windows and licensure cycles
**Buyer:** Director of Examinations / Chief Assessment Officer
