## Why These Niches

Engineering consulting fragments along discipline (MEP, civil, structural, environmental), firm size (sole practitioner vs. 200-person multi-discipline), client type (private developer vs. municipality vs. insurance/legal), and business function (design delivery vs. business development vs. financial management). These 8 niches cover the two dominant revenue segments (MEP and civil/site), the two most digitally neglected segments (sole practitioners who run everything on spreadsheets and forensic engineers whose litigation-driven workflow has no purpose-built software), the two most underserved client-facing niches (municipalities that need infrastructure engineering but get treated as low-margin afterthoughts, and residential structural PEs who are too small for commercial software), and the two highest-ROI automation targets (proposal assembly and project budget forecasting, which together consume 15-25% of non-billable hours at most firms). Excluded: geotechnical engineering (specialized enough for its own industry entry), traffic/transportation engineering (heavily public-sector with distinct procurement), and surveying (covered separately as land-surveyors).

## Why These Pockets

Two qualifiers, and one of them is a repeat of a shape already in the index — which is the point worth recording. Building code publishers are structurally identical to the electrical code body indexed under `electrical-contractors`: a model document developed on a fixed cycle, adopted into law, sold as the publication. Two separate organizations, two technical disciplines, the same business. That is now the second archetype to recur across unrelated industries in this sweep, after the regulatory content publishers.

What differentiates this instance is the gap. The Pass 1 analysis names jurisdiction-specific code compliance as a chronic manual burden — engineers cross-referencing PDFs of local amendments against national standards on every project. The publisher authors the base document and has no view of what its own code says in force anywhere, so thousands of firms independently reconstruct the same amendment picture for the same jurisdictions. The amendment layer is a research operation the publisher is uniquely positioned to run and has never been structured for, and building it would also close a loop the code development process has never had: a direct measure of which provisions jurisdictions routinely delete, which is evidence about where the model code does not fit practice.

Forensic engineering is the second, and it is the engineering analogue of the forensic accounting practice indexed from the reference industry — the written opinion is the invoice, the clock is a court schedule, and the proprietary asset is the record of which methodologies survived challenge, which reaches only the expert who was on the stand.

Eleven pockets logged without qualifying, and the pattern that dominates this industry is client ownership of work product. The largest insight workforce found anywhere in the sweep sits here — up to forty thousand engineers in the global AEC firms — and their accumulated project experience cannot be pooled, which is the identical finding to the Big Four risk practices and the systems integrator data practices. Design liability insurers hold the definitive record of how engineering projects actually go wrong and sell insurance.
