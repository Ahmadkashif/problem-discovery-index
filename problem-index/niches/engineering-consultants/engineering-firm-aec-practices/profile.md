# Large AEC Firm Technical Practices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/engineering-consultants|Engineering Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The technical practice and knowledge management organizations at AECOM, Jacobs, Stantec, and WSP produce the engineering deliverables clients pay for at enormous scale, and attempt to make the resulting expertise reusable across tens of thousands of engineers and thousands of concurrent projects.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5,000-40,000 engineers, with 50-300 in knowledge and technical practice functions
**Output:** Engineering deliverables, technical standards, practice guidance
**Proprietary data:** Project experience across tens of thousands of engagements — held under client confidentiality and organized by project rather than by problem
**Clock:** Project milestones and permit submission deadlines
**Buyer:** Chief Technical Officer / Director of Knowledge Management
