# Construction Materials Testing Laboratories

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/engineering-consultants|Engineering Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Materials testing firms verify that concrete, soil, asphalt, and steel placed on a project meet specification — sampling, testing, and issuing the reports the engineer of record and the building official rely on to accept the work. A failing result stops placement.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-1,000 technicians, engineers, and reviewers at the larger firms
**Output:** Test reports, non-conformance findings, acceptance recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Test results across thousands of projects, suppliers, and mix designs — a record of which producers and mixes actually perform
**Clock:** Construction schedules; results gate the next placement
**Buyer:** VP of Testing Services / Practice Leader
