# Owner's Representative & Programme Management

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/engineering-consultants|Engineering Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Programme management firms represent owners against their design and construction teams — reviewing designs, validating schedules and costs, and producing the independent assessments an owner acts on when a project is going wrong. They sit on the paying side of the same work engineering consultants deliver.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-500 programme managers, cost and schedule analysts, and technical reviewers
**Output:** Independent design and cost reviews, schedule analyses, programme status reporting
**Proprietary data:** Estimated-versus-actual cost and schedule outcomes across an owner's programme portfolio
**Clock:** Project milestones and owner governance cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Programme Management / Practice Leader
