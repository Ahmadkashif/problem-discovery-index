# Engineering Standards Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/engineering-consultants|Engineering Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
ASCE, AISC, ACI, and ASHRAE develop and publish the design standards that codes reference and engineers design to — load standards, concrete and steel design provisions, ventilation and energy requirements. Committee work is volunteer; staff run the process and publish the result, which is the organizations' principal revenue.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 staff engineers, standards developers, and editors per society
**Output:** Design standards, commentaries, design guides, certification
**Proprietary data:** Standards development records and the research basis behind each provision, accumulated across editions
**Clock:** Standard revision cycles aligned to code adoption
**Buyer:** Director of Engineering / VP of Standards
