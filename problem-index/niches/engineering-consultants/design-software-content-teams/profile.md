## What They Do

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 engineers, code specialists, and content developers
**Output:** Code and standards libraries, calculation engine provisions, jurisdiction configurations
**Proprietary data:** Encoded design provisions across standards and editions, plus support enquiry history showing where engineers misapply them
**Clock:** Standards revision and code cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Product / Director of Engineering Content
