# Design Professional Liability Insurers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/engineering-consultants|Engineering Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The specialist professional liability underwriters for design firms research claim causes by discipline, project type, and contract structure, price the coverage, and publish the loss prevention guidance the profession follows on contract language and scope management.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 underwriters, claims analysts, and risk management staff
**Output:** Rating models, loss prevention guidance, claim trend research
**Proprietary data:** Claims experience across design firms — the definitive record of how engineering projects actually go wrong, which no other party holds
**Clock:** Rate filings and annual renewal cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer / VP Risk Management
