# Forensic Engineering Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/engineering-consultants|Engineering Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Exponent, Rimkus, EFI Global, and the forensic practices investigate why things failed — structures, products, vehicles, fires, industrial systems — and produce the written opinion that insurers use to settle claims, manufacturers use to decide on recalls, and litigants use in court. The report and the expert testimony behind it are the entire invoice, and both are adversarially tested.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-1,000 engineers, scientists, and technical staff at the larger firms
**Output:** Failure investigation reports, expert opinions, testimony
**Proprietary data:** Decades of failure investigations across disciplines, plus the record of which methodologies and opinions survived deposition, challenge, and cross-examination
**Clock:** Court schedules, claim deadlines, and recall decision windows
**Buyer:** Practice leader / Managing Director
