# BIM Object & Manufacturer Content Libraries

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/engineering-consultants|Engineering Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Providers maintain the manufacturer product objects designers place into building models — geometry, performance attributes, and specification data — mapped to the classification systems specifiers use, so that a design carries real product information rather than generic placeholders.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 content modellers, taxonomists, and manufacturer liaison staff
**Output:** Product object libraries, specification content, classification mapping
**Proprietary data:** Normalized product attribute data across manufacturers, plus specification and download behaviour showing what designers actually select
**Clock:** Manufacturer product launches and specification cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Content / Head of Product Data
