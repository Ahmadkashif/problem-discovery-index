Specialist brokers value and sell independent HVAC companies into the consolidating platforms, pricing them on maintenance agreement base, technician retention, and replacement mix.

**Output:** Valuations, offering materials, buyer introductions
**Proprietary data:** Private transaction comparables in a rapidly consolidating trade
**Clock:** Transaction timelines
**Buyer:** Principal
