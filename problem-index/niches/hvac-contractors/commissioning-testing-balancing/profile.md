# Commissioning & Test/Balance Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hvac-contractors|HVAC Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Independent commissioning agents and test-and-balance contractors verify that installed HVAC systems actually perform as designed, measuring airflow, water flow, and control sequences, and issuing the report an owner or code official accepts before occupancy.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-80 commissioning agents, engineers, and technicians
**Output:** Commissioning reports, deficiency logs, test and balance certification, functional performance test results
**Proprietary data:** Measured as-built performance against design intent across thousands of buildings — the only record of how often HVAC systems are installed wrong, and how
**Clock:** Certificate of occupancy and project closeout deadlines
**Buyer:** Principal / Director of Commissioning
