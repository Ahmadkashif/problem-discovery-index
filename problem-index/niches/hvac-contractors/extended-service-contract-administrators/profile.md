# HVAC Extended Service Contract Administrators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hvac-contractors|HVAC Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Administrators underwrite and manage the extended labour and parts warranties sold with HVAC equipment, pricing them against how often specific systems fail and what repairs cost.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 actuaries, claims analysts, and programme managers
**Output:** Pricing and reserve models, claims adjudication, contractor network performance analysis
**Proprietary data:** Failure claims by equipment model, age, installation climate, and installing contractor — the empirical reliability record manufacturers publish nothing about
**Clock:** Annual programme renewal and reserve reporting
**Buyer:** Chief Actuary / VP of Product
