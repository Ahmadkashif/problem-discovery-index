# Field Service Platform Data Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hvac-contractors|HVAC Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The data groups at the dominant field service platforms run dispatch, pricing, and invoicing for a large share of US residential HVAC, and see every job, quote, and conversion the trade produces.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 data scientists, analysts, and product data staff
**Output:** In-product pricing and performance analytics, benchmarking, dispatch and capacity tooling
**Proprietary data:** Job-level pricing, technician performance, quote-to-close, and maintenance agreement retention across tens of thousands of contractors
**Clock:** Seasonal demand peaks and emergency service surges
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Product
