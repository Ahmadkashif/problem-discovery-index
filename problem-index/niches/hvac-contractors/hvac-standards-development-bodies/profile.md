# HVAC Standards Development Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hvac-contractors|HVAC Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The engineering societies write the ventilation, energy, and thermal comfort standards that commercial building codes adopt by reference, and sell the standards and their interpretations to the industry required to follow them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-250 staff engineers, standards administrators, and researchers, supported by large volunteer committees
**Output:** Consensus standards, interpretations and formal enquiry responses, research reports, design guidance
**Proprietary data:** The standards corpus with its full development history — every proposed change, comment, and committee resolution across decades
**Clock:** Standard revision cycles and code adoption dates
**Buyer:** Executive director / Chief Technology Officer
