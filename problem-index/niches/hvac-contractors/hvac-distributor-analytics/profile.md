# HVAC Distributor Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hvac-contractors|HVAC Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The wholesale distributors that sit between manufacturers and contractors run analytics groups covering equipment sell-through by contractor, branch, and geography — the earliest read on what is actually being installed in the US.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-200 category analysts, pricing analysts, and data staff
**Output:** Assortment and inventory planning, contractor segmentation, pricing and rebate programme analysis
**Proprietary data:** Equipment sell-through at contractor level across a large share of US installations, with pricing and seasonality
**Clock:** Seasonal peaks and pre-buy cycles ahead of efficiency standard changes
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Category Management
