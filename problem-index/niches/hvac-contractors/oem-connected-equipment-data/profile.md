# HVAC OEM Connected Equipment Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hvac-contractors|HVAC Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The manufacturers' connected product groups receive runtime, setpoint, and fault telemetry from millions of installed systems and thermostats, supporting warranty analysis, product development, and dealer service programmes.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 reliability engineers, data scientists, and connected product staff
**Output:** Fault diagnosis, warranty and reliability analysis, dealer service tooling, product development input
**Proprietary data:** Operating telemetry across millions of installed systems joined to warranty claims — the largest equipment reliability dataset in the trade
**Clock:** Warranty periods and seasonal failure peaks
**Buyer:** VP of Connected Products / Director of Reliability
