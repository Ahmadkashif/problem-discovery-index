# Utility Efficiency Programme Implementers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hvac-contractors|HVAC Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Implementation contractors run utility energy efficiency programmes — recruiting contractors, processing HVAC equipment rebates, verifying installations, and reporting claimed savings to the utility and its regulator.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-400 programme staff, engineers, and analysts
**Output:** Rebate processing, installation verification, savings claims, programme design recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Verified installation and equipment data across large customer populations, joined to claimed savings
**Clock:** Programme year targets and regulatory filing dates
**Buyer:** VP of Programme Delivery
