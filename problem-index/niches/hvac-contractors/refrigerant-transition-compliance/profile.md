# Refrigerant Transition & Allowance Compliance

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hvac-contractors|HVAC Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Federal programme staff and their contractors administer the hydrofluorocarbon phasedown — allocating production and consumption allowances, tracking reclaim, and enforcing the transition that is retiring the refrigerants most installed US systems run on.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 programme analysts, engineers, and enforcement staff
**Output:** Allowance allocations, compliance determinations, reclaim reporting, enforcement actions
**Proprietary data:** Production, import, and reclaim reporting for the entire US refrigerant supply
**Clock:** Statutory step-down dates that retire product catalogues industry-wide
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — federal programme
