# Contractor Design Standards Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hvac-contractors|HVAC Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The contractor association authors the residential load calculation, equipment selection, and duct design manuals that most jurisdictions require by code, and licenses them to the software vendors contractors actually use.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 technical staff, engineers, and education developers
**Output:** Design standards and manuals, software licensing, technician certification, technical education
**Proprietary data:** The design methodology corpus and its revision history, plus certification performance data
**Clock:** Code adoption cycles and standard revisions
**Buyer:** VP of Technical Services
