# HVAC Equipment Performance Certification

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hvac-contractors|HVAC Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Certification bodies test and certify the rated performance of every HVAC system combination sold in the US, and publish the directory that contractors, code officials, and rebate programmes look up to confirm a matched system's efficiency. A system that is not in the directory cannot be permitted in most jurisdictions and cannot claim a rebate or a tax credit — the certified rating is the equipment's licence to be installed.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 certification engineers, test programme managers, statisticians, and standards staff
**Output:** Certified performance ratings, the searchable directory of matched combinations, test procedure development, challenge testing programmes
**Proprietary data:** Verified performance for hundreds of thousands of equipment combinations, plus the challenge test history showing which manufacturers' claimed ratings survived independent retest
**Clock:** Federal minimum efficiency standard effective dates and the refrigerant transition, which retire entire product catalogues on fixed calendar dates
**Buyer:** VP of Certification / Chief Technical Officer
