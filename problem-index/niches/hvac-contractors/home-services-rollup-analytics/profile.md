The private equity backed home services platforms acquiring HVAC contractors run corporate analytics and development groups that underwrite acquisitions, standardize pricing, and benchmark performance across hundreds of formerly independent shops.

**Output:** Acquisition models, pricing standardization, branch performance benchmarking
**Proprietary data:** Job-level financials and technician productivity across hundreds of acquired businesses in many markets
**Clock:** Acquisition pipeline and seasonal cash cycles
**Buyer:** No external buyer — internal cost centre
