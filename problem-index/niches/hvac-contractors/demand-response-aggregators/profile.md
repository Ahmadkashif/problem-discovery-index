# Demand Response & Connected Thermostat Aggregators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hvac-contractors|HVAC Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Aggregators enrol residential thermostats and HVAC systems into utility demand response programmes, curtail load during grid events, and are paid on the reduction they can demonstrate. The measurement of that reduction is the transaction.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 data scientists, measurement analysts, and programme staff
**Output:** Load reduction estimates, event dispatch decisions, settlement reporting to utilities
**Proprietary data:** Thermostat-level runtime, setpoint, and response behaviour across large enrolled populations, joined to grid events
**Clock:** Grid events in real time, and utility settlement periods
**Buyer:** VP of Data Science / Head of Grid Services
