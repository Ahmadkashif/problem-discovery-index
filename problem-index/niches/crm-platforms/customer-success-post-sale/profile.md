# Customer Success & Post-Sale

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/crm-platforms|CRM Platforms]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in customer success software is fighting to identify a renewal at risk early enough that intervention still works — and whoever predicts churn with real lead time takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3.1B US customer success, renewal management and post-sale revenue software
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of CRM revenue, against a majority of recurring revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — health scores are widespread and are mostly configured rather than learned
**Target Buyer:** Chief customer officers and customer success leaders at recurring-revenue businesses
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the product usage and support signal is abundant and is turned into a weighted average

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
In a recurring-revenue business most of the revenue arrives after the sale, and the function responsible for it inherited a toolset built for acquisition. The CRM models an opportunity that closes; a subscription relationship does not close, it persists, expands, contracts and eventually ends, and the events that matter — a champion leaving, usage declining in one team, a support escalation handled badly, a competitor entering the account — have no representation in an opportunity-shaped data model. The customer success platforms that grew up alongside filled part of the gap with health scores, which are typically a weighted combination of a handful of inputs with weights chosen by a customer success leader in a configuration screen. The outcome data to learn those weights — thousands of accounts that renewed, expanded or churned, with their full usage and support history — sits in the same system and is used to colour a dashboard red, amber or green.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Gainsight, ChurnZero, Totango, Catalyst and the CRM incumbents' own offerings provide health scoring, playbooks, renewal tracking and customer communication. Product analytics platforms hold usage. Support platforms hold tickets. The gaps: health scores are configured rather than fitted, so nobody knows whether the weights predict anything; lead time is not measured, so a score that turns red thirty days before a renewal is treated the same as one that turns red at nine months, though only one of them is useful; expansion prediction is far less developed than churn prediction despite expansion being the larger revenue lever in most portfolios; and the intervention side — which playbook actually changes an outcome — is entirely untested, exactly as in the sales coaching case.
