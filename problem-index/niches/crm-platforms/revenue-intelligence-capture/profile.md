# Revenue Intelligence & Capture

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/crm-platforms|CRM Platforms]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every competitor is fighting to capture what actually happened between seller and buyer without asking the seller — but recording conversations and reconstructing an engagement graph are different products with different objections. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$4.2B US revenue intelligence, conversation analytics and activity capture
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of CRM revenue and the category's fastest-growing block
**Digital Adoption:** High — adoption has been rapid, in enterprise sales organisations especially
**Target Buyer:** Sales leadership and enablement; revenue operations as the technical owner
**Automation Potential:** Very High — both halves are capture-and-inference problems on data already flowing

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Revenue intelligence exists because someone noticed that the CRM's record is an opinion and the surrounding exhaust is not. Emails, calendar entries, call recordings and document engagement are behavioural, unfalsifiable and predictive, and they flow through systems the enterprise already operates. Gong, Clari, Outreach and their competitors built substantial businesses on that observation, largely on data passing through the incumbents' own platforms, which is the most consequential product failure in horizontal SaaS. What the category name conceals is that it contains two products. One records and analyses conversations — a speech and language product whose defining constraints are consent, recording law and the fact that representatives experience it as being listened to. The other reconstructs the engagement graph from metadata — a resolution and completeness product with no recording, a different privacy conversation, and an entirely different technical core.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Gong and Chorus anchor conversation intelligence; Clari leads forecasting from activity; Outreach, Salesloft and the engagement platforms capture and orchestrate outbound. The CRM incumbents have added capture features of varying depth. The gaps that span both halves: the captured signal is used for coaching and dashboards far more than for prediction, so the most valuable use is the least developed; nothing measures whether the coaching derived from it changes outcomes, which is the claim the category is sold on; and representatives, whose activity is the raw material, receive very little back from it — which is the adoption risk the category carries and mostly manages by making adoption non-optional.
