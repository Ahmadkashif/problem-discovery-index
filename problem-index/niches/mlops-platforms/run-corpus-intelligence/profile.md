# Run Corpus Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mlops-platforms|MLOps Platforms]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn millions of recorded training runs into answers the field currently gives as folklore — and whoever does that owns the empirical account of how machine learning actually works, which no individual product in the category can match.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$230M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** None — millions of runs, shipped as a chart
**Target Buyer:** The vendors themselves, and their customers as the beneficiaries
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the corpus is already structured and complete

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
These platforms hold the most complete record of how machine learning is actually practised: millions of training runs with hyperparameters, datasets, code versions, metrics and outcomes, across thousands of organisations. That corpus answers questions the field currently answers with folklore — which search strategies pay for themselves, how much a given architecture change is typically worth, whether a run at epoch three is worth continuing, how much of the published improvement in a given area survives a change of seed. Every vendor sits on this and ships a chart. The contest is whoever converts it into empirical guidance, because that is a product no amount of dashboard work can replicate and it defends itself.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Run tables, sweep visualisations, and per-customer reporting. The gaps: no cross-customer analysis of what works, despite the data being the vendor's only truly unique asset; no early-stopping recommendation informed by millions of comparable curves; no warm-starting a new project's search from the population; and no published empirical account of practice, which the field would use and which nobody has produced.
