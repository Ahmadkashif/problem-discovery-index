## What They Do
Substantiate the safety of products applied to the body and keep them compliant with cosmetic regulation — the regime tattoo inks and aftercare products fall under, and the one European restrictions on pigment chemistry have already reshaped.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-120 toxicologists, regulatory scientists, and safety assessors
**Output:** Safety assessments and substantiation dossiers, ingredient reviews, claim substantiation, adverse event evaluation, registration filings
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated safety assessments and ingredient positions across products and jurisdictions
**Clock:** Regulatory compliance dates and product launch schedules
**Buyer:** Director of Regulatory & Safety
