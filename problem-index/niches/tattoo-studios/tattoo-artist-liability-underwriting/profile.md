# Tattoo Artist & Studio Liability Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tattoo-studios|Tattoo Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Underwrite the professional and general liability programmes studios and artists carry, and analyse the infection, allergic reaction and dissatisfaction claims that follow.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 programme underwriters and claims analysts
**Output:** Programme pricing and eligibility, claims handling, hygiene and consent requirements, risk guidance
**Proprietary data:** Claims joined to studio practice, artist experience and procedure type
**Clock:** Annual renewals
**Buyer:** Programme Underwriting Manager
