# Artist Discovery & Booking Marketplaces

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tattoo-studios|Tattoo Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator/rollup

## What They Do
Match clients to artists by style and location, hosting portfolios and handling enquiries — the discovery layer that has largely moved onto general social platforms rather than industry-specific ones.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-30 analysts and product staff
**Output:** Style matching and search, artist ranking, enquiry routing, portfolio hosting
**Proprietary data:** Style-tagged portfolio imagery and enquiry-to-booking behaviour
**Clock:** Continuous enquiry flow
**Buyer:** Head of Data
