# Tattoo Supply Distributor Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tattoo-studios|Tattoo Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator/rollup

## What They Do
The category and demand analytics functions inside the distributors that supply studios with inks, needles, machines and disposables — the only parties with a transactional view of what the industry actually consumes.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 analysts and category managers
**Output:** Assortment and pricing decisions, demand forecasting, new product screening, studio purchasing analytics
**Proprietary data:** Order-level purchasing across thousands of studios, joined to studio survival
**Clock:** Ordering cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Merchandising
