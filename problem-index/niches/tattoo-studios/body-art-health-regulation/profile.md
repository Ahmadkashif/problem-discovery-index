# Body Art Health Regulation & Inspection

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tattoo-studios|Tattoo Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
License studios and artists, inspect for sterilisation and bloodborne pathogen compliance, and investigate infection complaints — administered county by county with rules that differ everywhere.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-40 per jurisdiction — environmental health inspectors and programme staff
**Output:** Licensing, inspection findings, complaint investigations, closure orders
**Proprietary data:** Inspection and infection complaint records held locally, with no aggregation across jurisdictions
**Clock:** Annual licence renewals and inspection schedules
**Buyer:** Environmental health director
