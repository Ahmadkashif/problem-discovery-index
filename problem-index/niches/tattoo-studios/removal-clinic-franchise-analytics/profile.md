# Tattoo Removal Clinic Chain Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tattoo-studios|Tattoo Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator/rollup

## What They Do
Corporate analytics inside the tattoo removal clinic chains — session pricing, treatment course forecasting, clinic performance and territory expansion.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-25 analysts
**Output:** Pricing and package design, treatment course forecasting, clinic performance reporting, territory planning
**Proprietary data:** Treatment records with ink characteristics, session counts and clearance outcomes — the closest thing to a removal outcomes dataset that exists
**Clock:** Treatment scheduling and marketing cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Operations
