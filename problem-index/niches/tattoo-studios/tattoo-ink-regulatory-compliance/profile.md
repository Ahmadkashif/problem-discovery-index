# Tattoo Ink Regulatory & Pigment Compliance

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tattoo-studios|Tattoo Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Reformulate and document tattoo inks against pigment restrictions and impurity limits, and prepare the safety and composition dossiers manufacturers need to keep selling into regulated markets — work created almost entirely by the European chemical restrictions that removed a large share of the industry's pigment palette.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 regulatory chemists, toxicologists, and compliance specialists across the specialist consultancies
**Output:** Formulation compliance reviews, impurity and contaminant assessments, safety dossiers, labelling and declaration compliance, market authorisation support
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated pigment and impurity assessments across formulations and jurisdictions
**Clock:** Regulatory compliance dates that removed products from the market on a fixed day
**Buyer:** Director of Regulatory Affairs
