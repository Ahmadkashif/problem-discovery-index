# Ink Manufacturer Pigment R&D

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tattoo-studios|Tattoo Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Formulate tattoo inks — pigment selection, carrier chemistry, sterility and stability — and reformulate around pigment restrictions while trying to preserve colour performance in skin.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 chemists and formulation scientists across the manufacturers
**Output:** Formulations, stability and sterility validation, colour performance testing, compliance documentation
**Proprietary data:** Formulation libraries and in-skin performance observations, largely anecdotal
**Clock:** Regulatory compliance dates and product releases
**Buyer:** Director of R&D
