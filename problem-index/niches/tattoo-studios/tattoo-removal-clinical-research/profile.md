# Laser Tattoo Removal Clinical Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tattoo-studios|Tattoo Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The clinical and applications research groups inside the aesthetic laser manufacturers, who develop and validate removal protocols by ink colour, skin type and depth, and generate the evidence behind device clearances.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 clinical scientists, applications specialists, and regulatory staff across the device manufacturers
**Output:** Treatment protocols and parameter guidance, clinical studies, device clearance submissions, practitioner training
**Proprietary data:** Treatment outcome data by ink, skin type and device parameters, gathered through sponsored studies and practitioner networks
**Clock:** Device clearance programmes and product launches
**Buyer:** VP of Clinical Affairs
