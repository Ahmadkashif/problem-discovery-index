# Catering Rollup Corporate Development

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/catering-companies|Catering Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The small deal teams inside regional catering and hospitality groups acquiring independent caterers and commissary operations. They screen targets, model event mix and margin, and integrate acquired books of business.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 2-6
**Output:** Target screens, diligence models, integration analyses
**Proprietary data:** Event-level margin data across the acquired portfolio
**Clock:** Deal timelines
**Buyer:** VP Corporate Development
