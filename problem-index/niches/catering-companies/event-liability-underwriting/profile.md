# Event & Hospitality Liability Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/catering-companies|Catering Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Specialty underwriters and programme administrators price liability, liquor liability, and event cancellation cover for caterers and event operators. Underwriters research loss experience by event type, venue, alcohol service model, and geography to set rates and appetite.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 underwriters, actuaries, and analysts
**Output:** Rating models, underwriting guidelines, loss analyses
**Proprietary data:** Claims experience by event type and service model — narrow, and the segment is small
**Clock:** Rate filing cycles and policy renewal seasons
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
