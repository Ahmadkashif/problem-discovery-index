# Broadline Distributor Category Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/catering-companies|Catering Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The category management and business solutions groups at Sysco, US Foods, and Performance Food Group analyze what their operator customers buy, build menu and pricing advisory tools, and sell shelf and promotional insight to the manufacturers whose products they carry.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 category analysts, data scientists, and consultants
**Output:** Category plans, operator-facing menu and margin analytics, manufacturer insight reporting
**Proprietary data:** Purchase data across hundreds of thousands of foodservice operators at line-item granularity
**Clock:** Category review cycles and manufacturer planning calendars
**Buyer:** VP of Category Management / Chief Merchandising Officer
