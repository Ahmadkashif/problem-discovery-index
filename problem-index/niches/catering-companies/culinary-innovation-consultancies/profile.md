# Culinary Innovation & Product Development Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/catering-companies|Catering Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Mattson, CuliNex, and Sterling-Rice Group develop food concepts and formulations for manufacturers and large operators. Food scientists and chefs run consumer research, build and iterate formulations, and hand over a specification the client commercializes. The developed concept is the invoice.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-120 food scientists, culinary developers, and consumer researchers
**Output:** Concepts, formulations, and the supporting consumer research
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated formulation and sensory knowledge across hundreds of projects — largely owned by the clients who commissioned it
**Clock:** Client launch calendars and category review dates
**Buyer:** Managing Partner / VP of Innovation
