# Foodservice Market Intelligence Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/catering-companies|Catering Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Datassential, Technomic, CHD Expert, and Circana's foodservice practice sell what is happening on menus and in operator kitchens. Analysts scrape and code hundreds of thousands of menus, run consumer and operator panels, and maintain operator location and segment databases, then sell menu trend tracking, flavour adoption curves, concept scoring, and custom studies to food manufacturers, distributors, and large operators.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-400 analysts, menu coders, panel managers, and consultants
**Output:** Menu and flavour trend databases, operator segment data, custom insight studies
**Proprietary data:** A longitudinal menu database spanning hundreds of thousands of US operators, coded against a proprietary ingredient and preparation taxonomy, plus panels no competitor can rebuild
**Clock:** Client innovation and range planning calendars
**Buyer:** VP of Insights / Chief Research Officer
