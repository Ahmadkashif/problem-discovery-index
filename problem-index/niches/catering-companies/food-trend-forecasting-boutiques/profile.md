# Food Trend Forecasting Boutiques

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/catering-companies|Catering Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Baum+Whiteman, af&co, and comparable studios publish the annual food and beverage trend forecasts that shape menu planning across the hospitality industry. The forecast is the product and it is widely cited, but the operations behind them are one or two named principals.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1-5
**Output:** Annual trend forecasts, consulting engagements
**Proprietary data:** The principal's accumulated observation and industry network
**Clock:** Annual forecast publication timed to menu planning season
**Buyer:** The principal
