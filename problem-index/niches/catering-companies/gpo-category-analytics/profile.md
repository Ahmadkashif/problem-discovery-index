# Foodservice GPO Category Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/catering-companies|Catering Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Foodbuy, Entegra, Avendra, and the contract caterer purchasing arms aggregate foodservice spend across thousands of operators, negotiate manufacturer agreements, and publish category analytics and savings reporting to member operators. Their category managers research price movement, substitution, and supply risk across the food basket.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-200 category managers, procurement analysts, and data staff
**Output:** Category strategies, savings and compliance reporting, supply risk analyses
**Proprietary data:** Line-item purchase data across billions of dollars of foodservice spend, by operator segment and geography
**Clock:** Contract negotiation cycles and member reporting calendars
**Buyer:** VP of Category Management / Chief Procurement Officer
