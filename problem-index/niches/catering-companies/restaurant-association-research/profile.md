# Foodservice Association Economic Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/catering-companies|Catering Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The National Restaurant Association's research group and the International Foodservice Manufacturers Association produce industry sales forecasts, operator sentiment tracking, and workforce studies, and supply the economic analysis behind labour and regulatory advocacy.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-30 economists, survey researchers, and analysts
**Output:** Industry forecasts, operator surveys, workforce and regulatory impact studies
**Proprietary data:** Long-running operator survey series and member-contributed data
**Clock:** Monthly and annual publication schedule plus legislative sessions
**Buyer:** Chief Economist / VP of Research
