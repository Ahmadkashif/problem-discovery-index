# Catering & Event Industry Benchmarking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/catering-companies|Catering Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Small research operations that survey caterers and event venues on pricing, food cost percentage, labour ratios, and booking patterns, and publish benchmark reports and annual industry outlooks that operators use to price events and evaluate their own margins.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-20 researchers and analysts
**Output:** Benchmark reports, pricing surveys, industry outlooks
**Proprietary data:** Survey responses from participating operators, collected annually
**Clock:** Annual publication schedule, self-set
**Buyer:** Director of Research
