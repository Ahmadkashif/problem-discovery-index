# Third-Party Food Safety Audit & Certification

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/catering-companies|Catering Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
NSF, SGS, AIB International, and Steritech audit catering kitchens, commissaries, and foodservice operations against GFSI schemes and customer-specific standards. Auditors inspect, score, and issue a written report; large clients require certification as a condition of supply, so the report gates commercial access.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,500 auditors, technical reviewers, and scheme managers
**Output:** Audit reports, certification decisions, non-conformance findings
**Proprietary data:** Non-conformance findings across tens of thousands of facilities, joined to recurrence and to eventual recall or outbreak events
**Clock:** Annual and semi-annual certification cycles, plus customer-mandated audit windows
**Buyer:** VP of Food Safety Services / Global Technical Director
