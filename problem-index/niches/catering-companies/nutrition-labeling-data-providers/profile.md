# Nutrition & Labeling Data Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/catering-companies|Catering Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Trustwell (ESHA), Nutritionix, and MenuTrinfo maintain food composition databases and the analysis tooling that turns a recipe into a compliant nutrition panel and allergen declaration. Caterers, chains, and manufacturers rely on them because menu labeling and allergen disclosure carry regulatory and liability consequences.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 food scientists, database analysts, and regulatory specialists
**Output:** Composition databases, nutrition analyses, allergen and labeling compliance outputs
**Proprietary data:** Curated food composition data assembled from laboratory analysis, manufacturer submissions, and public databases over decades
**Clock:** FDA labeling rule effective dates and menu change cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Director of Regulatory Content
