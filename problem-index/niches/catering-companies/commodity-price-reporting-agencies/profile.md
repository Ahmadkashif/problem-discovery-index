# Food Commodity Price Reporting Agencies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/catering-companies|Catering Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Urner Barry, Expana, Mintec, and their peers publish the price quotations that protein, produce, and dairy contracts settle against. Market reporters call buyers and sellers daily in markets that have no exchange, assess where trade actually occurred, and publish a number. Caterers, distributors, and manufacturers write contracts that reference those quotations by name, which means the reporter's judgment is the price.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 market reporters, analysts, and editors
**Output:** Daily and weekly price assessments, market commentary, forecasts — the subscription product
**Proprietary data:** Decades of reporter-collected transaction intelligence from markets with no public price discovery, plus the assessment history behind every published number
**Clock:** Daily publication deadlines; contracts settle on the published figure
**Buyer:** Editor-in-Chief / VP of Market Reporting
