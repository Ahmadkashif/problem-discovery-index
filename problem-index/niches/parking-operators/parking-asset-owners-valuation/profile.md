# Parking Real Estate Owners, REITs & Valuation Advisers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/parking-operators|Parking Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The capital behind garages and the advisers who value them. Mobile Infrastructure Corporation (NYSE American: BEEP) owns 36 facilities with ~13,500 spaces in 19 markets, reports RevPAS and in 2025 earned 63.1% of its parking revenue at sites run by Metropolis. Infrastructure funds back the operators: Argo's funds invest in LAZ, and Eldridge led Metropolis's 2024 financing. Appraisers and brokers value garages deal by deal.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analysts per owner or fund on parking (BEEP reported 18 employees in total at end-2025); valuation work is per engagement
**Output:** Acquisition models, asset-management reviews, appraisals
**Proprietary data:** Owners' own facility revenue; appraisers' deal files
**Clock:** Deal closings, lender reporting and the REIT's own 10-Q and 10-K dates (Q4 3 at most)
**Buyer:** Chief investment officer or asset-management head
