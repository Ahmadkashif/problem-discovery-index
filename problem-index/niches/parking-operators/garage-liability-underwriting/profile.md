# Garage & Parking Operator Liability Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/parking-operators|Parking Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Insurers and specialty programmes that cover parking operators: garagekeepers legal liability for customers' cars, premises liability for slips and assaults in garages, valet damage, and crime cover for cash handling. Operators buy through brokers, and the nationals often self-insure a layer. SP+'s 2023 10-K listed insurance (general, workers' compensation and health care) among its ancillary service revenues.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under ~10 dedicated parking underwriters and analysts per carrier or programme (estimate)
**Output:** Policies, pricing, loss-control guidance
**Proprietary data:** The carrier's own parking claims history
**Clock:** Policy renewal dates (client clocks)
**Buyer:** Programme manager or underwriting head
