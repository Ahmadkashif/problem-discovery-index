# Parking Reservation Marketplace Pricing & Demand Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/parking-operators|Parking Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The data, pricing and demand teams at parking reservation marketplaces: SpotHero, ParkWhiz and the reservation side of Arrive's ParkMobile. They sell drivers prepaid spaces in operators' facilities, take a commission, forecast event demand and give operators dashboards and pricing suggestions. SpotHero was hiring a Senior Data Engineer I in Chicago (first posted 2026-09-18).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~15–40 data, pricing and analytics staff per marketplace (estimate)
**Output:** Reservation pricing and inventory allocation, event demand forecasts, operator performance dashboards
**Proprietary data:** Search and booking records across thousands of facilities and events, including demand the operator never sees (searches that did not book)
**Clock:** Venue and event schedules and operators' contract renewals (client clocks)
**Buyer:** VP of data or pricing at the marketplace
