# National Operator Revenue & Analytics Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/parking-operators|Parking Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The central pricing, revenue-analysis and business-intelligence teams at the national operators: Metropolis (with SP+), LAZ (5,300+ locations after its 2025 INDIGO Park Canada deal), Towne Park, Impark/Reef and the larger regionals. They set or review rates, build RevPAS reports and owner statements, model bids for RFPs and renewals, and analyse portfolio performance. Their output is part of the management fee or the lease margin, not sold separately.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–60 analysts and data staff per national (estimate); facility managers and revenue-control clerks are production
**Output:** Rate recommendations, RevPAS and variance reports, owner reporting packs, bid and renewal models
**Proprietary data:** Transactions across thousands of facilities; at managed sites (~88% of SP+'s commercial locations in 2023) the revenue records are the owner's, held by the operator as agent
**Clock:** Monthly owner statements due under each contract and owners' budget and renewal dates (client clocks)
**Buyer:** Chief revenue officer or VP of revenue at the operator

**Kill switches:** live on the managed half. Mobile Infrastructure Corporation's 10-K says its operators "act as agents collecting revenues on our behalf", and management contracts leave the revenue data with the owner. Pooling it across owners needs each contract's permission, and none was checked. The leased half (~12% of SP+'s commercial locations) is the operator's own and carries no switch. The managed half carries the verdict because it is most of the portfolio.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch (31). It is the closest parking match to [[niches/hotels-boutique/centralized-revenue-management-services/profile|Centralized Revenue Management Services]] (50, kill switch on the same owner-data ground). The 19-point gap is Q1 −6 (hotel revenue management is invoiced to many independent hotels, while operator analytics are internal, 2 against 4), Q2 −4 (central teams of tens against revenue managers who scale with every hotel signed, 3 against 5), Q4 −4 (a monthly owner statement against the hotel pocket's daily pricing cycle, 3 against 5; that Q4 5 on daily pricing predates this run's clock rulings, which put the hotel pocket at ~44–46 and the honest gap at ~13–15), Q3 −3 (the owners' data cannot be pooled, 3 against 4) and Q5 −2 (a handful of nationals against thousands of independent hotels, 2 against 3).
