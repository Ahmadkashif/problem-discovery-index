# The Member Whose Plate Changed

**Niche:** [[niches/parking-operators/frictionless-entry-capture/profile|Frictionless Entry & Paid-at-Exit Capture]]
**Industry:** [[industries/parking-operators|Parking Operators]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A member buys a new car or borrows one, the plate no longer matches the account, and the "frictionless" exit becomes an unpaid session.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #worker-facing #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to have every car pay before it leaves, and whoever collects the highest share of parking sessions as payment at or before exit, with no notice needed, takes the account.

## The Problem
Member accounts are keyed on plates. Rentals, new cars and family cars break the match, and the member finds out from a notice or a support call.

## Why It's Still Broken
Apps ask members to add plates but do not prompt at the moment a mismatch happens.

## What a Fix Looks Like
When an unmatched car exits within minutes of a member's app being active nearby, or the member's usual facility sees an unknown plate at the member's usual time, send the member a one-tap "Was this you?" to add the plate and pay.

## Who Feels the Pain
Members, the support agents who field their calls, and the notice processors handling disputes from paying customers.

## Impact If Fixed
If 10% of unpaid sessions at member-heavy facilities are mismatched members (estimate), a one-tap prompt converts most of them to payments and removes the same share of disputes.
