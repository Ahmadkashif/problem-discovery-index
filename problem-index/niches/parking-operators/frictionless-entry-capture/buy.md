# Toll Pay-by-Plate Image Review Adapted to Garages

**Niche:** [[niches/parking-operators/frictionless-entry-capture/profile|Frictionless Entry & Paid-at-Exit Capture]]
**Industry:** [[industries/parking-operators|Parking Operators]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Toll roads have billed by plate image for years, with review queues for unreadable plates; garages are rebuilding the same pipeline.
**Tags:** #cnns #object-detection #workflow-orchestration #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to have every car pay before it leaves, and whoever collects the highest share of parking sessions as payment at or before exit, with no notice needed, takes the account.

## The Problem
Gateless garages need what electronic tolling built: automatic reads, a human review queue for doubtful images, account matching and image retention rules. Most parking platforms built theirs from scratch.

## What Already Exists
Image-review and pay-by-plate back offices used by toll-road operators (not built as an industry) and their system integrators.

## The Customization Gap
Parking sessions have two reads, entry and exit, not one. Member accounts are opt-in apps rather than state-issued transponders, so prompts must reach the driver before they leave, and review must finish in seconds, not days. Each state's rules on private plate recognition and image retention differ from the public tolling statutes.

## Target Customer
Operators and PARCS vendors converting to camera entry at scale.

## Impact If Solved
Reusing a mature review workflow could cut the share of sessions needing manual review by ~50% (estimate) and avoid building a second back office for each new market.
