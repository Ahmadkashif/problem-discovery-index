# Owner-Side Parking Revenue & Contract Auditors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/parking-operators|Parking Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Auditors who check an operator's revenue, expenses and contract compliance for the owner: city and county internal auditors, outsourced internal-audit firms and parking specialists. RSM's report for Arlington County, dated 2026-05-20, audited the SP+ garage contract covering ~$3.6M of 2025 revenue and rated living-wage monitoring gaps High. Owners pay per engagement.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Production only — auditors test one contract or facility at a time; no dedicated parking research team found
**Output:** Audit reports with findings and ratings
**Proprietary data:** Work papers on clients' contracts and transactions
**Clock:** The owner's audit plan and contract terms (client clocks)
**Buyer:** Owner's internal-audit director or chief financial officer
