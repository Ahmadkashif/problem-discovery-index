# Commercial & Mixed-Use Garage Management

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/parking-operators|Parking Operators]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to return the most parking income per space to the owner after fees and expenses, and whoever delivers the highest net parking income per space against the owner's budget takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$4.0B a year in operator management fees, lease margins and on-site staff at office, retail and mixed-use garages (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~40% of operator receipts (estimate, against $10.7B of 812930 receipts in 2022)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — PARCS, apps and marketplaces are digital, but rate decisions, renewal pitches and owner budgets run on spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** Owner's asset manager (REIT, institutional owner, developer or city); the operator's regional vice president on the sell side
**Automation Potential:** Medium — forecasting, budgeting and reporting automate; staffing and owner relationships do not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is the core contract of the industry: an owner hands a garage to an operator, usually by RFP, under a management contract (1–3 years at SP+) or a lease (3–10 years). About 88% of SP+'s commercial locations were managed in 2023. Mobile Infrastructure Corporation converted its leases to management contracts in 2024 so that operator incentives follow revenue, and in 2025 Metropolis ran the sites producing 63.1% of its revenue. The owner judges the operator on one number, net parking income per space, and rebids when it slips. Competitors are the nationals (Metropolis/SP+, LAZ, Towne Park, Impark), regionals, tech-led operators such as AirGarage, and owners who self-manage.

## Current Tools & Gaps
PARCS (FLASH, Amano McGann, SKIDATA), Metropolis's camera platform, marketplace channels (SpotHero, ParkWhiz, ParkMobile), owner portals and Excel budgets. The gaps: no facility-level bridge from rate, volume, mix and expense to net income per space; renewal proposals that promise uplift without evidence; and no comparison against nearby facilities beyond the area manager's memory.
