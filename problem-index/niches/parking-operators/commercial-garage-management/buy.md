# Hotel Comp-Set Benchmarking Adapted to Garages

**Niche:** [[niches/parking-operators/commercial-garage-management/profile|Commercial & Mixed-Use Garage Management]]
**Industry:** [[industries/parking-operators|Parking Operators]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Hotels have judged operators against a competitive set for decades; garage owners still compare their facility with nothing but last year.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #k-nearest-neighbors #feature-engineering #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to return the most parking income per space to the owner after fees and expenses, and whoever delivers the highest net parking income per space against the owner's budget takes the account.

## The Problem
A hotel owner knows whether the management company beat the market because a benchmarking service reports occupancy, rate and RevPAR against a chosen set of nearby hotels. A garage owner sees only its own RevPAS and the operator's explanation.

## What Already Exists
Hotel benchmarking (the comp-set reports analysed at [[niches/hotels-boutique/hotel-performance-benchmarking/profile|Hotel Performance Benchmarking]]), retail foot-traffic panels, and property data platforms used by commercial real-estate owners.

## The Customization Gap
Parking needs its own comparison set: facilities within walking distance that compete for the same drivers, matched on type (office deck, event lot, hospital garage), size and access hours. It needs public demand signals as the market line: city meter occupancy feeds such as Seattle's, posted marketplace rates and event calendars. It also needs RevPAS split into transient, monthly and event, and a contribution model in which operators share anonymised facility metrics with owners' consent. Without that consent rule the benchmark cannot exist under management contracts.

## Target Customer
Owners with multi-market garage portfolios (REITs, infrastructure funds, universities, cities) and operators who want to show they beat their market.

## Impact If Solved
A credible comparison set turns a renewal from a negotiation over anecdotes into a test against the market, and would flag the ~10–20% of facilities in a portfolio that trail comparable sites by more than 10% of RevPAS (estimate).
