# Private-Property Parking Notice Processors & Collections

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/parking-operators|Parking Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Firms that turn unpaid sessions on private lots into mailed notices and collections. Parking Revenue Recovery Services (PRRS) is the named example in Crostarosa v. LAZ Parking, where the court summarised the complaint as PRRS obtaining motor-vehicle records for unpaid vehicles at LAZ-managed lots and mailing notices with timestamped photos. Operators and owners pay a share of collections or a fee per notice.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Production only — notice preparation, mailing, call-centre and dispute staff; analytics under ~10 (estimate)
**Output:** Notices, payments collected, dispute responses
**Proprietary data:** Notice and payment histories built on operators' session data and state motor-vehicle records
**Clock:** Per-notice payment windows set by state law (production deadlines, not analytics)
**Buyer:** Owner or chief operating officer of the processor

**Kill switches:** would be live — registered-owner records are obtained under the Driver's Privacy Protection Act's permissible uses, a statutory confidentiality regime, and the session data is the operator's or owner's.
**Verdict:** Logged — fails gate (production work with no insight function of 10). Their contest is [[niches/parking-operators/parking-notice-processing/profile|Parking Notice Processing & Disputes]]. Two boundaries: private-property towing ([[niches/towing-companies/consumer-towing-regulation/profile|Consumer Towing Regulation & Enforcement]]) removes the car under signage and impound rules and holds it until the fee is paid, while a notice processor leaves the car alone and mails a demand to pay. Debts still unpaid after the notice window pass to [[industries/collections-agencies|Collections Agencies]], where FDCPA and Regulation F letter, contact and dispute rules take over from the state parking-notice rules.
