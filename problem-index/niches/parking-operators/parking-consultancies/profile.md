# Parking Planning & Operations Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/parking-operators|Parking Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Consultancies that size, design and audit parking: demand and rate studies, parking ratios for zoning, operator RFPs and contract reviews, revenue-control audits, garage design and restoration. Walker Consultants (founded 1965, employee-owned, ~527 staff in 25–26 offices, search summary), Kimley-Horn's parking practice, DESMAN, Dixon Resources Unlimited and Timothy Haahs are examples. Cities, universities, hospitals, developers and owners commission them engagement by engagement.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Consultants are staffed per engagement (production); any standing research or benchmark team is under ~10 (estimate)
**Output:** Demand studies, rate studies, operator RFPs, audit reports, garage designs
**Proprietary data:** Past study files and counts, mostly built on clients' transaction data
**Clock:** Clients' planning, procurement and council schedules (client clocks)
**Buyer:** Principal or practice leader
