# City Parking Pricing & Enforcement Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/parking-operators|Parking Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
City transport departments and parking authorities set meter rates, run on-street enforcement, license garages and publish data. SFMTA moves each block's rate $0.25 about every quarter on an 80%/60% occupancy rule (latest change 2026-07-13). Seattle's SDOT targets 70–85% occupancy (search summary) and publishes per-minute paid occupancy and meter transactions as open data. New York City's consumer-protection agency licenses garages and lots. State attorneys general take complaints about private notices. City enforcement is the municipal side of parking, covered under [[industries/municipal-services|Municipal Services]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** Pricing and data analysts under ~10 per city (estimate); enforcement officers are production
**Output:** Rate changes, annual parking studies, open data, citations, licences
**Proprietary data:** Meter and citation records, published as open data in many cities
**Clock:** The agency's own rate-change and budget calendar
**Buyer:** Parking programme manager or transport director
