# Curb Management & Camera Enforcement Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/parking-operators|Parking Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors that sell cities the data and tools to manage the kerb: digital curb inventories, occupancy and turnover analytics for loading zones and meters, permit and pricing tools, and camera-based enforcement of loading, bus and bike lanes. Populus, Automotus, Hayden AI and Passport's curb products are examples. This is the municipal side of parking. Cities, not private operators, are the buyers, and city parking enforcement belongs to [[industries/municipal-services|Municipal Services]]. Private operators meet it as a competitor for short-stay demand and as a bidder for city enforcement contracts, a service SP+ listed in its 2023 10-K.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 data scientists, transport analysts and policy staff per vendor (estimate); citation reviewers are production
**Output:** Curb inventories, occupancy and dwell analytics, pricing and zoning recommendations, camera-issued citations
**Proprietary data:** Camera captures and fleet and permit data collected under city contracts; public meter and citation data are inputs, not a moat
**Clock:** City budget years, pilot reviews and council schedules (client clocks)
**Buyer:** City parking or curb programme manager

**Kill switches:** live — the camera captures, citation records and fleet data behind the analytics are captured for the city under service agreements whose terms were not seen, and client data under service agreements carries the switch unless checked terms grant product rights. Public city open data is not a switch, and government procurement is not one either; the switch is the client-owned capture data.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch (32). Analytics are partly invoiced and hundreds of cities are possible buyers, but teams are small, the clocks are clients' budget cycles, and the data that would make the analysis unique belongs to each city.
