# EV Charging Networks Sited in Garages

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/parking-operators|Parking Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Charging networks and charging-as-a-service providers that install and run chargers in operators' garages and lots under site-host agreements, sharing revenue or paying rent. Their site-planning teams forecast utilisation to decide which garages to equip. Demand is being pushed by mandates: New York City's Local Law 55 of 2024 requires existing licensed garages and lots with 10+ spaces to have Level 2 chargers on 20% of spaces by 2035 (search summary).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 site-planning and utilisation analysts per network (estimate)
**Output:** Site selection and utilisation forecasts for the network's own build-out; host reports
**Proprietary data:** Charging-session records across the network
**Clock:** The network's build plans and garage owners' compliance dates (client clocks)
**Buyer:** VP of site development at the network
