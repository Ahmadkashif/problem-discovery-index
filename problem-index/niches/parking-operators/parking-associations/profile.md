# Parking Industry Associations (NPA & IPMI)

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/parking-operators|Parking Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The National Parking Association represents operators and owners. The International Parking & Mobility Institute (IPMI) represents parking and mobility professionals, including the university, hospital and city programmes, and runs the CAPP certification and APO accreditation. IPMI seated its 2026–27 board at its June 2026 conference in Milwaukee (search summary). Both publish magazines, run conferences and commission occasional member surveys. Members pay dues.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Small staffs with volunteer committees; research under ~10 people (estimate)
**Output:** Conferences, certification, magazines, occasional industry surveys
**Proprietary data:** Member survey responses
**Clock:** The associations' own conference and publication calendars
**Buyer:** Executive director or chief executive
