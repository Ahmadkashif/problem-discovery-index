# Apartment & Residential Parking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/parking-operators|Parking Operators]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a building's resident and guest parking into assigned, billed spaces, and whoever collects the highest parking income per apartment unit takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M a year in residential parking software, enforcement services, towing coordination and on-site staff time at apartment, condo and mixed-use buildings (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~4% of operator-related spend (estimate); most residential parking is run by the property manager, not an operator
**Digital Adoption:** Low — stickers, hang tags, a spreadsheet of assignments and a towing company on call
**Target Buyer:** Multifamily regional or community manager; condo or HOA board; mixed-use asset manager
**Automation Potential:** High — assignment, guest passes, billing and plate-based enforcement are rule-bound

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The audience is the property manager who runs parking as a side job. They assign spaces, issue stickers and guest passes, bill reserved spaces and call a tow truck when someone parks in the wrong place. National operators rarely serve these buildings because the revenue per site is small. The work sits between [[niches/property-management/multifamily-apartment/profile|Multifamily Apartment]] management, HOA [[niches/hoa-management/violation-enforcement-operations/profile|Violation Enforcement]], and private-property towing, which is regulated at [[niches/towing-companies/consumer-towing-regulation/profile|Consumer Towing Regulation & Enforcement]]. Parkade sells a B2B2C app for apartments, mixed-use and offices that replaces "stickers, hang tags, and spreadsheets" and says it closed a Series A. It was hiring a Staff Front End Software Engineer (remote, interviews in San Francisco, first posted 2026-10-15).

## Current Tools & Gaps
Sticker and hang-tag systems, property-management platform add-on fees (Yardi, RealPage, Entrata), Parkade and similar apps, plate-reader patrols by towing or enforcement firms. Gaps: assignments not priced by demand; guest-pass abuse; wrongful tows that become resident disputes; parking income not tracked per unit.
