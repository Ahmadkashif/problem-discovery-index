# Property-Platform Amenity Billing Adapted to Parking

**Niche:** [[niches/parking-operators/apartment-residential-parking/profile|Apartment & Residential Parking]]
**Industry:** [[industries/parking-operators|Parking Operators]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Property-management platforms already bill pet rent, storage and amenity fees on the resident ledger; parking needs assignments, plates, guest passes and enforcement on top.
**Tags:** #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #revenue-impact #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a building's resident and guest parking into assigned, billed spaces, and whoever collects the highest parking income per apartment unit takes the account.

## The Problem
Parking fees sit on the resident ledger as a flat charge with no link to which space, which plate or which guest pass. Enforcement is a separate phone call to a towing company.

## What Already Exists
Add-on fee billing in Yardi, RealPage and Entrata; amenity reservation modules; resident portals.

## The Customization Gap
The parking version needs a space inventory by type with assignments tied to plates, and guest passes with time limits a patrol can check by plate. Charges have to start and stop automatically with move-in, move-out and space changes. It needs a log of warnings and tows that satisfies the state's private-property towing notice rules, and an export the towing firm and the board can read.

## Target Customer
Multifamily and condo managers already on a property-management platform who want parking inside it.

## Impact If Solved
Billing every assigned space accurately typically recovers ~3–8% of parking charges missed at move-ins and space changes (estimate), with no new resident-facing app.
