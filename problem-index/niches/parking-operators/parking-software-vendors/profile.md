# Parking Permit, Enforcement & PARCS Software Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/parking-operators|Parking Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors of the systems operators and institutions run on: PARCS gate equipment and cloud platforms (FLASH, Amano McGann, SKIDATA, TIBA), permit and citation software for universities, hospitals and cities (T2 Systems, Passport), residential parking apps (Parkade) and event-parking tools (ParkHub). They sell hardware, licences and per-transaction fees. Parkade was hiring a Staff Front End Software Engineer (remote, first posted 2026-10-15). FLASH could not be confirmed on its own careers pages.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Product and engineering teams; analytics or research staff under ~10 per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Software, hardware and in-product reports
**Proprietary data:** Clients' transaction, permit and citation records held in the vendor's systems
**Clock:** Release cycles and clients' go-live dates (client clocks)
**Buyer:** Head of product or CEO
