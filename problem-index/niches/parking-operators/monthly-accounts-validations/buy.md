# Subscription Billing Adapted to Parking Credentials

**Niche:** [[niches/parking-operators/monthly-accounts-validations/profile|Monthly Accounts, Access Cards & Validations]]
**Industry:** [[industries/parking-operators|Parking Operators]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Subscription billing platforms handle recurring payments, dunning and proration; they know nothing about an access card that should stop opening a gate.
**Tags:** #logistic-regression #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a facility's monthly-contract capacity sold and paid, and whoever bills and collects the highest share of that capacity each month takes the account.

## The Problem
Monthly parkers pay late, cancel without returning cards, and switch between facilities. Coordinators chase payments by email and deactivate cards by hand, often weeks after an account lapses.

## What Already Exists
Recurring-billing and subscription platforms with card-on-file, smart retries, dunning emails, proration and revenue reporting.

## The Customization Gap
Parking needs the credential tied to the subscription, whether card, tag, plate or app pass. The credential must suspend automatically when payment fails and restore on payment. Tenant-employer invoicing needs headcount by lease parking ratio, and reserved and unreserved tiers need waitlists. PARCS anti-passback rules have to be enforced, and each facility's monthly revenue has to be reported in the owner's statement format.

## Target Customer
Operators and self-managing owners with large monthly bases; office property managers administering tenant parking.

## Impact If Solved
Cutting monthly delinquency from ~5% to ~2% of billings (estimate) on a $2M-a-year monthly base recovers ~$60K a year per garage and removes most manual card deactivation.
