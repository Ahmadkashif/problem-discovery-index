# Hotel Revenue Management Adapted to Garages

**Niche:** [[niches/parking-operators/rate-setting-yield-pricing/profile|Rate Setting & Yield Pricing]]
**Industry:** [[industries/parking-operators|Parking Operators]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Hotel revenue-management systems already forecast demand and recommend rates daily; a garage sells hours, not nights, on top of a monthly base.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #bayesian-inference #optimization-fundamentals #revenue-impact #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to price every hour and every event at the rate that fills the facility without turning cars away, and whoever earns the highest revenue per available space against comparable nearby facilities takes the account.

## The Problem
Operators that want yield pricing either build it or do without. Hotel systems are the nearest products, but their unit is a room-night booked days ahead.

## What Already Exists
Hotel revenue-management systems and centralised revenue services (scored at [[niches/hotels-boutique/centralized-revenue-management-services/profile|Centralized Revenue Management Services]], 50 with a kill switch), and apartment rent-pricing software.

## The Customization Gap
Parking demand arrives in minutes, mostly unbooked, so forecasts must run hourly from gate counts, not from a booking pace. The monthly base sits underneath transient demand and has its own show-up pattern. Event days, early-bird caps and evening flats are rate products hotels lack. Owners' contracts cap or require approval of changes. Rates must publish to PARCS and marketplaces together, and be explained to an owner in RevPAS terms.

## Target Customer
National and regional operators; hotel and hospital owners whose garages sit next to their rooms business.

## Impact If Solved
Adapting a proven forecasting and optimisation core could cut build time from ~12–18 months to ~4–6 (estimate) for an operator starting a pricing function.
