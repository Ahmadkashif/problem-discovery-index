# Independent Warehouse Distributor Consolidators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-parts-distributors|Auto Parts Distributors & Stores]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Private-equity-backed and family-owned platforms that buy independent warehouse distributors and jobber stores and integrate them. Parts Authority (Lake Success, NY) closed its purchase of NPW Companies, a 46-location Miami-based warehouse distributor that also runs Bumper to Bumper and Auto Value stores, on 11 August 2026, taking its network to 325 locations. Driven Distribution Group, backed by Oridian Capital Partners (formerly HCI Equity Partners), bought Quality Parts Warehouse and, in January 2026, Battery Service Corp.; Fisher Auto Parts bought Smyth Auto Parts' 25 locations in January 2026 (search summary). Genuine Parts buys independent NAPA owners on the same pattern (50+ acquisitions in 2025). Their corporate development staff screen targets and model synergies in buying, inventory and delivery.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 corporate development and integration analysts per independent platform (estimate); Genuine Parts' team sits inside a large company and is internal
**Output:** Target screens, valuation and synergy models, integration plans
**Proprietary data:** Acquired companies' customer, inventory and margin histories
**Clock:** Deal timetables and lender covenants
**Buyer:** Platform CEO or CFO
