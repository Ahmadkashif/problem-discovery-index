# Tire Shipment Statistics & Tire Distribution Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-parts-distributors|Auto Parts Distributors & Stores]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The U.S. Tire Manufacturers Association (USTMA), whose 11 member companies operate 55 tire plants in 16 states, publishes shipment forecasts and sells an annual Factbook; its 6 August 2026 forecast cut 2026 U.S. shipments to 330.3 million units, with replacement passenger tires at 218.3 million. The Tire Industry Association, the dealers' and service trade body, runs technician training, recertification and a certified-dealer directory. Trade publications such as Modern Tire Dealer cover and rank wholesalers and dealers, including American Tire Distributors' restructuring. Members receive the statistics; publishers sell advertising and subscriptions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 statistics and research staff per body (estimate)
**Output:** Shipment statistics and forecasts, rankings, market commentary
**Proprietary data:** Members' reported shipments by segment; publishers' surveys of wholesalers and dealers
**Clock:** The bodies' own release calendars (Q4 2 under the rulings, were it scored)
**Buyer:** Association president; publisher
