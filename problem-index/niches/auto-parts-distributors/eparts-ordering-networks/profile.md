# Electronic Catalog & Parts-Ordering Networks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-parts-distributors|Auto Parts Distributors & Stores]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The networks repair shops use to search many suppliers at once and order: WHI's Nexpart (370,000 active buyers and 43,000 seller locations by its own description, search summary), PartsTech, an OEC company connecting shops to 30,000+ parts and tire distributors and 35+ shop management systems, and OEC's RepairLink for dealer OEM parts. Sellers pay subscriptions or transaction fees; shops use them free or bundled with their shop software.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analysts beyond product engineering per network (estimate); support and onboarding staff are production
**Output:** Ordering software and seller reports
**Proprietary data:** Search, quote and order logs across buyers and sellers, including searches that ended with another seller or no order
**Clock:** Shops' repair orders, minute by minute (production)
**Buyer:** CEO or head of product
