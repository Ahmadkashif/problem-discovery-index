# Deduction Recovery Adapted to Core Banks

**Niche:** [[niches/auto-parts-distributors/core-and-warranty-credits/profile|Core Returns & Warranty Credit Recovery]]
**Industry:** [[industries/auto-parts-distributors|Auto Parts Distributors & Stores]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Deduction-management and recovery tools already chase money owed between suppliers and retailers; they need core-bank logic to chase it in auto parts.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #logistic-regression #feature-engineering #automation #workflow-orchestration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn every core and warranty return into the credit the supplier owes, and whoever recovers the highest share of the credits a distributor is owed takes the account.

## The Problem
Returns clerks chase supplier credits by email and phone, armed with spreadsheets. Each supplier's programme document sets different acceptance rules, return windows and allowances.

## What Already Exists
Deduction-management and order-to-cash platforms (HighRadius and similar) and contingency recovery services chase short payments and deductions between consumer-goods suppliers and retailers, reading remittances and building dispute packets.

## The Customization Gap
In auto parts the money flows the other way (supplier to distributor) and is tied to physical units. The tool needs supplier core-bank tables (which casting numbers and part families are interchangeable), acceptance criteria, ageing limits, warranty defect codes and labour allowances, and it must read lump credit memos and split them to units. Dispute packets need unit evidence: tag scans, photos at acceptance, shipment manifests.

## Target Customer
Controllers at warehouse distributors and programme groups that negotiate supplier programmes for members.

## Impact If Solved
If a clerk's chase time per disputed credit fell from ~30 minutes to ~5 (estimate), a distributor handling ~300 disputes a month would free ~125 clerk hours a month.
