# OEM Wholesale Parts Programmes & Dealer Parts Networks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-parts-distributors|Auto Parts Distributors & Stores]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vehicle makers' parts-marketing and wholesale groups that push OEM parts through franchised dealers' wholesale counters to independent repair and collision shops, in direct competition with the aftermarket, and the networks that connect them: OEC's RepairLink orders OEM mechanical parts from dealers "with VIN-level accuracy," and OEC cites $30B+ in annual global parts transactions. The makers' teams set wholesale price programmes, track conquest of aftermarket categories and design dealer incentives. The dealers' side is [[industries/franchised-car-dealers|Franchised Car Dealers]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–50 parts-marketing and pricing analysts per vehicle maker (estimate)
**Output:** Wholesale pricing programmes, conquest analyses, dealer parts incentives
**Proprietary data:** The maker's own parts sales to dealers, VIN-level build data and warranty parts records, plus dealer wholesale data reported under franchise agreements
**Clock:** The maker's own programme calendar
**Buyer:** Director of parts marketing or aftersales
