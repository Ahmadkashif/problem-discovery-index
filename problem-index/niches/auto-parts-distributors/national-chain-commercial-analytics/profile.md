# National Parts Chains' Pricing, Assortment & Commercial Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-parts-distributors|Auto Parts Distributors & Stores]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The pricing science, category and assortment, supply-chain and commercial analytics groups inside AutoZone, O'Reilly, Advance and Genuine Parts' NAPA. They set store and hub assortments against the local vehicle parc (AutoZone's mega hubs carry ~80,000–110,000 SKUs; O'Reilly's 32 distribution centres reach 156,000+), price commercial matrices against each other, plan hub openings and track commercial share. The output is decisions for the chain itself; nobody outside pays for it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~50–200 analysts per chain across pricing, category, supply chain and commercial analytics (estimate); store and counter staff are production and are not counted
**Output:** Assortment and pricing decisions, hub-network plans, commercial account analytics
**Proprietary data:** Lookup, sale and return records across thousands of stores each (4,305 Advance, 6,447 O'Reilly and 6,863 AutoZone U.S. stores in their latest 10-Ks), commercial account histories and licensed vehicle-in-operation data
**Clock:** The chain's own planning and line-review calendar; earnings dates cap Q4 at 3 under the rulings, and the analysis does not run to them
**Buyer:** Chief merchandising officer or SVP of commercial
