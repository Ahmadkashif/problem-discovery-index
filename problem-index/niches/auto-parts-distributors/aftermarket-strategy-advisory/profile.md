# Aftermarket Strategy, Research & M&A Advisory

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-parts-distributors|Auto Parts Distributors & Stores]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Boutique aftermarket research and consulting firms, vehicle-population analysts, price-intelligence services that track the chains' online prices, and investment banks' aftermarket M&A teams. Hedges & Company of Hudson, Ohio is a named example: an automotive aftermarket digital-marketing agency that also sells market research and analysis of vehicle registration data, which it reports rather than resells (search summary). Suppliers, distributors and investors buy studies, price checks and deal advice one engagement at a time.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1–15 people per firm or practice (estimate); per-engagement consultants are production under the rulings
**Output:** Market studies, price-comparison feeds, valuation and deal advice
**Proprietary data:** Surveys, scraped public prices and deal histories, mostly reproducible
**Clock:** Clients' planning cycles and deal timetables (client clocks)
**Buyer:** Supplier or distributor strategy head; private-equity deal partner
