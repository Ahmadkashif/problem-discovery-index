# Aftermarket Programme Groups & Warehouse Distributor Cooperatives

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-parts-distributors|Auto Parts Distributors & Stores]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Member-owned or shareholder-owned groups that negotiate supplier programmes and rebates for independent warehouse distributors and jobbers, and supply brands, catalog, marketing and shop programmes: the Aftermarket Auto Parts Alliance (Auto Value, Bumper to Bumper; "over 50 shareholders"), Automotive Parts Associates (~60 warehouse distributor shareholders, search summary), Federated Auto Parts, The Pronto Network and others. Members pay dues and share in rebates; suppliers pay to be in the programme.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–20 supplier-programme, rebate and member-analytics staff at the largest group (estimate); catalog and marketing production staff are not counted
**Output:** Supplier programme performance and rebate calculations, member purchase benchmarks, category reports for members and suppliers
**Proprietary data:** Members' purchase and rebate reporting across hundreds of suppliers, which no single distributor or supplier sees
**Clock:** Annual supplier programme negotiations and rebate periods, set by the group and its suppliers (Q4 2 under the clock ruling)
**Buyer:** Group president or head of member services

**Kill switches:** **data isn't theirs** — live. Members' purchases are reported under programme terms to calculate rebates; under the rulings, data contributed under programme terms is membership data, and members compete with each other.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. Analysis is bundled into membership rather than invoiced, and the corpus is members' data.
