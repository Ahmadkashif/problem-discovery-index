# Same-Day Parts Delivery Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-parts-distributors|Auto Parts Distributors & Stores]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Courier marketplaces and dispatch-software vendors that parts stores and distributors use for overflow or outsourced same-day delivery to repair shops, sold per delivery or per vehicle. Roadie markets auto parts delivery to parts and tire companies, with same-day and hot-shot service "in as fast as 2 hours" to shops, dealerships or customers and billing through a UPS account; GoShare sells on-demand auto parts and tire delivery by app or API (search summary). They route stops and supply drivers; the store owns the shop relationship.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analysts per vendor on parts delivery (estimate); dispatchers and drivers are production
**Output:** Delivery capacity, routing, proof of delivery
**Proprietary data:** Delivery records held for store clients
**Clock:** Each order's promised window (production)
**Buyer:** Head of product or operations at the vendor
