# Auto Parts Tariff, Origin & Counterfeit Enforcement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-parts-distributors|Auto Parts Distributors & Stores]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The federal offices that decide which imported parts pay Section 232 duties and enforce origin and counterfeit rules: Commerce's BIS and ITA run the automobile-parts inclusions process, with two-week request windows each January, April, July and October under a September 2025 interim final rule; Customs and Border Protection reviews origin claims (USMCA-qualifying parts are exempt) and seizes counterfeits; NHTSA acts on counterfeit safety parts such as airbags.

## Insight Function
**Size:** A handful of staff on the auto-parts inclusions docket (estimate); customs entry reviewers and seizure officers are production
**Output:** Inclusion determinations, rulings, seizures and safety notices
**Proprietary data:** Inclusion requests and comments (public); entry records (confidential)
**Clock:** The inclusion windows themselves, set by rule
**Buyer:** Office director
