# Parts Distributor Business Systems & Catalog Software Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-parts-distributors|Auto Parts Distributors & Stores]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The vendors of the point-of-sale, inventory and catalog systems independent parts stores and warehouse distributors run on: Epicor's aftermarket business systems and catalog, MAM's Autopart, Autologue and smaller players (named from recall). They sell licences, hosting and catalog subscriptions, and increasingly AI features; their analytics staff mostly build product.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 product analytics and data staff per vendor working on the aftermarket (estimate)
**Output:** Product analytics, demand and replenishment features, occasional market reports
**Proprietary data:** Hosted customers' transaction and lookup data, where contracts allow, and implementation experience across thousands of stores
**Clock:** The vendor's release cycles
**Buyer:** Distributor owner or CIO buying the system
