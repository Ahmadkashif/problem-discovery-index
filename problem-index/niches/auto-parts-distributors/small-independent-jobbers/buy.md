# Retail Analytics Delivered as a Programme-Group Member Service

**Niche:** [[niches/auto-parts-distributors/small-independent-jobbers/profile|Small Independent Jobbers & Programme-Group Stores]]
**Industry:** [[industries/auto-parts-distributors|Auto Parts Distributors & Stores]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Assortment and account analytics exist for chains; the way to reach a ten-person jobber is through the programme group it already pays.
**Tags:** #survival-analysis #time-series-forecasting #gradient-boosting #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep the jobber's best shop accounts from moving their first call to a chain hub, and whoever keeps the largest share of its top accounts' parts spend year on year takes the account.

## The Problem
A small store cannot buy, integrate or staff an analytics tool. It already pays a programme group for catalog, marketing and supplier programmes, and takes the group's recommended assortment.

## What Already Exists
Retail assortment and forecasting software serves chains; vehicle-in-operation data is sold by S&P Global Mobility and Experian ([[niches/auto-dealers-independent/registration-market-intelligence/profile|Vehicle Registration & Market Intelligence Data]]); groups run member portals and supplier programme reporting.

## The Customization Gap
The service must run on each member's own data, with consent, under the group's terms: local parc-based assortment suggestions that respect what the group's warehouse stocks, share-of-wallet estimates for the member's shops, and a member benchmark that never exposes one member's customers to another (members in the same metro compete). Delivery is a monthly report and a weekly text, not a dashboard.

## Target Customer
Programme groups and warehouse distributors buying for their member stores.

## Impact If Solved
If a group's members lifted first-call fill on their top shops by ~3 points (estimate), across ~1,000 member stores selling ~$1.5M each that is ~$45M of retained member sales a year.
