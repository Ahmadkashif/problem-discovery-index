# Parts Manufacturers' Category Management & Line-Review Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-parts-distributors|Auto Parts Distributors & Stores]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The category management and customer analytics teams at aftermarket parts makers (brake, filter, chassis, engine-management and hard-parts suppliers selling to the chains and programme groups). They analyse a retailer's point-of-sale, lookup and return data to recommend assortments and coverage by vehicle parc, argue for their lines at the retailer's line review, and act as category captain where a retailer appoints one. Their pay-off is shelf space, not a fee.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 category, pricing and customer analysts per larger supplier (estimate)
**Output:** Line-review cases, coverage and assortment recommendations, category reviews for retailers
**Proprietary data:** Retailers' point-of-sale and return data shared under supply agreements, plus the supplier's own shipment, warranty and coverage records
**Clock:** Retailers' line reviews and programme renewals, a client's schedule (Q4 3 under the clock ruling)
**Buyer:** VP of category management or chief customer officer at the supplier
