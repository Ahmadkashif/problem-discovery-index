# Core & Warranty Return Processors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-parts-distributors|Auto Parts Distributors & Stores]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The remanufacturers' core departments, reverse-logistics providers and core brokers that receive, sort, grade and credit returned cores and warranty parts for suppliers and distributors. Cardone, a large remanufacturer, is a named example: its published core policies allow credit only for units in current programmes returned in the original box, cut credit by 25% or 50% for missing or damaged components, and run billing and credits through the warehouse distributor holding a Cardone account; its consumer core-rebate site stopped processing rebates on 23 January 2024 (search summary). They decide which units earn credit and settle the money between supplier and distributor.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Production sorters, graders and claims processors; under 10 analysts per firm beyond that (estimate)
**Output:** Core acceptance decisions, credit reports, sorted cores for remanufacture
**Proprietary data:** Grading outcomes by core family and source, held for supplier and distributor clients
**Clock:** Supplier credit cycles and claim windows, which bind each claim (production), not analysis
**Buyer:** Operations director at the processor
