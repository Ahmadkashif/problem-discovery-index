# EV & Hybrid Repair Parts Supply

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-parts-distributors|Auto Parts Distributors & Stores]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to supply the parts independent shops need to keep hybrids and EVs out of the dealer, and whoever fills the largest share of hybrid and EV repair-part requests from aftermarket stock takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3–5B a year of hybrid and EV repair parts reaching independent shops (estimate), against a parc of 6.2 million EVs and 13.4 million hybrids among 299.9 million light vehicles (Experian, Q2 2026; search summary)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1% of the three codes' receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — catalog coverage is thin and many listings read "dealer only"
**Target Buyer:** Category or product manager for hybrid and EV lines at a distributor or chain
**Automation Potential:** Low-Medium — the gap is coverage and stocking judgement, more than workflow

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The audience is the independent shop that wants to keep servicing a customer whose next car is a hybrid or an EV. Hybrids are now a large share of new sales (Experian put hybrids at 16.80% of new-vehicle share, search summary), and as they age out of warranty they arrive at independent shops needing hybrid battery packs, inverter and battery coolant pumps, 12-volt batteries, brake parts that wear differently under regenerative braking, EV-rated tyres and ADAS-related parts. Aftermarket coverage lags: many parts exist only from the vehicle maker, remanufactured packs come from a handful of specialists, and catalogs list few applications. Distributors are unsure which lines to stock for a parc that is still small store by store. The shops' side is [[industries/auto-repair-shops|Auto Repair Shops]]; fleet electrification is [[niches/fleet-managers/mixed-fuel-ev-fleets/profile|Mixed Fuel & EV Transition Fleets]], and used EVs at wholesale are [[niches/wholesale-auto-auctions/used-ev-wholesale/profile|Used EVs at Wholesale]]. Data access for independent repair is the subject of [[niches/auto-repair-shops/right-to-repair-advocacy/profile|Right to Repair Policy Research]]. Battery makers are the queued battery manufacturers row.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Catalogs carry what suppliers have coded, and dealer OEM parts are reachable through OEC's RepairLink and dealer wholesale counters. Nobody maps, store by store, which hybrid and EV repair requests the aftermarket can fill, which it cannot, and which are worth stocking now.
