# The Platform Engineer

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/internal-developer-platforms|Internal Developer Platforms]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor that takes this seriously is fighting to stop a platform team being the help desk for its own abstractions — and whoever does that takes the platform function, because that support load is what prevents the platform improving.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$210M US attributable to internal platform support
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~11% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** None — the support channel has no ticketing, no metrics and no memory
**Target Buyer:** Platform leadership; the beneficiary is the platform engineer
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the questions are repetitive and the answers are in the platform

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Platform engineers spend their weeks answering questions about the abstractions they built, in a support channel with no ticketing, no metrics and no way to tell whether the same question has been asked forty times. The load has a specific shape: most questions are about how to do something the platform supports and does not explain, a portion are about something the platform does not support and does not say so, and a portion are genuine defects. All three arrive in the same channel, addressed to individuals, and are answered by whoever is free. The consequence is the one this pattern always produces — the work that would reduce the questions is displaced by answering them, so the platform stays confusing and the channel stays busy, and the team's capacity is consumed by the symptom of their own product's difficulty.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A chat channel, documentation, and occasionally an office-hours rota. The gaps: the channel has no metrics, so nobody knows the volume, the composition, or the repeat rate; answers given in chat are not retained in any form that answers the question next time; the documentation is not informed by what is actually asked; the questions are a precise specification of where the abstraction is confusing and nobody reads them as one; and the load is invisible to management because it lands as messages to individuals.
