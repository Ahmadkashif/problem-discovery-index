# Federal Offshore G&G Permitting & Data Archive

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/geophysical-survey-firms|Geophysical Survey Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
BOEM permits geological and geophysical surveys on the Outer Continental Shelf, receives the data and processed information, uses it to evaluate lease bids, and releases it after proprietary terms of 10 years for geological data, 25 years for geophysical information and 50 years for geophysical data (30 CFR 550.197). NOAA Fisheries authorises incidental take of marine mammals for the same surveys.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-150 geoscientists in resource evaluation (estimate)
**Output:** Bid-adequacy and fair-market-value assessments, resource assessments, released datasets
**Proprietary data:** The most complete archive of US offshore seismic, held in confidence
**Clock:** Lease sale schedules
**Buyer:** Chief, Resource Evaluation (government)
