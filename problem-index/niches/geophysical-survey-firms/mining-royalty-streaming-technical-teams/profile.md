# Mining Royalty & Streaming Technical Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/geophysical-survey-firms|Geophysical Survey Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Royalty and streaming companies finance mines in return for a share of production, and their technical teams evaluate deposits, exploration upside and the geophysics behind it before pricing a deal.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 geologists and mining engineers per company (estimate)
**Output:** Internal deal evaluations
**Proprietary data:** Operator data rooms received under confidentiality
**Clock:** Deal timelines
**Buyer:** Chief Geologist / Head of Technical
