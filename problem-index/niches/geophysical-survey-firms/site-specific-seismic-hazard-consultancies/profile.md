# Site-Specific Seismic Hazard & Site Characterisation Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/geophysical-survey-firms|Geophysical Survey Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Measure shear-wave velocity profiles with surface-wave, refraction and downhole methods and run probabilistic seismic hazard and site-response analyses for hospitals, bridges, dams, energy facilities and tall buildings where codes require a site-specific analysis. The work sits in earthquake-engineering groups inside geotechnical firms and a few specialist consultancies; owners and their structural engineers pay per site.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-80 seismologists, geophysicists and earthquake engineers per practice (estimate)
**Output:** Site-specific hazard reports, design response spectra, Vs30 and site-class determinations
**Proprietary data:** An archive of measured velocity profiles and site-response studies across thousands of sites, partly client-owned
**Clock:** Design schedules and building-department, DOT or regulator review dates
**Buyer:** Practice Leader, Earthquake Engineering
