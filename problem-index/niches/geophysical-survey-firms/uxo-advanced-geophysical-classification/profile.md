# UXO Advanced Geophysical Classification

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/geophysical-survey-firms|Geophysical Survey Firms]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to cut digs of harmless debris while holding DoD's zero-missed-target line, and whoever delivers the shortest defensible dig list under DAGCAP accreditation takes the munitions response task order.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M US munitions response geophysics and classification within DoD's Military Munitions Response Program, plus offshore wind and civil UXO surveys (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of US geophysical survey spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High — multi-axis time-domain EM sensors, inversion and library matching are digital end to end
**Target Buyer:** Program Manager or Project Geophysicist at a munitions response contractor; USACE or Navy project geophysicist as the client's reviewer
**Automation Potential:** High — the workflow is inversion, library matching and thresholding against a fixed acceptance rule

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Munitions response sites — former ranges and training areas — are cleared by detecting buried metal and digging it. DoD experience is that about 95% of excavated objects are not munitions. Advanced geophysical classification (AGC), developed through SERDP and ESTCP, inverts multi-axis EM responses into polarizabilities and matches them to a library of munitions signatures, so debris can be left in the ground. Organisations performing it must hold DAGCAP accreditation, and the demonstration of capability fails if a single target of interest is missed. The contest is pure: the shortest dig list with zero misses.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Advanced EM sensors (TEMTADS, MetalMapper-class), Seequent UX-Analyze in Oasis montaj for inversion, library matching and ranked dig lists, and DAGCAP quality systems. Gaps: dig thresholds set site by site; prior sites' dig results not pooled; and the classification decision record assembled by hand for QAPP review.
