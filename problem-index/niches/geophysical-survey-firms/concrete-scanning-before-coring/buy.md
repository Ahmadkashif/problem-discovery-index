# Field Service Software With Liability-Grade Scan Records

**Niche:** [[niches/geophysical-survey-firms/concrete-scanning-before-coring/profile|Concrete Scanning Before Cutting & Coring]]
**Industry:** [[industries/geophysical-survey-firms|Geophysical Survey Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Field service platforms dispatch technicians and close tickets; a scanning job also needs the scan files, the marked layout, the client's acknowledgment and the later core result attached to one record that will be read by a lawyer if a tendon is cut.
**Tags:** #cnns #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to mark every tendon, rebar mat and conduit in a slab correctly before the core drill arrives, the same day it is called, and whoever's marks never lead to a cut tendon becomes the contractor's default scanner.

## The Problem
Scanning firms run hundreds of same-day jobs a week. Dispatch is easy; documentation is not. When something is cut, the question is what was scanned, what was marked, what the client was told and whether the core was drilled where marked.

## What Already Exists
ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro and similar field service management platforms handle scheduling, dispatch, invoicing and photos.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: GPR scan files attached to the ticket automatically from the device; marked-layout photos with scale and location; client sign-off on limitations (scan depth, slab type, areas not scannable); a follow-up step to capture core photos and outcomes; and structured job records that feed both insurance renewals and the classifier training described in the build note.

## Target Customer
Operations and risk managers at regional and national scanning firms.

## Impact If Solved
Claims defended from records rather than recollection, and a growing verified dataset as a by-product of normal work.
