# Geological Modelling Adapted to a Six-Hole Company

**Niche:** [[niches/geophysical-survey-firms/junior-mining-exploration-geophysics/profile|Junior Mining Exploration Geophysics]]
**Industry:** [[industries/geophysical-survey-firms|Geophysical Survey Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** 3D geological modelling software is built for resource geologists with dense drilling; a junior with three surveys and six holes needs a targeting workspace that shows uncertainty and produces a record its qualified person can sign.
**Tags:** #gaussian-processes #bayesian-inference #monte-carlo-methods #data-integration #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a ground or airborne survey into a ranked drill-target list that a junior explorer's board will fund and its qualified person will sign, and whoever's targets hit mineralisation most often keeps the claim-holder's next season.

## The Problem
Juniors receive inversion models and anomaly maps from their geophysics contractor and must combine them with sparse geology, geochemistry and historical holes to choose drill sites. Most lack the staff to run modelling software and rely on the contractor's slides.

## What Already Exists
Seequent Leapfrog and Oasis montaj, and comparable 3D modelling and inversion packages, used by majors and consultancies.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: a guided targeting workflow that overlays geophysical inversions, geochemistry and geology with explicit uncertainty; target ranking with the reasons recorded; a disclosure-ready record of data, methods and QA/QC for the qualified person under NI 43-101 or S-K 1300; and pricing and support for a company with no geoscience IT.

## Target Customer
VPs of exploration at junior explorers and the consultants who act as their geoscience department.

## Impact If Solved
Targets chosen with stated uncertainty and documented reasoning, and disclosure that the qualified person can sign without rebuilding the analysis.
