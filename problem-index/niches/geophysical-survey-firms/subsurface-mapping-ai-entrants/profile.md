# AI Subsurface Mapping Entrants

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/geophysical-survey-firms|Geophysical Survey Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Venture-funded companies that combine several geophysical sensors with AI fusion to map utilities and subsurface conditions, and sell the map directly to infrastructure owners rather than through SUE practices. Exodigo raised a $96M Series B in July 2025, $214M in total, and lists Amtrak, Florida DOT, the California High-Speed Rail Authority, Kansas DOT, LA Metro and SEPTA as customers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-150 geophysicists, data scientists and interpretation analysts per company, excluding field crews (estimate)
**Output:** Fused subsurface maps and utility models delivered for design
**Proprietary data:** Multi-sensor datasets joined to excavation verification on customer projects
**Clock:** Owners' design and procurement schedules
**Buyer:** Chief Technology Officer
