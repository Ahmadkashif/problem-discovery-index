# Multi-Client Seismic Data Libraries

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/geophysical-survey-firms|Geophysical Survey Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Acquire seismic surveys at their own risk, usually with prefunding from a few operators, process and reprocess them, and license the same data non-exclusively to many companies. TGS (merged with PGS in July 2024), Viridien's Earth Data business ($406M of 2025 revenue), Seismic Exchange in the US onshore and Pulse Seismic in Canada (C$51.1M of 2025 revenue from a library of about 65,310 km² of 3D and 829,207 km of 2D) are representative. Exploration and new-ventures groups at E&P companies pay per licence; licences are non-assignable and charge transfer or change-of-control fees when licensees merge. Onshore crews such as Dawson Geophysical work mainly for these libraries.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~100-600 imaging geophysicists, geoscientists, data managers and new-ventures analysts at the two large libraries (estimate; TGS headcount not found); regional libraries are whole companies of 10-40 (Pulse Seismic has 15 staff) and fail the gate on their own
**Output:** Processed and reprocessed seismic volumes and derived products — well-tied depth volumes, regional interpretations, attribute studies — licensed by survey or area
**Proprietary data:** Decades of owned surveys and processing vintages, the licensing history by company and area, and, unused, the outcome of every well later drilled through the data, whose logs and formation tops become public through state and federal regulators
**Clock:** Federal and state lease sales and international licensing rounds, operators' annual budget and drilling cycles, and M&A closings that trigger change-of-control fees
**Buyer:** Head of Data Library / Head of Imaging

**Kill switches:** none — the library owns its data outright and its licences restrict licensees, not the owner; no MNPI, privilege or client-owned data enters the core product. Prefunding partners' short exclusivity windows delay sales but do not restrict the library's own use.
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. Licensing data the library already owns is revenue that does not grow with headcount (Pulse Seismic booked C$51.1M in 2025 with 15 staff), so Q2 is 3; and the buyer market is five firms (TGS-PGS, Viridien, Shearwater, SEI, Pulse), so Q5 is 2. The ungraded depth-error record is still the clearest gradable asset in the sweep.
