# Protected Species Observer & Acoustic Monitoring Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/geophysical-survey-firms|Geophysical Survey Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Supply protected species observers and passive acoustic monitoring operators to marine seismic and offshore construction projects, enforce exclusion zones and shutdowns under NOAA incidental take authorisations, and compile the sightings and detection reports the permits require. Seismic contractors and developers pay per vessel-day.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 bioacoustic analysts and reporting staff per provider; observers themselves are field crew (estimate)
**Output:** Real-time mitigation calls and post-survey monitoring reports for the permit holder and regulator
**Proprietary data:** Sightings and acoustic detections across surveys, delivered to clients and regulators
**Clock:** Permit reporting deadlines and real-time shutdown rules
**Buyer:** Director, Marine Mammal Services
