# Induced Seismicity & Storage Monitoring Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/geophysical-survey-firms|Geophysical Survey Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Operate dense seismic arrays around produced-water disposal wells, hydraulic fracturing, geothermal projects and carbon storage sites; locate and size events; and report to operators and regulators under traffic-light protocols. In Texas, the Railroad Commission's seismic response areas rely on USGS, TexNet and private operator stations, and the agency warns of a 12–18 month lag between injection cuts and quieter ground. Operators pay for the monitoring.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 seismologists and analysts per provider (estimate)
**Output:** Event catalogues, magnitude alerts within regulator response windows, periodic monitoring and storage-site reports
**Proprietary data:** Private array recordings and event catalogues joined to injection volumes and pressures
**Clock:** Traffic-light thresholds, regulator reporting windows and carbon-storage monitoring plans
**Buyer:** Chief Seismologist / VP Monitoring

**Kill switches:** data isn't theirs to use — where the provider runs arrays an operator paid for, the recordings are the operator's, so the cross-operator waveform archive an engine would need cannot be reused without each operator's consent; the published catalogues are public and add no moat.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. A strong clock and a real analytical product, but the valuable corpus belongs to the operators and the buyer pool is the operators inside a few regulated areas.
