# Geophysical Societies & Contractor Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/geophysical-survey-firms|Geophysical Survey Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Society of Exploration Geophysicists maintains standards such as SEG-Y and publishes research; the International Association of Geophysical Contractors represents contractors on regulation and marine science; the Environmental and Engineering Geophysical Society serves near-surface practitioners.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research or standards staff per body (estimate)
**Output:** Standards, journals, conferences, advocacy
**Proprietary data:** Member and publication archives
**Clock:** Conference and regulatory comment cycles
**Buyer:** Executive Director
