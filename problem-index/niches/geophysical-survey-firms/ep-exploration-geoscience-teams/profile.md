# E&P Exploration Geoscience Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/geophysical-survey-firms|Geophysical Survey Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Geophysicists and geologists at oil and gas companies who commission proprietary surveys, license multi-client data, interpret it and recommend leases and wells. They are the industry's largest buyer and its most sophisticated interpreters.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Tens to hundreds per large operator (estimate)
**Output:** Prospect, lease and well recommendations, used internally
**Proprietary data:** Proprietary surveys and well results, plus licensed library data
**Clock:** Lease sales, drilling commitments and budget cycles
**Buyer:** VP Exploration / Chief Geophysicist
