# USGS Earth MRI & State Geological Survey Geophysics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/geophysical-survey-firms|Geophysical Survey Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The USGS Earth Mapping Resources Initiative contracts airborne magnetic and radiometric surveys over critical-mineral regions with state geological surveys — Missouri, Illinois, Iowa and Wisconsin from late 2025, eastern Maine and the Blue Mountains, New Mexico in 2026 — and publishes the data free. Taxpayers fund it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-300 geophysicists and geologists across USGS and state survey programs (estimate)
**Output:** Public geophysical datasets, maps and mineral-potential assessments
**Proprietary data:** The national airborne geophysical archive
**Clock:** Appropriation and program cycles
**Buyer:** Program Coordinator (government)
