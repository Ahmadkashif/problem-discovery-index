# Mining Geophysical Interpretation Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/geophysical-survey-firms|Geophysical Survey Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Independent consultancies that design surveys, run inversions and interpret geophysics for mining companies, often acting as a junior explorer's outsourced geophysics department. Clients pay by the project or the day.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-80 geophysicists per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Interpretation reports, 3D inversion models and drill-target recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Project archives of client surveys and the drill results that tested their targets
**Clock:** Drill seasons and disclosure dates
**Buyer:** Managing Director / Principal Geophysicist
