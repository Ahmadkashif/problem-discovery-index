# Seismic Processing & Imaging

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/geophysical-survey-firms|Geophysical Survey Firms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to deliver a depth image whose velocity model ties the wells and that the client's interpreters trust, before the lease sale or drilling decision, and whoever gets there with the fewest re-runs takes the reprocessing work.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600M US-serving seismic processing, reprocessing and imaging (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~25% of US geophysical survey spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Very High — the work is entirely compute and screens
**Target Buyer:** Processing Manager or Chief Geophysicist at an imaging house; Geophysics Manager at the client
**Automation Potential:** High for picking, editing and QC; low for velocity model building and the judgement of when an image is good enough

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Processing turns recorded traces into an image: geometry, noise attenuation, statics, velocity analysis, migration, and increasingly full-waveform inversion. The contest is the image, not the field — Viridien's Geoscience segment alone reported $444M of 2025 revenue — and reprocessing old surveys with new algorithms is a large share of the work, timed to lease sales, licensing rounds and drilling programs. The winner is whoever delivers a trustworthy depth image fastest; the crew that recorded the data is irrelevant to that contest.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Proprietary processing systems at Viridien, TGS, Shearwater (Reveal) and DUG, cloud and on-premise HPC, and Petrel or Kingdom on the client side for interpretation. Gaps: velocity model building still rests on a senior processor's edits, which are kept only as successive model versions with no reasons attached; processing flows and parameters are poorly recorded, so reprocessing years later starts from scratch; and test panels are compared by eye, panel against panel, with no written basis for the choice. (Whether delivered images held up against wells drilled later is a library-side record; see [[niches/geophysical-survey-firms/multi-client-seismic-data-libraries/profile|Multi-Client Seismic Data Libraries]].)
