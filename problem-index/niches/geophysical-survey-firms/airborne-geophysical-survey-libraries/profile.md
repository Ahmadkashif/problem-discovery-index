# Airborne Geophysical Survey & Data Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/geophysical-survey-firms|Geophysical Survey Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Fly fixed-wing and helicopter magnetic, radiometric, gravity and electromagnetic surveys for mining companies and government programs such as USGS Earth MRI, and in some cases acquire and license multi-client airborne data. Mining companies and governments pay per line-kilometre or per licence.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 processing geophysicists and interpreters per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Processed airborne grids, inversions and interpretation reports; multi-client licences
**Proprietary data:** An archive of flown surveys, a minority owned outright for multi-client licensing
**Clock:** Exploration seasons and government contract deadlines
**Buyer:** Chief Geophysicist

**Kill switches:** data isn't theirs to use — contract surveys are assigned to the commissioning mining company under confidentiality, and government surveys become public; only the minority flown for multi-client licensing is the firm's own, so the cross-survey archive an engine would need cannot be reused without each client's consent.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch.
