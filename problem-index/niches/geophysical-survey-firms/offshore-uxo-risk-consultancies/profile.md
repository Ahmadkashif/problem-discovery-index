# Offshore & Civil UXO Risk Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/geophysical-survey-firms|Geophysical Survey Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Produce unexploded-ordnance desk studies and risk assessments before construction — archive research into wartime bombing, minefields, munitions dumping and former ranges — then review geophysical survey targets, recommend which to investigate, and certify that risk has been reduced to as low as reasonably practicable before cables, piles or foundations go in. The specialist firms are UK-centred and serve offshore wind developers and cable installers; in the US, munitions and environmental contractors produce similar studies for formerly used defense sites and civil projects. Developers and installers pay per project.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-120 historians, UXO specialists and geophysicists per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Desk studies, risk assessments, target-investigation recommendations and risk-mitigation sign-off certificates
**Proprietary data:** Archive research libraries of historical bombing, range and dumping records, plus the outcomes of past target investigations — how many survey targets turned out to be ordnance — which mostly sit in client-owned survey reports
**Clock:** Marine licence and consent conditions, cable-lay and foundation installation windows
**Buyer:** Technical Director / Head of UXO Risk
