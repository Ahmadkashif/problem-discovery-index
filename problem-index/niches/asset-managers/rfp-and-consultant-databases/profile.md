# RFP & Consultant Database Responses

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/asset-managers|Asset Managers]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to answer each consultant question correctly for the exact strategy, vehicle and quarter, consistent with everything previously submitted — and whoever does that clears the consultant screen faster and without the inconsistency that disqualifies a manager.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.5–2.5B estimated annual global spend on RFP, consultant-relations and database-maintenance staff and tools at asset managers
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of distribution cost (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — answer libraries plus copy-paste and manual tie-out
**Target Buyer:** Head of Consultant Relations / Head of RFP
**Automation Potential:** Very High — repeated questions on structured data

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Institutional mandates are gated by investment consultants and their databases. The work is large, repetitive and deadline-driven, and its failure mode — a number that contradicts a database profile or a prior answer — costs mandates. Its buyer is the distribution side of the firm, not the investment side.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Loopio, Responsive and Qvidian libraries; eVestment and other consultant database upload portals; performance data feeds. The gaps: answers not modelled by strategy, vehicle and date; no consistency checking across consultants; numeric answers re-keyed rather than sourced. The buyer-side mirror of this work sits with consultants and wealth platforms — see [[niches/wealth-management-rias/manager-research-due-diligence/profile|Third-Party Manager Research & Due Diligence]].
