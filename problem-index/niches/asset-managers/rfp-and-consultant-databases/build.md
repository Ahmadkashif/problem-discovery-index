# One Source of Truth for What the Firm Has Told Every Consultant

**Niche:** [[niches/asset-managers/rfp-and-consultant-databases/profile|RFP & Consultant Database Responses]]
**Industry:** [[industries/asset-managers|Asset Managers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A manager describes its process, team and risk controls to dozens of consultants a year and has no record of which version each one received.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #word-embeddings #k-nearest-neighbors #evaluation-metrics #compliance #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to answer each consultant question correctly for the exact strategy, vehicle and quarter, consistent with everything previously submitted — and whoever does that clears the consultant screen faster and without the inconsistency that disqualifies a manager.

## The Problem
Consultants compare managers' answers over time and against database profiles. A changed description of the investment process, an inconsistent headcount, or a performance number on a different basis triggers questions or quiet disqualification. Managers answer each RFP from the library and the most recent similar response, and nobody checks the whole set.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
RFP tools are horizontal and treat each response as a separate project. Consultant databases are separate portals. Performance systems are owned by operations. The join is nobody's job.

## What to Build
A submission ledger: every answer submitted to every consultant and database, stored with strategy, vehicle, date and recipient, searchable semantically. New drafts are checked against it automatically for material divergence, numeric answers are filled from the book of record with their source, and changes in the firm's narrative are made deliberately and propagated, not discovered by a consultant.

## Target Customer
Heads of consultant relations, RFP and institutional marketing at asset managers.

## Impact If Built
Inconsistency risk falls sharply, RFP turnaround compresses, and the firm controls its own story across consultants.
