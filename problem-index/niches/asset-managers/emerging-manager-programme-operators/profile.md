# Emerging Manager Programme Operators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/asset-managers|Asset Managers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Manager-of-managers firms that run emerging and diverse manager programmes for public pensions — for example Attucks Asset Management and Xponance — selecting, monitoring and allocating to small managers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically under 10 manager research staff per firm
**Output:** Manager selection and monitoring for programme clients
**Proprietary data:** Due diligence files on small managers
**Clock:** Client programme reviews
**Buyer:** Chief Investment Officer

Not scored — fails the gate. Manager research teams at these firms are typically below ten people, so the research function is too small for the engine.
