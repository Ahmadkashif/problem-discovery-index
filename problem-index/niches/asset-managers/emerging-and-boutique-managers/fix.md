# The COO Who Writes Every RFP

**Niche:** [[niches/asset-managers/emerging-and-boutique-managers/profile|Emerging & Boutique Managers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/asset-managers|Asset Managers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** At a twenty-person manager the COO answers RFPs, refreshes consultant databases and runs operations, and the RFPs win.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #word-embeddings #worker-facing #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give a sub-$10B manager institutional-grade evidence — clean track record, consultant-ready answers, credible risk and research process — without an institutional-sized operations team, and whoever does that gets boutiques through consultant screens that currently filter them out on infrastructure, not performance.

## The Problem
Each RFP takes days of the COO's time, competing with operations, compliance and hiring. Quarterly database updates slip.

## Why It's Still Broken
RFP library tools are priced and designed for teams; a boutique's library is last year's responses in a folder.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A drafting assistant over the firm's past responses and documents, filling numbers from the administrator's data, at boutique pricing, with consistency checks against database profiles.

## Who Feels the Pain
COOs and founders of small managers.

## Impact If Fixed
Days per RFP fall to hours, and database profiles stay current.
