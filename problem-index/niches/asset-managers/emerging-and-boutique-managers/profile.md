# Emerging & Boutique Managers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/asset-managers|Asset Managers]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give a sub-$10B manager institutional-grade evidence — clean track record, consultant-ready answers, credible risk and research process — without an institutional-sized operations team, and whoever does that gets boutiques through consultant screens that currently filter them out on infrastructure, not performance.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1–2B estimated annual spend on outsourced operations, data and tools by small independent asset managers
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of industry technology and operations spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — outsourced platforms, thin internal tooling
**Target Buyer:** Founder / Chief Operating Officer
**Automation Potential:** High — the evidence-production work is the same as at large firms

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Independent boutiques, often founded by PMs leaving large firms, run a handful of strategies with ten to fifty people. They must clear the same consultant due diligence and RFP processes as giants, maintain GIPS-compliant composites, and produce the same reports, without the teams. Many sell through sub-advisory relationships or emerging-manager programmes. Enterprise platforms like Aladdin are priced for scale; their operations are outsourced and stitched together.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Outsourced trading and middle office, fund administrators, cloud order management, consultant database portals, Excel. The gaps: no research record to evidence process; manual RFP and database work by the COO; performance and composite maintenance outsourced at cost.
