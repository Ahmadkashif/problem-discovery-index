# Startup Compliance Automation

**Niche:** [[niches/asset-managers/emerging-and-boutique-managers/profile|Emerging & Boutique Managers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/asset-managers|Asset Managers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Software startups obtain SOC 2 by connecting a compliance-automation tool to their systems; boutique managers answer operational due diligence by hand.
**Tags:** #data-integration #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation #large-language-models
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give a sub-$10B manager institutional-grade evidence — clean track record, consultant-ready answers, credible risk and research process — without an institutional-sized operations team, and whoever does that gets boutiques through consultant screens that currently filter them out on infrastructure, not performance.

## The Problem
Operational due diligence asks about cybersecurity, business continuity, vendor oversight, trade errors, valuation and compliance testing. A boutique answers from policies and memory.

## What Already Exists
Compliance automation platforms for software companies continuously collect evidence from cloud systems and map it to control frameworks, producing audit-ready reports.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: (1) investment-adviser control frameworks — SEC compliance programme rule, custody, code of ethics, trade allocation; (2) evidence from outsourced providers (administrator, outsourced trading, custodian) rather than in-house systems; (3) mapping to consultant ODD questionnaires; (4) GIPS composite maintenance evidence; and (5) pricing for ten-person firms.

## Target Customer
COOs and CCOs of boutique managers.

## Impact If Solved
Operational due diligence becomes a report the firm generates rather than an exam it crams for.
