# Fund Administrator & Custodian Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/asset-managers|Asset Managers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Custodians and fund administrators — State Street, BNY, Northern Trust, J.P. Morgan, Citi and specialist administrators — sell data, performance and analytics services on top of their servicing of asset managers' funds.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-2,000 data and analytics staff across the main banks
**Output:** Performance and risk services, data management, investor analytics
**Proprietary data:** Servicing data across a large share of the industry's assets
**Clock:** Daily NAV and month-end reporting deadlines
**Buyer:** Head of Data & Analytics Services
