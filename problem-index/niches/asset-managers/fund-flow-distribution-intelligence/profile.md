# Fund Flow & Distribution Intelligence Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/asset-managers|Asset Managers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Sell asset managers data on where their and competitors' products are sold — flows by channel, intermediary and region — used by distribution, sales and product teams. ISS Market Intelligence (including Simfund), Broadridge's distribution data business and Morningstar flow data are the main sources.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 analysts and data staff
**Output:** Flow datasets, market-share reports, sales intelligence tools
**Proprietary data:** Transaction and flow data assembled from intermediaries and filings
**Clock:** Monthly flow releases and clients' quarterly sales planning
**Buyer:** Head of Market Intelligence
