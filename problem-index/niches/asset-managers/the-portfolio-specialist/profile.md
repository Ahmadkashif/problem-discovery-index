# The Portfolio Specialist

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/asset-managers|Asset Managers]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce a PM-approved, number-accurate performance narrative for every strategy within days of quarter-end — and whoever does that gets commentary to clients first, without errors, while competitors are still chasing PM sign-off.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2–3B estimated annual global spend on portfolio specialists, investment writers and client reporting staff at asset managers
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~4% of distribution and client-service cost (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — attribution exported to Excel, narrative written in Word, reviewed by email
**Target Buyer:** Head of Product Specialists / Head of Client Reporting
**Automation Potential:** High — grounded drafting and derivative formats

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The portfolio specialist translates the investment team to clients: quarterly commentary, client meetings, finals presentations, reports. The role is squeezed between PMs whose time is scarce, compliance whose edits are mandatory, and clients who want the story fast. It is served by no tool built for it; performance systems produce numbers and document tools produce layouts.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Performance and attribution systems (FactSet PA, Bloomberg PORT, platform modules), Word, PowerPoint, fact sheet engines, content management for compliance review. The gaps: no grounded drafting; no single master narrative feeding derivative documents; PM review cycles by email.
