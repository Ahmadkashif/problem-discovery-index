# A Master Narrative Bound to the Numbers

**Niche:** [[niches/asset-managers/the-portfolio-specialist/profile|The Portfolio Specialist]]
**Industry:** [[industries/asset-managers|Asset Managers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Each quarter one performance story is rewritten by hand into a dozen documents, and any of them can drift from the attribution it describes.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #evaluation-metrics #descriptive-statistics #worker-facing #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce a PM-approved, number-accurate performance narrative for every strategy within days of quarter-end — and whoever does that gets commentary to clients first, without errors, while competitors are still chasing PM sign-off.

## The Problem
The specialist writes the commentary once and then rewrites it for the fact sheet, the client report, the database narrative, the website, the shareholder report and the pitch book, at different lengths. Late changes to attribution or PM edits must be propagated manually.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Document tools do not understand financial claims; performance systems do not generate narrative; and generative drafting without grounding is unacceptable under the marketing rule.

## What to Build
A master narrative in which every quantitative claim is bound to a source figure; drafts generated from attribution, trades and research rationales; claim-level verification; and derivative documents generated from the master with traceable cuts, so a change to the master flows everywhere.

## Target Customer
Heads of product specialists, investment writing and client reporting.

## Impact If Built
Commentary cycle time falls from weeks to days and numerical errors in client documents become rare.
