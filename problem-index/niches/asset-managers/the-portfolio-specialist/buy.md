# Newsroom Data Journalism Tooling

**Niche:** [[niches/asset-managers/the-portfolio-specialist/profile|The Portfolio Specialist]]
**Industry:** [[industries/asset-managers|Asset Managers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Newsrooms automated earnings and sports stories from structured data years ago; asset managers still hand-write performance stories from attribution tables.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #seq2seq #evaluation-metrics #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce a PM-approved, number-accurate performance narrative for every strategy within days of quarter-end — and whoever does that gets commentary to clients first, without errors, while competitors are still chasing PM sign-off.

## The Problem
Performance commentary has a regular structure — what happened, what helped, what hurt, what changed — that recurs every quarter for every strategy.

## What Already Exists
News organisations have long generated routine earnings and results stories from structured data feeds with templates, and now with language models and fact-checking against the feed.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: (1) attribution methodologies (Brinson, factor) as the data model; (2) the PM's rationale, which is not in any feed, as an input; (3) marketing-rule and compliance constraints; (4) a house voice per strategy; and (5) review by the PM as a mandatory step.

## Target Customer
Investment writing and client reporting teams.

## Impact If Solved
The structural part of commentary is generated, and human time goes to the investment view.
