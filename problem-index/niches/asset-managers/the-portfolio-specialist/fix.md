# Waiting for the PM

**Niche:** [[niches/asset-managers/the-portfolio-specialist/profile|The Portfolio Specialist]]
**Industry:** [[industries/asset-managers|Asset Managers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Commentary sits in a PM's inbox for days because reviewing a blank-page draft is a writing task, not a review.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #workflow-orchestration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce a PM-approved, number-accurate performance narrative for every strategy within days of quarter-end — and whoever does that gets commentary to clients first, without errors, while competitors are still chasing PM sign-off.

## The Problem
The specialist's draft goes to a PM who disagrees with the framing and rewrites it, or does not get to it. The specialist chases while other strategies queue.

## Why It's Still Broken
The draft asks the PM to read everything rather than answer the few judgment questions only the PM can answer.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Send the PM a short set of explicit questions — "was the overweight in X a deliberate call?", "outlook: more or less cautious than last quarter?" — with proposed answers drawn from trades and notes, and fold the answers into a grounded draft. Review time drops to minutes.

## Who Feels the Pain
Portfolio specialists and investment writers; PMs interrupted at quarter-end.

## Impact If Fixed
The bottleneck shifts from the PM's inbox to a five-minute review.
