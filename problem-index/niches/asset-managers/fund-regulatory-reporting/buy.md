# Pharmaceutical Labelling Change Control

**Niche:** [[niches/asset-managers/fund-regulatory-reporting/profile|Fund Regulatory Reporting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/asset-managers|Asset Managers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Drug makers keep every product label consistent with a core data sheet across markets under change control; fund complexes keep prospectuses and reports consistent by review.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #compliance #workflow-orchestration #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make every figure in every filed fund document traceable to the book of record and correct under the specific rule that requires it — and whoever does that turns the annual reporting crunch into exception review.

## The Problem
A fee change, a new share class, or a portfolio manager change must be reflected across prospectus, statement of additional information, shareholder reports, fact sheets and websites, in multiple jurisdictions.

## What Already Exists
Regulated labelling management in life sciences tracks a core reference document, the downstream labels derived from it, change requests, impact assessment and approval workflow.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: (1) fund data as the core reference rather than a clinical document; (2) SEC and UCITS document types and their filing calendars; (3) share-class granularity; (4) annual update cycles plus ad hoc supplements; and (5) language-model detection of statements in narrative sections that have become stale after a change.

## Target Customer
Fund legal, reporting and product governance teams.

## Impact If Solved
Document consistency becomes a controlled process with an audit trail.
