# Lineage From Every Printed Figure to the Book of Record

**Niche:** [[niches/asset-managers/fund-regulatory-reporting/profile|Fund Regulatory Reporting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/asset-managers|Asset Managers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Thousands of numbers appear in a fund complex's filings each year, and for most of them nobody can show in one click where they came from.
**Tags:** #data-integration #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make every figure in every filed fund document traceable to the book of record and correct under the specific rule that requires it — and whoever does that turns the annual reporting crunch into exception review.

## The Problem
When an examiner, auditor or board asks why a figure in a shareholder report differs from the fact sheet, the reporting team reconstructs the calculation from extracts and spreadsheets.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Document vendors bind data at the field level but treat the upstream as a black box; administrators own the accounting but not the documents.

## What to Build
A figure registry: every published number stored with its source query, data snapshot, rule reference and the documents it appears in; validation rules expressed per regulation; and an impact view showing all documents affected when a source changes.

## Target Customer
Fund treasurers and heads of fund reporting at US and European fund complexes.

## Impact If Built
Tie-out becomes automatic, corrections propagate reliably, and audit questions are answered in minutes.
