# The Late Correction

**Niche:** [[niches/asset-managers/fund-regulatory-reporting/profile|Fund Regulatory Reporting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/asset-managers|Asset Managers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A corrected accrual three days before the filing deadline means finding every document the old figure reached, by hand.
**Tags:** #data-integration #descriptive-statistics #compliance #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make every figure in every filed fund document traceable to the book of record and correct under the specific rule that requires it — and whoever does that turns the annual reporting crunch into exception review.

## The Problem
Late corrections from the administrator or the performance team are routine in reporting season. The team searches documents for the old number, misses one, and a filing carries inconsistent figures.

## Why It's Still Broken
Documents are bound to data through templates per vendor, and no single map records which figures feed which documents.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Even before full lineage, a dependency map built by scanning drafts for figures and matching them to source extracts lets the team see every affected document when a value changes.

## Who Feels the Pain
Fund reporting analysts in filing season; treasurers signing certifications.

## Impact If Fixed
Late corrections stop producing inconsistent filings.
