# Fund Regulatory Reporting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/asset-managers|Asset Managers]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make every figure in every filed fund document traceable to the book of record and correct under the specific rule that requires it — and whoever does that turns the annual reporting crunch into exception review.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3–5B estimated annual global spend on fund document production, regulatory reporting vendors and staff
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of operations cost (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — data feeds into templates, validation by people
**Target Buyer:** Head of Fund Reporting / Fund Treasurer
**Automation Potential:** Very High — same data, many documents, explicit rules

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Fund complexes publish tailored shareholder reports, N-PORT, N-CSR, N-CEN, prospectus updates, fact sheets, KIDs and SFDR disclosures, and N-PX vote records. The rules are explicit, the data is shared, and errors are public. The buyer is the fund treasurer and reporting function, a different budget from investment or distribution.

## Current Tools & Gaps
DFIN Arc suite, Broadridge, Workiva, fund administrator reporting, Kurtosys and other fact sheet engines. The gaps: figure-level lineage; rule-aware validation; change propagation across documents; narrative-to-number consistency.
