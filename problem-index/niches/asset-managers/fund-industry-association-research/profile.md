# Fund Industry Association Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/asset-managers|Asset Managers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Industry associations publish data and research on funds and asset management — the Investment Company Institute's research department and Fact Book in the US, EFAMA in Europe, the CFA Institute on standards including GIPS, and SIFMA's asset management group.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 economists and research staff at the largest associations
**Output:** Annual fact books, flow statistics, policy research, comment letters
**Proprietary data:** Member-submitted fund data series
**Clock:** Regulatory comment deadlines and annual publication cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Economist / Head of Research
