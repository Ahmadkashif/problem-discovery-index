# Credit Rating Agency Analytical Governance

**Niche:** [[niches/asset-managers/esg-ratings-providers/profile|ESG Ratings & Sustainability Research Providers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/asset-managers|Asset Managers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Credit rating agencies have operated under methodology, conflict and committee rules for over a decade; ESG raters are now entering a similar regime with analyst processes built for a lighter one.
**Tags:** #evaluation-metrics #hypothesis-testing #descriptive-statistics #compliance #workflow-orchestration #large-language-models
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this pocket is fighting to show that its ratings measure something real and are produced by a documented, consistently applied method — and whoever can evidence that, issuer by issuer, keeps its asset-manager clients through the EU authorisation regime and the political backlash against ESG labels.

## The Problem
ESG providers rate thousands of issuers with analyst teams applying methodologies that change frequently. Consistency across analysts and over time is hard to evidence, and conflicts — such as selling consulting to rated companies — need separation that regulators will now examine.

## What Already Exists
Credit rating agencies, supervised in the EU under the CRA Regulation, run documented methodologies with periodic review, rating committees, separation of commercial and analytical functions, rating histories, and statistical performance disclosure.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: (1) far more issuers per analyst and more data-driven scoring than credit ratings, so governance must sample and test rather than committee every rating; (2) separate E, S and G outputs, as the EU regulation requires where an aggregate is not disclosed with its weights; (3) analyst-consistency testing — do two analysts score the same evidence the same way — using language models to compare rationale texts; (4) methodology version control linked to every issued rating; and (5) issuer-data verification workflows at scale.

## Target Customer
Heads of methodology, compliance and analytical quality at ESG ratings providers.

## Impact If Solved
A defensible, evidenced analytical process ready for supervision, built from practice a neighbouring regulated industry already proved.
