# ESG Ratings & Sustainability Research Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/asset-managers|Asset Managers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this pocket is fighting to show that its ratings measure something real and are produced by a documented, consistently applied method — and whoever can evidence that, issuer by issuer, keeps its asset-manager clients through the EU authorisation regime and the political backlash against ESG labels.

## What They Do
Rate thousands of listed issuers and funds on environmental, social and governance risk and publish controversy research, carbon and climate data, and fund-level sustainability scores. MSCI ESG Research, Morningstar Sustainalytics, ISS ESG and S&P Global's sustainability business are the large players. Asset managers pay for these ratings to build ESG-labelled funds, run exclusions, meet SFDR and client reporting, and inform stewardship.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-3,000 ESG analysts, controversy researchers, data operations staff and methodologists per large provider
**Output:** Issuer ESG and risk ratings, controversy assessments, climate and carbon datasets, fund sustainability scores, methodology documents
**Proprietary data:** Years of issuer-level ratings history, analyst assessments and controversy records, plus issuer feedback correspondence — the analytical layer exists nowhere else, although most inputs are public disclosures
**Clock:** EU Regulation 2024/3005 on ESG rating activities applies from 2 July 2026, with authorisation applications due by early November 2026; plus client SFDR periodic-reporting cycles and annual issuer review calendars
**Buyer:** Head of ESG Research / Chief Methodology Officer
