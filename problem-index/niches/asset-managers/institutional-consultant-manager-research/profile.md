# Institutional Investment Consultant Manager Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/asset-managers|Asset Managers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Research and rate asset managers for pension funds, endowments, foundations and insurers, and run the searches through which institutional mandates are awarded — Mercer, Aon, WTW, Callan, NEPC, Meketa, Cambridge Associates and others. This is the institutional buyer side of the asset manager's RFP work; the wealth-platform equivalent is [[niches/wealth-management-rias/manager-research-due-diligence/profile|Third-Party Manager Research & Due Diligence]], and the two share a method.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-800 manager research analysts and operational due diligence specialists per large consultant
**Output:** Manager ratings and buy lists, search reports, operational due diligence reports, watch and terminate recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Meeting notes, ratings and due diligence records on thousands of strategies over decades, joined to subsequent manager performance
**Clock:** Client investment committee calendars and search deadlines
**Buyer:** Global Head of Manager Research
