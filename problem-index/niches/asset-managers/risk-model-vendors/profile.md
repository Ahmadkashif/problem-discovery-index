# Risk Model & Factor Research Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/asset-managers|Asset Managers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Build and sell the factor risk models and portfolio analytics used by asset managers — MSCI Barra, SimCorp Axioma, Northfield, Bloomberg PORT.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-200 quantitative researchers per vendor
**Output:** Factor risk models, covariance forecasts, optimisers, risk analytics
**Proprietary data:** Long histories of factor returns and model forecasts
**Clock:** Model update and release schedules set by the vendor
**Buyer:** Head of Model Research
