# Investment Risk & Portfolio Construction

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/asset-managers|Asset Managers]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make the ex-ante risk forecast something a portfolio manager believes — explaining why realised tracking error and drawdowns differed from the model — and whoever does that becomes the risk system PMs actually consult before trading.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$6–8B estimated annual global spend on investment risk models, analytics platforms and risk staff at asset managers
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of investment-function technology and staff cost (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High — factor models and risk platforms are standard
**Target Buyer:** Chief Risk Officer / Head of Investment Risk
**Automation Potential:** High — reporting, explanation and limit monitoring

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every active portfolio is constructed against a benchmark and a risk budget. Risk teams run factor models from MSCI Barra, SimCorp Axioma, Northfield or Bloomberg PORT, or the risk layer inside Aladdin, to forecast tracking error, decompose exposures and stress portfolios, and they report to PMs, the CIO, boards and clients. The contested ground is not the model — the leading models are broadly comparable — but whether PMs trust and use the forecast, which depends on explaining the gap between forecast and realised risk.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Commercial factor models, platform risk modules, in-house Python analytics, liquidity risk tooling for the SEC liquidity rule, and derivatives risk tooling for Rule 18f-4. The gaps: forecast-versus-realised reviews done ad hoc; risk reports that list exposures without saying which matter; and risk analysts who spend most of their time producing reports PMs skim.
