# Fund Board 15(c) Reporting Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/asset-managers|Asset Managers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Produce the independent fee, expense and performance comparisons that US mutual fund boards review each year when approving advisory contracts under Section 15(c) of the Investment Company Act. Broadridge, which acquired Morningstar's 15(c) board consulting business in 2017, is the leading provider; independent consultants and law firms also serve boards.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 analysts constructing peer groups and preparing board materials
**Output:** Annual 15(c) peer group analyses of fees, expenses and performance, and board presentations
**Proprietary data:** Fund expense and performance databases plus a history of custom peer group constructions
**Clock:** Annual board contract-renewal meetings for each fund complex
**Buyer:** Head of Fiduciary / Board Services
