# Securities Regulators — Asset Management Supervision

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/asset-managers|Asset Managers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The SEC's Division of Investment Management and Division of Examinations, FINRA for distribution, and the FCA, ESMA and national regulators abroad, analyse fund filings and supervise managers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-3,000 analysts, examiners and economists across the main US and European bodies
**Output:** Rulemaking analysis, examination findings, risk monitoring of filings such as N-PORT
**Proprietary data:** Regulatory filings and examination records
**Clock:** Statutory and rulemaking calendars
**Buyer:** Division Director
