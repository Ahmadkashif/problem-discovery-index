# Clinical Decision Support Practice

**Niche:** [[niches/asset-managers/fundamental-equity-research/profile|Fundamental Equity Research]]
**Industry:** [[industries/asset-managers|Asset Managers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Medicine learned to build tools that support an expert's judgment without replacing it and to evaluate them against outcomes; equity research needs the same discipline.
**Tags:** #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #hypothesis-testing #causal-inference #tacit-knowledge-ml #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to capture and grade the analyst's read of management before the numbers confirm it — and whoever does that turns the most expensive judgment on the research floor into something the firm keeps when the analyst leaves.

## The Problem
Every attempt to put a model in front of a portfolio manager meets the same objection: investment judgment cannot be reduced to a score. The objection is partly right and is used to block measurement entirely.

## What Already Exists
Clinical decision support has a mature practice for exactly this tension: alerts designed around clinician workflow, explicit handling of alert fatigue, prospective evaluation against outcomes, documentation of when the expert overrode the tool and what happened, and reader studies comparing experts with and without assistance.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: (1) outcomes that are noisy and market-confounded, so evaluation must be factor-adjusted and framed as information content rather than accuracy; (2) an adversarial element, since management teams adapt their language once tools that parse it are widespread; (3) information-barrier constraints on what the tool may show to whom; (4) a far smaller number of decisions per expert than in medicine, which widens every confidence interval; and (5) override logging that records the PM's reasoning without becoming a compliance burden.

## Target Customer
Directors of research and investment risk teams responsible for research process quality.

## Impact If Solved
A borrowed evaluation discipline lets the firm introduce assistance without asking the floor to accept a scorecard on faith.
