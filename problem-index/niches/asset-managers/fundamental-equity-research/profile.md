# Fundamental Equity Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/asset-managers|Asset Managers]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to capture and grade the analyst's read of management before the numbers confirm it — and whoever does that turns the most expensive judgment on the research floor into something the firm keeps when the analyst leaves.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$9–12B estimated annual global spend on long-only equity research staff, data and tools
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~18% of active-manager investment cost (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — transcripts and terminals digitised, judgment not
**Target Buyer:** Director of Equity Research / Head of Equities
**Automation Potential:** High for monitoring and recall; low for the judgment itself

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
An equity analyst's edge is a forecast of upside built from earnings models and, crucially, from reading people: management credibility, tone shifts, strategic drift. Every listed company produces the same public inputs — filings, transcripts, investor days — for every competitor; what differs is the analyst's interpretation and the meetings she has had. The contest is therefore over capturing and grading that interpretation, which no data vendor can sell because it is generated inside the firm.

## Current Tools & Gaps
AlphaSense and terminal transcript search with AI summaries, research management systems, Visible Alpha consensus models, Excel. The gaps: AI summarises what was said, not how it differs from what this analyst expected; post-meeting judgments are unrecorded; and no record links reads of management to subsequent outcomes.
