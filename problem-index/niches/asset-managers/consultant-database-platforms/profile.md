# Consultant Database & Manager Search Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/asset-managers|Asset Managers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Collect manager-supplied strategy data — performance, holdings, characteristics, team and process — and sell analysis and search tools to consultants and asset owners. eVestment (part of Nasdaq) is the largest independent database; several consultants run their own.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 data operations, research and product staff
**Output:** Manager and strategy databases, peer universes, search and analytics tools
**Proprietary data:** Manager-submitted data histories across tens of thousands of strategies
**Clock:** Quarterly manager submission deadlines
**Buyer:** Head of Data / Head of Product
