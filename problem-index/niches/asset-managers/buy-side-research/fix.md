# The Coverage Handover Is a Folder

**Niche:** [[niches/asset-managers/buy-side-research/profile|Buy-Side Research]]
**Industry:** [[industries/asset-managers|Asset Managers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** When an analyst leaves, the successor gets a list of tickers, a shared drive and a two-week notice period, and the firm's accumulated view of forty companies is reconstructed from scratch.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #tacit-knowledge-ml #worker-facing #quick-win #data-integration
**Contested on:** This niche is not terminal — an equity analyst forecasting upside from management and a credit analyst underwriting downside from documents are different contests with different winners, and they are stated separately in the sub-niches.

## The Problem
Buy-side analysts move between firms routinely, and a departing analyst typically leaves within days, sometimes on garden leave immediately. What remains is a set of models of varying quality, notes in whatever system the analyst used, and the PM's memory. The successor spends their first quarter re-meeting management teams and rebuilding views the firm already held, during which coverage is effectively thin.

## Why It's Still Broken
Nobody owns handover as a process. Documentation discipline is left to individuals, and the analysts whose notes are worst are often the most senior. The judgment that matters most — which management teams have earned credibility, where the thesis is fragile — was never written down at all.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A standing coverage dossier per company, generated continuously from the analyst's notes, models and recommendations: current thesis, history of rating changes with reasons, management credibility record, open questions, and what would change the view. The analyst confirms or edits it quarterly in minutes rather than writing it. When anyone leaves, the dossier is already current. The structured fields double as the tacit-knowledge labels the sub-niches need.

## Who Feels the Pain
Successor analysts, PMs who lose coverage at the worst moment, and directors of research who watch franchise knowledge walk to a competitor.

## Impact If Fixed
Analyst turnover stops resetting the firm's knowledge of its coverage universe, and the cost of a departure falls from a lost quarter to a handover meeting.
