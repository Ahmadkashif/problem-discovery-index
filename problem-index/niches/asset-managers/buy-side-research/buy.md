# Knowledge Management From Consulting

**Niche:** [[niches/asset-managers/buy-side-research/profile|Buy-Side Research]]
**Industry:** [[industries/asset-managers|Asset Managers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Strategy consulting firms solved the problem of reusing past engagements across consultants who leave; buy-side research has the same problem and a stricter compliance perimeter.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #word-embeddings #k-nearest-neighbors #compliance #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** This niche is not terminal — an equity analyst forecasting upside from management and a credit analyst underwriting downside from documents are different contests with different winners, and they are stated separately in the sub-niches.

## The Problem
Every analyst who joins inherits a coverage list and must rebuild the firm's view of each company from documents. The firm has years of prior notes, models and recommendations on the same names, but they are not organised for reuse, and much of what matters — why a thesis was abandoned, what management promised two years ago — is buried in prose.

## What Already Exists
Large consulting firms run knowledge management functions: tagged case libraries, expert directories, practice-area curation and, now, enterprise search with generative answering. Enterprise search and retrieval products are widely available, and research management systems offer basic search within their own notes.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: (1) information-barrier and restricted-list awareness, so that a search never surfaces content an employee is walled off from — the single non-negotiable requirement; (2) a time model, since a research view is valid as of a date and a stale answer presented as current is worse than none; (3) entity resolution across tickers, issuers, subsidiaries and corporate actions, so a renamed or merged company keeps its history; (4) linkage to recommendations and outcomes, which consulting knowledge bases never needed; and (5) treatment of licensed content — sell-side research, transcripts, expert calls — whose reuse terms differ from the firm's own notes.

## Target Customer
Directors of research, research operations and investment technology leads at active managers.

## Impact If Solved
A new analyst reaches a useful view of inherited coverage in weeks rather than quarters, and the firm stops paying twice for knowledge it already produced.
