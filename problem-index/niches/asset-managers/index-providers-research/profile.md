# Index Providers' Research & Methodology Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/asset-managers|Asset Managers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this pocket is fighting to design, test, document and govern custom and thematic indexes faster than rivals without compromising methodology integrity — and whoever does that wins the ETF, separate-account and direct-indexing launches that drive licensing revenue.

## What They Do
Design, calculate and govern the benchmarks asset managers are measured against and the indexes their passive products track — MSCI, S&P Dow Jones Indices, FTSE Russell, Bloomberg, Morningstar, STOXX, Solactive, Nasdaq and others. Asset managers pay licence fees, often basis-point based, to benchmark against and to track these indexes, and increasingly commission custom, thematic and factor indexes.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-2,000 index researchers, methodologists, corporate-actions and index-operations staff per large provider
**Output:** Index methodologies, consultations, rebalances and reconstitutions, custom and thematic index designs, back-tests, factor and thematic classifications
**Proprietary data:** Constituent and methodology history, classification systems (such as GICS, jointly maintained by MSCI and S&P), proprietary back-test data and client-specific custom index specifications
**Clock:** Pre-announced rebalance and reconstitution dates contractually relied on by licensees; client ETF and mandate launch dates; methodology consultation timelines and benchmark-regulation oversight requirements
**Buyer:** Head of Index Research / Head of Custom Indexes
