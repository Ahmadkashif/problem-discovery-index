# Investment Decision Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/asset-managers|Asset Managers]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a portfolio manager's own trade history into a measured account of where their decisions add and lose value — sizing, timing, selling — that the PM accepts as fair, and whoever does that becomes the feedback loop active management never had.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300–600M estimated annual spend today on decision and behavioural analytics for portfolio managers, with a much larger latent market
**Share of Parent Industry:** <1% of investment-function cost (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — specialist vendors with early adopters
**Target Buyer:** Chief Investment Officer
**Automation Potential:** Very High — all inputs are already digital

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Distinct from research grading (which asks whether the analyst was right), this asks whether the PM's decisions — position entry, sizing, adding, trimming, selling — add value relative to simple alternatives. Specialist vendors such as Essentia Analytics and Alpha Theory have shown the approach; most managers have not adopted it. The data is the firm's own trade and holdings history.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Performance attribution, transaction cost analysis, specialist behavioural analytics vendors, in-house quant studies. The gaps: attribution answers "what" rather than "which decision"; the PM's reasons are not recorded; and cultural resistance to individual measurement.
