# Performance Analysis From Elite Sport

**Niche:** [[niches/asset-managers/investment-decision-analytics/profile|Investment Decision Analytics]]
**Industry:** [[industries/asset-managers|Asset Managers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Elite athletes review every decision on video with an analyst; portfolio managers review a quarterly attribution report.
**Tags:** #evaluation-metrics #descriptive-statistics #hypothesis-testing #worker-facing #causal-inference
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a portfolio manager's own trade history into a measured account of where their decisions add and lose value — sizing, timing, selling — that the PM accepts as fair, and whoever does that becomes the feedback loop active management never had.

## The Problem
PMs receive almost no structured feedback on their decisions, only outcomes aggregated over periods where luck dominates.

## What Already Exists
Sports performance analysis breaks play into decisions, measures each against expected value, and delivers private, coach-mediated feedback that athletes accept because it helps them win.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: (1) far noisier outcomes, so feedback must be statistical over many decisions; (2) decision tagging from trades rather than video; (3) privacy and coaching framing rather than league tables; (4) linkage to the PM's recorded reasons; and (5) a coach-like role inside the firm, often the CIO or a dedicated analyst.

## Target Customer
CIOs building a development culture for PMs.

## Impact If Solved
Decision feedback becomes routine and developmental rather than punitive.
