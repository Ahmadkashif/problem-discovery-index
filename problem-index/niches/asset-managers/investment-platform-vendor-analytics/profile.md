# Investment Management Platform Vendor Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/asset-managers|Asset Managers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors of the investment book of record — BlackRock Aladdin, State Street Charles River, SimCorp, SS&C — run analytics, benchmarking and product research teams that study client usage and the industry.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-1,000 analytics and product research staff across the main vendors
**Output:** Platform analytics modules, industry benchmarking, product research
**Proprietary data:** Operational and position data processed for clients
**Clock:** Release cycles and client contract renewals
**Buyer:** Head of Analytics / Chief Product Officer
