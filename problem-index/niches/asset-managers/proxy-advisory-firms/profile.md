# Proxy Advisory Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/asset-managers|Asset Managers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Research every shareholder meeting of listed companies and issue vote recommendations under benchmark and custom policies, plus voting platforms that execute managers' votes. ISS and Glass Lewis dominate; Egan-Jones and others are smaller. Asset managers are the core clients.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-2,000 research analysts globally at the largest firms, heavily seasonal
**Output:** Meeting research reports, vote recommendations under benchmark and custom policies, voting execution and N-PX reporting
**Proprietary data:** Decades of meeting-level recommendations, policy histories, and aggregated client vote data
**Clock:** Meeting dates and vote cut-offs set by issuers, concentrated in proxy season
**Buyer:** Head of Research / Chief Research Officer
