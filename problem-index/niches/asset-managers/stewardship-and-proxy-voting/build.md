# Engagement Records That Show an Outcome

**Niche:** [[niches/asset-managers/stewardship-and-proxy-voting/profile|Stewardship & Proxy Voting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/asset-managers|Asset Managers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Stewardship teams hold hundreds of company engagements a year and cannot show, for most of them, what changed afterwards.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #causal-inference #evaluation-metrics #compliance #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show that each vote and engagement follows the firm's own stated policy rather than a proxy adviser's default — and whoever can evidence that, item by item, keeps the clients and survives the political scrutiny now aimed at stewardship from both sides.

## The Problem
Engagement is the substance stewardship codes and clients ask about. Analysts meet boards, write notes, and set objectives — sometimes. Whether the company later changed its pay plan, board composition or disclosure is rarely linked back, so the annual report describes activity rather than results.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Engagement notes are free text in CRM or documents; company changes are found in later proxy statements and reports; attribution of change to engagement is contestable. No vendor owns the engagement record, since it is the firm's own.

## What to Build
Structured engagement objectives with milestones, automatic monitoring of subsequent filings for evidence of each milestone using language models, and an outcome log that distinguishes "changed after engagement" from "changed after engagement by many investors" — with honest framing of attribution. Escalation history and votes link to each engagement.

## Target Customer
Heads of stewardship and responsible investment at large managers.

## Impact If Built
Stewardship reports move from activity counts to evidenced outcomes, which is what clients and regulators are now asking for.
