**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show that each vote and engagement follows the firm's own stated policy rather than a proxy adviser's default — and whoever can evidence that, item by item, keeps the clients and survives the political scrutiny now aimed at stewardship from both sides.

## The Problem
Consistency is the stewardship team's defence: similar items should be voted similarly unless a documented reason differs. Finding the similar items — a say-on-pay with comparable features, a climate proposal with similar wording — is manual.

## What Already Exists
Legal research platforms provide semantic precedent search, citation of reasoning, and flags where a precedent has been superseded.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: (1) a schema for ballot items capturing proposal type, proponent, company context and policy section; (2) the firm's own rationale as the reasoning text; (3) the policy version in force at the time, since policies change annually; (4) Form N-PX categories as a standard index; and (5) proposal-text similarity that sees through proponents' reworded resubmissions.

## Target Customer
Stewardship analysts and their heads.

## Impact If Solved
Voting consistency becomes checkable before the vote, not reconstructed after a complaint.
