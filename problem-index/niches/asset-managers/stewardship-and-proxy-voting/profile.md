# Stewardship & Proxy Voting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/asset-managers|Asset Managers]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show that each vote and engagement follows the firm's own stated policy rather than a proxy adviser's default — and whoever can evidence that, item by item, keeps the clients and survives the political scrutiny now aimed at stewardship from both sides.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1–1.5B estimated annual global spend on stewardship teams, proxy research and voting platforms at asset managers
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% of investment-function cost (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low to Medium — votes cast on platforms, rationale and engagement tracked in spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** Head of Stewardship / Head of Investment Stewardship
**Automation Potential:** High — routine items and documentation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Stewardship sits between the investment team, clients and regulators. Votes are cast at thousands of meetings, concentrated in a short season; policies are custom; rationales must be recorded; Form N-PX and stewardship codes (such as the UK Stewardship Code) require disclosure; and clients increasingly ask for pass-through or choice voting. The contest is consistency and evidence, under scrutiny that has intensified in both directions.

## Current Tools & Gaps
ISS and Glass Lewis research and voting platforms with custom policy implementation; Broadridge ProxyEdge for vote execution; engagement tracking in spreadsheets or CRM. The gaps: divergence from adviser recommendations hard to justify at scale; engagement outcomes untracked; precedent search across past votes manual.
