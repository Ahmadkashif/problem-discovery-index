# Tax Resolution & Audit Defence Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tax-prep-firms|Tax Preparation Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Represent taxpayers who owe more than they can pay or whose returns are under examination — negotiating instalment agreements, offers in compromise and penalty abatements, and defending positions through audit and appeal.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 enrolled agents, tax attorneys, and case analysts
**Output:** Resolution strategy and negotiated agreements, offer in compromise submissions, penalty abatement requests, audit representation, appeals
**Proprietary data:** Thousands of cases with the position taken and the outcome the authority accepted — a record of what the tax authority actually settles for that it does not publish
**Clock:** Statutory response deadlines, collection due process windows, and assessment statutes
**Buyer:** Managing Director / Director of Tax Resolution
