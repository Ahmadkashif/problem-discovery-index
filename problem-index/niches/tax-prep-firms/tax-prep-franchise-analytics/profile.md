# Tax Preparation Franchise Corporate Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tax-prep-firms|Tax Preparation Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator/rollup

## What They Do
Corporate analytics inside the national preparation chains and franchise systems — season demand forecasting, office staffing, pricing, preparer productivity and client retention across thousands of locations that exist for ten weeks a year.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 analysts, data scientists, and operations planners
**Output:** Season demand and staffing models, office location and hours planning, pricing and product attach analytics, preparer performance measurement, client retention modelling
**Proprietary data:** Tens of millions of prepared returns with client, preparer, price and retention outcomes across many seasons
**Clock:** A season with a fixed start and a fixed end and no possibility of catching up
**Buyer:** VP of Analytics / Chief Data Officer
