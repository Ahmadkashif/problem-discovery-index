# Tax Research Content Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tax-prep-firms|Tax Preparation Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Read the entire body of federal and state tax law — code, regulations, rulings, cases and administrative guidance — and turn it into the annotated, cross-referenced explanatory content preparers rely on when a return raises a question the software cannot answer.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-400 tax attorneys, CPAs, and editorial analysts
**Output:** Annotated code and regulations, explanatory analysis, state-by-state comparisons, practice guides, alerts on new authority
**Proprietary data:** Decades of accumulated editorial interpretation across a body of law that changes every session and every filing season
**Clock:** Legislative enactments, regulatory releases, and the filing season deadline
**Buyer:** Chief Content Officer / VP of Editorial
