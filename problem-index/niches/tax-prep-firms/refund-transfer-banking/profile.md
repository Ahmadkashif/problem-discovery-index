# Refund Transfer & Advance Banking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tax-prep-firms|Tax Preparation Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Fund the products that let a taxpayer pay preparation fees out of their refund, and advance cash against a refund not yet issued — the financial infrastructure most independent and franchise preparation runs on, and a substantial share of the industry's economics.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 credit analysts, risk modellers, fraud analysts, and programme managers
**Output:** Advance underwriting and limits, refund offset and delay prediction, fraud screening, preparer-level programme risk monitoring
**Proprietary data:** Millions of returns joined to whether the refund arrived, when, at what amount, and whether it was offset or held — a record of tax authority behaviour that the authority itself does not publish
**Clock:** A ten-week season in which the entire year's volume, risk and revenue occurs
**Buyer:** Chief Risk Officer / VP of Tax Products
