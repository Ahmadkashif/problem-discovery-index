## What They Do
Buy media and generate filer demand during a ten-week window, qualify it, and route it to preparers and software products — one of the most concentrated advertising markets of the year.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 media analysts, data scientists, and campaign managers
**Output:** Campaign attribution and bidding, lead qualification and pricing, seasonal demand forecasting, offer optimisation
**Proprietary data:** Media spend joined to filing behaviour and retention across seasons
**Clock:** A ten-week season with an immovable deadline
**Buyer:** VP of Growth Analytics
