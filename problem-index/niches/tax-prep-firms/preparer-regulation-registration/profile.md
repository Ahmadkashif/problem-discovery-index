# Preparer Registration & Oversight

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tax-prep-firms|Tax Preparation Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Register paid preparers, administer the voluntary credential programmes that partially fill the gap left by the absence of mandatory federal licensure, and pursue preparers whose returns show systematic error or fraud.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 programme analysts, investigators, and compliance staff
**Output:** Preparer registration, credential programme administration, preparer misconduct investigations, injunctions and penalties
**Proprietary data:** Preparer-level return characteristics joined to examination outcomes — the direct measure of which preparers produce bad returns
**Clock:** Annual registration renewal and filing season
**Buyer:** Programme director
