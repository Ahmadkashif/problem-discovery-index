# Tax Administration Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tax-prep-firms|Tax Preparation Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The research and analytics functions inside the federal and state tax authorities — return selection for examination, compliance research, refund fraud detection, and the tax gap estimates that set enforcement policy.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-3,000 economists, statisticians, data scientists, and research analysts across federal and state administrations
**Output:** Examination selection scoring, compliance and tax gap research, refund integrity models, taxpayer behaviour studies, service demand forecasting
**Proprietary data:** Every return filed in the country joined to examination outcomes and collection results — the largest and best-labelled compliance dataset that exists
**Clock:** Filing seasons, examination cycles, and statutory assessment periods
**Buyer:** Director of Research
