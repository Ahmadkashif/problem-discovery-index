# E-File Transmission Infrastructure

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tax-prep-firms|Tax Preparation Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Transmit returns to federal and state systems, validate them against schemas before submission, and manage the acknowledgement and rejection cycle for the preparers and software providers that depend on them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 engineers, schema analysts, and operations staff
**Output:** Schema validation and pre-submission checks, transmission and acknowledgement handling, rejection diagnosis, throughput monitoring
**Proprietary data:** Rejection codes and resubmission patterns across enormous return volumes — the clearest available picture of where returns actually go wrong
**Clock:** E-file opening, peak transmission days, and the filing deadline
**Buyer:** VP of Engineering / Director of Compliance
