# Consumer Tax Behaviour Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tax-prep-firms|Tax Preparation Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Study how taxpayers choose preparers, respond to refunds, and take up credits — largely at policy research centres and advocacy organisations concerned with low-income filers and refundable credits.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 researchers and policy analysts
**Output:** Take-up and behaviour studies, filing burden research, preparer market analysis, policy evaluation
**Proprietary data:** Survey and administrative data obtained through research agreements
**Clock:** Legislative and filing season cycles
**Buyer:** Research Director
