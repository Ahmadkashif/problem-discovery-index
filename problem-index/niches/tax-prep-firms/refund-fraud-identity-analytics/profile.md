# Refund Fraud & Identity Verification Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tax-prep-firms|Tax Preparation Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Score filed returns for identity theft and fabricated refund claims before a refund is released, and verify that the person filing is who they say they are — the layer that decides whether a taxpayer's refund is paid in days or held for months.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 data scientists, fraud analysts, identity specialists, and investigators
**Output:** Refund fraud scoring, identity proofing and verification, filing anomaly detection, scheme intelligence, case referral
**Proprietary data:** Identity and filing behaviour data joined to confirmed fraud outcomes across many jurisdictions and filing seasons
**Clock:** The filing season, which concentrates the entire year's volume and the entire year's fraud into ten weeks
**Buyer:** Chief Analytics Officer / VP of Fraud Solutions
