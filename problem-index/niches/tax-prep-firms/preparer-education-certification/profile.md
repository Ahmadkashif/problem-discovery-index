# Preparer Education & Certification Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tax-prep-firms|Tax Preparation Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Deliver the continuing education and examination preparation preparers need to maintain credentials, and administer the enrolled agent and voluntary programme examinations.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 curriculum developers, tax instructors, and assessment staff
**Output:** Continuing education courses, examination preparation, credential examinations, annual update training on new law
**Proprietary data:** Course completion and examination performance records across the preparer population
**Clock:** Annual credential renewal deadlines and the pre-season update cycle
**Buyer:** Director of Education
