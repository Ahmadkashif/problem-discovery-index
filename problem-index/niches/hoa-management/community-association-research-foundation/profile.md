# Community Association Research Foundation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hoa-management|HOA Management]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The industry's research foundation produces the national statistics on community associations — how many exist, how many people live in them, what they collect and spend — and the governance and best-practice research managers cite.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-20 researchers and policy staff
**Output:** National statistical reviews, governance research, best practice guidance
**Proprietary data:** The long-running national association census and member survey series
**Clock:** Annual publication
**Buyer:** Research director — member and donor funded
