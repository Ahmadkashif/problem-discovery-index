# Milestone Structural Inspection Engineering

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hoa-management|HOA Management]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Engineering firms perform the milestone and structural integrity inspections that several states now mandate for older multifamily buildings, reporting on structural condition and required repairs. Florida's post-Surfside regime made these compulsory on a fixed age-and-cycle schedule for a large share of the coastal condominium stock.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-80 structural engineers and inspectors
**Output:** Milestone inspection reports, structural condition findings, repair scoping
**Proprietary data:** Structural observations across a large building population, in a jurisdiction and time period where nobody else is looking
**Clock:** Statutory inspection deadlines with occupancy consequences
**Buyer:** Principal / Managing Engineer
