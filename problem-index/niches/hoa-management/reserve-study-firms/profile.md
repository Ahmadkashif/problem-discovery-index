# Reserve Study Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hoa-management|HOA Management]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Reserve study firms inventory every physical component a community association is responsible for — roofs, elevators, pavement, pool systems, cladding — estimate remaining useful life and replacement cost for each, and produce the multi-decade funding plan that sets the association's assessment. A growing number of states mandate the study on a fixed cycle, and Florida's structural integrity reserve study requirement made it statutory for a large share of the condominium stock.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 reserve analysts, engineers, and inspectors per firm
**Output:** Component inventories, remaining-life and cost estimates, funding plans, statutory reserve studies
**Proprietary data:** Observed component conditions, actual replacement timing, and realized costs across thousands of communities over decades — the only empirical basis for how long building components actually last
**Clock:** Statutory study cycles and budget adoption dates
**Buyer:** Firm principal / President
