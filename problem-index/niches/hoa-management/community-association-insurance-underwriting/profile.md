# Community Association Insurance Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hoa-management|HOA Management]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Specialty insurers underwrite the master property and liability policies condominium and homeowner associations must carry, pricing building age, construction type, roof condition, water loss history, and — since Surfside — structural maintenance posture. The market hardened severely, and in coastal states association insurance is now a primary driver of assessment increases.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-200 underwriters, actuaries, and risk engineers
**Output:** Rating models, inspection-based risk assessments, loss control recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Loss histories by building characteristic across large books of associations — the closest thing to an empirical failure record for multifamily building components
**Clock:** Annual renewal, concentrated at fixed dates
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
