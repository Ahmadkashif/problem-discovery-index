# Community Association Banking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hoa-management|HOA Management]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Specialty banking divisions hold association operating and reserve deposits and lend for capital projects, underwriting the association itself — reserve adequacy, delinquency rate, assessment collectability, board governance — rather than any individual borrower.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 credit analysts, relationship managers, and portfolio staff
**Output:** Credit decisions, covenant structures, portfolio risk analysis
**Proprietary data:** Association financial statements and deposit behaviour across thousands of communities — the only aggregated financial view of the sector
**Clock:** Loan approval and covenant reporting cycles
**Buyer:** Division president / Chief Credit Officer
