# Condominium Project Review Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hoa-management|HOA Management]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Before a lender can make a conventional mortgage on a condominium, the project itself must be found eligible — reserves adequate, no disqualifying deferred maintenance, litigation and special assessments within limits, owner-occupancy and single-entity ownership within thresholds. Project review firms collect the association's financials, budget, insurance, and questionnaire responses and render the determination. After Surfside, the agencies tightened those requirements sharply and added structural and deferred-maintenance criteria, turning a clerical checklist into a substantive analytical review.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 project reviewers, analysts, and underwriting staff
**Output:** Project eligibility determinations, condition findings, exception documentation
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated determinations across tens of thousands of condominium projects, with the financial and structural evidence behind each — the only assembled view of US condominium project condition
**Clock:** Loan closing dates, plus annual project recertification
**Buyer:** VP of Operations / Chief Credit Officer
