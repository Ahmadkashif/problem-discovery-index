# HOA Collections & Lien Enforcement Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hoa-management|HOA Management]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Specialist law firms pursue delinquent assessments on behalf of associations — demand letters, statutory notices, liens, and foreclosure — at very high volume against strict statutory sequences that differ by state.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-200 attorneys, paralegals, and collections staff
**Output:** Demand and notice sequences, lien filings, recovery analysis, settlement recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Delinquency-to-recovery outcomes across tens of thousands of accounts — which payment patterns cure, which escalate, and what each step actually recovers
**Clock:** Statutory notice and lien deadlines, strict and unforgiving
**Buyer:** Managing partner
