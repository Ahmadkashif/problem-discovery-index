# State Condominium & HOA Regulators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hoa-management|HOA Management]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State divisions regulating condominiums and community associations register associations, license managers, arbitrate disputes, and — in several states, expanded sharply after Surfside — collect structural and reserve compliance filings.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-80 analysts, investigators, and arbitrators per state division
**Output:** Registration and compliance decisions, dispute determinations, enforcement actions
**Proprietary data:** Association filings, complaint and dispute records, newly mandated structural reporting
**Clock:** Statutory filing and dispute timelines
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — fee-funded state agency
