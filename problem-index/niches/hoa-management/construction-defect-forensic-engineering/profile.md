# Construction Defect Forensic Engineering

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hoa-management|HOA Management]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Forensic engineering firms investigate building defects on behalf of associations pursuing developers and contractors — water intrusion, envelope failure, structural deficiency — and produce the technical findings that support or settle the claim.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 forensic engineers, building scientists, and technicians
**Output:** Investigation reports, defect findings, repair cost estimates, expert testimony
**Proprietary data:** Defect patterns by construction era, method, and builder — accumulated across investigations and locked under litigation confidentiality
**Clock:** Statutes of repose and litigation schedules
**Buyer:** Principal / Practice leader
