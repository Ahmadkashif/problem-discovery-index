# Resale Certificate & Association Document Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hoa-management|HOA Management]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
When a home inside an association sells, the closing requires an estoppel or resale certificate — current assessments, delinquencies, pending special assessments, violations — plus the governing documents. Document services assemble and deliver these packages at scale for management companies and title agents.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 document processors and support staff, above a small analytical core
**Output:** Resale certificates, estoppel letters, governing document packages
**Proprietary data:** Association documents, financials, and assessment data across a large share of US communities, refreshed on every sale
**Clock:** Closing dates — the certificate gates a real estate transaction
**Buyer:** VP of Operations
