# The Solutions Engineer

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/synthetic-data-providers|Synthetic Data Providers]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a prospect's dataset into credible evidence without a person building it by hand — and whoever does that takes the account, because proof-of-concept turnaround is what decides these deals.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$140M US in loaded cost and opportunity
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of category revenue equivalent
**Digital Adoption:** None — every proof is assembled manually
**Target Buyer:** Vendor solutions and sales engineering organisations
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the work is nearly identical each time

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every synthetic data deal runs through a proof of concept, and every proof of concept is hand-built by a solutions engineer. They receive a prospect's schema, profile it manually, configure a generation run, tune it when the output is wrong, assemble a fidelity comparison, run whatever privacy evidence the prospect asked for, build a deck, and present. It takes one to three weeks per prospect. The work is close to identical across prospects and is rebuilt each time because the product ships generation and treats demonstration as a person's job. The engineer is simultaneously the scarcest resource in the sales motion and the least-tooled role in the company.

## Current Tools & Gaps
The generation product itself, notebooks, spreadsheets, presentation software, and accumulated personal scripts. The gaps: no automated profiling of a prospect schema into a proposed configuration; no reusable evidence pack; no library of past proofs to draw on; no way to run the proof inside the prospect's environment when the data cannot leave, which is the common case; and no record of which proofs converted and what they contained, so the organisation learns nothing across cycles.
