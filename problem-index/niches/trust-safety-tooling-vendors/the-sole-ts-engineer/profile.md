## What Makes This a Distinct Niche

At a platform past its first growth stage, trust and safety is frequently one engineer. They own abuse, harassment, spam, fraud, child safety, self-harm, the appeals process, the law enforcement request channel and the regulatory reporting.

Each of those is a specialism. Child safety has its own legal regime, reporting obligations and a specialised ecosystem. Fraud is a different discipline with different tooling. Harassment requires policy judgement. Regulatory reporting requires an understanding of several jurisdictions' requirements. And one person holds all of it alongside an engineering backlog.

Their tools are vendor products whose accuracy they cannot verify, configured with thresholds they set with no framework, producing a review queue they and perhaps a small operations team work. They are asked by leadership whether the platform is safe and have no way to answer. And when a regulator or a journalist asks a question, they are the person who has to.

The role is a known pattern in the industry and is served by nobody. The tooling is built for platforms with a trust and safety organisation. The professional resources assume specialisation. And the person holding it is usually early in their career and learning every one of these domains at once.

## Current Tools & Gaps

Vendor classification products with dashboards. A case management system, sometimes. Vendor customer success support. Industry communities of varying usefulness. Legal counsel for the regulatory questions, when available.

The gaps are about breadth and verification. Nothing helps them prioritise across harm types with wildly different frequencies and severities. Nothing helps them verify vendor accuracy claims, so they operate tools they cannot evaluate. Nothing supports the regulatory reporting, which is a specialist task landing on a generalist. Child safety obligations in particular carry specific legal requirements they may not know exist. And they have no peer with the same breadth, because everyone senior enough to advise them specialised years ago.
