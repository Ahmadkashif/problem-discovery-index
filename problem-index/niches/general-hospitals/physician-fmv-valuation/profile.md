# Physician Compensation & Fair Market Value Advisory

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/general-hospitals|General Hospitals]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
These firms write fair market value and commercial reasonableness opinions for hospital–physician arrangements: employment, call coverage, medical directorships, co-management agreements and joint ventures. The Stark Law and Anti-Kickback Statute effectively require such opinions before the hospital signs (recalled). VMG Health, HealthCare Appraisers, Pinnacle Healthcare Consulting and SullivanCotter's advisory practice are representative (named from recall). The physician compensation surveys they rely on (MGMA, SullivanCotter, AMGA) are a specialised branch of [[niches/hr-consultants/compensation-survey-publishers/profile|🔍 Compensation Survey Publishers]] (54).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~30-300 valuation analysts and consultants per larger firm (estimate)
**Output:** FMV and commercial-reasonableness opinions, compensation plan designs, transaction valuations
**Proprietary data:** An archive of opinions and arrangement terms across many hospitals, plus licensed survey data whose licences restrict redistribution
**Clock:** Contract signing and renewal dates set by the client; no third-party filing date
**Buyer:** Managing Director of the valuation practice

**Kill switches:** **data isn't theirs**. Opinions are client work product, often commissioned through the hospital's counsel to support privilege in Stark and AKS matters, and the core benchmarks are licensed surveys with use restrictions. This applies the vault's majority rule for counsel-commissioned work.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. A repeatable, opinion-is-the-invoice business with many hospital buyers, but its archive belongs to clients and counsel, and its clock is the client's own contract calendar. When a hospital buys a practice, the seller's side of the same deal is advised by [[niches/physician-practices/practice-sell-side-ma-advisors/profile|🔍 Physician Practice Sell-Side M&A Advisors]] (32).
