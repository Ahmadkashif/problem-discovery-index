# Clinical Outcomes Registries

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/general-hospitals|General Hospitals]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
These are specialty-society registries that collect abstracted clinical data on procedures and conditions from participating hospitals and return risk-adjusted benchmark reports. The American College of Cardiology's NCDR, the STS National Database, the American College of Surgeons' NSQIP and the American Heart Association's Get With The Guidelines are representative (named from recall). Hospitals pay participation fees. Some registries publish public ratings, and CMS coverage-with-evidence decisions require registry participation for certain devices, such as transcatheter valves (recalled).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-150 statisticians, analysts and clinical data managers per registry programme (estimate); hospital abstractors are production and sit at the hospitals
**Output:** Quarterly risk-adjusted outcome reports, public star ratings for some programmes, research publications, device post-market evidence
**Proprietary data:** Detailed abstracted clinical data on millions of procedures, collected to standard definitions
**Clock:** Each registry sets its own quarterly submission and harvest deadlines. CMS coverage rules require participation but set no deadline on the registry's analysis.
**Buyer:** Chief Science Officer or Registry Director at the society

**Kill switches:** **HIPAA**. Registries receive patient-level records with identifiers from hospitals under participation agreements and business associate or data use agreements. Research use is permitted under governance, but tooling touches PHI.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. The richest outcome data in the industry, held by a handful of societies, each with its own governance.
