# State Hospital Association Data Programs

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/general-hospitals|General Hospitals]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
These are the data subsidiaries and programmes of state hospital associations. They collect member hospitals' discharge and financial data and return market-share, utilisation and benchmark reports to members, sometimes acting as the state's discharge-data agent. The AHA's national Annual Survey Database, the source of the hospital counts used in this industry's hub, is licensed separately.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically under 10 analysts per state association (estimate); the AHA's survey operation is larger but produces one annual census-style database
**Output:** Member market-share reports, discharge databases, advocacy analyses
**Proprietary data:** Member-contributed discharge abstracts
**Clock:** Legislative sessions and annual data cycles
**Buyer:** Association Vice President of Data
