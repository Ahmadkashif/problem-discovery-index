# Service-Line Demand Forecasting & Strategy Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/general-hospitals|General Hospitals]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
These firms sell forecasts of inpatient and outpatient demand by service line and market, site-of-care shift projections, and market-share analytics. Health system strategy teams use them to plan service lines, sites and capital. Sg2 (part of Vizient), Trilliant Health and the strategy analytics of Kaufman Hall are representative (named from recall). Kaufman Hall's monthly National Hospital Flash Report is built on data from 1,300+ hospitals collected by Strata Decision Technology (checked).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-100 analysts, health economists and data scientists per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Ten-year demand forecasts by service line, market-share and leakage analytics, strategy subscriptions
**Proprietary data:** Forecast models built on licensed all-payer claims and public data (HCRIS, Census); some firms add operational data from their own software clients
**Clock:** Self-set annual forecast releases; health systems' budget and strategic-planning cycles
**Buyer:** Head of research or analytics at the firm
