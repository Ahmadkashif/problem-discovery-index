# Physician Advisor & Utilization Review Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/general-hospitals|General Hospitals]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
These firms provide second-level physician review of inpatient-versus-observation status, concurrent denial peer-to-peer calls and status-related appeals for hospitals, plus software that predicts the right status at admission. Optum's Executive Health Resources, Sound Physicians' physician advisory unit and Xsolis are representative (named from recall). Hospitals pay per review, per bed or by subscription. They are the hospital's mirror of the payer's utilization management function, logged from the payer side under [[industries/health-insurance-carriers|Health Insurance Carriers]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-60 analysts and data scientists per firm (estimate); physician advisors reviewing cases one by one are production
**Output:** Status determinations, peer-to-peer outcomes, appeal letters, status-prediction scores
**Proprietary data:** Cross-hospital records of status decisions, payer responses and appeal outcomes, held on client charts
**Clock:** Payer concurrent-review and peer-to-peer windows (often 24–72 hours) and appeal deadlines (recalled). Each binds a single review, which is production work, so Q4 is capped at 3
**Buyer:** Medical Director or Chief Analytics Officer at the firm
