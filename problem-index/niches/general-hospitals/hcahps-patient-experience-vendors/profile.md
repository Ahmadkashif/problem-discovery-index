# Hospital Patient Experience Survey Vendors (HCAHPS)

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/general-hospitals|General Hospitals]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
These are CMS-approved vendors that field the HCAHPS patient experience survey for hospitals, submit the results to CMS every quarter, and sell benchmarking, comment analytics and improvement programmes built on the wider survey database. Press Ganey and NRC Health are the large players, with a long tail of smaller approved vendors (named from recall). HCAHPS scores feed public star ratings and the patient-experience domain of Hospital Value-Based Purchasing (recalled). The same firms field plan-side surveys, logged as [[niches/health-insurance-carriers/cahps-hos-survey-vendors/profile|🔍 CAHPS & HOS Survey Vendors]] (37, kill switch).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-150 survey scientists, analysts and improvement advisors per major vendor (estimate); interviewers and fulfilment staff are production
**Output:** Fielded HCAHPS surveys submitted to CMS; benchmark and driver reports; text analytics on patient comments
**Proprietary data:** Response-level survey and comment data across thousands of hospitals, including non-HCAHPS custom items
**Clock:** CMS quarterly HCAHPS data-submission deadlines, binding on the vendor itself (recalled)
**Buyer:** Chief Analytics or Chief Science Officer at the vendor
