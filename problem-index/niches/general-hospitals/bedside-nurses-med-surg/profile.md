# Bedside Nurses on Med-Surg Units

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/general-hospitals|General Hospitals]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to cut the bedside nurse's documentation time without degrading the flowsheet data that quality measures, audits and billing depend on, and whoever gives back the most charting minutes per shift with audit-clean flowsheets takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1B a year of nursing documentation, ambient capture, handoff and nurse-workflow tooling (estimate), serving the 1.75M registered nurses in general hospitals (BLS OEWS, May 2023)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.1% of revenue in tools; RN wages are the largest single expense line (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — everything is charted in the EHR, but by hand, in flowsheet rows, often after the work and after the shift
**Target Buyer:** Chief Nursing Officer / Chief Nursing Informatics Officer
**Automation Potential:** Medium-High — capture and drafting are automatable; assessment judgement and confirmation stay with the nurse

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
AI documentation has served physicians first. Ambient scribes from Abridge, Ambience, Microsoft and Epic draft physician notes, and health systems bought them for physician burnout. Nurses outnumber physicians about nine to one in hospitals (1.75M RNs to 189,800 physicians, BLS), chart more often, and chart into structured flowsheet rows rather than narrative notes. A physician scribe does not fit that pattern. RN turnover is 17.6% a year and each departure costs ~$60,000 (NSI 2026), so nurse time is a budget line, not just a morale issue. Epic released a nursing ambient tool in March 2026, which makes this an active contest.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Epic flowsheets and Rover mobile charting; Epic "Chart with Art" nursing ambient (March 2026); vendor pilots of nurse-focused ambient capture; handoff tools; Vocera/Stryker communication badges. Gaps: accuracy of flowsheet rows (not narrative) from speech; reassessment timers and overdue tasks; handoff summaries generated from the shift's actual changes; measurement of documentation time that nurses trust.
