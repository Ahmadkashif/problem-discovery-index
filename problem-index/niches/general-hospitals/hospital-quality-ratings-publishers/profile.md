# Hospital Quality Ratings Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/general-hospitals|General Hospitals]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
These organisations publish consumer-facing ratings and rankings of hospitals and nursing homes:
- U.S. News & World Report's Best Hospitals, and its Best Nursing Homes, which rates ~15,000 homes on short-term rehabilitation and long-term care.
- The Leapfrog Hospital Safety Grade.
- Healthgrades awards.

All are named from recall, and the nursing-home figure is from the Skilled Nursing Facilities build. Their inputs are largely public CMS data: the Overall Hospital Quality Star Rating and measure files on Care Compare for hospitals, and the Five-Star rating, inspection and staffing files for nursing homes. Leapfrog adds its own annual hospital survey. Much of the revenue comes from rated providers licensing the badge or logo for marketing, plus advertising. U.S. News runs one health ratings team across both settings. The nursing-home side is therefore folded into this pocket rather than scored separately. [[industries/skilled-nursing-facilities|Skilled Nursing Facilities]] has no ratings pocket of its own, and its [[niches/skilled-nursing-facilities/_overview|niche overview]] points here.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-40 analysts and methodologists per publisher (estimate)
**Output:** Grades, rankings, award lists and badge licences for hospitals and nursing homes
**Proprietary data:** Leapfrog's annual hospital survey responses; methodologies and rating histories; inputs otherwise public CMS data
**Clock:** Self-set publication cycles (Leapfrog's spring and fall grades; annual U.S. News rankings); CMS refreshes its inputs on its own schedule
**Buyer:** Chief Methodologist or head of ratings
