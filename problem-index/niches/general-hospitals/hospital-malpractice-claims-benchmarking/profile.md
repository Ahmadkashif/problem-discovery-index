# Hospital Malpractice Claims Benchmarking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/general-hospitals|General Hospitals]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
These are hospital professional-liability insurers, captives and the comparative claims databases built from them. They code malpractice claims by clinical failure mode and sell benchmarking and risk-management guidance back to hospitals and insurers. CRICO Strategies' Candello database, MedPro, Coverys and the captives of large academic systems are representative (named from recall). Insurers price the insight into premium. The benchmarking arm sells it as a subscription.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-100 claims analysts, nurse coders and risk researchers per major organisation (estimate)
**Output:** Coded claims benchmarks by specialty and failure mode, risk-management reports, premium pricing
**Proprietary data:** Coded closed-claim files contributed by member insurers and captives, with clinical narratives
**Clock:** Policy renewal and actuarial reserving cycles, set by the insurers themselves
**Buyer:** VP of Patient Safety Analytics at the insurer or benchmarking organisation
