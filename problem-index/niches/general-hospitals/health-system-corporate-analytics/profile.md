# Health System Corporate Strategy & Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/general-hospitals|General Hospitals]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
These are the central analytics, strategy and clinical-operations teams of multi-hospital systems. HCA Healthcare, CommonSpirit, Ascension, Tenet and the large academic systems are representative (named from recall). They benchmark their hospitals against one another, run system-wide clinical and operational programmes, model acquisitions and service-line moves, and, at the largest systems, build their own predictive models on enterprise data warehouses. 70% of community hospitals belong to a system (AHA).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~50-500 analysts, data scientists and clinical informaticists at the largest systems (estimate); far fewer at regional systems
**Output:** Internal benchmarks, operating decisions, models deployed into the EHR, M&A analysis
**Proprietary data:** Complete clinical, financial and operational records for every hospital in the system
**Clock:** Budget cycles, bond disclosure and board calendars; CMS programme performance periods
**Buyer:** Chief Analytics Officer or Chief Data Officer of the system
