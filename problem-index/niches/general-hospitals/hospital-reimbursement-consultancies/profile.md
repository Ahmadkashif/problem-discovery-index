# Hospital Reimbursement & Cost Report Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/general-hospitals|General Hospitals]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
These firms prepare hospitals' Medicare cost reports and recover reimbursement the hospital is owed under Medicare's add-on formulas: disproportionate share (DSH) Medicaid-eligible days, Worksheet S-10 uncompensated care, wage index corrections, bad debt, and transfer-DRG and outlier reviews. They also file Provider Reimbursement Review Board appeals. Toyon Associates, Besler, Forvis and Eide Bailly are representative (named from recall). Much of the work is paid on contingency, as a share of the reimbursement recovered. The home-health counterpart is [[niches/home-health-agencies/medicare-cost-report-firms/profile|🔍 Medicare Cost Report Preparation]] (43).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-150 reimbursement analysts and healthcare accountants per firm (estimate); cost-report compilers doing routine preparation are production
**Output:** Filed cost reports, DSH and S-10 recovery findings billed on contingency, wage index revision requests, PRRB appeals
**Proprietary data:** An archive of MAC audit adjustments, S-10 audit outcomes and appeal results across client hospitals; the filed cost reports themselves are public through HCRIS
**Clock:** Cost reports are due by the last day of the fifth month after the cost-reporting period closes (42 CFR 413.24(f)). PRRB hearing requests must reach the Board within 180 days of the final contractor determination (42 CFR 405.1835). CMS also sets annual wage-index revision deadlines and MAC audit response windows (recalled).
**Buyer:** Practice leader, healthcare reimbursement

**Kill switches:** **HIPAA**, and **data isn't theirs**. DSH eligible-days matching and S-10 charity-care audits are run on patient-level listings matched against state Medicaid eligibility files, under business associate agreements, and every finding is computed on the client hospital's records. The home-health cost-report pocket carries no kill switch because its allocation work stays at cost-centre level.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. A finding-is-the-invoice business on hard federal clocks, but its recoveries are built from patient-level PHI the hospital owns.
