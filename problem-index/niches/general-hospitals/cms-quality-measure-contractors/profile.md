# CMS Hospital Quality Measure Contractors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/general-hospitals|General Hospitals]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
These contractors develop and maintain the measures CMS uses to pay and rate hospitals: readmission and mortality measures for HRRP and Hospital VBP, the Overall Hospital Quality Star Rating methodology, and hospital-specific reports sent before public release. Yale CORE and Mathematica are representative (named from recall). HRRP cut payments to 2,334 hospitals (80%) for FY2027, the first year with Medicare Advantage patients in the measures (CMS via Healthcare Dive).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~50-200 statisticians, clinicians and analysts per contractor's CMS hospital portfolio (estimate)
**Output:** Measure specifications, annual re-evaluation reports, star-rating calculations, hospital-specific reports
**Proprietary data:** None of their own; Medicare claims, MA encounter data and hospital-reported data held under CMS data use agreements
**Clock:** IPPS and OPPS rulemaking calendars, annual public-reporting releases and contract deliverable dates. CMS sets them all, and CMS is the client, so the clock is firm but belongs to the buyer.
**Buyer:** Project Director of the CMS contract
