# State Hospital Cost & Price Oversight Agencies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/general-hospitals|General Hospitals]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
These are state agencies that collect hospital financial and discharge data and regulate or monitor hospital prices and spending growth. Maryland's Health Services Cost Review Commission sets all-payer global budgets. Massachusetts' Health Policy Commission and CHIA monitor cost growth against a benchmark, and California's HCAI and Office of Health Care Affordability set spending-growth targets (named from recall).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~30-150 analysts, economists and data staff per agency (estimate)
**Output:** Rate orders and global budgets, cost-growth reports, hospital financial databases, hearings
**Proprietary data:** All-payer discharge abstracts and audited hospital financial filings for the state
**Clock:** Statutory annual reporting and rate-setting calendars
**Buyer:** Executive Director of the agency
