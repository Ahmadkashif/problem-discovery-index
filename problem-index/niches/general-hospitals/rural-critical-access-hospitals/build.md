# A Cost Report Kept Current All Year

**Niche:** [[niches/general-hospitals/rural-critical-access-hospitals/profile|Rural & Critical Access Hospitals]]
**Industry:** [[industries/general-hospitals|General Hospitals]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A critical access hospital's Medicare revenue is set by its cost report, which is assembled once a year from ledgers, time studies and square-footage statistics nobody maintained in between.
**Tags:** #linear-regression #descriptive-statistics #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #compliance #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to run a 25-bed hospital's revenue cycle, Medicare cost report and quality reporting with the handful of staff it has, and whoever secures the most cost-based reimbursement with every CMS submission on time takes the account.

## The Problem
Cost-based reimbursement depends on allocating overhead correctly across inpatient, swing-bed, outpatient, rural health clinic and non-reimbursable cost centres, using statistics such as square feet, time studies and meals served. Small hospitals gather these at year end from memory. Errors leave money on the table or invite MAC audit adjustments. The report is due by the last day of the fifth month after the year closes (42 CFR 413.24(f)).

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Cost-report software is a filing tool for consultants, not a running ledger for the hospital. The market looks small to venture-backed vendors, and the rules (CMS Pub. 15-2) are dense.

## What to Build
A continuous cost-report ledger that maps the general ledger to cost centres monthly and collects allocation statistics from source systems (time and attendance, space inventory, dietary counts). It projects interim settlement and year-end payment, flags allocation choices with material reimbursement effects, and assembles the filing package for the preparer with audit support attached.

## Target Customer
CFOs of critical access hospitals and the rural networks and Flex programmes that support them.

## Impact If Built
Better-supported allocations and fewer audit adjustments are worth ~$50,000–$300,000 a year per hospital (estimate), on margins where that decides whether a service line stays open.
