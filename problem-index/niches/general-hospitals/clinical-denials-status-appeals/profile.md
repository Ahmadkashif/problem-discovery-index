# Clinical Denials & Inpatient Status Appeals

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/general-hospitals|General Hospitals]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to overturn clinical denials of the hospital stay itself (inpatient status, medical necessity and DRG validation) before the payer's appeal window closes, and whoever wins back the most denied dollars per reviewer hour takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$12B a year of hospital and outsourced spend on clinical denial prevention, physician advisor services and appeals (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1% of general hospital revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — denials arrive electronically; the argument is assembled by nurses reading charts and payer policies
**Target Buyer:** VP Revenue Integrity / Director of Denials Management, with the physician advisor programme
**Automation Potential:** High for triage, evidence assembly and drafting; peer-to-peer stays human

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The disputes are medical. A Medicare Advantage plan says a three-day stay should have been observation. A commercial payer reviews a paid sepsis claim and downgrades the DRG on a different sepsis definition. Premier's 2025 survey put MA overturn rates at 68.1%. The winner is whoever builds the clinical-plus-policy argument fastest. That means a nurse, a physician advisor and increasingly an AI drafter, against payer-specific deadlines.

Post-acute authorisation is outside this niche. When the MA plan refuses the skilled nursing discharge, the hospital's side is placement, covered in [[niches/general-hospitals/case-management-post-acute-placement/profile|Case Management & Post-Acute Placement]]. The facility's side is [[niches/skilled-nursing-facilities/ma-authorization-denials/profile|Medicare Advantage Authorisation & Continued-Stay Reviews]]. The payer's concurrent review is [[niches/health-insurance-carriers/inpatient-post-acute-review/profile|Inpatient & Post-Acute Concurrent Review]]. Billing companies' generic appeal work is [[niches/medical-billing/denial-management-appeals/profile|Denial Management & Appeals]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Epic denial worklists; physician advisor firms (Optum's Executive Health Resources, Sound Physicians, recalled); Xsolis for status prediction; AI appeal drafting from AKASA, Commure and Smarter Technologies; MCG and InterQual criteria. Gaps: no learning from outcomes by payer and argument, no contract clause in the appeal, and peer-to-peer briefs assembled by hand on the morning of the call.
