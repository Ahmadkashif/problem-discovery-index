# Capacity, Patient-Flow & Perioperative Optimization Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/general-hospitals|General Hospitals]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
These vendors sell software that predicts discharges and census, coordinates bed placement, forecasts OR block use and nudges release, and schedules infusion chairs and staff. Qventus, LeanTaaS (iQueue for Operating Rooms and Inpatient Flow), Opmed.ai and GE HealthCare's command centres are representative. All three specialists had engineering or forward-deployed roles open on their own boards on 24 October 2026: Qventus a Forward Deployed AI Solutions Architect and Senior Data Scientist, Opmed.ai a Forward Deployed Engineer and Data Science Tech Lead, and LeanTaaS an Engineering Manager. Hospitals pay subscriptions, sometimes tied to outcomes.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-100 data scientists and analysts per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Software subscriptions; outcome studies used in sales
**Proprietary data:** Client ADT, OR and scheduling data held under business associate agreements, plus cross-client benchmarks
**Clock:** Health systems' go-live and renewal dates; no third-party deadline on the analysis
**Buyer:** Chief Product Officer or Head of Data Science at the vendor

**Kill switches:** **HIPAA**, and **data isn't theirs**. The models train on client hospitals' identified operational and clinical records under business associate agreements.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. A software market, not an insight one, and a strong target for engineering placement rather than for a research engine.
