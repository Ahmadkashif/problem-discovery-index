# De-identified Hospital Discharge Databases & Benchmarking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/general-hospitals|General Hospitals]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
These firms assemble chargemaster-level discharge, cost and outcome data from hundreds or thousands of hospitals into de-identified databases. They sell two things:
- risk-adjusted benchmarks back to hospitals, covering mortality, length of stay, cost per case and complications;
- real-world evidence studies and data licences to drug and device makers, payers and researchers.

Premier's PINC AI database holds "over 20 years of data from more than 1,000 hospitals". Premier's FY2025 10-K lists data licences as revenue and describes a research team of clinicians, epidemiologists and health economists delivering real-world evidence with life-science companies. Patient Square Capital took Premier private for $2.6B in November 2025.

Vizient's Clinical Data Base covers 1,300+ hospitals and feeds its annual Quality and Accountability ranking. Its life-sciences page offers suppliers "clinical utilization, demographic, and cost data" from those hospitals to "build real-world evidence". Truveta and IQVIA's hospital data work the same ground (named from recall). The group purchasing side of the same firms uses a different corpus and is already profiled as [[niches/medical-device-mfg/gpo-purchasing-analytics/profile|🔍 Group Purchasing Organization Analytics]] (46).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~50-300 epidemiologists, health economists, clinical analysts and data scientists per major firm (estimate); data-intake operations excluded
**Output:** Risk-adjusted outcome and cost benchmarks, quality rankings, real-world evidence studies, licensed de-identified datasets
**Proprietary data:** Two decades of all-payer, chargemaster-level inpatient and outpatient records from contributing hospitals, de-identified. They include item-level drug, device and supply use that claims data do not show.
**Clock:** The firms set their own quarterly refreshes and annual ranking cycles. Life-science clients' regulatory and HTA submission dates bind specific studies, but those dates belong to the client.
**Buyer:** Chief Data / Analytics Officer or head of applied sciences at the database firm

**Kill switches:** none.
- The databases are de-identified under the HIPAA Privacy Rule.
- Both large holders sell the data commercially. Premier's 10-K reports data licences as revenue (checked). Vizient's life-sciences page markets Clinical Data Base data to suppliers for real-world evidence (checked; the contributor terms themselves were not found). That commercial use is evidence of product rights over the de-identified data.
- Scored consistently with [[niches/chiropractic-practices/healthcare-cost-benchmark-nonprofits/profile|🔍 Healthcare Cost Benchmark Data Organizations]] (50) and [[niches/pharmacy-independents/prescription-market-intelligence/profile|🔍 Prescription Market Intelligence Providers]] (54) on data, with no third-party deadline on the work itself.

**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. The firms own a unique, licensable hospital corpus, and the insight is the invoice. But no outside party sets a deadline on their work. The unmeasured defect is that benchmarks drift with each hospital's coding intensity and with panel churn, and no firm publishes by how much.
