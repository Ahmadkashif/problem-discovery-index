# Post-Acute Assessment Software Adapted to the IPF-PAI

**Niche:** [[niches/psychiatric-hospitals/ipfqr-ipf-pai-reporting/profile|IPFQR Reporting & IPF-PAI Submission]]
**Industry:** [[industries/psychiatric-hospitals|Psychiatric Hospitals]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Software for the MDS, IRF-PAI and OASIS already captures, validates and submits standardised assessments to CMS, and needs the psychiatric instrument's items and FHIR route added.
**Tags:** #data-integration #compliance #automation #descriptive-statistics #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce a psychiatric facility's IPFQR measures and its new IPF-PAI assessments straight from the record, and whoever submits the highest share of fully complete IPF-PAI assessments takes the account.

## The Problem
Psychiatric facilities have never submitted patient-level assessments to CMS, and their EHRs have no assessment engine.

## What Already Exists
Post-acute assessment and validation software used by nursing homes, rehabilitation hospitals and home health agencies, and CMS's free PARIT application for manual entry.

## The Customization Gap
- the IPF-PAI item set, with mobility collected at admission only and special services, treatments and interventions at discharge only (Federal Register 2026-15588);
- mapping from behavioral EHR assessments rather than therapy and nursing assessments;
- the FHIR submission route, which CMS says is a first for a statutory quality programme;
- psychiatric unit staffing, where no MDS coordinator exists.

## Target Customer
Freestanding hospitals, health-system units and behavioral EHR vendors.

## Impact If Solved
CMS estimates the instrument at 12 minutes per patient (Federal Register 2026-15588). For a 100-bed hospital with ~3,000 stays a year that is ~600 staff hours of entry, most of which adapted software could fill from the record (estimate).
