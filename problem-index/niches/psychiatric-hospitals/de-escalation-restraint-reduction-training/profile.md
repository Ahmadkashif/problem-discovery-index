# De-escalation & Restraint-Reduction Training Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/psychiatric-hospitals|Psychiatric Hospitals]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Training companies certify hospital staff in verbal de-escalation and safe physical intervention, which CMS requires before staff apply restraint or seclusion (42 CFR 482.13). The Crisis Prevention Institute (CPI) markets Nonviolent Crisis Intervention to psychiatric hospitals and units for seclusion and restraint reduction, citing a 2004 study in which restraint on one unit fell 98% after training (search summary); the Mandt System is a named competitor (named from recall). Hospitals pay per trainer certification and per staff course.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research and evaluation staff at the largest provider (estimate); curriculum writers, instructional designers and trainers are production
**Output:** Training curricula, certification programmes, outcome claims and white papers
**Proprietary data:** The firm's own curriculum and training records; restraint outcomes stay with client hospitals
**Clock:** Staff certification renewal and accreditation readiness, both clients' clocks
**Buyer:** Chief nursing officer or director of staff development at the hospital
