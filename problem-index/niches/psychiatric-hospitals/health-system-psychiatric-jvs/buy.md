# Capacity Command Centres Extended to Psychiatric Beds

**Niche:** [[niches/psychiatric-hospitals/health-system-psychiatric-jvs/profile|Health-System Joint Ventures & Hospital-Based Psychiatric Units]]
**Industry:** [[industries/psychiatric-hospitals|Psychiatric Hospitals]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Health systems already run capacity command centres for medical beds, and they need psychiatric admission rules before the JV's beds appear on the same board.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to run a health system's inpatient psychiatric capacity for it, and whoever keeps the largest share of the system's psychiatric admissions in the system's own beds takes the account.

## The Problem
The command centre shows every medical bed in the system and none of the psychiatric beds the system half-owns.

## What Already Exists
Capacity and patient-flow platforms, profiled from the vendor side at [[niches/general-hospitals/capacity-periop-optimization-vendors/profile|🔍 Capacity, Patient-Flow & Perioperative Optimization Vendors]], plus EHR bed boards.

## The Customization Gap
- psychiatric bed attributes: unit type (adult, adolescent, geriatric, detox), gender and acuity mix, locked and seclusion rooms;
- admission exclusions and medical-clearance rules;
- legal status of the patient (voluntary, emergency hold);
- a feed from the JV's separate behavioral EHR.

## Target Customer
Health systems with a psychiatric JV or their own units, and the JV operator's admissions director.

## Impact If Solved
Putting JV beds on the system's board could cut transfers out by ~10% (estimate), about 120 admissions a year for a system sending 1,200 elsewhere.
