# Transfer-Centre Software Taught Psychiatric Admission Criteria

**Niche:** [[niches/psychiatric-hospitals/intake-ed-referrals-medical-clearance/profile|Psychiatric Intake, ED Referrals & Medical Clearance]]
**Industry:** [[industries/psychiatric-hospitals|Psychiatric Hospitals]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Health systems already run transfer-centre software that logs every inbound request and decision, and it needs psychiatric criteria and registry links before a freestanding psychiatric hospital can use it.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #large-language-models #automation #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn emergency-department referrals into admissions the unit can treat safely, and whoever has the lowest share of admissions transferred back to an acute hospital within 72 hours takes the account.

## The Problem
Psychiatric intake runs on a notebook and a fax machine while the referring hospital's transfer centre has a full case log.

## What Already Exists
Hospital transfer-centre and referral management software, and state bed registries from Bamboo Health, Carematch, CommCare and BH Link (NRI).

## The Customization Gap
- psychiatric exclusion criteria (medical instability, withdrawal risk, mobility, age bands, aggression);
- legal status of the hold and its expiry time;
- unit type and gender and acuity mix;
- two-way updates to the state registry;
- 42 CFR Part 2 consent handling for detox referrals.

## Target Customer
Freestanding psychiatric hospitals and chains' central intake teams.

## Impact If Solved
A structured log would let a hospital review every decline. If ~5% of the ~6,000 referrals a 100-bed hospital receives a year are declined for reasons a rule change could fix (estimate), that is ~300 reviewable decisions.
