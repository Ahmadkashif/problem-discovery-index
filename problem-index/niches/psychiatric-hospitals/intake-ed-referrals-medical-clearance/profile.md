# Psychiatric Intake, ED Referrals & Medical Clearance

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/psychiatric-hospitals|Psychiatric Hospitals]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn emergency-department referrals into admissions the unit can treat safely, and whoever has the lowest share of admissions transferred back to an acute hospital within 72 hours takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$0.4B a year of intake and assessment staff, transfer-centre time, registry software and telepsychiatry ED consults (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1.3% of receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — referrals arrive by phone and fax, and accept or decline decisions are logged in a spreadsheet or not at all
**Target Buyer:** Director of intake and admissions; chief nursing officer
**Automation Potential:** Medium-High for packet reading and routing; the accept decision stays clinical

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A freestanding psychiatric hospital has no medical floor. Every referral from an emergency department must be "medically cleared", and the intake clinician must judge from a faxed packet whether the patient's withdrawal, delirium, overdose, diabetes or injury can be managed on a psychiatric unit. A wrong yes sends the patient back to an acute hospital within days; a wrong no leaves them boarding in the ED, where older adults waited a median of 44 hours in one recent cohort (search summary). Twenty-five states run crisis bed registries, 14 of which include private psychiatric beds, on their own software or on Bamboo Health, Carematch, CommCare or BH Link (NRI, January 2026). Competitors are hospitals' own intake teams, health-system transfer centres, registry vendors and telepsychiatry ED-consult groups. Outpatient intake matching is a different contest, at [[niches/behavioral-health-clinics/intake-triage-matching/profile|⚡ Intake Triage & Patient-Clinician Matching]], and transport to the bed is [[niches/non-emergency-medical-transport/behavioral-health-transport/profile|🟣 Behavioral Health Transport]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Phone, fax, state registries and the intake clinician's experience. Gaps: no structured record of referrals, declines and reasons; no check of the packet against the unit's medical exclusion criteria; and no feedback on which accepted patients went back to a medical hospital.
