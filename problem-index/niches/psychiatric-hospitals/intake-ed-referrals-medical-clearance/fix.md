# Referral Packets Faxed Without the Labs

**Niche:** [[niches/psychiatric-hospitals/intake-ed-referrals-medical-clearance/profile|Psychiatric Intake, ED Referrals & Medical Clearance]]
**Industry:** [[industries/psychiatric-hospitals|Psychiatric Hospitals]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Emergency departments fax psychiatric referral packets that miss the toxicology, the blood alcohol level or the EKG the receiving unit needs, and the intake clinician spends the night calling back for them.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #automation #worker-facing #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn emergency-department referrals into admissions the unit can treat safely, and whoever has the lowest share of admissions transferred back to an acute hospital within 72 hours takes the account.

## The Problem
Each receiving hospital wants a slightly different set of tests, and the ED nurse faxing to five hospitals sends what she has.

## Why It's Still Broken
There is no standard psychiatric medical-clearance packet across hospitals, faxes cannot be checked automatically, and the ED gets no list of what is missing until someone calls.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Read each incoming fax or portal packet on arrival, check it against the unit's own clearance list, and send the ED a specific request for the missing items within minutes, logged against the referral.

## Who Feels the Pain
Night intake clinicians, ED nurses and social workers, and the patient waiting in the ED.

## Impact If Fixed
If each incomplete packet costs ~30 minutes of calls and half of ~6,000 annual referrals arrive incomplete (estimate), automating the request saves ~1,500 intake hours a year.
