# Legal Docketing Software Adapted to Psychiatric Holds

**Niche:** [[niches/psychiatric-hospitals/involuntary-hold-commitment-paperwork/profile|Involuntary-Hold & Commitment Paperwork]]
**Industry:** [[industries/psychiatric-hospitals|Psychiatric Hospitals]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Law firms already run rules-based docketing that computes deadlines from court rules, and a psychiatric hospital needs the same engine keyed to hold statutes and wired to its EHR.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #compliance #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep every involuntary hold, certification and court petition on its statutory clock, and whoever has the fewest holds ended by a procedural defect rather than a clinical decision takes the account.

## The Problem
Hospitals compute hold deadlines by hand, with weekends, holidays and court calendars varying by county.

## What Already Exists
Court-rules docketing and calendaring software used by litigation departments, and court e-filing portals.

## The Customization Gap
- mental health statutes as rule sets (hold types, durations, extensions, notice of rights);
- hold events read from the EHR rather than a case-management system;
- clinical roles (attending, designee, patient advocate) instead of attorneys;
- county hearing calendars and hearing-officer contacts.

## Target Customer
Chains' legal departments and risk managers at freestanding hospitals.

## Impact If Solved
If automated clocks prevent ~10 holds a year at a 100-bed hospital from lapsing on a missed deadline (estimate), that is ~10 unsafe releases or unlawful holdovers avoided.
