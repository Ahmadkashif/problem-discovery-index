# Coding-Audit Software Extended to Psychiatric Certification Rules

**Niche:** [[niches/psychiatric-hospitals/medicare-recertification-audit-defence/profile|Medicare Recertification, Active-Treatment Records & Audit Defence]]
**Industry:** [[industries/psychiatric-hospitals|Psychiatric Hospitals]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Hospital compliance teams already own audit-workflow software for coding reviews, and it needs the IPF documentation rules before it can test a psychiatric stay.
**Tags:** #compliance #data-integration #large-language-models #automation #evaluation-metrics
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make each Medicare and Medicaid psychiatric day defensible when an auditor reads the record later, and whoever has the lowest share of audited paid days recouped takes the account.

## The Problem
The compliance team audits psychiatric charts with the same checklist software it uses for DRG coding, and records findings by hand.

## What Already Exists
Audit-workflow and compliance tracking tools used by hospital coding auditors and by the firms profiled at [[niches/medical-billing/coding-audit-compliance-firms/profile|🔍 Coding Audit & Compliance Firms]].

## The Customization Gap
- 42 CFR 424.14 certification and recertification timing and signer rules;
- 42 CFR 482.61 treatment-plan and progress-note frequency checks;
- an active-treatment evidence test across groups, therapy and medication changes;
- Corporate Integrity Agreement sampling and reporting formats.

## Target Customer
Compliance officers at chains and at health systems with psychiatric units.

## Impact If Solved
Configuring existing audit software for IPF rules could cut each chart audit from ~90 to ~30 minutes (estimate), saving ~200 hours a year for a team auditing 200 charts.
