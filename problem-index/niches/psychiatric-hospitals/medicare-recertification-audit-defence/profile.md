# Medicare Recertification, Active-Treatment Records & Audit Defence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/psychiatric-hospitals|Psychiatric Hospitals]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make each Medicare and Medicaid psychiatric day defensible when an auditor reads the record later, and whoever has the lowest share of audited paid days recouped takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$0.4B a year of compliance staff, external audit and legal review, physician documentation time and audit software (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1% of receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — the record is electronic, but audit files are pulled and checked by hand
**Target Buyer:** Chief compliance officer; chain medical director
**Automation Potential:** Medium-High — the rules are written; the clinical judgement behind each day is not automatable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Medicare and fee-for-service Medicaid pay without reviewing the stay as it happens. Their check comes afterwards, through contractor audits, OIG reviews and, at the extreme, False Claims Act cases. The rules are specific. A physician certifies at admission, recertifies by day 12 and at least every 30 days (42 CFR 424.14). Each patient needs an individual comprehensive treatment plan, with progress notes at least weekly for the first two months (42 CFR 482.61), and the record must show active treatment rather than custody. Acadia's 2024 settlement and the DOJ Criminal Division investigation it disclosed both concern admissions, length of stay and billing (10-K). UHS signed a Corporate Integrity Agreement with OIG as part of its 2020 settlement (10-K exhibits). Competitors are chains' internal compliance audit teams, coding and compliance audit firms ([[niches/medical-billing/coding-audit-compliance-firms/profile|🔍 Coding Audit & Compliance Firms]]), healthcare law firms and physician-advisor services. The auditors themselves are [[niches/medical-billing/cms-program-integrity/profile|🔍 Federal Programme Integrity Contractors]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Sample-based internal audits, EHR documentation templates, and compliance tracking software built for coding. Gaps: no continuous check of every stay against IPF certification and active-treatment rules, and no way to see which units' records would fail an audit before one arrives.
