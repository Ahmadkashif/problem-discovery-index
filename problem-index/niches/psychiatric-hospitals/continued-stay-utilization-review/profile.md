# Continued-Stay Review & Medical-Necessity Defence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/psychiatric-hospitals|Psychiatric Hospitals]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get each psychiatric day the record supports paid and kept paid. But managed-care concurrent review is won by the lowest share of reviewed days denied, while Medicare recertification and audit defence is won by the lowest share of audited paid days recouped, with different staff, vendors and failure modes. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.0B a year of UR coordinator and physician-advisor time, outsourced review, denial and appeal work, and compliance audits across ~1,430 IPFs (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of receipts (estimate, against $32.0B in 2022 SUSB receipts)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — notes are electronic, but reviews are argued by phone and audit files are assembled by hand
**Target Buyer:** Director of utilization review / revenue cycle; chief compliance officer at chains
**Automation Potential:** High for evidence assembly; the necessity call stays with the psychiatrist

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every day in a psychiatric bed has to be justified twice: once to the payer reviewing it now, and once to whoever audits it later. The per diem falls from day 1 to day 10, Medicare needs recertification by day 12 and every 30 days after (42 CFR 424.14), and managed-care plans review every one to three days (estimate). Acadia, the largest chain, settled an investigation into medical necessity and length of stay for $19.9M in 2024 and disclosed a DOJ Criminal Division grand jury investigation into its admissions, length of stay and billing (10-K). The acute hospital's version is [[niches/general-hospitals/clinical-denials-status-appeals/profile|🎯 Clinical Denials & Inpatient Status Appeals]], and the payer's is [[niches/health-insurance-carriers/inpatient-post-acute-review/profile|🎯 Inpatient & Post-Acute Concurrent Review]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
UR modules in the EHR, InterQual and MCG behavioral criteria, payer portals, physician-advisor services ([[niches/general-hospitals/physician-advisor-utilization-review/profile|🔍 Physician Advisor & Utilization Review Services]], 44, kill switch), outsourced behavioral UR (SimiTree, Coronis Health; search summary) and compliance auditors. Gap: no tool reads psychiatric notes for necessity evidence, and the record argued to the payer is not the record an auditor checks.
