# Hospital UR Software With Behavioral Criteria Bolted On

**Niche:** [[niches/psychiatric-hospitals/continued-stay-utilization-review/profile|Continued-Stay Review & Medical-Necessity Defence]]
**Industry:** [[industries/psychiatric-hospitals|Psychiatric Hospitals]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Acute-care utilization review and case-management software already tracks reviews and criteria, but it reads necessity from labs and vital signs, not from behaviour described in notes.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #feature-engineering #data-integration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get each psychiatric day the record supports paid and kept paid. But managed-care concurrent review is won by the lowest share of reviewed days denied, while Medicare recertification and audit defence is won by the lowest share of audited paid days recouped, with different staff, vendors and failure modes. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Behavioral hospitals buy acute UR tools or work in spreadsheets, and the coordinator still reads every note herself.

## What Already Exists
EHR utilization management modules, admission-status tools such as XSOLIS, licensed InterQual and MCG behavioral health criteria, and payer portals.

## The Customization Gap
- note-level extraction of dangerousness, grave disability, failed lower levels of care and medication changes;
- payer-specific behavioral criteria and LOCUS or ASAM where states mandate them;
- legal-hold and court dates as review facts;
- Medicare's day-12 and 30-day recertification and weekly progress-note checks (42 CFR 424.14, 482.61).

## Target Customer
Freestanding psychiatric hospitals on Netsmart, Qualifacts or Kipu, and health systems whose units use the acute tool.

## Impact If Solved
Adapting an existing UR tool rather than running reviews from spreadsheets could save ~0.5 FTE of coordinator time per 50 beds, ~$40,000 a year (estimate).
