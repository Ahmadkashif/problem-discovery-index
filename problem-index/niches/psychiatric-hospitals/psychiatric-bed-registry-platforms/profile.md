# Psychiatric Bed Registries & Bed-Placement Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/psychiatric-hospitals|Psychiatric Hospitals]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Software vendors run the registries that show which psychiatric, crisis and detox beds are open across a state, and route referrals from emergency departments. Twenty-five states ran crisis bed registries in 2025; 14 include private psychiatric hospital beds, and the purchased systems include Bamboo Health (OpenBeds), Carematch, CommCare and BH Link (NRI, January 2026). Nebraska paid licensing fees for participating hospitals (search summary). States pay most of the licences; hospitals and health systems are users and data contributors.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 analysts and data staff per vendor across products (estimate)
**Output:** Live bed-availability boards, referral routing, utilisation and wait-time reports for state agencies
**Proprietary data:** Daily bed status and referral records submitted by hospitals in each state, the only cross-hospital view of psychiatric bed supply and demand
**Clock:** State contract renewals and legislative reporting dates, clients' clocks
**Buyer:** State behavioral health agency director; health-system behavioral service-line lead

**Kill switches:** The pocket mixes two data regimes, and the state-contract half carries the verdict.
- **State-contract registries: live (data isn't theirs).** Bed status reported by hospitals into a registry a state has bought is the state's and the hospitals' client data. Referrals that carry patient details move under business associate agreements, which are also live. No checked terms give the vendor product rights. All four vendors NRI names (Bamboo Health, Carematch, CommCare, BH Link) appear as systems that states purchased, so this half is where the occupants and the revenue are.
- **A private bed-finding network: undetermined.** A platform that sells its own referral network to hospitals and health systems would hold hospital-contributed data under its own platform terms, which were not read. No purely private network was sourced as an occupant.

**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. States buy software, not findings (Q1 2), the field is about four named vendors (Q5 2), and on the half that carries the verdict the data belongs to the hospitals and states. The registry is still the best public signal of psychiatric bed scarcity, and it feeds the intake niche's referral agent.
