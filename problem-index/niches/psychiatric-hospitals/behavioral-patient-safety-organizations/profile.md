# Behavioral Health Patient Safety Organizations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/psychiatric-hospitals|Psychiatric Hospitals]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Patient safety organizations (PSOs) listed by AHRQ collect safety events from member providers under federal privilege, analyse them and feed lessons back. Centerstone's PSO, certified in June 2022, was described as one of the first focused on behavioral health and one of 10 PSOs then serving behavioral providers (search summary). Providers join to get privileged analysis of suicides, assaults, restraint injuries and elopements. Fees are membership-based.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analysts and safety specialists per behavioral PSO (estimate)
**Output:** Event analyses, alerts and learning collaboratives for members
**Proprietary data:** Members' patient safety work product, pooled under the Patient Safety and Quality Improvement Act
**Clock:** None external; member meeting cycles
**Buyer:** Member chief quality officer
