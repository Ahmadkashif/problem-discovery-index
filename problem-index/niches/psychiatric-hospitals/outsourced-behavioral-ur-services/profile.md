# Outsourced Behavioral Utilization Review & RCM Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/psychiatric-hospitals|Psychiatric Hospitals]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation firms

## What They Do
Revenue cycle firms with behavioral health practices run concurrent reviews, benefit verification, billing and appeals for psychiatric and addiction facilities. SimiTree (through SimiTree Behavioral Health) and Coronis Health sell utilization review as part of behavioral RCM outsourcing; one Coronis case study describes a 450-bed Medicaid substance-use facility that had run billing, collections and UR in house with ~12 FTEs (search summaries). Facilities pay a percentage of collections or a per-review fee.

## Insight Function
**Size:** UR staff scale with client census, but they are per-case reviewers (production); analysts above them are under 10 per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Completed reviews, authorizations, appeals and collections
**Proprietary data:** Client charts and payer decisions held under business associate agreements
**Clock:** Payer review dates and appeal windows that bind each case, which is production work
**Buyer:** Facility chief financial officer
