# IPF PPS & Behavioral Payment Policy Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/psychiatric-hospitals|Psychiatric Hospitals]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation firms

## What They Do
Health policy consultancies read each year's IPF PPS proposed and final rules, model their impact on facilities and chains, draft comment letters, and advise on changes such as the FY2027 outlier cap and the new IPF-PAI. Applied Policy, which published summaries of both the FY2027 proposed and final IPF rules, is a sourced example (search summary). Hospitals, chains, state hospital associations and suppliers pay by subscription or engagement. The same firms cover every Medicare payment system, and their firm-wide policy teams are already profiled under health insurance carriers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** A few analysts per firm on the IPF rule (estimate), inside firm-wide Medicare policy teams of ~10–25 (estimate) that are counted at the linked pocket below
**Output:** Rule summaries, payment impact models, comment letters, regulatory briefings
**Proprietary data:** Little beyond public rules, CMS impact files and HCRIS cost reports; client cost and claims data for facility-level models is the client's
**Clock:** CMS's annual rule cycle (proposal in spring, final rule by early August, effective 1 October) and its 60-day comment windows, the regulator's own calendar
**Buyer:** Vice president of reimbursement or government relations at a chain or association
