# Psychiatric Hospital Association Survey (NABH)

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/psychiatric-hospitals|Psychiatric Hospitals]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The National Association for Behavioral Healthcare (NABH), successor to the National Association of Psychiatric Health Systems, surveys its member hospitals and systems each year on admissions, days, length of stay, payer mix, expenses and contracting. For 2020 the survey was run by its contractor, Dobson DaVanzo & Associates, and the report sold for $400 to non-members (search summaries). NABH also lobbies on the IPF PPS and IPFQR rules.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 staff on data and policy, with survey work contracted out (estimate)
**Output:** The annual survey report, comment letters and member briefings
**Proprietary data:** Member-reported operating data
**Clock:** NABH's own survey calendar and CMS comment windows
**Buyer:** Member hospital chief executives; NABH's policy director
