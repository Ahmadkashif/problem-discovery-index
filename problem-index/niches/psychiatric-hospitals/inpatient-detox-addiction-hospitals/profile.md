# Inpatient Detox & Addiction Hospitals

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/psychiatric-hospitals|Psychiatric Hospitals]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to carry patients through medically managed withdrawal into continuing treatment, and whoever has the highest share of detox discharges starting the next level of care within seven days takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$0.3B a year of software, placement, UR and aftercare-coordination spend at hospital-level detox and addiction units (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1% of receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — addiction EHRs such as Kipu are common, but placement into the next programme is phone calls and waitlists
**Target Buyer:** Addiction medicine director; chief executive of a detox hospital
**Automation Potential:** Medium — placement, authorization and consent paperwork automate; the withdrawal medicine does not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Hospital-level withdrawal management (ASAM Level 4, medically managed intensive inpatient; named from recall) treats patients whose withdrawal from alcohol, benzodiazepines or opioids is dangerous, under 24-hour nursing and physician care. Stays are short, three to seven days (estimate), and the outcome that matters happens after discharge: does the patient start residential treatment, an intensive outpatient programme or medication for opioid use disorder, or go back to using? Records fall under 42 CFR Part 2 as well as HIPAA. Acadia's specialty treatment facilities, mostly residential recovery facilities that also run detoxification and inpatient addiction programmes, earned $567.2M, 17% of its 2025 revenue (10-K); the residential half belongs to the queued residential treatment industry. Tools and teams were built for outpatient programmes, profiled at [[niches/behavioral-health-clinics/substance-use-disorder-programs/profile|🔵 Substance Use Disorder Treatment Programs]], or for residential treatment centres, which are queued as their own industry. Competitors are detox hospitals' own aftercare teams, chain-owned continuums that keep patients in-house, and placement and referral platforms. Billing is at [[niches/medical-billing/behavioral-health-billing/profile|Behavioral Health Billing]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Kipu, Netsmart and Qualifacts EHRs, ASAM criteria software, state registries that track detox beds in five states (NRI), and phone calls. Gaps: no live view of next-level openings with payer acceptance, and no record of which discharges actually started treatment.
