# Next-Level Placement Engine for Detox Discharges

**Niche:** [[niches/psychiatric-hospitals/inpatient-detox-addiction-hospitals/profile|Inpatient Detox & Addiction Hospitals]]
**Industry:** [[industries/psychiatric-hospitals|Psychiatric Hospitals]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A detox patient's best chance of staying in treatment is a confirmed next-level slot before discharge, and no product matches the patient to open, payer-accepted programmes while they are still on the unit.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #survival-analysis #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #worker-facing #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to carry patients through medically managed withdrawal into continuing treatment, and whoever has the highest share of detox discharges starting the next level of care within seven days takes the account.

## The Problem
On day three the aftercare coordinator starts calling residential programmes. Half have no bed, a third do not take the patient's plan, and the patient's motivation falls with every hour.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Downstream programmes do not publish availability, payer acceptance differs by plan and level, and Part 2 consent must be in place before any record moves.

## What to Build
A placement engine that keeps a live list of partner programmes by level, payer and opening; forecasts each patient's discharge day and risk of leaving early from withdrawal scores and engagement; starts consent and authorization on day one; and confirms a slot before discharge. It records whether the patient arrived. Demonstrations use synthetic patients.

## Target Customer
Detox hospitals and chains with detox units; payers and states funding care coordination.

## Impact If Built
If the share of detox discharges starting next-level care within seven days rises from ~40% to ~50% (estimate), a unit discharging 1,500 patients a year starts ~150 more of them in treatment.
