# Addiction EHRs Extended to Hospital-Level Withdrawal Units

**Niche:** [[niches/psychiatric-hospitals/inpatient-detox-addiction-hospitals/profile|Inpatient Detox & Addiction Hospitals]]
**Industry:** [[industries/psychiatric-hospitals|Psychiatric Hospitals]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Addiction-treatment EHRs were built for residential and outpatient programmes, and a hospital-level detox unit needs inpatient nursing, withdrawal scoring and hospital billing on top.
**Tags:** #data-integration #compliance #time-series-forecasting #automation #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to carry patients through medically managed withdrawal into continuing treatment, and whoever has the highest share of detox discharges starting the next level of care within seven days takes the account.

## The Problem
Detox hospitals run on software designed for programmes that bill per day of residential care, not for hospital units with withdrawal protocols, IV medications and IPF claims.

## What Already Exists
Kipu, Netsmart and Qualifacts, mapped from the vendor side at [[niches/healthcare-practice-software/behavioral-health-ehr/profile|🟠 Behavioral Health & SUD Practice Software]].

## The Customization Gap
- hourly CIWA-Ar and COWS scoring with trend alerts and protocol-driven orders;
- inpatient medication administration and IPF PPS claims;
- Part 2 consent tracking that travels with each disclosure to the next programme;
- a referral and placement module that records arrival.

## Target Customer
Freestanding addiction hospitals and psychiatric hospitals with detox units.

## Impact If Solved
If protocol alerts and placement in one system save each nurse ~30 minutes a shift (estimate), a 20-bed detox unit with eight nursing shifts a day saves ~4 nurse hours daily.
