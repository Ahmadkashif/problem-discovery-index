# Behavioral Environment-of-Care & Ligature-Risk Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/psychiatric-hospitals|Psychiatric Hospitals]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation firms

## What They Do
Specialist consultancies assess psychiatric units for ligature points and other suicide risks, advise architects on new units, and write the reference guidance. Behavioral Health Facility Consulting hosts the Behavioral Health Design Guide by James Hunt, David Sine and Kimberly McMurray, which grades unit areas into five risk levels and which The Joint Commission recommends hospitals use under NPSG 15.01.01 (search summaries). Hospitals and architects pay per assessment or project.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 consultants per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Environmental risk assessments, design reviews and the published guide
**Proprietary data:** The guide and the firm's library of past assessments, which are client work product
**Clock:** Joint Commission surveys and the 60-day window to correct ligature findings (search summary), clients' clocks
**Buyer:** Hospital facilities director or chief nursing officer; healthcare architects
