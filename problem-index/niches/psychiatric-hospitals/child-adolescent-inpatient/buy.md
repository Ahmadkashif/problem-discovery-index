# Family-Engagement Platforms Adapted to Locked Youth Units

**Niche:** [[niches/psychiatric-hospitals/child-adolescent-inpatient/profile|Child & Adolescent Inpatient Psychiatry]]
**Industry:** [[industries/psychiatric-hospitals|Psychiatric Hospitals]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Patient-engagement and telehealth platforms already schedule visits and video sessions, and youth psychiatric units need them reworked for guardians, custody rules and family therapy on a locked unit.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #compliance #worker-facing #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stabilise children and adolescents in crisis and return them to their families, and whoever has the lowest 30-day readmission rate for discharged youths takes the account.

## The Problem
Parents work, live far away or share custody, so family sessions are missed and the discharge plan is explained once, at the door.

## What Already Exists
Hospital patient-engagement and telehealth platforms, and the telehealth layer mapped at [[industries/telehealth-platforms|Telehealth Platforms]].

## The Customization Gap
- guardian and custody permissions, including who may visit or call;
- scheduled video family sessions with the unit therapist;
- the safety plan and medication changes in plain language for parents;
- school re-entry contacts with consent.

## Target Customer
Youth units at freestanding hospitals and children's hospitals.

## Impact If Solved
If video sessions lift family-session attendance from ~50% to ~75% (estimate), a 24-bed youth unit gains ~150 family sessions a year.
