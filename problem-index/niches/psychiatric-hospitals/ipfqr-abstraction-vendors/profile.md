# IPFQR & ORYX Measure Abstraction Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/psychiatric-hospitals|Psychiatric Hospitals]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Quality reporting vendors abstract chart-based measures and submit them for hospitals to CMS's quality programmes and to The Joint Commission's ORYX requirements. Medisolv publishes guidance on IPFQR reporting requirements and sells measure reporting to hospitals (search summary). Psychiatric facilities pay per measure set or by subscription, usually as a small add-on to acute-care contracts.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Abstractors are production; IPF-specific analysts number under 10 per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Abstracted measure files, submissions and dashboards
**Proprietary data:** Client charts and measure results held under business associate agreements
**Clock:** CMS's IPFQR submission deadlines, which bind the client facility
**Buyer:** Hospital quality director
