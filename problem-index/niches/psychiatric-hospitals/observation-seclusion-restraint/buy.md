# Virtual Sitting Systems Configured for Locked Units

**Niche:** [[niches/psychiatric-hospitals/observation-seclusion-restraint/profile|Observation Rounds, Seclusion & Restraint Records]]
**Industry:** [[industries/psychiatric-hospitals|Psychiatric Hospitals]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Virtual sitting was built for fall-risk patients on medical floors, and psychiatric units need it rebuilt around suicide risk, ligature points and patients who cover cameras.
**Tags:** #object-detection #cnns #workflow-orchestration #compliance #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep agitated and suicidal patients safe with the least coercion, and whoever has the fewest combined restraint and seclusion hours per 1,000 patient hours takes the account.

## The Problem
Psychiatric units spend whole technician shifts on 1:1 observation, and acute-care virtual sitting does not fit their rooms or risks.

## What Already Exists
AvaSure's virtual sitting, used for behavioral health and low-to-moderate suicide risk among its uses (search summary), and Oxehealth's contactless Oxevision, deployed at a Colorado behavioral campus in 2024 (search summary).

## The Customization Gap
- ligature-resistant, tamper-proof mounting and privacy zones for bathrooms;
- alerts for behaviours that matter on psychiatric units (door loitering, covering the camera, prolonged bathroom stays) rather than bed exits;
- policies on which observation levels may be virtual;
- consented or synthetic footage only for training and demonstration.

## Target Customer
Chief nursing officers at psychiatric hospitals and health-system units with high 1:1 hours.

## Impact If Solved
If a third of 1:1 hours for lower-risk patients move to virtual observation, a hospital averaging four 1:1s per shift frees ~32 bedside technician hours a day (estimate).
