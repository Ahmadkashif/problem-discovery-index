# Behavioral Health Facility Liability Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/psychiatric-hospitals|Psychiatric Hospitals]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Specialty insurers and programme administrators price professional and general liability for psychiatric hospitals and addiction facilities. MedPro Group markets liability coverage to behavioral health hospitals ($1M/$3M primary, excess to $5M/$5M), and Nationwide's Negley Associates programme covers alcohol and drug rehabilitation centres and mental health facilities (search summaries). Large chains self-insure and buy excess. Verdicts such as the 2024 Cumberland jury award against a UHS subsidiary ($60M compensatory, $180M trebled; UHS 10-K) drive pricing.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Behavioral-specialist underwriters and risk consultants under 10 per carrier (estimate); actuaries serve all lines
**Output:** Quotes, risk-control visits and programme pricing
**Proprietary data:** The carrier's own claims experience on behavioral facilities
**Clock:** Policy renewal dates, the client's clock
**Buyer:** Chief financial officer or risk manager at the facility
