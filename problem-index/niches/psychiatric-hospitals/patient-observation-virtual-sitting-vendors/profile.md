# Patient Observation & Virtual Sitting Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/psychiatric-hospitals|Psychiatric Hospitals]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors sell camera and contactless-sensor systems that let one remote observer watch several patients, replacing some 1:1 sitters. AvaSure describes 1,100–1,200+ hospital partners and virtual sitting used for behavioral health and low-to-moderate suicide risk; Oxehealth's Oxevision opened its first US behavioral deployment at SummitStone Health Partners in Colorado in 2024 (search summaries). Hospitals pay by subscription per monitored bed. Their clinical and data teams publish outcome studies and benchmarks to support sales.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 clinical, data science and outcomes staff per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Event detection and alerts, sitter-hour savings reports, outcome studies and customer benchmarks
**Proprietary data:** Observation events and alert histories across client hospitals; video stays with the client
**Clock:** None external; client budget cycles and Joint Commission survey readiness are clients' clocks
**Buyer:** Chief nursing officer at the hospital; the vendor's chief clinical officer
