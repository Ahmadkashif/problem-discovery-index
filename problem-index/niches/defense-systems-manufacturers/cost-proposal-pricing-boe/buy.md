# Proposal Content Tools Extended to Cost Volumes

**Niche:** [[niches/defense-systems-manufacturers/cost-proposal-pricing-boe/profile|Cost-Type Proposal Pricing & Bases of Estimate]]
**Industry:** [[industries/defense-systems-manufacturers|Defense Systems Manufacturers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Proposal platforms already reuse and draft technical narrative from a content library; the cost volume needs the same help tied to cost data and audit rules.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #evaluation-metrics #compliance #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce cost volumes whose bases of estimate survive government evaluation, and whoever's proposals draw the fewest questioned hours in DCAA and DCMA review takes the account.

## The Problem
Technical volumes now get AI drafting from content libraries, while the cost volume, which decides profit, is still assembled by hand from spreadsheets and old bases of estimate.

## What Already Exists
Proposal content and capture platforms (Loopio, GovDash, Deltek's proposal tools, Unanet) draft and reuse narrative and track requirements; ProPricer builds the cost model (recalled).

## The Customization Gap
The cost-volume layer needs: links from narrative to cost-system actuals; FAR 15.408 Table 15-2 structure; certified cost or pricing data rules that depend on award date and value ($10M after 30 June 2026); disclosed cost accounting practices and forward pricing rates; traceability that DCAA and DCMA can follow; and on-premises operation for export-controlled and classified programmes, which rules out most cloud proposal tools.

## Target Customer
Pricing and proposal operations leaders at contractors already using a proposal platform for technical volumes.

## Impact If Solved
Extending a familiar tool to the cost volume could cut basis-of-estimate drafting time by a third or more (estimate) without a new procurement, and puts technical and cost narratives on one record.
