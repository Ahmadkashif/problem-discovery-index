# Cost-Type Proposal Pricing & Bases of Estimate

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/defense-systems-manufacturers|Defense Systems Manufacturers]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce cost volumes whose bases of estimate survive government evaluation, and whoever's proposals draw the fewest questioned hours in DCAA and DCMA review takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$500M a year of pricing software, parametric tools, proposal support and estimator time on development, production and sole-source proposals at the five codes' manufacturers (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1% of the five codes' receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — pricing tools compute the numbers, but bases of estimate are Word documents written from old proposals
**Target Buyer:** Director of estimating and pricing at a prime or tier-1
**Automation Potential:** High for evidence retrieval and narrative drafting; the estimate and certification stay with the estimator and the company

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Most money in this industry is negotiated rather than competed on price alone: development contracts, sole-source production lots, multi-year missile buys and spares. Each proposal needs a cost volume in the FAR 15.408 format, bases of estimate for every work element, and, above the threshold, certified cost or pricing data; the FY2026 NDAA raised that threshold to $10M for contracts after 30 June 2026. DCAA reviewed $788B of contract costs and reported $18.8B of audit exceptions in FY2025 (advisory-firm summary). The work is mechanical in shape (find the analogy, show the actuals, write the narrative), repeated hundreds of times per proposal, under a contracting officer's due date. Research firms' administrative proposal compliance is a different contest, held in [[niches/contract-research-labs/solicitation-and-proposal-compliance/profile|Solicitation Monitoring & Proposal Compliance]], and small shops' spares quotes sit in [[niches/aerospace-parts-suppliers/small-shops-government-spares/profile|Small Shops Bidding Government Spare Parts]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
ProPricer and Excel for cost build-up, Deltek Costpoint or SAP for actuals, SEER and TruePlanning for parametric estimates, and proposal content tools for narrative reuse. Gaps: no link between a basis of estimate and the actuals it cites; analogies chosen from memory; and evaluation findings from past negotiations never fed back.
