# Test Data Platforms Adapted to Air-Gapped Ranges

**Niche:** [[niches/defense-systems-manufacturers/test-data-reduction-reporting/profile|Test Data Reduction & Test Reporting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/defense-systems-manufacturers|Defense Systems Manufacturers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Hardware test data platforms already ingest, store and plot telemetry for rocket and vehicle makers; defence ranges add air gaps, range formats and government test reporting.
**Tags:** #autoencoders #feature-engineering #data-integration #compliance #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn every test event's telemetry into findings before the next event, and whoever catches the most anomalies before the next test is run takes the account.

## The Problem
Defence test organisations want modern data platforms but test on government ranges with their own data formats, in facilities with no internet connection, and must report in formats their customers specify.

## What Already Exists
Test and operations platforms such as Nominal (which advertises self-hosted and air-gapped deployments for spacecraft and defence programmes), NI/Emerson SystemLink and DIAdem, and in-house data lakes (recalled except Nominal's job postings).

## The Customization Gap
Defence ranges need: ingest of range telemetry standards and recorder formats; deployment on accredited, disconnected networks with cross-domain transfer of approved results; channel-level classification markings; government test report and data package formats; and integration with failure reporting systems used by programme offices.

## Target Customer
Test organisations at primes and new entrants, and the government ranges that host their events.

## Impact If Solved
A platform that runs inside the range enclave replaces per-programme scripts and makes past events searchable, cutting quick-look preparation from a day to hours (estimate).
