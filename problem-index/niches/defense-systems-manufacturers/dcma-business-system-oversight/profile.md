# DCMA Business-System & EVMS Oversight

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/defense-systems-manufacturers|Defense Systems Manufacturers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Defense Contract Management Agency determines whether contractors' earned value systems comply with EIA-748 when DoD is the customer, reviews purchasing, property and other business systems, and runs surveillance at contractor plants through industrial specialists and quality assurance representatives (recalled). Its findings can trigger withholds and corrective action plans, and its earned value analysts test monthly contractor data.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–60 analysts in the earned value and business-system review functions (estimate); plant-level industrial specialists and quality assurance representatives work contract by contract and are production
**Output:** Compliance determinations, review findings, earned value data test results, surveillance reports
**Proprietary data:** Contractors' earned value data, system descriptions and review histories across the industrial base
**Clock:** Review schedules the agency sets, and the 180-day integrated baseline review window on each contract
**Buyer:** Agency leadership; a single buyer
