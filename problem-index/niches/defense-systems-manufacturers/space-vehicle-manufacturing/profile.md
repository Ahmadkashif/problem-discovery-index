# Space Vehicle & Satellite Bus Manufacturing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/defense-systems-manufacturers|Defense Systems Manufacturers]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver space vehicles to their contracted launch slots, and whoever ships the highest share of vehicles to their slot takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.0B a year of PLM, MES, integration-and-test, supply-chain and test data tooling and services at US space vehicle and bus makers (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% of the five codes' receipts (estimate); 336414, which holds complete space vehicles with missiles, took $19.5B of 2022 receipts (SUSB)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium-high — newer bus makers run modern factory software, but integration-and-test schedules and subsystem readiness are tracked in spreadsheets and daily stand-ups
**Target Buyer:** VP of production or programme director for a constellation contract
**Automation Potential:** Medium — schedule risk and test data automate; integration stays hands-on

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Proliferated constellations turned satellites from one-off builds into production runs, mostly on fixed-price contracts with launch dates set by the launch provider and the customer. The binding risk is a late subsystem: SDA's first operational tranche of more than 150 satellites slipped about six months because suppliers could not scale optical terminals and encryption devices as fast as promised (Air & Space Forces, November 2024), and SDA later hired an integrator for Tranche 3. Primes (Lockheed Martin, Northrop Grumman, L3Harris, Boeing's Millennium) compete with newer bus makers such as York, Apex and K2 Space (recalled). Operating the satellites is [[industries/satellite-operators|Satellite Operators]]; the manufacturers' on-orbit support teams are profiled there as [[niches/satellite-operators/manufacturer-on-orbit-support/profile|🔍 Satellite Manufacturers' On-Orbit Fleet Support]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
PLM (Siemens Teamcenter, 3DEXPERIENCE), factory and quality software (First Resonance ION, iBASEt), test data platforms (Nominal and in-house tools), and schedule tools. Gaps: subsystem readiness predicted from supplier promises; integration-and-test anomalies resolved vehicle by vehicle; no shared record of which subsystem lots caused rework across a tranche.
