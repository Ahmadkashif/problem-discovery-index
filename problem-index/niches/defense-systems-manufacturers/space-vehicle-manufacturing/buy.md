# Factory Software Adapted to Constellation Production

**Niche:** [[niches/defense-systems-manufacturers/space-vehicle-manufacturing/profile|Space Vehicle & Satellite Bus Manufacturing]]
**Industry:** [[industries/defense-systems-manufacturers|Defense Systems Manufacturers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Factory operating systems built for rockets and eVTOLs already run work instructions, quality and traceability; constellation programmes add tranche-level configuration, government security rules and launch-driven sequencing.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #feature-engineering #data-integration #compliance #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver space vehicles to their contracted launch slots, and whoever ships the highest share of vehicles to their slot takes the account.

## The Problem
Bus makers building dozens of near-identical vehicles need as-built configuration, nonconformance and test records per vehicle, rolled up by tranche, and they must show the government customer that every vehicle matches its baseline.

## What Already Exists
First Resonance's ION factory OS serves rocket, satellite and eVTOL makers with work instructions, quality and traceability, and its forward deployed engineers integrate it on site. iBASEt Solumina and Siemens Opcenter serve the primes (recalled).

## The Customization Gap
Constellation production needs: tranche-level configuration baselines with per-vehicle deviations; government-furnished encryption and payload items tracked under custody rules; integration sequencing driven by launch manifests; environmental test campaign records linked to vehicles; ITAR and, for some payloads, classified enclaves with no cloud dependency; and export of the government's acceptance data package.

## Target Customer
Manufacturing and quality leaders at newer bus makers and at primes moving satellite lines to rate.

## Impact If Solved
A configuration and quality record built per tranche cuts the acceptance data package for each vehicle from weeks of assembly to days (estimate) and makes cross-vehicle nonconformance trends visible before they repeat.
