# Defense Market & Programme Forecast Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/defense-systems-manufacturers|Defense Systems Manufacturers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Janes, Forecast International, GlobalData's aerospace and defence unit and similar firms (recalled) track weapons programmes, inventories and budgets, and sell forecasts of procurement quantities, funding and production by programme, country and contractor. Their raw material is largely public: budget justification books, appropriations, contract announcements and open-source reporting. Primes' market-intelligence and strategy teams, suppliers sizing markets and investors pay by subscription.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–150 defence analysts and editors per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Programme and market forecasts, inventory and equipment databases, budget analysis, contractor market shares
**Proprietary data:** Years of their own programme forecasts and equipment databases compiled from open sources, plus analyst judgement on quantities and timing
**Clock:** The vendor's own release calendar, timed to the President's Budget and appropriations; no deliverable is due to a third party
**Buyer:** Head of research or content at the vendor; primes' directors of market intelligence on the buy side

**Kill switches:** none — the products are built from public budget documents and open sources; classified programme detail is out of scope by construction.
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. The forecast archive is gradable (every quantity forecast resolves against enacted budgets and contract awards, and CNAS's FY2027 analysis shows how far requests and production can diverge), but inputs are public and anyone with a model can now read the budget books, the release clock is the vendor's own, and a handful of firms share the market. It scores with [[niches/aerospace-parts-suppliers/aerospace-fleet-production-forecasts/profile|Aerospace Fleet & Production Forecast Data]] (43) on every criterion but Q3, where it takes 3 rather than 4 because defence forecasts rest on public budget books with no curated aircraft-by-aircraft history behind them, and level with [[niches/satellite-operators/space-industry-research-houses/profile|Space Industry Research Houses]] (40). Opportunity forecasting for contractors is separate: [[niches/contract-research-labs/federal-opportunity-intelligence/profile|Federal R&D Opportunity Intelligence]] (46).
