# Discrete-Manufacturing MES Adapted to Government Ammunition Plants

**Niche:** [[niches/defense-systems-manufacturers/artillery-ordnance-lap-plants/profile|Artillery & Ordnance Metal Parts and Load-Assemble-Pack Plants]]
**Industry:** [[industries/defense-systems-manufacturers|Defense Systems Manufacturers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Commercial MES already digitises travellers, genealogy and SPC; ammunition plants add explosive hazard rules, government-owned equipment and lot acceptance testing.
**Tags:** #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn new and modernised artillery lines into accepted lots, and whoever raises the share of projectile bodies and loaded lots accepted on first submission takes the account.

## The Problem
Ammunition plants modernising for the ramp need digital travellers, lot genealogy and process monitoring, but most commercial systems assume a factory where any tablet can go anywhere and the company owns the equipment.

## What Already Exists
Discrete-manufacturing MES and quality suites (Siemens Opcenter, Rockwell's Plex and FactoryTalk, Tulip-style frontline apps) with electronic travellers, genealogy and SPC (recalled).

## The Customization Gap
Ammunition plants need: hazard-classified areas where only intrinsically safe devices are allowed; explosive weight and quantity-distance limits tracked by bay; genealogy from metal-parts lots through explosive-fill lots to packed rounds; lot acceptance sampling plans and destructive test results tied to lots; government property accountability for government-owned equipment; and records that survive a change of operating contractor.

## Target Customer
Operations and quality directors at contractor-operated Army ammunition plants and new metal-parts plants.

## Impact If Solved
A digital lot record lets a plant trace a lot acceptance failure to its process conditions in hours instead of weeks of paper review (estimate), and it outlasts the operating contract.
