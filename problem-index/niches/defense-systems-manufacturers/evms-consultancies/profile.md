# EVMS Consultancies & Surveillance Advisors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/defense-systems-manufacturers|Defense Systems Manufacturers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Specialist firms such as Humphreys & Associates and earned value practices inside government-contract consultancies (recalled) write contractors' EVMS system descriptions, prepare them for DCMA compliance reviews and integrated baseline reviews, train control account managers, and run mock surveillance. Contractors pay by engagement, often when a first large cost-type award or a failed review forces the issue.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Consultants writing one client's system description or running one mock review are per-engagement production; no cross-client research or benchmarking team of 10+ could be named at any firm (estimate)
**Output:** System descriptions and procedures, readiness assessments, mock review findings, CAM training
**Proprietary data:** Templates and accumulated knowledge of how DCMA tests systems; client system descriptions, schedules and earned value data held under NDA
**Clock:** DCMA compliance reviews and integrated baseline reviews within 180 days of award (DFARS 252.234-7999 under the February 2026 deviation) — dates on the client's contract, so a client clock
**Buyer:** Practice leader or firm principal
