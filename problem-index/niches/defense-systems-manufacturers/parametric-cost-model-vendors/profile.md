# Parametric Cost Estimating Model Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/defense-systems-manufacturers|Defense Systems Manufacturers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Galorath (SEER) and PRICE Systems (TruePlanning) sell parametric cost models that estimate development and production cost from technical parameters such as weight, complexity, quantity and technology readiness, calibrated on knowledge bases of past programmes (recalled). Prime estimating groups use them to cross-check bases of estimate and build should-cost positions; government cost agencies use them for independent estimates. Revenue is software licences, knowledge-base subscriptions, training and consulting.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 cost researchers and modellers per vendor (estimate); consultants supporting individual client estimates are per-estimate production and are not counted
**Output:** Calibrated cost estimating relationships and knowledge bases, model releases
**Proprietary data:** Each vendor's own knowledge base of programme cost and technical data, built over decades from published data, its own research and, reportedly, user-contributed calibration data (recalled)
**Clock:** Self-set model and knowledge-base releases; clients' proposal and milestone dates bind the users, not the vendor
**Buyer:** Chief methodologist or head of research at the vendor; directors of estimating at primes and cost agency chiefs on the buy side

**Kill switches:** none for the half built from published data and the vendors' own research; undetermined (terms unread) for the half built from user-contributed calibration data, whose aggregate-use terms were not read. Client estimates and any government contractor cost data stay on the client's systems and are outside the pocket.
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. A real moat in calibrated history, but the research team grows with technology domains rather than with estimate volume, releases are self-scheduled, and buyers are a few hundred estimating shops dominated by the primes and government cost agencies. Scored against [[niches/contract-manufacturing/should-cost-modeling-vendors/profile|🔍 Should-Cost Modelling Vendors]] (49, scored before the release-calendar ruling): one point lower on each of Q2 (domain growth, not volume), Q3 (defence cost data are thinner and partly government-held), Q4 (the release-calendar ruling) and Q5 (a concentrated buyer base). The prediction-without-grading shape is the same: neither group publishes how its estimates compared with actual costs.
