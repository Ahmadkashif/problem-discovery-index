# Defense Budget & Industrial-Base Think Tanks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/defense-systems-manufacturers|Defense Systems Manufacturers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Defence programmes at CSIS, CNAS, AEI, Hudson and similar institutes analyse budgets, munitions production and the industrial base and publish free reports; CNAS's October 2026 analysis of the FY2027 munitions request is an example. Funding comes from foundations, corporate donors and government grants rather than from buyers of the reports.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 fellows and research staff in a large institute's defence budget and industrial-base programmes (estimate)
**Output:** Free reports, budget analyses, testimony and events
**Proprietary data:** Their own budget and production databases built from public budget justification books and contract data
**Clock:** The President's Budget release, NDAA and appropriations calendars shape timing, but publication dates are the institute's own
**Buyer:** Programme director; funders rather than customers
