# Defense Strategy & Market-Entry Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/defense-systems-manufacturers|Defense Systems Manufacturers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Avascent, Renaissance Strategic Advisors and the aerospace and defence practices of larger consultancies (recalled) advise primes, suppliers, new entrants and investors on market entry, capture strategy, portfolio and M&A, and price-to-win. Engagements combine budget and programme analysis with interviews and the client's own data, and end in a strategy deck or a diligence report.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–150 consultants and analysts per firm or practice (estimate)
**Output:** Market assessments, capture and price-to-win analyses, commercial due diligence, portfolio strategy
**Proprietary data:** Engagement archives (client pipelines, cost data, competitor assessments) held under NDAs for named clients, plus the firm's own budget and programme models
**Clock:** Clients' bid and deal deadlines (a contracting officer's due date or a deal close), which are the client's clock
**Buyer:** Managing partner or practice leader
