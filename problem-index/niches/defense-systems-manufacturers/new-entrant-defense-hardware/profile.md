# New-Entrant Defense Hardware Makers Crossing into Production

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/defense-systems-manufacturers|Defense Systems Manufacturers]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor serving these firms is fighting to get a prototype maker through its first production contract's business-system reviews, and whoever gets the most clients' accounting, estimating and earned value systems approved without a payment withhold takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$250M a year of government-contract accounting, ERP, earned value, pricing and compliance tooling and advice bought by venture-backed and new defence hardware makers (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.5% of the five codes' receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — modern engineering and factory stacks, but government-contract finance bolted on late in spreadsheets and outsourced accounting
**Target Buyer:** CFO or VP of finance and contracts at a growth-stage defence hardware company
**Automation Potential:** Medium — policy drafting, cost tracking and readiness checks automate; negotiations and audits are human

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A new generation of missile, motor, munitions and space vehicle makers (Anduril, Castelion, Ursa Major, X-Bow, Apex and others, recalled) entered through prototype other transaction agreements, SBIR awards and fixed-price commercial-style deals that carry few cost-accounting obligations. GAO reported DoD prototype OTA obligations above $16B in FY2024 (search summary). A production award changes the rules: cost or incentive contracts bring Cost Accounting Standards, DCAA audits, earned value from $50M and, from 30 June 2026, certified cost or pricing data above $10M. A company with a world-class factory and a two-person contracts team can fail a business-system review and see payments withheld. This differs from research firms moving prototypes into the next phase, held in [[niches/contract-research-labs/defense-applied-research/profile|Defense & Intelligence Applied R&D]] and [[niches/contract-research-labs/sbir-microfirms/profile|SBIR-Funded Microfirms]], and from established small shops in [[niches/contract-manufacturing/veteran-owned-defense-contractors/profile|Veteran-Owned Defense Contractors]]. The accounting-system half overlaps [[niches/contract-research-labs/cost-accounting-indirect-rates/profile|Government Cost Accounting & Indirect Rates]], which holds the DCAA accounting-system review and incurred-cost submission for small research firms. The split: that niche wins on getting one set of books and indirect rates accepted at a 5–100-person lab; this one covers a manufacturer's whole set of business systems at once (accounting, estimating, purchasing, material, property and earned value) at production scale, judged by approvals without a payment withhold.

## Current Tools & Gaps
NetSuite or another startup ERP, then Deltek Costpoint or Unanet for government accounting; outsourced government-contract accountants (scored as [[niches/contract-research-labs/govcon-indirect-rate-advisors/profile|🔍 Government Contract Accounting & Indirect-Rate Advisors]]); no earned value system until one is required. Gaps: cost history from prototype work not captured in a form DCAA accepts; system descriptions written from templates; readiness unknown until the review.
