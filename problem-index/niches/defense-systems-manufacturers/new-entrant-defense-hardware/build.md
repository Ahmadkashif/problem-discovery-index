# Business-System Readiness for a First Production Contract

**Niche:** [[niches/defense-systems-manufacturers/new-entrant-defense-hardware/profile|New-Entrant Defense Hardware Makers Crossing into Production]]
**Industry:** [[industries/defense-systems-manufacturers|Defense Systems Manufacturers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A hardware startup that won on prototypes finds out at its first DCAA or DCMA review whether its accounting, estimating, purchasing and earned value systems meet the rules.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #evaluation-metrics #compliance #workflow-orchestration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor serving these firms is fighting to get a prototype maker through its first production contract's business-system reviews, and whoever gets the most clients' accounting, estimating and earned value systems approved without a payment withhold takes the account.

## The Problem
DFARS defines six contractor business systems (accounting, estimating, purchasing, earned value, material management and property), and significant deficiencies can lead to withholds from payments (recalled). A new entrant moving from an OTA prototype to a cost-type production contract must stand up all of them at once while ramping a factory. Its policies are written from templates, its timekeeping was built for a startup, and nobody has checked them against the criteria the government will test.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The market was small: for decades few new companies reached production scale in this industry. Advice was sold by consultants one engagement at a time, and the criteria are spread across the FAR, DFARS, the Cost Accounting Standards and DCAA and DCMA guidance.

## What to Build
A readiness engine that maps each business-system criterion to evidence in the company's own systems (timekeeping records, charging practices, estimating procedures, purchase files, the earned value system description) and runs mock tests the way auditors do: sampling transactions, tracing estimates to actuals and checking the earned value data. It drafts system descriptions from how the company actually works and tracks each gap to closure.

## Target Customer
CFOs and contracts leads at venture-backed defence hardware makers within a year of their first cost-type or large production award.

## Impact If Built
Avoiding one payment withhold on a production contract worth hundreds of millions of dollars (estimate) protects cash at the moment a growth company can least afford to lose it.
