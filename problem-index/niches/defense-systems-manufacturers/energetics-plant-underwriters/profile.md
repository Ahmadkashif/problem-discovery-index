# Energetics & Munitions Plant Underwriters

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/defense-systems-manufacturers|Defense Systems Manufacturers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Property, casualty and environmental insurers and brokers that underwrite contractor-owned energetics, propellant and ammunition plants, using hazard surveys, process safety reviews and loss histories. The October 2025 explosion at Accurate Energetic Systems in Tennessee, which killed 16 people (Breaking Defense), is the kind of loss they price. Government-owned plants are largely self-insured by the government (recalled).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically under 10 specialist underwriters and risk engineers per carrier or broker team for explosives risks (estimate)
**Output:** Risk surveys, underwriting decisions, premiums
**Proprietary data:** Plant hazard surveys and loss records
**Clock:** Policy renewal dates
**Buyer:** Head of the specialty line
