# Explosives Safety & Munitions Site Regulators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/defense-systems-manufacturers|Defense Systems Manufacturers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The DoD Explosives Safety Board and the services' explosives safety offices approve site plans and quantity-distance arrangements for munitions plants and storage, and investigate accidents; ATF licenses commercial explosives manufacturers, and OSHA's process safety rules apply to contractor plants (recalled). Their reviews gate new and expanded energetics and load-assemble-pack capacity.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Reviewers and inspectors working site plan by site plan are production; any cross-plant analysis function is plausibly under 10 (estimate)
**Output:** Site plan approvals, safety surveys, accident investigations, standards
**Proprietary data:** Site plans, hazard analyses and accident records
**Clock:** Applicants' construction schedules and the agencies' own review cycles
**Buyer:** Agency leadership; a single buyer
