# DoD Cost Assessment & Service Cost Agencies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/defense-systems-manufacturers|Defense Systems Manufacturers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
OSD's Cost Assessment and Program Evaluation office and the services' cost agencies (the Air Force, Navy and Army cost centres, and missile defence cost staff) build independent cost estimates for major programmes before milestone decisions, maintain the department's cost databases built from contractors' cost and software data reports, and review programme offices' estimates (recalled). Their output steers what the government will pay and when programmes proceed.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Analysts building one programme's estimate at a time are production; the cross-programme database and cost-estimating-relationship teams are plausibly under 10 in each organisation (CAPE and each service agency counted separately, estimate)
**Output:** Independent cost estimates, cost databases and cost estimating relationships, milestone cost reviews
**Proprietary data:** Decades of contractor-reported cost, hours and technical data by programme and work breakdown element, the deepest cost history of the defence industrial base
**Clock:** Milestone and budget decision dates set by the department itself
**Buyer:** Agency leadership; effectively a single government buyer
