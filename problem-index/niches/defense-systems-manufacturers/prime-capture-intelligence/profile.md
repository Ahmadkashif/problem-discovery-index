# Prime Capture & Competitive Intelligence Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/defense-systems-manufacturers|Defense Systems Manufacturers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms (operator check: the primes that aggregate the supply base)

## What They Do
Each large prime runs market-intelligence, capture and price-to-win teams that track budgets, requirements and competitors, forecast which programmes will be competed, and advise bid decisions and pricing (recalled). Their work feeds the company's own bids; nothing is sold.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–50 analysts at the largest primes (estimate); capture managers working one pursuit each are production
**Output:** Market forecasts, competitor assessments, bid/no-bid and price-to-win analyses for internal leaders
**Proprietary data:** Years of the company's own pursuit history (bids, prices, wins and losses, debriefs) alongside purchased market data
**Clock:** Request-for-proposal due dates set by contracting officers, a client clock
**Buyer:** VP of strategy or business development; a handful of firms

**Kill switches:** live on the classified-programme half — pursuits on classified programmes and any source-selection information are barred by statute and security rules; none on the unclassified market-analysis half, which scores the same 30 and is below threshold on its own.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch (classified half); the unclassified half is below threshold. The pursuit archive is a gradable asset (every bid resolves as a win or a loss on a known date) that primes rarely analyse, but the insight is not invoiced and only a handful of firms could buy a tool for it. The market data they purchase comes from [[niches/defense-systems-manufacturers/defense-market-forecast-data/profile|Defense Market & Programme Forecast Data]] and [[niches/contract-research-labs/federal-opportunity-intelligence/profile|Federal R&D Opportunity Intelligence]].
