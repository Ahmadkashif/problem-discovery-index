# Operational Test & Evaluation Oversight

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/defense-systems-manufacturers|Defense Systems Manufacturers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Director, Operational Test and Evaluation, and the services' operational test agencies plan and assess operational testing of weapon systems, and DOT&E reports to Congress on whether systems are operationally effective and suitable before full-rate production (recalled). Their assessments shape production decisions and fixes that manufacturers must make.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Tens to about a hundred DOT&E action officers and analysts working across programmes, supported by federally funded research centres (estimate); service-agency evaluators assigned to a single programme are production and are not counted
**Output:** Test plans, operational assessments, beyond-low-rate production reports, an annual report
**Proprietary data:** Operational test data, failure and reliability records across programmes, much of it classified
**Clock:** Statutory reporting before full-rate production decisions and the annual report to Congress
**Buyer:** Department leadership and Congress; no commercial buyer
