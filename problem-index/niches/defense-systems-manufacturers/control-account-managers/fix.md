# The Variance Explained the Same Way for Nine Months

**Niche:** [[niches/defense-systems-manufacturers/control-account-managers/profile|The Control Account Manager]]
**Industry:** [[industries/defense-systems-manufacturers|Defense Systems Manufacturers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The same cause is copied into the variance report month after month, with a corrective action that never closes, and nobody notices until the estimate jumps.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #evaluation-metrics #compliance #worker-facing #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give the control account manager an estimate at completion that holds, and whoever's estimates move least between the month they are signed and contract completion takes the account.

## The Problem
Under deadline, CAMs reuse last month's variance narrative with updated numbers. A corrective action ("supplier recovery plan in place") can appear unchanged for most of a year while the variance grows. Programme control reviews dozens of narratives each close and can't track every thread.

## Why It's Still Broken
Narratives are stored as text attached to a period, not as a thread of causes and actions per account. Nothing compares this month's explanation with last month's.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Compare each new narrative with the account's prior ones: flag repeated causes, corrective actions open beyond a set number of periods, and variances that grew while the explanation stayed the same. Show programme control a short list of accounts whose stories have stalled.

## Who Feels the Pain
CAMs who know the account is drifting but have no prompt to escalate, programme managers surprised by estimate jumps, and DCMA reviewers testing narrative quality.

## Impact If Fixed
Catching stalled explanations a few months earlier gives a programme time to act on a drifting account before the estimate change hits the quarter.
