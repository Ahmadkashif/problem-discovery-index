# Aviation MRO Software Adapted to Ground-Vehicle Reset

**Niche:** [[niches/defense-systems-manufacturers/combat-vehicle-production-reset/profile|Combat Vehicle Production & Depot Reset]]
**Industry:** [[industries/defense-systems-manufacturers|Defense Systems Manufacturers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Aviation maintenance software already handles routine and non-routine work, findings and parts demand at heavy checks; armoured vehicle reset needs the same workflow on different data.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to return vehicles from production and reset on the contracted date, and whoever cuts the share of vehicles held for parts discovered only at teardown takes the account.

## The Problem
Reset lines manage hundreds of task cards per vehicle and a stream of non-routine findings, much as an aircraft heavy check does, but without the software aviation built for it.

## What Already Exists
Aviation MRO systems (IFS Maintenix, Ramco, AMOS, TRAX) manage task cards, non-routine cards, findings, parts and labour against check packages, and aviation shops have years of findings history in them (recalled; see [[industries/aircraft-mro-shops|Aircraft MRO & FBO Shops]]).

## The Customization Gap
Ground-vehicle reset needs: technical manual references in Army formats; battle-damage assessment and hull weld repair procedures; configuration upgrades applied during reset; government-furnished parts and depot supply rules; ITAR-controlled technical data; and findings captured on rugged devices in a heavy industrial plant, with free-text notes turned into structured codes.

## Target Customer
Operations and IT leaders at armoured vehicle plants and Army depots, and contractors bidding reset work.

## Impact If Solved
Structured non-routine findings, which aviation already collects, would give reset lines their first analysable findings history within a year of adoption.
