# Supply Planning Suites Pointed at a Missile Ramp

**Niche:** [[niches/defense-systems-manufacturers/missile-interceptor-production-ramps/profile|Missile & Interceptor Production Ramps]]
**Industry:** [[industries/defense-systems-manufacturers|Defense Systems Manufacturers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Commercial supply planning software already models multi-tier materials and capacity; it was built for consumer and industrial volumes, not for qualified sources, government-furnished equipment and acceptance tests.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #monte-carlo-methods #feature-engineering #data-integration #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is: every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to raise delivered rounds per month on a qualified missile line, but the shortage that caps the rate sits in two places with different winners — sub-tier motors and their energetic ingredients, where whoever delivers qualified motors at rate wins, and the prime's own final assembly and acceptance test, where whoever raises first-pass yield wins. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Primes already plan materials in their ERP and, at some, in advanced planning tools. The ramp question breaks those tools: sources can't be switched without qualification, government-furnished equipment arrives on the government's schedule, and the last constraint is often test-station time or yield rather than material.

## What Already Exists
Kinaxis RapidResponse, o9, SAP Integrated Business Planning and Oracle supply chain planning model multi-echelon supply, capacity and scenarios, and are used in aerospace and defence (recalled).

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: qualification status and requalification lead time as hard constraints on any alternate source; government-furnished equipment and DPA-funded capacity as modelled supply; acceptance-test stations and first-pass yield as capacity nodes; lot-based energetics with shelf-life and hazard-class storage limits; and deployment on premises or in an accredited enclave, since the data are CUI or classified.

## Target Customer
Supply-chain and production planning directors at primes and tier-1s that already license a planning suite.

## Impact If Solved
Extending an existing planning investment to model qualification and test constraints avoids a new procurement and turns a monthly slide-deck exercise into a weekly, testable forecast.
