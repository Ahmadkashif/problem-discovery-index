# Defense Industry Association Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/defense-systems-manufacturers|Defense Systems Manufacturers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The National Defense Industrial Association, the Aerospace Industries Association and the Association of the US Army publish industrial-base reports, policy papers and member surveys, and run conferences that are their main revenue (recalled). NDIA's annual industrial-base report has been produced with an outside data partner (recalled).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research staff per association (estimate); policy and events staff are larger
**Output:** Industrial-base and policy reports, surveys, testimony
**Proprietary data:** Member surveys and partner data
**Clock:** Self-set publication calendars around conferences and the budget cycle
**Buyer:** Association president or research director
