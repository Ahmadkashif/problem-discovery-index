# Fleet Utilisation & Turnover

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/rideshare-fleet-operators|Rideshare Fleet Operators]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Whether the days a vehicle sits between renters can be predicted and compressed, rather than managed by whoever answers the phone.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.08B — 18% of US rideshare fleet services
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~18%
**Digital Adoption:** Low — a waiting list, a phone and a parking lot
**Target Buyer:** Fleet operations managers and owners
**Automation Potential:** High — demand forecasting and turnaround scheduling are well-posed

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche

A vehicle idle between renters earns nothing and depreciates anyway, and the gap between one driver leaving and the next starting is managed by whoever answers the phone.

Utilisation is the other half of fleet economics. Pricing determines revenue per productive day; utilisation determines how many productive days there are. An operator with a $340 weekly rate and 82% utilisation earns less than one at $310 and 94%, and most operators track the rate closely and the utilisation loosely.

The niche is distinct because the losses are operational and specific: the two days between a driver returning a car and the next renter being approved, the week a vehicle sits waiting for a part, the vehicle held for a renter who never turned up, the car parked because nobody realised the registration lapsed. Each has a cause, each is addressable, and together they are the difference between a fleet that works and one that does not.

## Current Tools & Gaps

Fleet management platforms track vehicle status, contracts and availability, and most operators maintain a waiting list of prospective renters. Telematics shows whether a vehicle is moving. The mechanics of the handover — inspection, cleaning, paperwork, insurance, platform re-approval — are manual and mostly ad hoc.

The gaps are prediction and sequencing. Nobody forecasts when a rental will end, though the signals are strong and visible weeks ahead. Nobody schedules the turnaround as a process with a target duration. And nobody prices the idle day, so the decision to hold a vehicle for a preferred renter versus releasing it to the next person on the list is made without knowing what the wait costs.
