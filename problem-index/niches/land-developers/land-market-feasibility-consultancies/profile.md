# Residential Land Market Feasibility Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/land-developers|Land Developers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Real estate economics firms write market and absorption studies for master-planned communities, land purchases, district bond offerings and lenders: how many lots of which product will sell at what price and pace. RCLCO publishes an annual ranking of top-selling MPCs (recalled), and the advisory arms of Zonda and John Burns do similar work.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Consultants each writing studies for one project at a time; any cross-client research group behind rankings is under 10 per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Market feasibility and absorption studies per project, lot pricing recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Study archives and the firms' own surveys of competing community sales
**Clock:** The client's land closing, bond pricing or loan date
**Buyer:** Managing principal
