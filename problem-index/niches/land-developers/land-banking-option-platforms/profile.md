# Land Banking & Homesite Option Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/land-developers|Land Developers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Capital providers that buy land or finished lots and hold them under option for production builders, who pay an option fee and take lots down on a schedule. Millrose Properties, spun off from Lennar in February 2025 with $5.5B of land and $1.0B of cash and managed by Kennedy Lewis, is the largest. Lot bankers do the same deal by deal: Forestar sold 927 lots in fiscal 2025 to lot bankers who expect to sell them on to D.R. Horton. The builder pays for capital, not analysis.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 portfolio research and analytics staff per platform (estimate); the underwriters pricing each option are production, and Millrose itself has no employees and is run by its external manager
**Output:** Option underwriting (lot price, fee, deposit, takedown schedule), portfolio monitoring, investor reporting
**Proprietary data:** Takedown performance, deposits and renegotiations across every builder, market and option the platform holds
**Clock:** Contractual takedown dates and quarterly investor reporting; the takedowns are dates in the platform's own contracts
**Buyer:** Chief investment officer or head of asset management
