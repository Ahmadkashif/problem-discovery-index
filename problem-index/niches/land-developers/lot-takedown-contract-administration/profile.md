# Lot Takedown & Option Contract Administration

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/land-developers|Land Developers]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to close every scheduled takedown on its date with the right lots, escalated price and deposit credit, and whoever posts the fewest takedown closings needing correction takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M a year of finance, contract-administration and closing labour at developers and land bankers (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% of receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — contracts are executed electronically, but takedown schedules, escalators and deposit credits are tracked in Excel
**Target Buyer:** Controller or VP of finance at a lot developer or land-banking platform
**Automation Potential:** High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Land-light homebuilding has turned the lot option into a large contract asset. Each agreement fixes quotas, deposits, escalators, delivery conditions and remedies. Land-banking platforms and lot bankers add a layer in which they own the lots and the builder pays an option fee. Lennar placed $5.5B of land into Millrose in 2025. Forestar held $193.3M of builder earnest money at the end of fiscal 2025 and sold 927 lots through lot bankers. Administering takedowns is mechanical, high in volume and easy to get slightly wrong, so it is the most automatable work in the business. It is separate from delivering the lots physically, which is [[niches/land-developers/finished-lot-supply-production-builders/profile|Finished-Lot Supply to Production Builders]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Contract lifecycle tools (Ironclad, DocuSign CLM), real estate and construction accounting (Yardi, MRI, Sage Intacct), [[industries/title-and-settlement-offices|Title & Settlement Offices]] for each closing, and builders' own land systems. Gaps: no lot-level ledger, escalators and deposit credits computed by hand, and quotas and cure periods tracked by memory.
