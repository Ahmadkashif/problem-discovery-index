# Contract Lifecycle Management Adapted to Lot Options

**Niche:** [[niches/land-developers/lot-takedown-contract-administration/profile|Lot Takedown & Option Contract Administration]]
**Industry:** [[industries/land-developers|Land Developers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** CLM tools store contracts and track dates; a lot option needs the lot, the quota and the escalator as computable objects.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to close every scheduled takedown on its date with the right lots, escalated price and deposit credit, and whoever posts the fewest takedown closings needing correction takes the account.

## The Problem
Developers who own a CLM still run takedowns in Excel, because the CLM knows the contract's key dates but not its lots or formulas.

## What Already Exists
Ironclad, DocuSign CLM and Agiloft for contract storage, clause extraction and obligation dates.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs lots as records linked to plats and phases; quota schedules with cure rules; escalator formulas (fixed, indexed, home-price share) as computations; deposit crediting per lot; delivery conditions tied to the developer's milestones; and the land-banker layer where a platform owns the lots.

## Target Customer
Finance and legal teams at developers and land bankers already on a CLM.

## Impact If Solved
Extending the CLM to compute takedowns gives one system of record for contract and closing, and saves ~1–2 days of reconciliation per quarterly takedown cycle (estimate).
