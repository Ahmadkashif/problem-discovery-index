# Land Feasibility & Site Intelligence Software Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/land-developers|Land Developers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Software vendors selling site feasibility, layout and land intelligence to developers and builders: TestFit (generative site layouts), TraceAir (drone site intelligence and a lot-yield estimator for homebuilders and land developers), Acres (land data and an AI research agent) and similar tools. Customers pay subscriptions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Product and engineering teams; customer projects are processed by production staff (TraceAir's careers board listed one GIS assistant in Almaty, Kazakhstan on 2026-10-24)
**Output:** Layouts, yields, earthwork quantities and constraint reports for customers' own tracts
**Proprietary data:** Customers' project files and scans
**Clock:** Customers' feasibility periods and construction schedules
**Buyer:** Head of product
