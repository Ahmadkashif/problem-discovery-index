# Homebuilder & Land-Use Association Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/land-developers|Land Developers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The National Association of Home Builders' economics group publishes the builder sentiment index, cost-of-construction surveys and studies such as the 2026 estimate that regulation adds $131,734 to a new home's price, $46,795 of it during lot development. The Urban Land Institute publishes development research and convenes land developers. Both are funded mainly by members, sponsors and events, and their studies support advocacy as much as business decisions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 economists and research staff per organisation (estimate, per organisation)
**Output:** Member surveys and indices, cost and regulation studies, forecasts, policy research
**Proprietary data:** Member and builder survey responses collected over decades
**Clock:** The associations' own release calendars and annual conferences
**Buyer:** Chief economist or head of research
