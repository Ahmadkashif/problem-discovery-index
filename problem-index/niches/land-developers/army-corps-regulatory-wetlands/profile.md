# Army Corps Regulatory Program & Wetlands Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/land-developers|Land Developers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The US Army Corps of Engineers' Regulatory Program decides Section 404 permits and jurisdictional determinations for fill in wetlands and streams. District project managers evaluate individual permits and verify nationwide permits, and headquarters policy staff and the Engineer Research and Development Center maintain the delineation manuals and wetland science behind them. The 2026 Nationwide Permits took effect on 15 March 2026 and run to 2031, and a complete pre-construction notice is deemed authorised after 45 days without a response.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Tens of policy and research staff at headquarters and ERDC who maintain methods and analyses (estimate); the district project managers who evaluate each permit are production
**Output:** Permit decisions, jurisdictional determinations, nationwide permit rules, delineation manuals and regional supplements
**Proprietary data:** The national record of permit applications, determinations, impacts and mitigation (ORM), much of it releasable on request
**Clock:** Regulatory clocks such as the 45-day PCN default and the five-year nationwide permit cycle
**Buyer:** Chief of the Regulatory Program
