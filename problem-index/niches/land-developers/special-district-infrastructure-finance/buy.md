# Construction Accounting Adapted to District Eligibility

**Niche:** [[niches/land-developers/special-district-infrastructure-finance/profile|Special-District Infrastructure Finance]]
**Industry:** [[industries/land-developers|Land Developers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Construction accounting already holds every cost the district will reimburse; it lacks the eligibility dimension the reimbursement needs.
**Tags:** #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #compliance #large-language-models #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a developer's infrastructure spend into district bond reimbursement, and whoever recovers the largest share of eligible cost in each bond series takes the account.

## The Problem
Developers code costs by job and cost code for their own books. District eligibility cuts across those codes, so the reimbursement is rebuilt outside the system.

## What Already Exists
Sage 300 CRE, Sage Intacct, Yardi and Viewpoint job-cost accounting; Procore for commitments and pay applications; lien-waiver tools.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs an eligibility dimension on every cost line (district, facility category, eligible share, developer contribution where the rules require one); support tracking (waiver, payment proof, engineer certification) per line; allocation rules for shared facilities; and an export in the format the district engineer's bond application and the auditor's review expect.

## Target Customer
Controllers at developers with several districts, already on a construction accounting platform.

## Impact If Solved
Coding eligibility at entry gives the developer a live figure for reimbursable cost outstanding and removes the annual rebuild, ~100–200 accountant hours per district per year (estimate).
