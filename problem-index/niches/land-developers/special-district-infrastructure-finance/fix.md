# The Missing Lien Waiver That Waits for the Next Bond Series

**Niche:** [[niches/land-developers/special-district-infrastructure-finance/profile|Special-District Infrastructure Finance]]
**Industry:** [[industries/land-developers|Land Developers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A cost without its final lien waiver or proof of payment is dropped from the reimbursement and waits years for the next bond sale.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #large-language-models #compliance #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a developer's infrastructure spend into district bond reimbursement, and whoever recovers the largest share of eligible cost in each bond series takes the account.

## The Problem
District auditors require evidence that each reimbursed cost was paid and released. Waivers are collected inconsistently at payment time, and by the bond application some are missing.

## Why It's Still Broken
Payment and reimbursement happen years apart and are run by different people. Nothing at payment time checks that the support the reimbursement will need is on file.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Check for the conditional and unconditional waiver and proof of payment on every district-eligible payment as it is made, and request any missing item from the subcontractor that week.

## Who Feels the Pain
Developer accountants, district engineers assembling bond applications, and developer CFOs waiting on cash.

## Impact If Fixed
If 2% of a ~$30M reimbursement lacks support, a payment-time check keeps ~$0.6M in the first series rather than one ~2 years later, ~$0.12M of financing cost at ~10% a year (estimates), at no extra cost.
