# Land-Use & Zoning Law Practices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/land-developers|Land Developers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Land-use attorneys and the planners who work with them carry developers' rezonings, planned developments, district creations and utility agreements through staff review and public hearings, and negotiate development agreements. Large firms have land-use groups in growth metros; much of the work is done by local specialists who know each council.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Attorneys and planners each working their own clients' cases; no cross-matter research function of 10+ was found at any firm (estimate)
**Output:** Applications, hearing presentations, negotiated conditions and development agreements, per case
**Proprietary data:** Case files and the attorneys' knowledge of each jurisdiction, held as client matters
**Clock:** Hearing dates and filing deadlines on the client's case, which can be continued
**Buyer:** Practice group leader
