# Special-District Management Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/land-developers|Land Developers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Firms that manage community development districts and utility districts for their boards: budgets, assessment rolls, meetings, bond compliance and continuing disclosure. In Florida a handful of district management firms run most of the 1,067 CDDs; in Texas, engineering, bookkeeping and operator firms serve MUD boards. The developer controls the board in the early years.

## Insight Function
**Size:** District managers each running a portfolio of districts; analytical staff under 10 per firm (estimate)
**Output:** District budgets, assessment rolls, meeting packets, disclosure filings
**Proprietary data:** Operating records across hundreds of districts; the records themselves are public
**Clock:** Statutory budget and assessment calendars and annual disclosure deadlines
**Buyer:** Firm principal
