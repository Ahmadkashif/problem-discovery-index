# Subdivision Improvement Bond Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/land-developers|Land Developers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Surety companies write the subdivision improvement bonds that cities and counties require so a developer can record a plat or sell lots before streets, utilities and sidewalks are finished. The bond guarantees the public improvements; the developer is the principal and the city the obligee. Letters of credit from banks do the same job.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Commercial surety underwriters handling subdivision bonds within broader contract-surety departments; dedicated staff under 10 per carrier (estimate)
**Output:** Bond approvals, indemnity terms, developer credit reviews
**Proprietary data:** Bond and claim history by developer and jurisdiction
**Clock:** The developer's plat recording and lot-sale dates
**Buyer:** Head of commercial surety
