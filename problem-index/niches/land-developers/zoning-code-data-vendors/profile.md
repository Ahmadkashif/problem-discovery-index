# Zoning & Land-Use Code Data Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/land-developers|Land Developers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Vendors that turn municipal zoning ordinances and maps into structured, parcel-level data: district standards (density, lot size, setbacks, height, parking), permitted and conditional uses, and overlays. Zoneomics, Gridics and UrbanFootprint sell this to developers, brokers, lenders and cities, and parcel platforms such as Regrid and LightBox carry zoning layers (recalled; not individually checked). Clients pay subscriptions or per-parcel API fees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 methodology and research staff per vendor (estimate); the analysts who encode each jurisdiction's code are production, the zoning counterpart of coders
**Output:** Structured zoning districts and standards per parcel, use permissions, change alerts, buildable-envelope estimates
**Proprietary data:** The encoded rule library across thousands of jurisdictions; the ordinances themselves are public
**Clock:** The vendor's own refresh cycle after cities amend their codes; no third-party deadline
**Buyer:** Head of data or product
