# Impact Fee & Proffer Study Consultants

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/land-developers|Land Developers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Consultants that prepare the impact-fee studies cities and counties adopt to charge new development for roads, parks, water, sewer and schools, and the fiscal-impact analyses behind proffers and development agreements. Their studies set fees that are a material part of the regulatory cost in a lot (NAHB's 2026 study put $46,795 of regulatory cost per home at the lot-development stage).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Small practices of a few to about twenty staff writing one jurisdiction's study at a time (estimate)
**Output:** Impact-fee and fiscal-impact studies per jurisdiction, fee schedules, nexus analyses
**Proprietary data:** Study models and fee schedules; adopted studies become public documents
**Clock:** Statutory update cycles where states require them, and city budget calendars
**Buyer:** Practice principal
