# Legislative Tracking Adapted to Local Zoning Cases

**Niche:** [[niches/land-developers/discretionary-rezoning-public-hearings/profile|Discretionary Rezoning & Public Hearings]]
**Industry:** [[industries/land-developers|Land Developers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Government-affairs software tracks bills through legislatures; a developer needs the same tracking for zoning cases through planning commissions and councils.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to carry rezoning and planned-development cases from filing to a council approval at the density the deal needs, and whoever gets the highest share of filed cases approved at the requested lot count takes the account.

## The Problem
Developers and their attorneys follow cases by reading agendas. They learn of competing or precedent-setting cases nearby by chance.

## What Already Exists
FiscalNote and Quorum track state and federal legislation, members and votes. Hamlet monitors local meetings and transcripts with keyword alerts.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: cases as objects (case number, parcel, request, district) rather than bills; staff reports and conditions parsed; links between agenda items for the same case; parcel-level alerts for cases near a tract; commissioner-level vote history on land-use items; and continuance and time-to-decision statistics.

## Target Customer
Land-use attorneys and entitlement teams; government-affairs staff at large builders.

## Impact If Solved
Case-level tracking replaces agenda-reading, ~3–5 hours a week per entitlement manager (estimate), and surfaces precedent cases before a hearing rather than after.
