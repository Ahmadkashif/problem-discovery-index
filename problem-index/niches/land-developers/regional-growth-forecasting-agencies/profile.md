# Regional Planning Agencies' Growth Forecasts

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/land-developers|Land Developers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Metropolitan planning organisations and councils of governments forecast population, households and jobs by small area for their long-range transportation plans, and in some states allocate housing targets to cities. Developers and builders use the forecasts to judge where growth and roads are going. The forecasts are free.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 demographers, land-use modellers and GIS analysts at each large MPO (estimate, per agency)
**Output:** Small-area population, household and employment forecasts; development pipeline inventories; land-use model runs
**Proprietary data:** Local development pipeline surveys and land-use model inputs assembled from member cities and counties
**Clock:** Federally required updates of the metropolitan transportation plan every four or five years (recalled)
**Buyer:** Director of research or planning at the agency
