# National Builders' Land Acquisition & Market Research Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/land-developers|Land Developers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The land and market research groups at national and large regional homebuilders, and at public lot developers such as Forestar (433 employees across 64 markets in fiscal 2025), underwrite land deals and lot contracts across many divisions. They set market assumptions, review land committee packages and track community performance. They are the largest buyers of finished lots and of land data.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–50 land analysts and market researchers at each national builder's headquarters (estimate, per builder); division land managers working deal by deal are production
**Output:** Land committee packages, market studies, community pro formas, post-investment reviews
**Proprietary data:** Every land deal and lot contract the builder has made, with its underwriting and its realised sales pace and margin
**Clock:** The builder's own land committee calendar and quarterly reporting
**Buyer:** Chief land officer or VP of land acquisition
