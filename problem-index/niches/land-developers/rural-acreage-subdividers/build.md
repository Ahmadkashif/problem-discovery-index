# Saleability Check for Every Tract Carved

**Niche:** [[niches/land-developers/rural-acreage-subdividers/profile|Rural Acreage Subdividers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/land-developers|Land Developers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Rural subdividers draw tract lines by eye and find the access, septic or floodplain problem after the sale, when public data could check each tract before it is cut.
**Tags:** #optimization-fundamentals #gradient-boosting #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to carve large rural acreage into tracts a buyer can legally reach, build on and finance, and whoever sells the highest share of the tracts it carves within a year takes the account.

## The Problem
A land company buying a 600-acre ranch decides how to cut it into tracts: how many, what sizes, where the roads and easements go, which tracts front the county road. Each tract has to meet the county's rules (platting exemption, frontage, access) and be buildable for its buyer (a septic-suitable area outside the floodplain, a likely well). The cut decides both price and how fast tracts sell.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The segment is thousands of small firms and landowners with no software budget, and county rules vary in ways no national tool has encoded.

## What to Build
A tool that takes a parcel and the county, pulls soils (septic suitability), floodplain, slope, existing roads and easements, and proposes tract layouts that satisfy the county's exemption or minor-plat and access rules. It scores each tract's buildability and likely price from comparable sales, and flags any tract that fails a rule before the survey.

## Target Customer
Rural land companies and investors subdividing several properties a year, and the surveyors who lay out their tracts.

## Impact If Built
Cutting tracts that all meet access and septic rules removes the unsaleable tract and its later dispute, ~$50–100K per avoided dispute (estimate). A layout tuned to what sells shortens each property's sell-out by ~6 months (estimate), which is half a year of carry and note servicing saved.
