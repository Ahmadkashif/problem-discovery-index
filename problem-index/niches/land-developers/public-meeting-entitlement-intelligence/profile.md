# Public-Meeting & Entitlement Intelligence Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/land-developers|Land Developers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Start-ups that record, transcribe and summarise city council, planning commission and zoning board meetings and sell searchable alerts to developers, data-center companies and others who need to know what local governments are deciding. Hamlet monitors 3,000+ bodies and counts real estate developers among its first customers (search summary). Zonda has announced a "Zonda Land AI" product.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Small software teams; summarisation is automated with light curation, and no analyst function of 10+ was found (estimate)
**Output:** Meeting transcripts and summaries, keyword and parcel alerts, topic sentiment indices
**Proprietary data:** An archive of transcribed meetings; the underlying meetings and agendas are public
**Clock:** Meeting calendars set by local governments
**Buyer:** Founder or head of product
