# State Special-District Bond Review

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/land-developers|Land Developers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State agencies that oversee development districts. In Texas, TCEQ reviews municipal utility district bond applications (engineering reports, feasibility and the amount that may be issued) and the Attorney General reviews each issue (search summary). Florida's Department of Commerce tracks special districts' registration and reporting.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Engineers and financial analysts each reviewing applications; no separate research function of 10+ was found (estimate)
**Output:** Bond approvals with conditions, district registries
**Proprietary data:** Every application's engineering report and feasibility analysis, which are public records
**Clock:** Statutory review steps for each application
**Buyer:** Program manager
