# Project Tracking Adapted to Entitlement Cases

**Niche:** [[niches/land-developers/entitlement-and-approvals/profile|Entitlement & Approvals]]
**Industry:** [[industries/land-developers|Land Developers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Land teams already track cases in Smartsheet or a land system; the tracker needs to understand jurisdictions, staff-comment rounds and hearing agendas to be more than a list.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #large-language-models #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is "every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a tract from contract to approved lots inside the purchase contract's feasibility and extension periods," but that fight splits two ways. Where the zoning must change, it is a public, political contest at planning commission and council, won by whoever gets the highest share of filed cases approved at the requested lot count. Where the land is already zoned, or sits in an unzoned county, the binding constraint is water and sewer, and the contest is won by whoever secures binding commitments covering the highest share of planned lots before closing. Different counterparties, consultants and clocks, so it is decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Case trackers are filled by hand and go stale. The entitlement manager still reads every agenda to find the case and keeps comment responses in Word files.

## What Already Exists
Smartsheet, Asana and Excel trackers; land-management modules in builder ERPs; Procore for later construction phases.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs jurisdiction objects with their meeting calendars and submittal deadlines; automatic agenda and staff-report matching to each case; staff comments parsed into trackable items by department and round; standard case types (rezoning, planned development, preliminary and final plat, variance, utility agreement, district creation); and dated milestones that feed a timeline forecast.

## Target Customer
Entitlement managers at developers and builders already using a shared tracker.

## Impact If Solved
A tracker that fills itself from public agendas and comment letters gives management real milestone dates and removes ~3–5 hours of agenda-reading a week per entitlement manager (estimate).
