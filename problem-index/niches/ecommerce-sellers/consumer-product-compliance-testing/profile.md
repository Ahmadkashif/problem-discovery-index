# Consumer Product Compliance Testing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ecommerce-sellers|E-Commerce Sellers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Testing laboratories certify consumer products against safety and chemical regulations — children's product certificates, restricted substance limits, state chemical disclosure rules — and issue the reports marketplaces increasingly demand before allowing a listing. A missing certificate is a suspended listing.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-1,000 chemists, engineers, and technical reviewers per softlines and hardlines division
**Output:** Test reports, certificates of compliance, restricted substance findings
**Proprietary data:** Failure history by product category, factory, and material across thousands of clients — held under client confidentiality
**Clock:** Listing deadlines, import entry, and regulatory effective dates
**Buyer:** VP of Consumer Products / Technical Director
