# E-Commerce Share Measurement Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ecommerce-sellers|E-Commerce Sellers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Stackline, Similarweb's commerce practice, and NIQ's e-commerce group combine brand-contributed retail sales data, panel measurement, and continuous marketplace scraping into category share, pricing, and assortment analytics sold to manufacturers and large retailers. The brands buying it cannot otherwise see their own share in channels that publish nothing.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 data scientists, category analysts, and taxonomists
**Output:** Category share, pricing and assortment analytics, digital shelf measurement — the subscription
**Proprietary data:** Contributed brand sales data joined to continuous marketplace observation and a product taxonomy maintained across millions of listings
**Clock:** Brand planning cycles and quarterly reporting
**Buyer:** VP of Insights / Chief Analytics Officer
