## What They Do
Bazaarvoice, PowerReviews, and Trustpilot syndicate customer reviews across retailer sites and analyze review content for brands — extracting product quality signals, comparing against competitors, and detecting fraudulent review activity.

**Clock:** Continuous; product launch cycles for clients
