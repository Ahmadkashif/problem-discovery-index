# E-Commerce Business Brokers & Valuation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ecommerce-sellers|E-Commerce Sellers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Quiet Light, Empire Flippers, Website Closers, and the specialist brokers value and market online businesses — reconstructing true earnings from marketplace statements, assessing account health and concentration risk, and producing the valuation a buyer diligences against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-80 analysts, brokers, and diligence staff
**Output:** Business valuations, diligence packages, market multiple reporting
**Proprietary data:** Completed transaction terms and post-sale performance in a market with no public price reporting
**Clock:** Deal timelines and marketplace account transfer windows
**Buyer:** Managing Director / Head of Valuation
