# Marketplace Trust & Safety Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ecommerce-sellers|E-Commerce Sellers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The trust and safety organizations inside Amazon, Walmart, and eBay detect counterfeit, abusive, and policy-violating sellers, adjudicate appeals, and set the enforcement rules that determine which businesses survive on the platform. The Pass 1 note identifies account health as an existential risk for sellers; this is the function that decides it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-5,000 investigators, policy analysts, and data scientists
**Output:** Enforcement decisions, policy rules, seller risk models
**Proprietary data:** Complete platform transaction, listing, and enforcement history — the most complete view of marketplace seller behaviour anywhere
**Clock:** Enforcement service levels and appeal windows
**Buyer:** No external buyer — internal platform function
