# Marketplace Aggregator Diligence Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ecommerce-sellers|E-Commerce Sellers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The diligence and portfolio analytics groups at the marketplace brand aggregators evaluate seller businesses for acquisition — assessing true margins, review authenticity, keyword defensibility, and supply concentration — and manage the acquired portfolio afterward.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-80 diligence analysts and portfolio operators
**Output:** Acquisition diligence, portfolio performance analytics
**Proprietary data:** Diligence findings and post-acquisition performance across hundreds of acquired brands — a direct record of which pre-purchase signals predicted anything
**Clock:** Deal timelines
**Buyer:** VP Corporate Development / Head of Diligence
