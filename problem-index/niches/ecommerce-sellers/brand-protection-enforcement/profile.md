# Brand Protection & IP Enforcement Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ecommerce-sellers|E-Commerce Sellers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Red Points, Corsearch, and MarkMonitor detect counterfeit and infringing listings across marketplaces, social platforms, and standalone sites, build the evidence package, and prosecute takedowns at volume. Analysts and investigators determine what is actually infringing — a judgment marketplaces will not make and rights holders cannot make at scale — and the enforcement outcome is what clients pay for.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-800 analysts, investigators, and data scientists
**Output:** Infringement determinations, evidence packages, takedown enforcement, seller network analysis
**Proprietary data:** Detection and takedown history across millions of listings joined to outcomes, plus the seller network mapping that shows which operations reconstitute after removal
**Clock:** Marketplace takedown windows, counterfeit sales during peak retail periods, and litigation timelines
**Buyer:** VP of Brand Protection / Chief Operating Officer
