# E-Commerce Lending Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ecommerce-sellers|E-Commerce Sellers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Clearco, Wayflyer, SellersFunding, and the marketplace lending arms underwrite sellers from their marketplace sales data rather than from financial statements, funding inventory and advertising against future revenue.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 credit analysts, modellers, and portfolio staff
**Output:** Underwriting models, portfolio performance analytics, advance pricing
**Proprietary data:** Marketplace sales and advertising data joined to repayment outcomes across thousands of sellers — the only dataset linking e-commerce operating behaviour to credit performance
**Clock:** Funding decisions in days; portfolio reporting cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Credit Officer / VP Data Science
