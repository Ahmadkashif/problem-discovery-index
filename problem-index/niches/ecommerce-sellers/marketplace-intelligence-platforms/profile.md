# Marketplace Intelligence Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ecommerce-sellers|E-Commerce Sellers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Jungle Scout, Helium 10, Keepa, and SmartScout scrape marketplace listings continuously and estimate what nobody publishes — unit sales, revenue, inventory levels, and competitive movement — then sell it to the sellers who make sourcing and pricing decisions on it. The estimate is the entire product; the raw scrape is worthless without it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-400 data scientists, marketplace analysts, and engineers
**Output:** Sales and revenue estimates, keyword and rank analytics, product opportunity scoring — the subscription
**Proprietary data:** Years of continuous marketplace observation, plus the calibration set from sellers who connect their own accounts and reveal true sales for the products they own
**Clock:** Continuous; no external deadline governs publication
**Buyer:** VP of Data Science / Chief Product Officer
