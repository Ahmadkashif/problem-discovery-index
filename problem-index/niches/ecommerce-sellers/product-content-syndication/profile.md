# Product Content & Syndication Operations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ecommerce-sellers|E-Commerce Sellers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Syndigo, Salsify, and 1WorldSync maintain the product content that retailers require before they will list an item — attributes, imagery, compliance declarations, and taxonomy assignment — mapped to each retailer's own specification, which differs for every one of them. Content teams do the mapping and the data quality work, and a rejected submission means a product that cannot be sold.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 content analysts, taxonomists, and data quality staff
**Output:** Enriched and mapped product content, retailer specification compliance, content quality scoring
**Proprietary data:** Retailer requirement specifications maintained across hundreds of retailers, plus the rejection history that shows what each one actually enforces
**Clock:** Retailer onboarding windows and seasonal listing deadlines
**Buyer:** VP of Content Operations / Chief Data Officer
