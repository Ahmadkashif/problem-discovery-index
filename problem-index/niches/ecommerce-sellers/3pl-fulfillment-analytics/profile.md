# Fulfilment Network Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ecommerce-sellers|E-Commerce Sellers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The analytics groups at third-party logistics providers serving e-commerce model inventory placement, shipping zone economics, and delivery promise reliability across their client base, and publish the benchmarks sellers use to evaluate fulfilment options.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 network engineers, analysts, and data scientists
**Output:** Placement and network models, cost and delivery benchmarking
**Proprietary data:** Shipment-level cost and transit performance across thousands of sellers and carriers
**Clock:** Peak season planning and carrier rate cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Network Strategy / Chief Analytics Officer
