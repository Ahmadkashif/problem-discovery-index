# Lease-Purchase Programme Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/owner-operator-trucking|Owner-Operator Trucking]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator/rollup

## What They Do
The analytics groups inside the large carriers and leasing companies that run owner-operator lease-purchase programmes, forecasting driver retention, lease completion rates, and residual exposure across thousands of contracts.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-80 analysts and portfolio managers
**Output:** Programme performance reporting, retention and default forecasting, residual exposure models
**Proprietary data:** Contract-level histories of lease-purchase drivers, including settlement detail and completion outcomes
**Clock:** Weekly settlement cycles and quarterly portfolio review
**Buyer:** VP of Owner-Operator Programmes
