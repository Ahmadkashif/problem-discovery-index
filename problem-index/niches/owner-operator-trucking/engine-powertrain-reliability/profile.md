# Engine & Powertrain Reliability Engineering

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/owner-operator-trucking|Owner-Operator Trucking]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The reliability and warranty analytics groups inside the engine and truck manufacturers, who analyse field failure data to drive design changes, warranty reserves, and service campaigns. Pass 1 notes a failed injector or turbo is existential for a one-truck business; this is the group that knows why they fail.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 reliability engineers, warranty analysts, and data scientists
**Output:** Failure mode analysis, warranty reserve models, service campaign decisions, design feedback
**Proprietary data:** Field failure and warranty claim histories joined to build records and engine telemetry across the entire population of units sold
**Clock:** Warranty reporting cycles and product programme milestones
**Buyer:** Director of Reliability Engineering
