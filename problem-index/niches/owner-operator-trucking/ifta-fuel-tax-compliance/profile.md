# IFTA & Fuel Tax Compliance Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/owner-operator-trucking|Owner-Operator Trucking]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Prepare the quarterly International Fuel Tax Agreement returns that apportion fuel tax across every jurisdiction a truck drove through, plus mileage tax filings for the states that run their own. Pass 1 names this as one of the compliance burdens falling on a person who is also driving 2,500 miles a week.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-200 preparers and compliance staff at the larger service bureaus
**Output:** Quarterly IFTA returns, mileage tax filings, audit support
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated filing and audit history, though the tax rates themselves are published
**Clock:** Statutory quarterly filing deadlines
**Buyer:** Director of Compliance Services
