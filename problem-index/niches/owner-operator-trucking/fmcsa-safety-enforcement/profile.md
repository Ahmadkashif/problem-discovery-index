# FMCSA Safety Measurement & Enforcement Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/owner-operator-trucking|Owner-Operator Trucking]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The federal analytics function that runs the Safety Measurement System, prioritising carriers for intervention from inspection and crash data, and setting the safety ratings that determine whether a carrier can operate and what it pays for insurance.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-800 analysts, statisticians, and contractor staff
**Output:** SMS percentile scores, intervention prioritisation, safety ratings, rulemaking analysis
**Proprietary data:** Every roadside inspection and reportable crash in the United States
**Clock:** Monthly SMS runs and continuous inspection feeds
**Buyer:** Federal programme office
