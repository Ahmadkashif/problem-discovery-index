# DOT Safety Compliance Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/owner-operator-trucking|Owner-Operator Trucking]]

**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Maintain operating authority, driver qualification files, drug and alcohol programme records, and hours-of-service compliance for small carriers, and defend them through DOT compliance reviews and audits.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-200 compliance specialists and auditors
**Output:** Compliance programmes, audit defence, corrective action plans, safety rating appeals
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated audit outcomes and what investigators in each region actually cite
**Clock:** Compliance review triggers, biennial updates, and CSA score thresholds
**Buyer:** Director of Compliance
