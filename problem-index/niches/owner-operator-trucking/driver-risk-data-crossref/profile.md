# Commercial Driver Risk Data Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/owner-operator-trucking|Owner-Operator Trucking]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Assemble commercial driving records, FMCSA inspection and crash history, CDL status, and employment verification into the risk products insurers price trucking policies from and carriers hire against. For an owner-operator this is the layer that decides what insurance costs — the second largest fixed expense after the truck.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 data operations staff, analysts, and actuarial modellers
**Output:** Driver risk scores, MVR monitoring, CSA and inspection analytics, hiring and underwriting reports
**Proprietary data:** Longitudinal violation and crash histories joined to insurance loss outcomes across the national commercial driver population
**Clock:** Continuous monitoring with policy renewal and hiring decision cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Insurance Solutions
