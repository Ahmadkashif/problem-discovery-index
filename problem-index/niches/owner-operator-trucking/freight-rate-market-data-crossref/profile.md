# Freight Rate & Lane Market Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/owner-operator-trucking|Owner-Operator Trucking]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
DAT and Truckstop operate the load boards every owner-operator works from, and sell the rate analytics derived from the transactions crossing them — lane-level spot rates, load-to-truck ratios, and market forecasts. Pass 1 identifies load selection under incomplete information as the core unsolved decision of the industry; this is the layer that sells the information.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 economists, data scientists, and market analysts
**Output:** Lane rate benchmarks, load-to-truck ratios, rate forecasts, market condition indices
**Proprietary data:** Transactional rate data from the boards where a large share of US spot freight is matched
**Clock:** Daily rate publication and weekly market reporting
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Analytics
