# Fuel Card Network Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/owner-operator-trucking|Owner-Operator Trucking]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Operate the fuel card networks owner-operators buy diesel on, set the discount programmes, and run the fraud and spend analytics over the transactions. Fuel is roughly a third of an owner-operator's cost base and every gallon of it crosses one of these networks.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 analysts, data scientists, and fraud operations staff
**Output:** Fuel price and discount optimisation, purchase fraud detection, spend reporting, route fuel planning
**Proprietary data:** Transaction-level diesel purchasing across the national truck stop network
**Clock:** Daily fuel price movement and continuous transaction flow
**Buyer:** VP of Analytics / Chief Risk Officer
