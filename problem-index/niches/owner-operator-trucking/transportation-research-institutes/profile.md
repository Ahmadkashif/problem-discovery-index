# Transportation Research Institutes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/owner-operator-trucking|Owner-Operator Trucking]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
ATRI and its peers conduct trucking industry research — operational cost benchmarks, congestion cost analysis, driver shortage studies, bottleneck rankings — funded by association membership and government grants rather than sold.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-30 researchers and analysts
**Output:** Annual operational cost of trucking reports, congestion analyses, policy research
**Proprietary data:** A large truck GPS dataset used for bottleneck and congestion analysis, genuinely unavailable elsewhere
**Clock:** Annual research calendar and legislative cycles
**Buyer:** Research Director
