# Freight Factoring Companies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/owner-operator-trucking|Owner-Operator Trucking]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Buy freight invoices from owner-operators and small fleets at a discount, advancing cash within a day of delivery so a one-truck business can make payroll and buy fuel before the broker pays in thirty to sixty days. The credit risk sits not on the carrier but on the broker or shipper who owes the money, so every purchase requires a credit judgment on the debtor.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 credit analysts, underwriters, collections staff, and risk data teams across the major factors
**Output:** Credit decisions and advance rates on individual invoices, debtor credit limits, broker days-to-pay ratings, fraud screening
**Proprietary data:** Actual payment behaviour of tens of thousands of freight brokers and shippers, observed invoice by invoice — the only place broker payment performance is systematically recorded
**Clock:** Daily invoice submission and same-day funding decisions
**Buyer:** Chief Credit Officer / VP of Risk
