# Fleet Telematics & Safety Analytics Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/owner-operator-trucking|Owner-Operator Trucking]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The data science organisations inside Motive, Samsara and their peers, who turn the ELD, dashcam and engine data the mandate forced every truck to generate into safety scores, coaching alerts, and fuel and maintenance analytics.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 data scientists, computer vision engineers, and safety analysts
**Output:** Driver safety scores, event detection and coaching workflows, fuel and idling analytics, predictive maintenance alerts
**Proprietary data:** Continuous telemetry and video from a large share of the US truck fleet
**Clock:** Continuous — the trucks generate data every second they move
**Buyer:** VP of Data Science / Chief Product Officer
