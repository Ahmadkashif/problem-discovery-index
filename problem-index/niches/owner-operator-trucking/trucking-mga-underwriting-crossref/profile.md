# Trucking Insurance MGAs & Program Underwriters

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/owner-operator-trucking|Owner-Operator Trucking]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Underwrite the commercial auto liability, physical damage, and cargo programmes that owner-operators and small fleets buy, on delegated authority from carriers who will not write the class directly. Trucking is one of the worst-performing commercial lines in the US, which makes the underwriting judgment the whole business.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-800 underwriters, actuaries, and programme analysts across the trucking MGAs
**Output:** Risk selection and pricing decisions, programme performance analysis, loss control recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Submission and loss histories on a class the standard market avoids, with telematics and inspection data appended
**Clock:** Annual policy renewal cycles and quarterly programme reviews
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
