# Commercial Driver Risk Data Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/charter-bus-operators|Charter Bus Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
SambaSafety, Tenstreet, and the driver monitoring bureaus hold agreements with state motor vehicle agencies to continuously monitor driving records, and sell driver risk scoring and violation alerting to insurers, fleets, and passenger carriers. A charter operator's licence to run depends on knowing within days that a driver has been cited; the vendors are how that is known. The pocket spans commercial transport generally and is logged here because passenger carriage is where driver record risk is most consequential.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 data operations staff, analysts, and data scientists
**Output:** Driver risk scores, continuous violation alerting, fleet risk analytics — the subscription product
**Proprietary data:** Continuous motor vehicle record monitoring across millions of drivers under state agreements, joined to employer and crash outcomes over years
**Clock:** Insurance renewal cycles, statutory drug and alcohol clearinghouse queries, and annual driver record review obligations
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Risk Products
