# Charter Brokerage Platform Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/charter-bus-operators|Charter Bus Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
GOGO Charters, US Coachways, and the online charter marketplaces broker trips to operator networks. Small teams price quotes, match capacity, and analyze booking conversion.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 2-10
**Output:** Quote pricing models, conversion and capacity analytics
**Proprietary data:** Quote and booking data across a large operator network — genuinely unique, since nobody else sees charter demand at aggregate
**Clock:** Customer quote turnaround
**Buyer:** Head of Operations
