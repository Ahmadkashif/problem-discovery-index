# Carrier Safety Scoring & Monitoring Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/charter-bus-operators|Charter Bus Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
RMIS, Carrier Assure, Highway, and similar firms monitor federal carrier safety data, insurance filings, and authority status, and sell carrier vetting and risk scores to brokers, shippers, and group travel organizers. A school district or tour operator hiring a motorcoach uses these to decide whether the carrier is acceptable.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 analysts, data staff, and underwriters
**Output:** Carrier risk scores, vetting reports, continuous monitoring alerts
**Proprietary data:** Longitudinal carrier safety and insurance status history joined to observed outcomes, layered on a public federal data foundation
**Clock:** Contract award and trip booking decisions, plus insurance renewal cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Product / Chief Risk Officer
