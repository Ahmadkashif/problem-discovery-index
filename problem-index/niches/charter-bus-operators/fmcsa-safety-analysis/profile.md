# Federal Motor Carrier Safety Analysis

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/charter-bus-operators|Charter Bus Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
FMCSA's analysis division operates the Safety Measurement System that scores every carrier in the country, runs crash causation research, and produces the analysis behind rulemaking. Its outputs determine which carriers get investigated and which are put out of service.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-200 analysts, statisticians, and researchers, plus contractors
**Output:** Carrier safety scores, crash causation studies, regulatory impact analyses
**Proprietary data:** The complete national inspection, violation, and crash record for commercial motor vehicles
**Clock:** Rulemaking schedules and congressionally mandated reporting
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — a federal administrator
