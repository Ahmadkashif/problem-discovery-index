# ELD & Fleet Safety Telematics Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/charter-bus-operators|Charter Bus Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The data science groups at Samsara, Motive, Lytx, and Netradyne process hours-of-service logs, video event capture, and driving behaviour telemetry from commercial vehicles, and turn it into coaching programmes, risk scoring, and compliance reporting. Motorcoach operators use these to satisfy hours-of-service rules and to manage driver risk.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 data scientists, safety researchers, and analysts
**Output:** Driver risk scores, coaching recommendations, compliance reporting
**Proprietary data:** Video-verified driving behaviour and telemetry across millions of commercial vehicles, joined to collision outcomes
**Clock:** Hours-of-service compliance is continuous; analytical publication is not externally driven
**Buyer:** VP of Data Science / Head of Safety Analytics
