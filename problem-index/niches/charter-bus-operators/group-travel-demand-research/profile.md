# Group Travel & Motorcoach Demand Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/charter-bus-operators|Charter Bus Operators]]

**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Small research operations that survey tour operators, group travel planners, and destination marketing organizations on booking patterns, itinerary preferences, and motorcoach demand, and publish the census and outlook reports the industry plans against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-20 researchers and analysts
**Output:** Demand outlooks, motorcoach census, group travel trend reports
**Proprietary data:** Long-running operator and planner survey series
**Clock:** Annual publication and trade show calendars
**Buyer:** Director of Research
