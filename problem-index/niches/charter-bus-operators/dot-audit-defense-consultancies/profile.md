# DOT Audit Defence & Compliance Review Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/charter-bus-operators|Charter Bus Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Specialist firms that prepare operators for FMCSA compliance reviews and new entrant audits, reconstruct records under investigation, and contest safety ratings and out-of-service orders. The written response to an audit finding is the deliverable, and an unfavourable rating can end a carrier.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 compliance specialists and former investigators
**Output:** Audit responses, corrective action plans, safety rating upgrade petitions
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated record of which arguments and corrective actions succeeded with which field offices
**Clock:** Audit response windows measured in days, and statutory petition deadlines
**Buyer:** Managing partner / Director of Compliance Services
