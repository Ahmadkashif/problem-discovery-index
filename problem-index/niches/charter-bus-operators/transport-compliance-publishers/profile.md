# Transportation Regulatory Compliance Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/charter-bus-operators|Charter Bus Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
J.J. Keller and comparable publishers turn federal and state transportation regulation into something a small operator can actually comply with — interpreted regulatory guidance, forms, training, audit preparation, and managed compliance services. Regulatory editors track every FMCSA, DOT, and state rulemaking, decide what it means operationally, and update thousands of dependent documents. Operators subscribe because a missed filing is a five-figure fine or an out-of-service order.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-200 regulatory editors, subject matter experts, and compliance analysts
**Output:** Interpreted regulatory content, compliance manuals, training, audit readiness assessments — the subscription and service revenue
**Proprietary data:** Decades of regulatory interpretation joined to the customer inquiry record, which maps exactly where the regulations are ambiguous in practice, plus audit and enforcement outcomes across a large customer base
**Clock:** Rulemaking effective dates, audit and filing deadlines, enforcement actions
**Buyer:** VP of Editorial / Director of Regulatory Content
