# Motorcoach Accident Investigation Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/charter-bus-operators|Charter Bus Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The NTSB investigates major motorcoach accidents, produces detailed causal analyses, and issues safety recommendations that drive regulation and manufacturer design change. Each investigation is a deep, multi-disciplinary research effort published in full.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-60 investigators and analysts across highway safety
**Output:** Accident investigation reports and safety recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Detailed investigative records including evidence unavailable from any other source
**Clock:** Investigation timelines and board meeting schedules
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer
