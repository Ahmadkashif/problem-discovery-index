# Student Transportation Contract Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/charter-bus-operators|Charter Bus Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The bid, pricing, and routing analytics groups at First Student, Student Transportation, and the large school bus contractors. They model route cost, driver supply, and fuel exposure to price multi-year district contracts, and analyze performance across thousands of routes to defend renewals.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 pricing analysts, routing engineers, and operations researchers
**Output:** Bid models, route cost analyses, contract performance reporting
**Proprietary data:** Route-level cost and performance data across thousands of districts and years of bid outcomes at known prices
**Clock:** District procurement calendars, which are fixed and unforgiving
**Buyer:** VP of Pricing / Director of Business Analytics
