# Motorcoach Insurance Underwriting Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/charter-bus-operators|Charter Bus Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Specialist passenger transportation underwriters — Lancer, National Interstate, and the motorcoach programme managers — research loss experience by operation type, route profile, driver history, and fleet age to price a line where a single catastrophic loss can exceed a small operator's entire annual revenue.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 underwriters, actuaries, and loss control analysts
**Output:** Rating models, underwriting guidelines, loss control recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Passenger carrier loss experience — deep for the segment, but the segment is small
**Clock:** Rate filings and annual renewal cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
