# Tracking-System AD Lists Extended to Card Text

**Niche:** [[niches/aircraft-mro-shops/ad-work-card-compliance/profile|AD & Work-Card Compliance Checking]]
**Industry:** [[industries/aircraft-mro-shops|Aircraft MRO & FBO Shops]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Maintenance tracking services already research ADs for every aircraft they track; their output is a due list, not a check of what the mechanic is about to sign.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #compliance #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to prove every work card cites the current AD revision and an approved method of compliance for that serial number, and whoever has the lowest missed-applicability rate takes the account.

## The Problem
Business aviation operators pay tracking services to keep AD status current, and the shop doing the work receives a due list. The shop's work cards are written separately, sometimes from templates years old. When the due list and the cards disagree, nobody notices until an inspection or a pre-buy.

## What Already Exists
CAMP Systems (whose careers board lists aviation maintenance analyst roles in Savannah, Montréal and Wichita), Traxxall (JSSI) and Veryon's Flightdocs, each with AD research and due-list reporting; airline maintenance systems with AD modules.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: (1) ingestion of the shop's work cards, which the tracking service never sees today; (2) card-to-AD matching on paragraph, revision and method, not just AD number; (3) AMOC records linked to the aircraft; (4) reconciliation in both directions, from due list to cards and from cards to due list; and (5) the result posted back into the tracking record as evidence. The tracking vendors have the AD research and the customer base; they lack the shop-side document step.

## Target Customer
Tracking-service vendors seeking a shop-facing module; large business aviation MROs that do work for operators on several tracking services.

## Impact If Solved
Closing the due-list-to-card gap could eliminate most AD discrepancies found at pre-buys on tracked aircraft (estimate). It also gives the tracking vendor a product for the ~4,000–5,000 repair stations (estimate) that do the work on its customers' aircraft.
