# NTSB Aviation Maintenance Investigations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/aircraft-mro-shops|Aircraft MRO & FBO Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The NTSB's Office of Aviation Safety investigates civil aviation accidents and serious incidents, including those traced to maintenance errors, engine failures and missed inspections, and issues safety recommendations to the FAA, manufacturers and operators. Its docket and accident database are public.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 investigators and specialists whose cases involve maintenance and powerplant factors, within a larger aviation office (estimate)
**Output:** Accident reports, dockets, safety recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Investigative records, wreckage examinations and interviews unavailable from any other source
**Clock:** Investigation timelines and board meeting schedules
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer
