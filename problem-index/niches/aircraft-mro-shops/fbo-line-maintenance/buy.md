# FBO Software Extended to Line Maintenance Work Orders

**Niche:** [[niches/aircraft-mro-shops/fbo-line-maintenance/profile|FBO Line Maintenance & Transient AOG Support]]
**Industry:** [[industries/aircraft-mro-shops|Aircraft MRO & FBO Shops]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** FBO software already knows the aircraft, the crew, the operator and the billing account the moment it lands; the maintenance work order is still started on paper.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to return a transient aircraft to service the same night it arrives with a fault, and whoever diagnoses, sources the part and signs it off before the crew's departure time takes the account.

## The Problem
When a transient aircraft needs maintenance, the FBO's customer-service desk has the arrival, the operator and the account in its system, but the maintenance shop opens a separate work order, often on paper, re-entering the same details. The repair, parts and sign-off are recorded in the shop's system, if it has one, and billed separately.

## What Already Exists
FBO management systems for fuel, handling, hangar and point of sale; maintenance ERPs (Corridor, Avsight, EBIS) at larger repair stations; generic work-order apps.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: (1) a maintenance work order opened from the arrival record with tail number, operator and authorised contacts; (2) Part 145 and 43.9 fields (approved data reference, return-to-service statement, certificate numbers) on a mobile form; (3) operator authorisation and purchase-order capture inside the order; (4) parts tag and serial capture by photo; and (5) a single invoice for fuel, handling and maintenance with the logbook entry attached for the crew.

## Target Customer
FBO chains and independent FBOs that run a line maintenance shop beside their fuel business.

## Impact If Solved
Removing double entry and paper hand-offs could save ~20–30 minutes per transient work order (estimate) and cut unbilled labour and parts, which FBO shops often lose on late-night jobs, by a few percent of maintenance revenue.
