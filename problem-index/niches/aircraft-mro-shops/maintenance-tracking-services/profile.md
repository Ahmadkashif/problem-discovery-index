# Maintenance Tracking & Engine Trend-Monitoring Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/aircraft-mro-shops|Aircraft MRO & FBO Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Subscription services that keep business aviation operators' maintenance status (inspections, AD compliance, component times, life-limited parts) and analyse engine trend data for exceedances and deterioration. CAMP Systems (Hearst), Traxxall (JSSI) and Veryon's Flightdocs are the main providers. Operators and management companies pay per aircraft; MROs read the due lists.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 engine health monitoring and reliability analysts per large provider (estimate). The far larger aviation maintenance analyst teams that update records (CAMP lists these roles in Savannah, Montréal and Wichita; JSSI lists records analysts in Pampanga, Philippines) are production headcount and not counted.
**Output:** Due lists, AD status reports, engine trend alerts, fleet reliability summaries
**Proprietary data:** Maintenance status and engine trend histories for tens of thousands of business aircraft (estimate)
**Clock:** FAA-mandated inspection and AD due dates on the operators' aircraft, which are the clients' clocks rather than deadlines on the vendor
**Buyer:** VP of product or head of engine health monitoring at the tracking provider (CAMP is hiring a technical product manager for engine health monitoring in Montréal)

**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. A huge, ungraded record of business aviation maintenance, held for owners. Below threshold even without the switch: the subscription is for record-keeping, and the analysis rides along.
