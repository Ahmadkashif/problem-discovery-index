# Repair Station, FBO & Technician Association Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/aircraft-mro-shops|Aircraft MRO & FBO Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Trade associations for the maintenance and FBO trades: the Aeronautical Repair Station Association (ARSA), the Aviation Technician Education Council (ATEC), the National Air Transportation Association (NATA) for FBOs, and the Aircraft Electronics Association (AEA). They lobby, publish guidance, and commission research rather than producing it in-house. ARSA distributes Oliver Wyman's fleet and MRO forecast to members, and ATEC publishes the annual Pipeline Report with Oliver Wyman.

## Insight Function
**Size:** under 10 research staff per association; the analysis is commissioned from consultancies (estimate)
**Output:** Member reports, workforce studies, regulatory comments
**Proprietary data:** Member surveys and school enrolment data
**Clock:** Annual report cycles and rulemaking comment periods
**Buyer:** Executive director
