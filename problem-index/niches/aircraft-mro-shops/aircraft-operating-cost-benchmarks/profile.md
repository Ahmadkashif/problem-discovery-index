# Aircraft Operating & Maintenance Cost Benchmarks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/aircraft-mro-shops|Aircraft MRO & FBO Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Publishers of per-hour operating and maintenance cost estimates by business aircraft model, used by buyers, flight departments, brokers and MROs to budget and compare aircraft. Conklin & de Decker, now part of JETNET, is the standard reference (recalled). Subscribers pay for the cost database and its comparison tools.

## Insight Function
**Size:** typically under 10 analysts on maintenance-cost data (estimate); the larger fleet and transaction research teams at the parent serve brokers and lessors
**Output:** Model-by-model cost estimates and comparison reports
**Proprietary data:** Cost models built from published maintenance programmes, labour rates and parts prices
**Clock:** Self-set annual updates
**Buyer:** Product head at the publisher
