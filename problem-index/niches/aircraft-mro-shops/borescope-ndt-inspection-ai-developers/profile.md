# Borescope & NDT Inspection AI Developers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/aircraft-mro-shops|Aircraft MRO & FBO Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling in

## What They Do
Makers of borescopes and NDT equipment, and the AI start-ups working with them, that build assisted defect recognition for engine and airframe inspection. Waygate Technologies (Baker Hughes) and Evident make the videoscopes most MROs use (recalled). Aviation Week named Aiir Innovations, GE Aerospace with Waygate, and GE's OC Robotics as developers of AI borescope and blade-inspection tools. MROs and operators buy the equipment and software; OEMs partner on engine-specific models.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 applications engineers and imaging scientists per larger developer (estimate); data labellers are production and not counted
**Output:** Inspection equipment, assisted-defect-recognition software, measurement tools, training content
**Proprietary data:** Labelled inspection images from customer and OEM partnerships, much of it collected under those agreements
**Clock:** Product release cycles; OEM approval of inspection methods
**Buyer:** Head of product or applications engineering at the equipment maker
