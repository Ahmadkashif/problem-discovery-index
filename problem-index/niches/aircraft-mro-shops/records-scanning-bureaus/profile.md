# Maintenance Records Scanning & Back-Office Bureaus

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/aircraft-mro-shops|Aircraft MRO & FBO Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling in

## What They Do
Firms and offshore teams that scan, index and key aircraft and engine records for owners, lessors and tracking services: Bluetail's crowdsourced on-site scanning network, records back offices in India and the Philippines (JSSI lists aircraft records and enrollment analysts in Pampanga), and the scanning arms of records platforms. Owners and vendors pay per book, per page or per analyst.

## Insight Function
**Size:** tens to hundreds of scanners, indexers and records analysts per bureau, all production headcount (estimate)
**Output:** Scanned and indexed records, keyed data entries
**Proprietary data:** None of their own; the records belong to the aircraft owners
**Clock:** Client transaction and transition dates
**Buyer:** Operations director
