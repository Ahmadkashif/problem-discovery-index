# Aviation Fault-Isolation & Troubleshooting Knowledge Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/aircraft-mro-shops|Aircraft MRO & FBO Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Platforms that turn manufacturers' fault-isolation logic and field feedback into guided troubleshooting for mechanics. Veryon's Guided Troubleshooting is the clearest example. Veryon says it is used by the technical help desks and field support of Dassault, Gulfstream and Rolls-Royce, and it signed a long-term agreement with Rolls-Royce Deutschland. Gulfstream's Diagnostics Direct app is built on it and "continuously updated with field experience feedback". OEMs and operators pay for the platform; mechanics use it at the aircraft.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-60 diagnostic content engineers, field-feedback analysts and help-desk specialists across the platform and its OEM programmes (estimate)
**Output:** Guided troubleshooting trees, fault-to-fix probabilities, chronic-defect analysis
**Proprietary data:** Field feedback on which fixes cleared which fault messages, by type, collected through OEM help desks and service centres
**Clock:** The aircraft-on-ground event; subscribers call when stuck, which is the customer's clock rather than a deadline on the vendor
**Buyer:** Head of diagnostics or product at the platform; OEM customer-support directors
