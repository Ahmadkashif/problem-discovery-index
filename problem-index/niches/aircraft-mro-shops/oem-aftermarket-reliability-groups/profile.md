# Engine & Airframe OEM Aftermarket Reliability Groups

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/aircraft-mro-shops|Aircraft MRO & FBO Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling in

## What They Do
The fleet reliability, customer-support engineering and service-bulletin teams at engine and airframe manufacturers (GE Aerospace, CFM, Pratt & Whitney, Rolls-Royce, Honeywell, Boeing, Airbus, Gulfstream, Textron and others, recalled). They analyse in-service events, shop findings and engine data across their fleets, write service bulletins and support the FAA on ADs, and price flight-hour service agreements. MROs depend on their manuals, repair licences and parts.

## Insight Function
**Size:** hundreds of reliability and customer-support engineers per major OEM (estimate)
**Output:** Service bulletins, fleet reliability reports, inspection programmes, flight-hour agreement pricing
**Proprietary data:** Fleet-wide in-service, shop and sensor data for their own products
**Clock:** AD and service-bulletin response timelines after in-service events; agreement renewals
**Buyer:** VP of customer support or aftermarket services at the OEM

**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. The deepest reliability data in the industry, bundled into products and service agreements rather than invoiced as analysis, and held by a handful of manufacturers that build their own tools (GE Aerospace's AI blade inspection with Waygate Technologies is one).
