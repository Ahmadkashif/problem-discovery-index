# Learning Platforms Adapted to OJT Sign-Off on Type

**Niche:** [[niches/aircraft-mro-shops/new-amt-workforce/profile|Newly Certificated A&P Mechanics]]
**Industry:** [[industries/aircraft-mro-shops|Aircraft MRO & FBO Shops]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Corporate learning platforms track courses completed; a repair station needs to track which tasks on which type each mechanic has performed under supervision and is authorised to sign.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #compliance #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a newly certificated A&P signing off work alone on a type in months rather than years, and whoever shortens time to independent sign-off without raising QA findings takes the account.

## The Problem
Part 145 stations must run an FAA-approved training programme (145.163) that ensures each person can perform the assigned tasks. In practice, competence on a task is established by on-the-job training. A senior watches the new mechanic perform it and signs a task list. Authorisations to sign off work are then issued by type and task. Learning management systems record classroom and online courses but not this.

## What Already Exists
Corporate LMS products (Cornerstone, Docebo, SAP SuccessFactors Learning) and aviation training-records modules in MRO systems.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: (1) task lists by aircraft type, ATA chapter and task from the maintenance programme; (2) OJT evidence captured from real work orders (performed under supervision, by whom, with what result); (3) authorisation rules that issue sign-off privileges when evidence thresholds are met; (4) QA findings linked back to the mechanic and task, so readiness is measured, not assumed; and (5) the 145.163 records format the FAA expects.

## Target Customer
Training managers at repair stations and airline maintenance bases.

## Impact If Solved
Generating OJT records from work orders could save ~2–4 hours a week per training coordinator (estimate) and give the station evidence for each authorisation it issues.
