# Hourly Engine Maintenance Programme Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/aircraft-mro-shops|Aircraft MRO & FBO Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Business aviation owners pay a fixed rate per flight hour into an engine or airframe maintenance programme, and the programme pays the MRO when a shop visit or unscheduled event comes due. JSSI (Jet Support Services, which says it supports over 30% of the global business jet fleet and owns Traxxall) is the largest independent provider. Engine OEMs run their own, among them Rolls-Royce CorporateCare, Honeywell MSP, Pratt & Whitney Canada ESP and GE OnPoint (recalled). The provider prices each enrolment from its forecast of the engine's maintenance cost, then audits and pays MRO invoices.

## Insight Function
**Size:** rate and cost forecasters only, plausibly under 10 per provider (estimate; no pricing or forecasting roles appear on JSSI's Lever board). The technical advisors who approve each shop visit's workscope and invoice (JSSI lists them in France and Canada, plus engine and APU maintenance analysts in Kentucky) and the invoice analysts on its underwriting team do per-case work, production under the batch P1 ruling on claim analysts, and are not counted.
**Output:** Hourly rates and enrolment pricing, shop-visit cost forecasts, invoice audits and workscope approvals
**Proprietary data:** Years of shop-visit invoices, workscopes and event costs across thousands of enrolled engines and many MROs, which is the payer's own claims history
**Clock:** Shop-visit events and MRO quote approvals; rate reviews on the provider's own cycle
**Buyer:** Chief technical officer or head of underwriting at the programme provider

**Verdict:** Logged — fails gate (the per-case advisors and invoice reviewers are production, and the rate and cost forecasting team that would be the insight function is plausibly under 10). It would stay below threshold even if it passed: the invoice is a risk-transfer rate, no third-party deadline governs the analysts, and the market is one large independent plus the OEM programmes.
