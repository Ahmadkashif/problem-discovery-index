# Aviation Technical Publication & AD Libraries

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/aircraft-mro-shops|Aircraft MRO & FBO Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Publishers that license manufacturers' maintenance manuals, illustrated parts catalogues and service bulletins, compile them with FAA airworthiness directives, and sell them as subscription libraries to repair stations, FBO shops and operators. Aircraft Technical Publishers (ATP) is the best-known in business and general aviation; it also sells ChronicX reliability analytics with ATA recoding. OEM portals are the alternative. Shops pay because 145.109(d) requires current technical data at the point of work.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-30 AD applicability researchers and reliability analysts per publisher (estimate); the document-conversion and indexing staff are production and not counted
**Output:** Subscription libraries of manuals, ADs and service bulletins, AD applicability indexes, reliability reports
**Proprietary data:** The compiled and indexed library and AD applicability research; the manuals themselves are licensed from OEMs and the ADs are public
**Clock:** FAA AD effective dates and OEM revision cycles, which set when the library must be current
**Buyer:** VP of content or product at the publisher
