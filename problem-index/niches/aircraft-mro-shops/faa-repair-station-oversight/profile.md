# FAA Flight Standards Repair-Station Oversight & SDR Analysis

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/aircraft-mro-shops|Aircraft MRO & FBO Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The FAA's Flight Standards Service certificates and surveils repair stations, air carriers' maintenance programmes and mechanics, and runs the Service Difficulty Reporting System. GAO counted just under 4,800 repair stations under FAA oversight in 2016. The SDR database took 67,652 reports in 2025, published as a yearly download. Production approval holders sit with the Aircraft Certification Service, logged under aerospace parts.

## Insight Function
**Size:** aviation safety inspectors are production headcount; staff dedicated to analysing repair-station risk and SDR trends are plausibly under 10 (estimate)
**Output:** Surveillance findings, enforcement actions, SDR data, safety alerts and input to ADs
**Proprietary data:** Surveillance findings and enforcement history across repair stations and operators; SDRs are public
**Clock:** The FAA's own surveillance plans, certificate actions and investigation timelines
**Buyer:** FAA leadership; a single buyer
