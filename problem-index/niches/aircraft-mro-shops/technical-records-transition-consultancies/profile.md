# Aircraft Technical Records & Transition Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/aircraft-mro-shops|Aircraft MRO & FBO Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Specialist firms that audit aircraft and engine records for lease transitions, sales and shop visits: back-to-birth LLP traces, AD compliance matrices, major repair and alteration files, and delivery-condition reports. Their technical representatives also oversee redelivery checks for lessors and airlines. Names in the field include SGI Aviation and the technical services arms of asset managers and MROs (recalled). Lessors, airlines and investors pay per project or per day.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-80 records auditors and technical representatives per larger firm (estimate), but both work aircraft by aircraft (auditing one records pack, attending one redelivery check), which is records-clerk and inspector work under the production rule; no separate non-production research or methods function of 10+ could be identified
**Output:** Records audit reports, gap lists, transition and redelivery reports
**Proprietary data:** Experience of which records gaps buyers and lessors reject; the records themselves belong to the aircraft owners
**Clock:** Lease return and delivery dates set in the client's contracts
**Buyer:** Managing director or head of technical services
