# Change Orders Argued After the Fact

**Niche:** [[niches/aircraft-mro-shops/airframe-heavy-checks/profile|Airframe Heavy Checks & Base Maintenance]]
**Industry:** [[industries/aircraft-mro-shops|Aircraft MRO & FBO Shops]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Non-routine work is done first and billed later, so every heavy check ends in a dispute over hours the customer's representative never saw justified.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #workflow-orchestration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to absorb a check's non-routine findings without moving the redelivery date, and whoever forecasts the non-routine load before the panels come off takes the account.

## The Problem
The customer's on-site representative approves non-routine findings as they come up, often verbally or in a daily meeting. The hours charged appear on the invoice weeks later, card by card, and the customer's finance team challenges those that look high. MROs write off a share of disputed hours to keep the relationship.

## Why It's Still Broken
Non-routine cards are written for the mechanic and the inspector, not for the customer's commercial review. Estimates per card are not recorded at approval time, so the comparison between estimated and actual hours that would settle a dispute doesn't exist.

## What a Fix Looks Like
At the moment a non-routine card is raised, draft a customer-facing line: the finding with a photo, the approved repair reference, an estimated hours range from the base's history for the same finding type, and a one-tap approval for the representative. At invoice, show each card's estimate against actual with the reason for any overrun.

## Who Feels the Pain
Customer programme managers and finance at the MRO; airline representatives approving dozens of findings a day; planners caught between the two.

## Impact If Fixed
Recovering even a third of the ~3–8% of non-routine hours typically written off in disputes (estimate) is worth ~$20,000–$60,000 per heavy check at a large base, and it shortens invoice settlement by weeks.
