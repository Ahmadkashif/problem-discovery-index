# General Aviation Owner Maintenance Management & Engine Data Analysis

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/aircraft-mro-shops|Aircraft MRO & FBO Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Services that act as the owner's maintenance manager for piston and light turbine aircraft. They agree workscopes with the shop before an annual inspection, review invoices, and analyse uploaded engine-monitor data for failing cylinders, injectors and ignition. Savvy Aviation, which grew from Mike Busch's owner-maintenance advocacy, is the best-known (recalled). Owners pay an annual fee for managed maintenance or per analysis.

## Insight Function
**Size:** account managers holding A&P and IA certificates handle each owner's maintenance events case by case, which is production; the engine-data analysts who would form the insight function are plausibly under 10 (estimate)
**Output:** Workscope approvals, maintenance plans, engine-monitor analysis reports, breakdown assistance
**Proprietary data:** A large corpus of uploaded piston-engine monitor flights (trade press cites 600,000 in one article and 1.5M+ in a later one, via search summary) and the maintenance outcomes the service saw on its managed aircraft
**Clock:** None set by a third party; annual inspections fall when the owner schedules them
**Buyer:** Founder or head of analysis at the service
