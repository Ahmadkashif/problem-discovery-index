# Manufacturing QMS Adapted to a Part 145 Manual

**Niche:** [[niches/aircraft-mro-shops/small-part-145-repair-stations/profile|Small Part 145 Repair Stations]]
**Industry:** [[industries/aircraft-mro-shops|Aircraft MRO & FBO Shops]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Quality management suites handle document control, CAPA and training for manufacturers; a repair station needs them organised around its FAA manual, its capability list and its customers' audit rules.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #compliance #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a small repair station's certificate and its customers' approvals with a one-person quality department, and whoever leaves the fewest findings per FAA and customer audit takes the account.

## The Problem
Small stations that buy a QMS find it built around ISO 9001 or AS9100 clauses, not the structure of a repair station manual and quality control manual. The station ends up keeping the FAA-facing manual in Word and the QMS as a second system.

## What Already Exists
ETQ, MasterControl, Qualio and similar suites for document control, corrective action, training and audits; aviation-specific modules inside repair-station ERPs.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: (1) a manual template organised to Part 145 subparts and 145.211's required procedures, with revision submission to the responsible Flight Standards office tracked; (2) the capability list and ratings linked to technical data currency per article; (3) the training programme mapped to 145.163 with two-year record retention; (4) customer audit rule sets (each airline's supplier requirements, EASA Part-145 supplement items) as checklists; and (5) 96-hour SDR reporting as a tracked workflow.

## Target Customer
Repair stations of 5–50 staff that hold FAA, and often EASA, approvals and supply airlines or operators.

## Impact If Solved
Collapsing two quality systems into one saves ~4–8 hours a week of duplicate document control (estimate) and gives the station a single answer for every auditor.
