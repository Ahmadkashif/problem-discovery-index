# Aviation Maintenance Litigation & Accident Experts

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/aircraft-mro-shops|Aircraft MRO & FBO Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Forensic engineering firms and independent A&P, IA and powerplant experts retained in litigation and insurance disputes after maintenance-related accidents, engine failures and disputed shop visits. They examine wreckage and parts, read the maintenance records, reconstruct what was done, and testify about whether the work met the standard of care. Aviation practices at larger forensic engineering firms and specialist aviation consultancies do this work (names recalled). Law firms and insurers retain them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 aviation engineers and maintenance experts at the larger forensic practices (estimate)
**Output:** Expert reports, depositions, trial testimony, failure analyses
**Proprietary data:** Case files of failures and maintenance disputes, which belong to the retaining counsel and their clients
**Clock:** Court-ordered expert disclosure and trial dates
**Buyer:** Practice leader at the forensic firm
