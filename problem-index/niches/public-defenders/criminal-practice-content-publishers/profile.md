# Criminal Practice Content & Research Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-defenders|Public Defenders]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Publish the criminal practice treatises, sentencing guidelines analysis, suppression and evidence manuals, and jurisdiction-specific practice content defenders work from — including the immigration consequences references that determine advice with permanent effects on a client's life.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 attorney-editors, analysts, and content specialists
**Output:** Practice treatises and manuals, annotated statutes and rules, sentencing analysis, immigration consequences references, continuing legal education content
**Proprietary data:** Decades of curated interpretation across a body of law that changes with every appellate term and differs in every jurisdiction
**Clock:** Legislative sessions, appellate decisions, and annual sentencing guideline amendments
**Buyer:** Editorial Director / VP of Content
