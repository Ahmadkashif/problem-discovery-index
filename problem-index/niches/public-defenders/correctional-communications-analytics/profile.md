# Correctional Communications & Data Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-defenders|Public Defenders]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Operate the phone, messaging and video systems incarcerated people use, and sell the recording, transcription and investigative analytics built over them to correctional agencies and prosecutors. Pass 1 notes clients pay predatory per-message prices for the same systems defenders must use to reach them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 data scientists, speech engineers, investigative analysts, and platform staff
**Output:** Call recording, transcription and search, contact network analysis, investigative alerting, facility reporting
**Proprietary data:** Recorded communications at enormous scale from a population with no alternative channel
**Clock:** Continuous
**Buyer:** VP of Analytics / Chief Technology Officer
