# Jail & Corrections Population Data Systems

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-defenders|Public Defenders]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Supply the jail management systems that hold booking, custody, classification and release data, and the analytics agencies use to manage population and length of stay.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 product and data staff across the vendors
**Output:** Booking and custody records, classification scoring, population and length-of-stay analytics, release planning
**Proprietary data:** Custody records across many facilities, owned by each agency
**Clock:** Continuous booking and release
**Buyer:** VP of Product
