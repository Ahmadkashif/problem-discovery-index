# Criminal Court Data & Litigation Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-defenders|Public Defenders]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Collect dockets, charges, motions and dispositions out of thousands of separate court systems, normalise them, and sell the analytics — how a judge rules on suppression motions, what charges typically plead to, how long a case takes in a given court. Pass 1 describes public defenders interfacing with court systems by re-keying data or overnight batch files, and legal research tools going underused because there is no time.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 court researchers, data acquisition staff, taxonomists, and analysts
**Output:** Judge and venue analytics, motion outcome rates, charge and disposition patterns, timing analysis, counsel histories
**Proprietary data:** Normalised trial court records assembled from thousands of county systems that publish inconsistently
**Clock:** Daily docket updates against live case deadlines
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Analytics
