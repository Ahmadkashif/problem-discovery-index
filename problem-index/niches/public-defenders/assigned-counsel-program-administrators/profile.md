# Assigned Counsel Programme Administration

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-defenders|Public Defenders]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Administer the panels of private attorneys who take indigent cases in jurisdictions without a defender office — appointment, voucher review, rate setting, and quality oversight. Pass 1 notes several states rely on this model at $50 to $100 an hour.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-200 programme administrators, voucher reviewers, and analysts
**Output:** Appointment decisions, voucher review and payment, panel qualification and monitoring, cost reporting
**Proprietary data:** Attorney-level case and cost records across panels, including hours claimed by case type
**Clock:** Voucher submission deadlines and budget cycles
**Buyer:** Programme Director
