# Forensic Science Services & Defence Experts

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-defenders|Public Defenders]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Re-examine the forensic evidence a prosecution rests on — DNA mixture interpretation, digital forensics, firearms and toolmark comparison, forensic pathology — and testify to what it does and does not support.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-800 forensic scientists and analysts across the private laboratories and consultancies
**Output:** Independent analyses and re-examinations, probabilistic genotyping interpretation, expert reports and testimony, laboratory protocol critique
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated case analyses and a record of which laboratory error modes recur, held per firm
**Clock:** Discovery deadlines and trial dates
**Buyer:** Laboratory Director / Managing Principal
