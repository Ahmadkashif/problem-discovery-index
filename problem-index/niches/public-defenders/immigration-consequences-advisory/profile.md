# Immigration Consequences Advisory

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-defenders|Public Defenders]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Advise defence counsel on the immigration consequences of a plea — constitutionally required advice, where the wrong answer can mean permanent removal from the country.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-25 attorneys per resource centre
**Output:** Plea consequence analyses, practice advisories, charge-by-charge consequence charts, training
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated analysis mapping criminal dispositions to immigration outcomes across a body of law that changes constantly
**Clock:** Plea deadlines and appellate developments
**Buyer:** Directing Attorney
