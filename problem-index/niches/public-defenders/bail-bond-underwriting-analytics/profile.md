# Bail Bond Underwriting Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-defenders|Public Defenders]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Underwrite and reinsure commercial bail bonds — deciding which defendants to write, at what collateral, and pursuing forfeitures when they fail to appear.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 underwriters, analysts, and recovery staff across the surety programmes
**Output:** Underwriting rules and collateral requirements, forfeiture and recovery analytics, agent performance monitoring
**Proprietary data:** Bond writing decisions joined to appearance outcomes — a large record of who actually returned to court
**Clock:** Court appearance dates and statutory forfeiture periods
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
