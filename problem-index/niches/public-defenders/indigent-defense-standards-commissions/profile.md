# Indigent Defence Standards & Oversight Commissions

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-defenders|Public Defenders]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State commissions that set caseload and performance standards for indigent defence, distribute funding, and evaluate whether counties are meeting constitutional obligations.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 across the state commissions — analysts, standards staff, and evaluators
**Output:** Caseload and performance standards, funding allocation formulas, compliance evaluations, workload studies
**Proprietary data:** Case and workload data collected from offices statewide, rarely comparable across states
**Clock:** Legislative appropriation cycles and annual reporting
**Buyer:** Executive Director
