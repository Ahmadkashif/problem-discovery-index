# Justice Case Management System Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-defenders|Public Defenders]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Supply the case management systems courts, prosecutors and defender offices run on. Pass 1 describes the defender-side products as legacy, slow, poorly supported and lacking meaningful analytics, with integration to court systems done by re-keying or overnight batch.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 product, implementation, and data staff across the vendors
**Output:** Case management workflow, reporting modules, data exchange between agencies, statistical reporting for funders
**Proprietary data:** Case processing records across many jurisdictions, held per client installation
**Clock:** Court calendars and statutory reporting deadlines
**Buyer:** VP of Product
