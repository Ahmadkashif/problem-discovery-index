# Criminal Justice Research Organizations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-defenders|Public Defenders]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Non-profit and university research centres producing the empirical work on caseloads, pretrial detention, sentencing disparity and indigent defence adequacy that the field's policy debates run on — including the caseload standards Pass 1 cites as being exceeded by two to five times.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 researchers, data scientists, and policy analysts across the major centres
**Output:** Empirical studies, caseload and workload standards, jurisdiction data dashboards, evaluation research, policy recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Multi-jurisdiction datasets assembled from agencies that publish nothing comparable, plus workload time studies
**Clock:** Grant cycles and legislative sessions
**Buyer:** Director of Research
