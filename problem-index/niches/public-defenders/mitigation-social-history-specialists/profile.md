# Sentencing Mitigation & Social History Investigation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-defenders|Public Defenders]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Reconstruct a client's life history — medical, psychiatric, educational, family, institutional — into the mitigation narrative and sentencing memorandum that argues for a lesser sentence. In capital cases this work is constitutionally required and its absence is itself grounds for reversal.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 mitigation specialists, social workers, and investigators across the specialist practices
**Output:** Social history investigations, mitigation reports and sentencing memoranda, records collection and synthesis, expert coordination
**Proprietary data:** Case histories and the record of what mitigation themes moved which courts, held individually
**Clock:** Sentencing dates and capital case timelines
**Buyer:** Practice Director
