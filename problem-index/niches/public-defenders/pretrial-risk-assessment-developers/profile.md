# Pretrial Risk Assessment Instrument Developers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-defenders|Public Defenders]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Build and validate the actuarial instruments courts use to decide who is released before trial and on what conditions, and the risk-need tools that inform supervision and sentencing.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 criminologists, statisticians, and implementation researchers
**Output:** Risk instruments and scoring, local validation studies, implementation guidance, fairness and disparate impact analyses
**Proprietary data:** Multi-jurisdiction outcome datasets linking assessed risk to failure to appear and rearrest
**Clock:** Jurisdiction implementation and revalidation cycles
**Buyer:** Research Director
