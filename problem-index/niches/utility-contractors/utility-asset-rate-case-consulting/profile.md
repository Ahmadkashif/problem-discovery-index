# Utility Asset Management & Rate Case Consulting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/utility-contractors|Utility Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Build the asset management strategies and depreciation, replacement and capital plans utilities defend before regulators, and provide the testimony that justifies the spending that becomes contractor work.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 asset management consultants, engineers, economists, and expert witnesses
**Output:** Asset management plans, replacement prioritisation, depreciation studies, capital programme justification, regulatory testimony
**Proprietary data:** Comparative asset performance and replacement practice across many utilities, held per engagement
**Clock:** Rate case filing and hearing schedules
**Buyer:** Practice Leader / Managing Director
