# Utility Damage Investigation & Claim Recovery

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/utility-contractors|Utility Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Investigate who is at fault when an excavator strikes a buried facility — was the locate late, wrong, or ignored — and recover the cost of repair from the responsible party.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 investigators, claims analysts, and recovery specialists
**Output:** Fault determinations, damage investigation reports, recovery claims, root cause categorisation, prevention recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Damage investigations joined to the underlying ticket, locate record and site conditions — the only record of why struck lines actually get struck
**Clock:** Statutory damage reporting and claim filing periods
**Buyer:** Director of Damage Prevention / VP of Claims
