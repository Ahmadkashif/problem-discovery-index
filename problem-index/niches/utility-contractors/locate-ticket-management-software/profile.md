**Output:** Ticket intake and routing, screening against facility maps, field workflow and documentation, positive response, compliance reporting
**Proprietary data:** Ticket lifecycle records across many operators and centres, held per client
**Clock:** Statutory response windows
**Buyer:** VP of Product
