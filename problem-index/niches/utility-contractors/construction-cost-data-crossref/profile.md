# Infrastructure Cost Data Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/utility-contractors|Utility Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Publish the unit cost and productivity databases utility and civil work is estimated against — cost per foot of trench by soil and depth, per pole set, per splice — used by owners, engineers and contractors who need a defensible number.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-400 cost engineers, data collectors, and analysts
**Output:** Unit cost databases, crew productivity rates, regional cost indices, annual cost books
**Proprietary data:** Crew productivity observations collected over decades against a task taxonomy nobody else maintains
**Clock:** Annual publication with quarterly updates
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Chief Product Officer
