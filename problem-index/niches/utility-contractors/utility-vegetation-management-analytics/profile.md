# Utility Vegetation Management Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/utility-contractors|Utility Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Survey every mile of overhead line from the air, model tree growth and fall-in risk against conductors, and produce the work plan that sends crews to trim — the largest single operating expense for many electric utilities and, since catastrophic wildfires, a regulator-scrutinised one.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 remote sensing analysts, foresters, data scientists, and programme managers
**Output:** LiDAR and imagery derived clearance measurements, growth and encroachment forecasts, fall-in and strike risk scoring, work prescriptions and crew planning, regulatory compliance documentation
**Proprietary data:** Repeated aerial surveys of the same circuits over years, joined to outage and ignition records
**Clock:** Regulatory inspection and mitigation plan cycles, plus fire season
**Buyer:** VP of Vegetation Analytics / Chief Forester
