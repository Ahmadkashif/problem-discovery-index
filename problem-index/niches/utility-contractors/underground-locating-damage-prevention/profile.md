# Underground Utility Locating & Damage Prevention

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/utility-contractors|Utility Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Answer the question every excavation in the United States legally has to ask first: what is buried here, and exactly where. A locate request goes to a one-call centre, is screened against facility maps to decide which operators are affected, and is dispatched to a technician who traces the line electromagnetically and paints the ground. Miss a line and someone hits a gas main. Pass 1's operators cannot put a shovel in the ground until this layer has answered.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-3,000 ticket screeners, mapping and GIS analysts, quality assurance staff, and data teams across the one-call centres and the national locating contractors
**Output:** Ticket screening and operator notification, locate marks and sketches, positive response documentation, damage investigation findings, risk-based ticket prioritisation
**Proprietary data:** Hundreds of millions of tickets joined to facility maps, locate outcomes and reported damages — the only record of what is actually buried where, and of where the maps are wrong
**Clock:** Statutory response windows, typically forty-eight hours, after which an excavator may legally dig
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Quality
