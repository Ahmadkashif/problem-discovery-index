# Pipeline Integrity Management & In-Line Inspection

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/utility-contractors|Utility Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Run instrumented tools through operating pipelines, interpret what the sensors saw, and tell the operator which of tens of thousands of detected anomalies must be excavated and repaired, in what order, before which date. Federal rules require periodic reassessment of pipelines in high-consequence areas, so the assessment is not optional and the deadline is statutory. The excavation crews that act on the findings are this industry's customers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-2,000 data analysts, NDE specialists, corrosion and materials engineers, integrity engineers, and tool developers across the inspection vendors and integrity consultancies
**Output:** Anomaly detection, classification and sizing from magnetic flux, ultrasonic and geometry data; dig prioritisation and remaining strength calculations; corrosion growth rate estimates; reassessment interval recommendations; integrity management plans
**Proprietary data:** Decades of inspection runs joined to dig verification measurements — every time an operator excavates, the true depth and extent of the anomaly is measured and compared to what the tool predicted, producing a labelled dataset about buried steel that exists nowhere else
**Clock:** Statutory reassessment intervals and regulator-enforced repair deadlines tied to anomaly severity
**Buyer:** VP of Integrity Services / Chief Engineer
