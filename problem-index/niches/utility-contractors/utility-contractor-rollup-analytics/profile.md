# Utility Contractor Rollup Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/utility-contractors|Utility Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator/rollup

## What They Do
Corporate analytics inside the large utility infrastructure contractors — crew productivity, unit price bidding, equipment utilisation, storm response mobilisation and acquisition screening across national operations.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 analysts, estimators, and corporate development staff
**Output:** Crew productivity and unit cost analytics, bid pricing models, equipment utilisation, storm mobilisation planning, acquisition screening
**Proprietary data:** Unit production rates by crew, task, geography and condition across enormous volumes of repetitive work
**Clock:** Bid dates, storm events, and quarterly reporting
**Buyer:** VP of Analytics
