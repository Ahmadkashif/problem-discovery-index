# Grid Reliability & Outage Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/utility-contractors|Utility Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Analyse outage causes, asset failure and system reliability for electric utilities, and turn it into the capital and maintenance plans that determine which lines get rebuilt and which poles get replaced — the work programme utility contractors bid on.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 reliability engineers, data scientists, and asset analysts
**Output:** Outage cause analysis and reliability indices, asset failure prediction, capital prioritisation, storm response modelling, regulatory reliability reporting
**Proprietary data:** Decades of outage records joined to asset attributes, weather and maintenance history across distribution systems
**Clock:** Annual regulatory reliability reporting and capital planning cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Asset Analytics / Chief Reliability Officer
