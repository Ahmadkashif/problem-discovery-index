# Buried Infrastructure Condition Assessment

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/utility-contractors|Utility Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Inspect buried water, sewer and gas distribution mains without excavation — acoustic, electromagnetic, CCTV and sonar survey — and tell the owner which segments are failing and in what order to replace them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 engineers, inspection technicians, data analysts, and asset management specialists
**Output:** Condition assessments, remaining life estimates, leak location, renewal prioritisation, capital planning input
**Proprietary data:** Decades of inspection records on buried pipe by material, diameter, age and soil, sometimes joined to subsequent breaks
**Clock:** Capital planning cycles; no statutory driver for the assessment itself
**Buyer:** VP of Asset Management
