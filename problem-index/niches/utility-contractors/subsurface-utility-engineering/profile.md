# Subsurface Utility Engineering & Mapping

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/utility-contractors|Utility Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Determine where buried utilities actually are before a project is designed — geophysical survey, vacuum excavation to expose and measure, and delivery of mapped utility locations at a stated quality level that a designer can rely on.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 engineers, geophysicists, survey technicians, and CAD specialists
**Output:** Utility location deliverables at defined quality levels, conflict analysis against proposed design, as-built mapping, risk assessments
**Proprietary data:** Verified utility positions from physical exposure, project by project, joined to what the record drawings claimed
**Clock:** Design and construction schedules
**Buyer:** Practice Leader / Director of SUE
