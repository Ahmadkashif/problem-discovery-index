# Damage Prevention & Pipeline Safety Regulators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/utility-contractors|Utility Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Set and enforce the excavation notification, locating and pipeline integrity rules the whole industry operates under, investigate serious incidents, and maintain the incident databases the field's safety knowledge rests on.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 across federal and state programmes — inspectors, engineers, and analysts
**Output:** Regulations and enforcement actions, incident investigation findings, integrity management oversight, national incident and damage reporting
**Proprietary data:** Reported incidents and enforcement histories nationally, plus operator integrity data submitted under rule
**Clock:** Statutory inspection and reporting cycles
**Buyer:** Programme office
