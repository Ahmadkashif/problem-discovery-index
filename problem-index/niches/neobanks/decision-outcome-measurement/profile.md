# Decision Outcome Measurement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/neobanks|Neobanks]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to join several million decisions a year to the outcomes already sitting in the ledger — and whoever assembles that dataset has the only thing that makes every other decision in the institution improvable.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.3B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — the join nobody makes
**Target Buyer:** Risk and data leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the data exists on both sides

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A neobank makes several million consequential predictions a year and observes the consequence of nearly all of them in its own ledger. Deposits resume or they do not. The disputed transaction is represented or written off. The frozen account is reinstated or closed. That is a labelled dataset assembling itself continuously inside the institution, and it is the one dataset nobody constructs, because the decision lives in a vendor's system, the outcome lives in operations, and no team owns the join. This is the category's corpus problem in its purest form and it is what every other niche here depends on.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Vendor decision logs, operational records of reinstatements and closures, dispute outcomes, and fraud loss reporting. The gaps: decisions and outcomes in separate systems with no join; reinstatement not recorded as a model error; declined applicants producing no evidence; no labelled dataset anywhere; and no team accountable for the connection.
