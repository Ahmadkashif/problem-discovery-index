# Ongoing Account Risk

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/neobanks|Neobanks]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell a fraudster from a customer having a bad month, using the institution's own ledger — and whoever does that stops freezing the wages of people who did nothing wrong.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.4B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~14% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** High — monitored heavily, evaluated barely
**Target Buyer:** Risk operations leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — abundant behavioural evidence

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Once someone is a customer, the institution has everything: every transaction, every deposit, every balance, the whole shape of their financial life. The question is whether an unusual pattern is fraud, money laundering, or a person whose circumstances changed — a new job, a family emergency, a side business, a house move. The evidence is abundant and behavioural, the adversary is different from the one at the door, and the error is freezing someone's access to their own wages. That combination makes it a completely different problem from the onboarding decision, and it is the one where the institution's own data is the advantage.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Transaction monitoring rules, velocity and anomaly thresholds, sanctions and structuring detection, and account restriction workflows. The gaps: anomaly treated as suspicion; no model of ordinary life events; freezes applied at full severity by default; no outcome feedback; and a restriction that is easy to apply and hard to lift.
