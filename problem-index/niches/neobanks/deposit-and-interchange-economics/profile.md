# Deposit & Interchange Economics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/neobanks|Neobanks]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to become the account a customer's wages arrive into rather than the card they use occasionally — and whoever does that earns from a relationship instead of from a transaction.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.5B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — the model is known, the mechanics are not
**Target Buyer:** Commercial and product leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — prediction and intervention are both modellable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The economics rest on interchange and a widening interest-income base, and both depend on the same thing: whether this is the account the customer's income arrives into. A primary account produces direct deposit, a stable balance, high card usage and a durable relationship; a secondary one produces a fraction of each. The difference in lifetime value between the two is enormous, the transition between them is observable in the ledger, and it is treated as a marketing outcome rather than as a measurable, influenceable state.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Direct deposit incentives, early-access-to-pay features, balance and usage reporting, and acquisition-focused marketing. The gaps: primacy treated as a binary rather than a trajectory; no prediction of which new customers will become primary; no identification of the moment a relationship is forming or failing; and churn measured after the balance has already gone.
