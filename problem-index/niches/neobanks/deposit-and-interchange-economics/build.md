# The Account Wages Arrive Into

**Niche:** [[niches/neobanks/deposit-and-interchange-economics/profile|Deposit & Interchange Economics]]
**Industry:** [[industries/neobanks|Neobanks]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Everything in the economics depends on whether this is the account a customer's income arrives into, and primacy is treated as a marketing outcome rather than as an observable, influenceable state.
**Tags:** #survival-analysis #gradient-boosting #causal-inference #confidence-intervals #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact #time-series-forecasting #descriptive-statistics
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to become the account a customer's wages arrive into rather than the card they use occasionally — and whoever does that earns from a relationship instead of from a transaction.

## The Problem
Two customers opened accounts the same week. One set up direct deposit, keeps a balance, uses the card for groceries and rent, and is worth a large multiple of the other, who funded once, spent forty dollars and left the account dormant. The difference was decided in the first few weeks, by a sequence of steps some customers completed and others abandoned, and the institution can see every one of those steps in its own data. Primacy is the central variable in the business model and is managed with an incentive offer and a hope.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Acquisition is measured and celebrated while the transition to primacy is diffuse and slow, so attention follows the metric that reports weekly — the funnel ends at account opening because that is where the marketing organisation's accountability ends. Direct deposit setup depends on an employer's payroll system the institution does not control, which makes it feel unaddressable. Primacy is treated as binary rather than as a trajectory with observable stages. And the customers who never became primary simply go quiet.

## What to Build
Treat primacy as a measurable state with a trajectory. Define the stages of the relationship forming — funding, first card use, recurring payment, direct deposit, balance retention — and measure progression through them, which is the foundation and turns a marketing outcome into an operational metric. Predict which customers will become primary from early behaviour, since the signal is strong within days and the institution currently treats every new account identically. Identify the moment a customer stalls, because the intervention that works in week two is useless in month four and the stall is visible. Remove the friction in direct deposit setup specifically, since it is the single highest-value step and is defeated by an employer portal, a paper form and a routing number the customer cannot find. Measure the causal effect of each incentive rather than its correlation with primacy, as the customers who take a direct deposit bonus are disproportionately those who would have anyway. Model the whole relationship's value including interchange, interest and fee income, so the investment in achieving primacy is priced against what it is worth. Detect primacy loss early, since a customer whose deposits move elsewhere is visible before the balance goes and is recoverable at that point. Segment interventions by predicted value, because the same effort is currently spent on customers worth wildly different amounts. Connect to the freeze problem, since a false freeze destroys primacy instantly and is the most expensive customer experience failure in the business. And report primacy rate as the headline commercial metric, because it is the one the whole model rests on.

## Target Customer
Commercial and product leadership at digital banks, the growth teams measured on account openings, and the payroll and deposit infrastructure providers in the path.

## Impact If Built
The funnel ends at account opening because that is where marketing's accountability ends, and the decisive weeks after it are unmanaged. Measuring progression through observable stages and predicting primacy from early behaviour turns the model's central variable into something operational.
