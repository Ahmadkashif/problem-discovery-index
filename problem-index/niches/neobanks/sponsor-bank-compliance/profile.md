# Sponsor Bank Compliance

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/neobanks|Neobanks]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce compliance evidence once and satisfy any sponsor bank with it — and whoever does that removes the binding constraint on every product decision in the category.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.2B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — bespoke packs per partner
**Target Buyer:** Compliance and partnership leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — evidence generation is mechanical

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every neobank builds the same compliance reporting pack from scratch in the format its particular sponsor bank happens to want, and rebuilds it when the bank changes. After the consent orders of 2023 and 2024 the sponsor relationship became the binding constraint on every product decision in the category, which means the reporting is no longer an administrative obligation but the mechanism through which a fintech's ability to ship anything is governed. It is done by hand, bespoke per partner, by small compliance teams.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Spreadsheets, manual data pulls, bespoke report templates per bank partner, and periodic audits. The gaps: every pack built from scratch; formats dictated per partner with no standard; evidence assembled manually each cycle; no continuous monitoring against the bank's expectations; and a rebuild whenever the partner changes.
