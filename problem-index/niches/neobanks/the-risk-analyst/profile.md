# The Risk Analyst

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/neobanks|Neobanks]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give the analyst the evidence and the time to decide correctly rather than a screenshot and a throughput target — and whoever does that changes who gets access to their own wages.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$900M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — a queue and a case count
**Target Buyer:** Risk operations leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — evidence assembly is automatable, judgement is assisted

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Risk analysts spend their shift deciding, from a screenshot and a bank statement, whether a stranger deserves access to their own wages, and are measured on how many cases they close. They are the human component of the freeze process, they make decisions with serious consequences on thin evidence at speed, and their performance metric is throughput. Whatever judgement they develop is not measured, not fed back and not retained, which is distinct from the decisioning systems niche: this is about the person the system escalates to.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Case queues, document uploads from members, risk system flags without explanations, and a throughput target. The gaps: evidence arriving as screenshots and unstructured documents; no context on why the case was flagged; no record of what similar decisions produced; measurement on volume rather than accuracy; and no feedback on whether a decision was right.
