# Adverse Action Notice Practice

**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Lending has been required for decades to give specific reasons for a decline, and account restriction gives a reference to the deposit agreement.
**Tags:** #compliance #worker-facing #evaluation-metrics #workflow-orchestration #confidence-intervals #automation #descriptive-statistics #logistic-regression
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let the person a frozen member reaches explain what happened and do something about it — and whoever does that turns the category's worst interaction into a resolvable one.

## The Problem
Consumer lending has an established obligation to give an applicant the principal reasons for an adverse decision. That obligation forced the creation of reason code generation from scoring models, constraints on models so that explanations are possible, standardised disclosure and a dispute route. It is imperfect and it exists. Freezing a customer's access to their own deposits is at least as consequential as declining a credit application, and the communication is a form letter citing the deposit agreement.

## What Already Exists
Adverse action reason code generation; explainability constraints on decisioning models; standardised notice templates; dispute and reconsideration processes; and fair lending analysis of decision patterns.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a fraud decision where disclosure can assist an adversary. It requires: (1) a disclosure design that distinguishes the genuinely adversarial minority from the majority who are not, since blanket non-disclosure is currently justified by a minority case and harms everyone — resolving that trade-off deliberately is the substantive work; (2) a decision that can be reversed rather than reapplied for, so the notice should say what would resolve it rather than only why it happened; (3) urgency, since a declined loan application is a disappointment and a frozen account is a person who cannot buy food this week; (4) obligations that in some cases prohibit disclosure entirely, which must be separated from the discretionary restrictions rather than used to justify silence about both; and (5) delivery through a live agent conversation rather than a mailed notice, which changes the format completely.

## Target Customer
Support and compliance leadership at digital banks, sponsor banks concerned about programme conduct, and the regulators whose expectations in this area are developing.

## Impact If Solved
Lending was compelled to give reasons and built the apparatus; freezing deposits is at least as consequential and gives a form letter. Separating the adversarial minority from the majority is the trade-off that blanket non-disclosure currently resolves in the worst possible way.
