# Cash-Flow Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/neobanks|Neobanks]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a complete view of a customer's financial life into something that helps them rather than into a balance screen — and whoever does that earns the primacy the whole model depends on.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$500M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — the data is complete, the product is a balance
**Target Buyer:** Product leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — forecasting from a complete ledger

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A neobank holding a customer's primary relationship sees their entire financial life: every deposit, every bill, every transfer, the rent, the groceries, the pattern of a month. Forecasting whether a customer will be short before payday, identifying a subscription they forgot, spotting an income disruption, predicting an overdraft — all of these are tractable from that ledger and most of them are not built. The product is a balance and a transaction list. This is distinct from the risk niches because the same data is being used to police the customer and not to help them.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Balance display, transaction categorisation of varying quality, spending summaries, and occasional low-balance alerts. The gaps: no cash-flow forecast; categorisation that is unreliable and unused for anything consequential; recurring commitments unidentified; income disruption undetected; and alerts that fire when the balance is already low rather than before.
