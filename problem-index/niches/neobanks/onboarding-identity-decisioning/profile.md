# Onboarding & Identity Decisioning

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/neobanks|Neobanks]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to judge a stranger from third-party data and a document against an organised adversary — and whoever does that well decides who can open a bank account at all.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.4B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~14% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** High — many vendors, little evaluation
**Target Buyer:** Risk and growth leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — a prediction with adversarial pressure

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
At the door the institution knows nothing about the applicant except what identity vendors, device signals and a submitted document say. There is no behavioural history. The adversary is organised, buys the same vendor data, tests defences systematically and operates at scale. The error on one side is admitting a fraud ring and on the other is refusing a legitimate person a bank account, which for the population these institutions serve is a significant harm. This is a data-vendor and identity contest with an adversary, and it has almost nothing in common with judging an existing customer's ledger.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Identity verification vendors, document and selfie checks, device and behavioural signals at application, consortium fraud data, and rules on top. The gaps: vendor scores unevaluated on this population; thin-file applicants systematically disadvantaged; declines that generate no feedback; document checks defeated by known techniques; and no measurement of who was wrongly refused.
