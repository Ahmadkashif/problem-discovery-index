# Disputes & Reg E Operations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/neobanks|Neobanks]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a member's description of what went wrong into a coded claim before a regulatory clock expires — and whoever does that handles volume growth without either breaching deadlines or writing off valid claims.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.0B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — free text and a deadline
**Target Buyer:** Operations and compliance leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — classification and evidence assembly are automatable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Disputes arrive as a member's free-text description of what went wrong and must become a coded claim against a network deadline, and the clock does not adjust for volume. The regulatory timetable is fixed, the classification determines the claim's success, the evidence must be gathered from several systems, and the consequence of missing a deadline is a provisional credit the institution cannot recover. This is a distinct operational contest with a hard external constraint, and it is handled by people reading messages and filling forms.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Dispute intake forms, case management of varying quality, network claim submission, provisional credit workflows, and manual evidence gathering. The gaps: free-text intake that must be interpreted; classification determining the outcome and made by a person under time pressure; evidence assembled by hand from several systems; deadlines tracked in a spreadsheet; and representment decisions made without evidence of what wins.
