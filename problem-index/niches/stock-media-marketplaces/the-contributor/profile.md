# The Contributor

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/stock-media-marketplaces|Stock Media Marketplaces]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell a contributor why their income moved — search ranking, licensing terms, or a model trained on their own work — and whoever explains it keeps the supply the entire business rests on.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$320M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — a dashboard and no explanation
**Target Buyer:** Contributor relations leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — attribution and explanation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Contributors build a catalogue over years and watch its income move for reasons they cannot see, decided by search ranking, licensing terms and now by models trained on their own work. They supply everything the marketplace sells, have no visibility into any of the mechanisms that determine their earnings, and have watched the relationship become adversarial. The supply side is the business's only real asset and it is the constituency with the least information.

## Current Tools & Gaps
An earnings dashboard, a download count and a policy announcement. The gaps: income changes unexplained; search ranking effects invisible; licensing terms changed unilaterally; no advance notice of changes that affect earnings; and no route to raise anything that works.
