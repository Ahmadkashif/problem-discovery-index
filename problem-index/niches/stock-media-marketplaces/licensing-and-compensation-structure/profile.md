# Licensing & Compensation Structure

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/stock-media-marketplaces|Stock Media Marketplaces]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to build a corpus licensing arrangement that contributors accept and model developers will pay for — and whoever designs one that holds owns the terms on which visual training data is traded.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$450M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~11% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — unilateral terms
**Target Buyer:** Executive, commercial and legal leadership
**Automation Potential:** Moderate — the design is commercial, the administration is mechanical

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This contest is commercial and legal design. What does the licence permit, how is consent obtained, what happens on opt-out, on what basis is the rate set, how is the money distributed, who represents the contributors, and what makes the arrangement durable rather than a term-of-service change that gets litigated. It is negotiated by executives and lawyers, it is proceeding now, and it does not depend on the attribution work existing — though it would be far stronger with it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Terms of service amendments, bilateral deals, opt-out toggles and a compensation fund. The gaps: consent obtained by amendment rather than sought; opt-out consequences unclear; rate basis undisclosed; no contributor representation; and no durable structure for the deals that will follow.
