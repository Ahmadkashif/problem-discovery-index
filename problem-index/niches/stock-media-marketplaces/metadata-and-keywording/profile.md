# Metadata & Keywording

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/stock-media-marketplaces|Stock Media Marketplaces]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to describe an asset well enough to be found without depending on the person who made it — and whoever does it stops excellent work earning nothing because its contributor is a poor keyworder.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — supplied by contributors
**Target Buyer:** Content operations leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — description with demand-aware vocabulary

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
An asset's income depends on metadata supplied by the person who made it. A photographer who is excellent at photography and indifferent at describing their work in the vocabulary buyers use will earn a fraction of what their assets are worth, and will never find out why. Keywording is simultaneously the determinant of discovery, a substantial operational cost, and a skill the supply side was never selected for.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Contributor keywording forms, suggestion tools, controlled vocabularies and a review step. The gaps: quality not measured against discovery outcomes; suggestions generic rather than demand-aware; the buyer's actual query vocabulary not fed back; conceptual and emotional description weak; and contributors never told their keywording is the problem.
