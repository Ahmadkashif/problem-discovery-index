# Contribution Attribution

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/stock-media-marketplaces|Stock Media Marketplaces]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to measure what an individual asset contributed to a trained model's behaviour and output — and whoever demonstrates an attribution that survives technical scrutiny gives the whole licensing question a basis it currently lacks.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$550M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~14% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — not attempted commercially
**Target Buyer:** Research and rights leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — an open research problem with unusual data

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This contest is measurement. Given a corpus and a model trained on it, determine what any individual asset contributed — to the model's general capability, to a particular output, to the substitution of a particular kind of commercial imagery. It is an active research area with real methods and real limitations, and the marketplaces hold something researchers rarely have: a fully provenanced corpus with descriptions and two decades of demand data attached. It is not a commercial design problem.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Nothing deployed. The gaps: influence and attribution methods not applied to licensed corpora; output similarity not systematically measured against source assets; the demand record not used as a value weight; no published methodology; and no independent verification.
