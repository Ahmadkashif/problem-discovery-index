# Influence Estimation From Machine Learning Research

**Industry:** [[industries/stock-media-marketplaces|Stock Media Marketplaces]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Training data attribution is an active research field with real methods, and the industry with the cleanest corpus has not used any of it.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to measure what an individual asset contributed to a trained model's behaviour and output — and whoever demonstrates an attribution that survives technical scrutiny gives the whole licensing question a basis it currently lacks.

## The Problem
Attributing a model's behaviour to its training data is a real research area: influence functions and their scalable approximations, datamodels, ablation and retraining studies, and memorisation and similarity analysis. The methods are imperfect, computationally demanding and improving. They are studied mostly on academic datasets and scraped corpora. The commercial question they would answer is being decided right now by parties who have not consulted any of it.

## What Already Exists
Influence function approximations at scale; datamodel and ablation approaches; memorisation and extraction analysis; output-to-training-example similarity measurement; and evaluation practice for attribution claims.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a commercial claim rather than a research finding. It requires: (1) an attribution that must survive an adversarial counterparty and possibly a court, which is a far higher bar than a research result and is the substantive difference; (2) attribution at the scale of hundreds of millions of assets, where the methods are demonstrated at smaller scale; (3) access to the model, which the marketplace may not have and which shapes what methods are even possible; (4) provenance, description and demand metadata that research corpora lack and that support additional approaches; and (5) a result that must be explainable to contributors, not only to researchers.

## Target Customer
Research and rights leadership, model developers, contributor representatives, and academic collaborators.

## Impact If Solved
The research field is active and studies scraped corpora, while the commercial question is being settled without it. Raising an attribution to a standard that survives an adversarial counterparty is the adaptation, and the clean corpus is what makes it feasible.
