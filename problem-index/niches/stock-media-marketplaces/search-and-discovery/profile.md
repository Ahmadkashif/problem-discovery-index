# Search & Discovery

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/stock-media-marketplaces|Stock Media Marketplaces]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to return the asset a buyer actually wanted from a library of hundreds of millions — and whoever ranks on what buyers licensed rather than on what they clicked decides which contributors earn anything.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~25% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** High — the category's strongest capability
**Target Buyer:** Product and search leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — an active modelling contest

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Search is the entire product for the buyer and the entire economy for the contributor. A library of hundreds of millions of assets is accessible only through the ranking, so the ranking determines which work is seen and which earns. Visual and semantic search have improved substantially, and the ranking objective is still largely engagement — clicks and downloads — in a business where the meaningful outcome is a licensed asset that satisfied a brief.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Keyword and visual search, embeddings, ranking models and filters. The gaps: ranking optimised on clicks rather than on licensed satisfaction; zero-result and abandoned searches unexploited; new contributor cold start unaddressed; near-duplicates crowding results; and buyer intent not modelled beyond the query.
