# Duplicate & Similarity Management

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/stock-media-marketplaces|Stock Media Marketplaces]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stop a catalogue of hundreds of millions of assets being mostly the same few million pictures — and whoever measures redundancy honestly knows what their library is actually worth.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$70M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — detected for policy, not for value
**Target Buyer:** Catalogue operations leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — embedding and clustering at scale

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Catalogue size is the industry's headline number and a large share of it is redundancy: near-identical frames from the same shoot, the same concept photographed ten thousand times, and assets so similar that no buyer would distinguish them. Redundancy degrades search, inflates review volume, dilutes contributor earnings and misrepresents the library's actual coverage — and the only place it is measured is in a policy check for outright duplicates.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Exact and near-duplicate detection for policy enforcement. The gaps: redundancy never quantified; effective catalogue size unknown; concept-level saturation unmeasured; contributor incentives rewarding volume; and the review and storage cost of redundancy uncounted.
