**Industry:** [[industries/stock-media-marketplaces|Stock Media Marketplaces]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let a reviewer assess technical quality, rights, similarity and policy in seconds without every rejection being an unexplained loss to someone — and whoever supports that decision improves both the catalogue and the relationship it depends on.

## The Problem
Processes that reject submissions at scale — grant review, academic peer review, platform moderation, insurance claims — all converge on the same apparatus: written criteria with examples, reviewer calibration, quality assurance sampling, measured inter-reviewer agreement, and an appeals route with independent review. They arrive there because inconsistent rejection destroys trust in the process. Stock review has criteria and a queue.

## What Already Exists
Written criteria with worked examples; reviewer calibration sessions; quality assurance sampling; inter-rater agreement measurement; and appeals with independent review.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to seconds per decision at enormous volume. It requires: (1) a decision budget of seconds where the source practices allow minutes or hours, so calibration must be embedded in tooling rather than in training — this is the binding constraint; (2) assessments that are partly aesthetic and commercial, where agreement is genuinely lower and must be measured rather than assumed; (3) a rejected submitter who is a supplier the marketplace depends on, not an applicant; (4) volumes that make full appeal review impossible, so appeals must be triaged; and (5) rejection reasons that need to teach, since the same contributor will submit again tomorrow.

## Target Customer
Review operations leadership, reviewers, contributors, and moderation and review platform vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Every scaled rejection process converges on calibration, sampling and appeals because inconsistency destroys trust. Embedding that in tooling at seconds per decision is the adaptation, and rejection reasons that teach are what the supplier relationship requires.
