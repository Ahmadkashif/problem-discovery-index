# Training Corpus Economics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/stock-media-marketplaces|Stock Media Marketplaces]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to establish what a contributor's work is worth when it trains a model that substitutes for the library — and the contest splits cleanly enough that it is not terminal.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~25% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — settled by formula and litigation
**Target Buyer:** Executive and rights leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — attribution measurable, structure designable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The libraries are among the most valuable training corpora in existence and a meaningful share of the imagery they sold can now be generated. Some marketplaces have licensed their corpora to model developers and others have litigated. Either way the contributors whose work constitutes the corpus are compensated by unilateral formula, and no participant has attempted to measure what an individual contribution is actually worth. This is the contest that decides the industry's future and it is two: measuring contribution and designing the arrangement.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Licensing deals, unilateral compensation formulas, opt-out mechanisms and litigation. The gaps: no measurement of individual contribution; compensation not derived from anything; consent and opt-out handled minimally; no collective representation; and the provenance advantage unexploited as an argument.
