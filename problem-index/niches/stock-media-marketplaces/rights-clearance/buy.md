# Clearance Practice From Broadcast

**Niche:** [[niches/stock-media-marketplaces/rights-clearance/profile|Rights Clearance]]
**Industry:** [[industries/stock-media-marketplaces|Stock Media Marketplaces]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Broadcast and film built rights clearance into a documented production discipline, and stock verifies at intake with a form.
**Tags:** #compliance #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #automation #object-detection #descriptive-statistics
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to determine whether an asset can be licensed commercially — releases, trademarks, property, editorial restrictions — without a person examining the image and the paperwork every time.

## The Problem
Film and broadcast production treats clearance as a tracked discipline: rights and clearances logged per element, releases obtained and filed at the point of capture, chain of title maintained, errors and omissions insurance requiring documented process, and clearance reports produced for distribution. It is thorough because distribution depends on it. Stock clearance happens once, at intake, by a reviewer who was not present when the image was made.

## What Already Exists
Clearance logs per element; release capture at the point of production; chain of title documentation; errors and omissions requirements and reporting; and clearance supervisor practice.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to assets made by strangers and cleared afterwards. It requires: (1) clearance performed after capture by someone who was not there, so the evidence is whatever the contributor supplied and cannot be gathered retrospectively — this is the fundamental difference; (2) volume that makes a clearance supervisor per asset impossible; (3) an unknown downstream use, so clearance must anticipate uses rather than clear a specific one; (4) the contributor rather than a production carrying the release obligation, with no professional training behind it; and (5) an asset licensed many times over decades, meaning a clearance defect surfaces long after and affects many buyers.

## Target Customer
Rights and legal leadership, contributors, buyers and their legal teams, and clearance software vendors serving production.

## Impact If Solved
Production clears at the point of capture with a supervisor present, and stock clears afterwards from whatever was supplied. Guiding the contributor to capture clearance correctly at the moment of the shoot is where the practice would actually transfer.
