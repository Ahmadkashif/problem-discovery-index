# Rights Clearance

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/stock-media-marketplaces|Stock Media Marketplaces]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to determine whether an asset can be licensed commercially — releases, trademarks, property, editorial restrictions — without a person examining the image and the paperwork every time.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$500M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — verified by a person
**Target Buyer:** Rights and legal leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — detection with targeted verification

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Whether an asset can be licensed commercially depends on model releases, property releases, trademark visibility, recognisable locations and editorial restrictions. Each is verified by a person looking at the image and the paperwork. Getting it wrong exposes the marketplace and the buyer to a claim; being over-cautious removes licensable assets from commercial use and costs contributors income. The volume grows continuously and the verification does not scale.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Release forms, a review step, policy documents and a restriction flag. The gaps: detection of trademarks, faces and landmarks not automated; release documents not validated or linked to the people in the image; restrictions applied coarsely; buyer-side risk not communicated; and no record of which restrictions ever mattered.
