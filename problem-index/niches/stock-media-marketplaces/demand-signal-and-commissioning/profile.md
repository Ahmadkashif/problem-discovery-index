# Demand Signal & Commissioning

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/stock-media-marketplaces|Stock Media Marketplaces]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn two decades of searches that returned nothing usable into a commissioning brief — and whoever does it fills the gaps a generative model cannot and competitors cannot see.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$130M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — the record is unread
**Target Buyer:** Content strategy leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — mining an existing query record

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The marketplace has a complete record of what buyers searched for, what they were shown, what they selected and what they licensed, over twenty years. Inside it are the searches that returned nothing, the searches where the buyer looked at fifty results and licensed none, and the concepts that are searched constantly and served badly. That is a commissioning brief of unusual precision, it identifies exactly where the library is weak, and it is used for search tuning if at all.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Search analytics, popular keyword lists and contributor guidance about trending topics. The gaps: zero-result and abandoned searches not treated as demand; unmet demand not quantified in value; contributors not directed to it; commissioning not driven by it; and no distinction between demand a generative model will absorb and demand it cannot.
