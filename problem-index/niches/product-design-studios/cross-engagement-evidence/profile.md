# Cross-Engagement Design Evidence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/product-design-studios|Product Design Studios]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a decade of engagements into evidence about what interface decisions actually do — and whoever accumulates that takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.1B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — nothing accumulates
**Target Buyer:** Studio practice leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — structured corpus over comparable measurements

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Twenty engagements a year across a decade is a substantial natural experiment in interface design, running continuously, with the results discarded at each handover. This half is about accumulation: comparable measurement across clients, a structured record of what was changed and what followed, and an evidence base that makes the studio's recurring arguments answerable. It serves the practice rather than any client, requires the per-engagement measurement as an input, and is what turns a studio from a portfolio into an authority.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A portfolio, a set of principles and the opinions of the senior people. The gaps: no comparable measurement across engagements; no structured record of design decisions and outcomes; no way to query the studio's own history; no synthesis across similar problems; and no mechanism for a finding to become practice.
