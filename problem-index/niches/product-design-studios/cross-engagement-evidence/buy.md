**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a decade of engagements into evidence about what interface decisions actually do — and whoever accumulates that takes the account.

## The Problem
Medicine, education and psychology all faced the problem of many small, variably designed studies and built systematic review and meta-analysis in response: structured extraction of what was tested and what was found, effect size pooling, quality weighting, explicit treatment of heterogeneity, and published syntheses that practitioners consult. The methods are mature and taught. Design has a comparable body of small real-world tests and no synthesis apparatus at all.

## What Already Exists
Structured study extraction protocols; effect size computation and pooling; quality and risk-of-bias weighting; heterogeneity analysis; and published synthesis formats practitioners use.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to commercial engagements rather than designed studies. It requires: (1) observations that were never designed as experiments, with confounding and no control in most cases, so quality weighting must carry far more of the work — this is the substantive difference and limits what can honestly be claimed; (2) a classification vocabulary for design decisions that does not yet exist and must be built; (3) commercial confidentiality preventing publication of specifics; (4) outcomes measured differently by every client, requiring normalisation; and (5) synthesis serving a studio's practice rather than a field's literature, so it must be usable mid-project rather than read as a review.

## Target Customer
Design studios, in-house design organisations, design professional bodies, and research synthesis tooling vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Evidence-based fields built synthesis to turn many small studies into knowledge, with mature and taught methods. Observations that were never designed as experiments is what makes quality weighting carry the load here.
