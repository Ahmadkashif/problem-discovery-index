# Review & Stakeholder Alignment

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/product-design-studios|Product Design Studios]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a design decision made when the objections are preferences and neither side has evidence — and whoever makes that process work takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~11% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — by seniority
**Target Buyer:** Delivery leadership
**Automation Potential:** Medium — decision process and evidence surfacing

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Design review is where engagements lose their schedule and their coherence. Rounds multiply, stakeholders who were not present earlier reopen settled questions, objections are preferences expressed as concerns, and there is no evidence available to either side. The studio can either concede, which degrades the work, or argue, which costs relationship and time. The process itself is the problem, and no studio has designed one.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A review meeting, a comments thread on a prototype, a revision count in the contract and a decision log nobody keeps. The gaps: no defined decision rights; no distinction between preference and requirement; no evidence surfaced at the point of disagreement; no record of what was settled; and no bound on reopening.
