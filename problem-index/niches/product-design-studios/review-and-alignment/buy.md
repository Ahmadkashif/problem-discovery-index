# Decision Rights From Architecture Governance

**Industry:** [[industries/product-design-studios|Product Design Studios]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Software architecture records decisions with their reasoning so they are not relitigated, and design review has a comments thread.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #compliance #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #descriptive-statistics #tacit-knowledge-ml #automation #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a design decision made when the objections are preferences and neither side has evidence — and whoever makes that process work takes the account.

## The Problem
Software architecture faced the same disorder — consequential decisions made in meetings, reopened by newcomers, with the reasoning lost — and answered it with lightweight practice: architecture decision records capturing the context, the options, the decision and its consequences; defined decision rights; and a convention that reopening requires new information rather than a new opinion. It is cheap, widely adopted and effective. Design review has none of it.

## What Already Exists
Decision records with context, options and consequences; defined decision rights and escalation; review board structures scaled to decision weight; conventions for reopening a decision; and searchable decision histories.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to decisions whose subject is visual and whose reviewers are not practitioners. It requires: (1) reviewers who are stakeholders rather than peers, so the reasoning must be legible to a marketing director rather than to an engineer — this is the substantive difference; (2) decisions about visual and interaction qualities where the options cannot be described adequately in text alone; (3) an external supplier with no authority to impose governance on the client; (4) engagements of weeks, so the record must cost minutes rather than a document; and (5) objections that are legitimately about taste, which architecture rarely has to accommodate.

## Target Customer
Design studios, client product leadership, in-house design organisations, and collaboration tooling vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Architecture decision records are cheap, widely adopted and stop relitigation. Reviewers who are stakeholders rather than peers, deciding about visual qualities, is what the format has to be rebuilt for.
