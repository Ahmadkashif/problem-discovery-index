# Deliverable Production & Handoff

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/product-design-studios|Product Design Studios]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a design from a file into something a development team can build without a designer spending days producing specifications — and whoever automates that handoff takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$0.6B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — partial
**Target Buyer:** Design operations
**Automation Potential:** Very high — export, specification and packaging

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Between a finished design and a shipped feature sits a body of production work: states and edge cases, responsive variants, specifications, assets at every density, copy in every locale, and documentation of behaviour that the file does not express. It is done by designers, under deadline, at the end of the engagement, and it is the least creative and most repeated part of the job. It is also where a large share of implementation error originates.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A design tool with a handoff mode, an export panel and a specification document someone wrote. The gaps: no automatic generation of states and variants; no completeness checking against what the build needs; no behavioural specification; no asset pipeline; and no verification that what was built matches what was designed.
