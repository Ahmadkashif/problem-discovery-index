# Staged Commitment From Research and Development

**Niche:** [[niches/product-design-studios/discovery-scoping/profile|Discovery Scoping & Pricing]]
**Industry:** [[industries/product-design-studios|Product Design Studios]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Research organisations fund uncertain work in stages with decision gates, and design studios quote a fixed fee for it.
**Tags:** #confidence-intervals #revenue-impact #evaluation-metrics #probability-distributions #descriptive-statistics #workflow-orchestration #hypothesis-testing #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to price fixed-fee discovery work that by definition does not know what it will find — and whoever prices it honestly and still wins takes the account.

## The Problem
Research and development funding solved the problem of committing money to work whose scope is unknown: fund a stage, gate on what it produced, then commit the next. Phased contracts, stage gates, option-based funding and go/no-go reviews are standard across research, pharmaceutical development and capital projects, precisely because a single upfront commitment to uncertain work is a bad instrument for both parties. Design discovery is quoted as a single fixed fee.

## What Already Exists
Phased contracting with stage gates; option-based and milestone funding; go/no-go decision criteria defined in advance; scope definition as a funded deliverable; and contingency reserves held against defined risks.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to engagements of weeks with clients who are buying a service rather than funding a programme. It requires: (1) stages measured in weeks rather than quarters, so the gating apparatus must be a paragraph rather than a governance process — this is the substantive difference; (2) a competitive bid against studios offering confident fixed prices, so the staged structure must be positioned as an advantage rather than a hedge; (3) a client procurement process that frequently requires a total figure; (4) deliverables that are judgement and direction rather than a testable result; and (5) studios with no commercial function to design contracts.

## Target Customer
Design studios, commercial leadership, clients commissioning discovery, and professional services contracting vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Staged funding with decision gates exists because a single upfront commitment to uncertain work is a bad instrument for both parties. Stages measured in weeks, against competitors quoting confidently, is what the adaptation must survive.
