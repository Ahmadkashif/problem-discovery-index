# Discovery Scoping & Pricing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/product-design-studios|Product Design Studios]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to price fixed-fee discovery work that by definition does not know what it will find — and whoever prices it honestly and still wins takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.2B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — from memory
**Target Buyer:** Commercial leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — estimation from a project history

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Studios price fixed-fee engagements for discovery work that by definition does not know what it will find, using an estimate somebody produced from memory of the last similar project. The client wants a number. The work's scope depends on what discovery uncovers. The studio absorbs the difference out of margin or fights a scope conversation halfway through, and neither side has a mechanism for handling the uncertainty honestly.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A proposal template, a rate card, a day-count estimate and a margin assumption. The gaps: no record of past estimates against actuals; no risk pricing; no staged commercial structure; no measurement of scope change frequency; and no way to say what is unknown without losing the work.
