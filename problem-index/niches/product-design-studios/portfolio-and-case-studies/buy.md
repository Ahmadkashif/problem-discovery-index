# Reference Management From Enterprise Sales

**Niche:** [[niches/product-design-studios/portfolio-and-case-studies/profile|Portfolio & Case Study Production]]
**Industry:** [[industries/product-design-studios|Product Design Studios]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Enterprise sales built reference programmes with consent captured up front and material matched to the deal, and studios have a website.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #automation #data-integration #revenue-impact #evaluation-metrics #compliance #descriptive-statistics #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn finished engagements into the material that wins the next one, when the work is done by whoever is between projects — and whoever automates that takes the account.

## The Problem
Enterprise software sales built customer reference management into a function: consent captured at the point of contracting, reference assets produced systematically, a library indexed by industry and use case, matching of the right reference to the right opportunity, and tracking of which references influence which deals. It exists because references win deals and cannot be produced on demand. Design studios sell on exactly the same basis and manage it informally.

## What Already Exists
Reference consent captured at contracting; systematic reference asset production; indexed reference libraries; matching references to opportunities; and influence tracking from reference to won deal.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to visual work under client confidentiality about unreleased products. It requires: (1) material that is largely visual and frequently concerns work not yet public, so consent has timing conditions rather than being binary — this is the substantive difference; (2) a matching dimension that is problem shape and craft rather than industry and use case; (3) studios of ten to fifty people with no sales operations function; (4) case studies that must show reasoning rather than only outcomes, since craft is what is being bought; and (5) engagements short enough that capture must be near-zero-cost to happen at all.

## Target Customer
Design studios, new business functions, in-house design teams, and sales enablement and content vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Enterprise sales made reference consent and production a function because references win deals and cannot be made on demand. Visual work on unreleased products, with timing conditions on consent, is what the adaptation must handle.
