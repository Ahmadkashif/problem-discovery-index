# Portfolio & Case Study Production

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/product-design-studios|Product Design Studios]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn finished engagements into the material that wins the next one, when the work is done by whoever is between projects — and whoever automates that takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$0.3B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — manual
**Target Buyer:** New business
**Automation Potential:** Very high — capture, assembly and approval

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A studio wins work on its portfolio, and the portfolio is produced sporadically by whoever has capacity, months after the engagement ended, from files nobody organised and memories that have faded. Client approval is chased late and frequently refused. The result is that a studio's strongest recent work is often absent from its own case studies, and proposals are assembled from whatever exists rather than from what is most relevant.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A website, a slide deck of past work and a shared drive. The gaps: nothing captured during the engagement; no approval sought while the relationship is warm; no structured record of what the work was; no matching of past work to a new opportunity; and no measurement of which material wins.
