**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to demonstrate that the design it sold produced the outcome it claimed, when the evidence lands in the client's analytics after the contract has closed — and whoever demonstrates it takes the account.

## The Problem
Management consulting faced the same credibility problem and answered it commercially. Benefit tracking establishes a baseline before the work, defines the measures, follows them after implementation and reports realised value; outcome-linked and success fees put part of the consultancy's income on the result. It changed what could be sold and at what margin. Design studios make an equally testable claim, bill for deliverables, and stop at handover.

## What Already Exists
Benefit tracking methodology with pre-engagement baselines; defined value measures agreed at contracting; post-implementation benefit realisation reviews; outcome-linked and success fee structures; and value case documentation.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a deliverable that is an interface and an outcome measured in user behaviour. It requires: (1) outcomes that are conversion, retention and support volume measured in the client's product analytics rather than in a finance model — this is the substantive difference and makes access the binding constraint; (2) an intervention shipped as software, so the comparison must handle everything else that shipped alongside it; (3) engagements of weeks rather than months, so the measurement machinery must be far lighter; (4) studios of ten to fifty people with no benefits practice; and (5) a design effect that frequently appears over weeks rather than at launch.

## Target Customer
Design studios and consultancy design arms, clients commissioning design, in-house design leadership, and outcome measurement vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Consulting built benefit tracking and outcome fees because clients stopped paying for advice alone, and it changed the margin. Outcomes measured in product analytics rather than a finance model makes client access the binding constraint here.
