# Outcome Evidence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/product-design-studios|Product Design Studios]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to demonstrate that the design it sold produced the outcome it claimed, when the evidence lands in the client's analytics after the contract has closed — and whoever demonstrates it takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.4B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~27% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — nothing returns
**Target Buyer:** Studio leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — measurement plus accumulation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A studio sells the claim that this design will work better, ships it, and the evidence that would settle the question lands in the client's analytics after the contract has closed. A studio running twenty engagements a year holds evidence for none of them. What comes back is a testimonial or silence, so the firm's accumulated knowledge is a portfolio of screenshots and a set of strongly held opinions.

### Contested sub-niches
This niche is not terminal. It names a capability, and two separately bought problems sit inside it:
- [[niches/product-design-studios/post-handover-measurement/profile|🎯 Post-Handover Measurement]] — what happened to this engagement's work
- [[niches/product-design-studios/cross-engagement-evidence/profile|🎯 Cross-Engagement Design Evidence]] — what twenty engagements a year have established

## Current Tools & Gaps
A case study, a testimonial and a portfolio. The gaps: no measurement agreement in any contract; no baseline captured before the work; no access to post-launch analytics; no comparable measurement across engagements; and no record of which claims turned out to be true.
