# Selling Evidence Instead of Taste

**Niche:** [[niches/product-design-studios/outcome-evidence/profile|Outcome Evidence]]
**Industry:** [[industries/product-design-studios|Product Design Studios]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A firm whose product is a causal claim has never checked one.
**Tags:** #causal-inference #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #hypothesis-testing #data-integration #descriptive-statistics #revenue-impact #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to demonstrate that the design it sold produced the outcome it claimed, when the evidence lands in the client's analytics after the contract has closed — and whoever demonstrates it takes the account.

## The Problem
The studio's proposition is that a better-designed flow will convert better, retain longer or generate fewer support contacts. The redesign ships, the engagement ends, and every one of those numbers is measured by the client, in a system the studio has no access to, after the studio has left. The firm therefore competes on portfolio and reputation, prices against taste, and cannot distinguish its own good work from its bad.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The engagement ends at delivery and nobody is paid for what follows. Measurement requires client analytics access that nobody asks for. A baseline must be captured before the work starts, which nobody thinks to do. And a studio that measures might find out it was wrong.

## What to Build
Put measurement into the engagement rather than after it. Agree the outcome metric and capture the baseline before the design work begins, which is the core — a measure agreed afterwards is chosen to fit, and a baseline not captured before is gone forever. Negotiate post-launch analytics access as a standard contract term rather than a favour, since most clients would grant it and none are asked. Design the comparison so the redesign's effect can be separated from everything else the client shipped, which is the part that requires real care. Offer a measured follow-on engagement as a paid service, which is how this becomes commercially sustainable rather than charity. Report to the client first, since the result is about their product and their trust is the constraint. Handle the negative result honestly, as a studio that only publishes wins is back to selling testimonials. Structure each engagement so its measurement is comparable to the others, which is what makes accumulation possible later. Use staged rollouts where the client can run them, turning an observational comparison into an experiment. Record the claim made at proposal time so it can be checked against the result. And price on evidence once there is some, which is the business this changes into.

## Target Customer
Product design studios and consultancy design arms, clients commissioning design work, design leadership in-house, and measurement and analytics vendors.

## Impact If Built
A measure agreed afterwards is chosen to fit and a baseline not captured before is gone forever, which is why no studio can grade its own work. Agreeing the metric and capturing the baseline before the work starts is the whole difference.
