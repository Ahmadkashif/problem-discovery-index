# The Designer Defending a Decision

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/product-design-studios|Product Design Studios]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give the designer something other than their own judgement to bring to the sixth round of the same argument — and whoever supplies that takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$0.9B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — no support
**Target Buyer:** Design leads
**Automation Potential:** Medium — evidence retrieval and framing

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A designer defends the same decision across six rounds of review against stakeholders whose objections are preferences, with no evidence available to either side. The work is defended on articulacy, which means the most persuasive designer produces the best outcomes rather than the best designer. It is exhausting, it is a large share of what the job actually is, and it is a significant reason people leave the profession — and no tooling addresses it because it is regarded as a soft skill rather than a structural problem.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Their own judgement, a rationale slide and whatever they can find online. The gaps: no evidence retrievable at the moment of disagreement; no record of the studio's own findings; no framing support; no distinction between a defensible position and a preference; and no support for the person in a hostile review.
