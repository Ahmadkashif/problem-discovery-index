# Post-Implementation Review From Systems Integration

**Niche:** [[niches/product-design-studios/post-handover-measurement/profile|Post-Handover Measurement]]
**Industry:** [[industries/product-design-studios|Product Design Studios]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Systems integration built post-implementation review into the delivery method, and design engagements end at handover.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #evaluation-metrics #compliance #confidence-intervals #causal-inference #data-integration #descriptive-statistics #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get one engagement's outcome back from a client whose contract has closed — and whoever makes that routine takes the account.

## The Problem
Systems integration and large-scale IT delivery formalised the post-implementation review: a defined checkpoint after go-live, against benefits stated at business case, with a report to the sponsor and lessons captured for the next programme. It is part of most delivery methodologies and of public sector procurement requirements. It exists because a programme that is never reviewed after delivery cannot be improved or defended. Design engagements have no equivalent checkpoint.

## What Already Exists
Post-implementation review checkpoints; benefits stated at business case and checked afterwards; sponsor reporting on realised outcomes; lessons-learned capture; and contractual review obligations.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to short engagements with no business case and no programme governance. It requires: (1) a review proportionate to a six-week engagement rather than a multi-year programme, so the whole apparatus must be a page rather than a workstream — this is the substantive difference; (2) benefits measured in user behaviour rather than in cost or process efficiency; (3) a supplier with no ongoing presence after handover, unlike an integrator running support; (4) clients who are product teams rather than programme sponsors, with no review culture to plug into; and (5) an effect that appears gradually as users encounter the change rather than at a go-live date.

## Target Customer
Design studios, clients commissioning design, in-house design leadership, and delivery methodology vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Systems integration made post-implementation review part of the method because undelivered benefits went unnoticed otherwise. A review proportionate to a six-week engagement, measured in user behaviour, is what has to be built down to size.
