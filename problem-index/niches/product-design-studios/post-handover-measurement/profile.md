# Post-Handover Measurement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/product-design-studios|Product Design Studios]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get one engagement's outcome back from a client whose contract has closed — and whoever makes that routine takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.3B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~14% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — no access
**Target Buyer:** Studio delivery and commercial leadership
**Automation Potential:** Medium — contracting and measurement per engagement

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This half is per-client and contractual. It requires a metric agreed at kickoff, a baseline captured, analytics access negotiated, and a comparison designed that survives everything else the client shipped. It produces one result for one engagement, which is immediately valuable — a studio that can show a client's realised outcome has something no testimonial provides — and it can be done for a single willing client without any of the apparatus that accumulating across clients would require.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A closing meeting and a request for a testimonial. The gaps: no measurement clause in any contract; no baseline; no analytics access; no comparison design; and no paid follow-on structure.
