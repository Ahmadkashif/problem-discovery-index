# The Producer Holding the Budget

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/product-design-studios|Product Design Studios]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let the person who sees scope creep three weeks before anyone else raise it with evidence rather than as an accusation — and whoever supplies that takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$0.8B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** Production leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — burn tracking and scope detection

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The producer holds the budget, the timeline and the utilisation target, notices the scope creeping three weeks before anyone else, and has to be the person who says so. They sit between a client who believes each request is small, a design team that wants to do the work, and a commercial target. Raising it early costs relationship; raising it late costs money. They do this on every engagement, with a spreadsheet, and the role burns people out quietly.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A time tracking tool, a project plan, a spreadsheet and a weekly check-in. The gaps: no early detection of scope drift; no evidence to bring to the conversation; no visibility for the client; no forecast from actual burn; and no shared view across concurrent projects.
