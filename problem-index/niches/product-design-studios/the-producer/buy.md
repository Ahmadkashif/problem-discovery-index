# Earned Value From Project Controls

**Industry:** [[industries/product-design-studios|Product Design Studios]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let the person who sees scope creep three weeks before anyone else raise it with evidence rather than as an accusation — and whoever supplies that takes the account.

## The Problem
Project controls solved early overrun detection with earned value management: compare work actually completed against work planned and cost incurred, produce a schedule and cost performance index, and forecast the completion position from the trend. It detects overrun far earlier than a burn chart and it produces a number rather than an opinion. It is standard on construction and engineering programmes of any size. Design engagements track hours.

## What Already Exists
Earned value and completion forecasting; schedule and cost performance indices; variance thresholds triggering review; formal change control with impact assessment; and contingency drawdown tracking.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to creative work where progress is not measurable in completed units. It requires: (1) progress that cannot be measured as percentage complete on a design task, so the earned value input must be inferred from deliverable state and review position rather than counted — this is the substantive difference and is the reason the method has not transferred; (2) engagements of weeks, needing something far lighter than a controls function; (3) scope change that arrives conversationally rather than through a change request; (4) a client relationship where formal change control reads as adversarial; and (5) producers with no project controls training.

## Target Customer
Design studios, producers and delivery leads, clients commissioning design, and professional services automation vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Earned value detects overrun far earlier than a burn chart and produces a number rather than an opinion. Progress that cannot be counted as percentage complete is what has to be inferred differently for creative work.
