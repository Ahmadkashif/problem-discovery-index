# The Credit Manager Selling to Independent Stores on Terms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/consumer-goods-wholesalers|Consumer Goods Wholesalers]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to decide which independent stores get net terms and how much, and whoever writes off the least receivables per dollar of terms sales takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M a year of bad debt and credit-desk cost on wholesalers' terms sales to independent stores (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.1% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — bureau reports for some accounts; trade references by phone, industry credit-group meetings and memory for the rest
**Target Buyer:** Credit manager, or the CFO at smaller houses
**Automation Potential:** Medium — scoring and monitoring automate; collection calls and judgement calls on loyal accounts stay human

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A gift, toy or outdoor wholesaler carries thousands of small store accounts on net 30 to net 90, many with thin or no commercial bureau file. One credit manager approves new accounts and limits, holds orders, chases slow payers and hears about closures from reps or competitors. The tools that compete to help are commercial credit bureaus (indexed at [[niches/independent-retailers/commercial-credit-bureaus/profile|🔍 Commercial Credit Bureaus]], 56; ~48 under current rulings, pending Stage E), industry credit groups such as the Toy Association's Credit Group, which reviews retail accounts at its meetings, trade credit insurers ([[niches/independent-retailers/trade-credit-insurance-underwriting/profile|🔍 Trade Credit Insurance Underwriting]]), factors ([[niches/specialty-design-studios/apparel-factoring-retailer-credit/profile|🔍 Apparel Factoring & Retailer Credit]]), and Faire, whose 60-day terms move the store's credit risk off the brand. The general operating contest of commercial credit is at [[niches/credit-bureaus/commercial-trade-credit/profile|Commercial Trade Credit]]. This niche is the wholesaler's own credit desk facing very small accounts.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Bureau reports and scores, AR modules in the ERP, collection software and credit-group meetings. The gaps: no score built on the wholesaler's own payment and ordering history, and no early warning from rep notes, order changes or other vendors' signals.
