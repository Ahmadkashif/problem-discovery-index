# Industry Credit Groups

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/consumer-goods-wholesalers|Consumer Goods Wholesalers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Groups of credit managers from companies selling to the same retailers who meet to share payment experience. The Toy Association's Credit Group meetings give "an update and analysis on the state of key retailers" and review retail accounts under antitrust guidelines. ABC-Amega manages GAIN, a gift-industry group of 100+ credit professionals, and NACM affiliates run 350+ industry credit groups, about 80 of them national (search summary). Members pay fees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** A secretary or a few association staff per group
**Output:** Meeting agendas, account reviews and shared payment experience
**Proprietary data:** Members' receivables experience on shared customers
**Clock:** Quarterly meeting calendars
**Buyer:** The group's administrator at the association or credit agency
