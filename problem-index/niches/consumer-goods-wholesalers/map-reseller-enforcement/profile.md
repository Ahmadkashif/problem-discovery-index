# MAP & Authorised-Reseller Enforcement Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/consumer-goods-wholesalers|Consumer Goods Wholesalers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Services that monitor where a brand's genuine goods are advertised below its minimum advertised price, or sold by resellers it never authorised, and then enforce against them. TrackStreet monitors pricing across 185K+ websites and marketplaces and sells managed takedowns of unauthorised resellers. Vorys eControl is a legal-led MAP and authorised-reseller practice whose monitoring platform comes from its affiliate Precision eControl (search summary). Importers with their own brands and distribution rights pay subscriptions or legal fees. Counterfeit takedown, a different problem, is indexed at [[niches/ecommerce-sellers/brand-protection-enforcement/profile|🔍 Brand Protection & Enforcement]] (53).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analysts per monitoring vendor (estimate); the law practice's attorneys and investigators work matter by matter (production)
**Output:** Violation reports, enforcement notices and takedowns
**Proprietary data:** Crawled public listings and price histories; client matter files at the law practice
**Clock:** None external
**Buyer:** Brand VP of eCommerce or general counsel
