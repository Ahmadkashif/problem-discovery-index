# Markdown Money Agreed by Phone, Deducted in March

**Niche:** [[niches/consumer-goods-wholesalers/seasonal-promotional-buys/profile|Seasonal & Promotional Buys]]
**Industry:** [[industries/consumer-goods-wholesalers|Consumer Goods Wholesalers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Markdown support is agreed in a January call and taken from a March remittance at a different number, and the importer cannot show what was agreed.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #workflow-orchestration #revenue-impact #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to pick and size one-shot seasonal and promotional items that sell through at full price, and whoever leaves the retailer claiming the least markdown money per dollar shipped takes the account.

## The Problem
Post-season markdown and return agreements are made between an account manager and a buyer, by phone or a short email. Weeks later a deduction appears with a code and an amount that may not match, and the deduction team has no backup.

## Why It's Still Broken
The agreement lives with one person, the retailer's claim backup arrives in a portal under a different reference, and nobody links them.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Capture each markdown or return agreement as a structured record when it is made (item list, rate, cap, season), match incoming markdown deductions against it automatically, and route mismatches to the account manager with both documents.

## Who Feels the Pain
Account managers, deduction analysts and the CFO reconciling seasonal profitability.

## Impact If Fixed
If ~10% of markdown deductions exceed what was agreed (estimate), matching them recovers ~$200K a year on $2M of markdown money.
