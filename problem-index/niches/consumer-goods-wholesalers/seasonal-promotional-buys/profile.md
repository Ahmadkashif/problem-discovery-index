# Seasonal & Promotional Buys

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/consumer-goods-wholesalers|Consumer Goods Wholesalers]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to pick and size one-shot seasonal and promotional items that sell through at full price, and whoever leaves the retailer claiming the least markdown money per dollar shipped takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$45B a year of holiday, summer, back-to-school and promotional items sold by importers to big-box retailers (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~11% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — orders by EDI; item choice, buy depth and markdown negotiations by judgement, phone and email
**Target Buyer:** VP of Sales or head of merchandising at the importer
**Automation Potential:** Medium — forecasting and claim tracking automate; the item pick stays a judgement

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Holiday toys and décor, summer outdoor goods, grills, pools, back-to-school items and promotional pallets are bought once, months ahead, with no reorder. If they do not sell through, the retailer claims markdown money or returns the goods after the season, and the importer pays twice: once in deductions, once in its own leftover stock. The contest is won at item selection and buy depth, the merchant's judgement described in the [[problems/consumer-goods-wholesalers/high-impact|high-impact problem]], not at replenishment. The maker-side in-season planning contest, setting re-order quantities from point-of-sale data before the last re-order date, is profiled at [[niches/consumer-products-makers/seasonal-pos-demand-planning/profile|Seasonal Demand Planning From Retailer POS]]. Here the bet is placed before any point-of-sale data exists.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Spreadsheets of past seasons, retailer portal exports, merchant memory and the retailer's own markdown tools, which the importer never sees. The gaps: no graded record of past seasonal bets, and markdown money agreed by phone and deducted without documentation.
