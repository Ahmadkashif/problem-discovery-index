# Items Rejected for an Attribute Nobody Asked For

**Niche:** [[niches/consumer-goods-wholesalers/retailer-item-setup-content/profile|Retailer Item Setup & Product Content]]
**Industry:** [[industries/consumer-goods-wholesalers|Consumer Goods Wholesalers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A retailer adds a required attribute to a category, and every pending item in it bounces a week before the launch date.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #automation #compliance #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a wholesaler's new items accepted into each retailer's catalogue with complete attributes, images and compliance documents, and whoever achieves the highest first-submission acceptance rate takes the account.

## The Problem
Retailers change templates and category requirements on their own schedule and announce them in portal notices that coordinators rarely read. The importer learns from a batch of rejections.

## Why It's Still Broken
Notices are scattered across portals and emails, and nobody compares new requirements with the items in the pipeline.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Monitor each retailer's portal notices and template downloads, diff the requirements against the last version, list affected pending items, and draft the missing values from the item's documents.

## Who Feels the Pain
Product data coordinators, account managers explaining a missed launch, and merchants whose seasonal item misses its set date.

## Impact If Fixed
Catching template changes before submission avoids ~1–2 missed launch windows a year per major retailer (estimate), each worth ~$50–200K of first-season sales.
