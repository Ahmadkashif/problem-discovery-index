# Big-Box Supplier Account Agencies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/consumer-goods-wholesalers|Consumer Goods Wholesalers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Agencies, many based in Bentonville, that run Walmart and Sam's Club accounts for suppliers too small to staff them: Retail Link analysis, replenishment and forecasting, on-time and in-full follow-up, merchant contact and line review preparation. Bentonville Merchants (a division of Select Sales Inc.) and Bentonville Support Team describe this work on their own sites, and SellCord and The Barcode Group advertise replenishment analysts (search summary). Suppliers pay retainers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Analysts each carry a set of supplier accounts (production); no agency plausibly has a 10-person insight function apart from account work (estimate)
**Output:** Replenishment, scorecard and line review support for each supplier client
**Proprietary data:** None of their own; each client's Retail Link and Luminate data
**Clock:** Retailers' line reviews and replenishment cycles, which are client clocks
**Buyer:** Supplier VP of Sales or owner
