# Line Review Decks From Harmonised POS

**Niche:** [[niches/consumer-goods-wholesalers/big-box-retail-programmes/profile|Big-Box Retail Programmes]]
**Industry:** [[industries/consumer-goods-wholesalers|Consumer Goods Wholesalers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** POS harmonisers already clean the retailer's sell-through; the line review needs margin after claims and competitors' items beside it.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #large-language-models #feature-engineering #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep its items through each retailer's annual line review. But the review is won on two different measures with different winners: everyday items on automatic replenishment are kept on how few of the retailer's ordered units the importer ships short, and one-shot seasonal and promotional buys on how little markdown money the retailer claims back. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Preparing a line review takes an analyst or an outside agency weeks of Retail Link exports, pivot tables and slides for each retailer, every year.

## What Already Exists
Crisp, Alloy.ai and SPS Analytics pull and normalise retailer sell-through and inventory into dashboards and forecasts. The maker-side scoring of these platforms is at [[niches/consumer-products-makers/pos-data-harmonisation-platforms/profile|🔍 Retail POS Data Harmonisation Platforms]].

## The Customization Gap
A line review pack needs net margin per item after claims, the retailer's scorecard history, competing items' prices and assortment online, and a forecast for proposed new items. It has to be laid out the way each retailer's buyer reviews it.

## Target Customer
Sales teams at importers already paying for a POS harmoniser.

## Impact If Solved
Cutting line review preparation from ~3 analyst-weeks to ~3 days per retailer (estimate) frees ~25 analyst-weeks a year at an importer with ten accounts.
