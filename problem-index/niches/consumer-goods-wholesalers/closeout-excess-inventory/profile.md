# Closeout & Excess-Inventory Wholesale

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/consumer-goods-wholesalers|Consumer Goods Wholesalers]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to price a closeout lot from a manifest, a few photos and a deadline, and whoever earns the highest realised margin on the lots it wins takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$15B a year of excess, discontinued and returned consumer goods bought and resold by closeout wholesalers (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~4% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — lots offered by email, spreadsheet and phone, inspected in person or not at all; B2B auction marketplaces are the digital part
**Target Buyer:** Owner or head buyer at a closeout wholesaler
**Automation Potential:** Medium — pricing from manifests automates; inspection and the sellers' relationships do not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every overbought container, cancelled retailer order, discontinued model and season's leftovers ends up for sale as a lot. Closeout wholesalers in the misc-durables and nondurables codes buy those lots from importers, brands and retailers, often within days, and resell to off-price and discount chains, dollar stores, exporters and online resellers. The buyer prices a lot from a manifest of SKUs, quantities and a condition note, sometimes a few photos, against what each item will fetch in each outlet. Experienced buyers say they can "smell" a bad lot. Competitors are other closeout houses, auction marketplaces such as B-Stock (logged in this industry's Pass 2), and brokers. Downstream online arbitrage resellers are profiled at [[niches/ecommerce-sellers/reseller-arbitrage-ops/profile|Reseller & Arbitrage Operations]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Spreadsheets, marketplace price lookups item by item, and the buyer's memory of what similar lots resold for. The gaps: no record joining past lots' manifests to their realised resale by outlet, and no adjustment for each seller's habit of overstating condition.
