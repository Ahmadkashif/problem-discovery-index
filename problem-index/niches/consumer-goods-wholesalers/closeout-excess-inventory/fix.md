# Manifests That Don't Match the Truck

**Niche:** [[niches/consumer-goods-wholesalers/closeout-excess-inventory/profile|Closeout & Excess-Inventory Wholesale]]
**Industry:** [[industries/consumer-goods-wholesalers|Consumer Goods Wholesalers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The lot arrives with missing lines, swapped items and "new" goods that are customer returns, and nobody records how far each seller's manifests drift.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #feature-engineering #workflow-orchestration #revenue-impact #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to price a closeout lot from a manifest, a few photos and a deadline, and whoever earns the highest realised margin on the lots it wins takes the account.

## The Problem
Receiving teams find shortages, substitutions and condition gaps against the manifest. A claim is sometimes made, but the variance is never stored by seller, so the next bid to the same seller repeats the mistake.

## Why It's Still Broken
Receiving is done fast on the floor with paper tallies, and the buyer who bid the lot never sees the count.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Scan-based receiving against the manifest, a variance record per lot and seller, automatic claims where terms allow, and a seller accuracy score that feeds the next bid's discount.

## Who Feels the Pain
Buyers who overpaid, receiving leads doing tallies, and owners whose margin disappears on the dock.

## Impact If Fixed
If manifest variance averages ~5% of lot value (estimate), pricing it into bids and claiming it where possible saves ~$1M a year on $30M of purchases (estimate).
