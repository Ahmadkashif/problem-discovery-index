# Consumer Durables Market Share Trackers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/consumer-goods-wholesalers|Consumer Goods Wholesalers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Survey-based trackers of who sells which appliances, electronics and other durables through which retailers, at what price. OpenBrand bought TraQline, a quarterly survey of US consumers tracking brand share, retailer share and price, and renamed it Durable IQ; OpenBrand cites 170+ categories, 2,500+ brands and 1,000+ outlets (search summary), alongside price and promotion tracking. Manufacturers, retailers and the larger distributors subscribe. Point-of-sale tracking of the same categories is part of [[niches/food-distributors/retail-measurement-data-providers/profile|🔍 Retail Measurement Data Providers]] (54, indexed; ~48–50 under the current clock ruling, pending Stage E), which is not profiled again here.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 research and analytics staff (estimate)
**Output:** Quarterly brand and retailer share data, price and promotion tracking, custom analyses
**Proprietary data:** The vendor's own consumer survey panel history and price and advertising archive
**Clock:** The vendor's own quarterly release calendar
**Buyer:** Chief research or product officer at the tracker
