# Independent-Store Wholesale Through Reps & Trade Shows

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/consumer-goods-wholesalers|Consumer Goods Wholesalers]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to be the line an independent store reorders between markets, and whoever earns the most reorders per active store takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$45B a year of gift, toy, hobby, outdoor specialty, pet and book wholesale sold to independent stores (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~11% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — paper show orders, rep visits, phone and email, with Faire and rep apps the digital exceptions
**Target Buyer:** VP of Sales for the specialty channel, or the owner at smaller houses
**Automation Potential:** Medium — reorder prompts and order capture automate; the rep's relationship does not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Tens of thousands of gift shops, toy stores, outdoor specialists, pet shops and bookstores buy from wholesalers through independent rep groups paid on commission, at regional markets (Atlanta and Las Vegas, run by ANDMORE), at Toy Fair, and now on Faire, which lists 100,000+ brands, gives eligible stores 60-day terms and charges brands 15% plus $10 on a first order. The first order is written at a show; the money is in the reorders between shows. Those are decided by an owner walking the floor, a problem seen from the store side at [[niches/independent-retailers/vendor-reorder-automation/profile|Vendor Reorder Automation]]. Wholesalers compete with each other, with brands selling direct and with Faire for that reorder. Rep agencies themselves belong to the queued manufacturers' representatives build, and the card-and-gift version of the rep group is logged at [[niches/greeting-card-publishers/card-sales-rep-groups/profile|🔍 Card & Gift Sales Rep Groups]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
RepSpark, Pepperi, NuORDER and Brandwise for order writing, Faire for marketplace orders, and ERPs for everything else. The gaps: no prediction of when each store will need to reorder, show orders keyed weeks late, and store relationships held in reps' own notebooks.
