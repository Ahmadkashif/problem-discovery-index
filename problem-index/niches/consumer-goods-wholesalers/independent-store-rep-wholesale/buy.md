# Order-Writing Apps That Know the Store

**Niche:** [[niches/consumer-goods-wholesalers/independent-store-rep-wholesale/profile|Independent-Store Wholesale Through Reps & Trade Shows]]
**Industry:** [[industries/consumer-goods-wholesalers|Consumer Goods Wholesalers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Rep order-writing apps capture what a store orders; they do not tell the rep what this store should order today.
**Tags:** #k-nearest-neighbors #gradient-boosting #feature-engineering #worker-facing #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to be the line an independent store reorders between markets, and whoever earns the most reorders per active store takes the account.

## The Problem
A rep walks into a store with a catalogue of hundreds of items and twenty minutes. What gets ordered depends on what the rep remembers about that store.

## What Already Exists
RepSpark, Pepperi, NuORDER (now part of Lightspeed) and Brandwise show live inventory, catalogues and order history, and work offline at shows.

## The Customization Gap
The app needs store-specific recommendations: items similar stores reorder, gaps in this store's assortment against comparable stores, seasonal items due, and lines at risk because the store has not reordered on its usual cadence.

## Target Customer
Wholesalers whose reps already write orders in one of these apps.

## Impact If Solved
Adding two recommended items to a typical ~$500 rep order (estimate) lifts order value by ~10–15%.
