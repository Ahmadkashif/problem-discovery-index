# Show Orders That Never Become Reorders

**Niche:** [[niches/consumer-goods-wholesalers/independent-store-rep-wholesale/profile|Independent-Store Wholesale Through Reps & Trade Shows]]
**Industry:** [[industries/consumer-goods-wholesalers|Consumer Goods Wholesalers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A store places its first order at a market, receives it weeks later, and never hears from the wholesaler again until the next market.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #automation #revenue-impact #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to be the line an independent store reorders between markets, and whoever earns the most reorders per active store takes the account.

## The Problem
Market orders are written on paper or in apps, keyed in batches, and shipped in waves. Nobody follows up on whether the goods sold, and the rep may never visit a small store again.

## Why It's Still Broken
Follow-up belongs to the rep group, which is paid on orders written and moves on to the next market. The wholesaler does not hold a clean contact record for the store's buyer.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Capture every show order with the buyer's contact, schedule a check-in timed to when the goods should be half sold, and send a reorder suggestion through the store's preferred channel, crediting the rep on any resulting order.

## Who Feels the Pain
Sales heads watching first-time accounts lapse, reps losing commission on reorders they never prompted, and store owners who forget where they bought a good seller.

## Impact If Fixed
If half of first-time show accounts never reorder (estimate), converting a fifth of them adds ~300 active accounts a year at a wholesaler opening 3,000 (estimate).
