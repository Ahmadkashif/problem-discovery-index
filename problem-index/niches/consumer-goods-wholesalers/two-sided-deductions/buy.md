# Brand Deduction Platforms With a Supplier Ledger

**Niche:** [[niches/consumer-goods-wholesalers/two-sided-deductions/profile|Retailer Deductions on Both Sides]]
**Industry:** [[industries/consumer-goods-wholesalers|Consumer Goods Wholesalers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Deduction platforms already recover invalid claims for brands; wholesalers need them to split valid claims by supplier and re-bill.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #feature-engineering #automation #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn every retailer deduction on a wholesaler's invoices into either a recovered credit from the retailer or a debit to the supplier who caused it, and whoever recovers the most deduction dollars from either side takes the account.

## The Problem
A wholesaler that buys a brand deduction platform gets recovery on the retailer side and nothing on the supplier side. Valid claims are closed as "accepted" when a part of each belongs to a supplier.

## What Already Exists
Glimpse, Confido, SPS Revenue Recovery, HighRadius, iNymbus and Vividly, which pull claims, match documents, dispute and apply cash. The vendor field is logged at [[niches/consumer-products-makers/deduction-recovery-vendors/profile|🔍 Deduction Recovery Software & Services]].

## The Customization Gap
The platform needs SKU-to-supplier mapping, supplier agreement clauses (shortage, labelling, defect and markdown funding), debit memo generation with the retailer's backup, supplier acceptance tracking and offsets against payables.

## Target Customer
Controllers at wholesalers already using, or evaluating, a brand deduction platform.

## Impact If Solved
Re-billing even a quarter of supplier-caused deductions (estimate ~20% of the total) returns ~$300K a year on $6M of deductions (estimate).
