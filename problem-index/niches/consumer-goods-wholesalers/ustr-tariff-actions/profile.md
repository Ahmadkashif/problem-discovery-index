# USTR Section 301 & Tariff Actions

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/consumer-goods-wholesalers|Consumer Goods Wholesalers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Office of the US Trade Representative investigates trading partners' practices under Section 301, issues determinations and tariff schedules, and runs exclusion processes. CBP collects the duties. In March 2026 USTR opened forced-labour investigations of 60 trading partners; final determinations on 23 July 2026 set tariffs of 10% or 12.5% from 24 July 2026, which importers are challenging in court. The China Section 301 tariffs of 2018–2019 still apply to many goods from China (estimate of scope; not re-checked). Importers comment in dockets, request exclusions and litigate. CBP's trade office is logged at [[niches/customs-brokers/cbp-trade-office/profile|🔍 CBP Office of Trade]] (40).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Tens of staff working on Section 301 investigations (estimate)
**Output:** Determinations, tariff action notices and exclusion decisions
**Proprietary data:** Public comment dockets and exclusion requests, some filed as business-confidential
**Clock:** Statutory investigation timelines and the agency's own rulemaking calendar
**Buyer:** USTR General Counsel or the chair of the Section 301 Committee
