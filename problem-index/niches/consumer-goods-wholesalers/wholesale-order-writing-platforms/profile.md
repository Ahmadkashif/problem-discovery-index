# Wholesale Order-Writing & Rep Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/consumer-goods-wholesalers|Consumer Goods Wholesalers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Software that brands and wholesalers give their reps and buyers to write orders at shows, on store visits and online. RepSpark ties a rep and buyer portal to the brand's ERP, Pepperi works offline at shows and visits, NuORDER (now part of Lightspeed) is retailer-facing and strongest in fashion and lifestyle (search summary), and Brandwise sells into the same market (its offer was not checked). Brands pay subscriptions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analytics staff per vendor (estimate); product and engineering teams are not an insight function
**Output:** Order-capture software (the invoice), with sales dashboards
**Proprietary data:** Brands' order, catalogue and store records, held under subscription terms
**Clock:** None external; trade-show seasons
**Buyer:** CEO or head of product at the vendor
