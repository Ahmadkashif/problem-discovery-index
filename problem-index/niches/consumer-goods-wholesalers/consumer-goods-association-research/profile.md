# Consumer Goods Trade Association Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/consumer-goods-wholesalers|Consumer Goods Wholesalers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The research departments of the trade associations whose members make, import and wholesale these goods. The Consumer Technology Association publishes US consumer technology forecasts each January at CES and updates them mid-year; its 2026 forecast was $565 billion. The Toy Association publishes industry sales from Circana data ($30.3 billion of tracked US retail toy sales in 2025). The sporting goods and pet product associations publish participation and owner surveys. Members get most research with their dues, and some reports are sold to non-members.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–20 at CTA's research team (estimate); under 10 at each of the others
**Output:** Forecasts, market sizing, consumer surveys and member briefings
**Proprietary data:** Member-contributed input plus the associations' own consumer surveys
**Clock:** Each association's own release calendar, with CTA's tied to its own show
**Buyer:** VP of research at the association

The **member-input forecast half**, which carries the verdict, is live (**data isn't theirs**): CTA states that its forecast is based on "qualitative and quantitative input from CTA member companies and industry participants", which is membership data contributed for the association's purposes. The **own consumer-survey half** (the associations' own household and participation surveys) has no switch and would sit below threshold at the same 27.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch (member-input forecast half). Research is a member benefit more than an invoice, only CTA's team plausibly reaches 10, and the corpus that matters is the members'.
