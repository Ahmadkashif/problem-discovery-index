# Returns & Excess-Inventory B2B Marketplaces

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/consumer-goods-wholesalers|Consumer Goods Wholesalers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Online marketplaces where retailers and brands auction customer returns, overstock and discontinued goods by the pallet and truckload to closeout wholesalers, exporters and resellers. B-Stock runs separate storefronts for retailers such as Amazon, Walmart, Target, Costco and Home Depot and is cited at ~130M+ items a year (search summary); Liquidity Services and Direct Liquidation run similar retail channels. Sellers pay commission and buyers a premium. Independent auctioneers' online marketplaces are logged at [[niches/auctioneers/online-auction-marketplace-operators/profile|🔍 Online Auction Marketplace Operators]] (27).

## Insight Function
**Size:** A handful of pricing and seller-analytics staff per platform (estimate); no source shows B-Stock or Liquidity Services with a 10-person insight function apart from marketplace operations
**Output:** Auctions and recovery-rate reporting to sellers; the invoice is commission on sales
**Proprietary data:** Auction results by retailer, category, condition and lot size, across many sellers' storefronts
**Clock:** Auction schedules set by each seller's storefront campaigns, which are client clocks
**Buyer:** Head of data or marketplace at the platform
