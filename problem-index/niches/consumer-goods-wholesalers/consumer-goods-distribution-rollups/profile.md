# Consumer Goods Distribution Rollups

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/consumer-goods-wholesalers|Consumer Goods Wholesalers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Platforms that grow by buying wholesalers and exclusive distribution rights. Alliance Entertainment has made sixteen acquisitions, including COKeM, Distribution Solutions and Mill Creek. It became exclusive physical-media distributor for Paramount from 1 January 2025 and for Amazon MGM Studios from 1 January 2026, and it bid for, then walked away from, Diamond Comic Distributors' assets in that company's bankruptcy. Private equity platforms do the same in gift, toy, pet and outdoor distribution. The makers' equivalent is logged at [[niches/consumer-products-makers/consumer-products-rollup-platforms/profile|🔍 Consumer Products Roll-Up & PE Platforms]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** 2–8 corporate development and integration staff per platform (estimate)
**Output:** Acquisition models and integration plans, used internally
**Proprietary data:** The acquired companies' sales, customer and supplier records
**Clock:** Deal timelines
**Buyer:** CFO or head of corporate development
