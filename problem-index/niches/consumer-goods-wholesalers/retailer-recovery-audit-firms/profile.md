# Retailer Recovery Audit Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/consumer-goods-wholesalers|Consumer Goods Wholesalers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Firms hired by retailers to audit their past payments to suppliers and claim back what they find: allowances not taken, promotional funding owed, price protection on inventory, duplicate payments. PRGX is the best-known; a 2026 vendor comparison also lists Cotiviti's retail practice (search summary). They are usually paid a share of what they recover. Their claims reach wholesalers as deductions on remittances, and one PRGX case study describes a retailer's post-audit process running nearly two years from event to collection (search summary).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 analytics and data-science staff at the largest firm (estimate); auditors working claim by claim are production and are not counted
**Output:** Recovery claims against suppliers, billed as a share of recovered money
**Proprietary data:** Claim-pattern libraries built across many retailers' payment records and vendor agreements, held under each retailer's service agreement
**Clock:** Audit look-back periods and claim windows in vendor agreements, which are per-claim deadlines that bind production work
**Buyer:** Retailer CFO or VP of accounts payable; at the firm, the chief data officer
