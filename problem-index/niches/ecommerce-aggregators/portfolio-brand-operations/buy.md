## The Problem
Running many acquired businesses at once is what private equity operating groups and multi-brand consumer companies do, and they learned things: which functions to centralise and which to leave alone, how to allocate scarce operating attention across a portfolio, how to measure a business against its own pre-acquisition trajectory rather than against a budget, and that the integration itself destroys value if done carelessly. This sector assembled portfolios faster than any of them and built its operating practice from scratch.

## What Already Exists
Private equity operating partner models with hundred-day plans and value creation tracking; shared services design with clear centralise-or-devolve criteria; portfolio company performance measurement against entry case; multi-brand consumer operating models; and post-merger integration methodology with value leakage analysis.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to businesses that are small, numerous and all dependent on one platform. It requires: (1) operating attention allocated as a scarce resource across dozens rather than a handful of businesses, which is a different problem from the private equity model and is the brand manager niche's subject; (2) centralisation decided per function with evidence, since this sector centralised almost everything on the assumption that scale helps and some of it — responsiveness, category judgement — demonstrably does not; (3) performance measured against the entry case per brand, which is standard private equity discipline and is largely absent here; (4) concentration risk treated seriously, since a private equity portfolio is diversified across industries and this one is entirely exposed to a single platform's policy and algorithm; and (5) integration sequencing designed to protect the asset, which post-merger practice knows and which the migration niche shows this sector does not.

## Target Customer
Aggregator operating leadership, their investors, and the private equity operating discipline whose practice transfers with a scale adaptation.

## Impact If Solved
Decades of portfolio operating practice exists and this sector built a playbook from scratch. Deciding centralisation per function with evidence is the correction, since the assumption that scale helps is demonstrably wrong for responsiveness and category judgement.
