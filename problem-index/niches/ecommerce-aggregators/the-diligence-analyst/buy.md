## The Problem
Establishing which of a target's earnings will recur is the entire purpose of quality-of-earnings analysis, a mature transaction services discipline that normalises for one-offs, owner-dependent arrangements, unsustainable cost bases and revenue that depended on something ending. It is standard on transactions of every size in every other sector. Aggregator diligence largely verifies that the reported earnings occurred, which is a different and much weaker question.

## What Already Exists
Quality-of-earnings methodology with normalisation adjustments; owner dependency assessment; customer and supplier concentration analysis; working capital normalisation; sustainability testing for cost bases; and the standard transaction services report structure.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a business whose durability is determined by an algorithm. It requires: (1) a quality-of-revenue analysis alongside quality-of-earnings, since the question here is whether the traffic and ranking persist rather than whether the costs were understated — this reframing is the specific adaptation and it puts marketplace analysis at the centre of a discipline that is normally accounting; (2) owner dependency assessed in operational terms — responsiveness, supplier relationship, category knowledge — rather than in the compensation terms the standard methodology uses; (3) concentration measured across products, keywords and the single marketplace, since the standard customer concentration test misses that the entire business depends on one platform's algorithm; (4) normalisation for advertising spend, since a brand's earnings can be inflated by under-investing in advertising in the months before a sale, which is a specific and common manipulation; and (5) a timeline that fits an auction, which the fix note and the underwriting niche both address.

## Target Customer
Acquisition teams, transaction services firms for whom this is an underserved deal type, and the capital requiring better diligence.

## Impact If Solved
Quality of earnings is standard everywhere and this sector verifies that revenue occurred. A quality-of-revenue analysis is the adaptation, and normalising for advertising under-investment before a sale catches a specific and common way the figures are dressed.
