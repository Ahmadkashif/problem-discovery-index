# Incident Response & Breach Forensics Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cybersecurity-mssp|Cybersecurity MSSPs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Mandiant, Kroll, and the incident response practices investigate breaches — establishing what happened, what data was accessed, and how the attacker got in — and produce the report that drives notification decisions, litigation posture, and insurance recovery. The investigation is unambiguously the invoice and it is delivered under extreme time pressure.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-2,000 incident responders, forensic analysts, and threat intelligence staff
**Output:** Incident investigation reports, root cause findings, notification scoping
**Proprietary data:** Investigation findings across thousands of real breaches — the definitive record of how attacks actually succeed, which no telemetry vendor observes at the same depth
**Clock:** Statutory breach notification deadlines, measured in days
**Buyer:** Managing Director / Head of Incident Response
