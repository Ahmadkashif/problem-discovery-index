# Sector Information Sharing Centres

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cybersecurity-mssp|Cybersecurity MSSPs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The sector ISACs pool incident and threat information across member institutions in finance, healthcare, energy, and other critical sectors, and produce the sector-specific analysis and alerting members act on. Membership is the price of access and the analysis is a benefit rather than a product.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-200 analysts, researchers, and intelligence staff at the larger centres
**Output:** Sector threat analysis, member alerting, incident pattern research
**Proprietary data:** Member-shared incident detail that no vendor sees, contributed under trust arrangements that took years to build
**Clock:** Active campaigns targeting the sector
**Buyer:** Director of Intelligence — member-funded rather than commercial
