# Security Certification Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cybersecurity-mssp|Cybersecurity MSSPs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The certification bodies define what a security professional is supposed to know, run job task analyses, write and calibrate examinations, and issue the credentials MSSPs hire and bill against. In a field with a persistent staffing shortage, the credential is the hiring signal.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-200 test developers, psychometricians, and subject matter coordinators
**Output:** Certification examinations, job task analyses, workforce research
**Proprietary data:** Item response and candidate performance data across hundreds of thousands of security practitioners, plus the job analyses defining the roles
**Clock:** Examination cycles, certification renewal, and the pace of technology change that forces blueprint revision
**Buyer:** VP of Certification / Chief Assessment Officer
