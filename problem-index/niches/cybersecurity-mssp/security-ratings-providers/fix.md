# Disputes Are Handled Case by Case and Never Aggregated

**Niche:** [[niches/cybersecurity-mssp/security-ratings-providers/profile|Security Ratings Providers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/cybersecurity-mssp|Cybersecurity MSSPs]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Rated organizations challenge their scores constantly, each challenge is worked as a support ticket, and the accumulated record of where the methodology was wrong is stored in a helpdesk.

## The Problem
An organization that discovers its rating is costing it business challenges it — an asset that is not theirs, a finding already remediated, a signal they consider irrelevant. The dispute is worked by a support team, and when the challenge is valid the individual rating is corrected. That is the end of it. The dispute is not classified, not counted, and not routed to the research organization that owns the methodology. So the single richest source of information about where the rating is wrong — thousands of specific, evidenced objections from the parties who know their own infrastructure best — accumulates in a ticketing system, while methodology development proceeds from internal judgment about what signals ought to matter.

## Why It's Still Broken
Disputes arrive at customer support because that is who has the queue, and support is measured on resolution time rather than on classification quality. Structurally, the rated organization is often not the paying customer, so the dispute is a cost to be minimized rather than a signal to be harvested. And there is a defensive instinct with legal weight: an aggregated record of valid disputes is a documented history of the product being wrong, which counsel views as risk in a segment that has already attracted litigation — even though the same record is the strongest available evidence of a functioning correction process.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Dispute intake as a structured research input. Each challenge is classified — attribution error, stale finding, signal relevance, data collection error — with the evidence and the resolution recorded against the specific rating component involved. Aggregated, that answers what nobody can answer today: which signals generate the most valid objections, which organization types are systematically mis-rated, and whether a methodology change reduced disputes or merely moved them. Valid dispute rate by signal becomes a standing quality metric that methodology development is accountable to, which is the reversal that matters — today methodology proposes and support absorbs. Rated organizations get a faster, more consistent process and a visible record that challenges lead somewhere, which is itself the answer to the fairness criticism the segment attracts. And the record demonstrates a functioning correction mechanism, which is a stronger legal position than having no record at all.

## Who Feels the Pain
Rated organizations losing business over errors they must fight case by case; support teams re-litigating the same objection repeatedly; the research organization improving a methodology while the evidence of its flaws sits in another department; and the vendor, whose reputational and legal exposure comes precisely from disputes it is not learning from.

## Impact If Fixed
Converts the segment's largest operational irritant into its best quality signal, and does it using data already collected. It also directly addresses the criticism that most threatens these businesses — that ratings are opaque, unaccountable, and hard to correct — with a demonstrable process rather than an assurance.
