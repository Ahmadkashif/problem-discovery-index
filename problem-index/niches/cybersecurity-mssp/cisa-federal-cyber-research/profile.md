# Federal Cyber Defence Analysis

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cybersecurity-mssp|Cybersecurity MSSPs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The national cyber defence agency analyzes incidents reported by critical infrastructure operators, issues advisories, maintains the known exploited vulnerability catalogue that federal agencies must remediate against, and coordinates response to significant campaigns.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 analysts, threat hunters, and researchers
**Output:** Advisories, exploited vulnerability catalogue, incident analysis, sector guidance
**Proprietary data:** Incident reporting from critical infrastructure and intelligence community access unavailable to any commercial party
**Clock:** Active campaigns and mandated remediation timelines
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer
