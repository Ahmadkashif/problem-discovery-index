# Threat Intelligence Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cybersecurity-mssp|Cybersecurity MSSPs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Recorded Future, Flashpoint, Intel 471, and comparable firms run collection infrastructure across criminal forums, marketplaces, malware ecosystems, and internet-scale scanning, and employ analysts who turn that into finished intelligence — threat actor profiles, campaign tracking, vulnerability prioritization, and client-specific exposure reporting. MSSP analysts triaging alerts are consuming this intelligence when they decide whether an indicator matters.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-1,500 intelligence analysts, collection engineers, linguists, and researchers
**Output:** Finished intelligence, actor and campaign profiles, indicator and vulnerability prioritization — the subscription itself
**Proprietary data:** Collection access and archive built over a decade — forum and marketplace content, malware telemetry, and analyst attribution work that cannot be reconstructed retrospectively
**Clock:** Active campaigns and disclosure timelines; intelligence that arrives after exploitation is worthless
**Buyer:** VP of Intelligence / Chief Research Officer
