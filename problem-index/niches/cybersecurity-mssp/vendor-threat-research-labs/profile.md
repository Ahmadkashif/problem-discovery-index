# Security Vendor Threat Research Labs

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cybersecurity-mssp|Cybersecurity MSSPs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
CrowdStrike Intelligence, Microsoft's threat intelligence centre, Unit 42, and Talos analyze telemetry from millions of endpoints and networks, track adversary groups, and publish the research that shapes how the industry understands the threat landscape. The research is partly a product and largely a demand generation asset for the platform.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-800 threat researchers, reverse engineers, and analysts
**Output:** Threat research publications, adversary tracking, detection content, intelligence subscriptions
**Proprietary data:** Endpoint and network telemetry across millions of hosts, joined to confirmed intrusions — the deepest observational dataset on attacker behaviour in existence
**Clock:** Emerging exploitation and disclosure timelines
**Buyer:** VP of Threat Intelligence / Chief Security Officer
