# MSSP Detection Engineering & Threat Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cybersecurity-mssp|Cybersecurity MSSPs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The detection engineering and threat research teams inside Arctic Wolf, Expel, Deepwatch, and Secureworks write and tune the detections their analysts triage against, hunt across client telemetry, and publish the research that positions the service. Detection quality is the entire product differentiator between MSSPs.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 detection engineers, threat researchers, and hunters
**Output:** Detection content, hunt findings, threat research publications
**Proprietary data:** Security telemetry and analyst dispositions across thousands of client environments — the only cross-organization view of what actually triggers and what it turns out to be
**Clock:** Active incidents and emerging exploitation, which set the pace
**Buyer:** VP of Threat Research / Chief Technology Officer
