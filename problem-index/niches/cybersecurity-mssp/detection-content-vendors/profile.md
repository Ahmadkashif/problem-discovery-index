# Detection Content Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cybersecurity-mssp|Cybersecurity MSSPs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors selling detection rule libraries, threat hunting content, and adversary emulation packages to security teams that cannot write their own at the pace threats evolve. The content is the product, mapped to common frameworks and maintained against changing attacker technique.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 detection engineers, threat researchers, and content staff
**Output:** Detection rules, hunt queries, emulation plans, framework mappings
**Proprietary data:** Detection content library plus, where telemetry is available, efficacy data on which rules actually fire on real intrusions
**Clock:** Emerging technique disclosure and exploitation
**Buyer:** VP of Content / Head of Detection Engineering
