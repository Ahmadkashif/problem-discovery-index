# Healthcare Professional Reference Directories for Life Sciences

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/directory-and-list-publishers|Directory & List Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Compiled directories of physicians, nurses, pharmacists and the institutions they work in, with specialties, affiliations and, for key opinion leaders, profiles built from publications, trials and conference activity; IQVIA OneKey, Veeva OpenData and Link and Definitive Healthcare are representative (named from recall). Pharmaceutical and device companies license them as master data for sales, medical affairs and compliance reporting. Distinct from [[niches/pharmacy-independents/prescription-market-intelligence/profile|🔍 Prescription Market Intelligence Providers]] (54), which sells prescribing analytics rather than the directory, and from [[niches/pharmacy-independents/drug-compendia-pricing-publishers/profile|🔍 Drug Compendia & Pricing Content Publishers]] (56).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~50-300 medical analysts and profile curators per large vendor (estimate), excluding data stewards who verify records by call
**Output:** Healthcare professional and organisation master data; expert and opinion-leader profiles
**Proprietary data:** Verified affiliations and curated expert profiles over public licensing, publication and trial records
**Clock:** Manufacturers' annual Open Payments reporting and congress calendars depend on the data, but no deadline falls on the vendor's own deliverable
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / Head of Reference Data
