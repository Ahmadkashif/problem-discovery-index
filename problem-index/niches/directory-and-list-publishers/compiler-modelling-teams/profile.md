# National Compiler Modelling & Data Science Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/directory-and-list-publishers|Directory & List Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The data science teams inside the national compilers that aggregate thousands of sources — Data Axle blends 4,000+ — and model what sources do not say: employee size and sales volume for private firms, propensity and segment scores, call prioritisation (Data Axle's Smart-Calling). Dun & Bradstreet, taken private by Clearlake in 2025, and Experian's business data unit run similar teams.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-150 data scientists per national compiler (estimate)
**Output:** Modelled fields sold inside the compiled record; scores and segments
**Proprietary data:** The compiled universe joined to decades of call outcomes and customer feedback
**Clock:** None external
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / Head of Data Science
