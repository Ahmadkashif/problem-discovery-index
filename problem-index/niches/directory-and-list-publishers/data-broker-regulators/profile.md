# Data Broker Regulators & Registries

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/directory-and-list-publishers|Directory & List Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
California's privacy agency, which runs the data broker registry and the DROP deletion platform reaching 600+ brokers; other states' data broker registries; the FTC; and the Justice Department's consumer protection lawyers, who brought the 2021 Epsilon and KBM list-fraud prosecutions. They set the deletion clocks and vetting duties this industry works to.

## Insight Function
**Size:** <10 at any single agency working on data brokers (estimate); a larger figure only appears if staff are summed across agencies
**Output:** Registries, deletion infrastructure, rules, enforcement actions
**Proprietary data:** Registration filings and deletion-request records
**Clock:** Statutory and rulemaking calendars they set themselves
**Buyer:** Agency executive director / enforcement head
