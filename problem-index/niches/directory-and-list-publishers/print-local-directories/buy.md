# Call Tracking Built for Agencies, Pointed at a Phone Book

**Niche:** [[niches/directory-and-list-publishers/print-local-directories/profile|Print Local Directories]]
**Industry:** [[industries/directory-and-list-publishers|Directory & List Publishers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Call tracking platforms measure calls per campaign for digital agencies; a phone book needs calls per heading, per edition and per delivery area across thousands of small advertisers.
**Tags:** #linear-regression #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to prove to a local advertiser that the printed book still rings the phone — tracked calls per heading, per edition — and whoever proves it keeps the renewal.

## The Problem
Publishers that use tracked numbers get per-number call logs and little else, and advertisers without a tracked number have no evidence at all.

## What Already Exists
Call tracking platforms (CallRail, Invoca, CallTrackingMetrics) provision numbers, record calls and attribute them to campaigns and keywords for agencies and multi-location brands.

## The Customization Gap
A print publisher needs: number pools organised by book, heading and edition; reporting that rolls up across thousands of single-ad advertisers; distribution-area mapping so calls can be tied to where the book was delivered; plain printed or emailed reports for owners who do not log into dashboards; and number-porting rules so an advertiser keeps its tracked number across editions.

## Target Customer
Print directory publishers adding measurement to their renewal process.

## Impact If Solved
Every advertiser gets call evidence at modest cost, and the publisher builds the data the renewal argument depends on.
