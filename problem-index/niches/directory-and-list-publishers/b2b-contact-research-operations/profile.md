# B2B Contact Data Research Operations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/directory-and-list-publishers|Directory & List Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
The research and data-quality organisations inside contact-data vendors and the national compilers — ZoomInfo, Dun & Bradstreet, Data Axle, Cognism, Lusha and others — that decide which contact and company records are true, set verification methods and measure quality. Most of their headcount is verification production: Data Axle alone runs 150+ data specialists making ~60,000 calls a day. Scored like [[niches/public-relations-agencies/media-database-research-teams/profile|🔍 Media Contact Database Research Teams]] (44) except labour, because sub-minute verification calls are production rather than an insight function.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-80 methodology, quality and research analysts per large vendor (estimate); callers excluded
**Output:** Verified contact and company records; accuracy and coverage statistics; verification rules
**Proprietary data:** Years of call outcomes, contributory-network sightings and customer bounce reports — the best accuracy labels in the industry, mostly unjoined
**Clock:** None external; continuous decay of records
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / SVP Data Operations
