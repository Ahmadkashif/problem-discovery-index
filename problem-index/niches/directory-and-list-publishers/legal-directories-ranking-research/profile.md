# Legal Directories & Peer-Ranking Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/directory-and-list-publishers|Directory & List Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
The publishers the legal profession itself calls "the directories": Chambers and Partners, Legal 500, Best Lawyers, Martindale-Hubbell and similar guides that rank law firms and individual lawyers by practice area and jurisdiction. Their researchers read firms' submissions and interview clients and peers as referees; Chambers reports a team of 320+ researchers conducting over 350,000 interviews and surveys a year. In-house counsel read the rankings, usually free; law firms pay for profiles, ranking-related marketing and events because of where they are ranked, and large firms run whole teams to prepare submissions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 320+ researchers at Chambers (checked); ~20-150 at the other guides (estimate)
**Output:** Annual rankings by practice area and jurisdiction with editorial commentary on each firm and ranked lawyer
**Proprietary data:** Hundreds of thousands of confidential referee interviews a year, plus every firm's submissions and matter descriptions, accumulated over decades — a corpus no competitor or law firm holds
**Clock:** Self-set: each guide fixes its own submission deadlines, research windows and publication dates
**Buyer:** Head of Research / Editorial Director

**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. The strongest near-miss in this sweep: a large, repeatable, labour-scaled research operation on a corpus nobody else holds. Q1 is 3 because the rankings are the hook rather than the invoice — they are read free and firms pay for promotion around them — and the pocket is further held back by a clock the publisher sets itself and a buyer market of a handful of guides.
