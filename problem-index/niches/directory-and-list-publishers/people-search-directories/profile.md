# People-Search & Public-Record Directories

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/directory-and-list-publishers|Directory & List Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Consumer-facing directories of individuals compiled from public records, phone and address data and web sources — Whitepages, Spokeo and BeenVerified are representative (named from recall) — sold to individuals by subscription and per report. They position themselves outside the Fair Credit Reporting Act and are among the registered data brokers most exposed to California's DROP deletion cycle. The professional version of the same identity question is indexed as [[niches/collections-agencies/identity-skip-trace-data-providers/profile|🔍 Identity Resolution & Skip Trace Data Providers]] (54): bureau identity graphs from LexisNexis Risk Solutions and TransUnion, built from header, utility, telecom and contributed sources under supply agreements, sold per search to collectors and investigators with a permissible purpose. The consumer sites resell thinner public and licensed records at retail to anonymous users, with no linkage team of that scale and no professional buyer, which is why they score far lower on labour, data and market.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-20 data scientists and record-matching analysts at the largest sites, fewer at most (estimate; data engineers and site operations excluded) — passes the gate only narrowly
**Output:** Person profiles and reports; reverse phone and address lookup
**Proprietary data:** Compiled public-record and contact histories; inputs are largely public or licensed from bureaus and aggregators
**Clock:** California DROP deletion processing on a rolling 45-day basis from 1 August 2026 — a regulator-set clock, but on deletion operations rather than on the lookup product
**Buyer:** CEO / Head of Data
