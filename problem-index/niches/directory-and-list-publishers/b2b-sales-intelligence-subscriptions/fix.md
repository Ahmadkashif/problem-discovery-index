**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor is fighting to be the database a revenue team works from — but sales development teams buy the contact that connects this week, while marketing and revenue operations buy the complete, correctly sized universe of accounts they target and carve into territories, and those are two contests with different winners. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
At renewal the vendor brings database growth figures and a usage report; the buyer brings screenshots of wrong numbers and a competitor's lower quote. Neither side has a measured figure for the buyer's own market, so the outcome turns on negotiation and memory.

## Why It's Still Broken
Reps rarely record why a contact failed, the dialer and CRM do not tie dispositions to the data source, and nobody owns the comparison until the contract is due.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Tag every enriched record with its source and date, require a failure reason on bad contacts, and produce a quarterly one-page report of reachability and dead-account rates per vendor and segment — a habit and a report, not a platform.

## Who Feels the Pain
Revenue operations leaders defending the spend, SDR managers missing activity targets, and vendors' customer success teams losing accounts they cannot diagnose.

## Impact If Fixed
Renewals move from anecdote to evidence within two quarters, and vendors get specific complaints they can fix.
