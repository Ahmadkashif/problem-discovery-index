# B2B Sales Intelligence Subscriptions

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/directory-and-list-publishers|Directory & List Publishers]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor is fighting to be the database a revenue team works from — but sales development teams buy the contact that connects this week, while marketing and revenue operations buy the complete, correctly sized universe of accounts they target and carve into territories, and those are two contests with different winners. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$4B US spend on B2B contact and company data subscriptions (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~50% of the ~$8B US buyer spend on directories, lists and compiled business data, including directory advertising (estimate; see overview)
**Digital Adoption:** High — sold as software seats with CRM integrations, export credits and APIs
**Target Buyer:** VP of Sales Development; Revenue Operations leader; CMO
**Automation Potential:** High for compilation and matching; verification of people still needs calls and outcome feedback

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is where the money in compiled business data now sits: subscription platforms from Dun & Bradstreet (Hoovers), ZoomInfo, Data Axle, Lusha, Cognism, Apollo and others, sold per seat to revenue teams and renewed every year. The renewal is the fight, and it is decided on two different tests. Sales development managers judge the platform by whether their reps' dials connect to the named person and their emails land; contact records decay at an estimated ~22–30% a year in vendor estimates, so the test is rerun every quarter. Marketing and revenue operations judge it by whether the vendor's universe of accounts matches their market — no dead or duplicate firms, employee bands that put companies in the right segment, a count they can plan territories on. The two buyers sit in the same company, often sign the same contract, and leave for different competitors.

## Current Tools & Gaps
The platforms compile from filings, web crawls, carrier listings and contributory networks, verify with calls and email checks, and integrate with Salesforce and HubSpot. Email verification services and dialer analytics sit around them. The gap is measurement: the customer cannot see reachability and universe correctness separately on their own territory, so renewals are argued on rep anecdotes and headline database sizes. Account structure inside the customer's CRM is a separate problem covered under [[niches/crm-platforms/account-data-hygiene/profile|CRM Platforms: Account Data Hygiene]].
