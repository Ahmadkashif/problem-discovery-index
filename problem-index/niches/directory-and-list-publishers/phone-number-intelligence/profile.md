# Phone Number Intelligence & Reassigned-Number Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/directory-and-list-publishers|Directory & List Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Suppliers that tell compilers and their customers whether a number is a landline, mobile or VoIP line, whether it is active, and whether it has been reassigned since a date — the checks behind phone-verified records and TCPA risk control. Carrier-derived lookup services and the FCC's Reassigned Numbers Database (recalled) are the main sources.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-50 data and product analysts (estimate)
**Output:** Line type, activity, carrier and reassignment status per number; risk scores
**Proprietary data:** Carrier-derived porting, activity and reassignment signals
**Clock:** TCPA exposure and reassignment checks before calling; no deadline on the supplier's own deliverable
**Buyer:** Head of Product / VP Data
