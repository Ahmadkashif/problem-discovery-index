# The Same Restaurant Under Three Spellings

**Niche:** [[niches/directory-and-list-publishers/in-language-community-directories/profile|In-Language Community Directories]]
**Industry:** [[industries/directory-and-list-publishers|Directory & List Publishers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** One restaurant appears under its native-script name, a transliteration and an English name, in different places, with different phone numbers.
**Tags:** #word-embeddings #k-nearest-neighbors #evaluation-metrics #quick-win #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to be the in-language directory a community actually opens — complete coverage of community-owned businesses, with names, services and hours in the community's own language — and whoever has the most complete in-language listing takes the advertisers.

## The Problem
Readers searching the directory app, the map and a review site find the same business three ways and cannot tell which is current. The publisher's own listings duplicate as well when advertisers send copy in different scripts from year to year.

## Why It's Still Broken
Matching tools compare strings in one script; transliteration is inconsistent, and nobody owns the link between the community listing and mainstream records.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Cross-script matching that compares names phonetically and by phone, address and owner, merges duplicates in the publisher's file, and records the mainstream listing identifiers each entry corresponds to, so changes can be checked against them.

## Who Feels the Pain
Readers who call the wrong number, business owners who lose customers, and publishers whose app looks unreliable.

## Impact If Fixed
One clean record per business across scripts, and a directory readers trust.
