# Bilingual Listing Capture From Community Sources

**Niche:** [[niches/directory-and-list-publishers/in-language-community-directories/profile|In-Language Community Directories]]
**Industry:** [[industries/directory-and-list-publishers|Directory & List Publishers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A community directory publisher knows which businesses matter to its readers but has no team to find, verify and keep them current in two languages.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #word-embeddings #evaluation-metrics #automation #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to be the in-language directory a community actually opens — complete coverage of community-owned businesses, with names, services and hours in the community's own language — and whoever has the most complete in-language listing takes the advertisers.

## The Problem
A two- or three-person publisher compiles its book from advertisers, community newspaper ads, flyers, word of mouth and members' messaging groups. New businesses open without English-language web presence; others close quietly. The book goes to press with gaps and stale entries, and the app is only as current as the last time someone had a free afternoon.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The publishers are tiny and fragmented, and the data sources — in-language flyers, newspaper ads, messaging posts, menu photos — are exactly what mainstream compilation pipelines ignore.

## What to Build
A capture tool that reads photos of flyers and ads and in-language posts, extracts business name in both scripts, services, languages served, phone and address, matches against the publisher's existing listings and public records across transliterations, and proposes new and changed entries for one-tap confirmation. It also exports verified listings in a form mainstream listing networks accept.

## Target Customer
Owner-publishers of in-language business directories and the community newspapers that run them.

## Impact If Built
A tiny team keeps a complete, current bilingual directory, and advertisers gain visibility in their own community and beyond it.
