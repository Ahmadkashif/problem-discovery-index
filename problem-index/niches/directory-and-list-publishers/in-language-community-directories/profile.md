# In-Language Community Directories

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/directory-and-list-publishers|Directory & List Publishers]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to be the in-language directory a community actually opens — complete coverage of community-owned businesses, with names, services and hours in the community's own language — and whoever has the most complete in-language listing takes the advertisers.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$80M US advertising in Korean, Chinese, Vietnamese, Spanish-language and other community business directories, print and app (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1% of the ~$8B US buyer spend on directories, lists and compiled business data, including directory advertising (estimate; see overview)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — small publishers compile by personal contact, community newspapers and phone, and still print annual books alongside simple apps and messaging-channel listings
**Target Buyer:** Owner-publisher of the directory; advertisers are community-owned restaurants, clinics, law offices, realtors and trades
**Automation Potential:** Medium — capture, transliteration and de-duplication automate; community trust and ad sales stay personal

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Immigrant communities in large metro areas sustain their own business directories, often published by a community newspaper or a family business, printed once a year and distributed at grocery stores, churches and temples, now paired with an app or a channel on the messaging platform the community uses. Their readers search in their own language, for businesses that serve in it: a Korean-speaking dentist, a Vietnamese-speaking tax preparer, a Mandarin-speaking immigration lawyer. Mainstream compilers and map platforms list many of these businesses under transliterated or English names, miss home-based and word-of-mouth businesses entirely, and do not record which languages a business serves in. The tools of the industry — compilation pipelines, listings management, AI listing assistants — are built for English.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Desktop publishing for the print book, spreadsheets of advertisers, simple directory apps, and the publisher's own relationships. Gaps: matching the same business across scripts and spellings; capturing services offered in each language; keeping hours and phones current without a call team; and connecting the in-language listing to mainstream platforms so advertisers get both.
