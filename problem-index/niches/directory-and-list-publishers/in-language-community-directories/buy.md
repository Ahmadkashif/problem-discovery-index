# Listings Platforms Localised for a Community

**Niche:** [[niches/directory-and-list-publishers/in-language-community-directories/profile|In-Language Community Directories]]
**Industry:** [[industries/directory-and-list-publishers|Directory & List Publishers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Listings management platforms push a business's details to dozens of English-language publishers; none treats a community directory, its script or its language-served field as first-class.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #word-embeddings #data-integration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to be the in-language directory a community actually opens — complete coverage of community-owned businesses, with names, services and hours in the community's own language — and whoever has the most complete in-language listing takes the advertisers.

## The Problem
Community-owned businesses want to be found by their own community and by everyone else, but their in-language name, services and languages served are lost when their details are syndicated through English-only tools.

## What Already Exists
Listings management platforms (Yext and others) and Google Business Profile manage a business's name, hours and categories across search, maps and directories.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: names and descriptions held in both scripts with transliteration variants for matching; a "languages served" attribute; community-specific categories (a herbal pharmacy, a hagwon tutoring centre); syndication to the community directory's print book, app and messaging channel; and pricing a small publisher can resell to its advertisers.

## Target Customer
Community directory publishers wanting a digital product to sell alongside print.

## Impact If Solved
The publisher gains a recurring digital product, and community businesses appear correctly in both worlds.
