# The Entry Carried Forward Unverified for Five Editions

**Niche:** [[niches/directory-and-list-publishers/trade-and-professional-directories/profile|Trade, Professional & Membership Directories]]
**Industry:** [[industries/directory-and-list-publishers|Directory & List Publishers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** An entry nobody returned is reprinted as if confirmed, and after a few editions it describes an organisation that no longer exists in that form.
**Tags:** #evaluation-metrics #descriptive-statistics #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get the listed organisations to confirm their own entries every edition cycle at near-zero cost, and whoever publishes the highest share of entries verified this edition keeps the library and industry subscribers.

## The Problem
When a listee does not respond, the publisher keeps the old entry rather than drop a paying or prominent organisation. Over several editions the entry drifts out of date while still appearing as current to the librarian or buyer who relies on it.

## Why It's Still Broken
Most directory databases record when an entry was edited, not when it was confirmed, so carried-forward entries are indistinguishable from verified ones, internally and to customers.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Store a last-confirmed date and a confirmation source per entry, report the share of entries confirmed this edition, show the date to subscribers, and require an editor's check on any entry unconfirmed for two editions.

## Who Feels the Pain
Librarians and procurement staff who rely on the directory, and publishers whose quality is invisible because it is unmeasured.

## Impact If Fixed
Stale entries become visible and fixable, and the publisher gains an honest quality number to sell on.
