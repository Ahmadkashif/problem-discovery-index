# Public-Records Feeds Pointed at Trigger Lists

**Niche:** [[niches/directory-and-list-publishers/trigger-and-new-record-lists/profile|Trigger & New-Record Lists]]
**Industry:** [[industries/directory-and-list-publishers|Directory & List Publishers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Property and business-records aggregators sell clean public-records feeds; turning them into a trigger list needs event inference they do not do.
**Tags:** #k-nearest-neighbors #logistic-regression #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver the new record first — the business that filed last week, the household that moved last weekend — with the fewest false triggers, and whoever delivers the freshest trigger at a measured false-trigger rate takes the account.

## The Problem
A compiler licenses deed and business-filing feeds and gets every recorded document, of which only some are the events its buyers want.

## What Already Exists
Property-data aggregators that normalise county recordings, state registry bulk files and APIs, and licence-board exports.

## The Customization Gap
The trigger product needs: event classification (an arm's-length sale versus a refinance, quitclaim or trust transfer; an operating business versus a holding entity); de-duplication when the same event arrives from several sources; mover inference from lawful sources only, never NCOALink, whose licence forbids building new-mover lists; household and business matching to the compiler's existing file to suppress people already known; and per-source latency tracking.

## Target Customer
Compilers building trigger products on licensed public-records feeds.

## Impact If Solved
Clean feeds become sellable events without hand-tuned rules that nobody can audit.
