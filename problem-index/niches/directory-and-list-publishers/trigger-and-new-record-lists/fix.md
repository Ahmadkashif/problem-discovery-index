# The New Business That Is a Registered-Agent Filing

**Niche:** [[niches/directory-and-list-publishers/trigger-and-new-record-lists/profile|Trigger & New-Record Lists]]
**Industry:** [[industries/directory-and-list-publishers|Directory & List Publishers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A weekly new-business list is padded with LLCs whose only address is a registered agent's office and whose only purpose is to hold an asset.
**Tags:** #logistic-regression #evaluation-metrics #feature-engineering #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver the new record first — the business that filed last week, the household that moved last weekend — with the fewest false triggers, and whoever delivers the freshest trigger at a measured false-trigger rate takes the account.

## The Problem
State registries record every new entity, including holding companies, single-property LLCs and out-of-state registrations of existing firms. Many list the registered agent's address. Sold as new businesses, they waste buyers' outreach and erode trust in the list.

## Why It's Still Broken
Filters key on obvious strings and miss the patterns an experienced compiler recognises: known agent addresses, naming conventions of real-estate holding entities, foreign qualifications of existing firms.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Maintain a list of registered-agent addresses, flag foreign qualifications and entity-name patterns, match new filings to existing businesses, and route uncertain cases to a "likely non-operating" segment rather than the main file.

## Who Feels the Pain
Sales teams at payroll, insurance, banking and merchant-services firms working new-business leads.

## Impact If Fixed
The weekly list shrinks to the businesses that can actually buy, and buyers renew.
