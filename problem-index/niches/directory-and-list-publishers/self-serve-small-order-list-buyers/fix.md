# The Bad List Discovered After the Postage Is Spent

**Niche:** [[niches/directory-and-list-publishers/self-serve-small-order-list-buyers/profile|Self-Serve Small-Order List Buyers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/directory-and-list-publishers|Directory & List Publishers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** For a small mailer the list is the cheap part; the postage and printing wasted on bad names is what hurts, and it is spent before anyone knows.
**Tags:** #evaluation-metrics #descriptive-statistics #automation #quick-win #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to hand a small buyer a few thousand names they can trust with no contract and no minimum — a count that matches what is delivered and a deliverability guarantee that actually pays out — and whoever makes the small order safe takes the buyer.

## The Problem
A small business buys a list, prints and mails, and learns from returns weeks later that a chunk of it was undeliverable or off-target. The refund covers the names, not the printing and postage.

## Why It's Still Broken
Small buyers do not know to run address hygiene before mailing, and the compiler delivers the file without a pre-mailing check unless asked and paid.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Run CASS and NCOA hygiene and a deliverability score on every delivered file by default, show the buyer a plain pre-mailing report of what will likely fail, and remove those records before the buyer prints.

## Who Feels the Pain
Small-business owners and local agencies mailing on thin budgets, and the compilers who lose them after one bad order.

## Impact If Fixed
Wasted postage drops on the orders that can least afford it, and first-time buyers come back.
