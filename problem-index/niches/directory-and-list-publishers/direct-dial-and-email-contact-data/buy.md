# Email Verification Extended to Phones and Job Changes

**Niche:** [[niches/directory-and-list-publishers/direct-dial-and-email-contact-data/profile|Direct-Dial & Email Contact Data]]
**Industry:** [[industries/directory-and-list-publishers|Directory & List Publishers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Email verification services can tell whether a mailbox accepts mail; they cannot tell whether the person still works there or whether a direct dial reaches them.
**Tags:** #logistic-regression #evaluation-metrics #feature-engineering #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to sell contacts that reach the named person this week — a direct dial that connects, an email that lands — and whoever shows the higher measured connect rate on the customer's own territory wins the renewal.

## The Problem
An email that is technically deliverable may belong to someone who left — many companies keep mailboxes open or route them to a catch-all. Phones have no equivalent of an SMTP check at all.

## What Already Exists
Email verification services (NeverBounce, ZeroBounce, Kickbox) test syntax, domain, mailbox and catch-all status at scale; phone intelligence services return line type, carrier and disconnection status.

## The Customization Gap
A contact vendor needs: employment-currency checks that combine bounce behaviour, company website staff pages, signature sightings and job-change signals; phone checks that distinguish a direct line from a switchboard and a reassigned number; catch-all domains scored by the company's observed behaviour rather than flagged as unknown; and every check written back as a dated field-level assertion the vendor's ledger can use.

## Target Customer
Data operations leads at contact data vendors and the larger compilers' contact divisions.

## Impact If Solved
Deliverability becomes reachability, and "verified" begins to mean the person can actually be reached.
