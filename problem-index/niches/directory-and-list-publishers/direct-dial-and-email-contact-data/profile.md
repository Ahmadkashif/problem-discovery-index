# Direct-Dial & Email Contact Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/directory-and-list-publishers|Directory & List Publishers]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to sell contacts that reach the named person this week — a direct dial that connects, an email that lands — and whoever shows the higher measured connect rate on the customer's own territory wins the renewal.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.5B US spend on person-level B2B contact data (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~31% of the ~$8B US buyer spend on directories, lists and compiled business data, including directory advertising (estimate; see overview)
**Digital Adoption:** High — browser extensions, CRM enrichment and dialer integrations
**Target Buyer:** VP of Sales Development; Head of Sales Operations
**Automation Potential:** High for email checks; medium for phones, which need calls or outcome feedback to verify

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The unit sold is a person at a company: name, title, work email, direct dial, mobile. People change jobs, titles and numbers far faster than companies change addresses, so a contact file is a perishable good. Lusha, Cognism, ZoomInfo, Apollo and Seamless.AI compete here with different verification methods — contributory networks, phone-verified "premium" records, real-time email checks — but the customer's test is the same: when my reps dial and email this list, how often do they reach the person? That test is objective, rerun constantly and visible in the customer's dialer.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Email verification (SMTP checks, catch-all detection), reassigned-number and line-type checks, contributory signature parsing and periodic re-verification calls. The gaps: phone reachability is rarely predicted per contact before the dial; customers' dialer outcomes — the best label in the business — almost never flow back to the vendor in structured form; and credits for bad contacts are paid without the correction propagating to every other customer holding the same record.
