# The Bounce Credit That Teaches Nothing

**Niche:** [[niches/directory-and-list-publishers/direct-dial-and-email-contact-data/profile|Direct-Dial & Email Contact Data]]
**Industry:** [[industries/directory-and-list-publishers|Directory & List Publishers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A customer reports bad contacts, the vendor refunds export credits, and the same bad record is sold to the next customer.
**Tags:** #evaluation-metrics #feature-engineering #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to sell contacts that reach the named person this week — a direct dial that connects, an email that lands — and whoever shows the higher measured connect rate on the customer's own territory wins the renewal.

## The Problem
Customers file bad-data reports as screenshots, spreadsheets or support tickets. Support refunds credits to keep the account. The correction, if it is made at all, is made in a separate queue weeks later and rarely reaches every copy of the record already delivered.

## Why It's Still Broken
Credits are owned by support and finance, corrections by data operations; the ticket format carries no record identifier, and nobody measures how many credited records are sold again.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A one-click "wrong contact" control in the extension and CRM integration that captures the record identifier, the failure type and the date, refunds automatically within policy, and drops the report straight into the verification queue as a labelled outcome.

## Who Feels the Pain
Reps who burn dials on the same dead contacts, support teams fielding repeat complaints, and data teams starved of labels.

## Impact If Fixed
Every complaint becomes a training label and a correction, and repeat sales of known-bad records stop.
