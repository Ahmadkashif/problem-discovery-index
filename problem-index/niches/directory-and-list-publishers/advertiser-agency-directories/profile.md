# Advertiser & Agency Intelligence Directories

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/directory-and-list-publishers|Directory & List Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Directories of brand marketers and their agencies — who runs marketing at each advertiser, which agency holds which account, when reviews and agency changes happen — sold by subscription to media sellers, agencies and martech vendors; Winmo is representative and the print ancestor is the Standard Directory of Advertisers, the "Red Books" (recalled). Scored consistently with [[niches/media-rep-firms/ad-spend-occurrence-intelligence/profile|🔍 Ad Spend & Occurrence Intelligence]] (45): the same absent clock, a narrower market, and a contact directory rather than a spend estimate.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-80 researchers tracking marketers, agencies and account moves (estimate)
**Output:** Advertiser and agency profiles, decision-maker contacts, agency-of-record change alerts, spend estimates
**Proprietary data:** Researched histories of agency relationships and marketing leadership changes
**Clock:** None external; planning seasons and agency reviews set by advertisers
**Buyer:** VP of Research / Chief Product Officer
