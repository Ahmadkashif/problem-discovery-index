# Directory & Data Marketing Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/directory-and-list-publishers|Directory & List Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Trade bodies that set list-industry practice and represent directory and data publishers: the ANA's data and marketing division (which absorbed the Data & Marketing Association, recalled) and its preference service, information-industry associations such as SIIA, and directory-publisher groups. They publish ethics codes and advocacy rather than research products.

## Insight Function
**Size:** <10 research staff
**Output:** Codes of practice, preference services, advocacy and event content
**Proprietary data:** Member surveys
**Clock:** Legislative sessions
**Buyer:** Executive director
