# Boutique Studio Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/fitness-wellness-software|Fitness & Wellness Software]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in studio software is fighting to identify a member who is going to cancel while they are still recoverable and put the studio in front of them — and whoever intervenes earliest and most effectively takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$920M US boutique fitness and studio management software
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~37% of fitness software revenue
**Digital Adoption:** High — booking, billing and check-in are fully systematised
**Target Buyer:** Studio owners and small multi-location operators; product leads at Mindbody, Glofox, WellnessLiving and their competitors
**Automation Potential:** Very High — attendance is a near-ideal behavioural signal and is used for a roster

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A boutique studio's economics are a subscription business's economics with an unusually clean leading indicator. Acquisition is expensive, margins are thin, and a member who lapses in month three has not repaid the cost of acquiring them. Cancellation is preceded, almost without exception, by attendance decay — a member who came three times a week starts coming twice, then once, then not for a fortnight, and then calls to cancel. The interval between the decay becoming visible and the cancellation is weeks, which is ample time to intervene, and the platform records every check-in for every member across tens of thousands of studios. What it does with that record is print a class roster and continue billing the card until the member calls. The gap between the predictive quality of the signal and the use made of it is the defining feature of the niche.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Mindbody is the incumbent with the widest footprint and an attached consumer marketplace; Glofox, WellnessLiving, Zen Planner and PushPress compete across studio formats. All handle booking, membership billing, check-in and class management competently, and payments is the principal revenue model for most of them. Retention analytics, where offered, is a dashboard reporting churn that has already happened. Nothing scores individual members on risk, nothing prescribes an intervention, and nothing measures whether an intervention worked. The consumer marketplaces complicate the picture further, since a member acquired through a marketplace behaves differently and studios generally cannot tell those cohorts apart in their own retention numbers.
