# Family Billing and Enrolment From Childcare Software

**Niche:** [[niches/fitness-wellness-software/martial-arts-youth-programs/profile|Martial Arts & Youth Programmes]]
**Industry:** [[industries/fitness-wellness-software|Fitness & Wellness Software]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Childcare and school management software solved family accounts, sibling discounts, seasonal enrolment and parent communication years ago, and martial arts schools run them on fitness software built for individual gym memberships.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #descriptive-statistics #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #automation #compliance #worker-facing #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor selling to martial arts and youth programmes is fighting to let a parent see progression, manage enrolment and pay without telephoning the school — and whoever makes the parent's side self-serve takes the account.

## The Problem
A family has three children in the programme at different levels, one of whom also does the competition team. The school offers a sibling discount and a family cap. The fitness software it uses models memberships as belonging to individuals, so the front desk maintains the family arrangement manually, applies the discount by hand each month, and produces an invoice the parent finds confusing. Enrolment for the autumn term is a paper form. Adding a summer camp means another transaction. Every one of these is a solved problem in childcare software.

## What Already Exists
Childcare and school management platforms handle family accounts with multiple children, sibling and family-cap discounting, term and session enrolment, waitlists, camp and programme registration, parent portals and messaging, authorised pickup lists and emergency contacts, all as standard. Payment plans and subsidy handling are mature. Parent communication tooling is well developed. The entire model matches this format far better than gym membership software does.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to the activity school's particular structure. It requires: (1) the family as the account and the student as the participant, which is the single structural change that fixes most of the billing friction; (2) programme and level as an attribute of the student's enrolment, so a child moving up a level is a change to an enrolment rather than a new membership — which is how it works in reality and not in the software; (3) events as first-class objects, since gradings, tournaments, recitals and camps are both revenue and the emotional centre of the parent relationship, and they are currently handled as ad hoc transactions; (4) participation consent, medical information and emergency contacts handled properly, since these are children and the fitness platforms' membership records were never designed for minors; and (5) parent communication scoped per programme and per level rather than a single broadcast list, which is what makes a message read rather than ignored.

## Target Customer
Martial arts, dance, gymnastics and youth activity schools, and the vendors serving them who built on a gym membership model.

## Impact If Solved
Family accounts and enrolment-based billing remove the largest source of administrative friction and parent confusion in these businesses, and the model is fully developed one category over. The minors-specific handling is not optional and is another respect in which generic fitness software is a poor fit for a business whose participants are children.
