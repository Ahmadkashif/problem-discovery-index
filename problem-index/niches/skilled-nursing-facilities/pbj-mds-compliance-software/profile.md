# PBJ & MDS Compliance Software Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/skilled-nursing-facilities|Skilled Nursing Facilities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Specialist compliance vendors such as Simple (SimplePBJ and MDS and quality-measure analytics) and the PBJ modules of workforce vendors such as SmartLinx validate staffing and assessment submissions before they go to CMS. They preview quality measures and star effects and publish regulatory explainers. Facilities pay a per-building subscription.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 analysts, clinical content specialists and data staff per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Pre-submission validation, QM and staffing-star previews, regulatory content
**Proprietary data:** PBJ and MDS submissions from thousands of client facilities, before CMS sees them
**Clock:** The 45-day PBJ deadline each quarter and MDS transmission windows
**Buyer:** VP of Product / Chief Clinical Officer
