# Per-Diem Staffing Marketplace Data Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/skilled-nursing-facilities|Skilled Nursing Facilities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Shift marketplaces heavily used by nursing homes, such as ShiftKey, IntelyCare, Clipboard Health and CareRev, run pricing, matching, fraud and reliability models over every posted and filled shift. ShiftKey was hiring a Staff AI Engineer for an agentic platform in October 2026 (Ashby). Occasional market reports aside, their analysis feeds the product and is not sold.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 data scientists and analysts per platform (estimate)
**Output:** Dynamic pricing, matching and reliability features inside the marketplace
**Proprietary data:** Shift postings, rates, fill times and cancellations across thousands of facilities
**Clock:** None set by a third party; markets clear in real time
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Chief Product Officer
