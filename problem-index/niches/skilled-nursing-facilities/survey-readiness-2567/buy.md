# Hospital Accreditation Tracer Tools Rebuilt for the LTCSP

**Niche:** [[niches/skilled-nursing-facilities/survey-readiness-2567/profile|State Survey Readiness & Plans of Correction]]
**Industry:** [[industries/skilled-nursing-facilities|Skilled Nursing Facilities]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Hospitals run continuous readiness for accreditation surveys with tracer software; nursing homes prepare in bursts with paper.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #compliance #workflow-orchestration #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to predict which F-tags this state's survey team will cite before the team walks in, and whoever's mock-survey findings best match the CMS-2567 that follows takes the account.

## The Problem
Readiness spikes when a survey is expected and decays afterwards. Audits done by unit managers on paper are not aggregated, so leadership cannot see whether readiness is improving.

## What Already Exists
Hospital accreditation-readiness and tracer tools let staff run mock tracers on tablets against accreditation standards, aggregate findings and assign corrective actions.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: (1) the Long-Term Care Survey Process's structure, meaning initial pool, resident sampling, facility tasks and the entrance-conference document list, instead of hospital accreditation chapters; (2) F-tags with scope and severity in place of accreditation elements; (3) observation tools for medication pass, dining and infection control used by unit managers in minutes a day; (4) linkage to the public 2567 history so internal findings are compared with what surveyors actually cite; (5) plan-of-correction tracking through revisit.

## Target Customer
Chains with a corporate compliance function and buildings on long survey cycles.

## Impact If Solved
Readiness becomes continuous and visible: unit managers spend ~10 minutes a day on observation audits (estimate) instead of a week of binder preparation when a survey is due.
