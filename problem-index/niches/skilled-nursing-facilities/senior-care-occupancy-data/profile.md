# Senior Care Market & Occupancy Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/skilled-nursing-facilities|Skilled Nursing Facilities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
NIC MAP Vision publishes a monthly skilled nursing report of occupancy, patient-day mix, revenue mix and revenue per patient day from a sample of multi-property operators in the NIC Skilled Nursing Data Initiative. It sells this alongside senior housing inventory and occupancy data to investors, lenders, REITs and operators who price acquisitions and loans.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 research and data staff on the skilled nursing and care side (estimate)
**Output:** Monthly skilled nursing reports, market-level subscriptions, investor briefings
**Proprietary data:** Monthly census and revenue data contributed by operators, published only in aggregate by design (the report states national trends reflect the contributing sample)
**Clock:** Self-set monthly release; buyers' deal and refinancing calendars
**Buyer:** Head of Research and Analytics
