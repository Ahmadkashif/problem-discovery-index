# Memory-Care Fall Detection Fitted to a Certified Nursing Home

**Niche:** [[niches/skilled-nursing-facilities/long-stay-dementia-residents/profile|Long-Stay Residents Living with Dementia]]
**Industry:** [[industries/skilled-nursing-facilities|Skilled Nursing Facilities]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** AI fall detection grew up in private-pay memory care; a Medicaid-certified nursing home needs it to fit survey rules, nurse-call, the MDS and a thinner budget.
**Tags:** #cnns #object-detection #transfer-learning #compliance #worker-facing #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to cut falls with major injury and antipsychotic use among long-stay residents with dementia, and whoever moves the MDS long-stay falls and antipsychotic measures that feed SNF VBP and Five-Star takes the account.

## The Problem
Unwitnessed falls are common among residents with dementia, who cannot say how they fell. Staff send residents to the ED to rule out injury.

## What Already Exists
SafelyYou's camera-based detection and remote review, Teton.ai's computer vision and Inspiren's sensors. SafelyYou's memory-care study found 8.3% of detected falls led to ED visits, against 24.5% in the comparison group.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: (1) consent and privacy processes that meet resident-rights rules and state camera laws in certified facilities; (2) integration with nurse-call and the EHR so falls create incident records and MDS entries automatically; (3) reporting on the long-stay falls-with-major-injury measure; (4) pricing that a Medicaid-heavy building can carry, possibly shared with MA plans that benefit from fewer ED visits; (5) evidence in SNF populations, which is thin.

## Target Customer
DONs and chain quality leaders; MA special needs plans for institutionalised members.

## Impact If Solved
In memory care, 8.3% of detected falls led to ED visits against 24.5% in the comparison group (SafelyYou study). If SNF results come anywhere near that, about two in three fall-related ED trips could be avoided (estimate), with better post-fall reviews on the measure that now carries VBP money.
