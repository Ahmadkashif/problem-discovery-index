# The Schizophrenia Diagnosis Added After Admission

**Niche:** [[niches/skilled-nursing-facilities/long-stay-dementia-residents/profile|Long-Stay Residents Living with Dementia]]
**Industry:** [[industries/skilled-nursing-facilities|Skilled Nursing Facilities]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A post-admission schizophrenia diagnosis removes a resident from the antipsychotic quality measure, and CMS now treats a pattern of them as a red flag.
**Tags:** #hypothesis-testing #descriptive-statistics #compliance #quick-win #evaluation-metrics
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to cut falls with major injury and antipsychotic use among long-stay residents with dementia, and whoever moves the MDS long-stay falls and antipsychotic measures that feed SNF VBP and Five-Star takes the account.

## The Problem
Residents with schizophrenia are excluded from the long-stay antipsychotic measure. A diagnosis added after admission, without psychiatric evaluation and history to support it, lowers the measure and conceals the drug use the measure exists to expose. In June 2026, 2,867 facilities were excluded from the risk-based survey on this criterion alone (CMS QSO-26-14-NH).

## Why It's Still Broken
Diagnoses are entered by many hands: attending physicians, psychiatric consultants, the MDS coordinator. Nobody reviews new psychiatric diagnoses against their support, and the incentive runs the wrong way.

## What a Fix Looks Like
An internal check that flags every new schizophrenia, bipolar or psychosis diagnosis after admission and asks for its supporting documentation. Pair it with a dashboard of antipsychotic starts, gradual dose reduction attempts and behaviour data per resident, reviewed monthly by the DON, the consultant pharmacist and the medical director.

## Who Feels the Pain
Residents given unnecessary antipsychotics; DONs exposed at survey; facilities losing risk-based survey eligibility.

## Impact If Fixed
Honest measures and fewer unnecessary antipsychotics. In June 2026, 2,867 facilities were excluded from the risk-based survey on this criterion (CMS QSO-26-14-NH), and each one gave up a survey with roughly half the onsite time.
