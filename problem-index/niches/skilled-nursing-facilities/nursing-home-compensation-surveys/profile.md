# Nursing Home Compensation Surveys

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/skilled-nursing-facilities|Skilled Nursing Facilities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Hospital & Healthcare Compensation Service publishes the annual Nursing Home Salary & Benefits Report with LeadingAge, supported by AHCA. The 48th edition covered 917 nursing homes and 111,600+ employees and reported March 2025 average base pay of $40.21 for RNs, $31.85 for LPNs and $20.16 for CNAs. It sells for $400.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 survey and analysis staff (estimate)
**Output:** Annual salary, benefits and turnover report
**Proprietary data:** Participant-submitted pay and benefits data
**Clock:** Annual survey cycle; buyers' budget seasons
**Buyer:** Owner / publisher
