# SNF EHR Platform Data & Analytics Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/skilled-nursing-facilities|Skilled Nursing Facilities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The data, analytics and applied AI groups at PointClickCare and MatrixCare build PDPM tools, quality-measure dashboards, benchmarking, care-coordination feeds sold to hospitals and health plans, and new AI features. PointClickCare reports 30,000+ provider organisations and describes its data as North America's largest long-term and post-acute dataset (vendor-reported). It was hiring a Senior Applied Researcher in AI/ML and Principal ML System Engineers in October 2026 (Lever).

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 data scientists, analysts, applied researchers and data product staff (estimate)
**Output:** In-product analytics and AI features; hospital and payer data products; benchmarking
**Proprietary data:** Clinical, MDS and operational records across a majority of US skilled nursing facilities (vendor claim)
**Clock:** MDS item-set and payment-rule changes effective each 1 October, which the vendor must ship; facilities' MDS and PBJ deadlines are client clocks
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Product
