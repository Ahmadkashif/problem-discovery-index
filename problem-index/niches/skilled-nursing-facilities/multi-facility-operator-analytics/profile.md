# Multi-Facility Operator Clinical & Reimbursement Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/skilled-nursing-facilities|Skilled Nursing Facilities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The large operators, such as The Ensign Group, PACS Group and Life Care Centers of America, run corporate clinical reimbursement, quality and acquisition analytics across hundreds of buildings. They track case-mix and star ratings by building, prepare for survey, underwrite turnaround acquisitions and manage MA contracts.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 clinical reimbursement, quality, finance and data staff per large operator (estimate)
**Output:** Building performance reviews, acquisition underwriting, star and VBP management, managed-care contracting analysis
**Proprietary data:** Clinical, MDS, staffing and financial records across every owned or leased building
**Clock:** CMS quality and payment cycles, survey windows, MA contract renewals
**Buyer:** No external buyer — internal cost centre (COO / Chief Clinical Officer)
