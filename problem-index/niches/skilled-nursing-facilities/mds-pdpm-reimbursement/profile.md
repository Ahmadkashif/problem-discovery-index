# MDS Assessment & PDPM Reimbursement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/skilled-nursing-facilities|Skilled Nursing Facilities]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn what the floor documents into MDS coding that captures each resident's full PDPM and Medicaid case-mix, and whoever captures the most case-mix that still survives Medicare and Medicaid MDS audits takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.5B a year in MDS coordinator staff time, clinical reimbursement consultants, MDS audit services and PDPM software (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1.1% of ~$219.9B facility spending (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — the MDS is completed in the EHR and transmitted electronically, but gathering the evidence for each item means reading notes, flowsheets and hospital records by hand
**Target Buyer:** Director of Clinical Reimbursement at a chain; Administrator and Director of Nursing at an independent
**Automation Potential:** High for evidence assembly, look-back checks and consistency flags; the MDS coordinator still signs and answers for each item

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The MDS coordinator, usually an RN or LPN, sets the facility's revenue more directly than anyone else in the building. PDPM pays Medicare Part A by five components built from MDS items. Since the Optional State Assessment ended on 30 September 2025, Virginia, Ohio, Mississippi and other states pay Medicaid on PDPM groupings too, so one coded item now moves two payers. The work runs on federal clocks: assessment reference dates in the first days of a stay, look-back windows per item, and transmission deadlines. It is audited from both directions. Medicare contractors review claims through Targeted Probe and Educate, and state contractors such as Myers and Stauffer review Medicaid case-mix, which they do in 35+ states. CMS's FY 2027 rule now requires MDS data for every resident receiving covered skilled care regardless of payer, and opened a Request for Information on PDPM case-mix upcoding.

## Current Tools & Gaps
PointClickCare and MatrixCare PDPM worksheets calculate groups from whatever is coded. Simple and similar tools flag item inconsistencies after the fact. Consultancies (Zimmet, Harmony Healthcare International, Proactive Medical Review) audit samples. The gaps: no tool links each coded item to its supporting evidence inside the look-back window before transmission; Section GG scores from nursing and therapy disagree with no reconciliation; and nobody measures over-coding as carefully as under-coding.
