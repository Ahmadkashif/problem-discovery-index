# Long-Term Care Liability Actuarial Benchmarking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/skilled-nursing-facilities|Skilled Nursing Facilities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Aon has published an annual actuarial analysis of long-term care general and professional liability with AHCA, estimating loss rates per occupied bed by state from thousands of closed claims. Specialty liability insurers and captive managers price nursing home coverage from similar data. Operators, insurers and associations use the results in renewals and tort-reform advocacy.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 dedicated actuaries on the long-term care study (estimate)
**Output:** Annual benchmark report; client-specific loss projections
**Proprietary data:** Pooled closed-claim data contributed by operators and insurers
**Clock:** Annual insurance renewals
**Buyer:** Practice leader
