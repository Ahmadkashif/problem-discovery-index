# Nursing Home Association Research Arms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/skilled-nursing-facilities|Skilled Nursing Facilities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The American Health Care Association (with its LTC Trend Tracker benchmarking service and quality awards) and LeadingAge (with its LTSS Center research partnership) model payment rules for members. They also write regulatory comment letters, survey members on workforce and finances, and publish the research used in advocacy, for example on the staffing rule and Medicaid rates.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-25 research, policy and data staff across the two associations (estimate)
**Output:** Rate impact analyses, comment letters, workforce and financial surveys, member benchmarking dashboards
**Proprietary data:** Member surveys and benchmarking built on public CMS data and member submissions
**Clock:** The annual SNF PPS proposed-rule comment period and federal and state legislative calendars
**Buyer:** VP of Research or Policy — member-funded
