# Nursing Home Survey Readiness & Regulatory Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/skilled-nursing-facilities|Skilled Nursing Facilities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Clinical compliance firms (Pathway Health, Health Dimensions Group, CMS Compliance Group and dozens of regional practices, many staffed by former state surveyors) run mock surveys and write plans of correction. They support informal dispute resolution, appeal civil money penalties and coach Special Focus Facilities toward graduation. Operators pay per mock survey, retainer or engagement, usually when a survey is due or has gone badly.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 nurse consultants, former surveyors and regulatory specialists per larger firm (estimate)
**Output:** Mock-survey reports, plans of correction, dispute and appeal packages, compliance programme reviews
**Proprietary data:** Mock-survey findings paired with the real CMS-2567 that followed, across many clients and states; the 2567s themselves are public
**Clock:** The 15-month survey requirement, unannounced arrival, plan-of-correction deadlines and revisits, with Five-Star ratings and risk-based survey eligibility at stake
**Buyer:** Practice leader / Managing Director
