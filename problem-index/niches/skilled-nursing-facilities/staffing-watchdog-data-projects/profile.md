# Nursing Home Staffing Watchdog Data Projects

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/skilled-nursing-facilities|Skilled Nursing Facilities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Long Term Care Community Coalition's NursingHome411 turns each quarter's public PBJ data into state and facility staffing alerts. Its Q4 2025 alert reported 3.76 total nurse hours per resident day and contract staff at 5.3% of hours. Newsroom tools such as ProPublica's Nursing Home Inspect (recalled) make inspection findings searchable. Families, journalists, advocates and legislators use them, and foundations and donors fund them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 staff on the data work (estimate)
**Output:** Quarterly staffing reports and alerts; searchable facility data
**Proprietary data:** None beyond cleaned public CMS files
**Clock:** CMS's quarterly PBJ releases
**Buyer:** Executive director — grant-funded
