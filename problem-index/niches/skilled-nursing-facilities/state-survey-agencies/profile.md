# State Survey Agencies & CMS Survey Oversight

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/skilled-nursing-facilities|Skilled Nursing Facilities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State survey agencies inspect every certified nursing home under contract to CMS. They investigate complaints and write the CMS-2567 deficiency statements that feed Five-Star. CMS says the federal survey budget has not risen since 2015 while complaint workloads grew over 20%. Its risk-based survey, launched 8 September 2026, shifts surveyor time away from the ~12% of facilities that qualify (QSO-26-14-NH).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Hundreds of surveyors per large state, who are production inspectors; the analytic staff behind Five-Star and survey policy is small and largely contracted (estimate)
**Output:** Survey findings, enforcement actions, public ratings inputs
**Proprietary data:** Survey and complaint records, published in part as 2567s
**Clock:** The 15-month survey requirement and complaint-investigation timelines
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — state and federal agencies
