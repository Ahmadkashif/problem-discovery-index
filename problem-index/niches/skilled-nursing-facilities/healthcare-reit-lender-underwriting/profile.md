# Healthcare REIT & HUD 232 Lender Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/skilled-nursing-facilities|Skilled Nursing Facilities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Healthcare REITs such as Omega Healthcare Investors, Sabra Health Care REIT, CareTrust and LTC Properties own nursing home real estate and lease it to operators. HUD Section 232 lenders and bridge lenders finance it. Their asset-management and underwriting teams track tenants' occupancy, payer mix, rent coverage, survey results and star ratings to price deals and catch failing operators early.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 underwriting and asset-management analysts per larger REIT or lender (estimate)
**Output:** Internal underwriting memos, tenant watch lists, investor disclosures; nothing sold
**Proprietary data:** Monthly tenant financial and census reports delivered under lease and loan covenants
**Clock:** Covenant reporting dates, HUD annual statements, and public REITs' quarterly earnings dates
**Buyer:** Chief Investment Officer — no external buyer for the analysis itself
