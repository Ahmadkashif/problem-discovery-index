# Hospital Utilisation Review Platforms Turned Around

**Niche:** [[niches/skilled-nursing-facilities/ma-authorization-denials/profile|Medicare Advantage Authorisation & Continued-Stay Reviews]]
**Industry:** [[industries/skilled-nursing-facilities|Skilled Nursing Facilities]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Hospitals have utilisation review software to defend inpatient days to payers; nursing homes defend SNF days with Word documents.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #workflow-orchestration #revenue-impact #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep every MA resident's authorised days matched to clinical need through weekly updates and appeals, and whoever wins the most continued-stay days that hold up on plan and QIO appeal takes the account.

## The Problem
The facility has no system that tracks each stay's authorisation status, the clinical evidence sent, and the plan's response.

## What Already Exists
Hospital utilisation review and case-management platforms track payer authorisations, apply criteria such as MCG and InterQual, manage denials and appeals, and support physician advisers.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: (1) SNF clinical evidence (therapy minutes and progress, Section GG function, skilled nursing needs) instead of inpatient severity; (2) post-acute criteria sets used by the plans' delegated managers; (3) weekly, not daily, review cadence; (4) NOMNC and Detailed Explanation of Non-Coverage timing for fast-track QIO appeals; (5) pricing and setup for a building without a utilisation review department.

## Target Customer
Chains' managed-care departments; hospital UR vendors seeking post-acute customers.

## Impact If Solved
SNF days are defended with the discipline hospitals use for inpatient days. Recovering ~2 covered days per denied stay (estimate), at denial rates near 12% among the largest MA insurers (OIG, June 2026), adds directly to managed-care revenue, which comes from 42% of admissions (CLA).
