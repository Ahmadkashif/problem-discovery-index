# Medicare Advantage Authorisation & Continued-Stay Reviews

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/skilled-nursing-facilities|Skilled Nursing Facilities]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep every MA resident's authorised days matched to clinical need through weekly updates and appeals, and whoever wins the most continued-stay days that hold up on plan and QIO appeal takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$900M a year in case-management and MDS staff time on MA authorisations, appeals work, and revenue-cycle services for managed-care stays (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.4% of facility spending (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium-Low — submissions go through each plan's portal or by fax, clinical updates are typed from notes, and tracking of authorised days lives in spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** Administrator and business office manager; VP of Revenue Cycle or Managed Care at a chain
**Automation Potential:** High for assembling and submitting weekly updates and tracking days; peer-to-peer conversations and appeal strategy stay human

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Traditional Medicare covers a qualifying SNF stay without prior authorisation. Medicare Advantage plans authorise the admission and then a few days at a time, asking for therapy and nursing updates weekly or more often. Managed care was 42% of SNF admissions in FY 2025 (CLA). OIG reported in June 2026 that the three largest MA insurers denied 12% of SNF requests, Humana 13.5%, with nearly all appealed denials overturned. The Senate PSI found UnitedHealthcare's post-acute denial rate rose from 10.9% in 2020 to 22.7% in 2022. This niche holds the Medicare Advantage post-acute authorisation field for the vault. It is the provider's side of the decision analysed for plans under [[niches/health-insurance-carriers/inpatient-post-acute-review/profile|🎯 Inpatient & Post-Acute Concurrent Review]] and [[niches/health-insurance-carriers/utilization-management/profile|🔵 Utilization Management & Prior Authorization]], and the delegated utilisation managers are profiled as [[niches/home-health-agencies/ma-plan-utilization-management/profile|🔍 Medicare Advantage Home Health Utilization Management]]. Hospital inpatient and status denials stay with [[niches/general-hospitals/clinical-denials-status-appeals/profile|🎯 Clinical Denials & Inpatient Status Appeals]]. Its clock is weekly and its craft is documentation and appeal, distinct from the hour-long admission decision.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Plan portals, fax, the EHR's therapy and nursing notes, spreadsheets of authorised days, and outsourced billing firms. New agents from ExaCare, Skypoint and Sunbound target pieces of it. Gaps: updates are rewritten by hand for each plan; nobody tracks denials and overturns by plan, reviewer and reason; appeals are dropped for lack of time.
