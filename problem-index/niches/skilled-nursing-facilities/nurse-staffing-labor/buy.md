# Hospital Workforce Platforms Sized for a Nursing Home

**Niche:** [[niches/skilled-nursing-facilities/nurse-staffing-labor/profile|Nurse Staffing & Labour Supply]]
**Industry:** [[industries/skilled-nursing-facilities|Skilled Nursing Facilities]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Hospitals run acuity-based staffing and internal float pools on enterprise workforce software; nursing homes need the same logic in a form a 100-bed building can run.
**Tags:** #optimization-fundamentals #time-series-forecasting #workflow-orchestration #automation #data-integration
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every competitor here is fighting to staff each shift at the hours residents need with the least premium labour. That is two contests with different winners: keeping the facility's own employees and float pool on the schedule, which workforce-management and scheduling vendors win, and supplying a credentialed clinician for a same-day hole, which marketplaces and agencies win. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Nursing homes staff to a fixed template by unit and shift and adjust by phone. Acuity changes with every admission, but the schedule does not.

## What Already Exists
Hospital workforce platforms such as UKG and other acuity-based staffing tools set target hours from patient classification, run internal float pools and enforce labour rules.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: (1) acuity from the MDS and PDPM nursing groups instead of hospital classification systems; (2) targets in PBJ terms (RN, LPN and aide hours per resident day, weekend coverage) that map directly to the staffing star; (3) state minimum-staffing rules where they exist; (4) a float pool shared across nearby buildings of one owner; (5) setup and support a building with no scheduling analyst can manage.

## Target Customer
Regional chains with several buildings within driving distance, able to share staff.

## Impact If Solved
Schedules follow acuity and the stars are protected by design, not by the coordinator's phone. A building that climbs from 2 to 3 staffing stars clears one of the bars for the risk-based survey, which 5,692 facilities failed on staffing in June 2026 (CMS QSO-26-14-NH).
