# The Bonus Text That Teaches Staff to Wait

**Niche:** [[niches/skilled-nursing-facilities/nurse-staffing-labor/profile|Nurse Staffing & Labour Supply]]
**Industry:** [[industries/skilled-nursing-facilities|Skilled Nursing Facilities]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Escalating shift bonuses sent by group text fill today's hole and teach staff that waiting pays.

**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every competitor here is fighting to staff each shift at the hours residents need with the least premium labour. That is two contests with different winners: keeping the facility's own employees and float pool on the schedule, which workforce-management and scheduling vendors win, and supplying a credentialed clinician for a same-day hole, which marketplaces and agencies win. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
When a shift is open, the coordinator texts the whole list with a bonus, then a bigger bonus an hour later. Staff learn that open shifts pay more if they hold out, and that scheduled shifts pay base rate. Some drop scheduled shifts and pick them up again as bonus shifts.

## Why It's Still Broken
Bonuses are set ad hoc by whoever is desperate that morning, and nobody records who accepted at what price. With no data, the policy cannot be tested, so it escalates.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Log every offer, price and acceptance. Set incentive rates by rule (shift, lead time, unit) rather than by mood, and test changes, for example a modest premium for early pickup against a large one at the last minute. Flag employees who drop and re-pick shifts.

## Who Feels the Pain
Coordinators and DONs; reliable staff who never see a bonus; administrators whose premium-pay line rises with no change in coverage.

## Impact If Fixed
Rule-based incentives could cut bonus spend by ~20–30% (estimate) while raising early pickup, and reliable employees are no longer paid less than people who game the system.
