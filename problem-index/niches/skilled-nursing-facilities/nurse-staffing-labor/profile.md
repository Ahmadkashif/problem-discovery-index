# Nurse Staffing & Labour Supply

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/skilled-nursing-facilities|Skilled Nursing Facilities]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every competitor here is fighting to staff each shift at the hours residents need with the least premium labour. That is two contests with different winners: keeping the facility's own employees and float pool on the schedule, which workforce-management and scheduling vendors win, and supplying a credentialed clinician for a same-day hole, which marketplaces and agencies win. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$7B a year in agency and per-diem labour, workforce-management software and scheduling staff time (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of ~$219.9B facility spending, inside a nursing payroll many times larger (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — timeclocks, payroll and scheduling software are digital, while call-offs, shift offers and agency bookings run on texts, phone calls and spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** VP of Operations or Chief Nursing Officer at a chain; Administrator and Director of Nursing at an independent
**Automation Potential:** Medium — forecasting, shift offers and credential checks automate well; retention and judgement about who can cover a unit do not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Nursing labour is the largest cost line and the most visible quality signal. Nursing assistants alone number 458,590 (BLS OEWS, 2023). Total staffing averaged 3.85 hours per resident day in July 2025 (KFF), and every hour is reported to CMS through PBJ, where it sets the staffing star and two SNF VBP measures. Agency labour peaked at 10.5% of nursing hours in Q3 2022 and fell to 5.3% by Q4 2025 (LTCCC), but each agency hour still costs far more: 80% more for nurse aides in 2021 (ASPE). The federal minimum-staffing rule was repealed effective February 2026, yet many states keep their own minimums (estimate) and stars still reward hours.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Scheduling and workforce management: SmartLinx, UKG, OnShift. Per-diem marketplaces: ShiftKey, IntelyCare, Clipboard Health, CareRev. Regional agencies for longer contracts. The gaps: no single view of cost per hour and hours per resident day across employees, float pool, marketplace and agency; call-offs not forecast; and retention managed by anecdote.
