# Workforce Management With PBJ-Aware Rules

**Niche:** [[niches/skilled-nursing-facilities/internal-scheduling-retention/profile|Internal Scheduling, Float Pools & Retention]]
**Industry:** [[industries/skilled-nursing-facilities|Skilled Nursing Facilities]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Enterprise scheduling tools build compliant schedules but do not know which shifts move a nursing home's staffing star.
**Tags:** #optimization-fundamentals #time-series-forecasting #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to fill the facility's schedule from its own employees and float pool before any outside labour, and whoever cuts agency hours furthest without letting reported hours per resident day fall below the staffing-star cut points takes the account.

## The Problem
A schedule can meet every labour rule and still produce weekend RN hours that pull the staffing rating down, because the rating is calculated from PBJ, adjusted for case-mix, with weekend staffing counted separately.

## What Already Exists
Workforce-management platforms such as UKG, SmartLinx and OnShift handle scheduling, timekeeping, overtime rules, self-scheduling and shift swaps.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: (1) live projection of each day's hours per resident day by RN, LPN and aide as the schedule is built, using the MDS census; (2) weekend coverage targets that mirror the Five-Star weekend staffing measure; (3) case-mix adjustment so targets rise with acuity; (4) turnover tracking in the CMS definition used for the VBP Nursing Staff Turnover measure; (5) float-pool scheduling across buildings of one owner.

## Target Customer
Chains already licensing a workforce platform that want it to protect stars, not just compliance.

## Impact If Solved
The schedule itself protects the staffing star. 5,692 facilities were excluded from the risk-based survey in June 2026 for a staffing rating below 3 stars (CMS QSO-26-14-NH), and the coordinator sees the effect of each change before the quarter closes.
