# Independent & Small-Chain Operators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/skilled-nursing-facilities|Skilled Nursing Facilities]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stand in for the regional nurse consultant a standalone facility cannot afford, and whoever flags the most problems before a surveyor, PBJ auditor or payment reviewer finds them takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$700M a year in outsourced clinical reimbursement, compliance and back-office services bought by independent and small-chain facilities (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.3% of facility spending (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium-Low — they run the same EHR as chains, but analytics, audits and benchmarking are done by an outside consultant a few days a month or not at all
**Target Buyer:** Owner-administrator, or the administrator and DON of a single-site non-profit, county-owned or family-owned home
**Automation Potential:** High for monitoring and audit preparation; the consultant's judgement is what they cannot afford full-time

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A chain's building has a regional nurse consultant, a reimbursement director, a compliance lead and an analyst behind it. A standalone facility, including many of the 20% that are non-profit and 7% government-owned (KFF), has an administrator who is also HR, compliance and IT, and one MDS coordinator who is a single point of failure. ASPE found that the cost of contract nurses relative to employed staff rose 43% in independent nursing homes after 2019, against 27% in chains. Independents feel every regulatory change (Medicaid PDPM transitions, the risk-based survey, MDS for all payers) with no one whose job is to read it. Tools priced and sold for chains rarely reach them.

## Current Tools & Gaps
The EHR's standard reports; state association services and group purchasing; part-time consultants for MDS, survey readiness and cost reports. Gaps: no continuous monitoring between consultant visits; no peer benchmark beyond Care Compare; knowledge leaves with each departing coordinator or DON.
