# A Virtual Regional Nurse Consultant

**Niche:** [[niches/skilled-nursing-facilities/independent-operators/profile|Independent & Small-Chain Operators]]
**Industry:** [[industries/skilled-nursing-facilities|Skilled Nursing Facilities]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A standalone facility gets the monitoring a chain's regional team provides only when its consultant visits, which is a few days a month at best.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #gradient-boosting #time-series-forecasting #evaluation-metrics #compliance #revenue-impact #ai-agent
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stand in for the regional nurse consultant a standalone facility cannot afford, and whoever flags the most problems before a surveyor, PBJ auditor or payment reviewer finds them takes the account.

## The Problem
Between visits nobody is watching the case-mix index slide, the weekend RN hours dip, the falls rise on one unit, or the antipsychotic measure creep up. The administrator finds out from the next Care Compare refresh or the next survey.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Analytics vendors sell to chains with analysts to read dashboards. Consultants sell hours. An independent needs findings and actions, not charts, and has never had a product shaped that way.

## What to Build
An agent that combines the facility's public data (PBJ, Care Compare, its 2567 history) with EHR exports it already owns (MDS, incidents, census). Each week it writes a one-page memo: what changed, what it likely means for stars, PDPM and survey risk, and three concrete actions, each tied to a regulation or measure. It escalates the few issues that need a human consultant, so the consultant's paid hours go to the highest-value problems.

## Target Customer
Owner-administrators and boards of single-site and 2–5 building operators, often via state associations or consultancies that resell it.

## Impact If Built
The independent sees problems ~1–2 months before the next Care Compare refresh or survey would show them (estimate). A 1% case-mix slide caught early is worth ~$18,000 a year in fee-for-service Part A revenue alone, on the ~$1.8M an average freestanding SNF receives (MedPAC; derived), and the consultant's paid days go where they pay back most.
