# Chain Shared Services Packaged for One Building

**Niche:** [[niches/skilled-nursing-facilities/independent-operators/profile|Independent & Small-Chain Operators]]
**Industry:** [[industries/skilled-nursing-facilities|Skilled Nursing Facilities]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Chains run billing, MDS review, PBJ and cost reports from a shared service centre; independents buy each piece from a different vendor or do without.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #compliance #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stand in for the regional nurse consultant a standalone facility cannot afford, and whoever flags the most problems before a surveyor, PBJ auditor or payment reviewer finds them takes the account.

## The Problem
A small operator contracts separately for billing, MDS audits, PBJ submission, Medicare cost reports and survey consulting. None of the vendors sees the whole picture, and the administrator coordinates them all.

## What Already Exists
Chain shared-service centres and healthcare business-process outsourcers run billing, MDS, PBJ, payroll and cost reporting at scale for large operators.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: (1) a single-building price and onboarding without a chain's IT team; (2) a combined monthly view across billing, MDS, PBJ and survey risk; (3) handling of the state's specific Medicaid PDPM variant and cost report; (4) continuity when the facility's own coordinator or DON leaves; (5) a named human lead, which independents value.

## Target Customer
Independent and small-chain administrators, and state associations offering member services.

## Impact If Solved
One partner replaces ~4–5 separate contracts for billing, MDS audit, PBJ, cost reports and survey consulting (estimate). That gives the administrator back ~1 day a week of vendor coordination (estimate), with one accountable partner.
