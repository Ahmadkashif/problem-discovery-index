# The Per-Diem Aide Who Arrives Without the Unit's Orientation

**Niche:** [[niches/skilled-nursing-facilities/per-diem-shift-marketplaces/profile|Per-Diem Shift Marketplaces & Agency Labour]]
**Industry:** [[industries/skilled-nursing-facilities|Skilled Nursing Facilities]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Every new per-diem clinician costs the charge nurse twenty minutes of orientation at the busiest moment of the shift, and some get none at all.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #worker-facing #compliance #quick-win #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to put a credentialed, reliable CNA or nurse on a same-day open shift, and whoever fills the most shifts inside two hours with the fewest no-shows takes the account.

## The Problem
The charge nurse starts the shift by orienting a stranger: who is a fall risk, who needs a two-person lift, where the crash cart is, how the kiosk works. If the shift is busy, the orientation is skipped. Falls and missed care that follow land on the facility's survey record.

## Why It's Still Broken
Orientation material is a binder at the nurses' station. Platforms treat orientation as the facility's job, and facilities treat per-diem staff as temporary. Nobody owns the gap.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A two-page, auto-generated shift brief for each per-diem assignment, sent to the clinician's phone when the shift is accepted. It covers unit layout, emergency procedures and a care card per assigned resident from the EHR, with a sign-off that is logged for surveyors.

## Who Feels the Pain
Charge nurses; per-diem aides set up to fail; residents cared for by someone who does not know them; administrators answering for the resulting citation.

## Impact If Fixed
Orientation moves out of the busiest minutes of the shift, saving the charge nurse ~20 minutes per new per-diem clinician (estimate), and the facility has a record that every outside clinician was briefed.
