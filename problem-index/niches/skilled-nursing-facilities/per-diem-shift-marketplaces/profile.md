# Per-Diem Shift Marketplaces & Agency Labour

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/skilled-nursing-facilities|Skilled Nursing Facilities]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to put a credentialed, reliable CNA or nurse on a same-day open shift, and whoever fills the most shifts inside two hours with the fewest no-shows takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$5B a year in agency contracts and per-diem marketplace shifts bought by nursing homes (estimate, from contract staff at ~5% of nursing hours and the ASPE cost premium)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2.3% of facility spending (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High on the supply side (marketplace apps, credential wallets); low on the facility side, where orientation and competency records are paper
**Target Buyer:** Director of Nursing and Administrator; VP of Operations for master agreements
**Automation Potential:** High for matching, credential verification and pricing; low for orienting a stranger to a unit in fifteen minutes

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is the outside supply of labour for holes the facility cannot fill itself. Nursing homes are a core market for per-diem apps such as ShiftKey, IntelyCare, Clipboard Health and CareRev, and for regional agencies. ASPE found 43% of nursing homes used contract nurses in 2021, against 19% in 2017. Contract share peaked at 10.5% of hours in Q3 2022 and was 5.3% in Q4 2025 (LTCCC), and CLA put contract labour at 5.2% in FY 2025 cost reports. Same-day fill rate and show-up reliability decide who gets the facility's next shift, and the competitors are platforms, not the operator's own staff. The staffing firm's side of this market, including credential tracking for placed clinicians, is analysed under [[niches/staffing-agencies/healthcare-travel-staffing/profile|🔵 Healthcare Travel & Per Diem Staffing]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Marketplace apps with credential checks and ratings; agency portals; master service agreements at chains. Gaps: reliability data is held by each platform and not comparable; facility orientation and competency checks are paper and repeated per shift; and contract hours often reach the PBJ file late or wrongly mapped.
