# Marketplaces With Facility-Side Orientation Built In

**Niche:** [[niches/skilled-nursing-facilities/per-diem-shift-marketplaces/profile|Per-Diem Shift Marketplaces & Agency Labour]]
**Industry:** [[industries/skilled-nursing-facilities|Skilled Nursing Facilities]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Per-diem apps verify licences and match shifts; none carries the facility's own unit orientation and competency record with the clinician.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #compliance #workflow-orchestration #worker-facing #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to put a credentialed, reliable CNA or nurse on a same-day open shift, and whoever fills the most shifts inside two hours with the fewest no-shows takes the account.

## The Problem
Surveyors expect staff to be competent for the residents they care for and oriented to the facility's emergency procedures. A per-diem aide arriving at 6:45 gets a hurried walk-through or none.

## What Already Exists
Marketplaces such as ShiftKey, IntelyCare and Clipboard Health verify licences and certifications, run background checks, handle pay and ratings, and publish shifts to local clinicians.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: (1) facility-specific micro-orientation (fire exits, code procedures, the unit's high-risk residents, lift equipment) delivered in the app before the shift; (2) a record of completed orientation and competencies per facility that surveyors can see; (3) resident-specific care cards for the assignment, pulled from the EHR at the right access level; (4) PBJ-ready hours data pushed to the facility automatically.

## Target Customer
Directors of nursing at buildings that use per-diem staff weekly, and marketplace product teams.

## Impact If Solved
Per-diem shifts start ~20 minutes sooner (estimate), with survey-ready proof of orientation for every outside clinician.
