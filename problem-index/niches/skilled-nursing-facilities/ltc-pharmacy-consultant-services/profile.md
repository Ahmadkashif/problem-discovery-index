# Long-Term Care Pharmacy Clinical Consulting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/skilled-nursing-facilities|Skilled Nursing Facilities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Institutional pharmacies (Omnicare, PharMerica and regional long-term care pharmacies) dispense to nursing homes and supply the consultant pharmacists who perform each resident's required monthly medication regimen review. They also run psychotropic and antibiotic stewardship programmes and send facility-level clinical reports. The service is usually bundled into the dispensing contract or billed per review.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 clinical programme and analytics staff at each national pharmacy (estimate), above a much larger field of consultant pharmacists who are production reviewers
**Output:** Medication regimen review recommendations, psychotropic and gradual-dose-reduction reports, stewardship dashboards for facilities
**Proprietary data:** Dispensing and review records for every resident served, across thousands of facilities
**Clock:** The monthly regimen review requirement and quarterly quality-measure cycles (recalled)
**Buyer:** VP of Clinical Services at the pharmacy
