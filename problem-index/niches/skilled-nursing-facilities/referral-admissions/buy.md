# Senior Living CRMs Adapted to the Hospital Case Manager

**Niche:** [[niches/skilled-nursing-facilities/referral-admissions/profile|Hospital Referral Intake & Admissions]]
**Industry:** [[industries/skilled-nursing-facilities|Skilled Nursing Facilities]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Senior living CRMs manage families touring for assisted living; a SNF's real customer is a hospital case manager placing a patient by this afternoon.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #feature-engineering #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to decide on a hospital referral within the hour with the payer authorisation, the cost of care and the risk of readmission already known, and whoever says yes fastest to the referrals that will be authorised, paid and not readmitted takes the account.

## The Problem
Hospital liaisons track relationships with case managers in notebooks, and leadership cannot see which hospitals, units and physicians send which referrals, at what acceptance and outcome rates.

## What Already Exists
Senior living CRMs such as Aline CRM and its Smart Referral CRM, plus general CRMs, manage leads, tours, move-ins and referral sources.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: (1) referral sources defined as hospital, unit, case manager and physician; (2) ingestion of referrals from CarePort, naviHealth and fax; (3) response-time tracking from referral to decision; (4) outcome data per referral source (length of stay, readmission, payer mix) fed back to liaisons; (5) MA plan and network status per hospital.

## Target Customer
Chains with hospital liaison teams; CRM vendors extending from assisted living into skilled nursing.

## Impact If Solved
Liaisons spend time on the hospitals and case managers whose referrals become good stays. Moving ~10% of liaison hours to the top referral sources (estimate) raises conversion where it pays.
