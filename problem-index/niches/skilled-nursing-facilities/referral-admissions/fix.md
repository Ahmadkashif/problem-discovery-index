# The Friday 4 p.m. Referral Lost to the Next Facility

**Niche:** [[niches/skilled-nursing-facilities/referral-admissions/profile|Hospital Referral Intake & Admissions]]
**Industry:** [[industries/skilled-nursing-facilities|Skilled Nursing Facilities]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Hospitals discharge into the evening and the weekend; most facilities' admissions offices work weekdays.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #automation #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to decide on a hospital referral within the hour with the payer authorisation, the cost of care and the risk of readmission already known, and whoever says yes fastest to the referrals that will be authorised, paid and not readmitted takes the account.

## The Problem
Referrals sent after hours sit in a portal or fax tray until Monday, or reach a weekend supervisor with no authority to accept. The case manager places the patient elsewhere.

## Why It's Still Broken
Admissions is staffed like an office, not like the hospital it serves. Weekend decisions need someone who can read the packet, check the payer and confirm a bed and staffing, and that person is off.

## What a Fix Looks Like
An after-hours intake service, part automated and part on-call: the packet is summarised and screened automatically, payer eligibility is checked, and a pre-approved decision rule lets the weekend supervisor accept routine referrals in minutes. Complex cases are escalated to an on-call clinical reviewer by phone.

## Who Feels the Pain
Administrators watching census slip; hospital case managers who stop calling; weekend supervisors put in an impossible position.

## Impact If Fixed
Converting two or three more evening and weekend referrals a month (estimate) adds ~25–35 short-stay admissions a year, against the ~150–180 an average SNF takes (estimate), which in short-stay terms is direct revenue.
