# MDS & PDPM Clinical Reimbursement Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/skilled-nursing-facilities|Skilled Nursing Facilities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Firms such as Zimmet Healthcare Services Group, Harmony Healthcare International, Proactive Medical Review and LW Consulting, plus many regional practices, audit facilities' MDS coding and run PDPM and Medicaid case-mix analytics across client buildings. They also defend Targeted Probe and Educate reviews and state case-mix audits, and place interim MDS coordinators when a building loses its own. Operators and their owners pay by the audit, the month or the engagement.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~5-30 analysts per larger firm who build case-mix and PDPM analytics across clients (estimate); the clinical reimbursement consultants auditing MDS case by case, and the interim coordinators they place, are production staff and are not counted
**Output:** MDS audit reports with item-level findings, case-mix and PDPM opportunity analyses, audit-defence packages
**Proprietary data:** Item-level audit findings and their payment effects across hundreds of client facilities, joined to the TPE and Medicaid review outcomes that followed
**Clock:** The consultancy's own hard clock is the response deadline on a TPE or additional documentation request it is defending; assessment reference dates, transmission deadlines and Medicaid case-mix picture dates are the client facility's clocks
**Buyer:** Practice leader / Managing Director
