# Medicare & Health Plan Consumer Research Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/health-insurance-carriers|Health Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms that survey health insurance shoppers and members and sell the findings to plans: Deft Research's syndicated Medicare and commercial studies and J.D. Power's health plan satisfaction studies are representative (named from recall). They track why seniors switch MA plans, which benefits drive choice, how members rate service, and how brokers and digital channels shape enrolment. Plans use the studies in benefit design before bids and in marketing before the Annual Enrollment Period. Consumer-experience benchmarking in another industry is logged as [[niches/wired-broadband-providers/isp-customer-experience-benchmarks/profile|🔍 ISP Customer Experience Benchmarks]] (41).

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 researchers and analysts per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Syndicated annual studies, custom research, satisfaction rankings
**Proprietary data:** Long-running question series and survey responses the firm collects itself; panels are often bought, the instrument and history are its own
**Clock:** Studies are timed to plans' calendars, the Annual Enrollment Period (15 October to 7 December, recalled) and the June bid cycle, which are the clients' dates rather than deadlines on the research itself
**Buyer:** Research Director / Head of Health Practice
