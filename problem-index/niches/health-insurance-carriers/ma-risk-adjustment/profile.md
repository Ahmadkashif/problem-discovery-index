# Medicare Advantage Risk Adjustment & RADV Defence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/health-insurance-carriers|Health Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to submit every diagnosis a member's chart supports, and none it doesn't, before CMS's final submission deadline, and whoever keeps the most submitted diagnoses standing through a RADV medical-record review takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$5B a year in chart retrieval, coding review, in-home assessments, suspecting analytics and audit defence (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~4% of carrier administrative spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — submissions and models are digital; the evidence is retrieved medical records read by certified coders
**Target Buyer:** VP of Risk Adjustment / Chief Financial Officer
**Automation Potential:** High for chart reading and suspecting; final code validation stays with certified coders

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Medicare pays an MA plan a monthly amount per member scaled by a risk score built from the diagnoses the plan submits. Medicare is 37% of carrier premium (NAIC 2025), so a small error in risk scores is a large error in revenue. For two decades the industry's contest was capture: finding every diagnosis. In May 2025 CMS said it would audit every eligible MA contract each year (~550 instead of ~60), sample 35 to 200 records per contract, and grow its coders from ~40 to ~2,000. Since then the contest has been the diagnosis that survives. A September 2025 court ruling against CMS's extrapolation rule is under appeal, but direct recoveries on sampled members continue. The change to the V28 model has also reshaped which conditions pay.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Chart retrieval and coding vendors (Datavant, Episource, Reveleer, Cotiviti, Optum), NLP coding platforms, in-home assessment programmes, and plan risk adjustment analytics teams. The gaps are review that adds codes far more readily than it deletes them, no per-diagnosis estimate of audit survival, and RADV defence assembled from scratch for each audit.
