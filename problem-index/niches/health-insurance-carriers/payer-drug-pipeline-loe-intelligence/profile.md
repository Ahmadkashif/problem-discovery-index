# Payer Drug Pipeline & Loss-of-Exclusivity Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/health-insurance-carriers|Health Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Subscription services that tell plan and PBM pharmacy teams which drugs are coming, when, at what likely price, and when brands will face generic or biosimilar competition. IPD Analytics is the clearest example: it reports 700+ subscribers, 220+ of them in its payer, provider and supply-chain segment (self-reported). Its clinical pharmacists, patent attorneys and analysts publish launch tracking, loss-of-exclusivity timing and patent litigation analysis. Plans use the output for pharmacy trend forecasts in rate filings and bids, formulary planning and P&T committee preparation. The pharma-facing pipeline databases are logged as [[niches/biotech-research-firms/pipeline-trial-intelligence-providers/profile|🔍 Drug Pipeline & Trial Intelligence Providers]] (49), and this pocket is scored consistently with them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 clinical pharmacists, attorneys and analysts per provider (estimate)
**Output:** Drug monographs, launch and loss-of-exclusivity date forecasts, litigation outlooks, budget-impact estimates
**Proprietary data:** A dated archive of its own launch, approval and generic-entry calls, which could be graded against what happened; inputs are public (FDA actions, patents, court dockets, trial registries)
**Clock:** FDA approval goal dates, patent rulings and settlement dates, and plans' rate filing and bid deadlines
**Buyer:** Head of Clinical Research / Chief Product Officer
