# Care Management Platforms Fitted to the Model of Care

**Niche:** [[niches/health-insurance-carriers/dual-eligible-snp-members/profile|Dual-Eligible & Special Needs Plan Members]]
**Industry:** [[industries/health-insurance-carriers|Health Insurance Carriers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Care management platforms already hold assessments, care plans and tasks; SNPs need them structured around the CMS-approved model of care and the state Medicaid contract.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #compliance #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to reach each new special needs plan member for a health risk assessment and care plan inside CMS's 90-day window, and whoever completes the most assessments with hard-to-reach dual-eligible members, and keeps them enrolled, takes the account.

## The Problem
Each SNP files a model of care describing its population, staff, assessments, care plans and interdisciplinary teams, and is audited against it. Integrated D-SNPs must also meet state contract terms for Medicaid services and coordination. Care managers work in a platform that knows none of this.

## What Already Exists
Payer care management platforms (HealthEdge's GuidingCare, ZeOmega's Jiva and others) with configurable assessments, care plans, tasks and reporting.

## The Customization Gap
SNPs need model-of-care elements as configured workflows: assessment timing, reassessment triggers, care plan components and interdisciplinary team documentation. They need Medicaid service authorizations and long-term services visible alongside Medicare care, and state reporting templates. They also need drafting of care plans from the assessment and claims history for the care manager to edit, and audit evidence generated as work is done.

## Target Customer
SNP compliance and care management leaders preparing model-of-care renewals and state contract audits.

## Impact If Solved
Care managers spend time with members rather than on documentation, and the plan can prove it delivers the model it filed.
