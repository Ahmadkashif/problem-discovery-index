# HEDIS Hybrid Chart Review & Stars Measurement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/health-insurance-carriers|Health Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to retrieve and abstract every sampled chart for the hybrid measures before the June HEDIS submission, and whoever closes the most numerator hits that pass the auditor's medical record review validation takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.2B a year in seasonal abstraction staff, chart retrieval, HEDIS engines and audit support (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1% of carrier administrative spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — measure engines are software; the evidence is retrieved records read by nurses
**Target Buyer:** VP of Quality / Director of HEDIS Operations
**Automation Potential:** High — finding dated values and procedures in records against fixed specifications is extraction work

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
HEDIS hybrid measures let plans add medical-record evidence for a sample of members to administrative data. Abstraction runs from January to the June submission (recalled), is validated by an NCQA-licensed auditor, and feeds MA Star Ratings and state Medicaid quality programmes. The 2027 Star Ratings, released on 8 October 2026, put 71% of MA-PD enrollees in contracts with four or more stars but only 37% of contracts. The difference between three and four stars is worth bonus payments a plan can measure. The work is seasonal, deadline-bound, highly specified and repetitive. NCQA's move of hybrid measures to electronic clinical data reporting will shrink it over several years, but has not yet removed it (recalled).

## Current Tools & Gaps
HEDIS engines (Inovalon, Cotiviti and others) with abstraction modules, retrieval vendors (Datavant and others), temporary nurse staffing and offshore abstraction. The gaps: abstraction still means a person scrolling for a value, chase lists ignore where the evidence probably is, and quality assurance happens too late to fix drift.
