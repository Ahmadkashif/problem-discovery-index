# Independent Review Organizations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/health-insurance-carriers|Health Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Accredited organisations that conduct external reviews of plans' adverse benefit determinations under state and federal rules, and that plans also hire for specialty peer review. Panels of contracted physicians read the case file and decide whether the denial stands. MCMC, Dane Street and AllMed are representative (named from recall). Plans or states pay per case.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Contracted physician reviewers paid per case; analytics staff typically under 10 (estimate)
**Output:** Case determinations
**Proprietary data:** Determination histories across plans
**Clock:** Statutory external review deadlines
**Buyer:** President / Medical Director
