# State Insurance Departments' Health Rate Review

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/health-insurance-carriers|Health Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The health-line slice of the state insurance departments already profiled as [[niches/public-adjusters/state-insurance-regulators/profile|🔍 State Insurance Departments & Market Conduct]]. Here that means the health actuaries and rate analysts who review proposed individual and small-group rates against carriers' actuarial memoranda, and who approve, cut or disapprove them before open enrolment. Many states contract part of this review out to consulting actuaries (recalled). Market conduct examiners test claims handling, directories and parity alongside them; their examination work papers are confidential by statute (recalled). The life-line slice is [[niches/life-insurance-carriers/state-insurance-life-examination/profile|🔍 State Insurance Departments' Life Actuarial & Financial Examination]], which also fails the gate. Licensing belongs to Licensing & Inspection Agencies, a sibling industry in the build queue.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 health rate-review actuaries in most departments (estimate); market conduct examiners are production staff and are not counted
**Output:** Rate decisions and review memos
**Proprietary data:** Carriers' filings and objection correspondence in the state
**Clock:** Statutory rate review timelines before open enrolment
**Buyer:** Chief Actuary, Health
