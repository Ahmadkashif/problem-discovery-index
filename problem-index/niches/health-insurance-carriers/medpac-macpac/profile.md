# MedPAC & MACPAC

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/health-insurance-carriers|Health Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The congressional advisory commissions on Medicare (MedPAC) and Medicaid and CHIP (MACPAC). Their analysts study MA payment, coding intensity, favourable selection, Star Ratings and Medicaid managed care, and publish reports and recommendations that shape CMS rate-setting and legislation. Their outputs are free; the buyers are Congress and the public.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 25-60 analysts per commission (estimate)
**Output:** Statutory reports to Congress, data books, comment letters
**Proprietary data:** Analyses built on CMS administrative and encounter data accessed as a government body
**Clock:** Statutory reporting dates (MedPAC by 15 March and 15 June, recalled)
**Buyer:** Executive Director
