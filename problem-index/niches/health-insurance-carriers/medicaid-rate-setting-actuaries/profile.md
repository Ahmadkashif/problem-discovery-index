# Medicaid Capitation Rate-Setting Actuaries

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/health-insurance-carriers|Health Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Actuarial firms that state Medicaid agencies hire to set and certify the monthly capitation rates they pay managed care organisations: Mercer's government practice, Milliman, Optumas and Guidehouse are representative (named from recall). Each year they build rates by population and region from encounter data and MCO financial reports. They certify them as actuarially sound for CMS review and defend them when plans dispute them. The rates decide whether a Medicaid MCO's contract is profitable. Payments to MCOs were about half of Medicaid spending in FY2024 (KFF).

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-400 actuaries and analysts per major practice (estimate)
**Output:** Annual rate certifications and rate-setting documentation submitted to CMS; rate-range and policy analyses for states
**Proprietary data:** Rate-setting methodology and cross-state experience; the encounter data and MCO financials belong to the state and its plans
**Clock:** Rate certifications must be ready for each contract rating period and CMS review (recalled), and state budget and contract cycles
**Buyer:** Practice Leader, Government Health

**Kill switches:** **data isn't theirs**, and **HIPAA** — the work runs on state-owned, member-level encounter data released under each state contract. Government procurement itself is not counted.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. A repeatable, regulator-dated certification business with a market of a few firms selling to a few dozen state buyers, built on data the states own.
