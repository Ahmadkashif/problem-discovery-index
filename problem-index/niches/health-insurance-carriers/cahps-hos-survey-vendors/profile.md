# CAHPS & HOS Survey Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/health-insurance-carriers|Health Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
CMS-approved survey vendors that field the member experience (CAHPS) and Health Outcomes Survey instruments for MA and Part D contracts each spring, following CMS's protocols, and submit the results to CMS. The results feed Star Ratings, and the 2027 ratings tripled the weight of the health-improvement measures drawn from HOS (CMS). Press Ganey, DSS Research and the Center for the Study of Services are representative (named from recall). Plans pay per contract surveyed and buy analysis of their scores.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 survey methodologists and analysts per vendor (estimate); interviewers and fulfilment staff are production
**Output:** Fielded surveys submitted to CMS, plan-level score reports and driver analyses
**Proprietary data:** Response-level survey data across many contracts, held under CMS protocol and plan contracts
**Clock:** CMS's fixed annual fielding and submission windows (spring, recalled)
**Buyer:** VP of Health Plan Surveys
