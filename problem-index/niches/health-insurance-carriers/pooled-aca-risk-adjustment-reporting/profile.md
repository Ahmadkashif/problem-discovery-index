# Pooled ACA Risk Adjustment Reporting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/health-insurance-carriers|Health Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Services that pool issuers' ACA data to estimate each issuer's risk adjustment transfer before HHS announces it. Wakely's National Risk Adjustment Reporting (WNRAR) is the established example. About 80 participating issuers, including nearly all major multi-market ACA issuers, covering 35+ states and 19M lives, receive five reports a year. Each shows year-to-date and projected transfers and the issuer's risk scores and condition prevalence against the market (Wakely). Issuers run Wakely's code on their own enrolment and claims files and upload only summary output. The 2026 participation guide states that no detailed claim data, protected health information or member-level data is sent to Wakely. Market results are released only once every participant in a state and market has submitted. Issuers use the estimates for quarterly accruals and for pricing the next year's rates.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-30 actuaries and analysts (estimate)
**Output:** Market-level and issuer-relative transfer estimates, risk score and prevalence benchmarks, model-change impact papers
**Proprietary data:** A pooled, multi-issuer view of state risk pools that no single issuer or public source provides before the HHS summary report
**Clock:** Wakely sets its own release dates (mid-month after each upload window, per the participation guide). HHS's annual notice of transfers by 30 June (recalled) is the answer key that grades the estimates, not a deadline on the work
**Buyer:** Practice Leader, Risk Adjustment

**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. A genuinely unique pooled dataset that HHS grades every June. But the release dates are self-set, the work scales with computation rather than analysts, and it is a market of one or two providers.
