# Health Plan Trade Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/health-insurance-carriers|Health Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Associations that represent health plans in Washington and the states: AHIP, the Association for Community Affiliated Plans, Medicaid Health Plans of America and the Better Medicare Alliance are representative (named from recall). They lobby, file comments and commission occasional studies, usually from consultancies. Blue Health Intelligence's claims analytics are covered under the claims consortium pocket, through its contributions to HCCI.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 dedicated research staff each (estimate); policy and advocacy staff dominate
**Output:** Comment letters, issue briefs, commissioned studies
**Proprietary data:** Occasional member surveys
**Clock:** Rulemaking comment deadlines
**Buyer:** Chief Policy Officer
