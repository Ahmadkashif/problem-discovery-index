# NCQA Health Plan Measurement & Accreditation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/health-insurance-carriers|Health Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The National Committee for Quality Assurance develops and maintains the HEDIS measures, licenses the specifications, certifies measure software and auditors, accredits health plans and publishes plan ratings. Its Quality Compass benchmarks, built from plans' audited submissions, are the reference percentiles every quality team plans against. CMS and most states require HEDIS reporting, which is why the measures matter (recalled). Pharmacy measure stewardship is logged as [[niches/pharmacy-independents/pharmacy-quality-measurement/profile|🔍 Pharmacy Quality Measurement Organizations]] (44).

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-150 measure developers, researchers and analysts (estimate)
**Output:** Annual HEDIS specifications, Quality Compass benchmarks, plan ratings, accreditation standards
**Proprietary data:** Audited, plan-level measure results across almost every US health plan over decades
**Clock:** NCQA's own annual HEDIS calendar and specification releases, which CMS and states adopt (recalled)
**Buyer:** Chief Research Officer / VP of Performance Measurement
