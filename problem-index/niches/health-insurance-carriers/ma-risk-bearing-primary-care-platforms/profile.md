# Medicare Advantage Risk-Bearing Primary Care Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/health-insurance-carriers|Health Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Companies that take delegated financial risk from MA plans for their attributed members, through full or partial capitation, and run or partner with primary care practices to manage it: agilon health, Privia, ChenMed and Oak Street (now part of CVS) are representative (named from recall). Their actuarial, risk adjustment and care management analytics teams model medical cost, coding completeness and Star measures for each plan contract. They earn the margin between capitation and cost. The practices themselves belong to Physician Practices, a sibling industry in the build queue.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-300 analysts, actuaries and data scientists per platform (estimate)
**Output:** Medical cost and risk score forecasts, care-gap and high-risk member lists, plan contract performance reports; none of it sold
**Proprietary data:** Plan claims and capitation reports for attributed members, plus their own clinical records
**Clock:** Plan contract reconciliation cycles, CMS risk adjustment submission deadlines and the Star Ratings calendar
**Buyer:** Chief Medical Economics Officer / Chief Analytics Officer
