# Multi-Payer Claims Research Consortia

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/health-insurance-carriers|Health Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Nonprofit and plan-owned bodies that pool carriers' de-identified claims for research on prices, spending and use. The Health Care Cost Institute is the clearest example. Its 2022 report drew on de-identified commercial claims contributed by CVS Health/Aetna, Humana and Blue Health Intelligence, and the dataset has covered 55M+ people a year. Researchers access it through a NORC enclave under non-commercial licences, and a contributor committee oversees use (HCCI; NORC). The out-of-network benchmark repository is logged as [[niches/chiropractic-practices/healthcare-cost-benchmark-nonprofits/profile|🔍 Healthcare Cost Benchmark Data Organizations]] (50, indexed).

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-50 researchers and data staff (estimate)
**Output:** Annual cost and utilisation reports, research briefs, research data licences
**Proprietary data:** Multi-carrier, de-identified commercial claims histories that no single insurer holds
**Clock:** Self-set publication cycles and grant timelines
**Buyer:** Executive Director / Head of Research
