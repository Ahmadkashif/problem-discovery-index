# Medicaid External Quality Review Organizations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/health-insurance-carriers|Health Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Private contractors that states must hire to review each Medicaid managed care plan every year: Health Services Advisory Group, IPRO, Qlarant, Comagine Health and Acentra Health are representative (named from recall). Their mandatory activities are validating performance measures, performance improvement projects and network adequacy, and reviewing compliance with federal standards. They write the annual EQR technical report, which each state must finalise and post by 30 April. Reports due from 30 April 2028 must include quantitative network adequacy results (42 CFR 438.364; Medicaid.gov).

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 analysts, statisticians and report writers per organisation (estimate); medical record reviewers and auditors doing validation over-reads are production staff and are not counted
**Output:** The annual EQR technical report per state, plan-level validation findings, focused studies
**Proprietary data:** Validation findings and methods across many plans and states over many years; the measure data, records and encounter files belong to the states and plans
**Clock:** The federal 30 April technical report deadline, and validation cycles tied to HEDIS and state reporting calendars
**Buyer:** Executive Director, State Medicaid Programmes

**Kill switches:** **HIPAA**, and **data isn't theirs** — performance measure validation re-reads members' medical records, and every input belongs to the state or the plan under the state contract. Government procurement itself is not counted.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. A private contractor whose report is a federal deliverable with a statutory date, built entirely on others' protected data.
