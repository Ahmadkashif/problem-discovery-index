# Medicare & Health Policy Advisory Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/health-insurance-carriers|Health Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Consultancies that model what federal and state policy does to health plans: Avalere Health, ATI Advisory, Health Management Associates and Manatt Health are representative (named from recall). They estimate the effect of each year's MA Advance Notice and Rate Announcement, risk-model and Star Ratings changes, Medicaid financing rules and Marketplace subsidy changes on plans' revenue. They also draft comment letters and advise on bids and market entry. Plans, provider groups, pharmaceutical companies and investors commission the work. Investor-facing policy research is logged as [[niches/hedge-funds/policy-intelligence-consultancies/profile|🔍 Policy & Political Intelligence Consultancies]] (43).

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-500 analysts, actuaries and policy staff per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Payment-impact analyses, policy briefs, comment letters, market and benefit studies
**Proprietary data:** Models and databases built from public CMS files (rate books, plan benefit packages, enrolment, Star Ratings) and an archive of past impact estimates; some work uses CMS research files under data use agreements (recalled)
**Clock:** CMS's Advance Notice and Rate Announcement releases (announcement by the first Monday in April, recalled) and proposed-rule comment deadlines frame the work, but the core deliverable is analysis timed to clients' own decisions, including their June bids; comment letters are a minority of the output
**Buyer:** Practice Leader / Managing Director
