# Star Ratings Forecasting & Quality Strategy Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/health-insurance-carriers|Health Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Consultancies and analytics firms that forecast MA Star Ratings cut points, model each contract's likely rating, check plans' calculations during CMS's plan preview periods, and advise on which measures to invest in. Gorman Health Group and ATTAC Consulting are representative, alongside the Stars analytics of the large quality vendors (named from recall). The 2027 ratings, published 8 October 2026, rated 508 MA-PD contracts. Only 15 earned five stars, down from 22, and measure weights changed (CMS).

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 analysts and quality consultants per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Cut-point forecasts, contract rating projections, plan preview validation, measure strategy
**Proprietary data:** Forecasting models and an archive of past cut-point forecasts that can be graded against published results; the inputs are public CMS data, and gap-closure work uses clients' member data
**Clock:** CMS's plan preview windows in late summer, the October ratings release and the 31 December measurement-year close
**Buyer:** Practice Leader, Stars and Quality
