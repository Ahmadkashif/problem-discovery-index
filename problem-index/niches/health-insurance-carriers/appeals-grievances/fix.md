# The Overturn That Was Never Paid

**Niche:** [[niches/health-insurance-carriers/appeals-grievances/profile|Appeals & Grievances Case Management]]
**Industry:** [[industries/health-insurance-carriers|Health Insurance Carriers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The appeals team reverses a denial, and the authorization or payment it promised depends on another department acting on an email.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #automation #compliance #quick-win #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to close every appeal and grievance inside its regulatory clock with a case file a CMS program auditor will pass, and whoever has zero untimely, misclassified or uneffectuated cases in the audit universe takes the account.

## The Problem
After an overturn, the plan must authorize the service or pay the claim within a set time (recalled). The appeals analyst emails UM or claims, marks the case closed and moves on. If the email is missed or the claim reprocesses wrongly, the member or provider is still unpaid weeks later. Auditors find it when they test effectuation dates.

## Why It's Still Broken
Appeals, UM and claims run on different systems with no shared task, and the appeals case closes at the decision rather than at effectuation.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Keep the case open until effectuation is confirmed from the system of record: the authorization is loaded or the claim has been reprocessed and paid. Create the downstream task automatically with the clock attached, and escalate before it expires.

## Who Feels the Pain
Members still billed after winning; providers waiting on payment; compliance officers facing effectuation findings.

## Impact If Fixed
Overturns that actually pay, on time, and one of the commonest audit findings closed at low cost.
