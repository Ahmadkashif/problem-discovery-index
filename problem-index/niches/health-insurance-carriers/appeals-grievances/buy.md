# Case Management Platforms With the Regulatory Clocks Built In

**Niche:** [[niches/health-insurance-carriers/appeals-grievances/profile|Appeals & Grievances Case Management]]
**Industry:** [[industries/health-insurance-carriers|Health Insurance Carriers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** General case management platforms already route, track and escalate cases; payer appeals need dozens of overlapping legal clocks that start, pause and restart by line of business.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #large-language-models #compliance #automation #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to close every appeal and grievance inside its regulatory clock with a case file a CMS program auditor will pass, and whoever has zero untimely, misclassified or uneffectuated cases in the audit universe takes the account.

## The Problem
One plan may run MA, Part D, Medicaid in several states, Marketplace and employer-group business, each with different appeal levels, clocks, extension rules, notice templates and external review routes. Analysts track deadlines across them by hand.

## What Already Exists
Salesforce Service Cloud, Pega and similar case management platforms, with SLA timers, routing and document generation.

## The Customization Gap
The payer layer needs clock libraries per line of business and state, including expedited conversion, extensions and tolling. It needs auto-forwarding of adverse MA reconsiderations to the independent review entity with the file attached. It needs notice templates that meet CMS and state content rules, links to UM and claims records for case assembly, and effectuation tasks pushed to the departments that must act, with confirmation back.

## Target Customer
Appeals and grievances leaders at multi-line plans consolidating several legacy case systems.

## Impact If Solved
One case system that knows the law for every line of business, and clocks that the system tracks rather than the analyst.
