# Utilization Management & Prior Authorization

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/health-insurance-carriers|Health Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor is fighting to decide authorization requests inside the CMS-0057-F clocks with determinations that survive appeal. But outpatient prior authorization and inpatient and post-acute concurrent review are won on different inputs, clocks, vendors and outcomes, so the niche is decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$12B a year in UM nursing and medical director staff, licensed criteria, specialty benefit manager fees and authorization platforms (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of carrier administrative spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — criteria are licensed software and portals exist, but clinical evidence still arrives as faxes and uploads that nurses read
**Target Buyer:** VP of Utilization Management / Chief Medical Officer
**Automation Potential:** High for evidence extraction and approvals; deliberately low for denials

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Utilization management is where a plan decides, case by case, whether care is covered before or while it happens. Medicare Advantage plans alone made 52.8M prior authorization determinations in 2024 (KFF). From January 2026 CMS-0057-F gives MA and Medicaid plans 72 hours for expedited and seven days for standard requests, and requires public metrics on approvals, denials and decision times. From January 2027 it requires a prior authorization API. No other carrier function combines this volume, this clinical judgement and this level of regulatory and political scrutiny. Algorithm-assisted denials have drawn class actions, state laws and congressional attention.

## Current Tools & Gaps
UM platforms (HealthEdge's GuidingCare, ZeOmega's Jiva, plan-built systems) hold cases and queues. MCG and InterQual supply criteria. Specialty benefit managers (Evolent, Carelon, EviCore) run delegated programmes for imaging, cardiology, oncology and musculoskeletal care. Newer vendors (Cohere, Availity) automate submission and some approvals. The gaps are a missing evidence layer between the document and the criterion, review queues ordered by arrival rather than by doubt, and no record joining a denial to what happened afterwards.
