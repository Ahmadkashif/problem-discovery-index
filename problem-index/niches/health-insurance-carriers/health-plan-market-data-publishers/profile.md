# Health Plan Market, Enrollment & Financial Data Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/health-insurance-carriers|Health Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Publishers that turn statutory filings and CMS enrolment files into market-share, enrolment and financial databases on health plans: Mark Farrah Associates and AIS Health (part of MMIT) are representative, alongside the insurance data units of S&P Global and AM Best (named from recall). Plans, consultants, investors and vendors subscribe to track competitors county by county and line by line. The underlying regulatory record is logged as [[niches/independent-insurance-agents/insurance-regulatory-data-organization/profile|🔍 Insurance Regulatory Data Organizations]] (49). The life-insurer equivalent, scored in line with this pocket, is [[niches/life-insurance-carriers/insurer-financial-surveillance-research/profile|🔍 Life Insurer Financial Surveillance & Carrier Due-Diligence Research]] (36).

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 analysts and editors per publisher (estimate)
**Output:** Subscription databases, market-share reports, newsletters
**Proprietary data:** Cleaned, linked histories of public filings; the inputs (NAIC statements, CMS monthly enrolment files) are public
**Clock:** Quarterly statutory filing dates and CMS's monthly enrolment releases
**Buyer:** Publisher / Head of Research
