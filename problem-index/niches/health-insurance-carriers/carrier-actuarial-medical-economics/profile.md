# Health Plan Actuarial & Medical Economics Departments

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/health-insurance-carriers|Health Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The carriers' own insight functions: pricing and reserving actuaries, medical economics teams that decompose cost trend by service and provider, and the analytics groups behind bids, rate filings, MLR reporting and provider contract modelling. National carriers employ hundreds. The 2,730 actuaries and 5,310 data scientists counted in NAICS 524114 (BLS OEWS, May 2023) are a floor, because many carriers' analysts sit in affiliates coded elsewhere. Their output sets premiums and bids; it is never sold.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-1,000 actuaries, medical economists and analysts at large carriers; 5-30 at regional plans (estimate)
**Output:** Bids, rate filings, reserves, trend analyses, contract models, MLR reports
**Proprietary data:** The plan's complete claims, enrolment, authorization and provider contract history
**Clock:** The MA bid deadline in June, state rate filing deadlines, quarterly reserving and the annual MLR report (31 July, recalled)
**Buyer:** Chief Actuary / VP of Medical Economics
