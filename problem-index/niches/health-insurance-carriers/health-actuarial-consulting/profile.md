# Health Actuarial Consulting for Rate Filings & MA Bids

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/health-insurance-carriers|Health Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
The health practices of Milliman, Wakely, Oliver Wyman, Lewis & Ellis, Axene Health Partners and others (named from recall). They price ACA individual and small-group rates and write the actuarial memoranda filed with states, prepare and certify Medicare Advantage and Part D bids, set reserves, and run MLR and risk adjustment analyses. Regional and small plans without large actuarial staffs depend on them most. Behavioural health policy studies from the same firms are logged as [[niches/behavioral-health-clinics/behavioral-actuarial-research/profile|🔍 Behavioral Health Actuarial & Disparity Research]] (49, kill switch).

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-1,000 health actuaries and analysts per large practice (estimate)
**Output:** Rate filings and actuarial memoranda, certified bids, reserve opinions, pricing models
**Proprietary data:** Engagement benchmarks across many clients; the claims experience, enrolment and models belong to each client plan
**Clock:** The MA bid deadline (first Monday in June, recalled) and state and federal rate filing deadlines, which fall on the certifying actuary
**Buyer:** Health Practice Leader / Principal
