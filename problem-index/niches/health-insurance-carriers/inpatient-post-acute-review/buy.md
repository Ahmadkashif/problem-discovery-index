**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to set each inpatient and post-acute stay's length from the clinical record in daily review, and whoever's length-of-stay decisions hold through appeals and peer-to-peer reviews with no rise in readmissions takes the account.

## The Problem
Hospital utilization review and case management teams use software that reads the EHR continuously, applies admission criteria and forecasts discharge readiness. The plan's concurrent review nurse reviewing the same patient works from a clinical summary faxed or uploaded once a day, and skilled nursing facilities send even less.

## What Already Exists
Hospital-side admission-status and discharge-planning platforms, EHR-embedded criteria, and shared-review tools some hospital–payer pairs have adopted.

## The Customization Gap
A payer version needs several things. Ingestion from many hospitals' and facilities' feeds of uneven quality. The plan's post-acute network and contracted rates. MA rules that bind plans to traditional Medicare's inpatient admission and post-acute coverage criteria (recalled). Notice requirements before a covered stay ends. And a shared view the hospital can see, so disagreements are resolved on the same evidence before a denial rather than in an appeal after.

## Target Customer
Concurrent review leaders at MA and Medicaid plans with large hospital volumes concentrated in a few systems.

## Impact If Solved
Reviewers see current evidence rather than yesterday's fax, disputes with hospitals move upstream, and decisions rest on the same record the hospital used.
