# Appraisal Management & Review Operations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/home-inspection|Home Inspection]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Appraisal management companies assign residential appraisals to independent appraisers on behalf of lenders and run the review function that decides whether a completed appraisal is acceptable — comparable selection, adjustment support, condition ratings, and regulatory conformity.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 review appraisers, quality control staff, and analysts
**Output:** Appraisal review findings, revision requests, quality scoring, vendor performance management
**Proprietary data:** Completed appraisals with review findings and downstream loan outcomes across large volumes
**Clock:** Loan closing dates and lender turnaround requirements
**Buyer:** Chief Appraiser / VP of Quality
