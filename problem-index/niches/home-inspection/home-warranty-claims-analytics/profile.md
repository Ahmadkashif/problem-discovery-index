# Home Warranty Claims Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/home-inspection|Home Inspection]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Home warranty companies pay for system and appliance failures after a sale, and their analytics groups price coverage and manage the contractor network against what actually breaks.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 actuaries, analysts, and network managers
**Output:** Pricing models, claim frequency analysis, contractor performance management
**Proprietary data:** Component failure claims across millions of homes by system, age, and brand — the empirical record of what fails after purchase, which is precisely what an inspection is trying to predict
**Clock:** Annual contract renewal
**Buyer:** Chief Actuary / VP of Analytics
