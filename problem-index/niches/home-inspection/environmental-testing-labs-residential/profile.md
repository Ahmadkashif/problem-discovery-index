# Residential Environmental Testing Laboratories

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/home-inspection|Home Inspection]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Laboratories analyze the radon, mould, asbestos, lead, and water samples inspectors collect during transactions, returning results that frequently determine whether a sale proceeds or a remediation is negotiated.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-80 analysts and laboratory technicians
**Output:** Analytical results, interpretive summaries, remediation thresholds
**Proprietary data:** Results by address and region accumulated over decades — a geographic hazard record nobody has assembled
**Clock:** Inspection contingency periods, typically days
**Buyer:** Laboratory director
