# Specialty Inspection Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/home-inspection|Home Inspection]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Sewer scope, thermal imaging, pool, and chimney specialists take the ancillary work inspectors refer out, charging separately for a narrow technical assessment.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1-5 technicians per firm
**Output:** Specialty inspection reports and video documentation
**Proprietary data:** Local defect frequency by neighbourhood and construction era, held informally
**Clock:** Inspection contingency periods
**Buyer:** Owner-operator
