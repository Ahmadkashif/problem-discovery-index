# Inspection Software Data Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/home-inspection|Home Inspection]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Spectora, HomeGauge, ISN, and the report-writing platforms process the reports nearly every US home inspection produces — defect narratives, severity ratings, and millions of annotated photographs.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 product and data staff
**Output:** Report templates, defect libraries, scheduling and business analytics
**Proprietary data:** The largest structured corpus of residential defect observations and photographs in existence, contributed by 25,000 inspectors
**Clock:** Transaction turnaround pressure on report delivery
**Buyer:** Head of Product
