# Property Condition & Risk Data Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/home-inspection|Home Inspection]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
CoreLogic, Verisk, Cape Analytics, and their peers assemble what is known about the physical condition of American residential property — roof age and condition from imagery, construction attributes, repair cost schedules, peril exposure — and sell it to the insurers, lenders, and investors who must price a property they will never visit. Where a home inspector produces one report for one buyer, these firms produce a condition estimate for every property in the country.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-2,000 data scientists, geospatial engineers, cost analysts, and researchers
**Output:** Property attribute and condition scores, repair and replacement cost estimates, peril risk models, underwriting data feeds
**Proprietary data:** Property attribute databases assembled over decades, aerial and street imagery archives with derived condition labels, and repair cost schedules maintained from actual claims — none of it publicly reconstructible
**Clock:** Underwriting and renewal cycles, catastrophe response, quarterly model releases
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / SVP of Product
