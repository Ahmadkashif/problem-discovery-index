# Residential Structural Engineering Referrals

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/home-inspection|Home Inspection]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Small structural engineering practices take the referrals home inspectors generate — foundation movement, framing questions, load-bearing alterations — and issue the letter that resolves a transaction contingency.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 2-10 engineers per practice
**Output:** Structural evaluation letters, repair recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Foundation and framing observations across a local market
**Clock:** Contingency deadlines, days
**Buyer:** Principal engineer
