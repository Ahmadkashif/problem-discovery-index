# Commercial Property Condition Assessment Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/home-inspection|Home Inspection]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Engineering firms perform the property condition assessments lenders and buyers require before a commercial real estate transaction closes — walking a building, documenting every system's condition, and producing immediate repair and replacement reserve tables that go directly into the loan. This is the same act a home inspector performs, at institutional scale, for a buyer who reads the numbers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-800 assessors, engineers, and technical reviewers per national firm
**Output:** Property condition reports, immediate repair schedules, replacement reserve tables, cost-to-cure estimates
**Proprietary data:** Component conditions, observed ages, and cost-to-cure figures across tens of thousands of buildings nationally, accumulated over decades of assessments
**Clock:** Loan closing dates — the report gates the transaction
**Buyer:** National practice leader / Chief Technical Officer
