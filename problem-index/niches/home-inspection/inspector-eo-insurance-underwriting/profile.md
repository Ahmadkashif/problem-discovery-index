# Inspector Errors & Omissions Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/home-inspection|Home Inspection]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Specialty programmes underwrite professional liability for home inspectors, pricing the risk that an inspector misses something a buyer later sues over.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 underwriters, claims analysts, and risk managers
**Output:** Rating and eligibility decisions, claims analysis, loss prevention guidance
**Proprietary data:** Claims by defect type, inspection scope, and report language — a direct record of what inspectors miss and what wording protects them
**Clock:** Annual renewal
**Buyer:** Programme manager / Chief Underwriting Officer
