# Insurance Property Inspection Networks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/home-inspection|Home Inspection]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
National inspection networks perform underwriting and loss control inspections for property insurers at very high volume — exterior surveys, interior inspections, and replacement cost estimates on policies at new business and renewal.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 inspectors, reviewers, and analysts above a large field workforce
**Output:** Underwriting inspection reports, hazard findings, replacement cost valuations
**Proprietary data:** Standardized condition observations across millions of homes, joined for their carrier clients to the policies that followed
**Clock:** Carrier underwriting turnaround requirements
**Buyer:** VP of Operations / Chief Product Officer
