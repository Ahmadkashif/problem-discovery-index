The inspector associations write the standards of practice, run the certification examinations, and supply the continuing education a large share of the 25,000 US inspectors take.

**Output:** Standards of practice, certification examinations, continuing education libraries
**Proprietary data:** Examination performance and course completion across most of the profession
**Clock:** Licensing renewal and continuing education cycles
**Buyer:** Executive director / Director of Education
