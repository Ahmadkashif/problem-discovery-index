# Laboratory Animal Programme Accreditation & Oversight

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/biotech-research-firms|Biotech Research Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
AAALAC International accredits animal care and use programmes through periodic site visits. The USDA's Animal Care programme inspects registered research facilities under the Animal Welfare Act, and the NIH Office of Laboratory Animal Welfare oversees institutions receiving federal funds. Every biotech and CRO vivarium answers to some combination of them, and sponsors increasingly ask whether a CRO is accredited.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 professional staff at the accreditor, supported by volunteer site visitors; federal inspectors spread across all regulated facilities (estimate)
**Output:** Accreditation decisions and site-visit reports; inspection reports
**Proprietary data:** Site-visit findings across hundreds of programmes, held confidentially
**Clock:** Fixed site-visit and inspection cycles
**Buyer:** Executive Director
