# Life-Science Venture Debt & Royalty Investors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/biotech-research-firms|Biotech Research Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Venture lenders such as Hercules Capital and Oxford Finance lend to venture-backed biotechs against their cash runway and pipeline. Royalty investors such as Royalty Pharma buy future royalties on drugs. Both must research the science and the clinical odds to price risk, and both are paid through interest and returns, not fees for analysis.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 underwriting and scientific diligence staff per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Credit and royalty underwriting memos, portfolio monitoring
**Proprietary data:** Loan and royalty performance across hundreds of biotech borrowers and assets, plus borrower data received under confidentiality
**Clock:** Borrowers' financing rounds and covenant tests; client-driven
**Buyer:** Chief Credit Officer / Chief Investment Officer
