# Biopharma Deal & Licensing Benchmark Databases

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/biotech-research-firms|Biotech Research Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
DealForma, Clarivate's Cortellis Deals, BioCentury's BCIQ and the deal modules of the pipeline vendors record licensing, collaboration and M&A terms — upfronts, milestones, royalties, stage at signing — from filings and press releases. Biotech CFOs and business development teams use them to benchmark terms before negotiating, and bankers and pharma do the same.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 analysts per provider (estimate)
**Output:** Deal records and term benchmarks, quarterly and annual deal reviews
**Proprietary data:** Curated deal terms over many years, assembled from public filings with confidential-treatment redactions; the inputs are public, the structure is the provider's
**Clock:** Client negotiations and the partnering season around the J.P. Morgan Healthcare Conference and BIO; none binding
**Buyer:** Founder / Head of Research
