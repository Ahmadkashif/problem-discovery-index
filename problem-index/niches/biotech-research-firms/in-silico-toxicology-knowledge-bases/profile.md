# In Silico Toxicology Knowledge Bases

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/biotech-research-firms|Biotech Research Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Lhasa Limited (a not-for-profit), Instem's Leadscope and MultiCASE sell expert-rule and statistical systems that predict mutagenicity and other toxicity from chemical structure, with curated toxicity databases behind them. Biotechs use them for early hazard flags and impurity assessments under ICH M7. Lhasa coordinates data-sharing consortia in which pharma companies pool proprietary results, such as Vitic Intermediates, begun in 2006.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 scientists and curators per provider (estimate)
**Output:** Licensed prediction software, curated toxicity databases, expert alert knowledge bases
**Proprietary data:** Expert-written structural alerts and curated toxicity data; consortium data is pooled under member-only terms
**Clock:** Customers' submission timelines; none set on the provider
**Buyer:** Chief Executive / Head of Science
