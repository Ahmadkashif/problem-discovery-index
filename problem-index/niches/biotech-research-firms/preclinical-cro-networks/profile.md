# Preclinical CRO Networks' Scientific Functions

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/biotech-research-firms|Biotech Research Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Charles River, Labcorp's early development business, Inotiv, Altasciences and WuXi AppTec's safety arm have consolidated dozens of toxicology and discovery labs into networks. Their study directors, toxicologists and pathologists design and interpret the studies that make up IND packages, and sponsors pay per study. Charles River's Discovery and Safety Assessment segment alone booked $2.40B in 2025.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-2,000 study directors, toxicologists and pathologists across the large networks (estimate)
**Output:** GLP and non-GLP study reports, toxicology interpretation, package design advice
**Proprietary data:** Historical control data across thousands of studies is the network's own; every study's results belong to its sponsor
**Clock:** Sponsors' IND targets and financing milestones, a client's schedule rather than a regulator's
**Buyer:** Chief Scientific Officer / Head of Safety Assessment
