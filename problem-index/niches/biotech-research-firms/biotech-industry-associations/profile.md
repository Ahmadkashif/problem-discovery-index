# Biotech Industry Associations' Research Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/biotech-research-firms|Biotech Research Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Biotechnology Innovation Organization (BIO) and regional groups such as MassBio publish funding, pipeline and policy reports, usually with data partners. BIO's clinical success-rate study, for example, was produced with Informa and QLS. Members pay dues for advocacy, partnering events and these reports.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Fewer than 10 analysts in each association's research function (estimate)
**Output:** Annual funding and pipeline reports, policy analyses
**Proprietary data:** Member surveys; most analysis rests on partners' data
**Clock:** Self-set annual publication and policy calendars
**Buyer:** Head of Industry Analysis
