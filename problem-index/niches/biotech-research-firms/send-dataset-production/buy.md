# Clinical Data Mapping Tools Fitted to Nonclinical Domains

**Niche:** [[niches/biotech-research-firms/send-dataset-production/profile|SEND Dataset Production]]
**Industry:** [[industries/biotech-research-firms|Biotech Research Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Clinical trials map data to SDTM with mature, metadata-driven tools; nonclinical SEND has different domains, terminology and source systems.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #word-embeddings #data-integration #automation #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce SEND datasets that pass FDA's technical rejection criteria and match the study report the first time, from whatever system the study ran on, and whoever closes the gap between report and dataset fastest takes the account.

## The Problem
Studies with contributing sites, legacy systems or bioanalytical data from another lab are converted by programmers writing study-specific mappings, and each study repeats the work.

## What Already Exists
Clinical SDTM automation tools, metadata repositories and mapping platforms used by clinical CROs and sponsors.

## The Customization Gap
SEND needs: nonclinical domains (MI microscopic findings, MA macroscopic, OM organ measurements, BW body weights, PC/PP toxicokinetics) and their implementation-guide versions; nonclinical controlled terminology, including mapping free-text pathology terms to it; pooling and group definitions for animal studies; and source connectors to Provantis, Pristima and contributing-lab exports. Language models can propose terminology mappings for review.

## Target Customer
CRO data standards groups and specialist SEND vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Study-specific programming replaced by reusable, metadata-driven mappings.
