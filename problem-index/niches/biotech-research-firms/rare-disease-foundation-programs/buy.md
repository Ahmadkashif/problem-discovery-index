# Clinical Data-Sharing Platforms Extended to Preclinical Evidence

**Niche:** [[niches/biotech-research-firms/rare-disease-foundation-programs/profile|Rare-Disease & Foundation-Funded Programmes]]
**Industry:** [[industries/biotech-research-firms|Biotech Research Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Platforms already exist for sharing patient-level clinical and natural-history data across sponsors; none carries the preclinical studies and disease-model records a foundation's next sponsor needs.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #word-embeddings #data-integration #compliance #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make the evidence a patient foundation owns or can broker — natural-history data, characterised disease models, and earlier sponsors' toxicology under rights of reference — citable by the next small sponsor's IND, and whoever makes one programme's evidence reusable by the next takes the account.

## The Problem
Foundations increasingly pool registry and natural-history data so that sponsors can design trials. The preclinical side — toxicology reports, SEND datasets, model characterisation, pharmacology studies — is shared, if at all, by emailing PDFs under ad hoc confidentiality agreements.

## What Already Exists
Clinical data-sharing platforms such as Vivli, and the Critical Path Institute's rare-disease data platform, host patient-level clinical and natural-history data with access controls and data-use agreements. Registry platforms run foundations' patient registries.

## The Customization Gap
Preclinical sharing needs: study-report and SEND dataset hosting, with metadata on modality, species, duration and findings; licence and letter-of-authorisation terms attached to each study so a new sponsor knows what it may cite; disease-model records linked to the natural-history data they mirror; and access tiers for academic, foundation and commercial users.

## Target Customer
Foundations already running a registry or natural-history study that want their preclinical investments to compound the same way.

## Impact If Solved
The foundation's preclinical evidence becomes as reusable as its patient data, without building a new platform.
