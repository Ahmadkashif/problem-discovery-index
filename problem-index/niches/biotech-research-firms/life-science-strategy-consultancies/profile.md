# Life-Science Strategy & Asset Valuation Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/biotech-research-firms|Biotech Research Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Consultancies such as Health Advances, ClearView Healthcare Partners, Trinity Life Sciences, Back Bay Life Science Advisors and the life-science practices of strategy firms size markets, forecast revenue and value assets for biotechs preparing to partner or raise, and for pharma and investors evaluating them. The output is a forecast model and a recommendation. Licensing deal value reached over $252B in 2025 (J.P. Morgan), and much of it was priced on this kind of work.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-500 consultants per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Commercial assessments, revenue forecasts, risk-adjusted NPV valuations, indication prioritisation, due-diligence reports
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated forecast models and primary-research interviews, though most deliverables are owned by the commissioning client
**Clock:** Client deal processes, financing timetables and partnering seasons around the J.P. Morgan Healthcare Conference; none set by a regulator
**Buyer:** Managing Partner
