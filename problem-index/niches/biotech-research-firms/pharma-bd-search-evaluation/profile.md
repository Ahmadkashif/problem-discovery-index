# Pharma Business Development Search & Evaluation Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/biotech-research-firms|Biotech Research Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Every large pharma company runs a search and evaluation group that scouts biotech assets, runs scientific diligence in data rooms and recommends licensing or acquisition. They are the buyers of most biotech partnerships: licensing deal value exceeded $252B in 2025 and 129 biopharma M&A deals totalled $138B (J.P. Morgan). Their analysis decides deals but is never invoiced.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-100 scientists and analysts per large pharma (estimate)
**Output:** Asset evaluations and diligence reports for internal deal committees
**Proprietary data:** Years of evaluated assets, including those passed on, with confidential data-room material
**Clock:** Competitive auction processes run by bankers, and internal portfolio reviews
**Buyer:** Head of Search & Evaluation
