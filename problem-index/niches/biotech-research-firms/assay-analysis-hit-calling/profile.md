## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every discovery biotech converts plate-reader output into numbers that steer the next design cycle: potency, selectivity, efficacy, hit or no hit. The task recurs weekly, has a defined output and a known failure set — edge effects, compound precipitation, outliers, bell-shaped curves. Large pharma runs enterprise screening analytics; smaller biotechs run spreadsheets, and research associates spend hours on it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Genedata Screener at pharma and large biotechs; Dotmatics, CDD Vault and Benchling analysis modules; GraphPad Prism; homegrown Excel and Python scripts. Gaps: masking decisions made by eye without reasons; analysis delayed until someone has time; and enterprise tools priced and configured for high-throughput screening, not a biotech's ten plates a week.
