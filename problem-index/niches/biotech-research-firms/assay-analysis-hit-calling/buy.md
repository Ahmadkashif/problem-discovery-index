# Enterprise Screening Analytics Cut Down for a Biotech

**Industry:** [[industries/biotech-research-firms|Biotech Research Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Pharma's screening analytics already standardise plate QC and curve fitting; a forty-person biotech cannot configure or afford them.
**Tags:** #bayesian-inference #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn plate-reader output into curve fits and hit calls scientists accept without re-checking, with every masking and refit decision recorded, and whoever makes analysis both same-day and auditable takes the account.

## The Problem
Small biotechs run a handful of assays repeatedly, some in-house and some at CROs, and analyse them in Prism and Excel because enterprise systems need an informatics team.

## What Already Exists
Genedata Screener, Dotmatics and similar platforms provide plate QC, curve fitting, hit calling and audit trails at enterprise scale.

## The Customization Gap
The small-biotech case needs: preconfigured templates for common assay formats; direct import of CRO-run assay files as well as in-house instruments; registry write-back to Benchling or CDD Vault; per-assay pricing; and recorded masking reasons by default rather than as an optional field.

## Target Customer
Series A–C biotechs with five to fifty biologists.

## Impact If Solved
Pharma-grade consistency in assay analysis without an informatics hire.
