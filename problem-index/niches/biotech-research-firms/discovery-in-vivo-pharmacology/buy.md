**One-liner:** Clinical trials solved randomisation and blinding with dedicated systems decades ago; animal efficacy studies still randomise in a spreadsheet, if at all.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make a non-GLP efficacy study trustworthy enough to move a programme — randomised, blinded and completely recorded — and whoever makes the efficacy signal reproduce in the next study takes the account.

## The Problem
Groups are allocated by a scientist sorting animals by tumour volume in Excel, and the technician measuring tumours knows which cage got the drug. Reviewers and partners increasingly ask for ARRIVE-style reporting, which most studies cannot reconstruct.

## What Already Exists
Randomisation and trial supply management systems used in clinical trials (Medidata Rave RTSM, Suvoda and others) handle stratified randomisation, blinding and unblinding logs.

## The Customization Gap
Animal studies need: stratified allocation by baseline tumour volume or body weight at the cage level; blinding of measurement staff through coded cage labels; integration with caliper and balance capture; interim unblinding rules for humane endpoints; and an automatically generated methods record matching ARRIVE 2.0 items, at a price per study rather than per trial.

## Target Customer
In vivo pharmacology leads at biotechs and discovery CROs whose results face partner diligence.

## Impact If Solved
Documented randomisation and blinding at negligible effort, which removes the commonest reason an efficacy result is discounted.
