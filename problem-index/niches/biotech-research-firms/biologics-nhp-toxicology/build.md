# A Weight-of-Evidence Builder for Primate-Sparing Packages

**Niche:** [[niches/biotech-research-firms/biologics-nhp-toxicology/profile|Biologics & NHP-Dependent Toxicology]]
**Industry:** [[industries/biotech-research-firms|Biotech Research Firms]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** FDA now invites sponsors to replace primate studies with integrated non-animal evidence, but nobody has a tool that assembles that evidence into a case a reviewer can follow.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bayesian-inference #graph-neural-networks #causal-inference #compliance #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver a biologics safety package FDA will accept using the fewest non-human primates — purpose-bred animals secured where they are unavoidable, validated non-animal evidence substituted where the 2025 roadmap allows — and whoever can show FDA-accepted primate-sparing designs takes the account.

## The Problem
A sponsor arguing that a six-month primate study is unnecessary must combine target biology, expression across human and animal tissues, binding and cross-reactivity data, in vitro functional assays, organ-chip results, class experience with similar antibodies and any human data. Today a toxicologist writes that argument from scratch in a document, compound by compound, without knowing which arguments FDA has accepted for comparable targets.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The policy is new (April 2025, with draft guidance from December 2025). The evidence types sit with different vendors. And the precedent — FDA's responses to individual sponsors — is confidential, so no vendor has a corpus to build from.

## What to Build
A structured weight-of-evidence workspace: a target safety profile assembled from public expression atlases, literature and the sponsor's own data; risk hypotheses per organ with the evidence for and against each; explicit mapping of each NAM result to the hazard it addresses and its stated context of use; and an output formatted for the pre-IND package. Precedent from public FDA review documents and published case studies is attached where it exists.

## Target Customer
Nonclinical safety heads at antibody developers; CROs selling NAMs-based packages.

## Impact If Built
A credible primate-sparing case can remove a study costing millions of dollars and many months (estimate), and the sponsor that can make it consistently wins on both cost and calendar.
