# Organ-Chip Platforms With a Regulatory Evidence Layer

**Niche:** [[niches/biotech-research-firms/biologics-nhp-toxicology/profile|Biologics & NHP-Dependent Toxicology]]
**Industry:** [[industries/biotech-research-firms|Biotech Research Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Organ-on-chip and organoid systems exist and are improving; what they lack is the documentation that turns a result into evidence FDA can weigh.
**Tags:** #hypothesis-testing #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #compliance #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver a biologics safety package FDA will accept using the fewest non-human primates — purpose-bred animals secured where they are unavoidable, validated non-animal evidence substituted where the 2025 roadmap allows — and whoever can show FDA-accepted primate-sparing designs takes the account.

## The Problem
Sponsors running non-animal assays to support a biologics IND find that the assay result is the easy part. Reviewers want to know what the system can and cannot detect, how it performed on reference compounds, and how the result maps to a human hazard.

## What Already Exists
Emulate's organ-chips, CN Bio's liver models, organoid and microphysiological system vendors, and FDA's ISTAND pilot for qualifying novel drug development tools.

## The Customization Gap
Regulatory use needs: a context-of-use statement per assay; performance data against reference antibodies with known clinical toxicities, with sensitivity and specificity and their confidence intervals; data integrity and documentation approaching GLP expectations; standard report sections that drop into Module 2.6; and version control of protocols so results from different years are comparable.

## Target Customer
NAMs platform vendors and the CROs reselling their assays to antibody sponsors.

## Impact If Solved
Non-animal results that arrive submission-ready, shortening the path from experiment to an accepted primate-sparing package.
