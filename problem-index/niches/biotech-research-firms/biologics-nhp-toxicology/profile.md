# Biologics & NHP-Dependent Toxicology

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/biotech-research-firms|Biotech Research Firms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver a biologics safety package FDA will accept using the fewest non-human primates — purpose-bred animals secured where they are unavoidable, validated non-animal evidence substituted where the 2025 roadmap allows — and whoever can show FDA-accepted primate-sparing designs takes the account.

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Monoclonal antibodies and many other biologics bind only human and primate targets, so ICH S6 has pushed their safety testing into cynomolgus monkeys. Supply broke in 2023 when Charles River halted Cambodian imports under a grand jury subpoena. Analysts projected per-animal prices of $33,000 that year against $2,500 in 2019 (as reported). Then FDA's 10 April 2025 plan to phase out animal testing requirements for monoclonal antibodies, and its December 2025 draft guidance on reducing six-month primate studies, changed what a package must contain. The contest moved from who has monkeys to who can justify not using them.

## Current Tools & Gaps
NHP colonies and import contracts at the large CROs; tissue cross-reactivity studies; in vitro cytokine-release assays; organ-on-chip systems (Emulate) and in silico tools. Gaps: no standard way to assemble a weight-of-evidence case, little public precedent on what FDA has accepted, and NAMs validated case by case.
