# A Lab Notebook for a Company With No Bench

**Niche:** [[niches/biotech-research-firms/virtual-biotechs/profile|Virtual Biotechs Running on Outsourced Labs]]
**Industry:** [[industries/biotech-research-firms|Biotech Research Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Biotech ELNs and registries are built around bench scientists entering their own experiments; a virtual company has no one at a bench, only vendors sending files.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #feature-engineering #data-integration #automation #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let a team of two to fifteen people with no lab of its own run a portfolio of outsourced studies and make go/no-go calls on data it never generated, and whoever turns every CRO's output into one decision record takes the account.

## The Problem
Virtual biotechs buy an ELN because investors expect one, then find it empty: nobody enters experiments, and CRO data arrives as attachments.

## What Already Exists
Benchling, Dotmatics and CDD Vault provide registries, assay schemas and notebooks, with import tools and APIs.

## The Customization Gap
The virtual case needs: inbound intake from CRO deliverables and portals as the main entry path; vendor sample-code mapping to the company's compound and batch registry; study objects that represent outsourced work with quotes, protocols and milestones; external collaborator access scoped per CRO; and data-room export by default.

## Target Customer
Virtual biotechs at seed to Series B already paying for an ELN they barely use.

## Impact If Solved
The tool the company already licenses becomes its actual scientific record, without hiring a data manager.
