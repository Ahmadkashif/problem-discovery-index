# A Decision Record Assembled From Other People's Data

**Niche:** [[niches/biotech-research-firms/virtual-biotechs/profile|Virtual Biotechs Running on Outsourced Labs]]
**Industry:** [[industries/biotech-research-firms|Biotech Research Firms]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A virtual biotech's every result arrives from a vendor in the vendor's format, and the company's own scientific record exists only in its CSO's head and inbox.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #seq2seq #feature-engineering #data-integration #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let a team of two to fifteen people with no lab of its own run a portfolio of outsourced studies and make go/no-go calls on data it never generated, and whoever turns every CRO's output into one decision record takes the account.

## The Problem
Five or more CROs deliver reports, workbooks and interim emails. The CSO reads each, decides whether the programme is on track, and tells the board. When the CSO leaves or a partner asks why the lead was chosen over its backup, the reasoning has to be reconstructed from files with different compound codes.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Lab software sells to labs; CRO portals show one vendor's work; and the buyer is a tiny company that vendors treat as too small to serve. Reading heterogeneous scientific reports reliably was not practical before recent language models.

## What to Build
An agent-run record: every deliverable ingested and structured against the company's compounds and studies; results compared with plan and prior data, with conflicts flagged; each go/no-go decision captured with the evidence the team relied on; and portfolio timelines updated from CRO milestones. The record, not a slide deck, becomes what the board and partners see.

## Target Customer
CEOs and CSOs of virtual biotechs; venture creators and investor platform teams running several companies.

## Impact If Built
The company owns its science as a structured asset, and a two-person team can manage a portfolio that now needs a project manager and a data scientist.
