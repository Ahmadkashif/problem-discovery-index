# The Data Room Built in the Fortnight Before a Financing

**Niche:** [[niches/biotech-research-firms/virtual-biotechs/profile|Virtual Biotechs Running on Outsourced Labs]]
**Industry:** [[industries/biotech-research-firms|Biotech Research Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Every financing or partnering process starts with the CSO spending two weeks finding, renaming and reconciling the reports that prove the science.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #quick-win #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let a team of two to fifteen people with no lab of its own run a portfolio of outsourced studies and make go/no-go calls on data it never generated, and whoever turns every CRO's output into one decision record takes the account.

## The Problem
Investors and partners ask for the data behind every claim in the deck: the potency table, the PK study, the tox range-finder, the batch analysis. The files are spread across inboxes and drives, named by the CRO, sometimes superseded by later versions.

## Why It's Still Broken
Nobody owns filing in a company of five, and data rooms are created per transaction by the bankers or lawyers, then abandoned.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A continuously maintained data room: each incoming deliverable filed by study and compound, version-checked, linked to the claims it supports in the current deck, with gaps listed.

## Who Feels the Pain
CSOs and CEOs during fundraising; investors running diligence; partners' search and evaluation teams.

## Impact If Fixed
Diligence starts on day one instead of week three, which matters when runway is measured in months.
