# GLP Quality Assurance & Audit Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/biotech-research-firms|Biotech Research Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Independent GLP auditors and small quality consultancies audit CRO facilities for sponsors, act as contract QA units for small labs, and prepare facilities for FDA inspection.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 1-15 auditors per firm
**Output:** Audit reports, QA statements, inspection readiness assessments
**Proprietary data:** Audit findings across facilities, held confidentially for each client
**Clock:** Study schedules and inspection dates
**Buyer:** Principal
