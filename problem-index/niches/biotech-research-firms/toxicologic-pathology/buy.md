# Clinical Digital Pathology Systems Fitted to GLP Studies

**Niche:** [[niches/biotech-research-firms/toxicologic-pathology/profile|Toxicologic Pathology & Histopathology Reads]]
**Industry:** [[industries/biotech-research-firms|Biotech Research Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Human diagnostic pathology already runs on validated whole-slide systems; toxicologic pathology needs species, nomenclature and GLP records those systems were not built for.
**Tags:** #cnns #object-detection #compliance #data-integration #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver a pathology read that survives peer review and FDA scrutiny on the study's timeline despite a thin bench of board-certified toxicologic pathologists, and whoever multiplies each pathologist's throughput without weakening the diagnosis takes the account.

## The Problem
CROs that scan slides still read many studies on glass because the viewer, the study system and the regulatory record do not join up, and remote pathologists need both.

## What Already Exists
Whole-slide imaging systems from Leica, Philips and others, some cleared by FDA for primary diagnosis in human pathology, with image management and analysis platforms such as Visiopharm and Indica Labs HALO.

## The Customization Gap
GLP use needs: species-specific tissue lists and the INHAND nomenclature used in toxicology; direct write-back of findings and severity to the study system and the SEND MI domain; Part 11 audit trails of what was viewed and when; a structured peer-review workflow; and validation documentation a CRO's QA unit will accept.

## Target Customer
CRO pathology and IT leads planning digital adoption; pathology groups reading remotely for several CROs.

## Impact If Solved
Remote, fully digital reads without a second record, which widens the usable pool of pathologists.
