# Drug Pipeline & Trial Intelligence Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/biotech-research-firms|Biotech Research Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
These firms track every drug in development worldwide — target, modality, indication, phase, sponsor, licensing history, trial design and results — and sell the database, analysis and forecasts by subscription. Norstella (Citeline's Pharmaprojects, Trialtrove and Biomedtracker, plus Evaluate; 1,600+ employees across five brands since its November 2022 merger), Clarivate's Cortellis, GlobalData's pharma intelligence and BioCentury lead, with smaller specialists behind them. Biotechs use them to size competition and pitch partners. Pharma business development, competitive intelligence and portfolio teams, investors and consultancies pay for enterprise licences. Citeline's count of 22,940 drugs in development at the start of 2026 is the industry's reference figure.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-800 analysts, editors and data curators per large provider (estimate)
**Output:** Drug and trial records, phase-transition and likelihood-of-approval estimates, sales forecasts, conference and readout coverage, landscape reports
**Proprietary data:** Decades of curated, dated programme histories (Pharmaprojects dates to the 1980s, recalled) with analyst-assigned status and probability calls; inputs are mostly public, but the curated longitudinal layer exists nowhere else
**Clock:** FDA PDUFA goal dates, embargo lifts for major medical conferences (ASCO, ASH, AACR, ESMO) and companies' scheduled readouts, around which subscribers expect same-day updates and calls
**Buyer:** Chief Content Officer / Head of Research

**Kill switches:** none — sources are public disclosures, registries, filings and conference abstracts, and the records and estimates are the provider's own intellectual property; no MNPI or client data enters the product. Q4 is held at 4 because a late update costs subscriber trust rather than a regulatory penalty, and Q5 at 3 because the buyer set is concentrated in a few large providers.
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. The buyer market is three or four large providers (Norstella, Clarivate, GlobalData, BioCentury), so Q5 is 2, as for the chemistry reference databases in this sweep. The ungraded record of dated approval-odds calls remains the clearest gradable asset beside the operators.
