# Chemistry & Bioactivity Reference Databases

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/biotech-research-firms|Biotech Research Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
CAS (a division of the American Chemical Society), Elsevier's Reaxys and Clarivate's Integrity abstract and index chemical substances, reactions, patents and bioactivity data from the literature. Medicinal chemists use them to check novelty, find routes and see what is known about a scaffold. Biotechs, pharma, CROs and universities pay by subscription.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Hundreds of scientist-indexers and curators at the largest producer, tens to low hundreds at the others (estimate)
**Output:** Curated substance, reaction, patent and bioactivity records; search and retrosynthesis tools
**Proprietary data:** Decades of expert-indexed chemistry that cannot be rebuilt from the public literature at the same depth
**Clock:** None external; indexing speed competes on currency, but no third party sets a deadline
**Buyer:** Chief Content Officer / VP Content Operations
