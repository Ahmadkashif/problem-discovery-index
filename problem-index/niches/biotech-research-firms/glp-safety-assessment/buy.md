**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every preclinical CRO is fighting to deliver an audit-clean IND-enabling package on the date the sponsor's IND depends on, but small-molecule and biologics programmes are won on different things — compressing the calendar across disciplines, versus securing or replacing non-human primates — so it is decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Package timelines are managed in Gantt charts that treat every duration as fixed. The real uncertainties — animal supply, dose-selection outcomes, histology backlog, peer-review changes — are known to experienced programme managers and absent from the plan.

## What Already Exists
Oracle Primavera P6 and Microsoft Project, with Monte Carlo risk add-ons such as Safran Risk, run probabilistic critical-path schedules for construction and engineering projects, where multi-trade dependencies and uncertain durations are routine.

## The Customization Gap
A toxicology package needs: a library of study templates with GLP-defined phases (acclimation, dosing, recovery, necropsy, histology, read, peer review, QA, report); dependency rules from ICH M3(R2) and S6 (which studies gate which); duration distributions per species and study type from the CRO's history; resource pools specific to labs (vivarium rooms, pathologists, bioanalytical instruments); and sponsor-visible views that exclude other clients' data.

## Target Customer
Programme management offices at mid-size and large preclinical CROs.

## Impact If Solved
Proven scheduling methods adopted in months rather than a greenfield build, with the slip risk visible weeks earlier.
