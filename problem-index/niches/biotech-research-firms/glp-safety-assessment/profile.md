# IND-Enabling GLP Safety Assessment

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/biotech-research-firms|Biotech Research Firms]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every preclinical CRO is fighting to deliver an audit-clean IND-enabling package on the date the sponsor's IND depends on, but small-molecule and biologics programmes are won on different things — compressing the calendar across disciplines, versus securing or replacing non-human primates — so it is decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$5B global outsourced regulated safety assessment spend (estimate; Charles River's whole Discovery and Safety Assessment segment, which also includes discovery services, was $2.40B in 2025)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of NAICS 541714 receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — study data is captured in validated systems such as Provantis and Pristima, but pathology is read on glass and reports are assembled by hand
**Target Buyer:** VP Nonclinical Development at the sponsor; Head of Safety Assessment at the CRO
**Automation Potential:** Medium — reporting, scheduling and data conversion automate; in-life work and pathology judgement do not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is the largest single pool of outsourced spend in the industry and the only one fully inside FDA's GLP regime (21 CFR Part 58). The deliverable is fixed by guidance — ICH M3(R2) for small molecules, ICH S6 for biologics — and the buyer's clock is its IND. Charles River, Labcorp, Inotiv, Altasciences and WuXi AppTec compete with a long tail of mid-size labs. Volumes fell in 2025 as early-stage R&D budgets tightened, and Charles River's DSA segment declined 2.6% organically.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Provantis and Pristima capture in-life and clinical pathology data, and Instem and Pinnacle 21 tools produce and validate SEND. Scheduling runs in resource planners and spreadsheets. The gaps are integrated planning across disciplines, report assembly, and a decision record that survives the move from the CRO to the sponsor's IND.
