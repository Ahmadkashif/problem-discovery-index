# Outsourced Discovery Chemistry & DMTA Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/biotech-research-firms|Biotech Research Firms]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to shorten the sponsor's design–make–test cycle — designs in on Monday, compounds made, purified and assayed within days — from a location the sponsor's partners and acquirers will accept once the BIOSECURE list is published, and whoever turns cycles fastest from an acceptable jurisdiction takes the account.

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Most discovery biotechs do not employ enough chemists to run their own design–make–test cycle, so they rent chemists by the full-time-equivalent year from CROs such as WuXi AppTec, Pharmaron, Aragen, Syngene and Sai Life Sciences, mostly in China and India. Speed of the cycle is what the sponsor buys. The BIOSECURE Act, enacted on 18 December 2025, requires a federal list of biotechnology companies of concern by 18 December 2026. The law names no company and bites first on federal contracts and grants, but partners and acquirers already ask where the chemistry was done.

## Current Tools & Gaps
The CRO's own registry and ELN, the sponsor's Benchling or Dotmatics instance, retrosynthesis tools (Synthia, CAS SciFinder, open-source ASKCOS) and weekly progress decks. The gaps are a shared view of where each design is in the cycle, early warning of failing routes, and a measured cycle time a sponsor can compare across CROs and countries.
