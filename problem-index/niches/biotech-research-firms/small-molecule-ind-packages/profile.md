# Small-Molecule IND Packages

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/biotech-research-firms|Biotech Research Firms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to compress the small-molecule IND-enabling package — dose-range finding, GLP repeat-dose studies in two species, safety pharmacology and genetic toxicology — into the fewest calendar months from candidate nomination, and whoever plans the disciplines as one critical path rather than as separate study quotes takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3B global spend on small-molecule IND-enabling safety packages (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of NAICS 541714 receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — validated data capture, manual planning and reporting
**Target Buyer:** VP Nonclinical Development or Head of Development Operations at the sponsor
**Automation Potential:** Medium-High for planning and reporting; low for in-life work

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Small-molecule packages follow ICH M3(R2): rodent and non-rodent (usually dog or minipig) repeat-dose studies matched to clinical duration, safety pharmacology core battery, and genetic toxicology. Animals are available and capacity loosened as 2025 volumes fell, so price alone no longer separates CROs. What separates them is calendar time: a package of ~9–15 months (estimate) against a sponsor whose next financing tranche is written against the IND. Integrated providers such as Altasciences and Inotiv sell speed; large networks sell breadth.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Study-level scheduling in each CRO, sponsor slides, and dose selection by toxicologist judgement from range-finder data. Gaps: no package-level critical path, test-article readiness tracked by email, and dose selection that waits for each study to finish before the next is designed.
