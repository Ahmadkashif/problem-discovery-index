**One-liner:** Pharmacometric tools already project human doses from animal data; the same machinery, pointed the other way, could set toxicology doses and exposure margins earlier.
**Tags:** #bayesian-inference #numerical-methods #feature-engineering #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to compress the small-molecule IND-enabling package — dose-range finding, GLP repeat-dose studies in two species, safety pharmacology and genetic toxicology — into the fewest calendar months from candidate nomination, and whoever plans the disciplines as one critical path rather than as separate study quotes takes the account.

## The Problem
Toxicokinetic exposure targets and safety margins are worked out in spreadsheets as each study reports. The package plan does not show, until late, whether the doses chosen will deliver the margins the clinical starting dose will need.

## What Already Exists
Certara's Phoenix and Simcyp PBPK, Simulations Plus GastroPlus and open-source pharmacometrics tools model absorption and exposure across species and are used for first-in-human dose projection.

## The Customization Gap
Toxicology use needs: species and strain parameter sets for the GLP species at toxicology doses, where saturation and solubility limits dominate; formulation constraints for high-dose suspensions; direct calculation of exposure margins against the proposed clinical dose for the Module 2.6 summaries; and a workflow that runs when the first TK samples arrive, not after the report.

## Target Customer
Toxicologists and DMPK scientists at integrated CROs and sponsor nonclinical teams.

## Impact If Solved
Exposure margins visible at dose selection rather than at reporting, which prevents a repeat study, the costliest delay in a package.
