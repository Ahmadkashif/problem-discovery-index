# The Test Article That Arrives Without Its Certificate

**Niche:** [[niches/biotech-research-firms/small-molecule-ind-packages/profile|Small-Molecule IND Packages]]
**Industry:** [[industries/biotech-research-firms|Biotech Research Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** GLP studies cannot dose a test article whose identity, purity and stability are not documented, and late batches and missing certificates of analysis are the commonest reason a package slips before it starts.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #compliance #workflow-orchestration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to compress the small-molecule IND-enabling package — dose-range finding, GLP repeat-dose studies in two species, safety pharmacology and genetic toxicology — into the fewest calendar months from candidate nomination, and whoever plans the disciplines as one critical path rather than as separate study quotes takes the account.

## The Problem
The GLP batch of drug substance comes from a chemistry CRO or CDMO, ships to the tox lab, and must arrive with characterisation the study can cite: certificate of analysis, purity, stability for the dosing period, and formulation analysis. A batch that slips, or arrives with a certificate missing a test, holds every study that depends on it.

## Why It's Still Broken
Test-article logistics sit between the sponsor's CMC lead, the supplier, the CRO's test article control unit and the study director, coordinated by email. No one owns a single readiness view, and requirements are checked when the batch arrives rather than when it is ordered.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A test-article readiness tracker shared by sponsor and CRO: each study's required documents and quantities, the supplier's batch schedule, automated reading of incoming certificates against the requirements, and alerts weeks before a gap blocks dosing.

## Who Feels the Pain
Study directors waiting to dose; sponsor programme managers explaining slips to their boards; CRO schedulers holding empty rooms.

## Impact If Fixed
Avoidable pre-study delays caught while they can still be fixed, protecting the calendar this niche competes on.
