# FDA Nonclinical Review & GLP Inspection

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/biotech-research-firms|Biotech Research Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Pharmacology/toxicology reviewers in FDA's drug and biologics review offices assess the nonclinical package in every IND within the 30-day window before it takes effect, and in every marketing application later. FDA's bioresearch monitoring inspectors audit GLP test facilities for compliance with 21 CFR Part 58. Since April 2025 the agency has also been writing the policy that replaces animal studies with non-animal methods. The work is funded by appropriations and user fees, not sold.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Hundreds of pharmacology/toxicology reviewers and GLP inspectors across the review offices (estimate)
**Output:** IND safety reviews, clinical hold decisions, advice at pre-IND meetings, approval-package reviews, GLP inspection reports, guidance
**Proprietary data:** Every nonclinical package ever submitted, with review correspondence and inspection history, the complete record the consulting layer infers from public summaries
**Clock:** The statutory 30-day IND review and PDUFA review goals
**Buyer:** Office director; federal procurement
