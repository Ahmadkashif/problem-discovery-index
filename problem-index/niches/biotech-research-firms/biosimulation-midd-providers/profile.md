# Biosimulation & Model-Informed Drug Development Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/biotech-research-firms|Biotech Research Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Certara, Simulations Plus and specialist pharmacometrics firms sell modelling software and the modelling itself: PBPK predictions of drug interactions and first-in-human doses, PK/PD analyses, and the modelling sections of regulatory submissions. Biotechs without pharmacometricians buy the analysis as a service. Certara reported FY2025 revenue of $418.8M, $235.6M of it from services.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-700 modellers, pharmacometricians and regulatory scientists per large provider (estimate)
**Output:** First-in-human dose projections, PBPK and PK/PD model reports, regulatory modelling summaries
**Proprietary data:** Virtual population and compound libraries built from literature and consortium work; most project data is the sponsor's
**Clock:** Mostly sponsor schedules for IND and FDA meetings; agency information requests occasionally impose dates
**Buyer:** President of Services / Head of Drug Development Solutions
