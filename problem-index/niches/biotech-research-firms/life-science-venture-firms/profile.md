# Life-Science Venture Firms & Venture Creation Studios

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/biotech-research-firms|Biotech Research Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Specialist venture firms such as ARCH, Third Rock and Atlas, and venture creators such as Flagship Pioneering, found and fund biotechs. Several run many companies from a shared platform team. They are the industry's main source of capital: global biopharma venture funding was $26B across 435 rounds in 2025 (DealForma). Their scientific diligence and portfolio analytics are monetised through equity returns.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 scientific and investment staff per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Investment memos, company theses, portfolio operating support
**Proprietary data:** Full data access across their own portfolio companies, plus diligence material from companies they passed on
**Clock:** Fund cycles and portfolio financing rounds; self-set
**Buyer:** Managing Partner
