**Industry:** [[industries/biotech-research-firms|Biotech Research Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Regulatory information management systems already track every submission, commitment and agency interaction; they record what happened, not what it teaches about the next programme.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #word-embeddings #compliance #data-integration #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this pocket is fighting to tell a sponsor, before the pre-IND package is due, exactly which nonclinical studies FDA will require for this modality and indication — and whoever can back that answer with what FDA actually accepted for comparable programmes wins the engagement.

## The Problem
Consultancies and sponsors log submissions and FDA correspondence for tracking and compliance. The content of each interaction — the question asked, FDA's answer, whether the sponsor's position held — sits in attached PDFs that nobody searches when the next similar programme arrives.

## What Already Exists
Veeva Vault RIM and similar regulatory information management platforms manage submission content, registration status, correspondence and commitments across products and markets.

## The Customization Gap
Strategy use needs: question-level structuring of meeting packages and FDA responses (topic, modality, the sponsor's proposal, FDA's position, outcome); a taxonomy of nonclinical questions aligned to ICH guidance sections; de-identification so lessons transfer between clients without disclosing them; and retrieval inside the drafting workflow, at the moment a consultant writes the next question.

## Target Customer
Consultancies and CRO regulatory groups already licensing a RIM platform; sponsor regulatory teams with several programmes.

## Impact If Solved
Existing correspondence archives become usable precedent without a new system of record.
