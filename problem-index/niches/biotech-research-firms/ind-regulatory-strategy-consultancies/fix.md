# FDA's Answers Filed and Forgotten

**Niche:** [[niches/biotech-research-firms/ind-regulatory-strategy-consultancies/profile|Nonclinical & IND Regulatory Strategy Consultancies]]
**Industry:** [[industries/biotech-research-firms|Biotech Research Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Every pre-IND meeting ends with FDA's written answers to the firm's questions, and those answers go into the client folder, never to be compared with the next one.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #compliance #quick-win #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this pocket is fighting to tell a sponsor, before the pre-IND package is due, exactly which nonclinical studies FDA will require for this modality and indication — and whoever can back that answer with what FDA actually accepted for comparable programmes wins the engagement.

## The Problem
A consultancy may run dozens of pre-IND interactions a year (estimate). FDA's preliminary responses and minutes show where the agency agreed, where it asked for more, and how it treated a reduced package. The knowledge stays with the consultant who attended, and leaves when they do.

## Why It's Still Broken
Client confidentiality makes firms cautious about reuse, and recording outcomes in a structured way is unbilled work after the engagement has ended.

## What a Fix Looks Like
After each interaction, a short structured outcome record drafted from the minutes and confirmed by the consultant: question type, modality, proposal, FDA's position, and outcome, stripped of client identifiers. The records become searchable precedent for the next package.

## Who Feels the Pain
Senior consultants who are the only memory; junior staff writing questions without precedent; clients paying for rediscovery.

## Impact If Fixed
The firm's own experience becomes an asset that survives staff turnover, at the cost of a few minutes per meeting.
