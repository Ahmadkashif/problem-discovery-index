# Nonclinical & IND Regulatory Strategy Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/biotech-research-firms|Biotech Research Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this pocket is fighting to tell a sponsor, before the pre-IND package is due, exactly which nonclinical studies FDA will require for this modality and indication — and whoever can back that answer with what FDA actually accepted for comparable programmes wins the engagement.

## What They Do
Regulatory consultancies design a biotech's path to its first IND: which nonclinical studies the package needs under ICH M3(R2), S6 or S9, which questions to put to FDA, and how to argue for reduced or non-animal testing. They write the pre-IND briefing package and the nonclinical sections of the IND, and prepare the sponsor for the meeting. Biologics Consulting (65+ regulatory consultants, founded by former FDA officers), ProPharma, Parexel's consulting arm, Certara's regulatory science group and many smaller firms compete. Biotechs pay by the project or the hour. Most biotechs have no in-house regulatory staff before their first IND, so they buy the strategy itself.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-60 regulatory scientists, toxicologists and medical writers in the nonclinical and early-development practice of each larger firm (estimate; Biologics Consulting's checked 65+ consultants span all specialties)
**Output:** Nonclinical development plans, pre-IND briefing packages and question lists, eCTD Module 2.4/2.6 summaries, FDA meeting preparation, responses to clinical holds and information requests
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated precedent of what FDA asked and accepted across hundreds of programmes, held as the firm's experience; built on the public record of FDA pharmacology/toxicology reviews, guidance and published cases
**Clock:** FDA cancels or reschedules a pre-IND meeting if the package arrives less than 30 days before it — a deadline the agency sets on the consultancy's own deliverable
**Buyer:** Practice leader / Managing Director, Regulatory Sciences
