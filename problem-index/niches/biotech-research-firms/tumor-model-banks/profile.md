# Translational Tumour Model Banks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/biotech-research-firms|Biotech Research Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Champions Oncology, Crown Bioscience and The Jackson Laboratory maintain banks of patient-derived xenograft and organoid models, each characterised by genomics and by its response to standard drugs. They sell efficacy studies in those models to oncology biotechs, and some license the characterisation data itself.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 scientists per provider (estimate)
**Output:** Efficacy study reports, model selection advice, licensed characterisation datasets
**Proprietary data:** Banked models with genomic profiles and baseline drug-response data, which the provider owns; results of sponsor-funded studies belong to the sponsor
**Clock:** Sponsors' programme decisions and financing timelines; none external
**Buyer:** Chief Scientific Officer / VP Translational Sciences
