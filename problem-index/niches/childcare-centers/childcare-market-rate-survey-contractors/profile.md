# Childcare Market Rate Survey Contractors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/childcare-centers|Childcare Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Federal childcare subsidy rules require every state to establish payment rates on evidence, so states commission market rate surveys and alternative cost-of-care studies from university research centres and specialist consultancies. Researchers survey providers, model the price distribution by care type and region, and deliver a report that sets what subsidy pays. The report is the invoice and it determines billions in payments.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 survey researchers, economists, and analysts per contractor
**Output:** Market rate survey reports and cost estimation models — the contracted deliverable
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated provider price and cost data across states and survey waves, plus the record of which methodologies federal reviewers accepted
**Clock:** Statutory — surveys are required on a fixed cycle and feed state plan submission deadlines
**Buyer:** Principal investigator / Director of the research centre
