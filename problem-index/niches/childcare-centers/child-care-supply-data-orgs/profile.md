# National Childcare Supply & Cost Data Organizations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/childcare-centers|Childcare Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Child Care Aware of America and comparable bodies aggregate state-level provider, capacity, and price data into the national reporting that policymakers and the press use to describe childcare cost and childcare deserts. Output is published to influence policy rather than sold.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 researchers, data analysts, and policy staff
**Output:** National cost and supply reports, childcare desert analyses, state fact sheets
**Proprietary data:** Aggregated state and referral-agency provider data assembled over years
**Clock:** Annual publication schedule and legislative sessions
**Buyer:** VP of Research / Chief Policy Officer
