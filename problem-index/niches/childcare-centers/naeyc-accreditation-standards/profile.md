# Early Childhood Accreditation & Standards Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/childcare-centers|Childcare Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
NAEYC accredits several thousand early childhood programmes against its own standards, publishes the position statements that define developmentally appropriate practice, and maintains the professional standards states build licensure and quality systems around. Programmes pay for accreditation; states and funders treat it as a quality marker.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-120 standards staff, assessors, and researchers
**Output:** Standards, accreditation decisions, position statements, aggregate quality findings
**Proprietary data:** Assessment findings across thousands of accredited programmes over multiple cycles
**Clock:** Accreditation cycles and standards revision schedules
**Buyer:** VP of Accreditation / Chief Programme Officer
