# Early Childhood Curriculum Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/childcare-centers|Childcare Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Teaching Strategies' Creative Curriculum, HighScope, Frog Street, and Big Day publish the curricula that centres teach from, along with the professional development that accompanies them. Content teams design learning sequences, align them to state early learning standards, and maintain the evidence base states require for approved-curriculum lists.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-120 curriculum designers, early childhood specialists, and standards analysts
**Output:** Curricula, standards alignment documentation, efficacy evidence
**Proprietary data:** Standards alignment mappings across fifty states, plus accumulated implementation and professional development records
**Clock:** State early learning standards revisions and adoption cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Academic Officer / VP of Curriculum
