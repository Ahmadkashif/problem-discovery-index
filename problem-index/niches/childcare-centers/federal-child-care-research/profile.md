# Federal Early Childhood Research Offices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/childcare-centers|Childcare Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Office of Planning, Research and Evaluation within ACF designs and oversees the national studies that define what is known about childcare supply, subsidy use, and programme effects, and administers the data collections states report into.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-120 researchers and programme officers, plus contractors
**Output:** National studies, administrative data collections, evaluation reports
**Proprietary data:** Federal administrative data on subsidy receipt and provider participation across every state
**Clock:** Congressional reporting requirements and study milestones
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer
