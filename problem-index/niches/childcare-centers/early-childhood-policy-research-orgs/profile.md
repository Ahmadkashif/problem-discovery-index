# Early Childhood Policy Research Organizations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/childcare-centers|Childcare Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Child Trends, NIEER, the Urban Institute's early childhood group, and comparable research nonprofits conduct the commissioned studies that shape childcare policy — programme evaluations, state system analyses, workforce studies. Federal agencies, states, and foundations commission the work and the report is the deliverable.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 researchers, methodologists, and analysts
**Output:** Commissioned evaluation and policy reports
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated methodology and study history; the underlying administrative and survey data is held under data use agreements rather than owned
**Clock:** Contract deliverable dates, legislative sessions, and federal reporting cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Research / Programme Area Director
