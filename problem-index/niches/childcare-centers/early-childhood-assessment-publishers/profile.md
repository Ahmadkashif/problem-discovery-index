# Early Childhood Assessment Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/childcare-centers|Childcare Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Teaching Strategies, HighScope, Curriculum Associates, and Brookes publish the observational assessment systems that most state pre-K and Head Start programmes run on. Teachers record what children can do against developmental progressions several times a year; the publisher's researchers design the progressions, establish validity and reliability, maintain the norms, and produce the aggregate reporting states use for accountability. The assessment system is licensed per child, so the research is the product.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-200 developmental researchers, psychometricians, and content specialists
**Output:** Assessment systems, developmental progressions, validity and norming studies, state accountability reporting
**Proprietary data:** Longitudinal developmental observation data on millions of children across years and states — the largest early childhood dataset in existence and one no research body could assemble
**Clock:** Checkpoint periods, state accountability reporting deadlines, and Head Start reporting cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Research / Chief Academic Officer
