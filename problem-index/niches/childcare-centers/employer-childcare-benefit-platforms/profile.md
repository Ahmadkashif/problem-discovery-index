# Employer Childcare Benefit Platform Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/childcare-centers|Childcare Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Bright Horizons, Vivvi, Kinside, and the back-up care providers contract with employers to deliver childcare benefits. They compete on demonstrated retention and productivity impact, so their analytics teams produce the utilization and ROI reporting that employers renew against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 analysts, researchers, and client insight staff
**Output:** Utilization and ROI reports, workforce impact research, network capacity analyses
**Proprietary data:** Employee childcare utilization joined to employer retention and absence outcomes across hundreds of employers
**Clock:** Benefits plan year renewals and open enrolment cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Client Analytics / Chief Research Officer
