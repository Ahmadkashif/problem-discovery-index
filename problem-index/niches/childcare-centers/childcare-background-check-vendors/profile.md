# Childcare Background Check & Screening Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/childcare-centers|Childcare Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Federal law requires comprehensive multi-jurisdiction background checks on every childcare worker, renewed on a fixed cycle. Screening vendors and state-contracted processors run fingerprint submissions, sex offender registry checks across states, and child abuse registry searches, and issue the clearance determination that lets a person work with children.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-800 screening analysts, adjudicators, and compliance staff
**Output:** Clearance determinations and adjudication decisions
**Proprietary data:** Adjudication history across millions of screenings and every state's idiosyncratic registry structure and disqualification rules
**Clock:** Statutory — a worker cannot begin unsupervised work before clearance, and renewal is on a fixed cycle
**Buyer:** VP of Operations / Chief Compliance Officer
