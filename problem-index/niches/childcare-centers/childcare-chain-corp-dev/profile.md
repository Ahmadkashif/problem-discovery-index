# Childcare Chain Corporate Development

## What They Do
The development and analytics groups at KinderCare, Learning Care Group, Primrose, and the private equity platforms consolidating centres. They run site selection against demographic and demand modelling, screen acquisitions, and benchmark centre-level enrolment and margin.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 analysts across development, real estate, and operations
**Output:** Site selection models, acquisition screens, centre performance analytics
**Proprietary data:** Centre-level enrolment, staffing, and margin data across hundreds or thousands of locations
**Clock:** Development pipeline and deal timelines
**Buyer:** SVP Development / VP Business Analytics
