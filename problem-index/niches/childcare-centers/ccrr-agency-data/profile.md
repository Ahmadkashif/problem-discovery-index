# Child Care Resource & Referral Agencies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/childcare-centers|Childcare Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
CCR&R agencies maintain the local provider database, refer families to care, deliver provider training, and supply the provider counts and price data that state and national reporting is built from. They are the only party holding a current picture of local childcare supply.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 3-15 per agency, with larger state networks
**Output:** Provider databases, referral data, local supply and price reporting
**Proprietary data:** Current provider listings, capacity, and price at local level — genuinely unique and assembled by hand
**Clock:** State contract reporting requirements
**Buyer:** Executive Director
