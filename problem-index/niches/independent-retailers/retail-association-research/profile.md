# Retail Association Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-retailers|Independent Retailers]]

## What They Do
The retail associations publish sales forecasts, consumer spending surveys, and the loss prevention and policy research their membership cites.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 economists, researchers, and policy analysts
**Output:** Retail sales forecasts, consumer surveys, shrink and loss prevention studies, policy analysis
**Proprietary data:** Member survey series and contributed shrink data
**Clock:** Holiday forecast cycles and legislative sessions
**Buyer:** VP of Research — member funded
