# Retail Crime Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-retailers|Independent Retailers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Retail crime intelligence units and the industry-wide organized retail crime associations link theft incidents across retailers, identify organized groups and fencing operations, and build the cases law enforcement prosecutes.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-200 intelligence analysts, investigators, and data staff
**Output:** Incident linkage and case packages, organized group profiles, trend and hot-spot analysis, law enforcement referrals
**Proprietary data:** Incident and suspect data contributed across many retailers — the only cross-retailer view of theft that individual stores cannot see
**Clock:** Investigation windows and prosecution timelines
**Buyer:** VP of Asset Protection / Director of Intelligence
