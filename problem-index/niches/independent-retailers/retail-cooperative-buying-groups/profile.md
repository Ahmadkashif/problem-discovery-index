# Retail Cooperative Buying Groups

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-retailers|Independent Retailers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup

## What They Do
The hardlines and specialty retail co-ops aggregate purchasing for thousands of independent stores, negotiate with vendors on their behalf, and run category management and assortment planning functions that give member stores something close to chain-level merchandising.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 category managers, retail analysts, and data staff
**Output:** Assortment recommendations, planograms, pricing guidance, member performance benchmarking
**Proprietary data:** Point-of-sale and purchase data from thousands of independent stores across many local markets — the only assembled view of what independent retail actually sells
**Clock:** Seasonal buying calendars and vendor negotiation cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Merchandising / Chief Data Officer
