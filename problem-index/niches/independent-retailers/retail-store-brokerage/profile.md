# Retail Store Brokerage

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-retailers|Independent Retailers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Business brokers value and sell independent retail stores, pricing them on inventory, lease terms, and the owner's own trading history.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 2-8 brokers and analysts
**Output:** Valuations, offering materials, buyer matching
**Proprietary data:** Transaction comparables in a private, thinly traded market
**Clock:** Transaction timelines
**Buyer:** Principal
