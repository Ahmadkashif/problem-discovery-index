# Retail Leasing Market Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-retailers|Independent Retailers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Retail real estate research groups analyze trade areas, tenant mix, and rent comparables for landlords and retailers deciding where to lease — the decision that determines an independent store's foot traffic for the length of its lease.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 researchers, analysts, and geographers
**Output:** Trade area analyses, rent and vacancy comparables, tenant mix studies, market reports
**Proprietary data:** Lease comparables and tenant performance in a market with limited public transaction data
**Clock:** Lease negotiation and quarterly market reporting
**Buyer:** Head of Research
