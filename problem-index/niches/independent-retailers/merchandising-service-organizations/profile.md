# Merchandising Service Organizations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-retailers|Independent Retailers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Acosta, Advantage, and the retail service agencies put field representatives into stores on behalf of brands — resetting shelves, checking compliance with planograms, auditing pricing and stock — and report what they found back to the manufacturer.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 analysts and reporting staff above a very large field workforce
**Output:** Store condition audits, planogram compliance reporting, out-of-stock and pricing observations
**Proprietary data:** Direct physical observation of shelf conditions across hundreds of thousands of store visits — ground truth that no point-of-sale feed contains
**Clock:** Reset calendars and promotional windows
**Buyer:** Chief Analytics Officer / VP of Client Solutions
