# Weights, Measures & Retail Consumer Protection

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-retailers|Independent Retailers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State and county weights and measures programmes inspect scales, scanners, and price accuracy at retail, verifying that what a customer is charged matches what is advertised.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 inspectors and analysts per jurisdiction
**Output:** Inspection findings, device certifications, price verification results, enforcement actions
**Proprietary data:** Price accuracy and device compliance records by establishment
**Clock:** Inspection cycles
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer
