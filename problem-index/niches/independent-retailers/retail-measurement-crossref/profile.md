# Retail Measurement Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-retailers|Independent Retailers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
The syndicated retail trackers measure sell-through at the point of sale across retail and sell share and category movement to manufacturers. Their panels are built from chain retailers, which is why the 500,000 independent establishments in this industry are largely absent from the measurement everyone else plans against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-1,000+ analysts, data engineers, and client service staff
**Output:** Share and volume reporting, category movement, new item tracking
**Proprietary data:** Retailer point-of-sale feeds under long-term contracts, plus panel and modelling layers
**Clock:** Weekly and monthly reporting cadence
**Buyer:** VP of Data Products
