# Commercial Credit Bureaus

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-retailers|Independent Retailers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Dun & Bradstreet, Experian Business, Equifax Commercial, and Creditsafe collect trade payment experience from suppliers and turn it into the credit scores and reports that decide whether a business gets terms, how much, and at what price. Pass 1 describes independent retail buying across dozens of wholesale reps and trade shows; every one of those relationships is opened on the strength of one of these files.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-3,000 data analysts, statisticians, entity resolution specialists, and researchers
**Output:** Credit scores and risk ratings, business credit reports, portfolio monitoring, supplier risk assessment
**Proprietary data:** Trade payment experience contributed by thousands of suppliers under reciprocity, joined to a business identity graph covering millions of establishments — a network no new entrant can assemble
**Clock:** Credit decisions at point of application, and continuous portfolio monitoring with alerting
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / SVP of Analytics
