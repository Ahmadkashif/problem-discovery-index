# Trade Credit Insurance Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-retailers|Independent Retailers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Trade credit insurers underwrite the risk that a supplier's customers will not pay, setting credit limits on individual retailers and covering the receivable — which means their limit decisions determine how much stock an independent store can buy on terms.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 credit analysts, underwriters, and risk staff
**Output:** Buyer credit limits, risk gradings, portfolio monitoring, claims analysis
**Proprietary data:** Payment and default experience on individual buyers across many suppliers, plus realized claim outcomes
**Clock:** Limit decisions on demand and continuous portfolio review
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
