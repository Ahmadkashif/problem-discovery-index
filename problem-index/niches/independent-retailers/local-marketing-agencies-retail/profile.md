**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-retailers|Independent Retailers]]

## What They Do
Agencies run local acquisition campaigns for independent retailers on budgets Pass 1 puts at $500-2,000 a month, coordinating search, social, and local listings for owners who have neither the time nor the expertise.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 media buyers, analysts, and account staff
**Output:** Campaign performance reporting, local audience targeting, listings and reputation management
**Proprietary data:** Creative and conversion results across many local markets and retail categories
**Clock:** Seasonal retail calendars
**Buyer:** Agency principal
