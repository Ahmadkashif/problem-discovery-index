# Retail POS Platform Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-retailers|Independent Retailers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The data groups at Shopify, Square, Lightspeed, and their peers process transactions and hold inventory data for a large share of independent retail — including, per Pass 1, the analytics and inventory modules most of their customers pay for and never open.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-800 data scientists, analysts, and product data staff
**Output:** In-product reporting, inventory and reorder features, merchant lending underwriting, commerce trend research
**Proprietary data:** Item-level sales and inventory across hundreds of thousands of independent merchants, by category and local market
**Clock:** Seasonal buying and daily trading
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Product
