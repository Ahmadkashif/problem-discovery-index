# Wholesale Trade Show Operators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-retailers|Independent Retailers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Trade show and wholesale marketplace operators bring independent buyers and vendors together, and see order intent across categories months before product reaches a shelf.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 category, data, and content staff
**Output:** Buyer-vendor matching, category trend reporting, show planning and floor layout
**Proprietary data:** Order and buyer interest data across thousands of independent retailers and vendors, ahead of season
**Clock:** Show calendars and buying seasons
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Head of Content
