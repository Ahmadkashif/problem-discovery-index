# Media & Communications Subscriber Data Trackers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wired-broadband-providers|Wired Broadband Providers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms that track broadband, video and voice subscribers, pricing and footprints for every operator and sell the series and analysis to operators, investors, vendors and regulators. S&P Global Market Intelligence's Kagan unit is the largest; Leichtman Research Group publishes the industry's quarterly net-add tallies with a very small team. Buyers are strategy, investor relations and planning teams at operators and equipment vendors, and investors.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-50 media and communications analysts at Kagan (estimate); under 5 at Leichtman Research Group
**Output:** Subscriber and revenue databases by operator and market, forecasts, pricing trackers and reports
**Proprietary data:** Decades of operator-level subscriber estimates and system-level history, modelled largely from public filings and disclosures
**Clock:** Operators' quarterly earnings dates, after which every series is updated; the deadline is self-imposed rather than owed to anyone
**Buyer:** Head of research at the data vendor
