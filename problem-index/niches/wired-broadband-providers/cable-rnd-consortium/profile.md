# Cable R&D Consortium

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wired-broadband-providers|Wired Broadband Providers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
CableLabs, the cable industry's member-funded research and development consortium, writes the DOCSIS specifications and the proactive network maintenance methods operators use, runs interoperability testing and publishes technical research; SCTE, now part of CableLabs, trains and certifies the workforce. Member operators fund it through dues.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Over 100 engineers and researchers (estimate)
**Output:** Specifications, reference methods, technical papers, interoperability events and training
**Proprietary data:** Test results and member-contributed technical data
**Clock:** Self-set specification and event calendars
**Buyer:** Chief technology officer
