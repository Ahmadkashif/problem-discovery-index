# Rural Telco Cost-Study & Universal Service Consultants

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wired-broadband-providers|Wired Broadband Providers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation firms

## What They Do
Specialist firms that prepare the cost studies, separations, universal service and tariff filings rural local exchange carriers depend on: annual cost studies for high-cost support, FCC Form 481 filings, A-CAM and legacy support elections, and state commission reports. JSI files Form 481 reports for rural carriers (its filings appear in North Dakota PSC dockets); regional accounting firms with telecom practices and a few boutiques make up the rest. Carriers pay per study and on retainer.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-60 cost and regulatory analysts per specialist firm (estimate)
**Output:** Cost studies, support calculations, Form 481 and tariff filings, regulatory strategy
**Proprietary data:** Years of client carriers' cost studies and support outcomes, held per client under confidentiality
**Clock:** FCC and USAC filing dates, such as the annual Form 481 deadline and cost-study submission dates
**Buyer:** Practice leader at the consultancy
