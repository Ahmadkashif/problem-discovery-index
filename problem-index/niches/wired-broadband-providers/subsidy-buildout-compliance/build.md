# A Location Evidence Ledger Across Every Programme

**Niche:** [[niches/wired-broadband-providers/subsidy-buildout-compliance/profile|Subsidy & Buildout Compliance]]
**Industry:** [[industries/wired-broadband-providers|Wired Broadband Providers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A rural carrier's subsidised locations sit under four or five programmes with different rules and clocks, and no system holds one record per location with the evidence each programme will ask for.
**Tags:** #k-nearest-neighbors #large-language-models #time-series-forecasting #compliance #data-integration #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (regulatory consultants, compliance and grants software vendors and carriers' own regulatory staff) is fighting to prove, location by location, that each subsidised address was served on time at the required speed with evidence USAC verification and state BEAD audits accept, and whoever produces that evidence best takes the account.

## The Problem
A compliance manager answers, for each programme, which committed locations are served, at what speed, since when, with what proof, and whether the next milestone will be met. The answers come from billing, construction trackers, field photos and speed tests, reconciled by hand for each filing.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Each programme has its own portal and format, so vendors build for one (usually grants accounting) or none. The buyers are small and many, and the consultants who do the work by hand have no reason to productise it.

## What to Build
One ledger keyed by Fabric location ID: every programme obligation on that location, its required speed and milestone, construction status, install date, speed-test and photo evidence pulled from operating systems, and a forecast of milestone attainment from the construction pipeline. It generates each programme's upload or report, flags duplicated funding, and warns months ahead when a milestone is at risk so construction can be re-sequenced.

## Target Customer
Rural telcos, co-ops and fibre ISPs carrying two or more subsidy programmes; BEAD subgrantees facing their first milestones; consultants serving them.

## Impact If Built
Milestones stop being missed by surprise, evidence requests are answered in hours, and support isn't withheld for paperwork reasons.
