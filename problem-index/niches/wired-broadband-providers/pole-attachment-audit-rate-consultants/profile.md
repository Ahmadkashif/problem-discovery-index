# Pole Attachment Audit & Rate Consultants

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wired-broadband-providers|Wired Broadband Providers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation firms

## What They Do
Firms that inventory attachments on utility poles for joint-use audits and the economists who testify on pole attachment rates and make-ready cost disputes before the FCC and state commissions. The FCC's 2025 pole attachment order took effect May 8, 2026, changing timelines for large orders and adding self-help rights. Make-ready engineering for fibre builds is covered under the contractor's [[niches/utility-contractors/telecom-fiber/profile|Telecom & Fiber]] niche.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Field audit crews (production headcount); rate experts are individuals or teams under 10
**Output:** Attachment inventories; rate testimony and complaint support
**Proprietary data:** Pole inventories collected for clients
**Clock:** Audit cycles; complaint and hearing schedules
**Buyer:** Firm principal
