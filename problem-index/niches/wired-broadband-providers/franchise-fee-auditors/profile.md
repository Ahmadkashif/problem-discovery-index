# Cable Franchise Fee Auditors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wired-broadband-providers|Wired Broadband Providers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation firms

## What They Do
Small consultancies hired by cities and counties to audit cable operators' franchise fee payments, negotiate franchise renewals and review customer-service compliance under local franchise agreements.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 2-10 per firm
**Output:** Franchise fee audits, renewal studies and needs assessments
**Proprietary data:** Audit files held for client municipalities
**Clock:** Franchise renewal dates and audit periods
**Buyer:** Firm principal
