# Broadband Trade Association Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wired-broadband-providers|Wired Broadband Providers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Trade associations that publish member surveys and industry research: NTCA's annual Broadband/Internet Availability Survey (201 responding members in 2025, run with Industry Insights), the Fiber Broadband Association's homes-passed studies, and NCTA's industry data.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research staff each, with surveys often outsourced
**Output:** Annual surveys and policy research
**Proprietary data:** Member survey responses
**Clock:** Self-set; timed to policy debates
**Buyer:** Association chief executive
