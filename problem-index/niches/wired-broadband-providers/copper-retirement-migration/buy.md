# Smart-Meter Rollout Tools Adapted to Copper Migration

**Niche:** [[niches/wired-broadband-providers/copper-retirement-migration/profile|Copper Retirement & Customer Migration]]
**Industry:** [[industries/wired-broadband-providers|Wired Broadband Providers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Electric and gas utilities have run mass, deadline-driven premises swap campaigns for smart meters; copper retirement is the same shape of campaign with harder customer consent.
**Tags:** #optimization-fundamentals #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #automation #descriptive-statistics
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (migration programme integrators, field-service contractors and telcos' own transformation offices) is fighting to move each remaining copper line, attached device included, onto fibre or a working substitute before the wire center's retirement date, and whoever completes those migrations with the fewest lost lines and failed devices takes the account.

## The Problem
Telcos plan migrations as individual orders and phone campaigns, without campaign tooling for appointment windows, access problems and repeat attempts.

## What Already Exists
Utility AMI deployment and workforce tools (deployment management modules in field service platforms and installer-run campaign systems) that schedule millions of premises visits, manage customer notifications and refusals, track "unable to complete" reasons and report progress against a regulatory schedule.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: migration paths per line (fibre install, fixed wireless, voice substitute) instead of one swap; device discovery for alarm, elevator, fax and medical lines; customer consent and a choice of substitutes rather than a mandated meter; FCC discontinuance notice requirements per wire center; and churn tracking, because a utility customer cannot leave and a telco customer can.

## Target Customer
Telco network transformation leads and the field service contractors who run migrations for them.

## Impact If Solved
Mature campaign tooling cuts failed visits and missed deadlines without a bespoke build.
