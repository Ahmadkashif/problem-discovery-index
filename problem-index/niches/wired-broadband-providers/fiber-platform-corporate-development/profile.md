# Fibre & Cable Platform Corporate Development

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wired-broadband-providers|Wired Broadband Providers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The corporate development, market planning and integration teams at consolidating operators and investor-backed fibre platforms (for example Brightspeed, Ziply, Metronet and Lumos), and at large operators buying them. They decide which markets to build or buy, model passings, take rates and overbuild risk, and integrate acquired systems.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-40 analysts per platform (estimate)
**Output:** Market-selection models, acquisition cases and integration plans for internal use
**Proprietary data:** Their own take-rate curves by market and competitive situation
**Clock:** Deal timetables set with counterparties and lenders
**Buyer:** Chief strategy officer
