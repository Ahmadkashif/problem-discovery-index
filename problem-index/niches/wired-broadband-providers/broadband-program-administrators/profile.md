# Broadband Programme Administrators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wired-broadband-providers|Wired Broadband Providers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The public bodies that decide where subsidies go and check that they were earned: NTIA's BEAD office, the 56 state and territorial broadband offices running subgrants and challenge processes, USAC's high-cost programme (location uploads, verification) and the FCC's BDC and enforcement staff. They analyse availability claims, adjudicate challenges, select subgrantees and monitor milestones. This pocket covers USAC's high-cost side; its contribution audits and Lifeline programme integrity are [[niches/telecom-resellers/usac-program-integrity/profile|USAC Contribution Audits & Lifeline Program Integrity]] (36) under telecom resellers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-40 analysts per state broadband office, more at USAC and the FCC (estimate)
**Output:** Eligibility maps, challenge decisions, subgrant awards, milestone verification and enforcement
**Proprietary data:** Applications, challenge evidence, provider milestone reports and verification samples
**Clock:** Statutory and NTIA deadlines, such as BEAD's four-year deployment requirement and FCC filing windows
**Buyer:** State broadband director or programme head
