# Co-op Member Systems Extended to Broadband Operations

**Niche:** [[niches/wired-broadband-providers/coop-and-municipal-entrants/profile|Electric Co-op & Municipal Fibre Entrants]]
**Industry:** [[industries/wired-broadband-providers|Wired Broadband Providers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Co-ops already run member billing, call centres and outage tools for electric service; broadband needs the same member relationship with very different service operations.
**Tags:** #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #large-language-models #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor serving this segment is fighting to run a first-time operator's network, billing and care up to the take rate its loan or bond model assumed, and whoever gets a new entrant to that take rate inside its first three years becomes its long-term operating partner.

## The Problem
Members expect one account, one bill and one phone number. The co-op's systems handle meters and rates, not serviceability checks, install appointments, ONT activation or Wi-Fi support.

## What Already Exists
Co-op enterprise platforms (NISC and peers) covering member billing, customer information, call handling, outage management and mapping for electric service, with broadband billing modules.

## The Customization Gap
Broadband needs: address serviceability tied to the fibre build phase; online sign-up and install scheduling; provisioning integration with OLTs and ONTs; tier-1 internet and Wi-Fi support scripts; device telemetry in the care screen; BDC filing data drawn from the same records; and take-rate reporting by phase for the board.

## Target Customer
Electric co-ops already on a member-system platform that are launching fibre subsidiaries.

## Impact If Solved
A new broadband service rides on systems and staff the co-op already has, shortening time to first revenue and avoiding a second, unfamiliar platform.
