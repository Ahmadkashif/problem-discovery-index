# Pre-Registrations That Never Convert

**Niche:** [[niches/wired-broadband-providers/coop-and-municipal-entrants/profile|Electric Co-op & Municipal Fibre Entrants]]
**Industry:** [[industries/wired-broadband-providers|Wired Broadband Providers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Members sign up for interest months before fibre reaches them, and when it does, many never complete an order because no one follows up at the right time.
**Tags:** #logistic-regression #gradient-boosting #automation #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor serving this segment is fighting to run a first-time operator's network, billing and care up to the take rate its loan or bond model assumed, and whoever gets a new entrant to that take rate inside its first three years becomes its long-term operating partner.

## The Problem
A pre-registration list is the entrant's best demand signal. By the time construction reaches a street a year later, contact details are stale, enthusiasm has faded and some members have signed with fixed wireless.

## Why It's Still Broken
Construction schedules slip and marketing isn't told; the billing system doesn't know which pre-registrations are now serviceable.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Link pre-registrations to build phases, trigger outreach automatically when an address becomes serviceable, prioritise follow-up calls by conversion likelihood and competing options at the address, and report conversion by phase.

## Who Feels the Pain
Broadband general managers chasing take-rate targets; member services staff working cold lists; boards watching revenue lag.

## Impact If Fixed
More of the demand the entrant already captured turns into subscribers, at almost no extra cost.
