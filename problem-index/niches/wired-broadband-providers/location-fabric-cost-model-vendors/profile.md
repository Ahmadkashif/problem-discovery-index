# Location Fabric & Cost-Model Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wired-broadband-providers|Wired Broadband Providers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
The firms that build the location data and cost models broadband policy runs on. CostQuest is the FCC's Fabric contractor: it builds and revises the Broadband Serviceable Location Fabric every six months, licenses it to every filer, and has built cost models used for universal service support. Providers, states and consultants license the Fabric; the FCC pays for its production.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-60 GIS analysts, data engineers and economists (estimate)
**Output:** The Fabric and its revisions, challenge adjudication data, cost models and licensed datasets
**Proprietary data:** A national location dataset built from parcel, address and imagery sources, with the challenge history behind each revision
**Clock:** The FCC's semiannual Fabric and BDC calendar (for example the September 1, 2026 challenge deadline for the December 2026 Fabric)
**Buyer:** Chief executive or head of data products
