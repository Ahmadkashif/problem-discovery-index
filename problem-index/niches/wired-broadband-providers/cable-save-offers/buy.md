# Retail Markdown Optimisation Adapted to Promo Roll-Off

**Niche:** [[niches/wired-broadband-providers/cable-save-offers/profile|Cable Save Offers Against Fixed Wireless & Fibre]]
**Industry:** [[industries/wired-broadband-providers|Wired Broadband Providers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Retailers already optimise how deep to discount which item where, against local competition and a measured response; cable roll-off is the same decision made per household.
**Tags:** #causal-inference #bayesian-inference #gradient-boosting #optimization-fundamentals #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (BSS churn modules, retention analytics vendors and operators' own data science teams) is fighting to price each save offer to the alternative the household can actually get at its address, and whoever prices save offers to each address's real alternative best takes the account.

## The Problem
Cable operators set roll-off offers by tier and risk band, revised quarterly by a pricing team with spreadsheets.

## What Already Exists
Markdown and promotion optimisation software in retail and grocery (from vendors such as Revionics, Blue Yonder and o9), which estimates price elasticity by product and store, accounts for local competitors and optimises margin under constraints.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: households instead of SKUs, with subscription survival as the outcome rather than a single purchase; competitor presence defined per address from the FCC map and fixed wireless eligibility, not by store catchment; offer types that are not only price (speed upgrades, mobile bundles, price locks, credits after outages); federal broadband label disclosure rules; and the fairness and regulatory exposure of charging similar households different prices.

## Target Customer
Pricing teams at cable operators and large telcos.

## Impact If Solved
A proven optimisation discipline gives the pricing team a defensible, testable offer policy instead of quarterly guesswork.
