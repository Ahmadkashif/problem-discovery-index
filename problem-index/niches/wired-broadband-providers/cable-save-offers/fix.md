# The Price Jump Discovered on the Bill

**Niche:** [[niches/wired-broadband-providers/cable-save-offers/profile|Cable Save Offers Against Fixed Wireless & Fibre]]
**Industry:** [[industries/wired-broadband-providers|Wired Broadband Providers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Many customers learn their promotion ended when the higher bill arrives, and the first contact after that is an angry cancellation call.
**Tags:** #survival-analysis #gradient-boosting #automation #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (BSS churn modules, retention analytics vendors and operators' own data science teams) is fighting to price each save offer to the alternative the household can actually get at its address, and whoever prices save offers to each address's real alternative best takes the account.

## The Problem
The promotion expires, the bill rises, and the customer calls already shopping. By then a fixed wireless or fibre sign-up page is open in another tab and the save desk is negotiating from behind.

## Why It's Still Broken
Billing systems know the expiry date but outreach before roll-off is treated as inviting discount requests, so many operators stay quiet and hope customers don't notice.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Contact households before roll-off in a sequence chosen by their alternatives: households with no alternative get a clear notice; households with a live alternative get a pre-emptive offer sized by uplift; households with recent outages get a service credit and a fix. Measure each path against a holdout.

## Who Feels the Pain
Save-desk agents fielding hostile calls; customers surprised by the bill; retention leaders paying for deep discounts that an earlier, smaller offer would have avoided.

## Impact If Fixed
Fewer cancellation calls, smaller average discounts, and fewer customers lost before the operator ever got to speak to them.
