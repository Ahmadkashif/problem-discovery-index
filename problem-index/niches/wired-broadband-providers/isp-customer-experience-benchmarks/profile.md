# ISP Customer Experience Benchmarks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wired-broadband-providers|Wired Broadband Providers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Survey-based benchmark studies of internet customers' satisfaction, sold to operators as detailed data and licensed as awards for advertising. J.D. Power's U.S. Residential ISP Satisfaction Study drew on 27,971 responses in 2025, rating providers by region on service quality, value, ease of doing business, trust, people, digital tools and problem resolution; the American Customer Satisfaction Index publishes a telecom ranking.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-25 researchers and analysts per firm's telecom practice (estimate)
**Output:** Annual studies, provider-level diagnostic data, award licences, custom research
**Proprietary data:** Years of respondent-level survey data by provider and region
**Clock:** Self-set annual study calendar
**Buyer:** Telecom practice lead
