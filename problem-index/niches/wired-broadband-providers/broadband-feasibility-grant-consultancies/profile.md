# Broadband Feasibility & Grant Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wired-broadband-providers|Wired Broadband Providers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation firms

## What They Do
Consultancies that tell a co-op, rural telco, municipality or state whether a broadband build pencils out and then write the application that funds it: market and take-rate studies, cost and financial models, BEAD, ReConnect and state grant applications, challenge-process work and state broadband office planning. JSI (which says its team led 40% of ReConnect Round 3 applications), CTC Technology & Energy (Oregon's BEAD planning consultant and author of Minnesota's BEAD cost-gap analysis), Vantage Point, Finley Engineering and Magellan Advisors are representative. Providers, municipalities and state offices pay per study or application.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-40 market, financial and grant analysts at the larger firms (estimate), excluding network design engineers, whose work is production
**Output:** Feasibility studies, financial models, grant applications, state plans and challenge filings
**Proprietary data:** Cost, take-rate and pricing outcomes from past client builds, plus cost models; client network and financial data used per engagement
**Clock:** Grant application windows and challenge deadlines set by NTIA, USDA and state broadband offices
**Buyer:** Managing principal or practice lead

**Kill switches:** none. The deliverables belong to clients, but the analysis runs mainly on public map and census data and the firm's own cost models, with client data used per engagement (scored in Q3, as for [[niches/biotech-research-firms/ind-regulatory-strategy-consultancies/profile|Nonclinical & IND Regulatory Strategy Consultancies]]).
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. Studies and applications are the invoice and the deadlines are hard and set by agencies, but each study is bespoke to one community, the largest window (BEAD) closed with all 56 final proposals approved in 2026, and the cross-client outcome archive can only be used per engagement.
