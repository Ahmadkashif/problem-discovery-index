**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce a semiannual availability filing reconciled to the current Fabric that survives the challenge process, and whoever delivers it with the fewest engineer-hours and the fewest sustained challenges takes the account.

## The Problem
A consumer, state or competitor challenges availability at a set of locations. The provider must show it can serve them within ten business days at the claimed speed — install records, plant maps, speed tests — or concede. Under a BEAD challenge, rebutting providers had to supply speed tests for at least 10% of customers in the challenged area.

## Why It's Still Broken
Evidence lives in billing, field service and engineering systems, and nobody keeps it organised by Fabric location between filings.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Keep an evidence pack per claimed location updated continuously (nearest plant, latest install nearby, delivered speeds, test results), so a challenge response is generated and reviewed rather than researched.

## Who Feels the Pain
Regulatory managers and engineers at small providers; operators who concede valid coverage because they ran out of time.

## Impact If Fixed
Valid claims are defended on time, and territory isn't lost to paperwork.
