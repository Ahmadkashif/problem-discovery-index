# Network Performance & Speed-Test Data Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wired-broadband-providers|Wired Broadband Providers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms that collect crowdsourced and background network measurements and sell the data, benchmarks and reports to operators, regulators and state broadband offices. Ookla (part of Ziff Davis) says it collects hundreds of millions of US speed tests a year, and NTIA's challenge-process guidance lists it among approved speed-test applications; Opensignal is the other commercial player and M-Lab publishes open data. Operators buy competitive benchmarks and use the data in challenge filings; states buy it to test coverage claims.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-60 analysts and data scientists per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Performance datasets, market benchmarks, coverage analyses, published reports and awards
**Proprietary data:** The test corpus itself, with device, location and time for every measurement
**Clock:** Mostly self-set reporting cycles; challenge windows set by NTIA and the FCC apply to their customers rather than to them
**Buyer:** Head of research or enterprise analytics at the vendor
