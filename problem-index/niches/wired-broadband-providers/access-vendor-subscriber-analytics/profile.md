# Access Equipment Vendors' Subscriber Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wired-broadband-providers|Wired Broadband Providers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Access equipment and home-network vendors that sell operators cloud analytics on their own subscribers: Calix (about 1,600 service-provider customers) sells role-based cloud applications for marketing, operations and support built on data from its equipment, and other access and Wi-Fi vendors do similar. Operators pay subscriptions; the vendors' data teams build segmentation, churn and network-health products.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-80 data scientists and analysts per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Subscriber, Wi-Fi and network analytics inside SaaS subscriptions
**Proprietary data:** Pooled device telemetry across many operators' subscribers
**Clock:** Product release cycles
**Buyer:** Chief product or data officer at the vendor
