# Rural Broadband Lenders

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wired-broadband-providers|Wired Broadband Providers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The lenders that finance rural telcos, co-ops and fibre entrants: CoBank, USDA's Rural Utilities Service (loans and ReConnect), and the Rural Telephone Finance Cooperative, plus banks lending against subsidy-backed builds. Their credit teams underwrite take-rate assumptions, subsidy dependence and construction risk.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-50 credit analysts per lender's communications portfolio (estimate); sector research economists are a handful
**Output:** Credit decisions and loan terms; occasional published sector research
**Proprietary data:** Loan performance and borrower financials across rural carriers
**Clock:** Borrowers' grant and construction schedules
**Buyer:** Chief credit officer
