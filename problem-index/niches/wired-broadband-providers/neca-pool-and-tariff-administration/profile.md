# NECA Pool & Tariff Administration

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wired-broadband-providers|Wired Broadband Providers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The National Exchange Carrier Association files the interstate access tariff (Tariff F.C.C. No. 5) on behalf of its rate-of-return member carriers, administers the traffic-sensitive and switched-access pools that settle revenue among them, and files members' Connect America Fund intercarrier compensation data with USAC alongside the tariff. Each spring it notifies pool members of the coming tariff year's rate changes (an 18.4483% cut in capped switched access rates for July 2025, 0.5711% for July 2026), and member carriers file matching state tariff revisions. Members fund the work through pool administration charges.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~30-80 cost, demand and tariff analysts (estimate)
**Output:** Annual interstate tariff and cost support, pool settlements, rate-change notices, CAF ICC data filings
**Proprietary data:** Cost and demand data from hundreds of rural rate-of-return carriers, pooled across years
**Clock:** FCC-set annual access tariff cycle (filed June 16, 2025 for rates effective July 1; the FCC's 2026 notice set rates effective July 1, 2026)
**Buyer:** NECA's head of tariffs and pool administration

**Kill switches:** none. The cost and demand data belongs to the member carriers, but they submit it to NECA precisely so it can be pooled and analysed for the tariff and settlements, which is the work scored here. That differs from the cost-study consultants, whose cross-client comparison is barred; reuse outside NECA's mandate would still need members' consent.
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. Hard FCC clocks and the deepest cost and demand record of rural carriers, scored like [[niches/telecom-resellers/usac-program-integrity/profile|USAC Contribution Audits & Lifeline Program Integrity]] (36): the work is administration paid by pool charges rather than invoiced insight, there is exactly one such body, and the pools shrink as carriers move to model-based support.
