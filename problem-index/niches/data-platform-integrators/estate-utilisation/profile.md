# Estate Utilisation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/data-platform-integrators|Data Platform Integrators]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to establish which of the models and dashboards it has built are actually used, when the query log holds the answer and nobody has ever run it — and whoever answers it takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$4.5B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~25% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — never measured
**Target Buyer:** Data leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — measurement plus retirement

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A firm delivers three hundred transformation models and forty dashboards, the engagement closes, and nobody establishes how many are ever queried. Practitioners know the proportion is low — a small number of assets carry nearly all the use and the rest is inventory — and no firm has quantified it across engagements, so every project keeps building at the same rate. The query log, the lineage and the cost attribution all exist, and joining them is a report nobody produces.

### Contested sub-niches
This niche is not terminal. It names a property, and getting the benefit requires two things that fail differently:
- [[niches/data-platform-integrators/usage-instrumentation/profile|🎯 Usage Instrumentation]] — establishing what is used
- [[niches/data-platform-integrators/retirement-and-rationalisation/profile|🎯 Retirement & Rationalisation]] — being able to remove it

## Current Tools & Gaps
A catalogue listing everything, a lineage graph and a cost report. The gaps: no join between delivery record and query log; no usage measurement per asset; no distinction between inventory and working estate; no retirement process; and no feedback into how much is built.
