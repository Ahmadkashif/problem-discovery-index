# Migration Scoping

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/data-platform-integrators|Data Platform Integrators]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to establish which of a decade's warehouse logic and four thousand reports actually needs to move — and whoever scopes that honestly takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3.6B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~20% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — translate everything
**Target Buyer:** Programme leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — usage-driven scoping and translation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Migrating a decade of warehouse logic and four thousand reports is done by translating everything, because nobody can establish which of it anyone still uses. The consequence is a programme priced on the full estate, running for years, carrying inventory onto a new platform where it will cost money to run and complexity to maintain. The scoping decision determines the size, the cost and the risk of the entire programme, and it is made by default rather than by analysis.

## Current Tools & Gaps
An inventory of objects, a translation tool and a plan to do all of it. The gaps: no usage-based scoping; no dependency-aware carve-out; no assessment of what can be rebuilt rather than translated; no staged decommissioning of the source; and no measurement of what was carried and then never used.
