# The Analytics Engineer

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/data-platform-integrators|Data Platform Integrators]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let an engineer answer "can you add a column" without spending the day working out which of four similarly-named models is the right one — and whoever removes that takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.8B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — tracing by hand
**Target Buyer:** Engineering leads
**Automation Potential:** High — lineage-aware navigation and impact analysis

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Requests arrive as a request to add a column and the engineer spends the day tracing which of four similarly-named models is the right one, then adds the four hundredth. The work is archaeology rather than engineering: reading transformations written by people who have left, establishing what depends on what, and deciding whether to extend something existing or add another model to an estate that is already too large. It is most of the role and nothing supports it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A transformation framework, a lineage graph, a naming convention and a search box. The gaps: no way to find the right model from a business question; no impact analysis before a change; no indication of which models are authoritative; no discouragement of adding another; and no context about why a model is as it is.
