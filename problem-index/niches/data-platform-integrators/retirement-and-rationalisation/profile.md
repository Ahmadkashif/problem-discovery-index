# Retirement & Rationalisation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/data-platform-integrators|Data Platform Integrators]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to actually remove an unused asset, which requires proving nothing depends on it and finding someone willing to say so — and whoever makes deletion safe takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.1B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — nothing gets deleted
**Target Buyer:** Data platform leadership
**Automation Potential:** Medium — lineage, process and accountability

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Knowing an asset is unused is a long way from removing it. Removal requires establishing that nothing depends on it — including things outside the platform's lineage — finding an owner willing to agree, and accepting the risk that something breaks. Every rationalisation effort stalls precisely here, after the usage report has been produced and circulated. It is a risk and accountability problem with lineage underneath it, and it fails for reasons the measurement never touches.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A lineage graph covering what the platform can see, and a nervousness about deleting anything. The gaps: no lineage beyond the platform boundary; no owner for most assets; no deprecation process; no safe-removal procedure; and no accountability for the estate's growth.
