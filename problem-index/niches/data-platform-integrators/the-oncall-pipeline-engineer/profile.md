# The On-Call Pipeline Engineer

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/data-platform-integrators|Data Platform Integrators]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stop one engineer rerunning, backfilling and explaining overnight failures before the morning reports go out — and whoever automates that recovery takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.4B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — rerun and backfill
**Target Buyer:** Platform operations
**Automation Potential:** Very high — recovery, backfill and communication

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Pipelines fail overnight on a schedule set by somebody else's batch window, and the engineer on call reruns, backfills and explains it before the morning reports go out. The failures are mostly the same handful of causes, the recovery is mostly the same sequence of actions, and the explanation is mostly the same message. It recurs several times a week, the person doing it is alone at an unsociable hour, and the role has high turnover for reasons nobody connects to it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
An orchestrator, an alert, a runbook if someone wrote one, and a chat channel. The gaps: no automatic retry and recovery for known causes; no automated backfill; no impact assessment on failure; no consumer notification; and no record of what recurs.
