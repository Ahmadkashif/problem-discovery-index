# Usage Instrumentation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/data-platform-integrators|Data Platform Integrators]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn query logs, lineage and cost data into a standing account of what each asset is worth — and whoever produces that takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.4B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — logs unjoined
**Target Buyer:** Data leadership and platform teams
**Automation Potential:** Very high — joining data the platform already produces

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This half is measurement over data the platform already emits. Who queried what, when, how often, through which tool, at what cost, with what depending on it. It is a reporting product, it requires no one to agree to delete anything, and it is valuable on its own — a data leader who can state which fifth of the estate carries the load has something they have never had, whether or not they ever act on it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Query history in the platform, a catalogue and a cost dashboard, none joined. The gaps: no asset-level usage report; no consumer breakdown; no separation of human from pipeline reads; no cost per asset against use; and no standing cadence.
