# Cost Attribution From Cloud FinOps

**Industry:** [[industries/data-platform-integrators|Data Platform Integrators]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Cloud cost management made spend visible to the engineers who cause it, and data platform cost arrives as a monthly total.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make the cost consequences of a transformation layer visible during delivery rather than on a bill — and whoever surfaces that takes the account.

## The Problem
Cloud financial management established the principle that cost must be visible to the people who cause it, at the moment they cause it. Tagging attributes spend to teams and services, showback and chargeback create accountability, anomaly detection catches regressions, and cost appears in engineering workflows rather than only in a finance report. The discipline has a name, a body of practice and a vendor category. Data platform spend is reported as a warehouse total.

## What Already Exists
Cost tagging and attribution to teams and services; showback and chargeback models; cost anomaly detection; rightsizing and commitment recommendations; and cost surfaced in engineering tooling.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to cost caused by query patterns rather than by provisioned resources. It requires: (1) attribution to a transformation model or a dashboard rather than to an infrastructure resource, which means parsing query history and lineage rather than reading tags — this is the substantive difference; (2) cost that depends on how a query is written rather than on what is provisioned, so the recommendations are code changes rather than resizing; (3) the whole upstream chain contributing to an asset's true cost; (4) consumption that is partly driven by end users running ad hoc queries; and (5) an engineer whose tooling is a transformation framework rather than an infrastructure console.

## Target Customer
Data platform teams and integrators, platform and finance leadership, cost management vendors, and platform providers.

## Impact If Solved
Cloud financial management made spend visible to the engineers who cause it and built a vendor category around it. Attribution to a model rather than a resource, with recommendations that are code changes, is what the data version requires.
