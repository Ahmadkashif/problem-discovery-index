# Consumption Cost Engineering

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/data-platform-integrators|Data Platform Integrators]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make the cost consequences of a transformation layer visible during delivery rather than on a bill — and whoever surfaces that takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.2B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — found on the bill
**Target Buyer:** Platform and finance leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — cost attribution and optimisation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
In consumption-priced platforms, cost is a direct and visible consequence of how the models are written. A poorly structured transformation layer costs real money every day it runs, and the client discovers it on a bill rather than during delivery. The engineering decisions that determine it — materialisation strategy, incremental logic, partitioning, join order, schedule frequency — are made without anyone seeing their cost, and revisited only after the finance function asks a question.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A monthly bill, a cost dashboard by warehouse and a conversation after the fact. The gaps: no cost visibility at development time; no attribution to model or query; no cost regression detection on change; no optimisation recommendations; and no budget against which a design is assessed.
