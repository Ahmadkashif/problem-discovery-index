# A Hedge Plan the Lender Signs Before Planting

**Industry:** [[industries/commodity-brokers|Commodity Brokers]]

**One-liner:** Farmers hedge on their broker's advice and fund margin calls from a bank line sized without reference to the hedge, so the plan breaks in exactly the rally it was meant for.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a farmer's futures and options hedges matched to the bushels actually grown, insured and contracted, with a margin plan the farmer's lender has agreed in advance, and whoever keeps the most farmer hedges on through a margin spike takes the account.

## The Problem
A farmer selling new-crop corn futures in February to lock in a margin over cost faces margin calls if prices rally before harvest. In spring 2022 corn rose about $1 in two weeks. The futures loss is offset by the higher value of the crop, but the cash leaves the bank months before the crop is sold. If the line runs out or the banker gets nervous, the hedge is lifted at the worst price.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Brokers, lenders and insurance agents work for different firms with different incentives, and the farmer is the only common party. Brokers fear that putting stress scenarios on paper discourages hedging. Lenders lack the futures expertise to size a line to a hedge.

## What to Build
A planning tool the broker runs with the farmer before planting. It simulates revenue with and without the proposed hedge across yield, price and basis scenarios, accounting for revenue-protection insurance, and computes the margin calls each scenario would generate. The output is a one-page plan the lender reviews and approves as a dedicated hedge line, with alerts to all three parties when margin use approaches the agreed limit.

## Target Customer
Ag introducing brokers and FCM farmer desks, offered jointly with the farm lenders who fund hedge lines: Farm Credit associations and [[industries/community-banks|Community Banks]], whose farm lending is profiled in [[niches/community-banks/agricultural-lending/profile|🟠 Agricultural Lending]].

## Impact If Built
Hedges that survive rallies, lenders who understand what they are funding, and a reason for farmers to hedge more of their crop rather than less.
