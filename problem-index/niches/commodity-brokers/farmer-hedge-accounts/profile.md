# Farmer Hedge Accounts & Marketing Advice

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commodity-brokers|Commodity Brokers]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a farmer's futures and options hedges matched to the bushels actually grown, insured and contracted, with a margin plan the farmer's lender has agreed in advance, and whoever keeps the most farmer hedges on through a margin spike takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$350M a year in advisory tools, data and brokerage services spend on farmer hedge accounts (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of technology and services spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — advice is given by phone and text, positions are reconstructed by the broker, and the lender learns of margin needs when the farmer calls the bank
**Target Buyer:** Principal of an ag introducing broker / head of farmer brokerage at an FCM
**Automation Potential:** Medium — position assembly and plan simulation automate; trust and advice stay with the broker

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A farmer's hedge only works if three parties stay aligned: the broker who recommends it, the farmer who has to deliver the grain, and the lender who funds the margin calls in a rally. The classic failure is the one recorded from 1988, when banks pulled hedge lines and brokers liquidated short corn near the high, though none of the lines had used even 30% of its limit. The winner here is whoever turns hedging from a phone conversation into a plan all three have agreed: hedge ratio against expected production, instruments chosen against insurance coverage, and a pre-approved margin line sized to stress scenarios.

## Current Tools & Gaps
FCM portals and statements; Barchart and DTN for prices and basis; farm management software such as FBN or Granular for agronomy and some marketing; crop insurance agents' quoting tools; marketing advisory newsletters. Nothing ties the hedge plan to the lender's line, and nothing tests the plan against the margin calls it will generate.
