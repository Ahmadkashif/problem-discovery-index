# Futures Manipulation & Spoofing Litigation Economists

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commodity-brokers|Commodity Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Economic consulting practices (Cornerstone Research, Compass Lexecon, Analysis Group and Charles River Associates are representative) that analyse order-book messages and trade data in CFTC and DOJ spoofing and manipulation cases, benchmark-manipulation class actions and exchange disciplinary matters. They test for order patterns consistent with intent to cancel, estimate price impact, model damages and testify. Defendants, plaintiffs and occasionally regulators pay through counsel.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–60 economists and analysts per practice working on commodity and derivatives matters (estimate)
**Output:** Expert reports, rebuttals, testimony, damages and price-impact models
**Proprietary data:** Methods and code built across past matters, and licensed exchange order-book histories; the case data itself (the client's orders and trades, the counterparties' records) is produced in discovery under protective orders
**Clock:** Court-ordered expert report and deposition deadlines, and settlement schedules in CFTC enforcement matters
**Buyer:** Practice leader / principal responsible for the derivatives litigation group
