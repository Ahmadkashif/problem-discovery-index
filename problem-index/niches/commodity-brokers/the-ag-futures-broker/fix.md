# The Recommendation Nobody Wrote Down

**Niche:** [[niches/commodity-brokers/the-ag-futures-broker/profile|The Ag Futures Broker]]
**Industry:** [[industries/commodity-brokers|Commodity Brokers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A broker recommends a hedge on a recorded line, and the only record of what was advised is a year of audio nobody will search.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #compliance #worker-facing #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let one registered broker serve a few hundred farm accounts (positions, cash sales, insurance, margin) with a morning view and a compliant record of every recommendation, and whoever lets one broker serve the most farms without a missed call or an undocumented recommendation takes the account.

## The Problem
When a hedge goes wrong, the farmer remembers the advice differently from the broker. The recorded call exists for a year under CFTC rules, but finding it, and knowing what was actually recommended, means listening through hours of tape.

## Why It's Still Broken
Writing a note after every call takes time a broker with two hundred accounts does not have, so nobody does it consistently.

## What a Fix Looks Like
After each recorded call, a two-line summary of the recommendation, the instruments and quantities discussed and the client's decision is drafted automatically. The broker confirms it with one tap, and it is filed against the account.

## Who Feels the Pain
Brokers defending their advice; compliance officers answering complaints; farmers who want to know what they agreed.

## Impact If Fixed
A searchable advice record at almost no time cost, which settles complaints quickly and protects good brokers.
