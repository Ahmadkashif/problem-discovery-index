# The Ag Futures Broker

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commodity-brokers|Commodity Brokers]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let one registered broker serve a few hundred farm accounts (positions, cash sales, insurance, margin) with a morning view and a compliant record of every recommendation, and whoever lets one broker serve the most farms without a missed call or an undocumented recommendation takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$120M a year in broker-facing software, data and compliance tools for agricultural associated persons (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of technology and services spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — the broker's working tools are a quote screen, the FCM portal, a phone, a notebook and memory
**Target Buyer:** The broker personally, or the IB principal or FCM ag desk head who equips a team of them
**Automation Potential:** Medium — assembly, drafting and record-keeping automate; trust and advice do not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The agricultural associated person is the industry's frontline: a Series 3 registrant in a farm-town office carrying roughly 150–400 farm, ranch and elevator accounts (estimate), up before 5 a.m. for the overnight session, on the phone through USDA report days, and personally responsible to clients who see the broker, not the FCM, as their hedge adviser. The tools of the industry are built for the FCM (clearing, margin, statements) or for the self-directed trader (charts, order entry). None is built for a person whose job is to understand two hundred farms. The broker's book is also the broker's livelihood, and a portable one. When brokers change firms, or their clearing firm changes hands as R.J. O'Brien did in 2025, the accounts follow the person who serves them best.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Quote and charting platforms (CQG, Barchart, DTN), FCM back-office portals, CRM or spreadsheets, recorded phone lines, newsletters. The gaps are a client-level position view across futures, cash contracts and insurance, triage of who needs a call today, and drafting help for commentary and recommendation records.
