# Wealth Adviser Workstations Adapted to Farm Hedging

**Niche:** [[niches/commodity-brokers/the-ag-futures-broker/profile|The Ag Futures Broker]]
**Industry:** [[industries/commodity-brokers|Commodity Brokers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Financial advisers have workstations that show every client's holdings, tasks and notes in one place; ag futures brokers have a quote screen and a notebook.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #feature-engineering #data-integration #worker-facing #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let one registered broker serve a few hundred farm accounts (positions, cash sales, insurance, margin) with a morning view and a compliant record of every recommendation, and whoever lets one broker serve the most farms without a missed call or an undocumented recommendation takes the account.

## The Problem
The ag broker's book is a set of relationships with farms, but the broker's tools are organised around contracts and accounts.

## What Already Exists
Adviser workstations and CRMs built for wealth management, with household views, holdings aggregation, task lists, meeting notes and compliance archiving.

## The Customization Gap
The farm version needs households as farm operations with acres, crops, cattle and storage. It needs holdings that include futures, options, elevator cash contracts and crop insurance and LRP coverage, measured in bushels and head. It needs tasks keyed to the crop calendar (insurance dates, USDA reports, option expiries, HTA rolls) and notes linked to recorded calls under NFA rules.

## Target Customer
Ag introducing brokers and FCM farm desks with five or more brokers.

## Impact If Solved
A proven adviser workflow, adapted rather than invented, so brokers serve farms rather than accounts.
