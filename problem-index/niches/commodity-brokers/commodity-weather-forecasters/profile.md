# Commodity Trading Weather Forecasters

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commodity-brokers|Commodity Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Meteorology firms that forecast the weather variables commodity prices move on, such as heating and cooling degree days for natural gas and power and rainfall and temperature over the corn, soybean and wheat belts, and sell the forecasts, model commentary and analyst calls to energy and agricultural traders, hedge funds and futures brokers. Atmospheric G2, Commodity Weather Group and World Weather Inc. are representative, along with the ag-weather desks of the large data vendors.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 meteorologists and data scientists per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Daily and intraday forecasts, degree-day and crop-weather outlooks, model-change commentary
**Proprietary data:** The firm's forecast archive and verification record; the inputs are public or licensed numerical weather models (GFS, ECMWF) available to every rival
**Clock:** The major model runs, after which forecasts must be updated before the market reacts, and the weekly natural gas storage and USDA crop progress releases traders position around
**Buyer:** Chief Meteorologist / Head of Commercial
