# Livestock Hedging Desks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commodity-brokers|Commodity Brokers]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to pick, for each pen or barn of animals, the cheapest effective protection among an LRP endorsement, a put option and a futures hedge, and to document why, and whoever protects the most head at the lowest net premium takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$80M a year in brokerage tools, data and advisory services spend on cattle and hog hedge accounts (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% of technology and services spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — cattle and hog hedges are planned on the phone and in pencil around placement and marketing dates; insurance endorsements are written on agency software that never meets the futures account
**Target Buyer:** Livestock desk head at an FCM / principal of a livestock-focused IB or crop-and-livestock insurance agency with a brokerage arm
**Automation Potential:** Medium — instrument comparison and documentation automate; reading a producer's operation does not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Cattle feeders, cow-calf operators and hog finishers face price risk between placement and sale. Since federal subsidy increases in 2019–2020 they have had a real alternative to futures and options: Livestock Risk Protection. LRP is a USDA-backed endorsement with no margin calls, sold through crop insurance agents. In 2025 producers bought 61,060 feeder-cattle LRP policies covering 6.11 million head and $12.47B of liability, and 2026 liability was already higher by August. The broker's question is no longer only "how many contracts" but "which instrument for this group of animals". The answer depends on subsidised premium, coverage price, basis, the producer's ability to fund margin calls, and lot sizes that don't match a 50,000-pound feeder cattle futures contract. The insurers' rating of LRP is profiled in [[niches/livestock-operations/cattle-risk-protection-underwriting/profile|🔍 Livestock Risk Protection Underwriting]] (41); the brokerage choice is here.

## Current Tools & Gaps
FCM platforms for futures and options; RMA's LRP price and premium tables and agency quoting tools; university decision aids; spreadsheets. The gap is a side-by-side comparison per lot that includes subsidy, basis, margin liquidity and timing, and a record of why the choice was made.
