# Coverage Bought by Habit, Not by Comparison

**Niche:** [[niches/commodity-brokers/livestock-hedging-desks/profile|Livestock Hedging Desks]]
**Industry:** [[industries/commodity-brokers|Commodity Brokers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Producers who started with LRP keep buying LRP, and producers who started with puts keep buying puts, whichever is cheaper this week.
**Tags:** #probability-distributions #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to pick, for each pen or barn of animals, the cheapest effective protection among an LRP endorsement, a put option and a futures hedge, and to document why, and whoever protects the most head at the lowest net premium takes the account.

## The Problem
The relative cost of LRP and exchange options shifts with subsidy, implied volatility and basis. A producer insuring at about $2,440 a head of feeder-cattle value, the 2026 average implied by RMA data, can overpay by ~$5–15 a head (estimate), or ~0.2–0.6% of insured value, by not comparing. On a 400-head placement that is roughly $2,000–6,000.

## Why It's Still Broken
The broker or agent who placed last year's coverage repeats it, and the comparison takes longer than the sale.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A short post-season report per producer comparing what was bought with the cheapest alternative available on the same day for the same animals, and a pre-placement prompt that runs the comparison before coverage is renewed.

## Who Feels the Pain
Cattle and hog producers paying more than necessary; brokers and agents whose advice is questioned after the fact.

## Impact If Fixed
Cheaper protection and a documented comparison, with no new product required.
