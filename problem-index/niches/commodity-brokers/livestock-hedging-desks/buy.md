# Insurance Comparative Raters Adapted to LRP and Options

**Niche:** [[niches/commodity-brokers/livestock-hedging-desks/profile|Livestock Hedging Desks]]
**Industry:** [[industries/commodity-brokers|Commodity Brokers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Insurance agents already compare carriers' quotes side by side in seconds; livestock brokers compare LRP with exchange options by hand.
**Tags:** #probability-distributions #monte-carlo-methods #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to pick, for each pen or barn of animals, the cheapest effective protection among an LRP endorsement, a put option and a futures hedge, and to document why, and whoever protects the most head at the lowest net premium takes the account.

## The Problem
Producers want a quick answer on what protection costs for a given group of animals, often the same day coverage prices are published.

## What Already Exists
Comparative rating platforms used by property-casualty agencies, which pull quotes from many carriers into one screen and record what was offered.

## The Customization Gap
Livestock needs daily RMA LRP coverage prices, premiums and subsidy rates by endorsement length. It needs exchange option premiums and futures prices for the matching contract months, basis by sale location, and conversion between head and weights and contract sizes. It needs margin-call cash flow for futures, and a combined record satisfying both insurance and NFA communications rules.

## Target Customer
Agencies and brokerage desks licensed for both crop insurance and futures, which serve most of the producers buying LRP.

## Impact If Solved
A quote-and-compare step that takes minutes rather than an evening, built on proven rating software.
