# Clearing House Margin & Risk Methodology Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commodity-brokers|Commodity Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The risk departments of the derivatives clearing organisations that stand between every FCM and every other (CME Clearing, ICE Clear US and OCC are the largest US futures and options clearers). They design and backtest the margin models (CME's SPAN and its SPAN 2 successor), set margin rates, run daily and intraday stress tests, size default funds and monitor clearing members' concentration. Every FCM's margin calls to its customers start with their numbers. Clearing members pay for clearing, not for the analysis.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~50–150 risk modellers, quants and analysts per large DCO (estimate)
**Output:** Margin rates and methodology, stress-test results, default fund sizing, member risk reviews
**Proprietary data:** Complete position, price and margin histories for every clearing member and, through them, customer omnibus accounts. No one else holds this.
**Clock:** Daily settlement cycles, intraday margin calls in volatile markets, and CFTC Part 39 requirements for margin model review and backtesting
**Buyer:** Chief Risk Officer of the clearing house
