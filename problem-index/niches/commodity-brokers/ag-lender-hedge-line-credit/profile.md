# Agricultural Lenders' Hedge-Line Credit Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commodity-brokers|Commodity Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Farm Credit associations and [[industries/community-banks|Community Banks]] that fund farmers' and elevators' margin calls through hedge lines of credit, deciding how much to lend against a hedge account and when to stop. Their lending side is profiled in [[niches/community-banks/agricultural-lending/profile|🟠 Agricultural Lending]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 per lender; hedge lines are underwritten by general agricultural credit analysts and loan officers as part of operating loans (estimate)
**Output:** Credit decisions on hedge lines
**Proprietary data:** Borrower financials and the line's draw history
**Clock:** Margin calls as they arrive
**Buyer:** Chief credit officer
