# Futures Trade Surveillance & Risk Analytics Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commodity-brokers|Commodity Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors that sell FCMs, proprietary trading firms and exchanges the software that scans order and trade data for spoofing, layering, wash trades and position-limit breaches, and that scores trading risk. Nasdaq Trade Surveillance, NICE Actimize, Eventus and Trading Technologies' TT Score are representative. Their analytics teams build and tune the detection models; clients pay software licences.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 surveillance analysts and model developers per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Detection models, alert scenarios, case-management software
**Proprietary data:** Model libraries and alert-tuning experience; the order and trade data the models run on belongs to each client
**Clock:** Clients' obligations to review alerts and supervise trading continuously, sharpened by CFTC and exchange spoofing enforcement
**Buyer:** Head of product / head of surveillance analytics
