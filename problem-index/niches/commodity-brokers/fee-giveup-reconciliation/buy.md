# Telecom Expense Auditing Adapted to Futures Fees

**Niche:** [[niches/commodity-brokers/fee-giveup-reconciliation/profile|Exchange Fee, Give-Up & Brokerage Reconciliation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/commodity-brokers|Commodity Brokers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Expense-auditing tools already compare millions of usage-based charges against contracted rates; futures fees are the same problem with different units.
**Tags:** #feature-engineering #decision-trees #data-integration #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to match every exchange, clearing, NFA and give-up fee to the trade that incurred it before month-end invoices are paid, and whoever recovers the most mis-billed fees before payment takes the account.

## The Problem
FCMs and their clients pay usage-based fees from several parties per trade and audit them by sampling.

## What Already Exists
Telecom and freight expense audit platforms that load contracted rate tables, re-rate each usage record and dispute overcharges.

## The Customization Gap
Futures needs effective-dated exchange and clearing fee schedules by product, venue, membership status and volume tier. It needs give-up agreements between named executing and clearing brokers, NFA assessments, client-specific rate cards and pass-through rules. And it needs inputs from back-office trade files and FIA Tech utilities rather than carrier bills.

## Target Customer
FCM operations and the buy-side operations teams that pay executing and clearing brokers.

## Impact If Solved
Every line is re-rated instead of sampled, and recoveries are realised through a proven audit workflow.
