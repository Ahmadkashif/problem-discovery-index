# AIS-Derived Commodity-Flow & Freight Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commodity-brokers|Commodity Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms that turn vessel position signals (AIS), port and terminal data, customs records and analyst knowledge into cargo-level estimates of what crude, refined products, LNG, coal, grains and metals are moving where. They aggregate these into country and regional import, export and inventory series, freight-market analytics and supply-demand balances. Kpler is the leader. It assembled the field by acquisition (ClipperData in 2021, JBC Energy in 2022, MarineTraffic and FleetMon in 2023, and in 2025 Spire's satellite-AIS data business, with the satellites retained by Spire) and passed $100M of annual recurring revenue in January 2024. Vortexa is the main independent rival, alongside the flow products of LSEG, S&P Global and Signal Ocean. Commodity traders, hedge funds, oil majors, refiners, shipowners and the futures desks they clear through pay subscriptions to see flows days or weeks before official statistics.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~50–150 market and research analysts at Kpler (its Insight team alone listed 30+ in its brochure; the company now cites over 850 staff); ~15–40 market analysts at Vortexa; smaller teams at the others (estimates, per firm)
**Output:** Cargo-level flow data, import and export nowcasts by country, floating storage, freight analytics, supply-demand balances and price forecasts, analyst notes
**Proprietary data:** Years of cargo-matching history (vessel, grade, volume, origin and destination) and its error record against official releases, terminal and port databases, and at Kpler MarineTraffic's terrestrial AIS receiver network plus Spire's satellite-AIS data business (satellites retained by Spire). Peers license AIS that anyone can buy.
**Clock:** Official releases the estimates anticipate and are graded against: the EIA's Weekly Petroleum Status Report (Wednesdays, 10:30 a.m. Eastern), monthly customs trade data such as US Census and China customs, and IEA and OPEC monthly reports
**Buyer:** Chief Product Officer / Head of Research
