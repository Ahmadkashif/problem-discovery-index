# Non-Bank FCM Consolidator Corporate Development

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commodity-brokers|Commodity Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The acquisition teams at the non-bank FCMs that roll up smaller brokers and clearing books. StoneX bought R.J. O'Brien in 2025, adding about 300 introducing brokers and 75,000 accounts. Other non-bank FCMs, Marex among them by trade-press accounts (not checked here), buy smaller books from time to time. They screen targets, model client retention and capital synergies, and run integrations.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Deal models and integration plans for internal use
**Proprietary data:** The acquirer's own client and capital data, and the target's in diligence
**Clock:** Deal timetables
**Buyer:** Chief executive / head of corporate development
