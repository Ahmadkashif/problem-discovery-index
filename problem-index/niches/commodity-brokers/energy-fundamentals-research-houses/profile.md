# Independent Energy Fundamentals Research Houses

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commodity-brokers|Commodity Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Research firms that build oil, refined product, natural gas and LNG supply-demand balances and price forecasts and sell them, with analyst access, to commodity traders, hedge funds, producers, refiners and the futures desks serving them. Energy Aspects, FGE, Rystad Energy's market teams, Wood Mackenzie's oil markets research and ESAI are representative. Clients buy them to form a view before positioning in crude, products and gas futures.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–80 market analysts per firm's commodity-markets team (estimate; the larger consultancies employ far more in upstream and asset research that is not counted here)
**Output:** Monthly and weekly balances, price forecasts, research notes, analyst calls
**Proprietary data:** Their own balance models and a record of past forecasts against outcomes; most inputs are public (EIA, IEA, JODI, customs) or licensed flow data
**Clock:** Official releases that confirm or refute the balances: the EIA's Wednesday petroleum report, IEA and OPEC monthly reports, OPEC+ meetings
**Buyer:** Head of Research / Managing Director
