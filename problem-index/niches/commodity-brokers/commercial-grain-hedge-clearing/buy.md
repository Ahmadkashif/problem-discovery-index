# Energy Trading Risk Systems Adapted to Country Elevators

**Niche:** [[niches/commodity-brokers/commercial-grain-hedge-clearing/profile|Commercial Grain Hedge Account Clearing]]
**Industry:** [[industries/commodity-brokers|Commodity Brokers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Energy and metals merchants run trading and risk systems that link physical contracts to futures in real time; mid-size grain hedgers and their FCMs do it in spreadsheets.
**Tags:** #feature-engineering #monte-carlo-methods #time-series-forecasting #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to clear an elevator's or processor's hedge book against its cash-contract position every day, hedge-to-arrive and basis contracts included, and to fund its margin calls through a price spike without the lender pulling the line, and whoever keeps an elevator's hedges funded through the steepest rally takes the account.

## The Problem
Commercial grain hedgers need position, mark-to-market and margin forecasts across thousands of contracts, but most mid-size operations cannot justify a full trading and risk system.

## What Already Exists
Energy and commodity trading and risk management (ETRM/CTRM) platforms used by oil, gas, power and metals merchants, which track physical deals and derivatives together and compute exposures and margin.

## The Customization Gap
Grain needs contract types the platforms handle awkwardly (HTA, basis, minimum price, delayed price), grade and discount schedules, and storage. It needs country-elevator scale and pricing. Above all it needs an FCM-facing view: daily reconciliation exported to the clearing FCM's credit team and to lenders, and margin projections under CME's margin changes.

## Target Customer
FCM agricultural clearing desks offering a risk service to elevator clients; mid-size elevators and processors.

## Impact If Solved
Proven exposure and margin logic delivered at a price country elevators can pay, with the FCM as distributor.
