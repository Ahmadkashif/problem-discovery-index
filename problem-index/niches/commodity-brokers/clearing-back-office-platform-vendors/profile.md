# Clearing & Back-Office Platform Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commodity-brokers|Commodity Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors of the clearing, margin, accounting and regulatory-reporting systems FCMs run on. FIS's Cleared Derivatives Suite (descended from SunGard's GMI) and ION's XTP are the main platforms: BNP Paribas signed a ten-year FIS deal, and Advantage Futures moved to XTP in 2024. FCMs pay licence and service fees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Product, engineering and implementation teams; no research or insight function sells analysis (estimate)
**Output:** Software and managed services
**Proprietary data:** None of their own; client positions and balances sit in the vendors' schemas but belong to each FCM
**Clock:** Clients' regulatory deadlines, such as the noon segregation computation
**Buyer:** Head of product
