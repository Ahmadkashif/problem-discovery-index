# Farm Marketing Advisory Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commodity-brokers|Commodity Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Subscription advisers that tell farmers when and how to sell grain and hedge. Pro Farmer, Allendale, AgMarket.Net and Total Farm Marketing are representative. They sell newsletters, marketing plans and hotlines, often alongside a brokerage arm that executes the hedges.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically under 10 market analysts per firm; the larger headcounts are brokers and sales staff serving accounts (estimate)
**Output:** Market commentary, marketing recommendations, subscriber plans
**Proprietary data:** A record of past recommendations and subscriber relationships
**Clock:** USDA report days and the crop calendar
**Buyer:** Founder / head of research
