# Futures Industry & Introducing Broker Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commodity-brokers|Commodity Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Trade bodies for the futures industry and its brokers. FIA publishes monthly exchange volume statistics and represents FCMs and clearing firms. The National Introducing Brokers Association represents IBs, and agricultural groups such as NGFA, whose members are elevators, run trade rules and arbitration. Member dues and conference revenue fund them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research and statistics staff per association (estimate)
**Output:** Volume statistics, member surveys, comment letters, conference content
**Proprietary data:** Member surveys and compiled exchange volume series
**Clock:** Monthly statistics releases and rulemaking comment deadlines
**Buyer:** Chief executive
