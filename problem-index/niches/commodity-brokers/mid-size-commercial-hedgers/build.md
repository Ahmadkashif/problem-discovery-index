# A Hedging Programme in a Box for One-Person Finance Teams

**Niche:** [[niches/commodity-brokers/mid-size-commercial-hedgers/profile|Mid-Size Commercial Hedgers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/commodity-brokers|Commodity Brokers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Mid-size companies exposed to diesel, propane, grain or natural gas either don't hedge or hedge ad hoc, because nobody sells them a programme a controller can run.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #monte-carlo-methods #large-language-models #feature-engineering #workflow-orchestration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give a company too small for a bank's OTC desk a hedging programme (exposure measurement, futures execution and hedge-accounting documentation) that one finance person can run, and whoever gets a programme running on the least finance-staff time and through a clean audit takes the account.

## The Problem
A company buying 5 million gallons of diesel a year (estimate) knows fuel hurts its margin but cannot measure its exposure month by month, set a policy, or show its board what hedging would have done. When it does hedge, it is a phone call to a broker after a price spike.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The work spans forecasting the company's own consumption, choosing instruments, executing futures and documenting hedge relationships. That covers four specialities no single vendor serves at this size. Brokers earn commissions on execution, not on programme design.

## What to Build
A service that connects to the company's purchasing and sales data, forecasts physical exposure by month, proposes a policy-based hedge ratio with backtested outcomes, routes orders to the FCM, and generates the ASC 815 hedge designation memo and periodic effectiveness tests automatically. A monthly board report shows exposure, hedges and results.

## Target Customer
CFOs and controllers at regional fuel distributors, food processors, fleets and manufacturers, sold through FCM commercial desks.

## Impact If Built
Hedging moves from reaction to policy, auditors get documentation at inception, and FCMs reach commercial clients they currently cannot serve economically.
