# Introducing Broker Back Office & Commission Reconciliation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commodity-brokers|Commodity Brokers]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn the clearing FCM's daily statements and commission runs into a per-account, per-broker book an introducing broker can reconcile, pay its brokers from and show an NFA examiner, and whoever closes the month with no payout disputes and a clean NFA exam takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$150M a year in back-office software, outsourced bookkeeping and compliance services spend by introducing brokers (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~4% of technology and services spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — statements arrive as PDFs and portal extracts and are reconciled in Excel by the principal or an office manager
**Target Buyer:** IB principal / chief compliance officer at an introducing broker
**Automation Potential:** High — the reconciliation is rule-based once statements are parsed

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
NFA counted 900 introducing brokers in September 2026. An IB solicits and accepts futures orders but cannot hold customer money, so its whole economic and compliance life runs through another firm's records: the clearing FCM's statements. Its revenue is a commission payment it must split among its associated persons. Its supervisory duty under NFA rules covers brokers whose trades it sees only in those statements, and its customers' complaints arrive about numbers it did not produce. Clearing consolidation adds churn: StoneX's 2025 acquisition of R.J. O'Brien moved a network of roughly 300 IBs onto a new clearing relationship.

## Current Tools & Gaps
FCM portals built on FIS or ION back offices, some IB reporting dashboards from the larger FCMs, QuickBooks for the IB's own books, Excel for everything else. The gaps: no tool spans multiple clearing FCMs, no split-schedule engine knows futures conventions, and supervision views are rebuilt from statements by hand.
