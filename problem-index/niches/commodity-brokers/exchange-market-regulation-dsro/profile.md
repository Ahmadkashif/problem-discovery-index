# Exchange Market Regulation & DSRO Audit

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commodity-brokers|Commodity Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The self-regulatory arms of the futures markets. Exchange market regulation departments (CME Group's and ICE Futures US's) surveil order books for spoofing, wash trading and position-limit breaches and bring disciplinary cases. The designated self-regulatory organisations (CME Group for many FCMs, NFA for others) examine FCMs' capital and segregation, receive their daily and monthly filings, and run financial surveillance. They are funded by exchange fees and NFA assessments, not by selling their analysis.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~40–80 market surveillance and trade-practice investigation analysts at CME Group, the largest body (estimate). The CFTC's 2013 rule enforcement review counted 40 market surveillance staff in a Market Regulation department approved for 160. ICE Futures US's team is smaller. DSRO financial auditors and examiners are not counted: they are production staff working through filings and examinations.
**Output:** Investigations, disciplinary actions, FCM examinations, financial surveillance alerts
**Proprietary data:** Complete order-book and account-level trading records, and every FCM's daily segregation and monthly capital filings
**Clock:** Daily segregation filings due before noon, monthly financial reports, examination cycles, investigation and hearing schedules
**Buyer:** Chief Regulatory Officer / head of financial surveillance
