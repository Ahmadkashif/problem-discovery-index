# CFTC Market Surveillance & Enforcement Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commodity-brokers|Commodity Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The CFTC's market surveillance, enforcement and economic analysis staff, who analyse large-trader positions, order data and FCM financial filings. They publish the weekly Commitments of Traders report, monitor FCM segregation through the published monthly table, and investigate manipulation and supervision failures such as the April 2020 negative-price episode at Interactive Brokers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Hundreds of surveillance analysts, economists, investigators and examiners (estimate)
**Output:** Surveillance alerts, enforcement cases, Commitments of Traders, research and rulemaking analysis
**Proprietary data:** Large-trader reports, ownership and control data, and order-level data from every US futures exchange
**Clock:** The weekly Commitments of Traders schedule, enforcement statutes of limitation, and rulemaking calendars
**Buyer:** Director of Market Oversight / Chief Economist
