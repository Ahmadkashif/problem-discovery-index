# Commodity Hedge Programme Advisory for End Users

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commodity-brokers|Commodity Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Advisory firms and the consulting arms of FCMs that design hedging programmes for corporate end users such as food processors, fuel distributors, airlines and manufacturers. They measure exposure, write hedge policies, model hedge ratios, support hedge accounting and report results to boards. Chatham Financial (mainly rates and FX, with a commodity practice), FCM risk-consulting desks and independent commodity risk consultancies are representative. Clients pay retainers or project fees, sometimes bundled with execution.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–50 commodity risk advisers and analysts per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Hedge policies, exposure models, programme performance reports, hedge-accounting documentation
**Proprietary data:** Methods and templates, and a cross-client view of programme design; the exposures and positions analysed are the clients' own, provided under engagement agreements
**Clock:** Clients' budget cycles and quarter-end close and audit
**Buyer:** Practice head / managing director, commodity risk advisory
