# Exchange Economic Research & Market Data Products

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commodity-brokers|Commodity Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The economists, product researchers and data teams at the futures exchanges, chiefly CME Group, who publish market commentary, volatility and positioning research and education to grow trading in their contracts. They also package historical tick, settlement and order-book data for sale. FCMs and their clients read the research free. The historical data is sold to quants, vendors and academics.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 economists and research staff, with separate market data sales and engineering teams (estimate)
**Output:** Research notes, volatility indices, education content, historical data products
**Proprietary data:** The complete trade, quote and settlement history of the exchange's own contracts
**Clock:** Product launches, contract expiries and macro events; no third-party deadline governs the research
**Buyer:** Chief Economist / head of market data products
