# Deal Memory & Judgement Reuse

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/private-equity-firms|Private Equity Firms]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make a sponsor's past deal judgements — what it saw, why it passed or invested, and what happened next — retrievable at the moment a similar deal arrives, within what the firm's NDAs let it keep.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$900M US (senior and associate time spent re-researching sectors and companies the firm has already evaluated, plus knowledge-management spend)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of sponsor operating spend
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — memos on a shared drive, reasons in a dropdown
**Target Buyer:** Managing partner / COO
**Automation Potential:** Very High — retrieval and outcome-joining over a corpus the firm already holds

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A sponsor ten years old has evaluated thousands of companies, written hundreds of screening memos and dozens of IC memos, and watched the outcomes. Almost none of it is reusable: memos are prose on a shared drive, pass reasons are a dropdown, CIMs are destroyed under NDA, and the partner who remembers left. This is the high-impact problem's niche — the partner's tacit screening judgement, captured and graded — and it is distinct from sourcing because it concerns judgement on companies the firm has already seen.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Deal CRMs (DealCloud, Affinity, Salesforce) storing pipeline stages; SharePoint or Box for memos; enterprise search; LLM research tools over current documents. The gaps: no record of reasons in partners' own words; no outcome joining for passed companies; no retention design for NDA material; and nothing that surfaces the prior view when a company or its competitor reappears.
