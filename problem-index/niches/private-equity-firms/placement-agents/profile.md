# Placement Agents & Fundraising Advisers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/private-equity-firms|Private Equity Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation firms

## What They Do
Advisers raising capital for sponsors — Campbell Lutyens, Evercore's private capital advisory group, Park Hill and others — who research LP appetite, position the fund and manage the process.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 per firm across fundraising, research and secondary advisory
**Output:** Fundraising strategy, LP targeting, positioning materials, market intelligence
**Proprietary data:** LP preference and commitment histories across many fundraises
**Clock:** Fund close deadlines
**Buyer:** Head of private fund group
