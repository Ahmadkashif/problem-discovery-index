# Financial Consolidation Software

**Niche:** [[niches/private-equity-firms/portfolio-monitoring/profile|Portfolio Monitoring & KPI Reporting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/private-equity-firms|Private Equity Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Corporate consolidation tools solved mapping many subsidiaries' ledgers to one chart of accounts; a sponsor's portfolio is a group that never shares a ledger.
**Tags:** #feature-engineering #descriptive-statistics #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a dozen portfolio companies' differently-defined monthly packages into comparable, on-time KPIs — and whoever does that is the system the deal partner opens when something starts going wrong.

## The Problem
Conceptually, the sponsor is consolidating a group — but without ownership of the subsidiaries' systems, chart of accounts or close calendar.

## What Already Exists
Consolidation and CPM platforms — OneStream, Oracle Hyperion/EPM, Workday Adaptive Planning, Prophix — map subsidiary ledgers to a group chart of accounts, handle intercompany and produce group reporting.

## The Customization Gap
The sponsor case needs: (1) ingestion from packages rather than direct ledger connection; (2) per-company mappings maintained by the sponsor, not the subsidiary; (3) metric definitions from credit agreements; (4) pro forma treatment for add-ons mid-period; and (5) LP reporting outputs (ILPA) rather than statutory consolidation.

## Target Customer
Fund finance teams at larger mid-market sponsors and buy-and-build platforms.

## Impact If Solved
A proven mapping discipline from corporate finance applied to the portfolio gives sponsors consolidated, consistent data without imposing an ERP on every company.
