# Portfolio Monitoring & KPI Reporting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/private-equity-firms|Private Equity Firms]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a dozen portfolio companies' differently-defined monthly packages into comparable, on-time KPIs — and whoever does that is the system the deal partner opens when something starts going wrong.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.5B US (monitoring software, fund finance and associate time spent on portfolio data collection and quarterly reviews)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of sponsor operating spend
**Digital Adoption:** Low — platforms bought, data still rekeyed from Excel and PDF packages
**Target Buyer:** Fund CFO / COO
**Automation Potential:** Very High — mapping and anomaly detection on structured monthly data

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
After close, the sponsor's view of each company comes through a monthly package built by the company's CFO in the company's own format. The monitoring platforms (iLEVEL, Chronograph, Cobalt, Allvue) store and report KPIs well once they are in. Getting them in — consistently defined, on time, with definition changes caught — is the contest, and it is still largely manual at mid-market sponsors.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Monitoring platforms with collection portals; Excel templates sent to portfolio CFOs; fund administrator data for fund-level accounting. The gaps: portal templates portfolio CFOs bypass; definitions that drift between companies and over time; covenant, management and sponsor EBITDA unreconciled; and alerts based on budget variance rather than trajectory.
