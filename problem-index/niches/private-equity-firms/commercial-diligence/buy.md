# Qualitative Research Analysis Software

**Niche:** [[niches/private-equity-firms/commercial-diligence/profile|Commercial Diligence]]
**Industry:** [[industries/private-equity-firms|Private Equity Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Qualitative coding tools serve academic and UX researchers well; CDD needs them on a three-week clock against a fixed commercial framework.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #word-embeddings #k-means-clustering #evaluation-metrics
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to say, from evidence gathered in four weeks, whether the target's revenue will still be there and growing in five years — because that judgement is the exit multiple.

## The Problem
CDD teams code interview notes into themes in Excel.

## What Already Exists
Qualitative data analysis software (NVivo, ATLAS.ti, Dovetail) supports transcript coding, theme building and quote retrieval, increasingly with AI-assisted coding.

## The Customization Gap
CDD requires: (1) a standard commercial codebook (purchase criteria, switching cost, wallet share, pricing, competitive set) pre-loaded; (2) quantification — themes as shares of interviewed revenue, not counts of mentions; (3) linkage of each interviewee to their spend with the target so findings can be revenue-weighted; (4) confidentiality controls per engagement; and (5) output in slide form on the readout clock.

## Target Customer
CDD boutiques and strategy firm PE practices.

## Impact If Solved
Existing qualitative tools become CDD-native, cutting synthesis time and making findings revenue-weighted by default.
