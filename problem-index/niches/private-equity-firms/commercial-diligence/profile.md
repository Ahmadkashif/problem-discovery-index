# Commercial Diligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/private-equity-firms|Private Equity Firms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to say, from evidence gathered in four weeks, whether the target's revenue will still be there and growing in five years — because that judgement is the exit multiple.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2B US (commercial due diligence fees on sponsor transactions plus expert network and survey spend)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of sponsor deal-related spend
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — survey panels, expert calls and market models in Excel and PowerPoint
**Target Buyer:** Deal partners; CDD practice heads on the provider side
**Automation Potential:** High — interview synthesis, survey analysis and market sizing are repeatable in shape

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Commercial diligence answers the questions the ledger cannot: is the market growing, is the company gaining or losing share, would customers switch, can it raise prices. The work is primary research under deadline — customer interviews, customer and non-customer surveys, competitor and expert calls — synthesised into a market model and a view on the business plan. It is delivered by L.E.K., Bain, other strategy firms' PE practices and dozens of CDD boutiques. The evidence is completely different from QoE's, and so is the failure mode: not a wrong number but a wrong story about the future.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Expert networks (GLG, AlphaSights, Guidepoint, Third Bridge), transcript libraries (Tegus, AlphaSense), survey platforms (Qualtrics, panel providers), market data subscriptions and Excel market models. The gaps: interviews synthesised by hand into slides; surveys re-fielded from scratch on markets the firm has studied before; and no record of whether past CDD market forecasts were right.
