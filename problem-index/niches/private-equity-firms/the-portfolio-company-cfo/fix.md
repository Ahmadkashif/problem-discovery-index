# The Ad Hoc Request With No Priority

**Niche:** [[niches/private-equity-firms/the-portfolio-company-cfo/profile|The Portfolio Company CFO]]
**Industry:** [[industries/private-equity-firms|Private Equity Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Deal partners, operating partners and associates each send the CFO analysis requests, none ranked, all urgent.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #workflow-orchestration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let a sponsor-backed CFO maintain one mapping from the company's ledger to the sponsor's, the lender's and the board's definitions — and whoever does that owns the reporting relationship between owner and company.

## The Problem
Requests arrive by email from several people at the sponsor, often overlapping, rarely prioritised, and often answerable from data the sponsor already holds.

## Why It's Still Broken
No one at the sponsor sees the total load on the CFO; requests are cheap to send and expensive to answer.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A shared request queue between sponsor and company: requests deduplicated, answered automatically where the mapped data already covers them, and the rest prioritised by the deal partner with the load visible to everyone.

## Who Feels the Pain
Portfolio CFOs and their small teams; sponsors whose operating priorities get crowded out by their own requests.

## Impact If Fixed
Making the request load visible is usually enough to cut it, and auto-answering the routine share returns days per month.
