# Account-Based Sales Prioritisation

**Niche:** [[niches/private-equity-firms/deal-sourcing/profile|Deal Sourcing & Origination]]
**Industry:** [[industries/private-equity-firms|Private Equity Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** B2B sales teams solved prioritising a large target list by intent signal years ago; sponsors are selling to founders who sell once in a lifetime, which breaks most of the assumptions.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #logistic-regression #survival-analysis #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to know that a founder-owned company is becoming sellable — and to be the trusted buyer in the room — before a banker is hired and the price is set by auction.

## The Problem
Sourcing looks like enterprise sales: a large account list, limited reps, and a need to know who is in-market. Sponsors increasingly run it like one, with sequences and CRM dashboards, and get results that look like sales activity metrics rather than deals.

## What Already Exists
Account-based marketing and revenue intelligence platforms — 6sense, Demandbase, ZoomInfo intent data, Salesforce Einstein lead scoring — score accounts on intent signals and engagement, route them to reps, and measure conversion.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is from a repeat purchase to a once-in-a-lifetime event. It requires: (1) a target event — the founder deciding to sell — that is rare, slow and driven by life events (age, health, partner disputes, a failed succession) rather than web intent; (2) a "conversion" that may come years after the first contact, so engagement metrics on a quarterly horizon mislead; (3) competition visible only indirectly, through which bankers are circling and which other sponsors have met the founder; (4) relationship depth as the main driver of win probability, which is a graph property of the firm's partners rather than a marketing touch count; and (5) outcomes that must be joined from transaction databases rather than from the firm's own funnel.

## Target Customer
Sourcing teams at sponsors already using DealCloud or Affinity with a revenue-intelligence mindset.

## Impact If Solved
The sales tooling world has a mature playbook for prioritisation under scarce rep time. Adapted to life-event timing and relationship graphs, it gives sponsors a measurable sourcing function in place of activity counting.
