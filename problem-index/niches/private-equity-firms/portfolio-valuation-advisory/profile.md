# Portfolio Valuation Advisory

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/private-equity-firms|Private Equity Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation firms
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce a quarterly fair value mark the sponsor, its auditor and its LPs all accept — defensible against observable market evidence, consistent across hundreds of companies and delivered before the reporting deadline — and whoever does that most credibly at volume takes the valuation mandate.

## What They Do
Independent valuation firms — Lincoln International's valuations and opinions group, Houlihan Lokey's portfolio valuation practice, Kroll, Stout and a long tail of specialist valuation boutiques — value sponsors' portfolio companies every quarter at fair value under ASC 820, using comparable companies, precedent transactions and discounted cash flow. Sponsors and private credit lenders pay for the marks (or for positive assurance on marks the sponsor prepared); auditors and LPs rely on them. Lincoln alone reports valuing more than 7,000 portfolio companies for over 225 sponsors and lenders each quarter, and publishes the Lincoln Private Market Index from that data.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,500 valuation professionals across the major practices; 10-60 at specialist boutiques
**Output:** Quarterly fair value reports and positive assurance opinions per portfolio company or debt instrument; valuation policy reviews; aggregate private-market indices
**Proprietary data:** Quarter-by-quarter financials, forecasts, capital structures and concluded valuations for thousands of private companies across hundreds of sponsors — a panel no public dataset approximates
**Clock:** Quarter-end fund reporting, annual audit sign-off, LP reporting deadlines and, for BDC and private credit clients, public filing dates
**Buyer:** Head of portfolio valuation practice
