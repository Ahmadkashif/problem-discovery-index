# Private Capital Performance Benchmark Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/private-equity-firms|Private Equity Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Providers of LP-sourced private fund cash-flow data and benchmarks — MSCI Private Capital Solutions (formerly Burgiss), Cambridge Associates benchmarks, StepStone's SPI, Preqin's performance data — used by LPs and GPs to rank funds by quartile, compute public-market equivalents and build pacing models.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 data operations, quantitative research and methodology staff
**Output:** Fund performance benchmarks and quartiles, PME analytics, cash-flow pacing models, private-market indices
**Proprietary data:** Cash-flow-level fund data contributed by LPs from their own books, covering thousands of funds over decades
**Clock:** Quarterly reporting cycle and LP fundraising diligence
**Buyer:** Head of private capital research / product
