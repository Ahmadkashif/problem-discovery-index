# Full-Population Audit Analytics

**Niche:** [[niches/private-equity-firms/quality-of-earnings-review/profile|Quality of Earnings Review]]
**Industry:** [[industries/private-equity-firms|Private Equity Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Audit firms built analytics that test every journal entry; QoE reviews the same kind of ledger for a different question and mostly does not use it.
**Tags:** #feature-engineering #gradient-boosting #descriptive-statistics #evaluation-metrics #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to establish a defensible adjusted EBITDA — which add-backs are real and which are recurring costs relabelled — inside the exclusivity window, because that number multiplied by the multiple is the price.

## The Problem
QoE work and audit work both start from a general ledger, but QoE is asking what the sustainable earnings are, not whether the statements are free of material misstatement.

## What Already Exists
Audit data analytics: MindBridge's AI-assisted ledger risk scoring, CaseWare IDEA, and the large firms' proprietary journal-entry testing platforms, which ingest a full GL and flag unusual entries.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: (1) a target question of recurrence and normalisation rather than misstatement; (2) mapping of messy, small-company ledgers (QuickBooks, older mid-market ERPs) that audit tooling assumes are cleaner; (3) add-back schedules as an input to be tested line by line; (4) working-capital normalisation and seasonality analysis as first-class outputs; and (5) a databook export that drops straight into the buyer's model template.

## Target Customer
QoE providers and sponsors' in-house diligence teams.

## Impact If Solved
Reusing proven ledger analytics, re-aimed at sustainable earnings, raises the floor of QoE quality across the long tail of boutique providers serving lower-middle-market deals.
