# SEC Private Fund Examinations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/private-equity-firms|Private Equity Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The SEC Division of Examinations' private fund unit and related enforcement staff, which examine registered private fund advisers on fees and expenses, valuation, conflicts and marketing claims.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-400 examiners and specialists dedicated to private funds
**Output:** Examination findings, deficiency letters, risk alerts, enforcement referrals
**Proprietary data:** Form ADV and Form PF filings joined to examination findings across registered advisers
**Clock:** Examination cycles and statutory filing deadlines
**Buyer:** Programme office
