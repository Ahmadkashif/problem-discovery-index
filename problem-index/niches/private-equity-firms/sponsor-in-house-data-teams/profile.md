# Sponsor In-House Data & Sourcing Analytics Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/private-equity-firms|Private Equity Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Data science and sourcing analytics teams inside sponsors themselves and inside their buy-and-build platforms. At lower-middle and middle-market sponsors these are typically zero to five people; the rollup platforms' own corporate development analytics are already mapped in the vault, for example [[niches/hvac-contractors/home-services-rollup-analytics/profile|Home Services Rollup Analytics]], [[niches/plumbing-contractors/plumbing-rollup-pe-analytics/profile|Plumbing Rollup PE Analytics]] and [[niches/med-spas/medspa-rollup-analytics/profile|Med Spa Rollup Analytics]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** 0-5 at mid-market sponsors; larger only at the largest buyout firms, outside this industry's boundary
**Output:** Sourcing models, portfolio dashboards
**Proprietary data:** The sponsor's own CRM and portfolio data
**Clock:** Deal timetables
**Buyer:** COO / head of data

## Scorecard
Not scored — fails the gate.

**Kill switches:** not assessed
**Verdict:** Logged — fails gate. Mid-market sponsors rarely employ a dedicated data function of 10+; their research capacity is the deal team itself, which is the subject of Pass 1.
