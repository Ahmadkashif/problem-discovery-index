**Size:** 15-100 investment and analytics staff per major secondaries buyer
**Output:** Portfolio-level and company-level valuations underpinning bids
**Proprietary data:** Underlying company data across thousands of funds and portfolio companies seen in prior bids
**Clock:** Bid deadlines set by the seller's adviser
**Buyer:** Head of secondaries / pricing
