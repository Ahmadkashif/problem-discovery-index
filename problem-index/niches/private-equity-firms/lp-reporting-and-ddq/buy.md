# RFP Response Software

**Niche:** [[niches/private-equity-firms/lp-reporting-and-ddq/profile|LP Reporting & Fundraising Due Diligence]]
**Industry:** [[industries/private-equity-firms|Private Equity Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Answer-library tools built for sales RFPs already handle repeated questionnaires; DDQs add performance figures that must reconcile and statements a regulator may read.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #word-embeddings #evaluation-metrics #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to answer the same LP questions consistently — across quarterly reports, forty due diligence questionnaires and the data room — with every track-record figure reconciling to the administrator, because inconsistency is what an LP or examiner notices first.

## The Problem
DDQ responses are assembled from last fund's answers in Word.

## What Already Exists
RFP and security questionnaire response platforms — Loopio, Responsive (formerly RFPIO) — maintain answer libraries, match incoming questions with AI and route review.

## The Customization Gap
DDQs need: (1) the ILPA DDQ structure as the base taxonomy; (2) live numerical answers pulled from the track-record master rather than static text; (3) compliance review on marketing statements, given SEC marketing rule obligations for registered advisers; (4) cross-LP consistency checks; and (5) versioning per fund vintage.

## Target Customer
IR and compliance teams at registered private fund advisers.

## Impact If Solved
A proven tool category adapted to performance data and marketing-rule review shortens DDQ turnaround during the period when it most affects close timing.
