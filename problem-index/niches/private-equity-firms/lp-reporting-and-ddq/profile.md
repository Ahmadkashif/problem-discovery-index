# LP Reporting & Fundraising Due Diligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/private-equity-firms|Private Equity Firms]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to answer the same LP questions consistently — across quarterly reports, forty due diligence questionnaires and the data room — with every track-record figure reconciling to the administrator, because inconsistency is what an LP or examiner notices first.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.2B US (investor relations, fund finance and compliance time on LP reporting and DDQs, plus portal and administrator reporting fees)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~4% of sponsor operating spend
**Digital Adoption:** Low — Word questionnaires, Excel track records and document portals
**Target Buyer:** Head of investor relations / CFO / CCO
**Automation Potential:** High — answer reuse and figure reconciliation are mechanical

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Fundraising is a research product in reverse: the sponsor must evidence its own track record, process and controls to dozens of LPs, each with a questionnaire based on the ILPA DDQ but phrased differently. Quarterly, it reports performance and fees, increasingly in ILPA template formats. The contest is consistency and reconciliation across many outputs from one set of facts.

## Current Tools & Gaps
LP portals (Juniper Square, Intralinks, Allvue), fund administrator reports, Word DDQ libraries, RFP response tools borrowed from sales. The gaps: answers drift across LPs; track-record calculations (gross and net IRR, MOIC, attribution) repeated by hand per request; and no check that what one LP was told matches what another was.
