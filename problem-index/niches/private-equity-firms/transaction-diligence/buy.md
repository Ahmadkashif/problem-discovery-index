# Audit Issue Tracking

**Niche:** [[niches/private-equity-firms/transaction-diligence/profile|Transaction Diligence]]
**Industry:** [[industries/private-equity-firms|Private Equity Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Internal audit and GRC tooling already tracks findings from many reviewers to resolution; diligence needs the same discipline on a six-week clock with external providers.

## The Problem
Diligence findings are tracked in a spreadsheet maintained by an associate, with columns for workstream, issue and status. It is out of date the moment a new draft arrives.

## What Already Exists
Audit management and GRC platforms — AuditBoard, Workiva, ServiceNow GRC — track findings from multiple reviewers, assign owners, link them to controls and record remediation through to closure.

## The Customization Gap
Diligence needs: (1) a deal-scoped, disposable instance spun up at LOI and archived at close; (2) external providers as contributors, each with access only to its workstream; (3) findings linked to financial model lines and price, not to controls; (4) resolution categories specific to deals — price reduction, escrow, specific indemnity, R&W insurance exclusion, post-close remediation; and (5) automatic ingestion from provider drafts, since providers will not type into a sponsor's tool.

## Target Customer
Sponsor operations and diligence leads; also diligence providers who would standardise on a format accepted by many sponsors.

## Impact If Solved
A proven discipline from audit applied to the deal clock replaces the associate-maintained spreadsheet and gives IC a defensible view of what was found and how each item was handled.
