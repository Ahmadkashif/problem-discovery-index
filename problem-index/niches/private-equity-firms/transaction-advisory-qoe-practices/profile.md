# Transaction Advisory & Quality of Earnings Practices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/private-equity-firms|Private Equity Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation firms

## What They Do
The transaction advisory services practices of accounting firms — RSM, BDO, Grant Thornton, Baker Tilly, Crowe and a long tail of QoE boutiques — that test adjusted EBITDA, normalise working capital and analyse net debt for sponsors on buy-side deals and for sellers on sell-side QoE. The sponsor pays per engagement; the report is relied on by the lender and the R&W insurer.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1,000-5,000 professionals across the national and regional firms; 10-80 at boutiques
**Output:** Quality of earnings reports and databooks, net working capital analyses, net debt schedules, sell-side QoE
**Proprietary data:** General ledger detail and workpapers from thousands of target companies — but the data belongs to the targets and arrives under the buyer's NDA
**Clock:** LOI exclusivity periods of four to eight weeks, financing commitment dates, signing
**Buyer:** Transaction advisory practice leader

**Kill switches:** **client data ownership** — every ledger analysed belongs to a target company and is received under a confidentiality agreement, and professional confidentiality rules restrict using one client's information for another. Tooling for the live engagement is unaffected; any corpus-level product is not.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. Scores above threshold on every dimension except corpus ownership: the firms hold the most complete picture of how mid-market add-backs behave across thousands of deals and cannot lawfully pool it without a consent and de-identification regime nobody has built.
