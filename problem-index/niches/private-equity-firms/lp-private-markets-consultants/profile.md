# LP Private Markets Consultants & GP Due Diligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/private-equity-firms|Private Equity Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Investment consultants and private markets advisers — Cambridge Associates, Hamilton Lane, StepStone, Aksia and others — who research and rate private equity managers for pensions, endowments, foundations and family offices, recommend commitments, and in many cases manage discretionary programmes.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 research, due diligence and analytics staff per major firm
**Output:** GP due diligence reports and ratings, commitment recommendations, pacing models, portfolio reporting, private markets benchmarks
**Proprietary data:** Fund-level and often deal-level cash flows and diligence files on thousands of GPs, plus the firm's own manager ratings history
**Clock:** LP investment committee dates and GP fund close deadlines
**Buyer:** Head of private equity research / CIO
