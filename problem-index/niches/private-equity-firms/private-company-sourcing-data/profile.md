# Private Company Sourcing Data Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/private-equity-firms|Private Equity Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Vendors whose product is data on founder-owned and privately held companies for deal sourcing — Grata, SourceScrub, Inven and the private-company modules of larger data platforms — built from web crawling, conference lists and research staff.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-300 data research and engineering staff
**Output:** Company profiles, sector lists, signals of growth and ownership, sourcing workflows
**Proprietary data:** Curated private-company records and signal histories
**Clock:** None imposed by a third party; subscription renewal cycle
**Buyer:** Head of data / product
