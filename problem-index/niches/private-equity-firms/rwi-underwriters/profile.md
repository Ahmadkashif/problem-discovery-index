# Representations & Warranties Insurance Underwriters

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/private-equity-firms|Private Equity Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
MGAs and insurers writing R&W insurance on sponsor acquisitions: underwriters review the buyer's diligence, hold an underwriting call, set exclusions and price the policy, and later handle claims for breaches.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 underwriters and claims staff per active market participant
**Output:** Underwriting reports, exclusions, policy pricing, claims determinations
**Proprietary data:** Diligence reports and underwriting files across thousands of deals, joined to the claims that later arose
**Clock:** Signing dates — the policy must bind at signing
**Buyer:** Head of underwriting
