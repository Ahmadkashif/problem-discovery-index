# Market Research Survey Automation

**Niche:** [[niches/private-equity-firms/commercial-diligence-consultancies/profile|Commercial Due Diligence Consultancies]]
**Industry:** [[industries/private-equity-firms|Private Equity Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Survey platforms and panels let market researchers field and analyse studies in days; CDD needs B2B respondents verified as real buyers in a niche market, analysed against a revenue-weighted commercial framework in under a week.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #descriptive-statistics #confidence-intervals #k-means-clustering #evaluation-metrics #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver, inside a sponsor's exclusivity window, the market and customer evidence that decides whether the deal team believes the revenue plan — and whoever arrives with the sharpest answer fastest, on a market they already know, wins the next mandate from that sponsor.

## The Problem
Customer surveys are central to CDD and are fielded through panels and analysed in Excel under extreme time pressure, in niche B2B markets where qualified respondents are scarce.

## What Already Exists
Survey and experience platforms (Qualtrics), panel providers and B2B sample marketplaces, and automated analysis features including open-text coding.

## The Customization Gap
CDD needs: (1) respondent verification that a respondent is genuinely a buyer in a niche B2B category, where panel fraud and professional respondents are common; (2) revenue weighting by respondent spend and segment; (3) a standard CDD question bank (purchase criteria, switching propensity, wallet share, price sensitivity, vendor ratings) with benchmarks from prior studies; (4) analysis outputs — net promoter by segment, share of wallet shifts, price elasticity indications — produced automatically as responses arrive; and (5) a three-to-five-day field-to-readout cycle.

## Target Customer
CDD practices and their survey operations teams.

## Impact If Solved
Faster, verified, revenue-weighted surveys raise both the speed and credibility of the commercial case inside the exclusivity window.
