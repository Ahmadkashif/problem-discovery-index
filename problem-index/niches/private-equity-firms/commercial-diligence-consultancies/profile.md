# Commercial Due Diligence Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/private-equity-firms|Private Equity Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation firms
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver, inside a sponsor's exclusivity window, the market and customer evidence that decides whether the deal team believes the revenue plan — and whoever arrives with the sharpest answer fastest, on a market they already know, wins the next mandate from that sponsor.

## What They Do
Strategy consultancies' private equity practices — L.E.K., Bain, and the PE groups of other strategy firms — plus dozens of specialist CDD boutiques, which assess a target's market, competitive position and customer relationships for sponsors on buy-side deals (and for sellers on vendor due diligence). The sponsor pays for a report delivered in three to six weeks; the deal team underwrites the revenue case to it and often shares it with lenders.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-2,000 consultants per firm in PE-dedicated practices; 15-150 at boutiques
**Output:** CDD reports, market models, customer and non-customer survey results, interview syntheses, red-flag reviews, vendor due diligence
**Proprietary data:** Prior market models, survey instruments and responses, and interview notes across hundreds of studies — much of it in markets the firm returns to repeatedly for different sponsors
**Clock:** Sponsor exclusivity periods, bid deadlines and IC dates — the hardest clock in professional services
**Buyer:** Head of PE practice / CDD practice leader

**Kill switches:** none live — target-specific information sits under the sponsor's NDA and must stay walled to the engagement, but the market-level primary research (surveys, market models, interview themes about the market rather than the target) is the consultancy's working knowledge and is routinely reused across studies. The restriction defines what a reuse product may contain; it does not stall procurement. This is the difference from technology due diligence, where the findings are about the target itself.
**Verdict:** Qualified — indexed
