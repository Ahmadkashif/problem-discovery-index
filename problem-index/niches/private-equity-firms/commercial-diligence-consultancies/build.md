# The Same Market Studied Six Times for Six Sponsors

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver, inside a sponsor's exclusivity window, the market and customer evidence that decides whether the deal team believes the revenue plan — and whoever arrives with the sharpest answer fastest, on a market they already know, wins the next mandate from that sponsor.

## The Problem
A CDD practice that has worked in, say, specialty distribution or veterinary services has studied the same market for several sponsors over several years. Each study re-sizes the market, re-fields a customer survey, re-interviews industry experts and re-derives the growth view. What carries over is a partner's memory and a folder of old decks. The partner's market judgement — which segments are growing, which customer claims to trust, which data sources overstate — is the firm's real product and is held tacitly.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Studies are delivered as slides, not data. Engagement confidentiality makes teams cautious about reuse, so the safe default is to start fresh. Partners' market knowledge is a personal franchise. And CDD is billed per study, so faster reuse looks like discounting until a competitor does it.

## What to Build
**A market evidence base.** Every study decomposed into reusable, de-identified market-level evidence: segment sizes and growth estimates with sources and date; survey questions and response distributions; interview themes about market dynamics; and the forecast made — all walled off from target-specific findings.

**Living market models.** Market sizes and growth views updated as each new study adds evidence, with uncertainty that narrows as studies accumulate, so a new engagement starts from the firm's current best estimate.

**Prior-informed survey design.** New surveys built from instruments that worked, with prior response distributions used to size samples and spot anomalies.

**Forecast grading.** Each past growth forecast compared with what the market did, so the evidence base knows which sources and which partners' views have been reliable.

## Target Customer
CDD practice leaders at strategy firms' PE groups and boutiques; knowledge management heads at those firms.

## Impact If Built
Starting a study from a maintained, graded market view rather than an old deck shortens time to a credible answer within the exclusivity window — which is how CDD providers win mandates — and turns partners' tacit market knowledge into a firm asset.
