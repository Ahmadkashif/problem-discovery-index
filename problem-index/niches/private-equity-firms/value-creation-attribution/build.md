# The Value Creation Plan Nobody Measured

**Niche:** [[niches/private-equity-firms/value-creation-attribution/profile|Value Creation Attribution]]
**Industry:** [[industries/private-equity-firms|Private Equity Firms]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Every deal has a value creation plan and every exit has a value bridge, and nothing connects the initiatives to the result.
**Tags:** #causal-inference #linear-regression #hypothesis-testing #confidence-intervals #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show which value creation initiatives actually moved EBITDA and which growth came from add-ons, the market or multiple expansion — because that evidence is what the next fundraise is sold on.

## The Problem
The hundred-day plan lists initiatives with expected EBITDA impact. Three to five years later, the exit materials attribute growth to them. In between, initiatives start at different times in different sites or product lines, overlap with market movements and add-ons, and no one estimates their actual effect.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Initiative start dates and scopes are not logged; operating partners measure success anecdotally; and a rigorous answer might show the playbook matters less than the market.

## What to Build
An initiative log captured as initiatives launch, with scope (which sites, products, customers). Panel causal estimates — difference-in-differences or synthetic control across sites, regions or portfolio companies — of each initiative's effect with intervals. A portfolio-wide library of effect sizes by initiative type, used to underwrite the next plan.

## Target Customer
Heads of value creation and operating partners at sponsors with an operating team.

## Impact If Built
Measured effect sizes turn the operating playbook from belief into evidence, improving both underwriting and the fundraising story.
