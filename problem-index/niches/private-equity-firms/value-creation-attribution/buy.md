# Marketing Experimentation and Incrementality Tools

**Niche:** [[niches/private-equity-firms/value-creation-attribution/profile|Value Creation Attribution]]
**Industry:** [[industries/private-equity-firms|Private Equity Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Marketing measurement learned to estimate incremental effect with geo-experiments and synthetic controls; value creation needs the same discipline across sites and portfolio companies.
**Tags:** #causal-inference #bayesian-inference #hypothesis-testing #confidence-intervals #evaluation-metrics
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show which value creation initiatives actually moved EBITDA and which growth came from add-ons, the market or multiple expansion — because that evidence is what the next fundraise is sold on.

## The Problem
Sponsors attribute growth to initiatives without a counterfactual.

## What Already Exists
Incrementality and marketing-mix tooling — Google's open-source Meridian and CausalImpact, Meta's Robyn and GeoLift — estimate causal effects from geo-level panels and staggered rollouts.

## The Customization Gap
Value creation needs: (1) units that are sites, branches or product lines in a single company, or companies across a portfolio; (2) monthly financial outcomes with few periods; (3) staggered, overlapping initiatives designed by operators rather than experimenters; (4) add-on acquisitions changing the unit set; and (5) outputs in value-bridge form for IC and LPs.

## Target Customer
Operating teams and data leads at sponsors; value creation consultancies.

## Impact If Solved
Established incrementality methods applied to operating initiatives give sponsors defensible effect estimates from data they already collect.
