# Distribution Market Research Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/industrial-distributors|Industrial Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Publishers that size distribution markets, rank distributors and report on the channel. Modern Distribution Management, owned by NAW since January 2024, publishes market-sizing reports such as its 2024 U.S. MRO report (US MRO demand ~$907B, industrial MRO products ~$303B, by its estimate) alongside daily news; trade titles such as Industrial Distribution and Industrial Supply Magazine publish rankings and surveys. Distributors, manufacturers and investors pay for subscriptions and reports.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 market analysts at the largest publisher (estimate); editorial staff are writers, not analysts
**Output:** Market-size reports, rankings, survey reports
**Proprietary data:** Survey panels and a long archive of market estimates
**Clock:** Self-set publication calendars
**Buyer:** Distributor and manufacturer strategy and marketing leaders
