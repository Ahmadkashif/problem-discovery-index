# Savings Claims Nobody Audits

**Niche:** [[niches/industrial-distributors/national-account-mro-supply/profile|National-Account & Integrated MRO Supply]]
**Industry:** [[industries/industrial-distributors|Industrial Distributors]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Distributors log savings for national accounts every week, and procurement teams discount most of it at re-bid because no claim comes with a baseline it can check.
**Tags:** #causal-inference #linear-regression #time-series-forecasting #hypothesis-testing #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to prove, site by site, savings that the customer's procurement and finance teams will sign off at re-bid, and whoever brings the largest audited documented-savings total to the contract review takes the account.

## The Problem
A national-account rep records a saving when they consolidate three glove SKUs into one, switch a plant to a longer-life bearing, set up vending that cuts consumption, or expedite a part that keeps a line running. Each entry is a sentence and a dollar figure chosen by the rep. At re-bid, the incumbent presents a large total, and the customer's category manager, who has seen inflated numbers before, accepts a fraction. Competitors make similar promises with no history at all.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Savings programmes were designed as sales tools, so their incentive is volume, not rigour. The data needed for honest baselines — the customer's production volumes, prior prices, downtime records — sits partly with the customer, and distributors have not asked for it. Measuring savings properly also exposes claims that didn't hold up.

## What to Build
A savings ledger that attaches a method to every claim. Price savings are measured against a dated price baseline and market indices; consumption savings are normalised to the site's production or labour hours; product-life savings are tracked from install date to replacement; downtime avoided requires an event record and the customer's sign-off. Each claim carries confidence, evidence and an approval trail, and the ledger rolls up by site, category and year in the format procurement uses.

## Target Customer
VPs of national and strategic accounts at the top ~50 industrial distributors and integrated-supply operators, with renewals worth tens of millions a year at stake.

## Impact If Built
If an audited ledger lets the incumbent defend ~2–5 more points of claimed savings at re-bid (estimate), on a contract worth ~$10–50M a year (estimate) that protects far more margin than the system costs, and it gives the customer a savings number its CFO can repeat.
