# Off-Contract Buying at Every Site

**Niche:** [[niches/industrial-distributors/national-account-mro-supply/profile|National-Account & Integrated MRO Supply]]
**Industry:** [[industries/industrial-distributors|Industrial Distributors]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A plant technician who can't find the contract item in the punchout catalogue in two minutes buys it on Amazon Business, and both the distributor and the customer's contract lose.
**Tags:** #transformers #k-nearest-neighbors #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to prove, site by site, savings that the customer's procurement and finance teams will sign off at re-bid, and whoever brings the largest audited documented-savings total to the contract review takes the account.

## The Problem
Contract compliance at a multi-site customer erodes in the tail: urgent, one-off buys by technicians and supervisors who search the punchout catalogue in their own words, find nothing that looks right, and buy elsewhere on a card. The contract's savings disappear line by line, and the incumbent's share report shows it at re-bid.

## Why It's Still Broken
Punchout catalogues are searched with keyword engines tuned for office supplies, while technicians search with part numbers from the old machine, plant nicknames and partial descriptions. The distributor sees only failed searches if it logs them at all, and the off-contract purchase shows up months later in the customer's spend file.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Log every punchout search and its outcome, and use resolved searches to train identification that understands plant language and legacy part numbers. Flag repeat failed searches to the account team weekly, with the item the customer probably needed, and add the customer's own codes to the catalogue as aliases.

## Who Feels the Pain
Strategic account managers watching compliance fall, customer category managers losing negotiated savings, and technicians wasting time on searches.

## Impact If Fixed
Recovering ~2–5 points of tail spend per site (estimate) is pure share gain for the incumbent and a savings claim it can actually prove.
