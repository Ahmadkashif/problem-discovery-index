# Spend Analytics Pointed at the Distributor's Own Contract

**Niche:** [[niches/industrial-distributors/national-account-mro-supply/profile|National-Account & Integrated MRO Supply]]
**Industry:** [[industries/industrial-distributors|Industrial Distributors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Procurement teams already classify their MRO spend with mature analytics; the distributor serving them has no equivalent view of the same contract.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #k-means-clustering #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to prove, site by site, savings that the customer's procurement and finance teams will sign off at re-bid, and whoever brings the largest audited documented-savings total to the contract review takes the account.

## The Problem
The customer arrives at the quarterly business review with a spend cube: every MRO purchase across sites, classified by category and supplier, with the distributor's contract compliance highlighted. The distributor arrives with its own invoice history, which shows what it sold but not what the customer bought elsewhere, or why.

## What Already Exists
Spend classification and analytics tools built for procurement, mapped in [[niches/procurement-spend-platforms/spend-classification-supplier-master/profile|Spend Classification & Supplier Master]], plus the distributor's ERP reports and BI dashboards.

## The Customization Gap
The distributor needs the same rigour from the other side: line-level classification of its own sales into the customer's categories; site-by-site contract compliance using whatever spend files the customer shares; identification of equivalent items the customer buys off contract; and the link between classified spend and the savings ledger, so each claimed saving maps to real spend. Procurement tools classify by supplier; this needs item-level matching to the distributor's catalogue.

## Target Customer
National-account analysts and strategic account managers at distributors holding multi-site contracts.

## Impact If Solved
If the reconciled view finds ~5–15% of a customer's MRO spend going off contract to items the distributor stocks (estimate), the review turns from defence into a plan to win that share, worth ~$0.5–2M a year on a large account (estimate).
