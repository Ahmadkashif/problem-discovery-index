# Every Quote Waits for the Owner

**Niche:** [[niches/industrial-distributors/small-independent-distributors/profile|Small Independent Industrial Distributors]]
**Industry:** [[industries/industrial-distributors|Industrial Distributors]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** At a small distributor only the owner knows how to price non-stock and special items, so every such quote queues behind the owner's day while the plant buyer waits.
**Tags:** #linear-regression #gradient-boosting #feature-engineering #revenue-impact #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let a single-branch distributor answer a plant's quote request as fast and as accurately as a national broadliner, and whoever cuts the small house's quote turnaround from hours to minutes without adding staff takes the account.

## The Problem
Counter and inside staff can quote stocked items from the ERP. Anything else — a non-stock bearing, a special-order gearbox, a manufacturer's new price after an increase — goes to the owner, who knows the cost, the customer's sensitivity and the margin the house can take. The owner is also on sales calls and at the counter, so these quotes wait hours or until evening, and some are lost before they go out.

## Why It's Still Broken
Pricing software is priced and designed for distributors with pricing analysts. The owner's pricing rules were never written down, the ERP offers only matrix pricing, and manufacturer cost changes arrive as files and letters that the owner updates in costs but not in sell prices.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Learn the owner's pricing from past quotes and invoices — margin by item visibility (searchable commodity versus specialist part), customer type and order size — and give staff a suggested price with a confidence band for non-stock items, routing only out-of-band quotes to the owner. Apply incoming cost changes to suggested sell prices monthly for the owner to approve in bulk.

## Who Feels the Pain
Owners who are the bottleneck on their own quotes, staff who can't answer customers, and plant buyers who go elsewhere while they wait.

## Impact If Fixed
If staff can price ~60–70% of non-stock quotes without the owner (estimate), turnaround on those quotes falls from hours to minutes, and consistent pricing recovers ~1–2 points of gross margin (estimate) on a few million dollars of sales.
