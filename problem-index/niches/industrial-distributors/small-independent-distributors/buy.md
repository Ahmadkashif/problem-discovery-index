# Broadliner-Grade Search on a Twenty-Year-Old ERP

**Niche:** [[niches/industrial-distributors/small-independent-distributors/profile|Small Independent Industrial Distributors]]
**Industry:** [[industries/industrial-distributors|Industrial Distributors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** B2B e-commerce platforms give a distributor a storefront; a small distributor needs one that answers a plant buyer's request with a price and stock on thin product data and an ERP with no modern API.
**Tags:** #transformers #word-embeddings #k-nearest-neighbors #data-integration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let a single-branch distributor answer a plant's quote request as fast and as accurately as a national broadliner, and whoever cuts the small house's quote turnaround from hours to minutes without adding staff takes the account.

## The Problem
Local plant buyers increasingly start on a search box. If the local distributor's site can't find a bearing by its common designation or a belt by a competitor's number, or shows "call for price," the request becomes a phone call or an email that waits for the owner — and the buyer orders from a national site that answered instantly, even when the local branch has the part on the shelf.

## What Already Exists
B2B e-commerce platforms and the practices mapped under [[niches/b2b-commerce-platforms/part-search-and-selection/profile|Part Search & Selection]], plus buying-group web templates.

## The Customization Gap
Search that works on sparse item data by drawing on pooled manufacturer content and part-number patterns; customer-specific prices pulled from an old ERP through file exports or screen reading rather than an API, so the answer comes with a price; branch stock shown honestly; and a "can't find it? send a photo" path that drafts a quote for the counter to confirm instead of a dead end.

## Target Customer
Owner-presidents of single- and two-branch industrial distributors.

## Impact If Solved
If even ~20–30% of the requests that now arrive by phone or email are answered on the site with a price (estimate), the owner's quote queue shrinks by the same share, and local stock stops losing orders to national sites for lack of search.
