# Federal MRO Buyers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/industrial-distributors|Industrial Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Federal buying organisations that purchase maintenance, repair and operating supplies at scale for bases, depots and agencies — the Defense Logistics Agency through prime-vendor contracts, and GSA through its schedules and catalogue programmes — and the cooperative contracts that state and local buyers use. Their contract and category analysts research prices, compliance and supplier performance. Broadliners and regional distributors compete for these contracts.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–100 contract, pricing and category analysts across the relevant programmes (estimate)
**Output:** Price reasonableness reviews, contract compliance audits, category strategies and award evaluations
**Proprietary data:** Transaction-level government purchase records across suppliers and sites
**Clock:** Fiscal-year obligation deadlines and contract option periods
**Buyer:** Contracting officers and programme directors; no commercial market for the analysis
