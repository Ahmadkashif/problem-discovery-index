# Industrial Vending & Crib Technology Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/industrial-distributors|Industrial Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Makers of the vending machines, lockers, weight bins and crib-management software that distributors and plants use for point-of-use inventory — AutoCrib, CribMaster, Apex and SupplyPro among them — selling hardware, software subscriptions and support to distributors, which place them in customers' plants. The largest programmes (Fastenal's FMI, Grainger KeepStock) run on the distributors' own platforms and count as operator work.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 data and analytics staff per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Usage reports and dashboards inside the software
**Proprietary data:** Dispense logs across installed machines, which describe customers' consumption
**Clock:** None external
**Buyer:** Distributor VP of inventory solutions
