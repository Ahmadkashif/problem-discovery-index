# Rebate & SPA Claim Administration Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/industrial-distributors|Industrial Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Software and service firms that sit between manufacturers and distributors on rebate and special-pricing money: programme administration, claim preparation and adjudication, and recovery audits. Enable and Vistex serve mainly the manufacturer side; Band, Claro and recovery specialists target distributors' claim desks. Enable also publishes an annual State of Volume Rebates survey. Clients pay subscriptions or a share of recovered money. The distributor's own claim function is the level-1 niche [[niches/industrial-distributors/rebates-and-spa-claims/profile|Supplier Rebates & SPA Claims]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~3–10 leakage-analytics and recovery-insight staff at the largest providers (estimate). Claim analysts, programme administrators and recovery auditors who prepare, adjudicate and examine claims are production headcount and are not counted.
**Output:** Leakage and denial-pattern findings, programme performance reports; the claim files themselves are production output
**Proprietary data:** Claim and denial histories across many distributors and manufacturers, held under each client's service agreement
**Clock:** Claim windows and programme periods set in each manufacturer's agreements
**Buyer:** Distributor CFO or controller; manufacturer VP of channel or trade programmes
