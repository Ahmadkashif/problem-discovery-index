# Fluid Power & Hose Assembly Counters

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/industrial-distributors|Industrial Distributors]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a failed hose or fitting carried to the counter into a working assembly on the first build — right thread, right pressure rating, right length — and whoever gets the machine back with zero refits takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$25B a year of US fluid power distribution sales (hydraulic and pneumatic components, hose and fittings), of which ~$5B is hose assembly and counter repair work (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~6% of the two codes' receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — identification by thread gauge and eye, hand-written tickets, and crimp specs from binders or manufacturer apps
**Target Buyer:** Fluid power division manager or hose shop lead at the distributor
**Automation Potential:** Medium — identification and spec checks automate; cutting, crimping and testing stay hands-on

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Hydraulic and pneumatic distributors (Parker and Eaton channel partners, regional fluid power houses, and the hose shops inside broadline branches) sell a build-to-order product. A technician walks in with a burst hose from an excavator, a press or a refuse truck, and the counter person must identify both end fittings among dozens of thread and seal families (JIC, ORFS, NPT, BSPP, metric and more), match hose construction to pressure, temperature and fluid, measure the length and orientation, and crimp it to the manufacturer's spec. A mistake leaks, blows off, or doesn't fit. The skill is tactile and visual, built over years, and the stakes include injury: a hydraulic hose failure at working pressure is dangerous.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Thread gauges and calipers, manufacturer hose and crimp selection apps, mobile hose-repair vans, and counter tickets in the ERP. The gaps: no record of which assembly went on which machine, cross-brand fitting identification depends on the person, and the spec checks (pressure, temperature, media compatibility) are done from memory under time pressure.
