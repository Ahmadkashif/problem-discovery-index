# Hose Configurators With the Counter's Cross-Brand Knowledge

**Niche:** [[niches/industrial-distributors/fluid-power-hose-counters/profile|Fluid Power & Hose Assembly Counters]]
**Industry:** [[industries/industrial-distributors|Industrial Distributors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Every hose maker has a configurator for its own hose and fittings; the counter builds with whatever is on the shelf and needs the checks across brands.
**Tags:** #decision-trees #large-language-models #k-nearest-neighbors #compliance #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a failed hose or fitting carried to the counter into a working assembly on the first build — right thread, right pressure rating, right length — and whoever gets the machine back with zero refits takes the account.

## The Problem
The person building the assembly must check that the hose, both fittings and the ferrules are a compatible, rated combination for the pressure, temperature and fluid, and set the crimp diameter correctly. Manufacturers warn against mixing components across brands, yet branches stock more than one line and customers bring in other brands' assemblies to copy.

## What Already Exists
Manufacturers' hose and crimp selection apps and printed spec charts, crimper spec tables, and ERP item records.

## The Customization Gap
A distributor-side configurator that knows the branch's actual stock across brands, translates a competitor's assembly into an approved equivalent built from in-stock components, enforces each manufacturer's compatibility and crimp rules, checks the application against pressure, temperature and fluid limits, and prints a tag that records the spec.

## Target Customer
Hose shop leads and fluid power branch managers at multi-line distributors.

## Impact If Solved
If cross-brand checks remove most out-of-spec builds, which cause an estimated ~1–3% of assemblies to come back (estimate), and let the counter build from in-stock components ~20–30% more often when the original brand is missing (estimate), every hose sold also leaves with a documented spec.
