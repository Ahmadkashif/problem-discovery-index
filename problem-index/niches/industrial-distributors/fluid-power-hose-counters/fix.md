# Rebuilding the Same Hose Without a Record

**Niche:** [[niches/industrial-distributors/fluid-power-hose-counters/profile|Fluid Power & Hose Assembly Counters]]
**Industry:** [[industries/industrial-distributors|Industrial Distributors]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The same machine's hose fails every year, and every year the counter identifies and measures it from scratch because nobody recorded the last build.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #feature-engineering #quick-win #automation #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a failed hose or fitting carried to the counter into a working assembly on the first build — right thread, right pressure rating, right length — and whoever gets the machine back with zero refits takes the account.

## The Problem
Counter sales are written as a ticket with a hose type, two fittings and a length, often against a cash or generic account. The machine and position the hose came from are not captured, so the next failure on the same machine starts over.

## Why It's Still Broken
Counter tickets are built for speed and billing. Nobody has a reason to record the machine at the moment of sale, and tags on assemblies, when used, aren't linked to anything the branch can search.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A tag with a scannable code on every assembly that links to its full spec and the customer's machine and position, captured in two taps at the counter. The next time the customer brings it in or scans it in the field, the branch rebuilds it without measuring, and the record shows how often each position fails.

## Who Feels the Pain
Counter staff repeating work, equipment owners waiting for a rebuild, and fleets with no record of hose replacements.

## Impact If Fixed
Repeat builds take ~5 minutes instead of ~20–30 for a re-identification (estimate), and failure frequency per position becomes visible to the customer, which sells preventive replacement.
