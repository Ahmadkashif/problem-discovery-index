# Distributor Rollup & Platform Integration Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/industrial-distributors|Industrial Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Serial acquirers and private-equity platforms that buy regional industrial distributors and integrate them: Applied Industrial (~$5.0B in fiscal 2026 revenue), DXP, Bearing Distributors Inc and PE-backed platforms, with Motion due to become a separate public company in the first quarter of 2027. Their corporate development and integration teams screen targets, model synergies and track post-close performance.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 corporate development, pricing and integration analysts per platform (estimate)
**Output:** Target screens, valuation and synergy models, integration scorecards
**Proprietary data:** Acquired companies' customer, pricing and margin histories, plus the platform's integration track record
**Clock:** Deal timetables, lender covenants and sponsor holding periods
**Buyer:** Head of corporate development or the platform CFO
