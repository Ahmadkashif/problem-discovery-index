# Industrial Buying & Marketing Groups

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/industrial-distributors|Industrial Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Member-owned or privately run groups that negotiate supplier programmes and rebates for independent distributors and give them marketing, content and benchmarking. Affiliated Distributors reported record 2025 member sales of $100B across 1,000+ members and 9,000+ branches in construction and industrial supply; NetPlus Alliance lists 400 distributor members and $62B in sales on its site. Members pay dues and share rebates; suppliers pay to be preferred.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 analytics, supplier-programme and member-insight staff at the largest group (estimate)
**Output:** Supplier programme performance, member benchmarks, rebate calculations, category and market reports for members and suppliers
**Proprietary data:** Members' purchase and rebate-reporting data across hundreds of suppliers, which no single distributor or manufacturer sees
**Clock:** Annual supplier programme negotiations and rebate periods
**Buyer:** Group CEO or head of member services

**Kill switches:** **data isn't theirs** — members report purchases to the group to calculate rebates, and that data belongs to the members, who compete with each other. Scored below [[niches/independent-retailers/retail-cooperative-buying-groups/profile|Retail Cooperative Buying Groups]] (48) because retail co-ops are wholesalers that own the transaction, while industrial groups mostly see members' reports of purchases made direct from suppliers.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. Analysis is bundled into membership rather than invoiced, and the corpus is members' data.
