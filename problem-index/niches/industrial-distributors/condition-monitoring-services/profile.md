# Rotating-Equipment Condition Monitoring Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/industrial-distributors|Industrial Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Service firms that collect vibration, temperature and oil readings on plants' motors, pumps, fans and gearboxes and sell diagnoses: which bearing is failing, why, and how long it has. The pocket as defined covers route-based and independent reliability contractors, manufacturers' monitoring services (SKF, Schaeffler, Timken), and the reliability groups inside BPT distributors such as Motion and Applied. Plants pay by route, asset or programme. Sensor-subscription vendors such as Augury and Tractian sell into the same budget under a different data regime, discussed under the kill switch. The market contest for this budget is mapped as the sub-niche [[niches/industrial-distributors/reliability-condition-monitoring/profile|Reliability & Condition Monitoring Services]]; this pocket scores the providers as an insight business.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–200 vibration analysts and reliability engineers reviewing machine data at a larger provider (estimate); installers and route technicians collecting readings not counted
**Output:** Fault diagnoses, remaining-life estimates, route reports and reliability recommendations per asset
**Proprietary data:** Years of vibration, oil and process readings across many plants, with some confirmed failure outcomes, held per client engagement
**Clock:** The plant's own shutdown and maintenance windows; failures follow physics, not a third party's deadline
**Buyer:** Plant reliability or maintenance manager; corporate reliability director at multi-site manufacturers

**Kill switches:** **data isn't theirs**, for the service firms the pocket is defined as — route-based contractors, OEM services and distributors' reliability groups analyse each client's readings under a service agreement, with no checked evidence they may pool them, consistent with [[niches/contract-packagers/packaging-machinery-service-analytics/profile|Packaging Machinery Service & Line-Performance Analytics]]. The sensor-subscription half differs: Augury states publicly that its generic model is "crowdsourced from all of Augury's customers" (https://www.augury.com/blog/machine-health/big-news-at-scale-augury-achieves-continuous-learning-for-its-machine-health-ai/), the same kind of public practice evidence the vault has accepted from a company's own filings, so scored on its own that half would carry no kill switch and would sit **below threshold** at the same 44, not under a kill switch.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. The diagnosis is the product and analysts scale with monitored assets, but there is no external clock, and for the service firms the cross-plant corpus rests on clients' data.
