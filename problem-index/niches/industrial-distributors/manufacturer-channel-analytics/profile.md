# Bearing & PT Manufacturers' Channel Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/industrial-distributors|Industrial Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The channel, pricing and market-intelligence teams at bearing, power transmission and fluid power manufacturers (SKF, Timken, Schaeffler, Regal Rexnord, Gates, Parker and peers) that analyse distributor point-of-sale and claim data, set distributor programmes and special pricing, and size end markets. Their work serves the manufacturer's own sales and pricing decisions. The manufacturers themselves are a queued build (power transmission component makers).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–50 channel, pricing and market analysts per large manufacturer (estimate)
**Output:** Distributor performance and programme analysis, SPA approval and pricing decisions, end-market sizing
**Proprietary data:** Point-of-sale and SPA claim data reported by distributors, showing end-customer demand by account and region
**Clock:** Internal quarterly programme and pricing cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Channel or Distribution Sales
