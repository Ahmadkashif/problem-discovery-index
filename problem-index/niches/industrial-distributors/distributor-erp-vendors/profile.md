# Distributor ERP Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/industrial-distributors|Industrial Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The vendors of the ERPs industrial distributors run on — Epicor (Prophet 21 and Eclipse), Infor (CSD, formerly SX.e), DDI System and others — which sell licences, hosting and services, and increasingly embedded AI (Epicor began rolling out its Prism agents, 18+ of them, into Prophet 21 in 2026). Some publish benchmarks or industry reports; their analytics teams mostly build product features.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 product analytics and industry-solutions staff per vendor working on distribution (estimate)
**Output:** Product analytics and AI features, occasional benchmark reports, implementation guidance
**Proprietary data:** Hosted customers' transaction data, where contracts allow, and implementation experience across thousands of distributors
**Clock:** Release cycles set by the vendor
**Buyer:** Distributor CIO or CFO buying the ERP
