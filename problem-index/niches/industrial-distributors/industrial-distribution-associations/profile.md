# Industrial Distribution Trade Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/industrial-distributors|Industrial Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Associations for the channel: PTDA for power transmission and motion control (its 2025 report sized the North American market at $104B, US $85B), the Industrial Supply Association for MRO supply, the Fluid Power Distributors Association, MHEDA for material handling dealers, and NAW across wholesale distribution. NAED plays the same role in electrical distribution. They run conferences, education, benchmarking surveys and market reports, funded by dues and events.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research staff at each; market reports and benchmarks are commonly produced with outside research firms (estimate)
**Output:** Market-size reports, operating benchmarks, forecasts, education
**Proprietary data:** Member survey responses and benchmark histories
**Clock:** Annual conference and report calendars
**Buyer:** Association CEO
