## The Problem
BPT distributors employ application specialists who know drives, bearings and couplings in depth. There are few of them, each covering many branches, and their time is consumed by routine questions that a well-supported rep could answer, while the hard cases wait.

## Why It's Still Broken
Questions reach the specialist by phone and email with missing information — no photo, no shaft size, no operating speed — so every case starts with a round of questions. Answers given once are never captured for the next rep with the same question, and specialists rarely have time to write things down.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A structured intake that collects photos, dimensions, speeds and loads before a case reaches the specialist, with answers drafted from similar past cases. Every resolved case is stored with its inputs and answer, so routine questions are answered from the archive and only new ones reach the specialist.

## Who Feels the Pain
Application specialists drowning in routine questions, branch reps waiting on answers while a customer's line is down, and plants that get a guess instead of a fix.

## Impact If Fixed
If ~50% of routine questions are answered from captured cases (estimate), each specialist gains back ~3–5 days a month for on-site work and failure analysis, and branches respond in ~2–4 hours instead of ~2–3 days (estimate).
