# Sold Below Cost and Never Claimed

**Niche:** [[niches/industrial-distributors/rebates-and-spa-claims/profile|Supplier Rebates & SPA Claims]]
**Industry:** [[industries/industrial-distributors|Industrial Distributors]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A rep sells at the agreed SPA price, the claim never goes in, and the distributor has sold below its own cost with nothing coming back.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to file every claim the distributor has earned under each manufacturer's agreement terms before the claim window closes, and whoever recovers the most claim dollars at the lowest denial rate takes the account.

## The Problem
The most expensive SPA failure is silent: a line priced at the special price but never matched to its agreement, because the rep sold an approved equivalent, the agreement was keyed under another number, or the customer's ship-to didn't match. Nothing flags it, and the claim window closes.

## Why It's Still Broken
Claims are built from the agreement side — which lines qualify under this SPA — rather than from the sales side — which lines were sold below normal cost. Margin reports show the loss as low margin, not as an unclaimed recovery, and nobody owns the gap between pricing and accounting.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A weekly sweep of invoice lines sold below standard cost, each matched to the most likely agreement with a reason when no match is found, routed to the claim desk while the window is still open. Repeat causes (wrong part number in the agreement, ship-to mismatch) are fixed at the source.

## Who Feels the Pain
Controllers closing the month, branch managers whose margins look worse than they are, and owners whose net profit depends on claims.

## Impact If Fixed
If unclaimed SPA lines run at ~5–15% of eligible claims (estimate), finding most of them is the fastest margin gain available to a distributor, with no new sales required.
