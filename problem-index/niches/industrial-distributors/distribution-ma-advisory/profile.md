# Industrial Distribution M&A Advisory

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/industrial-distributors|Industrial Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Investment banks' distribution groups and boutique advisors that value and sell independent industrial distributors to strategic acquirers and private-equity platforms, and publish multiple and deal-activity reports that owners plan exits against. Fees are success-based on closed deals.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 3–15 bankers and analysts per distribution practice (estimate)
**Output:** Valuations, offering memoranda, buyer lists, sector deal reports
**Proprietary data:** Deal files and buyer feedback from past processes
**Clock:** Deal timetables set with buyers and lenders
**Buyer:** Owner-president selling the business
