# Breakdown Replacement & Interchange

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/industrial-distributors|Industrial Distributors]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to identify the exact replacement, or an interchange the plant will accept, from whatever arrives — a worn bearing, a nameplate photo, a superseded number — and put it in the maintenance technician's hand inside the downtime window, and whoever closes the most breakdown calls on the first identification takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$15B a year of unplanned and emergency BPT sales by US distributors (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~4% of the two codes' receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — the request arrives by phone, text or at the counter, and identification happens in a person's head
**Target Buyer:** Branch manager and inside sales manager at BPT distributors
**Automation Potential:** High for identification and multi-branch stock search; the delivery stays physical

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Breakdown supply is a speed contest decided at the moment of the call. The plant doesn't care about the brand, the catalogue or the price nearly as much as about getting a part that fits within hours. The distributor wins on three things in sequence: identifying correctly from poor inputs, knowing an acceptable interchange when the exact part isn't on the shelf, and having it somewhere within driving distance. Local branch density and veterans' identification skill decide it. Online channels lose here because the buyer can't name the part. Reliability services are a different contest, scored over months.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Manufacturer cross-reference apps (each covers its own brand), distributor ERPs with multi-branch stock, catalogue search, and the general part-search problem mapped in [[niches/b2b-commerce-platforms/part-search-and-selection/profile|Part Search & Selection]]. The gaps: no cross-brand interchange graph with evidence of what fitted, no record of plant-specific acceptance, superseded numbers that dead-end, and no capture of the request that started the call.
