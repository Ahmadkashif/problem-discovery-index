# The Superseded Number That Returns Nothing

**Niche:** [[niches/industrial-distributors/breakdown-replacement-interchange/profile|Breakdown Replacement & Interchange]]
**Industry:** [[industries/industrial-distributors|Industrial Distributors]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A gearbox or motor number from a brand that was acquired twice returns nothing in any catalogue, and only a veteran remembers what replaced it.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #graph-theory #transformers #data-integration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to identify the exact replacement, or an interchange the plant will accept, from whatever arrives — a worn bearing, a nameplate photo, a superseded number — and put it in the maintenance technician's hand inside the downtime window, and whoever closes the most breakdown calls on the first identification takes the account.

## The Problem
Power transmission brands have consolidated repeatedly, and product lines have been renumbered, merged and discontinued. Plants keep running machines for decades, so the number on the nameplate often belongs to a product family that no current catalogue lists. The rep searches, finds nothing, and starts calling around.

## Why It's Still Broken
Supersession chains live in old printed catalogues, manufacturers' internal systems and veterans' memories. Current catalogues drop discontinued items, and no shared record links old numbers to current equivalents with the dimensions that matter.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A supersession index built from archived catalogues, manufacturer notices and the distributor's own resolved history, mapping each old number to its successor with the dimensional and ratio data that confirm the match. Every time a rep resolves an obsolete number, the resolution is added.

## Who Feels the Pain
Reps on breakdown calls, plant technicians nursing old machines, and veterans who field every obsolete-number question in the region.

## Impact If Fixed
Obsolete-number calls that take ~1–3 hours of phoning resolve in ~5 minutes (estimate), and knowledge that used to retire with a person stays in the branch.
