# Bearing Trade-Remedy Enforcement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/industrial-distributors|Industrial Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Commerce Department's antidumping and countervailing duty operations and U.S. Customs and Border Protection, which set and collect duties on imported bearings. The long-running antidumping order on tapered roller bearings from China (case A-570-601) is reviewed every year; final results of its 2024–2025 administrative review, published on 21 September 2026, kept a 92.84% China-wide rate. Distributors importing bearings, and their customs brokers, must classify, declare and pay accordingly. The economic analysis behind trade-remedy cases is profiled as [[niches/customs-brokers/trade-remedy-economic-analysis/profile|Trade Remedy Economic Analysis]] (51, kill switch).

## Insight Function
**Size:** A handful of case analysts and accountants assigned to the bearing orders at any time (estimate); Commerce's wider AD/CVD operations are larger but serve hundreds of other orders and are not this pocket's function
**Output:** Preliminary and final review results, dumping margins, scope rulings and liquidation instructions
**Proprietary data:** Respondents' questionnaire responses, sales and cost data submitted in each review
**Clock:** Statutory deadlines for preliminary and final results in each review
**Buyer:** None in the commercial sense; the agency funds its own work
