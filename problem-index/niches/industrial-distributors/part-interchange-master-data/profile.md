# Part Interchange & MRO Master Data Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/industrial-distributors|Industrial Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms that clean and enrich plants' MRO material masters and supply part data: cataloguing services that standardise descriptions and remove duplicates (PiLog, Verdantis and similar), parts-data and logistics databases such as Accuris's Haystack, and manufacturers' own cross-reference tools. Plants pay per record or per project; distributors use the output to quote and stock. Spend classification on the buyer side is mapped under [[niches/procurement-spend-platforms/spend-classification-supplier-master/profile|Spend Classification & Supplier Master]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** Large cataloguing teams (often offshore) but under 10 analysts doing anything beyond record production (estimate)
**Output:** Cleaned material masters, part attributes, cross-references
**Proprietary data:** Normalised part records across many plants; the interchange layer is mostly manufacturers'
**Clock:** Client ERP migrations and data projects
**Buyer:** Plant or corporate MRO and master-data leads
