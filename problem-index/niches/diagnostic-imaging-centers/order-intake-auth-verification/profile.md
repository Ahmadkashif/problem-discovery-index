# Referral Order Intake & Authorization Verification

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/diagnostic-imaging-centers|Diagnostic Imaging Centers]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn each faxed or electronic order into a scheduled exam whose authorization matches the exact CPT code, site and date performed, and whoever schedules the highest share of orders with no downstream authorization denial takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.2B a year of intake and pre-service staff, outsourced verification, fax and document automation and authorization-status tools at freestanding centres (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of centre revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — many orders arrive by fax, authorization numbers are read off paper or obtained by phone, and the match between approval and exam is checked by eye
**Target Buyer:** Director of patient access or revenue cycle at a multi-site operator; centre administrator at an independent
**Automation Potential:** Very High — order facts, approval facts and the scheduled exam are all structured once extracted

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Medicare requires every IDTF test to be ordered in writing by the treating physician, with the diagnosis stated (42 CFR 410.33). Commercial and Medicare Advantage plans authorize a specific CPT code, site and window through radiology benefit managers. Obtaining the authorization is the ordering practice's job: see [[niches/physician-practices/imaging-procedure-authorization/profile|🎯 Imaging & Procedure Authorization]] under [[industries/physician-practices|Physician Practices]]. Hospitals' scheduling-side check is [[niches/general-hospitals/patient-access-auth-estimates/profile|⚡ Patient Access: Prior Authorization, Eligibility & Estimates]]. The payer's decision is [[niches/health-insurance-carriers/outpatient-prior-authorization/profile|🎯 Outpatient Prior Authorization & Gold-Carding]]. This niche is the freestanding centre's own intake. It turns paper orders into pending exams, finds and verifies the approval, catches the mismatch between what was approved and what the radiologist protocolled, and amends it before the scan. Speed also wins the patient: an order faxed to several centres goes to whichever books first (Capitol Imaging, search summary).

## Current Tools & Gaps
Fax servers and inboxes, RIS intake screens, Availity and benefit-manager portals, Experian Health, Waystar and Infinx authorization tools, AbbaDox's fax parsing and voice agent (search summary), outsourced intake teams. Gaps: fax facts re-keyed; approvals checked once and never re-checked after protocol, date or site changes; denials traced back to intake weeks later, if at all.
