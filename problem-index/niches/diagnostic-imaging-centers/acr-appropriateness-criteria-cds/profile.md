# ACR Appropriateness Criteria & Imaging Order Decision Support

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/diagnostic-imaging-centers|Diagnostic Imaging Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The American College of Radiology's expert panels write and update the Appropriateness Criteria: 279 diagnostic and interventional topics with more than 1,350 clinical variants and 4,100 scenarios, rating each imaging option for each clinical situation. The topic list is free to read, filtering the ratings needs a login, and any reuse needs ACR permission. Commercially the criteria reach clinicians through CareSelect Imaging (Optum), decision support embedded in health-system EHRs that scores orders as they are placed. The programme was built to serve CMS's Appropriate Use Criteria mandate. CMS paused that programme and rescinded its regulations from 1 January 2024 and has set no date to resume.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-30 ACR methodologists, literature analysts and staff supporting volunteer physician panels, plus the decision-support licensee's content and analytics team (estimate)
**Output:** Published criteria documents and evidence tables; licensed decision-support content and order-scoring analytics
**Proprietary data:** Panel ratings and evidence syntheses (published); order-scoring logs held by the decision-support vendor for its health-system customers
**Clock:** The ACR's own update cycle. The PAMA mandate that would have set a federal clock was rescinded
**Buyer:** ACR's quality and safety leadership; the decision-support licensee's product head

**Kill switches:** none on the criteria, which carry no patient data. The order-scoring logs that the decision-support vendor holds for health systems are customer data under terms not read here (undetermined for that half).
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. This is the pocket behind the owner's MVP idea (an order-versus-ACR-criteria checker). With AUC rescinded there is no outside deadline and no mandated buyer. Payers decide appropriateness with their own benefit-manager guidelines (see [[niches/health-insurance-carriers/utilization-management/profile|🔵 Utilization Management & Prior Authorization]]). The criteria cannot be reused commercially without permission, and the benefit managers' own guidelines (EviCore, Carelon) are copyrighted too (recalled), so a checker built on either needs a licence. The licence-free route for Medicare Advantage, Medicaid, CHIP and federally facilitated exchange plans is the CMS-0057-F Prior Authorization API. From 1 January 2027 it must list the services needing authorization and identify their documentation requirements. The criteria publishers that do qualify are the licensed payer-guideline houses at [[niches/acupuncture-practices/conservative-care-guideline-publishers/profile|🔍 Conservative-Care Treatment Guideline Publishers]] (54).
