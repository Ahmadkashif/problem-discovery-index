# Imaging OEM Remote Service & Installed-Base Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/diagnostic-imaging-centers|Diagnostic Imaging Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The service data science groups at the scanner makers (GE HealthCare, Siemens Healthineers, Philips, Canon) stream machine logs from connected MRI and CT units. They predict component failures (coils, gradients, cold heads, helium levels), schedule proactive service and report utilisation back to customers. Centres pay for uptime-guaranteed service contracts and fleet dashboards, not for the analysis itself (recalled).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~50-300 service data scientists and reliability engineers per OEM (estimate)
**Output:** Failure predictions, proactive service dispatch, uptime and utilisation reports
**Proprietary data:** Machine logs from each OEM's installed base, which no competitor or independent servicer sees in full
**Clock:** Contracted uptime guarantees, which are the OEM's own commitments
**Buyer:** Head of services analytics at the OEM
