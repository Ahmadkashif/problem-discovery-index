# Care-Gap Closure Engines From Health Plans Pointed at Radiology

**Niche:** [[niches/diagnostic-imaging-centers/follow-up-recommendation-tracking/profile|Follow-Up Recommendation Tracking]]
**Industry:** [[industries/diagnostic-imaging-centers|Diagnostic Imaging Centers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Health plans and ACOs already run engines that find care gaps, assign outreach and confirm closure; a radiology follow-up is a care gap with a due date written in the report.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get each radiologist's follow-up recommendation done by the date the report gives, and whoever gets the highest share of recommended follow-up exams completed on time takes the account.

## The Problem
Centres track follow-ups with spreadsheets and navigators, while the machinery for closing care gaps at scale sits with payers and value-based groups.

## What Already Exists
Care-gap and quality platforms used for HEDIS measures and value-based contracts identify open gaps, rank patients, run outreach and record closure, analysed under [[industries/health-insurance-carriers|Health Insurance Carriers]] and [[industries/physician-practices|Physician Practices]].

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation has to create gaps from report text rather than claims. Intervals must come from radiology guidelines (Fleischner, Lung-RADS, ACR incidental-findings guidance). Completion has to count when the follow-up happened at another provider. The radiologist's medicolegal record needs documentation of each outreach attempt.

## Target Customer
Operators with value-based or ACO partners willing to share completion data, and health-system imaging joint ventures.

## Impact If Solved
Configuring an existing outreach engine gets a centre to automated follow-up tracking in a quarter instead of a year (estimate), with closure counts that partners already trust.
