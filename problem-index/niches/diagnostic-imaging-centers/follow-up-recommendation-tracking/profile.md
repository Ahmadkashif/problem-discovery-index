# Follow-Up Recommendation Tracking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/diagnostic-imaging-centers|Diagnostic Imaging Centers]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get each radiologist's follow-up recommendation done by the date the report gives, and whoever gets the highest share of recommended follow-up exams completed on time takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$0.4B a year of follow-up tracking software, navigators and outreach for non-breast incidental and surveillance findings tied to outpatient imaging (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% of centre revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — recommendations are written into report text, and most centres track them only when a navigator reads reports by hand
**Target Buyer:** Operator's chief medical officer or quality director; radiology group's chief of quality
**Automation Potential:** Very High — recommendations follow standard lexicons and intervals, and completion is visible in later orders

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Radiology reports routinely recommend follow-up: a lung nodule rescan in six or twelve months under Lung-RADS or Fleischner intervals, an adrenal or thyroid incidental, a liver lesion, an indeterminate kidney cyst (recalled lexicons). The recommendation goes to the referring physician, and many are never completed. That is a patient-safety and malpractice exposure for the reading radiologist, and lost follow-up exams for the centre. Breast recalls run under MQSA and their own pathway (Breast Imaging Centers under MQSA). This niche covers everything else. Rad AI is hiring engineers for its Continuity product, which tracks follow-ups. Detection, extraction and scheduling of the follow-up are all automatable once the recommendation is a structured field.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Rad AI Continuity, follow-up modules in PowerScribe and reporting platforms, health-system incidental-findings programmes, navigators reading reports. Gaps: recommendations not extracted as structured data; no feed showing whether the follow-up happened elsewhere; outreach to referrers by fax and phone.
