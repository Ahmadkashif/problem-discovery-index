# The Nodule Recommendation Buried in Paragraph Six

**Niche:** [[niches/diagnostic-imaging-centers/follow-up-recommendation-tracking/profile|Follow-Up Recommendation Tracking]]
**Industry:** [[industries/diagnostic-imaging-centers|Diagnostic Imaging Centers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A recommendation for a follow-up CT sits at the end of a long report the referring physician skims, and nothing flags it.
**Tags:** #bert #large-language-models #quick-win #worker-facing #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get each radiologist's follow-up recommendation done by the date the report gives, and whoever gets the highest share of recommended follow-up exams completed on time takes the account.

## The Problem
Incidental findings on an abdominal CT ordered for pain often appear in the findings section, with the follow-up recommendation folded into the impression among other items. Busy referrers read the first line.

## Why It's Still Broken
Report templates do not force a structured recommendation field, and radiologists resist extra clicks.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Automatic extraction of any follow-up recommendation into a highlighted block at the top of the report and a separate notice to the referrer, with the radiologist confirming in one click.

## Who Feels the Pain
Radiologists exposed when follow-ups are missed; referrers who never saw the line; patients whose nodule grows unwatched.

## Impact If Fixed
Making every recommendation visible and acknowledged raises completion before any outreach system is built. A few points of completion at a large centre is hundreds of follow-up exams a year (estimate).
