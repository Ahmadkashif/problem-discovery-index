# Radiology Business Management Benchmarks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/diagnostic-imaging-centers|Diagnostic Imaging Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Trade associations for radiology business managers and imaging administrators, such as RBMA and AHRA, run member surveys on staffing, productivity, compensation and operations, and publish benchmarks for members (recalled). The ASRT's staffing survey for technologists is a similar member product (CT vacancy 19.4% and MRI 17.4% in 2025).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Fewer than 10 research staff per association (estimate)
**Output:** Member benchmark surveys and reports
**Proprietary data:** Member survey responses
**Clock:** Self-set survey cycles
**Buyer:** Association executive director
