# De-identified Medical Imaging Data Licensors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/diagnostic-imaging-centers|Diagnostic Imaging Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms that source imaging studies and reports from provider partners, many of them freestanding centres, de-identify and standardise them, and license curated cohorts to AI developers, device makers and pharmaceutical companies. Segmed is representative (Series A $10.4M in 2024; claims partners across thousands of provider locations, search summary), alongside Gradient Health and data-exchange features in imaging platforms (recalled). The curated dataset and cohort-building service is the invoice.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-50 data scientists, clinical curators and data engineers per vendor (estimate); de-identification operations and annotators are production
**Output:** Licensed de-identified imaging cohorts, cohort-search tools, curation and labelling services
**Proprietary data:** Licensed rights to large de-identified archives from partner sites; the underlying images originate with providers, and comparable archives are available from competing brokers
**Clock:** The vendor's own release and delivery calendar; buyers' FDA submission timelines are the client's dates
**Buyer:** Head of data partnerships at the vendor; head of data at AI developers
