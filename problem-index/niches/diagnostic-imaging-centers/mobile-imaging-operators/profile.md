# Mobile Imaging Operators & Rural Shared Units

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/diagnostic-imaging-centers|Diagnostic Imaging Centers]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep each mobile MRI, CT or PET unit scanning on the days it is parked at a host site, and whoever fills the highest share of booked unit-days with completed, paid scans takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$0.3B a year of routing, scheduling, RIS and coordination spend at mobile imaging operators and their host sites (estimate), on a mobile service base of perhaps $2B–$3B (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1% of centre revenue in tooling (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — unit rotations are planned in spreadsheets, host sites book patients in their own systems, and paperwork travels with the driver
**Target Buyer:** Operations director at a mobile imaging company; radiology manager at a rural or critical access host hospital
**Automation Potential:** Medium-High — rotations, host bookings and authorization checks are schedulable; the driving and set-up are not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Trailer-mounted MRI, CT and PET-CT units bring advanced imaging to rural hospitals, clinics and smaller centres that cannot justify a fixed scanner, parking at each host one or two days a week. The economics are unit-days: a unit parked at a host with half a schedule loses money, and a unit that breaks down strands a week of patients across three towns. Medicare rules treat mobile units specially: an IDTF must enrol and bill for all mobile diagnostic services unless they are furnished under arrangement with a hospital (42 CFR 410.33). Tools are built for fixed sites with their own front desks. They ignore a scanner whose schedule is booked by three different host hospitals, whose staff drive between towns, and whose authorization windows must line up with the day the trailer is in town. Rural hospitals as buyers are covered under [[industries/general-hospitals|General Hospitals]]. Mobile population screening (biometrics, hearing, sleep testing) belongs to Health Screening Services, which is still in the build queue.

## Current Tools & Gaps
RIS and PACS from the operator, host hospitals' own scheduling systems, phone and spreadsheet rotation planning, OEM remote service. Gaps: no shared view of a unit-day's bookings across host systems; authorizations that expire before the unit returns; breakdowns rebooked by phone.
