# MQSA & State Radiation Control Inspection Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/diagnostic-imaging-centers|Diagnostic Imaging Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
FDA's MQSA programme certifies the 9,137 mammography facilities and has inspections carried out each year, mostly by state inspectors under contract (recalled). State radiation control programmes register and inspect X-ray and CT equipment, and some states run certificate-of-need review for MRI and CT. Fees are charged per facility or unit.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Inspectors and certification staff are production; programme analysts are typically fewer than 10 per agency (estimate)
**Output:** Inspection reports, certificates, national statistics, enforcement actions
**Proprietary data:** Inspection and certification records for every mammography facility, and state equipment registries
**Clock:** Statutory annual inspection cycles
**Buyer:** Programme director
