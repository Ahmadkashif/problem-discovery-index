# Radiologist Reading Capacity

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/diagnostic-imaging-centers|Diagnostic Imaging Centers]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor here is fighting to get every study a centre acquires read and reported. But outsourced reading contracts are won by whoever covers the highest share of a centre's after-hours and overflow studies with major-discrepancy rates under the contract threshold, and reading software is won by whoever raises studies finalised per radiologist hour without more addenda. Different buyers, vendors and costs of failure, so the niche is decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$4B a year of professional reading for freestanding centres, plus ~$0.6B of reading, reporting and worklist software used on those studies (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~20% of centre-related spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High — reading is fully digital and remote, but worklists, prior retrieval and dictation still consume radiologist time by hand
**Target Buyer:** Centre COO (for reading contracts); radiology group president or CIO (for reading software)
**Automation Potential:** Medium — drafting, prior retrieval and routing are automatable; the interpretation and signature are not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A centre can scan only as many patients as someone can read. Radiologists enrolled to treat Medicare patients numbered 37,482 in 2023, and supply is projected only to keep pace with rising imaging demand to 2055 (Neiman Health Policy Institute, search summary). Centres buy reads in two ways: from a contracted group or teleradiology firm, or by employing or partnering with radiologists who use the centre's or group's reading platform. The first contest is coverage and read quality at a per-study price: how much of the centre's after-hours and overflow volume gets read, and how often a second reader finds a major discrepancy. The second is productivity per radiologist hour. They have different winners: vRad, USARAD, StatRad or a local group in the first, Rad AI, PowerScribe, Mosaic Reporting, DeepHealth, Sirona and New Lantern in the second. Hospital radiology departments buy the same services and tools for inpatient and emergency work under [[industries/general-hospitals|General Hospitals]]. This niche covers outpatient centre volume.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Reading contracts with local groups and teleradiology firms; PACS and cloud reading platforms; PowerScribe dictation; report-drafting and detection AI. Gaps: worklists sorted by arrival; priors scattered across sites; turnaround measured by group, not by study type; no joined record of reads, addenda and discrepancies per reader.
