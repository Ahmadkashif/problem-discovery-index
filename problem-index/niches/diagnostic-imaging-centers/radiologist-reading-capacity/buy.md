# Workforce Management From Contact Centres for the Reading Pool

**Niche:** [[niches/diagnostic-imaging-centers/radiologist-reading-capacity/profile|Radiologist Reading Capacity]]
**Industry:** [[industries/diagnostic-imaging-centers|Diagnostic Imaging Centers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Contact centres have forecast arrivals and staffed skills-based queues for decades; a radiology reading pool is the same problem with licences and subspecialties as skills.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #optimization-fundamentals #monte-carlo-methods #workflow-orchestration #data-integration
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor here is fighting to get every study a centre acquires read and reported. But outsourced reading contracts are won by whoever covers the highest share of a centre's after-hours and overflow studies with major-discrepancy rates under the contract threshold, and reading software is won by whoever raises studies finalised per radiologist hour without more addenda. Different buyers, vendors and costs of failure, so the niche is decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Reading groups schedule radiologists in spreadsheets and route studies by modality, with turnaround breaches discovered after the fact.

## What Already Exists
Workforce management suites for contact centres (NICE, Verint, Genesys) forecast arrivals by interval, staff skills-based queues, and track service levels in real time.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs skills defined as state licences, hospital and centre credentialing, and subspecialty. Handle times must vary by modality, body part and prior count. Service levels have to be set per client contract (one-hour stat, 24-hour routine). The feed must come from PACS worklists rather than telephony, and the rules must respect radiologist preferences and fairness so hard cases are not left for the last reader.

## Target Customer
Teleradiology firms and multi-site groups reading for many centres and hospitals.

## Impact If Solved
A proven forecasting and routing engine, configured for licensure and subspecialty, can lift contracted turnaround compliance by several points within a quarter (estimate) without hiring.
