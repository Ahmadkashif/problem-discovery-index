# Clinical Trial Imaging Core Labs

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/diagnostic-imaging-centers|Diagnostic Imaging Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Central imaging laboratories run the imaging side of drug and device trials. They qualify imaging sites (often freestanding centres), write imaging charters, check incoming scans for protocol compliance, and organise blinded central reads that produce endpoints such as tumour response or lesion volume. Clario, ICON's medical imaging unit, Imaging Endpoints and Calyx are representative (recalled). Pharmaceutical and device sponsors pay per study, and the cleaned, adjudicated imaging endpoint dataset is the deliverable. Radiology AI developers' reader studies for FDA clearance are a related service, run mostly by device CROs (see below).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~50-300 imaging scientists, medical physicists, biostatisticians and project scientists per large core lab, excluding central readers and image-QC technicians, who are production (estimate)
**Output:** Imaging charters, site qualification and QC reports, adjudicated endpoint datasets and statistical summaries for regulatory submissions
**Proprietary data:** Cross-trial records of site image quality, protocol deviations and reader variability, which no single sponsor sees; the images and endpoints themselves belong to sponsors
**Clock:** Sponsors' database-lock and submission dates, which are the client's schedule
**Buyer:** Head of imaging or clinical operations at the sponsor; the core lab's chief scientific officer as the pocket's own buyer

**Kill switches:** **Data isn't theirs**, plus the validated-system regime. Trial images and endpoints are sponsor-owned under study contracts, and the systems that touch them fall under 21 CFR Part 11 validation. The same switch holds [[niches/medical-device-mfg/device-clinical-research-organizations/profile|🔍 Device Clinical Research Organizations]] (52, kill switch), which run most AI reader studies. That pocket's Q4 5 predates the ruling that a sponsor's submission date is a client clock (Q4 4 at most).
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. The deepest cross-site view of image quality in the industry, built on data the core lab is paid to handle and may not reuse.
