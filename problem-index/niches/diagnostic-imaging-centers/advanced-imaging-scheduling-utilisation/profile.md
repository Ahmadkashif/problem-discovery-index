# Advanced Imaging Scheduling & Scanner Utilisation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/diagnostic-imaging-centers|Diagnostic Imaging Centers]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn each MRI and CT referral into a completed, paid exam in the next open scanner slot, and whoever completes the most paid exams per scanner hour takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.5B a year of scheduling staff, RIS scheduling modules, reminder and voice-agent services and capacity tools for MRI and CT at freestanding centres (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of centre revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — every booking is in the RIS, but slot templates are fixed by body part, overbooking is by gut feel and gaps are filled by whoever is free to phone
**Target Buyer:** COO or VP Operations at a multi-site operator; centre administrator at an independent
**Automation Potential:** High — attendance and table time are predictable from booking and protocol data the RIS already holds

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
MRI and CT carry most of a centre's margin: RadNet did 523,405 MRI and 299,041 CT procedures in Q4 2025 alone, with same-center MRI volume up 11.4%. A scanner's revenue is capped by hours, and its hours are lost to three things: no-shows (19.3% of outpatient MRI appointments in one published series before targeted calls), exams that overrun or finish early against fixed slots, and referrals lost to a competitor that booked faster. Unlike the generic booking problem in [[industries/scheduling-booking-platforms|Scheduling & Booking Platforms]], the slot here depends on the protocol, contrast, implants, coils and a technologist pool short by 17%–19% (ASRT 2025). Hospital outpatient scheduling has its own patient-access niche under [[industries/general-hospitals|General Hospitals]]. This niche is the freestanding centre's scanner.

## Current Tools & Gaps
RIS scheduling modules (DeepHealth, Merative, Fujifilm, AbbaDox), Luma Health and similar reminder and waitlist tools, AbbaDox's voice agent for outbound booking (search summary), and spreadsheets for templates. Gaps: slot lengths not derived from protocols; overbooking unpriced; waitlists that ignore authorization windows; utilisation reported as exams per day rather than paid exams per scanner hour.
