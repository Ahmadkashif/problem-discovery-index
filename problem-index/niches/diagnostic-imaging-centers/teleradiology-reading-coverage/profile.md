# Teleradiology & Outsourced Reading Coverage

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/diagnostic-imaging-centers|Diagnostic Imaging Centers]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to read the evening, weekend, subspecialty and overflow studies a centre's own radiologists cannot cover, and whoever covers the highest share of the centre's after-hours and overflow studies with peer-review major-discrepancy rates under the contract threshold takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.5B a year of outsourced, overflow and after-hours reading bought by or for freestanding centres (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of centre-related spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High — images move over the network, but coverage is planned by roster, licences are tracked by hand, and peer-review results stay inside the reading firm
**Target Buyer:** Centre COO or medical director; radiology group president buying overflow
**Automation Potential:** Medium — routing, licensure matching and discrepancy sampling are mechanical; the read is not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Centres with evening, weekend or subspecialty volume their own group cannot cover buy reads by the study from teleradiology firms or larger groups. vRad (Radiology Partners) reads close to nine million studies a year across its network (search summary). USARAD (Nanox), StatRad and Teleradiology Solutions compete with it, and so do regional groups that read for many centres. The contract specifies per-study rates, which studies and subspecialties are covered, and turnaround by priority. What decides renewal is coverage and quality. Studies left unread for the morning group, or sent back because no licensed subspecialist was available, are the coverage failure. A rate of major discrepancies, found when the centre's own radiologists or a peer-review sample re-read outsourced studies, is the quality failure. The winner is the reader that takes the most of the centre's uncovered volume and keeps its major-discrepancy rate under the threshold the contract sets, not the one with the best software. In India, 5C Network runs the same model at scale for 2,000+ hospitals and centres.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Teleradiology firms' own platforms (the vRad platform was commercialised in November 2025, search summary), PACS-to-PACS links, credentialing services, peer-review programmes such as RADPEER run inside each group (recalled). Gaps: licence and credentialing not enforced in routing, so eligible studies go uncovered; peer-review discrepancy results kept by the reading firm and never reported to the centre per contract; no shared ledger of which studies were sent, covered or returned.
