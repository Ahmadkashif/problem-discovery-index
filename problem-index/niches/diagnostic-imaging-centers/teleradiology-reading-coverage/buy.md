# Supplier Quality Scorecards for Reading Contracts

**Niche:** [[niches/diagnostic-imaging-centers/teleradiology-reading-coverage/profile|Teleradiology & Outsourced Reading Coverage]]
**Industry:** [[industries/diagnostic-imaging-centers|Diagnostic Imaging Centers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Manufacturers score every supplier on delivered volume and defect rate against contract thresholds; a reading contract is a supplier delivering reads, and its defects are coverage gaps and major discrepancies.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #hypothesis-testing #confidence-intervals #workflow-orchestration #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to read the evening, weekend, subspecialty and overflow studies a centre's own radiologists cannot cover, and whoever covers the highest share of the centre's after-hours and overflow studies with peer-review major-discrepancy rates under the contract threshold takes the account.

## The Problem
Centres and reading firms argue about outsourced reads with anecdotes. The centre remembers the missed fracture and the studies left for morning. The reading firm points to its overall peer-review score, which pools all its clients.

## What Already Exists
Supplier quality management and scorecard systems in manufacturing and procurement track each supplier's delivered volume, defect rates with sample sizes, and corrective actions against contract thresholds, and give buyer and supplier the same record.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs a ledger of every study sent, covered, returned or left for morning, built from DICOM and HL7 events. It needs peer-review results per contract, with discrepancies graded on the group's major and minor scale, recorded by the centre's radiologists or a sampled re-read, and adjusted for case mix by modality and subspecialty. It needs confidence intervals so a small sample is not read as a trend, and a corrective-action workflow when a reader or study type crosses the threshold.

## Target Customer
Centre operators buying outsourced reads from several firms, and teleradiology firms selling to them.

## Impact If Solved
A shared scorecard ends quality disputes. It lets a centre move volume to the reader that covers more studies with fewer major discrepancies. Shifting even 10% of overflow to the better-scoring reader could cut major discrepancies on outsourced studies by a fifth (estimate).
