# Medical Physics Consulting & Radiation Dose Monitoring

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/diagnostic-imaging-centers|Diagnostic Imaging Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Consulting medical physicists perform acceptance testing of new scanners, annual equipment surveys (including the annual MQSA physicist survey of each mammography unit), shielding design and accreditation support for imaging centres that do not employ a physicist. Dose-monitoring software vendors collect CT and fluoroscopy dose data for review (recalled). Centres pay per survey or by subscription.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Physicists writing one survey report per unit are production; analytics staff above them are typically fewer than 10 per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Equipment survey and acceptance reports, shielding plans, dose reports
**Proprietary data:** Survey histories for the units each firm services
**Clock:** Annual survey and accreditation renewal dates, which fall on each unit
**Buyer:** Centre administrator or chief technologist
