# Radiology AI & Reporting Vendors' Clinical Data Science Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/diagnostic-imaging-centers|Diagnostic Imaging Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The machine learning, clinical science and validation teams at radiology software vendors: Aidoc, Annalise, Lunit, Qure.ai, DeepHealth (RadNet, which bought iCAD in 2025 and Gleamer in 2026, search summary), Rad AI, HOPPR and others. About three in four of the FDA's ~1,500 AI-enabled devices are radiology tools (search summary). They train detection, triage and report-drafting models on images and reports from partner sites, run the validation studies FDA clearance needs, and monitor deployed models. Customers pay for software licences, not for the analysis.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-200 ML engineers, clinical scientists and regulatory analysts per vendor (estimate); annotators are production
**Output:** FDA-cleared algorithms, validation studies, performance monitoring for customers
**Proprietary data:** Training and validation corpora assembled from partner sites, and deployed-model logs
**Clock:** FDA submissions on the vendor's own schedule; customers' go-live dates
**Buyer:** Chief scientific or chief medical officer at the vendor
