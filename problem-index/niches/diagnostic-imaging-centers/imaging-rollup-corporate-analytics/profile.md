# Imaging Center Rollup Corporate Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/diagnostic-imaging-centers|Diagnostic Imaging Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The corporate finance, operations analytics and development teams at multi-site operators. RadNet runs 418 centres in nine states and reported $2.04B revenue for 2025. Lumexa Imaging runs 192 centres, 88 of them in health-system joint ventures, in 14 states (10-Q, search summary). SimonMed, Akumin (owned by Stonepeak since its 2024 restructuring, search summary) and the health-system imaging joint ventures follow. These teams analyse site performance, payer mix, procedure growth, acquisitions and new joint ventures for their own management, lenders and public-market investors.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-100 analysts at the largest operators, ~5-30 at the rest (estimate)
**Output:** Internal site and payer analytics, acquisition and joint-venture models, investor reporting
**Proprietary data:** Procedure, scheduling, payer and cost data across hundreds of centres
**Clock:** Earnings and lender reporting dates set the reporting calendar, but the analysis itself is internal and self-paced
**Buyer:** CFO or COO of the operator
