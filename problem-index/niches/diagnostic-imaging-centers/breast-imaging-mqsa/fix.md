# The Outside Priors Nobody Chased

**Niche:** [[niches/diagnostic-imaging-centers/breast-imaging-mqsa/profile|Breast Imaging Centers under MQSA]]
**Industry:** [[industries/diagnostic-imaging-centers|Diagnostic Imaging Centers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A screening exam is read as incomplete pending outside priors, and the request sits in a fax queue while the 30-day clock for a final assessment runs.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #automation #compliance #quick-win #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get every woman recalled from a screening mammogram through her diagnostic workup, and whoever gets the highest share of recalled patients to a final assessment within 30 days takes the account.

## The Problem
Comparison with prior mammograms cuts unnecessary recalls, so radiologists ask for outside priors. The request goes by fax or phone to another facility, which under MQSA must transfer copies within 15 days of a request, but nobody tracks the request.

## Why It's Still Broken
Image exchange between unaffiliated centres still runs on CDs, faxed requests and portal invitations, and the requesting clerk has no queue with due dates.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A tracked outside-priors request with the 15-day and 30-day dates set automatically. Escalation through image-exchange networks and phone, and an alert to the reading radiologist when priors arrive.

## Who Feels the Pain
Breast radiologists holding incomplete reads; clerks chasing other facilities; patients recalled for comparisons that already existed.

## Impact If Fixed
Getting priors in time avoids some recalls altogether. If comparison avoids even one recall in ten pending-priors cases (estimate), a centre reading 20,000 screens a year saves dozens of diagnostic visits, and every incomplete read closes inside the 30-day rule.
