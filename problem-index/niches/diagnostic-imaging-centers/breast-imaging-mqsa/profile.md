# Breast Imaging Centers under MQSA

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/diagnostic-imaging-centers|Diagnostic Imaging Centers]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get every woman recalled from a screening mammogram through her diagnostic workup, and whoever gets the highest share of recalled patients to a final assessment within 30 days takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$0.5B a year of breast imaging tracking and reporting software, lay-letter production, patient navigators and MQSA compliance work at freestanding breast centres (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% of centre revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — images and reports are digital, but recall tracking, letters, outside-film requests and the annual outcomes audit run on worklists, mail and spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** Breast centre director or lead interpreting physician; operator's director of women's imaging
**Automation Potential:** High — the screening-to-diagnosis pathway is a defined sequence with fixed BI-RADS states and federal deadlines

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Mammography is the only imaging modality with its own federal statute. MQSA certifies 9,137 facilities and 27,865 units, which reported 45.1M procedures (FDA, 1 October 2026). Since 10 September 2024 the amended rule requires every lay summary to state whether breast tissue is dense. Suspicious or highly suggestive results must reach the patient and her physician within seven calendar days, and an "incomplete, need priors" exam needs a final assessment within 30 days. Facilities must also track positive results through to biopsy outcome for the annual medical outcomes audit (recalled). The commercial contest is loop closure: a screening programme is judged, by referrers and by health plans' quality measures, on whether recalled women actually complete diagnostic imaging and biopsy. A recall that leaks to another centre or is never completed is both a patient-safety failure and lost diagnostic revenue.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Breast-specific reporting and tracking systems (MagView, PenRad and RIS modules, recalled), FDA-cleared detection and density AI (Hologic, Lunit with Volpara, and DeepHealth, which bought iCAD in 2025; recalled and search summary), letter vendors and patient navigators. Gaps: recalls tracked on worklists that do not follow the patient when she schedules elsewhere; outside priors requested by fax with no tracking; the outcomes audit assembled by hand once a year.
