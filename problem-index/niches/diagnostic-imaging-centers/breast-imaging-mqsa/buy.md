# Cancer-Screening Navigation Software Adapted to MQSA Deadlines

**Niche:** [[niches/diagnostic-imaging-centers/breast-imaging-mqsa/profile|Breast Imaging Centers under MQSA]]
**Industry:** [[industries/diagnostic-imaging-centers|Diagnostic Imaging Centers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Health systems already buy navigation software for lung and colorectal screening programmes; breast centres need it bound to MQSA's letter and audit rules.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #compliance #automation #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get every woman recalled from a screening mammogram through her diagnostic workup, and whoever gets the highest share of recalled patients to a final assessment within 30 days takes the account.

## The Problem
Breast centres track recalls in worklists while navigators work from spreadsheets.

## What Already Exists
Screening-programme and navigation software used by health systems for lung cancer screening and incidental findings tracks patients through multi-step pathways with outreach and dashboards.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs BI-RADS states and assessment categories as the pathway, and MQSA's seven-day lay-summary rule for suspicious findings and 30-day final-assessment rule as hard deadlines. Density-notification wording must be checked in every letter. It needs self-referred patient handling (MQSA requires referral to a provider for some results), outcome capture for the annual audit, and integration with breast-specific reporting systems.

## Target Customer
Breast centres inside larger operators that already license navigation tools for other screening lines.

## Impact If Solved
One navigation platform across screening lines saves a breast centre the cost of a separate tracking build and brings MQSA deadline compliance into one auditable view before the next inspection. Replacing spreadsheet tracking could free half a navigator FTE per 20,000 screens a year (estimate).
