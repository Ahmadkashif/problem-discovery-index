# Radiology Health Policy Research Institutes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/diagnostic-imaging-centers|Diagnostic Imaging Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Harvey L. Neiman Health Policy Institute, affiliated with the American College of Radiology, and the economics staff of radiology societies study imaging use, payment and workforce. Their 2025 JACR projections of radiologist supply and imaging demand to 2055 are a recent example (search summary). The work feeds the societies' comments on Medicare fee-schedule and OPPS rulemaking and is published openly.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-25 researchers and analysts (estimate)
**Output:** Peer-reviewed studies, policy briefs, data for rulemaking comments
**Proprietary data:** Medicare claims and other datasets licensed under federal data-use agreements, plus society surveys
**Clock:** CMS comment deadlines on annual payment rules shape the advocacy calendar, but the research runs on its own schedule
**Buyer:** The sponsoring society's leadership
