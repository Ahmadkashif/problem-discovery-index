# Teleradiology Groups' Quality & Operations Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/diagnostic-imaging-centers|Diagnostic Imaging Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The national reading groups and teleradiology firms that read for centres and hospitals run central analytics and AI teams on their read volume: routing, turnaround, peer-review discrepancy rates, and increasingly their own models. Radiology Partners is the largest. Its affiliated practices serve 3,400+ facilities, vRad reads close to nine million studies a year, and its Mosaic Clinical Technologies division launched Mosaic Reporting on in-house foundation models in June 2026 (search summary). USARAD (Nanox), StatRad and Teleradiology Solutions are smaller. In India, 5C Network reads for 2,000+ hospitals and centres. Their revenue is reads, and the analytics serve throughput and quality.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~50-200 data, AI and quality staff at Radiology Partners and Mosaic (estimate); under 10 at most other firms; reading radiologists are production
**Output:** Internal routing, turnaround and quality analytics; AI products (Mosaic Reporting, the vRad platform) now sold externally as software
**Proprietary data:** The largest corpus of read studies, reports, addenda and discrepancy reviews in the country
**Clock:** Client contracts' turnaround terms and the firm's own product calendar
**Buyer:** Chief technology or chief medical officer of the reading firm
