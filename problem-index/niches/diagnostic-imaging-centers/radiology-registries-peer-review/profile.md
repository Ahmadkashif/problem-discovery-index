# Radiology Registries & Peer Review (ACR NRDR)

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/diagnostic-imaging-centers|Diagnostic Imaging Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The American College of Radiology's National Radiology Data Registry runs the Dose Index Registry (CT, fluoroscopy and digital radiography dose benchmarks), the National Mammography Database (recall, positive predictive value and cancer detection benchmarks) and related registries. It is a CMS-approved QCDR supporting twelve QCDR measures and the new diagnostic and interventional radiology MIPS Value Pathways in 2026. Peer-review programmes such as RADPEER sit alongside (recalled). Imaging centres and groups pay participation fees and submit data to get benchmark reports and MIPS reporting.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-60 registry analysts, statisticians and data managers (estimate); facility staff who submit data are production and sit at the centres
**Output:** Facility and radiologist benchmark reports, dose and mammography performance comparisons, MIPS submissions
**Proprietary data:** The national pool of facility-submitted dose indices, mammography outcomes and quality measures, with history no commercial vendor holds
**Clock:** MIPS data submission closes on 31 March after the performance year, a hard date on the registry's submission work; benchmark releases run on the ACR's own calendar
**Buyer:** ACR's registry and quality leadership
