# One Reading Surface That Measures Its Own Effect on Addenda

**Niche:** [[niches/diagnostic-imaging-centers/reading-workflow-report-drafting/profile|Reading Workflow & Report Drafting Software]]
**Industry:** [[industries/diagnostic-imaging-centers|Diagnostic Imaging Centers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Every reading tool claims minutes saved; none reports whether the drafted reports needed more corrections, which is the condition groups need before they trust drafting at volume.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #causal-inference #hypothesis-testing #evaluation-metrics #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to cut the radiologist's time from opening a study to signing the report, and whoever raises studies finalised per radiologist hour with no rise in addenda takes the account.

## The Problem
A group pilots a drafting tool and sees reads get faster. It cannot tell whether addenda, peer-review discrepancies or referrer callbacks rose, because those live in other systems and nobody joins them to which reports were drafted.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Vendors are rewarded for speed claims. Quality outcomes take months to accrue and require the group's peer-review and addenda data, which vendors rarely see.

## What to Build
A reading surface that joins worklist, priors and drafting. Every report records which sentences were drafted, edited or written fresh. A quality ledger links each report to its addenda, peer-review results and follow-up outcomes, so speed and quality are measured together by radiologist, study type and tool version, with staggered rollouts serving as natural experiments.

## Target Customer
Large groups and teleradiology firms deciding which drafting tools to standardise on.

## Impact If Built
A group that can show drafting raised reads per hour by 10%–15% with addendum rates unchanged (estimate) can roll it out across hundreds of radiologists. That is the decision this sub-niche turns on.
