# Reading Workflow & Report Drafting Software

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/diagnostic-imaging-centers|Diagnostic Imaging Centers]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to cut the radiologist's time from opening a study to signing the report, and whoever raises studies finalised per radiologist hour with no rise in addenda takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$0.6B a year of worklist, viewer, dictation, report-drafting and detection-AI licences used on outpatient centre studies (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of centre-related spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High — every read is on screen; the friction is in the steps between opening the study and signing it
**Target Buyer:** Radiology group president, CIO or chief medical informatics officer
**Automation Potential:** High for drafting, prior retrieval and measurement; low for the impression and signature

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Here the buyer is the reading radiologist's group, and the measure is studies finalised per radiologist hour at unchanged quality. The field is crowded and moving: Microsoft PowerScribe for dictation; Rad AI for drafted impressions and follow-up tracking; Mosaic Reporting, launched commercially by Radiology Partners' Mosaic Clinical Technologies in June 2026 on its own foundation models (search summary); DeepHealth's Diagnostic Suite with Reporting Pro; and cloud reading platforms from Sirona Medical and New Lantern. About three in four of the FDA's AI-enabled devices are radiology tools (search summary), mostly detection and triage. Radiology dictation and clinical speech vendors' own data science is analysed under [[industries/document-preparation-services|Document Preparation Services]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
PACS viewers, PowerScribe, drafting tools, detection AI marketplaces. Gaps: each tool saves seconds in its own window while the radiologist still switches between five; priors not summarised; quality (addenda, discrepancies) not measured alongside speed, so productivity claims are unverified.
