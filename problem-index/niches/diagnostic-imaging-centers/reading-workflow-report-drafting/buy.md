# Ambient Clinical Documentation Rebuilt for Structured Radiology Reports

**Niche:** [[niches/diagnostic-imaging-centers/reading-workflow-report-drafting/profile|Reading Workflow & Report Drafting Software]]
**Industry:** [[industries/diagnostic-imaging-centers|Diagnostic Imaging Centers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Ambient scribes turn a clinic conversation into a note; radiology needs the same speech-and-language stack turned toward structured templates, measurements and standard lexicons.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #seq2seq #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to cut the radiologist's time from opening a study to signing the report, and whoever raises studies finalised per radiologist hour with no rise in addenda takes the account.

## The Problem
Radiologists dictate long reports into speech recognition and fix errors by hand. Structured templates exist but are slow to fill.

## What Already Exists
Ambient documentation vendors for clinic visits (Abridge, Ambience, Microsoft Dragon Copilot) and radiology dictation (PowerScribe) already transcribe speech and generate text with large language models.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs template-aware generation (findings by organ system, laterality, measurements carried into the impression). It needs standard lexicons and scoring systems (BI-RADS, LI-RADS, PI-RADS, Lung-RADS), comparison language tied to the prior report, and automatic extraction of follow-up recommendations. It has to work on dictation fragments and voice commands rather than conversation.

## Target Customer
Groups already standardised on a dictation platform that want drafting without changing the radiologist's habits.

## Impact If Solved
Reusing a production speech-and-language stack can cut report editing time by a minute or more per study (estimate), about two hours per 120-study shift.
