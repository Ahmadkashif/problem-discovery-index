# Medical Record Retrieval & Chronology Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/personal-injury-law|Personal Injury Law Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Retrieve medical records from every provider a plaintiff saw, then read the resulting stack — Pass 1 puts a typical case at 200 to 2,000 pages — and produce a chronology that establishes the injury timeline, links injuries to the accident, and flags the pre-existing conditions the defence will exploit. Pass 1 identifies this as the single highest labour cost in a personal injury practice and entirely manual.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-5,000 record retrieval staff, nurse reviewers, and chronologists across the domestic and offshore providers
**Output:** Medical chronologies, records summaries, deposition and demand support, missing-records analysis
**Proprietary data:** Very large volumes of reviewed records and the summarisation judgments over them, held under authorisation for a specific matter
**Clock:** Statute of limitations, discovery deadlines, and demand deadlines
**Buyer:** VP of Operations / Chief Clinical Officer

**Kill switches:** **HIPAA** — the entire corpus is protected health information held as a business associate. **Client data ownership** — the records and the work product belong to the law firm and its client, and are used for one matter only. **Attorney work product** — the chronology is prepared in anticipation of litigation.
**Verdict:** Logged — scores above threshold but carries three live kill switches. The largest labour mass in this industry's insight layer sits behind a wall that prevents any of it accumulating into an asset the provider owns.
