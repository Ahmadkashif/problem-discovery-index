# Independent Medical Examination & Peer Review Organizations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/personal-injury-law|Personal Injury Law Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Supply the defence-side medical opinion: examining physicians who assess whether the claimed injury is consistent with the accident, and peer reviewers who read the treatment record and opine on medical necessity. The report is the exhibit that opposing counsel builds a defence on.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 physician panels, nurse reviewers, and quality staff at the larger organisations
**Output:** Examination reports, peer review opinions, causation and medical necessity determinations, impairment ratings
**Proprietary data:** Large volumes of examinations and reviews joined to the claim outcomes that followed
**Clock:** Litigation and claim adjudication deadlines
**Buyer:** Chief Medical Officer / VP of Clinical Operations
