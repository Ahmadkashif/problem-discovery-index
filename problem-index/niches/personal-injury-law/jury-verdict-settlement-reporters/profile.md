# Jury Verdict & Settlement Reporters

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/personal-injury-law|Personal Injury Law Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Collect verdicts and reported settlements case by case — injury, venue, liability facts, counsel, award — and publish them as the comparables attorneys and adjusters value cases against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 reporters, editors, and researchers across the regional publications
**Output:** Verdict and settlement reports, comparables searches, regional award trend summaries
**Proprietary data:** Hand-collected case reports going back decades, including settlements that appear in no public record
**Clock:** Trial calendars and continuous reporting
**Buyer:** Editorial Director / VP of Content
