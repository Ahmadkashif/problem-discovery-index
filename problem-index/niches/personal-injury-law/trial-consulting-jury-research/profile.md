# Trial Consulting & Jury Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/personal-injury-law|Personal Injury Law Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Run mock trials and focus groups to test case themes before trial, advise on jury selection, and predict how a specific venue's jury pool will receive a specific set of facts.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-200 trial consultants, social scientists, and research staff at the larger firms
**Output:** Mock trial and focus group findings, theme testing, juror profiling, venue assessments, damages range estimates
**Proprietary data:** Mock juror deliberations and damages awards accumulated over hundreds of exercises, occasionally joined to the real verdict that followed
**Clock:** Trial dates
**Buyer:** Managing Director
