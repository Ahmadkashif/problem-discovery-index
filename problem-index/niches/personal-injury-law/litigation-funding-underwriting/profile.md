# Litigation Funding Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/personal-injury-law|Personal Injury Law Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Advance capital against future settlements — to plaintiffs waiting on a case, and to the firms carrying case costs — and price it on a judgment about whether the case will win and what it will be worth.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 underwriters, case analysts, and portfolio managers
**Output:** Funding decisions and advance pricing, case merit and value assessments, portfolio performance analysis
**Proprietary data:** Thousands of funded cases with their eventual resolutions — a rare record of predicted case value against realised outcome
**Clock:** Case milestones and settlement events
**Buyer:** Chief Investment Officer / Head of Underwriting
