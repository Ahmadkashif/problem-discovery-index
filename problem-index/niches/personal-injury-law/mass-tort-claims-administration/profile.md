# Mass Tort & Class Action Claims Administration

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/personal-injury-law|Personal Injury Law Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Run the settlement machinery for mass torts and class actions — notice programmes, claim intake, eligibility review, allocation under a court-approved matrix, and distribution — under court supervision and on court deadlines.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-2,000 claims analysts, notice specialists, data staff, and programme managers
**Output:** Notice plan design and reach analysis, claim eligibility determinations, allocation calculations, distribution reporting, fraud detection
**Proprietary data:** Claim populations and adjudication outcomes across hundreds of settlements
**Clock:** Court-ordered claim deadlines and distribution dates
**Buyer:** Chief Operating Officer / VP of Claims Administration
