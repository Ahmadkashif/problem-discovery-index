# Legal Lead Generation & Mass Tort Marketing Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/personal-injury-law|Personal Injury Law Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator/rollup

## What They Do
Buy media at scale for personal injury and mass tort campaigns, qualify the responses, and sell or place the resulting cases with firms — running the attribution, qualification and pricing analytics over one of the most expensive advertising markets in the United States.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 media analysts, data scientists, qualification staff, and campaign managers
**Output:** Campaign performance and attribution models, case qualification scoring, lead pricing, retention forecasting
**Proprietary data:** Media spend joined to enquiry, qualification, retention and eventual case outcome across many campaigns
**Clock:** Continuous media buying against filing deadlines in active torts
**Buyer:** Chief Revenue Officer / VP of Analytics
