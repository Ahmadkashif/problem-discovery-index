# Court Data & Litigation Analytics Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/personal-injury-law|Personal Injury Law Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Collect dockets, filings, motions and dispositions out of thousands of separate federal, state and county court systems, normalise them into a single structure, and sell the analytics built on top — how a specific judge rules on a specific motion type, how long a case takes in a given venue, what opposing counsel has done before, what similar cases resolved at. Pass 1 names case valuation as the second broken pillar of personal injury practice, built on attorney intuition about jurisdiction, judge, opposing counsel and insurer that is not systematised and does not transfer between attorneys. This is the layer selling exactly that information.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 data acquisition staff, court researchers, taxonomists, data scientists, and legal analysts
**Output:** Judge and venue analytics, motion outcome rates, timing-to-disposition models, party and counsel litigation histories, case comparables
**Proprietary data:** Normalised state trial court records assembled from thousands of county systems that publish inconsistently, in incompatible formats, and sometimes on paper — plus decades of entity resolution over parties, firms and judges
**Clock:** Daily docket updates against live litigation deadlines
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Analytics
