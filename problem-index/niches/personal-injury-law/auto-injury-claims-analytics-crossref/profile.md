# Bodily Injury Claims Evaluation & Medical Review Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/personal-injury-law|Personal Injury Law Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The insurer-side layer that values bodily injury claims and reprices the medical bills inside them — the system on the other side of the table when a personal injury attorney makes a demand. It reads the treatment record, assigns severity, benchmarks the charges against provider norms, and produces the evaluation range the adjuster negotiates within.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-800 data scientists, clinical reviewers, actuaries, and content analysts
**Output:** Injury severity and general damages evaluations, medical bill repricing, provider treatment pattern analytics, fraud and build-up scoring
**Proprietary data:** Bodily injury claim files at national scale joined to treatment records, billed charges, and settlement outcomes
**Clock:** Continuous claim flow with settlement negotiation windows
**Buyer:** Chief Analytics Officer / VP of Claims Solutions
