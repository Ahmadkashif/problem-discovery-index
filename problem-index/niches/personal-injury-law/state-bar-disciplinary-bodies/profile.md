# State Bar Disciplinary & Regulatory Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/personal-injury-law|Personal Injury Law Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Investigate complaints against attorneys, enforce advertising and solicitation rules that bear heavily on personal injury practice, and administer trust account compliance.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-40 investigators and counsel per state, mostly under 10 in the analytical function
**Output:** Disciplinary determinations, advertising rule opinions, trust account audits
**Proprietary data:** Complaint and discipline histories held by state
**Clock:** Complaint intake and statutory investigation timelines
**Buyer:** Chief Disciplinary Counsel
