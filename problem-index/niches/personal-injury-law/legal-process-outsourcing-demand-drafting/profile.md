# Legal Process Outsourcing & Demand Package Drafting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/personal-injury-law|Personal Injury Law Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Offshore and domestic teams that draft demand packages, summarise depositions, prepare discovery responses, and run case intake for personal injury firms working on contingency economics that will not support the same headcount in-house.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-5,000 paralegals, drafters, and case analysts
**Output:** Demand letters and packages, deposition summaries, discovery drafts, intake screening
**Proprietary data:** Very large volumes of drafted demands, none of it retained across clients
**Clock:** Statute of limitations and discovery deadlines
**Buyer:** VP of Legal Operations
