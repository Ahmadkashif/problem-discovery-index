# Medicare Set-Aside & Secondary Payer Compliance

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/personal-injury-law|Personal Injury Law Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Calculate how much of a personal injury or workers' compensation settlement must be set aside to cover future injury-related medical care that Medicare would otherwise pay, and defend that allocation through CMS review. The number goes into the settlement, so it has to be right in both directions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 allocation nurses, physicians, pharmacists, and compliance analysts
**Output:** Set-aside allocation reports, conditional payment resolution, future medical cost projections, CMS submission and appeal
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated allocation submissions joined to CMS determinations — which projections were accepted and which were countered higher, an outcome record CMS does not publish
**Clock:** Settlement timing and CMS review windows
**Buyer:** Chief Compliance Officer / VP of Settlement Services
