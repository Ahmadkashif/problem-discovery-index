# Life Care Planning & Economic Damages Experts

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/personal-injury-law|Personal Injury Law Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Produce the two expert reports that quantify a catastrophic injury claim: the life care plan projecting a lifetime of medical and attendant care needs and costs, and the economic report projecting lost earnings and household services, discounted to present value.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 certified life care planners, nurses, and forensic economists at the larger practices
**Output:** Life care plans, economic loss reports, present value calculations, expert testimony
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated cost research by region and service type, plus the record of which projections survived cross-examination
**Clock:** Expert disclosure deadlines and trial dates
**Buyer:** Managing Partner
