# Funeral Home Valuation & Benchmarking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/funeral-homes|Funeral Homes]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Johnson Consulting Group and the specialist advisors value funeral homes and cemeteries, broker sales in a consolidating market, and publish the performance benchmarks — call volume, average revenue per call, cremation rate — that owners run their businesses against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-60 analysts, valuation specialists, and brokers
**Output:** Valuations, transaction advisory, performance benchmark reports
**Proprietary data:** Completed transaction terms and operating benchmarks across a market where nothing is publicly reported
**Clock:** Transaction timelines and annual benchmark publication
**Buyer:** President / Director of Valuation
