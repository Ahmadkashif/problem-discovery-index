# Funeral Association Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/funeral-homes|Funeral Homes]]
**Category:** Insight Layer

## What They Do
The National Funeral Directors Association produces the industry's cost and consumer preference statistics, publishes the Funeral Rule compliance guidance the profession relies on, and runs the continuing education licensure requires.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-100 research, compliance, and education staff
**Output:** Industry statistics, consumer research, compliance guidance, continuing education
**Proprietary data:** Long-running member cost and consumer preference survey series
**Clock:** Annual publication and licensure renewal cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Research / Chief Compliance Counsel
