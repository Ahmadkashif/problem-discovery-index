# Death Verification Data Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/funeral-homes|Funeral Homes]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
LexisNexis, Evadata, and the specialist death data compilers assemble verified death records from funeral homes, obituaries, state vital records, and other sources, and license them to insurers, pension administrators, banks, and government programmes who must stop payments, trigger benefits, and detect fraud. Since federal restrictions narrowed public access to the national death index, private compilation became the operative source.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 data acquisition staff, record analysts, and data scientists
**Output:** Verified death records, match and confirmation services, mortality analytics — the licensed product
**Proprietary data:** Death records assembled from thousands of independent sources under supply relationships built over years, with matching and confirmation logic no competitor can reconstruct
**Clock:** Every day an unreported death goes undetected is an improper payment or an unpaid benefit
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Data Products
