# Preneed Insurance Actuarial Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/funeral-homes|Funeral Homes]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Homesteaders Life, the preneed carriers, and the trust administrators fund prearranged funerals decades before they are performed, which requires modelling mortality, funeral cost inflation, and contract persistency over horizons longer than almost any other consumer product.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 actuaries, analysts, and trust administrators
**Output:** Product pricing, reserving analyses, growth and inflation modelling, state filings
**Proprietary data:** Preneed contract performance across decades — mortality, at-need conversion, and realized funeral cost inflation — an actuarial series no other party holds
**Clock:** Statutory reserving, rate filings, and state trust reporting
**Buyer:** Chief Actuary / VP Product
