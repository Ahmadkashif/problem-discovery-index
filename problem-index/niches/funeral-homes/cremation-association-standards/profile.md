# Cremation Association Standards & Statistics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/funeral-homes|Funeral Homes]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The cremation association tracks and forecasts cremation rates by state, sets operator certification and identification standards, and publishes the projections that determine how funeral homes invest in facilities.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 research and certification staff
**Output:** Cremation rate statistics and forecasts, operator certification, identification standards
**Proprietary data:** The longest-running cremation rate series, by state, with forecasts the industry plans capital against
**Clock:** Annual statistics release and certification cycles
**Buyer:** Executive Director / Director of Research
