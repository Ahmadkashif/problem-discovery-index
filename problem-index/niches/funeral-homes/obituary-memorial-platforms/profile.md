# Obituary & Memorial Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/funeral-homes|Funeral Homes]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Legacy.com and the memorial platforms aggregate obituaries from funeral homes and newspapers, host memorial pages, and monetize through funeral home subscriptions, advertising, and floral commerce. They are also, incidentally, one of the earliest and broadest signals that a death has occurred.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 content operations, data, and product staff
**Output:** Obituary distribution and hosting, memorial engagement, audience products
**Proprietary data:** Obituary records at national scale with earlier arrival than official registration in many cases
**Clock:** Obituaries publish within days of death; service dates drive urgency
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Chief Product Officer
