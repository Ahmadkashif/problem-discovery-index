# Medical Examiner & Coroner Offices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/funeral-homes|Funeral Homes]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Medical examiner and coroner offices investigate and certify deaths that are sudden, violent, or unexplained, producing the determinations that drive death certificates, criminal proceedings, and public health surveillance.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-200 pathologists, investigators, and technicians per large jurisdiction
**Output:** Cause and manner determinations, autopsy reports, investigative findings
**Proprietary data:** Detailed investigative and toxicological records — the primary surveillance signal for overdose and injury mortality
**Clock:** Statutory certification timelines and criminal proceedings
**Buyer:** Chief Medical Examiner — public procurement
