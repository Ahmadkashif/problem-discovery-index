# Media Asset Management Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/video-production-smb|SMB Video Production]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Supply the systems that index, tag, search and distribute video libraries, and the automated metadata generation — speech, object, face and scene recognition — that makes an archive findable.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 machine learning engineers, metadata specialists, and product staff
**Output:** Automated transcription and tagging, visual and semantic search, rights and version tracking, distribution workflow
**Proprietary data:** Customer archives held per tenant, plus taxonomies built for the domain
**Clock:** Delivery deadlines
**Buyer:** VP of Product
