# Music & Media Rights Licensing Administration

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/video-production-smb|SMB Video Production]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Clear and administer the rights a production needs — music synchronisation, archival footage, talent and location releases — and resolve who owns what across catalogues whose chains of title exist in no authoritative registry.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 150-800 rights researchers, licensing administrators, and royalty analysts
**Output:** Clearances and licences, ownership determinations, royalty calculation and distribution, catalogue research, infringement resolution
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated ownership and clearance determinations across fragmented catalogues
**Clock:** Production and delivery schedules, plus royalty distribution periods
**Buyer:** VP of Rights & Licensing
