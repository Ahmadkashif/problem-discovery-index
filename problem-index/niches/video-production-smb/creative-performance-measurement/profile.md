# Creative Performance Measurement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/video-production-smb|SMB Video Production]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Test and score video creative — attention, recall, branding, emotional response — before and after it runs, and tell advertisers which executions work and why.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 research scientists, behavioural analysts, and data scientists
**Output:** Creative pre-testing results, attention and recall scoring, normative benchmarks, in-flight performance diagnostics, creative best practice research
**Proprietary data:** Normative databases of tested creative accumulated across categories and years, with response data attached
**Clock:** Production and campaign flight schedules
**Buyer:** Chief Research Officer / VP of Analytics
