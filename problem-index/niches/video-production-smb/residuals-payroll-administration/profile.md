# Production Payroll & Residuals Administration

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/video-production-smb|SMB Video Production]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Pay crew and talent under union agreements, and compute the residual and reuse payments owed when material runs again in a different medium, market or territory — a calculation defined by hundreds of pages of collective bargaining agreements that change every negotiation cycle.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 payroll specialists, residuals analysts, union agreement experts, and compliance staff
**Output:** Union-compliant payroll, fringe and pension contribution administration, residual and reuse calculations, rate and agreement interpretation, audit response
**Proprietary data:** Decades of encoded agreement logic and the accumulated record of how ambiguous reuse cases were resolved with the guilds — none of which exists in the agreements themselves
**Clock:** Weekly payroll, statutory wage timing, and contractual residual payment windows
**Buyer:** VP of Residuals / Chief Compliance Officer
