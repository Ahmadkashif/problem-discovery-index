# Stock Media Licensing Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/video-production-smb|SMB Video Production]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator/rollup

## What They Do
Licence footage, images, music and motion graphics to producers, and run the search, tagging and pricing analytics over archives of hundreds of millions of assets.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 metadata specialists, machine learning engineers, and marketplace analysts
**Output:** Content tagging and search, visual similarity retrieval, pricing and demand analytics, trend reporting, contributor guidance
**Proprietary data:** Enormous licensed archives with human and machine metadata, joined to search, download and licensing behaviour across many years
**Clock:** Continuous, with campaign and seasonal demand cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Data Science / Chief Content Officer
