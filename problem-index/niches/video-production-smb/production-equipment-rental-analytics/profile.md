# Production Equipment Rental Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/video-production-smb|SMB Video Production]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator/rollup

## What They Do
The utilisation, pricing and fleet planning analytics inside the camera, lighting and grip rental houses that most productions hire from rather than own.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 analysts and fleet planners
**Output:** Utilisation and yield analytics, rental pricing, fleet purchase and retirement planning, package configuration
**Proprietary data:** Rental histories showing which equipment configurations are actually used together and how demand shifts with format changes
**Clock:** Booking cycles and production seasons
**Buyer:** VP of Operations
