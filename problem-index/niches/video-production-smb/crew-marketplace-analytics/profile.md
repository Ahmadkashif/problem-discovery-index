# Freelance Crew Marketplace Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/video-production-smb|SMB Video Production]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator/rollup

## What They Do
Match producers to freelance crew and run the rate guidance, availability and quality analytics over bookings in a workforce that is almost entirely project-based.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-200 data and marketplace staff
**Output:** Crew matching and search, day rate benchmarks, availability forecasting, reliability and quality signals
**Proprietary data:** Bookings, rates and repeat-hire behaviour across a freelance workforce with no other pricing reference
**Clock:** Production schedules
**Buyer:** Head of Data
