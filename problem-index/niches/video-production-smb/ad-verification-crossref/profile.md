# Ad Verification & Measurement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/video-production-smb|SMB Video Production]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Verify that video advertising was actually delivered, viewable, to a human, in a brand-safe context, and measure what it did — the layer that determines whether the work a production company made is judged to have performed.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 measurement scientists, data engineers, and analysts
**Output:** Viewability and invalid traffic measurement, brand safety and suitability classification, attention metrics, brand lift and outcome studies
**Proprietary data:** Impression-level measurement across a very large share of digital video delivery, with accreditation and methodology built over years
**Clock:** Campaign flights and monthly billing reconciliation
**Buyer:** Chief Measurement Officer / VP of Data Science
