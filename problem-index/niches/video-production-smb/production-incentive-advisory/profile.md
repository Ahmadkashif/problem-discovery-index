# Production Incentive & Tax Credit Advisory

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/video-production-smb|SMB Video Production]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Determine which state and national production incentives a project qualifies for, structure the production to maximise them, prepare the applications and audits, and monetise the resulting credits — often the difference between a project being viable and not.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 incentive specialists, accountants, and auditors
**Output:** Jurisdiction comparison and qualification analysis, application preparation, qualified expenditure tracking and audit, credit certification and monetisation
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated determinations of what each jurisdiction's administrators actually allow, which differs materially from the published statutes
**Clock:** Application windows, allocation caps that exhaust, and audit deadlines
**Buyer:** Practice Leader / Director of Incentives
