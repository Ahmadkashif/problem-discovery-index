# Production Insurance & Completion Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/video-production-smb|SMB Video Production]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Underwrite production packages — equipment, liability, cast, errors and omissions — and, at the larger end, guarantee that a production will actually be delivered.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 underwriters, risk analysts, and claims staff
**Output:** Package pricing and eligibility, schedule and budget risk assessment, claims handling, completion guarantee analysis
**Proprietary data:** Production claims joined to shoot type, location, schedule compression and crew composition
**Clock:** Production start dates
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
