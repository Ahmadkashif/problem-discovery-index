# Video Platform Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/video-production-smb|SMB Video Production]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator/rollup

## What They Do
The data organisations inside the video hosting and distribution platforms, running recommendation, retention analytics and creator reporting over essentially all published video.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1,000-5,000 data scientists, machine learning engineers, and content analysts
**Output:** Recommendation and ranking, second-by-second retention analytics, audience and reach reporting, monetisation and pricing, content classification
**Proprietary data:** Complete watch behaviour at second resolution across billions of videos and viewers
**Clock:** Continuous
**Buyer:** VP of Data Science
