# Jewellers Block & Pawnbroker Insurance Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pawnshops|Pawnshops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Specialist insurers and brokers underwrite jewellers block and related cover for jewellery retailers and pawnshops: stock and customers' property in the insured's care against theft, robbery, transit and fire. Jewelers Mutual is the largest US specialist (estimate); its acquisition of CJB Insurance Services added a Canadian brokerage that places cover for jewellers and niche businesses including pawnshops. Pawnbrokers pay premiums; underwriters price safes, alarms, stock levels and loss history.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 underwriters, loss-control and claims analysts on the jewellery line per carrier (estimate)
**Output:** Rating and eligibility decisions, security requirements, loss-control inspections
**Proprietary data:** Loss experience across jewellery and pawn risks by security level, stock value and location
**Clock:** Annual renewal
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
