# Pawn & Secondhand Transaction Reporting Databases

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pawnshops|Pawnshops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Run the systems pawnbrokers and secondhand dealers must report every transaction to under local ordinance, and sell search and investigative analytics to police. LeadsOnline lets agencies search 1.2+ billion transactions from pawnshops, secondhand dealers, scrap yards and kiosks in all 50 states, with nightly uploads; BWI's RAPID serves other jurisdictions. Law-enforcement agencies pay; reporting businesses are required to submit.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–80 investigative-support, analytics and training staff (estimate)
**Output:** Searchable transaction database, item and suspect matching, investigative analytics and case support for agencies
**Proprietary data:** The largest cross-dealer record of secondhand transactions in the country
**Clock:** Investigation and prosecution timelines set by the agencies and courts, not the vendor
**Buyer:** CEO / Head of Law Enforcement Solutions; agency buyers are police chiefs and sheriffs
