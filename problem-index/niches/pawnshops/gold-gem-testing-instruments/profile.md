# Gold & Gem Testing Instrument Makers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pawnshops|Pawnshops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Make the XRF analysers, electronic gold testers and diamond testers that chains and some independents use at the jewellery counter — Thermo Fisher's Niton, Bruker, SciAps and Evident among XRF makers. Pawnshops buy instruments and service contracts.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 application scientists focused on jewellery and pawn per maker (estimate), within much larger instrument businesses
**Output:** Instruments, calibration libraries, application notes
**Proprietary data:** Calibration spectra and field-performance data
**Clock:** Product cycles
**Buyer:** Product line manager
