# Precious Metals Supply & Recycling Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pawnshops|Pawnshops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Independent precious-metals consultancies — Metals Focus, LSEG's GFMS team and the research desks of metals groups such as Heraeus — survey mines, refiners, fabricators and scrap collectors to publish gold and silver supply-demand balances. Recycled gold is one of their estimates: 1,404 tonnes in 2025, a 13-year high, part of it old jewellery bought over pawn and jeweller counters. Miners, refiners, banks, investors and industry bodies pay through annual surveys, subscriptions, commissioned studies and consulting.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–60 analysts per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Annual and quarterly supply-demand surveys, scrap and recycling estimates, price forecasts, commissioned research
**Proprietary data:** Years of field-survey relationships with refiners, fabricators and scrap collectors, and the recycling series built from them, which no public source produces
**Clock:** Survey launch dates and quarterly updates the firms set themselves; client commissions with launch dates
**Buyer:** Managing Director / Head of Research
