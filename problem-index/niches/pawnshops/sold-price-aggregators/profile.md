# Secondhand Sold-Price Aggregators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pawnshops|Pawnshops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Collect realised prices for used and collectible goods and sell lookup access: WorthPoint aggregates auction and marketplace results for antiques and collectibles, PriceCharting tracks games and cards, and eBay's Terapeak exposes eBay sold data to sellers. Pawnbrokers, dealers and collectors use them to value items at the counter.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 data, catalogue and editorial staff per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Searchable sold-price databases and price guides
**Proprietary data:** Mostly licensed or scraped marketplace and auction results, with some own catalogue work
**Clock:** None set by a third party
**Buyer:** Founder / Head of Data
