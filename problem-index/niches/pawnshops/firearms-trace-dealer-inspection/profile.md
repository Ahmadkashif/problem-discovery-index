# Firearms Tracing & Dealer Inspection

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pawnshops|Pawnshops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The federal firearms regulator inspects licensed dealers, pawnbrokers among them, for recordkeeping compliance and traces crime guns through dealers' acquisition-and-disposition records, on which pawn redemptions and sales appear. Inspections can lead to warnings or licence revocation; traces support police investigations. Funded by appropriation.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Hundreds of inspectors and tracing staff nationally (estimate), doing production inspection and tracing work; no dealer-focused analytics unit of 10+ was found
**Output:** Inspection findings, licence actions, trace results, crime-gun intelligence
**Proprietary data:** Trace records and out-of-business dealer records accumulated over decades
**Clock:** Inspection cycles and investigation timelines
**Buyer:** Assistant Director, Field Operations
