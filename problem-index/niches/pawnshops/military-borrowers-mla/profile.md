# Military Service-Member Borrowers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pawnshops|Pawnshops]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to lend to a covered service member inside the Military Lending Act's 36% MAPR cap and still make a small ticket pay, and whoever verifies covered status and prices the ticket inside the cap without losing money takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$50–150M a year in potential pawn lending to covered borrowers near military installations (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1–2% of revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — covered-status checks are online; product design and counter handling are manual
**Target Buyer:** Chief compliance officer at a chain; owners of stores near bases
**Automation Potential:** High for status checks and pricing, Low for collateral judgement

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The Military Lending Act caps credit to active-duty service members and their dependants at a 36% military annual percentage rate, fees included, and it covers pawn loans. A standard ticket priced at a state maximum such as Florida's 25% of the amount financed per 30 days is far above that, so the usual product cannot be offered. FirstCash disclosed that it began offering an MLA-compliant pawn product only in 2025. Pawnshops cluster near installations, and service members who need small cash fast are among the customers the standard ticket excludes.

## Current Tools & Gaps
The Department of Defense covered-borrower check (recalled), POS flags, and compliance policies that often amount to declining the customer. The gaps: no product engineered to pay at 36% on a few hundred dollars; covered status not re-checked consistently at renewal; and clerks who refuse rather than risk an error.
