# Trade-In Grading Adapted to the Pawn Counter

**Niche:** [[niches/pawnshops/general-merchandise-lending/profile|General Merchandise Lending]]
**Industry:** [[industries/pawnshops|Pawnshops]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Device trade-in programmes already grade and price used electronics in seconds; a pawn counter needs that across tools, instruments and gaming, for a loan rather than a purchase.
**Tags:** #cnns #transfer-learning #gradient-boosting #evaluation-metrics #automation #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to price a used branded item — a drill kit, a games console, a guitar, a laptop — from its exact model and condition against what that model actually resells for locally, in seconds at the counter, and whoever prices it closest to its realised resale takes the account.

## The Problem
Condition drives value, but pawn clerks record it inconsistently or not at all, so two identical models lent on by two clerks carry different loans for no recorded reason.

## What Already Exists
Phone trade-in and kiosk programmes (ecoATM kiosks, carrier and retailer trade-in diagnostics) run automated device checks — lock status, screen and battery health — and grade-based pricing. Recommerce platforms run graded intake at warehouse scale; see [[niches/recommerce-platforms/condition-grading/profile|Condition Grading]] under Recommerce Platforms.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: categories with no diagnostic port (power tools, instruments, lawn equipment) graded from photographs and a short checklist; a loan amount, which must allow for the chance the item is redeemed and never resold, rather than a buy price; condition captured in the fields the police form and POS already hold; grades calibrated to the operator's own realised prices rather than a national grading scale; and a flow that takes seconds at a counter, not minutes in a warehouse.

## Target Customer
Lending operations leaders at chains; POS vendors adding grading to the ticket.

## Impact If Solved
A consistent condition grade on every ticket makes general-merchandise lending explainable and gives comparables and valuation models the input they currently lack.
