# Pawn Business Brokers & Lenders to Pawnshops

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pawnshops|Pawnshops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Brokers value and sell independent pawnshops, often to the chains, whose acquisitions run to dozens of stores a year (EZCORP added 52 acquired stores in fiscal 2025); community banks and specialty lenders extend credit lines secured on pawn loan books and inventory.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 2–10 per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Valuations, sale processes, credit decisions
**Proprietary data:** Private deal comparables and borrower financials
**Clock:** Deal timetables and loan renewals
**Buyer:** Principal / Credit officer
