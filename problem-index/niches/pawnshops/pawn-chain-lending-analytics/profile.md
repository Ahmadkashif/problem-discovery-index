# Pawn Chain Lending & Pricing Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pawnshops|Pawnshops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The central teams at FirstCash (1,207 US stores) and EZCORP (545 US stores), and at the larger regional groups, set lending guides by category, price merchandise, track forfeits, aged stock and scrap, and model store performance and acquisitions. Their output is used inside the company; nobody pays for it directly.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–50 lending, pricing and analytics staff per public chain (estimate); fewer at regional groups
**Output:** Lending guides, pricing rules, store and category performance analysis, acquisition models
**Proprietary data:** Millions of tickets with collateral descriptions, loan amounts, redemption, forfeit and realised sale prices — the best graded valuation record in the industry
**Clock:** Internal planning; quarterly earnings dates govern disclosure, not the analytical work
**Buyer:** Chief Operating Officer / Head of Lending
