# Police Pawn Detail Units

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pawnshops|Pawnshops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Detectives and civilian analysts in city police and sheriff's departments who review pawn and secondhand transaction reports, match items to burglary and theft reports, place holds, recover property and inspect shops. Funded by the department's budget.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 per department, mostly investigators rather than analysts (estimate)
**Output:** Holds, recoveries, arrests, shop inspections
**Proprietary data:** Access to transaction reports and local crime reports
**Clock:** Investigation and court timelines
**Buyer:** Property crimes commander
