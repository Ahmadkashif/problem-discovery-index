# Small-Dollar Credit Advocacy Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pawnshops|Pawnshops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Nonprofit consumer-finance research and advocacy groups — the National Consumer Law Center and the Center for Responsible Lending are the best known — study small-dollar credit, including pawn, payday and title loans, publish rate surveys and state-law analyses, and argue for rate caps such as the 36% limit. Funded by grants and donations; NCLC also sells legal manuals.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 researchers and policy analysts per organisation (estimate)
**Output:** Reports, state-law surveys, testimony and rulemaking comments
**Proprietary data:** Compiled state-law and rate surveys; little transaction data of their own
**Clock:** Legislative sessions and rulemaking comment deadlines
**Buyer:** Executive Director / Director of Research
