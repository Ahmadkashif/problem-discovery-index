# Forfeit-Risk Outreach Timed to Each Ticket

**Niche:** [[niches/pawnshops/loan-servicing-renewals/profile|Loan Servicing, Renewals & Notices]]
**Industry:** [[industries/pawnshops|Pawnshops]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Every open ticket gets the same reminder on the same schedule, when the store could tell which customers will forfeit without a nudge and reach them in time.
**Tags:** #survival-analysis #gradient-boosting #causal-inference #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a ticket that is about to forfeit into a renewal or redemption, with reminders and payment options that stay inside each state's notice and fee rules, and whoever converts the most at-risk tickets takes the account.

## The Problem
About a third of tickets forfeit (33.4% in Oregon's 2025 report). Some customers abandoned the item deliberately; many meant to come back. A fixed reminder schedule spends effort on customers who always redeem and reaches the at-risk ones too late or not in a way they act on.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Pawn POS reminders are simple schedules; generic messaging tools know nothing about tickets; and nobody has measured which reminders change outcomes.

## What to Build
Score each open ticket's risk of forfeit from the customer's history, the loan-to-value ratio and timing; contact the at-risk ones earlier, in their language and channel, with the renewal or partial-payment options their state allows and a payment link; and run randomised timing to measure the uplift, so the outreach budget goes where it changes outcomes.

## Target Customer
Lending operations leaders at chains; POS vendors adding servicing.

## Impact If Built
Fewer accidental forfeits, more fee income retained, and customers who keep the property they meant to keep.
