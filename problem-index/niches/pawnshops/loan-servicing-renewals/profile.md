# Loan Servicing, Renewals & Notices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pawnshops|Pawnshops]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a ticket that is about to forfeit into a renewal or redemption, with reminders and payment options that stay inside each state's notice and fee rules, and whoever converts the most at-risk tickets takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$80–150M a year in messaging, payments, phone handling and servicing software across US pawnshops (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1–2% of revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — text reminders and online fee payments exist at chains and in some POS systems; most servicing is still by phone and in person
**Target Buyer:** Director of Lending Operations at a chain; owner-pawnbroker
**Automation Potential:** High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A pawnshop's fee income comes from tickets that renew or redeem; a forfeit ends the fee stream and converts it into stock of uncertain value. Most customers want their items back — many pawn the same items repeatedly — and many forfeits are customers who forgot the date or could not get to the store. The clocks are statutory: in Florida a 30-day maturity, a 30-day grace period and automatic forfeiture after. Renewals are new credit for Military Lending Act purposes, and messaging needs consent.

## Current Tools & Gaps
POS reminder features, generic SMS and messaging tools (Podium, Twilio-based services), payment links, store phones. The gaps: reminders on a fixed schedule rather than by forfeit risk; no conversational handling of "how much to get my ring back"; state notice rules applied by hand; and no measurement of which outreach actually prevents forfeits.
