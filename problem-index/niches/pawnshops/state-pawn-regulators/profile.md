# State Pawn Regulators & Examiners

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pawnshops|Pawnshops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
License and examine pawnshops under state pawn acts: the Texas Office of Consumer Credit Commissioner, whose licensees file annual volume reports (its 2024 consolidated report covers ~1,338 reporting companies); Florida's Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services; Oregon's Division of Financial Regulation, which publishes forfeiture and police-pickup rates. Funded by licence fees and appropriations.

## Insight Function
**Size:** A handful of examiners and analysts per state on pawn (estimate)
**Output:** Licences, examinations, enforcement orders, aggregate annual reports
**Proprietary data:** Licensee annual reports and examination files
**Clock:** Annual report deadlines and examination cycles
**Buyer:** Commissioner / Director of Consumer Finance
