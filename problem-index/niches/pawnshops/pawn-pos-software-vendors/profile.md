# Pawn Point-of-Sale Software Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pawnshops|Pawnshops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Sell the ticketing, inventory, police-reporting and online-storefront software independents run on — Bravo Store Systems, with Bravo and its older PawnMaster Classic, is the best-known vendor. Pawnshops pay subscriptions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 data or analytics staff (estimate) inside product and support organisations
**Output:** Software and built-in reports; no billable insight product
**Proprietary data:** Hosted ticket and inventory data for many shops, owned by the shops
**Clock:** Release cycles and state reporting-format changes
**Buyer:** CEO / Head of Product
