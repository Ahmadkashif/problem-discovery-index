# Precious-Metal Refiners & Scrap Buyers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pawnshops|Pawnshops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Buy pawnshops' and jewellers' scrap gold and silver, melt and assay it, and pay for the fine metal at spot less refining charges and deductions. Revenue comes from refining charges and metal spreads.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Assay laboratories of tens of chemists and technicians at the large refiners (estimate), doing production testing
**Output:** Assays and settlements — a production service, not an analytical product
**Proprietary data:** Lot assays by customer over many years
**Clock:** Settlement terms and spot price fixes
**Buyer:** Head of Trading / Recycling Sales
