# Firearm Value Guides & Gun Auction Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pawnshops|Pawnshops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Publish the values used-gun counters price against: the Blue Book of Gun Values and similar printed and online guides, and the completed-auction data of online gun marketplaces such as GunBroker. Pawnbrokers, dealers and collectors pay for guide subscriptions; marketplace data is a by-product of auction fees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 editorial and pricing staff per guide (estimate); marketplace analytics teams are similarly small
**Output:** Value guides by make, model and condition grade; completed-sale lookups
**Proprietary data:** Contributor and dealer pricing networks; marketplace sale records
**Clock:** Annual editions and none set by a third party
**Buyer:** Publisher
