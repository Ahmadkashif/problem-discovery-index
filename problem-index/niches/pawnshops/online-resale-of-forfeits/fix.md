# The Item Sold Online While Under Police Hold

**Niche:** [[niches/pawnshops/online-resale-of-forfeits/profile|Online Resale of Forfeits]]
**Industry:** [[industries/pawnshops|Pawnshops]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A detective places a hold on an item that is already listed online, nobody pulls the listing, and the store ships evidence to a stranger.
**Tags:** #compliance #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a forfeited one-of-one item identified, photographed, priced and listed to buyers beyond the store in minutes rather than a quarter-hour, and whoever lists the most forfeits at retail rather than wholesale takes the account.

## The Problem
Police holds arrive at the store by phone or letter; online listings are managed by someone else or another system. The two do not talk.

## Why It's Still Broken
Holds are recorded, if at all, as a POS status that does not propagate to marketplace listings.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Treat a police hold as an event that withdraws every live listing for the item within minutes and blocks relisting until release, with the withdrawal logged.

## Who Feels the Pain
Store managers facing an angry detective; owners exposed to a compliance action.

## Impact If Fixed
A rare but serious failure is closed off by a simple integration.
