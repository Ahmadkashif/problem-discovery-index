# Diamond & Coloured-Gem Price Guide Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pawnshops|Pawnshops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Publish the reference prices the jewellery trade, including pawnbrokers, values stones against. The Rapaport Price List, issued weekly since 1978, quotes Rapaport's opinion of high cash asking prices by shape, size, colour and clarity; trades are quoted as a discount to it, and Rapaport sells a pawnbroker plan at $1,000 a year to members of a recognised pawn association. IDEX and GemGuide (coloured stones) are smaller rivals. Revenue comes from subscriptions and the trading networks built around the lists.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 pricing and research staff per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Weekly price lists, market reports, price indexes
**Proprietary data:** Listings and asking prices on their trading networks, plus decades of the list itself
**Clock:** Weekly publication the publisher sets; no contract settles against it on a fixed date
**Buyer:** Head of Pricing / Publisher
