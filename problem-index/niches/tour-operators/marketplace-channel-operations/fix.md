# The Refund Issued on One Side Only

**Niche:** [[niches/tour-operators/marketplace-channel-operations/profile|Experience Marketplace Channel Operations]]
**Industry:** [[industries/tour-operators|Tour Operators]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A weather cancellation is processed in the booking system, but the marketplace still shows the booking as active, so the traveller is not refunded or the seat is double-sold.
**Tags:** #automation #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #descriptive-statistics
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep one live inventory of seats, prices and cancellation terms across the booking system and every marketplace, and whoever sells the last seat without an overbooking, a stale price or a mismatched refund takes the account.

## The Problem
When an operator cancels a departure for weather, low numbers or a vehicle failure, the cancellation must reach every channel with the right refund under each channel's rules. Some connections pass cancellations both ways and some do not. Partial changes, such as a moved time or a reduced party size, are worse. Mismatches surface as angry travellers, chargebacks and marketplace penalties.

## Why It's Still Broken
Connections were built for selling, with cancellations added later and handled inconsistently across marketplaces. Staff assume the sync worked.

## What a Fix Looks Like
After any operator-initiated cancellation or change, an automatic check confirms the booking's state and refund on every channel within minutes. Exceptions are listed for a person to resolve while the traveller can still be reached.

## Who Feels the Pain
Reservations staff handling complaints, finance teams facing chargebacks, and travellers waiting for refunds.

## Impact If Fixed
Weather-day cancellations handled cleanly across channels, protecting review scores and marketplace standing (estimate).
