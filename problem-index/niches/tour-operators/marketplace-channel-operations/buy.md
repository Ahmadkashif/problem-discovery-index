# Marketplace Seller Reconciliation, Built for Departures

**Niche:** [[niches/tour-operators/marketplace-channel-operations/profile|Experience Marketplace Channel Operations]]
**Industry:** [[industries/tour-operators|Tour Operators]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** E-commerce sellers already use tools to reconcile Amazon and Shopify payouts against orders; tour operators match marketplace payouts to bookings and departures by hand.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #large-language-models #data-integration #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep one live inventory of seats, prices and cancellation terms across the booking system and every marketplace, and whoever sells the last seat without an overbooking, a stale price or a mismatched refund takes the account.

## The Problem
Marketplaces pay operators net of commission, after the activity date or on a schedule, in batches that mix bookings, amendments, cancellations and adjustments. Finance staff match each batch against the reservation system, line by line, and chase differences with marketplace support.

## What Already Exists
Marketplace accounting and reconciliation tools for e-commerce sellers (A2X, Link My Books, Webgility) map payouts to orders, fees and refunds and post to accounting systems.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs:
- matching at booking and departure level, so channel revenue can be compared with each departure's cost;
- commission rates that vary by product and promotion programme;
- recognition of cancellations and no-shows under each channel's policy, including partial refunds;
- multi-currency payouts for operators selling internationally;
- dispute packs with booking evidence, generated automatically.

## Target Customer
Finance managers at operators taking a large share of bookings through two or more marketplaces.

## Impact If Solved
Payout matching cut from days to hours each month, with unexplained differences recovered rather than written off (estimate).
