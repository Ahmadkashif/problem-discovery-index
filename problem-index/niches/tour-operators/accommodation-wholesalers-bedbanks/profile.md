# Accommodation Wholesalers & Bedbanks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tour-operators|Tour Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Bedbanks contract hotel rooms at net rates, sometimes with allotments, and resell them to tour operators, travel agencies and online sellers through APIs. HBX Group (Hotelbeds), whose main clients are tour operators and travel agencies, reported €3.4B of total transaction value in the first half of 2025; WebBeds is the other large player. Operators use them to fill gaps in their own allotments and to price dynamic packages. Bedbanks' pricing and revenue teams decide markups, which hotels to contract and how much allotment risk to take.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 revenue management, pricing and data science staff per large bedbank (estimate)
**Output:** Net rates and availability sold to operators; internal markup and contracting analysis
**Proprietary data:** Contracted rates, allotment take-up and booking patterns by hotel, market and source country across many thousands of properties
**Clock:** Hotel contracting seasons and release dates, which are commercial terms between the bedbank and its hotels rather than an outside deadline
**Buyer:** Chief commercial officer or head of revenue management
