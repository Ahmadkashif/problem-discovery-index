# Tour Director & Guide Training and Certification Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tour-operators|Tour Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Schools and bodies that train and certify tour directors and guides: tour-director schools such as ITMI; wilderness medicine providers such as NOLS, whose first-aid certifications adventure operators require; mountain and climbing guide associations such as AMGA; and city or national guide badges abroad. National Park Service Commercial Use Authorizations set operating conditions for guided tours in parks, but they are permits rather than certification.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 staff working on standards and curriculum per body (estimate)
**Output:** Courses, certifications, standards
**Proprietary data:** Graduate and certification records
**Clock:** Course calendars and certification renewal cycles
**Buyer:** Executive director
