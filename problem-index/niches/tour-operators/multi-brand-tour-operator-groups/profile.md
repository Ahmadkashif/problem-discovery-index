# Multi-Brand Tour Operator Groups

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tour-operators|Tour Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Groups that own several tour brands and run central revenue management, pricing, contracting and data teams across them. Lindblad's Land Experiences segment (Natural Habitat, Off the Beaten Path, DuVine, Classic Journeys, Thomson Group) reported $275.4M of tour revenue in 2025. Private-equity-backed groups of specialist brands and the family-owned escorted groups follow the same model. Central teams decide pricing, inventory commitments and which brands to grow; their analysis is internal.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-80 revenue management, pricing, contracting-analytics and data staff per large group (estimate)
**Output:** Internal pricing, inventory and acquisition analysis; brand performance reporting to owners
**Proprietary data:** Bookings, departures, supplier contracts, guest surveys and margins across brands, the richest departure-level record in the industry, held by a handful of firms
**Clock:** Brochure and pricing launch dates set by the group itself; quarterly reporting for the listed ones
**Buyer:** Chief commercial officer or head of revenue management
