# Tour Operator M&A & Valuation Advisers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tour-operators|Tour Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Boutique advisers and brokers who sell tour operators and DMCs to strategic buyers and private-equity groups, valuing businesses on booking trends, customer deposits, supplier contracts and brand. Deals such as Booking.com's 2018 purchase of FareHarbor and the 2023 Checkfront–Rezdy merger show the software side; operator deals are smaller and less public.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 2-10 per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Valuations, sale memoranda, buyer introductions
**Proprietary data:** Deal files and comparables, all under client confidentiality
**Clock:** Transaction timetables set by the client
**Buyer:** Managing partner
