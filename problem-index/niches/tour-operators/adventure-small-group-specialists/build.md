# A Partner Safety Record Built From Every Departure

**Niche:** [[niches/tour-operators/adventure-small-group-specialists/profile|Adventure & Small-Group Specialists]]
**Industry:** [[industries/tour-operators|Tour Operators]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Specialists vet local partners with a questionnaire and an annual visit, while the evidence of how each partner actually performs is scattered across leader reports, traveller comments and incident forms.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #bayesian-inference #survival-analysis #compliance #evaluation-metrics
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to prove that a remote, active itinerary run through local partners it rarely sees is safe and well run, and whoever can show travellers and advisors the lowest incident and partner-failure record takes the account.

## The Problem
A 40-person specialist may work with a hundred local ground operators, lodges, drivers and guide outfits across thirty countries (estimate). Each departure produces a leader's trip report, traveller feedback and sometimes an incident form. Signals about a partner, such as a vehicle that broke down twice, a guide who skipped a safety briefing, or a lodge with no working radio, appear one departure at a time and are forgotten by the next season's contracting.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The volume per operator is small, the text is unstructured and multilingual, and incidents are rare. Single operators cannot see patterns, and no one aggregates across operators. Insurers and associations see only claims or survey answers.

## What to Build
A partner record that reads every leader report, traveller comment and incident form, extracts partner-specific safety and service signals, and maintains a per-partner track record. Rare events are smoothed with hierarchical models so a single incident is neither ignored nor over-weighted. The output is a contracting view showing which partners to re-audit, renegotiate or drop, plus a summary of the record the operator can share with advisors and insurers.

## Target Customer
Heads of operations and safety at adventure and small-group specialists; with consent, the insurers who underwrite them.

## Impact If Built
Problems caught after one bad departure rather than three, and evidence to win advisor and traveller trust beyond a policy PDF (estimate).
