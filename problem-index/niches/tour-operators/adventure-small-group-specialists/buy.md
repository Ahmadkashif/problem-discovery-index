# Aviation-Style Safety Reporting for Ground Operators

**Niche:** [[niches/tour-operators/adventure-small-group-specialists/profile|Adventure & Small-Group Specialists]]
**Industry:** [[industries/tour-operators|Tour Operators]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Safety management systems in aviation and industry turn near-miss reports into trend analysis; adventure travel collects incidents, rarely near-misses, and seldom analyses either.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #descriptive-statistics #compliance #workflow-orchestration #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to prove that a remote, active itinerary run through local partners it rarely sees is safe and well run, and whoever can show travellers and advisors the lowest incident and partner-failure record takes the account.

## The Problem
Adventure operators record serious incidents because insurers and lawyers require it. Near-misses, such as a slip on a ledge, a late rescue call or a guide without a first-aid kit, go unrecorded, though they are the leading indicator.

## What Already Exists
Safety management and incident-reporting systems (EHS platforms such as Intelex, Cority and SafetyCulture; aviation safety reporting systems) support non-punitive reporting, classification, investigation and trend analysis.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs:
- reporting on a phone, offline, by leaders in the field and in several languages;
- a taxonomy for adventure activities (trekking, cycling, water, altitude, wildlife, vehicle);
- linkage of each report to the departure, itinerary segment and local partner;
- a privacy model that keeps traveller medical details apart;
- trend reports shaped for partner contracting decisions and for insurer renewals.

## Target Customer
Operations and safety leads at specialists with active itineraries and seasonal field leaders.

## Impact If Solved
A working near-miss culture with evidence that improves partner choice and insurance conversations.
