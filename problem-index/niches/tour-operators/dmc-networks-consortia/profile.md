# DMC Networks & Receptive Consortia

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tour-operators|Tour Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Networks and associations that group independent destination management companies so meeting planners and overseas operators can buy through one relationship: Global DMC Partners, Hosts Global and the ADMEI association. They run shared sales, quality standards and referral flows, and some report on request volumes for their members.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 central analysts per network (estimate)
**Output:** Member referrals, quality audits, occasional trend reports
**Proprietary data:** Request and booking flows routed through the network
**Clock:** Meetings-industry sales cycles
**Buyer:** Network president
