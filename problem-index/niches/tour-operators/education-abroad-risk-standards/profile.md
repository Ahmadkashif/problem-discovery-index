# Education-Abroad Risk & Standards Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tour-operators|Tour Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The bodies and offices that set the rules educational tour operators must pass. The Forum on Education Abroad publishes standards of good practice for study-abroad programmes; university education-abroad and risk-management offices review faculty-led programmes against travel policies and State Department advisories; school districts approve field trips. Operators answer their checklists. Duty-of-care risk intelligence is logged under Travel Agencies as [[niches/travel-agencies/travel-risk-intelligence/profile|🔍 Travel Risk Intelligence Providers]] (45).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 per body or institution (estimate)
**Output:** Standards, approval decisions, incident guidance
**Proprietary data:** Institutional incident and approval records
**Clock:** Academic calendars and approval deadlines
**Buyer:** Director of education abroad
