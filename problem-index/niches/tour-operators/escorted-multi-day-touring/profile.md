# Escorted Multi-Day Touring

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tour-operators|Tour Operators]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to guarantee departures early enough for advisors to sell them without running them half empty, and whoever guarantees the most departures while holding load above break-even takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.5B US operator receipts from escorted coach, rail and river-land programmes (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~25-30% of the ~$9.0B employer receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium. Booking platforms and advisor portals are digital; departure reviews and allotment decisions run on exported reports and spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** VP of Operations or Head of Product at an escorted brand
**Automation Potential:** High for forecasting and supplier paperwork, medium for the go/no-go call itself

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Escorted brands such as Globus, Collette, Trafalgar, Insight and Tauck sell a fixed itinerary on fixed dates with a tour director, typically to travellers over 50 and heavily through travel advisors. Group sizes run from the mid-20s to the mid-40s. Every departure carries hotel allotments in each city, a coach, a tour director and local guides committed months ahead. The defining promise is the guaranteed departure, which Globus marks "GD": once guaranteed, the date runs at any load. Advisors sell guaranteed dates more readily, so guaranteeing early wins bookings and guaranteeing wrongly loses money. Coach companies that run their own multi-day tours face a related version, logged under [[niches/charter-bus-operators/multi-day-tour-operators/profile|🟣 Multi-Day Tour Operators]] in Charter Bus Operators. There the fleet and driving hours dominate; here it is the package and its inventory.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Large brands run in-house reservation and inventory platforms connected to advisor booking tools. Mid-sized operators use Tourplan, TourCMS or legacy systems with Excel on top. Bedbanks fill shortfalls. The gaps: departure decisions are made from exported booking reports without a forecast or a record of the call; allotment release dates are tracked in spreadsheets; consolidation options are worked out by hand; and nothing grades last season's guarantees against their outcomes.
