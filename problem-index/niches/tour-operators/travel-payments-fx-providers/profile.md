# Travel Payments, FX & Merchant Risk Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tour-operators|Tour Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Currency specialists and payment providers serving tour operators. FX firms such as Convera, Corpay, Kantox and Ebury, and the banks, hedge supplier payments and offer layered hedging programmes, several with travel-specific marketing. Card acquirers and trust-account providers process deposits for travel taken months later, and their risk teams set rolling reserves and delivery-risk limits on each operator. Their market commentary is free; their risk analysis is internal.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 travel-vertical risk and market-strategy staff per provider; the 10-40 figure first logged here summed several firms (estimate)
**Output:** FX commentary and hedging proposals; merchant risk limits, reserves and holdbacks on deposits
**Proprietary data:** Payment flows by currency, season and operator; deposit and chargeback histories, including which operators failed to deliver
**Clock:** Supplier payment dates and card-scheme chargeback windows
**Buyer:** Head of travel vertical or chief risk officer at the provider
