# Tour Operator Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tour-operators|Tour Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
USTOA (whose Active Members each post $1M of security for its Travelers Assistance Program), NTA for group and packaged travel, ATTA for adventure travel, SYTA for student and youth travel, and ETOA for operators selling Europe. They run advocacy, consumer-protection programmes, trade shows and member surveys. ATTA's annual Adventure Travel Trends & Insights report is in its 18th year, built on an operator survey and limited in full to paying members.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research staff per body (estimate)
**Output:** Member surveys, trend reports, advocacy briefs
**Proprietary data:** Member survey series; USTOA's member financial security filings
**Clock:** Annual survey and conference calendar
**Buyer:** Chief executive or head of research
