# Corporate FX Risk Platforms, Fed by Itineraries

**Niche:** [[niches/tour-operators/tour-costing-currency-risk/profile|Tour Costing, Pricing & Currency Risk]]
**Industry:** [[industries/tour-operators|Tour Operators]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** FX-risk platforms automate hedging for any business with forecast foreign cash flows; a tour operator's forecast must be built from cost lines and bookings first.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #monte-carlo-methods #data-integration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to publish a tour price a year or more ahead that still earns its target margin after the hotels, coaches and guides are paid in other currencies, and whoever lands closest to costed margin after currency moves takes the account.

## The Problem
Operators that do hedge rely on a bank relationship manager or a specialist, and feed them a spreadsheet forecast quarterly. Hedge execution is fine. The forecast is the weak point.

## What Already Exists
Currency-management automation platforms (Kantox's layered and micro-hedging, Convera, Corpay, Ebury, bank portals) take forecast exposures or transaction feeds, execute forwards and options to a policy, and report hedge effectiveness. Some market specifically to travel companies.

## The Customization Gap
The platforms need a tour-specific front end:
- a connector to costing and tour-operations systems that turns itineraries and departure forecasts into dated exposures;
- handling of cancellations, which unwind exposure and leave supplier refunds in local currency;
- hedge allocation back to itineraries, so product managers see their own margin after currency;
- links to the operator's price list and surcharge terms, so repricing decisions and hedges are made together.

## Target Customer
Finance teams at mid-sized outbound operators already hedging, or ready to, through an FX specialist.

## Impact If Solved
An existing hedging relationship becomes an exposure-driven programme, with no new execution vendor.
