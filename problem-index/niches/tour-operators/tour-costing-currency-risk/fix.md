# The Surcharge Decision Made Too Late

**Niche:** [[niches/tour-operators/tour-costing-currency-risk/profile|Tour Costing, Pricing & Currency Risk]]
**Industry:** [[industries/tour-operators|Tour Operators]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Booking terms allow a currency surcharge up to a set date, and operators argue about using it until the date has passed.
**Tags:** #probability-distributions #descriptive-statistics #compliance #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to publish a tour price a year or more ahead that still earns its target margin after the hotels, coaches and guides are paid in other currencies, and whoever lands closest to costed margin after currency moves takes the account.

## The Problem
Many operators' terms permit price increases or surcharges for currency or fuel up to a stated number of days before departure, within limits. When the dollar falls, the decision to surcharge pits finance against sales: advisors hate it and travellers complain. It is debated without numbers until the window closes.

## Why It's Still Broken
Nobody can say quickly what the currency move has cost each departure, or what a surcharge would recover against the bookings it might lose. The terms' deadlines sit in the legal text, not in any system.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A standing report showing, per itinerary and departure, margin at today's rates against costed margin, the surcharge permitted under the terms, the last date it can be applied, and the bookings exposed. Thresholds agreed in advance trigger the decision automatically rather than by argument.

## Who Feels the Pain
CFOs and product managers watching margin erode, sales teams and advisors handling surcharge complaints, and travellers hit with late increases.

## Impact If Fixed
Surcharge decisions made on time and on evidence, or confidently avoided. Either way, no more margin lost to indecision.
