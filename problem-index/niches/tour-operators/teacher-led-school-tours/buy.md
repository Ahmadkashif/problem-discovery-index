# Sales Engagement Software, Pointed at Teacher-Leaders

**Niche:** [[niches/tour-operators/teacher-led-school-tours/profile|Teacher-Led School Tours]]
**Industry:** [[industries/tour-operators|Tour Operators]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Sales engagement platforms sequence outreach to prospects; a school tour operator's prospect is a teacher who must in turn sell the trip to thirty families.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #logistic-regression #workflow-orchestration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a teacher's interest into a departure that reaches its enrolment minimum, with parents paying in instalments over a year, and whoever converts the most teacher sign-ups into groups that actually travel takes the account.

## The Problem
Reps coach teachers through recruiting: scheduling the parent night, sending flyers home, answering parents' safety and cost questions, chasing approval. They do it by phone and email, from memory, across hundreds of teachers each.

## What Already Exists
Sales engagement tools (Outreach, Salesloft, HubSpot sequences) automate multi-step outreach and log activity. Operators' teacher portals provide marketing kits.

## The Customization Gap
The customer journey has two steps: the operator sells to the teacher, and the teacher sells to the parents. Sequences need:
- steps tied to the school calendar and the parent-night date;
- prompts triggered by the group's enrolment pace against its minimum;
- parent-facing content in the families' languages;
- reminders keyed to district approval deadlines;
- rep tasks ranked by which groups are at risk, not by deal value.

## Target Customer
Sales leaders at school tour operators running teacher recruitment teams.

## Impact If Solved
Reps spend time on the groups that need it, and teachers get help before the enrolment deadline rather than after.
