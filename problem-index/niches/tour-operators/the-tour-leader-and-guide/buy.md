# Workforce Scheduling for a Contractor Season

**Niche:** [[niches/tour-operators/the-tour-leader-and-guide/profile|The Tour Leader & Local Guide]]
**Industry:** [[industries/tour-operators|Tour Operators]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Shift-scheduling tools assign staff to shifts; guide scheduling assigns multi-day departures to contractors who work for several operators and must be booked a season ahead.
**Tags:** #optimization-fundamentals #dynamic-programming #workflow-orchestration #worker-facing #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to lock in the tour leaders and guides travellers rate highest before rival operators book their season, and whoever confirms those guides' seasons first takes the account.

## The Problem
Operations staff match hundreds of departures to leaders by language, itinerary qualification, availability and preference, then rework it as departures are cancelled or consolidated. Leaders are told late. Gaps are filled by whoever answers first.

## What Already Exists
Workforce management and scheduling platforms (Deputy, When I Work, UKG, Shiftboard) handle availability, rules, swaps and notifications for employees on shifts.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs:
- multi-day assignments that cannot be split;
- qualification matching by itinerary, language and activity certification (wilderness first aid, for example);
- availability shared with contractors who also work for rivals;
- assignments that adjust when departures are guaranteed, consolidated or cancelled, with the guide's cancellation fee terms applied;
- an offer process that tells top guides their season early and fills the rest by preference.

## Target Customer
Guide and tour-director staffing teams at escorted, small-group and educational operators.

## Impact If Solved
Season calendars confirmed earlier, fewer last-minute gaps, and guides who can plan their income.
