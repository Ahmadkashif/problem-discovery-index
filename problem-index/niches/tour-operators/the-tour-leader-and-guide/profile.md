# The Tour Leader & Local Guide

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tour-operators|Tour Operators]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to lock in the tour leaders and guides travellers rate highest before rival operators book their season, and whoever confirms those guides' seasons first takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600M a year paid by tour operators to tour directors, leaders and local guides in day rates, fees and per diems, excluding tips (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of the ~$9.0B employer receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low. Guides are booked by email and WhatsApp, scheduled in spreadsheets and assessed from survey averages
**Target Buyer:** Director of tour operations or head of guide staffing at an escorted, small-group or educational operator
**Automation Potential:** Medium. Scheduling and fair assessment automate; the guiding is irreducibly human

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The guide is the product travellers remember. Guides are also mostly seasonal contractors who work for several operators, earn modest base pay plus tips (~46,760 tour and travel guides nationally, median ~$36,060 in May 2023), and are booked months ahead for peak season. The best are scarce, and operators compete for their calendars. Each guide is assessed by end-of-tour surveys and marketplace reviews that mix their performance with the hotel, the coach and the weather. Tools serve the operator's inventory and the traveller's booking. None serves the guide as a person whose calendar, income and reputation the operator must win.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Staffing by spreadsheet and messaging apps; contractor management and payroll tools; survey platforms; marketplace and Tripadvisor reviews naming guides. Gaps: no fair, adjusted measure of guide quality; offers of days made late and piecemeal; guides' availability across operators invisible; and no development path informed by evidence.
