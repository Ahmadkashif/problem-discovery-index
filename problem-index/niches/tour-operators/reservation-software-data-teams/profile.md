# Tour Reservation & Operations Software Data Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tour-operators|Tour Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The reservation and tour-operations systems operators run on: FareHarbor (Booking Holdings since 2018), Peek, RC TopCo (Rezdy, Checkfront and Regiondo, merged in 2023; Checkfront alone reported 5,000+ clients), Bókun (Tripadvisor), Ventrata and Xola for day tours and activities; Tourplan, Tourwriter and TourCMS for multi-day and inbound operators. Their analytics teams build reporting, dynamic-pricing and forecasting features and publish industry benchmark reports as marketing.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 data and analytics staff per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** In-product dashboards, pricing suggestions, aggregate "state of the industry" reports given away to win customers
**Proprietary data:** Booking, availability, price and cancellation histories for thousands of operators, the closest thing to a cross-operator departure ledger
**Clock:** The vendor's own release and marketing calendar
**Buyer:** Head of product or data at the software vendor
