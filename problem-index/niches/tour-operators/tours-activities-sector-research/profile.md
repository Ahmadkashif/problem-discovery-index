## What They Do
Arival is the research and events company for the in-destination experiences sector. Its Global Operator Landscape (3rd edition) drew on ~7,000 operators surveyed in 2024, and it sells traveller and operator reports, such as the 2025 U.S. Experiences Traveler Outlook at $399 to non-members, alongside membership and conferences. Phocuswright and Skift Research cover the same sector within broader travel research, already logged as [[niches/travel-agencies/travel-market-research-publishers/profile|🔍 Travel Market Research Publishers]] (38).
