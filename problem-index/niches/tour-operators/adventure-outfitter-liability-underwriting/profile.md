# Adventure & Tour Operator Liability Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tour-operators|Tour Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Specialist carriers, managing general agents and brokers write general and professional liability, abuse and molestation cover for student travel, and accident cover for tour operators, outfitters and adventure specialists. They price risk by activity, destination, group type and the operator's safety programme, and investigate claims after falls, vehicle accidents and medical evacuations.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 underwriters, actuaries and loss-control staff per carrier or MGA on this class; the 10-40 figure first logged here summed several firms (estimate)
**Output:** Rates, underwriting guidelines, loss-control requirements and renewal terms
**Proprietary data:** Claims and exposure histories by activity, destination and operator, the only aggregated record of what actually goes wrong on tours
**Clock:** Policy renewal dates and state rate filings
**Buyer:** Head of specialty underwriting or programme manager
