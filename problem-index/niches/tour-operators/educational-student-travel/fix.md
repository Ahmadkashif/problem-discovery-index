# The Medical and Consent Forms Collected by Email

**Niche:** [[niches/tour-operators/educational-student-travel/profile|Educational & Student Group Travel]]
**Industry:** [[industries/tour-operators|Tour Operators]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A tour director handed a group of thirty teenagers often has their allergy, medication and consent details on paper, in an inbox, or not at all.

The other is getting a university's faculty-led programme through risk, academic and procurement review at a per-student price that holds. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Parents submit medical, dietary and consent information through forms, emails and scanned PDFs, and update it by email when something changes. The group leader, the tour director, hotels and restaurants each need different parts of it. When a student has a reaction or goes missing, the person on the ground needs the right page in seconds.

## Why It's Still Broken
The data is sensitive, so it is kept out of the main systems and handled by hand. Updates arrive unstructured. Nobody checks that what reached the restaurant matches what the parent wrote.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Structured capture at enrolment. Late changes sent as emails are read automatically and merged after a staff check. Role-based views give the leader and tour director a phone card per student, the restaurants a dietary list and the hotel a rooming list. Every update is logged with who saw it.

## Who Feels the Pain
Tour directors and teacher-leaders responsible for minors abroad, operations staff answering parents, and the students themselves.

## Impact If Fixed
Faster, safer responses to medical incidents. Fewer dietary failures at meals. A clear record when a parent or school asks what was known and when.
