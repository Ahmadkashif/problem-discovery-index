# Experience Marketplace Data Science

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tour-operators|Tour Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The experience marketplaces sell operators' tours and activities to travellers for a commission: Tripadvisor's Viator (~$4.7B gross bookings in 2025), GetYourGuide, Klook, and the experiences arms of Expedia, Booking.com and Airbnb. Their data science and analytics teams run search ranking, pricing and promotion programmes, fraud and quality controls, and the supplier dashboards and tips operators see. Revenue is the take rate (a supplier quoted Viator's basic commission at 27%; GetYourGuide moved some suppliers to 30% in 2025), not the analysis.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 data scientists, analysts and marketplace economists per large marketplace (estimate)
**Output:** Ranking and pricing systems; supplier performance dashboards and benchmarks given free to operators; demand trend reports used as marketing
**Proprietary data:** Search, click, booking, cancellation and review data across hundreds of thousands of products. It is the widest view of tour demand in existence, and it sees intent before any operator does
**Clock:** Continuous; no third-party deadline governs the work
**Buyer:** VP of supply or head of marketplace analytics
