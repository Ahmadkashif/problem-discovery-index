# The Cost Line Nobody Remembered

**Niche:** [[niches/tour-operators/receptive-dmc-operators/profile|Receptive Operators & Destination Management Companies]]
**Industry:** [[industries/tour-operators|Tour Operators]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A quote wins, the group travels, and the margin disappears into a resort fee, a driver's hotel room and a park entrance charge nobody priced.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #descriptive-statistics #feature-engineering #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn an overseas operator's or agency's request into an accurate, costed proposal within hours, and whoever quotes fastest without missing a cost line takes the account.

## The Problem
Fast quoting invites omissions. Conditional costs are the usual leaks: per-vehicle park entrances, overnight accommodation and meals for the driver and guide, baggage handling, resort fees, coach parking in cities, overtime when a day runs long. Each is small, but together they can erase a thin group margin, and they surface only when supplier invoices arrive after the trip.

## Why It's Still Broken
The knowledge of which costs apply where is held by experienced specialists. Post-trip reconciliation of invoices against the quote is rarely done systematically, so the same omissions repeat.

## What a Fix Looks Like
After every trip, reconcile supplier invoices against the quote line by line and log each unquoted cost with its trigger (city, hotel, route, group size). Before a quote goes out, check it against that log and the itinerary's features, and flag likely missing lines.

## Who Feels the Pain
DMC owners watching margin, product specialists blamed for omissions, and finance staff matching invoices.

## Impact If Fixed
A few points of group margin recovered on trips that currently leak (estimate), and a quoting team that learns from each trip.
