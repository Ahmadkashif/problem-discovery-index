# CPQ Software for Itineraries

**Niche:** [[niches/tour-operators/receptive-dmc-operators/profile|Receptive Operators & Destination Management Companies]]
**Industry:** [[industries/tour-operators|Tour Operators]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Configure-price-quote software assembles complex quotes from product catalogues with rules; a receptive's catalogue is hundreds of supplier contracts with seasons, release dates and conditional charges.
**Tags:** #optimization-fundamentals #large-language-models #data-integration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn an overseas operator's or agency's request into an accurate, costed proposal within hours, and whoever quotes fastest without missing a cost line takes the account.

## The Problem
Pricing an itinerary means applying many contract rules: season dates, day-of-week rates, child and single policies, minimum stays, group thresholds, resort and parking fees, driver and guide accommodation, mandatory gratuities, and markup by buyer market. Specialists hold many of these rules in their heads.

## What Already Exists
CPQ platforms (Salesforce CPQ, DealHub, Conga) manage catalogues, pricing rules and approvals for manufacturers and software firms. Tourplan and Tourwriter cover parts of this for travel.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs:
- a catalogue built from supplier contracts that expire and renew each season, with rates extracted rather than typed;
- time-dependent rules (season, weekday, release date);
- per-group cost lines that do not scale with travellers, such as coach, guide and driver room;
- multi-currency output for foreign buyers;
- a check that every day of the itinerary has accommodation, transport and meals accounted for.

## Target Customer
Receptive operators and DMCs with more contracts than a spreadsheet can safely hold.

## Impact If Solved
Consistent pricing across specialists and fewer quotes that lose money when they run.
