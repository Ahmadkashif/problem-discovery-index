# Proposal Automation Built for University Risk Review

**Niche:** [[niches/tour-operators/faculty-led-university-programs/profile|Faculty-Led University Programmes]]
**Industry:** [[industries/tour-operators|Tour Operators]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** RFP and proposal tools draft answers from a content library; a faculty-led proposal must answer each university's risk, insurance and procurement checklist in its own format.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a faculty-led programme through the university's risk, academic and procurement review and deliver it at the per-student price students were quoted, and whoever clears approval fastest with a price that holds to departure takes the account.

## The Problem
Each university asks for a different packet:
- provider insurance certificates;
- emergency response plans;
- supplier vetting for each hotel and transport provider;
- State Department advisory level and mitigation for each location;
- health and safety briefings;
- itinerary with academic contact hours;
- procurement forms.

Providers assemble these by hand for every programme, and approval waits on the slowest document.

## What Already Exists
Proposal and RFP response platforms (Loopio, Responsive) maintain answer libraries and draft responses. Study-abroad management systems hold the university's own forms.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs:
- a library of itinerary-specific risk content (location advisories, hospital access, supplier safety records) that updates when advisories change;
- mapping to each university's checklist and template;
- contact-hour calculation for academic approval;
- insurance certificate tracking by programme dates;
- a change log when the itinerary moves after approval.

## Target Customer
Custom-programme providers and university-programme teams at educational operators answering dozens of institutional packets a year.

## Impact If Solved
Approval packets ready in days instead of weeks, and fewer programmes missing the student application deadline.
