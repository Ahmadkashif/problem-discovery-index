# Tour Operator Bonding & Financial-Failure Cover

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tour-operators|Tour Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Surety companies and banks provide the bonds and letters of credit tour operators post for USTOA's $1M Travelers Assistance Program and for state seller-of-travel requirements, and some insurers sell supplier-default cover to travellers. Underwriters judge each operator's financial strength at issue and renewal. Traveller-side cancellation and default claims are already logged under Travel Agencies as [[niches/travel-agencies/travel-insurance-claims-analytics/profile|🔍 Travel Insurance Underwriting & Claims Analytics]] (36).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 underwriters specialising in travel bonds (estimate)
**Output:** Bond approvals, premiums and collateral requirements
**Proprietary data:** Operator financial statements submitted for bonding
**Clock:** Bond renewal dates
**Buyer:** Surety underwriting manager
