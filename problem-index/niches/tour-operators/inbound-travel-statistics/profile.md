# Inbound Travel Statistics Agencies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/tour-operators|Tour Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Commerce Department's National Travel and Tourism Office (NTTO) produces the federal statistics receptive operators plan against. It publishes international arrivals by country from I-94 records and runs the Survey of International Air Travelers on visitor profiles and spending. Receptive operators and DMCs use the figures to judge which source markets are growing.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-25 statisticians and analysts at NTTO (estimate)
**Output:** Monthly arrivals by country, traveller profile and spending tables, forecasts
**Proprietary data:** Administrative arrivals records and a long-running traveller survey
**Clock:** The office's own release calendar
**Buyer:** Director of NTTO
