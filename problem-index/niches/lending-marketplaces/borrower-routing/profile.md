# Borrower Routing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/lending-marketplaces|Lending Marketplaces]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to send each borrower to the lender who will actually approve them on the best terms — and the contest splits cleanly enough that it is not terminal.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.9B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~32% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — excellent ranking on the wrong target
**Target Buyer:** Data and product leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — both halves are unexploited

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Routing is the product. The marketplace collects a profile and decides which lenders the borrower sees, and that decision determines whether the borrower is approved, what they pay, whether the lender considers the lead worth buying, and whether the borrower absorbs a wasted credit inquiry. It is ranked on click-through, because that is the signal that comes back. This is the category's largest contest and it is two contests: predicting the outcome from what is known, and changing what comes back at all.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Ranking models trained on click-through, bid-weighted ordering, eligibility filters and funding notifications where they exist. The gaps: no approval prediction; outcomes not returned by most lenders; ranking objective misaligned with borrower outcome; selection bias unmodelled; and no measurement of routing quality at all.
