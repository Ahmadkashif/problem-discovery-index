# Consent & Lead Compliance

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/lending-marketplaces|Lending Marketplaces]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to prove that every contact made to every consumer was covered by a consent that survives a courtroom — and whoever makes that provable at lead level removes the largest litigation exposure in the business.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$700M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — screenshots and spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** Compliance and legal leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — evidence capture and lineage

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A lead is worthless unless it can lawfully be contacted, and the record proving that — what the consumer saw, which parties were named, what they clicked, when, and what was disclosed — is the difference between a business and a class action. Leads are resold, so the consent must travel with them and must cover every party who eventually calls. This is the category's largest single legal exposure and it is administered with screenshots, spreadsheets and trust.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Consent capture widgets, TrustedForm-style certificates, partner attestations and archived disclosure pages. The gaps: consent not linked to each downstream contact; resale chains untracked; page versions not preserved; no automated check that a caller was named in the consent; and no measurement of coverage.
