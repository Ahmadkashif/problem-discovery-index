# Acquisition Economics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/lending-marketplaces|Lending Marketplaces]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to bid on a borrower's expected funded value rather than on their click probability — and whoever gets the funded signal into the bid takes the traffic everyone else is overpaying for.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.2B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~20% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** High — excellent machinery, wrong target
**Target Buyer:** Performance marketing leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — bidding against a better-defined value

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Almost all of a lending marketplace's cost is paid acquisition. The bidding, keyword and creative machinery is genuinely sophisticated, and it optimises toward a form submission because that is where the measurable signal ends. Two form submissions with identical cost can differ by an order of magnitude in funded value, and the bidding cannot distinguish them. The contest is getting the downstream value into the bid, which makes this the commercial mirror of the routing problem.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Paid search and social bidding, SEO, affiliate and comparison placements, and cost-per-lead targets. The gaps: bidding to form fills rather than funded value; no value prediction at the click; channel quality differences invisible; SEO and paid measured separately from lead outcome; and no feedback from the routing layer into acquisition.
