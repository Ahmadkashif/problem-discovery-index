# Product Information Management

**Niche:** [[niches/lending-marketplaces/product-and-rate-catalogue/profile|Product & Rate Catalogue]]
**Industry:** [[industries/lending-marketplaces|Lending Marketplaces]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Retail solved keeping millions of supplier products accurate with product information management and supplier self-service, and lending marketplaces maintain hundreds by hand.
**Tags:** #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #evaluation-metrics #large-language-models #compliance #descriptive-statistics #change-point-detection
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep hundreds of lender products, eligibility rules, rate structures and state restrictions accurate without maintaining them by hand — and whoever does it stops showing borrowers offers that do not exist.

## The Problem
Retail and distribution faced exactly this at far larger scale and built product information management: a governed catalogue, supplier portals where the supplier maintains their own data, syndication standards, validation rules, and data quality scoring with accountability. It works because the party who knows the truth is the party who enters it. Lending marketplaces have the same structure with a hundredth of the volume and none of the apparatus.

## What Already Exists
Product information management platforms; supplier self-service portals; data syndication standards; validation rule engines; and data quality scoring and governance.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a product that is a set of eligibility rules rather than a physical item. It requires: (1) modelling conditional logic — score bands, state restrictions, amount and term interactions — rather than attributes, which is the substantive difference and is what no PIM schema expresses; (2) validation against observed outcomes rather than against format rules, since the real test is whether the stated rules match actual decisions; (3) regulated advertised terms, where an error is a compliance matter rather than a customer service one; (4) lenders who consider parts of their criteria proprietary and will not publish them fully; and (5) rates that change frequently and asynchronously, closer to a pricing feed than to a catalogue.

## Target Customer
Partner operations and compliance leadership, lender partners, and PIM vendors for whom financial products are unentered.

## Impact If Solved
Retail solved this by making the supplier maintain their own data. The adaptation is a schema that expresses conditional eligibility rather than attributes, which is the part no existing platform can represent.
