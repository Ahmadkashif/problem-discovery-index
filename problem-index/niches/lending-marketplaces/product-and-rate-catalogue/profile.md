# Product & Rate Catalogue

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/lending-marketplaces|Lending Marketplaces]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep hundreds of lender products, eligibility rules, rate structures and state restrictions accurate without maintaining them by hand — and whoever does it stops showing borrowers offers that do not exist.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$800M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — spreadsheets and manual entry
**Target Buyer:** Partner operations leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — extraction, validation and drift detection

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Everything the borrower sees rests on the catalogue: which products exist, who qualifies, what rates apply, which states are served, what amounts and terms are available. It is maintained by people reading lender emails and updating rows. A stale entry means a borrower is shown an offer that does not exist, a filter fails, or a lender receives applicants it cannot serve. The contest is accuracy at scale over data that changes constantly and arrives unstructured.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Internal admin tools, spreadsheets, email from lender partners, and periodic manual audits. The gaps: no automated extraction from lender materials; no validation against observed outcomes; changes not detected until someone notices; no freshness indicator; and no measurement of catalogue error rate.
