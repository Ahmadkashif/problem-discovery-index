# The Loan Consultant

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/lending-marketplaces|Lending Marketplaces]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell a licensed consultant which lenders will actually approve the person they are calling — and whoever supplies that turns a dialling job back into an advisory one.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$550M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — dialler and CRM, no intelligence
**Target Buyer:** Sales and call centre leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — prioritisation and match guidance

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
In the models that run a call centre, licensed consultants call consumers minutes after a form submission. They must be persuasive, compliant and useful about a borrowing decision, without knowing which lenders will approve this person or on what terms. They are measured on conversion of leads whose quality was determined before they arrived. The role sits at the point where the marketplace's missing feedback loop becomes a person's daily experience.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A dialler, a CRM record, a product list and a script. The gaps: no approval guidance per borrower; leads worked in arrival order rather than by likelihood; no record of which pitches work; conversion targets set without reference to lead quality; and compliance obligations carried entirely by the individual.
