# Sales Enablement for Licensed Selling

**Niche:** [[niches/lending-marketplaces/the-loan-consultant/profile|The Loan Consultant]]
**Industry:** [[industries/lending-marketplaces|Lending Marketplaces]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Sales enablement and conversation intelligence are mature products built for unregulated selling where saying the wrong thing is a lost deal rather than a violation.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #worker-facing #workflow-orchestration #compliance #evaluation-metrics #automation #descriptive-statistics #gradient-boosting
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell a licensed consultant which lenders will actually approve the person they are calling — and whoever supplies that turns a dialling job back into an advisory one.

## The Problem
Sales enablement is well developed: lead scoring, sequencing, conversation intelligence with call analysis and coaching, real-time assist, and playbooks derived from what top performers do. It is built for selling a product the seller controls, in conversations where the risk of a bad line is a lost opportunity. Licensed lending consultants sell a third party's credit decision under disclosure rules where the wrong line is a regulatory matter.

## What Already Exists
Lead scoring and prioritisation engines; conversation intelligence and call coaching; real-time agent assist; sequence and cadence management; and playbook derivation from performance data.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to selling a decision made by someone else, under licence. It requires: (1) lead scoring based on approval likelihood at third-party lenders rather than on buying intent, which is the substantive difference and no enablement product can compute; (2) conversation analysis for disclosure compliance as well as for technique, since both are being assessed in the same call; (3) real-time assist that must be accurate about regulated terms rather than merely persuasive; (4) consultant performance separated from lead quality, which enablement platforms conflate and which is central to fairness here; and (5) a consent-bounded contact window, where cadence tooling assumes the seller may keep trying.

## Target Customer
Sales and compliance leadership, licensed consultants, and sales enablement vendors for whom regulated selling is unentered.

## Impact If Solved
Enablement assumes the seller controls the product and a bad line costs a deal. Scoring on third-party approval likelihood and analysing calls for disclosure alongside technique is what the regulated version requires.
