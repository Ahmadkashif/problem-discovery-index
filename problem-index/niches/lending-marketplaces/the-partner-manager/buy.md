# Partner Management From Channel Sales

**Niche:** [[niches/lending-marketplaces/the-partner-manager/profile|The Partner Manager]]
**Industry:** [[industries/lending-marketplaces|Lending Marketplaces]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Channel and partner management platforms handle joint planning, performance dashboards and tiering, and none of it addresses a partner disputing the quality of what they bought.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give a partner manager evidence that answers the lender's actual question when the answer lives in the lender's own systems — and whoever arms that conversation changes what the relationship can be negotiated on.

## The Problem
Channel sales has developed partner management: tiering, joint business planning, performance dashboards, deal registration, incentive design and quarterly business reviews. It assumes partners who resell a product and whose performance is measured in sales made — a shared, observable outcome. The lending marketplace relationship is a wholesale purchase of leads whose quality is assessed entirely inside the buyer, which is the one thing the discipline has no answer for.

## What Already Exists
Partner relationship management platforms; tiering and incentive frameworks; joint business planning; partner performance dashboards; and quarterly business review structures.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a partner whose performance data is withheld. It requires: (1) quality assessed by the partner and not shared, which inverts the dashboard model and is the substantive gap — every PRM assumes shared outcome visibility; (2) tiering based on data contribution as well as spend, which is the structural lever this relationship needs; (3) a product whose value is probabilistic, so disputes are about distributions rather than about defects; (4) a regulated context where what may be shared about applicants is constrained; and (5) a spot-market dynamic where lenders adjust bids continuously rather than committing annually.

## Target Customer
Partner leadership, lender partnership teams, and partner management vendors for whom lead wholesale relationships are unaddressed.

## Impact If Solved
Every partner platform assumes both sides see the outcome. Tiering on data contribution and handling disputes about distributions rather than defects is the adaptation this relationship actually needs.
