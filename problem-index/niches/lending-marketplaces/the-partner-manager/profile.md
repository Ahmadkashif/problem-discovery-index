# The Partner Manager

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/lending-marketplaces|Lending Marketplaces]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give a partner manager evidence that answers the lender's actual question when the answer lives in the lender's own systems — and whoever arms that conversation changes what the relationship can be negotiated on.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — decks and spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** Partner leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — reporting and evidence assembly

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The partner manager owns the lender relationship: defending lead quality, negotiating bids upward, managing volume commitments, and handling complaints. The lender's question is always some version of "why are these leads bad", and the answer requires the lender's own approval data, which the marketplace does not have. So the conversation is conducted with volume reports, anecdotes and goodwill. This is a distinct audience whose entire job is constrained by the same missing feedback loop.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Spreadsheets, quarterly decks, volume dashboards and email threads. The gaps: no lead quality evidence; no segment-level performance view; bid negotiation without a value model; complaints handled case by case; and no early warning when a lender is disengaging.
