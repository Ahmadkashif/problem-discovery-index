# Postback and Conversion Exchange

**Niche:** [[niches/lending-marketplaces/outcome-data-recovery/profile|Outcome Data Recovery]]
**Industry:** [[industries/lending-marketplaces|Lending Marketplaces]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Performance marketing built postback infrastructure so advertisers return conversion events as a routine condition of buying, and lending marketplaces never adopted the convention.
**Tags:** #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact #compliance #descriptive-statistics #confidence-intervals
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make returning the decision a condition of participating in the marketplace — and whoever changes that term first gets the feedback loop the whole category has been operating without.

## The Problem
Performance marketing solved this. Advertisers send conversion postbacks because the ecosystem made it standard, the tooling made it trivial, and the optimisation that follows is worth more to the advertiser than the data is worth to withhold. Affiliate networks, app attribution and ad platforms all run on it. Lending marketplaces are structurally an affiliate channel with a much higher value per conversion and have none of the convention.

## What Already Exists
Postback and server-to-server conversion APIs; conversion event taxonomies; attribution platforms and identifier matching; privacy-preserving conversion measurement; and the commercial norms making contribution standard.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a conversion that is a regulated credit decision. It requires: (1) a decline as the valuable event rather than only a conversion, which inverts the usual taxonomy and is the substantive adaptation — postback infrastructure reports success and the marketplace most needs failure; (2) applicant-level data subject to credit and privacy regimes, so the exchange must be designed around what may lawfully move; (3) terms and reason bands rather than a binary conversion flag; (4) an outcome that continues for years, since loan performance is the deepest signal and arrives long after; and (5) a small number of high-value partners rather than a long tail, which makes negotiation viable where ecosystem convention is absent.

## Target Customer
Commercial and product leadership, lender partners, attribution and postback vendors, and industry bodies.

## Impact If Solved
The infrastructure and the commercial norm both exist in an adjacent channel. The adaptation is a taxonomy where the decline is the event worth reporting, which no postback system was built to express.
