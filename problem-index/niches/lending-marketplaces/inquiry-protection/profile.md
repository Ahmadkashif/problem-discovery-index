# Inquiry Protection

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/lending-marketplaces|Lending Marketplaces]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stop spending the borrower's credit file on applications that will be declined — and whoever treats the inquiry as a cost rather than as free takes the trust the category has never had.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$150M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — inquiry treated as free
**Target Buyer:** Product and compliance leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — a filtering and sequencing decision

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every application the marketplace generates that ends in a decline costs the borrower something real: a hard inquiry on their credit file, a small score reduction, and a record that other lenders will see. The marketplace bears none of that cost and the lender bears very little, which is why nobody manages it. It is the clearest example in the category of an externality the business is structurally arranged not to notice, and it is entirely addressable.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Soft-pull prequalification where lenders support it, and disclosure language. The gaps: no accounting for inquiries generated; soft-pull options not preferred systematically; applications not sequenced to minimise inquiries; rate-shopping windows not explained to borrowers; and no measure of inquiries per funded loan.
