# Cross-Lender Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/lending-marketplaces|Lending Marketplaces]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn the view of the same borrower shopping across many lenders over years into answers no individual lender can produce — and whoever organises it owns the only empirical picture of how credit is actually priced and granted.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — asset unexploited
**Target Buyer:** Data and commercial leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — modelling over data already collected

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The marketplace sees the same borrower shopping across many lenders, with the full profile they submitted, repeatedly over years. No lender sees that. It is the only vantage point from which questions like which lender actually approves this profile, what terms they actually offer, how those terms vary by channel and season, and how the market's appetite is shifting can be answered empirically. The asset is used to sell leads.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Internal reporting, occasional research pieces and rate table displays. The gaps: shopping behaviour across lenders not modelled; approval and pricing patterns never characterised; market appetite shifts not detected; no product built on the view; and the borrower's repeat shopping history unused even for their own benefit.
