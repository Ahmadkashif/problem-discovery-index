**Contested on:** Every serious competitor here is fighting to convert use of freely available software into revenue — and that contest is fought against the free edition in one model and against a hyperscaler in the other, which is why this niche is not terminal and is decomposed below.

## The Problem
Freemium is a studied commercial model: which limits produce upgrades, how a free tier should be constrained, where the paywall should sit, how to measure the effect of moving it. Software companies run experiments on all of it continuously. Open core is structurally the same decision with a much higher stake and is made by argument in a leadership meeting, informed by intuition about a community.

## What Already Exists
Freemium conversion analysis with a substantial practitioner literature; propensity and uplift modelling for conversion; pricing and packaging research; experimentation infrastructure; and cohort analysis. All mature and practised routinely one category over.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a free tier the vendor cannot take back and users they cannot observe. It requires: (1) working without complete usage data, since the free users are largely invisible, which means inference from the sources the adoption visibility niche describes rather than from a product analytics stream; (2) irreversibility as a constraint, because a feature released openly can be forked and cannot be withdrawn — which makes the boundary decision one-way in a manner freemium pricing is not, and argues for caution in the open direction and experimentation in the commercial one; (3) community response as a modelled cost rather than an externality, since a boundary move that damages contribution and goodwill has a real and delayed cost that consumer freemium analysis has no analogue for; (4) the fork as the substitute good, meaning the elasticity that matters is against a community reimplementation rather than against a competitor's price; and (5) honest treatment of the fact that the natural experiments are few and confounded, since a company changes its licence at a moment when other things are also happening, and over-reading a single event is the obvious failure mode.

## Target Customer
Open-source company leadership, the investors evaluating these models, and the pricing and packaging advisors serving this market.

## Impact If Solved
A well-studied commercial model maps onto the category's central decision and has not been applied, largely because the free tier is invisible. Treating the fork as the substitute good and community response as a modelled cost are the two adaptations that make the analysis fit this setting rather than a consumer one.
