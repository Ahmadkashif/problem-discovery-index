## The Problem
Determining what customers will pay for which combination of features is a mature commercial research discipline: conjoint and discrete choice analysis, price sensitivity measurement, and feature bundling optimisation, all with established methodology and available practitioners. Open-core vendors set the boundary by internal debate, and several of them are large enough to have every other commercial function professionalised.

## What Already Exists
Conjoint and discrete choice analysis with standard designs and software; price sensitivity measurement methods; bundling and versioning theory from economics; survey instrumentation; and the commercial research industry that runs these studies routinely.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a market where the free alternative is the vendor's own product. It requires: (1) the free edition as an explicit option in every choice set, since a conjoint study that omits it measures the wrong thing entirely — the relevant comparison is against doing without, and this is the single most important design point; (2) the effort of the workaround as an attribute, because the respondent's real alternative is building or assembling the capability themselves and its difficulty varies enormously by feature and by organisation; (3) respondents who are the actual decision-makers, which in this market is a platform engineer rather than a procurement function and requires reaching a population that does not answer commercial surveys; (4) the community relationship as a constraint rather than an attribute, since some boundaries are commercially optimal and community-destroying and the study cannot price that; and (5) validation against actual behaviour, since stated preference in this population is unusually unreliable and the observational evidence from past boundary moves is the check.

## Target Customer
Open-source company leadership, product management, and the commercial research firms who could serve this market and mostly do not.

## Impact If Solved
Established willingness-to-pay methodology has not reached a category that makes this decision existentially. Including the free edition as an option and the workaround effort as an attribute are the two design points that make the study measure the real decision.
