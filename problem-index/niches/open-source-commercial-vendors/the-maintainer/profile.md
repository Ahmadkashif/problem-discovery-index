# The Maintainer

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/open-source-commercial-vendors|Open Source Commercial Vendors]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor that takes this seriously is fighting to make maintaining a widely used project sustainable for the person doing it — and whoever does that takes the projects, because maintainer departure is the category's most common and least addressed failure.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$840M US attributable to maintainer support, funding and sustainability
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** None — the problem is acknowledged in conference talks and addressed by nothing
**Target Buyer:** Vendors, foundations and funders; the beneficiary is the maintainer
**Automation Potential:** High for the load, and the underlying issue is structural rather than technical

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Maintainers carry an unbounded queue from a community far larger than themselves, absorb its frustration personally, and leave — which the industry acknowledges regularly and addresses rarely. The specifics are consistent and are worth naming precisely: the volume is unbounded because anyone can open an issue and nobody can close the tap; the emotional load is direct, since complaints arrive addressed to a person by name rather than to a support queue; the work is invisible, because maintenance produces no artefact anyone celebrates; the obligation is asymmetric, with users expecting responsiveness they are not paying for; and the exit is silent, because a maintainer who stops does not announce it. This is a distinct constituency because the remedies are specific and mostly structural — bounding the queue, sharing the load, making the work visible and compensated — and because almost nothing in the category is built for them.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Issue templates, labels, stale bots, contribution guides, funding platforms, and codes of conduct. The gaps: every tool manages the queue and none bounds it, so the underlying arithmetic is unchanged; the emotional load has no mechanism at all, although the abusive subset of interactions is identifiable and could be intercepted; maintainer load is unmeasured, so burnout is recognised only after departure; succession is unplanned in almost every project; funding platforms distribute money by visibility rather than by load; and corporate users who depend on a project contribute nothing back in the ordinary case and have no mechanism to.
