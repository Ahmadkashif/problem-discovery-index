# Co-Manufacturer Matching Narrowed to Packaging Capability

**Niche:** [[niches/contract-packagers/emerging-short-run-brands/profile|Emerging Brands & Short-Run Clients]]
**Industry:** [[industries/contract-packagers|Contract Packagers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Matching marketplaces already connect food and beverage brands with co-manufacturers; brands that only need packing need matching on lines, formats and certifications.
**Tags:** #k-nearest-neighbors #word-embeddings #feature-engineering #automation #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make a run of a few thousand units pay for its own setup and changeover, and whoever can quote and schedule short runs at a margin, without a minimum order the brand cannot meet, takes the account.

## The Problem
A brand looking for someone to band a multipack, build a small display or relabel an import searches directories and calls around. Co-packers waste time on requests their lines cannot run.

## What Already Exists
Co-manufacturer matching marketplaces and trade association directories, mostly organised around food and beverage production capability.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: capability records at line and format level (blister, shrink bundling, hand kitting, display build, labelling), certifications (SQF, cGMP, organic), minimum and preferred run sizes, and open capacity. Matching should run on the brand's actual spec and timing, not on a category.

## Target Customer
Matching platforms and associations serving emerging brands; co-packers wanting qualified short-run leads.

## Impact If Solved
Brands reach a suitable packager in days; packagers see only requests their lines can run.
