# Short-Run Pricing That Shows the Changeover

**Niche:** [[niches/contract-packagers/emerging-short-run-brands/profile|Emerging Brands & Short-Run Clients]]
**Industry:** [[industries/contract-packagers|Contract Packagers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A short run's unit price is dominated by setup and changeover, and co-packers either hide it in a minimum order or lose money on the job.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #linear-regression #optimization-fundamentals #feature-engineering #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make a run of a few thousand units pay for its own setup and changeover, and whoever can quote and schedule short runs at a margin, without a minimum order the brand cannot meet, takes the account.

## The Problem
For a 3,000-unit run, setup, changeover, line clearance and first-article checks can cost as much as the run itself (estimate). Co-packers respond with minimums or padded prices, and small brands either cannot buy or cannot tell why the price is what it is.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Few co-packers measure changeover time by format pair, so they cannot price it. Small brands are seen as risky, low-value accounts not worth a pricing tool.

## What to Build
A short-run pricer that uses the packager's measured changeover times and run rates to quote a setup charge and a run charge separately. It suggests slotting the run next to a similar format to cut changeover, and offers the brand a lower price for flexible timing. The quote shows the brand how volume and timing move the unit cost.

## Target Customer
Co-packers that want to serve emerging brands profitably; incubators and marketplaces that refer brands to them.

## Impact If Built
Short runs priced honestly and filled into gaps in the schedule, turning a money-losing segment into a feeder for future programme accounts.
