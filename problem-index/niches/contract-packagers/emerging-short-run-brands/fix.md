# The Brand Onboarding That Takes Three Months

**Niche:** [[niches/contract-packagers/emerging-short-run-brands/profile|Emerging Brands & Short-Run Clients]]
**Industry:** [[industries/contract-packagers|Contract Packagers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A first-time brand does not know what specs, certificates and component counts its packager needs, and the back-and-forth eats the launch window.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #automation #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make a run of a few thousand units pay for its own setup and changeover, and whoever can quote and schedule short runs at a margin, without a minimum order the brand cannot meet, takes the account.

## The Problem
New brands arrive without specifications, component overage plans, retailer requirements or the certificates a co-packer must file. The packager's account manager teaches them by email, one missing item at a time, while the retailer's launch date approaches (estimate: weeks to months).

## Why It's Still Broken
Every packager has its own intake checklist in someone's head, and brands do not know what they do not know.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A guided intake that asks the brand for exactly what the job needs, explains why, checks uploads for completeness (spec sheet, artwork, component counts with overage, certificates) and produces a ready-to-quote job record.

## Who Feels the Pain
Founders racing a launch date; co-packer account managers repeating the same lessons.

## Impact If Fixed
Onboarding in days, fewer launches missed, and account managers freed for paying work.
