# Broker Compliance Workflows Extended to the Rework Floor

**Niche:** [[niches/contract-packagers/import-relabelling-rework/profile|Import Relabelling, Re-marking & Rework]]
**Industry:** [[industries/contract-packagers|Contract Packagers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Customs brokerage and trade compliance software already tracks entries, holds and deadlines; the packager doing the fix needs a view into it.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #compliance #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a held or non-compliant import into marked, correctly labelled, sellable goods before Customs or the retailer's deadline runs out, and whoever clears the most held units back into compliance before liquidation or the ship date takes the account.

## The Problem
The broker knows the entry, the notice and the liquidation date. The packager knows the units, the labels and the progress. They coordinate by email.

## What Already Exists
Customs brokerage and global trade management systems that file entries, track holds and notices, and keep entry records, with trade content databases behind them (see [[niches/customs-brokers/global-trade-content-databases/profile|Global Trade Content Databases]]).

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: a shared rework case linked to the entry, visible to broker, importer and packager; a unit-level progress count; label and instruction files attached to the case; and evidence fields matched to what CBP or the retailer will accept.

## Target Customer
Customs brokers offering rework through partner packagers, and the packagers themselves.

## Impact If Solved
One case record from notice to proof, instead of an email chain three parties must reconstruct.
