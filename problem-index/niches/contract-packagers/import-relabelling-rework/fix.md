# The Re-marking Nobody Can Prove Was Supervised

**Niche:** [[niches/contract-packagers/import-relabelling-rework/profile|Import Relabelling, Re-marking & Rework]]
**Industry:** [[industries/contract-packagers|Contract Packagers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The goods were re-marked on time, but the importer cannot show it was done under Customs supervision, so the marking duty stands.
**Tags:** #cnns #object-detection #compliance #quick-win #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a held or non-compliant import into marked, correctly labelled, sellable goods before Customs or the retailer's deadline runs out, and whoever clears the most held units back into compliance before liquidation or the ship date takes the account.

## The Problem
CBP rulings hold that re-marking alone does not cancel marking duty; it must be done under Customs supervision, or accepted on a certification of marking backed by samples. Packagers do the physical work well and document it badly, with scattered photos and counts and no link to the entry.

## Why It's Still Broken
Nobody owns the evidence. The packager thinks the broker handles it; the broker assumes the packager kept records.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A standard evidence pack captured on the floor: entry number, unit counts, time-stamped photos of marked units and samples, and supervision or certification records, sent to the broker automatically when the job closes.

## Who Feels the Pain
Importers paying avoidable duty; brokers arguing cases without evidence; packagers losing repeat business.

## Impact If Fixed
Marking duty avoided on work that was done correctly but could not be proven.
