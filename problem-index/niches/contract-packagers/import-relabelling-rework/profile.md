# Import Relabelling, Re-marking & Rework

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-packagers|Contract Packagers]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a held or non-compliant import into marked, correctly labelled, sellable goods before Customs or the retailer's deadline runs out, and whoever clears the most held units back into compliance before liquidation or the ship date takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600M US receipts for relabelling, re-marking, stickering and reworking imported or non-compliant goods (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of contract packaging receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — notices arrive by email or broker portal, rework instructions are written by hand, and evidence is photos in a shared folder
**Target Buyer:** Importer's logistics or compliance manager; owner of a port-adjacent packager or 3PL rework department
**Automation Potential:** Medium-High for instructions, labels and evidence; low for the physical rework

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The work is set off by a defect someone else found. CBP examines a shipment and issues a notice to mark (Form 4647) because the goods lack a legal country-of-origin mark. FDA holds food or cosmetics over a labelling problem. A retailer rejects goods for a wrong barcode or missing warning. The importer then needs every unit fixed — stickered, relabelled, repacked — before a deadline: unmarked goods owe a 10% marking duty unless re-marked under Customs supervision before liquidation, and retail goods must still make their ship date. Tariff changes since 2025 have made origin marking and labels costlier to get wrong. Competitors are port-area packagers, 3PL value-added service departments, and customs brokers' partner networks.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Customs brokers' entry systems and the trade content they draw on (mapped under [[industries/customs-brokers|Customs Brokers]], including [[niches/customs-brokers/country-of-origin-verification/profile|Country of Origin & Forced Labour Verification]]); label printers and design software at the packager. The gaps: the notice, the rule, the rework instruction and the proof of supervised re-marking live in separate emails, and nobody tracks the liquidation clock per entry.
