# Co-Pack Software & Co-Manufacturer Matching Marketplaces

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-packagers|Contract Packagers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Software vendors built for contract packaging — Nulogy, whose Shop Floor product it says has been used at hundreds of contract packaging sites over 20+ years, plus food co-packer ERPs such as NutraSoft — and marketplaces and directories that match brands with co-manufacturers and co-packers. They sell subscriptions to co-packers and brands, or charge for matches.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analysts per firm (estimate); product, engineering, implementation and sales staff are not counted
**Output:** Software, dashboards and benchmarks inside the product; matches and leads
**Proprietary data:** Production, labour and inventory records from customers' sites, and request histories on the marketplaces
**Clock:** Customers' production schedules
**Buyer:** Chief product officer or founder
