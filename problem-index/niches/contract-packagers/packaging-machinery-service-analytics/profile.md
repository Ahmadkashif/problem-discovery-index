# Packaging Machinery Service & Line-Performance Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-packagers|Contract Packagers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The service and digital teams of packaging machinery makers — cartoners, case packers, blister and bottle lines, shrink bundlers, labellers — that monitor connected machines at customer sites, analyse stops and wear, and sell service contracts, parts and upgrades. Contract packagers are an important customer group: PMMI found 87% of contract packagers plan new equipment within three years, and 53% of them cite faster changeover. The machine builders themselves are a queued industry (industrial machinery builders) and are not profiled here.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 service data analysts and performance engineers at a large machine builder (estimate); field technicians not counted
**Output:** Line-performance reports, predictive maintenance alerts, changeover and upgrade recommendations, bundled into service contracts
**Proprietary data:** Telemetry and stop histories from connected machines across many customers' lines
**Clock:** Customers' production schedules and service-level response times
**Buyer:** Head of digital services or aftermarket at the machine builder

**Kill switches:** **data isn't theirs** — machine telemetry describes the customer's production (products, speeds, volumes, stoppages) and is collected under service agreements; for pharmaceutical customers it can also fall inside validated GMP systems.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. The analysis supports parts and service sales rather than being sold as insight, the clock is the customer's, and the cross-customer data that would make it valuable is the customers'.
