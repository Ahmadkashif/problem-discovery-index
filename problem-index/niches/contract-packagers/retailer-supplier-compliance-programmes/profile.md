# Retailer Supplier Compliance & Packaging Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-packagers|Contract Packagers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The supplier compliance, packaging engineering and supply chain analytics teams at the large retailers and warehouse clubs — Walmart, Sam's Club, Costco, Target, Amazon — that set and enforce rules on case labels, pallet builds, display construction, transit testing and delivery performance. They measure suppliers, and through them the co-packers that build displays and club packs, and charge for failures: Walmart charges ~3% of cost of goods on cases that miss its on-time, in-full rules. Retailers also require food safety certification before a co-packer is approved for food programmes.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–50 compliance and packaging analysts per large retailer (estimate)
**Output:** Supplier requirement manuals, compliance scorecards, chargebacks, packaging guidelines
**Proprietary data:** Receiving, delivery and defect records on every supplier shipment
**Clock:** Self-set — the retailers' own measurement periods and seasonal programme calendars
**Buyer:** Vice president of supplier compliance or supply chain at the retailer
