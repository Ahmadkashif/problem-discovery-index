# The Quote Nobody Checks After the Run

**Niche:** [[niches/contract-packagers/quoting-line-rate-estimating/profile|Quoting, Line-Rate Estimating & Job Costing]]
**Industry:** [[industries/contract-packagers|Contract Packagers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The job ships, the invoice goes out, and nobody compares the run with the rate the price assumed.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to predict what a new job will actually cost to run before the quote goes out, and whoever quotes closest to the run's actual labour per unit, changeover time and component scrap takes the account.

## The Problem
Once a job closes, attention moves to the next one. The quoted rate, crew and scrap allowance are never compared with what happened, so the same formats are underquoted year after year.

## Why It's Still Broken
The comparison takes an afternoon of matching spreadsheets to labour sheets, and nobody's job description includes it.

## What a Fix Looks Like
An automatic job-close report: quoted versus actual rate, ramp, changeover, scrap and margin, with the variance attributed to a cause, plus a running league table of formats the firm underquotes.

## Who Feels the Pain
Owners who cannot see where margin goes; estimators who never learn how their quotes turned out.

## Impact If Fixed
Underquoted formats are found after one or two jobs instead of years. If the firm's rate error on its worst formats falls from ~15% to ~5%, that recovers ~2–4 points of margin on those jobs (estimate). The estimator also sees how each quote turned out.
