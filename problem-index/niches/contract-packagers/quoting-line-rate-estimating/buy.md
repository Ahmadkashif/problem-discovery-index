# Job Costing Software With a Quote-Versus-Actual Ledger

**Niche:** [[niches/contract-packagers/quoting-line-rate-estimating/profile|Quoting, Line-Rate Estimating & Job Costing]]
**Industry:** [[industries/contract-packagers|Contract Packagers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Job-shop costing software already compares estimated and actual hours; a co-packer needs it in units per labour-hour, with ramp, changeover and client-owned scrap.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #linear-regression #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to predict what a new job will actually cost to run before the quote goes out, and whoever quotes closest to the run's actual labour per unit, changeover time and component scrap takes the account.

## The Problem
Co-packers seldom know their margin by job, because the quote and the actual run are never put side by side.

## What Already Exists
Job-shop and contract manufacturing ERP modules that estimate jobs, collect labour and material actuals, and report variance, built for machine shops and fabricators (estimate).

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: estimates expressed as units per labour-hour with a ramp curve for new crews; changeover and line clearance as separate cost lines; scrap of client-owned components tracked against the client's allowance; variance broken down by cause (rate, ramp, disruption, component); and quote templates that put the assumptions and a throughput band in front of the client.

## Target Customer
General managers and controllers at mid-sized co-packers.

## Impact If Solved
Margin known by job and format, and prices adjusted on evidence at the next quote.
