# Packaging & Contract Packaging Market Research Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-packagers|Contract Packagers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Research publishers that size and forecast packaging and outsourced packaging markets, sold as reports, subscriptions and custom studies to packaging suppliers, machinery makers, private equity and brand owners. Syndicated publishers such as Mordor Intelligence and Towards Packaging sell contract packaging market studies, and packaging-specialist research houses such as Smithers cover formats and materials (Smithers' packaging coverage recalled, not checked). Their headline numbers disagree openly: Mordor puts the global contract packaging market at ~$79.7B in 2025, Towards Packaging at ~$96.9B, and Towards Packaging's own page also gives a second 2025 figure.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 packaging analysts at the largest publisher (estimate); most cover packaging as one sector among many
**Output:** Market sizing and forecast reports, format and material trend studies, custom research
**Proprietary data:** Interview programmes and survey panels with packaging buyers and suppliers, and long-running market model series
**Clock:** Self-set publication cycles; clients' strategy and investment calendars
**Buyer:** Head of packaging research or managing director

**Kill switches:** none — the models and interview programmes are the publishers' own.
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. The report is the invoice, but output is a modest number of reports per analyst rather than a high-volume repeatable product, no third party sets the deadline, and a few firms do the work. Their published market sizes disagree by tens of billions of dollars with no record of which was right, which is the unmeasured-accuracy gap the vault's indexed data vendors share, at a scale too small to qualify.
