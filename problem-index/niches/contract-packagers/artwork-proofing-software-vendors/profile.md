# Artwork, Label Proofing & Print Inspection Software Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-packagers|Contract Packagers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors of artwork management, proofing and print-comparison software — GlobalVision and Esko among them — used by brand owners, printers, packaging artwork studios and some packagers to check that printed labels and cartons match approved files. They sell licences and subscriptions. Packaging artwork work itself is mapped under [[industries/graphic-design-studios|Graphic Design Studios]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analysts per firm (estimate); engineering and support staff are not counted
**Output:** Comparison and inspection software, artwork workflow tools
**Proprietary data:** None beyond product usage; artwork belongs to the brands
**Clock:** Customers' launch and print schedules
**Buyer:** Chief product officer
