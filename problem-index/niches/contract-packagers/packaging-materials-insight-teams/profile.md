# Packaging Materials Suppliers' Market & Application Insight Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-packagers|Contract Packagers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The market insight, application development and design centres of large packaging materials suppliers — film, blister materials, corrugate and displays, closures — which study end markets, test new formats with brand owners and design displays and packs that co-packers then build. The insight supports the sale of materials and is not usually billed on its own. The material makers are queued industries in their own right (paperboard packaging makers, plastic film and bag makers) and are not profiled here.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 market and consumer insight analysts at a large supplier (estimate); structural designers and application engineers are production and not counted
**Output:** Market and trend analysis, format trials, display and pack designs supplied with material orders
**Proprietary data:** Order histories, trial results and design libraries across many brand customers
**Clock:** Brand owners' launch and reset calendars
**Buyer:** Head of market insight or innovation at the supplier
