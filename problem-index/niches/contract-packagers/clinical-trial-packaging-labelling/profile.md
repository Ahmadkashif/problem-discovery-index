# Clinical Trial Packaging & Labelling

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-packagers|Contract Packagers]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a protocol and its amendments into blinded, correctly labelled patient kits at sites before they are needed, and whoever goes from approved label text to released kits in the fewest days takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.1B US spend on clinical trial packaging, labelling and expiry relabelling services (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of contract packaging receipts, counting packagers classified elsewhere (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — randomisation and supply systems are digital; label text, translations and expiry updates move by email and document review
**Target Buyer:** Head of clinical supply at the packaging organisation; clinical supply manager at the sponsor
**Automation Potential:** Medium-High for label text and kit design, low for release

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Clinical packaging is low-volume, high-variety work: blinding active and placebo into identical kits, multi-language booklet labels for each country in a trial, kits built to a randomisation scheme, and relabelling stock in the field when the expiry is extended on new stability data. Speed matters more than unit cost. A protocol amendment, a new country or a dose change sends the packager back to label text, translation, approval and a new packaging run. Competitors are the clinical supply divisions of the large platforms (Almac, Thermo Fisher's clinical services, PCI, Sharp, Catalent) and sponsors' in-house clinical supply units.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Interactive response technology (IRT) systems for randomisation and site supply; label text management and translation tools; document control in QMS. The gaps: label text assembled country by country from templates and phrase libraries by hand, expiry relabelling planned by spreadsheet, and kit design changes re-checked from scratch for each amendment.
