# CPG External Manufacturing & Co-Packer Management Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-packagers|Contract Packagers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The external manufacturing, co-packer management and supplier quality groups inside large consumer-goods brand owners, which select, audit, price and schedule co-packers and contract manufacturers. PMMI found 67% of brand owners plan to keep or increase their use of contract services and 11% use a contract provider as their entire production operation, so these teams are the industry's main buyers. Their analysis decides which co-packer gets which programme at what price.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 external manufacturing analysts and supplier quality staff at the largest brand owners (estimate); far fewer at mid-sized brands
**Output:** Co-packer selection and scorecards, should-cost and price benchmarks, capacity plans, audit findings
**Proprietary data:** Quotes, actual costs and quality records across the brand's co-packer network
**Clock:** The brand's own launch, promotion and budget calendar
**Buyer:** Director of external manufacturing at the brand owner

**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. These teams hold the best cross-co-packer view of quoting accuracy, but their analysis is a procurement cost centre, its clock is internal, and only the largest brands staff the function at all.
