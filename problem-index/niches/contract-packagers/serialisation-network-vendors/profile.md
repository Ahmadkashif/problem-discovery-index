# Serialisation & Traceability Network Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-packagers|Contract Packagers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors of line-level serialisation systems (Antares Vision, Systech, Optel) and of the networks that carry serial and transaction data between manufacturers, contract packagers, wholesalers and dispensers (TraceLink, SAP's advanced track and trace). TraceLink reported in November 2025 that 279,800 companies, including more than 124,000 dispensers, could access DSCSA transaction data on its network. Drug sponsors and packagers pay licence and subscription fees. Regulatory teams track serialisation rules country by country.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 regulatory intelligence and network data analysts at the larger vendors (estimate); engineers and implementation staff not counted
**Output:** Compliant serialisation software and network connections; country requirement updates; network-level readiness and exception reporting for clients
**Proprietary data:** Serialisation and transaction events flowing across the network and the vendor's country-requirements library
**Clock:** One-time regulatory go-live dates on the clients' compliance, now mostly past — FDA's DSCSA exemption for manufacturers and repackagers ended 27 May 2025, for wholesale distributors 27 August 2025 and for larger dispensers 27 November 2025 — plus comparable dates abroad; day to day, the clients' shipping schedules
**Buyer:** Chief product officer or head of regulatory intelligence at the vendor

**Kill switches:** **data isn't theirs** — serial numbers and transaction records belong to the trading partners exchanging them, and network agreements restrict their use; plus **FDA computer-system validation** on systems used in GMP release.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. The network holds the most complete record of where drug units move, but the vendors sell compliance software, not analysis, a handful of firms dominate, and the event data is their customers'.
