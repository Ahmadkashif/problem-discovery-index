# Contract Packaging & Packaging Trade Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-packagers|Contract Packagers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Contract Packaging Association, managed by PMMI since 2018 and merged with the Foundation for Supply Chain Solutions in 2025, and PMMI's business intelligence unit, which publishes outsourcing and packaging machinery studies (its October 2024 contract packaging and manufacturing report and an annual state-of-the-industry report). Packaging professional and sector groups sit alongside them. Members pay dues; reports are a member benefit or sold to non-members.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 dedicated research staff across these bodies (estimate); CPA itself has no research staff of its own beyond PMMI's support
**Output:** Member surveys, outsourcing trend reports, machinery market data, directories
**Proprietary data:** Member surveys and PMMI's machinery shipment series
**Clock:** Self-set annual reports and trade-show calendars
**Buyer:** Association executive director; PMMI vice president of business intelligence
