# Food Repacking & Multipack Assembly

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-packagers|Contract Packagers]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to run many brands' foods through one set of lines with allergen and label control that a GFSI auditor and a retailer will accept, and whoever passes audits and changeovers with no undeclared-allergen hold takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2B US receipts for repacking bulk food into retail units, multipacks and variety packs, and banding or bundling finished food packs (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15–20% of contract packaging receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — HACCP and allergen changeover records, label checks and SQF audit evidence are mostly paper binders
**Target Buyer:** Owner or food safety manager at a food co-packer; quality lead at the brand
**Automation Potential:** Medium — the documentation and label checks automate; sanitation and hand-packing do not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This niche packs food without making it: repacking nuts, candy, coffee or snacks from bulk into retail bags, assembling variety packs from several brands' finished goods, and bundling multipacks. Processing that changes the product is manufacturing, and is mapped under [[industries/food-manufacturing|Food Manufacturing]]. Because packing and holding food are FDA-regulated activities, these sites register with FDA and keep food safety plans (estimate). Retailers want GFSI certification such as SQF before they approve a co-packer. The defining risk is the label: with many brands' products on shared lines, the wrong film, label or product in a pack is an undeclared allergen. Allergens drove 27.1% of FDA product recall records over FY2013–FY2025.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Food safety and HACCP software; GFSI audit schemes and certification bodies (mapped as [[niches/restaurant-suppliers/food-safety-auditing-certification/profile|Food Safety Auditing & Certification Bodies]]); food ERPs such as NutraSoft and Deacom. The gaps: allergen changeover verification signed on paper, label checks by eye, and audit preparation done by hand from binders.
