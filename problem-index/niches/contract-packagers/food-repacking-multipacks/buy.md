# Food Safety Software for Packers That Hold Other People's Food

**Niche:** [[niches/contract-packagers/food-repacking-multipacks/profile|Food Repacking & Multipack Assembly]]
**Industry:** [[industries/contract-packagers|Contract Packagers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Food safety management software is built for manufacturers with their own recipes; a repacker needs it organised around many brands' finished foods and their labels.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #compliance #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to run many brands' foods through one set of lines with allergen and label control that a GFSI auditor and a retailer will accept, and whoever passes audits and changeovers with no undeclared-allergen hold takes the account.

## The Problem
A repacker's food safety plan covers receiving, storage, packing and labelling of products it did not make, under each brand's specifications and allergen statements, and it is audited against a GFSI scheme and by brands' own auditors.

## What Already Exists
Food safety and quality management software with HACCP plans, monitoring checklists, supplier approval, corrective actions and audit preparation, sold mainly to food manufacturers (estimate).

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: brand-by-brand product records with each brand's allergen statement and approved label; receiving checks against the brand's specification; changeover verification tied to the allergen profiles of the two jobs; label-version checks at the line; and audit evidence packaged per brand as well as per scheme.

## Target Customer
Food safety managers at repackers and variety-pack co-packers.

## Impact If Solved
Audit preparation in days rather than weeks, and brand audits answered from records instead of binders.
