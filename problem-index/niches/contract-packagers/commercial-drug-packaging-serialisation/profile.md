# Commercial Drug Packaging & Serialisation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-packagers|Contract Packagers]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a sponsor's bulk batch into serialised, QA-released commercial packs without a labelling deviation, and whoever releases the most lots right first time at the lowest cost per serialised pack takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.4B US spend on outsourced commercial packaging of drugs, generics, OTC products and supplements (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~20% of contract packaging receipts, counting packagers classified elsewhere (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High at the line (serialisation, vision), Medium in release and exception handling
**Target Buyer:** VP of Operations at a contract packaging organisation; external manufacturing lead at a generic or specialty pharma sponsor
**Automation Potential:** High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Commercial work is volume work: blister and bottle lines running the same products for years, under contracts priced per pack. Since FDA's DSCSA exemption for manufacturers and repackagers ended on 27 May 2025, every saleable unit carries a serialised identifier, cases and pallets are aggregated, and the data must reach the sponsor and its trading partners without errors. The packager wins on throughput, first-pass release and cost per pack. Its competitors are the sponsors' own packaging lines and the large platforms: PCI Pharma Services, Sharp, Tjoapack (being acquired by Alcami) and others. Its clock is the sponsor's supply plan and the wholesalers' order cycle.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Line serialisation (Antares Vision, Systech, Optel), networks such as TraceLink (~280,000 companies with DSCSA data access), checkweighers and vision. The gaps: serialisation exceptions such as aggregation breaks, rework and sample pulls are worked by hand; line OEE is measured but not tied to the cost per pack quoted; and release still waits on manual review.
