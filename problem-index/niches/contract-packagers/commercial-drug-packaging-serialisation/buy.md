# Line Performance Monitoring Tied to Cost per Pack

**Niche:** [[niches/contract-packagers/commercial-drug-packaging-serialisation/profile|Commercial Drug Packaging & Serialisation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/contract-packagers|Contract Packagers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Overall equipment effectiveness software measures uptime well; a contract packager needs it to say which sponsor, product and pack format is losing money against the quoted price.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #descriptive-statistics #feature-engineering #revenue-impact #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a sponsor's bulk batch into serialised, QA-released commercial packs without a labelling deviation, and whoever releases the most lots right first time at the lowest cost per serialised pack takes the account.

## The Problem
Commercial packaging contracts are priced per pack, on assumed speeds, changeover times and yields. Lines report stops and speed, but the numbers are seldom joined to the contract, so the packager learns a product is unprofitable at the annual price review, if at all.

## What Already Exists
OEE and line-monitoring software that captures machine states, stop reasons, speed and quality losses, and is widely sold to packaging lines (estimate).

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: OEE attributed by sponsor, product, pack format and lot; changeover and clearance time measured separately, since cGMP clearance is a fixed cost per run; serialisation rejects and data holds counted as losses; and a cost-per-pack view that compares actual against the contract assumptions, ready for the sponsor's price review.

## Target Customer
Operations and finance leads at commercial pharmaceutical packagers.

## Impact If Solved
Contract prices set on measured performance, products that lose money identified within weeks, and a factual basis for renegotiating with sponsors.
