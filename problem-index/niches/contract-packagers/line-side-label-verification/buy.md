# Artwork Comparison Moved From the Studio to the Dock

**Niche:** [[niches/contract-packagers/line-side-label-verification/profile|Line-Side Label Verification, Coding & Inspection]]
**Industry:** [[industries/contract-packagers|Contract Packagers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Artwork comparison software already catches a one-character change between proof and file; the co-packer needs it at receiving, against the brand's approved version for the job.
**Tags:** #cnns #transformers #evaluation-metrics #compliance #automation #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to catch every wrong label, missing lot code and unreadable barcode at full line speed, and whoever reaches zero escapes with the fewest false rejects stopping the line takes the account.

## The Problem
Printed labels and cartons arrive from the brand's printers. The co-packer must confirm each lot is the approved version before it reaches a line, and usually does so by holding a printed sample against a proof.

## What Already Exists
Artwork and print inspection software (GlobalVision, Esko) used by brands, printers and packaging artwork studios (see [[industries/graphic-design-studios|Graphic Design Studios]]) to compare files and printed samples, including text, barcodes and colour.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: a receiving workflow keyed to the brand's purchase order and job; retrieval of the approved artwork version from the brand rather than the co-packer's own files; scanning with a phone or flatbed rather than a press-side system; and the result recorded on the job for the brand's auditor.

## Target Customer
Receiving and QA leads at co-packers handling printed components for many brands.

## Impact If Solved
Wrong-version components stopped at the dock instead of found after the run.
