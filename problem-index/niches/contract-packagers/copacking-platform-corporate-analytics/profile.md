# Contract Packaging Platform Estimating & Corporate Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-packagers|Contract Packagers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The estimating, pricing and corporate development functions of the private-equity-backed and acquisitive packaging platforms: consumer co-packers such as MSI Express (bought by Nonantum Capital from HCI Equity Partners in 2025) and display and value-added packaging groups, and on the pharmaceutical side platforms such as PCI Pharma Services (Bain Capital investment, July 2025) and Tjoapack (being acquired by Alcami). They price programmes across sites, integrate acquisitions and benchmark site performance.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 estimating and pricing analysts per consumer platform (estimate); the pharmaceutical platforms' larger finance teams serve a CDMO business, not packaging insight
**Output:** Programme quotes, site benchmarks, acquisition models — all internal
**Proprietary data:** Quote and job-cost histories across the platform's sites
**Clock:** Brand owners' RFQ dates
**Buyer:** Chief operating officer or chief financial officer
