# Small-Manufacturer ERP With Client-Owned Stock

**Niche:** [[niches/contract-packagers/small-independent-copackers/profile|Small Independent Contract Packagers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/contract-packagers|Contract Packagers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Cloud ERPs for small manufacturers are cheap and quick to start, but they assume the manufacturer owns its stock and makes its own products.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to become the first system a ten-person co-packer runs its jobs on, and whoever gets client-owned inventory, job labour and lot records off the whiteboard in a week, without an ERP project, takes the account.

## The Problem
Small packagers that try a small-manufacturer ERP find it wants item masters, bills of materials and purchasing for goods they never buy, and has no clean way to hold each client's stock apart.

## What Already Exists
Cloud MRP and ERP tools for small manufacturers (MRPeasy, Business Central add-ons and similar) with inventory, work orders and basic traceability; some support issuing components to subcontractors.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: client-owned inventory pools by brand and purchase order; jobs created from a client's spec rather than an internal product; per-job labour capture; component reconciliation reports in the client's format; and pricing per unit packed rather than per unit sold.

## Target Customer
Owners of small co-packers outgrowing spreadsheets.

## Impact If Solved
A system priced at ~$200–500 a month and live in about a week (estimate), with client stock, job labour and reconciliations kept as a co-packer actually works. It saves the owner ~5–10 hours a week of spreadsheet reconciliation and lot tracing (estimate), instead of a manufacturing ERP bent around assumptions that do not fit.
