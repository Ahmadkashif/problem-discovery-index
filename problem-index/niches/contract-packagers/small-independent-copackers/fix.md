# The Lot Trace Rebuilt From Paper When a Brand Calls

**Niche:** [[niches/contract-packagers/small-independent-copackers/profile|Small Independent Contract Packagers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/contract-packagers|Contract Packagers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A brand asks which of its lots went into which shipped packs, and the owner spends a day digging through job tickets and bills of lading.
**Tags:** #cnns #descriptive-statistics #compliance #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to become the first system a ten-person co-packer runs its jobs on, and whoever gets client-owned inventory, job labour and lot records off the whiteboard in a week, without an ERP project, takes the account.

## The Problem
Brands running a mock recall or a real one need to know which incoming lots went into which outgoing packs and shipments. Small packagers rarely record lot codes at the line, so the trace is rebuilt from paper, partially.

## Why It's Still Broken
Writing lot codes down by hand is slow and error-prone, and nobody asks until there is a problem.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Phone photos of the incoming lot labels and of the finished case labels, read automatically and tied to the job, so a trace is a search, not a project.

## Who Feels the Pain
Owners answering brand audits; brands running recalls through small partners.

## Impact If Fixed
Mock recalls passed in minutes, and small packagers kept on approved-supplier lists that increasingly require it.
