# Packaging EPR Compliance Data & Reporting Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-packagers|Contract Packagers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Software and service firms that help brand owners meet state extended producer responsibility (EPR) laws for packaging: tracking each jurisdiction's rules, classifying each SKU's packaging components by material, weight and recyclability, calculating fees under each state's schedule, and preparing the annual supply reports producers file with the producer responsibility organisation (the PROs themselves are logged at [[niches/waste-haulers/packaging-epr-pros/profile|🔍 Packaging EPR Producer Responsibility Organisations]]). Lorax EPI, formed in 2019 from Lorax Compliance and Environmental Packaging International, sells a packaging regulatory matrix subscription with monthly alerts, obligation assessments, fee modelling and its ENVI reporting software, and has partnered with Assent on supplier data collection. Trayak and Certivo publish state-by-state guides alongside reporting software. Brand owners pay. Contract packagers are the source of much of the component data, because the trays, film, display corrugate and inserts they add become covered materials in the brand's report.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–25 regulatory analysts and consultants at the largest specialist, Lorax EPI (estimate; team size not published); the staff who classify SKUs and assemble reports are production and not counted
**Output:** Regulatory matrices and alerts, obligation assessments, fee models, and supply reports prepared for producers to file
**Proprietary data:** A multi-jurisdiction library of packaging EPR rules and a mapping of materials and formats to each state's covered-material categories and fee rates; the clients' SKU-level packaging data is held under service agreements
**Clock:** Annual supply reporting dates set by the producer responsibility organisation under state law, and extended once: Circular Action Alliance gave Oregon producers a grace period from 31 March to 30 April 2025 on the first cycle, and lists 31 May 2026 for 2025 reports in Oregon, Colorado and California
**Buyer:** Head of regulatory services or managing director at the vendor

**Kill switches:** **data isn't theirs** — the SKU-level packaging specifications, unit volumes and sales by state are each client's data, supplied under service agreements for that client's filings, and no checked terms give the vendor rights to use it in aggregate.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. The rules library and regulatory alerts are the vendors' own and sold directly, and a regulator-backed annual reporting date governs the work, but the date belongs to the producer's filing and was extended on the first cycle. The corpus that would make the work distinctive, packaging data across many brands' SKUs, belongs to the clients.
