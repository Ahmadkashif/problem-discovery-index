# Pharmaceutical MES Scaled Down for Packaging-Only Sites

**Niche:** [[niches/contract-packagers/pharmaceutical-contract-packaging/profile|Pharmaceutical Contract Packaging]]
**Industry:** [[industries/contract-packagers|Contract Packagers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Manufacturing execution systems already enforce electronic batch records in drug plants; packaging-only sites need the packaging steps without the price and validation load of a full plant system.

Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Drug manufacturers use MES and electronic batch records to enforce each step and capture signatures. Mid-sized packagers serving many sponsors still run paper or hybrid batch records. Every sponsor brings its own master batch record format, so a packager can carry dozens of templates.

## What Already Exists
Pharmaceutical MES and electronic batch record platforms built for formulation and fill-finish plants, with configurable workflows, signatures under 21 CFR Part 11 and integration to ERP and QMS (estimate).

## The Customization Gap
A packaging-only site needs: packaging step libraries (clearance, labelling issue, in-process checks, reconciliation, serialisation hand-off) rather than process recipes; quick creation of a new sponsor's batch record from that sponsor's template; client-owned material handling; integration with line serialisation and vision systems from several vendors; and a validation package priced for a site with a few lines, not a plant.

## Target Customer
Operations and quality heads at pharmaceutical and supplement packagers with 5–30 lines (estimate).

## Impact If Solved
Paper batch records retire without a plant-scale project, and onboarding a new sponsor's product takes days rather than weeks of template work.
