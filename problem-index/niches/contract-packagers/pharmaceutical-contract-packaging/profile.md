# Pharmaceutical Contract Packaging

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-packagers|Contract Packagers]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to release packaged drug product with no label mix-up and a batch record QA can sign, but commercial and clinical packaging reward different things: commercial lines are won on cost per serialised, released pack at volume, and clinical lines on how few days it takes to turn final label text and a randomisation scheme into blinded kits at sites. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3.5B US spend on outsourced drug and supplement packaging and labelling (estimate), much of it in establishments Census files under drug manufacturing rather than 561910
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~25–30% of contract packaging receipts, counting packagers classified elsewhere (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium-High — serialisation, vision inspection and electronic batch records are standard at the large packagers; smaller ones still run paper batch records
**Target Buyer:** VP of Quality or VP of Operations at a contract packaging organisation; external supply lead at the drug sponsor
**Automation Potential:** High for verification and documentation, low for QA release decisions

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Under FDA's cGMP rules, packaging and labelling a drug count as part of manufacturing. The packager inspects and clears the line before every run and records it in the batch record (21 CFR 211.130), reconciles every label issued against labels used and returned, and works under a quality agreement with the sponsor. Since FDA's DSCSA exemption for manufacturers and repackagers ended on 27 May 2025, each saleable unit also carries a serial number tied to an electronic record shared with trading partners. The firms are consolidating into large platforms. PCI Pharma Services took a Bain Capital investment in July 2025 at a reported ~$10B valuation, and Alcami agreed in April 2026 to buy Tjoapack (~400 staff, 23 lines in Tennessee and the Netherlands).

## Current Tools & Gaps
Line serialisation from Antares Vision, Systech and Optel; networks such as TraceLink; vision inspection; electronic batch record and QMS software. The gaps: line clearance and label reconciliation are still paper-heavy at mid-sized packagers, serialisation exceptions are worked by hand, and clinical label text and expiry updates run on email and spreadsheets.
