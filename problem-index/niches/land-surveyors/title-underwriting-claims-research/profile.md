# Title Underwriting & Claims Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/land-surveyors|Land Surveyors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Title insurance underwriters set the underwriting rules agents apply, decide which risks to insure over and which to except, and analyze the claims that result — including the boundary and encroachment claims a survey is meant to prevent.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 underwriting counsel, claims analysts, and actuaries
**Output:** Underwriting guidelines and bulletins, exception standards, claims analysis, reserve setting
**Proprietary data:** Claim experience by defect type, jurisdiction, and transaction characteristic — the empirical record of what actually goes wrong with property titles
**Clock:** Transaction volume and statutory reserve reporting
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
