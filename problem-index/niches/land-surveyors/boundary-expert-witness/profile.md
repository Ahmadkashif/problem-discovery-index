# Boundary Retracement Expert Witnesses

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/land-surveyors|Land Surveyors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Senior surveyors act as expert witnesses in boundary disputes, retracing original surveys from deeds, monuments, and historical evidence, and testifying to where a line legally falls. Pass 1's workforce numbers make this the scarcest expertise in the profession — the practitioners are exactly the cohort retiring.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1-5 people, usually one licensed surveyor
**Output:** Retracement analyses, expert reports, testimony
**Proprietary data:** A career of accumulated judgment about original survey practice, monument evidence, and local recording conventions — entirely undocumented
**Clock:** Litigation schedules
**Buyer:** The expert
