# Surveyor Licensing Boards

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/land-surveyors|Land Surveyors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State boards license professional land surveyors, administer the examinations, and discipline licensees — the gatekeeper on a profession Pass 1 says is losing licensees to retirement twice as fast as it gains them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-30 licensing analysts and investigators per state
**Output:** Licensing decisions, examination administration, complaint findings, disciplinary actions
**Proprietary data:** Licensee demographics and pipeline data, plus complaint and discipline records
**Clock:** Examination and renewal cycles
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer
