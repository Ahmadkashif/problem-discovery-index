# Parcel & Boundary Data Aggregators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/land-surveyors|Land Surveyors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Parcel data companies assemble county assessor and recorder parcel geometry and attributes into national coverage, normalizing thousands of jurisdictions that each publish differently or not at all.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-200 GIS analysts, data engineers, and county researchers
**Output:** National parcel geometry and attributes, ownership and zoning attribution, boundary data products
**Proprietary data:** Assembled and normalized coverage over records that are public county by county and have never been unified by anyone else
**Clock:** County update cycles and customer refresh commitments
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Head of Product
