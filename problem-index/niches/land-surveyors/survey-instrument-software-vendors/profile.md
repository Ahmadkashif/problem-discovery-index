# Survey Instrument & Software Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/land-surveyors|Land Surveyors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The instrument and field software vendors supply the total stations, GNSS receivers, scanners, and data collection software surveying crews work with, and their engineering teams define how field data is captured and processed.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 engineers, geodesists, and software developers
**Output:** Instruments and firmware, field and office software, processing algorithms, workflow design
**Proprietary data:** Measurement and processing algorithms; field observation data belongs to the surveying firms
**Clock:** Product release cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Engineering / Director of Product
