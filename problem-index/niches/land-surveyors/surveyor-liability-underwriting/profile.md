# Surveyor Professional Liability Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/land-surveyors|Land Surveyors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Specialty programmes underwrite professional liability for surveying and engineering firms, pricing the risk that a boundary determination or an ALTA survey turns out to be wrong.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 underwriters, claims analysts, and risk managers
**Output:** Rating and eligibility decisions, claims analysis, loss prevention guidance
**Proprietary data:** Claims by survey type, firm characteristic, and error mode — a direct record of how surveys go wrong
**Clock:** Annual renewal
**Buyer:** Programme manager
