# Surveying Association Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/land-surveyors|Land Surveyors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The surveying associations write the standards commercial surveys are performed to, publish workforce and practice research, and advocate on licensure and scope-of-practice questions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-25 standards and policy staff
**Output:** Survey standards, practice guidance, workforce studies, model legislation
**Proprietary data:** Standards development history and member survey series
**Clock:** Standard revision cycles and legislative sessions
**Buyer:** Executive director — member funded
