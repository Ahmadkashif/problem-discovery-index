# Title Plant & Search Operations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/land-surveyors|Land Surveyors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Title plants are geographically indexed reconstructions of the entire recorded property history of a county — deeds, mortgages, liens, judgments, easements, and plats, organized by parcel rather than by grantor name. Search and examination operations run against them to produce the title commitment that determines whether a property can be sold or financed. Pass 1 records the surveyor's place in the same transaction: no title can be insured without a survey.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-3,000 title searchers, examiners, plant maintenance staff, and data engineers
**Output:** Title searches, commitments and exception schedules, curative recommendations, underwriting determinations
**Proprietary data:** Plants built and maintained county by county over a century, indexing records that are public individually and have never been assembled this way by anyone else — plus the examination decisions and claim outcomes that followed
**Clock:** Closing dates, which do not move
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / SVP of Title Operations
