# Right-of-Way & Land Acquisition Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/land-surveyors|Land Surveyors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Right-of-way firms research ownership, value the property interests, and negotiate the acquisitions that infrastructure and utility projects require, producing the appraisals and offer packages that must withstand condemnation proceedings.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-800 agents, appraisers, title researchers, and analysts
**Output:** Ownership and encumbrance research, valuations, offer packages, relocation assistance, condemnation support
**Proprietary data:** Acquisition outcomes — offers made, settlements reached, cases condemned — across many corridors and jurisdictions
**Clock:** Project schedules with funding deadlines and statutory notice periods
**Buyer:** National practice leader
