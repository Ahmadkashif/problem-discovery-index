# County Recorder & Surveyor Offices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/land-surveyors|Land Surveyors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
County recorders maintain the official record of every deed, plat, and encumbrance, and county surveyor offices review and accept the plats and records of survey that establish boundaries. Nothing in this industry is authoritative until it is recorded.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 recording clerks, examiners, and county survey staff per jurisdiction
**Output:** Recording and indexing, plat review and acceptance, monument records, corner restoration
**Proprietary data:** The authoritative recorded chain for every parcel in the county, plus survey monument records
**Clock:** Statutory recording and review timelines
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — county government
