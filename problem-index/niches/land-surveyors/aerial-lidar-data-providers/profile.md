# Aerial & LiDAR Data Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/land-surveyors|Land Surveyors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Aerial survey firms fly imagery and LiDAR at scale and deliver the point clouds, orthophotos, and derived terrain products surveying and engineering firms build on, replacing field time that Pass 1 identifies as the profession's dominant cost driver.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-400 photogrammetrists, geospatial analysts, and processing engineers
**Output:** Point clouds, classified terrain models, orthoimagery, planimetric feature extraction, accuracy reporting
**Proprietary data:** Acquisition archives and the classification and extraction models built over them
**Clock:** Flying seasons, project schedules, and post-disaster response
**Buyer:** VP of Geospatial Production / Chief Technology Officer
