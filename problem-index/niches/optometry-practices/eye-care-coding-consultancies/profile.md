# Eye-Care Coding, Reimbursement & Compliance Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/optometry-practices|Optometry Practices]]

## What They Do
Consultancies that teach and audit eye-care coding: routine versus medical billing, diagnostic test documentation, modifiers and Medicare rules. Corcoran Consulting Group serves mostly ophthalmology. Optometry relies on individual coding consultants, AOA coding resources and EHR vendors' trainers (recalled). Practices pay for audits, seminars and coding guides. General coding audit and content are already profiled at [[niches/medical-billing/coding-audit-compliance-firms/profile|🔍 Coding Audit & Compliance Firms]] (46, kill switch) and [[niches/medical-billing/medical-coding-content-publishers/profile|🔍 Medical Coding Content Publishers]] (54, indexed).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 consulting staff per eye-care firm once per-chart auditors, who are production, are excluded (estimate)
**Output:** Chart audits, coding guides, seminars, audit defence
**Proprietary data:** Error patterns from audited eye-care charts
**Clock:** Payer audit response deadlines (the client's clock) and annual code updates
**Buyer:** Practice owner or administrator
