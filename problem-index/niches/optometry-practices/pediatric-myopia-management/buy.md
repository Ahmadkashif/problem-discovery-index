# Biometer Software Adapted to Multi-Product Programmes

**Niche:** [[niches/optometry-practices/pediatric-myopia-management/profile|Pediatric Myopia Management Programmes]]
**Industry:** [[industries/optometry-practices|Optometry Practices]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Biometers already plot a child's axial length on growth curves; adding treatment-specific expected curves and programme-level reporting would turn them into practice management tools.
**Tags:** #gaussian-processes #bayesian-inference #data-integration #worker-facing #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to slow eye growth in children enrolled in myopia control, and whoever shows the lowest measured axial elongation per child per year across a practice's programme takes the account.

## The Problem
Biometer software compares a child with population growth centiles, but not with the expected growth for the treatment they are on. Programme-level results are invisible.

## What Already Exists
Topcon Myah, Oculus Myopia Master and Zeiss software plot axial length centiles and export reports for families.

## The Customization Gap
The software needs expected curves per treatment (MiSight, Stellest, orthokeratology, combinations), drawn from published trials and updated with the practice's own data. It also needs a treatment-change log and a programme dashboard across all enrolled children.

## Target Customer
Biometer makers and their optometry channels, or a software add-on sold to practices that already own the devices.

## Impact If Solved
Saving ~5 minutes per myopia visit on manual charting and family explanation, across ~400 visits a year, frees ~33 hours of OD time (estimate).
