# Vision Benefit Billing Adapted to Each State's Medicaid Rules

**Niche:** [[niches/optometry-practices/medicaid-childrens-vision/profile|Medicaid Children's Vision & School-Screening Follow-Up]]
**Industry:** [[industries/optometry-practices|Optometry Practices]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Optometry EHRs bill commercial vision plans routinely; adding each state's Medicaid and CHIP vision rules and administrators would make children's Medicaid care workable for more practices.
**Tags:** #decision-trees #automation #compliance #data-integration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get children who fail a vision screening through a full eye exam, and whoever completes exams for the highest share of referred children takes the account.

## The Problem
Medicaid children's vision benefits differ by state and managed-care plan. Exam frequency, which frames are covered, prior approval for some lenses and the vendor that administers it all vary. Practices that try Medicaid often stop because claims are denied for rules they did not know.

## What Already Exists
EHR claim builders, vision-plan portals and state Medicaid portals; Medicaid vision administrators' own provider manuals.

## The Customization Gap
A rules library per state and plan for children's exams and eyewear, eligibility checks against the right administrator, and frame-and-lens selection limited to what the plan covers before the child leaves the chair.

## Target Customer
EHR vendors and billing services serving practices that accept Medicaid.

## Impact If Solved
Cutting first-pass denials on Medicaid children's claims by half at a practice submitting ~800 a year would avoid ~100 reworked claims (estimate), enough to keep some practices accepting Medicaid.
