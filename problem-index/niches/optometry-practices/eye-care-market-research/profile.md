# Eye-Care Market Research & Association Statistics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/optometry-practices|Optometry Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
The Vision Council's research programme (the VisionWatch consumer panel and member market reports), Jobson's optical research and Vision Monday rankings, and the American Optometric Association's research and health policy staff publish eye exam volumes, eyewear spending, channel shares and optometrist workforce figures (recalled). Manufacturers, retailers, investors and the associations' own members pay through subscriptions, membership or advertising. Most of the eyewear side belongs to the queued optical retail industry; the exam and workforce side is this industry's.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research staff at each organisation (estimate); panel fieldwork is bought from survey vendors
**Output:** Consumer panel reports, channel and market sizing, workforce and practice surveys
**Proprietary data:** A long-running consumer panel on eyewear and exams; member-reported sales and survey responses
**Clock:** Each organisation's own release calendar
**Buyer:** Research director at the association or publisher
