# Diabetic Eye Exam Report-Back & Plan Gap Credit

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/optometry-practices|Optometry Practices]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get each diabetic eye exam the practice performs counted by the patient's health plan, and whoever gets the highest share of completed exams credited in the plan's eye-exam measure takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$150M a year of staff time on result letters, codes and plan gap lists, plus gap-closure programmes plans run with eye practices (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1% of practice spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — letters are faxed, CPT II codes are often missing, and gap lists arrive as spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** Owner optometrist or group operations director; on the plan side, the quality director who funds gap closure
**Automation Potential:** High — the result, codes, letter and submission follow from the exam findings

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The diabetic eye exam is the one optometry service that health plans are graded on. The Eye Exam for Patients With Diabetes measure counts toward Medicare Advantage Star Ratings and became administrative-only in 2025 (search summary). So an exam counts only when its result reaches the plan as a code or supplemental data. Adults with diabetes reported an annual eye exam at 66.0% in 2023, against a 70.3% national target (Healthy People 2030). Medicare beneficiaries with diabetes were at 54.1% in 2017, with lower rates for Black and Hispanic beneficiaries (CDC). The optometrist's side of this loop, getting each completed exam credited and reported to the PCP, is a different job from the plan's chart chase, which sits at [[niches/health-insurance-carriers/hedis-hybrid-chart-review/profile|⚡ HEDIS Hybrid Chart Review & Stars Measurement]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
EHR letter templates, Direct messaging and fax, plan provider portals, and the plans' own gap-closure vendors. Autonomous retinal screening at the PCP (LumineticsCore, EyeArt, AEYE) covers some patients before referral. Nothing turns the optometrist's findings into the correct CPT II codes and plan submission automatically, or reconciles a plan's gap list against exams already done.
