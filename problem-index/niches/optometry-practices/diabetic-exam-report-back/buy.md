# PCP Letter Templates Adapted to Plan Supplemental Data

**Niche:** [[niches/optometry-practices/diabetic-exam-report-back/profile|Diabetic Eye Exam Report-Back & Plan Gap Credit]]
**Industry:** [[industries/optometry-practices|Optometry Practices]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** EHRs already generate the PCP letter; extending the same output to each plan's supplemental-data format and code rules would make one exam count three times.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #compliance #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get each diabetic eye exam the practice performs counted by the patient's health plan, and whoever gets the highest share of completed exams credited in the plan's eye-exam measure takes the account.

## The Problem
The letter to the PCP is written, but the plan receives only the claim, often without the right result code. The PCP's office may forward the letter to the plan, or may not.

## What Already Exists
EHR letter generators, Direct messaging, claim-level CPT II entry and plan provider portals for supplemental data.

## The Customization Gap
The EHR needs a mapping from exam findings to the measure's result codes, the plan-specific supplemental-data layouts and submission windows, and a check that each diabetic exam produced a code on the claim.

## Target Customer
Optometry EHR vendors, and medical-billing services serving eye practices.

## Impact If Solved
If 20% of a practice's ~600 yearly diabetic exams currently go uncoded, fixing the template credits ~120 exams a year (estimate) without extra staff time.
