# The Gap List Full of Patients Already Seen

**Niche:** [[niches/optometry-practices/diabetic-exam-report-back/profile|Diabetic Eye Exam Report-Back & Plan Gap Credit]]
**Industry:** [[industries/optometry-practices|Optometry Practices]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Every autumn plans send eye practices lists of members missing a diabetic eye exam, and staff find many were examined months earlier.
**Tags:** #logistic-regression #data-integration #automation #worker-facing #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get each diabetic eye exam the practice performs counted by the patient's health plan, and whoever gets the highest share of completed exams credited in the plan's eye-exam measure takes the account.

## The Problem
A plan's quality team sends a spreadsheet of "open gaps". A medical secretary looks up each name, finds the exam, prints the record and uploads it to the portal. The same names often reappear the next year.

## Why It's Still Broken
The original claim lacked the result code, or the plan's data run missed it. Nobody fixes the cause, so staff answer the symptom by hand every season.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Match each gap list to the practice's exams automatically. Submit the result for any patient already seen, and invite those who were not. Trace each matched gap back to the coding error that caused it, so it does not recur.

## Who Feels the Pain
Medical secretaries and scribes who lose days each season, and plans whose Star measure suffers for exams that did happen.

## Impact If Fixed
Answering a season's ~200 gap-list names automatically instead of at ~10 minutes each saves ~33 staff hours per practice a year (estimate).
