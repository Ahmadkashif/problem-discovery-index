# Contact Lens Prescriptions, Verification & Annual Supply

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/optometry-practices|Optometry Practices]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep fitted contact lens wearers buying their lenses through the prescribing practice, and whoever puts the highest share of fitted wearers on an annual supply or subscription takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M a year of contact lens ordering, verification handling, subscription and rebate software and the staff time spent on them in optometry practices (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% of practice spend, against a much larger contact lens revenue line (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — prescriptions are released on paper or by email, verification requests arrive by fax and robocall, and annual supplies are quoted by hand
**Target Buyer:** Owner optometrist; contact lens coordinator at larger practices
**Automation Potential:** High — release, verification and reorder are rule-bound and repetitive

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Contact lenses are a medical device sold on prescription, and the FTC's Contact Lens Rule governs how the prescriber hands the sale over. The practice must give every patient their prescription at the end of a fitting and keep a confirmation for three years. It has eight business hours to answer a seller's verification request before the prescription counts as verified. Prescriptions must last at least a year. FTC staff sent 37 warning letters to prescribers in June 2025, with penalties up to $53,088 per violation (Contact Lens Spectrum). The same moments where the rule applies are the moments where the patient decides whether to buy lenses from the practice or from an online seller. Compliance and capture are one workflow.

## Current Tools & Gaps
EHRs print prescriptions. E-fax, phone systems and Weave-style messaging carry requests and releases. Distributors and manufacturers offer direct-to-patient shipping and practice webstores, and manufacturers run annual-supply rebates. Nothing ties the release confirmation, the verification inbox, the patient's run-out date and the practice's offer together. The resupply logic resembles [[niches/medical-supply-retail/recurring-supply-fulfillment/profile|⚡ Recurring Supply Fulfillment]] in medical supplies, but under a different rule set.
