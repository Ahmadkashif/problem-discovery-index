# Retinal Imaging & OCT Makers' Clinical Science and Normative Databases

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/optometry-practices|Optometry Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Zeiss, Topcon, Heidelberg Engineering, Optos (Nikon) and iCare make the OCT scanners, fundus and widefield cameras, perimeters and tonometers in optometry exam lanes (recalled). Their clinical science teams collect the normative databases that colour every OCT printout green, yellow or red, run validation studies, and build progression analysis into their review platforms (Zeiss FORUM, Topcon Harmony). Practices pay for devices and software. The normative data and analyses ship inside the product.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-100 clinical scientists, algorithm engineers and clinical affairs staff per maker (estimate)
**Output:** Normative databases, progression algorithms, validation studies, clinical education
**Proprietary data:** Normative and validation datasets collected under the maker's own study protocols; installed-base data on cloud platforms
**Clock:** Product releases and FDA submissions on the maker's own schedule
**Buyer:** Head of clinical affairs or R&D at the maker
