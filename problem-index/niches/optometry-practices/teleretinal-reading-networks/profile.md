# Teleretinal Screening & Reading Networks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/optometry-practices|Optometry Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Networks that place retinal cameras in primary care clinics, safety-net clinics and pharmacies and route the images to optometrists or ophthalmologists for remote grading, as EyePACS and Intelligent Retinal Imaging Systems do (recalled). Health systems and plans pay per read or per programme. They compete with the autonomous AI vendors at pocket 13 and feed referable patients to eye practices.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analytics and programme staff per network (estimate); graders reading one image set at a time are production
**Output:** Graded retinal reads, referral recommendations, programme reports for health systems and plans
**Proprietary data:** Large graded retinal image archives from partner clinics
**Clock:** Clients' quality-measure seasons (the client's clock)
**Buyer:** Medical director or programme lead at the network
