# Optometry Licensure Examinations (NBEO)

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/optometry-practices|Optometry Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The National Board of Examiners in Optometry writes and administers the national examinations that state boards rely on to license optometrists: Part I ABS (Applied Basic Science), Part II PAM (Patient Assessment and Management) with its TMOD component, and Part III PEPS (Patient Encounters and Performance Skills Examination). It also offers the Injection Skills Examination, the Laser and Surgical Procedures Examination and the Advanced Competence in Medical Optometry credential (NBEO). It runs the job analyses that define entry-level competence (recalled). Candidates pay the exam fees. All 25 accredited US optometry schools now teach laser procedures (Optometric Management, 2026), and scope expansion keeps changing what the exams must test. The Association of Regulatory Boards of Optometry (ARBO) runs continuing-education accreditation alongside.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-25 test development, psychometric and research staff (estimate)
**Output:** Licensure examinations, job analyses, score reports to candidates, schools and boards, technical reports
**Proprietary data:** Decades of item performance and candidate outcomes across almost every US optometrist
**Clock:** NBEO's own testing windows and job-analysis cycle (its own calendar)
**Buyer:** Executive director or director of psychometrics at NBEO
