# Optometric Buying Groups & Alliances

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/optometry-practices|Optometry Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Membership networks of independent optometry practices negotiate supplier rebates, offer billing, bookkeeping and marketing services, and publish benchmarks to members. Vision Source, IDOC, PECAA and CECA are the largest (recalled). IDOC's 2025 benchmarks, drawn from more than 175 practices on its bookkeeping service, put median practice net at 31% of collected revenue and median collections at $1.57M (Optometry Times). Members pay dues or share rebates; the benchmarks are a member benefit.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analytics and benchmarking staff per group (estimate); bookkeepers and billers are production
**Output:** Member benchmarks, rebate programmes, vendor negotiation
**Proprietary data:** Member practices' bookkeeping, purchasing and production records
**Clock:** Self-set; annual rebate and benchmark cycles
**Buyer:** Chief executive or head of member services at the group
