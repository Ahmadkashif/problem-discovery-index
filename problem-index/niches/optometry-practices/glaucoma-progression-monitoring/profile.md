# Glaucoma Monitoring & Progression Detection

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/optometry-practices|Optometry Practices]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to catch glaucoma progression at the first visit it shows, and whoever leaves the smallest share of progressing eyes flagged a visit late takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600M a year of OCT and perimetry devices, review and progression software, and technician time spent on glaucoma suspects and patients in optometry practices (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~40% of the medical eye care niche (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High for capture, low for analysis — every scan is digital, but progression is judged by eye across printouts
**Target Buyer:** Owner optometrist or group clinical director
**Automation Potential:** Medium-High — artefact checks and trend analysis automate; the treatment and referral decision stays with the OD

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Glaucoma care is a long series of tests: OCT of the nerve fibre layer and ganglion cells, threshold visual fields, pressures and photographs, repeated every few months for years. Progression is slow, noisy and permanent once it happens. Every device maker has its own progression analysis, which reads only its own scans. When a practice replaces its OCT, it loses the baseline. Optometrists manage a growing share of glaucoma suspects and early glaucoma, and in 14 states can perform SLT themselves (Optometric Management, 2026). This sub-niche's contest is early detection, a different contest from dry eye's.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Zeiss FORUM Glaucoma Workplace, Topcon Harmony, Heidelberg's glaucoma module and Optos's review software analyse their own devices. Altris AI analyses OCT biomarkers across devices, and iCare sells home tonometry. None combines structure and function across devices and years into one progression call, and none flags myopic false positives before the doctor sees red.
