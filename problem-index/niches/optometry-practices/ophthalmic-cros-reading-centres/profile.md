# Ophthalmic CROs & Image Reading Centres

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/optometry-practices|Optometry Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Contract research organisations that specialise in eye drugs and devices run trials for sponsors, often using optometry practices as sites for dry eye, allergy, myopia control and contact lens studies. Ora, based in Andover, Massachusetts, claims more than 400 site partnerships and runs its own clinic (search summary). University and commercial image reading centres grade retinal photographs and OCT scans centrally to produce trial endpoints. Sponsors pay per study, and the cleaned, adjudicated endpoint dataset is the deliverable.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~30-200 clinical scientists, biostatisticians, imaging scientists and study designers per CRO or large reading centre, excluding site staff and graders, who are production (estimate)
**Output:** Trial designs and challenge models, monitored site data, graded imaging endpoints, statistical analyses for regulatory submissions
**Proprietary data:** Cross-trial records of site performance, patient recruitment, grading variability and challenge-model results that no single sponsor sees; the trial data itself belongs to sponsors
**Clock:** Sponsors' database-lock and submission dates (the client's schedule)
**Buyer:** Head of clinical operations at the sponsor; the CRO's chief scientific officer as the pocket's own buyer
