# Contact Lens & Spectacle Lens Makers' Professional Affairs and ECP Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/optometry-practices|Optometry Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
A handful of manufacturers supply almost every contact lens and most spectacle lenses an optometrist prescribes: Johnson & Johnson Vision, Alcon, CooperVision and Bausch + Lomb in contact lenses, EssilorLuxottica, Zeiss and Hoya in spectacle lenses (recalled). Their professional affairs, clinical research and market research teams run practice panels and surveys of eye care professionals, measure fitting and prescribing patterns, sponsor trials for products such as MiSight and Stellest, and fund the education and practice-building programmes that shape how optometrists use their products. The output supports sales; it is given to practices, not sold.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~30-150 professional affairs, clinical research and market research staff per large manufacturer (estimate)
**Output:** Clinical evidence, ECP survey and panel reports, prescribing and channel analytics, practice-building programmes
**Proprietary data:** Shipment and sell-out data, rebate redemptions, practice panels and sponsored trial results
**Clock:** Product launch and regulatory submission dates on the maker's own schedule; annual rebate programme cycles
**Buyer:** Head of professional affairs or global market research at the manufacturer

**Kill switches:** none on the half that carries the verdict: shipment, rebate and panel data, and trials the maker sponsors, are the maker's own. The practice-programme half, such as myopia programme registries holding enrolled children's measurements, would be live (HIPAA through the participating practices).
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. Rich data spent on selling lenses, held by a market of a few firms. Contact lens distributors belong to the queued medical supply distributors industry.
