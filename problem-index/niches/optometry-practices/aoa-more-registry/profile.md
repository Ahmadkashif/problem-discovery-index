# AOA MORE Registry

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/optometry-practices|Optometry Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The American Optometric Association's Measures and Outcomes Registry for Eyecare (AOA MORE) launched in 2015 as a qualified clinical data registry. It pulls data from members' EHRs (Compulink, Crystal PM, Eyefinity, MaximEyes, OfficeMate and RevolutionEHR were partners in 2018) to report quality measures and return benchmarks. 7,492 AOA members were registered in 2018 (search summary). It is optometry's counterpart to the ophthalmologists' IRIS Registry at [[niches/physician-practices/specialty-society-office-registries/profile|🔍 Office-Based Specialty Registries & QCDRs]] (43, kill switch), but far smaller and without a commercial data partner.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 registry and analytics staff at the association (estimate); EHR extraction runs through a technology vendor
**Output:** Quality-measure reporting for members, benchmark dashboards, occasional research
**Proprietary data:** Identified EHR extracts from participating practices
**Clock:** MIPS data submission closes on 31 March after the performance year (CMS's deadline on the registry's submission work)
**Buyer:** AOA's director of research or quality programmes
