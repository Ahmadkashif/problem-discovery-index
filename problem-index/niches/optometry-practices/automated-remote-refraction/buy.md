# Digital Phoropter Software Adapted to Technician-Led Refraction

**Niche:** [[niches/optometry-practices/automated-remote-refraction/profile|Automated & Remote Refraction]]
**Industry:** [[industries/optometry-practices|Optometry Practices]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Digital phoropters already run programmable refraction sequences; adapting the software to guide technicians and hand off cleanly to a supervising OD would make delegated refraction routine.
**Tags:** #decision-trees #automation #workflow-orchestration #worker-facing #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce final spectacle prescriptions without the optometrist's full manual refraction, and whoever has the fewest prescription-related remakes per hundred refractions takes the account.

## The Problem
Practices buy digital phoropters but the OD still runs most refractions, because the software assumes a skilled operator and state rules on delegation vary.

## What Already Exists
Digital phoropter and automated refraction software from Topcon, Visionix, Reichert and others, with programmable sequences and EHR export; remote-OD platforms for supervision by video.

## The Customization Gap
The software needs plain-language technician guidance, patient-response checks that catch confused answers, a one-screen summary for the supervising OD, and state-by-state configuration of what a technician may do.

## Target Customer
Phoropter makers and their dealers, and groups rolling out technician-led workups.

## Impact If Solved
Moving even a third of refractions to technician-led sequences in a three-OD practice frees ~1 OD-day a week (estimate) for medical visits.
