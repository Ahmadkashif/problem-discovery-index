# Medical Eligibility Tools Adapted to Vision-Plan Allowances

**Niche:** [[niches/optometry-practices/benefits-verification-estimates/profile|Vision & Medical Benefits Verification and Patient Estimates]]
**Industry:** [[industries/optometry-practices|Optometry Practices]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Medical eligibility and estimate tools already automate deductibles and coinsurance; adding vision plans' allowance, frequency and materials logic would cover optometry's other half.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #decision-trees #automation #data-integration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell each patient before the visit what their plans will pay for the exam, glasses and contacts, and whoever leaves the smallest gap between the pre-visit estimate and the adjudicated claim takes the account.

## The Problem
Practices that buy a medical eligibility tool still check vision plans by hand, because the tool understands deductibles, not frame allowances or 24-month lens frequencies.

## What Already Exists
Clearinghouse eligibility (270/271), patient-estimate tools sold to medical practices and hospitals, and dental eligibility tools that read frequency limits.

## The Customization Gap
Vision benefits need their own schema: materials allowances, overage discounts, lens option coverage, contact lens allowance in place of glasses, and frequency by benefit. It must be filled from portals, since vision plans' electronic eligibility rarely carries this detail (estimate).

## Target Customer
Eligibility and patient-estimate vendors wanting to enter optometry, and optometry EHR vendors.

## Impact If Solved
For a practice checking ~8,000 visits a year, removing the vision-plan half of manual checks saves ~600 staff hours a year (estimate).
