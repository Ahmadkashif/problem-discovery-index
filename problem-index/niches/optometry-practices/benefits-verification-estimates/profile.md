# Vision & Medical Benefits Verification and Patient Estimates

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/optometry-practices|Optometry Practices]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell each patient before the visit what their plans will pay for the exam, glasses and contacts, and whoever leaves the smallest gap between the pre-visit estimate and the adjudicated claim takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$700M a year of front-desk time and eligibility tools spent checking vision and medical benefits across ~22,800 optometry offices (estimate from Census establishment counts and BLS receptionist pay)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3–4% of practice revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — portals and electronic eligibility exist, but staff work them one patient and one plan at a time
**Target Buyer:** Practice manager; front-office director at multi-OD groups
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the inputs are structured or semi-structured, the task repeats daily, and the adjudicated claim checks the answer

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Optometry is the only office-based specialty where a typical patient carries two kinds of coverage with entirely different structures. Vision plans pay a fixed exam fee and fixed allowances for frames, lenses and contacts, with benefit frequencies of 12 or 24 months. Medical plans carry deductibles and coinsurance. VSP alone sent 21.4M private-practice claims in 2024 (VSP Vision), and each vision plan describes the same benefit in different words on a different portal. The estimate is only right if it combines both coverages with the frames and lenses the patient actually chooses. That is why it fails at the optical counter rather than at check-in. Medical-only verification is analysed at [[niches/urgent-care/insurance-verification-coding/profile|⚡ Insurance Verification & Coding]], and dental benefits at [[niches/dental-practices/insurance-verification/profile|⚡ Insurance Verification & Benefit Breakdown]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Eyefinity (VSP), each plan's portal, Anagram's multi-plan eligibility and claims, medical clearinghouses' 270/271 eligibility, and optical point-of-sale pricing in the EHRs. None produces one standard benefits card across vision and medical coverages and turns it into a priced estimate for the frames and lenses chosen.
