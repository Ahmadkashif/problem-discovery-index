# Package Billing Adapted to Dry-Eye Series

**Niche:** [[niches/optometry-practices/dry-eye-treatment-courses/profile|Dry Eye Treatment Courses]]
**Industry:** [[industries/optometry-practices|Optometry Practices]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Membership and package billing tools from aesthetics and med-spa software already handle prepaid series; adapting them to dry eye's mixed insurance and cash rules would remove the checkout friction.
**Tags:** #decision-trees #automation #compliance #revenue-impact #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to turn dry-eye diagnoses into completed in-office treatment series, and whoever completes the most full series per diagnosed patient takes the account.

## The Problem
A dry-eye course mixes billable medical services (the workup, some testing, some drugs) with cash procedures (IPL, thermal pulsation). Patients want one price. Staff build it by hand and often discount or misquote.

## What Already Exists
Med-spa and aesthetics software sells prepaid packages, memberships and financing. Optometry EHRs bill insurance well and cash packages poorly.

## The Customization Gap
Package rules need to separate insured and non-covered services correctly, with an advance beneficiary notice for Medicare patients. Each session must be tracked against the prepaid course. Financing must be offered at the moment of diagnosis.

## Target Customer
Dry-eye clinics inside optometry practices.

## Impact If Solved
If prepaid packages lift the share of patients who buy the full course by ~10 points on ~150 courses a year, the practice adds ~15 completed courses (estimate) and stops misquoting at checkout.
