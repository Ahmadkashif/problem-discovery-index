# Course Planner That Predicts Who Completes

**Niche:** [[niches/optometry-practices/dry-eye-treatment-courses/profile|Dry Eye Treatment Courses]]
**Industry:** [[industries/optometry-practices|Optometry Practices]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A planner that recommends a dry-eye treatment course each patient is likely to finish, books every session at diagnosis, and intervenes when completion risk rises.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #survival-analysis #causal-inference #logistic-regression #workflow-orchestration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to turn dry-eye diagnoses into completed in-office treatment series, and whoever completes the most full series per diagnosed patient takes the account.

## The Problem
Practices diagnose dry eye far more often than they complete treatment. Patients accept a plan in the chair, pay for the first session, then stop. No one knows which patients will drop out or why.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Device makers measure devices sold and consumables used, not courses completed. Practices run dry-eye clinics out of spreadsheets. Outcome data (symptom scores, gland imaging) sits in diagnostic devices that are not linked to the schedule.

## What to Build
A completion model on demographics, symptom scores, gland imaging, price quoted, financing and history. A plan recommender that offers the course the patient is most likely to complete. Automatic booking of every session at diagnosis, and outreach triggered when a session is missed. Symptom and gland outcomes per completed course, for the doctor and the patient.

## Target Customer
Optometry practices with dedicated dry-eye clinics, and groups that want to standardise the service line.

## Impact If Built
Raising course completion from ~40% to ~55% for a practice starting ~150 courses a year, at ~$1,200 a course, adds ~$27,000 a year (estimate) and more patients who actually improve.
