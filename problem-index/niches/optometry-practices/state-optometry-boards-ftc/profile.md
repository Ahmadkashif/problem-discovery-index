# State Optometry Boards & FTC Prescription Rule Enforcement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/optometry-practices|Optometry Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State boards of optometry license optometrists, set scope rules (14 states now permit lasers such as SLT and YAG capsulotomy, Optometric Management 2026) and investigate complaints. The Federal Trade Commission enforces the Contact Lens Rule and Eyeglass Rule; its staff sent 37 warning letters to prescribers in June 2025, citing penalties up to $53,088 per violation (Contact Lens Spectrum). Funding comes from licence fees and appropriations.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 investigators and analysts per state board, and a small FTC staff team for the eye-care rules (estimate)
**Output:** Licences, scope rulings, disciplinary orders, warning letters
**Proprietary data:** Licensure records and complaint and investigation files
**Clock:** Statutory licence renewal cycles and legislative sessions
**Buyer:** Executive director of the board; FTC Bureau of Consumer Protection staff
