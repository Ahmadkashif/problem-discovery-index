## The Problem
The security testing profession worked out how to run this kind of business: published testing methodologies and scoping standards, a shared taxonomy of weakness classes, a common severity scale, structured finding formats, retest and verification workflows, and the distinction between a continuous scanning programme and a specialist engagement. AI red teaming has the same shape of business, credible expertise, and almost none of the surrounding practice.

## What Already Exists
Published testing methodologies and scoping guides; shared weakness taxonomies with stable identifiers; common severity scoring frameworks with published rubrics; structured finding exchange formats; retest and remediation verification workflows; and the programme structure separating continuous scanning from periodic deep assessment.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a target whose failures are probabilistic and contested. It requires: (1) a weakness taxonomy with stable identifiers for model failure classes, which does not exist and whose absence is why no two firms' findings can be compared — creating it is the single most valuable piece of shared infrastructure available here; (2) severity scoring adapted to harms that are contextual rather than technical, which the severity niche develops and which is the hardest transfer; (3) findings expressed probabilistically, since a model failure has a success rate rather than a binary reproduction, and the finding format has no field for that; (4) retest workflow adapted to a target that changes underneath the tester, which is the revalidation problem and has no analogue in conventional security where the code does not change on its own; and (5) programme structure distinguishing continuous coverage from deep assessment explicitly, which the profession learned to separate and which this industry conflates.

## Target Customer
Assessment firms, their clients, regulators writing testing requirements, and the application security community whose practice transfers with real adaptation.

## Impact If Solved
Two decades of testing programme practice exists and this industry is at the artisanal stage. A stable weakness taxonomy for model failure classes is the most valuable shared infrastructure available and its absence is why no two firms' findings compare.
