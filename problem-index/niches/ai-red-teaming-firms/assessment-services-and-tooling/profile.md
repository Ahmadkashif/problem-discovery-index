## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is what the industry sells: assessments of whether a deployed model can be induced to behave harmfully, delivered as an engagement or as a continuously-running product.

It is **not terminal**, because the two delivery forms compete on unrelated axes. An expert engagement is bought for judgement and novelty — finding the thing that automation and the client's own team did not think of — by a small number of high-value clients, under confidentiality, priced per engagement, competing against the client's internal capability. An automated probing platform is bought for breadth and currency by an engineering or security team, priced per system per month, competing against freely available jailbreak datasets and a script somebody could write in an afternoon. One business scales with expert headcount and the other with software; one is judged on a single non-obvious finding and the other on how much ground it covers cheaply and how fast it incorporates new techniques. Filter Notes in the overview records the two rejected alternatives; the sub-niches below are the split.
