# Manual Expert Assessment

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ai-red-teaming-firms|AI Red Teaming Firms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to produce the finding the client's own team and every automated suite would have missed — and whoever does that takes the engagement, because a client with an internal red team is paying for exactly one thing.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$100M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~17% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — the valuable part is judgement
**Target Buyer:** Labs and regulated enterprises with real internal capability
**Automation Potential:** Low for the core, high for the scaffolding

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Expert engagement work is bought by organisations that already have skilled people. They are not buying capacity; they are buying an outside view, an approach their team did not consider, or a finding whose discovery internally would have been institutionally awkward. One genuinely novel finding justifies an engagement. That makes this a judgement business with a scarce input — researchers who are good at this — whose work is unmeasurable by the metrics that suit automated testing, and whose value is concentrated in a small number of moments per engagement that no process guarantees.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Researcher expertise, shared literature, bespoke technique development, and reporting. The gaps: no way to demonstrate novelty, so a genuinely original finding and a catalogue rediscovery read the same; no accumulation across engagements, so the firm's knowledge lives in individuals; the scaffolding work around the research consumes time that should go to research; and the scarce input is being spent down, which the researcher niche develops.
