# Research Methodology and Structured Analytic Technique

**One-liner:** Intelligence analysis developed structured techniques to counter the biases of expert judgement, and red teaming relies on expert judgement with no such scaffolding.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to produce the finding the client's own team and every automated suite would have missed — and whoever does that takes the engagement, because a client with an internal red team is paying for exactly one thing.

## The Problem
A discipline whose product is expert judgement, working under uncertainty against an adaptive subject, describes intelligence analysis exactly — and that field spent decades learning that unaided expert judgement has predictable failure modes and building structured techniques to counter them: competing hypotheses, devil's advocacy, key assumptions checks, structured brainstorming. Red teaming is the same kind of work and has adopted almost none of it, relying instead on individual skill and informal collaboration.

## What Already Exists
Structured analytic techniques including analysis of competing hypotheses and key assumptions checks; red cell and devil's advocacy methods; structured brainstorming and divergent thinking protocols; confidence and uncertainty expression conventions; and after-action review practice for improving analytic process.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a subject that can be probed empirically rather than only reasoned about. It requires: (1) hypotheses about where a system is weak, generated structurally and then tested rather than argued, which is a stronger position than intelligence analysis enjoys and is underexploited — the field's generation techniques combined with empirical testing is the specific combination available here; (2) key assumptions checks applied to the engagement's own scoping, since the most consequential failure is assuming a harm category is not relevant and never examining it; (3) structured divergence early in an engagement, because researchers converge quickly on the approaches they know and the value is in the ones they do not; (4) after-action review of the analytic process rather than only of the findings, which is how a firm's method improves and is currently entirely informal; and (5) confidence expression conventions, so a finding's strength and an absence of findings are both communicated with a stated meaning.

## Target Customer
Assessment firms, their researchers, clients receiving judgement-based conclusions, and the analytic methodology community.

## Impact If Solved
A discipline built to counter the failure modes of expert judgement exists and this field relies on unaided judgement. Structured hypothesis generation combined with empirical testing is a stronger method than either field has alone, and a key assumptions check on scoping targets the most consequential failure.
