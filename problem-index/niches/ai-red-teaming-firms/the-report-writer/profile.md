# The Report Writer

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ai-red-teaming-firms|AI Red Teaming Firms]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a finding from a researcher's head into two audiences' hands without the researcher writing documents for a week — and whoever does that takes the account, because that week is the industry's most expensive time spent on its least skilled task.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$30M US in loaded researcher time
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of category revenue equivalent
**Digital Adoption:** None — the best researchers write documents
**Target Buyer:** Assessment delivery organisations
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the translation is the automatable part

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The most experienced researchers spend the end of every engagement writing documents, translating what they found into something an engineering team can act on and a compliance team can file. Those are two different documents for two different audiences, produced by hand, by the scarcest people in the business, at the moment their research time has the most remaining value. The content is largely mechanical — a finding has a description, a reproduction, a severity, an affected component, a recommendation and a regulatory mapping — and the parts that need the researcher's judgement are a small fraction of what they end up writing.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Report templates, word processors, and the researcher's notes. The gaps: findings are not captured in structured form during the work, so the report is a reconstruction; one document serves two audiences and serves neither; regulatory mapping is done by hand per client; nothing is reusable across engagements; and the writing happens at the end, when memory has degraded and the engagement is over.
