## The Problem
Automated scanning against a signature catalogue, run continuously across an asset estate, with findings triaged and tracked to closure, is an industry with twenty-five years of accumulated practice and accumulated scar tissue: signature currency matters more than signature count, false positives destroy the programme's credibility, an incomplete asset inventory means the scan misses what matters, and a findings list without prioritisation is ignored. AI probing platforms are rebuilding the same product and are early enough in the curve to skip the mistakes.

## What Already Exists
Vulnerability scanning platforms with signature feeds and currency guarantees; asset discovery and inventory as a prerequisite to scanning; risk-based prioritisation combining severity with exposure and exploitability; false positive management and tuning workflows; findings lifecycle tracking with remediation verification; and the programme metrics the profession converged on.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a target with no inventory and a probabilistic result. It requires: (1) discovery of deployed model-backed applications, since an organisation frequently does not know how many it has and the scanning profession learned that inventory is the prerequisite everything else rests on — this is a real and unbuilt capability; (2) false positives redefined, since a probe that succeeds once in two hundred is neither a false positive nor a finding in the conventional sense and the triage workflow has no category for it; (3) prioritisation by what the deploying team can actually change, which differs from conventional exploitability and is the practical ranking; (4) signature currency as a contractual commitment, which the scanning industry made standard and which this market has not; and (5) closure verification on a probabilistic finding, where a retest must establish a rate rather than a binary and the workflow assumes the latter.

## Target Customer
Probing platform vendors, security and engineering teams, and the vulnerability management profession for whom model-backed applications are a new asset class.

## Impact If Solved
Twenty-five years of scanning programme scar tissue is available to a market early enough to skip it. Discovery of deployed model-backed applications is the unbuilt prerequisite, and signature currency as a contractual commitment is the norm this market has not adopted.
