# Assessment Revalidation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ai-red-teaming-firms|AI Red Teaming Firms]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to re-establish an assessment's conclusions after the system changes, at a fraction of the original cost — and whoever does that takes the account, because the alternative is a report that expires on the client's next model upgrade.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$55M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — the report goes stale with no cheap way back
**Target Buyer:** Clients on a model upgrade path, which is all of them
**Automation Potential:** Very High — re-running a recorded assessment is mechanical

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A client changes model version and the entire assessment becomes stale. The findings may no longer reproduce; findings that did not exist may now; the mitigations verified against the old version may be ineffective. The report's conclusions are undefined and the only established route back to a valid position is another engagement, which is expensive enough that most clients simply continue relying on a document about a system they no longer run. Every input to solving this exists: the assessment's probes were executed and could be recorded, and re-running them is cheap compared to the judgement that produced them. Nobody has built revalidation as a product, which leaves the industry's output with an undefined and short shelf life.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Reports as static documents, periodic re-engagement, and continuous probing products sold separately. The gaps: probes are not retained in executable form, so nothing can be re-run; no notification when a client's model changes; no cheap revalidation tier between a full engagement and nothing; no statement of a report's validity conditions; and no distinction between findings that would be expected to persist and those tied to a specific version.
