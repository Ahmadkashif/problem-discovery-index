# The Red Team Researcher

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ai-red-teaming-firms|AI Red Teaming Firms]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep skilled researchers able to do this work for years rather than months — and whoever does that holds the scarce input the entire industry runs on.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$35M US in attrition, replacement and lost capability
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~6% of category revenue equivalent
**Digital Adoption:** None — exposure treated as a job requirement
**Target Buyer:** The firms employing researchers, and their leadership
**Automation Potential:** Medium — exposure can be reduced, the work cannot be removed

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Red team researchers spend their working days deliberately eliciting the worst outputs a model can produce, reading them, cataloguing them and iterating toward worse ones. The content moderation industry took years and considerable external pressure to acknowledge what sustained exposure to harmful material does to people, and this industry — younger, smaller, staffed by people who identify as security researchers rather than as moderators — has largely not had that reckoning. The researchers are the scarce input the entire industry depends on, their expertise takes years to build, and nothing in the standard operating model accounts for the cumulative cost of the work.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Individual coping, informal team culture, and whatever support the firm generally offers staff. The gaps: no exposure limits or measurement; no rotation between harm categories; no automation of the most repetitive exposure; no specialist support appropriate to the work; no acknowledgement of the effect in career or workload planning; and no measurement of attrition or its cause.
