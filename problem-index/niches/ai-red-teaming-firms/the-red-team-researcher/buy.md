**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep skilled researchers able to do this work for years rather than months — and whoever does that holds the scarce input the entire industry runs on.

## The Problem
Several occupations require sustained exposure to harmful material and have developed real practice around it: exposure limits and rotation, mandatory breaks, image blurring and greyscale by default, specialist psychological support with practitioners who understand the work, peer support structures, and screening and preparation before people start. Child protection investigation and digital forensics have decades of this. Content moderation acquired it recently and expensively. AI red teaming has the exposure and none of the practice.

## What Already Exists
Exposure limit and rotation policies from moderation and investigative work; default obscuring techniques including blurring, greyscale and preview-before-reveal; specialist psychological support models designed for this exposure; peer support and structured debriefing; pre-employment preparation and informed consent about the material; and occupational health monitoring.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to work where the exposure is sought rather than encountered. It requires: (1) accounting for active elicitation, since a moderator reviews what arrives and a researcher iterates deliberately toward the worst output, which is a more intense form of the same exposure and has no established guidance — this distinction is the reason the existing limits cannot simply be copied; (2) practice that fits senior autonomous professionals rather than a managed queue-based workforce, since the delivery model that works for moderation will be ignored by researchers; (3) rotation across harm categories rather than across shifts, since engagements are weeks long and the category is what determines the exposure; (4) obscuring adapted to text, where blurring is less applicable and staged reveal, summarisation and classifier-first review are the available analogues; and (5) framing that researchers will accept, since the occupational health framing used in moderation does not fit how this workforce sees itself and a version that does is a prerequisite for any of it being used.

## Target Customer
Assessment firms, their researchers, occupational health practitioners specialising in this exposure, and the trust and safety community whose practice transfers.

## Impact If Solved
Decades of practice exists in adjacent occupations and none of it has been adopted here. Active elicitation is a more intense exposure than reviewing a queue, which is why the limits need adapting rather than copying, and a framing this workforce will accept is a prerequisite for any of it being used.
