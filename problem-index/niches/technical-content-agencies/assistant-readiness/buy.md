# Structured Content From Information Architecture

**Industry:** [[industries/technical-content-agencies|Technical Content Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Structured authoring separated content from presentation decades ago, and most documentation is still written as pages.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make a corpus that produces correct answers when the reader is a machine synthesising from it — and whoever establishes that takes the account.

## The Problem
Structured authoring solved the separation of content from presentation long ago: content typed by what it is rather than how it looks, topics that stand alone and can be reused in any context, conditional content by audience and version, and metadata that makes meaning machine-readable. The discipline exists, has standards, and is used in regulated and complex documentation. Most technical documentation is written as pages in a format that encodes appearance.

## What Already Exists
Semantic content typing; topic-based authoring with standalone topics; conditional content by version and audience; structured metadata and taxonomy; and single-source publishing to several outputs.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a machine consumer nobody controls rather than a publishing pipeline. It requires: (1) the consumer being a third-party assistant with its own retrieval and synthesis behaviour, so the structure must help a system that was not designed around it and cannot be configured — this is the substantive difference from any single-source publishing scenario; (2) a documentation culture that moved decisively toward lightweight markup and will not return to heavy structured authoring; (3) statements that must be self-contained at a much finer granularity than a topic; (4) version and deprecation semantics that must survive extraction; and (5) verification against what the assistant actually produces, which has no analogue in publishing.

## Target Customer
Documentation teams and agencies, developer experience leadership, documentation platform vendors, and structured content tooling providers.

## Impact If Solved
Structured authoring separated meaning from presentation and has standards and tooling. A third-party consumer that cannot be configured, read by a culture that will not adopt heavy authoring, is what the adaptation must work within.
