# Assistant Readiness

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/technical-content-agencies|Technical Content Agencies]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make a corpus that produces correct answers when the reader is a machine synthesising from it — and whoever establishes that takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~20% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — new question
**Target Buyer:** Documentation and product leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — structure plus verification

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Developers increasingly get answers from assistants that synthesise from documentation without the reader ever arriving. That breaks pageview measurement entirely and makes a different question consequential: whether the corpus is accurate, unambiguous and structured enough to be synthesised correctly. It is a quality question the field is unusually well equipped to answer and has never been asked, and it is now the one that determines whether the documentation does its job.

### Contested sub-niches
This niche is not terminal. It names a property, and achieving it requires two different kinds of work:
- [[niches/technical-content-agencies/corpus-structure/profile|🎯 Corpus Structure & Disambiguation]] — writing and structuring for correct synthesis
- [[niches/technical-content-agencies/synthesis-verification/profile|🎯 Synthesis Verification]] — checking what assistants actually answer

## Current Tools & Gaps
A documentation site optimised for human reading and search ranking. The gaps: no structural markup expressing meaning; no disambiguation of versioned or context-dependent statements; no testing of what assistants produce; no monitoring of wrong answers in circulation; and no measure of the growing share of readers who never arrive.
