# Engineering Review Dependency

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/technical-content-agencies|Technical Content Agencies]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a technical detail confirmed by an engineer whose sprint commitment does not include documentation review — and whoever unblocks that takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$330M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~11% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — goodwill
**Target Buyer:** Engineering and documentation leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — verification and routing

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A technical writer cannot ship until an engineer confirms the details are right, and the engineer has a sprint commitment that documentation review is not part of. The review sits in a queue behind committed work, the writer waits, the documentation ships late or ships unverified. It is the single largest source of delay in the documentation pipeline, it is an organisational design problem rather than a writing one, and it recurs on every page.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A pull request awaiting review, a message in a channel and a personal relationship. The gaps: no engineering commitment to review; no automated verification reducing what needs a human; no routing to whoever actually knows; no service expectation on review; and no measurement of review-driven delay.
