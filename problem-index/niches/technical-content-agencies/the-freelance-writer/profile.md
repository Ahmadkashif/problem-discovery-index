# The Freelance Technical Writer

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/technical-content-agencies|Technical Content Agencies]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let a freelance writer produce accurate technical content without access to the engineers, the product or the outcome — and whoever equips them takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$250M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — no access
**Target Buyer:** Freelance practitioners
**Automation Potential:** Medium — access, context and credentialing

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A large tier of technical content is produced by freelancers, engaged per piece, frequently through an agency, with limited access to the product and none to the engineers who built it. They are asked for accuracy on systems they cannot run, paid per word or per piece regardless of the research required, and never told whether what they wrote worked. The commissioning model assumes the writer can establish the facts and provides nothing that would let them.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A brief, a public documentation site and an email address for questions. The gaps: no product access; no route to a subject matter expert; no context beyond the brief; no visibility of what happened to the content; and no recognition of the research effort in the rate.
