**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Brands monitor what is said about them across channels they do not control, and documentation teams monitor their own site.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #descriptive-statistics #confidence-intervals #automation #data-integration #word-embeddings #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to find out what assistants are actually telling developers about a product, from systems nobody controls — and whoever monitors that takes the account.

## The Problem
Brand and reputation monitoring established the practice of watching what is said about you in channels you do not control: media monitoring, social listening, review tracking, sentiment analysis, and a workflow for correcting significant inaccuracies. Organisations run it continuously because what third parties say shapes what people believe. Documentation teams monitor their own site's analytics and have no view of the third-party systems now answering on their behalf.

## What Already Exists
Continuous monitoring of third-party channels; accuracy and sentiment classification; alerting on significant misstatements; correction and engagement workflows; and trend reporting over time.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a channel that answers questions rather than publishes statements. It requires: (1) monitoring by asking rather than by listening, since an assistant produces an answer only when queried, so the monitoring must generate the interactions — this is the substantive difference and makes the question set the central design problem; (2) correctness assessed against technical truth rather than sentiment; (3) no publisher to contact for a correction, so the remedy is usually changing the corpus; (4) answers that vary between users and over time, requiring repeated sampling; and (5) a technical audience acting on the answer immediately rather than forming an impression.

## Target Customer
Product and documentation leadership, technical content agencies, developer relations functions, and brand monitoring vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Brand monitoring made watching uncontrolled channels continuous and routine. Monitoring by asking rather than listening makes the question set the central design problem, which is what has to be built.
