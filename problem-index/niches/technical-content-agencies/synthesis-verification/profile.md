# Synthesis Verification

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/technical-content-agencies|Technical Content Agencies]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to find out what assistants are actually telling developers about a product, from systems nobody controls — and whoever monitors that takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$270M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — never attempted
**Target Buyer:** Product and documentation leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — continuous question testing

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This half faces outward at systems the company does not control. What do assistants say when asked about the product, how often are they wrong, what specifically causes each wrong answer, and does it improve when the corpus changes. It is measurement rather than craft, the finding may be that a correct corpus is being synthesised into a wrong answer for reasons no authoring change will fix, and it is a continuous monitoring product rather than a one-off assessment.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Nothing. The gaps: no question set; no periodic testing against assistants; no comparison against product truth; no attribution of a wrong answer to a source; and no visibility of the reader who never arrives.
