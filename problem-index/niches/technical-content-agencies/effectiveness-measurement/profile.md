# Effectiveness Measurement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/technical-content-agencies|Technical Content Agencies]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to establish whether a reader with a task completed it, when every documentation site holds the exact record of where readers failed and reports pageviews — and whoever uses that record takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$800M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~27% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — pageviews
**Target Buyer:** Documentation leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very high — signals already in the logs

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every documentation site has the exact record of where readers failed — the searches that returned nothing, the rephrasing, the ticket opened straight after — and reports pageviews instead. A page with high traffic and a high bounce may be the most-needed and worst-written page on the site, and the metric cannot distinguish that from a page that worked. The signal is continuous, high-volume, free, and sits unexamined in every site's logs and every support system.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Site analytics, a search box, a support ticketing system and a pageview report. The gaps: no analysis of failed searches; no detection of rephrasing sequences; no link between a page visit and a subsequent ticket; no measure of task completion; and no prioritisation of what to write next from any of it.
