# Integrity Due Diligence & Corporate Investigations Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/investigation-and-screening-firms|Investigation & Background Screening Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Kroll, Control Risks, Nardello & Co., Mintz Group, K2 Integrity and hundreds of boutiques investigate companies and people for clients: pre-transaction and pre-investment due diligence, third-party and anti-corruption due diligence, internal and fraud investigations, asset tracing and dispute support. General counsel, compliance officers, private equity deal teams and law firms pay by the report tier or by the hour. The work behind the pocket's Pass 1 counterpart is described at [[niches/investigation-and-screening-firms/corporate-due-diligence-vetting/profile|🟠 Corporate Due Diligence & Executive Vetting]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~50-300 research analysts per large firm, excluding field investigators and retained local researchers (estimate, from the firms' described due diligence and research practices); boutiques far fewer
**Output:** Due diligence reports by tier, investigative memoranda, asset-tracing findings
**Proprietary data:** Years of prior reports, source maps by country and a network of retained local researchers; the inputs are public records, press and human sources
**Clock:** Deal signing, board and investment committee dates set by the client — a client's schedule rather than a third-party deadline
**Buyer:** Head of Due Diligence Practice / Global Head of Research

**Kill switches:** live — data isn't theirs to use. Reports belong to and are confidential to the commissioning client, and internal-investigation and dispute work is routinely commissioned through counsel to preserve privilege, so the archive that would power an insight engine cannot be reused across engagements. This matches the vault's ruling on [[niches/expert-networks/commercial-due-diligence-advisors/profile|Commercial Due Diligence Advisors]] (50, kill switch) and its majority rule for work through counsel.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch (42). Even without the switch, tiered reports are only partly repeatable (Q2 3), the inputs are public (Q3 3) and the dates are the client's (Q4 3). The gap to Commercial Due Diligence Advisors (50) is mostly rule drift: that pocket took Q4 5 on bid deadlines, a client's clock, and would sit at ~46 under the current rule.
