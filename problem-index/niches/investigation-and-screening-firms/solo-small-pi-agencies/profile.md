# Solo & Small PI Agencies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/investigation-and-screening-firms|Investigation & Background Screening Firms]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to win and keep a place on the approved-vendor panels of insurers, TPAs and law firms, and whoever delivers reports in each panel's template by its deadline with the fewest rejected invoices takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.0B receipts at the 3,249 employer firms with fewer than ten employees (Census SUSB 2022), plus nonemployer sole proprietors not counted
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of employer receipts
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — database searches and email are digital; case notes, billing and evidence handling are often spreadsheets, phone galleries and memory
**Target Buyer:** Owner-investigator
**Automation Potential:** Medium

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Most firms in the code are tiny: 2,806 of the 3,767 employer firms had fewer than five employees in 2022. The owner is a licensed investigator — often a former police officer, insurance adjuster or military investigator — who works cases, sells to attorneys and adjusters, keeps the licence and continuing education current, and does the books at night. Work comes from law firms (witness location and interviews, background on parties, asset searches), insurers and TPAs (surveillance, recorded statements), individuals (domestic, missing persons) and overflow from larger firms. Process-serving stakeouts sit with [[niches/legal-support-services/hard-to-serve-skip-trace-service/profile|Hard-to-Serve & Skip-Trace Service]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Case management tools built for investigators (Trackops, CaseFleet), licensed people-search databases (TLOxp, IRB, idiCORE, Accurint), accounting software, and state association listservs for out-of-area referrals. Gaps: panel onboarding and scorecards consume time the owner does not have; database providers audit permissible-purpose use and suspend accounts that cannot show it; and out-of-state work is subcontracted by phone among people who barely know each other.
