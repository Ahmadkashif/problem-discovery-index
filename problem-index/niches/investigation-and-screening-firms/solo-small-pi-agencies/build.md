# A Vetted Exchange for Out-of-Area Assignments

**Niche:** [[niches/investigation-and-screening-firms/solo-small-pi-agencies/profile|Solo & Small PI Agencies]]
**Industry:** [[industries/investigation-and-screening-firms|Investigation & Background Screening Firms]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A small agency whose client needs a witness interviewed three states away finds a subcontractor through a listserv post and hopes the person is licensed, insured and competent.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to win and keep a place on the approved-vendor panels of insurers, TPAs and law firms, and whoever delivers reports in each panel's template by its deadline with the fewest rejected invoices takes the account.

## The Problem
Clients expect their investigator to cover wherever the case goes. A two-person agency in Ohio gets a request for surveillance in Arizona or a records pull in rural Georgia. The owner posts to a state association listserv or calls a contact, negotiates a rate, sends the case details by email, and receives a report in the subcontractor's format that must be rewritten for the client. Licence status, insurance and quality are taken on trust; a bad subcontractor costs the client relationship.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Agencies are wary of platforms that might poach their clients, and licensing differs by state, so a national directory alone is not enough. Associations run listservs, not marketplaces.

## What to Build
A members-only exchange where agencies post assignments without naming the end client, with automatic licence verification against state boards, certificates of insurance on file, rate cards, report templates the posting agency specifies, and ratings from completed work. Matching suggests subcontractors by geography, specialty and past performance; the exchange handles payment and keeps the client relationship with the posting agency.

## Target Customer
Owners of small investigation agencies, and state and national investigator associations that could sponsor it.

## Impact If Built
Small agencies can accept out-of-area work they now turn down, subcontracting risk falls, and reports reach clients in a consistent format.
