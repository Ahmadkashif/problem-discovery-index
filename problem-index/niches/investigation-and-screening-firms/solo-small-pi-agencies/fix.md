# Permissible-Purpose Logs Kept in the Investigator's Head

**Niche:** [[niches/investigation-and-screening-firms/solo-small-pi-agencies/profile|Solo & Small PI Agencies]]
**Industry:** [[industries/investigation-and-screening-firms|Investigation & Background Screening Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Licensed people-search databases audit investigators' searches for a lawful purpose, and a small agency that cannot match each search to a case and a reason risks losing the tool its business runs on.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #k-nearest-neighbors #compliance #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to win and keep a place on the approved-vendor panels of insurers, TPAs and law firms, and whoever delivers reports in each panel's template by its deadline with the fewest rejected invoices takes the account.

## The Problem
Investigative databases such as TLOxp, IRB, idiCORE and Accurint give licensed investigators access to data restricted under the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act and the Driver's Privacy Protection Act, on condition that each search has a permissible purpose. Providers audit accounts and ask investigators to justify specific searches. A solo investigator who ran hundreds of searches last quarter, some for quick checks on a phone call, must reconstruct from memory which case and purpose each served.

## Why It's Still Broken
The databases record the search; the case management tool records the case; nothing links them. Investigators enter a purpose code at search time from a drop-down, often the same one every time.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Search from inside the case record, or import database search logs and match them to open cases by subject name, date and client, with a short purpose note drafted from the case and confirmed by the investigator. Flag searches with no matching case for the investigator to explain or stop.

## Who Feels the Pain
Owner-investigators facing provider audits; their clients, whose evidence can be challenged if data was obtained improperly; subjects whose data was searched without a reason.

## Impact If Fixed
Audit responses take minutes, accounts are not suspended over paperwork, and the agency can show clients and courts that its data was lawfully obtained.
