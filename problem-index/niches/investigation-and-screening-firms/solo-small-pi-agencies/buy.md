# Practice Management Built for Lawyers, Not Investigators

**Niche:** [[niches/investigation-and-screening-firms/solo-small-pi-agencies/profile|Solo & Small PI Agencies]]
**Industry:** [[industries/investigation-and-screening-firms|Investigation & Background Screening Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Small agencies borrow law-firm practice management and generic accounting, which handle matters and invoices but not carrier billing guidelines, evidence custody or licence and continuing-education tracking.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #compliance #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to win and keep a place on the approved-vendor panels of insurers, TPAs and law firms, and whoever delivers reports in each panel's template by its deadline with the fewest rejected invoices takes the account.

## The Problem
The owner tracks cases in one tool, time and mileage in another, evidence on a phone and a hard drive, and licences, firearm permits and continuing-education hours in a calendar. Panel clients demand reports in their own templates and invoices that follow their guidelines; rejected invoices are paid weeks late or not at all.

## What Already Exists
Law-firm practice management (matters, time, billing, document storage), small-business accounting, and investigator-specific case tools such as Trackops and CaseFleet.

## The Customization Gap
An investigator's version needs client-specific report templates and billing-guideline checks before an invoice is sent; evidence logging with hashes, timestamps and chain of custody for photos and video; licence, insurance and continuing-education tracking per state with renewal alerts; database search logs linked to each case for permissible-purpose audits; and a panel compliance pack generated on demand.

## Target Customer
Owner-investigators running agencies of one to ten people.

## Impact If Solved
Hours a week of administration move back to billable work, invoice rejections fall, and the agency can pass panel and database audits without a scramble.
