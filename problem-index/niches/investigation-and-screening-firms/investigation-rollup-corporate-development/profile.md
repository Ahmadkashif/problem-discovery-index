# Screening & Investigation Roll-up Corporate Development

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/investigation-and-screening-firms|Investigation & Background Screening Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Consolidators buy screening agencies and claims investigation firms: First Advantage absorbed Sterling and reported $55M of synergies actioned in 2025, HireRight went private under General Atlantic and Stone Point in 2024, and private equity platforms roll up regional investigation firms. Corporate development teams screen targets, model synergies and integrate.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~3-10 per acquirer (estimate)
**Output:** Acquisition screens, synergy models, integration plans
**Proprietary data:** Target diligence files under NDA
**Clock:** Deal timetables
**Buyer:** Head of Corporate Development
