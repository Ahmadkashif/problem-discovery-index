# The Post Deleted Between Finding and Capture

**Niche:** [[niches/investigation-and-screening-firms/social-media-evidence-capture/profile|Social Media & Open-Source Evidence Capture]]
**Industry:** [[industries/investigation-and-screening-firms|Investigation & Background Screening Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** An investigator spots a relevant post, finishes the search, comes back to capture it, and it is gone.
**Tags:** #change-point-detection #descriptive-statistics #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to find a subject's public posts and preserve them with provable authenticity before they are deleted or locked, and whoever delivers the most captured posts that survive an authentication challenge takes the account.

## The Problem
Claimants often tighten privacy settings or delete posts once they hire a lawyer or sense surveillance. Investigators who search first and capture later lose content; profiles switched to private between the desk report and the hearing leave only a screenshot of a thumbnail.

## Why It's Still Broken
Capture is a separate manual step after search, and nothing captures by default. Firms bill per report and see re-checks as unpaid time.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Capture every page viewed during a subject search automatically, with hashes and timestamps, and keep only what the investigator marks relevant. Re-check confirmed accounts on a schedule and flag deletions or privacy changes as events in the report, because a sudden lockdown after a claim is itself worth noting.

## Who Feels the Pain
Desk investigators re-doing searches; adjusters and defence counsel losing evidence; firms whose reports are challenged for missing context.

## Impact If Fixed
Relevant content is preserved the first time it is seen, at no extra investigator time.
