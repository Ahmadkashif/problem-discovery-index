# Social Listening Pointed at One Person

**Niche:** [[niches/investigation-and-screening-firms/social-media-evidence-capture/profile|Social Media & Open-Source Evidence Capture]]
**Industry:** [[industries/investigation-and-screening-firms|Investigation & Background Screening Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Social listening platforms monitor millions of posts about brands; an investigation needs every public post by one person, attributed and preserved, with clear limits on what may be viewed.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #k-nearest-neighbors #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to find a subject's public posts and preserve them with provable authenticity before they are deleted or locked, and whoever delivers the most captured posts that survive an authentication challenge takes the account.

## The Problem
Desk investigators search each platform by hand for a claimant, witness or litigant, try name variants and usernames, check photos to confirm identity, and then watch the accounts for new posts until a hearing. The work is repetitive and the monitoring is often dropped.

## What Already Exists
Social listening and media monitoring platforms (Brandwatch, Meltwater and others) with keyword search, alerts and dashboards; open-source investigation tools with username and image search.

## The Customization Gap
An investigation needs subject-centric search (name variants, known usernames, relatives and locations), identity confirmation against reference photos and facts, monitoring of confirmed accounts for new public content with capture on arrival, a hard rule against viewing non-public content or interacting with the subject, and output in the claims or legal report format with each item's provenance.

## Target Customer
Desk-investigation managers at claims investigation firms and litigation support investigators.

## Impact If Solved
A desk investigator can cover several times more subjects with continuous monitoring up to the hearing date, within ethical limits that are enforced by the tool rather than remembered.
