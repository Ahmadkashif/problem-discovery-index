# Claims Investigation Firms' Desk Intelligence Units

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/investigation-and-screening-firms|Investigation & Background Screening Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The national and PE-backed regional claims investigation firms centralise desk work in intelligence units: claimant background reports, social media canvasses, address and vehicle confirmation before surveillance, and prior-claims and litigation searches. Carriers, TPAs and self-insured employers buy these reports per item or bundle them with surveillance. The field side of the same firms is the operator layer analysed at [[niches/investigation-and-screening-firms/claims-investigation-surveillance/profile|🔵 Insurance Claims Investigation & Surveillance]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~15-80 desk investigators per national firm, each producing per-claimant reports (production); analysts working across claims — quality review, trend and vendor-yield reporting for carriers — number under 10 per firm (estimate, from the firms' public service descriptions, which present intelligence as a per-report service line rather than an analytics team)
**Output:** Background and social media reports per claimant; pre-surveillance intelligence packs
**Proprietary data:** Years of reports and surveillance outcomes, held per carrier client; the inputs are public content and the carrier's claim facts
**Clock:** Exam, deposition and hearing dates on the carrier's claims — dates that bind the claim, not the vendor
**Buyer:** Chief Operating Officer / Head of Intelligence at the investigation firm
