# The Package Nobody Has Re-Scoped Since It Was Signed

**Niche:** [[niches/investigation-and-screening-firms/enterprise-employment-screening/profile|Enterprise Employment Background Screening]]
**Industry:** [[industries/investigation-and-screening-firms|Investigation & Background Screening Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Screening packages are set at contract signature by job family and then left alone while laws, job duties and the employer's footprint change underneath them.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #decision-trees #compliance #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every competitor is fighting to return a complete, defensible report inside the employer's hiring window — but the high-volume hourly employer and the regulated employer buy on different evidence: one renews on the share of applicants cleared the same day, the other on never missing a disqualifying record, licence lapse or exclusion after hire. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
An employer picks packages for each job family when it signs: a seven-year county search for warehouse staff, credit reports for finance roles, motor vehicle records for drivers. Three years later the company hires in new states, some roles are remote, a city has restricted credit checks for most jobs, and a package still orders searches the employer may not lawfully use — or omits a licence check a new role requires.

## Why It's Still Broken
Re-scoping is nobody's job. The screener's account team is paid on volume, the employer's talent team inherited the configuration, and legal reviews it only after a demand letter. Package rules sit in the vendor's platform as configuration, not as documented decisions.

## What a Fix Looks Like
An annual, automated package review: map each package to the jobs and locations it is used for, check every component against current federal, state and local rules and the job's duties, flag unlawful, unnecessary and missing components, and record the decision and its reason. Draft justifications for job-relatedness where a component stays.

## Who Feels the Pain
Employment counsel defending class actions over credit checks or over-broad criminal searches; candidates screened for records the job does not warrant; screening account managers fielding audit questions they cannot answer.

## Impact If Fixed
A few hours a year per employer removes a common source of fair-chance and FCRA claims and trims spend on components nobody needs.
