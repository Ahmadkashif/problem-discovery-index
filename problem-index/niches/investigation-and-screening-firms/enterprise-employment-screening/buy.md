# Procurement Scorecards That Measure Only Speed

**Industry:** [[industries/investigation-and-screening-firms|Investigation & Background Screening Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Employers evaluate screening vendors with generic supplier-scorecard tools that track turnaround and cost per report and have no field for accuracy, disputes or adverse-action defensibility.

**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every competitor is fighting to return a complete, defensible report inside the employer's hiring window — but the high-volume hourly employer and the regulated employer buy on different evidence: one renews on the share of applicants cleared the same day, the other on never missing a disqualifying record, licence lapse or exclusion after hire. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
A screening RFP compares vendors on average turnaround, price, integration and references. Quarterly business reviews report the same numbers. Accuracy — wrong-person matches, missed records, dispositions misread, disputes upheld — is visible only when a candidate complains or a lawsuit arrives, and employers carry the litigation risk for decisions made on a vendor's error.

## What Already Exists
Supplier performance and vendor risk tools such as Coupa, SAP Ariba and third-party risk platforms, plus the screening firms' own client dashboards, which report turnaround by package and component.

## The Customization Gap
A screening scorecard needs measures generic supplier tools do not have: dispute rate and upheld-dispute rate per thousand reports, by component and jurisdiction; share of reports amended after delivery; turnaround distributions rather than averages, split by county; adverse action timeliness; and fair-chance process compliance for the employer's own side. It also needs a sampling audit — re-checking a random set of reports against the source — whose results feed renewal.

## Target Customer
Procurement and talent acquisition leaders running screening RFPs at large employers, and the screening firms willing to compete on audited accuracy.

## Impact If Solved
Vendors would be selected and renewed on the error that creates liability as well as on speed, and the screeners that invest in accuracy would finally be able to show it.
