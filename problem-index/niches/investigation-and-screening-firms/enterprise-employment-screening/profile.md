# Enterprise Employment Background Screening

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/investigation-and-screening-firms|Investigation & Background Screening Firms]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every competitor is fighting to return a complete, defensible report inside the employer's hiring window — but the high-volume hourly employer and the regulated employer buy on different evidence: one renews on the share of applicants cleared the same day, the other on never missing a disqualifying record, licence lapse or exclusion after hire. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$4.5B US pre-employment and workforce screening revenue, including screeners coded outside NAICS 561611 (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~55% of the industry's activity (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High — orders, consent, status and delivery run through APIs and applicant tracking integrations; court and verification fulfilment behind them does not
**Target Buyer:** VP of Talent Acquisition or Head of Global Screening at a large employer
**Automation Potential:** High for ordering, matching and status; medium for adjudication

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is where most of the industry's revenue sits: First Advantage reported $1.57B in 2025 with more than 80,000 customers and 200 million screens a year, and HireRight $722M in 2023. Employers buy a package — county and national criminal searches, sex offender registries, employment and education verification, motor vehicle records, drug tests — priced per report under multi-year contracts, and renew on turnaround, candidate experience and compliance record. The FCRA governs every report; state fair-chance laws govern what the employer may do with it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
First Advantage, HireRight, Checkr, Accurate, Cisive, Certiphi and Asurint, integrated into Workday, iCIMS, SAP SuccessFactors and Greenhouse. The gap is that one contract covers two different contests: the logistics employer hiring thousands of hourly workers a month, and the hospital or bank that must keep its workforce cleared after hire.
