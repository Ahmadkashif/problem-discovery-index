# Expungement Tools That Stop at the Courthouse Door

**Niche:** [[niches/investigation-and-screening-firms/candidates-with-records/profile|Candidates With Records]]
**Industry:** [[industries/investigation-and-screening-firms|Investigation & Background Screening Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Record-clearing eligibility tools help people petition to seal a record; nothing carries the granted order to the screeners and data aggregators that still sell the old one.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #seq2seq #compliance #data-integration #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a candidate's wrong, sealed or incomplete record corrected or explained before the employer's response window closes, and whoever resolves the most candidate challenges inside that window takes the account.

## The Problem
A person wins an expungement or benefits from an automatic sealing law. The court updates its own record. Screening firms that pull fresh from the court will stop reporting it; databases built from earlier bulk pulls, booking data and prior reports may not, and the person discovers this only when a report shows the old case.

## What Already Exists
Legal aid and civic-technology eligibility screeners and petition generators, state automatic record-clearing programmes, and screeners' dispute portals.

## The Customization Gap
The order needs to travel: a structured notice of sealing or expungement (court, case number, date, scope) sent with the person's authorisation to the major screeners and wholesale record vendors, tracked to confirmation, with a certificate the person can show an employer. It needs state-specific rules on what sealing means for each use (employment, licensing, housing) and a re-check when the person applies for jobs.

## Target Customer
Legal aid organisations and re-entry programmes, funded partly by fair-chance employers and screening firms that want fewer disputes.

## Impact If Solved
A record a court has sealed stops costing people jobs, and screeners avoid a class of FCRA accuracy claims.
