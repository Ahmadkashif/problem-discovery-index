# A Readiness Check Before the Application

**Niche:** [[niches/investigation-and-screening-firms/candidates-with-records/profile|Candidates With Records]]
**Industry:** [[industries/investigation-and-screening-firms|Investigation & Background Screening Firms]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A candidate with a record learns what the report says from a pre-adverse notice, with five business days to fix years of court paperwork.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #decision-trees #compliance #worker-facing #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a candidate's wrong, sealed or incomplete record corrected or explained before the employer's response window closes, and whoever resolves the most candidate challenges inside that window takes the account.

## The Problem
Someone with an old conviction, a dismissed charge or a common name applies for jobs without knowing what a background check will return. The first sight of the report is the employer's pre-adverse notice. By then the window to dispute an error, prove a sealing, or gather evidence of rehabilitation is a few business days, and court records from another county take longer than that to obtain.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Screening firms sell to employers, not candidates, and consumer file disclosure under the FCRA is a compliance obligation, not a product. Legal aid groups help with expungement but rarely see the commercial screening report. Nobody owns the candidate's preparation.

## What to Build
A candidate-side service, offered through fair-chance employers, workforce boards and re-entry programmes: request one's own consumer file from the major screeners, read the records it would contain, flag likely errors (wrong person, missing disposition, sealed or expunged record still reported, non-conviction older than seven years), check eligibility for sealing under the state's law, and assemble a mitigation packet ready for an individualized assessment. Disputes are filed before any application.

## Target Customer
Employers running fair-chance hiring programmes, re-entry and workforce organisations, and screening firms that want fewer contested reports.

## Impact If Built
Errors are corrected before they cost a job, sealing is used by more of the people eligible for it, and fair-chance employers see more of these candidates reach day one.
