# Third-Party Risk Platforms That Stop at the Screening Hit

**One-liner:** Third-party risk management platforms run questionnaires and watchlist screening for thousands of suppliers, then hand the risky few to an investigation firm by email and get a PDF back.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #compliance #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to surface the disqualifying fact about a person or counterparty that a database screen misses before the signing or appointment date, and whoever delivers the most corroborated red flags inside that date takes the account.

## The Problem
A multinational's compliance team onboards agents, distributors and joint-venture partners through a third-party risk platform. High-risk third parties are escalated to an outside firm for enhanced due diligence. The order, scope and report travel by email; the findings come back as narrative that someone re-keys into the platform as a risk rating, losing the detail.

## What Already Exists
Third-party risk management platforms (Aravo, Coupa Risk Aware, Diligent, LexisNexis and others) with questionnaires, screening integrations and workflow; investigation firms' client portals.

## The Customization Gap
The hand-off needs a structured order (subject, scope tier, jurisdictions, specific questions), status visibility through the investigation, and findings returned as structured red flags — category, severity, source, corroboration level — mapped to the client's risk taxonomy and remediation actions, with the narrative report attached. It also needs re-investigation triggers tied to contract renewal and to new screening hits.

## Target Customer
Chief compliance officers at companies with anti-corruption programmes and the investigation firms that serve them.

## Impact If Solved
Escalated due diligence feeds directly into onboarding decisions and monitoring, and findings are comparable across hundreds of third parties instead of buried in PDFs.
