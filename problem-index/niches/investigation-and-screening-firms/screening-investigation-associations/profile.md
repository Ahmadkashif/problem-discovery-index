# Screening & Investigation Trade Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/investigation-and-screening-firms|Investigation & Background Screening Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Professional Background Screening Association (~600–900 member companies by conflicting counts) runs the Background Screening Credentialing Council's accreditation programme, lobbies on FCRA and court-access issues and publishes occasional member surveys. The National Council of Investigation and Security Services, the International Association of Special Investigation Units and state investigator associations offer training, continuing education, legislative tracking and referral listservs.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research staff per organisation (estimate); most are run by small staffs and volunteer committees
**Output:** Accreditation audits, member surveys, conference programmes, legislative alerts
**Proprietary data:** Member surveys and accreditation files
**Clock:** Legislative sessions and annual conferences
**Buyer:** Executive Director
