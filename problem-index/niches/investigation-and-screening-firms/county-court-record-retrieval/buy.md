# Field-Service Dispatch Adapted to Courthouse Runners

**Niche:** [[niches/investigation-and-screening-firms/county-court-record-retrieval/profile|County Court Record Retrieval]]
**Industry:** [[industries/investigation-and-screening-firms|Investigation & Background Screening Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Field-service and freelance dispatch platforms route technicians to jobs; court runners need routing to courthouses with clerk limits, terminal hours and per-search evidence that those platforms do not model.
**Tags:** #optimization-fundamentals #dynamic-programming #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to return a county criminal search with the full disposition and the identifiers needed to match it inside the screener's turnaround window, from courts that restrict or refuse online access, and whoever covers the most slow and restricted counties without a re-pull takes the account.

## The Problem
A retriever receives hundreds of search requests a day for courts across several states. Coordinators batch them by courthouse and send lists to runners by email or text; runners visit, search at public terminals or submit to clerks, type results into a spreadsheet or portal, and are paid per search. Missed batches, clerks who cap requests per day and terminals closed for lunch push searches into the next day.

## What Already Exists
Field service management and freelance dispatch platforms (job assignment, mobile apps, proof of completion, payment) and general route optimisation.

## The Customization Gap
Runner dispatch needs a court calendar model (terminal hours, holidays, per-day request limits, which searches need clerk handling versus self-service), batching against each order's turnaround deadline, mobile capture of the screen or docket image with time and location as proof of what was seen, structured entry of charge and disposition fields with validation, and per-search payment reconciled to the screener's billing.

## Target Customer
Operations managers at court-runner networks and screening firms that run their own runners.

## Impact If Solved
More searches clear on the first visit, results arrive structured instead of typed, and the retriever can prove what the court showed if a result is disputed.
