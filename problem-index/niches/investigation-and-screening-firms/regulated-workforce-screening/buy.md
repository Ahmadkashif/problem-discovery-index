# Physician Credentialing Tools Stretched to the Whole Workforce

**Niche:** [[niches/investigation-and-screening-firms/regulated-workforce-screening/profile|Regulated-Workforce Screening & Monitoring]]
**Industry:** [[industries/investigation-and-screening-firms|Investigation & Background Screening Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Healthcare credentialing software verifies physicians' licences, boards and sanctions well and was never built for the aides, drivers, techs and contractors who make up most of a regulated workforce.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #compliance #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to catch every disqualifying event on a workforce already hired — a new arrest, a lapsed or disciplined licence, an exclusion-list entry — and whoever raises the fewest false alerts while missing none that an auditor or surveyor later finds takes the account.

## The Problem
A hospital's medical staff office runs physicians through a credentialing platform with primary-source verification and ongoing monitoring. Its thousands of other employees — nurses, aides, transport staff, environmental services, agency contractors — are checked at hire by a screening firm and then tracked in HR spreadsheets of licence expiry dates, with monitoring bought as a separate add-on that nobody reconciles.

## What Already Exists
Credentialing and verification platforms built for medical staff offices and credentials verification organisations; screening firms' monitoring add-ons; exclusion screening services.

## The Customization Gap
Extending to the whole workforce needs role-based requirement profiles (which roles need which licences, lists, motor vehicle checks and drug tests, at what frequency, under which state rules), ingestion of agency and contractor rosters, licence board feeds for allied professions, a single eligibility status per person that HR, scheduling and payroll can read, and escalation rules that block scheduling of someone no longer eligible.

## Target Customer
VPs of HR and compliance officers at multi-state hospital systems and home health agencies.

## Impact If Solved
One eligibility record per person replaces three disconnected processes, and the employer can show a surveyor that nobody worked a shift while ineligible.
