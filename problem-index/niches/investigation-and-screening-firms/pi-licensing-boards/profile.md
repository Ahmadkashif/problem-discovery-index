# State Private Investigator Licensing Boards

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/investigation-and-screening-firms|Investigation & Background Screening Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State agencies license private investigators and agencies — examinations, experience requirements, fingerprinting, insurance, continuing education and complaints — among them California's Bureau of Security and Investigative Services, the Texas Private Security Program and Florida's Division of Licensing. A handful of states have no statewide licence (a 2005 count found 13, including Idaho, Mississippi, South Dakota and Wyoming), leaving it to cities.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Licensing and enforcement staff are production; analytical staff under 10 per state (estimate)
**Output:** Licences, disciplinary actions, licensee lookups
**Proprietary data:** Licensee and complaint records, largely public
**Clock:** Renewal cycles and statutory complaint timelines
**Buyer:** Bureau Chief
