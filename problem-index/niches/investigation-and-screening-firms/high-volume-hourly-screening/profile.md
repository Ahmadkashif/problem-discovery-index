# High-Volume Hourly & Gig Screening

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/investigation-and-screening-firms|Investigation & Background Screening Firms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to clear an hourly or gig applicant before that applicant accepts another offer, and whoever returns the largest share of complete, accurate reports within one business day takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.5B screening spend by retail, logistics, staffing, hospitality and gig-platform employers (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~30% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High — mobile consent, API ordering and status; fulfilment still waits on courts
**Target Buyer:** Head of Talent Acquisition Operations at a high-volume employer; Head of Trust & Safety at a gig platform
**Automation Potential:** High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The applicant is applying to several jobs at once and the employer measures time from offer to first shift in days. Volumes run to hundreds of thousands of reports a year per customer, the package is narrow (criminal searches, registries, often motor vehicle records for drivers) and price per report is low. A report held open by one slow county is a lost hire. The platform side of this market, where the gig company is the buyer, is analysed in [[niches/gig-delivery-platforms/onboarding-and-background-checks/profile|Onboarding & Background Checks]]; this niche is the screening firm's fulfilment of it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Checkr, First Advantage, HireRight and Accurate dominate, with applicant tracking integrations and candidate-facing mobile flows. Gaps: applicants enter incomplete or wrong identifiers (maiden names, prior addresses, a mistyped date of birth), which stalls matching downstream; employers see a report status, not which component is holding it or when it will clear; and stand-alone disclosure forms drift out of compliance as state notices are added.
