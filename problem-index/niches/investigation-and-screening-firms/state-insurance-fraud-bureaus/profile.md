# State Insurance Fraud Bureaus

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/investigation-and-screening-firms|Investigation & Background Screening Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State fraud bureaus and divisions inside insurance departments receive suspected-fraud referrals from insurers' special investigation units, investigate with sworn staff, and refer cases to prosecutors. California's Insurance Code requires insurers to report a claim they believe fraudulent to the Fraud Division within 60 days, and workers' compensation fraud to the Fraud Division and the district attorney within 60 days. The bureaus are the end point of much of the evidence this industry's claims investigators produce. The industry-funded side (the National Insurance Crime Bureau and insurer SIUs) is profiled at [[niches/chiropractic-practices/insurance-fraud-investigation-bureaus/profile|Insurance Fraud Investigation Bureaus]] (37).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-40 analysts per large state bureau (estimate); sworn investigators are case staff
**Output:** Case referrals to prosecutors, fraud trend reports to legislatures
**Proprietary data:** Statutory SIU referrals and investigative files
**Clock:** Statutory referral windows (binding insurers) and prosecution timelines
**Buyer:** Deputy Commissioner for Fraud / Chief of the Fraud Division
