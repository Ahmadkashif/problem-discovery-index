# Claims Vendor Management Without Investigation Logic

**Niche:** [[niches/investigation-and-screening-firms/claims-investigation-surveillance/profile|Insurance Claims Investigation & Surveillance]]
**Industry:** [[industries/investigation-and-screening-firms|Investigation & Background Screening Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Claims platforms assign and pay vendors generically; investigations need hour authorisations, licence checks per state, video delivery and guideline audits that the generic vendor module does not know about.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #compliance #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to put claim-changing evidence on the file before the claim's next decision date — the independent medical exam, the deposition, the hearing or the settlement conference — inside the carrier's hour caps, and whoever produces the most usable activity per billed hour takes the account.

## The Problem
An adjuster orders surveillance from inside the claims system; the order reaches the vendor by email or portal; the authorisation (hours, days, budget) is a line of text; the report and video come back as attachments; the invoice is checked by a person against billing guidelines. Re-authorisations, licence and insurance certificates for each state, and video storage are handled outside the system.

## What Already Exists
Guidewire ClaimCenter and Duck Creek Claims vendor management, independent-adjuster and appraisal marketplaces, and legal e-billing platforms that audit law firm invoices against guidelines.

## The Customization Gap
Investigation needs: an authorisation object with hour and day caps, conditional extensions and expiry; vendor and investigator licence validation by state at assignment; video intake with hashing, retention rules and secure sharing with defence counsel; report templates per line of business; invoice audit against the carrier's investigation guidelines (mileage, report time, video minutes); and results fields structured enough to measure yield.

## Target Customer
Claims operations and SIU leaders at carriers already on a core claims platform.

## Impact If Solved
Investigation ordering, authorisation, delivery and billing move into one auditable flow, removing manual invoice review and giving the carrier data it can manage vendors on.
