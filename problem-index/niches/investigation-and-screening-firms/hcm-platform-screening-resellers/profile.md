# Payroll & HCM Platforms Reselling Screening

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/investigation-and-screening-firms|Investigation & Background Screening Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Payroll and human capital management platforms sell background checks to their small and mid-sized employer customers inside the hiring workflow, either reselling a screening firm's product or operating as an accredited screener themselves: Paycom announced Background Screening Credentialing Council accreditation in April 2025. The employer buys the check as a line item next to payroll; the platform earns a margin and keeps the customer inside its suite.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 screening product and compliance analysts per platform (estimate)
**Output:** Embedded screening packages, adverse action workflows, compliance content for SMB employers
**Proprietary data:** Order and outcome data held for employer customers
**Clock:** Employers' hiring dates
**Buyer:** VP of Product for talent acquisition modules
