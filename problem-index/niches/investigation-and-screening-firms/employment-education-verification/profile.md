# Employment, Education & Licence Verification

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/investigation-and-screening-firms|Investigation & Background Screening Firms]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to close an employment, education or licence verification without a human phone call, and whoever answers the most verifications from direct, instant sources takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$800M a year of verification components sold inside background screening packages, before database pass-through fees (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — instant databases cover many large employers and most colleges; the rest is phone, email and fax
**Target Buyer:** VP of Operations at a screening firm
**Automation Potential:** High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Verifying that a candidate worked where they said, earned the degree they listed and holds the licence they claim is the most mechanical part of a background check, and the part that most often holds a report open. Instant sources — Equifax's The Work Number, the National Student Clearinghouse, licensing boards' online lookups — answer many requests at a pass-through fee; the rest go to verification specialists who call, email and wait. The verification database business itself, sold to lenders, landlords and agencies, is analysed under [[niches/credit-bureaus/employment-income-verification/profile|Employment & Income Verification]]; this niche is the screener's job of closing each verification by whatever route works. The database operators' insight layer is logged as [[niches/credit-bureaus/employment-verification-databases/profile|Employment & Income Verification Databases]] (36, kill switch).

## Current Tools & Gaps
Instant databases, third-party verifiers that employers outsource to, screeners' internal contact books, and offshore verification teams. Gaps: route choice is left to each specialist; contact knowledge built over years is not shared; employers acquired or closed leave records with unknown custodians; and international verifications are slow and manual.
