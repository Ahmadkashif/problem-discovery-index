# Voice Agents Adapted to Verification Calls

**Niche:** [[niches/investigation-and-screening-firms/employment-education-verification/profile|Employment, Education & Licence Verification]]
**Industry:** [[industries/investigation-and-screening-firms|Investigation & Background Screening Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Contact-centre voice agents can now hold a scripted phone call; verification calls need release handling, recording-consent rules, IVR navigation and structured capture of dates and titles that general agents lack.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #seq2seq #compliance #automation #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to close an employment, education or licence verification without a human phone call, and whoever answers the most verifications from direct, instant sources takes the account.

## The Problem
Much of a verification specialist's day is calls that follow the same script: reach HR or the registrar, state the request, offer the candidate's signed release, ask for dates, title and eligibility for rehire or degree and graduation date, and record the answer. Hold queues, phone trees and voicemail consume most of the time.

## What Already Exists
AI voice agent platforms sold to contact centres for scheduling, collections and customer service, with telephony integration, transcription and structured outputs.

## The Customization Gap
Verification needs: navigating employers' phone trees and holding for HR; sending the candidate's release by fax or email mid-call when asked; complying with two-party recording consent in some states; capturing dates, titles and degree details into structured fields with the source person's name; recognising when to stop and hand off (an employer that only verifies through a third party, a request for a subpoena); and an audit trail showing the information came from the employer, as the FCRA accuracy standard expects.

## Target Customer
Verification operations heads at screening firms and their outsourced verification centres.

## Impact If Solved
Routine calls and follow-ups run unattended, specialists handle the difficult cases, and turnaround on the slowest report component falls.
