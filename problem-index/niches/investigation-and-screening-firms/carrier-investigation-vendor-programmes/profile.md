# Carrier & TPA Investigation Vendor Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/investigation-and-screening-firms|Investigation & Background Screening Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
[[industries/property-casualty-insurers|Insurers']] and TPAs' claims and SIU departments run the panels this industry sells into: approving investigation vendors, setting billing guidelines and hour caps, auditing invoices and reports, and allocating surveillance and field assignments. Their fraud analytics and referral work is profiled under [[niches/chiropractic-practices/insurance-fraud-investigation-bureaus/profile|Insurance Fraud Investigation Bureaus]] (37) and [[niches/insurance-tpa/claims-fraud-analytics-vendors/profile|Claims Fraud Analytics Vendors]] (46); this pocket is the vendor-programme function alone.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 vendor-management and invoice-audit analysts per carrier or TPA (estimate)
**Output:** Panel rosters, vendor scorecards, billing guidelines, invoice audits
**Proprietary data:** Assignment, invoice and result history across vendors — the carrier's own claim data
**Clock:** Claim events and annual panel reviews
**Buyer:** SIU Director / Claims Vendor Manager
