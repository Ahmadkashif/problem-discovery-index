# Workplace Drug Testing Labs' Index Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/investigation-and-screening-firms|Investigation & Background Screening Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The laboratories that process workplace drug tests ordered through screening firms publish annual positivity reports from their test volumes, the best known being Quest Diagnostics' Drug Testing Index. The reports are free marketing and policy material; the labs' revenue is the tests.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analysts behind the published index (estimate)
**Output:** Annual positivity trend reports and press releases
**Proprietary data:** De-identified results from millions of employer-ordered tests
**Clock:** Self-set annual publication
**Buyer:** Head of Employer Solutions marketing at the lab
