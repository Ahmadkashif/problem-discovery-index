# Executive & Board Candidate Vetting Practices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/investigation-and-screening-firms|Investigation & Background Screening Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Investigation firms' executive screening practices produce in-depth background reports on finalists for C-suite and board roles — litigation, regulatory history, media, business affiliations, credential checks and discreet reference work — for executive search firms, boards and private equity owners. Mintz Group has been the AESC's due diligence partner for more than 12 years, and the AESC–Mintz 2024 survey found screenings ended a candidacy or raised concern in 22% of searches. The search firms left this work to this industry: see [[industries/executive-search-firms|Executive Search Firms]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-30 research analysts in the executive screening practice of a leading firm, excluding field researchers and reference interviewers (estimate)
**Output:** Candidate due diligence reports, staged from light public-record reviews to full investigations
**Proprietary data:** Prior reports on executives and board members, many of whom recur across searches; inputs are public records, press and source interviews
**Clock:** Appointment, announcement and proxy dates set by the client and its board
**Buyer:** Head of Executive Screening Practice
