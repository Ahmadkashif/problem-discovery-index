# Screening & Investigation Software Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/investigation-and-screening-firms|Investigation & Background Screening Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Platform vendors sell the software small screening agencies run on (Tazworks, Accio Data, ClearStar), case management to investigators (Trackops, CaseFleet) and open-source investigation tools to desk investigators. Their research is product and market work for a niche customer base.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 product research and analytics staff per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Software releases, integration catalogues, occasional industry surveys
**Proprietary data:** Usage and order data held for customers
**Clock:** Release cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Product Officer
