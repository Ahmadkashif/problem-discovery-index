# Litigation Support Investigation Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/investigation-and-screening-firms|Investigation & Background Screening Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Investigators retained by plaintiff and defence law firms locate and interview witnesses, canvass scenes, research the opposing party's background and assets, find prior claims and lawsuits, and support trial preparation and judgment collection. The larger firms employ teams of former police detectives, journalists and researchers; most of the market is small agencies analysed at [[niches/investigation-and-screening-firms/solo-small-pi-agencies/profile|🟣 Solo & Small PI Agencies]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-40 investigators and researchers at the larger litigation-focused firms, all working cases (production); analytical staff separate from case work under 10 per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Witness statements and locate reports, background and asset reports, investigative memoranda to counsel
**Proprietary data:** Case files and interview notes held for counsel
**Clock:** Counsel's court schedules — discovery cutoffs, disclosure deadlines and trial dates that bind the case, not the investigator
**Buyer:** Managing Director / Head of Litigation Investigations
