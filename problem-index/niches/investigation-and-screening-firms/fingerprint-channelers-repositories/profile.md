# Fingerprint Channelers & State Criminal History Repositories

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/investigation-and-screening-firms|Investigation & Background Screening Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Fingerprint-based checks, required by law for many regulated jobs, run through state criminal history repositories and FBI-approved private channelers that capture prints and route submissions. Screening firms and employers order them alongside name-based searches; disposition completeness in the repositories sets how useful the result is.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Production staff capture and process submissions; analytical staff under 10 per organisation (estimate)
**Output:** Fingerprint-based record responses and clearance letters
**Proprietary data:** State arrest and disposition records, held under statute
**Clock:** Statutory clearance requirements for regulated roles
**Buyer:** Repository Bureau Chief / channeler operations head
