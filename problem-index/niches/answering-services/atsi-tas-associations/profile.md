# ATSI & Answering-Service Trade Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/answering-services|Telephone Answering Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Association of TeleServices International (ATSI), founded in 1942, represents live answering services in North America. It runs the annual conference, site and agent certification, and the Award of Excellence, for which independent judges place test calls over about six months. Regional associations and the trade magazines Connections and AnswerStat sit alongside it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** A small association staff, well under ten (estimate); judging is contracted out
**Output:** Certifications, awards, conference programming, member communications
**Proprietary data:** Award test-call scores and certification records for participating members
**Clock:** Annual conference and award cycle (self-set)
**Buyer:** Executive director / board
