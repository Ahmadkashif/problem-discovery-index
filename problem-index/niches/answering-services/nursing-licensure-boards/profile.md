# NCSBN & State Boards of Nursing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/answering-services|Telephone Answering Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State boards of nursing license the registered nurses who staff telephone triage lines, and the National Council of State Boards of Nursing (NCSBN) administers the Nurse Licensure Compact, enacted in 41 states and two territories as of October 2025, and the Nursys licence-verification database. Whether a triage nurse may advise a caller in another state depends on their rules.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-30 research staff at NCSBN, working mainly on licensure examination and workforce studies (estimate); board licensing staff are production
**Output:** Workforce surveys, examination research, compact implementation guidance, licensure verification
**Proprietary data:** National licensure and discipline records
**Clock:** Statutory compact implementation dates and state legislative sessions
**Buyer:** NCSBN chief executive / research director
