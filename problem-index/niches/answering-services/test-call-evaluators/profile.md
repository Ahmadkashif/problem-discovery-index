# Test-Call & Mystery-Shop Evaluators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/answering-services|Telephone Answering Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Small firms and independent judges who place scripted test calls to answering services, for ATSI's Award of Excellence or for clients checking their own service, and score answer speed, courtesy, accuracy and account knowledge.

## Insight Function
**Size:** A few evaluators per firm (estimate); callers are production
**Output:** Scored test-call reports
**Proprietary data:** Test-call scores over time for the services evaluated
**Clock:** Award and client review cycles
**Buyer:** Owner
