# Nurse Triage Providers' Clinical Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/answering-services|Telephone Answering Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Specialist after-hours nurse triage providers (TriageLogic, Fonemed, Conduit Health Partners and others) take overflow and after-hours symptom calls for hundreds of practices and health systems, using Schmitt-Thompson protocols and nurses licensed across many states. Practices pay per call or per minute; health systems pay for a branded nurse line. The larger providers run clinical quality and reporting teams that produce disposition and utilisation reports for clients.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-40 clinical informatics, protocol-QI and reporting staff at the largest providers (estimate); the triage nurses and QA auditors themselves are production
**Output:** Client utilisation and disposition reports; protocol quality-improvement findings; call-volume analytics
**Proprietary data:** Millions of structured triage encounters with complaint, protocol, override and disposition across many practices, though the outcome after the call sits in each practice's records
**Clock:** None external beyond the call itself; client contract reviews
**Buyer:** Chief medical officer or VP of clinical operations

**Kill switches:** live — HIPAA. Every encounter is protected health information held as a business associate of the practice or health system, and reuse beyond each client's own reporting needs that client's agreement.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. Clients pay for answered triage calls, not for the analysis, and the cross-practice corpus that would make the analysis valuable is fenced by business associate agreements.
