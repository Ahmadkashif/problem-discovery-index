# Answering-Service Platform Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/answering-services|Telephone Answering Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Startel (which absorbed Professional Teledata in 2015 and Alston Tascom in 2017), Amtelco (Infinity and the software-only Genesis, which Amtelco says runs at nearly two hundred call centres) and Telescan sell the switching, scripting, on-call, messaging and billing platforms answering services run on, premise-based or hosted.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Product, engineering and support teams; no dedicated research or insight function of ten (estimate)
**Output:** Software licences, hosting and support
**Proprietary data:** None of their own; the calls, scripts and messages on their platforms belong to the answering services and their clients
**Clock:** Customer upgrade cycles
**Buyer:** Product leadership
