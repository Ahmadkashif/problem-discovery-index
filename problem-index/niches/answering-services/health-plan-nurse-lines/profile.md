# Health Plan Nurse Advice Lines

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/answering-services|Telephone Answering Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Health insurers and Medicaid managed-care plans run or contract 24/7 nurse advice lines for members, partly to steer callers away from unnecessary emergency visits and partly because state Medicaid contracts require them. The lines compete with practice after-hours services for the same 2 a.m. call, and the plans' analytics teams measure the lines' effect on utilisation.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Only a few analysts work on the nurse line itself (estimate); the plans' wider care-management and utilisation analytics teams, ~20-200 people at large plans, serve the whole book of business
**Output:** Internal utilisation and avoided-ED analyses; contract reports to state Medicaid agencies
**Proprietary data:** Nurse-line encounters joined to the plan's own claims, so the outcome after each call is visible, which no answering service or triage provider has
**Clock:** State Medicaid contract reporting cycles
**Buyer:** VP of care management or medical economics
