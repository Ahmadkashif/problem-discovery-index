# Field-Service Booking Built for an Outside Operator

**Niche:** [[niches/answering-services/trades-after-hours-dispatch/profile|Home Services & Trades Answering]]
**Industry:** [[industries/answering-services|Telephone Answering Services]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Field-service platforms give the contractor's own customer service reps a booking screen with zones, skills and capacity; the answering service working the same calls at night gets a phone number to text.
**Tags:** #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #large-language-models #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn each after-hours call to a contractor into either a booked job on the contractor's dispatch board or a page for a genuine emergency, and whoever books the most billable jobs without waking a technician for a non-emergency takes the account.

## The Problem
Daytime calls at a mid-size contractor are booked directly into the dispatch board by in-house reps who see technician capacity, service zones and membership status. At night the answering service sees none of this, so it takes a message, and the morning dispatcher calls the customer back, by which time some have booked elsewhere.

## What Already Exists
ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro and Jobber booking interfaces and APIs; online booking widgets; AI voice agents that integrate with the same calendars.

## The Customization Gap
An outside operator needs: scoped booking credentials per contractor, limited to the job types and windows the contractor allows; the contractor's emergency rules (temperature thresholds, active-water definitions, member-priority rules) expressed as checks the operator screen enforces; dispatch-fee quoting from the contractor's price book; and a hand-off that writes the call summary into the job. All of this must work across dozens of contractors on three or four platforms.

## Target Customer
Answering services serving residential trades; platform vendors' integration partners.

## Impact If Solved
Night calls become booked jobs instead of morning callbacks. That recovers revenue the contractor currently loses, and gives the live service parity with AI agents on the booking itself.
