# Home Services & Trades Answering

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/answering-services|Telephone Answering Services]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn each after-hours call to a contractor into either a booked job on the contractor's dispatch board or a page for a genuine emergency, and whoever books the most billable jobs without waking a technician for a non-emergency takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$350M a year of live answering for HVAC, plumbing, electrical, restoration and similar contractors (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% of answering-service receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium and rising fast — contractors run ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro or Jobber, but many answering services still deliver a text message that someone re-keys
**Target Buyer:** Contractor owner or call-centre manager at a multi-truck residential service company
**Automation Potential:** High — the most exposed answering-service segment to AI voice agents

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
For a residential [[industries/hvac-contractors|HVAC]] or [[industries/plumbing-contractors|plumbing]] contractor, an after-hours call is revenue: a no-heat call on a January night is a premium-rate job if booked and a competitor's job if not. It is also a cost: every page wakes a technician who is paid on-call premiums and will quit if woken for thermostat questions. The answering service is judged on conversion and on the technician's sleep, which is a different contest from medical answering. It is fought against AI voice agents built for trades (Sameday, Avoca, Rosie) and the AI built into ServiceTitan.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Live services with trades scripts; AI voice agents that book into the field-service calendar; field-service platforms with call booking. Gaps: the live service rarely writes the job into the dispatch board; emergency rules ("no heat below 40 degrees", "active water only") are prose; nobody measures booked revenue per after-hours call by service.
