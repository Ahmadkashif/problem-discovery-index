## The Problem
In a hospital, the on-call schedule, the paging system and the clinician's secure-messaging app are connected, and an operator console shows who is on and whether they have acknowledged. An independent practice has none of this. Its schedule lives in a spreadsheet or a scheduler the answering service cannot read, its physicians carry a mix of pagers and personal phones, and acknowledgement is a callback nobody logs.

## What Already Exists
Hospital clinical communication suites: Spok's Care Connect (contact-centre console, paging and secure texting), clinician messaging platforms, and physician schedulers such as Amion and QGenda. Answering-service platforms add their own secure-messaging apps.

## The Customization Gap
Adaptation for the small practice means: a schedule connector that reads whatever the practice uses; per-physician acknowledgement with timeouts and backup chains set by the practice; delivery to EHR task inboxes for non-urgent messages; and pricing and setup a five-physician group can absorb without an IT department. The answering service, not the practice, operates the integrated stack as part of its service.

## Target Customer
Answering services serving independent and small multi-specialty groups, and the platform vendors that supply them.

## Impact If Solved
The practice gets hospital-grade escalation reliability without hospital infrastructure. Misdirected and unacknowledged pages, the failure mode behind the negligence cases, become visible and timed.
