# AAACN & Telehealth Nursing Standards Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/answering-services|Telephone Answering Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The American Academy of Ambulatory Care Nursing (AAACN) publishes scope and standards for telehealth nursing practice, including telephone triage, and runs education and a member forum where triage nurses discuss licensure and protocol questions. Smaller groups and accreditors cover health call centres.

## Insight Function
**Size:** A small professional-society staff with volunteer committees, under ten dedicated (estimate)
**Output:** Practice standards, courses, conference content
**Proprietary data:** None of substance beyond member surveys
**Clock:** Self-set revision cycles
**Buyer:** Executive director
