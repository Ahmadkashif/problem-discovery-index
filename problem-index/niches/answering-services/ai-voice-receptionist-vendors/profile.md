# AI Voice Agent & Virtual Receptionist Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/answering-services|Telephone Answering Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors selling AI-answered or AI-plus-human receptionist services directly to the answering industry's clients: trades-specific voice agents (Sameday, Avoca, Rosie and others), hybrid services such as Smith.ai, and AI built into field-service and practice software. They compete with live answering services on routine calls and increasingly sell to answering services as a technology layer.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-60 ML, conversation-design and analytics staff per vendor (estimate); this is product engineering, not a research function
**Output:** The voice agent itself; call analytics dashboards for clients
**Proprietary data:** Recordings and transcripts of the calls they answer, with booking outcomes where integrated
**Clock:** None external
**Buyer:** CTO or head of AI

**Kill switches:** live — data isn't theirs to use. The calls are the clients' conversations with their own customers under service terms, and no agreement permitting aggregate research use was checked; medical deployments add HIPAA.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. Clients pay for answered calls. The insight that would matter (which escalation decisions were right) is exactly what these products cannot yet see.
