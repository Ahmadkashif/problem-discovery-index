# Telephone Triage Protocol Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/answering-services|Telephone Answering Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Authors and publishers of the symptom-based protocols triage nurses follow. Schmitt-Thompson protocols, created and maintained by Drs Barton Schmitt and David Thompson, are licensed through software and service vendors such as TriageLogic and ClearTriage. The publisher reported use in over 400 call centres and more than 20 million calls a year as of 2015, with annual updates. Print protocol texts for nurses fill the rest of the market.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 dedicated editorial staff (estimate); the 100-plus expert reviewers are part-time volunteers and the quality-improvement input comes from a handful of client call centres
**Output:** Annually updated adult, pediatric, after-hours and office-hours protocol sets, licensed per seat or per centre
**Proprietary data:** The protocol content and its revision history; not the outcomes of the calls it governs
**Clock:** Self-set annual update cycle
**Buyer:** Protocol authors / licensing partner
