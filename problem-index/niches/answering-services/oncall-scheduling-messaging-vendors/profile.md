# On-Call Scheduling & Clinical Messaging Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/answering-services|Telephone Answering Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors of physician scheduling (Amion, QGenda), hospital paging and clinical communications (Spok, which reported ~720,000 paging units in service at the end of 2024) and secure messaging (including answering-service apps such as Amtelco Secure Messages). They sell to hospitals, medical groups and answering services; their product holds who was on call and whether they answered.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-50 analytics and data staff per vendor, mostly building product reporting (estimate)
**Output:** In-product dashboards (coverage, response times, workload) and occasional benchmark reports for marketing
**Proprietary data:** Schedules, pages and acknowledgement events across thousands of clinicians, the timing record that would show how fast on-call physicians actually respond
**Clock:** None external; client renewals
**Buyer:** VP product or head of analytics

**Kill switches:** live — data isn't theirs to use. Schedules and messages belong to the hospital or group under its contract, and message content is protected health information held under business associate agreements.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. A software business whose analytics serve its own product, sitting on client-owned timing data.
