# Contact-Centre Speech Analytics Sized for a Fifty-Seat Service

**Niche:** [[niches/answering-services/call-quality-scoring/profile|Call Quality & Escalation Compliance Scoring]]
**Industry:** [[industries/answering-services|Telephone Answering Services]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Enterprise contact centres already auto-score every interaction; the products are priced, configured and trained for one large client's queue, not for a small service answering for a thousand businesses.
**Tags:** #bert #transformers #evaluation-metrics #compliance #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to prove, call by call, that each client's script was followed and every escalation was made inside its time limit, and whoever can put that evidence for every call in front of the client at renewal takes the account.

## The Problem
Speech analytics and automated QA tools score sentiment, script adherence and compliance phrases across all calls in large centres. Answering services could use the same capability but cannot justify enterprise pricing or the configuration effort.

## What Already Exists
Contact-centre speech-analytics and automated-QA platforms sold to enterprises and BPOs, with transcription, categorisation and scorecards.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: account-level rule sets loaded from the TAS scripts rather than one global scorecard; escalation-timing checks that join the recording to paging and acknowledgement logs; HIPAA identity-verification and disclosure checks; per-client reporting a service can hand to its own customers; and pricing per seat that suits services of 10-100 operators.

## Target Customer
Multi-site answering services and the TAS platform vendors that could resell a configured version.

## Impact If Solved
Proven technology reaches the services that need it most, without building from scratch, and closes the gap between "we record everything" and "we know how every call went".
