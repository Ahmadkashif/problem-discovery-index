# Call Quality & Escalation Compliance Scoring

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/answering-services|Telephone Answering Services]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to prove, call by call, that each client's script was followed and every escalation was made inside its time limit, and whoever can put that evidence for every call in front of the client at renewal takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$60M a year of QA staff time, recording and scoring tools, test-call programmes and HIPAA audit work at answering services (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% of answering-service receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — every call is recorded, but review is a supervisor listening to a small sample against a generic rubric
**Target Buyer:** Quality or operations manager at a multi-site answering service; compliance officer for HIPAA accounts
**Automation Potential:** Very high — speech recognition and classification can score the full population against each account's rules

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Quality in this industry is defined per client. The right call is the one that followed that account's script and escalated on time, not one that sounded pleasant. The industry's own external benchmark, ATSI's Award of Excellence, has independent judges place test calls over about six months and score response time, courtesy, accuracy, account knowledge and overall impression. That is a credential, not a measurement of the calls clients actually send. HIPAA adds a second dimension for medical accounts: identity checks, minimum-necessary disclosure and secure delivery. Full-population scoring for BPO contact centres is covered in [[niches/digital-bpo-operations/full-population-scoring/profile|Full-Population Resolution Scoring]]; the difference here is scoring against hundreds of client-specific rule sets rather than one client's resolution target. Contestants are TAS platform QA modules, contact-centre speech-analytics vendors moving down-market, and test-call evaluators.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Call recording and screen capture in the TAS platforms; supervisor scorecards; ATSI test calls; occasional client mystery calls. Gaps: a sample of a few calls per operator per month; rubrics that cannot see account rules; no escalation-timing audit; no client-facing compliance report.
