# Contact-Centre Benchmarking & Research Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/answering-services|Telephone Answering Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Independent research and benchmarking firms such as ContactBabel and SQM Group sell benchmark reports, first-call-resolution studies and certifications to contact-centre operators and the vendors selling to them. ContactBabel publishes survey-based industry guides; SQM benchmarks centres from the post-call surveys it runs for its clients. Answering services are a small, rarely separated segment in their samples.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under ten per firm on benchmarking analytics (estimate); ContactBabel is essentially a one-analyst firm, and SQM's survey and QA staff run client programmes, which is production
**Output:** Annual benchmark reports, survey-based studies, certifications and awards
**Proprietary data:** ContactBabel's own surveys of contact-centre managers; SQM's benchmark corpus is built from client-commissioned post-call surveys, which belong to the commissioning clients
**Clock:** Self-set publication calendars
**Buyer:** Founder or research director
