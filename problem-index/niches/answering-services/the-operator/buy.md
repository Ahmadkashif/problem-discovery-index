# Agent Assist Rebuilt for a Thousand Small Accounts

**Niche:** [[niches/answering-services/the-operator/profile|The Answering Service Operator]]
**Industry:** [[industries/answering-services|Telephone Answering Services]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Contact-centre agent assist retrieves answers from one large client's knowledge base; the answering-service operator needs the one rule that applies on whichever of a thousand small accounts just rang.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #bert #worker-facing #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make an operator two weeks out of training handle any of hundreds of client accounts correctly on the first call, and whoever gets new operators account-accurate fastest takes the account.

## The Problem
Operators answer for many clients an hour. The information they need is short and specific (this account's escalation trigger, its delivery method, its exceptions), and it is buried in notes written by whoever set the account up.

## What Already Exists
Real-time agent-assist products for large contact centres (transcription, knowledge retrieval, next-best-action prompts), mostly sold to enterprises and BPOs.

## The Customization Gap
Adaptation needs: retrieval scoped to the account on screen, from the account's rule table rather than a knowledge base; prompts tied to escalation triggers ("caller mentioned post-op; this account pages for post-op within 14 days"); pre-filled messages from the transcript; HIPAA handling for medical accounts; and pricing that works for a fifty-seat service rather than a five-thousand-seat centre.

## Target Customer
Answering services and the TAS platform vendors that would embed the capability.

## Impact If Solved
Operators stop searching notes mid-call, handle time and errors fall together, and new operators can be routed to unfamiliar accounts sooner.
