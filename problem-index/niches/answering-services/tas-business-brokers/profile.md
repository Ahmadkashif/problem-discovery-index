# Answering-Service Brokers & Valuation Advisers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/answering-services|Telephone Answering Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Business brokers and M&A advisers who value and sell independent answering services, typically to rollups or neighbouring services, pricing on recurring client revenue and client mix.

## Insight Function
**Size:** One to a few advisers per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Valuations, buyer introductions, transaction management
**Proprietary data:** Private records of past transaction multiples
**Clock:** Deal timetables
**Buyer:** Principal
