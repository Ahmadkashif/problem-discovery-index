# Call-Tracking & Conversation Intelligence Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/answering-services|Telephone Answering Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Call-tracking and conversation-intelligence vendors such as CallRail and Invoca assign tracking numbers to a small business's marketing channels, record and transcribe inbound calls, and score each call for lead quality, booking and outcome. Home-services contractors, law firms and medical practices, the same clients answering services serve, pay monthly subscriptions to learn which ads produce booked jobs. Some publish call benchmarks by industry as marketing.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-50 data scientists, analysts and benchmark-report staff per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Call scoring and attribution inside the product; periodic industry call benchmarks
**Proprietary data:** Recorded and transcribed inbound calls across hundreds of thousands of small-business accounts, joined to marketing source, which includes after-hours calls routed to answering services
**Clock:** None external; clients' monthly marketing budget reviews
**Buyer:** Head of data science or VP product
