# Answering-Service Rollups & PE Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/answering-services|Telephone Answering Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Consolidators buying independent answering services and virtual-receptionist firms, such as ECI-backed Moneypenny, which bought the Atlanta service VoiceNation in 2020 to expand in the US, alongside private-equity-backed domestic platforms. They integrate acquired services onto shared platforms and staffing pools.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Corporate development and integration analytics of a few people per platform (estimate)
**Output:** Acquisition models, integration plans, internal operating dashboards
**Proprietary data:** Acquired services' operating and client data, used internally
**Clock:** Deal timetables
**Buyer:** CEO / corporate development lead
