# Hiring, Onboarding & Attrition

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/digital-bpo-operations|Digital BPO Operations]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Whether an industry that replaces a third of its workforce every year has ever examined why.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3.6B — 4% of US outsourced support and back-office spend
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~4%
**Digital Adoption:** Moderate — high-volume recruiting is automated; the analysis is not
**Target Buyer:** BPO recruiting and people leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very high — screening, onboarding and attrition prediction are all well posed

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche

Attrition in this industry runs at levels that would be an emergency anywhere else and is treated as a parameter. Recruiting pipelines are sized for it, training classes are scheduled around it, and the cost is budgeted rather than attacked.

The niche is distinct because the recruiting machine is genuinely sophisticated — high-volume sourcing, automated screening, assessment, scheduled training cohorts — and is pointed entirely at replacing people rather than at understanding why they leave. A workforce of hundreds of thousands generates an enormous amount of evidence about the causes, in the operational telemetry, and almost none of it is examined.

It is separable from the agent niche because that concerns the conditions and this concerns the flow: who is hired, how they are trained, who leaves, when and why.

## Current Tools & Gaps

High-volume applicant tracking and sourcing. Automated screening and assessment, frequently language and typing tests plus a situational judgement instrument. Structured training cohorts with a defined nesting period. Exit interviews, conducted inconsistently and analysed rarely. Attrition dashboards reporting the rate.

The gaps are prediction, cause and validation. Nobody predicts individual attrition from the daily operational signal, though the features are unusually rich and the horizon short. Nobody analyses cause beyond exit interview free text. And nobody validates whether the screening assessments predict tenure or performance at all, which for instruments applied to hundreds of thousands of applicants is a remarkable omission.
