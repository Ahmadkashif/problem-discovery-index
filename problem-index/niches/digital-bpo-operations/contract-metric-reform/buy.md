# Buy: Outcome-Based Contracting Frameworks Adapted to Contact Operations

**Niche:** [[niches/digital-bpo-operations/contract-metric-reform/profile|Contractual Metric Reform]]
**Industry:** [[industries/digital-bpo-operations|Digital BPO Operations]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Outcome-based contracting is well developed in facilities, healthcare and IT services; contact operations have far shorter cycles, far more volume and an outcome the client partly determines.
**Tags:** #compliance #descriptive-statistics #confidence-intervals #evaluation-metrics #hypothesis-testing #causal-inference #revenue-impact #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Whether outcome-contracting frameworks from longer-cycle services transfer to millions of short interactions.

## The Problem

Outcome-based and performance-based contracting is an established discipline. Facilities management pays for building availability rather than hours. Healthcare has value-based arrangements with risk sharing and quality gates. IT services contracts carry availability and resolution commitments with credits and bonuses. Managed services generally has moved this way over two decades.

The frameworks assume outcomes that are relatively few, relatively slow and relatively attributable. Contact operations have millions of short interactions a month, an outcome determined partly by the client's own product and policy, and a workforce whose individual performance the vendor controls while the resolvability of the issue it faces is the client's.

## What Already Exists

Performance-based contracting methodology and its literature. Gainshare and risk-share structures from managed services and healthcare. SLA credit regimes with earn-back provisions. Benchmarking services for vendor comparison. Procurement evaluation frameworks including total-cost and value scoring. Contract lifecycle management platforms.

## The Customization Gap

**Attribution is the crux and the frameworks assume it away.** IT availability is the vendor's responsibility; building cleanliness is the vendor's responsibility. Contact resolution is jointly determined, and no existing framework offers a method for splitting it. Building the contact-type taxonomy and the unresolved-cause attribution is the substantive adaptation.

**The measurement cadence is continuous, not periodic.** Outcome contracts typically settle quarterly against a handful of measures. Here the measurement is per contact at enormous volume, which makes statistical confidence excellent and makes the settlement mechanics — smoothing, thresholds, seasonality adjustment — something that has to be designed rather than borrowed.

**Procurement's scorecard has to change and it is not the vendor's to change.** Evaluation frameworks in this category score unit price prominently. A value-based evaluation framework that procurement functions will actually adopt is the gating artefact, and producing one is an industry-level piece of work rather than a vendor's.

**The measurement is produced by the party being paid on it.** Independent verification is a requirement here in a way it is not when the measure is server uptime. Joint audit rights, an agreed rubric and an independent recomputation path are contract terms with no standard form in this industry.

**Work composition shifts underneath the contract.** Deflection continuously changes the mix of what reaches a human, so a resolution baseline set in January describes different work by June. Contracts need a mix-adjustment mechanism, which no existing framework contemplates because their scopes are stable.

## Target Customer

BPO commercial and legal leadership constructing outcome proposals, and client procurement and vendor management functions with a mandate to move beyond cost per contact. Also industry bodies and advisory firms, who are the plausible authors of the evaluation framework that unblocks the rest.

## Impact If Solved

The performance-contracting methodology, gainshare structures and credit mechanics get reused, and the joint attribution, continuous settlement, procurement evaluation framework, independent verification and mix adjustment get built. Concretely: a contract form that a procurement function can score and a vendor can sign.
