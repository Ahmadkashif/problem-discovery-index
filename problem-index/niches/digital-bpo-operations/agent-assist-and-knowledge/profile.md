# Agent Assist & Knowledge

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/digital-bpo-operations|Digital BPO Operations]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Whether the answer the assist tool surfaces is right, and whether the agent can tell when it is not.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$18B — 20% of US outsourced support and back-office spend
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~20%
**Digital Adoption:** High and rising fast — retrieval and suggestion are now standard
**Target Buyer:** BPO operations; assist vendors; clients supplying the content
**Automation Potential:** Very high — and the risk scales with it

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche

The agent is answering from a knowledge base the client maintains inconsistently, and the assist tool retrieves confidently from whatever is in it.

Assist has moved quickly from suggested articles to generated answers, and it works well enough to have changed the job. The agent's role increasingly is to judge whether the suggested answer is correct and appropriate, which is a different skill from knowing the answer and is neither trained nor measured.

The niche is distinct because the failure mode is specific and new. A wrong answer delivered confidently by a tool, repeated across thousands of contacts before anyone notices, is a different kind of error from an agent not knowing something. And the root cause usually sits with the client's content rather than with the model — which makes the accountability diffuse in exactly the way that leaves it unaddressed.

## Current Tools & Gaps

Agent assist platforms with retrieval over the client's knowledge base, suggested responses, real-time guidance and next-best-action. Knowledge management systems on the client side. Post-contact summarisation, which works well. Screen guidance and process automation.

The gaps are confidence, verification and feedback. Assist tools present answers with uniform confidence regardless of the underlying content's quality or coverage. Agents have no signal about when to distrust a suggestion, and no fast way to verify one. And when an agent knows a suggestion is wrong, there is usually no route for that to reach the content owner, so the same wrong answer is served again tomorrow.
