# Energy Efficiency Potential Study Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/energy-auditors|Energy Auditors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Consultancies produce the achievable and economic potential studies utilities must file with regulators to justify their efficiency budgets — modelling how much savings exists in a service territory, at what cost, by measure and segment. The study sets the utility's targets for years and is litigated by intervenors.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 analysts, engineers, and economists
**Output:** Potential studies, measure cost-effectiveness analyses, target recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated measure characterization and building stock modelling across territories, plus the record of which assumptions regulators accepted
**Clock:** Regulatory filing deadlines and rate case schedules
**Buyer:** Practice Leader / Managing Director
