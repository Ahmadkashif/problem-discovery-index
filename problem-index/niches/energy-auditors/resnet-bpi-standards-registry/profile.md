# Home Energy Rating Standards & Registry Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/energy-auditors|Energy Auditors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
RESNET and the Building Performance Institute define how homes are rated and how auditors are certified, accredit the providers, and — in RESNET's case — operate the national registry through which every rated home's score is issued. The standard determines what a rating means, and the registry holds the resulting data.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 standards developers, technical staff, and quality assurance personnel
**Output:** Rating standards, certification schemes, quality assurance findings, registry data
**Proprietary data:** A national registry of rated homes with modelled characteristics and scores — a building stock dataset no other party holds at that resolution
**Clock:** Code cycle alignment, programme requirement updates, and annual quality assurance cycles
**Buyer:** Executive Director / VP of Standards
