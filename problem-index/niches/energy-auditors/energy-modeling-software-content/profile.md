# Energy Modelling Software & Library Content

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/energy-auditors|Energy Auditors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Ekotrope, TREAT, OptiMiser, and REM/Rate maintain the modelling engines and the measure, equipment, and construction assembly libraries auditors translate field observations into. The Pass 1 analysis identifies that translation as where most error and time waste occurs; the library content is what governs it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 building scientists, engineers, and content staff
**Output:** Modelling engines, measure and assembly libraries, programme rule configurations
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated measure characterization and the model runs flowing through the software across millions of audits
**Clock:** Energy code cycles and utility programme rule changes
**Buyer:** VP of Product / Director of Building Science
