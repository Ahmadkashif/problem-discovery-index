# Energy Efficiency Policy Research Organizations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/energy-auditors|Energy Auditors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
ACEEE and comparable research nonprofits produce the state efficiency scorecards, programme cost-effectiveness analyses, and policy research that shape how states set efficiency targets and design programmes.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-80 researchers, analysts, and policy staff
**Output:** Policy research, state scorecards, cost-effectiveness studies
**Proprietary data:** Long-running programme spending and savings series across states, assembled from filings nobody else compiles
**Clock:** Legislative sessions and annual scorecard publication
**Buyer:** Director of Research — philanthropically funded rather than commercial
