# Utility Meter Data Analytics Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/energy-auditors|Energy Auditors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Uplight, Bidgely, and the meter analytics vendors disaggregate interval consumption data into end uses, identify efficiency opportunities without a site visit, and target programme outreach — automating from the meter much of what an auditor collects on a clipboard.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 data scientists, building scientists, and analysts
**Output:** End use disaggregation, opportunity identification, programme targeting analytics
**Proprietary data:** Interval meter data across millions of premises joined to programme participation and, in some deployments, to audit findings
**Clock:** Utility programme cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Data Science / Chief Product Officer
