# Building Stock & Characteristics Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/energy-auditors|Energy Auditors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Providers assemble parcel, assessor, permit, and imagery data into building characteristic datasets — vintage, size, construction type, heating fuel — that programme designers and contractors use to target retrofit opportunity without visiting the property.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 data engineers, analysts, and building scientists
**Output:** Building characteristic datasets, retrofit opportunity scoring, territory analysis
**Proprietary data:** Assembled and inferred building attributes across national housing stock, with inference models tuned against audited properties
**Clock:** Programme planning cycles
**Buyer:** Head of Data Products
