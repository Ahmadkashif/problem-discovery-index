# ESCO Engineering & Measurement Verification

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/energy-auditors|Energy Auditors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The engineering groups at Ameresco, NORESCO, and the energy service companies produce investment-grade audits and the measurement and verification reports that determine whether a performance contract's guaranteed savings were delivered. Under a guaranteed savings contract, the ESCO pays the shortfall, which makes the analysis a financial commitment rather than a recommendation.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 energy engineers, M&V analysts, and modellers
**Output:** Investment-grade audits, savings guarantees, annual M&V reports
**Proprietary data:** Modelled versus measured savings across thousands of guaranteed projects — the only large dataset that grades energy models against outcomes with money on the line
**Clock:** Performance contract anniversaries and guarantee reconciliation dates
**Buyer:** VP of Engineering / Director of M&V
