# Building Diagnostic Equipment Technical Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/energy-auditors|Energy Auditors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The technical and applications groups at blower door, duct tester, and combustion analyzer manufacturers develop test protocols, publish measurement guidance, and support the standards that define how field measurements are taken.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 3-15 per manufacturer
**Output:** Test protocols, calibration standards, measurement guidance
**Proprietary data:** Instrument performance and field measurement data
**Clock:** Standards revision and product cycles
**Buyer:** Technical Director
