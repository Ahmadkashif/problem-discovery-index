# Utility Efficiency Programme Implementers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/energy-auditors|Energy Auditors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
CLEAResult, ICF, TRC, and Franklin Energy run utility efficiency programmes under contract — recruiting contractors, processing rebates, verifying installations, and reporting claimed savings. They are the layer that turns an auditor's findings into a paid retrofit, and their analytics teams forecast participation and manage the savings pipeline the utility is regulated against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 programme analysts, engineers, and data staff
**Output:** Savings claims, participation forecasts, programme performance reporting
**Proprietary data:** Participation, measure installation, and rebate data across many utilities and years — the most complete record of what efficiency measures households and businesses actually adopt
**Clock:** Utility savings targets and regulatory reporting periods
**Buyer:** VP of Analytics / Chief Programme Officer
