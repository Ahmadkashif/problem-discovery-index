# Energy Code Compliance Verification

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/energy-auditors|Energy Auditors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Building departments and third-party verifiers check that new construction meets the adopted energy code — plan review, duct and envelope testing, and the compliance documentation that gates a certificate of occupancy.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 plan reviewers, inspectors, and verifiers per jurisdiction or firm
**Output:** Compliance determinations, test results, correction notices
**Proprietary data:** Compliance and failure findings across new construction in a jurisdiction
**Clock:** Occupancy dates and inspection scheduling
**Buyer:** Chief Building Official / Verification firm principal
