# Weatherization Assistance Programme Agencies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/energy-auditors|Energy Auditors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State and community action agencies administer the federally funded weatherization programme, running audits under prescribed protocols, prioritizing measures by savings-to-investment ratio, and reporting outcomes to the federal programme office.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-80 programme staff, monitors, and analysts per state office
**Output:** Programme monitoring reports, measure prioritization, outcome reporting
**Proprietary data:** Audit and retrofit records across hundreds of thousands of low-income homes, with prescribed protocol consistency no commercial dataset matches
**Clock:** Federal grant reporting and programme year deadlines
**Buyer:** State programme director
