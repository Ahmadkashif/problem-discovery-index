# Efficiency Programme Evaluation Contractors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/energy-auditors|Energy Auditors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Independent evaluation, measurement, and verification contractors determine whether utility energy efficiency programmes actually saved the energy they claimed. Evaluators analyze billing data, run field verification, estimate free-ridership and spillover, and file the report with the regulator that decides how much savings the utility may book and what shareholder incentive it earns. The written evaluation is the deliverable and hundreds of millions of dollars turn on it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 evaluators, engineers, statisticians, and field staff per firm
**Output:** Impact and process evaluation reports, realization rates, net-to-gross estimates — the contracted deliverable
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated evaluation results across programmes, measures, and jurisdictions, plus the record of which methodologies regulators and utilities accepted
**Clock:** Regulatory filing deadlines that determine whether a utility can claim its savings for the year
**Buyer:** Principal / Practice Leader for evaluation services
