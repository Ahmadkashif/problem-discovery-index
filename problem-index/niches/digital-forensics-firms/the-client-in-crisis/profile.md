## What Makes This a Distinct Niche

On the other side of the engagement is a general counsel who has never handled a breach, a chief executive who must decide what to say publicly, and a board asking questions nobody can yet answer. They are the decision-makers and they are the least prepared participants in the room.

Their decisions are consequential and time-bound. Whether to notify, whom, when and in what terms. Whether the incident is material for disclosure purposes. Whether to pay a ransom, which raises sanctions questions. What to tell customers, staff, partners and regulators, and in what order. Each has a statutory or contractual clock and each depends on a scope that the forensic team can only bound.

What they receive is a bridge call twice a day and an evolving technical narrative. What they need is a decision framework: what is established, what the range of exposure is, what each option costs, and what happens if the picture changes.

The gap is met by breach coach counsel, who are genuinely good at this and are expensive, scarce and brought in after the incident has started — and whose own knowledge is as tacit as the responders'.

## Current Tools & Gaps

Breach coach law firms directing the response and advising on notification. Incident response plans, of varying quality and rarely rehearsed. Crisis communications support. Cyber insurance claims handlers. Bridge calls as the primary information channel.

The gaps sit with the decision-maker. Scope arrives as narrative rather than as the bounded range the notification decision needs. Nothing models the notification options and their consequences — a broad early notification against a narrow later one, with the cost and risk of each. Regulatory obligations across multiple jurisdictions are assembled by counsel under time pressure with no supporting tooling. Communication artefacts are drafted from scratch. And the executives have no preparation, because incident response plans are written for the technical team and rehearsed, if at all, as a technical exercise.
