# Buy: Claims Automation From Insurance Operations

**Niche:** Engagement & Retainer Operations
**Industry:** [[industries/digital-forensics-firms|Digital Forensics Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Insurance has automated first notice of loss, panel assignment and vendor authorisation across every other claim type, and cyber activation runs on phone calls.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #evaluation-metrics #compliance #automation #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Whether the commercial and administrative shell around an activation runs itself, or consumes hours of the first day.

## The Problem

Receiving a loss notification, assigning an approved vendor, authorising work and managing the claim is core insurance operations, and it is thoroughly automated in the mature lines.

Motor claims assign an approved repairer automatically with pre-agreed rates and authority limits. Property claims dispatch a loss adjuster from a panel with defined scope and standing authorisation. Health claims authorise providers against pre-agreed schedules. In each case first notice of loss triggers a workflow that assigns, authorises and begins within minutes, because the insurer worked out long ago that delay increases loss.

Cyber claims are handled by phone. The insured calls the broker, the broker calls the claims handler, the handler identifies a panel firm and calls them, and the firm begins the process of getting authorised. The mechanism the same insurer uses to dispatch a loss adjuster to a flooded building in fifteen minutes is not applied to dispatching a forensic team to an active intrusion.

## What Already Exists

Claims platforms: Guidewire, Duck Creek, Sapiens and the claims modules in the large insurance systems, with first notice of loss intake, automated assignment, authority limits, vendor management and payment.

Panel and vendor management: approved vendor networks with pre-agreed rates, performance tracking and automated assignment across motor, property and health.

Straight-through processing: automated authorisation within limits, with exception routing for anything outside them.

Cyber-specific: panel arrangements with pre-agreed rates, claims portals, and in some cases a hotline — the commercial layer without the automation.

Broker platforms: the systems through which most notifications actually arrive.

## The Customization Gap

**First notice of loss is a phone call.** Every other line has structured intake that triggers the workflow. Cyber notification arrives as a conversation, which is where the automation stops before it starts.

**Assignment is manual.** Panel assignment in other lines considers availability, specialism, geography and performance automatically. Cyber assignment is a claims handler choosing from a list and telephoning.

**Authority limits exist and are not automated.** An initial cap could authorise automatically the way a motor repair authority does, and instead it is approved by a person.

**Vendor capacity is invisible.** Motor panels know which repairers have capacity. Cyber panels do not know which firms have responders available, which matters enormously during a multi-client event.

**Urgency is not encoded.** Property claims have escalation for active damage. An active intrusion is the same category of continuing loss and is treated as a standard notification.

**Substantiation is assembled afterwards.** Other lines capture what the claim requires during the work. Cyber billing is substantiated retrospectively from time records not kept for that purpose.

## Target Customer

Cyber insurers, for whom this is applying their own existing claims automation to a line where they have not — and where the loss reduction from faster activation is direct and measurable.

Claims platform vendors, for whom cyber is a growing line whose operational model is behind the rest of the book.

Brokers, who hold the notification moment and could structure intake rather than passing on a phone call.

## Impact If Solved

An insurer's own claims automation, mature across every other line, applied to the line where speed most directly reduces loss.

Structured first notice of loss is the enabling step, because a phone call cannot trigger a workflow and everything else follows from intake being data.

And panel capacity visibility would matter most during exactly the multi-client events when every firm is stretched and assignment is currently made by ringing round.
