# Niche Analysis — Digital Forensics Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/digital-forensics-firms|Digital Forensics Firms]]

## Niche Selection

This industry is asked to establish what happened from evidence that frequently was never retained, under a statutory clock, for clients whose lawyers want certainty. The eight niches below follow that: the engagement itself, the question that governs everything downstream, the evidence work underneath it, the claims made on top, the two people at either end of a crisis, and the mechanical layers.

## Why These Niches

The engagement is the commercial core and scope determination is the question everything downstream turns on — notification, contractual obligation, litigation exposure and public statement. Evidence collection and timeline assembly is the labour underneath it, and attribution and testimony are the claims built on top, both made with confidence language nobody has calibrated. The two underserved people are the responder working a pattern the field admits is unsustainable, and the client's general counsel making a notification decision on a statutory clock with an answer that can only be bounded. Reporting and engagement operations are mechanical.

## Filter Notes

Seven of the eight are terminal — each names one contest every serious competitor is fighting over, and decomposing further would produce features rather than markets.

**Scope determination** is not, and the split is temporal: the same question — what does this evidence support — addressed at two different moments, to two different buyers, by two different products. Evidence-bounded inference happens during the incident. Given the logs that exist, what can be established, what can only be bounded, and what cannot be addressed at all — stated as calibrated bounds rather than as narrative, under a statutory clock, for a client whose counsel wants a number. It is an inference problem over whatever evidence survived, it is sold to the party already in crisis, and it improves an engagement that is already underway. Pre-incident evidence readiness happens before anything has occurred. Which of this organisation's logging gaps will make the notification question unanswerable, ranked by the scenarios that would actually matter — an instrumentation assessment sold to a CISO with a budget cycle, delivered as a remediation plan, with a return that appears only if an incident later occurs. Different moment, different buyer, different sales motion, different deliverable. A firm can build the inference layer and never build readiness, and most would, because the inference layer serves the engagement they are already being paid for.

Two adjacent candidates were rejected as belonging elsewhere: **ongoing detection and monitoring** is the subject of [[industries/cybersecurity-mssp|Cybersecurity MSSP]], and **adversary tracking as a product** belongs to [[industries/threat-intelligence-vendors|Threat Intelligence Vendors]].
