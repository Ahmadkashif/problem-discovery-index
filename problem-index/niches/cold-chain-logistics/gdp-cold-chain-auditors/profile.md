# Good Distribution Practice Auditors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cold-chain-logistics|Cold Chain Logistics]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Third-party auditors assess cold chain operators against pharmaceutical Good Distribution Practice requirements on behalf of manufacturers who remain accountable for their distributors. The audit report determines whether a 3PL can handle a manufacturer's product, so it gates commercial access to the highest-value cold chain freight.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 auditors, quality specialists, and technical reviewers
**Output:** Audit reports, non-conformance findings, qualification recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Findings across hundreds of distribution sites, including recurrence patterns invisible to any individual manufacturer
**Clock:** Audit cycles and manufacturer qualification deadlines
**Buyer:** VP of Quality Services / Global Audit Director
