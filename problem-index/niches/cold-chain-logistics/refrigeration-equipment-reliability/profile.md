# Refrigeration Equipment Reliability Engineering

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cold-chain-logistics|Cold Chain Logistics]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The reliability and connected-fleet groups at Carrier Transicold, Thermo King, and the industrial refrigeration manufacturers analyze telemetry and warranty data from reefer units and warehouse systems, publish service guidance, and feed findings into product design.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 reliability engineers, data scientists, and technical service staff
**Output:** Failure analyses, service bulletins, predictive maintenance products
**Proprietary data:** Telemetry and failure history across hundreds of thousands of refrigeration units in service
**Clock:** Product programme milestones and refrigerant regulation deadlines
**Buyer:** Director of Reliability / VP Connected Solutions
