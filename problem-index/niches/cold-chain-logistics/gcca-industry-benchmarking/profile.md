# Cold Chain Association Benchmarking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cold-chain-logistics|Cold Chain Logistics]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Global Cold Chain Alliance and its affiliated bodies produce the global cold storage capacity report, warehouse productivity and energy benchmarks, and the food handling research that underpins industry practice.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-50 researchers, analysts, and technical staff
**Output:** Capacity reports, productivity and energy benchmarks, technical guidance
**Proprietary data:** Member-contributed facility capacity, productivity, and energy data
**Clock:** Annual publication cycle
**Buyer:** VP of Research / Technical Director
