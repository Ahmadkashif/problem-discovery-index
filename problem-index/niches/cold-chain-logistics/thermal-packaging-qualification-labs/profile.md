# Thermal Packaging Qualification Laboratories

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cold-chain-logistics|Cold Chain Logistics]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Sonoco ThermoSafe, Cold Chain Technologies, and specialist test labs qualify shipping systems against defined thermal profiles — running chamber tests that establish how long a packaging configuration holds product in range under summer and winter lane conditions. Pharmaceutical shippers cannot distribute without a qualification report, and the report defines the operating envelope every downstream shipment works inside.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 packaging engineers, thermal scientists, and test technicians
**Output:** Qualification reports and thermal performance dossiers
**Proprietary data:** Thermal performance data across packaging configurations, payloads, and ambient profiles, accumulated over decades of chamber testing
**Clock:** Product launch dates and regulatory submission deadlines that cannot move without the report
**Buyer:** VP of Engineering / Director of Thermal Sciences
