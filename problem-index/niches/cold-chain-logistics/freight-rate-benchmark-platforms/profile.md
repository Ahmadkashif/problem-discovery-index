# Freight Rate Benchmark Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cold-chain-logistics|Cold Chain Logistics]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Xeneta, DAT, and Freightos collect contracted and spot freight rates — including the reefer and temperature-controlled segments — from shippers and carriers, normalize them by lane, equipment, and service level, and sell the benchmarks that both sides take into contract negotiations. The benchmark is the product and it is used adversarially across the table.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 market analysts, data scientists, and rate operations staff
**Output:** Rate benchmarks, market indices, negotiation analytics
**Proprietary data:** Contributed contracted rates that neither shippers nor carriers publish anywhere, accumulated by lane and equipment type over years
**Clock:** Annual and quarterly contract negotiation cycles, which are fixed and expensive to enter unprepared
**Buyer:** Chief Analytics Officer / Head of Market Intelligence
