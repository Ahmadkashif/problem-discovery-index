# Cold Storage Real Estate Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cold-chain-logistics|Cold Chain Logistics]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
The industrial research groups at CBRE, JLL, and Cushman & Wakefield track cold storage capacity, rents, absorption, and development pipeline, and publish the sector reports that operators and investors underwrite against. Cold storage is capital-intensive and opaque, so the published research substantially shapes where capital goes.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 researchers and analysts within the industrial practices
**Output:** Capacity and rent reports, absorption analysis, development pipeline tracking
**Proprietary data:** Transaction and leasing data from the firm's own brokerage flow, which no public source contains
**Clock:** Quarterly publication and client underwriting cycles
**Buyer:** Head of Industrial Research
