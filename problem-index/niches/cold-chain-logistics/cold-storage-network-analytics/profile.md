# Cold Storage Network Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cold-chain-logistics|Cold Chain Logistics]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The data science and corporate development groups at Lineage, Americold, and NewCold model network design, slotting, energy consumption, and acquisition economics across hundreds of temperature-controlled facilities. These are the largest consolidators in a fragmented sector and their analytics teams are correspondingly substantial.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 data scientists, industrial engineers, and corp dev analysts
**Output:** Network design models, slotting and throughput optimization, acquisition analyses
**Proprietary data:** Throughput, temperature, energy, and labour data across hundreds of facilities and years — the most complete operational picture of cold storage anywhere
**Clock:** Deal timelines and customer contract cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / SVP Corporate Development
