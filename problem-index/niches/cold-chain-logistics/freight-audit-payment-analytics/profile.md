# Freight Audit & Payment Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cold-chain-logistics|Cold Chain Logistics]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Firms that audit, correct, and pay freight invoices on behalf of shippers, then analyze the resulting spend for savings opportunities and carrier performance. They see the settled cost of every shipment across their client base, including the accessorial charges that make temperature-controlled freight expensive.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-800 auditors, analysts, and data staff
**Output:** Audit corrections, spend analytics, carrier performance and benchmarking reports
**Proprietary data:** Settled freight invoices with full accessorial detail across thousands of shippers and lanes
**Clock:** Invoice payment terms and monthly reporting cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Analytics / Chief Operating Officer
