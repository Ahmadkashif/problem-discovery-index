# Cold Chain Engineering Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cold-chain-logistics|Cold Chain Logistics]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Refrigeration and facility engineering firms design cold storage systems, model energy and load, and advise on refrigerant transition and automation. The design and the engineering study are the deliverables.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-80 refrigeration engineers, energy modellers, and designers
**Output:** Facility designs, energy and load studies, refrigerant transition plans
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated design and commissioning performance data across facilities — mostly client-owned
**Clock:** Construction schedules and refrigerant phase-down compliance dates
**Buyer:** Principal / Director of Engineering
