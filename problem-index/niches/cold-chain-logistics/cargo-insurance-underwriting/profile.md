# Temperature-Controlled Cargo Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cold-chain-logistics|Cold Chain Logistics]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Specialty cargo underwriters price the risk of temperature-sensitive shipments and adjudicate spoilage claims, researching loss experience by commodity, lane, packaging, and carrier to set terms on a line where a single reefer failure can destroy a full container of biologics.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 underwriters, claims analysts, and risk engineers
**Output:** Rating models, underwriting guidelines, loss prevention recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Spoilage claim experience by commodity, lane, and equipment
**Clock:** Renewal cycles and claim adjustment deadlines
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
