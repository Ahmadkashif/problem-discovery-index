# Pharmaceutical Cold Chain Qualification Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cold-chain-logistics|Cold Chain Logistics]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Specialist consultancies design and execute lane qualification programmes for pharmaceutical distribution — mapping ambient profiles on a route, selecting and testing packaging, writing the validation protocols, and producing the documentation that satisfies regulators that a product travelled within its stability envelope.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 validation engineers, cold chain specialists, and technical writers
**Output:** Qualification protocols, lane mapping studies, validation reports
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated lane profile and qualification outcomes across products and geographies — largely client-owned under quality agreements
**Clock:** Product launch and regulatory submission deadlines
**Buyer:** Managing Director / Practice Lead
