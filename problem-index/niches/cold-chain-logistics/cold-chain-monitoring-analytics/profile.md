# Cold Chain Monitoring & Excursion Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cold-chain-logistics|Cold Chain Logistics]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Sensitech, Controlant, Tive, and Emerson's cargo business instrument shipments with temperature loggers and sell the analysis that follows: whether the load stayed in range, where it went out, what caused it, and whether the product can be released. Pharmaceutical and food shippers cannot make a release decision or satisfy Good Distribution Practice documentation without it, so the report rather than the device is what is actually being bought.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 data scientists, cold chain engineers, and analysts
**Output:** Excursion analyses, root cause investigations, lane performance and risk reporting
**Proprietary data:** Temperature and location profiles across millions of shipments, lanes, carriers, and packaging configurations — the only record of what actually happens to product in transit, at industry scale
**Clock:** Product release decisions measured in hours, and GDP documentation obligations on every consignment
**Buyer:** VP of Analytics / Chief Technology Officer
