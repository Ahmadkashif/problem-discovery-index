# Validation & Experimental Ground Truth

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/marketing-attribution-vendors|Marketing Attribution Vendors]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make experiments the ground truth and every model an interpolator between them — and whoever does that credibly makes their own error rate visible and resets the terms the whole category competes on.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$700M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~23% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — almost nobody backtests
**Target Buyer:** Vendor leadership and client measurement functions
**Automation Potential:** Very High — backtesting is mechanical once experiments exist

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The product is a number that reallocates a marketing budget, derived from observational data, and essentially nobody in the category backtests it against an experiment that could show it was wrong. This is the largest concentration of contested value here because it is the category's defining absence: a business whose entire product is measurement has no accepted measurement of itself. It is distinct from experiment operation — that runs the tests; this uses them to grade the models continuously and publishes the result.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Occasional validation studies presented in sales materials, in-sample fit statistics, practitioner confidence, and client triangulation. The gaps: no systematic backtest against experimental results; in-sample fit reported as accuracy; no error rate published; no record of predictions made before outcomes were known; and no way for a client to compare vendors on correctness.
