# Niche Analysis — Marketing Attribution Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/marketing-attribution-vendors|Marketing Attribution Vendors]]

## Niche Selection

This category exists because advertisers need a causal answer and the data available is observational. Every technique it sells is a different way of guessing at a counterfactual, and the one method that does not guess is treated as an occasional supplement rather than as the foundation. Two vendors modelling the same business return materially different answers and the client is advised to triangulate. The eight niches below split the category by what competitors actually fight over.

## Why These Niches

Validation takes the largest share because the category's entire product is a number that reallocates budget and almost nobody checks it against an experiment that could show it wrong — the first vendor to do that credibly resets the category's terms. The modelling stack is second because it is what customers think they are buying, and because its two halves are entirely different disciplines. The two low-digitized niches are the ones rebuilt by hand at every client: the conversion record every model depends on, and the reconciliation between methods that disagree. The two underserved audiences are the scientist defending an estimate they know is less certain than the chart implies, and the client-side analyst asked which of five numbers is right before Thursday. The two automatable niches are the experiments that should be the foundation and the cross-client knowledge no vendor accumulates.

## Filter Notes

Seven of the eight level-1 niches are terminal. The Modelling Stack is not: the label names the product category rather than a contest, and writing the contested statement produces two sentences describing different winners. Path-based attribution assigns fractional credit along observed journeys and lives or dies on identity resolution and path completeness — a data and identity contest, undermined first by its correlational logic and then by the collapse of cross-site identity. Mix modelling works on aggregate time series and lives or dies on identification: collinear spends, adstock and saturation as specification choices, and priors that frequently do more work than the data. Different data, different failure modes, different expertise, and visibly different vendors. It therefore decomposes into **Path-Based Attribution** and **Mix Modelling**.

Two candidates were considered and rejected. **Mobile measurement and install attribution** overlaps heavily but is the contest of [[industries/app-marketing-firms|App Marketing Firms]], where the attribution question has its own distinct constraints. **Platform-native reporting** — each platform's own account of what it caused — is the object these vendors measure against rather than a niche here, and the contest over it belongs to [[industries/programmatic-ad-platforms|Programmatic Ad Platforms]].
