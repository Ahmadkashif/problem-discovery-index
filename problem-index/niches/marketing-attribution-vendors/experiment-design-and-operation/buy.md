**Industry:** [[industries/marketing-attribution-vendors|Marketing Attribution Vendors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Product organisations industrialised experimentation into a platform anyone can use, and marketing experiments are still consulting projects.
**Tags:** #hypothesis-testing #causal-inference #monte-carlo-methods #confidence-intervals #evaluation-metrics #automation #workflow-orchestration #descriptive-statistics
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make experiments cheap and continuous rather than occasional and expensive — and whoever does that supplies the ground truth the whole category needs and does not have.

## The Problem
Experimentation platforms made running a valid experiment a self-service operation. Assignment, power analysis, guardrail metrics, sequential testing, automated readouts and statistical guardrails that prevent the common errors are all built in, and a product manager with no statistical training can run a defensible test. The transformation took experiments at large technology companies from dozens a year to thousands. Marketing experiments remain bespoke projects designed by a specialist, which is why there are so few of them.

## What Already Exists
Experimentation platforms with assignment and randomisation; automated power analysis and sample size planning; sequential testing with valid stopping; guardrail metrics and automated alerts; and self-service design with statistical safeguards.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to randomisation the platform does not control. It requires: (1) assignment at geography or audience level enacted through advertising platforms rather than in a product the experimenter owns — the execution layer is the hard part and is what product platforms get for free; (2) treatment that is a spend change with imperfect compliance, since suppression leaks and delivery varies, requiring analysis that accounts for it; (3) far lower power, as geographic units number in the dozens where product experiments have millions of users, which makes design choices consequential rather than routine; (4) outcomes measured in the advertiser's own systems with delay, connecting to the conversion plumbing work; and (5) media cost as the experiment's price, which makes design efficiency financially significant in a way product experimentation never faces.

## Target Customer
Measurement vendors, client measurement teams, and experimentation platform vendors for whom media experimentation is an unserved adjacent market.

## Impact If Solved
Product experimentation went from dozens a year to thousands by industrialising the platform, and marketing experiments are still consulting projects. Execution through advertising platforms the experimenter does not control is the hard part product platforms get for free.
