# Experiment Design & Operation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/marketing-attribution-vendors|Marketing Attribution Vendors]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make experiments cheap and continuous rather than occasional and expensive — and whoever does that supplies the ground truth the whole category needs and does not have.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$250M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — occasional and bespoke
**Target Buyer:** Vendor and client measurement teams
**Automation Potential:** Very High — design, execution and analysis are all automatable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Incrementality experiments answer the question correctly and cost money, take weeks, and at most advertisers' scale detect only large effects. They are treated as an occasional supplement to modelling when the category's whole problem is that it has no ground truth. This is distinct from validation: that grades the models against experiments, this makes the experiments cheap enough and frequent enough to grade against. The contest is an operational and design one — automating power analysis, geographic assignment, execution across platforms, and analysis — which is entirely separable from any modelling.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Bespoke geo experiment designs, platform-native lift tests of varying credibility, manual assignment and execution, and analysis in a notebook. The gaps: designs built per test rather than generated; power frequently unexamined; execution across platforms manual and error-prone; analysis inconsistent; and cost and duration that limit frequency severely.
