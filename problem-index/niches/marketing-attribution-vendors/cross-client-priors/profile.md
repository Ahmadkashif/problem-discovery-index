# Cross-Client Priors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/marketing-attribution-vendors|Marketing Attribution Vendors]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a portfolio of clients into estimated priors that replace an analyst's judgement — and whoever does that makes every new engagement start from evidence instead of from an assumption.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$150M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — experience rather than estimates
**Target Buyer:** Vendor science leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — hierarchical pooling is the method

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A vendor with a hundred clients has fitted a hundred models and run some number of experiments, across verticals, spend levels and business types. Those results are exactly the population from which a prior should be estimated — what a channel's effectiveness typically is, how adstock typically behaves in this vertical, how much platforms typically overstate. Instead each engagement's priors come from an analyst's recollection. This is a distinct contest because it is a portfolio asset question rather than a per-client modelling one, and it is what would make the priors in the mix model defensible rather than chosen.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Analyst experience, published benchmarks of uncertain provenance, occasional internal reference documents, and per-client model fitting. The gaps: no pooled estimation across clients; priors chosen rather than estimated; no record of past model results in a comparable form; confidentiality treated as a blanket prohibition; and no transfer to new engagements.
