## The Problem
Communicating uncertainty to people who must act is a studied problem with real conventions. Weather forecasting settled on probability of precipitation and the public learned to use it. Intelligence analysis has standardised confidence language. Clinical communication has established formats for risk. In each case the field decided that hiding uncertainty produced worse decisions than presenting it, designed a convention, and taught its audience. Marketing science presents point estimates because that is what the deck does.

## What Already Exists
Probabilistic forecast communication conventions; standardised analytic confidence language; risk communication formats tested for comprehension; visual conventions for uncertainty; and audience education programmes accompanying each.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to an audience with a stake in the answer. It requires: (1) recipients who are not neutral, since a channel owner benefits from one answer and will use admitted uncertainty selectively — the meteorological audience has no preference about rain and this audience has a budget to defend, which is the central difference; (2) a decision rather than an interpretation as the output, so the convention should express what the evidence supports doing rather than what is likely; (3) no institutional authority behind the convention, unlike a meteorological service, so a vendor adopting one alone is at a competitive disadvantage; (4) uncertainty that is largely about specification rather than sampling, which is harder to express than a probability and is what the existing conventions do not cover; and (5) a commercial relationship where the communicator is also the seller.

## Target Customer
Measurement vendor science teams, client measurement functions, and risk communication practitioners for whom commercial analytics is an unserved domain.

## Impact If Solved
Every field that faced this decided hiding uncertainty produced worse decisions, designed a convention and taught its audience. A non-neutral audience with a budget to defend is the central difference, and specification uncertainty is what the existing conventions do not cover.
