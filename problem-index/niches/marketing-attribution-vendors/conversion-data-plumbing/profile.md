# Conversion Data Plumbing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/marketing-attribution-vendors|Marketing Attribution Vendors]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to build the conversion record once instead of rebuilding it at every client — and whoever does that owns the input every model in the category depends on.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — bespoke pipelines everywhere
**Target Buyer:** Data engineering and client analytics leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the integration is repeated

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every measurement model depends on a conversion record that is now partial, consent-dependent and silently modelled by each platform in its own favour, and the plumbing to fix it is rebuilt from scratch at every client. This is the least glamorous and most load-bearing part of the category: no model is better than the conversion record it is fitted to, the record is degrading, and the work of assembling it consumes a large share of every engagement. It is distinct from the modelling contests because it is an engineering and integration problem with no statistics in it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Bespoke pipelines per client, tag managers, server-side conversion interfaces, warehouse integrations, and consent platforms. The gaps: rebuilt per client with no reuse; consent handling inconsistent; server-side implementations partial; platform-modelled conversions mixed with observed; and no standard conversion schema.
