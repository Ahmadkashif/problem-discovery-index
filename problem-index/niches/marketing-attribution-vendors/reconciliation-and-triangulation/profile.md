# Reconciliation & Triangulation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/marketing-attribution-vendors|Marketing Attribution Vendors]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to combine three methods with unknown biases into one defensible number — and whoever does that properly replaces an instruction to triangulate that nobody can execute.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — a spreadsheet and a judgement
**Target Buyer:** Client measurement leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — combination is a statistical problem

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Two vendors modelling the same business return materially different channel contributions, and the client is advised to triangulate. Triangulation between three methods with unknown biases is not a methodology, and everybody involved knows it. The combination step is where the client's actual decision is made, it is performed with no method at all, and no vendor sells it because every vendor's interest is in their own number being the answer. That makes it a genuinely unoccupied contest.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Side-by-side comparison in spreadsheets, a measurement lead's judgement, occasional averaging, and a preference for whichever vendor is trusted. The gaps: no method for combining estimates; biases unknown and possibly correlated; no weighting by each method's reliability in context; no use of experiments to adjudicate; and a combination step nobody owns.
