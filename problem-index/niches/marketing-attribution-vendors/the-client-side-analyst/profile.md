# The Client-Side Analyst

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/marketing-attribution-vendors|Marketing Attribution Vendors]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give the analyst a defensible answer to which number is right before Thursday — and whoever does that serves the person the entire category's output actually lands on.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$250M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — five numbers and a deadline
**Target Buyer:** Client analytics leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — reconciliation and explanation are automatable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The client-side analyst has platform numbers, site analytics, the attribution vendor, the mix model and finance, all disagreeing, and is asked which one is right before Thursday. They are the point where every vendor's output, every platform's self-report and the company's own financial record converge, and they have no tooling for that convergence — it happens in a spreadsheet, under time pressure, with a career consequence attached to being wrong. Every other party in this category produces a number; this person has to produce an answer.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Spreadsheets, vendor dashboards, platform interfaces, analytics tools, and the finance system. The gaps: five sources with incompatible definitions; no reconciliation tooling; no record of past reconciliations; no support for explaining the differences; and a recurring deadline against work that is genuinely hard.
