# The Same Reconciliation Every Month

**Niche:** [[niches/marketing-attribution-vendors/the-client-side-analyst/profile|The Client-Side Analyst]]
**Industry:** [[industries/marketing-attribution-vendors|Marketing Attribution Vendors]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The differences between the five sources have the same handful of causes every month, and the analyst rediscovers them from scratch every month because nothing was written down.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #descriptive-statistics #evaluation-metrics #quick-win #automation #worker-facing #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give the analyst a defensible answer to which number is right before Thursday — and whoever does that serves the person the entire category's output actually lands on.

## The Problem
The platform total exceeds finance because of view-through conversions and a longer window. Site analytics is lower because of consent-related loss. The attribution vendor differs from the mix model because one includes brand search and the other does not. These explanations are the same every month. The analyst works them out again each time, because last month's spreadsheet has been overwritten, the reasoning was never written down, and the person who understood it best was on leave in March. Across the industry this exact exercise is performed by thousands of people monthly and is documented nowhere.

## Why It's Still Broken
The reconciliation is treated as an output to produce rather than as a model to maintain, so each month starts from the sources rather than from the last reconciliation — the framing guarantees the repetition. Nobody is asked to document it. The spreadsheet is the artefact and spreadsheets do not carry reasoning. And the analyst is measured on delivering the meeting, not on making next month easier.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Write the reconciliation down as a model. Record each known difference with its cause, its expected magnitude and its direction, which is the fix, takes an afternoon once, and converts a monthly investigation into a monthly check. Flag only the deviations from expectation, since most of the difference is explained and only the unexplained part deserves attention. Automate the calculation of each known difference, which is arithmetic once the cause is named. Keep the history so a difference that changes size is visible as a change rather than as this month's number. Version the definitions of every source, since they change and an unexplained shift is usually a definition change nobody was told about. Produce the explanation narrative automatically for the meeting, which is most of the deliverable. Share the model across the team, so the knowledge is not one person's and leave is not a crisis. Identify differences that should be eliminated rather than explained, because some are genuine defects that have been normalised as expected variance. Report the unexplained residual as a standing metric, since it is the honest measure of how well the measurement stack is understood. And measure the time to close, because reducing it from two days to two hours is the value and nobody currently counts either.

## Who Feels the Pain
Analysts rediscovering the same explanations monthly; leadership receiving a reconciliation whose reasoning left with someone; and organisations where the measurement stack is understood by one person.

## Impact If Fixed
The reconciliation is treated as an output rather than a model, which guarantees it starts from the sources every month. Recording each known difference with its expected magnitude converts a two-day investigation into a check on the unexplained residual.
