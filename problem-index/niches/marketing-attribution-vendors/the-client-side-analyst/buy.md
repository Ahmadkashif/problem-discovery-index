**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give the analyst a defensible answer to which number is right before Thursday — and whoever does that serves the person the entire category's output actually lands on.

## The Problem
The financial close is a mature operational discipline: reconciliation of every account against a source, variance analysis with documented explanations, sign-off at defined levels, a retained audit trail and continuous improvement of close time. It exists because the numbers matter and because unexplained differences are unacceptable. Marketing measurement performs a structurally identical monthly exercise with none of it — no defined reconciliation, no variance documentation, no sign-off and no trail.

## What Already Exists
Close management platforms with task orchestration; account reconciliation with exception handling; variance analysis and documented explanation; sign-off and approval workflow; and close time measurement and reduction practice.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to sources that disagree legitimately. It requires: (1) differences that are expected and correct rather than errors to be eliminated, since three methods estimating a causal quantity will differ and finance's zero-tolerance model does not apply — the practice must classify differences rather than resolve them all, which is the central adaptation; (2) no authoritative source for most of the numbers, unlike a general ledger, so reconciliation anchors partially to finance and partially to nothing; (3) estimates with uncertainty rather than transactions, meaning variance analysis must distinguish a difference within uncertainty from one outside it; (4) a monthly cycle driven by a marketing meeting rather than by a statutory deadline, so the discipline must be self-imposed; and (5) an analyst with no accounting training, which shapes what process will actually be followed.

## Target Customer
Client analytics and marketing operations teams, finance functions receiving marketing numbers, and close management vendors for whom marketing reconciliation is an adjacent market.

## Impact If Solved
The close discipline exists because unexplained differences are unacceptable, and marketing's differences are frequently correct. Classifying differences rather than eliminating them is the adaptation, and it makes a mature operational practice applicable to estimates rather than transactions.
