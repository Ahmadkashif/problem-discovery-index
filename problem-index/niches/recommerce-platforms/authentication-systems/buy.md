## The Problem
Determining whether an object is what it claims to be, against a skilled forger, is what questioned document examination and forensic materials analysis do. Those disciplines developed instrumentation, comparison methodology, proficiency testing, conclusion scales that express degrees of confidence rather than binaries, and — after hard experience — standards about what an examiner may claim. Product authentication makes a binary call, under time pressure, with no proficiency testing and no confidence expression.

## What Already Exists
Questioned document and materials examination methodology; instrumental analysis including microscopy and spectroscopy; conclusion scales expressing support for a proposition rather than certainty; proficiency testing and blind trials for examiners; error rate research; and the evidence-interpretation framework that expresses findings as likelihood ratios.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to seconds per item rather than hours per case. It requires: (1) a conclusion scale rather than a binary, since an authenticator frequently has doubt and the system offers only yes or no — expressing degrees of support and routing the uncertain ones is the most transferable idea here and the one the forensic disciplines learned the hard way; (2) proficiency testing with seeded items, which forensic laboratories run routinely and which produces the error rate nobody in this sector has; (3) instrumentation scaled to throughput and unit value, since the forensic toolkit assumes a case worth hours and this needs seconds; (4) the likelihood ratio framing applied to features, which is how forensic evidence is properly combined and which would let weak individual signals be aggregated correctly rather than judged separately; and (5) the honest acknowledgement of limits, since the forensic world's reckoning with overclaiming is a warning this sector has not had yet.

## Target Customer
Authentication operations, platforms whose trust depends on them, and the forensic science community for whom commercial authentication is an adjacent and under-examined practice.

## Impact If Solved
Forensic examination solved authenticating against skilled forgery including how to state a conclusion honestly. A confidence scale rather than a binary is the most transferable idea, and seeded proficiency testing produces the error rate this sector does not have.
