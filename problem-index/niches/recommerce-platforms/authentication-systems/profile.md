# Authentication Systems

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/recommerce-platforms|Recommerce Platforms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to distinguish genuine from counterfeit against opponents who study exactly what is being checked — and whoever does that holds the category, because a single publicised false accept destroys the trust the whole platform rests on.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.8B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~11% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Moderate — reference data and imaging alongside expertise
**Target Buyer:** Authentication operations in luxury, sneaker and collectible categories
**Automation Potential:** Medium — imaging and reference matching help, the adversary adapts

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Authentication combines trained expertise, reference databases and increasingly imaging, and every platform still makes the final call as a human judgement under time pressure with asymmetric consequences on both sides. It is adversarial: counterfeiters study which features are checked and produce goods specifically to pass them, which means any published or inferable check degrades over time and the capability must keep moving. A false accept is a fraud loss and, if publicised, a reputational event that affects every listing on the platform. A false reject wrongs a legitimate seller and is invisible in the metrics because nobody appeals successfully. The contest is maintaining a capability against an adapting opponent, which is a different kind of problem from anything else in the intake pipeline.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Trained authenticators, reference databases of genuine examples, imaging and microscopy in some categories, and physical tagging after verification. The gaps: no measurement of the false reject rate, because rejected items leave; reference data is assembled per platform rather than shared; counterfeit examples are the scarcer and more valuable training data and are not systematically retained; imaging is used to assist rather than to detect what a human cannot; and the checks in use are not rotated as they become known.
