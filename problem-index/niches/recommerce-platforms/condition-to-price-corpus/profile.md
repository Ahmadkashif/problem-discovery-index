# Condition-to-Price Corpus

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/recommerce-platforms|Recommerce Platforms]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn millions of items' observed condition and realised price into an empirical account of what secondhand value actually is — and whoever does that defines the market, because no other party can produce it.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.0B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~6% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** None — a unique dataset, used once per item
**Target Buyer:** The platforms themselves, and the brands entering resale
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the corpus is structured, imaged and labelled by outcome

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
These platforms have built the only large-scale dataset connecting a physical item's observed condition to what someone actually paid for it. Every intake produces photographs, a grade, attributes and eventually a realised price and time to sell — a labelled example linking appearance to value, generated millions of times a year. That corpus answers questions nobody else can: what condition is actually worth in each category, how quickly value decays with age and use, which items are worth accepting, which brands hold value, and what durability is worth in resale. It is used to set the price for the item in front of the grader and for nothing else, while brands, insurers, lenders and the platforms themselves would all pay for what it contains.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Per-item pricing lookups and a sales history. The gaps: the photographs are stored and not learned from; condition and realised price are not modelled together across the corpus; no value retention index by brand or category is produced; the corpus is not offered to brands entering resale despite being what they most need; and nothing from it improves acceptance, grading or markdown.
