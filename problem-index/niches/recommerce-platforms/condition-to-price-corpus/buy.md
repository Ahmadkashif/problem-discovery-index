# Index Construction and Depreciation Modelling

**Industry:** [[industries/recommerce-platforms|Recommerce Platforms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Used vehicle guides, property indices and asset depreciation modelling are established practices with published methodology, and secondhand goods have no equivalent despite the data existing.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #linear-regression #confidence-intervals #survival-analysis #descriptive-statistics #evaluation-metrics #hypothesis-testing #gradient-boosting
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn millions of items' observed condition and realised price into an empirical account of what secondhand value actually is — and whoever does that defines the market, because no other party can produce it.

## The Problem
Constructing a price index from heterogeneous transactions, adjusting for quality so that a change in the index reflects price rather than mix, and modelling depreciation over an asset's life are all established practices. Used vehicle guides have done it for decades and are the reference for an entire industry. Property indices handle the quality adjustment problem formally. Asset depreciation modelling is standard in accounting and lending. Secondhand goods have the transaction data and no index.

## What Already Exists
Hedonic price index construction with quality adjustment; repeat-sales index methodology; residual value and depreciation modelling from vehicle and equipment finance; published index methodology standards; and the guide-book model of turning transaction data into a market reference.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to items with no serial identity and enormous heterogeneity. It requires: (1) quality adjustment on condition observed from photographs rather than on declared attributes, which is the distinctive capability and is what turns a mixed bag of transactions into a comparable series; (2) no repeat sales, since most items sell once, which rules out the repeat-sales approach and makes the hedonic method the only option; (3) index granularity at brand and category rather than at model, since there is no model identity for most inventory; (4) mix effects handled carefully, because what is supplied to a platform changes over time and an unadjusted index would measure the supply mix rather than value; and (5) a published methodology, since an index whose construction is opaque will not be adopted as a market reference and adoption is the entire point.

## Target Customer
Recommerce platforms, brands entering resale, insurers and lenders valuing used goods, and the index construction community.

## Impact If Solved
Vehicle guides became an industry's reference by turning transaction data into a published index and this sector has the data and no index. Photograph-based quality adjustment is the distinctive capability, and a published methodology is what makes the index a market reference rather than a proprietary table.
