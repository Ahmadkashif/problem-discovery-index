## The Problem
Knowing whether your inspectors agree with each other and with the truth is a solved discipline. Measurement system analysis quantifies how much of the observed variation comes from the inspectors rather than from the parts. Attribute agreement analysis handles categorical judgements specifically. Calibration programmes bring inspectors to a common standard and verify it. Acceptance sampling sets inspection levels against risk. Recommerce operations write a rubric, train against it, and audit a sample.

## What Already Exists
Measurement system analysis including repeatability and reproducibility studies; attribute agreement analysis for categorical inspection; calibration and standardisation programmes with reference samples; acceptance sampling plans; statistical process control on inspection outcomes; and inspector performance monitoring.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to items that are all different and to a judgement with commercial rather than physical consequences. It requires: (1) repeatability studies on genuinely unique items, which is the methodological obstacle — the classic study passes the same part to several inspectors and here every item is different, so the equivalent requires a circulating reference set and photographic replication; (2) reference standards constructed as a physical or photographic library rather than as a specification, which is buildable and is the calibration mechanism this sector lacks; (3) consequence-weighted error measurement, since a grade error on a five-dollar item and on a five-hundred-dollar item are not equivalent and manufacturing tolerance thinking treats defects uniformly; (4) the adversarial case handled entirely separately, since acceptance sampling assumes a non-adversarial defect process; and (5) throughput and accuracy traded explicitly, because the quota is the variable most affecting error and is set without reference to it.

## Target Customer
Intake operations leadership, quality functions at recommerce platforms, and the quality engineering profession for whom heterogeneous item inspection is an unusual variant.

## Impact If Solved
A century of inspection measurement exists and this sector spot-checks. A circulating reference set solves the repeatability study on unique items, and consequence-weighted error measurement is what makes a grading programme economic rather than merely consistent.
