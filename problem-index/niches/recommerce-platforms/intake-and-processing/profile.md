## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Intake is the managed platform's whole cost base and its whole quality position: receive, inspect, grade, authenticate, photograph, list, store. It is where the capital goes and where the differentiation against peer-to-peer platforms lives.

It is **not terminal**, because the two judgements inside it are unrelated contests. Condition grading is a calibration problem on a continuous scale with no adversary: the same jacket should receive the same grade from any grader at any site, the failure is inconsistency, and the remedies are rubric design, measurement, calibration and imaging. Authentication is an adversarial binary against counterfeiters who study exactly what is checked: the failure is a wrong call, the costs are asymmetric and severe in both directions, and the remedies are reference data, imaging technology, expertise and process design. One has no opponent and is wrong by degrees; the other is defined by its opponent and is wrong absolutely. Filter Notes in the overview records the two rejected alternatives; the sub-niches below are the split.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Intake stations, grading rubrics, photography setups, computer vision for category and attribute recognition, reference databases and physical verification technologies. The gaps are specific to each judgement and are stated in the sub-niches.
