## Why These Niches

Every unit is one of a kind and must be priced individually at a cost per item that is unforgiving, because the item may sell for twenty dollars. Price too high and it occupies warehouse space for months; too low and margin is given away on something that took real labour to process. That decision is the economic core of the managed model, it is made hundreds of times a shift, and it remains largely manual or crudely automated. Pricing is therefore the largest contested surface here and the one on which the sector's persistent unit-economics difficulty turns.

Intake and processing **failed the filter as one niche**. Condition grading is a calibration contest on a continuum: the same jacket must receive the same grade from any grader at any site, against a rubric, with no adversary — the enemy is inconsistency and the remedy is measurement and calibration. Authentication is an adversarial binary against counterfeits designed specifically to defeat the checks being made, with a fraud loss on one side of the error and a wronged seller on the other. The expertise, the failure economics, the tooling and the nature of the judgement have nothing in common. Decomposed below.

The two underdigitised areas are both decisions about what the platform should be carrying. Intake cost is fixed per item and revenue is not, which makes the decision to accept an item at all the most consequential and least examined one in the model. And markdown is run on rules rather than on demand, so items that will never sell occupy space that costs money while items that would have sold at a higher price are discounted on a schedule.

The two underserved constituencies are the intake processor making economically decisive judgements against a quota with no feedback on whether they were right, and the authenticator deciding in minutes against adversaries designing specifically to defeat them, with asymmetric consequences on both sides of the error.

The automation niches are catalogue and attribute generation, where recognition is deployed and listing is still manual, and the corpus connecting observed condition to realised price — the only dataset of its kind and used once per item.

## Filter Notes

Seven of the eight level-1 niches are terminal. **Intake & Processing** is not: it names the physical pipeline rather than a contest, and the two judgements inside it are unrelated. Grading is a consistency problem on a graded scale, where the failure is two people disagreeing and the remedies are rubric design, calibration and measurement. Authentication is an adversarial classification against opponents who study the checks, where the failure is a wrong binary call and the remedies are reference data, imaging and expertise. One has no adversary and one is defined by having one; one is wrong by degrees and one is wrong absolutely. Decomposed into two contested sub-niches.

Two candidates were rejected. *Reverse logistics and warehouse automation* was rejected because it belongs to the logistics and warehousing industries covered separately in this vault, and the platform's role is procurement and integration. *Peer-to-peer listing discovery* was rejected because its contest — surfacing unique items to buyers who cannot name them — is covered as a contested sub-niche under online marketplaces.
