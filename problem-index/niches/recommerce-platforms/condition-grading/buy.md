**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to make the same item receive the same grade from any grader at any site — and whoever does that fixes the whole pipeline, because grading is the input that price, listing, buyer expectation and returns all depend on.

## The Problem
Several fields make subjective categorical judgements at scale and take consistency seriously. Radiology measures inter-reader agreement and runs calibration sessions. Sensory science trains panels against reference standards and validates them statistically. Manufacturing runs attribute agreement analysis on inspectors. Wine, coffee and diamond grading all maintain reference materials and certification. Every one of those disciplines concluded that a written rubric and training are insufficient without measurement and reference standards, and recommerce grading has exactly a written rubric and training.

## What Already Exists
Attribute agreement analysis with appropriate agreement statistics; inter-rater reliability methodology; sensory panel training against physical reference standards; calibration sessions with consensus adjudication; certification and recertification programmes; and reference material libraries in the established grading industries.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to unique items graded at very high throughput. It requires: (1) photographic rather than physical reference standards, since the items cannot be circulated and images can — this substitution makes the whole practice applicable and is the specific adaptation; (2) calibration embedded in production rather than run as sessions, since throughput does not permit regular offsite calibration and seeded reference images can be inserted into the normal queue; (3) agreement measured on a scale with unequal consequences, since disagreement by one band on a cheap item and on an expensive one are not equivalent; (4) drift detection per grader, because grading standards drift over months and the established industries recertify periodically for exactly this reason; and (5) the rubric rewritten in observable terms, which the grading industries did long ago and which is the precondition for any of the measurement to mean anything.

## Target Customer
Grading operations and quality functions, recommerce platforms, and the sensory and inspection calibration communities.

## Impact If Solved
Every discipline that grades subjectively at scale concluded that a rubric and training are insufficient without reference standards and measurement. Photographic references and seeded calibration items make the whole practice work at recommerce throughput.
