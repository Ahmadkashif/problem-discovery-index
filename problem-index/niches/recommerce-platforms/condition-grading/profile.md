# Condition Grading

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/recommerce-platforms|Recommerce Platforms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to make the same item receive the same grade from any grader at any site — and whoever does that fixes the whole pipeline, because grading is the input that price, listing, buyer expectation and returns all depend on.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.6B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~17% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Moderate — vision deployed, subtleties unresolved
**Target Buyer:** Grading operations and quality functions
**Automation Potential:** Very High — this is a measurement consistency problem

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Grading rubrics are written, trained and audited at every serious platform, and two graders looking at the same jacket still assign different grades — which propagates into price, listing and returns. The rubric describes categories in words that people read differently; the items are all different, so there is no reference part to calibrate against; the grader has seconds; and nothing tells them afterwards whether the buyer agreed. It is a measurement system with substantial operator variation, applied to the most economically consequential attribute in the business, measured by a spot-check audit that compares a grader to another grader rather than to anything.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Written rubrics, training programmes, spot-check audits, and computer vision for some defect types. The gaps: no measurement of inter-grader agreement; no reference library to calibrate against; the grade discards the detailed observation that produced it; the buyer's implicit verdict — returns, complaints, realised price — is not fed back; and vision is used to detect defects rather than to enforce consistency.
