# The Intake Processor

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/recommerce-platforms|Recommerce Platforms]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give the person making the platform's most economically consequential judgements the support and the feedback to make them well — and whoever does that takes the quality, because the whole model's economics are decided at that station.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.3B US in intake labour
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** None — quota work with no feedback
**Target Buyer:** Operations leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — the support and the feedback are both buildable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Intake processors inspect, grade, photograph and list hundreds of items a shift against a quota, making judgements that determine the platform's economics with no feedback on whether they got them right. The grade they assign sets the price, shapes the buyer's expectation and determines the return probability; the photographs they take substantially determine whether the item sells; the attributes they enter determine whether it is findable. All of that is decided in under a minute per item, by somebody measured on items per hour, who will never learn what happened to any of them. It is the highest-leverage minute in the business performed under conditions designed for throughput.

## Current Tools & Gaps
An intake station, a rubric, a photography setup, a quota and a spot-check audit. The gaps: no feedback from outcomes; no decision support at the moment of judgement; the quota is uniform across items of wildly different value and difficulty; ergonomics and pacing are designed for volume; and nothing captures the observations the processor makes beyond the fields they fill.
