# OASIS Coding & Review Outsourcers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/home-health-agencies|Home Health Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Third-party clinical coding firms review the OASIS assessments agencies submit — checking diagnosis coding, functional scoring, and internal consistency — before they go to CMS. Pass 1 puts OASIS at 45-90 minutes per patient with timing errors triggering claim denials; these firms exist because agencies cannot get it right at volume.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-400 clinical coders, OASIS specialists, and quality reviewers
**Output:** Corrected assessments, coding recommendations, agency-level error pattern reporting
**Proprietary data:** Assessment error patterns across many agencies, joined to the payment and denial consequences that followed
**Clock:** OASIS submission windows tied to admission, recertification, and discharge, federally fixed
**Buyer:** VP of Clinical Services / COO
