# Electronic Visit Verification Aggregators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/home-health-agencies|Home Health Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Sandata, HHAeXchange, and their peers operate the state EVV aggregators the 21st Century Cures Act mandated, collecting a verified record of every home visit — who, where, when, how long — for entire state Medicaid programmes.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-200 data, integration, and programme staff
**Output:** Visit verification, state programme reporting, exception and fraud flagging
**Proprietary data:** Verified visit-level records for entire state Medicaid home care populations — location, duration, and caregiver for millions of visits
**Clock:** Federal mandate and state programme reporting deadlines
**Buyer:** VP of Data / State Programme Director
