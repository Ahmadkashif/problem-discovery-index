# Post-Acute Market & Benchmarking Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/home-health-agencies|Home Health Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms such as Trella Health and Strategic Healthcare Programs sell referral pattern, market share, and outcome benchmarking to home health and hospice providers — where a hospital's discharges are going, how an agency's rehospitalization rate compares to peers, which referral sources are winnable. This is the one substantial insight pocket in home health that operates outside the clinical data perimeter, on licensed CMS claims and voluntarily contributed benchmarking data.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-200 analysts, data engineers, and client-facing researchers
**Output:** Referral and market share analytics, quality benchmarking, sales targeting intelligence
**Proprietary data:** Licensed Medicare claims extracts plus a voluntary contributor network of thousands of agencies submitting outcomes — the network took two decades to assemble and cannot be licensed
**Clock:** Quarterly data releases and publicly reported quality refreshes
**Buyer:** Chief Analytics Officer / VP of Data Products
