# Medicare Advantage Home Health Utilization Management

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/home-health-agencies|Home Health Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Health plans and their delegated utilization management vendors authorize home health episodes and visit counts for Medicare Advantage members, deciding how much care each patient receives.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-400 nurse reviewers, medical directors, and analysts
**Output:** Authorization determinations, utilization analytics, network performance review
**Proprietary data:** Authorization requests joined to claims and downstream outcomes — the only view of whether care denied or approved changed anything
**Clock:** Regulatory turnaround requirements on authorization decisions
**Buyer:** VP of Utilization Management / Chief Medical Officer
