# Medicare Cost Report Preparation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/home-health-agencies|Home Health Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Specialist accounting practices prepare the annual Medicare cost reports home health agencies must file, allocating costs across cost centres under prescribed methods and defending the result on audit.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-70 cost report preparers and healthcare accountants
**Output:** Filed cost reports, allocation strategies, audit responses
**Proprietary data:** Allocation approaches and audit outcomes across many agencies; the filed reports themselves are public
**Clock:** Statutory filing deadlines with payment consequences
**Buyer:** Practice leader
