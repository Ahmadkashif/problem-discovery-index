# Home Health Revenue Cycle & Denials Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/home-health-agencies|Home Health Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Outsourced revenue cycle firms specializing in home health bill, follow up, and appeal on behalf of agencies. Under the Patient-Driven Groupings Model, payment depends on assessment coding and timing that Pass 1 records as a routine denial source, and the firm's business is recovering what the agency's own documentation cost it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-800 billers, appeal specialists, and analysts
**Output:** Claim submission, denial analysis, appeal narratives, recovery reporting
**Proprietary data:** Denial reasons joined to appeal arguments and outcomes across many agencies and payers — the only record of what actually overturns a denial
**Clock:** Timely filing limits and appeal deadlines, absolute and short
**Buyer:** COO / VP of Operations
