# Home Health EHR Platform Data Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/home-health-agencies|Home Health Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The data groups at WellSky, Homecare Homebase, and Axxess run scheduling, clinical documentation, OASIS, and billing for most of the industry, and see visits, assessments, and outcomes across a very large share of US home health.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 data scientists, analysts, and product data staff
**Output:** In-product analytics, benchmarking, scheduling and routing features
**Proprietary data:** Clinical assessments, visit records, and outcomes across a majority of the industry — with the routing and deterioration signals Pass 1 says the platforms do not use
**Clock:** OASIS and billing cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Product
