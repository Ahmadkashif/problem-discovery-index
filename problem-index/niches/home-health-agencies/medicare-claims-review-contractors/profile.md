# Medicare Claims Review Contractors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/home-health-agencies|Home Health Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Administrative and program integrity contractors process home health claims, run medical review, and pursue improper payment recovery on behalf of Medicare — the entity whose determinations define the rules every agency and billing firm is trying to satisfy.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-2,000 clinical reviewers, analysts, and data scientists per contractor
**Output:** Claim determinations, medical review findings, improper payment analyses, provider education
**Proprietary data:** The complete national claims and determination record for the benefit
**Clock:** Statutory claim processing and appeal timelines
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — federal contract
