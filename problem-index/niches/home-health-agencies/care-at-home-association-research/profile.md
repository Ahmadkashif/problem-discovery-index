**Parent Industry:** [[industries/home-health-agencies|Home Health Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The industry association's research and policy staff analyze payment rule proposals, model rate impacts on members, and produce the workforce and access research used in advocacy.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-20 policy analysts and researchers
**Output:** Rate impact analyses, regulatory comment, workforce and access research
**Proprietary data:** Member surveys and modelling built on public claims and rate files
**Clock:** Annual payment rule comment periods, which are fixed and short
**Buyer:** VP of Research and Policy — member funded
