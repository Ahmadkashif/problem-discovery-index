# Home Health Regulatory & Survey Consulting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/home-health-agencies|Home Health Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Consulting firms prepare agencies for state and accreditation surveys, respond to conditions-level deficiencies, defend additional documentation requests and audits, and build the compliance programmes the conditions of participation require. A failed survey can end an agency's Medicare participation.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 clinical compliance consultants, nurses, and regulatory specialists
**Output:** Mock surveys, deficiency responses, plans of correction, audit defence
**Proprietary data:** Deficiency patterns and correction outcomes across many agencies and survey regions — what surveyors in a given region actually cite
**Clock:** Survey cycles and statutory correction deadlines with participation at stake
**Buyer:** Practice leader / Managing Director
