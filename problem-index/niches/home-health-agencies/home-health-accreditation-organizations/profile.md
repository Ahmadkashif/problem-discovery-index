# Home Health Accreditation Organizations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/home-health-agencies|Home Health Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
CHAP, ACHC, and the Joint Commission's home care programme accredit agencies against published standards, and their accreditation carries Medicare deemed status — an agency that loses it loses its ability to bill.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 surveyors, standards staff, and analysts
**Output:** Accreditation decisions, survey findings, standards development, education
**Proprietary data:** Survey findings across thousands of agencies over multiple cycles — an organizational quality record about the agency rather than about any patient
**Clock:** Three-year accreditation cycles with Medicare participation attached
**Buyer:** Chief of Standards / VP of Accreditation
