# Broadcast Media Asset Management With Licensing Logic

**Niche:** [[niches/film-distributors/stock-and-archival-footage-licensing/profile|Stock & Archival Footage Licensing]]
**Industry:** [[industries/film-distributors|Film Distributors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Broadcasters' media asset management systems now auto-tag footage with AI; they are built to find material for in-house use, not to quote and license clips to outside producers by the second.
**Tags:** #cnns #transformers #data-integration #automation #revenue-impact #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to put a clearable clip of exactly the shot a producer asked for in front of them within hours, and whoever answers the research request first with footage that clears takes the licence.

## The Problem
An archive needs search, preview, quoting, licensing and delivery in one flow. It usually runs a DAM for storage, a website for previews, a spreadsheet for rate cards and email for quotes.

## What Already Exists
Enterprise media asset management platforms used by broadcasters and news organisations, with AI auto-tagging, transcription and face recognition.

## The Customization Gap
An archive needs:
- per-second quoting from rate cards by use, media, territory and term;
- clip-level rights flags (identifiable people, music, third-party copyright) that block or condition a quote;
- client-facing screeners with timecode burn-in and watermarks;
- licence documents generated from the quote;
- delivery of the licensed segment only;
- pricing for a library with a few staff rather than a network.

## Target Customer
Small and mid-sized archival footage libraries and film archives that license commercially.

## Impact If Solved
Quote-to-licence in one session instead of a day of emails, and an archive that can sell without a researcher handling every step.
