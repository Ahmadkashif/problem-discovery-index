# Streaming Demand & Title Valuation Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/film-distributors|Film Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Vendors such as Parrot Analytics, Luminate (film and TV) and Samba TV estimate how much audiences want each title across platforms and territories, and sell the estimates as dashboards and custom valuations. Parrot markets a content-valuation product that converts demand into dollar estimates, and pitches it for anchoring minimum guarantees and licence fees by territory. Distributors, sales agents, platforms and investors pay by subscription and per project.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-200 data scientists, analysts and research staff per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Title-level demand indices, territory demand comparisons, title and slate valuations, custom licensing studies
**Proprietary data:** Multi-year, cross-platform demand series per title and territory built from the vendor's own signal collection. Most inputs are public behaviour, but the assembled history and models are the vendor's.
**Clock:** The licensing market calendar and individual negotiations, set by the clients
**Buyer:** Head of content strategy or insights at a distributor or platform; vendor's chief data officer
