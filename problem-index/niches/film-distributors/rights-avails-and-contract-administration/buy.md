# Enterprise Rights Platforms Sized for a 300-Title Library

**Niche:** [[niches/film-distributors/rights-avails-and-contract-administration/profile|Rights, Avails & Contract Administration]]
**Industry:** [[industries/film-distributors|Film Distributors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** The rights and royalties platforms the studios use already model grants and windows correctly; they are sold, priced and implemented for catalogues fifty times the size of a typical independent's.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #sets-and-logic #data-integration #compliance #automation #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to answer whether a title is free for a given territory, medium and window on a given date correctly and in minutes, from contracts written in prose, and whoever never grants the same right twice keeps both its licensors and its licensees.

## The Problem
An independent distributor that outgrows its spreadsheet faces an enterprise sales process, an implementation project and a licence fee sized for a studio. So it stays on the spreadsheet until a double grant forces the issue.

## What Already Exists
Rightsline (including FilmTrack and RSG Media since 2024) and other enterprise rights and royalties suites, with mature avails engines, window templates, participations and royalty modules. A third-party review notes enterprise-only sales and no public pricing.

## The Customization Gap
The downmarket version needs:
- self-serve onboarding where contracts are loaded as documents and extracted, not keyed;
- a default media and window vocabulary matching independent contracts (including AVOD and FAST);
- pricing per title or per active licence;
- prebuilt reports for the questions independents ask before markets (open territories by title, expiring licences, reversions due);
- a producer-facing view of the rights held from each producer.

## Target Customer
Independent distributors and sales agents with 100 to 2,000 titles, and the rights platforms themselves if they choose to serve them.

## Impact If Solved
Rights management reaches the firms where errors are most frequent and least affordable, on a cost that fits their revenue.
