# Audience Tracking & Test-Screening Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/film-distributors|Film Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Film research firms (NRG, Screen Engine/ASI with its PostTrak exit polls, MarketCast, and Greenlight Analytics, which bought The Quorum in 2025) run recruited test screenings before picture lock, track awareness and intent in the weeks before release, and poll audiences leaving theatres. Studios and distributors pay per study and by subscription. The results shape the cut, the campaign, the release date and the opening-weekend forecast that the trade press reports.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-60 research analysts and statisticians per leading firm, counting analysts only; recruiters and screening moderators are excluded as production staff (estimate)
**Output:** Test-screening scores and diagnostics, tracking reports, opening-weekend forecasts, exit-poll scores
**Proprietary data:** Decades of screening scores and tracking curves for thousands of films, joined to their actual openings and runs. These are the normative databases every score is benchmarked against.
**Clock:** The client's release date and the pre-lock edit schedule. Both are fixed weeks ahead, but they are set by the distributor that commissions the work.
**Buyer:** Head of research at a studio or distributor; firm's chief research officer
