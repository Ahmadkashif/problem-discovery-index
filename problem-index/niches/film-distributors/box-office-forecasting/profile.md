# Box Office Forecasting & Release-Calendar Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/film-distributors|Film Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Small analytics firms and trade publishers forecast market-level and title-level box office and analyse the release calendar for studios, distributors, exhibitors and investors. Gower Street Analytics publishes and revises a global box office projection ($34.7B for 2026 as of April 2026) and sells client reports. Boxoffice's forecasting service and independent analysts do the same at title level.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 3-10 analysts per firm; no single firm plausibly runs a forecasting team of 10+ (estimate)
**Output:** Market and title forecasts, release-date competition analysis, client briefings
**Proprietary data:** Archives of their own past forecasts against actuals, plus licensed box office history. Most inputs are licensed or public.
**Clock:** The distributors' release calendar and annual planning cycles
**Buyer:** Founder or head of analytics
