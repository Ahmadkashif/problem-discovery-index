# Profit Participation & Royalty Auditors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/film-distributors|Film Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Entertainment accounting practices that audit distributors' and studios' participation and royalty statements for producers, talent, financiers and licensors. They test distribution fees against contract terms, recoupable expenses against caps and receipts, cross-collateralisation, interest and overhead charges, and unreported receipts, then present and negotiate a claim. Participants pay hourly or on contingency against amounts recovered.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 auditors per leading practice, across perhaps 10-20 specialist firms (estimate)
**Output:** Audit reports and claims; settlement negotiation support; expert testimony in litigation
**Proprietary data:** A cross-distributor archive of audit findings: which counterparties misapply which terms, by how much, and how claims settled. No single participant or distributor sees this.
**Clock:** Contractual incontestability periods (statements become binding unless objected to within a set term, commonly two to three years, estimate) and, where claims escalate, court and arbitration dates
**Buyer:** Managing partner of the entertainment audit practice

**Kill switches:** two are judged separately.
- *Data isn't theirs to use:* live. The distributor's statements and books are opened to the auditor under the contract's audit clause, for that audit only, and settlements usually carry confidentiality terms. This differs from the catalogue valuers logged under music publishing as [[niches/music-publishers/music-catalogue-valuation-specialists/profile|🔍 Music Catalogue Valuation Specialists]] (47, no live switch). Their data comes from their own client under engagement confidentiality, and publishing aggregate multiples from it is established practice. An auditor's data comes from the opposing party under a use-limited audit right, and no comparable aggregate publication exists. The ruling matches [[niches/music-publishers/music-royalty-audit-practices/profile|🔍 Music Royalty Audit Practices]] (46).
- *Compliance gate (privilege and work product):* live for the litigation share of the work. Expert testimony and litigation consulting are retained through counsel as attorney work product, which follows the vault's majority rule for litigation work done through counsel. The contractual audit itself sits outside privilege, so this switch alone would narrow the product rather than stall it.

**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. This is the strongest near-miss on shape: a repeatable audit with a contractual deadline and a cross-counterparty record nobody else holds. That record is locked behind the use limits that make the audits possible, and the litigation work runs through counsel.
