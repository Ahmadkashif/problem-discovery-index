# Studio & Library-Aggregator Distribution Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/film-distributors|Film Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The firms that aggregate distribution at scale (the majors' theatrical and worldwide distribution arms, the mini-majors, and library aggregators such as FilmRise and Cineverse with tens of thousands of titles) run in-house teams that forecast releases, model windows, plan dating and price library deals. Their analysis serves their own decisions and is never invoiced.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 analysts at the largest distribution arms; under 10 at most aggregators (estimate)
**Output:** Internal release forecasts, window and dating models, library performance reports, licence pricing support
**Proprietary data:** Complete title-level revenue by window, territory and licensee across the firm's slate and library. This is the outcome record the rest of the industry lacks.
**Clock:** Release dates and licence negotiations, set by the firm itself
**Buyer:** Head of distribution strategy or business intelligence
