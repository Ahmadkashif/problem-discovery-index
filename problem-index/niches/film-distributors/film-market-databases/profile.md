# Film Market Databases & Buyer Directories

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/film-distributors|Film Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Online databases of films for sale, sales companies and buyers by territory, used to plan market meetings and track who bought what. Cinando, run by the Marché du Film, The Film Catalogue and newer platforms such as Vitrina are representative. Sales agents and buyers pay subscriptions, often bundled with market accreditation.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-25 data entry, editorial and product staff per platform (estimate)
**Output:** Searchable listings of titles, companies, contacts and announced deals
**Proprietary data:** Listings and screening logs that companies supply, plus announced deals. Prices are rarely included.
**Clock:** The market calendar
**Buyer:** Platform director
