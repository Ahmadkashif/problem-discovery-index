# Library E-Resource Licensing Adapted to Film Screenings

**Niche:** [[niches/film-distributors/non-theatrical-and-educational-licensing/profile|Non-Theatrical & Educational Licensing]]
**Industry:** [[industries/film-distributors|Film Distributors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Libraries already license journals and e-books through mature subscription and usage-reporting systems; film public performance is still bought title by title, screening by screening.
**Tags:** #data-integration #feature-engineering #time-series-forecasting #automation #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to be the one place a school, library or community group can license a public screening of the title it wants, and whoever covers the most studios' titles with the simplest licence takes the venue's renewal.

## The Problem
A university licenses thousands of e-resources with standard terms, usage statistics and renewals. The same institution handles film screenings through per-title orders, separate classroom-streaming licences that often exclude public performance, and inconsistent records of what was shown.

## What Already Exists
Library e-resource management and usage-reporting standards (link resolvers, COUNTER-style usage reports, consortium licensing), educational streaming platforms with per-title licences, and agency annual site licences for K-12.

## The Customization Gap
Film needs:
- licence types that separate classroom use, streaming and public performance;
- screening-level usage reporting tied to the licence;
- consortium pricing across campuses;
- renewal forecasting from screening history;
- catalogue coverage spanning several booking agencies and distributors in one interface.

## Target Customer
Educational distributors and booking agencies selling to colleges and library consortia.

## Impact If Solved
Film licensing fits the procurement systems institutions already use, so renewals rise and administrative cost on both sides falls.
