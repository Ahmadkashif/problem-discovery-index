# Collection Account Managers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/film-distributors|Film Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Neutral administrators such as Fintage House and Freeway Entertainment hold a financed film's receipts in a dedicated account. They pay lenders, sales agents, guild reserves, investors and producers in the order the collection agreement sets, and issue statements to every beneficiary. They charge a setup fee and a percentage of collections.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Collection and royalty administration teams of tens per firm, but these are accountants and administrators processing statements rather than analysts (estimate)
**Output:** Collection statements and disbursements
**Proprietary data:** Receipts and waterfall histories across many financed films, held as fiduciary for the beneficiaries
**Clock:** Distribution periods in each collection agreement
**Buyer:** Managing director
