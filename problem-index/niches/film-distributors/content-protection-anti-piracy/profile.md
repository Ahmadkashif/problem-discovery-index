# Content Protection & Anti-Piracy Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/film-distributors|Film Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors such as Vobile, Friend MTS, Irdeto and MarkMonitor fingerprint films, watch torrent, cyberlocker, streaming-site and social platforms for copies, verify matches and send takedown notices at volume. They also report where and how fast a title leaked. Studios, distributors, streamers and sports rights holders pay for detection, enforcement and piracy intelligence. The studios' coalition, the Alliance for Creativity and Entertainment, runs investigations and litigation alongside them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 analysts, investigators and data scientists per leading vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Verified infringement detections, takedown enforcement, leak-source and piracy-demand reports per title
**Proprietary data:** Years of detection and takedown history per title and site, joined to release dates and windows. This record shows where piracy demand rises when a window closes or a title is withheld from a territory.
**Clock:** The release window itself, when a leak does most damage, plus platform takedown response times. The release date is the client's own, not a third party's.
**Buyer:** Head of content protection at a studio or distributor; vendor's head of anti-piracy operations
