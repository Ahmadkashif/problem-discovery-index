# Real-Estate Comparable Pricing for Licence Packages

**Niche:** [[niches/film-distributors/library-licence-pricing/profile|Library Licence Pricing]]
**Industry:** [[industries/film-distributors|Film Distributors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Appraisers and pricing engines already value heterogeneous, rarely traded assets from adjusted comparables; library licences are priced the same way, by hand.
**Tags:** #k-nearest-neighbors #gradient-boosting #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to price a library title or package for a specific buyer, territory and window close to what that buyer will actually pay, and whoever knows the clearing price best closes the deal without leaving money on the table.

## The Problem
Each licence is a unique bundle (titles, territory, window, term, exclusivity) traded rarely and privately. Salespeople price it by recalling a few similar deals and adjusting in their heads.

## What Already Exists
Automated valuation models and comparable-adjustment tools in real estate and equipment appraisal; demand and valuation data from entertainment vendors.

## The Customization Gap
Licensing needs:
- comparables defined over title features, buyer, territory, window and term rather than location and size;
- adjustments for exclusivity and package composition;
- the firm's own confidential deal history as the comparable base, kept private;
- an explanation of each adjustment a salesperson can use in negotiation.

## Target Customer
Library sales teams and the lenders and acquirers valuing licence pipelines.

## Impact If Solved
Pricing becomes explainable and repeatable, and junior salespeople price like senior ones sooner.
