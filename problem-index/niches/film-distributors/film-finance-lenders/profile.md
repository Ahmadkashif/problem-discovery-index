# Film Finance Lenders & Gap Financiers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/film-distributors|Film Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Banks' entertainment lending groups and specialist funds lend against distribution contracts, minimum guarantees and unsold territories (gap), using sales agents' estimates as collateral evidence. They price each loan on how much of the estimate they believe.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 3-10 credit and production finance staff per lender (estimate)
**Output:** Credit decisions and loan terms
**Proprietary data:** Loan books joined to the sales estimates they relied on and the collections that followed
**Clock:** Production start and delivery dates
**Buyer:** Head of entertainment lending
