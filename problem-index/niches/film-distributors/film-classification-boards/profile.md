# Film Classification & Ratings Boards

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/film-distributors|Film Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Before a film is released theatrically, distributors submit it for an age rating in each territory. In the US that is CARA, the MPA's ratings board, which rates roughly 700 films a year with a board of about ten parents and is funded by distributors' fees (CNBC, 2022). Abroad, statutory or industry classifiers such as the BBFC do the same. The rating decides which audiences can be sold to, and in the US whether most exhibitors will book the film at all.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 raters, examiners and compliance staff per board (estimate)
**Output:** Ratings and content descriptors per title; appeals decisions; classification guidelines
**Proprietary data:** Decades of rating decisions with the reasons and edits behind them, by scene and content type
**Clock:** Each distributor's release date. Exhibitors won't book an unrated film, but the date is set by the submitter.
**Buyer:** Chair of the ratings board; chief executive of a national classifier
