# Box Office Reporting & Measurement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/film-distributors|Film Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Box office measurement collects daily theatre-level grosses from exhibitors and reports them to distributors, exhibitors and the press. Comscore is the industry's reporting currency in the US and many other markets. Distributors use the theatre-level data to book, expand and settle; exhibitors benchmark against it; and weekend figures become the public scoreboard (~$8.9B in US/Canada in 2025 on Comscore's figure as cited in AMC's FY2025 10-K; Box Office Mojo's calendar table, with narrower coverage, shows $8.66B across 670 releases).

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 data operations staff and analysts on box office measurement (estimate)
**Output:** Daily and weekend theatre-level grosses, market share reports, historical box office databases
**Proprietary data:** The complete theatre-by-day gross history for the market, reported directly by exhibitors
**Clock:** The daily and weekend reporting cycle, an industry convention run by the vendor and the distributors themselves
**Buyer:** General manager of the movies measurement business
