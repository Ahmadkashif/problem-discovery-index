# Rights & Royalties Management Software

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/film-distributors|Film Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Enterprise software for contracts, rights, avails, windows, participations and royalties, sold to studios, broadcasters, distributors and other rights holders. Rightsline consolidated the category in 2024 by acquiring FilmTrack (nearly 200 entertainment customers) from City National Bank in May and RSG Media from Naviga in September.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 20 analytics and research staff; most headcount is engineering, implementation and support (estimate)
**Output:** Software licences, implementations, and reporting modules inside the product
**Proprietary data:** None of its own. It holds clients' structured rights and royalty data in its schema.
**Clock:** Clients' implementation and renewal cycles
**Buyer:** Chief product officer

**Kill switches:** Data isn't theirs to use: the rights grids and royalty records belong to clients. After the 2024 consolidation the vendor is also the entrenched incumbent whose schema holds the workflow, which makes it a channel or a competitor for any insight product rather than a buyer.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. It matters to the map as the system where the industry's structured rights data already lives at the top end of the market.
