# International Sales & Territory Licensing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/film-distributors|Film Distributors]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to issue territory sales estimates that buyers actually meet and lenders will finance against, and whoever's estimates land closest to the minimum guarantees finally closed gets the next package from producers and financiers.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$200M a year in sales-agent commission and fees on ~$1.5B of independent film rights sold territory by territory worldwide (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of US independent distribution and library receipts, counting commission only (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — market databases and screening platforms are digital; estimates, avails sheets and deal tracking are PDFs and spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** Managing director or head of sales at an international sales company; head of production finance at a lender
**Automation Potential:** Medium for estimates, avails and deal tracking; low for the market selling itself

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Sales agents license independent films to one distributor per territory, often before the film is shot. Their sales estimates (a low and high "ask" and "take" per territory) are what gap lenders and equity financiers lend against, so an estimate is a forecast with money behind it. The market has contracted sharply. FilmTake estimates that a strong title once sold 20–35 territories and now typically closes ~10–14, with advances down 30–70% from the mid-2010s, and Pay-1 output deals have largely gone. The work runs on the market calendar: the European Film Market, Cannes, Toronto and AFM. A miss is visible within a season.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Cinando and other market databases list films and buyers; screening links run through secure platforms; deal tracking runs in Excel; estimates are issued as PDFs. The gaps: no archive of past estimates graded against closed deals, territory by territory; buyer-level appetite lives in each salesperson's memory; and lenders have no independent check on an agent's numbers.
