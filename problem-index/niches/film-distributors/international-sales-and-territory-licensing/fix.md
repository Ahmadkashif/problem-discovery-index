# The Estimate Written From Last Year's Market

**Niche:** [[niches/film-distributors/international-sales-and-territory-licensing/profile|International Sales & Territory Licensing]]
**Industry:** [[industries/film-distributors|Film Distributors]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A new package's territory estimates are usually the last comparable package's estimates adjusted by feel, so figures the market stopped paying keep reaching lenders.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #feature-engineering #k-nearest-neighbors #data-integration #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to issue territory sales estimates that buyers actually meet and lenders will finance against, and whoever's estimates land closest to the minimum guarantees finally closed gets the next package from producers and financiers.

## The Problem
Producers need estimates fast to take a package to financiers. The head of sales opens the estimate for a similar film, changes the title and cast, and nudges each territory up or down. Recent evidence is never consulted systematically: which buyers passed on similar films, and what the last three comparable deals in Germany or Japan actually closed at. FilmTake estimates European advances fell 30–60% against 2014–2017. An estimate template that drifts slowly lags a market that moved fast.

## Why It's Still Broken
Closed prices and passes sit in deal memos and salespeople's inboxes rather than in a table the estimate can draw on. Nobody is asked to show the evidence behind each territory figure.

## What a Fix Looks Like
When an estimate is drafted, pull the most recent closed prices and passes for comparable packages in each territory from the agent's own deal records. Show them beside each figure, and flag any territory where the estimate exceeds everything closed for that tier in the past two years.

## Who Feels the Pain
Heads of sales whose credibility rides on the estimates; producers who finance against them; gap lenders who discover the shortfall at collection.

## Impact If Fixed
Estimates move with the market rather than a year behind it. That protects the agent's standing with lenders at almost no extra drafting time.
