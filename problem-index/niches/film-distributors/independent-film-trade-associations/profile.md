# Independent Film Trade Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/film-distributors|Film Distributors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Independent Film & Television Alliance runs the American Film Market, publishes model distribution agreements and operates an arbitration tribunal for distribution disputes. The Motion Picture Association publishes market statistics and runs the ratings board and anti-piracy coalition for the majors. Members pay dues; AFM exhibitors pay for stands.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Fewer than 10 research and statistics staff in each (estimate)
**Output:** Market statistics, model contracts, arbitration awards, policy submissions
**Proprietary data:** Arbitration case files and member-reported statistics
**Clock:** The market and policy calendar
**Buyer:** Association president
