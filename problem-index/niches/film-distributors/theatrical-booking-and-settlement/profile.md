# Theatrical Booking & Settlement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/film-distributors|Film Distributors]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to settle every engagement at the terms actually agreed within days of the run ending, and whoever settles fastest with the fewest write-downs keeps exhibitors booking its films and producers trusting its statements.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$60M a year in booking, settlement and collections labour and tooling at independent distributors and the booking agents who work for them (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1.5% of US independent distribution and library receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — grosses arrive digitally; terms are agreed by phone and settlements are negotiated by email
**Target Buyer:** Head of theatrical sales; VP of distribution finance
**Automation Potential:** High for reconciliation and settlement worksheets, low for the negotiation with each film buyer

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is the distributor's side of film rent; the exhibitor's side is [[niches/movie-theaters/exhibitor-film-rent-and-settlement/profile|Exhibitor Film Rent & Settlement]] under movie theaters. Every engagement is a separate licence "on a film-by-film and theatre-by-theatre basis" (AMC's FY2025 10-K), usually a box office share that slides by week, sometimes a fixed rate agreed after the run. Exhibitor accruals are "based on estimates of the final settlement". Film rent across the industry comes to roughly half of admissions. A specialty distributor runs dozens to hundreds of engagements per release with a booker or two, and some hire outside booking agents (a 512199 activity) to place films in independent theatres. The agencies that book for theatres are logged under movie theaters as [[niches/movie-theaters/independent-film-booking-agencies/profile|🔍 Independent Film Booking Agencies]] (fails gate). The 2020 termination of the Paramount consent decrees, with a two-year sunset on the block-booking and circuit-dealing bans, removed the old rulebook without replacing the practice.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Comscore box office reporting, exhibitor statements in PDF and spreadsheet, accounting packages for invoicing, email and phone for terms. The gaps: no engagement-level terms record; reconciliation of reported grosses against box office reports done by eye; no history of how each circuit settles relative to terms.
