# Collection Account Management Without the Minimum Budget

**Niche:** [[niches/film-distributors/the-licensor-producer/profile|The Licensor Producer]]
**Industry:** [[industries/film-distributors|Film Distributors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Collection account managers give financed films an independent waterfall and statements; their setup costs and minimums keep most small films outside it.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #compliance #automation #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give producers a distribution statement they can verify line by line against the receipts behind it, and whoever is most verifiably transparent wins the next film from the filmmakers who were burned last time.

## The Problem
A film financed by a lender or several equity investors usually has a collection account: receipts go to a neutral account and a manager pays out under the agreed waterfall. Small films, the majority, are paid directly by distributors with no neutral party.

## What Already Exists
Collection account managers such as Fintage House and Freeway Entertainment, with setup fees around $8,000 on a low-budget film plus a percentage of receipts (Film Independent, 2018). Fintech escrow and payment-splitting tools exist outside film.

## The Customization Gap
A lightweight version needs:
- the film waterfall (fees, expenses, recoupment order, deferments, guild residual reserves) expressed as configurable rules;
- direct ingestion of distributor and platform statements;
- automated payouts to many small participants;
- investor reporting;
- pricing that works on a film earning tens of thousands of dollars a year rather than millions.

## Target Customer
Producers of low-budget features and documentaries, crowdfunded films with many small investors, and the distributors who want to offer neutral accounting as a selling point.

## Impact If Solved
Neutral accounting reaches the films that most need it, and distributors gain a credible way to show they pay.
