# The Expense Line With No Receipt

**Niche:** [[niches/film-distributors/the-licensor-producer/profile|The Licensor Producer]]
**Industry:** [[industries/film-distributors|Film Distributors]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Recoupable expenses appear on statements as round totals, and a producer asking what they were for waits months for an answer that never comes with receipts.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #compliance #workflow-orchestration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give producers a distribution statement they can verify line by line against the receipts behind it, and whoever is most verifiably transparent wins the next film from the filmmakers who were burned last time.

## The Problem
"Marketing — $48,000" or "Delivery and servicing — $12,500" sits on the statement between gross receipts and the producer's share. Lawyers advise producers to demand receipts for every expense, but distributors keep expenses in an accounting system organised by vendor and month, not by film and statement line.

## Why It's Still Broken
Allocating shared costs (market attendance, a trailer used for several titles, a FAST delivery batch) to individual films is done in a spreadsheet at quarter end. The allocation logic is never shown, so the distributor can't answer quickly even when it wants to.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Tag expenses to titles when they are incurred and keep the allocation rule for shared costs. Generate a backup schedule for every statement line, with the invoices attached, available to the producer through a portal.

## Who Feels the Pain
Producers waiting for answers; distributor royalty staff fielding the questions; distributors losing films to rivals with better reputations.

## Impact If Fixed
Most statement disputes end at the backup schedule rather than at an audit, and the distributor's accounting becomes a selling point instead of a liability.
