# FAST & AVOD Library Programming

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/film-distributors|Film Distributors]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn thousands of low-value library titles into ad-supported viewing hours at a delivery and metadata cost per title below what each title earns, and whoever programmes and delivers the most titles profitably takes the platforms' channel slots and the rights holders' libraries.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600M a year in US ad-supported and FAST revenue earned by independent library holders and aggregators (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of US independent distribution and library receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium-High — delivery, playout and ad reporting are digital; title selection and channel scheduling are manual
**Target Buyer:** Head of FAST and AVOD at a library aggregator; channel programming lead
**Automation Potential:** High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Ad-supported platforms (Tubi, Pluto TV, The Roku Channel and others) pay a revenue share on ads served against viewing, and FAST channels run linear schedules built from library titles. Revenue per title is small, often tens or hundreds of dollars a month (estimate), so the work is industrial. Each platform needs its own delivery spec, metadata, artwork, captions and ad-break markers, and channels need schedules that keep viewers watching. Aggregators such as FilmRise and Cineverse hold the volume. The winner has the lowest cost per delivered title and the schedules with the best minutes watched per hour.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Platform partner portals, playout and scheduling software for FAST channels, ad-revenue dashboards per platform, and metadata spreadsheets. The gaps: no title-level forecast of ad-supported earnings before paying to deliver; ad-break placement done by eye; schedules built by hand on hunches about adjacency.
