# Workers' Compensation Pharmacy Benefit Management

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/insurance-tpa|Insurance Third-Party Administrators (TPAs)]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Specialist PBMs manage prescriptions on workers' compensation claims, applying formularies, flagging opioid and polypharmacy risk, and reporting drug spend and clinical intervention outcomes to payers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 clinical pharmacists, analysts, and data scientists
**Output:** Formulary decisions, prescriber and utilization analysis, clinical intervention programmes, drug spend reporting
**Proprietary data:** Prescription histories on injured workers joined to claim duration and outcome — a longitudinal view no other party holds
**Clock:** Point-of-sale adjudication in real time; regulatory formulary effective dates
**Buyer:** Chief Clinical Officer / VP of Analytics
