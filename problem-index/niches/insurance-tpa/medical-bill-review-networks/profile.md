# Medical Bill Review & Repricing Networks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/insurance-tpa|Insurance Third-Party Administrators (TPAs)]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Bill review companies reprice every medical bill in a workers' compensation or liability claim against state fee schedules, network contracts, and usual-and-customary benchmarks, and are frequently paid a share of the reduction they achieve.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-2,000 bill reviewers, clinical coders, and analysts
**Output:** Repriced bills, coding and utilization findings, savings reporting, network performance analysis
**Proprietary data:** Provider billing patterns and repricing outcomes across millions of bills — the record of which providers bill what and which reductions survive dispute
**Clock:** Statutory bill payment deadlines with interest penalties
**Buyer:** Chief Operating Officer / VP of Analytics
