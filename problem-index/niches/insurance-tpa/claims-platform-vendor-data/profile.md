# Claims Platform Vendor Data Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/insurance-tpa|Insurance Third-Party Administrators (TPAs)]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The data groups at Origami Risk, Guidewire, FINEOS and their peers supply the claims administration systems TPAs run on, and see claim structures and workflows across many administrators — while Pass 1 records that many TPAs still operate on legacy mainframes with manual workarounds.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 data and product staff
**Output:** In-product analytics, benchmarking modules, workflow and reserve tooling
**Proprietary data:** Claim data across many administrators, held under client agreements
**Clock:** Client SLA and reporting cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Product Officer / VP of Data
