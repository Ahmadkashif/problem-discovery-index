# TPA Claims Analytics Organizations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/insurance-tpa|Insurance Third-Party Administrators (TPAs)]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup

## What They Do
The analytics organizations inside the large TPA platforms administer millions of workers' compensation and casualty claims for carriers and self-insured employers, and produce the outcome, cost containment, and reserve analysis those clients judge them on. Pass 1 identifies what the analysis is competing with: an examiner carrying 150-200 claims a month whose pattern recognition for suspicious claims and reserve accuracy is tacit and fragile to turnover.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,500 data scientists, actuaries, and claims analysts
**Output:** Reserve adequacy and development analysis, litigation and duration prediction, cost containment reporting, client outcome benchmarking
**Proprietary data:** Claim files with reserves, payments, litigation status, return-to-work, and closure across millions of claims and many jurisdictions
**Clock:** Contractual SLAs of 15-30 days on initial decisions, with financial penalties
**Buyer:** Chief Analytics Officer / Chief Data Officer
