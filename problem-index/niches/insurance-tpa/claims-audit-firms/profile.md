# Claims Audit Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/insurance-tpa|Insurance Third-Party Administrators (TPAs)]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Independent auditors review TPA claim files on behalf of carriers and self-insured employers, testing whether coverage decisions, reserves, and payments were handled correctly — the check on the administrator the client cannot perform itself.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 claims auditors and consultants
**Output:** File audit findings, reserve adequacy assessments, TPA performance reports, remediation recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Handling error patterns across many administrators and lines
**Clock:** Annual audit cycles and TPA contract renewals
**Buyer:** Practice leader
