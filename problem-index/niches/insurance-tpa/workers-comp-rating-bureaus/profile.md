# Workers' Compensation Rating Bureaus

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/insurance-tpa|Insurance Third-Party Administrators (TPAs)]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
The workers' compensation statistical and rating organizations collect unit statistical data on every policy and every claim from every carrier in their states, file the advisory loss costs that set premium, and compute the experience modification factor applied to individual employers. The mod is unusual in insurance: it is a published, individualized rating of one employer's loss record, and it directly multiplies what they pay.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-1,500 actuaries, statisticians, data analysts, and classification staff
**Output:** Advisory loss costs by class and state, experience rating factors for individual employers, classification systems, statistical plans and rate filings
**Proprietary data:** The complete premium and loss record for workers' compensation — every policy, every claim, at unit level, across the covered states
**Clock:** Annual filing calendars and experience rating anniversary dates, both fixed
**Buyer:** Chief Actuary / Chief Data Officer
