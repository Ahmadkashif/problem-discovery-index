# Workers' Compensation Research Institutes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/insurance-tpa|Insurance Third-Party Administrators (TPAs)]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Independent research institutes compare state workers' compensation systems on cost, duration, medical price, and outcomes, producing the studies legislators and regulators use when they change the rules.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-70 economists, researchers, and analysts
**Output:** Interstate comparison studies, medical price and utilization benchmarks, policy evaluations
**Proprietary data:** Claim-level data contributed by participating carriers and states for research use
**Clock:** Legislative sessions and annual study cycles
**Buyer:** Research director — member and grant funded
