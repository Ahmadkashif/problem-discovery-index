# TPA Selection & Brokerage Advisory

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/insurance-tpa|Insurance Third-Party Administrators (TPAs)]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Specialist advisers run the selection processes through which self-insured employers choose a TPA, comparing service models, fee structures, and claimed outcomes across bidders.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 3-12 consultants and analysts
**Output:** Selection criteria, bid evaluations, fee benchmarking, contract negotiation support
**Proprietary data:** TPA fee and performance comparables across selection processes
**Clock:** Contract renewal cycles
**Buyer:** Principal
