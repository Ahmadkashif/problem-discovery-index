# Self-Insured Actuarial Consulting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/insurance-tpa|Insurance Third-Party Administrators (TPAs)]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Consulting actuaries produce the reserve opinions and funding studies self-insured employers and captives are required to file, projecting ultimate claim cost from development patterns on a book the client has not seen an actuary's view of before.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 actuaries and analysts per practice
**Output:** Reserve opinions, funding and collateral studies, loss projections, programme design analysis
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated development patterns across many self-insured programmes and industries
**Clock:** Statutory reserve opinion filing deadlines and fiscal year ends
**Buyer:** Practice leader / Principal
