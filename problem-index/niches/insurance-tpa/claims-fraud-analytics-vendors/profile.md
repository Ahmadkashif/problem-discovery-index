# Claims Fraud Analytics Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/insurance-tpa|Insurance Third-Party Administrators (TPAs)]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Fraud analytics providers score claims for indicators of misrepresentation, link claimants and providers across carriers, and refer organized activity to special investigation units.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 data scientists, investigators, and analysts
**Output:** Fraud scores, link analysis and network detection, referral packages, industry alerts
**Proprietary data:** Cross-carrier claim contribution creating link visibility no single carrier or TPA has
**Clock:** Claim payment deadlines — a referral after payment is worth far less
**Buyer:** VP of Analytics / Chief Data Officer
