# State Workers' Compensation Boards

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/insurance-tpa|Insurance Third-Party Administrators (TPAs)]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State workers' compensation commissions receive every injury report and claim filing in their jurisdiction, adjudicate disputes, approve settlements, and set the medical fee schedules the whole system prices against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-800 adjudicators, analysts, and administrative staff per state
**Output:** Dispute decisions, settlement approvals, fee schedules, system performance reporting
**Proprietary data:** Every injury, claim, and dispute in the state, with decisions and settlement values
**Clock:** Statutory adjudication and filing deadlines
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — state agency
