# Disability Duration & Treatment Guideline Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/insurance-tpa|Insurance Third-Party Administrators (TPAs)]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Guideline publishers produce the evidence-based treatment protocols and expected disability durations that claims examiners and utilization reviewers adjudicate against — how long a given injury should keep a worker off the job, and which treatments are supported for it. Several states adopt them by regulation.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-250 physicians, epidemiologists, medical editors, and analysts
**Output:** Treatment guidelines, disability duration tables, return-to-work protocols, utilization review criteria
**Proprietary data:** Curated evidence synthesis and duration data derived from large claims populations
**Clock:** Annual guideline updates and state adoption cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Medical Officer / VP of Content
