# Nurse Case Management Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/insurance-tpa|Insurance Third-Party Administrators (TPAs)]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Case management firms assign nurses to workers' compensation claims to coordinate treatment, communicate with treating physicians, and drive return to work — the single largest lever on claim duration and therefore cost.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-2,000 case manager nurses, clinical reviewers, and analysts
**Output:** Case management plans, treatment coordination, return-to-work outcomes, clinical reporting
**Proprietary data:** Intervention decisions joined to claim duration and return-to-work outcomes across large populations
**Clock:** Claim milestones and disability duration benchmarks
**Buyer:** Chief Clinical Officer / VP of Operations
