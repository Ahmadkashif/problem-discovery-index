# Property Management Platform Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/property-management|Property Management]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The data organisations inside AppFolio, Yardi, RealPage and Buildium, running leasing funnel analytics, delinquency prediction, maintenance triage and benchmark publication over the operating records of a very large share of US rental housing.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-1,500 data scientists, analysts, and product researchers
**Output:** Leasing and marketing analytics, delinquency and renewal prediction, maintenance workflow automation, portfolio benchmarking, published market reports
**Proprietary data:** Rent ledgers, maintenance requests, lease events and resident communications across millions of units
**Clock:** Monthly rent cycles and continuous leasing activity
**Buyer:** VP of Data Science / Chief Product Officer
