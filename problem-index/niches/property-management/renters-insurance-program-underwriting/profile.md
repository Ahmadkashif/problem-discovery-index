# Renters Insurance & Resident Programme Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/property-management|Property Management]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Underwrite the renters insurance, security deposit alternatives and damage waiver programmes sold through property managers, and analyse the claims and losses they produce.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 underwriters, actuaries, and claims analysts
**Output:** Programme pricing and eligibility, deposit alternative underwriting, claims analysis, loss ratio reporting
**Proprietary data:** Resident damage and default outcomes joined to screening attributes at lease inception — one of the few places where screening decisions and their consequences sit in the same file
**Clock:** Annual programme renewals and continuous policy issuance
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
