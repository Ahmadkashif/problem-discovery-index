# Fair Housing Compliance & Testing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/property-management|Property Management]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Audit leasing practices for discriminatory effect, conduct paired testing, train leasing staff, and advise on screening criteria that will withstand disparate impact challenge.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 testers, analysts, and compliance consultants
**Output:** Testing results, disparate impact analyses, policy reviews, training programmes, litigation support
**Proprietary data:** Paired testing results and accumulated enforcement outcomes
**Clock:** Enforcement actions and litigation timelines
**Buyer:** Practice Director
