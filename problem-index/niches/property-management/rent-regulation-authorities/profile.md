# Rent Regulation & Housing Authorities

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/property-management|Property Management]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Administer rent stabilisation and registration regimes, set allowable increases, adjudicate tenant petitions, and enforce habitability and registration requirements.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 across the larger jurisdictions — analysts, hearing officers, and inspectors
**Output:** Allowable increase determinations, registration and compliance data, petition adjudications, housing condition reporting
**Proprietary data:** Registered rent rolls and petition histories, held municipally
**Clock:** Annual increase-setting cycles and petition deadlines
**Buyer:** Executive Director
