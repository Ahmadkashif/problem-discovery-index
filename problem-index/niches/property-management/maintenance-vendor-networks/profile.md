# Maintenance Vendor Networks & Marketplaces

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/property-management|Property Management]]
**Category:** Insight Layer

## What They Do
Source, dispatch and manage maintenance contractors on behalf of property managers, with the pricing, quality scoring and dispatch analytics that a national vendor network requires.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 analysts, vendor managers, and operations staff
**Output:** Vendor quality and cost scoring, dispatch routing, price benchmarking by trade and market, work order triage
**Proprietary data:** Work orders across many managers joined to vendor, cost, cycle time and repeat-visit outcomes
**Clock:** Emergency dispatch requirements and work order aging
**Buyer:** VP of Operations Analytics
