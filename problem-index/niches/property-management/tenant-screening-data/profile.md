# Tenant Screening Data & Scoring

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/property-management|Property Management]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Decide who gets to rent. Assemble credit, eviction, criminal and rental payment history into the screening reports and proprietary scores that property managers approve or decline applicants on, at a volume of tens of millions of applications a year. Pass 1 identifies tenant screening as one of the three areas where property managers rely on experience-based judgment — reading between the lines on income documentation, rental history gaps and reference patterns. This is the layer that supplies the file that judgment is applied to, and increasingly the recommendation itself.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-3,000 data acquisition staff, court record researchers, scoring analysts, data scientists, and compliance specialists
**Output:** Screening reports, proprietary tenant scores and approve/decline recommendations, income and identity verification, portfolio risk analytics
**Proprietary data:** Rental payment and lease outcome histories contributed by property managers, plus eviction records assembled from thousands of county courts that publish inconsistently — neither of which appears in any credit bureau file or public source in usable form
**Clock:** Application turnaround expectations measured in minutes, against units that lease in days
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Analytics
