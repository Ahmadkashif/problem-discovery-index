# Multifamily Market Data & Rent Benchmarking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/property-management|Property Management]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Track rents, occupancy, concessions, absorption and new supply across submarkets, and sell the benchmarks owners underwrite acquisitions against and managers set asking rents from.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-2,000 researchers, data collectors, economists, and analysts
**Output:** Rent and occupancy benchmarks, submarket forecasts, supply pipeline tracking, comparable property analytics
**Proprietary data:** Property-level research assembled by direct survey and data contribution over decades, across a market with no public transaction record
**Clock:** Monthly and quarterly data releases against underwriting and budgeting cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / Chief Economist
