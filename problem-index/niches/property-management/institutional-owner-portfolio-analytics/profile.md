# Institutional Owner & Operator Portfolio Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/property-management|Property Management]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator/rollup

## What They Do
Corporate analytics inside the national managers and institutional owners — portfolio performance, acquisition underwriting, renovation return analysis, and operating benchmarking across hundreds of thousands of units.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 analysts, data scientists, and underwriting staff
**Output:** Portfolio performance reporting, acquisition underwriting models, renovation and capital return analysis, operating benchmarks, disposition analysis
**Proprietary data:** Unit-level operating history across large portfolios, plus a record of underwriting assumptions against realised performance
**Clock:** Quarterly investor reporting and acquisition pipelines
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / Head of Portfolio Analytics
