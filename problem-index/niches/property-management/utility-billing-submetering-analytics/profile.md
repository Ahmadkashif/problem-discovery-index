# Utility Billing & Submetering Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/property-management|Property Management]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Meter, allocate and bill utilities back to residents, and analyse consumption to find leaks, equipment faults and inefficiency across managed portfolios.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 billing analysts, energy engineers, and data staff
**Output:** Resident utility billing and allocation, consumption analytics, leak and anomaly detection, benchmarking and efficiency reporting, regulatory compliance filings
**Proprietary data:** Interval consumption data at unit level across large portfolios, joined to building characteristics
**Clock:** Monthly billing cycles and utility rate changes
**Buyer:** VP of Energy Services
