# Affordable Housing Compliance Consulting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/property-management|Property Management]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Keep tax credit and subsidised properties compliant — income certifications, rent and income limit application, file audits, and the annual reporting that determines whether an owner keeps its credits or faces recapture.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 compliance specialists, file auditors, and trainers
**Output:** Income certifications and file reviews, compliance audits, agency reporting, corrective action plans, training and certification
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated agency review findings by state — what each monitoring agency actually cites, which differs materially from the published rules
**Clock:** Annual owner certifications, income limit publication dates, and state agency monitoring visits, with credit recapture as the penalty
**Buyer:** Director of Compliance Services
