# Property Tax Appeal Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/property-management|Property Management]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Challenge assessed values on managed properties, building the valuation case and arguing it before assessors and appeal boards — usually on contingency, so the analysis is the entire product.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-1,000 appraisers, analysts, and appeal specialists
**Output:** Assessment challenges, valuation analyses, appeal filings and hearings, savings reporting
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated appeal outcomes by jurisdiction, assessor and property type — a record of what arguments actually reduce an assessment, which no assessor publishes
**Clock:** Statutory appeal deadlines that vary by jurisdiction and forfeit the right if missed
**Buyer:** Managing Director / Practice Leader
