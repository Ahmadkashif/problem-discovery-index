# Revenue Management & Rent Pricing Systems

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/property-management|Property Management]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Recommend the asking rent for every unit every day, based on demand signals, competitor pricing, lease expiration exposure and inventory — the software that sets a large share of professionally managed apartment rents in the United States.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 data scientists, pricing analysts, economists, and revenue management consultants
**Output:** Daily unit-level price recommendations, renewal pricing, demand forecasts, lease expiration management, concession optimisation
**Proprietary data:** Lease transaction, traffic and pricing data contributed by participating operators across overlapping submarkets
**Clock:** Daily pricing runs against lease expiration exposure
**Buyer:** VP of Revenue Management / Chief Data Scientist
