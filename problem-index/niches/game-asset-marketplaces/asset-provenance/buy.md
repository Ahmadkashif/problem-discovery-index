**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to establish that a listing is what the seller says it is, when verification currently rests on the creator ticking a box — and whoever can establish it takes the account.

## The Problem
Music and video platforms confronted this at a far larger scale and built the response: content fingerprinting robust to transformation, reference databases supplied by rights holders, automated matching at upload, claim and dispute workflows, and policy tiers ranging from blocking to revenue sharing. The systems are expensive, imperfect and thoroughly operational. Game asset marketplaces face the same problem across geometry, textures and audio and largely operate a reactive email-based takedown process.

## What Already Exists
Transformation-robust content fingerprinting; rights holder reference databases; automated matching at upload; claim and dispute resolution workflows; and graduated enforcement policy.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to 3D content, where the transformations are far more varied than in audio or video. It requires: (1) fingerprints over geometry, materials and textures that survive retopology, rescaling, remeshing and format conversion — transformations with no audio or video equivalent, which is the substantive difference and is genuinely harder; (2) legitimate derivation being common, since assets are meant to be modified, so a match is not automatically an infringement; (3) no rights holder reference database at all, so the reference set must be the catalogue itself; (4) a generative provenance question that music and video are only now facing; and (5) a marketplace of individual creators rather than a small number of large rights holders.

## Target Customer
Asset marketplaces, rights holders and creators, commercial studios, and content identification vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Music and video built fingerprinting and rights matching at scale and operate it daily. Geometry that survives retopology and remeshing, with legitimate derivation being the norm, is what makes this a harder matching problem.
