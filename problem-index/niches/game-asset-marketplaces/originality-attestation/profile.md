# Originality Attestation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/game-asset-marketplaces|Game Asset Marketplaces]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make a creator's claim about how an asset was made worth something, when the file cannot answer the question — and whoever builds that regime takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$130M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — a checkbox
**Target Buyer:** Trust, legal and policy leadership
**Automation Potential:** Medium — governance with technical support

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This half is not computable from the asset. Whether a generative model was trained on licensed material, whether a creator held the rights to the source they derived from, whether the person uploading made the thing — none of it is visible in the geometry. The contest is designing a regime in which a claim carries weight: what evidence is required, what consequence attaches to a false claim, and what a commercial buyer can rely on. It is a governance product sold to policy and legal leadership, not a detector sold to engineering.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A disclosure checkbox and terms of service. The gaps: no evidence requirement behind any claim; no consequence for a false declaration; no chain of custody for derived work; no buyer-facing provenance statement; and no indemnity structure anyone relies on.
