# Chain of Custody From Supply Chain Assurance

**Industry:** [[industries/game-asset-marketplaces|Game Asset Marketplaces]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Physical supply chains built certification, audit and chain of custody for claims that cannot be seen in the product, and asset marketplaces use a checkbox.
**Tags:** #compliance #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #evaluation-metrics #automation #descriptive-statistics #sets-and-logic #confidence-intervals
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make a creator's claim about how an asset was made worth something, when the file cannot answer the question — and whoever builds that regime takes the account.

## The Problem
Supply chain assurance solved the general version of this problem: establishing claims about a product's origin that cannot be verified by inspecting the product. Organic certification, conflict-mineral tracing, timber legality, fair trade and food origin schemes all rest on the same machinery — documented chain of custody, accredited auditors, sampling-based verification, graduated certification tiers, and consequences for false claims. The structure is mature and works across industries where the claim is invisible in the item.

## What Already Exists
Chain of custody documentation standards; accredited third-party auditing; sampling-based verification regimes; graduated certification tiers; and enforcement and decertification mechanisms.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to individual creators producing digital goods at high volume and low unit value. It requires: (1) suppliers who are individuals selling assets for small amounts, so per-item audit is economically impossible and verification must be statistical and cheap — this is the substantive difference from every physical scheme; (2) a production process that leaves digital traces which can be captured at source, which physical supply chains would envy; (3) a legal question that is genuinely unsettled, unlike organic or timber standards where the rule is defined; (4) instant global distribution with no customs or border checkpoint to anchor the chain; and (5) buyers who need the assurance at purchase time rather than through an annual certification cycle.

## Target Customer
Asset marketplaces, commercial studios, creator organisations, and assurance and certification bodies.

## Impact If Solved
Supply chain assurance established invisible claims through custody, audit and consequence across many industries. Individual creators at low unit value make per-item audit impossible, so the verification has to be statistical and captured at source.
