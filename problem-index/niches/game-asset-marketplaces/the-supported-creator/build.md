# Bounding an Unbounded Obligation

**Niche:** [[niches/game-asset-marketplaces/the-supported-creator/profile|The Supported Creator]]
**Industry:** [[industries/game-asset-marketplaces|Game Asset Marketplaces]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** The sale was a one-off and the obligation is perpetual.
**Tags:** #worker-facing #automation #workflow-orchestration #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact #data-integration #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to bound the support obligation a creator takes on when they sell an asset once and owe help across every engine version for years — and whoever bounds it takes the account.

## The Problem
A creator sells an asset for a modest one-time price. Years later they are answering questions about engine versions that did not exist at the time of sale, pipelines they never targeted and platforms they do not own. Each engine release breaks something and generates a wave of requests. Not answering produces poor reviews, which suppress the listing. There is no contractual, commercial or technical mechanism for bounding any of it.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The marketplace's revenue model is transactional and support is externalised to creators for free. Individual creators have no leverage to change terms. The obligation is invisible to buyers, who see a product rather than a person. And the successful creators who feel it most are too busy to organise.

## What to Build
Deflect what is answerable and price what is not. Build a shared answer base across creators facing the same engine-version problems, which is the core — the same twenty questions recur across the whole marketplace and every creator answers them privately. Deflect common questions automatically at the point of contact, since most requests are already answered somewhere. Test each creator's assets against new engine versions automatically and tell them what broke before buyers do, which converts a wave into a task list. Provide migration notes generated from the detected differences, which is the expensive part of each answer. Let creators offer paid ongoing support as a distinct product, as the obligation should be priced rather than free. Support an explicit sunset with a stated supported-versions policy, which is normal in software and absent here. Separate support responsiveness from product quality in ratings, because conflating them is what makes the obligation coercive. Route buyers to the version of the asset that matches their engine rather than the latest. Show creators the support load per asset so they can price and prune rationally. And make the supported-version policy visible to buyers at purchase, which sets expectations honestly on both sides.

## Target Customer
Asset creators and small studios selling assets, asset marketplaces, engine vendors, and creator support tooling providers.

## Impact If Built
The same twenty questions recur across the whole marketplace and every creator answers them privately. A shared answer base plus automatic engine-version testing converts a perpetual obligation into a bounded task list.
