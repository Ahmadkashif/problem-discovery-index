# The Supported Creator

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/game-asset-marketplaces|Game Asset Marketplaces]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to bound the support obligation a creator takes on when they sell an asset once and owe help across every engine version for years — and whoever bounds it takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$150M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — no tooling
**Target Buyer:** Asset creators
**Automation Potential:** High — support deflection and compatibility maintenance

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
An asset sold once generates support obligations for years across every engine version, pipeline and platform the buyers happen to use, and the revenue arrived a long time ago. The creator is usually an individual. Each engine release produces a wave of breakage and a wave of requests. Ratings punish non-response. There is no mechanism for ending, pricing or bounding the obligation, and the successful creators are the ones most trapped by it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
An email inbox, a forum thread, a review section and a changelog nobody reads. The gaps: no support deflection; no compatibility testing against new engine versions; no way to price ongoing support; no sunset mechanism for old assets; and no shared answer base across creators with the same problem.
