# Automated Asset Validation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/game-asset-marketplaces|Game Asset Marketplaces]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to catch what is wrong with an asset at upload rather than after a buyer finds it, and whoever automates that review takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$120M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — manual review
**Target Buyer:** Marketplace operations
**Automation Potential:** Very high — mechanical checks at ingestion

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Everything that arrives on these marketplaces passes through a review that is partly human, partly absent and entirely inconsistent. Broken references, missing textures, non-functional prefabs, wrong formats, absent documentation, mislabelled categories and packages that simply do not import all reach buyers. Every one of these is mechanically detectable at upload. It is the cheapest quality improvement available in the category and it is done by a curation team reading descriptions.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A submission form, a manual review queue and a policy document. The gaps: no automated import test; no broken-reference detection; no completeness check against category norms; no documentation requirement enforcement; and no consistency between reviewers.
