# The Long-Tail Creator

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/game-asset-marketplaces|Game Asset Marketplaces]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a good asset in front of a buyer when discovery is concentrated and most listings sell almost nothing — and whoever redistributes that attention takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$100M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — no visibility
**Target Buyer:** Asset creators
**Automation Potential:** High — ranking, pricing and positioning

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Discovery in these marketplaces is heavily concentrated. A small number of listings capture most of the attention and most of the revenue, and the great majority sell very little regardless of quality. A creator who has made something genuinely good has no reliable path to being seen, no information about why their listing is not performing, and no lever other than price. The catalogue's depth is the platform's asset and the creators producing it are unsupported.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A listing page, a view count and a popularity sort that rewards what is already popular. The gaps: no diagnostic of why a listing underperforms; no positioning or pricing guidance; no cold-start path for new listings; no niche demand signal; and no feedback from buyers who looked and did not buy.
