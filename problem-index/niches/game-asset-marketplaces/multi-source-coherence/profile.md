# Multi-Source Asset Coherence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/game-asset-marketplaces|Game Asset Marketplaces]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make assets from fifteen creators with fifteen conventions look like one game, and whoever automates that work takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$150M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — manual
**Target Buyer:** Studio art leads
**Automation Potential:** High — normalisation and style transfer

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Someone has to take assets from fifteen creators with fifteen conventions and make them look like one game, and that work is invisible until it is missing. Scale differs, pivot points differ, naming differs, texture density differs, material setups differ, and the art styles were never meant to sit together. This is the true cost of buying rather than making, it falls on one technical artist, and no tooling addresses it — the marketplace's responsibility ends at download.

## Current Tools & Gaps
The engine's import settings, a naming convention document, and hours of manual correction. The gaps: no scale and pivot normalisation; no texture density audit across sources; no material standardisation; no style consistency assessment; and no view of which assets in a project clash.
