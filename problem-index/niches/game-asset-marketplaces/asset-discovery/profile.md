# Asset Discovery & Search

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/game-asset-marketplaces|Game Asset Marketplaces]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let a buyer find the right asset among hundreds of thousands without guessing the word the creator happened to type — and whoever solves that takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$200M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — keyword only
**Target Buyer:** Marketplace product leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very high — content-based retrieval

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Hundreds of thousands of assets are searched by keyword against creator-written titles and tags, so finding the right one depends on guessing the word the creator used. A buyer looking for a particular kind of building, a specific material feel or a sound with a certain character has no vocabulary in common with the catalogue. The content itself — the geometry, the texture, the audio — is fully available and entirely unindexed, and search is where most purchase intent is lost.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A keyword search box, category facets and a popularity sort. The gaps: no visual or content-based search; no style or similarity browsing; no semantic understanding of queries; no filtering by technical profile; and no ranking informed by what buyers actually kept.
