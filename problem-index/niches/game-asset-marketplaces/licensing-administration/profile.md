# Licensing & Rights Administration

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/game-asset-marketplaces|Game Asset Marketplaces]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let a studio know what it is actually permitted to do with the several hundred assets in its project, and whoever tracks that takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$80M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — templates
**Target Buyer:** Compliance and legal
**Automation Potential:** Very high — licence tracking and obligation checking

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A shipped game may contain assets from hundreds of purchases across several marketplaces, each under a licence with its own terms about seat counts, redistribution, modification, attribution and revenue thresholds. Nobody maintains the register. When a question arises — an audit, an acquisition, a platform certification, a dispute — the studio reconstructs it from purchase emails. It is a records problem with clear rules and it is handled by memory.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Purchase confirmation emails, a licence PDF somewhere, and a spreadsheet if someone started one. The gaps: no register of what is licensed under what terms; no link from licence to the assets actually shipped; no attribution obligation tracking; no seat count management; and no alerting when terms are exceeded.
