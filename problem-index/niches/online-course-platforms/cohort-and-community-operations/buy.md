# Programme Operations From Executive Education

**Industry:** [[industries/online-course-platforms|Online Course Platforms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Executive education runs cohorts as a professional operation with programme managers, and online cohorts are run by the instructor between lectures.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to run cohorts and communities that produce the accountability self-paced courses lack, without the operational load that makes them uneconomic — and whoever automates the running keeps the format that actually finishes.

## The Problem
Executive and professional education has run cohorts for decades with a defined operation: programme managers, deliberate cohort composition, structured group work, attendance management, alumni community, and outcome tracking. It produces high completion and strong networks, and it costs a great deal per participant because it is staffed. Online cohort courses want the outcomes at a fraction of the price and have the instructor absorbing the programme manager's job.

## What Already Exists
Programme management practice; deliberate cohort composition for diversity and networking; structured group work design; attendance and engagement management; and alumni community operations.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to remove the programme manager. It requires: (1) the operational role automated rather than staffed, since the price point cannot support a person per cohort and that is the entire economic problem; (2) participants distributed across time zones with no shared schedule, where executive programmes convene; (3) cohorts of hundreds rather than dozens, which changes group formation from a judgement into an optimisation; (4) voluntary participants with no employer sponsorship, so accountability cannot be borrowed from an organisation; and (5) instructors who are subject experts rather than faculty supported by a school.

## Target Customer
Community and product leadership, instructors and facilitators, learners, and programme management tooling vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Executive education gets the outcomes by staffing a programme manager per cohort. Automating that role is the whole adaptation, and cohort sizes here make group formation an optimisation rather than a judgement.
