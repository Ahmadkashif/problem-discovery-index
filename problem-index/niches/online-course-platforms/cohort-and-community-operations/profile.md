# Cohort & Community Operations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/online-course-platforms|Online Course Platforms]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to run cohorts and communities that produce the accountability self-paced courses lack, without the operational load that makes them uneconomic — and whoever automates the running keeps the format that actually finishes.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$800M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — scheduling tools, manual operations
**Target Buyer:** Community leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — scheduling, grouping and nudging

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Cohort-based and community-supported courses complete at far higher rates than self-paced ones, because a start date, a peer group and a visible schedule supply the accountability a video library does not. They are also operationally heavy: scheduling across time zones, forming groups, chasing attendance, moderating, rescheduling, and keeping a community alive between sessions. That load is why the format does not scale and why most of the catalogue is self-paced.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Scheduling tools, a chat community, a calendar and manual coordination. The gaps: group formation by hand; attendance and participation not tracked usefully; community activity not sustained between sessions; facilitator load unmeasured; and no reuse of what a cohort produced for the next one.
