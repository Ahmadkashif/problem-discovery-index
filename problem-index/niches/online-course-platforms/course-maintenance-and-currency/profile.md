# Course Maintenance & Currency

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/online-course-platforms|Online Course Platforms]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to know which courses have gone materially wrong as the tools they teach changed — and whoever detects decay before a learner complains stops selling instruction that no longer matches reality.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — detected by complaint
**Target Buyer:** Catalogue and quality leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — decay detection from external and internal signals

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A technical course is materially wrong eighteen months after recording, ranking signals lag by years, and the only mechanism for noticing is a learner complaining in the Q&A. The catalogue therefore contains a large body of confidently taught, obsolete instruction that continues to rank on accumulated ratings and continues to sell. Maintenance is expensive for the instructor, invisible to the platform, and unrewarded by the ranking.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A last-updated date, learner reviews and the Q&A section. The gaps: decay not detected; the last-updated date gameable and uninformative; ranking indifferent to currency; instructors not prompted or rewarded for updates; and learners not warned.
