# Learning Measurement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/online-course-platforms|Online Course Platforms]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to say whether a learner can now do the thing the course was about — and the contest splits cleanly enough that it is not terminal.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$6.5B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~26% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — enrolment measured, learning not
**Target Buyer:** Product and academic leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — an unexploited behavioural record

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The platform records every pause, rewind and quiz attempt of millions of learners and cannot say whether any of them can do the thing the course was about. The business monetises enrolment, the mission claims capability, and nothing connects them. As recorded explanation is commoditised by generative tools, a platform that can credibly say this person can now do this thing has a product that a video library does not — which makes this the category's largest contest and its only defensible position.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Completion percentages, multiple choice quizzes, unreviewed projects and certificates of attendance. The gaps: mastery never estimated; the behavioural record unused; assessment not diagnostic; the real outcome unobserved; and a certificate that certifies attendance.
