# Practice & Feedback

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/online-course-platforms|Online Course Platforms]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give a learner structured practice and real feedback on their work at a cost that scales — and whoever does it owns the part of learning that recorded video never provided and generative explanation does not replace.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3.5B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~14% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — quizzes and unreviewed projects
**Target Buyer:** Product and instructional leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — exercise generation and automated feedback

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A competent explanatory video on almost any subject is now abundant and often free, and a generative assistant will explain a concept interactively on demand. What remains scarce is structured practice, real assessment, feedback on work, and a credential somebody believes — and practice with feedback is the one the sector has invested in least while it is the one that produces learning. Most courses offer multiple choice and a project nobody looks at.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Multiple choice quizzes, downloadable exercise files, unreviewed capstone projects and occasional peer review. The gaps: exercises not generated at scale; no feedback on submitted work; peer review unreliable and unmeasured; practice not spaced or scheduled; and no connection between practice performance and what the learner is shown next.
