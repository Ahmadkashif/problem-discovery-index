**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give a learner structured practice and real feedback on their work at a cost that scales — and whoever does it owns the part of learning that recorded video never provided and generative explanation does not replace.

## The Problem
Domains that reliably produce capability — musical training, athletic coaching, aviation and surgical simulation — are built on the same structure: a progression of tasks at the edge of current ability, immediate specific correction, repetition until automatic, and a coach who diagnoses the error rather than reporting it. The research on deliberate practice is well established. Online course platforms deliver explanation and hope.

## What Already Exists
Deliberate practice methodology; progressive task design at the edge of ability; immediate corrective feedback protocols; simulation-based training with debrief; and coaching frameworks for error diagnosis.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a coach who is not there. It requires: (1) diagnosis and correction produced automatically, since the coach is the expensive component and the entire adaptation is about replacing that function — this is the substantive problem and is what has recently become possible; (2) subjects where the correct answer is not a single form, so feedback must address an approach rather than mark a result; (3) a voluntary learner who will stop if practice is too hard or too dull, where aviation and music training have external motivation; (4) no physical performance to observe, so the artefact submitted is all the evidence there is; and (5) consumer economics, which rules out the human coach the source domains all assume.

## Target Customer
Product and instructional leadership, learners, instructors, and practice and simulation vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Every domain that reliably produces capability is built on practice with a coach, and consumer economics ruled the coach out. Producing diagnosis and correction automatically is the adaptation, and it is what makes the proven structure affordable here.
