# Credential & Outcome Verification

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/online-course-platforms|Online Course Platforms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to issue a credential an employer actually believes and to observe what happened to the learner afterwards — and whoever does both has the only evidence in the sector that any of this works.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — attendance certified, outcomes unobserved
**Target Buyer:** Credential and partnerships leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — assessment design and outcome linkage

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This contest is evidence for someone outside the platform. An employer reading a credential wants to know whether this person can do the work; a learner wants a credential that changes what happens to them. Both require assessment that is hard to fake and an outcome record that shows what followed — a job, an interview, an applied project. It is assessment design, identity assurance and external data linkage, which has nothing in common with modelling a learner's knowledge from telemetry.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Certificates of completion, unproctored quizzes, occasional reviewed projects and a verification page. The gaps: assessment trivially passable; no identity assurance; no employer input into what a credential should mean; outcomes after the course never tracked; and no evidence that any credential predicts anything.
