**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to issue a credential an employer actually believes and to observe what happened to the learner afterwards — and whoever does both has the only evidence in the sector that any of this works.

## The Problem
Professional certification — in accounting, medicine, project management, cloud infrastructure — is built on a defined practice: job task analysis establishing what the credential should test, item development and banking, standard setting to fix the pass mark, proctored administration, and recertification as the field moves. The credentials carry weight because the process is rigorous and visible. Course platform certificates carry none because none of that exists.

## What Already Exists
Job task analysis; item development, banking and rotation; standard setting methodologies; proctoring and identity verification; and recertification and continuing education requirements.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to consumer scale and voluntary learners. It requires: (1) a cost per credential of dollars rather than hundreds, which rules out human proctoring at volume and is the binding constraint — the adaptation is largely about achieving credibility cheaply; (2) subjects that change every eighteen months, so item banks and standards must move far faster than professional certification's cycle; (3) learners who will not sit a formal examination, which pushes assessment into applied work; (4) no professional body with authority, so credibility must be earned through demonstrated predictive validity instead; and (5) thousands of courses rather than one certification, which makes centralised item development impossible.

## Target Customer
Credential leadership, employers, certification bodies entering the space, and assessment technology vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Professional certification earns credibility through an expensive, visible process. Achieving comparable credibility at consumer cost, on subjects that change every eighteen months, is the adaptation — and demonstrated predictive validity is the substitute for an authorising body.
