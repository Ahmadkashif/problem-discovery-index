# A Credential Somebody Believes

**Niche:** [[niches/online-course-platforms/credential-and-outcome-verification/profile|Credential & Outcome Verification]]
**Industry:** [[industries/online-course-platforms|Online Course Platforms]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** The sector issues millions of credentials a year and cannot show that any of them predicted anything.
**Tags:** #compliance #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #hypothesis-testing #causal-inference #data-integration #survival-analysis #descriptive-statistics
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to issue a credential an employer actually believes and to observe what happened to the learner afterwards — and whoever does both has the only evidence in the sector that any of this works.

## The Problem
The outcome that matters — the learner applying a skill, passing an interview, doing the job — happens outside the platform and is never observed. The credential that is supposed to represent it is issued on completion, assessed by an unproctored quiz, and discounted by every employer who has seen one. The sector therefore cannot demonstrate that its product works, has no way to distinguish the courses that do from the ones that do not, and has surrendered the credential's meaning by default.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Outcomes occur after the transaction, so observing them requires effort with no revenue attached — a business that has already been paid has to choose to go and look. Rigorous assessment reduces issuance and increases cost. Employers were never brought into the design. And a credential that predicted outcomes would reveal that most do not, which is uncomfortable for everyone currently issuing them.

## What to Build
Make the assessment real and follow the learner. Design assessment that is hard to pass without the capability — applied work, timed problems, reviewed projects — which is the core and is the precondition for any credential that means something. Add identity assurance proportionate to the claim, since an unverified credential cannot support a strong one. Involve employers in defining what the credential should attest, as they are the readers and have never been consulted. Track what happened after the course through learner follow-up, connected accounts and employment signals where consented, because that is the evidence nobody has. Report predictive validity — whether the credential relates to the outcome — which is the claim that would distinguish a serious issuer from the rest. Rank and recommend courses by outcome rather than by rating, since that changes what gets bought and therefore what gets made. Issue different credentials for different levels of evidence, so a reviewed applied project is not confused with a quiz. Make verification easy for an employer, as friction there is why credentials go unchecked. Publish the outcome data honestly, including for courses that perform badly. And accept fewer credentials of higher value, because the alternative is the current position where none of them count.

## Target Customer
Credential and partnerships leadership, employers, learners seeking a credential that matters, and assessment and verification vendors.

## Impact If Built
A business that has already been paid has to choose to go and look, so outcomes after the transaction went unobserved. Following the learner and reporting predictive validity is the only evidence that would make a credential worth reading.
