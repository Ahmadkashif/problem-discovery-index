# The Instructor

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/online-course-platforms|Online Course Platforms]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let someone who knows a subject spend their week teaching rather than on funnels, pricing and platform changes — and whoever removes that overhead keeps the experts the whole catalogue depends on.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — a creator running a small business badly
**Target Buyer:** Creator and instructor leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — business operations and teaching feedback

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Someone who knows a subject well ends up spending their week on funnels, pricing, refunds, launch emails and platform algorithm changes, and teaching whenever there is time left. They are simultaneously the subject expert, the instructional designer, the marketer, the support desk and the small business operator, and the platform serves the last four badly and the first two not at all. The supply of good instruction depends entirely on these people continuing.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A course authoring tool, a sales dashboard, an email feature and a payout report. The gaps: no teaching feedback at all; marketing and pricing handled by the instructor from scratch; platform changes unannounced and unexplained; support burden unbounded; and no view of what their teaching actually achieved.
