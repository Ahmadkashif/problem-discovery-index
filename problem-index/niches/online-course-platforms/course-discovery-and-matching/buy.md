# Marketplace Ranking on Post-Purchase Outcomes

**Niche:** [[niches/online-course-platforms/course-discovery-and-matching/profile|Course Discovery & Matching]]
**Industry:** [[industries/online-course-platforms|Online Course Platforms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Commerce marketplaces learned to rank on returns and satisfaction after use rather than on sales volume, and course ranking is still sales volume.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #evaluation-metrics #causal-inference #confidence-intervals #matrix-decompositions #revenue-impact #survival-analysis #hypothesis-testing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to match a learner to a course they will finish and benefit from rather than to the one most people bought — and whoever ranks on fit rather than on popularity changes what the marketplace rewards.

## The Problem
Commerce marketplaces went through exactly this. Ranking on sales created a rich-get-richer dynamic that rewarded listings which sold and not products which satisfied, so they introduced return rates, post-delivery reviews, defect rates and seller performance into ranking. It changed what sellers made. Course marketplaces rank on enrolment and an immediate rating, which is the position commerce abandoned.

## What Already Exists
Post-purchase outcome signals in ranking; return and defect rate integration; seller performance scoring; cold start handling for new listings; and measurement of ranking changes on marketplace composition.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a product whose failure looks like the buyer's fault. It requires: (1) a non-completion that learners attribute to themselves rather than to the product, so the equivalent of a return is never filed — this is the substantive difference and means the negative signal must be inferred from behaviour; (2) an outcome that occurs months later outside the platform, unlike a delivery; (3) fit that depends on the buyer's prior knowledge, where a product's suitability rarely depends on the buyer's prior state to this degree; (4) content that decays, so a listing's quality changes without the seller doing anything; and (5) an immediate rating that is entrenched as the visible signal and cannot simply be removed.

## Target Customer
Product and marketplace leadership, instructors, learners, and marketplace ranking vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Commerce abandoned sales-volume ranking because it rewarded the wrong things, and course marketplaces still use it. The learner blaming themselves rather than returning the product is why the negative signal has to be inferred rather than collected.
