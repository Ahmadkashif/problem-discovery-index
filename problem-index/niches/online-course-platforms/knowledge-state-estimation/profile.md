# Knowledge State Estimation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/online-course-platforms|Online Course Platforms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to infer what a learner currently knows from pauses, attempts, abandonments and returns — and whoever estimates mastery accurately can tell each learner what to do next instead of what to watch next.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3.5B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~14% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — data logged and discarded
**Target Buyer:** Product and data leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — a studied problem with unusual data

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This contest is inference inside the platform. Given everything a learner has done — which segments they replayed, where they stopped, how many attempts a question took, how long before they returned — estimate what they know and what they should do next. It is a long-studied problem with established model families, applied here to a behavioural record of unprecedented size. It has nothing to do with convincing an employer of anything.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Progress bars, quiz scores and a linear course order. The gaps: no mastery model; prerequisites not diagnosed; the same sequence served to everyone; struggle signals unused; and no adaptive path.
