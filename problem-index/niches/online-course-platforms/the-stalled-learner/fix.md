# A Reminder Email That Says Keep Going

**Niche:** [[niches/online-course-platforms/the-stalled-learner/profile|The Stalled Learner]]
**Industry:** [[industries/online-course-platforms|Online Course Platforms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The learner stopped because they did not understand section four, and the platform sends them a message encouraging them to continue.
**Tags:** #quick-win #worker-facing #evaluation-metrics #automation #descriptive-statistics #confidence-intervals #workflow-orchestration #survival-analysis
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to reach the learner in the week they quietly stop — and whoever does it addresses the sector's defining statistic instead of reporting it.

## The Problem
Re-engagement emails encourage. They say the learner is partway through, that they can do it, that others completed it. The learner did not stop from lack of motivation; they stopped because something was incomprehensible and they had no way past it. An encouraging message addressed to a motivation problem when the problem is comprehension reads as though nobody looked at what happened, which is accurate, and confirms the learner's sense that this is not for them.

## Why It's Still Broken
Re-engagement was built as a lifecycle marketing function, so the message is a nudge — a communication designed by a growth team addresses engagement because that is the problem growth teams solve. The stall's cause is not diagnosed. Nobody tested whether encouragement works. And the email's success is measured on opens.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Say something about where they stopped. Reference the specific section they stopped at, which is the fix and is the minimum evidence that somebody looked. Offer the thing that would help — the prerequisite, an alternative explanation, a worked example, a practice exercise — rather than encouragement. Report where learners stop in each course, since that is one query and will show a small number of cliffs. Ask what happened with one question, as the answers would be the most useful data the platform could collect and nobody asks. Send it within days rather than weeks, because the learner's context is gone after a month. Distinguish the learner who paused deliberately from the one who was defeated, since encouraging the first is fine and encouraging the second is insulting. Test alternatives properly, as this is a clean experimental surface with an obvious outcome. Tell the instructor where their learners stop, because they would fix it and cannot see it. Measure resumption rather than opens, which is the only outcome that matters. And stop sending encouragement to someone who hit a wall, since it is the specific thing that makes them feel worse.

## Who Feels the Pain
Learners who concluded they were not capable; instructors whose course has a fixable cliff; platforms reporting the same statistic for a decade; and the learners' confidence, which is the durable harm.

## Impact If Fixed
A communication designed by a growth team addresses engagement because that is the problem growth teams solve, and the problem here is comprehension. Naming the section and offering the missing prerequisite is the minimum evidence that somebody looked.
