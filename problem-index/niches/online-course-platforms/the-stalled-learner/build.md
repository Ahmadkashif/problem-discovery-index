# Reaching Them in Week Two

**Niche:** [[niches/online-course-platforms/the-stalled-learner/profile|The Stalled Learner]]
**Industry:** [[industries/online-course-platforms|Online Course Platforms]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** The learner stops at a specific concept in a specific week, and the platform notices a month later and sends a reminder.
**Tags:** #survival-analysis #gradient-boosting #causal-inference #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #hidden-markov-models #automation #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to reach the learner in the week they quietly stop — and whoever does it addresses the sector's defining statistic instead of reporting it.

## The Problem
Abandonment is not random. It concentrates at specific points, usually where a prerequisite is assumed, where difficulty jumps, or where the material becomes abstract before it becomes useful. The learner hits it, tries twice, does not understand, and stops — attributing the failure to themselves. All of this is visible: the repeated attempts, the rewinds, the lengthening gap between sessions. The intervention arrives, if at all, as a reminder weeks later.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Revenue was collected at enrolment, so a learner who stops costs nothing measurable — a business with no financial consequence for abandonment will report it rather than address it. The sector normalised the statistic by repeating it. Intervention requires diagnosing the cause, which requires the knowledge model. And the learner's self-blame means they do not complain.

## What to Build
Predict the stall and address its cause. Predict abandonment from the behavioural precursors — lengthening gaps, repeated failed attempts, rewind clusters — which is the core and gives days of warning. Diagnose the specific cause rather than treating all stalls alike, since a missing prerequisite, an unclear explanation and a life circumstance need entirely different responses. Intervene at the point of difficulty rather than after the learner has gone, because a prompt offering the missing prerequisite at the moment of confusion is a different thing from a reminder a month later. Identify the structural stall points across all learners in a course and fix the course, as one bad section can account for a large share of a course's abandonment. Distinguish a pause from a departure, since many learners intend to return and the intervention differs. Ask the learner what happened, which nobody does and which would produce the sector's most useful dataset. Test interventions properly, as this is an easy experimental surface and the current ones are untested. Offer the prerequisite course, the simpler explanation or the practice, rather than encouragement. Measure resumption and eventual completion, not email open rates. And report abandonment by cause, which would tell instructors and the platform what is actually wrong.

## Target Customer
Learner success and product leadership, learners who blame themselves, instructors whose courses have a structural cliff, and retention vendors.

## Impact If Built
A business with no financial consequence for abandonment will report it rather than address it, which is why a public statistic went unchanged for a decade. The behavioural precursors give days of warning and the stall points are specific and fixable.
