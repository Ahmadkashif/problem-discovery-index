# The Stalled Learner

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/online-course-platforms|Online Course Platforms]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to reach the learner in the week they quietly stop — and whoever does it addresses the sector's defining statistic instead of reporting it.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — measured, never acted on
**Target Buyer:** Learner success leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — prediction and intervention

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The sector's central statistic is that a large majority of enrolments are never completed, and it has been stable and publicly known for over a decade. Behind it is an individual who bought something, started it, hit a specific difficulty in a specific week, and stopped — usually concluding the failure was theirs. They are the single largest population in the category, they are entirely visible in the data before they leave, and nothing reaches them.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A reminder email, a progress bar and a refund window. The gaps: stall not predicted; the cause not diagnosed; interventions generic and untested; the learner's own explanation never collected; and no distinction between deliberate pausing and defeat.
