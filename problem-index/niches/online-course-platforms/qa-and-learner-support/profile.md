# Q&A and Learner Support

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/online-course-platforms|Online Course Platforms]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to answer the same twenty questions once instead of forever — and whoever does it removes an unbounded obligation that makes instructors abandon their own courses.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.2B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — a thread that grows forever
**Target Buyer:** Support and community leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — a corpus of questions with answers

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A course Q&A accumulates thousands of questions, most of them the same twenty, and the instructor either answers them forever or lets the course look abandoned. It is an unbounded obligation attached to a one-time sale, it is the most visible signal of whether a course is maintained, and it contains — in the questions themselves — the clearest available diagnosis of what the course fails to explain.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A threaded Q&A, search within it and email notifications. The gaps: recurring questions not identified or answered once; existing answers not surfaced before asking; the question corpus never analysed as course feedback; no distinction between a content gap and a personal one; and the instructor's obligation unbounded.
