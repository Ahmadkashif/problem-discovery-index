# Knowledge Base Practice From Support

**Niche:** [[niches/online-course-platforms/qa-and-learner-support/profile|Q&A and Learner Support]]
**Industry:** [[industries/online-course-platforms|Online Course Platforms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Customer support built deflection, knowledge bases and ticket-to-article pipelines, and course Q&A is a forum thread.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #workflow-orchestration #automation #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #k-means-clustering #descriptive-statistics
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to answer the same twenty questions once instead of forever — and whoever does it removes an unbounded obligation that makes instructors abandon their own courses.

## The Problem
Customer support solved the repeated question: knowledge base articles derived from ticket clusters, deflection at the point of asking, suggested answers for agents, ticket volume analysis by cause, and a feedback loop from support into the product. The practice is mature and measurably effective. Course Q&A has none of it and the person absorbing the volume is the instructor, who has no support background and no tooling.

## What Already Exists
Ticket clustering and knowledge base derivation; deflection at point of contact; agent answer suggestion; volume-by-cause analysis; and support-to-product feedback loops.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a responder who is the author and not a support team. It requires: (1) the instructor as the sole responder with no team and no obligation beyond reputation, so deflection must be near-total rather than partial — this is the substantive difference; (2) questions that are about understanding rather than about a malfunction, which changes what a good answer is; (3) a knowledge base that should ideally be the course itself, since a recurring question means the content failed; (4) an obligation with no service level and no end date, unlike a support contract; and (5) a public thread whose emptiness or fullness affects sales, which gives the queue a commercial dimension support queues do not have.

## Target Customer
Support and community leadership, instructors, learners, and support platform vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Support solved the repeated question with deflection and knowledge bases derived from the tickets. Here the ideal knowledge base is the course itself, since a recurring question is evidence that the content failed at that exact point.
