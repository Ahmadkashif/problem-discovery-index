# Bank Fit-and-Proper Supervisors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/executive-search-firms|Executive Search Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Bank supervisors that must approve directors and senior executives before or soon after appointment, which makes them a gate on every bank board and C-suite search. ECB Banking Supervision handled 1,672 individual fit-and-proper procedures for significant institutions in 2025, two-thirds of them for supervisory board members, taking 113 days on average and identifying concerns in 40.85% of assessments; the UK's Senior Managers regime works similarly. Decisions are not published.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Case assessors in supervisory teams at each authority; examination-style production rather than a research function
**Output:** Approval decisions with conditions, one candidate at a time
**Proprietary data:** Questionnaires, interview records and decisions on every proposed bank director, held under supervisory confidentiality
**Clock:** The joint ESMA–EBA guidelines' four-month maximum for a decision
**Buyer:** None — a supervisor, not a seller
