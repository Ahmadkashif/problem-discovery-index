# Proxy-Disclosed Executive Pay Data Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/executive-search-firms|Executive Search Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Vendors that extract, standardise and benchmark executive and director pay from proxy statements — Equilar (Russell 3000 named-executive and Section 16 officer data), ISS-Corporate's compensation analytics, S&P's ExecuComp and smaller extractors. Compensation committees, pay consultants, search firms building offers and investors buy peer-group pay, pay-versus-performance and director-pay benchmarks.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~30–150 data and research analysts per vendor (estimate), much of it extraction
**Output:** Standardised pay datasets, peer-group benchmarks, pay-versus-performance and director-pay analytics
**Proprietary data:** Standardised multi-year history of proxy disclosures; the inputs are public filings
**Clock:** The proxy season shapes demand, but the SEC deadlines bind the issuers, not the vendor; nothing external dates the vendor's own work
**Buyer:** Head of data and research at the vendor
