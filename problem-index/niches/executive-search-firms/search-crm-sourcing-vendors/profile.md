# Search CRM & AI Sourcing Software Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/executive-search-firms|Executive Search Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors of the search firm's system of record and sourcing tools: Thrive TRM, Clockwork, Invenias (Bullhorn), Ezekia, Cluen Encore and FileFinder for mandates and candidates; Pin, SeekOut and hireEZ for AI sourcing. They also publish the industry's technology surveys — Clockwork's 2023 survey of 179 firms and Cluen's early-2026 survey on generative AI use.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Product analytics and research teams under ten at most vendors (estimate)
**Output:** Software subscriptions; surveys and benchmarks as marketing
**Proprietary data:** Usage telemetry; the candidate and mandate data in the system belongs to each customer firm
**Clock:** Release cycles, self-set
**Buyer:** Head of product
