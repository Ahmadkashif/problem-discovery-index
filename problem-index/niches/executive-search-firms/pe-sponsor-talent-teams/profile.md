# Private-Equity Sponsor Talent Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/executive-search-firms|Executive Search Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Talent and operating partners at private-equity sponsors who buy portfolio-company searches, run search-firm panels, commission pre-deal management assessments and track portfolio leadership. They sit between the portfolio company that pays the fee and the search firm that does the work, and see every placement's outcome in the portfolio's results.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~1–5 people at most sponsors (estimate); 10+ only at the largest buyout firms
**Output:** Internal panel decisions, leadership assessments of portfolio companies, succession plans for portfolio CEOs
**Proprietary data:** Portfolio leadership histories joined to deal outcomes — the best placement-outcome record in the industry, held privately
**Clock:** Hold periods and exits, set by the sponsor
**Buyer:** Head of portfolio talent
