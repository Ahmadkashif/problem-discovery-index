# Board Director Search

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/executive-search-firms|Executive Search Firms]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to fill the gap on a board's skills matrix with a director the nominating committee can put in the next proxy, and whoever brings candidates the committee did not already know, clear of interlocks and independence problems, takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$800M global client fee spend on non-executive director searches and board composition advice (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~4% of the ~$20B global industry (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — the board practice runs on partners' director networks and a skills matrix kept as a table in the proxy
**Target Buyer:** Nominating/governance committee chair; the firm's board practice leader
**Automation Potential:** Medium for screening and vacancy forecasting, low for the approach and assessment

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Director search is a small, prestigious, relationship-driven market with its own rules. Seats are scarce — Spencer Stuart's 2026 Board Index counted 364 new independent S&P 500 directors, the fewest since 2016, and 0.7 new directors per board — and the nominating committee, not management, is the client. Every candidate must clear independence standards, interlocks and over-boarding policies, and the proxy must describe any third party paid to identify or evaluate nominees. Since the Fifth Circuit vacated Nasdaq's board diversity rules in December 2024, the disclosure pressure on slates has shifted from demographic quotas to skills, so the skills matrix is now the brief.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Board practice partners' personal networks, BoardEx and Equilar BoardEdge for director data, board portals (Diligent, Nasdaq Boardvantage) that hold skills matrices, and proxy disclosures. Gaps: vacancies seen only when the committee calls; slates recycled from the same networks; independence and interlock checks done by hand.
