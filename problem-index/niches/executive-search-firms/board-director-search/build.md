# A Board Refreshment Forecaster

**Niche:** [[niches/executive-search-firms/board-director-search/profile|Board Director Search]]
**Industry:** [[industries/executive-search-firms|Executive Search Firms]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Which boards will open a seat next proxy season is mostly predictable from what proxies already disclose, and board practices still learn of vacancies when the committee chair calls.
**Tags:** #survival-analysis #gradient-boosting #large-language-models #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to fill the gap on a board's skills matrix with a director the nominating committee can put in the next proxy, and whoever brings candidates the committee did not already know, clear of interlocks and independence problems, takes the account.

## The Problem
Director turnover follows rules boards publish: mandatory retirement ages, tenure limits, over-boarding policies, committee rotation and skills gaps the matrix makes visible. Activist settlements and mergers add seats on short notice. A board practice that knew which boards were likely to open seats, and which skills each would seek, could prepare candidates before the call; most rely on relationships to hear first.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Board data vendors sell profiles and connections rather than forecasts, and board practices are small teams of senior partners whose business development runs on personal networks.

## What to Build
A forecaster reading every proxy and director 8-K: ages against retirement policies, tenure, committee chairs due to rotate, over-boarded directors, skills matrix gaps, and settlement agreements. A survival model gives each board's probability of adding a director in the next cycle and the skills most likely sought; candidates in the firm's director pool are pre-screened for independence and interlocks against each likely opening.

## Target Customer
Board practice leaders at large and mid-sized search firms; governance advisers to nominating committees.

## Impact If Built
Board practices arrive with prepared, screened candidates before competitors know there is a seat, in a market where seats are at a decade low.
