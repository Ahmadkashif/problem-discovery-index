# Board Portal Skills Matrices Turned Into Search Briefs

**Niche:** [[niches/executive-search-firms/board-director-search/profile|Board Director Search]]
**Industry:** [[industries/executive-search-firms|Executive Search Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Board portals already hold each board's skills matrix and evaluation results; nothing turns that matrix into a screened candidate brief.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #word-embeddings #k-nearest-neighbors #compliance #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to fill the gap on a board's skills matrix with a director the nominating committee can put in the next proxy, and whoever brings candidates the committee did not already know, clear of interlocks and independence problems, takes the account.

## The Problem
A nominating committee describes the director it wants in terms of its matrix — audit committee financial expertise, cybersecurity, a sector, international operations. The search partner translates that into a brief by conversation, and the research team searches director data by hand.

## What Already Exists
Board portals such as Diligent and Nasdaq Boardvantage host board papers, evaluations and skills matrices; BoardEx and Equilar BoardEdge hold director profiles and interlocks.

## The Customization Gap
Search needs: the matrix as structured requirements rather than a disclosed table; matching of candidates' records to those requirements with evidence (an "audit committee financial expert" designation, a cyber role, a regulated-sector board seat); automatic screening for interlocks, independence relationships under listing standards and over-boarding limits; and a record of how the slate was assembled, which the committee may need to describe in the proxy.

## Target Customer
Board practice research teams and governance committee chairs who share their matrix with the firm.

## Impact If Solved
Briefs that match how the board actually describes its gaps, and slates screened before a partner spends a relationship on an ineligible candidate.
