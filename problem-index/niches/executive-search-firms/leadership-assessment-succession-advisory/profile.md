# Leadership Assessment & Succession Advisory

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/executive-search-firms|Executive Search Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
The leadership advisory arms of the search firms (Korn Ferry's Assessment & Succession, Heidrick Consulting, the leadership practices of Russell Reynolds, Spencer Stuart and Egon Zehnder) and independent assessment firms assess named executives for boards and chief people officers: CEO succession readiness, top-team assessments, pre-hire and pre-deal assessments. The billable output is the assessment report on each executive. Instrument publishers such as SHL and Hogan are a different layer, already indexed as [[niches/corporate-training/talent-assessment-publishers/profile|Talent Assessment Publishers]] (52).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~30–300 assessors, consultants and psychologists per firm (estimate; Heidrick Consulting had 93 consultants at 30 September 2025)
**Output:** Individual assessment reports, succession readiness ratings, team diagnostics, benchmark comparisons
**Proprietary data:** Benchmark databases of assessed executives (interview ratings joined to psychometrics) and the firm's assessment frameworks
**Clock:** Board succession calendars and CEO departures, set by the client
**Buyer:** Global head of leadership advisory / assessment practice leader

**Kill switches:** data isn't theirs to use — the assessments are of the client's own executives, commissioned by the board or chief people officer and delivered under confidentiality; reuse beyond anonymised norms is restricted, the same rule the vault applies to engagement survey vendors' client response data.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. The insight is unambiguously the invoice, and assessments are the one place the industry generates structured evidence about executives before outcomes are known; but the evidence belongs to the client, and the work is bespoke enough per executive that Q2 is 3.
