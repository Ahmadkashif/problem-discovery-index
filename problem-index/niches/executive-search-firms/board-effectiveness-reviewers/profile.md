# Board Effectiveness Review Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/executive-search-firms|Executive Search Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Independent reviewers, governance consultancies and the board practices of search firms run externally facilitated board evaluations: questionnaires and interviews with every director, observation of meetings, a report to the chair and, increasingly, individual director peer reviews. In the UK the Corporate Governance Code expects FTSE 350 companies to have an externally facilitated review at least every three years; in the US, Spencer Stuart's 2026 Board Index found 33% of S&P 500 boards use an independent third party, up from 3% a decade ago. The board pays.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~5–20 reviewers per firm (estimate), with the largest specialists and search-firm board practices passing ten
**Output:** Board review reports, director peer-review feedback, disclosure language for the annual report
**Proprietary data:** Interview and questionnaire archives across many boards, each confidential to its board
**Clock:** The UK Code's three-year external review cycle and annual-report disclosure; annual evaluations under US listing standards
**Buyer:** Managing partner of the review firm or head of a search firm's board advisory practice

**Kill switches:** data isn't theirs to use — review findings and director interviews are owned by and confidential to each board, and the Chartered Governance Institute's code of practice for board reviewers binds reviewers to client confidentiality.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. A clean external cycle and a report that is plainly the invoice, but interview-based work that scales poorly, a small field of reviewers, and an archive that belongs to the boards reviewed.
