# Early Warning for Failing Placements

**Niche:** [[niches/executive-search-firms/placed-executive-first-18-months/profile|The Placed Executive's First 18 Months]]
**Industry:** [[industries/executive-search-firms|Executive Search Firms]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Failing executive integrations show themselves in stakeholder sentiment months before anyone resigns, and nobody collects the signal in a form that can trigger help.
**Tags:** #survival-analysis #gradient-boosting #large-language-models #bert #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #tacit-knowledge-ml
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a newly placed executive in role through the first eighteen months, and whoever spots a failing integration early enough for the client to intervene before the departure is announced takes the account.

## The Problem
A consultant's 90-day check-in call with a placed CFO and the CEO who hired them produces reassurance on both sides. The real picture — the CEO's frustration with pace, the leadership team closing ranks, the board chair's doubts — emerges later and informally. By the time the problem is visible, the decision to part ways is often made.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Follow-up earns no fee, the guarantee gives the search firm a reason not to look too hard inside the guarantee window, and the client's HR team owns the employee relationship. Consultants' instincts about which placements are wobbling are real and unrecorded.

## What to Build
Structured, confidential check-ins at fixed points (30, 90, 180, 365 days) with the executive, the hiring manager and two or three peers or direct reports, captured as short conversations and coded against the derailment patterns the firm has seen in its own failed placements. A risk model combines the check-in signals with what the search assessment flagged, the role and the ownership context, and triggers an intervention offer — coaching, a facilitated reset with the CEO — when risk rises.

## Target Customer
Practice leaders at search firms, offered to chief people officers as a paid integration service bundled with the guarantee.

## Impact If Built
Some failing placements rescued, others ended earlier and more cheaply, and the firm learning which assessment signals predicted trouble.
