# Coaching Platforms Wired to the Search Assessment

**Niche:** [[niches/executive-search-firms/placed-executive-first-18-months/profile|The Placed Executive's First 18 Months]]
**Industry:** [[industries/executive-search-firms|Executive Search Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Coaching platforms deliver development at scale; an integrating executive needs a coach who starts from what the search learned about them and the organisation.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a newly placed executive in role through the first eighteen months, and whoever spots a failing integration early enough for the client to intervene before the departure is announced takes the account.

## The Problem
When a company buys integration coaching for a new executive, the coach begins with a blank sheet: interviews, a 360, goal setting. The search firm already knows the executive's assessed strengths and derailers, the reasons the CEO hesitated, and the organisation's politics as candidates and referees described them.

## What Already Exists
Coaching platforms such as BetterUp and CoachHub match employees with coaches and track progress; independent executive coaches and CCL programmes serve senior leaders; the large search firms sell integration programmes through their leadership advisory arms.

## The Customization Gap
Integration coaching needs: a consented hand-off of the search assessment to the coach (development needs, flagged risks, the brief and what changed during the search); an agenda built around the first 18 months' predictable crises rather than generic development; confidential stakeholder check-ins; and an outcome link back to the search firm's placement record.

## Target Customer
Chief people officers buying onboarding for senior hires; search firms extending their offer past acceptance.

## Impact If Solved
Coaching that starts months ahead and aims at the specific risks of this hire, at the moment those risks are highest.
