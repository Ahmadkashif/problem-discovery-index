# The Assessment That Stops at the Offer

**Niche:** [[niches/executive-search-firms/placed-executive-first-18-months/profile|The Placed Executive's First 18 Months]]
**Industry:** [[industries/executive-search-firms|Executive Search Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The search firm documented exactly where the new executive was likely to struggle, and the document ended in a client report that nobody reopened after the start date.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #seq2seq #workflow-orchestration #worker-facing #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a newly placed executive in role through the first eighteen months, and whoever spots a failing integration early enough for the client to intervene before the departure is announced takes the account.

## The Problem
A candidate report names development areas and risks — limited experience with a PE board, a tendency to over-rely on a trusted deputy, no exposure to a unionised workforce. Once the offer is accepted the report becomes a historical document. The executive never sees it, the CEO forgets it, and the onboarding plan is the company's standard.

## Why It's Still Broken
The report is written to persuade a client to hire, not to guide integration, and nobody is assigned to translate it into an onboarding plan. Sharing assessment content with the executive needs care and consent.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A short integration brief generated from the candidate report and references at acceptance: the three risks most worth watching, the relationships to build first, and the questions the CEO should ask at 30 and 90 days — reviewed by the consultant, shared with the hiring manager, and, with consent, with the executive.

## Who Feels the Pain
New executives walking into known traps; CEOs who hired with doubts and then forgot them; consultants running a free replacement search for a failure they predicted.

## Impact If Fixed
The search's best insight used at the moment it matters, for an hour of the consultant's time.
