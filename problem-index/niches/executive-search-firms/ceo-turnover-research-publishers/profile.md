# CEO Turnover & Succession Research Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/executive-search-firms|Executive Search Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Organisations that track executive and director transitions and publish the benchmark reports boards and search firms cite: the Conference Board's CEO succession research, Challenger, Gray & Christmas's turnover tracking, and the search firms' own indices — Russell Reynolds' Global CEO Turnover Index (234 CEO exits across 13 indices in 2025) and Spencer Stuart's Board Index. Membership dues, sponsorship or the search firm's marketing budget pay for them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 researchers at the largest (estimate); the search firms' indices come from small teams
**Output:** Annual succession-practice reports, turnover indices, board composition indices
**Proprietary data:** Longitudinal transition datasets assembled from public filings and announcements
**Clock:** Self-set annual publication, timed to proxy season
**Buyer:** Head of governance research
