# Faculty-Hiring Systems Fitted to Confidential Presidential Searches

**Niche:** [[niches/executive-search-firms/higher-ed-nonprofit-search/profile|Higher Education & Nonprofit Leadership Search]]
**Industry:** [[industries/executive-search-firms|Executive Search Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Universities already run faculty searches through dedicated applicant systems; a presidential search needs the same committee workflow with confidentiality rules that change at the finalist stage.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #compliance #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to bring a committee-run search to a finalist the governing board confirms in a single cycle, and whoever's searches least often fail and reopen takes the account.

## The Problem
Committee members receive dossiers by email or shared drive, download them, and forward them; access logs do not exist; nominations from the community arrive through a web form and a consultant's inbox. In open-records states, what is a public record and when is a legal question the committee cannot afford to get wrong.

## What Already Exists
Faculty-hiring and review platforms such as Interfolio handle applications, committee review and scoring for academic searches; general document portals handle sharing.

## The Customization Gap
Leadership search needs: confidentiality tiers that change by stage under the state's records law (for example applicants shielded until finalists in Florida since 2022); access logging and NDA tracking for every committee member; nominations and sourced candidates who never applied held separately from applicants; redaction for any records request; and the search firm, not the institution, as administrator, so the firm's sourcing notes stay its own.

## Target Customer
Search firms' education practices and university general counsel offices.

## Impact If Solved
A defensible confidentiality record and less exposure to leaks that drive away sitting presidents, who will not apply if their candidacy might become public.
