# A Committee Calibration Tool

**Niche:** [[niches/executive-search-firms/higher-ed-nonprofit-search/profile|Higher Education & Nonprofit Leadership Search]]
**Industry:** [[industries/executive-search-firms|Executive Search Firms]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A search committee drawn from many constituencies discovers it disagrees about what it wants only when it votes on finalists.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #hypothesis-testing #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to bring a committee-run search to a finalist the governing board confirms in a single cycle, and whoever's searches least often fail and reopen takes the account.

## The Problem
Presidential search committees agree a leadership profile early, often a long list of qualities nobody could object to. Members then read dossiers through their own constituency's priorities — research, fundraising, student life, finances — and score candidates on paper or in spreadsheets the consultant collates. The disagreement inside the committee stays hidden until the longlist-to-semifinal cut or the finalist vote, when it splits along constituency lines or the board overrides the committee.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Committee facilitation is treated as the consultant's craft, and scoring is seen as a formality before a discussion. Search firms sell judgement and process, not tools, and a committee portal is a document store.

## What to Build
Structured, confidential scoring against the agreed profile, captured per member and per criterion, with the consultant seeing where members' implicit weights differ (one group scoring only fundraising, another only academic record) and where a candidate splits the committee. Early calibration exercises on anonymised sample dossiers expose disagreement before real candidates are at stake; the tool tracks whether the committee's revealed priorities match its published profile.

## Target Customer
Education and nonprofit practice leaders at specialist and large firms, offered to committee chairs as part of the engagement.

## Impact If Built
Disagreement surfaced and resolved at the profile stage rather than the final vote, and fewer searches that end without an appointment.
