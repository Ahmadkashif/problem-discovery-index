# Private Equity Portfolio Leadership Search

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/executive-search-firms|Executive Search Firms]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to place portfolio-company CEOs and CFOs who are still in the seat at exit, and whoever can show a sponsor the survival record of its placements across that sponsor's own past portfolios takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3B global client fee spend on CEO, CFO and top-team searches for PE-backed companies, plus pre-deal management assessments (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of the ~$20B global industry (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — sponsors track portfolio leadership in spreadsheets; search firms in CRMs
**Target Buyer:** Sponsor talent partner or operating partner; portfolio company board
**Automation Potential:** Medium — benches and benchmarking automate, assessment does not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Private-equity sponsors are the industry's most frequent repeat buyers. A sponsor with dozens of portfolio companies replaces a large share of CEOs and CFOs during a hold, often in the first year, and buys management assessments of incumbents before a deal closes. The buyer is a talent or operating partner who sees every placement's outcome in the portfolio's results, the clock is the value-creation plan and the exit, and the definition of success is unusually concrete: did the executive deliver the plan and stay to exit. Most large firms run dedicated private-capital practices, and specialists compete hard for sponsor panels.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Search CRMs, sponsor panel agreements, management assessment frameworks and psychometrics, and sponsors' own portfolio-leadership spreadsheets; sponsor in-house data teams are small at most firms (see [[niches/private-equity-firms/sponsor-in-house-data-teams/profile|Sponsor In-House Data Teams]]). Gaps: no shared record of placement survival by sponsor and deal type; pre-deal assessments not linked to later outcomes; CFO searches started after close.
