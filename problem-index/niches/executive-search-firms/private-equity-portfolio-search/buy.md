# Management Assessment Frameworks Fitted to the Diligence Window

**Niche:** [[niches/executive-search-firms/private-equity-portfolio-search/profile|Private Equity Portfolio Leadership Search]]
**Industry:** [[industries/executive-search-firms|Executive Search Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Psychometric and structured-interview assessments are mature; assessing a target's management team inside a few weeks of diligence, under NDA, and handing the result to the hundred-day plan is not what they were built for.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #descriptive-statistics #workflow-orchestration #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to place portfolio-company CEOs and CFOs who are still in the seat at exit, and whoever can show a sponsor the survival record of its placements across that sponsor's own past portfolios takes the account.

## The Problem
Before signing, a sponsor wants to know whether the target's CEO and CFO can deliver the plan. A search or assessment firm interviews them, often alongside psychometrics, inside the diligence timeline, and reports a view. After close, that view rarely travels with the deal: the operating team starts again and the replacement search starts late.

## What Already Exists
Assessment publishers (SHL, Hogan, Korn Ferry's instruments) and structured-interview methods used by leadership advisory firms; see [[niches/corporate-training/talent-assessment-publishers/profile|Talent Assessment Publishers]] for the instrument layer.

## The Customization Gap
Deal assessment needs: compressed delivery against the diligence calendar; evaluation against the sponsor's value-creation plan rather than generic leadership competencies; confidentiality across a deal that may not happen; a hand-off format that feeds the hundred-day plan and pre-positions any replacement search; and a record linking each assessment to the executive's later outcome so the sponsor learns which red flags mattered.

## Target Customer
Sponsor talent and operating partners; private-capital practices at search and advisory firms.

## Impact If Solved
Assessments that change the plan rather than sitting in the data room, and earlier, better-targeted leadership changes.
