# Global Search Firm Research & Knowledge Centres

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/executive-search-firms|Executive Search Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
The central research, knowledge and execution teams of the large search firms — Korn Ferry, Heidrick & Struggles, Spencer Stuart, Russell Reynolds, Egon Zehnder and the largest mid-sized firms and networks. They build the target company lists, market maps, longlists, candidate research and benchmark slates behind every mandate, which clients pay for through a retainer of about one-third of first-year compensation whether or not a placement results.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~100–500 researchers, associates and knowledge staff per large firm (estimate; Heidrick reports 565 associates and engagement managers against 418 search consultants, Korn Ferry 1,696 search employees against ~551 consultants)
**Output:** Market maps, longlists, candidate research profiles and benchmark slates — the substance of the retained search
**Proprietary data:** Decades of candidate interviews, assessments, compensation learned in conversation, approach histories and placements; the identification layer itself is mostly public
**Clock:** Set by the client — a CEO's announced departure, a board's proxy cycle, a sponsor's plan — with no third-party deadline on the research itself
**Buyer:** Chief Knowledge Officer / Global Head of Research

**Kill switches:** none live — candidate records are the firm's own compilation, held as data controller under GDPR, which governs notice and retention rather than blocking use; client briefs and off-limits terms are confidential but are not the corpus. Assessments of a client's own executives sit in the advisory pocket, which does carry the switch.
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. The strongest clean near-miss in the industry: research labour that grows with mandates (Korn Ferry opened 6,300+ in fiscal 2025) on an archive no rival holds. Q1 is 4 because clients hire on partner relationships and pay for a placement process, not a research product; Q2 is 4 because part of the headcount counted (Heidrick's engagement managers) does execution and coordination rather than research; Q4 is 3 because every deadline is the client's own; Q5 is 3 because research functions of this size exist at the five global firms and a few dozen others (estimate).
