# Executive & Board Profile Data Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/executive-search-firms|Executive Search Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Vendors that compile profiles of executives and directors and the connections between them from public sources — BoardEx (part of Altrata with Boardroom Insiders and RelSci), Equilar's BoardEdge and ExecAtlas, and the people data inside the large financial data terminals. Search firms license them for board and C-suite mapping and relationship paths; banks, consultancies and wealth managers buy the same data for business development.

## Insight Function
**Size:** BoardEx reports 350+ analysts maintaining profiles, mostly production; methodology, quality and analytics teams of ~20–60 per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Executive and director profiles, relationship maps, board composition and tenure data
**Proprietary data:** 1.7 million executive profiles at BoardEx with accumulated career and connection history, compiled from public sources
**Clock:** None binding — appointments and proxies arrive continuously, with a seasonal peak
**Buyer:** Head of data / chief product officer at the vendor
