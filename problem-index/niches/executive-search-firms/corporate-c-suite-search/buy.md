## The Problem
The evidence behind a C-suite recommendation is the interview, and it is captured in whatever notes the partner takes. Two partners on the same search write differently, the associate writing the report works from fragments, and the evidence for each competency in the brief is reconstructed afterwards.

## What Already Exists
Interview intelligence platforms such as BrightHire and Metaview record and transcribe interviews, extract evidence against a scorecard and draft feedback for corporate recruiting teams; general meeting transcription tools do the same for any call.

## The Customization Gap
C-suite search needs: capture without recording the candidate — the partner's own post-interview voice debrief structured against the brief — because senior candidates rarely consent to being recorded while employed; the firm's proprietary competency and derailer framework rather than the employer's scorecard; separation of what can go in a client report from what stays internal; references treated as a distinct evidence type; and retention rules for personal data of candidates who were not hired.

## Target Customer
Practice leaders and knowledge teams at global and mid-sized firms that run a common assessment framework across partners.

## Impact If Solved
Consistent, comparable interview evidence across partners and offices, faster candidate reports, and an assessment record that can later be graded against outcomes.
