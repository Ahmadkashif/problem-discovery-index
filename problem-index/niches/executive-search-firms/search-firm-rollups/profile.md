# Search Firm Rollups & Consolidators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/executive-search-firms|Executive Search Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Private-equity-backed platforms and acquisitive firms buying boutique search firms, plus the take-private of Heidrick & Struggles by a consortium led by Advent International and Corvex, completed in December 2025 at about $1.3B. Their corporate development teams screen targets, model partner retention and integrate acquired practices.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Corporate development teams under ten per platform (estimate)
**Output:** Internal acquisition theses and integration plans
**Proprietary data:** Acquired firms' financials and partner books, held privately
**Clock:** Deal timetables, self-set
**Buyer:** Head of corporate development
