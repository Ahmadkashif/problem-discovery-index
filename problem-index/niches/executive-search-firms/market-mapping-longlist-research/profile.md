# Market Mapping & Longlist Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/executive-search-firms|Executive Search Firms]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce a complete, correctly levelled map of who holds every target role at every target company, and whoever's map is still accurate on the day the partner makes the first call takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.5B global spend on search research labour, executive data licences and sourcing tools (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of the ~$20B global industry (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — the inputs are digital, the assembly is manual
**Target Buyer:** Global head of research or chief knowledge officer at large firms; managing partner at smaller ones
**Automation Potential:** High — identification, levelling and refresh are mechanical once the target universe is defined

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Identification is the most repetitive part of a search and the one that scales with headcount: target company list, org-chart reconstruction, title levelling, longlist, first approach. Large firms run research and knowledge centres for it — Heidrick reports 565 associates and engagement managers against 418 search consultants — and smaller firms buy it from outsourcers or do it at night. The output is checkable: a map is complete or not, a person holds the role or does not, and the first call reveals which. Generative AI has already reached the edges: 68.9% of firms in Clockwork's 2023 survey wanted AI to suggest target company lists and 54.4% benchmark candidate profiles.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Search CRMs, executive and board data (BoardEx with 1.7 million executive profiles, Equilar ExecAtlas), professional networks, AI sourcing tools (Pin, SeekOut, hireEZ), company filings and press releases, and outsourced research (Intellerati, Corporate Navigators). Gaps: coverage that thins below the C-suite; titles that mean different things at different companies; maps that decay within weeks and are rebuilt per mandate.
