# The Longlist That Was Stale on Arrival

**Niche:** [[niches/executive-search-firms/market-mapping-longlist-research/profile|Market Mapping & Longlist Research]]
**Industry:** [[industries/executive-search-firms|Executive Search Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A longlist built over weeks reaches the client with people who have already moved, been promoted or become off-limits.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #automation #quick-win #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce a complete, correctly levelled map of who holds every target role at every target company, and whoever's map is still accurate on the day the partner makes the first call takes the account.

## The Problem
Research is done in the order targets are worked, so the first names on a longlist were checked weeks before the list is presented. Executives change roles continuously; a client's new mandate can make a whole company off-limits overnight. The client spots a name that left months ago and the firm's credibility drops.

## Why It's Still Broken
Verification is a final manual pass, squeezed by the deadline for the first client meeting, and nothing re-checks a name once it is entered.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Re-verify every name automatically the day before presentation: current role from filings, announcements and change alerts; off-limits status against the latest client list; GDPR notice status for European candidates; and a flag on anything the researcher should confirm by hand.

## Who Feels the Pain
Researchers embarrassed in front of partners; partners in front of clients; clients who wonder what else is wrong.

## Impact If Fixed
A longlist that is accurate on the day it is shown, at the cost of an overnight check.
