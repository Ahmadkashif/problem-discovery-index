# Executive Databases Extended Below the Proxy Line

**Industry:** [[industries/executive-search-firms|Executive Search Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Board and executive databases are thorough on directors and proxy-named officers; most search candidates sit one or two levels below, where coverage thins.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #word-embeddings #k-nearest-neighbors #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce a complete, correctly levelled map of who holds every target role at every target company, and whoever's map is still accurate on the day the partner makes the first call takes the account.

## The Problem
A CFO search targets sitting CFOs and the divisional finance leaders and treasurers who could step up. Commercial executive data covers the first group well, from proxies and announcements, and the second patchily.

## What Already Exists
BoardEx (Altrata) reports 1.7 million executive profiles maintained by 350+ analysts from public sources, with relationship mapping; Equilar's BoardEdge and ExecAtlas cover directors and Section 16 officers; Boardroom Insiders profiles senior executives for sellers.

## The Customization Gap
Search needs: the layer below the named officers, assembled from segment disclosures, conference programmes, patents, press releases and the firm's own interview records; consistent levelling; links from each person to the firm's approach history; refresh triggered by change signals; and an export into the firm's CRM. The vendor data is the frame; the firm's history and the below-proxy layer are the customisation.

## Target Customer
Research directors at large firms who already license executive data and fill the gaps by hand.

## Impact If Solved
Licensed data used as the start of the map rather than the whole of it, with the expensive gap-filling done once per universe instead of once per search.
