# Executive Search Research Outsourcers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/executive-search-firms|Executive Search Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Firms that sell search research unbundled — candidate identification, talent mapping, org-chart research, benchmark profiles — to boutiques, corporate talent teams and occasionally larger firms, priced per project or per name. Intellerati, Corporate Navigators and Achivix are US examples; offshore research desks serve the same need.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~3–20 researchers per firm (estimate), most under ten
**Output:** Market maps, name lists and org charts delivered to the commissioning firm
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated maps, held under the commissioning firm's confidentiality
**Clock:** The commissioning firm's search timeline
**Buyer:** Founder
