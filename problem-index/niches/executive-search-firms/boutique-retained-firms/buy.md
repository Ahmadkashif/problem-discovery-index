# AI Sourcing Tools Fitted to Retained Research Discipline

**Niche:** [[niches/executive-search-firms/boutique-retained-firms/profile|Boutique Retained Search Firms]]
**Industry:** [[industries/executive-search-firms|Executive Search Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** AI sourcing tools rank millions of profiles against a job description; a retained search needs a documented map of a defined universe, not a ranked list of keyword matches.
**Tags:** #word-embeddings #k-nearest-neighbors #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give a firm of fewer than twenty people the research bench it cannot afford to hire, and whoever turns the boutique's own past searches into a longlist its partner trusts takes the account.

## The Problem
Boutiques buy AI sourcing subscriptions to replace the researcher they cannot hire. The tools return plausible profiles quickly, but the client paying a retainer expects a defensible account of the market: which companies were targeted, who holds the role at each, why each person is in or out.

## What Already Exists
AI sourcing tools such as Pin, SeekOut and hireEZ search large profile databases with natural-language queries and automate outreach; Loxo bundles sourcing with an applicant system.

## The Customization Gap
Retained research needs: a target-company-first workflow rather than profile search; coverage reporting (how many targets mapped, how many roles found); levelling of titles across companies; off-limits screening against the boutique's clients; a research narrative for the client's progress report; GDPR notices for European candidates; and export into the boutique's CRM with the reasoning attached.

## Target Customer
Boutique partners who currently pay for both a sourcing tool and occasional outsourced research.

## Impact If Solved
Volume tooling bent to the discipline the client is paying for, and a research record the boutique can show.
