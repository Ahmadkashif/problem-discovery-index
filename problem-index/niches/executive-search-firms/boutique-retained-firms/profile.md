# Boutique Retained Search Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/executive-search-firms|Executive Search Firms]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give a firm of fewer than twenty people the research bench it cannot afford to hire, and whoever turns the boutique's own past searches into a longlist its partner trusts takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M global spend by boutique firms on search software, data, sourcing tools and outsourced research (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% of the ~$20B global industry as tooling spend (estimate); boutiques earn a far larger share of fees
**Digital Adoption:** Medium-Low — a search CRM or a spreadsheet, a sourcing subscription, Outlook
**Target Buyer:** Founding partner or managing partner
**Automation Potential:** High for research and reporting, which is exactly where boutiques are thinnest

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
County Business Patterns counted 5,897 executive search establishments in 2023, 93% of them with fewer than 20 employees. Most are boutiques: a founder who left a large firm, a sector specialist, a few partners and a researcher or two. They win on sector depth, seniority of attention and price, and compete with firms whose knowledge centres employ hundreds. The partner does the research at night or buys it piecemeal from research outsourcers such as Intellerati. Tools are built either for the large firms (enterprise CRMs, data licences priced per seat) or for volume recruiting (AI sourcing tools that rank profiles by keyword fit). Boutiques sit between.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Search CRMs priced for small firms (Clockwork, Ezekia, FileFinder), AI sourcing tools (Pin, SeekOut, hireEZ), professional-network subscriptions, and outsourced research. Gaps: no research bench; the firm's own past searches unused as a starting point; research knowledge lost when the one researcher leaves; no levelling or off-limits discipline beyond the partner's memory.
