# Law-Firm Conflict Checking Fitted to Candidates

**Niche:** [[niches/executive-search-firms/off-limits-and-candidate-data-compliance/profile|Off-Limits, Conflicts & Candidate-Data Compliance]]
**Industry:** [[industries/executive-search-firms|Executive Search Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Law firms already run mature conflict-checking against corporate families; search firms need the same engine pointed at people and at time-limited terms.
**Tags:** #graph-theory #k-nearest-neighbors #compliance #workflow-orchestration #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to clear every name on a longlist against every client's off-limits terms and every candidate's privacy obligations before the first approach, and whoever keeps the most names callable without a single client breach takes the account.

## The Problem
Large search firms face a conflict problem as hard as a law firm's — thousands of clients, overlapping terms, global offices — with tooling built for a small firm.

## What Already Exists
Intapp's conflicts and relationship-intelligence products resolve parties to corporate families and route clearance for law and professional-services firms; BoardEx data already feeds Intapp through a partnership. Search CRMs flag client companies.

## The Customization Gap
Search adaptation needs: the candidate's current employer as the party checked; off-limits terms with start and end dates and scope rules; "source but do not approach" as an outcome; sponsor-portfolio scope for private-equity clients; integration with the search CRM so the check runs at longlist entry, not at a weekly review; and client-facing evidence that the firm honoured its terms.

## Target Customer
Global and large mid-sized search firms with a central risk or legal function.

## Impact If Solved
A proven conflict engine adapted rather than rebuilt, with the search-specific rules layered on top.
