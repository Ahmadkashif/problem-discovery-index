# A Standing Benchmark Between Successions

**Niche:** [[niches/executive-search-firms/ceo-succession-benchmarking/profile|CEO Succession & Insider-Outsider Benchmarking]]
**Industry:** [[industries/executive-search-firms|Executive Search Firms]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Boards compare their internal CEO candidates with the outside market once, when a succession starts, using a slate assembled in weeks; the comparison could be kept current every year.
**Tags:** #survival-analysis #gradient-boosting #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #feature-engineering #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give a board a defensible comparison of its internal CEO candidates against the external market before the incumbent's departure is announced, and whoever has already assessed the internal bench when the CEO resigns takes the account.

## The Problem
A board's succession plan names two or three internal candidates and a development plan for each. When the CEO signals departure, the board asks whether those insiders are the best available, and the search firm assembles an external benchmark slate: sitting CEOs and divisional presidents in comparable companies, profiled from public information and the firm's own records. The slate is built under time pressure and is out of date within months.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Benchmarking is sold as part of a CEO search, so it is produced only when a search is bought. Boards do not want to pay for an external slate every year, and firms do not want to give away the research that wins the search.

## What to Build
A continuously maintained external benchmark for each client's CEO role: a defined peer set of sitting and ready-soon leaders, refreshed from filings, appointments and the firm's own interview records, with each internal candidate's assessed profile compared on the same dimensions. Annual board readouts show how the internal bench's relative position has moved, and departures or promotions in the external set trigger updates. A record of the firm's past insider-versus-outsider recommendations and their outcomes calibrates the comparison.

## Target Customer
Global CEO & Board practice leaders at the large firms, sold as a retained annual service to board chairs and nominating committee chairs.

## Impact If Built
The board enters a succession with a current, evidenced comparison rather than a rushed slate, and the firm holding it is in place when the CEO resigns.
