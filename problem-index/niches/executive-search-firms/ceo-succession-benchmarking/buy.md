# Talent Review Software Lifted to Board Level

**Niche:** [[niches/executive-search-firms/ceo-succession-benchmarking/profile|CEO Succession & Insider-Outsider Benchmarking]]
**Industry:** [[industries/executive-search-firms|Executive Search Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** HR suites run talent reviews and nine-box grids for thousands of managers; CEO succession is a board matter that sits outside the company's own HR system and needs the outside market in the same frame.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #descriptive-statistics #workflow-orchestration #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give a board a defensible comparison of its internal CEO candidates against the external market before the incumbent's departure is announced, and whoever has already assessed the internal bench when the CEO resigns takes the account.

## The Problem
Internal CEO candidates are assessed by the search firm or a leadership advisory practice; the results are presented in board sessions as slides and kept in the firm's files and the board portal. Readiness ratings, development actions and external comparisons are not tracked over time in any system.

## What Already Exists
Succession modules in Workday and SAP SuccessFactors run talent reviews, readiness ratings and succession slates for the management population; board portals such as Diligent hold board papers.

## The Customization Gap
Board-level succession needs: access restricted to the board and the external adviser, outside the company's HR system that the candidates' own teams administer; external benchmark candidates held alongside internal ones; assessment evidence from the adviser's framework rather than the HR competency model; an emergency-succession plan with named interim leaders and a review date; and a history across years so the board sees movement, not a snapshot.

## Target Customer
Nominating committee chairs and chief people officers, through the search or advisory firm that runs the assessment.

## Impact If Solved
A board-owned succession record that persists across chairs and advisers, and less reinvention each time the question returns.
