# A Function Map That Keeps Itself Current

**Niche:** [[niches/executive-search-firms/functional-c-suite-search/profile|Functional C-Suite Search (CFO, CHRO, CIO, CMO)]]
**Industry:** [[industries/executive-search-firms|Executive Search Firms]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Every CFO search maps the same few thousand finance leaders again from scratch, when the map could be kept current between searches and scored for who is ready to move.
**Tags:** #word-embeddings #k-nearest-neighbors #gradient-boosting #survival-analysis #feature-engineering #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to put a calibrated slate of sitting functional leaders in front of the CEO within weeks of the brief, and whoever has the function already mapped, with current compensation and move-readiness, takes the account.

## The Problem
The pool for a functional C-suite search is finite. In a given sector and size band there may be a few hundred sitting CFOs and a few hundred credible deputies (estimate). A practice research team maps them for one search, the map goes stale, and the next search rebuilds it. Move-readiness — tenure, vesting, a new CEO above them, a recent acquisition — is assessed by memory.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Research is organised by mandate, so no one owns a map between mandates. Commercial executive databases cover board members and proxy-named officers but thin out one level down, where most candidates sit.

## What to Build
A practice-level map maintained continuously: every incumbent and deputy in the practice's universe, levelled consistently, linked to the firm's interview history and every approach made, refreshed from filings, appointments and announcements. A move-propensity score combines tenure, vesting calendars from proxies and Form 4s, company events and the firm's own approach history; compensation is estimated from proxy data and the firm's interview records. New searches start from the map with a ranked first cut.

## Target Customer
Global functional practice leaders and their research directors at large and mid-sized firms.

## Impact If Built
Slates in days rather than weeks, consistent levelling across offices, and research effort spent on assessing candidates rather than finding them again.
