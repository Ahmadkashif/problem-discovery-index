# Job-Change Alerts Adapted to Executive Pools

**Niche:** [[niches/executive-search-firms/functional-c-suite-search/profile|Functional C-Suite Search (CFO, CHRO, CIO, CMO)]]
**Industry:** [[industries/executive-search-firms|Executive Search Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Sales-intelligence tools already alert sellers when a buyer changes jobs; a search practice needs the same signal on its candidate pool, tied to levels, off-limits and the firm's own approach history.
**Tags:** #word-embeddings #k-nearest-neighbors #feature-engineering #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to put a calibrated slate of sitting functional leaders in front of the CEO within weeks of the brief, and whoever has the function already mapped, with current compensation and move-readiness, takes the account.

## The Problem
A function map decays continuously as leaders are promoted, move or leave. Practices find out when an associate calls a candidate who left months ago.

## What Already Exists
Sales-intelligence and revenue platforms track job changes among a seller's contacts and alert account teams; BoardEx and Equilar track board and named-officer changes; Form 8-K Item 5.02 filings report public-company officer departures and appointments within four business days.

## The Customization Gap
Search needs: change detection on the deputy layer that sales tools cover patchily; re-levelling of the new role, since a lateral move at a larger company can be a promotion; a check of the new employer against off-limits terms, which may make a candidate unapproachable overnight; linkage to the firm's interview history so the partner who knows the person is told; and business-development triggers when a client's executive leaves, which is also a new mandate.

## Target Customer
Functional practice research directors and the business-development leads of the same practices.

## Impact If Solved
A map that stays accurate without a refresh project, and earlier knowledge of the vacancies that become the next mandates.
