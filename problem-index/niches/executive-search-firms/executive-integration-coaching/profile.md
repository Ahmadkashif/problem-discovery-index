# Executive Integration & Onboarding Coaching

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/executive-search-firms|Executive Search Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Coaches and programmes that support newly appointed executives through their first months: independent executive coaches, the Center for Creative Leadership, coaching platforms, and integration offers from the search firms' leadership advisory arms. The hiring company pays, usually per engagement.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Individual coaches or small practices; the large providers' staff deliver sessions rather than research
**Output:** Coaching engagements and integration plans
**Proprietary data:** Session notes, confidential to the executive and the client
**Clock:** The executive's first 90 days and first year, set by the client
**Buyer:** Practice founder or programme director
