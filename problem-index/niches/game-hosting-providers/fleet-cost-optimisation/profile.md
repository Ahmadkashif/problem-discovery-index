# Fleet Cost Optimisation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/game-hosting-providers|Game Hosting Providers]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to serve the same concurrency for less money across regions, instance types and pricing models, on margins thin enough that the difference decides who wins the contract — and whoever optimises it takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$100M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — manual
**Target Buyer:** Cost and infrastructure leads
**Automation Potential:** Very high — packing, pricing and placement

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Given a required capacity, the cost of delivering it varies substantially with instance type, region, pricing model and how densely sessions are packed onto machines. Margins in this business are thin, and the difference between a well-optimised fleet and an average one is a large share of them. The decisions are mechanical, repeat continuously, and are mostly made once at setup and left — instance families chosen years ago, pricing commitments unreviewed, and sessions packed conservatively because nobody measured how far they could go.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A cloud bill, a reserved capacity commitment and an instance type chosen at some point. The gaps: no session density measurement against player experience; no automated instance family evaluation; no spot or preemptible strategy compatible with live sessions; no regional price arbitrage; and no per-title cost attribution.
