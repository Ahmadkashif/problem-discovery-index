# Launch Day Operations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/game-hosting-providers|Game Hosting Providers]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make a one-time event at a fixed hour, in front of everyone, run by a small team on somebody else's estimate, into something rehearsed rather than improvised — and whoever does it takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — improvised
**Target Buyer:** Solutions architects
**Automation Potential:** Medium — rehearsal, runbooks and readiness

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A launch happens once, at a fixed hour, in front of everyone, and a small infrastructure team is responsible for whether it works. There is no second attempt and no gradual ramp. The preparation is a checklist someone wrote, a load test that may not resemble the real traffic shape, and a war room. Every provider does this repeatedly across many titles and almost none of the learning accumulates — each launch is prepared as though it were the first.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A load test, a runbook document, a shared war room channel and a set of dashboards. The gaps: load tests that do not match real traffic shape; no rehearsal of failure modes; no readiness assessment against past launches; no defined decision authority during the event; and no structured post-mortem feeding the next one.
