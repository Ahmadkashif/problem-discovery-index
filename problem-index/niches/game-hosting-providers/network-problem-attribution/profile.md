# Network Problem Attribution

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/game-hosting-providers|Game Hosting Providers]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to establish which layer is responsible when a player says the game is lagging, across four parties who can each prove their own component is fine — and whoever can attribute it takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — nobody owns it
**Target Buyer:** Support and platform leads
**Automation Potential:** High — path measurement and inference

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A player reports lag. The cause may be their own hardware or wireless connection, their internet provider, a peering path, the region they were allocated to, or the server itself. Each party measures only its own component and each can demonstrate that its component is healthy. The player is left with a problem nobody owns, and the sector has no shared instrument that spans the layers. The commercial consequence is that the provider and the studio each spend support effort proving innocence rather than resolving anything.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Server health dashboards, a client-side ping display, and a support macro asking the player to restart their router. The gaps: no end-to-end path measurement; no correlation across players on the same route; no history per player to compare against; no shared evidence format between parties; and no ownership when the cause is between networks.
