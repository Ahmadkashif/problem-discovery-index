## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Demand for game servers is extraordinarily spiky and geographically specific. A launch, a seasonal event, a free weekend or a streamer moment can multiply concurrency several-fold within an hour in particular regions, and the capacity decision must be made in advance. Over-provisioning is a visible cost on thin margins; under-provisioning is a queue, a disconnection or a failed launch discussed publicly within minutes. The signals that would predict the spike — wishlists, preorders, streamer schedules, regional interest — are observable and largely unused.

## Current Tools & Gaps
An autoscaler with a lag, a capacity spreadsheet, a studio's estimate and an engineer's judgement. The gaps: no demand forecast from external signals; no regional granularity in planning; no asymmetric cost model for over versus under; no event calendar integration; and no learning from past forecast errors.
