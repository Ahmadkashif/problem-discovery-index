# Livestock Genetic Evaluation Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/livestock-operations|Livestock Operations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
The breed associations and dairy evaluation bodies compute the genetic merit estimates — expected progeny differences and genomic indices — that determine what a bull, a cow, or a straw of semen is worth. Pass 1 describes an industry where most operations still run on paper and generational observation; the genetic evaluation is the one rigorous quantitative product the whole sector transacts on.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 quantitative geneticists, statisticians, data managers, and breed analysts
**Output:** Expected progeny differences and genomic predictions, selection indices, evaluation runs, breed improvement research
**Proprietary data:** Multi-decade pedigree, performance, and now genomic databases covering tens of millions of animals — assembled through member reporting over a century and impossible to reconstruct
**Clock:** Scheduled evaluation runs and sale catalogue deadlines, both fixed and consequential
**Buyer:** Director of Genetic Evaluation / Chief Science Officer
