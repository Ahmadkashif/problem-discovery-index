# Feed & Nutrition Technical Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/livestock-operations|Livestock Operations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The feed companies employ the livestock nutritionists Pass 1 identifies as part of the industry's workforce, formulating rations and advising producers on feeding programmes. The advice is how the feed is sold.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 nutritionists, animal scientists, and technical service staff per major supplier
**Output:** Ration formulations, feeding programme recommendations, performance troubleshooting, on-farm trial results
**Proprietary data:** Feed conversion and performance outcomes across many operations, plus proprietary formulation research
**Clock:** Feeding cycles and commodity ingredient price movements
**Buyer:** Director of Nutrition / VP of Technical Services
