# Breed Association Programme Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/livestock-operations|Livestock Operations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Beyond genetic evaluation, the breed associations run member education, verified production programmes, and the market development research that positions a breed's cattle to feeders and packers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 programme staff and analysts
**Output:** Verified programme administration, market development research, member education, breed promotion
**Proprietary data:** Membership and programme participation data
**Clock:** Annual meetings and programme enrolment cycles
**Buyer:** Director of Programmes — member funded
