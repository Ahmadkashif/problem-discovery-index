# Livestock & Meat Price Reporting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/livestock-operations|Livestock Operations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Price reporting agencies publish the meat and livestock price assessments packers, feeders, and retailers contract against, in markets where much of the trade is not transparent.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 market reporters, analysts, and economists
**Output:** Daily price assessments, market commentary, forward curves and analytics
**Proprietary data:** Reporter-collected transaction and offer data, and the assessment methodology built around it
**Clock:** Daily publication that settles physical contracts
**Buyer:** Editorial director / Chief Data Officer
