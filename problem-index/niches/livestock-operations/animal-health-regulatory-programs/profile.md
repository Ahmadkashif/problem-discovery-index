# Animal Health Regulatory Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/livestock-operations|Livestock Operations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Federal and state animal health authorities run disease surveillance, control movement during outbreaks, administer identification and traceability programmes, and decide when a herd or a region is quarantined.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 veterinarians, epidemiologists, and analysts
**Output:** Surveillance findings, outbreak response decisions, movement controls, traceability administration
**Proprietary data:** Movement records, surveillance results, and outbreak investigations across the national herd
**Clock:** Outbreak response, measured in hours
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — federal and state agencies
