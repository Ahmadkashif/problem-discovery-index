# Livestock Genetics Companies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/livestock-operations|Livestock Operations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The genetics companies breed, prove, and market the bulls and boars whose semen producers buy, running their own genomic selection programmes alongside the breed associations' evaluations.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 geneticists, data scientists, and product staff
**Output:** Breeding programme decisions, proprietary genomic predictions, product line development, mating recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Proprietary nucleus herd performance and genomic data, plus customer herd records where producers share them
**Clock:** Breeding cycles and proof release schedules
**Buyer:** Chief Science Officer / Head of Genetics
