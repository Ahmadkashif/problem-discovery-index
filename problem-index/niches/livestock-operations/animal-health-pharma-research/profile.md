# Animal Health Research Organizations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/livestock-operations|Livestock Operations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The animal health companies run the research and regulatory organizations that develop vaccines, parasiticides, and therapeutics for livestock, generate the efficacy evidence regulators require, and provide the technical services that tell producers what to use and when.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-2,000 veterinarians, researchers, regulatory scientists, and technical services staff
**Output:** Product development and trial data, regulatory dossiers, treatment protocols, technical service and diagnostics support
**Proprietary data:** Efficacy trial data and field surveillance across many production systems and regions
**Clock:** Regulatory submission timelines and seasonal disease pressure
**Buyer:** VP of Research and Development / Chief Veterinary Officer
