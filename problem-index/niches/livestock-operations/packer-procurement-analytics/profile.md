# Packer Procurement Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/livestock-operations|Livestock Operations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The meat packers' procurement and carcass analytics groups decide what to pay for livestock, forecast supply, and grade every carcass through the plant — generating the yield and quality data that determines what a producer is actually paid under grid marketing.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 procurement analysts, economists, and carcass data staff
**Output:** Bid pricing and supply forecasting, carcass grading and yield data, grid pricing structures, producer feedback reports
**Proprietary data:** Carcass outcomes joined to source herds and feeding programmes — the only measurement of how a producer's genetics and management actually performed
**Clock:** Daily kill scheduling and weekly cash trade
**Buyer:** VP of Procurement / Chief Analytics Officer
