# Veterinary Diagnostic Laboratories

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/livestock-operations|Livestock Operations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Veterinary diagnostic laboratories identify the pathogen behind a disease outbreak in a herd or flock, and their result determines treatment, movement restrictions, and in reportable disease cases a regulatory response.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-200 veterinary pathologists, microbiologists, and technicians per lab
**Output:** Diagnostic results, disease surveillance reporting, outbreak investigation, antimicrobial susceptibility data
**Proprietary data:** Diagnoses and susceptibility patterns across regions, species, and production systems over decades
**Clock:** Urgent — an unidentified pathogen spreads through a herd while the sample is in transit
**Buyer:** Laboratory director — mostly university and state labs
