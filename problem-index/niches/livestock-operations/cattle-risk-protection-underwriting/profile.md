# Livestock Risk Protection Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/livestock-operations|Livestock Operations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Insurers and the federal programme behind them underwrite livestock price and margin protection, pricing the risk that cattle or hog prices fall between placement and marketing.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 actuaries, underwriters, and agricultural economists
**Output:** Rating and endorsement pricing, programme design, loss analysis, producer education
**Proprietary data:** Policy and indemnity experience across producers and regions, layered over public price series
**Clock:** Coverage endorsement periods tied to marketing dates
**Buyer:** Chief Actuary / Programme Director
