# Herd Management Software Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/livestock-operations|Livestock Operations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Herd management platforms and the emerging sensor vendors record individual animal events — weights, treatments, breeding, movements — for the minority of operations that keep digital records. Pass 1 notes most operations under 500 head still run on paper and memory.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 product and data staff
**Output:** Herd records and reporting, breeding and treatment tracking, sensor-derived alerts
**Proprietary data:** Individual animal event histories across participating operations, plus sensor time series
**Clock:** Breeding and marketing calendars
**Buyer:** Head of Product
