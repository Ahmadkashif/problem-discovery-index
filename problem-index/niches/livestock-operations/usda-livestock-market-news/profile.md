# Federal Livestock Market Reporting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/livestock-operations|Livestock Operations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Federal market news reporters collect and publish mandatory price reporting for cattle, hogs, and meat — the transparency mechanism that exists because packer concentration made voluntary reporting inadequate.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-400 market reporters, analysts, and statisticians
**Output:** Mandatory price reports, market news, slaughter and inventory statistics, situation and outlook analysis
**Proprietary data:** Transaction-level packer purchase data submitted under statute, published in aggregate
**Clock:** Daily and weekly statutory reporting deadlines
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — federal agency
