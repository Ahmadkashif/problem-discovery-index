**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pet-services|Pet Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer

## What They Do
Match owners to sitters, walkers and boarding hosts, and run the matching, pricing, trust and safety analytics over millions of bookings.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 data scientists, trust and safety analysts, and marketplace researchers
**Output:** Matching and ranking models, dynamic pricing, provider quality and safety scoring, demand forecasting
**Proprietary data:** Booking, review, incident and repeat-usage records across a large provider population
**Clock:** Continuous booking flow with strong holiday seasonality
**Buyer:** VP of Data Science
