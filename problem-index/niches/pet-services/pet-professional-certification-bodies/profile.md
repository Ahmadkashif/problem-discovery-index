# Pet Care Professional Certification Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pet-services|Pet Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Certify groomers, dog trainers and pet care providers against voluntary standards, and administer the examinations and continuing education behind those credentials.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 certification, curriculum, and examination staff across the bodies
**Output:** Certification examinations, curricula, practice standards, continuing education
**Proprietary data:** Candidate performance records at modest volume
**Clock:** Certification and renewal cycles
**Buyer:** Executive Director
