# Pet Care Management Software Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pet-services|Pet Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The analytics functions inside the boarding, daycare and grooming management platforms, producing occupancy forecasting, pricing guidance and industry benchmark reports.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 analysts and product staff
**Output:** Occupancy and demand forecasting, pricing benchmarks, retention analysis, operational dashboards
**Proprietary data:** Booking, service duration, incident and cancellation records across thousands of facilities
**Clock:** Continuous booking with severe holiday seasonality
**Buyer:** VP of Product
