# Pet Care Facility & Animal Bite Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pet-services|Pet Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Underwrite the liability programmes daycare, boarding and grooming operators buy, and analyse the bite, injury and death claims that come out of group play. Pass 1 names group play safety as the industry's highest-stakes operational challenge.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 programme underwriters, claims analysts, and risk consultants
**Output:** Programme pricing and eligibility, claims analysis, operating protocol requirements, loss control guidance
**Proprietary data:** Incident and claim records across facilities, with group size, supervision ratio and breed mix sometimes attached
**Clock:** Annual policy renewals
**Buyer:** Programme Underwriting Manager
