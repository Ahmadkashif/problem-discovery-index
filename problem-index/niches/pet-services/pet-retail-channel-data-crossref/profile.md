# Pet Category Retail Measurement Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pet-services|Pet Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Measure what pet products sell, where, at what price, through point-of-sale feeds and panels, and sell the resulting share and category analytics to the brands and retailers whose entire commercial planning runs on them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1,000-5,000 analysts, data scientists, and operations staff across the measurement firms
**Output:** Category and share reporting, price and promotion analysis, distribution tracking, panel-based buyer analytics
**Proprietary data:** Retailer point-of-sale feeds and household panels assembled over decades under contracts no new entrant can replicate
**Clock:** Weekly and monthly data releases against brand planning cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / SVP of Measurement Science
