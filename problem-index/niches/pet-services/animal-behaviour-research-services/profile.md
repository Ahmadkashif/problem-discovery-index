# Applied Animal Behaviour Consulting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pet-services|Pet Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Veterinary behaviourists and applied animal behaviourists who assess aggression, anxiety and problem behaviour, and design intervention plans — including the assessments that determine whether a dog is safe in group settings.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 across the larger practices and university services
**Output:** Behaviour assessments, intervention plans, expert opinions in bite cases, research
**Proprietary data:** Case records of assessed animals and intervention outcomes, held per practice
**Clock:** Referral flow
**Buyer:** Practice director
