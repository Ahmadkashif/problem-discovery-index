# Pet Food Regulatory & Label Compliance

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pet-services|Pet Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Get pet food and treat formulations through the state-by-state registration and labelling regime, substantiate nutritional adequacy claims, and manage recalls when a product fails.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 regulatory specialists, nutritionists, and label reviewers
**Output:** Label reviews and registrations, nutritional adequacy substantiation, claim assessments, recall management
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated state review outcomes and claim precedent
**Clock:** State registration renewals and product launch schedules
**Buyer:** Director of Regulatory Affairs
