# Pet Insurance Underwriting & Claims Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pet-services|Pet Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Price and adjudicate pet health insurance — setting premiums by breed, age, region and coverage, and deciding claim by claim what is covered and what is a pre-existing condition.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 actuaries, data scientists, claims analysts, and veterinary reviewers
**Output:** Premium rating models, claim adjudication rules, loss ratio and reserve analysis, breed and condition cost curves
**Proprietary data:** Millions of veterinary invoices with diagnosis, procedure and cost attached to breed, age and geography — the only structured record of what veterinary care actually costs, since no equivalent of a health claims clearinghouse exists for animals
**Clock:** Annual policy renewals and continuous claim flow
**Buyer:** Chief Actuary / VP of Analytics
