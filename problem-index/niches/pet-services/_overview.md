## Why These Niches

Dog walking and boarding/daycare together represent over 75% of pet services revenue, making them essential high-market-share segments — but they face very different operational challenges (solo mobile workers vs. facility-based businesses). Medical/senior pet sitting and mobile grooming are fast-growing specializations where operators use generic tools poorly adapted to their workflows. Rural pet owners and exotic pet owners are systematically underserved by platforms built for urban dog owners. Client communication and health documentation were selected as automation targets because pet service providers spend 1-3 hours daily on texts, updates, and care logs that follow predictable patterns.

## Why These Pockets

No qualifiers. The pet services value chain above the operator is thin, and everything in it is either small, internal, or attached to products rather than services.

The one genuinely notable pocket is pet insurance, at 47, and it is notable for a reason that has nothing to do with its score. It holds millions of veterinary invoices with diagnosis, procedure and cost attached to breed, age and geography — the only structured record of what animal healthcare actually costs, because there is no clearinghouse, no coding mandate and no claims infrastructure for veterinary medicine. And uniquely among the claims corpora in this entire index, it carries no HIPAA obligation. Animal health records are outside the privacy perimeter that fences the analytical layer of every human healthcare industry swept so far. That combination — a large, clean, outcome-linked claims dataset with no regulatory wall around it — does not exist anywhere else in this sweep. It is used to set premiums for a small insurance line.

The rest fails on scale rather than on shape. Facility liability underwriters hold the only cross-facility record of what precedes a serious incident in group play, which Pass 1 names as the industry's highest-stakes operational risk, inside a programme too small to fund the analysis. The management platforms hold the service-duration data that would fix the scheduling problem Pass 1 describes — a standard groom is forty-five minutes or two and a half hours depending on the dog, and both are booked into the same slot — and use it for dashboards. Certification bodies certify an occupation nobody is required to be certified in.

The largest data business touching this industry, pet category retail measurement, belongs to the products side and is already indexed under food distributors.
