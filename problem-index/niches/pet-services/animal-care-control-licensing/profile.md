# Animal Care & Control Licensing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pet-services|Pet Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Municipal and county animal services that license facilities and animals, investigate bite incidents and cruelty complaints, and maintain the dangerous dog determinations that follow an animal across jurisdictions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 across the larger jurisdictions — inspectors, investigators, and records staff
**Output:** Facility licensing and inspection, bite investigations, dangerous dog determinations, intake and outcome reporting
**Proprietary data:** Bite and incident investigation records, held municipally and rarely comparable across jurisdictions
**Clock:** Annual licensing cycles and complaint intake
**Buyer:** Director of Animal Services
