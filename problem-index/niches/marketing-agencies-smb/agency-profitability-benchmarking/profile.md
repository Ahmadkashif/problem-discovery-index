# Agency Profitability Benchmarking

## What They Do
Benchmarking and coaching firms collect agency financial and utilization data and publish the operating benchmarks agencies compare themselves against — addressing exactly the gap Pass 1 identifies, where agencies are highly equipped on campaign tools and run their own businesses on spreadsheets and gut instinct.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-70 analysts, consultants, and coaches
**Output:** Profitability and utilization benchmarks, pricing and staffing guidance, peer comparison, advisory engagements
**Proprietary data:** Agency financial statements and utilization records contributed over years, in a segment where nothing is public
**Clock:** Quarterly and annual reporting cycles
**Buyer:** Principal / Managing Director
