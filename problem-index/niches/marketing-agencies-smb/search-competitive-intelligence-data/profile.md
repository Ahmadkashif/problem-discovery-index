# Search & Competitive Intelligence Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/marketing-agencies-smb|SMB Marketing Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Semrush, Ahrefs, Similarweb and their peers crawl the web, maintain keyword and backlink indices, run clickstream panels, and sell the estimates of search volume, ranking, traffic, and competitor performance that every agency's SEO and paid search work is planned against. Pass 1 notes agencies are highly equipped on execution tools and run their own businesses on spreadsheets — these are the execution tools.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 data engineers, crawler and index engineers, data scientists, and analysts
**Output:** Keyword volume and difficulty estimates, ranking and visibility tracking, backlink indices, traffic and audience estimates, competitive reports
**Proprietary data:** Continuously refreshed crawl and link indices at web scale, plus panel-based behavioural data and the models that turn both into estimates
**Clock:** Continuous ranking volatility and monthly reporting cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Data Products
