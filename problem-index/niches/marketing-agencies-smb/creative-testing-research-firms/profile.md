# Creative Testing & Advertising Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/marketing-agencies-smb|SMB Marketing Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Advertising research firms pre-test creative against consumer panels and predict in-market performance, producing the score that decides whether an advertisement runs.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 researchers, methodologists, and panel operations staff
**Output:** Creative pre-test scores and diagnostics, normative benchmarks, in-market performance prediction
**Proprietary data:** Decades of tested advertisements with panel responses and normative databases by category
**Clock:** Production and media booking deadlines
**Buyer:** Chief Research Officer / VP of Product
