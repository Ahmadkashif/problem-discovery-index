# Marketing Association Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/marketing-agencies-smb|SMB Marketing Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The advertising and marketing associations publish effectiveness research, maintain measurement standards, and run the industry initiatives that define what counts as an impression or a viewable ad.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 researchers, standards staff, and policy analysts
**Output:** Effectiveness research, measurement standards and guidelines, industry benchmarks, policy analysis
**Proprietary data:** Member-contributed campaign data and long-running effectiveness study archives
**Clock:** Standards revision cycles and annual research calendars
**Buyer:** Chief Research Officer — member funded
