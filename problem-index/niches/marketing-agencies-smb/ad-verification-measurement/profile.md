# Ad Verification & Measurement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/marketing-agencies-smb|SMB Marketing Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Verification providers measure whether digital advertising was actually seen by a human, in a brand-safe context, in the intended geography — and their measurement determines what advertisers pay for and what publishers get credited with.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 data scientists, fraud researchers, measurement engineers, and analysts
**Output:** Viewability and invalid traffic measurement, brand safety and suitability classification, attention metrics, campaign verification reporting
**Proprietary data:** Measurement telemetry across trillions of impressions, fraud detection signatures, and classification models over publisher content
**Clock:** Campaign flights and monthly billing reconciliation
**Buyer:** Chief Product Officer / VP of Data Science
