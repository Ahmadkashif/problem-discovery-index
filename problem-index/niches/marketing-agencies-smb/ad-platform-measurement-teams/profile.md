# Ad Platform Measurement Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/marketing-agencies-smb|SMB Marketing Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The measurement and ads science organizations inside the large advertising platforms build the attribution, conversion modelling, and experimentation tools agencies use to judge whether campaigns worked — on the platform's own inventory.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-3,000 data scientists, economists, and measurement engineers
**Output:** Attribution and conversion modelling, incrementality testing tools, audience and bidding systems, advertiser-facing reporting
**Proprietary data:** Impression, click, and conversion data at population scale, with identity resolution no third party has
**Clock:** Continuous auction operation and quarterly advertiser planning
**Buyer:** VP of Ads Measurement / Director of Ads Science
