# Local Listings & Reputation Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/marketing-agencies-smb|SMB Marketing Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Listings management providers maintain business location data across search engines, maps, and directories, and monitor the reviews that determine whether a local business appears and how it ranks — the core deliverable of most SMB agency retainers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 data engineers, listings analysts, and product staff
**Output:** Listing accuracy and syndication, duplicate suppression, review monitoring and response, local visibility reporting
**Proprietary data:** Business location records reconciled across dozens of publishers, with observed listing change and ranking behaviour
**Clock:** Publisher refresh cycles and continuous review flow
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Chief Product Officer
