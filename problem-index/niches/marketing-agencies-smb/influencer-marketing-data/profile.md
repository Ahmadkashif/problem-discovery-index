# Influencer Marketing Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/marketing-agencies-smb|SMB Marketing Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Influencer platforms maintain profiles of creators — audience size, composition, engagement, brand affinity, and authenticity — and sell the vetting and matching that decides which creators a brand pays.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-200 data scientists, analysts, and platform integration staff
**Output:** Creator audience and engagement analytics, fraud and fake-follower detection, campaign matching and performance reporting
**Proprietary data:** Historical creator performance and audience data; the underlying platform content is observed, not owned
**Clock:** Campaign booking cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Chief Product Officer
