# Marketing Mix Modelling Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/marketing-agencies-smb|SMB Marketing Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Measurement consultancies build the econometric models that attribute sales to marketing spend across channels, producing the analysis that decides how a budget is allocated — increasingly the primary measurement approach as tracking-based attribution has degraded.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-200 econometricians, data scientists, and consultants
**Output:** Mix models, channel contribution and saturation estimates, budget optimization scenarios, incrementality test design
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated model structures and priors across categories; the underlying spend and sales data belongs to clients
**Clock:** Annual and quarterly planning cycles
**Buyer:** Practice leader / Managing Director
