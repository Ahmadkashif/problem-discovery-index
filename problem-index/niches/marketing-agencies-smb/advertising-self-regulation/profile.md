# Advertising Self-Regulation & Enforcement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/marketing-agencies-smb|SMB Marketing Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The advertising self-regulatory bodies and the federal consumer protection agency review advertising claims for substantiation, adjudicate competitor challenges, and enforce against deceptive practice — setting the boundary every agency's copy operates inside.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-200 attorneys, analysts, and investigators
**Output:** Claim substantiation decisions, challenge adjudications, enforcement actions, guidance and policy statements
**Proprietary data:** Case files and substantiation records across decades of decided matters
**Clock:** Case timelines and enforcement schedules
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — industry-funded or federal
