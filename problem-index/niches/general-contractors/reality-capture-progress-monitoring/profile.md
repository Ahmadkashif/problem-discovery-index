# Reality Capture & Progress Monitoring

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/general-contractors|General Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
OpenSpace, Buildots, and the reality capture vendors record the site continuously and compare what was built against the model and schedule, producing the objective progress and deviation record that change order disputes turn on.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 computer vision engineers, construction technologists, and analysts
**Output:** Progress verification, deviation detection, as-built documentation
**Proprietary data:** Time-indexed visual records of construction across hundreds of projects, aligned to schedule and model
**Clock:** Weekly progress cycles and pay application dates
**Buyer:** VP of Product / Chief Technology Officer
