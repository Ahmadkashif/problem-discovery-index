# Builder's Risk Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/general-contractors|General Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Builder's risk and construction liability underwriters price the exposure on a project under construction, assessing contractor experience, project complexity, and site conditions, and analyze the losses that follow.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 underwriters, engineers, and claims analysts
**Output:** Underwriting assessments, risk engineering recommendations, loss analyses
**Proprietary data:** Construction loss experience joined to contractor and project characteristics
**Clock:** Policy inception at project start and renewal cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
