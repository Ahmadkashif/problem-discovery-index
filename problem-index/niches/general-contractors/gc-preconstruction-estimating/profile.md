# General Contractor Preconstruction & Estimating

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/general-contractors|General Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The preconstruction organizations at the large general contractors produce conceptual estimates, guaranteed maximum price proposals, and constructability analysis — often as a fee-based service to owners before construction begins. The Pass 1 analysis calls estimating the industry's most consequential judgment task and notes it takes a decade to develop.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-500 estimators, preconstruction managers, and cost analysts at a large GC
**Output:** Conceptual and detailed estimates, guaranteed maximum price proposals, constructability reviews
**Proprietary data:** Historical bid and actual cost across the firm's own completed projects — the record that would calibrate the estimator's judgment, organized by project rather than by cost driver
**Clock:** Bid dates and owner budget milestones
**Buyer:** VP of Preconstruction / Chief Estimator
