# Construction Research Consortia

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/general-contractors|General Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
University-based construction research institutes run member-funded studies on productivity, project delivery methods, and safety, publishing the benchmarks and best practices that shape how large owners structure projects.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-60 researchers and programme staff
**Output:** Research reports, benchmarking studies, best practice guidance
**Proprietary data:** Member-contributed project performance data across owners and contractors
**Clock:** Research programme and annual conference cycles
**Buyer:** Director — member funded
