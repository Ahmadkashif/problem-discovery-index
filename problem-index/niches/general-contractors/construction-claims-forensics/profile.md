# Construction Claims & Schedule Forensics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/general-contractors|General Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Specialist practices reconstruct what actually happened on a troubled project — delay analysis, disruption and productivity loss quantification, entitlement assessment — and produce the expert reports that decide multi-million dollar claims in arbitration and litigation. The Pass 1 analysis identifies change order management as where general contractor profitability leaks; this is where the leak becomes a dispute.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-400 schedulers, cost engineers, and forensic analysts
**Output:** Delay and disruption analyses, entitlement opinions, expert testimony
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated claim methodology and the record of which analytical approaches tribunals accepted
**Clock:** Arbitration and litigation schedules, plus contractual notice deadlines
**Buyer:** Practice leader / Managing Director
