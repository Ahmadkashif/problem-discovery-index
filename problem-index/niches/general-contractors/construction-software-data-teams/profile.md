# Construction Software Data Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/general-contractors|General Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The data groups at Procore, Autodesk Construction Cloud, and Buildertrend analyze the project execution data flowing through their platforms — schedules, RFIs, submittals, change orders, safety observations — and publish the industry benchmarks contractors plan against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 data scientists and product analysts
**Output:** In-product analytics, industry benchmark reporting, risk indicators
**Proprietary data:** Project execution data across a large share of US commercial construction — RFI and change order patterns that precede schedule and cost failure
**Clock:** Release cycles, internal
**Buyer:** VP of Data Science / Chief Product Officer
