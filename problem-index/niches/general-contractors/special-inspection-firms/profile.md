# Special Inspection Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/general-contractors|General Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Code-required special inspectors observe structural work as it is performed — welding, bolting, concrete placement, fireproofing — and issue the reports the building official requires before the work can be covered or occupancy granted.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-1,000 inspectors and reviewers at the larger firms
**Output:** Inspection reports, discrepancy notices, final compliance letters
**Proprietary data:** Observed defect and correction findings across projects, trades, and contractors
**Clock:** Work proceeds only after inspection; a missed inspection means opening completed work
**Buyer:** VP of Inspection Services / Practice Leader
