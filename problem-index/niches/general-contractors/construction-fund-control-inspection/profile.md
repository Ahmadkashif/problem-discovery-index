# Construction Fund Control & Draw Inspection

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/general-contractors|General Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Fund control firms and construction loan inspectors visit projects each month, verify that the work billed has actually been performed, assess cost to complete, and issue the report the lender releases the draw against. A project stops if the draw stops, and the inspector's judgment about whether a job is on track is the earliest independent signal that it is not.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-800 field inspectors, cost reviewers, and analysts
**Output:** Draw inspection reports, percentage complete determinations, cost-to-complete assessments
**Proprietary data:** Progress, billing, and cost-to-complete observations across thousands of projects, joined to which of those projects ultimately went bad — the only independent longitudinal record of construction project health
**Clock:** Monthly draw cycles; a delayed report stops payment to everyone on the job
**Buyer:** VP of Construction Risk / Chief Operating Officer
