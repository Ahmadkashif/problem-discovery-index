# Construction Safety Enforcement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/general-contractors|General Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Federal and state occupational safety agencies inspect construction sites, issue citations, and investigate fatalities, producing the enforcement record and the standards contractors build their safety programmes against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-2,000 compliance officers, with a few hundred in standards and analysis
**Output:** Citations, fatality investigations, standards and guidance
**Proprietary data:** Inspection, citation, and fatality investigation records across the industry — largely public and rarely analyzed as a body
**Clock:** Inspection cycles and statutory contest periods
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer
