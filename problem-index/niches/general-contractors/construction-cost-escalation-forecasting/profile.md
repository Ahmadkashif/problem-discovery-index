# Construction Cost Escalation Forecasting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/general-contractors|General Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Cost index publishers and the large contractors' research groups forecast construction cost escalation by trade and market — the number owners and contractors use to price work that will be built two years from now.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 economists and cost analysts
**Output:** Cost indices, escalation forecasts, market condition commentary
**Proprietary data:** Long-running index series and, at the contractor-published ones, the firm's own bid data
**Clock:** Quarterly publication and client budget cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Economist / Director of Cost Research
