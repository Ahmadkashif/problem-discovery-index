# Subcontractor Prequalification Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/general-contractors|General Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
TradeTapp, Highwire, Avetta, and the qualification platforms collect and assess subcontractor financial statements, safety records, insurance, and capacity, and issue the qualification determinations general contractors use to decide who is allowed to bid. The Pass 1 analysis names subcontractor reliability as the second major profitability variable and notes that the knowledge is informal and lost when a key employee leaves; this is the layer attempting to institutionalize it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 credit analysts, safety reviewers, and data staff
**Output:** Qualification determinations, financial capacity limits, risk scoring
**Proprietary data:** Financial statements, safety records, and capacity data across tens of thousands of subcontractors who file nothing publicly, joined across many general contractor clients
**Clock:** Bid invitation deadlines and project start dates
**Buyer:** VP of Product / Chief Risk Officer
