# Building Product Specification Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/general-contractors|General Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The architectural specification and technical services groups at building product manufacturers produce the installation details, performance documentation, and specification language that gets their products written into projects.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 specification writers, architectural representatives, and technical staff
**Output:** Specification language, installation details, performance documentation, technical support
**Proprietary data:** Specification tracking across projects and the technical enquiry record showing where installation goes wrong
**Clock:** Design and specification milestones
**Buyer:** Director of Architectural Services / Technical Director
