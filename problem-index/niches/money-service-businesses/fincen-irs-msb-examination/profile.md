# FinCEN & IRS MSB Examination

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/money-service-businesses|Money Service Businesses]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
FinCEN administers MSB registration (26,276 registered MSBs in March 2023) and the BSA rules, receives the SARs and CTRs that MSBs file, and issues geographic targeting orders such as the 2025 Southwest Border order. Federal BSA examination of nonbank MSBs is delegated to the IRS's small business division (estimate), and the CFPB supervises the roughly 28 nonbank larger participants in international money transfer. Federal consumer-finance research is already profiled as [[niches/collections-agencies/cfpb-consumer-research/profile|🔍 Federal Consumer Finance Research]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** No MSB-dedicated research or intelligence unit of 10+ is publicly identified; FinCEN's analysts cover all financial sectors, and IRS BSA examiners are production staff (estimate)
**Output:** Advisories, targeting orders, examination findings and enforcement actions
**Proprietary data:** The national BSA report database — every SAR and CTR filed
**Clock:** Statutory reporting cycles and examination schedules
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer; public procurement
