# GRC Platforms Fitted to Money Transmission Law

**Niche:** [[niches/money-service-businesses/licensing-exam-management/profile|Multi-State Licensing & Examination Management]]
**Industry:** [[industries/money-service-businesses|Money Service Businesses]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** GRC platforms track obligations and evidence for any regulation; none ships with fifty states' money transmission rules, the NMLS call report or the multistate exam request list.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #data-integration #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep every state licence in good standing — net worth, surety bond, permissible investments, quarterly call report, change-of-control notices — with evidence a multistate examination team accepts the first time, and whoever keeps that evidence current every day instead of rebuilding it for each exam takes the account.

## The Problem
Transmitters that buy a GRC platform for their broader compliance programme find that licensing still runs beside it in spreadsheets, because the platform has no money transmission content.

## What Already Exists
AuditBoard, LogicGate, ServiceNow's integrated risk module and similar platforms, analysed under [[industries/grc-compliance-platforms|GRC & Compliance Platforms]]; regulatory intelligence content profiled as [[niches/compliance-consulting/regulatory-content-publishers/profile|🔍 Regulatory Intelligence Publishers]].

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs a money transmission content pack: each state's licence obligations, MTMA status and effective dates, financial tests and filing deadlines; the NMLS call report's four sections as controls with data lineage to the ledger; change-of-control and key-individual workflows with state notice periods; agent-list and FinCEN registration renewal tasks; and exam request templates mapped to evidence.

## Target Customer
Compliance leaders at transmitters already running a GRC platform.

## Impact If Solved
Licensing joins the rest of the compliance programme on one platform and one audit trail, without a separate procurement.
