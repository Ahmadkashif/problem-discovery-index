# Multi-State Licensing & Examination Management

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/money-service-businesses|Money Service Businesses]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep every state licence in good standing — net worth, surety bond, permissible investments, quarterly call report, change-of-control notices — with evidence a multistate examination team accepts the first time, and whoever keeps that evidence current every day instead of rebuilding it for each exam takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$700M a year on licensing staff, licensing counsel and consultants, bond administration and exam preparation across US money transmitters (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~6% of MSB revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — NMLS is the filing portal, but the reconciliations, calculations and evidence behind each filing live in spreadsheets, email and counsel's files
**Target Buyer:** Chief Compliance Officer / Head of Licensing and Regulatory Reporting
**Automation Potential:** High for calculations, reconciliations and filing preparation; judgement remains for change-of-control strategy and exam negotiation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Money transmitters are licensed state by state, with no federal charter to replace the patchwork. The Money Transmission Modernization Act has narrowed the differences — 35 states adopted it in full or in part by August 2026, covering licensees that account for about 99% of reported activity — but each state still sets its own net worth, bond and permissible-investment terms. The NMLS MSB Call Report is due within 45 days of every quarter end, and late filings can block renewal. Multistate examinations coordinated by state regulators ask for the evidence behind all of it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
NMLS, generic GRC platforms ([[industries/grc-compliance-platforms|GRC & Compliance Platforms]]), regulatory intelligence subscriptions, licensing counsel and spreadsheets. Gaps: no daily view of permissible investments against obligations by state; call report numbers re-derived each quarter; exam request lists answered from scratch.
