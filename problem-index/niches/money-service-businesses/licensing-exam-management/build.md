# A Live Licence Position Across Every State

**Niche:** [[niches/money-service-businesses/licensing-exam-management/profile|Multi-State Licensing & Examination Management]]
**Industry:** [[industries/money-service-businesses|Money Service Businesses]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A transmitter learns whether it met fifty states' financial requirements at quarter end, when it must meet them every day.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #time-series-forecasting #data-integration #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep every state licence in good standing — net worth, surety bond, permissible investments, quarterly call report, change-of-control notices — with evidence a multistate examination team accepts the first time, and whoever keeps that evidence current every day instead of rebuilding it for each exam takes the account.

## The Problem
Outstanding transmission obligations rise and fall daily with volume, paydays and payout timing; permissible investments must cover them at all times. Bond requirements scale with each state's volume. Net worth tests differ. The licensing team checks all of this quarterly when it prepares the call report, so a mid-quarter shortfall can go unseen until an examiner finds it.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
State rules were too varied to model before MTMA adoption spread, licensing teams are small, and the data sits in treasury and the general ledger rather than in compliance tools.

## What to Build
A state rule model (MTMA and legacy statutes, read from statutes and bulletins with language models and confirmed by counsel) applied daily to ledger, obligations and volume data: permissible-investment coverage by state, bond adequacy against trailing volume, net worth headroom, and forecasts of when a threshold will be breached. Each quarter it drafts the NMLS call report with reconciliations, and it keeps an evidence library mapped to multistate exam request lists.

## Target Customer
Heads of licensing at transmitters holding 20 or more state licences, including fintechs with money transmitter licences.

## Impact If Built
Shortfalls are fixed the day they open, call reports take hours rather than weeks, and exams start from a maintained record.
