# Permissible Investments Computed the Night Before Quarter End

**Niche:** [[niches/money-service-businesses/licensing-exam-management/profile|Multi-State Licensing & Examination Management]]
**Industry:** [[industries/money-service-businesses|Money Service Businesses]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The schedule that proves customer money is covered is assembled once a quarter by one analyst from treasury exports, and nobody can say whether it held on any other day.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #descriptive-statistics #data-integration #compliance #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep every state licence in good standing — net worth, surety bond, permissible investments, quarterly call report, change-of-control notices — with evidence a multistate examination team accepts the first time, and whoever keeps that evidence current every day instead of rebuilding it for each exam takes the account.

## The Problem
Before each call report a licensing analyst exports outstanding transfers, agent receivables, bank balances and investments, classifies each against the permissible-investment definitions, and builds the schedule. Holiday weekends, when obligations spike, are the days it is most likely to fail and least likely to be checked.

## Why It's Still Broken
The data belongs to treasury, the definitions to compliance, and the calculation to whoever has the spreadsheet. It works at quarter end, so it is left alone.

## What a Fix Looks Like
An automated daily calculation from treasury and ledger feeds, with the classification rules versioned and approved by counsel, a breach alert, and a history examiners can sample for any date.

## Who Feels the Pain
The licensing analyst rebuilding the schedule quarterly, the treasurer surprised by a shortfall, and the compliance officer asked by an examiner about a date nobody checked.

## Impact If Fixed
A quarterly scramble becomes a daily report, and an examiner's question about any date is answered in minutes.
