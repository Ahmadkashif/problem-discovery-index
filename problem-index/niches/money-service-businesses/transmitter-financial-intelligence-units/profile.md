# Large Transmitter Financial Intelligence Units

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/money-service-businesses|Money Service Businesses]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The largest transmitters — Western Union, MoneyGram, Ria, Intermex, Remitly — aggregate tens of thousands of agents or millions of app senders, and run financial intelligence units above their alert-clearing teams. These units research typologies (scam rings, structuring crews, trade-based schemes), map networks of senders, receivers and agents across corridors, liaise with law enforcement, and feed agent-oversight and monitoring rules. Their output goes to FinCEN and law enforcement as SARs and to internal decisions, not to paying clients.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-150 intelligence analysts and investigators per large transmitter, excluding alert clearers (estimate)
**Output:** Typology research, network investigations, SARs and law-enforcement responses, rules and agent-action recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Years of global transfer data, agent histories and investigation outcomes that no outsider sees
**Clock:** The statutory SAR filing window and law-enforcement subpoena deadlines
**Buyer:** Chief Compliance Officer / Head of Financial Intelligence
