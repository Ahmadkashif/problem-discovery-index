# Remittance Price Monitoring Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/money-service-businesses|Money Service Businesses]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The World Bank's Remittance Prices Worldwide database measures the cost of sending $200 across 367 country corridors and publishes the global average (6.49% in Q1 2025) that tracks the G20 and Sustainable Development Goal target of 3%. Think tanks and development research programmes publish corridor studies. The bilateral flow estimates long produced by the World Bank's KNOMAD programme have lapsed. Price collection itself is supplied by a commercial data vendor, profiled in this sweep as Cross-Border Payments Pricing Intelligence.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 dedicated staff (estimate), with data collection contracted out
**Output:** Quarterly price reports, corridor database, policy notes
**Proprietary data:** The public RPW price time series, corridor by corridor
**Clock:** Quarterly publication and G20 monitoring cycles
**Buyer:** Development-finance programme managers; no commercial buyer
