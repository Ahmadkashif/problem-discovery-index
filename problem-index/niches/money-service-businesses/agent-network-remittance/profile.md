# Agent-Network Retail Remittance

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/money-service-businesses|Money Service Businesses]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell a complicit or careless agent from a merely busy one before a regulator, prosecutor or sponsor bank does, and whoever tells them apart earliest takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.5B a year spent on agent oversight staff, field audits and agent-risk tooling at US agent-based transmitters (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~55% of US-originated remittance volume still starts at an agent counter (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — transfers are captured digitally at the terminal, but agent oversight runs on field-visit forms, case tools and spreadsheets that do not join up
**Target Buyer:** Chief Compliance Officer / Head of Agent Oversight
**Automation Potential:** High for ranking and targeting field work; low for suspension and termination decisions, which stay with investigators

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Western Union, MoneyGram, Ria, Intermex and dozens of Latin American and South Asian corridor operators sell mostly through independent stores — about 360,000 locations transacted with Western Union alone in the past year. The transmitter is legally responsible for each agent's compliance but sees it only through transaction logs, visits and complaints. Western Union's 2017 $586M resolution, in which 26 of its agents were convicted and prosecutors found its own guidelines would have triggered action against more than 2,000 agents, defines the stakes. No other part of the industry carries this vicarious liability at this scale.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Proprietary agent terminals and monitoring at the large transmitters; MSB monitoring vendors such as Alessa; case management tools; contracted field auditors. The gaps: field-visit findings live in forms nobody models; investigator reasoning is not recorded; sweep returns sit with treasury; and there is no consistent, graded record of which signals predicted a bad agent.
