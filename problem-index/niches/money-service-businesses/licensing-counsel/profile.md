# Money Transmitter Licensing Counsel

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/money-service-businesses|Money Service Businesses]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Financial services regulatory practices at law firms prepare and maintain money transmitter licence applications in up to 49 states, advise on whether a product is money transmission at all, run change-of-control filings when a transmitter is sold, and represent clients in multistate examinations and enforcement. Fintechs entering payments, acquirers of transmitters (Western Union's ~$500M agreement to buy Intermex needed state approvals) and existing licensees pay by the hour or by the application package. State-by-state requirement content sold by subscription is a different pocket, already indexed as [[niches/compliance-consulting/regulatory-content-publishers/profile|🔍 Regulatory Intelligence Publishers]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-40 lawyers, paralegals and licensing specialists per practice (estimate)
**Output:** Licence applications and renewals, regulatory opinions on the money transmission definition, change-of-control filings, exam and enforcement responses
**Proprietary data:** Each firm's archive of state regulators' positions, application questions and negotiated outcomes across many clients
**Clock:** NMLS annual renewal, quarterly call reports, state change-of-control notice periods and MTMA effective dates — the clients' deadlines, which the practice files against
**Buyer:** Practice group leader, financial services regulatory
