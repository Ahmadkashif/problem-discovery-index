# The Account Closure Letter With No Reason

**Niche:** [[niches/money-service-businesses/corridor-specialist-msbs/profile|Small Corridor-Specialist MSBs]]
**Industry:** [[industries/money-service-businesses|Money Service Businesses]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A bank gives a small transmitter a few weeks to move its accounts and no explanation, and the owner cannot tell whether the cause is a fixable control gap or a policy decision about the whole corridor.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #descriptive-statistics #compliance #workflow-orchestration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to evidence its compliance programme corridor by corridor in a form a bank's MSB desk will accept and keep accepting, and whoever evidences it most convincingly takes the account.

## The Problem
Closure notices rarely give reasons, and banks cannot disclose whether a SAR played a part. The owner guesses, rushes to find another bank, and repeats the same weaknesses — or fixes things that were never the problem.

## Why It's Still Broken
Banks have no obligation to explain, and the MSB has no structured record of what the bank asked for, flagged or questioned during the relationship.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Keep every bank request, review question, finding and response in one log, map them to programme areas, and track the bank's quarterly requests over time, so changes in tone show before a closure and the next bank's review starts with documented remediation.

## Who Feels the Pain
Owners and BSA officers of small transmitters, their staff, and the corridor communities that lose a licensed channel.

## Impact If Fixed
Closures become predictable and contestable rather than sudden, and the next banking relationship starts from evidence instead of from scratch.
