# Third-Party Risk Exchanges Run in Reverse

**Niche:** [[niches/money-service-businesses/corridor-specialist-msbs/profile|Small Corridor-Specialist MSBs]]
**Industry:** [[industries/money-service-businesses|Money Service Businesses]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Vendors answer one standard security questionnaire once and share it with every customer; a small MSB answers a different bank questionnaire for every bank it approaches.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #data-integration #compliance #workflow-orchestration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to evidence its compliance programme corridor by corridor in a form a bank's MSB desk will accept and keep accepting, and whoever evidences it most convincingly takes the account.

## The Problem
When a bank account is threatened, the MSB's owner fills in long due-diligence questionnaires for several banks at once, each phrased differently, with attachments assembled by hand.

## What Already Exists
Third-party risk assessment exchanges, where a vendor completes a standard assessment once and shares it with many customers — profiled as [[niches/compliance-consulting/tprm-assessment-exchanges/profile|🔍 Third-Party Risk Assessment Exchanges]] — and language-model questionnaire tools used by software vendors.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs an MSB-specific standard: BSA programme elements, FinCEN registration and agent lists, state licence status, corridor and receiver-country risk, monitoring and screening coverage statistics, independent review results, and SAR-confidentiality-safe metrics; mapping of each bank's questionnaire onto the standard answers; and refresh on each quarterly call report.

## Target Customer
Small transmitters and check cashers seeking or defending bank accounts.

## Impact If Solved
Questionnaires that take an owner weeks take days, and answers stay consistent across banks.
