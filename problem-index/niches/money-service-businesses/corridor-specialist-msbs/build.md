# A Corridor Risk Case a Bank Can Underwrite

**Niche:** [[niches/money-service-businesses/corridor-specialist-msbs/profile|Small Corridor-Specialist MSBs]]
**Industry:** [[industries/money-service-businesses|Money Service Businesses]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Banks exit small MSBs because assessing them costs more than they earn; nothing gives a bank a cheap, standard, evidence-backed view of one small transmitter's corridor risk.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #gradient-boosting #descriptive-statistics #evaluation-metrics #compliance #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to evidence its compliance programme corridor by corridor in a form a bank's MSB desk will accept and keep accepting, and whoever evidences it most convincingly takes the account.

## The Problem
A bank's MSB desk must understand each MSB customer's corridors, agents, monitoring and screening before it can justify the relationship to its own examiners. For a small transmitter the work is bespoke, the fee income small, and the answer is often to exit. The transmitter usually cannot show its risk in the form the bank needs: corridor-level volumes, receiver concentration, alert and SAR rates, screening coverage, review findings and remediation.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Small MSBs are a fragmented, low-budget market, and banks build their own questionnaires. SAR confidentiality limits what can be shared, so the package has to be built from statistics and controls rather than case details.

## What to Build
A maintained risk case generated from the MSB's own transaction, monitoring, screening and review records: corridor risk metrics against peer and public benchmarks, control evidence, trends, and remediation status, in a standard format banks can ingest and compare across MSB customers, refreshed quarterly.

## Target Customer
Owners and BSA officers of small transmitters, and secondarily bank MSB desks, which could subsidise it to lower their own review cost.

## Impact If Built
The bank's cost of keeping a small MSB falls, the MSB's account becomes defensible, and a corridor community keeps a trusted, licensed channel instead of drifting to informal ones.
