# Small Corridor-Specialist MSBs

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/money-service-businesses|Money Service Businesses]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to evidence its compliance programme corridor by corridor in a form a bank's MSB desk will accept and keep accepting, and whoever evidences it most convincingly takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M a year on compliance staff, independent reviews, banking fees and send-platform licences across small US transmitters serving one or a few diaspora corridors (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of US-originated remittance volume (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low to Medium — a white-label send platform and one monitoring tool, with the compliance programme in documents and spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** Owner / BSA Officer (often the same person or two)
**Automation Potential:** Medium — evidence assembly and monitoring statistics automate; the bank relationship does not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
These are the transmitters serving one community's corridor — US to Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal, Somalia, Ethiopia, Haiti or a single Central American country — often founded by members of that diaspora and trusted for payout reach the large brands lack. The US–Pakistan corridor alone carried about $3.72B of Pakistan's record FY2025 inflows, and incentive changes on the receiving side (Pakistan ended its bank transfer-charge reimbursement scheme in July 2026) move their pricing. Treasury's 2023 De-risking Strategy named small and medium MSBs among the customers banks most often drop, with profitability the main driver. Losing one bank account can stop the business within weeks.

## Current Tools & Gaps
White-label send platforms, MSB monitoring tools such as Alessa, sanctions screening data, outsourced independent reviews, and consultants. Gaps: no standard way to show a bank corridor-level risk and control evidence; bank questionnaires answered from scratch; every vendor priced for larger firms.
