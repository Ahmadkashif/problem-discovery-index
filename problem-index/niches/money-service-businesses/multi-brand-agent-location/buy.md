# Small-Business Bookkeeping Fitted to Trust Funds

**Niche:** [[niches/money-service-businesses/multi-brand-agent-location/profile|The Multi-Brand Agent Location]]
**Industry:** [[industries/money-service-businesses|Money Service Businesses]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Bookkeeping software treats money-transfer cash as store revenue; it is the transmitter's money held in trust, and treating it as revenue is how agents get into trouble.
**Tags:** #feature-engineering #descriptive-statistics #data-integration #worker-facing #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep the agent's single bank account solvent across every brand's ACH sweep, so the store never has a returned debit, and whoever makes its sweeps most predictable for the agent takes the account.

## The Problem
Store owners run QuickBooks or a point-of-sale ledger. Money-transfer collections and payouts flow through the same register as groceries and phone cards, and the commission is the only part that is the store's income.

## What Already Exists
QuickBooks, Xero and point-of-sale accounting for small retailers, with bank feeds and categorisation.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: transmitter liabilities as trust balances per brand, separate from revenue; daily imports of each brand's terminal settlement report; reconciliation of sweeps against collections; commission income recognised correctly; money order inventory tracked per issuer; and alerts when trust balances are being spent on store expenses.

## Target Customer
Owners of agent stores carrying several transmitter brands, reached through transmitters' agent programmes or small-business accountants serving immigrant-owned retail.

## Impact If Solved
Owners see what they owe each transmitter at a glance, returned sweeps fall, and the accountant stops overstating store income.
