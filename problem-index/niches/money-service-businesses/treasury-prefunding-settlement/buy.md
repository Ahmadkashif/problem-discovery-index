# Treasury Management Systems Fitted to Prefunding

**Niche:** [[niches/money-service-businesses/treasury-prefunding-settlement/profile|Treasury, Prefunding & Settlement Reconciliation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/money-service-businesses|Money Service Businesses]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Treasury systems manage a corporation's bank accounts and FX exposures; a transmitter's exposures are hundreds of prefunded partner balances that move with consumer demand.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to prefund each payout partner and currency just enough each morning — no failed payouts, no cash idle abroad — and whoever forecasts payout demand by partner-day most accurately takes the account.

## The Problem
Transmitters run a treasury system for bank accounts and a separate spreadsheet world for partner prefunding, agent sweeps and settlement.

## What Already Exists
Kyriba, GTreasury and similar treasury management systems with cash positioning, bank connectivity, payments and FX exposure management.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: payout partners modelled as accounts with prefunding, cut-offs and confirmation files; agents modelled as receivables collected by sweep, with returns as exceptions; consumer-demand forecasts by corridor rather than corporate cash forecasts; outstanding transmission obligations computed for the licensing team; and links to FX pricing so treasury cost is visible in corridor margins.

## Target Customer
Treasurers at mid-sized and large transmitters already licensing a treasury system.

## Impact If Solved
One system shows the whole cash position, from agent drawers to partner balances abroad, replacing the spreadsheets between them.
