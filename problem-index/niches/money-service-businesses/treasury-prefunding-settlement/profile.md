# Treasury, Prefunding & Settlement Reconciliation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/money-service-businesses|Money Service Businesses]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to prefund each payout partner and currency just enough each morning — no failed payouts, no cash idle abroad — and whoever forecasts payout demand by partner-day most accurately takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$500M a year on treasury and settlement staff, banking and FX services, and reconciliation tooling at US transmitters (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~4% of MSB revenue, governing most of the industry's working capital (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — bank and partner files arrive electronically; forecasting and matching run in spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** Treasurer / CFO
**Automation Potential:** High — forecasting, matching and exception routing are mechanical; FX hedging decisions stay with treasury

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A transmitter pays out before it is paid. Payout partners abroad — banks, cash-pickup networks, mobile wallets — are prefunded in local currency, while agents' collections arrive days later by ACH sweep and card funding settles on its own cycle. Every corridor has its own paydays, holidays and cut-offs. Too little prefunding stops payouts and breaks the delivery promise; too much traps cash abroad in currencies that can devalue. The same desk reconciles thousands of agent sweeps, returns and partner confirmations every day, and its records feed the permissible-investment calculation that state licences require.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Corporate treasury systems (Kyriba, GTreasury), bank portals, partner settlement files, ERP and spreadsheets. Gaps: prefunding set by rule of thumb per partner; returned sweeps found days late; partner confirmations matched by hand.
