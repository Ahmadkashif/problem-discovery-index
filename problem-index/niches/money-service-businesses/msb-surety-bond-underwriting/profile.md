# Money Transmitter Surety Bond Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/money-service-businesses|Money Service Businesses]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Nearly every state requires a licensed transmitter to post a surety bond sized to its volume in that state. Surety carriers underwrite these bonds from the transmitter's financial statements and compliance history, and pay claims when a failed transmitter leaves consumers unpaid. Customs bonds, a comparable line, are logged under Customs Brokers as [[niches/customs-brokers/customs-bond-surety-underwriting/profile|🔍 Customs Bond Surety Underwriting]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 underwriters dedicated to money transmitter bonds per carrier (estimate); the line sits inside commercial surety
**Output:** Bond approvals, pricing and collateral requirements
**Proprietary data:** Applicants' financial statements and claims history
**Clock:** State licence renewals and bond adequacy reviews
**Buyer:** Head of commercial surety
