# Bank MSB Due-Diligence Desks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/money-service-businesses|Money Service Businesses]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The few banks willing to hold MSB accounts run desks that assess each MSB customer's licences, BSA programme, corridors and agents at onboarding and periodically, and monitor the account. Treasury's 2023 De-risking Strategy found profitability the main reason banks exit small MSBs; the desk's cost is part of that calculation. Bankers' banks serving community banks are profiled as [[niches/community-banks/bankers-banks-correspondent-analytics/profile|🔍 Bankers' Banks & Correspondent Analytics]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** Due-diligence analysts are production staff; research capacity dedicated to MSB risk is under 10 per bank (estimate)
**Output:** Customer risk ratings, enhanced due-diligence files, retain-or-exit decisions
**Proprietary data:** MSB customers' account activity and due-diligence files
**Clock:** The bank's own exam cycle and periodic review schedule
**Buyer:** Head of financial institutions or MSB banking
