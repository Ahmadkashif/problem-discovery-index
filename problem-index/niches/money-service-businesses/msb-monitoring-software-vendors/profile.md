# MSB Monitoring Software Content Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/money-service-businesses|Money Service Businesses]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors selling transaction monitoring, screening and case management to MSBs — Alessa markets an MSB-specific product with CTR and SAR population; Unit21 and Hummingbird sell monitoring and case tools across fintechs and MSBs — employ financial crime specialists who write detection scenarios, typology content and tuning guidance for customers. The software licence is the invoice; the content team supports it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-40 financial crime and typology specialists per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Detection scenarios, typology libraries, tuning recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Rule libraries and anonymised performance observations; the transaction and case data itself belongs to each customer
**Clock:** Customers' SAR and CTR deadlines, which bind the customer rather than the vendor
**Buyer:** Head of Product / Head of Financial Crime Content
