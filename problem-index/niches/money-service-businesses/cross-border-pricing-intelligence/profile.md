# Cross-Border Payments Pricing Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/money-service-businesses|Money Service Businesses]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms whose product is data about what transmitters charge. FXC Intelligence is the reference: its pricing data covers more than 25,000 corridors and about 700 providers, it sells market sizing, competitor benchmarking and pricing feeds to Western Union, MoneyGram, Remitly, Intermex, Visa and Mastercard, and it supplies data used for the FSB's G20 cross-border payments work and the World Bank's remittance price database. Consumer comparison sites and a few consultancies sit around it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-80 research analysts and data staff (estimate; no headcount published)
**Output:** Daily and historical corridor pricing feeds, competitor benchmarks, market sizing reports
**Proprietary data:** A multi-year, provider-by-corridor record of quoted fees and FX margins that no single transmitter collects
**Clock:** Clients' pricing cycles and earnings dates; no deadline set by a third party governs the vendor's own work
**Buyer:** Head of Research / Chief Data Officer
