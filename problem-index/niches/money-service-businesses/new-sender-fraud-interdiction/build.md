# Scam-Victim Detection From the Sender's Own Behaviour

**Niche:** [[niches/money-service-businesses/new-sender-fraud-interdiction/profile|New-Sender Fraud & Scam Interdiction]]
**Industry:** [[industries/money-service-businesses|Money Service Businesses]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A scam victim passes identity, device and card checks because they are the real customer; the signal is in how the transfer differs from what that customer would normally do.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #transformers #graph-neural-networks #evaluation-metrics #compliance #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to approve a genuine first-time sender's card- or bank-funded transfer in minutes while stopping stolen funds and scam victims' savings before payout, and whoever separates the two most accurately takes the account.

## The Problem
Romance, impersonation, grandparent and online-purchase scams push genuine customers to send to receivers they have no real tie to. The transmitter's fraud stack is built to catch impostors, and a victim is not one. Losses fall on the victim, complaints and regulatory attention fall on the transmitter.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Victims often never report, so labels under-count the problem; reported cases arrive weeks later. Intervening risks insulting genuine senders in a price-sensitive market. And the strongest signals sit on the receiver side, across many senders, which fraud tools organised around the sender do not model.

## What to Build
A model scoring each send for victimisation: first-time corridor or receiver with no prior tie, amount jumps, accelerating frequency, stated purpose mismatches, in-app hesitation patterns, and receivers who collect from many unrelated senders. It selects a typology-specific intervention — a question, a cooling-off delay, a call — and learns from outcomes, using positive-unlabelled methods for unreported cases.

## Target Customer
Heads of fraud and consumer protection at app-first transmitters, and incumbents' digital channels.

## Impact If Built
Fewer customers lose savings to scams, complaint and regulatory exposure falls, and interventions land on the few transfers that warrant them.
