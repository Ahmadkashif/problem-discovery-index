# Agent Field Audit Contractors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/money-service-businesses|Money Service Businesses]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Contractors and mystery-shopping firms that visit agent locations for transmitters: checking ID handling, posted disclosures, recordkeeping and clerk knowledge, and filing a visit report. Transmitters with large networks buy thousands of visits a year (estimate).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Field auditors are production headcount; analysts aggregating findings across visits are under 10 per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Visit reports and checklists, delivered to the transmitter that ordered them
**Proprietary data:** None retained in useful form — each report belongs to the commissioning transmitter
**Clock:** Transmitters' visit schedules and regulator expectations for agent oversight
**Buyer:** Agent oversight manager at the transmitter
