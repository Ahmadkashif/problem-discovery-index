# Remittance & Payments Industry Analysts

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/money-service-businesses|Money Service Businesses]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Sell-side equity analysts covering the listed transmitters (Western Union, Remitly, Euronet) and payments research and advisory firms with cross-border coverage publish forecasts, surveys and competitive reviews for investors and for transmitters' strategy teams.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analysts covering MSBs at any one firm (estimate); remittance is a small slice of broader payments coverage
**Output:** Equity research notes, industry reports and advisory projects
**Proprietary data:** Analyst models and survey panels
**Clock:** Quarterly earnings dates for the listed transmitters
**Buyer:** Director of research
