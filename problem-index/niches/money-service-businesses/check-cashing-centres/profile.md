# Check Cashing & Neighbourhood Financial Service Centres

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/money-service-businesses|Money Service Businesses]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to cash a stranger's payroll, government or insurance check on the spot without eating a returned item, and whoever judges check risk most accurately at the counter takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.5–2B a year in check cashing fees across ~10,000–13,000 outlets (estimate, from older industry association figures)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of US MSB revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — the decision is a teller looking at a check and a customer, sometimes backed by a verification call and a regular-customer file
**Target Buyer:** Owner-operator / regional operations director at multi-store chains
**Automation Potential:** Medium — check image analysis and payor history can be automated; the counter read of a customer stays human

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Check cashers advance cash against a check that will clear days later, for a fee set by state caps in many states. A returned item is a straight loss, and the people presenting checks are disproportionately unbanked or paid by employers who issue paper. The store also sells money orders, bill payments and money transfers as an agent, but check cashing is where its own capital is at risk. Experienced tellers "just know" a payroll check from a local employer that looks wrong, or a customer whose story does not fit the check — a tacit read built over thousands of checks. Some [[industries/pawnshops|Pawnshops]] cash checks too; their collateral lending is outside this niche.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Check-cashing point-of-sale systems with customer files, check verification and guarantee services built for retailers, phone calls to the employer or issuing bank, and the store's own list of known payors. Gaps: returns are not fed back into any model; payor histories live in binders and teller memory; and counterfeit payroll checks are caught by eye.
