# The Payor Verification Call Done From Memory

**Niche:** [[niches/money-service-businesses/check-cashing-centres/profile|Check Cashing & Neighbourhood Financial Service Centres]]
**Industry:** [[industries/money-service-businesses|Money Service Businesses]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Which employers' checks are always good, which ones bounce and who to call to confirm a payroll lives in a binder and in the head of the teller who has been there longest.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #feature-engineering #descriptive-statistics #worker-facing #quick-win #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to cash a stranger's payroll, government or insurance check on the spot without eating a returned item, and whoever judges check risk most accurately at the counter takes the account.

## The Problem
On payday a line of workers from the same construction firm or staffing agency arrives with checks. The teller calls the employer, or does not, depending on what they remember about that employer. A new teller at another branch knows none of it.

## Why It's Still Broken
Point-of-sale systems store customers, not payors. The knowledge is collected informally and never shared across branches, and returns are recorded in accounting without the payor details tellers need.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A shared payor file built automatically from every cashed check: payor name and account, cashing history, returns, typical amounts and pay days, verification contact and the result of past calls, surfaced on the teller's screen when a check from that payor is scanned.

## Who Feels the Pain
Tellers on payday lines, branch managers absorbing returns, and owners losing knowledge every time a senior teller leaves.

## Impact If Fixed
Verification decisions become consistent across branches within weeks, using data the store already captures at the counter.
