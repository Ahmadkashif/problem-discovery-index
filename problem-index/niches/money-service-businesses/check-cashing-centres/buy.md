# Retail Check Verification Adapted to Walk-In Cashing

**Niche:** [[niches/money-service-businesses/check-cashing-centres/profile|Check Cashing & Neighbourhood Financial Service Centres]]
**Industry:** [[industries/money-service-businesses|Money Service Businesses]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Retailers verify personal checks written by their customers; a check casher is presented with third-party payroll, government and insurance checks, and the verification question is different.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to cash a stranger's payroll, government or insurance check on the spot without eating a returned item, and whoever judges check risk most accurately at the counter takes the account.

## The Problem
Check cashers use whatever verification is available — a guarantee service, a bank's automated line, the payor's phone number — and fall back on the teller's judgement.

## What Already Exists
Retail check verification and guarantee services such as TeleCheck and Certegy, which score the check writer's account history at the point of sale. These services are consumer reporting agencies; their own work belongs to the queued credit bureaus industry.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: verification of the payor (employer, insurer, government agency) rather than the person presenting the check; payroll-check counterfeit signals from images; payee identity tied to the store's customer file; state check-cashing fee caps and record rules built into the decision; and returns reported back into the score at payor level.

## Target Customer
Check-cashing chains and independent stores with meaningful returned-item losses.

## Impact If Solved
A verification answer fitted to third-party checks reduces reliance on phone calls and memory for the checks that carry the most loss.
