# MSB Trade Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/money-service-businesses|Money Service Businesses]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Financial Service Centers of America (FiSCA) represents check cashers and neighbourhood financial service providers; the Money Services Business Association and similar groups represent transmitters on licensing, de-risking and federal rules such as the 2026 remittance tax. They run conferences, training and advocacy, and occasionally commission surveys.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research or policy staff per association (estimate)
**Output:** Advocacy positions, comment letters, training, occasional member surveys
**Proprietary data:** Member surveys where run
**Clock:** Rulemaking comment periods and legislative sessions
**Buyer:** Executive director
