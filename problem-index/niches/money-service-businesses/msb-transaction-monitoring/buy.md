# Bank Case Management Tuned to MSB Thresholds

**Niche:** [[niches/money-service-businesses/msb-transaction-monitoring/profile|MSB Transaction Monitoring & SAR Operations]]
**Industry:** [[industries/money-service-businesses|Money Service Businesses]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** AML case management platforms streamline alert-to-SAR work for banks; MSB cases centre on resolved persons, agents and corridors that the bank data model does not have.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #seq2seq #evaluation-metrics #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to resolve each sender across agents, brands and name spellings into one person before a structuring alert is worked, and whoever resolves senders most accurately takes the account.

## The Problem
MSB analysts adapt bank-oriented case tools, entering agents and receivers as free text because the data model expects accounts and customers.

## What Already Exists
Hummingbird for investigations and SAR filing, Unit21 combining monitoring and cases, and similar platforms with SAR narrative assistance.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: resolved person, agent and receiver as first-class case entities; MSB thresholds and recordkeeping rules; agent-involvement flags that route a case to agent oversight; corridor and payout-location context; GTO and CTR aggregation across agents; SAR narrative templates in FinCEN's structure for MSB typologies; and statistics for independent reviews and bank due diligence that respect SAR confidentiality.

## Target Customer
BSA officers at mid-sized MSBs replacing spreadsheets or adapting a bank tool.

## Impact If Solved
A case tool that fits MSB work removes re-keying and makes agent-involved cases visible to oversight teams.
