# Cross-Border Payout Network Aggregators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/money-service-businesses|Money Service Businesses]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Wholesale networks that connect sending transmitters to payout banks, cash-pickup agents and mobile wallets in receiving countries through one integration — Thunes, TerraPay and the card networks' cross-border services are representative. They earn per-transaction fees and FX margin, and their corridor and partner analytics support sales and treasury.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-30 corridor, data and partner analytics staff per network (estimate)
**Output:** Internal corridor analytics, partner performance reports and client volume dashboards
**Proprietary data:** Payout volumes, success rates and timing across many sending transmitters and receiving partners
**Clock:** Settlement cut-offs and prefunding windows — operational, not a research deadline
**Buyer:** Chief Commercial Officer / Head of Data
