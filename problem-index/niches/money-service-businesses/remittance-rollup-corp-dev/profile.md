# Remittance Rollup Corporate Development

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/money-service-businesses|Money Service Businesses]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Transmitters and private-equity owners that buy corridor specialists and agent networks: Western Union's agreement to acquire Intermex for about $500M in 2025, Intermex's own purchases of smaller transmitters, and the take-private of MoneyGram by a private-equity owner (estimate: 2023). Corporate development teams value targets' corridors, agent networks and licence portfolios and plan integration.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 2-8 corporate development staff per acquirer (estimate)
**Output:** Target screens, valuation models, integration plans
**Proprietary data:** Deal files and targets' data rooms
**Clock:** Deal timetables and regulatory approvals
**Buyer:** Head of corporate development
