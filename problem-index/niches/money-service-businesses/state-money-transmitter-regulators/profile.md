# State Money Transmitter Regulators & Multistate Exams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/money-service-businesses|Money Service Businesses]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State banking and financial regulators license money transmitters and check cashers, collect the quarterly NMLS MSB Call Report, and examine licensees, often jointly through multistate programmes coordinated with the Conference of State Bank Supervisors, which also maintains the Money Transmission Modernization Act model law. Their licensing and inspection work belongs to the queued licensing agencies industry.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Examiners and licensing staff are production; analytics on MSB call report data is under 10 per state and in the coordinating body (estimate)
**Output:** Licences, examination reports, consent orders, model-law guidance
**Proprietary data:** Call report data and examination findings across licensees
**Clock:** Statutory examination cycles and licence renewals
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer
