# Multiemployer Plan Actuaries & Zone Certification

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pension-funds|Pension Funds]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Consulting actuaries who value Taft-Hartley pension plans for their joint boards of trustees. Segal, Milliman, Cheiron, Horizon Actuarial and a set of smaller firms are typical. Each year they certify the plan's funding zone (green, endangered, critical, critical and declining), project solvency, design rehabilitation and funding improvement plans, calculate employers' withdrawal liability, and prepare the projections behind Special Financial Assistance applications. The trustees pay from plan assets. The public-plan equivalent is logged under Municipal Services as [[niches/municipal-services/public-pension-actuarial/profile|🔍 Public Pension Actuarial Services]], and this pocket is scored consistently with it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 actuaries and analysts in the multiemployer practice of each major firm (estimate)
**Output:** Annual zone certifications, actuarial valuations, solvency projections, rehabilitation plan schedules, withdrawal liability calculations, SFA projections
**Proprietary data:** Contribution, hours and demographic experience across many plans in the same trades, plus decades of assumption-setting and projection history
**Clock:** The zone certification must reach the Treasury and the plan sponsor by the 90th day of each plan year (26 U.S.C. 432(b)(3)), a statutory deadline on the actuary itself
**Buyer:** Multiemployer practice leader

**Kill switches:** **data isn't theirs** — member census, hours and contribution data belong to each plan and are held under the engagement, the same constraint logged on the public-plan actuarial pocket. Withdrawal liability work is also often commissioned through fund counsel for arbitration and litigation, bringing privilege into the file.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. The only pocket in this sweep with a hard statutory deadline on the insight producer, and an archive of projections joined to what each plan's hours and contributions actually did, but it is built on plan-owned data.
