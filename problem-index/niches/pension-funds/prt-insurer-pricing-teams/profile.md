# Pension Risk Transfer Insurer Pricing Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pension-funds|Pension Funds]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Life insurers that buy pension liabilities through buy-outs and buy-ins, such as Prudential, MetLife, Athene and Legal & General's US business. Their pricing actuaries and underwriters review each plan's census, benefit specifications and data quality, set mortality and expense assumptions, and bid. In 2025 they wrote $31.3B of buy-outs across 683 contracts and $17.5B of buy-ins across 17 (LIMRA). The insurer's balance sheet belongs to [[industries/life-insurance-carriers|Life Insurance Carriers]], where the same transaction-only data pattern is logged for [[niches/life-insurance-carriers/inforce-block-acquirers/profile|🔍 In-Force Block Acquirers & Closed-Block Consolidators]]. This pocket logs the pricing teams as they appear from the pension side, as the buyers of plans' liabilities.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 pricing actuaries, underwriters and data analysts per active insurer (estimate)
**Output:** Bids, underwriting of plan data, mortality and expense assumptions per transaction
**Proprietary data:** Experience on the pension annuitants each insurer has taken on, joined to the census data of every plan it has priced
**Clock:** Bid deadlines in each transaction, set by the sponsor's consultant
**Buyer:** Head of PRT pricing

**Kill switches:** **data isn't theirs** — census data for plans the insurer bids on but does not win belongs to the sponsor and is supplied under confidentiality for that transaction only.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. The insight is priced into a premium rather than invoiced, and the richest data (every plan bid on) is the sponsors'.
