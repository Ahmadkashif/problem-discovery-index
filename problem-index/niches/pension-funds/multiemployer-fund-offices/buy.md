# Workers' Compensation Premium Audit Analytics for Payroll Compliance

**Niche:** [[niches/pension-funds/multiemployer-fund-offices/profile|Multiemployer Pension Fund Offices]]
**Industry:** [[industries/pension-funds|Pension Funds]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Workers' compensation insurers audit employer payrolls to verify what premiums were owed; multiemployer funds audit the same employers to verify what contributions were owed, with far less tooling.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to post every contributing employer's monthly remittance to the right participant's hours and pension credit, and whoever credits every reported hour correctly with the fewest delinquencies and credited-service disputes takes the account.

## The Problem
Payroll compliance audits compare an employer's payroll records with its remittances to find unreported hours. Funds audit on rotation, and auditors spend their time on employers that turn out to be clean.

## What Already Exists
Workers' compensation carriers run premium audit programmes with risk-based audit selection, payroll classification checks and audit-findings analytics. These are mature tools for verifying that employer-reported payroll matches the employer's books.

## The Customization Gap
The fund version needs: hours by member rather than payroll by class code; collective bargaining agreement rates by trade and period; signals from the remittance stream (drops, new hires never reported, hours inconsistent with known projects); links to delinquency collection under ERISA through fund counsel; and audit findings that update members' credited hours.

## Target Customer
Fund administrators and trustees of trades pension funds; the payroll audit firms they engage.

## Impact If Solved
Audit hours go to the employers most likely to be under-reporting, and the fund collects contributions that would otherwise be missed until an employer goes out of business.
