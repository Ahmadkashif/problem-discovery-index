# PBGC & EBSA Plan Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pension-funds|Pension Funds]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The federal bodies that insure and oversee private defined benefit plans. The Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation runs the single-employer and multiemployer insurance programmes, reviews distress and standard terminations, and administers Special Financial Assistance: about $70.0B approved to 108 plans covering about 1,245,000 people by January 2025. The Department of Labor's Employee Benefits Security Administration collects Form 5500 filings, publishes the Private Pension Plan Bulletin and enforces ERISA. Both are funded by premiums or appropriations, not by clients. This pocket is the pension slice; EBSA's health and welfare enforcement is profiled as [[niches/self-insured-benefit-pools/ebsa-health-plan-enforcement/profile|🔍 EBSA Health Plan & MEWA Enforcement]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~100-300 actuaries and financial analysts at PBGC across its actuarial, multiemployer programme and policy research staff (estimate); EBSA's pension-side research and actuarial analytics ~20-60 (estimate)
**Output:** SFA application reviews, insurance programme projections, Form 5500 statistics, enforcement targeting
**Proprietary data:** Premium filings, SFA applications and plan actuarial data submitted under statute, plus the full Form 5500 history
**Clock:** Statutory review periods for SFA applications (recalled as 120 days) and annual filing and projection cycles
**Buyer:** Chief actuary / office director
