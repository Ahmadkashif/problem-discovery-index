# State Pension Oversight Commissions

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pension-funds|Pension Funds]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State bodies that oversee public pension plans without running them, such as the Texas Pension Review Board, Pennsylvania's Public Employee Retirement Commission and similar councils and divisions (recalled examples). They collect actuarial and financial reports from state and local plans, review funding soundness, write actuarial notes on pension bills, and in some states administer state aid to local plans.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 3-15 staff, of whom a handful do actuarial and policy review (estimate)
**Output:** Actuarial notes on legislation, plan compliance reviews, funding soundness reports
**Proprietary data:** Filings from every overseen plan
**Clock:** Legislative sessions and statutory filing dates
**Buyer:** Executive director
