# A Compliance Calendar That Runs the Plan for a Part-Time Board

**Niche:** [[niches/pension-funds/small-local-public-plans/profile|Small Local Public Plans]]
**Industry:** [[industries/pension-funds|Pension Funds]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A five-trustee police pension board meets quarterly and carries the same statutory obligations as a statewide system, with nobody whose job is to track them.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #compliance #automation #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to run a small board's whole compliance calendar, from census data for the actuary to GASB 67/68 schedules, state filings and investment monitoring, without the plan hiring staff, and whoever keeps the most small plans off the state regulator's late-filing and non-compliance lists takes the account.

## The Problem
The valuation needs member data by a date. The employer's auditor needs GASB 68 schedules before the town's financial statements close. The state wants its annual report. The investment policy requires a quarterly review. Trustees need their training hours. Each obligation is known to one of the outside firms, and none of them owns the whole calendar. A missed filing in a state with oversight can trigger penalties or withheld state aid (estimate; varies by state).

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Each outside firm sells its own service and tracks its own deadlines. The plans are too small to justify custom software, and the state-by-state rules make a national product look fragmented.

## What to Build
A plan-level obligations engine for each state's local-plan regime. It sets dates from the plan's fiscal year and the employer's audit calendar, collects documents from each outside firm, prepares the state filing and GASB schedules from the actuary's and auditor's outputs, tracks trustee education, and drafts the board agenda for each meeting. It is sold through the actuarial and administration firms that already serve these plans.

## Target Customer
Board chairs and part-time administrators of local police, fire and municipal plans; the plan administration and actuarial firms serving many of them.

## Impact If Built
Filings go in on time without the plan hiring anyone, and trustees spend their meetings on fiduciary decisions rather than on chasing documents.
