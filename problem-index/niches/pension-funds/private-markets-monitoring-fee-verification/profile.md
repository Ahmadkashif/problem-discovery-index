# Private Markets Monitoring & Fee Verification

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pension-funds|Pension Funds]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn every GP statement, notice and partnership agreement into a record of NAV, cash flows, fees and carry that reconciles to the custodian and to the LPA's own terms, and whoever closes each quarter with the fewest unexplained differences before the board's review takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600M a year of monitoring platforms, private markets accounting services and internal reconciliation staff at US pension plans (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~4% of pension funds' non-investment-management operating spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — documents are captured and extracted; terms are not executed against them
**Target Buyer:** Head of Investment Operations / Private Markets Operations Manager
**Automation Potential:** High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The work is quarterly and document-driven. Capital account statements, call and distribution notices, and ILPA fee templates come from every GP in different layouts, and the plan must book them, reconcile them to custodian cash, and roll them into performance. Two outside clocks bind it. The board's quarterly review needs the numbers. And public plans must publish fees and carry: California's Government Code 7514.7 requires an annual public report, and ILPA's updated template applies from Q1 2026. The winner is whoever reconciles most completely, not whoever decides best.

## Current Tools & Gaps
MSCI's Burgiss, FactSet's Cobalt, Chronograph, Nasdaq's Solovis and Colmore extract and store GP data. Custodians offer private markets accounting. Consultants compile annual fee reports for smaller plans. The gaps: fees and carry are taken as reported rather than recomputed from the LPA and side letter; reconciliations to custodian cash are done in spreadsheets; and differences are not classified, so recurring GP errors are not seen as patterns.
