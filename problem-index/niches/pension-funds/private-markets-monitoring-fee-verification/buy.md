# Recovery Audit Logic Applied to Fund Fees

**Niche:** [[niches/pension-funds/private-markets-monitoring-fee-verification/profile|Private Markets Monitoring & Fee Verification]]
**Industry:** [[industries/pension-funds|Pension Funds]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Retailers and hospitals have long hired recovery auditors to recompute what suppliers billed against contract terms; pension plans pay private fund managers billions with no equivalent check.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn every GP statement, notice and partnership agreement into a record of NAV, cash flows, fees and carry that reconciles to the custodian and to the LPA's own terms, and whoever closes each quarter with the fewest unexplained differences before the board's review takes the account.

## The Problem
GP fee and expense charges are deducted inside the fund, not invoiced, so they bypass the plan's accounts-payable controls. Errors are found only by the plan's own analysts or by the GP's auditor, whose client is the fund.

## What Already Exists
Recovery audit and contract-compliance software in retail, healthcare and procurement recomputes supplier charges against contract terms: pricing, rebates, allowances. It runs exception analytics and manages the claim back to the supplier.

## The Customization Gap
Fund fees need: terms that change over the fund's life (investment period, step-downs, extensions); offsets computed on portfolio-company fees the plan never sees directly; expense categories defined by the LPA and ILPA template; waterfall logic for carry with clawback; and a claim process that runs through the LPAC and the GP relationship rather than a supplier dispute desk. Public plans also need the output in the shape of their statutory disclosure.

## Target Customer
Public pension investment operations and audit committees; internal audit at large plans.

## Impact If Solved
A proven recovery-audit method gives plans an independent fee check without building one from scratch, and puts private fund fees under the same discipline as every other contract the plan pays.
