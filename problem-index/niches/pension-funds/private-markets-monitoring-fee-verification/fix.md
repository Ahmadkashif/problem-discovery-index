# The Annual Fee Report Built From What GPs Chose to Send

**Niche:** [[niches/pension-funds/private-markets-monitoring-fee-verification/profile|Private Markets Monitoring & Fee Verification]]
**Industry:** [[industries/pension-funds|Pension Funds]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The public fee and carry report a California plan presents each year is compiled from GP submissions in whatever format and completeness each GP chose.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #compliance #data-integration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn every GP statement, notice and partnership agreement into a record of NAV, cash flows, fees and carry that reconciles to the custodian and to the LPA's own terms, and whoever closes each quarter with the fewest unexplained differences before the board's review takes the account.

## The Problem
Government Code 7514.7 requires the report, but each GP answers its own way. Some send ILPA templates, some send a line in a letter, and older funds that predate the 2017 cutoff are covered only by "reasonable efforts". The analyst who compiles the report chases missing items for weeks before the board meeting and footnotes what never arrived.

## Why It's Still Broken
The obligation falls on the plan, but the data sits with the GP. Templates are voluntary. Each year's report is assembled from scratch because nothing tracks, fund by fund, which fields were supplied, by whom and on what basis.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A standing fee register per fund, updated every quarter from statements and templates, with each field marked as supplied, derived or missing and linked to its source document. Chasers to GPs are drafted automatically for missing fields. The annual report becomes an export from the register, and the footnotes write themselves.

## Who Feels the Pain
The private markets operations analyst who owns the report; the CIO presenting it in public; journalists and stakeholders who read it.

## Impact If Fixed
Weeks of annual chasing are replaced by quarterly upkeep, and the public report shows where each number came from.
