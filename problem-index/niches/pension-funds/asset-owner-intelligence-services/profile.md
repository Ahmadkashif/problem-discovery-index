# Asset Owner Board & Search Intelligence Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pension-funds|Pension Funds]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms that read every public pension board packet, set of minutes and RFP and sell what they find to asset managers' sales and consultant-relations teams. That includes allocation changes, manager hires and terminations, private markets commitments, consultant changes and open searches with due dates. Dakota Marketplace says it tracks 6,400+ board minutes and counted 22 searches across 19 allocators, at least $16B of mandates, in August 2026. With Intelligence, Pensions & Investments' research products, FIN News and Fundfire work the same seam. The private-markets slice of the same public records is collected by [[niches/financial-data-vendors/private-markets-research-teams/profile|🔍 Private Markets Research & Collection Teams]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 researchers and reporters per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Searchable allocator profiles, board-meeting summaries, search and RFP alerts, commitment histories, contact data
**Proprietary data:** A compiled, verified history of each plan's allocations, searches, hires and commitments, linked to consultants and contacts; the underlying documents are public
**Clock:** Board meeting dates and RFP response deadlines set by the pension funds bind the vendor's clients, not the vendor; scored as a soft clock, consistent with the public-records collection in [[niches/financial-data-vendors/private-markets-research-teams/profile|🔍 Private Markets Research & Collection Teams]] (Q4 3)
**Buyer:** Head of Research / Editor-in-Chief
