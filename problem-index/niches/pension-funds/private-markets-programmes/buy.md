# Relationship CRM Pointed at the Re-Up Calendar

**Niche:** [[niches/pension-funds/private-markets-programmes/profile|Private Markets Programmes at Public Plans]]
**Industry:** [[industries/pension-funds|Pension Funds]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Private markets CRMs were built for GPs tracking deals and LPs; an LP tracking hundreds of GPs through a fundraising cycle needs the same tool turned around.

Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
A public plan's private markets team meets GPs constantly. There are annual meetings, LPAC calls, pitch meetings for new funds and reference calls. The notes go into email, OneNote or a shared drive. When a GP announces its next fund, the officer must reconstruct the relationship from memory and folders.

## What Already Exists
Relationship CRMs built for private capital, such as Intapp DealCloud and Salesforce-based platforms, track contacts, meetings, pipelines and documents for GPs managing deals and investor relations.

## The Customization Gap
The LP version needs a different spine. Each GP is a fund family with successor funds. Each fund has a re-up window tied to the GP's fundraising timeline and to the plan's board calendar. Meetings must be tagged to the concern categories the plan grades (team, size, marks, terms, governance). And the relationship record must link to the monitoring platform's cash flows and to the board approvals, which are public records. Public records exposure also changes what can be written and how.

## Target Customer
Private markets teams at public plans and the consultants who support them, especially plans of 5–20 investment staff that cannot build their own system.

## Impact If Solved
The officer walks into a re-up with every prior interaction, concern and outcome for that GP on one page. A relationship survives a staff departure instead of starting over.
