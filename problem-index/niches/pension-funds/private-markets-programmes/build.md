# A Re-Up Ledger Graded Against Vintage Peers

**Niche:** [[niches/pension-funds/private-markets-programmes/profile|Private Markets Programmes at Public Plans]]
**Industry:** [[industries/pension-funds|Pension Funds]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A public plan decides dozens of re-ups a year and has never measured whether the signals its officers worried about predicted anything.

**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor is fighting to own the plan's private markets record, but it splits into two contests with different winners: reconciling what GPs report each quarter to custodian records and partnership terms before the board's review, and forecasting calls and distributions well enough to set next year's commitments without breaching allocation ranges in a drawdown. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Each re-up recommendation is written for a board meeting and a consultant's co-signature. It records the GP's track record, the strategy and the terms. It rarely records the concerns the officer weighed and discounted, such as a departing partner, a fund twice the size of the last, or marks that did not follow public comparables. Ten years later the fund's net multiple and public-market equivalent are known, but no one joins them back to those concerns. The plan cannot say whether its officers' worries were informative, so it cannot teach the next officer which ones to take seriously.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Monitoring vendors are paid to report performance, not to grade the plan's decisions. Consultants co-sign the recommendations and have no incentive to score them. Inside the plan, accountability for a re-up is shared between staff, consultant and board. Fund outcomes take a decade, longer than most officers' tenure.

## What to Build
A ledger attached to every commitment and re-up, drafted from the officer's notes, GP letters and statements and confirmed in a minute. It records the thesis, the concerns by category, the signals observed, the conviction and the alternatives declined. Each record is graded against vintage- and strategy-adjusted outcomes, including the later funds of GPs the plan declined, which public pension disclosures make observable. Over time it learns which recorded concerns predicted underperformance. It then surfaces them, with the track record, at the next fundraise.

## Target Customer
Heads of private markets and CIOs at state systems and large county plans with 100+ active fund relationships; private markets consultants who want to evidence their recommendations.

## Impact If Built
The plan can show its board which signals carried information, can keep a departed officer's read of each GP, and can redirect even a few re-ups a cycle away from deteriorating managers. On a multi-billion programme that is worth tens of basis points.
