# Missing Participant & Uncashed Check Search Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pension-funds|Pension Funds]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Firms that locate deferred participants and beneficiaries who have lost contact with a plan, confirm deaths, and manage outreach before lump-sum windows, plan terminations and buy-outs. They are paid per search or per project by sponsors and administrators. Death matching for continuing payees overlaps with [[niches/funeral-homes/death-verification-data-providers/profile|🔍 Death Verification Data Providers]], which is logged under Funeral Homes.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Search and locate staff are production work; analytics teams are small (estimate)
**Output:** Located addresses, death confirmations, outreach results
**Proprietary data:** Licensed public-records and credit-header data; participant files belong to the plans
**Clock:** Transaction and lump-sum window dates set by the sponsor
**Buyer:** Head of Operations
