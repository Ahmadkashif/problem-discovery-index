# Plan Sponsor Performance Universes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pension-funds|Pension Funds]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Peer universes of total-fund returns for public and corporate plans, used in every quarterly board report to rank a plan against "public funds over $1B" or similar. Examples are Wilshire's Trust Universe Comparison Service, MSCI's InvestorForce universes, custodian peer groups and consultants' own public fund databases. They are built from custodian or consultant client data and sold to consultants and plans.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 data and methodology staff per universe (estimate)
**Output:** Quarterly peer percentile rankings, universe statistics by plan type and size
**Proprietary data:** Total-fund and asset-class returns contributed through custodians and consultants
**Clock:** Quarterly universe close, ahead of client board meetings
**Buyer:** Head of Performance Products
