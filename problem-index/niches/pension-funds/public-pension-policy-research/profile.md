# Public Pension Associations & Policy Research Groups

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pension-funds|Pension Funds]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Associations of public retirement systems and their administrators, such as NASRA, NCPERS and NCTR, and the policy groups that publish public pension data and studies, such as Boston College's Center for Retirement Research (Public Plans Data), the Equable Institute, the Pew Charitable Trusts and the Reason Foundation's Pension Integrity Project (recalled examples). They publish surveys, funding analyses and policy briefs, funded by dues, grants or donors.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 dedicated pension researchers at most organisations (estimate)
**Output:** Member surveys, public plan databases, funding and policy reports
**Proprietary data:** Survey responses and compiled plan financial data, most of it from public reports
**Clock:** Annual survey and publication cycles, self-set
**Buyer:** Executive director / research director
