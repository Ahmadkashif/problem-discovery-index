# Bank Account-Takeover Models Adapted to Pension Payroll

**Niche:** [[niches/pension-funds/payee-verification-overpayment/profile|Payee Verification & Overpayment Prevention]]
**Industry:** [[industries/pension-funds|Pension Funds]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Banks have spent years modelling account takeover; a pension payroll is an account that pays out monthly and is defended by a clerk with a phone.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stop payments to dead or impersonated payees before each monthly payroll runs, and whoever stops the most improper payments before the cutoff without holding a living retiree's check takes the account.

## The Problem
Fraudsters use stolen identity data to pass portal identity checks or impersonate retirees on paper. Pension systems see the attempt only as a change form.

## What Already Exists
Banks and payment providers run account-takeover and payee-change fraud models (device and session analytics, behavioural biometrics, payee-account risk signals from consortium data) and step-up authentication.

## The Customization Gap
Pensions need: risk signals for paper and fax channels that banks rarely see; an older population for whom step-up authentication by app is a barrier; events specific to pensions (a death report, a beneficiary starting payments, a member abroad); verification steps that fit plan rules (notarisation, callback to verified numbers); and a hold decision timed to the monthly payroll cutoff rather than to a real-time payment.

## Target Customer
Retirement systems' benefits operations and IT security teams; PAS vendors adding fraud controls.

## Impact If Solved
Proven account-takeover analytics protect retirees' payments without forcing every 85-year-old through app-based authentication.
