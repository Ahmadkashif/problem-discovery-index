# Investment Consultant Performance Reporting & Asset-Liability Studies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pension-funds|Pension Funds]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
General investment consultants to pension plans, such as Meketa, NEPC, Callan, Verus, RVK, Wilshire, Aon, Mercer and WTW, produce the quarterly performance report every board reviews. They also run the asset-liability and asset allocation studies that set policy every three to five years, and attend board meetings. This is separate from their manager research, logged under Asset Managers as [[niches/asset-managers/institutional-consultant-manager-research/profile|🔍 Institutional Investment Consultant Manager Research]]. Plans pay a retainer.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 performance analysts and asset-allocation researchers per large consultant (estimate)
**Output:** Quarterly performance and attribution reports, asset-liability studies, policy benchmark recommendations, board education
**Proprietary data:** Many clients' total-fund returns, allocations and policy histories, plus the firm's own study archive
**Clock:** Clients' quarterly board meetings and allocation review cycles (client calendars, not third-party deadlines)
**Buyer:** Head of Consulting / Head of Performance Measurement

**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. A highly repeatable quarterly product on client-owned data. Each consultant also holds every asset allocation it ever recommended next to what each client then earned, a record of its own advice it does not grade.
