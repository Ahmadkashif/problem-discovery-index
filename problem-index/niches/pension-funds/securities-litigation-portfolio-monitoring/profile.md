# Plaintiff Firms' Portfolio Monitoring for Pension Funds

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pension-funds|Pension Funds]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Securities class action law firms, such as Robbins Geller, Labaton and Bernstein Litowitz, offer public and multiemployer pension funds free monitoring of their holdings against securities fraud. They report the losses a fund suffered on stocks with possible claims and invite the fund to serve as lead plaintiff, which is how the firm wins the case. The fee comes later, as a court-awarded share of any settlement. Claims filing after a settlement is a separate service, logged under Trust Companies as [[niches/trust-companies/class-action-claims-recovery/profile|🔍 Securities Class Action Claims Recovery Services]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 analysts, investigators and financial economists per firm working on case evaluation and portfolio monitoring (estimate)
**Output:** Portfolio loss reports, case evaluation memos, lead-plaintiff recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Clients' transaction histories across many funds, plus the firm's case evaluation and outcome archive
**Clock:** Court deadlines; the lead-plaintiff motion window after notice of a filed case (recalled as 60 days under the PSLRA) is hard and third-party
**Buyer:** Managing partner for institutional client services
