# LP Outsourced Private Markets Monitoring Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pension-funds|Pension Funds]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Firms that take over a pension plan's private markets monitoring as a managed service. Their analysts collect the plan's GP documents, reconcile capital account statements and cash flows to the custodian, check fees, and deliver the plan's quarterly monitoring and fee reports. Colmore and the custodians' private markets data and reporting services are typical; some consultants run the same desk for smaller plans. Plans pay a service fee per fund interest or per programme. This pocket is narrowed on purpose. Software built by product teams and licensed to sponsors and LPs, such as Chronograph, FactSet's Cobalt, S&P iLEVEL and Nasdaq's Solovis, belongs to [[niches/private-equity-firms/portfolio-monitoring-software-vendors/profile|🔍 Portfolio Monitoring & Fund Software Vendors]] (38, kill switch) and is not re-profiled here. What is distinct is the deliverable: analyst-produced reconciliation and reporting for one LP, where effort grows with the plan's fund count, rather than a software licence.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-100 reporting and analytics staff per provider (estimate); document collection and data entry are production and not counted
**Output:** Quarterly LP monitoring reports, cash-flow and NAV reconciliations, fee and carry checks, inputs to statutory fee disclosures
**Proprietary data:** Reconciled document-level records for every client LP's fund interests
**Clock:** Quarterly GP reporting and each client's board meetings (client calendars)
**Buyer:** Head of LP services / practice leader

**Kill switches:** **data isn't theirs** — the documents belong to each LP client and are covered by GP confidentiality clauses, and there is no checked evidence that service agreements permit cross-client aggregate use. This is the same client-ownership constraint logged on the software vendors' pocket.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. A labour-linear service whose report is the deliverable, sold by a handful of providers on documents that belong to each client.
