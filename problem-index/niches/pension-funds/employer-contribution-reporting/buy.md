# Enrolment File Validation From Health Carriers

**Niche:** [[niches/pension-funds/employer-contribution-reporting/profile|Employer Contribution Reporting & Pensionable Pay Review]]
**Industry:** [[industries/pension-funds|Pension Funds]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Health carriers validate thousands of employer enrolment files against eligibility rules every month; pension systems receive the same kind of file and check far less.
**Tags:** #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to validate each employer's payroll report against the plan's pensionability rules in the pay period it arrives, and whoever catches the most non-pensionable pay and tier errors before they reach a benefit calculation takes the account.

## The Problem
Employer files arrive in many layouts from many payroll systems, and staff spend their time fixing rejects rather than reviewing content.

## What Already Exists
Health carriers and benefits administrators run EDI enrolment processing (ANSI 834 files) with eligibility rule engines, employer-specific mapping, exception queues and employer-facing error reports.

## The Customization Gap
Pension files need: rules on pay and service, not just enrolment status; tier and bargaining-unit rules that change with legislation; member history across years, since a spike is visible only over time; contribution reconciliation to the cent with interest; and correction workflows that preserve an audit trail a compensation review can rely on decades later. Public employers also report on fiscal-year and school-year calendars that health files ignore.

## Target Customer
Employer services teams at public retirement systems; PAS vendors looking to strengthen their employer portals.

## Impact If Solved
Mature file-processing machinery handles format and mapping, freeing employer reporting staff to review the pay that affects benefits.
