# Tax Preparation Rules Engines Adapted to Benefit Formulas

**Niche:** [[niches/pension-funds/benefit-estimates-final-calculations/profile|Benefit Estimates & Final Calculations]]
**Industry:** [[industries/pension-funds|Pension Funds]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Tax software encodes thousands of changing rules, shows its working line by line and compares scenarios; pension calculation engines often do none of this in a form a member or counselor can read.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #automation #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce an estimate that matches the final benefit and a final benefit that never needs recalculating, and whoever issues final calculations within the plan's service standard with the fewest post-retirement recalculations takes the account.

## The Problem
Counselors explain benefit calculations from screens built for processors, and members compare payment options and retirement dates through repeated estimate requests.

## What Already Exists
Consumer and professional tax preparation engines maintain versioned rule sets, explain each computed line with its rule, run side-by-side scenarios and flag missing inputs.

## The Customization Gap
Pension rules need: versioning by legislation date and member tier rather than tax year; inputs from decades of service and salary history rather than one year's documents; option factors from actuarial tables adopted by the board; scenario comparison across retirement dates and payment options, including survivor benefits; and an explanation layer a counselor can read aloud to a member.

## Target Customer
Member services teams at public systems; PAS vendors' product teams.

## Impact If Solved
Members see why their benefit is what it is and how each choice changes it, and counselors answer option questions in one call rather than three.
