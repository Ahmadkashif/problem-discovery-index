# State Investment Boards & Consolidated Local Pension Pools

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pension-funds|Pension Funds]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Bodies that pool and invest assets for many retirement systems or local plans: state investment boards that manage several statewide systems' assets, and statewide funds created to consolidate the investments of hundreds of local police and fire plans (Illinois is a recalled example). They run the investment office once for many plans and charge participating plans their cost.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 investment research, strategy and risk staff per board (estimate)
**Output:** Asset allocation research, manager and fund selection, risk reporting to participating plans and the board
**Proprietary data:** Pooled portfolio, manager and private fund histories across all participating plans
**Clock:** Board meeting calendars and fiscal year-end reporting
**Buyer:** Chief Investment Officer

**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. The aggregator of this industry is itself an investment office: its research is spent on returns, not invoiced. It is the natural first buyer of a commitment ledger, not an insight business.
