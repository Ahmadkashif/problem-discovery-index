# Pension Longevity Analytics & Pooled Mortality Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pension-funds|Pension Funds]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms that pool member-level mortality experience from many pension plans and sell plan-specific longevity assumptions to plans, actuaries, insurers and asset managers. Club Vita built its first US model from 800,000+ retirees in 108 large plans, using ZIP+4, pension amount, collar and health status, and reported liability effects of up to 6% against standard Society of Actuaries tables. Actuarial consultants also maintain internal experience databases. The public alternative is the SOA's mortality tables, which every plan can use for free.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 longevity analysts and data scientists (estimate)
**Output:** Plan-specific mortality curves, longevity reports for valuations and buy-out pricing, research on longevity trends
**Proprietary data:** Pooled member-level mortality experience across many plans, a data set no single plan or competitor can reproduce
**Clock:** Client valuation and transaction dates; no third-party deadline on the provider
**Buyer:** Head of Longevity Research / Chief Actuary
