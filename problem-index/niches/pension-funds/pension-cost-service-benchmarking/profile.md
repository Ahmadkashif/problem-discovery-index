# Pension Cost & Service Benchmarking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pension-funds|Pension Funds]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Benchmark pension funds' investment costs and returns, and their benefits administration cost and member service, against peer funds that contribute the same detailed survey. CEM Benchmarking is the main provider. Its 2025 administration cycle covered 81 systems (34 US, 21 Canadian, 23 UK, 3 elsewhere), and a 2024 investment report compared North Carolina with 283 funds. Boards use the reports to judge cost-effectiveness: Washington DRS at $98 per member against a $146 peer average, North Carolina at $33 against $130 alongside a service score of 75 against a peer median of 82. Retirement systems pay an annual participation fee.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~30-100 analysts and researchers (estimate)
**Output:** Annual investment and administration benchmarking reports per fund, peer comparisons, best-practice research
**Proprietary data:** Decades of participant-submitted, line-item cost, return, staffing and service data across several hundred funds, submitted for exactly this comparison
**Clock:** Each participant's fiscal year and board reporting cycle; no third-party deadline
**Buyer:** CEO / Head of Research
