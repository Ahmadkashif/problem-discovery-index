# Consultant Capital Market Assumptions Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pension-funds|Pension Funds]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Investment consultants and OCIO providers publish annual long-horizon capital market assumptions: expected returns, volatilities and correlations by asset class. These drive every asset-liability study, policy portfolio and, indirectly, the assumed rates of return that set public employer contributions. Each consultant's research group produces them. Large asset managers publish their own. Plans receive them as part of the consulting retainer. The retail planning equivalent is logged under Wealth Management RIAs as [[niches/wealth-management-rias/financial-planning-assumptions/profile|🔍 Financial Planning Software & Capital Market Assumptions]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 researchers per consulting firm (estimate)
**Output:** Annual capital market assumptions, methodology papers, inputs to client asset-liability studies
**Proprietary data:** The firm's own archive of past assumptions, which can be compared with realised returns over 10 years or more; inputs are public market data
**Clock:** Annual publication timed to the start of the year and client study calendars
**Buyer:** Head of Capital Markets Research
