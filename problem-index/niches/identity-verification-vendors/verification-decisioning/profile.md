# Verification Decisioning

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/identity-verification-vendors|Identity Verification Vendors]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to confirm that a person is who they claim to be in seconds — and the contest splits cleanly enough that it is not terminal.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.2B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~37% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** High — strong matching, weak accounting
**Target Buyer:** Data science and product leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — two separate technical races

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is the product: decide in seconds whether the person presenting an identity is that person. It is the largest contest in the category and it is two contests that happen to be sold together. One is a computer vision problem — is this document genuine, is this a live face, is it the same face. The other is a data problem — does this identity exist in the record, does this address history hold together, is this phone number attached to this person. The leading vendors in each are largely different companies.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Document classification, forgery detection, liveness, face matching, database resolution and composite scoring. The gaps: error distribution across populations unpublished; false rejects unmeasured; thresholds set without a cost model for either error; and the two capabilities blended into one score whose components the customer cannot separate.
