# Document & Biometric Matching

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/identity-verification-vendors|Identity Verification Vendors]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell a genuine document from a good forgery and a live person from a presentation attack, on a photograph taken by whoever happens to be holding the phone — and whoever does it best under bad capture conditions wins the populations everyone else rejects.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.2B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~20% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** High — an active vision contest
**Target Buyer:** Computer vision and product leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — a modelling race with a moving adversary

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This contest is computer vision. Classify the document, read it, detect tampering and synthetic generation, confirm the face is live rather than a photograph or a replay, and match it to the document portrait — all from images captured on unknown devices, in unknown lighting, by people of varying dexterity. The adversary improves continuously as generative tools get better. It shares almost nothing with resolving an identity against database records.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Document classifiers, optical character recognition, tamper and security feature detection, liveness models and face matchers. The gaps: capture quality treated as the applicant's problem; error rates by device and condition unpublished; synthetic document and deepfake detection lagging generation; and retry behaviour not used to improve capture.
