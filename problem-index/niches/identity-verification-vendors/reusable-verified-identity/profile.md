# Reusable Verified Identity

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/identity-verification-vendors|Identity Verification Vendors]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let a person verify once and prove it everywhere without the verifier becoming a tracker — and whoever makes that acceptable to relying parties and to the person removes the repetition the whole category is built on.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$250M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~4% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — verification repeated everywhere
**Target Buyer:** Network and product leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — credential issuance and presentation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The same person verifies their identity at a bank, a payment app, a marketplace, a rental platform and a government service, each time from scratch, each time with the same document, each time with the same probability of failing. Verification is a repeated cost for everyone and a repeated risk of exclusion for the person. Standards for verifiable credentials exist. What does not exist is a model that relying parties will accept and that does not turn the issuer into a surveillance point.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Per-relationship verification, some vendor-side reuse within one customer, and early verifiable credential pilots. The gaps: no cross-relying-party reuse at scale; liability for a reused verification unallocated; freshness and revocation unaddressed; privacy model not settled; and the person has no portable proof of their own.
