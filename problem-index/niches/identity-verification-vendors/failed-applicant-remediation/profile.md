# Failed-Applicant Remediation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/identity-verification-vendors|Identity Verification Vendors]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give a person who could not be verified a documented route to prove who they are — and whoever builds that route stops the category's silent failure from being permanent.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$550M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — no route exists
**Target Buyer:** Product and support leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — alternative evidence and escalation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A person fails verification. They are shown a generic message, the application ends, and they disappear. There is usually no appeal, no explanation, no alternative evidence path and no human to reach — and passing is a precondition for a bank account, a payment app, a loan or a benefit. This is the point at which a technical error becomes a person's exclusion, and it is the part of the product nobody has built.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A rejection message, occasional manual review, and a support queue where the vendor operates one. The gaps: no alternative evidence path; no appeal; failure reason not communicated; abandonment unmeasured; and no handoff back to the institution that can decide.
