# The Solutions Engineer

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/identity-verification-vendors|Identity Verification Vendors]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let a solutions engineer explain why one specific person could not open an account — and whoever makes the decision articulable changes what the escalation conversation can be.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — logs and guesswork
**Target Buyer:** Solutions leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — explanation and case reconstruction

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Solutions engineers field escalations about specific people who could not open an account, from customers who are sometimes hearing about it from the applicant themselves. They must explain a decision made by a composite score across several checks, in a pipeline they did not design, about an image they may not be able to see, for a person whose data they must handle carefully. They are the only human interface between the model's decision and the person it affected, and they have logs.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Transaction logs, score breakdowns if exposed, internal escalation paths and customer calls. The gaps: no case reconstruction tool; scores not explainable in human terms; images and evidence access-restricted and hard to retrieve; recurring escalation patterns not catalogued; and no route to change the outcome.
