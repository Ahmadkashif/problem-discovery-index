# Database Identity Resolution

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/identity-verification-vendors|Identity Verification Vendors]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to confirm that a claimed identity exists and belongs to this person from records that were never built for the purpose — and whoever resolves thin-file and frequently-moving populations best serves the people everyone else cannot see.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.0B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~17% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — coverage-bound
**Target Buyer:** Data and product leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — record linkage over assembled sources

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This contest is data. Confirm that a name, date of birth, address and identifier correspond to a real person, and that the applicant is that person, using credit header files, telecom records, address histories, public records and utility data. It rests on coverage, source quality and record linkage rather than on vision, and it fails for a specific and predictable population: people who move frequently, share housing, have recently arrived, are young, or have never held credit. Those failures have nothing in common with a blurred photograph.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Credit header and public record matching, phone and address verification, velocity and consistency checks, and composite scores. The gaps: thin-file coverage poor and unmeasured; failure reasons not distinguished; alternative sources underused; error rates by mobility and tenure unpublished; and no route for a person whose record does not exist.
