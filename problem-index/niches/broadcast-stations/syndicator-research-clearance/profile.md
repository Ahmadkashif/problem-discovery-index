# Syndicator Research & Station Clearance Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/broadcast-stations|Broadcast TV & Radio Stations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Distributors of first-run and off-network syndicated programmes run research departments that analyse ratings market by market to sell shows to stations: which slots a show wins, what it delivers as a lead-in, and how it compares with competing product. Their output supports licence sales and renewals and is given to stations rather than sold.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-20 research staff per large syndicator (estimate)
**Output:** Market-by-market performance presentations, clearance and upgrade cases, renewal arguments
**Proprietary data:** Clearance, licence-fee and time-period history for their own shows across markets
**Clock:** Selling seasons and station renewal windows
**Buyer:** SVP of research at the distributor
