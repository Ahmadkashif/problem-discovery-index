# Broadcast Engineering Consulting Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/broadcast-stations|Broadcast TV & Radio Stations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Consulting engineering firms prepare the technical exhibits behind FCC applications: coverage and interference studies, AM directional proofs, RF exposure analysis, and channel and facility planning, including for ATSC 3.0 conversions. Stations pay per project.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 3-15 engineers per firm (estimate)
**Output:** FCC engineering exhibits and studies
**Proprietary data:** Project files; the underlying FCC and terrain data is public
**Clock:** FCC filing windows and construction deadlines
**Buyer:** Principal engineer
