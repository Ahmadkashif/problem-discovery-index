# Local Direct Sales

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/broadcast-stations|Broadcast TV & Radio Stations]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (broadcast CRM vendors, attribution and call-tracking services, and the groups' own sales research teams) is fighting to show a local business owner, in the owner's own terms (calls, leads, booked jobs, showroom visits), that the station's schedule produced customers before the annual renewal, and whoever proves that most convincingly takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.2B a year in sales tooling, prospecting data, results reporting and digital fulfilment bought by stations for local selling (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2–3% of station revenue spent on the function's tools and services (estimate); the revenue it governs is roughly half of core advertising (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — CRM and proposal tools are standard, but results reporting to the advertiser is a hand-built recap or none at all
**Target Buyer:** Local sales manager or GSM; group VP of local sales
**Automation Potential:** Medium — reporting and prospecting automate well; the relationship does not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Local direct is the business a station's own account executives write with car dealers, personal-injury firms, home-services companies, furniture stores and hospitals, without an agency in between. The buyer is an owner or marketing manager who does not read ratings and compares the station's invoice with a Google Ads dashboard that reports clicks every morning. National spot is sold by reps and priced on ratings, analysed under [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]]. Local direct is renewed on whether the owner believes the schedule worked, and the station holds the airtime log that could prove it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Salesforce or broadcast CRMs, Nielsen ratings for packaging, Vivvix and MediaRadar for prospecting, and Miller Kaplan's pooled revenue for share. Stations also sell digital and streaming add-ons fulfilled by in-house or white-label teams. The gaps: no standard way to join the station's minute-by-minute airtime log to the advertiser's calls and web traffic, results recaps assembled by the AE in slides, and no early warning that an account is about to cancel.
