# CRM Built for Seats, Adapted for Dayparts

**Niche:** [[niches/broadcast-stations/local-direct-sales/profile|Local Direct Sales]]
**Industry:** [[industries/broadcast-stations|Broadcast TV & Radio Stations]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Salesforce and HubSpot run any sales pipeline; a station's pipeline is priced in dayparts and ratings, bundled with streaming and digital, and renewed on results the CRM never sees.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #gradient-boosting #survival-analysis #feature-engineering #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (broadcast CRM vendors, attribution and call-tracking services, and the groups' own sales research teams) is fighting to show a local business owner, in the owner's own terms (calls, leads, booked jobs, showroom visits), that the station's schedule produced customers before the annual renewal, and whoever proves that most convincingly takes the account.

## The Problem
Local AEs carry books of ~50–150 active accounts (estimate) and prospect against occurrence data. Their CRM records meetings and opportunities. The schedule, its delivery, the digital add-on's results and the renewal date sit elsewhere, in the traffic system, a fulfilment partner's portal and a spreadsheet.

## What Already Exists
Salesforce and HubSpot, broadcast-oriented CRMs sold alongside traffic systems, and prospecting data from Vivvix and MediaRadar.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs order and schedule sync from WideOrbit, Imagine or Marketron so every account shows what aired and what was billed. It needs the results service's output on the account record, and a churn-risk score from schedule changes, payment behaviour, results trend and competitor spending seen in occurrence data. It also needs proposal templates that combine linear dayparts with streaming and digital packages, and political-window awareness so local accounts are not preempted without warning.

## Target Customer
Local sales managers and group sales operations leaders standardising CRM across many stations.

## Impact If Solved
AEs see which accounts are at risk before the cancellation call. Sales managers coach on results rather than activity counts, and the group finally has one view of local direct across markets.
