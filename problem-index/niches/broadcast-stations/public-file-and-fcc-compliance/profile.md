# Public File & FCC Compliance Reporting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/broadcast-stations|Broadcast TV & Radio Stations]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (broadcast law firms, compliance software vendors and state associations' inspection programmes) is fighting to keep every licence's public file complete and on time (issues/programs lists, political file, EEO and children's reports, carriage elections) with content that survives a petition to deny at renewal, and whoever does that for the most licences with the least station staff time takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$250M a year in compliance staff time, legal advisories, alternative inspection programmes and filing tools across ~17,800 full-power radio, full-power TV and Class A licences (estimate; licence count summed from the FCC's 30 June 2026 totals)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.5% of station revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium-Low — the public file is online, but the contents are compiled by hand from rundowns, orders and HR records
**Target Buyer:** Group compliance officer or general counsel; station GM at independents
**Automation Potential:** High for compilation, timing and gap checks; human sign-off stays

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The FCC-hosted online public inspection file is a station's continuous licence evidence. Quarterly issues/programs lists are due by the 10th of January, April, July and October, with a $10,000 base fine for late or missing lists. Political file items must be uploaded promptly through every election window. Annual EEO reports, children's programming reports for TV and the triennial carriage election (due 1 October 2026) sit beside them. The work is repetitive, deadline-driven and the same across hundreds of licences. Candidate rate quoting is sold by reps and analysed under [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]]. This niche is the station's own record of what it aired and disclosed.

## Current Tools & Gaps
The FCC's online public file system, law-firm advisories and checklists, state broadcaster associations' alternative inspection programmes, traffic-system political exports, and spreadsheets. Gaps: no tagging of programming by community issue as it airs, no gap check before deadlines, and no renewal-ready history per licence.
