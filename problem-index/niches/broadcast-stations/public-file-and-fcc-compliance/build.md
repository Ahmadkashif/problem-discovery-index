# A Renewal-Ready Record for Every Licence

**Niche:** [[niches/broadcast-stations/public-file-and-fcc-compliance/profile|Public File & FCC Compliance Reporting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/broadcast-stations|Broadcast TV & Radio Stations]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A licence is renewed on eight years of public-file evidence that was compiled quarter by quarter in a hurry and never checked as a whole until a petition arrives.
**Tags:** #bert #large-language-models #transformers #k-means-clustering #evaluation-metrics #compliance #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (broadcast law firms, compliance software vendors and state associations' inspection programmes) is fighting to keep every licence's public file complete and on time (issues/programs lists, political file, EEO and children's reports, carriage elections) with content that survives a petition to deny at renewal, and whoever does that for the most licences with the least station staff time takes the account.

## The Problem
At renewal, a station certifies that its public file was complete throughout the term. Gaps, late uploads and thin issues lists surface when the FCC or a challenger looks, and each becomes a fine or a delay.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Compliance has been treated as a legal service and a calendar, not a data problem. The evidence sits in the newsroom system, the traffic system, HR records and the FCC's file, and no vendor joins them.

## What to Build
A continuous record per licence. Newscasts and public-affairs segments are tagged by community issue as they air, using captions and rundowns. The quarterly list is drafted from the tags, political orders are matched to public-file uploads with timestamps, and EEO, children's and election filings are tracked. A renewal view shows every quarter's completeness and flags weak issues coverage early enough to fix it in programming, not in paperwork.

## Target Customer
Group compliance officers and general counsel at multi-station TV and radio groups; broadcast law firms serving independents.

## Impact If Built
Renewals filed on a clean, documented record. Compliance staff time per licence falls sharply (estimate), and challenges based on thin lists or late files are rarer.
