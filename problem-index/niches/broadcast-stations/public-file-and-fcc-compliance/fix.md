# Political File Uploads That Arrive Late

**Niche:** [[niches/broadcast-stations/public-file-and-fcc-compliance/profile|Public File & FCC Compliance Reporting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/broadcast-stations|Broadcast TV & Radio Stations]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** In the busiest weeks of an election, political orders are uploaded to the public file when someone gets to them, and the timestamps tell the story.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #descriptive-statistics #compliance #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (broadcast law firms, compliance software vendors and state associations' inspection programmes) is fighting to keep every licence's public file complete and on time (issues/programs lists, political file, EEO and children's reports, carriage elections) with content that survives a petition to deny at renewal, and whoever does that for the most licences with the least station staff time takes the account.

## The Problem
AdImpact projects $5.6B of 2026 political spending on broadcast TV. Each order, change and disclosure must reach the station's political file promptly, but the uploads are done by traffic and sales assistants already overloaded with the same orders.

## Why It's Still Broken
The traffic system holds the order and the FCC file holds the upload, and nothing checks one against the other in real time.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A daily match of political orders and revisions in traffic against political-file uploads, flagging any order without a matching upload and drafting the document for one-click filing.

## Who Feels the Pain
Traffic and sales assistants in election season, compliance officers, and GMs facing complaints.

## Impact If Fixed
Political-file timeliness becomes a checked process rather than a hope, for the cost of a daily report.
