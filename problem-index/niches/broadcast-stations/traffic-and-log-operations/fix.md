# Makegoods Discovered at Month-End

**Niche:** [[niches/broadcast-stations/traffic-and-log-operations/profile|Traffic, Log Building & Continuity]]
**Industry:** [[industries/broadcast-stations|Broadcast TV & Radio Stations]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A spot that will air outside its ordered window is visible on the log the day before, and is discovered when the affidavit fails to match the invoice weeks later.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #feature-engineering #quick-win #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (traffic-system vendors, order-exchange and document-extraction services, and hubbed traffic centres) is fighting to turn every order, copy instruction and late change into an on-air log that runs without a missed spot, wrong copy or separation conflict, and whoever drives discrepancies toward zero with the fewest traffic staff takes the account.

## The Problem
Discrepancies (wrong window, wrong copy, missed spot) are reconciled at billing time, when the only remedy is a credit or a makegood in a later, often sold-out week.

## Why It's Still Broken
The traffic system checks the log for scheduling rules, not against each order's specific terms, and nobody has time to check every spot before air.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A pre-air check of tomorrow's log against every order's window, copy and separation terms, listing likely discrepancies for the traffic director to fix that evening.

## Who Feels the Pain
Traffic directors, sales managers giving away inventory as makegoods, and billing staff reconciling disputes.

## Impact If Fixed
Problems are fixed when they cost a log edit rather than a credit, using data the station already has.
