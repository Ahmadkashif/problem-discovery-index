# Traffic, Log Building & Continuity

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/broadcast-stations|Broadcast TV & Radio Stations]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (traffic-system vendors, order-exchange and document-extraction services, and hubbed traffic centres) is fighting to turn every order, copy instruction and late change into an on-air log that runs without a missed spot, wrong copy or separation conflict, and whoever drives discrepancies toward zero with the fewest traffic staff takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$800M a year in traffic and continuity staff, traffic software and hubbed traffic services across TV and radio (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1.5% of station revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — the log is digital, but copy instructions, conflict resolution and late changes are handled by people reading email
**Target Buyer:** Group VP of traffic or revenue operations; station traffic director
**Automation Potential:** High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Traffic starts once an order is in the station's system and ends when the log has aired and the affidavit matches it. In between: copy matched to orders from rotation instructions, competitive separations resolved, preemptions and makegoods placed by the sales manager's priorities, and late changes applied without breaking anything. The movement of avails, orders and revisions between agency and station systems, and the post-buy settlement of national flights, belong to the rep side and are analysed under [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]]. Lumine Group now owns both WideOrbit and Imagine Communications, the leading TV traffic and ad-management vendors, so the core system is concentrated.

## Current Tools & Gaps
WideOrbit, Imagine's Landmark Sales, Marketron and RCS traffic modules; order-exchange feeds; Ad-ID and ISCI registries; automation and playout systems. Gaps: copy instructions read by hand, conflicts resolved by moving spots one at a time, and discrepancies found at month-end rather than before air.
