# Contract Lifecycle Management for Retrans and Affiliation Terms

**Niche:** [[niches/broadcast-stations/retransmission-and-affiliation/profile|Retransmission & Network Affiliation Economics]]
**Industry:** [[industries/broadcast-stations|Broadcast TV & Radio Stations]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Contract lifecycle platforms already track obligations and renewal dates; retrans and affiliation agreements need per-subscriber rate schedules, most-favoured-nation clauses and FCC election dates modelled as data, not stored as PDFs.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #feature-engineering #compliance #data-integration #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor in this niche (distribution advisers, contract audit firms, and contract and royalty software vendors) is fighting to raise the station's net distribution revenue per subscriber after reverse compensation, but that splits into two contests with different winners: estimating what a blackout would cost each side in negotiations with each distributor, and testing distributors' self-reported subscriber counts after the agreement is signed. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Key terms such as rates by year, tiers counted, MFN triggers, out-of-market rights and digital carriage are scattered across agreements and amendments. They are looked up by hand when a dispute or renewal arrives.

## What Already Exists
Contract lifecycle management platforms such as Icertis, Ironclad and DocuSign CLM, with clause extraction and obligation tracking.

## The Customization Gap
The platform needs a retrans and affiliation clause model: per-subscriber rates by year and tier, minimum guarantees, MFN definitions and comparators, carriage-area and out-of-market terms, vMVPD territorial rules, and reverse-comp formulas that reference the same counts. It needs the FCC triennial election date and 30-day deletion-notice rules on the calendar. And extracted terms have to feed the margin model and the subscriber audit directly.

## Target Customer
Distribution and legal teams at station groups.

## Impact If Solved
Terms become queryable across the portfolio, MFN exposure is visible before a concession is made, and the two sub-niche workflows run from one source of contract truth.
