# Broadcaster Association Policy & Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/broadcast-stations|Broadcast TV & Radio Stations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The National Association of Broadcasters represents stations before the FCC and Congress. It produces economic, spectrum and technical research behind its filings on ownership limits, retransmission, ATSC 3.0 and the AM Radio for Every Vehicle Act. The Television Bureau of Advertising and the Radio Advertising Bureau, which research selling, are profiled under media reps as [[niches/media-rep-firms/broadcast-sales-bureaus/profile|🔍 Broadcast Sales Bureaus & Rep Trade Bodies]] (fails gate). Members fund all of them through dues.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-30 policy, economics and technology research staff (estimate)
**Output:** FCC comments and studies, technical standards work, member research
**Proprietary data:** Member surveys and engineering test results; most inputs licensed or public
**Clock:** FCC comment and reply deadlines and legislative calendars
**Buyer:** Chief economist or EVP of research and policy
