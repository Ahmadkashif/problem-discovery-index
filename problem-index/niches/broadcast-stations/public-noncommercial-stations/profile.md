# Public & Noncommercial Stations After CPB

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/broadcast-stations|Broadcast TV & Radio Stations]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (public-media membership CRMs, fundraising consultancies and donor-analytics vendors) is fighting to move a station's members up the giving ladder, from occasional donor to sustaining member to major donor, fast enough to replace lost Corporation for Public Broadcasting grant money, and whoever moves members up that ladder fastest takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M a year in membership systems, fundraising services, development staff tools and underwriting sales support across public radio and TV (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** under 1% of the parent's revenue; public stations' own revenues are not in the parent's commercial figures (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — membership databases are digital, but donor strategy, pledge scheduling and underwriting sales run on habit and small staffs
**Target Buyer:** Station general manager or chief development officer
**Automation Potential:** Medium — donor scoring and stewardship automate; major-gift relationships do not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The FCC counted 4,806 educational FM and 387 educational TV stations at 30 June 2026. Congress rescinded ~$1.1B in CPB funding in July 2025, and CPB began winding down, with more than 1,500 stations losing grant money. For small and rural stations, that grant was a large share of the budget (~30–50%+, estimate). Noncommercial stations cannot sell advertising: underwriting announcements may identify a sponsor but cannot carry calls to action or prices (estimate). So the replacement has to come from listeners, viewers, donors and foundations. Commercial tools ignore them.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Public-media membership CRMs and general nonprofit CRMs (Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud, Blackbaud), pledge-drive consultants, and shared services from national program providers. Gaps: no propensity scoring for upgrade and major gifts built on membership history, pledge drives scheduled by tradition, and underwriting sold without audience evidence.
