# Nonprofit CRM Adapted to Sustaining Members and Underwriting

**Niche:** [[niches/broadcast-stations/public-noncommercial-stations/profile|Public & Noncommercial Stations After CPB]]
**Industry:** [[industries/broadcast-stations|Broadcast TV & Radio Stations]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Nonprofit CRMs manage donors for any charity; a public station's donors are also its audience, pledge on air, and receive thank-you gifts tied to programmes.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #feature-engineering #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (public-media membership CRMs, fundraising consultancies and donor-analytics vendors) is fighting to move a station's members up the giving ladder, from occasional donor to sustaining member to major donor, fast enough to replace lost Corporation for Public Broadcasting grant money, and whoever moves members up that ladder fastest takes the account.

## The Problem
Membership, pledge-drive results, underwriting contracts and audience data sit in different systems, so the station cannot see a donor's full relationship or an underwriter's renewal risk.

## What Already Exists
Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud, Blackbaud Raiser's Edge and public-media-specific membership systems.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs on-air pledge attribution by programme and hour, sustaining-member billing and retry logic, and thank-you gift fulfilment. Underwriting contracts must be checked for credit copy that stays within noncommercial rules. Audience data (streaming logs, ratings where available) should sit beside donor records, with the propensity scores on each member.

## Target Customer
Development and membership directors at public stations.

## Impact If Solved
One view of each member and underwriter, which lets a small team target, steward and renew without stitching reports by hand.
