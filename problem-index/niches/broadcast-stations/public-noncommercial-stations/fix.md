# Pledge Drives Scheduled by Habit

**Niche:** [[niches/broadcast-stations/public-noncommercial-stations/profile|Public & Noncommercial Stations After CPB]]
**Industry:** [[industries/broadcast-stations|Broadcast TV & Radio Stations]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** On-air fundraising runs in the same weeks, hours and programmes every year, though the station's own pledge records show which hours actually raise money.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #hypothesis-testing #time-series-forecasting #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (public-media membership CRMs, fundraising consultancies and donor-analytics vendors) is fighting to move a station's members up the giving ladder, from occasional donor to sustaining member to major donor, fast enough to replace lost Corporation for Public Broadcasting grant money, and whoever moves members up that ladder fastest takes the account.

## The Problem
Drives interrupt programming and tire the audience. Their timing and length are set by tradition, and results are reported as totals rather than by hour, programme and pitch.

## Why It's Still Broken
Pledge data is recorded by the membership system, but rarely joined to the on-air schedule, and small staffs have no time to analyse it.

## What a Fix Looks Like
After each drive, join pledges to the hour and programme that prompted them. Rank hours by dollars per break and new-versus-renewing mix, and shorten or move the weak ones in the next drive.

## Who Feels the Pain
Development directors, on-air hosts asked to pitch, and listeners who hear the same drive every season.

## Impact If Fixed
Shorter drives that raise the same or more, using data the station already holds.
