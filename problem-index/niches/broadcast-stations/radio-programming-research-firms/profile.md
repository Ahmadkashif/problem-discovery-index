# Radio Programming & Music Research Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/broadcast-stations|Broadcast TV & Radio Stations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Firms such as Coleman Insights run custom research for radio stations: perceptual studies of format and brand position, auditorium music tests, online and callout song testing, and strategy advice to program directors. Radio groups pay per study. National syndicated listening surveys are a different product, profiled under podcasting as [[niches/podcasting-networks/consumer-audio-behaviour-research/profile|🔍 Consumer Audio Behaviour Research]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-30 researchers and consultants per larger firm (estimate)
**Output:** Music test scores, perceptual studies, format and positioning recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Song and perceptual scores across markets and years from client studies
**Clock:** Ratings releases and format-change decisions set by clients
**Buyer:** Firm president or head of research
