# Station Group Corporate Research & Programming Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/broadcast-stations|Broadcast TV & Radio Stations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Large TV and radio groups run corporate research teams that analyse ratings for programming and news, build sales research for local and national selling, model retrans and distribution, and support acquisitions. Nexstar held more than 200 stations before its 2026 TEGNA combination. The group's national sales and pricing unit is profiled under media reps as [[niches/media-rep-firms/station-group-national-sales-units/profile|🔍 Station Group National Sales & Pricing Units]] (31). This pocket is the research and programming analytics team beside it, and is scored the same way.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-40 per large group (estimate)
**Output:** Ratings and programming analysis, sales research, distribution modelling, acquisition support
**Proprietary data:** The group's own ratings licences, traffic and revenue data, and retrans terms across its markets
**Clock:** Ratings releases, the 1 October triennial election, renewal and budget cycles
**Buyer:** Group SVP of research or chief revenue officer
