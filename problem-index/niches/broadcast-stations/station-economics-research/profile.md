# Retransmission & Station Economics Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/broadcast-stations|Broadcast TV & Radio Stations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
S&P Global Market Intelligence's Kagan unit and BIA Advisory Services estimate station-level and industry revenue, retransmission fees per subscriber, reverse compensation, station transaction values and multi-year forecasts. Lenders, investors, station groups, distributors and the FCC's commenters cite them. BIA's local advertising forecasts are profiled under local news as [[niches/news-media-local/local-market-ad-research/profile|🔍 Local Market Advertising Research]] (40). This pocket is the station-economics and retrans product, scored consistently with it. Kagan's broadcast work sits in the same media and communications unit that tracks pay-TV and broadband subscribers, profiled under wired broadband as [[niches/wired-broadband-providers/subscriber-data-trackers/profile|🔍 Media & Communications Subscriber Data Trackers]], and is scored on the same basis.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-30 broadcast analysts at Kagan, inside its wider media and communications unit; under 10 at BIA's station-economics practice (estimates, per firm)
**Output:** Retrans and station revenue estimates and forecasts, deal databases, station financial profiles
**Proprietary data:** Long series of modelled station revenues, retrans estimates and transaction records, assembled from filings, surveys and disclosed deals
**Clock:** The public station groups' and distributors' quarterly earnings dates, after which estimates are updated, plus annual forecast cycles; the triennial retrans election sets demand, not a deadline
**Buyer:** Head of media research
