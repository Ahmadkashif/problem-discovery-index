# Syndicated & Local Programming Acquisition

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/broadcast-stations|Broadcast TV & Radio Stations]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (ratings vendors' analytics tools, programming consultants and the groups' own research teams) is fighting to predict what a syndicated show or local programme will deliver in a specific market's time period, and as a lead-in to the news, before a multi-year licence is signed, and whoever forecasts those time-period ratings most accurately takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$150M a year in programming research, ratings analysis and decision support for TV acquisition (estimate); the licence fees it governs run to billions (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** under 0.5% of TV station revenue spent on the decision (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — ratings are digital, but deals are made by phone with syndicators' sales staff, on comparables and memory
**Target Buyer:** Group VP of programming; station program director at independents
**Automation Potential:** Medium for forecasting; Low for the negotiation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Outside network and news hours, a TV station fills its day with syndicated game shows, talk, court and off-network sitcoms licensed for one or more seasons, plus local lifestyle and paid programming. Groups buy centrally for dozens of markets. The decision turns on a forecast nobody makes rigorously: what this show will rate at 4 p.m. in this market against this competition, and how much audience it hands to the 5 p.m. news, which earns the station's best rates. Radio has no equivalent buying decision. Its programming judgement is in the cluster PD's music calls, a separate niche.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Nielsen local ratings and analysis tools, syndicators' own research presentations, programming consultants and the program director's experience. Gaps: no time-period forecast with uncertainty, no lead-in value attached to a show, and renewals priced on last year's book.
