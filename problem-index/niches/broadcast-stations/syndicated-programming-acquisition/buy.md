# Ratings Analysis Tools Extended to Forecast

**Niche:** [[niches/broadcast-stations/syndicated-programming-acquisition/profile|Syndicated & Local Programming Acquisition]]
**Industry:** [[industries/broadcast-stations|Broadcast TV & Radio Stations]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Ratings analysis software shows every past book in detail and stops at the present, which is exactly where an acquisition decision starts.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #time-series-forecasting #feature-engineering #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (ratings vendors' analytics tools, programming consultants and the groups' own research teams) is fighting to predict what a syndicated show or local programme will deliver in a specific market's time period, and as a lead-in to the news, before a multi-year licence is signed, and whoever forecasts those time-period ratings most accurately takes the account.

## The Problem
Programming staff export ratings into spreadsheets to build comparables by hand for each pitch and renewal.

## What Already Exists
Nielsen's local TV analysis tools, Comscore and VideoAmp data, and general BI tools layered on top.

## The Customization Gap
The extension needs a forecasting layer by show, market and slot. It needs lead-in and lead-out effects, competitive schedules, licence terms and fees attached to each show, and a link to unit rates so ratings become revenue. Every forecast also needs a record of what was decided, so it can be graded later.

## Target Customer
Programming research staff at station groups.

## Impact If Solved
Analysis moves from describing last season to valuing the next deal, using the data the group already licenses.
