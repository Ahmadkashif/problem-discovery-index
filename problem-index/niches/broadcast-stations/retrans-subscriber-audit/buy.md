# Royalty Management Software Adapted to Per-Subscriber Fees

**Niche:** [[niches/broadcast-stations/retrans-subscriber-audit/profile|Retrans Subscriber Reporting & Fee Audit]]
**Industry:** [[industries/broadcast-stations|Broadcast TV & Radio Stations]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Licensors of music, film and brands already run royalty statements through rights-management software; a station's retrans income is a royalty stream with geography and tiers the software does not understand.
**Tags:** #feature-engineering #descriptive-statistics #hypothesis-testing #data-integration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (contract audit firms, royalty-management vendors and in-house distribution finance teams) is fighting to test every distributor's reported subscribers, system by system, against an independent estimate of what each system should report, and whoever finds the shortfalls earliest and most completely takes the account.

## The Problem
Distributor reports arrive in dozens of formats. Each must be parsed, mapped to systems and markets, priced at the right tier and year, and matched to cash.

## What Already Exists
Rights and royalty platforms such as Rightsline and Flowhaven, used by content and brand licensors to manage contracts, statements and receivables.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs report parsers per distributor, a system-to-DMA and zip-footprint map with carriage-area rules, tier eligibility from each agreement, escalators and minimum guarantees, and vMVPD territorial logic. It also needs a link to reverse-comp calculations and an audit-ready trail under each agreement's confidentiality and audit clause.

## Target Customer
Controllers and distribution finance staff at TV groups.

## Impact If Solved
Monthly reconciliation becomes routine instead of a quarter-end scramble, and every receivable is traceable to a contract term and a system count.
