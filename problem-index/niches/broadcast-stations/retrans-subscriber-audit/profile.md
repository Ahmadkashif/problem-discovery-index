# Retrans Subscriber Reporting & Fee Audit

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/broadcast-stations|Broadcast TV & Radio Stations]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (contract audit firms, royalty-management vendors and in-house distribution finance teams) is fighting to test every distributor's reported subscribers, system by system, against an independent estimate of what each system should report, and whoever finds the shortfalls earliest and most completely takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$60M a year in collection staff, audit fees and reconciliation tooling across TV groups (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.15% of TV station revenue spent on it (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — reports arrive as files and portal downloads and are checked in spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** Group VP of distribution finance or controller
**Automation Potential:** High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Once an agreement is signed, the money depends on counts the distributor reports, system by system and tier by tier, every month or quarter. The work is collection and verification, not negotiation. Different people do it, the clock is the agreement's audit clause and limitation period, and the winners are whoever reconciles fastest and most completely. Errors come from migrations, tier definitions and vMVPD territorial counting more often than from intent (estimate).

## Current Tools & Gaps
Spreadsheets, ERP receivables, contract audit rights exercised through specialist audit firms, and royalty-management software at a few groups. Gaps: no expected-count model per system, no mapping of systems to DMAs and carriage areas kept current, and audits that are reactive and infrequent.
