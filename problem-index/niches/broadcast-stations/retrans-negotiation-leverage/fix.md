# Every Renewal Starts From Last Cycle's Spreadsheet

**Niche:** [[niches/broadcast-stations/retrans-negotiation-leverage/profile|Retrans Negotiation & Blackout Leverage]]
**Industry:** [[industries/broadcast-stations|Broadcast TV & Radio Stations]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Three years after the last renewal, the person who built the model has moved on and the negotiation restarts from a file nobody fully understands.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #descriptive-statistics #feature-engineering #quick-win #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (distribution consultants, economic advisers and the groups' own distribution teams) is fighting to estimate, distributor by distributor, what a blackout would cost each side in lost subscribers, fees and ratings, and whoever estimates that blackout cost most accurately takes the account.

## The Problem
Renewals with any one distributor recur every few years. Between them, staff change, groups merge, and the reasoning behind the last deal's concessions is lost.

## Why It's Still Broken
Negotiation records are kept as email, redlines and spreadsheet versions, and confidentiality keeps them in few hands.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A renewal file per distributor: the final terms, each offer and counter with its modelled value, the reasons for concessions, and the subscriber and ratings context at the time. Each entry is drafted from the negotiation's documents and confirmed by the negotiator when the deal closes.

## Who Feels the Pain
New distribution executives inheriting a portfolio after a merger, and groups that repeat old concessions without knowing why.

## Impact If Fixed
The next renewal starts from evidence of what worked, at the cost of an hour's record-keeping per deal.
