# Planning Software for Renewal Scenarios

**Niche:** [[niches/broadcast-stations/retrans-negotiation-leverage/profile|Retrans Negotiation & Blackout Leverage]]
**Industry:** [[industries/broadcast-stations|Broadcast TV & Radio Stations]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Enterprise planning tools model any business scenario; a retrans renewal needs subscriber decline, MFN ripple and network reverse comp modelled together for each offer on the table.
**Tags:** #monte-carlo-methods #time-series-forecasting #feature-engineering #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (distribution consultants, economic advisers and the groups' own distribution teams) is fighting to estimate, distributor by distributor, what a blackout would cost each side in lost subscribers, fees and ratings, and whoever estimates that blackout cost most accurately takes the account.

## The Problem
During a renewal, offers and counters arrive quickly, and each must be valued across the term, net of reverse comp, and checked for MFN effects on other agreements. That work is done in a spreadsheet by one or two people overnight.

## What Already Exists
Enterprise planning platforms such as Anaplan, Workday Adaptive Planning and Oracle planning tools, plus Excel.

## The Customization Gap
The model needs a subscriber forecast per distributor and market, rate schedules with escalators and tiers, MFN comparators across the group's other agreements, reverse-comp formulas, and the blackout cost model's estimates as scenario inputs. It also needs a negotiation log so the next renewal starts from what was offered and why.

## Target Customer
Distribution finance staff supporting the group's negotiators.

## Impact If Solved
Every offer is valued consistently within minutes, MFN surprises are avoided, and the negotiation history becomes an asset rather than a memory.
