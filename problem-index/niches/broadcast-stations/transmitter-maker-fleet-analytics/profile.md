# Transmitter Maker Fleet Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/broadcast-stations|Broadcast TV & Radio Stations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Transmitter manufacturers such as GatesAir, Nautel and Rohde & Schwarz sell TV and radio transmitters with remote monitoring and service contracts. Their service teams see fault data from the stations that opt in. Communications equipment makers are queued separately in this plan; this pocket is only their view into station transmitter fleets.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Service and R&D engineers; fleet reliability analysis is done by a handful (estimate)
**Output:** Service support, firmware updates, product reliability findings
**Proprietary data:** Telemetry and fault logs from monitored transmitters, under customer service terms
**Clock:** Customer outages and product cycles
**Buyer:** VP of service or product
