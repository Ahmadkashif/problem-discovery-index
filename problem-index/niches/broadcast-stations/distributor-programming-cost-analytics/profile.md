# Distributor Programming-Cost Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/broadcast-stations|Broadcast TV & Radio Stations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Cable, satellite, telco and virtual distributors pay retransmission fees and model them: what each station is worth to their subscribers, what a blackout would cost, and how much programming cost they can pass through. Their content-acquisition analytics teams use set-top and app viewing data alongside contract terms. The cost comes out of the distributor's own margin, so nobody else pays for the analysis.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-50 analysts per large distributor's content-acquisition finance and analytics team (estimate)
**Output:** Station valuations for negotiation, blackout scenarios, programming-cost forecasts
**Proprietary data:** Subscriber viewing data from set-top boxes and apps, churn records and every programming contract
**Clock:** Contract expiries, mostly at year-end within the FCC's triennial election cycle
**Buyer:** SVP of content acquisition
