# Public Media Fundraising Benchmarks & Station Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/broadcast-stations|Broadcast TV & Radio Stations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Membership and service organisations for public stations, such as Greater Public, publish fundraising benchmarks, train development staff and run peer comparisons of membership and major-gift results. National program providers offer station-services teams. Demand has grown since the 2025 CPB rescission. Stations pay dues and fees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research and benchmarking staff per organisation (estimate)
**Output:** Benchmark reports, training, peer comparisons
**Proprietary data:** Station-submitted fundraising results
**Clock:** Fiscal years and drive calendars set by stations
**Buyer:** Executive director
