# Small-Operator Programming Cooperative

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/broadcast-stations|Broadcast TV & Radio Stations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The National Content & Technology Cooperative negotiates programming agreements on behalf of hundreds of small and independent cable operators. For broadcast stations it is one of the counterparties on the payer side of distribution. Its members pay through cooperative fees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analysts beside its negotiators (estimate)
**Output:** Master agreements and rate cards for members
**Proprietary data:** Member subscriber counts and programming terms
**Clock:** Agreement expiries
**Buyer:** CEO
