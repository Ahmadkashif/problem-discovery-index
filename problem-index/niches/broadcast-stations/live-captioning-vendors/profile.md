# Live Captioning & Audio Description Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/broadcast-stations|Broadcast TV & Radio Stations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors supply real-time captioning for live local news and sports, by human stenocaptioners or automatic speech recognition, plus audio description, so stations meet the FCC's captioning rules. Offline captioning for finished programmes is analysed under postproduction as [[niches/postproduction-studios/captions-subtitles-and-accessibility/profile|⚡ Captions, Subtitles & Accessibility Files]]. Stations pay per hour or by subscription.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Large captioning workforces, but no research or insight function beyond quality monitoring (estimate)
**Output:** Live caption streams and description tracks
**Proprietary data:** Caption archives and accuracy logs
**Clock:** Air time
**Buyer:** Head of operations
