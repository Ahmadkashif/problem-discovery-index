# The Cluster Radio Program Director

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/broadcast-stations|Broadcast TV & Radio Stations]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (music research firms, music-scheduling vendors and listening-data analytics services) is fighting to tell a program director which songs to add, rotate and rest, and where listeners are tuning away, from signals measured between ratings releases and without a callout budget, and whoever gives the PD the music read they trust most takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$150M a year in music research, scheduling software, programming consultants and listener analytics for radio (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1% of local radio revenue (estimate on BIA/RAB's $12.3B for 2025)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — scheduling is software, but the add-and-rest decisions run on the PD's ear, trade charts and thin research
**Target Buyer:** Cluster operations manager or PD; group head of programming
**Automation Potential:** Medium for music decisions; Low for talent and format strategy

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Consolidation and cost cuts have left one program director running several stations in a cluster, often with voice-tracked airstaff and a music director role folded into the job (estimate). Callout and auditorium music tests, once standard, are now a luxury for most clusters. Ratings arrive monthly from Nielsen Audio's 48 PPM markets, and from diaries in 200+ smaller markets. The PD decides weekly which songs to add and how hard to rotate them. That is a tacit judgement, the "ear", made with less evidence than a decade ago and by one person across several formats. Music scheduling tools place songs by rules, but they don't decide what the rules should be.

## Current Tools & Gaps
RCS Selector and MusicMaster for scheduling, Mediabase-style airplay and chart data, Nielsen Audio ratings and analysis tools, streaming dashboards for the station's own app, and programming consultants. Gaps: no song-level fatigue signal between tests, streaming and PPM minute data not joined to the music log, and no record of why songs were added or rested.
