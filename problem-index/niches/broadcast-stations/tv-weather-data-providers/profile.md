# TV Weather Data & Graphics Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/broadcast-stations|Broadcast TV & Radio Stations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Firms such as Baron Weather, The Weather Company and DTN sell TV stations the weather data, radar products, forecast models and on-air graphics systems that station meteorologists build their forecasts and severe-weather coverage on. Stations pay annual licences, often the largest data contract a newsroom holds (estimate).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-100 meteorologists, data scientists and product analysts per provider (estimate)
**Output:** Forecast and radar data feeds, severe-weather alerts and model products, graphics platforms
**Proprietary data:** Modelled forecasts and derived products built largely on public weather data, plus some proprietary observation networks (estimate)
**Clock:** Weather events themselves, which set real-time delivery demands rather than filing deadlines
**Buyer:** Head of broadcast products or chief meteorologist on the vendor side
