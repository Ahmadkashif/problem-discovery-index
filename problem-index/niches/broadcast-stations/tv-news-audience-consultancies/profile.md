# TV News & Audience Research Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/broadcast-stations|Broadcast TV & Radio Stations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Consultancies such as Frank N. Magid Associates and SmithGeiger run perceptual research for TV stations and groups: market surveys on news brand, anchors, weather and image, talent testing, newscast content studies and strategy advice. They also coach talent and producers. Stations and groups pay per study or on multi-year consulting contracts, usually with market exclusivity.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-80 researchers, analysts and consultants on broadcast work per large firm (estimate)
**Output:** Market perceptual studies, talent and content research, strategy recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Decades of station-commissioned surveys across markets and the firm's normative benchmarks built from them
**Clock:** Station planning cycles and ratings calendars set by the clients; no third-party deadline on the study itself
**Buyer:** Head of the broadcast practice

**Kill switches:** none live. Each market study is commissioned by a station and exclusive to it in its market, but the firms keep cross-market normative archives and their own methods as standard practice (estimate). That archive and method, not the resale of any one client's results, is what an insight engine would run on. The kill switch is live only where the analysis cannot be done without data the client owns, as for experts working on data under protective order. The vault rules the same way for [[niches/public-relations-agencies/public-affairs-polling-message-testing/profile|🔍 Public Affairs Polling & Message-Testing Firms]].
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. Research is the invoice and the archive is deep, but each study is bespoke to a market, the clock is the client's, and the buyer field is a handful of firms.
