# Bar Examination & Attorney Licensure Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/small-law-firms|Small Law Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Develop and administer the examination that determines who may practise law, maintain the item banks and scoring behind it, and set the competency framework the profession's entry is judged against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-400 psychometricians, item developers, subject matter experts, and grading operations staff
**Output:** Examinations and item banks, scoring and equating, practice analyses, passing standard research, character and fitness processes
**Proprietary data:** Item response data across the entire entering profession over decades, plus practice analysis surveys of what lawyers actually do
**Clock:** Fixed examination administrations and admission cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Assessment Officer / Director of Testing
