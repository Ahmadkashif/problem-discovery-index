# Law Firm Financial & Rate Benchmarking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/small-law-firms|Small Law Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Collect law firm financial performance and billing rates and publish the benchmarks firms set rates and compensation against — in a market where no firm publishes anything.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 analysts, economists, and survey researchers
**Output:** Rate benchmarks by practice area and market, financial performance surveys, compensation studies, demand and productivity indices
**Proprietary data:** Confidential financial submissions and billing rate data assembled over decades
**Clock:** Annual rate-setting and compensation cycles
**Buyer:** Head of Research / Chief Economist
