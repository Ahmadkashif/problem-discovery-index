# Attorney Directories & Legal Lead Generation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/small-law-firms|Small Law Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator/rollup

## What They Do
Operate the directories and matching services consumers find lawyers through, and run the ranking, rating and lead pricing analytics behind them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 data scientists, marketplace analysts, and content operations staff
**Output:** Attorney ratings and rankings, lead matching and pricing, practice area demand analytics, consumer legal content
**Proprietary data:** Consumer legal enquiries at scale joined to attorney response and retention
**Clock:** Continuous enquiry flow
**Buyer:** VP of Data Science
