# Court Data & Litigation Analytics Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/small-law-firms|Small Law Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Normalise dockets and dispositions from thousands of court systems and sell the analytics — judge behaviour, motion outcome rates, timing, and opposing counsel histories — that a small firm uses to set expectations with a client.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 court researchers, data acquisition staff, taxonomists, and analysts
**Output:** Judge and venue analytics, motion outcome rates, timing models, party and counsel litigation histories
**Proprietary data:** Normalised state trial court records assembled from thousands of county systems
**Clock:** Daily docket updates against live deadlines
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Analytics
