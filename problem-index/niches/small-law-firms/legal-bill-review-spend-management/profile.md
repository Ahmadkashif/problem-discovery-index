# Legal Bill Review & Spend Management

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/small-law-firms|Small Law Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Review outside counsel invoices line by line against billing guidelines, reduce or reject entries, and report to corporate and insurer clients on what their legal work costs and whether it was worth it — the layer deciding what a firm actually gets paid.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 bill reviewers, attorneys, analysts, and data scientists
**Output:** Line-item adjudication and reductions, rate benchmarking, matter budgeting and forecasting, firm performance scoring, guideline compliance reporting
**Proprietary data:** Billing line items across many firms, matters and practice areas — the only comparative record of what legal work actually costs and how long each task takes
**Clock:** Invoice submission and payment cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Analytics Officer / VP of Legal Operations
