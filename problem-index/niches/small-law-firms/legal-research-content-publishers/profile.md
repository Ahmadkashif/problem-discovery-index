# Legal Research & Practice Content Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/small-law-firms|Small Law Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Publish the law. Every case decided by every American court is collected, read, summarised into headnotes, classified into a subject taxonomy built over more than a century, and connected by a citator that says whether a decision is still good law — plus annotated statutes, regulatory tracking, practice guides, forms and treatises for every practice area. A small firm's entire ability to answer a legal question rests on this layer, and it is one of the largest line items on the firm's overhead.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1,000-5,000 attorney-editors, classification specialists, citator analysts, taxonomists, and data scientists across the major publishers
**Output:** Case law databases with editorial headnotes and classification, citators and treatment flags, annotated statutes and regulations, practice guides and treatises, forms, litigation and transactional analytics
**Proprietary data:** More than a century of accumulated editorial classification over the entire body of American case law — headnotes, the subject taxonomy, and the treatment judgments in the citator — none of which exists in the public record of the opinions themselves
**Clock:** Continuous — opinions issue daily, statutes change with every legislative session, and a citator that is stale is actively dangerous
**Buyer:** Chief Content Officer / VP of Editorial Operations
