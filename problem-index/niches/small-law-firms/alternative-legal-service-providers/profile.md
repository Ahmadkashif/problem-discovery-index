# Alternative Legal Service Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/small-law-firms|Small Law Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Perform legal work outside the law firm model at scale — contract review and abstraction, due diligence, compliance operations, and managed legal services — for corporate clients and for firms that cannot staff it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1,000-10,000 lawyers, paralegals, process specialists, and technologists
**Output:** Contract abstraction and review, diligence reports, compliance operations, managed services delivery, process design
**Proprietary data:** Very large volumes of reviewed contracts and abstracted terms, held per client engagement
**Clock:** Transaction and compliance deadlines
**Buyer:** Chief Operating Officer / VP of Delivery Innovation
