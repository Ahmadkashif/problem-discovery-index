# Title Plant & Search Operations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/small-law-firms|Small Law Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Maintain geographically indexed records of every instrument affecting title in a county, and run the searches and examinations that determine whether a property can be conveyed — the work many small firms perform or rely on in real estate practice.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-3,000 title plant operators, examiners, abstractors, and data staff
**Output:** Title searches and examinations, chain of title, exception and requirement determinations, plant maintenance
**Proprietary data:** Decades of geographically indexed instrument records built county by county, which the public records themselves do not provide in usable form
**Clock:** Closing dates
**Buyer:** VP of Title Operations / Chief Data Officer
