# E-Discovery Service Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/small-law-firms|Small Law Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Collect, process, cull and review electronic evidence for litigation — the largest cost centre in modern discovery, and the place where technology-assisted review became mainstream legal practice.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1,000-10,000 review attorneys, data engineers, analytics specialists, and project managers
**Output:** Processed and culled document sets, technology-assisted review workflows, privilege logs, production sets, defensibility documentation
**Proprietary data:** Review decisions at enormous volume, all of it belonging to a specific matter and a specific client
**Clock:** Court-ordered discovery deadlines
**Buyer:** VP of Analytics / Chief Innovation Officer
