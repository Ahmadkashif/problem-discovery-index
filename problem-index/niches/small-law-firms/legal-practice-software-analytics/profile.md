# Legal Practice Management Platform Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/small-law-firms|Small Law Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The analytics functions inside the practice management platforms small firms run on, producing benchmark reports on rates, realisation, matter duration and intake conversion across a very large firm base.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 data scientists, analysts, and product researchers
**Output:** Legal trends and benchmark reports, intake and conversion analytics, billing and realisation dashboards, matter duration analysis
**Proprietary data:** Matter, time entry, billing and payment records across tens of thousands of small firms — a view of small firm economics that exists nowhere else
**Clock:** Monthly billing cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Head of Insights
