# Legal Malpractice Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/small-law-firms|Small Law Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Underwrite the professional liability policies small firms carry, and adjudicate the claims — the majority of which trace to missed deadlines, conflicts, and inadequate client communication rather than to bad legal judgment.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 underwriters, actuaries, claims counsel, and risk managers
**Output:** Programme pricing and eligibility, claims defence and adjudication, loss control and practice management guidance, risk profiling by practice area
**Proprietary data:** Malpractice claims joined to practice area, firm size and practice management characteristics — the only systematic record of how legal work actually goes wrong
**Clock:** Annual policy renewals
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
