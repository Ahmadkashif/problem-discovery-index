# Which Caskets to Stock in Which Service Centre

**Niche:** [[niches/consumer-products-makers/burial-caskets/profile|Burial Caskets]]
**Industry:** [[industries/consumer-products-makers|Consumer Products Makers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** As burial declines, every service centre must carry fewer casket models without missing a service, and the stocking decision is still made by a regional manager's feel for local taste.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #gradient-boosting #k-nearest-neighbors #survival-analysis #tacit-knowledge-ml #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get the casket a family chose into the funeral home before the service, and whoever fills the highest share of orders before the service takes the account.

## The Problem
A service centre covers dozens or hundreds of funeral homes. It must hold the models those homes display and the substitutes families accept when a model is out, across metal gauges, woods, colours and interiors. Experienced managers know that one county buys bronze and another wants oak; demand per model is a handful a month and falling.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The market is concentrated in a few makers whose systems were built for a growing burial base, and order data is thin per model.

## What to Build
A forecasting and stocking model by service centre and model that pools similar models and regions, uses funeral-home display sets, death statistics and local cremation trends, and estimates the chance of missing a service for each stocking plan. It recommends which slow models to consolidate to a regional hub with a longer lead time.

## Target Customer
Distribution and supply-chain leaders at casket makers and large regional distributors.

## Impact If Built
Service-centre inventory falls by ~10–20% (estimate) while the share of orders filled before the service holds at ~99% (estimate), which is what keeps service-centre economics viable as NFDA's projected burial share falls from 31.6% toward 13.0%.
