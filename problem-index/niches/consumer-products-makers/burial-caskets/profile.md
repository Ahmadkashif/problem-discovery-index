# Burial Caskets

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/consumer-products-makers|Consumer Products Makers]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get the casket a family chose into the funeral home before the service, and whoever fills the highest share of orders before the service takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$711M in 2022 receipts across 71 employer firms (Census SUSB 2022, NAICS 339995)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of the four codes' receipts
**Digital Adoption:** Low — funeral homes order by phone, fax and rep visit; service-centre stock and delivery runs are planned by dispatchers from memory
**Target Buyer:** VP of Operations or Distribution at a casket maker; owner of a regional casket distributor
**Automation Potential:** Medium — order capture, stocking and routing automate; welding, finishing and interiors stay by hand

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Four firms with 500+ employees hold ~72% of 339995 receipts (SUSB 2022), led by Batesville, which Hillenbrand sold to LongRange Capital in 2023 for $761.5M, and Matthews. Below them are small wood-casket shops and importers. The product is ordered at the worst moment in a family's life, during the arrangement conference described in [[niches/funeral-homes/arrangement-conference/profile|Arrangement Conference]], and must reach the funeral home within a day or two for the service. Makers and distributors keep regional service centres stocked with the models funeral homes display. The volume base is shrinking: NFDA projects burial at 31.6% of dispositions in 2025, falling to 13.0% by 2045, so each service centre carries fewer, slower-moving models. The funeral home stays loyal to the supplier that never misses a service date.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Maker ERPs, funeral-home ordering by rep and phone, printed and digital selection catalogues, and in-house fleets. The gaps: no demand model by service centre as burial declines; after-hours orders taken on paper; delivery routes planned by hand; no link between what funeral homes display and what families choose.
