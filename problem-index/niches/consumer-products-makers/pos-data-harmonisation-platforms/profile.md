# Retail POS Data Harmonisation Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/consumer-products-makers|Consumer Products Makers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Software firms that pull a supplier's sales, inventory and order data from each retailer's portal, map items and calendars, and sell dashboards, forecasts and alerts to the maker's sales and supply-chain teams: Alloy.ai, Crisp, the retail analytics modules of EDI networks, and smaller analytics shops. Makers pay subscriptions; some platforms add analyst services.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10–50 customer analytics and data-science staff at the larger platforms (estimate)
**Output:** Harmonised POS and inventory datasets, forecasts, out-of-stock alerts, analyst reviews
**Proprietary data:** Connector code, item and calendar mappings, and normalised history across many suppliers; the sales data itself is each retailer's, released to the supplier under the retailer's portal terms
**Clock:** Self-set refresh cycles; retailers' line reviews are the client's clock
**Buyer:** Head of data or analytics products at the platform; the platform's customers are makers' sales and supply-chain teams, but the buyer market for an insight engine is a handful of such platforms

For the **analyst-services half** (analysts reviewing one maker's retailer data on commission), **data isn't theirs** is live: the work is done on client-supplied retailer data under service agreements with no checked product rights. The verdict follows the subscription half, which is the core of the business.
