# EDI and Warehouse Checks Tuned per Retailer

**Niche:** [[niches/consumer-products-makers/otif-routing-edi-compliance/profile|OTIF, Routing-Guide & EDI Compliance]]
**Industry:** [[industries/consumer-products-makers|Consumer Products Makers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** EDI networks and warehouse systems already hold every rule and document; they need the retailer's routing guide turned into pre-shipment checks.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #automation #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a maker's shipments inside each retailer's routing guide and on-time, in-full targets, and whoever keeps the maker's compliance fines lowest as a share of shipments takes the account.

## The Problem
Routing guides change: a new label format, a tighter ship-notice timing rule, a different appointment window. The maker's warehouse and 3PL run on last year's version until the fines arrive, and each retailer's guide is a long PDF that nobody reads end to end after onboarding.

## What Already Exists
EDI networks that maintain retailer document maps, warehouse systems with label templates and pack-out checks, transport systems that book appointments, and 3PLs with compliance teams.

## The Customization Gap
Routing-guide text turned into machine-checkable rules per retailer; alerts when a guide changes, with a summary of what is different; carton and pallet label checks at pack-out against the current rule; ship-notice timing tied to the carrier's pickup scan; and appointment rules checked at booking. It is rule extraction and configuration on top of systems the maker already runs.

## Target Customer
Operations leads at makers and their 3PLs.

## Impact If Solved
Fines caused by guide changes, perhaps a tenth of all compliance fines (estimate), are largely avoided: ~$25K–$50K a year at a $50M maker with ~$250K of fines (estimate).
