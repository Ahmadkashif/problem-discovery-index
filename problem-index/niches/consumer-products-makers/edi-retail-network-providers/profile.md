# EDI & Retail Network Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/consumer-products-makers|Consumer Products Makers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The networks that carry purchase orders, ship notices, invoices and remittances between makers and retailers, and maintain each retailer's document requirements: SPS Commerce (which bought SupplyPike for $205.8M, effective 31 July 2024, and Carbon6 in early 2025, now sold as SPS Revenue Recovery), TrueCommerce, Cleo, Orderful and AI-first entrants such as Crstl. Makers pay per connection and per document, plus add-ons for analytics and deduction recovery.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10–50 analysts in retail analytics and revenue-recovery products at the largest network (estimate); the much larger map-building and onboarding teams are production
**Output:** EDI connectivity and retailer maps (the invoice), with sell-through analytics and deduction recovery as add-ons
**Proprietary data:** Document flows across a large network of trading partners and the maintained library of retailer requirements
**Clock:** Retailer compliance and mapping change dates, which are client clocks
**Buyer:** Head of retail analytics or revenue-recovery product at the network
