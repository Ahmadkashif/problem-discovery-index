# Retailer Compliance, Deductions & Chargebacks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/consumer-products-makers|Consumer Products Makers]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stop retailers keeping money a maker earned, but it splits into two contests with different winners: recovering deductions already taken, won by whoever recovers the most invalid dollars before each dispute window closes, and preventing compliance fines before they are charged, won by whoever keeps fines lowest as a share of shipments. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600M+ a year of deductions and compliance fines across makers in the four codes (estimate: deduction ranges of a few percent of sales from the CRF survey and vendor benchmarks applied to ~$21.4B receipts)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — the data is in EDI and portals; the work is manual
**Target Buyer:** CFO or Controller, with the VP of Operations for fines
**Automation Potential:** High — portal retrieval, document matching and dispute filing are repetitive and rule-bound

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Retailers deduct from payments for promotions, damages, shortages, pricing, returns and compliance failures, and fine suppliers for late, incomplete or mislabelled shipments: Walmart charges 3% of the cost of goods on non-compliant cases (search summary). The Credit Research Foundation's 2023 deduction survey found median non-trade deductions of 1.1–2% of sales, and its results as compiled by Overdeduct give a median 5.1–10% of deduction dollars invalid, and a vendor puts all deductions at 3–7% of gross revenue for mid-market makers (Finortal, vendor figure). It is the most mechanical money in the business, and it is being automated now by funded vendors. But it holds two contests. Recovery is a finance contest against dispute windows; prevention is an operations contest against routing guides, labels, advance ship notices and appointment times. The buyers, data and winners differ.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Retailer portals, EDI providers, order-to-cash suites (HighRadius), CPG deduction platforms (Confido, Glimpse, SPS Revenue Recovery), 3PL and carrier systems. The gaps: fines and deductions never joined to the shipment that caused them; finance disputing what operations could have prevented.
