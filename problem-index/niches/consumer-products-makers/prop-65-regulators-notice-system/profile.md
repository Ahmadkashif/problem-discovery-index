# Prop 65 Regulators & Notice System

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/consumer-products-makers|Consumer Products Makers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
California's Office of Environmental Health Hazard Assessment maintains the Prop 65 list of chemicals and the safe-harbour levels, and the Attorney General's office receives 60-day notices and settlement reports from private enforcers. Settlement Insight's analysis of the AG's reporting export counts 1,535 settlements and about $66M paid in 2025, and 7,602 settlements totalling $205.5M since 2016, 87.5% of it attorney's fees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–60 scientists and staff on Prop 65 listing and safe-harbour work, plus a small AG unit (estimate)
**Output:** The chemical list, safe-harbour levels, warning regulations and public notice and settlement records
**Proprietary data:** None that is exclusive: notices and settlements are published
**Clock:** Listing and warning effective dates set by the agency itself; the 60-day clocks run on the firms and enforcers
**Buyer:** OEHHA's Proposition 65 programme lead (a single state buyer)
