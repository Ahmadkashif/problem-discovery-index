# Consumer Product Recall Execution & Returns Processors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/consumer-products-makers|Consumer Products Makers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Firms that run recalls and returns for makers and retailers: customer notification, call centres, claim and refund processing, product retrieval and destruction, and the reconciliation reports CPSC asks for. Some publish recall trend reports as marketing.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Analytical teams of a few people; notification, call-centre and processing staff are production (estimate)
**Output:** Executed recalls and returns, with completion reports
**Proprietary data:** Recall response histories, held for each client
**Clock:** CPSC corrective-action plans and retailer return deadlines
**Buyer:** Head of recall services
