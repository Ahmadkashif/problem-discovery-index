# EDI Starter Bundles Extended to Full Supplier Onboarding

**Niche:** [[niches/consumer-products-makers/first-big-box-programme/profile|Small Makers Entering Big-Box Retail]]
**Industry:** [[industries/consumer-products-makers|Consumer Products Makers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** EDI providers already sell starter packages to first-time suppliers; onboarding needs everything around the EDI as well.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #automation #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to carry a first-time supplier through onboarding and its first retail season, and whoever keeps the most first-season makers net-positive after deductions and fines takes the account.

## The Problem
A new supplier has weeks to set up EDI documents, item setup, GS1 barcodes, carton labels, insurance certificates, testing, chemical registrations and the routing guide. EDI is one task on a list of twenty, and the rest is a checklist the owner builds from a supplier manual of hundreds of pages. Missed steps show up as fines and deductions on the first shipments.

## What Already Exists
EDI starter bundles and onboarding services from SPS Commerce, TrueCommerce and newer AI-first providers such as Crstl, plus retailer supplier-onboarding portals and consultants who coach first-time suppliers.

## The Customization Gap
A per-retailer onboarding plan generated from its supplier manual and routing guide; item-setup data built once and reused across retailers; testing and certificate requirements for the maker's product type (ASTM F2417 for candles, art-material labelling for markers, child-resistance for lighters); label and carton templates checked before the first shipment; and the first deductions and fines watched from day one, so a mistake is caught after one load instead of ten.

## Target Customer
First-time suppliers, and the EDI providers that want to keep them past the first season.

## Impact If Solved
Onboarding takes ~4–6 weeks instead of ~3 months (estimate), and first-season fines fall by roughly half, ~$20K–$40K on a ~$2M first programme (estimate).
