# Matching a Pro's Feel With a Cheaper Filament Blend

**Niche:** [[niches/consumer-products-makers/brushes-brooms-paint-applicators/profile|Brushes, Brooms, Mops & Paint Applicators]]
**Industry:** [[industries/consumer-products-makers|Consumer Products Makers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** When filament prices or tariffs move, a brush maker re-blends by trial and error until its senior product people say the new brush "feels right", with no model of what feel is made of.
**Tags:** #tacit-knowledge-ml #gaussian-processes #bayesian-optimization #gradient-boosting #feature-engineering #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep professional painters and cleaning crews reaching for its brush, roller or broom at the paint store and home centre, and whoever keeps the largest share of pros' repeat purchases takes the account.

## The Problem
A paint brush's performance comes from filament type, diameter, taper and tipping, the blend ratio, the flagging, the length out and the trim profile. Experts judge it by hand and by brushing out paint. Every reformulation is a round of samples and opinions.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Makers are small and family-owned, performance tests are informal, and nobody has paired measured performance with blend and machine data.

## What to Build
A standard bench test that measures paint pickup, release, stroke length and line edge with a load cell and camera, a structured record of blend and machine settings for each sample, and a model that predicts performance for a proposed blend and suggests the next sample to try. The senior painter's rating stays as a label, scored against the measurements.

## Target Customer
Product development leads at brush and roller makers selling to paint-store chains and home centres.

## Impact If Built
Reformulation drops from ~4–6 sample rounds to ~2–3 (estimate), and a cheaper blend that holds performance can save a few percent of material cost (estimate) without losing the pros who notice.
