# Retailer-Owned Shopper Data Products

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/consumer-products-makers|Consumer Products Makers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Data businesses owned by retailers that sell their own transaction, loyalty and shopper research back to the suppliers who stock their shelves: Walmart Data Ventures' Scintilla (the platform formerly called Luminate, with a free Basic tier and a paid Charter tier, search summary), Kroger's 84.51° and its Stratum product, Tesco's dunnhumby, and similar units at other chains. Makers pay subscriptions and custom-research fees for item, basket, shopper and perception insight on their own categories at that retailer. Walmart's September 2026 supplier event drew more than 2,000 suppliers and merchants.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Hundreds of analysts, data scientists and insight consultants per large unit; 84.51° began in 2015 with 500+ former dunnhumbyUSA staff and current estimates range from ~600 to ~1,500 (search summary)
**Output:** Subscription dashboards, shopper and basket analyses, customer perception studies and custom research for suppliers
**Proprietary data:** The retailer's own transaction and loyalty records and shopper panels, which no outside vendor can buy
**Clock:** Self-set release cadences; the supplier's line reviews and joint business plans are the retailer's own calendar, a client clock for the maker
**Buyer:** President or GM of the retailer's data venture
