# Trade Promotion & Revenue Growth Management Advisory

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/consumer-products-makers|Consumer Products Makers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Revenue growth management practices at strategy and pricing consultancies, and the analyst services of trade promotion software vendors, that evaluate a maker's promotions, price-pack architecture and trade terms and recommend where to cut or move spend. McKinsey puts consumer goods trade promotion at about 20% of revenue and found 72% of US promotions lose money (Nielsen 2016, cited; search summary). Makers pay project fees or analyst retainers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20–300 RGM consultants and analysts at the larger practices; under 20 analysts at software vendors' insight teams (estimate)
**Output:** Promotion post-event analyses, price elasticity studies, trade-terms recommendations, joint business plan support
**Proprietary data:** Method libraries and benchmark knowledge; the promotion, shipment and deduction data analysed is the client's
**Clock:** Annual joint business planning and retailer line reviews, which are client clocks
**Buyer:** VP of Revenue Management or Chief Commercial Officer at the maker
