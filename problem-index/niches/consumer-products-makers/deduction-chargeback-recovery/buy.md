# CPG Deduction Platforms Extended to General Merchandise

**Niche:** [[niches/consumer-products-makers/deduction-chargeback-recovery/profile|Deduction & Chargeback Recovery]]
**Industry:** [[industries/consumer-products-makers|Consumer Products Makers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** CPG deduction platforms already pull, match and dispute claims for food and beverage brands; a candle, pen or brush maker needs its own retailers and terms added.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #gradient-boosting #revenue-impact #workflow-orchestration #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn invalid retailer deductions into credits before each retailer's dispute window closes, and whoever recovers the most invalid deduction dollars per maker takes the account.

## The Problem
A general-merchandise maker evaluates a deduction platform and finds connectors and reason-code maps for grocery chains and food distributors but not for its home centres, craft chains, off-price retailers or dollar stores. Its promotions are agreed by email rather than in a trade promotion system, so the platform has nothing to match allowances against.

## What Already Exists
Confido (Series B, 250+ brands), Glimpse (200+ customers on its own account), SPS Revenue Recovery (SupplyPike plus Carbon6) and HighRadius already pull, match and dispute deductions, with forward deployed teams that build new retailer connectors.

## The Customization Gap
Connectors and code maps for home-centre, craft, off-price and club retailers; defective-allowance, markdown and seasonal return logic; Amazon Vendor Central shortage claims; evidence from makers' own 3PLs and LTL carriers; and allowance matching from emails and buyer letters where no promotion system exists.

## Target Customer
Makers with ~$20M–$500M in retail sales outside grocery (estimate of the addressable band).

## Impact If Solved
For a $50M maker with ~$1.5M of deductions (estimate), raising recovery of invalid dollars from ~40% to ~75% (estimate) is worth ~$50K–$100K a year, without paying for its own build.
