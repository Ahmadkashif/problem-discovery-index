# Disputing the Deductions Below the Write-Off Line

**Niche:** [[niches/consumer-products-makers/deduction-chargeback-recovery/profile|Deduction & Chargeback Recovery]]
**Industry:** [[industries/consumer-products-makers|Consumer Products Makers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Makers write off deductions under $50–$100 because a person cannot afford to research them, and that is exactly the work an agent can do for cents.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #gradient-boosting #bert #feature-engineering #tacit-knowledge-ml #revenue-impact #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn invalid retailer deductions into credits before each retailer's dispute window closes, and whoever recovers the most invalid deduction dollars per maker takes the account.

## The Problem
Shortage, damage and fine deductions under the threshold arrive by the thousand. Each would take a clerk twenty minutes to research and file, so they are written off unread, invalid or not.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Until agents could operate retailer portals reliably, the research cost exceeded the value. The newer vendors started with large claims for large brands.

## What to Build
An agent that pulls every deduction from each portal, matches proof of delivery, advance ship notice and carrier data, predicts validity and recovery from the maker's own outcome history (the experienced analyst's feel, made explicit), and files disputes in bulk where expected recovery beats the retailer's tolerance for weak claims. It learns per retailer which evidence wins.

## Target Customer
Controllers at general-merchandise makers with thousands of small deductions a year.

## Impact If Built
For a $50M maker, recovering small invalid claims now written off by policy is worth ~$30K–$60K a year (estimate) without new headcount, and the maker learns which small claims recur.
