# Fragrance Houses' Home-Care Category & IFRA Compliance Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/consumer-products-makers|Consumer Products Makers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The large fragrance houses (Givaudan, IFF, dsm-firmenich, Symrise) and specialist candle-fragrance suppliers run consumer research, scent-trend work, application labs that test fragrance in wax bases, and regulatory teams that keep every fragrance within the IFRA Standards for category 12 products such as candles. The insight is given away to sell fragrance oil.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Tens to a few hundred consumer, application and regulatory staff across home care at each major house, of which candles are a slice (estimate)
**Output:** Scent briefs and trend decks, application test results in candle bases, IFRA certificates and safety data sheets
**Proprietary data:** Fragrance formulas, olfactive consumer tests and years of burn and throw tests across wax systems
**Clock:** IFRA amendment dates: the 51st Amendment's restrictions applied to existing fragrance creations from 30 October 2025, and a 52nd is expected in January 2027 (search summary); these are due dates on the houses' own reformulation work
**Buyer:** Head of Home Care Category Insight or Regulatory Affairs at a fragrance house; four majors plus dozens of mid-sized and candle-specialist fragrance suppliers
