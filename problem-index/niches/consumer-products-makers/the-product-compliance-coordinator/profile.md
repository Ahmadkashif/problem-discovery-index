# The Product Compliance Coordinator

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/consumer-products-makers|Consumer Products Makers]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to have every SKU's certificate, test report and warning ready before listing and import, and whoever clears the most SKUs to listing without a compliance hold takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M a year of compliance staff time, test coordination and software at makers in the four codes (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — shared drives, spreadsheets and email with labs and retailers
**Target Buyer:** Director of Quality & Compliance, or the owner at smaller makers
**Automation Potential:** High for document work; low for the test judgement itself

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
At most makers one person keeps every SKU's compliance file: General Certificates of Conformity for candles with metal-cored wicks, lighters and art materials; third-party test reports to ASTM F2417 and F2058 for retailers; IFRA certificates and safety data sheets for fragrances; Prop 65 warning decisions; and retailer chemical registrations. From 8 July 2026 certificate data must also be eFiled at import for every CPSC-regulated product. Regulatory-content vendors, testing labs, customs brokers and Prop 65 counsel each cover a slice, and the coordinator stitches them together. The tool or service that wins is the one that gets the most SKUs listed without a hold.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Regulatory-intelligence subscriptions (scored in the vault as [[niches/testing-laboratories/product-compliance-regulatory-intelligence/profile|🔍 Product Compliance Regulatory Intelligence]]), lab portals, broker eFiling, outside counsel. The gaps: no per-SKU file linking recipe, components, tests, certificates and warnings; manual re-work for each retailer; no trigger when a supplier changes a formula.
