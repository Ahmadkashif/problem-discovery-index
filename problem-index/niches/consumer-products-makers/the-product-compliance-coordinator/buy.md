# Regulatory-Content Subscriptions Mapped to a Maker's Catalogue

**Niche:** [[niches/consumer-products-makers/the-product-compliance-coordinator/profile|The Product Compliance Coordinator]]
**Industry:** [[industries/consumer-products-makers|Consumer Products Makers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Regulatory-intelligence services already track product rules worldwide; a small maker needs them filtered to its products and joined to its SKUs.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #compliance #data-integration #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to have every SKU's certificate, test report and warning ready before listing and import, and whoever clears the most SKUs to listing without a compliance hold takes the account.

## The Problem
A coordinator subscribes to regulatory alerts and receives far more than applies to candles, pens or brushes: electronics, cosmetics, packaging and chemical notices from many countries. Each relevant alert then has to be traced by hand to the SKUs it touches, through formulas, components and supplier declarations kept in different places.

## What Already Exists
Regulatory-content services such as Enhesa, 3E and Compliance & Risks track product and chemical rules worldwide, and the large testing labs run update services of their own; the vendor field is mapped in the indexed pocket [[niches/testing-laboratories/product-compliance-regulatory-intelligence/profile|🔍 Product Compliance Regulatory Intelligence]].

## The Customization Gap
A filter by product type and material that drops irrelevant alerts; a map from each alert to the maker's SKUs through recipes and bills of materials; rule packs for candles (lead-wick ban, ASTM F2417 and F2058), lighters (16 CFR 1210), art materials (ASTM D4236) and fragranced goods (IFRA, Prop 65); and tasks created for the tests, certificates or label changes each alert implies, with due dates.

## Target Customer
Coordinators at makers too small for an enterprise compliance platform.

## Impact If Solved
Alert triage falls from about a day a week to about an hour (estimate), and each relevant change arrives as a short list of affected SKUs with the action each needs.
