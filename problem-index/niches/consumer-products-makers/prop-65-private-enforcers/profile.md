# Prop 65 Private Enforcers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/consumer-products-makers|Consumer Products Makers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Private organisations that buy consumer products, have them tested for listed chemicals such as lead, and send 60-day notices that usually end in settlements; Settlement Insight counts 168 enforcers since 2016, with the ten most active collecting about 60% of fees. Their income is settlement payments and attorney's fees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 1–10 people per enforcer, with testing outsourced to labs (estimate)
**Output:** Notices and settlements
**Proprietary data:** Their own test results and notice history; notices and settlements are public
**Clock:** The statutory 60-day notice period, which they start
**Buyer:** The enforcer's principal or counsel
