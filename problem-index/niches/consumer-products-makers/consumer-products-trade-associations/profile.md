# Candle, Brush, Writing Instrument & Casket Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/consumer-products-makers|Consumer Products Makers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The trade associations of this industry's product groups: the National Candle Association (whose members make about 80% of US candles and which publishes the facts most cited for the trade, including a 2013 retail sales estimate), the American Brush Manufacturers Association, the Writing Instrument Manufacturers Association, and the Casket & Funeral Supply Association of America. They run standards participation (NCA in ASTM's candle subcommittee), meetings and member surveys, paid by dues.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 staff each, often run by management firms (estimate)
**Output:** Member communications, safety information, occasional surveys and standards input
**Proprietary data:** Member lists and small survey series
**Clock:** Meeting and standards-committee calendars
**Buyer:** Executive director
