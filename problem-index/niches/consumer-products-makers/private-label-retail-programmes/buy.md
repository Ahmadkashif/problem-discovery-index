# Retailer Spec Portals Mirrored Inside the Plant

**Niche:** [[niches/consumer-products-makers/private-label-retail-programmes/profile|Private-Label Programmes for Mass Retailers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/consumer-products-makers|Consumer Products Makers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Retailers already manage own-brand specifications and artwork in portals; the maker needs that record pushed into its own production and inspection steps.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #compliance #data-integration #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a retailer's own-brand programme through its annual re-bid, and whoever ships the retailer's specification with the lowest defect-and-return rate takes the account.

## The Problem
Each retailer keeps the approved specification, artwork and test requirements for its own-brand items in its own portal. The maker downloads PDFs and re-keys the critical dimensions, fill weights, label copy, pack counts and test limits into work instructions and inspection sheets, by hand, for each retailer and each revision. A maker with three own-brand programmes may carry several hundred live specifications (estimate), and the plant often works from a printout that was current when someone last checked.

## What Already Exists
Retailer-side PLM and artwork platforms (Trace One and in-house systems) hold the master specification and approval history; maker-side quality management software and digital work-instruction tools run inspections and nonconformance records.

## The Customization Gap
Extraction of each retailer's spec PDFs into structured inspection points; version tracking so a revision changes the line instruction the same day; retailer-specific test protocols (ASTM F2417 for candles, drop and pull tests for tools, cap-off dry-out tests for markers) attached to lots; and evidence packs formatted the way each retailer's quality team asks for them at audit and re-bid.

## Target Customer
Quality and plant managers at mid-sized private-label makers supplying two or more retailer own-brand programmes.

## Impact If Solved
Re-keying drops by ~2–3 hours per specification revision (estimate), and shipments built to a superseded spec, each a refused load or chargeback worth ~$20K–$50K (estimate), fall toward zero.
