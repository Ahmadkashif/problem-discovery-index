# Consumer Products Liability & Recall Underwriters

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/consumer-products-makers|Consumer Products Makers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Insurers and specialist underwriters that write general and products liability and product recall cover for consumer goods makers and importers, pricing exposures such as candle fires, lighter malfunctions and art-material toxicity, and handling claims. Makers pay premiums, often because retailers require certificates of insurance before the first order.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10–40 underwriters, risk engineers and claims specialists per carrier writing the class (estimate), all production: they price and handle individual accounts and claims, and no separate research function of 10 sits above them
**Output:** Underwriting decisions and premiums; risk-control recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Each carrier's own claims and loss history by product class
**Clock:** Policy renewal dates, which are client clocks
**Buyer:** Head of products liability underwriting at a carrier
