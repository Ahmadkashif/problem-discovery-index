# Art Materials Toxicology Certification

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/consumer-products-makers|Consumer Products Makers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Art & Creative Materials Institute certifies art materials with its AP and CL seals after board-certified toxicologists review each formulation against ASTM D4236 and the federal hazardous art materials labelling law, overseen by a toxicological advisory board. Member manufacturers of pens, markers, crayons, paints and craft supplies pay for certification.

## Insight Function
**Size:** A few contracted toxicologists and staff (estimate)
**Output:** Certification decisions and required label statements
**Proprietary data:** Confidential member formulations and review history
**Clock:** Product launch dates set by members
**Buyer:** ACMI's executive director
