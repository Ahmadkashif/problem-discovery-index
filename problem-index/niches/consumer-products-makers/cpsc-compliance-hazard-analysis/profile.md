# CPSC Compliance & Hazard Analysis

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/consumer-products-makers|Consumer Products Makers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Consumer Product Safety Commission's compliance, epidemiology and import-surveillance staff analyse injury and incident data, investigate defects, negotiate recalls and target shipments at ports. For this industry they enforce the 2003 lead-wick ban, child-resistant lighter rules and art-material labelling, track candle incidents with the ASTM F15.45 subcommittee (quarterly incident meetings logged by CPSC staff, search summary), and since 8 July 2026 receive certificate data eFiled at entry.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Hundreds across compliance, epidemiology and port surveillance (estimate)
**Output:** Recalls and corrective actions, hazard analyses, the national injury surveillance estimates, import targeting
**Proprietary data:** Incident and injury reports, firms' defect reports, hospital emergency-department surveillance data, and now certificate data at entry
**Clock:** Statutory and negotiated timelines on firms; the eFiling start date shapes demand but is not a due date on the agency's analysis
**Buyer:** Director of the Office of Compliance and Field Operations (a single federal buyer)
