# Deduction Recovery Software & Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/consumer-products-makers|Consumer Products Makers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Software and service firms that retrieve, validate and dispute retailer deductions for consumer goods makers: Confido and Glimpse (New York), SPS Revenue Recovery (SupplyPike and Carbon6), HighRadius's deductions module, iNymbus, and contingency recovery firms paid a share of what they collect. Makers pay subscriptions or recovery fees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 people per vendor on benchmark and root-cause analysis (estimate); the dispute analysts, claim processors and automation agents doing per-deduction work are production
**Output:** Filed disputes and recovered credits (the invoice), with root-cause dashboards attached
**Proprietary data:** Dispute outcomes by retailer and reason code across clients, held under each client's service agreement
**Clock:** Retailers' dispute windows, which are client clocks
**Buyer:** Controller or CFO at the maker
