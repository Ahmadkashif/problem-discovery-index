# Consumer Products Roll-Up & PE Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/consumer-products-makers|Consumer Products Makers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Private equity firms and corporate owners that buy and combine makers in this industry, such as LongRange Capital, which bought Batesville from Hillenbrand in 2023 for $761.5M, and diversified owners of candle, writing-instrument and cleaning-tool brands. Their deal and integration teams screen targets and track portfolio performance.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 2–8 deal and portfolio staff per platform (estimate)
**Output:** Investment memos, integration plans, portfolio reviews (internal)
**Proprietary data:** Portfolio company financials and deal files
**Clock:** Deal timetables and lender reporting
**Buyer:** Operating partner or head of corporate development
