# Senior Living Referral & Placement Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/assisted-living-communities|Assisted Living Communities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
A Place for Mom (~14,000 communities in its network), Caring.com and local placement agents (named from recall beyond APFM) advise families searching for assisted living and memory care and are paid by the community when a family moves in, about the first month's rent and care in APFM's own description (Senate Aging Committee letter, 2024). Their marketplace and data teams model which communities families will choose, route leads, and publish consumer guides and awards.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 data science and marketplace analytics staff at the largest platform (estimate); family advisors are production
**Output:** Lead routing, community recommendations, consumer content and awards; the invoice is the placement fee
**Proprietary data:** Family inquiries, needs, budgets, tours and move-ins across thousands of communities, plus consumer reviews
**Clock:** None set by a third party
**Buyer:** Chief Product or Chief Data Officer at the platform
