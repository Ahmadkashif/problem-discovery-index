# Medicaid Waiver Billing & Level-of-Care Reassessments

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/assisted-living-communities|Assisted Living Communities]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep each waiver resident's authorisation current and the month's claim paid on first submission, and whoever files every reassessment and reauthorisation before it lapses takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$250M a year in business-office and nursing time, billing software and outsourced billing spent on Medicaid waiver residents (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.3% of NAICS 6233 revenue (estimate); a far larger share of revenue at communities with many waiver residents
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — claims are electronic, but service plans, state assessments, reauthorisations and managed care portals are tracked by hand
**Target Buyer:** Business office manager and executive director at Medicaid-heavy communities; revenue cycle director at a regional chain
**Automation Potential:** High — deadline tracking, packet assembly, claim scrubbing and denial follow-up are rule-bound

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Medicaid pays for services in assisted living, never room and board, for ~200,000 residents, about one in five, through programmes in 41 of 47 responding states (KFF, 2025). Each waiver resident has a state or managed care level-of-care assessment, a person-centred service plan, an authorised rate, and annual or semi-annual reassessment dates. Where long-term services are run by managed care organisations, the community bills each plan in its own format. A lapsed authorisation means care delivered and not paid. This residential, per-diem billing is different from the visit-based, EVV-driven waiver work of home care agencies in [[niches/home-health-agencies/medicaid-waiver-personal-care/profile|🟠 Medicaid Waiver Personal Care Programs]], and from waiver group homes for people with intellectual and developmental disabilities, which belong to [[niches/idd-group-homes/residential-habilitation-billing/profile|Residential Habilitation Billing & ISP Documentation]] under [[industries/idd-group-homes|IDD Group Homes]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
EHR billing modules, billing outsourcers (Sunbound among them), state portals and managed care portals. Mon Ami sells case management and billing systems to the public agencies on the other side of the transaction. Gaps: no single calendar of every resident's reassessment and authorisation dates across payers, no pre-check that the service plan matches the authorisation, and denials worked one by one.
