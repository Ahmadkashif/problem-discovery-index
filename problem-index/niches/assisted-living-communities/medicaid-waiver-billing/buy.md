# Home-Care Waiver Billing Tools Adapted to Residential Per Diems

**Niche:** [[niches/assisted-living-communities/medicaid-waiver-billing/profile|Medicaid Waiver Billing & Level-of-Care Reassessments]]
**Industry:** [[industries/assisted-living-communities|Assisted Living Communities]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Waiver billing software was built for home care visits and EVV; assisted living bills a daily rate for services while the resident pays room and board separately.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #feature-engineering #compliance #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep each waiver resident's authorisation current and the month's claim paid on first submission, and whoever files every reassessment and reauthorisation before it lapses takes the account.

## The Problem
Communities bill waiver services through tools that expect visit logs, or through state portals by hand, while separately invoicing the resident or family for room and board under state caps.

## What Already Exists
Home care waiver platforms with authorisation tracking, EVV and claims; EHR billing modules; clearinghouses.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: (1) per-diem or tiered daily rates rather than visits; (2) the split between Medicaid services and resident-paid room and board, with state limits; (3) leave-of-absence and hospital-hold rules; (4) each state's assisted living service categories and plan-specific codes; (5) reassessment and service-plan dates as billing prerequisites.

## Target Customer
Regional operators with Medicaid units in several states; billing outsourcers serving them.

## Impact If Solved
Cutting first-pass denials on waiver claims from ~10% to ~3% (estimate) saves the business office several hours a month per community and shortens cash collection by weeks.
