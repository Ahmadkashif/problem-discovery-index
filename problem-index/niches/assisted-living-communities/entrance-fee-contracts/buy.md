# Deferred-Revenue Accounting Fitted to Type A, B and C Contracts

**Niche:** [[niches/assisted-living-communities/entrance-fee-contracts/profile|Entrance-Fee Contracts & Refund Liabilities]]
**Industry:** [[industries/assisted-living-communities|Assisted Living Communities]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Accounting systems book deferred revenue; a life plan community's entrance fees also carry refund promises, care obligations and resale contingencies.
**Tags:** #survival-analysis #probability-distributions #data-integration #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to price entrance fees and refund terms the community's reserves can honour, and whoever keeps the refund liability fully covered by reserves at every actuarial study takes the account.

## The Problem
Business offices amortise entrance fees, track refunds owed and estimate the obligation to provide future services in spreadsheets beside the general ledger.

## What Already Exists
General ledgers and senior living financial suites with deferred-revenue schedules; CPA firms' year-end workpapers for refundable and non-refundable fees and the future service obligation.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: (1) contract terms per resident (refund percentage, declining-balance schedule, resale contingency); (2) amortisation over actuarially estimated remaining life rather than a fixed schedule; (3) a refund queue tied to unit resales; (4) future service obligation inputs refreshed from census and cost data; (5) state disclosure and escrow reports where required.

## Target Customer
Controllers and CFOs at life plan communities.

## Impact If Solved
Moving year-end entrance-fee schedules out of spreadsheets saves ~2–4 weeks of controller and auditor time a year (estimate) and gives the board a monthly view of refund obligations.
