# Owned-Lead Response & Tour Conversion

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/assisted-living-communities|Assisted Living Communities]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get an adult child who has just inquired onto a tour, and whoever converts the highest share of inquiries to tours takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.5B a year in sales counselor time, CRM and marketing automation, call centres and response tools for leads the community generates itself (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3.2% of NAICS 6233 revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — every chain has a CRM and web forms, but evening and weekend inquiries wait for office hours, and follow-up depends on each counselor's habits
**Target Buyer:** VP of Sales & Marketing; regional sales director
**Automation Potential:** High for first response, qualification, scheduling and nurture; the tour itself and the family conversation stay human

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Most families inquire within days of a crisis and contact several communities at once. Aline's Q2 2026 benchmark put assisted living inquiry-to-tour at ~30% and tour-to-move-in at ~46% (search summary), so the tour is the hinge. Benchmark Senior Living reported raising inquiry-to-move-in from 10% to 13% by focusing on the leads most likely to convert (Senior Housing News, search summary). Paid lead aggregators supply many inquiries but convert poorly (Bild & Co.: 32% of inquiries, ~4% conversion, search summary). Speed decides whether the family is still looking when the community calls back. The contest is distinct from who gets credit for the lead, which is [[niches/assisted-living-communities/referral-agency-placements/profile|🎯 Referral Agency Placements & Fee Disputes]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Aline's CRM and Aline Intelligence, Yardi's CRM, marketing automation, after-hours [[industries/answering-services|Answering Services]] and new voice-AI agents. Gaps: weekend and evening inquiries answered on Monday, generic nurture emails, no ranking of which families to call first, and tours booked by phone tag.
