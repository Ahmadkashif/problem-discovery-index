# CRM Lead Scoring Fitted to Crisis-Timed Decisions

**Niche:** [[niches/assisted-living-communities/owned-lead-tour-conversion/profile|Owned-Lead Response & Tour Conversion]]
**Industry:** [[industries/assisted-living-communities|Assisted Living Communities]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Generic CRM lead scores reward engagement; in assisted living the family that will move in next week may never open an email.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #logistic-regression #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get an adult child who has just inquired onto a tour, and whoever converts the highest share of inquiries to tours takes the account.

## The Problem
Counselors with 100–300 open leads (estimate) call in the order the CRM lists them, usually newest first or by last activity, not by who is closest to moving in.

## What Already Exists
CRM-native predictive lead scoring (Salesforce, HubSpot) trained on clicks, opens and form fills, and senior living CRMs with basic stage-based prioritisation.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs features that matter here: (1) the trigger event (hospital discharge date, recent fall, spouse's death); (2) who is calling and whether the resident agrees; (3) care type and whether the community can serve the need; (4) house sale or long-term care insurance timing; (5) a 60-day move-in label rather than an "opportunity" stage; (6) separate models by care type, since memory care families decide faster.

## Target Customer
Regional sales directors managing several counselors; CRM vendors serving senior living.

## Impact If Solved
Calling the top-scored decile first lifts tour conversion by ~3–5 points (estimate), worth ~1–2 move-ins a year per community, ~$6,000–12,000 of revenue from shorter vacancies.
