# A 24/7 Inquiry Agent That Books the Tour

**Niche:** [[niches/assisted-living-communities/owned-lead-tour-conversion/profile|Owned-Lead Response & Tour Conversion]]
**Industry:** [[industries/assisted-living-communities|Assisted Living Communities]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A family calling at 8 p.m. after a fall wants a person who understands, and an appointment; most communities offer voicemail.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #gradient-boosting #workflow-orchestration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get an adult child who has just inquired onto a tour, and whoever converts the highest share of inquiries to tours takes the account.

## The Problem
Inquiries arrive in the evening and at weekends, when adult children have time. Answering services take a message. By Monday the family has toured two other communities.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Until voice LLMs, an automated first conversation about a parent's decline sounded cold, and families hung up. CRMs were built to record counselors' work, not to do it.

## What to Build
A voice and text agent that answers inquiries around the clock in the community's name. It asks about what happened, care needs, the decision-maker, timing and budget, explains care levels and pricing ranges, and books a tour into the counselor's calendar while the family is on the line. It writes a summary and propensity score into the CRM and hands anything sensitive (a crisis discharge today, a complaint) to an on-call person.

## Target Customer
Sales leaders at chains and regional operators; CRM vendors adding agents, such as Aline, which lists a Software Engineer (AI & Agentic Solutions) on its own careers page (checked 2026-10-24).

## Impact If Built
If after-hours inquiries are ~30–40% of the total (estimate) and same-day tour booking lifts their tour rate by 5 points, an 80-unit community gains ~2–3 move-ins a year, ~$12,000–37,000 of revenue from shorter vacancies at ~$6,200 a month (estimate).
