# State CCRC Financial Regulators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/assisted-living-communities|Assisted Living Communities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
In states that regulate continuing care contracts, an insurance department or social services agency registers life plan communities, reviews their disclosure statements, audited financials, reserve and escrow arrangements and, in some states, periodic actuarial reports (recalled; the states and intervals vary). They are the only financial check on refund promises outside the bond market.

## Insight Function
**Size:** A few financial analysts or examiners per state programme, under 10 (estimate)
**Output:** Certificates of authority, filing reviews, occasional enforcement
**Proprietary data:** Annual filings and actuarial reports, partly public
**Clock:** Annual filing deadlines and statutory review intervals
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — state agencies
