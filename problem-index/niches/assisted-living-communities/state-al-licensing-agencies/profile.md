# State Assisted Living Licensing Agencies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/assisted-living-communities|Assisted Living Communities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Each state's licensing agency (for example, California's Community Care Licensing Division for residential care facilities for the elderly) licenses assisted living communities, inspects them on its own cycle and after complaints, receives incident reports and issues citations and fines. Assisted living has no federal survey, so these agencies are the only regulator. Many publish inspection reports; the Washington Post found 19 states that publish no downloadable reports.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Inspectors by the dozen or hundred in large states, who are production staff; analytic and policy staff are a few people per state (estimate)
**Output:** Licences, inspection reports, citations, rule changes
**Proprietary data:** Inspection, complaint and incident records, published in part
**Clock:** State inspection intervals and complaint-investigation timelines
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — state agencies
