# Operator Revenue Management & Sales Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/assisted-living-communities|Assisted Living Communities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The largest owner and lessee operators, such as Brookdale (541 communities in 41 states in June 2026) and Sonida, plus private chains, run corporate teams that set street rates and annual increases, forecast census, analyse sales funnels and referral-source costs, and underwrite acquisitions. Their output steers pricing and capital inside the company and feeds investor reporting; Brookdale is ending monthly occupancy reporting after December 2026.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 revenue management, sales analytics, finance and corporate development staff per large operator (estimate)
**Output:** Rate sheets and increase plans, census forecasts, sales performance reviews, acquisition underwriting
**Proprietary data:** Rents, care fees, inquiries, tours, move-ins and move-outs across the operator's own communities
**Clock:** Self-set monthly and quarterly reporting; public operators' earnings dates apply to the company, not to this analysis
**Buyer:** No external buyer — internal cost centre (CFO / Chief Revenue Officer)

**Kill switches:** none for owner and lessee operators, whose rent and sales data are their own. The third-party management half of the field (operators running REIT-owned communities under management agreements) analyses the owner's data and would carry a "data isn't theirs" switch; it is scored here on owners and lessees only.
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. A buyer for pricing and forecasting tools, not an insight business. Scored below the nursing-home operators' clinical and reimbursement groups ([[niches/skilled-nursing-facilities/multi-facility-operator-analytics/profile|🔍 Multi-Facility Operator Clinical & Reimbursement Analytics]], 43, kill switch) because there is no CMS payment or quality calendar (Q4 2) and the teams work on rent and sales data rather than full clinical records (Q3 4).
