# CCRC Actuarial & Entrance-Fee Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/assisted-living-communities|Assisted Living Communities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Consulting actuaries prepare the actuarial studies life plan communities need for entrance-fee pricing, reserve adequacy, refund liabilities and future service obligations, under ASOP No. 3. Some states require a study every three or five years for care-inclusive contracts (American Academy of Actuaries). Communities pay per study; boards, auditors, bondholders and state regulators read them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** A handful of credentialed actuaries per specialist practice, under 10 in each (estimate)
**Output:** Actuarial studies and opinions, pricing analyses
**Proprietary data:** Resident census, contract terms and experience across client communities, held under engagement
**Clock:** State filing intervals, audit dates and bond financings
**Buyer:** Practice leader at the consulting firm
