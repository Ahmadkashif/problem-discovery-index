# Senior Housing Association Research (Argentum, ASHA)

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/assisted-living-communities|Assisted Living Communities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Argentum and the American Seniors Housing Association (named from recall) represent private-pay senior living operators and owners. They publish operator rankings, member surveys and policy research, and lobby on state and federal rules. NCAL, the assisted living arm of AHCA, publishes facts and figures and an annual review of state regulations (18 states changed rules in 2025).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research staff at each association (estimate)
**Output:** Member surveys, rankings, policy papers, regulatory reviews
**Proprietary data:** Member survey responses; otherwise public data
**Clock:** State legislative sessions and federal policy calendars
**Buyer:** Head of research or policy — member-funded
