# Life Plan Community Credit Ratings & Bond Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/assisted-living-communities|Assisted Living Communities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Not-for-profit life plan communities borrow in the tax-exempt bond market. Fitch, S&P and Moody's rate the bonds (Fitch tracks 169 rated communities, median BBB, Senior Housing News 2026), and specialist underwriters such as Ziegler (named from recall) publish sector research and maintain databases of life plan communities to support their banking. Issuers pay for ratings; research is marketing for underwriting.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 life plan community analysts per rating agency or underwriter (estimate); Fitch's sector team is led by one named head, Margaret Johnson
**Output:** Ratings, median ratio reports, sector outlooks, underwriter research notes
**Proprietary data:** Issuer financials and disclosures, rating histories, underwriters' community databases
**Clock:** Bond pricing dates and annual surveillance
**Buyer:** Head of U.S. life plan communities at an agency; head of senior living finance at an underwriter
