# Move-In Sales & Lead Conversion

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/assisted-living-communities|Assisted Living Communities]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor is fighting to turn a family's search into a signed move-in. That produced two contests with different winners: which channel gets credit and a fee for the family, settled by whoever holds a first-contact record both sides accept, and what share of the inquiries it already owns the community turns into tours, won by sales teams and response tools. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3.5B a year in sales counselors, marketing, CRM software and referral-agency fees (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~4.5% of NAICS 6233 revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — chains all run a CRM, but tours, follow-up calls and referral reconciliation still run on phone calls, sticky notes and the counselor's memory
**Target Buyer:** VP of Sales & Marketing at a chain; executive director at an independent
**Automation Potential:** Medium-High for first response, scheduling, scoring and attribution; the tour and the family's decision stay human

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Assisted living is mostly private pay, so occupancy is the business. An 80-unit community needs ~30–40 move-ins a year just to stand still (estimate, from a ~22-month average stay). The buyer is usually an adult child deciding within weeks of a fall or hospital stay, comparing three or four communities, often handed to them by a referral agency. A Place for Mom lists ~14,000 communities and is paid about a month's rent and care per placement (Senate Aging Committee letter, 2024). Aline's Q2 2026 benchmark put assisted living inquiry-to-tour at ~30% and tour-to-move-in at ~46% (search summary). This is a different sale from a nursing home's hospital referral, described in [[niches/skilled-nursing-facilities/referral-admissions/profile|🟠 Hospital Referral Intake & Admissions]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Senior living CRMs (Aline's Sherpa and Enquire, Yardi's CRM, Salesforce-based builds), call tracking, referral-agency partner portals, and outsourced after-hours answering. Gaps: no first-contact record shared with agencies, slow response after hours, little scoring of which inquiries will move in, and pipeline knowledge that leaves with the counselor.
