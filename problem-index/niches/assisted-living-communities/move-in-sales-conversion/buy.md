# Marketing Attribution Fitted to a Sixty-Day Decision

**Niche:** [[niches/assisted-living-communities/move-in-sales-conversion/profile|Move-In Sales & Lead Conversion]]
**Industry:** [[industries/assisted-living-communities|Assisted Living Communities]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Marketing attribution tools credit the last click; an assisted living move-in follows weeks of searches, calls, a tour, a referral agency's list and a sibling's veto.
**Tags:** #causal-inference #logistic-regression #feature-engineering #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor is fighting to turn a family's search into a signed move-in. That produced two contests with different winners: which channel gets credit and a fee for the family, settled by whoever holds a first-contact record both sides accept, and what share of the inquiries it already owns the community turns into tours, won by sales teams and response tools. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Marketing directors split budget between search ads, the community website, paid lead aggregators and referral agencies with little idea which channel produced move-ins rather than inquiries.

## What Already Exists
Web analytics and multi-touch attribution tools, call tracking, and CRM source fields that record whichever channel the counselor typed in first.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: (1) attribution to the move-in, often 30–90 days after first contact, not to the inquiry; (2) household-level identity, because several siblings call; (3) referral-agency leads and their fees treated as a channel with a cost per move-in; (4) care type and unit revenue, since a memory care move-in is worth more; (5) offline events (tours, events, hospital and physician referrals).

## Target Customer
Marketing directors at regional chains spending ~$50,000–250,000 per community a year on marketing and fees (estimate).

## Impact If Solved
Shifting ~10% of spend from the worst to the best channel per move-in (estimate) saves ~$5,000–25,000 per community a year, or adds two or three move-ins at the same budget.
