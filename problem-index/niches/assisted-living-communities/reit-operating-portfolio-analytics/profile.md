# Senior Housing REIT Operating-Portfolio Asset Management

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/assisted-living-communities|Assisted Living Communities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Welltower and Ventas hold much of their senior housing in operating structures (Welltower's senior housing operating portfolio, Ventas's SHOP) under RIDEA, which lets a REIT keep a community's operating income through a taxable subsidiary while an independent operator manages it (recalled). The REIT, not a tenant, carries the occupancy and pricing risk, so its asset managers and data teams track every community's census, rates, labour and margins monthly, push operators on pricing, and pick acquisitions. Welltower cites a "Data Science platform" in capital allocation and Ventas credits "VentasOI" with calibrating rate and occupancy; Welltower's SHO same-store NOI grew 20.5% in Q2 2026.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–80 asset management, data science and acquisitions analysts per large REIT (estimate)
**Output:** Operator scorecards, pricing and labour guidance to operators, acquisition underwriting, investor disclosures; nothing sold
**Proprietary data:** Monthly property-level operating results for hundreds of communities, delivered by managers under management agreements for exactly this use
**Clock:** Self-set monthly operator reviews and the REIT's own acquisition closing dates; the REIT's quarterly earnings and SEC filing dates bind the company, not this analysis
**Buyer:** Chief Investment Officer — no external buyer for the analysis
