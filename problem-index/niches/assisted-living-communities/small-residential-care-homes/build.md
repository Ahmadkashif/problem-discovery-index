# A Shared Relief Pool Across Small Homes

**Niche:** [[niches/assisted-living-communities/small-residential-care-homes/profile|Small Residential Care Homes]]
**Industry:** [[industries/assisted-living-communities|Assisted Living Communities]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A chain has a float pool; a six-bed home has the owner.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #gradient-boosting #workflow-orchestration #worker-facing #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to take the owner-operator off the night and weekend schedule of a six-to-sixteen-bed home, and whoever covers the home's round-the-clock staffing requirement with the fewest owner hours takes the account.

## The Problem
Small homes need trained, state-cleared caregivers on site around the clock. A call-off at 9 p.m. leaves the owner to cover or break the rules. Agencies rarely send staff for one shift at a six-bed home, and those that do charge premium rates.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Per-diem marketplaces target nursing homes and hospitals with dozens of shifts a week. Small homes are too small individually, and each state's training and background-check rules differ, so a pool must be local and credential-aware.

## What to Build
A cooperative relief pool for small homes within a few miles of each other. Caregivers are pre-cleared once against the state's training, background and TB requirements, oriented to each member home, and offered open shifts by text in order of familiarity. Call-off forecasting from each home's history lets the pool pre-book cover for high-risk shifts. Billing between homes is automatic.

## Target Customer
Owner-operators of one to five small homes; state associations of small residential care providers that could sponsor pools.

## Impact If Built
If the owner covers ~8–12 overnight shifts a month today (estimate) and the pool takes half, she reclaims ~50–70 hours a month; at ~$17 an hour (BLS mean for aides) the shifts cost ~$800–1,200 a month, far below the value of an owner's time and health.
