# Long-Term Care Cost-of-Care Surveys

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/assisted-living-communities|Assisted Living Communities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
CareScout, Genworth's long-term care services unit, publishes the annual Cost of Care Survey: median prices for assisted living, nursing homes, adult day and home care by metropolitan area, gathered from providers between July and November each year (2025 national assisted living median $6,200 a month). Consumers, financial planners, insurers and reporters use it free; it supports the parent's insurance and care-navigation businesses rather than being sold.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research staff on the survey (estimate); provider calls and data collection are production
**Output:** An annual public price survey and calculator
**Proprietary data:** Provider-reported prices by metro area and care type over two decades
**Clock:** Self-set annual release
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer for the survey itself
