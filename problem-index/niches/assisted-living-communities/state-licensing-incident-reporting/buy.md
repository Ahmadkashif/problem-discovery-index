# Nursing-Home Survey Tools Rewritten for State Assisted Living Rules

**Niche:** [[niches/assisted-living-communities/state-licensing-incident-reporting/profile|State Licensing Inspections & Incident Reporting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/assisted-living-communities|Assisted Living Communities]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Survey-readiness software is built around the federal nursing-home F-tags; assisted living answers to fifty different state codes.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #compliance #data-integration #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a community out of a state citation, and whoever gets every reportable incident filed inside the state's deadline takes the account.

## The Problem
Directors prepare for unannounced state visits with generic checklists that miss what their own state's inspectors actually cite.

## What Already Exists
Nursing-home survey-readiness tools and mock-survey services built on federal F-tags and the public CMS-2567 record; policy-manual subscriptions for assisted living.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: (1) each state's regulation sections in place of F-tags; (2) the state's public inspection history, parsed into the citations most often written; (3) memory care and medication-aide rules that apply only in some states; (4) the state's own plan-of-correction format and deadlines; (5) self-audit checklists generated per state and per community type.

## Target Customer
Executive directors and regional compliance nurses at chains operating in several states.

## Impact If Solved
Focusing self-audits on a state's ten most-cited areas (estimate) could avoid one or two citations per community per cycle, each costing ~10–20 hours of correction work and, in some states, a fine (estimate).
