# Resident Acuity Assessments & Level-of-Care Billing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/assisted-living-communities|Assisted Living Communities]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to bill each resident for the care staff actually deliver, and whoever turns caregivers' daily documentation into an evidenced level-of-care change before the next invoice takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.5B a year in wellness-nurse assessment time, business-office billing work, assessment and billing software, and care-pricing consultants (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% of NAICS 6233 revenue (estimate); the care fees it sets are a much larger slice of revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — assessment tools sit in the EHR at most chains, but scoring happens on a schedule, care delivered is logged as checkboxes, and many independents still use paper points sheets
**Target Buyer:** VP of Clinical Services and the CFO at a chain; executive director and wellness director at an independent
**Automation Potential:** Medium-High — evidence gathering, drift detection and notices automate; the assessment and the conversation with the family stay with the nurse

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Most assisted living residents pay base rent plus a care fee set by a nurse's assessment: a points system or a tiered level that prices help with bathing, dressing, medications, mobility and behaviours (operator practice). The residents are frail: 62% need help with three or more daily activities and 44% have a dementia (NCHS, 2022). Needs rise between scheduled reassessments, so aides deliver care the resident does not yet pay for. This is the in-building half of the tacit judgement in the industry's high-impact note; the move-in assessment is where it starts. Medicaid waiver residents are assessed by the state's tool instead and sit in [[niches/assisted-living-communities/medicaid-waiver-billing/profile|⚡ Medicaid Waiver Billing & Level-of-Care Reassessments]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Assessment and service-plan modules in Yardi, PointClickCare, August Health and Aline; caregiver point-of-care apps; billing outsourcers such as Sunbound. Gaps: no link from logged care, incidents and medication changes to a proposed level change; reassessments triggered by the calendar; family notices drafted by hand.
