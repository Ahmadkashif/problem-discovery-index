# Call Tracking Fitted to Agency Registration Rules

**Niche:** [[niches/assisted-living-communities/referral-agency-placements/profile|Referral Agency Placements & Fee Disputes]]
**Industry:** [[industries/assisted-living-communities|Assisted Living Communities]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Call-tracking tools tell a marketer which ad rang the phone; a community also needs to know whether that caller was already registered by an agency.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #data-integration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to decide which move-ins a referral agency is owed a fee for, and whoever holds the timestamped first-contact record that both the community and the agency accept takes the account.

## The Problem
Agencies' contracts define when a referral "counts": registration windows, exclusivity periods, rules on families already in the community's database. Counselors rarely check them before touring.

## What Already Exists
Call-tracking and recording platforms with dynamic numbers, transcription and CRM integration, used widely in senior living marketing.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: (1) each agency's registration and lookback rules encoded per contract; (2) a check at first call against existing CRM households and open agency referrals; (3) a prompt to the counselor to ask and record the referral source; (4) an alert when an agency notification arrives for a household already known; (5) a monthly reconciliation of agency invoices against the record.

## Target Customer
Sales operations managers at chains working with several agencies.

## Impact If Solved
Catching duplicates at first contact rather than at invoice time cuts dispute workload by ~half (estimate) and avoids ~2–4 unnecessary fees per community a year, ~$12,000–24,000.
