# Senior Living Software Data & Benchmark Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/assisted-living-communities|Assisted Living Communities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The data and analytics groups at senior living software vendors, including Aline (CRM, clinical, dining and billing; publishes quarterly sales and marketing benchmarks), Yardi Senior Living, PointClickCare Senior Living and August Health (EHR, move-ins, billing and Insights), build in-product dashboards, benchmarks and new AI features from the records their customers keep in their systems. Aline's benchmark reports inquiry-to-tour and tour-to-move-in rates by care type and lead source. Operators pay for the software; the benchmarks are marketing and product features.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–50 data, analytics and AI staff per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** In-product analytics, published benchmarks, AI features such as Aline Intelligence
**Proprietary data:** Leads, tours, move-ins, care records, medication administration and billing for thousands of communities, held under customers' service agreements
**Clock:** Self-set product releases and quarterly benchmark publication
**Buyer:** Chief Product or Chief Data Officer at the vendor
