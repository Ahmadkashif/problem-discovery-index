# Senior Living Valuation & Feasibility Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/assisted-living-communities|Assisted Living Communities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Seniors housing valuation groups at the national brokerages and appraisal firms, and the senior living practices of CPA firms such as CliftonLarsonAllen, Forvis Mazars and Plante Moran (named from recall), value communities for lenders and buyers and write the market and financial feasibility studies that lenders, bond underwriters and boards require before a community is built, expanded or refinanced. Studies for not-for-profit life plan communities are often printed in the bond offering document. Lenders, developers and borrowers pay per engagement.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 appraisers, market analysts and feasibility consultants per specialist practice (estimate)
**Output:** Appraisals, market studies, financial feasibility studies and forecasts
**Proprietary data:** Transaction comparables, operating statements and fill-up histories gathered over years of engagements, in a market where little operating data is public
**Clock:** Loan closings, bond pricing dates and board approvals, set by clients' financing schedules
**Buyer:** Practice leader or national director of seniors housing valuation
