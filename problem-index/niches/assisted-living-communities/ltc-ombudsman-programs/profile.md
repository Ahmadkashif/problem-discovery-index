# State Long-Term Care Ombudsman Programs

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/assisted-living-communities|Assisted Living Communities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Every state runs a long-term care ombudsman programme under the Older Americans Act (recalled), with paid staff and volunteers who visit assisted living communities and nursing homes, investigate residents' complaints and advocate for them. They report complaint data to the federal Administration for Community Living. Mon Ami, which was hiring a Senior Rails Engineer on its own jobs page in October 2026, sells an ombudsman module among its systems for aging agencies.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Local ombudsmen and volunteers are case workers, production staff; state-level data analysis is a few people (estimate)
**Output:** Complaint resolutions, annual federal reports, advocacy
**Proprietary data:** Complaint records, confidential by statute
**Clock:** Annual federal reporting
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — state units on aging
