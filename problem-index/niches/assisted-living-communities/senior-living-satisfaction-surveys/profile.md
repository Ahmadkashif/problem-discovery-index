# Resident & Family Satisfaction Survey Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/assisted-living-communities|Assisted Living Communities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms such as Pinnacle Quality Insight, Activated Insights and Holleran (named from recall) run resident, family and employee satisfaction surveys for senior living operators, often by monthly phone interviews, and sell benchmark reports and awards. Consumer ratings such as U.S. News's senior living ratings draw on resident and family surveys (recalled).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analysts per firm (estimate); interviewers and survey operations are production
**Output:** Satisfaction scores, benchmarks, comment reports, awards
**Proprietary data:** Survey responses and norms across client communities
**Clock:** Client-set survey cycles and award calendars
**Buyer:** Head of research at the survey firm
