# Long-Term Care Insurance Claims & Benefit Eligibility

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/assisted-living-communities|Assisted Living Communities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Long-term care insurers, with Genworth the largest remaining writer (recalled), pay daily or monthly benefits when a policyholder needs help with two or more daily activities or has a severe cognitive impairment (recalled), and assisted living is one of the main settings where claims are paid. Their claims, care-management and actuarial teams assess eligibility, monitor claimants and price the rate increases they file with state insurance departments on mostly closed blocks of policies. Genworth's CareScout unit also publishes the annual Cost of Care Survey (median assisted living $6,200 a month in 2025).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~50–200 actuaries, claims analysts and care-management analysts per major carrier (estimate); benefit eligibility assessors are production
**Output:** Claim decisions, reserve and morbidity studies, rate-increase filings; nothing sold as research
**Proprietary data:** Decades of claim histories by care setting, diagnosis, duration and cost
**Clock:** Insurers' own rate-filing cycles and statutory reserve reporting; claim decision timelines in state law (recalled)
**Buyer:** Chief Actuary or head of LTC claims — no external buyer for the analysis
