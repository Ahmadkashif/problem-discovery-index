**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to earn money on a molecule after the brand's exclusivity ends, but first-to-file launches and mature-molecule supply reward different things: a launch is won by whoever is first lawfully on the market with exclusivity, a mature molecule by whoever keeps shipping an awarded contract at an eroded price without a failure-to-supply penalty. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Portfolio analysts assemble each molecule's case from many sources: Orange Book patents and exclusivities, FDA's Paragraph IV list, tentative and final approvals, DMF holders, court dockets, IQVIA volumes and prices. They rebuild the same assembly for each candidate.

## What Already Exists
Clarivate's Cortellis (including Cortellis Generics Intelligence), Norstella's Citeline and Evaluate, IQVIA, GlobalData, DrugPatentWatch and PharmaCompass. The brand-facing tools are covered in [[niches/biotech-research-firms/pipeline-trial-intelligence-providers/profile|🔍 Drug Pipeline & Trial Intelligence Providers]].

## The Customization Gap
The generic view needs molecule-level, dosage-form-level records joining patent and exclusivity expiry, Paragraph IV status and first-filer identity, counts of approved and tentatively approved ANDAs, API DMF holders with their FDA inspection status, and volume and price history, refreshed as FDA posts approvals. It also needs the firm's own capability (dosage forms, sites, API sources) to filter candidates.

## Target Customer
Portfolio and business development analysts at generic makers and generic-focused CDMOs.

## Impact If Solved
Cutting each candidate's assembly from days to hours (estimate) lets a team of a few analysts screen several hundred more molecules a year.
