# Contract Analytical, Stability & Nitrosamine Testing Labs

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pharmaceutical-manufacturers|Pharmaceutical Manufacturers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
GMP contract laboratories that test drug products and ingredients for manufacturers and sponsors: release and stability testing, method development and validation, extractables and leachables, and the nitrosamine confirmatory testing FDA asked companies to complete and report progress on by 1 August 2025. Eurofins' BioPharma Product Testing network, SGS, Intertek, Element and Pace's life-sciences labs are examples. The ISO 17025 product and materials labs outside drug GMP are covered under [[industries/testing-laboratories|Testing Laboratories]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–150 method-development and impurity specialists per network (estimate); routine analysts are production and not counted
**Output:** Certificates of analysis, stability reports, validated methods, nitrosamine risk and test reports
**Proprietary data:** Method and impurity know-how across many products; the results belong to the clients
**Clock:** Clients' release, stability and filing schedules
**Buyer:** Head of scientific services
