# FDA Inspection & Enforcement Intelligence Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pharmaceutical-manufacturers|Pharmaceutical Manufacturers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Vendors that assemble FDA inspection documents (483s, establishment inspection reports, firms' responses, warning letters) mostly through Freedom of Information Act requests, convert them to searchable text, classify the findings and sell the library with investigator profiles and site risk scores. Redica Systems is the main player: about 120 staff in Pleasanton, California and Bangalore, describing itself as the largest FOIA requester of FDA documents and covering EMA, MHRA and Health Canada findings too (search summary). Quality and compliance teams at manufacturers buy it to prepare for inspections, qualify suppliers and write responses; the owner's CAPA-drafting MVP draws on the same public corpus.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–60 data analysts and quality specialists curating and classifying documents and writing analyses (estimate; the 120 is total company headcount)
**Output:** Searchable inspection document library, investigator and site profiles, citation trends, supplier risk scores, readiness reports
**Proprietary data:** A FOIA-assembled archive of released 483s, EIRs and responses that FDA publishes only in part; each document is a public record, but the assembled, classified archive is not
**Clock:** None on the vendor; clients use it before inspections FDA schedules on them, and FOIA releases arrive on FDA's timetable
**Buyer:** Chief product officer / head of data
