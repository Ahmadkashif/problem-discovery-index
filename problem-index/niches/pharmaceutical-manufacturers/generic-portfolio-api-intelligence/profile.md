# Generic Portfolio, Patent & API Sourcing Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pharmaceutical-manufacturers|Pharmaceutical Manufacturers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Databases and analyst services that tell generic makers which molecules to pursue and where to buy the API: patent and exclusivity expiries, Paragraph IV status and first filers, counts of approved and pending ANDAs, API manufacturers and their Drug Master Files, and forecasts of entry dates and price erosion. Clarivate's Cortellis Generics Intelligence (formerly Newport), GlobalData, DrugPatentWatch, PharmaCompass and the API marketplace Pharmaoffer serve this market. Portfolio, business development and sourcing teams at generic makers and CDMOs pay. The brand- and payer-facing twins are [[niches/biotech-research-firms/pipeline-trial-intelligence-providers/profile|🔍 Drug Pipeline & Trial Intelligence Providers]] (49) and [[niches/health-insurance-carriers/payer-drug-pipeline-loe-intelligence/profile|🔍 Payer Drug Pipeline & Loss-of-Exclusivity Intelligence]] (47), and this pocket follows their Q1–Q3 and Q5 logic while applying the current client-clock ruling to Q4.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20–150 analysts, patent specialists and data curators per provider (estimate)
**Output:** Molecule opportunity profiles, patent and litigation status, API supplier lists with DMF and inspection status, entry and erosion forecasts
**Proprietary data:** A curated API manufacturer database and a dated archive of the provider's own entry-date and competitor-count calls, which could be graded against what happened; inputs are public (Orange Book, FDA's Paragraph IV list, DMF lists, dockets)
**Clock:** None on the provider's own deliverable: profiles and forecasts are delivered on demand, while first possible Paragraph IV filing dates, 30-month stays and court rulings fall on the clients' filings and launches
**Buyer:** Head of product / head of generics content
