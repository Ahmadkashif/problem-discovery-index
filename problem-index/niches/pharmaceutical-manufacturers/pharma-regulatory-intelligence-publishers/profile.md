# Pharmaceutical Regulatory Intelligence Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pharmaceutical-manufacturers|Pharmaceutical Manufacturers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Subscription services that track drug regulation, guidance and agency practice across many markets and tell manufacturers what changed, what it requires for CMC, labelling, safety and GMP, and by when. Clarivate's Cortellis Regulatory Intelligence, IQVIA's regulatory intelligence services, RegASK and the intelligence arms of regulatory consultancies such as Freyr are examples. Regulatory affairs, CMC and quality teams at manufacturers pay per seat or per enterprise licence. This is the pharmaceutical branch of an archetype indexed elsewhere as [[niches/compliance-consulting/regulatory-content-publishers/profile|🔍 Regulatory Intelligence Publishers]] (55) and [[niches/testing-laboratories/product-compliance-regulatory-intelligence/profile|🔍 Product Compliance Regulatory Intelligence]] (51).

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30–200 regulatory analysts, pharmacists and translators per larger vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Curated and translated regulations and guidances, change alerts, country requirement comparisons (for example, what each market requires to register a new API source), expert summaries
**Proprietary data:** Years of maintained classification and interpretation of public regulatory text, plus subscriber questions; the inputs are public
**Clock:** None on the publisher's own deliverable: alerts are produced on demand as rules change, and health authorities' effective dates bind the clients' compliance
**Buyer:** Head of regulatory content / chief product officer
