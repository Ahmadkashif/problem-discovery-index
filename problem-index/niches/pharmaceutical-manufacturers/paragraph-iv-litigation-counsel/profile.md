# Paragraph IV Litigation Counsel & Scientific Experts

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pharmaceutical-manufacturers|Pharmaceutical Manufacturers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Law firm practices and testifying scientists who litigate Hatch-Waxman cases for generic challengers and brand owners: invalidity and non-infringement analyses, claim construction, expert reports on formulation and chemistry, trial and settlement strategy. AmLaw life-sciences IP practices, litigation boutiques and expert firms of chemists and formulators compete.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20–150 lawyers, technical advisers and experts per practice (estimate)
**Output:** Paragraph IV notice letters and detailed statements, expert reports, briefs, settlement analyses
**Proprietary data:** Case files, expert work product and protective-order material from many cases
**Clock:** Court schedules (claim construction, expert report and trial dates) and the 30-month stay, which are hard third-party deadlines on the practice's own deliverables
**Buyer:** Practice chair
