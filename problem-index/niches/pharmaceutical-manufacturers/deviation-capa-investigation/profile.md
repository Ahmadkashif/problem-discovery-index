# Deviation & CAPA Investigation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pharmaceutical-manufacturers|Pharmaceutical Manufacturers]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to close each deviation with a root cause that holds, and whoever leaves the fewest repeat deviations from causes already declared fixed takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.5B a year in QMS investigation modules, quality consulting and AI tooling aimed at deviations and CAPAs at US drug-product sites (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1% of US receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — the workflow sits in a QMS, but the investigation itself is free text written from memory and keyword searches
**Target Buyer:** Head of Quality / Site QA Director
**Automation Potential:** High for retrieval of related cases and drafting; low for the root-cause decision, which the investigator must own and sign

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every departure from procedure becomes a deviation that must be investigated, dispositioned and closed with a CAPA, usually against an internal target of ~30 days (estimate), while the affected batch waits. FDA cited failure to thoroughly investigate discrepancies (21 CFR 211.192) 164 times on drug 483s in FY2025, plus 32 times for incomplete investigation records, second only to quality-unit procedures. Repeat deviations under closed CAPAs are what investigators look for and what turn a 483 into a warning letter. This is the owner's MVP territory. The device-side twin, built on the same QMS vendors, is [[niches/medical-device-mfg/capa-investigation-workflow/profile|CAPA Investigation Workflow]]; the drug side differs in batch disposition (every deviation can hold a batch) and in FDA's drug-specific citation pattern.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Honeywell Sparta TrackWise, Veeva Vault Quality, MasterControl, ETQ and Qualio hold the workflow; Leucine markets deviation root-cause assistance and Seal deploys forward deployed engineers into GxP processes. The gaps: no system links a closed root cause to later deviations of the same failure mode, investigators search old reports by keyword, and "human error, retrain" closes cases nobody revisits.
