# QMS Workflow With No Investigation Content

**Niche:** [[niches/pharmaceutical-manufacturers/deviation-capa-investigation/profile|Deviation & CAPA Investigation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/pharmaceutical-manufacturers|Pharmaceutical Manufacturers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Every plant already owns a QMS that routes deviations perfectly and helps with none of the thinking.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #k-nearest-neighbors #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to close each deviation with a root cause that holds, and whoever leaves the fewest repeat deviations from causes already declared fixed takes the account.

## The Problem
Investigators open a deviation in the QMS, then leave it to search old reports, pull batch records, read maintenance logs and interview operators, and come back to type the narrative. The system tracks due dates and signatures.

## What Already Exists
Honeywell Sparta TrackWise, Veeva Vault Quality, MasterControl and ETQ, each with configurable deviation and CAPA workflows, reporting and trending dashboards.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is drug-plant content inside the existing workflow: retrieval of related deviations across renamed equipment, links to batch record exceptions and historian alarms for the affected lot, product-impact checklists by dosage form, and a draft checked against the language FDA uses in warning letters when it calls an investigation inadequate. It must run as a validated extension of the QMS the plant already owns, since replacing a QMS is a multi-year project.

## Target Customer
QA directors at sites that will not replace their QMS but need investigations that survive inspection.

## Impact If Solved
Cutting the search phase of each investigation from hours to minutes (estimate) gives investigators back a day or more a week across a busy site's open deviations.
