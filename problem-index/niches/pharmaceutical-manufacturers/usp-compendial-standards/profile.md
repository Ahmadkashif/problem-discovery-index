# USP Compendial Standards & Medicine Supply Map

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pharmaceutical-manufacturers|Pharmaceutical Manufacturers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The United States Pharmacopeia, a nonprofit standards body, writes the monographs and general chapters in USP–NF that federal law makes enforceable for drug quality, and sells the physical reference standards labs test against. It also runs the Medicine Supply Map, which models where drugs and their ingredients are made and how vulnerable each supply chain is. Manufacturers buy the compendium and reference standards; governments and health systems use the supply analyses.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Hundreds of scientists across standards development, reference standards and supply analysis (estimate)
**Output:** Monographs and general chapters, reference standards (physical), supply-chain vulnerability analyses
**Proprietary data:** The official compendium and its development record; the supply map combining public and licensed data
**Clock:** USP's own revision cycles
**Buyer:** Chief science officer
