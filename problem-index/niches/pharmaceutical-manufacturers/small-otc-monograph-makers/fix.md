# The Drug Amount Report Nobody Filed

**Niche:** [[niches/pharmaceutical-manufacturers/small-otc-monograph-makers/profile|Small OTC Monograph & Private-Label Makers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/pharmaceutical-manufacturers|Pharmaceutical Manufacturers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Annual drug amount reporting has been required since the 2020 CARES Act, and about half of OTC monograph products still go unreported.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #compliance #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a small maker's monograph products lawful to ship under FDA's reformed OTC system and CGMP inspection, and whoever keeps the most of a maker's SKUs on retail shelves without an FDA enforcement action takes the account.

## The Problem
Registrants must report each year how much of each listed drug they made for commercial distribution. For CY2024, reports covered 52% of OTC monograph NDCs against 80% of ANDA NDCs. FDA wrote to 9,571 registrants in October 2025 about missing reports and published a noncompliant list in March 2026, which retailers' quality teams can read.

## Why It's Still Broken
The report draws on production and shipment records in ERP or spreadsheets, must be mapped to NDCs and submitted through FDA's electronic system, and small makers often do not know it applies to them.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A tool that maps the maker's listings to its production records, computes the amounts per NDC, prepares the submission and keeps the confirmation for the retailer's audit file.

## Who Feels the Pain
Owners of small OTC makers; retailer quality teams checking suppliers; FDA's shortage staff, who use the data to see supply.

## Impact If Fixed
A filing that takes a day a year with the right tool (estimate) keeps the maker off a public noncompliance list that can cost it a retail programme.
