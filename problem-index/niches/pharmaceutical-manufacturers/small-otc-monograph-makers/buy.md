# Enterprise QMS Sized for a Small Plant

**Niche:** [[niches/pharmaceutical-manufacturers/small-otc-monograph-makers/profile|Small OTC Monograph & Private-Label Makers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/pharmaceutical-manufacturers|Pharmaceutical Manufacturers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** QMS platforms for life sciences assume a QA department and a validation team; a small OTC maker has neither.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a small maker's monograph products lawful to ship under FDA's reformed OTC system and CGMP inspection, and whoever keeps the most of a maker's SKUs on retail shelves without an FDA enforcement action takes the account.

## The Problem
Small makers keep SOPs in folders, deviations in a notebook and complaints in email, and fail inspections on the paperwork rather than the product.

## What Already Exists
Qualio, MasterControl, Veeva QualityOne and ETQ sell QMS for life sciences and consumer products, with document control, training, CAPA and audit modules.

## The Customization Gap
A small OTC maker needs a pre-validated, pre-configured package: SOP templates for monograph products, batch record templates for creams, liquids and aerosols, complaint and adverse event handling tuned to OTC reporting, retailer audit packs, and a price and setup effort a small plant can carry without a validation consultant.

## Target Customer
Quality managers, often the only one, at small OTC and private-label makers.

## Impact If Solved
A turnkey QMS could take a small maker from paper to an inspectable system in weeks rather than a year (estimate) and remove the documentation failures behind many of their 483 observations.
