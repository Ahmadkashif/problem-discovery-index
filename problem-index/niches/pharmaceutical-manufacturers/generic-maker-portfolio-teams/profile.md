# Generic Makers' Portfolio Selection & Business Development Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pharmaceutical-manufacturers|Pharmaceutical Manufacturers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The in-house teams at large generic makers (Teva, Viatris, Sandoz, Amneal, Hikma and the Indian majors) that choose which molecules to develop, file and challenge, which to in-license or acquire, and which to exit. They combine patent, market and API sourcing data with the firm's own capabilities and costs.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–60 portfolio, competitive intelligence and business development analysts per large maker (estimate)
**Output:** Molecule selection cases, first-to-file decisions, in-licensing evaluations, exit recommendations — all internal
**Proprietary data:** The firm's own development costs, launch results, prices and supply history per molecule
**Clock:** First possible Paragraph IV filing dates and patent expiries on the firm's own filings
**Buyer:** SVP portfolio strategy
