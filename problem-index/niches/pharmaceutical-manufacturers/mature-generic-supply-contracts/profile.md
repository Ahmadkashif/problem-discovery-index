# Mature Generic Supply & Shortage Exposure

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pharmaceutical-manufacturers|Pharmaceutical Manufacturers]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep shipping a wholesaler's or GPO's awarded generic at an eroded price, and whoever ships the highest share of awarded volume in full takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2B a year in supply planning, API sourcing, supplier quality and shortage-risk tooling and services for mature US generics (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1.5% of US receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — ERP and planning systems exist, but contract awards, allocation and supplier risk run on email and spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** VP Supply Chain / Head of US Generics Operations
**Automation Potential:** Medium — forecasting and risk scoring automate; exit and allocation calls stay human

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
After launch, a generic is sold through a few buyers: the big three wholesalers' sourcing programmes and GPO contracts. Awards go to the low bidder who can supply, and contracts carry failure-to-supply penalties. Prices erode toward a floor at which one quality failure or API problem ends supply. ASHP counted 227 active shortages in Q2 2026; 48% of new 2026 shortages were sole-source products, and manufacturing issues were the stated cause of 15% of 2025 shortages (59% had no stated cause). The supply base is concentrated offshore: India held 21.2% and China 14.7% of API manufacturing locations for US-based application products in 2025. Generics are excluded from the April 2026 Section 232 tariffs only "at this time", with a Commerce review due within a year.

## Current Tools & Gaps
SAP IBP, Kinaxis and o9 for planning; QMS supplier modules; [[niches/contract-manufacturing/supply-chain-risk-intelligence/profile|🔍 Multi-Tier Supply Chain Risk Intelligence]] vendors (Exiger, Resilinc) for sub-tier mapping; shortage trackers used by buyers ([[niches/pharmacy-independents/drug-shortage-supply-intelligence/profile|🔍 Drug Shortage & Supply Intelligence]]). Gap: no tool prices each molecule's supply risk against its margin, so exits happen by surprise.
