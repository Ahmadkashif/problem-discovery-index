# FDA CDER Pharmaceutical Quality Surveillance

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pharmaceutical-manufacturers|Pharmaceutical Manufacturers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The drug centre's Office of Pharmaceutical Quality and Office of Compliance analyse the global manufacturing base: a Site Catalog of 5,953 sites, a site selection model that ranks sites for surveillance inspection, quality assessment of applications, and the annual Report on the State of Pharmaceutical Quality. Investigators who inspect sites and reviewers who assess each application are production. The packaging slice of the same organisation is logged as [[niches/contract-packagers/fda-drug-packaging-oversight/profile|🔍 FDA Drug Packaging & Labelling Oversight]] (25), and the device and nonclinical counterparts as [[niches/medical-device-mfg/fda-device-review/profile|🔍 Device Review & Enforcement]] and [[niches/biotech-research-firms/fda-nonclinical-review-glp-inspection/profile|🔍 FDA Nonclinical Review & GLP Inspection]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** Tens to low hundreds of surveillance analysts and quality data staff (estimate); investigators and application assessors are production and not counted
**Output:** Inspection selection, site risk scores, the annual quality report, warning letters and import alerts
**Proprietary data:** Every inspection, application, drug amount report and quality defect report for the US market
**Clock:** Statutory and programme cycles set by Congress and the agency itself
**Buyer:** Office director — a single federal buyer
