# Pharmacovigilance Case Processing & Aggregate Safety Reporting Outsourcers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pharmaceutical-manufacturers|Pharmaceutical Manufacturers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Service providers that run drug safety for manufacturers: case intake and processing, literature screening, periodic safety reports and signal management, inside the manufacturer's safety database or their own. IQVIA, Parexel, ProPharma, Cognizant, Genpact, TCS and Accenture's life-sciences operations compete, priced per case or per full-time equivalent.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–50 signal-management and safety-science staff per larger provider (estimate); case processors and the writers who assemble each client's periodic reports product by product are production and not counted
**Output:** Signal evaluations and safety-science input to labelling updates; individual case reports and periodic reports are production
**Proprietary data:** None of its own — case data sits in each manufacturer's safety database and includes patient information
**Clock:** 15-calendar-day alert reports and quarterly then annual periodic reports under 21 CFR 314.80, all filings of the manufacturer that holds the application
**Buyer:** Head of PV services
