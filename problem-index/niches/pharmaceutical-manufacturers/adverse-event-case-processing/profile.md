# Adverse Event Case Intake & Processing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pharmaceutical-manufacturers|Pharmaceutical Manufacturers]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn every incoming adverse event report into a coded, assessed case FDA accepts, and whoever leaves the fewest late or missed cases for an FDA inspection to find takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.5B a year in US pharmacovigilance case processing, safety databases and outsourced PV services for marketed small-molecule products (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1% of US receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — safety databases are digital; intake from calls, emails, literature and partners is still read and keyed by people
**Target Buyer:** Head of Drug Safety / Pharmacovigilance at a generic, OTC or specialty maker
**Automation Potential:** High for intake, coding, duplicate detection and narratives; causality and signal judgements stay with safety physicians

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every application holder, generic makers included, must report serious and unexpected adverse experiences to FDA within 15 calendar days of first receiving them, and report the rest quarterly for three years after approval and annually after that (21 CFR 314.80). A generic maker with hundreds of molecules receives cases through its call centre, email, partners and literature screening, each needing the four minimum elements, MedDRA coding, seriousness and expectedness against the label, a narrative and a duplicate check. FAERS has received more than two million reports a year recently (search summary). The work is high-volume, rule-bound and clocked, which makes it the most mechanical regulated work a manufacturer does outside the plant. Biologics safety belongs with the queued biologics-manufacturers industry; device complaints are covered in [[niches/medical-device-mfg/postmarket-surveillance-vendors/profile|🔍 Post-Market Surveillance & Complaint Handling]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Oracle Argus, ArisGlobal LifeSphere and Veeva Vault Safety hold the cases, with Veeva announcing safety AI agents (search summary); IQVIA, Cognizant, Genpact and other outsourcers process cases. Gaps: intake across channels is manual; generic makers pay big-pharma prices for databases sized for brands; literature cases are found late in the 15-day window.
