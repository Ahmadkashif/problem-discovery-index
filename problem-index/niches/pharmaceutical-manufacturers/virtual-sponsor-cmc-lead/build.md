# One Quality View Across Every CDMO

**Niche:** [[niches/pharmaceutical-manufacturers/virtual-sponsor-cmc-lead/profile|The Virtual Sponsor's CMC Lead]]
**Industry:** [[industries/pharmaceutical-manufacturers|Pharmaceutical Manufacturers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A sponsor that outsources every step has no system of its own that shows what is open at each supplier and what it must decide.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #compliance #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor serving the virtual sponsor's CMC lead is fighting to give one or two people control of batches made at several CDMOs, and whoever lets the sponsor release the most CDMO batches with no deviation discovered after release takes the account.

## The Problem
The CMC lead receives batch records, deviation reports, change notifications, certificates of analysis and stability data from each supplier by email or portal, in each supplier's format. They must spot which deviations affect which batches, approve or challenge them, and release product, while also running tech transfers and answering FDA.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
QMS vendors sell to plants. CDMO portals are built to serve their own plant's clients. Small sponsors are a fragmented market and buy consultants rather than software.

## What to Build
A sponsor-side workspace that ingests supplier documents from email and portals, extracts each deviation, change and batch into a common record, links them by product and lot, and drafts the sponsor's assessment (impact on registered process, need for a regulatory filing, batch disposition) for the CMC lead to confirm. It keeps the sponsor's own audit trail for FDA.

## Target Customer
Heads of CMC at specialty pharma, 505(b)(2) and small generic license holders with marketed products at two or more CDMOs.

## Impact If Built
Giving a two-person CMC team a single queue could save each of them a day a week (estimate) and catch deviations that today surface only when FDA or a complaint finds them.
