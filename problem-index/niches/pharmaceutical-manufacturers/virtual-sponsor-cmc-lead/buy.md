# Supplier Quality Modules Built for Plant Owners

**Niche:** [[niches/pharmaceutical-manufacturers/virtual-sponsor-cmc-lead/profile|The Virtual Sponsor's CMC Lead]]
**Industry:** [[industries/pharmaceutical-manufacturers|Pharmaceutical Manufacturers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** QMS supplier modules manage incoming materials for a plant; a virtual sponsor's suppliers are the plant.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor serving the virtual sponsor's CMC lead is fighting to give one or two people control of batches made at several CDMOs, and whoever lets the sponsor release the most CDMO batches with no deviation discovered after release takes the account.

## The Problem
Sponsors try to run CDMO oversight in a QMS designed for a manufacturing site and end up maintaining it in parallel with email.

## What Already Exists
Supplier quality management in Veeva Vault Quality, MasterControl, TrackWise and Qualio: supplier qualification, audits, scorecards and supplier CAPAs.

## The Customization Gap
The sponsor case needs quality agreements encoded as obligations (who approves which deviation class, notification windows for changes), ingestion of each CDMO's documents in their own formats, batch disposition records the sponsor signs, and links to the sponsor's registered CMC so every supplier change is assessed for a filing.

## Target Customer
Quality and CMC leads at sponsors already licensing a cloud QMS.

## Impact If Solved
Encoding quality agreements and supplier documents in the QMS the sponsor already pays for would cut missed change-notification windows and late approvals by half or more (estimate), each a potential FDA observation for the sponsor.
