# Pharma Manufacturing Trade & Technical Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pharmaceutical-manufacturers|Pharmaceutical Manufacturers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
ISPE and PDA publish technical guides and reports on manufacturing and quality, written largely by volunteer task forces of member companies; AAM (generics) and CHPA (OTC) represent their members to Congress and FDA and publish economic reports such as AAM's annual savings report.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 paid research staff per association (estimate)
**Output:** Technical guides, conference programmes, policy and economic reports
**Proprietary data:** Member surveys and committee work
**Clock:** Self-set publication and conference calendars
**Buyer:** Executive director
