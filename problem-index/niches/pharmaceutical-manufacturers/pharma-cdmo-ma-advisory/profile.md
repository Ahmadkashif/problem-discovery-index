# Pharma & CDMO M&A Advisory

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pharmaceutical-manufacturers|Pharmaceutical Manufacturers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Healthcare investment banks and boutiques that advise on buying and selling generic makers, CDMO plants and product portfolios, from bulge-bracket healthcare groups to specialists such as Bourne Partners and Results Healthcare. Consolidation of the CDMO base keeps them busy.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Deal teams of 5–20 per transaction (estimate)
**Output:** Valuations, buyer lists, information memoranda, fairness opinions
**Proprietary data:** Deal comparables and buyer relationships
**Clock:** Deal timetables set by the parties
**Buyer:** Managing director
