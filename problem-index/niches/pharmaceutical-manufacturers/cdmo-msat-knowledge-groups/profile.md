# CDMO Process Modelling & Tech-Transfer Knowledge Groups

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pharmaceutical-manufacturers|Pharmaceutical Manufacturers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Central process-modelling, scale-up and knowledge-management groups inside large CDMO networks (Thermo Fisher's Patheon, Lonza, Catalent, Recipharm, Siegfried, Piramal) that support tech transfers and troubleshooting across plants and sell the result inside manufacturing programmes. The per-project MSAT engineers who run each transfer are the production layer.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–50 per large network (estimate); per-project MSAT engineers are production and not counted
**Output:** Scale-up and process models, transfer risk assessments, platform knowledge used in bids and transfers
**Proprietary data:** Process and equipment data from many sponsors' products across the network's trains
**Clock:** Sponsors' validation and launch dates and FDA's pre-approval inspection on the sponsor's application
**Buyer:** Chief technology officer / head of global MSAT
