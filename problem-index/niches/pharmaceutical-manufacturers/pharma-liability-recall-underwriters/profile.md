# Pharmaceutical Product Liability & Recall Underwriters

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pharmaceutical-manufacturers|Pharmaceutical Manufacturers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Life-sciences desks at commercial insurers and Lloyd's syndicates that write product liability, clinical trial and product recall cover for drug makers, pricing each account from submissions, loss history and quality records.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 dedicated research or risk-engineering staff per carrier (estimate); underwriters pricing individual accounts are production
**Output:** Policies and pricing, risk-engineering visits
**Proprietary data:** The carrier's own loss and claims history
**Clock:** Policy renewal dates
**Buyer:** Head of life-sciences underwriting
