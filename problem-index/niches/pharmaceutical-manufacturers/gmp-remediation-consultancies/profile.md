# GMP Quality Remediation & Data-Integrity Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pharmaceutical-manufacturers|Pharmaceutical Manufacturers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Consultancies that assess drug plants against CGMP, run mock inspections, write 483 and warning-letter responses, and carry out remediation, including the independent third-party certifications FDA requires under consent decrees and in some warning letters. Lachman Consultants, NSF Health Sciences, Parexel's consulting arm, ValSource and the life-sciences practices of large accounting firms compete.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 cross-client staff per firm (estimate), in knowledge-management and regulatory-intelligence roles; the ~40–300 auditors, remediation consultants and response writers at a larger firm (estimate) work engagement by engagement and are production
**Output:** Gap assessments, mock-inspection findings, response letters, remediation plans, data-integrity reviews, third-party certification reports
**Proprietary data:** Cross-client experience of how FDA cites and closes findings; the records reviewed are the clients'
**Clock:** FDA's 15-business-day review window for 483 responses and consent-decree milestones, both set on the client
**Buyer:** Managing director / practice leader
