# CMC Regulatory Affairs & ANDA Submission Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pharmaceutical-manufacturers|Pharmaceutical Manufacturers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Consultancies that prepare and defend the chemistry, manufacturing and controls side of drug applications: ANDA and NDA quality modules, post-approval change strategy and supplements, Drug Master File management, and responses to FDA information requests and deficiency letters. Freyr, ProPharma, Parexel's regulatory consulting, Cencora's PharmaLex and many Indian and US boutiques compete, paid by the project or the month.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–60 CMC strategy specialists per larger firm (estimate); dossier writers and publishers doing per-dossier work are production and not counted
**Output:** Filing strategy for new applications and changes, Module 3 sections, deficiency responses, regulatory impact assessments of changes
**Proprietary data:** Experience of what FDA questioned across many clients' applications; the documents themselves are the clients' dossiers and FDA correspondence
**Clock:** FDA's response windows for information requests and deficiency letters, set on the client's application
**Buyer:** Practice leader, regulatory CMC
