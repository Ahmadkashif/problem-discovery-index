# QMS, eBR & Manufacturing Software Vendors' Data Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pharmaceutical-manufacturers|Pharmaceutical Manufacturers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The data, AI and industry-content teams inside the software vendors that hold plants' GMP records: Veeva (Vault Quality, QualityOne, LIMS), Honeywell's Sparta TrackWise, MasterControl, Körber PAS-X, Emerson Syncade, Tulip, Leucine, Seal and Aizon. They build product features and, rarely, benchmark reports; manufacturers pay software licences.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–50 data scientists and industry specialists at the larger vendors (estimate); engineers and implementation staff not counted
**Output:** Software features, AI agents, occasional benchmark or trend reports
**Proprietary data:** Quality, batch and lab records across many customers, held in the vendor's cloud
**Clock:** The vendor's own release calendar
**Buyer:** Chief product officer
