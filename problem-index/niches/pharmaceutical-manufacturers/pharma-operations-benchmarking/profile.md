# Pharma Operations & Quality Benchmarking Consortia

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pharmaceutical-manufacturers|Pharmaceutical Manufacturers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Programmes that collect operating and quality data from many manufacturing sites and return benchmarks: equipment effectiveness, right-first-time rates, deviations per batch, lab productivity, cost per unit. McKinsey's pharma operations benchmarking and the University of St. Gallen's operational excellence benchmarking, which also fed quality metrics research with FDA, are the best-known examples. Site and network heads pay to see where they stand.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 analysts and researchers per programme (estimate)
**Output:** Site benchmark reports, peer comparisons, improvement diagnostics
**Proprietary data:** Contributed site data across hundreds of plants over many years, held nowhere else
**Clock:** Self-set benchmarking rounds
**Buyer:** Programme or practice lead
