# Freelance-Marketplace Milestones Adapted to a Record

**Niche:** [[niches/recording-studios/self-funded-artists/profile|Self-Funded Independent Artists]]
**Industry:** [[industries/recording-studios|Recording Studios]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Freelance marketplaces long ago solved staged payment against agreed deliverables; independent recording still runs on a deposit and an argument at the end.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #automation #compliance #quick-win #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to sell a self-funded artist a fixed price for a release-ready record and then deliver it without the hours running over, and whoever estimates a project's real hours best keeps the artist's next release.

## The Problem
An artist pays a deposit and then by the day or hour. Neither side knows exactly what has been delivered at any point, disputes arise over whether a mix revision was included, and studios sometimes hold session files hostage against unpaid invoices while artists post angry reviews.

## What Already Exists
Freelance marketplaces such as Upwork and Fiverr, and music marketplaces such as SoundBetter, use milestone payments held in escrow, released on approval, with dispute processes and deliverable definitions.

## The Customization Gap
A record needs milestones per song and per phase (tracking, overdubs, editing, mix, master), each with defined included work: takes, revision rounds, alternate mixes. It needs explicit transfer of session files and stems on final payment, credits captured at each stage for later royalty paperwork, and change orders priced against the studio's rate card when the artist adds a song or a string section. The milestone record doubles as the progress report the artist sees.

## Target Customer
Independent studios with mostly self-funded clients, and booking or payment platforms that want a studio-specific product.

## Impact If Solved
Fewer payment disputes and bad reviews, clearer scope, and artists who trust the studio enough to come back for the next release.
