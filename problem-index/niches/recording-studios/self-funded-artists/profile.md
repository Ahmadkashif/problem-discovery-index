# Self-Funded Independent Artists

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/recording-studios|Recording Studios]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to sell a self-funded artist a fixed price for a release-ready record and then deliver it without the hours running over, and whoever estimates a project's real hours best keeps the artist's next release.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600M US studio spend by artists paying out of pocket, with no label recording budget (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~18% of receipts including the solo layer (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — artists find studios on Instagram and Google and negotiate by direct message; quotes, payment and progress tracking are informal
**Target Buyer:** The artist or their manager; the owner-engineer of a local studio as the vendor that serves them
**Automation Potential:** Medium — scoping, quoting and progress reporting can be systematised; the recording itself cannot

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Most clients at independent studios aren't signed to labels. They pay from savings, crowdfunding or day jobs, with a fixed budget, often a few thousand dollars for an EP (estimate), and no experience of how many hours a song takes. Studio workflows and contracts assume a label's budget and producer: hourly billing, open-ended overdubs and mixes priced separately. The result is the industry's most common failure: the money runs out at song seven, with an unmixed album and an unhappy client. Tools built for studios or for label projects ignore this buyer's main need, cost certainty.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Instagram and Google for discovery; studio websites with hourly rate cards; generic schedulers and payment links; SoundBetter and similar marketplaces for remote mixing. The gaps: no estimator of hours from a demo and the band's readiness, no per-song milestone billing, no weekly view of budget burned against songs finished, and ambiguous ownership of session files until the last invoice is paid.
