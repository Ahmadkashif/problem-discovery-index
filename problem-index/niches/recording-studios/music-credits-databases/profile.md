# Music Credits & Recording Metadata Databases

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/recording-studios|Recording Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Databases that record who wrote, performed, produced, mixed and engineered each recording: Muso.AI, which sells verified credit profiles and analytics to producers and engineers; Discogs and AllMusic, built on community and editorial contributions; and the credits that streaming services now display. Jaxsta, which built an "official" credits database fed by labels and publishers and claimed 150,000 producers and 100,000 engineers, closed in November 2025, saying its model did not scale.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 data and editorial staff at the larger databases (estimate)
**Output:** Credit profiles, verified credit records, data licences and API access
**Proprietary data:** Verified and community-edited credits across tens of millions of recordings, partly overlapping labels' own feeds
**Clock:** None external beyond award-entry and release calendars
**Buyer:** Founder or head of data at the database
