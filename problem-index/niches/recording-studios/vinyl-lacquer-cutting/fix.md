# Test-Pressing Notes That Arrive as "Sounds Off"

**Niche:** [[niches/recording-studios/vinyl-lacquer-cutting/profile|Vinyl Lacquer Cutting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/recording-studios|Recording Studios]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** When a test pressing is rejected, the cutting engineer gets a vague email, and can't tell a cutting fault from a plating, pressing or turntable problem.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to cut lacquers that pass the test pressing first time, and whoever wastes the fewest scarce lacquers and plant-queue weeks on recuts keeps the label's vinyl work.

## The Problem
An artist plays a test pressing on a home turntable and writes that side B "sounds off" or "crackles at the end". The plant may report its own defects (non-fill, groove noise, off-centre) in a different email. The engineer has to work out whether the cut, the plating, the pressing or the playback is at fault, often recutting to be safe.

## Why It's Still Broken
Three parties (cutting room, plant and client) each hold part of the evidence, and there is no common format for a test-pressing report. Clients don't know the vocabulary; plants report in their own terms; nobody records timestamps.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A shared test-pressing report, a short web form tied to the job, that asks the client for side, track and approximate time, and offers plain-language defect choices (distortion on loud vocals, crackle throughout, a repeating click, a skip) that map to likely causes. The plant's QC findings attach to the same record. The cutting engineer sees a structured history per job and per plant, which over time shows which faults are theirs.

## Who Feels the Pain
Cutting engineers who recut faults that weren't theirs; labels waiting on approvals; artists who don't know how to describe what they hear.

## Impact If Fixed
Fewer unnecessary recuts and faster sign-off, plus the outcome record that the predictive build above needs.
