# Casting Narrators Against What Listeners Rewarded

**Niche:** [[niches/recording-studios/frontlist-narration-casting/profile|Frontlist Casting & Directed Narration]]
**Industry:** [[industries/recording-studios|Recording Studios]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Publishers cast lead titles by a director's ear and an agent's pitch, and never check which casting choices listeners actually rewarded.
**Tags:** #tacit-knowledge-ml #gradient-boosting #contrastive-learning #causal-inference #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to cast and direct the narration that listeners and reviewers single out on a publisher's lead title, ready for the print on-sale date, and whoever delivers performances that lift a title's audio sales keeps the publisher's frontlist.

## The Problem
A casting director listens to auditions and demo reels and chooses the narrator for a major release. The choice is a tacit judgement about voice, genre and the author's tone. Afterwards the publisher sees sales, completion rates and listener reviews that often name the narrator, but never joins them back to the casting decision. The same mismatches recur: a narrator miscast across genres, a male narrator for a female-led thriller listeners rejected.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Sales data sits with retailers and is shared with publishers in aggregate; completion data stays with the platforms. Casting is a small, relationship-driven decision made a few hundred times a year at each large publisher, so it has never been treated as something to measure. Print sales confound everything.

## What to Build
A casting record that stores each decision with the shortlisted voices, the chosen narrator and the director's reasons, joined to audio outcomes: audio-to-print sales ratio against comparable titles, return rates, review mentions of the narrator and their sentiment, and series continuation. On top, voice embeddings of narrators' samples let a director search for "voices like the one that worked on this series" and see the record of how such voices performed in a genre. It advises; the director decides.

## Target Customer
Audio executives at the large trade publishers and their imprint-level casting directors.

## Impact If Built
Fewer costly miscasts on titles where the audio edition is a large share of revenue, and a casting process a publisher can teach rather than lose when a director leaves.
