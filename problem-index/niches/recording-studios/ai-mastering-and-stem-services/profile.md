# AI Mastering, Mixing & Stem-Separation Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/recording-studios|Recording Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Online services and plug-ins that master, mix or separate audio automatically: LANDR (a single master for ~$9.99), eMastered, RoEx Automix (mixes from labelled stems), AudioShake and Moises/Music.AI (stem separation for remixes, sync and spatial upmixes), and the assistants built into Ozone 12. Artists, labels and studios pay per track or by subscription.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Machine-learning and audio engineering staff of ~20–100 per vendor (estimate), all building the product
**Output:** Processing models and processed audio
**Proprietary data:** Audio uploaded by customers and their choices among processed versions
**Clock:** None external
**Buyer:** Head of product or machine learning
