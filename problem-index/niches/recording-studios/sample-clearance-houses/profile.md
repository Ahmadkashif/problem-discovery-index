# Sample Clearance Houses

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/recording-studios|Recording Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Small firms that clear samples used in new recordings before release: identifying who owns the sampled master and the underlying composition, negotiating fees or royalty shares with both, and documenting the licences. Artists, producers and labels pay per clearance or on retainer.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~2–15 per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Ownership research, licence negotiations, clearance documentation
**Proprietary data:** Records of who controls which masters and compositions, and of the fees owners have accepted
**Clock:** The client's release date
**Buyer:** Founder
