# Production Music & Stock Sound Libraries

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/recording-studios|Recording Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Libraries that commission composers and studios to record music and sound effects, then license the catalogue to video makers, broadcasters, advertisers and creators: Epidemic Sound (2024 revenue SEK 1.921B, 250,000+ tracks and effects), APM Music, Extreme Music, Musicbed, Artlist and sample libraries such as Splice. The NAICS code merged into this industry lists stock music and stock sound libraries by name.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 data and insight analysts at the largest libraries, apart from curators and metadata taggers (estimate)
**Output:** Internal demand analysis that steers commissioning, search ranking and trend reports for marketing
**Proprietary data:** Search, download and usage data across billions of plays of owned or controlled tracks
**Clock:** None external; commissioning calendars are self-set
**Buyer:** Head of content or data
