# DAW & Plug-in Makers' Product Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/recording-studios|Recording Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The makers of the software every session runs on: Avid (Pro Tools), Apple (Logic Pro), Steinberg (Nuendo, Cubase), Ableton, and plug-in makers such as Native Instruments with iZotope, Universal Audio and Waves. Their user research, product analytics and market teams study how engineers work in order to set roadmaps and prices.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–50 user research, analytics and market staff per larger vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Internal roadmap and pricing research; the products themselves
**Proprietary data:** Feature-usage telemetry from users who opt in, licence and subscription histories, support tickets
**Clock:** None external; release cycles and trade-show dates are self-set
**Buyer:** Chief product officer
