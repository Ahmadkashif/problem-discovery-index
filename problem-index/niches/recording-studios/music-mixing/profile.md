# Mixing for Artists & Labels

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/recording-studios|Recording Studios]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver a mix the artist signs off in the fewest revision rounds, and whoever translates an artist's notes and reference tracks into the right moves first time keeps that artist's next record.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$500M US spend on music mixing, freelance and studio-based (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of receipts including the solo layer (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High — mixing is file-based and largely remote, but notes, revision counts and approvals run on email, text and review links
**Target Buyer:** Freelance mix engineer; head of the mix team at a multi-room studio
**Automation Potential:** Medium — balance and processing are increasingly assisted by AI tools; interpreting notes and taste is not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Mixing is sold by the song, not the hour: a flat fee (~$75–400 on SoundBetter, far more for established names) for a stereo mix plus a set of alternates (instrumental, a cappella, TV mix, clean version, stems) and a few rounds of revisions. It no longer needs a tracking room, so the competitive set is national and increasingly global, and an artist chooses a mixer by their credits and their reputation for "getting it". The work that decides profit is the revision cycle, which is why the contest is about first-time translation rather than sound quality in the abstract.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Pro Tools and Logic sessions with Universal Audio, Waves and iZotope plug-ins; AI-assisted mixing from Neutron and RoEx Automix; client review on Pibox, Filepass and Samply; payments through marketplaces or invoices. The gaps: nothing links a client's note to the session change that answered it, revision rounds are counted by memory, alternate versions are re-bounced by hand after every change, and an engineer's history of what each artist means by each word lives only in their head.
