# Video Review Tools Rebuilt Around Mix Notes

**Industry:** [[industries/recording-studios|Recording Studios]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Video post settled on frame-accurate review with versioned comments years ago; music mixing still gets "the second chorus feels small" in a text message.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #worker-facing #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver a mix the artist signs off in the fewest revision rounds, and whoever translates an artist's notes and reference tracks into the right moves first time keeps that artist's next record.

## The Problem
Notes arrive in every format: voice memos, texts, emails with three people's opinions merged, and comments on a streaming link. The mixer spends time collating them, guessing which version a note refers to, and arguing later about whether a change was in scope.

## What Already Exists
Frame-accurate review platforms for video, such as Frame.io, with versioned uploads, timestamped comments, approvals and integrations into editing software. Audio-specific review tools (Pibox, Filepass, Samply) offer timestamped comments and version stacks for music.

## The Customization Gap
Mixing needs what video review never had to solve: notes tied to a song section (verse two, the drop) rather than only a timestamp, so they survive an edit; a way to tell artist, producer and A&R notes apart and resolve conflicts before work starts; automatic translation of vague wording into a clarifying question with A/B snippets; a revision counter against the agreed scope that drafts a change order when exceeded; and a hook into the DAW so each resolved note links to the session change. Loudness-matched playback of versions is also essential, because "louder" is otherwise mistaken for "better".

## Target Customer
Mix engineers already paying for a review tool, and the review-tool vendors themselves, who can add mix-specific features faster than a newcomer can win the users.

## Impact If Solved
Fewer rounds lost to misunderstood or conflicting notes, and an approval trail that settles scope disputes in seconds rather than through a week of emails.
