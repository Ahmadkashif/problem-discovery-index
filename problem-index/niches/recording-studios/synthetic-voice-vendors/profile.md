# Synthetic Voice & AI Narration Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/recording-studios|Recording Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors of text-to-speech and voice-cloning models (ElevenLabs, WellSaid Labs and others), and retailers' own AI narration services, Audible's among them since May 2025. They sell synthetic voices to publishers, e-learning producers, advertisers and studios, and license voices from actors for their libraries. AI-narrated titles earned 0.03% of US audiobook revenue in 2025, though the Audio Publishers Association reports that more of them were published that year.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ML research and engineering staff of ~50–300 per leading vendor (estimate), all building the product; no team produces analysis for clients
**Output:** Voice models, APIs and narration services
**Proprietary data:** Licensed voice recordings with consent records, plus usage data from customers' generations
**Clock:** State digital-replica laws such as Tennessee's ELVIS Act and California's AB 2602, which govern the vendor's contracts rather than a billable deadline
**Buyer:** Head of product or research
