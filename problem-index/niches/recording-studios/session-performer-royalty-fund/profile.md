# AFM & SAG-AFTRA Non-Featured Performer Fund

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/recording-studios|Recording Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The AFM & SAG-AFTRA Intellectual Property Rights Distribution Fund pays out the share of US statutory sound-recording royalties that the law sets aside for non-featured musicians and vocalists, union or not: 2.5% each of the receipts SoundExchange collects (17 U.S.C. §114(g)(2)). Its staff work out who played and sang on covered recordings from union session reports, liner notes and claims. It keeps a list of credited performers it cannot reach and investigates claims from performers who were never credited. It reported a record $70M+ distribution in April 2021 and more than $500M since inception.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 research, claims and distribution staff (estimate)
**Output:** Performer identification and annual distributions
**Proprietary data:** Union session contracts joined to covered recordings: the closest thing in the industry to a record of who actually performed on what
**Clock:** Annual distribution dates and claim periods
**Buyer:** Fund executive director, answering to union and industry trustees

- **Q1 = 2.** Like a society's commission, the fund's costs come out of the money it distributes, so performers do pay for the identification work. But the statute sends the non-featured share to this fund and nowhere else. No performer chooses it or can take their share to a rival, so the quality of the determination never decides who is hired. That is the difference from [[niches/recording-studios/soundexchange-royalty-collective/profile|🔍 Sound-Recording & Performer Collecting Societies]] (Q1 4), where rightholders can choose a society and an international collection route.
- It is logged apart from that pocket because it doesn't match plays. It receives a fixed share of SoundExchange's matched receipts and allocates it among non-featured performers.

**Kill switches:** none
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. A unique record of session performers, held by a single statutory fund. Rights holders have publicly alleged that flawed identification held back up to ~$25M, which shows the research matters, but no performer can buy it elsewhere.
