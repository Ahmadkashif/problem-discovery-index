# Broadcast QC Software Adapted to Music Delivery

**Niche:** [[niches/recording-studios/mastering-and-delivery/profile|Mastering & Delivery for Streaming and Spatial Formats]]
**Industry:** [[industries/recording-studios|Recording Studios]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Picture post runs every deliverable through automated QC against the distributor's spec; music masters are checked by an engineer reading meters.
**Tags:** #automation #compliance #evaluation-metrics #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver masters, stereo and Dolby Atmos, that pass every platform's and label's delivery checks first time and still sound right on earbuds, cars and club systems, and whoever has the fewest rejections and re-requests keeps the label's catalogue work.

## The Problem
A catalogue job can mean hundreds of tracks delivered in several formats each. A wrong sample rate, an inter-sample peak over the limit, missing ISRCs, a silent channel in an Atmos file or a stem set that doesn't sum to the master is found by the label or a platform after delivery, and the batch goes back.

## What Already Exists
Automated file QC systems used in television and film post check picture and sound deliverables against distributor specs and produce pass/fail reports (picture-side delivery QC is analysed in [[niches/postproduction-studios/deliverables-qc-and-mastering/profile|⚡ Deliverables QC & Mastering]] under Postproduction Studios). Loudness meters and DDP verification tools exist for music.

## The Customization Gap
Music delivery needs a spec registry per platform and label (loudness and true-peak targets, sample rates and bit depths, Atmos object and bed limits, file naming); checks for embedded ISRC, ISWC and credits metadata; stem-sum and alternate-version null tests; binaural and fold-down rendering checks for Atmos; DDP and vinyl pre-master verification; and batch reports a label's production team accepts as evidence.

## Target Customer
Mastering studios doing catalogue and Atmos work, and label production departments receiving it.

## Impact If Solved
Batches pass on first delivery, labels stop re-checking every file, and mastering rooms win catalogue work on reliability.
