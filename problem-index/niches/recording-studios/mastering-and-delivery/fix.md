# Delivery Specs Kept in an Engineer's Head

**Niche:** [[niches/recording-studios/mastering-and-delivery/profile|Mastering & Delivery for Streaming and Spatial Formats]]
**Industry:** [[industries/recording-studios|Recording Studios]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Every label, distributor and platform has its own delivery spec, and the only complete list is in the mastering engineer's memory and an old email folder.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #automation #compliance #quick-win #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver masters, stereo and Dolby Atmos, that pass every platform's and label's delivery checks first time and still sound right on earbuds, cars and club systems, and whoever has the fewest rejections and re-requests keeps the label's catalogue work.

## The Problem
Spotify recommends -14 LUFS and true peak below -1 dBTP; Apple has its own programme for high-resolution masters; labels ask for specific naming, sample rates and alternate versions; Atmos deliverables have their own rules; vinyl plants want pre-masters a particular way. Specs change without notice. When an assistant or a new engineer prepares a delivery, mistakes follow.

## Why It's Still Broken
Specs are published in scattered help pages and PDFs or sent in emails. Mastering rooms are small and rely on the senior engineer's memory. Nothing turns a spec into an export preset and a check.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A maintained spec library: each platform's and label's requirements extracted from their documents into structured rules, versioned when they change, linked to export presets in the mastering software and to automated checks on the output. When a client emails new requirements, they are parsed into the same structure and confirmed by the engineer.

## Who Feels the Pain
Mastering engineers and assistants preparing deliveries; label coordinators bouncing files back.

## Impact If Fixed
Fewer rejected deliveries, faster onboarding for assistants, and no more re-delivering a whole album for a naming rule.
