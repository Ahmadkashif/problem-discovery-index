# Record Label A&R and Recording-Budget Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/recording-studios|Recording Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Labels fund most professional recording budgets and hire the studios, producers, mixers and mastering engineers. Data teams at the major labels and the larger independents analyse artist signings, streaming trajectories, marketing spend and recording costs to decide whom to sign, how much to advance and what a record should cost. The work is internal and pays off through the label's own releases.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–150 data scientists and analysts at a major, far fewer at independents (estimate)
**Output:** Internal signing models, budget and recoupment forecasts, catalogue reports for investors
**Proprietary data:** Recording costs, advances and royalty accounts per project, joined to streaming and sales outcomes across the roster
**Clock:** Release calendars the label sets, and earnings dates for the listed majors
**Buyer:** Head of data or chief financial officer at a label group
