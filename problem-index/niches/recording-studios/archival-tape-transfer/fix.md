# Transfer Logs Written by Hand Next to the Machine

**Niche:** [[niches/recording-studios/archival-tape-transfer/profile|Archival Tape Transfer & Restoration]]
**Industry:** [[industries/recording-studios|Recording Studios]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The settings that make a transfer trustworthy (speed, EQ curve, azimuth, baking) are written on a clipboard and typed into a spreadsheet days later, if at all.
**Tags:** #automation #data-integration #compliance #quick-win #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to transfer degrading tape and disc collections to files and metadata that meet archive standards such as IASA TC-04 and FADGI, at a cost per hour a grant budget can carry, and whoever proves preservation-grade quality at production throughput wins the institutional contracts before working playback machines run out.

## The Problem
A transfer engineer running several machines at once notes each carrier's condition and the settings used on paper, because the machines are analogue and the work is hands-on. The notes are transcribed later, errors creep in, and the archive's required metadata fields are completed from memory or left blank.

## Why It's Still Broken
The playback machines predate any data connection, and the DAW capturing the transfer doesn't know what the machine is doing. Archive metadata templates differ by client. Typing during a transfer takes attention away from listening for problems.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A tablet log at each machine with presets for common carrier types, barcode or photo capture of the carrier label, and quick fields for the settings that matter, written straight into the job record and embedded into the file's metadata on export. Where the DAW can detect them, speed and level are captured automatically. Each client's template is filled from the same record.

## Who Feels the Pain
Transfer engineers doing the paperwork after the shift; archivists receiving incomplete metadata; transfer houses losing hours to rework on large jobs.

## Impact If Fixed
Complete, accurate process metadata on every file at no extra time per carrier, and hours of transcription per large job removed.
