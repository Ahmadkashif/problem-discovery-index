# Backlist & Independent Volume Narration

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/recording-studios|Recording Studios]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to cut the edit, proof and pickup hours behind each finished hour without letting misreads through publisher QC, and whoever gets closest to one clean pass per finished hour keeps backlist programmes from moving to AI narration.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$250M US spend on backlist, genre-volume and self-published audiobook production (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High — recorded in home booths and small studios, edited and delivered digitally, with proofing still largely by ear
**Target Buyer:** Owner of an audiobook production house; publisher's backlist audio programme manager; independent narrator-producer
**Automation Potential:** High — editing, proofing and spec mastering are mechanical and checkable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is where the volume is and where AI narration is aimed. When Audible opened AI narration to publishers, its CEO estimated that audiobooks exist for only 2–5% of available books and presented AI as a way to expand the catalogue; backlist, genre series and self-published titles are most of the rest. Publishers and authors pay modest per-finished-hour rates (from about $200 for newer narrators toward the $250 SAG-AFTRA ACX minimum) and need fast turnaround. The narrator or production house absorbs the hours: about ten per finished hour for a newer narrator, six for an experienced one. Whoever compresses those hours keeps human narration economic.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Reaper, Audition and Pro Tools with punch-and-roll recording; iZotope RX for cleanup; Pozotron for script-aligned proofing; ACX's own file checks at upload; marketplaces such as ACX for finding work. The gaps: editing, proofing and spec checks are separate tools and passes, retailer rejections are found after a whole book is mastered, and nobody measures hours per finished hour across a house's narrators.
