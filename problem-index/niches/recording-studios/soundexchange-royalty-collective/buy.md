# Claims Case Management Adapted to Ownership and Line-Up Disputes

**Niche:** [[niches/recording-studios/soundexchange-royalty-collective/profile|Sound-Recording & Performer Collecting Societies]]
**Industry:** [[industries/recording-studios|Recording Studios]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Customer-service platforms track tickets to closure; a society's disputed recordings need the same discipline plus evidence, partner societies and a statutory deadline on every case.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this pocket is fighting to identify every recording played, and every rights owner and performer on it, and pay them inside the distribution window before unidentified money becomes non-distributable, and whoever matches plays to recordings and line-ups most accurately and fastest keeps the labels and performers, and the international mandates, whose repertoire makes its licence worth paying for.

## The Problem
Two labels claim the same recording after a catalogue sale. A session player says they were on a track that lists only the band. A featured artist disputes a percentage under a letter of direction. Each case is worked in email and spreadsheets, evidence is scattered, and money stays on hold while nobody can see which cases are about to cross the three-year line.

## What Already Exists
Case-management and service platforms such as Salesforce Service Cloud and Zendesk, with queues, service levels, routing, attachments and audit trails.

## The Customization Gap
Societies need cases keyed to recordings (ISRC) and performers (IPN), with the money held and the distribution year attached. Deadlines should come from Article 13 or the society's own rules rather than generic service levels. Evidence should be typed (session report, contract, liner notes, label chain-of-title documents), and language models should summarise conflicting claims for a researcher. Outcomes should propagate to the repertoire database, the VRDB and partner societies, with a record members and regulators can audit.

## Target Customer
Heads of repertoire, claims and member services at sound-recording and performer societies.

## Impact If Solved
Disputed money released faster, fewer cases lapsing into non-distributable funds, and a defensible record of every determination.
