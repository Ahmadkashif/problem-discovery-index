# Audiobook Publishers & Producer Rollups

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/recording-studios|Recording Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Audio-first publishers and production groups that own narration studios and buy or license audio rights at scale: RBmedia (Recorded Books and its acquired imprints), Audible Studios, Podium, Blackstone, Tantor and the audio arms of the large trade publishers. They commission or produce tens of thousands of finished hours a year (estimate) and sell audiobooks to retailers, subscription services and libraries. Audio First titles earned $136M in 2025, 6% of US audiobook revenue.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 acquisitions, catalogue and consumer analysts at the larger groups (estimate)
**Output:** Internal title acquisition and pricing models, narrator and genre performance analysis
**Proprietary data:** Sales, library circulation and listening data per title, joined to narrator, production cost and finished hours
**Clock:** Publishers' on-sale dates and retailer promotions, which are client and partner schedules rather than hard deadlines
**Buyer:** Chief content or chief financial officer
