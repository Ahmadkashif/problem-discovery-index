# Self-Service Studio Chains

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/recording-studios|Recording Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Operators of networks of rehearsal and recording rooms booked online and opened by keycode, with automated recording in some rooms. Pirate Studios is the best-known: it raised $20M in 2018 to expand across the UK, Germany and the US, when it ran ~350 studios in 21 locations. Musicians, producers and podcasters pay by the hour.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Fewer than 10 analytics staff (estimate)
**Output:** Internal pricing, utilisation and site-selection analysis
**Proprietary data:** Booking and utilisation data across sites
**Clock:** None external
**Buyer:** Chief operating officer
