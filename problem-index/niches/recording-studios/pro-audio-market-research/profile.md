# Pro Audio & Music Products Market Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/recording-studios|Recording Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Research firms and trade publishers that size and forecast the markets for studio equipment, microphones, interfaces, monitors and music software, and survey musicians and engineers: consumer-electronics research houses with pro-audio practices, the NAMM trade association's industry reports, and trade magazines' annual data issues. Equipment makers and retailers pay for the reports.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~2–10 analysts on pro audio and music products at any one firm (estimate)
**Output:** Market sizing reports, forecasts, survey studies
**Proprietary data:** Shipment and sell-through panels from manufacturers and retailers, and survey series
**Clock:** Annual report cycles and trade-show dates the firms set
**Buyer:** Research director
