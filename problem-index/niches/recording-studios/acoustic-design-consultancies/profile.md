# Studio Acoustic Design Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/recording-studios|Recording Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Acoustic consultancies and studio designers (Walters-Storyk Design Group is the best-known) that design recording rooms, control rooms, mix stages and broadcast studios: isolation, room acoustics, HVAC noise and layout. Studio owners, universities, broadcasters and developers pay design fees per project.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Design and engineering staff of ~5–60 per firm, all on project delivery (estimate)
**Output:** Room designs, acoustic models and specifications
**Proprietary data:** Measurements and designs from completed rooms
**Clock:** Construction schedules set by the client
**Buyer:** Principal
