# Forensic Audio Authentication Labs

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/recording-studios|Recording Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Audio engineers who enhance and authenticate recordings for legal use: clarifying speech on calls and surveillance audio, testing whether a recording has been edited, identifying speakers, and, increasingly, assessing whether a voice is synthetic. They grew out of studio restoration skills and are retained by attorneys, insurers and courts.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~1–10 per practice (estimate)
**Output:** Enhanced recordings, authentication reports, expert testimony
**Proprietary data:** Case files and reference recordings
**Clock:** Court and discovery schedules
**Buyer:** Retaining counsel
