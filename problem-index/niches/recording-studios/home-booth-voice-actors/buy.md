# Applicant-Tracking Shortlists Adapted for Voice Casting

**Niche:** [[niches/recording-studios/home-booth-voice-actors/profile|Home-Booth Voice Actors]]
**Industry:** [[industries/recording-studios|Recording Studios]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Casting clients get hundreds of auditions per job and choose from the first few dozen they hear; recruiting software solved ranked, shared shortlists for job applicants years ago.
**Tags:** #contrastive-learning #transformers #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #compliance #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to put a home-booth actor's audition in front of the casting decisions it can win, and whoever turns auditions into bookings at the highest rate keeps the actor's membership fees and the client's jobs.

## The Problem
A producer posts a 60-second e-learning script and receives a flood of auditions within a day (estimate). They listen in arrival order, stop after a few dozen, and forward three to their client by email. Strong auditions submitted later are never heard, the client's feedback doesn't reach the platform, and the actor learns nothing.

## What Already Exists
Applicant-tracking systems for recruiting (Greenhouse, Lever, Workable) rank applicants against a job, move them through stages, collect structured scorecards from several reviewers, and share shortlists with hiring managers. Casting marketplaces offer basic favourites and response inboxes.

## The Customization Gap
Voice casting needs an audio-first version: auditions loudness-matched so the loudest doesn't sound best, ranked by fit to the brief and to voices the client has already starred, playable in quick-listen mode, and shared with the end client for comments without exporting files. Booking terms belong in the same record as structured fields: usage scope, term and any digital-replica permission, which California's AB 2602 now requires to be described with reasonable specificity. Every decision should flow back so the platform learns which auditions win.

## Target Customer
Voice casting marketplaces and agents, and the production companies and casting directors who hire through them.

## Impact If Solved
Clients book from a better shortlist in less time, actors who fit the brief get heard regardless of when they submitted, and booking rates rise on both sides of the marketplace.
