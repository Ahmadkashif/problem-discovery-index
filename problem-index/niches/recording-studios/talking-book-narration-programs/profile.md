# Talking-Book & Accessibility Narration Programs

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/recording-studios|Recording Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Public and nonprofit programmes that commission human-narrated recordings of books and magazines for readers who are blind or print-disabled, such as the Library of Congress's National Library Service for the Blind and Print Disabled and state and nonprofit recording services. They contract narration studios and run their own reviews of narration and audio quality.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Quality-review and production staff only; reviewers check recordings rather than produce analysis (estimate)
**Output:** Commissioned recordings and quality reviews
**Proprietary data:** Production and circulation records for accessible titles
**Clock:** Programme budgets and contract cycles
**Buyer:** Programme director at a federal or nonprofit agency
