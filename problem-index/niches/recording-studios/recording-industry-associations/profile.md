# Recording Industry Associations & Standards Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/recording-studios|Recording Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Recording Academy's Producers & Engineers Wing advocates for studio professionals, including on credits and the AMP Act. The Audio Engineering Society writes technical standards and recommendations through volunteer committees. The Audio Publishers Association publishes the annual audiobook sales and consumer surveys, fielded for it by Toluna and by Edison Research at SSRS. Narrator and producer groups such as PANA and the Music Producers Guild run education and advocacy.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Fewer than 10 staff on research at any of them; standards work is done by volunteers and surveys are outsourced (estimate)
**Output:** Annual sales and consumer surveys, standards documents, advocacy briefs
**Proprietary data:** Member-reported sales (APA) and survey series
**Clock:** Annual survey and conference cycles the associations set
**Buyer:** Association executive director
