# State Music Production Incentive Programs

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/recording-studios|Recording Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
A few states offer tax credits for qualified sound-recording spending in the state, typically administered by the economic development or film office alongside screen incentives and certified on CPA-audited cost reports (Louisiana and Georgia are the usual examples, estimate). Studios use them to attract label and artist projects.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~1–5 programme staff per state, with certification done by outside CPAs (estimate)
**Output:** Credit certifications and programme reports
**Proprietary data:** Applications and audited cost reports
**Clock:** Application and certification windows the state sets
**Buyer:** Programme director at a state agency
