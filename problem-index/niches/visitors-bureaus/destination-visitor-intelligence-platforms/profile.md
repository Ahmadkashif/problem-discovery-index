# Destination Visitor Intelligence Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/visitors-bureaus|Convention & Visitors Bureaus]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Tourism-focused platforms such as Zartico and Datafy, and Placer.ai's tourism offering, combine licensed mobile-device location panels with card-spend and other feeds into dashboards of visitor counts, origin markets, dwell time and spending, plus campaign attribution. Bureaus and state offices subscribe; some platforms also sell media activation.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 data scientists, engineers and destination strategists per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Visitor dashboards, monthly and seasonal reports, campaign attribution studies, strategist briefings
**Proprietary data:** A normalised cross-destination archive built on panels licensed from third-party suppliers
**Clock:** Clients' board meetings and budget hearings; no regulator or market sets a date
**Buyer:** Chief Product Officer / Head of Data Science
