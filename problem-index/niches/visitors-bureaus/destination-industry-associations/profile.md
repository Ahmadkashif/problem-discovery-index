# Destination & Travel Industry Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/visitors-bureaus|Convention & Visitors Bureaus]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Destinations International runs the DMAP accreditation programme (nine new accreditations and 24 reaccreditations in July 2026), the members-only Destination Organization Performance Reporting benchmark, the DestinationNEXT futures study and the Event Impact Calculator, licensed to more than 300 destination organisations. U.S. Travel Association publishes national travel forecasts and economic impact, and Sports ETA publishes sports tourism research. Most of the modelling is contracted to Tourism Economics and agency research arms.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Research staff under 10 per association (estimate)
**Output:** Benchmarks, accreditation standards, futures studies, licensed impact calculators
**Proprietary data:** Member benchmark submissions and accreditation files
**Clock:** Annual conventions and accreditation cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Research Officer / Vice President of Research
