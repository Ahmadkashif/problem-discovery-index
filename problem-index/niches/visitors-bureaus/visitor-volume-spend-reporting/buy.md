# Financial Close Reconciliation Applied to Visitor Data

**Niche:** [[niches/visitors-bureaus/visitor-volume-spend-reporting/profile|Visitor Volume & Spend Reporting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/visitors-bureaus|Convention & Visitors Bureaus]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Finance teams already reconcile many ledgers into one close every month with explained variances; bureaus do the same for visitor data, by hand.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #large-language-models #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce one monthly visitor count and spend figure reconciled across device panels, card data, hotel data and lodging-tax receipts, and whoever's number survives the city budget hearing takes the account.

## The Problem
Each month's report requires matching sources, explaining why they differ, and documenting choices so next month is consistent. None of it is recorded.

## What Already Exists
Close and reconciliation software (BlackLine, FloQast) and BI semantic layers that define metrics once.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: connectors to tourism data vendors' exports and APIs; definitions of visitor, overnight and day-tripper and origin-market rules held as a metric layer; tolerances per source pair (panel versus hotel, card versus tax); variance explanations drafted and stored; and publication calendars matching each vendor's lag.

## Target Customer
Research and data managers at large bureaus.

## Impact If Solved
A documented, repeatable monthly close for visitor data that survives staff turnover.
