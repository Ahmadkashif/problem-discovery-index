# Visitor Volume & Spend Reporting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/visitors-bureaus|Convention & Visitors Bureaus]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce one monthly visitor count and spend figure reconciled across device panels, card data, hotel data and lodging-tax receipts, and whoever's number survives the city budget hearing takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$250M a year of visitor-data licences, research staff time and annual visitor and economic impact studies across US destination organisations (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of bureau spending (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High — every input is a licensed feed or a public table; the reconciliation is manual
**Target Buyer:** Director of Research / Vice President of Strategy and Insights
**Automation Potential:** Very high

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A large bureau licenses several overlapping measures of the same visitors: STR hotel data, AirDNA or Key Data rental data, a location panel (Placer.ai, Zartico, Datafy), card spend (Visa Spend Signals, Mastercard), and an annual visitor volume and economic impact study, typically from Tourism Economics or a survey firm. Each vendor sells its own dashboard, and the bureau's monthly and annual numbers are assembled by hand from them. The work recurs monthly and is the same every time, which makes it the most automatable function in the bureau. The number matters because it is cited in funding fights.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Vendor dashboards; Simpleview's Data Engine and dashboards; Tourism Economics' Symphony platform; Excel and Power BI. Competitors are the visitor-data platforms, each positioning as the bureau's single source, and the economic impact modellers. Gap: a source-neutral estimate with stated uncertainty, and a reconciliation the bureau can show.
