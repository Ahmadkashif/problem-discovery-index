# Convention Housing Bureaus

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/visitors-bureaus|Convention & Visitors Bureaus]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Housing companies, such as onPeak, Orchid Event Solutions and Maritz's housing services, and some bureaus' in-house housing services, run hotel reservations for citywide conventions on behalf of the association. They distribute attendees into contracted blocks across many hotels, manage cut-off dates and report pickup by hotel and night. Associations pay fees or the hotels pay commission.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-30 housing analysts and reporting staff per large firm; reservation agents are production and excluded (estimate)
**Output:** Pickup reports, attrition exposure analysis, block-sizing recommendations for future years
**Proprietary data:** Night-by-night pickup across hundreds of citywides and hotels, the best record anywhere of how groups actually fill blocks
**Clock:** Hotel cut-off dates and attrition review dates in the group's contracts
**Buyer:** Vice President of Housing / Head of Client Analytics
