# Municipal Auditors & Hotel-Tax Oversight

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/visitors-bureaus|Convention & Visitors Bureaus]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
City auditors, county finance offices and in some states the attorney general or comptroller review how bureaus spend hotel-tax money: performance audits of bureau contracts, opinions on eligible uses, and annual reviews of grantee spending. Tax collection and remittance audits sit with the lodging tax authorities, logged under [[niches/hotels-boutique/lodging-tax-authorities/profile|🔍 Lodging Tax Authorities]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** Occasional engagements by small audit teams; no standing function of 10 or more on bureau oversight (estimate)
**Output:** Performance audit reports, legal opinions, compliance findings
**Proprietary data:** Bureau contracts, expenditure records and audit files
**Clock:** Audit plans and budget cycles
**Buyer:** City Auditor
