# Event Submissions Moderated One at a Time

**Niche:** [[niches/visitors-bureaus/listings-calendars-inquiries/profile|Listings, Event Calendars & Visitor Inquiries]]
**Industry:** [[industries/visitors-bureaus|Convention & Visitors Bureaus]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Community event submissions arrive with duplicates, missing details and spam, and a staff member approves each one by hand.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #word-embeddings #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a destination's partner listings, event entries and venue facts accurate and machine-readable, and whoever's records AI assistants and search engines cite when visitors and planners ask about the destination takes the account.

## The Problem
Organisers submit the same event several times, recurring events arrive as single entries, details are missing, and spam is mixed in. Staff spend hours a week moderating, so the calendar lags real events.

## Why It's Still Broken
Calendar modules were built as forms with an approval queue; nothing checks submissions before a person does.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Automatic deduplication against existing entries, extraction of date, venue and price from the submission and linked pages, recurrence detection, spam filtering, and a queue that shows staff only the uncertain cases.

## Who Feels the Pain
Content coordinators; organisers whose events appear late; visitors using an incomplete calendar.

## Impact If Fixed
Moderation time cut by most of its hours (estimate), and a calendar that matches what is actually happening.
