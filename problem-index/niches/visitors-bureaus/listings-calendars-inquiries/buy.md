# Retail Product Feeds Adapted to Destination Records

**Niche:** [[niches/visitors-bureaus/listings-calendars-inquiries/profile|Listings, Event Calendars & Visitor Inquiries]]
**Industry:** [[industries/visitors-bureaus|Convention & Visitors Bureaus]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Retailers already syndicate structured product feeds to search engines and marketplaces; a bureau needs the same for places, events and venues.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #word-embeddings #data-integration #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a destination's partner listings, event entries and venue facts accurate and machine-readable, and whoever's records AI assistants and search engines cite when visitors and planners ask about the destination takes the account.

## The Problem
The bureau's records are web pages, not data. Search engines and AI assistants get destination facts from whoever publishes structured data best.

## What Already Exists
Product information management and feed tools (Akeneo, Feedonomics, Google Merchant Center) that publish structured catalogues to many channels.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: schemas for places, events, venues and meeting spaces (schema.org types for events and local businesses) instead of products; partner consent and attribution; event recurrence and cancellations; meeting-space specs planners query (capacity, ceiling height, breakout rooms); and feeds to planner sourcing tools as well as search and assistants.

## Target Customer
Digital marketing leads at bureaus on Simpleview CMS, Tempest or WordPress.

## Impact If Solved
The bureau's content reaches AI and search channels as data, not just pages.
