# Card-Spend Destination Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/visitors-bureaus|Convention & Visitors Bureaus]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Payment networks package aggregated card transactions into destination products: Visa Spend Signals (formerly Visa Destination Insights), built on "aggregated, depersonalized spend data derived from card-present transactions", and Mastercard's tourism insights. They show visitor and resident spending by origin market, category and period, with peer-destination comparisons. Bureaus and state tourism offices license them, often through a state co-op programme.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 data scientists, analysts and client consultants on each network's destination product (estimate)
**Output:** Destination spend dashboards, origin-market and category reports, peer benchmarks
**Proprietary data:** The network's own transaction records, which no destination vendor can replicate
**Clock:** Bureau budget and board cycles; no external deadline governs the product
**Buyer:** Head of the network's destination or public-sector data product
