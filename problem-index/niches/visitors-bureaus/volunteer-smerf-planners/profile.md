# Volunteer & SMERF Group Planners

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/visitors-bureaus|Convention & Visitors Bureaus]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a volunteer planner's loose inquiry for a reunion, church convention or fraternal meeting into a hotel block the group can actually fill, and whoever sizes those blocks so the groups avoid attrition penalties and come back takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$150M a year of bureau SMERF sales and services staff, reunion and group planning services and related marketing (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of bureau spending (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — planners call or fill in a web form; bureaus respond by email and phone
**Target Buyer:** SMERF Sales Manager / Director of Sales
**Automation Potential:** Medium — intake, matching and planning guides automate well; trust with first-time planners is personal

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
SMERF groups (social, military, educational, religious, fraternal) include family reunions, military unit reunions, church and denominational conventions, fraternal and sorority gatherings and school trips. They are planned by volunteers who may do it once in their lives. They are price-sensitive, book weekends and off-peak periods hotels need filled, and often overestimate how many members will come. Planning tools are built for professional planners (Cvent) and hotels' own group desks. For a volunteer, the bureau is often the only neutral help available.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Bureau web forms and CRM leads; hotel group desks; Groups360; reunion planning websites; Cvent for the few professional SMERF planners. Competitors are hotel direct sales, third-party sourcing firms and online group-booking tools. Gaps: no help sizing the block, contract terms volunteers do not understand, and no follow-up that brings the group back.
