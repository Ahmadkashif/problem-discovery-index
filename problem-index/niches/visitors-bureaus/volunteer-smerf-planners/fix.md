# Attrition Clauses Signed by People Who Have Never Read One

**Niche:** [[niches/visitors-bureaus/volunteer-smerf-planners/profile|Volunteer & SMERF Group Planners]]
**Industry:** [[industries/visitors-bureaus|Convention & Visitors Bureaus]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Volunteer planners sign hotel group contracts with attrition, cancellation and food-and-beverage minimums they do not understand.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #worker-facing #compliance #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a volunteer planner's loose inquiry for a reunion, church convention or fraternal meeting into a hotel block the group can actually fill, and whoever sizes those blocks so the groups avoid attrition penalties and come back takes the account.

## The Problem
The contract the hotel sends is the standard group agreement. A volunteer signs it, personally or for a small club, and discovers the attrition clause only when the bill arrives.

## Why It's Still Broken
Bureaus avoid advising on contracts to stay neutral among partner hotels, and hotels use one template for all groups.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A plain-language contract explainer the bureau offers every SMERF planner: key clauses highlighted, what they would cost at different pickup levels, and standard questions to ask the hotel, without the bureau taking sides.

## Who Feels the Pain
Volunteer planners and the members who end up paying; hotels and bureaus that lose the repeat booking.

## Impact If Fixed
Fewer surprise penalties and more groups that return.
