# Consumer Event Planning Apps Adapted to Hotel Contracts

**Niche:** [[niches/visitors-bureaus/volunteer-smerf-planners/profile|Volunteer & SMERF Group Planners]]
**Industry:** [[industries/visitors-bureaus|Convention & Visitors Bureaus]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Consumer apps already collect RSVPs and payments for weddings and parties; a reunion or church convention also needs a hotel block managed against its contract.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #automation #worker-facing #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a volunteer planner's loose inquiry for a reunion, church convention or fraternal meeting into a hotel block the group can actually fill, and whoever sizes those blocks so the groups avoid attrition penalties and come back takes the account.

## The Problem
Volunteer planners track RSVPs in spreadsheets and group chats, separately from the hotel block, so they learn about pickup shortfalls only when the hotel reports at the cut-off date.

## What Already Exists
Consumer event apps and RSVP tools (Eventbrite, Evite, The Knot's room-block features for weddings, group chat).

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: RSVPs linked to the hotel block, showing pickup against the block and the cut-off date; reminders to members before the cut-off; plain-language explanation of the contract's attrition and cancellation terms; the bureau's local information and discounts; and a hand-off to the bureau when the group is at risk.

## Target Customer
Bureaus offering free planning services to reunion and SMERF organisers.

## Impact If Solved
Volunteers see trouble before the cut-off, and bureaus intervene in time.
