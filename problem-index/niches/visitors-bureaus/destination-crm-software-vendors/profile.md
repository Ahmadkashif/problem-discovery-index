# Destination CRM & Website Platform Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/visitors-bureaus|Convention & Visitors Bureaus]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Granicus Destinations (Simpleview, bought by Granicus in 2024) and Tempest (iDSS) sell bureaus their CRM, website CMS, partner extranet and data dashboards. Simpleview claimed more than 1,000 destination clients and more than 90% of the top 50 North American meeting destinations. Both sell data products on top: Simpleview's Data Engine, pace reporting and meetings database; Tempest's Sports Tourism Index.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-50 data, insights and analytics staff per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Benchmark dashboards, pace reports, meetings and groups databases, industry trend reports
**Proprietary data:** Lead, booking, partner and website records from hundreds of bureaus' systems
**Clock:** None external; release cycles and client renewals
**Buyer:** Head of Data & Insights / Chief Product Officer

**Kill switches:** live — the CRM, lead and partner records belong to the bureau clients under service agreements, and the platforms are entrenched incumbents whose schema holds the workflow.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. These vendors are the natural channel for the pickup and lead-routing tools in the niche layer, but their cross-client data is not theirs to sell.
