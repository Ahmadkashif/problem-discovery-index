# Convention Center & Headquarters-Hotel Feasibility Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/visitors-bureaus|Convention & Visitors Bureaus]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
A small group of consultancies writes the market and feasibility studies cities and bureaus commission before expanding a convention centre or subsidising a headquarters hotel next to it: CSL International, HVS's convention and sports practice, Hunden Partners, Crossroads Consulting and Johnson Consulting are representative. The study projects events, attendance, room nights and economic impact, and supports a council vote and often a bond issue. Cities, county authorities and bureaus pay per study.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 analysts and consultants per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Market demand and feasibility studies, financial projections, economic and fiscal impact estimates
**Proprietary data:** Benchmarks on competing convention facilities, past projections and event inventories compiled across decades of studies, plus planner surveys the firm runs; much of the facility data is public
**Clock:** Council votes, bond offerings and legislative sessions on the client's project schedule
**Buyer:** Practice leader / firm principal
