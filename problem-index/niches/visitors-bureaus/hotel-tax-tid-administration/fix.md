# The District Renewal Built From Last-Minute Evidence

**Niche:** [[niches/visitors-bureaus/hotel-tax-tid-administration/profile|Hotel Tax & Tourism Improvement District Administration]]
**Industry:** [[industries/visitors-bureaus|Convention & Visitors Bureaus]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A tourism improvement district runs for years, then the bureau assembles the case for renewal in a few months, from reports never designed to answer the hoteliers' question.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #causal-inference #data-integration #compliance #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce, for every hotel-tax and assessment dollar, a record that ties the spending to an eligible purpose and to the overnight stays it produced, and whoever's record holds up through the city audit and the district renewal vote takes the account.

## The Problem
Districts carry fixed terms, after which hoteliers vote on renewal. The bureau must show what the assessment bought. It reaches for annual reports written for a different audience, and the case becomes a presentation rather than evidence.

## Why It's Still Broken
Nobody owns the renewal question during the term, and the bureau's regular reporting tracks activity, not results per assessed hotel or submarket.

## What a Fix Looks Like
From the district's first year, keep a standing renewal file: assessment collected, spending by plan category, and results by submarket (room-night trend against comparable markets, group business placed in assessed hotels). Update it quarterly and share it with the district board.

## Who Feels the Pain
Bureau CEOs facing renewal votes; hotel owners asked to keep paying without clear evidence.

## Impact If Fixed
Renewal becomes a review of evidence collected all along, not a campaign.
