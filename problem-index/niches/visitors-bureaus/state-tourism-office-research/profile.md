# State Tourism Office Research Units

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/visitors-bureaus|Convention & Visitors Bureaus]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
State tourism offices, some government agencies and some nonprofits like the bureaus below them, fund local bureaus through matching grants and co-op programmes, and increasingly buy visitor data licences that local bureaus share. Their research units publish statewide visitor volume, spending and economic impact figures, mostly produced under contract by modelling and survey firms.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 2-8 in-house research staff per state office, with modelling contracted out (estimate)
**Output:** Statewide visitor and economic impact reports, co-op data programmes, legislative briefings
**Proprietary data:** State lodging-tax and sales-tax series, statewide visitor surveys
**Clock:** Legislative appropriation cycles
**Buyer:** Director of Research
