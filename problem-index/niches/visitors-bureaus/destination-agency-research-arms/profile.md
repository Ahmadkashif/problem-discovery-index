# Destination Marketing Agency Research Arms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/visitors-bureaus|Convention & Visitors Bureaus]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The agencies of record that run bureaus' brand and media work keep research groups: MMGY Global's travel intelligence unit (which publishes the long-running Portrait of American Travelers survey) and its NextFactor consultancy (which runs the DestinationNEXT study with Destinations International), and the research and strategy teams at Miles Partnership and Madden Media. They produce syndicated traveller studies, brand tracking and strategy work, mostly to win and keep agency accounts.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-30 researchers and strategists per agency (estimate)
**Output:** Syndicated traveller surveys, brand health tracking, destination strategy and master plans
**Proprietary data:** Long-running traveller panel surveys and years of client campaign results
**Clock:** Agency reviews and bureau planning cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Strategy Officer / Head of Research
