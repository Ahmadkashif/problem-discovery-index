# Event Grants Paid on the Organiser's Estimate

**Niche:** [[niches/visitors-bureaus/sports-tourism-event-bidding/profile|Sports Tourism & Event Bidding]]
**Industry:** [[industries/visitors-bureaus|Convention & Visitors Bureaus]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Sports event grants are approved and paid on room nights the organiser projects, with no reconciliation afterwards.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #evaluation-metrics #compliance #data-integration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to prove how many room nights a tournament actually generated, and whoever counts the teams that booked outside the stay-to-play block most completely takes the account.

## The Problem
An organiser asks for a grant, projecting attendance and room nights. The commission approves and pays, partly or fully before the event. Afterwards it receives a short report repeating the projection.

## Why It's Still Broken
Commissions compete with other cities for the same events and fear that conditions will lose them; the data to check stays is not easily available.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Pay the grant in two parts: a base before the event and a balance per verified room night, documented by the housing company's report and participating hotels' certifications.

## Who Feels the Pain
Commission directors defending grants; hoteliers whose tax funds them; auditors asking how room nights were counted.

## Impact If Fixed
Grants tied to verified stays, at little administrative cost.
