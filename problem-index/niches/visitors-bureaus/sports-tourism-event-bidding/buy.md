# Event Housing Platforms Adapted to Youth Sports Leakage

**Niche:** [[niches/visitors-bureaus/sports-tourism-event-bidding/profile|Sports Tourism & Event Bidding]]
**Industry:** [[industries/visitors-bureaus|Convention & Visitors Bureaus]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Convention housing platforms already manage blocks and pickup for professional meetings; youth tournaments need the same with volunteer team managers and heavy leakage.
**Tags:** #logistic-regression #feature-engineering #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to prove how many room nights a tournament actually generated, and whoever counts the teams that booked outside the stay-to-play block most completely takes the account.

## The Problem
Stay-to-play rules push teams to book through a housing company, but enforcement is uneven and team managers are parents. Leakage undermines both the bureau's count and the tournament's hotel contracts.

## What Already Exists
Convention housing systems (Passkey, onPeak, Cvent's housing tools) manage blocks, attendee booking and pickup reports for meetings.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: team-level rather than attendee-level booking, with one manager booking for many families; enforcement hooks into tournament registration (a team cannot get its schedule until housing is confirmed); rebates split between tournament and team; and reporting in the bureau's room-night and grant format, including estimated leakage.

## Target Customer
Sports commissions and tournament operators running recurring events with stay-to-play policies.

## Impact If Solved
Higher capture of team stays through the block, and a reported room-night figure closer to the truth.
