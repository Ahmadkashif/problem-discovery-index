# Citywide Convention Bids & Incentives

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/visitors-bureaus|Convention & Visitors Bureaus]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to forecast, before the incentive is offered, how many of a citywide convention's contracted room nights will actually be occupied, and whoever's forecast holds up against the post-event housing report takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$500M a year of citywide sales staff, incentive funds, site visits and bid tooling across bureaus with convention centres (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~55% of the convention and group sales niche (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — bids are tracked in the CRM, but the incentive decision and the pickup forecast are judgement and spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** Vice President of Convention Sales / Chief Sales Officer
**Automation Potential:** Medium — forecasting is automatable once pickup data is pooled; the relationship selling is not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A citywide is a meeting big enough to need the convention centre and several hotels at once. The buyer is usually an association whose meeting rotates between a short list of cities and is booked three to ten years out (estimate). Bureaus compete with incentive packages: rebates per realised room night, waived centre rent, transport and marketing support. The bureau commits that money against a block it cannot verify, so the contest is forecasting realised stays. The buyer side of this market is described under [[niches/trade-associations/conference-driven-associations/profile|Conference-Driven Associations]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Simpleview CRM and iDSS pace reports; Knowland and MINT+ meeting histories; the Event Impact Calculator for projected impact; housing companies' pickup reports after the event. Competitors for the bureau's spend are the CRM vendors, the meeting-history vendors and sales consultancies. The gap is a forecast of realised pickup, by group, before the offer.
