# The Housing Report That Stays in the Planner's Inbox

**Niche:** [[niches/visitors-bureaus/citywide-convention-bids/profile|Citywide Convention Bids & Incentives]]
**Industry:** [[industries/visitors-bureaus|Convention & Visitors Bureaus]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** After every citywide the housing company sends a pickup report to the association, and the bureau that bid for the meeting often never gets a usable copy.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #data-integration #evaluation-metrics #workflow-orchestration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to forecast, before the incentive is offered, how many of a citywide convention's contracted room nights will actually be occupied, and whoever's forecast holds up against the post-event housing report takes the account.

## The Problem
Citywide housing is run by a housing company or the bureau's own housing service under the association's contract. The pickup report by hotel and night goes to the association's planner. Unless the bureau asked in advance, it receives a summary or nothing, and its record of the meeting keeps the contracted block.

## Why It's Still Broken
The housing data belongs to the association. Bureaus do not make data sharing a condition of the incentive, because they fear losing the bid.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Write a pickup-report clause into every incentive agreement: the incentive is paid against realised room nights, documented by the housing report and hotel actuals. The bureau gets the data, and the group has a reason to share it.

## Who Feels the Pain
Sales directors who cannot learn from their own bids; finance staff paying incentives on unverified figures.

## Impact If Fixed
Every citywide becomes a labelled outcome, which is what the pickup forecast needs.
