# Hotel Group Displacement Analysis for a Whole City

**Niche:** [[niches/visitors-bureaus/citywide-convention-bids/profile|Citywide Convention Bids & Incentives]]
**Industry:** [[industries/visitors-bureaus|Convention & Visitors Bureaus]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Every hotel already runs displacement analysis to decide whether a group is worth taking; a citywide needs the same analysis across the whole city's hotel inventory and calendar.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #time-series-forecasting #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #feature-engineering #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to forecast, before the incentive is offered, how many of a citywide convention's contracted room nights will actually be occupied, and whoever's forecast holds up against the post-event housing report takes the account.

## The Problem
Some citywides are worth far more to a city than others, depending on the dates. A meeting in a slow week fills rooms that would sit empty; the same meeting in a compressed week displaces transient guests who would have paid more. Bureaus judge this by feel and by the convention centre's need periods.

## What Already Exists
Hotel revenue management systems (IDeaS, Duetto, Amadeus) and group displacement tools evaluate each group against the hotel's forecast transient demand.

## The Customization Gap
The city-level version needs: forecast demand across all hotels in the package rather than one; the convention centre calendar and competing events; a pickup forecast for the group rather than the requested block; incentives and centre-rent waivers as costs; and hotel-tax and visitor-spend value as the objective, not one hotel's profit. Hotels share demand forecasts reluctantly, so the bureau version must work from STR submarket data and the bureau's own calendar.

## Target Customer
Convention sales leaders and convention centre directors deciding which dates to protect for citywides.

## Impact If Solved
Bids and incentives go to groups and dates that add stays the city would not otherwise get.
