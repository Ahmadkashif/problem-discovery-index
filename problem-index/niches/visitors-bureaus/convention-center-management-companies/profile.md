# Convention Center Management Companies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/visitors-bureaus|Convention & Visitors Bureaus]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Private venue managers run publicly owned convention centres under management agreements. Legends completed its acquisition of ASM Global in August 2024, creating a business of roughly 350 venues, and Oak View Group is the other main player. Their corporate teams set booking policy, pricing and food-and-beverage strategy across the portfolio and report to the public owners.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analytics and business-intelligence staff per firm working on convention-centre performance; booking, sales and event staff are production and excluded (estimate)
**Output:** Utilisation and booking pace reports, rate and revenue benchmarks across managed venues, owner reports
**Proprietary data:** Event calendars, space utilisation and event financials across hundreds of venues
**Clock:** Owner reporting cycles and booking windows
**Buyer:** Chief Revenue Officer / Head of Business Intelligence
