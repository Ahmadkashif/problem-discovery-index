# Brand USA International Co-op Marketing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/visitors-bureaus|Convention & Visitors Bureaus]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Brand USA, the national destination marketing organisation, runs international campaigns and sells co-op programmes in which state offices and bureaus buy into national campaigns, trade missions and media in overseas markets. It reports campaign results and international visitation research to partners.

## Insight Function
**Size:** A small research and insights team, under 10 (estimate)
**Output:** International market profiles, campaign return studies, partner reports
**Proprietary data:** Campaign exposure and partner programme data; arrivals data is public
**Clock:** Federal funding and fiscal-year cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Marketing Officer
