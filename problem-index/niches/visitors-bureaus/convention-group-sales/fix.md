# Definite Room Nights Reported at Contract, Never Restated

**Niche:** [[niches/visitors-bureaus/convention-group-sales/profile|Convention & Group Sales]]
**Industry:** [[industries/visitors-bureaus|Convention & Visitors Bureaus]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A bureau counts a booking's full contracted block as room nights produced the day the contract is signed, and almost never corrects the figure after the meeting.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #compliance #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn group demand into hotel room nights that actually happen, but a citywide convention bid and a single-hotel group lead are won on different things: a credible pickup forecast and incentive offer years ahead for the first, speed and hotel fit within days for the second. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Definite room nights are the headline of most bureau sales reports and many city contracts. They are counted at contract signing from the hotel block, often years before the meeting. When the meeting happens, the group may pick up 70% of the block or 110%; the bureau's reported figure stays the same. Over a portfolio, the KPI drifts away from the stays it claims to measure.

## Why It's Still Broken
The industry's own reporting handbook accepts that post-event pickup may not be obtainable, and nothing in the funding contract asks for it. Restating would lower the reported number, and the data has to be requested from housing companies and hotels that have no obligation to provide it.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Report two numbers: room nights contracted, and room nights realised for meetings held, with the realisation rate by segment. Make the post-event pickup request a standard step in the CRM after each meeting, with the housing report attached where one exists.

## Who Feels the Pain
Sales leaders defending numbers they know are soft; boards and councils funding on them; groups whose oversized blocks trigger attrition penalties.

## Impact If Fixed
A small reporting change makes the bureau's main KPI verifiable, and gives every later model its training label.
