# Sales Pipeline Forecasting Pointed at Room Nights

**Niche:** [[niches/visitors-bureaus/convention-group-sales/profile|Convention & Group Sales]]
**Industry:** [[industries/visitors-bureaus|Convention & Visitors Bureaus]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** B2B sales teams already forecast pipeline with probability-weighted deals and win/loss analytics; a bureau's sales team needs the same forecasting, measured in stays rather than dollars.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #logistic-regression #evaluation-metrics #feature-engineering #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn group demand into hotel room nights that actually happen, but a citywide convention bid and a single-hotel group lead are won on different things: a credible pickup forecast and incentive offer years ahead for the first, speed and hotel fit within days for the second. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Bureau sales leaders report a pipeline of leads, tentatives and definites in room nights, weighted by stage percentages that nobody calibrates. The board sees a number that assumes every tentative converts at the same rate and every definite fills its block.

## What Already Exists
Revenue intelligence tools (Salesforce Einstein forecasting, Clari, Gong) score deal probability from activity and history and track forecast accuracy against closed deals.

## The Customization Gap
The bureau version needs: the unit of value changed from contract dollars to realised room nights and visitor spend; a second outcome after the close (pickup against block, years later), which B2B forecasting never models; rotation and competing-city features from meeting-history data; incentives treated as an input whose effect must be estimated, not a discount; and the reporting definitions of the industry's performance handbook (lead, tentative, definite, lost with reason) built in. It must read from the destination CRM rather than replace it.

## Target Customer
Chief sales officers and directors of sales operations at large bureaus already on Simpleview CRM or iDSS.

## Impact If Solved
A calibrated pipeline in room nights that the board can rely on, and a forecast-accuracy record per segment that shows where the sales effort pays.
