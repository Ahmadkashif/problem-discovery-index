# Convention & Group Sales

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/visitors-bureaus|Convention & Visitors Bureaus]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn group demand into hotel room nights that actually happen, but a citywide convention bid and a single-hotel group lead are won on different things: a credible pickup forecast and incentive offer years ahead for the first, speed and hotel fit within days for the second. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$900M a year of bureau spending on convention and group sales: sales and services staff, incentive funds, trade shows and sales missions, destination CRMs and lead platforms (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~30-35% of bureau budgets at destinations with a convention centre, much less at leisure-only bureaus (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — every sizeable bureau runs a destination CRM and receives RFPs through Cvent, but the bid decision and the pickup that follows live in the director's head and the hotels' systems
**Target Buyer:** Vice President of Sales / Chief Sales Officer
**Automation Potential:** High for lead handling and proposals, medium for bid and incentive decisions

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Group business is the part of a bureau's work that produces a countable result: a contract with hotel room nights in it. It is also the part its funders scrutinise most, because definite room nights are written into most city services contracts. The work splits into two motions. National and citywide sales chase large associations whose meetings rotate between convention cities and are booked years out, with incentive money on the table. In-house group sales route a steady flow of smaller RFPs to partner hotels and win or lose within days. The same team often does both, but the inputs, clocks and competitors differ.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Destination CRMs (Simpleview CRM from Granicus Destinations, Tempest's iDSS) track leads and room nights; Cvent carries planner RFPs; Knowland and MINT+ supply meeting history; housing companies manage citywide reservations. Gaps: no record of the director's forecast at the time of the bid, pickup rarely reported back, and lead routing done from memory.
