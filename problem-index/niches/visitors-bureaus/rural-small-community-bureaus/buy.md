# Small-Business Reporting Assistants Pointed at Lodging Tax

**Niche:** [[niches/visitors-bureaus/rural-small-community-bureaus/profile|Rural & Small-Community Bureaus]]
**Industry:** [[industries/visitors-bureaus|Convention & Visitors Bureaus]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** AI reporting assistants already turn small businesses' spreadsheets into summaries; a one-person bureau needs one that knows its funder's questions.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #descriptive-statistics #automation #data-integration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give a bureau of one to four people a defensible answer to "what did the lodging tax buy?" from data it already has, and whoever delivers that board-ready answer without the bureau hiring an analyst takes the account.

## The Problem
The director spends days each quarter turning website analytics, social stats, visitor centre counts and lodging-tax receipts into a board packet and a report to the county.

## What Already Exists
General AI assistants and BI tools with natural-language summaries (Microsoft Copilot in Excel and Power BI, Google Sheets with Gemini, Looker Studio templates).

## The Customization Gap
The bureau version needs: connectors to the small set of sources these bureaus use (Google Analytics, Meta, Mailchimp, the county's lodging-tax spreadsheet, a state co-op dashboard); the funder's report template and statutory categories; tourism definitions (visitor, overnight, day-tripper) applied consistently; and caveats written in plain language rather than left out.

## Target Customer
Directors of bureaus with one to four staff.

## Impact If Solved
Quarterly reporting drops from days to hours, and the reports say the same thing the same way each time.
