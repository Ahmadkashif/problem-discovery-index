# Rural & Small-Community Bureaus

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/visitors-bureaus|Convention & Visitors Bureaus]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give a bureau of one to four people a defensible answer to "what did the lodging tax buy?" from data it already has, and whoever delivers that board-ready answer without the bureau hiring an analyst takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$500M a year in combined budgets of bureaus with fewer than five employees, including chamber-run and county tourism offices (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~58% of employer establishments but ~15-20% of spending (County Business Patterns 2023 for the establishment share; spending share estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — a website, social accounts and email newsletters, usually without a CRM or any visitor data licence
**Target Buyer:** Executive Director (often the only full-time employee)
**Automation Potential:** High for reporting and content; the bureau's local relationships are not automatable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Most bureaus are small: 545 of 940 establishments had fewer than five employees in 2023, and many more sit inside chambers of commerce or county governments outside that count. The director runs marketing, the visitor centre, the board, the lodging-tax report and partner relations alone. Small bureaus received about 90% of their funding from public sources in the last public financial-profile study, so they face the same "prove it" scrutiny as large bureaus with none of the staff. Tools are priced and designed for metro bureaus.

## Current Tools & Gaps
WordPress or a low-tier Simpleview or Tempest site; Mailchimp; Canva; sometimes a Placer.ai licence or a share of a state tourism office's co-op data programme; Key Data or AirDNA for rentals. Competitors are the state office co-op programmes, the low tiers of the destination platforms, and local agencies. Gaps: no one to turn data into an answer, and no way to compare with similar towns.
