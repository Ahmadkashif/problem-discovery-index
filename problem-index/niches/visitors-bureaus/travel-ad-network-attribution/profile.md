# Travel Ad Networks' Destination Attribution Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/visitors-bureaus|Convention & Visitors Bureaus]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Travel media networks such as Sojern, Expedia Group Media Solutions and Adara sell bureaus campaigns targeted with their own traveller search and booking data, and report results as attribution studies: hotel bookings, flights and room nights by people who saw the ads. The attribution report is what decides whether the bureau renews the media buy.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 analysts and data scientists per network serving destination clients (estimate)
**Output:** Campaign attribution and booking-lift reports, traveller-intent and source-market insight decks
**Proprietary data:** Search, shopping and booking records from the network's own platforms and partners
**Clock:** Campaign flights and bureaus' fiscal-year budget decisions
**Buyer:** Head of Destination Partnerships / Director of Measurement
