# Tourism Improvement District Advisors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/visitors-bureaus|Convention & Visitors Bureaus]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Specialist advisors, Civitas being the best known, help bureaus and hotel associations form and renew tourism improvement districts: drafting the management district plan, calculating assessments, organising the hoteliers' petition and steering the process through council. They also track district counts nationally (211 districts across 23 states in a 2024 Civitas-sourced handout).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Firms of roughly 5-20 people, with analysts under 10 (estimate)
**Output:** District formation and renewal plans, assessment models, legal and procedural guidance
**Proprietary data:** District plans and assessment histories across many districts
**Clock:** Statutory formation and renewal procedures and district expiry dates
**Buyer:** Firm principal
