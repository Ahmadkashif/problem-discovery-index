# Single-Hotel Group Lead Distribution

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/visitors-bureaus|Convention & Visitors Bureaus]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to put each planner's RFP in front of the two or three partner hotels that fit it and get a proposal back inside the planner's deadline, and whoever converts the most RFPs into contracted blocks per sales-manager hour takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M a year of in-house group sales staff time, lead platforms and proposal tooling (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~35% of the convention and group sales niche (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — RFPs arrive digitally and CRMs distribute them, but fit and routing are judged by memory
**Target Buyer:** Director of Sales / Director of Sales Operations
**Automation Potential:** High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Most group business a bureau touches is a meeting of 10-300 peak rooms in one hotel (estimate). The planner wants options fast and will drop a city that answers slowly. Here the bureau competes with routes that bypass it: Cvent sends RFPs straight to hotels, third-party sourcing firms such as HelmsBriscoe place meetings for a commission (see [[niches/event-planning/venue-sourcing-intermediaries/profile|🔍 Venue Sourcing Intermediaries]]), and hotel brands run their own group sales. The bureau's value is knowing which local hotels fit, and proving it quickly.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Simpleview CRM and iDSS lead distribution and hotel extranets; Cvent's sourcing platform; Groups360's GroupSync; proposal builders such as DIGIDECK. Gaps: duplicate leads from several routes, routing by distribution list, no record of which hotels respond and win for which groups.
