# One RFP, Three Leads, Three Credits

**Niche:** [[niches/visitors-bureaus/single-hotel-group-leads/profile|Single-Hotel Group Lead Distribution]]
**Industry:** [[industries/visitors-bureaus|Convention & Visitors Bureaus]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The same meeting reaches a hotel through Cvent, a sourcing firm and the bureau, and each party claims the booking.
**Tags:** #word-embeddings #k-nearest-neighbors #data-integration #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to put each planner's RFP in front of the two or three partner hotels that fit it and get a proposal back inside the planner's deadline, and whoever converts the most RFPs into contracted blocks per sales-manager hour takes the account.

## The Problem
A planner sends an RFP through Cvent, a sourcing firm sends the same meeting, and the bureau forwards it again. Hotels answer the same request three times; when it books, the bureau, the sourcing firm and the hotel each count it.

## Why It's Still Broken
Each channel has its own record and no shared identifier for the meeting. Bureaus count leads generated, so duplicates inflate their reports.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Near-duplicate detection on incoming RFPs (organisation, dates, room pattern) before distribution, one lead record per meeting with every source noted, and a credit rule agreed with partner hotels in advance.

## Who Feels the Pain
Hotel sales managers answering duplicates; bureau managers in credit disputes; boards reading inflated lead counts.

## Impact If Fixed
Cleaner lead counts, fewer wasted hotel hours and fewer credit fights.
