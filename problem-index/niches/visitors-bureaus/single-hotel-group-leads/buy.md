# Sales Engagement Routing Adapted to Hotel Partners

**Niche:** [[niches/visitors-bureaus/single-hotel-group-leads/profile|Single-Hotel Group Lead Distribution]]
**Industry:** [[industries/visitors-bureaus|Convention & Visitors Bureaus]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Inbound lead routing and SLA tracking are solved for B2B sales teams; a bureau routes to a network of independent hotels it does not manage.
**Tags:** #logistic-regression #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #automation #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to put each planner's RFP in front of the two or three partner hotels that fit it and get a proposal back inside the planner's deadline, and whoever converts the most RFPs into contracted blocks per sales-manager hour takes the account.

## The Problem
Once a lead is sent, the bureau has no control over the hotels' response. Deadlines slip, the proposal waits on the slowest hotel, and the planner moves on.

## What Already Exists
Lead routing and engagement platforms (Salesforce lead assignment, LeanData, HubSpot workflows) assign leads by rules, track response SLAs and escalate.

## The Customization Gap
The bureau version needs: routing to external partners, through the CRM extranet, with no authority over them; deadlines set by each planner's RFP rather than an internal SLA; partner-level response and win tracking reported back to hotels as a service; duplicate detection across Cvent, email and third-party routes; and the industry's lead definitions (ten peak rooms and above) built into the record.

## Target Customer
Sales operations managers at bureaus on Simpleview CRM or iDSS.

## Impact If Solved
Proposals go out on time with every fitting hotel included, and hotels see their own response record.
