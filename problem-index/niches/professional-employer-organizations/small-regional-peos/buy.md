# Broker Renewal Analytics Adapted to a Small PEO's Book

**Niche:** [[niches/professional-employer-organizations/small-regional-peos/profile|Small & Regional PEOs]]
**Industry:** [[industries/professional-employer-organizations|Professional Employer Organizations]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Benefits brokerages already run renewal and benchmarking analytics for mid-sized employers; a small PEO needs the same tools pointed at its pooled book and its carrier negotiations.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #bayesian-inference #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #revenue-impact #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give a PEO with a few thousand worksite employees a health plan and a workers' comp programme it can sell against ADP TotalSource, Insperity and TriNet without an actuarial department of its own, and whoever keeps that PEO's carriers renewing its programmes on workable terms takes the account.

## The Problem
At renewal, the small PEO receives its carrier's proposal and has no independent view of whether it is fair: what its book should cost, how its client mix compares, and where its losses came from.

## What Already Exists
Benefits brokerage analytics groups project claims, model plan designs and benchmark employers against their book of business, as logged under HR Consultants as [[niches/hr-consultants/benefits-brokerage-analytics/profile|🔍 Employee Benefits Brokerage Analytics]]. Comp brokers produce loss-run analyses and mod reviews for employers.

## The Customization Gap
The analysis must treat the PEO's book as a pool of clients rather than one employer. That means cohort and size-band results, the effect of new business versus renewals, and large-claim pooling at the programme's terms. It must cover both lines the PEO buys, health and workers' comp, with client-level comp loss data in NCCI's master-policy format, and produce carrier-ready renewal submissions instead of employer benefit reports.

## Target Customer
Small and regional PEOs buying health and comp programmes through brokers or consultants each year.

## Impact If Solved
A small PEO negotiates its renewal with its own evidence, using analytics brokerages already maintain, instead of accepting the carrier's view.
