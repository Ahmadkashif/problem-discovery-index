# The Workers' Comp Programme Lost at Renewal

**Niche:** [[niches/professional-employer-organizations/small-regional-peos/profile|Small & Regional PEOs]]
**Industry:** [[industries/professional-employer-organizations|Professional Employer Organizations]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A small PEO learns ninety days before renewal that its comp carrier will not renew the programme, and spends the quarter rebuilding submissions while competitors call its clients.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #time-series-forecasting #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #compliance #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give a PEO with a few thousand worksite employees a health plan and a workers' comp programme it can sell against ADP TotalSource, Insperity and TriNet without an actuarial department of its own, and whoever keeps that PEO's carriers renewing its programmes on workable terms takes the account.

## The Problem
PEO comp programmes are underwritten on the whole book: payroll by class, loss history, loss control and claims handling. Programmes such as Amwins' decline accounts with inadequate payroll detail or poor loss history and no remediation plan. A small PEO sends its data once a year, so the carrier's concerns build silently until a non-renewal notice arrives. The replacement market then wants the same data in another format.

## Why It's Still Broken
The data lives in the PEO platform, the claims system and spreadsheets. Nobody at a small PEO owns a monthly programme report, and carriers do not share their internal view until renewal.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A standing programme dashboard built from payroll by class and client, claims and loss development, and loss-control activity, refreshed monthly and shared with the carrier quarterly. It flags the drift (class mix moving toward higher hazard, large claims, late reporting) the carrier will act on, and keeps a submission-ready package for any market at any time.

## Who Feels the Pain
Small-PEO owners, their risk managers, and clients whose rates change abruptly when a programme moves.

## Impact If Fixed
Fewer surprise non-renewals, faster remarketing when they happen, and a carrier relationship run on shared evidence.
