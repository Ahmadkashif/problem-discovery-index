# ESAC Accreditation & Financial Assurance

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/professional-employer-organizations|Professional Employer Organizations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Employer Services Assurance Corporation accredits PEOs against financial and operating standards and backs them with surety. It buys a $15M aggregate bond each year for accredited PEOs, each of which also buys an individual bond of $250,000-$1M. Accredited PEOs submit audited statements and quarterly independent CPA verification that payroll taxes, benefit contributions and insurance premiums were paid. PEOs pay accreditation fees and market the seal to clients and brokers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Small staff (estimate under 10); the verification work is done by the PEOs' own CPAs
**Output:** Accreditation decisions and the surety programme
**Proprietary data:** Quarterly compliance submissions from accredited PEOs
**Clock:** Quarterly submission dates
**Buyer:** ESAC executive director
