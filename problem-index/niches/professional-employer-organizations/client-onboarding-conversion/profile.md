# New-Client Onboarding & Mid-Year Conversion

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/professional-employer-organizations|Professional Employer Organizations]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to convert a signed client's year-to-date wages, deductions, benefit elections, state tax accounts and workers' comp classes onto the PEO by the effective date, and whoever delivers a correct first payroll with coverage active on day one, and no amended filings afterwards, takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$800M a year of implementation staff time, conversion services and onboarding tooling at US PEOs (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of PEO industry gross profit (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — the target platform is digital; the inputs are spreadsheets, PDFs and email
**Target Buyer:** VP of Implementation / Client Onboarding
**Automation Potential:** High for data mapping and reconciliation; medium for chasing the client

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A PEO replaces close to a fifth of its clients each year (Insperity's retention was about 83% in 2025) and onboards them on fixed effective dates, often mid-year. Each conversion is a project with a hard deadline. Year-to-date wages and taxes must be loaded so wage bases and W-2s are right. Benefit elections must reach the carrier before employees need care, and state accounts must be set up under the right reporting regime. Workers' comp classes have to be assigned, new hire paperwork completed, and the ACA measurement history carried over. NAPEO's own ACA best practice starts at onboarding. Errors made here surface for a year as amended filings, benefit denials and invoice disputes.

## Current Tools & Gaps
The PEO platform's import tools, implementation checklists in ClientSpace-style workflow tools, carrier enrolment portals, and spreadsheets. The PEO-alternative consultants who replicate this work without co-employment are covered under HR Consultants as [[niches/hr-consultants/peo-alternative-consultants/profile|PEO Alternative Consultants]]. Gaps: year-to-date data rekeyed; carrier eligibility lagging the effective date; prior-state accounts left open; no reconciliation of first quarterly filings against the prior provider's.
