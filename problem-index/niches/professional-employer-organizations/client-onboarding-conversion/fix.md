# Coverage That Starts After the First Pharmacy Visit

**Niche:** [[niches/professional-employer-organizations/client-onboarding-conversion/profile|New-Client Onboarding & Mid-Year Conversion]]
**Industry:** [[industries/professional-employer-organizations|Professional Employer Organizations]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A new client's employees are covered from the effective date on paper, but the carrier's eligibility file lags, and the first week brings denied prescriptions and angry calls to the client owner.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to convert a signed client's year-to-date wages, deductions, benefit elections, state tax accounts and workers' comp classes onto the PEO by the effective date, and whoever delivers a correct first payroll with coverage active on day one, and no amended filings afterwards, takes the account.

## The Problem
Benefit elections are collected late, keyed into the PEO's system and sent to the carrier on the next scheduled eligibility file. ID cards follow later. An employee refilling a prescription on day three is told they have no coverage. The client owner, who joined the PEO for its health plan, hears about it first.

## Why It's Still Broken
Carrier eligibility files run on fixed schedules. Elections arrive incomplete, and implementers prioritise payroll, the more visible deadline. No one owns the gap between "elected" and "active in the carrier's system".

## What a Fix Looks Like
An onboarding tracker that treats carrier eligibility as a go-live criterion. It chases missing elections before the effective date, triggers an off-cycle eligibility submission for new clients, confirms each member's active status with the carrier, and gives employees temporary proof of coverage until cards arrive.

## Who Feels the Pain
Worksite employees at the pharmacy counter, client owners, and implementation teams fielding the calls.

## Impact If Fixed
The first impression of the PEO's health plan becomes a non-event, removing an early cause of client dissatisfaction and churn.
