# PEO Brokers & Placement Consultants

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/professional-employer-organizations|Professional Employer Organizations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Independent advisers and insurance agencies that help small businesses choose a PEO: they gather the census, request proposals from several PEOs, translate per-employee and percentage-of-payroll pricing into comparable totals, and flag contract terms such as termination and fee-increase rights. Buyer guides describe them as free to the client and paid by the PEO, which shapes the options a client is shown. Insperity's own sales force, an average of 612 Business Performance Advisors in 2025, competes with this channel.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 1-15 people per firm (estimate)
**Output:** PEO comparisons and placement
**Proprietary data:** Proposal files and placement history
**Clock:** The client's renewal or effective date
**Buyer:** Agency principal
