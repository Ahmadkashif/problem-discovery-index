# NAPEO & State PEO Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/professional-employer-organizations|Professional Employer Organizations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The National Association of Professional Employer Organizations and state PEO associations lobby on PEO licensing, tax and benefits rules and publish the industry's statistics. The research is commissioned: the industry footprint (523 PEOs, 4.5M worksite employees at end-2022), the 2025 client profile and the 2024 economic-value study were all written by Laurie Bassi and Dan McMurrer of McBassi & Company. The 2024 study found PEO clients grew 4.3% against 1.6% and were 50% less likely to fail, with the authors flagging selection effects. Members fund the work through dues.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research and policy staff (estimate), with major studies outsourced
**Output:** Footprint and economic-value white papers, state legislative tracking, best-practice guidance
**Proprietary data:** Member surveys and a client database drawn from member submissions
**Clock:** Legislative sessions; study cycles self-set
**Buyer:** NAPEO President and CEO
