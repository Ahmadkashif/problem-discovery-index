# Renewal Increases Sent Without a Reason

**Niche:** [[niches/professional-employer-organizations/renewal-rate-action-retention/profile|In-Force Renewal Rate Action]]
**Industry:** [[industries/professional-employer-organizations|Professional Employer Organizations]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** An account manager receives a 14% renewal for a client with no explanation they can repeat, and the client's broker fills the silence with a competitor's quote.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #descriptive-statistics #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to set each in-force client's health renewal from a year of claims too thin to be credible on its own, and whoever keeps the plan's loss ratio on target while losing the fewest low-claim clients to an ACA small-group or level-funded quote at renewal takes the account.

## The Problem
Renewal rates come out of the underwriting system as numbers. The account manager who must deliver them cannot say how much is plan trend, how much is the client's own experience, and how much is an age or tier shift in the census, and cannot discuss member-level claims anyway. Clients hear "the market went up" and shop.

## Why It's Still Broken
HIPAA limits what can be said about a small group's claims. Underwriting has no incentive to write explanations, and the account manager's script is generic.

## What a Fix Looks Like
An automatically drafted renewal explanation that decomposes the change into trend, demographic shift, plan-design effects and experience at a level of aggregation safe to share. It adds the client's options (a plan-design buy-down, a contribution change) priced on the spot, with a compliance check that no member-level detail leaks.

## Who Feels the Pain
Account managers delivering bad news, client owners making benefit decisions blind, and the PEO losing clients it priced correctly.

## Impact If Fixed
Fewer departures from clients whose increase was justified but unexplained, at almost no cost to produce.
