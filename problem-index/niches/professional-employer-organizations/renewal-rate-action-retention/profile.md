# In-Force Renewal Rate Action

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/professional-employer-organizations|Professional Employer Organizations]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to set each in-force client's health renewal from a year of claims too thin to be credible on its own, and whoever keeps the plan's loss ratio on target while losing the fewest low-claim clients to an ACA small-group or level-funded quote at renewal takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$350M a year of renewal underwriting, actuarial and account-management effort on PEO health renewals (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3.5% of PEO industry gross profit (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — renewal rates come out of actuarial spreadsheets and carrier reports; the conversation with the client is by phone
**Target Buyer:** Chief Actuary / VP of Client Retention
**Automation Potential:** Medium — rate calculation automates; the retention conversation does not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Once a client is inside, the PEO knows it: twelve months of claims, enrolment changes and service history. The renewal decision is a credibility problem, how much to trust a 25-person group's own year, and a retention problem. A client that had a quiet year can buy elsewhere: an ACA small-group plan, a level-funded product or another PEO's pool. A client that had a bad year will stay at almost any price. The PrestigePEO deck shows the tension: smaller groups pay higher premiums and run lower loss ratios (81% for 1-9 subscribers against 97% for 100+), and its growth arithmetic assumes a plan loses about 12% of enrolment a year. Insperity's overall client retention was about 83% in 2025.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Actuarial credibility formulas in spreadsheets, carrier renewal calculations, and account managers' judgement about who will walk. Gaps: no model of each client's outside alternative or price sensitivity; renewals sent without explanation; and no measure of which rate actions caused which departures.
