# Carrier Large-Group Credibility Rating Adapted to Client Renewals

**Niche:** [[niches/professional-employer-organizations/renewal-rate-action-retention/profile|In-Force Renewal Rate Action]]
**Industry:** [[industries/professional-employer-organizations|Professional Employer Organizations]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Health carriers have long rated large employer groups by blending their experience with manual rates; a PEO needs the same machinery applied to hundreds of tiny groups inside one plan.
**Tags:** #bayesian-inference #maximum-a-posteriori-estimation #confidence-intervals #evaluation-metrics #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to set each in-force client's health renewal from a year of claims too thin to be credible on its own, and whoever keeps the plan's loss ratio on target while losing the fewest low-claim clients to an ACA small-group or level-funded quote at renewal takes the account.

## The Problem
PEO renewal actuaries maintain credibility tables in spreadsheets and apply them client by client, with manual caps and exceptions negotiated by account managers.

## What Already Exists
Carrier underwriting systems for large-group renewals apply experience rating with credibility weighting, large-claim pooling, trend and plan-design adjustments, and produce a documented renewal. Actuarial consultancies build the same models for self-funded employers.

## The Customization Gap
For a PEO, credibility for a 20-person group is near zero, so the model must borrow strength from cohorts of similar clients rather than from a manual rate alone. Pooling points are set by the PEO's plan, not the client. Renewals must respect the client service agreement's pricing period (one year at Insperity, two for its middle market). And output has to feed the PEO's bundled invoice, where the health rate sits next to admin fees, workers' comp and unemployment charges.

## Target Customer
Renewal underwriting teams at PEOs large enough to employ actuaries but still pricing renewals in spreadsheets.

## Impact If Solved
Consistent, documented renewals at scale, built on rating logic carriers already trust, with exceptions visible rather than negotiated away.
