# PEO Health Plan & Underwriting Consultants

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/professional-employer-organizations|Professional Employer Organizations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Consultancies and brokerage practices that advise PEOs on their master health plans: plan funding and carrier negotiation, new-business risk assessment and pricing, and renewal strategy. PEO Velocity (whose former senior VP told a 2025 webinar she had worked with 40+ PEOs on master-plan risk) and OneDigital's PEO practice, which advertises a pricing consultant to advise PEOs on new-business pricing and "when to walk away", are examples. PEOs pay consulting fees or commissions. The cross-PEO studies these firms run, such as the 26,000-member plan analysis and a size-cohort table spanning 10,726 subscriber worksites across PEOs and carriers, are the best public evidence of how PEO plans actually perform.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 underwriters, actuaries and analysts per practice (estimate)
**Output:** Master-plan funding and renewal analyses, new-business risk assessments and pricing guidance, carrier marketing, cross-PEO performance studies
**Proprietary data:** Plan and client-level experience across many PEOs' master plans, held under engagement and business associate agreements
**Clock:** Master-plan renewal and carrier contract dates, hard but set by contract rather than a regulator
**Buyer:** PEO VP of Benefits / CFO

**Kill switches:** **HIPAA, and data isn't theirs** — the cross-PEO experience is the PEOs' plans' claims and questionnaire data, protected health information held under business associate and engagement terms, and it belongs to each PEO's plan. Scored and switched consistently with [[niches/hr-consultants/benefits-brokerage-analytics/profile|🔍 Employee Benefits Brokerage Analytics]] (47) and [[niches/health-insurance-carriers/health-actuarial-consulting/profile|🔍 Health Actuarial Consulting for Rate Filings & MA Bids]] (48).
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. Q5 is 2 because the market is a handful of practices (PEO Velocity, OneDigital's PEO team and a few others). The only parties that see many PEOs' plans side by side, selling insight PEOs pay for, built on data that is neither theirs nor shareable.
