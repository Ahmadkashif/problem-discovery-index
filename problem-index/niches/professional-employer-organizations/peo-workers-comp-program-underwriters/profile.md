# PEO Workers' Comp Program Underwriters & MGAs

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/professional-employer-organizations|Professional Employer Organizations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Carriers and programme administrators that write workers' compensation for PEOs, on a master policy covering all clients or on multiple coordinated policies, one per client. Chubb has written Insperity's fully insured programme since 2007, with Insperity retaining the first $1.5M per occurrence. Amwins Specialty Casualty Solutions runs a PEO programme for mixed-class payrolls across NCCI hazard groups. These underwriters decide which PEOs to write, price the book from payroll by class and client-level loss history, set deductibles and loss-control conditions, and, through the PEO's own guidelines, shape which clients the PEO may accept.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 underwriters, actuaries and loss-control staff per carrier or programme (estimate)
**Output:** Programme pricing and terms, PEO eligibility decisions, client acceptance guidelines, loss-control requirements
**Proprietary data:** Payroll by class and client-level losses across the PEO books they write, the systematic record of comp outcomes for co-employed small employers
**Clock:** Programme effective dates (Insperity's current Chubb coverage runs to 30 September 2026) and NCCI's client-level reporting for master policies; renewal dates are contractual
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer / Programme Director
