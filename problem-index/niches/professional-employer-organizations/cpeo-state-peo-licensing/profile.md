# IRS CPEO Program & State PEO Licensing Units

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/professional-employer-organizations|Professional Employer Organizations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The IRS certifies PEOs under its voluntary CPEO programme and maintains the public list (122 active certified entities as of August 2026), reviews quarterly assertions, CPA attestations, audited statements and bonds, and suspends or revokes certifications. In the 42 states that regulate PEOs, a state agency unit recognises, licenses or registers them and checks their financial statements and bonds. PEO health plans regulated as multiple employer welfare arrangements fall to the state and federal bodies logged under Self-Insured Benefit & Risk Pools.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Small review units, typically a few staff per state and a modest IRS programme office (estimate); reviewers are production
**Output:** Certifications, licences, suspensions
**Proprietary data:** Filed assertions, financial statements and bonds
**Clock:** Statutory filing dates
**Buyer:** IRS programme manager / state licensing director
