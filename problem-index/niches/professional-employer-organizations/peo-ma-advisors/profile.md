# PEO M&A Advisers & Valuation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/professional-employer-organizations|Professional Employer Organizations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Boutique bankers, brokers and valuation firms that sell independent PEOs to rollups such as Vensure, private equity and the payroll majors, value books of worksite employees and gross profit, and run the diligence on retained workers' comp, health plan funding and payroll-tax exposures. Sellers pay success fees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 2-10 per boutique (estimate)
**Output:** Valuations, offering memoranda, deal processes
**Proprietary data:** Deal comparables and buyer relationships
**Clock:** Deal timetables
**Buyer:** Managing partner
