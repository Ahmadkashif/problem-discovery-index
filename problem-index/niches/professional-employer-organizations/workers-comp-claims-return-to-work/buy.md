# Claims Platforms Adapted to the Co-Employer's Seat

**Niche:** [[niches/professional-employer-organizations/workers-comp-claims-return-to-work/profile|Workers' Comp Claims & Return-to-Work at Client Worksites]]
**Industry:** [[industries/professional-employer-organizations|Professional Employer Organizations]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Claims administration systems are built for the adjuster; the PEO coordinator sits between adjuster, client and worker and needs a different view of the same claim.
**Tags:** #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #evaluation-metrics #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get an injury at a client site the PEO never controls reported within a day and the worker back on modified duty at an employer with fifteen staff, and whoever closes the most claims inside the PEO's deductible at the lowest incurred cost takes the account.

## The Problem
PEO coordinators read claim status in carrier portals, track client contacts in email and keep deductible-layer costs in spreadsheets. When a PEO uses more than one carrier, or changes carriers, history splits across systems.

## What Already Exists
Carrier and TPA claims platforms and risk management information systems manage reserves, payments, diaries, medical bills and regulatory reporting, and roll up loss runs for risk managers.

## The Customization Gap
The PEO needs the client as the organising unit. That means each client's contacts, safety history, transitional-duty capacity and charge-backs under its service agreement, a deductible-layer cost view per claim and per client feeding renewal pricing, and state injury forms pre-filled from the PEO's own payroll data. It also needs messaging to client owners and workers who will never log into a portal, and client-level loss data in the shape NCCI's PEO rules require for master policies.

## Target Customer
PEO risk departments with comp programmes on multiple carriers or with large retained deductibles.

## Impact If Solved
One view of every claim from the employer's seat, tied to the client and to the PEO's own money, on infrastructure risk managers already buy.
