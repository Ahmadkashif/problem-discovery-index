# Transitional Duty Matched to a Fifteen-Person Employer

**Niche:** [[niches/professional-employer-organizations/workers-comp-claims-return-to-work/profile|Workers' Comp Claims & Return-to-Work at Client Worksites]]
**Industry:** [[industries/professional-employer-organizations|Professional Employer Organizations]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Lost-time days drive comp costs, and the cheapest way to cut them, modified duty, fails at small clients because the owner cannot think of a job the injured worker could do.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #k-nearest-neighbors #survival-analysis #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #workflow-orchestration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get an injury at a client site the PEO never controls reported within a day and the worker back on modified duty at an employer with fifteen staff, and whoever closes the most claims inside the PEO's deductible at the lowest incurred cost takes the account.

## The Problem
A tile installer at a 12-person contractor is released for work with a 10-pound lifting limit. The owner says there is no light work. The worker stays home on temporary total disability, and every week is paid from the PEO's deductible layer. The coordinator knows modified duty shortens claims, but has no practical suggestions for a tile contractor. Large employers have transitional-duty programmes and job banks; small clients have nothing.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Return-to-work programmes are designed for large self-insured employers with many job types. Claims systems record work status but not available duties. And the knowledge of what light work exists at a dental office or a landscaping firm lives with experienced coordinators and loss-control consultants.

## What to Build
A transitional-duty engine. It holds a library of tasks by industry and job (inventory, estimating support, safety-training delivery, customer calls, vehicle inspections), each tagged with physical demands. Each restriction note is matched to tasks the client can actually offer, with a drafted offer letter that meets the state's requirements. Outcomes feed back by industry and restriction: offered, accepted, days to full duty. Where a client truly has nothing, it can point to a community or non-profit placement programme.

## Target Customer
Risk and claims leaders at PEOs retaining comp deductibles, and the programme administrators who write PEO comp.

## Impact If Built
More injured workers back on modified duty within days rather than weeks. On a book where lost-time claims carry most of the cost, that is a direct cut in retained losses (estimate), and injured workers keep earning and stay connected to their job.
