# PEO Rollup Platforms' Corporate Development

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/professional-employer-organizations|Professional Employer Organizations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Private-equity-backed acquirers that buy small PEOs and payroll bureaus and migrate their clients onto one platform. Vensure Employer Solutions announced its 108th acquisition in April 2026, backed by a $450M financing, and counts ~4 million worksite employees on its platform including payroll-only clients. Their corporate development teams screen targets and value books of clients, including the workers' comp, health plan and payroll-tax liabilities that come with them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically under 10 corporate development staff (estimate); diligence on insurance and tax liabilities is bought from accountants and actuaries
**Output:** Acquisition models and integration plans, used internally
**Proprietary data:** Diligence files on acquired and declined targets
**Clock:** Deal timetables, self-set
**Buyer:** Chief Development Officer
