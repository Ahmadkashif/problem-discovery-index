# State UI Tax Experience-Rating Units

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/professional-employer-organizations|Professional Employer Organizations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State workforce agencies' unemployment tax divisions register employer accounts, compute each account's contribution rate from its benefit charges and taxable payroll, issue annual rate notices, and police transfers of experience under the SUTA-dumping rules states adopted after 2004. For PEOs they also decide, by statute, whether a PEO reports under its own account or each client's, and how a client's experience moves when it joins or leaves a PEO. Indiana's agency, for example, gives a PEO reporting at PEO level one account for all its clients. The benefit-paying side of the same agencies belongs to the queued Benefits Administration Agencies industry, not to this pocket.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Large tax divisions, but rate computation is automated and the staff are account, audit and collections production (estimate)
**Output:** Rate notices, account registrations, audit findings
**Proprietary data:** Every covered employer's wage and benefit-charge history in the state
**Clock:** Annual rate cycles and quarterly filing dates, set by statute
**Buyer:** State UI tax director
