# Self-Funded Plan Analytics Adapted to a Multi-Client Pool

**Niche:** [[niches/professional-employer-organizations/master-health-plan-pricing/profile|Master Health Plan Pricing & Underwriting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/professional-employer-organizations|Professional Employer Organizations]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Health plan analytics platforms already turn claims feeds into cost and risk dashboards for one employer; a PEO needs the same view cut by hundreds of clients, cohorts and rate actions.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #bayesian-inference #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #revenue-impact #automation
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a PEO's master health plan at its target loss ratio while still undercutting what small employers would pay on their own. But that is two contests with different winners: selecting and pricing prospects from a census and a few morbidity flags before a broker's quote deadline, which new-business underwriters and scoring vendors win, and setting each in-force client's renewal so low-claim groups stay and the plan does not spiral, which renewal actuaries and account teams win. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Large PEOs with minimum-premium or self-funded arrangements receive claims data, but analyse it in spreadsheets built by one actuary. Smaller PEOs rely on whatever the carrier's monthly report shows.

## What Already Exists
Self-funded plan analytics platforms used by employers, brokers and TPAs ingest medical and pharmacy claims, group conditions, flag large claimants, benchmark costs and project renewals (the category is logged under Self-Insured Benefit & Risk Pools as [[niches/self-insured-benefit-pools/self-funded-plan-analytics-vendors/profile|🔍 Self-Funded Plan Analytics Platforms]]).

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: the PEO's client as a first-class dimension, with clients entering and leaving monthly; admission cohort, broker and underwriter attached to every client; premium yield against manual rate by client; large-claim pooling at the PEO's own pooling point; and reporting that respects HIPAA minimum-necessary rules when results are shown to sales and account teams who must not see member-level detail.

## Target Customer
VPs of benefits at PEOs with 10,000+ plan members whose carriers supply claims-level data under a business associate arrangement.

## Impact If Solved
A dashboard the PEO can run monthly instead of an annual actuarial study, built on tooling that already exists rather than a custom data warehouse.
