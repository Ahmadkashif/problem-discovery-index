# CPEO Attestation & PEO Audit Practices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/professional-employer-organizations|Professional Employer Organizations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
CPA firms with PEO practices that perform the examination-level attestations on CPEOs' quarterly assertions, audit their annual statements (due within six months of year end with an unmodified opinion), carry out ESAC's quarterly verifications, and issue service-organisation reports. Firms such as Withum publish guidance on ESAC accreditation to win this work. PEOs pay audit and attestation fees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** PEO practices of perhaps 10-50 auditors at the larger firms (estimate), almost all of them testing staff
**Output:** Attestation reports, audit opinions, verification letters
**Proprietary data:** Audit work papers, owned by the firm but built on client records under professional confidentiality
**Clock:** The IRS and ESAC quarterly deadlines and the six-month audit deadline, hard and third-party
**Buyer:** Audit partner
