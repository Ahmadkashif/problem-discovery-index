# Enterprise HCM Adapted to Co-Employment Billing

**Niche:** [[niches/professional-employer-organizations/peo-operating-platforms/profile|PEO Payroll, Benefits & HR Operating Platforms]]
**Industry:** [[industries/professional-employer-organizations|Professional Employer Organizations]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Enterprise HCM suites give a small client a far better HR experience than PEO platforms do, but they were built for one employer, not for a co-employer billing thousands of them.
**Tags:** #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #evaluation-metrics #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to run payroll, benefit deductions, payroll tax and workers' comp reporting for thousands of small co-employed clients on one multi-tenant system, and whoever lets a PEO take on a new client and run its first payroll and benefit deductions correctly with the fewest manual configuration steps takes the account.

## The Problem
PEOs compete on client experience against HR tech firms such as Rippling, which Insperity's 10-K names as a competitor. Their own platforms look dated to the client owner.

## What Already Exists
Enterprise HCM suites (Workday, and mid-market suites) provide core HR, self-service, analytics and modern interfaces. Insperity's HRScale is the clearest test: it gives PEO clients access to Workday Human Capital Management, built through a strategic partnership whose 2025 cost was $48M.

## The Customization Gap
The suite must learn co-employment. That means a client as a tenant within the PEO's tenant, and responsibility splits recorded per employee. It needs PEO invoicing that grosses up wages with the admin fee, health premiums, workers' comp by class and unemployment tax by account. Workers' comp payroll must be reportable by client for master policies, and filings made both as the PEO, a CPEO for federal purposes, and as the client where states require client-level reporting. The PEO's underwriting and pricing data has to flow into the bill.

## Target Customer
PEOs with 50,000+ worksite employees deciding whether to rebuild their platform or partner with an HCM vendor.

## Impact If Solved
A client experience competitive with HR tech start-ups without abandoning the PEO's insurance and liability model. Insperity's rollout will show what the adaptation costs.
