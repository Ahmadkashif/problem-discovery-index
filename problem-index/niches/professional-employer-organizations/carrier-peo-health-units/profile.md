# Health Carriers' PEO Master-Plan Units

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/professional-employer-organizations|Professional Employer Organizations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The units inside health insurers that underwrite and administer PEO master plans. UnitedHealthcare provides about 85% of Insperity participants' coverage under a contract running to 31 December 2028. Insperity also uses Kaiser Permanente, Blue Shield of California, HMSA and Harvard Pilgrim, and other PEOs place plans with national and Blue carriers. The carrier sets the plan's rates and funding arrangement (Insperity retains an obligation to fund plan cost), approves underwriting guidelines for admitting new clients, and reports experience back to the PEO.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 underwriters, actuaries and account analysts per carrier's PEO business (estimate)
**Output:** Master-plan rates and funding terms, new-client underwriting guidelines, experience reports
**Proprietary data:** Member-level claims for every PEO plan the carrier insures
**Clock:** Plan years and contract renewal dates, contractual
**Buyer:** Carrier's head of PEO and specialty markets
