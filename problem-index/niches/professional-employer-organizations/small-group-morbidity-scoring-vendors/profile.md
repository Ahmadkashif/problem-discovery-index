# Small-Group Morbidity Scoring Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/professional-employer-organizations|Professional Employer Organizations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Vendors that score a prospective small group's expected health cost from prescription and medical-claims histories pulled from data aggregators under members' authorisations. The PrestigePEO underwriting deck names SAIL and Curv forecasted morbidity scores (e.g. 1.25) as standard inputs. PEO new-business underwriters, level-funded carriers and stop-loss underwriters pay per group scored. The deck notes these tools lean on retail pharmacy data "because it is more commonly captured", and that a single high-cost specialty drug can make a group unprofitable.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 data scientists, actuaries and clinical staff per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Group morbidity scores, flagged high-cost conditions and drugs, look-back reports
**Proprietary data:** Licensed prescription and claims histories plus the vendor's scoring models; outcome feedback only where clients return claims experience
**Clock:** Broker quote deadlines and 1 January effective dates, set by the client's sales cycle
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer at PEOs, level-funded carriers and stop-loss MGUs

**Kill switches:** **compliance gate** — the scores are built from protected health information released under individual authorisations and licensed from pharmacy and claims sources with use restrictions, and the reports may be consumer reports. Scored consistently with the life-insurance sibling [[niches/life-insurance-carriers/au-evidence-score-vendors/profile|🔍 Accelerated Underwriting Evidence & Mortality Score Vendors]] (43, kill switch), one point lower on Q3 because group products rarely see the claims outcome that life vendors see in deaths.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. The score is the invoice, but machines produce it, not analysts, and every step runs through HIPAA and source-licence review.
