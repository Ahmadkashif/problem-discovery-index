# GRC Platforms Adapted to PEO Licensing Obligations

**Niche:** [[niches/professional-employer-organizations/certification-licensing-filings/profile|CPEO Certification, State Licensing & Regulatory Filings]]
**Industry:** [[industries/professional-employer-organizations|Professional Employer Organizations]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Governance, risk and compliance platforms already track obligations, evidence and owners for regulated firms; a PEO needs them loaded with 42 states' PEO rules, the IRS CPEO calendar and ESAC's requirements.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #evaluation-metrics #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver the CPEO quarterly assertion, the annual audited statements, state licence renewals and every client's ACA returns on their statutory dates from one reconciled employment-tax record, and whoever gets each attestation signed with no manual tie-out and no suspension notice takes the account.

## The Problem
A PEO's compliance director keeps the regulatory calendar in a spreadsheet: state licence renewals and their financial statements, bond amounts, CPEO quarterly and annual deadlines, ESAC submissions, ACA and EEO-1 dates, and benefit plan filings.

## What Already Exists
GRC platforms provide obligation registers, control mapping, evidence collection, task assignment and audit trails, and are sold widely to financial services firms. They are covered as a category under [[industries/grc-compliance-platforms|GRC & Compliance Platforms]].

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs a maintained content library of PEO-specific obligations: each state's PEO licensing statute, renewal dates, net worth or working-capital tests and bonding, the CPEO maintenance rules under Rev. Proc. 2023-18, and ESAC's accreditation requirements. It must link each obligation to the evidence the PEO's systems already produce, and track rule changes as states amend their PEO acts.

## Target Customer
Compliance directors at PEOs licensed in ten or more states.

## Impact If Solved
One maintained calendar with evidence attached, on a platform category that already exists, instead of a spreadsheet only one person understands.
