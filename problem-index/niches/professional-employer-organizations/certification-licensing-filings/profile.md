# CPEO Certification, State Licensing & Regulatory Filings

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/professional-employer-organizations|Professional Employer Organizations]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver the CPEO quarterly assertion, the annual audited statements, state licence renewals and every client's ACA returns on their statutory dates from one reconciled employment-tax record, and whoever gets each attestation signed with no manual tie-out and no suspension notice takes the account.

**Automation Potential:** High — fixed calendars, defined evidence, repeatable reconciliations

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A PEO's licence to operate is a stack of dated obligations. The IRS lists 122 certified CPEO entities. Each must file a quarterly assertion, with an examination-level CPA attestation, that it withheld and deposited every federal employment tax, by the last day of the second month after the quarter. It must also file audited statements within six months of year end and keep a bond of 5% of its section 3511 liability ($50,000 to $1M), or face suspension. Forty-two states recognise, license or register PEOs, each with its own renewal, financial and bonding rules. ESAC-accredited PEOs add quarterly CPA verification of taxes, benefit contributions and premiums. Then come the clients' own returns: ACA forms, and EEO-1 and other reports the PEO prepares. The contest is producing all of it from one record rather than re-proving the same numbers to each audience.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Payroll platforms' tax reports, CPA firms' attestation work, GRC and licence-tracking tools, and ACA reporting modules. Gaps: the employment-tax tie-out (withholding to deposits to returns, by client) rebuilt each quarter for the CPA; state renewals tracked in spreadsheets; ACA facts chased by email (see [[problems/professional-employer-organizations/low-impact-2|ACA Reporting for Hundreds of Co-Employed Clients]]).
