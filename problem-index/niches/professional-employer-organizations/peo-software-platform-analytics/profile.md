# PEO Software Platforms' Data & Benchmarking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/professional-employer-organizations|Professional Employer Organizations]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The analytics and benchmarking groups inside the software vendors PEOs run on. PrismHR, which says 60% of the industry uses its technology and that it powers 100,000+ organisations, sells Prism Intelligence analytics on top of its Core PEO Platform. Other HCM vendors with PEO editions do the same. They see payroll, benefits enrolment and HR records for a large share of the industry's worksite employees, and sell dashboards and benchmarks back to their PEO customers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 product analytics and data staff per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Embedded PEO analytics and dashboards, platform benchmarks, product research
**Proprietary data:** Payroll and HR records of PEOs' clients, processed under each PEO's service agreement
**Clock:** Product release cycles, self-set
**Buyer:** Vendor's Chief Product Officer

**Kill switches:** **data isn't theirs** — the records belong to the PEOs and their clients and are held under service agreements; no checked evidence shows the agreements permit cross-customer use.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. Analytics here is a product feature sold to keep platform customers, and the vendor market is a handful of firms. The same pattern holds for [[niches/hr-consultants/payroll-platform-research-institutes/profile|🔍 Payroll Platform Research Institutes]] (48).
