# Dealership CPA & Tax Practices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/franchised-car-dealers|Franchised Car Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Accounting firms with dedicated dealership practices that audit and review dealer financial statements, compute LIFO inventory reserves, prepare tax returns, advise on buy-sell structuring and publish benchmark commentary. Dealers and dealer groups pay audit, tax and advisory fees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Practices of tens of accountants at the larger firms (estimate), but auditors and tax preparers are production, one client's books at a time; benchmark publishing is a handful of people
**Output:** Audited statements, tax returns, LIFO calculations, transaction advice
**Proprietary data:** Clients' financial records, held under professional confidentiality
**Clock:** Tax filing deadlines and OEM statement and lender reporting dates
**Buyer:** Practice leader
