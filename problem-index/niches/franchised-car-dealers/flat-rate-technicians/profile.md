# Flat-Rate Technicians

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/franchised-car-dealers|Franchised Car Dealers]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to cut the unpaid minutes between jobs — waiting for parts, approvals and repair information — and whoever raises technicians' flagged hours per clocked hour takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M a year in technician-facing workflow, repair information and training tools bought by franchised stores (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** Technicians are the capacity constraint on the $164B service and parts business (NADA Data 2025)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — technicians use OEM diagnostic tools and service information daily, but dispatch, parts requests and approvals still run by walking to the counter and waiting
**Target Buyer:** Service Manager / Fixed Operations Director (the technician is the user)
**Automation Potential:** Medium — the repair is manual; the waiting around it is not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Most dealership technicians are paid flat rate: a set number of hours per job from the labour guide, whatever the job actually takes (estimate of prevailing practice). Time spent waiting for a part, an approval, a road-test slot or a repair procedure is unpaid. The independent-shop version of this pay pressure is mapped at [[problems/auto-repair-shops/worker-life-1|Flat-Rate Pay Pressure on Technicians]]. What the franchise adds: warranty jobs pay the OEM's own labour-guide time, which technicians commonly regard as tighter than customer-pay time (estimate); warranty and recall work waits on OEM prior authorisation and parts campaigns; and the OEM's technician certification levels decide who may touch which jobs. Dealer software is bought for managers and advisors; the technician's day is an afterthought, which is why a good technician can flag ~100–130% of clocked hours and an average one much less (estimate). Diagnostic knowledge itself is mapped at [[niches/auto-repair-shops/confirmed-fix-databases/profile|Confirmed-Fix Diagnostic Knowledge Databases]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
OEM diagnostic tools and service information portals; DMS technician dispatch and clocking; technician tablet apps from inspection vendors (myKaarma, Xtime); repair procedure publishers ([[niches/auto-body-shops/repair-procedure-publishers/profile|Repair Procedure & Service Information Publishers]]). Gaps: no view of where a technician's unpaid time goes; parts requests by walking to the counter; approval status invisible from the bay.
