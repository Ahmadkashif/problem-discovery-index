# OEM Dealer Survey Programme Operators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/franchised-car-dealers|Franchised Car Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Research and customer-experience firms contracted by manufacturers to survey every buyer and service customer of every dealer of the brand, score each dealer on sales and service satisfaction, and deliver monthly dealer scorecards. OEMs tie parts of dealer incentive pay and programme eligibility to these scores (estimate), so the scorecard moves money. The specific OEM-to-vendor pairings were not checked.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~50–300 researchers, analysts and programme staff per large programme vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Per-dealer satisfaction scores, verbatim analysis and network reports for the OEM
**Proprietary data:** None owned: the survey responses and scores belong to the OEM that commissions the programme
**Clock:** The OEM's monthly scorecard and programme calendar
**Buyer:** OEM Head of Customer Experience

**Kill switches:** live — data isn't theirs to use; the OEM owns the responses and the scores. The firm-owned, syndicated alternative is [[niches/franchised-car-dealers/syndicated-satisfaction-studies/profile|Syndicated Automotive Satisfaction Studies]] (41).
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch (34). Large, repeatable and consequential for dealer pay, but client-owned data on a client's calendar sold to a few dozen brands.
