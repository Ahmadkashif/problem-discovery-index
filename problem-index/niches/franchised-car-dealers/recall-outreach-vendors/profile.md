# Recall Outreach & Open-Recall Data Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/franchised-car-dealers|Franchised Car Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors that match open safety recalls to current owners using licensed registration and owner data, then contact the owners and book recall repairs at the dealer. BizzyCar (Recall Outreach and Recall Scout, more than 3,000 dealerships by its own account) and Recall Masters (named from recall) are examples. Dealers pay per programme or per appointment; recall repairs are paid by the OEM as warranty work.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Data and analytics staff under ~10 per vendor (estimate); outreach agents and dealer relations staff are production
**Output:** Owner lists, outreach campaigns, booked recall appointments
**Proprietary data:** Licensed owner and registration data matched to public NHTSA recall data
**Clock:** OEM recall launches and completion-rate pressure
**Buyer:** Head of Product
