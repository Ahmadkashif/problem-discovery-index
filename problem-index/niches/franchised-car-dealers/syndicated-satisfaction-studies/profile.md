# Syndicated Automotive Satisfaction Studies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/franchised-car-dealers|Franchised Car Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Survey firms that field their own studies of new-vehicle buyers and service customers and sell the brand and dealer results to manufacturers, with award licences sold for advertising. J.D. Power's U.S. Customer Service Index study is the best-known dealer-facing one: the 2025 edition, released 13 March 2025, surveyed 55,210 owners of one- to three-year-old vehicles between July and December 2024, alongside its sales satisfaction study. OEMs buy the detailed diagnostics to rank their dealer networks against competitors' and to set improvement targets.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–60 researchers and analysts in the automotive retail practice (estimate); J.D. Power's vehicle valuation business is a separate pocket, profiled at [[niches/auto-dealers-independent/vehicle-valuation-guides/profile|Vehicle Valuation Guide Publishers]]
**Output:** Annual syndicated studies, brand and dealer-level diagnostic reports, award licences
**Proprietary data:** Decades of owner survey responses by brand, segment and service attribute, collected by the firm with respondents' consent
**Clock:** The firm's own annual study calendar
**Buyer:** OEM Head of Customer Experience / Dealer Network Quality
