# Dealership Buy-Sell & Franchise Transfer

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/franchised-car-dealers|Franchised Car Dealers]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a dealership sale through OEM approval at the price the parties signed, and whoever closes the highest share of signed buy-sells without an OEM rejection, right-of-first-refusal exercise or price retrade takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$500M a year in buy-sell advisory fees, transaction legal work, diligence and valuation for franchised dealerships (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** <1% of dealership sales; a large share of principals' net worth (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — diligence runs on DMS financial statement exports, OEM sales-effectiveness reports, facility agreements and PDFs exchanged by email
**Target Buyer:** Dealer principal selling; CFO or corporate development lead at the acquiring group
**Automation Potential:** Medium for diligence and approval packets; low for negotiation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A franchised store's goodwill ("blue sky") exists only because the franchise does, so every sale needs the OEM's consent under the dealer agreement and state franchise law, and the OEM may hold a right of first refusal (estimate). The market is busy: Kerrigan Advisors counted a record 462 transactions covering 724 franchises in the twelve months to mid-2026, with its Blue Sky Index 78% above 2019 and a $100 million per-dealership blue sky benchmark set by Hennessy's sale to Group 1. The advisory firms' own research business is profiled under independent dealers at [[niches/auto-dealers-independent/dealership-ma-advisory/profile|Dealership Valuation & M&A Advisory]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Sell-side advisors (Kerrigan Advisors, Haig Partners, Presidio Group), dealer franchise attorneys, CPA firms' dealership practices, OEM approval portals and data rooms. Gaps: the buyer's diligence on the target's OEM obligations (facility commitments, sales-effectiveness standing, open warranty and incentive audits) is manual; the OEM approval packet is assembled by hand for each brand; state notice windows are tracked in email.
