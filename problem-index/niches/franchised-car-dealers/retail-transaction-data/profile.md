# New-Vehicle Retail Transaction Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/franchised-car-dealers|Franchised Car Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Vendors that collect deal-level sales data directly from franchised dealers' DMSs and sell transaction prices, incentive spend, retail share and sales forecasts to manufacturers, lenders, lessors and analysts. J.D. Power's Power Information Network (PIN) is the main one, described as ~250 data points per transaction from ~16,000 North American dealerships, updated daily (search summary); a 2017 release said it covered more than 40% of franchised retail transactions and fed J.D. Power's residual values (search summary). Dealers contribute in exchange for benchmark reports.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~30–100 analysts, forecasters and data engineers (estimate)
**Output:** Transaction-price and incentive benchmarks, weekly retail share reports, monthly retail sales forecasts, inputs to residual values
**Proprietary data:** Daily deal-level transactions (price, trade, incentives, finance terms) from a large share of franchised stores, a history no outside source can rebuild
**Clock:** The firm's own weekly and monthly release calendar
**Buyer:** OEM Head of Sales Analytics / Lender and Lessor Residual Risk Lead

**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold (41). The deepest new-vehicle price record in the market, but it scores well under the indexed [[niches/auto-dealers-independent/registration-market-intelligence/profile|Vehicle Registration & Market Intelligence Data]] (52). The gap: Q1 4 vs 5, because the transaction benchmarks are partly a give-back to contributing dealers; Q2 3 vs 5, a ~30–100 person team against that pocket's 200–800; and Q5 2 vs 3, a few dozen OEMs and lessors against a buyer base that includes parts suppliers and insurers. That sibling's Q4 of 3 on self-scheduled releases predates the ruling that a vendor's own release calendar is Q4 2, so the true gap is ~9 points, not 11. It is logged for Stage E.
