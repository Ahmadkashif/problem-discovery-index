# New-Vehicle Allocation & Inventory Turn

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/franchised-car-dealers|Franchised Car Dealers]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to put the configurations this market buys on the lot without losing allocation, and whoever cuts average days-to-turn on new units takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.5B a year in new-vehicle inventory, pricing, merchandising and market-data tools for franchised stores (estimate), steering the department that sold 16.2 million new vehicles in 2025 (NADA Data 2025)
**Share of Parent Industry:** New vehicles are roughly half of dealership sales dollars (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — pricing and listing tools are digital; ordering and allocation decisions are made in OEM portals by feel
**Target Buyer:** General Manager / New-Car Director
**Automation Potential:** Medium — forecasting automates; the order decision stays with the manager but can be guided

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Only franchised stores face OEM allocation formulas, order banks, dealer trades and stair-step programmes, and only new units carry floorplan from the day they land, ~$8–10 a unit-day at a $50,562 average transaction price (estimate; Kelley Blue Book for the ATP). Used-vehicle acquisition and pricing, which franchised stores also do, is already mapped under independent dealers at [[niches/auto-dealers-independent/wholesale-auction-flippers/profile|Wholesale & Auction Flippers]] and is not repeated here.

## Current Tools & Gaps
OEM allocation and order portals; DMS inventory; new-vehicle pricing and merchandising tools (vAuto's new-car tools, Lotlinx; named from recall); licensed registration data ([[niches/auto-dealers-independent/registration-market-intelligence/profile|Vehicle Registration & Market Intelligence Data]]). Gaps: no tool joins the order decision to the unit's outcome; allocation offers arrive as portal screens or spreadsheets; stair-step arithmetic lives in a manager's notebook.
