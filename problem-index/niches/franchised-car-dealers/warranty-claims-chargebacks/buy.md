# Medical Claim Scrubbers for OEM Warranty

**Niche:** [[niches/franchised-car-dealers/warranty-claims-chargebacks/profile|Warranty Claims & Chargeback Defense]]
**Industry:** [[industries/franchised-car-dealers|Franchised Car Dealers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Hospitals scrub every claim against payer rules before it goes out; dealers send warranty claims to the OEM unscrubbed.
**Tags:** #bert #gradient-boosting #evaluation-metrics #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get each warranty repair paid on first submission and kept through the OEM's audit window, and whoever posts the lowest share of warranty dollars rejected or charged back takes the account.

## The Problem
Warranty is a payer-claims problem: a provider (the dealer) submits coded work to a payer (the OEM) that edits, denies and audits it.

## What Already Exists
Healthcare claim scrubbers and denial-management tools check codes against payer rules, predict denials and manage appeals (see [[industries/medical-billing|Medical Billing]]).

## The Customization Gap
The warranty version needs OEM op codes and labour time guides instead of CPT codes, the complaint-cause-correction story as the clinical note, parts retention and return logistics, prior authorisation by technical assistance centre, and state-statute clocks for payment and chargebacks in place of payer contracts.

## Target Customer
Controllers and warranty administrators at dealer groups; outsourced warranty administration firms.

## Impact If Solved
Claim scrubbing is a measured discipline in healthcare, where clean-claim rates are tracked per payer. Brought to warranty, it gives the fixed ops director a first-pass paid rate per OEM. Lifting that rate from ~90% to ~95% (estimate) on ~3,000 warranty claims a year (estimate) saves ~150 resubmissions, about a week of administrator time a year, before any chargeback effect.
