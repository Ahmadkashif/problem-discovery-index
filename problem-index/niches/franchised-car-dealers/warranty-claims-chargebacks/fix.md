# The Audit Letter Answered From Memory

**Niche:** [[niches/franchised-car-dealers/warranty-claims-chargebacks/profile|Warranty Claims & Chargeback Defense]]
**Industry:** [[industries/franchised-car-dealers|Franchised Car Dealers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A warranty audit arrives months after payment and the store rebuilds each disputed claim from paper, often after the window to contest has narrowed.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #compliance #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get each warranty repair paid on first submission and kept through the OEM's audit window, and whoever posts the lowest share of warranty dollars rejected or charged back takes the account.

## The Problem
The OEM's auditor selects claims and proposes chargebacks. The administrator must find the repair order, technician notes, photos, authorisation numbers and returned-part records for each one, then argue the case, while new claims pile up.

## Why It's Still Broken
Claim evidence is scattered across the DMS, the OEM portal, technician tablets and paper; nobody tracks which paid claims are still inside the statutory window (six months in Virginia, for example) or flags a chargeback that falls outside it.

## What a Fix Looks Like
An evidence file assembled per claim at submission, an exposure calendar by claim and state, and draft responses citing the policy manual and statute, including rejection of chargebacks proposed outside the window.

## Who Feels the Pain
Warranty administrators; fixed ops directors who lose gross months after it was booked.

## Impact If Fixed
Faster, better-documented audit responses keep money already earned and stop out-of-window chargebacks; a store facing ~$20,000 of proposed chargebacks a year (estimate) that overturns a quarter of them keeps ~$5,000 of gross it had already booked, and the administrator's audit week shrinks to a day.
