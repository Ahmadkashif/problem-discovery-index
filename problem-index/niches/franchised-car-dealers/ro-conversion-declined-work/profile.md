# Repair Order Conversion & Declined Work

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/franchised-car-dealers|Franchised Car Dealers]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get inspection findings approved while the car is still in the bay, and whoever lifts approved labour hours per repair order from multipoint-inspection findings takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1B a year in multipoint and video inspection tools, declined-service follow-up and service retention marketing for franchised stores (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~33% of fixed-ops software and services spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — video inspections are widespread at larger stores; paper inspection sheets and verbal selling remain common
**Target Buyer:** Service Manager / Fixed Operations Director
**Automation Potential:** Medium — capture and follow-up automate well; the recommendation and the trust behind it stay with the advisor

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Once the car is on the lift, the technician's inspection finds worn brakes, leaking seals and overdue maintenance. Whether the customer approves depends on how the finding is shown, priced and explained, and on whether the advisor reaches them before the car is finished. Declined items are future revenue only if someone follows up. The buyer, data and vendors differ from appointment booking. The independent-shop version is mapped at [[problems/auto-repair-shops/low-impact-1|Digital Vehicle Inspection Intelligence]]. What the franchise adds is a mixed repair order: customer-pay findings sit beside warranty and recall lines that the OEM must approve and pays at guide time. The advisor has to separate what the customer approves from what the OEM covers, and the customer's experience of it feeds the OEM satisfaction survey that can move dealer pay.

## Current Tools & Gaps
myKaarma, Xtime and Dealer-FX video inspections and text approvals (named from recall), DMS service modules, service retention marketing from BizzyCar and Affinitiv. Gaps: inspection findings are graded red, yellow and green by each technician differently; declined work is rarely tracked to its return; nobody measures which presentations earn approvals.
