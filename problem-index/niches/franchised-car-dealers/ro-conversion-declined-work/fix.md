# The Red Item Graded Yellow

**Niche:** [[niches/franchised-car-dealers/ro-conversion-declined-work/profile|Repair Order Conversion & Declined Work]]
**Industry:** [[industries/franchised-car-dealers|Franchised Car Dealers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Technicians grade the same brake pad red, yellow or green by habit, so the customer's recommendation depends on who inspected the car.
**Tags:** #cnns #object-detection #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get inspection findings approved while the car is still in the bay, and whoever lifts approved labour hours per repair order from multipoint-inspection findings takes the account.

## The Problem
Inspection grading is inconsistent: one technician flags pads at 4 mm, another at 3 mm; tyre photos are blurry; some never mark yellow. Customers notice when the next visit contradicts the last.

## Why It's Still Broken
Grading rules exist on paper, but nobody audits grades against the measurements and photos already captured.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Grade checks against the store's thresholds using the recorded measurement, plus image checks for tyre tread and brake rotor photos, with per-technician consistency reports for the shop foreman.

## Who Feels the Pain
Advisors defending contradictory recommendations; customers who lose trust; technicians whose honest findings get declined because others cry wolf.

## Impact If Fixed
Consistent grading makes recommendations credible, which is what approvals rest on; lifting the approval rate on red safety items from ~30% to ~35% (estimate) costs only a rules check on data already collected.
