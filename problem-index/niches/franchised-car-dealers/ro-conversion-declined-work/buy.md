**Industry:** [[industries/franchised-car-dealers|Franchised Car Dealers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Online retailers recover abandoned carts with timed, personalised follow-ups; service drives let declined repairs expire in the DMS.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #survival-analysis #large-language-models #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get inspection findings approved while the car is still in the bay, and whoever lifts approved labour hours per repair order from multipoint-inspection findings takes the account.

## The Problem
A declined front-brake job is a known, priced future sale that usually gets one generic reminder or none.

## What Already Exists
Abandoned-cart and lifecycle marketing tools (e-commerce email and SMS platforms) time messages by propensity and personalise offers.

## The Customization Gap
The service version needs the inspection measurement (pad millimetres, tread depth) to estimate urgency, mileage accrual to time the message, safety-item rules that keep messages honest, OEM recall and warranty status, and booking into the scheduler rather than a checkout page.

## Target Customer
Service managers and fixed-ops marketing leads.

## Impact If Solved
Declined work becomes a tracked pipeline; recapturing ~10% more of declined safety items (estimate) is direct customer-pay gross.
