# Every Bulletin as a Rule, Every Deal Checked

**Niche:** [[niches/franchised-car-dealers/oem-incentive-reconciliation/profile|OEM Incentive & Stair-Step Reconciliation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/franchised-car-dealers|Franchised Car Dealers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** OEM programme bulletins arrive as documents; no dealer-side system turns them into rules and tests every delivered deal against them.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #seq2seq #transformers #data-integration #revenue-impact #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to claim every programme dollar each sale qualifies for and keep it through OEM audit, and whoever recovers the most incentive dollars per retail unit net of chargebacks takes the account.

## The Problem
An incentive clerk reads each month's bulletins and remembers which deals qualify. Programmes missed are never claimed; programmes claimed without the right documents are charged back in audit.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Incentive data vendors structure programmes for quoting to shoppers, not for reconciling what the OEM owes the store; DMS vendors leave programme logic to the OEM portal.

## What to Build
Bulletin-to-rule extraction (eligible models, trims, model years, dates, customer types, stacking, documents), checked once by a person; nightly testing of every delivered deal against every live rule; claim drafting with document checklists; payment-to-claim reconciliation with exceptions; audit exposure by deal.

## Target Customer
Controllers at single stores and central accounting teams at dealer groups.

## Impact If Built
~$50 more per retail unit recovered net of chargebacks (estimate) is ~$47,000 a year for a store retailing ~950 units, plus days of month-end reconciliation removed.
