# OEM Incentive & Stair-Step Reconciliation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/franchised-car-dealers|Franchised Car Dealers]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to claim every programme dollar each sale qualifies for and keep it through OEM audit, and whoever recovers the most incentive dollars per retail unit net of chargebacks takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M a year in incentive clerk and controller time, accounting outsourcing and reconciliation tooling at franchised stores (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** Incentives ran at 6.6% of the September 2026 average transaction price, ~$3,359 a vehicle (Kelley Blue Book), much of it flowing through dealer claims
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — claims are filed in OEM portals and posted in the DMS, but eligibility is checked by reading bulletins
**Target Buyer:** Controller / Office Manager
**Automation Potential:** Very high — rule extraction, deal matching and payment reconciliation are mechanical

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Only franchised stores receive OEM programme money: dealer cash, bonus cash, stair-step volume bonuses, CSI-gated payments, facility and loaner programmes. The rules change monthly, stack in complicated ways and are audited after payment, with chargebacks limited by state law (six months for sales compensation in Virginia, for example). The work is mechanical once the rules are structured, which is what makes it automatable.

## Current Tools & Gaps
DMS incentive receivable schedules, OEM claim portals and statements, spreadsheets, incentive data services that feed programme amounts to desking tools (see [[niches/franchised-car-dealers/incentive-data-services/profile|Automotive Incentive Data Services]]), and accounting outsourcers. Gaps: bulletins never become machine-checkable rules on the dealer's side; unclaimed eligibility is invisible; stair-step progress is tracked by hand.
