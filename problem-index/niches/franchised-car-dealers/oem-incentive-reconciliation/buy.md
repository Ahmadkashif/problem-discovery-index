# Supplier Rebate Management for OEM Programmes

**Niche:** [[niches/franchised-car-dealers/oem-incentive-reconciliation/profile|OEM Incentive & Stair-Step Reconciliation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/franchised-car-dealers|Franchised Car Dealers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Distributors already use rebate-management software to claim supplier programmes; dealers claim OEM programmes by hand.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #evaluation-metrics #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to claim every programme dollar each sale qualifies for and keep it through OEM audit, and whoever recovers the most incentive dollars per retail unit net of chargebacks takes the account.

## The Problem
OEM programmes are supplier rebates by another name: volume tiers, product-specific payments, claims and audits.

## What Already Exists
Rebate and special-pricing management tools used by wholesale distributors track agreements, accrue earnings, file claims and reconcile payments (the distributor version is discussed under [[industries/industrial-distributors|Industrial Distributors]]).

## The Customization Gap
The dealer version needs VIN-level eligibility instead of SKU-level, retail delivery reporting dates, consumer-facing versus dealer-facing programme separation, stair-step tiers measured on retail sales, CSI-gated conditions, the OEM portal's claim formats and state chargeback limits.

## Target Customer
Dealer group CFOs and controllers.

## Impact If Solved
Recovered dollars are measurable per unit from the first month. A group retailing ~10,000 new units a year (estimate) that recovers ~$50 more per unit net of chargebacks (estimate) gains ~$500,000 a year, against incentive spend averaging $3,359 a vehicle (Kelley Blue Book, September 2026).
