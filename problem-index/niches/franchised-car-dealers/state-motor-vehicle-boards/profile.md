# State Motor Vehicle Boards & Dealer Licensing Divisions

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/franchised-car-dealers|Franchised Car Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State bodies that license dealers and hear disputes under state franchise laws: protests against a new or relocated dealer in a market, terminations, transfer refusals, and warranty and incentive chargeback disputes. Examples include California's New Motor Vehicle Board and the motor vehicle division of the Texas Department of Motor Vehicles (named from recall). Statutes such as Virginia § 46.2-1571 set the rules they enforce on warranty pay and chargebacks.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Small staffs of administrative law judges, counsel and licensing staff per state (estimate); no research function
**Output:** Licences, hearing decisions, orders
**Proprietary data:** Case files and licensing records, much of them public
**Clock:** Statutory hearing and decision deadlines
**Buyer:** Executive director
