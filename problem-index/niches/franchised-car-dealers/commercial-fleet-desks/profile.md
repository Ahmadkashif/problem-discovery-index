# Commercial & Fleet Desks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/franchised-car-dealers|Franchised Car Dealers]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver an upfitted work vehicle on the date the business customer was promised, and whoever shortens order-to-delivery days on upfitted units takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M a year in commercial inventory, quoting and upfit-coordination tools and services used by franchised commercial departments (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** Commercial and small-fleet sales are a minority of units at most stores but a large share at truck-heavy Ford, Ram, GM and commercial-van stores (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — quotes, upfitter coordination and delivery tracking run on email, spreadsheets and phone calls to body builders
**Target Buyer:** Commercial Sales Manager / Commercial Account Manager
**Automation Potential:** Medium — quoting and tracking automate; upfit engineering does not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The customer is a plumber, landscaper, utility contractor or municipality buying work trucks and vans, often with a body, shelving, ladder rack or lift added by an upfitter before delivery. Retail tools are built for one consumer buying one finished car. The commercial desk juggles chassis allocation, upfitter queues, OEM commercial incentives and fleet discounts, government bid paperwork and the customer's start date for a new crew. The fleet side of the same relationship is mapped at [[industries/fleet-managers|Fleet Managers]], with the body-builder work at [[niches/fleet-managers/vocational-upfit-engineering/profile|Vocational Upfit Engineering]] and a typical small-business buyer at [[niches/fleet-managers/hvac-plumbing-service-fleets/profile|HVAC/Plumbing Service Fleets]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
OEM commercial programmes and portals; commercial inventory marketplaces such as Work Truck Solutions (named from recall); upfitters' own scheduling; DMS deal records. Gaps: no shared order-to-delivery tracker across chassis, ship-through upfitter and dealer; quotes rebuilt by hand for each body configuration; delays discovered when the customer calls.
