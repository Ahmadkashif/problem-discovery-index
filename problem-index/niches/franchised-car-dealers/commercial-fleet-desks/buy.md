# Build-to-Order Configure-Price-Quote for Work Trucks

**Niche:** [[niches/franchised-car-dealers/commercial-fleet-desks/profile|Commercial & Fleet Desks]]
**Industry:** [[industries/franchised-car-dealers|Franchised Car Dealers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Industrial equipment makers quote configured products with CPQ software; commercial truck desks quote chassis plus body plus options in spreadsheets.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver an upfitted work vehicle on the date the business customer was promised, and whoever shortens order-to-delivery days on upfitted units takes the account.

## The Problem
A quote for a service-body pickup combines the OEM chassis configuration, the upfitter's body and options, commercial incentives and fleet pricing, and compatibility rules (payload, wheelbase, axle ratings).

## What Already Exists
Configure-price-quote software used by machinery and industrial equipment makers handles option rules, pricing and approvals.

## The Customization Gap
The commercial-vehicle version needs OEM body-builder rules and chassis specifications, upfitter catalogues and lead times, OEM commercial incentive eligibility, government and fleet bid formats, and a link from the quote to the chassis order and the upfit work order.

## Target Customer
Commercial account managers and upfitter sales coordinators.

## Impact If Solved
Quotes in ~2 hours instead of ~2–3 days (estimate) and fewer configuration errors that send a truck back to the upfitter, which also shortens delivery.
