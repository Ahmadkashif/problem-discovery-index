# The Upfit Delay Found When the Customer Calls

**Niche:** [[niches/franchised-car-dealers/commercial-fleet-desks/profile|Commercial & Fleet Desks]]
**Industry:** [[industries/franchised-car-dealers|Franchised Car Dealers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The commercial manager learns a truck is late from the customer, then spends the day phoning the upfitter to find out why.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #survival-analysis #workflow-orchestration #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver an upfitted work vehicle on the date the business customer was promised, and whoever shortens order-to-delivery days on upfitted units takes the account.

## The Problem
Status lives in upfitters' emails and phone answers. A manager with forty open commercial orders (estimate) cannot chase every one weekly, so delays surface late.

## Why It's Still Broken
Upfitters send status when asked; there is no standard feed and no one assigned to ask.

## What a Fix Looks Like
An automated weekly status request to each upfitter per open order, parsed from the reply, with orders past their expected stage flagged to the manager before the customer notices.

## Who Feels the Pain
Commercial managers, business customers waiting on trucks, upfitters fielding repeated calls.

## Impact If Fixed
Delays are known days or weeks earlier and the manager drops ~5–10 hours a week of status calls (estimate), so the customer can be told, loaned a unit or switched to stock, which preserves the account.
