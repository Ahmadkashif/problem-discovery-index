# Dealer Network Planning & Sales-Effectiveness Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/franchised-car-dealers|Franchised Car Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Consultancies and OEM network-development teams that decide where dealerships should be, how many a market supports and whether each dealer is selling its share. Urban Science, a Detroit firm serving nearly every OEM and rooted in network planning (Wikipedia, via search summary), is the best known. The work produces each dealer's sales-effectiveness rating against its assigned area, open-point and relocation studies, and the market analyses that OEMs use in performance notices and in add-point and termination disputes heard by state motor vehicle boards.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~50–300 analysts, geographers and consultants at the largest firm (estimate); OEM network teams of ~10–40 each (estimate)
**Output:** Network plans, sales-effectiveness calculations, open-point studies, expert reports in franchise disputes
**Proprietary data:** OEM sales and dealer data supplied by each client, combined with licensed registration data
**Clock:** OEM network planning cycles; state board hearing dates on the litigation work
**Buyer:** OEM Director of Network Development
