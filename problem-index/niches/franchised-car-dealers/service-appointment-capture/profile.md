# Service Appointment Capture

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/franchised-car-dealers|Franchised Car Dealers]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn every inbound service call, text and web request into a kept appointment in the dealer's own scheduler, and whoever books the highest share of inbound service contacts into kept appointments takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.2B a year in call tracking, BDC outsourcing, online scheduling and AI voice and text agents for franchised service departments (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~40% of fixed-ops software and services spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — online scheduling exists at most stores, but most service demand still arrives by phone
**Target Buyer:** Fixed Operations Director / BDC Manager
**Automation Potential:** Very high for booking, rescheduling, status and recall calls

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The contest ends at a booked, kept appointment; what happens in the bay is a different contest with different vendors. The demand is large and leaky: Car Wars tracked roughly 53 million inbound service calls across its dealer network in 2025, about 19 million of them missed opportunities, and only about 4 million booked appointments (CBT News, March 2026). Booking well needs the dealer's scheduler, technician capacity, loaners and recall data, which generic receptionists don't have (compare [[niches/answering-services/ai-voice-receptionist-vendors/profile|AI Voice Agent & Virtual Receptionist Vendors]]).

## Current Tools & Gaps
Xtime and myKaarma scheduling, BDC outsourcers, Car Wars and CallRevu call tracking, and AI agents from BizzyCar, Numa, Impel, Toma, Sandra AI and Clearline. Engineering hiring on 2026-10-24: BizzyCar (remote US), Numa (Montreal or Toronto, Canada), Impel (Tbilisi, Georgia, or remote Australia), myKaarma (Noida, India). Gaps: most agents measure calls answered, not appointments kept; DMS scheduler integrations are uneven; status and approval calls still reach advisors.
