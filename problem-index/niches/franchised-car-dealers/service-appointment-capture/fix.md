# The No-Show Nobody Predicted

**Niche:** [[niches/franchised-car-dealers/service-appointment-capture/profile|Service Appointment Capture]]
**Industry:** [[industries/franchised-car-dealers|Franchised Car Dealers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A booked appointment that doesn't show wastes a technician's hours, and the store confirms every appointment the same way.
**Tags:** #logistic-regression #gradient-boosting #evaluation-metrics #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn every inbound service call, text and web request into a kept appointment in the dealer's own scheduler, and whoever books the highest share of inbound service contacts into kept appointments takes the account.

## The Problem
Stores send a reminder text the day before and lose a share of appointments anyway (~10–15% no-shows, estimate), leaving booked technician hours empty while other customers wait days.

## Why It's Still Broken
Confirmation is a batch reminder; nobody scores which appointments are at risk or backfills them.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A no-show risk score per appointment from booking channel, lead time, customer history, concern type and weather, with extra confirmation for high-risk bookings and a standby list offered the freed slot.

## Who Feels the Pain
Service managers balancing the board; flat-rate technicians paid only for hours they flag.

## Impact If Fixed
Cutting no-shows from ~12% to ~8% (estimate) on ~1,500 monthly appointments refills ~60 slots a month of paid technician time.
