# Hospitality Reservation Agents Fitted to the DMS Scheduler

**Niche:** [[niches/franchised-car-dealers/service-appointment-capture/profile|Service Appointment Capture]]
**Industry:** [[industries/franchised-car-dealers|Franchised Car Dealers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Restaurants and clinics already run AI reservation agents; a service drive needs one that understands VINs, recalls and op codes.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #seq2seq #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn every inbound service call, text and web request into a kept appointment in the dealer's own scheduler, and whoever books the highest share of inbound service contacts into kept appointments takes the account.

## The Problem
Generic AI receptionists answer and take messages; a message is not an appointment.

## What Already Exists
AI voice receptionists for restaurants, clinics and trades, and the hybrid services profiled under [[niches/answering-services/ai-voice-receptionist-vendors/profile|AI Voice Agent & Virtual Receptionist Vendors]], book into generic calendars.

## The Customization Gap
The dealership version needs VIN or plate capture and decoding, open-recall lookup, op-code menus and labour-time quotes, loaner and shuttle logic, warranty versus customer-pay triage, write-back to CDK, Reynolds, Dealertrack or Tekion schedulers, and escalation rules for safety concerns.

## Target Customer
Single-point and small-group stores without a BDC.

## Impact If Solved
After-hours and overflow calls become appointments instead of voicemails, with the store's own menu and capacity rules. A store with ~400 after-hours and overflow service calls a month (estimate) that books a quarter of them gains ~100 appointments, ~$35,000 a month in repair orders at ~$350 each (estimate).
