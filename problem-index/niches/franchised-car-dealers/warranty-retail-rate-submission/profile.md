# Warranty Retail-Rate Submission Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/franchised-car-dealers|Franchised Car Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Firms that compute a dealer's retail labour rate and parts markup from its customer-pay repair orders and file the statutory request that obliges the OEM to pay warranty work at those rates. Armatus Dealer Uplift, the largest, says it has helped over 7,500 dealers to 19,500+ rate increases, is endorsed by 25 state dealer associations and helped draft 21 warranty reimbursement statutes. Dealers pay a fee, often tied to the uplift.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Analysts preparing submissions are production, one dealer's repair orders at a time; the statutory and OEM-response know-how sits with a small core (under ~10, estimate)
**Output:** Per-dealer rate submissions and responses to OEM challenges
**Proprietary data:** Each client's customer-pay repair orders, plus the firm's history of OEM responses by brand and state
**Clock:** Statutory OEM response windows in each state's franchise law
**Buyer:** Managing Partner
