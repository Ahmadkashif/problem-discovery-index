# Dealer Group Corporate Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/franchised-car-dealers|Franchised Car Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The corporate development, pricing and operations analytics teams at public dealer groups (AutoNation, Lithia, Penske Automotive, Group 1, Sonic, Asbury) and the largest private groups. They screen acquisitions in a market that traded a record 462 dealerships in the twelve months to mid-2026 (Kerrigan Advisors), set used-vehicle and new-vehicle pricing policy, benchmark fixed-ops productivity and F&I penetration across hundreds of rooftops, and report to investors.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–100 analysts and data scientists per group (estimate)
**Output:** Acquisition screens and valuations, pricing and inventory policy, store benchmarks; all internal
**Proprietary data:** Deal, repair-order and inventory history across the group's stores and brands
**Clock:** Quarterly earnings dates for the public groups; deal timetables
**Buyer:** Chief Financial Officer / VP Corporate Development
