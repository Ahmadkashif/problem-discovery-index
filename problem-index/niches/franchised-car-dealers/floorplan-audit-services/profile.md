# Floorplan Inventory Audit Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/franchised-car-dealers|Franchised Car Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Field audit firms hired by floorplan lenders (captive finance companies, banks and independent floorplan lenders) to visit dealer lots unannounced, scan every financed VIN and report units sold but not paid off ("sold out of trust"), missing or in transit. The lender pays per audit; the report goes to the lender's floorplan risk team.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Field auditors are production, counting vehicles store by store; analysis of results sits with each lender's floorplan risk team
**Output:** Audit reports per dealer visit
**Proprietary data:** Lot-check histories owned by the lender clients
**Clock:** Lender audit frequency, set by the client
**Buyer:** Lender floorplan risk manager
