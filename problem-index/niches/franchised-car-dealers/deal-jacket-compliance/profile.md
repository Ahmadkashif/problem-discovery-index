# Deal-Jacket Compliance Review

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/franchised-car-dealers|Franchised Car Dealers]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to catch a defective deal jacket — a missing add-on consent, a price that doesn't match the total first quoted, an unsigned disclosure — before the contract is funded, and whoever catches the highest share of defective deal jackets before funding takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$500M a year in dealer compliance programmes, deal audits, F&I menu and e-contracting compliance features and compliance staff time (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** One review per deal, on 16.2 million new vehicles sold in 2025 plus used retail (NADA Data 2025)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — e-contracting is common, but much of the jacket is still scanned paper and audits are monthly samples
**Target Buyer:** F&I Director / Dealer Compliance Officer
**Automation Potential:** Very high for document completeness and rule checks; low for judgement on disputes

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The rules govern the dealer's sale, not the lender's loan. The FTC's CARS Rule was vacated in January 2025, but California's CARS Act took effect on 1 October 2026 with total-price disclosure in the first written communication, add-ons disclosed as optional verbally and in writing in 12-point type, a ban on valueless add-ons, a three-business-day cancellation right on used vehicles at or under $50,000, and two years of records. Lenders check the same jacket for funding (the lender-side contest is [[niches/sales-finance-companies/contract-funding-verification/profile|Contract Funding & Stipulation Verification]]), and product refunds after cancellation sit at [[niches/sales-finance-companies/ancillary-product-refunds/profile|Ancillary Product Cancellations & Refunds]]. Dealer compliance consultants are logged at [[niches/auto-dealers-independent/dealer-compliance-consultancies/profile|Dealer Compliance & F&I Consultancies]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Compliance programmes and training (KPA, ComplyAuto; named from recall), F&I menu software, e-contracting through RouteOne and Dealertrack, Reynolds' Docupad (named from recall), outside deal audits. Gaps: checks are sampled monthly, not run on every deal; the first written quote isn't joined to the final contract; state-specific rules arrive as memos rather than checks.
