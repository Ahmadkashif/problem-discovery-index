# Auto Retail Consumer Protection Enforcement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/franchised-car-dealers|Franchised Car Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The federal and state units that police dealer advertising, pricing, add-ons and financing: the FTC's consumer protection staff (whose CARS Rule the Fifth Circuit vacated on 27 January 2025), state attorneys general, and in California the DMV and Bureau of Automotive Repair enforcing the CARS Act that took effect on 1 October 2026, with civil penalties of up to $2,500 per violation under the Unfair Competition Law.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Investigators and attorneys who work cases one dealer at a time; dedicated auto-retail analysts under 10 per agency (estimate)
**Output:** Investigations, consent orders, guidance
**Proprietary data:** Complaints, subpoenaed deal files, investigation records under statutory confidentiality
**Clock:** Statutes of limitation and litigation schedules
**Buyer:** Bureau or division director
