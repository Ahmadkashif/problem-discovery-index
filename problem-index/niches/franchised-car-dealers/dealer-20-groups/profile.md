# Dealer 20 Groups & Composite Benchmarking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/franchised-car-dealers|Franchised Car Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Peer groups of about twenty non-competing dealers of the same brand who share monthly financial statements, meet a few times a year with a facilitator, and receive composite benchmarks of every department line. NCM Associates (named from recall) and NADA's own 20 Group programme run most of them, alongside consulting and training. Dealers pay membership fees for the composites and the facilitated comparison.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–80 facilitators, analysts and consultants at the largest provider (estimate)
**Output:** Monthly composites by brand and department, facilitated meeting books, consulting reviews
**Proprietary data:** Years of member dealers' monthly financial statements in a standard format
**Clock:** Monthly statement cycle and meeting calendar, set by the group
**Buyer:** Head of 20 Group Programmes
