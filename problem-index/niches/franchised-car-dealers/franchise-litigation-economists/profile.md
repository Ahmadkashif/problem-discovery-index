# Franchise Dispute Economists & Market-Study Experts

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/franchised-car-dealers|Franchised Car Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Economists and market analysts retained by dealers or manufacturers, through their lawyers, to testify in franchise disputes: whether a market can support an added dealer, whether a sales-effectiveness standard is fair to a dealer's area, what a terminated franchise was worth, and whether warranty reimbursement rates were set lawfully.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 1–10 per firm (estimate); the larger OEM-side analytics sit in [[niches/franchised-car-dealers/network-planning-sales-effectiveness/profile|Dealer Network Planning & Sales-Effectiveness Analytics]]
**Output:** Expert reports and testimony
**Proprietary data:** Case files and client data produced in discovery
**Clock:** Board hearing and court dates
**Buyer:** Retaining counsel
