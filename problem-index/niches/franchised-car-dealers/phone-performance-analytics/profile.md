# Dealership Phone Performance Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/franchised-car-dealers|Franchised Car Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Automotive call-tracking and grading vendors, such as Car Wars and CallRevu, record and score dealers' sales and service calls for appointment set, missed opportunity and agent behaviour, and sell the scorecards and coaching to dealers. Car Wars tracked roughly 53 million inbound service calls across its network in 2025 and published that about 19 million were missed opportunities (CBT News, March 2026). The new AI voice-agent vendors sit beside them and are logged generically at [[niches/answering-services/ai-voice-receptionist-vendors/profile|AI Voice Agent & Virtual Receptionist Vendors]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 analysts and data scientists per vendor (estimate); call graders are production
**Output:** Call scorecards, missed-opportunity alerts, industry phone reports used as marketing
**Proprietary data:** Tens of millions of recorded, graded dealership calls with outcomes
**Clock:** None external; dealers' monthly reviews
**Buyer:** VP Product / Chief Data Officer
