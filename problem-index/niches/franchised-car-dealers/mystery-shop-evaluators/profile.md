# Dealership Mystery-Shop Evaluators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/franchised-car-dealers|Franchised Car Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Firms that shop dealerships in person, by phone and online for manufacturers and dealer groups, scoring follow-up, product presentation and process adherence; some publish an annual industry ranking from their own shops, Pied Piper's Prospect Satisfaction Index being the best known (named from recall).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Mystery shoppers are production; analysts and report writers under ~10 per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Shop reports and scores per store; annual industry study
**Proprietary data:** Shop results, mostly commissioned and owned by OEM and dealer-group clients
**Clock:** Client programme cycles; the firm's own annual study date
**Buyer:** Founder / managing director
