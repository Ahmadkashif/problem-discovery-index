# Catalogue Intake at Scale

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/digital-audio-platforms|Digital Audio Platforms]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to ingest well over a hundred thousand tracks a day without letting the bad ones through or losing the good ones — and whoever does it well controls what the catalogue actually is.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$800M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~4% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — automated pipeline, thin judgement
**Target Buyer:** Catalogue engineering leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — detection and classification at volume

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Over a hundred thousand tracks arrive every day. Each needs classification, metadata validation, rights checking, duplicate and impersonation detection, and a decision about whether it belongs in the catalogue at all. The volume makes human review impossible and the consequences of getting it wrong run in both directions: infringing or fraudulent material reaching listeners, and legitimate releases delayed or rejected by an automated check nobody can appeal.

## Current Tools & Gaps
An ingestion pipeline, format validation, fingerprint matching and a small review team. The gaps: duplicate and near-duplicate flooding unmeasured; impersonation detection weak; classification quality unmeasured against discovery; rejection reasons uninformative; and no view of what the intake is actually adding.
