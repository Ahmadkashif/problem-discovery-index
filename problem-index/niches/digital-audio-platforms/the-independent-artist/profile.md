# The Independent Artist

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/digital-audio-platforms|Digital Audio Platforms]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give an artist a defensible account of what happened to their music and their money — and whoever provides it addresses the relationship the entire catalogue depends on.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.3B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — a dashboard and an inference
**Target Buyer:** Artist relations leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — explanation and evidence

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The independent artist supplies the catalogue and has the least information about how any of it works. They cannot audit the royalty calculation, cannot see why their release received no exposure, cannot tell a ranking change from a demand change, cannot find out whether unmatched money relates to them, and cannot reach anyone. Every structural problem in the category lands on them, and their only recourse is a public complaint.

## Current Tools & Gaps
An artist dashboard with stream counts, a support form and a help centre. The gaps: royalty not derivable; exposure not explained; no route to a person; unmatched money invisible; and no explanation when something changes.
