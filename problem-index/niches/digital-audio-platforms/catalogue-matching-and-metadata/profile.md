# Catalogue Matching & Metadata

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/digital-audio-platforms|Digital Audio Platforms]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to match a recording to a rights record cleanly enough that its money reaches an owner — and whoever resolves the unmatched pool releases a real sum to people who do not know it exists.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.5B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~14% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — matching by supplied metadata
**Target Buyer:** Rights operations leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — matching with audio evidence

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A recording arrives with metadata supplied by distributors and labels, and when it does not match a rights record cleanly the resulting royalty sits unattributed. The recording still plays, still earns and still has an owner — who generally does not know the money exists. The accumulated unmatched pool is a persistent and real sum, and the matching that would resolve it runs on the same text fields that failed in the first place.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Identifier matching, supplied metadata, manual claims and an unmatched pool. The gaps: audio and content evidence not used for matching; the platform's own listening data unused as a matching signal; unmatched money not reported to potential claimants; no proactive resolution; and match confidence not expressed.
