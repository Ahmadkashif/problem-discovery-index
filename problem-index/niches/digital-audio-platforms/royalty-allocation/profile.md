## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Royalties are allocated pro-rata: a rights holder receives a share of the pool proportional to their share of total streams, which means a subscriber's fee is pooled and redistributed toward whatever was most streamed overall. Fraud sits directly on top of that structure, because artificially inflated streams do not create money — they take it from everyone else. The two are the same argument from different directions and they are the industry's central economic question.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Pro-rata calculation, minimum thresholds introduced since 2024, internal fraud detection and a statement. The gaps: alternative allocations never computed and published; nobody can audit the division; fraud rates unpublished; threshold policies' effects unmeasured; and the debate conducted without numbers.
