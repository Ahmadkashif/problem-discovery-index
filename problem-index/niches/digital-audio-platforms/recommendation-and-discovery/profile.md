# Recommendation & Discovery

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/digital-audio-platforms|Digital Audio Platforms]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to surface the right track from a catalogue receiving a hundred thousand arrivals a day, against an objective that reliably favours what is already familiar.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$4.5B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~25% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very High — the category's best capability
**Target Buyer:** Product and data leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — an active modelling contest

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Recommendation determines what is listened to, and because royalties follow streams, it determines who earns. These are among the largest deployed recommender systems in existence, they are optimised for engagement, and engagement optimisation reliably favours the familiar — which in a pro-rata pool means the recommender is also an allocation mechanism. Over a hundred thousand tracks arrive daily into a system whose incentives push against surfacing any of them.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Collaborative and content-based models, playlists, radio and personalised mixes. The gaps: cold start for new releases largely unsolved; engagement objective favouring the familiar; the recommender's role as an income allocator unexamined; catalogue exposure concentration unreported; and discovery outcomes not measured for the artist side.
