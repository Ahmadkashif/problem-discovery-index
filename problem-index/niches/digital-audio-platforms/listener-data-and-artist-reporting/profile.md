# Listener Data & Artist Reporting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/digital-audio-platforms|Digital Audio Platforms]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn the most detailed record of listening behaviour ever assembled into something an artist can act on — and whoever reports usefully gives artists the decisions they are currently making blind.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — counts without direction
**Target Buyer:** Artist services leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — analysis over data already held

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The platform knows what every listener played, skipped, saved, repeated and abandoned, in what context, over years. The artist receives stream counts, a map and a demographic summary. The decisions an artist actually makes — where to tour, what to release next, which track to promote, whether an audience is building or churning — are all supported by the underlying data and by none of the reporting.

## Current Tools & Gaps
An artist dashboard with streams, listeners, playlist adds and a territory map. The gaps: skip and save behaviour not surfaced; audience retention across releases not reported; no distinction between a growing and a churning audience; no actionable interpretation; and the reporting the same for a bedroom artist and a major act.
