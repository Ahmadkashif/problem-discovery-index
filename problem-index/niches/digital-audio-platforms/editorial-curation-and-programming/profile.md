# Editorial Curation & Programming

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/digital-audio-platforms|Digital Audio Platforms]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let a small editorial team decide what tens of millions of people hear, from more pitches than they could listen to in a year — and whoever supports that decision changes who gets a career.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~11% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — listening and judgement at volume
**Target Buyer:** Editorial leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — triage and evidence, not the judgement

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A small editorial team decides what tens of millions of people hear, receives more pitches in a week than it can listen to in a year, and knows exactly what a placement is worth to the artist who does not get one. Editorial placement is the most consequential single act in an artist's commercial life on the platform, it is performed under impossible volume, and the process by which a pitch reaches a human ear is opaque to everyone outside it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A pitching tool, a playlist management system, a listening queue and professional judgement. The gaps: pitch triage by hand; no evidence assembled per pitch; placement effects measured inconsistently; the relationship between pitching access and outcomes unexamined; and no feedback to the artist who was not selected.
