**Industry:** [[industries/digital-audio-platforms|Digital Audio Platforms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Advertising and payments both built accredited detection with published categories and measured rates, and streaming integrity is an internal matter.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to determine whether a stream was a person, against an adversary improving in step — and whoever detects manipulation accurately without penalising legitimate artists protects a pool that every other artist is paid from.

## The Problem
Advertising built invalid traffic detection with defined categories, accredited methodologies and reported filtration rates. Payments built fraud detection with published loss rates, model governance and dispute processes. Both are adversarial, both improved under scrutiny, and both accept external accreditation because the market demanded it. Streaming integrity is performed privately, reported minimally and audited by nobody.

## What Already Exists
Invalid activity taxonomies; accredited detection methodologies; published filtration and loss rates; model risk governance; and dispute and remediation processes.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a fixed pool where the victim is another participant. It requires: (1) a harm that falls on other rights holders rather than on the platform or an advertiser, which removes the commercial pressure that drove accreditation elsewhere — this is the substantive difference and means the accountability must come from somewhere else; (2) an enforcement action that removes a release or withholds an artist's income, which is far more consequential to an individual than filtering an impression; (3) an artist who may have bought manipulation unknowingly, a category advertising handles as a vendor issue; (4) detection across a catalogue with a hundred thousand daily arrivals; and (5) no accrediting body, so credibility must be built rather than certified.

## Target Customer
Trust and integrity leadership, artists and their representatives, licensors, and detection vendors from advertising and payments.

## Impact If Solved
Both adjacent fields accepted accreditation because the paying party demanded it, and here the harmed party is not the paying party. Building the accountability without that pressure is the adaptation, and quantifying the transfer is what creates the demand.
