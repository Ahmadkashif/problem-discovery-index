# Stream Integrity

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/digital-audio-platforms|Digital Audio Platforms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to determine whether a stream was a person, against an adversary improving in step — and whoever detects manipulation accurately without penalising legitimate artists protects a pool that every other artist is paid from.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.3B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — detection real, rates unpublished
**Target Buyer:** Trust and integrity leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — adversarial behavioural detection

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Because the pool is fixed, artificially inflated streams do not create money — they take it from everyone else. That makes manipulation a transfer between artists rather than a cost to the platform, which changes both its economics and the platform's incentive to invest in stopping it. Detection is adversarial and improving on both sides, the platforms publish little about their own rates, and the enforcement falls on artists who frequently cannot tell what triggered it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Internal detection models, penalties and takedowns, and a policy statement. The gaps: detection rates unpublished; the redistribution effect unquantified; enforcement unexplained to the affected artist; the artist who bought a fraudulent promotion service treated as a perpetrator; and no independent verification.
