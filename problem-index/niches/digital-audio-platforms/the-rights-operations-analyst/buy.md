**Industry:** [[industries/digital-audio-platforms|Digital Audio Platforms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Financial operations reconcile by exception with automated matching and break workflow, and royalty operations reconcile everything by hand.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stop analysts matching recordings to owners and reconciling statements across a dozen intermediaries by hand — and whoever equips them ends a month spent explaining a payment they cannot derive.

## The Problem
Reconciliation between counterparties is a solved operational discipline in financial services: automated matching with tolerance rules, exception-only workflow, break classification and ageing, and full lineage so any figure can be decomposed on demand. It exists because the volumes made manual reconciliation impossible and the consequences of error were serious. Royalty operations face comparable volumes and complexity and reconcile in spreadsheets.

## What Already Exists
Automated matching with tolerances; exception-only reconciliation workflow; break classification and ageing; lineage and decomposition; and reconciliation control frameworks.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to counterparty statements with no shared reference. It requires: (1) no common identifier across sources, so matching is probabilistic rather than key-based — this is the substantive difference from financial reconciliation, which reconciles against a shared trade reference; (2) formats set unilaterally by senders and changed without notice; (3) a calculation whose inputs are held by one counterparty and not disclosed, making full derivation impossible rather than merely difficult; (4) many small amounts across many rights holders rather than large transactions; and (5) a team of a few people rather than an operations department.

## Target Customer
Operations leadership, analysts, rights holders, and reconciliation platform vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Financial operations moved to exception-only reconciliation because the volumes demanded it, and this domain has the volumes without the discipline. Probabilistic matching in place of a shared reference is the adaptation the absent identifier forces.
