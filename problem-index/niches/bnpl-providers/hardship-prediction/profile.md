# Hardship Prediction

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/bnpl-providers|BNPL Providers]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to identify a consumer heading into difficulty before the missed payment rather than after it — and whoever does that intervenes while it still helps and while the regulator is still watching rather than acting.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$5B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of originations
**Digital Adoption:** Low — hardship identified on disclosure
**Target Buyer:** Collections and product leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the signals are in the repayment record

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A consumer heading into difficulty is visible before they miss a payment: the pattern of plan-taking changes, payments start arriving on the retry rather than the first attempt, basket values fall, the interval between plans shortens. All of that is in the provider's own data. Hardship is currently identified when the consumer discloses it, which requires them to initiate a difficult conversation, usually after the fees have started. This is distinct from collections: that is what happens after the miss, this is the window before it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Delinquency triggers, self-disclosed hardship processes, and forbearance offered on request. The gaps: hardship identified only after a failure; no prediction from repayment behaviour; no proactive intervention; the earliest signals visible and unused; and the consumer required to ask for help they may not know exists.
