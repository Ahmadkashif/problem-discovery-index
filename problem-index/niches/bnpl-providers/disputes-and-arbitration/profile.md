# Disputes & Arbitration

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/bnpl-providers|BNPL Providers]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to adjudicate between a consumer and a merchant on evidence neither supplies — and whoever builds that evidence base decides a dispute rather than splitting the difference.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$10B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of originations
**Digital Adoption:** Low — two accounts and a judgement
**Target Buyer:** Operations and compliance leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — evidence assembly automatable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
One team arbitrates between consumers who say the goods never arrived and merchants who say they did, on evidence neither side supplies, several hundred times a week. The provider is neither the merchant nor the card issuer and is treated as both — it holds the consumer's payment obligation, it has a commercial relationship with the merchant, and it has no independent knowledge of what happened. It must decide anyway, quickly, at volume, with a consumer relationship and a merchant relationship on either side of every decision.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Consumer dispute forms, merchant responses, tracking information where supplied, and a decision by an agent. The gaps: evidence supplied selectively by two interested parties; no independent verification; no record of what past similar disputes produced; consistency unmeasured; and a decision that damages one relationship whichever way it goes.
