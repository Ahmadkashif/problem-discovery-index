# Thin-File Credit Decisioning

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/bnpl-providers|BNPL Providers]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to infer capacity from device, behaviour, basket and their own history when the bureau has nothing to say — and whoever does that best approves the people competitors decline and still gets repaid.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$16B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of originations
**Digital Adoption:** High — the stack is modern, the science varies
**Target Buyer:** Credit risk and data science leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — a modelling contest with fast outcomes

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This contest is winnable alone. A provider with better features, better models and better validation approves people its competitors decline and is repaid, which is the whole commercial advantage in a category competing on approval rate and loss rate simultaneously. The evidence — device, behavioural, basket, merchant, and the provider's own repayment history — is available to any provider, the outcome arrives in six weeks, and the quality of the science is the differentiator. It requires no industry coordination whatsoever, which is what separates it from the accumulation problem.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Bureau data where available, device fingerprinting, behavioural signals during the checkout, basket and merchant context, and repeat-customer history. The gaps: features limited to what the checkout exposes; models validated on approved applicants only; no reject inference; repayment history from other providers unavailable; and the six-week outcome loop not exploited for continuous learning.
