## The Problem
Lending to people without credit files is not new. Microfinance, emerging-market digital lenders and thin-file specialists have developed alternative data scoring over two decades, with real evidence about which signals predict and which are noise, established approaches to fairness and validation, and hard lessons about signals that correlate with repayment in a training period and stop. The literature and the operational experience exist. Instalment providers built their own stacks quickly and largely independently of it.

## What Already Exists
Alternative data scoring methodology; psychometric and behavioural credit assessment; validation approaches for thin-file populations; fairness analysis of alternative features; and documented evidence on signal stability.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a purchase-moment decision with a six-week horizon in a developed market. It requires: (1) evidence gathered passively in a checkout rather than through an application or an interview, so the feature set is what the flow happens to expose rather than what an assessment asks — designing the checkout to gather more without harming conversion is the substantive product question; (2) a six-week rather than a multi-month or annual outcome, which changes what is predictable and permits far faster iteration; (3) a regulatory environment with developed consumer credit protections, unlike many of the markets where this practice matured; (4) repeat interaction with the same consumer across purchases, which builds a behavioural record no single-loan lender accumulates; and (5) the merchant and the basket as features, which is genuinely new information no prior credit context has had.

## Target Customer
Credit risk teams at instalment providers, alternative data vendors, and thin-file lending practitioners for whom the point-of-sale instalment population is new territory.

## Impact If Solved
Two decades of thin-file lending experience exists and the sector built independently of it. The basket and the merchant as credit features are genuinely new information, and the six-week horizon changes both what is predictable and how fast it can be learned.
