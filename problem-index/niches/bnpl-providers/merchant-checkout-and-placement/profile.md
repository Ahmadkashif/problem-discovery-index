# Merchant Checkout & Placement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/bnpl-providers|BNPL Providers]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make the offer appear at the right moment in a checkout the provider does not control — and whoever does that wins the conversion that decides which provider a merchant keeps.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$22B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~20% of originations
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — integrated everywhere, tuned by hand
**Target Buyer:** Merchant and commercial leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — placement is testable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Conversion depends on where and how the offer appears in a merchant's checkout, every merchant's checkout is different, and the integration is tuned by account managers reading dashboards. The provider's commercial performance with a merchant — and therefore whether the merchant keeps them, adds a competitor or drops them — is largely determined by placement decisions made in someone else's codebase. It is a conversion optimisation problem conducted across thousands of sites the provider cannot change directly.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Integration libraries and widgets, placement guidelines, account manager dashboards, and merchant-reported conversion. The gaps: placement decided by the merchant's developer and rarely revisited; no experimentation across merchants; messaging tuned by convention; eligibility signalling inconsistent; and the provider's conversion performance attributed to the product rather than to the placement.
