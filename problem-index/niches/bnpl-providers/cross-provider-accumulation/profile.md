# Cross-Provider Accumulation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/bnpl-providers|BNPL Providers]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make concurrent obligations across providers visible to somebody before the regulator makes it visible to everyone — and whoever assembles that sharing defines the sector's terms rather than receiving them.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$14B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% of originations
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — structurally invisible
**Target Buyer:** Sector leadership, bureaus and regulators
**Automation Potential:** High — the mechanism is infrastructure, not modelling

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
No amount of modelling inside one provider can reveal what a consumer owes at five others. This is an infrastructure and coordination contest: the winner is whoever assembles the furnishing, the bureau mechanism or the consortium that makes concurrent obligations visible, not whoever builds the best model. It has a different buyer, a different skill set and a different obstacle — collective action rather than data science — and it is the sector's central risk and its largest regulatory exposure.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Partial and inconsistent bureau furnishing, some providers reporting longer-term products only, occasional bilateral data sharing, and no real-time mechanism. The gaps: short instalment products largely unfurnished; bureau formats poorly suited to six-week obligations; reporting cycles slower than the accumulation; no consortium; and a collective action problem nobody has convened around.
