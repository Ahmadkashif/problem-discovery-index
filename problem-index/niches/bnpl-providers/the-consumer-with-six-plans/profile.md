# The Consumer With Six Plans

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/bnpl-providers|BNPL Providers]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give a consumer a view of what they have actually committed to across providers — and whoever does that serves the only party who could see all of it and has no tool for it.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$10B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of originations
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — six apps and a calendar
**Target Buyer:** Product leadership and consumer finance vendors
**Automation Potential:** High — aggregation and forecasting are both feasible

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The consumer is the one party who could see all six plans, and has no tool that shows them. Each provider's app shows its own schedule. The payments land on different dates against a debit card, and the consumer discovers the total when one of them fails. They are the sector's customer, the person the accumulation risk falls on, and the participant with the least support. This is distinct from the accumulation niche: that is about making the exposure visible to lenders and regulators, this is about making it visible to the person carrying it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Each provider's own app and schedule, calendar reminders set by the consumer, and bank statements. The gaps: no aggregate view of obligations; no forecast of the coming weeks' payments against income; no warning before a payment that will fail; no route to restructure across providers; and the total position knowable only by manual assembly.
