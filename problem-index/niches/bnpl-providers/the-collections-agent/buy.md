**Industry:** [[industries/bnpl-providers|BNPL Providers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Consumer collections has modelled who to contact, how and when for decades, and instalment collections works an undifferentiated queue.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell the agent who needs a payment plan and who needs to be left alone — and whoever does that changes both the recovery rate and what this job does to the people doing it.

## The Problem
Collections analytics is developed. Accounts are scored for payment probability, segmented by treatment, matched to channel and timing, and routed to the intervention with the best expected outcome. Self-cure prediction avoids contacting people who would have paid anyway. Hardship identification and forbearance pathways are increasingly built in under regulatory pressure. The practice exists across banking, utilities and telecommunications. Instalment collections frequently runs a queue ordered by days past due.

## What Already Exists
Payment probability and self-cure models; treatment and channel segmentation; contact timing optimisation; hardship identification and forbearance pathways; and champion-challenger testing of collections strategies.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to very small balances and a population with concurrent obligations elsewhere. It requires: (1) unit economics where a single phone call can exceed the balance, so the treatment selection is dominated by cost in a way conventional collections is not — the cheap treatments must do nearly all the work and the expensive ones are reserved almost entirely for hardship; (2) customers holding plans at several providers, so the person's total position is worse than this provider's view and the treatment should account for what cannot be seen; (3) a short cure window, since a six-week plan's delinquency resolves or does not quickly; (4) a customer relationship the provider wants to keep, which is different from conventional collections where the relationship is usually over; and (5) a regulatory environment forming specifically around this sector's treatment of vulnerable customers.

## Target Customer
Collections leadership at instalment providers, collections analytics vendors, and regulators whose expectations of the sector are developing.

## Impact If Solved
Collections analytics is mature and instalment collections runs a days-past-due queue. Balances where one phone call exceeds the amount owed make cost the dominant term, which inverts the treatment design conventional collections uses.
