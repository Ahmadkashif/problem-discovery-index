# The Collections Agent

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/bnpl-providers|BNPL Providers]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell the agent who needs a payment plan and who needs to be left alone — and whoever does that changes both the recovery rate and what this job does to the people doing it.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$12B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~11% of originations
**Digital Adoption:** Low — a queue and a recovery target
**Target Buyer:** Collections leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — segmentation and routing are automatable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Agents work a queue of very small delinquent balances belonging to people in genuine difficulty, under contact rules and a recovery target, with no way to tell who needs a payment plan and who needs to be left alone. The balances are small enough that the economics barely support contact at all, the people behind them are frequently in real hardship, the regulatory constraints are tightening, and the agent is given a list and a number. It is a distinct role with a distinct problem: the information that would make the job humane and effective exists and is not given to them.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Dialler and messaging queues, contact rules, payment plan options, and a recovery target. The gaps: no segmentation of who will pay, who needs a plan and who is in hardship; no history of what worked with similar customers; contact attempts consuming more than the balance is worth; hardship identified only when disclosed; and an agent measured on recovery against people with nothing to recover from.
