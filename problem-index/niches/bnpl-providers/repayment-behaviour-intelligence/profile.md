# Repayment Behaviour Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/bnpl-providers|BNPL Providers]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to establish what small-instalment repayment behaviour actually predicts about a person's capacity — and whoever answers that owns genuinely new empirical ground about a population the bureaus cannot describe.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$7B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~6% of originations
**Digital Adoption:** Low — used to score the next approval
**Target Buyer:** Credit and data leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the dataset exists and is large

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
BNPL generated, in under a decade, one of the largest repayment datasets on consumers with thin or no credit file — tens of millions of people making small, frequent, observable payment decisions. That is genuinely new empirical ground, and the sector has used it almost entirely to score its own next approval. What small-instalment repayment behaviour predicts about a person's broader capacity, measured against outcomes rather than against a bureau score, is an open and answerable question that nobody has taken up.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Internal scoring models trained on the provider's own repayment data, occasional bureau comparisons, and portfolio reporting. The gaps: the dataset used for approval scoring only; no research on what the behaviour predicts more broadly; no validation against outcomes beyond the provider's own; nothing published; and a population the wider credit system still describes as unscoreable.
