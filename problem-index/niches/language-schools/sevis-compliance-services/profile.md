# Student Visa Compliance Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/language-schools|Language Schools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Compliance service providers and software vendors manage the international student reporting obligations Pass 1 describes — attendance thresholds, full-time enrolment verification, programme extensions — for schools whose certification and students' status depend on getting them right.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 compliance analysts, regulatory specialists, and product staff
**Output:** Reporting workflows and submissions, compliance monitoring and alerting, recertification support, regulatory guidance
**Proprietary data:** Reporting error patterns and enforcement outcomes across many institutions
**Clock:** Statutory reporting deadlines measured in days
**Buyer:** VP of Compliance / Head of Product
