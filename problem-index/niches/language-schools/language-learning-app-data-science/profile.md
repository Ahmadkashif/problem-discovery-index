## What They Do
The consumer language app data science teams run the adaptive sequencing, spaced repetition, and engagement systems behind products Pass 1 says dominate self-study — and increasingly sell proficiency certification alongside them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 data scientists, learning scientists, and psychometricians
**Output:** Adaptive learning sequences, difficulty estimation, retention and engagement modelling, proficiency certification
**Proprietary data:** Billions of learning interactions with fine-grained response and timing data across tens of millions of learners
**Clock:** Product release cadence, internal
**Buyer:** VP of Data Science / Chief Learning Scientist
