# ELT Publishers & Learner Corpora

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/language-schools|Language Schools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The English language teaching publishers produce the coursebooks, syllabi, and level frameworks language schools teach to, and maintain the reference corpora — including learner corpora of actual student errors — that decide what vocabulary and structures belong at each proficiency level.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 lexicographers, corpus linguists, editors, and assessment specialists
**Output:** Course materials and syllabi, level and can-do frameworks, dictionaries and reference works, placement tests
**Proprietary data:** Reference and learner corpora built over decades, including annotated error data from millions of student texts
**Clock:** Academic year adoption cycles and framework revisions
**Buyer:** Publishing director / Head of Assessment and Research
