# Language School Chain Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/language-schools|Language Schools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup

## What They Do
The international language school chains run centralized analytics across campuses in many countries, managing enrolment forecasting, pricing, and curriculum standardization at a scale independent schools cannot reach.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 analysts, curriculum staff, and revenue managers
**Output:** Enrolment forecasts, pricing and yield management, campus performance review, curriculum standards
**Proprietary data:** Enrolment, placement, progression, and retention across many campuses and source markets
**Clock:** Enrolment seasons and academic terms
**Buyer:** No external buyer — internal cost centre
