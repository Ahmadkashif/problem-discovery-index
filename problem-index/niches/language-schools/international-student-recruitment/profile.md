# International Student Recruitment Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/language-schools|Language Schools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Recruitment platforms and agent aggregators channel international students to schools and universities, matching applicants to institutions and managing the application and visa process on commission.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 market analysts, admissions specialists, and data staff
**Output:** Applicant-institution matching, market demand analysis, visa success reporting, agent performance management
**Proprietary data:** Applicant profiles, institution preferences, offers, visa outcomes, and enrolments across many source countries — the only view of global student demand flow
**Clock:** Application rounds and visa processing windows
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Analytics
