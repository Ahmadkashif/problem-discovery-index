# English Proficiency Testing Organizations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/language-schools|Language Schools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
The English proficiency testing bodies produce the scores that gate university admission, professional registration, and immigration for millions of people a year across the world. A language school's entire purpose, for most of its students, is reaching a number one of these organizations will certify.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-3,000 psychometricians, applied linguists, item developers, human raters, and research scientists
**Output:** Test forms and item banks, scaled scores mapped to proficiency frameworks, automated and human scoring of speaking and writing, validity and comparability research
**Proprietary data:** Decades of item response data and rated speaking and writing performances from a global test-taker population — the largest corpus of second-language production and its expert assessment in existence
**Clock:** University application rounds and visa deadlines, immovable for the candidate
**Buyer:** Chief Research Officer / VP of Assessment
