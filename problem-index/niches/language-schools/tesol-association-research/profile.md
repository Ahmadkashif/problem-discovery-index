# Language Teaching Association Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/language-schools|Language Schools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The language teaching associations publish professional standards for instructors, run research journals and conferences, and advocate on the visa and funding policy that determines international enrolment.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 standards, research, and policy staff
**Output:** Teaching standards and qualifications frameworks, research publications, policy analysis, enrolment surveys
**Proprietary data:** Member survey series and enrolment data collection
**Clock:** Annual reporting and conference cycles
**Buyer:** Director of Research — member funded
