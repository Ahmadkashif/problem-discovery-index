# Student Visa Programme Oversight

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/language-schools|Language Schools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The federal student visa programme certifies schools to enrol international students, operates the reporting system every enrolment and attendance event must flow through, and investigates schools whose reporting suggests non-compliance. Pass 1 records the consequence: a missed report can terminate a student's visa.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-1,000 adjudicators, analysts, and field representatives
**Output:** School certification and recertification decisions, compliance findings, programme statistics, enforcement actions
**Proprietary data:** Every international student enrolment, transfer, and status event in the country, joined to school compliance history
**Clock:** Statutory reporting windows measured in days, with a student's status at stake
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — federal agency
