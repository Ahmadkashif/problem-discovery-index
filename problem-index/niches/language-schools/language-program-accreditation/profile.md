# Language Programme Accreditation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/language-schools|Language Schools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The English language programme accreditor evaluates intensive programmes against standards covering curriculum, faculty qualifications, student services, and administration. Accreditation is a practical precondition for enrolling international students.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 standards staff, site reviewers, and analysts
**Output:** Accreditation decisions, site visit reports, standards development, programme improvement guidance
**Proprietary data:** Self-study and visit findings across the accredited programme population over multiple cycles
**Clock:** Accreditation cycles with enrolment eligibility attached
**Buyer:** Executive director
