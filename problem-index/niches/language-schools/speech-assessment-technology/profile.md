# Speech Assessment Technology

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/language-schools|Language Schools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Speech assessment vendors score spoken language automatically — pronunciation, fluency, and increasingly content — for placement, practice feedback, and certification, addressing the placement bottleneck Pass 1 describes where two testers can place the same student a full level apart.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 speech scientists, applied linguists, and machine learning engineers
**Output:** Automated speaking scores, pronunciation feedback, placement engines, rater agreement research
**Proprietary data:** Recorded learner speech with expert ratings, across languages and proficiency levels
**Clock:** Placement and certification cycles at customer institutions
**Buyer:** Chief Science Officer / VP of Research
