# Placement Testing Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/language-schools|Language Schools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Individual assessment consultants design and validate the placement instruments language schools use to slot students into levels — the process Pass 1 says is subjective enough that two testers can place the same student a full level apart.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1-6 consultants, often one applied linguist
**Output:** Placement instrument design, rater training, validation studies
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated judgment about placement construct and level boundaries, undocumented
**Clock:** Enrolment intake cycles
**Buyer:** The consultant
