# Email Inbox Placement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/email-sms-marketing-platforms|Email & SMS Marketing Platforms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to infer where a message landed inside a mailbox provider that will not say — and whoever does it accurately from a cross-brand corpus owns the number the whole channel should be managed on.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.3B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~14% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — seed lists and reputation scores
**Target Buyer:** Platform deliverability and data leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — an inference problem with abundant signal

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The mailbox providers hold the answer and publish fragments of it: authentication results, complaint feedback where a programme exists, aggregate reputation indicators, and the engagement behaviour of real recipients. Reconstructing placement from those fragments is a statistical inference problem, and it is tractable for a platform that sees the same provider across thousands of senders and intractable for any individual brand. The contest is who builds that inference well, and it has nothing in common with the carrier problem next door beyond the word deliverability.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Seed list panels, authentication configuration, reputation dashboards from third parties, complaint feedback loops, and practitioner heuristics. The gaps: seed panels that providers can detect and that do not represent the real audience; reputation scores with no stated method; no inference from real engagement; no cross-brand baseline; and diagnosis by expert intuition.
