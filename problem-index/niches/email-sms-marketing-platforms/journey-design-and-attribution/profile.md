# Journey Design & Attribution

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/email-sms-marketing-platforms|Email & SMS Marketing Platforms]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell a brand which branches of its automated journeys earn anything — and whoever does that turns a hand-drawn rule tree into something that can be improved.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.0B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~11% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — builders everywhere, evaluation nowhere
**Target Buyer:** Lifecycle and CRM leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — branch attribution is a computation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every brand builds the same dozen automated journeys as hand-drawn rule trees, and then nobody can say which branches earn anything. The journey builder is the category's most visible product feature and its output is a diagram that accumulates branches over years, is understood by nobody in full, and is evaluated only in aggregate. This is a distinct contest because the problem is evaluating a structure the platform itself generated and stores — the information is entirely inside the system and is simply never computed.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Visual journey builders, aggregate flow revenue reporting, occasional message-level testing, and version history of varying quality. The gaps: no branch-level attribution; no counterfactual for a branch; revenue attributed to the flow rather than to the path taken; no detection of dead branches; and no way to test a structural change.
