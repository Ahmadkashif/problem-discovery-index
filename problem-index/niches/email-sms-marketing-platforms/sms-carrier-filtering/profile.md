# SMS Carrier Filtering

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/email-sms-marketing-platforms|Email & SMS Marketing Platforms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to decode why carriers filter what they filter and navigate the registration regime that gates every brand — and whoever does that turns an opaque gatekeeper relationship into something a brand can manage.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$900M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — error codes and folklore
**Target Buyer:** Platform carrier relations and compliance leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — code decoding and pattern detection are both learnable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A small number of carriers decide whether text messages reach handsets, filter on criteria they do not publish, and return error codes that conflate several distinct outcomes. Sitting in front of them is a registration regime — brand and campaign registration, use case classification, throughput allocation — with no email equivalent, which every brand must pass through and which most navigate badly. This is a gatekeeper relations and decoding contest with a handful of counterparties, which makes it a fundamentally different business from statistical inference against mailbox providers.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Carrier error codes, registration workflows, aggregator relationships, throughput limits, and practitioner community knowledge. The gaps: error codes that mean several things; no attribution of filtering to a cause; registration handled as paperwork rather than as a determinant of performance; throughput allocation opaque; and no cross-brand view of carrier behaviour change.
