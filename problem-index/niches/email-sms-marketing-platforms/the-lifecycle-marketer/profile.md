# The Lifecycle Marketer

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/email-sms-marketing-platforms|Email & SMS Marketing Platforms]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make fifty automated journeys comprehensible and monitored by one person — and whoever does that changes how much of a brand's lifecycle programme actually runs.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$900M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — diagrams and a calendar
**Target Buyer:** Marketing operations leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — monitoring and documentation are both automatable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A lifecycle marketer maintains fifty automated journeys nobody fully understands, builds a campaign calendar that changes weekly, and finds out a flow stopped firing in March when someone notices in June. They are operating a production system with the tooling of a design application: no monitoring, no documentation, no change control, no alerting, and a diagram as the only representation. Their capacity to hold the system in their head is the practical limit on how much lifecycle programme a brand can run.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Journey builders, campaign calendars in spreadsheets, platform reporting, and personal knowledge. The gaps: no operational monitoring of automated flows; no documentation that stays current; no change history with reasons; no view of the whole programme; and no prioritisation of what needs attention.
