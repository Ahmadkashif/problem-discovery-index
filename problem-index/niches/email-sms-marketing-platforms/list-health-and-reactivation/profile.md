# List Health & Reactivation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/email-sms-marketing-platforms|Email & SMS Marketing Platforms]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell a brand which of its subscribers are still reachable and worth reaching — and whoever does that stops a list from being both a liability and an unworked asset at the same time.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$700M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — a count and a sunset rule
**Target Buyer:** Lifecycle and deliverability teams
**Automation Potential:** Very High — reachability is a well-posed prediction

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A list contains people who will buy again, people who will never open anything, addresses that no longer exist, addresses that exist and are filtered, people who want a different frequency, and people who would return with the right message. Every one of those requires a different action and the brand treats them as one number, reported as list size and managed with a single sunset rule. This is distinct from the engagement signal work — that rebuilds the metric, this decides what to do with each person given it — and it is a well-posed prediction the platform has all the data for.

## Current Tools & Gaps
List size reporting, sunset rules on engagement recency, occasional win-back campaigns, and bounce handling. The gaps: reachability and value conflated; no prediction of who will return; win-back campaigns sent to everyone dormant; no distinction between filtered and disengaged; and list size reported as a growth metric.
