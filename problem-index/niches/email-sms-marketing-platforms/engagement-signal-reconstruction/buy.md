**Industry:** [[industries/email-sms-marketing-platforms|Email & SMS Marketing Platforms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Official statistics has formal practice for changing a measurement without destroying the time series, and marketing changed one by accident and carried on.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #hypothesis-testing #confidence-intervals #bayesian-inference #evaluation-metrics #time-series-forecasting #compliance #monte-carlo-methods
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to rebuild the segmentation, sunsetting and send-time machinery on signals that still mean something — and whoever does that replaces an industry running on a metric a privacy feature destroyed five years ago.

## The Problem
Statistical agencies change methodologies regularly and have formal practice for it: parallel running of old and new measures, back-casting to restate history on the new basis, published discontinuity notices, and bridging studies that quantify the difference. They do this because a broken time series destroys the ability to make decisions. Marketing had its central engagement metric change meaning overnight through an external privacy feature, and the industry response was to keep reporting it.

## What Already Exists
Parallel running and bridging study methodology; back-casting and series restatement; discontinuity notices and documentation; index rebasing practice; and methodological change governance.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a discontinuity nobody chose, affecting a metric with no governing body. It requires: (1) a break that was imposed externally rather than planned, so there is no parallel run and the bridging must be estimated retrospectively from population composition — this retrospective bridging is the substantive statistical work; (2) no industry body to declare the discontinuity, so each platform must handle it alone and comparability across platforms is lost; (3) effects that vary by audience composition, meaning the adjustment is sender-specific rather than a single index factor; (4) downstream systems rather than reports depending on the metric, so the transition is an engineering programme rather than a publication note; and (5) commercial reluctance, since restating history downward looks like admitting a performance decline.

## Target Customer
Messaging platform data teams, marketing measurement functions, and industry bodies who could declare and standardise the discontinuity.

## Impact If Solved
Statistical agencies change metrics without destroying the series and marketing had one broken externally and kept reporting it. Retrospective bridging from audience composition is the substantive work, and the adjustment is sender-specific rather than a single factor.
