# Engagement Signal Reconstruction

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/email-sms-marketing-platforms|Email & SMS Marketing Platforms]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to rebuild the segmentation, sunsetting and send-time machinery on signals that still mean something — and whoever does that replaces an industry running on a metric a privacy feature destroyed five years ago.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.6B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~18% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — a destroyed metric, still everywhere
**Target Buyer:** Platform data leadership and lifecycle teams
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the replacement signals are already collected

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Mail Privacy Protection pre-fetches images for a large share of the email population, which means open rates are inflated by an amount that varies by audience composition and cannot be recovered. Opens still drive engagement-based segmentation, sunset policies, send-time models and reported campaign performance across the industry. This is a distinct contest from placement: placement is unobservable and must be inferred, while this signal was observable, was destroyed, and the machinery built on it is still running.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Open tracking pixels, engagement segments defined on opens, sunset policies triggered by open recency, send-time models trained on open timestamps, and campaign reporting led by open rate. The gaps: inflation that varies by audience and cannot be corrected per recipient; segmentation that misclassifies; sunsetting that retains the wrong people; send-time models trained on machine behaviour; and reporting that is not comparable across audiences or over time.
