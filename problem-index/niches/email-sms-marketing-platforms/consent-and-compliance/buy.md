**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make consent provable and bulk sender requirements automatic rather than advisory — and whoever does that removes a litigation exposure and a delivery gate in the same product.

## The Problem
Financial services, healthcare and pharmaceuticals maintain evidence trails designed to be examined: immutable audit logs, disclosure capture showing what a customer was shown, records retained to a defined schedule, and evidence assembly for regulators and litigation. The practice exists because these organisations are routinely asked to prove what happened. Marketing consent — which is now examined in litigation and by carriers — is stored as a row with a timestamp and a free-text source.

## What Already Exists
Immutable audit logging with tamper evidence; disclosure and presentation capture; records retention scheduling; evidence assembly and legal hold; and consent management platforms from the privacy world.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to consent captured on someone else's website at enormous volume. It requires: (1) capture of the presentation layer on a site the platform does not control, which is the hard technical piece and the reason existing consent management products do not solve this — they record a decision, not what was shown; (2) volume and cost suited to millions of consents rather than to a regulated transaction, ruling out anything with per-record cost; (3) an evidence standard set by litigation and by carrier assessment rather than by a regulator's written rules, so what suffices must be inferred from case outcomes; (4) retroactive assessment of existing lists whose provenance was never captured, which is where the current exposure sits and which no forward-looking product addresses; and (5) an owner who is a marketer, so the capture must be invisible in the sign-up flow.

## Target Customer
Brand legal and compliance teams, messaging platforms, and consent management vendors for whom marketing-scale provenance is unserved.

## Impact If Solved
Regulated industries capture what the customer was shown because they are examined on it, and consent platforms record only the decision. Capturing the presentation layer on a site the platform does not control is the hard piece, and retroactive assessment is where the current exposure actually sits.
