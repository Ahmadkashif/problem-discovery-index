# Consent & Regulatory Compliance

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/email-sms-marketing-platforms|Email & SMS Marketing Platforms]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make consent provable and bulk sender requirements automatic rather than advisory — and whoever does that removes a litigation exposure and a delivery gate in the same product.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.1B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — checkboxes and assertions
**Target Buyer:** Legal, compliance and platform product leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — provenance and enforcement are both mechanisable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Compliance here has real teeth. Bulk sender requirements made authentication, one-click unsubscribe and a hard complaint threshold enforceable conditions of delivery rather than best practice. Text messaging litigation is an active and expensive risk, consent provenance is frequently poor, and registration sits between every brand and the carriers. These are not policy questions; they are the difference between a channel working and not, and between a marketing programme and a class action. That combination of delivery consequence and legal consequence makes it a distinct contest.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Consent checkboxes, timestamp records of varying quality, unsubscribe handling, authentication configuration, and complaint rate dashboards. The gaps: consent recorded without the context that proves it; no capture of what the subscriber actually saw; requirements treated as advisory settings; complaint thresholds monitored rather than enforced; and no evidence package if challenged.
