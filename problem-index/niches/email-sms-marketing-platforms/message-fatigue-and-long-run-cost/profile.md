# Message Fatigue & Long-Run Cost

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/email-sms-marketing-platforms|Email & SMS Marketing Platforms]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to price what an extra message costs in the recipient's future willingness to hear from a brand — and whoever measures that changes how much the whole channel sends.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$900M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — revenue today, unsubscribe over a year
**Target Buyer:** Platform data leadership and brand leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the closed loop makes it measurable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every platform ships send-time optimisation trained on opens, and none of them models the thing that actually matters, which is what an extra message costs in the recipient's future willingness to hear from you. The platform holds the message, the recipient, the engagement and the order in one system, which makes this measurable in a way it is nowhere else. It is not measured because the revenue arrives today, the unsubscribe arrives over a year, and the incentive of a platform paid by volume points away from asking.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Send-time optimisation trained on opens, frequency caps set by convention, unsubscribe rate reporting, and per-campaign revenue attribution. The gaps: no cost term for sending; frequency caps chosen without evidence; unsubscribe treated as a rate rather than as a consequence; no model of declining responsiveness; and optimisation that maximises today's revenue with no charge for tomorrow's.
