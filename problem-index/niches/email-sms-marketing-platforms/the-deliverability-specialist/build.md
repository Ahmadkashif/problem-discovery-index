# A Few Hundred People and Everyone Who Needs Them

**Niche:** [[niches/email-sms-marketing-platforms/the-deliverability-specialist/profile|The Deliverability Specialist]]
**Industry:** [[industries/email-sms-marketing-platforms|Email & SMS Marketing Platforms]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A small profession is responsible for whether a company's messages reach anyone, using tools that do not show placement, against filters whose operators do not explain themselves.
**Tags:** #worker-facing #tacit-knowledge-ml #evaluation-metrics #workflow-orchestration #gradient-boosting #confidence-intervals #automation #large-language-models
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a craft practised by a few hundred people into a system anyone can operate — and whoever does that makes the scarcest expertise in the channel available to everyone who needs it.

## The Problem
A brand's email stops working. The people who can diagnose this reliably number in the hundreds worldwide. They work from a set of checks they hold in their heads, developed over years, tested against providers that change without notice, supported by a community that shares knowledge informally and a set of tools that show reputation scores rather than causes. Most senders have no access to one. They read a blog post, adjust something, and wait. The expertise is real and demonstrably valuable, and it exists almost entirely as individual tacit knowledge in a channel that carries a large share of commerce.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Deliverability grew as a craft in a domain where the rules are undisclosed and change, which makes codification feel impossible — and it is genuinely harder than in a documented domain, which is why nobody has tried properly. The experts' value derives partly from the scarcity. Platforms treat it as a support function. And the knowledge is distributed across a community rather than held anywhere that could be systematised.

## What to Build
Codify the practice and give it to everyone. Write down the diagnostic sequence as an explicit decision procedure — what to check, in what order, what each result implies — which is the fix and is a substantial exercise that produces something no individual currently has written down. Automate every check that can be automated, which is most of them: authentication, configuration, list composition, complaint patterns, engagement distribution by provider, sending pattern changes. Rank the candidate causes with evidence rather than returning a score, since the specialist's output is a diagnosis and a score is not one. Prescribe remediation with expected timelines, because the most common question after a diagnosis is how long recovery takes and the honest answer is knowable from past cases. Learn from resolved incidents across the platform's whole customer base, which is the corpus that makes this better than any individual expert and is currently discarded. Escalate genuinely hard cases to a human, since the residual will always need judgement and pretending otherwise discredits the system. Give senders without a specialist a competent first answer, which is the market and is most of the channel's users. Support the specialist rather than replacing them, because they will be the ones validating and improving it and their cooperation determines whether it is any good. Capture the community's knowledge with attribution, which is both fair and how the practice actually accumulated. And measure diagnostic accuracy against resolved outcomes, since an expert system that does not check itself is a blog post with a database.

## Target Customer
Deliverability specialists and the platforms employing them, senders with no access to expertise, and the service providers selling deliverability consulting.

## Impact If Built
The rules are undisclosed and changing, which makes codification harder than in a documented domain and is why nobody has tried properly. Writing the diagnostic sequence down and learning from resolved incidents across a whole customer base produces something no individual expert has.
