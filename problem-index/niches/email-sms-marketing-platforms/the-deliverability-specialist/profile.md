# The Deliverability Specialist

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/email-sms-marketing-platforms|Email & SMS Marketing Platforms]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a craft practised by a few hundred people into a system anyone can operate — and whoever does that makes the scarcest expertise in the channel available to everyone who needs it.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — expertise held in heads
**Target Buyer:** Deliverability and platform operations leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — diagnosis and remediation are codifiable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A small profession is responsible for whether a company's messages reach anyone, using tools that do not show placement, against filters whose operators do not explain themselves. There are a few hundred genuinely expert deliverability practitioners and tens of thousands of senders who need one. The expertise is real, is held individually, is transmitted through a practitioner community rather than through tooling, and is the difference between a working channel and a dead one. This is distinct from the placement inference contest: that builds the measurement, this is about the person who must act on it and the knowledge that does not scale.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Reputation dashboards, seed testing, authentication checkers, community forums, and individual experience. The gaps: no codified diagnostic process; expertise transmitted informally; no record of what worked in past incidents; no prioritised remediation; and no way for a sender without a specialist to get a competent answer.
