# Pre-Classification for Reviewers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ugc-video-platforms|UGC Video Platforms]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to decide automatically everything that does not need a person, so the queue that reaches a human is as small and as bearable as it can be — and whoever does it reduces a documented harm with capability already in the building.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.5B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~4% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — capability present, not pointed here
**Target Buyer:** Trust and safety engineering
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the models already exist

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The volume of distressing material a human sees is a function of how much pre-classification is deployed to spare them. That is an engineering objective distinct from enforcement accuracy: the goal is not to decide better but to decide more, and to present what remains in the least harmful form that still supports a decision. The platforms' own models are the most capable tool available and are optimised for enforcement outcomes rather than for exposure reduction.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Classifiers tuned for enforcement accuracy, escalation thresholds and a review interface. The gaps: no objective of minimising human exposure; thresholds set without reference to reviewer harm; presentation not minimised by default; summary and abstraction unused; and exposure volume not measured as a system output.
