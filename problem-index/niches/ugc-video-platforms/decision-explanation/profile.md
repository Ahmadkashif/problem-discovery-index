# Decision Explanation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ugc-video-platforms|UGC Video Platforms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make a classifier state which passage of a video triggered its decision and how confident it was — and whoever does it turns an unaccountable determination into one a person can act on.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$10B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~14% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — capability present, unused
**Target Buyer:** Trust and safety engineering leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — within existing model capability

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This contest is a capability inside the model. A system that identifies subject, context and tone well enough to rank a billion videos can localise what it objected to and quantify how sure it was. Producing that, calibrating it, and presenting it safely is a modelling and product problem owned by engineers. It is entirely separable from whether an appeal process exists to act on the explanation.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Classifier outputs, a policy label and internal review tooling. The gaps: no localisation of the triggering passage; confidence not surfaced; automated and reviewed decisions indistinguishable; explanation not generated for the creator; and no measurement of whether explanations reduce repeat violations.
