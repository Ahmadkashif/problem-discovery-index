**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make an automated decision that changes someone's income explainable and contestable — and the contest splits cleanly enough that it is not terminal.

## The Problem
Systems that make determinations affecting people's livelihoods — benefits, licensing, employment, regulatory sanction — converged on the same requirements: notice of the decision and its basis, an opportunity to respond, a reasoned outcome, and an independent review. The principles exist because unexplained determinations by an unaccountable process are intolerable regardless of whether they are usually right. Platform enforcement makes determinations of comparable consequence with none of it.

## What Already Exists
Reasoned decision requirements; notice and opportunity to respond; independent review and appeal tiers; decision record keeping; and published statistics on outcomes and reversals.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to millions of decisions a day made by classifiers. It requires: (1) a reasoned decision generated automatically rather than written by a decision-maker, which is the substantive adaptation and is exactly what the platforms' own models could do; (2) volume that makes a human reviewer per appeal impossible, so review must be triaged by likely error rather than applied uniformly; (3) an adversary who will use the explanation, which administrative law rarely contemplates and which calibrates how much detail is given; (4) no statutory framework in most jurisdictions, so the standard is emerging rather than settled; and (5) a decision that is commercial rather than governmental, which changes the obligation's source but not its substance.

## Target Customer
Trust and safety, policy and legal leadership, creators, regulators, and governance and compliance vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Administrative law arrived at reasoned decisions and appeal because unexplained determinations about livelihoods are intolerable. Generating the reasoned decision automatically is the adaptation, and the platforms' own models are the best available tool for it.
