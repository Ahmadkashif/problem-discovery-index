# Enforcement & Governance

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ugc-video-platforms|UGC Video Platforms]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make an automated decision that changes someone's income explainable and contestable — and the contest splits cleanly enough that it is not terminal.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$18B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~26% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — classifiers that cannot explain themselves
**Target Buyer:** Trust and safety leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — explanation exists, adjudication does not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A classifier removes a video, demonetises a channel or quietly reduces its distribution, and the person whose income just changed receives a policy category and no way to find out what happened. Enforcement at this scale is automated by necessity and consequential in the individual case, and the platforms have built the most capable content understanding systems in existence and pointed almost none of that capability at explaining their own decisions. Regulatory requirements for reasoned decisions are closing the gap from the outside.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Classifiers, policy categories, a notification and an appeal queue. The gaps: no statement of what triggered a decision; confidence not expressed; distribution suppression invisible; appeals unresourced and uninformative; and no way for a creator to learn from an enforcement.
