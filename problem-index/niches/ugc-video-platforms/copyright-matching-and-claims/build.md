# A Claim With a Cost Attached

**Niche:** [[niches/ugc-video-platforms/copyright-matching-and-claims/profile|Copyright Matching & Claims]]
**Industry:** [[industries/ugc-video-platforms|UGC Video Platforms]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** The claimant gets the revenue while the dispute runs and loses nothing if the claim was wrong, which is the whole design.
**Tags:** #compliance #gradient-boosting #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #contrastive-learning #revenue-impact #workflow-orchestration #descriptive-statistics
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to run a claims system where being wrong costs the claimant something — and whoever introduces that cost stops a dispute mechanism being used as a revenue weapon.

## The Problem
The incentive structure is the problem. Matching generates candidate claims cheaply, the claimant asserts them at essentially no cost, revenue is redirected to them while a dispute runs, and an unsuccessful claim ends with the revenue returning and nothing else happening. A rational claimant therefore claims broadly. The creator on the other side has their income redirected on an automated match against material that may be public domain, their own, or a fragment too short to matter.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The system was built to satisfy rights holders whose cooperation the platform needed, so the asymmetry is structural rather than accidental — a mechanism designed to keep one party satisfied will not impose costs on that party. Creators have no collective leverage. Wrong claims are individually small. And the dispute outcome data that would show the pattern is not published.

## What to Build
Make claim accuracy consequential. Score claimants on their dispute outcomes and restrict claiming privileges accordingly, which is the core and introduces the missing cost without adjudicating individual claims differently. Hold disputed revenue in escrow rather than paying it to the claimant during a dispute, since the current arrangement funds the behaviour. Assess fragment length and context before a claim is allowed, because the shortest and most contested claims are mechanically identifiable. Detect public domain and widely-licensed material, as claims on it are a recurring and obviously wrong category. Detect a claim against a creator's own original audio, which is both common and embarrassing and is checkable. Report dispute outcomes by claimant, which is the transparency that would change behaviour. Support the fair use and transformative case with a real assessment path rather than a form that goes nowhere. Escalate repeat wrongful claiming as a policy matter, since a claimant with a poor record is an identifiable problem. Return the revenue with interest or a penalty where a claim fails, because symmetry is the entire fix. And publish the aggregate transfer, as the scale of it is the argument.

## Target Customer
Rights operations and policy leadership, creators subject to claims, rights holders with legitimate claims, and content identification vendors.

## Impact If Built
A mechanism designed to keep one party satisfied will not impose costs on that party, which is why claiming broadly is rational. Escrowing disputed revenue and scoring claimants on outcomes introduces the missing cost without changing how any individual claim is judged.
