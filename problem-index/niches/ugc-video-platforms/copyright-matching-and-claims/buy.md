**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Legal systems impose costs on unsuccessful claimants precisely to deter speculative claiming, and this one imposes none.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to run a claims system where being wrong costs the claimant something — and whoever introduces that cost stops a dispute mechanism being used as a revenue weapon.

## The Problem
Every mature dispute system faces the problem of speculative claiming and answers it structurally: costs follow the event, sanctions exist for unfounded claims, repeat vexatious claimants can be restricted, and the interim position does not hand the disputed asset to the claimant. Those features exist because a system without them is used as a weapon. Platform copyright claims have none of them and are used accordingly.

## What Already Exists
Adverse costs rules; sanctions for unfounded claims; vexatious litigant restrictions; interim relief principles that do not prejudge; and published outcome statistics.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to millions of automated claims and no court. It requires: (1) sanctions applied automatically at volume rather than by a judge, so claimant scoring substitutes for a costs order — this is the substantive adaptation; (2) an interim position that currently favours the claimant absolutely, which is the opposite of the principle and is the single change with the largest effect; (3) claims generated by matching software rather than asserted by a person, blurring who is responsible for an unfounded claim; (4) a platform that needs the large claimants' cooperation, which is the commercial reason the asymmetry persists; and (5) amounts too small to litigate, so the platform's own process is the only remedy available.

## Target Customer
Rights operations, policy and legal leadership, creators, rights holders, and regulators examining platform dispute mechanisms.

## Impact If Solved
Every mature dispute system imposes costs on unfounded claims because otherwise it gets weaponised. Escrowing the disputed revenue reverses an interim position that currently prejudges every case, and claimant scoring is the automatable form of a costs rule.
