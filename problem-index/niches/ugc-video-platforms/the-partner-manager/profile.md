# The Partner Manager

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ugc-video-platforms|UGC Video Platforms]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let the one person a creator can reach actually see what happened to them — and whoever gives that role visibility turns a sympathetic dead end into a resolution.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$4B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~6% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — a relationship with no access
**Target Buyer:** Creator support leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — case visibility and escalation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A creator's income drops overnight, they call the one person at the platform who answers, and that person has no more visibility into the reason than the creator does. Partner managers hold the platform's relationship with its most valuable supply, absorb the consequences of automated decisions they cannot see, and have no route to resolve most of what reaches them. They are simultaneously the platform's most human surface and its least equipped one.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A relationship, a customer record and an internal escalation form. The gaps: no access to enforcement or classification detail; no view of distribution changes affecting a creator; escalation without authority; recurring issues uncatalogued; and the role's inability to resolve unmeasured.
