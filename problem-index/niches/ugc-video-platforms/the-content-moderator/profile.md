# The Content Moderator

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ugc-video-platforms|UGC Video Platforms]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to reduce what a human has to see while keeping the judgement the work requires — and whoever does it addresses an occupational harm documented in litigation, in reporting and in the platforms' own policies.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$7B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — throughput targets and exposure
**Target Buyer:** Trust and safety operations leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the platforms' own models are the tool

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The material that classifiers escalate is reviewed by people, most of them employed through outsourcing vendors, under throughput targets, concentrated in lower-cost labour markets, with support provisions that reporting has repeatedly found inadequate. The occupational harm of sustained exposure to the worst content on the internet is documented. The volume of distressing material a person sees is a function of how much pre-classification is deployed to spare them, and the platforms' own models are the most capable tool available for reducing it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A review queue, policy guidance, throughput targets and a support provision. The gaps: exposure not minimised by available classification; queue composition not managed for wellbeing; throughput targets driving the harm; support provisions inadequate and unmeasured; and the workforce at arm's length through vendors.
