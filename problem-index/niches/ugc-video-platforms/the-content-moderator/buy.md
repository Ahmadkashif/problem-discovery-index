**Industry:** [[industries/ugc-video-platforms|UGC Video Platforms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Emergency services, forensic investigation and child protection built exposure management for the same material, and content moderation has a throughput target.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to reduce what a human has to see while keeping the judgement the work requires — and whoever does it addresses an occupational harm documented in litigation, in reporting and in the platforms' own policies.

## The Problem
Professions that require sustained exposure to traumatic material — emergency dispatch, forensic examination, child protection investigation, war crimes documentation — developed occupational health practice: exposure limits, mandatory rotation, supervision and debriefing, screening and support, and workload designed around sustainability rather than throughput. The practice exists because the harm was demonstrated and the professions were organised enough to require a response.

## What Already Exists
Exposure limits and rotation protocols; supervision and structured debriefing; trauma-informed support provision; screening and monitoring; and workload design for sustainability.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to an outsourced workforce under throughput targets. It requires: (1) practice imposed through vendor contracts rather than through employment, since the platform does not employ most of the workers — this is the structural difficulty and is where the standards currently fail; (2) volumes far higher than any of the source professions, so exposure reduction must be technological rather than procedural; (3) work concentrated in labour markets with weaker protections, which is a deliberate cost structure; (4) material that can be pre-filtered and abstracted by machine, which the source professions cannot do and is the largest available improvement; and (5) throughput economics that directly oppose every protective measure.

## Target Customer
Trust and safety operations leadership, vendor operators, moderators and their representatives, regulators and occupational health specialists.

## Impact If Solved
The source professions built exposure management because the harm was demonstrated and they were organised enough to demand it. Here the volumes require a technological reduction rather than a procedural one, which is the one advantage this setting has.
