# Live Operations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ugc-video-platforms|UGC Video Platforms]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to moderate, monetise and deliver a broadcast while it is happening — and whoever handles the real-time case wins the surface where every decision has to be made before anyone can review it.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.5B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** High — delivery solved, governance not
**Target Buyer:** Live platform leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — real-time classification and intervention

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Live changes every problem in the category. Moderation must act while the broadcast is running, because a removal after the fact addresses nothing. Chat moves faster than any human can read. Monetisation decisions are made in real time on content that does not exist yet. Enforcement against a live stream is the most consequential action the platform can take and is the least reviewable. The delivery engineering is excellent and the governance layer was designed for recorded video.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Live delivery infrastructure, chat moderation tools, automated detection and a reporting route. The gaps: governance designed for recorded content; chat moderation left to volunteer moderators; interventions binary rather than graduated; no review of a decision taken during a broadcast; and the streamer given no warning before enforcement.
