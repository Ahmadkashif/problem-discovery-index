# Creator Monetisation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ugc-video-platforms|UGC Video Platforms]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make the rules that determine a creator's income legible and predictable — and whoever does it retains the supply that every platform in the category is competing for.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$8B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~11% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — automated rules, opaque effects
**Target Buyer:** Monetisation leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — explanation, prediction and stability

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A creator's income is determined by advertiser suitability classifications, revenue share terms, eligibility thresholds, format-specific rates and policy changes, most of which move without notice and none of which are explained at the level of an individual video. The creator experiences income volatility they cannot attribute, and the platform experiences a supply base that is permanently anxious and increasingly diversifying away.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A monetisation dashboard, advertiser suitability ratings, a policy page and a revenue report. The gaps: suitability decisions per video unexplained; rate variation unattributed; policy changes unannounced; income volatility not decomposed; and no way to predict the monetisation outcome of a video before publishing.
