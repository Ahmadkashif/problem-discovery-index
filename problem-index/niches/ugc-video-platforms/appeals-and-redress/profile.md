# Appeals & Redress

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ugc-video-platforms|UGC Video Platforms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to run an appeal process that can actually overturn decisions at a volume nobody has resourced for — and whoever builds one that reverses the wrong calls converts an unaccountable system into a governed one.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$8B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~11% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — a queue and a repeat of the first answer
**Target Buyer:** Trust and safety operations leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — triage, prioritisation and reviewer support

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This contest is adjudication operations. An appeal arrives into a queue at a volume no process has ever been resourced to handle, is reviewed briefly or automatically, and frequently returns the original decision with no more explanation. Making it work means triaging by likely error, allocating scarce review capacity where reversal is probable, supporting the reviewer, and being willing to reverse — which is an operations and resourcing problem, not a modelling one.

## Current Tools & Gaps
An appeal button, a queue and a second decision. The gaps: appeals not triaged by likely error; reviewer given no more information than the creator; reversal rates unpublished; repeat appeals not handled; and no escalation tier for consequential cases.
