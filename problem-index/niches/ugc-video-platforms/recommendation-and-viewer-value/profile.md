# Recommendation & Viewer Value

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ugc-video-platforms|UGC Video Platforms]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to optimise for a viewer who is glad they watched rather than one who watched for longer — and whoever measures that instead of assuming it holds the attention everyone else is burning.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$20B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~29% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very High — the most advanced systems anywhere
**Target Buyer:** Product and data leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — an objective problem, not a modelling one

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The recommender allocates the attention that is the entire business. It optimises time spent, which is measurable and immediate, and nobody measures whether the viewer was glad afterwards. That proxy is not obviously wrong and has never been validated against anything, and the systems built on it are among the most capable operating anywhere — which makes the objective, rather than the modelling, the thing that is actually contested.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Very large ranking systems, extensive experimentation and engagement metrics. The gaps: satisfaction not measured; long-horizon effects on retention and wellbeing unexamined; the objective never validated; creator-side exposure allocation unreported; and regret and low-value sessions undetected.
