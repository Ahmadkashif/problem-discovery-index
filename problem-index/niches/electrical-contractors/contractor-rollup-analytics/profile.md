# Electrical Contractor Rollup Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electrical-contractors|Electrical Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The corporate development and operations analytics groups at the consolidating electrical and specialty trade platforms screen acquisitions in a market of seventy thousand mostly sub-ten-person firms, and benchmark bid win rates, job margin, and labour productivity across acquired operations.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-80 analysts across corp dev, estimating, and operations
**Output:** Acquisition models, bid and margin analytics, labour productivity benchmarking
**Proprietary data:** Job-level estimated-versus-actual labour data across many acquired firms — the direct measure of estimating accuracy the trade otherwise lacks
**Clock:** Deal timelines and bid calendars
**Buyer:** SVP Corporate Development / VP Operations
