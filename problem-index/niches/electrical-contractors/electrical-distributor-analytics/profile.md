# Electrical Distributor Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electrical-contractors|Electrical Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The category and business intelligence groups at Rexel, Sonepar, Graybar, and WESCO analyze what contractors buy, forecast demand, price competitively against project-specific quotes, and sell category insight to the manufacturers whose products they stock.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 category analysts, pricing analysts, and data scientists
**Output:** Category strategy, project quote pricing, demand forecasts, manufacturer reporting
**Proprietary data:** Purchase and quote data across tens of thousands of contractors — visible as an early indicator of project activity before any public reporting
**Clock:** Quote turnaround and project schedules
**Buyer:** VP of Category Management / Chief Analytics Officer
