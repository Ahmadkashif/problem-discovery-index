# Electrical Estimating Service Bureaus

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electrical-contractors|Electrical Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Outsourced estimating firms perform takeoffs and produce bid-ready estimates for electrical contractors who lack in-house estimating capacity — reading drawings, counting devices and runs, applying labour units, and delivering a priced estimate against a fixed bid date. The estimate is the entire deliverable and 80% of the trade is too small to employ a full-time estimator.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-200 estimators, takeoff technicians, and reviewers
**Output:** Takeoffs and priced estimates — the billable artefact
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated takeoff productivity and estimating judgment across thousands of projects; bid outcomes are usually not returned by the client
**Clock:** Bid dates, absolute and frequent
**Buyer:** Managing Director / Chief Estimator
