# Construction Project Lead & Plan Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electrical-contractors|Electrical Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Dodge Construction Network and ConstructConnect employ large research staffs who read permits, planning filings, bid documents, and drawings to identify projects before they go out to bid, classify their scope and value, and publish them as leads with plans attached. Electrical contractors subscribe because finding bidding opportunities early is the difference between a full and an empty backlog.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-1,500 project researchers, plan analysts, and data staff
**Output:** Project leads, scope and value classification, plans and specifications, market forecasts — the subscription
**Proprietary data:** A project database assembled by human research from thousands of jurisdictions and sources, with pre-bid intelligence that exists nowhere publicly aggregated
**Clock:** Bid dates, which are fixed and after which a lead is worthless
**Buyer:** VP of Research / Chief Data Officer
