# Electrical Inspection & Code Enforcement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electrical-contractors|Electrical Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Municipal authorities having jurisdiction and third-party inspection agencies inspect electrical installations against the adopted code and issue the approvals without which work cannot be energized or closed out. The inspector's determination is final at the point of inspection and is the trade's most consequential external judgment.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-200 inspectors and plan reviewers per large jurisdiction or agency
**Output:** Inspection results, correction notices, plan review findings
**Proprietary data:** Correction history across every installation in a jurisdiction — a direct map of which code provisions contractors most often get wrong
**Clock:** Inspection scheduling and project close-out dates
**Buyer:** Chief Building Official / Agency Director
