# Electrical Product Data Exchange

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electrical-contractors|Electrical Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
The Industry Data Exchange Association and Trade Service maintain the standardized product, pricing, and attribute data that flows between electrical manufacturers, distributors, and contractor software. Every estimating and purchasing system in the trade resolves part numbers against this data.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 data analysts, taxonomists, and manufacturer liaison staff
**Output:** Standardized product and price data, attribute enrichment, cross-reference tables
**Proprietary data:** Normalized attributes and cross-references across millions of electrical products from thousands of manufacturers who publish inconsistently
**Clock:** Manufacturer price file releases and product introductions
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Director of Content
