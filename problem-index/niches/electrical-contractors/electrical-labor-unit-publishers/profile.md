# Electrical Labour Unit & Estimating Database Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electrical-contractors|Electrical Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Trimble Accubid, ConEst, McCormick, and the NECA labour unit manuals publish how long each electrical installation task takes — per device, per conductor run, per condition — which is the number every electrical bid in the country is built from. Researchers maintain hundreds of thousands of line items against changing products, methods, and code requirements. The trade's Pass 1 analysis identifies labour estimating as its second great tacit-knowledge problem; this is the layer that sells the answer.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 estimating researchers, electrical engineers, and data staff
**Output:** Labour unit databases, material pricing integration, assembly libraries — the licensed product
**Proprietary data:** Decades of accumulated labour unit research by task, method, and installation condition, maintained across product generations
**Clock:** Code cycle revisions, product introductions, and the bid deadlines every subscriber works to
**Buyer:** VP of Content / Director of Estimating Data
