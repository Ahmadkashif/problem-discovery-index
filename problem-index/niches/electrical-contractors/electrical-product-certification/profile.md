# Electrical Product Certification Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electrical-contractors|Electrical Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
UL and Intertek write the product safety standards the electrical code references, test products against them, and issue the listings without which an inspector will not approve an installation. The standards development and the testing are both continuous research operations, and the listing mark controls market access.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-3,000 standards engineers, test engineers, and technical staff across electrical
**Output:** Product safety standards, test reports, certification listings
**Proprietary data:** Test failure history across product categories and manufacturers, plus the standards development record
**Clock:** Product launch dates and standards revision cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Standards / Engineering Director
