# Electrical Apprenticeship Training Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electrical-contractors|Electrical Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The joint apprenticeship training committees and their national curriculum body develop the multi-year apprenticeship curriculum and assessments that produce licensed electricians, and research the workforce pipeline the Pass 1 analysis identifies as failing to replace retirements.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-20 curriculum developers and researchers at the national level
**Output:** Apprenticeship curriculum, competency assessments, workforce research
**Proprietary data:** Apprentice progression and completion data across local training programmes
**Clock:** Code cycle curriculum updates and annual intake
**Buyer:** Director of Training
