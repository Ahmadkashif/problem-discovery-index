# Electrical Manufacturer Application Engineering

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electrical-contractors|Electrical Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The application engineering and technical support groups at Eaton, Schneider, ABB, and the electrical manufacturers answer contractor and engineer questions about how to apply their products under the code, publish selection guides and calculation tools, and feed field findings into product design.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 application engineers and technical support staff per major manufacturer
**Output:** Application guidance, selection tools, technical bulletins, field failure analysis
**Proprietary data:** Technical support enquiry history — a precise record of what the trade cannot work out from the code and the catalogue — plus field failure returns
**Clock:** Product launches and code cycle updates
**Buyer:** Director of Application Engineering / VP Technical Services
