# Construction Surety Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electrical-contractors|Electrical Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Surety underwriters assess whether an electrical contractor can complete the work it is bidding, analyzing financial capacity, backlog, and project history to issue the bonds that public and large private work requires. The bonding decision determines what a contractor is allowed to bid.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 underwriters, financial analysts, and claims staff
**Output:** Bond underwriting decisions, capacity assessments, default analyses
**Proprietary data:** Contractor financial and completion history joined to default outcomes across the book
**Clock:** Bid bond deadlines and annual programme renewals
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
