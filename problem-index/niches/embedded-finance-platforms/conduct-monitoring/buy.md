## The Problem
Conduct risk is a recognised discipline in banking, separate from financial crime and from credit. It concerns whether customers are treated fairly, whether products are sold appropriately, whether disclosures are adequate, and whether outcomes for customers are acceptable. Indicators are defined, monitored and reported, and supervisors examine them. Embedded finance platforms have acquired responsibility for exactly this and monitor with transaction rules built to detect structuring and sanctions matches.

## What Already Exists
Conduct risk frameworks and indicator sets; customer outcome testing; product governance and review; complaints analysis as a conduct signal; and supervisory reporting on conduct metrics.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to conduct performed by a third party and observed only through infrastructure. It requires: (1) indicators derived from transaction behaviour rather than from file reviews, customer surveys and complaint analysis, since the platform has none of the usual conduct evidence — constructing behavioural conduct indicators is the substantive contribution and would be useful well beyond this category; (2) complaints held by the programme rather than by the monitoring party, which removes the practice's single richest signal unless it is obtained contractually; (3) many products at once, requiring an indicator framework that adapts to product type rather than a fixed set; (4) the comparison across programmes as a substitute for the absent direct evidence, which is a genuinely new instrument; and (5) no direct relationship with the customer whose outcome is being assessed.

## Target Customer
Platform compliance and risk teams, sponsor bank conduct functions, and conduct risk vendors for whom third-party-observed conduct is unserved.

## Impact If Solved
Conduct risk is a distinct discipline and this layer monitors it with financial crime rules. Constructing conduct indicators from transaction behaviour alone is the substantive contribution and has value beyond this category.
