# Cross-Programme Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/embedded-finance-platforms|Embedded Finance Platforms]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn the simultaneous view of hundreds of financial products built on identical infrastructure into predictions a single programme could never make — and whoever does it first defines what the position is worth beyond billing.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$700M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — data held, used after the fact
**Target Buyer:** Platform data and product leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — a modelling problem over data already collected

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The platform sees the full transaction and behavioural record of dozens or hundreds of distinct financial products at once — different teams, different populations, identical rails. That is a vantage point nobody else in banking has, and it is currently used for billing and for incident response, which is to say after the fact. What it could support is prediction: which programmes will grow, which will fail, which designs produce good customer outcomes, which fraud pattern is about to arrive everywhere because it has appeared on three programmes already.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Billing analytics, incident postmortems, and per-programme dashboards. The gaps: no comparison across programmes; no archetype baselines; fraud patterns not propagated between programmes; programme health not predicted; and the asset's governance never established, which is what keeps it unused.
