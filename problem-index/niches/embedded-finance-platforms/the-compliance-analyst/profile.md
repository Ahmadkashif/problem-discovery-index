# The Compliance Analyst

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/embedded-finance-platforms|Embedded Finance Platforms]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give one analyst responsible for dozens of independently run products enough context to judge an alert without learning each product from scratch — and whoever supplies that context decides whether the oversight function is real or nominal.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$700M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — alert queues without product context
**Target Buyer:** Platform compliance operations leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — context assembly and triage

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Four analysts cover forty programmes. Each programme was designed by a different company for a different population with a different economic model, and the analyst reviewing an alert on one of them may never have seen that product. Judging whether a transaction pattern is suspicious requires knowing what normal looks like for that specific product, and that knowledge does not exist anywhere the analyst can reach. This is a distinct audience: the person on whom the whole oversight obligation actually rests, working without the context the judgement requires.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Alert queues, case management, transaction search, and whatever the programme's own team will explain over Slack. The gaps: no product context attached to alerts; no per-programme baseline to judge against; investigation evidence gathered by hand from several systems; and no measure of whether the analysts' judgements are any good.
