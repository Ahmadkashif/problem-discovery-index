**Industry:** [[industries/embedded-finance-platforms|Embedded Finance Platforms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Financial crime investigation tooling is mature and assumes one institution, one product set and an analyst who knows the book.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give one analyst responsible for dozens of independently run products enough context to judge an alert without learning each product from scratch — and whoever supplies that context decides whether the oversight function is real or nominal.

## The Problem
Anti-money-laundering investigation platforms are well developed: alert queues, case management, network visualisation, customer risk scoring, regulatory filing workflows, quality assurance sampling. All of it assumes a bank investigating its own customers on its own products, where the analyst's product knowledge is background. The embedded finance analyst has none of that background and the tooling has no way to supply it.

## What Already Exists
Transaction monitoring and case management platforms; network and link analysis; customer risk rating; filing workflow with regulatory forms; and quality assurance sampling regimes.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to many independently designed products under one analyst. It requires: (1) product context delivered as part of the case rather than assumed to be known, which is a capability the category has never needed and is the substantive gap; (2) risk models per programme rather than per institution, since a single customer risk rating across forty products is meaningless; (3) the customer relationship held by the programme rather than by the investigating party, so the know-your-customer evidence is one step removed; (4) escalation to a programme operator who is a customer rather than to a colleague, which changes the workflow's politics entirely; and (5) quality assurance across products with different norms, where sampling uniformly measures the wrong thing.

## Target Customer
Platform compliance operations, sponsor bank financial crime functions overseeing this, and AML vendors for whom the multi-programme analyst is unserved.

## Impact If Solved
The tooling assumes ambient product knowledge and the analyst has none. Delivering product context inside the case is a capability the mature category never had to build.
