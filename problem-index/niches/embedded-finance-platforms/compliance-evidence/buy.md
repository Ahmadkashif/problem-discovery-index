# Third-Party Risk Management Inverted

**Niche:** [[niches/embedded-finance-platforms/compliance-evidence/profile|Compliance Evidence]]
**Industry:** [[industries/embedded-finance-platforms|Embedded Finance Platforms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** A mature category exists for banks to assess their third parties, and nothing exists for the third party that must satisfy many banks at once while overseeing its own downstream parties.
**Tags:** #compliance #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #automation #large-language-models #graph-theory #evaluation-metrics #bert
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make one substantive oversight record satisfy every bank partner and examiner that asks for it differently — and whoever does that stops evidence production from scaling with the number of partners.

## The Problem
Third-party risk management is well developed. Banks have frameworks, questionnaires, tiering methodologies, continuous monitoring tools and examination expectations for assessing vendors. All of it is built for the assessing party. The embedded finance platform sits on the other side — assessed by many banks simultaneously while itself overseeing dozens of programmes — and has essentially no tooling for that position.

## What Already Exists
Third-party risk platforms; standardised questionnaire frameworks; vendor tiering and continuous monitoring; shared assessment exchanges; and the regulatory guidance on third-party relationships that drives all of it.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation inverts the perspective and adds a layer. It requires: (1) the respondent's position rather than the assessor's, with the answer library, evidence mapping and consistency tracking that a repeatedly assessed party needs — this is the substantive inversion and shared assessment exchanges only gesture at it; (2) many assessors at once with divergent requirements, which is the defining condition and is absent from single-relationship tooling; (3) the platform as both assessed and assessor, since it oversees its programmes and is overseen — one substrate should serve both directions; (4) evidence that is behavioural and continuous rather than documentary and annual, because the oversight being evidenced is transaction monitoring; and (5) examination exposure through partners rather than directly, which changes what the evidence must survive.

## Target Customer
Platform compliance and partner management teams, sponsor bank third-party risk functions, and TPRM vendors for whom the respondent side is unserved.

## Impact If Solved
TPRM is mature and entirely one-sided. The repeatedly-assessed party that is itself an overseer has no tooling, and the inversion is a real product rather than a configuration.
