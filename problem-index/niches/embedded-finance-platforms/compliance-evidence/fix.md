# The Quarterly Pack Assembled by Hand

**Niche:** [[niches/embedded-finance-platforms/compliance-evidence/profile|Compliance Evidence]]
**Industry:** [[industries/embedded-finance-platforms|Embedded Finance Platforms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Every quarter the compliance team reconstructs three months of oversight from alerts, email threads and memory, because nothing recorded it as it happened.
**Tags:** #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation #quick-win #data-integration #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make one substantive oversight record satisfy every bank partner and examiner that asks for it differently — and whoever does that stops evidence production from scaling with the number of partners.

## The Problem
The pack is due in two weeks. The team pulls alert counts from the monitoring system, searches email for what was decided about the ones that mattered, asks an analyst who remembers roughly what happened in May, finds the remediation in a project tracker if someone logged it, and writes it up. The oversight was real; the record of it is reconstructed. When an examiner asks how a specific finding was resolved, the answer is in somebody's sent folder.

## Why It's Still Broken
The pack is a document deadline, so the work looks like writing rather than recording, and nobody built the recording step because the writing step always got done. Alerts, decisions, remediation and reporting live in four systems with no links between them. Reconstruction has always worked well enough to file on time. And the cost is paid by a small team whose capacity nobody measures.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Record at the moment, generate at the deadline. Capture the disposition when an alert is closed, with the reason and the evidence, which is the fix and takes seconds at the point of decision instead of hours three months later. Link remediation to the finding that prompted it, since the chain is what gets asked about and is what is missing. Log decisions where they are made rather than in email, because email is where the record goes to disappear. Generate the pack's factual sections from the recorded data, which removes the reconstruction entirely for everything countable. Keep a running narrative for the parts that need judgement, so the writing is editing rather than remembering. Timestamp everything, since when oversight happened is a question examiners ask and reconstruction cannot answer credibly. Make the record searchable by programme, period and finding type, which turns a two-week assembly into a query. Show the pack's state continuously rather than at the deadline, so gaps appear when they can still be closed. Track questions asked after each submission, because they reveal what the pack fails to say. And measure preparation hours, which is the number that makes the case.

## Who Feels the Pain
Compliance teams losing two weeks a quarter to reconstruction; analysts asked what they remember about May; and banks receiving a record written from memory.

## Impact If Fixed
The pack is a writing deadline so nobody built the recording step. Capturing disposition at the moment of decision converts the quarterly reconstruction into generation and makes the finding-to-remediation chain answerable.
