# Compliance Evidence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/embedded-finance-platforms|Embedded Finance Platforms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make one substantive oversight record satisfy every bank partner and examiner that asks for it differently — and whoever does that stops evidence production from scaling with the number of partners.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.0B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — documents, spreadsheets and email threads
**Target Buyer:** Platform compliance leadership and bank partner management
**Automation Potential:** High — generation and mapping over a common substrate

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This contest is demonstration. Whatever the platform detects, it must prove to the banks that sponsor its programmes — each of whom specifies oversight differently, asks on a different cadence, wants a different format, and will be examined by a regulator with its own expectations. The work is assembling, mapping and defending records rather than finding problems. It is an evidence generation and partner management problem, and the skills, the buyer and the winning shape all differ from detection.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Spreadsheets, document templates, shared drives, quarterly reporting packs assembled by hand, and an escalating volume of bank questionnaires. The gaps: the same substance reproduced in every partner's format; no common evidence substrate underneath; findings and their remediation tracked separately from the reports that describe them; and examination preparation as a fire drill.
