# End-Customer Protection

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/embedded-finance-platforms|Embedded Finance Platforms]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to guarantee the end customer's baseline protections — access to funds, disputes, disclosures, error resolution — regardless of which programme they signed up with, and whoever makes those protections a property of the platform rather than of the programme takes the risk the whole category carries.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — protections implemented per programme
**Target Buyer:** Platform risk and product leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — enforceable defaults in the platform layer

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The end customer holds an account at a bank they have never heard of, through a company that is not a bank, on a platform they do not know exists. Their protections — timely access to their money, dispute rights, error resolution, disclosure of fees, notice before closure — are supposed to follow the account. In practice they depend on how each programme implemented them. When a programme implements them badly or not at all, the customer has no visible route to anyone who can help, and the exposure lands on the bank and the platform.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Per-programme dispute and support implementations, contractual obligations on programme operators, and escalation when something becomes visible. The gaps: no platform-level enforcement of baseline protections; no measurement of whether programmes honour them; no route for a customer to reach the platform; and closure and freeze handling left entirely to the programme.
