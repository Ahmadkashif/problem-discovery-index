# Consumer Protection Operations

**Niche:** [[niches/embedded-finance-platforms/end-customer-protection/profile|End-Customer Protection]]
**Industry:** [[industries/embedded-finance-platforms|Embedded Finance Platforms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Retail banking runs consumer protection as an operational function with defined timeframes and measured outcomes, and this layer delegated it by contract and never measured anything.
**Tags:** #compliance #workflow-orchestration #evaluation-metrics #automation #descriptive-statistics #data-integration #worker-facing #confidence-intervals
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to guarantee the end customer's baseline protections — access to funds, disputes, disclosures, error resolution — regardless of which programme they signed up with, and whoever makes those protections a property of the platform rather than of the programme takes the risk the whole category carries.

## The Problem
Retail banks run consumer protection as a real operation: error resolution with statutory timeframes, dispute and chargeback handling, complaint management with root cause analysis, fee disclosure controls, account closure procedures, and reporting that supervisors examine. The obligations attach to the account and the account exists in embedded finance too — but the operation was delegated to companies that are not banks and have never run one.

## What Already Exists
Error resolution and dispute operations with regulated timeframes; complaint management systems with root cause analysis; chargeback processing; disclosure and fee governance; and consumer compliance testing programmes.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to protections delivered by a third party and assured by a fourth. It requires: (1) monitoring and enforcement of another party's operation rather than running one's own, which is the substantive inversion and has no template; (2) visibility limited to events on the rails rather than to cases in a system, so protection performance must be inferred from timing rather than read from a queue; (3) many operators with different capabilities, which means the baseline must be enforceable rather than merely trained; (4) the customer's complaint arriving at the programme rather than at the protecting party, removing the richest signal unless a route is built; and (5) programme failure as a scenario, which retail banking's procedures do not contemplate because the bank does not disappear.

## Target Customer
Platform risk and compliance leadership, sponsor bank consumer compliance functions, programme operators lacking the operation, and consumer compliance vendors for whom assured-third-party delivery is unserved.

## Impact If Solved
The obligations attach to the account and the operation was delegated to firms that have never run one. Assuring another party's protection performance from rails-level timing is the adaptation and has no precedent in the retail playbook.
