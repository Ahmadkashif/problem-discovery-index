# Concentration Risk Management

**Niche:** [[niches/embedded-finance-platforms/bank-partnership-and-charter/profile|Bank Partnership & Charter Access]]
**Industry:** [[industries/embedded-finance-platforms|Embedded Finance Platforms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Banking measures, limits and reports concentration risk as a discipline, and platforms whose entire business depends on one charter do not measure it at all.
**Tags:** #compliance #confidence-intervals #descriptive-statistics #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #hypothesis-testing #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make the sponsor bank relationship a managed, diversified, transferable asset rather than a single dependency negotiated once — and whoever can move a programme between banks without rebuilding it removes the category's largest existential risk.

## The Problem
Concentration risk is a formal discipline in banking. Exposure to a single counterparty, sector or geography is measured, limited, reported and examined, and the limits bind. The embedded finance platform has concentration of the most extreme kind — all or most of its business on one charter — and treats it as a commercial relationship rather than as a measured exposure with a limit.

## What Already Exists
Concentration limit frameworks; counterparty exposure measurement; single-name and sectoral limits with escalation; stress testing against counterparty loss; and supervisory reporting on concentration.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to concentration in a capability rather than in a credit exposure. It requires: (1) exposure measured as operational dependency rather than as a dollar amount at risk, since the loss is the ability to operate rather than a write-off — defining that metric is the substantive work; (2) the counterparty's exit driven by its regulator or its risk appetite rather than by its own failure, which changes the early warning signals entirely; (3) mitigation through portability and second relationships rather than through hedging or limits, because there is no market to offload into; (4) the exposure sitting at a platform with no prudential regime requiring it to look, so the discipline must be adopted voluntarily; and (5) programme-level as well as platform-level concentration, since customers depend on this too.

## Target Customer
Platform executive and risk leadership, sponsor bank relationship managers, and risk platform vendors whose concentration modules assume credit exposure.

## Impact If Solved
The discipline is mature and measures dollars at risk. Measuring concentration as operational dependency on a capability, with exit signals driven by a regulator rather than a default, is the adaptation and the metric does not yet exist.
