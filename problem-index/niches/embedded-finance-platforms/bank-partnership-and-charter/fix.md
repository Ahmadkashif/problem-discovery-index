# The Relationship Nobody Instruments

**Niche:** [[niches/embedded-finance-platforms/bank-partnership-and-charter/profile|Bank Partnership & Charter Access]]
**Industry:** [[industries/embedded-finance-platforms|Embedded Finance Platforms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The most consequential relationship the platform has is assessed by how the last quarterly meeting felt.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #change-point-detection #compliance #quick-win #evaluation-metrics #automation #worker-facing #confidence-intervals
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make the sponsor bank relationship a managed, diversified, transferable asset rather than a single dependency negotiated once — and whoever can move a programme between banks without rebuilding it removes the category's largest existential risk.

## The Problem
Ask a platform how its sponsor bank relationship is doing and the answer is an impression. Meanwhile the signals are all there and none are tracked: review turnaround times lengthening, findings per review increasing, more questions about a particular programme type, a new person on the bank's side asking different questions, requests for information that used to be routine now escalated, approval of new programmes slowing. Each of those is a leading indicator and each is noticed individually by whoever experienced it.

## Why It's Still Broken
Relationship health is treated as a matter of judgement, so it was never instrumented — measuring it looked like measuring a friendship rather than a dependency. The signals arrive scattered across people and inboxes. Nobody owns the aggregate. And a deteriorating relationship is uncomfortable to name, which means the impression stays optimistic until it cannot.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Instrument the interaction that already happens. Track review turnaround and findings per review over time, which is the fix and comes free from records the platform already keeps. Log every bank request, question and escalation in one place, since the volume and shape of questions is the clearest available signal. Watch for changes in who asks and what they ask about, because personnel and focus shifts precede policy shifts. Track approval times for new programmes, which is the most direct measure of appetite. Flag the trend rather than the level, since relationships deteriorate gradually and the slope is the signal. Review the aggregate on a schedule with a named owner, because scattered observations without a reader are not information. Compare across banks where the platform has more than one, which calibrates what normal looks like. Correlate the signals with programme types, so a bank cooling on one product is distinguished from a bank cooling generally. Record what was said in reviews in a structured form, since the same concerns recur before they harden. And connect the reading to the portability work, because an early warning with no response is just an earlier worry.

## Who Feels the Pain
Partnership managers working from impressions; executives surprised by an exit; compliance teams noticing individual signals nobody aggregates; and programmes whose continuity depends on a trend nobody plotted.

## Impact If Fixed
Relationship health was treated as judgement so it was never instrumented. Turnaround times, findings per review and approval latency are already recorded and their trends are the earliest warning available.
