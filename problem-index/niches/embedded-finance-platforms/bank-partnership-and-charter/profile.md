# Bank Partnership & Charter Access

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/embedded-finance-platforms|Embedded Finance Platforms]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make the sponsor bank relationship a managed, diversified, transferable asset rather than a single dependency negotiated once — and whoever can move a programme between banks without rebuilding it removes the category's largest existential risk.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$900M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~11% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — relationship managed by email and meetings
**Target Buyer:** Platform executive and partnership leadership
**Automation Potential:** Moderate — portability modelling and relationship instrumentation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every programme runs on somebody's charter. The sponsor bank sets the constraints, absorbs the regulatory exposure, and can decide it no longer wants the business — and when it does, the platform's programmes have nowhere to go, because each one was built against that bank's specific requirements. The relationship is the platform's most consequential dependency and is managed with less rigour than its vendor contracts.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Contract negotiation, quarterly business reviews, email threads, and a partnership manager who knows the bank's team. The gaps: no instrumentation of the relationship's health; no model of what would make a bank exit; migration treated as a crisis rather than a capability; and bank-specific coupling accumulating invisibly in every programme.
