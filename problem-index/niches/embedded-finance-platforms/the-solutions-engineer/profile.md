# The Solutions Engineer

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/embedded-finance-platforms|Embedded Finance Platforms]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get the regulated-product knowledge a solutions engineer carries in their head into a form a first-time developer team can use without them — and whoever extracts it turns the category's scarcest people into leverage instead of a bottleneck.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — knowledge held in calls and Slack threads
**Target Buyer:** Platform solutions and developer experience leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — knowledge extraction and guided implementation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The solutions engineer sits between a developer team that has never built a regulated financial product and a bank whose requirements are unwritten. They know which design choices will fail review, which compliance decisions have to be made before the schema is set, which limits this particular sponsor will accept, and which of the customer's assumptions about how money moves are wrong. None of it is written down. They are the platform's real product for the first six months of every programme, and they do not scale.

## Current Tools & Gaps
API documentation, reference implementations, shared Slack channels, and scheduled calls. The gaps: documentation covering the API rather than the decisions; bank-specific knowledge undocumented by design; the same questions answered repeatedly; and no way for a developer to find out what they do not know to ask.
