# Programme Configuration

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/embedded-finance-platforms|Embedded Finance Platforms]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn the twenty programme archetypes that keep getting rebuilt by hand into configurable starting points that carry their bank-specific constraints with them — and whoever does it takes launch time from months to days.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.1B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~14% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — bespoke configuration per programme
**Target Buyer:** Platform implementation and product leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — a pattern library over a constraint model

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Launching a new fintech programme means configuring accounts, card products, limits, fee schedules, compliance rules, dispute handling and the particular constraints of whichever bank sponsors it — by hand, each time. The same twenty archetypes recur: the payroll card, the teen account, the business expense product, the marketplace payout, the savings wrapper. Each is rebuilt from primitives as though it had never been built before, and the bank-specific constraints that shape it are carried in a solutions engineer's head.

## Current Tools & Gaps
API primitives, configuration consoles, implementation runbooks, and reference applications. The gaps: no archetype library; bank constraints not modelled, only known; configuration validity discovered at review rather than at entry; and no way to tell whether a proposed programme is one the sponsor bank will approve.
