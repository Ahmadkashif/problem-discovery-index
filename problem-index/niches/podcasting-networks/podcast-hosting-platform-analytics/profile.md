# Podcast Hosting Platform Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/podcasting-networks|Podcasting Networks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The analytics functions inside the hosting and distribution platforms, producing the download, retention and audience reports every network runs its business on.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 data engineers, analysts, and product researchers
**Output:** Download and consumption analytics, episode retention curves, audience demographic estimates, network dashboards
**Proprietary data:** Server-side delivery logs and, on the platforms that own a player, second-by-second listening behaviour
**Clock:** Continuous delivery with weekly episode cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Head of Analytics
