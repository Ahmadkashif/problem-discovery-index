# Audio Rights & Music Licensing Administration

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/podcasting-networks|Podcasting Networks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Clear and administer the rights podcasts need — music synchronisation, archival audio, interview and contributor releases — and distribute the resulting royalties, resolving who owns what across catalogues where ownership is fragmented and poorly recorded.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 150-800 rights researchers, licensing administrators, and royalty analysts
**Output:** Rights clearances and licences, ownership determinations, royalty calculations and distributions, catalogue research
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated ownership and clearance determinations across catalogues whose chains of title exist in no authoritative registry
**Clock:** Production and release schedules, plus royalty distribution periods
**Buyer:** VP of Rights & Licensing
