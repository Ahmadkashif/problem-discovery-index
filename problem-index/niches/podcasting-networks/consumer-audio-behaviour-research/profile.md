# Consumer Audio Behaviour Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/podcasting-networks|Podcasting Networks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Run the recurring national surveys that establish how many people listen to podcasts, on what devices, in what contexts, and how that is changing — the figures the entire industry cites when it describes itself.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 researchers, survey methodologists, and analysts
**Output:** Annual and quarterly consumer behaviour studies, custom research, category forecasts, conference presentations
**Proprietary data:** Consistently fielded survey series going back to the beginning of the medium
**Clock:** Annual and quarterly fielding calendar
**Buyer:** President / Research Director
