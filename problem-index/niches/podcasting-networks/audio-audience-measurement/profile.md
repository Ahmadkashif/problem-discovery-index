# Audio Audience Measurement & Ratings

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/podcasting-networks|Podcasting Networks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Produce the audience numbers the audio advertising market trades on — broadcast radio ratings from metered panels and diaries, podcast download and listener measurement certified against industry technical standards, and the cross-platform audio estimates buyers use to compare the two. Advertising rates in a multi-billion dollar market are set against these figures, which makes them a currency rather than a report. Pass 1 notes that ad sales reps match sponsors to shows using spreadsheet-level demographic data; this is the layer that produces the demographics.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 methodologists, statisticians, panel operations staff, data scientists, and measurement analysts
**Output:** Market ratings and audience estimates, podcast download and unique listener metrics, demographic composition, reach and frequency planning data, cross-platform audio measurement
**Proprietary data:** Metered and diary panels maintained over decades, plus server-side download logs from a large share of podcast hosting — neither reproducible without the panel infrastructure or the hosting relationships
**Clock:** Fixed survey and ratings periods that the entire advertising market schedules around, plus monthly podcast reporting
**Buyer:** Chief Research Officer / SVP of Measurement Science
