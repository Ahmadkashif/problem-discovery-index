# Programmatic Audio Ad Marketplaces

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/podcasting-networks|Podcasting Networks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Run the dynamic ad insertion and programmatic exchanges through which podcast inventory is priced and sold, with the yield management, targeting and forecasting analytics that a real-time auction requires.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 data scientists, yield analysts, and ad technology engineers
**Output:** Yield optimisation, inventory forecasting, audience targeting segments, pricing and floor management, delivery reporting
**Proprietary data:** Impression-level bidding and delivery data across a large share of podcast inventory
**Clock:** Real-time auction and monthly campaign reconciliation
**Buyer:** VP of Revenue Analytics
