# Podcast Network Corporate Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/podcasting-networks|Podcasting Networks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer

## What They Do
Corporate analytics inside the large podcast networks and audio groups — show portfolio performance, talent deal economics, cross-promotion planning, and acquisition screening.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 analysts and data scientists
**Output:** Show performance and profitability reporting, talent deal modelling, cross-promotion planning, acquisition and greenlight analysis
**Proprietary data:** Audience, revenue and cost data across a portfolio of shows, plus the outcomes of past greenlight decisions
**Clock:** Quarterly reporting and slate planning cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Business Analytics
