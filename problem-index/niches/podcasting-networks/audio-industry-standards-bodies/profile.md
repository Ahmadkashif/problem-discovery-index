# Audio Measurement Standards Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/podcasting-networks|Podcasting Networks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Write and audit compliance with the technical measurement guidelines that define what counts as a podcast download, and accredit the measurement providers who claim to follow them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 technical standards staff and auditors
**Output:** Measurement technical guidelines, compliance certification, audit findings, taxonomy standards
**Proprietary data:** Compliance audit findings across measurement providers
**Clock:** Guideline revision cycles and annual certification
**Buyer:** VP of Technology & Standards
