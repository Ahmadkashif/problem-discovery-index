# Agency Audio Planning & Buying Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/podcasting-networks|Podcasting Networks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The audio investment and analytics teams inside the media agencies, who plan and buy podcast and radio schedules for advertisers and evaluate what the spend returned.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 planners, investment analysts, and measurement staff across the agency groups
**Output:** Reach and frequency plans, mix modelling contributions, post-campaign analysis, negotiated rate benchmarks
**Proprietary data:** Rates paid across many advertisers and shows — the only comparative view of a market with no rate card
**Clock:** Campaign planning cycles and upfront negotiation periods
**Buyer:** EVP of Investment Analytics
