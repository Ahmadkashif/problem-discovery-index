# Podcast Content Intelligence & Brand Safety

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/podcasting-networks|Podcasting Networks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Transcribe and classify podcast audio at scale to produce contextual targeting segments, brand safety and suitability ratings, and content search for advertisers who need to know what a show actually says before placing an ad in it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 machine learning engineers, taxonomists, and content analysts
**Output:** Transcripts, contextual and suitability classification, brand safety scoring, content search and discovery
**Proprietary data:** A large transcribed corpus with an advertiser-oriented taxonomy applied over it
**Clock:** Continuous ingestion against campaign timelines
**Buyer:** VP of Product / Chief Technology Officer
