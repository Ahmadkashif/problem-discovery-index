# Audio Ad Verification & Attribution

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/podcasting-networks|Podcasting Networks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Confirm that podcast advertisements actually ran, transcribe and verify the read, and attribute downstream conversions back to the episode and show that produced them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 data scientists, measurement analysts, and audio processing engineers
**Output:** Ad occurrence verification, brand lift studies, pixel and survey-based attribution, incrementality analysis
**Proprietary data:** Verified ad occurrence records joined to conversion data across many advertisers
**Clock:** Campaign flight schedules and monthly billing reconciliation
**Buyer:** VP of Measurement
