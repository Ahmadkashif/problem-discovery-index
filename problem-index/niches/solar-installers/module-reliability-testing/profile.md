# Module & Inverter Reliability Testing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/solar-installers|Solar Installers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Run extended reliability testing on solar modules and inverters beyond certification minimums, and publish the scorecards buyers and lenders use to decide which manufacturers are bankable.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 test engineers, materials scientists, and analysts
**Output:** Extended reliability test results, manufacturer scorecards, bill of materials analysis, factory audits, degradation projections
**Proprietary data:** Multi-year accelerated test results by manufacturer and bill of materials, across product generations
**Clock:** Product qualification cycles and annual scorecard publication
**Buyer:** Chief Technology Officer / VP of Testing
