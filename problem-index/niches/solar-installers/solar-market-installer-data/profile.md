# Solar Market & Installer Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/solar-installers|Solar Installers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Track installations, installer market share, pricing and pipeline by geography from permit records, interconnection filings and incentive registries, and sell it to manufacturers, financiers and installers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 data operations staff, analysts, and economists
**Output:** Installation volume and share reporting, system pricing benchmarks, installer league tables, market forecasts, permit and pipeline data
**Proprietary data:** Permit and interconnection records assembled from thousands of jurisdictions that publish inconsistently
**Clock:** Quarterly market reporting
**Buyer:** Head of Research / VP of Data
