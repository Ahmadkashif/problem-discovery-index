# Efficiency & DER Programme Evaluation Contractors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/solar-installers|Solar Installers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Measure and verify whether utility programmes — including distributed solar and storage incentives — actually delivered the savings and generation claimed, and file the evaluation reports regulators use to decide whether the programme continues.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 evaluators, statisticians, engineers, and field staff
**Output:** Impact and process evaluation reports, net-to-gross and free-ridership estimates, measurement and verification protocols, regulatory filings
**Proprietary data:** Multi-year programme evaluation results across jurisdictions, with metered outcomes attached
**Clock:** Regulatory filing deadlines and programme cycle dates
**Buyer:** VP of Evaluation Services
