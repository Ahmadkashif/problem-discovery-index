# Solar Asset Management & Performance Monitoring

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/solar-installers|Solar Installers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Monitor operating solar plants against expected production, detect underperformance, diagnose its cause, and manage the operations and maintenance response across large fleets.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 performance engineers, data scientists, and operations analysts
**Output:** Performance ratio and availability reporting, underperformance detection and diagnosis, soiling and degradation analysis, warranty claim substantiation, O&M dispatch
**Proprietary data:** Interval generation data across large fleets with equipment, design and site attributes attached, plus the maintenance action that resolved each event
**Clock:** Monthly performance reporting and warranty claim windows
**Buyer:** VP of Asset Performance / Head of Analytics
