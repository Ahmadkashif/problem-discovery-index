# Solar Design & Sales Software Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/solar-installers|Solar Installers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Supply the remote design and proposal tools installers size systems and quote customers with — shading analysis, layout, production simulation and savings projection — the software that produces the number a homeowner signs against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 engineers, data scientists, and simulation specialists
**Output:** Remote shade and irradiance analysis, system layouts, production simulations, savings and payback proposals, permit packages
**Proprietary data:** Millions of designed systems with simulated production, sold price and — where integrated with monitoring — realised output
**Clock:** Sales cycle turnaround
**Buyer:** VP of Engineering / Head of Data Science
