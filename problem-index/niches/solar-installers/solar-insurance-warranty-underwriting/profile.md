# Solar Insurance & Warranty Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/solar-installers|Solar Installers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Underwrite property and performance coverage for solar assets and back the long-duration manufacturer warranties and production guarantees the market depends on.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 underwriters, actuaries, and engineering risk staff
**Output:** Property and performance policy pricing, warranty backing, natural catastrophe exposure analysis for solar assets, claims analysis
**Proprietary data:** Loss experience by technology, mounting type, geography and installer
**Clock:** Annual renewals and catastrophe seasons
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
