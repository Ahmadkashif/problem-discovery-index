# Residential Solar Finance Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/solar-installers|Solar Installers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Underwrite the loans, leases and power purchase agreements that finance almost all residential solar, and manage the resulting portfolios — including the production guarantees that pay a homeowner when the system underperforms.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 credit analysts, actuaries, asset managers, and data scientists
**Output:** Credit decisioning, production guarantee pricing, portfolio performance and default forecasting, securitisation analytics, system performance monitoring
**Proprietary data:** Hundreds of thousands of systems with installer, equipment, design and realised production joined to credit outcomes and guarantee payments
**Clock:** Origination decisioning, monthly billing, and securitisation reporting
**Buyer:** Chief Credit Officer / VP of Asset Management
