# Residential Solar Lead Generation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/solar-installers|Solar Installers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator/rollup

## What They Do
Buy media and generate homeowner solar enquiries at scale, qualify them, and sell them to installers — the customer acquisition layer whose cost is one of the largest line items in a residential system price.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 media analysts, data scientists, and qualification staff
**Output:** Lead scoring and pricing, campaign attribution, qualification models, territory demand forecasting
**Proprietary data:** Enquiry-to-install conversion across many campaigns and territories
**Clock:** Continuous media buying against incentive deadlines
**Buyer:** VP of Analytics
