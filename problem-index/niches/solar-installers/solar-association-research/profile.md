# Solar Industry Association Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/solar-installers|Solar Installers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Publish the industry's installation and pricing statistics, policy analysis and workforce studies, largely to support advocacy on tax credits, tariffs and net metering.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 researchers and policy analysts
**Output:** Market reports, policy impact analyses, workforce and jobs studies, tariff and trade analysis
**Proprietary data:** Member surveys and licensed market data
**Clock:** Quarterly reporting and legislative sessions
**Buyer:** VP of Research
