# Solar Resource Assessment & Independent Engineering

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/solar-installers|Solar Installers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Produce the energy yield assessment every financed solar project rests on: how much electricity this array, at this site, with this equipment and this design, will generate over twenty-five years — expressed as a central estimate and a set of exceedance probabilities that lenders size debt against and investors price equity against. No project of any size closes without an independent engineer's report and a bankable resource dataset behind it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 solar resource scientists, meteorologists, energy analysts, and independent engineers across the resource data vendors and the independent engineering practices
**Output:** Satellite-derived irradiance time series, energy yield assessments with P50 and P90 exceedance estimates, uncertainty analyses, technical due diligence reports, degradation and performance ratio assumptions
**Proprietary data:** Decades of satellite-derived irradiance reprocessed and bias-corrected against ground stations, plus accumulated assessments joined — for the firms that stay engaged — to what the plants then actually produced
**Clock:** Financing closings and construction milestones; the report is a condition precedent and the date is fixed by the deal
**Buyer:** Chief Scientist / Head of Technical Advisory
