# State Utility Commissions & Interconnection Regulators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/solar-installers|Solar Installers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Set net metering and successor tariffs, approve interconnection rules, review utility rate cases, and adjudicate the disputes that determine whether residential solar is economic in a given state.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 across the larger commissions — analysts, economists, and engineers
**Output:** Tariff and rate decisions, interconnection rule orders, cost-benefit analyses of distributed generation, programme evaluations
**Proprietary data:** Utility filings and testimony records, plus commissioned analyses of distributed generation value
**Clock:** Statutory rate case and rulemaking schedules
**Buyer:** Commission staff director
