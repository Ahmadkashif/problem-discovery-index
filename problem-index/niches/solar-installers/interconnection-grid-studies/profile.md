# Interconnection & Grid Impact Studies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/solar-installers|Solar Installers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Model whether a proposed solar array can connect to the grid where it wants to, what upgrades that would require and what they would cost — the studies that decide whether a project proceeds and that projects wait years in a queue for.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 power systems engineers, modellers, and analysts across the consultancies and utility study groups
**Output:** Load flow and hosting capacity studies, interconnection cost estimates, protection and power quality analysis, queue position advisory
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated study results and upgrade cost outcomes across feeders and territories, plus knowledge of how each utility actually applies its criteria
**Clock:** Interconnection queue deadlines, deposit and withdrawal windows, and study cycle dates
**Buyer:** VP of Grid Advisory / Managing Engineer
