# Renewable Certificate & Incentive Administration

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/solar-installers|Solar Installers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Register systems, meter and certify generation, issue and track renewable energy certificates, and administer the state incentive programmes that determine a large part of a residential system's economics.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 registry operators, verification analysts, and programme administrators
**Output:** Certificate issuance and tracking, generation verification, incentive payment administration, compliance reporting for obligated entities
**Proprietary data:** Verified generation and certificate transaction records across state markets
**Clock:** Compliance year deadlines, true-up dates, and certificate retirement schedules
**Buyer:** VP of Registry Operations
