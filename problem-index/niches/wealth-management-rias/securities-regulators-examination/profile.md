# Securities Regulators & Examination Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wealth-management-rias|Wealth Management RIAs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Register and examine investment advisers, run the risk-based selection that decides which firms get examined, and bring enforcement where conduct falls short.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-3,000 examiners, economists, and risk analysts across federal and state programmes
**Output:** Examination selection and risk scoring, examination findings and deficiency letters, enforcement actions, risk alerts and observations, market structure research
**Proprietary data:** Registration filings joined to examination findings and enforcement outcomes for every registered adviser in the country
**Clock:** Examination cycles and statutory filing deadlines
**Buyer:** Programme office
