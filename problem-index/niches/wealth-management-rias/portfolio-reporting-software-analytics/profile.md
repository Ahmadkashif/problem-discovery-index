## What They Do
Supply the performance reporting, billing, rebalancing and client portal systems advisers run on, and the analytics layered over the portfolios flowing through them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 data engineers, quantitative analysts, and product researchers

**Proprietary data:** Position-level portfolios, trades and fee arrangements across thousands of advisory firms
**Clock:** Quarter-end reporting and billing cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Chief Product Officer
