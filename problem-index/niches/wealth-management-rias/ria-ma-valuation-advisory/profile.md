# RIA M&A & Practice Valuation Advisory

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wealth-management-rias|Wealth Management RIAs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Value advisory practices and run the sale processes in one of the most active consolidation markets in professional services, where the asset being bought is a book of client relationships whose retention through a transition is the whole question.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 bankers, valuation analysts, and integration consultants
**Output:** Practice valuations, transaction advisory and process management, deal structuring, client retention modelling, post-close integration planning
**Proprietary data:** Transaction terms and post-close client retention across many deals, in a market where nothing is publicly disclosed
**Clock:** Transaction timelines and earn-out measurement periods
**Buyer:** Managing Director
