# RIA Compliance Consulting & Examination Support

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wealth-management-rias|Wealth Management RIAs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Keep registered advisers compliant — annual disclosure filings, compliance programme design and testing, marketing rule review, custody and fee billing verification — and represent them through regulatory examinations.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 compliance consultants, former examiners, and testing analysts
**Output:** Annual filing preparation, compliance programme design and annual review, mock examinations, examination response and remediation, marketing and advertising review, fee billing audits
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated examination findings across hundreds of firms — what examiners in each region actually cite, which the regulator publishes only in aggregate
**Clock:** Annual disclosure filing deadlines, mandatory annual compliance reviews, and examination response windows measured in days
**Buyer:** Managing Director / Practice Leader
