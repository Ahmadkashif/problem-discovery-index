# Regulatory Filing & Adviser Data Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wealth-management-rias|Wealth Management RIAs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Assemble adviser registration filings, institutional holdings reports and disciplinary records into structured datasets, and sell the resulting view of who manages what, for whom, at what fee, to recruiters, asset managers and acquirers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 data operations staff, analysts, and engineers
**Output:** Adviser and firm profiles, assets and client type analytics, fee schedule extraction, holdings and flow analysis, disciplinary history, market maps
**Proprietary data:** Normalised filing data with entity resolution across firms, advisers and affiliations over time; the underlying filings are public
**Clock:** Annual and quarterly filing deadlines
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Head of Research
