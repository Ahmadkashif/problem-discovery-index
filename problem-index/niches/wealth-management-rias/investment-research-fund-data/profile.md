# Investment Research & Fund Data Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wealth-management-rias|Wealth Management RIAs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Collect, classify and rate every fund, share class and managed strategy an adviser can buy, and publish the categories, benchmarks, style boxes, risk statistics and analyst ratings that determine what gets recommended to clients. An RIA's investment committee, due diligence file, client reporting and regulatory documentation are all built on this layer's classifications, and the categories it defines are effectively the map of the investable universe.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1,000-5,000 analysts, quantitative researchers, data operations staff, methodologists, and content specialists
**Output:** Fund and strategy classification, category benchmarks and peer groups, performance and risk statistics, holdings-based analytics, analyst ratings and qualitative research, portfolio analysis tools, index construction
**Proprietary data:** Decades of holdings-level fund data with survivorship-corrected performance history, plus the classification taxonomy and analyst rating record — none of which exists in any public filing in comparable form
**Clock:** Daily net asset values, monthly and quarterly reporting cycles, and the holdings disclosure calendar the whole analytical apparatus is built around
**Buyer:** Chief Research Officer / Head of Data & Methodology
