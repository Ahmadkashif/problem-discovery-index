# Advisory Practice Benchmarking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wealth-management-rias|Wealth Management RIAs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Survey advisory firms on revenue, margin, compensation, growth and staffing, and publish the benchmarks practices set pay and pricing against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 researchers, consultants, and analysts
**Output:** Practice financial and productivity benchmarks, compensation studies, growth and client acquisition analysis, valuation multiples
**Proprietary data:** Voluntary survey submissions from advisory firms over many years
**Clock:** Annual survey and compensation planning cycles
**Buyer:** Head of Research
