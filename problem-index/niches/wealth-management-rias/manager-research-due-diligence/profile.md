# Third-Party Manager Research & Due Diligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wealth-management-rias|Wealth Management RIAs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Perform the operational and investment due diligence that decides which managers reach an adviser's approved list — meeting teams, examining process, verifying operations, and writing the recommendation an investment committee votes on.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 manager research analysts, operational due diligence specialists, and portfolio strategists
**Output:** Manager recommendations and approved lists, operational due diligence reports, model portfolios, watch and termination decisions, capital market assumptions
**Proprietary data:** Meeting notes, process assessments and conviction ratings across hundreds of managers over many years, joined to what those managers subsequently did
**Clock:** Investment committee calendars and quarterly review cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Investment Officer / Director of Manager Research
