# Insurance & Annuity Product Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wealth-management-rias|Wealth Management RIAs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Analyse and compare annuity and life insurance contracts for advisers — living benefit riders, crediting formulas, fee structures and guarantees — in a category where product complexity is the main obstacle to fiduciary recommendation.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 actuaries, product analysts, and illustration specialists
**Output:** Product comparison and screening, rider and guarantee analysis, illustration testing, suitability and fiduciary documentation, income projection modelling
**Proprietary data:** Structured contract feature data across thousands of products and share classes, with historical crediting and pricing changes
**Clock:** Product filing and rate change cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Product Research / Chief Actuary
