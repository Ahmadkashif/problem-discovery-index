# Financial Planning Standards & Certification Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wealth-management-rias|Wealth Management RIAs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Set the competency and ethics standards for financial planning credentials, administer the certification examination, and enforce conduct standards on certificants.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-250 psychometricians, standards staff, examination developers, and enforcement personnel
**Output:** Practice standards and ethics rules, certification examinations and item banks, job task analyses, continuing education requirements, disciplinary determinations
**Proprietary data:** Item response data across the certificant population, plus disciplinary histories
**Clock:** Examination administration windows and renewal cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Assessment Officer / Director of Standards
