# Financial Planning Software & Capital Market Assumptions

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wealth-management-rias|Wealth Management RIAs]]

## What They Do
Supply the planning engines advisers project client retirements with, and the capital market assumptions — expected returns, volatilities and correlations by asset class — that drive every Monte Carlo probability of success a client is ever shown.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 financial planners, quantitative researchers, economists, and product staff
**Output:** Planning engines and Monte Carlo simulation, capital market assumptions, tax and benefit rule content, goal probability reporting, income sustainability analysis
**Proprietary data:** Assumption sets and their revision history, plus millions of plans with the assumptions and goals they were built on
**Clock:** Annual assumption revisions and tax and benefit rule updates
**Buyer:** Chief Research Officer / Head of Planning Methodology
