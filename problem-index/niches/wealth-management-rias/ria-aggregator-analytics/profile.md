# RIA Aggregator Corporate Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wealth-management-rias|Wealth Management RIAs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator/rollup

## What They Do
Corporate analytics inside the consolidators buying advisory practices — organic growth measurement, adviser productivity, client retention, pricing harmonisation, and acquisition screening across dozens or hundreds of acquired firms.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 analysts, corporate development staff, and integration teams
**Output:** Practice performance reporting, organic growth and retention analytics, adviser productivity measurement, pricing and service model analysis, acquisition modelling
**Proprietary data:** Client, asset and fee records across many acquired practices, plus a growing record of which acquisitions retained clients and which did not
**Clock:** Quarterly reporting, earn-out measurement, and acquisition pipelines
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Corporate Development
