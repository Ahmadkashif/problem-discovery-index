# Custodian Platform Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wealth-management-rias|Wealth Management RIAs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Hold the assets, clear the trades and run the technology platform advisers operate on, and analyse the resulting flows — where assets move, which practices grow, what advisers actually buy.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-3,000 data scientists, business analysts, economists, and platform researchers
**Output:** Practice benchmarking and growth analytics, asset flow and product usage reporting, adviser transition analytics, published industry studies, platform pricing
**Proprietary data:** Custodied positions, trades and flows across a very large share of independent advisory assets, joined to practice characteristics
**Clock:** Daily settlement and quarterly reporting cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Data & Analytics / Chief Economist
