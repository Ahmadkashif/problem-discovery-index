# Forensic Engineering & Cause of Loss Investigation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-adjusters|Public Adjusters]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Determine why a building failed and whether the cause is covered — wind versus wear, storm versus long-term water intrusion, construction defect versus event damage. The report decides coverage, so both sides retain one.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-1,000 engineers, materials scientists, and investigators
**Output:** Cause and origin reports, damage causation opinions, repair scope opinions, expert testimony
**Proprietary data:** Decades of failure investigations across building types, materials and events
**Clock:** Claim adjudication timelines and litigation deadlines
**Buyer:** Managing Principal / VP of Forensic Services
