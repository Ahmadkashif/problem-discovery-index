# Property Claims Data & Fraud Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-adjusters|Public Adjusters]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Operate the industry claims databases carriers contribute to and query — prior loss history at property and claimant level — and sell the fraud scoring and investigation analytics built over them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-1,500 data scientists, investigators, analysts, and data operations staff
**Output:** Prior claims history reports, fraud and build-up scoring, claimant and provider network analysis, severity benchmarking
**Proprietary data:** A near-industry-wide contributed claims database that no single carrier could reproduce
**Clock:** Continuous claim flow with catastrophe surges
**Buyer:** Chief Analytics Officer / VP of Claims Solutions
