# Insurance Appraisal & Umpire Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-adjusters|Public Adjusters]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Resolve disputed claim amounts through the policy's appraisal clause — each side names an appraiser, the two select an umpire, and the resulting award binds. A parallel private adjudication system for property claim value.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 across the firms that do this at scale
**Output:** Appraisal positions, umpire decisions and awards, scope and pricing analyses
**Proprietary data:** Award histories against the positions both sides took — a record of where disputed claims actually land
**Clock:** Appraisal panel timelines set by policy and statute
**Buyer:** Managing Principal
