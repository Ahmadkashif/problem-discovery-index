# Contents Inventory & Valuation Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-adjusters|Public Adjusters]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Inventory and value the personal property destroyed in a loss — item by item, with replacement cost and depreciation applied — for the contents portion of a claim that policyholders are least equipped to document.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 inventory specialists, pricing researchers, and valuation analysts
**Output:** Itemised contents inventories, replacement cost pricing, depreciation schedules, contents claim packages
**Proprietary data:** Item pricing and depreciation histories across very large volumes of settled contents claims
**Clock:** Claim documentation deadlines and proof of loss requirements
**Buyer:** VP of Contents Services
