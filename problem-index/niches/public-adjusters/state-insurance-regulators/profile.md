# State Insurance Departments & Market Conduct

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-adjusters|Public Adjusters]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
License public adjusters, review rate and form filings, handle consumer complaints, and conduct market conduct examinations of how carriers actually handled claims after an event.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 across the larger departments — analysts, examiners, and actuaries
**Output:** Licensing, rate and form approvals, complaint data, market conduct examination findings, post-catastrophe claim handling reports
**Proprietary data:** Complaint and examination records, plus post-event claim outcome data collected from carriers
**Clock:** Filing review deadlines and post-catastrophe reporting requirements
**Buyer:** Commissioner's office
