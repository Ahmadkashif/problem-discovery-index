# Claims Practice & Bad Faith Litigation Support

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-adjusters|Public Adjusters]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Analyse carrier claim handling for compliance with policy terms and statutory claim practice standards, and testify about whether a claim was adjusted in good faith.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 consultants and former claims executives across the specialist firms
**Output:** Claim file analyses, claim practice opinions, expert testimony, damages analyses
**Proprietary data:** Reviewed claim files and case outcomes, per matter
**Clock:** Litigation deadlines
**Buyer:** Managing Principal
