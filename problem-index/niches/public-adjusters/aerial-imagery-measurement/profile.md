# Aerial Imagery & Property Measurement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-adjusters|Public Adjusters]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Fly and process imagery of the built environment and derive measured property reports from it — roof geometry, pitch, area, facets and condition — used to scope and price claims without sending anyone onto a roof.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 photogrammetrists, computer vision engineers, imagery analysts, and quality reviewers
**Output:** Measurement reports, roof condition and damage indicators, change detection between captures, property attribute data
**Proprietary data:** A multi-year national imagery library with historical captures of the same properties, which is what makes before-and-after comparison possible
**Clock:** Post-catastrophe capture windows and claim cycle times
**Buyer:** VP of Data Products / Chief Technology Officer
