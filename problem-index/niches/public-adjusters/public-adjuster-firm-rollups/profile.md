# Public Adjusting Firm Rollups

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-adjusters|Public Adjusters]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator/rollup

## What They Do
The small number of multi-state public adjusting firms with a corporate analytics function, tracking claim pipeline, settlement multiples and adjuster productivity across catastrophe deployments.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 analysts and operations staff at the largest firms
**Output:** Pipeline and settlement reporting, adjuster productivity analysis, catastrophe deployment planning, fee forecasting
**Proprietary data:** Claim files with initial carrier offer, documented scope, and final settlement — the closest thing to a record of what documentation is actually worth
**Clock:** Catastrophe surges and contingency cash flow cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Operating Officer
