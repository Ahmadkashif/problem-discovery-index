# Catastrophe Modelling Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-adjusters|Public Adjusters]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Build and sell the models that estimate how much a hurricane, wildfire, earthquake or severe convective storm will cost the insurance industry. Every property insurer's capital position, reinsurance purchase, rate filing and underwriting appetite is set against these models, and so is the price of catastrophe bonds. Pass 1 describes public adjusting as highly cyclical, surging after catastrophic events; this is the layer that priced those events before they happened and re-prices them afterwards.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-2,000 atmospheric scientists, seismologists, hydrologists, structural engineers, statisticians, and software engineers
**Output:** Peril models by region, event sets and exceedance probability curves, real-time event footprints and loss estimates, exposure databases, model change documentation for regulators and rating agencies
**Proprietary data:** Decades of claims calibration data contributed by insurers, engineering vulnerability research, and exposure databases describing the built environment — none of which exists in any public source at the required resolution
**Clock:** Reinsurance renewal dates, hurricane and wildfire seasons, rate filing calendars, and live events where a loss estimate is expected within days
**Buyer:** Chief Research Officer / Chief Science Officer
