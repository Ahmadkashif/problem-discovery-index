# Weather Verification & Peril Event Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-adjusters|Public Adjusters]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Produce the certified reports that state whether hail of a given size, or wind of a given speed, occurred at a specific address on a specific date — the evidence both public adjusters and carriers use to argue whether a storm caused the damage.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 meteorologists, data scientists, and report analysts
**Output:** Address-level hail and wind verification reports, historical peril archives, forensic meteorology opinions, catastrophe event footprints
**Proprietary data:** Radar-derived hail and wind estimates reprocessed into a continuous historical archive at address resolution, calibrated against ground reports
**Clock:** Claim filing deadlines and litigation timelines
**Buyer:** Chief Meteorologist / VP of Data Products
