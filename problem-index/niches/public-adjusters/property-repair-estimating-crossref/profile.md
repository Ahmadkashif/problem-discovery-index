# Property Repair Estimating Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/public-adjusters|Public Adjusters]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Publish the line-item unit costs every property insurance claim in the country is estimated and settled against, and own the software both sides write those estimates in. Pass 1 notes the industry runs on this platform — a public adjuster must write in it to negotiate with a carrier adjuster at all.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 400-1,500 cost researchers, pricing analysts, data operations staff, and content specialists
**Output:** Line-item unit cost databases by trade and region, pricing updates, estimating platform content, industry pricing analytics
**Proprietary data:** Market-survey pricing maintained across every US market and trade, plus the estimate corpus flowing through the platform
**Clock:** Monthly pricing updates against live claim settlements
**Buyer:** VP of Property Estimating Solutions / Chief Data Officer
