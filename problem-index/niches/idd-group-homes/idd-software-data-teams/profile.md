# IDD Agency Software Data Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/idd-group-homes|IDD Group Homes]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The data, analytics and AI groups at vendors of IDD agency record systems. Therap says it supports 15,000+ agencies in all 50 states and runs EVV and state systems for several states. MediSked (majority-owned by CaseWorthy, search summary), SETWorks, iCareManager, Kibu and Netsmart's human-services line are the others. They build in-product reports, state reporting feeds and AI documentation features from the notes, incident reports, MARs and claims their customers keep in their systems. Agencies and some states pay for the software; analysis is a feature.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10–50 data, analytics and AI staff per vendor (estimate); Therap's engineering and machine-learning departments sit in Dhaka (Therap BD site)
**Output:** In-product dashboards, state reporting extracts, AI note drafting
**Proprietary data:** Notes, general event (incident) reports, MARs, ISPs and claims for thousands of agencies, held under customer service agreements and business associate agreements, and as state data where the vendor runs the state's system
**Clock:** Self-set product releases; customers' state reporting deadlines
**Buyer:** Chief product or technology officer at the vendor
