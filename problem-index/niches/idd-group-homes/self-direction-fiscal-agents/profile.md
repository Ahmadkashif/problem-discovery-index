# Self-Direction Fiscal Management Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/idd-group-homes|IDD Group Homes]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Fiscal intermediaries that process payroll, taxes and timesheets for people with IDD who hire their own support workers under self-directed waiver options, the alternative many families choose instead of a group home. Public Partnerships, Acumen, GT Independence and Palco are representative (named from recall). States pay per participant per month.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Large payroll, enrolment and customer-service operations; analytics serve operations and state reporting and are small (estimate)
**Output:** Payroll, budget-utilisation statements, state reports
**Proprietary data:** Timesheets, wages and budgets for self-directing participants, held for the state
**Clock:** Payroll cycles and state contract reporting
**Buyer:** State Medicaid or DD agency
