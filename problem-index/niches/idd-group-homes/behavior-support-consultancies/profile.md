# Behaviour Support & Clinical Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/idd-group-homes|IDD Group Homes]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Practices of board-certified behaviour analysts, psychologists and nurses that agencies contract to write and review behaviour support plans, train DSPs, sit on human rights committees and consult on residents with complex medical needs (named from recall). Paid per hour or per plan, often billed to the waiver as a separate service.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Clinicians carrying caseloads, who are production headcount; few practices exceed a handful of non-clinical analysts (estimate)
**Output:** Behaviour support plans, plan reviews, staff training
**Proprietary data:** Behaviour data and clinical records of the people they serve
**Clock:** Plan review dates and human rights committee schedules set by agencies and states
**Buyer:** Agency clinical director
