# State-Contracted IDD Quality Review & Assessment Contractors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/idd-group-homes|IDD Group Homes]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Firms that state DD agencies contract to review providers, assess people's support needs and investigate deaths and incidents. Their work includes provider quality reviews against waiver standards, standardised support-needs assessments that set rate tiers, mortality reviews and, in some states, independent incident investigations. Qlarant, Liberty Healthcare, the Columbus Organization, Acentra Health and Maximus are representative (named from recall). The state pays per review, per assessment or per contract year, and receives findings and trend reports it uses for its CMS waiver assurances.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10–40 analysts and quality-data staff per contract (estimate); the reviewers, assessors and investigators who do the casework are production headcount and are not counted
**Output:** Provider review findings, assessment results, mortality and incident trend reports for the state
**Proprietary data:** Review, assessment and investigation records across a state's whole IDD population, held for the state
**Clock:** State contract reporting dates and the state's own federal reporting, including the Access Rule's incident and planning measures from July 2027 (client clocks)
**Buyer:** State DD agency quality director; contract lead at the firm

**Kill switches:** **HIPAA** and **data isn't theirs.** Every review, assessment and investigation reads identified records of people served, under state contracts that own the results.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. Scored below Medicare's review contractors, [[niches/home-health-agencies/medicare-claims-review-contractors/profile|🔍 Medicare Claims Review Contractors]] (49, kill switch), and the nursing-home case-mix auditors, [[niches/skilled-nursing-facilities/medicaid-case-mix-auditors/profile|🔍 State Medicaid Case-Mix & MDS Audit Contractors]] (44, kill switch). Only the analysts count toward the insight function, the clocks are the state's, and each contract serves one state. Several of the same firms, Qlarant and Acentra among them, also do the mandatory annual reviews of Medicaid managed care plans, logged as [[niches/health-insurance-carriers/medicaid-eqros/profile|🔍 Medicaid External Quality Review Organizations]] (46, kill switch). That work scores higher because its annual report is a federal deliverable with a statutory date (Q4 5), while the IDD provider reviews here run to state contract dates.
