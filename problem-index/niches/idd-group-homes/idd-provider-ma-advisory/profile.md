# IDD Provider M&A Advisory & Valuation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/idd-group-homes|IDD Group Homes]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Healthcare investment banks and valuation firms that advise on sales of IDD providers to rollups and private equity, the deal flow behind Sevita's purchase of ResCare Community Living. They value agencies on revenue, EBITDA, rate outlook and staffing stability, and run sale processes (named from recall). Sellers pay a success fee.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 bankers and analysts per firm on IDD and behavioural deals (estimate)
**Output:** Valuations, offering memoranda, sale processes
**Proprietary data:** Deal comparables and sellers' financials under engagement
**Clock:** Deal timelines set by clients
**Buyer:** Agency owners and private equity sponsors
