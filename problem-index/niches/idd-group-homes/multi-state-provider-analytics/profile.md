# Multi-State IDD Provider Quality & Operations Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/idd-group-homes|IDD Group Homes]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The largest residential providers run corporate quality, finance and operations analytics across hundreds of homes. Sevita serves about 50,000 people in 40 states and closed its $835M purchase of ResCare Community Living on 31 March 2026, a business with ~$1.2B of 2024 revenue (Home Health Care News). Dungarvin took 128 divested ICFs in the same deal (Behavioral Health Business). Mosaic, Benchmark Human Services and Volunteers of America run comparable networks (named from recall). These teams track incidents, overtime, occupancy, billing and survey results by home, underwrite acquisitions and prepare rate submissions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10–40 quality, finance and data staff per large provider (estimate)
**Output:** Home-level performance reviews, acquisition underwriting, incident and quality trending, rate and cost submissions
**Proprietary data:** Clinical, incident, staffing and financial records across every home they operate
**Clock:** State licensing and survey cycles, waiver audits, Access Rule deadlines, deal timelines
**Buyer:** No external buyer — internal cost centre (COO or chief quality officer)
