# Hospital Cost-Report Software Fitted to Waiver Services

**Niche:** [[niches/idd-group-homes/cost-reports-compensation/profile|Cost Reports & Direct Care Compensation Reporting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/idd-group-homes|IDD Group Homes]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Medicare cost-report software for [[industries/general-hospitals|hospitals]] and [[industries/skilled-nursing-facilities|nursing homes]] is mature; residential IDD providers file state templates it does not know.
**Tags:** #feature-engineering #data-integration #compliance #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce the agency's cost reports and direct care compensation figures straight from payroll, ledger and claims data, and whoever files with the fewest state audit adjustments takes the account.

## The Problem
IDD providers have no equivalent of the Medicare cost-report software hospitals use, so each state template is filled by hand or by an outside firm.

## What Already Exists
Medicare cost-report preparation software and services for hospitals, nursing homes and home health (the preparers are logged under home health as [[niches/home-health-agencies/medicare-cost-report-firms/profile|🔍 Medicare Cost Report Preparation]]); payroll-allocation features in ERPs.

## The Customization Gap
The product needs each state's ICF/IID and waiver cost-report templates and allowable-cost rules, mapping of waiver services and units, and the Access Rule's definitions of direct care worker, compensation and excluded costs. It needs separate reporting for services delivered in provider-operated settings, and exports in the formats states and rate consultants request.

## Target Customer
Providers already paying an accounting firm for state cost reports; the firms themselves.

## Impact If Solved
A repeatable, auditable filing process replaces a bespoke spreadsheet every year, and outside preparation fees of ~$10,000–40,000 a year per provider could roughly halve (estimate).
