# Nursing-Home eMARs Rebuilt for Delegated DSP Passes

**Niche:** [[niches/idd-group-homes/medication-administration/profile|Medication Administration by Med-Certified DSPs]]
**Industry:** [[industries/idd-group-homes|IDD Group Homes]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Nursing-home eMARs are mature and barcode-driven; they assume a licensed nurse at the cart and a resident who never leaves the building.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #compliance #worker-facing #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make every medication pass by a med-certified DSP match the current order, and whoever keeps medication errors that reach the critical-incident threshold lowest takes the account.

## The Problem
Agencies that adopt a nursing-home eMAR find it asks DSPs to make nursing judgements, does not handle home visits, and does not know the state's certification scope.

## What Already Exists
[[industries/skilled-nursing-facilities|Skilled nursing]] and [[industries/assisted-living-communities|assisted living]] eMARs with barcode scanning and pharmacy interfaces; IDD record-system MAR modules.

## The Customization Gap
The customisation needs four pieces. One is scope enforcement by the DSP's certification level and the state's delegation rules, such as which routes and which PRNs she may give. Another is plain-language prompts with each person's swallowing and dining precautions. A third is leave-of-absence and day-programme transfers with counts out and back. The fourth is PRN behaviour medications linked to the behaviour plan, with error classification against the state's critical incident threshold.

## Target Customer
Agencies with an existing eMAR that fits poorly; pharmacies offering eMARs to IDD clients.

## Impact If Solved
A reliable eMAR that DSPs can use safely without a nurse in the house. Scope checks and plain-language prompts could cut reportable medication errors by ~20–30% and after-hours calls to the delegating nurse by ~a third (estimate).
