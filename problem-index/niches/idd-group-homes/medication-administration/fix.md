# The Weekend Home Visit That Came Back Two Tablets Short

**Niche:** [[niches/idd-group-homes/medication-administration/profile|Medication Administration by Med-Certified DSPs]]
**Industry:** [[industries/idd-group-homes|IDD Group Homes]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Medications sent home with a resident for a family weekend come back with the wrong count, and nobody can say whether doses were missed or doubled.
**Tags:** #compliance #worker-facing #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make every medication pass by a med-certified DSP match the current order, and whoever keeps medication errors that reach the critical-incident threshold lowest takes the account.

## The Problem
For a family visit, DSPs pop doses into an envelope and fill a paper leave-of-absence form. On return the count is off. Was a seizure medication missed? The house reports a possible error and calls the nurse on Sunday night.

## Why It's Still Broken
Leave-of-absence handling sits outside most eMARs, and families are not given a simple way to record what they gave.

## What a Fix Looks Like
An eMAR leave-of-absence workflow that prints a labelled schedule and sends the family a text link to tick each dose given, with an automatic count reconciliation on return and a prompt to the nurse only when a dose was actually missed.

## Who Feels the Pain
DSPs who reconcile at handover, on-call nurses, families unsure what to give, and residents who miss anticonvulsants.

## Impact If Fixed
Unexplained leave-of-absence counts could fall by ~half, and Sunday-night nurse calls with them (estimate), for ~5 minutes of extra work per visit (estimate).
