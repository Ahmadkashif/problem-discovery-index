# Medication Administration by Med-Certified DSPs

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/idd-group-homes|IDD Group Homes]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make every medication pass by a med-certified DSP match the current order, and whoever keeps medication errors that reach the critical-incident threshold lowest takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600M a year in eMAR licences, pharmacy services and packaging fees attributable to group homes, delegating-nurse time and DSP medication-pass and reconciliation time (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1.6% of facility revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — eMARs are common at larger agencies; small agencies and many homes still use pharmacy-printed paper MARs
**Target Buyer:** Director of nursing or health services; long-term care pharmacy account manager
**Automation Potential:** High — order reconciliation, pass prompts, count checks, leave-of-absence transfers and error classification are rule-bound

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Group homes pass medications several times a day through unlicensed DSPs working under state certification and nurse delegation, with no nurse on site (state rules vary, estimate). Residents move between the house, a day programme and family homes with medications in tow. Medication errors that lead to a poison control call, an emergency or urgent care visit, a hospitalisation or a death are critical incidents every state must define and investigate from 9 July 2027 (89 FR 40542). The work is mechanical, repetitive and checkable, which is why it sits on this axis. The contest is fought by IDD record-system eMARs, pharmacy-integrated eMARs and the pharmacies themselves, whose consultant services are logged under skilled nursing as [[niches/skilled-nursing-facilities/ltc-pharmacy-consultant-services/profile|🔍 Long-Term Care Pharmacy Clinical Consulting]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
eMAR modules (Therap, MediSked, iCareManager), pharmacy eMARs, blister packaging, barcode scanning, paper MARs. Gaps: no automatic reconciliation of pharmacy fills, prescriber orders and the MAR; leave-of-absence counts on paper; errors classified against the state's threshold by hand.
