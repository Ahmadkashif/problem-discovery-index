# Olmstead Settlement Independent Reviewers & Court Monitors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/idd-group-homes|IDD Group Homes]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Independent reviewers appointed under Department of Justice settlements with states over unnecessary institutionalisation of people with IDD, following the Supreme Court's Olmstead decision. They review samples of individuals' services, community placements and incident handling, and report to the court on the state's compliance (named from recall). The state pays under the settlement.

## Insight Function
**Size:** A lead reviewer with a handful of consultants per settlement (estimate)
**Output:** Periodic compliance reports filed with the court
**Proprietary data:** Case reviews of individuals' records and services
**Clock:** Court-ordered reporting dates
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — appointed under each settlement
