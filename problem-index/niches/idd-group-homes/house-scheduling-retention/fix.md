# The Same Three DSPs Mandated Every Week

**Niche:** [[niches/idd-group-homes/house-scheduling-retention/profile|House Scheduling, Overtime & First-Year Retention]]
**Industry:** [[industries/idd-group-homes|IDD Group Homes]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Mandates fall on the people who always answer the phone, so the agency burns out its most dependable staff first.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #worker-facing #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to cover every house's shifts from the agency's own trained DSPs without forcing anyone to stay, and whoever cuts mandated overtime shifts per house furthest takes the account.

## The Problem
When no one takes an open shift, the house manager mandates whoever is on duty, usually the same reliable few. Payroll records the hours as overtime but not whether they were forced.

## Why It's Still Broken
No system records a mandate as a distinct event, so nobody sees the pattern until a valued DSP quits.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A one-tap "mandated" flag on the timecard, a rotation that spreads mandates fairly, and a weekly report to directors of mandates per person and per house.

## Who Feels the Pain
The dependable DSPs, their families, and the house managers who lose them.

## Impact If Fixed
Mandates become visible and fairly spread, with no DSP forced more than ~2 times a month (estimate), and the houses that rely on them are identified within weeks, for the cost of one field and one report.
