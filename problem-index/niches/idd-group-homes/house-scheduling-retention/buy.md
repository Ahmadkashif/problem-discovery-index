# Workforce Management With Per-Resident Qualification Rules

**Niche:** [[niches/idd-group-homes/house-scheduling-retention/profile|House Scheduling, Overtime & First-Year Retention]]
**Industry:** [[industries/idd-group-homes|IDD Group Homes]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Workforce-management suites already offer open shifts by app; they do not know which DSP may support which resident.
**Tags:** #optimization-fundamentals #feature-engineering #worker-facing #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to cover every house's shifts from the agency's own trained DSPs without forcing anyone to stay, and whoever cuts mandated overtime shifts per house furthest takes the account.

## The Problem
Open-shift apps broadcast to everyone, and the house manager then rejects people who are not trained on the residents, cannot pass medications or cannot drive the van.

## What Already Exists
UKG, When I Work and similar suites; scheduling inside IDD record systems; nursing-home staffing apps.

## The Customization Gap
Qualifications must be per resident and per house: person-specific training sign-offs, medication certification level, behaviour-intervention training, lifting and transfer training, gender preference for personal care, a driver's licence. Staffing rules are per house and per hour: awake overnight staff, one-to-one hours from the person's authorisation, minimum staff during community outings. Mandated overtime has to be a tracked event with fairness rules, not just extra hours.

## Target Customer
Agencies already licensing a workforce suite and unwilling to add another vendor.

## Impact If Solved
Shift offers reach only eligible DSPs, open shifts fill ~1–2 hours sooner, and ~20–30% fewer shifts end in a mandate (estimate).
