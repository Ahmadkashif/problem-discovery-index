# House Scheduling, Overtime & First-Year Retention

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/idd-group-homes|IDD Group Homes]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to cover every house's shifts from the agency's own trained DSPs without forcing anyone to stay, and whoever cuts mandated overtime shifts per house furthest takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.6B a year in scheduling software, scheduler and house-manager time spent filling shifts, retention programmes, and overtime premium (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~4% of facility revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium-Low — schedules sit in software or on a whiteboard; call-offs are filled by text and phone at all hours
**Target Buyer:** Director of residential services; regional director at a multi-state agency
**Automation Potential:** High for forecasting and shift offers; low for supervision and pay

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Group homes fill holes with their own people. Each resident needs staff who know their plans, so agency and marketplace labour is rare (estimate), and a call-off becomes a forced double for whoever is on shift. Mandated overtime is the clearest early sign of a new DSP's exit (estimate), and 64% of leavers go within a year (NCI, 2024). The contest is between scheduling modules in IDD record systems, generic workforce-management tools and the agency's own float pools and supervisors. The nursing-home version of this contest, where the goal is to avoid agency hours and protect reported staffing, is [[niches/skilled-nursing-facilities/internal-scheduling-retention/profile|🎯 Internal Scheduling, Float Pools & Retention]]. Here there is no PBJ file and the fill of last resort is the DSP already on shift.

## Current Tools & Gaps
IDD record-system scheduling (Therap, iCareManager and others), UKG and When I Work, group texts, paper float lists. Gaps: call-offs are not forecast; qualification per resident is not a hard constraint in shift offers; mandated overtime is not tracked per person; float pools are coordinated by phone.
