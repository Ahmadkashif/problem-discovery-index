# Protection & Advocacy Agencies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/idd-group-homes|IDD Group Homes]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Each state's federally mandated Protection and Advocacy organisation (often named Disability Rights followed by the state) has statutory authority to investigate abuse and neglect of people with developmental disabilities. They monitor group homes and institutions, bring litigation and publish investigation reports (named from recall). They are funded by federal formula grants.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Investigators and attorneys working cases, who are production headcount; research staff under 10 (estimate)
**Output:** Investigation reports, monitoring visits, litigation
**Proprietary data:** Case and investigation files obtained under statutory access authority
**Clock:** Case and litigation deadlines
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — federally funded
