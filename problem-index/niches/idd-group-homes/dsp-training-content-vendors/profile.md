# DSP Training & Credentialing Content Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/idd-group-homes|IDD Group Homes]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors of online DSP training and credentialing: Relias (which publishes DSP material on the Fatal Five), the College of Direct Support, and the National Alliance for Direct Support Professionals' credential programme (named from recall). Agencies pay per learner or per seat to meet state pre-service and annual training mandates. Course writers and instructional designers produce and update the courses as rules change, and some vendors add workforce reports on completions and competency.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~under 10 analytics staff on the IDD line per vendor (estimate); course writers and instructional designers produce the product and are production headcount, not counted
**Output:** Courses, competency assessments, training-compliance reports for agencies
**Proprietary data:** The course library, plus completion and assessment records held for customer agencies
**Clock:** States' training mandates (pre-service hours, annual renewals), which are customers' compliance clocks
**Buyer:** VP of content or product at the vendor
