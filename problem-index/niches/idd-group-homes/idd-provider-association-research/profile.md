# IDD Provider Association Research (ANCOR and State Associations)

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/idd-group-homes|IDD Group Homes]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
ANCOR, the national association of community IDD providers, and state provider associations survey members on wages, vacancies, turnover and costs. They publish workforce reports and use them in rate advocacy and legislative testimony (named from recall). Members pay dues; the research supports lobbying.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research and policy staff per association (estimate)
**Output:** Workforce reports, member surveys, testimony and comment letters
**Proprietary data:** Voluntary member survey responses
**Clock:** Legislative sessions, budget cycles and rate-study comment periods
**Buyer:** Association CEO or policy director
