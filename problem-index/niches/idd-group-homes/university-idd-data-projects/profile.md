# University IDD Data Projects (RISP and State of the States)

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/idd-group-homes|IDD Group Homes]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Federally funded university projects that compile national data on IDD services. The Residential Information Systems Project at the University of Minnesota's Institute on Community Integration, funded by the Administration for Community Living, publishes where people with IDD live, ICF/IID and waiver counts, and per-person spending, such as 60,264 ICF/IID residents at $159,520 each in 2022. The State of the States in Intellectual and Developmental Disabilities project publishes spending by state (named from recall).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 researchers per project (estimate)
**Output:** Annual status-and-trends reports, state profiles, data briefs
**Proprietary data:** State-reported aggregate counts and spending going back decades
**Clock:** Grant reporting cycles
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — federal grant funded
