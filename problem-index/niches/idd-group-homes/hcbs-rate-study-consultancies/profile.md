# IDD & HCBS Rate-Study Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/idd-group-homes|IDD Group Homes]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Consultancies that state DD and Medicaid agencies hire to set the fee schedules paid to IDD providers. They build independent rate models (wages, benefits, staff productivity, mileage, administration) per service, survey providers on costs and wages, run time studies, and present recommended rates for public comment. Burns & Associates, a division of Health Management Associates, did so for New Mexico's DD, Mi Via and Medically Fragile waivers, with comments closing 19 December 2025; Public Consulting Group ran New Mexico's previous study. Guidehouse, Myers and Stauffer, HSRI and Mercer are also active in this work (named from recall). The study is the invoice; the state is the client.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10–40 analysts and rate modellers per practice (estimate)
**Output:** Rate study reports, rate models, provider survey and time-study analyses, public-comment responses
**Proprietary data:** A cross-state library of rate-model structures and assumptions; the provider cost, wage and time-study data is collected for each state under contract
**Clock:** Waiver renewals every five years, legislated rate-review cycles and state budget calendars, all set by the client (estimate)
**Buyer:** State DD director or Medicaid rate-setting lead; practice leader at the firm

**Kill switches:** **Data isn't theirs.** The provider cost, wage and time-study data each study runs on is collected for the commissioning state under contract, and the resulting models are the state's work product. The firm's reusable asset is method, not data. Government procurement itself is not counted.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. Scored next to the actuaries who set Medicaid managed care capitation rates, [[niches/health-insurance-carriers/medicaid-rate-setting-actuaries/profile|🔍 Medicaid Capitation Rate-Setting Actuaries]], with the same switch. Their 46 is pre-ruling: it takes Q4 5 on a recalled CMS certification clock that is tied to each state's contract rating period, a client date that the current rule caps at Q4 4 (~44, logged for Stage E). On that basis the gap is one Q4 point: a waiver renewal or legislated rate review is equally the client's calendar, and rate studies have no certification filing behind them, so they take Q4 3. HMA's policy modelling for health plans is a separate pocket, [[niches/health-insurance-carriers/medicare-health-policy-advisory/profile|🔍 Medicare & Health Policy Advisory Firms]] (46, below threshold), which runs on public CMS files and so carries no switch. The rate-study practice works on state-commissioned provider data, which is why the switch is live here.
