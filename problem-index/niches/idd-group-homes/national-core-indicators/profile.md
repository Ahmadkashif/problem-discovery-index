# National Core Indicators (HSRI & NASDDDS)

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/idd-group-homes|IDD Group Homes]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
National Core Indicators–IDD is a collaboration between the National Association of State Directors of Developmental Disabilities Services, the Human Services Research Institute and participating states. It runs standard surveys of people receiving IDD services, of families and of provider agencies' DSP workforce, then publishes state and national reports. Participating states enter annual agreements, and the National Quality Forum endorsed 14 NCI-IDD measures in January 2022. Its 2024 State of the Workforce survey covered 3,936 agencies and 344,179 DSPs in 26 states and DC. It is the source of the industry's turnover (37%) and wage ($18.39 median) benchmarks.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15 people listed on the NCI team page, 11 at HSRI and 4 at NASDDDS, including emeritus and executive roles; a core of about 10–12 (checked 2026-10-24)
**Output:** Annual state and national survey reports, the State of the Workforce report, research datasets released under request and use agreements
**Proprietary data:** The only cross-state, person-level outcome survey of people with IDD, plus the workforce survey, collected by or for each participating state
**Clock:** NCI's own annual survey and release cycle; states' quality reporting uses it but does not set its dates
**Buyer:** State DD directors, through participation agreements with NASDDDS
