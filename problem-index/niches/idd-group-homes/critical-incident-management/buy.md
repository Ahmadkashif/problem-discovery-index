**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get each critical incident reported, investigated and closed with corrective action inside the state's timeframes, and whoever keeps the share of incidents closed late lowest takes the account.

## The Problem
Providers need case management for incidents, and the tools they find are built for workplace injuries or hospital patient safety.

## What Already Exists
Workplace safety and investigation case-management platforms; hospital patient-safety event reporting; incident modules in IDD record systems.

## The Customization Gap
The platform needs the state's critical incident taxonomy mapped to the federal minimum categories, with each state's reporting and investigation timeframes. It needs restrictive-intervention fields tied to the behaviour support plan, and medication-error severity thresholds (poison centre, ER, hospitalisation). It needs protected-health-information handling, separation of investigator and accused staff, submission to the state system, and reports on the three 90% measures.

## Target Customer
Provider compliance teams outgrowing their record system's incident module; state agencies replacing legacy systems.

## Impact If Solved
A proven case engine, configured for IDD rules, gets providers onto a tracked process before the July 2027 deadline without a custom build.
