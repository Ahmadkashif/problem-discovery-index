# An Investigation Clock That Assembles Its Own File

**Niche:** [[niches/idd-group-homes/critical-incident-management/profile|Critical Incident Reporting & Investigations]]
**Industry:** [[industries/idd-group-homes|IDD Group Homes]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Investigations run late mostly because gathering the file (statements, rosters, plans, MARs) takes weeks of chasing.
**Tags:** #ai-platform #bert #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #compliance #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get each critical incident reported, investigated and closed with corrective action inside the state's timeframes, and whoever keeps the share of incidents closed late lowest takes the account.

## The Problem
After a serious incident, a provider investigator must collect statements from every DSP on shift, the staffing roster, the person's behaviour and health plans, MAR records, prior incidents and any video or ER records, then write findings and a corrective action plan. Investigators carry dozens of open cases. Deadlines slip, and the state notices.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Incident modules record the report; they do not run the investigation. The evidence sits in scheduling, MAR, plan and HR systems that do not talk to each other.

## What to Build
A case system that starts the clock when the incident is classified, using the state's category and timeframes. It pulls the roster, plans, MAR, prior incidents and training records for everyone on shift automatically. It sends each witness a statement request with a deadline, drafts a summary of evidence and a findings template, and tracks the corrective action to verified closure. It shows each investigator's caseload against the deadlines and the agency's on-time rates for the three 90% measures states will report.

## Target Customer
Quality and compliance directors at providers that run their own investigations, and state DD agencies building to the 2027 and 2029 requirements.

## Impact If Built
If assembling the file drops from ~2–3 weeks to ~2–3 days (estimate), a provider closing ~80–85% of investigations on time today could clear the 90% minimum states must report from July 2027 (estimate). Staff on leave pending findings return sooner, and corrective actions stop being promised and forgotten.
