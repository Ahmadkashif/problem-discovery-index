# A Back-Office Agent That Runs the Compliance Calendar for Six Homes

**Niche:** [[niches/idd-group-homes/small-provider-agencies/profile|Small Provider Agencies]]
**Industry:** [[industries/idd-group-homes|IDD Group Homes]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A small agency's citations come from missed dates (an expired training, a late plan review, an unfiled incident) more than from bad care.
**Tags:** #ai-agent #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #compliance #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stand in for the compliance and billing office a small agency cannot afford, and whoever leaves the fewest findings per home across licensing reviews and Medicaid audits takes the account.

## The Problem
The owner of a four-home agency tracks staff trainings, fire drills, medication certifications, plan reviews, incident deadlines, licence renewals and billing in her head and on a spreadsheet. A licensing review or waiver audit finds the gaps all at once.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Record systems for small agencies focus on notes and billing. Compliance software is sold to large organisations with compliance officers. State rules differ, and nobody has encoded them at small-agency prices.

## What to Build
An agent that reads the agency's records (staff files, training certificates, plans, drills, incidents, claims) against the state's licensing and waiver requirements. It keeps a dated calendar of everything due per home and per person and chases staff by text for expiring items. Before a review it runs a mock review on a sample and drafts any corrective plan. It also checks each month's claims against notes before submission.

## Target Customer
Owner-operators running one to ten homes in a single state.

## Impact If Built
Citations and recoupments per home could fall by ~half (estimate), and the owner gets back ~5–10 hours a week of evening paperwork (estimate).
