# Small-Business Compliance Software With State IDD Rulebooks

**Niche:** [[niches/idd-group-homes/small-provider-agencies/profile|Small Provider Agencies]]
**Industry:** [[industries/idd-group-homes|IDD Group Homes]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Compliance-calendar and document-control tools already exist for small healthcare businesses; none ships with a state DD licensing rulebook.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stand in for the compliance and billing office a small agency cannot afford, and whoever leaves the fewest findings per home across licensing reviews and Medicaid audits takes the account.

## The Problem
Small agencies buy a generic task manager or a policy template pack and still miss the dates that matter to their licensor.

## What Already Exists
Small-practice compliance software for clinics, policy-template vendors, training-tracking modules in learning platforms.

## The Customization Gap
The product needs each state's DD licensing standards, waiver provider requirements and incident rules turned into dated tasks per home, per staff member and per resident. Examples: annual plan reviews, medication certification renewals, quarterly fire drills, required staff-to-resident ratios. It also needs mock-review checklists in the licensor's own format.

## Target Customer
Single-state owner-operators; state provider associations that could offer it to members.

## Impact If Solved
A small agency gets the compliance office it cannot hire for ~$200–500 a month (estimate), against ~$50,000 or more a year for a compliance coordinator (estimate).
