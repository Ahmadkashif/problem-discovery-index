# Retail Text-to-Hire Tools With State Clearance Rules

**Niche:** [[niches/idd-group-homes/dsp-hiring-onboarding/profile|DSP Hiring & Pre-Service Training]]
**Industry:** [[industries/idd-group-homes|IDD Group Homes]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Conversational hiring tools already screen and schedule hourly applicants in minutes; they stop at the offer, where a DSP's longest wait begins.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #ai-agent #workflow-orchestration #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a DSP applicant into a worker cleared to support residents alone, and whoever converts the highest share of applicants into DSPs working their first solo shift takes the account.

## The Problem
Agencies compete for the same applicants as retailers and warehouses, which hire in days. DSP hiring takes weeks because of clearances and training the retail tools know nothing about.

## What Already Exists
Text-based conversational hiring and interview scheduling for hourly roles; background-check vendors; online DSP curricula.

## The Customization Gap
The tool must carry the conversation past the offer. It has to know the state's required clearances and their typical turnaround, the pre-service hours, and the medication certification rules. It must answer the applicant's questions about the work honestly (behaviour support, personal care) and screen for the realistic job preview that NCI found 80% of agencies already use (NCI, 2024). It must hand each applicant to training with seats booked.

## Target Customer
HR leaders at multi-state agencies already using a text-to-hire product.

## Impact If Solved
One conversation from first text to first shift. At an agency making ~400 offers a year, recovering a third of the ~120 offers lost before day one (~30%, estimate) adds ~40 working DSPs a year.
