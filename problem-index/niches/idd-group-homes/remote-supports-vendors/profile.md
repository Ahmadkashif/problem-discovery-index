# Remote Supports & Enabling Technology Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/idd-group-homes|IDD Group Homes]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors of remote supports: sensors, two-way video or audio and a remote monitoring centre that let a home or apartment replace some awake overnight staff. Sengistix, SimplyHome and Rest Assured are representative (named from recall). Many waivers pay for remote supports as a service, and agencies or states pay per person per month.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Monitoring-centre staff, who are production headcount; under 10 data or analytics staff per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Remote monitoring, alerts, usage reports
**Proprietary data:** Sensor and event data from monitored homes
**Clock:** None external beyond service-level agreements
**Buyer:** Agency COO or state DD agency
