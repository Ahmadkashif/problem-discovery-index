# Hourly-Workforce Platforms Adapted to Person-Specific Training

**Niche:** [[niches/idd-group-homes/dsp-workforce/profile|DSP Workforce Supply]]
**Industry:** [[industries/idd-group-homes|IDD Group Homes]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Retail and logistics already run hiring and scheduling for hourly workers at scale; a group home's worker is not interchangeable between houses.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #feature-engineering #worker-facing #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every competitor here is fighting to staff every house on every shift with DSPs trained for its residents at the lowest overtime. That is two contests with different winners. One is turning applicants into DSPs cleared to work alone, which recruiting, background-screening and training vendors win. The other is covering shifts from the agency's own staff without forcing doubles, which scheduling tools and the agency's own supervisors win. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Agencies use the same high-volume hiring and scheduling tools as retailers, or none at all, and those tools treat every worker as able to cover every shift.

## What Already Exists
High-volume hourly hiring platforms with text-based applications, generic workforce management (UKG, When I Work), and scheduling modules inside IDD record systems.

## The Customization Gap
In a group home, qualification is per resident: medication certification level, the person's own behaviour and dining plans, lifting and transfer training, gender preference, a driver's licence for community outings. Hiring has to track state-specific clearances (abuse registries, fingerprints) and pre-service hours before solo work. Scheduling has to hold awake-overnight and one-to-one rules per house. The platform needs those qualifications as hard constraints in both hiring and shift offers.

## Target Customer
HR and operations leaders at multi-state agencies, where state rules multiply.

## Impact If Solved
Shift offers reach only people allowed to take them, rejected acceptances fall to near zero, and hiring shows the clearance each applicant still lacks, shortening the time vacancies sit open by ~1–2 weeks (estimate).
