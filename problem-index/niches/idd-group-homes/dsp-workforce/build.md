**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)

**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every competitor here is fighting to staff every house on every shift with DSPs trained for its residents at the lowest overtime. That is two contests with different winners. One is turning applicants into DSPs cleared to work alone, which recruiting, background-screening and training vendors win. The other is covering shifts from the agency's own staff without forcing doubles, which scheduling tools and the agency's own supervisors win. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
An agency hires hundreds of DSPs a year and loses most of them inside twelve months. HR sees applications and hires, training sees course completions, scheduling sees shifts, payroll sees overtime. None of them can say whether people are lost waiting for a background check, during pre-service training, in the first forced double or under a particular supervisor.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Each function buys its own tool, and the data lives in three or four vendors' exports. Workforce analytics products are built for large employers with HR analysts, which residential agencies lack.

## What to Build
A ledger that joins applicant tracking, background-check status, training completions, schedules, timeclock, mandated shifts and separations per DSP. It shows the funnel from application to first solo shift and the survival curve through the first year, broken down by house, supervisor, shift pattern and recruiting source. It names the stage where each cohort is lost. It feeds both sub-niches: the hiring side sees where applicants stall, and the scheduling side sees which shift patterns precede early exits.

## Target Customer
Chief HR officers and VPs of residential services at agencies with 300+ DSPs.

## Impact If Built
An agency hiring ~400 DSPs a year (estimate) that keeps 10% more of them past year one avoids ~40 replacement hires and the overtime that covers each vacancy while it is open.
