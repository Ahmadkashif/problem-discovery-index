# DSP Workforce Supply

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/idd-group-homes|IDD Group Homes]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every competitor here is fighting to staff every house on every shift with DSPs trained for its residents at the lowest overtime. That is two contests with different winners. One is turning applicants into DSPs cleared to work alone, which recruiting, background-screening and training vendors win. The other is covering shifts from the agency's own staff without forcing doubles, which scheduling tools and the agency's own supervisors win. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.5B a year in recruiting, screening, pre-service training, scheduling software, scheduler and supervisor time, and overtime premium above straight time (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of facility revenue, inside a payroll of ~$24.8B in 2025 (BLS QCEW wages; share estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — applicant tracking and payroll are digital; call-off cover runs on texts and phone calls
**Target Buyer:** Chief HR officer or VP of residential services at a multi-home agency
**Automation Potential:** Medium — screening, scheduling and shift offers automate; supervision and pay do not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Labour is the business. Wages alone were ~$24.8B across NAICS 623210 in 2025 (BLS QCEW). The average DSP turnover ratio was 37% in 2024 and 64% of leavers had stayed under a year, while 26% of agencies turned away referrals because they could not staff them (NCI State of the Workforce). Unlike nursing homes, where agencies and marketplaces are a real second source ([[niches/skilled-nursing-facilities/per-diem-shift-marketplaces/profile|🎯 Per-Diem Shift Marketplaces & Agency Labour]]), group homes rarely fill holes from them (estimate). Each resident needs staff trained on their own plan, so overtime is the default fill, and the contest splits into getting people in and keeping the schedule covered.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Applicant tracking (often generic or inside HR suites), state background-check and registry portals, training platforms (Relias, College of Direct Support), payroll and scheduling in HR suites or IDD record systems. Gaps: no view of the funnel from applicant to first solo shift; call-offs not forecast; mandated overtime not tracked by person.
