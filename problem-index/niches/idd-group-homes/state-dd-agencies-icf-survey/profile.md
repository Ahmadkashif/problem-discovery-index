# State DD Agencies & ICF/IID Survey Oversight

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/idd-group-homes|IDD Group Homes]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State DD agencies run the waivers, license group homes, manage waiting lists and, from July 2027, must meet the Access Rule's incident and planning minimums. State survey agencies inspect ICFs/IID under contract to CMS at least every 15 months, with a statewide average of 12 or less (42 CFR 442.109).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Licensing reviewers, surveyors and incident investigators by the dozen in large states, who are production headcount; quality and data units are small (estimate)
**Output:** Licensing and survey findings, waiver assurances to CMS, waiting-list management
**Proprietary data:** Licensing, survey, incident and claims records for the state's IDD system
**Clock:** The 15-month ICF/IID survey limit, waiver renewals and CMS reporting
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — state agencies
