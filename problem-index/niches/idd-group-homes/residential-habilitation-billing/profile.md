# Residential Habilitation Billing & ISP Documentation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/idd-group-homes|IDD Group Homes]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make every billed residential day stand up to a state or Medicaid Fraud Control Unit audit, and whoever keeps the share of billed days recouped on audit lowest takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.5B a year in record-system licences, DSP and house-manager documentation time, billing staff and outsourced billing spent on residential claims (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~4% of ~$38B facility revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — claims and most notes are electronic, but notes are written at the end of shifts, chased by house managers and checked against audit standards by hand
**Target Buyer:** COO or VP of compliance at a multi-home agency; billing manager
**Automation Potential:** High — note drafting, ISP mapping, attendance reconciliation and pre-bill audit checks are rule-bound per state

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Residential habilitation is the largest line of IDD waiver spending, and almost all of a group home's revenue (estimate). It is paid by the day, at a tier set by the person's assessed support needs. A day is earned only if the resident was present by the state's counting rule and the notes show the person-centred plan's supports were delivered. HCBS waiver spending averaged $55,812 for each of 963,273 people with IDD in 2022 (RISP). The niche differs from per-diem waiver billing in assisted living, where the hard part is keeping authorisations current ([[niches/assisted-living-communities/medicaid-waiver-billing/profile|⚡ Medicaid Waiver Billing & Level-of-Care Reassessments]]). Here the authorisation usually holds for the plan year, and the risk sits in whether each day's documentation would survive an auditor.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Therap, MediSked, SETWorks, iCareManager, Kibu and Netsmart myEvolv provide ISP-linked note templates, attendance and claims; AI drafting is arriving (Kibu, iCareManager's emrgen.ai). Gaps: notes are checked against the state's documentation standard only after an audit; no pre-bill score of which days would fail; and outcome coverage per person is not tracked between annual plan meetings.
