# Clinical AI Scribes Retrained on State IDD Documentation Standards

**Niche:** [[niches/idd-group-homes/residential-habilitation-billing/profile|Residential Habilitation Billing & ISP Documentation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/idd-group-homes|IDD Group Homes]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Ambient scribes already turn speech into structured notes for clinicians; an IDD agency needs habilitation notes an auditor will accept.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #compliance #worker-facing #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make every billed residential day stand up to a state or Medicaid Fraud Control Unit audit, and whoever keeps the share of billed days recouped on audit lowest takes the account.

## The Problem
DSPs write notes at the end of exhausting shifts, often in a second language, against plan outcomes they half remember. Notes come out generic, which is exactly what auditors reject.

## What Already Exists
Ambient clinical scribes for physicians and therapists; AI note features inside IDD record systems (Kibu, iCareManager's emrgen.ai, SETWorks); general speech-to-text on phones.

## The Customization Gap
The scribe has to be rebuilt around the ISP, not the clinical encounter. It must retrieve each resident's outcomes and support strategies and map the DSP's story to them. It must write in observable, person-centred language and in the state's format, and ask a follow-up question when an outcome is missing. It must handle several residents in one spoken report, recognise when a story describes a reportable incident, and write into the agency's record system with DSP sign-off. Accuracy has to be measured against audit acceptance, not physician satisfaction.

## Target Customer
VPs of compliance and operations at residential agencies; record-system vendors adding AI drafting.

## Impact If Solved
~20–30 minutes back per DSP per shift (estimate), and notes that pass review because they name the outcome and the support every time.
