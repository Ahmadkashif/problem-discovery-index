# Federal R&D Program Offices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-research-labs|Contract Research Labs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Program managers at DARPA, the Air Force Research Laboratory, the Office of Naval Research, Army DEVCOM, ARPA-E, NASA and agency SBIR offices write the topics, evaluate the proposals and choose the performers that fund contract research labs. DARPA alone obligated $4.2B for R&D in FY2024, $2.9B of it to businesses (NSF NCSES).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Hundreds of program managers and technical staff across the main R&D funders (estimate)
**Output:** Program designs, funding decisions and performer oversight — not a sold product
**Proprietary data:** Every proposal received, every evaluation and every performer's results — the definitive record of what research gets funded and why, protected as source-selection information
**Clock:** Appropriations and obligation deadlines on research funds, plus their own solicitation calendars
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer
