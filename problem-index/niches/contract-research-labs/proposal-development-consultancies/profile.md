# Proposal Development & SBIR Coaching Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-research-labs|Contract Research Labs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Proposal consultancies such as Shipley Associates and Lohfeld Consulting, and SBIR-specialist coaches and commercialisation programmes, help research firms plan capture, write and review proposals, run colour-team reviews and build commercialisation plans. Most SBIR coaching shops are a few people; the larger proposal firms exceed the gate. The grant-writing side of the same work is logged under [[industries/grant-writers|Grant Writers]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–80 proposal managers, writers and reviewers at the larger firms (estimate); most coaching shops under 10
**Output:** Proposal strategy, drafted and reviewed proposal volumes, capture plans, training
**Proprietary data:** Clients' past proposals, debriefs and outcomes — the clients' property, often with export-controlled technical content
**Clock:** Solicitation close dates, which are absolute
**Buyer:** Managing director or practice lead

**Kill switches:** data isn't theirs to use — every proposal, price and debrief belongs to the client, competing clients cannot share a corpus, and defense proposals often carry export-controlled content.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. The clock is perfect and the outcome is clean (win or lose), but the archive that would make the firm smarter is a stack of other people's confidential proposals.
