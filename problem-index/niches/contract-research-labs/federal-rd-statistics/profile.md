# Federal R&D Statistics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-research-labs|Contract Research Labs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The National Center for Science and Engineering Statistics at NSF runs the surveys that measure US research: federal R&D obligations by agency and performer, business R&D, higher-education R&D and the science workforce. Its tables — such as the FY2024 split of $194.2B in federal R&D obligations by performer — are the standard reference for the size and direction of this industry's funding.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~50–150 statisticians, survey managers and analysts (estimate)
**Output:** Survey data tables, InfoBriefs and the Science and Engineering Indicators
**Proprietary data:** Respondent-level survey microdata, confidential by statute
**Clock:** Annual survey and publication schedules
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer
