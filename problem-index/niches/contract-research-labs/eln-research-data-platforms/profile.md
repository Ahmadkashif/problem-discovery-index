# Electronic Lab Notebook & Research Data Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-research-labs|Contract Research Labs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Benchling, LabArchives, Revvity Signals and similar vendors sell electronic notebooks and research data platforms to research organisations, with scientific content and template teams who model workflows for each discipline. They hold enormous volumes of experimental records, all of them their customers'.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–60 scientific content, template and data staff per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Software, templates and integrations; no analytical deliverable
**Proprietary data:** Customer records hosted under contract, not theirs to analyse or pool
**Clock:** None external
**Buyer:** Chief product officer
