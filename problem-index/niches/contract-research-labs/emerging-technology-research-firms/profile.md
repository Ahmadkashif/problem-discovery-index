# Emerging-Technology & Deep-Tech Research Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-research-labs|Contract Research Labs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Firms such as Lux Research and IDTechEx assess emerging technologies — advanced materials, nanomaterials, batteries, sensors — and the startups and labs developing them, and sell subscriptions, scouting reports and market forecasts to corporate innovation teams deciding what to license, fund or partner with. Contract research labs and nanotech spinouts are among the subjects they rate.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~30–150 analysts per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Technology assessments, startup and lab ratings, market forecasts, scouting engagements
**Proprietary data:** Years of interviews and ratings of startups and technologies, whose later fates (funding, acquisition, failure, commercial launch) are observable
**Clock:** Self-set publication calendars and clients' annual innovation planning
**Buyer:** Head of Research or Chief Analyst
