# Timesheets Corrected After the Fact

**Niche:** [[niches/contract-research-labs/cost-accounting-indirect-rates/profile|Government Cost Accounting & Indirect Rates]]
**Industry:** [[industries/contract-research-labs|Contract Research Labs]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Researchers fill in timesheets at the end of the week from memory and fix the charges later, which is exactly the pattern a DCAA floor check is designed to catch.
**Tags:** #change-point-detection #k-nearest-neighbors #feature-engineering #compliance #quick-win #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a research firm's books through a DCAA accounting-system review and its incurred-cost submission accepted on time, and whoever makes a small firm audit-ready without a full-time government accountant takes the account.

## The Problem
A scientist working on three contracts, a proposal and an internal project in one week reconstructs where the hours went on Friday. Charges are later moved between contracts when a budget runs out or a manager notices a mistake. Each correction needs a documented reason, and frequent unexplained transfers look like cost mischarging.

## Why It's Still Broken
Researchers think in experiments, not charge codes; timekeeping tools ask for codes daily but offer no help choosing them; and nobody reviews patterns until audit.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Suggested daily charges pre-filled from the scientist's calendar, instrument bookings and notebook entries, confirmed in seconds; and a weekly pattern check that flags transfers, end-of-week bulk entry and charges to exhausted budgets for a documented explanation before they become an audit finding.

## Who Feels the Pain
Researchers nagged about timesheets; controllers explaining transfers; founders facing an adverse accounting-system finding.

## Impact If Fixed
Contemporaneous, defensible timesheets with less effort than the current Friday reconstruction remove the most common basis for an inadequate accounting system.
