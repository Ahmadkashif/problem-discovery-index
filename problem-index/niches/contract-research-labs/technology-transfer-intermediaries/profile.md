# Technology Transfer Offices & Partnership Intermediaries

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-research-labs|Contract Research Labs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
University technology transfer offices and federal partnership intermediaries such as TechLink assess invention disclosures, decide what to patent, and license university and federal-lab technology to companies, including the spinouts and SBIR firms in this industry. Their licensing associates judge which inventions will find a licensee — a tacit call made on every disclosure.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~15–60 licensing and commercialisation staff at the larger offices (estimate)
**Output:** Patent decisions, market assessments of inventions, licence agreements and startup formation
**Proprietary data:** Invention disclosures, patenting decisions and licensing outcomes across the institution's portfolio
**Clock:** Patent filing deadlines (one-year grace period, PCT national-phase dates) and agency reporting cycles
**Buyer:** Director of the technology transfer office
