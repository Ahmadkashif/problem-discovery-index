# The Readiness Level No Test Record Supports

**Niche:** [[niches/contract-research-labs/defense-applied-research/profile|Defense & Intelligence Applied R&D]]
**Industry:** [[industries/contract-research-labs|Contract Research Labs]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Final reports claim a technology readiness level that the test records behind them would not support, and program offices discount every claim as a result.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #evaluation-metrics #compliance #quick-win #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a funded prototype into a funded next phase with a transition sponsor, and whoever puts the evidence a program office needs — test data at a stated readiness level, a cost to field, a sponsor commitment — in front of it before the budget is built takes the follow-on.

## The Problem
"TRL 6 demonstrated" appears in many final reports. The program office asks what environment, what test article, what pass criteria, and the honest answer is a lab bench test of a partial prototype. Readiness claims are inflated because every firm inflates them, and evaluators have learned to ignore them, which hurts the firms whose claims are true.

## Why It's Still Broken
Readiness levels are defined in guidance but assessed by the firm itself, test data sits in engineers' folders rather than in the report, and nobody checks the claim against the evidence before it ships.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A short evidence check before every final report: each readiness claim is linked to the specific test records — conditions, article configuration, results against criteria — and an LLM drafts the supported level against the defense readiness assessment criteria, highlighting gaps. The firm ships a claim with its evidence attached.

## Who Feels the Pain
PIs whose genuine results are discounted; program offices that cannot tell strong prototypes from weak; business development staff trying to argue for Phase III.

## Impact If Fixed
Claims a program office can verify in minutes become a reputational asset, and the firm's strongest prototypes stop being priced like everyone else's.
