# Sales CRM Rebuilt Around the Budget Cycle

**Niche:** [[niches/contract-research-labs/defense-applied-research/profile|Defense & Intelligence Applied R&D]]
**Industry:** [[industries/contract-research-labs|Contract Research Labs]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Research firms track transition sponsors in a sales CRM built for quarterly pipelines, when defense follow-on money moves on a two-year budget calendar through program offices, not buyers.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #feature-engineering #logistic-regression #data-integration #revenue-impact #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a funded prototype into a funded next phase with a transition sponsor, and whoever puts the evidence a program office needs — test data at a stated readiness level, a cost to field, a sponsor commitment — in front of it before the budget is built takes the follow-on.

## The Problem
Transition is a relationship campaign with many actors: the technical point of contact who ran the Phase II, a program manager who might fund Phase III, a prime contractor who might integrate the technology, and a requirements office that has to recognise the need. Firms that track this at all use a CRM whose stages — lead, qualified, proposal, closed — fit none of it.

## What Already Exists
Salesforce, HubSpot and Microsoft Dynamics, plus government-contract CRM modules from Deltek and Unanet that add solicitation tracking and teaming partners.

## The Customization Gap
A defense research CRM needs: program elements and budget years as first-class objects; a stage model tied to the budget calendar rather than a sales quarter; each technology's evidence package (test reports, readiness level, cost estimate) attached to each sponsor conversation; prime-contractor integration paths; Phase III eligibility tracking from the original SBIR award; and export-control flags on what can be shared with whom.

## Target Customer
Business development staff and transition managers at SBIR firms with active Phase II portfolios.

## Impact If Solved
Transition stops depending on one PI's memory and inbox, and leadership can see which prototypes have a funded path and which are orphans while there is still time to act.
