# Government Contract Accounting & Indirect-Rate Advisors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-research-labs|Contract Research Labs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Government-contract practices at accounting and consulting firms build research firms' indirect-rate structures, prepare provisional billing rate requests and incurred-cost submissions, ready firms for DCAA accounting-system reviews and audits, and support pricing for cost-type proposals. SBIR firms moving to cost-type Phase II work are a large share of their small-client base.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~15–100 government-contract accountants and analysts per practice (estimate)
**Output:** Indirect-rate models and proposals, incurred-cost submissions, audit-readiness reviews, pricing support
**Proprietary data:** Rate structures, audit outcomes and agency positions across many contractor clients, held under client confidentiality
**Clock:** Incurred-cost submissions due six months after fiscal year end under FAR 52.216-7; DCAA audit and accounting-system review dates
**Buyer:** Government-contracts practice leader
