# Instrument Analysis Suites Fitted to Sponsor Reporting

**Niche:** [[niches/contract-research-labs/characterisation-data-analysis/profile|Characterisation & Microscopy Data Analysis]]
**Industry:** [[industries/contract-research-labs|Contract Research Labs]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Microscope and spectrometer vendors' analysis suites are powerful on their own instrument's data and blind to the other instruments, the sample history and the report the sponsor needs.
**Tags:** #cnns #semantic-segmentation #transfer-learning #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn raw micrographs, spectra and diffraction patterns into a quantified result with stated uncertainty that a sponsor accepts, and whoever does it fastest with a method two analysts would reproduce takes the account.

## The Problem
A single sample is imaged on one vendor's electron microscope, analysed on another's spectrometer and diffractometer, and reported in a Word template. Each vendor's software produces its own outputs in its own formats; analysts stitch them together by hand.

## What Already Exists
Thermo Fisher Velox and Avizo, Gatan DigitalMicrograph, Bruker and Malvern Panalytical analysis software, with growing AI-assisted segmentation features.

## The Customization Gap
For contract labs: cross-vendor ingestion keyed to the sample and the project; analysis templates per sponsor deliverable; uncertainty and method statements generated with each result; batch processing across a whole study; and export into the report and the lab's data record with funding provenance.

## Target Customer
Characterisation managers at labs with mixed-vendor instrument fleets.

## Impact If Solved
Analysts stop being the integration layer between instruments, and a study's results arrive in the report consistent and traceable.
