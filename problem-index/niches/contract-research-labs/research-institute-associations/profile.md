# Research Institute & Small-Business R&D Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-research-labs|Contract Research Labs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Associations of independent research institutes, small-business technology councils that advocate for SBIR, and industrial research management associations publish member surveys, policy positions and benchmarking on R&D practice, and lobbied through the 2025–2026 SBIR reauthorisation.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically under 10 staff, with research done by members and volunteers (estimate)
**Output:** Policy advocacy, member surveys, occasional benchmarking reports
**Proprietary data:** Member survey responses
**Clock:** Legislative calendars and annual meetings
**Buyer:** Executive director
