# Defense Contract Audit Agency

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-research-labs|Contract Research Labs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
DCAA audits defense contractors' accounting systems, forward-pricing proposals and incurred-cost submissions, including those of research firms on cost-type contracts, and advises contracting officers on what costs to accept. Its findings decide whether a research firm's accounting system is adequate and what indirect rates it will be paid.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Thousands of auditors (estimate)
**Output:** Audit reports and advisory opinions to contracting officers
**Proprietary data:** Contractors' cost records, rate histories and audit findings across the defense industrial base — contractor-proprietary and not shareable
**Clock:** A statutory deadline to complete incurred-cost audits within a year of an adequate submission (estimate of the FY2018 NDAA provision) and contracting officers' decision dates
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer
