# Raw Data That Leaves With the Postdoc

**Niche:** [[niches/contract-research-labs/experimental-records/profile|Experimental Records & Research Data]]
**Industry:** [[industries/contract-research-labs|Contract Research Labs]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** When a staff scientist or postdoc leaves, the meaning of their files leaves too, and the lab cannot reproduce results it has already reported to a sponsor.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #word-embeddings #k-nearest-neighbors #data-integration #quick-win #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to capture each experiment's conditions and raw data at the bench without slowing the scientist, and whoever makes that record complete enough to defend an invention date, a data-rights claim and a final report takes the account.

## The Problem
Folders named by the departed person's private convention, notebooks with abbreviations only they used, analysis scripts that depend on a file on their laptop. The sponsor asks a follow-up question on a reported result, and nobody can regenerate the figure.

## Why It's Still Broken
Exit procedures cover equipment and badges, not data; and no one has time to document while still employed.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A departure protocol run in the last month: an assistant inventories the person's files, clusters them by project, asks the person targeted questions about each cluster, and writes a short data guide linked to the files and the reports they fed. Done routinely, it becomes a running habit rather than an exit task.

## Who Feels the Pain
PIs answering sponsor questions; successors inheriting a project; the lab when a result is challenged.

## Impact If Fixed
A few hours of structured handover per departure saves weeks of reconstruction and keeps reported results defensible.
