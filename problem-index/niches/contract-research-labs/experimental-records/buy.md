# Electronic Notebooks Fitted to Sponsor and Export Segregation

**Niche:** [[niches/contract-research-labs/experimental-records/profile|Experimental Records & Research Data]]
**Industry:** [[industries/contract-research-labs|Contract Research Labs]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Electronic lab notebooks were built for life-science teams in one company, not for a lab where each record belongs to a different sponsor and some are export-controlled.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #compliance #data-integration #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to capture each experiment's conditions and raw data at the bench without slowing the scientist, and whoever makes that record complete enough to defend an invention date, a data-rights claim and a final report takes the account.

## The Problem
Labs buy an electronic notebook, scientists find it slower than paper for their work, and adoption stalls at the biology group. The compliance team then discovers the notebook cannot keep one sponsor's records invisible to staff on a competitor's project, or keep ITAR data in a US-only enclave.

## What Already Exists
Benchling, LabArchives, Revvity Signals and open-source notebooks, with templates, witnessing and audit trails.

## The Customization Gap
Contract labs need: record-level partitions by sponsor and agreement with walls between competing sponsors; export-control and CUI labels that restrict access and hosting; templates for physical-science and engineering work (test matrices, specimen genealogy, instrument settings) rather than plasmids and assays; witnessing that meets invention-disclosure practice; and an export of records with funding provenance for data-rights decisions.

## Target Customer
Chief scientists and security or compliance officers at labs with mixed government and commercial portfolios.

## Impact If Solved
A notebook scientists will actually use becomes the lab's legal record rather than an expensive duplicate of paper.
