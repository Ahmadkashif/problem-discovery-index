# Defense-Tech R&D Rollup Corporate Development

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-research-labs|Contract Research Labs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Private-equity-backed defense technology platforms and mid-tier defense contractors acquire SBIR-rich research firms for their technology, data rights and Phase III eligibility, and integrate them into larger product companies. Their corporate development teams screen targets and value SBIR portfolios.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~3–10 corporate development and integration staff per platform (estimate)
**Output:** Target screens, valuations and integration plans for internal use
**Proprietary data:** Diligence files on targets under NDA
**Clock:** Deal timelines
**Buyer:** Head of corporate development
