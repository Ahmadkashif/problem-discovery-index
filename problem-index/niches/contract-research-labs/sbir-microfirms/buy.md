# Opportunity Alerts Rebuilt for a Scientist-Founder

**Niche:** [[niches/contract-research-labs/sbir-microfirms/profile|SBIR-Funded Microfirms]]
**Industry:** [[industries/contract-research-labs|Contract Research Labs]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Government opportunity platforms are built for business-development professionals searching contracts by code and agency, not for a physicist who wants to know which topics match the last three papers they wrote.
**Tags:** #word-embeddings #transformers #k-nearest-neighbors #evaluation-metrics #quick-win #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor serving these firms is fighting to turn a capped number of proposal slots into Phase II awards — agencies set per-firm proposal limits from FY2027 — and whoever helps a firm pick the topics it can convert and set up Phase II from its Phase I results takes the account.

## The Problem
Keyword alerts miss topics phrased in another discipline's vocabulary and flood the founder with near-misses. Each agency runs its own portal and calendar, pre-release windows for talking to topic authors are short, and the founder hears about the best topic from a colleague after the window closed.

## What Already Exists
Deltek GovWin IQ, HigherGov, GovTribe and SBIR.gov search, with saved searches, alerts and award histories.

## The Customization Gap
For a scientist-founder: matching on technical meaning using the firm's own papers, reports and patents rather than keywords; one calendar across agencies with pre-release, question and close dates; the topic author's office and its past awards on similar topics; the firm's remaining proposal allowance per agency; and a price a three-person firm can pay.

## Target Customer
Founder-PIs and the one administrator at SBIR microfirms.

## Impact If Solved
The founder sees every genuinely relevant topic early enough to talk to the topic author, and stops spending scarce slots on near-misses.
