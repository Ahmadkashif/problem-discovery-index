# Nanoscale Research User Facilities

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-research-labs|Contract Research Labs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
DOE's Nanoscale Science Research Centers, NSF-funded shared nanofabrication and characterisation sites and university cleanrooms sell or grant tool time, training and staff-scientist support to outside users, including the nanotech spinouts and research firms of NAICS 541713. Staff scientists often run the hardest characterisation themselves.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–100 staff scientists and engineers per facility (estimate)
**Output:** Tool access, user support, characterisation results delivered to users
**Proprietary data:** Tool logs and process histories across many users — the results belong to the users
**Clock:** User proposal cycles and facility allocation calendars
**Buyer:** Facility director
