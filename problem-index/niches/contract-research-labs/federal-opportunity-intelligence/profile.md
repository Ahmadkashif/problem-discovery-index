# Federal R&D Opportunity Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-research-labs|Contract Research Labs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Deltek GovWin IQ, Bloomberg Government, HigherGov and GovTribe track federal budgets, forecast solicitations before release, profile agencies, incumbents and awards, and sell the result as subscriptions to contractors, including research firms scanning for R&D topics and broad agency announcements. GovWin's analysts call agency offices and write pre-solicitation intelligence; the newer entrants lean on public data and search.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~30–200 market analysts per large provider (estimate)
**Output:** Opportunity forecasts and profiles, agency and competitor analysis, alerts
**Proprietary data:** Analyst-gathered pre-solicitation intelligence and its history, layered on public budget, solicitation and award data
**Clock:** Agency solicitation releases, the President's budget and congressional budget documents
**Buyer:** Head of Content or Head of Research at the provider
