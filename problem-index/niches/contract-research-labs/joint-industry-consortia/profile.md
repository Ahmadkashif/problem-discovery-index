# Joint Industry Programmes & Research Consortia

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-research-labs|Contract Research Labs]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to produce pre-competitive test data no single member could afford to generate alone, and whoever turns each year's results into data members can drop straight into their own design and qualification decisions gets the renewals.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$170M a year in data management, member portals, analysis and reporting tooling and services for multi-client research programmes (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of contract research labs' tooling and services spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — test data is digital at source but delivered as annual PDF reports and slide decks
**Target Buyer:** Consortium or programme director at an institute
**Automation Potential:** High for data packaging and analysis, low for the test programme itself

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
In a joint industry programme a lab runs a shared research agenda — engine and fuel testing, materials fatigue, pipeline integrity, corrosion, battery abuse — funded by annual fees from many companies, often competitors. Members steer the agenda and share the results, which are confidential to the membership. The programme lives or dies on renewals, and members renew when the data is useful in their own engineering, not when the report is long.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Test-cell data acquisition systems; SharePoint or a members' extranet; Excel workbooks; annual reports and member meetings. Gaps: results delivered as figures rather than data; no consistent schema across years, so members cannot pool a decade of results; no view of which results members actually use.
