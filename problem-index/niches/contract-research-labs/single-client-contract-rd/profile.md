# Single-Client Contract R&D

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-research-labs|Contract Research Labs]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to answer one sponsor's technical question inside a fixed scope and hand over the results and IP with no ownership dispute, and whoever delivers a result the sponsor can act on with the cleanest handover gets the next statement of work.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$280M a year in scoping, agreement, project and reporting tooling and services for bilateral industry research (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~14% of contract research labs' tooling and services spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — accounting digital, scoping and reports in documents
**Target Buyer:** Division director or commercial program manager
**Automation Potential:** Medium — scoping from history and report drafting are automatable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A single sponsor brings a specific decision — will this alloy survive the duty cycle, why did this component fail, can this process scale — and buys a fixed scope of tests, modelling and analysis to inform it. The sponsor owns the foreground IP, judges the lab on whether the answer was usable and on time, and comes back if it was. Repeat sponsors are most of the business, so the contest is won project by project.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Project accounting in the institute ERP; agreements in contract-management tools; reports in Word. Gaps: effort estimates for fixed-price scopes made from memory; final reports organised around the work done rather than the decision the sponsor faces; no record of which past projects resembled this one and how they went.
