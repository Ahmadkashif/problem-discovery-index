# Research Security & Foreign-Influence Due Diligence Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-research-labs|Contract Research Labs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Analyst-led intelligence firms, Strider Technologies the most visible, build entity graphs of state-sponsored talent programmes, defence-linked universities, listed entities and corporate ownership chains from open and foreign-language sources, and screen researchers, applicants, partners and investors against them. Agencies pay them to screen SBIR and STTR applicants before award — the Department of the Air Force's commercial economic analysis office awarded Strider a contract worth up to $85M over five years for that work in October 2026 — and university and institute research security offices pay for researcher and partner screening.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~50–150 risk analysts, linguists and researchers at the largest provider; 10–40 at the next few (estimate; not published)
**Output:** Applicant and researcher risk assessments, partner due diligence reports, flagged-affiliation evidence packs, screening platform subscriptions
**Proprietary data:** Curated, analyst-verified entity and affiliation graph built from open and foreign-language sources, plus every screening decision and, where returned, the client's final adjudication
**Clock:** Screening must finish inside the agency's evaluation window before each award; P.L. 119-83 (April 2026) requires agencies to assess security risk and reject risky SBIR/STTR applicants; NSPM-33 programme certifications fall due agency by agency from mid-2026 into 2027
**Buyer:** Head of Research or Chief Analytics Officer at the provider (its own customers are agency SBIR due-diligence leads and university Research Security Officers)

**Kill switches:** none live for a corpus built on the provider's open-source entity graph and public outcomes (denials, Entity List and 1260H listings, enforcement actions). Agency adjudications and applicant disclosure forms are source-selection material that belongs to the agency and stays outside any reusable corpus, as with the program-office and SETA pockets in this sweep.
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. The assessment is the invoice and outcomes are observable but ungraded, the same shape as [[niches/grant-writers/grant-opportunity-prospect-platforms/profile|Grant Opportunity & Funder Research Platforms]] (51). What holds it at 49 is the buyer market: Strider and a handful of rivals, not a fragmented field, so Q5 is 3, as for federal opportunity intelligence in this sweep. Q4 is 4 because the award date belongs to the agency's evaluation cycle. Strongest near-miss in the sweep.
