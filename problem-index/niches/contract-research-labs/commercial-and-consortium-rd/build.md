# A Background-IP Ledger Across Sponsors

**Niche:** [[niches/contract-research-labs/commercial-and-consortium-rd/profile|Commercial & Consortium Contract R&D]]
**Industry:** [[industries/contract-research-labs|Contract Research Labs]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A lab that works for dozens of competing sponsors cannot say, for any piece of know-how, whether it is the lab's own background IP, one sponsor's foreground or a consortium's shared result.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #word-embeddings #graph-theory #compliance #data-integration
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every competitor is fighting to be the outside lab a company trusts with a technical problem it cannot solve in-house — but single-client work is won by solving one sponsor's problem inside a fixed scope and handing over results and IP cleanly, while consortium work is won by producing shared pre-competitive data that members keep paying for year after year. Different buyers, clocks and deliverables, so it is decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Research agreements distinguish background IP (what the lab brought), foreground IP (what the project created, usually the sponsor's) and, in consortia, results shared among members. Over years a lab's methods, test rigs, software and models accumulate contributions from many projects. When a new sponsor asks what it will own, the lab's answer is a lawyer's best reading of old agreements and a senior engineer's memory.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
IP management software tracks patents, and contract tools track agreements; neither tracks unpatented know-how — a calibration method, a test procedure, a simulation module — back to the projects that funded it. Labs live with the ambiguity until a dispute.

## What to Build
A ledger that reads the lab's agreements, proposals and final reports, extracts each defined deliverable and technology, and links reusable assets (methods, rigs, code, datasets) to the projects and funding that created them, with the ownership and licence terms attached. New statements of work are checked against it before signature.

## Target Customer
General counsel and division directors at independent research institutes and private contract labs serving competing industrial sponsors.

## Impact If Built
Clean answers to "what will we own" speed agreement negotiations by weeks (estimate) and remove the IP disputes that end repeat relationships — the thing both single-client and consortium work depend on.
