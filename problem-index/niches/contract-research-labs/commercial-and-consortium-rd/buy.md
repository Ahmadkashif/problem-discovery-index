# Services Automation for Milestone-Billed Research

**Niche:** [[niches/contract-research-labs/commercial-and-consortium-rd/profile|Commercial & Consortium Contract R&D]]
**Industry:** [[industries/contract-research-labs|Contract Research Labs]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Professional services automation tools assume billable hours on predictable tasks, while industry research is priced on milestones whose effort is uncertain and whose facilities are shared.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #linear-regression #feature-engineering #data-integration #revenue-impact #automation
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every competitor is fighting to be the outside lab a company trusts with a technical problem it cannot solve in-house — but single-client work is won by solving one sponsor's problem inside a fixed scope and handing over results and IP cleanly, while consortium work is won by producing shared pre-competitive data that members keep paying for year after year. Different buyers, clocks and deliverables, so it is decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
An industry project is typically fixed-price or milestone-billed, uses shared facilities (engine cells, environmental chambers, pilot plants) booked across many projects, and carries technical risk that makes effort uncertain. Project managers track it in a services tool that shows hours burned against budget, but not whether the technical milestone is on course or whether the test cell will be free when needed.

## What Already Exists
Professional services automation such as Kantata, Certinia and Planview, and institute ERPs with project modules.

## The Customization Gap
Research labs need: facility and equipment scheduling as a constrained resource shared across projects; milestone-based revenue recognition tied to technical acceptance; earned value against technical progress rather than hours; consortium billing with member tiers and annual renewals; and sponsor-specific confidentiality walls so one sponsor's project data is invisible to staff on a competitor's project.

## Target Customer
Commercial program managers and finance leads at independent institutes and private labs with large industry portfolios.

## Impact If Solved
Milestone slips and facility conflicts surface weeks earlier, write-offs on fixed-price research shrink, and consortium renewals are billed and tracked without a spreadsheet per programme.
