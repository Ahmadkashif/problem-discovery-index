## The Problem
Research firms reuse facilities descriptions, biographies, past-performance summaries and method descriptions across many proposals, which is exactly what response libraries do well. But reusable text in a research firm carries constraints commercial RFP content does not: export-controlled passages that cannot go to some agencies, text owned by a sponsor, and claims of prior results that must be current.

## What Already Exists
Loopio, Responsive (formerly RFPIO) and similar response-management platforms with AI drafting from content libraries.

## The Customization Gap
For research: library entries tagged by funding source, export-control status and owner; agency-specific formatting and section structures (technical volume, commercialisation plan, budget justification); automatic refresh of biographies and publication lists; prior-results claims linked to the report that supports them; and compliance checks against the specific solicitation rather than a generic questionnaire.

## Target Customer
Proposal managers and contracts administrators at research firms of 30–500 staff.

## Impact If Solved
Firms reuse what they safely can and nothing they should not, cutting assembly time without creating export-control or ownership problems.
