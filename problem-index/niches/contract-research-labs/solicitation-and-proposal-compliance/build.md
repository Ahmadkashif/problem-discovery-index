# One Calendar and Compliance Engine Across Every Agency

**Niche:** [[niches/contract-research-labs/solicitation-and-proposal-compliance/profile|Solicitation Monitoring & Proposal Compliance]]
**Industry:** [[industries/contract-research-labs|Contract Research Labs]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Every agency publishes its own solicitation rules in a long PDF, and every firm re-reads them to build its own checklist before every submission.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #word-embeddings #evaluation-metrics #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver every submission on the solicitation calendar with zero administrative defects — page limits, required volumes, cost-proposal formats, registrations current — and whoever does it with the least principal-investigator time takes the account.

## The Problem
A DoD SBIR cycle, an NSF solicitation, a NASA call and a DOE funding notice each specify format, volumes, page counts, budget rules, required certifications and disclosure forms, and the rules change between cycles. Firms rebuild checklists by hand and still miss changes buried in revised instructions.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Opportunity vendors stop at the alert; proposal software manages content, not rules; and no one has turned each agency's instructions into machine-checkable requirements that are kept current.

## What to Build
An engine that ingests every solicitation and its amendments, extracts requirements into structured checks (page limits per volume, font and margin rules, required forms and certifications, budget caps and cost-share rules, disclosure requirements), flags changes since the last cycle, maintains one calendar of pre-release, question and close dates across agencies, tracks each firm's remaining proposal allowance under the FY2027 caps, and checks a draft submission against the rules before upload.

## Target Customer
Proposal managers at research firms submitting 20 or more proposals a year across several agencies, and founders of smaller firms.

## Impact If Built
Administrative rejections fall toward zero and each proposal saves several hours of checklist work (estimate), freeing PI time for the technical volume that decides the award.
