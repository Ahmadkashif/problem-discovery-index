# Solicitation Monitoring & Proposal Compliance

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-research-labs|Contract Research Labs]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver every submission on the solicitation calendar with zero administrative defects — page limits, required volumes, cost-proposal formats, registrations current — and whoever does it with the least principal-investigator time takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M a year in opportunity alerts, proposal software, compliance review and proposal-production services bought by contract research labs (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of contract research labs' tooling and services spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — portals and alerts are digital; assembly and compliance checking are manual
**Target Buyer:** Proposal manager or contracts administrator; the founder at small firms
**Automation Potential:** High — monitoring, matching, format and form compliance, and boilerplate assembly are rule-based

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A research firm bidding across DoD, NASA, DOE, NSF and NIH faces a dozen portals, each with its own calendar, page limits, fonts, required volumes and forms, and the foreign-affiliation and security disclosures that agencies now screen before award. Missing a form or a page limit can get a proposal rejected without technical review. This is the most mechanical part of the research business, and it sits on the scarcest people's time. The grant-writing market's version is covered by [[industries/grant-writers|Grant Writers]]; here the buyer is the performing lab.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Agency portals and SBIR.gov; GovWin IQ, HigherGov and GovTribe alerts; proposal content tools such as Loopio and Responsive; Word templates and checklists. Gaps: no single calendar across agencies; compliance checked by eye the night before; boilerplate copied from whichever old proposal is nearest; proposal caps per agency tracked in nobody's system.
