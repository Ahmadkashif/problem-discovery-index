# The Proposal Rejected Before Anyone Read It

**Niche:** [[niches/contract-research-labs/solicitation-and-proposal-compliance/profile|Solicitation Monitoring & Proposal Compliance]]
**Industry:** [[industries/contract-research-labs|Contract Research Labs]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A proposal that took a PI ~40 hours (estimate) is rejected for an extra page, a missing certification or an outdated disclosure form, and the technical idea never reaches a reviewer.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #compliance #quick-win #automation #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver every submission on the solicitation calendar with zero administrative defects — page limits, required volumes, cost-proposal formats, registrations current — and whoever does it with the least principal-investigator time takes the account.

## The Problem
Administrative rejections are painful precisely because they are avoidable: a technical volume one page over, a budget that breaches a cap, a disclosure form left out or superseded by a new version. With foreign-affiliation disclosures now screened before award and proposal caps arriving in FY2027, a rejected proposal also wastes a slot.

## Why It's Still Broken
Checks happen at 11 p.m. on the closing night by the same exhausted person who wrote the proposal, against instructions they last read weeks ago.

## What a Fix Looks Like
An automated pre-submission check against the solicitation's current rules: page counts per volume, fonts, required sections, forms and certifications, budget caps, and disclosure versions, run the moment a draft is uploaded to the firm's workspace and again before submission, with each failure explained.

## Who Feels the Pain
PIs who lose weeks of work to a formatting error; founders who lose a slot; proposal managers blamed for missing it.

## Impact If Fixed
A few minutes of automated checking removes the most avoidable loss in the proposal process.
