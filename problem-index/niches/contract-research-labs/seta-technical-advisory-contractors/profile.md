# SETA & Technical Advisory Contractors to R&D Agencies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-research-labs|Contract Research Labs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Systems engineering and technical assistance (SETA) contractors embed scientists and engineers with DARPA, the service research labs and other R&D agencies to help program managers write solicitations, evaluate proposals technically, monitor performers and assess results. They sit between the funder and the research labs, and see every performer's proposals and progress.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~100–1,000 technical advisors across the firms supporting the main R&D agencies (estimate)
**Output:** Technical evaluations, program assessments and analyses, billed as labour hours
**Proprietary data:** None of their own — proposals, source-selection material and performer data belong to the government and are often controlled
**Clock:** Agency solicitation, evaluation and budget cycles
**Buyer:** SETA program manager at the contractor
