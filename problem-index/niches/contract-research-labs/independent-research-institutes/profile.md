# Independent Nonprofit Research Institutes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/contract-research-labs|Contract Research Labs]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
This entry records the operator check. The large independent institutes — Battelle, SRI International (~$438M revenue in 2024 on 990 data, as reported), Southwest Research Institute (~$966M research volume and 3,200 staff in FY2025), Draper, RTI International and university-affiliated institutes such as the Georgia Tech Research Institute — sell research itself to government and industry sponsors. Unlike most operators in the vault, they clear the headcount gate easily; the question is whether their research can be sold to as an insight engine.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Hundreds to thousands of researchers per institute; the function that would buy an insight engine is the institute's own business development and research management, ~10–50 people (estimate)
**Output:** Contract research reports, test data, prototypes and software delivered to sponsors
**Proprietary data:** Decades of proposals, project records and reports — but results largely belong to sponsors, carry government rights, or are export-controlled
**Clock:** Solicitation close dates and contract milestones, mostly set by sponsors' programme schedules
**Buyer:** Chief research officer or division vice president

**Kill switches:** data isn't theirs to use (sponsor-owned foreground and government data rights) and ITAR and CUI controls on defense work stall any procurement of outside tooling.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. The research is the invoice, but each project is bespoke, the archive belongs to sponsors or sits behind export controls, and there are only a few dozen buyers of this size.
