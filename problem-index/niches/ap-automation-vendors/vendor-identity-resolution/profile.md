# Vendor Identity Resolution

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ap-automation-vendors|AP Automation Vendors]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to determine which records refer to the same supplier across a decade of duplicates, abbreviations, mergers and typos — and whoever resolves most accurately using what every other buyer knows owns the record everything else depends on.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — exact-match checks only
**Target Buyer:** Data and operations leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — entity resolution with external evidence

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This contest is inference about identity. Given records with inconsistent names, tax identifiers that are sometimes present, addresses that changed, and invoice histories that overlap, determine which refer to the same commercial entity — and keep that determination correct as suppliers merge, rebrand and restructure. It is a well-defined entity resolution problem with an unusually strong external evidence source, and it is not a security problem.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Exact-match duplicate checks, manual searching before creation, and periodic cleanup projects. The gaps: no fuzzy or probabilistic matching; tax and bank identifiers not used as evidence; cross-buyer resolution unused; corporate structure unmodelled; and no confidence expressed on any match.
