**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to determine which records refer to the same supplier across a decade of duplicates, abbreviations, mergers and typos — and whoever resolves most accurately using what every other buyer knows owns the record everything else depends on.

## The Problem
Commercial business identity is a developed industry: universal identifiers, corporate hierarchy data, registry coverage across jurisdictions, and matching services that resolve a messy name and address to a known entity. Credit, compliance and procurement functions all use it. AP automation, whose core record is a supplier identity, generally does not, and matches on whatever string the invoice contained.

## What Already Exists
Business identity databases with universal identifiers; corporate hierarchy and ownership data; national and state registry coverage; commercial matching services; and the compliance screening infrastructure built on top.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a transactional record rather than a credit file. It requires: (1) invoice and payment behaviour as matching evidence, which registries do not have and which is often more discriminating than name and address — this is the substantive contribution; (2) resolution at invoice speed and cost, since paying a lookup fee per invoice is not viable and the network can substitute; (3) coverage of the small suppliers registries handle poorly, which is where most of the duplicate problem lives; (4) hierarchy that reflects how the buyer transacts rather than how ownership is structured, because a division that invoices separately is operationally separate; and (5) continuous maintenance as the file changes, rather than a one-off enrichment.

## Target Customer
Data and operations leadership, procurement and compliance teams, and business identity vendors for whom transactional evidence is unavailable.

## Impact If Solved
The registry industry resolves identity from name, address and ownership. Invoice and payment behaviour is stronger evidence for exactly the small suppliers registries cover worst, and the platform is the only party holding it.
