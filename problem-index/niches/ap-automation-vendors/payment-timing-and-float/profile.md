# Payment Timing & Float

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ap-automation-vendors|AP Automation Vendors]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to decide when each invoice should be paid by which method, balancing discount capture, working capital and supplier relationship — and whoever optimises it honestly, while float pays for the product, holds the category's central conflict.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~6% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — scheduled runs, unoptimised
**Target Buyer:** Payment operations leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — a well-posed optimisation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
When an invoice is paid, and by what method, determines whether an early payment discount is captured, how much working capital is tied up, what the payment costs, and whether the supplier relationship holds. Most platforms run payments on a fixed schedule with method chosen by default, and increasingly monetise float and payment method — which means the party choosing the timing benefits from one particular answer. That conflict, and the optimisation nobody runs, define the niche.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Scheduled payment runs, method defaults, discount terms recorded in the vendor record and occasional dynamic discounting offers. The gaps: discounts recorded and not captured; timing not optimised against cost of capital; method chosen by default rather than by economics; float incentives undisclosed; and no measurement of discounts missed.
