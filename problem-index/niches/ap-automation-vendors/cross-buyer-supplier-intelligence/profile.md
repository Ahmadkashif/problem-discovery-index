# Cross-Buyer Supplier Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ap-automation-vendors|AP Automation Vendors]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn the sight of one supplier invoicing hundreds of buyers into an assessment of that supplier nobody else can produce — and whoever builds it holds the most complete record of business-to-business commerce outside a bank.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — asset unexploited
**Target Buyer:** Data and commercial leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — aggregation over data already held

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The platform sees who buys what from whom, at what price, on what terms, how reliably they pay, and how all of it changes. Across its customer base it sees the same supplier invoicing hundreds of different buyers, which means it can observe a supplier's price dispersion, its payment behaviour, its bank detail changes and its financial trajectory from the outside. It uses this to process invoices.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Per-customer spend reporting and vendor lists. The gaps: no cross-buyer supplier view; price dispersion invisible; supplier distress signals unmodelled; concentration and dependency risk unassessed; and the network's own evidence unused even for verification.
