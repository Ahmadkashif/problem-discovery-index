# Trade Credit and Supplier Risk Data

**Industry:** [[industries/ap-automation-vendors|AP Automation Vendors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Trade credit agencies built businesses on pooled payment experience data, and AP platforms hold richer data and pool none of it.
**Tags:** #data-integration #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #time-series-forecasting #compliance #gradient-boosting #revenue-impact #descriptive-statistics
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn the sight of one supplier invoicing hundreds of buyers into an assessment of that supplier nobody else can produce — and whoever builds it holds the most complete record of business-to-business commerce outside a bank.

## The Problem
Trade credit agencies built substantial businesses on exactly this idea: collect payment experience from many suppliers about many buyers, aggregate it, and sell risk assessments. The contribution model, the governance, the scoring methodology and the commercial framing are all established. AP platforms hold the same relationship data at line-item granularity, updated daily, already inside one system, and have built none of it.

## What Already Exists
Trade credit databases built on pooled payment experience; contribution and reciprocity models; business risk scoring methodologies; supplier risk and monitoring products; and the legal frameworks around commercial credit data.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to data held rather than contributed, and to the supplier side. It requires: (1) governance for data already held under software agreements rather than contributed under data-sharing ones, which is the binding legal question and is different from the agency model; (2) the supplier as the subject rather than the buyer, since trade credit assesses who pays late and this asset can assess who supplies reliably; (3) line-item and price detail, which agencies never see and which enables price dispersion analysis; (4) no contribution incentive to engineer, because the data arrives regardless, replacing the usual problem with a consent one; and (5) real-time signal rather than periodic reporting.

## Target Customer
Data and commercial leadership, procurement and risk functions, trade credit agencies and insurers, and supplier risk vendors.

## Impact If Solved
The agency model proved the value of pooled commercial experience and had to assemble it. A pool that already exists inside one platform inverts the hard part into a consent question nobody has answered.
