## The Problem
Master data management solved this shape: golden record construction, survivorship rules, match and merge with confidence thresholds, data stewardship workflow, and quality measurement with governance. It works and it is sold as an enterprise programme costing more and taking longer than most AP automation deployments. The mid-market customer with a decade-old vendor file will never run one.

## What Already Exists
MDM platforms with match and merge; golden record and survivorship rules; stewardship workflow; data quality scoring and dashboards; and governance frameworks.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is MDM delivered as a feature rather than as a programme. It requires: (1) external reference evidence from the cross-buyer network rather than only internal record comparison, which is the substantive advantage and is something no MDM deployment has; (2) automatic operation with minimal stewardship, since the customer has no data steward and never will; (3) an adversarial dimension, because a fraudulent record is a deliberate corruption rather than a data quality issue and MDM has no threat model; (4) the customer's ERP remaining the system of record, so the resolution must inform rather than replace; and (5) resolution delivered at the moment of invoice or onboarding rather than in a batch cleanup.

## Target Customer
Data and operations leadership, AP and procurement teams, and MDM vendors for whom the mid-market vendor file is unreachable.

## Impact If Solved
MDM is mature and sold as a programme most of this market cannot buy. Delivering it as an automatic feature backed by cross-buyer reference evidence is the adaptation, and the adversarial dimension is genuinely new.
