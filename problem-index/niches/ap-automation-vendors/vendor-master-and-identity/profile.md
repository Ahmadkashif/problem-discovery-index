# Vendor Master & Identity

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ap-automation-vendors|AP Automation Vendors]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to know who a vendor actually is and whether their payment details are real — and the contest splits cleanly enough that it is not terminal.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.1B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~22% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — a decade of accumulated records
**Target Buyer:** Data and risk leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — both halves unexploited

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The vendor master holds the same supplier under four names, with three tax identifiers and two bank accounts, accumulated over a decade of acquisitions, typos, rebrands and duplicate onboarding. It is the root cause of a large share of invoice exceptions and it is the surface that business email compromise attacks, because changing a bank detail on a vendor record is how six-figure wires are stolen. Two problems, one record, and they are contested differently.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Master file maintenance screens, duplicate checks on exact matches, callback procedures and training. The gaps: no entity resolution across records; cross-buyer evidence unused; bank detail changes verified by phone call; no risk scoring on changes; and no measure of master data quality.
