# Exception Management From Manufacturing

**Niche:** [[niches/ap-automation-vendors/invoice-exception-handling/profile|Invoice Exception Handling]]
**Industry:** [[industries/ap-automation-vendors|AP Automation Vendors]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Manufacturing quality management treats a defect as something to trace to a root cause and eliminate, and AP treats an exception as something to process.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #evaluation-metrics #descriptive-statistics #automation #confidence-intervals #hypothesis-testing #compliance #gradient-boosting
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to resolve the fifteen to thirty percent of invoices that survive automation — and whoever learns from how humans resolved the last million of them automates the part the category has never touched.

## The Problem
Manufacturing developed a serious discipline around defects: classification taxonomies, root cause analysis, Pareto prioritisation, corrective and preventive action, and the principle that a recurring defect is a process failure rather than a workload. AP exception handling has the same structure — a stream of items that failed a check, each requiring intervention — and treats it as throughput. Nobody asks why the same vendor produces the same exception every month.

## What Already Exists
Defect classification taxonomies; root cause analysis methods; Pareto and priority analysis; corrective and preventive action processes; and statistical process control.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to defects originating outside the organisation. It requires: (1) root causes that sit with suppliers and with the buyer's own purchasing practices rather than on a production line, which is the substantive difference — corrective action means changing someone else's behaviour; (2) a defect taxonomy for invoice exceptions that does not exist and must be built, which is the first and most valuable step; (3) process control across many customers, where the platform can see that a supplier causes exceptions at every buyer; (4) resolution that is a judgement rather than a repair, so the record is a decision and not a measurement; and (5) no production stoppage, which removes the natural forcing function that makes manufacturing take defects seriously.

## Target Customer
Product and operations leadership, AP managers, procurement teams who could fix the upstream causes, and process improvement vendors with no AP presence.

## Impact If Solved
Manufacturing treats a recurring defect as a process failure and AP treats it as workload. Building the exception taxonomy and attributing root causes is the transfer, and the cross-customer view makes it stronger than the original.
