# Invoice Exception Handling

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ap-automation-vendors|AP Automation Vendors]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to resolve the fifteen to thirty percent of invoices that survive automation — and whoever learns from how humans resolved the last million of them automates the part the category has never touched.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.3B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~26% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — routed to a person
**Target Buyer:** Product and operations leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — an unread corpus of resolutions

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Capture works. What survives it is the exception: a purchase order that does not match the invoice quantity, a service invoice with no purchase order at all, a vendor name that differs from the master file, a tax line computed differently, a near-duplicate. Each goes to a person who resolves it, usually by emailing someone, and the resolution is recorded as a status change. This is where the cost of accounts payable actually lives, and it is the part the category's automation claim does not cover.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Matching rules, tolerance settings, exception queues and status workflow. The gaps: resolutions never captured as data; the same exception recurring monthly; no prediction of which invoices will except; no root cause analysis by vendor or type; and no measure of resolution cost.
