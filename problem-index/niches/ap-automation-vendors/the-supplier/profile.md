# The Supplier

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ap-automation-vendors|AP Automation Vendors]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make the supplier's side of the transaction worth their cooperation — and whoever does it gets accurate invoices, current details and reachable contacts, which is most of the buyer's problem solved.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$350M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — portals built for the buyer
**Target Buyer:** Network and product leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — status, self-service and submission quality

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every AP platform has a supplier portal, and every supplier hates them. They are built to serve the buyer: collect documents, accept invoices in a required format, display a payment status that is often wrong. A supplier working with two hundred customers has two hundred portals with two hundred logins. Yet the supplier controls the quality of the invoice, the currency of the bank details and the availability of a contact — which are exactly the inputs whose absence causes the buyer's exceptions and fraud exposure.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Supplier portals, invoice submission forms, remittance emails and support queues. The gaps: payment status unreliable or absent; submission errors surfaced after rejection rather than at entry; no reuse across buyers; the supplier's contact never verified; and the supplier's experience unmeasured.
