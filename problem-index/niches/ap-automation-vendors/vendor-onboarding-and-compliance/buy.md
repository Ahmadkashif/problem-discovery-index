**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Financial services built know-your-customer utilities and reusable verification to stop collecting the same evidence repeatedly, and supplier onboarding collects it every time.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to onboard a supplier once, verify them properly, and never ask them for the same document again — and whoever does it across a network collects the evidence every buyer currently gathers separately.

## The Problem
Financial services faced exactly this: every institution collecting the same identity and ownership evidence from the same corporate customers, repeatedly, at great cost. The response was KYC utilities, shared verification services, reusable credential models and standards for what constitutes adequate evidence. Supplier onboarding has the same duplication at greater volume and has adopted none of it.

## What Already Exists
KYC utilities and shared verification services; reusable digital credential models; sanctions and watchlist screening with ongoing monitoring; beneficial ownership verification; and the standards defining sufficient evidence.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a commercial rather than a regulatory obligation. It requires: (1) requirements set by buyer policy rather than by regulation, so the evidence set is negotiable and varies — which is why no standard emerged and is the substantive work; (2) suppliers who are small businesses rather than institutional counterparties, so the process must be light enough that they complete it; (3) evidence that includes insurance, tax and trade documents rather than only identity, which is a broader set than KYC contemplates; (4) no regulator forcing adoption, so the network must be assembled on commercial value alone; and (5) the payment detail as the highest-value verified element, which KYC treats as incidental.

## Target Customer
Vendor operations and compliance leadership, suppliers, procurement functions, and identity and KYC vendors for whom supplier onboarding is adjacent.

## Impact If Solved
Financial services built utilities because a regulator made the duplication intolerable. The supplier version has no such forcing function, which makes the commercial design the real work and the payment detail the element worth verifying most.
