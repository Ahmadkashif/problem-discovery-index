# Vendor Onboarding & Compliance

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ap-automation-vendors|AP Automation Vendors]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to onboard a supplier once, verify them properly, and never ask them for the same document again — and whoever does it across a network collects the evidence every buyer currently gathers separately.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$450M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — forms, email and PDFs
**Target Buyer:** Vendor operations leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — reusable verified credentials

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Before a supplier can be paid they must be onboarded: tax forms, banking details, insurance certificates, sanctions screening, diversity and compliance attestations, and whatever the buyer's policy adds. Every buyer collects the same documents from the same supplier independently, by email, and stores them where they expire unnoticed. The supplier fills in the same forms for every customer. It is the clearest duplicated-effort problem in business-to-business commerce.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Onboarding forms, document upload, sanctions screening services and a spreadsheet of expiry dates. The gaps: nothing reusable across buyers; documents not validated on receipt; expiries not tracked reliably; screening run once rather than continuously; and the supplier's experience never considered.
