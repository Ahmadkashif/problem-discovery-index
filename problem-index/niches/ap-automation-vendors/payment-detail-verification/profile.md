# Payment Detail Verification

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ap-automation-vendors|AP Automation Vendors]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to confirm that a bank detail change is genuine using evidence an attacker cannot forge — and whoever replaces the callback to a number from the email itself removes the category's largest concentrated loss.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$500M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — a phone call and training
**Target Buyer:** Risk and payment operations leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — corroboration against network evidence

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This contest is adversarial. Business email compromise works by persuading someone to change a supplier's bank details, and the control is a callback to a phone number that may have come from the same fraudulent email. The losses are large, concentrated and embarrassing, and the entire industry handles the problem with training. The task is independent corroboration against evidence the attacker does not control, which is a security problem rather than a data quality one.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Callback procedures, dual approval, training modules and payment holds. The gaps: verification against the caller's own supplied details; no cross-buyer corroboration; change risk not scored; social engineering patterns undetected; and no measurement of how often the control would fail a test.
