# ERP & Dimension Mapping

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ap-automation-vendors|AP Automation Vendors]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to map a customer's chart of accounts, dimensions and custom fields without six weeks of a consultant — and whoever makes the integration cover the non-standard fields wins the deals that die in implementation.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$650M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — consultants and mapping spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** Implementation leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — semantic mapping across thousands of instances

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The demo integration covers the standard fields. The customer's actual configuration — their chart of accounts, their departments, their project and class dimensions, their custom fields, their approval hierarchies, their tax handling — is mapped by a consultant over six weeks. That gap between the demo and the deployment is where implementations slip, where deals are lost, and where a large share of the category's service cost sits.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Standard connectors, field mapping screens, implementation questionnaires and consultant time. The gaps: no reuse of mappings across similar customers; custom fields treated as bespoke every time; ERP version differences discovered in flight; no validation that a mapping is correct before go-live; and no measurement of implementation time by cause.
