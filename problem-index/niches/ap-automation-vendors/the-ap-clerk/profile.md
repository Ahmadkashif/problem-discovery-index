# The AP Clerk

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ap-automation-vendors|AP Automation Vendors]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stop the clerk's job being email to people who do not reply — and whoever gets the answer without the chase changes what the role is.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$450M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — a queue and an inbox
**Target Buyer:** Product and customer leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — answer retrieval and chase automation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The clerk's job is not processing invoices. It is sending emails to people who do not reply, about invoices that cannot move until they do: which cost centre, was this received, did you approve this, is this the right purchase order, who ordered it. They chase approvers, requesters, suppliers and warehouse staff, none of whom report to them and all of whom have other priorities. The software processes the invoices that do not need them and leaves them the correspondence.

## Current Tools & Gaps
An exception queue, an email client, reminder templates and a spreadsheet of who owes what. The gaps: answers not retrieved from data the system already holds; chasing done manually; no escalation path with authority; no visibility of who is blocking what; and no measure of how the clerk's day is spent.
