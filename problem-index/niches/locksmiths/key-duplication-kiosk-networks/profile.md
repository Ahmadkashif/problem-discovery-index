# Automated Key Duplication Kiosk Networks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/locksmiths|Locksmiths]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Self-service kiosks in supermarkets, big-box and convenience stores that photograph a customer's key, identify the keyway and bitting with computer vision, and cut a copy on the spot. KeyMe Locksmiths passed 8,000 kiosks and more than 47.2 million keys duplicated in March 2026, in retailers including Kroger, Walmart and 7-Eleven. Retailers host the kiosks for a share of revenue; consumers pay per key. The networks take the simplest work from locksmith counters and hardware stores.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 computer-vision, data and product engineers at the largest network (estimate)
**Output:** Duplicated keys; image-based key identification; kiosk placement and pricing analytics
**Proprietary data:** Tens of millions of key images with keyway and bitting, and where and when people copy keys
**Clock:** None set externally
**Buyer:** Chief technology officer
