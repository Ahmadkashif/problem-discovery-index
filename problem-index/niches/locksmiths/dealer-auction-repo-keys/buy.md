# Reconditioning Workflow Tools Extended to Keys

**Niche:** [[niches/locksmiths/dealer-auction-repo-keys/profile|Dealer, Auction & Repossession Key Work]]
**Industry:** [[industries/locksmiths|Locksmiths]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Dealers and auctions already track every unit through reconditioning steps (inspection, mechanical, detail, photos); keys are a checkbox, not a step with a vendor, a part and a date.
**Tags:** #feature-engineering #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to put a working key in every keyless unit on a dealer lot, auction lane or repossession yard before it sells, and whoever turns around the most VINs per visit at a fixed per-unit price takes the account.

## The Problem
A unit's key status is noted on a check-in sheet or condition report: one key, no key, no remote. Ordering a key means a phone call or email to the locksmith with a list, and the result comes back on an invoice. In between, nobody can see whether the key is on its way.

## What Already Exists
Reconditioning workflow software at dealers, auction condition-report and inspection systems, and dealer management systems that hold inventory and repair orders.

## The Customization Gap
The extension makes keys a first-class reconditioning step. Each unit carries key count and type and a decoded key part. Missing keys route automatically to the contracted locksmith with the VIN batch, the locksmith's feasibility and price come back per unit, completion is tracked against the sale date, and the result updates the condition report. For repossessed units it records the ownership documents the NASTF rules require.

## Target Customer
Independent dealer groups, wholesale auctions and [[industries/repossession-services|repossession agencies]] that already run reconditioning or assignment software.

## Impact If Solved
Key work stops being an untracked phone call and becomes a step that finishes before the sale, inside the buyer's existing system.
