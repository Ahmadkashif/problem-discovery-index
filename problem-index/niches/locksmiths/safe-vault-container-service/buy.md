# Branch Maintenance Platforms Extended to Vaults and Safe Locks

**Niche:** [[niches/locksmiths/safe-vault-container-service/profile|Safe, Vault & Security Container Service]]
**Industry:** [[industries/locksmiths|Locksmiths]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Banks already manage branch repairs in facility work-order platforms that treat a vault door like a light fixture.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #descriptive-statistics #data-integration #compliance #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a locked-out safe, vault or security container open and back in service with the least damage, and whoever opens the most units without destroying the lock or the container's rating takes the account.

## The Problem
Branch networks route safe, vault and ATM-safe calls through the same facility platform as plumbing and HVAC. The ticket records the branch and a free-text description; the safe technician closes it with a note. Nothing captures lock model, rating, combination-change events or the method used.

## What Already Exists
Facility work-order platforms used by bank and retail networks, such as ServiceChannel and Corrigo, and bank security systems that log alarms and time-lock events.

## The Customization Gap
The extension adds containers as security assets. Each carries rating, lock type and serial, with restricted-visibility fields for method and combination-change records, logged without the combination itself. Service codes should be specific (electronic lock failure, time lock fault, bolt-work jam, drilled and repaired with rating restored), and link to the bank's security event log so a time-lock fault and its service visit sit together. Role-based access keeps method details from general facilities staff.

## Target Customer
Bank and retail security officers who already run a facility platform, and their safe and vault vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Container service becomes visible and auditable inside existing systems, without a separate tool for a small category.
