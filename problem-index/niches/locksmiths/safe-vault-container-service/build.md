# A Service and Lockout History for Every Container

**Niche:** [[niches/locksmiths/safe-vault-container-service/profile|Safe, Vault & Security Container Service]]
**Industry:** [[industries/locksmiths|Locksmiths]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A bank's ATM safes and vault time locks across two hundred branches have no shared service history, so the technician called to a lockout cannot see that this lock failed the same way last spring.
**Tags:** #survival-analysis #gradient-boosting #descriptive-statistics #data-integration #compliance #tacit-knowledge-ml
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a locked-out safe, vault or security container open and back in service with the least damage, and whoever opens the most units without destroying the lock or the container's rating takes the account.

## The Problem
Safe and vault lockouts are rare per unit but frequent across a fleet: a dead electronic lock, a time lock that won't release, a combination changed and forgotten. Each costs branch downtime and, if drilled, a repair and possibly a rating question for the insurer. Service records are kept per job, by whichever vendor attended, so failure patterns by lock model, age and environment stay invisible. So does the knowledge of which opening method worked.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The market is small and secretive. Technicians guard methods, and banks treat container data as security-sensitive. Bank facilities systems track the branch, not the container, and federal container records are paper.

## What to Build
A secure container registry for banks, retailers and agencies with many safes. It records make, model, lock type, rating, install date, every service and lockout, and the method used and its outcome, in restricted fields visible only to credentialed technicians. Failure-time models by lock model and age schedule preventive replacement of locks likely to fail. At a lockout, the attending technician sees the unit's history and the methods that worked on its siblings. For federal containers the record includes the repair and recertification paperwork.

## Target Customer
Safe and vault service firms holding multi-branch bank or retail contracts, and the security officers who buy those contracts.

## Impact If Built
Fewer emergency lockouts through planned lock replacement, faster openings with less damage, and a documented rating trail per container for insurers and auditors.
