# Master-Key Systems & Key Control

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/locksmiths|Locksmiths]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to hold the authoritative record of who can open which door, and whoever can answer a lost master or a termination with a costed rekey plan the same day takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M a year of master-key system design, restricted and patented key systems, interchangeable cores, rekeying and key-control services sold by locksmiths (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~11% of 561622 receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — bitting charts in binders or one-off spreadsheets, key-issue logs on paper or in a key cabinet, and system knowledge in the designing locksmith's head
**Target Buyer:** Lock shop supervisor or facilities director at a university, hospital, school district or government campus; property manager of large commercial buildings
**Automation Potential:** High for system modelling, cross-keying checks, exposure analysis and rekey planning; low for the pinning work itself

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A master-key system is a long-lived design asset. Once a campus standardises on a keyway and hierarchy it may run for decades, with every renovation, lost key and departure handled within it. Restricted and patented keyways (Medeco, Mul-T-Lock, Primus and others) add authorisation rules: only named signatories may order duplicates. Interchangeable-core systems let a lock shop swap a core in seconds instead of re-pinning. The buyer is often an in-house lock shop. Colleges and universities employed 1,090 locksmiths in May 2023 and schools 620, with an outside locksmith or the manufacturer's factory keying service holding the design. The contest is the record: whoever holds the live system can answer exposure and change questions nobody else can.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Small master-keying programs (MKExpress, Aero Key), manufacturers' factory keying and registration services, key cabinets and tracking (KeyTrak, Traka), CMMS door registers and spreadsheets. The gaps are set out in [[problems/locksmiths/low-impact-1|Master-Key Systems Kept in Binders]]: no model of which doors a given key opens, no cross-keying audit across the whole system, and no costed rekey planning.
