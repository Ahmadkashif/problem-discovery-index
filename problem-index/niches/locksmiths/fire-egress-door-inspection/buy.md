# Fire Alarm Inspection Software Extended to Doors

**Niche:** [[niches/locksmiths/fire-egress-door-inspection/profile|Fire & Egress Door Inspection and Deficiency Repair]]
**Industry:** [[industries/locksmiths|Locksmiths]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Fire-protection inspection software already schedules annual inspections, captures device results and reports deficiencies to authorities; doors fit it badly because every opening is a different assembly.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #cnns #compliance #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn the annual NFPA 80 fire-door inspection into closed deficiencies before the fire marshal or accreditation surveyor arrives, and whoever converts the most findings into documented repairs inside that window takes the account.

## The Problem
Facilities already buy inspection software for sprinklers and alarms, and some jurisdictions collect third-party inspection reports through portals. Fire doors are handled in the same tools as a generic device with pass or fail, or left on paper. The result does not support the repair conversation.

## What Already Exists
Inspection platforms such as Inspect Point and BuildingReports handle scheduling, barcoded device inventories, mobile checklists, deficiency reporting and customer portals for fire alarm, sprinkler and extinguisher work. Some include fire-door checklists.

## The Customization Gap
Doors need their own asset model and logic. Each record should hold the assembly (door, frame, hardware set, rating and label data) rather than a single device. Photo-first capture should cover labels and clearances. Deficiency codes should map to NFPA 80 items and to door-hardware repairs, with parts lists from hardware catalogues and pricing from the locksmith's book. Records should separate "field modification voids the label" from "adjustable defect", and egress checks should run alongside fire checks. Without this, the platform records that a door failed but not what it will take to pass.

## Target Customer
Locksmith firms that already use, or sell alongside, a fire-protection contractor's inspection platform, and facility clients who want one compliance portal.

## Impact If Solved
One portal for all life-safety inspections, with door deficiencies quoted and tracked like alarm deficiencies, and no second system to buy.
