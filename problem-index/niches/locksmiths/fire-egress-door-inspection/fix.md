# The Deficiency List That Never Became a Work Order

**Niche:** [[niches/locksmiths/fire-egress-door-inspection/profile|Fire & Egress Door Inspection and Deficiency Repair]]
**Industry:** [[industries/locksmiths|Locksmiths]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The inspection report lists 140 deficient fire doors; six months later nobody can say which were fixed, because the report was a PDF and the repairs were separate tickets.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #descriptive-statistics #compliance #workflow-orchestration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn the annual NFPA 80 fire-door inspection into closed deficiencies before the fire marshal or accreditation surveyor arrives, and whoever converts the most findings into documented repairs inside that window takes the account.

## The Problem
A facility receives its fire-door inspection as a long PDF. The maintenance team fixes some items, the locksmith is called for others, and a few doors are replaced in a renovation. When the surveyor arrives and asks about a door on the report, the facility has to reconstruct whether it was repaired, by whom and when.

## Why It's Still Broken
Inspection and repair run in different systems and often through different vendors. Reports are written for reading, not tracking, and nothing ties a repair ticket back to the line on last year's report.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Issue each deficiency as a tracked item with the door's identifier, the NFPA 80 reference, a photo and a status. Any repair ticket on that door closes or updates the item with an after photo. The facility and the locksmith both see one open-items list by building, which can be printed for the surveyor.

## Who Feels the Pain
Life-safety compliance managers facing surveys; maintenance teams repairing doors without knowing which items they close; locksmiths whose repair work goes uncredited.

## Impact If Fixed
A small change in how findings are issued turns an annual report into a live list. The facility can show a surveyor every deficiency closed with evidence, and the locksmith can see exactly which repairs are still open.
