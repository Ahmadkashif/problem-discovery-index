# Fire & Egress Door Inspection and Deficiency Repair

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/locksmiths|Locksmiths]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn the annual NFPA 80 fire-door inspection into closed deficiencies before the fire marshal or accreditation surveyor arrives, and whoever converts the most findings into documented repairs inside that window takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$200M a year of fire and egress door inspection and the deficiency repairs that follow, performed by locksmith and door-hardware firms (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of 561622 receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — inspections run on paper or spreadsheet checklists, and reports go to clients as PDFs
**Target Buyer:** Director of facilities or life-safety compliance manager at hospitals, nursing homes, schools and high-rise owners
**Automation Potential:** High for documentation, deficiency coding and quoting; the repair stays manual

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
NFPA 80 has required an annual inspection of every fire door assembly since its 2007 edition, and health facilities surveyed under CMS's Life Safety Code have had to complete them since July 2017. The standard asks for a "qualified person" without naming a credential, so DHI's Fire and Egress Door Assembly Inspector (FDAI) certification has become the working proof. A typical hospital has hundreds to thousands of rated openings (estimate). Common findings are painted-over or missing labels, oversized clearances, closers that don't fully close and latch, field-drilled holes and non-rated hardware. Each one is a repair a locksmith can quote. The inspector who also repairs wins by converting findings to fixed doors before the survey. That is the same shape as fire alarm work under [[niches/security-alarm-integrators/fire-alarm-itm/profile|Fire Alarm Inspection, Testing & Maintenance]]. The owner's portfolio calendar belongs to [[niches/facilities-management-firms/statutory-inspection-compliance/profile|Statutory Inspection & Life-Safety Compliance]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Inspection apps from fire-protection software vendors (Inspect Point covers fire doors), barcode-tag programmes, spreadsheets and clipboards. The gaps: label condition captured as a tick box rather than a photo, deficiencies not linked to quotes and repair tickets, and no proof of re-inspection after the fix.
