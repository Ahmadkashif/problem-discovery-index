# Locksmith, Safe & Door Hardware Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/locksmiths|Locksmiths]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The trade and standards bodies around the locksmith. ALOA Security Professionals Association runs certifications (CRL, CPL, CML), lobbying and a state licensing tracker. SAVTA serves safe and vault technicians. The Door and Hardware Institute trains architectural hardware consultants and runs the Fire and Egress Door Assembly Inspector credential. BHMA writes the ANSI/BHMA A156 performance standards that grade locks and exit devices. Members and manufacturers fund them through dues, exams, training and conferences.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Small staffs; standards and education work done mostly by member volunteers and contracted test labs, under 10 analysts per body (estimate)
**Output:** Certifications, exams, training, standards, legislative tracking, trade shows
**Proprietary data:** Certification and exam records, member rosters, standards test results submitted by manufacturers
**Clock:** Exam and conference calendars; standards revision cycles
**Buyer:** Executive director
