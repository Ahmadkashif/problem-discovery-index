# Home-Services Lead Platforms & Local Search Ads

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/locksmiths|Locksmiths]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The platforms that sit between a locked-out caller and the locksmith and charge for the connection: Google Local Services ads (pay per lead, with Advanced Verification mandatory for every locksmith business in the US and Canada), Angi, Thumbtack, Yelp, and pay-per-call networks that buy search traffic and sell calls. Their marketplace, pricing and trust-and-safety teams set lead prices, rank providers, verify businesses and fight fake listings. That fight is concentrated in this trade: Google and UC San Diego found 11% of search results for locksmiths fraudulent in 2017.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~50–150 marketplace, pricing and trust-and-safety analysts per platform across all home-service categories, a handful on locksmiths (estimate)
**Output:** Lead prices and ranking, provider verification decisions, fraud detection, demand reporting to advertisers
**Proprietary data:** Search and call demand by category and postcode, joined to bookings, disputes, reviews and verification outcomes, including the history of fake locksmith listings
**Clock:** None set externally; emergency demand is continuous and the platforms set their own policy calendars
**Buyer:** VP of marketplace or head of trust and safety
