# Field-Service Apps Rebuilt for One Truck

**Niche:** [[niches/locksmiths/solo-mobile-locksmiths/profile|Solo & Two-Truck Mobile Locksmiths]]
**Industry:** [[industries/locksmiths|Locksmiths]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Field-service apps are built for an office dispatching a crew; the solo locksmith is the office, the crew and the compliance department at once, usually from a phone in a dark parking lot.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #seq2seq #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to put the owner's own verified, licensed business in front of the emergency caller ahead of the lead-generation networks, and whoever lowers the owner's cost per completed job takes the account.

## The Problem
The owner takes a call while driving, quotes from memory, finds the address, does the job and must then create the customer, the job, the invoice and the payment, plus the ID and work-order record a licensing state requires. Most of this happens after the fact, if at all.

## What Already Exists
Workiz, Jobber, Housecall Pro and ServiceTitan offer scheduling, dispatch, quotes, invoices, payments, reviews and customer messaging, with mobile apps. Some offer locksmith templates and phone integrations.

## The Customization Gap
The solo version needs voice-first job creation from the call itself, quoting from the owner's price book, and one-screen capture at the door: ID, signature on the accepted price, photos. It should apply state record rules and licence numbers automatically, prefill NASTF authorisation forms for car jobs, and draft review replies and lead disputes from the job record. It must work with one hand, offline, and without an office. The feature lists exist in the big platforms; the flow does not.

## Target Customer
Owner-operators and two-truck firms who have tried a field-service app and gone back to paper.

## Impact If Solved
Records get made at the door rather than at midnight, compliance happens by default, and the owner's evening goes back to them.
