# Smart-Lock & Residential Access Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/locksmiths|Locksmiths]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The companies whose connected locks and cloud platforms are replacing the mechanical rekey in rental housing and homes: apartment access platforms such as SmartRent and Latch, and residential smart-lock brands from Schlage, Yale and Kwikset. Property managers pay hardware and per-unit software fees for keyless access, self-guided tours and access logs. Locksmiths increasingly install and service the locks. Aliro 1.0, released by the Connectivity Standards Alliance on 26 February 2026, aims to make phone-wallet credentials work across these locks.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–80 data and product analytics staff at the largest platform (estimate)
**Output:** Access and device-health analytics, vacancy and tour reporting, and lock battery and failure prediction inside the product
**Proprietary data:** Lock events, credential issuance and device telemetry across apartment portfolios and homes
**Clock:** Product release cycles; standards certification such as Aliro, which the industry sets for itself
**Buyer:** Chief technology officer or head of data

**Kill switches:** none live. SmartRent's US privacy policy (last updated 1 October 2026) says it processes residents' personal information on behalf of the landlord as a service provider, but reserves the right to analyse usage on an aggregated basis, to share aggregated or de-identified equipment information with landlords "and others" for research, and to use it for language models. That is a vendor product right over aggregate data, the same ruling as [[niches/security-alarm-integrators/interactive-services-platform-data/profile|🔍 Interactive Services Platform Data Teams]] (35), where Alarm.com reserves aggregated use for its own products. Mozilla's review of Schlage's policy reports de-identified sharing permitted. Latch (now DOOR) is the exception: its privacy page says it never shares residents' unit-level access data with landlords and never shares or sells residents' data with third parties, and shows no aggregate clause. The restriction bears on Q1 and Q3, not on the switch.
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. The insight is a feature of a SaaS fee, a few platforms make up each segment, and the access data can be pooled mainly to improve and maintain the platforms' own products. For locksmiths the pocket matters as the force turning rekeys into retrofits, covered in [[niches/locksmiths/rental-turnover-rekeying/profile|Rental-Turnover Rekeying & Smart-Lock Retrofit]].
