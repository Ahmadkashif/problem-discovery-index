# Locksmith & Security Hardware Distributors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/locksmiths|Locksmiths]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Wholesale distributors that stock locks, cylinders, key blanks, automotive keys and fobs, exit devices, closers, safes and tools for locksmiths and door-hardware firms. Banner Solutions, a private-equity platform that absorbed H.L. Flake and International Key Supply, is among the largest; IDN Global and Accredited Lock Supply are others, alongside regional houses. Their category, pricing and supplier teams manage tens of thousands of SKUs, set contractor pricing and report sell-through to manufacturers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 category, pricing and supplier analytics staff at the largest distributor (estimate)
**Output:** Category plans, contractor price files, demand forecasts, manufacturer sell-through and rebate reporting
**Proprietary data:** Purchase histories across thousands of locksmith accounts, with an early read on transitions such as smart-lock retrofits, new automotive key platforms and interchangeable-core adoption
**Clock:** Manufacturer programme years and price increases; customers' project bid dates
**Buyer:** VP of category management or purchasing
