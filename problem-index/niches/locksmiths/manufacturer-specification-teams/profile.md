# Lock & Door Hardware Manufacturers' Specification Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/locksmiths|Locksmiths]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The specification writers and architectural hardware consultants employed by the large lock and door-hardware groups (Allegion, ASSA ABLOY and dormakaba brands). They prepare door-hardware schedules and specifications for architects free of charge, to get their products written into Division 08 of the project specification. Locksmiths and distributors then bid against those schedules and install what was specified.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Tens of specification writers per manufacturer, each producing project schedules (estimate); no separate research function
**Output:** Hardware schedules and specifications, given away to win the specification
**Proprietary data:** Project-by-project opening schedules and the products specified on them
**Clock:** Architects' design and bid dates, set by the client's project schedule
**Buyer:** Vice president of specification sales
