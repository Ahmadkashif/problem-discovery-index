# Locksmith Franchise Systems

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/locksmiths|Locksmiths]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Franchisors that license a locksmith brand, call centre, marketing and fleet programmes to local owners. Pop-A-Lock (SystemForward America, Lafayette, La.) calls itself the largest US locksmith network, with franchise listings reporting roughly 380 to 430 units. Smaller brands and multi-trade home-service franchisors that include locksmithing make up the rest. Franchisees pay royalties and marketing fees; national accounts such as roadside programmes and property managers buy through the franchisor.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Franchisor operations and marketing staff with a few analysts; under 10 in any analytics role (estimate)
**Output:** Lead routing, franchisee benchmarking, national account management
**Proprietary data:** Call and job records across franchise territories
**Clock:** Franchise disclosure and renewal cycles the franchisor sets
**Buyer:** Franchisor chief executive or chief operating officer
