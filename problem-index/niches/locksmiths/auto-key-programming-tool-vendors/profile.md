# Automotive Key Programming Tool Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/locksmiths|Locksmiths]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The makers of handheld and PC-based key programmers that let locksmiths add and replace transponder and smart keys: Autel's MaxiIM line, Xhorse's VVDI tools, Advanced Diagnostics' Smart Pro, and peers such as Lonsdor, OBDStar and Abrites. Their vehicle security coverage teams acquire vehicles and modules, work out each immobilizer generation's programming procedure, and ship coverage in software updates sold as subscriptions and, for some tools, per-job tokens. Locksmiths buy the hardware and keep paying for updates.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~30–150 vehicle security coverage engineers per vendor, excluding support and sales technicians (estimate)
**Output:** Coverage releases and procedures inside the tool; coverage lists; technical support
**Proprietary data:** Reverse-engineered immobilizer procedures across makes and years, and, for online tools, logs of attempted and completed jobs by vehicle
**Clock:** New model years and mid-year security changes by automakers, which break coverage until the vendor catches up
**Buyer:** Head of vehicle coverage engineering or product director

**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold.
