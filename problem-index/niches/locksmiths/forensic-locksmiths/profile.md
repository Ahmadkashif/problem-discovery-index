# Forensic Locksmiths & Lock Examination Experts

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/locksmiths|Locksmiths]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Specialist locksmiths and examiners who inspect locks, keys and vehicle ignitions after a burglary or theft to say how entry or the theft was achieved: picked, bumped, forced, opened with a key, or a vehicle started with an original or programmed key. Insurers' special investigation units, defence and plaintiff lawyers and police commission the work for claims and court cases.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Mostly sole practitioners and firms of 1–5 examiners (estimate)
**Output:** Examination reports and expert testimony
**Proprietary data:** Case files and microscopy of examined locks and keys
**Clock:** Claim and court deadlines
**Buyer:** Insurer special investigations manager or litigating counsel
