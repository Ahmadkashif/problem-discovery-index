# DoD Lock Program & Security Container Standards

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/locksmiths|Locksmiths]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Department of Defense's technical authority for locks, safes, vaults, seals and containers that protect national security information and arms, ammunition and explosives, managed by the Naval Facilities Engineering Systems Command's Expeditionary Warfare Center. It evaluates and specifies hardware such as FF-L-2740 combination locks and GSA-approved containers, runs a technical support hotline and training, and manages programmes such as the phase-out of older GSA "black label" containers. Locksmiths and safe technicians who service federal containers work to its guidance.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–25 engineers and test staff, excluding trainers and hotline support (estimate)
**Output:** Specifications, approved-product guidance, technical bulletins, training, hotline support; funded by appropriations, not invoiced
**Proprietary data:** Test, failure and field-report records for security hardware across DoD installations
**Clock:** None external; programme schedules such as container phase-outs are set by the agency itself
**Buyer:** Programme manager
