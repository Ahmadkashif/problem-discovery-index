# Call Tracking Extended to Quote Accuracy

**Niche:** [[niches/locksmiths/lockout-call-dispatch/profile|Lockout Call Capture, Upfront Quoting & Dispatch]]
**Industry:** [[industries/locksmiths|Locksmiths]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Call-tracking software already records, transcribes and attributes every inbound call; it measures marketing, not whether the price quoted on that call was the price charged.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #descriptive-statistics #data-integration #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to answer the emergency lockout call first with a binding price and a verified, licensed technician, and whoever completes the most calls at the quoted price takes the account.

## The Problem
Locksmith firms use call tracking to see which ads ring the phone. The recordings also hold every quote given, and almost nobody checks them against invoices. Price complaints, the trade's biggest reputational problem, are argued from memory.

## What Already Exists
Call-tracking and conversation-analytics platforms such as CallRail offer number pools, recording, transcription, keyword spotting and attribution to ad campaigns, with integrations to field-service software.

## The Customization Gap
The extension extracts the quoted price and job description from each transcript, matches the call to the resulting job and invoice, and reports quote-to-bill variance by dispatcher, technician and job type. Large variances become flagged jobs for review. A customer-facing record shows the quoted and charged amounts side by side. Keyword lexicons need the trade's vocabulary (lockout, rekey, all keys lost, smart key, mortise, exit device) rather than generic sales terms.

## Target Customer
Locksmith firms already paying for call tracking, and the agencies that run their ads.

## Impact If Solved
Quote honesty becomes measurable and defensible inside a tool the firm already owns, which settles disputes and supports the firm's verified status with platforms.
