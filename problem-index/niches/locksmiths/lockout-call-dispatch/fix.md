# Paying for Leads That Were Never Jobs

**Niche:** [[niches/locksmiths/lockout-call-dispatch/profile|Lockout Call Capture, Upfront Quoting & Dispatch]]
**Industry:** [[industries/locksmiths|Locksmiths]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Pay-per-lead ads charge for spam calls, wrong services and out-of-area callers, and disputing them takes the owner an evening of listening to recordings.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #automation #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to answer the emergency lockout call first with a binding price and a verified, licensed technician, and whoever completes the most calls at the quoted price takes the account.

## The Problem
Lead platforms bill per lead and allow disputes for leads outside the advertiser's service or area. In a lockout business many charged leads are robocalls, solicitations, wrong services (garage doors, safes the firm doesn't service) or callers far outside the area. Each dispute must be filed by hand within the platform's window.

## Why It's Still Broken
Disputes require listening to calls and citing the platform's rule. Owners don't have time, so the credits go unclaimed. Platforms have little incentive to automate the process for them.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Classify each lead call from its transcript against the platform's dispute categories, draft the dispute with the relevant excerpt, and queue it for one-tap approval before the window closes. A monthly report of credited and lost disputes shows the real cost per job.

## Who Feels the Pain
Owners of lockout firms paying for junk leads; dispatchers fielding them.

## Impact If Fixed
Unclaimed credits come back for minutes of effort, and cost per completed job becomes visible by channel.
