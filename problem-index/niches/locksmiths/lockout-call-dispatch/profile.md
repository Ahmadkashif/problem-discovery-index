# Lockout Call Capture, Upfront Quoting & Dispatch

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/locksmiths|Locksmiths]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to answer the emergency lockout call first with a binding price and a verified, licensed technician, and whoever completes the most calls at the quoted price takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$450M a year of residential, commercial and vehicle lockout and emergency entry work by locksmith firms (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~17% of 561622 receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — demand arrives entirely through search, ads, apps and roadside programmes, while quoting and dispatch run on the phone and the owner's memory
**Target Buyer:** Owner or dispatch manager at a multi-truck locksmith firm
**Automation Potential:** High — answering, intake, quoting, ownership pre-checks, dispatch and record-keeping are mechanical once the price book is set

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A lockout is the industry's most frequent call and its most abused. The caller wants speed and a known price. The networks that game local search win by answering first and quoting low, then charging more on site. Ohio's attorney general sued one that quoted $19. Google now makes every locksmith pass Advanced Verification for its Local Services ads, and roadside assistance programmes dispatch many car lockouts through their own networks. Those networks are analysed under Towing Companies as [[niches/towing-companies/roadside-assistance-network-analytics/profile|🔍 Roadside Assistance Network Analytics]]. The legitimate firm's contest is mechanical. It has to answer every call, quote a price it will honour, send a licensed technician who verifies ownership, and finish at that price.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Google Local Services ads and Business Profile, call tracking, [[industries/answering-services|answering services]], field-service dispatch, roadside-programme portals. The gaps: no binding quote from the first minute of the call, no ownership pre-check before dispatch, quote accuracy unmeasured, and lead charges disputed by hand.
