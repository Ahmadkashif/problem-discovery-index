# Physical Therapy Outcomes Registries & Benchmarking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/physical-therapy|Physical Therapy Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Collect standardised functional outcome measures at intake and discharge across thousands of clinics, risk-adjust them, and report provider performance back to clinics, payers and networks. Pass 1 notes outcome measurement is theoretically required by most payers and accrediting bodies and that therapists find administering the instruments at the right intervals burdensome.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 biostatisticians, psychometricians, clinical researchers, and analysts
**Output:** Risk-adjusted outcome benchmarks, provider performance reporting, predicted recovery trajectories, payer network analytics
**Proprietary data:** Millions of episodes with functional status measured at multiple points and linked to treatment volume — one of the few large longitudinal functional outcome datasets in existence
**Clock:** Reporting cycles and payer contracting periods
**Buyer:** Chief Science Officer / VP of Outcomes
