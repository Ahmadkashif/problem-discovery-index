# Workers' Compensation Physical Medicine Networks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/physical-therapy|Physical Therapy Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Manage the therapy component of workers' compensation claims — provider networks, bill review against state fee schedules, treatment authorisation against state-adopted guidelines, and return-to-work coordination.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 clinical reviewers, bill review analysts, network managers, and outcome analysts
**Output:** Authorisation and bill review determinations, provider outcome profiling, return-to-work analytics, state guideline compliance
**Proprietary data:** Therapy episodes joined to indemnity duration and claim closure — outcome labels that commercial health data lacks entirely
**Clock:** State-mandated review timeframes and claim milestones
**Buyer:** VP of Medical Management / Chief Analytics Officer
