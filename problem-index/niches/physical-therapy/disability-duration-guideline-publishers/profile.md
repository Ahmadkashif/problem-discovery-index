# Disability Duration & Return-to-Work Guideline Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/physical-therapy|Physical Therapy Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Publish the expected disability duration tables and return-to-work guidelines that workers' compensation payers, disability insurers and employers set expectations against — how many days off work a given diagnosis, job demand and comorbidity profile should require.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 physicians, epidemiologists, statisticians, and content editors
**Output:** Duration tables by diagnosis and job demand, return-to-work guidelines, treatment guidelines, benchmarking analytics
**Proprietary data:** Duration distributions derived from very large disability and workers' compensation claim populations, published as reference content rather than data
**Clock:** Annual guideline revision and continuous claim benchmarking
**Buyer:** Chief Medical Officer / VP of Content
