# Remote Therapeutic Monitoring & Home Exercise Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/physical-therapy|Physical Therapy Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Deliver home exercise programmes to patients and capture adherence and movement data back to the clinic — a market created and funded by the remote therapeutic monitoring billing codes.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 clinical content developers, computer vision engineers, data scientists, and outcomes staff
**Output:** Exercise programme libraries, adherence tracking and prompting, movement capture and form feedback, outcome reporting
**Proprietary data:** Home adherence and movement data at a scale no clinic ever had, joined to clinical outcomes
**Clock:** Monthly billing periods and episode schedules
**Buyer:** VP of Clinical Product
