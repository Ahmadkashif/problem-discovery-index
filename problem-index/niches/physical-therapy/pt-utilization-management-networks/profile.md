# Physical Medicine Utilization Management Networks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/physical-therapy|Physical Therapy Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Sit between health plans and therapy providers, authorising visits, running concurrent review, and deciding when a course of care stops. Pass 1 describes the effect from below: most payers require authorisation every ten to twelve visits, and tracking authorisation status while requesting extensions is manual, error-prone, and directly causes revenue leakage.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-2,000 clinical reviewers, medical directors, analysts, and criteria developers
**Output:** Authorisation determinations, medical necessity criteria, provider profiling and network tiering, utilisation and outcome reporting
**Proprietary data:** Millions of authorisation decisions joined to submitted functional measures, subsequent utilisation and cost — one of the largest bodies of conservative-care decision data anywhere
**Clock:** Authorisation turnaround requirements and continuous concurrent review
**Buyer:** Chief Medical Officer / VP of Clinical Analytics
