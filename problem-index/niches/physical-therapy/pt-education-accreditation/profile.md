# Physical Therapy Education Accreditation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/physical-therapy|Physical Therapy Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Accredit physical therapy education programmes against standards, review self-studies, and conduct site visits that determine whether a programme's graduates can sit for licensure.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-20 accreditation staff
**Output:** Accreditation decisions, standards, programme outcome reporting
**Proprietary data:** Programme-level outcome data collected annually across accredited institutions
**Clock:** Accreditation cycles and annual reporting deadlines
**Buyer:** Director of Accreditation
