# Conservative-Care Treatment Guideline Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/physical-therapy|Physical Therapy Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Synthesise the evidence for non-surgical management of musculoskeletal conditions into the clinical practice guidelines and utilisation criteria that determine what conservative care is recommended, how much of it, and what payers will authorise.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-400 evidence reviewers, methodologists, clinicians, and editors
**Output:** Clinical practice guidelines, evidence syntheses, treatment criteria sets, care pathway recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Structured extraction and grading of a large and heterogeneous musculoskeletal literature, maintained across revision cycles
**Clock:** Guideline revision schedules and payer criteria update cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Medical Officer / VP of Clinical Content
