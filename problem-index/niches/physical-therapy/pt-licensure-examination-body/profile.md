# Physical Therapy Licensure Examination Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/physical-therapy|Physical Therapy Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Develop and administer the national licensure examination every physical therapist must pass, maintain the item banks and standards behind it, and operate the interstate licence compact and disciplinary data system.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-200 psychometricians, item development staff, subject matter experts, and analysts
**Output:** Licensure examinations and item banks, practice analyses, passing standards, licensure and discipline data exchange
**Proprietary data:** Item response data across the entire entering profession, plus practice analysis surveys of the working field
**Clock:** Examination administration windows, standard setting cycles, and licence renewal periods
**Buyer:** Chief Assessment Officer
