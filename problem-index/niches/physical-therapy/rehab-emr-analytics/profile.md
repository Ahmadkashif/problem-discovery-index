# Rehabilitation EMR Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/physical-therapy|Physical Therapy Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The analytics functions inside the therapy-specific EMR vendors, producing documentation compliance checking, productivity reporting, and industry benchmark publications from clinic activity.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 clinical informaticists, analysts, and product researchers
**Output:** Documentation compliance rules, productivity and scheduling analytics, industry benchmark reports, revenue cycle dashboards
**Proprietary data:** Treatment documentation, scheduling and billing records across a large share of outpatient therapy clinics
**Clock:** Continuous clinical documentation
**Buyer:** VP of Product / Chief Clinical Officer
