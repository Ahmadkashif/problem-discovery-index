# Cold-Start Stream Ranking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/live-commerce-platforms|Live Commerce Platforms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to rank a stream that has existed for four minutes against streams with hours of accumulated signal — and whoever gets the first minutes right decides which sellers survive.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.8B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~14% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** High — invested in, structurally mismatched
**Target Buyer:** Platform discovery and marketplace supply leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — priors and early signal are both computable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every stream is a cold-start item, every time, for its entire life. In a persistent catalogue cold start is a transient state an item passes through in hours or days; here it is the permanent condition, because the item expires before any history accumulates. The contest is over what can be known about a stream in its first four minutes — from the seller's record, the announced inventory, the category, the thumbnail, the opening audio and the first few viewers' behaviour — and how quickly that estimate can be updated. A competitor that is excellent at this and mediocre at objective design still wins the supply side of the marketplace, which is why it is a separate fight.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Seller follower counts, category placement, simple recency boosts, and exploration slots inherited from feed infrastructure. The gaps: no structured use of the seller's own history as a prior; no use of announced inventory before the stream starts; no fast in-stream signal loop; and exploration budgets calibrated for items that live for days.
