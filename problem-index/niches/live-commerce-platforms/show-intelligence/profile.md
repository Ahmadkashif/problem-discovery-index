# Show Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/live-commerce-platforms|Live Commerce Platforms]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn the second-by-second record of what was shown, said and bought into what a host should do next — and whoever does that owns the only proprietary asset the category has.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.2B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — collected, barely used
**Target Buyer:** Platform analytics and seller success
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the corpus is complete and aligned

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The platforms hold second-by-second records of what was shown, said and bought, which is the richest behavioural commerce data anywhere, and use it primarily to rank streams by engagement. Every other commerce format infers intent from clicks; here the platform has the item on camera, the words used to sell it, the room's reaction, and the purchase, all aligned on one timeline. No other commerce corpus has the stimulus and the response recorded together. The contest is over turning that into something a host can act on, which no platform currently attempts beyond a viewer count.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Viewer count, gross merchandise value per show, follower growth, and a post-show summary. The gaps: no attribution of sales to what was said or shown; no pricing feedback; no pacing or ordering analysis; no comparison against similar hosts; and no forward recommendation of what to show, when, and for how long.
