**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** This niche is not terminal — the fight over cold-start ranking and the fight over the ranking objective are different contests with different winners, and they are stated separately in the sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3.4B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~26% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** High — heavily invested, poorly matched
**Target Buyer:** Platform discovery leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — ranking is the product

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Discovery is the binding constraint of live commerce and the category says so itself. A stream is available for two hours and then gone, its inventory with it, so matching is a real-time problem with a hard deadline and the cost of failure is absolute rather than deferred — an unwatched stream is not a delayed sale, it is a seller who does not return. This is a distinct niche because no other commerce discovery problem has a deadline, and no other video discovery problem has a purchase at the end of it.

It is also the one niche here that is **not terminal**. Attempting the contested statement for discovery as a whole produces two sentences rather than one. A stream four minutes old with no history has to be ranked against streams with hours of accumulated signal — an item cold-start problem, solvable with content and seller priors. Separately, the ranking objective was inherited from short-form video and optimises watch time, which is not what a commerce platform is trying to produce — an objective problem, solvable only by changing what the system is trained on. A competitor can lead decisively on one and be ordinary at the other, which is the test the filter applies. The two contests are stated in the sub-niches.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Short-form video recommendation infrastructure applied largely unchanged, engagement-ranked feeds, category browsing, and follow graphs. The gaps: no usable treatment of the first minutes of a stream; an objective that rewards watching over buying; no use of what is happening inside the stream right now; and no accounting for the seller-side consequence of an empty room.
