# Trust & Safety Operations Practice

**Niche:** [[niches/live-commerce-platforms/the-live-moderator/profile|The Live Moderator]]
**Industry:** [[industries/live-commerce-platforms|Live Commerce Platforms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Trust and safety operations is a developed discipline with queue design, quality assurance and wellbeing practice, and all of it assumes the reviewer has time.
**Tags:** #worker-facing #workflow-orchestration #compliance #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #hypothesis-testing #automation #descriptive-statistics
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give the live moderator a defensible basis for a decision that cuts off someone's income in four seconds — and whoever does that changes both the accuracy of moderation and whether the job is survivable.

## The Problem
Large platforms have built a real operational discipline around content review: routing by severity and reviewer skill, calibration sessions, double review on consequential decisions, quality auditing against ground truth, appeals handling, and wellbeing programmes including exposure limits and counselling. It is a mature practice with vendors and standards. It was designed for asynchronous review of static content, and live commerce moderation has adopted the org chart without being able to use most of the method.

## What Already Exists
Review queue routing and prioritisation; reviewer calibration and inter-rater agreement measurement; double-review and escalation workflows; decision quality auditing and appeals; and wellbeing programmes with exposure management.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is from asynchronous review to real-time intervention. It requires: (1) decisions under a hard deadline with no option to defer, which removes the basic lever the entire practice relies on — queue depth as a shock absorber — and is the central adaptation; (2) calibration performed against live scenarios rather than static samples, since the skill being measured is reading an unfolding situation rather than classifying a fixed artefact; (3) double review that fits inside seconds, which means a parallel second opinion rather than a sequential one and is an architecture change; (4) an economic consequence attached to every decision, since cutting a stream destroys income in a way removing a post does not, which changes how severity thresholds should be set; and (5) exposure management for continuous unfiltered live video, which is more intense than reviewing pre-filtered clips and which existing wellbeing practice is not calibrated for.

## Target Customer
Live platform trust and safety teams, moderation service providers, and trust and safety tooling vendors whose products assume a queue.

## Impact If Solved
The practice relies on queue depth as a shock absorber and live removes it entirely. Parallel rather than sequential second opinions is the architecture change, and an economic consequence on every decision should reset where the thresholds sit.
