# The Live Moderator

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/live-commerce-platforms|Live Commerce Platforms]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give the live moderator a defensible basis for a decision that cuts off someone's income in four seconds — and whoever does that changes both the accuracy of moderation and whether the job is survivable.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$700M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — queues and judgement
**Target Buyer:** Trust and safety operations
**Automation Potential:** Medium — assistance rather than replacement

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Live moderators watch several concurrent streams in real time, deciding within seconds whether to interrupt a seller's income, on content that has already been broadcast either way. That is a materially different job from reviewing a queue of reported posts: the decision is irreversible in both directions, the evidence is still arriving, the consequence is somebody's livelihood, and there is no time to check anything. The contest here is not detection — that is the automatable niche — but the human decision surface: what the moderator sees, what they can act on, and what they can stand behind afterwards.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Multi-stream monitoring walls, automated flag queues, policy documents, and an escalation path. The gaps: no context on the seller or the stream before the decision; no graduated actions between nothing and cutting the stream; no confidence or evidence attached to the automated flag; no record supporting the decision afterwards; and no attention to the psychological load of continuous live review.
