# Seller Inventory & Listing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/live-commerce-platforms|Live Commerce Platforms]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a seller's stock into the platform without the seller typing it in — and whoever removes that step decides how much inventory the format can carry.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.1B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — stock is in boxes, not systems
**Target Buyer:** Seller operations and platform supply teams
**Automation Potential:** Very High — capture and description are automatable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Live commerce runs on inventory that was never entered anywhere. Sellers buy pallets, estates, collections and overstock, and sell them by holding them up. That works during the show and fails everywhere else: nothing can be searched, matched to a viewer's want, scheduled across shows, priced against comparables, or relisted if unsold. The contest is over removing the data entry step entirely rather than making it faster, because any workflow that requires a seller to describe items before selling them is slower than just selling them and will not be adopted.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Manual listing forms, spreadsheet imports, marketplace listing tools designed for fixed catalogues, and in practice nothing at all. The gaps: no capture at the point the item is first handled; no automatic description or attribute extraction; no comparable-based price suggestion; no carry-over of unsold items to the next show; and no visibility of what a seller has available for the discovery system to match against.
