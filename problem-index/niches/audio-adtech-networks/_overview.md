# Niche Analysis — Audio Adtech Networks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/audio-adtech-networks|Audio Adtech Networks]]

## Niche Selection

Audio advertising is bought on faith backed by a household IP match, in the only major channel with no click and no reliable exposure signal, and the consolidation of measurement into the platforms that sell the inventory has removed the one check the market had. What makes it tractable is that the exposure question is answerable from telemetry the streaming platforms already collect and do not use for it. ## Why These Niches

Measurement takes the largest share because a channel bought on an unpublished error rate, refereed by its own sellers, has its credibility as the contested asset. Inventory and yield is second because dynamic insertion turned a fixed slot into a perishable back-catalogue-heavy inventory that most publishers forecast with a spreadsheet. The two low-digitized niches are the ones with no systems at all: a host-read sponsorship market running on a shared drive, and brand suitability implemented as the same blunt blocklists that defunded news on the open web. The two underserved audiences are the operations specialist reconciling two incompatible businesses into one report and the host paid on a number that overstates their audience. The two automatable niches are the creative nobody measures and the insertion decision nobody optimises.

## Filter Notes

Seven of the eight level-1 niches are terminal. The Measurement Layer is not: the label names a function rather than a contest, and writing the contested statement produces two sentences describing different winners. Exposure modelling asks whether a listener reached the advertisement, and is answerable from position-in-episode listening curves, skip behaviour and completion telemetry the platforms already hold — a modelling contest over data that exists. Attribution asks whether hearing it caused anything, currently answered by matching a household IP address to a website visit, and its contest is as much about who is permitted to referee as about method, since the independent measurement companies were acquired by the platforms selling the inventory. One is a data problem with a technical answer; the other is a governance problem with a statistical component. It therefore decomposes into **Exposure Modelling** and **Attribution & Independence**.

Two candidates were considered and rejected. **Audio streaming and delivery infrastructure** is mature and the contest over it belongs to [[industries/edge-cdn-providers|Edge & CDN Providers]]. **Music rights and licensing** shares the streaming surface but is the contest of [[industries/music-distribution-platforms|Music Distribution Platforms]], where it is already analysed.
