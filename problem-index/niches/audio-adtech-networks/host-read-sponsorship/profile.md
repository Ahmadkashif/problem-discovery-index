# Host-Read Sponsorship

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/audio-adtech-networks|Audio Adtech Networks]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to run the host-read business as a market rather than as a set of email threads — and whoever does that prices the channel's most valuable and least systematised product.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.1B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~14% of category spend
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — a spreadsheet and a shared drive
**Target Buyer:** Sales and sponsorship leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — the workflow is entirely mechanisable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A host-read endorsement and a dynamically-inserted programmatic spot are priced in the same marketplace and are not the same product: the first carries the host's credibility and cannot be targeted, the second can be targeted and carries none. The host-read business runs on spreadsheets, shared drives and email — the brief, the script approval, the read confirmation, the airing verification, the invoice — for a product that commands the highest rates in the channel. This is distinct from the operations worker niche: that is about the person reconciling two businesses, this is about the business that has no systems.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Spreadsheets, shared drives, email threads, and manual verification by listening. The gaps: no workflow from brief to airing; scripts and approvals in email; airing verification done by someone listening; no systematic pricing; performance measured by promotional code alone; and renewals negotiated from scratch each time.
