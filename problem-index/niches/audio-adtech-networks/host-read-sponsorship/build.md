# The Channel's Best Product Runs on Email

**Niche:** [[niches/audio-adtech-networks/host-read-sponsorship/profile|Host-Read Sponsorship]]
**Industry:** [[industries/audio-adtech-networks|Audio Adtech Networks]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Host-read sponsorships live in a spreadsheet and a shared drive while commanding the highest rates in the channel, and nothing about them is systematised.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #automation #data-integration #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact #descriptive-statistics #compliance #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to run the host-read business as a market rather than as a set of email threads — and whoever does that prices the channel's most valuable and least systematised product.

## The Problem
A brand sponsors a show. The deal is agreed by email. The brief and talking points go in a document. The host writes or improvises a read. Somebody approves it, by email. The episode publishes and the read airs somewhere inside it. Someone verifies it by listening. A promotional code tracks response, imperfectly. An invoice follows. Every step is manual, every artefact is in a different place, and the whole thing is the highest-value product in audio advertising — the one that carries the host's credibility and cannot be replicated by any programmatic spot.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Host-read grew out of relationships rather than out of technology, and a business that grew from relationships keeps its processes in the medium relationships use, which is email. Each deal feels bespoke, which obscures that the workflow is identical every time. Platform vendors built for dynamic insertion because it was the technically interesting problem. And the revenue arrives regardless.

## What to Build
Build the workflow the product deserves. Run brief, script, approval, airing and invoice as one tracked process, which is the fix for most of the operational pain and is unglamorous software the category has simply never built. Verify airing automatically from the published audio, which is the fix note's subject. Price systematically from audience, engagement and past performance rather than from a rate negotiated by feel, since the pricing is currently the least evidenced part of the most valuable product. Measure performance beyond promotional codes, which capture a fraction of response and systematically favour direct-response advertisers over brand ones. Support the host's own workflow, since they are a participant with no tooling and their compliance determines whether anything runs on time. Handle the renewal as a continuation rather than a fresh negotiation, because repeat sponsorships are where the revenue concentrates and each one is currently rebuilt. Track category exclusivity and conflicts, which are managed by memory and occasionally breached expensively. Reconcile with the dynamically inserted business, connecting to the operations niche, so a client receives one report. Give advertisers a view of what they bought and what aired, which is currently supplied as a screenshot. And report the product's true economics, since host-read's premium is justified by an effect nobody measures and quantifying it would raise prices rather than lower them.

## Target Customer
Podcast networks and sales organisations, hosts selling their own inventory, and the brands buying the channel's most valuable format through email.

## Impact If Built
A business that grew from relationships keeps its processes in the medium relationships use, which obscures that the workflow is identical every time. Tracking it end to end and pricing from evidence rather than feel systematises the highest-value product in the channel.
