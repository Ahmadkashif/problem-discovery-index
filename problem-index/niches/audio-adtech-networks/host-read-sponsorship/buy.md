**Industry:** [[industries/audio-adtech-networks|Audio Adtech Networks]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Sports and events built sponsorship management with rights inventories, activation tracking and valuation models, and podcast sponsorship runs on a shared drive.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to run the host-read business as a market rather than as a set of email threads — and whoever does that prices the channel's most valuable and least systematised product.

## The Problem
Sponsorship is a developed commercial discipline in sport, events and entertainment. Rights inventories are catalogued, activation is tracked against contractual obligations, exclusivity and category conflicts are managed formally, valuation models price a package, and delivery is evidenced for the sponsor. Specialist software exists for all of it. Podcast host-read sponsorship is structurally the same transaction — a rights package, an obligation to deliver, an exclusivity concern, a valuation — and is administered by email.

## What Already Exists
Rights inventory and package management; activation and obligation tracking; category exclusivity and conflict management; sponsorship valuation models; and delivery evidence packages for sponsors.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a delivery that is a person's spoken words. It requires: (1) the deliverable being a read whose content and framing vary each time, so obligation tracking must verify what was actually said rather than that a logo appeared — that verification is the substantive new capability and it needs audio understanding; (2) volume of many small deals rather than a few large ones, which rules out the bespoke administration sports sponsorship tolerates; (3) a host who is a participant with their own judgement about what they will endorse, unlike a stadium which has no view; (4) valuation from a download number that overstates the audience, which makes the pricing input unreliable in a way stadium attendance is not; and (5) reconciliation with a programmatic business selling the same inventory, which has no sponsorship analogue.

## Target Customer
Podcast networks and sales teams, hosts, and sponsorship management vendors for whom audio is an unserved adjacent market.

## Impact If Solved
Sponsorship management is developed in sport and events and podcast sponsorship runs on email. Verifying what was actually said rather than that a logo appeared is the new capability, and many small deals rule out the bespoke administration the established practice assumes.
