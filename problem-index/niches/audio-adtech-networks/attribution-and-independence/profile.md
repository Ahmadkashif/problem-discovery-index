# Attribution & Independence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/audio-adtech-networks|Audio Adtech Networks]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to establish that hearing an advertisement caused something, credibly enough to be believed by a buyer — and whoever does that from a position the sellers do not own becomes the channel's only referee.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.0B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% of category spend
**Digital Adoption:** Low — an IP match and no error rate
**Target Buyer:** Advertiser measurement leadership and industry bodies
**Automation Potential:** High — the method is improvable, the governance is not automatable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Advertisers buy audio on a household IP matching a website visit, nobody publishes that method's error rate, and the independent measurement companies were acquired by the platforms selling the inventory. The contest here is only partly statistical: the method can be improved, but no improvement is believed while the measurer is also the seller. Winning requires both a better method and a defensible position outside the trade, which is a structurally different problem from modelling exposure from telemetry.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Household IP matching within a window, platform-reported conversion lift, promotional codes and vanity URLs, and survey-based brand studies. The gaps: matching that degrades under shared addressing and rotation; cross-device and out-of-household conversion invisible; no published error rate; no incrementality testing; and every provider now owned by a seller.
