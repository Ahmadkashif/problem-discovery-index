# Talent Representation Practice

**Niche:** [[niches/audio-adtech-networks/the-podcast-host/profile|The Podcast Host]]
**Industry:** [[industries/audio-adtech-networks|Audio Adtech Networks]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Entertainment built agents, rate cards and residual structures so talent could price their own value, and podcast hosts negotiate alone against a network's data.
**Tags:** #worker-facing #descriptive-statistics #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #compliance #revenue-impact #workflow-orchestration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell a host what their endorsement is actually worth — and whoever does that lets the person supplying the channel's only irreplaceable asset price it.

## The Problem
Entertainment industries developed an apparatus for this. Agents represent talent with knowledge of comparable deals, union rate minimums establish a floor, residual and participation structures share the upside of continued exploitation, and collective bargaining gave individuals leverage they could not have alone. It exists because the alternative — individuals negotiating against organisations with all the information — produced predictable outcomes. Podcast hosts, whose endorsement is the channel's premium product, negotiate individually with no rate reference, no floor and no data.

## What Already Exists
Agency representation with comparable deal knowledge; union minimums and standard terms; residual and participation structures; collective bargaining frameworks; and published rate schedules in some sectors.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a large population of small independent creators with no collective structure. It requires: (1) representation economics that work for a host earning modestly, since a traditional agency's percentage on small deals does not support the service — a data product rather than a human agent is the viable form and that is the substantive difference; (2) rate benchmarks assembled from contributed data rather than from an agency's private knowledge, which requires a contribution mechanism and a reason for hosts to participate; (3) value that continues from a back catalogue, which is structurally a residual and is currently paid as a one-off; (4) no collective body, so any floor must emerge from transparency rather than from bargaining; and (5) hosts who are frequently reluctant to discuss money publicly, which the contribution mechanism must accommodate.

## Target Customer
Podcast hosts and independent creators, creator business and finance vendors, and talent representatives seeking a scalable model for small creators.

## Impact If Solved
Entertainment built this apparatus because individuals negotiating against organisations with all the information produces predictable outcomes. A data product rather than a human agent is the viable form at these earnings, and back-catalogue value is structurally a residual paid as a one-off.
