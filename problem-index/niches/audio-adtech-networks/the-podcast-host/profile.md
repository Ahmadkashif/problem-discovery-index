# The Podcast Host

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/audio-adtech-networks|Audio Adtech Networks]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell a host what their endorsement is actually worth — and whoever does that lets the person supplying the channel's only irreplaceable asset price it.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$900M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~11% of category spend
**Digital Adoption:** Low — paid on a number that overstates
**Target Buyer:** Creators and creator business vendors
**Automation Potential:** High — the evidence exists on the platform side

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A podcast host lends their credibility to a product, is paid on a download number that overstates who heard it, and has no way to know what their endorsement is actually worth. They supply the only thing in this channel that cannot be replicated by a programmatic system — the trust of an audience that chose to listen to them — and they are compensated on a metric that measures file requests, negotiated against parties who know more about their audience than they do. This is distinct from the sponsorship market niche: that systematises the business, this is about the person whose credibility is the product.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Download reports from the hosting platform, promotional code redemptions, network-supplied summaries, and rate negotiation by feel. The gaps: paid on downloads rather than on exposure; no visibility of listening curves for their own show; no benchmark for what comparable hosts earn; no measure of the endorsement's effect; and no leverage in a negotiation where the other side has the data.
