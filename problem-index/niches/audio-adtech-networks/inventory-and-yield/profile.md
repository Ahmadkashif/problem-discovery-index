# Inventory & Yield

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/audio-adtech-networks|Audio Adtech Networks]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to forecast and price a perishable back-catalogue-heavy inventory that dynamic insertion created — and whoever does that properly stops publishers selling next month on last month's downloads.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.4B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~18% of category spend
**Digital Adoption:** Low — a spreadsheet trend
**Target Buyer:** Publisher yield and revenue leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — forecasting is well-posed

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Dynamic insertion turned a fixed slot into a perishable, back-catalogue-heavy inventory, and most publishers forecast it with a spreadsheet trend on last month's downloads. The inventory has genuinely unusual properties: a new episode's audience arrives over weeks rather than at once, the back catalogue produces a long tail of impressions indefinitely, and every slot expires as it is served. Forecasting and pricing this is a real quantitative problem and it is being done by extrapolating a monthly total.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Spreadsheet trend forecasts, ad server inventory reports, sell-through tracking, and rate cards by show. The gaps: forecasts that ignore the back catalogue's decay structure; no per-slot availability view; no price differentiation by expected exposure; oversell and undersell both common; and no optimisation across direct and programmatic demand.
