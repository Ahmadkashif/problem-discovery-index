**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to judge whether an episode is suitable for a brand rather than whether it contains a word — and whoever does that stops audio repeating the mistake that defunded news on the open web.

## The Problem
Classifying text against a suitability taxonomy is a commodity capability with strong models available cheaply. Beyond that, the open web has already run the experiment on keyword blocking: the cost in reach, the systematic defunding of journalism, the failure to catch the content advertisers actually cared about, and the eventual movement toward contextual judgement are all documented and widely discussed. Audio has the same capability available, the same lesson available, and is deploying the same blocklists.

## What Already Exists
Content classification models and safety taxonomies; contextual suitability products developed for the web; documented evidence of blocklist harms; industry frameworks for brand suitability tiers; and publisher appeal mechanisms.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to spoken conversation rather than written publication. It requires: (1) conversational speech where meaning depends on tone, irony and speaker in ways written text does not — a transcript strips the tone and a model reading it alone will misjudge, which means the audio itself should inform the assessment and no web product does that; (2) long-form content where a two-hour episode may contain one sensitive minute, so segment-level assessment is essential rather than a refinement; (3) dynamic insertion, which means the advertisement lands at a position and the suitability question is about the surrounding content rather than the whole episode; (4) a back catalogue that is monetised indefinitely, so a suitability decision affects years of content rather than a current page; and (5) publishers who are frequently individuals with no recourse, which makes an appeal route more important than on the web.

## Target Customer
Advertisers and agencies, audio platforms implementing suitability, and contextual suitability vendors for whom audio is an adjacent market.

## Impact If Solved
The web already ran this experiment and documented what blocklists cost, and audio is repeating it with the correction available. Tone and irony carried in the audio rather than the transcript, and segment-level assessment of long episodes, are what the web products do not supply.
