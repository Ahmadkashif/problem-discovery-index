# A Craft With No Feedback

**Niche:** [[niches/audio-adtech-networks/audio-creative-effectiveness/profile|Audio Creative Effectiveness]]
**Industry:** [[industries/audio-adtech-networks|Audio Adtech Networks]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Audio advertising creative is produced with no click, no measured exposure and a promotional code that a minority of responders use, so the craft develops on convention alone.
**Tags:** #transformers #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #hypothesis-testing #confidence-intervals #gradient-boosting #revenue-impact #causal-inference
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to establish what makes an audio advertisement work when there is no click and no visual — and whoever does that gives the channel the creative discipline every other medium has.

## The Problem
An advertiser produces an audio spot. Should it be thirty seconds or sixty? Should the offer come early or late? Should it be host-read or produced? Male or female voice, music or none, one message or three? These are the actual creative decisions and the channel has no evidence for any of them. Agencies have conventions, hosts have instincts, and the empirical basis is a handful of studies and a great deal of assertion. Meanwhile the creative is cheap to vary, the audio is fully transcribable and analysable, and the response signal — while weak — is not zero.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Creative measurement needs an outcome signal and the channel's outcome signal is the weakest in advertising, so the obvious prerequisite is missing — which is true and has been used as a reason not to try rather than as a problem to work around. The audio was not machine-analysable until recently. Creative is owned by agencies who are not measured on it. And nobody has assembled a corpus of audio advertisements with outcomes attached.

## What to Build
Build the corpus and the analysis. Extract attributes from the audio itself — length, voice characteristics, pacing, music, message structure, offer position, call to action, host-read or produced — which is now straightforward and is the input the category has never had. Assemble a corpus of advertisements with whatever outcome signal exists, accepting that it is weak, because a weak signal across thousands of advertisements is far better than a strong signal across none. Measure against exposure-adjusted response, connecting to the exposure niche, since an advertisement in an unreached position tells you nothing about its creative. Use structured testing where advertisers will run it, rotating creative variants across comparable inventory, which produces the clean evidence the observational corpus cannot. Analyse the host-read reads themselves, which are transcribed and are the channel's most valuable format with the least studied craft. Report findings at the attribute level so they transfer, rather than as a list of successful advertisements. Pool across advertisers and platforms, since no single advertiser has the volume and the findings generalise well in audio. Feed conclusions into production briefs, which is where the value lands. Publish the findings, since a channel with a published creative evidence base is easier to buy and the whole category benefits. And be honest about the uncertainty, because a confident creative rulebook built on a weak signal would be worse than the conventions it replaced.

## Target Customer
Advertisers and their audio creative agencies, platforms seeking to make the channel easier to buy, and the production houses making audio advertisements on convention.

## Impact If Built
The weak outcome signal has been used as a reason not to try rather than a problem to work around, while the audio became fully analysable. A weak signal across thousands of advertisements with extracted attributes is far better than the conventions the channel currently runs on.
