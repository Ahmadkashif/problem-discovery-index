**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to establish what makes an audio advertisement work when there is no click and no visual — and whoever does that gives the channel the creative discipline every other medium has.

## The Problem
Radio advertising has a research tradition. Recall and recognition testing, the effect of frequency, the role of music and voice, the position of the brand mention, the diminishing returns of length — these were studied over decades by broadcasters, agencies and industry bodies, and a body of practical knowledge exists. Digital audio inherited the format and almost none of the knowledge, partly because the practitioners came from digital rather than from radio and partly because nobody has tested whether the findings transfer to on-demand listening.

## What Already Exists
Radio creative recall and recognition testing; frequency and wear-out research; voice, music and pacing studies; brand mention timing research; and industry body effectiveness databases.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is from scheduled broadcast to on-demand personal listening. It requires: (1) testing whether the findings transfer at all, since on-demand listening with headphones on a personal device is a different attention context from broadcast radio in a car — establishing which classical findings survive is itself the substantive contribution and nobody has done it; (2) host-read as a format with no broadcast equivalent, where the endorser relationship is the mechanism and the classical research has nothing to say; (3) response measurable at the individual level, at least weakly, where radio research relied on recall surveys — a better outcome signal than the tradition had; (4) dynamic insertion allowing the same advertisement in many contexts, which permits testing designs radio could not run; and (5) a fragmented practitioner base with no industry research body, which is why the accumulation has not happened.

## Target Customer
Audio advertisers and agencies, platforms, and media research organisations for whom digital audio creative is an unclaimed territory.

## Impact If Solved
Radio accumulated decades of creative knowledge and digital audio inherited the format without it. Testing which classical findings survive the shift to personal on-demand listening is itself the contribution, and host-read has no broadcast equivalent at all.
