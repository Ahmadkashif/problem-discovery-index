# Insertion Decisioning

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/audio-adtech-networks|Audio Adtech Networks]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to decide which advertisement goes into which slot of which episode for which listener — and whoever optimises that properly extracts more value from the same inventory than anyone bidding harder can.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of category spend
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — serving solved, decisioning basic
**Target Buyer:** Platform product and yield teams
**Automation Potential:** Very High — a ranking and allocation problem

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Dynamic insertion made it possible to choose what plays in each slot for each listener, and the decisioning that fills those slots is largely rule-based: a priority order, a frequency cap, a targeting filter. The decision has several dimensions nobody optimises jointly — which advertisement, in which slot, in which episode, for which listener, at what point in the episode, and how it sits alongside the host-read content around it. This is a self-contained allocation contest, separable from measurement and from yield forecasting, and it is where the serving infrastructure's capability exceeds what is being asked of it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Priority-ordered ad serving, frequency capping, targeting filters, and fixed insertion points. The gaps: no joint optimisation across slot, episode and listener; frequency capping that does not account for a listener's whole audio consumption; no sequencing within an episode; no consideration of the surrounding content; and insertion positions set by convention rather than chosen.
