# The Measurement Layer

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/audio-adtech-networks|Audio Adtech Networks]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** This niche is not terminal — establishing that a listener reached the advertisement and establishing that hearing it caused anything are different contests with different winners, and they are stated separately in the sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.2B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~28% of category spend
**Digital Adoption:** Low — a download and an IP match
**Target Buyer:** Platform and advertiser measurement leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the telemetry exists and is unused

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The industry's currency is the download, which counts a file request rather than a listen and certainly not a listen that reached the advertisement. Attribution is then a household IP match against a website visit, whose error rate nobody publishes. This is the largest concentration of contested value in the category because the channel's credibility with advertisers rests on it, and because the independent measurement companies that provided the only external check were acquired by the platforms selling the inventory.

It is also **not terminal**. Two problems live here with different answers. Exposure — did the listener reach the advertisement — is a modelling problem over telemetry the streaming platforms already collect: position-in-episode listening curves, skip behaviour, completion. It has a technical answer and whoever builds it best wins. Attribution — did hearing it cause anything — is currently a probabilistic household match, and its contest is as much about who is permitted to referee as about method. One is a data problem, the other is a governance problem with statistics attached. The two contests are stated in the sub-niches.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Download counting to the industry standard, household IP attribution windows, platform-reported lift, and survey-based brand studies. The gaps: a currency that counts requests; no exposure signal despite the telemetry existing; an attribution method with no published error rate; cross-device and out-of-household conversion invisible; and the independent referees absorbed by the sellers.
