# The Ad Operations Specialist

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/audio-adtech-networks|Audio Adtech Networks]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let one person run two incompatible businesses and report them as one — and whoever does that removes the reconciliation that consumes the category's operational capacity.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$700M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of category spend
**Digital Adoption:** Low — two systems and a spreadsheet
**Target Buyer:** Ad operations leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the reconciliation is mechanical

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Host-read sponsorships live in a spreadsheet and a shared drive, dynamically inserted campaigns live in an ad server, and one person reconciles them both against a client who wants a single report. They are operating two businesses with different objects, different delivery mechanics, different measurement and different timelines, and producing a unified account of them by hand, every month, for every client. This is the operational cost of the category's split personality and it lands on one role.

## Current Tools & Gaps
An ad server for dynamic campaigns, spreadsheets for host-read, shared drives for assets, and manual monthly reporting. The gaps: two systems with no shared object model; delivery reconciled by hand; host-read verification manual; client reports assembled per client per month; and no single view of a campaign spanning both.
