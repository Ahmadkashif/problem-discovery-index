**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let one person run two incompatible businesses and report them as one — and whoever does that removes the reconciliation that consumes the category's operational capacity.

## The Problem
Media order management is a mature software category. Broadcast traffic systems and publisher ad platforms handle proposal, order, trafficking, delivery tracking, reconciliation and billing across direct-sold and programmatic demand in one workflow, because running them separately was untenable at scale. Television solved the equivalent of this decades ago with traffic and continuity systems. Audio publishers run a modern ad server alongside a spreadsheet and reconcile the two by hand.

## What Already Exists
Order and traffic management systems; unified direct and programmatic workflows; delivery tracking with reconciliation; billing integration; and proposal-to-invoice process management.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a placement delivered by a person rather than by a system. It requires: (1) host-read as a first-class placement type whose delivery is a spoken read in a published episode, which no order management system models — representing a human-delivered placement is the structural addition; (2) delivery that accrues over weeks as an episode's audience arrives, rather than at a scheduled broadcast time, which breaks the traffic system's scheduling model entirely; (3) a back catalogue that continues delivering indefinitely, so a campaign's delivery does not close; (4) publishers who are frequently very small, ruling out the enterprise deployment these systems assume; and (5) exposure-adjusted delivery, connecting to the measurement niche, since the unit being counted is under revision.

## Target Customer
Podcast networks and publishers, audio ad platform vendors, and media order management vendors for whom audio is an unserved vertical.

## Impact If Solved
Television solved unified direct and programmatic traffic decades ago and audio runs two systems and a spreadsheet. A human-delivered placement, and delivery that accrues over weeks and never closes, are what the established systems do not model.
