**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to establish whether a listener reached the advertisement, from telemetry the platforms already collect — and whoever does that converts a download into something an advertiser can recognise as an impression.

## The Problem
Display advertising had the same problem and resolved it. Advertisements were served and counted whether or not they entered the viewport; a viewability standard was defined, measurement was built, the industry accepted that a substantial share of served impressions had never been seen, inventory was restated and pricing adjusted. The transition was contested and it happened, and the market is healthier for it. Audio is in the pre-viewability position with a directly analogous problem and no standard, no measurement and no transition under way.

## What Already Exists
Viewability standards and definitions; measurement vendors and accreditation; viewable-impression trading as a norm; the precedent of an industry restating its inventory; and the operational playbook for such a transition.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is from a browser event to a listening inference. It requires: (1) exposure inferred from listening behaviour rather than observed from a rendering event, since there is no equivalent of an in-viewport signal and the measurement is a model rather than a detection — that makes the standard a statistical definition rather than a technical one, which is the substantive difference; (2) a time dimension, since an audio advertisement occupies thirty seconds during which a listener may stop, so partial exposure is the normal case rather than an edge case; (3) attention that is genuinely different, as audio is consumed while doing something else by design and a heard advertisement is not the same as a watched one; (4) no browser or software development kit to instrument, so measurement depends on the platform's own telemetry and therefore on the seller's cooperation; and (5) a fragmented publisher base with no equivalent of the display trading bodies that drove the previous transition.

## Target Customer
Audio platforms and publishers, advertiser and agency bodies, and measurement vendors who ran the display transition and could run this one.

## Impact If Solved
Display had the identical problem, defined a standard, restated its inventory and is healthier for it, and audio has not started. Exposure being inferred rather than detected makes the standard statistical rather than technical, which is the adaptation the precedent does not supply.
