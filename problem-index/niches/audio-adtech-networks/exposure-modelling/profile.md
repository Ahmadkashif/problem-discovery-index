# Exposure Modelling

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/audio-adtech-networks|Audio Adtech Networks]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to establish whether a listener reached the advertisement, from telemetry the platforms already collect — and whoever does that converts a download into something an advertiser can recognise as an impression.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.2B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of category spend
**Digital Adoption:** Low — the data exists, the model does not
**Target Buyer:** Platform product and measurement leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — a tractable modelling problem

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This contest has a technical answer and a dataset. Streaming platforms hold position-in-episode listening curves, skip behaviour and completion for enormous numbers of sessions. From that, the probability that a listener reached a given insertion point is estimable, which converts a served impression into a modelled exposure. It requires no new data collection, no governance change and no advertiser cooperation — a competitor can build it alone and demonstrate it, which is what makes it a completely different fight from the attribution question next door.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Download counting, occasional completion statistics, platform-internal listening analytics, and insertion position configuration. The gaps: listening telemetry unused for exposure; insertion points chosen without reference to where listeners stop; no per-episode or per-show exposure curve; no exposure-adjusted reporting; and download-based pricing regardless of position.
