# Cost-of-Insurance & Annuity Litigation Actuarial Experts

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/life-insurance-carriers|Life Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Actuarial and economic consulting firms retained as expert witnesses in class actions over cost-of-insurance increases on universal life blocks, annuity sales practices, and policy-administration errors. They rebuild policy values, test whether rate changes followed contract terms, and compute damages for plaintiffs or carriers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 actuaries and analysts per firm working this docket (estimate)
**Output:** Expert reports, damages models, rebuttal analyses and testimony
**Proprietary data:** Prior case models and testimony; case data is produced in discovery
**Clock:** Court-ordered expert disclosure and trial dates
**Buyer:** Practice Leader, Insurance Disputes
