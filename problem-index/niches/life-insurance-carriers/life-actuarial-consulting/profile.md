# Life Actuarial Consulting for PBR, Asset Adequacy & Transactions

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/life-insurance-carriers|Life Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
The life practices of Milliman, Oliver Wyman, WTW, the Big Four and specialist firms such as Lewis & Ellis. They build and review VM-20, VM-21 and VM-22 models, run asset adequacy testing, serve as or support appointed actuaries, implement LDTI, appraise blocks for M&A and reinsurance, and review state examinations. Carriers pay by engagement, and the report, opinion or model is the deliverable.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-1,000 life actuaries and analysts per large firm (estimate)
**Output:** Actuarial opinions and memoranda, PBR reports, asset adequacy analyses, actuarial appraisals, peer reviews
**Proprietary data:** Engagement files, cross-client benchmarks and model libraries built over many PBR and asset adequacy cycles; the underlying in-force, assumptions and models belong to the clients
**Clock:** Statutory and on the actuary personally: the annual Statement of Actuarial Opinion is filed with the annual statement (~1 March, estimate), the supporting memorandum follows on the regulator's calendar, and VM-22 took effect in January 2026
**Buyer:** Life Practice Leader
