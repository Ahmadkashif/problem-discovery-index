# Actuarial Projection Software Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/life-insurance-carriers|Life Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Moody's AXIS, FIS Prophet, WTW RiskAgility and Milliman's Integrate and MG-ALFA: the engines that project life and annuity cash flows for pricing, reserves and capital. Their product actuaries maintain regulatory model libraries for VM-20, VM-21, VM-22 and LDTI and support clients through each change.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 product actuaries and developers per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Software releases, model libraries, client support
**Proprietary data:** None of substance; models run on clients' data in clients' environments
**Clock:** Regulatory effective dates (VM-22 and GOES from January 2026)
**Buyer:** Head of Product
