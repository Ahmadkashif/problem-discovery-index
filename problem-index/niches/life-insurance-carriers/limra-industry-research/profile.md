# LIMRA Industry Research & Benchmarking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/life-insurance-carriers|Life Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
LIMRA (with LOMA) is the life and annuity industry's member-funded research association. It runs the quarterly sales surveys that produce the industry's headline figures (its annuity survey, covering 93% of the market, put 2025 retail annuity sales at a record $464.1B). It also publishes persistency and lapse studies, often with the SOA, the annual Insurance Barometer with Life Happens, and distribution and workforce benchmarks. Member carriers pay dues and buy studies and benchmarking services.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-150 researchers, analysts and survey staff (estimate)
**Output:** Quarterly sales reports, experience and persistency studies, consumer research, benchmarking for members
**Proprietary data:** Carrier-submitted sales and experience data across most of the market, collected for aggregate publication
**Clock:** Self-set quarterly release cycle; no third-party deadline
**Buyer:** Head of Research
