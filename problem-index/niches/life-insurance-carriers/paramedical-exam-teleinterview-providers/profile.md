# Paramedical Exam & Tele-Interview Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/life-insurance-carriers|Life Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors that send examiners to collect blood, urine and measurements from applicants and run telephone health interviews for carriers, plus the labs that test the specimens. Carriers pay per exam or interview.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Examiners, phlebotomists and interviewers in the thousands; analytical staff small (estimate)
**Output:** Exam results, lab panels, interview transcripts
**Proprietary data:** Lab results by applicant, used by the carrier that ordered them
**Clock:** Carrier service-level agreements
**Buyer:** VP of Insurance Services
