# Life & Annuity Trade Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/life-insurance-carriers|Life Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The American Council of Life Insurers (ACLI), whose ~275 member companies hold 93% of industry assets and which publishes the annual Life Insurers Fact Book from NAIC data, and the Insured Retirement Institute (IRI) for annuities. Both are primarily advocacy organisations whose research supports policy positions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Fewer than 10 research analysts each (estimate)
**Output:** Fact books, policy briefs, member surveys
**Proprietary data:** Tabulations of NAIC statutory data; member surveys
**Clock:** Legislative and regulatory calendars
**Buyer:** Head of Research / Chief Economist
