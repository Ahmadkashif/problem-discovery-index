# Enterprise Projection Software Packaged for Small Carriers

**Niche:** [[niches/life-insurance-carriers/small-carriers-fraternals/profile|Small Carriers & Fraternal Benefit Societies]]
**Industry:** [[industries/life-insurance-carriers|Life Insurance Carriers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** The projection engines large carriers use can do everything a small carrier needs, but arrive as a blank modelling platform that takes a specialist to configure.
**Tags:** #monte-carlo-methods #feature-engineering #workflow-orchestration #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a carrier with a handful of actuaries on the statutory calendar (PBR or its exemption, asset adequacy, filings and opinions), and whoever lets a five-person actuarial team meet every deadline without a consultant on retainer for each one takes the account.

## The Problem
A small carrier licensing an enterprise engine pays for flexibility it cannot use and still needs a consultant to build and maintain its models.

## What Already Exists
Moody's AXIS, FIS Prophet, WTW RiskAgility and Milliman's platforms, increasingly offered in the cloud, with sample model libraries.

## The Customization Gap
Small carriers need: pre-built models for the common small-carrier products (whole life, final expense, simple term, fixed deferred annuities, fraternal certificates); standard asset adequacy and VM-20 exemption workflows; regulator-ready output templates; pricing by policy count rather than by enterprise seat; and support that includes model maintenance as products change.

## Target Customer
Small carriers and fraternals currently running spreadsheets or a consultant's model.

## Impact If Solved
A maintained, auditable model owned by the carrier rather than by its consultant.
