# Life Reinsurers' Underwriting Research & Mortality Studies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/life-insurance-carriers|Life Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The underwriting research, medical, and experience-study teams at the large life reinsurers (RGA, Swiss Re, Munich Re, SCOR, Hannover Re). They write the underwriting manuals most carriers license and build the automated underwriting engines (AURA, Magnum, ALLFINANZ, hr | ReFlex). They also run mortality and accelerated-underwriting studies across cedents, which shape the treaty terms carriers sign. The reinsurer's balance sheet belongs to [[industries/reinsurers|Reinsurers]]. This pocket logs these teams as they appear from the carrier side, as suppliers of underwriting rules and mortality views.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 underwriting researchers, medical directors and experience-study actuaries per reinsurer (estimate)
**Output:** Underwriting manuals and rules, engine configurations, mortality and slippage studies, facultative decisions
**Proprietary data:** Cedents' seriatim experience across the market and decades of facultative underwriting files
**Clock:** Treaty renewal and pricing cycles; no regulator date on the research
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Research Officer / Head of Global Mortality
