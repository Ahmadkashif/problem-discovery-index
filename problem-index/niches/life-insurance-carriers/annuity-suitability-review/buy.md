# Broker-Dealer Supervision Adapted to Carrier Best-Interest Review

**Niche:** [[niches/life-insurance-carriers/annuity-suitability-review/profile|Annuity Suitability & Best-Interest Review]]
**Industry:** [[industries/life-insurance-carriers|Life Insurance Carriers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Broker-dealers supervise variable annuity sales with mature workflow and surveillance tools; carriers reviewing fixed and indexed annuities sold by independent producers mostly do not.
**Tags:** #bert #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to clear each annuity sale's best-interest review before issue with a record a market conduct examiner accepts, and whoever auto-clears the most files without missing an escalation a senior reviewer would have caught takes the account.

## The Problem
Carrier review queues run on new-business workflow tools with checklists. They have little pattern detection across producers and no consistent record of reasoning.

## What Already Exists
Broker-dealer supervision and surveillance suites used for variable annuity review under FINRA rules and Regulation Best Interest: exception-based queues, rules libraries, producer-level analytics and audit trails.

## The Customization Gap
Carriers need: the state-by-state best-interest model and New York Regulation 187 rule sets in place of FINRA rules; independent producers and IMOs rather than registered representatives; fixed and indexed product features (caps, participation rates, surrender schedules, income riders); replacement comparisons across carriers; and producer training and appointment checks the model regulation requires.

## Target Customer
Annuity compliance officers at carriers selling through independent distribution.

## Impact If Solved
Exception-based review with producer-level surveillance, built on proven supervision mechanics.
