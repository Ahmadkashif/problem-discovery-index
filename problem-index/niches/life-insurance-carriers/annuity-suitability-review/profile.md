# Annuity Suitability & Best-Interest Review

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/life-insurance-carriers|Life Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to clear each annuity sale's best-interest review before issue with a record a market conduct examiner accepts, and whoever auto-clears the most files without missing an escalation a senior reviewer would have caught takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$700M a year of suitability review staff, supervision tooling and related new-business spend at annuity writers (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.7% of US life carriers' operating expense (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — e-applications capture the forms; the review is a person reading them
**Target Buyer:** Head of Annuity New Business / Chief Compliance Officer
**Automation Potential:** Very high for clean files; reviewer judgement remains for replacements and vulnerable buyers

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The NAIC's 2020 best-interest revisions to the annuity suitability model had been implemented in 49 jurisdictions by August 2025, and New York runs its own Regulation 187. Each requires the carrier to supervise recommendations and review each sale before issue. With retail annuity sales at a record $464.1B in 2025 (LIMRA), much of it FIA, RILA and fixed-rate deferred business sold through independent producers, the review volume is large and repetitive. A minority of files carry real consumer risk, mostly replacements, older buyers and large shares of liquid assets.

## Current Tools & Gaps
E-application platforms with suitability questionnaires (iPipeline, Hexure FireLight), DTCC order processing, carrier rules engines, and broker-dealer supervision for variable products under FINRA rules. The adjacent adviser-side product analysis is logged as [[niches/wealth-management-rias/insurance-annuity-product-analytics/profile|🔍 Insurance & Annuity Product Analytics]]. Gaps: replacement comparisons read by hand; producer rationale never compared across files; thin records of why a file was cleared.
