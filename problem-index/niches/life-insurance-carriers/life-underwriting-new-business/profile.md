# Individual Life Underwriting & New Business

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/life-insurance-carriers|Life Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every competitor is fighting to place each applicant in the right risk class faster, but writing it produces two contests with different winners: issuing more cases with no fluids or APS while holding mortality slippage, and turning the referred cases' medical records into a defensible class in days. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$8B a year of carrier spend on underwriting evidence, underwriting and new-business staff, and new-business systems for individual life (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of US life carriers' operating expense (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — triage and data checks are automated at large carriers; medical evidence still arrives as faxed and scanned pages
**Target Buyer:** Chief Underwriter / Head of New Business
**Automation Potential:** High for evidence gathering and triage; medium for the final class on impaired cases

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Underwriting is where the carrier decides what risk it is buying, and the decision is locked in for the life of the policy. It is the largest operating spend outside distribution and the place where the new data sources concentrate: prescription and medical-claims histories, EHR feeds, credit-based mortality scores and MIB. So are the new regulatory tests: New York's Circular Letter No. 7 and Colorado's life governance regulation both bite on underwriting first.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Reinsurer rules engines and workbenches (Swiss Re Magnum, RGA AURA, Munich Re ALLFINANZ, Hannover Re hr | ReFlex), e-application platforms, MIB, Milliman IntelliScript, ExamOne, LexisNexis and TransUnion mortality scores, and paramedical exam vendors. The gaps: decisions recorded without reasoning; no join between class and later mortality at case level; requirement grids set years ago; and APS reading done entirely by people.
