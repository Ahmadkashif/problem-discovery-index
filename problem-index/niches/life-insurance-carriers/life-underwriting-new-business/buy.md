# Reinsurer Rules Engines Tuned to the Carrier's Own Experience

**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Most carriers decide cases with a reinsurer's rules engine and manual calibrated on pooled industry business, not on the carrier's own applicants and channels.

**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every competitor is fighting to place each applicant in the right risk class faster, but writing it produces two contests with different winners: issuing more cases with no fluids or APS while holding mortality slippage, and turning the referred cases' medical records into a defensible class in days. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
A carrier selling term through direct-to-consumer digital channels sees a different applicant pool from one selling permanent life through brokerage general agencies. Both run the same reinsurer manual and similar rule sets, with carrier tweaks made by hand.

## What Already Exists
Swiss Re Magnum, RGA AURA, Munich Re ALLFINANZ and Hannover Re hr | ReFlex: configurable rules engines with underwriting manuals and data-vendor integrations, often bundled with the reinsurance treaty.

## The Customization Gap
The carrier needs: rule performance measured on its own audited cases, by channel; a test harness to replay historical applications through proposed rule changes before deployment; documented fairness testing of each external data rule as New York and Colorado now require; and a change log that ties every rule edit to the evidence behind it.

## Target Customer
Underwriting rules and new-business analytics teams at carriers running a reinsurer engine.

## Impact If Solved
Rule changes go live in weeks with evidence rather than in quarters on judgement, and the carrier can answer a regulator's model-governance question from its own records.
