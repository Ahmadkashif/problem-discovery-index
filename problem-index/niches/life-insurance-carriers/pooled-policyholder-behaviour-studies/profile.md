# Pooled Policyholder Behaviour & Experience Study Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/life-insurance-carriers|Life Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Firms that pool policy-level experience from many carriers and sell the resulting studies of surrender, partial withdrawal, income-rider utilisation and mortality to the participants. Ruark Consulting's 2021 fixed indexed annuity study covered ~4 million policyholders from 16 companies and $264B of account value, and its variable annuity study drew on 21 companies. Detailed and company-level results are sold to participants. Actuarial consultancies run similar pooled studies, and LIMRA and the SOA publish industry studies.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 actuaries and analysts per provider (estimate)
**Output:** Annual pooled experience studies, company-versus-industry benchmarks, assumption-setting support
**Proprietary data:** Policy-level behaviour across many carriers' blocks, a dataset no single carrier or reinsurer reproduces
**Clock:** Participants' annual LDTI assumption reviews and year-end valuations; no third-party deadline on the provider
**Buyer:** Managing Director / Head of Industry Studies
