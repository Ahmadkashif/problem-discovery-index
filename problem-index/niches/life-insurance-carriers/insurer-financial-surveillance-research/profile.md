# Life Insurer Financial Surveillance & Carrier Due-Diligence Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/life-insurance-carriers|Life Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms that analyse life insurers' statutory filings to judge their financial strength for the banks, broker-dealers, IMOs and institutional buyers that distribute their annuities or buy their liabilities. Examples are ALIRT Insurance Research's quarterly carrier analyses, S&P Global Market Intelligence's statutory data and peer tools, AM Best's data products and ratings-adjacent research, and distributors' own carrier due-diligence desks. Financial strength ratings themselves sit with the rating agencies: AM Best, Demotech and KBRA under [[niches/property-casualty-insurers/insurer-financial-strength-ratings/profile|🔍 Insurer Financial Strength Rating Agencies]] (47, kill switch), and Moody's, S&P and Fitch under [[niches/financial-data-vendors/credit-rating-agency-analytics/profile|🔍 Credit Rating Agency Analytics]] (47, kill switch). The health-plan counterpart, which draws on the same S&P Global and AM Best data units, is [[niches/health-insurance-carriers/health-plan-market-data-publishers/profile|🔍 Health Plan Market, Enrollment & Financial Data Publishers]] (37). The underlying statutory record sits with [[niches/independent-insurance-agents/insurance-regulatory-data-organization/profile|🔍 Insurance Regulatory Data Organizations]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 insurer analysts per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Carrier scorecards, peer comparisons, watch lists and due-diligence reports refreshed each statutory quarter
**Proprietary data:** Analyst scores and commentary over time; the filings themselves are public statutory data
**Clock:** Statutory filing dates (quarterly, ~45 days after quarter end, and the annual statement), which set when each refresh can start
**Buyer:** Head of Insurer Research
