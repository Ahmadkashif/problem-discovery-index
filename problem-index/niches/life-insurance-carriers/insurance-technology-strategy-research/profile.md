# Insurance Technology & Strategy Research Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/life-insurance-carriers|Life Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Research and advisory firms that sell carriers studies of technology vendors, operating benchmarks and market strategy: Datos Insights (formerly Novarica and Aite), Celent, Gartner's insurance practice and Conning's insurance research publications. Carriers' CIOs and strategy heads buy subscriptions and vendor-selection advice. Vendors pay for briefings and rankings visibility.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-30 analysts per firm across insurance, with life and annuity a fraction (estimate)
**Output:** Vendor landscapes and rankings, IT spending benchmarks, strategy reports, inquiry calls
**Proprietary data:** Carrier survey responses and inquiry logs
**Clock:** None external; publication calendars are self-set
**Buyer:** Head of Insurance Research
