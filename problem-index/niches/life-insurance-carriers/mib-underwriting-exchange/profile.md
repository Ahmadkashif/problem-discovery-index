# MIB Underwriting Data Exchange

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/life-insurance-carriers|Life Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
MIB Group is the member-owned exchange through which life and health carriers report coded underwriting findings and check new applicants against them. Its members account for ~99% of individual life policies issued in the US and Canada (FTC figure, as cited). It stores coded flags rather than medical records and operates as a consumer reporting company. It also sells application-activity alerts and analytics, and EHR and other underwriting services.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 analysts and data scientists (estimate)
**Output:** Per-applicant checking results, activity alerts, fraud and misrepresentation analytics
**Proprietary data:** Decades of coded findings and application activity reported by members
**Clock:** None external
**Buyer:** Chief Analytics Officer
