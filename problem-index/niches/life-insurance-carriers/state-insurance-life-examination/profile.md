# State Insurance Departments' Life Actuarial & Financial Examination

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/life-insurance-carriers|Life Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The life-line slice of the state insurance departments already profiled as [[niches/public-adjusters/state-insurance-regulators/profile|🔍 State Insurance Departments & Market Conduct]]. Here that means the life actuaries in domiciliary states such as New York, Iowa, Texas and Connecticut who review PBR reports, actuarial opinions and asset adequacy memoranda, and who sign off on reinsurance and block transactions. Financial examiners run the risk-focused examinations alongside them. Licensing and market conduct belong to the queued Licensing & Inspection Agencies industry. The NAIC's shared valuation analysis, data and filing systems are logged as [[niches/independent-insurance-agents/insurance-regulatory-data-organization/profile|🔍 Insurance Regulatory Data Organizations]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 life actuarial reviewers in most departments (estimate); the financial examiners beside them are production staff and are not counted
**Output:** Reserve and opinion reviews, examination findings, transaction approvals
**Proprietary data:** Confidential PBR reports, examination work papers and carrier correspondence
**Clock:** Statutory examination cycles and filing review deadlines
**Buyer:** Chief Life Actuary
