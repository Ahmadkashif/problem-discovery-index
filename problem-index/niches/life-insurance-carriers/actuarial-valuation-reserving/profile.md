# Actuarial Valuation, Reserving & Quarter Close

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/life-insurance-carriers|Life Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to close each quarter's statutory, principle-based and LDTI reserves on the filing calendar with every movement attributed to a driver the auditor accepts, and whoever delivers an attributed close with the fewest overnight reruns takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$4B a year of actuarial projection software, compute, consulting and valuation staff spend at US life carriers (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~4% of US life carriers' operating expense (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High in modelling, low in the glue between models, spreadsheets and the ledger
**Target Buyer:** Chief Actuary / Head of Valuation
**Automation Potential:** High for data checks, attribution and memo drafting; low for assumption and model judgement

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Reserves are the largest liability on a life carrier's balance sheet, and they are produced under three regimes at once: statutory reserves with PBR (VM-20 for life, VM-21 for variable annuities, VM-22 for non-variable annuities from January 2026), GAAP under LDTI, and often an offshore or IFRS 17 basis. The NAIC calendar, quarterly statements and auditor reviews set the deadlines. The NAIC's new economic scenario generator, built by Conning, replaced the Academy generator from 2026, which changed every stochastic run at once.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Projection engines (Moody's AXIS, FIS Prophet, WTW RiskAgility, Milliman Integrate and MG-ALFA), cloud grid compute, close-management tools used by finance, and consultancies for PBR implementation and peer review. Gaps: extract and assumption checks done after the run; attribution by manual step-through; memos rewritten every quarter; assumption tables without governed version history.
