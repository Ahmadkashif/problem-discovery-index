# SOA Research Institute & Industry Experience Studies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/life-insurance-carriers|Life Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Society of Actuaries Research Institute and its volunteer committees publish industry mortality, lapse and accelerated-underwriting studies and develop the tables used in valuation, including tables built from experience carriers report to the NAIC under VM-51. Studies are free or member-funded, and the profession and regulators use them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-30 research staff plus volunteer committees (estimate)
**Output:** Experience studies, mortality and lapse tables, practice surveys
**Proprietary data:** Contributed and NAIC-collected experience, used under data-sharing arrangements
**Clock:** Table development tied to Valuation Manual adoption cycles
**Buyer:** Managing Director of Research
