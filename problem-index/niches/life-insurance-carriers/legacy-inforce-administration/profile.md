# Legacy In-Force Administration & Block Conversions

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/life-insurance-carriers|Life Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to move decades-old policies off legacy administration systems with every cash value, loan and dividend reconciled to the penny, and whoever converts a block with the fewest unreconciled policies at cut-over takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3B a year of legacy administration, life and annuity TPA and conversion spend (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of US life carriers' operating expense (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — product rules live in COBOL and in the heads of a few administrators; contracts are scanned images
**Target Buyer:** Head of In-Force Operations / COO
**Automation Potential:** Medium — reconciliation and rule extraction automate well; exception handling does not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Life policies outlive the systems that issued them. A carrier may run several admin platforms (CyberLife, LifePRO, Vantage, home-grown mainframes) for blocks written from the 1970s onward, many of them closed to new business. Every conversion, outsourcing to a TPA, or sale or reinsurance of the block to an in-force acquirer such as Fortitude Re or Talcott depends on recalculating each policy's values under its own contract terms and proving they match. This is a different contest from configuring new products on a modern platform, which [[industries/insurtech-platforms|Insurtech Platforms]] covers under [[niches/insurtech-platforms/policy-admin-and-billing/profile|🎯 Policy Administration & Billing]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Modern admin platforms (Equisoft, Sapiens, FAST, EIS), life and annuity TPAs (Infosys McCamish, DXC, Andesa, SE2), mainframe modernisation tooling, and consultancies running conversions. Gaps: product rules undocumented; value recalculation done in spreadsheets on samples; contract terms only in images; conversion testing that cannot cover every policy.
