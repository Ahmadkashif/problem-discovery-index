# The Contract Terms That Live Only in a Scanned Image

**Niche:** [[niches/life-insurance-carriers/legacy-inforce-administration/profile|Legacy In-Force Administration & Block Conversions]]
**Industry:** [[industries/life-insurance-carriers|Life Insurance Carriers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** When a policyholder asks what their 1990s policy guarantees, the answer is in a scanned contract an administrator has to find and read.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #data-integration #worker-facing #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to move decades-old policies off legacy administration systems with every cash value, loan and dividend reconciled to the penny, and whoever converts a block with the fewest unreconciled policies at cut-over takes the account.

## The Problem
Guaranteed interest rates, cost-of-insurance maximums, nonforfeiture options and dividend options sit in contract images and riders, with state amendments. Service staff and conversion teams look them up policy by policy. Mistakes become complaints and, in cost-of-insurance disputes, litigation.

## Why It's Still Broken
Imaging projects scanned the paper but never extracted the terms. Each form series looks different, and the people who knew them are leaving.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Extract the key terms from every form series once, verify them against a sample of administered values, and link each policy to its structured terms so service, conversion and actuarial teams query the same record.

## Who Feels the Pain
Policy service representatives; conversion analysts; compliance teams answering complaints; actuaries defending cost-of-insurance changes.

## Impact If Fixed
Minutes instead of hours per contract question, and a single source of contract truth before any conversion begins.
