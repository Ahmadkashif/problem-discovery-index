# Workers' Compensation Claim Analytics Adapted to Group LTD

**Niche:** [[niches/life-insurance-carriers/disability-claims-management/profile|Disability Income Claims Management]]
**Industry:** [[industries/life-insurance-carriers|Life Insurance Carriers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Workers' compensation carriers already buy claim-severity and duration analytics; group disability claims share the medicine but not the contract.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #survival-analysis #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #compliance #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to decide each disability claim on a complete, organised medical file before its ERISA deadline and to return more claimants to work, and whoever shortens average claim duration without raising the appeal-overturn and litigation rate takes the account.

## The Problem
Disability carriers build duration and escalation models in-house, slowly, while a mature analytics category serves workers' compensation claims with severity scoring, litigation prediction and provider analytics.

## What Already Exists
Workers' compensation claim analytics vendors and TPA platforms that score claims at intake for severity, duration and attorney involvement, using medical, wage and jurisdiction data.

## The Customization Gap
Group LTD needs: own-occupation and any-occupation definitions and the transition at the policy's change date; offsets for Social Security disability (with SSDI award prediction) and workers' compensation; elimination periods and benefit caps; pre-existing condition and mental-nervous limitations; ERISA claims-procedure deadlines; and the absence of a state-fee-schedule medical data stream.

## Target Customer
Claims analytics and operations leaders at mid-size group disability carriers without a large in-house data science team.

## Impact If Solved
Proven claim-scoring capability adapted in months rather than built over years, with outputs examiners already understand.
