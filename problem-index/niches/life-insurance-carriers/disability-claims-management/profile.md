# Disability Income Claims Management

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/life-insurance-carriers|Life Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to decide each disability claim on a complete, organised medical file before its ERISA deadline and to return more claimants to work, and whoever shortens average claim duration without raising the appeal-overturn and litigation rate takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2B a year of claims staff, nurse case management, vocational, IME and claims-system spend on group and individual disability income (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% of US life carriers' operating expense (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — claim files are faxed and scanned medical records; decisions are written letters
**Target Buyer:** VP of Disability Claims
**Automation Potential:** Medium — record organisation, deadline tracking and drafting automate; capacity and functional judgement do not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Disability income and AD&D sit inside NAICS 524113, and group long-term disability is written mostly by life carriers. A disability claim is a multi-year relationship: monthly benefit payments, periodic medical updates, the own-occupation to any-occupation transition (typically ~24 months), Social Security and workers' compensation offsets, and, for group claims, the ERISA claims-procedure deadlines and the litigation that follows a denial. The examiner's judgement about who will recover, and how fast, drives reserves and outcomes.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Disability claims systems (vendor and in-house), absence-management platforms, duration guidelines such as those logged under [[niches/insurance-tpa/disability-duration-guideline-publishers/profile|🔍 Disability Duration & Treatment Guideline Publishers]], nurse and vocational case management, and IME vendors. Gaps: records unorganised on arrival; diaries driven by dates rather than recovery likelihood; denial letters drafted from templates that miss specifics.
