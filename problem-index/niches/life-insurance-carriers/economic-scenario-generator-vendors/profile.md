# Economic Scenario Generator Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/life-insurance-carriers|Life Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Firms that build and calibrate the stochastic interest-rate, equity and credit scenarios that life carriers' reserve, capital and hedging models run on. Conning built the NAIC's Generator of Economic Scenarios (GOES), which replaced the Academy Interest Rate Generator in the Valuation Manual from January 2026, and publishes the prescribed scenario files. Moody's and Ortec Finance sell commercial generators and calibrations for internal, offshore and economic-capital use.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 economists and quantitative researchers per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Calibrated scenario sets for each valuation date, generator software, calibration documentation
**Proprietary data:** Calibration archives and methodology; inputs are public market data
**Clock:** Valuation dates for which the prescribed scenario files must be available, and the NAIC's phase-in schedule for GOES in reserves and capital
**Buyer:** Head of Economic Scenario Research
