# Accelerated Underwriting Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/life-insurance-carriers|Life Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to issue more applicants without fluids, exams or an APS while keeping audited mortality slippage inside the pricing load, and whoever proves the highest straight-through rate at a slippage the reinsurer will sign off takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.5B a year of data, rules-engine, audit and programme spend on accelerated underwriting (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of US life carriers' operating expense (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High — the decision is data-driven end to end
**Target Buyer:** Chief Underwriter / Head of Accelerated Underwriting
**Automation Potential:** Very high for the decision; monitoring needs actuarial judgement

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Accelerated programmes decide eligible applicants, usually under an age and face-amount cap, from MIB, prescription and medical-claims histories, motor vehicle records, credit-based scores and application answers. Cases that fail triage are referred to full underwriting. The contest is a trade-off with a hard, slow measurement: each extra point of straight-through rate risks misclassification that shows up only through random holdouts, post-issue audits and, years later, claims. The SOA's 2024 monitoring of 33,000+ lives across 30 programmes found about 81% of audited cases matched the class given. That is the number every programme is judged on, alongside the reinsurer's slippage load.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Reinsurer triage models and engines, data vendors' scores (Milliman IntelliScript, LexisNexis Risk Classifier, TransUnion TrueRisk Life), and carrier holdout and audit processes. Gaps: holdout samples too small per segment; slippage estimated once a year in spreadsheets; no continuous drift monitoring; fairness testing of external data only now being built.
