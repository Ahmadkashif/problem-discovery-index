**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to issue more applicants without fluids, exams or an APS while keeping audited mortality slippage inside the pricing load, and whoever proves the highest straight-through rate at a slippage the reinsurer will sign off takes the account.

## The Problem
New York's Circular Letter No. 7 (July 2024) says a carrier cannot rely on a vendor's claim that external data is non-discriminatory. Colorado's life governance regulation, effective November 2023, requires a risk-management framework and annual attestations from December 2024. Accelerated programmes stack several external data sources and a triage model, and few carriers have an inventory of them, let alone documented testing.

## What Already Exists
Model-risk management and fair-lending platforms built for banks under supervisory model-risk guidance: model inventories, validation workflows, monitoring, and disparate-impact testing with inferred demographics (BISG-style methods).

## The Customization Gap
Underwriting needs: risk class and premium as outcomes rather than approve/decline; testing that respects actuarial justification (mortality differences that are real); handling of medical and prescription data under HIPAA authorisations and FCRA; vendor-score lineage across version changes; and output in the forms New York and Colorado ask for.

## Target Customer
Chief underwriters, chief actuaries and model governance leads at carriers running accelerated programmes in New York or Colorado.

## Impact If Solved
A defensible, repeatable record for regulators and reinsurers, built on a tested discipline instead of a bespoke spreadsheet per programme.
