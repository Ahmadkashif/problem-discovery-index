# Brokerage General Agency Case Design & Underwriting Desks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/life-insurance-carriers|Life Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Brokerage general agencies and IMOs that place life cases with carriers for independent producers. Their case designers, advanced-markets specialists and underwriting advocates shop impaired cases informally across carriers and negotiate offers. The agencies themselves sit with [[industries/independent-insurance-agents|Independent Insurance Agents]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-50 case managers and underwriting advocates at large agencies (estimate)
**Output:** Informal offers, case design, placement
**Proprietary data:** Carrier offer histories on shopped cases
**Clock:** Producer and client timelines
**Buyer:** Head of Underwriting / Case Design
