# Life Expectancy Underwriters for Life Settlements

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/life-insurance-carriers|Life Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Medical underwriting firms that read an insured's medical records and issue a life expectancy (LE) report used to price the purchase of an in-force policy in the secondary market. ITM TwentyFirst and Fasano (both now under Longevity Holdings), AVS and LSI have supplied most LEs. Texas licenses LE providers and Florida registers them. Life settlement providers, investors and their lenders pay per report, and a policy is often priced on two or more LEs because providers disagree.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 medical underwriters, physicians and actuaries per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Per-insured LE reports and mortality multipliers; actual-to-expected studies of their own past LEs
**Proprietary data:** Years of LE estimates joined to tracked deaths across the insured lives they have underwritten; the firms self-publish A/E ratios (Fasano reported 97%-102% across trailing periods to mid-2023)
**Clock:** Transaction timelines set by providers and investors; no regulator or court date on the report
**Buyer:** Chief Medical Officer / Chief Actuary

**Kill switches:** none live. The firms read identified insureds' medical records under the insured's authorisation, and state life settlement laws restrict disclosure of insureds' identities and medical information (the statutory text was not checked). They do not bar the providers' own aggregate use of their archive, which the leading firms already practise when they self-publish actual-to-expected ratios. The HIPAA basis of the stop-loss pocket does not apply: LE providers are not covered entities.
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. The purest "insight is the invoice" pocket around life insurance, with a same-shaped deliverable at volume and a graded archive, held back by a market of a handful of firms and no third-party clock.
