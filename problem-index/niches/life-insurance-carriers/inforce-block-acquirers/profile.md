# In-Force Block Acquirers & Closed-Block Consolidators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/life-insurance-carriers|Life Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Capital-backed consolidators that buy or reinsure closed and in-force life and annuity blocks from carriers and then administer them at scale: Fortitude Re, Talcott, Wilton Re, Global Atlantic, Resolution Life and peers. Lincoln reinsured blocks to Fortitude Re in 2023 ($10.6B recoverable at end-2025) and ~$5.8B of guaranteed UL statutory reserves to Talcott effective October 2026. Unum ceded $3.4B of long-term care reserves to Fortitude Re in 2025. Their deal actuaries and data teams price each block from the seller's data room. Most of them are reinsurers by licence, so their balance sheets also belong to [[industries/reinsurers|Reinsurers]], where the P&C counterpart is profiled as [[niches/reinsurers/pc-legacy-runoff-acquirers/profile|🔍 P&C Legacy & Run-Off Acquirers]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 deal actuaries, experience analysts and integration staff per acquirer (estimate)
**Output:** Block valuations and bids, experience re-studies, integration and conversion plans
**Proprietary data:** Experience on blocks already acquired, plus data rooms from every block bid on
**Clock:** Bid deadlines set by the seller's adviser
**Buyer:** Head of Transaction Pricing / Chief Actuary
