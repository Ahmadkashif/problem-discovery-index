# Payroll Connector APIs Adapted to Agency Validation Rules

**Niche:** [[niches/mortgage-lenders/income-asset-verification/profile|Income, Asset & Employment Verification]]
**Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-lenders|Mortgage Lenders]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Payroll and bank-data APIs built for fintech onboarding and gig-economy lending return raw data; a mortgage lender needs a report the agency will accept for relief.
**Tags:** #data-integration #feature-engineering #compliance #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to verify income, employment and assets from third-party data so the agencies grant rep-and-warrant relief on that component, and whoever validates the largest share of files at the lowest cost per loan takes the account.

## The Problem
Consumer-permissioned payroll and bank connections cover many borrowers the payroll-data databases miss, but lenders often cannot use the output for validation because it is not delivered in an approved report from an authorised vendor or misses data requirements.

## What Already Exists
Payroll and bank-data APIs used in consumer lending, earned-wage access, tenant screening and fintech onboarding.

## The Customization Gap
The mortgage layer: delivery through vendors authorised under the DU validation service and Freddie Mac's programme; minimum history requirements (12 months of data for income validated from an asset report); report close-by dates (four months for income from an asset report, 15 days for employment); income calculation that matches DU's so validation does not fail on method; re-verification before closing; and integration into the origination system's verification ordering.

## Target Customer
Operations leaders and product managers at lenders and at verification vendors entering mortgage.

## Impact If Solved
More files validated through cheaper channels, and verification reports the underwriter does not have to re-check by hand.
