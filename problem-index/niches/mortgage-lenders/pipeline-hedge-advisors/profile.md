# Pipeline Hedge & Best-Execution Advisors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-lenders|Mortgage Lenders]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Independent capital-markets advisors run the interest-rate hedge for lenders that lock rates but have no trading desk of their own. MCT, MIAC (through its MarketShield service), Vice Capital Markets, ALM First, Apollo Hedge Advisors and Blue Water are among the firms Freddie Mac lists as integrated secondary market advisors. They model each client's pull-through, recommend and often execute forward TBA trades, deliver daily position and mark-to-market reports, and choose best execution for every closed loan across agency cash windows, MBS pooling and aggregator bids. Lenders pay monthly fees or basis points on volume. Optimal Blue's CompassPoint and Resitrader do the same work inside the pricing engine analysed as [[niches/mortgage-brokers/product-pricing-engines/profile|🔍 Product & Pricing Engines]] (53, indexed under Mortgage Brokers); this pocket is the independent advisory layer.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~15–80 hedge analysts, quantitative modellers and client advisors per firm (estimate); TBA traders and position-report production are not counted. MCT alone supported more than 150 lenders on its hedging and loan-sale programme in 2023
**Output:** Daily pipeline position and coverage reports, hedge trade recommendations, pull-through models, best-execution and loan-sale analyses, monthly hedge-results reviews
**Proprietary data:** Lock-to-fund outcomes, renegotiations and fallout across every client's pipeline; investor bids on every loan run through best execution; the firm's own dated recommendation history and the hedge results that followed
**Clock:** The market's daily open and close, the SIFMA TBA settlement and notification calendar, lock expirations, and mandatory commitment delivery dates with pair-off fees
**Buyer:** Head of Hedge Advisory or COO of the advisory firm

**Kill switches:** none, on checked evidence: MCT publishes monthly Lock Volume Indices and a daily Rate Lock Index aggregated from its client lenders' lock records, so its client terms permit aggregate use of client lock data. The evidence covers lock volumes and locked rates by purpose, not funded-or-fallout outcomes, which travel in the same records; any loan-level cross-client model must still be built on de-identified data under GLBA's service-provider limits. No incumbent owns the workflow: advisors connect to every major pricing engine and origination system.
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. Q2 is 3 because position reports come out of the advisors' systems and TBA execution is production, so analyst headcount grows more slowly than the client list (consistent with the MSR valuation pocket, where the same firms score 2). Q4 is 5, the same clock as the pricing-engine pocket, and Q5 is 3 for a field of roughly eight to ten firms.
