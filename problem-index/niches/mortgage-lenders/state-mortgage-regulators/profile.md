# State Mortgage Regulators & Multistate Examination

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-lenders|Mortgage Lenders]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State banking and financial regulators license nonbank lenders, branches and loan officers through NMLS (used by nearly 597,000 companies and individuals in 2024), collect the quarterly Mortgage Call Report, and examine licensees on their own or through multistate examinations the states coordinate collectively. Texas's regulator alone supervised 4,753 mortgage entities in late 2025.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Examiners are production staff working file by file; analytics units built on Mortgage Call Report data are under 10 per state (estimate)
**Output:** Licensing decisions, examination reports, enforcement actions
**Proprietary data:** Quarterly Mortgage Call Reports and licensing records for every state-licensed lender
**Clock:** Examination cycles and the call report's 45-day deadline after each quarter
**Buyer:** Commissioner or Director of Mortgage Supervision
