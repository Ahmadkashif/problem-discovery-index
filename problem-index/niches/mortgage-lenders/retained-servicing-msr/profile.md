# Retained Servicing & MSR Management

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-lenders|Mortgage Lenders]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is "Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to protect the value of the lender's mortgage servicing rights", but that value is defended in two contests with different winners: keeping the borrower when they refinance or move, which goes to whoever reaches them first with a credible offer, and servicing the loans that stay at a cost and audit standard below the MSR's assumptions, which goes to the subservicers and servicing platforms. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$4B a year in servicing platforms, subservicing fees, recapture tooling and MSR valuation and oversight services bought by nonbank lenders that retain servicing (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~30–35% of mortgage lenders' technology, data and outsourced-service spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium-High — payments and investor reporting are fully systematised; recapture targeting and subservicer oversight are not
**Target Buyer:** Chief Servicing Officer or Head of Portfolio Management
**Automation Potential:** High for payment processing and reporting; medium for loss mitigation and oversight

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Many independent mortgage banks keep the servicing rights on loans they sell to the agencies, because servicing income rises when rates rise and origination falls. FSOC found mortgage servicing rights make up 10–30% of nonbank lenders' assets, and nonbanks handled 66% of Agency servicing by January 2024. The asset is volatile: its value depends on how fast borrowers prepay, how many the lender recaptures when they do, and what it costs to service the rest. MBA's figures show how thin the offset can be: net servicing financial income fell from $301 per loan in 2024 to $89 in 2025.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Servicing runs on ICE MSP or Sagent, frequently through subservicers such as Cenlar, Dovenmuehle or LoanCare. Recapture runs through marketing platforms (Total Expert, Homebot) and the consumer-direct team. MSR values come from third-party valuation firms (MIAC, Incenter, MCT). The gap: the assumptions in the quarterly MSR mark (prepayment speed, recapture rate, cost to service, delinquency) are never systematically compared with what the lender's own book actually did.
