# Correspondent Aggregators' Seller Oversight Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-lenders|Mortgage Lenders]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Large aggregators that buy closed loans from smaller lenders through correspondent channels (Pennymac, AmeriHome within Western Alliance, NewRez and bank correspondent desks among them) approve and tier their sellers, review loans before purchase, and run seller-performance analytics on defect rates, early payment defaults and repurchase history, which set each seller's pricing and eligibility.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10–50 seller-oversight and analytics staff at the large aggregators (estimate); purchase-review underwriters are production staff and not counted
**Output:** Seller scorecards, approval and pricing tiers, purchase-review findings, repurchase demands to sellers
**Proprietary data:** Loan-level purchase-review findings and loan performance by seller across hundreds of correspondents
**Clock:** Commitment delivery and purchase deadlines
**Buyer:** Head of Correspondent Risk (an internal function)
