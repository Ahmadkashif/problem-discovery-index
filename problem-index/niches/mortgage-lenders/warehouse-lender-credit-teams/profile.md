# Warehouse Lenders' Mortgage Finance Credit Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-lenders|Mortgage Lenders]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Banks that provide warehouse lines (FSOC counted about 235 with ~$285B of total limits at the end of 2023) run mortgage finance credit teams that underwrite each lender client, set advance rates, haircuts, sublimits and aging limits, monitor financial covenants and collateral exceptions, and watch for fraud such as loans pledged on two lines. They also extend MSR-backed facilities to the same clients.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10–40 credit and portfolio analysts at the larger warehouse banks (estimate)
**Output:** Counterparty credit approvals, line terms and renewals, covenant monitoring, collateral exception and aging reports
**Proprietary data:** Funding, dwell, exception and investor-purchase data across dozens of lender clients, plus their quarterly financials
**Clock:** Daily funding and purchase cycles; monthly and quarterly covenant tests
**Buyer:** Head of Mortgage Warehouse Credit (an internal function)
