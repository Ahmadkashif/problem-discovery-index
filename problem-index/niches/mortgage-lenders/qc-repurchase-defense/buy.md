# Claims Management Adapted to Repurchase Demands

**Niche:** [[niches/mortgage-lenders/qc-repurchase-defense/profile|Quality Control & Repurchase Defence]]
**Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-lenders|Mortgage Lenders]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Insurers and legal departments run disputes through matter-management systems with deadlines, evidence and reserves; lenders run repurchase demands from a spreadsheet.
**Tags:** #compliance #workflow-orchestration #descriptive-statistics #large-language-models #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to find a file's material defects before the agency does and to resolve the ones the agency finds by cure or fee rather than buyback, and whoever lowers the lender's repurchase cost per loan sold takes the account.

## The Problem
Defect notices, repurchase demands from aggregators, indemnification agreements and make-whole requests each run on different clocks and counterparties. Reserves for repurchase losses are estimated in finance with little link to the open cases.

## What Already Exists
Claims and legal matter-management platforms used by insurers and corporate legal teams, with case intake, deadlines, document management, reserves and reporting.

## The Customization Gap
The mortgage layer: agency and aggregator notice types and response windows; defect taxonomies by investor; outcome types (cure, rescission, fee in lieu, repurchase, indemnification, make-whole) with their calculations; links to the loan file and QC history; recourse claims against brokers, correspondents and appraisers; and reserve estimates driven by open cases.

## Target Customer
Chief credit officers and controllers at lenders with recurring agency and aggregator demand volume.

## Impact If Solved
No missed deadlines, a reserve finance can defend, and a clear view of which counterparties and loan types generate losses.
