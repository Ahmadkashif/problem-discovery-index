# Quality Control & Repurchase Defence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-lenders|Mortgage Lenders]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to find a file's material defects before the agency does and to resolve the ones the agency finds by cure or fee rather than buyback, and whoever lowers the lender's repurchase cost per loan sold takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600M a year in prefunding and post-closing QC reviews, QC software, reverification fees and repurchase-defence staff and counsel (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of mortgage lenders' technology, data and outsourced-service spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — QC checklists run in software, but findings are prose, agency defect notices arrive through portals and rebuttals are assembled by hand from the file
**Target Buyer:** Chief Credit Officer or VP of Quality Control
**Automation Potential:** High for sampling, classification and rebuttal drafting; low for final rebuttal judgement

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every agency seller must run a QC programme to the Selling Guide's rules (monthly selection, a 10% random sample plus discretionary picks, a 90-day cycle) and must answer agency findings on the loans the agencies review themselves. Fannie Mae's 2024 Notice of Potential Defect gives the lender 30 days to cure or respond, and Freddie Mac has offered a fee instead of repurchase for performing loans with minor defects since 2025, so a well-argued response can now end several ways. The economics are stark: an average buyback cost ~$32,288 in the Reggora and STRATMOR study, and income and appraisal issues drove 57% of requests.

## Current Tools & Gaps
ACES Audit Technology and similar QC platforms; outsourced QC firms, analysed as [[niches/mortgage-brokers/mortgage-qc-compliance-audit/profile|🔍 Mortgage Quality Control & Compliance Audit]]; fraud and data-integrity tools before funding, analysed as [[niches/mortgage-brokers/loan-quality-fraud-analytics/profile|🔍 Loan Quality & Fraud Analytics]]; the agencies' own loan-quality portals. The gaps: discretionary selection by fixed rules; findings that cannot be trended; rebuttals rebuilt from scratch every time; and no join between agency outcomes and the origination decision.
