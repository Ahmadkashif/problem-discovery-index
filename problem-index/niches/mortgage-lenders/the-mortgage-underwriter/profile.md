# The Mortgage Underwriter

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-lenders|Mortgage Lenders]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let an underwriter clear a file in one full review with every income and asset figure traced to its source, and whoever cuts condition re-reviews without moving the lender's QC defect rate takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$700M a year in underwriting workbench tools, income calculators, contract underwriting and training spend (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~6% of mortgage lenders' technology, data and outsourced-service spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — the file is digital and AUS findings are automatic, but review is reading PDFs and condition clearing is manual
**Target Buyer:** VP of Underwriting or Chief Credit Officer
**Automation Potential:** Medium-High for calculation and condition matching; low for the judgement itself

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The underwriter sits between automated underwriting and the defect data. DU or LPA decides the credit; the underwriter decides whether the data behind it is true and durable, and signs the approval the lender later defends. The tools around them are built for processors (document collection) and for the machine (AUS), not for the person whose judgement the agencies second-guess. Their workforce swings with the rate cycle: hired in refinance booms, cut in busts, with contract underwriters filling the gaps. The tacit side of the job is analysed in the industry's [[problems/mortgage-lenders/high-impact|high-impact problem]]; this niche is the daily workbench.

## Current Tools & Gaps
The origination system's underwriting screens, AUS findings, income calculators (Fannie Mae's tools, LoanBeam, Ocrolus), verification reports, and contract underwriting firms for overflow. The broker-side processing problem is analysed in [[niches/mortgage-brokers/condition-clearing-automation/profile|⚡ Condition Clearing & File Processing]], and the brokers who package self-employed files for these underwriters in [[niches/mortgage-brokers/self-employed-borrower-specialists/profile|🟠 Self-Employed Borrower Specialists]]. The gaps: conditions come back piecemeal and each return means re-reading the file; nothing shows what changed between a document's versions; and the senior underwriter's way of reading a file is passed on only by sitting next to them.
