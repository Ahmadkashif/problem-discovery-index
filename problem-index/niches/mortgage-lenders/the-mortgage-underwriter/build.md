# A Case Library of the Best Underwriters' Calls

**Niche:** [[niches/mortgage-lenders/the-mortgage-underwriter/profile|The Mortgage Underwriter]]
**Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-lenders|Mortgage Lenders]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Junior underwriters learn to read a file by sitting next to a senior one, and every layoff cycle removes some of the seniors and most of the sitting.
**Tags:** #tacit-knowledge-ml #large-language-models #k-nearest-neighbors #word-embeddings #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let an underwriter clear a file in one full review with every income and asset figure traced to its source, and whoever cuts condition re-reviews without moving the lender's QC defect rate takes the account.

## The Problem
The judgement that separates a senior underwriter (which deposit to question, when a declining Schedule C needs a third year, which employer letter looks wrong) is taught informally. Lenders that cut underwriting staff by a third after the 2021 boom (estimate) rehired juniors and contract underwriters without the seniors' time to train them, and defect rates on income moved accordingly.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Training vendors teach the Selling Guide, not the lender's own hard cases. The hard cases exist in the lender's files, QC findings and defect notices, but nobody has turned them into something searchable at the moment a junior faces a similar file.

## What to Build
A case library built from the lender's own files where a senior underwriter's condition, a QC finding or an agency notice turned on a judgement call, each written up as a short structured case (the evidence, the question, the call, the outcome) and indexed by document features. When an underwriter opens a file, the tool retrieves the most similar past cases and shows how the best underwriters handled them and how those loans performed.

## Target Customer
VPs of underwriting at lenders with mixed-seniority teams and contract underwriting overflow.

## Impact If Built
Faster ramp for new underwriters, more consistent calls on hard files, and the seniors' judgement retained in a form that survives the next round of cuts.
