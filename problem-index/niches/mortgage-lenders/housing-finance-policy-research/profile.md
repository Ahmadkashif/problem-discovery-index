# Housing Finance Policy Research Centres

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-lenders|Mortgage Lenders]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Industry- and philanthropy-funded research centres (the Urban Institute's Housing Finance Policy Center and AEI's Housing Center among them) publish monthly chartbooks, credit-availability and mortgage-risk indices, and policy analysis on agency reform, credit access and lender practice; Urban's 2023 study of GSE repurchase activity, cited in this industry, is typical. Lenders, agencies and trade groups use the work in policy debates rather than paying for it per report.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10–40 researchers (estimate)
**Output:** Chartbooks, indices, policy papers and briefings
**Proprietary data:** Assembled public and licensed datasets; few proprietary series
**Clock:** Self-set monthly publication
**Buyer:** Centre director, funded by sponsors
