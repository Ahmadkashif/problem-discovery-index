# Vendor Risk Platforms Adapted to Subservicing

**Niche:** [[niches/mortgage-lenders/subservicer-oversight-cost/profile|Subservicer Oversight & Cost to Service]]
**Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-lenders|Mortgage Lenders]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Vendor-risk platforms track contracts, SOC reports and due-diligence questionnaires; subservicing oversight needs loan-level servicing rules on top.
**Tags:** #compliance #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #automation #evaluation-metrics
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to service the retained book below the cost and default assumptions priced into the MSR while passing agency, investor and CFPB servicing audits, and whoever proves the lowest cost per loan with clean audit results takes the account.

## The Problem
Owners manage their subservicer as one vendor among many, with an annual risk assessment and a document checklist, when it is the vendor whose failures most directly become their own regulatory findings.

## What Already Exists
Third-party risk management platforms (Ncontracts, Venminder and similar) used across banks and lenders for vendor due diligence, contract tracking and periodic reviews.

## The Customization Gap
The servicing layer: Regulation X loss-mitigation and error-resolution timelines encoded as tests; Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac, FHA, VA and Ginnie Mae servicing requirements by investor; ingestion of the subservicer's loan-level activity and complaint files; cost-per-loan benchmarks by delinquency status; and an audit trail tying each oversight finding to remediation.

## Target Customer
Compliance and oversight officers at MSR owners using one or more subservicers.

## Impact If Solved
Oversight that examiners and agencies recognise as risk-based, built on tools the owner already licenses.
