# Securitisation Due Diligence (Third-Party Review) Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-lenders|Mortgage Lenders]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Third-party review firms re-underwrite loans before they enter private-label securitisations (credit, regulatory compliance, valuation and data-tape integrity) and grade each loan from A to D under rating agency criteria. S&P's 2023 list of reviewed firms includes Clayton, Consolidated Analytics, Opus Capital Markets Consultants, Selene Diligence and SitusAMC, and each rating agency keeps its own acceptance list. Issuers, aggregators and whole-loan buyers pay per loan; a summary of the findings is disclosed before the deal's first sale. Non-QM issuance of ~$52B in the first nine months of 2025 (Morningstar DBRS) drives most of the volume, and the same firms review whole-loan trades.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 deal analytics, grading-methodology and client-reporting staff per large firm (estimate); the 100–600 review underwriters per firm (estimate) re-underwrite files at volume and are production staff, not counted for the gate
**Output:** Loan-level credit, compliance and valuation grades, exception reports, data-tape reconciliations, and the due diligence summary disclosed for the deal
**Proprietary data:** Hundreds of thousands of re-underwritten non-agency loans with grades and exceptions across issuers and vintages, joinable to public RMBS performance
**Clock:** Each deal's pricing and first-sale date, which the issuer sets, with the review summary disclosed beforehand
**Buyer:** President or Head of the due diligence practice

**Kill switches:** live — the review is performed on issuers' and sellers' loan files, full of borrower credit reports, tax returns and bank statements under GLBA and FCRA, and the grades belong to the client and the deal. This is the same judgement the vault applies to [[niches/mortgage-brokers/mortgage-qc-compliance-audit/profile|🔍 Mortgage Quality Control & Compliance Audit]] (52, kill switch), the agency-loan counterpart of this work.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch and below threshold. The gate is passed only on the analytics layer above the review floor; the mass of the work is re-underwriting production. Q4 is 4 because the deal date is the issuer's schedule, with a disclosure deadline hung on it.
