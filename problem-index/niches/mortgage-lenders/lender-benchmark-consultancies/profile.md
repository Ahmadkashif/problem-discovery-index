# Lender Operating Benchmark & Strategy Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-lenders|Mortgage Lenders]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Mortgage strategy consultancies sell lenders a view of how they compare with peers and what to change. STRATMOR Group's PeerView programme benchmarks lenders' costs, staffing, productivity and margins from contributed data, its MortgageSAT surveys measure borrower satisfaction, and its consultants advise on operations, technology selection and M&A; STRATMOR co-authored the 2023–24 repurchase cost study cited in this industry. MBA's performance reports are the association counterpart, analysed as [[niches/mortgage-brokers/mortgage-association-research/profile|🔍 Mortgage Association Research]] (42).

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10–40 analysts and consultants (estimate)
**Output:** Peer benchmark reports, borrower satisfaction scores, cost and productivity diagnostics, technology and M&A advice
**Proprietary data:** Lender-contributed cost, staffing and production data, and borrower survey responses
**Clock:** Self-set programme cycles
**Buyer:** Head of research and benchmarking
