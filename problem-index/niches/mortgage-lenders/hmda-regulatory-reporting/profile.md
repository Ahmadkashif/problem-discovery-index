# HMDA & Regulatory Data Reporting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-lenders|Mortgage Lenders]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce a HMDA register and NMLS Mortgage Call Report that pass every edit and an examiner's sample test without a pre-deadline scrub, and whoever catches data-field errors at the point of origination rather than in February takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M a year in HMDA and call-report software, data scrubbing, fair lending reporting services and compliance staff time (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2–3% of mortgage lenders' technology, data and outsourced-service spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High — the data comes from the origination system; the checking is manual and late
**Target Buyer:** Chief Compliance Officer
**Automation Potential:** Very high

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
HMDA requires every covered lender to report dozens of data fields for each application, from demographics and pricing to property and underwriting data, in an annual register due March 1, or quarterly within 60 days for lenders with 60,000 or more covered loans and applications. About 4,898 institutions filed for 2024 (CFPB). Licensed nonbanks also file the NMLS Mortgage Call Report, whose loan activity section is due 45 days after each quarter, and the HMDA data is the basis of every fair lending examination and redlining analysis. Errors above examiners' resubmission thresholds mean refiling and scrutiny.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Origination systems' HMDA modules, the federal HMDA platform's edit checks, compliance vendors' scrubbing and fair lending analytics, and spreadsheets. Market-share products built on the public HMDA data are analysed as [[niches/mortgage-brokers/mortgage-market-research/profile|🔍 Mortgage Market Research]]. The gaps: errors are found in a year-end scrub months after the loan officer or processor entered the data, and the same field errors recur because nobody traces them to their source.
