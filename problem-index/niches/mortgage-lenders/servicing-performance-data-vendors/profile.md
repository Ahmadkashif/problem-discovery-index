# Servicing Performance Data Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-lenders|Mortgage Lenders]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
ICE (McDash data and the Mortgage Monitor research, drawn from its servicing platform and contributing servicers) and Cotality's LoanPerformance data assemble monthly loan-level payment, delinquency, forbearance and prepayment records across most of the serviced US market, and sell datasets, benchmarks and research to lenders, servicers, investors and regulators.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50–300 data, research and product staff (estimate)
**Output:** Loan-level performance datasets, delinquency and prepayment benchmarks, monthly market research
**Proprietary data:** Monthly loan-level servicing records across tens of millions of loans and many years
**Clock:** Monthly data cycles
**Buyer:** Head of data products
