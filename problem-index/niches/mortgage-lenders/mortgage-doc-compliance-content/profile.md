# Mortgage Document & Compliance Content Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-lenders|Mortgage Lenders]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Document and compliance vendors (Wolters Kluwer, Docutech within First American, DocMagic, IDS) maintain the state-by-state library of disclosures, notes, riders and closing forms and the compliance tests that run on every loan: TRID tolerances, high-cost and higher-priced mortgage tests, state-specific disclosures and fee limits. Lenders pay per document package, often with a compliance warranty, through integrations with their origination system.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30–200 attorneys, compliance analysts and form specialists per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Compliant disclosure and closing packages, compliance test results, regulatory change bulletins
**Proprietary data:** A maintained library of federal and state mortgage document rules and the vendor's interpretations of them, plus test results across millions of packages
**Clock:** Federal and state effective dates: annual Regulation Z threshold adjustments, state law changes and agency form updates
**Buyer:** VP of compliance content
