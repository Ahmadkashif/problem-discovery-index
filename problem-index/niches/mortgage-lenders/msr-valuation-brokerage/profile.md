# MSR Valuation & Servicing-Rights Brokerage

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-lenders|Mortgage Lenders]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Valuation firms mark lenders' mortgage servicing rights each month or quarter for financial statements, MSR financing facilities and agency net-worth tests, and broker bulk and flow sales of servicing. MIAC, Incenter, MCT's MSR desk and SitusAMC are among them. MIAC reported valuing $64.65T of residential MSR UPB across its recurring marks for more than 200 institutions in 2023, and brokering $57.6B of MSRs that year; MCT says it distributes more than 400 third-party valuations a year.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 valuation and brokerage analysts per firm (estimate); MIAC states its MSR valuation team is 11 professionals
**Output:** MSR fair-value marks and assumption reports, bid tapes and trade analyses, monthly market updates
**Proprietary data:** Servicing tapes and marks across hundreds of portfolios, and every bid received in the sales it brokers
**Clock:** Quarter-end financial reporting and audit, MSR facility borrowing-base dates, bid deadlines in brokered sales
**Buyer:** Head of MSR valuation and brokerage

**Kill switches:** live — each valuation runs on the client's loan-level servicing tape under a service agreement, and no checked evidence shows those agreements permit aggregate reuse of client tapes; the firms' published monthly MSR market updates do not say what data they are built on. Auditor reliance on the marks also slows any method change.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch, and below threshold regardless. The mark is the invoice and the bid history is unique, but a team of about a dozen values trillions of dollars of servicing with a model, so growth comes from more portfolios per valuer rather than more valuers, and the market is a handful of firms.
