# Agency Counterparty & Issuer Risk Oversight

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-lenders|Mortgage Lenders]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Fannie Mae's and Freddie Mac's counterparty risk teams, Ginnie Mae's issuer oversight staff and FHFA monitor the nonbank lenders and servicers the government-backed market depends on (FSOC counts nonbanks servicing ~$6T for the Agencies), testing net worth, capital and liquidity, reviewing operations, tracking loan quality and servicing performance, and setting eligibility standards. Their guideline and collateral functions are analysed as [[niches/mortgage-brokers/gse-selling-guide-content/profile|🔍 Investor Guideline Content & Interpretation]] and [[niches/real-estate-appraisers/gse-collateral-policy-analytics/profile|🔍 Secondary Market Collateral Policy & Analytics]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–80 counterparty credit analysts and seller-servicer reviewers at each of Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac (estimate); Ginnie Mae's issuer oversight is a smaller staff of probably under 30 supported by contractors, and FHFA's counterparty supervision smaller again (estimates). The gate is passed on each enterprise's team, not on a sum
**Output:** Counterparty ratings, eligibility determinations, operational review findings, policy and requirement changes
**Proprietary data:** Every approved seller and servicer's financial submissions, loan-level delivery and performance data and review history
**Clock:** Quarterly financial submissions and eligibility tests
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — chartered entities in conservatorship and a government corporation
