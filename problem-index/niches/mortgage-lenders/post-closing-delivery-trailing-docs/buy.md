# Invoice Document AI Pointed at Recorded Instruments

**Niche:** [[niches/mortgage-lenders/post-closing-delivery-trailing-docs/profile|Post-Closing, Delivery & Trailing Documents]]
**Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-lenders|Mortgage Lenders]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Accounts-payable teams already use document AI to read and match thousands of invoices; post-closers still match recorded mortgages and title policies to loans by eye.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #cnns #automation #data-integration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to move a closed loan from the closing table to investor purchase and final documents with no suspense items in the fewest warehouse days, and whoever shortens dwell and clears trailing documents fastest takes the account.

## The Problem
Recorded instruments and final policies arrive as scans in every county's and every title agent's format. Each must be matched to the right loan, checked and uploaded to the investor.

## What Already Exists
Intelligent document processing platforms used for invoice and accounts-payable automation (ABBYY, Hyperscience, cloud document AI services) that classify documents, extract fields and match them to purchase orders.

## The Customization Gap
The mortgage layer: classification of recorded mortgages, deeds of trust, assignments, final title policies and MI certificates; extraction of recording stamps, book and page or instrument numbers, legal descriptions, insured amounts and endorsements; matching to the loan by borrower, property and amount; checks against the closing package and the investor's trailing-document rules; and delivery into each investor's portal.

## Target Customer
Post-closing managers at lenders and outsourced post-closing providers.

## Impact If Solved
Matching and checking move from minutes per document to seconds, so post-closers spend their time on the documents that are actually wrong.
