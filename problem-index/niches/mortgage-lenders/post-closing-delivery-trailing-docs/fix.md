# The Final Policy Nobody Is Paid to Send

**Niche:** [[niches/mortgage-lenders/post-closing-delivery-trailing-docs/profile|Post-Closing, Delivery & Trailing Documents]]
**Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-lenders|Mortgage Lenders]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Title agents are paid at closing and issue the final lender's policy months later as unpaid follow-up work, so the lender's investor deadline depends on someone with no reason to hurry.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #automation #descriptive-statistics #quick-win #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to move a closed loan from the closing table to investor purchase and final documents with no suspense items in the fewest warehouse days, and whoever shortens dwell and clears trailing documents fastest takes the account.

## The Problem
Final policies are the slowest trailing document at many lenders. The title agent must wait for recording, clear the commitment's requirements and issue the policy; by then the deal is closed and paid. Post-closers send repeated requests with little leverage.

## Why It's Still Broken
The agent's own post-closing workload is described under [[niches/title-and-settlement-offices/lien-release-final-policy-tracking/profile|🎯 Lien Release & Final Policy Tracking]]; the lender's problem is that it has no lever on it. The incentive is structural: the title premium and settlement fees are collected at closing, and nothing in the lender's relationship with the agent ties future referrals to post-closing performance. Lenders do not measure agent-level delivery times, so they cannot use them.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Track every final policy by title agent from closing to receipt, publish agent scorecards internally, share them with the agents, and route future orders (where the lender influences them) and escalations to the agent's underwriter on the basis of delivery history.

## Who Feels the Pain
Post-closers chasing policies; secondary teams paying aging fees; lenders facing repurchase requests over missing final policies.

## Impact If Fixed
Measuring and publishing agent delivery times is cheap and changes behaviour quickly; the backlog shrinks without any new technology at the agent.
