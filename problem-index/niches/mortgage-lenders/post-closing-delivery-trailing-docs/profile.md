# Post-Closing, Delivery & Trailing Documents

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-lenders|Mortgage Lenders]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to move a closed loan from the closing table to investor purchase and final documents with no suspense items in the fewest warehouse days, and whoever shortens dwell and clears trailing documents fastest takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$700M a year in post-closing staff tooling, outsourced post-closing and shipping services, custodial and document-retrieval fees (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~6% of mortgage lenders' technology, data and outsourced-service spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — eNotes and eClosings are growing, but most loans still close on wet-ink notes, recorded instruments come back from counties on their own schedule, and trailing documents are chased by email
**Target Buyer:** VP of Post-Closing and Secondary Operations
**Automation Potential:** High for document checking and tracking; low for the counties and title agents themselves

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Between funding and final document, a loan sits on a warehouse line that advances only 90–95% of its value (FSOC), so every suspended purchase and late document ties up the lender's own cash and line capacity. The work is document logistics across parties the lender does not control: settlement agents, title agents, county recorders, document custodians and investors, each with its own portal and deadline. It is where the closing table's paper meets the secondary market's data. The title agent's half of the loop, recording releases and issuing final policies, is analysed under [[niches/title-and-settlement-offices/payoffs-releases-trailing-docs/profile|🟠 Payoffs, Lien Releases & Trailing Documents]] in Title & Settlement Offices; this niche is the lender's half: getting the loan purchased, receiving each document and delivering it to the investor on time.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Origination systems' post-closing and investor delivery modules, investors' correspondent portals, document custodians, the MERS registry and eRegistry, and outsourced post-closing providers. The gaps: suspense conditions are discovered after shipping rather than predicted before it; trailing-document tracking lives in spreadsheets and email; and nobody measures which title agents and closers cause the most dwell.
