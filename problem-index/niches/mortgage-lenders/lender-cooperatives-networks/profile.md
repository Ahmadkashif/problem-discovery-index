# Lender Cooperatives & Buying Networks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-lenders|Mortgage Lenders]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Cooperative networks of independent lenders, The Mortgage Collaborative being the best known, negotiate vendor discounts, secondary-market and investor access, and preferred partner programmes (hedge advisors among them) for member lenders, and run peer events and member surveys.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analysts (estimate)
**Output:** Vendor programmes, member surveys, events
**Proprietary data:** Member surveys and programme usage
**Clock:** Self-set
**Buyer:** Network CEO
