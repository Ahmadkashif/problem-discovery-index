# Portfolio Recapture & Retention

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-lenders|Mortgage Lenders]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to reach a servicing-book borrower with a credible refinance or purchase offer before a competitor's rate alert or credit-trigger lead does, and whoever identifies the borrower's moment first takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$900M a year in recapture marketing platforms, data, consumer-direct staffing tools and trigger services used against servicing books (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7–8% of mortgage lenders' technology, data and outsourced-service spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — rate-drop campaigns are automated; purchase-move detection and loan officer follow-up are not
**Target Buyer:** Head of Consumer Direct or Chief Retention Officer
**Automation Potential:** High for targeting and outreach; medium for conversion

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A payoff ends the servicing income the MSR was valued on. If the lender originates the replacement loan, it keeps the borrower, earns a new gain on sale and usually retains the new servicing. Recapture is therefore the single assumption that most separates one lender's MSR from another's. The contest is a timing race on two kinds of event: rate-driven refinances, which every servicer sees at once, and moves and cash-out needs, which show up first in listing data, inquiries and life events.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Servicing-data marketing platforms (Total Expert, Homebot), the servicing system's own recapture modules, and credit-monitoring trigger services identify borrowers in the money or shopping. The gaps: models are tuned to rate incentive and miss purchase moves; the retention offer is rarely priced against what the borrower would be offered elsewhere; and recapture credit is split between retail loan officers and the consumer-direct team, who dispute it.
