# Marketing Automation With Servicing Data

**Niche:** [[niches/mortgage-lenders/portfolio-recapture-retention/profile|Portfolio Recapture & Retention]]
**Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-lenders|Mortgage Lenders]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Salesforce Marketing Cloud and HubSpot can run any campaign; they cannot tell whether a refinance would actually benefit the borrower or meet the agency's seasoning rules.
**Tags:** #feature-engineering #logistic-regression #compliance #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to reach a servicing-book borrower with a credible refinance or purchase offer before a competitor's rate alert or credit-trigger lead does, and whoever identifies the borrower's moment first takes the account.

## The Problem
Recapture campaigns sent to the whole book on every rate move generate complaints and unsubscribes, and offers that fail benefit tests waste loan officer time.

## What Already Exists
Enterprise marketing automation and CRM platforms (Salesforce, HubSpot) and mortgage CRMs that sit on top of them.

## The Customization Gap
The mortgage layer: rate-incentive and break-even calculations from the servicing record; FHA streamline and VA IRRRL net tangible benefit and seasoning rules; anti-churning constraints in Ginnie Mae pools; permissible-purpose limits on credit data; suppression of borrowers in loss mitigation or bankruptcy; and attribution of each recaptured loan back to the serviced loan it replaced.

## Target Customer
Marketing and consumer-direct leaders at lenders with retained servicing books.

## Impact If Solved
Fewer, better-targeted offers that convert and survive a compliance review, and recapture figures the MSR valuation can rely on.
