# Fair Lending Statistical Analysis Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-lenders|Mortgage Lenders]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Economics and compliance consultancies (BLDS, Charles River Associates, Treliant and the fair lending practices of compliance firms) run pricing and underwriting disparity regressions on a lender's HMDA and loan-level data, redlining peer analyses of lending and marketing footprints, and fairness reviews of credit and pricing models. Lenders hire them before examinations, after the annual HMDA data is published, and when responding to enforcement, usually through outside counsel so the analysis stays privileged. This is the vault's single profile of these consultancies: the same firms test card issuers' pricing and line decisions and indirect auto lenders' dealer markup, and that card and auto fair lending work, analysed from [[industries/credit-card-issuers|Credit Card Issuers]] and [[industries/sales-finance-companies|Sales Finance Companies]], is covered here.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10–60 economists, statisticians and analysts per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Fair lending risk assessments, pricing and denial disparity analyses, redlining peer comparisons, model fairness reviews, expert reports
**Proprietary data:** Methods and peer benchmarks built across engagements; client loan data is used per engagement only, and the peer data is public HMDA
**Clock:** Examination schedules, the annual HMDA filing and publication cycle, enforcement deadlines
**Buyer:** Practice leader
