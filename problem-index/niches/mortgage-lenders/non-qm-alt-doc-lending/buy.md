# Small-Business Cash-Flow Tools Adapted to Bank-Statement Loans

**Niche:** [[niches/mortgage-lenders/non-qm-alt-doc-lending/profile|Non-QM & Alternative-Documentation Lending]]
**Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-lenders|Mortgage Lenders]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Small-business lenders already analyse bank-account cash flow automatically; mortgage bank-statement programmes need the same analysis bent to their own income rules.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #feature-engineering #automation #compliance #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to underwrite bank-statement, DSCR and asset-depletion income in a form that securitisation due diligence firms grade A and rating agencies credit, and whoever produces income analysis that survives third-party review at the lowest cost per loan takes the account.

## The Problem
Underwriters spend hours per file classifying 12 or 24 months of deposits and applying expense factors, and two underwriters reach different answers on the same statements.

## What Already Exists
Cash-flow analytics used in small-business and merchant lending (Ocrolus, Plaid and bank-statement parsers from the commercial lending market) classify transactions, flag NSFs and transfers, and summarise revenue.

## The Customization Gap
Non-QM programmes add: personal versus business statement rules; ownership percentage; fixed or CPA-attested expense factors by programme; large-deposit sourcing thresholds; treatment of transfers between the borrower's own accounts; 12- versus 24-month averaging and trend tests; DSCR rent sources for investor loans; and outputs in the worksheet format the due diligence firm will re-calculate against, by buyer guideline version.

## Target Customer
Non-QM underwriting managers and the aggregators who set the guidelines.

## Impact If Solved
Consistent income calculations in minutes rather than hours, aligned with how the due diligence firm will check them.
