# Non-QM & Alternative-Documentation Lending

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-lenders|Mortgage Lenders]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to underwrite bank-statement, DSCR and asset-depletion income in a form that securitisation due diligence firms grade A and rating agencies credit, and whoever produces income analysis that survives third-party review at the lowest cost per loan takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$500M a year in underwriting tools, income-analysis services, guideline management and due diligence costs borne by non-QM lenders (estimate), against ~$108B of non-QM originations in 2025 (BofA Securities)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~4% of mortgage lenders' technology, data and outsourced-service spend (estimate), and ~5% of originations (derived)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — no automated underwriting system covers these loans; income is calculated from 12–24 months of bank statements in spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** Chief Credit Officer at a non-QM lender or aggregator
**Automation Potential:** Medium-High for statement analysis; medium for credit judgement

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Self-employed borrowers, investors buying rentals and borrowers with assets but irregular income fall outside the agencies' automated underwriting, so a non-QM lender underwrites them manually to its own or an aggregator's guidelines and sells the loans into private-label securitisations. Non-QM RMBS issuance hit a record $20.9B in Q3 2025 and ~$52B in the first nine months, more than all of 2024 (Morningstar DBRS). Every securitised loan is re-reviewed by a third-party due diligence firm and graded, so the underwriter's income analysis is checked by an outsider within months. The brokers who originate many of these loans for the lenders are analysed in [[niches/mortgage-brokers/non-qm-specialty-brokers/profile|🔵 Non-QM & Specialty Lending Brokers]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Bank-statement analysis tools and document-AI vendors, the origination system with custom fields, aggregators' guideline matrices in PDFs, and due diligence firms' grade reports after the fact. The gaps: each underwriter calculates bank-statement income slightly differently; guideline versions differ by buyer; and due diligence grades and later loan performance never flow back to the underwriting desk.
