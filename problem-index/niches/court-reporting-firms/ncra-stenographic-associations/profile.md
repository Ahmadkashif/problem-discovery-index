# NCRA & State Stenographic Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/court-reporting-firms|Court Reporting Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The National Court Reporters Association and state associations such as California's represent stenographic reporters and captioners, run the RPR, RMR, CRR and CRC certifications, lobby on licensing and AI, and commission occasional research such as Ducker Worldwide's 2013 outlook study. NCRA backs the Research and Oversight of AI in Courts Act introduced in March 2026.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 staff on research; certification testing and member services are production (estimate)
**Output:** Certifications, advocacy, commissioned workforce studies
**Proprietary data:** Membership and certification records
**Clock:** Legislative sessions and testing calendars
**Buyer:** Executive director
