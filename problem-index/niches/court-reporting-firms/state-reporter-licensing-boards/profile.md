# State Court Reporter Licensing Boards

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/court-reporting-firms|Court Reporting Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Boards that license and discipline reporters in the certification states: the Court Reporters Board of California, within the Department of Consumer Affairs, Texas's Judicial Branch Certification Commission, and their counterparts. California's board runs the CSR exam (pass mark cut to 95% accuracy in 2025), licenses voice writers, rules on questions such as AI-generated summaries, and administers the Transcript Reimbursement Fund for indigent civil litigants.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Small staffs; exam administration, licensing and complaint handling are production (estimate)
**Output:** Licences, exams, disciplinary actions, regulations
**Proprietary data:** Licensee, exam and complaint records
**Clock:** Exam calendars, sunset reviews and complaint timelines
**Buyer:** Executive officer
