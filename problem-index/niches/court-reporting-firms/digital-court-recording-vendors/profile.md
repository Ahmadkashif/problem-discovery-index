# Digital Court Recording System Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/court-reporting-firms|Court Reporting Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Companies that sell courtroom audio and video recording systems and annotation software to courts and agencies, such as For The Record, JAVS and CourtSmart, often alongside transcript-ordering portals. Their products are what states weigh against stenographic reporters, and Ducker found more than 45 states using some digital recording by 2013.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Product, engineering and support staff; no research function sold as such (estimate)
**Output:** Recording systems, annotation software, support contracts
**Proprietary data:** None of their own; recordings are court records
**Clock:** Court procurement cycles
**Buyer:** CEO or head of product
