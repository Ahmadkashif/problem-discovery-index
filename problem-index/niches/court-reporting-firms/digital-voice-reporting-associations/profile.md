# AAERT, NVRA & the Speech-to-Text Institute

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/court-reporting-firms|Court Reporting Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The bodies for the alternatives to stenography. AAERT certifies digital reporters and transcribers and sponsored the 2025 Court Reporting Industry Trends Report, which estimates as few as ~23,000 stenographers remain. NVRA certifies voice writers, whose licences California recognises by reciprocity. The Speech-to-Text Institute, backed by large agencies, publishes workforce figures supporting all capture methods.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 staff each; reports are commissioned from consultancies (estimate)
**Output:** Certifications, advocacy, commissioned trend reports
**Proprietary data:** Certification records and member surveys
**Clock:** Legislative sessions and certification cycles
**Buyer:** Executive director or board president
