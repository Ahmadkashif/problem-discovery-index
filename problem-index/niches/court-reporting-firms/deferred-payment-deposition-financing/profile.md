# Deferred-Payment Deposition Financing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/court-reporting-firms|Court Reporting Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Firms that let plaintiffs' lawyers defer reporting fees until the case resolves, carrying the receivable as a credit risk on the case. Steno, which raised a $49M Series C in March 2026 and about $150M including debt, sells this as DelayPay alongside its reporting service; litigation funders and some agencies offer similar terms.

## Insight Function
**Size:** A small credit and underwriting team per provider (estimate)
**Output:** Deferred-payment terms priced on case risk; internal
**Proprietary data:** Repayment and case-outcome history on financed jobs
**Clock:** Case resolution, set by the litigation
**Buyer:** Chief financial or credit officer
