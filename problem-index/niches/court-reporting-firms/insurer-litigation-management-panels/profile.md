# Insurance Carrier Litigation Management & Reporting Panels

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/court-reporting-firms|Court Reporting Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Large liability, auto and workers' compensation carriers pay for a large share of the country's depositions through the defence counsel they hire. Their litigation management and legal operations teams negotiate national volume programmes with reporting agencies (preferred rates, plus extras such as expedited delivery, deposition databases and summaries), set billing guidelines for vendor costs, and analyse litigation spend. Advocates count 16–17 states that restrict such contracting or require disclosure because of the neutrality risk.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-50 litigation-management and legal-operations analysts per large carrier (estimate)
**Output:** Vendor programmes, rate guidelines and internal litigation-spend analysis; not sold
**Proprietary data:** The carrier's own invoices, vendor performance and litigation outcomes across thousands of claims
**Clock:** Invoice cycles and annual budgets, set internally
**Buyer:** VP of litigation management or claims legal
