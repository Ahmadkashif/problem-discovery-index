# Read-and-Sign, Errata & Certified Delivery

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/court-reporting-firms|Court Reporting Firms]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to close every transcript's review window (signature, errata, certification and delivery to every party) on the jurisdiction's clock without a staff member chasing it, and whoever closes the most transcripts on time with a complete certification record takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$100M US spend on the back-office work after certification: read-and-sign administration, errata handling, copy orders, delivery and retention (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — e-transcripts and repositories at agencies; spreadsheets, scanned errata and mailed originals elsewhere
**Target Buyer:** Production or client-services manager at an agency; owner of an independent firm
**Automation Potential:** High — rule-driven clocks, document handling and distribution

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Once the reporter certifies, the transcript enters a tail of rule-bound steps. A witness who reserved signature gets 30 days under FRCP 30(e)(1), and states set their own windows. The officer must note in the certificate whether review was requested and attach changes. Every party that orders a copy must get the same version on the same terms, and the original goes into custody under the governing rule. Notes and audio must then be retained for years. None of this is billed separately, so it is done by whoever has time, and late or incomplete steps are what clients notice. The deadline content that lawyers use is analysed under [[niches/legal-practice-software/court-rules-deadline-content/profile|Court Rules & Deadline Content]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Agency repositories and e-transcript formats, e-signature tools, email, mail and spreadsheets. The gaps: windows tracked by hand, errata read but never analysed, copy orders and distribution handled one email at a time, and retention on personal drives.
