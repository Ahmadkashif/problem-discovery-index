# Secure File-Sharing Adapted to Certified Copies

**Niche:** [[niches/court-reporting-firms/read-sign-errata-delivery/profile|Read-and-Sign, Errata & Certified Delivery]]
**Industry:** [[industries/court-reporting-firms|Court Reporting Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Secure file-sharing portals already deliver documents to outside parties with access logs; transcript delivery adds per-party orders, certified versions and protective-order limits.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #compliance #automation #data-integration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to close every transcript's review window (signature, errata, certification and delivery to every party) on the jurisdiction's clock without a staff member chasing it, and whoever closes the most transcripts on time with a complete certification record takes the account.

## The Problem
After certification, the original goes to the noticing attorney and copies go to every party that ordered one, each billed at the copy rate. When errata arrive, every copy must be updated. Exhibits marked "confidential" under a protective order may go only to certain recipients.

## What Already Exists
Secure file-sharing and client portals (ShareFile, Box and others) with access controls, expiring links and audit logs; agency repositories.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs per-party copy orders tied to billing and a version history that pushes errata and amended certificates to every holder. It needs protective-order access rules on specific exhibits and pages, and an equal-terms check, so every party receives the same version, formats and add-ons at the same time.

## Target Customer
Independent and regional firms without an agency-grade repository.

## Impact If Solved
Delivery and updates handled once instead of per party, and an audit trail that answers any dispute over who received which version and when.
