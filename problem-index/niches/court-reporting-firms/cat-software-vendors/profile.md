# CAT Software & Realtime Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/court-reporting-firms|Court Reporting Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Make the computer-aided transcription software, steno writers and realtime streaming tools every stenographer works in. Stenograph sells Case CATalyst, whose version 22 (2024) records audio from web-conferencing platforms, and the Check It editing tool built on its Phoenix speech recogniser. Advantage Software sells Eclipse, and smaller vendors sell DigitalCAT and others. Reporters pay licence and support fees; agencies buy realtime and repository integrations.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-30 software and speech-recognition R&D staff per major vendor (estimate)
**Output:** CAT licences, realtime streaming, editing and translation features
**Proprietary data:** Product telemetry at most; the stroke files, dictionaries and audio stay on reporters' machines
**Clock:** Self-set release cycles
**Buyer:** Head of product or CEO

**Kill switches:** live. The richest dataset in the industry, every stroke and every edit, belongs to the reporters and the proceedings and sits on reporters' own machines; without checked terms permitting aggregate use, it is not the vendor's to pool.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. A software supplier to a few tens of thousands of users, best treated as the channel for the reporter-owned accuracy tools described in [[niches/court-reporting-firms/steno-scoping-proofreading/build|A Correction Model That Knows One Reporter's Writing]].
