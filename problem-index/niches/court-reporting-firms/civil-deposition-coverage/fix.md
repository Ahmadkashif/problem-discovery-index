# The Job Confirmed by Text at Nine at Night

**Niche:** [[niches/court-reporting-firms/civil-deposition-coverage/profile|Civil Litigation Deposition Coverage]]
**Industry:** [[industries/court-reporting-firms|Court Reporting Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Bookings are confirmed by text message between a scheduler and a contractor, so double bookings, wrong addresses and no-shows surface only when a lawyer calls asking where the reporter is.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #automation #quick-win #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to put a licensed reporter, or a capture method the venue and parties accept, into every booked deposition, including the ones noticed for tomorrow morning, and whoever fills the most short-notice bookings without a cancelled examination takes the account.

## The Problem
A reporter confirms tomorrow's job by replying "yes" to a text. The address, the video link, the add-ons and the change of start time arrive in later texts or emails. Several parties sometimes book separate reporters for the same proceeding, the waste California's AB 711 tried to curb for court hearings. When something goes wrong the agency reconstructs what was agreed from phone screenshots.

## Why It's Still Broken
Reporters work for several agencies and will not install each one's app, so texting is the common channel. Schedulers are measured on jobs filled, not on records kept, and the cost of a miss lands on the account manager.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Keep the text channel, but put a structured confirmation behind it. The reporter receives a single message with a link to the job card (venue, link, start time, add-ons, parties, licence check) and confirms there. Every change goes out as a new version that the reporter acknowledges. Notices are checked against existing bookings to catch duplicates before two reporters show up.

## Who Feels the Pain
Schedulers working evenings; reporters arriving at the wrong office; litigators whose witness waits for a reporter who was told the wrong day.

## Impact If Fixed
Fewer no-shows and duplicate bookings, and a confirmation record that settles disputes in seconds, at almost no cost to the reporter.
