# Shift Marketplaces Adapted to Licensed Officers

**Niche:** [[niches/court-reporting-firms/civil-deposition-coverage/profile|Civil Litigation Deposition Coverage]]
**Industry:** [[industries/court-reporting-firms|Court Reporting Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Per diem shift marketplaces already match credentialed contractors to open shifts by phone in minutes; deposition coverage needs the same with state licensing, certifications and neutrality built in.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #k-nearest-neighbors #optimization-fundamentals #workflow-orchestration #automation #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to put a licensed reporter, or a capture method the venue and parties accept, into every booked deposition, including the ones noticed for tomorrow morning, and whoever fills the most short-notice bookings without a cancelled examination takes the account.

## The Problem
Agencies fill open jobs by texting reporters one at a time or in batches and waiting for a yes. The reporters are independent contractors who work for several agencies, so the fastest offer wins. Smaller firms lose short-notice jobs because they cannot reach enough reporters quickly.

## What Already Exists
Per diem nursing and hourly-work marketplaces broadcast open shifts to credentialed workers, verify licences, take acceptances in an app and handle pay. Field-service dispatch tools assign technicians by skill and location.

## The Customization Gap
Deposition coverage needs venue-state licensing and reciprocity rules (California, Texas and Illinois require their own certification), certification matching for realtime, CART, legal video and interpreter pairing, remote-capable equipment and audio checks, and page-rate plus appearance-fee pay rather than hourly shifts. It also needs a neutrality check, so that a volume-contract discount offered to the insurer's counsel is offered to every party, and a record that the officer had no disqualifying interest under FRCP 28(c).

## Target Customer
Regional agencies and independent-firm cooperatives that compete with national rosters; rollups standardising acquired firms.

## Impact If Solved
Short-notice jobs filled in minutes from a wider pool, without a scheduler working the phone, and without the compliance mistakes that let opposing counsel challenge a deposition.
