# Translation Management Systems Adapted to Transcript Production

**Niche:** [[niches/court-reporting-firms/transcript-production/profile|Transcript Production (Scoping, Proofreading & Digital Transcription)]]
**Industry:** [[industries/court-reporting-firms|Court Reporting Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Translation management systems already route multi-step text jobs through freelance linguists with deadlines, QA gates and per-unit pay; transcript production runs the same shape of work by email.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #automation #data-integration #evaluation-metrics #large-language-models
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every competitor is fighting to turn a raw capture into a certifiable transcript by the delivery date at the lowest edit cost per page. But a stenographer's raw CAT file and a digital reporter's multi-channel recording are different inputs, worked by different people under different legal exposure, and the contests have different winners. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
An agency job moves from reporter to scopist to proofreader to reporter, or from digital reporter to transcriptionist to proofreader to certifier, with each handoff an email and a file share link, and status tracked by the production coordinator.

## What Already Exists
Translation management systems used across [[industries/localization-services|Localization Services]] route jobs through translators and reviewers, enforce deadlines, attach glossaries and pay per word.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs CAT file and dictionary handling, not just text segments, and audio-linked review for digital transcripts. It needs per-page pay with expedite tiers, jurisdiction-specific certification steps that must be done by the licensed officer, and confidentiality controls for protective-order material.

## Target Customer
Production directors at national and regional agencies running large freelance editing pools.

## Impact If Solved
Visible status on every transcript, fewer missed deadlines, and a production record that shows where time goes.
