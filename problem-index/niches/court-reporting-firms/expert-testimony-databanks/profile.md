# Expert Witness Testimony Databanks & Challenge Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/court-reporting-firms|Court Reporting Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Collect deposition and trial transcripts of expert witnesses, contributed by member law firms, and sell access plus research on an expert's testimonial history: prior opinions, Daubert and other admissibility challenges, disciplinary records and inconsistencies. TrialSmith serves the plaintiffs' bar and says 12,000 plaintiff firms share transcripts through it. DRI's defence-bar database, extended through a 2018 partnership with Courtroom Insight, covers more than 65,000 witnesses. Expert-recruiting firms and the legal research platforms sell expert profiles and challenge reports alongside. Litigators pay before deposing or challenging an opposing expert.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-40 researchers and analysts compiling challenge histories and expert reports at the larger providers (estimate)
**Output:** Transcript access, expert challenge studies, testimonial-history reports, impeachment research
**Proprietary data:** Member-contributed deposition and trial transcripts of experts, many of which are filed nowhere public, plus years of challenge outcomes linked to each expert
**Clock:** Expert-disclosure, expert-deposition and Daubert motion deadlines in court scheduling orders, which can be extended by motion
**Buyer:** Head of research or product at the databank

**Kill switches:** none, on the evidence available. Members contribute transcripts specifically so they can be shared, and deposition testimony is not privileged. That transcripts under protective orders or client restrictions are kept out at contribution is an estimate, not a checked policy. If member-contributed transcripts do carry protective-order or client restrictions, the data-ownership switch is live. The court-reporting associations' claim that copy-sharing undercuts reporters' copy sales is a commercial dispute, not a reuse restriction.
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. A genuinely unique corpus sold against court deadlines, held back by a market of a handful of providers split by bar affiliation and by research teams that stay small because most access is self-serve.
