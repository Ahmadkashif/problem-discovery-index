# Court Reporting M&A Advisors & Brokers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/court-reporting-firms|Court Reporting Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Advisers and brokers who sell independent reporting firms to the consolidators and track the deal flow. Trackers list dozens of acquisitions by Veritext, Lexitas, Magna Legal Services, Esquire and U.S. Legal Support since 2022. They value firms on reporter rosters, client books and copy-sale revenue.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 2-10 per adviser (estimate)
**Output:** Sell-side advice, valuations, deal trackers
**Proprietary data:** Deal history and private financials from engagements
**Clock:** Buyers' deal timelines
**Buyer:** Managing partner
