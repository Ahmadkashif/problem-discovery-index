# Agency Deposition Repositories & Summary Desks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/court-reporting-firms|Court Reporting Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The national agencies (Veritext, Esquire, U.S. Legal Support, Lexitas, Magna Legal Services) keep online repositories of every transcript, exhibit and synchronised video they produce, sell copies to the other parties in each case, and increasingly add AI summaries, testimony search and exhibit management. Veritext alone drew on a network of more than 11,500 independent reporters in 2018, so these archives are the largest bodies of deposition testimony in the country.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Repository, client-services and summary staff, who are production; corporate development is profiled separately (estimate)
**Output:** Copies, e-transcripts, summaries and exhibit portals, sold per transcript
**Proprietary data:** Millions of transcripts and videos, each produced for the parties to one case (estimate)
**Clock:** Each case's discovery and trial dates
**Buyer:** Chief product or operations officer
