# Arbitration Case Management Adapted to the Hearing Record

**Niche:** [[niches/court-reporting-firms/administrative-arbitration-hearings/profile|Administrative, Arbitration & Public Hearings]]
**Industry:** [[industries/court-reporting-firms|Court Reporting Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Arbitration and agency case platforms manage parties, filings and hearing dates; the reporter, the realtime feed and the transcript are booked and delivered outside them.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #automation #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to be the reporter of record under a forum's or agency's master contract, delivering transcripts in its format with exhibits tied to its docket before post-hearing briefs are due, and whoever meets the forum's turnaround at the contract rate takes the account.

## The Problem
An arbitration case administrator schedules a hearing in the provider's case system, then emails the contracted reporting firm, which emails the parties about realtime, daily copy and cost-splitting. Transcripts come back by email and are uploaded by hand.

## What Already Exists
Arbitration providers' case management portals, agency e-docket and hearing-scheduling systems, and generic legal case management platforms.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs a reporter booking tied to the hearing record, with the forum's contract rates and per-party cost allocation, and realtime and daily-copy options offered identically to every party. It needs transcript delivery straight into the case file in the forum's format, an exhibit list synchronised with the case's exhibit register, and turnaround tracking against the contract's service levels.

## Target Customer
Arbitration providers and agencies running modern case platforms; reporting firms that hold their master contracts.

## Impact If Solved
One booking and delivery path per hearing, fewer cost-allocation disputes, and contract performance a forum can see without asking.
