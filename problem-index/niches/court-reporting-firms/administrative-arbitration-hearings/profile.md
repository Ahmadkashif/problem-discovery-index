# Administrative, Arbitration & Public Hearings

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/court-reporting-firms|Court Reporting Firms]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to be the reporter of record under a forum's or agency's master contract, delivering transcripts in its format with exhibits tied to its docket before post-hearing briefs are due, and whoever meets the forum's turnaround at the contract rate takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$250M US spend on reporting state and local agency hearings, utility and regulatory commission proceedings, arbitrations and public meetings (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — paper exhibit binders, procurement by bid and purchase order, and a different caption, format and filing rule at every agency
**Target Buyer:** Hearing office or docket manager at a state agency or public utility commission; arbitration case administrator; counsel in regulatory proceedings
**Automation Potential:** Medium — formatting, exhibit linking and delivery automate; capture stays human or recorded

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Outside the courts, thousands of forums need a verbatim record: workers' compensation boards, unemployment appeals, professional licensing boards, utility rate cases, zoning and planning hearings, legislative committees and private arbitrations. The buyer is usually the forum itself, under a contract awarded by bid or by an arbitration provider's preferred-vendor programme. The American Arbitration Association, for example, publishes a reporting schedule with OptimaJuris that sets realtime at $1.75 a page per party and same-day expedite surcharges up to 110%. The clocks are post-hearing brief dates and statutory decision deadlines in rate cases. Formats, exhibit numbering and filing systems differ by forum, and many agencies record digitally and send the audio out.

## Current Tools & Gaps
CAT software, agency e-docket systems, arbitration providers' case portals and Word templates per forum. The gaps: exhibits referenced in testimony are not linked to docket filings, each agency's format is maintained by hand, and contract performance (turnaround, page counts, disputes) is reported manually at renewal.
