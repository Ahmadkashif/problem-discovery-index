# Deposition Summary & Transcript AI Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/court-reporting-firms|Court Reporting Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Sell software that summarises deposition transcripts, extracts issues and admissions, flags inconsistencies across a witness's testimony and makes a case's transcripts searchable. Customers are reporting agencies, which resell summaries as an add-on (Steno's Transcript Genius launched in 2024), and law firms directly. California's Court Reporters Board ruled in 2025 that AI-generated summaries do not breach its rule against reporters writing summaries, while asking whether both sides receive the same product and whether client transcripts train the models.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-60 ML and product staff per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Summaries, issue digests, testimony search and inconsistency reports, sold per transcript or per seat
**Proprietary data:** None of their own; the corpus is customers' transcripts
**Clock:** Customers' motion and trial dates in court scheduling orders, extendable by motion (scored as the expert-testimony databanks and court data analytics are)
**Buyer:** CEO or head of product
