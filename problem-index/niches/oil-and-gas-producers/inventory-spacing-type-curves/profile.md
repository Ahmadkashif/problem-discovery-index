# Inventory, Spacing & Type-Curve Planning

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/oil-and-gas-producers|Oil & Gas Producers]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to rank an operator's undrilled locations and set their spacing so the wells drilled land on the forecasts that funded them, and whoever's location forecasts come closest to realised 24-month production takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.2B a year of US spend on offset-well data, development-planning and forecasting software, and subsurface analytics used to rank and space locations (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.2% of producer receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High — the inputs are digital and the large operators run sophisticated tools, but the decision record is a slide and a saved curve
**Target Buyer:** VP of Development or VP of Reservoir Engineering at an operator
**Automation Potential:** Medium — forecasting and analog retrieval automate well; the location call stays with the asset team

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is where the producer's money is decided. Every rig line and frac crew executes a schedule built from ranked locations, and every acquisition is priced on the count and quality of remaining locations. With the Permian at about 48% of US crude output in 2025 (EIA) and the core already drilled, most remaining locations are infill or lower-tier benches, where the spacing to neighbours and the age of the parent wells decide whether the type curve holds. The contest is not who has the data, which vendors sell to everyone, but whose forecasts for undrilled locations prove right when the wells are drilled.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Offset data and type-curve tools from Enverus, Novi Labs and S&P Global; economics in ARIES, PHDwin and ComboCurve; subsurface in Petrel and DecisionSpace; spacing and frac simulation in ResFrac. Gaps: type curves built as averages of selected analogs, parent-child effects handled as manual haircuts, and no record of which analogs and haircuts were used. No vendor or operator publishes how its type curves performed.
