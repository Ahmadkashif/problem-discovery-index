# Owner Relations & Revenue Accounting Outsourcers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/oil-and-gas-producers|Oil & Gas Producers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Firms that run owner relations, revenue distribution, division orders and joint interest billing for operators that do not want to staff them, from software vendors' service arms to independent accounting outsourcers. Enverus was recruiting an Owner Relations Agent in October 2026 to answer owner calls on revenue, land, division orders and JIB while "managing multiple clients", at $43,000–$58,000 a year, which points to a managed service.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Case workers and accountants in the tens per firm, each handling owner calls or a client's monthly close (estimate)
**Output:** Owner responses, revenue runs, division-order changes, JIB statements
**Proprietary data:** Clients' owner, revenue and partner records held under service agreements
**Clock:** Clients' monthly close and state payment deadlines (client clocks)
**Buyer:** Head of accounting services
