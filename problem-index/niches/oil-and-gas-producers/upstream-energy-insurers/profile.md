# Upstream Energy Package & Control-of-Well Underwriters

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/oil-and-gas-producers|Oil & Gas Producers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Specialist insurers and Lloyd's syndicates that write upstream energy packages for producers: control-of-well (blowout and redrill) cover, pollution liability, property and general liability. They price each operator's drilling programme and producing assets from submissions, loss history and engineering review, and pay claims when a well goes out of control (recalled).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 actuarial, pricing-model and catastrophe-modelling staff dedicated to upstream energy per specialist carrier (estimate). The underwriters, ~10-30 per carrier (estimate), price one account at a time, which is production, not an insight function.
**Output:** Rating models and portfolio reviews behind the underwriting decisions
**Proprietary data:** The carrier's own policy, exposure and claims history across operators and cycles; submissions are supplied by insureds for underwriting
**Clock:** Policy renewal dates (client clocks)
**Buyer:** Head of upstream energy underwriting
