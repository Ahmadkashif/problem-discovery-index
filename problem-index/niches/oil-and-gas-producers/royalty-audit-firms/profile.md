# Royalty Audit & Owner-Side Accounting Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/oil-and-gas-producers|Oil & Gas Producers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Accounting and audit firms that review operators' royalty payments for mineral owners, mostly large ones such as university and endowment land offices, tribes, trusts and family mineral companies. They check volumes, prices, deductions and decimals against leases and public data, file claims and recover underpayments, often on contingency (recalled).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~3-15 auditors per firm (estimate), each working one owner's audit at a time
**Output:** Audit findings and claims for recovery
**Proprietary data:** Client owners' statements and the operator records obtained under each client's audit rights
**Clock:** Lease audit windows and limitation periods (client clocks)
**Buyer:** Managing partner
