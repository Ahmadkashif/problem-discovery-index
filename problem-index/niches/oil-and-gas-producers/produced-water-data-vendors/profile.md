# Produced Water & Disposal Data Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/oil-and-gas-producers|Oil & Gas Producers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Data firms that assemble water production, disposal-well injection volumes and pressures, and water pipeline infrastructure from state filings, and sell water forecasts, disposal-capacity analytics and seismicity-risk screens. B3 Insight is the specialist (its figure of about four barrels of water per barrel of oil in the Permian circulates in trade press, search summary), and the general upstream data platforms carry some of the same filings. Operators, water midstream companies, disposal operators and investors subscribe, increasingly because Texas tightened Permian disposal permits from 1 June 2025 with pressure and volume caps (RRC, search summary).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~15-50 data engineers, hydrogeologists and analysts per specialist vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Well-level water volumes, disposal-well injection and pressure datasets, disposal-capacity forecasts, seismicity and permit-risk screens
**Proprietary data:** Normalised state injection and production filings plus the vendor's own estimates where states do not report water by well; the inputs are public
**Clock:** The vendor's own data-release cadence; commission permit decisions and rule changes are clients' clocks
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer or head of water analytics
