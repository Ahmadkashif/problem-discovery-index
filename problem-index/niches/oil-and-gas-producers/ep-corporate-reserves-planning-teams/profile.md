# E&P Corporate Reserves & Planning Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/oil-and-gas-producers|Oil & Gas Producers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The central reservoir engineering, reserves and development-planning groups at the large and consolidated operators: the 76 firms with 500 or more employees took 82% of the industry's receipts in 2022 (Census SUSB). They produce the reserves booked in SEC filings, the development plans and type curves behind each year's budget, the borrowing-base submissions sent to banks, and the inventory books used in mergers. The work is internal: its value is realised through drilling and capital allocation.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-30 corporate reserves, planning, type-curve and data-science staff per large operator who work across the whole portfolio (estimate). The asset-team engineers forecasting one well or one pad at a time are production and are not counted.
**Output:** Internal reserves reports, development plans, portfolio type curves, borrowing-base and acquisition evaluations
**Proprietary data:** The operator's own well, completion and production history, development decisions and outcomes, plus licensed vendor data
**Clock:** SEC year-end reserves disclosure and the banks' semi-annual redeterminations, both set outside the team
**Buyer:** VP of Reservoir Engineering or Chief Operating Officer — internal function
