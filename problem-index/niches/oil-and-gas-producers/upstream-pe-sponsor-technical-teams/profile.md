# Upstream Private Equity Sponsors' Technical Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/oil-and-gas-producers|Oil & Gas Producers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Energy private equity firms back management teams to build operators, buy acreage and sell to public companies, and run a few technical staff who screen deals and monitor portfolio companies' drilling and reserves (recalled). The insight is monetised through exits, not invoiced.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~3-10 engineers and geoscientists per sponsor (estimate); detailed evaluation is done by portfolio companies and outside reserve engineers
**Output:** Investment memos, portfolio monitoring
**Proprietary data:** Portfolio companies' data and acquisition data rooms received under confidentiality
**Clock:** Fund cycles and deal bid dates
**Buyer:** Managing partner
