# Production, Revenue & JIB Accounting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/oil-and-gas-producers|Oil & Gas Producers]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to close each production month with every purchaser dollar booked to the right well and every owner and partner paid or billed correctly, and whoever leaves the fewest dollars in prior-period adjustments and unexplained suspense takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.5B a year of US spend on upstream accounting software, revenue distribution, joint interest billing and outsourced accounting services (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.3% of producer receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — every operator runs an accounting system, but purchaser statements, gas statements and partner invoices still arrive as PDFs and spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** Controller or VP of Accounting at an operator
**Automation Potential:** High for matching and coding; review stays human

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every operator, from the 76 firms with 500 or more employees to the 3,761 with fewer than 20 (Census SUSB 2022), runs the same monthly cycle. Field volumes are allocated to wells, purchaser and midstream statements are booked against them, revenue is distributed to every owner's decimal, and costs are billed to working-interest partners under the joint operating agreement's accounting procedure, usually a COPAS form (recalled). The work is high-volume and rule-bound, and errors surface months later as prior-period adjustments, owner complaints and partner audit claims. That makes it the largest software purchase most producers make.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Quorum, W Energy, PakEnergy (WolfePak), P2 (BOLO) and Excalibur for accounting and revenue distribution; OpenInvoice-style invoice networks for vendor bills; spreadsheets for gas-statement reconciliation. Gaps: purchaser and midstream statements re-keyed or loaded and reconciled by hand; gas statements arriving after close; partner disputes worked by email. Owner-side questions are the separate niche [[niches/oil-and-gas-producers/royalty-mineral-owners/profile|🟣 Royalty & Mineral Owners]], and the federal BIA and ONRR reporting version of the work is [[niches/oil-gas-field-services/tribal-land-operators/profile|🟣 Tribal Land Operators]].
