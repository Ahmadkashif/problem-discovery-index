# Invoice Capture Networks Adapted to Joint Interest Billing

**Niche:** [[niches/oil-and-gas-producers/production-revenue-jib-accounting/profile|Production, Revenue & JIB Accounting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/oil-and-gas-producers|Oil & Gas Producers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Invoice automation already captures and codes vendor bills; an operator also has to split each one across partners by decimal and AFE, under the joint operating agreement's accounting rules.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #feature-engineering #compliance #automation #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to close each production month with every purchaser dollar booked to the right well and every owner and partner paid or billed correctly, and whoever leaves the fewest dollars in prior-period adjustments and unexplained suspense takes the account.

## The Problem
A drilling or workover invoice has to be coded to a well and an AFE, checked against the approved budget, and billed to each working-interest partner at its decimal. Overhead, non-consent penalties and the accounting procedure's rules on what is chargeable all apply. Partners audit these bills and claim back what was wrongly charged.

## What Already Exists
Accounts-payable automation and invoice networks (OpenInvoice, Coupa, Stampli and similar) capture invoices, match them to purchase orders and route approvals. Upstream accounting systems produce the JIB.

## The Customization Gap
The oil and gas layer: AFE coding from field tickets and invoice text; cost classification against the joint operating agreement's accounting procedure (chargeable, non-chargeable, overhead-covered); partner decimals that change by well, depth and payout status; non-consent penalty tracking; and an audit-ready trail per line. The field ticket side is [[niches/oil-gas-field-services/field-ticket-digitization/profile|⚡ Field Ticket Digitization]] under field services.

## Target Customer
Operators with many non-operating partners and active drilling programmes.

## Impact If Solved
Cutting partner audit exceptions from ~2% of billed costs to under 1% (estimate) on a ~$300M annual JIB (estimate) avoids millions in later claims and the months of reconciliation each audit brings.
