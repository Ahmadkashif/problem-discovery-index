# Interstate Compact & Orphan Well Programme Offices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/oil-and-gas-producers|Oil & Gas Producers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Interstate Oil and Gas Compact Commission compiles state data into reports on marginal wells and on idle and orphaned wells, and Interior's orphaned wells programme administers the federal plugging grants created in 2021, which states said were paused and partly restarted in 2025 (search summary). State plugging programmes themselves sit with the commissions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research and data staff per office (estimate); most staff administer grants and compacts
**Output:** Periodic reports compiled from state submissions; grant guidance and awards
**Proprietary data:** State-submitted well counts and plugging records, largely public
**Clock:** Grant cycles and report calendars (self-set or statutory)
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — interstate compact and federal office
