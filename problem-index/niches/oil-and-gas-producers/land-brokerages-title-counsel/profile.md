# Contract Land Brokerages & Oil and Gas Title Counsel

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/oil-and-gas-producers|Oil & Gas Producers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Land brokerages supply field landmen and abstractors who search county records, build runsheets, negotiate leases and cure title, billed by the day per project. Oil and gas title attorneys examine the runsheets and write drilling and division order title opinions. Operators pay both, tract by tract and unit by unit.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Brokerages run tens to hundreds of contract landmen, but each works one tract or unit at a time; title practices have a few to a few dozen examining attorneys (estimate)
**Output:** Runsheets, ownership schedules, lease packages, title opinions
**Proprietary data:** Runsheets and abstracts built for clients, and opinions written for them
**Clock:** Operators' spud dates and lease deadlines (client clocks)
**Buyer:** Brokerage owner or title practice head
