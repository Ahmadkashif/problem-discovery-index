# Upstream Professional & Owner Associations (AAPL, COPAS, NARO)

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/oil-and-gas-producers|Oil & Gas Producers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The American Association of Professional Landmen certifies landmen and publishes model forms, including the widely used joint operating agreement; the Council of Petroleum Accountants Societies writes the accounting procedures attached to those agreements; and the National Association of Royalty Owners represents mineral owners (recalled). Members pay dues and buy forms, training and certifications.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Small staffs, under 10 research staff each (estimate); standards are written by volunteer committees
**Output:** Model forms and accounting procedures, education, member surveys, advocacy
**Proprietary data:** Two regimes: model-form text and its revision history, and member survey responses (membership data)
**Clock:** Form revision cycles and conference calendars (self-set)
**Buyer:** Executive director
