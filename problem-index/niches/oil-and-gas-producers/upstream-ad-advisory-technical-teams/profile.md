# Upstream A&D Advisory Technical Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/oil-and-gas-producers|Oil & Gas Producers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
The acquisitions-and-divestitures groups of energy investment banks and advisers, whose petroleum engineers and geologists prepare the technical case for selling upstream assets: type curves, inventory books, reserves evaluations and data rooms, then run the bid process. Energy groups at large banks and boutiques, and online auction platforms for smaller packages, are representative (recalled). Sellers pay success fees, and the technical package is part of why a seller hires one adviser over another.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 staff per group who work across deals, keeping bid histories, comparables and buyer intelligence (estimate). The ~10-40 engineers, geologists and analysts per leading group (estimate) each build one seller's package per deal, which is production, like the per-deal bankers ruled out elsewhere.
**Output:** Evaluation packages, inventory and type-curve books, data rooms, bid analysis
**Proprietary data:** Two regimes. Bid histories across many processes are the adviser's own record. The technical data in each data room is the seller's, supplied under the engagement, and bidders' submissions arrive under confidentiality.
**Clock:** Bid dates set by the adviser and the seller, which would score Q4 2
**Buyer:** Head of the A&D or energy technical group
