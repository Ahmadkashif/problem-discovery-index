# Federal Royalty Collection & Compliance (ONRR)

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/oil-and-gas-producers|Oil & Gas Producers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Interior Department's Office of Natural Resources Revenue collects royalties, rents and bonuses on federal offshore and onshore leases and on Indian leases, receives operators' monthly royalty and production reports, and audits and corrects payments. BLM manages the leases, permits and bonds on federal land (recalled). Producers on federal and Indian land report to it every month, and states share in onshore revenue.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-60 data-analytics, valuation and economic staff who mine the reporting data, set valuation guidance and choose what to audit (estimate). The auditors and compliance analysts working one payer's case at a time are production and are not counted.
**Output:** Compliance targeting and data-mining results, valuation guidance and index-price rules, revenue statistics; audits and orders to pay follow from them
**Proprietary data:** Every federal and Indian lease's monthly royalty and production reports, with payer-level prices, deductions and volumes
**Clock:** Monthly reporting cycles. The statute's seven-year limit on demands for royalties (30 U.S.C. 1724(b)) binds each audit case, which is production work, so it lifts Q4 only to 3 for the analytics.
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — federal agency

**Kill switches:** live — statutory confidentiality. Trade secrets and confidential information obtained under the federal royalty statute may be shared with states and tribes only for audits and investigations, and recipients are bound by the federal disclosure rules (30 U.S.C. 1733). Those rules include the Trade Secrets Act (18 U.S.C. 1905) and FOIA Exemption 4 (recalled). The payer-level corpus therefore cannot leave the agency. Government procurement is not counted as a switch.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. The state-level equivalent is [[niches/oil-gas-field-services/state-oil-gas-commissions/profile|🔍 State Oil & Gas Commissions]].
