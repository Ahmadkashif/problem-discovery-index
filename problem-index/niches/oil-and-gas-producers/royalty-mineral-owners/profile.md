# Royalty & Mineral Owners

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/oil-and-gas-producers|Oil & Gas Producers]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get mineral and royalty owners paid what their leases and decimals say they are owed, and whoever recovers the most underpaid, suspended or escheated royalty dollars per owner takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$500M a year of US spend by owners on mineral management, royalty audit, owner-side accounting and tax services (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.1% of producer receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — paper check stubs, operator portals with different logins, and shoeboxes of division orders
**Target Buyer:** The owner, family office or mineral manager; trustees and university or tribal mineral offices
**Automation Potential:** Medium — reconciliation automates; disputes need people

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Royalty owners are paid by the industry but served by almost none of its software. NARO puts them at 8 to 12 million (search summary), and a Montana State study estimated private royalties at about $22B in 2012 (search summary). Most hold small interests in wells they have never seen, across several operators, each sending its own stub with its own codes and deductions. They cannot easily check volumes, prices or deductions against their lease, and money they never claim ends up as unclaimed property; Texas presumes oil and gas proceeds abandoned after three years (search summary). The operator's tools serve the payer. This niche is tools and services on the owner's side.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Operator owner portals, mineral management firms, royalty auditors on contingency, MineralSoft-style owner platforms (MineralSoft, YC W2016, is listed as acquired), and spreadsheets. Gaps: no single view of an owner's interests across operators, no automatic check of payments against public production and posted prices, and no plain-language explanation of deductions. Trust departments face the same problem in [[niches/trust-companies/special-asset-administration/profile|🟠 Special Asset Administration]], and tribal and allotted owners in [[niches/oil-gas-field-services/tribal-land-operators/profile|🟣 Tribal Land Operators]].
