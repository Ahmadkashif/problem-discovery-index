# The Check Stub Nobody Can Read

**Niche:** [[niches/oil-and-gas-producers/royalty-mineral-owners/profile|Royalty & Mineral Owners]]
**Industry:** [[industries/oil-and-gas-producers|Oil & Gas Producers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Royalty stubs list product codes, interest types and deduction lines that the owner cannot interpret, so every question becomes a phone call.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #feature-engineering #worker-facing #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get mineral and royalty owners paid what their leases and decimals say they are owed, and whoever recovers the most underpaid, suspended or escheated royalty dollars per owner takes the account.

## The Problem
A stub shows lines like "RI 0.00156250, product 400, gross value, sev tax, GATH, CMP, net". Owners cannot tell whether a smaller check reflects price, volume, a new deduction or an error, so they call the operator, whose owner-relations staff answer by pulling records.

## Why It's Still Broken
Every operator's stub uses its own codes, and the legal standard for what may be deducted depends on the lease and the state.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A plain-language translation of each stub: what was produced and sold, at what price, what each deduction is and whether the lease's language appears to allow it, and what changed from last month. It could be offered by operators in their portals, which cuts their call volume, or by owner-side tools.

## Who Feels the Pain
Owners, especially elderly and inheriting owners, and owner-relations staff answering the same questions.

## Impact If Fixed
If an operator fields ~1,000 owner calls a month (estimate) and a third are "what does this line mean", a translation layer removes hundreds of calls and the frustration behind them.
