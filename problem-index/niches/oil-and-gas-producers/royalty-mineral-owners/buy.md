# Personal Finance Aggregation Adapted to Royalty Income

**Niche:** [[niches/oil-and-gas-producers/royalty-mineral-owners/profile|Royalty & Mineral Owners]]
**Industry:** [[industries/oil-and-gas-producers|Oil & Gas Producers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Aggregators already pull every bank and brokerage account into one view; royalty income arrives from a dozen operators in a dozen formats that no aggregator reads.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #feature-engineering #data-integration #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get mineral and royalty owners paid what their leases and decimals say they are owed, and whoever recovers the most underpaid, suspended or escheated royalty dollars per owner takes the account.

## The Problem
An owner with interests from several operators gets separate checks, stubs, 1099s and portal logins. Tax time means assembling gross income, severance taxes and depletion by hand, and nobody notices when an operator stops paying.

## What Already Exists
Personal finance and wealth aggregation platforms (Plaid-connected apps, Addepar for family offices, tax software) consolidate accounts and income.

## The Customization Gap
Royalty specifics: ingesting operator check detail files and stubs; tracking interests by well and decimal; alerting when a well stops paying or a decimal changes; producing depletion and severance summaries for tax; and keeping a document vault of division orders and leases. Owner portals already export check detail in some cases, which gives a data path.

## Target Customer
Family offices and wealth managers with mineral clients, and owners with interests from five or more operators.

## Impact If Solved
For an owner with ~20 interests across 6 operators (estimate), one view replaces hours of tax-time assembly and catches a stopped payment within a month, not a year.
