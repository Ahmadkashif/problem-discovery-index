# Runsheets & Drilling Title

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/oil-and-gas-producers|Oil & Gas Producers]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver a complete runsheet and ownership schedule for each drilling unit before the spud date, and whoever's ownership schedules need the fewest corrections after first production takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.5B a year of US spend on contract field landmen, abstracts, runsheets and drilling and division order title opinions (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.3% of producer receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — county grantor-grantee indexes, deed books and scanned images, many handwritten
**Target Buyer:** Title manager or VP of Land at an operator; owners of land brokerages
**Automation Potential:** Medium — index search and instrument extraction automate; examination stays with the attorney

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Before a horizontal well can be drilled and paid, someone must establish who owns every mineral and royalty interest in a unit that may span several sections and dozens of tracts. Field landmen search county indexes back to the patent or a stable root, pull every deed, probate, lease and assignment, and record them on a runsheet. A title attorney examines the runsheet and writes a drilling title opinion listing ownership and requirements, later updated into a division order title opinion. The work is per tract and per unit under a spud-date clock, done mostly by contract landmen paid by the day. Mistakes surface only after production, as owners missing from the schedule and decimals that need correcting.

## Current Tools & Gaps
County websites and paper books, title plants in some markets, Enverus Courthouse (which added a Runsheet Assistant in October 2026, search summary), Energy Domain's TitleLab, and Rowland (search summary). Gaps: handwritten older instruments, county indexing quirks, and runsheets delivered as spreadsheets that are not linked to the opinion or to later decimal changes. Title plants for residential work are [[niches/land-surveyors/title-plant-search-operations/profile|🔍 Title Plant & Search Operations]] (55, indexed).
