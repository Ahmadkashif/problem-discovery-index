## What They Do
The vendors of upstream accounting, revenue distribution, land and production-operations software (Quorum, W Energy, PakEnergy, P2 and Pandell, among others) hold years of their customers' transactions. Their analytics staff build reporting and product features; none sells benchmark research as a product (recalled).

## Insight Function

**Output:** Dashboards and reports inside the software
**Proprietary data:** Customers' accounting, revenue and land records held under SaaS agreements
**Clock:** Release cycles (self-set)
**Buyer:** Chief Product Officer
