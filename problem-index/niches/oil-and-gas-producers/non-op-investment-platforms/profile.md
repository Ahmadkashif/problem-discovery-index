# Non-Op Working Interest Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/oil-and-gas-producers|Oil & Gas Producers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Companies and funds that buy minority working interests in wells run by others and elect into new wells one AFE at a time. Northern Oil and Gas is the largest public example, with interests in more than 7,000 wells and exposure to about 95 operators (search summary); private funds and family offices run smaller versions. They earn the wells' cash flow, so their analysis is spent on which AFEs to join and which packages to buy.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~5-15 data, analytics and planning staff who build the cross-operator models at the largest platform, and none at most private funds (estimate). The engineers and land staff reviewing one AFE or one well at a time are production and are not counted.
**Output:** Operator scorecards and AFE screening models, used internally for elections and acquisition bids
**Proprietary data:** AFEs, joint interest bills, revenue statements and production from dozens of operators, received as a party to each joint operating agreement
**Clock:** JOA election windows set by each proposing operator (~30 days in common forms, estimate), plus acquisition bid dates
**Buyer:** Chief Technical Officer or head of engineering — internal function
