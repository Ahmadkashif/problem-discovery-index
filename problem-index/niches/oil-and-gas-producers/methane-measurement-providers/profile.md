# Aerial & Continuous Methane Measurement Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/oil-and-gas-producers|Oil & Gas Producers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Firms that measure methane at producers' sites from aircraft, drones, fixed sensors or survey crews, and deliver detections, emission-rate estimates and reports for operators' compliance and voluntary programmes. Bridger Photonics and Insight M fly aerial surveys, Project Canary and Qube install continuous monitors, and SeekOps flies drones (recalled), alongside LDAR contractors running OGI crews. Operators pay per survey, per sensor or per site.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-40 emissions scientists, data analysts and quantification staff per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Detection and quantification reports, site emission histories, programme summaries for regulators and certification schemes
**Proprietary data:** Detections and rate estimates made with the firm's own sensors and algorithms, but flown or installed over operators' sites under service agreements
**Clock:** Clients' survey schedules and regulatory reporting dates (client clocks); several OOOOb compliance dates moved to 22 January 2027 (EPA)
**Buyer:** Chief Science Officer or head of analytics

**Kill switches:** **data isn't theirs to use** — live. The detections are commissioned by operators over their own sites under service agreements, and no checked terms give the vendor rights to reuse site-level results; the geophysical precedent that commissioned survey results belong to the client applies.
