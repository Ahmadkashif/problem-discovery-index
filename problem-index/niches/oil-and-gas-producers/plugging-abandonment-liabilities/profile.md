# Plugging & Abandonment Liabilities

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/oil-and-gas-producers|Oil & Gas Producers]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to estimate what each idle well will cost to plug and when the regulator will force it, and whoever's plugging estimates land closest to the final plugging invoices takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M a year of US spend on plugging-liability estimation, idle-well management, bonding and asset-retirement-obligation services, excluding the plugging work itself (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.1% of producer receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — well files on paper, idle-well lists from commission reports, ARO estimates in spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** Controller (asset retirement obligations) and VP of Operations at an operator; buyers of mature assets
**Automation Potential:** Medium — cost estimation and deadline tracking automate; well-condition judgement needs field data

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every producing well becomes a plugging obligation. Most of the 918,000 producing wells in 2024 made 15 BOE/d or less, and stripper wells together produced about 6% of US oil and gas (EIA). Many will be sold down the chain to smaller operators before they are plugged. If the last owner fails, the state inherits the well: Texas had 10,029 orphaned wells in August 2025 and plugged 775 with state funds in the year (RRC, search summary). Federal rules moved in both directions: BLM raised the minimum lease bond to $150,000 in 2024 and proposed cutting it back to $10,000 in June 2026 (search summary). The operator's problem is estimating the liability well by well and planning when to plug, sell or reactivate each idle well before a state inactive-well rule or a bond call forces the timing.

## Current Tools & Gaps
ERP fixed-asset and ARO modules, spreadsheets of plugging-cost assumptions, state commission well records, and P&A contractors' bids. Gaps: plugging costs estimated as one flat number per well type rather than from depth, casing, age and condition; idle-well lists built from commission data by hand; no feedback from actual plugging invoices to the estimate. Plugging rigs and crews belong to [[industries/oil-gas-field-services|Oil & Gas Field Services]], and state programmes to [[niches/oil-gas-field-services/state-oil-gas-commissions/profile|🔍 State Oil & Gas Commissions]].
