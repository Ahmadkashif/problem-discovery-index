# Contract Lifecycle Management Adapted to Oil and Gas Leases

**Niche:** [[niches/oil-and-gas-producers/lease-obligations-hbp/profile|Lease Obligations & HBP Tracking]]
**Industry:** [[industries/oil-and-gas-producers|Oil & Gas Producers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** CLM software already extracts obligations and reminds people of deadlines; a lease's deadlines move with production, which CLM cannot see.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #feature-engineering #data-integration #compliance #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep every signed lease in force until the operator chooses to release it, and whoever loses the fewest net acres to a missed rental, shut-in payment or continuous-development deadline takes the account.

## The Problem
Lease obligations are contract obligations, and companies already buy software to track contract obligations. But a lease's key deadline is conditional: it depends on whether a well produced last month, whether a unit was formed, and when the last well was completed.

## What Already Exists
Contract lifecycle management platforms (Ironclad, Icertis, DocuSign CLM, Agiloft) with AI clause extraction, obligation tracking and reminders.

## The Customization Gap
A clause library for lease forms and riders (Pugh, continuous development, shut-in, depth severance, deduction clauses); event-driven deadlines tied to production, completion and unit data feeds rather than fixed dates; tract-level acreage and GIS links; and outputs land teams use, such as release notices, shut-in payment schedules and HBP reports.

## Target Customer
Operators whose legal teams already license a CLM platform and want land on the same system.

## Impact If Solved
Reusing an existing CLM licence avoids a separate land-obligation tool. Abstracting a ~5,000-lease portfolio (estimate) into conditional obligations puts every deadline on one calendar driven by production data.
