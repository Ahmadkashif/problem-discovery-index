# Plugging Bond Sureties

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/oil-and-gas-producers|Oil & Gas Producers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Surety companies that write the plugging and reclamation bonds states and BLM require of operators, and pay when an operator fails to plug. BLM's 2024 rule raised the minimum federal lease bond to $150,000, and a June 2026 proposal would cut it back to $10,000 (search summary), so bond demand moves with policy.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 energy surety underwriters per carrier (estimate)
**Output:** Bond underwriting decisions and indemnity terms
**Proprietary data:** The carrier's own bond and claim history, plus applicants' financial statements supplied for underwriting
**Clock:** State and federal bonding requirements and bond renewal dates (client clocks)
**Buyer:** Head of commercial surety
