# Producing-Well Forecasting & Reserves Updates

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/oil-and-gas-producers|Oil & Gas Producers]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to re-forecast every producing well as each month's volumes arrive, and whoever's proved-developed forecasts miss the following year's actual production by the least takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600M a year of US spend on reserves and forecasting software and the internal engineering time spent re-forecasting producing wells (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.1% of producer receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High — production data is digital and forecasting software is universal; the fitting is still done well by well by engineers
**Target Buyer:** Reserves manager or VP of Reservoir Engineering
**Automation Potential:** High — decline fitting on thousands of wells is repetitive, with engineers reviewing exceptions

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Undrilled locations are the inventory niche. This niche is the producing base: tens of thousands of wells whose forecasts set proved-developed reserves for SEC filings, borrowing bases redetermined twice a year, budgets and asset sales. The work is repetitive (fit a decline, set a terminal rate, check the last few months, repeat), done under year-end and redetermination deadlines that the operator does not set. Outside reserve engineers then audit or prepare the reports. The contest is which forecasts hold when the next year's production arrives.

## Current Tools & Gaps
ARIES, PHDwin, ComboCurve and Enverus Value Navigator, with auto-forecast features in several of them; spreadsheets for exceptions. Gaps: auto-forecasts that engineers distrust and re-do by hand, no systematic tracking of forecast error by well type, and downtime and offset frac hits treated as noise. Third-party reserve reports are [[niches/oil-gas-field-services/reserve-engineering-firms/profile|🔍 Reserve Engineering Firms]] (51, indexed), and the bank side is [[niches/oil-gas-field-services/reserve-based-lending-engineering/profile|🔍 Reserve-Based Lending Engineering]].
