# Recovery Audit Analytics Adapted to COPAS Review

**Niche:** [[niches/oil-and-gas-producers/non-op-working-interest-investors/profile|Non-Op Working Interest Investors]]
**Industry:** [[industries/oil-and-gas-producers|Oil & Gas Producers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Recovery-audit tools already scan every payable line for overcharges; joint interest bills need the same scan against the accounting procedure in each operating agreement.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #k-nearest-neighbors #feature-engineering #compliance #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to decide each AFE election inside the joint operating agreement's window on better information than the operator's own proposal, and whoever's elected wells return the most cash per dollar committed takes the account.

## The Problem
A non-op receives thousands of JIB lines a month across operators. The operating agreement's accounting procedure defines what is chargeable, how overhead works and what audit rights exist, usually for a fixed number of years (recalled). Reviews are by sampling, and overcharges are found late or never.

## What Already Exists
Recovery-audit and spend-analytics firms and software (PRGX-style recovery audit, AppZen-style expense audit) scan payables for duplicates, pricing errors and contract breaches.

## The Customization Gap
Joint-interest specifics: parsing JIB detail from each operator's format; encoding each agreement's accounting procedure and overhead rates; checking charges against AFE line items, partner decimals and payout status; flagging duplicate and non-chargeable charges and costs billed after a non-consent election; and building audit claims within the audit window.

## Target Customer
Non-op accounting and audit teams, and COPAS audit firms serving them.

## Impact If Solved
If ~1–2% of JIB charges are recoverable (estimate), continuous review of a ~$200M annual JIB spend (estimate) recovers millions a year that sampling misses.
