# Counterfeit Component Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electronics-contract-mfg|Electronics Contract Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
ERAI and the component authentication labs collect and publish suspect counterfeit part reports from across the industry, track the distributors and brokers implicated, and perform the physical and electrical testing that establishes whether a part is genuine. Manufacturers buying on the open market during allocation depend on it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 analysts, test engineers, and investigators
**Output:** Suspect part alerts, supplier risk profiles, authentication test reports
**Proprietary data:** Industry-contributed counterfeit incident reports joined to supplier identity and test findings — a record no single manufacturer could assemble
**Clock:** Allocation cycles, when open-market buying and counterfeit exposure both spike
**Buyer:** Executive Director / Technical Director
