# Electronics Manufacturing Roadmap Consortia

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electronics-contract-mfg|Electronics Contract Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
iNEMI and comparable consortia coordinate industry roadmapping and pre-competitive research projects across manufacturers, suppliers, and equipment makers, publishing technology roadmaps that shape investment across the sector.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 project managers, technical staff, and researchers
**Output:** Technology roadmaps, pre-competitive research reports, working group findings
**Proprietary data:** Member-contributed technical inputs and roadmap history
**Clock:** Roadmap publication cycles
**Buyer:** Executive Director
