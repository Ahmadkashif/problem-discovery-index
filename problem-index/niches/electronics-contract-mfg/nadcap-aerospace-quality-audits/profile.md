# Aerospace & Defence Special Process Auditing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electronics-contract-mfg|Electronics Contract Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Nadcap and the aerospace quality bodies audit special processes — soldering, conformal coating, cable and harness assembly — against industry-controlled standards, and issue the accreditations without which an electronics manufacturer cannot supply aerospace or defence programmes.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 auditors, technical staff, and task group coordinators
**Output:** Audit findings, accreditation decisions, standards development
**Proprietary data:** Non-conformance findings across hundreds of suppliers and process types, with recurrence and corrective action outcomes
**Clock:** Accreditation cycles and programme qualification deadlines
**Buyer:** VP of Accreditation / Technical Director
