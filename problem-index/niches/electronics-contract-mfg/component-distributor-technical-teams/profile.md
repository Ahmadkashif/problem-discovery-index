# Component Distributor Technical & Lifecycle Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electronics-contract-mfg|Electronics Contract Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The field application engineering and lifecycle management groups at Arrow, Avnet, Mouser, and DigiKey support design-in decisions, manage allocation and obsolescence communication, and identify form-fit-function alternates when a part becomes unavailable.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,500 field application engineers, product managers, and lifecycle analysts
**Output:** Design-in support, alternate part recommendations, allocation and lifecycle guidance
**Proprietary data:** Design registration, demand, and allocation data across the industry — an early view of what is being designed in and what is about to go short
**Clock:** Allocation events and product launch schedules
**Buyer:** VP of Supplier Marketing / Technical Director
