# Reliability Prediction & Physics-of-Failure Modelling

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electronics-contract-mfg|Electronics Contract Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Vendors selling physics-of-failure simulation and reliability prediction let a designer estimate how long an assembly will survive its thermal, vibration, and humidity environment before it is built. The models and the material property libraries behind them are the product.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-120 reliability engineers, materials scientists, and modellers
**Output:** Reliability models, material property libraries, prediction software
**Proprietary data:** Accelerated life test data and material characterization accumulated over decades and calibrated against field returns
**Clock:** Design cycle milestones
**Buyer:** VP of Engineering / Chief Scientist
