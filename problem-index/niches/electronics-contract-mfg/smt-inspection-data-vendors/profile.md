# SMT Inspection Data & Process Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electronics-contract-mfg|Electronics Contract Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Koh Young, CyberOptics, and the inspection equipment makers capture solder paste and placement measurements on every board through every line they equip, and sell the process analytics and closed-loop control built on it. Their data science teams hold the most granular record of electronics assembly process behaviour in existence.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 data scientists, process engineers, and applications staff
**Output:** Process analytics, closed-loop control, yield diagnostics
**Proprietary data:** Paste volume, placement offset, and defect measurements across thousands of production lines worldwide — the most granular manufacturing process dataset in the industry
**Clock:** Production continuous; analytical release cycles internal
**Buyer:** VP of Software / Chief Technology Officer
