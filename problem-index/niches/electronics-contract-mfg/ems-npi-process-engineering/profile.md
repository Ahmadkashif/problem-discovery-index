# EMS New Product Introduction Process Engineering

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electronics-contract-mfg|Electronics Contract Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The process and NPI engineering organizations inside the large EMS providers establish reflow profiles, stencil designs, placement programmes, and paste parameters for every new customer programme, and spend the first three to six months of production empirically tuning them against the five to fifteen percent yield loss the Pass 1 analysis identifies as the industry's defining economic problem.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,500 process engineers, NPI engineers, and quality engineers across a large EMS
**Output:** Process parameter sets, yield ramp analyses, DFM feedback to customers
**Proprietary data:** Process parameters joined to realized yield across thousands of programmes, board designs, and component mixes — the direct record of what makes a new product ramp fast
**Clock:** Customer NPI schedules and production ramp commitments
**Buyer:** VP of Engineering / Director of NPI
