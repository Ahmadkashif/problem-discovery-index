# Electronics Assembly Failure Analysis Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electronics-contract-mfg|Electronics Contract Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Foresite, DfR Solutions, and comparable firms determine why assemblies fail — ionic contamination testing, cross-sectioning, dendrite and electrochemical migration analysis — and issue the report that assigns cause between the design, the components, the assembly process, and the operating environment. A production line stops until they answer.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 materials scientists, reliability engineers, and analysts
**Output:** Root cause analyses, process contamination findings, reliability assessments
**Proprietary data:** A failure signature library linking observed physical evidence to confirmed assembly process causes, accumulated across decades and manufacturers
**Clock:** Production stoppages and warranty dispute timelines
**Buyer:** Principal / Technical Director
