# Material Declaration & Product Compliance Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electronics-contract-mfg|Electronics Contract Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Assent, Source Intelligence, and comparable providers collect, validate, and maintain the material and substance declarations that every electronic assembly needs to be sold — restricted substance content, conflict minerals sourcing, forced labour attestations, and the per-part chemical data that regulations across dozens of jurisdictions require. Analysts chase declarations from tens of thousands of suppliers who do not want to provide them, validate what comes back, and roll it up to the assembly level.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-1,500 compliance analysts, supplier engagement staff, and data validators
**Output:** Validated part-level declarations, rolled-up product compliance status, regulatory impact analyses — the subscription
**Proprietary data:** A validated declaration corpus across millions of components and tens of thousands of suppliers, plus the record of which suppliers respond, how accurately, and where declarations later proved wrong
**Clock:** Regulatory declaration deadlines and customer compliance demands that gate shipment
**Buyer:** VP of Content & Compliance / Chief Data Officer
