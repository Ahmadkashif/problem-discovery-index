# Solder & Assembly Materials Applications Labs

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electronics-contract-mfg|Electronics Contract Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The applications laboratories at Alpha, Indium, and Henkel characterize solder pastes, fluxes, and coatings, publish the reflow profiles and process windows manufacturers work to, and troubleshoot customer process problems in the field.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 process chemists, applications engineers, and lab technicians
**Output:** Process guidance, material characterization, customer process troubleshooting
**Proprietary data:** Material behaviour across process conditions and customer troubleshooting history across thousands of assembly lines
**Clock:** Product launches and customer process crises
**Buyer:** VP of Technology / Applications Director
