# EMS Market Research Boutiques

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/electronics-contract-mfg|Electronics Contract Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Small research firms track EMS provider revenues, capacity, and market share, publish industry rankings, and advise on outsourcing decisions and acquisitions in a sector where almost nothing is publicly reported.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 3-15 analysts
**Output:** Market sizing, provider rankings, outsourcing advisory
**Proprietary data:** Provider revenue estimates and capacity data assembled from industry relationships over decades
**Clock:** Annual publication cycle
**Buyer:** Principal
