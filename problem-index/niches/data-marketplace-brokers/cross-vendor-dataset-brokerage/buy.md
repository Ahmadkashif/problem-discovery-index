# Sourcing and Matching Practice From Procurement

**Niche:** [[niches/data-marketplace-brokers/cross-vendor-dataset-brokerage/profile|Cross-Vendor Dataset Brokerage]]
**Industry:** [[industries/data-marketplace-brokers|Data Marketplace Brokers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Strategic sourcing has structured requirement specification, supplier discovery and evaluation scoring as a mature discipline, and data sourcing is done in email threads.
**Tags:** #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #compliance #descriptive-statistics #workflow-orchestration #automation #revenue-impact #hypothesis-testing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to turn a buyer's vague requirement into a shortlist of suppliers that actually exist, including the ones with no digital shopfront — and whoever does that takes the account, because the buyer's alternative is a month of cold outreach.

## The Problem
Turning a business need into a specification, finding qualified suppliers, running a structured evaluation, scoring against weighted criteria and documenting the decision is the procurement profession's core competence, supported by an entire software category. Data sourcing — which is procurement of an unusually hard-to-evaluate good — is conducted through email threads, sample files and a spreadsheet, largely outside the procurement function.

## What Already Exists
Strategic sourcing platforms with structured requirement templates and supplier discovery; request-for-proposal workflows with weighted scoring; supplier qualification and risk assessment frameworks; category management practice with spend analysis; and contract lifecycle management with term extraction.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a good whose quality cannot be inspected before purchase and whose lawfulness is uncertain. It requires: (1) requirement templates specific to datasets — entity, attribute, coverage, granularity, freshness, permitted use — since a generic sourcing template does not capture what determines fit here and the specification is where most failures originate; (2) evaluation criteria that include the pre-purchase measurements the market cannot currently produce, which makes this niche dependent on the evaluation niche and is why sourcing here feels harder than it should; (3) supplier qualification covering provenance and consent rather than only financial and operational risk, which is a new dimension for procurement and is the one with regulatory teeth; (4) trial and pilot structures suited to data, where a conventional pilot is not available and a measured evaluation must substitute; and (5) recording of unmet requirements as a category management output, which procurement does well for goods and nobody does here.

## Target Customer
Data sourcing teams, procurement functions taking on data categories, brokers, and the sourcing software vendors for whom data is an unserved category.

## Impact If Solved
Procurement's discipline is mature and data sourcing sits outside it. Dataset-specific requirement templates would fix where most failures start, and provenance as a supplier qualification dimension is the one procurement has never had to assess.
