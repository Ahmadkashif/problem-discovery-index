# Marketplace Outcome Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/data-marketplace-brokers|Data Marketplace Brokers]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn what the marketplace already sees — evaluations, rejections, churn, field usage — into the quality signal the market lacks, and whoever publishes it defines how datasets are judged.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$340M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** None — the signal exists and would embarrass suppliers
**Target Buyer:** The marketplaces themselves, and their buyers as beneficiaries
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the data is transactional and complete

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A marketplace intermediating datasets sees what neither buyers nor sellers see: which datasets were evaluated and rejected, which were bought and then churned, which fields buyers actually query, how long a dataset stays in production, and how providers covering the same domain compare on the dimensions that matter. That is precisely the quality signal the market conspicuously lacks. It does not exist because the marketplace's revenue comes from transactions rather than from outcomes, and publishing comparative quality would make some of its own suppliers unsaleable — which is the clearest statement of the conflict at the centre of this industry.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Transaction reporting, listing analytics for providers, and revenue dashboards. The gaps: no churn or renewal signal published to buyers; no record of evaluation-to-purchase conversion by provider; no field usage reporting, despite it being the most direct evidence of what is valuable; no comparative quality across providers in a domain; and no outcome tracking, so the marketplace cannot say whether a transaction succeeded.
