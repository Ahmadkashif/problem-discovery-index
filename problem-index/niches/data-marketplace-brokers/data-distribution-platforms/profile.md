**Digital Adoption:** High
**Target Buyer:** Platform-standardised enterprises; separately, sourcing functions
**Automation Potential:** High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is the part of the market that works: getting a dataset from a provider to a buyer, with entitlements, delivery mechanics and billing handled. It is the largest revenue line and the most visibly competitive.

It is **not terminal**, because distribution describes a function performed by two businesses with almost nothing in common. A cloud data platform's marketplace wins by delivering data without moving it, with governance and lineage intact, to a buyer who has already standardised on that platform and for whom the alternative is the competing cloud platform — an infrastructure contest decided by platform gravity and the quality of the sharing primitive. A specialist broker wins by knowing the supply landscape, translating a vague requirement into a shortlist of candidates including suppliers with no cloud presence at all, and handling the commercial and compliance work around a transaction — a search and matching contest decided by coverage of the market and by the quality of the match, where the alternative is the buyer going direct. Filter Notes in the overview records the two rejected alternatives; the sub-niches below are the split.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Zero-copy sharing within cloud platforms, delivery pipelines and file transfer, entitlement and billing systems, and aggregated discovery catalogues. The gaps are specific to each side and are stated in the sub-niches.
