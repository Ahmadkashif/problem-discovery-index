# Provenance & Consent Evidence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/data-marketplace-brokers|Data Marketplace Brokers]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to replace a contractual warranty about lawful collection with evidence a buyer can rely on — and whoever does that takes the account, because the buyer now carries the regulatory risk and a warranty does not discharge it.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$680M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~14% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — a warranty rather than evidence
**Target Buyer:** Legal, compliance and model training functions
**Automation Potential:** Medium — the chain is recordable, the judgement is not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Buyers increasingly need to know how data was collected, under what consent, from which jurisdiction's residents, and whether it may lawfully be used to train a model. What they receive is a representation in a contract: the provider warrants lawful collection. A warranty allocates liability after the fact and does not tell a regulator anything, does not survive the provider's insolvency, and does not help when the data has already been used to train a model that cannot be untrained. The gap between what buyers need and what the market supplies has turned provenance from a compliance footnote into the risk that stops deals — and it is almost entirely undigitised, with no chain of custody, no consent records and no evidence beyond a questionnaire.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Contractual warranties and indemnities, supplier questionnaires, privacy policy review, and legal opinion. The gaps: no chain of custody from collection to sale, so a dataset assembled from other datasets has untraceable origins; no consent records passed with the data; no jurisdictional breakdown, so a buyer cannot tell which regimes apply; no training-permission statement, which is now the most-asked question; and no mechanism to propagate a withdrawal of consent to downstream buyers.
