# Steel Fabricators' Detailing Procurement & Coordination

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/drafting-services|Drafting Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Larger structural steel fabricators buy detailing from several bureaus, onshore and offshore, or run their own teams, and employ detailing coordinators who assign packages, track submittals, route design revisions and argue back-charges. They aggregate detailing demand across hundreds of projects.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 detailing coordinators, checkers and project managers at the largest fabricators (estimate)
**Output:** Internal package schedules, vendor assignments and back-charge decisions
**Proprietary data:** Submittal histories, engineer comments, shop queries and field-fix records by vendor — the outcome data detailers lack
**Clock:** AISC 303 approval windows and erection schedules
**Buyer:** Director of detailing or chief operating officer at the fabricator
