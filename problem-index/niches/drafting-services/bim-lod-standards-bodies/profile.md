# BIM & LOD Specification Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/drafting-services|Drafting Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
BIMForum's Level of Development Specification, the US National BIM Standard and US National CAD Standard maintained through the National Institute of Building Sciences, and buildingSMART's open standards define what a model or drawing at each level must contain, which drafting contracts reference.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 3-10 staff; committee work is volunteer (estimate)
**Output:** Specifications and standards
**Proprietary data:** None beyond committee records
**Clock:** Self-set publication cycles
**Buyer:** Programme director
