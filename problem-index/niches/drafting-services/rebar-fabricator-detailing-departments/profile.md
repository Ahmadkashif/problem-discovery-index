# Rebar Fabricators' In-House Detailing Departments

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/drafting-services|Drafting Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The large reinforcing-steel fabricators run detailing departments that produce placing drawings and bar lists for most of the projects they supply, and subcontract overflow to independent and offshore bureaus. Detailing is part of the price of supplied, fabricated rebar.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 in any analytical role; the 50-400 detailers and checkers at the largest fabricators are production staff (estimate)
**Output:** Placing drawings and bar lists, bundled with fabricated steel
**Proprietary data:** Decades of placing drawings, bar lists, revisions and short-shipment records
**Clock:** Pour schedules and engineer approval before bending
**Buyer:** Detailing manager or vice president of operations
