# Technical Illustration Tools Taught the USPTO Rules

**Niche:** [[niches/drafting-services/patent-drawing-bureaus/profile|Patent Drawing Bureaus]]
**Industry:** [[industries/drafting-services|Drafting Services]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Illustration and CAD tools produce beautiful line drawings that fail on margins, shading, numeral height or view labels.
**Tags:** #cnns #decision-trees #compliance #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver figures that show every claimed feature and never draw a drawing objection, inside the filing deadline at a per-figure price, and whoever turns a law firm's sketches into compliant sheets fastest takes its docket.

## The Problem
Illustrators convert CAD and photographs into line art in general-purpose tools and check the 37 CFR 1.84 form rules by eye: sheet margins, line weight, shading, numeral height, lead lines, "FIG." labels and sheet numbering.

## What Already Exists
Adobe Illustrator, AutoCAD and SolidWorks drawing exports; vectorising tools for photographs.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: a sheet template locked to USPTO margins and sizes; automatic numeral sizing and lead-line placement; shading and grey-tone checks; view and sheet labelling; and a pre-filing form check that flags every rule failure with its paragraph.

## Target Customer
Patent illustration bureaus and in-house IP illustrators.

## Impact If Solved
Form objections nearly eliminated and illustrator time spent on drawing rather than rule-checking.
