# The Reference Numeral That Does Not Match the Specification

**Niche:** [[niches/drafting-services/patent-drawing-bureaus/profile|Patent Drawing Bureaus]]
**Industry:** [[industries/drafting-services|Drafting Services]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The attorney renumbers elements in the final draft and the figures keep the old numerals.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #compliance #workflow-orchestration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver figures that show every claimed feature and never draw a drawing objection, inside the filing deadline at a per-figure price, and whoever turns a law firm's sketches into compliant sheets fastest takes its docket.

## The Problem
Specification and figures are revised separately. A late renumbering or added element in the text leaves the figures out of step, and the mismatch is found by the examiner or by a paralegal reading both side by side.

## Why It's Still Broken
There is no shared element list between the drafting tool and the illustrator; changes travel by email.

## What a Fix Looks Like
One element and numeral list maintained from the specification, pushed to the illustrator as a change list whenever the text changes, with a final cross-check before filing.

## Who Feels the Pain
Patent paralegals, illustrators redoing sheets under deadline, and attorneys answering avoidable objections.

## Impact If Fixed
Numeral mismatches caught before filing rather than by the Office.
