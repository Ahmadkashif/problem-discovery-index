# Patent Drawing Bureaus

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/drafting-services|Drafting Services]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver figures that show every claimed feature and never draw a drawing objection, inside the filing deadline at a per-figure price, and whoever turns a law firm's sketches into compliant sheets fastest takes its docket.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$200M a year in patent figure preparation for US utility and design applications (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~4% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — inputs are inventor sketches, photos and CAD exports; outputs are PDF sheets to USPTO rules
**Target Buyer:** Patent prosecution paralegal or docketing manager at an IP law firm; patent agent; solo inventor
**Automation Potential:** High — line drawings from CAD and photos, numeral placement and rule checks are mechanical

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Patent drawings are drafting with a legal clock and a rulebook. 37 CFR 1.84 fixes sheet size, margins, line quality, shading, numeral height and view labelling, and MPEP 608.02 sets out when the Office objects; the figures must also show every feature the claims recite and use the same reference numerals as the specification (rule recalled rather than web-checked; see source log). Law firms buy figures per sheet or per figure from specialist bureaus, many offshore, under filing deadlines and in response to drawing objections with a fixed response period. The audience is ignored by both CAD vendors and legal software: patent drafting software handles the text, CAD handles the geometry, and nothing checks one against the other. Classification is a boundary case — some patent illustrators file under graphic design — but the work is drafting to a regulator's standard.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Illustrator, AutoCAD, SolidWorks exports and specialist bureau workflows; patent drafting tools such as ClaimMaster and PatentOptimizer check the specification text. The gaps: no automatic check that every claimed element appears and is numbered consistently with the specification, and objections answered by hand.
