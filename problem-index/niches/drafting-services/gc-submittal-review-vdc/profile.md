# General Contractor Submittal Review & VDC Departments

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/drafting-services|Drafting Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Large general contractors and construction managers review every shop drawing before forwarding it to the architect, and run virtual design and construction teams that coordinate trade models. They sit between the bureaus that draw and the designers who approve.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-100 VDC staff and project engineers at the largest contractors (estimate)
**Output:** Coordinated models, submittal reviews and clash reports, used internally
**Proprietary data:** Submittal registers, review histories and coordination records across many projects
**Clock:** Project submittal schedules and the construction schedule
**Buyer:** Director of VDC
