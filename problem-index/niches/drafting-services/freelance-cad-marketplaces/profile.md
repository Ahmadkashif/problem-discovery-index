# Freelance CAD & Drafting Marketplaces

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/drafting-services|Drafting Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Cad Crowd, Upwork and Fiverr broker drafting and CAD jobs between buyers and freelancers or bureaus, taking a fee per transaction and publishing rate guides.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 working on drafting-specific analysis (estimate)
**Output:** Matching, escrow and rate guides
**Proprietary data:** Job postings, bids, prices and ratings across thousands of drafting jobs
**Clock:** Client deadlines on individual jobs
**Buyer:** Head of category
