# Delegated Steel Connection Design Engineers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/drafting-services|Drafting Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Engineering firms that design steel connections for fabricators when the engineer of record delegates connection design to the fabricator's side, as AISC 303 permits. They take member forces from the design drawings, design and seal each connection type, and hand calculations and sketches to the detailer, who models them. The fabricator pays, usually per project or per ton.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-150 licensed engineers and designers at the larger firms; most are smaller (estimate)
**Output:** Sealed connection calculation packages and connection sketches for submittal
**Proprietary data:** Archives of connection designs, calculations and engineer-of-record review comments across thousands of projects
**Clock:** The AISC 303 14-day approval window and the fabricator's release schedule
**Buyer:** Principal engineer at the connection design firm; chief engineer at the fabricator
