# AI Drawing-Automation Startups

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/drafting-services|Drafting Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Venture-funded companies automating drawing production and checking: SWAPP generates construction documents inside Revit (an $11.5M Series A in 2023), Qnect automates steel connections inside Tekla, ClearHandoff reviews steel shop drawings at about fifty cents a sheet.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-30 machine learning staff at the larger ones, fewer at most; no insight function (estimate)
**Output:** Software subscriptions
**Proprietary data:** Training data drawn from customer models under licence
**Clock:** Funding and release cycles
**Buyer:** Founder or head of product
