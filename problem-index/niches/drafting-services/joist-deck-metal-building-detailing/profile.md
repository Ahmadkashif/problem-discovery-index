# Joist, Deck & Metal Building Manufacturers' Detailing Departments

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/drafting-services|Drafting Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Manufacturers of steel joists, metal deck and pre-engineered metal buildings run large in-house engineering and detailing departments that turn each order into designs, erection drawings, submittals and shop data. The drawings ship with the product; the customer pays for steel, and the detailing speed decides whether the manufacturer wins the schedule.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 in any analytical role; the 50-500 designers, detailers and engineers per large manufacturer are production staff (estimate)
**Output:** Erection drawings, submittal packages and design calculations bundled with the product
**Proprietary data:** Every order's geometry, loads, designs and approval history over decades
**Clock:** The general contractor's submittal schedule and engineer-of-record approval
**Buyer:** Vice president of engineering or detailing operations
