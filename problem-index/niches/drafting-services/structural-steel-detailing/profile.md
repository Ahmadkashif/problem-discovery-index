# Structural Steel Detailing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/drafting-services|Drafting Services]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver checked, approval-ready shop and erection drawings inside the fabricator's schedule with the fewest errors reaching the shop floor, and whoever can prove the lowest field-fix rate per ton takes the fabricator's account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$900M a year in structural and miscellaneous steel detailing fees for US projects, onshore and offshore (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~18% of US-serving outsourced drafting and detailing spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High in modelling, low in checking and outcome tracking — every serious detailer models in Tekla, SDS2 or Advance Steel, but checking is a person with a PDF and field fixes come back by phone
**Target Buyer:** Fabricator's detailing manager or chief of project management; owner of the detailing bureau
**Automation Potential:** Medium-High — connection modelling, sheet generation and CNC output automate; design-conflict resolution and checking remain judgement

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Steel detailing is the largest and most formalised specialty in the industry. The detailer turns the engineer's design drawings into a 3D model and then into erection drawings, shop drawings for every piece, bills of material and CNC files the fabricator's saws and drill lines run from. The work is priced per ton (vendor-published ranges of ~$90–165 a ton on typical commercial work and ~$25–40 on large repetitive industrial jobs), governed by the AISC Code of Standard Practice, including its 14-day approval clock, and supported by a professional body, NISD, with its own individual certification and quality programme. Fabricators often spread work across onshore and offshore bureaus or run their own teams, as Canam does in Romania, India and the Philippines. The buyer can see the result directly: a piece fits at erection or it does not. Bridge detailing is a distinct certification discipline but competes on the same contest.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Tekla Structures, SDS2 and Advance Steel for modelling; Qnect and the packages' own connection libraries for connection design; Tekla PowerFab and FabSuite on the fabricator side; Bluebeam for checking; ClearHandoff for AI review of exported PDFs. The gaps: no record joining a field fix or back-charge to the piece mark, detailer and checker responsible; checking capacity set by one senior person; and design revisions found by overlay.
