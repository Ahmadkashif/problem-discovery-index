# Patent Office Drawing Review

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/drafting-services|Drafting Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The USPTO checks application drawings for form under 37 CFR 1.84 during pre-examination and publication processing, and examiners object to drawings that omit claimed features or cannot be reproduced. Objections drive much of the patent drawing bureaus' correction work.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 staff on drawing review within application processing (estimate), plus examiners' own review
**Output:** Drawing objections and notices
**Proprietary data:** Every filed drawing and every objection, by art unit and defect type
**Clock:** Statutory and regulatory response periods
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer; a federal agency procurement office
