# Model Reinforcement Adapted to Fabricator Bar Lists

**Niche:** [[niches/drafting-services/rebar-detailing-bar-lists/profile|Rebar Detailing & Bar Lists]]
**Industry:** [[industries/drafting-services|Drafting Services]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** BIM tools can model every bar, but their output is not the bar list, marks and release structure a rebar fabricator's shop runs on.
**Tags:** #decision-trees #data-integration #automation #compliance #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to release placing drawings and bar lists pour by pour ahead of the concrete schedule with no shape, quantity or lap errors, and whoever keeps the rebar fabricator's shop fed without a short shipment takes the account.

## The Problem
Engineers and contractors increasingly model reinforcement in Revit or Tekla, but fabricators still need detailing in their own bar-list systems, so the model is redrawn rather than reused.

## What Already Exists
Revit and Tekla reinforcement modelling with schedules; aSa and RebarCAD bar-list and production systems.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: export of modelled bars into the fabricator's mark and shape conventions (CRSI shape codes), stock-length optimisation, release grouping by pour, tagging and bundling data, and change tracking when the model is revised after release.

## Target Customer
Rebar fabricators receiving reinforcement models, and the detailers serving them.

## Impact If Solved
Models reused instead of redrawn, and fewer transcription errors between design model and shop.
