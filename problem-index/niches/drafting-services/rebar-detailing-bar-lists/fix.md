# The Bar List and the Placing Drawing That Disagree

**Niche:** [[niches/drafting-services/rebar-detailing-bar-lists/profile|Rebar Detailing & Bar Lists]]
**Industry:** [[industries/drafting-services|Drafting Services]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A revision updates the placing drawing but not the bar list, or the reverse, and the shop bends what the list says while the ironworker places what the drawing shows.
**Tags:** #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #compliance #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to release placing drawings and bar lists pour by pour ahead of the concrete schedule with no shape, quantity or lap errors, and whoever keeps the rebar fabricator's shop fed without a short shipment takes the account.

## The Problem
In 2D detailing, the placing drawing and the bar list are kept in step by hand. Through revisions and partial releases they drift, and the mismatch appears as a short shipment or wrong bars on site the morning of a pour.

## Why It's Still Broken
Many detailers still draw in 2D and list in a separate system, and checking compares two documents by eye.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Automatic reconciliation of every placing drawing against its bar list before release: marks, quantities, sizes and shapes compared, with mismatches flagged and revision history kept.

## Who Feels the Pain
Rebar fabricators shipping replacement bars, ironworkers waiting on site, and detailers blamed for a manual reconciliation nobody had time to do.

## Impact If Fixed
Short shipments from list-drawing mismatches eliminated, and pours kept on schedule.
