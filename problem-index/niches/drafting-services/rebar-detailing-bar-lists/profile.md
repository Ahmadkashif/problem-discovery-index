# Rebar Detailing & Bar Lists

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/drafting-services|Drafting Services]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to release placing drawings and bar lists pour by pour ahead of the concrete schedule with no shape, quantity or lap errors, and whoever keeps the rebar fabricator's shop fed without a short shipment takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M a year in reinforcing-bar detailing for US projects, by independent bureaus and offshore teams working for rebar fabricators (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium-High — bar-list software is universal, but much detailing is still 2D and engineers' drawings arrive as PDFs
**Target Buyer:** Detailing manager at a rebar fabricator
**Automation Potential:** High — bar shapes, laps, development lengths and bar lists follow codified rules (ACI 318, ACI 315, CRSI practice)

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
ACI 315 defines the detailer as the drafter who prepares placing drawings and bar lists from the engineer's structural drawings, and requires approval before bars are bent where approval is specified. Large rebar fabricators run their own detailing departments; independent and offshore bureaus fill the rest, often paid per ton. The work is released in pour sequences — footings, walls, decks by level — so the detailer's schedule is the concrete contractor's schedule. It is the most rule-bound drafting in the industry: bar marks, shape codes, laps and quantities are checkable, and errors appear immediately as short shipments or bars that cannot be placed.

## Current Tools & Gaps
aSa Rebar and RebarCAD for detailing and bar lists; Tekla Structures and Revit for 3D reinforcement on complex work; fabricator production systems that read bar lists. The gaps: placing drawings redrawn from PDF structural drawings rather than generated from models, bar lists and drawings that drift apart through revisions, and congestion found in the field.
