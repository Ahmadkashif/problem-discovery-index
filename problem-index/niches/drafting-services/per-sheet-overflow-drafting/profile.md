# Per-Sheet Overflow Drafting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/drafting-services|Drafting Services]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a one-off marked-up set into correct, standards-conforming sheets by the client's deadline at a fixed per-sheet price, and whoever needs the fewest clarification rounds takes the overflow work.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$550M a year in one-off per-sheet and per-project drafting for design firms and contractors (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~11% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High — PDFs and DWGs in, sheets out, often through marketplaces or email
**Target Buyer:** Project architect or engineer with a deadline and no spare staff; small-firm principal
**Automation Potential:** High — and the most exposed to AI documentation tools

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Overflow work is bought one set at a time when a deadline outruns the firm's staff. The buyer chooses on price and turnaround, often from a marketplace listing or a bureau they used once before, with vendor-published rates of ~$150–400 a sheet and one or two revision rounds included. There is no time to teach the bureau the firm's standards or habits, so the contest is reading the markups right the first time. Small US bureaus and freelancers compete here with offshore firms, and a two-day turnaround is normal.

## Current Tools & Gaps
AutoCAD and Revit; Bluebeam markups; marketplaces such as Cad Crowd and Upwork for discovery and payment; email for questions. The gaps: no structured intake of markups, revision rounds priced but never tracked, and no record of which clients' packages generate rework.
