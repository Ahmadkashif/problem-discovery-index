# Marketplace Scoping Adapted to Drawing Sets

**Niche:** [[niches/drafting-services/per-sheet-overflow-drafting/profile|Per-Sheet Overflow Drafting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/drafting-services|Drafting Services]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Freelance marketplaces handle discovery, escrow and ratings for drafting work, but scope a forty-sheet set the way they scope a logo.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #feature-engineering #gradient-boosting #workflow-orchestration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a one-off marked-up set into correct, standards-conforming sheets by the client's deadline at a fixed per-sheet price, and whoever needs the fewest clarification rounds takes the overflow work.

## The Problem
Overflow jobs are posted with a few PDFs and a sentence of scope. Bureaus bid blind, the definition of "a sheet" and "a revision" differs on each side, and disputes follow when the client's third round of changes is refused.

## What Already Exists
Upwork, Fiverr and Cad Crowd provide job posting, bidding, milestones, escrow and ratings; Cad Crowd specialises in CAD and design work.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: automatic reading of the posted set to count sheets by type and flag missing information; a standard scope schedule (sheets, sheet sizes, standards to follow, revision rounds, turnaround) agreed before work starts; milestones tied to sheet delivery and revision rounds; and ratings that record first-pass acceptance rather than stars.

## Target Customer
Drafting marketplaces and the larger bureaus that win most of their overflow work through them.

## Impact If Solved
Comparable bids, fewer disputes over revision rounds, and a rating that means something about drawing quality.
