# Scan-to-BIM & Existing-Conditions Modelling Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/drafting-services|Drafting Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Firms that laser-scan existing buildings and plants and model them in Revit or CAD for renovation design, facility management and prefabrication, often with offshore modelling teams. Owners, architects and contractors pay per square foot or per project.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 in any analytical role; the 20-300 modellers and scan technicians at the larger providers are production staff (estimate)
**Output:** Existing-conditions models and drawings with stated accuracy
**Proprietary data:** Point clouds and models — held under client ownership
**Clock:** Design and renovation schedules set by the client
**Buyer:** Operations director at the provider

**Kill switches:** Data isn't theirs to use — point clouds and models of a client's building are the client's property, delivered under contract and often under security restrictions for plants and government sites.
**Verdict:** Logged — fails gate (production modelling, not an insight function; client ownership of the scans would also block it).
