# Field Changes That Never Return to the Model

**Niche:** [[niches/drafting-services/mep-fabrication-modelling/profile|MEP Fabrication & Spool Modelling]]
**Industry:** [[industries/drafting-services|Drafting Services]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The installer cuts and re-joins a spool to make it fit, the model still shows the original, and the next floor repeats the mistake.
**Tags:** #object-detection #cnns #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn the coordinated MEP model into spool, duct and hanger packages that the prefabrication shop can build and the installer can hang without field modification, and whoever cuts model-to-spool hours while holding first-time fit takes the mechanical contractor.

## The Problem
Field modifications are made under time pressure and recorded, if at all, on a marked-up spool sheet that stays on site. The model, and the bureau that built it, never learn what went wrong, so typical floors repeat the same misfit.

## Why It's Still Broken
There is no simple path from a foreman's phone to the modeller's model, and the bureau is outside the contractor's field reporting.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A field capture step: the installer photographs the modified spool and its tag, a short reason is selected, and the change is logged against the spool in the model, with typical-floor spools flagged before they are fabricated.

## Who Feels the Pain
Installers repeating field fixes, prefabrication managers watching savings disappear, and modellers blamed without being told.

## Impact If Fixed
Misfits fixed once rather than on every floor, and a fit-rate record that shows which modelling choices fail.
