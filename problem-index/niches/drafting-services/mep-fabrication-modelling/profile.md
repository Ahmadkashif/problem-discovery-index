# MEP Fabrication & Spool Modelling

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/drafting-services|Drafting Services]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn the coordinated MEP model into spool, duct and hanger packages that the prefabrication shop can build and the installer can hang without field modification, and whoever cuts model-to-spool hours while holding first-time fit takes the mechanical contractor.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$700M a year in outsourced MEP coordination and fabrication modelling for mechanical, plumbing, sprinkler and electrical subcontractors (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~14% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High — LOD 400 models, fabrication content and spool output are digital end to end
**Target Buyer:** VDC or prefabrication manager at a mechanical or plumbing contractor
**Automation Potential:** High — routing to rules, spooling, hanger placement and spool-sheet generation are repetitive and rule-governed

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Mechanical contractors increasingly prefabricate duct and pipe in their own shops, and prefabrication runs on fabrication-level models: duct and pipe drawn as the real fittings from the contractor's own fabrication content, coordinated against other trades, then cut into spools with hangers, sleeves and inserts placed. Bureaus, many offshore, sell this as LOD 400 modelling and spool drawings to subcontractors without enough VDC staff, with SMACNA guiding sheet-metal construction. The design belongs to the MEP engineer, and the bureau's job is to make it buildable. The contest is first-time fit: a spool that does not fit in the field throws away the prefabrication saving.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Autodesk Fabrication (CADmep, ESTmep, CAMduct) and Revit with fabrication parts; Navisworks for clash detection; Trimble SysQue; spool and hanger add-ins. The gaps: spool breakpoints chosen by hand from installer preference, field changes that never return to the model, and no measured fit rate by spool type.
