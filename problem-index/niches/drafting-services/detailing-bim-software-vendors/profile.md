# Detailing & BIM Authoring Software Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/drafting-services|Drafting Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Trimble (Tekla Structures), Nemetschek (SDS2) and Autodesk (Revit, Advance Steel, AutoCAD) sell the authoring tools every bureau uses, and maintain connection libraries, code-specific content and detailing standards behind them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-200 content, standards and product research staff across the detailing product lines (estimate)
**Output:** Software releases, component libraries and code updates — the licence, not insight
**Proprietary data:** Product usage telemetry; customers' models are the customers' property
**Clock:** Annual release cycles and code-edition updates
**Buyer:** Product management vice president
