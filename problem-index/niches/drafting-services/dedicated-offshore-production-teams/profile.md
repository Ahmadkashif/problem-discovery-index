# Dedicated Offshore Production Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/drafting-services|Drafting Services]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a stable, trained offshore team working inside one firm's standards and templates as if it sat in the office, and whoever holds drafter retention and first-pass acceptance highest takes the multi-year contract.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$850M a year in dedicated-team contracts serving US design firms (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~17% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High — remote access to the firm's own servers, Revit models and project platforms
**Target Buyer:** Chief operating officer or BIM director at a design firm of 50+ staff
**Automation Potential:** Medium-High — production automates, but the value sold is continuity of a team that knows the client

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Larger design firms buy a team, not a set: named drafters and modellers in India, the Philippines or Vietnam who work on the firm's projects month after month, on its templates, often on US-aligned shifts, at offshore rates of ~$8–35 an hour (vendor-published). The contract runs for years. Large enterprises such as Pinnacle Infotech, which self-reports more than 3,400 staff, and many mid-size firms compete for these contracts. The winner is whoever keeps the team stable and the learning curve flat: when a trained drafter leaves, the client's habits and standards leave too, and first-pass acceptance drops until the replacement learns them.

## Current Tools & Gaps
The client's own Revit and AutoCAD environment over VPN or virtual desktops; Bluebeam; Teams or Slack; time tracking for billing. The gaps: client-specific know-how held in individual drafters' heads, no structured record of a client's recurring corrections, and no shared quality metric between vendor and client.
