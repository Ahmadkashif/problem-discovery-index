# Home Design Software Adapted to Local Checklists

**Niche:** [[niches/drafting-services/residential-permit-drafting/profile|Residential Plan & Permit Drafting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/drafting-services|Drafting Services]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Residential design software draws a code-aware house from the model code, but knows nothing of the town's checklist, forms and amendments that decide whether the set is accepted.
**Tags:** #decision-trees #large-language-models #compliance #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a homeowner's or builder's plans approved by the local building department on the first submission, and whoever knows each jurisdiction's checklist and plan-check habits best takes the repeat builder and remodeller.

## The Problem
Drafters keep their own checklists per jurisdiction: which sheets, notes, details and forms each town wants. Missing one item means a rejected intake or a correction letter.

## What Already Exists
Chief Architect and SoftPlan generate plans, elevations, sections and framing from a building model with model-code defaults; AutoCAD LT with personal templates is common.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: jurisdiction profiles holding the adopted code edition, local amendments, required sheets, standard notes and forms; automatic insertion of the right notes and details; a pre-submittal checklist run against the set; and updates when a town adopts a new code edition.

## Target Customer
Small residential drafting firms and home designers; the software vendors that serve them.

## Impact If Solved
Fewer rejected intakes and less time keeping personal checklists current across a dozen towns.
