# Residential Plan & Permit Drafting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/drafting-services|Drafting Services]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a homeowner's or builder's plans approved by the local building department on the first submission, and whoever knows each jurisdiction's checklist and plan-check habits best takes the repeat builder and remodeller.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$500M a year in plan drafting for single-family homes, additions, ADUs, decks and remodels where no architect is required (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — work starts with a tape measure, a site visit and a client's sketch, and permit portals and checklists differ by town
**Target Buyer:** Small home builders, remodelling contractors and homeowners
**Automation Potential:** Medium — drafting automates; field measurement and local plan-check knowledge do not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
In many states single-family and small residential work can be drawn without an architect's seal (estimate: rules vary by state and by building size), and a large share of the industry's ~2,500 firms with fewer than five employees live here: local drafters and home designers who measure existing houses, draw plans to the locally adopted International Residential Code with its amendments, and shepherd them through plan check. The buyer is local, the work is face to face, and the quality test is whether the building department approves the set without a correction letter. Each jurisdiction has its own submittal checklist, energy-code forms and reviewer habits, and the drafter's value is knowing them.

The split with architecture is the seal: this niche is unsealed residential plan drafting for owners and builders, while sealed commercial code analysis and permit sets sit with [[niches/architecture-firms/code-analysis-permit-sets/profile|Code Analysis & Permit Set Preparation]] under architecture firms.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Chief Architect, SoftPlan and AutoCAD LT for drafting; laser measures and paper sketches in the field; municipal permit portals that each work differently. The gaps: field measurements transcribed by hand, jurisdiction knowledge held in the drafter's memory, and correction letters answered one at a time.
