# Raster-to-Vector Converters Taught Drawing Semantics

**Niche:** [[niches/drafting-services/legacy-drawing-conversion/profile|Legacy Drawing Conversion & As-Built Documentation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/drafting-services|Drafting Services]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Raster-to-vector tools turn a scan into lines and arcs; the owner needs walls, doors, rooms and dimensions on the right layers.
**Tags:** #cnns #object-detection #semantic-segmentation #transfer-learning #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn paper archives, scanned blueprints and field measurements into accurate, layered CAD that the owner's facilities team can maintain, and whoever proves dimensional fidelity without a return site visit takes the facility portfolio.

## The Problem
Vectorised output is a tangle of segments that a drafter must rebuild into meaningful objects, layers and blocks. Most conversion hours are spent on that cleanup, not on the vectorising.

## What Already Exists
Scan2CAD, WinTopo and AutoCAD Raster Design convert raster images to vectors and recognise text, with tuning for line quality.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: recognition of architectural objects (walls by type, doors, windows, stairs, fixtures, room labels) and engineering symbols; output on the owner's CAD standard with blocks and layers; dimension text read and checked against measured geometry, with conflicts flagged; and handling of hand-lettered and degraded originals.

## Target Customer
Conversion bureaus and in-house facility CAD teams.

## Impact If Solved
Cleanup time per sheet cut sharply, and conversion priced on outcome rather than tracing hours.
