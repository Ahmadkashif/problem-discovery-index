# The Record Set That Was Never As-Built

**Niche:** [[niches/drafting-services/legacy-drawing-conversion/profile|Legacy Drawing Conversion & As-Built Documentation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/drafting-services|Drafting Services]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Contractors hand over "as-built" drawings that record only the changes someone remembered to mark, and bureaus convert them as if they were truth.
**Tags:** #bayesian-inference #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #compliance #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn paper archives, scanned blueprints and field measurements into accurate, layered CAD that the owner's facilities team can maintain, and whoever proves dimensional fidelity without a return site visit takes the facility portfolio.

## The Problem
Record drawings are required at closeout and are often marked up hastily from field sets. Conversions built on them inherit every omission, and the error surfaces when the next renovation opens a wall.

## Why It's Still Broken
Nobody checks record sets against reality at handover, and the owner pays the conversion bureau for drawings, not verification.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Treat every record set as a claim with a source and date. Flag areas where record sets conflict with later work orders, renovation drawings or space data, and package targeted verification into the conversion scope.

## Who Feels the Pain
Facility managers, design teams on renovation projects, and the contractors who open walls expecting something else.

## Impact If Fixed
Fewer surprises in renovations and conversion budgets spent where verification matters.
