# Detailing & Drafting Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/drafting-services|Drafting Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The National Institute of Steel Detailing (founded 1969; Individual Detailer Certification and a Quality Procedures Program) and the American Design Drafting Association (tiered drafter credentials) set professional standards for detailers and drafters.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 staff; committee work is volunteer (estimate)
**Output:** Certifications, standard bid forms, guidelines
**Proprietary data:** Certification records
**Clock:** Conference and exam cycles they set themselves
**Buyer:** Executive director
