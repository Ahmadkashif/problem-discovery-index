# The Review Hours Nobody Charges to Outsourcing

**Niche:** [[niches/drafting-services/outsourced-cd-production/profile|Outsourced Construction Document Production]]
**Industry:** [[industries/drafting-services|Drafting Services]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A project architect spends evenings checking and re-marking sheets that came back from the bureau, and the time disappears into the project budget, so outsourcing looks cheaper than it is.

**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is "every serious competitor is fighting to produce an architect's or engineer's construction documents for less than the firm's own staff cost", but it yields two contests with different winners: one-off per-sheet overflow sets, won on turnaround and clarification rounds, and dedicated offshore production teams, won on retaining a trained team inside one firm's standards. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
The bureau's invoice is visible; the reviewer's hours are not. A project architect who receives forty sheets back checks every one, finds the same mistakes repeated across sheets, re-marks them and sends them back. Nobody logs that time as an outsourcing cost.

## Why It's Still Broken
Timesheets record hours by project and phase, not by activity, and firms have no habit of measuring return on outsourcing beyond the rate.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A lightweight review log in the markup tool: every returned set reviewed is timed and every re-markup counted and classified. A monthly summary shows cost per outsourced sheet including review, rounds per set, and the recurring error types to send to the bureau as standing instructions.

## Who Feels the Pain
Project architects and engineers who absorb the review; principals who believe outsourcing is saving more than it is; bureaus blamed for errors that trace back to unclear markups.

## Impact If Fixed
The real cost of outsourcing becomes visible, so firms can fix the package, change the bureau or stop sending that work out.
