# Practice Management That Counts Outsourced Sheets

**Niche:** [[niches/drafting-services/outsourced-cd-production/profile|Outsourced Construction Document Production]]
**Industry:** [[industries/drafting-services|Drafting Services]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Architecture and engineering practice-management systems track staff hours and consultant invoices, but not the sheet-level cost and rework of outsourced production.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #feature-engineering #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is "every serious competitor is fighting to produce an architect's or engineer's construction documents for less than the firm's own staff cost", but it yields two contests with different winners: one-off per-sheet overflow sets, won on turnaround and clarification rounds, and dedicated offshore production teams, won on retaining a trained team inside one firm's standards. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
A firm pays a bureau per sheet or per hour and records the invoice as a consultant cost. The in-house hours spent preparing markups, answering questions and reviewing returned sheets land on the project as staff time with no link to the outsourced work. The firm cannot see its true cost per outsourced sheet.

## What Already Exists
Deltek Vantagepoint and Ajera, BQE CORE and Monograph track projects, phases, timesheets and consultant invoices for design firms.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: sheets as a unit of work linked to both the bureau invoice and the staff hours spent on them; tagging of staff time as preparation, clarification or review of outsourced sheets; revision rounds per sheet; first-pass acceptance by bureau and by project type; and a comparison report against in-house cost per sheet type.

## Target Customer
Principals and finance leads at design firms spending six figures a year on outsourced production.

## Impact If Solved
An honest cost per outsourced sheet, which tells the firm which work to send out, which bureau to use, and where AI documentation tools would beat both.
