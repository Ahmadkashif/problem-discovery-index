# Field Dimensions That Arrive After the Drawings Are Due

**Niche:** [[niches/drafting-services/trade-subcontractor-shop-drawings/profile|Trade Subcontractor Shop Drawings]]
**Industry:** [[industries/drafting-services|Drafting Services]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Shop drawings are due before the walls exist, so the trade draws from design dimensions, gets approval, then redraws when field measurements arrive.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #worker-facing #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a small trade subcontractor's shop drawings approved on the first submittal inside the general contractor's submittal schedule, and whoever turns architect comments into a clean resubmittal fastest takes the subcontractor's repeat work.

## The Problem
Trades must submit early but fabricate to field-verified dimensions. The drawings are approved on design dimensions, then revised once the site is measured, sometimes requiring a second approval and always requiring the drafter to redo work.

## Why It's Still Broken
The submittal schedule and the construction schedule are set separately, and nobody tracks which dimensions on an approved drawing are still pending field verification.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Mark every dimension on the shop drawing as design or field-verified, keep a list of pending ones linked to the general contractor's schedule, and generate the revised sheets and clouds automatically when field measurements are entered.

## Who Feels the Pain
Drafters redoing approved sheets; trade project managers chasing measurements; shops waiting to release work.

## Impact If Fixed
Revisions after field measure become a quick update rather than a redraw, and fabrication release dates become predictable.
