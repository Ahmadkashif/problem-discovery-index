# Contract Analytics for Ticketing Agreements

**Niche:** [[niches/ticketing-agencies/primary-ticketing-contracts/profile|Primary Ticketing for Venues, Tours & Teams]]
**Industry:** [[industries/ticketing-agencies|Ticketing & Reservation Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Contract lifecycle software already extracts and tracks obligations in commercial agreements, but not the fee splits, exclusivity carve-outs and data-sharing clauses that decide a ticketing deal.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #bert #evaluation-metrics #compliance #data-integration
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to win a venue's or rights holder's exclusive primary ticketing contract, now that the DOJ settlement caps fees at 15% and opens venues to rival ticketers. A tour onsale and a team's season-ticket base are won on different evidence, by different buyers, on different clocks. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
A venue management company may hold dozens of ticketing agreements, each with its own per-ticket fee schedule, rebate tiers, minimum guarantees, exclusivity carve-outs for promoter-controlled shows, data-ownership terms and termination rights. The settlement terms and any remedy order will change what those clauses permit.

## What Already Exists
Contract lifecycle platforms such as Icertis, Ironclad and DocuSign CLM extract clauses with language models and track renewals and obligations.

## The Customization Gap
Ticketing agreements need: a schema for fee and rebate tiers by event type and price band; exclusivity mapping that knows which shows a promoter can take to its own ticketer; data-sharing and buyer-data ownership terms; clauses affected by the 15% fee cap and open-venue obligations; and a link from each clause to the sales data so a rebate can be recomputed rather than trusted.

## Target Customer
General counsel and finance leads at venue management companies, arena owners and college athletics departments.

## Impact If Solved
Every agreement becomes a searchable set of terms tied to the money they move, ready for the renegotiations any remedy order forces.
