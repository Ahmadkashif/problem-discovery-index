# The Refund Waiting on the Promoter

**Niche:** [[niches/ticketing-agencies/cancellation-refunds-chargebacks/profile|Event Cancellation Refunds & Chargebacks]]
**Industry:** [[industries/ticketing-agencies|Ticketing & Reservation Agencies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The ticketer holds the buyer's money as agent, the promoter decides whether to refund it, and the buyer, told nothing, disputes the charge.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #compliance #worker-facing #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to refund, credit or reissue every affected order within the organiser's policy and the card network's dispute window when an event is cancelled or moved, and whoever loses the fewest dollars to chargebacks per cancelled show takes the account.

## The Problem
For a postponed show, refunds often need the promoter's authorisation and sometimes the return of funds already paid out. Days or weeks pass. The buyer sees no refund option, support agents can only say "we're waiting", and many buyers go to their bank.

## Why It's Still Broken
The ticketer sells as agent and cannot refund on its own authority; the promoter delays to protect cash and to see whether a new date will hold the audience; and nobody tracks the decision against the dispute clock.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A refund-authorisation workflow with a deadline set by the dispute window, automatic status messages to buyers and agents, and a default refund window in the ticketing contract when the promoter does not decide.

## Who Feels the Pain
Buyers, fan-support agents and the ticketer's payments team.

## Impact If Fixed
Fewer disputes filed out of uncertainty, and support contacts answered with a date instead of an apology.
