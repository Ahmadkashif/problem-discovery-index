# Airline Disruption Rebooking Adapted to Postponed Events

**Niche:** [[niches/ticketing-agencies/cancellation-refunds-chargebacks/profile|Event Cancellation Refunds & Chargebacks]]
**Industry:** [[industries/ticketing-agencies|Ticketing & Reservation Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Airlines already rebook thousands of passengers automatically when a flight is cancelled; a postponed tour date strands just as many buyers with fewer tools.
**Tags:** #optimization-fundamentals #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #automation #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to refund, credit or reissue every affected order within the organiser's policy and the card network's dispute window when an event is cancelled or moved, and whoever loses the fewest dollars to chargebacks per cancelled show takes the account.

## The Problem
When a date moves, buyers need to know immediately whether their seats carry over, how to get a refund and by when. Ticketers send one email and wait.

## What Already Exists
Airline disruption-management systems that reaccommodate passengers on new flights, offer self-service choices and issue refunds or vouchers under policy rules.

## The Customization Gap
Ticketing needs: seat-for-seat carryover to a new date or a different venue map; organiser-specific refund authorisation, which may come days later; separate rules for primary and resale tickets; state refund laws; marketplace guarantees; and card-network dispute timing built into the outreach order.

## Target Customer
Customer operations leaders at ticketers, marketplaces and large venues.

## Impact If Solved
Buyers choose their outcome in self-service within hours of the announcement, and support queues and disputes shrink with them.
