# Event Cancellation Refunds & Chargebacks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ticketing-agencies|Ticketing & Reservation Agencies]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to refund, credit or reissue every affected order within the organiser's policy and the card network's dispute window when an event is cancelled or moved, and whoever loses the fewest dollars to chargebacks per cancelled show takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M a year of refund-operations labour, chargeback losses and fees, and dispute tooling across ticketing and reservation services (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1.6% of industry receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium. Refunds are processed in software, but authorisation, policy and dispute evidence move by email and manual review
**Target Buyer:** Head of payments or customer operations at a ticketer or marketplace; finance lead at a reservation service
**Automation Potential:** Very high for policy application, outreach and dispute evidence

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
When a show is cancelled, postponed or moved, every order on it needs an outcome: refund, credit, exchange or nothing yet. The primary ticketer waits for the promoter to authorise refunds, and the resale marketplace applies its own guarantee to tickets the organiser may never refund. Card networks give buyers months to dispute (estimate). Vivid Seats reported that cancellations cut its 2025 marketplace order value by $60.7M. Each cancelled order is a contact, a refund and a possible chargeback that counts against the merchant's dispute ratio. Timeshare exchanges and park reservation systems face the same wave after storms and closures. The work is mechanical and deadline-bound, and mistakes surface as chargebacks weeks later.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Platform refund tools, card-network alert programmes (Verifi, Ethoca), chargeback representment vendors analysed under [[niches/payment-fraud-vendors/chargeback-representment/profile|Chargeback Representment]], and ticket protection insurance sold at checkout. Gaps: refund status not visible to buyers or agents, no chargeback prediction on the day a show moves, and evidence packs assembled by hand.
