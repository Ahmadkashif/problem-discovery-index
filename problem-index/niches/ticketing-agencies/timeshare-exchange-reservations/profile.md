# Timeshare Exchange & Vacation-Club Reservations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ticketing-agencies|Ticketing & Reservation Agencies]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to confirm owners' first-choice exchanges without devaluing the weeks they deposit, and whoever confirms the most first-choice requests per deposited week keeps the developers' affiliations and takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.3B of US exchange, membership and club reservation fees (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of industry receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium. Online booking exists, but many owners, older on average, still call to deposit, search and confirm, and deposit values are opaque to them
**Target Buyer:** Exchange company head of member operations; resort developer's head of owner services, who chooses the exchange affiliation
**Automation Potential:** Medium-High for matching forecasts and routine calls; disputes over value stay human

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Timeshare exchange is named in NAICS 561599. Owners deposit their week or points with an exchange company and request a stay elsewhere. The exchange matches deposits to requests across thousands of resorts. RCI, part of Travel + Leisure Co., had 3.3M paid member families and ~3,600 affiliated resorts at the end of 2025. Interval International, owned by Marriott Vacations Worldwide, is the other large network, and developers' own points clubs compete with both. Developers choose which exchange their owners join, so the exchange's real customer is the developer. Its product is a member who gets the stay they asked for.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Exchange platforms with online search and ongoing-search requests, call centres, developer club systems. Gaps: deposit "trading power" values that members cannot predict or check; ongoing searches that sit for months with no estimate of the odds; and match quality not reported to developers.
