# Airline Award-Inventory Management for Exchange Weeks

**Niche:** [[niches/ticketing-agencies/timeshare-exchange-reservations/profile|Timeshare Exchange & Vacation-Club Reservations]]
**Industry:** [[industries/ticketing-agencies|Ticketing & Reservation Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Airline loyalty programmes already price and release award seats against forecast demand; an exchange prices deposited weeks with the same problem and older tools.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #bayesian-inference #optimization-fundamentals #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to confirm owners' first-choice exchanges without devaluing the weeks they deposit, and whoever confirms the most first-choice requests per deposited week keeps the developers' affiliations and takes the account.

## The Problem
Exchanges set the value of each deposited week and the cost of each requested stay, then decide how much inventory to rent to non-owners for cash. Set values too low and popular weeks drain; too high and owners feel cheated.

## What Already Exists
Airline loyalty and revenue management systems that forecast award demand by route and date and set dynamic award prices and availability.

## The Customization Gap
An exchange needs: supply that arrives from member deposits rather than a schedule; unit sizes and resort quality tiers instead of cabins; developer affiliation rules and home-resort priority; a fairness constraint so a deposit's value is explainable to its owner; and the trade-off between exchange matches and cash rentals.

## Target Customer
Revenue and inventory leads at exchange companies and large developer points clubs.

## Impact If Solved
Deposit values and availability set on forecast demand, with fewer stranded deposits and fewer complaints about value.
