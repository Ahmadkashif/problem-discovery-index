# The Deposit Value Nobody Can Explain

**Niche:** [[niches/ticketing-agencies/timeshare-exchange-reservations/profile|Timeshare Exchange & Vacation-Club Reservations]]
**Industry:** [[industries/ticketing-agencies|Ticketing & Reservation Agencies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** An owner deposits their week and receives a trading value they cannot predict, compare or challenge, and calls to ask why.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #decision-trees #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to confirm owners' first-choice exchanges without devaluing the weeks they deposit, and whoever confirms the most first-choice requests per deposited week keeps the developers' affiliations and takes the account.

## The Problem
Deposit values depend on resort, unit, week, how early the deposit is made and demand, through formulas the owner never sees. Agents field calls from owners who deposited the same week as last year and got less, and can only read the number off the screen.

## Why It's Still Broken
The valuation logic is treated as proprietary, and call-centre tools show the result, not the drivers.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A plain explanation generated with each valuation: the three factors that moved it most since last year, what depositing earlier or choosing a different week would change, and the same explanation on the agent's screen.

## Who Feels the Pain
Owners, call-centre agents, and developers whose owner-satisfaction scores suffer.

## Impact If Fixed
Shorter calls, fewer escalations, and owners who deposit earlier because they understand the incentive.
