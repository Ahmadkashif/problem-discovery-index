# Public Park & Campground Reservations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ticketing-agencies|Ticketing & Reservation Agencies]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to allocate scarce campsites, permits and timed entries so that people rather than bots and cancellation scrapers get them, and whoever can show the agency the lowest share of bookings captured by automated tools takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$500M of reservation fees and reservation-system contracts at federal, state and county parks (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% of industry receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium-High for booking; low for any measurement of who actually gets the sites
**Target Buyer:** State parks director of reservations or revenue; federal programme managers for Recreation.gov
**Automation Potential:** High for bot detection, lottery design and cancellation handling

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Public campsites, backcountry permits and timed-entry slots are reserved through contracted systems. Recreation.gov, run by Booz Allen under a 2016 Forest Service contract and paid a fixed fee per reservation, handled ~9M reservations in FY2023, up from 3.5M in FY2019, across 120,000+ campsites. States contract vendors such as Aspira (ReserveAmerica), US eDirect and Brandt. Popular sites sell out within minutes of the six-month release window, and cancellations are taken by paid alert services and scripts before a person can refresh the page. The audience the systems underserve is the occasional camper and the first-time visitor, competing against tools they don't know exist. Congress has questioned the equity of access, and land managers have turned to lotteries and timed entry.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Reservation platforms with rolling release windows, lotteries for permits, CAPTCHAs and account limits; third-party cancellation-alert services. Gaps: no measure of the share of bookings made by automation, release and lottery designs untested for fairness, and resale of reservations hard to police.
