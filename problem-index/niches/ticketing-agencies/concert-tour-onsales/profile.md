# Concert Tour Onsales & Allocation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ticketing-agencies|Ticketing & Reservation Agencies]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to put a tour's tickets into fans' hands at the price the artist chose, against bots and broker account farms, and whoever lets the smallest share of the house reach resale in the first 48 hours takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.5B of US primary fee revenue on concert and touring onsales (estimate; concerts drove most of Ticketmaster's 6% growth in fee-bearing transactions in 2025)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of industry receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High for the sale; low for the scaling and release decisions behind it
**Target Buyer:** Promoter's head of ticketing; artist's manager and business manager for the price ceiling
**Automation Potential:** High for allocation and pricing support; the artist's price decision stays human

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A tour onsale is a single, adversarial event. Registration for verified-fan presales, card and fan-club presales, a general onsale, and in some cases dynamic pricing all happen within days, across dozens of cities, and the resale market prices every seat within hours. The promoter and artist, not the venue, choose the ticketer for promoter-controlled shows, so this account turns on fan outcomes: how many tickets reached fans at face, how fast brokers relisted, and how loud the backlash was. The jury found Live Nation tied amphitheatres to its promotion, and the DOJ settlement opens venues to rival ticketers, so the promoter's choice of ticketer is now a live contest rather than a given.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Ticketmaster Verified Fan and SafeTix, AXS's mobile ID, SeatGeek and DICE (which locks resale to face value); Queue-it waiting rooms; promoters' scaling spreadsheets. Gaps: no measured share of each onsale that reaches resale, scaling and release decisions unrecorded, and presale codes that leak.
