# The Presale Code Shared in a Group Chat

**Niche:** [[niches/ticketing-agencies/concert-tour-onsales/profile|Concert Tour Onsales & Allocation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/ticketing-agencies|Ticketing & Reservation Agencies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Presales meant for fan-club members and cardholders open with a shared code that brokers have within minutes.
**Tags:** #graph-theory #logistic-regression #evaluation-metrics #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to put a tour's tickets into fans' hands at the price the artist chose, against bots and broker account farms, and whoever lets the smallest share of the house reach resale in the first 48 hours takes the account.

## The Problem
Many presales still run on a single code per offer, emailed to fans or cardholders. The code circulates on forums and broker chats before the presale opens, and the inventory meant for fans goes to accounts that were never invited.

## Why It's Still Broken
Codes are cheap to issue and easy for sponsors to market. Unique, identity-bound access needs registration, which adds friction and cost that card and sponsor partners resist, and the leak is invisible because nobody measures which presale buyers were actually invited.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Single-use or account-bound presale access for every partner offer, with a post-presale report matching buyers to the invited list and tracing presale seats that appear on resale within 48 hours.

## Who Feels the Pain
Fans who registered and lost out, sponsors whose offer was devalued, and the promoter answering for it.

## Impact If Fixed
Presale inventory reaches the people it was promised to, and leakage becomes a measured number in each presale partner's contract.
