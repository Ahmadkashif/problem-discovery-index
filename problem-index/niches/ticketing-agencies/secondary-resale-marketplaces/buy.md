# Marketplace Seller-Risk Tools Adapted to Brokers

**Niche:** [[niches/ticketing-agencies/secondary-resale-marketplaces/profile|Secondary Resale Marketplaces]]
**Industry:** [[industries/ticketing-agencies|Ticketing & Reservation Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** General marketplaces already score sellers for fraud and non-fulfilment; a ticket seller sells a unique seat with an expiry date and a transfer lock.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #graph-neural-networks #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to guarantee that every listed seat is real, deliverable and lawfully priced in the venue's state, and whoever settles the fewest failed orders, speculative-listing complaints and chargebacks per thousand tickets takes the account.

## The Problem
Resale marketplaces onboard thousands of sellers, from fans with two seats to brokers with tens of thousands, and must decide whom to trust with payouts and ranking.

## What Already Exists
Seller-risk and trust-and-safety tools used by eBay-style marketplaces and payment platforms (Sift, Signifyd, Sardine), with identity verification and network linking.

## The Customization Gap
Tickets need: proof of possession checked against the primary platform's transfer status; links between seller accounts and the buyer accounts that bought at the onsale, which matters under the BOTS Act; event-date expiry so risk rises as the show nears; venue transfer restrictions; jurisdiction rules on resale caps and speculative listings; and penalty and collateral policies tied to delivery outcomes.

## Target Customer
Trust and safety and seller operations leads at resale marketplaces.

## Impact If Solved
Seller risk decisions made on ticket-specific evidence rather than generic marketplace signals, with fewer good fans caught and fewer bad sellers missed.
