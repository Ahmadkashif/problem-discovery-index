# Promoter Settlement Software Extended to the Box Office

**Niche:** [[niches/ticketing-agencies/box-office-holds-settlement/profile|Box Office Holds, Comps & Show Settlement]]
**Industry:** [[industries/ticketing-agencies|Ticketing & Reservation Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Promoters already settle shows in purpose-built software; the box office's side of the same settlement still arrives as a PDF statement and a spreadsheet.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to close a show's settlement with a hold, comp and kill record that the promoter, the artist's tour accountant and the venue all accept, and whoever produces a signed settlement fastest with the fewest disputed seats takes the account.

## The Problem
The promoter models the show, the artist deal and expenses in its own system; the venue produces the box office statement from the ticketing system; the two are reconciled by hand on the night.

## What Already Exists
Promoter booking and settlement software such as Prism.fm calculates artist payouts and show profit from ticket counts and expenses entered by the promoter.

## The Customization Gap
Extending it to the box office needs: a live feed of sales by price level and channel from each ticketing platform; a hold and comp ledger shared by venue, promoter and tour; per-venue facility fee and tax rules; union and house rules on comps and kills; and a shared settlement that all three parties edit, rather than three versions compared at midnight.

## Target Customer
Independent promoters and venue groups already using promoter settlement tools.

## Impact If Solved
One settlement record for all parties, built during the day rather than reconciled after the show.
