# Comps Requested by Text at 4 p.m.

**Niche:** [[niches/ticketing-agencies/box-office-holds-settlement/profile|Box Office Holds, Comps & Show Settlement]]
**Industry:** [[industries/ticketing-agencies|Ticketing & Reservation Agencies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Tour managers, agents and sponsors request comps by text and email on show day, and the box office pulls seats from holds with no record of who asked.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #workflow-orchestration #worker-facing #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to close a show's settlement with a hold, comp and kill record that the promoter, the artist's tour accountant and the venue all accept, and whoever produces a signed settlement fastest with the fewest disputed seats takes the account.

## The Problem
Comp lists change through the day. A tour manager texts for twenty more, an agent emails names for will call, a sponsor wants upgrades. The treasurer pulls seats from holds and writes names on a list. At settlement, the tour's comp count and the venue's do not match.

## Why It's Still Broken
Requests come through personal channels from people who will not use a portal, and the ticketing system has no concept of a request, only of a seat being comped.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A shared inbox and text number that turns each request into a structured entry (requester, quantity, category, names) against the right hold, with approval in one tap and a running comp count visible to the tour.

## Who Feels the Pain
Box office treasurers, tour accountants and promoters settling disputed comp counts.

## Impact If Fixed
Comp disputes at settlement disappear, and the treasurer's show day is spent on the window rather than the phone.
