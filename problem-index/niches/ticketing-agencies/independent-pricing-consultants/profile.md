# Independent Ticket Pricing & Ticketing RFP Advisors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ticketing-agencies|Ticketing & Reservation Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Small consultancies and independent advisors who set pricing and subscription strategy for arts organisations, run ticketing-vendor RFPs and contract negotiations for venues and college athletics departments, and audit ticketing fees and rebates. Clients pay project fees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 2-10 consultants per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Pricing and scaling recommendations, RFP evaluations, contract term sheets
**Proprietary data:** Engagement files and benchmarks from past clients
**Clock:** Ticketing contract expiries and season on-sale dates, which are the clients' own schedules
**Buyer:** Principal
