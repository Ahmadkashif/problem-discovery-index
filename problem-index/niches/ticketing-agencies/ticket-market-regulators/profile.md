# Ticket Market Enforcement Units

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ticketing-agencies|Ticketing & Reservation Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The FTC staff who enforce the BOTS Act and the Rule on Unfair or Deceptive Fees (in force for live-event tickets since May 12, 2025), and the consumer protection and antitrust units of state attorneys general, 33 of whom with D.C. won the April 2026 jury verdict against Live Nation and Ticketmaster. They investigate brokers, platforms and fee displays, and now enforce new state resale caps such as Vermont's.

## Insight Function
**Size:** A handful of staff per office working ticketing matters, scaled up by outside counsel and experts for major cases (estimate)
**Output:** Investigations, complaints, settlements, consumer guidance
**Proprietary data:** Subpoenaed records and complaints
**Clock:** Court schedules and statutory effective dates
**Buyer:** Bureau director or section chief
