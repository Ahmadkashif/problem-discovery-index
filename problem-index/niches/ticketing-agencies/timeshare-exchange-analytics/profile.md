# Timeshare Exchange Network Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ticketing-agencies|Ticketing & Reservation Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The inventory, pricing and member-analytics teams at the two large exchange networks: RCI (Travel + Leisure Co.; 3.3M paid member families and ~3,600 affiliated resorts at the end of 2025) and Interval International (Marriott Vacations Worldwide). They set deposit values and exchange costs, decide how much inventory to rent for cash, and report to affiliated developers. Revenue is membership, exchange and rental fees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 analysts and data scientists per network (estimate); call-centre agents are production and excluded
**Output:** Deposit valuation and exchange pricing, inventory forecasts, developer affiliation reports
**Proprietary data:** Decades of deposits, requests, matches and stays across thousands of resorts
**Clock:** Seasonal travel calendars and developer affiliation renewals; no third-party deadline
**Buyer:** Head of revenue management or member analytics at the exchange
