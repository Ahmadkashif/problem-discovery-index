# Resale Marketplace Pricing & Trust Data Science

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ticketing-agencies|Ticketing & Reservation Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The data science, pricing and trust-and-safety analytics teams at StubHub, Vivid Seats, SeatGeek, TickPick and Gametime. They run search ranking, price suggestions for sellers, buyer-fee strategy, seller risk and delivery guarantees, and publish demand and price trend reports as marketing. Revenue is the take on each sale (StubHub's revenue was ~19% of its ~$9.2B gross merchandise sales in 2025), not the analysis.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-200 data scientists, analysts and trust-and-safety analysts per large marketplace (estimate); support and fulfilment agents are production and excluded
**Output:** Ranking and pricing systems, seller pricing tips and dashboards given free to brokers, consumer price reports used as press
**Proprietary data:** Listing, sold-price, delivery and dispute histories for millions of resale orders across every major event, the clearest view of what each seat was actually worth
**Clock:** Event dates set by promoters and teams; no third-party deadline governs the analysis itself
**Buyer:** Chief product officer or head of marketplace analytics
