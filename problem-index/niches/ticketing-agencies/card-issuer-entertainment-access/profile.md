# Card-Issuer Entertainment Access Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ticketing-agencies|Ticketing & Reservation Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Card issuers that buy presale access and reserved inventory for tours, festivals and sports from promoters and ticketers, and offer it to cardholders as a benefit: American Express, Citi and Capital One run the best-known programmes. The issuer pays for the access as marketing and earns it back through card acquisition and spend.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Partnership and marketing analytics staff on entertainment are typically under 10 per issuer (estimate)
**Output:** Deal evaluations, campaign performance reports
**Proprietary data:** Cardholder engagement with each presale
**Clock:** Tour announcements and presale dates set by promoters
**Buyer:** Head of entertainment partnerships
