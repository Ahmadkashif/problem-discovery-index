# Primary Ticketing Platform Data Science

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ticketing-agencies|Ticketing & Reservation Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The data science, pricing and client-analytics groups at the primary ticketers: Ticketmaster above all, then AXS, SeatGeek, Paciolan and Etix. They build dynamic pricing, demand forecasts for onsales, fraud and bot defences, fan marketing tools and the sales dashboards clients see. Clients pay through ticket fees and contract terms; the analysis is bundled into the service.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-400 data scientists and analysts at Ticketmaster; 10-60 at each smaller platform (estimate)
**Output:** Pricing and allocation systems, onsale demand forecasts, client sales and marketing dashboards, fan-protection models
**Proprietary data:** Seat-level sales, queue, scan and transfer records for hundreds of millions of tickets a year (Ticketmaster sold 346M fee-bearing tickets in 2025)
**Clock:** Onsale and event dates set by promoters, teams and venues
**Buyer:** Chief data officer or head of pricing at the ticketer
