# Bank Entity Resolution Adapted to Buyer Accounts

**Niche:** [[niches/ticketing-agencies/onsale-bot-enforcement/profile|Onsale Bot & Purchase-Limit Enforcement]]
**Industry:** [[industries/ticketing-agencies|Ticketing & Reservation Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Banks already resolve thousands of accounts into the people and networks behind them for anti-money-laundering; a ticketer needs the same answer in the hour before an onsale.
**Tags:** #graph-neural-networks #k-nearest-neighbors #graph-theory #evaluation-metrics #compliance #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stop one buyer from holding many accounts at an onsale without blocking real fans, and whoever catches the most limit-breaking account clusters at a false-block rate the artist will tolerate takes the account.

## The Problem
Ticketers need to know which accounts belong to the same person or organisation before they buy, not after.

## What Already Exists
Entity resolution and network analytics platforms used in bank AML and fraud (Quantexa, Senzing) link records across names, addresses, devices and payment instruments.

## The Customization Gap
Ticketing needs: resolution running in near real time across millions of fan accounts before an onsale; device, queue and transfer signals in the match; household and office sharing treated as legitimate; links to resale seller identities on outside marketplaces; and thresholds set per onsale with the artist's tolerance for false blocks.

## Target Customer
Trust-and-safety and data platform leads at primary ticketers.

## Impact If Solved
A proven resolution engine applied to the onsale problem instead of a homegrown one rebuilt after every major tour.
