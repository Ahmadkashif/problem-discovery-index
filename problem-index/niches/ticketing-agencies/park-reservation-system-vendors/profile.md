# Park Reservation System Contractors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ticketing-agencies|Ticketing & Reservation Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Contractors that build and run reservation systems for public lands: Booz Allen Hamilton for Recreation.gov (~9M reservations in FY2023, paid a fixed fee per reservation), and Aspira (ReserveAmerica), US eDirect and Brandt Information Services for state parks. They run booking, lotteries, permits and call centres and report usage to agencies.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-30 analytics and reporting staff per contractor (estimate)
**Output:** Usage and revenue reports for agencies, lottery operations, capacity and demand dashboards
**Proprietary data:** Booking, cancellation and check-in histories across thousands of parks
**Clock:** Contract reporting cycles and the agencies' budget years
**Buyer:** Programme director at the contractor

**Kill switches:** live — data isn't theirs. Reservation records belong to the contracting agencies under the system contracts; federal data is partly published by the agencies themselves. Government procurement on its own would not be a kill switch; client ownership of the data is.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. Paid per transaction, judged on uptime, and holding the agencies' data, the contractors have no reason or right to sell analysis of who gets the sites, which is the open question in [[niches/ticketing-agencies/park-campground-reservations/profile|Public Park & Campground Reservations]].
