# Embedded Ticket Protection Insurers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ticketing-agencies|Ticketing & Reservation Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Insurers and programme administrators that sell refund protection at ticket checkout: Allianz Partners' Event Ticket Protector, XCover, Booking Protect and Refund Protect. The buyer pays a few percent of the order (estimate) to recover the ticket price if illness, travel disruption or another covered reason stops them attending. Ticketers and marketplaces earn commission on each policy sold.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 underwriting, pricing and claims analysts per programme (estimate); claims handlers are production and excluded
**Output:** Premium rates by event type, price and lead time; claims decisions; partner conversion and loss reports
**Proprietary data:** Policies and claims by event, reason and buyer, joined to cancellations and postponements
**Clock:** Event dates and claim filing windows; rate filings where the product is regulated as insurance
**Buyer:** Head of pricing or chief underwriting officer for the programme
