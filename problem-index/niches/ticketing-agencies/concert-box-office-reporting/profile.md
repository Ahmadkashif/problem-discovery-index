# Concert Box Office Reporting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ticketing-agencies|Ticketing & Reservation Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Trade publications that collect and publish reported concert grosses and attendance by show and tour: Pollstar's Boxoffice database (owned by Oak View Group, itself a venue operator) and Billboard Boxscore. Promoters and venues submit box office reports voluntarily, and agents, promoters and investors subscribe to the data to route tours and benchmark venues.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 data and charts staff per publication (estimate)
**Output:** Box office databases, year-end charts, tour and venue rankings
**Proprietary data:** Decades of self-reported grosses by show
**Clock:** Weekly publication and year-end chart deadlines set by the publication
**Buyer:** Editor or head of data at the publication
