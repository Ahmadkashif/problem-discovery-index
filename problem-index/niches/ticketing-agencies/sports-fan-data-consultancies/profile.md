# Sports & Entertainment Fan-Data Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ticketing-agencies|Ticketing & Reservation Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Firms that analyse teams' and venues' ticketing, CRM and fan data to set prices, target renewals and sell premium seating: KORE Software and StellarAlgo on the platform side, Two Circles and smaller agencies on the consulting side. Teams, college athletics departments and venues pay retainers, project fees and subscriptions. The analysis is the product, delivered as pricing recommendations, renewal-risk lists and revenue strategy.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 analysts and data scientists at the larger firms (estimate)
**Output:** Season pricing and scaling recommendations, renewal and churn models, premium-seat sales targeting, benchmark reports across a firm's clients
**Proprietary data:** Methods and benchmarks built across many teams; the ticketing, scan and CRM records themselves belong to each client
**Clock:** Season-ticket renewal deadlines and season calendars, which are the clients' own schedules
**Buyer:** Chief revenue officer or VP of ticketing at a team
