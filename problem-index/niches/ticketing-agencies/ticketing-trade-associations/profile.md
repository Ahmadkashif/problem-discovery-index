# Ticketing, Broker & Timeshare Trade Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ticketing-agencies|Ticketing & Reservation Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
INTIX (the International Ticketing Association) for box office and ticketing professionals; the National Association of Ticket Brokers for resellers; ARDA for the timeshare industry, whose annual State of the Vacation Timeshare Industry study is produced by Ernst & Young; and The Broadway League, which publishes weekly Broadway grosses and audience demographics. Members pay dues; the research supports advocacy and conferences.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research staff at each; ARDA's main study is outsourced (estimate)
**Output:** Industry studies, grosses, demographic reports, policy positions on resale and fee laws
**Proprietary data:** Member surveys and voluntarily reported figures
**Clock:** Annual conference and legislative calendars
**Buyer:** Association president or head of research
