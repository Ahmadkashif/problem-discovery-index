# Broker Point-of-Sale & Resale Pricing Data Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ticketing-agencies|Ticketing & Reservation Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Software and data vendors that brokers use to manage inventory and set resale prices: SkyBox (owned by Vivid Seats), Automatiq, TicketNetwork's broker tools and Ticket Evolution, plus smaller services that sell resale price histories and onsale demand estimates. Brokers pay subscriptions or a share of sales for the software, and the pricing recommendations are a large part of what they buy.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 data and pricing staff per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Automated repricing rules, price and demand dashboards, buy recommendations for upcoming onsales
**Proprietary data:** Brokers' inventory, costs and sales across marketplaces; scraped listing histories
**Clock:** Event dates and onsale dates set by promoters and teams
**Buyer:** Head of product or data at the vendor
