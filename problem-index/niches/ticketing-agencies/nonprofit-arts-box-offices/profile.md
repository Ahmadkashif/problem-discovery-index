# Nonprofit Arts & Small-Venue Box Offices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/ticketing-agencies|Ticketing & Reservation Agencies]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a first-time single-ticket buyer into a returning subscriber or donor using only the box office's own records, and whoever lifts the second-visit rate for a box office of two people takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M of ticketing, patron-CRM software and outsourced box office services for US nonprofit theatres, orchestras, dance companies and small venues (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1.6% of industry receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium. Online sales and email are standard; analysis of who returns and why is mostly absent
**Target Buyer:** Director of ticketing and patron services, or the managing director at a small company
**Automation Potential:** High for segmentation, offer timing and data hygiene

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A regional theatre, chamber orchestra or community arts centre sells tickets, subscriptions and memberships, and asks the same people for donations. Its box office is often two to five people (estimate), part-time in the off season, who also answer the phone and run the house. The ticketing system is also the donor database: Tessitura (750+ organisations), Spektrix, AudienceView, OvationTix, Ludus and ThunderTix compete for these buyers. The tools the big ticketers build for arenas, such as dynamic pricing and verified-fan queues, are aimed at a different problem. A small arts organisation's problem is that most first-time buyers never come back. Its revenue depends on converting them into subscribers and donors with no analyst on staff.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Arts ticketing and CRM platforms with built-in email and reporting; benchmark studies from arts service organisations; consultants for pricing and subscription campaigns. Gaps: no second-visit prediction, duplicate patron records across ticketing, donations and email, and pricing set by last season's chart.
