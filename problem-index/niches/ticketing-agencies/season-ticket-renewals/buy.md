# Customer-Success Churn Platforms for Season-Ticket Members

**Niche:** [[niches/ticketing-agencies/season-ticket-renewals/profile|Season-Ticket & Membership Ticketing]]
**Industry:** [[industries/ticketing-agencies|Ticketing & Reservation Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Software companies already run health scores and playbooks to save subscription accounts; a season-ticket base is a subscription business with a fixed renewal date and a resale market.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #survival-analysis #feature-engineering #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to tell a team which season-ticket members will not renew while there is still time to save them, and whoever's renewal forecast holds up at the renewal deadline takes the account.

## The Problem
Teams run renewal campaigns as outbound sales pushes. They do not run the continuous account-health process that subscription software companies use.

## What Already Exists
Customer-success platforms such as Gainsight, ChurnZero and Totango compute health scores from product usage, trigger playbooks and forecast renewals.

## The Customization Gap
A season-ticket version needs: usage defined as scans, transfers and resale per game rather than logins; a single fixed renewal deadline per season and payment-plan schedules; on-field performance as a covariate the team cannot control; broker detection, since a broker renews for different reasons; seat-relocation and price-increase events; and integration with the ticketer's account system and the team's CRM.

## Target Customer
Ticket sales and service leaders at teams that already run Salesforce or Dynamics.

## Impact If Solved
The renewal season becomes the end of a year-long retention process rather than a six-week sales sprint.
