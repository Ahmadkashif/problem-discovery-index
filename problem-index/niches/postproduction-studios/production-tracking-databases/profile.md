# Production Tracking & Project Intelligence Databases

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/postproduction-studios|Postproduction Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Maintain databases of film and TV projects in development, production and post (who is attached, which studio or streamer, shoot dates, expected post windows) and sell subscriptions to the vendors, crews and agencies who need to know what work is coming. Luminate Film & TV (formerly Variety Insight) runs a daily-updated production pipeline and credits database; Production Weekly and Vitrina are smaller players. Post houses use them to find shows before the vendor shortlist is set.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-60 researchers and data editors at the largest vendor, fewer elsewhere (estimate)
**Output:** Project records, production pipeline listings, credits, development tracking feeds
**Proprietary data:** Years of project status histories assembled from trade sources, calls and filings, with credits linked to projects
**Clock:** None set by a third party; listings matter while a show is crewing and booking post, but no filing or regulatory date governs the work
**Buyer:** Head of business development at a post or VFX vendor; head of data products at the database vendor

**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. The subscription is the data, so Q1 is 5, as for the other subscription data products in the vault ([[niches/music-publishers/music-consumption-data-providers/profile|🔍 Music Consumption Data & Chart Providers]], Luminate's music side, and [[niches/film-distributors/streaming-demand-title-valuation/profile|🔍 Streaming Demand & Title Valuation Data]], which includes Luminate's film and TV demand data). The project status history is genuinely the vendor's. It scores two points below the streaming demand pocket (45) on Q4 alone. Demand data is bought against licence negotiations and the licensing market calendar, while project tracking has no third-party deadline at all. The research team is modest, and the vendor never learns which listed projects turned into post work for its subscribers.
