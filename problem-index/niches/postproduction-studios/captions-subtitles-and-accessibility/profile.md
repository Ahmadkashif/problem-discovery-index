# Captions, Subtitles & Accessibility Files

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/postproduction-studios|Postproduction Studios]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver caption, subtitle and audio-description files that pass a platform's timed-text spec and the FCC quality rules on first submission while machines do the first pass, and whoever spends the fewest human minutes per programme hour without a rejection takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600M in US offline closed captioning, same-language subtitling, SDH and audio description for film and television (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of US postproduction revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High — speech recognition does the first pass; review and timing are human
**Target Buyer:** Head of accessibility or captioning operations at a vendor; deliverables lead at a post house; network or streamer accessibility manager
**Automation Potential:** Very high for transcription and timing; medium for SDH sound descriptions and audio description writing

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
NAICS 512191 includes closed captioning and subtitling. Every broadcast programme needs FCC-compliant captions judged on accuracy, synchronicity, completeness and placement, and every streamer needs timed text to its own style guide: reading-speed limits, line breaks, shot-change timing, SDH tags for sound and speaker identification, and placement that avoids burned-in text. Audio description is a growing requirement. Vendors range from large captioning companies (Verbit/VITAC, 3Play Media, Rev) to post houses' in-house teams. Speech recognition has collapsed the cost of transcription and moved the competition to review, timing and spec conformance. Translation into other languages belongs to [[industries/localization-services|Localization Services]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Speech-recognition engines, subtitle editors (EZTitles, Ooona, Subtitle Edit), platform style guides and validators, QC tools with timed-text checks. The gaps: human review covers every line rather than the risky ones; placement conflicts with on-screen text are fixed by hand; and SDH sound tags and audio description remain largely manual.
