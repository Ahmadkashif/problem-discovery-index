# Caption Quality & Loudness Regulators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/postproduction-studios|Postproduction Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The FCC sets closed-captioning obligations and the 2014 caption quality standards (accuracy, synchronicity, completeness, placement), and enforces the CALM Act's limits on commercial loudness. Enforcement is largely complaint-driven, handled by the Consumer and Governmental Affairs Bureau's disability rights staff and the Enforcement Bureau.

## Insight Function
**Size:** A small team handles captioning complaints and rules; loudness complaints are processed in bulk (estimate)
**Output:** Rules, complaint dispositions, occasional enforcement actions
**Proprietary data:** Consumer complaint records
**Clock:** Rulemaking dockets and complaint response windows
**Buyer:** Bureau chief
