# Remote Review Adapted to Supervised HDR Sessions

**Niche:** [[niches/postproduction-studios/long-form-picture-finishing/profile|Long-Form Picture Finishing]]
**Industry:** [[industries/postproduction-studios|Postproduction Studios]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Review platforms carry comments on frames; a remote colour session needs calibrated HDR, and notes that land on the right grade node.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to carry a series' look consistently across every episode, colorist, HDR grade and SDR trim inside the streamer's delivery window, and whoever does it with the fewest supervised sessions and redeliveries takes the show.

## The Problem
Since 2020, many supervised colour sessions happen remotely: the DP on another continent, the showrunner in a writers' room, the colorist in the suite. Notes come by voice over a call and are written down by an assistant, and HDR review on uncalibrated displays produces notes about problems that aren't there.

## What Already Exists
Frame.io and MediaSilo for frame-accurate comments on review files; low-latency streaming systems for live grading sessions; calibrated client displays and reference monitors.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: verification that the remote viewer is on a calibrated, spec-appropriate display before HDR notes are accepted; voice notes transcribed and attached to the shot and timecode under discussion; notes classified by type (look, continuity, technical, VFX) and routed, so a VFX note doesn't sit in the colorist's list; a session log of which notes were addressed in which grade version; and SDR-trim notes kept separate from HDR. Generic review platforms treat a colour session like a cut review.

## Target Customer
Finishing facilities running supervised remote sessions for series with dispersed creative teams.

## Impact If Solved
Fewer repeat notes and fewer sessions spent on display problems, and an audit trail of what the DP approved when a dispute arises at delivery.
