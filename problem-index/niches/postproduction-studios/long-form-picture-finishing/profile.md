# Long-Form Picture Finishing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/postproduction-studios|Postproduction Studios]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to carry a series' look consistently across every episode, colorist, HDR grade and SDR trim inside the streamer's delivery window, and whoever does it with the fewest supervised sessions and redeliveries takes the show.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.4B in US conform, online, colour grading and HDR mastering for features and scripted series (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~22% of US postproduction revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High in the suite, medium around it — grading is digital, look decisions live in the colorist's head and the DP's notes
**Target Buyer:** Head of finishing or managing director at a finishing facility; the show's post producer
**Automation Potential:** Medium — conform, shot matching and trims automate; the look and the supervised session do not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Finishing turns the locked offline cut into the master: the online editor conforms from camera originals, the colorist grades every shot with the cinematographer and showrunner, and the facility produces a Dolby Vision HDR master and derived SDR trim to the streamer's spec. On a series, a lead colorist sets the look in the first episodes and other colorists carry it through later ones, on a weekly cadence. Cinematographers and showrunners follow specific colorists between facilities, so the talent relationship wins the show; consistency and delivery keep it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
DaVinci Resolve and Baselight for grading and conform; Dolby Vision tools for HDR analysis and trims; Colorfront and Resolve for transcodes; remote review via calibrated streaming. The gaps: look drift between episodes and colorists is caught by eye in review; conform of reframes, speed ramps and effects breaks in translation and is fixed by hand; and supervised session time is spent on notes that could have been caught beforehand.
