# Streamer Delivery-Spec & Post-Technology Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/postproduction-studios|Postproduction Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The production and post technology groups at streamers and studios write the delivery specifications post houses must meet (HDR and Dolby Vision rules, IMF profiles, loudness targets, timed-text style guides), build the inspection tools that check incoming deliveries, and vet partner facilities. Netflix's Backlot help site and its Post Partner Program (NP3) are the most visible example.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-30 spec writers and delivery-quality analysts per major platform (estimate); engineers who build the inspection tools are not counted
**Output:** Delivery specifications, inspection tools and error codes, partner vetting, workflow guidance
**Proprietary data:** Every inspection result for every delivery from every vendor, which is the only cross-vendor record of where deliverables fail
**Clock:** The platform's own launch and premiere dates
**Buyer:** VP of production technology or content operations
