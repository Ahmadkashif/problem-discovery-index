# Post Facility Rollups

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/postproduction-studios|Postproduction Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Groups that have bought and combined post facilities. Streamland Media owns Picture Shop, Formosa Group (sound), Picture Head, Ghost VFX, The Farm and Finalé, and bought Technicolor's post business in 2021. Company 3 and a few independents (FotoKem, Harbor) run multi-site operations. They sell editorial, finishing, sound and VFX to studios and streamers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Corporate development and FP&A teams of roughly 3-15 (estimate)
**Output:** Internal acquisition models, utilisation and margin reporting
**Proprietary data:** Project financials and utilisation across their facilities
**Clock:** Lender covenants and owners' reporting cycles
**Buyer:** CFO
