# Post & VFX Trade Associations and Guilds

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/postproduction-studios|Postproduction Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Hollywood Professional Association (HPA), Visual Effects Society (VES), Motion Picture Sound Editors (MPSE), Colorist Society International and UK Screen Alliance run conferences, awards, training and occasional surveys. The Motion Picture Editors Guild (IATSE Local 700) negotiates wages and conditions for editors, assistants, colorists and sound editors, and IATSE now represents some studio-employed VFX crews.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Small staffs; research is occasional surveys and white papers (estimate)
**Output:** Member surveys, best-practice papers, wage scales, conference programmes
**Proprietary data:** Member rosters and survey responses
**Clock:** Bargaining cycles and annual events
**Buyer:** Executive director
