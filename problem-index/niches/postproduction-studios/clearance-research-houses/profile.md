# Script, Title & Archival Clearance Researchers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/postproduction-studios|Postproduction Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Independent clearance firms and freelance researchers prepare script clearance reports (names, places and businesses that could raise claims) and archival clearance logs, and order or interpret title reports, all of which E&O insurers and distributors require before delivery. Production counsel usually commission the work, and documentary archival producers track licences clip by clip. Most film title reports themselves are produced by the entertainment title-search units of the trademark research firms, Clarivate's CompuMark and Corsearch. Those units run on the same databases and analyst operations as trademark clearance and are profiled as [[niches/graphic-design-studios/trademark-clearance-search-providers/profile|🔍 Trademark Clearance & Design-Mark Search Providers]] (47). They are excluded here so one organisation gets one profile, and this pocket gates only the independent clearance firms.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 2-10 researchers per independent firm (estimate)
**Output:** Script clearance reports, archival licence logs, title-report reviews
**Proprietary data:** Prior clearance files and research notes per production
**Clock:** E&O insurance binding and distributor delivery dates
**Buyer:** Firm owner; production counsel
