# Tentpole Feature VFX Packages

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/postproduction-studios|Postproduction Studios]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to commit hundreds of artists, in the jurisdiction with the richest VFX credit, to a studio's fixed release date under a supervisor the studio trusts, and whoever can guarantee that capacity without idling it between turnovers takes the award.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.2B in US-billed VFX for studio features and high-end limited series (estimate); the global market for this tier is several times larger
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~19% of US postproduction revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High — mature pipelines, render farms and tracking
**Target Buyer:** Vendor CEO and head of production; studio VFX executive and production VFX supervisor
**Automation Potential:** Medium — capacity planning and prep automate; hero creature, FX and environment work does not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A tentpole package runs ~1,000 to 3,000 shots (estimate) over a year or more, is split across several vendors, and is awarded on capacity and supervision as much as price. The studio's release date is fixed months in advance; turnovers arrive late because the edit is still moving. Vendors must hire ahead of awards in the right jurisdictions and then hold teams through gaps, which is how a strong vendor runs out of cash. A handful of large vendors (Wētā FX, ILM, Framestore, DNEG, Rodeo FX and a few others) compete for this tier.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Production tracking, render-farm schedulers, in-house resourcing spreadsheets and studio-side bid comparisons. The gaps: artist demand by department is forecast from the award schedule, not from how turnovers actually slip; idle time between turnovers is absorbed rather than evidenced; and capacity commitments across shows are negotiated without a shared forecast.
