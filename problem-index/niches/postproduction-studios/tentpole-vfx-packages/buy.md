# Workforce Planning Adapted to Shot Pipelines

**Niche:** [[niches/postproduction-studios/tentpole-vfx-packages/profile|Tentpole Feature VFX Packages]]
**Industry:** [[industries/postproduction-studios|Postproduction Studios]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Enterprise workforce planning tools forecast headcount by role and quarter; a VFX vendor needs it by department, week, site and shot dependency.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #linear-regression #optimization-fundamentals #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to commit hundreds of artists, in the jurisdiction with the richest VFX credit, to a studio's fixed release date under a supervisor the studio trusts, and whoever can guarantee that capacity without idling it between turnovers takes the award.

## The Problem
Large vendors run 500 to several thousand artists across sites and hire freelancers and contract artists in waves. Planning is done per show in spreadsheets and reconciled weekly in resourcing meetings.

## What Already Exists
Workforce planning and financial planning platforms (Anaplan, Workday Adaptive Planning, Pigment) and the resource-scheduling modules in production trackers.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: demand expressed in artist-weeks by VFX department (layout, animation, FX, lighting, comp, prep) and seniority; dependencies between departments, since lighting cannot start until animation is approved; site-level constraints from incentive jurisdictions and visa lead times; turnover slippage as an input, not a surprise; and freelancer pools with notice periods. Generic tools model roles and quarters, not pipelines and weeks.

## Target Customer
Heads of production and resourcing managers at vendors with 300 or more artists.

## Impact If Solved
One plan across shows replaces per-show spreadsheets, so a resourcing manager sees a three-week gap in comp two months ahead and fills it with pipeline work instead of a layoff.
