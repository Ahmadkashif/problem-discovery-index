# Offshore Roto, Paint & Prep Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/postproduction-studios|Postproduction Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Large prep and stereo-conversion teams, mostly in India and other lower-cost centres, that do roto, paint, matchmove and cleanup for VFX vendors worldwide, priced per frame or per shot. Some are divisions of global VFX groups; others are independent subcontractors.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Hundreds to thousands of artists per vendor; analytics and estimating staff in single digits (estimate)
**Output:** Finished roto shapes, paint frames and tracks delivered back to the commissioning vendor
**Proprietary data:** Per-frame effort records, which describe client shots
**Clock:** The commissioning vendor's turnover schedule
**Buyer:** Head of production
