# A Deliverables Quote Read From the Delivery Schedule

**Industry:** [[industries/postproduction-studios|Postproduction Studios]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** An independent producer learns what a distributor's deliverables will cost when the post house's invoice arrives, after the budget is spent.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to take a locked independent cut to a deliverables package a distributor or streamer will accept, inside a finishing budget set before anyone read the delivery schedule, and whoever quotes the full deliverables cost up front and holds it takes the filmmaker.

## The Problem
A documentary sells at a festival. The streamer's schedule asks for a Dolby Vision master and SDR trim, a 5.1 mix with M&E and dialogue stems, textless backgrounds, closed captions and subtitles in two languages, a cue sheet, E&O insurance and archival licences for worldwide all media. The film was finished in Rec. 709 with a stereo mix and festival-only archival clearances. The producer now needs a budget for what is effectively a second finish, and post houses quote item by item over weeks.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Each post house prices deliverables from its own rate card and experience. Independent producers buy finishing a few times in a career, and the knowledge of what each schedule item really costs sits with post supervisors who are hired too late.

## What to Build
Upload the delivery schedule and a description of the film's current state (resolution, colour pipeline, mix format, archival share, languages). The tool parses the schedule, identifies the gap between what exists and what is owed, and prices each gap from a rate model built on real post house quotes. It flags the expensive surprises first: an HDR regrade, an Atmos remix, re-clearing archival for all media.

## Target Customer
Independent producers and documentary producers between sale and delivery; post houses wanting to quote indie deliverables faster.

## Impact If Built
Producers can negotiate delivery terms or minimum guarantees knowing the real cost, and post houses stop discovering half the work after quoting an indie rate.
