# Motion-Imaging Standards Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/postproduction-studios|Postproduction Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
SMPTE writes the standards postproduction delivers to, including the IMF (ST 2067) and DCP (ST 429) families and HDR transfer functions. Digital Cinema Initiatives (DCI) maintains the studios' digital cinema specification. Work is done by volunteer committees of engineers from vendors, studios and facilities, supported by small staff.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Small professional staff; committee work is volunteer (estimate)
**Output:** Published standards, recommended practices and test materials
**Proprietary data:** Standards drafts and committee records
**Clock:** Self-set ballot and publication cycles
**Buyer:** Executive director
