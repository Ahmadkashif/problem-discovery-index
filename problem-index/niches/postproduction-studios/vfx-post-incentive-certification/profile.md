# VFX & Post Incentive Certification Agencies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/postproduction-studios|Postproduction Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Government units that certify productions and their VFX and post spend for tax incentives: the BFI's certification unit for the UK Audio-Visual Expenditure Credit (with its 39% VFX rate from 2025), Screen Australia and the ATO for the 30% PDV Offset, and provincial bodies in Quebec, Ontario and British Columbia. They review applications, cultural tests and audited expenditure, and issue the certificates that let credits be claimed.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 certification staff per agency (estimate)
**Output:** Interim and final certificates, eligibility rulings, programme statistics
**Proprietary data:** Applications and audited expenditure for every certified production, including the split of VFX and post spend by jurisdiction
**Clock:** Statutory application windows and tax-return deadlines
**Buyer:** Head of certification or programme director
