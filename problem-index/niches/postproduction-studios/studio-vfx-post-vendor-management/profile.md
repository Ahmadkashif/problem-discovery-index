# Studio & Streamer VFX and Post Vendor Management

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/postproduction-studios|Postproduction Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The VFX and post-production departments at major studios and streamers send packages out to bid, compare vendors' bids, award work, negotiate change orders and track vendor cost and delivery across every show. They are the post industry's paying customer and see more bids than any vendor.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 bid managers, VFX and post cost analysts and production finance staff per major studio or streamer (estimate); the executives who award work are the buyers, not the function
**Output:** Bid comparisons, award recommendations, vendor scorecards, cost reports to production finance
**Proprietary data:** Competing bids for the same breakdowns from many vendors, with awarded and final costs per show
**Clock:** Release and premiere dates, set by the studio itself
**Buyer:** SVP of VFX or post production
