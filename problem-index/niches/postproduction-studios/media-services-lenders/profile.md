# Media Services Lenders & Incentive Financiers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/postproduction-studios|Postproduction Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Banks, specialist lenders and funds that lend to post and VFX vendors against studio purchase orders and receivables, finance equipment and render infrastructure, or advance cash against tax-credit claims before the certificate is issued.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 3-10 credit staff covering media services (estimate)
**Output:** Credit decisions and facility monitoring
**Proprietary data:** Borrower financials and receivable histories, supplied under confidentiality
**Clock:** Drawdown requests and incentive claim timelines
**Buyer:** Head of media lending
