# Post & VFX Software Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/postproduction-studios|Postproduction Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Sell the software postproduction runs on: Avid (Media Composer, Pro Tools), Blackmagic Design (DaVinci Resolve), Autodesk (Maya, Flow Production Tracking), Foundry (Nuke), Adobe (Premiere Pro, Frame.io), SideFX (Houdini), and AI-native entrants (Runway, Colourlab.ai, Flawless). Each has product managers and engineers; a few have market intelligence or user research analysts covering the post and VFX market.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 2-10 market intelligence and user research analysts per vendor on the post and VFX market (estimate); product managers and engineers are not counted
**Output:** Internal competitive analysis and workflow research feeding product roadmaps
**Proprietary data:** Usage telemetry and support tickets across the installed base
**Clock:** NAB and IBC launch cycles and fiscal quarters, chosen by the vendor
**Buyer:** VP of product
