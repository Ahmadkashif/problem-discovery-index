# Media QC & Content Supply-Chain Software

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/postproduction-studios|Postproduction Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Sell automated file-based QC (Interra BATON, Venera Pulsar and Quasar, Telestream Vidchecker and Qualify) and content supply-chain orchestration (SDVI Rally) to post houses, studios and streamers. A small team at each vendor reads every platform's delivery spec and turns it into QC templates; solutions engineers and support staff deploy the software. Facilities pay licences and cloud usage fees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 2-8 spec and template analysts per vendor (estimate); solutions and product engineers are not counted
**Output:** QC templates and rule libraries per platform; QC reports generated for each customer file
**Proprietary data:** Aggregate defect statistics from cloud QC runs, which describe customers' content rather than the vendor's
**Clock:** Platform spec revisions and customers' delivery dates
**Buyer:** VP of product at the vendor
