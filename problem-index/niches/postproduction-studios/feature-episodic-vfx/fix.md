# The Incentive Split Decided in a Spreadsheet

**Niche:** [[niches/postproduction-studios/feature-episodic-vfx/profile|Feature & Episodic VFX]]
**Industry:** [[industries/postproduction-studios|Postproduction Studios]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Which shots are done in London, Montreal, Sydney or Mumbai is decided by tax credit as much as by talent, and the per-shot record of where the work happened is rebuilt by hand at claim time.
**Tags:** #linear-regression #descriptive-statistics #compliance #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #quick-win
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is "every serious competitor is fighting to price shot packages on fixed bids accurately enough to win the award and still make money," but the winners differ: tentpole feature packages are won by whoever can commit hundreds of artists under a trusted supervisor in the richest incentive jurisdiction, while episodic and invisible-effects work is won by whoever turns around high shot counts at a known cost per shot inside a weekly schedule. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Studios award VFX partly on where the work qualifies for the richest credit: the UK's 39% VFX rate with no cap on UK VFX costs from 2025, Australia's 30% PDV Offset, Quebec and Ontario credits. Multi-site vendors split packages across studios to fit, then must prove qualifying spend by jurisdiction to the production's incentive accountants. Artists move between shots and sites, and the split agreed at award drifts.

## Why It's Still Broken
Time is logged per shot, payroll is per site, and incentive rules are per jurisdiction. Nobody joins them until the claim is due, so coordinators reconstruct the evidence from timesheets and emails, and the production's accountants query every gap.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Tag every time entry with site and jurisdiction at source, keep a live forecast of qualifying spend per jurisdiction against the award, and alert when re-allocation pushes a show below a threshold or over a cap. Export the evidence pack in the format each incentive auditor expects.

## Who Feels the Pain
VFX producers and finance teams at multi-site vendors; the production accountants and incentive advisers preparing claims, covered as a pocket under [[industries/video-production-smb|SMB Video Production]]; and studios relying on the credit to close a budget.

## Impact If Fixed
Weeks of claim-time reconstruction disappear, and the vendor can show a studio its incentive delivery against the award during production, not after.
