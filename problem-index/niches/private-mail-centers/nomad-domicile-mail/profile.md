# Domicile Mail for Full-Time Travellers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/private-mail-centers|Private Mail Centers]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a traveller's address legally usable for a driver's licence, vehicle registration, voting and banking while their mail reaches them wherever they are, and whoever's address keeps passing the state DMV, the election office and the bank takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$80M a year of mail-service, forwarding and domicile-support fees paid by full-time RVers, expatriates, travel nurses and other nomads (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% of receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — scanning and online forwarding requests are common; domicile paperwork and agency acceptance are manual
**Target Buyer:** The traveller; mail-service operators such as Escapees and America's Mailbox
**Automation Potential:** Medium — forwarding and scanning automate; agency acceptance is a rules problem that changes by state

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Full-time travellers have no home address, yet the law expects one: for a driver's licence, vehicle registration and insurance, jury duty, voting, taxes and bank identity checks. Mail services in South Dakota, Texas and Florida (Escapees offers all three) provide an address and forward mail, and travellers use it to establish domicile, subject to state rules that differ and change. Generic mailbox tools treat these customers like any box holder, though for them the address is a legal status as well as a mailbox.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Specialist mail services (Escapees, America's Mailbox and others), virtual-mailbox networks (iPostal1, Anytime Mailbox), and RV club guidance. Gaps: no current map of which agencies and banks accept which mail-service addresses, forwarding schedules that miss legal deadlines, and travellers learning the rules by rejection.
