# USPS CMRA Oversight & Postal Inspection

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/private-mail-centers|Private Mail Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
USPS Retail and Post Office Operations sets CMRA policy and runs the CMRA Customer Registration Database; local postmasters approve CMRA applications, sample mail and review data; the Postal Inspection Service monitors CMRA data for fraud, identity theft and narcotics and can order mail withheld from a box. The USPS Inspector General audits the programme (Report 24-146-R25, June 2025).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Programme staff and inspectors with CMRA among many duties; no CMRA-dedicated analytics unit of 10+ found (estimate)
**Output:** Policy, approvals, suspensions, withholding orders, audits
**Proprietary data:** Every registered PMB holder's Form 1583 and IDs for nearly 12,000 CMRAs and over 1.6 million customers, about 39% migrated to the database by February 2025
**Clock:** Quarterly certifications (15 January, April, July, October)
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer
