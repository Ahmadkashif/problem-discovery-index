# Pack-and-Ship Franchisor Corporate Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/private-mail-centers|Private Mail Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The franchisors of the category (The UPS Store, Inc., with 5,603 US locations in September 2026; PostNet, part of MBE Worldwide since 2017; Annex Brands with Pak Mail and sister brands) run corporate teams that benchmark store sales, set retail shipping prices and carrier terms, design mailbox and returns programmes, and choose sites. Their analysis supports royalty revenue and franchise sales rather than being sold.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-40 analysts at The UPS Store, Inc.; under 10 at the other franchisors (estimate)
**Output:** Store benchmarks, pricing and programme decisions, site and territory analysis
**Proprietary data:** Point-of-sale transactions across thousands of stores, carrier volumes, mailbox counts and guarantee claims
**Clock:** Peak season, carrier general rate increases each January and franchise disclosure update cycles, all market or self-set dates, so no external deadline governs the analysis
**Buyer:** Chief financial officer or vice president of strategy at the franchisor
