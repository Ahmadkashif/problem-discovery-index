# Distribution Packaging Science for One-Off Parcels

**Niche:** [[niches/private-mail-centers/packing-damage-claims/profile|Packing & Damage-Claim Liability]]
**Industry:** [[industries/private-mail-centers|Private Mail Centers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Packaging engineers already test product packaging against standard drop and vibration profiles; nobody has turned that science into rules for a one-off parcel packed in a minute at a counter.
**Tags:** #decision-trees #evaluation-metrics #feature-engineering #worker-facing #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to pack each walk-in item so it arrives intact at a price the customer accepts, and whoever can show a carrier or insurer how a damaged parcel was packed, and so wins the claim, takes the account.

## The Problem
Counter packing rules are folklore passed between associates. Consistent, explainable rules would cut damage and give the store a standard to point to in a claim.

## What Already Exists
Distribution packaging test procedures (ISTA-type drop, vibration and compression protocols), cushion-curve data from foam and fill suppliers, and the carriers' own published packaging guidelines, used by manufacturers and the testing laboratories that serve them.

## The Customization Gap
A counter needs: item classes rather than engineered products (lamp, framed print, laptop, dishes); materials limited to what is on the store's wall; a decision table that fits on one screen and takes seconds; carrier-specific handling differences; the price impact of each option through dimensional weight; and a printed packing standard per class that matches what the guarantee and insurer require.

## Target Customer
Franchisor training departments, buying groups (Retail Shipping Associates, AMBC) and packaging suppliers selling to stores.

## Impact If Solved
A shared, explainable packing standard reduces the variation between associates that causes most avoidable damage, and gives the store a documented method when a claim is argued.
