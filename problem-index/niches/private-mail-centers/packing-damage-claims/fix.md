# The Claim Lost Because Nobody Kept the Box

**Niche:** [[niches/private-mail-centers/packing-damage-claims/profile|Packing & Damage-Claim Liability]]
**Industry:** [[industries/private-mail-centers|Private Mail Centers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A damaged parcel's recipient throws away the box, the insurer asks for the packaging, and a claim the store could have won is denied.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #automation #compliance #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to pack each walk-in item so it arrives intact at a price the customer accepts, and whoever can show a carrier or insurer how a damaged parcel was packed, and so wins the claim, takes the account.

## The Problem
Claims have conditions: inspection of the packaging, photographs, proof of value, filing inside a window (U-PIC requires notice within 90 days of shipment). The store learns of damage when the sender calls, often after the recipient has discarded the box. Shipsurance's terms say claims can be denied for insufficient packing if the packaging is not kept.

## Why It's Still Broken
The store is not the recipient and has no channel to them; the claim process differs by carrier and insurer; and the owner handles claims as occasional exceptions with no checklist.

## What a Fix Looks Like
At the counter, give the sender a printed and texted "if it arrives damaged" card: keep everything, photograph before unpacking, call within days. When a claim starts, generate the carrier- or insurer-specific checklist and deadline, attach the store's own packing photographs if captured, and track it to resolution.

## Who Feels the Pain
Owners paying guarantee claims from margin; senders who bought coverage and get nothing; associates blamed for packing that may have been fine.

## Impact If Fixed
A simple instruction at the counter and a claim checklist convert some currently denied claims into paid ones (estimate) and stop the store paying guarantees it could have recovered.
