# Counter Shipping & Packing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/private-mail-centers|Private Mail Centers]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor is fighting to win the walk-in shipment at a retail price, but writing it produced two contests with different winners: choosing the carrier and service that keeps the store's margin after incentives and adjustments, which rate engines and carrier-programme terms decide, and packing so the parcel arrives and any claim is defensible, which packing method and guarantee backing decide. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.6B a year of counter shipping and packing receipts, including carrier charges passed through (estimate, ~half of 2022 receipts)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~50% of receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — rating and labels are on screen; measuring, packing and claims are manual
**Target Buyer:** Store owner; at franchisors, the vice president of retail operations
**Automation Potential:** Medium — quoting and claims automate, packing stays by hand

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is the core of the store: a customer with an item and no box, label or carrier account, served at the counter with packing, a multi-carrier quote at retail prices and a receipt. The store competes with the carriers' own retail counters, the post office and the customer's ability to print a label at home. What it sells is the packing and the choice. The margin on each sale depends on the service steered to, the box chosen, and what the carrier and insurer later do with the parcel.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Pack-and-ship point-of-sale systems (PostalMate, ShipRite NEXT, franchise systems), carrier retail programmes (FedEx Authorized ShipCenter, UPS Authorized Shipping Outlet, DHL ServicePoint, USPS Approved Shipper), parcel insurers (U-PIC, Shipsurance) and franchise guarantees. Gaps: the transaction ends at the label; adjustments, damage notices and claims arrive later through other channels and are never joined back.
