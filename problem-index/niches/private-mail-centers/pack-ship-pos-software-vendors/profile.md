# Pack-and-Ship POS & Mailbox Software Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/private-mail-centers|Private Mail Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Small software firms that sell point-of-sale, multi-carrier shipping and mailbox management to independent stores: PC Synergy (PostalMate) and ShipRite (ShipRite NEXT, Smart Mailboxes, the Rosie drop-off kiosk), alongside franchise systems' in-house platforms.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Development and support teams; under 10 doing analysis (estimate)
**Output:** Software licences and support
**Proprietary data:** Transaction data stored on stores' own systems, often on-premise desktop installations
**Clock:** Carrier rate and label-format changes each January
**Buyer:** Founder or product lead
