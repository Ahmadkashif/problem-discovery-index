# Retail Shipping Trade Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/private-mail-centers|Private Mail Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer

## What They Do
Retail Shipping Associates (which describes itself as 8,000+ strong and runs the Retail Shippers Expo) and AMBC, the Association of Mail & Business Centers (founded 1982, member-owned since 2014, claiming 1,100+ stores), negotiate carrier programme access and group freight discounts, run forums and training, and represent independents to the carriers and USPS.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Small staffs; under 10 doing research (estimate)
**Output:** Programme access, training, newsletters, trade shows
**Proprietary data:** Member lists and programme enrolment; no published benchmark surveys found
**Clock:** Annual trade show and carrier programme renewals
**Buyer:** Executive director or board
