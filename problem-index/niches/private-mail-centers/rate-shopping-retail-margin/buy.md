# Ecommerce Rate Engines Turned Toward the Counter

**Niche:** [[niches/private-mail-centers/rate-shopping-retail-margin/profile|Multi-Carrier Rate Shopping & Retail Margin]]
**Industry:** [[industries/private-mail-centers|Private Mail Centers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Ecommerce shipping platforms already rate-shop four carriers in milliseconds and audit invoices; they optimise the shipper's cost, not a reseller's margin at a walk-in counter.
**Tags:** #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #revenue-impact #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show the walk-in customer the service they will accept at the store's best net margin after programme incentives, surcharges and adjustments, and whoever prices the counter quote closest to what the carrier finally bills takes the account.

## The Problem
Independent stores need modern carrier integrations and invoice auditing, but their point-of-sale vendors are small and their ecommerce-grade alternatives assume the user is the shipper.

## What Already Exists
ShipStation, Shippo and EasyPost provide multi-carrier rating, label purchase, address validation, tracking webhooks and, in some plans, invoice auditing and refund claims, all through mature APIs.

## The Customization Gap
A counter needs: retail and account price shown side by side with the store's margin; carrier-programme incentives (drop-off payments, tier discounts) included in the ranking; packing and materials sold with the shipment; walk-in customers without accounts; adjustments traced to the associate and box; and a customer-facing screen that shows two or three sensible options rather than every service.

## Target Customer
Point-of-sale vendors that would license an engine rather than maintain carrier integrations themselves, and multi-unit independent owners.

## Impact If Solved
Stores get ecommerce-grade rating and auditing without rebuilding their counter, and adjustments become visible per transaction rather than per month.
