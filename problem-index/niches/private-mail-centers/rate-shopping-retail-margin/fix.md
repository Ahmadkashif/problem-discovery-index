# The Dimension Adjustment Nobody Passes Back

**Niche:** [[niches/private-mail-centers/rate-shopping-retail-margin/profile|Multi-Carrier Rate Shopping & Retail Margin]]
**Industry:** [[industries/private-mail-centers|Private Mail Centers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A carrier re-measures a parcel, bills the store more, and nobody at the store can say which associate measured it or whether the customer's box was the cause.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #feature-engineering #data-integration #revenue-impact #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show the walk-in customer the service they will accept at the store's best net margin after programme incentives, surcharges and adjustments, and whoever prices the counter quote closest to what the carrier finally bills takes the account.

## The Problem
Adjustments appear on the carrier invoice as lines against tracking numbers. The owner pays them in bulk. The same associate rounds down, the same oversize box from the wall triggers additional handling, and the pattern repeats because nobody connects the line to the counter.

## Why It's Still Broken
Invoice lines and counter transactions sit in different systems, and the weekly reconciliation that would connect them is the first task dropped when the store is busy.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Match each adjustment to its counter transaction by tracking number automatically. Report adjustments weekly by associate, box size and carrier service. Flag the boxes in the store's stock whose real dimensions differ from the catalogue values in the point-of-sale system, a common silent cause.

## Who Feels the Pain
Owners paying adjustments out of margin; associates blamed in general for errors nobody can attribute.

## Impact If Fixed
A few weeks of attributed adjustments usually reveal a handful of fixable causes (a mis-entered box size, one associate's habit) that stop recurring once seen (estimate).
