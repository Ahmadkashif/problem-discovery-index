# Virtual Mailbox & Virtual Office Networks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/private-mail-centers|Private Mail Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Networks that sell digital mailboxes at addresses operated by partner stores and office centres: iPostal1 (which claims 4,250+ locations run by independent partners), Anytime Mailbox (~2,500 by a competitor's count), Earth Class Mail, Traveling Mailbox and virtual-office networks. Customers subscribe online; partners image, scan, forward and shred.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Product, operations and support teams; under 10 doing analysis (estimate)
**Output:** Subscriptions, scanned mail, forwarding
**Proprietary data:** Envelope images and scanned contents belong to the mailbox holders; subscription and location data are the network's own
**Clock:** Customers' instructions and the CMRA quarterly certification at each partner
**Buyer:** Chief operating officer
