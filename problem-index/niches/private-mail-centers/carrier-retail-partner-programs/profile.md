# Carrier Retail-Partner Program Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/private-mail-centers|Private Mail Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The carriers run programmes that turn independent stores into retail outlets: FedEx Authorized ShipCenter, UPS Authorized Shipping Outlet and Access Point, DHL ServicePoint and USPS Approved Shipper. Programme teams set discounts and drop-off incentives (FedEx pays ShipCenters quarterly for packages dropped off on customers' accounts and can change terms on 30 days' notice), recruit locations and plan retail coverage against the carriers' own stores and lockers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-30 analysts per carrier on retail-partner networks (estimate)
**Output:** Discount tiers, incentive schedules, location coverage plans
**Proprietary data:** Drop-off and retail shipment volumes by partner location across thousands of stores
**Clock:** Peak season and annual rate changes, set by the carriers themselves
**Buyer:** No external buyer; internal programme leadership
