# Parcel Locker & Smart-Mailbox Networks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/private-mail-centers|Private Mail Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Locker operators and vendors (Amazon Hub Locker, Quadient's Parcel Pending, Luxer One) and store-installed smart mailboxes such as ShipRite's give recipients unattended package pickup. They compete with the mail center's package-receiving service and also sell into stores and apartment buildings.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Operations analytics inside hardware and platform businesses; under 10 on locker utilisation per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Locker hardware, software subscriptions, pickup service
**Proprietary data:** Deposit-to-pickup times by site, largely held for property-owner clients
**Clock:** None external
**Buyer:** Vice president of product
