# International Counter Shippers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/private-mail-centers|Private Mail Centers]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get an international parcel through destination customs without a hold, a surprise duty bill or a return, and whoever gets the declaration and the restricted-items check right at the counter the first time takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M a year of international parcels shipped at mail-center counters, including carrier charges (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — labels and customs forms are generated on screen from whatever the associate types
**Target Buyer:** Store owner; the customers are immigrant families shipping to relatives and micro-exporters without carrier accounts
**Automation Potential:** Medium-High — classification, restricted-item checks and landed-cost estimates automate

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A family sending clothes, medicine and electronics to relatives abroad, or a craft seller shipping one order to Canada, does not have a carrier account or know a tariff code. They rely on the associate, who types "gifts" or "used clothing" on the declaration, picks a value and hopes. Destination customs hold, tax or return parcels for vague descriptions, undeclared batteries or medicine, and value mismatches. Carriers' ecommerce cross-border tools serve account shippers. The walk-in shipper, often more comfortable in another language, gets a form and no guidance. Large-item crating for export is a different contest that belongs to the queued Crating & Export Packers industry.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Point-of-sale customs-form generation, carrier international services and duty-paid options, and ecommerce cross-border tools (Zonos, Avalara) for online merchants. Gaps: no item-level classification at the counter, no destination restricted-items check for walk-ins, no landed-cost estimate in the customer's language, and no record of which declarations caused holds.
