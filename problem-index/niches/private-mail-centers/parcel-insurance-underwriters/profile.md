# Parcel Insurance & Shipping-Protection Underwriters

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/private-mail-centers|Private Mail Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Third-party parcel insurers and shipping-protection providers (U-PIC, Shipsurance, UPS Capital's InsureShield and newer checkout-protection firms) cover lost and damaged parcels for a premium per shipment, often sold through store counters and shipping software, and adjudicate claims. Their terms carry the industry's packing standard in practice: U-PIC excludes parcels packed insufficiently and requires notice within 90 days.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-40 underwriting, pricing and claims analysts at the larger providers (estimate); claims adjusters are production
**Output:** Premium rates by declared value, carrier and commodity; claim decisions; exclusions
**Proprietary data:** Claims and loss history by commodity, carrier, service, lane and packing evidence, held as the insurer's own records
**Clock:** Claim windows set in their own policy terms; no third-party deadline
**Buyer:** Head of underwriting or chief risk officer

**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. A risk-bearer prices its own coverage rather than selling analysis, and the clock is self-set. The insurer holds the one label the stores lack, which parcels arrived damaged and how they were packed, and uses it to deny claims rather than to price store-packed parcels with evidence lower. That gap is the commercial wedge for the packing record in [[niches/private-mail-centers/packing-damage-claims/profile|Packing & Damage-Claim Liability]].
