# Pack-and-Ship Business Brokerage & Resale Valuation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/private-mail-centers|Private Mail Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Business brokers and franchise-resale specialists value and sell existing pack-and-ship stores, most of them franchise resales, pricing them on seller's discretionary earnings, mailbox count and lease terms.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 2-10 brokers per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Valuations, listings, buyer introductions
**Proprietary data:** Private resale comparables
**Clock:** Transaction and franchisor-approval timelines
**Buyer:** Principal broker
