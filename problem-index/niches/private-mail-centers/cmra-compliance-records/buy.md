# Identity Verification Given the CMRA Rulebook

**Niche:** [[niches/private-mail-centers/cmra-compliance-records/profile|CMRA Compliance & Form 1583 Records]]
**Industry:** [[industries/private-mail-centers|Private Mail Centers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Identity verification platforms already read IDs, match selfies and detect forgeries; none knows what a Form 1583 needs or that the check must stay true every quarter.
**Tags:** #cnns #transformers #evaluation-metrics #compliance #automation #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep every holder's Form 1583, IDs, terminations and remail addresses current in the USPS database by each quarterly certification date, and whoever lets a store certify on 15 January, April, July and October with no expired ID and no evening of re-keying takes the account.

## The Problem
Remote mailbox onboarding, now allowed by real-time video witnessing, needs strong identity proofing, and stores have none of their own.

## What Already Exists
Persona, Onfido and Jumio provide document capture, authenticity checks, selfie matching and watchlist screening; Proof and Notarize run remote notarial sessions with identity proofing.

## The Customization Gap
The CMRA version needs: a secondary address document (lease, utility bill, registration) matched to the permanent address on the form, which generic flows do not ask for; one verification per addressee including each spouse; authorised individuals for business boxes; expiry dates kept and re-verified before each quarter; outputs formatted for the USPS database; and a witnessing step that satisfies DMM 508.1.8.3 whether done by store staff or a US-commissioned notary.

## Target Customer
Virtual-mailbox networks and franchisors onboarding holders remotely; point-of-sale vendors integrating one verification partner for all stores.

## Impact If Solved
Remote onboarding with a verification record that meets the DMM, so stores can accept out-of-area holders without weakening the identity check.
