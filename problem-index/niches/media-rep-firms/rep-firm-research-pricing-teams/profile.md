# Rep Firm Research & Pricing Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The central research, marketing and pricing staff at the large rep firms. They post ratings, build market and category cases for sellers, produce pricing guidance for represented stations and prepare renewal presentations. Katz and CoxReps each aggregate hundreds of stations, which makes these teams the widest view of national spot anywhere. They are paid out of commission and support selling; they do not sell research.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-80 per large firm (estimate)
**Output:** Ratings posts, market and category presentations, pricing guidance, station renewal decks
**Proprietary data:** Proposal, order and posting histories across hundreds of represented stations
**Clock:** Quarterly selling cycles and contract renewals; political windows in even years
**Buyer:** EVP of research and marketing
