# Broadcast Sales Bureaus & Rep Trade Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Television Bureau of Advertising and the Radio Advertising Bureau produce research that helps stations and reps sell their medium against others. They work on electronic transaction standards and present forecasts with partners such as BIA. The Station Representatives Association speaks for the rep firms themselves. Members fund them through dues.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically under 10 dedicated research staff per body (estimate)
**Output:** Member research, sales presentations, standards work, conferences
**Proprietary data:** Member surveys and licensed third-party data
**Clock:** Annual conference and forecast calendars
**Buyer:** President
