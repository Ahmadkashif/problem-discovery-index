# Broadcast Sales Training & Revenue Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Train station and rep sellers, audit sales and pricing practice, and advise station groups on revenue strategy and on whether to keep a rep or take national sales in-house. The Center for Sales Strategy and smaller specialists are examples. Station groups and occasionally rep firms pay per engagement or on retainer.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-40 consultants and analysts at the larger firms (estimate)
**Output:** Training programmes, sales and pricing audits, hiring assessments, revenue strategy reviews
**Proprietary data:** Engagement files and seller assessment results; little structured market data
**Clock:** Budget and planning cycles set by clients
**Buyer:** Managing partner
