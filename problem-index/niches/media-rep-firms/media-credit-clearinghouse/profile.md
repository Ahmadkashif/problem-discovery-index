# Media Credit Clearinghouse

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The Broadcast Cable Credit Association, a subsidiary of the Media Financial Management Association, pools credit information on advertisers, agencies and buying services for media companies deciding whether to extend credit on a buy. It holds tens of thousands of records (estimate). It also carries the industry's position that agencies and advertisers are jointly liable for unpaid media. Member media companies pay.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 staff (estimate)
**Output:** Credit reports, collection referrals, credit seminars
**Proprietary data:** Member-contributed payment experience on advertisers and agencies
**Clock:** None set by a third party
**Buyer:** Executive leadership of the association
