# An Always-On Avails Desk for Stations Nobody Calls

**Niche:** [[niches/media-rep-firms/small-market-station-representation/profile|Small-Market & Independent Station Representation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A small station misses national buys because nobody answered the avails request in time; an agent that answers every request inside the buyer's deadline changes that at almost no labour cost.
**Tags:** #ai-agent #large-language-models #transformers #seq2seq #automation #workflow-orchestration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get small-market and independent stations onto agency buy lists at all (avails answered inside the buyer's deadline, ratings the buyer trusts, orders confirmed electronically), and whoever makes these stations as easy to buy as a group-owned station captures the national dollars currently skipped.

## The Problem
Buyers send avails requests with a deadline. A small station's rep AE juggles many stations and answers the big ones first; a small station's own manager may see the email a day late. The proposal arrives after the buyer has allocated, or never.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The commission on a small station doesn't pay for a dedicated seller, and software vendors sell to the groups that can pay for integration.

## What to Build
An agent that receives every avails request addressed to the station or its rep. It extracts the spec, pulls inventory from the station's traffic system (or a maintained grid where there is no integration), prices within floors the station manager set in advance, and submits a proposal before the deadline. Orders and revisions are confirmed back electronically, and the manager gets a daily digest of what was proposed, won and lost.

## Target Customer
Boutique reps serving small-market and independent stations; small station groups; large reps that want to serve the long tail of their roster profitably.

## Impact If Built
Stations that buyers skipped get into the buy, and representing a small station becomes economic.
