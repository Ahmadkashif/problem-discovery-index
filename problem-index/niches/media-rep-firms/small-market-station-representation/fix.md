# The Requests the Station Never Saw

**Niche:** [[niches/media-rep-firms/small-market-station-representation/profile|Small-Market & Independent Station Representation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A small station cannot tell whether it lost national business on price or was never asked, because unanswered and unseen avails requests leave no trace on its side.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #descriptive-statistics #data-integration #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get small-market and independent stations onto agency buy lists at all (avails answered inside the buyer's deadline, ratings the buyer trusts, orders confirmed electronically), and whoever makes these stations as easy to buy as a group-owned station captures the national dollars currently skipped.

## The Problem
The station sees the orders it wins. It doesn't see the requests its rep received and didn't answer, or the buys in its market it wasn't included in. When national revenue falls, it blames the market.

## Why It's Still Broken
Requests sit in the rep's inboxes and exchange platforms, and reps don't report unanswered requests to the stations they represent.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A monthly report per station: requests received for its market, requests answered and on time, proposals won and lost, and market buys detected through occurrence monitoring that didn't include it. Built from the rep's own request log.

## Who Feels the Pain
Independent station owners; small-group national sales managers; boutique reps who want to prove they answer everything.

## Impact If Fixed
The station can tell a coverage problem from a price problem, and choose its rep on evidence.
