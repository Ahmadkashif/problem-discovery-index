# Small-Market & Independent Station Representation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get small-market and independent stations onto agency buy lists at all (avails answered inside the buyer's deadline, ratings the buyer trusts, orders confirmed electronically), and whoever makes these stations as easy to buy as a group-owned station captures the national dollars currently skipped.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$80M in commission from stations in the smaller markets and stations outside the large groups (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1% of NAICS 541840 receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — many small stations lack order-exchange integrations and answer avails by email
**Target Buyer:** Owner or general manager of an independent or small-group station; principal of a boutique rep
**Automation Potential:** High — the work is answering requests and confirming orders, which is what automation does best

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
National buyers covering 210 markets prioritise the markets and stations that carry most of the budget. A station in a small market, or an independent or low-power station outside the big groups, may bill too little nationally to get a rep AE's attention (estimate). It may answer avails late or not at all, and it may not be on electronic ordering. The buyer, under deadline, skips it. The large reps represent these stations as part of a roster and prioritise the stations that move their commission. The station's problem is not price but being in the buy at all.

## Current Tools & Gaps
The same exchange tools as large stations (WideOrbit, Hudson MX, Mediaocean) where the station is integrated, and email where it isn't. The gaps: no low-cost way for a small station to answer every avails request on time; ratings that look unreliable in small samples; and no visibility of which national requests the station never saw.
