# Channel Managers From Independent Hotels

**Niche:** [[niches/media-rep-firms/small-market-station-representation/profile|Small-Market & Independent Station Representation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Independent hotels reach every booking channel through a channel manager that keeps inventory and rates in sync; small stations need the same thing to reach every agency buying system.
**Tags:** #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get small-market and independent stations onto agency buy lists at all (avails answered inside the buyer's deadline, ratings the buyer trusts, orders confirmed electronically), and whoever makes these stations as easy to buy as a group-owned station captures the national dollars currently skipped.

## The Problem
A small station without integrations cannot receive electronic orders from the agency systems national buyers use.

## What Already Exists
Hotel channel managers such as SiteMinder and Cloudbeds publish one property's rooms and rates to many booking channels, receive reservations back and keep availability in sync.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation publishes avails by daypart and program with ratings projections to Mediaocean, Strata and Hudson MX. It receives orders and revisions back into the station's traffic system, or a hosted grid where none exists, and handles preemptions and makegoods, which hotels do not have. Rates must be per rating point, varying by buyer request.

## Target Customer
Small and independent stations and the boutique reps who serve them.

## Impact If Solved
The station becomes reachable through the systems buyers already use, without a custom integration per system.
