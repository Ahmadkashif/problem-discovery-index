# Ratings Accreditation Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Media Rating Council is the industry's self-regulatory body. It audits and accredits audience measurement services against documented standards, and its approval decides whether a methodology, such as Nielsen's Big Data + Panel for local TV, is treated as accredited currency. Members (broadcasters, agencies, advertisers) fund it, and measurement companies pay for the audits, carried out with independent audit firms.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-30 staff, with audits performed by contracted audit firms (estimate)
**Output:** Accreditation decisions, audit findings, measurement standards
**Proprietary data:** Audit findings across measurement services, held confidentially
**Clock:** Accreditation reviews timed to measurement vendors' methodology changes
**Buyer:** Executive director
