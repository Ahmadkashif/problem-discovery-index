# Broadcast Sales, Traffic & Order Exchange Software

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Sell the systems spot transactions run on: station traffic and billing (WideOrbit, Imagine Communications, RCS), order exchange between buying and selling systems (WO Order Connect, Hudson MX), and agency buying platforms (Mediaocean, Strata). Their analytics and product research teams study transaction flows to improve products. Stations, groups and agencies pay licence fees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-100 product analytics and research staff across vendors (estimate)
**Output:** Product features, benchmark reports for customers, market commentary
**Proprietary data:** Transaction flows (orders, revisions, makegoods, invoices) across many stations and agencies
**Clock:** Product release cycles and customer contract renewals
**Buyer:** Chief product officer
