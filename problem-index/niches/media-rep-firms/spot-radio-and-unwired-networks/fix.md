# Affidavits Assembled From a Hundred Inboxes

**Niche:** [[niches/media-rep-firms/spot-radio-and-unwired-networks/profile|Spot Radio & Unwired Network Sales]]
**Industry:** [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Proof that a national radio buy aired arrives as station affidavits in different formats, weeks apart, and the agency won't pay until the set is complete.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #data-integration #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to package hundreds of small radio stations into one buy an agency can place, verify and pay as a single line item, and whoever makes a 200-station radio buy as easy as one network order takes the national radio dollar.

## The Problem
Each station certifies what aired, in its traffic system's format or as a scanned form. The rep's operations staff chase missing affidavits, check each against the order and assemble the package the agency needs to pay. The payment, and the rep's commission, wait for the last station.

## Why It's Still Broken
Many small stations aren't on electronic invoicing, and the affidavit is a legal certification the station must issue, so the rep can't produce it.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Read affidavits in any format as they arrive, match each line to the order, cross-check against airplay monitoring where available, chase only the missing ones automatically, and release the package as soon as it is complete.

## Who Feels the Pain
Rep radio operations staff; agency payables waiting on complete packages; small stations paid late.

## Impact If Fixed
Weeks come off the payment cycle on network buys, with no change required at the stations.
