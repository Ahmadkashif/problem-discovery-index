# Freight Consolidation Software for Radio Clearance

**Niche:** [[niches/media-rep-firms/spot-radio-and-unwired-networks/profile|Spot Radio & Unwired Network Sales]]
**Industry:** [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Freight consolidators already sell one shipment carried by many small carriers and track each leg to proof of delivery; an unwired radio network is the same problem with spots instead of pallets.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #time-series-forecasting #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to package hundreds of small radio stations into one buy an agency can place, verify and pay as a single line item, and whoever makes a 200-station radio buy as easy as one network order takes the national radio dollar.

## The Problem
The rep tenders a schedule to many independent stations and must prove delivery of the whole.

## What Already Exists
Transportation management systems used by freight brokers and consolidators tender loads to many carriers, track status by API, email or portal, collect proof of delivery and settle carrier invoices against the customer's single bill.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation turns loads into spots with dayparts and audience values, and carriers into stations with traffic systems of uneven integration. Proof of delivery becomes affidavits and airplay monitoring, and delivery is measured in audience rather than units, so a missed spot in morning drive is worth more than one overnight. Makegoods replace claims.

## Target Customer
Radio network sales and operations teams at rep firms.

## Impact If Solved
Clearance tracking and settlement come from a mature category rather than a spreadsheet, and the network can be sold with a delivery report buyers trust.
