# One Clearance Record for a Two-Hundred-Station Buy

**Niche:** [[niches/media-rep-firms/spot-radio-and-unwired-networks/profile|Spot Radio & Unwired Network Sales]]
**Industry:** [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** An unwired radio network is sold as one product and run on two hundred separate traffic systems, so nobody knows mid-flight how much of the buy actually cleared.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #logistic-regression #time-series-forecasting #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to package hundreds of small radio stations into one buy an agency can place, verify and pay as a single line item, and whoever makes a 200-station radio buy as easy as one network order takes the national radio dollar.

## The Problem
The rep sells a national advertiser a network of many stations at a combined audience. Each station must schedule the spots in its own traffic system and air them. Some won't clear: a local sale, a sports preemption, a missed log. The rep learns which ones after the flight, from affidavits and monitoring, and negotiates makegoods station by station. The advertiser bought one product and receives a reconciliation project.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Small stations run a mix of traffic systems, many without order-exchange integrations, and the rep's network business is a thin-margin product inside a declining category.

## What to Build
A clearance record across every station in the network. It ingests confirmations, as-run logs and airplay monitoring as they arrive. A model predicts each station's probability of clearing each spot from its history, so the network can be over-scheduled or re-routed before the flight under-delivers. The combined delivery is posted against the guarantee daily.

## Target Customer
Rep firms' radio network sales units and the larger radio groups that sell their own networks.

## Impact If Built
The network behaves like the single product it is sold as. That is the contest for national radio dollars against digital audio, which offers buyers exactly that.
