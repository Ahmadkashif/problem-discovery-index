# Media Auditing & Agency Performance Benchmarking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Audit, for advertisers, what their agencies paid for media and whether it delivered, including local spot buys placed through reps. They benchmark pricing against pooled data from many clients and review agency contracts. Ebiquity is the best-known independent; consultancies and smaller specialists compete. Advertisers' procurement and marketing finance teams pay.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~100-400 analysts on media pricing and performance audit (estimate)
**Output:** Media audit reports, price benchmark comparisons, agency contract compliance reviews, pitch support
**Proprietary data:** Pooled, anonymised media pricing and delivery from many advertisers' audits
**Clock:** Annual audit cycles and agency reviews set by the client
**Buyer:** Head of media audit / chief product officer
