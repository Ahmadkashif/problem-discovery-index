# Post-Buy Posting, Makegoods & Reconciliation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to settle each flight (ratings posted against the guarantee, preempted spots made good, invoice matched to affidavit and order) before the agency's payment cycle closes, and whoever settles cleanest gets the station paid first and keeps audience-deficiency liability from piling up.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$200M a year in rep and station labour and tools for posting, makegoods and reconciliation (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of NAICS 541840 receipts, counted as the cost of this function inside rep firms (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — ratings and invoices are digital, settling them is spreadsheet work
**Target Buyer:** Rep firm head of research or sales operations; station group business office leads
**Automation Potential:** Very High — posting and matching are rule-based; makegood selection is an optimisation problem

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
National spot buys are usually guaranteed on ratings in the buyer's demo. After the flight, delivery is posted against the guarantee. Under-delivery is owed back as audience-deficiency units, preempted spots need makegoods, and the invoice must match the order and the station's affidavit. In 2019 buyers said they waited about 60 days for an invoice to resolve makegoods. Since January 2025 local ratings currency is Nielsen Big Data + Panel, with older buys posted on Panel-Only, so the posting rules depend on when the buy was sold.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Ratings from Nielsen and Comscore with posting tools, traffic-system as-run logs and electronic invoices, Mediaocean's invoice matching on the buy side, and Excel. The gaps: posting happens after the flight rather than during it, makegoods are offered from whatever is left rather than planned, and discrepancies are resolved by email.
