# Accounts-Receivable Matching for Media Invoices

**Niche:** [[niches/media-rep-firms/post-buy-and-makegoods/profile|Post-Buy Posting, Makegoods & Reconciliation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Accounts-receivable automation already matches payments to invoices and works the exceptions; media invoices need a three-way match against order and affidavit before they can be paid.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #gradient-boosting #data-integration #automation #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to settle each flight (ratings posted against the guarantee, preempted spots made good, invoice matched to affidavit and order) before the agency's payment cycle closes, and whoever settles cleanest gets the station paid first and keeps audience-deficiency liability from piling up.

## The Problem
Short payments and disputes on national spot invoices are worked by hand between the station's business office, the rep and the agency.

## What Already Exists
Accounts-receivable automation platforms such as HighRadius, which the Media Financial Management Association has partnered with for media credit management, match remittances to invoices, predict payment dates and route disputes.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs a three-way match of invoice, order (with all revisions) and affidavit or as-run log, plus a fourth leg for ratings-guaranteed buys. It must classify disputes by cause (missed spot, wrong rate, under-delivery, makegood not accepted), and handle the long-running question of whether the agency or the advertiser is liable for unpaid media.

## Target Customer
Station group business offices and rep firm finance teams.

## Impact If Solved
Disputes are classified and resolved by cause, and cash arrives faster on the national business that the rep's commission depends on.
