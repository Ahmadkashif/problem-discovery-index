# Two Ratings Systems in One Quarter's Posting

**Niche:** [[niches/media-rep-firms/post-buy-and-makegoods/profile|Post-Buy Posting, Makegoods & Reconciliation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** After Nielsen switched local currency to Big Data + Panel in January 2025, buys sold on one methodology were posted on another unless someone tracked which was which.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #hypothesis-testing #data-integration #quick-win #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to settle each flight (ratings posted against the guarantee, preempted spots made good, invoice matched to affidavit and order) before the agency's payment cycle closes, and whoever settles cleanest gets the station paid first and keeps audience-deficiency liability from piling up.

## The Problem
Nielsen advised clients to keep posting late-2024 buys on Panel-Only data while new buys used Big Data + Panel. Analysts had to know, for each buy, which methodology it was sold on. A mismatch could swing a post from delivered to under-delivered, and back.

## Why It's Still Broken
The methodology a buy was negotiated on is not a field in most orders. It lives in the negotiation email.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Record the ratings source and methodology on every proposal and order as a required field. Post automatically against the recorded source, and report the difference between methodologies wherever a buy spans the change.

## Who Feels the Pain
Research analysts at reps and stations; buyers disputing posts; AEs explaining why a delivered buy now shows a deficit.

## Impact If Fixed
Posts are computed on the basis agreed, and the next methodology change, such as Nielsen's 2026 crediting changes (estimate), does not reopen the same dispute.
