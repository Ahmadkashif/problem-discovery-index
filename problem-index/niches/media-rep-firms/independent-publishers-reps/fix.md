# The Commission Statement That Omits a Sale

**Niche:** [[niches/media-rep-firms/independent-publishers-reps/profile|Independent Publishers' Representatives]]
**Industry:** [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Each publisher's commission statement arrives in its own format, and a sale that is missing or short-paid is noticed only if the rep reconciles every line by hand.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #data-integration #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show each publisher a live, account-level pipeline for its titles (who was pitched, what is pending, what renewed and why not), and whoever makes a commission rep as visible as an in-house salesperson keeps the title when the publisher reviews its reps.

## The Problem
Publishers send monthly or quarterly statements listing collected revenue and commission. Missing lines are usually innocent: the advertiser paid late, a house-account rule applied, or a makegood was credited. But they cost the rep money, and finding them takes hours.

## Why It's Still Broken
The rep's record of what it sold is a spreadsheet; the publisher's is its billing system. Nothing matches the two automatically, and formats differ by publisher.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Read each statement as it arrives, match lines to the rep's insertion orders, and list what is missing, short or held, with the likely reason (unpaid, credited, excluded). Send the publisher one query with the evidence attached.

## Who Feels the Pain
Rep principals reconciling at night; associates whose pay depends on the statement; publisher business offices answering piecemeal queries.

## Impact If Fixed
Recovered commission and recovered evenings, plus a cleaner relationship with each publisher's business office.
