# Issue Rates Set Without Last Cycle's Record

**Niche:** [[niches/media-rep-firms/issue-and-pac-inventory-pricing/profile|Issue & PAC Inventory Pricing]]
**Industry:** [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Two years after the last election, the desk sets issue rates from memory, because what cleared at what price in which week was never kept in a usable form.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #time-series-forecasting #data-integration #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to price non-candidate issue and PAC spots as close as possible to what the market will clear in the final weeks before an election, while deciding which regular advertisers to displace, and whoever forecasts that demand best books the most political revenue per spot.

## The Problem
The orders exist in traffic systems, but nobody assembled them into a station-week record of issue rate, volume and sell-out. Political staff change between cycles, and the experienced coordinator's memory is the record.

## Why It's Still Broken
The work is seasonal and the post-election period is spent on billing and claims. Analysis is nobody's job in an odd year.

## What a Fix Looks Like
After each cycle, extract a station-week table of issue and PAC rates, volumes, sell-out and displacement from the rep's own orders, and keep it alongside the race context. It is the seed for any forecast, and useful on its own.

## Who Feels the Pain
Political desk heads setting August rates; station groups comparing results across cycles.

## Impact If Fixed
The next cycle starts from evidence, and a departing coordinator's knowledge stays behind.
