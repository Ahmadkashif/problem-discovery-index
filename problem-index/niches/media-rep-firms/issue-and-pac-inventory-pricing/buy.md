# Event Ticketing Dynamic Pricing for October Inventory

**Niche:** [[niches/media-rep-firms/issue-and-pac-inventory-pricing/profile|Issue & PAC Inventory Pricing]]
**Industry:** [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Ticketing platforms already reprice fixed seats daily as an event approaches; political inventory is fixed seats with an event date set by law.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #bayesian-inference #optimization-fundamentals #revenue-impact #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to price non-candidate issue and PAC spots as close as possible to what the market will clear in the final weeks before an election, while deciding which regular advertisers to displace, and whoever forecasts that demand best books the most political revenue per spot.

## The Problem
Issue rates are set by hand and revised irregularly as the desk senses demand rising.

## What Already Exists
Dynamic pricing systems in live-event ticketing forecast demand by days-to-event and section and recommend price changes as inventory sells.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation has to price per spot by daypart against ratings rather than per seat. It must keep candidate inventory under a separate legal regime, because candidate rates may not move above the lowest unit charge. It needs to price displacement of regular advertisers, who have their own value beyond the election. And it must handle a buyer set of a few dozen organisations per market, not thousands of fans.

## Target Customer
Political sales and pricing leads at rep firms and station groups.

## Impact If Solved
Issue rates follow demand week by week instead of jumping when the desk notices, without touching the candidate regime.
