# Communications Law & Political Broadcasting Counsel

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Communications law practices advise stations, station groups and reps on political broadcasting rules: lowest unit charge, reasonable access, equal opportunities, sponsorship identification, political file and issue-ad disclosure. They also act in rebate disputes and FCC complaints. Stations and groups pay hourly or on retainer, with demand peaking in even years.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-40 attorneys and specialists in political broadcasting across the leading communications practices (estimate)
**Output:** Guidance memos, window calendars, disclosure templates, dispute representation
**Proprietary data:** Matter files and accumulated rulings on how the rules apply to edge cases
**Clock:** The 45- and 60-day lowest-unit-charge windows before each primary and general election, fixed by 47 CFR 73.1942 to the election dates
**Buyer:** Practice group leader
