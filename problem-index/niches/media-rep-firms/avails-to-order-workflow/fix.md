# The Order That Differs From the Proposal

**Niche:** [[niches/media-rep-firms/avails-to-order-workflow/profile|Avails, Proposals & Order Exchange]]
**Industry:** [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The buyer's order rarely matches the agreed proposal line for line, and differences in rate, week or daypart surface only when the invoice is disputed.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #descriptive-statistics #data-integration #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to move an avail request, proposal, order and every revision between the agency's buying system and each station's traffic system without anyone re-keying it, and whoever closes the re-keying gap for the most agency–station pairs becomes the default pipe for spot.

## The Problem
After negotiation, the buyer issues an order from the buying system. A rate typed wrong, a week shifted or a daypart mislabelled slips into the station's traffic system unnoticed. The discrepancy shows up a month later as a short payment.

## Why It's Still Broken
The proposal and the order live in different systems, and the comparison is done by eye, when it is done at all.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Compare every incoming order and revision automatically with the last agreed proposal and flag line-level differences before the order is accepted into traffic. Route each flag to the AE with the evidence attached.

## Who Feels the Pain
Sales assistants who discover the error at invoice; station business offices; agency payables.

## Impact If Fixed
Discrepancies are caught at order time, when they cost one email, instead of at payment time, when they cost a dispute.
