# Avails, Proposals & Order Exchange

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to move an avail request, proposal, order and every revision between the agency's buying system and each station's traffic system without anyone re-keying it, and whoever closes the re-keying gap for the most agency–station pairs becomes the default pipe for spot.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M a year in rep and station labour and software spent moving spot transactions between systems (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of NAICS 541840 receipts, counted as the cost of this function inside rep firms (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — electronic exchange exists for some agency–station pairs; WideOrbit put ~95% of local spot TV transactions on manual workflows in 2020
**Target Buyer:** Rep firm COO or head of sales operations; station group VP of sales operations
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the work is structured transcription between known schemas

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A spot transaction passes through four documents (avails request, proposal, order, revision) and at least two systems owned by different companies: the agency's buying platform and the station's traffic system. The rep sits in the middle. Where both ends are integrated, the transaction flows. WideOrbit's Order Connect, Mediaocean's avails workflow and Hudson MX each cover part of the map. Where they aren't, a sales assistant types. The contest is coverage of agency–station pairs, and especially of revisions, which are the most frequent and least integrated step.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Mediaocean and Strata on the buy side; WideOrbit, Imagine and RCS traffic on the sell side; Order Connect and Hudson MX in between; TVB electronic transaction standards. The gaps: free-form requests and revisions by email, uneven integration among smaller stations and agencies, and makegoods still negotiated outside the pipe.
