# Political Media Buying Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Plan and place broadcast, cable and streaming buys for campaigns, party committees, PACs and issue groups. They are the reps' political counterparties, placing national political orders through rep firms' political desks. Firms work for one party's side, are paid a commission or fee on the buy, and compete on targeting, speed and rates.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically under 10 research and targeting analysts per firm; most staff are buyers placing and negotiating orders (estimate)
**Output:** Media plans, market and station targeting, post-buy reporting for clients
**Proprietary data:** Clients' buying histories and campaign strategy, held under strict confidentiality
**Clock:** Election day and the lowest-unit-charge windows before it
**Buyer:** Founder / managing partner
