# Agency Local Broadcast Buying Hubs

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Holding companies and large independent agencies run centralised local broadcast buying units. They send avails requests to reps, negotiate CPPs, place orders and post buys for many advertiser clients. Their analytics staff benchmark station pricing and audit delivery. Advertisers pay the agency a fee or commission for buying, not for the analysis. Agency-side media buying belongs to [[industries/programmatic-ad-platforms|Programmatic Ad Platforms]] in this vault; this pocket is logged here as the reps' counterparty.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-25 pricing, post-buy and investment analysts at the largest holding-company hubs, counting analysts only and not the buyers who negotiate (estimate); smaller agencies fall below the gate
**Output:** Post-buy analyses, station pricing benchmarks and negotiation guidance for internal use
**Proprietary data:** Years of proposals, orders and posts across stations and markets, under client contracts
**Clock:** Quarterly buying cycles and client flight dates
**Buyer:** Head of local investment analytics
