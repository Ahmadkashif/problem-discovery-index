# Hotel Revenue Management Pointed at Spot Inventory

**Niche:** [[niches/media-rep-firms/national-spot-tv-representation/profile|National Spot TV Representation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Hotels and airlines have priced perishable inventory against demand forecasts for decades; spot TV is perishable inventory priced by a seller's feel for the market.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #gradient-boosting #bayesian-inference #optimization-fundamentals #revenue-impact #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to lift each represented station's power ratio, its share of the national dollars in its market against its share of the audience, and whoever proves that lift, market by market, keeps the station group when the rep contract comes up.

## The Problem
An unsold spot is gone when the hour airs. Stations set rate grids, and reps price each avails request against them, adjusting by judgement as the quarter fills. Sell-out pacing reports show how full each week is, but they do not forecast what each buyer will pay or recommend a rate for each request.

## What Already Exists
Revenue management systems from IDeaS and Duetto in hotels, and PROS in airlines, forecast demand by date and segment and recommend prices that maximise revenue across perishable capacity. Broadcast traffic systems include basic pricing and pacing tools.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs several things. Prices have to be set per rating point rather than per room, with the rating itself uncertain. Demand must be forecast per buyer and agency office, since a few buyers make up most national demand in a market. The rep's objective is share and power ratio as well as revenue, because losing share costs the contract. The tool must handle competition among stations in the same market whose prices respond to each other. And it must run across firewalled divisions without sharing rates.

## Target Customer
Pricing and inventory leaders at rep firms and at station groups' national sales units.

## Impact If Solved
Rates set from forecast demand rather than feel recover margin in tight weeks and win share in soft ones, using a discipline other perishable-inventory industries have already proved.
