# National Spot TV Representation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to lift each represented station's power ratio, its share of the national dollars in its market against its share of the audience, and whoever proves that lift, market by market, keeps the station group when the rep contract comes up.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$450M a year in rep commission on ~$6B of national spot TV sold through reps (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of NAICS 541840 receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — order exchange exists, but pricing and share analysis run in spreadsheets and email
**Target Buyer:** Rep firm president or EVP of sales; station group EVP of national sales
**Automation Potential:** High for proposal and order mechanics, medium for pricing support, low for the negotiation itself

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is the core of the industry by money. Two firms dominate TV representation. Katz represents over 400 TV stations outside iHeartMedia, and CoxReps describes 350+ stations covering over 90% of US TV households through its TeleRep, HRP, MMT and Gamut divisions. Each division sells a roster of stations to national and regional agency buyers. The divisions exist partly so that competing stations in one market can be represented by the same firm behind firewalls. The rep is paid on what airs. It is evaluated on power ratio at renewal, and threatened by station groups that take national sales in-house.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Agency-side Mediaocean and Strata, station-side WideOrbit and Imagine traffic, Hudson MX and WO Order Connect for exchange, Nielsen ratings, Miller Kaplan revenue share, Guideline's SQADCosts for CPP benchmarks, and Salesforce. The gaps: no record joins each pricing decision to its order and share outcome; power ratio is reported but not explained; and senior AEs' buyer reads are never captured.
