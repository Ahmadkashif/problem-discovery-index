# Displaced Advertisers Learn by Invoice

**Niche:** [[niches/media-rep-firms/political-ad-sales/profile|Political Advertising Sales]]
**Industry:** [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** When political orders take a station's October inventory, regular national advertisers are preempted, and their agencies often find out when the spot is missing from the invoice.
**Tags:** #optimization-fundamentals #time-series-forecasting #workflow-orchestration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every competitor is fighting to capture national political dollars for its stations in the final weeks before an election. But candidate orders are a compliance contest, won by quoting each station's lowest unit charge correctly and moving disclosure paperwork in hours. Issue and PAC orders are a pricing contest over scarce inventory, won by forecasting demand. The two have different inputs, clocks and winners. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Political spots take priority, and preemptible regular business is bumped. Makegood offers for the bumped advertisers are assembled late, in a sold-out month, and some agencies learn of the preemption only from the invoice. The relationship damage outlasts the election.

## Why It's Still Broken
Displacement is decided station by station in traffic, while the rep's assistants manage the regular advertisers' orders. Nobody forecasts which regular orders are at risk before political demand peaks.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A displacement forecast per station and week, from political bookings and demand, flags which regular orders are likely to be preempted. Makegood offers are proposed in advance, and buyers are warned before the spot is missed.

## Who Feels the Pain
Rep assistants and AEs handling regular national accounts in October; agency buyers whose clients' schedules collapse; stations that lose the regular advertiser in November.

## Impact If Fixed
Political revenue is taken without burning the regular advertisers the rep needs for the other 22 months of the cycle.
