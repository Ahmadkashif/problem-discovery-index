**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every competitor is fighting to capture national political dollars for its stations in the final weeks before an election. But candidate orders are a compliance contest, won by quoting each station's lowest unit charge correctly and moving disclosure paperwork in hours. Issue and PAC orders are a pricing contest over scarce inventory, won by forecasting demand. The two have different inputs, clocks and winners. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Political orders come with required disclosure paperwork, sponsor identification and, for issue ads, information about the sponsoring organisation. The desk collects, checks and forwards these for every order at every station, at volume, against election-day deadlines.

## What Already Exists
Tax-practice platforms such as client portals with document checklists, e-signature and automated reminders handle seasonal, rule-bound document intake, with validation that flags missing fields before work starts.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs political form templates, with validation of required fields for candidate and issue orders. It needs a candidate-versus-issue classification that routes the order to the right pricing regime, and links from each packet to the station's political-file upload with confirmation. It must also handle many stations per order and many revisions per order.

## Target Customer
Political sales coordinators at rep firms and station groups.

## Impact If Solved
Complete paperwork arrives before the order is transmitted rather than after the spot airs, shrinking the political-file gaps and late corrections that stations' counsel dread.
