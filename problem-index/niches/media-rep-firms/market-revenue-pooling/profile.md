# Market Revenue Pooling & Share Reporting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Collect booked advertising revenue from competing radio and TV stations, cable interconnects and outdoor companies in each market. They pool it under confidentiality and return each participant its share of the market, by advertiser, agency, category and salesperson. Miller Kaplan Arase is the dominant US provider: its Market Revenue Analysis and Market X-Ray reports are relied on by more than 2,900 media outlets. Stations and groups pay; reps use the reports to compute power ratio and argue share at renewal.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~15-50 staff in the media reporting practice (estimate)
**Output:** Monthly market revenue share reports; account-level X-Ray reports; category power ratios and proposal guidance
**Proprietary data:** Years of station-reported booked revenue by account, agency and category across most US radio and many TV markets. No other source has actual revenue at that grain, and it is used mainly for share tables
**Clock:** Monthly closes and quarterly reporting set by the participants' own accounting calendars
**Buyer:** Partner leading the media practice
