# Ad Spend & Occurrence Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Monitor which advertisers run which ads on which stations, publications and sites, and estimate what they spent. Vivvix, MediaRadar, Media Monitors and Guideline's Standard Media Index are examples. Reps and stations use them to prospect, finding the advertisers spending in a market but not on their stations, and to estimate competitors' share. Agencies and brands use them for competitive tracking. Commercial monitoring is distinct from political tracking, which is profiled under local news media.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~100-500 occurrence coders, analysts and data staff across providers (estimate)
**Output:** Occurrence and spend estimates by advertiser, market, outlet and category; prospect lists; competitive reports
**Proprietary data:** Monitoring archives of aired and published ads, coded by advertiser and brand, built over years
**Clock:** Monthly and quarterly releases; no third-party deadline governs the work
**Buyer:** Chief data officer / head of research
