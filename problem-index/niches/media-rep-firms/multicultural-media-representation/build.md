# An Audience Case a Planner Can Defend

**Niche:** [[niches/media-rep-firms/multicultural-media-representation/profile|Multicultural & In-Language Media Representation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** In-language media reps argue their audiences are under-counted with decks of mixed evidence, and agency planners discount them because nothing combines that evidence into one estimate with an error bar.
**Tags:** #bayesian-inference #confidence-intervals #feature-engineering #data-integration #revenue-impact #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to prove in-language audiences that standard panels under-count, with evidence an agency's multicultural planner can defend to the advertiser, and whoever makes that case credibly wins the multicultural line item.

## The Problem
A Spanish-language radio station's panel rating in a mid-size market rests on a handful of in-tab households (estimate), and moves wildly month to month. The rep brings streaming logs, event attendance, social reach and census language data. The planner, who must justify the buy to a brand manager, cannot combine them and falls back on the panel number.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Measurement companies protect their currency, and specialist reps are small. Agencies' multicultural units have budget but not the mandate to build measurement.

## What to Build
A Bayesian audience estimate per station and demo. It uses the panel rating as one noisy observation and the station's first-party streaming and digital audiences, census language and household data for the market, and comparable stations' histories as others. It reports an estimate with an interval and shows how much each source contributed. A planner-facing export puts the estimate beside the currency number with the reasoning.

## Target Customer
Specialist multicultural rep firms and minority-owned station groups' national sales leaders.

## Impact If Built
The station's case becomes a number a planner can defend, which is what moves a line item from "nice to have" to bought.
