# A Zero Rating Where the Sample Was Two Homes

**Niche:** [[niches/media-rep-firms/multicultural-media-representation/profile|Multicultural & In-Language Media Representation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A month when a station's few panel homes don't tune in shows as a near-zero rating, and buys are posted and makegoods owed against it.
**Tags:** #bayesian-inference #descriptive-statistics #time-series-forecasting #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to prove in-language audiences that standard panels under-count, with evidence an agency's multicultural planner can defend to the advertiser, and whoever makes that case credibly wins the multicultural line item.

## The Problem
Small in-language samples produce volatile ratings. A low month means the station under-delivers its guarantee on paper, owes audience-deficiency units, and loses the next buy, even though nothing about its real audience changed.

## Why It's Still Broken
Buys are posted against the currency as published, and nobody routinely shows the sampling error alongside it.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Report every in-language rating with its sample size and an interval, flag months where movement is within sampling noise, and propose posting against a smoothed estimate where the buyer agrees in advance.

## Who Feels the Pain
Specialist rep AEs explaining collapses that aren't real; station owners giving away makegoods; planners who suspect the number but must use it.

## Impact If Fixed
Fewer unjustified makegoods and a fairer conversation about what the station delivers.
