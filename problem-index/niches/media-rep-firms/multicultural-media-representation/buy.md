## The Problem
Panel samples for in-language audiences in a market are too small for stable ratings.

## What Already Exists
Small-area estimation methods used by census bureaus and public-health agencies, such as Fay-Herriot style models and multilevel regression with post-stratification, combine thin direct survey estimates with auxiliary data to stabilise estimates for small groups. They are available in standard statistical packages.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs auxiliary data specific to media: streaming logs, digital audiences and comparable stations. Estimates have to be produced by daypart and demo, the units buys are priced in. Outputs must be framed for planners rather than statisticians, and the method must be documented well enough to survive an agency research department's scrutiny.

## Target Customer
Research leads at specialist reps and multicultural media groups.

## Impact If Solved
Borrowing a respected public-statistics method lends the estimate credibility that a rep's own deck cannot.
