# Media Cost Benchmark Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Publish what spot TV, radio and local digital actually cost: cost per point and per thousand by market, daypart and demo, with forecasts several quarters ahead. Guideline's SQADCosts Local, which Guideline calls "the standard for local pricing transparency in linear", is the reference product. It is built from actual buy costs contributed by agencies and buyers (estimate). Agencies plan and negotiate with it; reps and stations use it to see where their rates sit against the market.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~30-120 analysts and data staff on cost products (estimate)
**Output:** CPP and CPM benchmarks and forecasts by DMA, daypart and demo; local digital CPM benchmarks
**Proprietary data:** Contributed actual buy costs across all DMAs over many years, which no single agency or seller holds
**Clock:** Quarterly planning and negotiation cycles, upfront and scatter
**Buyer:** Head of pricing products / chief product officer
