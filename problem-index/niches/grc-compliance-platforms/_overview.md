# Niche Analysis — GRC & Compliance Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/grc-compliance-platforms|GRC & Compliance Platforms]]

## Niche Selection

These platforms hold continuous control state across tens of thousands of organisations — the only dataset in existence that could relate control implementation to security outcomes — and use it to produce audit artefacts. The eight niches below start from that unasked question, then follow the product that generates the data, the reconciliation work the category has not automated, the two people who carry the burden, and the mechanical layers underneath.

## Why These Niches

Evidence collection is the product and the largest niche; efficacy measurement is the question the product's entire market value rests on and nobody has asked. Vendor risk is the same apparatus pointed outward at suppliers, and is the most mechanical large niche in the category. Framework mapping and security questionnaires are the two places where the promised consolidation did not happen — four frameworks reconciled by hand, and a three-hundred-question questionnaire that the certificate was supposed to replace and did not. The two underserved people are the engineer asked repeatedly for evidence they consider a distraction, and the compliance manager running four programmes alone. Policy management is mechanical.

## Filter Notes

Seven of the eight are terminal — each names one contest every serious competitor is fighting over, and decomposing further would produce features rather than markets.

**Control efficacy measurement** is not, and the split is the oldest one in empirical work: the independent variable and the dependent variable are two different data problems with two different owners. Control state normalisation asks what "implemented" actually means — a control passing at one organisation and passing at another may describe configurations with nothing in common, because frameworks specify intent and platforms verify whatever their integrations can see. Making control state comparable across tens of thousands of organisations is entirely internal to the platform's own corpus, produces value immediately in the form of a defensible maturity measure, and is testable against expert review this quarter. Outcome linkage asks what happened afterwards — incidents, breaches, claims, regulatory findings — and every one of those lives outside the platform, with customers who will not volunteer them, insurers who hold them commercially, and public disclosure that captures only the largest events. It is confounded by everything, returns nothing for years, and produces an answer that would tell a certification business which of the controls it certifies do not matter. A platform can normalise its corpus and never attempt the linkage, and every one of them would, because the first improves the product it already sells.

Two adjacent candidates were rejected as belonging elsewhere: **the audit engagement itself** is the subject of [[industries/soc2-audit-firms|SOC 2 Audit Firms]], and **privacy-specific obligations and data subject rights** belong to [[industries/privacy-tech-vendors|Privacy Tech Vendors]].
