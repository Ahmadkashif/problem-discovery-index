# Medical Claim Scrubbers Adapted to Repossession Fee Schedules

**Niche:** [[niches/repossession-services/updates-invoicing-closeout/profile|Client Updates, Invoicing & Close-Out]]
**Industry:** [[industries/repossession-services|Repossession Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Medical billing solved "check the claim against the payer's rules before submitting"; repossession invoices fail for the same reasons against client fee schedules and nobody checks them first.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #decision-trees #evaluation-metrics #compliance #revenue-impact #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to report every open file inside each client's update rule and get every fee paid at first submission, and whoever closes files with complete documentation and the fewest denials takes the account.

## The Problem
A close-out invoice can carry a recovery fee plus storage, keys, transport, personal property handling and skip fees. Each client allows a different list, caps some, and requires specific proof for each. Invoices are denied or short-paid, and resubmitting costs office time and delays cash.

## What Already Exists
Claim scrubbing in medical revenue cycle software, which checks every claim against payer-specific edits before submission and learns from denials, as analysed under [[industries/medical-billing|Medical Billing]].

## The Customization Gap
The repossession version needs: each client's fee schedule and caps encoded; proof requirements per fee (photo types, police notice, condition report, mileage); file-level checks that the update history satisfies the client's rules before the invoice goes; denial reasons captured and mapped to the rule that failed; and output in each portal's invoice format.

## Target Customer
Agency office managers and owners with several national clients.

## Impact If Solved
Higher first-pass payment, fewer short-paid invoices, and a denial log the agency can take into fee negotiations.
