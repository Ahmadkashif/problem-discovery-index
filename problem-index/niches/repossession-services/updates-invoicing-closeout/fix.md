# The Fee Denied for a Missing Photo

**Niche:** [[niches/repossession-services/updates-invoicing-closeout/profile|Client Updates, Invoicing & Close-Out]]
**Industry:** [[industries/repossession-services|Repossession Agencies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A recovery is completed cleanly and the fee is still denied because one required photo — the odometer, the VIN plate, the property bag — never made it from the agent's phone to the invoice.
**Tags:** #cnns #object-detection #workflow-orchestration #compliance #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to report every open file inside each client's update rule and get every fee paid at first submission, and whoever closes files with complete documentation and the fewest denials takes the account.

## The Problem
Clients require specific photos with specific invoices: VIN, odometer, all four sides, keys, the property inventory. Agents take dozens of photos on scene and on the lot, unlabelled, and the office picks through them at invoice time. A missing or unreadable shot means a denial and a resubmission, sometimes after the car has left the lot and the photo cannot be retaken.

## Why It's Still Broken
Photos are captured without knowing which client rule they serve, and nothing checks completeness before the car leaves.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A photo checklist per client generated on the file at recovery, with on-device classification that recognises which required shot each photo satisfies and whether the VIN and odometer are legible, blocking release from the lot until the set is complete.

## Who Feels the Pain
Office staff resubmitting invoices; owners waiting on cash; agents asked to return to a car that is already at auction.

## Impact If Fixed
Photo-related denials largely disappear, at the cost of a few seconds per shot on the lot.
