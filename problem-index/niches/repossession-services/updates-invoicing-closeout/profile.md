# Client Updates, Invoicing & Close-Out

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/repossession-services|Repossession Agencies]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to report every open file inside each client's update rule and get every fee paid at first submission, and whoever closes files with complete documentation and the fewest denials takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$80–150M a year in agency office labour on status updates, invoicing, denials and close-out (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~6–10% of agency cost base (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — every step happens in a portal, but staff move the information between portals by hand
**Target Buyer:** Agency office manager or owner; forwarders that score update compliance
**Automation Potential:** High — rule-bound reporting and documentation drawn from records that already exist

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every open file carries reporting duties: acknowledge within the client's window, update on its cadence (a forwarder may want an update on every skip every few days), narrate attempts in its vocabulary, and close out with an invoice whose every fee carries proof. Agencies work several sources at once — RDN, forwarder systems, direct clients — so the same attempt is re-keyed into each. Forwarders' scorecards measure update timeliness alongside recoveries, and fee denials for missing photos or unlisted fee codes are a direct loss. Competitors are RDN's own update and invoicing tools, forwarder portals, towing back-office software, offshore back-office services and robotic process automation vendors.

## Current Tools & Gaps
RDN updates and invoicing; forwarder portals; Towbook-style invoicing; spreadsheets of client rules. Gaps: no layer that holds each client's update and fee rules per file; updates typed from memory rather than drafted from the field record; denial reasons not captured, so the same rejection recurs.
