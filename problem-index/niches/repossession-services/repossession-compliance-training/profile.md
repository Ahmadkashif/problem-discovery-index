# Repossession Compliance Training, Certification & Lot Inspection

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/repossession-services|Repossession Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
RISC's Certified Asset Recovery Specialist (CARS) programme, updated annually with the consumer finance law firm Hudson Cook and approved in a state edition by the Illinois Commerce Commission, is the de facto training standard lenders and forwarders ask agencies to hold; RISC also inspects repossession lots against compliance standards. Agencies pay per agent for courses and per location for inspections, often because a client requires it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Production trainers, course administrators and lot inspectors; the content is written with outside counsel, and no dedicated analytical team of 10+ (estimate)
**Output:** Certifications, continuing education, lot inspection reports
**Proprietary data:** Certification and inspection records
**Clock:** Annual recertification and client audit cycles
**Buyer:** Founder or president
