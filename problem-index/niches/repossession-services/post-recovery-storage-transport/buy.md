# Impound Yard Software Adapted to Lender-Owned Units

**Niche:** [[niches/repossession-services/post-recovery-storage-transport/profile|Post-Recovery Storage, Transport & Release]]
**Industry:** [[industries/repossession-services|Repossession Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Towing companies already run impound yards with software that tracks every vehicle, fee and release; a repossession lot holds cars for a lender under different rules and needs the same tool rewired.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a recovered car into a redeemed or auction-delivered unit with the fewest days on the lot and no damage or storage-fee dispute, and whoever clears units fastest with a documented chain of custody takes the account.

## The Problem
A repossession lot is a custody operation: vehicles in, photographs, keys tagged, property stored, vehicles out to a borrower, a carrier or an auction, with fees accruing daily. Many agencies track it on paper or in a spreadsheet beside the field app.

## What Already Exists
Towing management software such as TOPS and Towbook tracks impounded vehicles, storage fees, lien notices and releases for police and private-property tows, analysed under [[niches/towing-companies/lien-title-processing/profile|Lien, Title & Auction Processing for Impound]].

## The Customization Gap
Impound software assumes the yard operator holds a lien and sells the car; a repossession lot holds the lender's car on its instructions. The adaptation needs: the client, not the yard, as owner of each unit; release rules from the lender (redemption payoff confirmed, identity checked, property returned separately); fee schedules per client rather than per municipality; transport orders from RDN and forwarder portals; condition reconciliation at check-out against check-in; and personal property tracked as a separate custody chain.

## Target Customer
Agencies with secure lots, especially those that also run towing and already license yard software.

## Impact If Solved
One custody record per unit from hook to hand-off, and fewer releases and storage invoices disputed for want of documentation.
