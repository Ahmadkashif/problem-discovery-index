# Commercial Licence-Plate Location Data Networks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/repossession-services|Repossession Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Companies that sell plate-reader cameras to repossession agencies and others, pool every scan the cameras capture into a time- and location-stamped database, and sell hits and location data back to lenders, insurers and recovery firms. Digital Recognition Network, part of Motorola Solutions since its $445M purchase of DRN's parent in 2019, is the dominant network and claims 4.6M+ vehicles recovered and 1,000+ customers across lending, insurance and recovery; MVTRAC and smaller subscription services sit beneath it. Lenders pay for hits and location intelligence on their portfolios; agencies pay for cameras and access.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–80 data, analytics and data-product staff per network (estimate); scan capture is done by affiliates' cameras and matched automatically
**Output:** Hot-list hits, location histories and portfolio location reports for lenders and insurers
**Proprietary data:** Billions of plate scans with time and place, captured over more than a decade by affiliate cameras under the network's agreements — a corpus no competitor can rebuild
**Clock:** Hits are perishable because cars move, but no third-party deadline governs the work; data is delivered on demand and the urgency is the lender's own
**Buyer:** General manager or VP of data products at the network

**Kill switches:** none live on the evidence available — the judgement rests on the network owning the scan corpus under its affiliate agreements, which DRN's model implies (it sells the pooled data to lenders, insurers and others) but which this build did not check in the agreements themselves; if affiliates or lenders retain rights in the scans, a "data isn't theirs" kill switch would apply. State limits on private plate readers and federal rules on plate-to-owner lookups govern how it is sold, routine operating practice here as permissible-purpose rules are for [[niches/collections-agencies/identity-skip-trace-data-providers/profile|identity and skip-trace data]]; the lenders' hot lists are client data, but the corpus is not
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. The richest proprietary dataset in the industry, sold largely as hardware and hits by a small team to a market of two or three networks; Q2, Q4 and Q5 hold it down because licensing an owned dataset needs few analysts, no third-party deadline governs delivery, and the market is a handful of firms.
