# Fleet Routing Adapted to Coverage Instead of Stops

**Niche:** [[niches/repossession-services/lpr-camera-operations/profile|Licence-Plate Camera Operations & Hit Conversion]]
**Industry:** [[industries/repossession-services|Repossession Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Fleet telematics and routing software optimises vehicles between known stops; a camera car's job is to sweep areas where unknown targets might be, which is a different problem on the same platform.
**Tags:** #optimization-fundamentals #time-series-forecasting #markov-decision-processes #data-integration #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn camera hours into confirmed hits on the hot list before the car moves, and whoever converts the most scans into verified, recoverable hits per driving hour takes the account.

## The Problem
Agencies already track their trucks and camera cars with telematics, and some use routing tools for transports. Neither helps decide which lots to sweep tonight.

## What Already Exists
Fleet telematics and routing platforms such as Samsara and Verizon Connect: GPS tracking, driver behaviour, route optimisation between addresses, and coverage tools used by street-sweeping and patrol operators.

## The Customization Gap
A camera route needs: areas weighted by forecast hot-list density by hour rather than fixed stops; credit for scanning each lot once per window, not for arriving at it; coordination so two cars do not sweep the same complex; live insertion of a confirmed hit as a dispatch job for the nearest truck; and driver-facing display of expected yield, so the driver can see why the route goes where it goes.

## Target Customer
Agencies with camera fleets already on a telematics platform.

## Impact If Solved
Camera cars sweep the right areas at the right times without new hardware, and overlap between cars disappears.
