# Agency Management Software & Low-Cost Plate-Reader Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/repossession-services|Repossession Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Smaller vendors selling to agencies outside the RDN and DRN families: agency management systems such as iRepo, which integrated with DRN, and subscription plate-reader services such as RecoveryNet from PRA Location Services, which lets agents check up to 500 plates for $299 a month, and phone-based camera offerings marketed against $15,000 camera kits. Agencies pay monthly subscriptions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analytical staff per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Software subscriptions
**Proprietary data:** Customers' operational records
**Clock:** None set by a third party
**Buyer:** Founder or product lead
