# Recovery Assignment & Case Management Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/repossession-services|Repossession Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The software layer that carries repossession assignments from lenders and forwarders to agencies and status back. The Recovery Database Network, bought by OPENLANE in 2010 and paired with the Clearplan field app and Cleardata property module, connects 600+ lenders with 2,500+ service providers and is described in OPENLANE's 10-K as "a proprietary digital platform for repossession management"; trade sources estimate it carries ~90% of assignments. Smaller portals, such as Consolidated Asset Recovery Systems' IBEAM, serve particular forwarders. Lenders and agencies pay subscription and per-file fees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 product analytics and data staff (estimate)
**Output:** The assignment platform itself; occasional volume statistics reused by trade press
**Proprietary data:** The most complete record of US repossession assignments, statuses and outcomes across lenders and agencies
**Clock:** None set by a third party
**Buyer:** General manager of the recovery business unit
