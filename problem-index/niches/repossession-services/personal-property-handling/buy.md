# Police Property-and-Evidence Systems Adapted to Repossession Lots

**Niche:** [[niches/repossession-services/personal-property-handling/profile|Personal Property Inventory & Return]]
**Industry:** [[industries/repossession-services|Repossession Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Police property rooms already barcode, store, track and release other people's belongings under retention rules; repossession lots do the same job with paper bags and a marker.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to inventory and return a borrower's belongings inside each state's clock with a record that ends the complaint, and whoever produces the complete, timestamped property file fastest takes the account.

## The Problem
Agencies store belongings for weeks or months, return them by appointment and dispose of what is never claimed. Bags get mislabelled, items move between shelves, and nobody can show who touched what.

## What Already Exists
Property-and-evidence management systems used by police departments: barcode intake, storage location, chain of custody, retention schedules, release with identity check and signature, and disposal records.

## The Customization Gap
Repossession needs: intake linked to the recovered vehicle and the lender's file; retention periods by state (California's guidance calls for at least 60 days) rather than by case disposition; notice-to-debtor generation and proof of mailing; release appointments booked by the borrower online; client-specific fee and release rules, including no fee as a condition of return where the CFPB view applies; handling rules for firearms and medication; and disposal documented when the period expires.

## Target Customer
Agencies with secure lots and steady volume; forwarders that set property standards for their panels.

## Impact If Solved
A chain of custody for every item, retention handled by the system, and fewer belongings lost between the car and the borrower.
