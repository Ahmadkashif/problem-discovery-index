# Interior Photos to a Defensible Inventory

**Niche:** [[niches/repossession-services/personal-property-handling/profile|Personal Property Inventory & Return]]
**Industry:** [[industries/repossession-services|Repossession Agencies]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Clerks photograph a car's interior and then write the inventory by hand, so the record a complaint turns on is the least careful document in the file.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #object-detection #cnns #compliance #automation #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to inventory and return a borrower's belongings inside each state's clock with a record that ends the complaint, and whoever produces the complete, timestamped property file fastest takes the account.

## The Problem
A borrower says a laptop and $400 in cash were in the console. The agency's inventory lists "misc. clothing, papers, child seat". The photos show a bag that might have held a laptop. The lender refunds the borrower to end the complaint and the forwarder marks the agency. The inventory, written at the end of a shift from memory, lost the argument.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Property handling earns little, agencies are small, and vision models good enough to list items in cluttered, badly lit car interiors arrived only recently.

## What to Build
A guided interior capture on the clerk's phone — seats, footwells, console, glovebox, trunk, each photographed in order — and a multimodal model that drafts the itemised inventory from the photos, flags regulated items (firearms, medication, cash, identity documents) for special handling, and populates the state's notice to the debtor with the deadline diarised. The clerk confirms or corrects; the photos, the confirmed list and the notice are sealed together with timestamps. The release step captures the borrower's signature against the same list.

## Target Customer
Agencies recovering dozens of cars a month for forwarders that score complaints; forwarders that want consistent property files across their panels.

## Impact If Built
Inventories that match the photos, notices that go out inside the state's clock, and property complaints that end when the agency sends the file.
