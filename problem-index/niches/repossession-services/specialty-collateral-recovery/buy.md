# Heavy-Haul Dispatch and Permitting Adapted to Recovery Jobs

**Niche:** [[niches/repossession-services/specialty-collateral-recovery/profile|Equipment, Truck, Marine & Powersports Recovery]]
**Industry:** [[industries/repossession-services|Repossession Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Heavy-haul carriers already plan rigs, routes and oversize permits for machinery moves; recovery firms need the same planning for a job that may have to happen on two hours' notice with no shipper on site.
**Tags:** #optimization-fundamentals #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to locate collateral that carries no plate a camera can read or moves for a living — equipment on job sites, tractors on the road, boats at marinas — and move it intact with the right rig and permits, and whoever recovers high-value units without damage or a wasted long-distance trip takes the account.

## The Problem
Once a machine is located, the recovery is a logistics job: the right trailer and tractor, loading equipment, oversize permits where dimensions require them, a route, and a window when the site is accessible. Specialist firms plan it by phone and experience, often for a unit whose exact model and dimensions the lender's file gets wrong.

## What Already Exists
Heavy-haul and specialised freight software for carriers: rig selection, route surveys, state oversize and overweight permit ordering, load securement checklists.

## The Customization Gap
Recovery needs: rig selection from the collateral record and photos, correcting the lender's description; rapid permit ordering for unplanned moves; site-access constraints (gates, hours, the debtor's presence, breach-of-peace risk on private job sites); a pre-move condition record for high-value machinery; and delivery to the lender's chosen auction or storage yard with its own receiving rules.

## Target Customer
Specialist equipment and truck recovery firms, and general agencies taking occasional heavy assignments.

## Impact If Solved
Fewer failed moves for want of the right rig or permit, and documented condition on units worth tens or hundreds of thousands of dollars.
