# Telematics, Registrations and Job-Site Records Fused into One Locate Plan

**Niche:** [[niches/repossession-services/specialty-collateral-recovery/profile|Equipment, Truck, Marine & Powersports Recovery]]
**Industry:** [[industries/repossession-services|Repossession Agencies]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Locating a defaulted excavator, tractor-trailer or boat means stitching together a telematics ping, a permit filing, a marina call and a dealer's service record, and specialist firms do it by phone every time.
**Tags:** #tacit-knowledge-ml #gradient-boosting #k-nearest-neighbors #large-language-models #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to locate collateral that carries no plate a camera can read or moves for a living — equipment on job sites, tractors on the road, boats at marinas — and move it intact with the right rig and permits, and whoever recovers high-value units without damage or a wasted long-distance trip takes the account.

## The Problem
An equipment lender assigns a skid steer and two excavators financed to a contractor who stopped paying. The contractor's yard is empty. The specialist recovery firm's investigator knows where to look — building permits pulled in the contractor's name, the dealer that services the machines, rental yards, a last telematics ping three weeks ago, the subcontracting relationships on local jobs — and that knowledge is personal, slow and expensive.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Volumes are small per firm, data sources are fragmented by collateral type, and telematics access depends on the lender's rights and each manufacturer's portal.

## What to Build
A locate workbench per specialty file: telematics pings where the lender has access; public permit, contract-award and registration records matched to the borrower; dealer and rental relationships; and the firm's own history of where similar borrowers' units were found. A model ranks candidate sites by recency and likelihood and drafts the investigator's call list; outcomes are logged to sharpen the ranking.

## Target Customer
Specialist equipment, truck and marine recovery firms, and recovery managers at equipment and commercial truck lenders.

## Impact If Built
Fewer long-distance trips to empty sites and faster recoveries on units that depreciate and get damaged while they are missing.
