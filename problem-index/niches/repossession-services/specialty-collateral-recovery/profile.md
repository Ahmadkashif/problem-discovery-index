# Equipment, Truck, Marine & Powersports Recovery

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/repossession-services|Repossession Agencies]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to locate collateral that carries no plate a camera can read or moves for a living — equipment on job sites, tractors on the road, boats at marinas — and move it intact with the right rig and permits, and whoever recovers high-value units without damage or a wasted long-distance trip takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$150–300M a year in recovery, transport and storage fees on equipment, commercial trucks, trailers, boats, RVs and powersports (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10–15% of agency revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — locating runs on telematics portals where they exist, phone calls to job sites and marinas, and investigator legwork
**Target Buyer:** Owner of a specialist recovery firm; recovery managers at equipment, truck and marine lenders
**Automation Potential:** Low-Medium — locating and planning can be assisted; the lift and haul are specialised physical work

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The consumer-auto toolkit barely applies here. An excavator has no licence plate and sits on a different job site each month; a Class 8 tractor is on the road for days; a boat is in a slip, a dry-stack or a backyard; a motorcycle fits in a garage. Fees per unit are higher, and so are the costs of failure: the wrong trailer, a missing permit for an oversized load, a damaged hydraulic line, or a three-state drive to a site the unit left last week. The lenders are equipment and dealer finance programs such as those analysed in [[niches/sales-finance-companies/equipment-dealer-finance-programs/profile|Equipment Dealer & Vendor Finance Programs]], commercial truck lenders, and marine and recreational lenders. Competitors are specialist equipment, truck and marine recovery firms, general agencies with heavy wreckers, and national networks that subcontract the lift. It is not accident recovery, which is [[niches/towing-companies/rural-heavy-duty-recovery/profile|Rural & Highway Heavy-Duty Recovery]] under Towing Companies.

## Current Tools & Gaps
OEM and aftermarket telematics portals on newer equipment and trucks; marine and equipment registries; generic heavy-haul dispatch tools; lender asset lists. Gaps: telematics access depends on the lender's rights and the OEM; locations go stale; nobody combines telematics, registrations, job-site and dealer records into a locate plan.
