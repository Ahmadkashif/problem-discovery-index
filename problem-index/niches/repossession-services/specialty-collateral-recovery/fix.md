# The Excavator That Was Never Where the Lender Said

**Niche:** [[niches/repossession-services/specialty-collateral-recovery/profile|Equipment, Truck, Marine & Powersports Recovery]]
**Industry:** [[industries/repossession-services|Repossession Agencies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Specialty assignments arrive with a stale address, a wrong model and a serial number nobody checked, and the recovery firm discovers it after driving a lowboy three hundred miles.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to locate collateral that carries no plate a camera can read or moves for a living — equipment on job sites, tractors on the road, boats at marinas — and move it intact with the right rig and permits, and whoever recovers high-value units without damage or a wasted long-distance trip takes the account.

## The Problem
Equipment and marine files are built at origination and rarely updated. The collateral description may say "excavator" without a model; the serial may be mistyped; the address may be the borrower's office, not the yard. Recovery firms plan rigs and trips on that record.

## Why It's Still Broken
Lenders do not verify collateral details until default, and firms absorb wasted trips because they are paid on recovery.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A pre-dispatch verification step: decode and check serials against manufacturer formats, match the model to dimensions and weight, confirm the last known location against telematics or a phone check, and return discrepancies to the lender before the truck rolls, with a mobilisation fee agreed if the lender wants the trip anyway.

## Who Feels the Pain
Specialist recovery firms paying for empty trips; lenders whose units sit unrecovered while files are corrected.

## Impact If Fixed
Fewer wasted long-distance mobilisations and faster recoveries once the file is right.
