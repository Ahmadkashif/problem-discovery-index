# Forwarder Portals Scaled Down to a Twenty-Car Lender

**Niche:** [[niches/repossession-services/direct-lender-clients/profile|Credit Union, Community Bank & Buy-Here-Pay-Here Clients]]
**Industry:** [[industries/repossession-services|Repossession Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** National lenders get assignment tracking, status, photos and invoicing through forwarder and RDN portals; a small lender gets a phone call, and the generic client-portal tools that could fill the gap know nothing about repossession.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #compliance #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to be the recovery department a credit union, community bank or buy-here-pay-here dealer does not have, and whoever runs the file from assignment to sale — notices, property, redemption and auction delivery — so the lender never has to touch it takes the account.

## The Problem
Direct clients assign by email, ask for status by text and get invoices as PDFs. Holds and payments are phoned in. Nothing is logged in a form either party can rely on later.

## What Already Exists
Generic client-portal and job-tracking tools for service businesses (Jobber, Housecall Pro client hubs) and the forwarder and RDN portals used by large lenders.

## The Customization Gap
A repossession client portal needs: assignment fields that match a lender's file (VIN, addresses, references, collateral type); a hold and cancel button with timestamped confirmation pushed to the truck; recovery photos, condition report and police notice attached automatically; property and redemption request handling; storage and fee schedule applied per client; and a closing document set for the lender's deficiency file. It must cost a small agency little, because it is a feature of the service rather than a product the client buys.

## Target Customer
Local agencies whose revenue leans on direct credit union, bank and dealer clients.

## Impact If Solved
Direct clients get national-lender visibility, holds stop travelling by voicemail, and the agency's records protect both sides.
