# Credit Union, Community Bank & Buy-Here-Pay-Here Clients

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/repossession-services|Repossession Agencies]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to be the recovery department a credit union, community bank or buy-here-pay-here dealer does not have, and whoever runs the file from assignment to sale — notices, property, redemption and auction delivery — so the lender never has to touch it takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$200–350M a year in agency revenue from direct small-lender clients (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15–20% of agency revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — assignments by email or phone, notices sent by the lender's loan officer from a template, status by text message
**Target Buyer:** Credit union collections manager; community bank loan officer; BHPH dealer principal
**Automation Potential:** Medium-High — the file steps are rule-bound; the relationship is local

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The national lenders reach agencies through forwarders and RDN, with compliance teams that write the rules. Thousands of smaller lenders do not: a credit union with a few dozen repossessions a year, a community bank (see [[industries/community-banks|Community Banks]]), a buy-here-pay-here dealer that carries its own paper. They assign directly to a local agency they trust, pay better fees, and lean on the agency for things they lack staff to do — knowing which notice the state requires before sale, holding property properly, handling a redemption call, getting the car to auction. The lender carries the legal risk for notices and deficiency; the agency that keeps it out of trouble keeps the account. These buyers are the credit union and dealer lenders analysed in [[niches/credit-unions/auto-lending-cus/profile|Auto Lending CUs]] and [[niches/auto-dealers-independent/buy-here-pay-here/profile|Buy Here Pay Here (BHPH) Dealers]]. Competitors are local agencies, forwarders courting smaller lenders, and full-service recovery management firms.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Email and phone; some smaller lenders use RDN; dealers rely on GPS devices from PassTime and Spireon to locate cars. Gaps: no structured channel for holds and cancellations; notice timelines kept by the lender's loan officer; the agency's extra work unpriced and undocumented.
