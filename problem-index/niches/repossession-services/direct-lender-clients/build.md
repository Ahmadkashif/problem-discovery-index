# A Recovery Desk for Lenders With No Recovery Desk

**Niche:** [[niches/repossession-services/direct-lender-clients/profile|Credit Union, Community Bank & Buy-Here-Pay-Here Clients]]
**Industry:** [[industries/repossession-services|Repossession Agencies]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A credit union that repossesses forty cars a year has nobody who knows the state's notice sequence, the property rules or the best auction, and the local agency that could supply that knowledge has no product to deliver it.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #decision-trees #workflow-orchestration #compliance #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to be the recovery department a credit union, community bank or buy-here-pay-here dealer does not have, and whoever runs the file from assignment to sale — notices, property, redemption and auction delivery — so the lender never has to touch it takes the account.

## The Problem
After a recovery the small lender must send the right notices in the right order under its state's Article 9 enactment and retail installment sales law, respond to redemption and reinstatement requests, return property, choose a sale channel and later compute a deficiency it can defend. A loan officer does this between other jobs, from a template that may be years old. When something goes wrong, the agency is asked why it did not warn them.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Recovery-management software is sold to large lenders and forwarders; small lenders are too small to buy it; agencies sell trucks and hours, not workflow.

## What to Build
A white-label file portal the agency offers its direct clients: assignment and hold/cancel by one click; live status and photos; the state's post-recovery steps generated as a checklist with draft notices for the lender to approve and send; property and redemption requests handled through the portal; auction delivery and sale results posted back; and a closing pack with every document the lender needs for the deficiency.

## Target Customer
Agencies with a book of credit union, community bank and dealer clients; credit union service organisations that could offer it to members.

## Impact If Built
Small lenders get a compliant process without hiring, and agencies turn local relationships into a service they can price.
