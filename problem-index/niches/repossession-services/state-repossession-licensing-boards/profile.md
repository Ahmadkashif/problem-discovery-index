# State Repossession Licensing Regulators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/repossession-services|Repossession Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The state bodies that license repossession agencies and agents and discipline them: California's Bureau of Security and Investigative Services under the Collateral Recovery Act, Florida's Division of Licensing under Chapter 493 (Class R agencies, Class E agents), the Illinois Commerce Commission under the Illinois Collateral Recovery Act, and their counterparts elsewhere; many states license nothing specific to repossession. They process applications, investigate complaints and take licence actions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 staff per state on repossession specifically, mostly licensing and investigation (estimate)
**Output:** Licences, complaint investigations, disciplinary orders, guidance
**Proprietary data:** Licence rolls and complaint files
**Clock:** Statutory licensing and renewal cycles
**Buyer:** Bureau chief
