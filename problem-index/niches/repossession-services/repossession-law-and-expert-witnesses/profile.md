# Repossession Law Practices & Expert Witnesses

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/repossession-services|Repossession Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Consumer finance defence firms that advise lenders and agencies on self-help repossession law and write industry training content; plaintiff consumer lawyers who bring wrongful-repossession, breach-of-peace and notice claims; and individual repossession-practice experts who testify on industry standards. Lenders, agencies, insurers and borrowers pay by the hour or on contingency.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 people on repossession at any one firm (estimate)
**Output:** Legal opinions, policies, training content, expert reports, litigation
**Proprietary data:** Matter files held under privilege or for clients
**Clock:** Court deadlines on individual cases
**Buyer:** Practice group leader
