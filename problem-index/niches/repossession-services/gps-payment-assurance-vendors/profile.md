# GPS Payment-Assurance & Starter-Interrupt Device Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/repossession-services|Repossession Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors such as PassTime and Spireon sell GPS trackers and starter-interrupt devices that buy-here-pay-here dealers and subprime lenders install in financed vehicles, so they can remind borrowers, disable the car after default where state law allows, and give the repossession agent a live location. They are used heavily by the dealer lenders analysed in [[niches/auto-dealers-independent/buy-here-pay-here/profile|Buy Here Pay Here (BHPH) Dealers]] and by rideshare fleet lessors. Dealers and lenders pay per device and per month.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 data and analytics staff per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Device location and status feeds; payment-risk and portfolio dashboards for dealers
**Proprietary data:** Location and disable-event histories from devices across many dealers' portfolios
**Clock:** None set by a third party; state GPS-disclosure rules govern use, not timing
**Buyer:** VP of product or data at the device vendor
