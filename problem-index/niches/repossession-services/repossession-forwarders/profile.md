# Repossession Forwarding & Recovery Management Companies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/repossession-services|Repossession Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Forwarders take repossession assignments from national lenders and place them with local agencies, managing the agency panel, compliance oversight, updates, personal property and transport on the lender's behalf. Resolvion reported ~1M assignments a year and 40+ of the top 50 auto finance companies as clients after its 2019 merger; PAR North America (which KAR owned alongside RDN), Primeritus and agency-owned cooperatives such as Relliance compete. Lenders pay per recovery and management fees; agencies receive a share.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 analysts and vendor-performance staff at the larger forwarders (estimate); case managers handling files are production staff
**Output:** Agency scorecards, allocation decisions, recovery and compliance reporting to lenders
**Proprietary data:** Cross-lender assignment outcomes and agency performance histories
**Clock:** Lender service levels and monthly reporting; the state notice clocks belong to the lender
**Buyer:** Chief operating officer or VP of agency network

**Kill switches:** data isn't theirs — the assignment and borrower account data a forwarder analyses is the lender's, held under service agreements, and no evidence was found that those agreements permit aggregate reuse
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. The forwarder sees more agency outcomes than anyone, but the files are its clients' and its revenue is per recovery, not per insight.
