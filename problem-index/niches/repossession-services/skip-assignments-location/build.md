# One Ranked List of Every Lead on a Skip

**Niche:** [[niches/repossession-services/skip-assignments-location/profile|Skip Assignments & Location Work]]
**Industry:** [[industries/repossession-services|Repossession Agencies]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A skip file accumulates addresses from searches, references, camera hits and tips, and the skip tracer decides which to drive by feel because nothing ranks them against each other or records which kind of lead finds cars.
**Tags:** #tacit-knowledge-ml #gradient-boosting #k-nearest-neighbors #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to find a vehicle whose borrower has moved or is hiding it, and whoever locates the most skips per dollar of skip-trace searches, camera time and field investigation takes the account.

## The Problem
After a month a skip file can carry a dozen candidate locations: two addresses from an identity search, a reference's apartment, an employer, a camera hit at a shopping centre five weeks ago, a cousin's house mentioned by a neighbour. Each drive-by costs fuel and an hour. Experienced skip tracers know that a recent camera hit at night near a reference's address beats a fresh search address with no hits, and that a car seen twice at an employer on weekdays is the best lead of all, but they cannot write the rule down and do not record which lead type paid off.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Each lead source belongs to a different vendor and arrives in a different screen; the outcome — where the car was finally recovered — is rarely joined back to the leads that preceded it; and agencies never pooled their skip histories.

## What to Build
A lead-ranking engine per skip file: every candidate location, from every source, scored on the chance the car is there in the next week using lead type, recency, hit timing, distance between leads and the agency's historical conversion by lead type. The recovered location is captured at close-out and joined back, so the ranking learns which sources earn their search fees.

## Target Customer
Agency skip managers and forwarders' skip desks working thousands of skips a month.

## Impact If Built
Fewer wasted drive-bys per skip found, and evidence for which search and camera subscriptions are worth their cost.
