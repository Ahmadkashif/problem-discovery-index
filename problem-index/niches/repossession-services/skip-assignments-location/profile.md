# Skip Assignments & Location Work

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/repossession-services|Repossession Agencies]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to find a vehicle whose borrower has moved or is hiding it, and whoever locates the most skips per dollar of skip-trace searches, camera time and field investigation takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300–500M a year in recovery and skip fees on files with no working address, plus the search and camera spend behind them (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~20–25% of agency revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium-High — searches and camera hits are digital; the investigation that joins them is phone calls and drive-bys
**Target Buyer:** Agency skip manager or owner; forwarder skip desks
**Automation Potential:** High for lead ranking, medium for field confirmation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A skip is a file where the borrower has moved, the addresses are stale, or the car is being hidden at a relative's, a workplace or a storage lot. The contest is not at the curb but before it: combining identity-graph searches, references, social media, camera hits and canvassing into a location good enough to send a truck. Licence-plate cameras have changed the economics — they find the cars parked in the open first, which, as trade commentary argues, leaves agencies the hardest skips at flat fees set for easy ones. Competitors are camera networks and their affiliate agencies, forwarders' in-house skip teams, skip-trace data vendors and local agencies with investigators. It differs from collections skip tracing, profiled as [[niches/collections-agencies/skip-tracing-operations/profile|Skip Tracing Operations]], because the target is a vehicle's physical location, not a debtor's phone number.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Identity searches from the vendors profiled as [[niches/collections-agencies/identity-skip-trace-data-providers/profile|Identity Resolution & Skip Trace Data Providers]]; DRN and MVTRAC hits; social media and people-search sites. Gaps: leads from different sources are never ranked against each other; nobody records which lead type actually found the car; skip effort is unpaid unless the car is found.
