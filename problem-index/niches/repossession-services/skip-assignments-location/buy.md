# Collections Skip-Trace Orchestration Adapted to Vehicle Location

**Niche:** [[niches/repossession-services/skip-assignments-location/profile|Skip Assignments & Location Work]]
**Industry:** [[industries/repossession-services|Repossession Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Collection agencies are learning to orchestrate skip-trace searches by cost and hit rate; repossession skips need the same discipline aimed at where a car sleeps, not where a debtor answers the phone.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to find a vehicle whose borrower has moved or is hiding it, and whoever locates the most skips per dollar of skip-trace searches, camera time and field investigation takes the account.

## The Problem
Agencies buy identity searches per query, camera access by subscription and investigator time by the hour, and spend them in the same order on every skip regardless of what the file looks like.

## What Already Exists
The orchestration problem is analysed for collections as [[niches/collections-agencies/skip-tracing-operations/profile|Skip Tracing Operations]]: choosing which vendor to query, in what order, and when to re-trace, scored on contact outcomes. Collection platforms and some data vendors offer waterfall rules.

## The Customization Gap
For repossession the outcome is a recovered vehicle at a place, not a right-party phone contact. The adaptation needs: address-level rather than phone-level scoring; camera hits and scan recency as first-class sources; vehicle features (a work truck goes to job sites, a sports car to a garage); the cost of a drive-by alongside the cost of a search; permissible-purpose and state privacy rules for each source; and re-trace triggers driven by new hits rather than the calendar.

## Target Customer
Forwarder skip desks and multi-state agencies spending materially on searches and camera subscriptions.

## Impact If Solved
Lower search and camera spend per skip located, and a re-trace cadence that follows the evidence.
