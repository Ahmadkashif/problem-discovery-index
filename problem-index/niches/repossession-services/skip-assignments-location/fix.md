# Skip Work Priced as if It Were a Hot File

**Niche:** [[niches/repossession-services/skip-assignments-location/profile|Skip Assignments & Location Work]]
**Industry:** [[industries/repossession-services|Repossession Agencies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A skip takes weeks of searches, drive-bys and camera time and pays the same flat fee as a car parked at a known address, so agencies quietly stop working skips.
**Tags:** #survival-analysis #logistic-regression #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to find a vehicle whose borrower has moved or is hiding it, and whoever locates the most skips per dollar of skip-trace searches, camera time and field investigation takes the account.

## The Problem
Flat recovery fees were set when most files had good addresses. Trade commentary argues they have barely moved since 2000 — a $350 fee then would be ~$576 in 2021 dollars — while camera networks find the easy cars first, leaving agencies the hard skips. Some clients pay a skip or close fee, but often only on request and only with proof of effort.

## Why It's Still Broken
Agencies cannot show what a skip costs because they do not record the effort per file, and they negotiate one at a time against forwarders who see the whole market.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Track search fees, drive-by miles and hours, and camera time per skip from the field app and vendor invoices, attach the evidence to the file, and present clients with the documented cost per located skip by file type — the basis for a skip fee schedule rather than a favour.

## Who Feels the Pain
Agency owners carrying skips at a loss; skip tracers whose work is invisible; lenders whose hardest collateral is the least worked.

## Impact If Fixed
Skips priced to their cost get worked, which is where the remaining unrecovered collateral sits.
