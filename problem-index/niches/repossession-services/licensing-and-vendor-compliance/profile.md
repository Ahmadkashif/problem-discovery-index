# Licensing & Vendor-Audit Compliance

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/repossession-services|Repossession Agencies]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stay eligible for assignments by satisfying each state's licensing rules and each lender's and forwarder's vendor audit with complete evidence, and whoever can produce a clean audit file on demand for every location takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$30–60M a year in compliance staff time, training, lot inspections and audit preparation at agencies, excluding insurance premiums (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2–4% of agency revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — binders, shared drives and certificate PDFs emailed on request
**Target Buyer:** Agency owner or compliance manager at multi-state agencies
**Automation Potential:** High for tracking and evidence assembly, low for the judgement in an audit interview

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Agencies are licensed where states require it — California through the Bureau of Security and Investigative Services, Florida through the Division of Licensing with Class R agency and Class E agent licences, Illinois through the Illinois Commerce Commission under its Collateral Recovery Act — and are audited by the lenders and forwarders they serve, whose own vendor-management obligations have pushed requirements down: insurance certificates, background checks, CARS certification (a 2020 RISC release cited 60% of agencies holding it), lot security standards, police and property notice evidence on sampled files. Failing an audit, or letting a licence lapse in one state, can stop assignments overnight. Competitors are RISC's training and lot-inspection services, compliance consultants, forwarders' vendor-compliance teams and agencies' own office staff.

## Current Tools & Gaps
RISC's CARS courses and lot inspections; generic document storage; forwarders' audit questionnaires. Gaps: no single record of licences, certifications and insurance by location and person; audit evidence assembled from scratch each time; file-level compliance (police notices, property notices, photos) checked only when an auditor samples it.
