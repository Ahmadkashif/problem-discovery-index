# Lender Third-Party Risk Platforms Turned Around for the Vendor

**Niche:** [[niches/repossession-services/licensing-and-vendor-compliance/profile|Licensing & Vendor-Audit Compliance]]
**Industry:** [[industries/repossession-services|Repossession Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Lenders run vendor oversight on third-party risk platforms that collect documents from vendors; the agencies being overseen have nothing comparable on their side to keep those documents ready.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #compliance #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stay eligible for assignments by satisfying each state's licensing rules and each lender's and forwarder's vendor audit with complete evidence, and whoever can produce a clean audit file on demand for every location takes the account.

## The Problem
Agencies answer the same requests — certificate of insurance, licence copies, background check attestations, policies — for every client portal, in every format, and each request has a deadline.

## What Already Exists
Third-party risk management platforms used by banks and lenders (Venminder, ProcessUnity and similar) collect vendor documents, track expiry and score vendor risk; contract and document management tools track renewal dates.

## The Customization Gap
The vendor-side adaptation needs: a repository organised by state licence class, location and individual agent; repossession-specific evidence types (CARS certificates, lot inspection reports, police notice logs, property notices); automatic expiry tracking against each state's renewal rules; one-click responses formatted for each forwarder's and lender's portal; and a file-level evidence sampler, which no lender platform provides because lenders audit files themselves.

## Target Customer
Compliance managers at multi-state agencies and agency cooperatives that could offer it to members.

## Impact If Solved
Document requests answered in minutes, no expired certificate surprising a client, and a compliance record the agency owns rather than one scattered across client portals.
