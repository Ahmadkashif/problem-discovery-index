# Auto Finance Recoveries

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/repossession-services|Repossession Agencies]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor is fighting to close more of a lender's auto assignments inside its window without a wrongful-repossession event — but a file with a good address is won at the curb on the first attempt, while a skip is won before the truck moves by finding the car, and the two contests have different inputs, pay and rivals. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.0–1.5B a year in involuntary recovery, skip and close fees on consumer auto loans and leases (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~60–65% of agency revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — assignments, hits and updates are digital; choosing which file to work and how is not
**Target Buyer:** Agency owner or operations manager; forwarder and lender vendor managers who allocate the files
**Automation Potential:** Medium — planning, verification and paperwork automate; the recovery is a person with a truck

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Consumer auto finance is the volume of the industry: Cox Automotive estimated ~1.73M repossessions in 2024, the most since 2009, and platform data analysed by CURepossession counted ~2.4M assignments and ~706,000 recoveries in the second quarter of 2025 alone. Banks, captives, credit unions and subprime finance companies assign through RDN, directly or through forwarders, at a flat fee paid on recovery. The lender's own side of this — when to assign, notices, auction channel and deficiency — is [[niches/sales-finance-companies/repossession-and-remarketing/profile|Repossession, Remarketing & Deficiency]] under Sales Finance Companies; this niche is the agency's side, getting the car.

## Current Tools & Gaps
RDN and Clearplan for assignment and field work, forwarder portals, DRN and MVTRAC cameras, skip-trace searches, towing dispatch software. Gaps: no record of attempts the agency can learn from; no way to tell a hot file from a skip at assignment; scorecards built on recovery rate without adjusting for file difficulty.
