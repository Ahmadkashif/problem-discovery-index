# The Assignment That Ages Unworked

**Niche:** [[niches/repossession-services/auto-finance-recoveries/profile|Auto Finance Recoveries]]
**Industry:** [[industries/repossession-services|Repossession Agencies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Files that look hard sink to the bottom of the list, go weeks without a real attempt, and are pulled and reassigned — after the agency has already spent update time on them and earned nothing.

## The Problem
With recoveries at ~29% of assignments, most files an agency holds will never pay. Agents naturally work the promising ones; the rest are carried, updated on the client's cadence to stay compliant, and eventually closed or reassigned to another agency, sometimes with a "close fee" and often without one.

## Why It's Still Broken
Nothing in the workflow distinguishes a file worth one more attempt from one that should be returned now. Agencies fear that returning files hurts their scorecard, so they keep them, and clients reassign on a calendar rather than on evidence.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Score every open file weekly on its chance of closing in the next 30 days, given its age, attempts so far, hits and address freshness. Surface the files that deserve a different approach (daylight, workplace, second agent) and the ones to return with a documented attempt history so the next agency starts informed.

## Who Feels the Pain
Agency office staff carrying update obligations on dead files; agents sent on attempts with no plan; clients whose collateral depreciates while files age.

## Impact If Fixed
Agency time shifts from carrying files to closing them, and files that are returned arrive at the next agency with a usable record instead of a blank.
