# Known-Address Recoveries

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/repossession-services|Repossession Agencies]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to recover a car whose address is known on the first attempt, without an objection, a damage claim or a recovery on a cancelled order, and whoever posts the highest clean first-attempt recovery rate on the forwarder's scorecard takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600–900M a year in involuntary recovery fees on files with a working address (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~40% of agency revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — the order is digital, the attempt is not recorded beyond a status line
**Target Buyer:** Agency owner; forwarder vendor manager
**Automation Potential:** Low for the recovery, medium for timing, status checks and evidence

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
When the lender has a current address, finding the car is not the problem; executing is. The car is at the borrower's home or work most days, and the contest is getting there at an hour it is present and the borrower is not outside, hooking it in a minute or two, and leaving without a breach of the peace. The clocks are the client's assignment window, the order's live status — a payment or extension can cancel it an hour before the attempt, and the CFPB found in 2022 that servicers had repossessed cars even after borrowers acted to prevent it — and, in California, a police notice within an hour of the recovery. Competitors are local agencies, large multi-state agencies and the preferred-agency panels forwarders build; the forwarder's scorecard decides allocation.

## Current Tools & Gaps
RDN's On-Hook Status gives a proceed, hold or cancel flag to agents on Clearplan; DRN sells body-worn cameras; towing software handles the truck. Gaps: status checks that cover direct clients and every portal, not only RDN; no record of what earlier attempts found at the address; no evidence trail of the attempt itself unless an agent remembers to record it.
