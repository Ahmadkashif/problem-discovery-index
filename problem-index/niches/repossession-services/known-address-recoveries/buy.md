# Body-Worn Camera Evidence Systems Adapted to Recovery Attempts

**Niche:** [[niches/repossession-services/known-address-recoveries/profile|Known-Address Recoveries]]
**Industry:** [[industries/repossession-services|Repossession Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Police and security firms already capture, store and retrieve body-worn video as evidence; recovery attempts need the same chain of custody tied to the assignment, the state's rules and the client's dispute process.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #compliance #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to recover a car whose address is known on the first attempt, without an objection, a damage claim or a recovery on a cancelled order, and whoever posts the highest clean first-attempt recovery rate on the forwarder's scorecard takes the account.

## The Problem
Whether a recovery was peaceful is decided after the fact, on the borrower's word against the agent's. A wrongful-repossession or breach-of-peace claim can cost the agency its panel place, and the agency's evidence is usually a status note and a few photos.

## What Already Exists
Body-worn camera and digital evidence platforms used by police and private security, with automatic upload, tamper-evident storage, redaction and retrieval by incident. DRN sells a body-worn camera alongside its plate readers.

## The Customization Gap
The repossession version needs: recording that starts automatically on arrival at an assignment address; footage indexed to the file, the attempt and the GPS trace; a transcript and summary of any exchange, flagging an objection so the agent's decision to stop is documented; retention matched to the state's statute of limitations and the client's dispute window; retrieval packaged for the forwarder's complaint process; and privacy handling for bystanders filmed on private property.

## Target Customer
Agencies working high-volume known-address files for national forwarders, and their insurers.

## Impact If Solved
Disputed recoveries are resolved on evidence, agencies keep panel places they would otherwise lose to unprovable complaints, and agents who stop at an objection have a record that they did.
