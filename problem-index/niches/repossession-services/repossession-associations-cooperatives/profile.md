# Repossession Trade Associations & Agency Cooperatives

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/repossession-services|Repossession Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
National and state associations of recovery agencies lobby on licensing and fees, run conferences and training, and publish member news; agency-owned cooperatives such as National Finance Adjusters (described in 2013 as the largest industry-owned forwarding cooperative) and Relliance pool members to compete with forwarders for national lender volume. Members pay dues or a share of placed fees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 staff per organisation (estimate)
**Output:** Advocacy, member services, pooled placements
**Proprietary data:** Member rosters; cooperatives hold members' assignment outcomes
**Clock:** Legislative sessions and annual conventions
**Buyer:** Executive director or cooperative president
