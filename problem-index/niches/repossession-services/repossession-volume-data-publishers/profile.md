# Repossession Volume & Market Data Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/repossession-services|Repossession Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
The few sources of national repossession numbers: Cox Automotive's industry insights team, whose estimate of ~1.73M repossessions in 2024 is the figure press and policymakers quote, and the trade publisher CURepossession, which turns RDN assignment data into quarterly volume and recovery-rate reports (~29% of assignments recovered in 2025). There is no official count. Readers get the numbers free; the publishers earn from advertising, conferences and their wider businesses.

## Insight Function
**Size:** A handful of people on repossession at each (estimate)
**Output:** Annual and quarterly volume estimates and commentary
**Proprietary data:** Platform-derived assignment counts; auction and loan-performance data at Cox
**Clock:** Self-set publication schedules
**Buyer:** Editor or chief economist
