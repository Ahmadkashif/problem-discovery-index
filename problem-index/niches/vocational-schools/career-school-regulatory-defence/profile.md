# Career School Regulatory & Litigation Defence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/vocational-schools|Vocational Schools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Defend career schools against programme reviews, borrower defence claims, state attorney general actions and enforcement over recruitment and outcome claims — a sector under near-continuous regulatory pressure.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 attorneys, compliance consultants, and analysts
**Output:** Enforcement response and settlement negotiation, substantiation of outcome claims, recruitment practice review, wind-down and teach-out planning
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated enforcement outcomes and what substantiation regulators accepted
**Clock:** Response deadlines set by regulators and litigation schedules
**Buyer:** Practice Leader
