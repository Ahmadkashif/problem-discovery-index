# Career School Group Corporate Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/vocational-schools|Vocational Schools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator/rollup

## What They Do
Corporate analytics inside the multi-campus career school groups — enrolment forecasting, programme portfolio decisions, campus performance, placement measurement and acquisition screening.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 analysts, institutional researchers, and corporate staff
**Output:** Enrolment and retention forecasting, programme launch and closure analysis, campus performance reporting, placement verification, compliance dashboards
**Proprietary data:** Student records joined to completion, placement and, where verified, employer and wage across many campuses
**Clock:** Enrolment starts and regulatory reporting deadlines
**Buyer:** VP of Institutional Research
