# Workforce Programme Administration

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/vocational-schools|Vocational Schools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Administer the public workforce funding that pays for a share of vocational training — eligible provider lists, training vouchers, participant tracking and the outcome reporting the funding is judged against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 across boards and their contractors — programme analysts, case managers, and reporting staff
**Output:** Provider eligibility determinations, training authorisation, participant outcome tracking, federal performance reporting, labour market prioritisation
**Proprietary data:** Participant records joined to state wage records — one of the few places training is actually linked to subsequent earnings
**Clock:** Federal performance reporting periods and funding cycles
**Buyer:** Workforce board director
