# Enrolment Marketing & Lead Generation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/vocational-schools|Vocational Schools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator/rollup

## What They Do
Generate prospective student enquiries at scale and route them to schools — historically the sector's largest discretionary expense and the focus of most of its regulatory trouble.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 media analysts, data scientists, and qualification staff
**Output:** Lead scoring and pricing, campaign attribution, enrolment likelihood modelling, channel mix optimisation, compliance monitoring of representations made
**Proprietary data:** Enquiry-to-enrolment-to-completion behaviour across campaigns and institutions
**Clock:** Enrolment start dates
**Buyer:** VP of Analytics
