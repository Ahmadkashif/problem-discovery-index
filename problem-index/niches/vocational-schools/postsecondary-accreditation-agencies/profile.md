# Postsecondary Accreditation Agencies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/vocational-schools|Vocational Schools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Review and accredit career schools against institutional standards — an accreditation being the precondition of federal aid eligibility, and its loss being the end of the institution.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 review staff, standards specialists, and volunteer visiting teams
**Output:** Accreditation decisions and conditions, standards and revisions, institutional review findings, outcome reporting requirements
**Proprietary data:** Institutional outcome submissions — completion, placement and licensure pass rates — collected annually across all accredited schools
**Clock:** Accreditation review cycles and annual reporting deadlines
**Buyer:** President / Director of Accreditation
