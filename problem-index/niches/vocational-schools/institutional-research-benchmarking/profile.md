# Institutional Research & Outcomes Benchmarking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/vocational-schools|Vocational Schools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Assemble federal institutional reporting into comparative datasets on enrolment, completion, cost, aid and earnings, and sell the benchmarking and market analysis schools and investors use.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 analysts, data engineers, and researchers
**Output:** Institutional benchmarking, programme-level market analysis, competitive enrolment tracking, outcome comparisons
**Proprietary data:** Assembled and normalised federal reporting across institutions and years; the underlying data is public
**Clock:** Federal reporting release cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Research
