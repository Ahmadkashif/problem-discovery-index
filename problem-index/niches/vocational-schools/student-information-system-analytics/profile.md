# Student Information System Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/vocational-schools|Vocational Schools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Supply the systems career schools run admissions, aid, attendance, grading and placement in, with the regulatory reporting and retention analytics attached.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 product, implementation, and data staff
**Output:** Enrolment and attendance tracking, aid disbursement workflow, regulatory reporting, retention risk alerting, placement documentation
**Proprietary data:** Student lifecycle records across many institutions, held per client
**Clock:** Attendance and disbursement reporting deadlines
**Buyer:** VP of Product
