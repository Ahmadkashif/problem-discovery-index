# Trade & Industry Certification Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/vocational-schools|Vocational Schools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Develop and administer the industry certifications a vocational graduate needs to be employable — automotive, welding, HVAC, information technology, allied health — and define the competency standards programmes are built to teach.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 psychometricians, item developers, subject matter experts, and programme accreditation staff across the certifying bodies
**Output:** Certification examinations and item banks, job task analyses, competency standards, programme accreditation, credential registries
**Proprietary data:** Item response data across large candidate populations, job task analyses of the trades themselves, and pass rates by training programme
**Clock:** Examination windows, recertification cycles, and standards revision periods
**Buyer:** Chief Assessment Officer / VP of Certification
