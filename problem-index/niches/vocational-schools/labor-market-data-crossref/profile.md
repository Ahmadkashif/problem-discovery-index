# Labour Market & Skills Taxonomy Data Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/vocational-schools|Vocational Schools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Maintain the occupation and skills taxonomies and the job posting data that describe what employers are hiring for, where, and at what wage — the evidence a career school uses to justify launching a programme and a regulator uses to judge whether it should exist.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-800 taxonomists, data scientists, economists, and data operations staff
**Output:** Skills and occupation taxonomies, job posting analytics, regional demand and wage estimates, programme alignment analysis
**Proprietary data:** Continuously maintained taxonomies and deduplicated posting corpora assembled over years
**Clock:** Programme approval cycles and quarterly reporting
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / Head of Labour Market Research
