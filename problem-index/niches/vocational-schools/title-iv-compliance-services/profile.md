# Title IV Compliance & Financial Aid Administration

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/vocational-schools|Vocational Schools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Keep career schools eligible for federal student aid, which is the revenue of almost every one of them — administering aid packaging and disbursement, running return-of-funds calculations when students withdraw, preparing the annual compliance audit, and defending the school through programme reviews. Losing eligibility closes the school.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 financial aid specialists, compliance analysts, and auditors across the servicers and consultancies
**Output:** Aid packaging and disbursement administration, return of funds calculations, compliance audits and corrective action, programme review defence, cohort default rate management, recertification support
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated findings across many programme reviews — what auditors and regional offices actually cite, which is not published
**Clock:** Annual compliance audit deadlines, disbursement and return windows measured in days, recertification dates, and cohort default rate publication
**Buyer:** Director of Compliance Services
