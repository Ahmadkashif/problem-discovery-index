# Vocational Curriculum & Simulation Suppliers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/vocational-schools|Vocational Schools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Supply the curricula, training equipment and simulators career programmes teach on — welding and driving simulators, clinical manikins, diagnostic trainers — with the assessment and progression logic built in.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 instructional designers, subject matter experts, and simulation engineers
**Output:** Curricula aligned to certification standards, simulation scenarios and scoring, competency assessments, instructor tools
**Proprietary data:** Simulator performance data across thousands of learners performing the same task repeatedly, with objective scoring
**Clock:** Programme adoption and academic calendars
**Buyer:** VP of Curriculum / Chief Learning Officer
