# The Daily Writer

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/newsletter-media|Newsletter Media]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make a deadline that arrives every morning forever sustainable for the person who has to meet it — and whoever compresses the reading that precedes the writing gives that person their day back.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$200M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — reading everything by hand
**Target Buyer:** Editorial leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — sourcing, drafting support and continuity

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A daily newsletter is a deadline that arrives every morning forever. Most of the work before writing is reading everything to find out what happened, filtering it for what this audience cares about, and deciding what matters — which takes hours and produces nothing visible. The writer is the product, the schedule has no slack, and the failure mode of the business is that person stopping. Nothing in the category's tooling serves them.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Feed readers, bookmarks, an inbox, a document and a deadline. The gaps: sourcing is manual and repeated daily; the archive of what was already covered is unsearchable; no drafting support that preserves voice; no continuity if the writer is unavailable; and no measure of what the reading actually costs.
