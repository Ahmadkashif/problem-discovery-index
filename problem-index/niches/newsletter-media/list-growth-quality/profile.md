# List Growth & Acquisition Quality

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/newsletter-media|Newsletter Media]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to buy subscribers who read rather than subscribers who count — and whoever prices acquisition on downstream engagement rather than on cost per signup stops paying to damage their own delivery.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$350M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~14% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — bought on cost per subscriber
**Target Buyer:** Growth leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — source quality attribution

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Subscribers are acquired from paid social, co-registration, recommendation networks, giveaways and cross-promotion, at wildly varying quality. A cheap subscriber who never opens is worse than no subscriber, because they dilute engagement, raise complaint risk and damage the sender reputation that governs delivery to everyone else. Acquisition is nonetheless bought and reported on cost per subscriber, which is the one metric that cannot distinguish the two.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Acquisition channels, a cost-per-subscriber target and a growth dashboard. The gaps: source quality not tracked beyond signup; engagement and complaint behaviour not attributed to source; lifetime value per source never computed; bad sources not cut; and growth targets set in subscriber counts.
