# Personalisation From Retail Email

**Industry:** [[industries/newsletter-media|Newsletter Media]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Retail email personalises send time, content and frequency per recipient as standard, and editorial email sends one thing to everyone.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to decide who receives what and when, per subscriber rather than per list — and whoever does it lifts engagement without writing a single additional word.

## The Problem
Retail and ecommerce email has done this for years: send time optimisation per recipient, content blocks selected by behaviour, frequency governed by engagement and fatigue models, and lifecycle programmes that differ by segment. The features are standard in marketing platforms and the lift is well documented. Editorial email, delivered by similar infrastructure, uses almost none of it.

## What Already Exists
Send time optimisation; behavioural content block selection; frequency and fatigue management; lifecycle and onboarding programmes; and engagement-based suppression tiers.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to an editorial product with a single voice. It requires: (1) an artefact that must remain coherent as a publication, so content selection cannot assemble a personalised bundle the way a product email does — this is the substantive constraint and defines what may be varied; (2) advertising sold against whole-list delivery, which means variation has a commercial consequence retail personalisation never faces; (3) a small team with no marketing operations capacity, so the features must be automatic rather than configured; (4) engagement signals degraded by privacy features, where retail has purchase data; and (5) a relationship with a writer rather than a brand, which changes what feels acceptable to vary.

## Target Customer
Audience leadership, newsletter platform vendors, advertisers, and email marketing platform vendors serving publishers.

## Impact If Solved
The features are standard in retail email and unavailable in editorial practice. Varying delivery and emphasis while keeping the publication coherent — and pricing the advertising consequence — is the adaptation.
