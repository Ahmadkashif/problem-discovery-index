# Paid Subscription & Retention

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/newsletter-media|Newsletter Media]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to convert free readers to paid and keep them past the first renewal — and whoever knows which reader will pay and which will lapse stops running the paid tier on hope.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$250M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — a paywall and a hope
**Target Buyer:** Publisher leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — propensity, timing and churn prediction

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The paid tier is the revenue line that is not dependent on advertisers or on list size, and it is run with almost no instrumentation. Conversion is a paywall and an occasional appeal. Pricing is a guess. Churn is discovered in the billing report. The publisher does not know which free readers are close to paying, which paid readers are about to lapse, or what the paid product would need to be worth its price to the people who declined.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A paywall, a subscription platform and a churn number. The gaps: no conversion propensity model; paywall timing and placement untested; pricing never tested; churn not predicted; and the reasons for cancelling never collected or used.
