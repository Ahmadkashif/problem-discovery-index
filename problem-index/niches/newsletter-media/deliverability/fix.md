# Open Rate Stopped Meaning Anything in 2021

**Niche:** [[niches/newsletter-media/deliverability/profile|Deliverability]]
**Industry:** [[industries/newsletter-media|Newsletter Media]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The metric the whole business is managed on was broken by a privacy feature four years ago and everyone still reports it.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #quick-win #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #compliance #hypothesis-testing #automation #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get the newsletter into the inbox rather than the promotions tab or the spam folder — and the contest splits cleanly enough that it is not terminal.

## The Problem
Open rate is quoted in every media kit, used to price advertising, reported to sponsors, and used internally to judge whether a send worked. Since Apple's Mail Privacy Protection, a large and varying share of opens are pre-fetched rather than read, which makes the number a mixture of real reading and automatic loading in proportions that differ by audience and change over time. Everyone in the industry knows this. The number is still the headline metric everywhere.

## Why It's Still Broken
Open rate was the industry's currency, so abandoning it means abandoning the number advertisers ask for — a metric embedded in commercial negotiation outlives its own validity because nobody can drop it unilaterally. The replacement requires agreeing a new standard. Reporting a lower, more honest number looks like worse performance. And no publisher wants to be first.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Report what can be trusted and say what cannot. Lead with click-based and reading-based metrics, which is the fix and is what is actually measurable. Separate machine opens from probable human opens where the signals allow, since timing and user agent patterns distinguish them imperfectly but usefully. Report the affected share of the list, as the distortion varies enormously by audience and knowing yours is the first step. Track the metric's own trend rather than its level, because the level is contaminated and the trend is still informative. Say plainly in the media kit what open rate now means, which is an honesty advantage in a market where every number is discounted. Price advertising on clicks and confirmed engagement rather than on opens, as that is where the industry will end up. Keep a stable, internally consistent measure for year-on-year comparison, since switching metrics destroys history if not handled. Segment by provider, because the distortion is concentrated and the picture differs sharply. Push for a shared standard through industry bodies, as this needs a collective answer. And stop making editorial decisions from a contaminated number, which is the quiet cost nobody counts.

## Who Feels the Pain
Publishers managing on a broken metric; advertisers paying against it; writers judged by it; and an industry whose currency everyone privately discounts.

## Impact If Fixed
A metric embedded in commercial negotiation outlives its own validity because nobody can drop it unilaterally. Leading with click-based measures and stating plainly what open rate now means is an honesty advantage in a market where every number is already discounted.
