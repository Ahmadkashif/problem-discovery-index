# Deliverability

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/newsletter-media|Newsletter Media]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get the newsletter into the inbox rather than the promotions tab or the spam folder — and the contest splits cleanly enough that it is not terminal.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$700M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~28% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — managed without an instrument
**Target Buyer:** Publisher and growth leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — inference and operations, both unbuilt

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Whether the product is delivered at all is decided by mailbox providers who report almost nothing. The metric publishers used to infer placement was largely broken by a privacy feature in 2021, and the 2024 bulk sender requirements attached hard consequences to complaint rates while mandating authentication and one-click unsubscribe. This is the largest contest in the category — the business rests entirely on it — and it is two: inferring where mail lands, and managing the inputs that decide it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Seed list testing, postmaster tools covering part of the audience, authentication configuration and list hygiene rules of thumb. The gaps: placement not inferred from the publisher's own data; seed tests generalised from a few dozen synthetic mailboxes; provider-level behaviour not modelled; and reputation managed reactively after a problem.
