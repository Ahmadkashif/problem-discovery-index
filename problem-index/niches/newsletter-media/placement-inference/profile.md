# Placement Inference

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/newsletter-media|Newsletter Media]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to estimate where a send actually landed, per provider and per segment, from behaviour rather than from a seed test — and whoever does it replaces a few dozen synthetic mailboxes with the publisher's own hundred thousand.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~16% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — not attempted
**Target Buyer:** Publisher and data leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — inference over an existing record

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This contest is measurement. Given the publisher's own record — every send, every subscriber's engagement, by provider, by segment, over years — estimate where the mail landed. It is an inference problem with a strong natural control in the provider dimension, and the current alternative is a seed list test sampling a few dozen synthetic mailboxes that receive mail no real subscriber receives. It has nothing to do with managing complaint rates or configuring authentication.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Seed list tests, partial postmaster data, and inference by feel. The gaps: publisher's own data not used; provider-level comparison not exploited; no baseline or change detection; no confidence expressed; and no pooled view of provider behaviour.
