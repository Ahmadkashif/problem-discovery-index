# Campaign Operations From Agencies

**Niche:** [[niches/newsletter-media/the-ad-ops-manager/profile|The Ad Operations Manager]]
**Industry:** [[industries/newsletter-media|Newsletter Media]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Agencies and publishers built campaign operations as a function with trafficking, quality control and standardised reporting, and a newsletter has one person with a spreadsheet.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #automation #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #data-integration #compliance #descriptive-statistics #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stop one person assembling every sponsor's report by hand in whatever format that sponsor asked for — and whoever automates it removes the bottleneck the whole revenue line runs through.

## The Problem
Campaign operations is an established function in agencies and large publishers: trafficking with defined specifications, quality control before launch, delivery monitoring, discrepancy resolution between buyer and seller counts, standardised reporting formats, and reconciliation to billing. It is process-heavy for good reasons and assumes systems and staff. A newsletter publisher performs every one of those steps manually.

## What Already Exists
Trafficking workflow with creative specifications; pre-launch quality control; delivery monitoring and pacing; discrepancy resolution practice; standardised reporting formats; and reconciliation to billing.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a single irreversible send. It requires: (1) no pacing or mid-flight correction, since the send happens once and an error is permanent — which moves all quality control before the send and makes pre-send proofing the critical control; (2) no third-party counts, so discrepancy resolution has no independent reference and trust substitutes; (3) bespoke reporting per sponsor rather than a standard format, because these sponsors are often not agencies and ask for what they want; (4) a one-person function, so process must be embodied in software rather than in roles; and (5) copy read in the writer's voice, which makes creative approval an editorial as well as an operational matter.

## Target Customer
Revenue operations leadership, publishers, newsletter ad networks, and ad operations platform vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Campaign operations is process-heavy because errors are expensive, and here every error is permanent at send. Moving all control before the send and generating bespoke sponsor reports from one dataset is the adaptation.
