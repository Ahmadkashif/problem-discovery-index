# The Role That Is All Mechanical Work

**Niche:** [[niches/newsletter-media/the-ad-ops-manager/profile|The Ad Operations Manager]]
**Industry:** [[industries/newsletter-media|Newsletter Media]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** The entire revenue line passes through one person doing work a system should do.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #automation #worker-facing #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #large-language-models #descriptive-statistics #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stop one person assembling every sponsor's report by hand in whatever format that sponsor asked for — and whoever automates it removes the bottleneck the whole revenue line runs through.

## The Problem
The role consists of: checking what is booked, chasing creative that has not arrived, formatting copy into the template, proofing that each sponsor appears in the right issue in the right position with the right link, tracking delivery, and then producing a report per sponsor in that sponsor's preferred format. Every part of it is deterministic. All of it is done by hand, under weekly deadline, by one person who is also the only holder of the process.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The role emerged as a coping mechanism rather than as a designed function, so it absorbed whatever was not automated — a person hired to hold a process together will hold it together, which removes the pressure to systematise it. The publisher is small and the role is one headcount. Tooling for this exists only for web publishing. And the work is invisible when it goes right.

## What to Build
Automate the deterministic parts and protect the judgement. Generate the insertion from the booking rather than by copying text, which is the core and eliminates the highest-consequence error. Chase creative automatically against the deadline, since persistence rather than judgement is what that task requires. Proof automatically against the booking — right sponsor, right issue, right position, right link, right tracking — because a checklist executed by a system does not get tired. Generate each sponsor's report in their required format from one underlying dataset, as reformatting the same numbers many ways is the single largest time cost. Maintain a per-sponsor requirements record, so the formats live in a system rather than in one person's memory. Produce the pre-send checklist and enforce it, which is what makes absence survivable. Track delivery and make-goods, since these are currently agreed verbally and forgotten. Give sales live availability, removing the constant interruption. Flag anything unusual for human attention rather than requiring everything to be reviewed. Measure the role's hours by task, because nobody knows where they go. And document the process as a by-product of running it, since the continuity risk here is real and unaddressed.

## Target Customer
Revenue operations leadership and the managers themselves, publishers whose revenue passes through one person, newsletter platforms, and ad operations tooling vendors.

## Impact If Built
A person hired to hold a process together will hold it together, which removes the pressure to systematise it. Generating insertions and per-sponsor reports from one booking record automates nearly the whole role and makes the publisher's absence risk survivable.
