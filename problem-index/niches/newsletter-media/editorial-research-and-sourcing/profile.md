# Editorial Research & Sourcing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/newsletter-media|Newsletter Media]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to surface what is genuinely new and genuinely relevant to one specific audience from a stable source set — and whoever learns relevance from the publication's own archive replaces the hours that precede every issue.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$120M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — feed readers and manual filtering
**Target Buyer:** Editorial leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — relevance learned from the archive

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The sources are stable, the audience's interests are known from years of coverage and engagement, and the question every morning is the same: what happened that these particular readers should hear about. It is a filtering and ranking problem over a bounded corpus with an excellent training signal — the publication's own archive and the engagement each item generated — and it is performed manually from scratch every day.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Feed readers, alerts, social monitoring and a set of bookmarks. The gaps: relevance not learned from the archive; novelty against prior coverage not checked; engagement outcomes not fed back into sourcing; the discarded material not retained; and source coverage never reviewed.
