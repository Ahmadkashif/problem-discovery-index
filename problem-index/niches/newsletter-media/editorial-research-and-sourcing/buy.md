# Media Monitoring and Wire Filtering

**Industry:** [[industries/newsletter-media|Newsletter Media]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Media monitoring and wire services solved high-volume filtering for newsrooms and corporate communications, and neither knows what one small audience cares about.
**Tags:** #bert #word-embeddings #k-nearest-neighbors #evaluation-metrics #transformers #confidence-intervals #gradient-boosting #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to surface what is genuinely new and genuinely relevant to one specific audience from a stable source set — and whoever learns relevance from the publication's own archive replaces the hours that precede every issue.

## The Problem
Media monitoring is a substantial industry: broad source coverage, keyword and entity filtering, deduplication, alerting and analysis, sold to communications teams and newsrooms. Wire services pre-filter for newsworthiness at scale. Both work. Neither has any way to encode "what a newsletter about a specific niche, read by a specific kind of reader, should cover today", which is the entire job.

## What Already Exists
Media monitoring platforms with broad coverage; entity and keyword filtering; story clustering and deduplication; wire service curation; and alerting and analysis tooling.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a relevance function specific to one publication. It requires: (1) relevance learned from a publication's own archive and engagement rather than configured with keywords, which is the substantive contribution — keyword filters are why these tools are abandoned; (2) novelty judged against the publication's coverage rather than against the general record; (3) a ranked list for a human rather than an alert, since the writer decides and an alert stream is noise; (4) engagement feedback as a training signal, which monitoring products have no access to and the publisher does; and (5) an audience of one editorial operation, so the model can be small and specific rather than general.

## Target Customer
Editorial leadership and writers, newsletter platforms, media monitoring vendors, and multi-title publishers.

## Impact If Solved
Monitoring products configure relevance with keywords, which is why writers abandon them. Learning it from the archive and the engagement record is the difference between a noisy alert stream and a usable morning list.
