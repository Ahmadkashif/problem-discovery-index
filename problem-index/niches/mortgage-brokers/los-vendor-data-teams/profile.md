# Loan Origination System Data Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-brokers|Mortgage Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The loan origination platform vendors run the systems most originations are processed in, seeing application through funding for a very large share of the market.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 data scientists, analysts, and product data staff
**Output:** In-product analytics, benchmarking, workflow and condition management, market volume reporting
**Proprietary data:** Application-to-funding pipelines across most of the origination market, including pull-through, cycle time, and fallout
**Clock:** Rate lock and closing commitments
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Product
