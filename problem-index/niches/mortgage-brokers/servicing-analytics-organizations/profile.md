# Mortgage Servicing Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-brokers|Mortgage Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Servicers' analytics organizations model prepayment, default, and recapture across large loan portfolios, valuing servicing rights and deciding which borrowers to solicit for a refinance.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 quantitative analysts, modellers, and portfolio staff
**Output:** Prepayment and default models, servicing rights valuation, recapture targeting, loss mitigation analytics
**Proprietary data:** Loan-level payment behaviour across millions of borrowers over decades
**Clock:** Monthly remittance cycles and rate-driven prepayment waves
**Buyer:** Chief Risk Officer / Head of Portfolio Analytics
