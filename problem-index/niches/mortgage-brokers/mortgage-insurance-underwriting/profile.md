# Mortgage Insurance Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-brokers|Mortgage Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Mortgage insurers underwrite the credit risk on low-down-payment loans, setting the eligibility rules and premium rates that determine which borrowers can buy with limited equity.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 credit analysts, actuaries, and data scientists
**Output:** Underwriting guidelines and eligibility, risk-based pricing, portfolio and reserve analysis, market credit research
**Proprietary data:** Insured loan performance across decades and multiple cycles, including the crisis period, at loan level
**Clock:** Rate filings and continuous portfolio monitoring
**Buyer:** Chief Risk Officer / Chief Data Officer
