# Mortgage Quality Control & Compliance Audit

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-brokers|Mortgage Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Independent quality control firms re-underwrite samples of closed loans against investor guidelines and regulatory requirements, producing the findings that determine whether a lender faces repurchase demands or enforcement.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 underwriters, auditors, and compliance analysts
**Output:** Post-close quality control findings, defect rate reporting, repurchase risk assessment, regulatory examination readiness
**Proprietary data:** Loan defect patterns across many originators and investor programmes, joined to repurchase outcomes
**Clock:** Investor-mandated quality control cycles and examination schedules
**Buyer:** Practice leader / VP of Operations
