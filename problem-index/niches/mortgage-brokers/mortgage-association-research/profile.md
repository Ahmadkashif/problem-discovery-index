# Mortgage Association Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-brokers|Mortgage Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The mortgage associations publish origination forecasts, cost-to-originate studies, and the performance surveys the industry benchmarks against, and analyse the regulatory changes that shape broker economics.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 economists, researchers, and policy analysts
**Output:** Origination forecasts, cost and profitability studies, application indices, regulatory analysis
**Proprietary data:** Member-contributed cost and volume series, including long-running weekly indices
**Clock:** Weekly index publication and quarterly forecasts
**Buyer:** Chief Economist — member funded
