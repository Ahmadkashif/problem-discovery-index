Boutique advisers value and sell mortgage originators and servicing portfolios, pricing them on production consistency, loan officer retention, and servicing rights.

**Output:** Valuations, offering materials, transaction execution
**Proprietary data:** Private transaction comparables and servicing portfolio pricing
**Clock:** Transaction timelines and rate cycles
**Buyer:** Managing partner
