# Product & Pricing Engines

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-brokers|Mortgage Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Product and pricing engines hold every wholesale lender's rate sheet, eligibility rules, and adjustment grids, and return — for a specific borrower, property, and loan structure — which lenders will lend and at what price. Pass 1 identifies exactly this gap at the broker level: decision intelligence around lender selection and rate timing is almost entirely absent, and the engines are where it would live.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 pricing analysts, eligibility content staff, data engineers, and data scientists
**Output:** Real-time eligibility and pricing across the lender panel, lock desk support, margin management, hedging and secondary market analytics
**Proprietary data:** Rate sheets, adjustment grids, eligibility rules, searches, and executed locks across a very large share of originations — the only view of what lenders actually price and what actually gets locked
**Clock:** Intraday rate movements and lock expiry, the fastest clock in mortgage
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Product
