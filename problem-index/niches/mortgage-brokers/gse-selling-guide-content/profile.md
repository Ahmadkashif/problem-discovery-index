# Investor Guideline Content & Interpretation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-brokers|Mortgage Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The government-sponsored enterprises maintain the selling guides that define what a saleable loan is — thousands of pages of eligibility, documentation, and underwriting requirements that every lender's own guidelines are derived from and that change several times a year.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 credit policy staff, guideline authors, and analysts
**Output:** Selling and servicing guides, eligibility rules, automated underwriting logic, policy announcements
**Proprietary data:** Loan performance across the entire conventional market, joined to the guideline versions loans were originated under
**Clock:** Announcement effective dates every lender must implement
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — chartered entity
