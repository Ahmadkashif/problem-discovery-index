# Mortgage Market Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-brokers|Mortgage Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Research firms track origination volume, lender market share, and channel mix, and publish the rankings and forecasts lenders and investors plan against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 analysts, economists, and data staff
**Output:** Origination volume and share estimates, channel and product mix analysis, forecasts, lender rankings
**Proprietary data:** Assembled estimates from public disclosure filings, servicer data, and survey work
**Clock:** Quarterly reporting and forecast cycles
**Buyer:** Head of Research
