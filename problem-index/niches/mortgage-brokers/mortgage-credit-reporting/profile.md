# Mortgage Credit Reporting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-brokers|Mortgage Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Mortgage credit reporting agencies merge and reconcile bureau files, verify employment and income, and supply the verified borrower data every loan file is built on.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 data operations, verification, and product staff
**Output:** Merged credit reports, verification products, supplements and reissues, borrower data reconciliation
**Proprietary data:** Reconciliation logic across bureaus plus verification outcomes; the underlying files belong to the bureaus
**Clock:** Application processing and credit report validity windows
**Buyer:** VP of Product / Chief Data Officer
