# Loan Quality & Fraud Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-brokers|Mortgage Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Analytics vendors score loan files for misrepresentation and defect risk before funding, flagging income, occupancy, and identity inconsistencies that would otherwise surface as a repurchase demand.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-200 data scientists, fraud analysts, and content specialists
**Output:** Fraud and defect risk scores, red flag detection, verification recommendations, industry risk reporting
**Proprietary data:** Loan application data across many originators joined to confirmed defect and fraud outcomes
**Clock:** Pre-funding decisioning
**Buyer:** Chief Product Officer / VP of Analytics
