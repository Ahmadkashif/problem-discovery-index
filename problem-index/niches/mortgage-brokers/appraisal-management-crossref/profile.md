# Appraisal Management & Review

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mortgage-brokers|Mortgage Brokers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Appraisal management companies assign valuations and run the review function that decides whether a completed appraisal supports the loan.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 review appraisers, quality control staff, and analysts
**Output:** Appraisal review findings, revision requests, quality scoring, vendor management
**Proprietary data:** Completed appraisals with review findings and downstream loan outcomes
**Clock:** Loan closing dates
**Buyer:** Chief Appraiser / VP of Quality
