# Large Grower Group Agronomy & Trialing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/greenhouse-horticulture|Greenhouse Horticulture]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup

## What They Do
The consolidated growers — Costa Farms, Metrolina, Color Point and their peers — run internal agronomy and trialing groups that evaluate varieties, set production protocols, and standardize practice across many acres and multiple sites.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 agronomists, trial staff, and production technologists
**Output:** Production protocols, variety evaluations, internal crop scheduling standards
**Proprietary data:** Production records across many acres and years, joined to actual yield, quality, and shrink — the outcome data the breeders and climate vendors lack
**Clock:** Crop cycles and retail programme commitments
**Buyer:** No external buyer — internal cost centre
