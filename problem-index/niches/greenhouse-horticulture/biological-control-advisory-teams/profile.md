# Biological Control Field Advisory Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/greenhouse-horticulture|Greenhouse Horticulture]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Koppert, Biobest, and the beneficial-insect suppliers employ field advisors who walk customer greenhouses, assess pest pressure, and design the release programmes growers run all season. The advice is the reason growers buy from one supplier over another; the insects themselves are close to interchangeable.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-400 field advisors, entomologists, and technical staff per major supplier
**Output:** Release programmes, scouting assessments, IPM strategy for individual greenhouses
**Proprietary data:** Pest pressure and biological control outcomes observed across thousands of greenhouses, by crop, region, and week — an industry-wide pest surveillance network operated by a private company
**Clock:** Pest population dynamics; a thrips outbreak doubles on a fixed biological schedule
**Buyer:** Technical Director / Head of Crop Advisory
