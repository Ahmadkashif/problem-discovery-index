# Cannabis Compliance Testing Laboratories

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/greenhouse-horticulture|Greenhouse Horticulture]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State-licensed laboratories run the mandatory panel — potency, pesticide residue, microbial contamination, heavy metals, residual solvent — that every cannabis batch must pass before it can legally be sold. The certificate of analysis is not advice about the product; it is the document that decides whether the product exists commercially.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-60 analytical chemists, microbiologists, and data staff per licensed lab
**Output:** Certificates of analysis, failure investigations, stability and shelf-life findings
**Proprietary data:** Every result the lab has produced, joined to cultivator, cultivar, and growing method — a record of which growing operations produce contamination and which cultivars carry what, held nowhere else in that form
**Clock:** Absolute — a harvested batch is unsellable inventory, losing value daily, until the panel clears
**Buyer:** Laboratory director or owner-operator
