# Independent Horticultural Consultants

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/greenhouse-horticulture|Greenhouse Horticulture]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Individual consultants — often retired head growers — advise greenhouse operations on climate strategy, crop scheduling, and problem diagnosis. They are the industry's repository of exactly the tacit knowledge Pass 1 identifies: setpoint intuition that experienced growers cannot articulate to junior staff.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1-5 people, usually one
**Output:** Site visits, climate strategy, crop troubleshooting
**Proprietary data:** A career of observed crop responses, entirely in one person's head
**Clock:** Crop cycles
**Buyer:** The consultant
