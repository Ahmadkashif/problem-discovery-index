# Greenhouse Engineering & Design Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/greenhouse-horticulture|Greenhouse Horticulture]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Engineering firms design greenhouse structures and their environmental systems — glazing, heating, screening, lighting, irrigation — sizing everything against the crop, climate, and energy costs of a specific site.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 engineers and designers
**Output:** Facility designs, energy and light models, capital cost estimates
**Proprietary data:** Design and as-built performance records across projects, rarely fed back
**Clock:** Construction schedules
**Buyer:** Principal / Engineering Director
