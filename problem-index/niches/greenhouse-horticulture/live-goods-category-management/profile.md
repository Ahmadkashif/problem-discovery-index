# Live Goods Category Management & Vendor-Managed Inventory

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/greenhouse-horticulture|Greenhouse Horticulture]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The service organizations that manage live plant inventory inside big-box garden centres — restocking, culling, and merchandising on behalf of growers — sit between production and the consumer, and see sell-through and shrink at store-week-SKU resolution across thousands of stores.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 category analysts and planners, above a large field merchandising workforce
**Output:** Category plans, replenishment recommendations, shrink and sell-through reporting
**Proprietary data:** Point-of-sale and shrink by store, week, and item — the only view of what live goods actually sell, as opposed to what growers shipped
**Clock:** The spring season, which is short, weather-driven, and unforgiving
**Buyer:** VP of Category Management
