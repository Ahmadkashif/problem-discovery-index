# Horticultural Research Institutes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/greenhouse-horticulture|Greenhouse Horticulture]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The industry's research foundations fund and disseminate horticultural research — production, pest management, and business practice — on behalf of member growers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 3-15 research programme staff
**Output:** Funded research summaries, production guidance, industry surveys
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated research library and member survey data
**Clock:** Annual grant and conference cycles
**Buyer:** Research director — member funded
