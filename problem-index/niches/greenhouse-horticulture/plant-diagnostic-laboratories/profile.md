# Plant Diagnostic Laboratories

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/greenhouse-horticulture|Greenhouse Horticulture]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Diagnostic labs identify the pathogen behind a crop problem — botrytis, powdery mildew, pythium, a virus, or a physiological disorder that resembles one — from submitted tissue. The diagnosis determines whether a grower treats, discards a crop, or quarantines a range.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 8-40 plant pathologists, diagnosticians, and technicians
**Output:** Diagnostic reports, pathogen identifications, management recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated diagnoses tied to crop, symptom, season, and region — the closest thing that exists to a disease surveillance record for the industry
**Clock:** Urgent; an unidentified pathogen spreads through a greenhouse range while the sample is in transit
**Buyer:** Lab director — many are university extension units on state funding
