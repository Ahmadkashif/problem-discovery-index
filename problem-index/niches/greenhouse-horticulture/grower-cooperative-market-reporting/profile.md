# Horticultural Market Price Reporting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/greenhouse-horticulture|Greenhouse Horticulture]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Price reporting services and grower cooperatives publish the wholesale price and volume series for cut flowers, potted crops, and nursery stock that growers and buyers negotiate against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 market reporters and analysts
**Output:** Price indices, volume reports, seasonal market commentary
**Proprietary data:** Reported transaction prices contributed by members
**Clock:** Weekly reporting against a compressed selling season
**Buyer:** Director of Market Information — often cooperative funded
