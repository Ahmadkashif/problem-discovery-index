# Climate Computer Vendor Agronomy Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/greenhouse-horticulture|Greenhouse Horticulture]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The agronomy and data groups at Priva, Ridder, and Argus support growers running their climate control systems, and sit on minute-resolution temperature, humidity, CO2, and light logs from thousands of greenhouses. Pass 1 records that none of these systems offers predictive optimization — the data is collected and the setpoints stay manual.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 agronomists, control specialists, and data staff per vendor
**Output:** Setpoint strategies, commissioning support, control system configuration
**Proprietary data:** Continuous climate telemetry across a large installed base — unmatched in resolution, and largely unjoined to crop outcomes
**Clock:** Growing season and energy price cycles
**Buyer:** Head of Agronomy / Director of Digital
