# Horticultural Analytical Laboratories

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/greenhouse-horticulture|Greenhouse Horticulture]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Commercial laboratories analyze substrate, irrigation water, and plant tissue for growers, reporting nutrient concentrations, pH, and electrical conductivity against sufficiency ranges. The grower adjusts the fertigation recipe on the result.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 chemists, technicians, and agronomists per lab
**Output:** Analysis reports with interpretation against crop-specific sufficiency ranges
**Proprietary data:** Decades of results tied to crop, substrate, region, and season — though reported against published standards rather than against the lab's own history
**Clock:** Turnaround time; a nutrient problem must be corrected within days of sampling
**Buyer:** Laboratory director or owner
