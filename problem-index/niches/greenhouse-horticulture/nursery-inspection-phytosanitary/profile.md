# Nursery Inspection & Phytosanitary Certification

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/greenhouse-horticulture|Greenhouse Horticulture]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State department of agriculture inspectors examine nursery stock and issue the phytosanitary certificates required to move plants across state and national borders. Without the certificate the shipment does not travel.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 inspectors and plant health specialists per state programme
**Output:** Inspection findings, phytosanitary certificates, quarantine and interception actions
**Proprietary data:** Inspection and pest interception records by nursery, crop, and origin — the record of which operations ship clean and which do not
**Clock:** Shipping schedules; a certificate delayed is a truck that does not leave
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — state appropriation
