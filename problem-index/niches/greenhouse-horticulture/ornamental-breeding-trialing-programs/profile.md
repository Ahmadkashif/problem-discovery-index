# Ornamental Breeding & Variety Trialing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/greenhouse-horticulture|Greenhouse Horticulture]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Ball, Syngenta Flowers, Dümmen Orange, and the breeding houses develop ornamental genetics and trial them across many locations and seasons before release, producing the culture guides that tell growers how to schedule and finish each variety.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 breeders, trial managers, and technical staff per major breeder
**Output:** New varieties, culture guides, scheduling and finishing recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Multi-year, multi-location trial records of genetics against environment — the deepest genotype-by-environment dataset in ornamental horticulture
**Clock:** Breeding and catalogue cycles, annual and fixed
**Buyer:** Head of Breeding Technology / R&D Director
