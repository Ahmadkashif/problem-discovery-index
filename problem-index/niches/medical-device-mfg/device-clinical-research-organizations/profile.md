# Device Clinical Research Organizations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/medical-device-mfg|Medical Device Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Contract research organizations design and run the clinical studies device manufacturers need for approval and for post-market evidence, managing sites, data, and the statistical analysis regulators will scrutinize.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-2,000 clinical operations staff, biostatisticians, and data managers
**Output:** Study design and protocols, site management, clinical data management, statistical analysis and reports
**Proprietary data:** Study execution and enrolment performance across many trials, sites, and therapeutic areas
**Clock:** Enrolment timelines and regulatory submission deadlines
**Buyer:** Chief Operating Officer / VP of Data
