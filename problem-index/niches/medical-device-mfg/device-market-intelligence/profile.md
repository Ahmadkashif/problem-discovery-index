# Medical Device Market Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/medical-device-mfg|Medical Device Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Specialist research firms size device markets by segment and geography, track procedure volumes and competitive share, and sell the forecasts manufacturers plan portfolios and acquisitions against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 analysts and researchers
**Output:** Market sizing and share estimates, procedure volume forecasts, competitive landscape reports, pipeline tracking
**Proprietary data:** Assembled estimates from procedure data, company disclosures, and clinician surveys
**Clock:** Annual and quarterly publication cycles
**Buyer:** Managing Director / Head of Research
