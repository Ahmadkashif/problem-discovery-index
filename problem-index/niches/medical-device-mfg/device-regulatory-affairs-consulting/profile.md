# Medical Device Regulatory Affairs Consulting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/medical-device-mfg|Medical Device Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Regulatory consultancies build the submissions that let a device be sold — clearance and approval dossiers, predicate and classification strategy, clinical evidence plans, and the technical documentation the European regime demands. Nothing ships until this work is accepted, and the strategy chosen at the outset determines whether market entry takes nine months or four years.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-800 regulatory scientists, clinical affairs staff, engineers, and technical writers
**Output:** Clearance and approval submissions, classification and predicate strategy, technical files, agency meeting preparation, post-market regulatory strategy
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated submission precedent and agency interaction history — which predicates were accepted, which questions were asked, which strategies cleared and which stalled
**Clock:** Agency review clocks, launch commitments, and the European transition deadlines that have made certification capacity the binding constraint across the industry
**Buyer:** Practice leader / Managing Director
