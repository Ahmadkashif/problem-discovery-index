# Reimbursement & Health Technology Assessment Strategy

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/medical-device-mfg|Medical Device Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Reimbursement consultancies determine how a new device will be paid for — securing a code, establishing coverage with payers, and building the economic evidence that justifies payment. A cleared device with no coverage and no code has no market, and this work runs on a longer clock than the regulatory submission it follows.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-400 health economists, coding specialists, coverage analysts, and policy staff
**Output:** Coding strategy and applications, coverage dossiers and payer submissions, economic models and budget impact analyses, evidence generation plans
**Proprietary data:** Coverage decisions and payer responses across many technologies and plans — the record of what evidence actually moved a coverage policy
**Clock:** Annual code application cycles and payer policy review calendars, both fixed
**Buyer:** Practice leader / Managing Director
