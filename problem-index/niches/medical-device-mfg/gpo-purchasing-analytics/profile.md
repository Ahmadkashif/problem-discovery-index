# Group Purchasing Organization Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/medical-device-mfg|Medical Device Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Group purchasing organizations negotiate device and supply contracts on behalf of thousands of hospitals, and their analytics groups see purchasing volume, price variation, and utilization across the provider market.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 category analysts, clinical value analysts, and data staff
**Output:** Contract negotiation analysis, price benchmarking, clinical value assessments, utilization and standardization studies
**Proprietary data:** Purchase order and contract data across thousands of hospitals — the only view of what devices actually sell for
**Clock:** Contract renewal cycles and member budget seasons
**Buyer:** Chief Analytics Officer / VP of Category Management
