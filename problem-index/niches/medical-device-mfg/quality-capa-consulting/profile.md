**Parent Industry:** [[industries/medical-device-mfg|Medical Device Manufacturing]]

## What They Do
Quality consultancies remediate manufacturers after inspection findings — rebuilding corrective action systems, complaint handling, and design controls under consent decrees and warning letters where the alternative is losing the ability to ship.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-250 quality engineers, auditors, and remediation specialists
**Output:** Gap assessments, remediation plans, corrective action systems, inspection readiness, response letters
**Proprietary data:** Inspection finding patterns and remediation outcomes across many manufacturers
**Clock:** Inspection response deadlines and consent decree milestones
**Buyer:** Practice leader
