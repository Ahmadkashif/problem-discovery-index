# Device Review & Enforcement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/medical-device-mfg|Medical Device Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The device centre reviews every submission, inspects manufacturing sites, monitors adverse event reporting, and decides what may be marketed — the body whose behaviour the entire regulatory consulting industry exists to predict.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1,000-4,000 reviewers, engineers, scientists, and inspectors
**Output:** Clearance and approval decisions, guidance documents, inspection findings, recall classifications, safety communications
**Proprietary data:** Every submission ever filed with its review correspondence, plus the complete inspection and adverse event record
**Clock:** Statutory review clocks
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — federal agency
