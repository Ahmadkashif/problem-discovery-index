# Device Standards Development Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/medical-device-mfg|Medical Device Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Standards bodies write the consensus standards regulators recognize — sterilization, biocompatibility, electrical safety, software lifecycle, risk management — and conformity to them is how most submissions demonstrate safety.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 standards staff and technical committee support
**Output:** Consensus standards, technical reports, interpretation responses, harmonization work
**Proprietary data:** Standards corpus with full development history — every proposal, comment, and committee resolution
**Clock:** Standard revision cycles and regulatory recognition dates
**Buyer:** Executive director / VP of Standards
