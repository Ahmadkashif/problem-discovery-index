# Device Testing & Certification Laboratories

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/medical-device-mfg|Medical Device Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Testing laboratories perform the biocompatibility, sterilization validation, electrical safety, and performance testing that regulatory submissions require, and notified bodies certify quality systems and technical files against the European regime.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-800 test engineers, toxicologists, microbiologists, and certification auditors
**Output:** Test reports supporting submissions, sterilization and biocompatibility validation, certification decisions and audit findings
**Proprietary data:** Test outcomes across many device types and materials, plus accumulated audit findings across manufacturers
**Clock:** Submission deadlines and, in the European regime, certification capacity that has become the industry's binding constraint
**Buyer:** Laboratory or certification body director
