# Post-Market Surveillance & Complaint Handling

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/medical-device-mfg|Medical Device Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Surveillance providers and outsourced complaint handling operations triage device complaints, determine reportability, file adverse event reports on statutory deadlines, and produce the periodic safety reports regulators require.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-800 complaint analysts, regulatory reporting specialists, and safety engineers
**Output:** Complaint investigations and reportability decisions, adverse event filings, signal detection, periodic safety update reports
**Proprietary data:** Complaint and adverse event histories across many manufacturers and device types, joined to reportability determinations
**Clock:** Statutory adverse event reporting deadlines measured in days
**Buyer:** VP of Quality and Regulatory / Chief Safety Officer
