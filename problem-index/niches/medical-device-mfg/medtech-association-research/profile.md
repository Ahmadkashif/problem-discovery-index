## What They Do
The medtech association analyses regulatory and reimbursement policy for member manufacturers, publishes industry economic research, and coordinates the sector's response to regulatory change.

**Output:** Policy analysis and comment, industry economic research, regulatory guidance interpretation, member surveys
**Proprietary data:** Member survey data and accumulated policy positions
**Clock:** Comment periods and legislative sessions
**Buyer:** VP of Research and Policy — member funded
