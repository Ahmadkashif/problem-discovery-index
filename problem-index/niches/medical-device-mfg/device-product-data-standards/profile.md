# Device Product Data & Identification

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/medical-device-mfg|Medical Device Manufacturing]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Product data providers and the identification registries maintain the device identifiers, attributes, and catalogue data that hospitals order against and that regulators require for traceability.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 data stewards, taxonomists, and integration engineers
**Output:** Device identification and attribute data, catalogue synchronization, contract and pricing data exchange, recall traceability support
**Proprietary data:** Reconciled product and pricing catalogues across manufacturers and provider systems
**Clock:** Contract cycles and regulatory identification deadlines
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Head of Product
