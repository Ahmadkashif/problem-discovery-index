# Vehicle History Report Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-dealers-independent|Independent Auto Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Carfax and AutoCheck assemble a vehicle's history from tens of thousands of independent sources — state title and registration records, service shops, insurers, auctions, police reports, fleet operators — and sell the resulting report. Independent dealers buy it to price inventory and to reassure buyers; consumers buy it before purchase. The report is the entire product.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-800 data acquisition staff, analysts, and engineers
**Output:** The vehicle history report and the branding and valuation-impact analytics sold alongside it
**Proprietary data:** Data supply relationships built over decades with sources that have no obligation to share, plus the accumulated record of how history events actually affected resale price
**Clock:** No hard external deadline — reports are produced on demand
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Product
