# Listing Marketplace Pricing Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-dealers-independent|Independent Auto Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The data science groups behind CarGurus' Instant Market Value, Cars.com's pricing tools, and Autotrader's market analytics. They model fair market price by VIN configuration and geography from live listing inventory and observed time-to-sell, then surface deal ratings to shoppers and pricing guidance to dealers. Dealers pay for listing placement and leads; the analytics are the differentiator rather than the line item.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-200 data scientists, analysts, and product staff
**Output:** Market value estimates, deal ratings, dealer pricing and inventory analytics
**Proprietary data:** Live listing inventory with price change history and time-to-sell across a large share of US used inventory, plus shopper engagement behaviour
**Clock:** Continuous; no third-party deadline governs publication
**Buyer:** VP of Data Science / Chief Product Officer
