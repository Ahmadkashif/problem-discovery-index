# Dealer Compliance & F&I Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-dealers-independent|Independent Auto Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Boutique firms advising dealers on FTC Safeguards Rule compliance, the CARS Rule, advertising review, and F&I process audits. They produce written compliance assessments and training, and defend dealers under state attorney general inquiry.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 2-8
**Output:** Compliance assessments, policy documentation, audit findings
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated audit findings across dealer clients
**Clock:** Regulatory effective dates and enforcement inquiries
**Buyer:** Principal consultant
