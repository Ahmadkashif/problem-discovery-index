# Dealer Association Economic Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-dealers-independent|Independent Auto Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
NADA's economics and industry analysis group and NIADA's research function publish dealership financial profiles, sales forecasts, and used vehicle market commentary, and supply the economic analysis behind legislative and regulatory advocacy. Output is a member benefit and a public-facing credibility asset.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-25 economists, analysts, and research staff
**Output:** Dealership financial profiles, market forecasts, regulatory impact analyses
**Proprietary data:** Member-contributed dealership financial statement data, aggregated over decades
**Clock:** Monthly and annual publication schedule, plus regulatory comment periods
**Buyer:** Chief Economist / VP of Research
