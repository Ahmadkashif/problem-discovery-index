# Dealer Management System Vendor Data Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-dealers-independent|Independent Auto Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The data and analytics groups inside dealer management system vendors — CDK Global, Reynolds & Reynolds, Frazer, DealerCenter, Dealertrack. They build benchmarking and reporting products on the transaction data flowing through their systems and sell derived data access to third parties.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 data engineers, analysts, and product staff
**Output:** Dealer benchmarking reports, derived data feeds, operational analytics modules
**Proprietary data:** Deal-level transaction, service, and inventory data across thousands of dealerships
**Clock:** Software release cycles, set internally
**Buyer:** VP of Data Products / Chief Technology Officer
