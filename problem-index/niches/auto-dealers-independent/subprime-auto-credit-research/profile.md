# Subprime Auto Credit Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-dealers-independent|Independent Auto Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The credit risk and portfolio analytics groups at the lenders that finance independent dealer sales — Credit Acceptance, Westlake Financial, Exeter, Santander Consumer. They build scorecards, set advance rates by vehicle and borrower, monitor vintage performance, and price securitizations. Their models determine which deals an independent dealer can actually fund.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 credit analysts, modellers, and portfolio staff
**Output:** Scorecards, advance rate policy, vintage and loss forecasting, securitization analytics
**Proprietary data:** Loan performance across millions of deep-subprime borrowers joined to vehicle, dealer, and collateral recovery outcomes
**Clock:** Securitization calendars and regulatory reporting cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Credit Officer / SVP Portfolio Analytics
