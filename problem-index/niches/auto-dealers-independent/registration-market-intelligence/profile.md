# Vehicle Registration & Market Intelligence Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-dealers-independent|Independent Auto Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
S&P Global Mobility and Experian Automotive licence state registration and title data, normalize it to VIN-level vehicle configuration, and sell market share, vehicle population, loyalty, and forecast products. Manufacturers, lenders, insurers, parts suppliers, and dealer groups buy it because it is the only measurement of what is actually registered and on the road.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-800 analysts, forecasters, data engineers, and consultants
**Output:** Registration analytics, vehicle-in-operation counts, share and loyalty studies, production and demand forecasts
**Proprietary data:** State registration and title feeds under long-standing licences, normalized to a VIN decode and vehicle configuration taxonomy built over decades
**Clock:** Monthly and quarterly data releases, client planning and forecast cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Automotive Insights / Head of Data Products
