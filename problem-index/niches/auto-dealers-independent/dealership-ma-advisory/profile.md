# Dealership Valuation & M&A Advisory

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-dealers-independent|Independent Auto Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Kerrigan Advisors, Haig Partners, and Presidio Group advise on dealership sales and publish the quarterly blue sky multiple reports that set price expectations across the sector. The published research is both a genuine analytical product and the marketing engine for the advisory practice.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-25 including research staff
**Output:** Blue sky multiple reports, valuation opinions, sell-side transaction analyses
**Proprietary data:** Completed transaction terms and multiples that are not publicly reported anywhere
**Clock:** Quarterly publication and transaction closing timelines
**Buyer:** Managing Director / Head of Research
