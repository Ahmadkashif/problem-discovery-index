# National Title & Odometer Data Administration

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-dealers-independent|Independent Auto Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
AAMVA operates the National Motor Vehicle Title Information System, the federally mandated database through which states, insurers, and salvage operators report title brands, total loss declarations, and odometer readings. Access is sold to data providers and consumers; the system exists to prevent title washing and odometer fraud.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-200 across program administration, data quality, and standards
**Output:** Title brand and odometer verification, state reporting compliance analysis
**Proprietary data:** The consolidated national title and brand record — a dataset that exists nowhere else and that no commercial party could assemble
**Clock:** Statutory state reporting deadlines and federal compliance requirements
**Buyer:** Program Director — a member-governed non-profit rather than a commercial buyer
