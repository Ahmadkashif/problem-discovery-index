# Buy-Here-Pay-Here Portfolio Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-dealers-independent|Independent Auto Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The analytics functions inside the larger buy-here-pay-here operators and their capital providers — America's Car-Mart, regional BHPH chains, and the specialty lenders that buy their paper. They underwrite in-house financing, set down payment and term policy, and manage collections and repossession recovery on portfolios they originated themselves.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 analysts
**Output:** Underwriting policy, portfolio performance and recovery analyses
**Proprietary data:** Payment behaviour on borrowers invisible to conventional credit files, joined to vehicle and repossession outcomes
**Clock:** Lender covenant reporting and audit cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Portfolio Management
