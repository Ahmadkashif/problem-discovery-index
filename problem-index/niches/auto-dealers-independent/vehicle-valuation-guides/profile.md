# Vehicle Valuation Guide Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-dealers-independent|Independent Auto Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
J.D. Power (NADA Guides), Kelley Blue Book, Black Book, and Manheim Market Report publish the numbers the entire used vehicle economy transacts against. Analysts ingest auction results, retail listings, and transaction feeds, then apply editorial judgment to publish wholesale and retail values by VIN configuration, refreshed as often as daily. Lenders set advance rates against them, insurers settle against them, lessors set residuals against them, and dealers buy and price against them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 valuation analysts, economists, and data scientists
**Output:** The published value guide and residual forecasts — the licensed product
**Proprietary data:** Wholesale auction transaction history at scale, joined to retail listing and sale data, plus decades of the publisher's own forecast record
**Clock:** Daily and weekly publication cadence, lease-end residual setting calendars, and lender policy refresh cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Analytics Officer / VP of Valuation Products
