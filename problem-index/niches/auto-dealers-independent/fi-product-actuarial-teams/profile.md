# F&I Product Actuarial & Claims Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-dealers-independent|Independent Auto Dealers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The actuarial and claims analytics groups at vehicle service contract and GAP administrators — Assurant, Endurance, APCO, Safe-Guard. They price coverage by vehicle, component, term, and mileage band, file rates with state regulators, and analyze claims experience to reserve and reprice. Independent dealers sell these products at high margin, and the administrator carries the risk.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 actuaries, claims analysts, and pricing staff
**Output:** Rate filings, reserving analyses, product pricing and loss studies
**Proprietary data:** Component-level failure and claims cost experience by vehicle configuration, mileage, and term — a reliability dataset manufacturers themselves do not assemble across brands
**Clock:** State rate filing deadlines and statutory reserving cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Actuary / VP of Product Pricing
