# Self-Storage Contact Centres & AI Rental Agents

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-storage-operators|Self-Storage Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
They answer the phones, chats and gate intercoms of storage stores. OpenTech Alliance's OpenTech Live! supplies storage counsellors and AI voice, SMS and chat agents with human escalation. Storable sells the Contact Connect call-centre add-on and was hiring engineers to build AI agents for rentals, payments and gate codes (2026-09-23). CubeSmart runs its own sales centre and Public Storage a customer care centre. Operators pay per call, per store or per month.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Agents are production; any call-analytics team is under 10 per provider (estimate)
**Output:** Rentals, payments, resolved calls, call recordings
**Proprietary data:** Call recordings and outcomes, held for the operators the centre serves
**Clock:** Live calls (production)
**Buyer:** Head of contact centre or VP of operations
