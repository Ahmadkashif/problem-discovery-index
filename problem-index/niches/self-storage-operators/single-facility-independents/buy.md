# Small-Landlord Software Adapted to Month-to-Month Units

**Niche:** [[niches/self-storage-operators/single-facility-independents/profile|Single-Facility Independent Owners]]
**Industry:** [[industries/self-storage-operators|Self-Storage Operators]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Small-landlord rental software already handles listings, screening, online payments and rent reminders for millions of units. A storage owner needs the same simplicity for hundreds of small units, with no lease end date and a lien process instead of eviction.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #automation #compliance #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to lift a one-store owner's rent roll without a revenue manager on staff, and whoever grows the owner's same-store rental income most over twelve months takes the account.

## The Problem
A one-store owner wants the cheapest software that collects rent, rents units online and sends reminders. Storage software built for portfolios is too much; free tools are thin on pricing and liens.

## What Already Exists
Small-landlord property management software for apartments and homes, the market served under [[industries/property-management|Property Management]]: listings, applications, rent collection, maintenance requests and owner statements. OpenUnit already gives storage management software away free.

## The Customization Gap
Storage needs: a unit map by size, climate and access type rather than addresses; online move-in with lease signature, gate code and protection-plan choice in one flow; month-to-month billing with prorating and anniversary-based increases; state lien notices instead of eviction; integration with gate keypads and smart locks; and listing feeds to storage marketplaces. Pricing has to be built in rather than sold as an add-on, because the owner's main gap is rates, not paperwork.

## Target Customer
Owners of one to five stores, often family owners who built drive-up sites decades ago and run them with one manager.

## Impact If Solved
Moving an owner from paper or desktop software to online move-ins and automated reminders typically saves ~5–10 office hours a week (estimate). Built-in rate and increase rules can add ~2–3% to rental income (estimate), ~$10,000–15,000 a year at a $500,000 store.
