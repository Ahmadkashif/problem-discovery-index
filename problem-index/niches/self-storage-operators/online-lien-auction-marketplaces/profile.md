# Online Lien-Sale Auction Marketplaces

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-storage-operators|Self-Storage Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Websites where operators sell lien units to registered bidders. StorageTreasures is sold by OpenTech Alliance as its lien-auction product, and Lockerfox and Bid13 run independent marketplaces (search summary). Operators or buyers pay listing and buyer fees. Many states now let the online listing serve as the sale venue and part of the advertisement.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analytics staff per platform (estimate)
**Output:** Listings, bidder alerts, lien-law reference pages
**Proprietary data:** Listing photos and bid histories for every unit sold
**Clock:** Each unit's statutory sale date (production)
**Buyer:** Platform general manager
