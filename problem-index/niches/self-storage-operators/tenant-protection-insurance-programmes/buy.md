# Renters-Insurance Programmes Adapted to Storage Units

**Niche:** [[niches/self-storage-operators/tenant-protection-insurance-programmes/profile|Tenant Protection & Tenant Insurance Programmes]]
**Industry:** [[industries/self-storage-operators|Self-Storage Operators]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Apartment renters-insurance programmes already enrol residents at lease signing and track compliance across portfolios. Storage needs them reshaped for a unit, a month-to-month term and a fifty-state split between insurance and protection plans.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #feature-engineering #compliance #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to attach tenant protection or tenant insurance at move-in under each state's insurance rules, and whoever earns the most protection margin per occupied unit takes the account.

## The Problem
Operators want higher attachment and lower claims without building an insurance operation. Most storage programmes run on administrators' own systems and a checkbox in the facility software, with little analysis of who buys, who claims or which coverage level fits the unit.

## What Already Exists
Renters-insurance and deposit-alternative programmes sold through property managers enrol residents at lease signing, check proof of third-party coverage and analyse claims against screening attributes. They are profiled at [[niches/property-management/renters-insurance-program-underwriting/profile|Renters Insurance & Resident Programme Underwriting]] (42).

## The Customization Gap
Storage needs: a per-state switch between a licensed insurance product and an operator-run protection plan, with the licence or filing each requires; coverage sized to the unit (Storable already markets an insured-to-value feature that sets minimum coverage by unit size) rather than to a household; month-to-month billing and cancellation on move-out; and claims rules for stored goods (burglary, water, pests and mould excluded or not). Lien-sale interaction matters too: protection lapses when rent does, and a sold unit cannot be claimed. The enrolment script must also fit an unstaffed rental flow, where the tenant chooses coverage on a kiosk or phone.

## Target Customer
Administrators and carriers entering storage from apartments; operators choosing between an administrator's programme and their own plan.

## Impact If Solved
A programme that lifts attachment from ~50% to ~60% (estimate) at ~$12 a month adds ~$7,000 a year at a 500-unit store. It carries the apartment programmes' claims discipline without rebuilding it.
