# Self Storage Association & State Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-storage-operators|Self-Storage Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The national Self Storage Association and state associations lobby on lien and pricing law, sell standard leases and run research. The SSA's 2025 Self Storage Demand Study was led by the consultancy C+R Research and surveyed 3,456 households and 935 businesses. Earlier editions ran in 2007, 2013, 2017, 2020 and 2022 (search summary). The Illinois association sells its standard lease to members for $295. The California association and the SSA sponsored California's AB 498 on email lien notices.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Research commissioned to outside firms; association research staff under 10 (estimate)
**Output:** Demand studies, fact sheets, members' data portal, legislative tracking, standard leases
**Proprietary data:** Commissioned survey data and member-contributed figures
