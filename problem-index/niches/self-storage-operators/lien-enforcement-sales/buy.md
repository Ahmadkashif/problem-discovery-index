**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor is fighting to turn a delinquent unit back into money without a wrongful-sale claim. But the money comes from two places with different competitors: tenants who pay before the sale, where outreach and payment tools compete on past-due rent recovered per delinquent unit, and contents sold at the sale, where auction marketplaces compete on the share of the balance owed that each auctioned unit recovers. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Storage operators need state-aware rules, contact limits and an audit trail, which is what collections compliance software provides. They are building it from scratch inside facility software, one state at a time.

## What Already Exists
Collections compliance and audit platforms used by agencies, analysed at [[niches/collections-agencies/compliance-audit-automation/profile|Compliance Audit Automation]], encode federal and state contact rules, validation notices and consent, and log every action for regulators.

## The Customization Gap
Storage liens add: a possessory lien rather than a debt claim, with denial of access and overlock as steps; a statutory notice sequence ending in a public sale; advertising rules (newspaper, online or both); sale-venue rules, now including online auction sites in many states; vehicle, boat and trailer title and lienholder searches; federal military-status checks before sale; excess-proceeds custody; and city overlays such as New York's licensing. The rules engine, the evidence types and the endpoints (auction sites, newspapers, title agencies) all need storage-specific content.

## Target Customer
Collections leaders at multi-state operators; compliance vendors looking for a regulated vertical next to agencies.

## Impact If Solved
Adapting a compliance engine instead of hand-coding fifty states could cut rule maintenance from ~1 full-time person to ~0.25 (estimate) for a 20-state operator. It also gives the operator the audit trail regulators now expect before a sale.
