# Pre-Sale Delinquency Recovery

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-storage-operators|Self-Storage Operators]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to get past-due tenants paying again before their units reach the lien sale, and whoever recovers the most past-due rent per delinquent unit before the sale date takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$200–300M a year of past-due rent, late fees and lien fees recovered before sale, and the collections labour and tools around it (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1–1.5% of receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — automated texts and emails exist; prioritisation and payment plans are manual
**Target Buyer:** Director of collections at an operator or third-party manager
**Automation Potential:** High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Most delinquent tenants pay before the sale. The money recovered here comes from tenants who still hold the keys and want their belongings, so the contest is persuasion and convenience: reaching the right tenant, through a channel they answer, with a payment they can make, before the statutory sale date. The competitors are facility platforms' collections modules (Storable Collections, OpenTech's automated collections), call centres and the store manager's phone. Collection agencies come in only after the sale, on any deficiency, and are analysed under [[industries/collections-agencies|Collections Agencies]]. The winner is measured in rent recovered per delinquent unit before the sale date, not in contact rates.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Scheduled text, email and voice reminders inside facility software; Storable's AI-agent work on delinquency outreach under consent, quiet-hour and frequency rules (Storable's AI Agents job posting, 2026-09-23); payment links; store managers calling from a printed list. Gaps: outreach in days-past-due order rather than by likelihood to pay; stale phone numbers and emails; no payment plans that the statute timing can accommodate; gate codes suspended with no message explaining how to restore them.
