# Collection-Agency Dialler Analytics Adapted to Tenants Who Still Hold the Keys

**Niche:** [[niches/self-storage-operators/pre-sale-delinquency-recovery/profile|Pre-Sale Delinquency Recovery]]
**Industry:** [[industries/self-storage-operators|Self-Storage Operators]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Collection agencies already score accounts for likelihood to pay and pace diallers to match. A storage tenant is different: they still have belongings behind a locked door, so the right message is about the unit, not the debt.
**Tags:** #logistic-regression #gradient-boosting #evaluation-metrics #compliance #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to get past-due tenants paying again before their units reach the lien sale, and whoever recovers the most past-due rent per delinquent unit before the sale date takes the account.

## The Problem
Operators need the account scoring and contact discipline that agencies use. Their reminder systems send the same text to every tenant on the same day count.

## What Already Exists
Collection agencies run dialler and contact-strategy analytics that score accounts, choose channel and time, and track consent; the market is analysed under [[industries/collections-agencies|Collections Agencies]].

## The Customization Gap
Storage needs: features no agency has (access since overlock, unit contents category, vehicles in the unit, protection-plan status, tenure); the lien clock as the deadline that frames every message ("your belongings are scheduled for sale on…"), which agencies never have; payment plans that must complete before the statutory date or pause the sale where the state allows; consent and quiet-hour rules applied to a tenancy rather than a debt; and immediate gate-code restoration on payment. The model must also avoid tactics that read as threats under consumer-protection rules, now that New York City has made lockout and auction due process an enforcement term.

## Target Customer
Collections leaders at operators; dialler and contact-strategy vendors looking at storage.

## Impact If Solved
Borrowing agency-grade contact scoring could raise right-party contact rates by ~10–20% (estimate) and cut collections calls per recovered dollar by a similar share.
