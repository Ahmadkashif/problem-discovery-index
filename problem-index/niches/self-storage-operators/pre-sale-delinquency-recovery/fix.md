# Gate Code Suspended, No Way Back

**Niche:** [[niches/self-storage-operators/pre-sale-delinquency-recovery/profile|Pre-Sale Delinquency Recovery]]
**Industry:** [[industries/self-storage-operators|Self-Storage Operators]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A tenant a few days late finds the gate code dead at 7 p.m., with nobody in the office, no message on the keypad, and no way to pay and get in.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #automation #quick-win #worker-facing #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to get past-due tenants paying again before their units reach the lien sale, and whoever recovers the most past-due rent per delinquent unit before the sale date takes the account.

## The Problem
Facility software suspends access automatically on a day count. The tenant learns at the gate, often after hours. The call centre gets an angry call. The tenant may pay by phone, but the code is restored only when someone re-syncs the gate system, sometimes the next morning. Some tenants never call back, and the unit drifts toward sale.

## Why It's Still Broken
Access control and the facility ledger are separate systems from separate vendors, linked by a sync that runs on a schedule. The keypad cannot show a balance or take a payment. The suspension rule was written to protect the operator, not to bring the tenant back.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Warn the tenant by text a day before suspension, with a payment link. Show "see your phone" or a QR code on the keypad when a suspended code is entered. Restore access within a minute of payment through a real-time link between ledger and gate. Log every suspension and restoration in the tenant's file.

## Who Feels the Pain
Tenants; call-centre agents taking the call; store managers opening the next morning to a voicemail backlog; operators losing recoverable tenants.

## Impact If Fixed
If a third of suspended tenants pay within a day when given a link (estimate), an operator suspending ~1,000 codes a month recovers ~$100,000 a month sooner. Gate-lockout calls to the call centre fall by a similar share.
