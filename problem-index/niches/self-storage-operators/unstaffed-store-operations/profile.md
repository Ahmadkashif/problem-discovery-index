# Unstaffed Stores: Kiosks, Smart Access & Remote Rental

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-storage-operators|Self-Storage Operators]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to run a store with nobody on site without losing the rent a manager would have kept, and whoever delivers the highest net operating income per store run with nobody on site takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$500–800M a year of kiosk, smart-lock, access-control, call-centre and remote-management spend (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — growing fast at REITs and new builds, thin at older independents
**Target Buyer:** VP of operations at an operator or third-party manager; owner of a new-build or rural store
**Automation Potential:** Very high

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A storage store is a property that customers can rent and use without ever meeting anyone, if the systems around it work. Tenants rent online or at a kiosk, sign the lease, choose protection, get a code or a phone key, and enter through gates and doors that log every event. Remote agents answer the intercom. Public Storage's app gives tenants "digital access to our properties"; OpenTech Alliance sells INSOMNIAC access control, rental kiosks, the OpenTech Live! call centre and AI voice and chat agents; Janus's Nokē Smart Entry puts a smart lock on every door; Storable sells Access, Contact Connect and, from its September 2026 hiring, AI agents that complete move-ins and reissue gate codes. The vendors compete on what the unstaffed store earns: rent kept, minus the systems and remote support that replaced the manager. Net operating income per unstaffed store is the number owners check.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Kiosks, gate keypads and smart locks; online move-in flows in Storable and OpenUnit; call centres (CubeSmart's sales centre, Public Storage's customer care centre, OpenTech Live!); AI voice agents, analysed for answering services at [[niches/answering-services/ai-voice-receptionist-vendors/profile|AI Voice Agent & Virtual Receptionist Vendors]] (26). Gaps: no evidence for which stores can go unstaffed; gate lockouts with no remote recovery; incident handling (break-ins, people living in units) without anyone on site; fragmented systems per client at call centres.
