# Lien Auction Execution & Sale Proceeds

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-storage-operators|Self-Storage Operators]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to sell each lien unit for as much of the unpaid balance as its contents will fetch, and whoever recovers the highest share of the balance owed per auctioned unit takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$100–200M a year of lien-sale proceeds and auction fees (estimate, on ~155,000 units a year at a few hundred dollars each; the unit count is a third-party estimate cited by the California Senate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.5–1% of receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — most sales now run online; cataloguing is a store manager with a phone camera
**Target Buyer:** VP of operations at an operator; product lead at an auction marketplace
**Automation Potential:** Medium — listing and scheduling automate, the cut lock and the photo do not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Once the statutory steps are complete, the contest changes. The store manager cuts the lock, photographs the contents from the doorway without touching them, and lists the unit. Bidders buy the whole unit blind, on those photos. Proceeds pay the lien and costs, and any surplus is held for the tenant (California holds it for a year). The competitors are online lien-auction marketplaces: StorageTreasures (owned by OpenTech Alliance per an aggregator guide, search summary), Lockerfox, Bid13 and Storable's Auctions module, plus live auctioneers. They win operators on how much of each unpaid balance their sales recover. Unlike the auction firms analysed under [[industries/auctioneers|Auctioneers]], nobody here catalogues lots or offers an estimate. The sale is one blind lot per unit, and the auction is a legal step as much as a market.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Marketplace listing tools with photo upload, statute-aware scheduling in some platforms, and email alerts to registered bidders. The general online auction marketplaces are profiled at [[niches/auctioneers/online-auction-marketplace-operators/profile|Online Auction Marketplace Operators]] (27). Gaps: dark, inconsistent photos; no estimate of what a unit will fetch; no use of the marketplace's own bid history to set reserves or timing; surplus tracking by spreadsheet.
