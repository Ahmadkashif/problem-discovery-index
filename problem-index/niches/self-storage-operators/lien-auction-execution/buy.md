# General Online Auction Platforms Adapted to Statutory Lien Sales

**Niche:** [[niches/self-storage-operators/lien-auction-execution/profile|Lien Auction Execution & Sale Proceeds]]
**Industry:** [[industries/self-storage-operators|Self-Storage Operators]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Timed online auction software already runs millions of lots for independent auctioneers. A storage lien sale is a single blind lot governed by statute, with a sale date that can be cancelled up to the minute the tenant pays.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #compliance #data-integration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to sell each lien unit for as much of the unpaid balance as its contents will fetch, and whoever recovers the highest share of the balance owed per auctioned unit takes the account.

## The Problem
Operators and smaller marketplaces want the bidder reach and payment handling of the big online auction platforms. Those platforms were built for catalogued lots with estimates, not for one lot per unit whose sale must stop the moment rent is paid.

## What Already Exists
Timed-auction marketplaces and software for independent auctioneers (Auction Technology Group's platforms, HiBid, Proxibid and others), profiled at [[niches/auctioneers/online-auction-marketplace-operators/profile|Online Auction Marketplace Operators]] (27), with cataloguing work analysed at [[niches/auctioneers/lot-cataloguing-and-listing/profile|Lot Cataloguing & Online Listing]].

## The Customization Gap
Storage needs: a sale date fed from the lien evidence file, so a listing cannot go live before the statute allows; instant cancellation when the tenant pays, with bidder notices; listing text that meets each state's advertising rule (some states accept an online listing as the advertisement for a set number of days); doorway-only photo rules; buyer cleanout deadlines and deposits; and surplus-proceeds records per unit. Fees must also fit lien economics, where a unit may sell for less than a listing fee elsewhere.

## Target Customer
Auction software vendors entering storage; operators running their own branded online sales.

## Impact If Solved
A statute-linked listing flow removes the manual date checks behind wrongful-sale risk, and cancels the ~10–20% of scheduled sales that end in payment (estimate) without staff intervention.
