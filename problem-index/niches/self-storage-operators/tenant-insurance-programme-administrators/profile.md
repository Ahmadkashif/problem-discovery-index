# Tenant Insurance & Protection Programme Administrators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-storage-operators|Self-Storage Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
They underwrite, administer and reinsure the tenant insurance and protection plans storage tenants buy at move-in. The REITs run captives: a Public Storage subsidiary reinsures policies sold at its own and managed stores ($250.7M of premiums in 2025), and Extra Space booked $352.9M of tenant reinsurance revenue. Storable Tenant Insurance (the StorSmart and Bader programmes, administered by Property First Group) is sold inside Storable's software, and MiniCo and SBOA run programmes for independents (search summaries). Tenants pay a monthly premium or plan fee with their rent; operators earn commission or keep the margin.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~2–8 actuarial and pricing analysts per programme (estimate); captives commonly rely on consulting actuaries. Underwriters, claims adjusters and claims analysts are production and are not counted
**Output:** Programme pricing and coverage tiers, state rate filings for the insurance form, loss analysis per operator and facility
**Proprietary data:** Claims by cause and amount joined to the facilities and units insured, across many operators for an administrator and across its own portfolio for a captive
**Clock:** Programme and reinsurance renewals (contract dates) and rate filings timed by the filer; per-policy effective dates bind production work
**Buyer:** Chief underwriting officer or programme head
