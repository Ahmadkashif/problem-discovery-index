# Consumer-Protection, Lien & Insurance Regulators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-storage-operators|Self-Storage Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The bodies that write and enforce the rules on storage pricing, liens and tenant insurance. New York City's Department of Consumer and Worker Protection has licensed every self-storage facility in the city since 2026-08-25 (Local Law 171 of 2025). It settled with Extra Space for $1.7M on 2026-07-09. State legislatures rewrite lien statutes, as California did with AB 498, and attorneys general enforce emergency price caps such as Penal Code 396. State insurance departments license tenant-insurance sellers under limited lines, as Public Storage's 10-K describes.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analysts on storage at any one body (estimate)
**Output:** Laws, licences, complaints, enforcement actions and settlements
**Proprietary data:** Consumer complaints (DCWP reviewed more than 100 against one operator), licence records
**Clock:** Legislative sessions, rulemaking and court calendars
**Buyer:** Commissioner or enforcement director
