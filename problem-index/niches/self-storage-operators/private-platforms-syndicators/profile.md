# Private Self-Storage Platforms & Syndicators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-storage-operators|Self-Storage Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Private owner-operators and investor-backed platforms that buy, develop and run storage outside the REITs. Go Store It, StorageMart, StorQuest, Storage Asset Management and Towne Storage are examples named in Slate's January 2025 reporting. They raise equity from funds or syndicated investors and compete with the REITs for acquisitions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Acquisition, revenue and asset-management teams under 10 per firm (estimate); store and district staff are production
**Output:** Acquisition underwriting, pricing, investor reports, all internal
**Proprietary data:** Their own stores' ledgers and the deals they underwrote
**Clock:** Acquisition closings and investor reporting dates (client and own clocks)
**Buyer:** Chief investment officer
