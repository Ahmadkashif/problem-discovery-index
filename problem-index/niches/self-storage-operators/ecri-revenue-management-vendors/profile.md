# Revenue Management & ECRI Software Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-storage-operators|Self-Storage Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Independent vendors that recommend street rates and existing-customer increases to operators that lack a REIT's in-house team. Prorize (founded 2006) sells a Self Storage Revenue Optimizer and reported 35% growth in 2024 and 60+ new clients over three years. Monument markets automated ECRI recommendations with churn-sensitivity analysis, and Cubby and Storeganise sell pricing and increase scheduling (search summaries). Operators pay per facility.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–25 pricing scientists and analysts at the largest vendor (estimate); fewer elsewhere
**Output:** Daily rate and increase recommendations delivered in software
**Proprietary data:** Clients' ledgers and rate histories, and the outcome of every recommendation, held under each vendor's SaaS terms
**Clock:** None external; clients' increase cycles (client clocks)
**Buyer:** Founder or head of data science at the vendor
