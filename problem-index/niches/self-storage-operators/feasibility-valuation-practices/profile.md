# Self-Storage Feasibility & Valuation Practices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-storage-operators|Self-Storage Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Firms that write market and feasibility studies and appraisals for storage development, acquisition and SBA or bank loans. Wert-Berater reports 4,000+ engagements. MMCG Invest prices studies from $4,900, Cedar Creek Capital charges $3,000 for an acquisition study and $9,000 for a development study, and StorageStudy charges $9,500 for a full study (search summaries). Developers, buyers and lenders commission them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Analysts and appraisers write one study per engagement (production); no standing research team of 10 found (estimate)
**Output:** Feasibility studies, market studies, appraisals
**Proprietary data:** Past study files and supply surveys, mostly built for clients
**Clock:** Clients' land, loan and closing dates (client clocks)
**Buyer:** Principal
