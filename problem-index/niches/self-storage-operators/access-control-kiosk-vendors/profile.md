# Smart-Lock, Access-Control & Kiosk Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-storage-operators|Self-Storage Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
They sell the hardware and software that let a store run without staff. Janus International, which earns ~68% of its revenue from self-storage, sells the Nokē Smart Entry System of per-unit smart locks with new builds and retrofits. OpenTech Alliance sells INSOMNIAC access control, rental kiosks, SmartMotion unit sensors and electronic locks. Operators buy hardware, installation and software subscriptions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 product analytics and data staff at the largest vendor (estimate); installers and support staff are production
**Output:** Access software, door and gate event dashboards, alerts; the analysis is part of the product, not invoiced
**Proprietary data:** Door, lock, gate and motion events from client facilities
**Clock:** None external; product releases and construction schedules
**Buyer:** General manager or VP of product for the access line
