# Self-Storage Investment Sales Brokerage Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-storage-operators|Self-Storage Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Brokers that sell storage facilities to REITs, private platforms and investors, and publish research to win listings. Marcus & Millichap publishes semiannual self-storage reports for 42 markets. Argus Self Storage Advisors' broker network says it closed ~$500M of sales in 2025 (Market Monitor I-2026), and SkyView Advisors publishes a Transaction Intelligence Report (search summaries). Sellers pay commission on the sale; the research is free.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research staff on storage per firm (estimate); brokers are production
**Output:** Free market reports, broker opinions of value, offering memoranda
**Proprietary data:** The firm's own closed deals and listings, plus public deed records
**Clock:** Self-set publication; sellers' listing dates (client clocks)
**Buyer:** Head of the storage brokerage group
