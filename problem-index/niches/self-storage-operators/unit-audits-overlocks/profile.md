# Unit Audits, Overlocks & Facility Walks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-storage-operators|Self-Storage Operators]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make every door match the ledger, and whoever recovers the most rent lost to ledger-door mismatches per store takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300–500M a year of store-manager and district-manager time on lock checks, audits and walk-throughs, plus smart-lock, sensor and audit-app spend (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% of receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — clipboard walk sheets at most stores; smart locks and sensors at a minority
**Target Buyer:** VP of operations or district manager at an operator or third-party manager
**Automation Potential:** High where smart locks or sensors exist, Medium elsewhere

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A storage facility is an inventory of doors, and the ledger is only right if someone checks them. The audit finds vacant units with locks on them (squatters, unpaid move-outs, mistakes), overlocked units standing open, rented units with no lock, and units used for living, dumping or business without permission. Each mismatch is lost rent, a liability or a claim. The work is physical, repetitive and done mostly on paper by the store manager. District managers repeat it on audit visits, and third-party managers report it to owners. Vendors compete to replace the walk with door data. Janus International's Nokē Smart Entry reports lock events per unit, and OpenTech Alliance sells SmartMotion unit sensors and electronic locks. Their pitch is measured in recovered rent, not in hours saved.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Paper or spreadsheet walk sheets; audit checklists in facility software; smart locks and sensors at newer or retrofitted stores (Janus sells Nokē with new construction and "R3" retrofits). Gaps: walk results typed in late or never; no reconciliation between door events, gate codes and the ledger; audits scheduled by calendar, not by risk; no photo record.
