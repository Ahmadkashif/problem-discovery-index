# Retail Store-Audit Apps Adapted to Storage Doors

**Niche:** [[niches/self-storage-operators/unit-audits-overlocks/profile|Unit Audits, Overlocks & Facility Walks]]
**Industry:** [[industries/self-storage-operators|Self-Storage Operators]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Retail and facilities teams already run photo-verified audits from a phone with routes, checklists and image checks. A storage audit is the same walk with one question per door: does this lock match the ledger?
**Tags:** #cnns #object-detection #transfer-learning #worker-facing #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make every door match the ledger, and whoever recovers the most rent lost to ledger-door mismatches per store takes the account.

## The Problem
Most stores have no smart locks and will not for years. Their managers need a faster way to do the walk they already do, and a record that it was done.

## What Already Exists
Retail execution and store-audit apps, and facility-inspection apps used by property managers, guide workers through checklists, capture photos with location and time, and use image recognition to check shelves or equipment.

## The Customization Gap
Storage needs: the ledger loaded as the checklist, door by door in walking order across floors and buildings; an image classifier for lock types (tenant disc lock, company overlock, vacancy lock, none) and lock position, trained on door photos; instant mismatch alerts against the ledger; and results written back to the facility software. Overlock and vacancy-lock workflows must link to the lien file, and photos must be framed on the door and lock only, with no people. Generic audit apps know shelves and planograms, not padlocks.

## Target Customer
Operators and third-party managers with older, lock-less portfolios; audit-app vendors looking for a vertical.

## Impact If Solved
A photo-checked walk with door-order routing could cut a full lock check from ~4 hours to ~2 (estimate) and put a time-stamped record behind every audit an owner or district manager reviews.
