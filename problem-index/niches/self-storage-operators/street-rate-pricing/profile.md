# Street-Rate Pricing & Move-In Promotions

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-storage-operators|Self-Storage Operators]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to set each vacant unit type's web rate and promotion every day against nearby competitors, and whoever earns the most rental revenue per available square foot takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$150–250M a year of revenue-management software, market data and pricing analyst spend (estimate), steering the move-in rents on the industry's $20.8B of receipts
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1% of receipts as spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High at REITs, Medium at regional operators, Low at independents
**Target Buyer:** VP of revenue management; owner at a single store
**Automation Potential:** Very high

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
New-customer pricing is a daily, rules-based decision on public information, the most mechanical judgement in the business. Extra Space's revenue management systems "analyze, set and adjust rental rates daily", and more than half of Public Storage's new customers came through Google search in 2025, so the web rate is the shop window. The price is set by unit type: size, climate control, floor, drive-up access. It is set against competitors' posted rates and promotions and the store's own vacancy, then the first-month promotion is chosen. Yardi Matrix reported national advertised rates down 1.8% year over year in September 2026. The work is separate from existing-customer increases: different data, a daily clock, and an outcome (move-ins at a rate) measured within days. The parking and hotel equivalents are [[niches/parking-operators/rate-setting-yield-pricing/profile|Rate Setting & Yield Pricing]] and [[niches/hotels-boutique/dynamic-rate-optimization/profile|Dynamic Rate Optimization]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
REIT in-house systems; Prorize's Self Storage Revenue Optimizer; Cubby and Monument pricing tools; market data from TractIQ (monthly street and web rates since March 2018), Yardi Matrix, Radius+ and StorTrack. The moving-industry view of this data is [[niches/moving-companies/storage-facility-market-data/profile|Self-Storage Market Data]] (42; ~40 under the current clock ruling, pending Stage E). Gaps: competitor promotions compared at face value; vacancy not forecast by unit type; web, phone and walk-in rates out of step; independents still price from a weekly survey.
