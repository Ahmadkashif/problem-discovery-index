# Third-Party Management Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-storage-operators|Self-Storage Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
They run storage facilities for owners under their own brand, for a fee. Extra Space managed 1,856 stores for third parties at the end of 2025, CubeSmart 862, and Public Storage 417 by September 2026, plus the 313-store PS-NSA joint venture it manages. SmartStop bought the third-party manager Argus Professional Storage Management on 2025-10-01 and manages ~270 properties it does not own. Private managers such as White Label Storage and Storage Asset Management run smaller books. The manager sets street rates and increases, collects rent and runs liens, and reports monthly to the owner. Extra Space earns fees "based on a percentage of revenues collected", and the REITs use their managed books as an acquisition pipeline.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 revenue-management, pricing and data staff per platform (estimate); owner-reporting accountants, store, district and call-centre staff are production and are not counted
**Output:** Owner statements, pricing and increase programmes for owners' stores, budgets, conversion proposals to new owners
**Proprietary data:** Owners' rent rolls and tenant ledgers, held under management services agreements
**Clock:** Monthly owner statements and owners' budget and refinancing dates (client clocks)
**Buyer:** SVP or head of third-party management
