# Storage REIT Revenue Management & Data Science Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-storage-operators|Self-Storage Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The central pricing, increase and data science teams at the five listed storage REITs and U-Haul's storage business. Public Storage now holds interests in 4,347 facilities after buying National Storage Affiliates (closed 2026-07-22). Extra Space's ~2,445,000 tenants are priced daily by revenue management systems "developed by our management team". CubeSmart owns 662 stores, and U-Haul runs 2,113 storage locations. SmartStop (NYSE: SMA) owned 180 operating properties outright at 2026-06-30, with 10 more in Canadian joint ventures, and manages ~270 it does not own. Global Self Storage (Nasdaq: SELF) owns or manages 13 stores. These teams set web rates, size increases ("every six to twelve months" at Public Storage), steer search spend and underwrite acquisitions. Their output is rent, not an invoice.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–60 revenue-management, pricing and data science staff at each of the three largest REITs (estimate); fewer at SmartStop, and none dedicated at Global Self Storage
**Output:** Daily rates, increase batches, marketing bids and acquisition underwriting, all internal
**Proprietary data:** Increase-and-response histories for millions of tenancies, the deepest price-response record in storage
**Clock:** Self-set increase cycles; quarterly 10-Q and annual 10-K dates bind the company, not this analysis
**Buyer:** Chief revenue officer or head of revenue management
