# Storage Search Marketplaces & Peer-to-Peer Storage

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-storage-operators|Self-Storage Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Marketplaces between renters and storage space. SpareFoot, now Storable's Marketplace, lists operators' units and charges for move-ins. Neighbor (Lehi, Utah) runs a peer-to-peer marketplace where individuals and businesses rent out garages, driveways and spare rooms for storage and parking. Its job postings say it has raised over $75M and operates in all 50 states. Revenue is commission or take rate.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–20 data and analytics staff at the largest marketplace (estimate). Neighbor's Data & Analytics team runs production lifetime-value, unit-economics and forecasting models (internship posting, 2026-09-28)
**Output:** Search ranking, lead pricing, host price suggestions, demand forecasts
**Proprietary data:** The marketplace's own search, booking and host records
**Clock:** None external
**Buyer:** Head of data or chief product officer
