# Storage Management Software Data Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-storage-operators|Self-Storage Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The facility-software platforms that hold operators' ledgers. Storable says it is "trusted by more than 33,000 facilities" through Sitelink, Storable Edge and Storable Easy, and publishes a quarterly Industry Pulse from 30,000+ facilities. The Q3 2026 Pulse put average length of stay at 19.0 months and occupancy at 78.1%. OpenUnit (YC W20, Toronto) gives management software away free, and Stora sells a self-storage operating platform from Belfast. Operators pay subscriptions and payment fees. Storable's SpareFoot marketplace is scored with the search marketplaces at [[niches/self-storage-operators/storage-search-p2p-marketplaces/profile|Storage Search Marketplaces & Peer-to-Peer Storage]], not here.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 analytics, data and data-platform staff at Storable (estimate); under 10 at the smaller vendors. Storable was hiring a Senior Software Engineer, Analytics Platform in Seattle (first posted 2026-09-23) for a Snowflake data foundation across its Storage, Marine and RV/Campground products
**Output:** The free quarterly Industry Pulse; business-intelligence dashboards sold with the software
**Proprietary data:** Facility ledgers across Storable's products. Only the Storable Edge half is counted for Q3, because only its terms were checked
**Clock:** Self-set quarterly report; product release cycles
**Buyer:** Chief product officer or head of data

**Kill switches:** none on the Storable Edge half, which carries Q3 and the verdict. Its terms (§5.2, effective 2025-10-08) read in full: "You agree that the Company may use the data generated by and stored on our servers anonymously, in support of our own internal business purposes, including but not limited to the development of anonymous marketing and sales collateral materials, statistical analysis of data regarding rental rates, unit availability, traffic sources, vacancy, and other relevant data to construct yield optimization models, and publication solely in an aggregated deidentified form of operating data in industry benchmark reports. You shall at all times retain ownership of your data." That purpose limit covers yield models and aggregated benchmark reports such as the Industry Pulse. Sitelink's terms of service were not read, so the Sitelink half is undetermined and is not counted in Q3.
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold (30). Storable's terms already let it build yield models and publish aggregated benchmarks from Edge ledgers, and it spends that right on a free marketing report and product features. A handful of vendors is not a fragmented market. Its tenant-facing agents and analytics hiring make it the industry's main engineering employer (hub and source log), not an insight buyer.
