# Existing-Customer Rate Increases (ECRI)

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-storage-operators|Self-Storage Operators]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to size each existing tenant's increase so the added rent outweighs the move-outs it causes, and whoever earns the most net rent per increase notice after twelve months of induced move-outs takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600M–1.2B of annual rent added by existing-customer increases across the industry (estimate, ~3–6% of $20.8B of receipts), with ~$50–80M a year of pricing software, analyst and notice spend
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3–6% of receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High at the REITs, which size increases in their own systems; Low at most independents, where the owner picks a number
**Target Buyer:** VP of revenue management at a regional operator or third-party manager; the owner at a single store
**Automation Potential:** High for sizing and notices, Medium for exceptions and complaints

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Storage is sold on a low web rate and paid for later. Public Storage raises long-term tenants "every six to twelve months", sizing each increase against "the negative impact of incremental move-outs", and Extra Space raises existing tenants "at least annually". These letters drive most of the growth in rent per occupied foot, and the work is separate from setting street rates. The inputs are a tenant's own history rather than competitors' signs, the clock is the tenant's anniversary, and the rules are consumer-protection rules. California requires 30 days' notice to amend, and New York City's 2026 settlement with Extra Space ($1.7M) bars luring customers with low prices and then raising rates excessively. Senior housing has the same two-price structure, analysed at [[niches/assisted-living-communities/street-rate-renewal-pricing/profile|Street-Rate & Renewal Pricing]]. Storage tenancies are month to month, so an increase can come in any month.

## Current Tools & Gaps
The REITs build their own systems. Prorize's Self Storage Revenue Optimizer, Monument's ECRI tool with churn-sensitivity analysis, Cubby's ML recommendations and Storeganise's scheduled increases serve everyone else (search summaries). White Label Storage built an in-house ECRI Hub for its managed stores. The gaps: no randomised holdouts, so nobody knows what a smaller increase would have kept; override reasons never recorded; notice and cap rules checked by hand; results reported as gross uplift rather than net rent after move-outs.
