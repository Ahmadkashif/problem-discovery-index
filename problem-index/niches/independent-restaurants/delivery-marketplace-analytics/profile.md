# Delivery Marketplace Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-restaurants|Independent Restaurants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The analytics organizations at the third-party delivery platforms model demand, set fees and promotions, and produce the merchant-facing reporting restaurants use to judge a channel that takes 15-30% of every order.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,500 data scientists, economists, and analysts
**Output:** Demand forecasting, pricing and incentive design, merchant analytics, marketplace matching
**Proprietary data:** Order-level demand across the country by restaurant, item, time, and weather — the strongest consumer food demand signal in existence
**Clock:** Real-time dispatch and daily demand cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Data Science / Head of Merchant Products
