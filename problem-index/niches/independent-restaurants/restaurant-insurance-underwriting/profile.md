# Restaurant Insurance Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-restaurants|Independent Restaurants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Specialty programmes underwrite property, liability, liquor liability, and workers' compensation for restaurants, pricing an industry with high employee turnover, fire exposure, and slip-and-fall frequency.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 underwriters, actuaries, and loss control specialists
**Output:** Rating and eligibility decisions, loss control recommendations, claims analysis
**Proprietary data:** Loss experience by concept, cooking method, alcohol mix, and operating hours across a large book
**Clock:** Annual renewal
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
