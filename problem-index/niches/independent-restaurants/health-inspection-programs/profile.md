# Health Inspection Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-restaurants|Independent Restaurants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
County and city health departments inspect restaurants on a risk-based schedule, score them, and in many jurisdictions require the grade to be posted where customers can see it — which makes the inspection finding a revenue event as much as a safety one.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-200 environmental health specialists and analysts per jurisdiction
**Output:** Inspection reports and scores, violation findings, closure orders, outbreak investigation
**Proprietary data:** Violation histories by establishment across a jurisdiction, with the outbreak record they can be tested against
**Clock:** Inspection cycles and outbreak response
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — municipal budget
