# Restaurant Group Operating Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-restaurants|Independent Restaurants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup

## What They Do
Multi-unit restaurant groups and the platforms acquiring independent concepts run internal analytics covering food cost, labour, and unit performance across their portfolios — the capability Pass 1 says exists only in the top 5% of independents.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 analysts and operations staff
**Output:** Food cost and labour analysis, menu contribution reporting, unit performance review, acquisition diligence
**Proprietary data:** Recipe-level cost and item-level sales across many concepts and locations
**Clock:** Weekly period close and daily service
**Buyer:** No external buyer — internal cost centre
