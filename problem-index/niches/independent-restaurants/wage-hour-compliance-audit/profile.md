## What They Do
Specialist firms audit restaurant payroll practices — tip credit, tip pooling, side work, overtime, and predictive scheduling compliance — in the most wage-and-hour-litigated industry in the country.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-80 compliance analysts, attorneys, and payroll specialists
**Output:** Compliance audits, exposure quantification, remediation plans, litigation support
**Proprietary data:** Error patterns across many operators and jurisdictions, with the enforcement and settlement outcomes that followed
**Clock:** Statutory limitations periods and audit or claim notices
**Buyer:** Practice leader
