# Restaurant Site Selection Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-restaurants|Independent Restaurants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Site selection firms model trade areas and forecast unit volumes for restaurant operators deciding where to open — the single highest-stakes decision in the business, made once and irreversible for a decade of lease.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-70 analysts, geographers, and modellers
**Output:** Trade area analyses, sales forecasts for proposed sites, cannibalization studies, portfolio reviews
**Proprietary data:** Site characteristics matched to realized unit performance across many operators and markets
**Clock:** Lease negotiation and development committee timelines
**Buyer:** Principal / Managing Director
