# Foodservice Market Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-restaurants|Independent Restaurants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer

## What They Do
The foodservice research firms track menu items, pricing, and operator counts across the US restaurant population and sell the read to manufacturers, distributors, and chains deciding what to make and sell.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-400 analysts, menu coders, and panel managers
**Output:** Menu trend and penetration data, operator databases, forecast and sizing reports
**Proprietary data:** Coded menu corpora and operator panels built over decades
**Clock:** Menu change cycles and annual reporting
**Buyer:** Practice head / Chief Data Officer
