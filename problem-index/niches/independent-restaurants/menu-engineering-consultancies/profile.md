# Menu Engineering Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-restaurants|Independent Restaurants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Small consultancies analyze a restaurant's menu by contribution margin and popularity and redesign it — the contribution margin analysis Pass 1 says most independents replace with gut feel and competitor imitation.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1-8 consultants, often one former chef or operator
**Output:** Menu profitability analysis, pricing recommendations, menu redesign
**Proprietary data:** Recipe costings and item performance across the consultant's own client history
**Clock:** Menu change seasons
**Buyer:** The consultant
