# Restaurant POS Data Organizations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-restaurants|Independent Restaurants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The data groups at Toast, Square, and Clover process transactions for a large share of US independent restaurants and hold item-level sales, labour, and payment data across roughly a hundred thousand operators.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 data scientists, analysts, and product data staff
**Output:** In-product reporting, industry trend publications, lending underwriting, benchmarking
**Proprietary data:** Item-level sales, staffing, and payment records across the independent segment — precisely the operational intelligence Pass 1 says is near-zero outside the top 5%
**Clock:** Daily service periods and weekly payroll
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Product
