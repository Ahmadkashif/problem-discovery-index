# Restaurant Association Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-restaurants|Independent Restaurants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The industry association publishes the annual restaurant industry forecast, operations reports, and workforce research, and analyzes the labour and tax policy that hits operators hardest.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 researchers, economists, and policy analysts
**Output:** Industry forecasts, operations and workforce reports, policy analysis
**Proprietary data:** Member operating surveys layered over public statistics
**Clock:** Annual forecast cycle and legislative sessions
**Buyer:** VP of Research — member funded
