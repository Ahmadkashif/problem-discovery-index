# Restaurant Performance Benchmarking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-restaurants|Independent Restaurants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Benchmarking services collect same-store sales, traffic, and labour metrics from participating restaurant companies and publish the weekly and monthly indices the industry manages against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 analysts, economists, and data staff
**Output:** Same-store sales and traffic indices, labour and turnover benchmarks, segment comparisons
**Proprietary data:** Contributed operating data from a large share of chain restaurant units, assembled under reciprocity over two decades
**Clock:** Weekly and monthly reporting
**Buyer:** VP of Research / Managing Director
