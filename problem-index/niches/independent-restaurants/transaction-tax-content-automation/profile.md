# Transaction Tax Content & Determination

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-restaurants|Independent Restaurants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Avalara, Vertex, and their peers maintain the rates and taxability rules for more than thirteen thousand US taxing jurisdictions and calculate the correct tax on every transaction their customers process. Prepared food is the hardest category in the entire system — whether an item is taxable can turn on whether it was heated, sliced, eaten in, or sold with utensils, and the rule differs by state and often by city.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-800 tax content researchers, attorneys, taxonomists, and data engineers
**Output:** Jurisdiction rates and boundaries, product taxability rules, exemption logic, filing and remittance content
**Proprietary data:** A continuously maintained rules corpus covering every US jurisdiction and product category, with the research history behind each determination
**Clock:** Rate changes with statutory effective dates, and monthly filing deadlines that do not move
**Buyer:** VP of Tax Content / Chief Content Officer
