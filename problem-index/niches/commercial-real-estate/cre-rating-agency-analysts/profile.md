# CRE Securities Rating Analysts

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commercial-real-estate|Commercial Real Estate]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The commercial mortgage backed securities groups at Moody's, KBRA, Morningstar DBRS, and Fitch analyze loan pools, re-underwrite properties, and publish the ratings that determine what a securitization can be sold for. The rating and its supporting presale report are the product, and the market prices off them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 credit analysts, property specialists, and surveillance staff
**Output:** Ratings, presale reports, surveillance updates
**Proprietary data:** Re-underwriting conclusions across thousands of loans and years of surveillance outcomes against original assumptions
**Clock:** Deal pricing timelines and periodic surveillance obligations
**Buyer:** Managing Director / Head of CMBS
