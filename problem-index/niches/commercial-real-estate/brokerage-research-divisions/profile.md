# Brokerage Research Divisions

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commercial-real-estate|Commercial Real Estate]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The research organizations at CBRE, JLL, Cushman & Wakefield, and Colliers publish the quarterly market reports, vacancy and absorption statistics, and outlook pieces that the entire industry cites. They are large, genuinely analytical, and funded as marketing for the brokerage business.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-800 researchers, economists, and analysts globally
**Output:** Market reports, forecasts, occupier and investor advisory research
**Proprietary data:** The firm's own leasing and investment sale flow, which is the largest private transaction dataset in the market and precedes public recording by months
**Clock:** Quarterly publication and client underwriting cycles
**Buyer:** Global Head of Research / Chief Economist
