# Property Tax Appeal Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commercial-real-estate|Commercial Real Estate]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Ryan, Altus Group's property tax practice, and the regional appeal firms review assessments on commercial property, build the valuation case that the assessment is too high, and prosecute the appeal through informal review, board hearing, and litigation. The analysis is the entire service and it is adversarially tested by a jurisdiction that would rather keep the revenue.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-1,000 property tax consultants, valuation analysts, and agents at the largest firms
**Output:** Appeal filings, valuation arguments, negotiated assessment reductions
**Proprietary data:** Assessment, appeal, and settlement outcomes across thousands of parcels and hundreds of jurisdictions, including which arguments each assessor and board actually accepted
**Clock:** Statutory appeal deadlines — miss one and the year is gone
**Buyer:** Practice leader / National Director of Property Tax
