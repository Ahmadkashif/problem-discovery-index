# Institutional Property Index Benchmarking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commercial-real-estate|Commercial Real Estate]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
NCREIF and comparable bodies collect property-level appraisal and cash flow data from institutional owners and publish the return indices that pension funds and their consultants measure real estate performance against. Allocation decisions worth hundreds of billions are benchmarked to these numbers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-50 index researchers, data analysts, and methodologists
**Output:** Return indices, property-level performance data products, methodology research
**Proprietary data:** Member-contributed property-level appraisals and cash flows across decades — a series no commercial vendor can assemble
**Clock:** Quarterly index publication on fixed dates
**Buyer:** Director of Research / Chief Executive
