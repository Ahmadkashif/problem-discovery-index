# Property Condition Assessment Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commercial-real-estate|Commercial Real Estate]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Engineering consultancies produce the property condition assessments and capital reserve studies lenders require alongside the appraisal — walking a building, assessing remaining useful life of every major system, and projecting the capital expenditure the borrower will face over the loan term.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-500 building engineers, assessors, and reviewers
**Output:** Condition assessment reports and capital reserve schedules
**Proprietary data:** Observed component condition and remaining life across tens of thousands of buildings, which could establish actual system life expectancy and instead sits in individual reports
**Clock:** Loan closing deadlines
**Buyer:** National Practice Leader / VP of Due Diligence
