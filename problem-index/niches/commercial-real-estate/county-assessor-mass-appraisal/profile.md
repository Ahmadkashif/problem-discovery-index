# County Assessor Mass Appraisal Offices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commercial-real-estate|Commercial Real Estate]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
County assessors value every commercial parcel in their jurisdiction on a statutory cycle, using mass appraisal models built from sales and income data, and defend those values against the appeal consultancies. Their valuations determine property tax revenue for the whole jurisdiction.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-200 appraisers, analysts, and modellers in large counties
**Output:** Assessed values, mass appraisal models, ratio study reports
**Proprietary data:** Complete parcel-level characteristics, income statements filed by owners, and sales verification for the entire jurisdiction
**Clock:** Statutory assessment and certification calendars, absolutely fixed
**Buyer:** County Assessor — a public official with public procurement
