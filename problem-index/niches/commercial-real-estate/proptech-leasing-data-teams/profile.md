# Leasing Platform Data Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commercial-real-estate|Commercial Real Estate]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The data groups at VTS, Yardi, and MRI analyze the leasing pipeline and portfolio operations data flowing through their platforms, and publish demand indices that the market watches as a leading indicator of office and industrial absorption.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 data scientists and analysts
**Output:** Demand indices, portfolio benchmarking, in-product analytics
**Proprietary data:** Leasing pipeline activity across a large share of institutional office and industrial space, visible months before a lease is signed or recorded
**Clock:** Index publication cadence, self-set
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Chief Product Officer
