# Commercial Appraisal Practices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commercial-real-estate|Commercial Real Estate]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The valuation practices at CBRE, Newmark, JLL, and the independent appraisal firms produce the written opinion of value that federally regulated lenders are required to obtain before closing a commercial mortgage. Appraisers select comparables, apply income and sales approaches, and defend a number that determines loan proceeds. The report is the invoice and it is reviewed, challenged, and occasionally litigated.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-1,000 appraisers, analysts, and review staff at the largest practices
**Output:** Appraisal reports and valuation opinions — the billable artefact
**Proprietary data:** Thousands of prior appraisals with their comparable selections, adjustments, capitalization rate conclusions, and review outcomes, retrievable today only by property name
**Clock:** Loan closing deadlines and regulatory appraisal requirements, both fixed
**Buyer:** Valuation practice leader / National Director of Valuation
