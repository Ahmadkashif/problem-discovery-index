# CRE Investment Research Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commercial-real-estate|Commercial Real Estate]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Green Street, Trepp, and comparable firms produce independent research on property sectors, REIT valuation, and CMBS loan performance, sold by subscription to institutional investors, lenders, and advisors. The analysis is the entire product and the firms are known for calls the brokerage-affiliated research will not make.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-400 analysts, economists, and data staff
**Output:** Sector research, valuation models, loan performance analytics — the subscription
**Proprietary data:** Long-running proprietary cap rate and valuation series, plus loan-level surveillance data assembled over decades
**Clock:** Publication cadence and client investment cycles, largely self-set
**Buyer:** Director of Research / Chief Analytics Officer
