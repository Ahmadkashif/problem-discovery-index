# CRE Market Study Boutiques

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commercial-real-estate|Commercial Real Estate]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Small firms producing the market and feasibility studies developers need for lender underwriting and entitlement — demand analysis, absorption forecasts, and competitive supply surveys for a specific site. The study is the deliverable and lenders require it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 2-10
**Output:** Market and feasibility studies, absorption forecasts
**Proprietary data:** The firm's accumulated local market knowledge and prior studies
**Clock:** Loan closing and entitlement hearing dates
**Buyer:** Principal
