# Environmental Site Assessment Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commercial-real-estate|Commercial Real Estate]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Environmental consultancies produce the Phase I assessments lenders require before closing a commercial mortgage — reviewing historical records, regulatory databases, aerial imagery, and site conditions to conclude whether recognized environmental conditions exist. The written finding gates the loan and establishes the buyer's liability defence.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-1,000 environmental professionals, researchers, and reviewers at the larger firms
**Output:** Phase I and Phase II assessment reports and findings
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated site history research across hundreds of thousands of properties, including the historical record review that is redone from scratch each time a property transacts
**Clock:** Closing deadlines, and assessments go stale after a fixed period under the standard
**Buyer:** VP of Due Diligence Services / National Practice Leader
