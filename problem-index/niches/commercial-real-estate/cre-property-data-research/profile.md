# Commercial Property Data & Research Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commercial-real-estate|Commercial Real Estate]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
CoStar, MSCI Real Assets, and Altus employ enormous research staffs whose job is to establish facts about buildings that no public record contains — who occupies which floor, at what rent, on what term, and what a property actually traded for. Researchers call brokers, landlords, and tenants to verify each fact. Brokers, lenders, appraisers, and investors subscribe because the alternative is calling around themselves.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-2,000 field researchers, verification analysts, and data scientists
**Output:** Verified property, lease, sale, and tenant data plus market analytics — the subscription itself
**Proprietary data:** Phone-verified lease and transaction detail assembled building by building over decades, in a market with no disclosure requirement in most states
**Clock:** Continuous verification cycles; no third-party deadline governs publication
**Buyer:** Chief Research Officer / VP of Data Operations
