# Clinical Reference & Decision Support Content Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/urgent-care|Urgent Care]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Read the entire clinical literature so that clinicians do not have to, and turn it into the point-of-care reference they consult mid-consultation: what this presentation is likely to be, what to test for, what to treat with, and how strong the evidence for each recommendation is. In urgent care — undifferentiated presentations, no prior relationship with the patient, minutes per encounter — this layer is doing a large share of the diagnostic reasoning.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1,000-5,000 physician authors and editors, evidence methodologists, drug information specialists, and content engineers across the major publishers
**Output:** Topic reviews with graded recommendations, diagnostic and treatment pathways, drug information and interaction content, calculators, patient education, guideline synthesis
**Proprietary data:** Decades of accumulated editorial synthesis over a literature far too large for any clinician to read, with a recommendation grading apparatus and a citation trail that no public source assembles
**Clock:** Continuous — new trials, revised guidelines and safety communications arrive constantly, and content that lags is clinically dangerous
**Buyer:** Chief Medical Officer / VP of Clinical Content
