# Occupational Health Networks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/urgent-care|Urgent Care]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Manage employer-directed care — injury treatment, drug screening, physicals and return-to-work determinations — routing employees to urgent care clinics and reporting to the employer on cost and time lost.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 case managers, occupational physicians, analysts, and network staff
**Output:** Care routing and authorisation, return-to-work determinations, injury cost and lost time analytics, employer reporting, provider network scoring
**Proprietary data:** Workplace injuries joined to treatment, cost and days away from work across many employers
**Clock:** Return-to-work timelines and injury reporting deadlines
**Buyer:** VP of Analytics / Chief Medical Officer
