# Urgent Care Revenue Cycle Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/urgent-care|Urgent Care]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Bill and collect for urgent care encounters, appeal denials, and analyse where reimbursement leaks — coding level, eligibility, prior authorisation, or contract underpayment.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 coders, denial analysts, data staff, and revenue integrity specialists
**Output:** Claim submission and denial management, coding audit and levelling review, underpayment identification, payer behaviour reporting
**Proprietary data:** Claims joined to payer response across many clinics — the direct record of which payers deny what, and which appeals succeed
**Clock:** Timely filing deadlines and appeal windows
**Buyer:** VP of Revenue Integrity / Chief Analytics Officer
