# Urgent Care Malpractice Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/urgent-care|Urgent Care]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Underwrite professional liability for urgent care — a setting with undifferentiated presentations, no prior patient relationship, no follow-up and heavy use of advanced practice clinicians — and defend the missed diagnosis claims that dominate it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 underwriters, actuaries, claims counsel, and risk managers
**Output:** Programme pricing and eligibility, claims defence, risk management guidance, clinician-level risk profiling
**Proprietary data:** Claims recording exactly which presentations were missed, by which clinician type, in which staffing configuration
**Clock:** Annual renewals
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
