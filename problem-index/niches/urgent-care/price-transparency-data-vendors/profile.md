# Healthcare Price Transparency Data Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/urgent-care|Urgent Care]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Ingest the machine-readable price files hospitals and insurers are now required to publish — terabytes of inconsistently structured, frequently wrong data — normalise them, and sell the resulting view of what everybody actually pays for the same service.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 data engineers, healthcare economists, terminology specialists, and analysts
**Output:** Negotiated rate benchmarks by payer, provider and service, contract competitiveness analysis, market rate positioning, network design support
**Proprietary data:** Normalised negotiated rates assembled from files that are published in incompatible ways, frequently incomplete, and change monthly — the normalisation is the entire product
**Clock:** Monthly file publication cycles and payer contracting calendars
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / Head of Analytics
