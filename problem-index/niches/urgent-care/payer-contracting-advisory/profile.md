# Payer Contracting & Rate Negotiation Advisory

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/urgent-care|Urgent Care]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Negotiate commercial payer contracts for urgent care operators — rate schedules, coding and levelling terms, and network participation — and model what a proposed contract is actually worth given the group's case mix.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 contract analysts, negotiators, and reimbursement modellers
**Output:** Rate modelling against case mix, contract term analysis, negotiation strategy, network participation decisions, underpayment recovery
**Proprietary data:** Negotiated rates and terms observed across many clients and payers — the only comparative view of what urgent care actually gets paid
**Clock:** Contract renewal and notice periods
**Buyer:** Managing Director
