# Urgent Care Accreditation & Certification Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/urgent-care|Urgent Care]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Accredit urgent care centres against operational and clinical standards, and certify that a centre is what it claims to be in a category with no licensure of its own.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 surveyors, standards staff, and reviewers
**Output:** Accreditation standards, site surveys and findings, certification decisions, quality measure specifications
**Proprietary data:** Survey findings across accredited centres
**Clock:** Accreditation cycles
**Buyer:** Director of Accreditation
