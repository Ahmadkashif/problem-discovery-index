# Public Health Syndromic Surveillance

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/urgent-care|Urgent Care]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Collect near real-time chief complaint and diagnosis data from emergency and urgent care settings to detect outbreaks, monitor respiratory season, and trigger public health response.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 epidemiologists, informaticians, and analysts across federal and state programmes
**Output:** Syndromic trend monitoring, outbreak detection and alerting, seasonal severity assessment, situational awareness reporting
**Proprietary data:** Near real-time encounter-level chief complaint feeds from a large share of acute care settings
**Clock:** Daily monitoring and outbreak response
**Buyer:** State epidemiologist / programme director
