# Out-of-Network Dispute Resolution Entities

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/urgent-care|Urgent Care]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Decide what an out-of-network provider gets paid when the provider and the insurer cannot agree. Both sides submit a final offer with supporting evidence, a certified entity picks one, and the decision binds — a private adjudication system created by statute and running at a volume nobody forecast.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 reviewers, clinicians, health economists, and case analysts across the certified entities
**Output:** Payment determinations, offer evaluations against statutory factors, eligibility screening, decision documentation
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated determinations showing which offers, supported by which evidence, actually win — a record of how disputed medical prices resolve that exists nowhere else
**Clock:** Statutory determination deadlines measured in days from selection
**Buyer:** Chief Operating Officer / Head of Dispute Resolution

**Kill switches:** **federal programme dependency** — the entities are federally certified, the process exists only by statute, and it has been repeatedly suspended by litigation over the rules it operates under.
**Verdict:** Logged — reaches the threshold and carries a live kill switch. Determination-to-determination consistency in a binding adjudication is the obvious unmeasured quantity, and the entities are structurally discouraged from measuring it.
