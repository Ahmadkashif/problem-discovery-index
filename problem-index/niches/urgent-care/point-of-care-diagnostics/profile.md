# Point-of-Care Diagnostics Manufacturers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/urgent-care|Urgent Care]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Develop and validate the rapid tests urgent care runs in the room — respiratory panels, strep, influenza, pregnancy, basic chemistry — and generate the clinical performance evidence behind their clearances.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 clinical affairs staff, biostatisticians, assay developers, and regulatory specialists
**Output:** Assay development and validation, clinical performance studies, regulatory submissions, connected device result reporting
**Proprietary data:** Validation study results and, for connected platforms, aggregate positivity data across deployed instruments
**Clock:** Regulatory clearance programmes and respiratory season
**Buyer:** VP of Clinical Affairs
