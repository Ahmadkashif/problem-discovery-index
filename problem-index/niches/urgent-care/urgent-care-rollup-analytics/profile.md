# Urgent Care Platform Corporate Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/urgent-care|Urgent Care]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator/rollup

## What They Do
Corporate analytics inside the multi-site urgent care platforms — visit volume forecasting, staffing to demand, site selection, payer mix and acquisition screening across hundreds of clinics.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 analysts, data scientists, and corporate development staff
**Output:** Demand forecasting and staffing models, site selection and market analysis, payer mix and margin reporting, acquisition screening, clinical quality measurement
**Proprietary data:** Encounter, staffing and financial records across large clinic networks, joined to local demographics and competitive supply
**Clock:** Seasonal illness surges and quarterly reporting
**Buyer:** VP of Analytics / Chief Strategy Officer
