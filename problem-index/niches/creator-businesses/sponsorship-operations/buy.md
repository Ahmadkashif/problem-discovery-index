# Media Sales Operations

**Industry:** [[industries/creator-businesses|Creator Businesses]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Media companies run rate cards, inventory planning, insertion orders and campaign reporting as a discipline, and creators run the same business from a phone.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn the highest-margin revenue in the business from an inbox and a guess into a priced, tracked, repeatable operation — and whoever does it captures the money creators are currently leaving on the table.

## The Problem
Selling audience attention to advertisers is one of the oldest businesses there is and it has a full operational apparatus: rate cards by placement and audience, inventory forecasting, insertion orders, trafficking, delivery verification, campaign reporting and renewal management. Media companies run it with dedicated teams. A creator with a larger audience than a regional publication runs the identical business with an email account.

## What Already Exists
Rate card and yield management; inventory forecasting and avails; insertion order and contract workflow; delivery verification and campaign reporting; and renewal and account management practice.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to inventory that is one creator's attention. It requires: (1) inventory measured in future videos rather than impressions, which cannot be forecast reliably because distribution is not the creator's to control — this is the substantive difference and breaks the yield model; (2) the creator as the product, so brand fit and audience trust constrain what can be sold in ways placement-based selling does not; (3) a one-person sales team who is also the talent, meaning the operation must be near-zero effort or it will not be used; (4) no third-party verification, so delivery and performance reporting rest on platform analytics the brand cannot audit; and (5) deal terms that are private across the market, removing the price transparency media sales takes for granted.

## Target Customer
Creators and business managers, management companies, brands, and media sales platform vendors with no creator presence.

## Impact If Solved
Media sales has the whole apparatus and assumes forecastable inventory and a sales team. Rebuilding it for unforecastable inventory sold by the talent themselves is the adaptation, and price transparency is the piece that would move the most money.
