# Sponsorship Operations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/creator-businesses|Creator Businesses]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn the highest-margin revenue in the business from an inbox and a guess into a priced, tracked, repeatable operation — and whoever does it captures the money creators are currently leaving on the table.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$6B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~24% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — email and spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** Creator and business management
**Automation Potential:** High — pricing, pipeline and delivery tracking

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Brand partnership is the highest-margin revenue a creator has and the least professionally run. Inbound email is the pipeline, rate-setting is a guess anchored on whatever the last brand paid, deliverables are tracked in a spreadsheet, and the performance the brand cares about is reported from a screenshot. The gap between what this revenue is worth and how it is operated is the widest in the whole business.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Email, a rate card in a note, spreadsheets, and occasional marketplace platforms. The gaps: no pricing basis; no pipeline management; deliverables and usage rights tracked by memory; performance reporting ad hoc; and no record of what each brand paid or how it went.
