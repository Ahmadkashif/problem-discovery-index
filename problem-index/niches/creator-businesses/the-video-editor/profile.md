# The Video Editor

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/creator-businesses|Creator Businesses]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give the editor a brief they can act on and feedback that means something — and whoever does it retains the people the whole production depends on.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — timestamps in a message thread
**Target Buyer:** Production leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — feedback structure and revision tracking

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Editors work to a publishing schedule that never pauses, on feedback delivered as timestamps in a message thread, for a business whose success they cannot see themselves in. They make hundreds of judgement calls per video — pacing, cuts, music, emphasis — and receive direction that is a list of moments to change with no statement of what is wrong or what the video is trying to do. Turnover in the role is high and is treated as a labour market fact rather than as a design outcome.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Editing software, a review tool or a message thread, and a deadline. The gaps: no structured brief; feedback delivered as corrections rather than as direction; revision rounds unbounded; performance never fed back to the editor; and no visibility of which of their decisions worked.
