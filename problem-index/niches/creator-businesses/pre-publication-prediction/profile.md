# Pre-Publication Prediction

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/creator-businesses|Creator Businesses]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to rank a creator's candidate titles, thumbnails, topics and formats before anything is published — and whoever predicts best against that creator's own audience changes what gets made.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3.8B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — intuition and small tests
**Target Buyer:** Creator and channel leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — a ranking problem with a rich record

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This contest is selection. Before publishing, a creator has candidate topics, several title options, three thumbnails and choices about length and structure. The question is which combination will do best with their audience. It is a ranking and forecasting problem, answerable from the catalogue and from pooled data across similar channels, and it is used at the front of the process by a person deciding what to make.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Platform thumbnail testing where available, intuition, and title advice from other creators. The gaps: no prediction from the creator's own record; candidate options not compared systematically; topic selection unsupported; format and length choices unguided; and testing limited to what the platform chooses to offer.
