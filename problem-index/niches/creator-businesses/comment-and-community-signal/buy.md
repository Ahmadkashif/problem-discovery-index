**Industry:** [[industries/creator-businesses|Creator Businesses]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Product and market research built rigorous methods for analysing what customers say, and creators have more of it than any company and analyse none.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn the audience's own written feedback into the research the creator never commissions — and whoever reads it as data holds the only direct account of what the audience actually wants.

## The Problem
Voice of customer analysis is an established practice: systematic coding of open-ended feedback, theme extraction, sentiment tracking, linking feedback to segments and outcomes, and feeding it into product decisions with a defined process. Companies pay substantially for it and often have thin source material. Creators have an enormous, continuous, freely given stream of exactly this and treat it as a social feature.

## What Already Exists
Open-ended feedback coding methodologies; theme extraction and topic modelling; sentiment tracking over time; feedback-to-outcome linkage; and research operations practice for feeding it into decisions.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a public, performative, unsolicited corpus. It requires: (1) feedback given in public to an audience as well as to the creator, so it is performative and self-selecting in ways survey responses are not — this is the substantive difference and affects every inference; (2) attachment to specific content with measured outcomes, which is richer than most voice-of-customer data ever gets; (3) a parasocial relationship that shapes what is said and how; (4) volume that rules out manual coding entirely, where research practice assumes a sample; and (5) a one-person consumer with no research function, so the output must be a short list of decisions rather than a report.

## Target Customer
Creators and audience managers, management companies, research tooling vendors, and brands seeking audience understanding.

## Impact If Solved
Research practice is mature and usually starved of source material, and this profession is drowning in it. Handling a public, performative corpus attached to measured outcomes is the adaptation, and the outcome linkage makes it better data than surveys.
