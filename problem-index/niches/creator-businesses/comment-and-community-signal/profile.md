# Comment & Community Signal

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/creator-businesses|Creator Businesses]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn the audience's own written feedback into the research the creator never commissions — and whoever reads it as data holds the only direct account of what the audience actually wants.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.2B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — read at random, used for nothing
**Target Buyer:** Creator and audience leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — a text corpus with attached outcomes

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Under every upload the audience writes thousands of words about what they thought, what they wanted, what confused them, what they would like next, and what they are struggling with. Across a channel that is a large corpus of unsolicited audience research attached to specific pieces of content and specific outcomes. Creators read the first twenty comments, reply to a few, and use none of it systematically — while paying for audience insight they already have.

## Current Tools & Gaps
The comment section, a moderation tool, and occasional manual reading. The gaps: comments never analysed as a corpus; requests and questions not extracted; sentiment not tracked against content decisions; community platforms separate and unread; and the corpus discarded as the channel scales.
