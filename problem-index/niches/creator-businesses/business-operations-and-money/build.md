# One View of a Multi-Source Business

**Niche:** [[niches/creator-businesses/business-operations-and-money/profile|Business Operations & Money]]
**Industry:** [[industries/creator-businesses|Creator Businesses]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Revenue arrives from eight sources on eight schedules and the business is run from a spreadsheet updated when someone remembers.
**Tags:** #data-integration #time-series-forecasting #revenue-impact #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #automation #workflow-orchestration #descriptive-statistics
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to run a multi-source, volatile, contractor-heavy business without a finance function — and whoever automates it gives the creator back the hours the spreadsheet takes and the confidence the volatility removes.

## The Problem
Platform payouts arrive monthly with a lag and vary by a factor of three. Brand deals are invoiced and paid on their own terms, sometimes late. Affiliate revenue trickles. Memberships recur. Product sales spike. Costs go to contractors across several countries with different payment methods and tax treatments. The creator's view of all this is a spreadsheet, a bank balance and an instinct, and they make hiring and production decisions from it.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Accounting software was built for businesses with invoices and a chart of accounts, so a creator's revenue sources do not fit the model — a product that expects conventional transactions cannot describe a platform payout report. Each platform reports differently and none integrate. The creator is not a finance person and is not going to become one. And the accountant's involvement is annual, which is too late for every decision that matters.

## What to Build
Consolidate and forecast. Integrate every revenue source into one view with a consistent structure, which is the core and is the thing no existing product does. Forecast income with its volatility expressed as a range, since a single number is misleading and a range is actionable for the decisions the creator actually makes. Report revenue by source and by content, as the margin per video and the value of each revenue line are both unknown and both decision-relevant. Track cost per production including contractor time, which is what tells a creator whether a format is viable. Automate contractor payment across countries with the tax documentation handled, because this is a real operational burden and an area where creators routinely get it wrong. Flag cash shortfalls ahead of time, since volatility plus fixed costs is how these businesses fail. Plan tax as the year progresses rather than in a panic afterwards. Separate the business from the household deliberately, as the boundary is habitually blurred and causes problems later. Give the accountant a clean continuous feed instead of a year-end reconstruction. Detect late or missing payments, since brand deals are frequently unpaid and unchased. And report profitability rather than revenue, which is the number creators most often do not know.

## Target Customer
Creators and business managers, accountants serving creators, management companies, and small business financial software vendors whose model does not fit.

## Impact If Built
A product that expects conventional transactions cannot describe a platform payout report, so accounting software does not fit this business. Consolidating the sources and forecasting with an honest range is what turns instinct into a decision basis.
