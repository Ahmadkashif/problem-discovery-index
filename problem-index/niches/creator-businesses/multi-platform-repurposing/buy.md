# Broadcast Versioning and Localisation

**Industry:** [[industries/creator-businesses|Creator Businesses]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Broadcast and film built industrial versioning pipelines to produce dozens of variants of one asset, and creators re-edit by hand.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make one piece of content become six without six times the work — and whoever breaks the linear relationship between platforms and production hours changes what a small team can cover.

## The Problem
Broadcast and film solved producing many versions of one asset: conform and versioning pipelines, automated deliverable specifications per distributor, subtitle and caption workflows, aspect ratio conversion, and quality control against each destination's technical requirements. A feature film ships in dozens of versions and the pipeline handles it. Creators produce a similar number of variants per week, manually.

## What Already Exists
Versioning and conform pipelines; deliverable specification management; automated subtitling and caption conformance; aspect ratio and reframing tools; and automated quality control against destination specs.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to editorial rather than technical versioning. It requires: (1) selecting different content for each destination rather than reformatting the same content, which is an editorial judgement broadcast pipelines never make — this is the substantive difference; (2) platform conventions that are audience expectations rather than technical specifications, so conformance cannot be checked mechanically; (3) a one-person or small team with no engineering capacity, where broadcast pipelines assume specialists; (4) feedback from derivative performance, which broadcast has no equivalent of and which is the learning signal here; and (5) a weekly cadence at a cost point that rules out per-asset human conform.

## Target Customer
Production leadership, creators and editors, management companies, and media supply chain vendors with no creator offering.

## Impact If Solved
Broadcast versioning handles technical variation and this problem is editorial. Selecting different content per destination, learned from derivative performance, is what the industrial pipeline has no concept of.
