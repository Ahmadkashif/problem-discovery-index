# Multi-Platform Repurposing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/creator-businesses|Creator Businesses]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make one piece of content become six without six times the work — and whoever breaks the linear relationship between platforms and production hours changes what a small team can cover.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3.5B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~14% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — manual re-editing per platform
**Target Buyer:** Production leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — segmentation, reframing and adaptation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A creator publishes to six platforms, each with a different format, length convention, aspect ratio, caption style and audience expectation. One long piece becomes a set of short vertical cuts, a different set for another platform, a text post, a podcast segment and a newsletter item. Every derivative is cut by hand. The work scales linearly with platforms and the team does not, which is the binding constraint on how many surfaces a creator can occupy.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Editing software, clip-generation tools, scheduling platforms and a lot of manual work. The gaps: clip selection not driven by what performs; reframing and captioning still supervised heavily; platform conventions applied by hand; performance of derivatives not fed back; and no plan for which platform gets what.
