# Content Performance

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/creator-businesses|Creator Businesses]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell a creator which choices will work and which choices caused what happened — and the contest splits cleanly enough that it is not terminal.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$7B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~28% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — dashboards without mechanism
**Target Buyer:** Creator and channel leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the record exists and is unused

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Everything else in a creator business follows from this. Which topic, which title, which thumbnail, which length, which posting time — the creator chooses and the platform decides how many people see it. The analytics return click-through and retention curves that conflate the creator's decision with the platform's distribution choice, and the entire craft of the profession is inference from that confound. It is the category's largest contest and it is two: choosing before publication and explaining after it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Platform analytics, thumbnail testing where offered, third-party dashboards and a great deal of anecdote. The gaps: the creator's own back catalogue never treated as an experimental record; distribution and decision effects not separated; no prediction before publishing; no controlled comparison; and advice built entirely on other people's results.
