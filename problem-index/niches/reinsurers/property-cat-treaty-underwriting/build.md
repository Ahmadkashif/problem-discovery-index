# A Graded Ledger of Every Load Against the Model

**Niche:** [[niches/reinsurers/property-cat-treaty-underwriting/profile|Property Catastrophe Treaty Underwriting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/reinsurers|Reinsurers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** The adjustment from modelled loss cost to bound price is the most consequential judgement in property cat, and no reinsurer can say which of its underwriters' loads predicted anything.
**Tags:** #tacit-knowledge-ml #gradient-boosting #bayesian-inference #causal-inference #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a cedent's submission into a quoted own view of risk, with every load against the vendor model explained, before the broker's firm-order deadline, and whoever's loads still look right after the next large event takes the account.

## The Problem
A lead underwriter loads the model for insured values they believe are understated, a cedent's claims culture, litigation in its states and perils the model treats lightly. Those loads can add or remove a large share of the technical price (estimate). They are stored as one factor, without reasons, and a large event that grades them may not reach the cedent for a decade.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Model vendors record model output, not the human adjustment to it. Pricing workbooks are personal, and recording reasons during the renewal crunch is a cost with no visible return. Catastrophe labels are rare and correlated, which has been allowed to stop anyone from trying.

## What to Build
A ledger that records every load as a separate, reasoned component, drafted from the workbook and memo and confirmed in seconds. It grades each component against the cedent's experience-to-model ratio in every event, including moderate storms that never touched the layers, using PCS and PERILS estimates as the market benchmark. Hierarchical models pool evidence across cedents so the first useful conclusions arrive within a few seasons.

## Target Customer
Chief underwriting officers and heads of cat pricing at reinsurers that lead property programmes, and the capital teams that defend the own view of risk to rating agencies and the Bermuda Monetary Authority.

## Impact If Built
Loads that can be defended to brokers in a soft market, the ones that do not predict can be dropped, and a retiring lead underwriter's reads stay with the firm.
