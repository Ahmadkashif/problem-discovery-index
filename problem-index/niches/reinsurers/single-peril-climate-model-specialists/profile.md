# Single-Peril & Climate Model Specialists

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/reinsurers|Reinsurers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms that build hazard and loss models for one peril or region the large vendors cover thinly, or forward-looking climate views of existing perils: flood (JBA, Fathom, which Swiss Re owns), tropical cyclone (Reask), US flood and wildfire (KatRisk) and climate-conditioned event sets (all recalled). Reinsurers license them to build an own view of risk that does not depend on one vendor, and to price perils where the main models are weak.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 hydrologists, meteorologists, catastrophe modellers and data scientists per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Hazard maps and event sets, peril loss models, climate scenario views, model documentation for validation
**Proprietary data:** The firm's own hazard simulations, hydraulic and climate model runs, and claims calibration contributed by clients
**Clock:** Renewal dates, model validation cycles under Solvency II and the Bermuda regime, and event response
**Buyer:** Chief Scientist / Head of Insurance Products
