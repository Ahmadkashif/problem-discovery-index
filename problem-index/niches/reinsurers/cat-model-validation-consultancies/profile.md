# Cat Model Validation & Own-View-of-Risk Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/reinsurers|Reinsurers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Actuarial and catastrophe consultancies that review vendor models for a reinsurer, adjust them to an internal view and document the validation that rating agencies and supervisors expect. Milliman sells catastrophe model review and adjustment: changing vulnerability assumptions, blending models and calibrating to a cedent's experience. Other actuarial firms and independent cat specialists do similar work (recalled). S&P has said it takes a negative view of reinsurers that do not vet and validate vendor models.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-80 catastrophe and actuarial consultants per firm's practice (estimate)
**Output:** Model validation reports, adjusted views of risk, model change impact studies, documentation for internal model approval
**Proprietary data:** Cross-client experience of model performance; the portfolios and model runs are the clients', run on the clients' vendor licences
**Clock:** Annual model validation for Solvency II and the Bermuda regime, rating agency reviews, vendor model version releases
**Buyer:** Catastrophe Practice Leader
