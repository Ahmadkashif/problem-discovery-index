# Legal Contract AI Taught Treaty Clauses

**Niche:** [[niches/reinsurers/treaty-wording-contract-certainty/profile|Treaty Wording & Contract Certainty]]
**Industry:** [[industries/reinsurers|Reinsurers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Contract AI already finds and compares clauses in commercial agreements; it does not know a reinsurance treaty's clause types or which market model clause a wording departs from.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #bert #word-embeddings #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to compare each renewal wording with the expiring contract, the agreed slip and the market model clauses clause by clause before signing, and whoever catches the material deviations before a loss tests them takes the account.

## The Problem
Generic tools flag every changed sentence with equal weight and miss changes in meaning when the drafting moves between sections.

## What Already Exists
Kira, Luminance, Harvey and contract lifecycle platforms, with clause libraries for commercial, finance and real-estate agreements.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: a treaty clause taxonomy (event definition, hours clause, ultimate net loss, reinstatements, territorial scope, exclusions, claims cooperation, commutation, insolvency and offset); recognition of market model clauses and deviations; per-cedent clause history across renewals; and slip-to-wording comparison.

## Target Customer
Reinsurer wording teams and broker contract-certainty functions already licensing legal AI.

## Impact If Solved
Reviews focused on the material clauses, cutting time per wording from about a day to about an hour (estimate).
