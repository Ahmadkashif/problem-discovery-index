# Reinsurance Trade Associations & Loss Development Study

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/reinsurers|Reinsurers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Reinsurance Association of America, which compiles a historical loss development study from reinsurers' casualty data used as a benchmark across the market, and the Association of Bermuda Insurers and Reinsurers, which represents the Bermuda market (recalled). Both mostly advocate on collateral, tax and regulation.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research staff per association (estimate)
**Output:** Loss development study, policy papers, member surveys
**Proprietary data:** Pooled member loss development data
**Clock:** Self-set study cycles
**Buyer:** President / chief executive
