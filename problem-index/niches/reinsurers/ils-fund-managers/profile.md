# ILS Fund Managers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/reinsurers|Reinsurers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Investment managers that pool institutional capital into cat bonds, collateralised reinsurance, industry loss warranties, sidecars and retrocession. Fermat Capital Management reached ~$11B of ILS assets at 1 July 2026, and Twelve Securis formed in 2025 with ~$8.5B (Artemis). Nephila, Elementum, Schroders Capital, LGT ILS Partners and others are peers (recalled). Third-party capital reached a record $136B at end-2025 (Aon). Managers are paid management and performance fees by investors.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 portfolio managers, cat modellers and analysts per manager (estimate)
**Output:** Portfolio construction, cat bond and private deal pricing, investor reporting and event loss estimates
**Proprietary data:** Pricing and allocation history across the cat bond market and the private deals each manager has seen
**Clock:** Renewal dates, cat bond issuance windows and investor reporting cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Investment Officer / Head of Risk
