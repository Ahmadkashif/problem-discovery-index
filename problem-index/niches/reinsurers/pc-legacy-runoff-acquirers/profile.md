# P&C Legacy & Run-Off Acquirers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/reinsurers|Reinsurers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Consolidators that take on closed property-casualty liabilities through loss portfolio transfers, adverse development covers and outright purchases of run-off companies, then manage the claims to closure: Enstar, Catalina, Compre, Premia and peers (recalled). Their deal actuaries and claims teams price each reserve block from the seller's data room. They are the P&C counterpart of the life consolidators logged as [[niches/life-insurance-carriers/inforce-block-acquirers/profile|🔍 In-Force Block Acquirers & Closed-Block Consolidators]] (34, kill switch).

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 deal actuaries, claims specialists and analysts per acquirer (estimate)
**Output:** Reserve valuations and bids, claims run-off strategies, commutation programmes
**Proprietary data:** Run-off experience on blocks already acquired, plus data rooms from every block bid on
**Clock:** Bid deadlines set by the seller's adviser
**Buyer:** Head of Transactions / Chief Actuary
