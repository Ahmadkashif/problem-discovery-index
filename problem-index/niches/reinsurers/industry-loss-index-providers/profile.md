# Industry Loss Index Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/reinsurers|Reinsurers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms that estimate the insured loss to the whole market from each catastrophe and publish the figure that settles industry loss warranties, index-triggered cat bonds and retro covers. Verisk's Property Claim Services (PCS) assigns catastrophe serial numbers to events above a ~$25M insured-loss threshold (estimate) and surveys insurers for its estimates. PERILS AG in Zurich builds its indices from exposure and loss data reported by participating insurers and extended severe convective storm reporting to Europe, Japan and New Zealand from August 2025. Industry loss triggers, mostly PCS, covered almost 27% ($8.3B) of outstanding cat bond capital at end-2017 (Artemis). Reinsurers, ILS funds, brokers and modellers subscribe.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 catastrophe analysts, actuaries and data staff per provider (estimate)
**Output:** Event serial numbers; preliminary, resurvey and final industry loss estimates by event, peril and region; industry exposure databases
**Proprietary data:** Insurer-reported losses and exposures by zone and line, collected for the purpose of index construction; decades of event estimates and their revisions
**Clock:** Publication timetables after each event, fixed in the providers' own methodologies and written into the contracts that reference them
**Buyer:** Head of PCS / Chief Executive of PERILS

**Kill switches:** none — insurers report data to PERILS so that it can build aggregate indices (its published CORE methodology), and PCS publishes market-level estimates; the aggregate use is the stated basis of contribution.
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. The cleanest new pocket: the estimate is the invoice and the corpus is uniquely theirs, and every estimate is later graded by its own final figure. It falls short because analysts grow with the number of events rather than with clients (Q2 3), the reporting dates are the providers' own (Q4 3, lesson (a)), and the market is two firms (Q5 2, lesson (h)). This is the pocket [[niches/property-casualty-insurers/_overview|Property & Casualty Insurers]] left to this industry.
