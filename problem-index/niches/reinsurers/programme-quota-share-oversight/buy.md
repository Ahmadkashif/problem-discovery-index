# Lloyd's Bordereaux Platforms for US Programme Quota Shares

**Niche:** [[niches/reinsurers/programme-quota-share-oversight/profile|MGA & Programme Quota Share Oversight]]
**Industry:** [[industries/reinsurers|Reinsurers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** The Lloyd's delegated-authority market built bordereaux standards and platforms over many years; US programme reinsurers still process the same data in spreadsheets.
**Tags:** #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to see a delegated-authority programme's underwriting drift in its monthly bordereaux before the loss ratio shows it, and whoever detects drift early enough to reprice or exit at the next renewal takes the account.

## The Problem
US programmes ceded through fronting carriers report on each MGA's own templates, and the reinsurer validates them by hand.

## What Already Exists
Bordereaux standards and processing platforms used for Lloyd's coverholders, which validate, map and aggregate premium and claims bordereaux (recalled).

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: US state and class coding; fronting-carrier layouts and the fronting fee and ceding commission structure; links to the reinsurer's treaty terms (share, commission scale, loss corridors); rate adequacy measures per exposure unit by class; and drift alerts rather than only validation errors.

## Target Customer
Programme reinsurance teams at reinsurers and fronting carriers' reinsurance panels.

## Impact If Solved
Monthly bordereaux checked and comparable within days of receipt, using standards already proven in a neighbouring market.
