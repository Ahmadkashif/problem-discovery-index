# MGA & Programme Quota Share Oversight

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/reinsurers|Reinsurers]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to see a delegated-authority programme's underwriting drift in its monthly bordereaux before the loss ratio shows it, and whoever detects drift early enough to reprice or exit at the next renewal takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$500M a year of reinsurer spend on programme underwriting audit, bordereaux processing and portfolio monitoring for quota shares behind MGAs and fronting carriers (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of reinsurers' property-casualty analytics and tooling spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — bordereaux are spreadsheets in each MGA's layout, audited on site or by sample
**Target Buyer:** Head of Programme Business / Delegated Underwriting Authority Manager
**Automation Potential:** High for bordereaux normalisation and drift detection; medium for audit

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A growing share of US casualty and specialty premium is written by MGAs on fronting carriers' paper, with most of the risk ceded to reinsurers on quota share. The reinsurer bears the loss but never sees the submissions; it sees monthly premium and claims bordereaux and an annual audit. The contest is detection: noticing that a programme's mix, rate adequacy or claims pattern has shifted before a year of losses proves it. The fronting carriers' own oversight is logged as [[niches/property-casualty-insurers/fronting-carrier-programme-oversight/profile|🔍 Fronting Carriers' Programme Oversight]], and the MGAs as [[niches/independent-insurance-agents/mga-program-underwriters/profile|🔍 MGAs & Program Underwriters]]. This niche is the capacity provider's side of the same programmes.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Bordereaux management platforms from the Lloyd's delegated-authority market, spreadsheets, and audit firms (recalled). The gaps: bordereaux in forty layouts; rate change and mix shift visible only after manual comparison; and audits that sample files once a year.
