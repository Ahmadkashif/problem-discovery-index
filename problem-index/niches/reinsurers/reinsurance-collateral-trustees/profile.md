# Reinsurance Collateral Trustees & Letter-of-Credit Banks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/reinsurers|Reinsurers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Banks and trust companies that hold reinsurance collateral: Reg 114 trusts for unauthorised reinsurers, letters of credit, and collateral trusts for cat bonds and collateralised reinsurance. Since 2022 qualifying reciprocal-jurisdiction reinsurers post no US collateral (NAIC), but ILS capacity remains fully collateralised. Related custody work is described under [[industries/trust-companies|Trust Companies]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** Operations and relationship staff; no research function (estimate)
**Output:** Trust administration, collateral statements, draws and releases
**Proprietary data:** Collateral balances and movements, held for clients
**Clock:** Quarter-end collateral true-ups and draw notices
**Buyer:** Head of Insurance Trust Services
