# Reinsurance Supervisors (Bermuda Monetary Authority & NAIC Reinsurance Groups)

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/reinsurers|Reinsurers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The supervisors whose reviews are specific to reinsurance. The Bermuda Monetary Authority supervises the Class 3B and Class 4 reinsurers that make Bermuda the largest property cat domicile. It requires them to file a Catastrophe Risk Return within their Capital and Solvency Return, and its staff analyse those returns for an annual Catastrophe Risk Modelling Report (BMA). In the US, NAIC working groups review certified and reciprocal-jurisdiction reinsurers for collateral purposes; all 56 NAIC jurisdictions adopted the reciprocal-jurisdiction collateral rules by September 2022 (NAIC). That NAIC slice belongs to the state regulator family logged as [[niches/public-adjusters/state-insurance-regulators/profile|🔍 State Insurance Departments & Market Conduct]] (36) and is not scored again here.

## Insight Function
**Size:** No named BMA analytics or catastrophe-modelling unit of 10+ was found. Supervision is carried out by examination and supervisory staff, which is production headcount under lesson (j), and the NAIC reinsurance groups are a handful of state staff (estimate)
**Output:** Solvency reviews, catastrophe stress assessments, the annual catastrophe risk report, reinsurer approvals and collateral determinations
**Proprietary data:** Statutory capital and catastrophe exposure returns from every regulated reinsurer
**Clock:** Supervisory review timetables; the statutory filing deadlines bind the reinsurers, not the supervisor's own work
**Buyer:** Director of Insurance Supervision
