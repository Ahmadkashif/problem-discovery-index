# Cat Bond Risk Modelling & Calculation Agents

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/reinsurers|Reinsurers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
The capital-markets teams at the catastrophe modelling firms (Verisk, Moody's RMS and KCC, recalled) that act as modelling agent for cat bonds. They produce the risk analysis printed in each offering circular (attachment and exhaustion probabilities and expected loss), run annual resets for bonds whose risk is re-modelled, and act as calculation agent after events for parametric and modelled-loss triggers. Sponsors pay per deal; the cat bond market reached a record almost $65.6B outstanding in mid-2026 (Artemis).

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 modellers and analysts per vendor's capital-markets team (estimate)
**Output:** Offering circular risk analyses, reset reports, post-event calculation reports
**Proprietary data:** The vendor's models, applied to the sponsor's exposure data for each bond
**Clock:** Deal pricing and closing timetables set by sponsors and bookrunners, reset dates and post-event calculation deadlines in bond terms
**Buyer:** Head of Capital Markets Modelling
