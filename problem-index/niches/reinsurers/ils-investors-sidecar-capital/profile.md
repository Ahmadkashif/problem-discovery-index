# ILS Investors & Sidecar Capital Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/reinsurers|Reinsurers]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give the end investor an independent estimate of event losses and trapped collateral within days of a catastrophe, and whoever's early estimates still hold after loss creep takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$100M a year of analytics, reporting and consultant spend by end investors in cat bonds, ILS funds, sidecars and collateralised reinsurance (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% equivalent of reinsurers' property-casualty analytics and tooling spend; the spend itself sits with end investors and their consultants (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — cat bond prices and offering circulars are digital; sidecar and collateralised reinsurance reporting is periodic PDFs from the manager or sponsor
**Target Buyer:** Head of Alternatives or ILS allocation at a pension fund, endowment or sovereign fund; investment consultant covering ILS
**Automation Potential:** High for event exposure mapping and collateral tracking; medium for loss estimation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Third-party capital reached a record $136B at end-2025 (Aon), and the cat bond market a record almost $65.6B outstanding in mid-2026 (Artemis). Behind that capital sit pension funds and other institutions, many of them profiled under [[industries/pension-funds|Pension Funds]], who hold the risk through ILS managers and reinsurer-sponsored sidecars. After an event they need to know what they have lost and when their collateral will come back. Collateral is often held for months while losses develop, and early estimates have crept upward after past storms (recalled). Every tool in the chain serves the cedent, the reinsurer or the manager. The end investor gets the manager's report.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Manager and sponsor reports, cat bond price indices and dealer marks, Artemis and the trade press, and investment consultants' qualitative reviews. The gaps: no independent event-loss view across an investor's holdings; trapped collateral not forecast; and manager estimates never compared against final outcomes.
