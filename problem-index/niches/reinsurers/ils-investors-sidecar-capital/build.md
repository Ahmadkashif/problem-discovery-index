# An Independent Event Loss and Collateral Tracker for Allocators

**Niche:** [[niches/reinsurers/ils-investors-sidecar-capital/profile|ILS Investors & Sidecar Capital Providers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/reinsurers|Reinsurers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A pension fund with ILS across three managers and two sidecars learns its loss from a big storm in five different formats over several months, with no independent check.
**Tags:** #monte-carlo-methods #bayesian-inference #time-series-forecasting #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give the end investor an independent estimate of event losses and trapped collateral within days of a catastrophe, and whoever's early estimates still hold after loss creep takes the account.

## The Problem
Each manager reports its own estimate on its own timetable. The investor cannot see overlap between holdings in the same event, compare managers' estimates or forecast when collateral will be released.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Tools are sold to managers and reinsurers, who have the data and the budget. End investors are numerous, small buyers of analytics, and position data sits with managers.

## What to Build
A tracker that maps an investor's cat bond holdings (from public offering terms and triggers) and reported private positions to each event. It estimates loss ranges from industry loss index estimates and vendor footprints, forecasts collateral release from the development patterns of past events, and grades each manager's early estimates against final outcomes.

## Target Customer
Heads of alternatives at pension funds and endowments with ILS allocations, and the consultants who advise them.

## Impact If Built
An independent view within days of an event and a record of which managers estimate honestly, which is what allocators most want to know.
