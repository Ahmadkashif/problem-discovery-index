# Private Markets Monitoring Adapted to ILS

**Niche:** [[niches/reinsurers/ils-investors-sidecar-capital/profile|ILS Investors & Sidecar Capital Providers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/reinsurers|Reinsurers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Institutional investors already use portfolio monitoring platforms that ingest manager reports for private equity and credit; ILS reporting needs the same plumbing with catastrophe logic.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #feature-engineering #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #evaluation-metrics
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give the end investor an independent estimate of event losses and trapped collateral within days of a catastrophe, and whoever's early estimates still hold after loss creep takes the account.

## The Problem
ILS reports arrive as PDFs and spreadsheets from each manager and are filed rather than analysed.

## What Already Exists
Private markets monitoring and document extraction platforms used by limited partners, of the kind profiled under pension funds' private markets monitoring pockets.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: event-level loss and reserve fields; collateral held and projected release dates; peril and region exposure by position; mapping of cat bonds to their triggers and industry loss index dependencies; and cross-manager aggregation by event.

## Target Customer
Pension funds and endowments already licensing a monitoring platform for other alternatives.

## Impact If Solved
ILS joins the rest of the alternatives book in one monitored view, with event exposure aggregated across managers.
