# Collateral Trapped With No Release Date

**Niche:** [[niches/reinsurers/ils-investors-sidecar-capital/profile|ILS Investors & Sidecar Capital Providers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/reinsurers|Reinsurers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** After a large event an investor's collateral is held against possible losses for months, and nobody tells them when it is likely to come back.
**Tags:** #survival-analysis #time-series-forecasting #evaluation-metrics #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give the end investor an independent estimate of event losses and trapped collateral within days of a catastrophe, and whoever's early estimates still hold after loss creep takes the account.

## The Problem
Collateralised reinsurance and sidecar capital is held back at maturity when a covered event may still develop into a loss. The investor sees the capital as illiquid and unproductive with no schedule for its release.

## Why It's Still Broken
Release depends on cedent loss development the investor cannot observe, and managers report trapped amounts without forecasting them.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A release forecast per position, built from the development of comparable past events, updated as cedents report, with the probability of release by date.

## Who Feels the Pain
Allocators planning liquidity; managers fielding investor calls; sponsors renewing capacity with investors who are still waiting.

## Impact If Fixed
Liquidity planned rather than guessed, and investors more willing to roll capital into the next season.
