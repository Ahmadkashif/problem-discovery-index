# Reinsurance Commutation, Claims Audit & Arbitration Support

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/reinsurers|Reinsurers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Consultancies and expert firms that audit cedents' claims for reinsurers, value reserves for commutations (one-off settlements that close a treaty's future obligations), and support reinsurance arbitrations as experts, umpires and analysts. Most disputes go to arbitration panels under treaty clauses rather than to court, and much of the work is commissioned through outside counsel (recalled).

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 claims auditors, actuaries and experts per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Claims audit reports, commutation valuations, expert reports and testimony
**Proprietary data:** Cross-engagement experience of cedent claims practices and settlement values; the files belong to the parties
**Clock:** Arbitration hearing schedules set by panels, commutation deal dates, audit windows in treaty access-to-records clauses
**Buyer:** Practice Leader, Reinsurance Disputes
