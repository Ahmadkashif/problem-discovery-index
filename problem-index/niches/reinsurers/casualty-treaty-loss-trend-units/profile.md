# Casualty Treaty Actuarial & Loss-Trend Units

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/reinsurers|Reinsurers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The casualty pricing actuaries, reserving teams and loss-trend analysts inside reinsurers. They fit trend and development to cedents' ceded triangles and large-loss listings, study social inflation by line and venue, and set the assumptions casualty treaties are priced and reserved on. Some publish trend research to clients; Swiss Re's 2024 social inflation index estimated a 57% rise in US liability claims over a decade.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 casualty actuaries and analysts per large reinsurer (estimate)
**Output:** Trend and development selections, treaty prices, reserve reviews, occasional published trend studies
**Proprietary data:** Ceded triangles and large-loss histories across hundreds of cedents, which no single carrier sees
**Clock:** Renewal dates and quarterly reserve reviews; contract and internal dates rather than a regulator's
**Buyer:** Chief Casualty Actuary
