# ILS Market Data, Indices & News Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/reinsurers|Reinsurers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Publishers and data services that track the insurance-linked securities market: Artemis's deal directory and quarterly cat bond reports, trade-press ILS desks, cat bond price indices run by banks and managers, and secondary-market pricing reports (recalled). Their deal directories underpin most public figures on the market, such as its record almost $65.6B outstanding in mid-2026.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically under 10 editorial and data staff per service (estimate)
**Output:** Deal directories, market reports, index levels, news
**Proprietary data:** Deal-by-deal records compiled from offering documents and sources
**Clock:** Publication calendars
**Buyer:** Publisher / editor
