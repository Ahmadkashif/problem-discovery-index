# Reinsurer Research Institutes & Published Catastrophe Databases

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/reinsurers|Reinsurers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The research arms the large reinsurers run as thought leadership for the carriers they sell capacity to: the Swiss Re Institute's sigma reports and catastrophe database, Munich Re's NatCatSERVICE, and the market reports of Gallagher Re, Aon and Guy Carpenter (recalled). They publish catastrophe loss tallies, market outlooks and trend studies, such as Swiss Re's 2024 social inflation index, free or to clients.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-100 economists, researchers and data staff at the largest institutes (estimate)
**Output:** Published reports, catastrophe loss databases, market outlooks, client briefings
**Proprietary data:** Decades of catastrophe loss records and market data compiled in house
**Clock:** Self-set publication calendars, often timed to renewals and conferences
**Buyer:** Head of the research institute
