# Commercial Underwriting Workbenches for Single-Risk Fac

**Niche:** [[niches/reinsurers/facultative-underwriting/profile|Facultative Reinsurance Underwriting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/reinsurers|Reinsurers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Primary commercial carriers already use underwriting workbenches that ingest broker submissions and prioritise them; fac desks need the same with reinsurance terms and engineering depth.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #feature-engineering #workflow-orchestration #automation #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to read an individual-risk submission (engineering survey, statement of values, loss runs) and return a priced line inside the broker's turnaround, and whoever answers fastest without missing the risk's worst feature takes the account.

## The Problem
Fac submissions arrive by email and are triaged by whoever sees them first, so attractive mid-sized risks wait behind large, slow ones.

## What Already Exists
Submission ingestion and underwriting workbench products built for primary commercial lines, which extract data from broker emails and attachments and route by appetite (recalled).

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: reinsurance terms (share, attachment, the cedent's retention and line), engineering survey extraction at the depth fac requires, cedent as well as insured history, and appetite rules that weigh the reinsurer's existing aggregate on the same location or account through treaties.

## Target Customer
Fac desks at reinsurers that already run a primary-style workbench in a commercial lines affiliate.

## Impact If Solved
Submissions triaged in minutes by appetite and attractiveness, with the reinsurer's existing exposure to the same risk shown before anyone quotes.
