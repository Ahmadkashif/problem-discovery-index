# Reinsurer Property-Cat Pricing & Portfolio Analytics Units

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/reinsurers|Reinsurers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The catastrophe modelling, pricing and portfolio management teams inside the reinsurers themselves. They re-run every cedent's exposure, price layers, manage the portfolio's tail against appetite, and support the capital model and rating agency submissions. Their output goes into the reinsurer's own price and capital. No outside client pays for it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 cat modellers, pricing actuaries and portfolio analysts at a large reinsurer; 10-40 at a mid-sized Bermuda company (estimate)
**Output:** Technical prices per layer, portfolio roll-ups, accumulation reports, capital model inputs, event loss estimates
**Proprietary data:** Every submission the reinsurer has priced, across hundreds of cedents and many years, joined to its own bound terms and losses
**Clock:** The 1 January, 1 April, 1 June and 1 July renewals, contract dates set by the market rather than a regulator
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer / Head of Catastrophe Risk
