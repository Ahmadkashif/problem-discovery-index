# The Cash Call Lost in the Broker Chain

**Niche:** [[niches/reinsurers/claims-accounting-collateral/profile|Reinsurance Claims, Technical Accounting & Collateral]]
**Industry:** [[industries/reinsurers|Reinsurers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** After a large loss, cedents issue cash calls through brokers to every reinsurer on the panel, and some sit unread for weeks.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #automation #quick-win #compliance #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to agree each cedent's ceded balance, loss and collateral position against the contract terms before the quarter-end, and whoever keeps the fewest balances past 90 days takes the account.

## The Problem
A cash call asks for payment of a large loss before the next statement. It travels from cedent to broker to each reinsurer's claims and accounting inboxes, often as a PDF attachment among dozens of post-event emails. Late payment strains the relationship and the cedent's recoverables.

## Why It's Still Broken
No shared channel exists across cedents, brokers and reinsurers for cash calls, and each party treats them as ordinary email.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Classify incoming post-event documents automatically, extract cash calls with the contract, amount and due date, and track them on one board until paid, with escalations before the due date.

## Who Feels the Pain
Claims and accounting staff in the weeks after an event; cedents' reinsurance managers waiting for funds; brokers chasing both sides.

## Impact If Fixed
Cash calls paid on time after every event, at negligible cost.
