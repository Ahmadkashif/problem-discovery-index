# Treaty Administration Systems That Read Documents

**Niche:** [[niches/reinsurers/claims-accounting-collateral/profile|Reinsurance Claims, Technical Accounting & Collateral]]
**Industry:** [[industries/reinsurers|Reinsurers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Reinsurers already run treaty administration systems that know every contract's terms and still key statements and loss advices into them by hand.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to agree each cedent's ceded balance, loss and collateral position against the contract terms before the quarter-end, and whoever keeps the fewest balances past 90 days takes the account.

## The Problem
The administration system is the book of record, but its inputs are documents from cedents and brokers that staff transcribe.

## What Already Exists
Treaty administration and reinsurance accounting platforms (Sapiens' reinsurance system and others, recalled) and generic intelligent document processing tools.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: extraction tuned to statements of account, loss advices, cash calls and collateral notices; mapping to the system's contract and section identifiers; validation against contract terms before posting; and Schedule F and collateral requirement views that update as documents post.

## Target Customer
Reinsurance operations teams already licensing an administration platform.

## Impact If Solved
Documents post themselves with exceptions routed for review, using the system already in place.
