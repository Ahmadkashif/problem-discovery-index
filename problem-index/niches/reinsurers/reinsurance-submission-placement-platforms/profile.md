# Reinsurance Submission & Placement Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/reinsurers|Reinsurers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Software firms that move reinsurance submissions and placements online: Supercede (formerly Riskbook, rebranded in 2021) prepares and validates submission packs and runs placements; Tremor runs programmatic auctions; Whitespace and others serve London market placement (recalled where not checked). Cedents, brokers and reinsurers license them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Product and engineering teams; no dedicated analytical function selling insight (estimate)
**Output:** Software, data rooms, placement workflows
**Proprietary data:** Submission and placement records held on clients' behalf
**Clock:** Renewal dates
**Buyer:** Chief Executive / Head of Product
