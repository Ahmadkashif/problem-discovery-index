# Portfolio Roll-Up Engines Packaged for the Retro Panel

**Niche:** [[niches/reinsurers/retrocession-buyers-sellers/profile|Retrocession Buyers & Sellers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/reinsurers|Reinsurers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Reinsurers already roll up their treaty portfolios for capital and rating agency purposes; the same engine could produce the retro submission if it spoke the retro writer's language.
**Tags:** #monte-carlo-methods #evaluation-metrics #feature-engineering #data-integration #automation #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to present a reinsurer's whole treaty portfolio as one loss distribution that a retrocession writer can price without seeing the underlying cedents, and whoever's roll-up the retro panel accepts with the fewest data requests takes the account.

## The Problem
Capital teams produce portfolio curves for internal models and AM Best's BCAR. The retro buyer reformats them into a submission by hand, with different peril splits and occurrence versus aggregate views.

## What Already Exists
Portfolio aggregation in vendor platforms and dedicated engines (Analyze Re, recalled) used for capital modelling and marginal pricing.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: retro structures (occurrence and aggregate excess, industry loss warranties, top-and-drop covers) priced against the rolled-up book; peril-region splits that match retro triggers; disclosure controls that separate what the writer may see; and a basis-risk view where the retro uses an industry loss trigger such as PCS or PERILS.

## Target Customer
Retro buyers and capital modelling teams at reinsurers.

## Impact If Solved
One portfolio engine serves capital, rating agency and retro needs, and the retro submission is produced in hours rather than days.
