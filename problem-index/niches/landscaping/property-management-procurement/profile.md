# Property Management Grounds Procurement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landscaping|Landscaping]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The facilities and property management groups that buy grounds maintenance across large portfolios set specifications, run bids, and evaluate contractor performance — deciding which landscaping firms get the recurring contracts that Pass 1 calls the industry's economic foundation.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 sourcing analysts and facilities managers
**Output:** Specifications, bid evaluations, contractor scorecards, portfolio cost benchmarking
**Proprietary data:** Bid pricing and contractor performance across large property portfolios and many markets
**Clock:** Contract renewal cycles and budget seasons
**Buyer:** VP of Facilities Sourcing — internal cost centre
