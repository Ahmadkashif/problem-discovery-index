# Turf & Plant Diagnostic Laboratories

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landscaping|Landscaping]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Diagnostic laboratories identify the pathogen, nutrient deficiency, or abiotic disorder behind a failing lawn or landscape planting from submitted samples, and the diagnosis determines whether a contractor treats, replaces, or renegotiates a warranty claim.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 8-40 plant pathologists, diagnosticians, and technicians
**Output:** Diagnostic reports, pathogen identifications, treatment recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated diagnoses by crop, symptom, season, and region
**Clock:** Urgent — an unidentified pathogen spreads while the sample is in transit
**Buyer:** Lab director — many are university extension units
