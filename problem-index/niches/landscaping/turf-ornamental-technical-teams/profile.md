# Turf & Ornamental Technical Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landscaping|Landscaping]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The turf and ornamental divisions of the crop protection and fertility manufacturers employ agronomists and technical representatives who advise contractors and grounds managers on programme design — advice given to sell product.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-200 agronomists, field technical representatives, and researchers per major supplier
**Output:** Programme recommendations, efficacy trial data, technical training, diagnostic support
**Proprietary data:** Trial results and field observations across many regions and turf types
**Clock:** Application timing windows tied to growing degree days and pest pressure
**Buyer:** Technical Director / Head of Agronomy
