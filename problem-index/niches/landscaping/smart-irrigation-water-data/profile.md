# Smart Irrigation & Water Management Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landscaping|Landscaping]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Irrigation controller manufacturers and water management providers run the connected controllers that schedule watering against weather and soil data, and report water savings to customers and to the utilities that rebate them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 hydrologists, agronomists, and data staff
**Output:** Irrigation scheduling algorithms, water use and savings reporting, leak and fault detection
**Proprietary data:** Controller run-time and site data across large installed bases, joined to weather
**Clock:** Daily irrigation cycles and drought restriction periods
**Buyer:** VP of Product / Director of Water Management
