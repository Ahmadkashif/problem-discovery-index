# Snow Contract Weather Risk Management

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landscaping|Landscaping]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Snow removal is sold on seasonal contracts that transfer weather risk from the property owner to the contractor, and specialist providers price, structure, and sometimes insure that exposure — including the parametric products that pay out on measured snowfall.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-70 meteorologists, actuaries, and analysts
**Output:** Seasonal contract pricing, weather risk structures, event verification, parametric settlement
**Proprietary data:** Long climatological records joined to contract performance and claim outcomes by market
**Clock:** The winter season and event-by-event settlement
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer / Head of Weather Risk
