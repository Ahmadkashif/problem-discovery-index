# Landscape Architecture Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landscaping|Landscaping]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Landscape architects design the site plans that installation contractors build to — grading, drainage, planting, hardscape, and increasingly stormwater management — and stamp the drawings municipalities permit against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 landscape architects, designers, and technical staff per firm
**Output:** Site designs and construction documents, planting plans, stormwater and grading design, permitting support
**Proprietary data:** Design and specification records across projects, and observed plant performance where firms revisit sites
**Clock:** Permitting and construction schedules
**Buyer:** Principal / Managing Director
