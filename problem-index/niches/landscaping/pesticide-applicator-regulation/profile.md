# Pesticide Applicator Regulation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landscaping|Landscaping]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State pesticide programmes license commercial applicators, require use reporting in some states, investigate drift and misapplication complaints, and enforce label compliance across the lawn care industry.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-80 licensing analysts, inspectors, and enforcement staff per state
**Output:** Licensing and certification, use reporting, complaint investigations, enforcement actions
**Proprietary data:** Applicator records, use reports, and complaint histories by firm
**Clock:** Licence renewal cycles and complaint response timelines
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer
