# Landscape Field Service Software Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landscaping|Landscaping]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The field service platforms serving landscape contractors run estimating, scheduling, routing, and job costing, holding the crew hours and job margins that determine whether a recurring contract is profitable.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 product and data staff
**Output:** Estimating and job costing tools, routing and scheduling, contract renewal analytics
**Proprietary data:** Estimated versus actual crew hours and material use across many contractors, properties, and service types
**Clock:** Weekly service routes and seasonal contract renewal
**Buyer:** Head of Product
