# Arborist & Tree Risk Consulting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landscaping|Landscaping]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Consulting arborists assess whether a tree is likely to fail and what it would hit, producing the risk assessment that decides removal, pruning, or retention — and that determines liability when a tree comes down on a person or a building.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 consulting arborists and technicians per firm
**Output:** Tree risk assessments, inventory and condition surveys, removal and mitigation recommendations, expert testimony
**Proprietary data:** Condition assessments across large tree populations; failure outcomes are rarely fed back
**Clock:** Storm response and municipal inspection cycles
**Buyer:** Principal / Consulting practice leader
