# Landscape Supply Distribution Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landscaping|Landscaping]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The national landscape supply distributors sell irrigation, fertilizer, nursery stock, and hardscape to contractors through hundreds of branches, and their analytics groups see what the industry actually buys, by contractor and by market.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-250 category, pricing, and supply chain analysts
**Output:** Assortment and inventory planning, pricing analysis, contractor segmentation, branch performance
**Proprietary data:** Contractor-level purchase histories across the industry — the only view of what landscape businesses actually install and when
**Clock:** Sharp seasonal peaks and weather-driven demand
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Category Management
