# Notary Bond & E&O Programs

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/notary-signing-services|Notary & Signing Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Surety companies that write the bonds many states require for a notary commission, and insurers that write notary errors-and-omissions policies, often sold through notary associations and supply vendors. Premiums are small and policies are issued almost automatically at commissioning.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 underwriting and claims staff dedicated to notary business at any one carrier (estimate)
**Output:** Bond and policy issuance; claims handling
**Proprietary data:** Claims on notarial acts, mostly forged-signature and impersonation cases
**Clock:** Commission start and renewal dates
**Buyer:** Program manager at the carrier or agency
