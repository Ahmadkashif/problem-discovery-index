# Forensic Document Examiners & Notary Expert Witnesses

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/notary-signing-services|Notary & Signing Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation firms

## What They Do
Handwriting and document examiners who testify on whether a signature, seal or journal entry is genuine, and notary-practice experts retained in suits over forged deeds, capacity disputes and notary negligence. Attorneys and insurers pay by the hour.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 1-5 per practice
**Output:** Examination reports and testimony
**Proprietary data:** Case files and reference exemplars
**Clock:** Court scheduling orders and trial dates
**Buyer:** Litigating attorney
