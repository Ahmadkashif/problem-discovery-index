# National Signing Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/notary-signing-services|Notary & Signing Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Companies that take signing orders from title companies and lenders nationwide and dispatch independent agents from their own panels, keeping the spread between the client's fee and the agent's. They compete on coverage, speed and redraw rate.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Coordinators and QC staff, typically under 10 doing any analysis (estimate); the rest is dispatch production
**Output:** Completed signings and status reports
**Proprietary data:** Their own order and agent outcome history
**Clock:** Closing dates and funding cutoffs
**Buyer:** Owner or operations director
