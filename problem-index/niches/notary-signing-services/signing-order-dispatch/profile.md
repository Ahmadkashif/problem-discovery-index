# Signing Order Dispatch

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/notary-signing-services|Notary & Signing Services]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to fill each signing order, within minutes of receipt, with the agent most likely to arrive on time and finish clean, and whoever predicts agent reliability best takes the title company's overflow orders.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$250M a year in signing-service margin and coordination labour on top of agent fees (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — orders arrive through portals and Snapdocs; confirmation and exception handling run on phones and email
**Target Buyer:** Signing-service owners and operations managers; title companies running in-house notary networks
**Automation Potential:** High — assignment, confirmation, tracking and status reporting are rule- and data-driven

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Signing services sit between title companies and thousands of independent agents. A coordinator receives an order, finds an agent in the right area with the right commission, certification and screening, negotiates the fee, confirms with the borrower, chases documents to the agent, tracks the appointment, and reports status back to the title company, often a dozen touches per order (estimate). Volume swings with interest rates and peaks at month end, so services cannot staff for the peaks. The service's margin is the spread between the title company's fee and what the agent accepts, and its reputation is its no-show and redraw rate.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Snapdocs for search, offers and tracking; signing-service portals; spreadsheets, group texts and email. Snapdocs' ranking uses order volume and quality, but individual services hold their own reliability history and rarely use it beyond memory. No tool predicts which agent will complete cleanly on this order at this hour.
