# Field-Service Dispatch Adapted to Notary Orders

**Niche:** [[niches/notary-signing-services/signing-order-dispatch/profile|Signing Order Dispatch]]
**Industry:** [[industries/notary-signing-services|Notary & Signing Services]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Plumbing and HVAC firms dispatch technicians with mature field-service software; signing services dispatch notaries by group text.
**Tags:** #decision-trees #time-series-forecasting #evaluation-metrics #workflow-orchestration #automation #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to fill each signing order, within minutes of receipt, with the agent most likely to arrive on time and finish clean, and whoever predicts agent reliability best takes the title company's overflow orders.

## The Problem
Signing services need routing, scheduling, confirmations, status tracking and client reporting, the same shape of problem field-service businesses solved years ago.

## What Already Exists
Field-service management platforms (ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro and others) with dispatch boards, technician apps, customer notifications and job status.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: eligibility constraints that are legal rather than skill-based (commission state and expiry, certification, annual screening, E&O, remote-act authorisation); documents that arrive late and must reach the agent before the appointment; borrower confirmations that check signer count and ID; status feeds into title companies' systems and Snapdocs; and contractor pay terms, since agents are independent and accept or decline each offer.

## Target Customer
Regional signing services outgrowing spreadsheets.

## Impact If Solved
A proven dispatch model adapted rather than reinvented, cutting coordinator touches per order.
