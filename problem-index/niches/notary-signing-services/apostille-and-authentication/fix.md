# The Notary Whose Signature Is Not on File

**Niche:** [[niches/notary-signing-services/apostille-and-authentication/profile|Apostille & Document Authentication]]
**Industry:** [[industries/notary-signing-services|Notary & Signing Services]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A document notarised years ago, or by a notary commissioned in another county, cannot be authenticated because the clerk has no signature to compare.
**Tags:** #decision-trees #compliance #quick-win #automation #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a document through the correct chain of certifications — notarial act, county clerk where required, secretary of state, and for non-Convention countries federal authentication and embassy legalisation — on the first submission, and whoever has the lowest rejection rate against the destination's deadline takes the immigration, adoption and corporate filing work.

## The Problem
Certifying offices authenticate a notary's signature against the specimen they hold. New York county clerks warn that they cannot guarantee they still hold signatures for notaries commissioned years ago, and the clerk must be the one where the notary's signature is filed. A document sent to the wrong clerk, or notarised by a notary whose commission has lapsed, comes back rejected weeks later.

## Why It's Still Broken
Customers choose the nearest notary, not one whose signature the right office holds, and nobody checks commission status and filing county before the document is signed.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Before notarising a document bound for apostille, check the notary's commission status and filing county, warn if the commission expires soon, and re-notarise old documents with a current notary rather than submitting them as they are.

## Who Feels the Pain
Families with a visa or adoption deadline; facilitators who absorb repeat courier costs; notaries blamed for rejections.

## Impact If Fixed
A five-minute check removes one of the most common avoidable rejections.
