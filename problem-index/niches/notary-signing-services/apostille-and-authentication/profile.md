# Apostille & Document Authentication

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/notary-signing-services|Notary & Signing Services]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a document through the correct chain of certifications — notarial act, county clerk where required, secretary of state, and for non-Convention countries federal authentication and embassy legalisation — on the first submission, and whoever has the lowest rejection rate against the destination's deadline takes the immigration, adoption and corporate filing work.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$150M a year in apostille facilitation, courier and notarisation fees (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~6% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — paper originals, wet seals, mailed or walked-in submissions to state offices
**Target Buyer:** Individuals with foreign immigration, marriage, study or adoption files; corporate paralegals filing abroad; immigration and family law firms
**Automation Potential:** Medium — routing and checklists automate; certifications are physical and issued by government offices

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A document for use abroad needs more than a notarisation: the notary's authority has to be certified by the right official, and the chain depends on the document, the state and the destination country. New York, for example, requires the county clerk to authenticate a notary's signature before the Department of State will issue an apostille, rejects documents that skip that step, and charges $10 per document; county clerks can only verify signatures they hold on file, so an old notarisation can fail. Deadlines are set by foreign consulates, courts and universities.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Apostille facilitation businesses that collect, notarise, route and courier documents; state online status checkers; consulate websites. Gaps: no maintained map of chains by state, document type and destination; no pre-check of the notarial certificate against what the certifying office will accept; and tracking by phone.
