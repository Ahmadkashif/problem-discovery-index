# Signing Marketplace & Closing Platform Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/notary-signing-services|Notary & Signing Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The data and analytics teams at Snapdocs, the dominant loan-signing marketplace and closing platform, and at smaller order platforms. They see orders, fees, acceptance times, completions, and agent rankings across hundreds of signing services and title companies, and publish reports such as the State of eClose Adoption survey as marketing. Lenders and title companies pay for the closing workflow; agents use the marketplace free.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-30 data scientists and analysts across product, ranking and marketing research (estimate)
**Output:** Agent ranking and fee suggestions inside the product; customer dashboards; annual industry reports
**Proprietary data:** The largest cross-market record of signing orders, offered and accepted fees, and agent performance
**Clock:** None of its own; closing dates and funding cutoffs belong to its customers' orders
**Buyer:** Head of data or product at the platform

**Kill switches:** **Data isn't theirs to use (live).** The order records, closing documents and borrower details belong to the lender and title customers under platform contracts and carry consumers' nonpublic personal information, so the corpus cannot be pooled or resold as analysis without consent; scored consistently with [[niches/title-and-settlement-offices/production-software-vendors/profile|Title & Escrow Production Software Vendors]] under [[industries/title-and-settlement-offices|Title & Settlement Offices]].
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. It would also fall below threshold: the insight serves the product rather than being sold, and there are only a few such platforms.
