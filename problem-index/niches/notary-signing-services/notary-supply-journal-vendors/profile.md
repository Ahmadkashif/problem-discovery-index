# Notary Seal, Journal & E-Journal Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/notary-signing-services|Notary & Signing Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Makers and sellers of notary seals and stamps, bound journals, thumbprint pads and e-journal apps, selling to every newly commissioned and renewing notary. Some bundle bonds and E&O. In some states a seal maker must see proof of the commission before making a stamp (recalled, not checked).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Order processing and app development; analytical staff under 10 (estimate)
**Output:** Physical supplies and apps
**Proprietary data:** Customer lists keyed to commissions; app usage
**Clock:** Commission start dates
**Buyer:** Owner or product lead
