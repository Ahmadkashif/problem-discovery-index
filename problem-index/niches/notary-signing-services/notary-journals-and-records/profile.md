# Notary Journals & Records

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/notary-signing-services|Notary & Signing Services]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make the journal entry the evidence that settles a later challenge — who appeared, how they were identified, what they signed — complete under each state's rules and retrievable in minutes years later, and whoever does that without slowing the signing takes the notary's record-keeping.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$60M a year on paper journals, e-journal apps, thumbprint scanners and record storage (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — bound paper journals dominate; several states restrict or do not address electronic journals for in-person acts
**Target Buyer:** The individual notary; signing services and employers who supply journals to staff notaries
**Automation Potential:** High for field completeness and retrieval; capture itself still happens at the table

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The journal is the notary's own legal record and their best defence in a lawsuit. California's §8206 shows how prescriptive it gets: one active sequential journal, locked under the notary's exclusive control, recording the act, the instrument, the signer's signature, the identity evidence or credible witnesses, the fee, and a right thumbprint for real property documents and powers of attorney; the journal belongs to the notary and cannot be surrendered to an employer. Other states require a journal only for some acts or not at all (roughly half require none, estimate). Remote acts add session recordings with their own retention rules.

## Current Tools & Gaps
NNA and supply-vendor paper journals; e-journal apps such as Notary eJournal and NotaryAssist; journal modules inside RON platforms. Gaps: no state-rule enforcement at entry; paper entries unsearchable; no link from an entry to the recorded instrument; and nothing helps a notary answer a records request or subpoena quickly.
