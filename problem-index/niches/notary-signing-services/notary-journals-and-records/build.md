# A Journal Entry That Can Be Found and Trusted Years Later

**Niche:** [[niches/notary-signing-services/notary-journals-and-records/profile|Notary Journals & Records]]
**Industry:** [[industries/notary-signing-services|Notary & Signing Services]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A notary's paper journals are the only record that a deed signing happened as certified, and they sit in a closet, unsearchable and alterable.
**Tags:** #cnns #transformers #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #compliance #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make the journal entry the evidence that settles a later challenge — who appeared, how they were identified, what they signed — complete under each state's rules and retrievable in minutes years later, and whoever does that without slowing the signing takes the notary's record-keeping.

## The Problem
When a deed is challenged, the questions are whether the signer appeared, what ID was examined and whether the entry was made at the time. A paper journal answers slowly, if the notary still has it, and an entry can be added later without trace.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The journal is the notary's personal property under statutes such as California's, so employers and title companies cannot run it for them; individual notaries will not pay much; and states differ on whether electronic journals are allowed for in-person acts.

## What to Build
A tamper-evident record per act: hashed and time-stamped at creation, with the state-required fields, the credential images and thumbprint where required, the signer's and witnesses' signatures, and a reference to the instrument (document type, parties, property, and later the recording number). Handwritten legacy journals are digitised with handwriting recognition into searchable entries. The notary owns and controls the record; title companies and courts receive verified excerpts on request.

## Target Customer
Professional signing agents and mobile notaries; approved-notary panels that require verifiable journals as a condition of membership.

## Impact If Built
A challenged act can be confirmed or refuted in minutes with evidence that is hard to fake, protecting honest notaries and exposing forged seals.
