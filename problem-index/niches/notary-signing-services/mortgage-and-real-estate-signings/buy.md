# Appraisal Panel Software Repurposed for Notary Panels

**Industry:** [[industries/notary-signing-services|Notary & Signing Services]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Lenders already manage appraiser panels with licence, insurance and performance tracking; title companies manage notary panels in spreadsheets.
**Tags:** #decision-trees #evaluation-metrics #compliance #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor is fighting for title and escrow order flow, but borrower loan signings are won by returning a clean, fully executed package before the funding cutoff, while seller and deed signings are won by proving the signer is the owner of record before the deed records — two contests with different winners. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
A title company's approved-notary list needs, per agent, a current commission, bond and E&O policy, NNA or equivalent certification, an annual background screening and the states they can work in. Most lists are spreadsheets maintained by a closing coordinator, refreshed when someone remembers.

## What Already Exists
Appraisal management and vendor-management platforms used by lenders track appraiser licences against state registries, insurance certificates, geographic coverage, scorecards and rotation rules. Generic vendor-compliance tools track certificates of insurance.

## The Customization Gap
The notary version needs: commission lookups against fifty different state databases with different formats; screening validity tied to the Signing Professionals Workgroup standard; separate eligibility for remote acts by state; seller-signing eligibility for agents on an approved panel; E&O minimums by underwriter; and expiry alerts that reach the agent before the order is assigned rather than after.

## Target Customer
Title agency and underwriter agency-management teams; signing services that must prove their panel's compliance to title clients.

## Impact If Solved
Panels stay current without a coordinator's manual checks, and title companies can show their underwriter that every notary on a file was eligible on the signing date.
