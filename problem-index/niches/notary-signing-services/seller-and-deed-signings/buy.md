# Account-Opening Identity Checks at the Seller's Table

**Niche:** [[niches/notary-signing-services/seller-and-deed-signings/profile|Seller & Deed Signings]]
**Industry:** [[industries/notary-signing-services|Notary & Signing Services]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Banks check a document and a live face to open a small savings account; a notary checks a licence by eye before a six-figure deed records.
**Tags:** #cnns #object-detection #logistic-regression #evaluation-metrics #compliance #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to prove that the person signing the deed is the owner of record before it records and the proceeds are wired, and whoever stops impostors without delaying genuine sellers becomes the title company's approved notary.

## The Problem
In-person notarial acts rely on the notary's visual inspection of a credential. A good counterfeit licence passes, and nothing records what was checked beyond the ID number in the journal.

## What Already Exists
Document authentication with barcode and security-feature checks, face matching and liveness detection are mature in account opening, analysed in [[industries/identity-verification-vendors|Identity Verification Vendors]]. Some title companies already send sellers a verification link before closing.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to an in-person legal act: capture on the notary's phone at the table, not on the signer's; a result that informs but never replaces the notary's statutory decision; state rules on what identity evidence a notary may rely on, including credible witnesses; storage as part of the journal record under that state's retention rules; a hand-off of the evidence to the title office, which owns the owner-of-record match ([[problems/title-and-settlement-offices/low-impact-2|Seller Identity Verification Built for Title]]); and false-reject measurement by age and issuing state, because refusing a genuine elderly seller is its own harm.

## Target Customer
Title agencies and approved-notary panels; signing services offering seller signings.

## Impact If Solved
Counterfeit and borrowed IDs become much harder to pass at the one point where the impostor must appear in person.
