# Language-Matched Booking With Interpreter Rules Built In

**Niche:** [[niches/notary-signing-services/limited-english-signers/profile|Limited-English Signers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/notary-signing-services|Notary & Signing Services]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A Spanish-, Vietnamese- or Haitian Creole-speaking signer needs a notary they can talk to tonight, and no directory can say whether one exists within reach.
**Tags:** #k-nearest-neighbors #time-series-forecasting #decision-trees #evaluation-metrics #compliance #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to complete a valid act for a signer who does not read the document's language — with a language-matched notary or a qualified interpreter where the state allows one, an oath the signer understands, and no "notario" misrepresentation — and whoever can put a fluent commissioned notary at the appointment takes the community's referrals.

## The Problem
Language ability is self-declared in directories and rarely verified. When no fluent notary is free, whether a relative or professional interpreter may help depends on state rules the requester does not know, and some notaries refuse rather than risk it.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Demand is spread thinly across many languages and places, supply is part-time and invisible, and directories make money from listings rather than completed matches.

## What to Build
Matching on verified language ability (a short assessment, not a checkbox), commission state, act type, location and availability, with honest wait-time estimates and fill-rate reporting by language and area. Where no fluent notary is available, the system applies the state's interpreter rules — who may interpret, whether the interpreter must take an oath or sign, what the journal records — and books a compliant interpreter.

## Target Customer
Notary networks and signing services serving immigrant communities; title companies with non-English-speaking borrowers; legal aid organisations.

## Impact If Built
Signers get a valid act in their own language, and the fill-rate data shows where supply is missing.
