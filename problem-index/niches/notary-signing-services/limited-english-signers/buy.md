# Machine Translation Adapted to Notarial Wording

**Niche:** [[niches/notary-signing-services/limited-english-signers/profile|Limited-English Signers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/notary-signing-services|Notary & Signing Services]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** General translation tools are good enough to mislead: a notary reading an oath through a phone app cannot tell whether the signer heard "swear" or "promise".
**Tags:** #transformers #large-language-models #seq2seq #evaluation-metrics #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to complete a valid act for a signer who does not read the document's language — with a language-matched notary or a qualified interpreter where the state allows one, an oath the signer understands, and no "notario" misrepresentation — and whoever can put a fluent commissioned notary at the appointment takes the community's referrals.

## The Problem
Notaries without the signer's language sometimes fall back on phone translation for the oath, the acknowledgment questions and the identity check. Legal meaning is easily lost, and translating the document itself could stray into explaining it.

## What Already Exists
Google Translate, DeepL and LLM-based translation; professional interpreter platforms by phone or video.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: pre-approved, human-reviewed translations of the fixed notarial wording (oaths, affirmations, acknowledgment questions, the notary's role and limits) in the main community languages; refusal to translate the instrument itself; a log of what was presented in which language for the journal; and state-rule checks on whether this form of communication is enough for the act or a qualified interpreter is required.

## Target Customer
Notaries and signing services in multilingual areas; RON platforms adding non-English sessions.

## Impact If Solved
The fixed parts of the act are communicated accurately, and the notary knows when a human interpreter is required.
