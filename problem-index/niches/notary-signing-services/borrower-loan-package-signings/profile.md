# Borrower Loan Package Signings

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/notary-signing-services|Notary & Signing Services]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to return a fully executed, error-free loan package before the lender's funding cutoff, and whoever has the lowest redraw rate per hundred signings takes the title company's order flow.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.1B a year in borrower-side signing fees for purchase, refinance, HELOC and reverse-mortgage closings (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~44% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — hybrid closings e-sign some pages, but the notarised instruments are mostly wet ink
**Target Buyer:** Title and escrow closing managers; signing-service owners
**Automation Potential:** High for package checks and logistics; low for the appointment itself

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The borrower is who they say they are almost every time — the lender has already verified them through underwriting — so the contest is execution. A ~100-150 page package (estimate) must come back with every signature, initial, date and notarial certificate correct, scanned back within hours and couriered by the cutoff, against a closing date fixed by the purchase contract or the rate lock and, on a refinance, a three-day rescission period that starts at signing. A redraw can cost the lock and push the closing.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Snapdocs and signing-service portals for orders and document delivery; closing platforms for hybrid closings; phone scanner apps for scanbacks; NNA checklists. Nobody checks the package against the lender's own requirements before the agent leaves the table, nobody re-dates a package when the appointment moves, and nobody predicts which orders are likely to fail.
