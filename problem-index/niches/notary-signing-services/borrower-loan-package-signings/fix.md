# Printed Dates That No Longer Match the Signing Day

**Niche:** [[niches/notary-signing-services/borrower-loan-package-signings/profile|Borrower Loan Package Signings]]
**Industry:** [[industries/notary-signing-services|Notary & Signing Services]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A refinance signing slips a day, and the rescission notice, the deed of trust and the notarial certificates all carry the old date, so the agent hand-corrects them at the table or waits for a redraw, with no clear rule on which the lender wants.
**Tags:** #decision-trees #large-language-models #compliance #quick-win #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to return a fully executed, error-free loan package before the lender's funding cutoff, and whoever has the lowest redraw rate per hundred signings takes the title company's order flow.

## The Problem
Packages are drawn with a planned signing date. On a refinance, the Notice of Right to Cancel prints the signing date and the last day to rescind; the rescission period runs three business days, counting Saturdays but not Sundays or federal holidays (Regulation Z's rescission definition, recalled). When the borrower moves the appointment, every date-bearing page is wrong. Some lenders let the agent strike and initial the dates; others insist on a redraw. Most say nothing until the agent calls from the table at 7 p.m. If the dates are corrected wrongly, the rescission period can be miscounted, the loan funds early, and the lender has to reopen rescission.

## Why It's Still Broken
Rescheduling happens between the borrower and the signing service, and nobody tells the lender's doc-drawing desk until the signing is under way. Lender policies on hand corrections live in emails and closing instructions that agents rarely see. The holiday-aware date arithmetic is done in the agent's head.

## What a Fix Looks Like
When an appointment changes, recompute the signing and rescission dates with federal holidays and the Sunday rule, list every page and certificate that carries a date, and read the lender's closing instructions to state its correction policy. Then ask for a redraw or written permission to hand-correct before the agent leaves home, not at the table. Log the corrected dates with the order so the funder can check them.

## Who Feels the Pain
Signing agents stuck at the table waiting for an answer; borrowers whose closing slips; funders who catch a miscounted rescission period after the fact.

## Impact If Fixed
Date errors from rescheduled signings stop turning into redraws and reopened rescission periods, which are among the costliest ways a clean-looking package goes wrong.
