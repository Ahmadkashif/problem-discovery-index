# Account-Opening Fraud Signals Adapted to RON Sessions

**Niche:** [[niches/notary-signing-services/remote-online-notarization-sessions/profile|Remote Online Notarization Sessions]]
**Industry:** [[industries/notary-signing-services|Notary & Signing Services]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Banks score device, location and behaviour on every login; many RON sessions still rest on five credit-file questions anyone with breached data can answer.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #logistic-regression #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #compliance #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to clear genuine signers through remote identity proofing on the first attempt while refusing impostors, with a session record that survives a later forgery claim, and whoever has the best trade-off between false rejects and false accepts takes the platforms' and title companies' session volume.

## The Problem
Remote impostors use stolen identity data to pass KBA and borrowed or forged IDs to pass credential analysis. The session's surrounding signals — device, network, location, timing, repeated attempts across platforms — say much more than the five questions.

## What Already Exists
Device fingerprinting, IP and carrier intelligence, behavioural biometrics and consortium fraud scores used in bank account opening and payments, supplied by the vendors in [[industries/identity-verification-vendors|Identity Verification Vendors]]. Proof has described risk scoring on location, device, carrier and prior rejections.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: scores that inform the notary rather than replace the statutory identity methods a state prescribes; transaction context from the title file (vacant land, absentee owner) joined to session signals; retention of the evidence as part of the act's record; per-state limits on how signer data may be used beyond the act; and fairness testing, since a false reject denies a legal act.

## Target Customer
RON platforms and title companies running seller RON sessions.

## Impact If Solved
Impostors using breached data are caught on signals they cannot easily fake, without adding friction for genuine signers.
