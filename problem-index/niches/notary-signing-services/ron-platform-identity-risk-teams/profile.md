# RON Platform Identity-Risk Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/notary-signing-services|Notary & Signing Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The trust, fraud and compliance teams inside remote online notarization platforms such as Proof (formerly Notarize), NotaryCam and BlueNotary. They tune identity-proofing thresholds, review flagged sessions, investigate fraud reports from title companies and lenders, and track state rule changes. Their work protects the platform's session revenue and its customers; it is not sold as a product in its own right, although Proof has moved toward selling identity products.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-50 fraud analysts, risk operations and compliance staff at the largest platform, far fewer at the rest (estimate)
**Output:** Internal risk scores, session reviews, fraud investigations, rule-change implementations
**Proprietary data:** Session recordings, credential images, KBA results, device and network signals, and notary decisions across millions of remote acts (Proof is reported at more than 5 million)
**Clock:** Customers' closing dates; state retention and rule effective dates
**Buyer:** Chief trust or compliance officer at the platform

**Kill switches:** **Data isn't theirs to use.** Session recordings and identity evidence are retained as the notary's statutory record of each act, state RON laws restrict how signers' personal information may be used beyond the act, and face and ID images bring biometric privacy exposure; the richest corpus in the industry cannot freely feed new products.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch (and below threshold: the insight protects session revenue rather than being invoiced, and the buyer market is a handful of platforms).
