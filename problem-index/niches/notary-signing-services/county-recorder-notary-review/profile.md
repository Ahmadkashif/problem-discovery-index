# County Recorder Notarial Review & Fraud Alerts

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/notary-signing-services|Notary & Signing Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
County recorders and clerks who accept or reject instruments for recording, including for defective notarial certificates, and in many counties run free property fraud alert services that notify owners when a document is recorded against their name. In some states clerks also authenticate notary signatures for apostilles.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Recording examiners per county; any fraud or quality analysis is a handful of staff at most (estimate)
**Output:** Recordings, rejections, owner alerts
**Proprietary data:** Recorded instruments and rejection reasons, held county by county
**Clock:** Recording queues and statutory recording requirements
**Buyer:** County recorder or clerk
