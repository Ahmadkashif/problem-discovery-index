# State Notary Commissioning & Enforcement Units

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/notary-signing-services|Notary & Signing Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Secretary of State (or equivalent) offices that commission notaries, set exam and training rules, approve RON platforms, and investigate complaints, including bans such as California's on translating "notary public" as "notario". Their output is commissions, rules and disciplinary actions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Commission processing and complaint intake staff; analytical or investigative staff typically under 10 per state (estimate)
**Output:** Commissions, rules, handbooks, disciplinary decisions
**Proprietary data:** Commission records, complaints and discipline files
**Clock:** Statutory effective dates and commission terms
**Buyer:** Secretary of State's notary division director
