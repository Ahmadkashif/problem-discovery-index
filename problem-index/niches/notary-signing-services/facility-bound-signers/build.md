# Identity Without an ID in Hand

**Niche:** [[niches/notary-signing-services/facility-bound-signers/profile|Hospital, Care-Facility & Jail Signers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/notary-signing-services|Notary & Signing Services]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** The signer who most urgently needs a power of attorney is often the one with an expired licence in a drawer at home, and most notaries simply leave.
**Tags:** #decision-trees #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #compliance #worker-facing #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to complete a valid act for a signer who cannot leave a hospital, care home or jail and often has no ID in hand — securing access within the facility's windows and identity evidence the state accepts — and whoever completes on the first visit takes the facility's and families' referrals.

## The Problem
States allow some alternatives to a current photo ID — credible identifying witnesses, recently expired IDs in some states, other documents in others — but the rules differ by state and the notary must get them exactly right, since an act on improper identification is defective and a liability. At a bedside or a jail window, notaries default to refusing.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The volume per notary is low, the rules are buried in statutes and handbooks, and identity products are built for people with smartphones and current IDs.

## What to Build
A pre-visit identity plan: given the state, the signer's situation and the document, list the acceptable evidence (which IDs, whether expired IDs count and for how long, how many credible witnesses and who may serve), what each witness must bring, and what the journal entry must record. On site, the notary follows the plan, captures the evidence, and the record shows why the identification was lawful. Families get a plain-language checklist before booking.

## Target Customer
Mobile notaries who specialise in facility work; hospital social work departments; elder law and criminal defence practices.

## Impact If Built
More first-visit completions for signers with no other way to act, with a record that protects the notary.
