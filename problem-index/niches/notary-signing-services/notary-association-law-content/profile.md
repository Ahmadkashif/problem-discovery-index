# Notary Association Law & Guidance Content

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/notary-signing-services|Notary & Signing Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The National Notary Association (founded 1957, Chatsworth, California; a mutual benefit corporation operating as NNA Services, LLC) tracks notary law in every state, publishes state handbooks and the Notary Bulletin, runs a telephone hotline, maintains the Model Notary Act that more than 40 states drew on, runs the five-yearly Notary Census and sells the Notary Signing Agent certification and screening. The American Society of Notaries and American Association of Notaries are much smaller. Members and certification buyers pay; the law content is bundled into training, supplies and insurance.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-30 legal research, editorial and hotline staff at the NNA (estimate; the whole association was reported at about 200 employees around 2017)
**Output:** State law primers, handbooks, hotline answers, certification course content, the Notary Census
**Proprietary data:** Decades of hotline questions, member surveys, certification and screening records, and a state-by-state law tracker
**Clock:** Legislative effective dates and commission renewal cycles
**Buyer:** NNA executive leadership
