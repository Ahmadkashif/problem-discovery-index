# The Witness Nobody Can Find Ten Years Later

**Industry:** [[industries/notary-signing-services|Notary & Signing Services]]

## The Problem
Witnesses are often neighbours, care-home staff or the notary's acquaintances. Their names are illegible on the will, their contact details are not recorded anywhere, and by the time a challenge arrives they have moved, retired or died. The notary's journal, if the state requires one, has the signer's ID but not the witnesses'.

## Why It's Still Broken
Nobody is responsible for witness records once the ceremony ends; the law firm keeps the original document, the notary keeps the journal, and the witnesses keep nothing.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Capture each witness's printed name, ID details and contact information at the ceremony in the same record as the notary's journal entry, with the witness's consent, and give the law firm a retrievable witness sheet filed with the will. Remind the firm to refresh contact details when it reviews the plan.

## Who Feels the Pain
Estate litigators and personal representatives defending a will; families caught in a contest; notaries subpoenaed alone.

## Impact If Fixed
A one-minute step at the signing makes a will far easier to defend when it matters.
