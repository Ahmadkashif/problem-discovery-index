# Booking Software That Understands Witnesses

**Niche:** [[niches/notary-signing-services/estate-and-legal-document-signings/profile|Estate & Legal Document Signings]]
**Industry:** [[industries/notary-signing-services|Notary & Signing Services]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Appointment tools book one provider into one slot; a will signing needs a notary plus two disinterested witnesses at a bedside by Friday.
**Tags:** #decision-trees #evaluation-metrics #workflow-orchestration #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to execute a will, trust or power of attorney with the witnesses the state requires and a contemporaneous record of the signer's identity, awareness and free will, and whoever's record holds up in a later probate or elder-abuse challenge takes the estate attorney's repeat work.

## The Problem
An estate signing fails if a witness is a beneficiary, if the wrong number turn up, or if the notary cannot attend when the witnesses can. Paralegals coordinate by phone, and a family's urgent request for a dying relative is often a scramble.

## What Already Exists
Calendly, Acuity and field-service schedulers book a provider into a slot; mobile notary directories list availability; law practice management systems hold the client file.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: per-state and per-instrument witness rules (count, eligibility, whether the notary may also witness, remote-witnessing permission); conflict checks that exclude beneficiaries and family; bundled booking of notary and witnesses as one unit; facility access requirements; and a document checklist that confirms self-proving affidavits and certificates are prepared for the state of execution.

## Target Customer
Estate planning paralegals and mobile notary businesses offering witness services.

## Impact If Solved
Fewer failed ceremonies and faster signings for clients who may not have long, with a valid witness set on the first visit.
