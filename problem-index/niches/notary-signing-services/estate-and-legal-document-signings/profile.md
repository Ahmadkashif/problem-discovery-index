# Estate & Legal Document Signings

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/notary-signing-services|Notary & Signing Services]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to execute a will, trust or power of attorney with the witnesses the state requires and a contemporaneous record of the signer's identity, awareness and free will, and whoever's record holds up in a later probate or elder-abuse challenge takes the estate attorney's repeat work.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$250M a year in mobile notary and witness fees for wills, trusts, powers of attorney, healthcare directives, affidavits and other legal documents (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — witnessed wet-ink ceremonies; remote witnessing is restricted for wills in many states
**Target Buyer:** Estate planning attorneys and their paralegals; families arranging signings for an elderly relative
**Automation Potential:** Low-Medium — scheduling and certificates automate; the observation of the signer does not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Estate documents are signed once and tested years later, after the signer has died or lost capacity, by relatives who disagree. The notary and witnesses are often the only neutral people who saw the signing. NNA guidance notes that capacity claims frequently arise in probate and elder-abuse matters, and the notary's journal is their main protection. Many firms in [[industries/estate-planning|Estate Planning Law Firms]] supervise signings themselves; the work here is the mobile notary and witness service they and families buy when the client cannot come to the office.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Law-firm calendars and practice management systems, mobile notary directories, NNA journals, and witness pools improvised from office staff or neighbours. Nothing captures a structured record of how the signer presented, who was present, and how the notary tested awareness and willingness, and nothing keeps witnesses findable a decade later.
