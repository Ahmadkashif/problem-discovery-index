# Specialty & Natural Wholesaler Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/specialty-food-retail|Specialty Food Retail]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator/rollup

## What They Do
The category, pricing and supply chain analytics functions inside the natural and specialty wholesalers that supply independent retailers — the layer that decides what an independent store can even stock.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 category managers, pricing analysts, demand planners, and data scientists
**Output:** Assortment and category decisions, demand forecasting and allocation, promotional planning, retailer performance analytics, new item screening
**Proprietary data:** Order-level purchasing across thousands of independent retailers joined to product performance and store survival
**Clock:** Weekly ordering cycles and category review calendars
**Buyer:** VP of Analytics / Chief Merchandising Officer
