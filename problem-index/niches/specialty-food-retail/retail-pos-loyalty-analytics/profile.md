# Independent Retail POS & Loyalty Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/specialty-food-retail|Specialty Food Retail]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The analytics functions inside the point-of-sale and loyalty platforms independent specialty retailers run on, producing basket analysis, assortment guidance and benchmark reporting.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 analysts, data scientists, and product staff
**Output:** Basket and margin analysis, assortment and pricing guidance, loyalty programme analytics, benchmark reports
**Proprietary data:** Item-level transactions across thousands of independent specialty stores — the channel conventional measurement covers worst
**Clock:** Continuous transaction flow
**Buyer:** VP of Product
