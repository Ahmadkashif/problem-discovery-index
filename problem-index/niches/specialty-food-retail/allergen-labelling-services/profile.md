# Ingredient & Allergen Labelling Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/specialty-food-retail|Specialty Food Retail]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Produce nutrition panels, allergen declarations and ingredient statements for small specialty producers, mapping recipes to a curated ingredient database.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 dietitians, food scientists, and data specialists
**Output:** Nutrition analysis, allergen and ingredient declarations, labelling compliance review, reformulation support
**Proprietary data:** Curated ingredient and formulation databases mapped to supplier products
**Clock:** Product launch and labelling compliance dates
**Buyer:** Director of Nutrition Services
