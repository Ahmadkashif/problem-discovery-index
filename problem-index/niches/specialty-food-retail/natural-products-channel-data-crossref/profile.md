# Natural Products Channel Data Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/specialty-food-retail|Specialty Food Retail]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Measure what sells through the natural, specialty and independent grocery channel — the part of retail conventional measurement covers worst — and sell it to the brands and retailers whose category decisions depend on it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 data operations staff, analysts, and category researchers
**Output:** Channel sales and share reporting, new item tracking, price and promotion analysis, category trend research
**Proprietary data:** Point-of-sale feeds negotiated store by store across a fragmented independent retail base that no conventional measurement panel reaches
**Clock:** Four-week and quarterly reporting against brand planning cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Chief Research Officer
