# Specialty Food Association Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/specialty-food-retail|Specialty Food Retail]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Publish the industry's market sizing, consumer trend and category forecast research, largely built around the trade shows where specialty buyers and producers meet.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 researchers and analysts
**Output:** Market sizing and category reports, consumer trend research, trend forecasting from show submissions, member benchmarking
**Proprietary data:** Product submissions and buyer behaviour at the industry's dominant trade events — an early view of what is entering the channel
**Clock:** Annual show and publication calendar
**Buyer:** VP of Research
