# Retail Measurement Data Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/specialty-food-retail|Specialty Food Retail]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Measure what sells, where, at what price through scanner feeds and household panels, and sell the share and category analytics the packaged food industry plans against — with specialty and independent retail the thinnest part of the coverage.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1,000-5,000 analysts, data scientists, and operations staff
**Output:** Category and share reporting, price and promotion analysis, distribution tracking, panel-based buyer analytics
**Proprietary data:** Retailer point-of-sale feeds and household panels assembled over decades under contracts no new entrant can replicate
**Clock:** Weekly and monthly releases against brand planning cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / SVP of Measurement Science
