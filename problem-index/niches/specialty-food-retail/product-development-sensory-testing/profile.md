# Product Development & Sensory Testing Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/specialty-food-retail|Specialty Food Retail]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Develop and test specialty food products before launch — formulation, shelf life, and trained-panel and consumer sensory evaluation — for producers who cannot run a laboratory.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 food scientists, sensory panellists, and statisticians across the specialist labs
**Output:** Formulation development, shelf life studies, descriptive sensory profiles, consumer acceptance testing, benchmarking against competitors
**Proprietary data:** Sensory panel and consumer test results accumulated across many products and categories
**Clock:** Product launch schedules
**Buyer:** Director of Sensory Science
