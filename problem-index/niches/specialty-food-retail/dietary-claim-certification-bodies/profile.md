# Dietary & Religious Claim Certification Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/specialty-food-retail|Specialty Food Retail]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Certify gluten-free, non-GMO, kosher and halal claims — inspecting facilities, reviewing formulations and supply chains, and licensing the marks that determine whether a product can be sold to an entire customer segment.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 inspectors, formulation reviewers, rabbinic and religious authorities, and standards staff
**Output:** Certification decisions, facility and ingredient reviews, standards, supply chain approvals, mark licensing
**Proprietary data:** Ingredient and supplier approval records built over decades — effectively a private map of what is in the food supply and where it came from
**Clock:** Annual renewal and inspection cycles, plus production scheduling that depends on certification status
**Buyer:** Director of Certification
