# Specialty Food Broker & Category Advisory

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/specialty-food-retail|Specialty Food Retail]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Represent specialty brands to retail buyers, build the category story that wins shelf space, and advise emerging producers on distribution strategy.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-30 category analysts per firm alongside a larger sales organisation
**Output:** Category reviews and shelf recommendations, retailer presentations, distribution strategy, promotional planning
**Proprietary data:** Retailer acceptance and velocity outcomes across represented brands, rarely retained systematically
**Clock:** Retailer category review windows
**Buyer:** VP of Category Management
