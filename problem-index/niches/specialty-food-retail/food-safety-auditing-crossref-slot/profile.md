# Food Safety Auditing & Certification Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/specialty-food-retail|Specialty Food Retail]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Audit producers and processors against recognised food safety schemes and issue the certifications retailers require before listing a product — a barrier that falls hardest on the small specialty producers this channel exists to sell.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 auditors, technical reviewers, and scheme managers
**Output:** Audit findings and non-conformance reports, certification decisions, scheme standards, auditor calibration
**Proprietary data:** Audit findings across tens of thousands of facilities
**Clock:** Annual certification cycles and unannounced audit windows
**Buyer:** VP of Certification
