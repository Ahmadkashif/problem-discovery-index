# Food Certification Standards Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/specialty-food-retail|Specialty Food Retail]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Write and enforce the certification standards specialty food shelves are organised around — organic, non-GMO, fair trade, regenerative — and license the marks that let a product make the claim a specialty retailer's customers pay a premium for.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 standards staff, auditors, certification reviewers, and researchers
**Output:** Certification standards and revisions, auditor protocols, certification decisions, non-conformance findings, impact research
**Proprietary data:** Audit findings and supply chain records across certified operations worldwide
**Clock:** Annual audit and certification renewal cycles, standard revision periods
**Buyer:** Chief Standards Officer / Director of Certification
