# Grocery Shrink & Loss Prevention Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/specialty-food-retail|Specialty Food Retail]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Analyse inventory shrink — theft, spoilage, markdown and process error — and design the controls that reduce it, in a format where perishable specialty product spoils fast and margins are thin.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 analysts, loss prevention specialists, and data scientists
**Output:** Shrink attribution and reporting, markdown optimisation, spoilage forecasting, exception-based reporting, control design
**Proprietary data:** Inventory, sale, markdown and disposal records across retail estates
**Clock:** Inventory count cycles and perishable shelf life
**Buyer:** VP of Loss Prevention Analytics
