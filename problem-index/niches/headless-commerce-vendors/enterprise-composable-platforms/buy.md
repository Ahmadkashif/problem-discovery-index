**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to be the commerce core a large retailer can build twelve business units and fifteen markets on for a decade — and whoever does that takes the licence, because the buyer is choosing what they will be unable to leave.

## The Problem
Large enterprise system replacements have a long and well-documented history of failure, and the profession responded with practice: conference room pilots that run real scenarios on the candidate system before selection, structured fit-gap analysis, phased rather than big-bang cutover, and explicit management of customisation as debt. Commerce replatforms are enterprise system replacements with the same failure modes, run by teams who frequently have not encountered that literature.

## What Already Exists
Conference room pilot methodology; fit-gap analysis with documented resolution paths; phased and parallel-run cutover strategies; customisation governance treating modifications as debt; benefits realisation tracking; and the substantial post-mortem literature on why these programmes fail.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a composed architecture and a customer-facing system. It requires: (1) the pilot covering the composition rather than the platform, since the retailer is selecting a stack and testing one component in isolation misses the integration failures that dominate — this is the specific adaptation and it makes the pilot larger and far more informative; (2) cutover strategies that work for a live storefront, where a phased approach means routing traffic rather than migrating departments and the customer must never see the seam; (3) customisation debt tracked across vendors, since the extensions live in services around several platforms rather than inside one; (4) fit-gap applied to the data model rather than to processes, because the gaps that matter here are representational; and (5) benefits tracked as customer-visible outcomes, since the programme is justified on conversion and speed and is measured on delivery milestones.

## Target Customer
Architecture committees, programme leadership, integrators, and the enterprise programme management profession whose hard-won practice is being rediscovered.

## Impact If Solved
Enterprise replacement failure is a documented literature and commerce replatforms are repeating it. Running the pilot across the composition rather than the platform is the adaptation, and it surfaces the integration failures that actually dominate these programmes.
