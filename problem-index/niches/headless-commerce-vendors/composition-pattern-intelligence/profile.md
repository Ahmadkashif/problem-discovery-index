# Composition Pattern Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/headless-commerce-vendors|Headless Commerce Vendors]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn hundreds of implementations into an empirical account of which compositions work — and whoever does that advises better than anybody, because every architect in the category is currently designing from anecdote.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** None — hundreds of implementations, no learning
**Target Buyer:** The vendors and integrators themselves
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the telemetry and the outcomes both exist

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A platform vendor sees hundreds of implementations of their product and every service composed around it, with the resulting performance, incident rate, extension footprint, upgrade currency and eventual customer outcome. An integrator sees dozens with the same detail plus how the programme ran. That is an empirical account of which compositions work, which service combinations produce incidents, which extension patterns prevent upgrades, and which programme shapes deliver — the questions every architect in the category answers from anecdote. It is the natural asset of a party that sits across many implementations, and both vendors and integrators use it to produce a reference architecture written from opinion.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Reference architectures, case studies and the accumulated experience of individuals. The gaps: implementations are not characterised in a comparable form; outcomes are not joined to compositions; the reference architecture is not derived from what works; nothing from production reaches the architects designing the next one; and the knowledge lives in people who move between firms.
