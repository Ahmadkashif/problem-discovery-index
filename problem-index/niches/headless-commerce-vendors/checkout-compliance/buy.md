**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver a checkout that is correct in every jurisdiction the retailer sells into, from components that each solve one part — and whoever does that takes the risk off the retailer, because the assembly is where all of it lands.

## The Problem
Operating under many changing rules is a managed discipline in financial services, healthcare and telecoms: a register mapping every obligation to a control, horizon scanning that catches rule changes and routes them to the affected controls, continuous testing of whether the controls operate, and evidence retained for an examiner. A commerce checkout selling into a dozen jurisdictions carries a comparable obligation set and is managed with a legal review before launch and nothing after.

## What Already Exists
Obligation registers mapping rules to controls; regulatory horizon scanning services; continuous control monitoring with automated evidence; conformance test suites for published standards; accessibility conformance testing tooling; and audit evidence retention practice.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to obligations implemented in a user interface across several vendors. It requires: (1) controls that are checks against a running web experience rather than against a process or a configuration, since the obligation is satisfied by what the customer sees and can do — expressing an obligation as an automated browser assertion is the specific translation and it is what makes the register operational; (2) the obligation mapped to the responsible component as well as to the control, since the remedy may sit with a vendor; (3) horizon scanning across five specialisms, which no single service covers and which is the practical assembly problem; (4) testing on every release, since the checkout changes weekly and a regulated firm's controls change rarely; and (5) evidence generated automatically, since a retailer will not staff a compliance function for a checkout and the register is worthless if maintaining it is manual.

## Target Customer
Retail legal and engineering functions, integrators, and the regulatory technology community for whom consumer checkout is an under-served obligation surface.

## Impact If Solved
Regulated industries manage many changing rules with registers, scanning and continuous testing, and a checkout under a dozen regimes has a launch review. Expressing each obligation as an automated assertion against the running experience is the translation that makes the register operational.
