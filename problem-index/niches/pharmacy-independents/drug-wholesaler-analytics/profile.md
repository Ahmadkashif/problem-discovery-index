# Pharmaceutical Wholesaler Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pharmacy-independents|Independent Pharmacies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The analytics organisations inside the three national drug wholesalers, running demand forecasting, allocation, contract pricing administration, and controlled substance monitoring across the distribution system every independent pharmacy buys through.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-1,500 analysts, data scientists, pricing and contract administration staff
**Output:** Demand forecasts and allocation decisions, chargeback and contract pricing administration, controlled substance order monitoring, generic sourcing analytics
**Proprietary data:** Distribution-level product flow across essentially the entire US pharmacy channel
**Clock:** Daily order cycles and contract pricing updates
**Buyer:** VP of Analytics
