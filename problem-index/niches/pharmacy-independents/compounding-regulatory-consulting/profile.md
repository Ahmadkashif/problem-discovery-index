# Compounding Pharmacy Regulatory Consulting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pharmacy-independents|Independent Pharmacies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Advise compounding pharmacies and outsourcing facilities on sterile and non-sterile compounding standards, facility design, quality systems, and FDA and state board inspection readiness.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 consultants, pharmacists, and quality specialists
**Output:** Gap assessments, quality system design, validation protocols, inspection readiness and response
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated inspection observations and what investigators cited
**Clock:** USP chapter revision dates, inspection cycles, and accreditation renewals
**Buyer:** Managing Principal
