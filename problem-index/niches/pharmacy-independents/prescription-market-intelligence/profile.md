# Prescription Market Intelligence Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pharmacy-independents|Independent Pharmacies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Assemble prescription and dispensing data from pharmacies, switches, and claims processors into a de-identified national picture of what is prescribed, by whom, to what patient populations, and sell the resulting analytics to manufacturers, payers, and investors. Independent pharmacies are a data source in this system and rarely a customer of it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1,000-5,000 data scientists, statisticians, projection methodologists, therapeutic area analysts, and data operations staff
**Output:** Volume and share analytics by product, prescriber and geography, patient-level longitudinal cohorts, treatment pathway analysis, market forecasts, real-world evidence studies
**Proprietary data:** National prescription capture assembled over decades under supplier contracts, plus the projection methodology that turns partial sample coverage into a full national estimate
**Clock:** Weekly and monthly data releases against manufacturer commercial planning cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / SVP of Data Science
