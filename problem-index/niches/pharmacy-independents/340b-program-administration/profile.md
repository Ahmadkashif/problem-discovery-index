# 340B Program Administration & Compliance

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pharmacy-independents|Independent Pharmacies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Determine which dispensed prescriptions qualify for 340B discount pricing, split accumulations between covered entities and contract pharmacies, and defend the determinations through HRSA audits and manufacturer challenges.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 program analysts, compliance specialists, auditors, and data staff
**Output:** Eligibility determinations, accumulation and replenishment calculations, duplicate discount prevention, audit defence, savings reporting
**Proprietary data:** Eligibility determinations joined to audit outcomes across many covered entities
**Clock:** HRSA audit cycles, manufacturer recertification, and continuous replenishment
**Buyer:** VP of 340B Services / Chief Compliance Officer
