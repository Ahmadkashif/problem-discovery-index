# Pharmacy Management System Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pharmacy-independents|Independent Pharmacies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The analytics functions inside PioneerRx, QS/1 and their peers — the systems Pass 1 describes as near-universally adopted by independents — producing workflow reporting, adherence dashboards and benchmark data from dispensing activity.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 analysts, clinical informaticists, and product staff
**Output:** Dispensing and workflow analytics, adherence dashboards, benchmark reporting, clinical service documentation
**Proprietary data:** Dispensing records across a large share of independent pharmacies
**Clock:** Continuous dispensing flow
**Buyer:** VP of Product / Chief Clinical Officer
