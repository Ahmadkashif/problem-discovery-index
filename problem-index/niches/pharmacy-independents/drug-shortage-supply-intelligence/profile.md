# Drug Shortage & Supply Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pharmacy-independents|Independent Pharmacies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Track drug shortages, manufacturer discontinuations, recalls and supply disruptions, and publish substitution and conservation guidance to the pharmacies and health systems that have to dispense around them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 pharmacists, supply analysts, and content staff
**Output:** Shortage bulletins and status tracking, substitution guidance, conservation strategies, resolution forecasts
**Proprietary data:** Manufacturer-reported and practitioner-reported shortage intelligence assembled over years, ahead of and beyond what regulators publish
**Clock:** Continuous — a shortage is urgent the day it appears
**Buyer:** Director of Drug Information
