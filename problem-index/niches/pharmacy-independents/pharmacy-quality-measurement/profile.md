# Pharmacy Quality Measurement Organizations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pharmacy-independents|Independent Pharmacies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Develop and steward the medication adherence and quality measures that feed Medicare Star Ratings and pharmacy performance programmes, and operate the platforms that score individual pharmacies against them. Pass 1 describes the downstream effect: adherence is a quality metric PBMs use to calculate DIR fees, creating a cycle the pharmacy cannot escape.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 measure developers, pharmacists, psychometricians, and analysts
**Output:** Measure specifications and stewardship, pharmacy performance scoring, benchmarking, measure validity research
**Proprietary data:** Measure performance distributions across the pharmacy population
**Clock:** Annual Star Ratings cycle and measure maintenance calendar
**Buyer:** Chief Science Officer / VP of Measure Development

**Kill switches:** **HIPAA** — measure computation runs on claims. **Federal programme dependency** — the measures matter because CMS uses them.
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold and carries live kill switches. Adherence measures built from refill gaps are known to be a poor proxy for whether medicine was taken, and the organisation stewarding them has no mechanism to find out.
