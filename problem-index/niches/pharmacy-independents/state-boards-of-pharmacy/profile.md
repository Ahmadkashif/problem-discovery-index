# State Boards of Pharmacy

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pharmacy-independents|Independent Pharmacies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
License pharmacists and pharmacies, inspect facilities, investigate complaints and diversion, and operate the prescription drug monitoring programmes that track controlled substance dispensing.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 per state — inspectors, investigators, licensing and PDMP analysts
**Output:** Licensure, inspection findings, disciplinary actions, PDMP reporting and alerts
**Proprietary data:** Controlled substance dispensing records statewide, plus inspection and discipline histories
**Clock:** Licence renewal cycles and inspection schedules
**Buyer:** Executive Director
