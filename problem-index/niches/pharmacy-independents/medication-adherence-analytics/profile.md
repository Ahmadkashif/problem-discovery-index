# Medication Adherence Analytics Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pharmacy-independents|Independent Pharmacies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Identify patients likely to lapse on refills and prompt intervention, sold to plans, PBMs and pharmacy chains as a lever on the quality measures that determine reimbursement.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 data scientists, clinical pharmacists, and outcomes analysts
**Output:** Risk stratification for non-adherence, intervention targeting, outreach programmes, programme impact reporting
**Proprietary data:** Refill histories joined to outreach and subsequent refill behaviour
**Clock:** Refill due dates and the annual Star Ratings measurement year
**Buyer:** Chief Analytics Officer
