# Pharmacy Services Administrative Organizations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pharmacy-independents|Independent Pharmacies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator/rollup

## What They Do
Negotiate and administer PBM network contracts on behalf of thousands of independent pharmacies, and reconcile the reimbursement those contracts actually produced — including the retroactive fees Pass 1 identifies as the industry's central financial problem.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 contract analysts, reconciliation staff, and network managers
**Output:** Contract negotiation and administration, reimbursement reconciliation, fee analysis, member reporting
**Proprietary data:** Claim-level reimbursement outcomes across thousands of independent pharmacies — the only aggregate view of what independents were actually paid
**Clock:** Contract cycles and monthly reconciliation
**Buyer:** VP of Network Services
