# Drug Compendia & Pricing Content Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pharmacy-independents|Independent Pharmacies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Maintain the drug knowledge bases every pharmacy system, EHR, and claims processor in the country runs on: the NDC-level product file, the pricing benchmarks reimbursement formulas reference, the therapeutic classification, the dosing and duplicate-therapy logic, and the interaction and allergy screening that fires at the point of dispensing. Pass 1 notes independent pharmacies have near-universal pharmacy management system adoption; every one of those systems is a shell around content licensed from this layer.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-1,500 pharmacists, clinical content editors, drug information specialists, terminologists, and pricing analysts
**Output:** Drug product and pricing files, interaction and contraindication knowledge bases, dosing and clinical decision support content, therapeutic classification, allergy and duplicate therapy logic
**Proprietary data:** Decades of curated clinical editorial work over a literature that is contradictory and continuously revised, plus NDC-level pricing history and the mapping between every drug identifier system in use — none of which exists in any public source in usable form
**Clock:** Daily — new approvals, NDC changes, price changes, recalls and safety communications flow continuously, and customers' dispensing systems block on stale content
**Buyer:** Chief Clinical Officer / VP of Content
