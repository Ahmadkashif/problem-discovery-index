# Pharmacy Benefit Consulting & Contract Audit

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pharmacy-independents|Independent Pharmacies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Advise plan sponsors on PBM contract terms, run competitive procurements, and audit whether the PBM delivered the pricing guarantees it promised — recomputing millions of claims against contract definitions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 consultants, clinical pharmacists, and audit analysts
**Output:** Contract term benchmarking, procurement analysis, guarantee reconciliation audits, clinical programme evaluation
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated PBM contract terms and realised pricing across many client procurements — the only comparative view of a market whose terms are all confidential
**Clock:** Annual plan renewals and contract audit cycles
**Buyer:** Managing Director / Practice Leader
