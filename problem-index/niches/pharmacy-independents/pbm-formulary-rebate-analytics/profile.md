# PBM Formulary & Rebate Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pharmacy-independents|Independent Pharmacies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Design formularies, negotiate manufacturer rebates, set pharmacy reimbursement, and administer the network terms independents operate under. Pass 1 describes the consequence from below: retroactive DIR fees assessed months after a prescription is filled, turning profitable prescriptions unprofitable and making margin planning impossible.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1,000-5,000 clinical pharmacists, actuaries, rebate analysts, network analysts, and data scientists
**Output:** Formulary placement decisions, rebate modelling, network reimbursement rates, utilisation management criteria, plan performance reporting
**Proprietary data:** National pharmacy claims at scale joined to negotiated net pricing that exists nowhere else
**Clock:** Annual plan year design, quarterly rebate reconciliation, and continuous claim adjudication
**Buyer:** Chief Analytics Officer / VP of Trade Analytics
