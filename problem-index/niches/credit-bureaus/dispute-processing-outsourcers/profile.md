# Dispute Processing Centres & Outsourcers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-bureaus|Credit Bureaus]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Business-process outsourcers and bureau-run offshore centres that open, key and route consumer disputes and send ACDVs for the nationwide and specialty bureaus, and that answer ACDVs on behalf of furnishers. Paid per case or per agent hour.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Hundreds of agents per centre (estimate); analytical staff under 10
**Output:** Processed disputes and ACDV responses
**Proprietary data:** None of their own — case data belongs to the bureau or furnisher client
**Clock:** The FCRA 30-day reinvestigation clock, carried for the client
**Buyer:** Client operations director
