# Enterprise Credit Score Validation & Approval

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-bureaus|Credit Bureaus]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
FHFA and the credit modelling teams at Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac validate and approve the credit score models agency mortgages may use. On 22 April 2026 FHFA and HUD opened a limited rollout of VantageScore 4.0, with FICO 10T to follow and historical score data to be published from summer 2026. This is the score slice of an organisation family profiled for counterparty work at [[niches/mortgage-lenders/agency-counterparty-oversight/profile|Agency Counterparty & Issuer Risk Oversight]]. The score developers being validated are [[niches/collections-agencies/credit-scoring-model-developers/profile|Credit Scoring Model Developers]] (54, kill switch).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 staff per organisation on score validation (estimate) — FHFA's team and each Enterprise's score-validation group counted separately
**Output:** Score model validations, approval decisions, implementation guidance, historical score datasets
**Proprietary data:** Enterprise loan-level performance history — their own
**Clock:** Phases set by FHFA and HUD themselves
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer
