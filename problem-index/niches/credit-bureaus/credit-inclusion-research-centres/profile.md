# Credit Inclusion & Alternative-Data Research Centres

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-bureaus|Credit Bureaus]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Nonprofit research groups that study whether alternative data — rent, utilities, telecom, cash flow — can score consumers the bureau file misses, and publish evaluations used by regulators, lenders and the bureaus. FinRegLab, PERC and the Urban Institute's credit research are representative (named from recall). Funded by foundations, industry sponsors and research contracts; reports are free.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-25 researchers per organisation (estimate)
**Output:** Empirical studies, policy reports, data-use frameworks
**Proprietary data:** Study datasets assembled from partner lenders, bureaus and data providers under research agreements
**Clock:** Grant cycles and regulatory comment periods
**Buyer:** Executive director / research director
