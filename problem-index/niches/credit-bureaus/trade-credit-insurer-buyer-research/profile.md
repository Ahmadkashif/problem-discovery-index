# Trade Credit Insurers' Buyer Risk Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-bureaus|Credit Bureaus]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Trade credit insurers — Allianz Trade, Atradius and Coface are the large three (named from recall) — insure suppliers' receivables and must research every buyer a policyholder sells to on credit, setting a credit limit per buyer and changing it as the buyer's condition moves. They are the commercial bureaus' most demanding customers and, in effect, a parallel commercial credit research operation.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~100-500 risk underwriters and buyer analysts per insurer (estimate)
**Output:** Buyer credit-limit decisions, buyer and sector risk ratings, country and industry outlooks
**Proprietary data:** Policyholders' overdue and claims reports and limit-request history across many sellers to the same buyers
**Clock:** Policyholders' limit requests and shipment dates; no regulatory deadline on the research
**Buyer:** Chief Risk Officer / Head of Risk Underwriting
