Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
The CFPB forwarded more than 5.9 million complaints to companies in 2025, most of them about credit reporting, and it now directs consumers to dispute with the bureau before complaining. Bureaus nonetheless receive complaints about disputes already open, disputes about complaints already answered, and the same credit repair template through several channels. Each is handled and answered on its own clock.

## Why It's Still Broken
Complaint response and dispute operations are often separate teams with separate systems, and matching a complaint narrative to an open dispute is manual.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Match every incoming complaint to open and recent disputes by consumer, items and text similarity; answer both from one investigation; flag duplicates for consistent handling without closing any of them unreviewed.

## Who Feels the Pain
Complaint response teams, dispute agents working the same case twice, and consumers who get two different answers.

## Impact If Fixed
One investigation, one consistent answer, and capacity returned to the disputes that are new.
