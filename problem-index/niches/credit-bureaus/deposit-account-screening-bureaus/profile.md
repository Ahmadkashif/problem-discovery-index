# Check & Deposit Account Screening Bureaus

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-bureaus|Credit Bureaus]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Specialty consumer reporting agencies that record how consumers have handled deposit accounts — closures for cause, unpaid overdrafts, returned items, suspected fraud — and sell account-opening screens and item-risk scores to banks and credit unions. ChexSystems (within FIS) and Early Warning Services, which describes itself as owned by seven of the largest US banks and runs the National Shared Database, are the main players; check verification services serve retailers. NCLC reported that over 80% of banks use an account screening agency.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-100 fraud analytics, modelling and data staff per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Account-opening screening reports and scores; item return-risk predictions; fraud alerts
**Proprietary data:** Account-closure and returned-item records contributed by member banks
**Clock:** Point-of-decision account opening and item clearing; no third-party deadline on the output
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / Head of Risk Analytics
