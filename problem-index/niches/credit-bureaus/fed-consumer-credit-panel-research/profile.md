# Federal Reserve Consumer Credit Panel Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-bureaus|Credit Bureaus]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The New York Fed's Center for Microeconomic Data maintains the Consumer Credit Panel, "constructed from a nationally representative random sample of Equifax credit report data", and publishes the quarterly Household Debt and Credit Report and research on delinquency, student loans and household balance sheets. It is the most widely cited public view of the US credit file. The CFPB's own panel research is profiled at [[niches/collections-agencies/cfpb-consumer-research/profile|Federal Consumer Finance Research]] (34).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-30 economists, research analysts and data staff working on the panel (estimate)
**Output:** Quarterly household debt and credit reports, research papers, regional data
**Proprietary data:** An anonymised longitudinal sample licensed from Equifax
**Clock:** The Fed's own quarterly publication calendar
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer
