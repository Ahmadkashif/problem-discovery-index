# Metro 2 Furnishing Software & Compliance Consultants

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-bureaus|Credit Bureaus]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors of Metro 2 file-building software, credit reporting modules for cores and servicing platforms, and consultants who audit furnishers' reporting and train their staff on the Credit Reporting Resource Guide. Small furnishers are the main customers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analytical staff per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Furnishing software, mapping reviews, compliance audits, training
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated knowledge of furnishers' mapping errors; no data corpus of their own
**Clock:** Monthly furnishing cycles and annual CRRG updates
**Buyer:** Owner / practice lead
