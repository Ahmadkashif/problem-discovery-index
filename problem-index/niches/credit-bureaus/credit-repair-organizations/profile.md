# Credit Repair Organizations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-bureaus|Credit Bureaus]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Firms paid by consumers to dispute items on their credit reports, governed by the Credit Repair Organizations Act and state laws. They generate a large share of the bureaus' dispute volume; the CFPB's 2025 report named them, alongside AI-generated submissions, as a cause of the doubling in complaints. The largest operator, Progrexion (Lexington Law), faced a CFPB judgment of ~$2.7B and wound down (recalled).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analytical staff per firm (estimate); the work is letter production and client service
**Output:** Dispute letters and complaints filed on clients' behalf
**Proprietary data:** Client files and dispute outcomes, held for the client
**Clock:** The bureaus' 30-day reinvestigation clock
**Buyer:** Owner / CEO
