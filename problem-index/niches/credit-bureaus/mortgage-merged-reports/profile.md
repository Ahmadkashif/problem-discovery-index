# Mortgage Merged Reports & Score Transition

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-bureaus|Credit Bureaus]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver the merged report and whichever score set each lender's investors now accept — Classic FICO, VantageScore 4.0, later FICO 10T — straight into the lender's pricing and underwriting engines, and whoever re-plumbs the score transition without breaking a lender's pricing or pull-through takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.5B a year of mortgage credit report, score and reissue charges (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High — reports ordered from the loan origination system and returned in minutes
**Target Buyer:** VP of Mortgage Operations / Secondary Marketing at a lender; CEO of a mortgage credit reseller
**Automation Potential:** Very high

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Agency mortgages require a tri-merge report: a reseller pulls all three bureau files, merges them and attaches scores. The work is mechanical, and in 2025–2026 its economics and plumbing are being rewritten. FHFA and HUD opened a limited rollout of VantageScore 4.0 on 22 April 2026, with FICO 10T to follow and the tri-merge kept; FICO's direct licence lets resellers pay $4.95 per score plus a $33 funded-loan fee, or $10 per score, instead of buying through the bureaus. Every score change ripples into loan-level price adjustments, investor eligibility and pricing engines. The resellers' insight layer is profiled at [[niches/mortgage-brokers/mortgage-credit-reporting/profile|🔍 Mortgage Credit Reporting]] (43).

## Current Tools & Gaps
Reseller merge platforms (Xactus, Factual Data, Credit Plus and others), credit interfaces in loan origination systems, product and pricing engines, and agency automated underwriting. The gaps: no loan-level view of what each score choice does to price and eligibility; merge logic built for one score model; rapid rescores run by document exchange.
