# State Attorneys General & State Credit Reporting Regulators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-bureaus|Credit Bureaus]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State attorneys general enforce state credit reporting and consumer protection laws — the 2015 multistate National Consumer Assistance Plan with the nationwide bureaus is the landmark (recalled) — and some states register or supervise consumer reporting agencies directly, such as New York's DFS (recalled). With the federal medical debt rule vacated in 2025, state medical debt reporting laws are where new rules now appear.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analysts per state working on credit reporting (estimate)
**Output:** Investigations, settlements, registration and examination findings
**Proprietary data:** Complaint files and investigation records, confidential
**Clock:** Statutory effective dates and litigation schedules
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer
