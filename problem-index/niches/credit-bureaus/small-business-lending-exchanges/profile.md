# Small-Business Lending Data Exchanges

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-bureaus|Credit Bureaus]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Lender-controlled exchanges that pool small-business loan, lease and card payment data and release it through certified vendors. The Small Business Financial Exchange is a member-controlled nonprofit whose data reaches the market through certified vendors (Dun & Bradstreet from 2015, Equifax, Experian, LexisNexis Risk Solutions), and only members may buy SBFE-based products. PayNet's equipment-finance exchange now sits inside Equifax (recalled), so its analysts belong to the commercial bureau organisation family profiled at [[niches/independent-retailers/commercial-credit-bureaus/profile|Commercial Credit Bureaus]] (56, indexed).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 data-quality and analytics staff at SBFE (estimate); the products are built by the certified vendors
**Output:** Pooled lender payment data, data-quality reporting to members, certified-vendor data feeds
**Proprietary data:** Small-business credit obligations contributed by member lenders
**Clock:** Members' credit decisions; no third-party deadline
**Buyer:** Executive director of the exchange
