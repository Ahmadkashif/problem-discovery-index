# Servicing Platform Workflows Adapted to e-OSCAR

**Niche:** [[niches/credit-bureaus/furnisher-acdv-response/profile|Furnisher ACDV Response]]
**Industry:** [[industries/credit-bureaus|Credit Bureaus]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Servicing and collection platforms hold every fact an ACDV asks about and make the dispute clerk copy them across by hand.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #decision-trees #evaluation-metrics #compliance #workflow-orchestration #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to answer each ACDV inside the bureau's response window with the account records actually checked, and whoever lets a furnisher's dispute team clear its e-OSCAR queue on time with an investigation record that holds up in an FCRA §623 suit takes the account.

## The Problem
An ACDV lists the disputed fields and a dispute code. The clerk opens the servicing system, the payment history, the collection notes and sometimes the original contract, compares each field, decides, and keys the response into e-OSCAR. Direct disputes arrive by mail and email and are tracked in spreadsheets.

## What Already Exists
Loan servicing and collection platforms with dispute flags and Metro 2 extract modules; e-OSCAR's web interface and batch files.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: ACDVs pulled into the servicing workflow automatically; each disputed field shown beside the system-of-record value and the value last reported; documents retrieved to the case; response codes and AUD corrections drafted from the comparison; compliance condition codes set and cleared correctly while a dispute is open; direct disputes on the same clock and record; and an investigation log written as the clerk works.

## Target Customer
Dispute operations managers at furnishers on mainstream servicing and collection platforms.

## Impact If Solved
Each ACDV takes minutes of checking rather than minutes of searching (estimate), and the investigation record exists by default.
