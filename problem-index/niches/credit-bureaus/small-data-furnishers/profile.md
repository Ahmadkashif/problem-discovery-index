# Small Data Furnishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-bureaus|Credit Bureaus]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a small furnisher's loan or rent ledger into an accepted, accurate Metro 2 file every month without a data-reporting specialist, and whoever drives the furnisher's reject and dispute rates lowest takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M a year of furnishing software, service bureaus, consultants and staff time at small furnishers (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — an extract mapped years ago, uploaded monthly, with reject reports read by one person
**Target Buyer:** VP of Lending Operations at a credit union or small lender; product heads at property-management and loan-servicing software vendors
**Automation Potential:** High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Most of the furnishers by count are small (estimate): credit unions, [[industries/community-banks|community banks]], buy-here-pay-here dealers, small finance companies, landlords and rent reporting services, utilities, medical and student lenders. They carry the same FCRA §623 accuracy and dispute duties as a national issuer and face the same Metro 2 rules, revised every year in the CDIA's Credit Reporting Resource Guide (recalled), with no data-reporting staff. The bureaus' tools — load edits, reject reports, furnisher support desks — were built for furnishers who have such staff. The tools ignore the person who actually does the work, profiled in [[problems/credit-bureaus/worker-life-2|the one-person credit reporting desk]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Metro 2 modules in core banking, loan servicing and property-management systems; Metro 2 file-building software and consultants; the bureaus' furnisher onboarding and support. The gaps: mappings nobody re-checks after upgrades; special situations (bankruptcy, forbearance, deceased, authorised users) coded by guess; reject reports in field numbers; no check before a file is submitted.
