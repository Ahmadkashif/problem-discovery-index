# The Reject Report Nobody Reads

**Niche:** [[niches/credit-bureaus/small-data-furnishers/profile|Small Data Furnishers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/credit-bureaus|Credit Bureaus]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Every month the bureaus send small furnishers a list of rejected and questionable records in field codes, and the list goes unread until a member complains.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #decision-trees #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #compliance #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a small furnisher's loan or rent ledger into an accepted, accurate Metro 2 file every month without a data-reporting specialist, and whoever drives the furnisher's reject and dispute rates lowest takes the account.

## The Problem
After each load, each bureau returns reject and warning reports listing records that failed edits or look inconsistent. For a small furnisher they arrive as files or portal pages with Metro 2 field numbers and error codes, three times over, one per bureau. Rejected records simply do not update, so the consumer's file shows last month's data — or the account not at all.

## Why It's Still Broken
The reports are written for data-reporting specialists, and each bureau formats them differently.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Consolidate the three bureaus' reports into one list of affected accounts, explain each error in plain language with the likely cause in the furnisher's own system, propose the correction, and track whether it cleared next month.

## Who Feels the Pain
The one person responsible for credit reporting at a small furnisher; members and tenants whose accounts are silently not updating.

## Impact If Fixed
Rejects corrected within the month rather than discovered in a dispute, and a furnisher who can see its own reporting quality.
