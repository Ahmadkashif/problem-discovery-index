# Credit Reporting Trade Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-bureaus|Credit Bureaus]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Consumer Data Industry Association represents consumer reporting agencies, stewards the Metro 2 format through the Credit Reporting Resource Guide (recalled), litigates and lobbies (it sued to vacate the CFPB medical debt rule in 2025); the National Consumer Reporting Association represents mortgage credit resellers and smaller agencies (recalled). Funded by member dues, training and the CRRG.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research or analytical staff (estimate)
**Output:** Format standards, comment letters, training, occasional commissioned studies
**Proprietary data:** None at scale
**Clock:** Rulemaking comment periods and annual format updates
**Buyer:** President / CEO
