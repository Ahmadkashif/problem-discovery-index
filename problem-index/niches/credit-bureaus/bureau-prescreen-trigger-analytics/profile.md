# Bureau Prescreen & Trigger Marketing Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-bureaus|Credit Bureaus]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
The marketing services units of the nationwide bureaus build prescreened lists for firm offers of credit, response and booking models, account-review and trigger alerts, and campaign analytics for card issuers, personal lenders, auto lenders and mortgage originators. Lenders pay per name and per model. The agency side of the same campaigns is [[niches/direct-mail-agencies/financial-services-prescreen-mail/profile|Financial Services Prescreen & Acquisition Mail]]. The Homebuyers Privacy Protection Act cut off most mortgage trigger leads from 5 March 2026.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~30-150 analysts, modellers and campaign consultants per bureau's marketing services unit (estimate)
**Output:** Prescreened firm-offer lists, response and booking models, trigger alerts, campaign performance analyses
**Proprietary data:** The bureau's own credit file and inquiry history, plus campaign response files returned by clients
**Clock:** The HPPA effective date (a one-time statutory deadline) and clients' mail-drop calendars
**Buyer:** Head of Marketing Services / VP of Acquisition Analytics at the bureau

**Kill switches:** live — data isn't theirs to use. Prescreen is built on consumer report data that furnishers supply under FCRA furnishing agreements and that may leave the bureau only for a §604(c) firm offer; the response files that train the models belong to the lender clients. Any outside engine working below published aggregates needs a permissible purpose and the bureau's legal review — the same ruling as [[niches/sales-finance-companies/bureau-auto-finance-analytics/profile|Credit Bureau Automotive Finance Analytics]] (41, kill switch).
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch (43). Three bureaus make three buyers (Q5 2), and the clocks are the clients' campaign dates apart from one statutory cut-off.
