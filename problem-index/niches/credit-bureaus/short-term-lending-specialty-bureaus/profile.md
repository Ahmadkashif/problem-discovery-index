# Short-Term & Subprime Lending Specialty Bureaus

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-bureaus|Credit Bureaus]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Specialty consumer reporting agencies that collect application and repayment data from online installment, payday, title and rent-to-own lenders whose customers have thin or damaged mainstream files, and sell reports and scores back to those lenders. Clarity Services (Experian), FactorTrust (TransUnion), DataX and MicroBilt are representative (named from recall).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-40 analysts and modellers per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Specialty credit reports, subprime risk and fraud scores, application-velocity alerts
**Proprietary data:** Application and repayment histories contributed by member lenders, covering borrowers mainstream files miss
**Clock:** Point-of-application decisions; no third-party deadline
**Buyer:** Head of Analytics at the specialty bureau
