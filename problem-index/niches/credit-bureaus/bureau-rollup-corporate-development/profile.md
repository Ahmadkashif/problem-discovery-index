# Bureau Roll-up Corporate Development

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-bureaus|Credit Bureaus]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The corporate development teams at the nationwide bureaus and at private-equity-backed screening and data platforms that buy specialty bureaus, verification databases and data assets — the route by which subprime bureaus, commercial exchanges and card benchmarking consortia ended up inside Experian, Equifax and TransUnion.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~3-10 corporate development staff per acquirer (estimate)
**Output:** Acquisition screens, valuations, integration plans
**Proprietary data:** Deal pipelines and integration records, internal
**Clock:** Deal timetables set by sellers and bankers
**Buyer:** Head of Corporate Development
