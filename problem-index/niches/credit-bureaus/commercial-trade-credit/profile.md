# Commercial Trade Credit Reporting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-bureaus|Credit Bureaus]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to have current payment experience on the small business a supplier is about to ship to on terms, and whoever covers the most businesses that have no bureau file yet takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3B a year of US commercial credit reports, scores and monitoring sold to suppliers, lenders and procurement teams (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low at the edge — large-company files are rich, but small-business trade experience still moves through credit applications, reference calls and credit group spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** Corporate Credit Manager at a manufacturer or distributor; Head of Commercial Data at a bureau
**Automation Potential:** Medium

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Commercial credit reporting sits mostly outside the FCRA and runs on a different data source: the accounts-receivable experience suppliers contribute about their customers, plus lender data pooled through the Small Business Financial Exchange, whose members own and control their contributed data. The large commercial bureaus are profiled as an insight pocket at [[niches/independent-retailers/commercial-credit-bureaus/profile|🔍 Commercial Credit Bureaus]] (56, indexed); this niche is the operating contest underneath. When a new small customer asks for net-30 terms, the credit manager often finds a thin or empty file and falls back on the trade references on the customer's credit application — calling or emailing three suppliers and asking how the account pays.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Dun & Bradstreet, Experian Business, Equifax Commercial and Creditsafe files and scores; credit application and receivables automation platforms; industry credit groups run through trade associations such as NACM. The gaps: reference checks done by phone and lost afterwards; receivables data that never reaches a bureau; and credit applications re-keyed from PDFs.
