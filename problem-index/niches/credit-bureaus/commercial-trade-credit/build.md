# Trade References Turned Into Contributed Tradelines

**Niche:** [[niches/credit-bureaus/commercial-trade-credit/profile|Commercial Trade Credit Reporting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/credit-bureaus|Credit Bureaus]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Every day credit managers phone each other to ask how a small customer pays, and the answer — the most current payment data on that business anywhere — is written on a sticky note and lost.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #k-nearest-neighbors #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to have current payment experience on the small business a supplier is about to ship to on terms, and whoever covers the most businesses that have no bureau file yet takes the account.

## The Problem
A small contractor applies for an account with a building-materials distributor and lists three suppliers as references. The distributor's credit analyst emails or calls each: how long the account has been open, the high credit, the terms, whether it pays on time. The references answer because they will want the favour returned. The result decides a credit line and is then discarded.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Reference exchange is informal and reciprocal, and suppliers distrust giving data to a bureau that will sell it back to them. Bureaus collect receivables files from larger suppliers and have not reached the long tail one reference at a time.

## What to Build
A reference exchange that sends structured requests to the listed suppliers, accepts answers by email or form, matches the business to an identity across name and address variants, and — with the respondents' agreement — turns each answer into a dated tradeline the requester sees immediately and the network accumulates.

## Target Customer
Commercial bureaus seeking small-business coverage; credit managers at distributors and manufacturers who run reference checks every week.

## Impact If Built
Reference checks in hours rather than days (estimate), and files on businesses that today have none.
