# Rent & Utility Payment Reporting Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/credit-bureaus|Credit Bureaus]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Services that collect rent or utility payment records from landlords, property-management software or consumers and furnish them to the bureaus as tradelines; Esusu and RentReporters are representative (named from recall). Paid by landlords as a resident benefit or by consumers by subscription.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analytical staff per firm (estimate); the rest is integration, furnishing operations and support
**Output:** Rent tradelines furnished to bureaus; resident credit-building reports
**Proprietary data:** Payment records they pass through on behalf of landlords and tenants
**Clock:** Monthly furnishing cycles
**Buyer:** CEO / Head of Partnerships
