# Short-Term Rental Insurance Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/short-term-rentals|Short-Term Rentals]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Underwrite the specialty policies covering properties rented on a nightly basis — a use most homeowners policies exclude — and adjudicate the liability and damage claims that follow.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 underwriters, actuaries, and claims analysts
**Output:** Rating and eligibility, claims adjudication, loss frequency analysis by property and use type, coverage design
**Proprietary data:** Claims joined to booking intensity, guest type and property characteristics
**Clock:** Annual renewals and continuous claim flow
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
