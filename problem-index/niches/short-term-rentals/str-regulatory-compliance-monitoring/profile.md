# Short-Term Rental Regulatory Compliance Monitoring

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/short-term-rentals|Short-Term Rentals]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Identify which listings in a city correspond to which physical addresses, determine which are unregistered or in violation of local ordinance, and run the registration, tax remittance and enforcement workflow for the municipality — the layer that decides whether a given host is operating legally.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 data scientists, address resolution analysts, compliance specialists, and policy staff
**Output:** Listing-to-address identification, compliance and violation determinations, registration administration, tax remittance reconciliation, ordinance impact analysis
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated listing-to-parcel resolutions across hundreds of jurisdictions, joined to registration and enforcement outcomes
**Clock:** Registration deadlines, tax remittance dates, and enforcement cycles set by ordinance
**Buyer:** VP of Product / General Manager
