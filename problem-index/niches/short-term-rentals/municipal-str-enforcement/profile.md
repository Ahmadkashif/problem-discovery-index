# Municipal Short-Term Rental Enforcement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/short-term-rentals|Short-Term Rentals]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Administer registration and permit regimes, investigate complaints, assess penalties, and collect transient occupancy tax from hosts within a jurisdiction.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 across the larger cities — permit administrators, investigators, and analysts
**Output:** Registration and permit decisions, violation notices and penalties, tax collection, ordinance impact reporting
**Proprietary data:** Registration, complaint and enforcement records held municipally, with no exchange between jurisdictions
**Clock:** Registration renewal periods and tax remittance deadlines
**Buyer:** Programme manager
