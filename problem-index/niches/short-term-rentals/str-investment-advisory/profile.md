# Short-Term Rental Investment Advisory

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/short-term-rentals|Short-Term Rentals]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Advise investors on acquiring properties for short-term rental use — market selection, revenue projection, regulatory risk and underwriting — usually as small consultancies or as an advisory arm of a brokerage.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-25 analysts per firm
**Output:** Market and property selection analysis, revenue projections, regulatory risk assessment, acquisition underwriting
**Proprietary data:** Projection accuracy against realised performance for advised acquisitions, rarely retained
**Clock:** Transaction timelines
**Buyer:** Managing Partner
