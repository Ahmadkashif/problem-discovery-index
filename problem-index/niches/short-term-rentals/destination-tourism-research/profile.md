# Destination & Tourism Research Organizations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/short-term-rentals|Short-Term Rentals]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Measure visitation, visitor spending and economic impact for destinations, and study how short-term rental supply affects local housing and tax revenue — the research municipalities cite when they write the ordinances that constrain the industry.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 researchers, economists, and analysts across destination organisations and consultancies
**Output:** Visitation and spending estimates, economic impact studies, housing impact research, visitor profile surveys
**Proprietary data:** Visitor surveys and mobility-derived visitation estimates, assembled per destination
**Clock:** Annual reporting and legislative cycles
**Buyer:** Director of Research
