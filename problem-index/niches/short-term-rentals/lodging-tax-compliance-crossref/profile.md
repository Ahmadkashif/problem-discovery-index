# Lodging Tax Content & Compliance

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/short-term-rentals|Short-Term Rentals]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Maintain the transient occupancy tax rules for thousands of separate taxing jurisdictions — rates, registration requirements, filing frequencies and remittance rules that differ city by city — and file on behalf of hosts and platforms.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-800 tax researchers, content analysts, and compliance staff
**Output:** Jurisdiction rate and rule content, registration and licensing determination, return preparation and filing, notice resolution
**Proprietary data:** Continuously maintained rule content across jurisdictions that publish inconsistently and change without notice
**Clock:** Statutory filing deadlines in every jurisdiction, monthly to annually
**Buyer:** VP of Tax Content / Chief Compliance Officer
