# Short-Term Rental Management Rollup Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/short-term-rentals|Short-Term Rentals]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator/rollup

## What They Do
Corporate analytics inside the national short-term rental management companies — portfolio revenue performance, owner acquisition and churn, cleaning and maintenance cost, and market entry decisions across tens of thousands of managed properties.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 analysts, data scientists, and revenue managers
**Output:** Portfolio revenue and occupancy reporting, owner economics and churn modelling, operating cost analytics, market and property selection
**Proprietary data:** Actual booking, revenue and cost data for tens of thousands of properties — the observed truth the market data vendors are inferring
**Clock:** Monthly owner statements and seasonal booking cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Analytics / Chief Revenue Officer
