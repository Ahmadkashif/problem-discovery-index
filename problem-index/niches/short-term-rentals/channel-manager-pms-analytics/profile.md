# Channel Manager & Property Management Software Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/short-term-rentals|Short-Term Rentals]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Supply the systems that synchronise listings and calendars across platforms and run the operations behind a rental — messaging, cleaning scheduling, and owner accounting — and analyse the resulting activity.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 product, data, and integration staff
**Output:** Channel synchronisation, operations dashboards, occupancy and revenue reporting, benchmark publications
**Proprietary data:** Reservation and operating records across tens of thousands of managed properties
**Clock:** Continuous booking synchronisation
**Buyer:** VP of Product
