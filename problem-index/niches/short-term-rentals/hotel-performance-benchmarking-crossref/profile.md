# Lodging Performance Benchmarking & Demand Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/short-term-rentals|Short-Term Rentals]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Collect operating performance from lodging supply and publish the occupancy, rate and revenue benchmarks the accommodation sector is valued, financed and managed against — increasingly including short-term rental supply alongside hotels.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 data operations staff, economists, analysts, and methodologists
**Output:** Occupancy, rate and revenue benchmarks by market and segment, competitive set reporting, demand forecasts, pipeline tracking
**Proprietary data:** Direct operating submissions from a large share of lodging supply, contributed under confidentiality over decades
**Clock:** Daily and weekly reporting against revenue management and budgeting cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Research Officer / SVP of Analytics
