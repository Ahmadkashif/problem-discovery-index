# Pesticide Registration & Regulatory Affairs Consulting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pest-control|Pest Control]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Get pesticide products registered and keep them registered. Design the study programme EPA will require, assemble the dossier, argue the risk assessment, run the fifty separate state registrations, and defend products through registration review — the mandatory cycle in which every registered pesticide is re-examined against current science. Nothing a pest control technician sprays exists commercially without this work having been done first.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-400 regulatory scientists, toxicologists, ecotoxicologists, exposure modellers, and registration specialists at the specialist consultancies
**Output:** Registration strategies and dossiers, human health and ecological risk assessments, exposure models, label negotiations, registration review responses, state registration programmes
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated regulatory precedent — which study designs, waiver arguments, exposure assumptions and label positions the agency accepted, from whom, and which it rejected — none of which the agency publishes in usable form
**Clock:** Statutory registration review cycles, PRIA decision deadlines, annual state renewals, and data call-ins that arrive with a fixed response window
**Buyer:** Managing Director / VP of Regulatory Sciences
