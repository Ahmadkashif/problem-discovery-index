# Termite Warranty & Damage Repair Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pest-control|Pest Control]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Underwrite and reinsure the termite bonds and repair guarantees pest control companies sell with treatment — the promise that if termites return and damage the structure, somebody pays for it. Every treatment sold with a damage warranty is an underwriting decision.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 underwriters, actuaries, claims adjusters, and inspectors
**Output:** Warranty pricing and eligibility rules, claim adjudication, reserve setting, treatment protocol requirements
**Proprietary data:** Treated structures joined to retreatment and damage claims over decades, with construction type, soil, region and applied protocol attached
**Clock:** Annual bond renewals and claim reporting
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
