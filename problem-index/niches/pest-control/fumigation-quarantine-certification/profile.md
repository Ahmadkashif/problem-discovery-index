# Fumigation & Quarantine Treatment Certification

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pest-control|Pest Control]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Administer the treatment schedules and certification regimes that govern quarantine fumigation of imported and exported commodities, and audit the providers performing them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 programme staff, auditors, and treatment specialists
**Output:** Approved treatment schedules, provider certification and audit, phytosanitary certificates, treatment monitoring
**Proprietary data:** Treatment monitoring records and interception outcomes
**Clock:** Shipment schedules and statutory treatment windows
**Buyer:** Programme manager
