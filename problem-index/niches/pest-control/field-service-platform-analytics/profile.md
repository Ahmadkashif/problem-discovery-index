# Pest Control Field Service Platform Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pest-control|Pest Control]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The analytics groups inside PestPac, FieldRoutes and their peers, who run route optimisation, churn modelling and benchmark reporting over the service records of a large share of the industry.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 data scientists, analysts, and product researchers
**Output:** Route and scheduling optimisation, customer churn models, pricing benchmarks, operational dashboards
**Proprietary data:** Millions of service visits with pest found, product applied, conditions noted, and whether the customer called back — the closest thing to a treatment outcome dataset that exists
**Clock:** Continuous service scheduling with seasonal pest cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Chief Product Officer
