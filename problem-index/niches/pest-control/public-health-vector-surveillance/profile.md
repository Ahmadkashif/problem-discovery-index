# Public Health Vector Surveillance

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pest-control|Pest Control]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Mosquito control districts and public health entomology programmes that trap, identify and test vectors, model disease risk, and direct area-wide treatment.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-800 across the larger districts and state programmes — entomologists, laboratory staff, and epidemiologists
**Output:** Vector abundance and infection rate surveillance, risk models, treatment decisions, public advisories
**Proprietary data:** Multi-decade trap and infection surveillance series tied to weather and land use
**Clock:** Seasonal vector activity and outbreak response
**Buyer:** District manager / state programme director
