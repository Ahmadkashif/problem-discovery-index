# GLP Efficacy & Toxicology Contract Laboratories

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pest-control|Pest Control]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Run the studies a pesticide registration requires — acute and chronic toxicology, ecotoxicology, environmental fate, residue, and field efficacy trials — under Good Laboratory Practice, and deliver the study reports that go into the dossier.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,500 study directors, toxicologists, field trial staff, and quality assurance personnel
**Output:** GLP study reports, efficacy trial results, environmental fate and residue data
**Proprietary data:** Study archives, though each belongs contractually to the sponsor
**Clock:** Registration submission deadlines and seasonal field trial windows
**Buyer:** VP of Scientific Services
