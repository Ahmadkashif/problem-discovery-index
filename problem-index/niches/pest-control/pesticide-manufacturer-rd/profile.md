# Pesticide Manufacturer Discovery & Field Development

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pest-control|Pest Control]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The discovery chemistry, formulation, resistance management and field development organisations inside the agrochemical manufacturers, who create the active ingredients and formulations the professional pest control market applies.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-3,000 chemists, entomologists, formulation scientists, and field development staff in the professional products divisions
**Output:** New actives and formulations, efficacy and resistance data, application guidance, label claims
**Proprietary data:** Screening libraries, formulation performance, and multi-year field trial results across geographies and pest species
**Clock:** Product development programmes and registration milestones
**Buyer:** VP of Research & Development
