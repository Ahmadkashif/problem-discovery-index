# Entomology Diagnostic & Identification Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/pest-control|Pest Control]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Identify submitted specimens to species and advise on the management implications — university extension diagnostic labs and the handful of commercial identification services.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 3-15 diagnosticians per laboratory
**Output:** Species identifications, management recommendations, distribution alerts
**Proprietary data:** Submission and identification records, valuable for range expansion tracking and almost never aggregated
**Clock:** Seasonal submission volume
**Buyer:** Laboratory director
