# Validity & Bias Measurement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/talent-assessment-platforms|Talent Assessment Platforms]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Whether the instrument predicts job performance where it is used, and whether it excludes people it should not.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.12B — 28% of US pre-employment assessment
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~28%
**Digital Adoption:** Uneven — bias audits are becoming routine, criterion validation is not
**Target Buyer:** Employers' assessment and legal functions; vendors; regulators
**Automation Potential:** High for the auditing; the validation is bounded by data access and time

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche

A test is bought on a validation study run by its seller and deployed for a role it was never validated against, and nobody ever checks whether the score predicted anything about the people hired.

That is the industry's central failure. The established test publishers carry genuine validation traditions; the newer entrants selling game-based, video-based and machine-learned assessments frequently assert validity from vendor-run studies that would not survive independent scrutiny. And regardless of the vendor, criterion validation at the client — does this score predict performance for this role in this organisation — is rarely established.

Adverse impact is the second property, and the one that has acquired legal force. Assessments producing different pass rates across demographic groups create exposure under US employment law and, more importantly, exclude people.

## Current Tools & Gaps

Vendor validation studies, technical manuals and validity generalisation arguments. Bias audit services, growing rapidly under regulatory pressure. Adverse impact ratio calculations. Some employers run local validation; most do not. Some vendors have withdrawn features under scrutiny, as HireVue did when it removed facial analysis from its assessments in 2021.

The gaps are local evidence and the interaction between the two properties. Criterion validity is asserted from elsewhere rather than established here. Bias audits are annual and aggregate, which is the least informative form available. And nobody measures the two together, though a fair instrument that predicts nothing is a worse outcome than either property alone suggests.
