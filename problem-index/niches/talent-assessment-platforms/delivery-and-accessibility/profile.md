# Assessment Delivery, Proctoring & Accessibility

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/talent-assessment-platforms|Talent Assessment Platforms]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Whether the person taking the assessment is measured on the construct or on their equipment, their connection and whether the interface works for them.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$360M — 9% of US pre-employment assessment
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9%
**Digital Adoption:** High for delivery; accessibility is an afterthought
**Target Buyer:** Vendors; employers; candidates with disabilities
**Automation Potential:** Very high — the delivery is solved and the accommodation path is not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche

Delivery is the layer where measurement error enters that has nothing to do with the construct. A slow connection times out a timed item. A phone screen renders a layout designed for a laptop. A shared household environment produces a proctoring flag. A screen reader cannot navigate an interactive item. A candidate who needs extra time cannot find how to ask for it before the deadline.

Each of these produces a score that measures circumstances rather than ability, and each falls unevenly — on candidates with less equipment, worse connectivity, less private space and disabilities. The instrument may be perfectly valid and the administration still invalid for that person.

The niche is distinct because it is entirely engineering and policy, with no psychometric uncertainty, and because the accessibility half carries legal obligations that the delivery half's design routinely fails.

## Current Tools & Gaps

Browser-based delivery with responsive layouts of varying quality. Mobile support, often partial. Proctoring ranging from none to webcam monitoring with behavioural flagging. Time limits enforced client-side or server-side. Accommodation processes, generally an email address.

The gaps are degradation, device parity and the accommodation path. Timed assessments frequently do not handle connection interruption gracefully, so a dropout becomes a failure. Mobile rendering of complex item types is often worse than desktop, which disadvantages candidates without a computer. And the accommodation request route is typically an email that arrives too late, to a mailbox that does not respond before the assessment deadline.
