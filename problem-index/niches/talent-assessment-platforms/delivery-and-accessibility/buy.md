# Buy: Proctoring and Accessibility Tooling Adapted to Employment Stakes

**Niche:** [[niches/talent-assessment-platforms/delivery-and-accessibility/profile|Assessment Delivery, Proctoring & Accessibility]]
**Industry:** [[industries/talent-assessment-platforms|Talent Assessment Platforms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Remote proctoring and accessibility tooling come from education; an employment candidate is not a student, has not consented to the institution's rules, and is being screened rather than examined.
**Tags:** #compliance #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #descriptive-statistics #cnns #workflow-orchestration #worker-facing #automation
**Contested on:** Whether education-derived proctoring is proportionate to a hiring screen.

## The Problem

Remote proctoring and accessible assessment delivery are established categories, built for education. Online exam proctoring with webcam monitoring, screen recording, behavioural flagging and identity verification is widely deployed in universities and certification, and accessibility tooling for digital assessment has real standards behind it.

The transfer to employment is uneasy. A student sits an exam under an institution's academic integrity policy they enrolled under. An employment candidate is a member of the public, has no relationship with the employer, is being screened rather than examined, and the stakes per administration are much lower — which makes intrusive monitoring disproportionate in a way it may not be for a licensure exam.

## What Already Exists

The remote proctoring vendors serving education and certification. Webcam and screen monitoring with behavioural flagging. Identity verification. Lockdown browsers. Accessible assessment delivery meeting WCAG and education accessibility standards. Alternative format generation. Extended time and accommodation workflows from education.

## The Customization Gap

**Proportionality is different and the defaults are wrong.** Webcam monitoring of a candidate in their home for a thirty-minute screening assessment is a different proposition from proctoring a final examination. The intrusiveness dial needs setting from the employment context, and the vendor defaults come from a higher-stakes one.

**Behavioural flagging penalises circumstances.** Looking away, background noise, another person entering the room, an unstable connection — these are flagged as suspicious and are, in a home with children and no private room, simply life. Base rates by environment, and a default interpretation that is benign, are what prevent proctoring from becoming a proxy for housing.

**Consent is not enrolment.** A student agreed to the institution's policies. A candidate is presented with a consent screen under evident pressure — decline and you are not considered. The privacy and biometric obligations that follow, which vary by jurisdiction, are stricter than education practice assumes.

**Accommodation has to work without an institution behind it.** In education, a disability services office holds documentation and arranges adjustments. Here there is no such office; the candidate must request from an employer they have no relationship with, on a deadline, and the request frequently goes nowhere. Building the workflow that substitutes for the institution is the central adaptation.

**Alternative formats drift out of equivalence.** Education accessibility practice maintains alternative versions; here they are frequently produced once and not updated as the instrument changes, so an accommodated candidate takes a different and unvalidated test. Equivalence has to be maintained and demonstrated.

## Target Customer

Assessment vendors deploying proctoring bought from education and finding the defaults disproportionate. Also employers' legal and accessibility functions, and the proctoring vendors, for whom employment is a large segment with a different proportionality and consent posture.

## Impact If Solved

The monitoring, identity verification and accessible delivery capability gets used at a setting appropriate to the stakes, and the proportionality calibration, benign-default flagging, consent posture, institution-free accommodation workflow and format equivalence get built. Concretely: a screening assessment that does not surveil someone's home to verify a thirty-minute test.
