**Contested on:** Whether a governance structure that works under regulatory compulsion can be adopted voluntarily.

## The Problem

The problem of an expert function whose advice is overridden by commercial pressure is solved elsewhere, under regulation. Banking's model risk management framework gives an independent validation function the authority to approve, restrict or reject a model's use, with documented findings, tracked limitations, periodic revalidation and escalation to a risk committee.

The structure works because a regulator requires it. Assessment science has the equivalent expertise, faces the same pressure, and has none of the standing, because no regulator has required it — though the direction of employment-tool regulation is toward more oversight, and the structure being adopted voluntarily now would meet it.

## What Already Exists

Model risk management frameworks and the governance literature behind them. Model inventory and governance platforms. AI governance tooling, growing quickly under regulatory pressure. Validation documentation standards. Risk committee structures and escalation practice. The psychometrics profession's own standards, which are advisory.

## The Customization Gap

**The authority has to be constructed rather than inherited.** Banking validation functions have standing because a regulator granted it. Here it must come from a board decision, written into policy, with a defined and narrow set of matters on which the science function can refuse. Designing that scope so it is respected rather than routed around is the substantive work.

**The unit of governance is a configuration, not a model.** Banking governs the model. Here the instrument is fixed and the risk is in how it is configured and deployed per client and per role — thousands of configurations rather than dozens of models. The inventory and review machinery has to operate at that granularity and cadence.

**Validation means something specific and different.** Model validation in banking tests a model's fitness for a defined use. Assessment validation is a psychometric procedure with its own methodology and evidentiary standards. The governance wrapper transfers; the technical content does not.

**The affected party is external and has rights.** A bank's model affects customers indirectly through pricing. An assessment configuration determines whether a specific person is considered for employment, which brings the candidate's interests into a governance structure designed around institutional risk.

**The override register is the key artefact and no governance product frames it that way.** These platforms track approvals; the useful instrument here is the pattern of exceptions granted under commercial pressure, which is a different report and the one that would change behaviour.

## Target Customer

Vendors' science leadership and general counsel building governance ahead of regulation, and large employers' assessment functions facing the same internal dynamic. Also the AI governance tooling vendors, for whom employment assessment is a distinct and regulated-adjacent segment.

## Impact If Solved

The inventory, approval workflow, documentation standards and escalation structure get borrowed from a discipline that solved this problem, and the constructed authority, configuration-level granularity, psychometric validation content, candidate interest and override register get built. Concretely: a science function whose advice has a recorded consequence.
