# The I-O Psychologist

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/talent-assessment-platforms|Talent Assessment Platforms]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Whether the person who knows the instrument is being misused has any mechanism to stop it.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$280M — 7% of US pre-employment assessment
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7%
**Digital Adoption:** Low — expertise delivered through documents and meetings
**Target Buyer:** Vendors' science functions; employers' assessment teams
**Automation Potential:** Moderate — the checks automate; the authority does not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche

The industrial-organisational psychologist knows the instrument is being applied outside its validated range, says so, and watches the configuration ship because the client asked for it.

The role is the industry's scientific conscience and has no authority. They sit inside vendors, advising sales and implementation teams whose incentives are to close and configure; or inside employers, advising talent acquisition leaders whose incentives are speed and volume. Their objection is a professional judgement delivered in a meeting, against a commercial decision with a deadline.

The niche is distinct because the expertise exists and is systematically overridden, and because the mechanisms that would give it effect — documented sign-off, configuration guardrails, escalation paths, professional standards with teeth — are absent by design rather than by oversight.

## Current Tools & Gaps

Technical documentation and validation reports. Implementation guidance. Professional standards from the psychometrics community, which are advisory. Internal review processes at the more careful vendors. Consulting engagements.

The gaps are enforcement and record. A recommendation delivered verbally and overridden leaves no trace, so the pattern of overrides is invisible and the psychologist carries the professional risk without the evidence. Configuration systems permit combinations the science does not support, with no warning and no block. And there is no escalation path when a configuration crosses from unwise into indefensible.
