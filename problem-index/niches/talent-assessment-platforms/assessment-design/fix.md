# Fix: Nobody Established What the Job Requires

**Niche:** [[niches/talent-assessment-platforms/assessment-design/profile|Assessment Design & Content]]
**Industry:** [[industries/talent-assessment-platforms|Talent Assessment Platforms]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The instrument measures conscientiousness and numerical reasoning because those are what the vendor sells, not because anyone established the role requires them.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #hypothesis-testing #compliance #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Whether anyone will establish what the role requires before choosing what to measure.

## The Problem

An employer deploys an assessment for a role. The instrument measures a set of constructs — cognitive ability, personality traits, situational judgement, a technical skill. Whether those constructs matter for this role was established by nobody.

The vendor's catalogue offered them. The hiring manager agreed they sounded relevant. The configuration was set in an implementation call. And now candidates are being excluded on measures whose connection to the job is an assumption.

Job analysis is the procedure that establishes the connection, it is the foundation of a defensible instrument, and it is skipped constantly — because it costs money and time, because nobody in the purchase asks for it, and because the instrument appears to work without it.

## Why It's Still Broken

Job analysis has a cost and no immediate payoff. The instrument functions, candidates get scored, hires get made, and the absence of the foundation is invisible until someone challenges the instrument or until a validation shows it predicts nothing.

The vendor's incentive is to sell what is in the catalogue, and a genuine job analysis might conclude that their instrument is not the right one.

And the employer's hiring manager, who knows the job best, is typically consulted for twenty minutes rather than through a structured procedure — which produces a list of adjectives rather than an analysis.

## What a Fix Looks Like

Do the analysis, cheaply and properly, before configuring the instrument.

Start from what already exists. Job descriptions, performance criteria for the role, the tasks incumbents actually spend time on, the reasons past hires succeeded or failed, and the competency frameworks the organisation already maintains. A structured reading of these produces a draft in hours rather than weeks.

Ask the incumbents, structured. A short survey of people doing the job — which tasks consume the most time, which are hardest, which distinguish the strong performers — is the core of a job analysis and is achievable in a week. Twenty responses is enough to be far better than a conversation with one manager.

Map each proposed construct to a task or competency the analysis established. Where a construct has no mapping, either the analysis missed something or the construct should not be measured. This is a table and it is the entire evidentiary basis for the instrument's job-relatedness.

Check the fit against the vendor's validation population using that mapping, rather than on job title, which is the comparison that actually matters.

Keep it as documentation. If the instrument is challenged, the job analysis is the first thing asked for, and an employer who has one is in a materially different position from one who does not.

And revisit it when the role changes. Roles drift, and an instrument justified by a job analysis from four years ago is justified by a description of a different job.

## Who Feels the Pain

Candidates excluded on constructs whose relevance to the job nobody established. Employers with legal exposure on an instrument whose job-relatedness they cannot evidence. Hiring managers who get candidates selected on the wrong criteria and cannot articulate why the process is not working. And the I-O psychologists inside vendors and employers who know the analysis was skipped.

## Impact If Fixed

The instrument measures things the role has been shown to require, with the mapping documented. Constructs with no job-related justification get removed, which usually shortens the assessment. And the employer has the first artefact any challenge will ask for, produced in a week from documents they already hold.
