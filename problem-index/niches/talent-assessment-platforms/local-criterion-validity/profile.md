# Local Criterion Validity

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/talent-assessment-platforms|Talent Assessment Platforms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Whether the score predicted anything about the people this employer actually hired.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$672M — 60% of the validity and bias niche
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~17% of US pre-employment assessment
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — asserted from elsewhere, rarely established here
**Target Buyer:** Large employers' assessment functions; vendors confident in their instruments
**Automation Potential:** Moderate — the method is known; the data access and the timeline are the constraint

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche

This is the half with no legal driver and the harder problem. Whether the assessment predicts performance for this role in this organisation requires the client's own outcome data — performance ratings, tenure, progression, productivity — joined to assessment scores for the people who were hired.

Three things make it distinct. The data lives inside the client's HR systems rather than the vendor's, so the vendor cannot do it alone and the client has no obligation to. The timeline is long: eighteen months before a cohort has accumulated enough outcome data to analyse. And the finding is commercially dangerous — a negative result invalidates a purchased, deployed instrument, embarrasses the person who selected it, and gives the vendor a problem in every other account.

The methodological difficulty is real too. Performance ratings are noisy and potentially biased, outcomes are observed only for those hired, and a single employer's sample is small.

## Current Tools & Gaps

Vendor validation studies and validity generalisation arguments. Consulting engagements for local validation, offered by the established publishers and taken up rarely. Employers' own HR analytics functions, which have the data and generally do not run this analysis.

The gaps are the join, the method and the will. The data exists on both sides and is rarely brought together. Where it is, range restriction and criterion contamination are frequently mishandled, producing either a falsely reassuring or a falsely damning result. And nobody in the transaction has an incentive to establish an answer that might be no.
