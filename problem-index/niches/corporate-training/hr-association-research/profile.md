# HR & Talent Association Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/corporate-training|Corporate Training]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
SHRM and the Association for Talent Development run the certifications the profession hires against, publish competency models, and produce the practice research and benchmarking their memberships plan around.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-150 researchers, psychometricians, and content staff
**Output:** Certification examinations, competency models, practice research and benchmarks
**Proprietary data:** Certification performance data across hundreds of thousands of candidates plus long-running practitioner survey series
**Clock:** Examination cycles and annual research calendars
**Buyer:** Chief Knowledge Officer / VP of Research
