# Labour Market & Skills Taxonomy Data Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/corporate-training|Corporate Training]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Lightcast, Revelio Labs, Draup, and the LinkedIn economic graph team parse hundreds of millions of job postings, profiles, and employment records into a structured skills and occupation taxonomy, then sell the resulting labour market analytics to employers building capability plans, universities designing programmes, and governments allocating workforce funding. Corporate learning functions buy it to answer what skills their workforce should be building.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-800 taxonomists, data scientists, labour economists, and research analysts
**Output:** Skills taxonomies, demand and supply analytics, compensation and talent flow research — the subscription
**Proprietary data:** A hand-built and continuously maintained skills ontology mapped over a longitudinal archive of hundreds of millions of postings and profiles, which no competitor can reconstruct retrospectively
**Clock:** Client planning cycles and public workforce funding calendars
**Buyer:** Chief Economist / VP of Data Science
