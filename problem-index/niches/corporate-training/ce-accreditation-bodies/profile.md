# Continuing Education Accreditation Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/corporate-training|Corporate Training]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
IACET, ACCME, and the profession-specific accreditors set the standards continuing education providers must meet and accredit them against those standards, which determines whether the credit a learner earns counts toward a licence.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 standards staff, reviewers, and researchers
**Output:** Accreditation standards, provider reviews, accreditation decisions
**Proprietary data:** Review findings across accredited providers and programme outcome data collected as an accreditation condition
**Clock:** Accreditation cycles and licensure renewal periods
**Buyer:** Executive Director / VP of Accreditation
