# Learning Platform Analytics Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/corporate-training|Corporate Training]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The data science groups at Cornerstone, Docebo, Degreed, and Workday Learning analyze learning behaviour across their enterprise customer base, build recommendation and skills inference features, and publish the benchmark reporting the industry cites.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 data scientists and product analysts
**Output:** Recommendation models, skills inference, benchmark reporting
**Proprietary data:** Learning consumption joined to role, tenure, and in some deployments to performance outcomes, across millions of employees
**Clock:** Release cycles, internal
**Buyer:** VP of Data Science / Chief Product Officer
