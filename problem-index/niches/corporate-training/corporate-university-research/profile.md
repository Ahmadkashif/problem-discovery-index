# Corporate University Learning Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/corporate-training|Corporate Training]]
**Category:** Insight Layer

## What They Do
The learning research and measurement functions inside large corporate universities design curricula, evaluate programmes, and analyze capability across the workforce for a single employer at very large scale.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 learning scientists, evaluators, and analysts
**Output:** Curriculum design, programme evaluation, capability analytics
**Proprietary data:** Learning, performance, and mobility data for one workforce, joined more completely than any vendor can achieve
**Clock:** Internal planning and budget cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Learning Officer
