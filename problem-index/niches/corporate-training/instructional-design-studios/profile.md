# Instructional Design & Content Development Studios

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/corporate-training|Corporate Training]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Custom content studios convert subject matter expert knowledge into structured curricula for corporate clients — running needs analyses, designing learning architectures, and producing the courseware. The design work is the invoice, and clients commission it because their own L&D teams cannot absorb the volume.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-200 instructional designers, learning strategists, and media developers
**Output:** Needs analyses, curriculum designs, courseware — the billable deliverable
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated design patterns and what worked across hundreds of programmes, almost all of it owned by the commissioning client
**Clock:** Client launch dates and compliance training deadlines
**Buyer:** Managing Director / Head of Learning Design
