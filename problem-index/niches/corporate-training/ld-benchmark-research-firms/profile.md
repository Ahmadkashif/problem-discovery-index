# Learning & Development Benchmark Research Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/corporate-training|Corporate Training]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Brandon Hall Group, i4cp, and the analyst practices covering human capital sell membership research on L&D practice — spend benchmarks, technology evaluations, maturity models, and case studies. L&D leaders subscribe because the research is what they cite in the budget justification cycles the Pass 1 analysis describes as their perpetual vulnerability.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 analysts, researchers, and survey managers
**Output:** Benchmark reports, technology evaluations, maturity assessments — the membership product
**Proprietary data:** Long-running practitioner survey series and accumulated maturity assessment data across member organizations
**Clock:** Annual research calendars and client budget cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Research Officer / Managing Director
