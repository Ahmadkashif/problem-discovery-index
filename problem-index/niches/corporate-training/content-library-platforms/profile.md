# Learning Content Library Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/corporate-training|Corporate Training]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
LinkedIn Learning, Coursera for Business, Udemy Business, and Skillsoft license enormous course catalogues to employers. Their content strategy and data teams decide what to commission, map courses to skills taxonomies, and analyze consumption across millions of learners.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 content strategists, taxonomists, and data scientists
**Output:** Catalogue strategy, skills mapping, consumption and outcome analytics
**Proprietary data:** Learning consumption and completion behaviour across millions of enterprise learners, mapped to roles and skills
**Clock:** Content release cycles and enterprise renewal calendars
**Buyer:** VP of Content Strategy / Chief Data Officer
