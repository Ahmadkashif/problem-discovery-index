# Talent Assessment Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/corporate-training|Corporate Training]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
SHL, Hogan, DDI, and Korn Ferry's assessment business develop and validate the instruments used to select, promote, and develop employees — cognitive tests, personality inventories, situational judgement tests, and assessment centre exercises. Psychometricians build the instruments, establish norms and job-relatedness, and defend the validity evidence that makes an assessment legally usable in employment decisions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-400 psychometricians, industrial-organizational psychologists, and research scientists
**Output:** Assessments, norm sets, validation studies, competency frameworks — the product and its legal defence
**Proprietary data:** Normative databases spanning millions of assessment administrations joined to job performance criteria collected in validation studies over decades
**Clock:** Client selection programme launches and periodic revalidation requirements
**Buyer:** Chief Science Officer / VP of Assessment Research
