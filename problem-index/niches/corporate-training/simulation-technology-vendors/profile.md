# Simulation & Practice Technology Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/corporate-training|Corporate Training]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors selling scenario simulation, conversational practice, and immersive training environments build the scenario libraries and scoring rubrics that make the practice meaningful, and analyze learner performance across those interactions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 learning scientists, content developers, and data staff
**Output:** Scenario libraries, scoring rubrics, performance analytics
**Proprietary data:** Learner performance in structured practice across many organizations — an unusually direct measure of capability rather than of completion
**Clock:** Release cycles and client deployment schedules
**Buyer:** Chief Learning Scientist / VP of Product
