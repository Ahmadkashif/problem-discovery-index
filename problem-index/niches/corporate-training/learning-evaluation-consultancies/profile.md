# Learning Evaluation Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/corporate-training|Corporate Training]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Specialist firms that measure whether training worked — designing evaluation studies, linking learning to performance data, and producing the impact analyses L&D leaders take into budget defence. The study is the invoice and it exists because the department cannot otherwise prove its value.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 evaluation researchers, methodologists, and analysts
**Output:** Impact evaluation studies, measurement frameworks, ROI analyses
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated evaluation methodology; the underlying performance data belongs to clients
**Clock:** Client budget cycles and programme review dates
**Buyer:** Managing Principal
