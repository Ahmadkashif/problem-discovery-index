# Education Benefit Administrators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/corporate-training|Corporate Training]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Guild, InStride, and the tuition assistance administrators manage employer-funded education programmes, curate the provider network, and produce the retention and mobility analytics employers renew against. Their whole commercial argument is a measured business outcome.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 outcomes researchers, data scientists, and analysts
**Output:** Programme outcome and ROI reporting, provider quality analysis, mobility research
**Proprietary data:** Employee education participation joined to retention, promotion, and internal mobility outcomes across many employers
**Clock:** Benefits plan year renewals
**Buyer:** VP of Research & Insights / Chief Data Officer
