# Print Outcome Prediction

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/print-on-demand-platforms|Print on Demand Platforms]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to know before printing whether this artwork on this product at this facility will come out acceptably — and whoever does that takes the margin, because reprints and refunds are the margin and they are almost entirely predictable.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.5B US attributable to the outcome question
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~25% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** None — printed once, no proof
**Target Buyer:** Every platform, and the merchants whose customers complain
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the corpus makes it a supervised problem

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The platform prints a unique file once with no proof, and reprints and refunds are the margin — while every past order records exactly which artwork, product and facility combinations went wrong. Commercial printing manages this risk with proofs, samples and operator expertise, none of which is economic on a single garment. The prediction is therefore the only available substitute: given this file, this product, this decoration method and this facility, will the result be accepted. Every element required to learn it exists in the platform's own history at a scale commercial printing never had, and the decision is currently made by a preflight check on resolution.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Resolution and dimension preflight, mockup generation, and operator judgement at the press. The gaps: no prediction of acceptance; colour reproducibility is not modelled against the process and substrate; placement against seams, pockets and product geometry is not checked; the facility's own record on similar work is not a factor; and no risk is surfaced to the merchant before the order is accepted.
