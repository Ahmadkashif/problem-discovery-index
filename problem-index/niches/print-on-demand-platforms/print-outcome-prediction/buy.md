**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Commercial printing built colour management, process capability measurement and prepress verification over decades, and this category checks resolution.
**Tags:** #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #hypothesis-testing #descriptive-statistics #numerical-methods #compliance #automation #probability-distributions
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to know before printing whether this artwork on this product at this facility will come out acceptably — and whoever does that takes the margin, because reprints and refunds are the margin and they are almost entirely predictable.

## The Problem
Knowing what a printing process can and cannot reproduce is a mature discipline. Colour management characterises a device and substrate combination and predicts what a given colour will actually become. Process capability measurement quantifies what a press can hold. Prepress verification checks a file against the specific process it will run on. Printing has done this for decades because a wrong print run is expensive, and this industry — where every job is a run of one and a wrong job is proportionally more expensive — checks the file's dimensions.

## What Already Exists
Colour management with device profiles and gamut mapping; process characterisation and capability indices; prepress verification against a defined output intent; soft proofing with calibrated displays; standardised print condition definitions; and quality measurement instrumentation.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a run of one on a textile substrate across a partner network. It requires: (1) device and substrate profiles per facility, machine and garment combination rather than per press, since the substrate varies per order and is the dominant variable — building and maintaining that profile set across a partner network is the specific work and is what nobody has done; (2) soft proofing shown to a merchant who has an uncalibrated consumer display, which makes the classical soft proof unreliable and argues for a predicted-outcome description rather than a rendered preview; (3) capability measured per partner facility and monitored for drift, which is straightforward with printed test targets and is the missing link between colour theory and this network; (4) verification against what the customer will accept rather than against a standard, since the tolerance here is a consumer's expectation rather than a brand specification; and (5) automation at a cost per job of pennies, which rules out the operator-mediated workflow commercial prepress assumes.

## Target Customer
Platform prepress and production engineering, partner facilities, and the colour management profession for whom a distributed textile network is an unserved application.

## Impact If Solved
Commercial printing solved knowing what a process can reproduce and this category checks dimensions. Per-facility, per-substrate profiles are the specific work, and capability measured with printed test targets is the missing link between colour theory and a partner network.
