# Artwork-Outcome Corpus

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/print-on-demand-platforms|Print on Demand Platforms]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn millions of artwork-to-physical-outcome pairs into a model of what will print well — and whoever does that owns a capability commercial printing never had the data to build.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** None — used for billing
**Target Buyer:** The platforms themselves
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the corpus is structured and labelled by outcome

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
These platforms hold something no printer has ever had: millions of artwork files paired with the product, the decoration method, the facility, the machine, and whether the result was accepted, reprinted, refunded or complained about. That is a direct mapping from digital input to physical outcome at a scale that makes the relationship learnable. Commercial printing has always relied on proofs and operator expertise because nobody had this data; print on demand generated it as a by-product and uses it for billing. Every other problem in this industry — prediction, preflight, routing, colour, merchant guidance — is a query against that corpus, and the corpus has never been assembled as one.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Order records, artwork storage, production logs and support tickets, in separate systems. The gaps: the artwork is not joined to the outcome; production parameters are not recorded against the order; the facility and machine are not consistently captured; complaint text is not classified into failure modes; and nothing from the corpus feeds any decision in the business.
