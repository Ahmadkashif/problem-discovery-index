# Library Supply & Circulation Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-publishers|Independent Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The library wholesalers and digital lending platforms supply collections to thousands of library systems and, in the digital case, see every hold, checkout, and wait list — the most granular reader demand signal in publishing.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-200 collection analysts, data staff, and product researchers
**Output:** Collection development recommendations, demand-driven acquisition, circulation analytics for library customers
**Proprietary data:** Circulation, holds, and wait lists across thousands of library systems — actual reader demand rather than purchase, at title level
**Clock:** Library budget cycles and on-sale dates
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Head of Collection Services
