# Title Management Platform Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-publishers|Independent Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Firebrand and Consonance run title management and metadata distribution for independent presses, holding catalogue, metadata, and sales history for the publishers that use them. Pass 1 notes adoption is uneven and many independents still run on spreadsheets.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 product and data staff
**Output:** Title and metadata management, ONIX distribution, sales and royalty reporting
**Proprietary data:** Catalogue metadata and sales history across many independent presses
**Clock:** On-sale dates and metadata deadlines
**Buyer:** Head of Product
