# Retail & Wholesale Buying Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-publishers|Independent Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The buying and category analytics groups at the major book wholesalers and retailers decide how many copies of a title to take, where to place it, and when to return it — decisions that largely determine whether an independent press's title succeeds.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 buyers, category analysts, and data staff
**Output:** Buy quantity models, assortment and placement decisions, returns forecasting
**Proprietary data:** Order, sell-through, and returns history across the full catalogue and every account — including the returns data publishers see only as a net number months later
**Clock:** Seasonal buy cycles and on-sale dates
**Buyer:** VP of Merchandising / Head of Analytics
