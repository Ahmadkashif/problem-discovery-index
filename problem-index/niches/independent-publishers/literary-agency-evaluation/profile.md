# Literary Agency Evaluation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-publishers|Independent Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Literary agencies filter the manuscript supply before it reaches publishers, deciding what to represent and where to submit it. They are the first and heaviest triage in the system, and Pass 1's acquisitions problem is the second pass over what they let through.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 agents and assistants at the larger agencies
**Output:** Representation decisions, submission strategy, deal negotiation
**Proprietary data:** Submission-to-offer outcomes across editors and imprints — which editor buys what, at what level, and how quickly
**Clock:** Submission rounds and auction timelines
**Buyer:** Agency principal — commission funded
