# Publisher Association Statistics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-publishers|Independent Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The publisher associations collect sales data from participating houses and publish the industry revenue series, alongside policy analysis on copyright, trade, and library licensing.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 researchers and policy staff
**Output:** Industry revenue estimates by format and category, policy analysis, member surveys
**Proprietary data:** Participant-contributed sales series covering a substantial share of trade publishing
**Clock:** Monthly and annual reporting cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Research — member funded
