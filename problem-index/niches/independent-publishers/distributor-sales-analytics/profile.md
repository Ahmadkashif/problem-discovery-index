# Publisher Distributor Sales Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-publishers|Independent Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup

## What They Do
The distributors serving independent presses handle sales representation, warehousing, and fulfilment for hundreds of publishers at once, and their analytics groups see every title's account-level performance across the whole client list.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 sales analysts, operations analysts, and data staff
**Output:** Sell-in and sell-through reporting, returns analysis, print quantity recommendations, account performance
**Proprietary data:** Order, shipment, and returns data across hundreds of publishers and thousands of accounts — the comparable-title evidence no individual press has
**Clock:** Seasonal sales conferences and on-sale dates
**Buyer:** VP of Client Services / Head of Analytics
