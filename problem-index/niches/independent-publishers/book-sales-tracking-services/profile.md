# Book Sales Tracking Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-publishers|Independent Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
The syndicated book sales trackers measure point-of-sale movement across retail accounts and sell it back to publishers as the industry's shared scoreboard. Pass 1 notes the consequence for this segment: the subscription cost strains small-press budgets, so many independents fly blind on their own sell-through.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-1,000+ analysts, data engineers, and client service staff
**Output:** Title-level sell-through, category and comparable title analysis, market share reporting
**Proprietary data:** Retailer point-of-sale feeds under long-term contract, plus title and category coding layers
**Clock:** Weekly reporting against a business organized around on-sale dates
**Buyer:** VP of Data Products
