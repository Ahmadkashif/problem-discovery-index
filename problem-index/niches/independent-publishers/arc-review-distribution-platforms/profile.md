# Review Copy Distribution Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-publishers|Independent Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Advance review copy platforms distribute pre-publication books to reviewers, librarians, and booksellers, and collect the requests and feedback that follow — the earliest signal any title generates.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 data, product, and community staff
**Output:** Request and review reporting, reader feedback, campaign analytics for publishers
**Proprietary data:** Pre-publication request volume, reader profile, and review sentiment across essentially every title released — months before any sale
**Clock:** On-sale dates and review lead times
**Buyer:** Head of Product / VP of Data
