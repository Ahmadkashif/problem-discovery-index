# Permissions & Rights Licensing Agencies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-publishers|Independent Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Collective licensing organizations sit between everyone who wants to reuse published content — corporations, universities, other publishers — and the rights holders who control it. The work is determining who actually holds which right in which territory for which use, then licensing it. Getting that determination right is the product; getting it wrong is an infringement claim.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 150-800 rights analysts, metadata specialists, licensing staff, and data engineers
**Output:** Rights determinations, licences and permissions, rights holder resolution, usage reporting and royalty distribution
**Proprietary data:** Rights ownership and permissions metadata across millions of works, assembled over decades from publisher agreements, registrations, and resolved enquiries — a chain-of-title map nobody else holds
**Clock:** Academic terms, corporate compliance cycles, and licensing renewals
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / SVP of Rights Operations
