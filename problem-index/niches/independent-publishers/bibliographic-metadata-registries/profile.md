# Bibliographic Metadata Registries

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-publishers|Independent Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
The bibliographic agencies issue identifiers, maintain the books-in-print register, and operate the metadata pipeline that carries title information from publishers to every retailer and library system. Pass 1 identifies what happens when it is neglected: backlist titles generating 50-70% of revenue sit with stale subject codes and no keyword optimization, invisible in search.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 metadata specialists, taxonomists, and data staff
**Output:** Identifier assignment, bibliographic records, metadata distribution, subject classification schemes
**Proprietary data:** The enriched books-in-print register and publisher-supplied metadata pipeline covering essentially every title published
**Clock:** On-sale dates — metadata must be complete months ahead or a title is invisible at launch
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Head of Publisher Services
