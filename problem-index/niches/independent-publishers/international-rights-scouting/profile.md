# International Rights Scouting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-publishers|Independent Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Scouts are retained by foreign publishers and film producers to tell them what is coming — which manuscripts are moving in the US market, what has sold, what is about to, and what is worth acquiring for a given territory. The report is the entire service.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-25 scouts and researchers per agency
**Output:** Scouting reports, manuscript recommendations, deal intelligence, market briefings
**Proprietary data:** Pre-publication manuscript pipeline intelligence and deal knowledge assembled from relationships, months ahead of anything public
**Clock:** Book fair calendars — Frankfurt, London, Bologna — and submission rounds
**Buyer:** Agency principal
