# Copyright Registration & Policy

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-publishers|Independent Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The national copyright office examines and registers copyright claims, maintains the public record of ownership and transfers, and produces the policy studies that shape how copyright applies to new uses — including machine training.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-600 examiners, attorneys, and policy analysts
**Output:** Registration decisions, the recordation record, policy reports and rulemaking
**Proprietary data:** The authoritative registration and transfer record for US works
**Clock:** Statutory examination timelines and rulemaking schedules
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — federal agency
