# Freelance Editorial & Production Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-publishers|Independent Publishers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Developmental editors, copy editors, designers, and indexers work as individual contractors across many presses, carrying the craft judgment that turns a manuscript into a book.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1-3 people, usually one
**Output:** Editorial assessments, manuscript development, design and production work
**Proprietary data:** A career of judgment about what makes a manuscript work, entirely undocumented
**Clock:** Production schedules tied to on-sale dates
**Buyer:** The freelancer
