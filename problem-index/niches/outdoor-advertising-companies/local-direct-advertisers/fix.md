# Creative Built in Ten Minutes for a Five-Second Read

**Niche:** [[niches/outdoor-advertising-companies/local-direct-advertisers/profile|Local Direct Advertisers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Local boards fail because the creative carries a paragraph, a phone number and four photos for a driver who has a few seconds.
**Tags:** #cnns #large-language-models #diffusion-models #evaluation-metrics #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show a small business that its board brought in customers, and whoever gives the local advertiser credible evidence of response before the renewal conversation keeps the contract.

## The Problem
Local advertisers bring their print ad or their website and want it on the board. Operator art departments turn creative around fast for many accounts, and pushing back on an owner's design risks the sale. The board then underperforms and the advertiser blames the medium.

## Why It's Still Broken
There is no objective check the account executive can show the owner, and no quick way to produce a better alternative for comparison.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A legibility check at upload: word count, type size and contrast scored against the face's read distance and approach speed, with a simulated view from the road. Alongside it, an automatically generated simplified alternative using the advertiser's brand assets, so the owner chooses between their version and one that can be read.

## Who Feels the Pain
Local advertisers paying for boards nobody can read; account executives losing renewals to bad creative; art departments caught between the two.

## Impact If Fixed
More readable boards, better response, and renewals that don't fail on avoidable creative.
