# Call Tracking and Local Search Analytics Pointed at Boards

**Niche:** [[niches/outdoor-advertising-companies/local-direct-advertisers/profile|Local Direct Advertisers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Small businesses already use call tracking and local search dashboards for digital ads; none of them knows a billboard went up on the 1st.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #causal-inference #data-integration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show a small business that its board brought in customers, and whoever gives the local advertiser credible evidence of response before the renewal conversation keeps the contract.

## The Problem
Account executives set up tracking numbers by hand, if at all, and the results sit in a tool the operator doesn't see.

## What Already Exists
Call tracking and marketing analytics platforms such as CallRail and local listings dashboards attribute calls and visits to digital campaigns and keywords for small businesses.

## The Customization Gap
For a board: numbers and short URLs provisioned per face and per creative at contract; flight dates and the face's trade area set automatically as the campaign window and geography; branded search and direction-request lift measured in the trade area against nearby control areas; and reporting written for an owner, with the posting photo and the response side by side. The operator, not the advertiser, has to be able to run it across hundreds of local accounts.

## Target Customer
Local sales leaders at operators; account executives as daily users.

## Impact If Solved
Measurement becomes a standard part of every local contract, not an extra few account executives remember.
