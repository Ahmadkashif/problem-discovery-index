# Response Measurement a Dentist Can Read

**Niche:** [[niches/outdoor-advertising-companies/local-direct-advertisers/profile|Local Direct Advertisers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Local advertisers renew on whether they believe the board worked, and nobody gives them a measurement scaled to one or two faces.
**Tags:** #causal-inference #time-series-forecasting #bayesian-inference #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show a small business that its board brought in customers, and whoever gives the local advertiser credible evidence of response before the renewal conversation keeps the contract.

## The Problem
Attribution studies built for national campaigns rely on large exposed audiences from location panels and cost more than a local advertiser's whole board budget. A dentist with one bulletin can't buy one. The account executive arrives at renewal with a posting photo and an impression figure, against a digital rep with a click report.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The signal for one board is small and noisy, and an honest tool will often say "can't tell". Operators fear that measurement will show some boards don't work. Attribution vendors serve agencies, where the budgets are.

## What to Build
A standard local measurement kit set up at contract signing: a tracked phone number and short URL on the creative; a baseline of the advertiser's branded search, web traffic and, where shared, bookings or sales by week in the face's trade area; and a synthetic-control comparison against similar trade areas without the board. The output is a one-page summary in plain language with an honest confidence statement, delivered a month before renewal, plus guidance on what to change (creative, location, duration) when the signal is weak.

## Target Customer
Operators' local sales organisations; delivered to advertisers by account executives.

## Impact If Built
Renewals are argued on evidence. Boards that work keep their advertisers, and those that don't get fixed rather than cancelled.
