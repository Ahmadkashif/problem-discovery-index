# Local Direct Advertisers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show a small business that its board brought in customers, and whoever gives the local advertiser credible evidence of response before the renewal conversation keeps the contract.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$4B a year of out-of-home spend placed directly by local businesses without an agency (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~40–50% of US out-of-home revenue (estimate; local is ~79% of Lamar's outdoor revenue, lower at operators weighted to national and transit)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — the advertiser buys on feel, creative is often made by the operator's art department in minutes, and results are judged by whether the phone rang
**Target Buyer:** Small-business owner: dentist, personal injury firm, car dealer, restaurant owner, home services contractor
**Automation Potential:** Medium — response measurement, creative checks and renewal alerts automate; the account executive relationship doesn't

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
These advertisers are served by sales but not by tools. Lamar's top categories (service 19%, health care 10%, restaurants 9%, retailers 8%, automotive 8% of billboard revenue) are mostly local businesses with no agency, no analyst and no attribution. Digital media gives the same owner a dashboard of clicks every morning. The board gives them a photo and an impression estimate, and the renewal decision follows that comparison. Agency-led national buyers have planners, attribution studies and audits; the dentist has none of those.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Proof-of-posting photos and Geopath impressions from the operator; call-tracking numbers and vanity URLs where the account executive thought of them; attribution studies sold to national advertisers by specialist agencies such as Billups. Gaps: no affordable response measurement for one or two boards, no creative guidance for five-second reading, and no early warning of a renewal at risk.
