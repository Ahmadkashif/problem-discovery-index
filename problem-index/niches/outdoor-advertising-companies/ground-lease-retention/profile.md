# Ground Lease Retention

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep each ground lease at a rent the face can carry before a lease aggregator or a rival operator reaches the landowner, and whoever knows each site's true value and renewal risk first keeps the location.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.2B a year in ground rent paid to landowners (estimate; Lamar ~$348M, Outfront ~$483M)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% of industry receipts (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — leases in scanned files, renewals worked from a critical-date list and the real estate manager's relationships
**Target Buyer:** VP of Real Estate / real estate managers
**Automation Potential:** High for monitoring and rent calculation; low for the landowner conversation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The counterparty is a landowner: a farmer, a church, a retailer, an estate. The competitors are lease aggregators that offer landowners a lump sum for the rent stream (Landmark Dividend markets valuations of 9–12× annual rent; landowners report offers of 6–7×) and rival operators who approach at expiry. Once a lease is assigned to an aggregator the operator faces a professional landlord at every renewal. Winning means knowing which sites matter, when they are exposed, and what rent each can carry, before someone else makes the landowner an offer.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Property management systems and critical-date lists; land purchase and easement programmes at the large operators. Gaps: no risk score combining revenue, term, landlord type and outside approaches; percentage rents calculated by hand; buyout solicitations seen only when a landlord mentions them.
