# OOH Specialist Planning & Buying Agencies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Agencies that plan, negotiate, buy and audit out-of-home campaigns for advertisers, either independent (Billups describes itself as North America's largest independent out-of-home specialist after acquiring OOH Labs in 2023) or inside holding companies. They choose which operators' faces make the plan, verify posting and increasingly sell attribution. Advertisers pay through fees or commission.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~30-150 planners, analysts, posting auditors and measurement staff per agency (estimate)
**Output:** Market plans, negotiated buys, posting audits, attribution and wrap reports
**Proprietary data:** Cross-operator rate, posting-compliance and campaign history, which is the most complete comparative view of what faces sell for and how operators perform
**Clock:** Campaign start dates and the advertiser's planning calendar
**Buyer:** Chief strategy officer / head of analytics
