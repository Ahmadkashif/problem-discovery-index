# Operator Research & Audience Insights Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Research, audience and analytics teams inside the large consolidated operators (Lamar, Outfront, Clear Channel Outdoor), which also buy independent plants as they come up for sale. They build audience-backed plans, case studies and attribution partnerships for national sales, run planning tools such as Outfront's smartSCOUT, and support acquisition and conversion analysis. Their work supports sales and capital decisions; it isn't sold.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~15-60 per large operator across research, audience analytics and sales planning (estimate)
**Output:** Audience plans, category research, sales collateral, internal conversion and acquisition analysis
**Proprietary data:** Face-level occupancy, rate, proof and play history across tens of thousands of faces, the industry's best location-value training set
**Clock:** Agency RFP windows and the operator's own budget cycle
**Buyer:** Chief marketing officer / head of research
