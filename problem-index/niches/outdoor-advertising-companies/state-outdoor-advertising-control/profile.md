# State Outdoor Advertising Control Programs

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State DOT units that license outdoor advertisers and permit structures along Interstate and federal-aid primary routes, as the Highway Beautification Act requires on pain of losing 10% of federal highway funds. They keep the permit inventory, process renewals (Texas charges $75 a year per permit), inspect for illegal and nonconforming signs, and handle digital display rules. State transportation departments as a whole are a queued industry in this plan (not yet built).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 in any analytical role; the ~10-60 permit, inventory and field staff in a large state are administrative (estimate)
**Output:** Permit decisions, inventory records, enforcement actions, reports to FHWA
**Proprietary data:** The authoritative permit inventory and enforcement history for controlled routes
**Clock:** Annual renewal cycles and federal oversight of effective control
**Buyer:** Outdoor advertising program manager
