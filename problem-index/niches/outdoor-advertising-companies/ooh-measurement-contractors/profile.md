# Audience Measurement Contractors to OOH Bureaus

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Research companies contracted by national out-of-home measurement bureaus to design and run the audience system: travel surveys, device panels, visibility modelling and the reach and frequency engine. Ipsos, which says it has built or supplied out-of-home measurement in more than 20 markets including the UK and Australia, was selected in March 2026 to support the US pilot, with transition from 2027 and full adoption in 2028. The bureau pays under a multi-year contract.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-100 methodologists, survey and panel staff, and modellers per national contract (estimate)
**Output:** The national audience dataset and its methodology, delivered on the bureau's release schedule
**Proprietary data:** Methods and tooling reused across countries; the survey data and audience outputs usually belong to the commissioning bureau
**Clock:** The commissioning bureau's release dates and, in the US, the pilot (second half of 2026), transition (2027) and adoption (2028) timetable set by Geopath and the OAAA; both are the client's schedule, not a third party's
**Buyer:** Global head of audience measurement at the contractor
