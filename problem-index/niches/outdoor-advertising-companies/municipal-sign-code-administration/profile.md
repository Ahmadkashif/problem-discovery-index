# Municipal Sign Code Administration

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
City and county planning departments that write and enforce sign codes, decide conversion and relocation applications, and amortise nonconforming billboards. After City of Austin v. Reagan National Advertising (2022), they may treat off-premises signs differently without strict scrutiny.

## Insight Function
**Size:** A few planners part-time per jurisdiction
**Output:** Code amendments, permit decisions, staff reports
**Proprietary data:** Local sign inventories and permit files
**Clock:** Council and planning commission calendars
**Buyer:** Planning director
