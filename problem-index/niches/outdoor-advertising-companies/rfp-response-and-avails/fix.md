# Availability Promised Twice

**Niche:** [[niches/outdoor-advertising-companies/rfp-response-and-avails/profile|Agency RFP Response & Avails]]
**Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The same premium face goes into a national RFP response and a local proposal in the same week, and one client is disappointed.
**Tags:** #optimization-fundamentals #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn an agency's out-of-home RFP into a costed, audience-weighted plan with maps, photos and confirmed availability inside the agency's response window, and whoever answers fastest with credible audience numbers gets onto the plan.

## The Problem
Holds on premium faces are placed informally, by email or in a field nobody checks, and rarely expire. National planners and local account executives sell from the same inventory. A face promised to an agency is sold locally, or held so long for a plan that never confirms that it sits empty.

## Why It's Still Broken
Holds are seen as a courtesy rather than a system object, and nobody owns the conflict between national and local sales.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Holds as first-class records with an owner, a client, an expiry and a priority rule; automatic release at expiry; conflict alerts when a held face is proposed elsewhere; and a weekly view of faces lost to stale holds.

## Who Feels the Pain
Agency planners who lose faces they were promised; account executives whose deals collapse; operators with premium faces sitting dark.

## Impact If Fixed
A small process change that protects both national and local revenue on the most valuable inventory.
