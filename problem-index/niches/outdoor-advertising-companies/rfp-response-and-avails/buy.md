# Configure-Price-Quote Adapted to Physical Faces

**Niche:** [[niches/outdoor-advertising-companies/rfp-response-and-avails/profile|Agency RFP Response & Avails]]
**Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Quote software already configures and prices complex products; a billboard face is a product that can be sold only once per period and whose value depends on where it stands.
**Tags:** #optimization-fundamentals #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn an agency's out-of-home RFP into a costed, audience-weighted plan with maps, photos and confirmed availability inside the agency's response window, and whoever answers fastest with credible audience numbers gets onto the plan.

## The Problem
Pricing and packaging logic for out-of-home sits in spreadsheets and in senior sellers' heads, so quotes vary by who builds them.

## What Already Exists
Configure-price-quote tools such as Salesforce CPQ handle product configuration, pricing rules, discount approvals and quote documents for complex B2B sales.

## The Customization Gap
Faces need: availability by period and loop slot with holds that expire; pricing by location value, illumination, format and season; production and installation costs bundled per face; audience metrics attached as quote attributes; geographic selection on a map rather than from a catalogue; and output in each agency's template rather than the vendor's.

## Target Customer
Sales operations teams at mid-sized and large operators already on a CRM.

## Impact If Solved
Building a priced quote for an RFP response drops from ~2–3 hours of spreadsheet work to ~20–30 minutes (estimate). For a planner handling ~10–15 responses a week (estimate), that frees ~15–30 hours a week. Rate rules and discount approvals applied by the system narrow the spread between sellers quoting the same faces, which now runs to ~10–20% (estimate).
