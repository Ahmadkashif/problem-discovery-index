# RFP to Costed Plan in an Hour

**Niche:** [[niches/outdoor-advertising-companies/rfp-response-and-avails/profile|Agency RFP Response & Avails]]
**Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Agency RFPs arrive as PDFs and spreadsheets with a short deadline, and a sales planner spends most of a day turning each one into a plan.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #optimization-fundamentals #k-nearest-neighbors #workflow-orchestration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn an agency's out-of-home RFP into a costed, audience-weighted plan with maps, photos and confirmed availability inside the agency's response window, and whoever answers fastest with credible audience numbers gets onto the plan.

## The Problem
An RFP specifies markets, dates, audience targets, formats, budget and sometimes points of interest to be near. The planner reads it, queries availability, selects faces, pulls audience figures, prices the package, builds maps and photo sheets, and formats everything into the agency's template. A planner may handle many RFPs a week, and late responses lose.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Each operator's inventory and rate logic is internal; agency templates vary; and until language models, reading free-form briefs reliably was not possible.

## What to Build
An RFP agent that extracts the brief into structured requirements, selects available faces that best meet audience, geography and proximity targets within budget, places temporary holds, prices with the operator's rate rules, generates maps, photo sheets and audience tables, and fills the agency's template. The planner reviews and sends, and win or loss data trains the selection.

## Target Customer
National sales and sales operations leaders at operators that answer hundreds of RFPs a year.

## Impact If Built
Responses go out in an hour rather than a day, more RFPs get answered, and planners spend time on strategy and negotiation.
