# Agency RFP Response & Avails

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn an agency's out-of-home RFP into a costed, audience-weighted plan with maps, photos and confirmed availability inside the agency's response window, and whoever answers fastest with credible audience numbers gets onto the plan.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.5B a year of agency-placed national and regional out-of-home spend competed through RFPs (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~25% of US out-of-home revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — inventory, audience and map data are digital, but responses are assembled by hand into spreadsheets and decks
**Target Buyer:** VP of National Sales / head of sales operations
**Automation Potential:** High — matching inventory to a brief, pulling audience data, pricing and formatting are repeatable steps

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
National advertisers buy out-of-home through agencies and specialist out-of-home agencies, which send RFPs to every operator in the target markets with a short response window. Each response is the same task with new inputs: find available faces that fit the brief's geography and audience, attach Geopath impressions and demographics, photos and maps, price them, and return a file in the agency's template. A late or thin response doesn't make the plan. Seventy percent of the top 100 out-of-home advertisers increased spending in 2025, and most of that money is placed through responses like these. Independent reps who answer RFPs for many owners belong to [[industries/media-rep-firms|Media Representative Firms]], whose [[niches/media-rep-firms/avails-to-order-workflow/profile|Avails, Proposals & Order Exchange]] niche is the broadcast version of this flow; this niche is the outdoor operator's own sales operations.

## Current Tools & Gaps
CRM systems, internal inventory and availability tools, Geopath data, planning tools such as Outfront's smartSCOUT, and agency-side planning platforms. Gaps: briefs read by hand, availability holds tracked loosely, audience numbers pulled face by face, and every agency's template rebuilt each time.
