# Transit & Airport Ad Concession Advisors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Consultants who help airports and transit agencies design advertising concession solicitations (guarantee levels, revenue shares, package structure and term), evaluate bids and benchmark concession performance. Airport and transit operators are queued industries in this plan (not yet built).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 2-10 in the advertising specialty
**Output:** Solicitation design, revenue benchmarks, bid evaluations
**Proprietary data:** Concession terms and performance across past engagements
**Clock:** Solicitation and award schedules
**Buyer:** Principal
