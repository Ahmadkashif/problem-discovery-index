# Retail Site Selection Analytics Turned Toward the Roadway

**Niche:** [[niches/outdoor-advertising-companies/digital-billboard-conversions/profile|Digital Billboard Conversions]]
**Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Retail chains already score candidate store sites from traffic, demographics and visit data; a billboard is a site too, but what it sells is readable time, not visits.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #feature-engineering #k-nearest-neighbors #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to pick the static faces whose conversion multiplies revenue rather than adding a little, and that will still clear the permit, and whoever predicts post-conversion yield before the ~$200,000 is spent wins the capital budget.

## The Problem
Real estate teams assemble site evidence by hand for each conversion memo: traffic counts from the state DOT, a Geopath figure, a map of competitors, photos. The evidence differs memo to memo and can't be compared across markets.

## What Already Exists
Retail and restaurant site-selection platforms such as Esri Business Analyst, Buxton and Placer.ai combine demographics, traffic, mobile-visit data and analogue stores to forecast sales at a candidate location.

## The Customization Gap
A billboard's value depends on things retail models never measure: the face's angle to the roadway, read side, approach distance and speed, obstruction by trees or other structures, digital dwell rules and loop length, competing faces on the same corridor, and which advertiser categories buy that stretch of road. The analogue set has to be the operator's own faces, with revenue history rather than store sales, and the output has to respect permit eligibility and spacing rules before ranking anything. Visit data is useful as one input (trade-area demand for local advertisers), not as the target.

## Target Customer
Real estate analysts and VPs of digital at operators that already license traffic or location data for sales and want it used for capital decisions.

## Impact If Solved
Every conversion memo rests on the same evidence, comparable across markets, at a fraction of the analyst time now spent assembling it.
