# Digital Billboard Conversions

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to pick the static faces whose conversion multiplies revenue rather than adding a little, and that will still clear the permit, and whoever predicts post-conversion yield before the ~$200,000 is spent wins the capital budget.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$500M a year in conversion capital, displays, structure work and digital network software (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of industry receipts reinvested in conversions each year (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High for the product, low for the decision — the face becomes a screen, but the choice of which face is a spreadsheet and a drive-by
**Target Buyer:** Regional VP of Real Estate / VP of Digital
**Automation Potential:** High for candidate ranking and uplift forecasting; low for permitting and construction

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Conversion is where the industry's growth comes from. Lamar's ~5,500 digital billboards, about 3.5% of its faces, produced roughly a third of billboard revenue in 2025, and it added about 500 that year. A 14×48 conversion costs a little over $200,000 plus structure work, and operators target payback inside four years. The decision is constrained by permits (many codes forbid conversion outright, and Austin's refusal to let existing off-premises signs go digital was upheld in 2022) and by the market itself: each new digital face takes some demand from the operator's other digital faces nearby.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Display manufacturers (Daktronics, Watchfire, Formetco) supply ROI calculators and financing; Geopath supplies impressions; real estate teams model payback in spreadsheets. The gaps: no uplift model trained on the operator's own past conversions, no estimate of cannibalisation within the market's digital inventory, and no record of the forecast each conversion was approved on, so nobody learns which kinds of site converted well.
