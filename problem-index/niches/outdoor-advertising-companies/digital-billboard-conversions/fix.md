# Conversions Approved on a Forecast Nobody Checks

**Niche:** [[niches/outdoor-advertising-companies/digital-billboard-conversions/profile|Digital Billboard Conversions]]
**Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Each conversion is approved on a revenue forecast in a spreadsheet, and two years later nobody compares that forecast with what the face earned.
**Tags:** #evaluation-metrics #descriptive-statistics #hypothesis-testing #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to pick the static faces whose conversion multiplies revenue rather than adding a little, and that will still clear the permit, and whoever predicts post-conversion yield before the ~$200,000 is spent wins the capital budget.

## The Problem
The approval memo for a conversion carries a forecast: a revenue multiple, a sell-through assumption, a payback period. The memo is filed. The face goes live, and its revenue is reported with every other digital face. Whether that market manager's forecasts run hot or cold, and which kinds of site beat or miss, is never measured.

## Why It's Still Broken
The forecast lives in a document, the outcome in billing, and no system joins them. A post-audit looks like an exercise in blame, so nobody asks for one.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Register every conversion forecast as structured data at approval: face, date, forecast revenue by year, key assumptions, who signed. Report actual against forecast automatically at 6, 12 and 24 months, by manager and by site type, framed as calibration rather than grading. Feed the record into the next round of approvals.

## Who Feels the Pain
CFOs allocating conversion capital; regional VPs defending budgets; market managers whose good calls go as unrecognised as their bad ones.

## Impact If Fixed
A low-cost change that turns hundreds of past conversions into a feedback loop. It is also the training set any uplift model will need.
