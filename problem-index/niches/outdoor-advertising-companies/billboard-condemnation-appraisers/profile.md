# Billboard Valuation & Condemnation Appraisers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Appraisers and expert witnesses who value billboard structures, leases and permits when a highway project or redevelopment takes them, using cost, income and market approaches that states treat differently (the OAAA's 2019 condemnation brief contrasts Oklahoma and Pennsylvania). Operators, condemning agencies and landowners hire them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 2-15 per firm
**Output:** Appraisal reports and expert testimony
**Proprietary data:** Files of past billboard valuations and settlements
**Clock:** Condemnation proceedings and trial dates
**Buyer:** Principal appraiser
