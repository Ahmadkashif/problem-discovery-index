# Billboard Structural Engineering & Inspection Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Engineering firms that design billboard structures, certify wind loads, assess structures for digital conversion (conversion work runs about $5,000–$50,000) and inspect after storms.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 2-20
**Output:** Stamped designs, inspection reports, conversion assessments
**Proprietary data:** Inspection files on the structures they have assessed
**Clock:** Permit applications and post-storm inspections
**Buyer:** Principal engineer
