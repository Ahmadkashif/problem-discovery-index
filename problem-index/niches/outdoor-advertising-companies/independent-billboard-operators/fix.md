# Inventory Data Too Dirty to List

**Niche:** [[niches/outdoor-advertising-companies/independent-billboard-operators/profile|Independent Billboard Operators]]
**Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A small plant's faces carry wrong coordinates, missing facing directions and ten-year-old photos, so measurement and national buyers pass them by.
**Tags:** #cnns #k-nearest-neighbors #data-integration #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make a small operator's faces show up, measured and bookable, in the systems where agencies and programmatic buyers spend, and whoever makes a 50-to-2,000-face plant buyable without back-office staff takes the account.

## The Problem
Inventory records were typed in when each structure was built or acquired: an address rather than coordinates, "north" for a face that reads to the north-east, a photo from before the trees grew. Audience measurement depends on exact location and facing; agency planners filter out faces without current photos; a programmatic platform rejects listings with missing fields.

## Why It's Still Broken
Cleaning inventory is tedious, and the owner who knows every face by sight sees no need until a national buy goes elsewhere.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A guided clean-up: coordinates and facing inferred from street-level imagery and the crew's next proof photo, attributes filled from photos (size, illumination, format), flagged faces queued for a crew visit, and a completeness score per face that shows what is blocking listing or measurement.

## Who Feels the Pain
Owners losing national and programmatic dollars; planners who skip unmeasured faces; buyers of plants who must clean the data at acquisition.

## Impact If Fixed
A one-time clean-up, then maintained by crew photos, makes the whole plant listable and measurable.
