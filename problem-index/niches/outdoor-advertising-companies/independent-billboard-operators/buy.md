# Billboard Packages Connected to Programmatic and Measurement

**Niche:** [[niches/outdoor-advertising-companies/independent-billboard-operators/profile|Independent Billboard Operators]]
**Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Independents already run their plants on a billboard package; the package stops at the operator's own sales team.
**Tags:** #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #k-nearest-neighbors #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make a small operator's faces show up, measured and bookable, in the systems where agencies and programmatic buyers spend, and whoever makes a 50-to-2,000-face plant buyable without back-office staff takes the account.

## The Problem
An independent's faces are invisible to agency planning tools and programmatic buyers unless someone exports, cleans and uploads inventory by hand, and keeps availability current.

## What Already Exists
Apparatix, BillboardPlanet, SignDash and HighSign Pro cover proposals, contracts, posting, proof, billing and leases for small operators; programmatic supply platforms such as Vistar Media and Broadsign accept digital inventory; Geopath measures faces for members.

## The Customization Gap
The package needs: one-click listing of clean inventory (coordinates, facing, size, illumination, photos) to agency marketplaces and programmatic platforms; live availability sync so a face sold locally drops out of national listings; Geopath submission and audience import; programmatic creative approval routed into the operator's own copy rules; and settlement reconciliation of programmatic revenue against plays.

## Target Customer
Independents with a handful of digital faces and statics they want in national plans.

## Impact If Solved
National and programmatic demand reaches small plants without a back-office hire.
