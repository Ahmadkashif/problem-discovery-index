# Pooled Yield Benchmarks for Plants Too Small to Learn Alone

**Niche:** [[niches/outdoor-advertising-companies/independent-billboard-operators/profile|Independent Billboard Operators]]
**Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** An independent with 300 faces can't tell whether its rates are low, its occupancy is normal or a conversion would pay, because it has nothing to compare against.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #k-nearest-neighbors #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make a small operator's faces show up, measured and bookable, in the systems where agencies and programmatic buyers spend, and whoever makes a 50-to-2,000-face plant buyable without back-office staff takes the account.

## The Problem
Owners price faces from memory and from what the local competitor is rumoured to charge. They decide on conversions and lease renewals with no view of how similar faces perform elsewhere. When a buyer comes to acquire the plant, the buyer knows more about its value than the owner does.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
No single independent has enough faces to model location value, and they don't share data with each other. The large operators who could build such models keep them private.

## What to Build
An opt-in, anonymised pool of face-level rate, occupancy, format and site attributes from independents on a shared software platform, producing benchmarks by market and format, a location-value estimate for each face trained on the pooled history, and conversion and renewal guidance. Contributors see aggregates and their own position, never another operator's data.

## Target Customer
Owners of independent plants; billboard software vendors as the distribution channel.

## Impact If Built
Small operators price, convert and renew with evidence, and negotiate a sale from knowledge rather than from the buyer's spreadsheet.
