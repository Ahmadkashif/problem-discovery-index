# OOH Attribution & Lift Study Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms that estimate what an out-of-home campaign caused: store visits, web traffic, app installs, brand lift. They model which devices in a location panel passed the faces and compare them with unexposed controls. Specialist agencies such as Billups sell these studies (it offers five types and says it cannot measure exact exposed devices), as do location-data vendors and survey firms. Advertisers, agencies and sometimes the operators themselves pay, often to justify a renewal.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-120 analysts and data scientists per provider (estimate)
**Output:** Post-campaign attribution and lift reports
**Proprietary data:** An archive of past studies with exposure and outcome; device data is licensed
**Clock:** Campaign flight end and the advertiser's planning calendar
**Buyer:** Head of measurement / VP of analytics
