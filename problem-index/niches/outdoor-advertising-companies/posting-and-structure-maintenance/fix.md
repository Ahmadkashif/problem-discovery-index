# Proof Photos That Say Nothing About the Structure

**Niche:** [[niches/outdoor-advertising-companies/posting-and-structure-maintenance/profile|Static Posting & Structure Maintenance]]
**Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Crews photograph every face they post, and the photos are used only to prove the ad went up, not to see whether the structure is failing.
**Tags:** #cnns #object-detection #semantic-segmentation #worker-facing #compliance #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to post every contracted face by its start date with proof the buyer accepts, because each late or wrong posting becomes a makegood that wipes out that face's margin for the period, and whoever posts on time with zero rejected proofs keeps the renewal.

## The Problem
Proof photos are framed for the advertiser: the face, straight on. They are checked, if at all, by someone in the office days later. Structure condition (rust at the base, a missing guardrail section, a cracked ladder rung, lamps out, trees encroaching on the sightline) is reported only when a crew member thinks to call it in, and structural inspections happen on a slow cycle or after storms.

## Why It's Still Broken
Proof and maintenance belong to different processes and different managers. Nobody asked crews for a second photo, and nobody would have had time to look at it.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Add one structure shot to each posting visit and screen both photos automatically: the face shot for right creative and right face, the structure shot for defect classes, with safety-critical findings routed to maintenance the same day and kept on the structure's record for the next crew.

## Who Feels the Pain
Billposters who climb structures they worry about; operations managers who find defects after a storm; agencies that reject proofs days late.

## Impact If Fixed
Hazard and condition data comes in from visits the company already pays for, and wrong postings are caught while the crew is still at the site.
