# Field Service Software Adapted for Posting Crews

**Niche:** [[niches/outdoor-advertising-companies/posting-and-structure-maintenance/profile|Static Posting & Structure Maintenance]]
**Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Field service platforms already dispatch technicians, capture photos and close work orders; none knows what a face, a vinyl or a proof of posting is.
**Tags:** #optimization-fundamentals #cnns #data-integration #worker-facing #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to post every contracted face by its start date with proof the buyer accepts, because each late or wrong posting becomes a makegood that wipes out that face's margin for the period, and whoever posts on time with zero rejected proofs keeps the renewal.

## The Problem
Operators run posting and maintenance as separate paper or spreadsheet processes, and crews juggle a route sheet, a camera roll and texts from the office.

## What Already Exists
Field service management platforms such as Salesforce Field Service, ServiceTitan and Jobber handle dispatch, mobile work orders, photo capture and job history for trades.

## The Customization Gap
Posting needs: work orders generated from contract start dates per face; vinyl and paper inventory matched to the work order; face IDs and facing directions so the photo proves the right face; proof-of-posting packages formatted for agencies; structure records with ladder, catwalk and lighting attributes; hazard flags that persist between crews; and wind limits by structure type. Pricing has to suit crews posting dozens of faces a day, not technicians closing a few jobs.

## Target Customer
Operations managers at mid-sized operators outgrowing their billboard package's posting module.

## Impact If Solved
Crews stop transcribing route sheets and bulk-uploading photos at the end of the day, saving ~30–45 minutes per crew per day (estimate). Proof-of-posting packages reach agencies the same day instead of ~3–5 days later (estimate), which pulls invoicing forward by the same margin. Maintenance requests a crew raises on site reach the work-order queue within the hour rather than the next time someone in the office reads the notes.
