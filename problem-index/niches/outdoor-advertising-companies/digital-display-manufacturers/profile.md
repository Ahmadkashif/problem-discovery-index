# Digital Billboard Display Manufacturers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Manufacturers of LED billboard displays and controllers, such as Daktronics, Watchfire and Formetco, which sell conversions to operators, sometimes with financing, and monitor installed displays remotely.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 in market research and application analysis for billboards; engineering and service teams are large but produce hardware, not insight
**Output:** Displays, ROI calculators and financing offers as sales support
**Proprietary data:** Remote diagnostics on installed displays (pixel, power and uptime data)
**Clock:** Operators' capital budget cycles
**Buyer:** VP of sales
