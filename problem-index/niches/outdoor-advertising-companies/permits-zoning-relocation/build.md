# An Entitlement Map From Every Sign Code

**Niche:** [[niches/outdoor-advertising-companies/permits-zoning-relocation/profile|Permits, Zoning & Relocation Rights]]
**Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** What an operator may build, convert or relocate is written in thousands of local codes that nobody has turned into a map.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #bert #feature-engineering #compliance #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep, convert and relocate structures under sign codes that forbid new ones, and whoever reads the codes and negotiates relocation and cap-and-replace terms best grows its digital footprint while rivals stand still.

## The Problem
For each structure the questions are concrete: may it be converted to digital, at what dwell and brightness; may it be rebuilt after storm damage; may it be relocated within the city if a project takes it; would the city trade a digital permit for removing statics elsewhere. The answers sit in municipal codes, state rules, past variances and council minutes, and senior real estate managers carry most of them in their heads.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Codes are long, inconsistent and amended often; parsing them took lawyers. The market is a few dozen operators of meaningful size, too small for legal publishers to build for.

## What to Build
A code-reading pipeline that extracts sign-specific provisions (bans, spacing, height, digital permissions, dwell and brightness, nonconforming rules, relocation and cap-and-replace provisions) into structured rules per jurisdiction, joined to the operator's structure inventory and GIS layers. Output: eligibility by face and parcel, an opportunity list ranked by expected value, and alerts when an agenda item proposes an amendment.

## Target Customer
Real estate and legal leadership at operators pursuing digital growth in code-restricted markets.

## Impact If Built
Entitlement opportunities are found systematically rather than by the manager who happened to read the code, and amendments are contested at the hearing rather than discovered afterwards.
