# Digital Loop Scheduling & Copy Approval

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to fill every slot of every digital loop at the best yield across direct and programmatic demand without breaking a dwell, proximity, lease or exclusivity rule, and whoever automates compliant scheduling takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3.4B of 2025 digital out-of-home revenue scheduled through loops (36.3% of ~$9.46B), with a tooling and ad operations spend of a few percent of that (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~36% of US out-of-home revenue runs through this function
**Digital Adoption:** High — the faces, players and content systems are all digital; the rules checks are still manual
**Target Buyer:** VP of Digital Operations / head of ad operations
**Automation Potential:** High — scheduling, rule checks and creative classification are mechanical and repeat thousands of times a week

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Digital out-of-home grew 10.5% in 2025 to 36.3% of US revenue. Each digital face is a loop of slots, sold partly by direct contract and increasingly through programmatic platforms such as Vistar Media, now owned by T-Mobile. Every creative must fit the slot and satisfy the face's rules: state display limits such as an eight-second minimum dwell, proximity bans such as Washington's 1,000-foot cannabis rule, lease restrictions, political disclosure and sold exclusivity. The work is high-volume and rule-bound, with a hard clock (the creative has to be live at flight start), which makes it the most automatable function in the industry.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Broadsign Control and similar content management systems schedule loops and log plays; supply platforms handle programmatic deals and creative queues; operators keep copy policies in documents. Gaps: no face-level rules registry, no automated creative classification, and no yield-aware scheduler that balances direct guarantees against programmatic floors.
