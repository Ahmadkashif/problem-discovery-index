# Content Management With a Compliance Layer

**Niche:** [[niches/outdoor-advertising-companies/digital-loop-operations/profile|Digital Loop Scheduling & Copy Approval]]
**Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Digital signage content systems already schedule and prove plays; they check a file's format, not whether its content is legal on this face.
**Tags:** #cnns #large-language-models #data-integration #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to fill every slot of every digital loop at the best yield across direct and programmatic demand without breaking a dwell, proximity, lease or exclusivity rule, and whoever automates compliant scheduling takes the account.

## The Problem
Operators run content management systems that do scheduling and proof of play well, and manage compliance in spreadsheets beside them.

## What Already Exists
Broadsign Control and comparable digital out-of-home content management systems handle loop scheduling, player management, play logs and proof-of-play reporting; supply platforms run creative approval for programmatic buys.

## The Customization Gap
The system needs: per-face rule attributes (dwell, brightness schedule, transitions, sensitive-site buffers, lease category bans); category and disclosure tags on every creative, assigned by a classifier rather than typed; exclusivity and separation constraints in the scheduler; blocks that apply equally to direct and programmatic creatives; and an audit view showing why each creative was allowed on each face.

## Target Customer
Digital operations leads already licensing a content management system.

## Impact If Solved
Compliance moves inside the system that schedules, and spreadsheets stop being the control.
