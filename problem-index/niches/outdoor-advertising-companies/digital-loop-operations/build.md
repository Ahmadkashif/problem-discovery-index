# A Face-Level Rules Registry Scheduling Can Enforce

**Niche:** [[niches/outdoor-advertising-companies/digital-loop-operations/profile|Digital Loop Scheduling & Copy Approval]]
**Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** The rules that decide whether an ad may run on a given face are spread across codes, leases and contracts, and no system holds them where scheduling can use them.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #cnns #optimization-fundamentals #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to fill every slot of every digital loop at the best yield across direct and programmatic demand without breaking a dwell, proximity, lease or exclusivity rule, and whoever automates compliant scheduling takes the account.

## The Problem
Ad operations staff decide, creative by creative, whether something may run on a face: is it near a school, does the lease bar the category, is a competitor holding exclusivity in this loop, does the political ad carry the right disclosure, is the dwell setting legal here. They decide from memory and lookups, and the volume rises as programmatic demand arrives with shorter notice.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Content systems were built to play files, supply platforms to clear auctions. The rules come from real estate, legal and sales, three departments that don't share a system.

## What to Build
A registry holding, for each digital face, its state and local display rules, sensitive-site buffers computed from GIS, lease use restrictions, sold exclusivities and separations, and political disclosure requirements. A creative classifier assigns category, claims and disclosures, and a scheduler fills loops by yield subject to every rule. Each decision is logged with the rule that drove it.

## Target Customer
Heads of digital operations at operators with hundreds of digital faces and growing programmatic share.

## Impact If Built
Compliant scheduling at programmatic speed, fewer rule breaches and makegoods, and ad operations time spent on genuine judgement calls.
