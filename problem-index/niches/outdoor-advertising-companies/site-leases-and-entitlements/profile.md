# Site Leases & Entitlements

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep every permitted location in the portfolio, but that splits into two contests with different counterparties and different winners: against landowners and lease aggregators over rent and tenure, and against cities and state DOTs over the right to keep, convert or relocate a structure. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.3B a year in ground rent, permit fees, land-use counsel and lease administration (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of industry receipts (estimate; ground rent is ~17% of billboard revenue at Lamar and 34% at Outfront)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — scanned leases, PDF permits, a property management system and the real estate manager's memory
**Target Buyer:** VP of Real Estate
**Automation Potential:** High for administration and monitoring; low for negotiation with landowners and cities

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The location is the asset. Lamar holds ~71,500 leased sites and ~11,200 owned parcels; Outfront ~19,600 leases with ~17,900 landlords. Each structure stands on a lease and on permits, and in many cities a lost permit can't be replaced because new billboards are no longer allowed. Lamar's 10-K lists permit, fee and condemnation proceedings and the amortisation of nonconforming signs among its routine risks. The work is paper-heavy and judgement-heavy, and it is where the industry's scarcity value is either kept or lost.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Large operators run internal property management systems; independents use the lease modules in Apparatix, BillboardPlanet or HighSign Pro, or Excel. State DOT permit lists and land-use counsel cover the regulatory side. The common gap is the joined record: lease, state permit, local permit, nonconforming status and the face's revenue in one place. Without it, neither the landowner side nor the city side can be managed by risk.
