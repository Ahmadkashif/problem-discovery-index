# Lease Administration Platforms Taught That Permits Matter

**Niche:** [[niches/outdoor-advertising-companies/site-leases-and-entitlements/profile|Site Leases & Entitlements]]
**Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Lease administration software tracks critical dates and payments well; it treats a billboard lease as a standalone tenancy when its value depends on permits attached to it.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep every permitted location in the portfolio, but that splits into two contests with different counterparties and different winners: against landowners and lease aggregators over rent and tenure, and against cities and state DOTs over the right to keep, convert or relocate a structure. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Operators run tens of thousands of small leases with critical dates, escalators and percentage rents, and the volume alone defeats manual tracking.

## What Already Exists
Visual Lease, CoStar Real Estate Manager, LeaseQuery and Yardi handle lease abstraction, critical dates, payments and lease accounting for corporate tenants.

## The Customization Gap
Billboard leases need: a link from each lease to the structure's state permit tag, local permit and nonconforming status; percentage-rent calculation from face revenue; use restrictions turned into rules the sales system enforces; easement and access rights for crews; sale and assignment notice tracking; and very high volumes of low-value leases, which breaks per-lease pricing built for office portfolios.

## Target Customer
Real estate administration leads at mid-sized and large operators.

## Impact If Solved
Percentage-rent statements, which take an administrator ~1–2 hours per lease per quarter to build from billing exports (estimate), are generated automatically. Across ~2,000 percentage-rent leases at a large operator (estimate), that is ~8,000–16,000 administrator hours a year returned. Permit renewals sit on the same record as the lease, so late fees ($100 per late permit in Texas) stop, and so do lapses that can cost a nonconforming structure its permit.
