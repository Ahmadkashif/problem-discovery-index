# DOOH Programmatic Supply Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Platforms that make digital out-of-home screens buyable through programmatic auctions and deals: Vistar Media (bought by T-Mobile in February 2025 for $621M), Broadsign and others. They connect operators' digital faces to buyers' demand-side platforms, convert plays into impressions using audience data, and take a share of each transaction. The wider programmatic stack is covered in [[industries/programmatic-ad-platforms|Programmatic Ad Platforms]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~50-300 data scientists, yield analysts and marketplace engineers per platform (estimate)
**Output:** Floor pricing and yield optimisation, impression multipliers per play, demand analytics for operators
**Proprietary data:** Bid, clearing and play-log data across many operators' screens, which shows what each screen and daypart is worth to programmatic buyers
**Clock:** Real-time auctions and monthly settlement
**Buyer:** VP of data science / chief revenue officer
