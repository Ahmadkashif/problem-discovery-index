# Billboard Ground Lease Aggregators & Lenders

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Investment firms that pay landowners a lump sum for the right to collect billboard (and cell tower) ground rent, and in some cases lend to operators. Landmark Dividend markets valuations of 9–12× annual rent to landowners, who report offers of 6–7×, and offers operators loans on 15–40-year terms. They earn from rent escalation, renewal leverage and portfolio sales.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~15-60 underwriting, origination analytics and asset management staff per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Purchase underwriting and portfolio valuation, for their own investment rather than for sale
**Proprietary data:** Lease-level rents, escalators, terms and landowner acceptance history across thousands of sites
**Clock:** Lease expiries and fund deployment targets
**Buyer:** Chief investment officer
