# OOH Audience Measurement Bureau

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Geopath, founded in 1933 as the Traffic Audit Bureau, is the industry-owned body that measures US out-of-home audiences. A tripartite board of advertisers, agencies and media companies governs it, members' dues fund it, and its impressions are the currency faces are planned and sold on. It reported revenue of $15.0M and expenses of $16.05M in 2024, with negative net assets. With the OAAA it selected Ipsos in March 2026 to pilot a replacement system.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-20 research, data and methodology staff (Geopath had 26 employees in total in 2022)
**Output:** Annual planning dataset of audited inventory with impressions, reach, frequency and audience composition
**Proprietary data:** The registry of measured US frames with audited locations and the audience model outputs; circulation and device inputs are licensed from Teralytics and Motionworks
**Clock:** The annual dataset release and the 2026–2028 transition, both set by its own board with the OAAA; no outside party imposes either
**Buyer:** President / chief research officer
