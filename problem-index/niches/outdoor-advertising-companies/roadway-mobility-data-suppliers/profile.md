# Roadway Mobility & Traffic Data Suppliers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms that turn connected-vehicle, app and network location feeds into road-segment traffic volumes, speeds and origin-destination flows. Teralytics supplies the roadway circulation data behind Geopath's audience model; others in the category sell mainly to state DOTs, planners and retailers. Out-of-home is one buyer among several, and usually reaches this data through the measurement bureau rather than directly.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~30-200 data scientists, transport modellers and analysts per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Segment-level volume and speed estimates, origin-destination matrices, annual or quarterly data releases
**Proprietary data:** Calibrated models that convert sampled device and vehicle traces into total traffic, plus years of segment history; the raw feeds are licensed from app and vehicle data providers
**Clock:** Customer planning cycles and the measurement bureau's annual dataset release; no hard external deadline
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Product
