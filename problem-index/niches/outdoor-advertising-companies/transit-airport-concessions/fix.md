# Screen Deployment Costs Nobody Tracks to Recoupment

**Niche:** [[niches/outdoor-advertising-companies/transit-airport-concessions/profile|Transit, Airport & Street Furniture Concessions]]
**Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Concessions oblige the operator to install screens that it may recoup only from revenue it never reaches, and the recoupment position is rarely visible until it is written off.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #time-series-forecasting #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to bid a minimum annual guarantee and revenue share that the concession's advertising will actually cover over a seven-to-ten-year term, and whoever forecasts concession revenue most honestly wins contracts without the write-downs that follow an overbid.

## The Problem
Large transit and airport concessions require the operator to fund digital screens, kiosks and installation, with recovery from revenue above the guarantee or through deductions defined in the contract. Outfront substantially completed its initial MTA deployment in 2024 and said it expected annual deployment spending to fall. Whether those costs come back depends on revenue paths that are uncertain, and the position is tracked in finance schedules, not in the operating view sales and network teams use.

## Why It's Still Broken
Deployment is managed by network operations, revenue by sales, and recoupment by finance under contract terms that only legal fully reads. Nobody owns the joined view per asset.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A per-asset ledger linking each screen's installed cost, its revenue, the contract's recoupment terms and the remaining unrecouped balance, with a forecast of recoupment under current sell-through. Assets unlikely to recoup are flagged early, while the decision to keep installing, to renegotiate or to redeploy is still open.

## Who Feels the Pain
CFOs facing impairment tests; transit division heads defending budgets; the authority, when its concessionaire's economics break mid-term.

## Impact If Fixed
Recoupment risk becomes visible years before an impairment, when it can still change a deployment schedule or a renegotiation.
