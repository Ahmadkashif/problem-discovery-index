# Planning Software Taught Guarantee-or-Percentage Rent

**Niche:** [[niches/outdoor-advertising-companies/transit-airport-concessions/profile|Transit, Airport & Street Furniture Concessions]]
**Industry:** [[industries/outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Corporate planning platforms already run multi-year scenarios; none of them knows how a guarantee, a percentage rent and a deployment obligation interact in a concession contract.
**Tags:** #monte-carlo-methods #time-series-forecasting #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to bid a minimum annual guarantee and revenue share that the concession's advertising will actually cover over a seven-to-ten-year term, and whoever forecasts concession revenue most honestly wins contracts without the write-downs that follow an overbid.

## The Problem
Finance teams model each concession in a separate workbook with its own logic for guarantees, escalators and revenue shares. Portfolio-level exposure to guaranteed payments is assembled by hand at quarter-end.

## What Already Exists
Enterprise planning platforms such as Anaplan, Workday Adaptive Planning and Oracle EPM handle driver-based forecasts, scenarios and consolidation.

## The Customization Gap
Concession contracts need: greater-of logic between a guarantee and tiered percentage rent; CPI escalators on the guarantee (the MTA guarantee steps up about 3% in 2026 to ~$161M); deferred guarantee balances from pandemic amendments; equipment deployment obligations recouped only from revenue above set levels; ridership- or traffic-linked guarantee formulas that newer solicitations use; and portfolio views of guaranteed exposure by year against simulated revenue. The drivers have to be riders, passengers and screen sell-through, not headcount and units.

## Target Customer
FP&A leads at operators with transit, airport or street furniture concessions.

## Impact If Solved
One consistent model of guaranteed exposure across every concession, refreshed monthly rather than rebuilt for each bid and each audit.
