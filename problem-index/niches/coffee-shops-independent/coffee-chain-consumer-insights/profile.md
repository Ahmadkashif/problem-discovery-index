# Coffee Chain Consumer Insights

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/coffee-shops-independent|Independent Coffee Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The consumer insights and analytics groups at the large coffee chains run menu testing, loyalty analytics, and site selection at a scale that shapes category expectations — and therefore what independents are compared against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 consumer researchers, data scientists, and analysts
**Output:** Menu and pricing research, loyalty analytics, site selection models
**Proprietary data:** Loyalty and transaction data across tens of millions of customers
**Clock:** Product launch and seasonal calendars, internally set
**Buyer:** VP of Consumer Insights
