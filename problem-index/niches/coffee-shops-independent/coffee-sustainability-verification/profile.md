# Coffee Origin Verification & Traceability Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/coffee-shops-independent|Independent Coffee Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Enveritas and comparable verification services send field surveyors to statistically sampled coffee farms across origin countries, collect structured data on farming practice, deforestation, labour conditions, and income, and sell roasters a verified picture of their supply base. Roasters buy it because they must make sourcing claims they can defend, and increasingly because EU deforestation rules require documented due diligence on every consignment.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 field surveyors, data scientists, agronomists, and analysts
**Output:** Verification reports and origin risk assessments — the billable deliverable
**Proprietary data:** Farm-level survey data across coffee origins, collected under statistical sampling frames that no government, certifier, or roaster maintains
**Clock:** EU Deforestation Regulation compliance deadlines and roasters' annual sustainability reporting cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Research Officer / VP of Verification Services
