# POS & Payment Transaction Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/coffee-shops-independent|Independent Coffee Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The data teams at Square, Toast, and Clover analyze transaction data flowing through hundreds of thousands of small hospitality businesses, build in-product benchmarking, and publish the industry trend reporting that shapes how the sector understands itself. Independent coffee shops are among the densest users of these platforms.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 data scientists, economists, and analysts
**Output:** In-product benchmarks, published trend reporting, merchant risk and lending analytics
**Proprietary data:** Item-level transaction data across hundreds of thousands of small hospitality merchants, in near real time — the only view of independent hospitality performance that exists at scale
**Clock:** Continuous; reporting cadence is self-set
**Buyer:** VP of Data Science / Head of Economic Research
