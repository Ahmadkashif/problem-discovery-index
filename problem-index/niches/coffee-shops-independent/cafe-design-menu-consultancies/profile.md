# Café Concept & Menu Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/coffee-shops-independent|Independent Coffee Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Boutique consultants who design café concepts, build menus and pricing, and advise on equipment and workflow for opening operators. The written concept and menu plan is the deliverable.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1-6
**Output:** Concept documents, menu and pricing plans, workflow designs
**Proprietary data:** The consultant's accumulated project experience
**Clock:** Opening dates
**Buyer:** Principal
