# Small Hospitality Lending Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/coffee-shops-independent|Independent Coffee Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The credit analytics groups at merchant cash advance providers, platform lenders, and SBA-focused banks that finance independent cafés. They underwrite from transaction flow rather than from financial statements, and their models decide which independents can fund an espresso machine or a second location.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 credit analysts, modellers, and portfolio staff
**Output:** Underwriting models, portfolio performance analytics, pricing policy
**Proprietary data:** Transaction flow joined to repayment outcomes across tens of thousands of small hospitality merchants
**Clock:** Funding decisions in days; securitization and reporting cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Credit Officer / VP Portfolio Analytics
