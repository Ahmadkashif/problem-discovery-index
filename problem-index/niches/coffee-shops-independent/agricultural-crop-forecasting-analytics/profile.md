# Agricultural Crop Forecasting Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/coffee-shops-independent|Independent Coffee Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms combining satellite imagery, weather modelling, and ground observation to forecast coffee and other soft commodity crops ahead of official estimates. Traders, roasters, and financial participants buy the forecasts because crop size drives price and the official numbers arrive late.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 remote sensing scientists, agronomists, and data scientists
**Output:** Crop production forecasts, yield and area estimates, weather risk analytics
**Proprietary data:** Calibrated models tying imagery and weather to observed yields over multiple seasons, and the accumulated forecast-versus-realized record
**Clock:** Crop cycles and official estimate release dates
**Buyer:** Chief Scientist / VP of Analytics
