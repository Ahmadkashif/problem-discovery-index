# Coffee Quality Grading & Certification Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/coffee-shops-independent|Independent Coffee Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Coffee Quality Institute and the Specialty Coffee Association define how coffee quality is measured, train and certify the graders whose cupping scores set specialty price premiums, and maintain the sensory lexicon and protocols the trade uses. A grader's score is what separates a commodity lot from a specialty one.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-50 sensory scientists, programme staff, and standards developers
**Output:** Grading protocols, grader certification, sensory standards, cupping score records
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated cupping scores and grader calibration data across origins and years
**Clock:** Certification and calibration cycles, plus harvest seasons
**Buyer:** Director of Programmes / Chief Standards Officer
