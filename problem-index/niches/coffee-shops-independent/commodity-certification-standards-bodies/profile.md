# Agricultural Certification Standards Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/coffee-shops-independent|Independent Coffee Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Rainforest Alliance, Fairtrade International, and the organic certifiers write the standards that coffee farms are audited against, oversee the certification bodies performing the audits, and license the seals that appear on the bag in every independent coffee shop. Standards development and assurance are continuous research operations, and the seal controls market access.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 standards developers, assurance staff, monitoring and evaluation researchers
**Output:** Standards, audit oversight findings, impact evaluations, chain of custody rules
**Proprietary data:** Audit findings and non-conformance history across hundreds of thousands of certified farms, joined to multi-year impact monitoring
**Clock:** Standard revision cycles, annual audit calendars, and now EU deforestation compliance deadlines
**Buyer:** Director of Standards & Assurance / Chief Impact Officer
