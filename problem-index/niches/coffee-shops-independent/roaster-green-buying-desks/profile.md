# Roaster Green Buying & Quality Control Desks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/coffee-shops-independent|Independent Coffee Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The green coffee buying and quality control functions at the roasters supplying independent cafés. Q-certified buyers cup samples, evaluate lots, negotiate differentials, and maintain the supplier relationships and quality records the roaster's product rests on.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 3-20 per roaster, larger at the biggest specialty roasters
**Output:** Lot evaluations, buying decisions, supplier quality records
**Proprietary data:** Multi-year cupping and supplier performance records at farm and lot level
**Clock:** Harvest and shipment windows
**Buyer:** Director of Coffee / Head of Green Buying
