# Coffee Trade House Research Desks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/coffee-shops-independent|Independent Coffee Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Volcafe, Neumann Kaffee Gruppe, ECOM, and Sucafina run market research desks that produce crop forecasts, differential commentary, and origin risk analysis from their own field networks. The research informs the firm's trading book and is circulated to roaster clients as a relationship service.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 analysts and origin market researchers
**Output:** Crop and market reports, differential commentary, origin risk assessments
**Proprietary data:** Origin intelligence from the firm's own buying stations and field agents — a view of crop conditions that official statistics reproduce months later
**Clock:** Crop cycles, contract settlement, and shipment windows
**Buyer:** Head of Research / Chief Trading Officer
