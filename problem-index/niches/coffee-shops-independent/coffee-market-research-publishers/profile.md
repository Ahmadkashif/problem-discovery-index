# Coffee Market Research Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/coffee-shops-independent|Independent Coffee Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Allegra World Coffee Portal and comparable publishers produce the sector reports the coffee industry plans against — outlet counts, market sizing, consumer behaviour, and category forecasts by country. Analysts survey operators and consumers and maintain outlet databases; roasters, chains, and investors subscribe.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 analysts, researchers, and survey managers
**Output:** Sector reports, outlet databases, consumer research — the subscription product
**Proprietary data:** Outlet-level databases and long-running consumer survey series across coffee markets
**Clock:** Annual report cycles and industry event calendars, largely self-set
**Buyer:** Director of Research / Managing Director
