# Coffee Association Consumption Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/coffee-shops-independent|Independent Coffee Shops]]

## What They Do
The National Coffee Association's National Coffee Data Trends study and comparable association research track consumption behaviour, preparation methods, and channel shifts, and supply the sector's advocacy with evidence.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-30 researchers and analysts
**Output:** Consumption trend studies, member reports, advocacy analysis
**Proprietary data:** Long-running consumer survey series with decades of comparable measurement
**Clock:** Annual publication schedule
**Buyer:** VP of Research
