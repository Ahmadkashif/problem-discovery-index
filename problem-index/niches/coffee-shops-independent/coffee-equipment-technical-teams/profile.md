# Espresso Equipment Technical Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/coffee-shops-independent|Independent Coffee Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The engineering and technical support groups at espresso machine and grinder manufacturers research extraction behaviour, publish brewing guidance, and support the service network that keeps café equipment running.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 3-15 per manufacturer
**Output:** Extraction research, technical documentation, service guidance
**Proprietary data:** Field service and failure records across the installed base
**Clock:** Product launch cycles
**Buyer:** Technical Director
