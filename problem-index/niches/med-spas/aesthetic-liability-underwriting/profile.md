# Aesthetic Practice Liability Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/med-spas|Med Spas]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Specialty insurers underwrite professional and general liability for med spas, pricing an unusual risk: procedures performed on healthy people by mid-level practitioners under variable physician supervision.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 underwriters, actuaries, and risk managers
**Output:** Rating and eligibility decisions, supervision and protocol requirements, claims analysis
**Proprietary data:** Claims by procedure, device, practitioner type, and supervision model — the only empirical record of which practice structures produce injuries
**Clock:** Annual renewal
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
