# Elective Procedure Financing Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/med-spas|Med Spas]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Point-of-sale lenders underwrite the financing that lets patients pay for elective aesthetic treatment over time — the mechanism that converts a consultation into a booked procedure in a cash-pay industry.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 credit analysts, data scientists, and risk staff
**Output:** Credit decisions and pricing, approval rate optimization, provider portfolio performance analysis
**Proprietary data:** Credit performance on elective aesthetic borrowers by procedure, provider, and ticket size — a population no traditional lender has depth on
**Clock:** Point-of-sale decisioning in seconds
**Buyer:** Chief Credit Officer / VP of Data Science
