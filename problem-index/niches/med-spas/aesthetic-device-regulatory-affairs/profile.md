# Aesthetic Device Regulatory Affairs

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/med-spas|Med Spas]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Regulatory affairs teams and consultancies prepare the clearance submissions energy-based aesthetic devices require, assemble the clinical and bench evidence supporting claimed indications, and manage post-market obligations.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-120 regulatory scientists, clinical affairs staff, and technical writers
**Output:** Clearance submissions, indication and claims strategy, post-market surveillance reporting, labelling
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated submission precedent and agency interaction history
**Clock:** Agency review timelines and product launch commitments
**Buyer:** VP of Regulatory Affairs
