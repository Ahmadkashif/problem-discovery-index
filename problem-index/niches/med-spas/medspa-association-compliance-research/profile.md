# Med Spa Association Compliance Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/med-spas|Med Spas]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The med spa association tracks the state-by-state legal framework governing medical aesthetics, publishes compliance guidance, and runs the training and certification the industry's operators rely on to stay inside the law.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 attorneys, compliance researchers, and education staff
**Output:** State law summaries and updates, compliance guidance, certification and training, industry survey research
**Proprietary data:** A maintained fifty-state legal framework for a field that no other publisher tracks closely
**Clock:** Legislative sessions and board rule changes
**Buyer:** Executive director — member funded
