# State Medical & Nursing Boards

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/med-spas|Med Spas]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State medical and nursing boards define who may perform aesthetic procedures and under what supervision, investigate complaints, and discipline practitioners — the regulatory boundary a fast-growing industry is continuously testing.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 investigators, analysts, and legal staff per state board
**Output:** Scope determinations, position statements, complaint findings, disciplinary actions
**Proprietary data:** Complaint and discipline records by practitioner and practice type
**Clock:** Complaint investigation and rulemaking timelines
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — state agency
