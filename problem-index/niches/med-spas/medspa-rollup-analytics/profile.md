# Med Spa Platform Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/med-spas|Med Spas]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup

## What They Do
The national med spa chains and private equity platforms run corporate analytics across many locations, managing consultation-to-treatment conversion, injector productivity, and membership retention.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-70 analysts, revenue managers, and clinical operations staff
**Output:** Conversion and retention analysis, pricing and membership design, injector performance review, acquisition diligence
**Proprietary data:** Consultation, treatment, and retention records across many locations and hundreds of thousands of patients
**Clock:** Membership cycles and treatment intervals
**Buyer:** No external buyer — internal cost centre
