# Independent Med Spa Consultants

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/med-spas|Med Spas]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Individual consultants — usually experienced injectors or former practice owners — advise new med spas on treatment menus, pricing, protocols, and staffing, carrying exactly the treatment judgment Pass 1 says has no decision support behind it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1-5 consultants, usually one
**Output:** Practice setup, menu and pricing design, protocol development, injector mentoring
**Proprietary data:** A career of observed treatment outcomes and practice economics, undocumented
**Clock:** Practice opening timelines
**Buyer:** The consultant
