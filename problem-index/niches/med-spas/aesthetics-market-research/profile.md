# Aesthetics Market Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/med-spas|Med Spas]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Specialist research firms size the aesthetics market, track procedure volumes and pricing, and survey practices and consumers on behalf of the device and injectable manufacturers planning launches.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 analysts and researchers
**Output:** Market sizing and forecasts, procedure volume tracking, pricing surveys, consumer research
**Proprietary data:** Practice and consumer survey panels in a segment with almost no public data
**Clock:** Annual reporting and product launch planning
**Buyer:** Managing Director
