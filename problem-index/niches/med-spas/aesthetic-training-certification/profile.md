# Aesthetic Training & Certification Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/med-spas|Med Spas]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Training organizations teach injection technique and device operation to the nurses and mid-level practitioners entering aesthetics, and issue the certifications practices and insurers increasingly require.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-70 clinical educators, curriculum staff, and assessment specialists
**Output:** Training curricula, hands-on certification, competency assessment, continuing education
**Proprietary data:** Trainee performance and certification records across a rapidly growing practitioner population
**Clock:** Course calendars and certification renewal
**Buyer:** Chief Clinical Officer / Director of Education
