# Med Spa Regulatory Compliance Advisory

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/med-spas|Med Spas]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Specialist advisers determine what a med spa may legally do in a given state — who may inject, what supervision is required, whether a non-physician may own the practice — across a patchwork of medical practice, nursing scope, and corporate practice of medicine rules that differ substantially by state and change often.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 healthcare attorneys, compliance specialists, and analysts
**Output:** Ownership and structure opinions, scope-of-practice determinations, supervision protocols, transaction and expansion support
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated state-by-state determinations and board enforcement outcomes
**Clock:** Expansion, transaction, and enforcement timelines
**Buyer:** Practice leader / Managing Director
