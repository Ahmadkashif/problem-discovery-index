# Practice Management & Photo Documentation Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/med-spas|Med Spas]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The practice platforms run scheduling, charting, and the before-and-after photo documentation every aesthetic practice depends on. Pass 1 records the gap directly: photo systems exist and lack standardized analysis, and treatment recommendation is entirely provider-dependent with no decision support.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 product and data staff
**Output:** Charting and consent workflow, photo capture and comparison, treatment and inventory tracking, marketing analytics
**Proprietary data:** Standardized before-and-after image series joined to treatment records across thousands of practices — an outcome dataset no other party holds
**Clock:** Treatment intervals and follow-up appointments
**Buyer:** Head of Product
