# Aesthetic Injectable Clinical & Medical Affairs

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/med-spas|Med Spas]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The neurotoxin and filler manufacturers run the clinical development and medical affairs organizations that generate the trial evidence supporting each indication, train injectors on technique, and manage the adverse event surveillance for products used millions of times a year on healthy people.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,500 clinical scientists, medical affairs staff, biostatisticians, and trainers
**Output:** Clinical trial programmes and publications, injection technique training and certification, safety surveillance, indication expansion evidence
**Proprietary data:** Registration and post-marketing trial data, plus adverse event surveillance across enormous real-world use
**Clock:** Regulatory submission timelines and product launch cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Medical Affairs / Chief Medical Officer
