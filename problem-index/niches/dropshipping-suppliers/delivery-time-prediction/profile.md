# Delivery Time Prediction

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/dropshipping-suppliers|Dropshipping Suppliers]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to publish a delivery distribution for a specific supplier, service and destination that holds up — and whoever does that lets merchants make a promise instead of a guess.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.4B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — tracking aggregated, nothing predicted
**Target Buyer:** Platform data and merchant success
**Automation Potential:** Very High — a complete labelled dataset exists

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Tracking aggregation is a mature service and merchants still promise delivery windows they invent, because nobody publishes a realistic distribution for a specific supplier, service and destination. The platform has routed millions of orders and observed the full journey of each one, which is a large, clean, continuously refreshed labelled dataset for one of the most consequential predictions in the category — and it is used to display a tracking page. Promised delivery dates drive conversion, disputes, refunds and marketplace standing, and every one of them is currently a merchant's guess.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Tracking aggregation services, carrier-published transit estimates, supplier-claimed handling times, and merchant-invented shipping policies. The gaps: no distribution, only a point estimate if anything; no resolution by supplier, service and destination; no seasonal or customs effects; no in-flight revision as a shipment progresses; and no feedback from actual outcomes to displayed promises.
