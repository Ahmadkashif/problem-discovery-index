**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give the dispute agent evidence instead of two accounts — and whoever does that makes the platform's adjudication defensible, which is what merchants are actually buying.

## The Problem
Insurance claims handling is a mature profession with mature software: structured evidence collection, severity and complexity triage, fraud indicators, reserve setting, adjuster authority limits, consistency auditing, documented rationale and a formal appeals path. All of it exists because deciding contested claims on incomplete evidence, consistently, at volume, is a known hard problem. Dispute agents on commerce platforms do the same job, at similar volume, with a ticketing system built for answering questions.

## What Already Exists
Claims management platforms with structured intake; triage and routing by complexity; fraud indicator scoring; adjuster authority and escalation frameworks; consistency auditing and file review; and appeals workflow.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is from a regulated bilateral claim to a three-party commerce dispute at far lower value. It requires: (1) three parties rather than two, since the end customer, the merchant and the supplier all have positions and the commerce dispute is genuinely triangular — the claims model has no representation for this; (2) decision economics at a few tens of dollars, where the insurance process costs more per claim than the claim is worth and every step must justify itself; (3) evidence that is transactional rather than documentary — tracking, timing, histories — which is both weaker and far more automatable than the affidavits and reports insurance works from; (4) a continuing commercial relationship after the decision, since both parties keep trading on the platform, which changes the objective from settling a claim to sustaining a marketplace; and (5) rationale capture that fits in seconds rather than in a written file, which is the practical constraint on everything.

## Target Customer
Platform support and trust operations, dispute resolution teams, and claims software vendors for whom low-value three-party commerce disputes are unserved.

## Impact If Solved
Insurance built this profession because the problem is hard, and commerce platforms run it on a question-answering ticket queue. The triangular structure has no representation in the claims model, and the per-claim economics rule out most of its process.
