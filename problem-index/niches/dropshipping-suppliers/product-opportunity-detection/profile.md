# Product Opportunity Detection

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/dropshipping-suppliers|Dropshipping Suppliers]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell a merchant which product will work before they spend on advertising it — and whoever predicts that from the platform's own order flow owns the decision every merchant makes first.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.2B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — scraped lists and guesswork
**Target Buyer:** Platform product and merchant success
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the demand signal is observed directly

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Product selection is the first and most consequential decision a merchant makes, and it is made from scraped bestseller lists, paid newsletters and social media videos — all of which describe products that are already saturated by the time they circulate. The platform sees every order across every merchant and therefore observes demand forming before any list does. This is a distinct contest because the prediction is about a product's future rather than a supplier's reliability, and because its output changes what merchants sell rather than how well they sell it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Scraped bestseller rankings, third-party product research subscriptions, social media trend watching, and competitor storefront copying. The gaps: signals that are lagging by construction; no distinction between demand and saturation; no profitability dimension; no supplier capacity check; and no measurement of whether any recommendation ever worked.
