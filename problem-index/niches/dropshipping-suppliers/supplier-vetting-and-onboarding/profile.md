# Supplier Vetting & Onboarding

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/dropshipping-suppliers|Dropshipping Suppliers]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to predict whether a supplier will perform before any orders have been routed to them — and whoever does that well controls who gets into the marketplace and therefore what it is worth.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.2B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — documents, a call and a sample
**Target Buyer:** Supplier operations leadership
**Automation Potential:** Medium — evidence gathering automatable, judgement assisted

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A sourcing analyst approves suppliers onto the platform from documents, a sample order and a video call, then never learns whether the approval was right. Admission is the platform's only real quality control — once a supplier is in, merchants will find them and orders will flow — and it is performed on the thinnest evidence anywhere in the chain. This is distinct from reliability measurement because it is a decision made in the absence of any performance record, which is a prediction problem rather than a measurement one, and the evidence available is documentary rather than transactional.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Business registration checks, document collection, sample orders, video calls, and a written assessment. The gaps: no feedback from approval decisions to outcomes; no structured record of what was assessed; no external evidence gathering beyond what the supplier supplies; no calibration across analysts; and no re-vetting as a supplier's circumstances change.
