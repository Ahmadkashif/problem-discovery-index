**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Financial services has an industrial due diligence apparatus for onboarding counterparties, and dropshipping platforms onboard suppliers with a video call.
**Tags:** #compliance #evaluation-metrics #workflow-orchestration #logistic-regression #confidence-intervals #automation #descriptive-statistics #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to predict whether a supplier will perform before any orders have been routed to them — and whoever does that well controls who gets into the marketplace and therefore what it is worth.

## The Problem
Onboarding an unknown counterparty is a mature industrial process in financial services and large-enterprise procurement: identity and registration verification, beneficial ownership, sanctions and adverse media screening, risk tiering, documented evidence files, periodic review and defined escalation. It is heavily tooled and heavily regulated. Dropshipping platforms onboard counterparties who will hold merchants' inventory, ship to their customers and carry their brand, with a document upload and a conversation.

## What Already Exists
Identity and business verification services; sanctions, watchlist and adverse media screening; beneficial ownership resolution; risk tiering frameworks with review cycles; and case management with evidence retention.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is from financial risk to operational performance risk. It requires: (1) the question being will they ship well rather than are they who they claim, which is a performance prediction the entire due diligence apparatus has no method for — the identity half transfers directly and the important half does not; (2) evidence from operational sources such as trade and shipping records rather than from financial and regulatory registries, which is a different data supply chain; (3) unit economics that work on a supplier generating modest revenue, since enterprise due diligence costs more per counterparty than a dropshipping supplier is worth; (4) cross-border coverage in jurisdictions where registry data is thin, which is where most suppliers are and where the tooling is weakest; and (5) a continuous rather than periodic review, driven by observed performance once orders start flowing, which is a signal financial onboarding never gets.

## Target Customer
Dropshipping and sourcing platforms, marketplace trust and supply teams, and verification vendors for whom operational performance risk is an unserved question.

## Impact If Solved
The identity half of due diligence transfers directly and the half that matters — will they ship well — has no method in the existing apparatus. Operational evidence from trade and shipping records is the different data supply chain the adaptation needs.
