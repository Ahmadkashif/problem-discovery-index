## The Problem
Retail buying is a profession with a developed toolkit: open-to-buy planning, margin and markdown management, assortment planning, vendor negotiation supported by performance data, and sell-through analysis by product and season. Retailers of any size run all of it. The dropshipping merchant performs the same function — choosing what to sell, from whom, at what price, with what capital — and has access to none of it, because the practice and its software are built for an organisation rather than a person.

## What Already Exists
Open-to-buy and assortment planning systems; margin, markdown and promotional analysis; sell-through and inventory productivity reporting; vendor performance and negotiation support; and category management frameworks.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is from a buying department to one person with two hours an evening. It requires: (1) no inventory commitment, since the merchant buys nothing in advance — which removes open-to-buy's central object and replaces it with advertising spend as the scarce capital, a substitution nobody has made; (2) advertising as the primary cost of sale rather than cost of goods, which reshapes every margin calculation and is where the real money goes; (3) zero configuration and zero training, because a merchant who must set up a planning system will not use one — this is the practical barrier that disqualifies the existing software entirely; (4) supplier performance data supplied by the platform rather than negotiated, since the merchant has no leverage and no vendor relationship; and (5) output as a few prioritised decisions rather than as a planning process, since there is no planning cycle and no team to run one.

## Target Customer
Solo and small merchants, dropshipping and storefront platforms, and retail planning vendors for whom the single-operator merchant is unserved.

## Impact If Solved
Open-to-buy's central object is inventory commitment and there is none here, so advertising spend becomes the scarce capital being planned. Zero configuration is the barrier that disqualifies the existing software regardless of how good its method is.
