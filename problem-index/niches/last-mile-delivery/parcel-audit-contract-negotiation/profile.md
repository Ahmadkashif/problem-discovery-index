# Parcel Audit & Contract Negotiation Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/last-mile-delivery|Last-Mile Delivery]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Parcel audit firms process shippers' carrier invoices line by line, recover refunds for service failures and billing errors, and benchmark and renegotiate carrier contracts. Because they do this for hundreds of shippers across both national carriers, they are the only party that can see what carriers actually charge — a market where every contract is confidential and discounts vary enormously between shippers of similar size.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 audit analysts, pricing analysts, and data engineers
**Output:** Refund recovery, invoice audit findings, rate benchmarking, contract negotiation and modelling
**Proprietary data:** Shipment-level rate, surcharge, and discount data across hundreds of shippers and both major carriers, joined to negotiated contract terms — the only assembled view of parcel pricing anywhere
**Clock:** Carrier refund claim windows measured in days, and annual general rate increase and contract renewal cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Analytics Officer / VP of Client Solutions
