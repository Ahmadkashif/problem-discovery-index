# Carrier Network Corporate Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/last-mile-delivery|Last-Mile Delivery]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup

## What They Do
The national carriers' network planning organizations design the sortation and linehaul networks, forecast volume, and plan peak season capacity across facilities handling tens of millions of packages a day.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 industrial engineers, forecasters, and operations researchers
**Output:** Network design and capacity plans, volume forecasts, peak season planning, facility performance analysis
**Proprietary data:** Package-level flow, timing, and cost across the entire national network
**Clock:** Peak season, which is fixed and unforgiving
**Buyer:** No external buyer — internal cost centre
