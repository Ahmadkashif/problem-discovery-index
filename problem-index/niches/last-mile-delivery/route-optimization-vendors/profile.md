# Route Optimization Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/last-mile-delivery|Last-Mile Delivery]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Route optimization providers plan the daily delivery sequences and time windows fleets run, and their algorithms determine how many stops a driver completes and how far they travel to do it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-200 operations researchers, data scientists, and solution engineers
**Output:** Route plans and sequencing, service time estimation, capacity and territory planning, dynamic re-routing
**Proprietary data:** Planned versus actual route execution across many fleets — arrival times, service durations, and failures at address level
**Clock:** Daily route generation, in the hours before dispatch
**Buyer:** VP of Data Science / Chief Product Officer
