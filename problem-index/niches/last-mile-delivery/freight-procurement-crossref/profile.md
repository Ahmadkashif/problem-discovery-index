# Freight Procurement Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/last-mile-delivery|Last-Mile Delivery]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Freight procurement consultancies run the bid events through which shippers award transportation contracts, modelling carrier proposals against network requirements.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 analysts, network modellers, and procurement specialists
**Output:** Bid design and analysis, carrier award optimization, network modelling, rate benchmarking
**Proprietary data:** Bid response and award data across many shippers and carriers
**Clock:** Annual bid cycles and contract effective dates
**Buyer:** Practice leader
