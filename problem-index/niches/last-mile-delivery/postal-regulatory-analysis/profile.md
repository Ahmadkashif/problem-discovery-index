# Postal Regulatory Analysis

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/last-mile-delivery|Last-Mile Delivery]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The postal regulator reviews rate changes, evaluates service performance against standards, and analyses the cost attribution that determines what postal products may be priced at — decisions that set the competitive floor for the entire parcel market.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-200 economists, analysts, and attorneys
**Output:** Rate determinations, cost attribution analyses, service performance reports, competitive product reviews
**Proprietary data:** Detailed cost and volume data submitted by the postal operator, much of it filed under seal
**Clock:** Statutory rate review and annual compliance timelines
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — federal agency
