# Parcel Carrier Pricing Science

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/last-mile-delivery|Last-Mile Delivery]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The pricing and revenue management organizations inside the national parcel carriers set list rates, surcharge structures, and the individual discount agreements every large shipper negotiates — the other side of the table from the audit firms.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 pricing analysts, economists, and data scientists
**Output:** Rate structures and general rate increases, surcharge design, customer-specific pricing, cost-to-serve modelling
**Proprietary data:** Complete shipment cost and revenue data at package level across the entire network, joined to every customer contract
**Clock:** Annual rate increases, peak season surcharges, and contract renewals
**Buyer:** VP of Pricing / Chief Revenue Officer
