# Delivery Fleet Insurance Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/last-mile-delivery|Last-Mile Delivery]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Specialty insurers underwrite the delivery service partners and local courier fleets that carry most last-mile volume, pricing high-frequency urban driving, heavy driver turnover, and severe liability exposure.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 underwriters, actuaries, and telematics analysts
**Output:** Rating and eligibility decisions, telematics-based scoring, loss control programmes, claims analysis
**Proprietary data:** Loss experience by fleet, route type, and driving behaviour across delivery operations
**Clock:** Annual renewal and contract requirements from the contracting carrier
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
