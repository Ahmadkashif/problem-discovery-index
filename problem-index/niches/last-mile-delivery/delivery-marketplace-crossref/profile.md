# Delivery Marketplace Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/last-mile-delivery|Last-Mile Delivery]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The on-demand delivery marketplaces dispatch independent couriers against real-time demand, and their analytics organizations set the incentives, pricing, and matching that govern the largest gig delivery workforce in the country.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,500 data scientists, economists, and analysts
**Output:** Demand forecasting, dispatch and matching, courier incentive design, delivery time estimation
**Proprietary data:** Order-level demand, courier behaviour, and delivery outcomes at minute resolution nationally
**Clock:** Real-time dispatch
**Buyer:** VP of Data Science
