# Stop-Loss Reimbursement Claims & Large-Claimant Notices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-insured-benefit-pools|Self-Insured Benefit & Risk Pools]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to recover every reimbursable dollar of a large claim from the stop-loss carrier inside the contract's paid-claim and filing windows, and whoever files complete packets that are paid without a records request or denial takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$250M a year of TPA stop-loss unit staff, recovery-audit fees and carrier-side reimbursement review attributable to self-insured funds and pools (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% of self-insured funds' and pools' non-claim spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — claims data is digital; packets are assembled by hand and filed through portals and email
**Target Buyer:** Fund Administrator / TPA stop-loss manager
**Automation Potential:** High — notices, packet assembly, deadline tracking and reconciliation are rule-bound

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Stop-loss is how a self-insured fund caps its exposure: carriers wrote ~$39B of stop-loss premium in 2024 (Oliver Wyman, via Insurance Business), and Segal recorded a 9.4% average premium increase that year. The money only comes back if the fund files correctly: specific claims above the deductible, within the contract's incurred-and-paid basis and filing deadline, with eligibility proved and the plan document followed. Large claims are multiplying (Sun Life: million-dollar claims up 29% in a year, 61% in four), so each filing is larger and the cost of a missed window rises. The work is mechanical, deadline-driven and repeated for every claimant, which is why it sits on the automatable axis.

## Current Tools & Gaps
TPA claims systems with large-claim reports, carrier and MGU portals, TPA stop-loss units, and recovery auditors working on contingency. Gaps: contract terms (lasers, run-out, eligible expense definitions) tracked in spreadsheets; packets assembled by hand; queries answered by email; no monthly reconciled receivable for trustees.
