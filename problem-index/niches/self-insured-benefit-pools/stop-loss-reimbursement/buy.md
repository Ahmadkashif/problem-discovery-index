# Denial Management Software Adapted to Stop-Loss Recoveries

**Industry:** [[industries/self-insured-benefit-pools|Self-Insured Benefit & Risk Pools]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Hospitals already have software that predicts and works payer denials; a fund chasing its stop-loss carrier has nothing like it.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #large-language-models #feature-engineering #workflow-orchestration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to recover every reimbursable dollar of a large claim from the stop-loss carrier inside the contract's paid-claim and filing windows, and whoever files complete packets that are paid without a records request or denial takes the account.

## The Problem
Carriers query or deny stop-loss filings for missing records, eligibility gaps, plan-document conformity and timing. Each query restarts a manual cycle.

## What Already Exists
Provider revenue-cycle denial management platforms that classify denials, predict them before submission, and route appeals with templates.

## The Customization Gap
The payer here is a stop-loss carrier and the rules are the policy, not a payer contract or a fee schedule: specific and aggregate deductibles, lasers, incurred-and-paid basis, eligible expense definitions, plan-document conformity and filing deadlines. Eligibility proof comes from hour banks or public payroll, not an HR feed. The workflow needs packet templates per carrier and an evidence trail for the auditor.

## Target Customer
TPA stop-loss managers and fund administrators.

## Impact If Solved
Fewer queries, faster payment and less staff time per claimant, using a proven pattern from provider billing.
