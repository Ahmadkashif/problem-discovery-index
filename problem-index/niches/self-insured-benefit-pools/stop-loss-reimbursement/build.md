# A Contract-Aware Claimant Tracker From First Signal to Reimbursement

**Industry:** [[industries/self-insured-benefit-pools|Self-Insured Benefit & Risk Pools]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Stop-loss recovery is tracked claimant by claimant in a spreadsheet that knows nothing about the contract it is meant to enforce.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #gradient-boosting #feature-engineering #workflow-orchestration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to recover every reimbursable dollar of a large claim from the stop-loss carrier inside the contract's paid-claim and filing windows, and whoever files complete packets that are paid without a records request or denial takes the account.

## The Problem
A fund with a dozen specific claimants a year (estimate) must follow each one against deductibles, lasers, the paid-through date and the filing deadline, while the claims keep arriving and the carrier keeps asking for records.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
TPA platforms treat stop-loss as a report, carriers build portals for their own intake, and each fund's volume is too small for anyone to have built a product around it.

## What to Build
Contracts read into rules at renewal. Every member tracked from the first large-claim signal: notices sent at the carrier's thresholds, packets assembled from claims, eligibility and plan-document sources, likely queries predicted from past filings, alerts before paid-through and filing dates, and a monthly reconciliation of filed, paid, pending and disputed amounts for trustees and auditors.

## Target Customer
TPAs serving multiemployer and public-sector health funds, and the larger funds that administer themselves.

## Impact If Built
Reimbursements recovered in full and sooner, and a stop-loss receivable trustees can trust.
