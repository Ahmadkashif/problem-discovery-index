# Benefits Administration Platforms Adapted to Hour Banks

**Niche:** [[niches/self-insured-benefit-pools/hour-bank-eligibility/profile|Hour-Bank Eligibility & Self-Pay at Health and Welfare Funds]]
**Industry:** [[industries/self-insured-benefit-pools|Self-Insured Benefit & Risk Pools]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Employer benefits platforms assume one employer and an HR feed; a multiemployer fund decides eligibility from hours sent by hundreds of employers.
**Tags:** #feature-engineering #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #worker-facing #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to decide every member's coverage for next month from hours reported late and by hand, and whoever pays the fewest claims for people who were not eligible while cutting off nobody who was takes the account.

## The Problem
Funds run eligibility on ageing fund-office systems or TPA platforms because modern benefits administration software does not understand how coverage is earned.

## What Already Exists
Employer benefits administration and enrolment platforms, COBRA administration services and eligibility file exchanges to carriers, PBMs and networks.

## The Customization Gap
Hour banks measured in hours or dollars; monthly requirements that differ by class (apprentice, journeyman, retiree); lag months; late-contribution rules; reciprocity agreements moving hours between funds; self-pay and COBRA interleaved with hours; retro-termination after payroll audits; and eligibility files to vendors that may not accept retroactive changes. The rules belong in configurable logic with each trustee resolution cited.

## Target Customer
TPAs and fund offices replacing legacy eligibility systems.

## Impact If Solved
Funds get a maintained platform instead of a home-grown database, with rules documented once and applied consistently.
