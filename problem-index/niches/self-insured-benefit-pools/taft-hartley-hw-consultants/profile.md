# Taft-Hartley Health & Welfare Consultants & Actuaries

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-insured-benefit-pools|Self-Insured Benefit & Risk Pools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Consulting actuaries and benefits consultants who advise multiemployer health funds' joint boards: Segal, Horizon Actuarial, Milliman and a set of regional firms and broker practices. They project cost per hour, recommend reserve targets and contribution allocations ahead of bargaining, price plan changes, run PBM, network and stop-loss procurements, and certify financial information for the trustees. Trustees pay fees from plan assets. The same firms' pension work is logged as [[niches/pension-funds/multiemployer-plan-actuaries/profile|🔍 Multiemployer Plan Actuaries & Zone Certification]] (49, kill switch), and single-employer brokerage analytics as [[niches/hr-consultants/benefits-brokerage-analytics/profile|🔍 Employee Benefits Brokerage Analytics]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-200 actuaries and analysts in each major firm's multiemployer health practice (estimate)
**Output:** Cost projections and reserve studies, plan-design costing, vendor procurement analyses, trend research (Segal's annual cost trend survey projects a 9.9% median medical trend for 2027)
**Proprietary data:** Claims, eligibility and hours experience across many funds in the same trades, plus decades of projections
**Clock:** Bargaining expirations and plan-year renewals: hard dates, but set by the client's contracts rather than a regulator
**Buyer:** National multiemployer practice leader
