# Federal Health Plan Reporting & Attestations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-insured-benefit-pools|Self-Insured Benefit & Risk Pools]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to assemble each federal health plan filing from data held by the fund's TPA, PBM and carve-out vendors before its statutory date, and whoever files on time with no vendor data gaps takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M a year of compliance reporting work for self-insured funds and pools, spread across TPA, PBM, consultant, auditor and compliance-vendor fees (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% of self-insured funds' and pools' non-claim spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — the filings are electronic; the data behind them is gathered from vendors by email and spreadsheet
**Target Buyer:** Fund Administrator / compliance lead at the TPA
**Automation Potential:** High — fixed templates, fixed dates, recurring data

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A self-funded health fund is the plan sponsor for federal purposes. It owes RxDC drug and spending data by June 1, a gag-clause attestation by December 31 (CMS), Form 5500 schedules, Medicare Part D creditable coverage notices, ACA reporting where applicable, and a mental health parity comparative analysis on demand. The May 15, 2025 non-enforcement of the 2024 parity rule did not lift the Consolidated Appropriations Act duty. The data sits with four or five vendors: the TPA, the PBM, behavioural health and dental carve-outs, and the stop-loss carrier. A fund office of a dozen people (estimate) has no HR department to absorb the work an employer plan would.

## Current Tools & Gaps
TPAs and PBMs file parts on the fund's behalf, compliance vendors aggregate RxDC submissions, consultants prepare parity analyses (logged as [[niches/behavioral-health-clinics/parity-compliance-analysis/profile|🔍 Mental Health Parity Compliance Analysis]]), and plan auditors prepare or review the 5500. Gaps: no single calendar of who owes what; vendor files late or inconsistent; no lineage from filing back to source.
