# Employer Compliance Reporting Software Adapted to Multiemployer Funds

**Niche:** [[niches/self-insured-benefit-pools/federal-health-plan-reporting/profile|Federal Health Plan Reporting & Attestations]]
**Industry:** [[industries/self-insured-benefit-pools|Self-Insured Benefit & Risk Pools]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** ACA and benefits compliance software is built around one employer's payroll; a Taft-Hartley fund is not the employer of anyone it covers.
**Tags:** #feature-engineering #data-integration #compliance #automation #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to assemble each federal health plan filing from data held by the fund's TPA, PBM and carve-out vendors before its statutory date, and whoever files on time with no vendor data gaps takes the account.

## The Problem
Funds buy or borrow employer-oriented compliance tools and work around their assumptions every filing season.

## What Already Exists
Employer ACA reporting software, benefits compliance platforms and RxDC submission services that collect plan data and generate filings.

## The Customization Gap
Funds need eligibility from hours rather than payroll; members employed by many contributing employers in a year; retirees and self-pay participants in the same plan; several PBMs or carve-outs; reciprocity; and the fund, not an employer, as plan sponsor and filer. RxDC requires premium-equivalent and contribution data defined for a fund financed per hour.

## Target Customer
TPAs and fund offices of multiemployer health funds.

## Impact If Solved
A maintained product replaces seasonal workarounds, and the same tool covers the fund's full calendar.
