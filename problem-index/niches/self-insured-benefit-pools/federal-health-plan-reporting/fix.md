# The PBM File That Arrives the Week Before the Deadline

**Niche:** [[niches/self-insured-benefit-pools/federal-health-plan-reporting/profile|Federal Health Plan Reporting & Attestations]]
**Industry:** [[industries/self-insured-benefit-pools|Self-Insured Benefit & Risk Pools]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The fund cannot file its drug-spending report until its PBM sends data it controls, and the PBM sends it at the last minute.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #feature-engineering #data-integration #compliance #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to assemble each federal health plan filing from data held by the fund's TPA, PBM and carve-out vendors before its statutory date, and whoever files on time with no vendor data gaps takes the account.

## The Problem
RxDC reporting depends on the PBM's rebate and drug-cost data. PBMs file their own portion or send files to the fund's aggregator on their own schedule, often close to June 1, leaving no time to reconcile with the fund's records.

## Why It's Still Broken
PBM contracts rarely set data-delivery dates or formats for federal reporting, and funds lack leverage mid-contract.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Add reporting-data delivery terms (dates, formats, reconciliation rights) to PBM and carve-out contracts at renewal, and run an automated pre-check on each file the day it arrives against the fund's own claims and enrolment totals.

## Who Feels the Pain
Fund administrators and compliance staff; trustees who sign attestations about data they never saw reconciled.

## Impact If Fixed
Filings reconciled before submission and a contractual lever over vendors that today set the fund's compliance timetable.
