# Large Statewide Pools' Underwriting & Risk Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-insured-benefit-pools|Self-Insured Benefit & Risk Pools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The biggest state municipal, county and school pools (each covering hundreds of members across several lines) run their own underwriting, claims analytics and loss-control teams. They set member contributions, buy reinsurance, design coverage and publish rating methods to members, as CIRSA does each year. Members pay contributions for coverage, not for the analysis.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 underwriters, analysts and loss-control specialists at the largest pools (estimate); most pools have far fewer
**Output:** Member rates and contribution allocations, loss trend reports, reinsurance submissions, loss-control programmes
**Proprietary data:** Decades of member claims, exposures and loss-control visits, owned by the pool, which its members own
**Clock:** Member renewal and reinsurance dates; board approval of contributions; annual audited financial statements
**Buyer:** Pool Executive Director
