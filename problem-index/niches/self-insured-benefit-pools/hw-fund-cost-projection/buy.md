# Employer Renewal Models Adapted to Hours-Based Funding

**Niche:** [[niches/self-insured-benefit-pools/hw-fund-cost-projection/profile|Health & Welfare Fund Cost Projection & Bargaining Allocation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/self-insured-benefit-pools|Self-Insured Benefit & Risk Pools]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Renewal and budgeting models for single-employer plans assume a premium per employee per month; a Taft-Hartley fund is paid per hour worked across hundreds of employers.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #linear-regression #monte-carlo-methods #feature-engineering #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to project a health fund's cost per hour worked across a three-to-five-year bargaining agreement, and whoever's projection keeps the fund's reserve inside the trustees' target band without a mid-contract benefit cut or emergency reallocation takes the account.

## The Problem
Funds and their consultants project costs with tools built for employer plans, then bolt on spreadsheets for everything that makes a fund different.

## What Already Exists
Broker and consultant renewal models, employer benefits budgeting tools and self-funded analytics platforms (Springbuk, Artemis Health, Milliman MedInsight) project claims per member per month, price plan-design changes and benchmark against peers.

## The Customization Gap
A fund needs: contributions per hour by bargaining agreement, each with its own rate and expiry date; hours volatility by craft and region; hour-bank and self-pay effects on who is covered; reciprocity flows from members working in other funds' areas; reserve targets in months of expenses as trustees define them; and retiree subsidies paid from the same fund. Output must be a contribution allocation per hour the bargaining parties can act on, not a premium.

## Target Customer
Consulting practices serving multiemployer funds and the larger fund offices that run their own projections.

## Impact If Solved
Projections that start from how a fund is actually financed would remove the manual rework at each cycle and make scenario testing quick enough to use during bargaining.
