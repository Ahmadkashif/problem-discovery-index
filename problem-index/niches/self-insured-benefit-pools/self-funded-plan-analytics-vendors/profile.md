# Self-Funded Plan Analytics Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-insured-benefit-pools|Self-Insured Benefit & Risk Pools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Vendors that ingest a self-funded plan's medical, pharmacy and eligibility data from its TPA and PBM and return dashboards, benchmarks, risk scores and savings opportunities: Springbuk, Artemis Health, Milliman MedInsight and others. Funds, pools, consultants and brokers subscribe. The equivalent inside claims platforms is logged as [[niches/insurance-tpa/claims-platform-vendor-data/profile|🔍 Claims Platform Vendor Data Teams]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-200 analysts, clinicians and data scientists per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Plan dashboards, peer benchmarks, high-cost claimant and risk reports, programme evaluations
**Proprietary data:** Normalised claims across many clients' plans, held under business associate agreements
**Clock:** Clients' renewal, bargaining and board cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Product Officer / Head of Analytics

**Kill switches:** **data isn't theirs** — every row is a client plan's protected health information held under a BAA, with no checked evidence that agreements permit cross-client reuse beyond de-identified benchmarks.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. Software-led, so revenue does not scale with analysts, and the corpus belongs to the clients.
