# State Oversight of Pools, MEWAs & Group Self-Insurers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-insured-benefit-pools|Self-Insured Benefit & Risk Pools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State insurance departments and workers' compensation agencies license and examine self-funded MEWAs, workers' compensation group self-insurers and, in some states, public entity pools, reviewing financial filings, actuarial opinions and security deposits. Workers' compensation boards that oversee individual self-insurers are logged as [[niches/insurance-tpa/state-workers-comp-boards/profile|🔍 State Workers' Compensation Boards]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** Small examination units, typically a few staff per state for these entities (estimate); examiners are production in any case
**Output:** Licences, financial examinations, solvency actions
**Proprietary data:** Filed financial statements and actuarial opinions
**Clock:** Statutory filing dates
**Buyer:** Deputy commissioner
