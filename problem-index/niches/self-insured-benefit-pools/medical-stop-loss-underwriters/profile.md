# Medical Stop-Loss Underwriters & MGUs

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-insured-benefit-pools|Self-Insured Benefit & Risk Pools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Carriers and managing general underwriters that price specific and aggregate stop-loss for self-funded health plans: Sun Life, Tokio Marine HCC, Voya, Symetra, Berkley and others, plus MGUs writing on carriers' paper. They quote from the plan's claims experience and large-claimant disclosures, set lasers on known claimants, and review reimbursement claims. Stop-loss carrier premium reached ~$39B in 2024 (Oliver Wyman, via Insurance Business). Commercial-lines MGAs are logged as [[niches/independent-insurance-agents/mga-program-underwriters/profile|🔍 MGAs & Program Underwriters]] (52, qualified). This pocket scores lower on two criteria, in line with the vault's other pockets that price risk into a premium: the insight is sold inside the stop-loss premium rather than invoiced (as for [[niches/self-insured-benefit-pools/public-entity-excess-underwriters/profile|🔍 Public Entity Excess & Reinsurance Underwriters]]), and the renewal date is a contract date, not a regulator's. It is also blocked by what the disclosures contain.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 underwriters, actuaries and clinical claim reviewers per major carrier or MGU (estimate)
**Output:** Quotes and renewal pricing, lasers and exclusions, large-claim reviews, reimbursement decisions, published high-cost claims research (Sun Life's annual report covered 65,000+ claims from 3,000 employers)
**Proprietary data:** Disclosure, quote, bind and reimbursement history across thousands of self-funded groups
**Clock:** Plan renewal effective dates, usually January 1 or July 1: hard, but set by the client's plan year rather than a regulator
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer / Head of Stop-Loss Analytics
