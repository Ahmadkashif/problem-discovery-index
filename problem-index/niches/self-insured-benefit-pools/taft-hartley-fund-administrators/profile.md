# Taft-Hartley Fund Administrators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-insured-benefit-pools|Self-Insured Benefit & Risk Pools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Third-party administrators that run multiemployer funds' offices: contribution posting, eligibility, claims, COBRA and self-pay, member service and trustee reporting. Zenith American, BeneSys, Wilson-McShane and regional firms are typical. Funds pay per-participant fees. The P&C and workers' compensation TPAs' analytics units are logged as [[niches/insurance-tpa/tpa-claims-analytics-organizations/profile|🔍 TPA Claims Analytics Organizations]] (55, indexed). Health fund TPAs are smaller, their staff are claims processors and eligibility clerks, and their data is each fund's protected health information.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Hundreds of claims, eligibility and member-service staff at the larger firms, but they are production; analytics and reporting teams are small (estimate)
**Output:** Administered claims and eligibility, trustee reports
**Proprietary data:** None of their own; each fund owns its records
**Clock:** Monthly eligibility runs and claim payment cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Operating Officer
