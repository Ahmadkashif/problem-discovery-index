# Public Entity Excess & Reinsurance Underwriters

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-insured-benefit-pools|Self-Insured Benefit & Risk Pools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The carriers and reinsurers that write excess and reinsurance above pools' retentions for liability, property and workers' compensation, plus the specialist public entity programme underwriters. They price from each pool's loss runs, exposure summaries and statements of values, and decide whether to stay on law enforcement liability at all; CIRSA saw long-time reinsurers leave before its 2024 renewal. The brokers who place these programmes are logged as [[niches/independent-insurance-agents/reinsurance-broking-analytics/profile|🔍 Reinsurance Broking Analytics]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 underwriters, actuaries and claims specialists per active market (estimate)
**Output:** Excess and reinsurance quotes and terms, retention and limit structures, large-loss claims oversight
**Proprietary data:** Submissions and excess loss experience across many pools in many states, the only cross-pool view of large public liability losses
**Clock:** Pool renewal dates, concentrated at July 1 and January 1
**Buyer:** Head of Public Entity Underwriting
