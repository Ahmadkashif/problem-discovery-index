# EBSA Health Plan & MEWA Enforcement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-insured-benefit-pools|Self-Insured Benefit & Risk Pools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Department of Labor's Employee Benefits Security Administration enforces ERISA for private health and welfare plans: fiduciary investigations of Taft-Hartley funds, mental health parity reviews and its annual report to Congress, gag-clause and disclosure rules with the other Departments, and Form M-1 filings by multiple employer welfare arrangements. Public entity plans are outside ERISA. This pocket is the health slice; the pension slice is logged as [[niches/pension-funds/pbgc-ebsa-plan-analytics/profile|🔍 PBGC & EBSA Plan Analytics]] (31, below threshold), scored one point higher on labour mass because its case reviews are same-shaped.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-100 analysts and policy staff on health plan standards and enforcement analytics, within a much larger investigative workforce (estimate)
**Output:** Investigations, parity enforcement findings, reports to Congress, guidance and FAQs
**Proprietary data:** Form 5500 and M-1 filings, investigation files, parity comparative analyses received on demand
**Clock:** Statutory reporting to Congress and rulemaking calendars
**Buyer:** Office director
