# Values Nobody Has Updated Since the Last Appraisal

**Niche:** [[niches/self-insured-benefit-pools/member-property-schedules/profile|Member Property Schedules & Reinsurance Submissions]]
**Industry:** [[industries/self-insured-benefit-pools|Self-Insured Benefit & Risk Pools]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Building values are appraised every few years and trended by one index in between, so a pool finds out a fire station was underinsured on the day it burns.
**Tags:** #linear-regression #time-series-forecasting #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver a complete, current, reinsurer-grade schedule of every member's buildings, contents, vehicles and infrastructure before the property renewal, and whoever's schedule draws the fewest valuation loadings and exclusions from property reinsurers takes the account.

## The Problem
Appraisal cycles leave years in which values are moved by a single construction cost index, regardless of occupancy, region or renovations. Specialised public buildings drift furthest from reality.

## Why It's Still Broken
Full appraisals are expensive and slow, members do not report renovations, and nobody compares scheduled values with what the pool actually paid to rebuild after losses.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Trend values by occupancy and region rather than one index, flag buildings whose value per square foot sits outside the range of recent appraisals and settlements, and schedule appraisals by risk rather than by calendar.

## Who Feels the Pain
Members facing a coinsurance-style shortfall or a fight after a loss; underwriters defending values to reinsurers; boards approving contributions that do not match exposure.

## Impact If Fixed
Values that stay close to rebuilding cost between appraisals, at a fraction of the cost of appraising everything more often.
