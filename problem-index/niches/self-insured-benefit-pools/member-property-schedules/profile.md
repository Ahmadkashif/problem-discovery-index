# Member Property Schedules & Reinsurance Submissions

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-insured-benefit-pools|Self-Insured Benefit & Risk Pools]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver a complete, current, reinsurer-grade schedule of every member's buildings, contents, vehicles and infrastructure before the property renewal, and whoever's schedule draws the fewest valuation loadings and exclusions from property reinsurers takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M a year of appraisal, schedule maintenance, pool-system modules and broker data work on public entity property schedules (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% of self-insured funds' and pools' non-claim spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — members send spreadsheets and PDFs; many pools keep exposures and rates in spreadsheets (AGRiP 2025 primer)
**Target Buyer:** Pool Underwriting Manager
**Automation Potential:** High for collection, matching and validation; medium for valuation of unusual public assets

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The pool insures assets it does not own and never sees: town halls, fire stations, water and wastewater plants, pump stations, parks, bridges, fleets. Each spring it collects schedules from every member, consolidates them, and presents one statement of values to property reinsurers before the renewal. Hail, wildfire and convective storms have made property a second volatile line: CIRSA had $15.4M of 2023 member hail claims reserved while inspections continued, and raised 2024 property contributions 7.4%. Montana's schools pool did its underwriting entirely in spreadsheets before moving to Origami Risk.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Pool management systems with member portals (Origami Risk), periodic appraisals by valuation firms, reinsurance brokers' catastrophe modelling and data-quality scoring. Gaps: schedules updated by members who do not know construction or roof data; values trended by index between appraisals; unscheduled buildings found only after a loss; data-quality feedback arriving after submission instead of during collection.
