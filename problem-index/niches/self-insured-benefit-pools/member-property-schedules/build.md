# Imagery-Checked Schedules for Public Assets

**Niche:** [[niches/self-insured-benefit-pools/member-property-schedules/profile|Member Property Schedules & Reinsurance Submissions]]
**Industry:** [[industries/self-insured-benefit-pools|Self-Insured Benefit & Risk Pools]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A pool can see every member's buildings from the air, but it learns about the unscheduled maintenance shed when the hail claim arrives.
**Tags:** #cnns #object-detection #k-nearest-neighbors #gradient-boosting #feature-engineering #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver a complete, current, reinsurer-grade schedule of every member's buildings, contents, vehicles and infrastructure before the property renewal, and whoever's schedule draws the fewest valuation loadings and exclusions from property reinsurers takes the account.

## The Problem
Members forget to add new buildings, misdescribe construction and roofs, and leave values unchanged for years. The pool depends on what they report, and reinsurers price the gaps.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Valuation and imagery products are built for commercial property portfolios owned by one company, not for a pool collecting from hundreds of small public owners. Public occupancies are thinly covered in valuation data.

## What to Build
Match every scheduled location to building footprints and aerial imagery, flag structures on member parcels that are not scheduled, estimate roof type, area and condition from imagery, and estimate replacement cost by public occupancy using comparables from appraisals and claim settlements across pools. Members confirm or correct flagged items in a portal during the spring collection.

## Target Customer
Underwriting managers at multi-line municipal, county and school pools that buy property reinsurance.

## Impact If Built
Fewer surprises after losses, values that track construction costs, and a schedule reinsurers can price without loadings.
