# Captive Managers & Feasibility Advisers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-insured-benefit-pools|Self-Insured Benefit & Risk Pools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Firms that form and run captive insurers for employers, associations and groups of public entities: accounting, regulatory filings with the domicile, board support and feasibility studies, usually with an outside actuary. Brokers' captive units and independent managers dominate. Captive owners pay management fees. The actuarial opinions they rely on are logged as [[niches/insurance-tpa/self-insured-actuarial-consulting/profile|🔍 Self-Insured Actuarial Consulting]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 feasibility, finance and analytics staff per larger manager (estimate)
**Output:** Feasibility studies, captive financial statements, domicile filings, programme structure recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Financials and loss histories across the captives under management, held for each owner
**Clock:** Domicile annual statement and audit deadlines
**Buyer:** Head of Captive Consulting
