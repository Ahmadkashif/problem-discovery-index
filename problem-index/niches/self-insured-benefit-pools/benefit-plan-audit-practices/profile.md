# Employee Benefit Plan Audit Practices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-insured-benefit-pools|Self-Insured Benefit & Risk Pools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
CPA firms with specialist employee benefit plan practices that audit health and welfare funds' financial statements for the Form 5500: contributions receivable, claims and IBNR, stop-loss receivables, and TPA controls. Many also run payroll compliance audits of contributing employers, the work logged under [[niches/independent-insurance-agents/premium-audit-services/profile|🔍 Premium Audit Services]] for insurance. Funds pay audit fees from plan assets.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Specialist practices employ 20-150 benefit plan auditors (estimate), but auditors performing testing are production staff. The technical, quality and methodology group that sets audit approach is a handful of people per firm (estimate), well under 10
**Output:** Audited financial statements and opinions attached to the Form 5500, findings on claims and eligibility testing, management letters to trustees
**Proprietary data:** Audit workpapers across many funds, each held for that fund under professional confidentiality
**Clock:** The Form 5500 filing date, the last day of the seventh month after plan year end, extendable by two and a half months (recalled from DOL instructions)
**Buyer:** Employee benefit plan practice leader
