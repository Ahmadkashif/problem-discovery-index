# Public Safety Policy & Liability Content Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-insured-benefit-pools|Self-Insured Benefit & Risk Pools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Publishers of state-specific policy manuals and daily training for police, jails, fire and EMS, updated as legislation and case law change. Lexipol is the dominant provider. Its law enforcement manual runs to about 150 policies built on federal and state law, and some risk pools discount premiums for members that subscribe or help fund the subscription (Texas Law Review, 2018). Departments pay subscriptions; pools are co-funders and referral channels.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-100 attorneys, former public safety officers and content editors (estimate)
**Output:** State-specific policy manuals, update bulletins, daily training bulletins, implementation guidance
**Proprietary data:** A policy library mapped to each state's statutes and case law, plus which agencies adopted which versions
**Clock:** Effective dates of new state laws and appellate decisions that make a policy outdated
**Buyer:** Head of Content / General Counsel
