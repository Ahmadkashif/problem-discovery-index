# Commercial Rating Engines Adapted to Pool Allocation

**Niche:** [[niches/self-insured-benefit-pools/member-liability-rating/profile|Member Liability Rating & Contribution Allocation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/self-insured-benefit-pools|Self-Insured Benefit & Risk Pools]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Insurance rating engines price each risk independently; a pool divides a fixed budget among members who own the pool and approve the formula.
**Tags:** #linear-regression #regularization #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #automation #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to price each member's liability coverage close to its own risk (sworn officers, jail beds, payroll, road miles, past losses), and whoever keeps its low-loss members from leaving for commercial quotes at renewal takes the account.

## The Problem
Pools rebuild their allocation formula in spreadsheets each year because commercial rating tools do not fit how pools are financed or governed.

## What Already Exists
Carrier rating engines and the rating modules of pool and policy administration systems, which apply rates, factors and minimum premiums to exposures.

## The Customization Gap
Pool allocation must sum to the budget the actuary and board approved (loss fund at a chosen confidence level, reserve fund, reinsurance, administration); handle member equity and reserve-fund caps; cap year-on-year changes for budget-bound members; apply deductible and limit options (CIRSA added $2M, $5M and $10M choices for 2024); and publish a transparent member-by-member reconciliation for the board. The engine must show each member why its number moved.

## Target Customer
Pool finance and underwriting directors; pool administrators managing several pools.

## Impact If Solved
A defensible, repeatable allocation each renewal without a spreadsheet only one person understands.
