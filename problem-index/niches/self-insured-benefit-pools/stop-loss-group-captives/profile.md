# Medical Stop-Loss Group Captive Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-insured-benefit-pools|Self-Insured Benefit & Risk Pools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Programmes that pool mid-size self-funded employers into a captive layer between their specific deductibles and a reinsurer, returning surplus to members or assessing them in bad years. Sponsors include stop-loss carriers, brokers and independent programme managers. Medical stop-loss captives were the fastest-growing area of captive activity in SIIA's 2025 survey, cited by 62% of respondents.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 underwriters, actuaries and cost-containment analysts per programme (estimate)
**Output:** Member selection and pricing, captive layer funding, dividends and assessments, cost-containment programmes
**Proprietary data:** Member employers' claims and stop-loss experience within the programme
**Clock:** Captive renewal dates and domicile filings
**Buyer:** Programme President / Head of Underwriting
