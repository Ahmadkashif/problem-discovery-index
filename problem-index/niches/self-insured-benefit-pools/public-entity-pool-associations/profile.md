# Public Entity Pool Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-insured-benefit-pools|Self-Insured Benefit & Risk Pools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
AGRiP (the Association of Governmental Risk Pools), PRIMA (the Public Risk Management Association) and state pool associations run education, governance standards, recognition programmes and conferences for pools and public risk managers. AGRiP's estimates of 500+ pools and 80% participation date from 2014.

## Insight Function
**Size:** AGRiP's 2025 primer lists a staff of seven; PRIMA and state associations are similar or smaller
**Output:** Education, pooling standards, member surveys, conferences
**Proprietary data:** Member directories and occasional surveys
**Clock:** Conference and recognition calendars
**Buyer:** Executive Director
