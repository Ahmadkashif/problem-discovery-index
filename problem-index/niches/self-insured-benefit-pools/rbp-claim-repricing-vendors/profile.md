# Reference-Based Pricing & Claim Repricing Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-insured-benefit-pools|Self-Insured Benefit & Risk Pools]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Firms that reprice self-funded plans' hospital and out-of-network claims against a reference (typically a multiple of Medicare) rather than a network discount, defend members against balance bills, and handle provider appeals and federal dispute resolution for out-of-network claims. Health funds, public pools and their TPAs pay a per-member fee or a share of savings. The workers' compensation and liability equivalent is logged as [[niches/insurance-tpa/medical-bill-review-networks/profile|🔍 Medical Bill Review & Repricing Networks]] (55, kill switch), and the health plans' wider payment integrity work as [[niches/health-insurance-carriers/payment-integrity-vendors/profile|🔍 Outsourced Payment Integrity Vendors]]. This pocket is scored consistently with both, counting only analytical staff, and lower on labour mass and market breadth because the vendors are fewer and smaller.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-60 pricing analysts, reimbursement methodologists and data scientists per vendor (estimate), excluding coders, nurse auditors and appeals staff, who are production
**Output:** Repriced claims, line-item audit findings, provider negotiation and appeal files, savings reports
**Proprietary data:** Repricing outcomes and provider dispute histories across many plans: which hospitals accept which multiple, and which appeals succeed
**Clock:** ERISA claim-determination timelines and federal independent dispute resolution deadlines for out-of-network claims
**Buyer:** Chief Operating Officer / VP of Pricing Analytics
