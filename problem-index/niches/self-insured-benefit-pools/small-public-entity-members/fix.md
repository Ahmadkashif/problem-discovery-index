# Policies Adopted on Paper and Never Trained

**Niche:** [[niches/self-insured-benefit-pools/small-public-entity-members/profile|Small Public Entity Members Without a Risk Manager]]
**Industry:** [[industries/self-insured-benefit-pools|Self-Insured Benefit & Risk Pools]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A small department adopts a model use-of-force or pursuit policy, files it, and cannot show after a claim that any officer was trained on it.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #compliance #worker-facing #workflow-orchestration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get members with no risk manager to fix what the loss-control visit found (the playground surface, the holding-cell door, the untrained part-time officer), and whoever gets the most recommendations completed before a claim takes the account.

## The Problem
Pools encourage members to adopt current model policies for law enforcement, harassment, youth programmes and driving. Adoption is recorded; training and acknowledgement often are not. In litigation, the policy without proof of training can hurt more than it helps.

## Why It's Still Broken
Small members lack a training coordinator, part-time and seasonal staff turn over, and the pool tracks adoption because it is easy to verify.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Tie each adopted policy to a short training and acknowledgement requirement per role, push it to staff by text or email, log completion, and show the pool and the member gaps before renewal.

## Who Feels the Pain
Chiefs and clerks in small members; pool claims managers defending failure-to-train allegations; members whose contributions absorb the losses.

## Impact If Fixed
Policies that can be shown to have been trained, which narrows a common route to liability for small departments.
