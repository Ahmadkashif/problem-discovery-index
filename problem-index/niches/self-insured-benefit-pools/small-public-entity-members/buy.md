# Inspection Apps Adapted to Pool Loss Control

**Niche:** [[niches/self-insured-benefit-pools/small-public-entity-members/profile|Small Public Entity Members Without a Risk Manager]]
**Industry:** [[industries/self-insured-benefit-pools|Self-Insured Benefit & Risk Pools]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Mobile inspection apps capture checklists and photos well; they know nothing about a pool's members, its hazard taxonomy or its claims.
**Tags:** #cnns #object-detection #large-language-models #worker-facing #workflow-orchestration #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get members with no risk manager to fix what the loss-control visit found (the playground surface, the holding-cell door, the untrained part-time officer), and whoever gets the most recommendations completed before a claim takes the account.

## The Problem
Consultants use generic inspection apps or paper templates, then re-key findings into the pool's system and write narrative reports separately.

## What Already Exists
Mobile EHS inspection and audit apps with checklists, photos and action tracking, and the loss-control modules of pool management systems.

## The Customization Gap
Pools need checklists for public occupancies (playgrounds, jails, pools, public works yards, council chambers); findings coded to the pool's hazard taxonomy and linked to claim causes; reports drafted from photos and voice notes in the pool's standard language; actions owned by the member, not the inspector; and the visit history and open items of each member pre-loaded before the drive.

## Target Customer
Pool risk control directors and consultants, and pool administrators running loss control for several pools.

## Impact If Solved
Report writing drops from hours to minutes per site, and every finding becomes a tracked member action tied to claims.
