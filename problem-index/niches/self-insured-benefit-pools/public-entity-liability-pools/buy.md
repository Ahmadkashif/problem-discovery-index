**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a public entity's liability programme affordable through the police-verdict cycle, but that is two contests with different winners: closing police and civil rights claims at the lowest ultimate cost, which claims managers and defence panels win, and pricing each member close enough to its own risk that low-loss members stay, which underwriters and actuaries win. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Pools want the claim triage and litigation analytics that large carriers and TPAs use, but public entity liability follows different law and different governance.

## What Already Exists
Claims analytics vendors and carrier platforms score bodily-injury and workers' compensation claims for severity and litigation likelihood, and legal spend management tools track defence bills against guidelines.

## The Customization Gap
Public liability needs features those models lack: federal civil rights claims under §1983 with qualified immunity and Monell liability; state tort claims acts with damages caps and notice deadlines that differ by state; consent decrees and pattern-and-practice history; body-camera evidence; and settlement authority that may require a public vote by the member's governing body. Pools also need models trained or recalibrated on public entity outcomes, not on private carriers' books.

## Target Customer
Claims directors at large state pools and the TPAs that administer smaller pools.

## Impact If Solved
Pools would get triage that understands their law and their members, without commissioning a model from scratch.
