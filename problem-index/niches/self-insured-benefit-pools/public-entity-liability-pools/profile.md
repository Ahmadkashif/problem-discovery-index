# Public Entity Liability Pools

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/self-insured-benefit-pools|Self-Insured Benefit & Risk Pools]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a public entity's liability programme affordable through the police-verdict cycle, but that is two contests with different winners: closing police and civil rights claims at the lowest ultimate cost, which claims managers and defence panels win, and pricing each member close enough to its own risk that low-loss members stay, which underwriters and actuaries win. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3B a year of claims administration, panel defence, underwriting, actuarial and reinsurance-placement spend on general, auto, law enforcement, public officials and employment practices liability at US public entity pools (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~20% of self-insured funds' and pools' non-claim spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — claims and policies sit in pool management systems; loss runs, defence reports and rating still move as PDFs and spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** Pool Executive Director, reporting to a board of member officials
**Automation Potential:** Medium — intake, data and rating mechanics automate; settlement and defence strategy stay human

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Public entities formed pools because commercial insurers left them, in the 1970s and again in the 1980s (AGRiP). Liability is the line that made pooling necessary and still drives its volatility: law enforcement, jails, playgrounds, roads and employment decisions by elected bodies. Pools retain the first layer of each claim and buy reinsurance above it; CIRSA's retention was $1M, with a move to $2M in view for 2024. A police verdict cycle hits both layers at once. In Colorado, CIRSA watched settlements climb from $8.75M to $19M, lost long-time reinsurers, and charged members with police departments 150% more for 2024 law enforcement cover. New Mexico's county pool lost Bernalillo County from its liability programme after state civil rights damages expanded.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Pool management systems (Origami Risk and others), outside actuaries using paid and incurred development, panel defence firms reporting by letter, reinsurance brokers presenting loss runs. No pool holds enough seven-figure police claims to model them alone, and loss runs are not coded by allegation, so neither the pool nor its reinsurer can price police exposure from evidence.
