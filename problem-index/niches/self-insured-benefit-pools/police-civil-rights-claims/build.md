# The First-Reserve Read, Recorded and Graded

**Niche:** [[niches/self-insured-benefit-pools/police-civil-rights-claims/profile|Law Enforcement & Civil Rights Claims Management]]
**Industry:** [[industries/self-insured-benefit-pools|Self-Insured Benefit & Risk Pools]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** The most valuable judgement in a pool, the claims manager's early read of a police claim, survives only as a reserve number.
**Tags:** #tacit-knowledge-ml #gradient-boosting #large-language-models #survival-analysis #evaluation-metrics #feature-engineering #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell, within weeks of notice, which use-of-force, false-arrest and employment claims will pierce the pool's retention and to settle or harden the defence accordingly, and whoever closes those claims at the lowest ultimate cost per claim takes the account.

## The Problem
At first notice and over the following weeks, a senior claims manager forms a view: expected outcome band, likelihood of dismissal, whether to seek early settlement. The view drives reserves, reinsurer notice and defence instructions. The reasoning is not written down in structured form, so the pool cannot check it, teach it or keep it.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Pool claims departments are small and busy. Writing reasons feels like discoverable risk. Outcomes take years, so any grading tool shows value only slowly. Claims software vendors build for workflow, not for capturing judgement.

## What to Build
A confirm-in-seconds claim-read record drafted from the complaint, police report and notes: allegations, evidence, venue, plaintiff counsel, expected band and settle-or-defend lean. Each is graded against closed value and time to close. A severity model trained on the pool's history (and a consortium where available) offers a calibrated probability of piercing the retention and the most similar closed claims, with privilege-aware storage designed with counsel.

## Target Customer
Claims directors at state municipal, county and school pools, and TPAs that administer pools' liability claims.

## Impact If Built
Earlier settlement of the claims that will go badly, and a retiring manager's judgement kept as a documented, graded asset.
