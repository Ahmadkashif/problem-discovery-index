# Geographic Test Validity

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mobile-game-publishers|Mobile Game Publishers]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to know whether a number measured in a soft-launch market will hold when the game scales globally — and whoever can predict the transfer takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — convention
**Target Buyer:** Publishing and UA leads
**Automation Potential:** High — transfer modelling

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Games are validated in a handful of conventional soft-launch markets and scaled globally on the assumption that the numbers transfer. They transfer inconsistently — retention, monetisation and acquisition cost all move differently by market, by genre and by monetisation model — and no one models the relationship. The market list is convention rather than analysis, chosen years ago for reasons that were about convenience, and the scaling decision treats a number from one population as a number about all of them.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A soft-launch checklist, a small set of test geographies, and a dashboard of the same metrics per market. The gaps: no transfer model between test market and target market; no genre conditioning on that transfer; market selection unexamined; no adjustment for differing acquisition cost structures; and no record of past transfer errors.
