**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give a team that is killed dozens of times a year some way to know whether any of the work was good — and whoever provides it takes the account.

## The Problem
Other fields with high rejection rates built practices to survive them. Design studios have structured critique that separates the work from the person. Research groups run post-mortems on failed experiments and treat a negative result as information. Both maintain portfolios that record what someone produced rather than only what succeeded. These are established, documented practices, and the prototype funnel — one of the highest-rejection working environments that exists — has adopted none of them.

## What Already Exists
Structured design critique formats; failed-experiment post-mortems; portfolio and body-of-work records; mentorship and craft progression frameworks; and peer review practice.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a verdict delivered by a metric rather than by a person. It requires: (1) critique generated from telemetry rather than from a reviewer's judgement, since the rejecting party is a number and cannot explain itself — this is the substantive difference and is why human critique practice does not transfer directly; (2) a cadence of twenty to thirty rejections a year, far faster than any critique practice assumes; (3) a portfolio recording near-misses and diagnostics rather than shipped work, because almost nothing ships; (4) an explicit account of statistical noise, which design critique has no concept of and which materially matters here; and (5) progression measured against the designer's own history rather than against a body of finished work.

## Target Customer
Mobile publishers, studio and team leads, game design educators, and talent development vendors.

## Impact If Solved
High-rejection fields built critique and post-mortem practice to make rejection survivable. Here the rejecting party is a metric that cannot explain itself, so the critique has to be generated from the telemetry.
