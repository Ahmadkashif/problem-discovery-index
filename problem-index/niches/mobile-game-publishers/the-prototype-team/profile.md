# The Prototype Team

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mobile-game-publishers|Mobile Game Publishers]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give a team that is killed dozens of times a year some way to know whether any of the work was good — and whoever provides it takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — no feedback
**Target Buyer:** Studio and team leads
**Automation Potential:** Medium — feedback, attribution and craft record

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A prototype team builds a concept in three weeks, watches a retention number fall below a threshold, and starts again — twenty or thirty times a year. The only feedback is a binary verdict against a number they cannot influence directly and do not believe in. There is no craft development, no sense of improvement, no way to tell a good idea executed badly from a bad idea executed well, and the attrition that follows is treated as a fact of the sector rather than as a design failure.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A kill email, a retention dashboard and the next concept brief. The gaps: no diagnostic feedback on why a concept failed; no record of a designer's contribution across attempts; no distinction between concept and execution; no comparison against the team's own history; and no recognition for work that was competent and unlucky.
