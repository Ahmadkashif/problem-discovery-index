# External Studio Partnerships

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mobile-game-publishers|Mobile Game Publishers]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to decide which external studio and which deal terms will produce a hit, using judgement about people that nobody has ever converted into evidence — and whoever does takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.5B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — relationships
**Target Buyer:** Partner managers
**Automation Potential:** Medium — selection, terms and portfolio

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A large share of published titles come from external studios rather than internal teams, and the decision to sign one is made on a pitch, a playable build and a partner manager's read of the team. Deal terms — advance, revenue share, milestone structure, platform rights — are negotiated per relationship with little reference to what past terms produced. It is the part of the publishing business that runs on judgement about people, and the one where none of that judgement has been written down.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A pipeline spreadsheet, a deal template, a partner manager's relationships and a milestone schedule in a contract. The gaps: no record of which signals in a pitch predicted outcome; no analysis of which deal structures produced better results; no view of the studio's delivery history; no portfolio-level risk view; and no post-mortem loop from outcome back to signing decision.
