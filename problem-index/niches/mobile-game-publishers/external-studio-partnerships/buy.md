# Deal and Portfolio Analytics From Venture

**Niche:** [[niches/mobile-game-publishers/external-studio-partnerships/profile|External Studio Partnerships]]
**Industry:** [[industries/mobile-game-publishers|Mobile Game Publishers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Venture firms instrumented their pipelines, terms and partner judgement years ago, and publishing partner teams run on a spreadsheet.
**Tags:** #data-integration #evaluation-metrics #logistic-regression #confidence-intervals #revenue-impact #descriptive-statistics #causal-inference #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to decide which external studio and which deal terms will produce a hit, using judgement about people that nobody has ever converted into evidence — and whoever does takes the account.

## The Problem
Venture capital has the same shape: a pipeline of pitches, a small number of investments, terms negotiated per deal, outcomes arriving years later, and returns concentrated in a few winners. The industry instrumented it — deal flow CRM, structured diligence records, term benchmarking against market data, portfolio construction analysis, and partner-level attribution of outcomes. A publishing partner team makes structurally identical decisions and has none of it.

## What Already Exists
Deal flow pipeline management; structured diligence and decision records; term sheet benchmarking; portfolio construction and concentration analysis; and partner-level performance attribution.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is from an equity stake to a publishing agreement, where the publisher also operates the asset. It requires: (1) a revenue share and advance structure with milestone gates rather than an equity round — the publisher participates in production and can intervene, which venture largely cannot, and that is the substantive difference; (2) a product outcome measured in monthly revenue rather than an exit event; (3) delivery risk as a first-class factor, since a studio missing milestones is the commonest failure and has no clean venture analogue; (4) a portfolio concentrated in genre and platform rather than in sector and stage; and (5) a far shorter feedback cycle, measured in quarters rather than in fund lifetimes.

## Target Customer
Mobile publishers, publishing groups, first-party publishing arms, and venture and deal analytics vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Venture instrumented pipeline, terms and partner judgement because returns were concentrated and outcomes were slow. Delivery risk and the publisher's ability to intervene in production are what make it a different model.
