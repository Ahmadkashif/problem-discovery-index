# The Monetisation Manager

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mobile-game-publishers|Mobile Game Publishers]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give the person tuning monetisation an instrument that says whether the small group producing most of the revenue is all right — and whoever builds it takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.5B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium, one-sided
**Target Buyer:** Monetisation managers
**Automation Potential:** High — welfare signals and guardrails

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A monetisation manager is targeted on revenue per player, can see that most of it comes from a very small group, and has no instrument that tells them whether any individual in that group is fine. Every tool they have measures the effect of a design on revenue; none measures its effect on the person. This is now also a regulatory exposure in several jurisdictions, but the immediate problem is a professional one: a capable person making consequential decisions about strangers with half the information.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Revenue dashboards, spend distribution charts, A/B testing frameworks and offer configuration tools. The gaps: no per-player welfare signal; no distinction between enthusiastic and distressed spending; no guardrail at the individual level; no feedback from players who stopped; and no professional standard for what the manager should not do.
