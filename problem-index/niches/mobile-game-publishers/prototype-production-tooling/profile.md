# Prototype Production Tooling

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mobile-game-publishers|Mobile Game Publishers]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to cut the cost and time of getting a testable prototype in front of a paid audience, because the funnel's economics are set by that number — and whoever cuts it takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — internal
**Target Buyer:** Engineering leads
**Automation Potential:** Very high — templates, pipelines and instrumentation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A publisher running dozens of prototypes a month is operating a factory, and the unit cost of a testable build determines how many concepts it can afford to try. Most of that cost is not the idea — it is rebuilding the same onboarding, the same analytics, the same store scaffolding, the same build and submission pipeline for every concept. The tooling is internal, inconsistent across teams, and almost entirely mechanical, which makes it the most automatable part of the business and one of the least invested in.

## Current Tools & Gaps
An engine, some internal templates of varying age, a build pipeline someone maintains, and a store submission process. The gaps: instrumentation reimplemented per prototype; no shared component library; slow build and submission turnaround; test campaign setup manual; and no standard scaffold for the parts every concept needs.
