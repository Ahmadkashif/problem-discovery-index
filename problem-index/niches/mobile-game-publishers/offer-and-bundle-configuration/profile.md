# Offer & Bundle Configuration

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mobile-game-publishers|Mobile Game Publishers]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to choose which offer to put in front of which player at which moment, a decision made thousands of times a day by hand-written rules — and whoever automates it well takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.5B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~6% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — manual config
**Target Buyer:** Live product managers
**Automation Potential:** Very high — targeting, pricing and scheduling

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The offer layer — limited-time bundles, starter packs, escalating value ladders, event passes — is configured by hand in a live operations tool: a product manager decides the contents, the price point, the audience segment and the schedule. The decision recurs constantly, has immediate measurable feedback, and is made from a small set of segments and a lot of habit. It is the most mechanical revenue decision in a live game and the least automated.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A live ops configuration console, a segment list, a price point table and a calendar. The gaps: coarse segmentation; price points chosen by convention; bundle contents composed by intuition; scheduling by calendar rather than by player state; and no learning from the offers that were declined.
