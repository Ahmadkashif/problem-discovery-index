# Next Best Offer From Retail and Telecoms

**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Retail and telecoms have run automated next-best-offer decisioning for years, and live games configure bundles in a console.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to choose which offer to put in front of which player at which moment, a decision made thousands of times a day by hand-written rules — and whoever automates it well takes the account.

## The Problem
Next best offer is a mature commercial discipline. Retailers and telecoms operators decide, per customer and per moment, which offer to present from a catalogue — with propensity models, uplift modelling, eligibility and frequency rules, contact fatigue management and permanent control groups. The vendors are established and the practice is standard. A live mobile game has richer per-customer state than either industry and selects offers from a handful of hand-drawn segments.

## What Already Exists
Propensity and uplift modelling; offer eligibility and arbitration engines; contact frequency and fatigue rules; permanent holdout control groups; and offer catalogue management.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a customer whose need is generated by the product itself, in a session measured in minutes. It requires: (1) offer relevance driven by in-game progression state, so the need is manufactured by the game's own design rather than discovered — this is the substantive difference and it also raises the ethical question retail does not face; (2) sub-second in-session decisioning rather than a campaign cadence; (3) bundle composition as a decision variable, where retail arbitrates between fixed offers; (4) revenue concentration so extreme that the optimum is dominated by a few players, which changes what the model should maximise; and (5) a welfare guardrail constraining the optimiser, which next-best-offer practice has no standard equivalent for.

## Target Customer
Mobile publishers, live ops platform vendors, monetisation tooling providers, and next-best-offer decisioning vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Next best offer is mature practice with established vendors. A need manufactured by the game's own design, decided in-session, with a welfare guardrail on the optimiser, is what the borrowed engine does not come with.
