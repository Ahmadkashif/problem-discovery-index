# Survivorship Correction

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mobile-game-publishers|Mobile Game Publishers]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to find out what the killed concepts would have done, because every threshold in the industry is validated against the population that passed it — and whoever assembles that evidence takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.2B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — the data does not exist
**Target Buyer:** Publishing leadership and finance
**Automation Potential:** Medium — experimental design, not modelling

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Nobody knows what the killed games would have done, because none of them were allowed to continue. Every threshold, every model and every piece of publishing intuition is calibrated on the survivors, and the survivors were selected by the very rule being evaluated. This is not a modelling problem — no amount of cleverness recovers an outcome that was never observed. It is an experimental design and data acquisition problem, and its solution costs money visibly and saves it invisibly, which is why nobody has funded it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Nothing. The killed concepts leave a test cohort and a decision record and no outcome. The gaps: no holdout policy; no continuation funding for below-threshold concepts; no follow-up on concepts killed then shipped elsewhere; no external outcome data joined back; and no statistical correction for the censoring in any model built on the record.
