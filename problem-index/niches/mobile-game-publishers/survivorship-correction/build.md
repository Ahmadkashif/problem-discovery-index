# Deliberately Funding the Failures

**Niche:** [[niches/mobile-game-publishers/survivorship-correction/profile|Survivorship Correction]]
**Industry:** [[industries/mobile-game-publishers|Mobile Game Publishers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Every threshold in mobile publishing is validated against the population that passed it, and the only way to fix that is to let some failures through on purpose.
**Tags:** #causal-inference #monte-carlo-methods #hypothesis-testing #confidence-intervals #evaluation-metrics #survival-analysis #revenue-impact #bayesian-inference
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to find out what the killed concepts would have done, because every threshold in the industry is validated against the population that passed it — and whoever assembles that evidence takes the account.

## The Problem
A publisher's entire selection apparatus is trained and validated on games that cleared the gate. The counterfactual — what the ninety percent that were killed would have earned — is unobserved by construction. This makes every threshold unfalsifiable: the games that passed and succeeded confirm it, the games that passed and failed are blamed on execution, and the games that were killed contribute nothing. No model can fix this, because the information was never collected.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
It requires knowingly spending money on concepts the current rule says to kill, and that cost appears in this quarter while the benefit is diffuse and arrives later. There is no individual whose objective it improves. The sample needed is larger than any single portfolio decision. And admitting the evidence base is censored implies past decisions may have been wrong.

## What to Build
Buy the counterfactual on purpose, at a controlled price. Run a randomised continuation holdout — a small fraction of below-threshold concepts funded to the next stage regardless of their number — which is the core and is the only mechanism that produces genuinely unbiased evidence. Size the holdout as a stated annual budget with an expected information return, so it can be defended as a line item rather than as an indulgence. Stratify the holdout near the threshold, where the decisions are marginal and the information density is highest. Follow concepts killed then published elsewhere and join their outcomes back, since that is free observational counterfactual the industry currently ignores. Correct existing models statistically for the censoring, which is the interim measure while the holdout accumulates. Report threshold error rates in both directions rather than only the visible one, as the industry measures bad passes and never bad kills. Pool evidence across publishers where the commercial structure permits, because no single portfolio produces enough marginal cases. Publish the holdout's findings internally on a fixed cadence so the spend is visibly earning. Attach an expected value to the information itself, which is how this gets approved. And keep the holdout running rather than treating it as a one-off study, since the thresholds and the market both move.

## Target Customer
Mobile publishers, publishing groups with multiple labels, platform holders with portfolio programmes, and games research and consortium bodies.

## Impact If Built
No model can fix an outcome that was never observed, which is why the thresholds are unfalsifiable. A small randomised continuation holdout, stratified near the threshold, is the only mechanism that produces evidence about the kills.
