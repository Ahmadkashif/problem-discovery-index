# Early Signal Modelling

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mobile-game-publishers|Mobile Game Publishers]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to predict a game's 180-day outcome from its first three days of player behaviour, and whoever makes that prediction reliable enough to act on takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.8B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~11% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** High — telemetry without modelling
**Target Buyer:** Publishing leadership and data science
**Automation Potential:** Very high — prediction from existing telemetry

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The publisher already holds everything the first days of a test produce: session lengths, progression, churn timing, early monetisation, funnel shape. It reduces that to a single retention figure and compares it to a threshold. The contest is what the full early signal actually implies about eventual performance — a prediction problem over data the publisher already owns, sold as a tool to the organisation that generated it. It is separable from the question of whether that data is representative, which is the sibling niche's problem entirely.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Cohort dashboards, retention curves, a day-one and day-seven figure, and analyst judgement. The gaps: nearly all early telemetry discarded in favour of one number; no genre conditioning; no uncertainty quantification; no modelling of the curve's shape as opposed to its level; and no calibration against realised outcomes.
