# Hybrid Monetisation Balance

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/mobile-game-publishers|Mobile Game Publishers]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to set the balance between advertising and in-app purchase when the two compete for the same player's attention and every test measures only one of them — and whoever measures both together takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$5.5B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~22% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** High, single-variable
**Target Buyer:** Monetisation leads
**Automation Potential:** High — joint optimisation and measurement

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Hybrid monetisation is now the default: a game earns from rewarded video, interstitials and in-app purchases simultaneously. The two revenue streams draw on the same finite player attention and session time, and an ad shown is sometimes a purchase not made. The industry tunes them with A/B tests that measure one at a time, on separate teams, with separate targets. Nobody optimises the joint outcome, and the interaction is where the money is.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Mediation platforms, ad placement configuration, IAP catalogues, and A/B testing frameworks pointed at a single metric. The gaps: no joint objective; substitution between ad revenue and purchase revenue unmeasured; separate teams with conflicting targets; no per-segment balance; and long-horizon retention effects excluded from every test.
