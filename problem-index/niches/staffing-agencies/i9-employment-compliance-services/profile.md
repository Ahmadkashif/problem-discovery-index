# I-9 & Employment Eligibility Compliance Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/staffing-agencies|Staffing Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Manage employment eligibility verification at scale — form completion, electronic verification, correction of defective records, and audit defence — for an industry that onboards enormous numbers of workers and faces per-form penalties for getting it wrong.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 compliance analysts, immigration specialists, and audit staff
**Output:** Verification processing, defect identification and remediation, internal audit programmes, government audit response, penalty mitigation
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated audit outcomes — which defects were penalised, at what level, by which enforcement office
**Clock:** Statutory completion deadlines measured in days from hire, and audit response windows measured in days from notice
**Buyer:** VP of Compliance Services
