# Labour Market & Skills Taxonomy Data Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/staffing-agencies|Staffing Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Maintain the occupation and skills taxonomies and the job posting and profile datasets that describe what the labour market is demanding, where, and at what pay — the market map a staffing agency's territory and pricing decisions rest on.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-800 taxonomists, data scientists, economists, and data operations staff
**Output:** Skills and occupation taxonomies, job posting analytics, talent supply and demand estimates, wage benchmarks, workforce planning data
**Proprietary data:** Continuously maintained taxonomies and deduplicated posting corpora assembled over years
**Clock:** Monthly and quarterly reporting against workforce planning cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / Head of Labour Market Research
