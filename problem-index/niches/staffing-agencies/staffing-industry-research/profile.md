# Staffing Industry Research & Advisory

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/staffing-agencies|Staffing Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Size and segment the contingent workforce market, benchmark bill rates and gross margins by skill and geography, and publish the buyer and supplier research the industry plans against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 analysts, researchers, and consultants
**Output:** Market sizing and forecasts, bill rate and margin benchmarks, buyer surveys, supplier league tables, workforce structure research
**Proprietary data:** Confidential rate and margin submissions from agencies and buyer programmes, accumulated over decades in a market with no published pricing
**Clock:** Quarterly and annual publication against planning cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Research Officer / VP of Research
