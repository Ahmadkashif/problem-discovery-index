# Job Board & Talent Marketplace Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/staffing-agencies|Staffing Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator/rollup

## What They Do
The data organisations inside the job boards and professional networks, running matching, ranking, pricing and labour market research over essentially the entire visible supply and demand of the US labour market.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1,000-5,000 data scientists, economists, machine learning engineers, and researchers
**Output:** Search and matching models, job recommendation, employer advertising pricing, hiring demand indices, published labour market research
**Proprietary data:** Job postings, applications, profiles, messages and hiring outcomes at national scale
**Clock:** Continuous, with hiring seasonality
**Buyer:** VP of Data Science / Chief Economist
