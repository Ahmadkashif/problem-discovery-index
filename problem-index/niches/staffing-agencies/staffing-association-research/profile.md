# Staffing Association Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/staffing-agencies|Staffing Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Publish employment statistics for the temporary workforce, member operating benchmarks and legislative analysis, largely in service of advocacy on classification and co-employment rules.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-25 researchers and policy analysts
**Output:** Temporary employment statistics, member operating surveys, legislative and regulatory analysis, workforce studies
**Proprietary data:** Member submissions at moderate participation
**Clock:** Quarterly statistics and legislative sessions
**Buyer:** VP of Research
