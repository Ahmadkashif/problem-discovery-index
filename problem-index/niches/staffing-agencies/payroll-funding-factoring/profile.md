# Staffing Payroll Funding & Factoring

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/staffing-agencies|Staffing Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Advance against staffing receivables so an agency can make weekly payroll while clients pay in forty-five days — the financing structure most of the industry runs on, and a credit decision on the agency's clients rather than the agency.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 credit analysts, underwriters, and collections staff
**Output:** Advance and credit decisions, client debtor credit limits, concentration and dilution analysis, collections
**Proprietary data:** Payment behaviour of thousands of staffing clients across agencies, plus a record of which agencies failed and why
**Clock:** Weekly payroll and daily funding decisions
**Buyer:** Chief Credit Officer
