# Staffing Platform & ATS Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/staffing-agencies|Staffing Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The analytics functions inside the applicant tracking and staffing operations platforms agencies run on, producing fill rate, redeployment and margin analytics plus industry benchmark reports.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 data scientists, analysts, and product researchers
**Output:** Requisition and fill analytics, candidate matching, redeployment and retention modelling, margin reporting, published benchmarks
**Proprietary data:** Requisition, submission, placement, assignment tenure and redeployment records across thousands of agencies
**Clock:** Requisition urgency and weekly payroll cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Chief Product Officer
