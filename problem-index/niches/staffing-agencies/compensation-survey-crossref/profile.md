# Compensation Survey Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/staffing-agencies|Staffing Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Collect pay data from employers under confidentiality and publish the benchmarks that set bill rates, pay rates and the spread between them across every job the industry places.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 survey methodologists, job matchers, analysts, and consultants
**Output:** Pay benchmarks by job, level and geography, survey participation reporting, pay structure design, market pricing
**Proprietary data:** Employer pay submissions matched to a maintained job architecture over decades, in a market where nobody publishes what they pay
**Clock:** Annual survey and compensation planning cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / Practice Leader
