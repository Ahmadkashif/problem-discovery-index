# Background Screening Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/staffing-agencies|Staffing Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Run the criminal, employment, education and drug screening that gates every placement, at the volume a high-turnover, high-throughput industry generates — screening is often the pacing item between an offer and a start date.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-2,000 court researchers, verification specialists, compliance analysts, and data scientists
**Output:** Criminal and civil record reports, employment and education verification, drug screening administration, continuous monitoring, adjudication matrices
**Proprietary data:** Court record acquisition infrastructure across thousands of jurisdictions, plus accumulated adjudication decisions
**Clock:** Start dates — a screen that takes an extra day can lose the placement
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Compliance
