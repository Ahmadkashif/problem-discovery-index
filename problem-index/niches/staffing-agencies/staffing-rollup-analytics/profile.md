# Staffing Group Corporate Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/staffing-agencies|Staffing Agencies]]

## What They Do
Corporate analytics inside the large staffing groups — branch and desk productivity, gross margin by client and skill, recruiter performance, and acquisition screening across hundreds of branches.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 analysts, data scientists, and corporate development staff
**Output:** Branch and recruiter performance reporting, client and skill margin analysis, pricing guidance, acquisition modelling, workforce demand forecasting
**Proprietary data:** Placement, margin and tenure records across a very large branch network
**Clock:** Weekly billing cycles and quarterly reporting
**Buyer:** VP of Analytics
