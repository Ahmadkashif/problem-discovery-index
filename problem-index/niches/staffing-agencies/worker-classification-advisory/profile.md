# Worker Classification & Co-Employment Advisory

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/staffing-agencies|Staffing Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Advise on whether workers are employees or contractors, and on the co-employment exposure a staffing arrangement creates — a determination that differs by federal agency, by state, and by the specific facts of each assignment.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 employment lawyers, consultants, and compliance analysts
**Output:** Classification determinations, co-employment risk assessments, contract and assignment structuring, audit and litigation support
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated determinations and their outcomes across agencies and states
**Clock:** Audit notices, litigation deadlines, and legislative effective dates
**Buyer:** Practice Leader
