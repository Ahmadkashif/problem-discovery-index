# Contingent Workforce MSP & VMS Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/staffing-agencies|Staffing Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Run the programmes through which large employers buy contingent labour — supplier panels, rate cards, requisition distribution and performance scoring. For a staffing agency this is not a customer but a gatekeeper: the programme decides which agencies see a requisition, at what rate, and whether they stay on the panel.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,500 programme analysts, rate benchmarking staff, supplier managers, and data scientists
**Output:** Rate card design and benchmarking, supplier scorecards, requisition and fill analytics, savings reporting, worker classification compliance
**Proprietary data:** Requisition, submission, fill, rate and tenure data across many suppliers and clients — the only comparative view of contingent labour pricing and supplier performance
**Clock:** Requisition cycle times and programme reporting periods
**Buyer:** VP of Programme Analytics / Chief Data Officer
