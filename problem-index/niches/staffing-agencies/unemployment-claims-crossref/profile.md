# Unemployment Claims Management

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/staffing-agencies|Staffing Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Contest unemployment claims on employers' behalf across fifty state systems within statutory response windows — an outsized cost for staffing agencies, where assignments end constantly and every ending is a potential claim.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,500 claims analysts, hearing representatives, and tax consultants
**Output:** Claim protests and position determinations, hearing representation, benefit charge auditing, tax rate forecasting and planning
**Proprietary data:** Millions of handled claims with separation facts, position taken, state, determination and appeal outcome
**Clock:** Statutory response deadlines measured in days, with forfeiture as the penalty
**Buyer:** Chief Analytics Officer / VP of Operations
