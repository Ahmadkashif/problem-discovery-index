## The Problem
Making consistent decisions across many similar matters, retrieving what was decided before and why, and defending a decision years later is what legal knowledge management is for, and the tooling is mature: matter management, precedent databases, clause and decision libraries, and increasingly model-assisted retrieval over an organisation's own prior work. Extraction compliance reviewers decide from memory and file the outcome in an email.

## What Already Exists
Matter and decision management platforms; precedent and knowledge libraries with structured retrieval; contract and terms analysis tooling; regulatory change tracking services; model-assisted research over an organisation's own materials; and approval workflow with audit trail.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a high-volume, low-value-per-decision review on unsettled questions. It requires: (1) volume-appropriate process, since legal tooling assumes a matter is worth hours and here a decision must take minutes for most requests and hours for the few that matter — triage into those tiers is the practical requirement; (2) the target website's own terms as a primary input, parsed and tracked over time, which no legal platform ingests and which is the most decision-relevant document; (3) explicit representation of unsettledness, because the platforms assume a rule exists and the honest output here is frequently a risk position rather than a conclusion; (4) precedent indexed by factual features rather than by legal citation, since the reviewer's question is what did we decide about a request like this one; and (5) commercial consequence recorded alongside the decision, so the firm can see what its risk posture costs and price it rather than absorbing pressure into individual judgements.

## Target Customer
Legal and compliance functions, extraction firms, and the legal operations software community for whom high-volume technical compliance is an unserved shape.

## Impact If Solved
Legal knowledge management assumes a matter worth hours and a rule that exists, and neither holds here. Precedent indexed by factual features answers the reviewer's actual question, and recording commercial consequence makes the risk posture a priced decision rather than a personal pressure.
