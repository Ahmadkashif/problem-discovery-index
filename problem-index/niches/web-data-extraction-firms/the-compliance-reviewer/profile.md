# The Compliance Reviewer

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/web-data-extraction-firms|Web Data Extraction Firms]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give the reviewer a precedent library and a framework instead of an unsettled legal landscape and a deadline — and whoever does that takes the account, because this decision gates every collection the firm runs.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$180M US in review cost and refused or mispriced work
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of category revenue equivalent
**Digital Adoption:** None — unsettled law, no tooling
**Target Buyer:** Legal and compliance functions at the firms
**Automation Potential:** Medium — the evidence and precedent are automatable, the judgement is not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A compliance reviewer approves or refuses collection requests against a legal landscape that is genuinely unsettled — the reach of computer misuse statutes over public data, contractual terms of use, copyright in collected content, privacy law over personal data, and the live question of model training — with no tooling, no precedent library and commercial pressure on one side of every decision. They make the same judgement repeatedly about similar requests and cannot see what they or their colleagues decided before. They are the control that makes this industry's risk manageable, operating with nothing, under pressure, on questions whose answers genuinely are not settled.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Legal research, outside counsel on significant questions, a request form, and the reviewer's own memory. The gaps: no precedent library of the firm's own decisions, so consistency is accidental; no structured request intake, so each arrives in a different shape; no automated evidence gathering about the target; no tracking of the legal landscape against affected decisions; and no way to express a graduated outcome, so the decision is binary when the reality is not.
