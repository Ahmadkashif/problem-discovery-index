# Request Log Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/web-data-extraction-firms|Web Data Extraction Firms]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a complete record of every request the firm makes into knowledge about targets, cost and risk — and whoever does that operates on evidence while everyone else operates on the last incident.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$250M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** None — complete records, no reasoning
**Target Buyer:** The firms themselves, and their customers as beneficiaries
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the logs are complete and structured

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
These firms hold a complete record of every request they make: which host, at what rate, from which address type, with what outcome, for which customer, with what extraction result. That record answers what nobody currently knows — which targets are hostile and becoming more so, what a collection truly costs per useful record, which targets are fragile and will break again, which customers' collections carry the most risk, and where the fleet's capacity is being consumed. The firms have built almost nothing that reasons about it. Every other problem in this industry — correctness, governance, repair, cost — is a question this corpus can answer and nobody is asking.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Billing aggregation, operational dashboards showing current failures, and per-incident investigation. The gaps: no target intelligence, so hostility and fragility are learned by being surprised; no cost model per target per customer; no risk scoring of collections; no prediction of which extractions will break next; and no feedback from the corpus into scheduling, routing or pricing.
