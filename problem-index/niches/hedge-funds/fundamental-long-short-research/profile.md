# Fundamental Long/Short Equity Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hedge-funds|Hedge Funds]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to become the analyst's single research surface — filings, transcripts, expert calls, broker research and the fund's own prior notes searchable together — and whoever puts the fund's own research memory next to the external corpus takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$4B global spend on research data, content and tooling by fundamental equity funds (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~35% of hedge fund research and data spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High for external content, low for the fund's own research record
**Target Buyer:** CIO, Director of Research, Head of Research Technology
**Automation Potential:** High — most of the reading is automatable; the judgement is not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Long/short equity is the largest hedge fund strategy by number of funds and analysts, and its research workflow is the archetype: an analyst covers a list of companies, reads everything published about them, talks to experts and management, builds a model, forms a thesis and argues it to a PM. The tools that serve it compete for the analyst's attention — AlphaSense for search across documents and expert transcripts, Bloomberg and FactSet for data, Visible Alpha for consensus detail, Bipsync and similar systems for notes. What distinguishes this niche from the rest of the fund is that the contest is for the research surface itself: the place the analyst starts the day.

## Current Tools & Gaps
External search is excellent and has consolidated: AlphaSense absorbed Sentieo (2022) and Tegus (2024), combining broker research, filings, transcripts and expert calls. The gap is the inside: the fund's own notes, model history and theses are held in a separate system or none, so the analyst can search everything ever published about a company except what their own firm concluded about it last year.
