# Legal AI Taught to Compute Baskets, Not Just Find Clauses

**Niche:** [[niches/hedge-funds/credit-intelligence-services/profile|Credit & Distressed Intelligence Services]]
**Industry:** [[industries/hedge-funds|Hedge Funds]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** General legal AI finds the clause; a covenant analyst's job is to resolve the definitions and compute what the clause permits.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #bert #evaluation-metrics #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this pocket is fighting to publish a reliable reading of a new credit document or restructuring filing before the market trades on it — and whoever reads fastest without losing legal accuracy takes the subscription.

## The Problem
New issues and amendments arrive in waves and every analyst reads each document end to end. Legal AI platforms speed up locating provisions but not the computation of debt, lien and payment capacity that the review actually reports.

## What Already Exists
Legal AI and contract analytics platforms used across law firms and legal departments.

## The Customization Gap
Definition resolution across the whole document; basket arithmetic from defined EBITDA; a house taxonomy of LME-enabling provisions; and output in the service's standard review format with clause citations.

## Target Customer
Covenant research teams at credit intelligence services.

## Impact If Solved
More documents covered per analyst, including the smaller issues currently skipped.
