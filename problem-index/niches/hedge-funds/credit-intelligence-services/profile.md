# Credit & Distressed Intelligence Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hedge-funds|Hedge Funds]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Subscription services that read credit documents, court dockets and restructuring situations for credit and distressed investors: covenant analysis, liability-management and restructuring news, legal analysis of filings, and recovery research. Octus (formerly Reorg), CreditSights (Fitch Solutions, including the former Covenant Review), 9fin and Debtwire (ION Analytics) are representative.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 150-700 per firm across legal analysts, credit analysts, reporters and data teams
**Output:** Covenant reviews per new issue and amendment, restructuring and LME coverage, docket analysis, recovery and capital-structure research
**Proprietary data:** An archive of structured covenant analyses across thousands of instruments and a record of resolved restructurings with their documents, paths and recoveries
**Clock:** New-issue launch and pricing windows, amendment and consent deadlines, and bankruptcy court dates
**Buyer:** Head of Research / Head of Legal & Covenant Analysis
