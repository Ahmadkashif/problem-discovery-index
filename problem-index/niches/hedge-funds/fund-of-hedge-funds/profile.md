# Fund-of-Hedge-Funds Manager Selection

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hedge-funds|Hedge Funds]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Multi-manager allocators that build portfolios of hedge funds for institutional clients, such as GCM Grosvenor and Blackstone's hedge fund solutions business, with manager research and portfolio construction teams.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-200 per firm
**Output:** Portfolios of hedge funds, manager research, risk reporting
**Proprietary data:** Long histories of manager meetings, ODD and portfolio transparency
**Clock:** Client reporting and redemption terms
**Buyer:** CIO / Head of Manager Research
