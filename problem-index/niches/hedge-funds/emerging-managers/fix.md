# The Due Diligence Questionnaire Answered From Scratch

**Niche:** [[niches/hedge-funds/emerging-managers/profile|Emerging Managers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/hedge-funds|Hedge Funds]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Every allocator sends a long due diligence questionnaire, and a small fund answers each one by hand from its last answers.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #word-embeddings #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a launch-stage fund through allocator operational due diligence on day one with a research and compliance stack its management fee can carry — and whoever becomes the default launch stack keeps the fund for its lifetime.

## The Problem
Due diligence questionnaires — commonly built on industry templates such as AIMA's — run to many pages and arrive from every prospective investor in slightly different form. A small fund's COO spends weeks a year answering them.

## Why It's Still Broken
Answers live in old Word files, and every allocator's version differs enough to defeat copy and paste.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A maintained answer library linked to source documents, with draft responses generated to each new questionnaire and changes since the last answer highlighted for review.

## Who Feels the Pain
COOs and founders of small funds raising capital.

## Impact If Fixed
Weeks of questionnaire work become days, during the period when the fund can least spare the time.
