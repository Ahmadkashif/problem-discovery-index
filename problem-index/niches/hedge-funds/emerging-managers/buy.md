# Outsourced Compliance With Research Clearance Built In

**Niche:** [[niches/hedge-funds/emerging-managers/profile|Emerging Managers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/hedge-funds|Hedge Funds]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Outsourced CCO services cover filings and codes of ethics; the research-input clearance a fundamental fund needs is left to the founder.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a launch-stage fund through allocator operational due diligence on day one with a research and compliance stack its management fee can carry — and whoever becomes the default launch stack keeps the fund for its lifetime.

## The Problem
Small funds outsource compliance to consultants who handle registrations, Form ADV, codes of ethics and annual reviews. Expert-call pre-clearance and dataset provenance review fall back on the PM, who is also the main user of those inputs.

## What Already Exists
Outsourced CCO and compliance consulting firms and compliance management software.

## The Customization Gap
Adding research-input clearance — expert call triage, transcript review, dataset policy checks — delivered as a service with the consultant reviewing only flagged items, scaled to a fund with a handful of calls a week.

## Target Customer
Emerging managers and the compliance consultancies that serve them.

## Impact If Solved
Small funds get an independent, documented MNPI process without hiring, which is something allocators check.
