# An Institutional Research Record for a Three-Person Fund

**Niche:** [[niches/hedge-funds/emerging-managers/profile|Emerging Managers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/hedge-funds|Hedge Funds]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** An allocator asks a new fund to evidence its research process, and the evidence is the founder's memory.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #workflow-orchestration #compliance #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a launch-stage fund through allocator operational due diligence on day one with a research and compliance stack its management fee can carry — and whoever becomes the default launch stack keeps the fund for its lifetime.

## The Problem
Allocators evaluating emerging managers look for a repeatable process: how ideas are sourced, how theses are documented, how positions are sized and exited. A small fund has the process but not the record, because recording costs time it does not have.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Research management platforms are sold on seat counts and implementation projects that small funds cannot justify.

## What to Build
A lightweight research record that captures theses and decisions as a by-product of normal work — notes, model changes, trades — and produces, on demand, an allocator-ready evidence pack: idea pipeline, documented theses, hit rates and process consistency over time.

## Target Customer
Founders and COOs of funds in their first five years, and seeding platforms that back them.

## Impact If Built
The fund can evidence its process to allocators from its first trade, which shortens fundraising for exactly the firms where fundraising is existential.
