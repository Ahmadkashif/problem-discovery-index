# Emerging Managers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hedge-funds|Hedge Funds]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a launch-stage fund through allocator operational due diligence on day one with a research and compliance stack its management fee can carry — and whoever becomes the default launch stack keeps the fund for its lifetime.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M global spend on research, data and compliance infrastructure by funds under ~$1B AUM (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of hedge fund research and data spend (estimate), spread across thousands of small firms
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — cloud tools, little infrastructure
**Target Buyer:** Founder/CIO, COO
**Automation Potential:** High — small teams need leverage most

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Most hedge funds by count are small: a founding PM, one or two analysts, and an outsourced COO/CCO. They launch into an allocator market that expects institutional-grade operations, research documentation and compliance — the same operational due diligence a large fund passes with dedicated teams. The tooling market prices for large funds, and the emerging manager buys a terminal and improvises the rest.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Bloomberg or FactSet, prime-broker-provided tools, fund administrators, outsourced compliance consultants and cloud office tools. Gaps: no affordable research management or compliance tooling designed for a three-person fund; documentation demanded by allocators assembled by hand; and research process that cannot be evidenced to an allocator.
