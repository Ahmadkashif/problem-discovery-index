# Entity Resolution Tuned for Merchants and Tickers

**Niche:** [[niches/hedge-funds/alt-data-evaluation/profile|Alternative Data Evaluation & Onboarding]]
**Industry:** [[industries/hedge-funds|Hedge Funds]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Entity resolution tools match names; alternative data needs merchant strings, brands and apps mapped to securities through time.
**Tags:** #word-embeddings #k-nearest-neighbors #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to prove or disprove, inside the trial window and on the fund's own universe, whether a dataset carries information not already in consensus — and whoever produces a defensible verdict before the trial expires decides which data gets bought.

## The Problem
Card panels report merchant descriptors, app datasets report app names, web datasets report domains. A fund needs each mapped to the listed company that earns the revenue, accounting for brands, subsidiaries, acquisitions and divestitures, and for the date each relationship held.

## What Already Exists
General entity resolution and master data tools, security master and symbology services, and vendors' own ticker tags of varying quality.

## The Customization Gap
Point-in-time corporate hierarchies; brand-to-parent relationships; handling of private companies and franchisees; confidence-scored suggestions reviewed once and reused across datasets; and change alerts when corporate actions break a mapping.

## Target Customer
Central data teams at funds licensing several consumer and web datasets.

## Impact If Solved
Mapping becomes a maintained asset rather than a per-dataset chore, which removes the largest single delay in evaluation.
