# Earnings Model Maintenance

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hedge-funds|Hedge Funds]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a reported quarter into the analyst's own model template, correctly mapped and source-linked, before the conference call starts — and whoever does it in the analyst's template rather than a vendor's takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$500M global spend on model data, extraction and earnings tooling by hedge funds (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~4% of hedge fund research and data spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — vendor model data is widely licensed, analyst models remain hand-maintained
**Target Buyer:** Director of Research, PM
**Automation Potential:** Very High — extraction and mapping are mechanical

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every fundamental analyst maintains company models, and every quarter those models must be updated with reported actuals and guidance within hours of release. Vendors including Daloopa, Visible Alpha (S&P Global) and Canalyst (now within AlphaSense) supply standardised historical model data. The analyst's own template — with its custom segments, KPIs and drivers — is where the work actually happens, and that is the contested ground.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Vendor model data and Excel add-ins, XBRL feeds, and terminal data. Gaps: mapping vendor data into each analyst's template; company-specific KPIs and non-GAAP items; and source links for audit.
