# Factor Risk Model Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hedge-funds|Hedge Funds]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Vendors of equity and multi-asset factor risk models used by almost every quant and fundamental fund for risk control, portfolio construction and attribution: MSCI (Barra), SimCorp Axioma, Bloomberg and Northfield.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-400 researchers per vendor
**Output:** Daily factor exposures, covariance matrices, specific risk forecasts, attribution
**Proprietary data:** Decades of factor return and estimation-universe history
**Clock:** Daily model delivery before market open
**Buyer:** Head of Risk Research
