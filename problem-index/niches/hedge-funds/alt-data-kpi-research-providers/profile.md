# Alternative Data KPI Research Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hedge-funds|Hedge Funds]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms that license consumer transaction, email-receipt, app, web and other panels and turn them into company-level KPI estimates — revenue, units, subscribers, same-store sales — sold to hedge funds ahead of earnings. YipitData, M Science (Jefferies), Consumer Edge, Earnest Analytics and Bloomberg Second Measure are representative. The research product, not the raw panel, is what funds pay for.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 per firm across data analysts, sector research analysts, data engineers and data scientists; the largest have several hundred staff
**Output:** Weekly and pre-earnings KPI estimates per covered ticker, sector research notes, and the cleaned, ticker-mapped panel
**Proprietary data:** Licensed and owned panel histories, the panel-to-KPI mappings built over years, and a complete archive of every estimate published against the reported number
**Clock:** Company earnings dates — an estimate delivered after the print is worthless
**Buyer:** Chief Research Officer / Head of Research Product
