# Portfolio Valuation Advisory for Credit Funds

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hedge-funds|Hedge Funds]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Independent valuation firms that mark hard-to-value positions — private loans, Level 3 credit, structured products — for hedge funds and private credit funds each quarter, providing the support auditors and boards require. Kroll, Houlihan Lokey, Lincoln International and Stout are representative.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-800 valuation professionals per firm
**Output:** Quarterly position-level fair value ranges and valuation reports
**Proprietary data:** Marks and inputs across thousands of private credit positions and many funds
**Clock:** Quarter-end NAV and audit deadlines
**Buyer:** Head of Portfolio Valuation
