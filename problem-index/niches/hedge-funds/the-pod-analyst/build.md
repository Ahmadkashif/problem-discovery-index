# The Coverage Handover Pack

**Niche:** [[niches/hedge-funds/the-pod-analyst/profile|The Pod Analyst]]
**Industry:** [[industries/hedge-funds|Hedge Funds]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** When a pod closes or an analyst leaves, the next analyst inherits forty tickers and a folder of models nobody else can read.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #word-embeddings #k-nearest-neighbors #tacit-knowledge-ml #worker-facing #evaluation-metrics
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor serving the pod analyst is fighting to make a coverage list transferable — so the analyst who inherits forty names gets the previous analyst's models, theses and management reads in usable form — and whoever solves the handover becomes the analyst's daily workspace.

## The Problem
A new analyst picking up a name needs to know what the fund has thought about it: past theses and outcomes, how management has behaved on calls, which experts were useful, where the model's key assumptions came from. Today they get a set of Excel files built in a predecessor's personal style and perhaps an hour's conversation if the predecessor is still around. It takes a quarter or more to rebuild context.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Handover is nobody's job: the departing analyst has left, the PM is busy, and the research system was never populated in a way that supports it.

## What to Build
A generated handover pack per name, assembled from notes, model versions, transcripts with annotations, expert-call history and position history: a timeline of the fund's views and outcomes, the model's key drivers explained cell by cell, the predecessor's recurring reads of management with examples, and open questions. Updated continuously so it exists before anyone leaves.

## Target Customer
Directors of research and PMs at multi-manager platforms and funds with frequent coverage changes.

## Impact If Built
Time for a new analyst to become useful on an inherited name falls from a quarter to weeks, and the fund keeps the departing analyst's judgement in usable form.
