# Fund Administrator Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hedge-funds|Hedge Funds]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Fund administrators (Citco, SS&C GlobeOp, NAV Consulting and others) calculate NAVs, process investor flows and produce investor reporting; several publish aggregate flow indicators such as the SS&C GlobeOp Capital Movement Index.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Thousands in operations; tens to low hundreds in analytics and data products
**Output:** NAV calculations, investor reporting, aggregate flow indices
**Proprietary data:** Position, NAV and flow data across a large share of the industry
**Clock:** Monthly NAV strike and investor reporting deadlines
**Buyer:** Head of Data Products
