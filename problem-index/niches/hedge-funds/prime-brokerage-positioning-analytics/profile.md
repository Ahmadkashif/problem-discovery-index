# Prime Brokerage Positioning & Risk Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hedge-funds|Hedge Funds]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Prime brokers finance and clear hedge fund positions and must research their clients to set margin; their prime services teams also publish aggregated positioning, crowding and flow analysis to clients.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 per major prime broker
**Output:** Margin and risk methodology, aggregated positioning and crowding reports, capital introduction
**Proprietary data:** Positions and financing across the prime's client base
**Clock:** Daily margin calls and market events
**Buyer:** Head of Prime Services Analytics
