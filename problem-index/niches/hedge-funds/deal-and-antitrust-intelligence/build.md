# Deal Risk Called Thousands of Times and Never Scored

**Niche:** [[niches/hedge-funds/deal-and-antitrust-intelligence/profile|Event-Driven Deal & Antitrust Intelligence]]
**Industry:** [[industries/hedge-funds|Hedge Funds]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A deal intelligence service has assessed regulatory and completion risk on thousands of transactions and holds every outcome, and nobody grades the assessments.
**Tags:** #logistic-regression #survival-analysis #confidence-intervals #evaluation-metrics #hypothesis-testing #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this pocket is fighting to be first and right on whether a pending deal clears its regulators and closes — and whoever has the best record of being right takes the merger-arbitrage desk.

## The Problem
Each pending deal gets a stream of published assessments — likely second request, remedies expected, Phase 2 likely, vote at risk. Each deal later closes, is restructured or is abandoned, on a known date. Merger-arbitrage funds trade on the assessments and keep private tallies of which services were right.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The product is journalism and analysis, culturally distant from forecasting; and a published accuracy record carries reputational risk.

## What to Build
Structure the archive into dated assessments per deal, grade them against outcomes and timing, and build base-rate models for regulatory outcomes by agency, sector, overlap type and remedy history. Publish calibrated probabilities alongside the reporting.

## Target Customer
Editors-in-chief and heads of content at deal and antitrust intelligence services.

## Impact If Built
A graded record and calibrated probabilities are a differentiator no competitor without the archive can produce.
