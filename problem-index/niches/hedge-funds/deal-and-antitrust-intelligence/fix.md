# The Reporter's Source Network Held in One Inbox

**Niche:** [[niches/hedge-funds/deal-and-antitrust-intelligence/profile|Event-Driven Deal & Antitrust Intelligence]]
**Industry:** [[industries/hedge-funds|Hedge Funds]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A deal service's knowledge of who has been reliable on which kinds of deals lives with individual reporters.
**Tags:** #evaluation-metrics #k-nearest-neighbors #worker-facing #compliance #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this pocket is fighting to be first and right on whether a pending deal clears its regulators and closes — and whoever has the best record of being right takes the merger-arbitrage desk.

## The Problem
Rumour and status reporting depends on judgement about source reliability by sector and deal type. That judgement is personal and leaves with the reporter.

## Why It's Still Broken
Sources must be protected, so nothing about them is recorded centrally.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A private, reporter-controlled record of how past published items attributed to anonymised source categories turned out, used to calibrate confidence without exposing identities.

## Who Feels the Pain
Editors relying on reporters' unrecorded judgement; new reporters starting without it.

## Impact If Fixed
Reporting accuracy becomes measurable and survives staff turnover without compromising source protection.
