# Regulatory Calendars That Know Each Agency's Track Record

**Niche:** [[niches/hedge-funds/deal-and-antitrust-intelligence/profile|Event-Driven Deal & Antitrust Intelligence]]
**Industry:** [[industries/hedge-funds|Hedge Funds]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Deal timetables are tracked in spreadsheets; what funds need is each deadline joined to how that agency has used it before.
**Tags:** #survival-analysis #time-series-forecasting #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this pocket is fighting to be first and right on whether a pending deal clears its regulators and closes — and whoever has the best record of being right takes the merger-arbitrage desk.

## The Problem
Merger timetables depend on statutory clocks that agencies extend, pause and restart. Services track them per deal by hand.

## What Already Exists
Deal databases and timetables in the services' products, and general legal docket tools.

## The Customization Gap
Agency-specific clock logic (HSR pull-and-refile, EU stop-the-clock, CMA phases, SAMR), joined to historical durations by agency and sector so each timetable carries an expected close distribution.

## Target Customer
Deal intelligence and regulatory reporting teams.

## Impact If Solved
Timetables become forecasts, which is how arbitrage funds use them.
