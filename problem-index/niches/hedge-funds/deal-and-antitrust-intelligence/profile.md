# Event-Driven Deal & Antitrust Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hedge-funds|Hedge Funds]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Services that sell event-driven and merger-arbitrage funds intelligence on pending and rumoured transactions: deal status, regulatory review risk at the FTC, DOJ, European Commission, CMA and SAMR, financing and shareholder-vote issues. Dealreporter and PaRR (ION Analytics), MLex (LexisNexis), The Capitol Forum and CTFN are representative.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-400 per firm across reporters, antitrust and regulatory analysts, and editors
**Output:** Deal intelligence reports, regulatory-risk analysis per transaction, timetables, and rumour reporting
**Proprietary data:** A dated archive of every intelligence item and regulatory assessment published, joinable to how each deal actually ended
**Clock:** Statutory regulatory review periods (HSR waiting periods, EU Phase 1 and Phase 2 deadlines), shareholder votes and outside dates
**Buyer:** Editor-in-Chief / Head of Content
