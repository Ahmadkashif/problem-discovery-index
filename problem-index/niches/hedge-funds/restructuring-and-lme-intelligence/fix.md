# The Filing Read Three Hours Late

**Niche:** [[niches/hedge-funds/restructuring-and-lme-intelligence/profile|Restructuring & LME Intelligence]]
**Industry:** [[industries/hedge-funds|Hedge Funds]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A material filing drops at 6 p.m., the analyst reads it after dinner, and the price has already moved.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to know, before the docket shows it, which creditor group is organising and how a restructuring or liability management exercise will treat each tranche — and whoever reads the documents and the creditor dynamics fastest prices the recovery.

## The Problem
Restructuring documents — plan amendments, term sheets, cleansing disclosures at the end of private negotiations — are long and arrive at unpredictable times. The first reader to understand them trades first.

## Why It's Still Broken
Reading takes time and coverage is thin outside working hours.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Immediate structured summaries of new filings and cleansing materials on the fund's positions, highlighting changed treatment of each class against the previous version, delivered to the analyst's phone.

## Who Feels the Pain
Distressed analysts on call around the clock; PMs trading on stale reads.

## Impact If Fixed
Time from filing to understood implication falls from hours to minutes.
