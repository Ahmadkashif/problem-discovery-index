# Restructuring & LME Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hedge-funds|Hedge Funds]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to know, before the docket shows it, which creditor group is organising and how a restructuring or liability management exercise will treat each tranche — and whoever reads the documents and the creditor dynamics fastest prices the recovery.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600M global spend on distressed intelligence, docket analytics and restructuring research by funds (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of hedge fund research and data spend (estimate), strongly cyclical
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — dockets are digital, analysis is manual
**Target Buyer:** Distressed PM, Head of Special Situations
**Automation Potential:** Medium — speed matters, but judgement dominates

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Once a borrower is stressed, the question changes from what the documents permit to what will actually happen: which creditors are forming cooperation agreements, which advisers have been hired, what the company's sponsor wants, how a court will rule. The inputs are court dockets, news and source-driven intelligence, and plan documents, and the clock is set by courts and maturities. Speed of reading and the quality of recovery analysis decide the contest.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Octus, Debtwire (ION Analytics) and similar services for restructuring news and analysis; PACER and docket services; in-house recovery models. Gaps: docket filings arrive in volume and are read late; recovery waterfalls are rebuilt by hand per situation; precedent from prior restructurings is held in individual memory.
