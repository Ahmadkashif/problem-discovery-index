# Contract Analytics for Credit Agreements

**Niche:** [[niches/hedge-funds/credit-and-distressed-research/profile|Credit & Distressed Research]]
**Industry:** [[industries/hedge-funds|Hedge Funds]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Contract-analytics platforms extract clauses from commercial agreements; credit documents need definitions resolved and baskets computed, not just clauses found.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #transformers #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** This niche is not terminal — reading the documents of a performing credit portfolio for what borrowers are permitted to do, and anticipating how a restructuring or liability management exercise will treat each tranche, are different contests with different winners, stated separately in the sub-niches.

## The Problem
Legal technology vendors extract and classify clauses in leases, supply agreements and NDAs well. Pointed at a credit agreement, they find the restricted payments covenant and stop. The value is in resolving the definitions it depends on, computing the capacity of each basket against the borrower's financials, and tracing how baskets interact.

## What Already Exists
Contract-analytics and legal AI platforms (e.g., Kira within Litera, Harvey, Luminance), and specialist credit research vendors' covenant scoring on covered issuers.

## The Customization Gap
Credit adaptation requires definition resolution across hundreds of pages; numeric basket computation from the borrower's reported EBITDA (including add-backs as defined in the document, not GAAP); a taxonomy of LME-enabling provisions; amendment chaining; and outputs comparable across the fund's book.

## Target Customer
Credit funds with in-house legal teams already using a legal AI platform.

## Impact If Solved
Adapting an existing legal AI licence to credit documents gives a fund portfolio-wide coverage that specialist vendors provide only for widely held credits.
