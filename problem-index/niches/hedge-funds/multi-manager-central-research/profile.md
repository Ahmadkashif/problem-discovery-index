# Multi-Manager Central Research & Data Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hedge-funds|Hedge Funds]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The central teams at multi-manager platforms — data strategy, data science, central research, risk and capital allocation — that serve dozens to hundreds of pods.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-300 per platform
**Output:** Licensed and processed alternative data, central research, risk and allocation analytics for pods
**Proprietary data:** Pod positions and decisions, licensed datasets, the platform's research record
**Clock:** Earnings seasons and drawdown limits
**Buyer:** Head of Data Strategy / CRO
