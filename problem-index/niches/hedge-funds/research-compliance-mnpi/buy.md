# Communications Surveillance Pointed at Research

**Niche:** [[niches/hedge-funds/research-compliance-mnpi/profile|Research Compliance & MNPI Control]]
**Industry:** [[industries/hedge-funds|Hedge Funds]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Communications surveillance already reads every message the fund sends; it was built to catch misconduct, not to clear research.
**Tags:** #bert #transformers #evaluation-metrics #compliance #automation #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to clear a research input — an expert call, a dataset, a management meeting — in minutes with a record an SEC examiner will accept, and whoever makes clearance both fast and defensible takes the account.

## The Problem
Funds already archive and surveil e-communications to meet recordkeeping obligations. Those platforms ingest email, chat and increasingly voice, and flag lexicon or model hits for review. Research inputs — expert transcripts, notes, datasets — are either outside them or treated as just more messages.

## What Already Exists
Global Relay, Smarsh and Behavox surveillance, with lexicons and machine-learning models for market abuse and conduct risk.

## The Customization Gap
Research clearance needs: ingestion of expert-network transcripts and analyst notes as a distinct document type; risk classification by MNPI category rather than conduct lexicon; linkage to holdings, restricted lists and the corporate events calendar; a pre-clearance workflow, not only post-hoc alerting; and dataset provenance documents reviewed against the fund's own policy.

## Target Customer
Compliance teams at funds already licensing a surveillance platform who want to extend it rather than add another vendor.

## Impact If Solved
Extending an existing surveillance investment to research clearance removes a manual process without a new procurement, and puts communications and research review on one audit trail.
