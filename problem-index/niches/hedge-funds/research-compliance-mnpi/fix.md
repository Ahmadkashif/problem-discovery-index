# The Clearance Decision With No Reason Recorded

**Niche:** [[niches/hedge-funds/research-compliance-mnpi/profile|Research Compliance & MNPI Control]]
**Industry:** [[industries/hedge-funds|Hedge Funds]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A call request is approved by reply email, and two years later nobody can show an examiner why.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #compliance #workflow-orchestration #evaluation-metrics #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to clear a research input — an expert call, a dataset, a management meeting — in minutes with a record an SEC examiner will accept, and whoever makes clearance both fast and defensible takes the account.

## The Problem
Pre-clearance decisions are made quickly and recorded thinly — an approval in an inbox, a tick in a spreadsheet. When an examiner asks how the fund assessed a particular expert or dataset, the answer has to be reconstructed. Inconsistency between officers is invisible until it matters.

## Why It's Still Broken
Recording reasons takes time a small compliance team does not have, and nothing in the current workflow prompts for it.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Structure every clearance as a record: the input, the risk factors checked, the decision, and a one-line reason drafted from the checks and confirmed by the officer. Make the records searchable so like cases are treated alike and an examiner can follow any decision in seconds.

## Who Feels the Pain
Chief compliance officers facing examinations; analysts waiting on inconsistent approvals; and funds whose defensibility depends on documents nobody wrote.

## Impact If Fixed
A small change in recording turns clearance from an undocumented habit into an auditable process at almost no cost in time.
