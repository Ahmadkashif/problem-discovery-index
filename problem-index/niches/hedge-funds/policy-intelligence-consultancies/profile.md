# Policy & Political Intelligence Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hedge-funds|Hedge Funds]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Firms selling policy, regulatory and political risk analysis to macro and event-driven funds: Eurasia Group, Beacon Policy Advisors, Capital Alpha Partners and Medley Global Advisors are representative.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-250 analysts per firm
**Output:** Policy outlooks, legislative and regulatory event analysis, client calls
**Proprietary data:** Analyst networks and a record of published calls; little structured data
**Clock:** Legislative calendars, elections, regulatory deadlines
**Buyer:** Head of Research
