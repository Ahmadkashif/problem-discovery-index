# Alternative Investment Industry Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hedge-funds|Hedge Funds]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Industry bodies such as AIMA, the Managed Funds Association and the CAIA Association, which publish research, due diligence questionnaire templates, standards and education for the hedge fund industry.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically under 10 in research per association
**Output:** Industry surveys, DDQ templates, policy papers, curricula
**Proprietary data:** Member surveys
**Clock:** Regulatory consultations and annual cycles
**Buyer:** Head of Research / Policy
