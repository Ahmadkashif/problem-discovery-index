# Independent Macro & Strategy Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hedge-funds|Hedge Funds]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Independent research houses selling macro, asset-allocation and strategy research to macro funds and multi-strategy funds: BCA Research, Gavekal, TS Lombard and Variant Perception are representative.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 strategists and analysts per firm
**Output:** Weekly and monthly strategy reports, proprietary indicators, trade recommendations, client calls
**Proprietary data:** Proprietary indicator histories and a decades-long archive of published calls
**Clock:** Central bank meetings and macro data releases
**Buyer:** Chief Strategist / Head of Research
