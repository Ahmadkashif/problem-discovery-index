# Hedge Fund Performance Databases

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hedge-funds|Hedge Funds]]

## What They Do
Databases and index providers that collect self-reported hedge fund returns and publish industry indices, capital-flow reports and peer groups: HFR, PivotalPath and With Intelligence are representative.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 per firm
**Output:** Fund databases, strategy indices, industry capital reports
**Proprietary data:** Self-reported return histories across thousands of funds
**Clock:** Monthly reporting cycle
**Buyer:** Head of Research
