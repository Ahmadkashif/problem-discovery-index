# Short-Activist Research Shops

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hedge-funds|Hedge Funds]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Firms that publish short reports on companies they have shorted — Muddy Waters and Citron are representative (Hindenburg Research wound down in January 2025).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 3-15
**Output:** Published short reports; monetised through their own positions
**Proprietary data:** Investigative files per target
**Clock:** Self-set
**Buyer:** Founder
