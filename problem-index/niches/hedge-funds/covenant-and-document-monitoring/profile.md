# Covenant & Document Monitoring

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hedge-funds|Hedge Funds]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn every credit agreement and amendment in a portfolio into a structured, comparable record of what the borrower is permitted to do — and whoever does it fastest after a document drops sees liability-management risk first.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$900M global spend on covenant analysis and credit document tooling by funds (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of hedge fund research and data spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — summaries are prose
**Target Buyer:** Head of Credit Research
**Automation Potential:** High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
In a performing credit book the borrower is healthy and the risk is latent: what the documents would let the borrower do if it chose to. The work is portfolio-scale document reading on a continuous stream of new issues and amendments, with private credit adding thousands of instruments no vendor covers. The winner is whoever produces comparable, clause-linked records fastest across the most documents.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Covenant research from Octus, CreditSights and 9fin on widely held issues; legal AI platforms; in-house reading. Gaps: private and smaller issues uncovered; the fund's own interpretations unstructured; amendment chains not tracked.
