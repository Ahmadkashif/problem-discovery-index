# Vendor Covenant Scores Extended to Private Credit

**Niche:** [[niches/hedge-funds/covenant-and-document-monitoring/profile|Covenant & Document Monitoring]]
**Industry:** [[industries/hedge-funds|Hedge Funds]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Vendor covenant analysis covers the liquid market; a fund's private loans need the same treatment and are not covered.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn every credit agreement and amendment in a portfolio into a structured, comparable record of what the borrower is permitted to do — and whoever does it fastest after a document drops sees liability-management risk first.

## The Problem
Funds subscribe to vendor covenant research for syndicated loans and high-yield bonds, and hold a growing book of private and club deals whose documents only they and a few other lenders have.

## What Already Exists
Covenant scoring and analysis from Octus, CreditSights and 9fin, increasingly LLM-assisted.

## The Customization Gap
Applying the vendor's taxonomy and scoring to the fund's own private documents, inside the fund's environment because the documents are confidential; reconciling vendor scores with in-house interpretations; and producing one comparable view across public and private holdings.

## Target Customer
Credit funds and private credit managers with mixed public and private books.

## Impact If Solved
One consistent document-risk view across the whole book instead of rigorous coverage of the liquid half and memory for the rest.
