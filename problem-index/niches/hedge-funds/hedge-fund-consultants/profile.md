# Hedge Fund Consultants & Allocator Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/hedge-funds|Hedge Funds]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Advisers that research hedge funds on behalf of pensions, endowments and sovereign funds — investment and operational due diligence, manager recommendations, portfolio construction. Albourne Partners, Aksia and Cambridge Associates are representative. The buyer-side equivalent for advisers is analysed in [[niches/wealth-management-rias/manager-research-due-diligence/profile|Third-Party Manager Research & Due Diligence]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 analysts per firm
**Output:** Manager research reports, ODD reports, recommended lists, portfolio reviews
**Proprietary data:** Years of manager meeting notes, ODD findings and position-level transparency across thousands of funds
**Clock:** Client investment committee calendars
**Buyer:** Head of Hedge Fund Research
