# MSP Profitability Benchmarking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/it-managed-services|IT Managed Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Benchmarking firms collect detailed financial and operating data from managed service providers and publish the profitability indices and operating metrics the industry manages against — the definition of what a well-run MSP looks like.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 analysts and consultants
**Output:** Profitability indices, operating benchmarks, peer group comparisons, advisory engagements
**Proprietary data:** MSP financial statements and operating metrics contributed over two decades, in a segment where nothing is public
**Clock:** Quarterly and annual reporting cycles
**Buyer:** President / Managing Director
