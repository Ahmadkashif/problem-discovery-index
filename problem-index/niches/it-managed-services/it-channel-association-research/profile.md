# IT Channel Association Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/it-managed-services|IT Managed Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The channel association surveys the IT services workforce and market, runs the technical certifications MSP technicians hold, and publishes the industry outlook research the channel cites.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-120 researchers, psychometricians, and content staff
**Output:** Industry outlook and workforce research, certification examinations, skills frameworks
**Proprietary data:** Certification performance across a large technician population and long-running member surveys
**Clock:** Certification cycles and annual research calendar
**Buyer:** VP of Research — member funded
