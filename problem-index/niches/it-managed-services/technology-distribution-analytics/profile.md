# Technology Distribution Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/it-managed-services|IT Managed Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The analytics groups at the broadline technology distributors see what the entire channel buys — hardware, software, and cloud subscriptions — by reseller, by vendor, and by end market, and sell that visibility back to vendors as channel intelligence.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 category, pricing, and channel analysts
**Output:** Channel sell-through reporting, vendor programme analysis, reseller segmentation, demand planning
**Proprietary data:** Sell-through by reseller and product across most of the technology channel — the earliest read on what SMB IT is actually deploying
**Clock:** Quarterly vendor programme cycles and product transitions
**Buyer:** VP of Business Intelligence / Chief Data Officer
