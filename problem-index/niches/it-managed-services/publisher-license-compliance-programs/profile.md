# Publisher Licence Compliance Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/it-managed-services|IT Managed Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The software publishers' own compliance organizations audit customers against their licence agreements, quantify shortfalls, and convert findings into settlements and new contracts. They are the enforcement body this industry's advisory layer exists to defend against.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-800 licence auditors, data analysts, and contract specialists per major publisher
**Output:** Audit findings, deployment reconciliations, settlement quantifications, compliance-driven sales
**Proprietary data:** Deployment and entitlement records across the entire customer base, plus the outcome of every audit ever conducted
**Clock:** Audit cycles and quarter-end settlement pressure
**Buyer:** No external buyer — an internal revenue function
