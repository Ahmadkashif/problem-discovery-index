# RMM & PSA Vendor Data Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/it-managed-services|IT Managed Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The data groups at ConnectWise, Kaseya, and NinjaOne run remote monitoring and professional services automation for tens of thousands of MSPs, seeing every ticket, every alert, and every managed endpoint the channel touches.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 data scientists, analysts, and product data staff
**Output:** In-product analytics, alert correlation and automation features, benchmarking, ticket routing
**Proprietary data:** Ticket text and resolution, alert streams, and device telemetry across millions of endpoints — exactly the L1 ticket volume and alert fatigue Pass 1 names as the industry's core bottlenecks
**Clock:** Service level agreements on ticket response
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Product
