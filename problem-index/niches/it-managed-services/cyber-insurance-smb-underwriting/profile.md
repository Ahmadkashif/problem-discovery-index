# SMB Cyber Insurance Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/it-managed-services|IT Managed Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Cyber insurers underwrite small and mid-sized businesses, increasingly requiring specific controls that MSPs are the ones to implement — which makes the underwriter's control requirements a de facto standard for the whole managed services industry.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 underwriters, security analysts, and actuaries
**Output:** Control requirements and eligibility rules, rating models, external scanning and risk assessment, claims analysis
**Proprietary data:** Incident and claim outcomes joined to the controls in place beforehand — the only empirical evidence about which security measures actually prevent losses
**Clock:** Annual renewal and rapid response to emerging attack campaigns
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
