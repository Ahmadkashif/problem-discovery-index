# Software Pricing & Catalogue Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/it-managed-services|IT Managed Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Catalogue and pricing data providers maintain the product, SKU, licensing metric, and price feeds that resellers, MSPs, and procurement teams quote and buy from — a corpus that changes daily across hundreds of thousands of items.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 catalogue analysts, taxonomists, and data engineers
**Output:** Normalized product catalogues, price and availability feeds, licensing metric mapping, benchmark pricing
**Proprietary data:** Normalized catalogue and transacted price history across the channel
**Clock:** Daily price and availability changes; quote validity windows
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Head of Content
