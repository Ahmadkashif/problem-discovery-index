# Technology Solutions Distributors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/it-managed-services|IT Managed Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Master agents and technology solutions distributors sit between MSPs and the carriers, cloud, and infrastructure providers they resell, producing the sourcing comparisons and market intelligence that decide which provider an MSP puts a client on.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-200 solution engineers, analysts, and research staff
**Output:** Sourcing comparisons, provider capability and pricing analysis, market intelligence reports
**Proprietary data:** Quoted and transacted pricing across many providers and deal types, which no single MSP or provider can see
**Clock:** Contract renewal and provider promotion cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Research / Chief Revenue Officer
