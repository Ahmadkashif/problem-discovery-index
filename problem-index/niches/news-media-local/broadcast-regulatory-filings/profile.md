# Broadcast Regulatory & Political File Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/news-media-local|Local News Media]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The broadcast regulator maintains the public files every station must keep — including the political advertising disclosures showing who bought what time at what rate — and administers licensing and ownership rules.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 analysts, attorneys, and engineers
**Output:** Licensing decisions, ownership rule administration, public file requirements, enforcement actions
**Proprietary data:** Station-level political file disclosures and ownership records across every US market
**Clock:** Licence renewal cycles and political window requirements
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — federal agency
