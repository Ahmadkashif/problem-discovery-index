# Journalism Training & Research Centres

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/news-media-local|Local News Media]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Journalism institutes train working reporters, publish research on newsroom practice and audience behaviour, and develop the standards and tooling small newsrooms adopt.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 trainers, researchers, and programme staff
**Output:** Training programmes, practice research, audience and revenue studies, newsroom tools
**Proprietary data:** Newsroom survey series and training outcome data
**Clock:** Programme and publication calendars
**Buyer:** Research director — philanthropically funded
