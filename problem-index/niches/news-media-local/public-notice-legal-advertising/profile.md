# Public Notice & Legal Advertising

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/news-media-local|Local News Media]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Public notice requirements oblige governments, courts, and businesses to publish legal notices in qualifying newspapers, and the aggregators and state press associations that administer them handle placement, verification, and the searchable archives statutes increasingly require.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-70 placement, compliance, and data staff
**Output:** Notice placement and affidavits, statewide notice databases, compliance verification, legislative analysis on notice requirements
**Proprietary data:** Notice archives spanning states and decades — foreclosures, probate, government contracting, and corporate actions, published nowhere else in one place
**Clock:** Statutory publication deadlines
**Buyer:** Executive director — press association funded
