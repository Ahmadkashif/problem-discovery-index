# Subscription Platform Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/news-media-local|Local News Media]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Paywall and subscription platform vendors run the metering, offer logic, and churn management that decides who is asked to pay and when, for publishers whose subscription revenue is now the difference between operating and closing.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-200 data scientists, growth analysts, and product staff
**Output:** Metering and paywall optimization, offer and pricing tests, churn prediction, engagement scoring
**Proprietary data:** Reader engagement, conversion, and churn across many publishers and titles — the only cross-publisher view of what actually converts a local news reader
**Clock:** Continuous conversion flow and renewal cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Data Science / Chief Product Officer
