## What They Do
State and national press associations survey members on revenue and staffing, publish industry economic research, and advocate on public notice, shield law, and access questions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-30 researchers and policy staff
**Output:** Revenue and employment surveys, industry economic research, legislative analysis, member benchmarking
**Proprietary data:** Long-running member revenue and staffing series through the industry's decline
**Clock:** Legislative sessions and annual surveys
**Buyer:** Executive director — member funded
