# Programmatic Advertising Exchanges

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/news-media-local|Local News Media]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The programmatic exchanges and supply-side platforms auction local publishers' inventory in real time, setting the price local news gets for a digital impression.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,500 data scientists, yield analysts, and marketplace engineers
**Output:** Auction and yield optimization, floor pricing, demand analytics, inventory quality scoring
**Proprietary data:** Bid and clearing price data across enormous inventory volumes, including what each publisher's audience is actually worth to buyers
**Clock:** Real-time auctions
**Buyer:** VP of Data Science / Chief Revenue Officer
