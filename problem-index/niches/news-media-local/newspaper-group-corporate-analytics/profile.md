# Newspaper Group Corporate Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/news-media-local|Local News Media]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup

## What They Do
The consolidated newspaper and broadcast groups run corporate analytics across hundreds of local titles and stations, managing subscription pricing, content investment, and the closure decisions that create news deserts.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 analysts, revenue managers, and audience staff
**Output:** Subscription pricing and retention analysis, content performance, market-level profitability, portfolio decisions
**Proprietary data:** Audience, subscription, and advertising performance across hundreds of local markets over decades
**Clock:** Quarterly reporting and renewal cycles
**Buyer:** No external buyer — internal cost centre
