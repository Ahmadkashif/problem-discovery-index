# Political Advertising Tracking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/news-media-local|Local News Media]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Political advertising trackers monitor every broadcast, cable, and digital political spot — who ran it, where, when, at what cost — and sell the read to campaigns, party committees, and the media buyers deciding where the next dollar goes. Local broadcast is the largest destination for that spending, which makes these firms the measurement layer above the local media market's most profitable revenue line.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 data analysts, occurrence monitors, media researchers, and engineers
**Output:** Ad occurrence and spend tracking by market and race, creative libraries, competitive share reporting, spend forecasting
**Proprietary data:** Comprehensive occurrence monitoring across markets joined to filed rate and buy data — a record of political spending far more complete and far faster than any public disclosure
**Clock:** Election cycles, and within them the daily buying decisions of campaigns that cannot buy time twice
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Research
