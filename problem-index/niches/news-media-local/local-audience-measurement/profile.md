# Local Audience Measurement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/news-media-local|Local News Media]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Audience measurement services estimate how many people watch, listen to, and read local media, producing the currency advertising is bought and sold on in every local market.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 methodologists, panel operations staff, statisticians, and analysts
**Output:** Market ratings and audience estimates, demographic breaks, cross-platform measurement, currency data for transactions
**Proprietary data:** Panel and return-path data across markets, and the modelling that turns partial observation into market-level currency
**Clock:** Ratings periods and the advertising sales calendar built around them
**Buyer:** Chief Research Officer / Chief Data Officer
