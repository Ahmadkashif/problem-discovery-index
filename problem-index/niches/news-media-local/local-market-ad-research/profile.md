# Local Market Advertising Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/news-media-local|Local News Media]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Research firms size local advertising markets by medium and category, and sell the estimates stations, publishers, and agencies use to set budgets and negotiate.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-70 analysts and researchers
**Output:** Local market ad revenue estimates, category and medium share, forecasts
**Proprietary data:** Assembled estimates from disclosures, surveys, and occurrence data
**Clock:** Annual and quarterly forecast cycles
**Buyer:** Head of Research
