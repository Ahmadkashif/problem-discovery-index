# Content Syndication & Licensing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/news-media-local|Local News Media]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Wire services and content licensing agencies syndicate reporting between outlets and license archives to aggregators, platforms, and increasingly to model developers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 licensing, rights, and analytics staff above a large editorial operation
**Output:** Syndication and licensing agreements, usage tracking and reporting, rights administration, archive productization
**Proprietary data:** Content archives spanning decades, with the rights position attached to each item
**Clock:** Licensing renewal cycles and news cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Licensing / Chief Data Officer
