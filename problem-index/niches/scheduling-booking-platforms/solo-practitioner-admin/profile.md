# Solo Practitioner Admin

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/scheduling-booking-platforms|Scheduling & Booking Platforms]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor that takes this seriously is fighting to remove the unpaid administrative hour from a day whose income is measured in booked time — and whoever does that takes the independent practitioner, who is the category's largest and least-served population.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$140M US attributable to administrative automation for independent practitioners
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — the admin is done manually between clients
**Target Buyer:** The practitioner themselves, paying out of their own income
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the tasks are repetitive, bounded and fully specified

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
An independent practitioner — a therapist, a trainer, a tutor, a groomer, a mobile technician — sells time and nothing else, which means every hour spent on administration is an hour of income foregone with no compensating benefit. The scheduling admin is a specific and recurring set: chasing a deposit, rearranging a cancellation, filling the resulting gap, confirming tomorrow's appointments, following up someone who did not rebook, sending an invoice, and reconciling what was paid. It is roughly an hour a day, done in fragments between clients, and it is the only unpaid time in the day. The category serves this person with a booking page and a reminder, which addresses the smallest part of it. They are the largest population in the market by count, the least able to absorb software cost or complexity, and the least served by products built for businesses with an administrator.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Booking pages, calendar sync, reminder messages, payment links, and in the more complete products a client record. The gaps: everything is a tool the practitioner must operate rather than something that acts on their behalf, which is the wrong shape for someone with fifteen minutes between clients; deposit chasing, gap filling and rebooking follow-up are all manual; the practitioner has no view of their own economics — what their real hourly return is after admin and no-shows — which is the number that would drive every decision they make; and products are priced and structured for businesses with staff.
