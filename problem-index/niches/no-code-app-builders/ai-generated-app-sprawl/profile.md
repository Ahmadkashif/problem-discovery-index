# AI-Generated App Sprawl

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/no-code-app-builders|No-Code App Builders]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor here is fighting to make a generated application ownable — attributable, reviewable and maintainable — at the rate generation now produces them, and whoever does that takes the enterprise account, because generation without ownership is a governance problem arriving faster than any governance process.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$690M US and growing quickly
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Rising fast for generation, absent for ownership
**Target Buyer:** Platform governance, IT leadership and security
**Automation Potential:** Very High — generated apps have complete provenance if anyone records it

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
AI-assisted building arrived in this category quickly and works: a person describes what they want and receives a functioning application in minutes. Every problem the category already had is a function of how many apps exist and how little is known about them, and generation multiplies the numerator while doing nothing for the denominator. The specific new problems are distinct from the old ones. Generated apps have no builder in the ordinary sense — the person prompted rather than designed, so their understanding of what was produced is shallower than a hand-builder's, which makes the inheritance problem worse from day one. The generation is cheap enough that people produce several and keep one, leaving the rest. And a generated app may contain patterns the prompter did not ask for and cannot evaluate: a permissive sharing default, a field that collects more than is needed, an automation that emails externally. The contest is ownership at generation speed.

## Current Tools & Gaps
AI app generation in most major platforms, prompt-to-app and prompt-to-automation features, and generated component suggestions. The gaps: provenance is not retained, so nobody knows later that an app was generated or from what prompt; generated apps are not reviewed against any policy before they run; the abandoned majority is never cleaned up; the prompter's comprehension of what was produced is not established or tested anywhere; and generation volume is not reported against governance capacity, so the gap widens invisibly.
