# Process Corpus Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/no-code-app-builders|No-Code App Builders]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor that gets here is fighting to turn a corpus of thousands of executable business processes into a product, and whoever does it holds a position no competitor can replicate, because the corpus was created by customers and exists nowhere else.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$360M US, almost entirely latent
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~6% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** None — the corpus renders interfaces and nothing else
**Target Buyer:** The platform vendors themselves; secondarily process and operations consultancies
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the apps are structured, executable and complete

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A no-code app is an executable description of how one company actually handles something — returns, approvals, onboarding, claims, scheduling — built by the person who does the work rather than by an analyst interviewing them about it. A platform with a large installed base therefore holds thousands of such descriptions across every industry, at a granularity no process documentation achieves and with a fidelity no interview produces, because these are the processes as run rather than as described. The vendors use this corpus to render interfaces. Nobody has asked what it says. The distinct opportunity is treating it as a dataset: what the common variants of a process actually are, which structures survive and which are abandoned, what a given company's process is missing relative to its peers, and where an app in the corpus indicates a software category that does not yet exist.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Template galleries authored by content teams, community template marketplaces, and usage analytics per app. The gaps: nothing analyses app structure across customers, so the corpus is inert; templates are written from imagination while thousands of observed implementations of the same process sit unexamined; there is no outcome signal attached to structure, so nobody knows which process designs work; and the governance framing for cross-customer structural analysis has not been articulated by any vendor, so the topic is avoided rather than designed.
