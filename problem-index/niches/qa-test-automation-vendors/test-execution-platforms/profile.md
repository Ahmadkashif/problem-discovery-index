# Test Execution Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/qa-test-automation-vendors|QA & Test Automation Vendors]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor here is fighting to be where and how an organisation's tests actually run — and that contest is an infrastructure business in one market and a trust business in the other, which is why this niche is not terminal and is decomposed below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.4B US test execution infrastructure and assisted authoring
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~35% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very High
**Target Buyer:** Device and browser operations; separately, quality engineering
**Automation Potential:** High — the grid economics and the authoring assistance are both optimisable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is the category's largest commercial block: somewhere, tests execute, against something that resembles the environments users have. It covers the commercial grids offering thousands of browser and device combinations and the newer wave of tooling that generates and repairs tests automatically, both of which are sold as ways of making automated testing viable.

The filter fails here. "Test execution" names where tests run rather than a contest, and the two markets have nothing in common beyond the word. Grids are an infrastructure business: the contest is matrix breadth, concurrency, price and how closely a virtualised environment behaves like a real device, bought by an operations function that decided not to run a lab. Generated and self-healing tooling is a trust business: the contest is whether a test that writes and repairs itself can be relied upon, bought by quality engineering on a maintenance promise, and lost the first time a suite heals past a regression. Decomposed below.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Commercial browser and device grids, self-hosted grids, generated test tooling, self-healing selectors and visual comparison. The gaps: grid usage is billed by minute and nobody reports which combinations ever found a defect, so the matrix is chosen by caution; emulated environments differ from real devices in ways that are discovered by a user rather than a test; generated tests produce volume without evidence of value; self-healing repairs without classifying, which is the hazard the maintenance niche describes; and neither half reports the outcome that matters, which is defects found in proportion to cost.
