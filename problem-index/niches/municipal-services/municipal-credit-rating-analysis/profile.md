# Municipal Credit Rating & Public Finance Analysis

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/municipal-services|Municipal Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The public finance groups at the rating agencies assess the creditworthiness of every municipality, utility, school district, and authority that issues debt. The rating determines what a city pays to borrow for the water mains, roads, and facilities Pass 1 describes municipal staff maintaining — a difference of tens of basis points across decades of debt service.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-1,500 credit analysts, sector specialists, economists, and criteria staff
**Output:** Credit ratings and outlooks, rating criteria and methodologies, sector outlooks, surveillance reports
**Proprietary data:** Financial statements, budgets, and operating data for essentially every US municipal issuer, collected over decades and joined to a default and distress record no one else holds
**Clock:** Bond issuance calendars, annual disclosure deadlines, and continuous surveillance obligations
**Buyer:** Chief Analytics Officer / Head of Public Finance Criteria
