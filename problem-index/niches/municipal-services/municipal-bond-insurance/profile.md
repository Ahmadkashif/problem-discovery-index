# Municipal Bond Insurance Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/municipal-services|Municipal Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Bond insurers guarantee municipal debt service, taking the credit risk of an issuer defaulting and doing their own independent underwriting of every credit they wrap.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-200 credit analysts, surveillance staff, and actuaries
**Output:** Credit underwriting and pricing, portfolio surveillance, workout and remediation analysis
**Proprietary data:** Independent credit assessments and surveillance across an insured portfolio, plus workout experience on distressed credits
**Clock:** Issuance calendars and continuous surveillance
**Buyer:** Chief Risk Officer
