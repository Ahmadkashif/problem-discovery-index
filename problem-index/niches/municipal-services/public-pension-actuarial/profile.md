# Public Pension Actuarial Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/municipal-services|Municipal Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Consulting actuaries value public pension and retiree health obligations, setting the contribution requirements that consume a growing share of municipal budgets and appear as liabilities on every balance sheet.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-200 actuaries and analysts
**Output:** Actuarial valuations, contribution requirements, experience studies, funding policy analysis
**Proprietary data:** Plan experience across many public systems, plus accumulated assumption-setting precedent
**Clock:** Statutory valuation and reporting deadlines
**Buyer:** Practice leader
