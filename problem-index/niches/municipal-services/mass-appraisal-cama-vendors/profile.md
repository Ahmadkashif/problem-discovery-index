# Mass Appraisal & Assessment Systems

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/municipal-services|Municipal Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Mass appraisal providers value every parcel in a jurisdiction for property tax purposes, building and running the models that set assessed values — the determination that produces most local government revenue and that homeowners appeal.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 appraisers, statisticians, and data staff
**Output:** Assessment rolls and valuation models, revaluation projects, appeal defence, ratio studies
**Proprietary data:** Property characteristics and sales data across jurisdictions, plus the valuation models and their historical performance
**Clock:** Statutory assessment roll certification dates
**Buyer:** VP of Valuation Services / Chief Appraiser
