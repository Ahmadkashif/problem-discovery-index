# Municipal League Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/municipal-services|Municipal Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
State municipal leagues and national local government associations survey members on fiscal conditions and service delivery, publish operating benchmarks, and analyse the state legislation that constrains local revenue.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 researchers and policy analysts
**Output:** Fiscal condition surveys, service benchmarking, legislative analysis, model ordinances
**Proprietary data:** Long-running member survey series on local fiscal conditions
**Clock:** Legislative sessions and budget seasons
**Buyer:** Director of Research — member funded
