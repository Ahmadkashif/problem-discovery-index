# State & Local Audit Agencies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/municipal-services|Municipal Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State auditors and comptrollers audit municipal financial statements and operations, publish fiscal distress monitoring, and intervene when a local government's finances fail.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 auditors, analysts, and fiscal monitors per state
**Output:** Audit findings, fiscal distress scores, performance audits, intervention decisions
**Proprietary data:** Financial and audit records for every local government in the state, with distress and intervention outcomes
**Clock:** Statutory filing and audit deadlines
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — state agency
