# Municipal Utility Rate Consultants

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/municipal-services|Municipal Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Rate consultants build the cost-of-service studies that set water, sewer, and stormwater rates, allocating costs across customer classes and defending the result at public hearings where residents contest every increase.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 rate analysts, engineers, and economists
**Output:** Cost-of-service studies, rate designs and schedules, financial plans, public hearing support
**Proprietary data:** Cost allocation methodologies and rate structures across many utilities
**Clock:** Budget adoption and rate hearing calendars
**Buyer:** Practice leader
