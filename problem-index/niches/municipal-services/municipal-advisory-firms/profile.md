# Municipal Advisory Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/municipal-services|Municipal Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Registered municipal advisors structure debt issues for cities and authorities — sizing, timing, and structuring the bonds that fund infrastructure, and owing a fiduciary duty to the issuer rather than to the underwriter.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 advisors, analysts, and quantitative staff
**Output:** Debt structuring and sizing, market timing advice, underwriter selection, disclosure support, refunding analysis
**Proprietary data:** Pricing outcomes and structures across many issues and issuers
**Clock:** Bond pricing dates and market windows
**Buyer:** Principal / Managing Director
