# Municipal Procurement Consulting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/municipal-services|Municipal Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer

## What They Do
Small consultancies help municipalities specify and run competitive procurements for services and systems, and help vendors respond to them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 2-10 consultants and analysts
**Output:** Specifications and solicitation documents, evaluation criteria, vendor selection support
**Proprietary data:** Solicitation and award outcomes across jurisdictions
**Clock:** Procurement calendars
**Buyer:** Principal
