# Infrastructure Asset Management Consulting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/municipal-services|Municipal Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Asset management consultancies inventory a municipality's pipes, pavement, and facilities, assess condition, and produce the capital plans that decide what gets replaced and when — the analysis that turns Pass 1's undocumented senior staff knowledge into a funded programme.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-200 engineers, asset planners, and analysts
**Output:** Asset inventories and condition assessments, deterioration models, capital improvement plans, level-of-service analysis
**Proprietary data:** Condition and replacement records across many systems, rarely fed back to test the deterioration models
**Clock:** Capital budget cycles and regulatory consent decrees
**Buyer:** Practice leader
