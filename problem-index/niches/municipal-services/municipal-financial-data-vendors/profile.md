# Municipal Financial Data Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/municipal-services|Municipal Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Data providers extract and normalize municipal financial statements, continuing disclosure filings, and trade data into comparable series for investors, analysts, and issuers benchmarking themselves.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 financial analysts, data engineers, and researchers
**Output:** Normalized financial series, peer benchmarking, disclosure monitoring, trade and pricing data
**Proprietary data:** Extraction and normalization over thousands of audited financial statements that are public documents and unusable as data
**Clock:** Annual disclosure deadlines and issuance calendars
**Buyer:** Head of Data / Managing Director
