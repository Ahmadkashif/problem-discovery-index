# Governmental Accounting Standards

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/municipal-services|Municipal Services]]

## What They Do
The governmental accounting standards board writes the rules every municipality's financial statements are prepared under, defining how pensions, infrastructure, and leases are reported — decisions that reshape thousands of balance sheets at once.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-120 technical staff, researchers, and project managers
**Output:** Accounting standards and implementation guidance, technical bulletins, research studies
**Proprietary data:** Standards development history, comment letters, and field test data
**Clock:** Standard effective dates every government implements
**Buyer:** Technical director — foundation funded
