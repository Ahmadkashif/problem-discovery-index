# Site Access Notes Kept in a Technician's Phone

**Niche:** [[niches/wireless-carriers/cell-site-field-operations/profile|Cell Site Field Operations & Fault Diagnosis]]
**Industry:** [[industries/wireless-carriers|Wireless Carriers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Gate codes, landlord rules and "the generator won't start below freezing" live in individual technicians' notes, so the person covering the territory tonight arrives without them.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to resolve a site fault on the first visit or without one, and whoever turns alarms and site history into the right part, person and decision before the truck leaves takes the field operations contract.

## The Problem
The official site record holds the address and equipment list. The useful knowledge is elsewhere: which gate code works, which farmer wants a call first, where the key box is, which rooftop needs a building engineer, and the site's habits. Technicians keep it in phone notes and texts. On-call coverage, territory changes and retirements lose it.

## Why It's Still Broken
Writing notes into the official system takes time and the forms don't have fields for it. Nobody is measured on keeping site memory, and the cost of a blocked visit lands on whoever was on call.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Let technicians record a voice note or photo at the end of each visit, turned automatically into structured site facts (access, contacts, hazards, quirks), shown at the top of the next ticket, and confirmed or corrected on each later visit.

## Who Feels the Pain
On-call technicians outside their home territory, new hires, and customers waiting while a technician hunts for a key.

## Impact If Fixed
Fewer blocked and repeat visits, faster restoration, and site knowledge that survives turnover, at almost no cost.
