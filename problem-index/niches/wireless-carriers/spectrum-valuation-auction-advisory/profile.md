# Spectrum Valuation & Auction Advisory

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wireless-carriers|Wireless Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Economists and valuation specialists who value spectrum licences and advise bidders and sellers: bidding strategy and valuation models for FCC auctions, price opinions for secondary sales such as EchoStar's 2025–26 sales to AT&T and SpaceX, fair-value work for licence impairment testing, and expert reports in regulatory proceedings. Spectrum practices at economic consultancies (Brattle Group and Compass Lexecon among them) and auction specialists such as Power Auctions do most of it; carriers and investors pay by engagement.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-40 per firm's spectrum practice (estimate)
**Output:** Valuation models in dollars per MHz-POP, bidding strategies, auction simulations, valuation and expert reports
**Proprietary data:** An archive of auction outcomes (public) and prior engagement models (client-owned and walled)
**Clock:** FCC auction schedules set the work's deadlines; AWS-3 Auction 113 bid from June 2 to June 23, 2026, and down payments were due July 13. Impairment work runs to clients' 10-K dates
**Buyer:** Spectrum practice leader

**Kill switches:** live. Bidding advice sits inside the FCC's anti-collusion rule (47 CFR 1.2105(c)), which bars sharing bids and strategies during an auction's quiet period, and every model is built on the client's plans and walled per client, so the data isn't the firm's to reuse.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. The clock is as hard as any in the sweep, but auctions are episodic, the bidder pool is small (17 qualified bidders in Auction 113), and the material is the client's.
