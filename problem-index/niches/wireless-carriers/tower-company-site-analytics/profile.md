# Tower Company Leasing & Site Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wireless-carriers|Wireless Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
American Tower, Crown Castle (a pure-play US tower company with more than 40,000 towers since it sold its fibre and small-cell businesses on May 1, 2026), SBA Communications and private owners such as Vertical Bridge lease tower space to carriers. Their analytics teams forecast carrier demand by site, price amendments, model churn from carrier consolidation and target sites for marketing. That work serves the tower company's own leasing and is never sold.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-40 leasing analytics, site marketing and planning staff per company (estimate)
**Output:** Internal leasing forecasts, amendment pricing, site prospect lists, investor disclosures
**Proprietary data:** Tenant, equipment and lease records on tens of thousands of towers each, with full amendment history
**Clock:** Carrier master lease agreement renewals and quarterly earnings
**Buyer:** VP of leasing or strategy analytics
