# Mobile Operator Association Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wireless-carriers|Wireless Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
GSMA Intelligence, the research arm of the global mobile operators' association, tracks every operator group, network and MVNO worldwide: connections, revenue, spectrum holdings, network launches and forecasts, plus consumer surveys. It sells subscriptions (140–200+ reports a year and a data platform with history and forecasts) to operators, equipment vendors, investors and regulators, and reviews its operator forecasts quarterly.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~50-100 analysts, data specialists and economists (estimate)
**Output:** Operator and market database, quarterly forecasts, research reports, consumer survey data, custom studies
**Proprietary data:** About 25 years of operator-level metrics and estimates, and the association's own consumer surveys; much of the operator reporting is public, the long harmonised series and the surveys are not
**Clock:** Operators' quarterly reporting and the unit's own quarterly forecast reviews
**Buyer:** Head of GSMA Intelligence
