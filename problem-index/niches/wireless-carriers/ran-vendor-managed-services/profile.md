# RAN Vendor Network Analytics & Managed Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wireless-carriers|Wireless Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Ericsson, Nokia and Samsung sell carriers not only radios but also network optimisation, performance management and managed operations, with their own engineers running parts of a carrier's network under service-level agreements. The vendors' network analytics and optimisation teams diagnose and tune sectors, prepare performance reports and run automation on the carrier's behalf, paid within large multi-year services contracts.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Hundreds of optimisation engineers and analysts per vendor serving US carriers (estimate)
**Output:** Optimisation actions, performance and SLA reports, network audits, automation playbooks
**Proprietary data:** None of their own at network level; they work on the carrier's counters, configuration and drive tests, plus their own product telemetry
**Clock:** Contract SLAs and carrier launch schedules
**Buyer:** Head of managed services for the account
