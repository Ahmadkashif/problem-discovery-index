# Spectrum Holding & Leasing Companies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wireless-carriers|Wireless Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Investors that hold spectrum licences without running national networks and lease or sell them to carriers. Examples include private spectrum investors such as Grain Management (recalled) and companies that bought licences at auction to lease them on. EchoStar's sales to AT&T and SpaceX, approved in May 2026, show the exit route. They earn lease income and sale gains.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Investment and regulatory teams typically under 10 per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Internal valuations and lease negotiations
**Proprietary data:** Their own licence and lease terms
**Clock:** FCC buildout deadlines on the licences they hold
**Buyer:** Managing partner
