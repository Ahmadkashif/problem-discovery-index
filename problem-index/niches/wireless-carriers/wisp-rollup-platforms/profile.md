# WISP Rollup Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wireless-carriers|Wireless Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Private-equity-backed operators that buy small WISPs and consolidate their towers, billing and spectrum, often while building fibre in the denser parts of their footprint with subsidy funding. Their corporate development teams value targets, plan integrations and prepare subsidy applications.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Corporate development and network planning teams typically under 10 per platform (estimate)
**Output:** Acquisition models, integration plans and subsidy applications, all for internal use
**Proprietary data:** Subscriber, sector and install records of the acquired operators
**Clock:** Deal timetables and subsidy programme windows
**Buyer:** Chief executive or head of corporate development
