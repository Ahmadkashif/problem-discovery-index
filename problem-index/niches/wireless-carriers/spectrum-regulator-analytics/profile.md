# Spectrum Regulators' Auction & Licensing Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wireless-carriers|Wireless Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The FCC's Wireless Telecommunications Bureau and Office of Economics and Analytics design and run spectrum auctions, license and police commercial spectrum, and test carriers' coverage claims. NTIA manages federal spectrum and studies which federal bands can be shared or cleared for commercial use. Their analysis decides what spectrum carriers can buy, when and under what buildout conditions. Nobody buys it: it is funded by appropriation and auction proceeds.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~100+ engineers, economists and analysts in the FCC's wireless bureau and economics office; NTIA's spectrum office is a separate team of similar order (estimate)
**Output:** Auction designs and procedures, licensing decisions, coverage verification findings, spectrum studies and reports to Congress
**Proprietary data:** Complete licence, auction-bidding and coverage-filing records, plus federal spectrum-use data nobody else holds
**Clock:** Statutory deadlines; Congress required the AWS-3 re-auction (Auction 113) to start within 18 months of its December 2024 enactment, by June 23, 2026, and bidding closed that day at $3.57B
**Buyer:** Bureau chief
