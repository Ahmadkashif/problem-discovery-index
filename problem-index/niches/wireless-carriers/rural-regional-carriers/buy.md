# Crowdsourced Network Data Cut for Roaming Negotiations

**Niche:** [[niches/wireless-carriers/rural-regional-carriers/profile|Rural & Regional Mobile Carriers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/wireless-carriers|Wireless Carriers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Crowdsourced performance data already measures roamers on rural networks, but it is packaged as national marketing benchmarks, not as evidence for a roaming renewal.
**Tags:** #feature-engineering #hypothesis-testing #confidence-intervals #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (drive-test contractors, crowdsourced-data vendors and coverage engineering consultants) is fighting to prove, road by road, the coverage and quality a rural network delivers to the national carriers' roaming customers and on the FCC's map, and whoever produces the most credible road-level proof takes the account.

## The Problem
Crowdsourced data vendors collect tests from phones on every network, including roamers. Their standard products rank operators by market and award marketing claims. A rural carrier needs something different: performance of other carriers' customers on its network, by road, compared with the partner's own coverage nearby.

## What Already Exists
Ookla and Opensignal datasets and dashboards, drive-test services, and the FCC's public challenge and speed-test data.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs roamer identification from network and device identifiers, aggregation to road segments and counties rather than metro markets, comparison with the partner's native coverage, sample-size and confidence reporting adequate for a negotiation exhibit, and output formatted for FCC coverage evidence as well.

## Target Customer
Regional carriers that already license some crowdsourced data or could buy it through a group arrangement.

## Impact If Solved
The carrier walks into renewals and FCC challenges with independent, quantified evidence instead of anecdotes.
