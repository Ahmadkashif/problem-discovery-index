# A Roaming Quality Ledger by Road Segment

**Niche:** [[niches/wireless-carriers/rural-regional-carriers/profile|Rural & Regional Mobile Carriers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/wireless-carriers|Wireless Carriers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A rural carrier negotiates its largest revenue line, inbound roaming, with less evidence about its own network's performance for roamers than the national carrier across the table.
**Tags:** #gaussian-processes #time-series-forecasting #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (drive-test contractors, crowdsourced-data vendors and coverage engineering consultants) is fighting to prove, road by road, the coverage and quality a rural network delivers to the national carriers' roaming customers and on the FCC's map, and whoever produces the most credible road-level proof takes the account.

## The Problem
National carriers measure their customers' experience on partner networks through their own analytics and crowdsourced data, and use it at renegotiation. The rural carrier sees aggregate usage on clearinghouse files and its own counters but rarely a road-by-road view of what roamers experienced. It enters the negotiation unable to show where its network is the only coverage and where performance meets the agreement.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The market is a few dozen small buyers; national-carrier tooling is priced far beyond them. Combining roaming records, network counters, crowdsourced tests and road geometry needs data engineering these operators don't have.

## What to Build
A ledger that maps inbound roaming sessions and performance by road segment and month, combining the carrier's counters, clearinghouse records, crowdsourced and drive-test samples, and coverage overlap with the national carrier's own network. It produces negotiation exhibits, highlights segments where performance is slipping before the partner notices, and reuses the same evidence for FCC coverage filings and challenges.

## Target Customer
CEOs and CTOs of regional and rural carriers, and their trade association as a group buyer.

## Impact If Built
Better-evidenced roaming renewals protect the carrier's largest revenue line, and one evidence base serves both roaming and FCC filings.
