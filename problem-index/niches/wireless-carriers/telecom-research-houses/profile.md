# Telecom Strategy Research & Forecasting Houses

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wireless-carriers|Wireless Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Independent research and consulting firms that sell syndicated research, forecasts and benchmarks on mobile operators: Analysys Mason's research division, Omdia's telecom practice, STL Partners, Tefficient and smaller specialists such as Mobile Experts. Operators buy them for strategy and benchmarking, vendors for market sizing, and investors and regulators for context. Most pair a subscription research product with consulting.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-80 telecom research analysts per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Operator KPI benchmarks, market forecasts, pricing and strategy reports, custom studies
**Proprietary data:** Each firm's own forecast archive and operator benchmarks, assembled largely from public reporting
**Clock:** Operators' reporting seasons and the firms' own publication calendars; occasional regulatory or auction events
**Buyer:** Head of research or research director

**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. Research is the product, but the inputs are mostly public, deliverables vary from report to report, and the clocks are self-set.
