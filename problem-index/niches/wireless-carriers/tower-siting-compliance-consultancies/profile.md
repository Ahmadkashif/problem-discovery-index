# Tower Siting Environmental & RF Compliance Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wireless-carriers|Wireless Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Firms that produce the per-site compliance work a new tower or modification needs: National Environmental Policy Act checklists and historic-preservation reviews through the FCC's tower notification process, RF exposure studies and signage, FAA obstruction analyses, and structural and mount analyses. Carriers, tower owners and site acquisition firms pay per site.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Field scientists, engineers and drafters in production teams; no separate research function (estimate)
**Output:** Per-site compliance reports and stamped analyses
**Proprietary data:** Archives of site reports, held per client
**Clock:** Deployment programme schedules and municipal shot clocks, which belong to the client
**Buyer:** Practice manager
