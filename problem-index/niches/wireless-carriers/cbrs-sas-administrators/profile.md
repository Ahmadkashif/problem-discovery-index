# CBRS Spectrum Access System Administrators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wireless-carriers|Wireless Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
FCC-certified operators of the Spectrum Access Systems that coordinate the 3.5 GHz Citizens Broadband Radio Service, deciding in real time which channels and power each WISP, carrier or enterprise radio may use while protecting incumbents. Google, Federated Wireless, CommScope, Sony and Key Bridge have held certifications (recalled). Users pay per device per year.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Engineering and operations teams; no analyst function that grows with device volume (estimate)
**Output:** Automated channel grants and interference protection
**Proprietary data:** Device registrations and grant history in the administrator's system
**Clock:** Real-time grant cycles under FCC rules
**Buyer:** General manager of the SAS business
