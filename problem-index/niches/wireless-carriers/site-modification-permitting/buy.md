# Deployment Trackers That Assemble the Paperwork

**Niche:** [[niches/wireless-carriers/site-modification-permitting/profile|Site Modifications, Leasing & Permitting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/wireless-carriers|Wireless Carriers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Carriers already track deployments in Sitetracker and similar tools, which record that an application is due without helping anyone write it.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #workflow-orchestration #compliance #automation #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to compress the weeks between "this site needs new radios" and "on air" — tower-owner application, structural and mount analysis, landlord consent and municipal approval inside the FCC shot clock — and whoever gets the most sites through that chain fastest takes the deployment programme.

## The Problem
Each step needs a document built from others: a tower-owner application from the equipment list and drawings, a municipal eligible facilities request from the approved drawings and a narrative citing Section 6409(a), a landlord notice from the lease terms. Staff rebuild these by hand for every site.

## What Already Exists
Sitetracker (on Salesforce) and similar deployment platforms for milestones and documents; tower-owner portals; municipal e-permitting systems.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs document drafting from the site record and prior filings for each tower owner's and municipality's format; shot-clock logic that starts, tolls and resets according to FCC rules and sends deemed-granted notices; lease-entitlement checks before applications go out; and extraction of conditions from approvals back into the site record.

## Target Customer
Site development teams and site acquisition contractors already working in a deployment tracker.

## Impact If Solved
Staff review documents instead of writing them, and fewer municipal clocks lapse unnoticed in either direction.
