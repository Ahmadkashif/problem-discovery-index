# Site Modifications, Leasing & Permitting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wireless-carriers|Wireless Carriers]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to compress the weeks between "this site needs new radios" and "on air" — tower-owner application, structural and mount analysis, landlord consent and municipal approval inside the FCC shot clock — and whoever gets the most sites through that chain fastest takes the deployment programme.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3B a year in site acquisition, architecture and engineering, structural and mount analysis, permitting and lease administration services and software (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1% of carrier revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — the chain runs on PDFs, drawings, email and portal uploads, with status tracked in spreadsheets and Sitetracker
**Target Buyer:** VP Site Development & Real Estate
**Automation Potential:** Medium — document assembly and tracking automate well; negotiations, engineering sign-off and municipal hearings don't

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every 5G overlay, new band and capacity add means touching existing sites, and each touch is a chain of approvals with different owners. The tower owner reviews an application and may require a lease amendment. A structural engineer and a mount analyst must confirm the tower and mounts can carry the new load. The landlord may have consent rights, and the municipality must approve. Federal law sets the municipal clocks: an eligible facilities request under Section 6409(a) is deemed granted after 60 days, and small cells get 60 days on existing structures and 90 on new ones. Rent negotiation under master lease agreements is a separate, once-a-decade contest and is not part of this niche.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Sitetracker for programme tracking, tower owners' application portals, lease administration systems, structural analysis software at engineering firms, municipal e-permitting portals. The gaps: no prediction of which sites will stall; no reuse of prior analyses and drawings; shot clocks tracked by hand; and lease entitlement unknown until someone reads the PDF.
