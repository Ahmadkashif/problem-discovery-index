# IT AIOps Taught Radio and Transport Topology

**Niche:** [[niches/wireless-carriers/noc-alarm-outage-reporting/profile|NOC Alarm Correlation & Outage Reporting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/wireless-carriers|Wireless Carriers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** AIOps platforms already reduce alert noise for IT operations; a radio network's alarms, topology and regulatory clock are different enough that they work poorly out of the box.
**Tags:** #transformers #k-means-clustering #feature-engineering #data-integration #automation #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a flood of network alarms into the handful of incidents that need action, with a cause attached, fast enough to restore service and file the FCC outage report on time, and whoever gives the shortest time from first alarm to correct incident takes the network-operations contract.

## The Problem
Carriers adopt AIOps tools to cut ticket volume, then find that clustering by text and time misses radio-specific patterns, such as a transport failure upstream of fifty sites or a power event across a county.

## What Already Exists
ServiceNow IT operations management, BigPanda, Moogsoft and similar platforms, with event ingestion, deduplication, clustering and ticket integration.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs vendor alarm dictionaries for radio, microwave, fibre and power equipment; site, transport and power topology as correlation context; storm and planned-maintenance awareness; outage-scope estimation in user-minutes; and a link to NORS deadlines that flags incidents likely to require a report.

## Target Customer
NOC leaders at carriers that already run a general AIOps or ITOM platform.

## Impact If Solved
The platform the carrier already owns starts correlating like an experienced radio NOC operator.
