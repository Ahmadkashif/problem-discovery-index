# Frequency Coordination & Interference Analysis Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wireless-carriers|Wireless Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Before a carrier, utility or WISP can license a point-to-point microwave backhaul link, it must show the path won't interfere with existing licensees: it sends prior coordination notices, analyses interference against every nearby path and earth station, and certifies coordination to the FCC. Most applicants hire a professional coordinator to do this, Comsearch being the long-established leader alongside a few smaller firms. Carriers pay per link, per study or under an annual arrangement; the same firms model interference for spectrum transitions such as the C-band clearing.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-60 coordination and spectrum engineers at the largest firm; under 10 at most others (estimate)
**Output:** Interference analyses, prior coordination notices and responses, coordination certifications, spectrum studies
**Proprietary data:** A decades-long database of coordinated microwave paths, earth stations and their technical parameters, more complete and current in places than the FCC's licence records (estimate)
**Clock:** The Part 101 response window: objections to a prior coordination notice are due within 30 days (47 CFR 101.103(d)(2)(iv)), and licence filings follow
**Buyer:** Head of spectrum engineering at the coordination firm
