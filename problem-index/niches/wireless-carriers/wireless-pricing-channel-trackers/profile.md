# Wireless Pricing & Channel Trackers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wireless-carriers|Wireless Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Small research firms that track carrier plan prices, promotions, device deals and store-level sales behaviour, such as Wave7 Research (store and channel surveys) and Consumer Intelligence Research Partners (device buyer surveys). Carriers, device makers and investors subscribe for competitive intelligence.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically under 10 per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Promotion and pricing trackers, channel survey reports, quarterly notes
**Proprietary data:** Historical promotion databases and survey panels
**Clock:** Device launch cycles and carriers' earnings dates
**Buyer:** Founder or principal analyst
