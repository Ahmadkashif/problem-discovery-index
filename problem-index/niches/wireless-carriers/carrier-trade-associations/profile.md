# Wireless Carrier Trade Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/wireless-carriers|Wireless Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
CTIA (the national carriers' association, which publishes the annual wireless industry survey), the Competitive Carriers Association for regional and rural carriers, and WISPA for fixed wireless ISPs. They lobby, file at the FCC, run events and certification programmes, and publish a few headline statistics.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Research staff typically under 10; most headcount is policy, legal and events (estimate)
**Output:** Annual survey highlights, policy filings, white papers
**Proprietary data:** Member survey responses
**Clock:** FCC comment cycles and the associations' own event calendars
**Buyer:** Association president or head of research
