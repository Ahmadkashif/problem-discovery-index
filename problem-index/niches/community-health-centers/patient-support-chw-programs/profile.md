# Patient Support Services & Community Health Worker Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-health-centers|Community Health Centers]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a health center's high-risk patients in care through outreach, transport, interpretation and case management, and whoever keeps the largest share of a care team's high-risk panel with a kept visit inside its clinical interval takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2B a year, mostly the 30,424 patient support FTEs counted in UDS 2025 plus transport, interpretation and care-management software (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of health center FTEs (UDS Table 5); ~4% of revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — outreach lists in spreadsheets, call notes in free text, transport booked by phone, interpreter time on paper logs
**Target Buyer:** Director of patient support or care coordination; health center COO
**Automation Potential:** Medium — prioritisation, scheduling and logging automate; the trust that brings a patient back does not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Health centers are required to provide what HRSA used to call enabling services and from CY2026 calls patient support services: case management, eligibility assistance, outreach, transportation, interpretation and health education (the OMB-approved 2026 UDS tables (ICR 202606-0915-002, approved 3 September 2026)). In 2025 they employed 14,199 case manager FTEs, 3,337 community health worker FTEs, 2,591 outreach FTEs, 1,227 interpretation FTEs and 1,219 transport FTEs (UDS Table 5). CY2026 asks for the first time how many patients received each service (Table 6A lines 35–38). This work decides whether high-risk patients stay in care. It is the tacit judgement in the industry's high-impact problem.

## Current Tools & Gaps
EHR care-management modules, Azara and Relevant gap lists, Unite Us and findhelp for referrals, ride brokers and interpreter vendors. The general social-services caseload layer is [[niches/nonprofits-social-services/case-management-platform-data/profile|🔍 Case Management Platform Data]]. Gaps: no record of whom a worker prioritised and why; services logged outside the EHR; no link from effort to outcome.
