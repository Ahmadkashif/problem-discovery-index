# CHW and Care Coordination Billing Configured for FQHC Rules

**Niche:** [[niches/community-health-centers/patient-support-chw-programs/profile|Patient Support Services & Community Health Worker Programmes]]
**Industry:** [[industries/community-health-centers|Community Health Centers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Care-management and CHW billing tools exist for physician practices, but health centers bill these services under different Medicare and state Medicaid rules, so most of the work goes unbilled.
**Tags:** #feature-engineering #descriptive-statistics #workflow-orchestration #revenue-impact #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a health center's high-risk patients in care through outreach, transport, interpretation and case management, and whoever keeps the largest share of a care team's high-risk panel with a kept visit inside its clinical interval takes the account.

## The Problem
From 2026, Medicare no longer accepts the FQHC care management code G0511. Centers must report the individual care-coordination codes instead (NACHC, CY2026 PFS factsheet). State Medicaid programmes vary in whether and how CHW services are billable at a health center, and whether those services count inside PPS or outside it.

## What Already Exists
Chronic care management platforms and EHR care-management modules built for physician practices, as in [[niches/physician-practices/chronic-care-management-programs/profile|🟠 Chronic & Advanced Primary Care Management Programs]]; CHW documentation tools.

## The Customization Gap
- Medicare FQHC billing of the individual care-coordination codes after G0511;
- state Medicaid CHW benefit rules, and whether a service is a PPS encounter, a carve-out or unbillable;
- time and consent capture that meets both programmes;
- reports of billed against delivered patient support for the new UDS lines.

## Target Customer
Directors of care coordination and revenue cycle at centers with established CHW teams.

## Impact If Solved
Billing even a fifth of eligible care-coordination time (estimate) could bring in ~$50,000–$100,000 a year, enough for one or two additional CHW positions per mid-sized center.
