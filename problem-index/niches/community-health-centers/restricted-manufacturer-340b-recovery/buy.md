# Administrator Data Feeds Mapped to Each Manufacturer's Condition

**Niche:** [[niches/community-health-centers/restricted-manufacturer-340b-recovery/profile|Restricted-Manufacturer Claims Data & 340B Rebate Claims]]
**Industry:** [[industries/community-health-centers|Community Health Centers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** 340B administrators already hold the claims manufacturers ask for, but each manufacturer's data condition differs, and a health center needs the feeds configured so that each condition restores access with the least data shared.
**Tags:** #data-integration #feature-engineering #compliance #revenue-impact #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to recover the 340B discount on restricted manufacturers' drugs through their claims-data conditions and, from January 2027, the rebate pilot, and whoever recovers the most 340B savings per eligible restricted-drug prescription takes the account.

## The Problem
Some manufacturers restore one contract pharmacy per entity, some restore all pharmacies in return for data, and others refuse. The center must decide manufacturer by manufacturer what to submit.

## What Already Exists
Administrator export tools, manufacturer-designated data platforms, and 340B counsel advising on conditions.

## The Customization Gap
- each manufacturer's current condition and the pharmacies it would restore;
- the savings restored against the data fields shared, per manufacturer;
- automatic, minimised file generation from administrator data;
- state contract-pharmacy laws (21 states as of February 2026, search summary) noted where they change the decision.

## Target Customer
340B managers at centers with several contract pharmacies.

## Impact If Solved
Restoring one or two restricted manufacturers at five contract pharmacies could recover ~$100,000–$300,000 a year (estimate).
