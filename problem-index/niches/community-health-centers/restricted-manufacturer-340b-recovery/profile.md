# Restricted-Manufacturer Claims Data & 340B Rebate Claims

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-health-centers|Community Health Centers]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to recover the 340B discount on restricted manufacturers' drugs through their claims-data conditions and, from January 2027, the rebate pilot, and whoever recovers the most 340B savings per eligible restricted-drug prescription takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$0.1B a year of claims-data submission, rebate-claim and recovery services for health centers (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~25% of the parent 340B niche's spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — submissions go through manufacturer and third-party portals, configured by hand for each manufacturer
**Target Buyer:** 340B programme manager or pharmacy director
**Automation Potential:** High — each manufacturer's condition and each rebate claim is a defined data file on a clock

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
About 40 manufacturers restrict contract-pharmacy shipments, and many restore them only if the covered entity uploads claims-level data (search summary). The revised rebate pilot covers the 10 drugs on Medicare's 2026 negotiation list and the 15 on the 2027 list, about 5.5% of 340B sales. Covered entities buy at the ordinary price and claim the rebate within 45 days of dispense. Manufacturers must pay or deny within 10 days of a complete claim (Holland & Knight). The winner recovers money that would otherwise be lost, so the work and its vendors are distinct from audit defence.

## Current Tools & Gaps
340B administrators' data-submission features, manufacturer-designated platforms (named from recall: 340B ESP, Kalderos, Beacon) and pharmacy systems. Gaps: no comparison of savings restored against data given away per manufacturer; rebate denials go unworked; the 45-day clock is tracked in spreadsheets.
