# Medicaid PPS & Managed-Care Wraparound Revenue

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-health-centers|Community Health Centers]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to collect the full Medicaid PPS rate on every qualifying health center visit, including managed-care wraparound and change-in-scope adjustments, and whoever leaves the smallest gap between PPS due and PPS collected per Medicaid visit takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.2B a year of revenue cycle staff, outsourcing, software and reimbursement consulting spent on collecting health centers' $25.4B of Medicaid revenue (estimate; revenue from UDS 2025 via KFF)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% of health center revenue spent on collection, against 45.8% of revenue at stake (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — claims and remittances are electronic, but PPS-due tracking, wrap reconciliation and change-in-scope work run in spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** Health center CFO or reimbursement manager
**Automation Potential:** High — encounter rules, rates and settlements are written down by each state and applied to thousands of near-identical visits

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
No other ambulatory provider is owed a cost-based per-visit rate that it has to piece together from two payers. A health center bills the managed care plan, receives the plan's rate, and claims the difference up to its PPS rate from the state, through per-claim wrap, monthly supplemental payments or an annual reconciliation, depending on the state. Each state defines a billable encounter differently, including same-day medical and behavioral visits, dental and which staff count as billable providers. A new service line entitles the center to a change-in-scope rate adjustment only if it files within a state window. That runs from 60 days in Nevada to nine months in Indiana (search summary).

## Current Tools & Gaps
EHR billing modules (NextGen, eClinicalWorks, athenaOne, OCHIN Epic), revenue cycle outsourcers and FQHC reimbursement consultancies. The shared claim and denial layer is [[niches/healthcare-practice-software/ambulatory-rcm-modules/profile|🔵 Ambulatory Revenue Cycle Modules]] and [[niches/medical-billing/denial-management-analytics-vendors/profile|🔍 Denial Management Analytics Vendors]]. Gaps: no system holds PPS due per visit; plan payment changes surface only at settlement; change-in-scope eligibility is noticed late or never.
