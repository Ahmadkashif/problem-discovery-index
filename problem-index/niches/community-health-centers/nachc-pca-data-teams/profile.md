# NACHC & Primary Care Association Data Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-health-centers|Community Health Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The National Association of Community Health Centers and the state and regional primary care associations (PCAs) analyse UDS and member survey data for advocacy, training and technical assistance. They publish workforce, salary and financial surveys and support members through UDS season and site visits. Members pay dues; HRSA funds PCAs through cooperative agreements.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–20 research and policy analysts at NACHC (estimate); PCAs typically have under 10 data staff each
**Output:** Policy research, chartbooks, member salary and workforce surveys, state data profiles
**Proprietary data:** Member survey responses (salaries, workforce, finances), plus public UDS
**Clock:** Congressional funding deadlines (the Community Health Center Fund expires 31 December 2026, NACHC), which govern advocacy, not a billable deliverable
**Buyer:** NACHC research director; PCA CEOs
