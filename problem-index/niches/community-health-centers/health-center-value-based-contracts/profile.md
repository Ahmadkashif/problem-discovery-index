# Health Center Value-Based Contracts & FQHC-Led ACOs

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-health-centers|Community Health Centers]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to earn quality and shared-savings payments for a health center's attributed Medicaid and Medicare patients on top of the PPS floor, and whoever books the most value-based revenue per attributed patient takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$0.5B a year of enablement fees, population health software and care-team analytics for health centers in Medicaid ACOs, Medicaid managed care incentive programmes, MSSP and MA contracts (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1% of revenue in tooling; the share of revenue tied to value-based contracts is unmeasured until HRSA asks in CY2026 (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — claims feeds and gap dashboards exist, but attribution churn and outreach still run on lists and phones
**Target Buyer:** FQHC network or ACO executive director; health center CMO
**Automation Potential:** Medium — attribution, gap lists and PPS-floor reconciliation compute; keeping patients engaged does not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A health center enters value-based contracts with a floor no physician group has: Medicaid must still pay at least the PPS amount, so a capitated or alternative payment methodology has to be reconciled back to PPS (Social Security Act §1902(bb)(6), recalled). Its patients churn on and off Medicaid, which wrecks attribution. Its networks are built by centers themselves, for example Medical Home Network in Illinois and Community Care Cooperative in Massachusetts (named from recall). From CY2026, HRSA asks every center what share of revenue is tied to value-based contracts (Appendix D of the OMB-approved 2026 UDS tables (ICR 202606-0915-002, approved 3 September 2026)).

## Current Tools & Gaps
Medical Home Network, Community Care Cooperative, Aledade and HCCN-run programmes; Azara DRVS and Relevant for gap lists; payer portals. The primary-care contract work is [[niches/physician-practices/value-based-primary-care-contracts/profile|🔵 Primary Care in Value-Based Contracts (ACOs & MA Risk)]]. Gaps: no reconciliation of APM payments to the PPS floor; attribution lost when patients cycle through Medicaid; outreach effort is not linked to the contract metric it moves.
