# Health Center Controlled Networks & OCHIN Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-health-centers|Community Health Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Health center controlled networks (HCCNs) are HRSA-funded collaboratives through which health centers share EHR hosting, data warehouses and analytics. HRSA funded 52 networks supporting 1,300+ centers in August 2025 (search summary). The largest, OCHIN, hosts a single Epic instance for 2,000+ delivery sites in 41 states and leads the PCORI-funded ADVANCE clinical research network, which pools community health center EHR data for research (search summary). Members pay for hosting and services; research is funded by PCORI, NIH and foundations.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–80 researchers and analysts at OCHIN (estimate); most other HCCNs have under 10
**Output:** Peer-reviewed studies and pragmatic trials, research datasets, member quality dashboards and UDS support
**Proprietary data:** Longitudinal EHR records of millions of safety-net patients, the deepest community health center corpus in the country
**Clock:** Funders' grant cycles and HRSA's HCCN reporting, plus members' UDS deadlines (a client clock)
**Buyer:** OCHIN's research leadership; HCCN executive directors
