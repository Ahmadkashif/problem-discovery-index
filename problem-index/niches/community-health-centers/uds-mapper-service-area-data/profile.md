# UDS Mapper & Service-Area Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-health-centers|Community Health Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
The UDS Mapper, built by HealthLandscape with the American Academy of Family Physicians and HRSA support (named from recall), maps each health center's patients by ZIP code against population, poverty and other centers' footprints. Applicants use it to define service areas, show unmet need and prepare Service Area Competition and New Access Point applications. Needs assessments built on it are a service sold by consultancies.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 developers and analysts (estimate)
**Output:** Service-area maps, penetration and unmet-need estimates
**Proprietary data:** ZIP-level patient origin data from UDS, combined with Census data, much of it public
**Clock:** HRSA's funding notice deadlines, which bind the applicant, not the tool
**Buyer:** HRSA; health center planning staff
