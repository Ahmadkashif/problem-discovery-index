# Title X Data & Evaluation Contractors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-health-centers|Community Health Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
HHS's Office of Population Affairs collects annual family planning data from Title X grantees and publishes the national summary, with research contractors compiling and analysing it (named from recall). The same office sets programme rules and funds training centres for grantees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analysts per contract (estimate)
**Output:** The annual family planning report, evaluations, programme guidance
**Proprietary data:** Grantee-reported service data, aggregated
**Clock:** The office's own reporting cycle
**Buyer:** HHS Office of Population Affairs
