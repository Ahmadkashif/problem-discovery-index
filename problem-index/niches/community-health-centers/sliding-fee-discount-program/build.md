# Income Evidence Captured Once and Reused Until It Expires

**Niche:** [[niches/community-health-centers/sliding-fee-discount-program/profile|Sliding Fee Discount Programme & Income Documentation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/community-health-centers|Community Health Centers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A health center collects pay stubs at the window, keys one income number and loses the link between the discount and the document, so the next site visit means pulling charts to rebuild the evidence.
**Tags:** #transformers #large-language-models #cnns #evaluation-metrics #compliance #automation #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make every sliding-fee discount traceable to documented household income and size under the center's board-approved schedule, and whoever leaves the fewest sliding-fee conditions at HRSA's operational site visit takes the account.

## The Problem
The discount tier in the billing system is a number with no history. Auditors and site visitors sample patients and ask for the evidence. Staff search scanned documents that may be expired, mislabelled or missing, and the household definition used at the window may not match the policy.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
EHR registration was designed for insurance cards, not income evidence. The rules belong to each center's board policy, so there is no national rule set to sell, and the work is unglamorous compliance.

## What to Build
A pre-visit text link or kiosk that collects household and income answers in the patient's language and reads photographed documents with a document model. It proposes the tier under the center's own schedule, stores the evidence with an expiry date, and reuses it until it lapses. Self-declaration remains a first-class path for cash income. A sampling report mirrors what site visitors check.

## Target Customer
Patient access directors and compliance officers at centers with 10,000+ patients and a site visit in the coming year.

## Impact If Built
At ~12 checks a day per representative, saving ~8 minutes each frees ~1.5 hours a day (estimate), and a site-visit sample that takes an afternoon instead of a week of chart pulls.
