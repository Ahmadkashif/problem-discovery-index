# Sliding Fee Discount Programme & Income Documentation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-health-centers|Community Health Centers]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make every sliding-fee discount traceable to documented household income and size under the center's board-approved schedule, and whoever leaves the fewest sliding-fee conditions at HRSA's operational site visit takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$0.3B a year of front-desk time, patient financial services staff and tools spent on income documentation and discount determination (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.5% of revenue (estimate); the discounts themselves apply to the 5.6M uninsured patients and many underinsured (UDS 2025)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — photocopied pay stubs, paper self-declarations and a registration field holding one income figure
**Target Buyer:** Director of patient access or revenue cycle; compliance officer
**Automation Potential:** Medium-High — the schedule and documentation rules are written down; the conversation with the patient is not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every Section 330 awardee and look-alike must keep a sliding fee discount programme: a board-approved schedule based on the federal poverty guidelines, full or nominal-charge discount at or below 100% FPL, sliding discounts to 200%, a documented policy on income, household and re-verification, and periodic evaluation of whether the fees are a barrier (HRSA Compliance Manual, chapter 9, recalled). HRSA checks it at operational site visits, and a condition on the award follows a failure. Hospital financial assistance tools follow a different statute and different rules.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Registration screens in NextGen, eClinicalWorks, athenaOne and OCHIN Epic hold a household size and income; scanned documents sit in the document tab. The vendor-side record design that would collect income once is in [[niches/healthcare-practice-software/fqhc-health-center-software/profile|🟣 FQHC & Community Health Center Software]]. Gaps: no evidence trail linking discount to document; no expiry tracking; no analysis of whether nominal charges deter visits.
