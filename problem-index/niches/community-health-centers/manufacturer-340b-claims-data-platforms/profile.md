# Manufacturer-Side 340B Claims-Data & Rebate Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-health-centers|Community Health Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
These platforms collect claims-level data from 340B covered entities and contract pharmacies on behalf of drug manufacturers. They match it against Medicaid and commercial rebate claims to find duplicate discounts, and from January 2027 they process rebate claims under HRSA's pilot. 340B ESP, Kalderos and Beacon Channel Management are representative (named from recall). Manufacturers pay for the matching, the duplicate-discount findings that support rebate disputes and audit requests, and the rebate administration. Health centers are the data suppliers, not the buyers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–80 data analysts, 340B specialists and data scientists per platform (estimate); claims-processing staff are production
**Output:** Duplicate-discount and diversion findings by covered entity and pharmacy, rebate validation decisions, manufacturer 340B exposure analytics
**Proprietary data:** Contract-pharmacy and covered-entity claims submitted as a condition of 340B access, matched to rebate claims across many manufacturers
**Clock:** From 1 January 2027 the rebate pilot sets a 10-day pay-or-deny window after each complete claim and a 45-day submission window for covered entities (Holland & Knight). The 10-day window binds each claim, which is production work, and the 45-day window binds covered entities, not the platform
**Buyer:** Head of 340B or trade operations at a manufacturer
