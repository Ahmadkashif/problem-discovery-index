# FQHC Reimbursement, PPS Rate-Setting & Change-in-Scope Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-health-centers|Community Health Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation firms

## What They Do
Accounting and consulting firms with health center practices prepare Medicare FQHC cost reports and Medicaid cost reports, model PPS and APM rates, file change-in-scope requests and reconcile wraparound settlements. Forvis Mazars, Wipfli, CLA, Eide Bailly and BerryDunn are representative (named from recall). Centers pay by engagement.

## Insight Function
**Size:** FQHC practices of ~10–40 per firm (estimate), almost all per-center consultants and preparers, who are production under the vault's ruling; rate-methodology specialists are under 10 per firm
**Output:** Filed cost reports, rate requests, settlement reviews
**Proprietary data:** Client cost reports and visit data held under engagement letters
**Clock:** State change-in-scope windows and cost-report due dates, clients' clocks
**Buyer:** Health center CFO
