# Health Center Financial Benchmarking & Capital Advisors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-health-centers|Community Health Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation firms

## What They Do
Capital Link, a nonprofit, maintains a database of health center audited financial statements joined to UDS data and publishes national and state performance analyses. Its June 2026 report covers 1,512 organisations and 1,204 FY2024 audits, holding 68% or more of national health center audits each year. It also sells performance evaluation profiles, capital planning studies and loan packaging to centers, PCAs and lenders. Health centers, PCAs, HRSA cooperative agreements and lenders pay.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–20 financial analysts and consultants (estimate)
**Output:** Benchmark reports, center-level performance profiles, capital feasibility studies
**Proprietary data:** A structured, multi-year history of audited financials, which are public documents through single audit filings, joined to public UDS data
**Clock:** Its own publication calendar; clients' financing and board dates
**Buyer:** Health center CFO; PCA or lender
