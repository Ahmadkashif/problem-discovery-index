# HRSA Bureau of Primary Health Care Data & Compliance Review

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-health-centers|Community Health Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
HRSA's Bureau of Primary Health Care runs the Health Center Program. It collects and reviews the UDS from 1,358 awardees and 171 look-alikes (Federal Register 2025-22443), publishes national, state and center-level data, conducts operational site visits against the Compliance Manual, and deems centers for FTCA coverage. Contracted UDS reviewers and site-visit consultants do much of the per-center work.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 federal and contractor staff on UDS analysis and data products (estimate); per-center UDS reviewers and per-site visit reviewers are production
**Output:** Published UDS data, programme performance analysis, compliance determinations and conditions on awards
**Proprietary data:** Every health center's UDS submission over decades, of which the aggregates are public
**Clock:** Its own reporting calendar (the UDS cycle, Q4 2 under the vault's ruling) and appropriations cycles
**Buyer:** The Bureau's data and quality leadership; a single federal buyer
