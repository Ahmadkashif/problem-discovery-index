# 340B at the Health Center (Covered-Entity Side)

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-health-centers|Community Health Centers]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a health center's 340B savings intact, but recovering the discount on restricted manufacturers' drugs and limiting adverse findings in HRSA's covered-entity audits are separate contests, with different vendors, different staff and different winners. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$0.4B a year of 340B administrator fees, split-billing software, claims-data submissions and audit consulting paid by health centers (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.7% of revenue in administration (estimate); 340B savings fund a much larger share of sliding-fee pharmacy and patient support services
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — claims flow electronically through administrators, but manufacturer conditions, rebate claims and audit evidence are assembled by a 340B coordinator
**Target Buyer:** Health center 340B programme manager, pharmacy director or CFO
**Automation Potential:** High — eligibility, exclusion-file checks, data submissions and rebate claims are rules applied to every prescription

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Health centers are 340B covered entities. They buy outpatient drugs at the ceiling price and must use the savings within the scope of the Section 330 project. They dispense through in-house pharmacies and contract pharmacies. Roughly 40 manufacturers now restrict contract-pharmacy shipments, many demanding claims data as a condition (search summary). From 1 January 2027, HRSA's revised rebate pilot lets approved manufacturers pay 340B pricing on 25 Medicare-negotiated drugs as a rebate. Covered entities then have 45 days from dispense to claim it (Holland & Knight). HRSA audits covered entities for diversion and Medicaid duplicate discounts. The pharmacy side is [[niches/pharmacy-independents/340b-contract-pharmacy/profile|🟠 340B Contract Pharmacy]], and administrators are [[niches/pharmacy-independents/340b-program-administration/profile|🔍 340B Program Administration & Compliance]] (46).

## Current Tools & Gaps
340B administrators (Sentry, Macro Helix, Verity), split-billing software, manufacturer data portals, and 340B legal and audit consultants. Gaps: no single view of savings lost to restrictions; rebate claims are a new workflow; Medicaid carve-in and carve-out status is tracked by hand per state and plan.
