# 340B Administrator Reporting Configured for Section 330 Scope

**One-liner:** 340B administrators report accumulations and replenishment, but a health center must also show that savings were used for its HRSA-approved project and how they funded sliding-fee pharmacy access.

**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a health center's 340B savings intact, but recovering the discount on restricted manufacturers' drugs and limiting adverse findings in HRSA's covered-entity audits are separate contests, with different vendors, different staff and different winners. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Boards, auditors and HRSA site visitors ask how 340B savings support the health center's scope of project. The administrator's reports answer a pharmacy question, not a programme question.

## What Already Exists
Sentry, Macro Helix and Verity administrator portals, pharmacy management systems and general ledger reports.

## The Customization Gap
- savings by site and service tied to the Section 330 scope of project;
- sliding-fee discounts given at the pharmacy, by patient tier;
- programme income reporting consistent with the audited financials;
- comparisons across administrators when a center uses more than one.

## Target Customer
CFOs and compliance officers at centers with multiple administrators.

## Impact If Solved
A single scope-aligned report saves ~2–3 days of finance staff time before each board review and site visit (estimate).
