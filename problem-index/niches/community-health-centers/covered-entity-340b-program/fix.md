# Savings Reported to the Board as One Number

**Niche:** [[niches/community-health-centers/covered-entity-340b-program/profile|340B at the Health Center (Covered-Entity Side)]]
**Industry:** [[industries/community-health-centers|Community Health Centers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The board sees one 340B savings figure that has fallen for three years, with no split between manufacturer restrictions, lost contract pharmacies, Medicaid carve-ins and dispensing volume.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #causal-inference #feature-engineering #revenue-impact #compliance
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a health center's 340B savings intact, but recovering the discount on restricted manufacturers' drugs and limiting adverse findings in HRSA's covered-entity audits are separate contests, with different vendors, different staff and different winners. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Leadership cannot decide whether to open an in-house pharmacy, drop a contract pharmacy or file manufacturer data without knowing which factor drove the decline.

## Why It's Still Broken
The causes sit in different systems: administrator accumulations, manufacturer notices, state Medicaid carve-in rules and pharmacy volume.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A variance bridge from last year's savings to this year's, by cause, built from the prescription ledger, with what-if estimates for an in-house pharmacy or for submitting claims data to each restricting manufacturer.

## Who Feels the Pain
CFOs, pharmacy directors and boards budgeting the services 340B pays for.

## Impact If Fixed
A center whose savings fell from $3M to $2M (estimate) can see which $1M of causes are recoverable before it cuts services.
