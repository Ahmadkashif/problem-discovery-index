# Medicaid Managed Care Plans' FQHC Payment & Incentive Units

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-health-centers|Community Health Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Medicaid managed care plans contract with health centers, pay their negotiated rates, report payments for the state's wraparound process, and run quality incentive and alternative payment programmes for centers. Provider reimbursement and network analytics teams inside each plan do the work.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analysts per plan dedicated to FQHC payment and incentives (estimate); claims processors are production
**Output:** Wrap reporting files, incentive calculations, APM settlements
**Proprietary data:** Member claims and encounter data
**Clock:** State contract reporting dates, the plan's own obligations
**Buyer:** Plan director of provider reimbursement or value-based programmes
