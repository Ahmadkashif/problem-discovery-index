# Grant and Payer Reporting Generated From the EHR

**Niche:** [[niches/community-health-centers/title-x-family-planning-centers/profile|Title X & Independent Family Planning Centres]]
**Industry:** [[industries/community-health-centers|Community Health Centers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** EHR reporting modules produce visit and revenue reports, but a family planning centre must report to Title X, state programmes and each payer in different shapes, and assembles them by hand each time funding rules change.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #data-integration #large-language-models #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to replace unstable grant dollars with insurer payments without breaking Title X confidentiality, and whoever bills the largest share of confidential visits to an insurer takes the account.

## The Problem
Title X family planning annual reporting, state family planning waiver reports and payer quality submissions each count users, services and payer source differently. Every funding change adds another report.

## What Already Exists
EHR report writers, BI tools, and the reporting templates published by HHS's Office of Population Affairs.

## The Customization Gap
- one mapping from visit data to each funder's definitions of user, encounter and service;
- payer-source tracking that keeps confidential visits correctly attributed;
- a way to re-run any report when a grant's terms change mid-year;
- checks for small-cell disclosure before data leaves the centre.

## Target Customer
Finance and data leads at Title X grantees and sub-recipients.

## Impact If Solved
Cutting each funder report from ~40 to ~10 hours (estimate) saves ~150 hours a year for a centre filing five reports.
