# Audit Software Configured for Health Center Patient Definitions

**Niche:** [[niches/community-health-centers/340b-duplicate-discount-audit/profile|Medicaid Duplicate-Discount Prevention & HRSA 340B Audit]]
**Industry:** [[industries/community-health-centers|Community Health Centers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** 340B compliance software is built mostly for hospitals, but a health center's patient definition turns on its Section 330 scope, its sites and providers, and its sliding-fee and Medicaid status.
**Tags:** #feature-engineering #data-integration #compliance #automation #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep every 340B purchase defensible against diversion and Medicaid duplicate-discount findings, and whoever has the fewest adverse findings per HRSA 340B audit takes the account.

## The Problem
Hospital-oriented audit tools test against hospital outpatient department rules. A health center's eligible sites are those in its HRSA-approved scope, and its providers include contracted and volunteer clinicians.

## What Already Exists
Administrator compliance dashboards, hospital 340B audit software and independent audit services.

## The Customization Gap
- eligibility tied to the Section 330 scope of project (Form 5A sites and Form 5B services);
- provider rosters that include contracted, locum and volunteer clinicians;
- state and plan carve-in rules for Medicaid managed care;
- reports in the form HRSA's audit protocol uses.

## Target Customer
Compliance officers at multi-site centers.

## Impact If Solved
Configured software can replace ~40 hours a quarter of manual self-audit sampling (estimate) with exception review.
