# UDS Reporting & Data Validation (CY2026 Tables)

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-health-centers|Community Health Centers]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce a health center's UDS tables directly from patient-level records, and whoever submits the CY2026 report with the fewest unresolved HRSA edit flags at review takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$0.2B a year of analyst time, UDS reporting software and consulting; OMB approved 304,616 burden hours for the 2026 UDS, including grant reports (ICR 202606-0915-002; dollars estimated)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.4% of revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium-High — data is electronic, but mapping, reconciliation and edit resolution are manual
**Target Buyer:** Health center quality director or UDS lead; HCCN data director
**Automation Potential:** High — table specifications, value sets and edit checks are published and repeat every year

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every awardee and look-alike, 1,605 UDS reports in the December 2025 notice's estimate, files the same report each February under the same manual, and HRSA publishes every center's results. The CY2026 report changes Tables 4, 6A, 8A, 9D, 9E and Appendix D (the OMB-approved 2026 UDS tables (ICR 202606-0915-002, approved 3 September 2026)). Moving Tables 6B and 7 to CMS's 2026 eCQM specifications was proposed in the December 2025 notice (FR 2025-22443); final manual not seen. HRSA paused patient-level UDS+ submission in April 2025 (search summary), so the submission of record is still the aggregate tables. This niche is the health center's own reporting function. The vendor-side contest, a single encounter record that yields UDS, sliding fee and 340B, is [[niches/healthcare-practice-software/fqhc-health-center-software/profile|🟣 FQHC & Community Health Center Software]], and the physician-practice counterpart is [[niches/physician-practices/mips-quality-reporting/profile|⚡ MIPS & Quality Measure Reporting]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
EHR UDS report packs, Azara DRVS, Relevant, HCCN shared reporting, and HRSA's own edit checks in the Electronic Handbooks. Gaps: no readiness check before submission; mapping for new lines left to the analyst; no benchmarking against the public data until a reviewer asks.
