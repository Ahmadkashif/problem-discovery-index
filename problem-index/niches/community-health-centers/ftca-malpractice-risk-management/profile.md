# FTCA Malpractice Coverage & Health Center Risk Management

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-health-centers|Community Health Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Deemed health centers and their clinicians are covered for medical malpractice under the Federal Tort Claims Act, so the federal government, not an insurer, defends and pays claims. Centers apply for deeming each year with credentialing, privileging and risk-management evidence. HRSA funds a risk-management training and content programme for deemed centers, run by ECRI (named from recall). Gap coverage for non-deemed activities is bought from commercial insurers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 per organisation on health-center-specific risk analysis (estimate); claims attorneys are case-by-case production
**Output:** Deeming determinations, risk-management curricula, claims defence
**Proprietary data:** FTCA claims files, which are privileged litigation records
**Clock:** Annual deeming applications, a client clock for centers
**Buyer:** HRSA's FTCA programme; health center risk managers
