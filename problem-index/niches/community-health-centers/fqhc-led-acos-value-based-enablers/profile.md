# FQHC-Led ACOs & Value-Based Enablers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-health-centers|Community Health Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
These organisations contract with Medicaid plans, states and Medicare on behalf of groups of health centers, then run the analytics and care-management support that earn quality and shared-savings payments. Medical Home Network, a public benefit corporation that has partnered with FQHCs since 2009 (its Lever posting), and Community Care Cooperative in Massachusetts (named from recall) are examples, alongside general enablers that also serve centers. They are paid a share of savings or a management fee.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–100 analysts, data engineers and care-management staff per enabler (estimate)
**Output:** Attribution and risk reports, gap lists, contract settlement estimates, care-team outreach lists
**Proprietary data:** Payer claims and member health centers' EHR data, joined across the network
**Clock:** Contract performance years and payer reporting dates, which are the clients' and payers' clocks
**Buyer:** Network executive director or chief analytics officer
