# UDS & Population Health Analytics Vendors for Health Centers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-health-centers|Community Health Centers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
These vendors sit on top of health center EHRs and compute UDS measures, quality gap lists, risk stratification and peer benchmarks. Azara DRVS reports 700+ health centers and 30+ PCAs and networks as clients (search summary). Relevant serves 90+ health centers with 25 staff (Relevant's own page). Population health platforms such as Arcadia and Innovaccer also serve some centers. Health centers and PCAs pay subscriptions; the insight is part of software, not a separate invoice.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 measure, data and clinical analysts at Azara (estimate); under 10 at Relevant, whose whole company is 25 people; implementation and data-mapping staff are production
**Output:** Measure dashboards, gap lists, UDS pre-submission reports, peer benchmarks across client centers
**Proprietary data:** Nightly EHR extracts from hundreds of health centers, mapped to UDS and HEDIS measures
**Clock:** Clients' February UDS submissions and payers' quality deadlines, client clocks (Q4 4 at most)
**Buyer:** Health center quality director or CIO; PCA data lead

**Kill switches:** **HIPAA**, and **data isn't theirs**. The extracts are client PHI held under business associate agreements, and no checked terms give the vendors product rights to pooled use.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. These vendors are where the engineering jobs are (Relevant's Lead Engineer, Azara's Implementation and Senior Software Engineer roles; see the hub), but they sell software on client PHI, not insight.
