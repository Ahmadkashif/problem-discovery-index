# Mobile Clinic Scheduling Adapted to Harvest Calendars

**Niche:** [[niches/community-health-centers/agricultural-worker-health-programs/profile|Agricultural Worker Health Programmes]]
**Industry:** [[industries/community-health-centers|Community Health Centers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Route-planning and scheduling software exists, but a migrant health programme must plan mobile clinics around crop calendars, camp locations and growers' shift times that change weekly.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #optimization-fundamentals #gradient-boosting #workflow-orchestration #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a mobile agricultural worker's chronic care continuous as the worker moves between seasons, farms and states, and whoever keeps the largest share of returning workers with hypertension or diabetes still controlled at their first visit of the season takes the account.

## The Problem
A mobile unit that arrives the week after the harvest crew leaves sees nobody. Outreach staff plan from memory and grower phone calls.

## What Already Exists
Field-service routing tools, EHR scheduling for mobile sites, and growers' own labour scheduling.

## The Customization Gap
- forecasts of worker arrivals by county from crop calendars, past seasons' visits and H-2A labour certification data;
- clinic placement around camp locations and shift ends, not 9-to-5 templates;
- staffing by language mix;
- linking each stop to the returning-patient list and their care summaries.

## Target Customer
Migrant health programme directors running two or more mobile units.

## Impact If Solved
Placing stops in the right week could raise visits per mobile day by ~20% (estimate), roughly 300 extra visits a season for a unit seeing 1,500.
