# IP Video Monitoring Extended to Hybrid Satellite Delivery

**Niche:** [[niches/satellite-operators/video-distribution-neighbourhoods/profile|Video Distribution & Contribution]]
**Industry:** [[industries/satellite-operators|Satellite Operators]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Streaming services monitor quality of every stream end to end; satellite video operators monitor the uplink and the transponder and stop there, so they can't show a broadcaster how satellite compares with its IP path.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche is fighting to keep broadcasters and pay-TV platforms on its established video neighbourhoods as channels move to IP delivery, and whoever proves, headend by headend and channel by channel, that satellite is still the cheaper and more reliable path keeps the capacity renewal.

## The Problem
Broadcasters increasingly run hybrid distribution: satellite to headends and dishes, IP to some distributors and apps. When they decide whether to keep a channel on satellite, they compare availability, latency and cost per channel. The satellite operator usually can't produce comparable numbers for its own path.

## What Already Exists
End-to-end video quality monitoring used by streaming and broadcast operations (Touchstream, Telestream, Mediaproxy-type products), and satellite carrier monitoring from Kratos.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: availability measured at the headend receive side, including rain fade and sun outage seasons; per-channel and per-region availability reports in the form broadcasters use for their IP paths; cost-per-channel models that include the headend's existing equipment; and side-by-side reporting of the satellite and IP paths for the same channel.

## Target Customer
Video operations and media sales teams at GEO operators defending renewals.

## Impact If Solved
The operator brings evidence to each renewal, and broadcasters can compare like with like instead of assuming IP is better.
