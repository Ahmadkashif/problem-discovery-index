# Aviation & Maritime Connectivity

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/satellite-operators|Satellite Operators]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep every aircraft and vessel connected at a guaranteed speed as it crosses beams, orbits and borders, and whoever can show the airline or ship operator per-flight and per-voyage performance it trusts takes the fleet contract.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$6B a year in global aviation and maritime satellite connectivity revenue (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~6% of global satellite services revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High — terminals report continuously; performance reporting to customers is still assembled by hand
**Target Buyer:** VP Mobility at the operator; head of connectivity at the airline or ship manager
**Automation Potential:** High for capacity steering and reporting

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Mobility is where satellite capacity is sold per platform rather than per site, and where the platform moves through dozens of beams, several satellites and many national licensing regimes in a day. Airlines buy passenger Wi-Fi and cockpit links; ship managers buy crew welfare, operational and cruise-passenger connectivity. Viasat (with Inmarsat), SES (with Intelsat's aviation business), Panasonic Avionics, Gogo, Anuvu, and Starlink Aviation and Maritime compete for fleets; Marlink, Speedcast and KVH resell and integrate for ships. Airlines now switch suppliers on passenger experience, and Starlink's entry made free, fast Wi-Fi a marketing promise rather than a premium product.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Network management and capacity steering in each operator's platform; terminal telemetry from aircraft and ship modems; airline IT service desks; monthly performance packs built in spreadsheets. The gaps: demand forecasting per beam that uses flight schedules and vessel routes, attribution of a bad flight's Wi-Fi to its real cause, and performance reports the customer believes.
