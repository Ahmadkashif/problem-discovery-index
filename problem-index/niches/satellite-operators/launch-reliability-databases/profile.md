# Satellite & Launch Reliability Databases

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/satellite-operators|Satellite Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Compilers of satellite and launch histories — every launch, satellite, anomaly and failure — used by insurers to price risk and by manufacturers and operators to benchmark reliability. Seradata's SpaceTrak, now part of Slingshot Aerospace, is the commercial reference; free catalogues such as Jonathan McDowell's and CelesTrak cover orbits and launches.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 compilers and analysts (estimate)
**Output:** Subscription database of launches, satellites, anomalies and insurance events; reliability statistics
**Proprietary data:** Decades of curated anomaly and failure records
**Clock:** None beyond insurers' underwriting cycles
**Buyer:** Product owner within the parent company
