# Catastrophe Event Imagery & Damage Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/satellite-operators|Satellite Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Earth-observation operators and analysts who image floods, wildfires and storms within hours and deliver flood extent and depth, burn scars and damage assessments by property. ICEYE (whose radar satellites see through cloud and at night), McKenzie Intelligence Services (which runs event intelligence for Lloyd's market participants) and Floodbase are representative. Property insurers, reinsurers, catastrophe bond investors and governments pay to estimate losses and triage claims.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-40 event analysts and geospatial scientists per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Event footprints, flood depth and damage layers by property, loss-triage reports
**Proprietary data:** The firm's own satellite collections over each event (for ICEYE) and an archive of event footprints matched to later claims
**Clock:** The first days after an event, when insurers estimate losses and reinsurers expect notifications; event timing is set by nature, not by a filing deadline
**Buyer:** Head of insurance solutions / Chief Product Officer
