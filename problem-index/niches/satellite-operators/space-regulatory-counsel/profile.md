# Space & Spectrum Regulatory Counsel

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/satellite-operators|Satellite Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
The space and satellite practices of communications law firms, such as Wiley, Hogan Lovells, Covington, Wilkinson Barker Knauer and Steptoe, which prepare FCC space station and earth station applications, petitions and oppositions, milestone extension and waiver requests, and advise on ITU matters and market access. Operators pay hourly or by matter.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-30 attorneys, engineers and analysts per larger space practice (estimate)
**Output:** Applications, pleadings, waiver and extension requests, regulatory opinions
**Proprietary data:** Matter files and precedent, held under privilege
**Clock:** FCC pleading cycles after public notice, milestone and bond dates, and the response periods in each proceeding
**Buyer:** Practice leader at the law firm
