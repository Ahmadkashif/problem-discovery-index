# EO Data Marketplaces & Tasking Aggregators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/satellite-operators|Satellite Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Platforms that resell archive imagery and tasking from many earth-observation operators through one interface and API: UP42 (owned by Airbus), SkyWatch, Arlula and SkyFi are representative. Developers, analytics firms and enterprises pay per image or by subscription; the platform takes a margin and handles licensing and delivery.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Platform engineering and sales; no dedicated analyst function (estimate)
**Output:** Search, ordering and delivery of other operators' imagery
**Proprietary data:** Order and search logs; the imagery belongs to the operators
**Clock:** Customers' delivery windows
**Buyer:** Chief Executive / head of product
