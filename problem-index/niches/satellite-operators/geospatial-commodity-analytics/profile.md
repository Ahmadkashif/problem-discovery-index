# Satellite-Derived Commodity & Economic Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/satellite-operators|Satellite Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms that buy optical and radar imagery from satellite operators and turn it into economic measurements: crude oil held in floating-roof tanks, stockpiles at ports and mines, activity at factories and car parks, construction progress. Ursa Space Systems, Kayrros, Privateer (which absorbed Orbital Insight), SpaceKnow and RS Metrics are representative. Commodity traders, hedge funds and energy analysts pay for the readings ahead of official statistics.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-40 analysts and data scientists per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Weekly and daily inventory estimates by site and region, activity indices, research notes
**Proprietary data:** The measurement archive per site, the site databases and the error history of past readings against official figures; the imagery itself is licensed non-exclusively from operators that sell it, archive included, to anyone
**Clock:** Official releases the readings anticipate, such as the US Energy Information Administration's weekly petroleum status report; a reading delivered after the release is worth much less
**Buyer:** Head of research / Chief Product Officer

**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. The shape resembles [[niches/hedge-funds/alt-data-kpi-research-providers/profile|Alternative Data KPI Research Providers]] (56) and [[niches/data-analytics-consultants/alternative-data-research-providers/profile|Alternative Data Research Providers]] (56, Q3 5), but Q3 is 3 rather than 5 for two reasons. Their panels are often held under exclusive agreements, while satellite imagery, archive included, is sold to anyone, so a rival can rebuild a site's history by buying the same back catalogue. And their moat is a learned, company-specific mapping from panel to reported financials, while a tank-fill or stockpile reading is geometry any competent rival can reproduce; only the error history against official releases is truly the firm's own. Measurement is also largely automated per site (Q2 3 against their 5, with teams of tens rather than hundreds), and the field is a handful of small firms, several of which have shrunk or been absorbed.
