# Space Industry Research & Forecasting Houses

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/satellite-operators|Satellite Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Research and consulting firms that sell market forecasts, capacity supply-and-demand models, pricing benchmarks and strategy studies on satellite communications, earth observation and the wider space economy. Novaspace (formed in 2024 from Euroconsult and SpaceTec Partners, ~130 professionals), NSR within Analysys Mason, Quilty Space and BryceTech are representative; BryceTech also writes SIA's annual State of the Satellite Industry report. Operators, manufacturers, investors, space agencies and regulators buy subscriptions and commissioned studies.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~15-60 research analysts per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Syndicated market reports and forecasts, capacity pricing and fill benchmarks, commissioned strategy and due-diligence studies
**Proprietary data:** Each firm's forecast archive and operator databases, assembled largely from public filings, launches and company reporting
**Clock:** The firms' own publication calendars and conferences (Novaspace's reports are timed to its Paris World Space Business Week), plus WRC cycles and investors' deal timetables
**Buyer:** Head of research / managing director
