# Space Situational Awareness & Tracking Data Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/satellite-operators|Satellite Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Companies that track objects in orbit with their own radars and telescopes or fuse others' data, and sell orbit catalogues, conjunction screening, manoeuvre detection and characterisation of satellites' behaviour. LeoLabs (ground radars for LEO), ExoAnalytic Solutions (a large telescope network watching GEO), Slingshot Aerospace, COMSPOC and Kayhan Space are representative. Satellite operators, insurers and, above all, the US Space Force and allied militaries pay.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-80 orbit analysts, astrodynamicists and data scientists per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Conjunction warnings and screening services, orbit catalogues, manoeuvre and anomaly reports on objects of interest
**Proprietary data:** Observations from the firm's own sensors, built up over years, and the orbit solutions derived from them
**Clock:** The time of closest approach on every conjunction, which no one negotiates, plus defence customers' tasking
**Buyer:** Chief Technology Officer / head of space domain awareness products

**Kill switches:** none for the commercial screening business scoped here; defence work that carries classification is left out of the pocket.
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. Owned sensor data and a hard physical clock, but screening is automated software that scales without analysts, a free government service (Space-Track, and TraCSS while it is funded) caps commercial pricing, and the market is a handful of vendors whose largest buyer is the military.
