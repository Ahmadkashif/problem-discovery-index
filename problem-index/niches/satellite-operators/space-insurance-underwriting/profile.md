# Space Insurance Underwriters & Brokers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/satellite-operators|Satellite Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The Lloyd's syndicates and specialist insurers that cover launch and in-orbit risk, and the brokers' space teams (Gallagher, Marsh, Aon, Willis) that place it. They assess each satellite's design heritage, the launcher's record and the operator's anomaly history, price the policy, and pay claims after launch failures or in-orbit losses. Premiums ran ~$557M in 2023 against losses that made it one of the worst years on record; Gallagher put 2025 at about $500M of net profit to insurers after rates rose and claims fell. Several insurers (Allianz, AIG, Swiss Re, Brit) left the market.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically under 10 underwriters, technical advisers and claims specialists per insurer or broker space team; no firm found with a 10+ space analysis function (estimate)
**Output:** Risk assessments and pricing per launch and satellite, placement reports, claims adjustments
**Proprietary data:** Loss and claims histories, and operator-supplied engineering disclosures under non-disclosure agreements
**Clock:** Launch dates and policy attachment, in-orbit renewal dates, and claims notification periods
**Buyer:** Head of space underwriting / space practice leader at the broker
