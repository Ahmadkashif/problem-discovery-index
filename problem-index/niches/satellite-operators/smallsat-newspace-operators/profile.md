# Small Satellite & NewSpace Operators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/satellite-operators|Satellite Operators]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to take a startup's satellites from licence to steady operations without the startup building its own ground network or 24/7 operations team, and whoever bundles ground contacts, mission operations and the regulatory paperwork into one dependable service takes the startup's ground and operations contract.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.5B a year in ground-station-as-a-service, mission operations software and services, and licensing support bought by small operators worldwide (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1.5% of global satellite services revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — cloud-native in the software, improvised in operations and licensing
**Target Buyer:** CTO or head of mission operations at a startup operator
**Automation Potential:** High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Thousands of satellites now belong to companies operating one to a few dozen spacecraft: earth-observation, IoT, radio-frequency mapping, technology demonstration and in-orbit servicing startups. SIA counted 4,434 satellites deployed in 2025 alone. These operators have the same obligations as a large fleet — FCC or other national licences, NOAA remote-sensing licences for imaging, ITU filings through an administration, five-year disposal, conjunction screening, 24/7 health monitoring — with a team of ten to fifty people (estimate). Tools built for SES-size operations assume staff they don't have.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Ground-station-as-a-service from KSAT, AWS Ground Station, Leaf Space, Atlas Space Operations and SSC; open-source and startup mission-control software; general observability stacks such as Grafana; licensing done by founders with counsel. The gaps: 24/7 anomaly coverage for a team that sleeps, licensing sequences that slip launches, and ground contacts that are cheap but not scheduled around mission priorities.
