# Satellite Service Integrator Rollups

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/satellite-operators|Satellite Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Investor-backed platforms that buy up satellite service providers, teleports and maritime and energy connectivity integrators, and operators that consolidate fleets: Marlink, Speedcast and similar maritime and remote-site integrators, and SES after its July 2025 purchase of Intelsat. Their corporate development and integration teams evaluate targets and merge networks and customer bases.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Corporate development teams of under 10 per platform (estimate)
**Output:** Acquisition models and integration plans for the platform's own use
**Proprietary data:** Their own and acquired customer, network and contract data
**Clock:** Deal timetables they negotiate themselves
**Buyer:** Chief strategy officer
