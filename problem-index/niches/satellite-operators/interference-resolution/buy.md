# Incident Management Platforms Fitted to RF Cases

**Niche:** [[niches/satellite-operators/interference-resolution/profile|Interference Detection & Resolution]]
**Industry:** [[industries/satellite-operators|Satellite Operators]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Software teams run incidents through on-call platforms with escalation, timelines and post-mortems; satellite interference cases run through email threads and a NOC log.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #k-nearest-neighbors #workflow-orchestration #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to find, identify and stop an interferer before the customer's service level is breached, and whoever closes cases fastest across operator boundaries — where the source is usually someone else's customer — takes the operator's interference-management budget.

## The Problem
Interference cases need an owner, a clock, escalation when the clock runs, customer updates and a record of what happened. NOCs track them in shift logs and email, so status depends on who is on shift and the post-case lessons are lost.

## What Already Exists
Incident management platforms (PagerDuty, Opsgenie, ServiceNow ITOM) with on-call schedules, escalation policies, incident timelines and post-incident reviews.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: incidents opened from carrier monitor alarms with traces attached; RF-specific fields (satellite, transponder, frequency, polarisation, interference type, geolocation result); escalation paths that include other operators' NOCs and regulators for deliberate cases; customer-facing status pages per affected carrier; and similar-case search across the operator's history.

## Target Customer
NOC managers at operators and teleports with recurring interference load.

## Impact If Solved
Every case has an owner and a clock, customers stop chasing engineers for updates, and the history becomes searchable.
