# Earth-Observation Tasking & Delivery

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/satellite-operators|Satellite Operators]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver a usable, cloud-free image of the customer's target inside the promised window, and whoever turns tasking orders into successful collections at the highest rate takes the tasking contract.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$5B a year in global commercial and government earth-observation imagery and tasking revenue (estimate; SIA reports remote sensing revenue grew 4% in 2025 without a figure in the release)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of global satellite services revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High — orders, collection and processing are digital; quality review and re-tasking decisions are manual
**Target Buyer:** VP Operations / Head of Collection Planning at the earth-observation operator
**Automation Potential:** Very high

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Earth-observation operators — Vantor (formerly Maxar Intelligence), Planet, BlackSky and Airbus for optical; ICEYE, Capella and Umbra for radar — sell either archive imagery or new collections of a customer's target. Tasking is a chain: feasibility against passes and competing orders, collection, downlink through a ground-station network, processing, quality review and delivery, often within hours. Optical tasking fails to cloud more often than to anything else, and every failed collection costs a satellite pass. Defence and intelligence agencies are the largest buyers and set the strictest delivery windows; insurers, commodity analysts and agricultural services buy on shorter, cheaper terms.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Operators' own ordering portals and collection planners; marketplaces such as UP42 and SkyWatch; ground-station networks from KSAT and AWS; cloud masks from the processing chain. The gaps: tasking promises not priced on collection success probability, quality review by analysts scene by scene, and re-tasking decided by hand.
