# E-Commerce Order Management Taught Areas, Passes and Licences

**Niche:** [[niches/satellite-operators/eo-tasking-delivery/profile|Earth-Observation Tasking & Delivery]]
**Industry:** [[industries/satellite-operators|Satellite Operators]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Online retailers run orders, fulfilment and returns through mature order management systems; imagery orders still pass through custom portals, email and account managers.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #automation #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver a usable, cloud-free image of the customer's target inside the promised window, and whoever turns tasking orders into successful collections at the highest rate takes the tasking contract.

## The Problem
An imagery order is a polygon, a time window, quality criteria, a product type and a licence (single user, enterprise, government, derivative rights). Changes, partial deliveries, re-collections and credits are handled by account managers, and the customer can't see status between order and delivery.

## What Already Exists
Order management platforms (Salesforce Order Management, commercetools, Shopify Plus back-ends) with order states, fulfilment tracking, returns and customer notifications.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: areas of interest and time windows as order lines; fulfilment states tied to collection attempts, downlink and processing; partial fulfilment when only part of an area is collected cloud-free; re-collection as the "return"; licence terms attached to each delivered file; and export-control and government-customer checks before delivery.

## Target Customer
Commercial operations leads at earth-observation operators and imagery resellers.

## Impact If Solved
Customers see order status in real time, account managers stop relaying updates, and credits and re-collections follow rules instead of negotiation.
