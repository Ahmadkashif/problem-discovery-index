# The Renewal Cliff Nobody Charted

**Niche:** [[niches/satellite-operators/geo-meo-capacity-services/profile|GEO & MEO Capacity and Managed Services]]
**Industry:** [[industries/satellite-operators|Satellite Operators]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Multi-year capacity contracts come up for renewal in clusters, and the operator finds out how much revenue is at risk when account managers start reporting bad news.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #logistic-regression #survival-analysis #revenue-impact #quick-win
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor is fighting to keep a fleet of fifteen-year satellites full while capacity prices fall, but writing it out produced two contests with different winners. In video, operators fight to keep broadcasters and pay-TV platforms on established orbital neighbourhoods as channels move to IP, and the incumbent with the most dishes pointed at its slot wins. In enterprise, backhaul and government data, they fight to sell guaranteed throughput across orbits at a price that survives comparison with LEO, and whoever can quote and prove a multi-orbit SLA wins. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Capacity contracts run for years and many were signed in waves: when a satellite launched, when a pay-TV platform moved, when a government programme started. Renewals therefore cluster. Each account manager tracks their own, but nobody sees the fleet-level renewal curve with the probability of each renewal and the price it is likely to clear at.

## Why It's Still Broken
Contract data lives in CRM and legal files; usage and price history in billing; customer health in the account manager's head. Satellite operators have historically had low churn and long contracts, so renewal risk was not a standing analysis. LEO price pressure and IP video migration changed that faster than the processes did.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A renewal register that lists every contract by end date, revenue and satellite, scored for renewal probability and expected price from past renewals, usage trends and the customer's alternatives, reviewed monthly with sales and fleet planning.

## Who Feels the Pain
Chief commercial officers explaining revenue misses; fleet planners making replacement decisions on backlog that won't renew; account managers left alone with the bad news.

## Impact If Fixed
Revenue at risk becomes visible a year or more ahead, giving time to re-price, bundle or migrate customers before the contract ends.
