# Ground Segment & Carrier Monitoring Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/satellite-operators|Satellite Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors of mission control, carrier monitoring, interference geolocation, modems and hubs: Kratos (OpenSpace, quantumCMD, Monics, satID), ST Engineering iDirect, Hughes, Gilat, Comtech and Integrasys. Beyond products, some run analysis services: interference geolocation on request, network performance studies and managed monitoring for operators and teleports. Operators pay licences, maintenance and service fees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-50 RF and network analysts per vendor in service and solutions roles (estimate)
**Output:** Interference geolocation reports, network performance studies, managed monitoring
**Proprietary data:** Monitoring data collected for operators on their satellites and networks, and the vendors' own signal libraries
**Clock:** Customers' interference cases and service-level obligations
**Buyer:** Head of services / product line manager at the vendor
