# ITU Filing & Satellite Coordination Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/satellite-operators|Satellite Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Spectrum engineering consultancies that prepare ITU satellite network filings, run the interference analyses that decide coordination (EPFD, ΔT/T, C/I), draft comments on other administrations' filings, and represent operators at coordination meetings. Transfinite Systems, LS telcom's space practice, Access Partnership's technical team and a scattering of independent consultants and former regulators are representative; some smaller administrations file for foreign operators. Operators and new constellations without in-house spectrum teams pay per filing, per study or on retainer.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically under 10 satellite spectrum engineers per firm; no firm found with a 10+ satellite coordination team (estimate)
**Output:** ITU filings, coordination studies, comments on BR IFIC publications, coordination meeting support
**Proprietary data:** Analysis tools and a record of past studies; the ITU's space network database is shared by everyone
**Clock:** The ITU's four-month comment windows after each fortnightly BR IFIC, the seven-year bringing-into-use limit and Resolution 35 milestones, all set by the Radio Regulations on the filing itself
**Buyer:** Head of spectrum engineering / VP Regulatory Affairs at the operator
