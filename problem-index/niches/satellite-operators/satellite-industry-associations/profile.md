# Satellite Industry Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/satellite-operators|Satellite Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Trade and technical bodies for satellite operators: the Satellite Industry Association (SIA), the Global Satellite Operators Association (GSOA), the World Teleport Association, the Satellite Interference Reduction Group (IRG), and the Space Data Association, through which operators share ephemerides for conjunction and interference work. They lobby, set best practices and publish; SIA's annual State of the Satellite Industry report is the most-cited industry figure.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically under 10 staff each; SIA's flagship report is written by a contracted research firm (estimate)
**Output:** Policy filings, best-practice guides, annual industry statistics
**Proprietary data:** Member surveys and shared operator data held for members
**Clock:** WRC preparations, FCC proceedings and annual publication dates
**Buyer:** Executive director / president
