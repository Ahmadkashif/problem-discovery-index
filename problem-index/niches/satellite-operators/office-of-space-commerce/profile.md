# Office of Space Commerce & Remote Sensing Licensing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/satellite-operators|Satellite Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Commerce Department's Office of Space Commerce, within NOAA, licenses US commercial remote-sensing satellites and runs TraCSS, the civil space traffic coordination service meant to take conjunction screening for commercial operators over from the military. TraCSS ran on $52.5M of FY2026 appropriations with 62 pilot users and more than 11,000 satellites covered; the FY2027 request of $11M for the office does not fund it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Licensing staff in single digits plus contractors; TraCSS is an automated screening service (estimate)
**Output:** Remote-sensing licences and conditions; conjunction screening data
**Proprietary data:** Licence applications and TraCSS's fused catalogue
**Clock:** Statutory licence review periods; conjunction times
**Buyer:** Office director
