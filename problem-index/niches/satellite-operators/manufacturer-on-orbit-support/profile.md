# Satellite Manufacturers' On-Orbit Fleet Support

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/satellite-operators|Satellite Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The on-orbit support and anomaly engineering teams at satellite manufacturers — Boeing, Airbus Defence and Space, Thales Alenia Space, Lanteris (formerly Maxar Space Systems), Northrop Grumman and others — that watch the health of every satellite built on their buses for operators under support contracts, investigate anomalies, and issue fleet-wide alerts when a component fails on one satellite. Operators pay through the satellite contract and separate support agreements.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-100 on-orbit support engineers per manufacturer (estimate)
**Output:** Anomaly investigations, fleet alerts, life-extension and end-of-life assessments, insurance-claim technical reports
**Proprietary data:** Telemetry and anomaly histories across every operator flying the same bus — the only cross-fleet view in the industry
**Clock:** Anomaly response in hours, and insurers' and operators' claim and renewal dates
**Buyer:** Head of on-orbit support / VP customer programmes at the manufacturer
