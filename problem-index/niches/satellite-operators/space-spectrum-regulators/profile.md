# Space Spectrum & Orbital Regulators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/satellite-operators|Satellite Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The FCC's Space Bureau, which licenses US satellites and earth stations and grants market access to foreign systems, and the ITU's Radiocommunication Bureau, whose space services staff examine and publish every administration's satellite filings and maintain the Master International Frequency Register. Their analysis decides who may use which orbits and frequencies and on what conditions. Nobody buys it: the FCC is funded by appropriation and fees, the ITU by member contributions and filing cost-recovery charges.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~50-100 engineers, lawyers and analysts in the FCC Space Bureau; the ITU's space services department is a separate team of similar order (estimate)
**Output:** Licences, milestone and waiver decisions, examination findings, the fortnightly BR IFIC and the rulemakings such as the July 2026 Part 100 order
**Proprietary data:** The complete record of filings, examinations, coordination status and licences, which is published but only these bodies hold whole
**Clock:** Statutory and Radio Regulations procedures and World Radiocommunication Conference cycles; WRC-27 sets the next agenda
**Buyer:** Bureau chief / director of the Radiocommunication Bureau
