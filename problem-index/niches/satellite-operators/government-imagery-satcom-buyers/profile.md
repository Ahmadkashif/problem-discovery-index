# Government Commercial Imagery & SATCOM Programme Offices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/satellite-operators|Satellite Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The government offices that buy commercial satellite services in bulk and analyse what they buy: the National Reconnaissance Office's commercial imagery contracts with Vantor, Planet and BlackSky, the National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency's analysts and commercial analytics programmes, and the Space Force's commercial satellite communications office. They are the largest single buyers of earth-observation imagery and a major buyer of GEO and LEO capacity.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Hundreds of imagery and geospatial analysts at the largest agency, NGA, alone (estimate)
**Output:** Intelligence products for government users; none is sold
**Proprietary data:** Classified and commercial imagery archives and the analyses built on them
**Clock:** Operational demands and annual appropriations and contract cycles
**Buyer:** Programme office directors
