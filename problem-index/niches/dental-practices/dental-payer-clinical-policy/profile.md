# Dental Payer Clinical Policy Units

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/dental-practices|Dental Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The dental consultant and clinical policy groups at Delta Dental, MetLife, Guardian, and the dental benefit administrators write the coverage criteria, review submitted radiographs and narratives, and set the frequency limitations and downgrade rules that determine what a practice actually gets paid.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 dental consultants, policy analysts, and utilization staff
**Output:** Coverage policy, claim determinations, utilization and network analytics
**Proprietary data:** Adjudicated dental claims across tens of millions of members joined to submitted clinical documentation
**Clock:** Plan year policy refresh and prompt-payment statutes
**Buyer:** Dental Director / VP Clinical Policy
