# Dental Association Health Policy Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/dental-practices|Dental Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The ADA's Health Policy Institute produces the dental economics research the profession and policymakers rely on — practice income surveys, workforce projections, care utilization studies, and coverage analysis — and supplies the evidence behind the association's advocacy.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-40 health economists, survey researchers, and analysts
**Output:** Economic research, workforce projections, utilization studies
**Proprietary data:** Long-running practitioner survey series with decades of comparable measurement
**Clock:** Annual publication calendar and legislative sessions
**Buyer:** Chief Economist / VP of Research
