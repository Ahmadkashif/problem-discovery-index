# Dental Practice Analytics Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/dental-practices|Dental Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Dental Intelligence, Jarvis Analytics, and comparable vendors sit on top of practice management systems and turn production, scheduling, treatment acceptance, and hygiene reappointment data into benchmarks and coaching analytics. Multi-location groups buy them because the practice management systems provide no cross-location view.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 data analysts, dental operations specialists, and product staff
**Output:** Practice benchmarks, provider productivity analytics, treatment acceptance analysis
**Proprietary data:** Production, scheduling, and acceptance data across tens of thousands of practices — the only cross-practice view of dental operations that exists
**Clock:** Reporting cadence set internally
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Chief Product Officer
