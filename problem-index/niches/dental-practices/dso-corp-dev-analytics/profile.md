# DSO Corporate Development & Clinical Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/dental-practices|Dental Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The corporate development and clinical analytics groups at Heartland, Aspen, Pacific Dental, and Smile Brands screen practice acquisitions, model de novo economics, and benchmark provider productivity and clinical protocol adherence across networks of hundreds to thousands of locations.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 analysts across corp dev, clinical operations, and revenue cycle
**Output:** Acquisition models, provider productivity analytics, protocol adherence reporting
**Proprietary data:** Clinical and production data across thousands of practices under common ownership — the only standardized cross-practice clinical dataset in dentistry
**Clock:** Deal timelines and lender covenant reporting
**Buyer:** SVP Corporate Development / VP Clinical Analytics
