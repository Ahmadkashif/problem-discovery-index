# Dental Imaging Analysis Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/dental-practices|Dental Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Overjet, Pearl, and VideaHealth build cleared software that detects caries, bone loss, and calculus on dental radiographs, and sell it two ways — to practices as a clinical and case-presentation aid, and to dental payers as an automated claims review layer. Their clinical science teams build and validate the models and maintain the annotated image corpus behind them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 clinical scientists, dentist annotators, and machine learning engineers
**Output:** Detection models, validation studies, payer-facing claim review analytics
**Proprietary data:** Millions of radiographs annotated by licensed dentists with adjudicated ground truth — an image corpus no academic group or manufacturer holds at comparable scale
**Clock:** Regulatory submission cycles and payer contract deployments
**Buyer:** Chief Clinical Officer / VP of Clinical Science
