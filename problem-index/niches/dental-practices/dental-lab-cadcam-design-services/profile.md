# Dental Laboratory CAD/CAM Design Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/dental-practices|Dental Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Large laboratories and offshore design centres convert intraoral scans into restoration designs — margin marking, occlusal design, contact adjustment — at industrial volume. The design is the billable deliverable and the practice judges it on whether the restoration seats without chairside adjustment.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-2,000 dental designers and technicians, with 20-80 in design standards and quality
**Output:** Restoration designs and design standards
**Proprietary data:** Scan-to-design-to-remake history across millions of restorations — a direct record of which design decisions produced work that fit
**Clock:** Case turnaround commitments measured in days
**Buyer:** VP of Digital Operations / Technical Director
