# Dental Licensure Examination Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/dental-practices|Dental Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Joint Commission on National Dental Examinations and the regional clinical testing agencies write and administer the examinations every state licenses dentists and hygienists on, run the practice analyses defining competence, and defend the psychometric basis of the whole apparatus.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-100 test development staff, psychometricians, and subject matter coordinators
**Output:** Licensure examinations, practice analyses, technical reports
**Proprietary data:** Decades of item performance and candidate outcome data across every dentist licensed in the country
**Clock:** Examination administration windows and accreditation review cycles
**Buyer:** Director of Testing / VP of Assessment
