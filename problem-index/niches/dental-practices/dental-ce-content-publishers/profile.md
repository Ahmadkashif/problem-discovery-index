# Dental Continuing Education Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/dental-practices|Dental Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Spear Education, the Kois Center, and the CE publishers produce the clinical protocol content and continuing education dentists need for licence renewal and for adopting new techniques. The curriculum is the product and practices pay per seat.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 clinical educators, content developers, and curriculum staff
**Output:** Clinical curricula, protocol content, continuing education programmes
**Proprietary data:** Curriculum development history and learner outcome data; the underlying clinical evidence is published
**Clock:** Licence renewal cycles and state CE requirements
**Buyer:** Chief Clinical Officer / VP of Curriculum
