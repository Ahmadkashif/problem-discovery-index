# Dental Materials Evaluation Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/dental-practices|Dental Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
The Dental Advisor, Clinicians Report, and the ADA's science institute test dental materials and equipment — bond strengths, wear, handling, longevity — combine laboratory results with structured clinician field evaluation, and publish comparative ratings by subscription. Dentists buy them because manufacturer claims are unverifiable otherwise.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 materials scientists, laboratory staff, and clinical evaluators
**Output:** Comparative product evaluations and ratings — the subscription itself
**Proprietary data:** Decades of standardized laboratory and clinician field evaluations across thousands of products, with longevity data no manufacturer publishes
**Clock:** Product launch cycles; publication cadence is self-set
**Buyer:** Editor-in-Chief / Director of Research
