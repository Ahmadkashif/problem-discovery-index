# Dental Distributor Category Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/dental-practices|Dental Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The category management and business intelligence groups at Henry Schein and Patterson analyze what practices actually buy, publish practice benchmarking to customers, and sell category insight to the manufacturers whose products they distribute.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 category analysts, data scientists, and consultants
**Output:** Category plans, practice benchmarking, manufacturer insight reporting
**Proprietary data:** Purchase data covering the large majority of US dental practices at line-item granularity
**Clock:** Category review and manufacturer planning cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Category Management / Chief Merchandising Officer
