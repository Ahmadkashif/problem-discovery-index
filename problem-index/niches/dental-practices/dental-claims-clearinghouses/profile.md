# Dental Claims Clearinghouses & Attachment Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/dental-practices|Dental Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Vyne Dental, DentalXChange, and the clearinghouses route claims and clinical attachments between practices and payers, scrub submissions against payer-specific rules, and publish the denial and adjudication analytics practices use to get paid faster.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 rules analysts, payer relations staff, and data staff
**Output:** Claim scrubbing rules, denial analytics, payer performance reporting
**Proprietary data:** Submitted claims joined to adjudication outcomes across most US dental practices and every major payer — the definitive record of what actually gets paid
**Clock:** Claim submission and timely-filing deadlines
**Buyer:** VP of Analytics / Chief Product Officer
