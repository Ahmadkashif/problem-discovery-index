# Dental Practice Transition & Valuation Advisors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/dental-practices|Dental Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Henry Schein Professional Practice Transitions, ADS Transitions, and the regional brokers value dental practices, market them to buyers, and advise on associate buy-ins and DSO sales. The written valuation is the invoice and it is contested by whichever side it disadvantages.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 valuation analysts, brokers, and transition advisors
**Output:** Practice valuations, transition analyses, market reports
**Proprietary data:** Completed transaction terms and multiples in a market where almost nothing is publicly reported
**Clock:** Closing timelines and lender diligence windows
**Buyer:** Managing Director / Head of Valuation
