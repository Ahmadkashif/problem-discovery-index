# State Dental Licensing Boards

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/dental-practices|Dental Practices]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State boards license dentists and hygienists, define scope of practice, and investigate and adjudicate complaints, maintaining the disciplinary record for the state.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 3-12 per state
**Output:** Licensure decisions, disciplinary findings, scope determinations
**Proprietary data:** Complaint and discipline history within the state
**Clock:** Statutory adjudication timelines and renewal cycles
**Buyer:** Executive Director
