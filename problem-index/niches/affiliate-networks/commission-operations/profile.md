# Commission Operations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/affiliate-networks|Affiliate Networks]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to run validation, clawback and rate changes as a system rather than as a person with a spreadsheet — and whoever does that removes the administrative weight that caps how many partners a programme can have.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.6B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% of category spend
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — approvals and email threads
**Target Buyer:** Merchant affiliate and finance teams
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the work is rules and reconciliation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Affiliate managers spend their months approving thousands of transactions one screen at a time, chasing returns that claw back commission after payout, and negotiating rates in email threads that live nowhere. The administrative weight of running a programme is the binding constraint on its size: a programme grows until the operations exceed what one person can process, and then stops. This is distinct from the manager's role as a whole, which is about partnerships and strategy; this is specifically the money machinery.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Network validation queues, bulk approval with limited rules, returns files reconciled by hand, rate changes made in the console after an email agreement, and spreadsheets throughout. The gaps: validation rules that cannot express the real policy; returns matched manually to commissions; rate agreements with no record; no audit trail of who agreed what; and payouts that precede return windows.
