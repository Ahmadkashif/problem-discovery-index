# Revenue Operations Practice

**Niche:** [[niches/affiliate-networks/commission-operations/profile|Commission Operations]]
**Industry:** [[industries/affiliate-networks|Affiliate Networks]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Sales commission software automates plan management, calculation, approval and dispute handling at scale, and affiliate commission is managed in a console and an inbox.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #automation #compliance #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #descriptive-statistics #revenue-impact #confidence-intervals
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to run validation, clawback and rate changes as a system rather than as a person with a spreadsheet — and whoever does that removes the administrative weight that caps how many partners a programme can have.

## The Problem
Incentive compensation management is a real software category. It handles complex commission plans with tiers, accelerators and clawbacks; calculates automatically across thousands of participants; routes approvals; manages disputes with an audit trail; and produces statements participants can understand. Companies buy it because managing sales commission by spreadsheet becomes impossible past a certain size. Affiliate commission has identical structure and larger participant counts, and is managed in a network console and an email thread.

## What Already Exists
Incentive compensation management platforms; complex plan modelling with tiers and clawbacks; automated calculation and approval workflow; dispute and adjustment handling with audit trails; and participant-facing statements and estimators.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is from employees under contract to external partners across several networks. It requires: (1) participants who are third parties on different networks with different data formats, so the calculation engine must sit above systems it does not control — unlike an employee plan where the company owns every input; (2) validation against order and fulfilment outcomes, which the sales commission world handles as a simple bookings adjustment and which here is the bulk of the work; (3) plan changes negotiated individually with hundreds of partners rather than set for a workforce, requiring negotiation and agreement tracking the category does not include; (4) reversal months after payment as a routine event rather than an exception, which changes the accrual model; and (5) a price point suited to a merchant affiliate team rather than to an enterprise sales operations function.

## Target Customer
Merchant affiliate and finance teams, agencies managing programmes, and incentive compensation vendors for whom external partner commission is an adjacent unserved market.

## Impact If Solved
Sales commission software exists because spreadsheets fail past a certain size, and affiliate has larger participant counts and no equivalent. Sitting above systems it does not control, and treating reversal as routine rather than exceptional, are the two adaptations.
