# Publisher Earnings Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/affiliate-networks|Affiliate Networks]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell a creator which piece of their work earned the money — and whoever attributes earnings back to content changes what a creator makes next.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.3B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~11% of category spend
**Digital Adoption:** Low — link-level reporting only
**Target Buyer:** Creators and publisher success teams
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the join is mechanical

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is an analytical contest with a clear deliverable: map every commission back to the content, the placement, the audience and the moment that produced it. Everything required is present — the networks hold the click and the conversion, the creator holds the content and its traffic — and the join between them is not made by anyone. A competitor can win this decisively without touching payments, terms or reconciliation, which is what separates it from the operational fight next door.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Link-level performance reports, sub-identifier tagging for those who configure it, and site analytics that stop at the outbound click. The gaps: no mapping from link to content; no placement or position analysis; no audience segmentation of earners; no connection between traffic and earnings; and reporting that requires the creator to have instrumented everything in advance.
