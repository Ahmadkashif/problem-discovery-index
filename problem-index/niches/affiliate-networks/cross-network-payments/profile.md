# Cross-Network Payments & Operations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/affiliate-networks|Affiliate Networks]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn five sets of terms, schedules and statements into one predictable income — and whoever does that makes affiliate a business a creator can run rather than a set of payments that arrive.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.1B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of category spend
**Digital Adoption:** Low — statements and bank transfers
**Target Buyer:** Creators, publisher operations, and creator finance vendors
**Automation Potential:** Very High — reconciliation and payment are mechanical

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is a payments, reconciliation and workflow contest with nothing analytical about it. Five networks, five approval processes, five payment schedules, five minimum thresholds, five clawback policies, five tax treatments, and a creator trying to run a business on top. The winner is whoever removes the most operational friction and makes the income predictable, which is a different company and a different build from whoever explains which article earned.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Per-network statements, bank transfers or payment processor payouts, minimum thresholds, manual reconciliation in spreadsheets, and annual tax document chaos. The gaps: no consolidated ledger; unpredictable approval and payment timing; clawbacks applied against future earnings with no notice; thresholds that trap small balances; and no forecast of what will arrive or when.
