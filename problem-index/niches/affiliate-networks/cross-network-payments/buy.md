## The Problem
Paying many small counterparties quickly, across borders, with tax handling and compliance, is a solved commercial problem. Marketplace payout infrastructure handles instant and scheduled payouts, multi-currency, tax form collection and reporting, balance management and dispute handling, and is available as a service any platform can integrate in weeks. Gig platforms, marketplaces and creator platforms all use it. Affiliate networks settle monthly in arrears with thresholds and opaque adjustments, on processes unchanged in fifteen years.

## What Already Exists
Marketplace payout and money movement infrastructure; instant and on-demand payout options; multi-currency and cross-border settlement; tax form collection and reporting; and balance, ledger and dispute management.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to earnings that are provisional for months and reversible. It requires: (1) a commission that is not final at the point of sale, since returns and validation can reverse it long afterwards — which makes instant payout an underwriting decision rather than a payments feature and is the central difference from every marketplace case; (2) consolidation across networks that are competitors and provide inconsistent programmatic access, so the ledger is assembled from partial and uncooperative sources; (3) reversal handling that is explicable to the earner, which the marketplace model handles through refunds tied to identifiable orders and affiliate currently does not; (4) risk assessment per network and merchant rather than per transaction, since clawback rates vary enormously by programme and that variation is the underwriting signal; and (5) an economics that works on modest balances, where per-payout fees can consume a meaningful share.

## Target Customer
Creators and publishers, creator finance vendors, affiliate networks modernising settlement, and payout infrastructure vendors for whom provisional reversible earnings are unserved.

## Impact If Solved
Payout infrastructure assumes a final sale, and affiliate commission is provisional for months and reversible. That turns instant payout into an underwriting problem, with per-programme clawback rates as the signal nobody currently computes.
