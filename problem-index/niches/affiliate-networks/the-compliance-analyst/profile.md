# The Compliance Analyst

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/affiliate-networks|Affiliate Networks]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to catch tactics that evolve faster than the rulebook, with an analyst who has rules and spot checks — and whoever gives them detection that learns replaces a policing model that is permanently one cycle behind.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$700M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~6% of category spend
**Digital Adoption:** Low — rules and manual review
**Target Buyer:** Network compliance operations
**Automation Potential:** High — the signals are in the network's own data

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Cookie stuffing, trademark bidding and incentivised traffic are policed by rules and manual spot checks, which catch the tactics that were common five years ago. The compliance analyst is the only thing standing between the commission pool and the people optimising against it, and they are equipped with a rulebook that is by construction a record of what has already been caught. This is distinct from attribution: attribution decides who deserves the money among legitimate partners, and this decides who is taking it illegitimately.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Rule-based violation detection, keyword monitoring for trademark bidding, manual spot checks of partner sites, complaint-driven investigation, and suspension. The gaps: rules that encode past tactics; detection that requires knowing the pattern in advance; no cross-merchant view of a partner's behaviour; manual review that cannot cover the partner base; and no measurement of what is being missed.
