# Channel Incrementality Verification

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/affiliate-networks|Affiliate Networks]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell a merchant whether affiliate produced any sales at all — and whoever runs that test credibly decides whether the channel's reported return survives contact with evidence.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$800M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of category spend
**Digital Adoption:** Low — a performance channel, unverified
**Target Buyer:** Merchant measurement and finance leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the experiment is simple and cheap

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Affiliate is a performance channel whose performance is unverified. Merchants pay commission on sales the network attributes, almost never with a holdout, so the channel reports a return that is definitionally close to infinite and close to unfalsifiable. This is distinct from the attribution contest, which asks which partner among several deserves the commission; this asks whether the commission should have been paid at all. It is the question the channel's structure makes hardest to ask and easiest to answer, because the experiment is cheap and the merchant can run it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Network-reported attributed revenue, return on spend calculated from attributed sales, occasional merchant-run tests, and marketing mix models that treat affiliate as a single line. The gaps: no holdouts; no partner-type level incrementality; no comparison against what would have happened; measurement produced by the party being paid; and a metric whose construction guarantees a favourable result.
