# The Small Merchant Programme

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/affiliate-networks|Affiliate Networks]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to run a working affiliate programme for a merchant with nobody to run it — and whoever does that opens a merchant base the networks priced themselves out of.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$900M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of category spend
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — mostly absent entirely
**Target Buyer:** Network growth teams and small merchants
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the whole programme can be operated automatically

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Affiliate is structurally ideal for a small merchant — you pay only on a sale — and is practically unavailable to them, because running a programme requires a person to recruit partners, negotiate rates, validate transactions, police fraud and handle payouts. Network minimums, setup fees and integration requirements formalise the exclusion. The result is a channel that costs nothing to try and is inaccessible to the merchants it would suit best, which is a distinct contest from anything happening inside the programmes that exist.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Network onboarding designed for enterprise merchants, plugin-based tracking integrations of varying reliability, self-service programme setup that still requires management, and agencies serving mid-market upward. The gaps: minimums and fees that exclude small merchants; no automated partner recruitment; validation and fraud policing requiring a person; tracking integration that breaks silently; and no route to a working programme without expertise.
