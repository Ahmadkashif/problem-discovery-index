## The Problem
Successful marketplaces invest heavily in seller tooling — analytics, forecasting, inventory and payment management, performance diagnostics, growth recommendations — because supply quality determines the marketplace's value and sellers choose where to operate. The pattern is well understood and visible across commerce, accommodation, freelancing and app distribution. Affiliate networks, which are marketplaces with the same structure, have built merchant tooling for two decades and given publishers a statement.

## What Already Exists
Seller analytics and performance dashboards; earnings forecasting and payout management; growth and optimisation recommendations; multi-channel seller management tools; and seller support and education programmes.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a seller whose inventory is content and who operates across several competing marketplaces at once. It requires: (1) the unit of supply being a piece of content rather than a listing, so performance must be attributed back through links to the work that produced it — a mapping no marketplace seller tool has ever needed; (2) simultaneous operation across competing networks as the normal case, which means a single-network tool is structurally incomplete and the useful product must span platforms that will not cooperate; (3) earnings with long and uncertain settlement, since commissions are approved, delayed and clawed back months later, unlike a marketplace sale that settles; (4) the creator's own publishing platform as the context, which the affiliate network has no visibility into and must integrate with; and (5) a buyer who is an individual with no budget, which constrains pricing to a share of earnings or to network sponsorship.

## Target Customer
Creators and content publishers, creator business platforms, and affiliate networks whose supply side is a commodity.

## Impact If Solved
Marketplaces learned supply tooling wins and affiliate spent twenty years on the demand side. Attributing performance back through links to the content that produced it is a mapping no seller tool has needed, and cross-network operation makes a single-network product structurally incomplete.
