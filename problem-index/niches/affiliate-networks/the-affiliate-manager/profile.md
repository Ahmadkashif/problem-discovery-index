# The Affiliate Manager

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/affiliate-networks|Affiliate Networks]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give the affiliate manager back the partnership work by taking the queue off them — and whoever does that changes how many partners one person can genuinely develop.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.1B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of category spend
**Digital Adoption:** Low — consoles and inboxes
**Target Buyer:** Merchant partnership leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — the administrative half is fully automatable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The affiliate manager is hired to build partnerships — to find publishers who suit the brand, understand what they need, and develop the ones that matter. In practice the month is validation queues, returns reconciliation, rate emails and reporting, and the partnership work happens with whoever is loudest. The manager is the merchant's entire relationship with hundreds of partners and their capacity is what determines how many of those relationships are real. This is distinct from the operations machinery: that is what should be automated, this is what should happen instead.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Network consoles, email, spreadsheets, and quarterly performance reviews for the top partners. The gaps: no prioritisation of which partners need attention; no view of a partner's performance across merchants; no support for the development conversation; no record of what was agreed or what worked; and capacity consumed by administration.
