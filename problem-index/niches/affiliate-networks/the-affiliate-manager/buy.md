**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give the affiliate manager back the partnership work by taking the queue off them — and whoever does that changes how many partners one person can genuinely develop.

## The Problem
Technology companies run indirect channels as a managed discipline: partner relationship management systems, formal tiers with defined benefits and requirements, enablement programmes, deal registration, co-marketing funds with approval workflow, and partner scorecards driving investment. It is a developed practice because indirect revenue is too large to run informally. Affiliate is an indirect channel with far more partners and comparable revenue, managed from a network console and an inbox.

## What Already Exists
Partner relationship management platforms; tiering frameworks with defined benefits; partner enablement and certification programmes; deal registration and conflict handling; and co-marketing fund management.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is from dozens of contracted resellers to hundreds of independent publishers on a shared network. It requires: (1) partner counts an order of magnitude higher with no contract negotiation per partner, so tiering and enablement must be automatic rather than administered — the channel practice assumes a manageable number and here it is not; (2) partners who work with the merchant's competitors simultaneously and always will, which makes exclusivity and conflict concepts inapplicable and changes what a tier can mean; (3) performance data held by the network rather than by the merchant, so the partner scorecard depends on a third party's willingness to share; (4) enablement that is content and creative rather than training and certification, since a publisher needs assets and product information rather than a course; and (5) economics that work for partners producing modest revenue, where the channel practice's per-partner investment is uneconomic.

## Target Customer
Merchant partnership teams, agencies managing affiliate programmes, and partner management vendors for whom high-count publisher channels are unserved.

## Impact If Solved
Channel practice assumes dozens of contracted partners and here there are hundreds with no contract, so tiering and enablement must be automatic. Partners permanently working with competitors makes exclusivity inapplicable and redefines what a tier can offer.
