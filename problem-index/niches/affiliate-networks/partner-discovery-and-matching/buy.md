## The Problem
Matching two sides of a marketplace is the core product of every marketplace that works. Freelance platforms, accommodation, logistics, recruitment and dating have all invested heavily in ranking, matching, cold-start handling and match quality measurement, because the quality of the match is the product. Affiliate networks, which are two-sided marketplaces with unusually good outcome data, present a searchable list.

## What Already Exists
Two-sided matching and ranking systems; collaborative filtering and embedding-based matching; cold-start strategies for new participants; match quality measurement and feedback loops; and capacity-aware allocation.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a marketplace where a match is an ongoing relationship rather than a transaction. It requires: (1) the match producing a partnership that unfolds over months rather than a single booking, so the outcome label is a revenue trajectory and the feedback loop is slow — far slower than any transactional marketplace and the main reason the practice has not transferred; (2) many-to-many participation as the norm, since publishers work with hundreds of merchants and merchants with hundreds of publishers, which breaks the allocation logic of matching systems built around exclusivity or capacity; (3) outcome quality that depends on the merchant's own terms and support rather than on the match alone, confounding the training signal; (4) cross-merchant performance as the key feature, which is commercially sensitive and must be used without being exposed; and (5) an incentive conflict, since the network earns most from the largest publishers and a fit-based ranking may reduce that in the short term.

## Target Customer
Affiliate networks and platforms, merchant recruitment teams, and marketplace matching vendors for whom relationship-based partner matching is unserved.

## Impact If Solved
Every working marketplace makes matching its product, and affiliate makes it a listings page. A months-long outcome trajectory and many-to-many participation are what distinguish this from the transactional matching the practice was built for.
