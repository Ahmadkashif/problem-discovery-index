# Community Health

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/developer-relations-agencies|Developer Relations Agencies]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to measure whether a developer community is working, when member count and message volume rise as it fills with unanswered questions — and whoever measures it properly takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~20% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — vanity metrics
**Target Buyer:** Community leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — outcome metrics from existing logs

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A developer community is measured by member count and message volume, both of which rise as a community fills with unanswered questions from people who leave. The metrics move in the same direction whether the community is thriving or failing, which makes them worse than useless — they provide false reassurance while the thing they describe deteriorates. Everything needed to measure it honestly is in the platform's own logs.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A member count, a message count and a monthly active figure. The gaps: no measure of whether questions get answered; no time-to-first-response; no tracking of who returns; no measure of member-to-member help; and no distinction between a community and a queue.
