# Identity Linking & Privacy Design

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/developer-relations-agencies|Developer Relations Agencies]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to connect the same pseudonymous developer across a conference, a community, a piece of content and a signup — and whoever does that acceptably takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$240M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — rightly cautious
**Target Buyer:** Data and privacy leadership
**Automation Potential:** Medium — linking with a governance regime

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This half would produce far stronger evidence and carries a genuine risk of being the wrong thing to build. The same developer appears as a conference registration, a community handle, a content reader, a repository account and a product signup, under different identities they may deliberately keep separate. Linking them is technically possible and raises consent questions the field is right to be careful about. It is a data engineering and governance problem where the governance is the harder and more consequential half.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Separate systems with separate identities and no linking. The gaps: no consent framework for cross-surface linking; no identity resolution approach; no minimisation or aggregation discipline; no transparency to the developers involved; and no position on what should and should not be linked.
