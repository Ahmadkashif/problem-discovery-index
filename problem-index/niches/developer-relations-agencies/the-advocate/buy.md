**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make a role built on constant travel, public performance and an always-on presence sustainable — and whoever does takes the account.

## The Problem
Professions built on touring and public performance — musicians, athletes, lecturers, touring theatre — learned that the schedule destroys people and built management around it: capped appearance counts, mandated recovery between engagements, travel structured to reduce load, support for the public exposure, and career paths that do not require the same intensity at fifty as at thirty. The practices exist because the alternative was demonstrably unsustainable. Advocacy schedules by whatever opportunity arises.

## What Already Exists
Capped appearance and travel loads; mandated recovery periods; tour scheduling optimised for load rather than logistics; support for public exposure and its consequences; and career progression away from peak intensity.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a role where the performance is technical credibility and the audience is a professional peer group. It requires: (1) credibility that must be continuously demonstrated through current technical practice, so an advocate who stops building loses the thing that makes them valuable — this is the substantive difference and means recovery cannot simply mean absence; (2) an always-on community presence with no equivalent in a touring schedule; (3) a personal reputation that is portable, making the employment relationship unusual; (4) a small team where capping one person's load raises everyone else's; and (5) an audience that values authenticity, which makes a managed presence read as corporate.

## Target Customer
Developer relations agencies and in-house functions, DevRel leadership, advocates, and professional bodies in the field.

## Impact If Solved
Touring professions built load management because the schedule demonstrably destroyed people. Credibility that requires continuing to build, and an always-on presence, is why recovery here cannot simply mean time away.
