# Sample & Tutorial Maintenance

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/developer-relations-agencies|Developer Relations Agencies]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep dozens of sample applications and tutorials working, because a developer's first experience of the product is running one — and whoever maintains them takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — unmaintained
**Target Buyer:** DevRel and engineering leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very high — continuous testing

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every advocacy programme accumulates dozens of sample applications and tutorials, and a developer's first experience of the product is running one that no longer works. Each was built for a talk, a workshop or a blog post, worked at the time, and has been decaying since — dependencies moved, the product changed, the platform deprecated something. Nobody owns them, they are the most consequential content the programme produces, and they are the least maintained.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A collection of repositories, a blog archive and an intention to tidy up. The gaps: no continuous testing of samples; no ownership; no deprecation or archival policy; no visibility of which samples are used; and no link between a sample and the product versions it works with.
