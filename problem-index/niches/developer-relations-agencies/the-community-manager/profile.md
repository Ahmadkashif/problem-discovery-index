# The Community Manager

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/developer-relations-agencies|Developer Relations Agencies]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to support the person running an unofficial support desk with no support tooling and no ticket queue — and whoever equips them takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$160M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — no tooling
**Target Buyer:** Community leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — triage, routing and answer reuse

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Developers ask their questions where the humans are, so the community channel becomes an unofficial support desk staffed by someone with no support tooling and no ticket queue. The community manager sees every question, remembers which are unanswered, answers what they can, chases engineers for the rest, and has no queue, no routing, no knowledge base and no service expectation. They carry the product's most visible support surface with the tooling of a chat client.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A chat platform, a set of notifications and a memory. The gaps: no queue or triage; no routing to people who can answer; no reuse of previous answers; no visibility of what is outstanding; and no boundary between community management and support.
