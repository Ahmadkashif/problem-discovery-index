# One Piece of Work, Every Surface

**Industry:** [[industries/developer-relations-agencies|Developer Relations Agencies]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get one piece of advocacy work in front of the developers who would want it, across a dozen surfaces, without doing it by hand each time.

## The Problem
An advocate spends weeks on a talk, gives it to a few hundred people, and that is where it ends. The recording is not published, or is published and never promoted. The content is not written up. The sample is not maintained. The material is not submitted elsewhere. The effort was in producing it and almost none goes into distribution, so the programme's reach is bounded by the number of rooms an advocate can stand in.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Distribution is treated as marketing rather than as part of the advocacy work. Repurposing is manual and competes with producing the next thing. Developer surfaces are fragmented and each has its own conventions. And nobody measures how far a piece actually reached.

## What to Build
Repurpose automatically and distribute where developers actually are. Turn each piece of work into every other form it can take — recording, written version, sample, summary, documentation contribution — automatically as a pipeline rather than as an intention, which is the core and multiplies the reach of the same effort. Distribute to the surfaces developers actually use rather than only the company's own, which is where the audience is. Respect each surface's conventions, since content posted in the wrong register on a developer forum does more harm than good. Resubmit and re-run talks elsewhere rather than producing a new one each time, which is normal in every other speaking profession. Measure which surfaces reach anyone, so the effort concentrates rather than spreading thinly. Feed the durable content into the documentation, which is the only surface that compounds. Reuse across clients and programmes where the content is general. Keep the advocate's voice, since the credibility is the point and a repurposed piece that sounds generic loses it. Schedule distribution as part of the work rather than after it. And report reach per piece rather than pieces produced, which is the measurement change that drives the rest.

## Target Customer
Developer relations agencies and in-house functions, content operations, developer platform vendors, and content automation providers.

## Impact If Built
Weeks of preparation reach one room, and the distribution that would multiply it is an intention rather than a pipeline. Automatic repurposing across surfaces makes the same effort go considerably further.
