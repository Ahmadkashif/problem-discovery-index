# Community Operations Automation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/developer-relations-agencies|Developer Relations Agencies]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to run the mechanical half of a developer community — onboarding, moderation, routing, reporting — without a person doing it by hand, and whoever automates that takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$120M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~6% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — partial
**Target Buyer:** Community operations
**Automation Potential:** Very high — onboarding, moderation and reporting

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Running a community has a large mechanical component: welcoming and orienting newcomers, enforcing the code of conduct, tagging and organising content, keeping channels tidy, recognising contributors, producing the reporting, and moving content between the community and the documentation. It is repetitive, it scales with the community rather than with the team, and it is what consumes the community staff's time before any of the relationship work begins.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A platform with some bot integrations and a set of manual routines. The gaps: no structured newcomer onboarding; no moderation support at volume; no automated organisation of content; no contributor recognition; and no generated reporting.
