# Sponsorship Evaluation From Sports and Trade Shows

**Industry:** [[industries/developer-relations-agencies|Developer Relations Agencies]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Trade show and sponsorship evaluation is an established discipline, and developer events are renewed on relationship.
**Tags:** #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact #descriptive-statistics #confidence-intervals #causal-inference #optimization-fundamentals #data-integration #hypothesis-testing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to decide which conferences, workshops and sponsorships are worth doing, when the spend is substantial and nobody has compared one against another — and whoever prices them takes the account.

## The Problem
Trade show and sponsorship evaluation is a real discipline with established practice: cost per qualified interaction, lead follow-up with defined stages, exposure valuation, comparison across events on a common basis, and a decision each year that is made rather than renewed. Exhibitors in industrial and consumer sectors run it because the spend is large and the alternatives are visible. Developer events are a comparable spend evaluated on attendance and impressions.

## What Already Exists
Cost per qualified interaction; structured lead capture and follow-up; exposure and impression valuation; cross-event comparison frameworks; and annual allocation decisions with alternatives considered.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to an audience that is repelled by lead capture and an outcome that is adoption rather than a sale. It requires: (1) developers who will actively avoid a booth that qualifies them and whose goodwill is the point, so the trade show lead machinery is not merely ineffective but counterproductive — this is the substantive difference; (2) an outcome that is a self-serve adoption months later rather than a sales conversation; (3) value that includes community standing and advocate credibility, which no trade show metric captures; (4) advocate time as the scarce resource rather than booth cost; and (5) events whose primary value may be recruitment or ecosystem relationships rather than adoption at all.

## Target Customer
Developer relations agencies and programme functions, marketing leadership, event organisers, and sponsorship evaluation providers.

## Impact If Solved
Trade show evaluation made sponsorship an annual decision rather than a renewal, on a common basis. An audience repelled by lead capture, adopting months later, is why the machinery must be rebuilt rather than borrowed.
