# Event & Programme Economics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/developer-relations-agencies|Developer Relations Agencies]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to decide which conferences, workshops and sponsorships are worth doing, when the spend is substantial and nobody has compared one against another — and whoever prices them takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$220M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~11% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — unmeasured
**Target Buyer:** Programme leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — comparison and allocation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Event and programme spend is a large share of a developer relations budget: conference sponsorships, booth presence, speaking travel, workshops, hackathons, meetup support. The decisions are made annually, largely by precedent and relationship, and nothing compares one event against another on any outcome. The same conferences are sponsored each year because they were sponsored last year, and the ones that would be better are not tried because the comparison has never been made.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A sponsorship invoice, an attendance figure and a badge scan list. The gaps: no cost per meaningful interaction; no comparison across events; no follow-up measurement; no distinction between event types; and no allocation model.
