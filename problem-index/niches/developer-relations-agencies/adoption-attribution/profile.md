# Adoption Attribution

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/developer-relations-agencies|Developer Relations Agencies]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show that advocacy drove adoption, when the moment of influence carries no identifier and the conversion arrives months later — and whoever demonstrates it takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$540M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~27% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — activity counts
**Target Buyer:** DevRel and marketing leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — cohort measurement and experiment design

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Developer advocacy plausibly drives a large share of adoption and reports talks given and community members, because the moment of influence carries no identifier and the conversion arrives months later. The function has argued about better metrics for a decade without settling on any, and its budget is cut first in every downturn for exactly that reason. It is the field's defining unsolved problem, acknowledged openly by its own practitioners.

### Contested sub-niches
This niche is not terminal. It names the problem, and there are two routes with different methods and different risks:
- [[niches/developer-relations-agencies/cohort-measurement/profile|🎯 Cohort-Level Measurement]] — what can be shown without identifying anyone
- [[niches/developer-relations-agencies/identity-and-privacy/profile|🎯 Identity Linking & Privacy Design]] — connecting the same developer across surfaces

## Current Tools & Gaps
A count of talks, articles, community members and event attendance. The gaps: no measure of adoption attributable to any activity; no holdout or comparison design; no cohort analysis; no link between exposure and eventual product use; and no defensible claim for a budget review.
