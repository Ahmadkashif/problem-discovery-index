# Cohort-Level Measurement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/developer-relations-agencies|Developer Relations Agencies]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to establish an advocacy effect without identifying a single developer — and whoever designs those comparisons takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — never designed
**Target Buyer:** DevRel and marketing leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — comparison design and analysis

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This half works entirely at the aggregate. Adoption in one region against another, one segment against another, periods with and without a programme, cohorts before and after an event — comparisons that need nobody's identity and can be run with data both parties already hold. It sacrifices precision for defensibility, it is the version that survives a privacy review, and a firm can adopt it this quarter without ever touching the question of linking individuals.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Activity counts and an adoption dashboard with no relationship between them. The gaps: no comparison design; no holdout discipline; no lag modelling; no pooling across programmes to gain power; and no reporting of effects with uncertainty.
