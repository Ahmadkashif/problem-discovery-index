# Feed & Surfacing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/membership-community-platforms|Membership & Community Platforms]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show a member the three things in this community that concern them rather than everything in reverse order — and whoever does it makes a large community feel like a small one.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$200M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — chronological or engagement-ranked
**Target Buyer:** Product leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — ranking for connection rather than engagement

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
What a member sees when they open the app determines whether the community feels relevant or overwhelming. A chronological feed in an active community is a wall; an engagement-ranked feed reproduces social media's dynamics inside a paid space, amplifying the loudest and burying the newcomer. The right answer — surfacing the few things that concern this specific member and the people they should meet — is a ranking problem with an unusually clear objective, and neither default attempts it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A chronological feed, spaces, notification settings and sometimes engagement ranking. The gaps: ranking optimised for activity rather than for connection; newcomers' posts buried; quiet members invisible; no personalisation by interest or relationship; and no measure of whether the feed produced a reply.
