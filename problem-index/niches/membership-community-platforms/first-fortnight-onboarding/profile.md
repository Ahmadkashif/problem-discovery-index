# First-Fortnight Onboarding

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/membership-community-platforms|Membership & Community Platforms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to put a new member in front of the two people they should know within days of joining — and whoever makes that introduction reliably decides whether the membership survives its first fortnight.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$900M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — checklists and an introductions channel
**Target Buyer:** Product and community leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — a matching problem with rich profiles

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every platform has an onboarding checklist and an introductions channel, and neither does the one thing that works, which is putting a new member in front of the two people they should know. The decision about whether this is their place is made within about two weeks, it depends almost entirely on whether a connection formed, and the mechanism that would form it is a matching problem the platform is well placed to solve and does not attempt.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A welcome sequence, a profile form, an introductions channel and a checklist. The gaps: no matching of newcomers to members; introductions posted into a room rather than made to a person; the checklist measuring product actions rather than connection; no follow-up when the first post is ignored; and no measurement of the fortnight's outcome.
