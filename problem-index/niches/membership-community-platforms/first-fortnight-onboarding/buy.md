# Matching From Mentoring Programmes

**Niche:** [[niches/membership-community-platforms/first-fortnight-onboarding/profile|First-Fortnight Onboarding]]
**Industry:** [[industries/membership-community-platforms|Membership & Community Platforms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Mentoring and buddy programmes match people deliberately and measure whether the pairing worked, and community onboarding hosts a channel.
**Tags:** #optimization-fundamentals #k-nearest-neighbors #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #graph-theory #automation #descriptive-statistics #gradient-boosting
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to put a new member in front of the two people they should know within days of joining — and whoever makes that introduction reliably decides whether the membership survives its first fortnight.

## The Problem
Organisations that need people to connect deliberately — mentoring schemes, employee onboarding buddies, professional networks, conference matchmaking — built matching practice: structured profiles, matching on complementary attributes, explicit introductions with a suggested first conversation, check-ins, and measurement of whether the pairing took. It works better than hoping people find each other. Community platforms hope people find each other.

## What Already Exists
Structured matching profiles; matching algorithms on complementary and shared attributes; facilitated introductions with conversation prompts; pairing check-ins; and match success measurement.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to voluntary participants with no obligation to each other. It requires: (1) both sides participating voluntarily, so an existing member can ignore the introduction and the design must make it easy and rewarding rather than assigned — this is the substantive difference from a workplace programme; (2) behavioural data on existing members that mentoring programmes never have, which makes the matching far better informed; (3) a window of days rather than a scheduled programme cycle; (4) matching for conversation rather than for a mentoring relationship, which is a lighter and more repeatable unit; and (5) a founder with no capacity to administer pairings, so it must be automatic.

## Target Customer
Product and community leadership, founders, members who would welcome newcomers, and matching platform vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Deliberate matching with facilitated introductions beats hoping, and every adjacent field knows it. The adaptation is that both sides are volunteers, which makes the invitation design as important as the match.
