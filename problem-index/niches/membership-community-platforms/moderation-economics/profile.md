# Moderation Economics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/membership-community-platforms|Membership & Community Platforms]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give a community of four hundred people the moderation capability that is currently priced for platforms with trust and safety departments — and whoever does it protects the communities where the harm is most personal.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$900M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — a rules page and a delete button
**Target Buyer:** Trust and safety leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — detection and policy support at small scale

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Serious moderation tooling is priced for platforms with trust and safety departments, and the communities that most need help have one founder and three volunteers. A small paid community is intimate enough that harm lands personally and small enough that nobody can fund a response. The result is that the category's moderation is a rules page, a delete button and a person absorbing whatever arrives, which is both a safety problem and the main cause of operator burnout.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Keyword filters, a report button, manual review and a rules page. The gaps: detection tooling unaffordable at this scale; no policy support for people who are not policy writers; no escalation path for serious matters; decisions inconsistent and undocumented; and moderator wellbeing unaddressed.
