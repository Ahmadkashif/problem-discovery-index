# Events & Programming

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/membership-community-platforms|Membership & Community Platforms]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give members a recurring reason to show up without the operator inventing one every week — and whoever automates the programming keeps the rhythm that holds a community together.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$700M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — a calendar and improvisation
**Target Buyer:** Community operations leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — scheduling, prompting and recurrence

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Communities are held together by rhythm: a weekly call, a monthly session, a recurring thread, a regular prompt. Someone has to plan it, schedule it across time zones, promote it, run it, follow up and do it again next week, forever. It is the most reliable mechanism for keeping people connected and the most reliable cause of operator exhaustion, and platforms provide a calendar and a video link.

## Current Tools & Gaps
An events calendar, a video integration, reminders and a recording. The gaps: programming planned from scratch each cycle; attendance not predicted or managed; time zones handled manually; recordings and outputs not reused; and no measure of which programming actually retains members.
