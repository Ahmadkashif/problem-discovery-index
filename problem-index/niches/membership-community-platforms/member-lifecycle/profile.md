# Member Lifecycle

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/membership-community-platforms|Membership & Community Platforms]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a paying member who decides in a fortnight and cancels in a quarter — and the contest splits cleanly enough that it is not terminal.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.7B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~28% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — activity reported, belonging unmeasured
**Target Buyer:** Product and community leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — a clean graph with a clean outcome

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The business is recurring revenue and the revenue depends on whether a member feels they belong. That is decided in the first fortnight, visible in the reply graph, and executed months later as a cancellation nobody saw coming. The platform holds the complete interaction record and the renewal outcome, which makes this the cleanest dataset in the category and the largest contest in it — and it is two: getting the first two weeks right, and noticing the long silence that follows when it goes wrong.

## Current Tools & Gaps
An onboarding checklist, an introductions channel, daily active user counts and a cancellation form. The gaps: belonging never measured; the reply graph unread; disengagement discovered at cancellation; interventions generic; and the member's own reason never collected.
