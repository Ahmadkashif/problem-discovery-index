**Industry:** [[industries/membership-community-platforms|Membership & Community Platforms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Subscription businesses built activation, engagement scoring and churn prediction as standard practice, and community platforms report attendance.
**Tags:** #survival-analysis #gradient-boosting #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #revenue-impact #causal-inference #descriptive-statistics #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a paying member who decides in a fortnight and cancels in a quarter — and the contest splits cleanly enough that it is not terminal.

## The Problem
Subscription software developed a full lifecycle discipline: an activation definition identifying the behaviour that predicts retention, onboarding designed to reach it, engagement scoring, churn prediction with intervention, and cohort retention analysis. The practice is standard and effective. Community platforms sell subscriptions, have richer behavioural data than most software products, and manage the lifecycle with a welcome email and a monthly active user count.

## What Already Exists
Activation metric definition and onboarding design; engagement scoring; churn prediction and save flows; cohort retention analysis; and lifecycle messaging programmes.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a product made of other members. It requires: (1) activation that depends on other people's behaviour rather than on the member's own, since a newcomer cannot activate alone and the intervention must therefore target the community rather than the individual — this is the substantive difference and is what makes the standard playbook insufficient; (2) value delivered by peers, so a save offer addresses nothing; (3) a graph rather than a feature-usage record as the behavioural substrate; (4) a founder rather than a lifecycle marketing team executing, so everything must be a short prioritised list; and (5) belonging as the outcome, which the discipline has no metric for.

## Target Customer
Product and community leadership, founders and operators, members, and subscription lifecycle vendors.

## Impact If Solved
The subscription playbook assumes the user can activate through their own behaviour, and here a newcomer cannot activate alone. That single difference redirects every intervention from the member to the community around them.
