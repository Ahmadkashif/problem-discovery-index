## The Problem
Organisational network analysis is an established practice: mapping who communicates with whom, identifying brokers and bottlenecks, finding disconnected groups, measuring the dependence of a network on individual people, and diagnosing where information does not flow. Consultancies do it inside companies using survey data and email metadata. Community platforms have a complete, continuous, high-fidelity version of exactly that data and do not analyse it.

## What Already Exists
Organisational network analysis methodology; centrality and brokerage measures; subgroup and silo detection; key person dependency analysis; and intervention design from network findings.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a voluntary group with a commercial outcome. It requires: (1) a complete behavioural graph rather than a survey snapshot, which is far better data and supports continuous rather than episodic diagnosis — this is the substantive advantage; (2) members who can leave, so the analysis must connect to retention rather than to performance; (3) a founder rather than a consultant acting on it, meaning findings must be short and directive; (4) privacy considerations, since analysing who talks to whom in a paid community needs to be handled openly rather than covertly; and (5) thousands of communities to compare against, which no organisational study ever has.

## Target Customer
Product and data leadership, founders and community managers, organisational network consultants, and community analytics vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Organisational network analysis diagnoses groups from survey data and email metadata, and this category has the complete graph. Connecting the structural measures to renewal is the adaptation and makes the diagnosis commercially load-bearing.
