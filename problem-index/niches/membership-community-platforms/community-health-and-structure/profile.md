**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.3B US

**Target Buyer:** Product and data leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — graph analysis over a complete record

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The structural pathology is well known: a small minority produce most of the content, newcomers post into a room that does not answer, a handful of loud voices set a tone that quietly excludes, and the founder's participation is load-bearing. Every one of those is visible in the interaction graph — reply centralisation, subgroup closure, tone concentration, dependency on one node — and none of it is surfaced, because the analytics were inherited from a business that measured volume.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Post counts, active user charts and a member list. The gaps: the reply graph never analysed; centralisation and dependency unmeasured; subgroup closure invisible; tone and exclusion unexamined; and no early warning of structural decline.
