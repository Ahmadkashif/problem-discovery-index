# Migration & Portability

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/membership-community-platforms|Membership & Community Platforms]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let an operator move a community without losing its history, its structure and half its members — and whoever makes the move survivable wins the operators every competitor is trying to take.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$150M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — an export file at best
**Target Buyer:** Platform and operator leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — structured migration

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Operators move platforms — for pricing, features, reliability or a policy change — and the move is where communities die. The member list may export; the threads, the structure, the relationships, the subscriptions and the accumulated history usually do not. Members are asked to re-register somewhere new, a substantial fraction do not, and the community that arrives on the other side is smaller and has lost its memory. Migration is simultaneously the operator's biggest fear and the challenger's biggest obstacle.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A member export, sometimes a content export, and manual re-invitation. The gaps: content and thread structure not portable; relationships and roles lost; subscriptions requiring re-entry of payment details; no migration tooling on either side; and the loss rate unmeasured.
