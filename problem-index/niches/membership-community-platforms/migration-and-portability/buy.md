# Migration Practice From Platform Switching

**Niche:** [[niches/membership-community-platforms/migration-and-portability/profile|Migration & Portability]]
**Industry:** [[industries/membership-community-platforms|Membership & Community Platforms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Ecommerce and email platforms built migration as a competitive weapon with importers and white-glove teams, and community migration is a comma-separated file.
**Tags:** #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #evaluation-metrics #compliance #confidence-intervals #revenue-impact #graph-theory
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let an operator move a community without losing its history, its structure and half its members — and whoever makes the move survivable wins the operators every competitor is trying to take.

## The Problem
Challenger platforms in ecommerce, email marketing, help desks and content management all treated migration as the central competitive problem and solved it: importers for every major incumbent, structure and history preservation, parallel running, and migration teams who do the work for the customer. It is how challengers win in categories with high switching costs. Community platforms compete for operators and offer a member list export.

## What Already Exists
Purpose-built importers per incumbent platform; structure and history preservation; parallel running and cutover practice; migration support teams; and migration success measurement.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to an asset made of people. It requires: (1) members who must individually choose to move, so migration is a persuasion problem as much as a data one — this is the substantive difference from migrating a product catalogue; (2) relationships and social structure as the valuable part, which no importer models; (3) active subscriptions with payment credentials that cannot transfer without provider cooperation; (4) a community that cannot run in parallel for long, since a split community is worse than either; and (5) the loss being invisible until afterwards, when the members who did not follow are simply absent.

## Target Customer
Challenger platform leadership, operators, members, and migration tooling vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Challengers in every adjacent category won by making migration painless, and here the asset is people who must each decide to follow. That makes the persuasion and the subscription transfer more important than the data export everyone focuses on.
