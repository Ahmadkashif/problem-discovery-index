# Succession and Delegation Practice

**Niche:** [[niches/membership-community-platforms/the-founder/profile|The Founder]]
**Industry:** [[industries/membership-community-platforms|Membership & Community Platforms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Voluntary organisations and small practices have managed key person dependency for a long time with deputies, rotation and handover, and communities have one person and no plan.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #worker-facing #evaluation-metrics #graph-theory #automation #descriptive-statistics #compliance #confidence-intervals
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make a community survivable for the person whose availability currently is its health — and whoever does it keeps the operator the whole business depends on.

## The Problem
Clubs, societies, congregations, small practices and volunteer organisations all face a version of this: an institution whose functioning depends on one or two committed people. The practices that emerged are unglamorous and effective — named deputies, rotating roles, term limits, documented handovers, and a culture where asking for help is normal. Online communities have the same dependency and none of the practice, partly because the founder is also the commercial owner.

## What Already Exists
Deputy and second-in-command structures; role rotation and term limits; documented handover practice; volunteer recruitment and recognition; and continuity planning for absence.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a commercial community built on one person's identity. It requires: (1) a founder who is the reason members joined, so full succession is not available and the goal is redundancy in operations rather than in identity — this is the substantive difference; (2) volunteers with no organisational membership or duty, recruited from paying customers; (3) a commercial relationship where delegating to members raises questions about what members are paying for; (4) no committee structure or constitution to hang roles on; and (5) a platform that could support all of this and currently supports none of it.

## Target Customer
Founders and operators, member volunteers, platform product leadership, and community management advisors.

## Impact If Solved
Volunteer organisations solved key person dependency with deputies, rotation and handover. The adaptation is that the founder cannot be succeeded in identity, only in operations, which is where all the load actually sits.
