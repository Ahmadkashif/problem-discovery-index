# The Volunteer Moderator

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/membership-community-platforms|Membership & Community Platforms]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to support people doing unpaid emotional labour with no tooling, no policy and nobody to escalate to — and whoever does it interrupts a burnout cycle that is currently treated as inevitable.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — a delete button and goodwill
**Target Buyer:** Trust and safety leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — support, rotation and escalation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Unpaid volunteers handle the harassment reports, the disputes and occasionally a member in crisis, with a rules page, a delete button and nobody to escalate to. They are members of the community they police, they know the people involved, they receive the abuse that follows a decision, and they have no training, no support and no recognition. They burn out on a predictable cycle and are replaced by someone who will burn out on the same one.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A moderator role, a delete button, a report queue and a private channel. The gaps: no training or policy support; no escalation for serious matters; exposure to harmful content unmanaged; decisions made alone; and recognition and load entirely unmeasured.
