# Silent Churn Detection

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/membership-community-platforms|Membership & Community Platforms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to reach the member who stopped showing up months before they cancel — and whoever detects the silence early enough to act recovers revenue that is currently written off as inevitable.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$800M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — discovered at cancellation
**Target Buyer:** Product and revenue leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — prediction over an absence

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A member stops opening the app in week three, stays subscribed out of inertia, and cancels at renewal four months later. By then the disengagement is a quarter old and unrecoverable. The contest is detecting an absence — a signal defined by nothing happening — early enough to intervene, and doing it for a member who has not complained, has not contacted anyone, and is still paying. It is a long-horizon prediction problem and shares little with designing a first-fortnight introduction.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A cancellation form, a win-back email and a monthly churn number. The gaps: disengagement not detected; intervention only after cancellation; no distinction between a lapse and a departure; the reason never learned; and the interval between disengagement and cancellation treated as normal.
