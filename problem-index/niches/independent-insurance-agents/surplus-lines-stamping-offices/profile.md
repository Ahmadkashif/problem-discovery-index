# Surplus Lines Stamping Offices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-insurance-agents|Independent Insurance Agents]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State stamping offices review and record every surplus lines policy placed in their state, verifying eligibility and collecting the premium tax — which makes them the only party with a complete picture of what the excess and surplus market is actually writing, class by class.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 analysts and examiners per state office
**Output:** Filing review, eligibility determinations, premium volume reporting, market trend publications
**Proprietary data:** Every surplus lines transaction in the state, with class, premium, and carrier
**Clock:** Filing and tax remittance deadlines
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — assessment funded
