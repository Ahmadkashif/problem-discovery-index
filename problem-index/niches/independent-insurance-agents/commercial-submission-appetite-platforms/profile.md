# Commercial Submission & Appetite Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-insurance-agents|Independent Insurance Agents]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Bold Penguin, Tarmika, and their peers take a commercial risk from an agent and route it to carriers likely to want it, returning quotes. Pass 1 is blunt about the state of it: producers report that appetite matching remains the critical gap these tools do not address well.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 data scientists, appetite analysts, and carrier integration staff
**Output:** Carrier routing and appetite matching, quote comparison, submission automation
**Proprietary data:** Submission-to-quote-to-bind outcomes across many carriers and classes — the only view of the full funnel, since each carrier sees only what reached it
**Clock:** Policy effective dates and quote turnaround
**Buyer:** Chief Product Officer / VP of Data
