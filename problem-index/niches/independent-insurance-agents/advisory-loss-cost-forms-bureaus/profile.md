# Advisory Loss Cost & Policy Form Bureaus

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-insurance-agents|Independent Insurance Agents]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
As the licensed statistical agent for the property and casualty industry, these organizations receive detailed premium and loss data from carriers writing the large majority of US premium, and return the advisory prospective loss costs, policy forms, and rating rules those carriers build their filed rates on. Almost every commercial policy an independent agent places is written on a form this organization drafted and priced off a loss cost it published.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1,000-4,000 actuaries, statisticians, forms attorneys, coverage analysts, and data engineers
**Output:** Advisory prospective loss costs by line and state, policy forms and endorsements, classification systems, rating rules, and the filings that put them into effect
**Proprietary data:** Aggregated premium and loss experience contributed by carriers under a regulator-recognized statistical reporting arrangement — a corpus that cannot be assembled by anyone else on any timescale
**Clock:** State rate and form filing calendars; a loss cost or form revision must be filed and approved before any carrier can use it
**Buyer:** Chief Actuary / SVP of Analytics
