# MGAs & Program Underwriters

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-insurance-agents|Independent Insurance Agents]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Managing general agents and program underwriters hold delegated authority to underwrite and bind on a carrier's paper within a defined appetite. Retail agents send them the risks that do not fit a standard market — which is exactly the appetite-matching problem Pass 1 describes producers spending 30-60 minutes per account on, solved commercially by people who specialize in one class.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-800 underwriters, actuaries, and analysts per major MGA platform
**Output:** Underwriting decisions and pricing, program design, appetite definition, carrier performance reporting
**Proprietary data:** Submission-to-quote-to-bind outcomes with loss experience across a specialized book — the record of what was declined and by whom, which no carrier sees because carriers only see what was submitted to them
**Clock:** Policy effective dates and quote expiry, absolute
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer / Head of Analytics
