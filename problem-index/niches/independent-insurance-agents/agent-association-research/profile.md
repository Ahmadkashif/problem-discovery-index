# Agent Association Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-insurance-agents|Independent Insurance Agents]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The independent agent association publishes the operating benchmarks and best-practices studies agencies compare themselves against, and analyzes regulatory and market developments for its membership.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 researchers and policy staff
**Output:** Best practices studies, market share and agency operating benchmarks, regulatory analysis
**Proprietary data:** Long-running member operating survey series
**Clock:** Annual study cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Research — member funded
