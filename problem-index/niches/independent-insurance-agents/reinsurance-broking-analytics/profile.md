# Reinsurance Broking Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-insurance-agents|Independent Insurance Agents]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The analytics arms of the reinsurance brokers model catastrophe exposure, structure treaty programmes, and produce the portfolio analysis carriers take to the reinsurance market at renewal.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 catastrophe modellers, actuaries, and analysts
**Output:** Exposure and cat model analyses, programme structuring, market placement strategy, rating agency capital support
**Proprietary data:** Placement precedent and pricing across the market, layered over catastrophe models licensed from third parties and cedent data held under confidentiality
**Clock:** Reinsurance renewal dates, concentrated and immovable
**Buyer:** Head of Analytics / Chief Actuary
