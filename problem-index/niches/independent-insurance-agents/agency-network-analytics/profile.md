# Agency Network & Broker Platform Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-insurance-agents|Independent Insurance Agents]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup

## What They Do
The agency networks, clusters, and acquisitive broker platforms aggregate the books of thousands of independent agencies, negotiate carrier contracts on their behalf, and run analytics groups covering placement, retention, and contingent commission performance across the whole aggregate.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 analysts, carrier relations staff, and corporate development
**Output:** Carrier performance analysis, contingent commission optimization, acquisition underwriting, book benchmarking
**Proprietary data:** Placement, retention, and loss ratio across thousands of agencies and many carriers — the only cross-agency view of which markets actually perform
**Clock:** Renewal cycles and annual carrier contract negotiations
**Buyer:** Chief Analytics Officer / Head of Carrier Relations
