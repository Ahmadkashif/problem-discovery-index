# Certificate Tracking & Compliance Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-insurance-agents|Independent Insurance Agents]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors collect and verify certificates of insurance on behalf of the parties who require them — general contractors, property managers, franchisors — determining whether each vendor's coverage actually satisfies the contract. Pass 1 records the other side of this: CSRs at agencies field 20-50 certificate requests a day.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 compliance reviewers and analysts
**Output:** Compliance determinations against contract requirements, deficiency notices, coverage tracking
**Proprietary data:** Millions of certificates and the contract requirements they were tested against — a record of what coverage vendors in each trade actually carry
**Clock:** Policy expiry dates and project or lease commencement
**Buyer:** VP of Operations / Head of Product
