# Agency Management System Data Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-insurance-agents|Independent Insurance Agents]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The data groups at Applied Epic, Vertafore, and HawkSoft hold policy, client, and workflow records for most US independent agencies, and operate the download infrastructure carriers push policy data through.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 data and analytics staff
**Output:** In-product reporting, benchmarking, carrier download and integration services
**Proprietary data:** Policy and book-of-business records across the large majority of independent agencies, with renewal and retention history
**Clock:** Renewal and download cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Product
