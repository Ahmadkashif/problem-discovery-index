# Premium Audit Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-insurance-agents|Independent Insurance Agents]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Premium auditors examine a policyholder's payroll, sales, and subcontractor records after the policy period to determine the exposure the premium should actually have been based on, producing the adjustment that settles what the insured owes.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-800 auditors and reviewers per national firm
**Output:** Audit findings, exposure determinations, classification corrections, premium adjustments
**Proprietary data:** Audited exposure against reported exposure across hundreds of thousands of policies — a direct measure of how wrong the original classification and estimate were
**Clock:** Post-expiry audit windows and statutory dispute periods
**Buyer:** VP of Operations / National Practice Leader
