# Insurance Regulatory Data Organizations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-insurance-agents|Independent Insurance Agents]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The organization supporting state insurance regulators collects statutory financial statements and market conduct data from every licensed US insurer, operates the electronic rate and form filing system every carrier files through, and develops the model laws the states adopt.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-800 analysts, actuaries, financial examiners, and data staff
**Output:** Financial analysis and solvency monitoring, market conduct analysis, model laws, filing system operation
**Proprietary data:** Statutory financials and filings for every US insurer, plus the complete rate and form filing record across all states
**Clock:** Statutory filing deadlines, quarterly and annual
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — regulator-funded membership organization
