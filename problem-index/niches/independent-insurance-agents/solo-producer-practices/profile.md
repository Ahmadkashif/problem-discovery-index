# Solo Producer Agencies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/independent-insurance-agents|Independent Insurance Agents]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
The operator layer: much of the 40,000-agency population is one producer and a service representative. The producer's value is knowing which carrier will write which risk — the appetite knowledge Pass 1 says takes 30-60 minutes per account to apply against 15-30 carrier guides.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1-5 people
**Output:** Placement, coverage advice, claims advocacy
**Proprietary data:** A career of observed carrier behaviour — who wrote what, who declined, who paid — entirely undocumented
**Clock:** Renewal and effective dates
**Buyer:** The principal
