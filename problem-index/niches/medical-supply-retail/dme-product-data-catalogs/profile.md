# DME Product Data & Catalogues

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/medical-supply-retail|Medical Supply Retail]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Product data providers maintain the equipment catalogues, coding crosswalks, and pricing files suppliers order and bill from, reconciling manufacturer product data against billing codes and payer fee schedules.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 data stewards, coding analysts, and integration engineers
**Output:** Product catalogues with coding attribution, fee schedule and pricing data, ordering integration
**Proprietary data:** Reconciled product-to-code-to-price mappings across manufacturers and payers
**Clock:** Fee schedule updates and annual code changes
**Buyer:** Head of Product
