# Medicare DMEPOS Programme Administration

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/medical-supply-retail|Medical Supply Retail]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Federal contractors administer durable medical equipment claims, set and run the competitive bidding programme that determines payment rates, and conduct the medical review that recovers improper payments from suppliers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-1,500 claims analysts, medical reviewers, economists, and programme staff
**Output:** Claim determinations, bidding programme design and rate setting, coverage policy, improper payment findings
**Proprietary data:** National DME claims with utilization, pricing, and review outcomes
**Clock:** Statutory bidding cycles and claim processing deadlines
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — federal contract
