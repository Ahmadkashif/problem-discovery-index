# DME Billing & Revenue Cycle Outsourcers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/medical-supply-retail|Medical Supply Retail]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Specialist billing operations submit, follow up, and appeal equipment claims for suppliers, in a segment where documentation requirements are unusually strict and denial rates unusually high.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 billers, appeal specialists, and analysts
**Output:** Claim submission, denial analysis and appeals, documentation improvement, collection reporting
**Proprietary data:** Denial reasons and appeal outcomes across many suppliers and equipment categories
**Clock:** Timely filing and appeal deadlines
**Buyer:** COO / VP of Operations
