# DME Manufacturer Reimbursement Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/medical-supply-retail|Medical Supply Retail]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Equipment manufacturers run reimbursement and market access teams that secure coding and coverage for their products and support suppliers with documentation guidance — because a product with an unfavourable code cannot be sold profitably regardless of its merits.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 reimbursement specialists, health economists, and clinical affairs staff
**Output:** Coding applications, coverage dossiers, supplier documentation guidance, economic evidence
**Proprietary data:** Coding and coverage precedent across a product portfolio
**Clock:** Coding application cycles and product launch commitments
**Buyer:** VP of Market Access
