# DME Accreditation Organizations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/medical-supply-retail|Medical Supply Retail]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Accrediting bodies evaluate equipment suppliers against quality standards, and accreditation is a condition of Medicare enrolment — a supplier that loses it loses its ability to bill.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 surveyors, standards staff, and analysts
**Output:** Accreditation decisions, survey findings, standards development
**Proprietary data:** Survey findings across thousands of suppliers over multiple cycles
**Clock:** Three-year accreditation cycles with billing eligibility attached
**Buyer:** Executive director
