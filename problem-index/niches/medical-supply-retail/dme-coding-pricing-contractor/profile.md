# DME Coding & Pricing Determination

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/medical-supply-retail|Medical Supply Retail]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The pricing and coding determination contractor decides which billing code a specific piece of equipment falls under — a determination that sets what a manufacturer's product is paid and whether it is competitive at all.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 coding analysts, clinicians, and engineers
**Output:** Product coding verifications, coding determinations, product classification lists
**Proprietary data:** Product-level coding determinations across the entire equipment market
**Clock:** Manufacturer submission and product launch timelines
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — federal contract
