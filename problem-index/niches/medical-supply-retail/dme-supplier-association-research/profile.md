# DME Supplier Association Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/medical-supply-retail|Medical Supply Retail]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The supplier associations analyse the payment policy and competitive bidding rules that determine whether independent suppliers survive, and publish the economic research used in advocacy.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 policy analysts and researchers
**Output:** Rate impact analysis, regulatory comment, supplier access research, member surveys
**Proprietary data:** Member survey series layered over public claims and rate data
**Clock:** Rule comment periods and bidding cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Government Relations — member funded
