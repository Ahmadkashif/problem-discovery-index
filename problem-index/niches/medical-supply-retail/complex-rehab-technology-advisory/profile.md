# Complex Rehab Technology Advisory

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/medical-supply-retail|Medical Supply Retail]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Rehabilitation technology specialists assess individuals with complex mobility needs and specify custom seating and wheeled mobility systems, producing the clinical justification that supports a five-figure funding request.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 assistive technology professionals and clinical specialists per larger provider
**Output:** Clinical evaluations, equipment specifications, funding justification letters, fitting and outcome documentation
**Proprietary data:** Configuration and outcome records across many complex clients, held informally
**Clock:** Funding decision timelines and clinical need
**Buyer:** Director of Rehab Services
