# Respiratory Therapy Adherence Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/medical-supply-retail|Medical Supply Retail]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The sleep and respiratory device manufacturers receive nightly usage and therapy data from millions of connected devices, and that data is what determines whether Medicare continues to pay for the equipment a supplier placed.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 data scientists, clinical researchers, and platform staff
**Output:** Adherence reporting to suppliers and payers, therapy optimization, clinical research, device performance monitoring
**Proprietary data:** Nightly therapy and adherence records from millions of patients over years — the largest longitudinal home therapy dataset in existence
**Clock:** Medicare adherence documentation windows, which decide whether the equipment is paid for
**Buyer:** VP of Data Science / Chief Medical Officer
