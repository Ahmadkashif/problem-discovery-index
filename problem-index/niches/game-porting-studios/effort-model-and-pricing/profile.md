# Effort Model & Bid Pricing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/game-porting-studios|Game Porting Studios]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn measured codebase properties and a history of past projects into a price with stated risk — and whoever prices it correctly takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$250M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — no history kept
**Target Buyer:** Commercial leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — modelling over a project corpus

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This half consumes measurements and produces a commercial position. Given what is known about the codebase and what the studio's own past projects cost, what should the price be, what contingency is warranted, and what should be excluded or made conditional. The input is the studio's project history rather than any single codebase, the buyer is whoever signs the contract, and the hard part is organisational — the studio must treat its own past as data, which is a different undertaking from buying a scanner.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A rate card, a spreadsheet, a margin assumption and a negotiation. The gaps: no project history in structured form; no relationship between assessment and realised effort; no risk pricing; no contingency methodology; and no estimate accuracy tracking.
