**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Construction and software engineering built parametric estimation with calibrated historical databases, and porting studios use a technical director's instinct.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to quote a fixed price and a fixed date for work whose difficulty depends entirely on properties of a codebase they cannot see until after the contract is signed — and whoever estimates it accurately takes the account.

## The Problem
Parametric estimation is a mature discipline in any industry that bids fixed-price work. Construction, defence and large-scale software estimation all use calibrated historical databases relating measurable project characteristics to realised effort, with explicit risk pricing, contingency methodology and estimate-to-actual tracking as a managed metric. The methods are documented and taught. Porting studios bid comparable risk on the judgement of whoever has done the most of them.

## What Already Exists
Parametric cost models calibrated on historical projects; risk and contingency pricing methodology; estimate-to-actual variance tracking; work breakdown standards; and staged estimate refinement as information improves.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a project whose defining inputs are properties of someone else's source code. It requires: (1) cost drivers that are codebase characteristics rather than scope or square footage, so the measurement itself is a technical problem — this is the substantive difference and is why generic models do not apply; (2) an estimate made with almost no access to the thing being estimated, which construction never faces; (3) a moving target, since the client keeps developing during the port; (4) project counts in the dozens rather than the thousands, so the model must be simple and borrow strength; and (5) a hard platform whose performance work is genuinely open-ended and resists parametric treatment.

## Target Customer
Porting and co-development studios, publishers commissioning work, services businesses with the same structure, and estimation methodology vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Parametric estimation is mature wherever fixed-price work is bid, with documented methods and calibrated databases. Cost drivers that are properties of someone else's source code, measured before access, is what has to be built here.
