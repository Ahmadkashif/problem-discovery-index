# Performance Optimisation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/game-porting-studios|Game Porting Studios]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to find out why a stranger's game runs at half the required frame rate on constrained hardware — and whoever shortens that search takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$350M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~17% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — expert-driven
**Target Buyer:** Technical directors
**Automation Potential:** High — profiling analysis and attribution

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The game runs at half the required frame rate on the hard platform and the work is finding out why, in a codebase written by strangers for different hardware. The hardest target defines the project. This work is the most skilled and the least estimable part of a port, it is where schedules are consumed, and it is performed by a handful of very experienced engineers reading profiler output and forming hypotheses. Nothing supports the search itself.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Platform profilers, a frame capture tool, and an expert's intuition about where to look. The gaps: no attribution of frame cost to source-level causes; no comparison against the source platform's profile; no history of what caused problems on past projects; no prioritisation by expected gain; and no record of what was tried.
