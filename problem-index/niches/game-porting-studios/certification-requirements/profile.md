# Certification & Platform Requirements

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/game-porting-studios|Game Porting Studios]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to satisfy several platforms' certification regimes from requirement documents written in prose, where a late failure costs a release window — and whoever makes compliance checkable takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$200M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — documents
**Target Buyer:** Certification specialists
**Automation Potential:** High — automated requirement checking

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every platform holder maintains a certification regime: hundreds of requirements covering suspend and resume behaviour, controller handling, storage, network loss, account states, accessibility, store metadata and error messaging. They are published as prose documents, they differ between platforms, they change between submissions, and a failure late in the project costs a release window. The work of tracking, implementing and verifying them is done by specialists reading documents and maintaining spreadsheets.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A requirements PDF per platform, a tracking spreadsheet, a test pass and a submission portal. The gaps: no machine-readable requirement set; no automated checking of testable requirements; no mapping across platforms; no change tracking between document versions; and no reuse of compliance work across projects.
