# Cross-Platform Verification

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/game-porting-studios|Game Porting Studios]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to verify every change on every platform when verification means people playing the game and the client keeps shipping updates — and whoever automates that takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~20% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — people playing
**Target Buyer:** QA leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — automated play, capture and comparison

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every change must be verified on every platform, verification is people playing the game, and the client keeps shipping updates that require doing it all again. The cost is multiplicative — platforms times builds times test passes — and it is the largest recurring line in most porting projects. Nobody has built verification that scales with the platform count, so the studio's answer is more testers, and the moving target means the same content is verified repeatedly.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A test plan, a team of testers, a bug database and devkits. The gaps: no automated playthrough; no cross-platform output comparison; no change-scoped test selection; no automated performance regression detection; and no reuse of verification across builds.
