# The Producer Explaining the Slip

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/game-porting-studios|Game Porting Studios]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let the person between a fixed date and a moving codebase explain the slip with evidence rather than apology — and whoever gives them that takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$150M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** Production leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — attribution and reporting

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The port is against a target that ships patches every fortnight, every merge invalidates completed testing, and the producer is the person explaining to both sides why the date is slipping. They sit between a client who believes the scope is fixed and a team absorbing changes nobody priced. They have no instrument that attributes the delay to its causes, so the conversation is a negotiation of impressions in which the producer is structurally the one at fault.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A schedule spreadsheet, a burndown, a weekly call and a changelog from the client. The gaps: no attribution of delay to client changes; no measurement of rework caused by merges; no variation tracking against the contract; no early warning; and no shared view of the schedule with the client.
