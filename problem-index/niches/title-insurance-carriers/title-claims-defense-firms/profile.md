# Title Claims Defense & Coverage Litigation Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/title-insurance-carriers|Title Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Law firms on underwriters' panels, and the underwriters' own captive law groups, defend insureds in title litigation. They also bring quiet-title and cure actions and litigate coverage disputes with insureds. The underwriter pays, hourly or through salaried in-house groups. This is the outside half of the work that [[niches/title-insurance-carriers/cure-defense-panel-counsel/profile|🎯 Cure, Defense & Panel Counsel Management]] manages.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10–60 title litigation attorneys at the largest specialist panel firms and captive law groups (estimate)
**Output:** Defence, cure and coverage litigation, billed as legal services
**Proprietary data:** Matter files, pleadings and settlement histories, owned by the underwriter client and privileged
**Clock:** Court deadlines and trial dates
**Buyer:** Underwriter's Chief Claims Counsel
