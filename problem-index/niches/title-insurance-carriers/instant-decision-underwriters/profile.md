# Digital Title Underwriters & Instant Decision Engines

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/title-insurance-carriers|Title Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Underwriters and vendors that decide title risk algorithmically on low-risk refinances and home-equity loans and issue a commitment or policy without a full examination. Doma (formerly States Title, now part of Title Resources Group) was the first vendor in Fannie Mae's title acceptance pilot. Westcor joined in July 2025 with X1 Analytics. WFG's DecisionPoint and ANTIC's REalSearch offer touchless refinance commitments. Lenders pay through the premium or, in the pilot, a fee in place of a policy.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10–50 data scientists, engineers and model-underwriting staff per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Automated clear-to-close decisions and the commitments built on them, sold inside a premium or pilot fee rather than invoiced as analysis
**Proprietary data:** Each firm's own decisions joined to claims on instantly decided policies, plus licensed property and lien data; Doma reports loss curves on instant files through year five
**Clock:** Lender turn-time expectations, softer than a contract date; the GSE pilot sets eligibility terms, not deadlines
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer / head of lender products
