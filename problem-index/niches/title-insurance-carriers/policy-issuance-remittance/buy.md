# Accounts-Receivable Automation Taught Title Rate Manuals

**Niche:** [[niches/title-insurance-carriers/policy-issuance-remittance/profile|Policy Issuance, CPLs & Premium Remittance]]
**Industry:** [[industries/title-insurance-carriers|Title Insurance Carriers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Cash application and AR automation tools already match remittances to invoices; an underwriter needs them to match remittances to policies and to check each premium against a state rate manual.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #decision-trees #evaluation-metrics #automation #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make every policy and closing protection letter an agent issues arrive reported, correctly rated and remitted without rekeying, and whoever closes the gap between CPLs issued and policies remitted fastest takes the account.

## The Problem
Remittance staff match agents' payments to the policies they report and check premiums by hand at month-end.

## What Already Exists
Accounts-receivable and cash-application platforms that extract remittance advice, match payments to open invoices and route exceptions.

## The Customization Gap
Title needs: the policy, not an invoice, as the matching object; premium recalculation from each state's rate manual (brackets, reissue and simultaneous-issue rules, endorsement charges); agent split terms per agency agreement; CPL linkage; and promulgated-rate states handled separately from file-and-use states.

## Target Customer
Underwriter finance teams already using AR automation for other lines of business.

## Impact If Solved
Month-end matching handled by exception, with every premium checked rather than sampled.
