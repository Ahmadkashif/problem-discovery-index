# Enterprise Knowledge Search Pointed at Underwriting Bulletins

**Niche:** [[niches/title-insurance-carriers/agency-channel-underwriting/profile|Agency-Channel Residential Underwriting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/title-insurance-carriers|Title Insurance Carriers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Enterprise search and LLM assistants already answer questions over a company's documents; an underwriter needs them to answer an agent's question with the right state's bulletin and the company's own precedent.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #word-embeddings #transformers #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep independent agents writing residential files on its paper by answering their approval and insure-over requests inside the closing window on terms it can stand behind, and whoever approves fastest without the approvals turning into claims takes the account.

## The Problem
Agents call underwriting counsel with questions the manual already answers, because finding the answer in a state manual, a stack of bulletins and the underwriter's practice notes takes longer than a phone call. Counsel spend their peak hours answering lookups instead of judging risks.

## What Already Exists
Enterprise search and retrieval-augmented assistants (Microsoft Copilot, Glean and the like) index a company's documents and answer questions over them. Underwriters publish bulletin libraries and manuals online for their agents.

## The Customization Gap
Title needs the following layered on: retrieval scoped by state and by policy form, because the same question has different answers in Texas and New York; awareness of which bulletin supersedes which; citation down to the manual section, so an agent can rely on the answer; a hard line between what an agent may decide under its own authority and what must go to counsel, with the second routed automatically; and the underwriter's own precedent approvals as a retrievable source alongside the published rules.

## Target Customer
Agency operations and underwriting departments that want to cut call volume to counsel without a new underwriting system.

## Impact If Solved
Routine lookups answered in seconds with a citation, and counsel's time returned to the risks that need judgement.
