# The Approval Email With No Reason Attached

**Niche:** [[niches/title-insurance-carriers/agency-channel-underwriting/profile|Agency-Channel Residential Underwriting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/title-insurance-carriers|Title Insurance Carriers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** An approval goes out as a two-line email, and when the claim arrives years later nobody can say what counsel knew, what conditions were set or why.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #compliance #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep independent agents writing residential files on its paper by answering their approval and insure-over requests inside the closing window on terms it can stand behind, and whoever approves fastest without the approvals turning into claims takes the account.

## The Problem
Approvals are given fast and recorded briefly: "OK to insure over, get seller indemnity." The commitment, the documents counsel reviewed and the reasoning stay in the agent's file or nowhere. When a claim arrives, claims counsel cannot tell whether the agent met the conditions, and underwriting cannot learn from the outcome.

## Why It's Still Broken
Writing a full record takes time counsel do not have at peak, and approval tools are built to move the request, not to keep the decision.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Capture each approval as a record drafted from the request: risk, facts relied on, conditions, policy number. Counsel confirms it with a one-line reason, and the agent's acceptance of the conditions is logged. Claims can then pull the approval record by policy number on the day a notice arrives.

## Who Feels the Pain
Underwriting counsel whose names are on old approvals, claims counsel reconstructing what was agreed, and agency managers trying to judge whether an agent met conditions.

## Impact If Fixed
At almost no cost in counsel time, every approval becomes defensible, and the approval-to-claim record a later grading system needs starts to exist.
