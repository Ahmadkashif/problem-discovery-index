# Lender Decision Engines Extended to Title Risk

**Niche:** [[niches/title-insurance-carriers/home-equity-refinance-lenders/profile|Home Equity & Refinance Lenders]]
**Industry:** [[industries/title-insurance-carriers|Title Insurance Carriers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Lenders already run automated credit and collateral decisions in their loan origination systems; title could be one more decision in the same engine rather than a separate order.
**Tags:** #decision-trees #gradient-boosting #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to become the lender's default title answer on low-risk refinance and home-equity loans, whether a limited policy, an attorney opinion letter or a GSE-accepted waiver priced from an automated decision, and whoever proves its automated decisions lose no more than a full policy at a fraction of the price takes the account.

## The Problem
On a home-equity loan the lender decides credit and value in seconds and then waits days for a title product ordered separately.

## What Already Exists
Automated underwriting and decision engines inside lenders' origination systems, and automated valuation waivers for collateral, the analogue the vault profiles as [[niches/real-estate-appraisers/gse-collateral-policy-analytics/profile|🔍 Secondary Market Collateral Policy & Analytics]].

## The Customization Gap
Title needs: an API returning a recommended product (full policy, limited policy, attorney opinion letter, waiver-eligible) with a price and the reasons; property and lien data checked in real time; seller and owner identity signals for forgery risk; GSE pilot eligibility rules; and the underwriter's binding commitment attached to the decision.

## Target Customer
Home-equity and refinance lenders, and the underwriters selling to them.

## Impact If Solved
Title decided in the same seconds as credit and value on the loans where risk allows it.
