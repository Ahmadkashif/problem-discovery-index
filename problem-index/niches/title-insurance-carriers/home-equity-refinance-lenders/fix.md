# A Refinance Examined Like a Purchase

**Niche:** [[niches/title-insurance-carriers/home-equity-refinance-lenders/profile|Home Equity & Refinance Lenders]]
**Industry:** [[industries/title-insurance-carriers|Title Insurance Carriers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A homeowner refinancing the house they have owned for ten years gets the same search, exam and requirement list as a buyer of a stranger's property.
**Tags:** #decision-trees #large-language-models #automation #quick-win #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to become the lender's default title answer on low-risk refinance and home-equity loans, whether a limited policy, an attorney opinion letter or a GSE-accepted waiver priced from an automated decision, and whoever proves its automated decisions lose no more than a full policy at a fraction of the price takes the account.

## The Problem
Many agents and underwriters run refinances through the purchase workflow. The search goes back as far as for a sale, the commitment raises the same requirements, and turn times run days where a lender expects minutes.

## Why It's Still Broken
Underwriting manuals are written for the purchase case, and agents earn the same fee either way, so nobody has an incentive to shorten the path.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A refinance track in the underwriting manual: search back to the vesting deed or the prior policy, standard requirements limited to open mortgages and liens, automatic clearance where the decision engine scores the file low-risk, and escalation only on specified triggers.

## Who Feels the Pain
Refinancing homeowners, lenders measured on turn time, and agents processing low-margin refinance volume.

## Impact If Fixed
Refinance commitments in hours rather than days, at a cost the lender will pay without reaching for a waiver.
