# Loss Evidence for Instantly Decided Files

**Niche:** [[niches/title-insurance-carriers/home-equity-refinance-lenders/profile|Home Equity & Refinance Lenders]]
**Industry:** [[industries/title-insurance-carriers|Title Insurance Carriers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Every instant-decision vendor claims its files lose no more than examined ones, but the claim rests on short loss histories that nobody outside the vendor can check.
**Tags:** #survival-analysis #causal-inference #gradient-boosting #confidence-intervals #hypothesis-testing #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to become the lender's default title answer on low-risk refinance and home-equity loans, whether a limited policy, an attorney opinion letter or a GSE-accepted waiver priced from an automated decision, and whoever proves its automated decisions lose no more than a full policy at a fraction of the price takes the account.

## The Problem
Doma reported instantly underwritten refinance policies with loss curves comparing favourably to traditional vintages through year five, at average premiums of 63–76% of traditional. GSE pilots, lenders and regulators all need that kind of evidence to trust a waiver or a limited product. But title losses emerge over many years, and fraud and forgery refinance claims averaged about $207,000 in Milliman's later study for ALTA. A five-year loss curve on a young product is not yet proof.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Each vendor holds only its own files. The comparison group, examined refinances of similar risk, sits with other underwriters. Selection bias is severe, because the engines approve the cleanest files.

## What to Build
A matched-cohort loss study and monitoring service. Instantly decided files are compared with examined refinances of similar property, lien history, owner tenure and geography. Claim emergence is modelled with survival curves and honest intervals, and losses are broken down by defect type, with forgery tracked separately. Each lender or GSE pilot receives a quarterly report.

## Target Customer
Underwriters running instant-decision programmes, lenders choosing among products, and GSE pilot teams.

## Impact If Built
The underwriter that can show credible loss evidence wins the lender's default slot. The contest stops being decided by vendor claims.
