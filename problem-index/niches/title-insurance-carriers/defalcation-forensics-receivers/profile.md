# Escrow Defalcation Forensics & Agency Receivers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/title-insurance-carriers|Title Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Forensic accountants and court- or regulator-appointed receivers who take over a failed title agency's escrow accounts after a defalcation. They reconstruct who was owed what, trace the missing funds and support the underwriter's closing protection letter claims and recoveries. The firm-level forensic practices that take this work are profiled under SMB Accounting Firms as [[niches/accounting-firms-smb/forensic-litigation-support/profile|🔍 Forensic Accounting & Litigation Support]] (53, indexed); a title defalcation is one occasional matter type inside them. The underwriter's own audit function is logged as [[niches/title-and-settlement-offices/underwriter-agency-audit-teams/profile|🔍 Underwriter Agency Audit & Agent Risk Teams]] (31).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Engagement teams of under 10 per matter; title defalcations are occasional work for general forensic practices (estimate)
**Output:** Account reconstructions, tracing reports, receivership accounts
**Proprietary data:** The failed agency's records, held under court order or through counsel
**Clock:** Court and receivership deadlines
**Buyer:** Underwriter's claims counsel or the court
