# Commercial Zoning Report Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/title-insurance-carriers|Title Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
National due-diligence firms produce the zoning reports that lenders require on commercial loans. Each report states a property's zoning, permitted use, conformity on setbacks, parking and density, and any open violations. Title underwriters rely on them to issue the ALTA 3-series zoning endorsements that lender's counsel ask for in the pro forma. Many are the same engineering consultancies that write property condition assessments, logged under Commercial Real Estate as [[niches/commercial-real-estate/property-condition-assessment-firms/profile|🔍 Property Condition Assessment Firms]] (45); the zoning report is a distinct deliverable with its own analysts. The borrower or lender pays per property.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Zoning analysts who each research and write one report per property, beside staff who request zoning verification letters and records from municipalities; no separate analytics or standards function of 10+ at any provider was found (estimate)
**Output:** Zoning and land-use reports per property, with conformance findings
**Proprietary data:** A library of municipal codes, verification letters and parcel-level conformance determinations accumulated across deals; the codes themselves are public
**Clock:** Commercial loan closing and funding dates, set by the deal contract rather than a regulator
**Buyer:** VP of Due Diligence / national zoning practice leader
