# Findings Written as Narrative and Never Tracked

**Niche:** [[niches/title-insurance-carriers/agency-audit-escrow-oversight/profile|Agency Audit & Escrow Oversight]]
**Industry:** [[industries/title-insurance-carriers|Title Insurance Carriers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** An audit report describes an agent's late reconciliations and unexplained balances in paragraphs, and nobody checks at the next visit whether anything changed.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #workflow-orchestration #compliance #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to find an agent's escrow shortage before the money is gone, and whoever detects shortages earliest per audit day spent takes the account.

## The Problem
Audit findings are written as narrative reports and filed. Remediation commitments are tracked in email, if at all, and an agent with the same findings three audits running looks no different in the system from a clean one.

## Why It's Still Broken
Audit tools were built to produce reports, not to keep a register of findings, and agency managers own the relationship and soften follow-up.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Findings extracted from reports into a register with type, severity, due date and status; repeat findings flagged automatically; and a quarterly view for the agency risk committee by agent and finding type.

## Who Feels the Pain
Field auditors repeating the same findings, agency risk leaders without a portfolio view, and claims counsel who discover after a defalcation that the warning signs were written down years before.

## Impact If Fixed
Repeat findings become a tracked risk signal instead of a forgotten paragraph.
