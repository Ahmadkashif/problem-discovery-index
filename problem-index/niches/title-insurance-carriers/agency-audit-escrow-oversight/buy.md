# Continuous Controls Monitoring Adapted to Escrow Accounts

**Niche:** [[niches/title-insurance-carriers/agency-audit-escrow-oversight/profile|Agency Audit & Escrow Oversight]]
**Industry:** [[industries/title-insurance-carriers|Title Insurance Carriers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Continuous controls monitoring tools already test every transaction in a company's ledger; underwriters need them to test agents' escrow ledgers they do not own.
**Tags:** #autoencoders #descriptive-statistics #evaluation-metrics #compliance #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to find an agent's escrow shortage before the money is gone, and whoever detects shortages earliest per audit day spent takes the account.

## The Problem
Auditors test samples of an agent's disbursements by hand, a few days a year, when every transaction could be tested continuously.

## What Already Exists
Audit analytics and continuous controls monitoring platforms that ingest general ledgers and bank data and flag exceptions against rules and statistical baselines; escrow reconciliation services sold to agents, logged as [[niches/title-and-settlement-offices/escrow-reconciliation-monitoring/profile|🔍 Escrow Reconciliation & Trust Account Monitoring Services]].

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: connectors to the agents' title production systems and escrow banks under the data rights in each underwriting agreement; escrow-specific tests (three-way reconciliation breaks, disbursement before funds are good, even-dollar transfers, negative file balances, payoffs with no release); joining to the underwriter's CPL and policy records; and a consent and privacy model that agents will accept.

## Target Customer
Agency audit departments that want continuous testing without building a data platform.

## Impact If Solved
Every agent transaction tested, not a sample, and auditors sent to explain exceptions rather than find them.
