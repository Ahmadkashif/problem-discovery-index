# Title Reinsurance Markets

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/title-insurance-carriers|Title Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Treaty and facultative reinsurance of title risk, written mostly by other title underwriters on large commercial liabilities and by a few outside markets for regional underwriters' capital needs. Cessions are small: the industry retained 99.5% of premium in 2025 (NAIC). Treaty and facultative reinsurance generally belongs to [[industries/reinsurers|Reinsurers]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** A few underwriters and treaty staff per market (estimate)
**Output:** Reinsurance placements and pricing
**Proprietary data:** Ceded large-risk files
**Clock:** Deal and renewal dates
**Buyer:** Chief underwriting officer of the assuming company
