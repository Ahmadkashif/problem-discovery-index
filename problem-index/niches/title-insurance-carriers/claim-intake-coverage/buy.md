# Insurance Document AI Fitted to Title Policies

**Niche:** [[niches/title-insurance-carriers/claim-intake-coverage/profile|Claim Intake & Coverage Determination]]
**Industry:** [[industries/title-insurance-carriers|Title Insurance Carriers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Insurance document AI already reads policies and claim correspondence for P&C carriers; title intake needs it to read Schedule B exceptions, endorsements and recorded instruments against the claim.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #bert #evaluation-metrics #automation #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a title claim notice into an acknowledged claim with a coverage position and a first reserve inside the statutory deadline, and whoever gets coverage and reserve right on the first read, with the fewest later reversals, takes the account.

## The Problem
Intake staff read the notice, find the policy, read the exceptions and endorsements, pull the recorded documents and draft the acknowledgement, all by hand.

## What Already Exists
Insurance document-processing and claims AI tools that classify incoming correspondence, extract claim facts and compare them to policy language for P&C lines.

## The Customization Gap
Title intake needs: ALTA policy forms and state variants as base text; Schedule B exceptions parsed from each policy and matched to the defect claimed; endorsement-by-endorsement coverage mapping; retrieval of recorded instruments by parcel and date; state claims-handling deadlines; and drafts in the underwriter's own coverage-letter language.

## Target Customer
Claims intake teams at underwriters that already license document AI for other purposes, or that want to avoid a bespoke build.

## Impact If Solved
The first coverage memo is ready when counsel open the file, and acknowledgement deadlines stop being at risk.
