# The Policy Nobody Can Find

**Niche:** [[niches/title-insurance-carriers/claim-intake-coverage/profile|Claim Intake & Coverage Determination]]
**Industry:** [[industries/title-insurance-carriers|Title Insurance Carriers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A claimant sends a twenty-year-old owner's policy, or none, and intake spends days proving which policy, which agent and which exceptions apply.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a title claim notice into an acknowledged claim with a coverage position and a first reserve inside the statutory deadline, and whoever gets coverage and reserve right on the first read, with the fewest later reversals, takes the account.

## The Problem
Owners rarely keep their policies. Agents close and their files scatter, and older policies were reported on paper. Intake staff search policy registers by address, name and approximate date, call successor agencies, and sometimes cannot confirm coverage within the deadline.

## Why It's Still Broken
Policy registers were built for remittance, not retrieval. Older records are incomplete, and agents' files are outside the underwriter's systems.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A policy lookup that searches by parcel, name variants, address history and date range across the underwriter's registers, CPL logs and imaged jackets, and returns ranked candidates with their exceptions. Unresolved lookups get a standard request to the successor agent.

## Who Feels the Pain
Intake staff, claimants waiting for an answer, and counsel up against acknowledgement deadlines.

## Impact If Fixed
Coverage confirmed in hours rather than days, and fewer positions taken on incomplete files.
