# Claim Intake & Coverage Determination

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/title-insurance-carriers|Title Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a title claim notice into an acknowledged claim with a coverage position and a first reserve inside the statutory deadline, and whoever gets coverage and reserve right on the first read, with the fewest later reversals, takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$100M of claims staff, systems and outside coverage review spent at intake (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.5% of premium (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — notices arrive by mail and email, and the policy and underwriting file are retrieved by hand
**Target Buyer:** Chief Claims Counsel, claims intake manager
**Automation Potential:** High for acknowledgement, file assembly and precedent retrieval; low for the coverage decision itself

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Intake is a deadline contest on documents. Unfair claims settlement practices acts set clocks for acknowledging a claim and taking a coverage position (roughly 10–45 days by state and step; estimate). The first reserve set here feeds the triangles the actuaries rely on. The work is assembling the policy, the exceptions, the commitment and any approval, and the recorded documents, then reading them against the claim. Its winner is whoever does that fastest and most accurately. The contest is different from litigation, which is won on negotiation and legal spend months later.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Claim systems, document management and the underwriters' own plant and imaging systems. Gaps: the underwriting file often sits with the agent, counsel's intake read is not captured, and reversals of coverage positions are not tracked as a quality measure.
