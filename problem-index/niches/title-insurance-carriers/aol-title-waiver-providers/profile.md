# Attorney Opinion Letter & Title Waiver Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/title-insurance-carriers|Title Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Firms that give lenders an attorney opinion letter on title in place of a lender's policy, sometimes backed by errors-and-omissions cover, on loans the GSEs accept (Fannie Mae reported buying more than 10,000 such loans). They compete with title insurers for refinance volume. ALTA argues the letters leave coverage gaps.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analytical staff per provider (estimate); the work is done by licensed attorneys on production volume
**Output:** Attorney opinion letters on title
**Proprietary data:** Their own opinions and any claims against them; small books
**Clock:** Lender closing timelines
**Buyer:** Founder or chief legal officer
