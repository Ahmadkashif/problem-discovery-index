# Root Cause Never Reaches Underwriting

**Niche:** [[niches/title-insurance-carriers/title-claims-administration/profile|Title Claims Administration & Litigation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/title-insurance-carriers|Title Insurance Carriers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Claims counsel learn exactly why each loss happened, and that knowledge stays in the claim file instead of reaching the underwriting counsel and agency managers who could prevent the next one.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #evaluation-metrics #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #compliance
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to resolve title claims at the lowest total cost of loss and expense. That splits into two contests with different winners: deciding coverage and setting the first reserve correctly inside the statutory clock at intake, and curing or defending covered claims through counsel for less than paying them. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
A claim closes with counsel knowing it came from an examiner who missed a judgment, an agent who ignored an approval condition, or an impostor using a fake notary stamp. That finding is written in a closing memo, if anywhere, and is rarely coded.

## Why It's Still Broken
Claims are measured on closing files and cost, not on teaching underwriting, and there is no shared taxonomy of root causes.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A short root-cause taxonomy, codes suggested by a classifier from the closing memo and confirmed by counsel, and a monthly feed to underwriting and agency management: losses by cause, agent and approval category.

## Who Feels the Pain
Underwriting counsel repeating approvals that have failed before, agency managers keeping agents with recurring errors, and actuaries without claim-type data.

## Impact If Fixed
A cheap change in closing practice turns every claim into feedback for the decisions that created it.
