# P&C Claims Platforms Adapted to Cure-First Title Claims

**Niche:** [[niches/title-insurance-carriers/title-claims-administration/profile|Title Claims Administration & Litigation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/title-insurance-carriers|Title Insurance Carriers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Property and casualty claims platforms already run intake, reserving, diaries and vendor management; title claims need them reshaped around cure, recorded documents and policy-year reserving.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to resolve title claims at the lowest total cost of loss and expense. That splits into two contests with different winners: deciding coverage and setting the first reserve correctly inside the statutory clock at intake, and curing or defending covered claims through counsel for less than paying them. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Title claims departments run on in-house systems or generic tools that track payments and diaries but not the documentary work a title claim needs.

## What Already Exists
Core claims platforms for P&C carriers (Guidewire ClaimCenter, Duck Creek and similar) handle intake, reserving, statutory diaries, vendor and counsel management, and payments.

## The Customization Gap
Title needs: the policy, Schedule B exceptions and endorsements as structured objects a coverage analysis can reference; parcel-level retrieval of recorded documents and the underwriting file; cure as a first-class resolution path with its own tasks (release requests, corrective deeds, quiet-title actions); policy-year rather than accident-year reserving; links to the agent and the underwriting approval behind the policy; and state-specific claims-handling deadlines.

## Target Customer
Title underwriters replacing legacy claim systems, especially regional underwriters without in-house development.

## Impact If Solved
A claims platform that matches how title claims are actually resolved, without a bespoke build.
