# Cure, Defense & Panel Counsel Management

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/title-insurance-carriers|Title Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to resolve covered title claims by cure, negotiation or defence for less than the policy loss, and whoever closes the most claims by cure at the lowest legal spend per claim takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$250M of panel-counsel fees, cure costs and in-house litigation time a year (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1.4% of premium (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — matters are managed by email and invoices; strategy lives in counsel's memos
**Target Buyer:** Chief Claims Counsel, litigation manager
**Automation Potential:** Medium for counsel selection, budget tracking and cure correspondence; low for litigation strategy

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Once coverage is accepted, the underwriter usually has a choice no other insurer has to the same degree: fix the title instead of paying for the defect. A release obtained from a servicer, a quiet-title judgement, a buyout of an adverse claimant or a corrective deed can cost a fraction of the policy loss. When the insured is sued, the underwriter defends at its own expense. The winners in this contest are the in-house counsel and panel firms who know which servicers, courts and opposing lawyers settle, and who keep legal spend in proportion to the exposure. The contest runs over months and is won on negotiation, venue knowledge and cost discipline, not on intake speed.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Panel counsel networks by state, legal bill review (see [[niches/small-law-firms/legal-bill-review-spend-management/profile|🔍 Legal Bill Review & Spend Management]]), matter management systems and recorded-document retrieval. Gaps: no data on which panel firms cure which claim types fastest and cheapest, budgets set by habit, and cure correspondence with servicers written from scratch each time.
