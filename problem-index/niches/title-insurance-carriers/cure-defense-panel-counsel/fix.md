# The Release Request Rewritten for Every Servicer

**Niche:** [[niches/title-insurance-carriers/cure-defense-panel-counsel/profile|Cure, Defense & Panel Counsel Management]]
**Industry:** [[industries/title-insurance-carriers|Title Insurance Carriers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The cheapest title cure, getting a lender to release a mortgage that was paid, is chased by letters drafted from scratch to servicers whose release departments each want something different.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #automation #quick-win #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to resolve covered title claims by cure, negotiation or defence for less than the policy loss, and whoever closes the most claims by cure at the lowest legal spend per claim takes the account.

## The Problem
Many title claims start as a mortgage paid at a closing and never released. Curing them means proving the payoff and getting the current servicer, often several transfers later, to record a release. Claims staff draft each demand by hand, trace servicing transfers and follow up for months.

## Why It's Still Broken
Each servicer has different requirements and addresses, servicing transfers obscure who holds the loan, and the task is too small per claim to systematise.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A servicer directory built from past cures (what each one requires, where, and how long it takes), automatic tracing of servicing transfers, generated demand packages with payoff evidence, and escalation to a statutory release-penalty demand where the state allows.

## Who Feels the Pain
Claims staff working release cures, insureds waiting to refinance or sell, and counsel who see cheap claims turn into lawsuits.

## Impact If Fixed
More of the cheapest cures closed in weeks, before they become quiet-title actions.
