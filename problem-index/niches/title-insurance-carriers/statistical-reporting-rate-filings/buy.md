# P&C Statistical Reporting Pipelines Re-coded for Title

**Niche:** [[niches/title-insurance-carriers/statistical-reporting-rate-filings/profile|Title Statistical Reporting & Rate Filings]]
**Industry:** [[industries/title-insurance-carriers|Title Insurance Carriers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** P&C carriers report to statistical agents through mature edit-and-submit pipelines; title needs the same discipline applied to data that mostly lives with agents.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn thousands of agents' production records into statistical-plan submissions and rate-filing exhibits that trace to source, and whoever's numbers the regulator and the opposing actuaries cannot impeach at the hearing takes the account.

## The Problem
Title statistical reporting is assembled per cycle without the edit packages, validation rules and submission tooling P&C carriers take for granted.

## What Already Exists
P&C statistical reporting pipelines with standard edits and reconciliations, and rate-filing consultancies, logged as [[niches/property-casualty-insurers/rate-form-filing-consultancies/profile|🔍 Rate, Rule & Form Filing Consultancies]].

## The Customization Gap
Title needs: transaction-level counts by rate bracket and policy type in place of exposure units; separation of escrow and closing fees from premium; agent income and expense schedules, which matter in Texas; policy-year loss development; and data sourced from agents' systems rather than the carrier's own policy administration.

## Target Customer
Underwriters and associations in promulgated and bureau states.

## Impact If Solved
Statistical submissions that pass edits the first time and reconcile to the statutory statement.
