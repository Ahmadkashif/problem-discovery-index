# Agent Expense Data Collected by Questionnaire

**Niche:** [[niches/title-insurance-carriers/statistical-reporting-rate-filings/profile|Title Statistical Reporting & Rate Filings]]
**Industry:** [[industries/title-insurance-carriers|Title Insurance Carriers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** In a state where agents keep most of the premium, the expense data that justifies the rate is gathered by survey from small offices that code their costs any way they like.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #large-language-models #data-integration #quick-win #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn thousands of agents' production records into statistical-plan submissions and rate-filing exhibits that trace to source, and whoever's numbers the regulator and the opposing actuaries cannot impeach at the hearing takes the account.

## The Problem
Rate proceedings need to know what agents spend to produce a policy. That data comes from agents' financial reports and questionnaires, completed by office managers who classify salaries, rent and software differently. Opposing parties question it at every hearing.

## Why It's Still Broken
Agents have no common chart of accounts, and the questionnaire is a once-a-cycle burden nobody owns.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A standard chart of accounts for title agents, mapping from the common small-business accounting packages, automated extraction with confirmation by the agent's accountant, and validation against the agent's remitted premium and transaction counts.

## Who Feels the Pain
Agents filling in forms, statistical staff cleaning them, and association actuaries defending the numbers at the hearing.

## Impact If Fixed
Expense data that is cheaper to collect and harder to challenge.
