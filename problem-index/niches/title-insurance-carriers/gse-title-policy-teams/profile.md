# GSE Title Policy & Title Acceptance Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/title-insurance-carriers|Title Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Fannie Mae's and Freddie Mac's title requirements in their selling guides, their acceptance of attorney opinion letters since 2022, and Fannie Mae's title acceptance pilot. Under the pilot, an automated review decides whether a refinance under 80% LTV needs a policy, and Westcor (with X1 Analytics) joined Doma as a vendor in July 2025. FHFA oversees both enterprises. Their larger analytics functions are logged as [[niches/real-estate-appraisers/gse-collateral-policy-analytics/profile|🔍 Secondary Market Collateral Policy & Analytics]] (48), [[niches/mortgage-brokers/gse-selling-guide-content/profile|🔍 Investor Guideline Content & Interpretation]] and [[niches/mortgage-lenders/agency-counterparty-oversight/profile|🔍 Agency Counterparty & Issuer Risk Oversight]] (39).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 staff on title policy at each enterprise (estimate); the automated title review is supplied by vendors
**Output:** Title requirements, pilot terms, eligibility rules
**Proprietary data:** Loan-level delivery data joined to title product type
**Clock:** Policy release cycles
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — chartered entities in conservatorship
