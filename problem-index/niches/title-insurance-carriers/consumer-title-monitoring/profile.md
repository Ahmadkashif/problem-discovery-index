# Consumer Title Monitoring Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/title-insurance-carriers|Title Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Subscription services that alert homeowners when a document is recorded against their property, marketed against deed fraud. Many county recorders offer the same alert free. They do not prevent a forged sale; ALTA's August 2025 post-policy forgery endorsements address the same fear through insurance.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 analytical staff per firm (estimate); marketing-led businesses
**Output:** Recording alerts
**Proprietary data:** Licensed or scraped recorder data
**Clock:** None
**Buyer:** Founder or head of marketing
