# Default & REO Title Clearing Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/title-insurance-carriers|Title Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Firms that run title searches and curative work for mortgage servicers on foreclosures and bank-owned properties. They clear defects before resale and assemble claims on lender's policies when a prior lien or defect surfaces after foreclosure.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Production searchers and curative staff; under 10 analytical (estimate)
**Output:** Foreclosure title reports, curative, claim packages
**Proprietary data:** Searches and curative files produced for servicer clients
**Clock:** Foreclosure and REO marketing timelines
**Buyer:** Servicer default operations head
