# Title Claims Studies & Rate-Hearing Actuaries

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/title-insurance-carriers|Title Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Consulting actuaries who run industry claims studies and present rate indications. Milliman's analysis for ALTA covered over 127,000 claims on 2013–2022 policies, and a later study nearly 162,000 claims. In each Texas rate hearing, TDI staff, the Texas Land Title Association and the Office of Public Insurance Counsel present competing actuarial calculations. The wider actuarial pockets are [[niches/property-casualty-insurers/rate-form-filing-consultancies/profile|🔍 Rate, Rule & Form Filing Consultancies]] (42) and [[niches/property-casualty-insurers/pc-reserve-opinion-actuaries/profile|🔍 P&C Reserve Opinion & Reserve Review Actuaries]] (48), which also sign title reserve opinions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 title-specialist actuaries per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Claims studies, rate indications, hearing testimony
**Proprietary data:** None of their own; claims and statistical data are contributed by underwriters and agents
**Clock:** Rate hearing schedules and association study calendars
**Buyer:** Association executive or underwriter chief actuary
