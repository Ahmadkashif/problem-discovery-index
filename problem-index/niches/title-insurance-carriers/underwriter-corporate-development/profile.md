# Underwriter Corporate Development & Direct-Operation Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/title-insurance-carriers|Title Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The national families are also the largest owners of title agencies. Their corporate development and direct-operations finance teams buy agencies, integrate them and measure each office's orders, closings, margin and capture of real estate agents' business. First American reported $2.35B of direct premiums and escrow fees in 2025 against $2.96B of agent premiums. Agency rollups by private equity, brokerages and lenders are logged separately under Title & Settlement Offices as [[niches/title-and-settlement-offices/title-agency-rollups/profile|🔍 Title Agency Rollups & Affiliated-Business Platforms]] (26).

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10–30 corporate development, integration and direct-operations analytics staff per national family (estimate)
**Output:** Acquisition models, office-level P&L and capture reporting, all internal
**Proprietary data:** Order, closing and margin history by office across the family's direct operations
**Clock:** Deal timetables and internal planning cycles
**Buyer:** No external buyer — CFO or head of corporate development
