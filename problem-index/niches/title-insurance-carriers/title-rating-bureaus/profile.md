# Title Rating Bureaus & Statistical Agents

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/title-insurance-carriers|Title Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Licensed rating organisations that file title rate manuals and forms on behalf of their member underwriters. TIRSA is licensed by New York's Department of Financial Services under Article 23 of the Insurance Law, and its manual (seventh revision, effective 1 October 2024) binds members unless the Superintendent approves a deviation. TIRBOP is licensed in Pennsylvania under Section 741 of the Insurance Company Law of 1921. Members fund them. In Texas the statistical and rate-setting role sits with the regulator, logged as [[niches/title-and-settlement-offices/state-title-regulators/profile|🔍 State Insurance Department Title Divisions]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 staff per bureau (estimate), with actuarial work contracted out
**Output:** Rate manuals, forms and filings for members
**Proprietary data:** Member statistical data, which belongs to the members
**Clock:** Filing and approval cycles
**Buyer:** Bureau executive director, answering to member underwriters
