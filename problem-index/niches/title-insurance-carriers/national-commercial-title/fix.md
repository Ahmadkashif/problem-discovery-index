# Co-Insurance Placed by Reply-All

**Niche:** [[niches/title-insurance-carriers/national-commercial-title/profile|National Commercial & High-Liability Title]]
**Industry:** [[industries/title-insurance-carriers|Title Insurance Carriers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** On a large deal the lead underwriter lines up co-insurers and facultative reinsurers by email thread, and nobody has a live view of who has committed what share on which terms.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #large-language-models #quick-win #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to commit large single-risk title capacity on a multi-state commercial deal, directly or through co-insurance and facultative reinsurance, on pro forma terms lender's counsel accepts before signing, and whoever commits the most capacity soonest while keeping the exceptions it needs takes the account.

## The Problem
When a liability exceeds what the lead underwriter wants to retain, it asks other underwriters to co-insure or reinsure a share. Commitments, terms and changes to the pro forma travel by email. When the pro forma changes late, every participant must re-approve, and a share that silently drops out can leave the lead carrying more than intended at signing.

## Why It's Still Broken
Few deals per underwriter need it, the counterparties are competitors, and nobody has built a shared placement record for a market this small.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A placement tracker per deal: liability, retention, each participant's share and conditions, the pro forma version each participant approved, and an alert when a change invalidates a commitment. Commitment emails are parsed into the tracker automatically.

## Who Feels the Pain
Commercial underwriters on deal nights, reinsurance and finance staff reconciling shares afterwards, and claims counsel who later need to know who owns what part of a loss.

## Impact If Fixed
No deal signs with an unintended retention, and the post-closing reconciliation of shares disappears.
