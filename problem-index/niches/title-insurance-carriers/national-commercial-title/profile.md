# National Commercial & High-Liability Title

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/title-insurance-carriers|Title Insurance Carriers]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to commit large single-risk title capacity on a multi-state commercial deal, directly or through co-insurance and facultative reinsurance, on pro forma terms lender's counsel accepts before signing, and whoever commits the most capacity soonest while keeping the exceptions it needs takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$500M a year of commercial underwriting staff, survey and zoning review, endorsement negotiation and reinsurance cost (estimate), spent on a ~$5.3B non-residential book (derived from NAIC: non-residential policies are 28.4% of $18.6B DPW, 1.1M policies averaging $4,918)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of premium in spend (estimate); the book itself is ~28% of direct premium (derived)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — pro formas, surveys and redlines are digital documents negotiated by email; capacity and reinsurance are placed by phone
**Target Buyer:** President of National Commercial Services, commercial chief underwriter
**Automation Potential:** Medium for exception and endorsement drafting, low for capacity and risk decisions

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Commercial title is few policies and large liabilities. A 1.1M-policy book carried more than a quarter of 2025 direct premium, and the average non-residential premium rose 22.1% in the year (NAIC); Fitch credited commercial activity for the industry's improved results. A portfolio refinance or a data-centre acquisition may insure hundreds of millions of dollars across dozens of parcels in several states. No single underwriter wants all of that on one balance sheet, so the risk is split through co-insurance and facultative reinsurance. Lender's counsel negotiate every exception, survey reading and endorsement into a pro forma policy before signing. The agent-side half of this work, reconciling exceptions to the survey, is the Title & Settlement Offices niche [[niches/title-and-settlement-offices/commercial-title-escrow/profile|🔵 Commercial Title & Escrow]]. Here the contest is the underwriter's: how much capacity to commit, on what terms, and how fast.

## Current Tools & Gaps
National commercial divisions of the four families, commercial production systems, survey review against [[niches/land-surveyors/alta-nsps-surveys/profile|🔵 ALTA/NSPS Surveys]], zoning reports and endorsement libraries. The gaps: no model of which exceptions and endorsements actually produce claims, co-insurance and reinsurance placed by email among a few underwriters, and portfolio deals priced from precedent and memory.
