# Contract Negotiation AI Retrained on Pro Forma Policies

**Niche:** [[niches/title-insurance-carriers/national-commercial-title/profile|National Commercial & High-Liability Title]]
**Industry:** [[industries/title-insurance-carriers|Title Insurance Carriers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Contract-review AI already redlines commercial agreements against a playbook; title underwriters need it to redline pro forma policies against an underwriting playbook and a survey.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #bert #evaluation-metrics #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to commit large single-risk title capacity on a multi-state commercial deal, directly or through co-insurance and facultative reinsurance, on pro forma terms lender's counsel accepts before signing, and whoever commits the most capacity soonest while keeping the exceptions it needs takes the account.

## The Problem
A portfolio deal generates dozens of pro formas and several rounds of redlines from lender's counsel, each reviewed by hand for exceptions deleted, endorsements added and wording changed. Round turnaround decides whether the deal stays on schedule.

## What Already Exists
Contract lifecycle and AI contract review tools (Ironclad, Luminance, Harvey and similar) compare drafts to a playbook, flag deviations and propose fallback language.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: the ALTA policy and endorsement forms as the base text, with state variants; an underwriting playbook expressed as rules (which survey exceptions may be deleted given which survey findings, which endorsements need a zoning report); cross-checking each requested deletion against the survey and zoning report actually in the file; tracking cumulative liability and co-insurance shares across a portfolio's pro formas; and a record of what was conceded, by whom and why.

## Target Customer
National commercial underwriting teams and the commercial agents who prepare pro formas with them.

## Impact If Solved
Redline rounds turned in hours rather than days, with every concession checked against the evidence in the file.
