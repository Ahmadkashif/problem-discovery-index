# Community Needs Assessment Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/nonprofits-social-services|Social Services Nonprofits]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Research firms conduct the community health and needs assessments hospitals, health departments, and funders are required or expected to produce, mapping unmet need across a service area.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-70 researchers, epidemiologists, and community engagement staff
**Output:** Needs assessments, service gap analysis, community survey research, implementation planning
**Proprietary data:** Assembled community indicator data and survey series across regions
**Clock:** Statutory triennial assessment cycles for tax-exempt hospitals
**Buyer:** Practice leader
