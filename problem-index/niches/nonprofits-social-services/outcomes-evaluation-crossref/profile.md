# Nonprofit Programme Evaluators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/nonprofits-social-services|Social Services Nonprofits]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Evaluation firms design and run the outcome studies funders require, producing the evidence a social service organization uses to report on and renew its funding.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-80 evaluators, methodologists, and analysts
**Output:** Evaluation designs, outcome studies, funder reporting, implementation research
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated evaluation methodology; the programme data belongs to the nonprofit and its funder
**Clock:** Grant reporting deadlines and renewal cycles
**Buyer:** Principal / Managing Director
