# Nonprofit Audit & Single Audit Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/nonprofits-social-services|Social Services Nonprofits]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Accounting firms audit nonprofit financial statements and perform the federal single audits organizations spending above a threshold must obtain, with findings that restrict future funding.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-500 auditors and compliance specialists in the larger practices
**Output:** Financial statement audits, single audit reports and findings, corrective action recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated finding patterns across grantees; the filed reports are public
**Clock:** Statutory submission deadlines with funding consequences
**Buyer:** Practice leader
