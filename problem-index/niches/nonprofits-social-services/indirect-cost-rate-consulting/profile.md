# Indirect Cost Rate & Grant Compliance Consulting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/nonprofits-social-services|Social Services Nonprofits]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Specialist consultants negotiate the federal indirect cost rates nonprofits recover overhead under, and build the cost allocation plans that determine whether an organization is funded for the infrastructure it actually needs.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-70 cost accountants, grant compliance specialists, and analysts
**Output:** Indirect cost rate proposals and negotiations, cost allocation plans, single audit preparation, subrecipient monitoring design
**Proprietary data:** Negotiated rate outcomes and agency positions across many organizations
**Clock:** Rate agreement cycles and grant application deadlines
**Buyer:** Practice leader
