## Why These Niches

Homelessness CoC and workforce development programs represent the largest federal funding streams in social services, commanding the most organizational capacity and compliance burden. Food banks and rural CAAs remain digitally neglected — food banks coordinate thousands of donation sources and distribution points using spreadsheets and phone trees, while rural CAAs serve multi-county areas with minimal IT infrastructure. Refugee resettlement, reentry services, and foster youth aging out are underserved populations where existing case management tools were designed for mainstream social services and miss critical workflow differences. Benefits eligibility screening is highly automatable because it involves matching client circumstances against complex but well-defined federal and state eligibility rules. Excluded: large national nonprofits (United Way, Salvation Army) with enterprise IT budgets, and advocacy-only organizations without direct service delivery.

## Why These Pockets

**No pocket qualified**, and the pattern matches faith organizations from an earlier batch: an industry whose value chain runs on donative and government funding, where almost nobody above the operator layer has a commercial buyer.

The one position with real analytical scale is a health plan. Medicaid managed care organizations administering home and community-based services contract with these nonprofits, authorize the services they deliver, and hold authorization, delivery, and outcome records across whole member populations — which is the outcome data the entire sector is asked for and cannot produce. It scores 50 and is protected health information under state contracts that own it.

The sector's own analytical functions all fail on the buyer. Community funders, foundation effectiveness researchers, and continuum-of-care data administrators hold genuinely valuable material — including, in the last case, the only shared longitudinal client record the social service sector has — and every one is grant or donation funded with no commercial procurement above it.

Two near misses are worth keeping. Indirect cost rate consultants negotiate the overhead recovery that determines whether an organization is funded for the infrastructure it actually needs, and hold negotiated rate precedent across the sector that they deliver one engagement at a time. And single audit firms file findings that are a public corpus — every federal grantee's compliance failures, year after year — which nobody analyses as a body.

Two pockets are logged as cross-references rather than double-entered: nonprofit sector data providers, indexed under faith organizations at 49, and programme evaluators, under grant writers at 40.
