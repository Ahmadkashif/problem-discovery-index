# Foundation Effectiveness Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/nonprofits-social-services|Social Services Nonprofits]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Research centres study what makes grantmaking effective — overhead norms, capacity building, general operating support — and publish the evidence that shapes how funders treat the organizations this industry is made of.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 researchers and analysts
**Output:** Grantmaking practice research, grantee perception surveys, field studies, funder guidance
**Proprietary data:** Grantee perception survey series across hundreds of foundations
**Clock:** Annual publication cycles
**Buyer:** Research director — philanthropically funded
