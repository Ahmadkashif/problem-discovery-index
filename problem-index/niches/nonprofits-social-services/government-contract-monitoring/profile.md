# Government Contract Monitoring

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/nonprofits-social-services|Social Services Nonprofits]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State and county agencies contracting social services monitor provider performance, audit expenditures, and decide which nonprofits keep their contracts — the funding relationship most of this sector depends on.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-250 contract managers, programme monitors, and auditors per agency
**Output:** Contract performance monitoring, fiscal audits, corrective action, procurement decisions
**Proprietary data:** Provider performance and expenditure records across the contracted network
**Clock:** Contract cycles and statutory audit deadlines
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — government agency
