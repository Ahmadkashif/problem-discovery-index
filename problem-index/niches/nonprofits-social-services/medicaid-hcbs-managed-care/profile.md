# Medicaid Home & Community-Based Services Managed Care

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/nonprofits-social-services|Social Services Nonprofits]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Managed care organizations administering long-term services and supports contract with the social service nonprofits that deliver them, authorize service levels, and report outcomes to the state — which makes them the entity determining most of the sector's funded activity.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,500 utilization analysts, care managers, actuaries, and quality staff
**Output:** Service authorization, network management and rate setting, quality and outcome reporting, utilization analysis
**Proprietary data:** Service authorization, delivery, and outcome records across whole populations of members receiving home and community services
**Clock:** State contract reporting and authorization turnaround requirements
**Buyer:** Chief Analytics Officer
