# Independent Nonprofit Consultants

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/nonprofits-social-services|Social Services Nonprofits]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Individual consultants advise social service organizations on strategy, board development, programme design, and funding — often former executive directors carrying deep sector knowledge for organizations too small to hire it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1-4 consultants, usually one
**Output:** Strategic plans, board and governance work, programme design, funding strategy
**Proprietary data:** A career of observed organizational outcomes across the sector, undocumented
**Clock:** Board and planning cycles
**Buyer:** The consultant
