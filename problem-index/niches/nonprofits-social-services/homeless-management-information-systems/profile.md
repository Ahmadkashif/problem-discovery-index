# Homeless Management Information Systems

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/nonprofits-social-services|Social Services Nonprofits]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Continuums of care operate the federally mandated data systems recording every shelter stay, housing placement, and service contact in a region — the closest thing the social service sector has to a shared longitudinal record.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 system administrators, data analysts, and evaluators per continuum
**Output:** Federal reporting, system performance measures, coordinated entry prioritization, point-in-time counts
**Proprietary data:** Client-level service histories across every participating provider in a region, longitudinally
**Clock:** Federal reporting deadlines and annual count requirements
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — federal grant funded
