# Case Management Platform Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/nonprofits-social-services|Social Services Nonprofits]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The case management platforms run intake, service tracking, and outcome recording for social service agencies, though Pass 1 records that the vast majority of the sector still runs on spreadsheets and paper intake forms.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 product and data staff
**Output:** Intake and case workflow, service and outcome tracking, funder reporting exports, programme dashboards
**Proprietary data:** Client service histories and recorded outcomes across many agencies and programme types
**Clock:** Funder reporting deadlines
**Buyer:** Chief Product Officer
