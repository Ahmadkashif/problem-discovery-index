# Community Funder Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/nonprofits-social-services|Social Services Nonprofits]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Community funding intermediaries assess local need, evaluate applicant organizations, and allocate pooled donations across a region's social service providers — deciding which programmes are funded.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-80 community impact staff, analysts, and evaluators
**Output:** Needs assessments, grantee evaluation and selection, outcome reporting to donors, community indicator research
**Proprietary data:** Applicant and grantee performance across a region over decades
**Clock:** Annual campaign and allocation cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Community Impact — donative funding
