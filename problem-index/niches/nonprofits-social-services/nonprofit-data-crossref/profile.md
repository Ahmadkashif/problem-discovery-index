# Nonprofit Sector Data Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/nonprofits-social-services|Social Services Nonprofits]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Sector data organizations assemble the financial and programme records of every registered US nonprofit from annual filings, and sell the resulting picture to funders, researchers, and nonprofits benchmarking themselves.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-400 data analysts, researchers, and taxonomists
**Output:** Organizational profiles and financial data, sector benchmarking, funder and grantmaking analysis, classification taxonomies
**Proprietary data:** Structured extraction over the entire filing corpus, with a maintained classification taxonomy
**Clock:** Annual filing cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer — partly philanthropically funded
