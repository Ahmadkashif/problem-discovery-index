# Outcomes-Based Financing Intermediaries

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/nonprofits-social-services|Social Services Nonprofits]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Outcomes-based financing intermediaries structure the pay-for-success contracts in which investors fund a social programme and government repays only if measured outcomes are achieved, and they design the measurement that decides whether payment happens.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-70 structurers, evaluators, and analysts
**Output:** Contract structuring, outcome metric design, independent evaluation coordination, performance reporting
**Proprietary data:** Outcome definitions and realized performance across a small number of structured transactions
**Clock:** Contract measurement periods and payment triggers
**Buyer:** Managing Director — partly philanthropically funded
