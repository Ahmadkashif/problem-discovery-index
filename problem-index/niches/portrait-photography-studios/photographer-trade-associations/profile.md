# Photographer Trade Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/portrait-photography-studios|Portrait Photography Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Professional Photographers of America and specialty groups for school, sports and event photographers run certification, print competitions, education, copyright advocacy and insurance benefits. PPA's financial benchmark survey, run since 2005 data and last completed in 2024, compares home and retail studios (entry required $25,000 in 2023 sales with at least half from portrait or wedding clients) and sets benchmarks such as a 25% cost of sales.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Fewer than 10 staff on benchmark research (estimate)
**Output:** Benchmark reports and member education
**Proprietary data:** Anonymous member financial submissions
**Clock:** Periodic survey cycles the association sets
**Buyer:** Association executive director
