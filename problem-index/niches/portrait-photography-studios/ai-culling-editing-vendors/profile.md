# AI Culling & Editing Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/portrait-photography-studios|Portrait Photography Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Aftershoot, Imagen, Narrative and Evoto sell software that culls, edits and retouches photographers' images, priced by subscription (Aftershoot $10–45 a month) or per photo (Imagen $0.05). Their machine-learning teams train selection and editing models and build per-photographer profiles from each customer's own catalogue. They sell a tool, not an analysis.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–100 machine-learning and data staff per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Culling, editing and retouching models; per-photographer style profiles
**Proprietary data:** Selections and edit histories that customers upload to train their own profiles
**Clock:** None external; release cycles self-set, demand peaks in wedding season
**Buyer:** Head of machine learning or product

**Kill switches:** none live — using a customer's catalogue to train that customer's own profile is the service itself, not reuse, though pooling across customers would raise the same objection as the gallery platforms.
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. Real ML teams, but their output is software sold per seat, and model improvement does not grow with analyst headcount.
