# Online Proofing, Gallery Sales & Print Fulfilment

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/portrait-photography-studios|Portrait Photography Studios]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a delivered session gallery into print, album and wall-art orders before the client's attention fades, without the photographer chasing, and whoever converts the most galleries into lab orders takes the studio's subscription.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$500M in US gallery-platform subscriptions and commissions plus the print and album margin they carry for session studios (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~6% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High — galleries, stores and lab ordering are online
**Target Buyer:** Owner-photographer; gallery platform product lead
**Automation Potential:** Very high

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
After a wedding, family or newborn session, the delivered gallery is the photographer's store. Many clients download the files and never order a print unless something prompts them, so print and album revenue depends on timed reminders, favourites lists, expiry dates, product previews on the client's own photos, and automatic routing to a pro lab. Pricing models differ sharply: Pixieset's free plan takes 15% of store sales while paid plans take none, ShootProof charges no commission, and Pic-Time is described by reviewers as strongest for automated print-sales campaigns. This niche covers session galleries; picture-day ordering for schools and leagues is part of the volume niches, and pro labs are a separate industry (photo labs, queued for a later batch).

## Current Tools & Gaps
Pixieset, ShootProof, Pic-Time and CloudSpot host galleries and stores with lab integrations; in-person-sales studios project wall-art layouts with ProSelect or Fundy. Gaps: sequences tuned by each studio's outcomes rather than defaults; room-scale previews for wall art; a view of which clients and products actually convert.
