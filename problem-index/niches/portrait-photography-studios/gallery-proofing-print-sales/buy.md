# Ecommerce Email Flows Adapted to Client Galleries

**Niche:** [[niches/portrait-photography-studios/gallery-proofing-print-sales/profile|Online Proofing, Gallery Sales & Print Fulfilment]]
**Industry:** [[industries/portrait-photography-studios|Portrait Photography Studios]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Online stores recover abandoned carts with behaviour-triggered email and text; most photographers send a gallery link and one reminder.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #logistic-regression #revenue-impact #automation #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a delivered session gallery into print, album and wall-art orders before the client's attention fades, without the photographer chasing, and whoever converts the most galleries into lab orders takes the studio's subscription.

## The Problem
Clients favourite images, put prints in a cart and leave; nothing follows unless the photographer remembers.

## What Already Exists
Ecommerce marketing platforms such as Klaviyo run behaviour-triggered flows (browse abandonment, cart abandonment, win-back) with segmentation and testing.

## The Customization Gap
Galleries need flows triggered by gallery events (favourites, downloads, shares to family, expiry approaching), messages featuring the client's own images, family members invited as additional buyers, holiday and anniversary reminders tied to the session date, and handoff to the lab order with the photographer's markup intact.

## Target Customer
Session photographers already using a gallery platform and an email tool separately.

## Impact If Solved
Abandoned carts recovered and family members turned into buyers, without manual follow-up.
