# The Gallery That Expires Unsold

**Niche:** [[niches/portrait-photography-studios/gallery-proofing-print-sales/profile|Online Proofing, Gallery Sales & Print Fulfilment]]
**Industry:** [[industries/portrait-photography-studios|Portrait Photography Studios]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Galleries are set to expire to create urgency, and many expire with nothing ordered and no one noticing.
**Tags:** #survival-analysis #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a delivered session gallery into print, album and wall-art orders before the client's attention fades, without the photographer chasing, and whoever converts the most galleries into lab orders takes the studio's subscription.

## The Problem
The expiry date is meant to prompt a decision; in practice clients download the files, forget the store, and the gallery closes. The photographer finds out months later when the client asks for the images again.

## Why It's Still Broken
Expiry is a single setting chosen when the gallery is created, and platforms report orders, not galleries that closed without one.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A weekly list of galleries approaching expiry with no order, ranked by engagement, with a prepared last-chance message featuring the client's favourites and an optional extension, sent with one tap.

## Who Feels the Pain
Session photographers who depend on print sales; clients who later regret not ordering.

## Impact If Fixed
Revenue recovered from galleries that would otherwise close silently, for minutes of work each week.
