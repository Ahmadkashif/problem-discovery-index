# Edtech Rostering Standards Applied to Picture Day

**Niche:** [[niches/portrait-photography-studios/k8-picture-day-contracts/profile|K-8 Picture Day Contracts]]
**Industry:** [[industries/portrait-photography-studios|Portrait Photography Studios]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Classroom software gets clean rosters from schools automatically; the picture-day photographer still gets a spreadsheet emailed by the office.
**Tags:** #data-integration #compliance #automation #feature-engineering #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver everything a K-8 school asks of picture day — every child photographed, ID cards within days, yearbook and SIS files, free retakes — right the first time and under a privacy agreement the district trusts, and whoever asks least of the school office keeps the contract.

## The Problem
Roster errors are the root of most picture-day errors: missing new students, legal names where the yearbook wants preferred names, homeroom codes that change after the export. The office sends whatever its student information system produces, and the studio fixes it.

## What Already Exists
Edtech rostering services such as Clever and ClassLink, and the OneRoster standard, sync student and class lists from the SIS to approved applications under the district's data-sharing controls.

## The Customization Gap
Picture day needs a round trip, not one-way sync: roster in before the shoot, images and ID data back into the SIS after; snapshots tied to picture day and retake day; preferred-name and opt-out fields mapped to yearbook and ID outputs; staff records; and data deleted on the schedule the district's privacy agreement sets. Photo vendors are rarely set up as rostering partners.

## Target Customer
Volume studios and their ordering-platform vendors; district technology directors who approve vendor data access.

## Impact If Solved
The office stops exporting spreadsheets, the studio starts from a correct roster, and the district manages the photographer like any other approved vendor.
