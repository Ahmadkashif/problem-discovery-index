# AI Editing Profiles Adapted to Multi-Shooter Studios

**Niche:** [[niches/portrait-photography-studios/culling-editing-retouching/profile|Culling, Editing & Retouching]]
**Industry:** [[industries/portrait-photography-studios|Portrait Photography Studios]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** AI editing profiles learn one photographer's look; an associate studio sells one look shot by six photographers on different cameras.
**Tags:** #cnns #transfer-learning #evaluation-metrics #automation #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to reproduce a photographer's own culling and editing choices on a 2,000–5,000-frame wedding closely enough that the photographer stops checking every frame, and whoever earns that trust takes the subscription.

## The Problem
Associate studios book more weddings than the owner can shoot and send associates and second shooters, each with their own cameras and habits. Galleries must look like the studio's brand regardless of who shot them, and lead and second shooter frames must match within one gallery.

## What Already Exists
Aftershoot and Imagen build personal AI editing profiles from a photographer's past edits; Lightroom presets and profiles standardise starting points.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: a studio-level profile trained on the owner's approved galleries and applied across associates; per-camera colour normalisation before the style is applied; matching of lead and second shooter frames within a gallery; and a review queue for the studio's post-production lead rather than each associate.

## Target Customer
Associate and multi-shooter wedding studios.

## Impact If Solved
One consistent look across every shooter, with the owner reviewing exceptions instead of re-editing associates' work.
