# Re-Checking Every Frame the AI Already Picked

**Niche:** [[niches/portrait-photography-studios/culling-editing-retouching/profile|Culling, Editing & Retouching]]
**Industry:** [[industries/portrait-photography-studios|Portrait Photography Studios]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The culling tool picks a few hundred keepers from thousands of frames in minutes, and the photographer spends hours checking all of them anyway.
**Tags:** #cnns #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to reproduce a photographer's own culling and editing choices on a 2,000–5,000-frame wedding closely enough that the photographer stops checking every frame, and whoever earns that trust takes the subscription.

## The Problem
One missed shot — the ring exchange, a grandparent's only portrait — costs more than all the hours saved, so photographers re-check rejects as well as picks.

## Why It's Still Broken
The tools present a verdict without telling the photographer where they are likely to be wrong, and they rarely guarantee coverage of the day's key moments.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A coverage check against the wedding timeline (processional, vows, rings, kiss, family formals, first dance, toasts) that confirms at least one strong frame of each moment and each family grouping, plus a short list of the rejects most likely to be wanted.

## Who Feels the Pain
Wedding photographers in peak season; couples waiting for galleries.

## Impact If Fixed
The photographer stops re-reviewing rejects, and the fear of a missed moment no longer cancels out the time saved.
