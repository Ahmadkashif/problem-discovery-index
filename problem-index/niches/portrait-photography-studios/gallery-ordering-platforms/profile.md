# Gallery, Proofing & Volume Ordering Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/portrait-photography-studios|Portrait Photography Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Pixieset, ShootProof, Pic-Time and CloudSpot host session photographers' client galleries and print stores; GotPhoto, PhotoDay, ImageQuix and ProofPix run picture-day prepay and proof shops for volume studios. They see galleries, views, favourites and orders across many thousands of studios. Their product is software and fulfilment; any research they publish is marketing for the subscription or commission.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–40 data and analytics staff at the largest platforms (estimate)
**Output:** Product analytics, occasional benchmark posts and sales-tips content
**Proprietary data:** Gallery engagement and order behaviour across many studios and millions of clients
**Clock:** Self-set product cycles; the studios' seasons
**Buyer:** VP of product or data
