# Education Purchasing Cooperatives

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/portrait-photography-studios|Portrait Photography Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Regional education service agencies and purchasing cooperatives vet vendors and award preferred-vendor status that member districts can use without running their own bid. In February 2026 the Kentucky Educational Development Corporation suspended Lifetouch as its preferred school photography vendor over social-media claims about its owner, then reinstated it a week later after review. They screen contracts, privacy terms and pricing; they do not research the photography market as a product.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~1–5 procurement staff touching photography contracts (estimate)
**Output:** Preferred-vendor awards and contract templates
**Proprietary data:** Member districts' contracts and vendor responses
**Clock:** Bid and renewal cycles they set with members
**Buyer:** Cooperative executive director
