# School Volume Platforms Adapted to Under-Fives

**Niche:** [[niches/portrait-photography-studios/preschool-childcare-photography/profile|Preschool & Childcare Centre Photography]]
**Industry:** [[industries/portrait-photography-studios|Portrait Photography Studios]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Volume platforms assume a child who sits, a barcode card and a homeroom; an infant room has none of the three.
**Tags:** #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #revenue-impact #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to photograph infants, toddlers and preschoolers in a centre with no office staff and no student information system, and whoever makes picture day cost the centre director nothing — no roster cleanup, no flyers, no package distribution — keeps the centre.

## The Problem
Preschool jobs are forced into school software: classrooms entered as homerooms, ages that change mid-year, siblings in different rooms, part-time children who attend only on some days, and sessions that need longer slots and more frames per child.

## What Already Exists
GotPhoto, PhotoDay and similar platforms run barcode sorting, prepay and proof shops, and PSPA exports built around school jobs.

## The Customization Gap
Under-five jobs need: attendance-day scheduling for part-time children; longer per-child slots and higher frame counts; sibling and family-group sessions; centre-level parent communication rules; no yearbook export but a classroom composite; and gentler retake policies for children who were asleep or upset.

## Target Customer
Preschool-specialist studios running roughly 50–500 centres a year (estimate).

## Impact If Solved
Fewer missed children, more sellable frames per child, and a platform that fits the segment instead of fighting it.
