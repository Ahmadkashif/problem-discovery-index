# Photo & Imaging Market Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/portrait-photography-studios|Portrait Photography Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Small research firms and practices that size the photo capture, printing and photo-merchandise markets for vendors, labs and retailers. Rise Above Research, founded in 2020 by Ed Lee and David Haueter, sells photo printing and photo merchandise advisory services and a capture forecast priced at $2,499; Keypoint Intelligence covers photo printing within its wider print research. Professional portrait studios are a minor segment of what they cover.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~2–8 analysts on photo topics across the field (estimate)
**Output:** Market forecasts, consumer surveys, advisory subscriptions
**Proprietary data:** Survey panels and forecast models
**Clock:** Annual forecast cycles and trade-show calendars
**Buyer:** Strategy or marketing heads at labs, printer makers and retailers
