# The Sign-Up Sheet in the Narthex

**Niche:** [[niches/portrait-photography-studios/church-directory-photography/profile|Church Directory Photography]]
**Industry:** [[industries/portrait-photography-studios|Portrait Photography Studios]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Sitting slots are booked on a clipboard after services, and the volunteer who keeps it is also the one calling households who forgot.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #logistic-regression #workflow-orchestration #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to fill a two-to-three-week sitting calendar with enough member households to make a free directory pay through portrait sales, and whoever does it without the sales pressure that gets them uninvited next cycle keeps the congregation.

## The Problem
Older members prefer the clipboard, younger ones never see it, and the coordinator reconciles paper with the photographer's schedule by phone. Double bookings, illegible names and no reminders produce empty slots.

## Why It's Still Broken
The volunteer has no budget and no mandate to change the process; the directory company leaves sign-up to the church to keep its own costs down.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Keep the clipboard as one channel among several: a text-to-book number and a link in the bulletin feeding the same calendar, automatic reminders by text or phone call, and a one-page printed list for the coordinator each Sunday showing open slots.

## Who Feels the Pain
Directory coordinators; photographers facing empty slots; members who never got a reminder.

## Impact If Fixed
A volunteer's month of phone calls replaced by reminders that send themselves, and fewer wasted sitting hours.
