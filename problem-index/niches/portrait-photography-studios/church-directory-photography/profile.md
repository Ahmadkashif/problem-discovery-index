# Church Directory Photography

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/portrait-photography-studios|Portrait Photography Studios]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to fill a two-to-three-week sitting calendar with enough member households to make a free directory pay through portrait sales, and whoever does it without the sales pressure that gets them uninvited next cycle keeps the congregation.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$150M in US directory-program portrait sales (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — sign-up sheets after services, phone reminders by volunteers, printed directory books, portraits sold in person at the sitting
**Target Buyer:** Church administrator or volunteer directory coordinator; the directory company's regional manager
**Automation Potential:** Medium — scheduling, reminders and directory layout automate; the sitting and the sale are in person

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The church directory is a decades-old business model built on a free product. The directory company photographs member households over a few weeks in the church hall, gives each family a complimentary 8×10 and the church a directory per family plus spare copies at no cost, and earns its money by selling portraits to families at the end of the sitting. Universal Church Directories describes exactly that (with a 20% overrun of directories for newcomers and "no pressure" to buy), and Lifetouch has long run a church directory line. The model only works if enough households sit, and it ends the moment the congregation feels sold to. Churches are covered under [[industries/faith-organizations|Faith Organizations]].

## Current Tools & Gaps
Directory companies run their own scheduling and layout systems; churches keep member records in church management software. Gaps: sign-ups still taken on paper; no-shows leave sitting slots empty; member updates after printing are lost until the next directory; the sale at the sitting is unmeasured beyond revenue.
