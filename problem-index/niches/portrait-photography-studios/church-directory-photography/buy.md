# Church Management Software Adapted to Directory Programs

**Niche:** [[niches/portrait-photography-studios/church-directory-photography/profile|Church Directory Photography]]
**Industry:** [[industries/portrait-photography-studios|Portrait Photography Studios]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Churches already keep member households, photos and contact details in church management software; the directory company retypes them every few years.
**Tags:** #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #quick-win #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to fill a two-to-three-week sitting calendar with enough member households to make a free directory pay through portrait sales, and whoever does it without the sales pressure that gets them uninvited next cycle keeps the congregation.

## The Problem
Member records, household groupings and contact preferences live in the church's database; the directory company collects them again on paper at the sitting, and the new portraits rarely flow back.

## What Already Exists
Church management platforms such as Planning Center, Realm and Breeze keep member directories, households, groups and communication tools, often with an online member directory.

## The Customization Gap
A directory program needs: household export with the church's consent and members' privacy choices (which entries are unlisted); sitting sign-up inside the church's own app; portraits written back to member profiles after the session; and print-layout export for the directory book.

## Target Customer
Directory companies wanting a lighter-touch program; church administrators on a management platform.

## Impact If Solved
No retyping, a directory that matches the church's records, and portraits that stay useful between editions.
