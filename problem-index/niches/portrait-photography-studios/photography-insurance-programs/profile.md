# Photographer & Wedding Insurance Programs

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/portrait-photography-studios|Portrait Photography Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Specialty insurance programs cover photographers' equipment, general liability and professional errors (a missed or lost wedding is the classic claim), often sold through associations or online; separate wedding-event policies cover couples against vendor no-shows. Underwriting is done by program managers and carriers pricing small, standard policies from rating tables.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Fewer than 10 underwriting and claims analysts dedicated to photographers at any one program (estimate)
**Output:** Policies and claims decisions
**Proprietary data:** Claims histories for photographer and wedding-vendor policies
**Clock:** Policy renewal cycles
**Buyer:** Program manager
