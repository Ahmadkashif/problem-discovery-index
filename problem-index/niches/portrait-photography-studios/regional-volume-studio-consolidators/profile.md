# Regional Volume Studios & Consolidators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/portrait-photography-studios|Portrait Photography Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The family-owned and specialist volume photographers below Lifetouch, which grow by buying school accounts and smaller studios: Inter-State Studio (which calls itself North America's largest family-owned school photography and yearbook company), Strawbridge Studios (founded 1923), and CADY, which took over Lifetouch high school and Prestige senior accounts in select regions in a deal announced in August 2025. Corporate development is the owners and a finance lead evaluating account books.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~1–5 people on acquisitions and account analytics (estimate)
**Output:** Internal deal and account evaluations
**Proprietary data:** Their own school sales histories
**Clock:** Deal timing and the school season
**Buyer:** Owner or CFO
