# Session CRMs Adapted to a Senior Class

**Niche:** [[niches/portrait-photography-studios/high-school-senior-portraits/profile|High School Senior Portraits]]
**Industry:** [[industries/portrait-photography-studios|Portrait Photography Studios]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Photographer CRMs run one client's inquiry, contract and gallery beautifully; a senior program needs the same thing for 400 clients against one deadline.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #automation #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to book seniors' portrait sessions ahead of the independent photographers who market to them directly, before the yearbook's fall deadline, and whoever books the most seniors while delivering yearbook-compliant images on time keeps the high school.

## The Problem
Senior sessions are sold like portrait sessions — consult, wardrobe guide, session, reveal, package — but at class scale, with the school's list, a shared deadline and a yearbook file at the end.

## What Already Exists
HoneyBook, Dubsado and Studio Ninja manage inquiries, questionnaires, contracts, invoices and workflows for individual clients.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: import of a school's senior list as pre-qualified leads; one workflow per class with the yearbook deadline as its anchor; parent-and-student dual contacts (the senior books, the parent pays); a yearbook-pose selection step and export in the publisher's format; and reporting by school for the contract review.

## Target Customer
Senior specialists and contract studios running senior programs at roughly 20–200 high schools (estimate).

## Impact If Solved
The studio sells senior sessions with the polish independents use, at class scale, without a second system.
