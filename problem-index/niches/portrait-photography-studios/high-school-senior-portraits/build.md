# Deadline-Driven Senior Booking

**Niche:** [[niches/portrait-photography-studios/high-school-senior-portraits/profile|High School Senior Portraits]]
**Industry:** [[industries/portrait-photography-studios|Portrait Photography Studios]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** The contract studio knows every senior's name and the exact yearbook deadline, and still books sessions as if each family found it by chance.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #survival-analysis #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to book seniors' portrait sessions ahead of the independent photographers who market to them directly, before the yearbook's fall deadline, and whoever books the most seniors while delivering yearbook-compliant images on time keeps the high school.

## The Problem
Seniors book through the summer and early fall; independents court them on Instagram with styled sessions and ambassador programs. The contract studio mails a brochure and opens a booking page, then discovers in November how many seniors went elsewhere or did nothing, and scrambles for make-up sessions before the deadline.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Volume platforms are built for picture day, not for appointment funnels; session CRMs are built for one client at a time, not for a class list with a hard deadline. Senior programs are a seasonal side line at most volume studios.

## What to Build
A class-level funnel: the senior list from the school; session invitations timed by expected booking date; a model of which seniors are likely to book elsewhere or not at all, based on past classes, and when to offer a short yearbook-only session; live status for the yearbook adviser showing booked, shot, submitted from outside, and missing. Every outside submission flows into the same record.

## Target Customer
Senior program managers at contract studios and senior specialists; yearbook advisers as co-users.

## Impact If Built
More seniors booked with the studio, fewer missing yearbook portraits at the deadline, and a visible service the school weighs at renewal.
