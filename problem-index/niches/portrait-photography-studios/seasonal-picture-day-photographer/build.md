# A Real-Time Station Coach

**Niche:** [[niches/portrait-photography-studios/seasonal-picture-day-photographer/profile|The Seasonal Picture-Day Photographer]]
**Industry:** [[industries/portrait-photography-studios|Portrait Photography Studios]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A first-week photographer sees every frame on the laptop and still can't tell, at 25 seconds a child, whether it is a keeper.
**Tags:** #cnns #object-detection #transfer-learning #evaluation-metrics #tacit-knowledge-ml #worker-facing #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a photographer hired with no experience producing sellable, correctly named portraits at 45–60 children an hour within days of hire, and whoever ramps new hires fastest staffs the fall season.

## The Problem
New hires miss blinks, half-closed eyes, gaze drifting off the lens, tilted heads, crops that will fail the yearbook guide, and forced smiles. Each one becomes a retake or a lost sale weeks later.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Tethering tools are built for capture and file handling; quality checks happen in post-production at the lab or studio, where they are cheap per image but far too late for the child who has gone back to class.

## What to Build
A model running on the station laptop that scores each frame within a second (eyes, gaze, expression, head angle, crop against the yearbook spec) and shows a green, amber or red cue plus a one-word reason. A short daily review compares the photographer's keeper rate and typical faults with the studio's veterans on similar ages, and suggests one habit to change tomorrow. Set-up checks verify lights and backdrop against the studio's reference before the first class arrives.

## Target Customer
Training and operations directors at volume studios hiring tens to hundreds of seasonal photographers each fall (estimate).

## Impact If Built
Seasonal hires reach a veteran's keeper rate in days, retakes fall, and the studio can hire from a wider pool with confidence.
