# Feedback That Arrives Three Weeks Late

**Niche:** [[niches/portrait-photography-studios/seasonal-picture-day-photographer/profile|The Seasonal Picture-Day Photographer]]
**Industry:** [[industries/portrait-photography-studios|Portrait Photography Studios]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A photographer learns that a whole morning's frames were soft or mis-cropped only when the retake list comes back, after they have repeated the mistake at ten more schools.
**Tags:** #cnns #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get a photographer hired with no experience producing sellable, correctly named portraits at 45–60 children an hour within days of hire, and whoever ramps new hires fastest staffs the fall season.

## The Problem
Editors and lab staff see the faults first, days after upload, and report them in aggregate to a supervisor; the photographer hears about it if at all when retakes are scheduled.

## Why It's Still Broken
Post-production quality control is organised for the product, not the person: it fixes images and moves on, and nobody routes the pattern back to the photographer the same evening.

## What a Fix Looks Like
An end-of-day summary generated at upload: keeper rate, the top two faults with example frames, and a comparison with the photographer's previous days, sent before the next morning's school.

## Who Feels the Pain
Seasonal photographers; supervisors managing retake volume; schools hosting extra retake days.

## Impact If Fixed
Mistakes corrected after one school rather than ten, using data the studio already has.
