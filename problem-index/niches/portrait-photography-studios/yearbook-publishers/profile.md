# Yearbook Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/portrait-photography-studios|Portrait Photography Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Jostens, Herff Jones, Walsworth and smaller online yearbook platforms print school yearbooks and receive the portrait studio's PSPA package — the INDEX.TXT roster file and images — to flow portraits onto class pages. Some volume studios publish yearbooks themselves. Publishers sell books to schools and families; their analytics serve sales and production.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Fewer than 10 people on market or customer research relevant to portraits (estimate); large production and sales staffs are not an insight function
**Output:** Yearbooks; internal sales analytics
**Proprietary data:** School accounts, page templates, sales by school
**Clock:** Yearbook production deadlines, set with the school
**Buyer:** Head of school sales or product
