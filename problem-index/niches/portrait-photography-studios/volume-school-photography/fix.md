# The Selling Model Chosen by Habit

**Niche:** [[niches/portrait-photography-studios/volume-school-photography/profile|Volume School Photography]]
**Industry:** [[industries/portrait-photography-studios|Portrait Photography Studios]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Whether a school is sold prepay, proof-first or both is decided by what the studio did last year, though one photographer's comparison put the participation gap at 25 points.
**Tags:** #causal-inference #gradient-boosting #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact #quick-win
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor is fighting to keep the school's photography contract, but what keeps it differs by grade band: in K-8 the contract goes to whoever asks least of the school office while delivering every required output, and in high school it goes to whoever books the seniors before independent photographers and the yearbook deadline do. Different buyers, products and winners, as the 2025 transfer of Lifetouch high school accounts to CADY showed. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Prepay asks families to buy blind before picture day and tends to bring broad participation with smaller orders; proof-first shows the photos and tends to bring larger orders from fewer families, plus more processing and retakes. One photographer reported 88% participation on prepay against 63% at a similar school sold proofs-only. Studios argue about it on forums and pick by temperament.

## Why It's Still Broken
Selling models were never assigned at random, so a studio's own history confounds model with school: affluent schools went proof-first, so proof-first looks better. Nobody runs the comparison properly, and the ordering platform reports totals, not counterfactuals.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A per-school recommendation built from the studio's own history matched on enrolment, demographics and grade mix, with a few deliberate switches each season to keep the estimate honest, reported as expected revenue per enrolled child after commission and production cost.

## Who Feels the Pain
Studio owners deciding the season plan; schools whose families find blind prepay frustrating or proof-first emails relentless.

## Impact If Fixed
Choosing the right model per school is a pricing decision with no capture cost, worth more per enrolled child than most operational savings (estimate).
