# Student Data Privacy Oversight

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/portrait-photography-studios|Portrait Photography Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The rules and offices that govern who may hold children's school photographs: FERPA, which lists a student's photograph as directory information; state student-privacy laws that require vendor contract terms (a Lifetouch addendum under Connecticut's law covers deletion on request, limited use and breach notice within 30 days); district technology offices that sign data privacy agreements; and biometric law such as Illinois's BIPA, under which Shutterfly paid $6.75M over face grouping. Enforcement and review are spread across federal, state and district offices.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~1–10 staff per state or large district on vendor privacy review (estimate)
**Output:** Guidance, contract terms, approvals and enforcement actions
**Proprietary data:** Vendor agreements and complaints
**Clock:** Contract renewals and statutory deadlines
**Buyer:** None as a buyer; district chief technology or privacy officers as reviewers
