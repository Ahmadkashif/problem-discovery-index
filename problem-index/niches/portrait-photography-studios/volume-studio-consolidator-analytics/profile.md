# National Volume Studio Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/portrait-photography-studios|Portrait Photography Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The pricing, sales and data teams inside the national volume photographer: Lifetouch, owned by Shutterfly (itself bought by Apollo-managed funds in 2019 for about $2.7B), which photographed more than 25 million children across more than 50,000 schools at its 2018 sale and reported $963.9M revenue in fiscal 2017. These teams set package ladders, choose selling models, forecast orders and production, and report school-level performance to account managers. Their work serves the company's own portrait sales; nobody buys it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20–80 pricing, analytics and data staff on the school business (estimate)
**Output:** Internal pricing, forecasting and account analytics
**Proprietary data:** Capture, proof and order records for tens of millions of sittings a year, the largest such archive in the industry
**Clock:** School picture-day calendars, yearbook deadlines and the selling season; set by schools, which are the clients
**Buyer:** Chief commercial or data officer

**Kill switches:** data isn't theirs to use — student images and records are held under district contracts and student-privacy agreements that limit use to the photography service, and the company has publicly said it does not use student images to train AI or facial recognition.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. The richest capture-to-purchase archive in the industry, held under restrictions that keep it out of any research product, inside a company of one.
