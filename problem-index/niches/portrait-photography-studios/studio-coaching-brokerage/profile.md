# Studio Business Coaches & Brokers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/portrait-photography-studios|Portrait Photography Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Photography business coaches, education platforms and the occasional studio broker sell pricing, sales and marketing programmes to portrait and wedding photographers, and help owners value and sell school accounts or studios. Most are former photographers selling courses, workshops and memberships.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 1–10 per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Courses, coaching, pricing templates, occasional valuations
**Proprietary data:** Student and client results, mostly anecdotal
**Clock:** Self-set launch calendars
**Buyer:** The photographer
