# Camera & Lighting Makers' Market Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/portrait-photography-studios|Portrait Photography Studios]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Camera, lens and studio-lighting manufacturers run market research and competitive intelligence on professional photographers, including portrait, wedding and volume studios, to plan products, pro-support programmes and dealer channels. The research is internal and serves product and marketing decisions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–50 market research and product-planning staff per major manufacturer, of which only part covers professional portrait work (estimate)
**Output:** Internal segment sizing, product requirements, pro-user surveys
**Proprietary data:** Registration, service and pro-programme data; dealer sell-through
**Clock:** Product launch cycles the manufacturer sets
**Buyer:** Head of product planning or market intelligence
