# Identity Resolution & Skip Trace Data Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/collections-agencies|Collections Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
LexisNexis Risk Solutions, TransUnion's TLOxp, and the bureau investigative products link billions of records — public filings, utility and telecom data, property, court, and contributed sources — into an identity graph that answers where a person now is and how to reach them. Collection agencies buy searches against it because a debtor who cannot be located is worthless, and the search result is priced individually.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-1,500 data scientists, linkage engineers, source acquisition staff, and analysts
**Output:** Identity and contactability search results, risk and verification products
**Proprietary data:** A linked identity graph assembled over decades from thousands of sources under supply agreements that cannot be replicated, joined to the outcomes of billions of resolution attempts
**Clock:** Searches are run on demand; no third-party deadline governs the work
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Data Products
