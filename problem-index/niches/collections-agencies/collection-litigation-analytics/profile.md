# Consumer Finance Litigation Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/collections-agencies|Collections Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Firms tracking consumer finance litigation — FDCPA, TCPA, and FCRA filings — by plaintiff firm, defendant, venue, and claim type, and selling the analysis to collection agencies, creditors, and their insurers. Agencies use it to gauge exposure by state and by practice, and defence counsel use it to price and posture cases.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-60 analysts, researchers, and legal data staff
**Output:** Litigation trend reporting, plaintiff firm profiling, exposure analyses
**Proprietary data:** Structured filing and outcome data across consumer finance dockets, assembled from court records that are public but not usably aggregated anywhere
**Clock:** Filing waves and regulatory effective dates
**Buyer:** Head of Research / Managing Editor
