# Receivables Compliance Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/collections-agencies|Collections Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Specialist publishers and law firm knowledge operations that interpret Regulation F, state collection statutes, and telephone consumer protection rules into operational guidance — model scripts, disclosure timing, validation notice content — that agencies build their processes on. A misinterpretation propagates into every call an agency makes.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 regulatory editors, attorneys, and analysts
**Output:** Interpretive guidance, model scripts and notices, regulatory change alerts
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated interpretation joined to enforcement and litigation outcomes across the client base
**Clock:** Regulatory effective dates and enforcement actions
**Buyer:** Editorial Director / Practice Leader
