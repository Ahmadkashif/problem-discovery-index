# Debt Sale Exchange Brokers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/collections-agencies|Collections Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Brokers and exchanges that intermediate charged-off portfolio sales, matching sellers to qualified buyers, running the auction, and publishing pricing indications the market uses as a reference.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 3-12
**Output:** Auction management, pricing indications, buyer qualification
**Proprietary data:** Transacted portfolio pricing across the market — genuinely unique and held by very few people
**Clock:** Auction timelines
**Buyer:** Principal
