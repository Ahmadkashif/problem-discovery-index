# Debt Portfolio Valuation & Due Diligence Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/collections-agencies|Collections Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Specialist firms that value charged-off receivable portfolios for buyers, sellers, and lenders — scrubbing account files, testing documentation completeness, modelling expected liquidation, and issuing the valuation opinion a transaction closes against. The written valuation is the invoice and it is challenged by whichever side it disadvantages.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 analysts, modellers, and due diligence staff
**Output:** Portfolio valuations, due diligence findings, documentation quality assessments
**Proprietary data:** Liquidation performance across portfolios previously valued, joined to the characteristics observed at diligence
**Clock:** Transaction closing timelines and lender diligence windows
**Buyer:** Managing Director / Head of Valuation
