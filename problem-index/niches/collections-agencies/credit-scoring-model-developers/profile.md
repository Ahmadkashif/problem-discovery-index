# Credit Scoring Model Developers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/collections-agencies|Collections Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
FICO, VantageScore, and the bureaus' analytics divisions develop and maintain the scores that determine who gets credit, on what terms, and how a delinquent account is treated once it deteriorates. Modellers build, validate, and monitor scorecards against enormous performance datasets, and the score is licensed per decision.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 modellers, statisticians, validation staff, and analytics researchers
**Output:** Scores, scorecard documentation, model validation and monitoring reports
**Proprietary data:** Development samples spanning tens of millions of consumers with multi-year performance outcomes, plus decades of accumulated model performance history
**Clock:** Model validation cycles, regulatory examination timelines, and annual monitoring requirements
**Buyer:** Chief Analytics Officer / VP of Scores
