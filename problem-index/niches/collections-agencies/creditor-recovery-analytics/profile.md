# Creditor Recovery Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/collections-agencies|Collections Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The recovery analytics groups inside banks, card issuers, and lenders decide which delinquent accounts to work internally, which to place with agencies, which to sell, and how to allocate placements across a panel of agencies whose performance they benchmark continuously.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 analysts and modellers
**Output:** Placement strategy, agency performance scorecards, sale versus retain analysis
**Proprietary data:** Recovery outcomes by account characteristic and treatment path, joined to agency-level performance across the panel
**Clock:** Monthly placement cycles and quarterly reporting
**Buyer:** VP of Recovery / Head of Collections Strategy
