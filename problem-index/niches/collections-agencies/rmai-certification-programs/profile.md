# Receivables Industry Certification Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/collections-agencies|Collections Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Receivables Management Association's certification programme sets the standards debt buyers and agencies are audited against — documentation, chain of title, consumer complaint handling — and conducts the audits that grant certification. Creditors increasingly require it before placing or selling paper.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-30 standards staff, auditors, and programme administrators
**Output:** Certification standards, audit findings, certification decisions
**Proprietary data:** Audit findings across certified buyers and agencies
**Clock:** Certification renewal cycles
**Buyer:** Executive Director / Director of Certification
