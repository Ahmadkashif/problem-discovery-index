# Federal Consumer Finance Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/collections-agencies|Collections Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The federal consumer financial regulator's research office analyzes complaint data, credit panel data, and supervisory findings to produce the studies that drive rulemaking and enforcement priorities across the collections industry.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-150 economists, researchers, and data staff
**Output:** Research reports, market monitoring, supervisory highlights
**Proprietary data:** The national consumer complaint database and a longitudinal consumer credit panel, plus supervisory examination findings unavailable anywhere else
**Clock:** Rulemaking and congressional reporting schedules
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer
