# Debt Buyer Portfolio Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/collections-agencies|Collections Agencies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The analytics organizations at Encore Capital, PRA Group, and the large debt buyers price charged-off portfolios, forecast liquidation curves, and optimize treatment strategy across tens of millions of accounts. They buy paper on the strength of their own modelling and live with the consequences.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-400 modellers, analysts, and data engineers
**Output:** Portfolio pricing models, liquidation forecasts, treatment strategy analytics
**Proprietary data:** Payment behaviour on tens of millions of charged-off accounts across decades and economic cycles — the deepest record of consumer financial distress anywhere
**Clock:** Portfolio auction timelines and quarterly reporting
**Buyer:** Chief Analytics Officer / SVP Portfolio Management
