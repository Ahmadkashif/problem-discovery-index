# National Student Clearinghouse & Its Research Center

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/colleges-and-universities|Colleges & Universities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
The National Student Clearinghouse receives enrollment and degree records from most US colleges, verifies enrollment and degrees for lenders and employers, and sells StudentTracker reports that show institutions where their applicants and former students enrolled. Its Research Center publishes national enrollment, transfer, completion and "Some College, No Credential" reports free; the fall 2025 final report and the 2025 SCNC report are cited in this industry's hub and niches.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-60 researchers and analysts at the Research Center (estimate), within a larger operations organisation
**Output:** Free national research reports; paid StudentTracker and verification services
**Proprietary data:** Term-by-term enrollment and degree records covering the large majority of US postsecondary students, received from institutions as their agent
**Clock:** Self-set report calendar; institutions' own reporting cycles
**Buyer:** Executive director of the Research Center

**Kill switches:** live for the invoiced half — StudentTracker and verification run on FERPA education records held as institutions' agent, usable only for the disclosed purposes. The Research Center's published aggregate reports show aggregate research use is permitted, but those reports are free, so the half that is paid for carries the switch.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. A near-census of US enrollment, with free research and paid services resting on records it does not own. One organisation, so no market behind it.
