# Higher-Education Credit Rating Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/colleges-and-universities|Colleges & Universities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The higher-education and nonprofit groups at Moody's, S&P Global Ratings and Fitch rate the bonds colleges and universities issue for buildings and refinancing, publish annual medians and sector outlooks, and run surveillance on every rated institution. Issuers pay for the rating; investors and lenders use it. All three entered 2026 with negative or deteriorating sector outlooks.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-40 analysts per agency's higher-education group (estimate)
**Output:** Ratings and outlooks, annual median ratio reports, sector outlooks, surveillance and rating-action reports
**Proprietary data:** Decades of rated institutions' financial and enrollment statistics, rating histories and outcomes, plus confidential information issuers supply during the rating process
**Clock:** Issuers' bond pricing schedules (a client clock), annual surveillance and EMMA disclosure cycles
**Buyer:** Head of US higher education ratings at the agency

**Kill switches:** live for the confidential half — analysts receive issuer-confidential, non-public information (draft budgets, enrollment projections, management forecasts) supplied under each rating engagement, which cannot be reused outside the rating process. The half built on public audited financials, EMMA disclosures and the agencies' own published medians is clean. The confidential half carries the switch, because the rating rests on it.
