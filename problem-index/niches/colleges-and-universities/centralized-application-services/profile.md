# Centralized Application Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/colleges-and-universities|Colleges & Universities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Platforms through which applicants apply to many institutions with one application: the Common App for undergraduate admission and Liaison's centralized application services for graduate and health-professions programmes (named from recall). Member institutions pay fees; applicants pay application fees or receive waivers. Their research teams publish application trend reports from the platform's records.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-40 research and data staff per platform (estimate)
**Output:** Application trend reports and deadline updates, member dashboards, applicant-pool analytics sold to members
**Proprietary data:** Millions of applications a year with applicant characteristics, school lists and, where members report them, admission decisions
**Clock:** Member institutions' application deadlines; the platforms' own reports run on self-set calendars
**Buyer:** Head of research or data at the platform
