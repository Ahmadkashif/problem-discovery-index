# Federal Student Aid & NCES Data Operations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/colleges-and-universities|Colleges & Universities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Department of Education's Office of Federal Student Aid runs the FAFSA, the Student Aid Index calculation, verification selection, loan servicing contracts and programme reviews; the National Center for Education Statistics runs IPEDS and publishes College Scorecard data with FSA. Together they hold the federal record of every aided student and every Title IV institution. Contractors do much of the systems and analytical work.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Per agency: Federal Student Aid has hundreds of federal analysts and programme staff plus contractors; NCES has a few dozen federal statisticians after its 2025 staff cuts, with IPEDS run largely by contractors (estimate; staffing recalled, not re-read)
**Output:** ISIRs and aid eligibility, verification selection, programme review findings, IPEDS releases, College Scorecard, financial responsibility scores
**Proprietary data:** FAFSA applications with IRS data received by direct exchange, the National Student Loan Data System, IPEDS submissions from every Title IV institution
**Clock:** Statutory and regulatory dates: FAFSA availability, award-year rules, IPEDS collection deadlines
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer; federal appropriations and contracts

**Kill switches:** live — federal tax information and student loan records are held under statutory confidentiality (the Privacy Act, IRS disclosure rules for federal tax information, and the Higher Education Act's limits on NSLDS use). Government procurement alone would not be a switch; the statutes are.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. The most complete dataset in the sector, behind statute and with no commercial buyer. The public outputs (IPEDS, College Scorecard) are the base of the vault's public-data MVP ideas.
