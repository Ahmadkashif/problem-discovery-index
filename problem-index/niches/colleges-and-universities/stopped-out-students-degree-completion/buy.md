# Subscription Win-Back Models for Former Students

**Niche:** [[niches/colleges-and-universities/stopped-out-students-degree-completion/profile|Stopped-Out Students & Degree Completion]]
**Industry:** [[industries/colleges-and-universities|Colleges & Universities]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Subscription businesses already score lapsed customers for win-back offers; universities need the same, with a degree audit in place of a purchase history.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #logistic-regression #survival-analysis #evaluation-metrics #feature-engineering #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to bring former students back to finish a degree, and whoever returns the most stop-outs to a completed credential per outreach dollar takes the account.

## The Problem
Re-enrollment outreach treats all former students alike and measures replies, not completions.

## What Already Exists
Churn and win-back modelling in subscription media, telecoms and software (in-house models and marketing platforms such as Braze and Iterable), which score lapsed customers and test offers.

## The Customization Gap
The university version needs: credits remaining under current requirements as the dominant feature; outstanding balance and its effect on return; the reason for leaving where known (academic suspension, financial, personal); eligibility for aid now, including Pell lifetime limits; course availability in formats working adults can take; a target of completed credential within a set time, not first enrollment; and FERPA-compliant outreach from the institution itself.

## Target Customer
Enrollment marketing teams and adult-learner programmes at universities.

## Impact If Solved
The win-back budget is spent on the former students who will finish. Doubling the conversion rate of a re-enrollment campaign halves its cost per completion (estimate).
