# Marketing Mix Modelling Rebuilt for the Enrollment Funnel

**Niche:** [[niches/colleges-and-universities/enrollment-and-net-tuition-revenue/profile|Enrollment Management & Net Tuition Revenue]]
**Industry:** [[industries/colleges-and-universities|Colleges & Universities]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Consumer brands already model how each marketing dollar moves sales; colleges need the same for a funnel that runs eighteen months and ends in a priced, need-tested sale.
**Tags:** #bayesian-inference #linear-regression #time-series-forecasting #causal-inference #feature-engineering #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is "every serious competitor is fighting to raise net tuition revenue from the next entering class", but it holds two contests with different winners: filling the funnel with students likely to enroll, won on enrolled students per recruitment dollar, and pricing each admit, won on the discount rate at target class size. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Enrollment offices allocate millions across name purchases, travel, digital ads, events and aid with little evidence of what each moves. Last-touch attribution in the CRM credits whatever happened just before the application.

## What Already Exists
Marketing mix models (Bayesian media-mix tools such as Google's Meridian and Meta's Robyn) and multi-touch attribution are standard in consumer goods and retail.

## The Customization Gap
The enrollment version needs: an annual cycle with one conversion event per prospect and a long lag from name purchase to deposit; channels that include price (institutional aid) as well as media; geography and high-school-level effects; fixed capacity (a target class, not unlimited sales); demographic decline by state as a baseline trend (WICHE projections); and outcomes beyond deposit, because melt and first-year retention change the value of a recruited student. Data is a few thousand enrolled students a year, so pooling across years and priors matter more than in consumer settings.

## Target Customer
Enrollment VPs and their analytics directors at private and regional public universities.

## Impact If Solved
Recruitment budget moves from the channels that generate inquiries to the ones that generate enrolled, retained students. Even a 10% reallocation of a $3M recruitment budget is a material gain (estimate).
