# Higher-Education Municipal & Restructuring Advisors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/colleges-and-universities|Colleges & Universities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Registered municipal advisors and financial consultancies that advise colleges on debt capacity, bond issues, refinancings, covenant compliance and, increasingly, mergers, affiliations and orderly closures: PFM, Prager & Co., Blue Rose Capital Advisors, Baker Tilly and the higher-education practices of larger consultancies (named from recall). Colleges pay per transaction or engagement.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~5-15 advisors per firm's higher-education practice, each staffing transactions one at a time, with no separate research team of 10+ (estimate)
**Output:** Debt capacity and financial plans, bond transaction advice, covenant and ratio projections, merger and affiliation analyses
**Proprietary data:** Client financial models, transaction histories and deal files across many institutions
**Clock:** Bond pricing and closing dates set by the issuer, covenant test dates, board meetings
**Buyer:** Managing director of the higher-education practice
