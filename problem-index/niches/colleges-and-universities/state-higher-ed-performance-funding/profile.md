# State Higher-Education Agencies & Performance Funding Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/colleges-and-universities|Colleges & Universities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State higher-education coordinating and governing boards (state higher education executive officer agencies) allocate appropriations to public universities, many through performance-based funding formulas, approve new programmes, run statewide longitudinal data systems and publish accountability reports. State departments that fund K-12 districts and run accountability testing belong to Education Agencies, which is queued separately.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-60 research and data staff at larger state agencies, fewer at most (estimate)
**Output:** Funding formula calculations, programme approval analyses, statewide outcome and workforce reports
**Proprietary data:** Student unit records from every public institution in the state, often linked to state wage records
**Clock:** State budget and legislative calendars
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer; state appropriations
