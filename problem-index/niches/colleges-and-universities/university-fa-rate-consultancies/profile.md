# University F&A Rate & Research-Cost Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/colleges-and-universities|Colleges & Universities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Higher-education practices at Huron, Attain Partners, Baker Tilly, MGT and the large accounting firms prepare research universities' facilities and administrative (F&A) rate proposals, run space and functional-use surveys, build cost allocation models, and support negotiation with the cognizant federal agency (named from recall). In 2025–26 they also modelled the cost of the proposed NIH 15% cap and the Joint Associations Group's FAIR alternative for clients.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-50 cost analysts and research-finance consultants per firm's higher-education practice, excluding space-survey field staff (estimate)
**Output:** F&A rate proposals and negotiation support, space survey analysis, cost allocation studies, cap and policy impact models
**Proprietary data:** Space, cost-pool and negotiation records across many client universities, held under client confidentiality; negotiated rates themselves are often public
**Clock:** Rate proposals are due after the university's fiscal year end ahead of each rate agreement's expiry; this is the client's extendable filing (Q4 4 at most)
**Buyer:** Research finance practice leader
