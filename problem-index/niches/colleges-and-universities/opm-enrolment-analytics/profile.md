# Online Program Managers' Enrolment Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/colleges-and-universities|Colleges & Universities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Online program managers (2U, Academic Partnerships, Noodle and similar) market, recruit for and often support universities' online degree programmes, historically on tuition revenue shares and increasingly on fee-for-service contracts (named from recall). Their analytics teams model lead quality, enrollment conversion and retention across partner universities' programmes.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-80 marketing analysts and data scientists per larger OPM (estimate)
**Output:** Lead scoring, channel and cost-per-enrollment optimisation, programme launch forecasts, retention analytics for partners
**Proprietary data:** Prospect-to-enrollment-to-completion records across many partner programmes, held under partner contracts
**Clock:** Partners' term start dates
**Buyer:** Chief data officer at the OPM

**Kill switches:** live — enrolled students' records are FERPA records held for each partner university; the prospect half (pre-application leads) is governed by the OPM's own marketing terms, unread and therefore undetermined. The enrolled-student half carries the switch.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. The analytics serve recruitment revenue rather than being sold, and the outcome data belongs to partners. Course platforms selling directly to learners are a separate industry, [[industries/online-course-platforms|Online Course Platforms]].
