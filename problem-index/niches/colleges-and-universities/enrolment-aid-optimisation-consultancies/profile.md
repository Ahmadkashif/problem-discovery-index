# Enrolment & Financial Aid Optimisation Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/colleges-and-universities|Colleges & Universities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Firms that model each client college's admit pool and build its leveraging grid, yield forecasts and aid strategy for the coming cycle, usually on annual retainers: EAB's financial aid optimisation (descended from Royall & Company), Ruffalo Noel Levitz, Maguire Associates, Human Capital Research Corporation and smaller shops (named from recall except EAB). Enrollment VPs and CFOs pay for the model and the advice; the discount rate and the class are the result.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~15-150 analysts, data scientists and strategy consultants per firm, largest at EAB and Ruffalo Noel Levitz (estimate)
**Output:** Annual leveraging grids, yield and melt forecasts, net tuition revenue scenarios, aid policy recommendations, in-cycle tracking
**Proprietary data:** Multi-year applicant, admit, aid offer and deposit records across hundreds of client campuses, plus enrolled students' aid, retention and graduation records, all received from each college under contract
**Clock:** The client's award release and deposit calendar, anchored by the FAFSA launch; these are client and federal dates that do not bind the consultancy's deliverable directly
**Buyer:** Chief analytics officer or head of financial aid strategy at the consultancy

**Kill switches:** live for the enrolled-student half — aid, retention and graduation records of students who enrolled are FERPA education records disclosed under the school-official exception, usable "only for the purposes for which the disclosure was made" (34 CFR 99.33(a)(2)), and no checked terms granting product rights were found. The applicant and admit half (people who never enrolled are not FERPA students) rests on client contracts that were not read: undetermined. The FERPA half carries the switch, because grading a grid needs the enrolled outcomes.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. The insight is the invoice and the work repeats every cycle, but the data that would let a firm grade its own grids across clients is the clients'.
