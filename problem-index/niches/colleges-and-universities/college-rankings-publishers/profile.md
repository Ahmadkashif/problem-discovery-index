# College Rankings & Guide Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/colleges-and-universities|Colleges & Universities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
U.S. News & World Report's Best Colleges, Niche, The Princeton Review, the Wall Street Journal's rankings and Forbes rank institutions from survey data colleges submit, federal data and their own surveys of students or academics (named from recall). Revenue comes from advertising, lead generation and badge or data licensing sold back to colleges.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-80 data and editorial staff per larger publisher (estimate)
**Output:** Annual rankings and guides, institution profiles, badge licences, data products for colleges
**Proprietary data:** Institution-submitted survey responses over many years, peer-assessment and student surveys, methodology histories
**Clock:** Each publisher's own release date; submission deadlines bind the colleges, not the publisher
**Buyer:** Chief data strategist or head of rankings at the publisher
