# Compliance Evidence Software Pointed at Accreditation

**Niche:** [[niches/colleges-and-universities/accreditation-and-assessment/profile|Accreditation Self-Study & Learning-Outcomes Assessment]]
**Industry:** [[industries/colleges-and-universities|Colleges & Universities]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** SOC 2 and ISO audit tools already map controls to evidence and collect it continuously; accreditation is the same pattern with standards instead of controls.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to assemble the evidence for every accreditation standard from the institution's own planning and assessment records, and whoever brings an institution through its comprehensive review with the fewest required follow-up reports takes the account.

## The Problem
Colleges assemble accreditation evidence in bursts before each review, then let it decay.

## What Already Exists
Governance, risk and compliance platforms (Vanta, Drata, AuditBoard) map frameworks to controls, pull evidence automatically from systems and track gaps continuously for SOC 2, ISO 27001 and internal audit.

## The Customization Gap
The accreditation version needs: each accreditor's standards, criteria and core components as frameworks, plus programme accreditors (AACSB, ABET, CCNE); evidence that is mostly narrative documents rather than system logs; learning-outcomes assessment cycles by programme; crosswalks between institutional and programme accreditation so one document serves several; the federal requirements accreditors must check (credit hour, distance education, complaints); and output as a narrative with citations, not a control matrix.

## Target Customer
Institutional effectiveness offices that already license Watermark or Anthology and want continuous evidence rather than another template.

## Impact If Solved
Evidence collected as a by-product of governance, and one evidence library serving institutional and programme reviews. At an institution with ten programme accreditations, avoiding duplicate evidence work saves ~2–4 staff weeks a year (estimate).
