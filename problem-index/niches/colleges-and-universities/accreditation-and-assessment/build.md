# A Living Evidence Map for Every Standard

**Niche:** [[niches/colleges-and-universities/accreditation-and-assessment/profile|Accreditation Self-Study & Learning-Outcomes Assessment]]
**Industry:** [[industries/colleges-and-universities|Colleges & Universities]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Every college rebuilds its accreditation evidence from scratch each cycle, when the documents that prove each standard are created continuously in the ordinary course of governance.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #word-embeddings #k-nearest-neighbors #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to assemble the evidence for every accreditation standard from the institution's own planning and assessment records, and whoever brings an institution through its comprehensive review with the fewest required follow-up reports takes the account.

## The Problem
A self-study team spends most of 18–24 months finding documents (estimate). Which committee approved the general-education revision, where the programme review shows changes made after assessment results, whether the complaint policy was actually followed. Each accreditor phrases expectations differently, and reviewers check citations closely.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Assessment vendors sell structured templates, and the evidence that matters sits outside them in minutes, policies and email. The cycle is long, so the pain is episodic and budgets appear only when the visit is near.

## What to Build
An index over the institution's document estate (governance minutes, policies, catalogues, programme reviews, assessment reports, budget documents) that breaks each accreditor's standards into checkable claims and proposes dated evidence for each, with the approving body and a confidence score. It flags claims with policy but no evidence of practice and numbers that disagree with IPEDS, and keeps the map current between cycles as new documents arrive.

## Target Customer
Accreditation liaison officers and institutional effectiveness offices, especially at colleges with fewer than five people in IR and assessment.

## Impact If Built
The evidence file is ready years before the visit, and the gaps that cause monitoring reports are found while there is time to fix them. Saving a third of a self-study team's time is several thousand staff hours per cycle (estimate).
