# Accreditation Self-Study & Learning-Outcomes Assessment

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/colleges-and-universities|Colleges & Universities]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to assemble the evidence for every accreditation standard from the institution's own planning and assessment records, and whoever brings an institution through its comprehensive review with the fewest required follow-up reports takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M a year in planning, assessment and accreditation software and consulting (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.05% of institutional spending (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — evidence lives in Word, PDFs, SharePoint and committee minutes; assessment platforms are used unevenly by programmes
**Target Buyer:** Accreditation Liaison Officer, usually an associate provost for assessment or institutional effectiveness
**Automation Potential:** High for evidence retrieval and consistency checks; the narrative and judgement stay human

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Accreditation is the gate to federal student aid, and losing it ends an institution. Four-year colleges are reviewed by one of six institutional accreditors (HLC, MSCHE, SACSCOC, WSCUC, NECHE, NWCCU) on eight-to-ten-year cycles with interim reports, plus programme accreditors for business, engineering, nursing and others. The field is in motion: six southern public systems launched the Commission for Public Higher Education in 2025 and it ran its first site visit in August 2026 (search summary). The work is retrieval and argument. Findings usually follow from evidence nobody could find, not from missing practice.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Watermark (Planning & Self-Study, Outcomes Assessment), Anthology (Planning, Accreditation, Outcomes), Weave and HelioCampus store plans, assessment results and templates mapped to standards. Gaps: evidence not linked to specific claims; "closing the loop" on assessment results undocumented; narrative and IPEDS figures inconsistent; and the whole file rebuilt every cycle.
