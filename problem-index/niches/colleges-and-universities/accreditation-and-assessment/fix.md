# Assessment Results Nobody Acts On

**Niche:** [[niches/colleges-and-universities/accreditation-and-assessment/profile|Accreditation Self-Study & Learning-Outcomes Assessment]]
**Industry:** [[industries/colleges-and-universities|Colleges & Universities]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Programmes file learning-outcomes assessment reports every year, and when the accreditor asks what changed as a result, the answer has to be reconstructed.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #descriptive-statistics #compliance #workflow-orchestration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to assemble the evidence for every accreditation standard from the institution's own planning and assessment records, and whoever brings an institution through its comprehensive review with the fewest required follow-up reports takes the account.

## The Problem
Faculty coordinators submit annual reports: outcomes, measures, results. Accreditors expect evidence that results led to changes in curriculum or teaching ("closing the loop"). The change, if made, is recorded in department minutes or not at all, and the next report starts fresh.

## Why It's Still Broken
Assessment is a compliance chore for faculty, platforms collect results without prompting for follow-up, and the assessment director cannot chase hundreds of programmes.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Carry last year's results and the planned action into each new report, prompt the coordinator to record what was done with a link to the evidence, and give the assessment director a list of programmes with no recorded action, ranked by how long it has been.

## Who Feels the Pain
Assessment directors, faculty coordinators and accreditation liaison officers facing reviewers.

## Impact If Fixed
A visible chain from result to action for every programme. An assessment director overseeing ~100 programmes replaces a month of chasing emails before each review with a standing list (estimate), and one of the commonest reasons for an assessment follow-up report goes away.
