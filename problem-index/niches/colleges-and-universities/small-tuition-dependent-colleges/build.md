# A Back Office Sized for Twelve Hundred Students

**Niche:** [[niches/colleges-and-universities/small-tuition-dependent-colleges/profile|Small Tuition-Dependent Private Colleges]]
**Industry:** [[industries/colleges-and-universities|Colleges & Universities]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A 1,200-student college must file the same federal reports and run the same aid processes as a university of 30,000, with a registrar, an aid director and an IR person who each have no backup.
**Tags:** #ai-agent #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #automation #compliance #data-integration #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to run a small college's registrar, aid, business-office and reporting systems as a managed service, and whoever misses the fewest federal disbursement and reporting dates takes the account.

## The Problem
At a small college the IPEDS keyholder also prepares the accreditation data, the Common Data Set and the board's enrollment report. The aid director packages every file and handles verification. The registrar runs degree audits and transfer evaluations. When one of them leaves mid-year, deadlines slip, and a missed disbursement or late IPEDS submission becomes a compliance finding at an institution that cannot afford one.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Vendors build for the large-institution buyer and treat small colleges as a long tail, while outsourcers sell people, not systems. The compliance calendar is the same for everyone, but nobody has packaged it as software for a staff of three.

## What to Build
A compliance calendar and agent layer over the college's SIS that knows every federal, state and accreditor deadline that applies to it. It prepares IPEDS and other survey drafts from the census snapshot, runs aid verification and disbursement checklists, documents each process so a replacement can follow it, and alerts the president when a date is at risk.

## Target Customer
Presidents and CFOs of private colleges under 2,000 students, and the managed-services firms that serve them.

## Impact If Built
Fewer findings and no stalled processes when someone leaves. Covering one vacant administrative role for six months without a missed federal date is worth more than the role's salary to a college this size (estimate).
