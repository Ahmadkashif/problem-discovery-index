# Financial Aid Processing Outsourcers & Third-Party Servicers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/colleges-and-universities|Colleges & Universities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Firms that process verification, packaging, return of Title IV funds and loan certification for colleges that outsource part or all of their aid office, operating as third-party servicers under Department of Education rules. Small colleges and online programmes are the main clients.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Aid counsellors and processors working file by file, which is production; analysis and compliance staff are few per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Processed aid files, verification outcomes, disbursement and reconciliation, compliance support
**Proprietary data:** Client students' aid files
**Clock:** Federal disbursement and return-of-funds windows and clients' award dates
**Buyer:** Owner or operations director
