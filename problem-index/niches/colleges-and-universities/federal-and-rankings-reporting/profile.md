# Federal & Rankings Survey Reporting (IPEDS, Common Data Set, Rankings)

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/colleges-and-universities|Colleges & Universities]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce an institution's IPEDS, Common Data Set and rankings submissions from one reconciled data snapshot, and whoever produces them with the fewest post-submission revisions takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$200M a year in IR reporting tools, data warehouses sold for reporting, and reporting consultants at four-year institutions (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.03% of institutional spending (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — the data is in the SIS and HR systems; the surveys are filled from spreadsheet extracts
**Target Buyer:** Director of Institutional Research
**Automation Potential:** Very high — definitions are written down, the questions repeat every year and the inputs are structured

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every Title IV institution must report to IPEDS several times a year on enrollment, completions, graduation rates, finance, human resources, admissions and aid; 5,819 institutions were Title IV-eligible in 2023-24 (search summary). The same office answers the Common Data Set, U.S. News and other rankings surveys, state reporting, accreditor data requests and the board, each with slightly different definitions and dates. IPEDS feeds College Scorecard and federal accountability measures. Rankings answers are scrutinised: misreported data has cost institutions their rankings and drawn lawsuits.

## Current Tools & Gaps
HelioCampus, Precision Campus, Evisions Argos, Ellucian Insights and in-house warehouses on Tableau or Power BI. Gaps: no single census snapshot that every survey draws from; definitions reconciled by hand each year; and no record of why a number changed from last year.
