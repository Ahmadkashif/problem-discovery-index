# The Same Headcount Reported Three Ways

**Niche:** [[niches/colleges-and-universities/federal-and-rankings-reporting/profile|Federal & Rankings Survey Reporting (IPEDS, Common Data Set, Rankings)]]
**Industry:** [[industries/colleges-and-universities|Colleges & Universities]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Enrollment reported to IPEDS, to a rankings publisher and to the board differs, and the IR director spends a week explaining why.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #data-integration #compliance #quick-win #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce an institution's IPEDS, Common Data Set and rankings submissions from one reconciled data snapshot, and whoever produces them with the fewest post-submission revisions takes the account.

## The Problem
Fall enrollment depends on the census date, which students count (non-degree, dual enrollment, study abroad) and how headcount converts to full-time equivalents. Each report uses a slightly different rule, extracts are run on different days, and the numbers disagree. Trustees, accreditors and journalists notice.

## Why It's Still Broken
Definitions are applied in queries written by different people in different years, and there is no published crosswalk inside the institution.

## What a Fix Looks Like
One definitions register listing each external and internal enrollment figure, its rule, its snapshot and its value, with an automatic reconciliation showing exactly which students account for each difference.

## Who Feels the Pain
IR directors, provosts presenting to boards and communications staff answering media questions.

## Impact If Fixed
Every enrollment figure the institution publishes comes with its reconciliation. A week of explanation per discrepancy becomes an hour (estimate).
