# Bank Regulatory Reporting Tools Rebuilt for IPEDS

**Niche:** [[niches/colleges-and-universities/federal-and-rankings-reporting/profile|Federal & Rankings Survey Reporting (IPEDS, Common Data Set, Rankings)]]
**Industry:** [[industries/colleges-and-universities|Colleges & Universities]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Banks already file call reports from mapped general-ledger data with validation rules and audit trails; colleges file IPEDS from spreadsheets.
**Tags:** #data-integration #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation #descriptive-statistics
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce an institution's IPEDS, Common Data Set and rankings submissions from one reconciled data snapshot, and whoever produces them with the fewest post-submission revisions takes the account.

## The Problem
IPEDS and survey reporting is a regulated filing done without filing software.

## What Already Exists
Regulatory reporting platforms for banks (Workiva, Wolters Kluwer OneSumX, Moody's regulatory reporting) map source data to report schedules, apply validation rules, track changes and keep audit trails.

## The Customization Gap
The higher-education version needs: IPEDS survey components and their edit checks as the schedule library; Common Data Set and rankings surveys as additional schedules with crosswalks; student-level cohorts (graduation-rate cohorts, outcome measures) rather than account balances; FERPA-safe handling; SIS and HR system connectors for Banner, Colleague, PeopleSoft and Workday; and pricing for offices of two.

## Target Customer
IR directors and, at small colleges, the registrar or provost's office that holds the IPEDS keyholder role.

## Impact If Solved
Edit-check failures and resubmissions fall, and each year's filing starts from last year's mappings. Each revision or resubmission costs ~2–3 staff days (estimate), so a two-person office that faces four or five a year gets about two weeks back.
