# Institutional Accreditors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/colleges-and-universities|Colleges & Universities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The six institutional accreditors of most four-year colleges (HLC, MSCHE, SACSCOC, WSCUC, NECHE, NWCCU) and the new Commission for Public Higher Education review institutions against standards, run peer visits and decide accreditation actions; accreditation is required for federal student aid. Institutions pay dues and fees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Staff liaisons each carrying a portfolio of institutions plus volunteer peer reviewers; the liaisons and reviewers do per-institution review, which is production, and data and research staff number under 10 per accreditor (estimate)
**Output:** Accreditation actions, team reports, annual institutional data collections
**Proprietary data:** Self-studies, team reports and annual data from member institutions
**Clock:** Review cycles and commission meeting dates the accreditor sets
**Buyer:** President of the accreditor
