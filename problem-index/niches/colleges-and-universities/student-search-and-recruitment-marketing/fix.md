# Search Names Judged by Inquiry Rates

**Niche:** [[niches/colleges-and-universities/student-search-and-recruitment-marketing/profile|Student Search & Recruitment Marketing]]
**Industry:** [[industries/colleges-and-universities|Colleges & Universities]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A purchased name list is renewed because it produced inquiries, even when it produced no enrolled students.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #hypothesis-testing #data-integration #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn purchased and owned prospect names into enrolled first-year students, and whoever delivers the most enrolled students per recruitment dollar takes the account.

## The Problem
Each spring the admissions office decides which lists and filters to buy again. The agency's report shows response and inquiry rates by list. Enrollment by list two years later is not in the report, and nobody has time to trace it.

## Why It's Still Broken
The source code on each name is often lost when the prospect re-enters through another channel, and the SIS that records enrollment does not carry CRM source fields.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Keep the original source on every record through to enrollment. Run a standing report of enrolled and retained students per list and per filter, with confidence intervals for small lists, delivered before the purchase decision each year.

## Who Feels the Pain
Admissions directors defending budgets; enrollment VPs asked by the CFO what search buys.

## Impact If Fixed
Renewal decisions rest on enrolled students per dollar. Dropping lists with no enrolled students typically frees ~10–20% of the search budget (estimate).
