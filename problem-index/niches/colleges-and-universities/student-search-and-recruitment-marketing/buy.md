# B2B Lead Scoring Pointed at Prospect Pools

**Niche:** [[niches/colleges-and-universities/student-search-and-recruitment-marketing/profile|Student Search & Recruitment Marketing]]
**Industry:** [[industries/colleges-and-universities|Colleges & Universities]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Sales teams already score leads on fit and engagement; admissions offices need the same scoring tuned to a once-a-year, capacity-limited enrollment decision.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #logistic-regression #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn purchased and owned prospect names into enrolled first-year students, and whoever delivers the most enrolled students per recruitment dollar takes the account.

## The Problem
Counsellors cannot work every inquiry, so they prioritise by gut and by territory. Generic CRM engagement scores reward students who open emails, who are often not the ones who enroll.

## What Already Exists
B2B lead and account scoring (Salesforce Einstein, HubSpot predictive scoring, 6sense) built for sales pipelines.

## The Customization Gap
The admissions version needs: a target of enrollment and first-year retention rather than a closed deal; features from high school, geography, intended major, visit and application behaviour; awareness of the aid budget, because a high-likelihood student who needs full aid has a different value; fairness checks after the 2023 Supreme Court ruling on race-conscious admissions, so the score uses no protected attributes or close proxies; and a yearly retrain on an annual cycle with few positive cases.

## Target Customer
Admissions directors running Slate or Salesforce who already have three or more years of prospect history.

## Impact If Solved
Counsellor time goes to the prospects most likely to enroll and stay. A 2-point rise in yield on 500 worked prospects is ~10 more enrolled students per counsellor (estimate).
