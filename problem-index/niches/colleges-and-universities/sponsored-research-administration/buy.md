# Contract Obligation Management Rebuilt for Federal Awards

**Niche:** [[niches/colleges-and-universities/sponsored-research-administration/profile|Sponsored Research Administration & Cost Recovery]]
**Industry:** [[industries/colleges-and-universities|Colleges & Universities]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Corporate contract lifecycle tools already extract obligations and track them to deadlines; research offices need the same for award terms under the Uniform Guidance.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #compliance #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to recover the full allowable cost of each federal research award as agency rules, caps and terminations change, and whoever leaves the least allowable cost unrecovered takes the account.

## The Problem
Award obligations — reporting dates, prior-approval requirements, restricted cost categories, subaward flow-downs — sit in documents that departmental administrators rarely read in full.

## What Already Exists
Contract lifecycle management platforms (Icertis, Ironclad, DocuSign CLM) extract obligations from commercial contracts and track renewals and deliverables.

## The Customization Gap
The research version needs: agency-specific term libraries (NIH Grants Policy Statement, NSF's Proposal and Award Policies and Procedures Guide, DOD and DOE terms); the cost principles of 2 CFR 200 as the reference for allowability; the negotiated F&A rate agreement and its bases; links to ERP grants ledgers so obligations meet actual spending; subaward flow-down checks; and effort and cost-sharing commitments tracked per person. Generic CLM knows contract parties, not principal investigators, budget periods or modified total direct costs.

## Target Customer
Post-award directors and compliance officers in sponsored programs offices.

## Impact If Solved
Obligations surface before they become audit findings. Single Audit findings on research awards each cost weeks of corrective action, so preventing even two a year matters (estimate).
