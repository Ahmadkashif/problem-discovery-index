# Sponsored Research Administration & Cost Recovery

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/colleges-and-universities|Colleges & Universities]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to recover the full allowable cost of each federal research award as agency rules, caps and terminations change, and whoever leaves the least allowable cost unrecovered takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.5B a year in research administration software, F&A rate studies and post-award services at research universities (estimate), administering $117.7B in research spending in FY2024 (NSF HERD)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~0.2% of institutional spending (estimate), but research is ~15% of the sector's total spend
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — proposal and award systems are digital; award terms, effort and policy changes are read and reconciled by people
**Target Buyer:** Associate Vice President for Sponsored Programs, reporting to the Vice President for Research and the CFO
**Automation Potential:** High for document reading and reconciliation, low for negotiation with agencies

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Universities ran $64B+ of federally funded research in FY2024 (NSF HERD). Each award carries direct costs and F&A recovery at a rate negotiated with the cognizant agency, and every dollar not recovered is subsidised from tuition or endowment. 2025 and 2026 turned post-award administration into crisis management. An NIH cap of 15% on indirect costs was announced and then enjoined, the injunction upheld on appeal in January 2026 (search summary). Awards were terminated or suspended and terms changed mid-stream. The office that keeps cost recovery whole through that churn is the one the vice president for research keeps funding.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Huron Research Suite, Kuali Research, Cayuse, InfoEd and the ERP grants modules (Workday, Oracle, Banner) track proposals, awards and spending; Huron, Attain Partners and Baker Tilly prepare F&A rate proposals. Gaps: award terms stored as PDFs, not structured data; no portfolio-wide answer to "which awards does this policy change touch?"; effort certification signed without review; and closeout costs left unbilled at termination.
