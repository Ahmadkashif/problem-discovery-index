# Faculty Productivity & Academic Program Benchmarking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/colleges-and-universities|Colleges & Universities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Vendors that tell provosts which departments and programmes to grow, merge or cut: Academic Analytics (faculty publications, grants and awards benchmarked by discipline), Gray DI (programme demand from search, enrollment and job data), instructional cost studies and academic portfolio consultancies such as rpk GROUP (named from recall). Budget cuts and closures in 2025–26 made programme portfolio reviews a board-level exercise.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-100 analysts and data staff per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Faculty productivity benchmarks, programme demand and market scores, instructional cost comparisons, portfolio review reports
**Proprietary data:** Assembled faculty-level scholarship records from public publication and grant data; programme demand indices built from public and licensed sources; contributed cost data from participating institutions under participation terms
**Clock:** Clients' programme review and budget cycles; vendors set their own data releases
**Buyer:** Chief executive or head of data at the vendor
