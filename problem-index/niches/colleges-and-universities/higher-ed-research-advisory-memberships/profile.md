# Higher-Education Research & Advisory Memberships

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/colleges-and-universities|Colleges & Universities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Firms that sell research to college leaders by membership or subscription: EAB's Research Advisory Services, Encoura's Eduventures Research, Gartner for education CIOs, Tyton Partners and Ithaka S+R, plus custom research shops such as Hanover Research (named from recall except EAB and Encoura). Members get best-practice studies, surveys of prospective students and leaders, benchmarks and analyst calls. EAB was recruiting directors and associate directors of Research Advisory Services on its own board.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-200 research analysts and advisors per firm, largest at EAB (estimate)
**Output:** Membership research reports, prospective-student and leadership surveys, benchmarks, advisory calls, custom studies
**Proprietary data:** The firms' own survey panels and interview archives (for example Eduventures' prospective student surveys), plus member benchmarks
**Clock:** Self-set publication and survey calendars
**Buyer:** Chief research officer or head of research advisory

**Kill switches:** none for the syndicated half, which runs on the firms' own surveys and interviews. Two halves would be live: the member-benchmark half, built from members' own institutional data supplied under membership terms, and the custom-research half (commissioned studies on a client's own students or alumni), which is client-owned work. The score is the syndicated half's.
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. The research is the invoice, but no outside party sets its deadline and survey panels are a moderate moat. The K-12 analogue is [[niches/tutoring-centers/education-market-research/profile|🔍 Education Market Research & District Data]] (41).
