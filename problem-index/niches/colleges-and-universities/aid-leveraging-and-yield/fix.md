# Appeals Decided in Email With No Record

**Niche:** [[niches/colleges-and-universities/aid-leveraging-and-yield/profile|Financial Aid Leveraging & Yield]]
**Industry:** [[industries/colleges-and-universities|Colleges & Universities]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Hundreds of aid appeals are decided each spring by reply email, and the next year nobody can say what was granted, why, or whether it worked.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #tacit-knowledge-ml #workflow-orchestration #evaluation-metrics #quick-win #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to set each admitted student's institutional aid so the class arrives at its target size and profile, and whoever brings in the target class at the lowest discount rate takes the account.

## The Problem
Appeals arrive as emails, uploaded letters and competitor award letters. Counsellors forward them to the director, who decides and replies. The aid system records the new award amount. The reason, the competitor's offer and the director's expectation are not recorded, so like cases are treated differently and the director's judgement cannot be checked or taught.

## Why It's Still Broken
Recording reasons takes time in the busiest weeks, and aid systems have no field for a decision rationale or a predicted outcome.

## What a Fix Looks Like
An appeal form that extracts the trigger and the competitor offer from the uploaded documents, records the decision with a one-line reason and the director's enrollment estimate, and links to similar past appeals and outcomes. Professional-judgment documentation required for federal aid is produced from the same record.

## Who Feels the Pain
Aid directors, counsellors, families treated inconsistently, and auditors reviewing professional-judgment files.

## Impact If Fixed
A searchable appeal history in one season at almost no extra time, consistency across counsellors, and the training data the uplift model needs. A typical private college handles ~300–800 appeals a year (estimate).
