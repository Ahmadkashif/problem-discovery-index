# Higher-Education Association Research Arms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/colleges-and-universities|Colleges & Universities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Professional associations publish the sector's benchmark studies: NACUBO (the Tuition Discounting Study cited in the hub, the endowment study), NASFAA (aid administration surveys), AACRAO (registrar and admissions surveys), NACAC (State of College Admission), CUPA-HR (salary and workforce surveys), EDUCAUSE (IT research) and COGR and NCURA (research administration) (named from recall except NACUBO). Members get the reports; some surveys are sold.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically under 10 research staff per association (estimate); CUPA-HR's survey team and EDUCAUSE's research unit are the largest and are borderline
**Output:** Annual benchmark studies, salary surveys, policy analysis
**Proprietary data:** Member-contributed survey data
**Clock:** Self-set survey calendars
**Buyer:** Association president or research director
