# SIS, ERP & Admissions CRM Vendor Data Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/colleges-and-universities|Colleges & Universities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The vendors whose systems hold colleges' student, aid and finance records — Ellucian (Banner, Colleague), Workday Student, Oracle (PeopleSoft Campus Solutions, Student Cloud), Anthology, Jenzabar — and the admissions CRMs (Slate, Salesforce Education Cloud, Element451). Their data and AI teams build analytics, benchmarks and AI features on top. Ellucian calls its platform "trained on the richest dataset available in higher education" (company claim) and was hiring modernization engineers (US remote) and data and AI roles in Reston, Dublin and Bengaluru.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-100 data, analytics and AI staff per larger vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Embedded analytics and dashboards, AI assistants, benchmark features, regulatory reporting modules
**Proprietary data:** Student lifecycle, aid, finance and HR records for thousands of institutions, held per client; prospect records in CRMs
**Clock:** Clients' disbursement, registration and federal reporting dates, which the software must meet
**Buyer:** Chief product officer or head of AI and data at the vendor
