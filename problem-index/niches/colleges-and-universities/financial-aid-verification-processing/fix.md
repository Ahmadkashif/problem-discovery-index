# Comment Codes Resolved From Memory

**Niche:** [[niches/colleges-and-universities/financial-aid-verification-processing/profile|Financial Aid Verification, ISIR & Packaging Operations]]
**Industry:** [[industries/colleges-and-universities|Colleges & Universities]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** ISIR comment codes and C-flags are resolved by whoever remembers what they mean this year, and new counsellors learn by asking the person next to them.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #compliance #worker-facing #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to take each student's ISIR to a final, disbursable aid offer, and whoever certifies the most files with no later correction or programme-review finding takes the account.

## The Problem
Each ISIR can carry comment codes about identity, citizenship, selective service history, prior defaults and data conflicts. Each code has resolution steps that change between award years. Veteran counsellors carry these in their heads; new ones search the handbook or ask. Files wait, and inconsistent resolution is a finding risk.

## Why It's Still Broken
Aid systems display codes but not resolution steps tied to the current year's guidance, and office procedure manuals go stale.

## What a Fix Looks Like
For every code on a file, show the current-year meaning, the resolution steps and the documents needed, with a link to the guidance, and record how the counsellor resolved it so the office's own practice is searchable.

## Who Feels the Pain
New counsellors, the veterans interrupted by their questions, and students whose files wait.

## Impact If Fixed
New counsellors work files independently weeks sooner. Answering questions takes ~3–5 hours a week of a veteran counsellor's time in peak season (estimate), and that time comes back.
