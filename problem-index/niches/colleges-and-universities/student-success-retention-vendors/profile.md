# Student Success & Retention Analytics Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/colleges-and-universities|Colleges & Universities]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors whose software gives advisors and student-success offices risk scores, early alerts, degree plans and nudges: EAB Navigate360, Civitas Learning (Anthology), Stellic, EdSights and Mainstay (named from recall except EAB and Stellic). Institutions pay subscriptions; the analytics team builds the risk models and the benchmarks shown to clients. Stellic was hiring engineers and data architects (San Mateo, US, and one support engineer in Pakistan); EAB data engineers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~30-200 data scientists, analysts and student-success consultants per larger vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Persistence risk scores, early-alert workflows, degree-plan analytics, client benchmark reports
**Proprietary data:** Term-by-term student records, LMS activity and advising interactions across many client institutions, held per client
**Clock:** Clients' term census and registration dates; no outside deadline binds the vendor's own deliverable
**Buyer:** Vice president of data science or product at the vendor
