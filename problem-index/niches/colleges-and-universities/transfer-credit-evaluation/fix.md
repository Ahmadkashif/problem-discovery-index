# Evaluations Posted After the Student Has Registered

**Niche:** [[niches/colleges-and-universities/transfer-credit-evaluation/profile|Transfer Credit Evaluation & Articulation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/colleges-and-universities|Colleges & Universities]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A transfer student meets an advisor and registers before the credit evaluation is finished, then discovers they repeated a course or missed a prerequisite.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #descriptive-statistics #automation #quick-win #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn an incoming transcript into posted, degree-applicable course equivalencies, and whoever applies the largest share of incoming credit toward degree requirements takes the account.

## The Problem
Evaluation queues peak exactly when transfer students are admitted and orientation runs. Courses awaiting faculty review sit unposted. Advisors build schedules from unofficial guesses, and students take courses they already had credit for.

## Why It's Still Broken
Nobody owns the deadline. The registrar processes in arrival order, faculty reviewers answer when they can, and the orientation date is set by another office.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Order the evaluation queue by each admitted student's orientation or registration date. Escalate faculty reviews that will miss it, and post a provisional evaluation marked as such so advisors know what is settled.

## Who Feels the Pain
Transfer students, advisors at orientation, and evaluators blamed for delays they do not control.

## Impact If Fixed
More students register knowing what counts. A student who repeats one 3-credit course pays for it twice, several hundred to over a thousand dollars of avoidable tuition (estimate).
