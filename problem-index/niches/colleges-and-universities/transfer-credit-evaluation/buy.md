# Invoice Document AI Rebuilt for Transcripts

**Niche:** [[niches/colleges-and-universities/transfer-credit-evaluation/profile|Transfer Credit Evaluation & Articulation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/colleges-and-universities|Colleges & Universities]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Accounts-payable teams already extract line items from any supplier's invoice layout; registrars need the same for any institution's transcript.
**Tags:** #cnns #transformers #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #automation #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn an incoming transcript into posted, degree-applicable course equivalencies, and whoever applies the largest share of incoming credit toward degree requirements takes the account.

## The Problem
Many transcripts still arrive as PDFs or scanned paper with institution-specific layouts, grading scales and term structures. Staff key course lines into the SIS by hand before evaluation can start.

## What Already Exists
Intelligent document processing for invoices and receipts (Rossum, Hyperscience, cloud document AI services) that learns layouts and extracts line items with confidence scores.

## The Customization Gap
The transcript version needs: course code, title, credits, grade and term per line; quarter, semester and clock-hour conversions; grading scales including pass/fail and repeats; institution identification against federal codes so the right catalogue is used; detection of altered or fraudulent transcripts; international transcripts routed to credential evaluators (see [[niches/immigration-law/credential-evaluation-services/profile|🔍 Foreign Credential Evaluation Services]]); and output in the PESC or EDI formats SIS imports accept.

## Target Customer
Registrar and admissions processing teams receiving many non-electronic transcripts.

## Impact If Solved
Manual keying of course lines ends for most transcripts. Keying a 40-line transcript takes ~10–15 minutes (estimate), and that time moves to evaluation.
