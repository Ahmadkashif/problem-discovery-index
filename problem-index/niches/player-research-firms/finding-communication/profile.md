# Finding Communication

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/player-research-firms|Player Research Firms]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to report a finding definitely enough to be acted on and honestly enough to be true — and whoever resolves that takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$70M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — decks
**Target Buyer:** Research leads
**Automation Potential:** Medium — evidence linking and confidence expression

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The reporting into development teams is frequently more definite than the evidence, because a hedged finding does not get acted on. This is a known and unresolved tension in the discipline: the researcher understands the uncertainty, the team needs a decision, and the deck resolves it in favour of decisiveness. The result is that confidence is systematically overstated in the direction of being useful, and nobody downstream can tell which findings rest on what.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A slide deck, a prioritised findings list, some video clips and a verbal caveat in the room. The gaps: no confidence expression anyone reads; no link from a finding to its supporting evidence; no indication of how many participants a finding rests on; no distinction between observation and inference; and no format that survives being forwarded.
