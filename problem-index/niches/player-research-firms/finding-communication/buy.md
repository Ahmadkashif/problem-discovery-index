# Evidence Grading From Clinical Guidelines

**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Clinical guidelines grade the strength of evidence behind every recommendation, and research decks grade nothing.
**Tags:** #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #compliance #descriptive-statistics #hypothesis-testing #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to report a finding definitely enough to be acted on and honestly enough to be true — and whoever resolves that takes the account.

## The Problem
Clinical practice guidelines confronted the identical tension — recommendations must be actionable by busy practitioners and honest about the evidence behind them — and resolved it with grading. Every recommendation carries an explicit strength and an explicit evidence quality, on a published scale, so a reader can see at a glance both what to do and how well established it is. The frameworks are standardised and widely adopted. Research findings carry nothing comparable.

## What Already Exists
Evidence quality grading frameworks; separate strength-of-recommendation ratings; explicit criteria for each grade; standardised presentation; and conflict-of-interest and limitation disclosure.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to small-sample qualitative research read by a development team. It requires: (1) grading criteria suited to eight moderated sessions rather than to randomised trials, so the scale must be built rather than borrowed — this is the substantive difference and no existing framework fits; (2) findings that are mechanisms rather than effect sizes; (3) a reader who is a designer under deadline rather than a clinician trained in the framework, so the notation must be immediately legible; (4) a commercial relationship where a low grade reflects on the supplier; and (5) no professional body to standardise the scale, so adoption must spread commercially.

## Target Customer
Games user research firms, in-house research functions, development teams, and research standards bodies.

## Impact If Solved
Clinical guidelines resolved the same tension by grading strength and evidence separately on a published scale. A scale suited to eight moderated sessions, legible to a designer under deadline, is what has to be built.
