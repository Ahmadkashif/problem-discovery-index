**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to find out what happened to the findings it delivered, when the evidence lives on the other side of an organisational boundary — and whoever closes that loop takes the account.

## The Problem
Clinical research confronted the same structural problem and built institutions for it. Studies are registered before they run, outcomes are pre-specified, follow-up is mandated, results are reported whether positive or null, and the accumulated record supports meta-analysis across studies. It exists because a field that only remembers its successes accumulates belief rather than knowledge. Games user research is methodologically careful within a study and has none of this across studies.

## What Already Exists
Study registration with pre-specified outcomes; mandated follow-up and reporting; null result publication norms; meta-analysis across studies; and evidence grading frameworks.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to commercial research whose outcomes are proprietary telemetry. It requires: (1) outcomes held by a commercial client rather than measured by the researcher, so access is a negotiation rather than a protocol — this is the substantive difference and is why the loop stays open; (2) an intervention that is a design change decided by a third party, not administered by the researcher; (3) no regulator or journal to compel registration or reporting; (4) commercially sensitive findings that cannot be published as clinical results are; and (5) study cadence measured in weeks rather than years, requiring something far lighter than a trial protocol.

## Target Customer
User research firms and platforms, in-house research functions, publishers, and research methodology bodies.

## Impact If Solved
Clinical research built registration and mandated follow-up because a field that only remembers its successes accumulates belief rather than knowledge. Outcomes held by a commercial client make access a negotiation, which is the piece to design.
