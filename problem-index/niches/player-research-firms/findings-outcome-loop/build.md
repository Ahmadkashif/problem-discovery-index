# Learning Whether the Finding Was Right

**Niche:** [[niches/player-research-firms/findings-outcome-loop/profile|Findings Outcome Loop]]
**Industry:** [[industries/player-research-firms|Player Research Firms]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A discipline whose entire product is a finding has no mechanism for learning whether any finding was correct.
**Tags:** #causal-inference #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #data-integration #hypothesis-testing #descriptive-statistics #workflow-orchestration #tacit-knowledge-ml
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to find out what happened to the findings it delivered, when the evidence lives on the other side of an organisational boundary — and whoever closes that loop takes the account.

## The Problem
A research firm delivers a finding, the development team decides whether to act on it, a change ships, and the telemetry shows what players did. Every part of that chain exists and the firm sees only the first. It cannot say which of its recommendations are typically implemented, which types of finding are reliably right, or where its own method is weakest — because nothing returns across the boundary, and nobody has asked for it.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The firm's engagement ends at the readout and nobody is paid for the follow-up. Asking about outcomes can feel like asking to be graded. Telemetry access requires an agreement nobody has requested. And the discipline has managed on methodological rigour rather than on outcome evidence for its whole history.

## What to Build
Record the findings properly and then go and get the outcomes. Capture every recommendation in a structured, trackable form rather than as prose in a deck, which is the core and is the prerequisite for everything else. Follow up on implementation status as a standard part of every engagement, since most clients would answer and are simply never asked. Link implemented changes to post-change telemetry where an agreement allows, which is the half that produces real evidence. Analyse across studies and titles to learn which kinds of finding predict behaviour, as that is the field-level asset and no firm holds it. Report the firm's own implementation rate, which is a measure of influence nobody currently has. Identify the findings that were implemented and did not work, because those are the most informative and the most avoided. Feed the evidence back into method — session counts, study designs, question types — which is what turns the loop into improvement. Offer outcome measurement as a paid follow-on service, which is how it becomes commercially viable. Anonymise and pool across clients where possible, since the cross-client corpus is the firm's unique position. And publish method findings, which is how a discipline improves rather than a single firm.

## Target Customer
Games user research firms and platforms, in-house research functions, publishers commissioning research, and research operations vendors.

## Impact If Built
Every link in the chain from finding to player behaviour exists and the firm sees only the first. Structured recommendations plus follow-up turns a discipline's accumulated anecdote into evidence about what kinds of finding are right.
