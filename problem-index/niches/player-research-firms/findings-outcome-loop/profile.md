# Findings Outcome Loop

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/player-research-firms|Player Research Firms]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to find out what happened to the findings it delivered, when the evidence lives on the other side of an organisational boundary — and whoever closes that loop takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$160M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~26% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — nothing returns
**Target Buyer:** Research leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — tracking plus measurement

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A firm may run hundreds of studies a year across dozens of titles and hold no record of which recommendations were implemented, which were ignored, and what happened either way. The product is a finding; the evidence about whether the finding was right sits in the game team's telemetry after a change nobody tells the researcher about. The consequence is that the accumulated experience of a methodologically serious field is anecdote rather than evidence.

### Contested sub-niches
This niche is not terminal. It names a mechanism, and two problems with different asks sit inside it:
- [[niches/player-research-firms/implementation-tracking/profile|🎯 Implementation Tracking]] — did the team act on it
- [[niches/player-research-firms/outcome-telemetry-linkage/profile|🎯 Outcome Telemetry Linkage]] — did the change work

## Current Tools & Gaps
A findings deck, a readout meeting and an invoice. The gaps: no record of what was recommended in a structured form; no follow-up on implementation; no access to post-change telemetry; no analysis across studies; and no evidence about which kinds of finding predict behaviour.
