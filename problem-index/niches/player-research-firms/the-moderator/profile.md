# The Moderator

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/player-research-firms|Player Research Firms]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make back-to-back moderated sessions sustainable for the person running them, who is also the person writing them up — and whoever does that takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$60M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — no support
**Target Buyer:** Research operations
**Automation Potential:** Medium — capture, load management and handover

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Session moderation is emotionally demanding concentration work performed back to back, and the person doing it is also the one who writes it up afterwards. They must maintain neutrality, manage a participant's frustration, observe closely, take notes, and keep the protocol — for several hours, several days running, and then produce the analysis on a deadline. The role is a known source of fatigue and turnover in a field where experienced moderators are scarce, and nothing supports it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A protocol document, a notepad, a recording setup and a schedule. The gaps: no structured note capture during sessions; no separation of moderation from analysis; no load limits; no support after difficult sessions; and no handover mechanism between moderator and analyst.
