## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A study produces hours of gameplay video, think-aloud audio, observer notes and telemetry, and turning that into findings is manual coding performed under deadline. It is the largest cost in a study and the part most compressed when the schedule tightens — which is exactly where the quality of the finding is determined. The material is rich and structured enough to support substantial automation, and the discipline still codes it by hand.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A video player, a spreadsheet or coding tool, a transcript if there was time, and observer notes. The gaps: no automatic transcription and alignment; no synchronisation of video to telemetry; no candidate event detection; no cross-session pattern surfacing; and no reuse of coding schemes across studies.
