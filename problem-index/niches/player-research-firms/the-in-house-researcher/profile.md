# The In-House Researcher of One

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/player-research-firms|Player Research Firms]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give the single researcher inside a studio the peer review, method support and standing that an agency team has by default — and whoever supplies that takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$40M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — alone
**Target Buyer:** Studio research functions
**Automation Potential:** Medium — method support and templates

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A large share of games user research is done by one person inside a studio. They design studies, recruit, moderate, analyse, present, and defend the findings to people who outrank them, with no peer to check a study design and nobody to say whether a conclusion is supported. They also have something agencies do not: continuity, telemetry access and relationships. The role is structurally isolated and it is where a growing share of the field's work happens.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A survey tool, a recording setup, a community of practice they may not have time for, and a slide template. The gaps: no peer review of study design; no method reference; no benchmarks against comparable studies; no standing when findings are inconvenient; and no route to escalate a methodological concern.
