# A Peer for the Researcher Who Has None

**Niche:** [[niches/player-research-firms/the-in-house-researcher/profile|The In-House Researcher of One]]
**Industry:** [[industries/player-research-firms|Player Research Firms]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** One person does the whole research function and has nobody to check any of it.
**Tags:** #worker-facing #workflow-orchestration #evaluation-metrics #large-language-models #tacit-knowledge-ml #data-integration #descriptive-statistics #confidence-intervals
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give the single researcher inside a studio the peer review, method support and standing that an agency team has by default — and whoever supplies that takes the account.

## The Problem
The solo in-house researcher designs a study with no one to review the design, runs it alone, analyses it alone, and presents conclusions to a team that may not want them. Nobody catches a leading question, an unrepresentative sample or an overreaching conclusion. Nobody supports them when a finding is unwelcome. They are often the only person in the building who knows what good method looks like, which means they are also the only person who could tell if they got it wrong.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Solo practitioners are individually small customers. Studios treat research as a function rather than as a discipline needing support. Communities of practice exist and require time the role does not have. And the isolation is invisible to everyone except the person in it.

## What to Build
Supply the peer review and the method support the role structurally lacks. Provide study design review before a study runs, which is the core and is the single highest-value intervention for a solo researcher. Offer method templates and decision guidance for the common study types, so design does not start from a blank page each time. Benchmark findings against comparable studies where anonymised data allows, which gives the researcher a reference they otherwise lack. Provide a review of conclusions against evidence before the readout, as an outside check on overreach is what a team would give. Connect solo researchers across studios in a structured way rather than an open community, since scheduled peer review happens and an open channel does not. Supply standing — an external methodological position the researcher can cite when findings are contested. Give them a route to escalate a methodological concern, which currently has nowhere to go. Offer analysis support at peak load, which is where the solo role breaks. Keep a record of their studies so their body of work is visible in a performance conversation. And design the whole thing around a person with no spare time, which is the binding constraint on anything they might use.

## Target Customer
In-house research functions at studios, research agencies offering support services, professional bodies in user research, and research tooling vendors.

## Impact If Built
The solo researcher is the only person in the building who would know if the method was wrong, which means nothing checks it. Pre-study design review plus structured peer connection supplies what an agency team has by default.
