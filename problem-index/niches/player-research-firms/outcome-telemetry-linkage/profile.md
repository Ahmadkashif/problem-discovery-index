# Outcome Telemetry Linkage

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/player-research-firms|Player Research Firms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to measure whether the change a finding produced actually improved anything, using telemetry the client holds — and whoever gets that arrangement working takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$75M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — no access
**Target Buyer:** Research and client data leadership
**Automation Potential:** Medium — measurement across a boundary

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This half cannot be done by asking. Establishing whether a design change improved player behaviour requires the client's post-release telemetry, a before-and-after comparison that accounts for everything else that changed, and a commercial agreement permitting the firm to see any of it. It is a measurement and data-sharing product sold into the client relationship rather than a process change inside the firm, and it is the half that would tell the discipline which kinds of finding predict what players actually do.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Nothing. The gaps: no telemetry access; no pre-specified outcome measure; no comparison design; no control for concurrent changes; and no agreement contemplating any of it.
