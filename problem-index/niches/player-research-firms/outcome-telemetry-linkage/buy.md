# Cross-Party Measurement From Marketing Clean Rooms

**Niche:** [[niches/player-research-firms/outcome-telemetry-linkage/profile|Outcome Telemetry Linkage]]
**Industry:** [[industries/player-research-firms|Player Research Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Marketing built clean rooms so two parties could measure a joint outcome without exposing each other's data, and research firms have no mechanism at all.
**Tags:** #causal-inference #compliance #data-integration #confidence-intervals #evaluation-metrics #hypothesis-testing #descriptive-statistics #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to measure whether the change a finding produced actually improved anything, using telemetry the client holds — and whoever gets that arrangement working takes the account.

## The Problem
Marketing measurement faced the identical structural problem — one party holds the intervention data, another holds the outcome data, and neither will hand over its records — and solved it with clean rooms, matched-cohort designs and agreed measurement protocols. The technical and contractual patterns are established and in routine commercial use. Research firms measuring whether their findings worked have the same two-party structure and no mechanism whatsoever.

## What Already Exists
Data clean room infrastructure; agreed measurement protocols between parties; aggregate-only output constraints; matched cohort and holdout designs; and independent measurement administration.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to an intervention that is a design change rather than an advertisement. It requires: (1) an intervention that is a product change affecting all players at once, so there is often no untreated group — this is the substantive difference and forces before-and-after designs with all their weaknesses; (2) outcomes that are behavioural game metrics rather than conversions; (3) a single client relationship rather than an advertising ecosystem with established norms; (4) small numbers of studies rather than continuous campaign measurement, so each design must be bespoke; and (5) a firm whose own performance is what is being measured, requiring independence in how the result is produced.

## Target Customer
User research firms, client analytics functions, publishers, and clean room and measurement vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Clean rooms let two parties measure a joint outcome without exposing their records, and they are in routine commercial use. A design change affecting all players at once removes the untreated group, which is the design problem to solve.
