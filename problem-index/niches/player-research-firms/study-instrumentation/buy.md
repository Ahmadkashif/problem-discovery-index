# Session Capture From Usability Platforms

**Industry:** [[industries/player-research-firms|Player Research Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Web usability platforms capture, index and clip sessions automatically, and games research records a video file.
**Tags:** #data-integration #automation #workflow-orchestration #evaluation-metrics #descriptive-statistics #large-language-models #quick-win #object-detection
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to capture a session as a synchronised, searchable record rather than as a video file and some notes — and whoever builds that capture takes the account.

## The Problem
Web and app usability platforms made session capture a solved problem. They record screen and audio, transcribe automatically, mark events, let observers tag moments live, generate clips for reports, and make everything searchable. They are inexpensive and widely used by product teams. Games user research, which produces far richer sessions, largely records a screen capture and takes notes in a document.

## What Already Exists
Automatic session recording and transcription; live observer tagging; event marking and clip generation; searchable session archives; and highlight reel assembly for reporting.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a game running on a console or a rig rather than in a browser. It requires: (1) capture from consoles and dedicated hardware rather than from a browser tab, which is the practical barrier and is why the category has not transferred; (2) game telemetry as a first-class stream alongside screen and audio, since game state is what makes a moment interpretable; (3) sessions of one to two hours rather than fifteen minutes, changing storage and navigation entirely; (4) unreleased builds under non-disclosure, which constrains cloud storage and sharing; and (5) a physical lab setup with observers present, rather than a remote unmoderated test.

## Target Customer
Games user research firms, in-house research functions, playtesting platforms, and usability platform vendors seeking a vertical.

## Impact If Solved
Usability platforms made session capture cheap, searchable and automatic for the web. Console capture with game telemetry on the same timeline, under non-disclosure, is what the games version has to add.
