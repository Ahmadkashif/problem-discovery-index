# Recommendation Tracking From Audit and Consulting

**Niche:** [[niches/player-research-firms/implementation-tracking/profile|Implementation Tracking]]
**Industry:** [[industries/player-research-firms|Player Research Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Audit and consulting track recommendation status to closure as standard practice, and user research hands over a deck.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #compliance #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #descriptive-statistics #automation #revenue-impact #confidence-intervals
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to find out which of its recommendations the development team actually acted on — and whoever establishes that takes the account.

## The Problem
Internal audit and management consulting both treat the recommendation as a tracked object. Each finding carries an identifier, a risk rating, a management response, an owner and a due date; status is followed to closure; and outstanding recommendations are reported to the committee that commissioned the work. It exists because a recommendation nobody follows up on is a document rather than an outcome. User research produces recommendations and stops.

## What Already Exists
Recommendation registers with identifiers and owners; formal management responses; follow-up to closure; overdue and outstanding reporting; and implementation rate metrics.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to an advisory relationship with no mandate and no committee. It requires: (1) no authority behind the recommendation, since a game team is free to ignore a research finding entirely and there is no audit committee to report to — this is the substantive difference and makes the follow-up voluntary on both sides; (2) recommendations that are design suggestions rather than control deficiencies, so priority is judgement rather than risk rating; (3) a client relationship where persistent follow-up damages goodwill rather than fulfilling an obligation; (4) study cadences of weeks rather than annual audit cycles; and (5) rejection being a legitimate and informative outcome rather than an exception to explain.

## Target Customer
User research firms, in-house research functions, publishers, and governance and recommendation tracking vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Audit tracks recommendations to closure because an unfollowed recommendation is a document rather than an outcome. No mandate and no committee makes the follow-up voluntary, which is what the design has to accommodate.
