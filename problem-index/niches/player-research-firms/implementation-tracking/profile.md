# Implementation Tracking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/player-research-firms|Player Research Firms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to find out which of its recommendations the development team actually acted on — and whoever establishes that takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$85M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~14% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — nothing asked
**Target Buyer:** Research leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — process and records

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This half needs nothing from the client but an answer. Which recommendations were accepted, which were rejected and why, and which were implemented. It requires no telemetry access, no measurement design and no commercial agreement — only a follow-up the firm does not currently make. A firm that knows its implementation rate knows something about its own influence, its clients' decision-making and which kinds of finding survive contact with a development schedule, all of which is actionable without ever establishing whether any finding was correct.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A readout meeting and an assumption. The gaps: no structured recommendation record; no acceptance or rejection capture; no implementation follow-up; no reasons for rejection; and no rate reported anywhere.
