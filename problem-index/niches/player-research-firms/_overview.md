# Niche Analysis — Player Research Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/player-research-firms|Player Research Firms]]

## Niche Selection

Games user research is a methodologically serious discipline whose entire product is a finding delivered across an organisational boundary, after which the evidence about whether the finding was right stays on the other side. The eight niches below follow that structure: what happens to a finding after it is handed over, what it cost to produce, who it was produced from, how it was expressed, and who carries the work.

## Why These Niches

The outcome loop and session analysis carry the money and the two defining problems: a discipline that never learns whether its findings were right, and an analysis cost that is the largest part of every study and the first thing compressed when the schedule tightens. Sampling and communication are the two places where rigour is quietly lost — conclusions generalised from whoever answered the screener, and findings reported more definitely than the evidence supports because a hedged finding does not get acted on. The two underserved audiences are the moderator performing demanding concentration work back to back and writing it up afterwards, and the lone in-house researcher with no peer review and no methodological support. The last two are mechanical: running the studies, and capturing them.

## Filter Notes

Seven of the eight are terminal — each names one contest that every serious competitor is fighting over, and decomposing further would produce features rather than markets.

**The findings outcome loop** is not. It names a mechanism, and inside it are two problems that require different things from the client and are bought separately. Implementation tracking asks whether the team acted on the finding at all — a records and process problem across an organisational boundary, needing only a status update the client would readily give, and valuable immediately because a firm that knows its implementation rate knows something about its own influence that it currently does not. Outcome telemetry linkage asks whether the change improved anything — a data-sharing and causal measurement problem requiring access to the client's post-release telemetry, a considerably larger ask, and the half that would let the discipline learn which kinds of finding actually predict what players do. A firm can establish the first this quarter by changing its close-out process; the second needs a commercial agreement and a measurement design, and most clients have never been asked.

Two adjacent candidates were rejected as belonging elsewhere: **the telemetry and reporting stack the studio runs on** belongs to [[industries/game-analytics-vendors|Game Analytics Vendors]], and **instrumenting a demo to read a commercial outcome** sits with [[industries/indie-game-studios|Indie Game Studios]].
