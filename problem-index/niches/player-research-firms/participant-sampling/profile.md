# Participant Sampling

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/player-research-firms|Player Research Firms]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to generalise from whoever answered the screener and turned up, when those people are systematically not the people the game is for — and whoever fixes the sample takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$90M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — screeners
**Target Buyer:** Research operations
**Automation Potential:** High — recruitment, weighting and reporting

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Findings are generalised from whoever answered the screener and turned up, and the people who do that are systematically not the people the game is for. Participants who respond to research recruitment skew toward the available, the enthusiastic and the already-engaged. Sessions are expensive so samples are small. The discipline knows this and manages it through care rather than through measurement, and the deck usually describes the participants rather than characterising how they differ from the target population.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A screener, a recruitment panel, an incentive and a participant table in the appendix. The gaps: no comparison of the sample against the target population; no weighting; no measurement of recruitment bias; no reach into under-represented groups; and no statement of generalisability.
