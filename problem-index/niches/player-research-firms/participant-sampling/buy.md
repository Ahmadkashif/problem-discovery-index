**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to generalise from whoever answered the screener and turned up, when those people are systematically not the people the game is for — and whoever fixes the sample takes the account.

## The Problem
Survey research spent a century on exactly this problem. Sampling frames define the target population, probability designs govern selection, non-response is analysed rather than ignored, weighting corrects known imbalances, and margins of error are reported alongside every estimate. The methods are public and taught, and the discipline is explicit that a self-selected sample supports no population claim. Games user research generalises from panel volunteers.

## What Already Exists
Sampling frame construction; probability and quota sampling designs; non-response bias analysis; post-stratification weighting; and margin of error reporting.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to samples of eight rather than eight hundred, where the observation is behaviour rather than an answer. It requires: (1) sample sizes small enough that weighting is meaningless and the correction must instead be in recruitment and in reporting — this is the substantive difference and rules out most survey machinery; (2) a target population defined by behaviour and preference rather than by demographics; (3) an observation that is a two-hour moderated session rather than a questionnaire, making each unit expensive; (4) a sampling frame that can sometimes come from the client's own telemetry, which survey research would envy; and (5) qualitative findings where the claim is about mechanisms rather than proportions, needing a different account of generalisability.

## Target Customer
Games user research firms, in-house research functions, recruitment providers, and survey methodology consultancies.

## Impact If Solved
Survey research is explicit that a self-selected sample supports no population claim, and built the machinery to fix it. Samples of eight make weighting meaningless, so the correction has to live in recruitment and in honest reporting.
