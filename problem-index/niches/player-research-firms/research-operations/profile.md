# Research Operations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/player-research-firms|Player Research Firms]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get the right participants into the right sessions at the right time without consuming the research team, and whoever automates that takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$30M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — partial
**Target Buyer:** Research operations
**Automation Potential:** Very high — recruitment, scheduling and administration

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Running a study means screening applicants, scheduling around participant and facility availability, handling consent and non-disclosure agreements, sending reminders, managing no-shows and replacements, paying incentives, and keeping records. It is repetitive administrative work, it consumes researcher time when it is not properly staffed, and it determines whether the study happens on schedule. It is also almost entirely automatable and is largely done in spreadsheets and email.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A recruitment panel, a scheduling tool, an email inbox and a spreadsheet. The gaps: no automated screening against study criteria; no scheduling that respects facility and moderator constraints; no consent and agreement handling; no no-show prediction or overbooking; and no incentive payment automation.
