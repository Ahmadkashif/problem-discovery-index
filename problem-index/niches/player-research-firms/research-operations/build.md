# Running the Study Without Running the Team

**Niche:** [[niches/player-research-firms/research-operations/profile|Research Operations]]
**Industry:** [[industries/player-research-firms|Player Research Firms]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Getting participants into sessions consumes researcher time that was supposed to go on research.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #automation #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #compliance #descriptive-statistics #quick-win #logistic-regression
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get the right participants into the right sessions at the right time without consuming the research team, and whoever automates that takes the account.

## The Problem
Between commissioning a study and running it sits a large block of administration: screening, scheduling against several constraints, consent and non-disclosure paperwork, reminders, replacements when people drop out, incentive payments and record-keeping. In smaller firms and in-house functions this falls on researchers. It is the work that most often delays a study and it has nothing to do with research.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Research operations tooling exists for general user research but handles games poorly — device requirements, studio facilities, non-disclosure over unreleased titles. Firms are small and build spreadsheets. The administration is invisible in the proposal. And nobody has treated it as a product for this vertical.

## What to Build
Automate the sequence from screener to incentive payment. Screen applicants automatically against structured study criteria rather than by reading responses, which is the core and removes the largest manual block. Schedule against participant availability, facility access, moderator limits and device requirements together, since those constraints interact and are currently reconciled by hand. Handle consent and non-disclosure agreements electronically, which matters more here than in general research because the titles are unreleased. Predict no-shows and overbook accordingly, as replacement scrambling is the commonest operational failure. Send reminders and confirmations automatically on a cadence that actually works. Pay incentives automatically on completion, which is a persistent friction and a reputational issue with participants. Maintain a participant history to avoid over-reliance on repeat participants, which is both an operations and a sampling concern. Track the recruitment funnel so the sampling picture is available rather than reconstructed. Handle remote and in-person studies in the same system, since most firms now run both. And keep it usable by a research coordinator rather than requiring configuration, which determines adoption in small teams.

## Target Customer
Games user research firms and platforms, in-house research functions, playtesting platforms, and research operations tooling vendors.

## Impact If Built
The administration between commissioning a study and running it is what delays studies and consumes researchers. Automated screening, constraint-aware scheduling and automatic incentive payment removes work that has nothing to do with research.
