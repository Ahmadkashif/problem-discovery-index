# Carrier Roof Underwriting Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/roofing-contractors|Roofing Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The property underwriting analytics functions inside the carriers, who now price, restrict or decline homeowners policies largely on roof age, material and condition — and who introduced the actual cash value roof schedules that changed the economics of residential roofing.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 actuaries, data scientists, and underwriting analysts
**Output:** Roof-based rating and eligibility rules, actual cash value schedules, non-renewal targeting, catastrophe exposure management, claim severity modelling
**Proprietary data:** Roof characteristics joined to claim frequency and severity across millions of insured properties over decades
**Clock:** Annual rate filings and renewal cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Actuary / VP of Property Analytics
