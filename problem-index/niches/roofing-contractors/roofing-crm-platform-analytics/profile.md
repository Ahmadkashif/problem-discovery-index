**Parent Industry:** [[industries/roofing-contractors|Roofing Contractors]]

## What They Do
The analytics functions inside the roofing-specific CRM and photo documentation platforms Pass 1 names, which hold job records, inspection photographs, estimates, supplements and outcomes for a large share of residential roofing contractors.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 data scientists, analysts, and product staff
**Output:** Sales pipeline and conversion analytics, production scheduling, supplement tracking, benchmark reporting, photo organisation
**Proprietary data:** Millions of job-site photographs paired with the damage assessment made, the estimate written, the supplement requested, and whether the carrier paid
**Clock:** Storm-driven demand surges
**Buyer:** VP of Product / Head of Data
