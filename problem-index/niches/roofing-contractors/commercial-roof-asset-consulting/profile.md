# Commercial Roof Asset Management Consulting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/roofing-contractors|Roofing Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Survey commercial roof portfolios for building owners and institutions, rate condition, estimate remaining life, and produce the multi-year capital plan that decides which roofs get replaced in which year.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 roof consultants, engineers, and asset management analysts
**Output:** Roof condition surveys and ratings, remaining service life estimates, capital replacement plans, specifications, construction observation
**Proprietary data:** Repeat condition surveys of the same roofs over decades, joined to when they were actually replaced and why
**Clock:** Capital budgeting cycles
**Buyer:** Managing Principal / Director of Roof Consulting
