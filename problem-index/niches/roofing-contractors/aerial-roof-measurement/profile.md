# Aerial Imagery & Roof Measurement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/roofing-contractors|Roofing Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Fly and process imagery and produce measured roof reports — area, pitch, facets, penetrations, waste factor — that Pass 1 records as integrated directly into the roofing contractor's CRM, so that no one climbs a roof to write an estimate.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 photogrammetrists, computer vision engineers, imagery analysts, and reviewers
**Output:** Roof measurement reports, condition and damage indicators, change detection between captures, property attribute data
**Proprietary data:** A multi-year national imagery library with repeated captures of the same roofs, before and after storms
**Clock:** Post-storm capture windows and estimate turnaround
**Buyer:** VP of Data Products / Chief Technology Officer
