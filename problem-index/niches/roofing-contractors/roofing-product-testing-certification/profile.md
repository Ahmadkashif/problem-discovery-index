# Roofing Assembly Testing & Approval Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/roofing-contractors|Roofing Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Test and approve roofing assemblies for wind uplift, hail impact and fire, and publish the approval listings that determine what may legally be installed in a given jurisdiction and what an insurer will credit. In high-wind regions no roof may be installed without a listing from this layer.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 test engineers, certification reviewers, standards staff, and factory auditors
**Output:** Assembly approvals and listings, wind uplift and impact ratings, test standards, factory inspection findings
**Proprietary data:** Decades of assembly test results — the only systematic record of how roofing systems actually behave under load, held across every manufacturer's submissions
**Clock:** Approval renewal cycles, standards revisions, and factory audit schedules
**Buyer:** VP of Certification / Chief Engineer
