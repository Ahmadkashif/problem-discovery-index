# Property Repair Estimating Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/roofing-contractors|Roofing Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Publish the line-item unit costs every property insurance claim settles on and own the software both sides write estimates in. Pass 1 states the dependency directly: roofing contractors must learn to write estimates in this platform to negotiate with adjusters at all.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 400-1,500 cost researchers, pricing analysts, and content specialists
**Output:** Line-item unit cost databases by trade and region, monthly pricing updates, estimating platform content, industry pricing analytics
**Proprietary data:** Market-survey pricing across every US market and trade, plus the estimate corpus flowing through the platform
**Clock:** Monthly pricing updates against live claim settlements
**Buyer:** VP of Property Estimating Solutions / Chief Data Officer
