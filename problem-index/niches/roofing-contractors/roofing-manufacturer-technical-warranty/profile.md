# Roofing Manufacturer Technical & Warranty Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/roofing-contractors|Roofing Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Approve contractors into certification programmes, inspect installations, issue system warranties, and adjudicate the warranty claims that follow — the manufacturers' technical apparatus that determines which contractors may offer a system warranty at all.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 technical services staff, field inspectors, warranty analysts, and product engineers
**Output:** Contractor certification and audit, installation inspections, warranty issuance and claim adjudication, product performance analysis, installation specifications
**Proprietary data:** Warranty claims joined to installing contractor, system, substrate, region and installation date across the entire installed base
**Clock:** Warranty terms and claim reporting
**Buyer:** VP of Technical Services
