# Hail & Wind Verification Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/roofing-contractors|Roofing Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Certify whether hail of a given size or wind of a given speed struck a specific address on a specific date — the evidence that decides whether storm damage is claimable, which Pass 1 identifies as the distinction driving significant residential roofing revenue.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 meteorologists, data scientists, and report analysts
**Output:** Address-level hail and wind verification reports, historical peril archives, storm footprints, forensic meteorology opinions
**Proprietary data:** Radar-derived peril estimates reprocessed into a continuous address-resolution archive, calibrated against ground reports
**Clock:** Claim filing deadlines and storm response windows
**Buyer:** Chief Meteorologist / VP of Data Products
