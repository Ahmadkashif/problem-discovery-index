# Property Condition & Roof Risk Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/roofing-contractors|Roofing Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Derive roof age, material, condition and hazard exposure at address level from imagery and records, and sell it to the insurers deciding whether to write or renew a policy — increasingly the layer that determines whether a roof is insurable at all.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-2,000 data operations staff, computer vision engineers, and modellers
**Output:** Roof condition and material scoring, replacement cost estimates, hazard exposure, non-renewal and inspection triggers
**Proprietary data:** National imagery and records assembly with condition attributes derived and maintained over years
**Clock:** Underwriting and renewal cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Property Data
