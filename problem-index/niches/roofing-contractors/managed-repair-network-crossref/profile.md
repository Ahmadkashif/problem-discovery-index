# Managed Repair Programme Administrators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/roofing-contractors|Roofing Contractors]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Run the carrier-preferred contractor networks that assign roofing claims, review scope and pricing, and guarantee the repair — the party a roofing contractor negotiates supplements with when working inside a programme.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,500 estimators, scope reviewers, network managers, and analysts
**Output:** Scope and estimate review, contractor assignment and scoring, cycle time and severity analytics, repair guarantees
**Proprietary data:** Claim scope, supplement and settlement histories joined to contractor performance at very large volume
**Clock:** Carrier cycle time targets and catastrophe surges
**Buyer:** VP of Claims Operations / Chief Analytics Officer
