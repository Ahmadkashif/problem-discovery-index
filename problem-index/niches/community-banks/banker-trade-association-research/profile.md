# Banker Trade Association Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-banks|Community Banks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Independent Community Bankers of America, the American Bankers Association and the state bankers' associations publish member surveys, economic commentary and policy research, and many state associations sell compliance hotlines and services. Research supports advocacy and member services rather than being sold as a product. Compliance hotline and interpretation content is part of the pocket profiled under Compliance Consulting as [[niches/compliance-consulting/regulatory-content-publishers/profile|🔍 Regulatory Intelligence Publishers]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 dedicated research staff at each association; the national associations' economics offices are the largest and sit near the threshold (estimate)
**Output:** Surveys, commentary and advocacy research
**Proprietary data:** Member survey responses
**Clock:** Legislative and rulemaking calendars
**Buyer:** Chief economist / head of research
