# Bank Compensation & Board Survey Consultants

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-banks|Community Banks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Consultancies and publishers that run executive, director and staff compensation surveys for community banks and advise boards on incentive plans, supplemental retirement plans and director pay, using peer data from survey participants and proxy statements.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 3-10 analysts on bank compensation per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Compensation surveys and board advisory reports
**Proprietary data:** Survey responses from participating banks
**Clock:** Annual budget and proxy cycles
**Buyer:** Practice leader
