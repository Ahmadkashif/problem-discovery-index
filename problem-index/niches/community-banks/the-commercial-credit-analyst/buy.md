# Spreading Software Adapted to the Borrower Family

**Niche:** [[niches/community-banks/the-commercial-credit-analyst/profile|The Commercial Credit Analyst]]
**Industry:** [[industries/community-banks|Community Banks]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Spreading tools already turn one entity's financial statements into ratios; small business credit needs the whole family of entities and guarantors spread as one structure that carries over year to year.
**Tags:** #graph-theory #large-language-models #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get an analyst from a borrower's raw returns to a credit memo the chief credit officer trusts in hours rather than days, and whoever makes every number traceable to its source and every senior edit reusable takes the account.

## The Problem
The analyst spreads an S corporation, its real estate holding LLC, a second operating company and two guarantors' personal returns separately, then reconciles them in a global cash flow workbook by hand, every year.

## What Already Exists
Abrigo, Moody's lending tools, nCino and Baker Hill spread financial statements and tax returns into standard templates with ratio analysis; several now add AI-assisted extraction of return line items.

## The Customization Gap
Analysts need: (1) an ownership graph linking entities, K-1s and guarantors, built once and carried forward; (2) automatic tracing of distributions, intercompany rent and notes so cash is not double counted; (3) the bank's own policy adjustments applied consistently; (4) source links from every spread figure to the return page and line; and (5) handling of mixed tax years when entities file on extension.

## Target Customer
Credit administration managers at community banks whose borrowers are predominantly pass-through small businesses.

## Impact If Solved
Global cash flow becomes a review task rather than a rebuild, and next year's annual review starts with last year's structure already mapped.
