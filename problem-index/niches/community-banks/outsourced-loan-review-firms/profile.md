# Outsourced Loan Review Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-banks|Community Banks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Most community banks too small for a full internal credit review department hire an outside firm to review a sample of their commercial loan book once or twice a year: re-underwriting files, testing whether each risk grade is supported, checking policy exceptions and documentation, and reporting grade disagreements to the board's audit or loan committee. The work is done by the financial institutions practices of regional CPA and consulting firms and by specialist loan review boutiques. Examiners read the loan review report and rely on it when sizing their own sample.

## Insight Function
**Size:** The ~10-60 reviewers per firm (estimate) re-underwrite sampled files at volume and are production staff, not counted for the gate; the analytics layer above them (review methodology, quality control of grades, portfolio trend reporting) is under ~10 per firm (estimate)
**Output:** The loan review report: grade changes recommended, file-level findings, portfolio trends, policy and administration observations
**Proprietary data:** None of its own; the files, grades and findings belong to the client bank
**Clock:** The bank's safety-and-soundness exam cycle (12 or 18 months), which the review is scheduled to precede
**Buyer:** Practice leader, financial institutions advisory
