# Managed Security Services Adapted to Bank Compliance

**Niche:** [[niches/community-banks/small-rural-banks/profile|Sub-$500M Rural & Single-Market Banks]]
**Industry:** [[industries/community-banks|Community Banks]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Managed security providers already run a small company's security operations as a shared service with a portal and monthly reports; small banks need the same model for compliance and BSA, shaped to what examiners ask for.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #large-language-models #compliance #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to be a small bank's outsourced second line (BSA, compliance, loan review and IT audit) under one evidence trail an examiner accepts as the bank's own function, and whoever the examiner stops writing up takes the account.

## The Problem
Small banks already buy managed IT and security services, because they cannot staff a security operations centre. They buy compliance and BSA help as periodic consulting instead, and get point-in-time reports rather than continuous coverage.

## What Already Exists
Managed security service providers, including bank-focused ones, run shared monitoring with tiered analysts, ticketing, a client portal and monthly reports mapped to the FFIEC cybersecurity expectations.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: (1) queues for BSA alerts, compliance calendar items, complaint handling and loan exceptions rather than security events; (2) staffing by certified BSA and compliance specialists; (3) reporting organised by exam area and regulation rather than by incident; (4) clear separation from the independent testing examiners require; and (5) board reporting that evidences the bank's oversight of the provider.

## Target Customer
Small banks already outsourcing IT and security that want one provider model for the rest of the second line.

## Impact If Solved
A proven shared-service model extended to the compliance functions that small banks struggle most to staff.
