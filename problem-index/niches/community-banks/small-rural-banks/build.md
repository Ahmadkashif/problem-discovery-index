# A Shared Second Line With Examiner-Grade Evidence

**Niche:** [[niches/community-banks/small-rural-banks/profile|Sub-$500M Rural & Single-Market Banks]]
**Industry:** [[industries/community-banks|Community Banks]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A small bank buys BSA review, compliance testing, loan review and IT audit from four firms that never talk to each other, and still has to show the examiner it oversees all of them.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #gradient-boosting #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation #ai-platform
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to be a small bank's outsourced second line (BSA, compliance, loan review and IT audit) under one evidence trail an examiner accepts as the bank's own function, and whoever the examiner stops writing up takes the account.

## The Problem
At a $250M bank, the second line of defence is part of two or three people's jobs plus a calendar of outside reviews. Each outside firm visits, samples, writes findings and leaves. Between visits, monitoring is thin: an alert queue, a compliance calendar, a spreadsheet of loan exceptions. Examiners expect the bank to own its risk management even when the work is outsourced, and small banks are routinely written up for oversight they cannot staff.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The services are sold by different kinds of firms (CPA firms, consultancies, associations, managed service providers) with different licensing and independence rules. Software vendors sell tools that need an operator the small bank does not have. A combined service requires both the people and the platform.

## What to Build
A platform-backed shared service for small banks: continuous monitoring of BSA alerts, compliance calendars, loan exceptions and IT controls on the bank's own data, with named specialists across many client banks working the queues, and one evidence trail organised by exam area that the bank's officers review and sign. Independent testing stays with a separate firm, as the rules require; the platform gives that firm a clean evidence trail to test.

## Target Customer
CEOs and boards of community banks under $500M, particularly after the departure of a key compliance or BSA officer.

## Impact If Built
Small banks get continuous second-line coverage at a shared cost, fewer oversight findings, and resilience against losing one person.
