# Deposit Placement & Reciprocal Deposit Networks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-banks|Community Banks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Networks that let a bank place a customer's large deposit across many banks in insured increments and receive matching deposits back, keeping the relationship while extending insurance coverage. IntraFi (ICS and CDARS) is dominant, with more than 3,000 participating institutions; listing services and brokered deposit platforms sit alongside. Usage surged after the 2023 bank failures. The networks publish bank surveys and deposit commentary but are paid for placement, not analysis.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-40 analytics and research staff (estimate)
**Output:** Network reporting to member banks, surveys and market commentary
**Proprietary data:** Placement flows and rates across thousands of banks, showing in near real time where large deposits move under stress
**Clock:** Quarter-ends and market stress events
**Buyer:** Chief strategy or data officer
