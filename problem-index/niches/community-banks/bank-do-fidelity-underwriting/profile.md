# Bank D&O & Fidelity Bond Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-banks|Community Banks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Insurers and specialist programme managers write directors' and officers' liability, financial institution bonds (fidelity and crime) and cyber cover for community banks, underwriting each bank from its Call Report, exam history, loan concentrations, management and claims. The analysis is priced into the premium.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-40 financial institutions underwriters per carrier or programme (estimate)
**Output:** Underwriting decisions and premiums
**Proprietary data:** Claims histories on bank D&O, fidelity and cyber losses
**Clock:** Policy renewal dates
**Buyer:** Head of financial institutions underwriting
