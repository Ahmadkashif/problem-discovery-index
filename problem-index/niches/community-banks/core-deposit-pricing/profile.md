# Core Deposit Franchise & Pricing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-banks|Community Banks]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell a bank's ALCO which deposit balances will stay, and at what rate, when the policy rate next moves, and whoever predicts beta and runoff by relationship rather than by product most accurately takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$900M a year in deposit pricing benchmarks, ALM deposit studies, deposit analytics and account-acquisition programmes bought by community banks (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of community bank technology, data and outside-services spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — rates are set from benchmark reports and ALM outputs, but the ALCO decision and exception pricing are made in meetings and at the branch
**Target Buyer:** CFO / ALCO chair
**Automation Potential:** High for estimation, moderate for the pricing decision

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Community banks fund themselves with local deposits, and the deposit franchise is most of what an acquirer pays a premium for. The 2022–2024 hiking cycle taught every CFO that the decay and beta assumptions in the interest rate risk model were wrong in both directions: some relationships never moved, others left in a week for a money market fund or a promotional CD across the street. Community bank net interest margin recovered to 3.77% in Q4 2025, the highest since 2018, largely on deposit cost discipline. Rate decisions are made monthly by ALCO and daily by branch managers granting exceptions. The competitors are pricing benchmark providers such as Curinos and RateWatch, the ALM modelling vendors that write the deposit assumptions, and deposit-analytics start-ups.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Deposit and loan pricing benchmarks from Curinos and S&P's RateWatch (the benchmark business is profiled as a Pass 2 pocket under Credit Unions: [[niches/credit-unions/deposit-loan-pricing-data/profile|🔍 Deposit & Loan Pricing Benchmark Data]]); deposit studies from ALM vendors such as ZM Financial and Darling Consulting Group; account-level analytics from core vendor reporting. Gaps: betas estimated by product, not relationship; exception pricing unrecorded; no link between a price decision and the balance that followed.
