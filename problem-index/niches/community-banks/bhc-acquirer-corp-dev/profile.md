# Serial Acquirer Corporate Development

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-banks|Community Banks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Community bank holding companies that grow by acquisition keep a small corporate development function, often the CFO and one or two analysts, that screens targets from Call Report data, models deals and runs integration with outside advisers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 2-8 (estimate)
**Output:** Internal target screens and deal models
**Proprietary data:** Target lists and prior diligence files
**Clock:** Deal timetables
**Buyer:** CEO / CFO
