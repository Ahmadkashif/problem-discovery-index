# Bank M&A Advisory & Fairness Opinions

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-banks|Community Banks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Investment banks and boutiques specialising in financial institutions (KBW within Stifel, Piper Sandler, Raymond James, Stephens, Janney, D.A. Davidson, Hovde and others) advise community banks on sales, acquisitions and mergers of equals, value the target, model pro forma capital and earnings, and deliver the fairness opinion summarised in the proxy. Bank M&A ran at its highest level since 2021 in 2025. The general fairness-opinion practice is a Pass 1 niche under Investment Banking Boutiques, [[niches/investment-banking-boutiques/fairness-and-valuation-opinions/profile|🟠 Fairness & Valuation Opinions]]; the credit union equivalent is [[niches/credit-unions/cu-merger-valuation-advisory/profile|🔍 Credit Union Merger & Valuation Advisory]] (42).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-100 bankers and analysts in each firm's depository institutions group (estimate)
**Output:** Valuation and pro forma analysis, buyer universe assessments, fairness opinions
**Proprietary data:** Private knowledge of buyer appetite and of deals that did not happen, plus target data received under NDA; completed deal terms are largely public
**Clock:** Merger agreement dates, regulatory approval timelines and shareholder meeting dates
**Buyer:** Head of financial institutions group
