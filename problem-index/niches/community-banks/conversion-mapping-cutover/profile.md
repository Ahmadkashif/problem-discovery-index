# Conversion Data Mapping & Cutover

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-banks|Community Banks]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to move every product, fee code, GL account and customer exception correctly on the first live weekend, and whoever's conversion gets through the first statement cycle without a complaint spike takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M a year in conversion and deconversion fees, conversion consulting and bank staff time on conversions (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~4% of community bank technology, data and outside-services spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — vendors' extract and load tools are automated; the business mapping and reconciliation are spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** COO / conversion project lead at the acquiring bank
**Automation Potential:** High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A conversion is a fixed-date, all-at-once migration: a weekend on which every account, balance, accrued interest figure, fee rule, card, online banking credential and statement setting moves to a new system, preceded by mock conversions and customer notices. Bank M&A in 2025 was the busiest since 2021 (S&P counted 76 announced deals in the first half and 105 in the second), and nearly every deal ends in one. What decides success is mapping accuracy and the capture of undocumented exceptions, not price, which is why it separates from contract renewal. The competitors are the core vendors' conversion teams and independent conversion consultancies.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Core vendors' conversion teams and tools, conversion consultancies, Excel mapping workbooks and reconciliation reports. Gaps: product mapping by name rather than economics; no library of prior maps for the bank; the acquired bank's exceptions captured by interview, if at all; reconciliation breaks investigated by hand.
