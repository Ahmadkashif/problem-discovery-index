# CMBS Surveillance Analytics Pointed at Portfolio Loans

**Niche:** [[niches/community-banks/cre-lending-concentration/profile|CRE Lending & Concentration Risk]]
**Industry:** [[industries/community-banks|Community Banks]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** CMBS investors already monitor every securitised CRE loan monthly against standard servicer data; community banks hold similar loans with none of the standardisation.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #time-series-forecasting #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show a CRE-concentrated bank's board and examiner, loan by loan, which properties will fail to refinance at today's rates before the maturity arrives, and whoever produces that answer from the bank's own rent rolls and maturity schedule fastest takes the account.

## The Problem
Community bank CRE monitoring runs on the annual review cycle and on Call Report concentration ratios. Between reviews the bank sees payments, not property performance.

## What Already Exists
CMBS surveillance platforms from Trepp and Moody's track securitised loans monthly through standard investor reporting packages: occupancy, NOI, debt service coverage, watch-list triggers and maturity dates. Trepp's T-ALLR consortium returns anonymised peer benchmarks to contributing banks, and CoStar supplies market rents and vacancies.

## The Customization Gap
Bank portfolio loans need: (1) ingestion of borrower-supplied rent rolls and operating statements in arbitrary formats, since there is no servicer report; (2) the bank's own grading scale and policy covenants in place of CMBS watch-list triggers; (3) guarantor global cash flow alongside the property, because community bank CRE is recourse lending; (4) owner-occupied versus investor classification consistent with Call Report definitions; and (5) output shaped as the concentration risk management package examiners request.

## Target Customer
Credit administration and portfolio management leaders at community banks with large investor CRE books.

## Impact If Solved
Monthly or quarterly property-level surveillance at a fraction of the cost of building it, using a method already accepted in the securitised market.
