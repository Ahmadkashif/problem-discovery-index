# CECL Allowance & Loss Data Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-banks|Community Banks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors that calculate community banks' allowance for credit losses under CECL (ASC 326): software plus advisory on methodology, segmentation, peer loss rates, economic forecasts and qualitative factors, with documentation for auditors and examiners. Abrigo is the largest community bank provider; Moody's, correspondent banks and accounting firms' tools serve others. Rate-risk models are logged separately under Credit Unions as [[niches/credit-unions/alm-risk-modeling-vendors/profile|🔍 Asset-Liability & Interest Rate Risk Modelling Vendors]], and the independent validators as [[niches/credit-unions/model-validation-firms/profile|🔍 Model Validation Firms]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~30-150 advisory, methodology and data staff at the larger vendors (estimate)
**Output:** Quarterly allowance calculations, methodology documentation, peer loss-rate data, qualitative factor support
**Proprietary data:** Loan-level loss and payment histories from thousands of client banks' portfolios across a full credit cycle
**Clock:** Quarter-end close and the Call Report due 30 days later; annual external audit
**Buyer:** Head of advisory services / Chief Product Officer

**Kill switches:** live — data isn't theirs. The batch test: client loan-level data held under a service agreement is a live kill switch unless checked evidence shows the agreement permits aggregate use. No such evidence was found for the CECL vendors (a search for Abrigo's pooled peer loss data returned nothing checkable), and the histories include consumer customer information under the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act. The models also fall under supervisory model risk expectations, as with the rate-risk vendors.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch (43/60 would also be below threshold)
