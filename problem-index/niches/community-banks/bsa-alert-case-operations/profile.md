# BSA/AML Alert & Case Operations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-banks|Community Banks]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to close the alerts that are noise without missing the one that becomes a SAR, and whoever can prove to an examiner that its deprioritised alerts were safe takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.1B a year in transaction monitoring, case management, sanctions screening, outsourced investigation and BSA staff time at community banks (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~11% of community bank technology, data and outside-services spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — detection is automated; investigation, documentation and SAR narratives are manual
**Target Buyer:** BSA Officer
**Automation Potential:** Very High for case assembly and drafting; the file-or-close decision stays human

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The Bank Secrecy Act requires every bank to monitor for suspicious activity and file a SAR within 30 days of determining activity is suspicious (60 at most if no suspect is identified), with an independent test of the programme typically every 12 to 18 months (estimate) and BSA reviewed at every safety-and-soundness exam. A community bank's BSA team is a few people facing an alert queue generated by rules and models tuned for false negatives, not false positives. The work is the most mechanical in the bank and the most dangerous to get wrong: a BSA consent order can halt an acquisition. The credit union version of the niche is profiled at [[niches/credit-unions/bsa-aml-compliance-ops/profile|⚡ BSA/AML Compliance Operations]]; the community bank version differs in its commercial customer mix (cash-intensive businesses, money services businesses of the kind analysed under [[industries/money-service-businesses|Money Service Businesses]], and for some banks fintech programmes run under a sponsor arrangement). The competitors are Verafin, Abrigo, the core vendors' AML modules and outsourced investigation firms.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Verafin (Nasdaq), Abrigo BAM+, Jack Henry and Fiserv AML modules, NICE Actimize at larger community banks; outsourced investigators for overflow. Gaps: case files assembled by hand from the core; dispositions documented in thin free text; alert tuning done by intuition with no measured evidence an examiner will accept.
