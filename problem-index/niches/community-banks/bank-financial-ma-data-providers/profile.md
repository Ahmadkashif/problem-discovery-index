# Bank Financial & M&A Data Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-banks|Community Banks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
S&P Global Market Intelligence (the former SNL Financial), FedFis and smaller providers turn quarterly Call Reports, holding company filings, branch deposit data and deal announcements into bank peer analytics, M&A deal databases with pricing multiples, and industry research. Bank executives, boards, M&A advisers, investors and regulators subscribe. The credit union equivalent is logged as [[niches/credit-unions/cu-performance-benchmark-data/profile|🔍 Credit Union Performance Analytics Providers]] (49).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~50-300 analysts, data collectors and journalists on bank coverage at the largest provider (estimate)
**Output:** Peer analytics, deal databases, research and news, the subscription
**Proprietary data:** Decades of normalised bank financial history, M&A deal terms and pricing, and editorial research layered on public filings
**Clock:** Call Reports due 30 days after quarter-end, public bank earnings dates, and deal announcements
**Buyer:** Head of financial institutions data and research
