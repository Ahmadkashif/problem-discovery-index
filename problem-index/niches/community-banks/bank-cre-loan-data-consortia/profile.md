# Bank CRE Loan Data Consortia

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-banks|Community Banks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Consortia in which commercial banks contribute loan-level commercial real estate performance data (balances, rates, maturities, risk grades, delinquency, losses) to a vendor that anonymises it and returns quarterly peer benchmarks by property type, geography and vintage. Trepp's T-ALLR is the main example. Banks use the benchmarks for CECL inputs, concentration risk reporting, stress testing and board packages. Trepp's CMBS research business is a separate pocket under Commercial Real Estate, [[niches/commercial-real-estate/cre-securities-research-firms/profile|🔍 CRE Investment Research Firms]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-30 analysts and data staff on the bank consortium products (estimate)
**Output:** Quarterly peer benchmark reports and loan-level comparisons
**Proprietary data:** Contributed bank portfolio loan performance that no regulator publishes at loan level and no CMBS dataset covers
**Clock:** Quarter-end CECL and Call Report cycles, and concentration reviews at exams
**Buyer:** Head of bank analytics products
