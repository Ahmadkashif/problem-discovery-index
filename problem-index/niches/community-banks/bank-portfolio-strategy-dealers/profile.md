# Bank Portfolio & Balance Sheet Strategy Dealers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-banks|Community Banks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Broker-dealers and advisory firms that specialise in community bank investment portfolios (Performance Trust, The Baker Group, ICBA Securities with its partners, depository strategy groups at larger dealers and others) provide portfolio analytics, interest rate risk and liquidity reports, bond swap and restructuring analyses, and strategy sessions for ALCO and the board. Much of the analysis is provided to win bond trades; some is sold as fee-based advisory.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-100 strategists and analysts per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Portfolio and balance sheet analytics, ALCO packages, swap proposals, strategy research
**Proprietary data:** Holdings and balance sheet data from hundreds of client banks, used for peer comparison and strategy research
**Clock:** Quarterly ALCO and board meetings, client-set
**Buyer:** Head of financial strategies
