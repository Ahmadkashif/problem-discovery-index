# Core Technology Contract Advisors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-banks|Community Banks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Advisory firms that benchmark and negotiate community banks' core processing, digital banking and payments contracts, and run core selections. Cornerstone Advisors (which says its pricing database holds more than 15,000 data points), Paladin fs and smaller independent consultancies are representative. The bank pays a fixed fee or a share of the savings because the advisor knows what peer banks pay and the bank does not.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-30 contract analysts and negotiators per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Pricing benchmark reports, negotiation strategy, selection scorecards, contract redlines
**Proprietary data:** Line-item pricing and contract terms from hundreds of client negotiations across all the major core vendors, which the vendors never publish
**Clock:** The client's contract renewal and notice dates, set by the vendor agreement rather than a regulator
**Buyer:** Managing director, contract negotiation practice
