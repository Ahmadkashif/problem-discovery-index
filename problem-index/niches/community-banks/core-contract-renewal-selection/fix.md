# Three Proposals on Three Different Unit Bases

**Niche:** [[niches/community-banks/core-contract-renewal-selection/profile|Core Contract Renewal & Vendor Selection]]
**Industry:** [[industries/community-banks|Community Banks]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Every core vendor prices its proposal on its own units and bundles, so the bank's selection team spends weeks rebuilding them into a comparison it still does not trust.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #feature-engineering #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to know what peer banks actually pay for the same core, digital and item-processing services before the bank's notice window closes, and whoever holds the deepest current pricing benchmark takes the account.

## The Problem
One vendor prices per account with digital banking bundled; another per account plus per item with digital separate; a third offers a flat monthly fee with volume bands. Each includes different services by default and lists the rest as options.

## Why It's Still Broken
Bundling makes comparison hard by design, and the selection team at a community bank does this once a decade.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A normaliser that extracts every priced line from each proposal, maps it to a standard service catalogue and unit, fills in the bank's actual volumes, and shows a like-for-like comparison with every assumption visible and every unpriced service flagged.

## Who Feels the Pain
COOs and CFOs running selections; boards asked to approve a decision they cannot check.

## Impact If Fixed
A trustworthy comparison in days instead of weeks, and negotiation focused on the lines that differ.
