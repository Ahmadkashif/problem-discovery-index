# Core Contract Renewal & Vendor Selection

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-banks|Community Banks]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to know what peer banks actually pay for the same core, digital and item-processing services before the bank's notice window closes, and whoever holds the deepest current pricing benchmark takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$150M a year in contract benchmarking, negotiation and core selection advisory fees paid by community banks (estimate), against several billion dollars of contract value
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1.5% of community bank technology, data and outside-services spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — proposals and contracts are PDFs, comparisons are spreadsheets, benchmarks are held privately by advisors
**Target Buyer:** CEO / CFO, with board approval
**Automation Potential:** Medium — normalisation and benchmarking automate; the negotiation is human

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
With three vendors serving most banks and pricing never published, a community bank negotiating a core renewal has one counterparty who knows every bank's price and one bank that knows its own. Advisors exist to close that gap: Cornerstone Advisors, for example, says its pricing database holds more than 15,000 data points from selections, renewals and benchmarks. The contest is information asymmetry on a contractual clock, which is why it separates from the conversion work that follows a decision. The competitors are independent advisors such as Cornerstone Advisors and Paladin fs, set against the core vendors' own pricing teams.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Independent advisors (Cornerstone Advisors, Paladin fs, and smaller firms) and the bank's own RFPs. Gaps: proposals priced on different units and bundles; total cost over the term rarely modelled with realistic volume growth; benchmarks available only through a paid engagement; deconversion costs and conversion risk left out of the comparison. The advisors themselves are logged as a Pass 2 pocket in this industry.
