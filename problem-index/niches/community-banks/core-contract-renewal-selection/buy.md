# Enterprise IT Sourcing Benchmarks Adapted to Bank Cores

**Niche:** [[niches/community-banks/core-contract-renewal-selection/profile|Core Contract Renewal & Vendor Selection]]
**Industry:** [[industries/community-banks|Community Banks]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Large enterprises benchmark outsourcing and software contracts against sourcing advisors' price databases; community banks need the same, normalised to banking's pricing units.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #k-nearest-neighbors #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to know what peer banks actually pay for the same core, digital and item-processing services before the bank's notice window closes, and whoever holds the deepest current pricing benchmark takes the account.

## The Problem
A community bank's core contract is often its largest vendor commitment, negotiated with little view of what similar banks pay.

## What Already Exists
Enterprise IT sourcing advisors and research firms run contract benchmarking against databases of deal terms for infrastructure outsourcing, application services and software licences, with peer selection by size and scope.

## The Customization Gap
Bank cores need: (1) units native to banking (accounts, items, transactions, digital users) with tier structures; (2) peer selection by asset size, account mix, core platform and service bundle; (3) separation of the core from bundled ancillary services so like is compared with like; (4) contract terms (escalators, deconversion formulas, term length) benchmarked alongside price; and (5) delivery at community bank budgets.

## Target Customer
Community bank CFOs who want a benchmark without a full advisory engagement.

## Impact If Solved
Benchmark-backed negotiation available to banks that cannot justify a large advisory fee.
