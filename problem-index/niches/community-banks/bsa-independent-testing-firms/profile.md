# BSA/AML Independent Testing Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-banks|Community Banks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Every bank's BSA/AML programme must be independently tested, typically every 12 to 18 months (estimate), by internal audit or an outside party. Community banks mostly hire outside firms: CPA firms' financial institutions practices, compliance consultancies and state bankers' association services. The tester samples alerts, dispositions, SARs, currency transaction reports and customer due diligence files, checks the monitoring system's rules and data feeds, and reports findings to the board. Examiners start their BSA review from the latest independent test. This is distinct from the consent-order lookbacks profiled under Compliance Consulting as [[niches/compliance-consulting/regulatory-remediation-firms/profile|🔍 Regulatory Remediation & Look-Back Firms]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** The ~10-50 testers per firm (estimate) sample files against the programme as production work, not counted for the gate; the analytical layer above them, transaction-monitoring model and threshold validation, is the AML model validation work already profiled under Credit Unions as [[niches/credit-unions/model-validation-firms/profile|🔍 Model Validation Firms]] (48, kill switch), and what remains is under ~10 per firm (estimate)
**Output:** The independent test report: findings, sample results, recommendations
**Proprietary data:** None of its own; the sampled files and SAR records belong to the client bank
**Clock:** The independent testing expectation and the BSA exam that follows it
**Buyer:** Practice leader, BSA/AML advisory
