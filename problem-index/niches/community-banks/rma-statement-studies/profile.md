# RMA Statement Studies & Credit Risk Association Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-banks|Community Banks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Risk Management Association, the professional body for bank credit risk staff, publishes the Annual Statement Studies: financial ratio benchmarks by industry compiled from borrower financial statements contributed by member banks (more than 182,000 statements across 627+ NAICS industries in the 2023–2024 edition). Every community bank credit analyst compares a borrower's ratios with these benchmarks. RMA also runs credit risk surveys, training and certification.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-25 research, data and product staff (estimate)
**Output:** Annual Statement Studies (print and online), surveys, credit risk research
**Proprietary data:** Decades of contributed commercial borrower financial statements by industry and size, which no other source compiles
**Clock:** Annual edition, self-set
**Buyer:** Head of products and research
