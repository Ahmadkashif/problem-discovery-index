# The Auto-Renewal Nobody Calendared

**Niche:** [[niches/community-banks/core-processing-conversions/profile|Core Processing & Conversions]]
**Industry:** [[industries/community-banks|Community Banks]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Core contracts renew automatically unless notice is given months ahead, and banks regularly discover the window closed after it did.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #compliance #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every competitor here is fighting over the bank's core relationship, but at renewal the contest is pricing leverage against a three-vendor market, won by whoever holds the best benchmark, and in a conversion the contest is first-time-right data mapping on a fixed weekend, won by whoever maps most accurately. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
A multi-year core agreement includes a renewal clause requiring written notice well before expiry (often six months to a year, estimate). Ancillary services added by amendment may have their own terms. Miss the window and the bank renews on existing terms, losing its one moment of leverage.

## Why It's Still Broken
The dates are in contract schedules filed by whoever signed them, executives change, and vendor management tracks risk reviews rather than commercial dates.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Extract every term, renewal, notice and escalator date from the agreement and amendments into the bank's calendar, with alerts to the CEO and board at 24, 18 and 12 months before the notice deadline and a checklist for starting a benchmark or selection in time.

## Who Feels the Pain
CEOs and CFOs who discover the deadline late; boards approving renewals without alternatives.

## Impact If Fixed
The bank keeps its negotiating window, which is where most core savings are won.
