# Banking Agency Community Bank Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-banks|Community Banks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The research and offsite-monitoring units of the FDIC, the Federal Reserve and the OCC, together with the Conference of State Bank Supervisors, study community banks as a sector: the FDIC's Quarterly Banking Profile community bank section and its periodic Community Banking Study, the annual CSBS community bank survey and the research conference it runs with the Federal Reserve, and the offsite models that flag banks for supervisory attention between exams. Examination staff themselves are logged under Compliance Consulting as [[niches/compliance-consulting/federal-regulator-examination-staff/profile|🔍 Regulatory Examination Staff]], and the credit union regulator's equivalent as [[niches/credit-unions/ncua-economic-research/profile|🔍 Federal Credit Union Regulator Research]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** Counted per body: ~20-60 economists and analysts in the FDIC's research and offsite-monitoring functions (estimate), which alone clears the gate; the Federal Reserve's, the OCC's and CSBS's community bank research groups are each under ~10-20 (estimate) and are not summed
**Output:** Sector statistics and studies, survey reports, offsite risk models
**Proprietary data:** Every insured bank's Call Reports plus confidential examination ratings and findings
**Clock:** Quarterly data releases and exam cycles
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer
