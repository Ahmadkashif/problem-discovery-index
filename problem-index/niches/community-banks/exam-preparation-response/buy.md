# Audit Evidence Platforms Adapted to Bank Exams

**Niche:** [[niches/community-banks/exam-preparation-response/profile|Examination Preparation & Response]]
**Industry:** [[industries/community-banks|Community Banks]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Evidence-collection platforms already automate SOC 2 and ISO audits for software companies; a bank exam is the same shape of problem with a different framework and a regulator as the auditor.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #compliance #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to have a bank's evidence for every request-letter item located, current and mapped before the letter arrives, and whoever turns the examination from a document hunt into a retrieval most completely takes the account.

## The Problem
Banks prepare for exams the way software companies prepared for security audits before continuous evidence platforms: a spreadsheet of requests and a scramble.

## What Already Exists
Continuous compliance platforms analysed under [[industries/grc-compliance-platforms|GRC & Compliance Platforms]] collect evidence against control frameworks, map it to auditor requests, and track remediation; bank-focused suites such as Ncontracts manage policies, vendor files and findings.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: (1) a request taxonomy built from FFIEC exam manuals and actual request letters by exam type, rather than a control framework; (2) connectors to bank systems (core report writers, board portals, ALM and CECL vendor outputs, loan review reports) instead of cloud consoles; (3) version and approval-date tracking for board-approved policies; (4) finding-to-remediation tracking in the form boards report on; and (5) upload packaging for the regulators' secure portals.

## Target Customer
Community banks that already license a compliance management suite and want exam response inside it.

## Impact If Solved
A proven evidence-automation pattern brought to a buyer that still answers request letters by hand.
