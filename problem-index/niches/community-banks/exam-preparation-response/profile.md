# Examination Preparation & Response

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-banks|Community Banks]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to have a bank's evidence for every request-letter item located, current and mapped before the letter arrives, and whoever turns the examination from a document hunt into a retrieval most completely takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600M a year in exam-preparation consulting, mock exams, compliance management software and staff time attributable to exams at community banks (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~6% of community bank technology, data and outside-services spend (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — evidence lives in shared drives, board portals and inboxes; the request letter is answered by hand
**Target Buyer:** CEO, CFO or compliance officer (whoever coordinates the exam)
**Automation Potential:** High for retrieval and tracking, low for the management response

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A community bank faces safety-and-soundness exams every 12 or 18 months (18 for well-rated banks under $3B since 2018), plus consumer compliance, CRA, BSA, IT and trust exams on their own cycles, alternating between federal and state regulators. Each begins with a request letter of a hundred or more items with a due date, and ends with a report of examination whose findings (matters requiring attention, recommendations, or worse) the board must remediate and report on. The exam rating sets deposit insurance premiums, constrains growth and M&A, and is the most consequential external judgement the bank receives. The work is document assembly under a regulator's deadline, distinct from compliance operations themselves. The competitors are bank compliance suites such as Ncontracts, GRC platforms moving into banking, and the consultancies that run mock exams.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Compliance and vendor management suites aimed at community banks (Ncontracts is the most common), board portals, generic document management, and the consultants who run mock exams (see [[industries/compliance-consulting|Compliance Consulting Firms]]). Gaps: no index of what the bank already holds against what examiners ask for; open findings tracked in board minutes; the same documents assembled separately for the exam, the external audit and loan review.
