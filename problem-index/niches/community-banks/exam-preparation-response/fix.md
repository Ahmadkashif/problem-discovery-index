# Findings Tracked in the Board Minutes

**Niche:** [[niches/community-banks/exam-preparation-response/profile|Examination Preparation & Response]]
**Industry:** [[industries/community-banks|Community Banks]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Exam, audit and loan review findings are recorded in three different reports and tracked in board minutes, so a repeat finding is often the first sign that remediation stalled.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #compliance #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to have a bank's evidence for every request-letter item located, current and mapped before the letter arrives, and whoever turns the examination from a document hunt into a retrieval most completely takes the account.

## The Problem
The report of examination lists matters requiring attention with expected corrective actions. The external auditor, internal audit and loan review each add their own findings. Management promises dates; the board receives updates in narrative form. A year later, the examiner asks for evidence that each was fixed.

## Why It's Still Broken
Findings come from different sources in different formats, owners change, and nothing ties a finding to the evidence of its closure. Repeat findings are treated seriously by examiners precisely because they show the tracking failed.

## What a Fix Looks Like
One findings register fed by every report: each finding extracted, assigned an owner and date, linked to remediation evidence as it is produced, and summarised for the board monthly with ageing. Before each exam, the register produces the status package the examiner will ask for.

## Who Feels the Pain
Compliance officers and CFOs chasing owners; boards reading narrative updates; management facing repeat findings.

## Impact If Fixed
Fewer repeat findings, a board that can see remediation status at a glance, and exam preparation that starts with the hardest question already answered.
