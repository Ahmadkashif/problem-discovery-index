# Bankers' Banks & Correspondent Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-banks|Community Banks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Bankers' banks (owned by the community banks they serve, such as TIB, PCBB and the regional bankers' banks) and correspondent divisions of larger banks sell community banks settlement, liquidity, loan participations and a growing set of analytics services: ALM and CECL modelling, liquidity stress tests, investment portfolio accounting and economic commentary. They are the closest thing community banks have to a shared back office.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-30 analysts in balance sheet and risk advisory services per institution (estimate)
**Output:** ALM, CECL and liquidity reports, participation credit analysis, commentary
**Proprietary data:** Balance sheet and participation data across their member banks
**Clock:** Quarterly ALCO and Call Report cycles
**Buyer:** Head of balance sheet advisory
