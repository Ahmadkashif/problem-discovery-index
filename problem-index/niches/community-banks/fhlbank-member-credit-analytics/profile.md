# FHLBank Member Credit & Collateral Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/community-banks|Community Banks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The Federal Home Loan Banks lend to member institutions, most of them community banks, against pledged collateral. Each runs a member credit team that rates members from Call Reports and other data to set borrowing capacity and collateral terms, and a collateral team that values pledged loan pools, sometimes at loan level. The analysis protects the FHLBank and is not sold.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-40 credit and collateral analysts per FHLBank (estimate)
**Output:** Internal member credit ratings, collateral haircuts and borrowing capacity
**Proprietary data:** Pledged loan-level collateral files and member credit histories across a district
**Clock:** Quarterly member reviews and collateral reporting cycles
**Buyer:** Chief risk officer (internal)
