**Contested on:** Whether verification methodology can be applied by a party whose revenue depends on the answer.

## The Problem

Verifying that a stated arrangement matches the actual one is exactly what audit and assurance practice exists to do. Sampling, evidence hierarchies, corroboration from independent sources, inquiry of parties other than management, analytical procedures to detect anomalies, and documented working papers are all established method with professional standards behind them.

The method assumes independence. The auditor is engaged by someone other than the party whose assertions are being tested, is professionally obliged to report adversely, and has no commercial interest in the conclusion. A platform verifying its own client's engagement has none of that, which is the whole difficulty.

## What Already Exists

Audit methodology and its professional standards. Internal audit practice and tooling. Continuous controls monitoring. Third-party risk assessment frameworks. Contractor compliance audit services from the accounting firms. Whistleblowing and inquiry mechanisms. Analytical review techniques for anomaly detection.

## The Customization Gap

**Independence has to be constructed or bought.** A determination verified by the party selling the service carries limited weight. The credible structures are an independent review function inside the platform with a reporting line that is not commercial, a third-party verifier engaged by the client, or periodic external assurance over the platform's own process — and choosing among them is a business model decision.

**Inquiry of the worker is the key procedure and the most delicate.** Audit's practice of inquiring beyond management maps directly onto asking the worker rather than the client. The adaptation is the protection: the worker's honest answer must not cost them their engagement, which audit methodology has no equivalent of because employees are not usually at risk from the audit.

**Analytical procedures are available from platform data and underused.** Audit's analytical review — looking for patterns inconsistent with the stated position — maps perfectly onto payment regularity, renewal patterns, exclusivity and duration. This is the cheapest procedure available here and requires no contact with anyone.

**The reporting obligation has to be defined in advance.** An auditor's duty to report adversely is professional. A platform's is contractual and unspecified, which means what happens on an adverse finding must be written into the service agreement before the first finding, or it will be negotiated under pressure.

**Continuous beats periodic here.** Audit is periodic by tradition. Engagement drift is continuous, and the monitoring should be too — closer to continuous controls monitoring than to an annual audit.

## Target Customer

Platforms building a verification function and needing an independence structure that is credible. Also the accounting and advisory firms, for whom contractor compliance verification is an existing service line that platform data would transform, and clients' internal audit functions.

## Impact If Solved

The sampling, evidence hierarchy, inquiry-beyond-management, analytical review and working paper discipline get borrowed, and the constructed independence, worker protection, platform-data analytics, pre-agreed reporting obligation and continuous cadence get built. Concretely: a verification that means something because the party doing it is not the party being paid to say yes.
