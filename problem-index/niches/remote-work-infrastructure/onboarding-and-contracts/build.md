# Build: Contracts Generated From the Determination

**Niche:** [[niches/remote-work-infrastructure/onboarding-and-contracts/profile|Onboarding, Contracts & Documentation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/remote-work-infrastructure|Remote Work Infrastructure]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Generate the contract from the determination's conclusions and the current jurisdictional rules, so the document cannot contradict the analysis it rests on.
**Tags:** #compliance #large-language-models #data-integration #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #workflow-orchestration #automation #descriptive-statistics
**Contested on:** Whether contract generation can be driven by a structured determination rather than by a template selection.

## The Problem

The contract is the artefact that gets examined. When an authority, a tribunal or a client's counsel looks at an engagement, they read the contract first — and it is generated by selecting a country template and filling in variables.

Two failure modes follow. The template may be stale: maintained by the same manual reading process as everything else in this industry, it can lag a legislative change by months, and every engagement created in that window carries the defect. And the template may not match the determination: a specialist concluded that this engagement requires employment treatment with particular terms, and the document produced is the standard contractor template for that country with the standard clauses.

Both are silent. Nothing compares the contract produced to the determination made, and nothing records which template version an engagement used.

## What to Build

Contract generation driven by the determination, with versioning throughout.

**Generate from the determination's conclusions.** The engagement type, the applicable statutory terms, the notice period, the entitlements, the termination provisions — as determined — flowing into the document, rather than a template selected by country. Where the determination concluded something the standard template does not reflect, the document should follow the determination.

**Version templates with effective dates.** Each template as an artefact with a version, an effective date and a link to the rule versions it implements. When a rule changes, the affected templates are flagged, and every engagement created under a superseded version is identifiable.

**Record the template version on the engagement.** Which template, which version, which rule versions, which determination — stored with the engagement permanently. This is the field that makes a later remediation possible and it is almost universally absent.

**Check the generated document against the determination.** An automated comparison — does the notice period in the contract match the one determined, is the engagement type consistent, are the statutory minimums reflected. A discrepancy is a generation defect and should block issuance. This check is cheap and catches the silent divergence.

**Flag clauses that are unenforceable locally.** Clients frequently request terms from their home jurisdiction — non-competes of a certain length, at-will provisions, IP assignments — that are void or restricted in the worker's country. Screening requested clauses against jurisdictional constraints at generation time prevents a contract that says something it cannot do.

**Remediate systematically when a template was wrong.** Given the version record, every affected engagement is a query, and the remediation is a bounded project rather than an archaeology exercise.

**Give the worker the document explained.** The contract plus a plain-language summary of the key terms in their language — notice, entitlements, termination, restrictions — which costs nothing to generate and is the difference between a signed document and an understood one.

## Target Customer

Platform legal and operations leadership, for whom a defective template propagated across hundreds of engagements is the highest-multiplier error the organisation can make. Also clients' legal functions reviewing the documents their workers are engaged under.

## Impact If Built

The contract follows the determination rather than a template selection, so the document and the analysis cannot silently diverge. Template versions are recorded per engagement, which makes remediation a query. Unenforceable clauses get caught at generation. And the worker receives terms they can understand.
