# Classification & Compliance Determination

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/remote-work-infrastructure|Remote Work Infrastructure]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Whether the green tick is a determination the customer can evaluate or an assertion they must accept.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.16B — 27% of US employer-of-record, global payroll and contractor management spend
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~27%
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — specialists, internal notes and a status indicator
**Target Buyer:** Clients' legal, tax and finance functions; platform compliance leadership
**Automation Potential:** High for the rule side; the fact side is a commercial decision

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche

A green tick says this engagement is compliant in this country, the determination behind it was a specialist reading an internal note of unknown vintage, and the customer carries the residual risk.

That is the product. Everything else these platforms do — payroll, payments, onboarding, documentation — is execution infrastructure that works. What the customer is buying is the determination: that this person can be engaged this way here, that no permanent establishment arises, that the benefits and termination terms satisfy local law.

The determination is a legal judgement applied to facts, made by a person, from guidance maintained by hand across dozens of jurisdictions, and presented as a settled status. When it is wrong the consequence arrives years later from a tax or labour authority and lands substantially on the client.

## Current Tools & Gaps

Internal knowledge bases maintained by compliance teams. Local counsel relationships in each jurisdiction. Contract templates per country. Classification questionnaires completed by the client at engagement setup. Status indicators in the product. Insurance and indemnity provisions of varying strength.

The gaps are provenance and verification. The rule applied, its source and the date it was last checked are not surfaced. The facts the rule was applied to come from the client's own description and are never tested against the engagement as it actually operates. And the platform's own history — the determinations it has made, the disputes it has handled, the outcomes — is not used to improve either.
