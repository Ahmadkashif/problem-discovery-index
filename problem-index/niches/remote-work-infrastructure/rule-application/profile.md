# Rule Application

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/remote-work-infrastructure|Remote Work Infrastructure]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Whether the platform has the right legal rule, current, traceable and applied consistently across every determination it makes.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.30B — 60% of the classification and determination niche
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~16% of US remote work infrastructure spend
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — a wiki and a team of readers
**Target Buyer:** Platform compliance leadership; regulatory intelligence vendors
**Automation Potential:** High — versioning, currency tracking and consistency checking all automate

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche

One team maintains the employment, tax and benefit rules for every country the platform operates in, by reading, and a green tick in the product depends on them having read the right thing in time.

This half is internal knowledge work. Which rule applies in this jurisdiction, what it currently says, where it came from, when it was last verified, and whether the same facts produce the same determination when two specialists look at them on two days.

The distinguishing property is that nobody opposes improving it. The specialist wants it — they are the one holding dozens of jurisdictions in their head. The platform's own exposure falls. The client gets a better answer at no cost to the commercial relationship. And it requires conceding nothing to anyone: a better rule base is strictly better for every party.

It is also self-sufficient. A versioned, dated, sourced rule base is valuable the day it exists, whether or not the determinations built on it are ever shown to a customer.

## Current Tools & Gaps

Internal wikis and knowledge bases. Local counsel relationships providing advice per question. Regulatory change monitoring subscriptions. Country guides from the major law firms. Contract templates per jurisdiction. Specialist expertise held personally.

The gaps are structure, currency and consistency. Rules are prose rather than structured objects, so they cannot be applied systematically or checked for conflict. Currency is untracked. And nobody measures whether two specialists given the same facts reach the same determination — the most basic quality measure of a judgement function, and unknown here as everywhere else in this vault's adjudication niches.
