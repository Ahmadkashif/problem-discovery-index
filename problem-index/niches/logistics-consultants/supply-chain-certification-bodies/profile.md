# Supply Chain Standards & Certification Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/logistics-consultants|Logistics Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
ASCM (the former APICS) maintains the SCOR Digital Standard, the process reference model many consultants use to structure supply chain assessments, and runs the CPIM and CSCP certifications — more than 100,000 people have earned CPIM since 1973 and more than 30,000 the CSCP since 2006. CSCMP publishes the annual State of Logistics report, written by Kearney. Their output is a body of knowledge, exams and benchmarks sold to practitioners; the broader association benchmarking pocket is logged under [[niches/warehouse-3pl/logistics-association-research/profile|Logistics Industry Research & Benchmarking]] (Warehouse & 3PL, 33).

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-60 content, standards and exam development staff (estimate)
**Output:** SCOR standard, certification curricula and exams, practice research
**Proprietary data:** Exam item banks and candidate performance data; member survey responses
**Clock:** Self-set exam and publication cycles
**Buyer:** Chief learning officer / VP content
