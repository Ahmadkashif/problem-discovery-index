# Savings Reported at Award, Not at Payment

**Niche:** [[niches/logistics-consultants/transportation-procurement/profile|Transportation Procurement & Bid Management]]
**Industry:** [[industries/logistics-consultants|Logistics Consultants]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A bid's savings are announced on the day of award and never compared with what the shipper actually paid after tender rejections, spot loads and accessorials.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #causal-inference #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #revenue-impact #quick-win
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor is fighting to lower a shipper's contracted freight cost, but truckload is won by designing awards carriers keep honouring when the market turns, and parcel and LTL by knowing what comparable shippers have been conceded. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
The award memo multiplies new rates by last year's volumes and reports a saving. During the contract year, carriers reject tenders when spot rates rise, loads move to backups and the spot market at higher rates, parcel surcharges rise outside the headline rate change, and LTL reweighs and reclasses add charges. The paid cost diverges from the award, and the award figure is the one in the procurement team's results.

## Why It's Still Broken
Award and payment data live in different systems — the sourcing tool and the freight audit or accounts payable file — owned by different teams. The consultant who reported the saving has left by the time the payment data exists.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A quarterly reconciliation that joins paid invoices to the awarded rate for each shipment, splits the variance into volume and mix, tender rejection and spot premium, accessorials and surcharges, and rate changes, and reports realised against awarded savings by mode and carrier.

## Who Feels the Pain
Transportation directors whose reported savings do not appear in the P&L; CFOs who stop trusting procurement numbers; consultants whose next bid is judged on the gap.

## Impact If Fixed
Bids judged on paid cost rather than award, which changes how awards are designed and which carriers keep the freight.
