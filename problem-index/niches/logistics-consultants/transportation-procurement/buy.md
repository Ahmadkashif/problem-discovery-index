# Strategic Sourcing Suites Adapted to Freight

**Niche:** [[niches/logistics-consultants/transportation-procurement/profile|Transportation Procurement & Bid Management]]
**Industry:** [[industries/logistics-consultants|Logistics Consultants]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Strategic sourcing suites run competitive events for any category; freight needs lanes, fuel tables, accessorials and capacity commitments the generic event model does not hold.

**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor is fighting to lower a shipper's contracted freight cost, but truckload is won by designing awards carriers keep honouring when the market turns, and parcel and LTL by knowing what comparable shippers have been conceded. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Procurement departments already license a sourcing suite for indirect spend and want freight run through it. A freight bid is not a list of items with unit prices: each lane has volume, equipment, service requirements and a rate structure, and the award must respect carrier capacity, incumbent preferences and backup coverage.

## What Already Exists
Coupa and Jaggaer sourcing suites run RFx events and award optimisation across categories; Keelvar specialises in sourcing optimisation and automates freight events, and integrated DAT market rates in 2024.

## The Customization Gap
The freight version needs: lane files with origin and destination zones, equipment and volume by week; fuel surcharge tables normalised to a common base; accessorial schedules priced into total cost; capacity commitments per carrier per lane and week; LTL proposals expressed as discount-off-tariff rather than lane rates; benchmark comparison per lane; and award outputs that load into the TMS routing guide rather than a purchase order.

## Target Customer
Shipper procurement teams moving freight into their enterprise sourcing suite, and consultants running bids on the client's licence.

## Impact If Solved
Freight sourced on the client's existing platform without a parallel Excel process, and awards that load straight into execution.
