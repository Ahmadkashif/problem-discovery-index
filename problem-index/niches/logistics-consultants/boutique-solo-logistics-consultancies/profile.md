# Boutique & Solo Logistics Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/logistics-consultants|Logistics Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Former shipper, carrier and 3PL managers working alone or in teams of a few, selling transportation assessments, small network studies, warehouse walk-throughs and bid support, mostly to mid-market shippers — the bulk of NAICS 541614, where 5,724 of 7,630 firms had fewer than five employees in 2022.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 1-15
**Output:** Assessments and studies, written by the principal
**Proprietary data:** The principal's own experience and client files
**Clock:** Client-set
**Buyer:** Principal
