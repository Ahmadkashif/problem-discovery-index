# Freight Classification Standards Body

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/logistics-consultants|Logistics Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The National Motor Freight Traffic Association maintains the National Motor Freight Classification, the class system LTL carriers price against, through a docket process run by its classification board, and sells access to it by subscription through its ClassIT tools. Docket 2025-1, effective 19 July 2025, moved many items to density-based classes on a 13-subprovision scale — the change that invalidated historical class assumptions in every LTL study logistics consultants run.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-30 classification analysts and staff (estimate)
**Output:** Classification dockets and decisions, the NMFC itself, ClassIT subscriptions
**Proprietary data:** Density and handling studies, carrier-submitted shipment data and the full docket history
**Clock:** Its own docket calendar — binding on the market, but self-set
**Buyer:** Executive director / head of classification
