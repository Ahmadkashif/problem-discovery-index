# Truckload Bid Design & Routing Guide Performance

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/logistics-consultants|Logistics Consultants]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to design truckload awards that carriers keep accepting when the spot market turns, and whoever's routing guides hold the highest primary tender acceptance through the contract year takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$700M US consulting and managed-bid fees for truckload and intermodal procurement (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% of US logistics and process consulting fees (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High for the event, Medium for the follow-through — tender acceptance data exists in the TMS but is rarely fed back into bid design
**Target Buyer:** Director of Transportation
**Automation Potential:** High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A truckload award is a promise the carrier can break. The shipper tenders each load to the primary carrier at the contracted rate; when spot rates rise above contract, primary carriers reject more tenders and loads cascade to backups, brokers and the spot market. The value of a bid is therefore not the rate on award day but how much freight moves at that rate through the year. The winning consultant designs lane packages, carrier mixes and award depth around predicted carrier behaviour, and decides when a lane needs a mini-bid rather than waiting for the annual event. The clock is the contract expiration, which is the shipper's own schedule.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Keelvar, Coupa and Transporeon run events; DAT and other benchmarks price lanes; TMS platforms log every tender and response; visibility platforms (project44, FourKites) record what carriers actually ran. Gaps: tender logs not joined to bid design, carriers awarded lanes they do not operate, and mini-bids triggered late. The bid-consultancy layer is analysed as a Pass 2 pocket at [[niches/freight-brokerage/freight-procurement-consultancies/profile|Freight Procurement & Bid Consultancies]]; this sub-niche covers the shipper-side contest.
