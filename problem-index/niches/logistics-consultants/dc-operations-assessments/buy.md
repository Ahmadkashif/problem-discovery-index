# Field Inspection Apps Adapted to Warehouse Diagnostics

**Niche:** [[niches/logistics-consultants/dc-operations-assessments/profile|DC Operations Assessments]]
**Industry:** [[industries/logistics-consultants|Logistics Consultants]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Mobile inspection apps already capture photos, checklists and notes on site; they cannot connect an observation to the task data that prices it.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #descriptive-statistics #feature-engineering #data-integration #worker-facing #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to find, in a two-to-five-day visit, the few constraints that actually cap a building's throughput, and whoever turns a walk-through into a quantified, defensible list fastest wins the implementation work that follows.

## The Problem
Assessors who use any app at all use general inspection tools built for safety audits and site checks. Their output is a list of photographed observations, which then has to be re-keyed into a spreadsheet, matched to the client's WMS reports and priced.

## What Already Exists
Mobile inspection and audit apps such as SafetyCulture, with configurable checklists, photo capture, issue assignment and reporting.

## The Customization Gap
Warehouse diagnostics need: a checklist and observation taxonomy keyed to warehouse process steps (receiving, put-away, replenishment, picking, packing, loading); ingestion of a WMS task extract so each observation can be checked against the zone's actual activity; standard quantification formulas for travel, idle time and touches; comparison of the building's measured metrics with WERC DC Measures benchmarks (36 metrics in 2026); and a findings report formatted as a prioritised savings case rather than an audit log.

## Target Customer
Small and mid-size operations consultancies, and in-house continuous improvement teams at multi-site distributors.

## Impact If Solved
An existing app the assessor already knows becomes a diagnostic instrument, cutting the re-keying and quantification work after each visit without a new platform.
