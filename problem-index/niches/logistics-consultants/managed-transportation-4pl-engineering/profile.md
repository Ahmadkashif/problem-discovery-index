# Managed Transportation & 4PL Network Engineering

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/logistics-consultants|Logistics Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Managed transportation and fourth-party logistics providers take over a shipper's freight management — procurement, routing guides, tendering, freight payment — and staff network engineering teams that run the optimisation studies a consultant would otherwise sell, paid through a management fee or a share of savings. They are the operational alternative to hiring a logistics consultant, and the reason this industry's boundary stops at advice: the providers run the freight, which places them with [[industries/freight-brokerage|Freight Brokerage]] and [[industries/warehouse-3pl|Warehouse & 3PL]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 network engineers, procurement analysts and data scientists at the larger providers (estimate)
**Output:** Continuous network and mode optimisation, bid events, routing guide management, savings reporting
**Proprietary data:** Execution records — tenders, acceptances, invoices — across every shipper under management
**Clock:** Shipper contract terms and bid cycles
**Buyer:** VP Network Engineering / Head of Managed Solutions
