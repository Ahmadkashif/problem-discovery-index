# Lean Management Software Adapted to Consultant-Led Rollouts

**Niche:** [[niches/logistics-consultants/lean-process-improvement/profile|Lean & Process Improvement Engagements]]
**Industry:** [[industries/logistics-consultants|Logistics Consultants]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Improvement-management platforms track ideas and projects inside one organisation; a consultant rolling a programme across sites needs templates, baselines and a clean handover.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show, from the plant's own MES, ERP or WMS records, that the targeted measure — units per labour hour, changeover minutes or dock-to-stock time — is still holding its promised gain over the pre-engagement baseline twelve months after handover, and whoever can evidence that twelve-month hold takes the client's next site.

## The Problem
A consultancy running a lean programme across eight plants rebuilds its event templates, A3 formats and KPI definitions in each plant's tools, or keeps them in its own spreadsheets, which the plant loses at handover. Results from one site cannot be compared with the next because each defined its baseline differently.

## What Already Exists
Improvement-management platforms such as KaiNexus, which capture improvement ideas, projects, A3s and impact for a single organisation's continuous improvement programme.

## The Customization Gap
Consultant-led rollouts need: a library of event templates and KPI definitions the consultancy brings to each client; baseline capture from plant systems before the first event, defined the same way at every site; a twelve-month holding view per improvement showing the measure against that baseline, comparable across sites, for the client's operational excellence leader; and a handover package that leaves the plant owning the standards, the KPIs and the monitoring once the consultant leaves.

## Target Customer
Lean consultancies running multi-site programmes, and client operational excellence teams who license the platform themselves.

## Impact If Solved
Results become comparable across sites, and the programme survives the consultant's exit because the plant inherits a working system rather than a binder.
