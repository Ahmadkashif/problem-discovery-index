# Sustainment Tracking Joined to the Plant's Own Data

**Niche:** [[niches/logistics-consultants/lean-process-improvement/profile|Lean & Process Improvement Engagements]]
**Industry:** [[industries/logistics-consultants|Logistics Consultants]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A kaizen event improves a line's changeover or output in a week, and nobody notices when the gain quietly slides back three months later.
**Tags:** #change-point-detection #time-series-forecasting #hypothesis-testing #descriptive-statistics #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show, from the plant's own MES, ERP or WMS records, that the targeted measure — units per labour hour, changeover minutes or dock-to-stock time — is still holding its promised gain over the pre-engagement baseline twelve months after handover, and whoever can evidence that twelve-month hold takes the client's next site.

## The Problem
At the end of an event the team reports a before-and-after — for example, changeover down from ninety minutes to forty (illustrative). The before figure was timed by hand during the week; the after figure is the best day of the event. Over the following months staff turn over, the standard work sheet goes missing and the old habits return. The plant notices only when the annual numbers disappoint, and the consultant's claimed results are by then unverifiable in either direction.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Consultants are paid for events, not for monitoring; plants own the MES and ERP data and rarely give outside firms continuing access. Lean practice also prizes on-the-floor observation over dashboards, so the trade has not pushed for system-based tracking.

## What to Build
A monitoring service that, for each improvement, defines the KPI and pulls it from the plant's own systems — MES cycle and changeover times, ERP output and labour, quality defect rates — establishes the baseline from the months of history before the event rather than a stopwatch during it, and watches the series afterwards with change-point detection that alerts when a gain starts to reverse. Each alert goes to the plant owner of the process and, by agreement, to the consultant, and at twelve months the service issues a holding report: the measure — units per labour hour, changeover minutes, dock-to-stock time — against the pre-engagement baseline, with the share of the promised gain still in place.

## Target Customer
Operational excellence leaders at multi-site manufacturers and distributors, and the lean consultancies that serve them.

## Impact If Built
Regression is caught in weeks rather than at year-end, and a consultancy can hand the next client a twelve-month holding report from its last site, drawn from that plant's own systems — the evidence that wins the next one.
