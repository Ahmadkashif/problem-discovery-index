# Lean & Process Improvement Engagements

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/logistics-consultants|Logistics Consultants]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show, from the plant's own MES, ERP or WMS records, that the targeted measure — units per labour hour, changeover minutes or dock-to-stock time — is still holding its promised gain over the pre-engagement baseline twelve months after handover, and whoever can evidence that twelve-month hold takes the client's next site.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3.0B US consulting fees for lean, kaizen, standard work and operations-improvement engagements in plants and distribution operations (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of US logistics and process consulting fees (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — value-stream maps are drawn on brown paper with sticky notes, gemba walks are recorded on clipboards, and results are tracked in spreadsheets kept by the plant
**Target Buyer:** Plant Manager, VP Operational Excellence
**Automation Potential:** Medium — KPI tracking and sustainment monitoring automate; facilitation and the shop-floor read do not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This is the "process" half of NAICS 541614: consultants who run kaizen events, map value streams, set up standard work, cut changeover times and coach supervisors in plants and warehouses. An engagement is typically a series of week-long events across lines or sites. The consultant's craft is reading a process on the floor — where the waiting, rework and motion hide — and getting operators to change it. The weakness of the model is well known inside the trade: gains made during an event decay once the consultant leaves, and few engagements measure whether they lasted.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Paper value-stream maps, A3 reports in PowerPoint, Excel trackers, and improvement-management platforms such as KaiNexus; the plant's own MES, ERP and quality systems hold the data that would show whether a gain held. The gaps: baselines are captured by hand during the event, not from systems; no one watches the KPI after handover; and the value-stream map stays on a wall rather than becoming a record.
