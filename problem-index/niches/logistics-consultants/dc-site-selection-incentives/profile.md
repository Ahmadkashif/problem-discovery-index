# DC Site Selection & Incentives Advisory

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/logistics-consultants|Logistics Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Location advisers who take a network study's answer — a new distribution centre somewhere in a region — and turn it into a site: screening labour markets for warehouse wages and availability, comparing candidate communities, and negotiating state and local incentives before the client announces. Paid by fee, sometimes with a contingency on incentives won. The industrial brokerage side of the same decision is covered under [[industries/commercial-real-estate|Commercial Real Estate]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 location analysts, labour economists and incentive negotiators at the larger specialists (estimate)
**Output:** Labour market studies, community comparisons, incentive negotiations and agreements
**Proprietary data:** A history of incentive deals and community responses across projects, plus labour survey work
**Clock:** The client's announcement and lease decisions; incentive approvals are scheduled around them
**Buyer:** Practice leader / managing director
