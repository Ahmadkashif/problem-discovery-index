# Supply Chain Operational Due Diligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/logistics-consultants|Logistics Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Logistics and operations specialists hired by private equity buyers, and by the commercial diligence teams working for them, to assess a target company's distribution network, freight contracts, warehouse operations and inventory during a sale process — and, after signing, to plan carve-out separations and transition services. The deliverable is a diligence report that quantifies operational risk and cost-out opportunity and feeds the buyer's bid and value-creation plan. It is a logistics-specific slice of the commercial diligence work logged under [[niches/expert-networks/commercial-due-diligence-advisors/profile|Commercial Due Diligence Advisors]] and scored consistently with it.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 operations and logistics diligence specialists at the specialist firms and in the strategy firms' private equity groups (estimate)
**Output:** Operational diligence reports, cost-to-serve and network savings estimates, carve-out and TSA plans, 100-day plans
**Proprietary data:** A library of logistics cost structures and savings outcomes across past deals — but built from target-company data received under confidentiality agreements
**Clock:** Sell-side bid and exclusivity deadlines set by the seller's banker, and signing dates
**Buyer:** Private equity operating partner / diligence practice leader

**Kill switches:** **data isn't theirs to use** — every analysis runs on a target's data from a virtual data room under a confidentiality agreement, and reuse across deals is contractually restricted, the same switch that stops the commercial diligence and quality-of-earnings pockets.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. The report is the invoice and the deadline is genuinely someone else's, which makes this the cleanest clock in the industry's value chain; the cross-deal library that would sharpen every estimate is the one asset the firm cannot lawfully assemble.
