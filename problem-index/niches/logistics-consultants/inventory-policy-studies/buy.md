# Inventory Optimisation Software in Study Mode

**Niche:** [[niches/logistics-consultants/inventory-policy-studies/profile|Inventory Policy & Safety Stock Studies]]
**Industry:** [[industries/logistics-consultants|Logistics Consultants]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Inventory optimisation software already computes multi-echelon safety stock; it is sold as a continuous planning system, not as a tool a consultant can run on an extract for eight weeks.
**Tags:** #probability-distributions #expectation-maximization #exponential-smoothing #monte-carlo-methods #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to release working capital without a service-level miss the client's sales team will blame on the study, and whoever's policies hold their projected fill rate in the first two quarters after go-live takes the account.

## The Problem
The consultant needs probabilistic demand modelling and multi-echelon optimisation for a one-off diagnostic. Licensing a planning system for the client is the outcome the study is meant to justify, not a prerequisite.

## What Already Exists
ToolsGroup, Slimstock, Netstock and Lokad, and the planning modules of Kinaxis, o9 and Blue Yonder, model demand probabilistically and size multi-echelon policies, delivered as subscriptions with integration and data-hygiene projects.

## The Customization Gap
Study mode needs: ingestion of raw ERP extracts with no integration; demand de-censoring for stockout periods; lead-time distributions rebuilt from purchase order and receipt history, including split deliveries; intermittent-demand handling for the long tail; a back-test of the proposed policy on history; output formatted for the client ERP's replenishment fields; and project-based licensing a consultant can pass through.

## Target Customer
Inventory consultancies and the vendors' own partner programmes, which gain a route to accounts that start with a study.

## Impact If Solved
Professional-grade modelling on a study budget, with a natural path from diagnostic to implementation.
