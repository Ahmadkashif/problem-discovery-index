# Logistics Expert Witness & Cargo Claims Consultants

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/logistics-consultants|Logistics Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Individual experts and small firms who give opinions in cargo loss and damage claims, warehouse liability disputes and broker-selection negligence cases, and who help shippers pursue freight claims against carriers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 2-10
**Output:** Expert reports, testimony and claim files
**Proprietary data:** Case files, held under protective orders or client confidentiality
**Clock:** Court schedules and claim filing windows
**Buyer:** Principal
