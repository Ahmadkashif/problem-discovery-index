# Network Design Platforms Adapted to the Consultant's Engagement Cycle

**Niche:** [[niches/logistics-consultants/network-design-strategy/profile|Distribution Network Design & Strategy]]
**Industry:** [[industries/logistics-consultants|Logistics Consultants]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Network design platforms are built for a shipper's in-house team modelling one network continuously; a consultancy models a new client's network every few weeks and has to forget it afterwards.
**Tags:** #optimization-fundamentals #large-language-models #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a client's raw shipment, order and invoice data into a baseline the CFO accepts as the real cost of today's network, and whoever closes that reconciliation fastest gets to run the scenarios that decide the footprint.

## The Problem
A consultancy licenses a design platform and then rebuilds the same scaffolding for every client: the data request, the mapping of the client's TMS and ERP fields into the platform's tables, the standard cost-to-serve structure, the scenario set, the readout charts. When the engagement ends, the engagement letter usually requires the client's data to be returned or destroyed, which takes the model and its history with it.

## What Already Exists
Coupa Supply Chain Design & Planning, Optilogic Cosmic Frog and AIMMS SC Navigator — solvers, data models and scenario management designed for a single organisation's continuing use, sold as subscriptions per seat or per model.

## The Customization Gap
The consultancy adaptation needs: reusable engagement templates by industry and channel; intake tooling for the data month (schema mapping from common TMS and ERP extracts, address resolution, imputation with every action logged); a separate engagement record of assumptions and cleansing decisions that survives client-data deletion in anonymised form; deletion and retention controls tied to each engagement letter; and multi-client licensing that does not charge for a new seat every time a project starts.

## Target Customer
Practice leaders at specialist logistics consultancies and solo modellers who currently rebuild the scaffolding per client.

## Impact If Solved
Each study starts from the firm's best previous version rather than a blank model, and the firm keeps what it learned when the client's data goes back.
