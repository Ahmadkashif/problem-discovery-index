# Industrial Brokerage Supply Chain Advisory

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/logistics-consultants|Logistics Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The supply chain and logistics advisory groups inside the large commercial real estate brokers run network and labour studies for occupiers, usually as the front end of an industrial leasing or build-to-suit mandate. They compete directly with logistics consultants for the network question and are paid, in most cases, through the leasing commission that follows. The same firms' research divisions are logged under [[niches/commercial-real-estate/brokerage-research-divisions/profile|Brokerage Research Divisions]] (Commercial Real Estate, 46); this pocket is the advisory function, scored consistently with that one on data and lower on how it is paid.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 supply chain consultants and labour analysts per large broker (estimate)
**Output:** Network and location studies, labour market analysis, occupancy cost comparisons
**Proprietary data:** The broker's leasing comparables and building inventory joined to labour data
**Clock:** Lease expiry dates and occupier decision timelines
**Buyer:** Head of supply chain advisory
