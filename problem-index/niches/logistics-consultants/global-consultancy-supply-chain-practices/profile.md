# Global Consultancy Supply Chain Practices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/logistics-consultants|Logistics Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The supply chain and operations practices of the strategy and technology consultancies — Accenture, Deloitte, McKinsey, Kearney (which writes CSCMP's annual State of Logistics report), BCG, EY and NTT DATA after its 2021 acquisition of Chainalytics — sell network redesigns, procurement transformations and planning programmes to the largest shippers. Their modelling centres of excellence run the same network design platforms as the specialists, at greater scale, and much of their work is classified outside NAICS 541614.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-2,000 network modellers, supply chain analysts and operations specialists in US supply chain practices (estimate; the firms' total headcounts are far larger)
**Output:** Network and footprint strategies, procurement and cost-out programmes, operating model designs, published practice research
**Proprietary data:** Methods, templates and anonymised benchmarks; the engagement data itself belongs to clients
**Clock:** Client board, budget and lease decisions — the client's schedule
**Buyer:** Supply chain practice leader / managing partner

**Kill switches:** **client confidentiality** — engagement data is walled per client and independence rules apply at the audit-affiliated firms, the same finding as the large risk, engineering and systems-integrator practices elsewhere in the sweep.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. The largest supply chain modelling workforce in the value chain, concentrated in a handful of firms (hence Q5), and unable to pool the engagement experience that is its real asset.
