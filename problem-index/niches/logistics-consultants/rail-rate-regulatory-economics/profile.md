# Rail Rate & Transportation Regulatory Economics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/logistics-consultants|Logistics Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Transportation economics consultancies that support shippers — chemical, coal, grain and other captive rail shippers — in rate disputes and regulatory proceedings before the Surface Transportation Board. In a stand-alone cost case the shipper must design a hypothetical stand-alone railroad, an operating plan and its costs, and the consultant builds that evidence and testifies to it; the same firms advise on rail contract negotiations and file comments in STB rulemakings.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 economists, rail operations analysts and cost modellers at the larger practices (estimate)
**Output:** Stand-alone cost and rate-reasonableness evidence, verified statements and testimony, contract negotiation analysis
**Proprietary data:** Models and operating plans from past cases; the railroads' traffic and cost data produced in discovery is restricted to the proceeding
**Clock:** STB procedural schedules — evidence, reply and rebuttal filing dates ordered by the Board
**Buyer:** Shipper general counsel or VP Transportation, through outside counsel

**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. A regulator's filing schedule is the hardest clock in the sweep, but full rate cases are rare, each is bespoke, and the evidence that would make the next case cheaper is sealed in the last one.
