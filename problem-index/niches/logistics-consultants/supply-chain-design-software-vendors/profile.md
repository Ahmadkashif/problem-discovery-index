# Supply Chain Design Software Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/logistics-consultants|Logistics Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The vendors of the network design platforms logistics consultants model in — Coupa Supply Chain Design & Planning (the former LLamasoft, bought for ~$1.5B in 2020), Optilogic's Cosmic Frog (which raised $40M in 2025), AIMMS and the planning suites — employ solution consultants, services teams and content staff who build models for customers, write templates and publish practice content. They partner with consultancies as a channel and compete with them for the same studies.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-200 solution consultants, services modellers and content staff per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Customer model builds, industry templates, training and published practice content
**Proprietary data:** Templates and anonymised usage data; customer models and data belong to customers
**Clock:** Release cycles and customer deadlines
**Buyer:** VP Services / Chief Product Officer
