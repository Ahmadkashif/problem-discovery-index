# Freight Audit Platforms Adapted to One-Time Studies

**Niche:** [[niches/logistics-consultants/mid-market-shippers/profile|Mid-Market Shippers Without Logistics Engineers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/logistics-consultants|Logistics Consultants]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Freight audit and payment platforms already turn carrier invoices into shipment records; they are built to process next month's invoices, not to back-load last year's for a study.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #feature-engineering #descriptive-statistics #data-integration #automation #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to reconstruct shipment-level history for a client with no TMS — freight invoices in PDFs, carrier portals, ERP order lines — and whoever can do it in days rather than weeks can deliver a study at a fee a mid-market CFO will approve.

## The Problem
The consultant needs a year of history now; the client has no appetite for a payment-processing contract before it knows whether the study will save anything.

## What Already Exists
Freight audit and payment providers receive, audit, code and pay invoices across modes and produce spend and lane reporting, as described under [[niches/warehouse-3pl/freight-audit-payment/profile|Freight Audit & Payment]]. Their ingestion handles hundreds of carrier formats and their data models already separate linehaul, fuel and accessorials.

## The Customization Gap
A study mode needs: bulk back-loading of twelve to twenty-four months of historical invoices in mixed formats, not a monthly feed; no payment, approval or general ledger workflow; a join to ERP orders and item masters so shipments carry product and customer; output shaped for a network design or bid model rather than a spend dashboard; and project pricing a consultant can pass through to a mid-market client. A consent path that lets the shipper convert the study data into continuous audit afterwards gives the provider a reason to offer it.

## Target Customer
Consultants serving mid-market shippers, and freight audit providers looking for a low-cost entry into accounts below their usual size.

## Impact If Solved
History reconstruction becomes a purchased step measured in days (estimate), and the consultant spends the engagement on the recommendation.
