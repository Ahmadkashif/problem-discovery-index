# The Study Scoped Around the Data the Client Can Find

**Niche:** [[niches/logistics-consultants/mid-market-shippers/profile|Mid-Market Shippers Without Logistics Engineers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/logistics-consultants|Logistics Consultants]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Mid-market studies are quietly narrowed to the carriers and months whose invoices the client could locate, and the recommendation inherits the gap.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #data-integration #quick-win #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to reconstruct shipment-level history for a client with no TMS — freight invoices in PDFs, carrier portals, ERP order lines — and whoever can do it in days rather than weeks can deliver a study at a fee a mid-market CFO will approve.

## The Problem
The client finds parcel invoices easily, LTL invoices for three of five carriers, and almost nothing for truckload booked through brokers by phone. To hold the fee, the consultant models what was found and extrapolates the rest. Inbound freight paid by suppliers and prepaid-and-add charges buried in product invoices are usually missing entirely.

## Why It's Still Broken
Nobody measures coverage. The readout presents a recommendation without stating what share of freight spend the history actually contains, so neither the CFO nor the consultant knows whether the missing truckload or inbound freight would change the answer.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Start every engagement by reconciling the reconstructed history to freight expense in the general ledger by mode and month, and publish the coverage figure on the first page. Where coverage is low, estimate the missing segment from ledger totals and carrier statements, and show how the recommendation changes across the plausible range.

## Who Feels the Pain
Mid-market CFOs acting on partial evidence; consultants whose recommendations fail on freight they never saw; shipping supervisors asked to dig out invoices.

## Impact If Fixed
A coverage line and a sensitivity range cost hours, not weeks, and stop the most common way a small study gives a confident wrong answer.
