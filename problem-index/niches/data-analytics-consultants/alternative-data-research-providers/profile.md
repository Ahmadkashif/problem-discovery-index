# Alternative Data Research Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/data-analytics-consultants|Data Analytics Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Yipit, M Science, Consumer Edge, and Placer.ai take raw behavioural exhaust — transaction panels, web activity, app usage, foot traffic — and turn it into company-level estimates of revenue, units, and share that investors trade on ahead of earnings. The raw data is a commodity; what is sold is the analyst work that maps it to a company's reported financials and defends the mapping.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 research analysts, data scientists, and coverage specialists
**Output:** Company-level estimates, coverage notes, sector reads — the research subscription
**Proprietary data:** Panel access under exclusive agreements plus, more importantly, the accumulated mapping from raw signal to reported financials for every covered company and the error history of every prior estimate
**Clock:** The earnings calendar, which is fixed, public, and unforgiving
**Buyer:** Head of Research / Chief Data Officer
