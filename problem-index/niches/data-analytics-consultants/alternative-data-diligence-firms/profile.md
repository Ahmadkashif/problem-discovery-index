# Alternative Data Diligence Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/data-analytics-consultants|Data Analytics Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Specialist firms assess whether an alternative dataset can be used lawfully and defensibly by an investment manager — tracing provenance, consent, contractual restrictions, and material non-public information exposure — and produce the diligence memorandum compliance functions rely on before licensing.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 diligence analysts, attorneys, and data specialists
**Output:** Data provenance and compliance diligence memoranda
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated diligence findings across vendors and the record of which sourcing structures held up under scrutiny
**Clock:** Licensing decisions and compliance review cycles
**Buyer:** Managing Director / Head of Data Diligence
