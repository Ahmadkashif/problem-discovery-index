# Survey Research & Panel Operators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/data-analytics-consultants|Data Analytics Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Ipsos, YouGov, Dynata, and the panel operators recruit, maintain, and sample the respondent panels that most quantitative consumer and business research runs on, and sell both raw fieldwork and analyzed studies. This sits on the data supply side of analytics consulting rather than inside it, and is logged here because the vault has no closer home for commissioned research operations.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-3,000 researchers, methodologists, panel managers, and analysts at the largest firms
**Output:** Fieldwork, weighted datasets, analyzed studies and trackers — the commissioned deliverable
**Proprietary data:** Recruited panels with deep profiling and years of longitudinal response history, plus the weighting and quality methodology that makes a non-probability panel usable
**Clock:** Client study deadlines and, for trackers, fixed publication schedules
**Buyer:** Chief Research Officer / Head of Methodology
