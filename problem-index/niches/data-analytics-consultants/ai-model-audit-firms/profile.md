# AI Model Audit & Assurance Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/data-analytics-consultants|Data Analytics Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
An emerging set of firms audit deployed models for bias, robustness, and documentation adequacy against the frameworks regulators and procurement teams are beginning to require. The audit report is the deliverable and increasingly gates deployment in regulated uses.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-80 auditors, statisticians, and technical writers
**Output:** Model audit reports, bias and robustness findings, conformity documentation
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated audit findings across models and sectors — small so far, and mostly client-owned
**Clock:** Regulatory conformity deadlines and deployment gates
**Buyer:** Practice leader / Head of AI Assurance
