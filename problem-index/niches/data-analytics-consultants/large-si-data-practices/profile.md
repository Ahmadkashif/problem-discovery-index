# Large Systems Integrator Data Practices

## What They Do
The data and AI practices at Accenture, Deloitte, Slalom, and the large integrators deliver the same work the boutiques do at enterprise scale — data platform builds, analytics products, and the strategy work around them — and publish survey-based thought leadership as business development.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 2,000-20,000 in data and AI practices at the largest firms
**Output:** Engagement deliverables, reference architectures, published research
**Proprietary data:** Engagement experience across thousands of clients, held under confidentiality that prevents it from being pooled
**Clock:** Client programme deadlines
**Buyer:** Practice leader / Chief Knowledge Officer
