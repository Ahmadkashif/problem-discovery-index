# Privacy & Data Governance Auditors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/data-analytics-consultants|Data Analytics Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Firms assessing organizations against privacy regimes — mapping data flows, testing consent and retention practice, and producing the assessments and records of processing that regulators expect. Analytics work is a frequent trigger, since new data uses require new assessments.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 privacy analysts, attorneys, and technical assessors
**Output:** Privacy impact assessments, data flow maps, compliance findings
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated assessment findings across clients, held under confidentiality
**Clock:** Regulatory deadlines, breach notification windows, and product launch gates
**Buyer:** Practice leader / VP of Privacy Services
