# Data Marketplaces & Brokers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/data-analytics-consultants|Data Analytics Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Snowflake Marketplace, Datarade, LiveRamp, and the data brokerage platforms intermediate the sale of third-party datasets — cataloguing supply, standardizing metadata, running identity resolution between datasets, and matching buyers to sources.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 data curators, identity engineers, and category analysts
**Output:** Catalogue metadata, identity resolution, dataset evaluation and matching
**Proprietary data:** Cross-dataset identity linkage and the record of what buyers actually search for and license
**Clock:** Continuous; no third-party deadline
**Buyer:** VP of Data Partnerships / Head of Catalogue
