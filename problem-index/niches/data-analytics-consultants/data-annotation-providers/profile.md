# Data Annotation & Evaluation Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/data-analytics-consultants|Data Analytics Consultants]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Scale AI, Surge, Appen, and the managed annotation providers operate large trained workforces producing labelled data and model evaluations. As frontier model work shifted toward expert judgment, the deliverable moved from bulk labelling to graded human assessment — which is analytical work sold by the unit, produced at industrial scale.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 500-5,000 annotators and evaluators, with 50-300 in taskonomy design, quality science, and programme management
**Output:** Labelled datasets, model evaluations, preference and quality judgments — the billable deliverable
**Proprietary data:** Annotator performance and agreement data across millions of tasks, plus the annotation guideline corpus — the delivered labels themselves belong to the client
**Clock:** Client model training and release schedules
**Buyer:** Head of Quality Science / VP of Operations
