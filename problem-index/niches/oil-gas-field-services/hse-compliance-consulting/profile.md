# HSE & Environmental Compliance Consulting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/oil-gas-field-services|Oil & Gas Field Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer

## What They Do
Environmental and safety consultancies handle emissions reporting, methane monitoring programmes, spill response planning, and the permitting that governs field operations — a burden that has grown sharply with methane regulation and emissions disclosure.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 environmental engineers, air quality specialists, and compliance staff
**Output:** Emissions inventories and reporting, monitoring programme design, permits and applications, incident investigation
**Proprietary data:** Emissions measurement and compliance findings across many operators and basins
**Clock:** Statutory reporting deadlines and permit renewal dates
**Buyer:** Practice leader
