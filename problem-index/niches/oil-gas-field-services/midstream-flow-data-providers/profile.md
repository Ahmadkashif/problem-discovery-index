# Midstream Flow & Infrastructure Data

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/oil-gas-field-services|Oil & Gas Field Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Flow data providers assemble pipeline nominations, capacity, and storage data into a real-time picture of where hydrocarbons are physically moving, sold to traders and midstream operators.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-200 data engineers, market analysts, and infrastructure researchers
**Output:** Pipeline flow and capacity data, storage inventories, infrastructure maps and projects, basis and constraint analytics
**Proprietary data:** Assembled and normalized flow data across hundreds of pipelines that publish separately and inconsistently
**Clock:** Intraday nomination cycles and daily flow
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Head of Analytics
