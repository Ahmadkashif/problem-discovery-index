# State Oil & Gas Commissions

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/oil-gas-field-services|Oil & Gas Field Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
State commissions permit wells, receive production and completion filings, adjudicate spacing and pooling, and enforce plugging obligations — generating the filings that the entire commercial data industry above them is built from.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-800 engineers, geologists, inspectors, and analysts per state
**Output:** Permits and spacing orders, production and completion records, inspection and enforcement actions, orphan well programmes
**Proprietary data:** The authoritative well record for the state, including confidential-period completion data
**Clock:** Permit and filing deadlines
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer — state agency
