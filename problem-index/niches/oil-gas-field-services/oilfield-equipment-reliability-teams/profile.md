# Oilfield Equipment Reliability Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/oil-gas-field-services|Oil & Gas Field Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The equipment manufacturers' reliability and technical groups receive operating telemetry from pumps, compressors, and downhole tools across large installed bases, supporting service contracts and product development.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 reliability engineers, data scientists, and technical service staff
**Output:** Failure prediction and service scheduling, root cause analysis, product development input, warranty analytics
**Proprietary data:** Operating telemetry and failure histories across enormous equipment populations in varied downhole and surface conditions
**Clock:** Service contract obligations and non-productive time penalties
**Buyer:** VP of Reliability / Director of Digital
