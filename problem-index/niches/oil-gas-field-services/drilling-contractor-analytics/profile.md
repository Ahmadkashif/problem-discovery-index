# Drilling Performance Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/oil-gas-field-services|Oil & Gas Field Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Drilling contractors and directional service providers run performance analytics on rig operations — rate of penetration, non-productive time, and wellbore placement — across large rig fleets and thousands of wells.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-200 drilling engineers, data scientists, and performance analysts
**Output:** Drilling performance benchmarking, automation and parameter optimization, non-productive time analysis, well planning support
**Proprietary data:** High-frequency drilling data across many rigs, crews, and formations
**Clock:** Daily rig operations, where a day of non-productive time is a large invoice
**Buyer:** VP of Drilling Performance / Chief Technology Officer
