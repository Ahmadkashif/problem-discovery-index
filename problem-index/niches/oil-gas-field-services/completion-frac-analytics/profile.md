# Completion & Frac Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/oil-gas-field-services|Oil & Gas Field Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Completion analytics providers instrument and interpret hydraulic fracturing operations — pressure response, fibre optic and microseismic data, offset well interference — to tell operators whether a completion design is working.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 40-250 engineers, geophysicists, and data scientists
**Output:** Completion diagnostics, frac hit and interference analysis, design optimization recommendations, monitoring services
**Proprietary data:** High-resolution completion monitoring across many wells and basins, joined to subsequent production
**Clock:** Completion operations, measured in hours
**Buyer:** VP of Technology / Chief Data Officer
