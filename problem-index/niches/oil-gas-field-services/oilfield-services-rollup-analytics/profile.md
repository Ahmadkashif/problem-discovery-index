# Oilfield Services Corporate Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/oil-gas-field-services|Oil & Gas Field Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup

## What They Do
The large service companies and their private equity backed consolidators run corporate analytics across many service lines and basins, managing pricing, asset utilization, and the acquisition programmes that reshape the sector each cycle.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 pricing analysts, operations researchers, and corporate development staff
**Output:** Pricing and bid strategy, fleet utilization and deployment, market share analysis, acquisition modelling
**Proprietary data:** Job-level pricing, cost, and utilization across service lines and basins through multiple cycles
**Clock:** Rig count movements and quarterly pricing cycles
**Buyer:** No external buyer — internal cost centre
