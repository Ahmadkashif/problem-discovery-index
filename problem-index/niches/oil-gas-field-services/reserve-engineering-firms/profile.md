# Reserve Engineering Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/oil-gas-field-services|Oil & Gas Field Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Independent petroleum engineering firms estimate how much oil and gas a company's properties will produce and what it is worth. Their reports are what public disclosure, bank borrowing bases, and acquisition prices rest on — a third-party reserve report is required for securities reporting and is the basis on which lenders size credit facilities twice a year.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-400 petroleum engineers, geologists, and economists per firm
**Output:** Reserve reports and certifications, property valuations, borrowing base and acquisition evaluations, expert testimony
**Proprietary data:** Decades of reserve estimates joined to what the properties actually produced afterwards — the only systematic record of how well the industry's forecasting works
**Clock:** Year-end reserve reporting and semi-annual borrowing base redeterminations, both immovable
**Buyer:** Managing partner / Practice leader
