# Reserve-Based Lending Engineering

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/oil-gas-field-services|Oil & Gas Field Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Bank energy groups employ petroleum engineers who independently evaluate borrowers' reserves and set the borrowing base — the credit limit that determines whether an operator can fund a drilling programme or must sell assets.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 engineers, credit analysts, and economists per bank
**Output:** Borrowing base determinations, price deck and risking assumptions, credit analysis, portfolio stress testing
**Proprietary data:** Borrower reserve and performance data across a lending book through multiple price cycles, joined to credit outcomes
**Clock:** Semi-annual redeterminations, on a fixed calendar
**Buyer:** Head of Energy Credit — internal function
