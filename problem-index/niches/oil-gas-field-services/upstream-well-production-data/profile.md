# Upstream Well & Production Data Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/oil-gas-field-services|Oil & Gas Field Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Enverus and its peers assemble every well permit, completion report, production filing, and land record filed with state regulators into a normalized national database, and sell it to the operators, service companies, investors, and lenders who make every drilling and capital decision against it. Pass 1 notes the field-level diagnosis still runs on experienced technicians' judgment; the strategic layer above them runs entirely on this data.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-1,500 data engineers, geologists, petroleum engineers, analysts, and land data specialists
**Output:** Normalized well and production databases, type curve and economics analytics, rig and permit intelligence, land and ownership data, forecasting products
**Proprietary data:** Decades of normalization, entity resolution, and interpretation over filings that are public state by state and were never designed to be compared — plus the well-level interpretation layer no filing contains
**Clock:** Monthly and quarterly state data releases, drilling and completion decision cycles, and quarterly reserve reporting
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / SVP of Product
