# Petroleum Association Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/oil-gas-field-services|Oil & Gas Field Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The petroleum associations write the recommended practices and standards field operations are conducted to, publish industry statistics, and analyse the regulation that governs drilling and emissions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 standards engineers, statisticians, and policy analysts
**Output:** Recommended practices and standards, industry statistics, regulatory analysis, certification programmes
**Proprietary data:** Standards corpus with development history, plus long-running industry statistical series
**Clock:** Standards revision cycles and rulemaking comment periods
**Buyer:** VP of Standards — member funded
