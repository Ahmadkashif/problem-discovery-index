# Energy Price Reporting Agencies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/oil-gas-field-services|Oil & Gas Field Services]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Price reporting agencies assess and publish the crude, gas, and refined product prices physical contracts settle against, in markets where much of the trade is bilateral and private.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 market reporters, analysts, economists, and methodology staff
**Output:** Daily price assessments, forward curves, market analysis, index licensing
**Proprietary data:** Reporter-collected transaction and offer data plus decades of continuous assessment series
**Clock:** Daily publication that settles physical contracts
**Buyer:** Editorial director / Chief Data Officer
