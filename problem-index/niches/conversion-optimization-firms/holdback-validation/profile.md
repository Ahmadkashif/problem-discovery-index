# Cumulative Holdback Validation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/conversion-optimization-firms|Conversion Optimization Firms]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to establish whether a year of implemented winners actually produced anything, using a holdback it could start tomorrow — and whoever runs it takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — almost nobody
**Target Buyer:** Programme and client leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — holdback infrastructure and analysis

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This half operates at the programme level and over months. A permanent randomised fraction of traffic never receives any implemented winner; the difference between that group and everyone else is the programme's actual accumulated effect. It grades the whole practice — including the tests that were run correctly — it requires no methodology change to start, and it is the one thing that would tell a firm within a year how much of its claimed value is real.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Implemented winners and an assumed uplift. The gaps: no permanent holdback; no accumulated effect measurement; no comparison against the sum of reported uplifts; no per-change contribution within the accumulated effect; and no client agreement to reserve traffic.
