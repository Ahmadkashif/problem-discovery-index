# Permanent Control Groups From Direct Marketing

**Niche:** [[niches/conversion-optimization-firms/holdback-validation/profile|Cumulative Holdback Validation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/conversion-optimization-firms|Conversion Optimization Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Direct marketing kept a permanent untreated control for decades because otherwise nobody knows what the programme did.
**Tags:** #causal-inference #hypothesis-testing #confidence-intervals #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact #probability-distributions #descriptive-statistics #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to establish whether a year of implemented winners actually produced anything, using a holdback it could start tomorrow — and whoever runs it takes the account.

## The Problem
Direct marketing established the permanent control group as basic practice: a randomly selected portion of the audience receives nothing, indefinitely, so the programme's total effect can be measured rather than assumed. It costs a small amount of foregone contact and it is the only way to know what the whole programme is worth rather than what each campaign appeared to do. The practice is old, well understood and standard in disciplined programmes. Conversion optimisation, which is closer to a controlled experiment than direct marketing ever was, does not do it.

## What Already Exists
Permanent randomised control groups; programme-level incrementality measurement; control group refresh and maintenance practice; contamination monitoring; and reporting programme effect rather than campaign sums.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a website rather than a contact list. It requires: (1) holding back a fraction of traffic from site changes rather than from contacts, which means maintaining a parallel experience over months and preventing every implemented change from reaching it — this is the substantive difference and is a genuine engineering discipline; (2) an anonymous visitor population rather than an addressable list, so holdback assignment must persist across sessions without identity; (3) changes that interact, so the accumulated effect is not a sum; (4) a client who sees the holdback as withholding improvements from real users; and (5) site releases outside the programme that can contaminate the holdback silently.

## Target Customer
Conversion optimisation firms and experimentation teams, client leadership, testing platform vendors, and measurement providers.

## Impact If Solved
Direct marketing kept permanent controls because otherwise a programme's total effect is unknowable. Maintaining a parallel site experience over months, for an anonymous population, is the engineering discipline the web version requires.
