## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Between a hypothesis and a result sits a body of operational work: configuring the test, setting up goals and tracking, quality-assuring the variants, launching, monitoring for anomalies, checking sample ratios, concluding, implementing and archiving. It is repetitive, it is done by hand, it recurs on every test, and it is what limits how many tests a team can run more often than the traffic does.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A testing platform, a checklist somebody wrote and a set of manual checks. The gaps: no automated quality assurance of variants; no sample ratio mismatch detection; no anomaly monitoring during a test; no automated conclusion against the stopping rule; and no archiving of what was run.
