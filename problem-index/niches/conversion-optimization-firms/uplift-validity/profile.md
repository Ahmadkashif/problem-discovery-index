# Reported Uplift Validity

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/conversion-optimization-firms|Conversion Optimization Firms]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to report uplift that is real, in a discipline whose standard workflow inflates false positives and whose reported wins do not replicate — and whoever can demonstrate validity takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$700M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~28% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — unchecked
**Target Buyer:** Experimentation leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — methodology plus programme validation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A year of reported test uplifts, if real, would have doubled the conversion rate, and the conversion rate did not double — and the industry has never made that comparison a standard part of its reporting. The standard workflow contains several practices that inflate false positive rates severely and are frequently combined, so the reported wins are a mixture of real effects and noise weighted toward noise, because noise is what gets stopped early.

### Contested sub-niches
This niche is not terminal. It names a property, and establishing it needs two things at different levels:
- [[niches/conversion-optimization-firms/statistical-practice/profile|🎯 Experiment Design & Statistical Practice]] — whether each test supports its conclusion
- [[niches/conversion-optimization-firms/holdback-validation/profile|🎯 Cumulative Holdback Validation]] — whether the year's accumulated effect is real

## Current Tools & Gaps
A testing platform, a significance indicator and a monthly report of wins. The gaps: no power analysis before launching; no fixed stopping rule; no correction for multiple comparisons; no holdback validating implemented winners; and no comparison between reported uplift and actual movement.
