# Result Reporting & Knowledge Base

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/conversion-optimization-firms|Conversion Optimization Firms]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep what its tests established in a form anyone can retrieve, and whoever builds that record takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$50M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — decks
**Target Buyer:** Programme operations
**Automation Potential:** Very high — generated reporting and a structured record

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A programme running fifty tests a year produces fifty results and retains them as slides. A year later nobody can say what was tested on a page, what was found, or whether a proposed test has been run before. The firm's accumulated knowledge — the one thing a long-running programme should build — exists as a folder, and the reporting that produces it is assembled by hand each month from data the platform already holds.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A platform results screen and a monthly slide deck. The gaps: no structured record of tests and findings; no retrieval by page or hypothesis type; no detection of repeated tests; no generated reporting; and no accumulation of effect sizes to inform future estimates.
