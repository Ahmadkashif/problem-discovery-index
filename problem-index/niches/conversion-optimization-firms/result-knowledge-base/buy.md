# Trial Registries From Research Infrastructure

**Industry:** [[industries/conversion-optimization-firms|Conversion Optimization Firms]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Research built registries so studies are recorded before they run and reported whatever they find, and test results live in slides.
**Tags:** #compliance #data-integration #evaluation-metrics #descriptive-statistics #hypothesis-testing #confidence-intervals #automation #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep what its tests established in a form anyone can retrieve, and whoever builds that record takes the account.

## The Problem
Research infrastructure built trial registries to solve two problems at once: a study registered before it runs cannot have its hypothesis changed afterwards, and a registry that requires reporting regardless of outcome prevents the literature filling with positive results only. The registries are public, searchable and increasingly mandatory. Experimentation programmes have the same two failures and no registry, so hypotheses drift and nulls disappear.

## What Already Exists
Pre-registration of hypothesis and analysis; unique identifiers per study; mandatory reporting of outcomes; searchable public records; and meta-analysis built on the accumulated registry.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to commercial tests that are confidential and run weekly. It requires: (1) a registry that is internal rather than public, since results are commercially sensitive, which removes the external enforcement that makes registries work and is the substantive difference; (2) registration that takes seconds rather than filling a protocol form, given the cadence; (3) records per client with pattern-level generalisation across them; (4) no regulator or journal requiring compliance, so the discipline must be self-imposed; and (5) a record that must also produce the client's monthly report, which is what makes it worth maintaining.

## Target Customer
Conversion optimisation firms and experimentation teams, testing platform vendors, programme operations, and research infrastructure providers.

## Impact If Solved
Trial registries fixed hypothesis drift and missing nulls by recording studies before they run. An internal confidential registry with no external enforcement is why the discipline here must be self-imposed and must pay for itself through the reporting.
