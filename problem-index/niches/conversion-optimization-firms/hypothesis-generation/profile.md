# Hypothesis Generation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/conversion-optimization-firms|Conversion Optimization Firms]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell which of an endless supply of observations describes a mechanism worth testing — and whoever makes that selection takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$375M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — observation piles
**Target Buyer:** CRO strategy leads
**Automation Potential:** High — pattern detection and mechanism framing

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Session recordings and heatmaps generate an endless supply of observations and no way to tell which of them describe a mechanism worth testing. A user hesitated here, scrolled past that, clicked something non-interactive — each is a fact and almost none is a hypothesis. The step from observation to a testable claim about why people do not convert is the discipline's actual craft, and the tooling supplies the observations in unlimited quantity while contributing nothing to the step.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Session recordings, heatmaps, funnel analytics and a workshop. The gaps: no aggregation of observations into patterns; no framing of an observation as a mechanism; no estimate of how many users a pattern affects; no prioritisation by plausible effect; and no learning from which hypothesis types have worked.
