# From Observation to Mechanism

**Niche:** [[niches/conversion-optimization-firms/hypothesis-generation/profile|Hypothesis Generation]]
**Industry:** [[industries/conversion-optimization-firms|Conversion Optimization Firms]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** The tooling produces unlimited observations and the scarce thing is a claim about why.
**Tags:** #k-means-clustering #evaluation-metrics #descriptive-statistics #change-point-detection #confidence-intervals #large-language-models #causal-inference #survival-analysis
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell which of an endless supply of observations describes a mechanism worth testing — and whoever makes that selection takes the account.

## The Problem
A strategist watches recordings and studies heatmaps and accumulates observations: hesitation at a form field, rapid scrolling past a section, clicks on non-interactive elements, abandonment at a particular step. Each is real and none is a hypothesis. Turning it into a claim — people abandon here because they cannot tell what happens next — requires judgement about mechanism, and the observations arrive faster than the judgement can process them.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Behavioural tooling is built to produce observations, which is the easy and saleable half. Aggregating them into patterns requires analysis nobody has automated. The step to a mechanism is craft and has not been articulated. And a pile of observations looks like research.

## What to Build
Aggregate the observations into patterns and estimate what each affects. Cluster observations into recurring patterns across sessions rather than presenting individual recordings, which is the core — one user hesitating is an anecdote and four hundred hesitating at the same field is a finding. Quantify how many users each pattern affects and what it costs in conversion, which is what makes a pattern worth a test. Detect the friction points automatically — abandonment concentrations, error repetition, backtracking, rage behaviour — rather than requiring a human to watch for them. Frame each pattern as a candidate mechanism with its implied test, which is the craft step and where assistance is most useful. Estimate the plausible effect size so the hypothesis can be prioritised against the capacity constraint. Link quantitative patterns to the qualitative recordings that illustrate them, since the recording is what persuades a client. Learn which hypothesis types have produced real effects in the programme's history, which nobody tracks and which would improve the selection permanently. Surface the patterns that contradict the team's assumptions, as those are the highest-value ones. Keep the strategist deciding, because mechanism is judgement and an automated hypothesis would be worse than none. And report patterns rather than observations, which is the change that makes the research usable.

## Target Customer
Conversion optimisation firms and experimentation teams, behavioural analytics vendors, product and UX research functions, and testing platform providers.

## Impact If Built
One user hesitating is an anecdote and four hundred hesitating at the same field is a finding, and the tooling produces the first in unlimited quantity. Clustering observations into quantified patterns is the step to a testable mechanism.
