# The Strategist

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/conversion-optimization-firms|Conversion Optimization Firms]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let the person who knows what day-four significance means say so, when the monthly report is expected to contain a win — and whoever supports that takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$250M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — no support
**Target Buyer:** CRO leadership
**Automation Potential:** Medium — reporting structure and expectation setting

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The test reached significance on day four in one segment, the strategist knows what that means, and the client is expecting a win in the monthly report. The position is structurally uncomfortable: they hold the knowledge that the result is unreliable and they carry the commercial relationship that needs a result. The discipline's bad practices are produced substantially by this pressure, and the person absorbing it has no mechanism, no policy and no authority to resolve it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A significance indicator, a monthly report template and a client expecting progress. The gaps: no policy they can point at; no reporting format that accommodates an honest null; no expectation set at the start of the engagement; no support from leadership for refusing to declare; and no recognition that the pressure produces the practice.
