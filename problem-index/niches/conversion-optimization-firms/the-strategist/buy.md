**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let the person who knows what day-four significance means say so, when the monthly report is expected to contain a win — and whoever supports that takes the account.

## The Problem
Advisory professions where a client wants a particular answer — audit, actuarial work, engineering certification, clinical advice — built standards that let a practitioner refuse. A professional body defines what may be asserted, individual sign-off makes the practitioner personally accountable, and the standard is something to point at when a client pushes. The structure exists because commercial pressure on individual judgement produces exactly what it produces here. Experimentation practitioners have none of it.

## What Already Exists
Professional standards defining permissible assertions; individual accountability through sign-off; professional bodies backing the standard; disciplinary consequences for breaches; and a norm that a client cannot overrule the practitioner.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a young commercial discipline with no professional body. It requires: (1) no professional body, licence or disciplinary structure, so the standard must be adopted by a firm unilaterally and defended commercially — this is the substantive difference and makes it a positioning decision rather than a compliance one; (2) practitioners who are marketers and analysts rather than certified professionals; (3) a client relationship where the firm can be replaced by one that will declare the win; (4) results that are statistical judgements rather than certifications, so the standard must be about method rather than outcome; and (5) no external verification of who is applying the standard.

## Target Customer
Conversion optimisation firms and experimentation leads, CRO leadership, client marketing leadership, and emerging professional bodies.

## Impact If Solved
Advisory professions built standards precisely because commercial pressure on individual judgement produces the failure seen here. No professional body means the standard has to be adopted unilaterally and defended as a commercial position.
