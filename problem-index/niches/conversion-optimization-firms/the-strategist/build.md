# A Report That Can Say Nothing Happened

**Niche:** [[niches/conversion-optimization-firms/the-strategist/profile|The Strategist]]
**Industry:** [[industries/conversion-optimization-firms|Conversion Optimization Firms]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** The person who knows the result is unreliable is the person who has to present it.
**Tags:** #worker-facing #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #hypothesis-testing #compliance #descriptive-statistics #workflow-orchestration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let the person who knows what day-four significance means say so, when the monthly report is expected to contain a win — and whoever supports that takes the account.

## The Problem
The strategist understands the statistics. They also manage a client relationship in which a month without a win reads as a month without progress. Declaring the early significance is the path that satisfies everyone immediately and is wrong; refusing to declare is correct and costs them the conversation. The discipline's malpractice is produced largely by people who know better, responding to a structure nobody has designed around.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The reporting cadence was set by client expectation rather than by experiment duration. No professional standard exists to point at. Firms compete on reported wins, so a firm that reports fewer looks worse. And the strategist is the one absorbing it, so it never surfaces as a firm-level problem.

## What to Build
Change what a good month looks like and give the strategist something to point at. Establish a reporting format in which a rigorous null result is a reportable outcome rather than an absence, which is the core and removes the pressure at its source. Set expectations at engagement start about how many tests produce real effects, which is the conversation that makes every later report survivable. Report tests completed to standard rather than wins found, so the programme's quality is what is being reported. Provide a firm-level policy on declaring results that the strategist can cite, which converts a personal judgement into a professional standard. Align the reporting cadence to test duration rather than to a calendar month, since the mismatch is what creates the pressure. Report the interval and the detectable effect alongside every result, which makes an honest presentation possible. Educate the client on what to expect, which most will accept and few are offered. Back the strategist when a client pushes, which is the leadership half and the one that matters most. Measure and report the firm's own replication rate, which reframes the whole relationship. And recognise that the practice follows the incentive, so changing the practice means changing the report.

## Target Customer
Conversion optimisation firms and in-house experimentation leads, CRO leadership, client marketing leadership, and professional bodies in experimentation.

## Impact If Built
The discipline's malpractice is produced by people who know better, responding to a reporting structure nobody designed. A report in which a rigorous null is an outcome removes the pressure at its source.
