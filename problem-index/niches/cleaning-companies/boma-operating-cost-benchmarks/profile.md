# Building Operating Cost Benchmark Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cleaning-companies|Cleaning Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
BOMA's Experience Exchange Report and IFMA's benchmarking programmes collect operating expense data from member buildings and publish the per-square-foot cost benchmarks — including cleaning — that property managers use to set budgets and challenge contractor pricing.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 research staff and data analysts
**Output:** Operating cost benchmark reports by building type and market
**Proprietary data:** Member-contributed operating expense data across thousands of buildings over decades
**Clock:** Annual data collection and publication cycle
**Buyer:** VP of Research
