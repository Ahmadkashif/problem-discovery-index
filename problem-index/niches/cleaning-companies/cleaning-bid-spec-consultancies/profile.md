# Janitorial Bid & Specification Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cleaning-companies|Cleaning Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Independent consultants who write cleaning specifications for building owners, run competitive janitorial procurements, and audit contractor performance against the spec. The written specification and audit finding are the deliverables.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1-6
**Output:** Cleaning specifications, bid evaluations, performance audits
**Proprietary data:** The consultant's accumulated specification and pricing knowledge
**Clock:** Procurement calendars
**Buyer:** Principal consultant
