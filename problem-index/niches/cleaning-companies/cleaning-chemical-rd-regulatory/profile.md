# Cleaning Chemical R&D and Regulatory Science

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cleaning-companies|Cleaning Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The formulation science, microbiology, and regulatory groups at Ecolab, Diversey, Zep, and Betco. They develop and validate chemistries, run the efficacy and safety programmes behind registration, and produce the protocols and training their customers' cleaning staff follow. The science is substantial and it ships attached to chemical sales and service contracts.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 formulation chemists, microbiologists, and regulatory scientists
**Output:** Formulations, registration dossiers, application protocols, customer-facing hygiene programmes
**Proprietary data:** Efficacy and application performance data across millions of customer sites and decades of formulation history
**Clock:** Registration cycles and product launch calendars
**Buyer:** VP of R&D / Chief Science Officer
