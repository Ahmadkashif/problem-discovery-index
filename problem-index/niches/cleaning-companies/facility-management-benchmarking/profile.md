# Integrated Facility Management Benchmarking

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cleaning-companies|Cleaning Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The research and benchmarking groups inside JLL, CBRE, Cushman & Wakefield, and Sodexo's facilities arm. They manage facilities on behalf of corporate occupiers, which means they set and audit the cleaning specifications and rates their subcontractors work to, and they publish cost-per-square-foot benchmarks that clients use to challenge those rates.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 analysts, benchmarking specialists, and researchers
**Output:** Facility cost benchmarks, service level analyses, occupancy and workplace research
**Proprietary data:** Cost and service level data across billions of square feet under management, by building type, region, and specification
**Clock:** Client budget cycles and contract renewal calendars
**Buyer:** Head of Research / VP of Benchmarking
