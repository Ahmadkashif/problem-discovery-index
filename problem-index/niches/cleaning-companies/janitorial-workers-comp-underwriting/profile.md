# Janitorial Workers' Compensation Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cleaning-companies|Cleaning Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Underwriters and programme administrators specializing in janitorial and facility services workers' compensation research injury patterns by task, chemical exposure, and site type to price a line with high frequency and heavy turnover, and produce the loss control guidance contractors follow.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 underwriters, actuaries, and loss control specialists
**Output:** Rating models, loss control programmes, injury trend analyses
**Proprietary data:** Injury experience by cleaning task and site type across the book
**Clock:** Rate filings and renewal cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer
