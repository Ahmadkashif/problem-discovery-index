# Facility & Construction Cost Data Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cleaning-companies|Cleaning Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Gordian's RSMeans, Whitestone Research, and comparable publishers maintain the unit cost databases that facility and construction estimates are built against — labour hours, crew composition, material cost, and productivity by task, localized to hundreds of cities. Cost engineers collect, verify, and maintain hundreds of thousands of line items. Estimators across facility services, janitorial contracting, and construction license the database because it is the defensible basis for a bid.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-400 cost engineers, data researchers, and analysts
**Output:** The cost database and its localization factors — the licensed product
**Proprietary data:** Decades of collected unit cost and productivity data, verified by researchers city by city and trade by trade, which no competitor can assemble retrospectively
**Clock:** Quarterly and annual release cycles, and the bid deadlines every subscriber works to
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Director of Cost Research
