# Environmental Product Certification Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cleaning-companies|Cleaning Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Green Seal and UL's ECOLOGO programme write the environmental and health criteria that cleaning products are certified against, review manufacturer submissions, and issue certifications that institutional purchasers increasingly mandate.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 15-50 standards developers, toxicologists, and certification reviewers
**Output:** Certification standards, submission reviews, certification decisions
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated formulation review findings across the product category
**Clock:** Standard revision cycles and certification renewal periods
**Buyer:** VP of Standards / Chief Science Officer
