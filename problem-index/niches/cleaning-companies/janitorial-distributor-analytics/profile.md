# Janitorial & Sanitation Distributor Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cleaning-companies|Cleaning Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The category management and data groups at Bunzl, Veritiv, and Imperial Dade analyze what cleaning contractors and facility operators actually purchase, build consumption and cost-per-occupant analytics for customers, and sell category insight to the manufacturers whose products they distribute.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 category analysts and data staff
**Output:** Category plans, consumption analytics, manufacturer insight reporting
**Proprietary data:** Purchase data across tens of thousands of cleaning operations at line-item granularity
**Clock:** Category review and manufacturer planning cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Category Management
