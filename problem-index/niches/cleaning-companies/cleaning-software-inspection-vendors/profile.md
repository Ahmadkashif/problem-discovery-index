# Cleaning Quality Inspection Software Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cleaning-companies|Cleaning Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
CleanTelligent, OrangeQC, and similar platforms digitize the quality inspection contractors perform on their own work, producing scored inspection records that get shown to clients as evidence of service level.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-25 product and data staff
**Output:** Inspection scoring, client-facing quality reporting
**Proprietary data:** Millions of scored inspection records across contractors and building types — the only structured record of cleaning quality that exists
**Clock:** Release cycles, internal
**Buyer:** Head of Product
