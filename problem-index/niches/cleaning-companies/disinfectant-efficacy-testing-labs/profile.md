# Disinfectant Efficacy Testing Laboratories

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cleaning-companies|Cleaning Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Microbac, Nelson Labs, Situ Biosciences, and comparable GLP laboratories run the efficacy studies that let a disinfectant make a kill claim. Every organism claim on a label — the pathogens a product is registered against, the contact time required — rests on a study report submitted to the EPA. Cleaning contractors' entire disinfection protocol is downstream of what those studies established.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 microbiologists, study directors, and quality staff
**Output:** GLP study reports supporting registration and label claims
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated efficacy results across formulations, organisms, surfaces, and contact times, plus the record of which study designs regulators accepted
**Clock:** EPA registration timelines and product launch dates that cannot move without the study
**Buyer:** Laboratory Director / VP of Scientific Services
