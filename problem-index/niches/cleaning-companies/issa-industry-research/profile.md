# Cleaning Industry Association Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cleaning-companies|Cleaning Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
ISSA produces the cleaning time standards the whole industry workloads against, the value-of-clean research used to justify cleaning budgets, and the CIMS certification programme that contractors are assessed under. The time standards in particular function as this industry's labour guide.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-60 research staff, standards developers, and certification assessors
**Output:** Cleaning time standards, industry research, certification assessments
**Proprietary data:** Time and productivity study data underpinning the standards, plus certification findings across assessed contractors
**Clock:** Standards revision and certification cycles, largely self-set
**Buyer:** Director of Research / VP of Certification
