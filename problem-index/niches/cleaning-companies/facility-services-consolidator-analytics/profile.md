# Facility Services Consolidator Bid Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/cleaning-companies|Cleaning Companies]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The pricing, industrial engineering, and business analytics groups at ABM, SBM, Harvard Maintenance, and the large janitorial contractors. They workload facilities, model labour and supply cost, price multi-site contracts, and analyze margin performance across thousands of sites.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-200 industrial engineers, pricing analysts, and operations researchers
**Output:** Bid models, workloading standards, contract margin analyses
**Proprietary data:** Site-level productivity and margin data across thousands of buildings, joined to bid outcomes at known prices
**Clock:** RFP submission deadlines, which are fixed and frequent
**Buyer:** VP of Pricing / Chief Operating Officer
