# RFS QAP, LCFS & Landfill Offset Verification Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landfill-operators|Landfill Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation firms

## What They Do
Third parties that audit and verify the environmental attributes landfill gas creates: quality-assurance plan providers that audit RIN generation under the Renewable Fuel Standard, verification bodies accredited for low carbon fuel standard reports, and verifiers of landfill gas destruction offsets registered under protocols such as the Climate Action Reserve's U.S. Landfill Project Protocol (verifiers such as Ruby Canyon Environmental and SCS Global Services). RNG developers and project owners pay per facility audit or verification.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Auditors and verifiers working facility by facility (estimate; tens at the larger firms), which is production; no team sells cross-project analysis
**Output:** QAP audit reports, verification statements, offset verification reports
**Proprietary data:** Facility meter, lab and production records, held under engagement
**Clock:** RFS and LCFS reporting and verification deadlines — the clients' clocks
**Buyer:** Verification practice director
