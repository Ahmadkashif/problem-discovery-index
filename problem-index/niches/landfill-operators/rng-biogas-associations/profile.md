# RNG & Biogas Trade Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landfill-operators|Landfill Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Coalition for Renewable Natural Gas and the American Biogas Council represent RNG developers, landfill owners, utilities and suppliers, track projects and policy, and lobby on the Renewable Fuel Standard, low carbon fuel standards and tax credits. Members and sponsors pay.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research and policy staff each (estimate)
**Output:** Project counts, policy analysis, comments on EPA volume rules
**Proprietary data:** Member surveys of production and projects; compilations of public EPA data
**Clock:** EPA rulemaking comment deadlines, which bind the associations' advocacy rather than a product
**Buyer:** Executive director
