# B2B Price Optimisation With a Haul-Distance Competitor Model

**Niche:** [[niches/landfill-operators/tip-fee-disposal-pricing/profile|Tip Fee & Disposal Contract Pricing]]
**Industry:** [[industries/landfill-operators|Landfill Operators]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Distributors already use price-optimisation software to set customer-specific prices from elasticity and competitive position; disposal pricing is the same problem with geography as the competitor.
**Tags:** #logistic-regression #gradient-boosting #optimization-fundamentals #revenue-impact #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to raise the price a landfill realises per ton at the gate and in disposal contracts without losing tonnage to rival landfills and transfer stations, and whoever loses the fewest tons per point of price increase takes the account.

## The Problem
Landfills price by posted rate, contract and quote, with increases set as a flat percentage.

## What Already Exists
B2B price-optimisation platforms (PROS, Pricefx, Zilliant) segment customers, estimate price response and recommend customer-level prices and increases for distributors and manufacturers.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: haul distance and drive time to competing disposal sites as the core competitive variable; integrated haulers' internal tons excluded from elasticity; municipal contracts with CPI escalators and renewal dates; special-waste quotes with their own cost drivers (testing, handling, airspace); host-community and state surcharges passed through; flow-control ordinances; and public-sector rate-setting procedures.

## Target Customer
Regional and national landfill owners pricing third-party tons; large public landfills with commercial customers.

## Impact If Solved
Distribution users of these tools commonly report ~1–3% margin gains (estimate); one point on ~$18B of disposal revenue is ~$180M across the industry.
