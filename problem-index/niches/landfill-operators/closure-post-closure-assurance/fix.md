# Assurance Instruments Out of Step With the Estimate

**Niche:** [[niches/landfill-operators/closure-post-closure-assurance/profile|Closure, Post-Closure & Financial Assurance]]
**Industry:** [[industries/landfill-operators|Landfill Operators]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** When a closure estimate rises, the bond, letter of credit or trust behind it must rise too, and when it falls, collateral should be released; both happen late because the estimate and the instrument live with different people.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #compliance #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to shrink the decades of closure and post-closure spending a landfill owes, and whoever wins regulator approval to reduce or end post-closure care at the most closed units takes the account.

## The Problem
Engineering updates the estimate; treasury manages bonds and letters of credit; the state holds the paperwork. Under-assurance draws notices, while over-assurance ties up credit capacity for years.

## Why It's Still Broken
No register links each estimate line to the instruments covering it, and inflation updates, permit modifications and instrument renewals run on separate calendars.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A register of every unit's current estimate, required amount, instruments, issuers, expiry dates and state approvals, updated when an estimate changes, with renewal and adjustment tasks and draft submissions to the state.

## Who Feels the Pain
Treasury and environmental teams at multi-site owners; public owners whose financial test must be re-certified.

## Impact If Fixed
Releasing excess assurance on ~$20M of over-collateralised obligations (estimate) frees credit capacity worth ~$200K a year in letter-of-credit fees at ~1% (estimate).
