# The Evidence Package for Ending Post-Closure Care

**Niche:** [[niches/landfill-operators/closure-post-closure-assurance/profile|Closure, Post-Closure & Financial Assurance]]
**Industry:** [[industries/landfill-operators|Landfill Operators]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Closed landfills keep paying for full monitoring for 30 years because nobody assembles decades of declining gas, stable leachate and clean groundwater into the case a regulator needs to reduce it.
**Tags:** #time-series-forecasting #survival-analysis #bayesian-inference #large-language-models #compliance #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to shrink the decades of closure and post-closure spending a landfill owes, and whoever wins regulator approval to reduce or end post-closure care at the most closed units takes the account.

## The Problem
Post-closure care costs money every year — gas system operation, leachate treatment, cover repair, sampling — and the rule lets a state shorten it on a showing that health and the environment stay protected. The data for that showing exists, scattered across years of consultant reports.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Each demonstration is a bespoke consulting engagement, the data spans several systems and formats, and the burden of proof is statistical and site-specific.

## What to Build
A platform that assembles each closed unit's history — gas flow and composition, leachate volume and chemistry, settlement, groundwater trends — projects each to the end of the care period with uncertainty, tests stability criteria from state guidance, and drafts the demonstration for a reduced monitoring schedule, an end to leachate management or termination of care.

## Target Customer
Closed-sites directors at consolidators and public owners with portfolios of closed units.

## Impact If Built
Ending care ~10 years early at a closed unit spending ~$300K a year (estimate) saves ~$3M undiscounted and releases the matching financial assurance.
