# Landfill RNG Developers' Environmental Attribute Desks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landfill-operators|Landfill Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Developers that lease landfill gas rights for a royalty, build RNG or power plants and sell the gas with its environmental attributes — OPAL Fuels, Archaea Energy (owned by bp), Montauk Renewables, Ameresco and others, alongside WM's own Renewable Energy segment. Their attribute desks register pathways, generate and separate RINs, file LCFS reports, manage quality-assurance audits and sell credits and gas under offtake contracts. Landfills receive royalties; refiners, utilities and fleet fuel buyers pay the developers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 market, pathway and offtake-pricing analysts at the largest developers (estimate); the staff who generate, separate and file RINs and LCFS reports work facility by facility against per-filing deadlines and are production, so they are not counted
**Output:** Carbon-intensity pathway applications, credit sales strategy and offtake pricing
**Proprietary data:** Their plants' production, gas quality, credit generation and realised prices; the host landfill's wellfield readings where the developer operates the field
**Clock:** EPA RIN reporting and LCFS quarterly reports — per-filing deadlines on the developer's own production, which bind the filers
**Buyer:** Chief commercial officer or head of environmental attributes

**Kill switches:** none on the developers' own plant, credit and sales records; would be live on host landfills' wellfield data held under gas rights agreements, which is the half a cross-site product would need.
**Verdict:** Logged — fails gate (fewer than 10 analysts once the credit filers, who are production, are set aside). Gas and credits are the invoice, not analysis, and a dozen developers make a thin market. Their revenue depends on the collection efficiency the hub's high-impact problem describes, which makes them strong buyers of operator-side tools rather than an insight pocket.
