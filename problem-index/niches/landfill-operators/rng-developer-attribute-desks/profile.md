# Landfill RNG Developers' Environmental Attribute Desks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landfill-operators|Landfill Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Developers that lease landfill gas rights for a royalty, build RNG or power plants and sell the gas with its environmental attributes — OPAL Fuels, Archaea Energy (owned by bp), Montauk Renewables, Ameresco and others, alongside WM's own Renewable Energy segment. Their attribute desks register pathways, generate and separate RINs, file LCFS reports, manage quality-assurance audits and sell credits and gas under offtake contracts. Landfills receive royalties; refiners, utilities and fleet fuel buyers pay the developers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10–30 environmental attribute, regulatory and commercial analysts at the largest developers (estimate)
**Output:** RIN and LCFS generation reports, carbon-intensity pathway applications, credit sales and offtake pricing
**Proprietary data:** Their plants' production, gas quality, credit generation and realised prices; the host landfill's wellfield readings where the developer operates the field
**Clock:** EPA RIN reporting and LCFS quarterly reports — per-filing deadlines on the developer's own production
**Buyer:** Chief commercial officer or head of environmental attributes

**Kill switches:** none for the developers' own plant, credit and sales records; would be live for host landfills' wellfield data held under gas rights agreements, which is the half a cross-site product would need.
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. Gas and credits are the invoice, not analysis; the filing deadlines bind production work; and a dozen developers make a thin market. Their revenue depends on the collection efficiency the hub's high-impact problem describes, which makes them strong buyers of operator-side tools rather than an insight pocket.
