# Haul Tickets That Never Match the Flow Meter

**Niche:** [[niches/landfill-operators/leachate-pfas-management/profile|Leachate Management & PFAS]]
**Industry:** [[industries/landfill-operators|Landfill Operators]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Leachate leaving by truck is counted on paper haul tickets, leachate pumped is counted by meters, and the two rarely agree when the state report or the treatment plant's invoice arrives.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #large-language-models #data-integration #compliance #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep leachate moving off the liner as treatment plants tighten PFAS acceptance, and whoever has the fewest days with leachate head above the 30 cm design limit takes the account.

## The Problem
Haulers leave paper or PDF tickets; plants invoice on their own measurements; meters drift. The environmental manager reconciles by hand each month and explains gaps to the state and the plant.

## Why It's Still Broken
Tickets arrive in different formats from several haulers, meters are not maintained as billing instruments, and nobody owns the reconciliation.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Tickets read automatically from photos and PDFs, matched to tank drawdowns and meter readings by time, with mismatches flagged within a day and a reconciled monthly volume for the state report and invoice check.

## Who Feels the Pain
Environmental managers, accounts payable, and plants that cap volumes and need accurate counts.

## Impact If Fixed
Catching a 5% over-billing on ~$600K of annual hauling and treatment charges (estimate) saves ~$30K a year and removes ~2 days of monthly reconciliation.
