# Air Permit Compliance Platforms for Landfill Rules

**Niche:** [[niches/landfill-operators/monitoring-data-regulatory-reporting/profile|Environmental Monitoring Data & Regulatory Reporting]]
**Industry:** [[industries/landfill-operators|Landfill Operators]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Chemical plants and oil and gas operators already run air permit compliance software that turns monitoring data into deviation logs and semiannual reports; landfills' NSPS and emission-guideline obligations are rarely loaded into them.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a landfill's monitoring data into regulatory reports that survive agency review, and whoever has the fewest deficiencies found by the agency per report takes the account.

## The Problem
Landfill air compliance — wellhead and surface exceedances, follow-ups, semiannual reports — is tracked in spreadsheets and consultant files.

## What Already Exists
EHS and air compliance platforms (Cority, Enablon, Sphera) hold permit conditions as tasks, ingest monitoring data, log deviations and generate periodic reports for process industries.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: Subpart XXX and Cf rules (5-day wellhead corrections, 10-day and one-month surface re-monitoring, 120-day new wells, 55 °C temperature limits); state plan and Title V variations; surface traverse data from walking or drone surveys; GHGRP Subpart HH calculations while they remain; and EPA's electronic reporting formats.

## Target Customer
Air compliance managers at landfill owners with many sites under Title V.

## Impact If Solved
Cutting report preparation from ~60 to ~20 hours per site per semiannual period (estimate) saves ~80 hours a year per site, ~8,000 hours across a 100-site network.
