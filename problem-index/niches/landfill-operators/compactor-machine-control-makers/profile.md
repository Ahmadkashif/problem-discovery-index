# Landfill Compactor & Machine-Control Makers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landfill-operators|Landfill Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Makers of landfill compactors and the machine-control and telematics systems on them — Caterpillar, Tana, BOMAG and Al-Jon for machines, Trimble and Carlson for GNSS guidance and pass mapping. Landfill owners buy machines (a large compactor costs ~$1M+, estimate) through dealers, with telematics subscriptions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Engineering and product teams, not an insight function; landfill application analytics under 10 per maker (estimate)
**Output:** Machines, telematics dashboards, application guides
**Proprietary data:** Machine telematics from customers' fleets
**Clock:** Product cycles and emissions-tier deadlines on the makers' engines
**Buyer:** Waste-industry product manager
