# Landfill Gas Collection & RNG Projects

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landfill-operators|Landfill Operators]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to pull more of the methane a landfill generates into the collection system and the RNG plant, and whoever delivers the most methane per ton of waste in place takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3B a year of landfill gas electricity, pipeline gas and RNG sales including RIN and LCFS credits, plus collection-system O&M and capital (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of landfill-related revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — readings are digital and automated wellheads are spreading, but most wells are tuned by hand monthly
**Target Buyer:** VP of renewable energy at a landfill owner, or head of operations at an RNG developer paying gas royalties
**Automation Potential:** Medium-high — wellhead tuning, anomaly flags and compliance follow-ups automate

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The gas a landfill generates is both its largest emission and a revenue stream. EPA's LMOP counted 542 operational energy projects at 488 landfills in September 2024 and 444 more candidate landfills. WM's Renewable Energy segment earned $478M in 2025 from 86 projects, and Republic was engaged in 84 projects with more than 30 in development; many sites lease their gas to developers such as OPAL Fuels, Archaea Energy (bp), Montauk and Ameresco for a royalty. RNG revenue moves with D3 RIN prices, which fell to ~$2.45 in 2025 from ~$3.13 (search summary), so every MMBtu collected counts. Competitors fighting over collection are in-house wellfield teams, O&M contractors running fields for public owners, automated wellhead vendors (Loci Controls), drilling and construction contractors, and the developers themselves. Whoever delivers the most methane per ton of waste in place wins the gas rights and the O&M contract.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Portable analysers (QED Landtec GEM) and vendor databases store readings; Loci Controls automates valve tuning, as often as hourly (search summary); EPA's LandGEM model estimates generation; RNG plants log inlet flow and quality. Gaps: no field-wide view of where generated gas is not being collected, valve changes recorded without reasons, and landfill and developer each holding half the data.
