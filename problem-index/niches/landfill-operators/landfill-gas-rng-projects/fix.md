# Royalty Disputes Over Gas Nobody Can Trace

**Niche:** [[niches/landfill-operators/landfill-gas-rng-projects/profile|Landfill Gas Collection & RNG Projects]]
**Industry:** [[industries/landfill-operators|Landfill Operators]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** When gas deliveries fall, the developer blames the landfill's wellfield and cover and the landfill blames the plant's downtime, and neither can prove it because each holds half the record.
**Tags:** #descriptive-statistics #causal-inference #data-integration #revenue-impact #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to pull more of the methane a landfill generates into the collection system and the RNG plant, and whoever delivers the most methane per ton of waste in place takes the account.

## The Problem
Gas rights agreements split duties: often the landfill runs the wellfield and the developer the plant, or the reverse, with royalties on gas delivered or revenue. Shortfalls trigger disputes over oxygen, flow and downtime.

## Why It's Still Broken
Wellfield readings, blower data and plant logs sit in separate systems owned by different companies, with no shared definitions of delivered gas or excused downtime.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A shared, time-aligned record of wellfield readings, header flows, blower status, plant inlet quality and downtime, with a monthly attribution of every shortfall to wellfield, cover, weather or plant cause under the contract's definitions.

## Who Feels the Pain
Developers' commercial managers, landfill renewable energy managers and the technicians caught between them.

## Impact If Fixed
Resolving a 5% delivery shortfall within a month instead of a year (estimate) on a site earning ~$5M a year from gas and credits keeps ~$250K a year from going unrecovered while the parties argue.
