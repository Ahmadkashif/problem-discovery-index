Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Landfill engineers sequence cells, forecast remaining life and calculate depletion for the accounts in a mix of CAD files, survey software and spreadsheets.

## What Already Exists
Mine planning and reserves software such as Deswik and Maptek Vulcan holds a 3D model of the deposit, schedules extraction by period, reconciles plan to survey, and feeds reserve depletion into the accounts.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: airspace instead of ore as the depleting volume; tons from scale tickets converted through measured density; daily, intermediate and final cover as a deduction; settlement that recovers airspace over time; permitted versus expansion capacity with permit status; cell construction schedules tied to the capital plan; and the depletion and closure-liability calculations the auditors test.

## Target Customer
Directors of landfill engineering at consolidators and large regional owners running dozens of sites.

## Impact If Solved
A network that books depletion on survey-reconciled airspace instead of annual estimates avoids the year-end adjustments WM shows as "changes in engineering estimates" (a net 11M-ton reduction in 2025), and plans each cell 6–12 months earlier (estimate).
