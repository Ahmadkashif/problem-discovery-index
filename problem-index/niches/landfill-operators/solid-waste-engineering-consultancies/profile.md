# Solid Waste Engineering & Landfill Permitting Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landfill-operators|Landfill Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation firms

## What They Do
Engineering firms that design cells, liners, caps and gas systems, prepare expansion and renewal permit applications, estimate closure and post-closure costs, run groundwater programmes and file compliance reports for landfill owners — SCS Engineers, Geosyntec, Weaver Consultants Group, Civil & Environmental Consultants, WSP (Golder), HDR and Barr among them. Public landfills rely on them almost entirely; consolidators use them alongside in-house engineers. Owners pay by engagement.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Hundreds of solid waste engineers and scientists at the largest firms (estimate), working site by site and engagement by engagement, which the rubric counts as production; no firm keeps a 10-person research function selling landfill analysis
**Output:** Permit applications, designs, closure cost estimates, monitoring and compliance reports
**Proprietary data:** Project files across many landfills, owned by the clients
**Clock:** Permit and reporting deadlines — clients' clocks on per-site work
**Buyer:** Solid waste practice leader
