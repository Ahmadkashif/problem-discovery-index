# Landfill Valuation, Property-Tax & M&A Advisers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landfill-operators|Landfill Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation firms

## What They Do
Investment bankers covering environmental services, appraisers who value landfills and their airspace for acquisitions, financing and property-tax appeals, and engineering due-diligence teams that check remaining capacity, permits and closure liabilities for buyers and lenders. Buyers, sellers, lenders and taxpayers pay per engagement.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 landfill specialists per firm (estimate), working deal by deal
**Output:** Valuations, appraisal reports, diligence reports, fairness opinions
**Proprietary data:** Deal files and comparable transactions under confidentiality
**Clock:** Deal timelines and tax appeal deadlines — the clients' clocks
**Buyer:** Practice leader
