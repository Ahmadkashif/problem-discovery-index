# Landfill Drone Survey & Airspace Volumetrics Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landfill-operators|Landfill Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Drone mapping platforms and survey service firms that measure landfill volumes, density and remaining airspace — Propeller, DroneDeploy and Firmatek among them — and Sniffer Robotics, whose methane-sensing drone EPA accepted in December 2022 as an alternative to walking surface surveys. Landfill owners pay per flight, per site subscription or per survey.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 landfill-focused analysts per vendor (estimate); pilots and processing staff work site by site and are production
**Output:** Surface models, volume and density reports, methane maps
**Proprietary data:** Survey archives of clients' sites
**Clock:** Clients' survey schedules and accounting periods
**Buyer:** Head of product or industry lead for waste
