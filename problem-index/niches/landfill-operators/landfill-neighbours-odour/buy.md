# Airport Noise Complaint Systems for Odour

**Niche:** [[niches/landfill-operators/landfill-neighbours-odour/profile|Landfill Neighbours & Odour Complaints]]
**Industry:** [[industries/landfill-operators|Landfill Operators]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Airports already run complaint systems that take neighbours' reports, match each one to the flight that caused it and answer publicly; landfills face the same neighbours with odour instead of noise.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stop landfill odour reaching the neighbours, and whoever has the fewest verified odour complaints per month takes the account.

## The Problem
Landfills learn about odour from regulators and respond case by case.

## What Already Exists
Airport noise and complaint management platforms collect complaints by app, web and phone, match them to flight tracks and noise monitors, publish responses and report trends to community boards.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: odour sensors, wind and dispersion in place of flight tracks; site activities as candidate sources; the air agency's complaint verification and nuisance rules; host-community agreement reporting; and response workflows that dispatch an odour patrol.

## Target Customer
Landfills under abatement orders or with expansions pending, and the host communities that negotiate with them.

## Impact If Solved
Answering each complaint directly within a day could cut repeat complaints by ~20% (estimate); at a landfill logging ~500 complaints a year (estimate) that is ~100 fewer agency cases.
