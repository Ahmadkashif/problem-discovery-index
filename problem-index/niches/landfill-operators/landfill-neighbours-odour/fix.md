# Complaints That Reach the Regulator First

**Niche:** [[niches/landfill-operators/landfill-neighbours-odour/profile|Landfill Neighbours & Odour Complaints]]
**Industry:** [[industries/landfill-operators|Landfill Operators]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Neighbours call the air district because there is nowhere better to call, and the landfill learns of the odour days later in an inspector's notice.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #descriptive-statistics #workflow-orchestration #compliance #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stop landfill odour reaching the neighbours, and whoever has the fewest verified odour complaints per month takes the account.

## The Problem
By the time an inspector confirms an odour and issues a notice, the cause has passed and the landfill can only explain. At Chiquita Canyon the count reached ~410 notices.

## Why It's Still Broken
Landfills do not offer neighbours a channel they trust, and agency complaint data is not shared in real time.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A public reporting line by text, app and phone that geotags and timestamps each report, dispatches an odour patrol within the hour, logs the finding and the action, and shares the record with the agency.

## Who Feels the Pain
Neighbours, landfill managers, and inspectors chasing complaints after the fact.

## Impact If Fixed
Responding within an hour instead of days could halve the complaints that become notices (estimate); at ~$10K per notice in penalties and response (estimate), 20 fewer a year saves ~$200K.
