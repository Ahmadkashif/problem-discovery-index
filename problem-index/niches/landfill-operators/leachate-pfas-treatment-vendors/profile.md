# Leachate Treatment & PFAS Destruction Technology Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landfill-operators|Landfill Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors that treat leachate on site or destroy the PFAS concentrated from it — evaporation and thermal systems (Heartland Water Technology, Veolia), membrane systems, and newer destruction technologies (Aclarity's electrochemical oxidation, Revive Environmental, 374Water's supercritical water oxidation). Landfill owners pay for equipment, treatment by the gallon or pilots, ahead of EPA's planned PFAS revision of the landfill effluent guideline.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 process and data analysts per vendor on landfill work (estimate)
**Output:** Treatability studies, pilot reports, performance data
**Proprietary data:** Treatment performance on clients' leachate
**Clock:** Clients' permit limits and outlet deadlines
**Buyer:** Head of applications or business development for landfills
