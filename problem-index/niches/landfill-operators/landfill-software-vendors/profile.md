# Scale-House, Wellfield & Compliance Software Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landfill-operators|Landfill Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Software sold to landfills: scale-house and billing systems (Paradigm's Compass, Carolina Software's WasteWORKS, AMCS), special-waste profile platforms (Wastebits), wellfield data and automation (QED's Landtec database, Loci Controls' WellWatcher, which added AI summaries in August 2026 using a model trained on anonymised monitoring data), and environmental data platforms. Landfills pay subscriptions and licences.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 data and analytics staff per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Software, benchmark features, AI summaries
**Proprietary data:** Tickets, readings and profiles held for clients
**Clock:** Product release cycles
**Buyer:** Chief product officer
