# Landfill Gas Wellfield O&M & Surface Emissions Monitoring Contractors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landfill-operators|Landfill Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Field-service firms that tune wellfields, run blowers and flares, pump liquids, walk the quarterly surface emission traverses and fly drone surveys for landfill owners and RNG developers, including the field-services arms of solid waste engineering firms and regional contractors. Owners pay per site per month or per survey.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Field technicians working site by site (estimate; dozens at the larger contractors), which is production; analysis is a few engineers per firm
**Output:** Monthly wellfield readings and adjustments, surface monitoring results, exceedance follow-ups
**Proprietary data:** Readings across many landfills, collected for and owned by the site owners
**Clock:** NSPS monthly wellhead and quarterly surface schedules — the clients' clocks
**Buyer:** Field services manager
