# EPA Landfill Methane & State Solid Waste Permitting Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/landfill-operators|Landfill Operators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
EPA writes the landfill air rules (NSPS Subpart XXX and the Cf emission guidelines, enforced through state plans and the 2021 federal plan), runs the Landfill Methane Outreach Program database of more than 2,600 MSW landfills and 542 operational energy projects, collects greenhouse gas reports under GHGRP Subpart HH (proposed for elimination in September 2025), and is revising the landfill effluent guideline for PFAS. State solid waste programmes permit and inspect landfills under Subtitle D, approve expansions, review closure cost estimates and hold financial assurance. Taxpayers and permit fees fund them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Hundreds of permit writers and inspectors across EPA and the larger states (estimate), who work case by case and are production; programme and data analysts number in the tens per agency (estimate)
**Output:** Rules, permits, inspection and enforcement decisions, LMOP and GHGRP data releases
**Proprietary data:** LMOP, GHGRP and permit files, which are published or public on request
**Clock:** The agencies' own rulemaking schedules and the LMOP refresh two to three times a year; statutory deadlines bind the regulated landfills, not the agencies' analysis
**Buyer:** Office director or state solid waste programme manager
