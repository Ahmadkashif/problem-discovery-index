# Fee & Interchange Optimisation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/payment-processors|Payment Processors]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get transactions qualifying at the interchange rate they are entitled to — and whoever does that returns money merchants are currently paying for data they had and did not send.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$12B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — schedules navigated by convention
**Target Buyer:** Merchant finance and processor commercial teams
**Automation Potential:** Very High — qualification is rule-based and checkable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Interchange is a schedule of hundreds of rates with qualification conditions covering data provided, transaction type, timing, card product and merchant category. A transaction that could have qualified at a lower rate and did not costs the merchant the difference, every time, forever. The conditions are published, the qualification is checkable, and most merchants and many processors navigate the schedule by convention and accept whatever rate the transaction received. This is distinct from routing: that changes where the transaction goes, this changes what it qualifies for once it gets there.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Published interchange schedules, processor statements showing applied rates, occasional interchange optimisation consulting, and level two and three data submission for commercial cards. The gaps: qualification failures invisible on a statement; optional data determining rate not submitted; downgrades unattributed; pricing models that obscure the underlying rate; and no monitoring of qualification performance.
