# The Integration Engineer

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/payment-processors|Payment Processors]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stop the same twenty questions arriving forever — and whoever fixes the product so they are not asked changes what a support function is for.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$9B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — log fragments and a screenshot
**Target Buyer:** Developer support leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — diagnosis is automatable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Support engineers debug other companies' checkout code from log fragments and a screenshot, answering the same twenty questions in a rotation that never ends. They are diagnosing software they cannot see, written by engineers they cannot talk to, against a payments domain with enough subtlety that the same misunderstandings recur constantly. It is a distinct role with a distinct failure: the questions recur because the product allows the mistakes, and the support function absorbs the consequence indefinitely rather than the product changing.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Ticketing, log search, documentation, sandbox environments, and the engineer's accumulated knowledge. The gaps: diagnosis from fragments the merchant chose to send; no view of the merchant's own implementation; recurring questions answered individually forever; no feedback from support volume to product design; and knowledge held by individuals.
