# Settlement & Reconciliation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/payment-processors|Payment Processors]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to prove that money moved across networks, currencies and fee schedules adds up — and whoever does that automatically removes the largest manual finance function in payments.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$16B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — spreadsheets at industrial scale
**Target Buyer:** Finance operations leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — matching is mechanical

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Money moves through networks, currencies, fee schedules and settlement timetables that each report differently, and the work of proving the totals agree is done by people with spreadsheets. Reconciliation is the function that establishes whether the institution actually received what it is owed and paid what it owes, across counterparties whose reports use different identifiers, different timing conventions and different levels of detail. It is a large, permanent, manual function inside businesses that are otherwise exceptional engineering organisations.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Network settlement files, bank statements, internal transaction records, spreadsheets and reconciliation tools of varying maturity. The gaps: identifiers that do not match across sources; timing conventions that differ; fee schedules applied after the fact and checked manually; breaks investigated by hand; and unexplained differences written off below a threshold.
