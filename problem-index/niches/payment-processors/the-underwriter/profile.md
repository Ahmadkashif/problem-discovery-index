# The Underwriter

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/payment-processors|Payment Processors]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give the underwriter evidence and feedback rather than a website and a clock — and whoever does that turns an unmeasured judgement into a skill that can improve.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$10B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — a website and an approval target
**Target Buyer:** Risk operations leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — evidence gathering automatable, judgement assisted

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Underwriters decide a business's fate by reading its website and its processing history under an approval-time target, and never learn which of their decisions was right. They are making a counterparty credit judgement with a genuine loss exposure attached, on evidence they assemble by browsing, against a clock set by a sales organisation, with no feedback loop of any kind. This is distinct from the underwriting systems niche: that is what should be automated and measured, this is the person doing it and what they are given to do it with.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Registry lookups, the applicant's website, processing history where supplied, a risk score, and an approval-time target. The gaps: evidence assembled by browsing; no structured view of the business model; no comparable cases; no outcome feedback; measurement on speed; and a decision whose consequence arrives months later to someone else.
