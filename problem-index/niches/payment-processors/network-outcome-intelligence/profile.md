# Network Outcome Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/payment-processors|Payment Processors]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a network-scale record of approvals, declines and settlements into knowledge no issuer and no merchant can have — and whoever does that owns the empirical basis for questions the industry answers with folklore.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$10B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — used for billing
**Target Buyer:** Processor data leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the data is complete and labelled

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A large processor observes the same cardholder, across thousands of merchants, being approved and declined by hundreds of issuers, with the eventual settlement outcome of every attempt. That is a vantage point nobody else in the system has — not the issuer, who sees only their own cardholders; not the merchant, who sees only their own transactions; not the network, whose role is different. It is the empirical basis for every question this category currently answers with convention, and it is generated as a by-product and used mainly for billing.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Transaction and settlement records used for billing and reporting, aggregate authorisation rate dashboards, and occasional analysis. The gaps: decisions not joined to settlement outcomes; issuer behaviour not modelled individually; cardholder-level cross-merchant view unused; no empirical basis for retry, routing or decline interpretation; and the asset treated as an operational record rather than as intelligence.
