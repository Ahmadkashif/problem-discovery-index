# Task Discovery & Unpaid Search Time

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/crowdsourcing-platforms|Crowdsourcing Platforms]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Whether finding work is part of the work, and who pays for the hours spent doing it.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$360M — 12% of the US microtask and research-participant market
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12%
**Digital Adoption:** Very low on-platform — workers have built their own
**Target Buyer:** Crowdworkers; platform product leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very high — matching and alerting are solved problems elsewhere

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche

Searching for tasks, reading instructions, completing qualification tests, and working on tasks that turn out to be broken or that time out are all uncompensated, and on platforms where task discovery is itself difficult this can be a large share of time spent.

The search problem is severe on the larger microtask platforms. Thousands of batches, most unsuitable, many already exhausted, with search and filtering that has barely changed in years. Workers refresh queues, scan listings, evaluate requester reputation from memory or from an external site, and open tasks to find out what they involve — all unpaid, and all before any earning begins.

The niche is distinct because the workers have already solved it themselves. Browser extensions, forum alert threads, scripts and shared watchlists exist precisely because the platform's own discovery is inadequate, and their existence is a direct measurement of the gap.

## Current Tools & Gaps

Task listings with basic filters — reward, requester, qualification, time allowed. Search over titles and keywords. Qualification-based visibility. Little personalisation and little alerting.

The gaps are matching, alerting and preview. Nothing learns which tasks a worker completes successfully and surfaces similar ones. Nothing alerts when a batch matching a worker's profile appears, though good batches are exhausted within minutes and this is the single most valuable feature for anyone earning here. And nothing shows what a task actually involves before the worker opens it and spends time finding out.
