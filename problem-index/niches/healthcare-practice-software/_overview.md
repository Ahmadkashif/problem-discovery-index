## Why These Niches

The ambulatory software market splits cleanly along the two numbers its customers actually watch. Revenue cycle is the larger half and the one where switching happens: a practice whose denial rate moves two points takes competitor calls within the quarter, which makes denial prediction the single contested capability of the biggest niche in the category. Specialty platforms are the other large block and are **not terminal as a single niche** — ophthalmology and dermatology are both specialty EHR markets and they contest on entirely different mechanics, so the niche is decomposed below rather than papered over.

Behavioral health and house-call practices are the two genuinely underdigitized ambulatory settings left, and both are underdigitized for a specific structural reason rather than for lack of interest: 42 CFR Part 2 makes ordinary record sharing legally hazardous, and home-based care happens where connectivity does not. FQHCs and solo practices are the two underserved buyers — one is underserved because its reporting obligations are unlike anyone else's, the other because the category's economics assume an implementation consultant it cannot afford.

The two automation niches are the parts of this industry that are pure recurring labour: maintaining payer edit content against thousands of plan-state combinations that change weekly, and hand-mapping a decade of a competitor's database on every migration. Both are contested on a measurable capability, not on price.

## Filter Notes

**Niche 2 failed the filter as stated and was decomposed.** "Specialty EHR platforms" cannot carry one contested sentence: an ophthalmology vendor wins on whether diagnostic images from six device manufacturers arrive in the chart already bound to the laterality and modifier structure the claim needs, and a dermatology vendor wins on whether the biopsy sent out on Tuesday comes back, gets matched to the lesion it came from, and closes the loop before the patient is lost. Those are different problems with different competitors. Both sub-niches are terminal and are written in full.

**Niches 1, 3–8 are terminal as stated.** Each contested sentence names a specific capability that a buyer can test in a bake-off.
