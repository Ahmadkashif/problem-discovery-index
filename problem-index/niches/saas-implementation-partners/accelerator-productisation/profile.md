# Accelerator Productisation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/saas-implementation-partners|SaaS Implementation Partners]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a known pattern into a deployable asset that survives three platform releases a year and a dozen client variations — and whoever maintains that takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$7B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — built once, rotted
**Target Buyer:** Practice and product leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — packaging, parameterisation and maintenance

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This half is product engineering. A pattern becomes an accelerator only if it is packaged, parameterised, deployable, tested against each platform release, and owned by someone with a roadmap. Every large partner has built accelerators and watched them rot, because nobody funded the maintenance and the platform moved underneath them. It is an investment decision with a running cost, sold to practice leadership, and it fails in an entirely different way from the extraction problem.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A handful of accelerators of uncertain currency and a slide claiming they exist. The gaps: no ownership or roadmap; no testing against platform releases; no parameterisation for client variation; no versioning or deployment tooling; and no measure of whether they are used.
