# Adoption Outcome Evidence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/saas-implementation-partners|SaaS Implementation Partners]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to know which of its configurations were actually adopted, when the evidence accumulates in the client's tenant the day after the partner leaves — and whoever collects it takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$13B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~22% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — nothing returns
**Target Buyer:** Firm leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — usage telemetry and joining

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The engagement ends at go-live and the evidence starts accumulating the day after. Which configurations get used, which workflows are abandoned, which fields are never populated, whether adoption held at ninety days — all of it sits in the customer's tenant, and the partner who built it is usually gone. The firm performs the same experiment hundreds of times and records the result zero times, which is why it sells consultants who know the platform rather than evidence about what works.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A go-live checklist, a hypercare period and a satisfaction survey. The gaps: no adoption measurement in any engagement; no access after handover; no baseline of what was expected to be used; no comparison across clients; and no record of which configurations succeeded.
