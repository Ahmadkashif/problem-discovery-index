# Configuration Reuse

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/saas-implementation-partners|SaaS Implementation Partners]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stop rebuilding what the firm has already built hundreds of times, and whoever industrialises that reuse takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$15B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~25% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — slides and memory
**Target Buyer:** Delivery leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — extraction and productisation

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Requirements workshops rediscover requirements near-identical to the last twelve engagements in the same industry, configurations are rebuilt rather than reused, and integration patterns implemented dozens of times are designed again because nobody catalogued them in a form that survives. The firm's four-hundred-implementation experience compounds into individual intuitions and a library of slides, and the labour cost of that is the single largest recoverable item in the business.

### Contested sub-niches
This niche is not terminal. It names a practice, and two independently failing problems sit inside it:
- [[niches/saas-implementation-partners/pattern-extraction/profile|🎯 Pattern Extraction]] — what the past implementations have in common
- [[niches/saas-implementation-partners/accelerator-productisation/profile|🎯 Accelerator Productisation]] — turning a pattern into a maintained asset

## Current Tools & Gaps
A slide library, a few accelerators of uncertain currency, and consultants who remember. The gaps: no catalogue of what has been configured; no abstraction of recurring patterns; no maintained deployable assets; no measurement of what is rebuilt; and no route from a past engagement to a current one.
