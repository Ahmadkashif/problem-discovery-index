# The Post-Go-Live Support Engineer

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/saas-implementation-partners|SaaS Implementation Partners]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let someone support a configuration built by people who have left, documented in a slide deck, with no record of why any of it is the way it is — and whoever supplies that context takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$5B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — no context
**Target Buyer:** Managed services leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — configuration comprehension and intent recovery

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
After go-live, someone has to support a configuration built by people who have left, documented in a slide deck, with no explanation of why any of it is the way it is. Every change request means reverse-engineering intent from metadata. Every incident means establishing what the system is supposed to do before establishing what went wrong. The role carries the client relationship for years and inherits none of the context that produced the thing it supports.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A ticketing system, access to the tenant, a handover deck and whoever is still at the firm. The gaps: no record of design intent; no map of what depends on what; no documented client-specific decisions; no route to the people who built it; and no way to tell a deliberate configuration from an accident.
