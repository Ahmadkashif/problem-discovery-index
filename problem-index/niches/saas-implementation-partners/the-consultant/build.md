# Making Reuse Easier Than Rebuilding

**Niche:** [[niches/saas-implementation-partners/the-consultant/profile|The Consultant on Two Engagements]]
**Industry:** [[industries/saas-implementation-partners|SaaS Implementation Partners]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** The consultant is measured on hours and rewarded for rebuilding what the firm already owns.
**Tags:** #worker-facing #workflow-orchestration #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #large-language-models #tacit-knowledge-ml #automation #descriptive-statistics
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stop a consultant measured on billable hours from spending the unbilled remainder rebuilding what the firm has already built — and whoever fixes that takes the account.

## The Problem
A consultant works across two or three clients, on the same platform, solving problems a colleague solved last quarter. They cannot find the colleague's work, do not know it exists, and have no time to look. The utilisation target rewards hours delivered rather than problems solved quickly. Rebuilding is the path of least resistance and the firm's cumulative expertise never reaches the person who needs it.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Utilisation is the measured metric and reuse reduces it. Prior work lives in client tenants and project folders with no index. The consultant is not the buyer of tooling. And the cost is absorbed in hours that get billed anyway.

## What to Build
Put the prior work in front of them and change what is measured. Surface relevant prior configurations and patterns at the moment a consultant is designing, rather than expecting a search, which is the core and is the only version that competes with rebuilding on effort. Make the firm's expertise locatable — who has done this, on which client, when — since a two-minute conversation beats a two-day rebuild and is currently impossible. Reduce the context-switching cost between clients with saved state, environment access and per-client notes in one place. Measure and reward reuse alongside utilisation, which is the incentive change the rest depends on. Show the consultant what the firm has built for similar clients as part of engagement onboarding. Capture what they build back into the catalogue as a by-product rather than as an extra task. Track how much of an engagement is rebuild rather than reuse, which is the number that justifies changing the incentive. Support the consultant across concurrent engagements rather than assuming one at a time. Give junior consultants a route to senior judgement that does not require knowing who to ask. And make the fast path the reuse path, which is the only durable version of this.

## Target Customer
Implementation partners and systems integrators, practice leadership, consultants themselves, and knowledge and collaboration tooling vendors.

## Impact If Built
Rebuilding is the path of least resistance because finding the prior work costs more than redoing it. Surfacing relevant prior configurations at design time, with reuse in the measured metrics, inverts that.
