# The Consultant on Two Engagements

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/saas-implementation-partners|SaaS Implementation Partners]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to stop a consultant measured on billable hours from spending the unbilled remainder rebuilding what the firm has already built — and whoever fixes that takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$6B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — utilisation only
**Target Buyer:** Practice leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — reuse surfacing and context switching support

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A consultant is measured on billable hours, staffed across two or three engagements at once, and spends the unbilled remainder rebuilding things the firm has already built four hundred times. The incentive structure rewards hours rather than outcomes, the context switching between clients is constant and unmeasured, and the knowledge that would make them faster is held by colleagues they have no way to reach. It is the most common role in the industry and nothing is designed around it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A timesheet, two client environments, a chat channel and a slide library. The gaps: no surfacing of relevant prior work; no reduction of context-switching cost; no recognition for reuse; no route to colleagues who have solved this; and no measure of the rebuild time anyone absorbs.
