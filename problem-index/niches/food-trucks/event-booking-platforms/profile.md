# Food Truck Event Booking Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/food-trucks|Food Trucks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Roaming Hunger, Best Food Trucks, and the booking marketplaces place trucks into corporate events, festivals, and office lunch programmes, taking a commission. They are the only party that observes what a given truck earns at a given type of location.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-25 operations and marketplace staff
**Output:** Booking placement, pricing guidance, demand matching
**Proprietary data:** Booking and revenue outcomes by truck, location type, and event — the only cross-operator record of what a location is actually worth
**Clock:** Event dates
**Buyer:** Head of Operations
