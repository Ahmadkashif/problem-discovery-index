# Municipal Health Inspection Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/food-trucks|Food Trucks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Local health departments permit and inspect mobile food units, set the commissary and equipment requirements the Pass 1 analysis describes as varying by jurisdiction, and issue the scores and closures that determine whether an operator can trade.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 inspectors and programme staff per large jurisdiction
**Output:** Permits, inspection results, enforcement actions
**Proprietary data:** Inspection findings across mobile units in the jurisdiction
**Clock:** Permit cycles and inspection schedules
**Buyer:** Health department director — public procurement
