## What They Do

**Size:** 5-30 underwriters and analysts
**Output:** Programme rating, underwriting guidelines, loss analyses
**Proprietary data:** Claim experience across mobile food operations
**Clock:** Renewal and permit cycles
**Buyer:** Programme manager
