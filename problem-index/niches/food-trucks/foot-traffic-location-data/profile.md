# Foot Traffic & Location Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/food-trucks|Food Trucks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Location analytics providers derive visit and dwell patterns from mobile device data, model foot traffic by place and hour, and sell the result to retailers, investors, and site selectors. This is directly the answer to the Pass 1 problem — a truck grossing $200 on a bad corner or $2,000 at the right event, with the operator betting on gut feel — and no food truck operator is the buyer.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 data scientists, panel engineers, and analysts
**Output:** Visit and traffic estimates, trade area analytics, site comparison — the subscription
**Proprietary data:** Device panel access and the place-resolution layer that maps signals to specific locations
**Clock:** Continuous; no external deadline governs publication
**Buyer:** Chief Data Officer / VP of Analytics
