# Commissary Kitchen Operators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/food-trucks|Food Trucks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer

## What They Do
Commissary operators provide the licensed prep and storage space health codes require mobile operators to base from, and know which of their tenants thrive and which fail.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1-8 per facility
**Output:** Facility operations, tenant compliance support
**Proprietary data:** Tenant turnover and utilization patterns
**Clock:** Health inspection and lease cycles
**Buyer:** Owner
