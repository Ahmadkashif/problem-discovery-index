# Mobile Vendor Permit Compliance Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/food-trucks|Food Trucks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Very small services help operators navigate the permitting the Pass 1 analysis calls a jurisdictional nightmare — different forms, fees, inspection cadences, and commissary rules in every city, county, and health district, where one lapse shuts a truck down mid-shift.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1-5
**Output:** Permit application support, jurisdiction requirement guidance, renewal tracking
**Proprietary data:** Assembled jurisdiction requirement knowledge — genuinely valuable and held by almost nobody
**Clock:** Permit expiry and inspection dates
**Buyer:** Owner-operator of the service
