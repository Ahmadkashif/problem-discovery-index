# Festival & Event Organizer Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/food-trucks|Food Trucks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Large festival and event organizers curate vendor line-ups, set fees and revenue shares, and analyze which food offerings drive attendee spend — decisions that determine which trucks get access to the highest-grossing days of their year.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-30 analysts and vendor managers per large organizer
**Output:** Vendor selection, fee structures, spend and attendance analytics
**Proprietary data:** Vendor sales by event and placement, across many events
**Clock:** Event calendars and vendor application deadlines
**Buyer:** Director of Vendor Operations
