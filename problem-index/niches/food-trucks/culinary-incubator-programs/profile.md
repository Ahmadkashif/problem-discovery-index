# Culinary Incubator Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/food-trucks|Food Trucks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Publicly and philanthropically funded food business incubators provide licensed kitchen space, permitting guidance, and business training to early-stage food entrepreneurs, and track outcomes for their funders.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 3-15 programme staff
**Output:** Business training, permitting support, outcome reporting to funders
**Proprietary data:** Participant business outcomes over time — one of the few longitudinal records of what happens to small food businesses
**Clock:** Grant reporting cycles
**Buyer:** Programme director — grant funded
