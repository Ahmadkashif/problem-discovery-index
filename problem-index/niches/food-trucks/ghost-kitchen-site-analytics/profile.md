# Ghost Kitchen & Food Hall Site Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/food-trucks|Food Trucks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The site selection and analytics groups at ghost kitchen and food hall operators model where delivery and walk-up demand exists, and place kitchen capacity against it — the fixed-location alternative many mobile operators eventually move into.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 analysts and real estate researchers
**Output:** Site selection models, demand estimates, unit economics
**Proprietary data:** Order and demand data across kitchen locations, joined to trade area characteristics
**Clock:** Lease and development timelines
**Buyer:** VP of Real Estate / Head of Analytics
