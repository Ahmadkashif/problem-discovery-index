# Mobile Food Association Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/food-trucks|Food Trucks]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The national and city food truck associations advocate on permitting and zoning, survey members, and produce the economic arguments used against restrictive local ordinances.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1-5, largely volunteer
**Output:** Member surveys, advocacy analysis, ordinance comparisons
**Proprietary data:** Member survey responses and assembled ordinance comparisons
**Clock:** Council calendars and ordinance hearings
**Buyer:** Executive Director
