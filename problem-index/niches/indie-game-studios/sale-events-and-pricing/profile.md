# Sale Events & Pricing

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/indie-game-studios|Indie Game Studios]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell a studio what a discount at a given depth and moment will actually earn over the title's remaining life — and whoever answers it takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$100M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — a guessed percentage
**Target Buyer:** Studio owners
**Automation Potential:** Very high — scheduling, depth selection and forecasting

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
After launch, a game's revenue comes largely from seasonal sales, bundles and regional pricing, and the decisions are made by picking a round number. Discount depth, frequency, timing against platform events and the interaction with the game's long-tail price expectation determine most of the post-launch income — and the studio has neither the comparison data nor the model to choose. It is the most mechanical decision in the industry and the least supported.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Platform sale scheduling, a discount percentage field, a bundle tool and a regional price table with defaults. The gaps: no forecast of a discount's effect; no memory of what previous depths achieved; no view of cannibalisation across sales; regional pricing left at conversion defaults; and bundle economics unmodelled.
