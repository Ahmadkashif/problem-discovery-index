# Signal Interpretation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/indie-game-studios|Indie Game Studios]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a studio's own wishlist shape, demo retention and playtest behaviour into a commercial forecast — and whoever reads those signals best tells a two-person team what three years of their life is worth while they can still change it.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — signals unread
**Target Buyer:** Studio leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — modelling against a benchmark

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This contest is modelling a studio's own data. Given a wishlist curve, a demo's retention profile, playtest session behaviour and a genre, produce a forecast and a recommendation. It is a tool sold to a studio, it operates on data the studio already generates, and its quality depends on but is separable from the benchmark corpus it is compared against.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A wishlist counter, store analytics and engine telemetry if the studio implemented it. The gaps: curve shape not analysed; demo retention not connected to outcomes; playtest stopping points not read as signal; no forecast; and no recommendation about what to change.
