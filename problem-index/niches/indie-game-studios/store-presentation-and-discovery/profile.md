# Store Presentation & Discovery

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/indie-game-studios|Indie Game Studios]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make capsule art, a trailer and the first paragraph convert a browsing player into a wishlist — and whoever tests those choices rather than asking a Discord decides the launch a year in advance.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$800M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~20% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — chosen by consensus of friends
**Target Buyer:** Studio and marketing leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — a testable conversion problem

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Capsule art, trailer, screenshots and the first paragraph determine whether anyone wishlists, and studios choose them by asking their Discord. Well over fifteen thousand games release on the largest store annually and attention is distributed extremely unequally, with visibility driven by wishlist accumulation before launch and by algorithmic surfacing that rewards early sales velocity. The store page is therefore the single highest-leverage artefact the studio produces, and it is designed by consensus among people who already like the game.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A store page editor, a Discord poll and a trailer. The gaps: presentation not tested with strangers; conversion rate not measured against comparable pages; tagging and categorisation chosen by intuition; algorithmic surfacing mechanics unexplained; and no feedback between page changes and wishlist rate.
