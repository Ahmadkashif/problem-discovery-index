# Benchmark Data Access

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/indie-game-studios|Indie Game Studios]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to assemble the cross-studio corpus of trajectories and outcomes that makes any individual game's signals readable — and whoever builds it ends the asymmetry publishers currently charge for.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$500M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — held by parties who benefit from holding it
**Target Buyer:** Data and platform leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — the obstacle is acquisition, not analysis

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This contest is data acquisition and aggregation. Interpreting a studio's signals requires knowing what those signals looked like for thousands of other games and how each turned out. That corpus sits across the platform layer, the aggregators and the publishers, and the studios hold none of it. Assembling it is a commercial and partnership problem — whose data, on what terms, with what incentive to contribute — and is entirely separate from modelling any individual game.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Public scraped data, occasional published postmortems and a publisher's private knowledge. The gaps: no outcome-linked corpus; platform data not available to studios; no contribution mechanism; the aggregators' data not structured for this; and the asymmetry commercially valuable to those who could end it.
