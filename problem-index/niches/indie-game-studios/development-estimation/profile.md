# Development Estimation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/indie-game-studios|Indie Game Studios]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell a small team when their game will actually be finished — and whoever forecasts from recorded velocity rather than from optimism prevents the failure that kills more studios than bad games do.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — nobody records velocity
**Target Buyer:** Studio leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — forecasting from recorded work

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Small teams plan eighteen months, take three years, fund the difference out of savings, and the overrun kills more studios than bad games do. Estimation in the sector is essentially absent: nobody forecasts completion from their own historical velocity because nobody records it, and the failure is financial rather than creative — the game is usually finished and the studio is not there to launch it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A spreadsheet, a task tracker if they use one, and a plan made at the start. The gaps: velocity not recorded; estimates never compared to actuals; scope changes untracked; runway not modelled against completion; and no mechanism to detect the overrun while there is still time to act.
