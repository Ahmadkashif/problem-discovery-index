# Playtest & Demo Instrumentation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/indie-game-studios|Indie Game Studios]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a playtest or a demo into a machine-readable account of where players stopped enjoying the game — and whoever does it best takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$200M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — watching over a shoulder
**Target Buyer:** Studio design leads
**Automation Potential:** Very high — telemetry, session analysis and reporting

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A playtest produces the only pre-launch evidence a studio has about whether the game works, and most indie studios run them by watching someone play and taking notes. The demo — increasingly the single largest determinant of wishlist conversion — is played by thousands of strangers whose behaviour the studio can see only as a download count. The instrumentation that would turn either into evidence is mechanical to build and almost nobody builds it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Recorded sessions, a feedback form, Discord comments and a store page download count. The gaps: no event telemetry in the build; no funnel from launch to quit; no comparison between playtest cohorts; sentiment scattered across channels; and no link between a demo player's behaviour and whether they wishlisted.
