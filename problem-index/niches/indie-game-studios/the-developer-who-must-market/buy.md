**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get eighteen months of consistent marketing out of someone who became a developer specifically because they did not want to do that — and whoever makes it survivable changes the commercial outcome of the whole sector.

## The Problem
Small business marketing software solved a version of this: an owner with no marketing background, no time and no inclination, who nonetheless needs consistent presence. The answer was scheduling, templates, asset generation, prompts and a cadence the tool maintains rather than the person. It works because it reduces the activity to approving things rather than originating them. Game developers face the same problem with a longer horizon and use raw platform accounts.

## What Already Exists
Scheduling and cadence tools; template and asset generation; content prompts and calendars; performance reporting for non-marketers; and multi-channel posting.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a product that does not exist yet and an audience built in advance. It requires: (1) marketing a thing that is not finished, so the content is development progress rather than a product — this is the substantive difference and is what makes generic tools unusable; (2) an eighteen-month horizon toward a single event, unlike an ongoing business presence; (3) a wishlist as the conversion rather than a sale, which changes what should be measured; (4) assets generated from the development work itself, which the tool should harvest rather than ask for; and (5) a developer who finds self-promotion uncomfortable, where a business owner usually accepts it as part of the job.

## Target Customer
Solo and small-team developers, publishers, festivals and events, and small business marketing vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Small business tools reduced marketing to approving rather than originating, for owners with the same constraints. Marketing something unfinished toward a single distant event is the adaptation, and harvesting assets from the development work is what makes the cadence possible.
