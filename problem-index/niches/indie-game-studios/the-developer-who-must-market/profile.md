# The Developer Who Must Market

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/indie-game-studios|Indie Game Studios]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get eighteen months of consistent marketing out of someone who became a developer specifically because they did not want to do that — and whoever makes it survivable changes the commercial outcome of the whole sector.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$500M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — sporadic effort and guilt
**Target Buyer:** The developer themselves
**Automation Potential:** High — cadence, asset production and targeting

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The commercial outcome depends on eighteen months of consistent marketing performed by someone who became a developer specifically because they did not want to do that. It is not a skills problem alone — it is a temperament, a time and a sustainability problem, and the consequence of not doing it is that three years of work reaches nobody. The person at the centre knows this, which adds guilt to a workload that is already impossible.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Social accounts, a Discord, a mailing list if they set one up, and advice from people who are naturally good at this. The gaps: no sustainable cadence; asset production competing with development; no measurement of what works; targeting by guesswork; and advice written by extroverts for extroverts.
