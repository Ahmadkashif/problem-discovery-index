# The Databook Rebuilt for Every Bidder

**Niche:** [[niches/investment-banking-boutiques/quality-of-earnings-providers/profile|Quality of Earnings & Financial Due Diligence Providers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The QoE databook is rebuilt by hand each time the numbers move, and every version must be re-tied to the CIM and the banker's model.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious QoE provider is fighting to deliver an adjusted EBITDA that survives the other side's diligence inside the banker's timetable — and whoever can show, from its own engagement record, which adjustments hold up takes the next mandate from the same sponsors and bankers.

## The Problem
During a process the target closes another month, management revises a forecast, a bidder asks for a new cut. Each change ripples through the databook, the report, the CIM and the model, and the QoE team re-ties them manually.

## Why It's Still Broken
The databook, report and banker artefacts live in different files owned by different firms.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A databook generated from a single structured dataset, with versioned refreshes and automated reconciliation against the banker's model and CIM figures, flagging every number that moved.

## Who Feels the Pain
QoE seniors and managers in the final weeks of a process; bankers waiting on the refreshed numbers.

## Impact If Fixed
Refreshes in hours rather than days, and fewer inconsistencies between the documents bidders compare.
