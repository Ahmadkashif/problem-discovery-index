# Quality of Earnings & Financial Due Diligence Providers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Accounting firms' transaction advisory practices — the Big Four and national firms such as RSM, BDO, Grant Thornton and Baker Tilly, plus specialist mid-market diligence firms — that produce the sell-side and buy-side quality of earnings reports every boutique-run sale depends on. The report establishes adjusted EBITDA, net working capital and net debt, and it is the document on which price is argued, re-traded and later disputed. Sellers pay for sell-side QoE; buyers and their lenders pay for buy-side.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Hundreds to several thousand transaction advisory professionals per large firm; 20-200 at mid-market specialists
**Output:** Quality of earnings reports, net working capital analyses, net debt schedules, databooks
**Proprietary data:** Adjustment-level working papers across thousands of engagements — which add-backs were proposed, accepted or rejected, how working capital pegs were set and later disputed — held as the firm's own work papers
**Clock:** The sale process timetable set by the banker's process letter: management presentation, IOI, LOI and signing dates
**Buyer:** Transaction advisory services practice leader
