# Private-Company Transaction Benchmarks

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Services that collect and publish multiples on private mid-market transactions that are otherwise undisclosed — GF Data (contributed by private equity sponsors) and BVR's DealStats among them — used by bankers, valuation firms and courts.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 researchers and data staff
**Output:** Quarterly reports and transaction databases of private-deal multiples and terms
**Proprietary data:** Contributed and collected private transaction details that are not otherwise public
**Clock:** Quarterly publication cycle, self-set
**Buyer:** Publisher / head of research
