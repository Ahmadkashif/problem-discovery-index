**Parent Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]

## What They Do
Economic consulting firms — Compass Lexecon, CRA International, Charles River, Analysis Group, Cornerstone Research — that analyse market definition and competitive effects for mergers under HSR review and second requests.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Several hundred economists and analysts per major firm
**Output:** Merger simulation, market definition and effects analyses, expert reports
**Proprietary data:** Prior engagement analyses and data, largely client-supplied
**Clock:** HSR waiting periods, second-request timing agreements and court schedules
**Buyer:** Practice leader
