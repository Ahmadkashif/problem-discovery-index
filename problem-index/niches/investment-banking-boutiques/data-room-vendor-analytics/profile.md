# Virtual Data Room Vendor Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Data room vendors — Datasite, Intralinks (SS&C), Ansarada, Firmex — that host every process and publish market-activity indicators derived from early-stage room openings, such as Intralinks' Deal Flow Predictor.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 analysts in research and insights teams
**Output:** Deal-flow forecasts, M&A reports, product analytics
**Proprietary data:** Early-stage process activity across a large share of the market
**Clock:** Quarterly report cycle
**Buyer:** Head of marketing insights / product
