# Sales Pipeline Analytics

**Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]

**One-liner:** Enterprise sales teams have mature funnel analytics; a sell-side process is a sales funnel run on behalf of a client, and its stage data sits in spreadsheets.
**Tags:** #logistic-regression #survival-analysis #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Not terminal as stated — every competitor is fighting to deliver the highest certain price, but a sponsor selling its fifth portfolio company and a founder selling the only company they will ever own buy that outcome on different evidence, from different banks, and the two contests are stated in the sub-niches below.

## The Problem
A sale process moves buyers through stages — contacted, NDA, CIM, indication, management meeting, LOI, final bid — exactly as a B2B sales team moves prospects. The deal team's view of that funnel is the tracker they update by hand, and the MD's question "where are we with each buyer" is answered from memory.

## What Already Exists
Salesforce and HubSpot pipeline analytics, stage conversion reporting, engagement scoring from email and content activity, and forecast tools such as Clari; DealCloud brings CRM structure to deal teams.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation requires: buyer entities that are funds, sponsors and portfolio companies at once; engagement signals from the data room and Q&A rather than from email opens; stages with deal-specific deadlines set in the process letter; strict information barriers between bidders; and an outcome that is a price, not a closed-won flag.

## Target Customer
Process-management and operations leads at boutiques already running DealCloud or Salesforce.

## Impact If Solved
Turns the live process from a spreadsheet the associate maintains into a funnel the whole team sees, and produces the cross-deal stage data that every other analysis in this niche depends on.
