# The Docket Read by Hand Every Morning

**Niche:** [[niches/investment-banking-boutiques/distressed-credit-intelligence/profile|Distressed & Restructuring Credit Intelligence]]
**Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Analysts monitor bankruptcy dockets filing by filing to catch the motion that matters among hundreds that do not.
**Tags:** #bert #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #workflow-orchestration #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious vendor in this pocket is fighting to tell readers first and correctly what a stressed company can do under its documents and what will happen next — and whoever can prove its calls were right keeps the restructuring desks' subscriptions.

## The Problem
Large Chapter 11 cases generate thousands of docket entries; a few — a DIP amendment, a plan supplement, a settlement — move recoveries.

## Why It's Still Broken
Docket alerts are keyword-based, and relevance depends on case context.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Classification and summarisation of every new filing against the case's open issues, with ranked alerts and links to the relevant passages.

## Who Feels the Pain
Docket analysts and the restructuring bankers and lawyers relying on their summaries.

## Impact If Fixed
Faster and more complete coverage of the events that change recoveries.
