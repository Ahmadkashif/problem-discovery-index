# Distressed & Restructuring Credit Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Subscription intelligence services covering stressed and distressed credits, bankruptcies and liability-management transactions — Octus (formerly Reorg), Debtwire, 9fin, CreditSights, PitchBook LCD and LevFin Insights among them. Reporters, legal analysts and financial analysts read credit agreements, dockets and filings and publish what restructuring advisers, distressed funds and law firms read every morning. Restructuring bankers are both heavy readers and frequent sources.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-400 reporters, legal analysts, covenant analysts and financial analysts per major vendor
**Output:** Breaking intelligence, covenant and capacity analyses, docket summaries, first-day and plan analyses, recovery and capital structure write-ups
**Proprietary data:** A structured archive of covenant analyses, liability-management transactions, docket events and plan outcomes across thousands of situations — built from public documents but structured by the vendor and not available elsewhere in that form
**Clock:** Court hearing dates, interest payment and grace-period deadlines, maturities and exchange-offer expiries — all set by debtors, courts and documents
**Buyer:** Head of research / editor-in-chief
