# Legal Document Analytics for Credit Agreements at Scale

**Niche:** [[niches/investment-banking-boutiques/distressed-credit-intelligence/profile|Distressed & Restructuring Credit Intelligence]]
**Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Legal AI tools review contracts one at a time for lawyers; a credit intelligence vendor needs every term across thousands of agreements, structured and comparable.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #bert #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious vendor in this pocket is fighting to tell readers first and correctly what a stressed company can do under its documents and what will happen next — and whoever can prove its calls were right keeps the restructuring desks' subscriptions.

## The Problem
Covenant analysts read each new or amended credit agreement and write up baskets, definitions and loopholes, while the universe of documents grows faster than the team.

## What Already Exists
Legal AI tools (Harvey, Kira, Luminance) and the vendors' own covenant databases built by hand.

## The Customization Gap
The vendor needs structured extraction of every basket and definition into a cross-document schema, computation of remaining capacity from financials, and detection of novel provisions that differ from market precedent — the thing readers most want flagged.

## Target Customer
Covenant and legal analysis teams at intelligence vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Coverage of far more documents per analyst, with analysts focused on novel terms rather than boilerplate.
