# The Status Call Built From Six Tools

**Niche:** [[niches/investment-banking-boutiques/the-process-associate/profile|The Process Associate]]
**Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Before every client status call the associate assembles where each buyer stands from the data room, the tracker, email and memory.
**Tags:** #data-integration #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #worker-facing #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor serving the associate is fighting to keep every number and every buyer status consistent across the model, the CIM, the data room and the tracker without a human re-checking each one — and whoever does that becomes the system of record for the live deal.

## The Problem
Status is spread across systems and updated by hand.

## Why It's Still Broken
Data rooms, CRMs and email each hold a fragment and nobody integrates them for a live process.

## What a Fix Looks Like
An automatically assembled deal state — per buyer stage, recent activity, open questions, next deadline — refreshed daily and exportable as the client update.

## Who Feels the Pain
Associates before every call; clients who receive inconsistent updates.

## Impact If Fixed
Hours a week returned per live deal, and status the whole team trusts.
