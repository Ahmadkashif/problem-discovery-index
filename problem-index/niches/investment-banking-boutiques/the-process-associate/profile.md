# The Process Associate

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor serving the associate is fighting to keep every number and every buyer status consistent across the model, the CIM, the data room and the tracker without a human re-checking each one — and whoever does that becomes the system of record for the live deal.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$400M addressable annual tooling spend for live-deal execution, as an estimate
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% of category fees
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — data room and CRM users, Excel as glue
**Target Buyer:** Head of M&A execution / COO
**Automation Potential:** High — consistency checks and status tracking are mechanical

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The associate (and VP) runs the live process: buyer tracker, NDA markups, data room permissions, Q&A routing, model updates, document tie-outs and the daily status call. They are accountable for consistency across artefacts that live in different tools, and the tools do not talk to each other. Analyst tooling helps them indirectly; nothing is built for the coordination load itself.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Datasite or Intralinks, DealCloud, Excel, Outlook. The gap is cross-artefact consistency and a live deal state assembled automatically.
