# Comps & Precedents

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce a peer set and precedent range for a private mid-market target that an informed buyer would accept as the right benchmark — and whoever does that with provenance on every multiple becomes the default valuation page.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$500M addressable annual spend, overlapping with existing data licences, as an estimate
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~2% of category fees
**Digital Adoption:** High for public data, low for private precedent knowledge
**Target Buyer:** Head of research / knowledge management, COO
**Automation Potential:** Very High — spreading and calendarisation are mechanical; peer selection is retrievable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Comparable companies and precedent transactions appear in every pitch, CIM and opinion. The public half is commoditised by data terminals; the private half — which peers suit a private target, what undisclosed mid-market deals traded at — is where a boutique's own knowledge matters and where offshore support teams spend much of their time.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Capital IQ, FactSet, PitchBook, LSEG; GF Data and DealStats for private multiples; offshore support from firms such as Acuity Knowledge Partners and Evalueserve. The gap is a firm precedent library with provenance and private-target peer selection.
