**Tags:** #word-embeddings #k-nearest-neighbors #transformers #evaluation-metrics #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce a peer set and precedent range for a private mid-market target that an informed buyer would accept as the right benchmark — and whoever does that with provenance on every multiple becomes the default valuation page.

## The Problem
Industry codes group a lab-testing business with unrelated healthcare services.

## What Already Exists
Semantic company search in sourcing platforms (Grata, SourceScrub) and terminal screening.

## The Customization Gap
Peer selection needs public-company comparables weighted by end-market, recurring revenue and margin, explained in terms a buyer will accept, and tuned on the firm's historical peer choices.

## Target Customer
Analysts and associates building comps.

## Impact If Solved
Better peer sets in minutes with a rationale per peer.
