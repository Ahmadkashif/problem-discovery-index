# Calendarisation by Hand

**Niche:** [[niches/investment-banking-boutiques/comps-and-precedents/profile|Comps & Precedents]]
**Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Analysts still adjust peers' fiscal years, one-off items and lease treatment by hand when the terminal's defaults do not match the firm's conventions.
**Tags:** #feature-engineering #descriptive-statistics #automation #quick-win #evaluation-metrics
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to produce a peer set and precedent range for a private mid-market target that an informed buyer would accept as the right benchmark — and whoever does that with provenance on every multiple becomes the default valuation page.

## The Problem
Terminal normalisations differ from the firm's house conventions, so analysts override them per spread.

## Why It's Still Broken
House conventions are tacit and undocumented.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Codify the firm's adjustment conventions as rules applied automatically to terminal data, with overrides logged.

## Who Feels the Pain
Analysts on every comps refresh.

## Impact If Fixed
Consistent spreads across teams and hours saved per pitch.
