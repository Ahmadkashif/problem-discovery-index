**Contested on:** Every serious competitor serving the analyst is fighting to turn an MD's markup into a correct, on-template page before morning — and whoever does that from the firm's own archive rather than from a generic template becomes the tool the analyst class refuses to work without.

## The Problem
Footnote fonts, chart colours, logo alignment and number formats are checked by hand on every page.

## What Already Exists
Deck plug-ins with formatting checks (Macabacus, UpSlide), brand compliance tools, and design-system linters for software.

## The Customization Gap
Bank standards cover number conventions (millions versus billions, fiscal versus calendar labels, negative formats), source footnotes and logo libraries; the adaptation codifies the firm's style guide and checks it, including in pasted images.

## Target Customer
Analyst programmes and presentation centres.

## Impact If Solved
Removes formatting from the human review loop.
