# The Page Library That Answers Questions

**Niche:** [[niches/investment-banking-boutiques/the-banking-analyst/profile|The Banking Analyst]]
**Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** The best industry overview page the firm ever built for specialty chemicals exists in someone's 2024 book, and the analyst rebuilding it tonight cannot find it.
**Tags:** #word-embeddings #large-language-models #k-nearest-neighbors #transformers #worker-facing #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor serving the analyst is fighting to turn an MD's markup into a correct, on-template page before morning — and whoever does that from the firm's own archive rather than from a generic template becomes the tool the analyst class refuses to work without.

## The Problem
Thousands of past books sit on shared drives indexed by deal code names.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Enterprise search indexes files, not pages; and confidentiality rules about client-specific content make firms nervous about broad retrieval.

## What to Build
Page-level indexing of every past book by sector, page type, companies shown and date, with permission-aware retrieval and one-click adaptation into the current template — refreshed data, new logos, stale figures flagged.

## Target Customer
Analyst programmes at boutiques with large archives.

## Impact If Built
Turns hours of rebuild into minutes of adaptation and makes the firm's best work the default starting point.
