# The Markup Round That Repeats

**Niche:** [[niches/investment-banking-boutiques/the-banking-analyst/profile|The Banking Analyst]]
**Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Each MD has the same preferences on every book, and each new analyst learns them by having their pages marked up.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #k-nearest-neighbors #worker-facing #quick-win #evaluation-metrics
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor serving the analyst is fighting to turn an MD's markup into a correct, on-template page before morning — and whoever does that from the firm's own archive rather than from a generic template becomes the tool the analyst class refuses to work without.

## The Problem
Storyline, chart choices and wording preferences repeat across a given MD's books.

## Why It's Still Broken
Markups are on paper or PDF and discarded.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Capture markups digitally, summarise each reviewer's recurring changes, and apply them to drafts before review.

## Who Feels the Pain
Analysts and associates on every book.

## Impact If Fixed
Fewer review rounds and less late-night rework.
