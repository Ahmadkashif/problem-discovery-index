# The Committee Book Read Line by Line

**Niche:** [[niches/investment-banking-boutiques/fairness-and-valuation-opinions/profile|Fairness & Valuation Opinions]]
**Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Committee members review each opinion book manually against the model, and the same mechanical questions are asked on every engagement.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #evaluation-metrics #compliance #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver an opinion whose assumptions survive a Delaware plaintiff's cross-examination — and whoever can show its analysis is consistent with its own prior opinions takes the special-committee engagement.

## The Problem
Senior committee members spend hours on mechanical checks — do the numbers tie, is the peer set justified, are sensitivities complete — before reaching the judgments only they can make.

## Why It's Still Broken
The checks are done because they must be, by the most expensive people, without tooling.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Automated tie-out of the book against the model, a standard checklist pre-filled with findings, and the prior committee questions for similar engagements surfaced in advance.

## Who Feels the Pain
Committee members; the deal team waiting on sign-off before a board meeting.

## Impact If Fixed
Committee time shifts to judgment, and turnaround before a board deadline shortens.
