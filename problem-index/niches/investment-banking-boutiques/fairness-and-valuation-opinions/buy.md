# Model Risk Management for Valuation Models

**Niche:** [[niches/investment-banking-boutiques/fairness-and-valuation-opinions/profile|Fairness & Valuation Opinions]]
**Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Banks govern credit and trading models under formal model risk guidance; the Excel models behind fairness opinions get a committee read.
**Tags:** #evaluation-metrics #compliance #confidence-intervals #feature-engineering #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to deliver an opinion whose assumptions survive a Delaware plaintiff's cross-examination — and whoever can show its analysis is consistent with its own prior opinions takes the special-committee engagement.

## The Problem
Opinion models are rebuilt per engagement in Excel, with formula errors and inconsistent conventions caught, if at all, in committee.

## What Already Exists
Model risk management practice under supervisory guidance such as the Federal Reserve's SR 11-7, spreadsheet audit tools, and model inventory software.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is lightweight: standard templates, automated formula checks, sensitivity tables generated consistently, and a record of committee challenges and responses — governance proportionate to an opinion practice rather than a bank's model risk function.

## Target Customer
Opinion committees and practice leaders.

## Impact If Solved
Fewer errors reaching proxies and a documented review trail that holds up under discovery.
