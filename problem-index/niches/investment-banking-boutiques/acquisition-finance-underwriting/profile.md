# Acquisition Finance & Direct Lending Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Direct lenders and leveraged-finance desks underwriting the debt in sponsor and strategic acquisitions run by boutiques, with credit teams researching each target.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-200 credit analysts per lender
**Output:** Credit memos and investment committee approvals — internal
**Proprietary data:** Portfolio performance across loans
**Clock:** Signing and financing commitment deadlines
**Buyer:** Head of underwriting
