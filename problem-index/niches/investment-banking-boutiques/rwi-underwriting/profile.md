# Representations & Warranties Insurance Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Underwriters at MGAs and insurers — Euclid Transactional, Ambridge, Liberty GTS, and others — who price R&W policies on mid-market deals by reviewing the diligence reports, the purchase agreement and the disclosure schedules.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 underwriters, many former M&A lawyers, per carrier or MGA
**Output:** Bound policies and exclusions — the insight prices the policy rather than being sold as such
**Proprietary data:** Thousands of underwritten deals joined to claims and their causes
**Clock:** Signing date of each deal
**Buyer:** Head of transactional risk
