# M&A Proxy & Vote Advisory

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Proxy advisers — ISS and Glass Lewis — whose vote recommendations on mergers requiring shareholder approval read the fairness opinion summaries and banker analyses in the proxy and can swing a contested vote.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 research analysts covering special situations and M&A
**Output:** M&A and contested-situation vote recommendations
**Proprietary data:** A long record of recommendations on deals and the eventual vote outcome
**Clock:** Shareholder meeting dates set by issuers
**Buyer:** Head of special situations research
