# Broker-Dealer & M&A Conduct Regulators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
FINRA and the SEC, which register and examine the broker-dealers that boutiques operate through, oversee fairness opinion disclosure under FINRA Rule 5150, and administer the 2023 federal M&A broker exemption.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Hundreds of examiners and analysts across the relevant programmes
**Output:** Examinations, guidance, enforcement
**Proprietary data:** Examination and filing records
**Clock:** Statutory and exam-cycle timetables
**Buyer:** Programme heads
