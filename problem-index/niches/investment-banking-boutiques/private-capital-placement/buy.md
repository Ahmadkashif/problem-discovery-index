# Fundraising CRM for Placement Agents

**Niche:** [[niches/investment-banking-boutiques/private-capital-placement/profile|Private Capital Placement]]
**Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Fund-raising CRMs serve funds raising from LPs; a placement agent raising for operating companies needs the same discipline on a different investor universe.
**Tags:** #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to know which growth-equity and private-credit investors are actually deploying into this profile this quarter — and whoever fills the round fastest with the fewest wasted meetings takes the next raise.

## The Problem
Outreach to hundreds of investors, tracked in spreadsheets, with feedback lost in email.

## What Already Exists
Fundraising CRMs for GPs (DealCloud, Backstop Solutions, Altvia) and investor databases.

## The Customization Gap
Operating-company raises need investor feedback captured as structured reasons for passing, term-sheet terms normalised for comparison, and confidentiality walls between competing issuers the bank is raising for.

## Target Customer
Placement groups at boutiques.

## Impact If Solved
A clean record of investor reasons and terms that informs the next raise's positioning.
