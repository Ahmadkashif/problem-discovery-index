# Investor Appetite From the Firm's Own Raises

**Niche:** [[niches/investment-banking-boutiques/private-capital-placement/profile|Private Capital Placement]]
**Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Every raise records which investors took meetings, which issued term sheets and which funded, and the next raise starts from a database list.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #survival-analysis #time-series-forecasting #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact #tacit-knowledge-ml
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to know which growth-equity and private-credit investors are actually deploying into this profile this quarter — and whoever fills the round fastest with the fewest wasted meetings takes the next raise.

## The Problem
Investor databases say what an investor's mandate is; the bank's own raises show what it actually funds, at what terms and how fast.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Same as sell-side: the record is in per-raise trackers.

## What to Build
An investor-behaviour model trained on the firm's raises plus public fund and deal data, ranking investors for a new raise by probability of term sheet and funding, with recency weighting because appetite shifts with fund cycles.

## Target Customer
Capital markets and private placement groups at boutiques.

## Impact If Built
Fewer wasted management meetings and faster rounds in a market where appetite moves quarter to quarter.
