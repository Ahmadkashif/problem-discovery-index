# Private Capital Placement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to know which growth-equity and private-credit investors are actually deploying into this profile this quarter — and whoever fills the round fastest with the fewest wasted meetings takes the next raise.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2B a year in placement fees for growth equity, minority stakes and private debt raised for mid-market companies, as an estimate
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~9% of category fees
**Digital Adoption:** Low — investor lists in spreadsheets and email
**Target Buyer:** Head of capital markets / private placements
**Automation Potential:** High — investor matching and outreach are repeatable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Boutiques raise growth equity, structured equity and private credit for companies that are not selling. The work resembles a sell-side process with investors instead of buyers, but the investor universe — growth funds, direct lenders, family offices, sovereign co-investors — changes appetite quickly with fund cycles and the credit market, and the private credit boom has multiplied the number of lenders. Placement agents must be registered broker-dealers, which keeps the work inside banks.

## Current Tools & Gaps
PitchBook and Preqin investor databases; DealCloud; Excel outreach trackers; data rooms. The gap is the firm's own record of which investors engaged and funded, by profile and period.
