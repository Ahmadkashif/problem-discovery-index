# The Lender Term Sheet Comparison in Excel

**Niche:** [[niches/investment-banking-boutiques/private-capital-placement/profile|Private Capital Placement]]
**Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A private credit raise produces a dozen term sheets with different definitions, and an associate normalises them by hand into a comparison grid.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #quick-win #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to know which growth-equity and private-credit investors are actually deploying into this profile this quarter — and whoever fills the round fastest with the fewest wasted meetings takes the next raise.

## The Problem
Pricing, OID, call protection, covenants, EBITDA definitions and baskets differ across lenders and must be compared like for like for the client's board.

## Why It's Still Broken
Term sheets are free-form documents; each lender's definitions differ subtly.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Extraction of terms into a standard schema with the source clause linked, and a computed all-in cost and flexibility comparison.

## Who Feels the Pain
Associates building the grid overnight; CFOs choosing between lenders.

## Impact If Fixed
Faster, more accurate lender selection and a reusable terms database across raises.
