# Close-Readiness Checklists

**Niche:** [[niches/investment-banking-boutiques/founder-owned-company-sales/profile|Founder-Owned Company Sales]]
**Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Audit-readiness and IPO-readiness tooling exists; a founder business needs sale-readiness assessed against what mid-market buyers actually diligence.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #evaluation-metrics #workflow-orchestration #automation #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make an undiligenced owner-operated business survive a buyer's diligence at the price it was marketed at — and whoever closes at the headline number instead of re-trading wins the referrals that source the next founder.

## The Problem
Preparing a founder business for sale means collecting contracts, normalising financials, resolving corporate housekeeping and anticipating diligence requests, under a timetable the founder underestimates.

## What Already Exists
Audit and SOX readiness tooling, IPO readiness assessments from accounting firms, and data room templates with standard request lists.

## The Customization Gap
Mid-market buyers diligence different things than auditors: customer contract assignability, owner-related expenses, working capital seasonality, key employee retention. The adaptation is a readiness assessment built from the bank's own history of diligence requests and re-trade causes, applied to the client's documents with language models, before launch.

## Target Customer
Boutiques and their client founders in the months before a process.

## Impact If Solved
Shortens the preparation phase and removes the surprises that otherwise surface after the LOI.
