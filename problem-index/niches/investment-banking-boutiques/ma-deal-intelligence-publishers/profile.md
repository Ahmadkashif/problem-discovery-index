# M&A Deal Intelligence Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Subscription services reporting on deals before announcement — Mergermarket and Dealreporter (ION), PitchBook news, The Deal — read by bankers for origination and by funds for event trading.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 journalists and analysts per major publisher
**Output:** Pre-announcement deal intelligence, process updates, league tables
**Proprietary data:** An archive of intelligence items each of which later resolved — the deal happened or did not
**Clock:** Largely self-set news cycle; league tables close on calendar quarters
**Buyer:** Editor-in-chief / head of content
