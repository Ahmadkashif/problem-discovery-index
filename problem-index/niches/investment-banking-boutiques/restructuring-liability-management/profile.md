**Parent Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Category:** High Market Share

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Restructuring is dominated by independent firms — PJT, Houlihan Lokey, Lazard, Moelis, Evercore, Perella Weinberg, Guggenheim and smaller specialists such as Ducera — advising debtors or creditor groups through Chapter 11, out-of-court exchanges and, increasingly, liability-management transactions (drop-downs, uptiers, non-pro-rata exchanges) that turn on the exact wording of credit agreements. The work runs on clocks set by others: maturities, interest payment dates, grace periods, court hearings. Fees are monthly retainers plus transaction fees, and the decisive moment is being retained early.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Octus, Debtwire, 9fin and CreditSights for intelligence and covenant analysis; Bloomberg and Capital IQ for capital structures; PACER and claims-agent sites for dockets; Excel for recovery waterfalls and liquidation analyses. The gap is a firm-owned map of which companies are approaching distress under which document terms, and a library of the firm's own past recovery analyses joined to actual outcomes.
