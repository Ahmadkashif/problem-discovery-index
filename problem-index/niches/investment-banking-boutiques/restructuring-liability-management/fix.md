# Recovery Estimates Never Compared With Recoveries

**Niche:** [[niches/investment-banking-boutiques/restructuring-liability-management/profile|Restructuring & Liability Management]]
**Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Restructuring advisers build recovery waterfalls for every class of claim, and nobody keeps a record of how those estimates compared with what creditors actually recovered.
**Tags:** #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #linear-regression #descriptive-statistics #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to be retained before the distress is public — by reading the capital structure, the credit documents and the maturity calendar earlier than rival advisers — and whoever spots the situation first takes the mandate.

## The Problem
The liquidation analysis and recovery waterfall drive negotiations, plan confirmation and creditor votes. Each is built fresh in Excel.

## Why It's Still Broken
Realised recoveries emerge months or years later, after the team has moved on; nothing links the file to the outcome.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A library of the firm's past waterfalls and valuations, joined to plan outcomes and post-emergence trading values, with calibration reported by industry and claim class — used as a reference when building the next one.

## Who Feels the Pain
Restructuring associates rebuilding from scratch; MDs negotiating on estimates with no track record attached.

## Impact If Fixed
Gives the firm an evidence base for the numbers it argues from, in a practice where credibility with the court and creditors is the asset.
