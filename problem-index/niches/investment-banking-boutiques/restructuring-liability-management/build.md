# The Distress Map Before It Is News

**Niche:** [[niches/investment-banking-boutiques/restructuring-liability-management/profile|Restructuring & Liability Management]]
**Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Restructuring mandates go to the adviser who calls first, and the signals that a company is heading into distress are in public filings, loan prices and credit documents months beforehand.
**Tags:** #survival-analysis #gradient-boosting #large-language-models #time-series-forecasting #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to be retained before the distress is public — by reading the capital structure, the credit documents and the maturity calendar earlier than rival advisers — and whoever spots the situation first takes the mandate.

## The Problem
By the time a company hires restructuring counsel or misses an interest payment, the adviser pitch is a beauty contest among firms with the same information. Earlier, the signals are diffuse: declining liquidity, a maturity wall, loan prices sliding, covenant headroom shrinking, auditor going-concern language.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Coverage is organised by banker relationships rather than by systematic monitoring, and the relevant data is split across loan pricing, filings and credit documents that require specialist reading.

## What to Build
A distress-likelihood model across the leveraged universe combining liquidity runway, maturity schedules, loan and bond prices, filing language and covenant terms, paired with a document layer that flags liability-management flexibility (baskets, unrestricted subsidiary capacity) — producing a ranked watchlist with evidence for each coverage banker.

## Target Customer
Restructuring groups at independent advisory firms, on both debtor and creditor sides.

## Impact If Built
Earlier, evidence-based outreach converts more mandates in a business where timing is the whole contest.
