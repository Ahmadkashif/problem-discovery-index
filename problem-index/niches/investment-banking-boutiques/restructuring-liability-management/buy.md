# Contract Analytics for Credit Agreements

**Niche:** [[niches/investment-banking-boutiques/restructuring-liability-management/profile|Restructuring & Liability Management]]
**Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Contract lifecycle tools extract clauses from commercial contracts; liability-management work needs the interaction of baskets and definitions across a three-hundred-page credit agreement.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #transformers #evaluation-metrics #automation #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to be retained before the distress is public — by reading the capital structure, the credit documents and the maturity calendar earlier than rival advisers — and whoever spots the situation first takes the mandate.

## The Problem
Whether a company can move collateral to an unrestricted subsidiary or prime existing lenders turns on defined terms, baskets, and carve-outs that reference each other across hundreds of pages.

## What Already Exists
Contract analytics tools (Kira, Luminance, Harvey for legal teams) and covenant review services from CreditSights and Octus.

## The Customization Gap
Generic clause extraction misses the computation: how much capacity remains after builder baskets and grower components, given the latest financials. The adaptation models definitions as a dependency graph and computes available capacity, and is reviewed by the restructuring team rather than counsel alone.

## Target Customer
Restructuring advisers and creditor-side analysts.

## Impact If Solved
Turns days of document reading per situation into hours, and makes the firm's capacity analysis reusable across the many companies with near-identical documents.
