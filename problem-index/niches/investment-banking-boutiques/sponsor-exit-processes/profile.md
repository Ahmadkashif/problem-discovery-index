# Sponsor Exit Processes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell a sponsor, before the process launches, which buyers will actually bid and at what price — and whoever proves that from their own process record wins the next exit from the same fund.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$5.5B a year in sell-side fees on sponsor-backed exits, as an estimate
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~25% of category fees
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — sponsors expect polished data and fast processes
**Target Buyer:** Sponsor coverage MD and head of M&A
**Automation Potential:** High — repeated buyer universes across exits

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The client is a repeat seller who knows the buyer universe almost as well as the bank does, holds its own view of value, and runs a bake-off for every exit. A sponsor's deal partner judges a bank on whether its buyer list contains anyone the sponsor did not already know, and whether its pre-launch price guidance proves right. The bank that sold a comparable asset last year and can show exactly who bid wins the meeting. Secondary buyouts make the buyer set heavily sponsor-to-sponsor, so the same few hundred firms appear in process after process — which makes their behaviour learnable.

## Current Tools & Gaps
PitchBook and Preqin for sponsor dry powder and portfolio; DealCloud for coverage; Excel trackers per process. The gap is the cross-process record of how each sponsor behaves as a buyer, and price guidance graded against outcomes.
