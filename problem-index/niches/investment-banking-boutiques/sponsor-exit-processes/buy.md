# Account-Based Intent Data

**Niche:** [[niches/investment-banking-boutiques/sponsor-exit-processes/profile|Sponsor Exit Processes]]
**Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** B2B marketers buy intent signals on which accounts are in-market; a sell-side banker needs the same signal for which sponsors are hunting in a sector this quarter.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #time-series-forecasting #large-language-models #feature-engineering #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to tell a sponsor, before the process launches, which buyers will actually bid and at what price — and whoever proves that from their own process record wins the next exit from the same fund.

## The Problem
Whether a sponsor is actively looking for a platform or add-on in a sector changes quarter to quarter, and the bank learns it from conversations and the sponsor's recent deals.

## What Already Exists
Account-based marketing intent platforms (6sense, Bombora, Demandbase) score which companies are researching a topic; PitchBook and Preqin record sponsor deals and fundraises.

## The Customization Gap
Sponsor intent is revealed in different places: new fund closes, recent add-ons by existing platforms, hiring of sector operating partners, NDAs signed in the bank's own and others' processes, and data room engagement. The adaptation fuses public deal data with the bank's private engagement record into a sector-level, quarterly intent score per sponsor.

## Target Customer
Sponsor-coverage MDs and the associates who draft buyer lists.

## Impact If Solved
Times outreach to when sponsors are actually deploying, and surfaces sponsors whose appetite has changed since the last time the bank saw them.
