# Offshore Investment Banking Research Support

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Knowledge process outsourcing providers — Acuity Knowledge Partners, Evalueserve, CRISIL and others — and banks' own captive centres that produce comps, company profiles, pitch pages, buyer lists and model updates for deal teams, usually from India.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Several hundred to several thousand analysts per provider
**Output:** Comps spreads, profiles, pitch book pages, buyer universes, model maintenance — billed per dedicated FTE or per deliverable
**Proprietary data:** Little of its own — the work product belongs to the client bank
**Clock:** The bank's pitch and process deadlines, often overnight
**Buyer:** Head of banking support / delivery head

**Kill switches:** **data isn't theirs to use** — every deliverable is client work product under confidentiality, so the provider cannot build a reusable corpus across banks.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. The labour mass of investment banking research sits here, which makes it a strong buyer of production tooling but not an insight business in its own right.
