# The Process Ledger

**Niche:** [[niches/investment-banking-boutiques/buyer-process-intelligence/profile|Buyer & Process Intelligence]]
**Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A decade of buyer behaviour across every process the firm ran is recoverable from its archives, and it is the one dataset a competitor cannot buy.
**Tags:** #tacit-knowledge-ml #gradient-boosting #graph-theory #k-nearest-neighbors #data-integration #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn the firm's own record of how buyers behaved in past processes into a ranked buyer list a client cannot get elsewhere — and whoever assembles that record first owns the one dataset no vendor can sell.

## The Problem
The firm's buyer knowledge exists in archived trackers and MDs' memories.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Entity resolution across messy trackers is tedious, and senior bankers treat buyer knowledge as their personal franchise.

## What to Build
Ingest and resolve every historical tracker into a buyer–process ledger with a sponsor–fund–portfolio graph; layer a propensity ranking on top; capture MD overrides as labels.

## Target Customer
Boutiques running thirty or more processes a year.

## Impact If Built
Institutional memory that survives banker departures and powers every buyer list.
