# Buyer & Process Intelligence

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn the firm's own record of how buyers behaved in past processes into a ranked buyer list a client cannot get elsewhere — and whoever assembles that record first owns the one dataset no vendor can sell.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M addressable annual spend today, with value expressed through mandate wins rather than licence fees, as an estimate
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1% of category fees, underpinning most of the rest
**Digital Adoption:** Very Low — per-deal spreadsheets
**Target Buyer:** Head of M&A / COO
**Automation Potential:** Very High — ingestion, entity resolution and ranking are all automatable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This niche is the data asset rather than the service: the cross-process ledger of buyer and investor behaviour, data room engagement and outcomes. Sell-side and placement niches consume it; this niche is about building it. It is distinct because the asset is the bank's own work product — unlike the CIM or data room contents, which belong to clients.

## Current Tools & Gaps
DealCloud and Salesforce hold relationships; data rooms hold engagement logs per process; trackers hold outcomes. Nothing joins them.
