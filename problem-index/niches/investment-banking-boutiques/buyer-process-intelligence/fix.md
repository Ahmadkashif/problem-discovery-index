# Data Room Engagement Thrown Away at Close

**Niche:** [[niches/investment-banking-boutiques/buyer-process-intelligence/profile|Buyer & Process Intelligence]]
**Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The data room records exactly how deeply each bidder diligenced, and the logs are archived with the room at close.
**Tags:** #data-integration #feature-engineering #survival-analysis #quick-win #evaluation-metrics
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn the firm's own record of how buyers behaved in past processes into a ranked buyer list a client cannot get elsewhere — and whoever assembles that record first owns the one dataset no vendor can sell.

## The Problem
Engagement logs are a strong signal of bidder seriousness and are discarded.

## Why It's Still Broken
Data room archives are handed to the client; the bank keeps no structured summary.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Export per-bidder engagement summaries at close — with client consent where the engagement letter requires it — and attach them to the process ledger.

## Who Feels the Pain
Deal teams who cannot learn which engagement patterns preceded bids.

## Impact If Fixed
A cheap new feature set for buyer propensity, captured before it is lost.
