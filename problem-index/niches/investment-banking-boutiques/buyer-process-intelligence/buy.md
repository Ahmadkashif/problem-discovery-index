# Entity Resolution From Customer Data Platforms

**Niche:** [[niches/investment-banking-boutiques/buyer-process-intelligence/profile|Buyer & Process Intelligence]]
**Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Customer data platforms resolve identities across marketing systems; a bank needs the same for sponsors, funds, platforms and partners across a decade of spreadsheets.
**Tags:** #k-nearest-neighbors #word-embeddings #graph-theory #data-integration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn the firm's own record of how buyers behaved in past processes into a ranked buyer list a client cannot get elsewhere — and whoever assembles that record first owns the one dataset no vendor can sell.

## The Problem
The same buyer appears under a dozen names.

## What Already Exists
Identity resolution in customer data platforms and master data management tools.

## The Customization Gap
Buyer entities are hierarchical and change over time — funds raised, platforms sold, partners moving firms — so resolution must be temporal and relationship-aware.

## Target Customer
Boutique data and operations teams.

## Impact If Solved
A clean buyer master that every downstream analysis depends on.
