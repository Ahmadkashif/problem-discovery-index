# Financial Reporting Valuation (Purchase Price Allocation & Impairment)

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/investment-banking-boutiques|Investment Banking Boutiques]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Valuation firms — Kroll, Houlihan Lokey's financial and valuation advisory practice, Stout, Marshall & Stevens, and the valuation arms of accounting firms — that value the intangible assets an acquirer must recognise after every deal under ASC 805, and test goodwill and intangibles for impairment under ASC 350. Every boutique-advised acquisition by a reporting company generates one of these engagements, and the auditor reviews every assumption.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-1,500 valuation professionals per firm
**Output:** Purchase price allocation reports, impairment tests, fair value measurements for financial reporting
**Proprietary data:** Thousands of engagements' worth of selected assumptions — customer attrition rates, royalty rates, contributory asset charges, discount rates — and the record of which ones auditors challenged
**Clock:** Quarterly and annual reporting deadlines and the acquirer's audit timetable
**Buyer:** Valuation practice leader
