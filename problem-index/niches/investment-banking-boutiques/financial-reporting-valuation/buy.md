**Contested on:** Every serious valuation firm in this pocket is fighting to deliver assumptions the acquirer's auditor accepts on the first review before the reporting deadline — and whoever can evidence each assumption from its own engagement record takes the recurring work.

## The Problem
Selecting comparable licence agreements and cost-of-capital inputs is a manual search through licensed databases, documented for review.

## What Already Exists
Royalty and licence databases (RoyaltySource, ktMINE), Kroll's Cost of Capital Navigator, and terminal data.

## The Customization Gap
Similarity search over licence agreements by technology and terms, plus auto-documentation of selection rationale in the format auditors' specialists expect.

## Target Customer
Valuation staff preparing PPAs and impairment tests.

## Impact If Solved
Faster, better-documented benchmark selection.
