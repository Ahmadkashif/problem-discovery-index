# Regulatory Remediation & Look-Back Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/compliance-consulting|Compliance Consulting]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Promontory, Treliant, StoneTurn, and the large remediation practices execute the file reviews and control assessments that consent orders and supervisory agreements require — often hundreds of thousands of accounts re-reviewed against a defined standard, with the findings reported to the regulator. The written review is the entire engagement and it is delivered under a regulator's supervision.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-2,000 reviewers, quality control staff, and subject matter experts on a large remediation
**Output:** File review findings, remediation reports, control assessments filed with the regulator
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated review methodology and the record of which approaches regulators accepted — the underlying files belong to the client
**Clock:** Consent order deadlines, which are among the hardest in commercial life
**Buyer:** Managing Director / Practice Leader
