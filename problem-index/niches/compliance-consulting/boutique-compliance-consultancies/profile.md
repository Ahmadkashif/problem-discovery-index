# Boutique Compliance Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/compliance-consulting|Compliance Consulting]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
The operator layer of this industry: firms of five to thirty consultants producing gap analyses, remediation roadmaps, and audit evidence packages for SMB and mid-market clients. The written deliverable is unambiguously the invoice and the work is exactly the shape this sweep looks for — it is simply too small to clear the gate.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 5-30 consultants, of whom a handful do the research
**Output:** Gap analyses, remediation roadmaps, audit evidence packages
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated engagement work product across clients in the same vertical, largely client-owned
**Clock:** Client audit and certification deadlines
**Buyer:** Managing partner
