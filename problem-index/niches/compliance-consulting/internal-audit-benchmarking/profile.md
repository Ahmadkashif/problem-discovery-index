# Internal Audit Benchmarking Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/compliance-consulting|Compliance Consulting]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Benchmarking programmes run by professional bodies and large consultancies collect internal audit function data — staffing, budget, coverage, methodology, and findings profile — and publish the comparisons chief audit executives use to justify budgets and defend their approach to audit committees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 researchers and analysts
**Output:** Benchmark reports, maturity assessments, practice surveys
**Proprietary data:** Contributed function-level data across hundreds of internal audit departments
**Clock:** Annual survey and publication cycles
**Buyer:** Director of Research
