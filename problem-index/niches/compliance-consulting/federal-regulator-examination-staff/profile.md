# Regulatory Examination Staff

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/compliance-consulting|Compliance Consulting]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Federal and state examination staff conduct the supervisory examinations that compliance consulting exists to prepare for, and their examination manuals and findings define what compliance means in practice.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-2,000 examiners and policy staff per agency
**Output:** Examination findings, supervisory guidance, examination manuals
**Proprietary data:** Examination findings across every supervised institution — the most complete picture of actual compliance practice anywhere, and confidential supervisory information by law
**Clock:** Examination cycles and statutory reporting
**Buyer:** No commercial buyer
