# GRC Platform Content Teams

## What They Do
The content and compliance research groups at Vanta, Drata, AuditBoard, LogicGate, and ServiceNow GRC build the control libraries, framework mappings, evidence collection logic, and policy templates their platforms ship with. Content quality is what actually differentiates these products, and it is bundled into the subscription.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 compliance researchers, content engineers, and framework specialists
**Output:** Control libraries, framework mappings, automated evidence definitions, policy templates
**Proprietary data:** Control implementation and audit outcome data across thousands of customer organizations — a view of what actually passes an audit that no consultancy or auditor can assemble
**Clock:** Framework revisions and customer audit cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Compliance Content / Chief Product Officer
