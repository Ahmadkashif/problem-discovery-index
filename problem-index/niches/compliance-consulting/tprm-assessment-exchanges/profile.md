# Third-Party Risk Assessment Exchanges

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/compliance-consulting|Compliance Consulting]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Shared Assessments, CyberGRX, Whistic, and ProcessUnity collect standardized control assessments from vendors once and distribute them to the many customers who would otherwise each send their own questionnaire. Analysts validate responses, maintain the question sets, and map answers across frameworks. The assessment is the product and it is sold many times over.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 assessment analysts, validators, and framework specialists
**Output:** Validated vendor assessments, framework mappings, risk profiles
**Proprietary data:** Completed and validated control assessments across thousands of vendors — the only cross-organization view of how third parties actually control themselves
**Clock:** Vendor onboarding deadlines and annual reassessment cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Assessment Operations / Chief Product Officer
