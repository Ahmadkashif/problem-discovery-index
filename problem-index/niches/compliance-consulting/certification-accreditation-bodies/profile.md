# Accreditation Bodies for Certification Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/compliance-consulting|Compliance Consulting]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
ANAB and the international accreditation forum members assess the certification bodies that certify everyone else — auditing the auditors against competence and impartiality requirements. Their accreditation is what makes a certificate mean anything internationally.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 assessors, scheme managers, and technical specialists
**Output:** Accreditation assessments, scheme requirements, non-conformance findings
**Proprietary data:** Assessment findings across certification bodies and the record of systemic weaknesses in the certification system itself
**Clock:** Accreditation cycles and international recognition arrangements
**Buyer:** VP of Accreditation / Technical Director
