# Cyber Insurance Controls Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/compliance-consulting|Compliance Consulting]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Cyber underwriters and their risk engineering teams assess an applicant's control posture, price the risk, and increasingly mandate specific controls as a condition of coverage — which has made insurers one of the strongest de facto compliance regulators in the market.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 underwriters, risk engineers, and actuaries
**Output:** Underwriting assessments, control requirements, loss and claims analyses
**Proprietary data:** Control posture at underwriting joined to subsequent claim experience — the only dataset showing which controls actually prevent losses
**Clock:** Renewal cycles and rate filings
**Buyer:** Chief Underwriting Officer / Head of Cyber Risk Engineering
