# Regulatory Intelligence Publishers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/compliance-consulting|Compliance Consulting]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Wolters Kluwer, Thomson Reuters Regulatory Intelligence, LexisNexis, and the specialist regulatory change vendors monitor rulemaking across hundreds of agencies and jurisdictions, interpret what each change requires operationally, and map it to the control frameworks their subscribers run on. Compliance consultants and in-house teams subscribe because tracking it themselves is the overhead their own clients refuse to pay for.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 regulatory analysts, attorneys, editors, and taxonomists
**Output:** Interpreted regulatory content, change alerts, obligation-to-control mappings — the subscription itself
**Proprietary data:** Decades of regulatory interpretation and obligation mapping, plus the subscriber inquiry record showing exactly where regulations are ambiguous in practice
**Clock:** Rulemaking effective dates, examination cycles, and enforcement actions — none of them movable
**Buyer:** VP of Content / Head of Regulatory Intelligence
