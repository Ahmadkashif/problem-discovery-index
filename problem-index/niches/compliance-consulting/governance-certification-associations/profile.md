# Governance & Audit Professional Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/compliance-consulting|Compliance Consulting]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
ISACA, the Institute of Internal Auditors, and OCEG define professional standards, run the certifications that compliance and audit staff are hired against, and publish the research and practice guidance the profession works from.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 standards developers, psychometricians, researchers, and editors
**Output:** Professional standards, certification examinations, practice guidance, member research
**Proprietary data:** Decades of item response and certification data, plus long-running practitioner survey series
**Clock:** Examination cycles, standards revisions, and annual research calendars
**Buyer:** Chief Knowledge Officer / VP of Certification
