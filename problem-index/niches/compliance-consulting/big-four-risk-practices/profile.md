# Large Firm Risk & Regulatory Practices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/compliance-consulting|Compliance Consulting]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The risk and regulatory practices at the Big Four and the large consultancies deliver gap assessments, control design, and regulatory readiness work at enterprise scale. They also publish the survey-based thought leadership that sets the industry's agenda, funded as business development.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 1,000-10,000 in risk and regulatory advisory at the largest firms
**Output:** Gap assessments, control frameworks, regulatory readiness reports, published research
**Proprietary data:** Engagement experience across thousands of clients — held under confidentiality and independence constraints that prevent it from being pooled
**Clock:** Client regulatory deadlines and examination cycles
**Buyer:** Practice Leader / Chief Knowledge Officer
