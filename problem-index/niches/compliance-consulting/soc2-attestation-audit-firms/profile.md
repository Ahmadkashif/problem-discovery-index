# Attestation & Certification Audit Practices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/compliance-consulting|Compliance Consulting]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
The service organization control and ISO certification practices at CPA firms and accredited certification bodies test a client's controls and issue the attestation report that the client's own customers demand before buying. The report is the invoice, it is relied upon by third parties, and it is what stands between a software company and an enterprise procurement process.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-1,000 auditors, testers, and quality reviewers at the larger practices
**Output:** Attestation reports, control test results, exception findings
**Proprietary data:** Control implementation and exception patterns across thousands of clients — commercially valuable and contractually sealed
**Clock:** Report period end dates and client sales cycles that stall without the report
**Buyer:** Partner, attestation practice / National Practice Leader
