# Food Safety Auditing & Certification Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/restaurant-suppliers|Restaurant Suppliers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Audit food manufacturers, processors and distributors against recognised food safety schemes and issue the certifications buyers require before they will purchase — the private regulatory layer that determines who may sell into the supply chain.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 200-1,000 auditors, technical reviewers, scheme managers, and standards staff
**Output:** Audit findings and non-conformance reports, certification decisions, scheme standards, auditor calibration
**Proprietary data:** Audit findings across tens of thousands of facilities — a record of what actually goes wrong in food handling, at a granularity no regulator possesses
**Clock:** Annual certification cycles and unannounced audit windows
**Buyer:** VP of Certification / Chief Technical Officer
