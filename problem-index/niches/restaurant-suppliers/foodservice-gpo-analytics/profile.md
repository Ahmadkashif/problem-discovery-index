# Foodservice Group Purchasing Organizations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/restaurant-suppliers|Restaurant Suppliers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Negotiate manufacturer pricing on behalf of pooled operators and administer the rebate and contract compliance that follows, sitting between manufacturers, distributors and the operators who buy through them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 contract analysts, category managers, and rebate administration staff
**Output:** Negotiated contract terms, purchasing compliance reporting, rebate calculation and distribution, savings analysis
**Proprietary data:** Purchase volumes and contract pricing across pooled members — the only comparative view of what foodservice products actually cost different buyers
**Clock:** Contract cycles and quarterly rebate settlement
**Buyer:** VP of Contracting / Chief Analytics Officer
