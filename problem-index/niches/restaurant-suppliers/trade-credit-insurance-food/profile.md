# Foodservice Trade Credit Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/restaurant-suppliers|Restaurant Suppliers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Insure and factor the receivables distributors carry on independent restaurants — a customer population with high failure rates, thin financials and no credit file worth the name.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 credit analysts, underwriters, and collections staff
**Output:** Credit limits and coverage decisions, restaurant failure risk scoring, receivable monitoring, collections
**Proprietary data:** Payment behaviour and closure outcomes across large independent restaurant populations
**Clock:** Invoice terms and continuous limit monitoring
**Buyer:** Chief Credit Officer
