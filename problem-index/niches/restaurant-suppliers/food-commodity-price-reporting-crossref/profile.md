# Food Commodity Price Reporting Agencies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/restaurant-suppliers|Restaurant Suppliers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Publish the assessed prices for proteins, produce, dairy and other food commodities that distributor and operator supply contracts are formula-priced against. Pass 1 notes procurement buyers must commit to perishable inventory three to seven days ahead of demand; these are the prices they commit at.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-250 price reporters, editors, and market analysts
**Output:** Daily and weekly price assessments, market commentary, supply and demand analysis, indices referenced in contracts
**Proprietary data:** Confidential transaction reporting and trade relationships accumulated over decades, plus the reporter judgment that resolves inconsistent submissions into a published number
**Clock:** Fixed daily publication windows against which real contracts settle
**Buyer:** Editorial Director / Head of Price Reporting
