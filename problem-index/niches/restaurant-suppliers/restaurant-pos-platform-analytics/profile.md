# Restaurant Technology Platform Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/restaurant-suppliers|Restaurant Suppliers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The data organisations inside the restaurant point-of-sale, ordering and inventory platforms, which see item-level sales, menu mix and purchasing at a large share of independent restaurants — the demand signal the supplier layer is guessing at.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 300-1,500 data scientists, analysts, and product researchers
**Output:** Sales and menu mix analytics, demand forecasting, inventory and ordering recommendations, published industry trend reports, lending underwriting
**Proprietary data:** Item-level transactions across tens of thousands of independent restaurants, joined to purchasing where inventory modules are used
**Clock:** Daily service and weekly ordering cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Data Science / Chief Product Officer
