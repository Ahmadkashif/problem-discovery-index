**Parent Industry:** [[industries/restaurant-suppliers|Restaurant Suppliers]]

## What They Do
Supply the ERP, warehouse and route planning systems independent distributors run on, with the forecasting and optimisation modules attached.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 product, implementation, and analytics staff
**Output:** Demand forecasting and replenishment, route and delivery optimisation, warehouse picking optimisation, margin reporting
**Proprietary data:** Order, pick and delivery records across many distributors, held per installation
**Clock:** Daily order and delivery cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Product
