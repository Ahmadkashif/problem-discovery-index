# Menu, Recipe & Nutrition Data Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/restaurant-suppliers|Restaurant Suppliers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Maintain the ingredient, nutrition and allergen databases restaurant menus are analysed against, and produce the nutritional and allergen disclosures menu labelling rules require.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 60-300 dietitians, food scientists, and data specialists
**Output:** Recipe nutrition analysis, allergen declarations, menu labelling compliance, ingredient databases, reformulation support
**Proprietary data:** Curated ingredient and formulation databases mapped to supplier products, maintained over years
**Clock:** Menu change cycles and labelling compliance requirements
**Buyer:** Director of Nutrition Services
