# Foodservice Market Intelligence Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/restaurant-suppliers|Restaurant Suppliers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Track what independent restaurants buy, what appears on menus, and how operator demand is shifting, and sell it to the manufacturers and distributors selling into that channel — the market map a supplier's category and sales strategy is built on.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-400 researchers, menu analysts, data scientists, and consultants
**Output:** Operator counts and segmentation, menu trend tracking, channel sizing and forecasts, custom research
**Proprietary data:** Operator databases and menu corpora built by direct research over decades, in a channel with no transaction census
**Clock:** Quarterly and annual publication against supplier planning cycles
**Buyer:** Chief Research Officer / VP of Insights
