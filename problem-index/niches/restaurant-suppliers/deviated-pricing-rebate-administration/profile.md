# Deviated Pricing & Rebate Administration

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/restaurant-suppliers|Restaurant Suppliers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Administer the bill-back economics the foodservice channel runs on — manufacturers grant deviated pricing to specific operators, distributors sell at the deviated price and claim the difference back, and somebody has to reconcile millions of claims against contract terms.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 claims analysts, contract administrators, and audit staff
**Output:** Claim adjudication and reconciliation, contract term administration, deduction dispute resolution, leakage and audit findings
**Proprietary data:** Claim and dispute histories across manufacturers and distributors, showing where deviated pricing systematically leaks
**Clock:** Monthly claim submission and settlement cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Trade Administration
