# Food Traceability Compliance Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/restaurant-suppliers|Restaurant Suppliers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer

## What They Do
Determine which products and handling steps fall under traceability requirements, design the recording that satisfies them, and prepare the ability to produce a coherent record within the window an outbreak investigation allows.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 food safety specialists, regulatory analysts, and implementation consultants
**Output:** Scope determinations, traceability programme design, record-keeping systems, mock recall exercises, regulatory submissions
**Proprietary data:** Accumulated scope determinations and which positions regulators accepted
**Clock:** Statutory compliance dates and outbreak response windows
**Buyer:** VP of Regulatory Services
