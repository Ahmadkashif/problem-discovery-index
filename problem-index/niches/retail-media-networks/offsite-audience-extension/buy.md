**Industry:** [[industries/retail-media-networks|Retail Media Networks]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Consumer goods companies have modelled purchase panels for decades with real method, and retail media exports a recency segment from a dataset a hundred times larger.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #survival-analysis #matrix-decompositions #k-means-clustering #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #time-series-forecasting #descriptive-statistics
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn purchase data into an offsite audience that actually performs and can be measured before the campaign ends — and whoever does that turns a commodity segment business into a measurable one.

## The Problem
Analysis of household purchase panels is a mature commercial discipline. Consumer goods companies have spent decades on buyer segmentation, repeat-rate and penetration decomposition, switching and loyalty analysis, and purchase cycle modelling — built on panels covering a small sample of households with substantial recall error. Retailers hold a census of actual transactions, orders of magnitude larger and perfectly accurate, and apply a recency filter to it.

## What Already Exists
Household panel segmentation methods; penetration and repeat-rate decomposition; brand switching and loyalty analysis; purchase cycle and interpurchase time modelling; and category trip-mission frameworks.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is from a small panel analysed retrospectively to a census activated in real time. It requires: (1) the output being an addressable audience rather than an insight, since panel practice ends in a strategy document and this must end in a targetable set — a different deliverable that changes what the model must optimise; (2) census scale, which removes the projection and recall problems the entire panel methodology was designed around and makes several of its techniques unnecessary and others far more powerful; (3) activation through third-party platforms with match loss, so the addressable audience is a degraded subset and the model must account for who survives the join; (4) privacy constraints on what may leave the retailer's boundary, which panel analysis never faced; and (5) a weekly rather than annual cadence, since the audience is built for a campaign rather than for a planning cycle.

## Target Customer
Retail media networks, consumer goods brand teams, and panel and audience vendors for whom transaction census data is a better and unused input.

## Impact If Solved
Decades of panel method were built around projection and recall error that a transaction census does not have, which makes several techniques unnecessary and others far stronger. Ending in an addressable audience rather than a strategy document changes what the model optimises.
