# Offsite Audience Extension

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/retail-media-networks|Retail Media Networks]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn purchase data into an offsite audience that actually performs and can be measured before the campaign ends — and whoever does that turns a commodity segment business into a measurable one.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$8B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% of category spend
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — sold widely, modelled crudely
**Target Buyer:** Retail media and brand media leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — propensity and measurement are both modellable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every network now sells offsite audiences built from purchase data, using generic propensity tooling and clean rooms that answer questions weeks after the campaign they were about has ended. Offsite is where retail media's growth is going, and it is the part where the retailer's structural advantage — the closed loop — is weakest, because the impression happens somewhere else. The contest is over whether purchase data produces genuinely better audiences than the alternatives, and whether the resulting campaign can be measured while there is still time to act on it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Segment exports to demand-side platforms, generic look-alike modelling, clean room match and measure workflows, and post-campaign lift reports. The gaps: segments built on crude recency and frequency rules; no use of basket structure or sequence; match rates that erode the audience silently; measurement arriving weeks late; and no feedback from offsite outcomes into audience construction.
