# Advertiser Self-Service

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/retail-media-networks|Retail Media Networks]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let a brand's media team plan, buy, optimise and report across a retail network without a spreadsheet and a phone call — and whoever does that gets the spend that currently goes wherever the paperwork is easiest.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$5B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of category spend
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — consoles exist, workflows do not
**Target Buyer:** Retail media product leadership and brand media teams
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the work is entirely mechanical

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A brand runs campaigns across eleven retail networks, each with its own console, its own taxonomy, its own reporting schema, its own billing cycle and its own account manager. The operational burden of that is now the binding constraint on where retail media budgets go — brands consolidate spend into the networks that are easiest to operate rather than the ones that perform best. This is a workflow contest, won by whoever removes the most manual steps for the brand, and it has a different buyer and a different winner from the decisioning fight next door.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Per-network self-service consoles, bulk upload templates, spreadsheet-based bid management, third-party aggregation tools of varying quality, and account managers as the integration layer. The gaps: no common taxonomy or schema across networks; reporting delivered as exports; no cross-network view or optimisation; approvals and budgets handled off-platform; and interfaces designed for specialists.
