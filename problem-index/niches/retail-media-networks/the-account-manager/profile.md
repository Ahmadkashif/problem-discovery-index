# The Account Manager

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/retail-media-networks|Retail Media Networks]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to take report-building and bid-tweaking off the account manager so they can give the advice the brand is paying for — and whoever does that changes how many brands one person can genuinely serve.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$4B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of category spend
**Digital Adoption:** Low — spreadsheets and slide decks
**Target Buyer:** Retail media sales leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the work is assembly

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Retail media account managers spend their weeks pulling keyword reports, adjusting bids in a spreadsheet and rebuilding the same quarterly business review for forty brands, none of which is the advice the brand is paying for. The account manager is the network's entire relationship surface for most advertisers and the main reason a brand renews or does not, and their week is consumed by assembly work that the network's own systems could produce. This is distinct from the advertiser self-service contest: that one removes the manager from the brand's workflow, this one changes what the manager does with their time.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Platform reporting exports, spreadsheet bid management, slide templates for business reviews, and customer relationship records. The gaps: reports rebuilt by hand every cycle; no automated insight generation; bid work that is mechanical and endless; no view of which accounts need attention; and no record of which advice actually worked.
