# The Category Merchant

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/retail-media-networks|Retail Media Networks]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to give the category merchant a number for what the sponsored placements in their category cost them — and whoever produces it gives the retailer's other half a seat at a table it has been excluded from.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3.5B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~6% of category spend
**Digital Adoption:** Low — no instrumentation at all
**Target Buyer:** Merchandising leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — the accounting is computable

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The category merchant is still accountable for category profit after the top of their search results was sold to the highest bidder, and nobody has given them a number for what it cost. Retail media revenue lands in one part of the business and its costs land in another, and only one of the two has been instrumented. The merchant is the person who knows which products should be promoted, which suppliers are performing, and what the category needs — and is the only significant stakeholder in the retailer with no representation in the auction that reorders their shelf.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Category performance reports, planogram and assortment tools, supplier scorecards, and media revenue reported separately at the retailer level. The gaps: no attribution of media revenue or cost to a category; no measure of displaced organic sales; no visibility of which suppliers are buying position; no merchant input into ranking; and no shared objective between merchandising and media.
