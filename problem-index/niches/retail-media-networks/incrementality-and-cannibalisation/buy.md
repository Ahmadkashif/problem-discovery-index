## The Problem
Establishing whether an intervention caused an outcome is the most thoroughly settled question in applied statistics. Randomised experiments, difference-in-differences, synthetic controls, regression discontinuity and instrumental variables are all standard, taught everywhere, and implemented in open libraries. Clinical medicine, public policy and product development all use them routinely under conditions far worse than these. Retail media, which has randomisation control, perfect outcome observation and a single identity per shopper, reports an attributed correlation.

## What Already Exists
Randomised controlled trial design and analysis; difference-in-differences and synthetic control methods; regression discontinuity; instrumental variable estimation; and open implementations of all of the above.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a commercial measurement whose result affects the measurer's earnings. It requires: (1) governance rather than methodology as the hard part, since the statistics are settled and the problem is who runs the experiment and who can check it — this inversion is what makes the niche unusual and defines the product; (2) displacement as the estimand, because the counterfactual is not no-advertisement-no-sale but the organic listing that would have shown instead, which is a substitution effect the standard advertising lift design does not measure; (3) interference between brands in the same category, since a holdout for one brand is contaminated by competitors' placements on the same page and the naive design is biased; (4) margin rather than revenue as the outcome, which requires cost data the media system does not hold and merchandising does; and (5) continuous operation at low cost, since a quarterly study cannot support a weekly buying decision.

## Target Customer
Brand measurement and finance teams, independent measurement vendors, retail media networks differentiating on credibility, and industry bodies setting standards.

## Impact If Solved
The statistics are settled and the hard part is governance, which inverts the usual product. Displacement rather than absence is the right counterfactual, and no standard advertising lift design measures it.
