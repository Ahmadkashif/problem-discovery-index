**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a broadcast, a dozen streams, several short-form platforms and a set of social accounts into one comparable unit of attention — and whoever defines that unit takes the account.

## The Problem
Social media analytics is a mature commercial category. Platforms pull from every major network's interface, normalise incompatible engagement metrics into comparable measures, deduplicate audiences where they can, benchmark against competitors and generate client-ready reporting on a schedule. Brands and agencies run on this. Esports organisations, whose entire product is cross-platform audience, do not use it — partly because the streaming and broadcast surfaces that matter most are not covered.

## What Already Exists
Multi-network data collection; engagement metric normalisation; audience overlap estimation; competitor benchmarking; and automated client reporting.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to live streaming and broadcast surfaces that social tools do not cover. It requires: (1) live streaming and tournament broadcast as first-class surfaces with concurrency and watch time, which no social analytics platform models — this is the substantive difference and is where most of the attention actually is; (2) attention distributed across a dozen individual player accounts as well as the organisation's own, requiring a roster-level rollup; (3) borrowed tournament audience distinguished from owned audience, which has no social equivalent; (4) reporting aimed at a sponsor's renewal decision rather than at a brand's content team; and (5) players who leave and take their audience with them, which the model has to handle explicitly.

## Target Customer
Esports organisations, leagues, sponsorship agencies, and social analytics vendors seeking a vertical.

## Impact If Solved
Social analytics already normalises incompatible platform metrics for brands daily. Live streaming and broadcast as first-class surfaces, rolled up across a roster, is what the category does not cover and is where the attention is.
