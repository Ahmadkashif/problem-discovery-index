# The Player Career

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/esports-organizations|Esports Organizations]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make a career that starts at seventeen and ends in the mid-twenties into something a person can survive and leave, and whoever builds that takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$150M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — nothing exists
**Target Buyer:** Players and player associations
**Automation Potential:** Medium — planning, records and transition

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A professional career that starts at seventeen and is usually over by the mid-twenties, with practice schedules that would concern any other sport, no collective representation in most titles, and no plan for afterwards. The players carry the health risk, the contract risk and the transition risk, and the sector's contraction has been absorbed disproportionately by them. Nothing exists that serves the player as the customer rather than the organisation.

## Current Tools & Gaps
An agent if they have one, a contract they may not have had reviewed, and whatever the organisation provides. The gaps: no earnings and career record; no contract benchmarking; no health monitoring for a career of this intensity; no financial planning for a compressed earning window; and no transition support into what comes next.
