# Athlete Career Services From Professional Sport

**Industry:** [[industries/esports-organizations|Esports Organizations]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Professional sport built player associations, transition programmes and career services after the same failures, and esports has none of it.

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to make a career that starts at seventeen and ends in the mid-twenties into something a person can survive and leave, and whoever builds that takes the account.

## The Problem
Professional sport learned this lesson expensively and built the response: players' associations with collective bargaining, minimum contract standards, published salary benchmarks, pension and welfare funds, mandated transition and education programmes, and medical standards for training load. It exists because generations of athletes finished their careers in their late twenties with nothing. Esports has a shorter career, a younger entry age, comparable intensity and almost none of the apparatus.

## What Already Exists
Player associations and collective agreements; minimum contract standards; salary benchmarking and transparency; career transition and education programmes; and training load and medical standards.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a sector with no associations, no tenure and a global player base. It requires: (1) no collective bargaining structure in most titles, so the benchmarking and standards must be built bottom-up by an intermediary rather than negotiated — this is the substantive difference and is why nothing has transferred; (2) careers ending in the mid-twenties rather than the mid-thirties, compressing every plan; (3) players distributed across nationalities and jurisdictions with no common employment framework; (4) health risks that are repetitive, sedentary and psychological rather than acute and physical, requiring an entirely different medical standard; and (5) a personal audience that is portable career value with no traditional sports analogue.

## Target Customer
Players and emerging associations, leagues and publishers with welfare policies, organisations under scrutiny, and athlete services providers.

## Impact If Solved
Sport built associations, standards and transition programmes after the same failures. With no collective bargaining structure in most titles, the benchmarks have to be built bottom-up rather than negotiated — which is why none of it has crossed over.
