# Three People, Five Platforms, Every Day

**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let two or three people produce the content that now carries more of the business than the competition does, across five platforms with five sets of rules — and whoever makes that workload survivable takes the account.

## The Problem
The content team watches hours of streams and matches, identifies the moments, cuts them, reformats for each platform's aspect ratio and length rules, writes copy, schedules, and posts. They do this daily across a roster of players and several platforms, with a sponsor's content commitments on top. The output is the organisation's primary revenue driver, and the production method is manual from end to end.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Creator tooling targets individual creators, not a team covering a roster. Broadcast tooling targets large media operations. The team is too small to be a market anyone has pursued. And the work looks like editing, which is assumed to be inherently manual.

## What to Build
Find the moments automatically and let the people choose between them. Detect candidate moments from game state, audio and chat reaction rather than requiring someone to watch everything, which is the core and removes the largest single time cost. Cut and reformat automatically for each platform's specifications, since reformatting the same clip five times is pure mechanical loss. Generate copy and captions as a starting draft rather than a finished product, as the team's judgement is worth more applied to selection than to typing. Feed performance back into which moment types to prioritise, which nobody does and which compounds over a season. Maintain a searchable asset library across the roster so past material is reusable rather than lost. Surface sponsor content obligations in the same queue as everything else, because the collision between them is what causes the overtime. Cover the whole roster's streams rather than one channel at a time, which is the structural difference from creator tools. Schedule across platforms from one place with per-platform rules encoded. Preserve the team's editorial control over selection, which is what makes the tool acceptable rather than resented. And measure the team's output and load, since a team this small carrying this much revenue is a risk nobody has quantified.

## Target Customer
Esports organisations and content teams, leagues and tournament operators, creator tooling vendors, and sports media operations.

## Impact If Built
The part of the business that generates most of the revenue runs on three people and consumer software. Automatic moment detection and per-platform reformatting removes the mechanical work and leaves the judgement.
