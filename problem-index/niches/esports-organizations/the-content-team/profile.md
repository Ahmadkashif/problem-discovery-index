# The Content Team

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/esports-organizations|Esports Organizations]]
**Category:** 🟣 Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to let two or three people produce the content that now carries more of the business than the competition does, across five platforms with five sets of rules — and whoever makes that workload survivable takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$100M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — manual
**Target Buyer:** Content leads
**Automation Potential:** Very high — clipping, formatting and scheduling

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Content is now a larger part of the business than competition, and it is produced by a small team clipping streams by hand and posting to five platforms with five sets of rules. They are the people generating most of the attention the organisation sells, working with consumer editing tools on an output schedule set by a sponsorship contract. The economics of the whole organisation rest on this team, and the team has no tooling.

## Current Tools & Gaps
An editing application, a scheduling tool, five platform interfaces and a shared drive. The gaps: clipping moments by hand from hours of stream; reformatting per platform manually; no performance feedback loop into what to make; no asset library; and no visibility of what sponsor obligations require.
