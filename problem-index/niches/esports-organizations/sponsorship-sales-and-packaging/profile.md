# Sponsorship Sales & Packaging

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/esports-organizations|Esports Organizations]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to package and price inventory whose value nobody can establish, against sponsors who have become far more demanding since the contraction — and whoever can price it credibly takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$300M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~20% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — manual decks
**Target Buyer:** Partnership directors
**Automation Potential:** High — inventory, pricing and delivery tracking

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Selling the sponsorship is a separate business from measuring the audience. It means knowing what inventory exists across jerseys, broadcasts, streams, content series, social posts and player appearances; pricing it; tracking whether it was delivered; and managing renewal. Most organisations do this in a spreadsheet and a set of decks, price by precedent and negotiation, and discover delivery shortfalls at the end of the term. Sponsors, having tightened considerably since the contraction, now ask questions the process cannot answer.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A deck template, a spreadsheet of commitments, an email thread and a CRM someone set up. The gaps: no inventory system; pricing by precedent rather than by value; no delivery tracking against contract; no renewal risk view; and no record of which activations performed.
