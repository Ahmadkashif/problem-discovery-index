# Inventory, Price and Delivery

**Niche:** [[niches/esports-organizations/sponsorship-sales-and-packaging/profile|Sponsorship Sales & Packaging]]
**Industry:** [[industries/esports-organizations|Esports Organizations]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** The organisation sells inventory it has never catalogued at prices set by precedent and delivers it without tracking.
**Tags:** #revenue-impact #workflow-orchestration #data-integration #evaluation-metrics #automation #confidence-intervals #descriptive-statistics #gradient-boosting
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to package and price inventory whose value nobody can establish, against sponsors who have become far more demanding since the contraction — and whoever can price it credibly takes the account.

## The Problem
An organisation's sellable inventory spans jersey placements, broadcast mentions, stream overlays, content series, social posts, player appearances and event activations. Nobody maintains a catalogue of it, so packages are assembled from memory, priced against what the last deal fetched, and committed to without checking whether the same slot has already been sold. Delivery is tracked informally and shortfalls surface at renewal, when they are most damaging.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The organisations are small and the sales team is two people. Sponsorship management software serves large rights holders at prices these organisations cannot pay. Inventory is genuinely awkward to define across owned and borrowed surfaces. And during the growth years demand exceeded supply, so none of this mattered.

## What to Build
Catalogue the inventory and track delivery against the contract. Maintain a structured inventory of every sellable asset with its availability and prior pricing, which is the core and prevents the double-sell and the underpriced package in one step. Track delivery against contracted commitments continuously rather than at term end, since a shortfall found in month three is fixable and one found at renewal is not. Price from delivered attention and past performance rather than from precedent, which is the argument that survives a demanding sponsor. Model package construction so a target budget maps to available inventory automatically. Flag renewal risk early from delivery and engagement signals, as most losses are visible months ahead. Keep a record of which activation formats performed, because that is the genuinely valuable asset and it currently lives in people's memories. Generate the reporting the sponsor receives from the same system that tracks delivery. Handle the roster dependency explicitly, since a player leaving can void a significant part of the inventory. Support multi-year and multi-title deals without a spreadsheet per deal. And keep it cheap enough for an organisation with two salespeople, which is the binding constraint.

## Target Customer
Esports organisations, leagues and tournament operators, sponsorship agencies, and sports rights management vendors.

## Impact If Built
A shortfall found in month three is fixable and one found at renewal is not, and nothing currently looks. An inventory catalogue with continuous delivery tracking prevents the double-sell, the underprice and the surprise.
