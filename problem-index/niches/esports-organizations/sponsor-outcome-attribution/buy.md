## The Problem
Sponsorship evaluation is a real industry in traditional sport. Media valuation of brand exposure, brand lift and awareness tracking against control markets, activation effectiveness studies, and agreed valuation methodologies are all standard, supplied by established firms, and used to justify eight-figure deals. Esports sponsorship is sold into the same marketing budgets by the same buyers, and almost none of this apparatus has been applied to it.

## What Already Exists
Media exposure valuation; brand lift and awareness tracking; control market study design; activation effectiveness measurement; and sponsorship valuation benchmarks.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to exposure that is digital, fragmented and largely on surfaces the rights holder does not own. It requires: (1) exposure valuation across streams, clips and social rather than broadcast camera time, so the underlying measurement is entirely different — this is the substantive difference and existing valuation methods do not apply; (2) audiences too small for geographic control market designs, requiring within-platform or audience-based controls; (3) a heavy creator and personality component, where the player's endorsement rather than the logo's placement is what was bought; (4) an audience that is younger and less reachable by the survey panels brand tracking relies on; and (5) rights holders who cannot afford the evaluation firms that serve traditional sport, needing a far cheaper delivery model.

## Target Customer
Sponsors and agencies, esports organisations and leagues, sponsorship evaluation firms, and brand measurement vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Traditional sport has an evaluation industry with agreed methods and established firms. Exposure across streams and clips rather than camera time, and audiences too small for control markets, is what has to be rebuilt.
