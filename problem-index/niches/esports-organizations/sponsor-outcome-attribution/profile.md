# Sponsor Outcome Attribution

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/esports-organizations|Esports Organizations]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show that the attention delivered produced something for the sponsor, using outcome data the sponsor holds and the organisation cannot see — and whoever gets that loop closed takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$200M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — nothing returns
**Target Buyer:** Sponsors and their agencies
**Automation Potential:** Medium — causal measurement across a boundary

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
This half cannot be built alone. Whether a sponsorship produced awareness, consideration, sign-ups or sales is answerable only with data inside the sponsor's business, and none of it comes back to the organisation. That makes it a fundamentally different product — sold into the sponsor relationship rather than into the organisation's analytics stack, dependent on a data-sharing agreement, and valuable precisely because the counterparty's cooperation is the scarce input rather than the modelling.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A promo code, occasionally a brand lift study the sponsor commissioned and did not share. The gaps: no outcome data returning; no control group; no brand measurement; no attribution model spanning the boundary; and no contractual mechanism obliging any of it.
