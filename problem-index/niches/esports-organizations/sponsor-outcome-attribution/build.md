# Closing the Loop With the Sponsor

**Niche:** [[niches/esports-organizations/sponsor-outcome-attribution/profile|Sponsor Outcome Attribution]]
**Industry:** [[industries/esports-organizations|Esports Organizations]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** The organisation delivers attention and the only party who can see whether it worked never sends anything back.
**Tags:** #causal-inference #hypothesis-testing #confidence-intervals #evaluation-metrics #data-integration #revenue-impact #descriptive-statistics #compliance
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to show that the attention delivered produced something for the sponsor, using outcome data the sponsor holds and the organisation cannot see — and whoever gets that loop closed takes the account.

## The Problem
A sponsor pays for visibility and hopes for an outcome. Whether awareness moved, consideration shifted, sign-ups rose or sales followed is knowable only inside the sponsor's own systems. Nothing flows back to the organisation, so the organisation cannot demonstrate value, cannot price correctly, cannot improve the activation and cannot defend a renewal. The measurement problem is not primarily statistical — it is that the necessary data sits on the other side of a commercial boundary nobody has arranged to cross.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
It requires the sponsor to share data they regard as confidential, with a party who has an interest in the result. No standard agreement or intermediary exists. Organisations are small and have no leverage to ask. And nobody has demonstrated the arrangement working, so there is no precedent to point at.

## What to Build
Design the data-sharing arrangement, then the measurement. Build a clean-room arrangement where the sponsor's outcome data and the organisation's exposure data meet without either party seeing the other's raw records, which is the core and is the mechanism that makes the whole thing possible. Establish exposure and control groups within the sponsor's audience, since without a comparison the result is a correlation nobody will act on. Measure brand outcomes with a proper study design where sales are not the objective, as most esports sponsorship is upper-funnel and sales attribution is the wrong instrument. Standardise a measurement addendum for sponsorship contracts, which is the commercial innovation rather than the technical one and is what unlocks everything else. Report to the sponsor first rather than to the organisation, because the sponsor's trust is the constraint. Accept aggregate rather than record-level data, which removes most of the objection. Measure activation quality as well as exposure quantity, since the same audience converts very differently by execution. Build the historical record so renewal conversations rest on evidence rather than on narrative. Offer independent administration, as a self-reported result from an interested party is worth nothing. And start with one willing sponsor and publish the case, which is how a precedent gets created.

## Target Customer
Sponsors and their agencies, esports organisations and leagues, measurement and clean-room vendors, and sports marketing consultancies.

## Impact If Built
The data that answers the question sits on the other side of a commercial boundary nobody has arranged to cross. A clean room plus a standard measurement addendum in the contract is the arrangement, and the statistics are the easy part.
