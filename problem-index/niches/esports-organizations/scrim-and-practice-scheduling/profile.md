# Scrim & Practice Scheduling

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/esports-organizations|Esports Organizations]]
**Category:** ⚡ Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get practice matches against the right opposition at the right time, across timezones, through a process that runs entirely in private group chats — and whoever systematises it takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$50M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~3% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — group chats
**Target Buyer:** Coaching staff
**Automation Potential:** Very high — matching, scheduling and review

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Practice matches against other professional teams are the core of competitive preparation, and they are arranged by a coach messaging other coaches in private chats. Scheduling depends on personal relationships, timezone overlap and who replies first. The quality of opposition is not matched to what the team needs to practise, the schedule collides with travel and content obligations, and none of it is recorded in a way that supports review. It is pure coordination overhead borne by the most expensive staff in the building.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A group chat, a shared calendar and a coach's contact list. The gaps: no matching by need or strength; no timezone-aware scheduling; no collision detection with travel and sponsor obligations; no record of scrims played and outcomes; and no review workflow attached.
