**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get practice matches against the right opposition at the right time, across timezones, through a process that runs entirely in private group chats — and whoever systematises it takes the account.

## The Problem
Matching two parties with mutual availability constraints and preferences is thoroughly solved software. Marketplaces do it for tutoring, medical appointments, interviews and sports fixtures: availability declaration, preference and compatibility matching, automatic timezone handling, confirmation and reminder flows, and cancellation policies. The problem of arranging a practice match between two professional teams is structurally identical and is handled by messaging people you know.

## What Already Exists
Two-sided availability matching; preference and compatibility scoring; timezone-aware scheduling; confirmation and reminder workflows; and cancellation and no-show policy handling.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to a market where the counterparty is a competitor. It requires: (1) matching between teams who are rivals, so what is disclosed to secure a session is itself sensitive — a marketplace where both sides withhold information is a different design problem and is the substantive difference; (2) reciprocity norms that determine access, which a neutral matching algorithm can easily violate; (3) compatibility defined by competitive strength and practice objective rather than by service and price; (4) five-person availability on both sides rather than two individuals; and (5) collision constraints against a competitive calendar, international travel and sponsor commitments.

## Target Customer
Esports organisations and coaching staff, leagues and publishers, tier-two and academy teams, and scheduling platform vendors.

## Impact If Solved
Two-sided availability matching is solved software used across many industries. A marketplace where both sides are rivals withholding information, governed by reciprocity norms, is what has to be designed rather than bought.
