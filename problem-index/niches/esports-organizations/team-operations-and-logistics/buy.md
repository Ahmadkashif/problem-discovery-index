# Global Mobility From Corporate Relocation

**Niche:** [[niches/esports-organizations/team-operations-and-logistics/profile|Team Operations & Logistics]]
**Industry:** [[industries/esports-organizations|Esports Organizations]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Corporate global mobility manages visas, relocation and compliance across jurisdictions as a mature service, and esports teams manage it in a spreadsheet.
**Tags:** #workflow-orchestration #compliance #automation #data-integration #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #descriptive-statistics #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to run an international competitive operation — visas, travel, bootcamps, contracts, welfare, sponsor deadlines — through one person with a spreadsheet, and whoever gives that person a system takes the account.

## The Problem
Global mobility is an established corporate function with established software: immigration case management, document and expiry tracking, relocation coordination, multi-jurisdiction employment compliance, and duty-of-care systems for employees abroad. Any multinational moving staff across borders runs on it. An esports organisation moves a roster of young foreign nationals into a shared house in another country every season and runs it on one person's memory.

## What Already Exists
Immigration case and document management; expiry and deadline tracking; relocation coordination; multi-jurisdiction employment compliance; and duty-of-care and traveller tracking.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to very young employees on short careers, moving constantly, under a competition calendar. It requires: (1) a tournament calendar as the hard constraint that every deadline is measured against — a filing that is merely late is a missed major, which is the substantive difference from corporate relocation's softer timelines; (2) frequent short-notice international travel for events, rather than one relocation per assignment; (3) shared team accommodation with welfare obligations toward people often under twenty; (4) an organisation with no HR function at all, so the tool must be the process; and (5) a price point suited to a business with fifteen staff, far below what mobility platforms charge.

## Target Customer
Esports organisations, team managers, leagues, and global mobility vendors seeking a small-organisation tier.

## Impact If Solved
Corporate mobility manages immigration and duty of care as a mature service with real software. A tournament calendar that turns a late filing into a missed major, and an organisation with no HR function, are what the adaptation must handle.
