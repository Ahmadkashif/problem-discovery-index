# Team Operations & Logistics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/esports-organizations|Esports Organizations]]
**Category:** 🟠 Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to run an international competitive operation — visas, travel, bootcamps, contracts, welfare, sponsor deadlines — through one person with a spreadsheet, and whoever gives that person a system takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$150M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~10% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — one person
**Target Buyer:** Team managers
**Automation Potential:** High — workflow and compliance

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
One person handles visas, flights, bootcamps, contracts, scrim scheduling, player welfare and the sponsor's content deadline, for a roster living in a house in another country. It is an international HR, immigration, travel and facilities operation carried by a single generalist with no system, and the failure modes are severe: a visa that does not arrive before a major event can end a season. The role is a single point of failure in every organisation and nobody builds for it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
A spreadsheet, a shared calendar, a group chat and an immigration lawyer on email. The gaps: no visa and document tracking with deadlines; no travel management; no contract and obligation register; no welfare or housing record; and no handover if the person leaves.
