# Audience Measurement From Broadcast and Media

**Industry:** [[industries/esports-organizations|Esports Organizations]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Broadcast built independent audience measurement because advertisers would not buy without it, and esports never did.
**Tags:** #data-integration #descriptive-statistics #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #causal-inference #revenue-impact #automation #probability-distributions
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to demonstrate the audience attention it sells, when that attention is spread across a broadcast, a dozen player streams, several short-form platforms and social accounts that report in incompatible units — and whoever can demonstrate it takes the account.

## The Problem
Television and radio solved this a long time ago, and the solution was institutional rather than technical: an independent measurement body, an agreed currency, panel and census methodologies, published standards, and audits. Advertisers would not buy on a broadcaster's own claims, so a third party was created. Digital media repeated the exercise with viewability and verification standards. Esports sells the same product to the same buyers and has neither a currency nor an auditor.

## What Already Exists
Independent measurement bodies and audience currencies; panel and census methodology; reach, frequency and gross rating point frameworks; cross-platform deduplication; and third-party verification and audit.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation is to an audience on platforms that report selectively and surfaces the seller does not own. It requires: (1) measurement across platforms that do not provide the underlying data, so the currency must be built from what is exposed rather than from a controlled panel — this is the substantive difference and it makes independence harder to achieve; (2) attention split between an organisation's own channels and a tournament's broadcast, requiring an ownership concept broadcast never needed; (3) a heavy short-form component where a view is three seconds; (4) sellers small enough that none can fund a measurement body alone, making collective action the only route; and (5) sponsors who buy a team's identity rather than only its reach, adding an affinity dimension broadcast currencies do not carry.

## Target Customer
Esports organisations, leagues, sponsors and agencies, and media measurement firms.

## Impact If Solved
Broadcast created an independent currency because advertisers would not buy on the seller's own claims. Building one from platform data nobody controls, for sellers too small to fund it alone, is what makes this collective rather than individual.
