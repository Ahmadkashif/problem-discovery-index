# The Screenshot in the Sponsorship Deck

**Niche:** [[niches/esports-organizations/audience-measurement/profile|Audience Measurement]]
**Industry:** [[industries/esports-organizations|Esports Organizations]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The renewal meeting is on Thursday and the evidence is a pasted chart, a follower count and a number that adds views to impressions.
**Tags:** #quick-win #data-integration #descriptive-statistics #evaluation-metrics #automation #confidence-intervals #revenue-impact #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to demonstrate the audience attention it sells, when that attention is spread across a broadcast, a dozen player streams, several short-form platforms and social accounts that report in incompatible units — and whoever can demonstrate it takes the account.

## The Problem
Sponsorship renewal decks are assembled by hand, a few days before the meeting, from screenshots of platform dashboards. The headline number sums incompatible metrics. The sponsor's marketing team knows it does, discounts everything in the deck, and negotiates down. The organisation has the underlying data spread across six accounts and no time or tooling to assemble it properly, so the same deck gets rebuilt every quarter.

## Why It's Still Broken
Nobody automated the collection — data that must be gathered by hand from six dashboards before every meeting will always be gathered late and presented badly, however important the meeting is. There is no reporting template. The sector's norms tolerated it during the growth years. And the person assembling it is a salesperson, not an analyst.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Automate the collection and stop adding unlike things. Pull platform metrics automatically into one store on a schedule, which is the fix and is a week of integration work against public APIs. Stop summing incompatible metrics and report each surface in its own unit, since an honest set of numbers outperforms a discounted composite. Add watch time wherever the platform exposes it, as it is the closest thing to a common unit available today. Show the trend rather than a snapshot, because direction is what a renewal conversation is actually about. Report delivery against what was contracted, which most decks omit entirely and which is the sponsor's real question. Include the branded content's own performance separately. Generate the deck from the store rather than by hand, so it takes an hour rather than three days. Keep the methodology on a slide, which is disarming rather than damaging. Flag where the data is weak instead of filling the gap with a bigger number. And send the report monthly rather than at renewal, since a sponsor informed continuously negotiates differently from one presented with a case.

## Who Feels the Pain
Partnership managers discounted before they open their mouths; organisations losing renewals they earned; sponsors unable to justify the spend internally; and a sector whose credibility problem is self-inflicted.

## Impact If Fixed
Data that must be gathered by hand from six dashboards before every meeting will always be gathered late and presented badly. Automated collection and honest per-surface reporting turns a three-day deck into an hour and a discount into a negotiation.
