# Audience Measurement

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/esports-organizations|Esports Organizations]]
**Category:** 🔵 High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to demonstrate the audience attention it sells, when that attention is spread across a broadcast, a dozen player streams, several short-form platforms and social accounts that report in incompatible units — and whoever can demonstrate it takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$450M US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~30% of category revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Very low — screenshots
**Target Buyer:** Commercial leadership
**Automation Potential:** High — aggregation and attribution

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Almost all organisation revenue comes from sponsors buying audience attention. That attention is fragmented across surfaces the organisation does not own, each reporting a different metric with a different definition, none of which connects to anything the sponsor cares about. The sector sold this on narrative through its growth years and is now being asked for evidence by considerably more demanding sponsors. It is the entire business, and it is run on screenshots of viewership charts.

### Contested sub-niches
This niche is not terminal. It names a capability, and two different problems sit inside it:
- [[niches/esports-organizations/cross-surface-attention/profile|🎯 Cross-Surface Attention Aggregation]] — turning incompatible platform numbers into one comparable unit
- [[niches/esports-organizations/sponsor-outcome-attribution/profile|🎯 Sponsor Outcome Attribution]] — showing that the attention produced something

## Current Tools & Gaps
Platform dashboards, a spreadsheet, screenshots pasted into a deck, and a third-party viewership tracker. The gaps: no common unit across surfaces; no deduplication of the same fan counted five times; no reach or frequency concept; no sponsor outcome data; and no independent verification of anything claimed.
