# One Number the Sponsor Can Check

**Niche:** [[niches/esports-organizations/audience-measurement/profile|Audience Measurement]]
**Industry:** [[industries/esports-organizations|Esports Organizations]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** The sector sells audience attention and has never built an instrument that measures it.
**Tags:** #data-integration #evaluation-metrics #descriptive-statistics #confidence-intervals #causal-inference #revenue-impact #automation #k-nearest-neighbors
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to demonstrate the audience attention it sells, when that attention is spread across a broadcast, a dozen player streams, several short-form platforms and social accounts that report in incompatible units — and whoever can demonstrate it takes the account.

## The Problem
An organisation's audience touches a tournament broadcast, individual player streams, clips on several short-form platforms and a set of social accounts. Concurrent viewers, views, impressions, plays and followers are not the same unit and are not additive, the same fan appears on several surfaces, and none of it maps to whether a sponsor got anything. The organisation adds the numbers anyway because there is nothing else to put in the deck, and sponsors have stopped accepting it.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
The platforms report what suits them and give organisations limited data. No independent measurement body serves this sector. Sponsors have not collectively demanded a standard, so no standard exists. And the organisations are individually too small to build one and have never acted together.

## What to Build
Define the unit first, then collect against it. Establish a common attention unit across surfaces — watch time or an equivalent — and convert every platform's reporting into it, which is the core and is the thing whose absence makes the whole deck unverifiable. Deduplicate the audience across surfaces so the same fan is not counted five times, since the inflation is obvious to sponsors and is why the numbers are discounted. Build reach and frequency rather than only totals, as that is the vocabulary media buyers actually use and its absence marks the sector as unserious. Distinguish the organisation's own audience from the tournament's borrowed audience, which is the distinction sponsors most want and organisations most avoid. Measure branded content separately from general content, because that is what was actually sold. Produce an auditable methodology document, since a number without a method is a claim. Allow independent verification of the underlying data, which is what would end the credibility problem outright. Report ranges rather than points where platform data is thin. Track the same measures over time so trends are visible. And make the output a standard report format that several organisations could share, as a common format is worth more than a better number.

## Target Customer
Esports organisations, leagues and tournament operators, sponsors and their agencies, and sports measurement firms.

## Impact If Built
Concurrent viewers, views, impressions and followers are not the same unit and are not additive, and the sector adds them anyway. A common attention unit with deduplication and a published method is what makes the claim checkable.
