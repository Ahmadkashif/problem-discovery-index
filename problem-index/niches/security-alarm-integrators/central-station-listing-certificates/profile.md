# Central-Station Listing & Alarm Certificate Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/security-alarm-integrators|Security Alarm Integrators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Quasi-regulatory certification of monitoring centres and installed systems that AHJs and insurers rely on. UL Solutions lists central stations to UL 827 and issues alarm certificates for systems installed and monitored by listed companies; Intertek (ETL) and FM Approvals run comparable programmes. Stations and dealers pay for audits and certificates.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Field auditors are production; programme analysts number fewer than 10 per body (estimate)
**Output:** Listings, audit findings, alarm certificates, certificate verification for insurers and AHJs
**Proprietary data:** Audit histories of listed stations and the database of certificated systems
**Clock:** Annual audits and certificate renewals
**Buyer:** Programme director at the certifier
