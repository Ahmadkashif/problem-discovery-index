# Dealer Programme Account Underwriting

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/security-alarm-integrators|Security Alarm Integrators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Authorised dealer programmes run by the largest residential providers buy newly originated accounts from independent dealers at a multiple of RMR, subject to credit, contract and installation standards, and charge back accounts that cancel within an agreed window. ADT spent $596M on dealer-generated accounts and bulk purchases in 2025. The underwriting teams set purchase criteria, score each submitted account, audit dealers and track chargebacks.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 underwriting, dealer-audit and portfolio analysts per programme (estimate)
**Output:** Account purchase decisions and pricing, dealer scorecards, chargeback and attrition curves by dealer and cohort
**Proprietary data:** Every purchased account's origination profile joined to its full payment and cancellation history, across hundreds of dealers
**Clock:** Purchase funding cycles and chargeback windows written into the programme's own dealer agreements
**Buyer:** VP of dealer channel or chief financial officer

**Kill switches:** none — the accounts are owned by the programme once purchased; the consumer credit pulls used at origination are a permissible-purpose matter, not a data-ownership one
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. An internal function with no invoice, clocks set by its own contracts, and two or three programmes in the country. It holds the best record of which originated accounts survive, which is exactly what small dealers and acquirers price blind.
