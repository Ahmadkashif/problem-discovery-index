# Municipal False-Alarm Permit & Billing Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/security-alarm-integrators|Security Alarm Integrators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Companies that run alarm permit and false-alarm programmes for cities and counties: registering alarm users, matching police dispatch records to permits, issuing invoices and fines, handling appeals and collecting. Public Safety Corporation's CryWolf and PM AM are the established names. They are commonly paid a share of collections (Reading, Pa. agreed 25–35% in 2012; Calexico, Calif. weighed bids of 12.3% and 18% in 2017), so the alarm user effectively funds them.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Fewer than 10 analysts per vendor; the staff are billing, customer-service and appeals clerks (estimate)
**Output:** Permits, invoices, fines, false-alarm reports to the police department
**Proprietary data:** Dispatch-to-permit matches and payment histories by jurisdiction, held on the city's behalf
**Clock:** Ordinance billing cycles and appeal deadlines
**Buyer:** Vendor CEO; on the client side, the police department's alarm coordinator
