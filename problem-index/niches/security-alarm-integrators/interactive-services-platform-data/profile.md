# Interactive Services Platform Data Teams

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/security-alarm-integrators|Security Alarm Integrators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The cloud platforms that connect alarm panels, cameras and smart-home devices to apps and central stations, resold by dealers under their own brands: Alarm.com, Resideo's Total Connect and Johnson Controls' Qolsys and PowerManage. Alarm.com reported $1.0112B of 2025 revenue, $689.4M of it SaaS and license fees paid monthly by service-provider partners, and data from more than 170 million connected devices routed to about 1,000 third-party central stations. Their data teams build device analytics, video AI, dealer business-intelligence reports and insurer partnerships.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 data scientists and analysts at the largest platform (estimate)
**Output:** Dealer analytics and retention insights, video and activity analytics inside the product, device-health reporting, insurer and utility data programmes
**Proprietary data:** Device telemetry, arming behaviour, video events and app engagement across tens of millions of properties, the widest view of how monitored homes and businesses actually use their systems
**Clock:** Product release cycles; carrier network sunsets set by telecoms
**Buyer:** Chief technology officer or head of data science

**Kill switches:** none live — Alarm.com's end-user privacy policy (last updated 9 July 2024) says it acts as a data processor for the subscriber's service provider, but reserves use of the data to improve and develop Alarm.com's own products, "including by aggregating information to understand how our products and Services are being used". That is a vendor product right over aggregate data, which the vault does not treat as a live switch. The dealer agreements limit the platform's use of each dealer's confidential customer information to performing the agreement, so aggregate insight sold to third parties such as insurers is not reserved. That bears on Q1 and Q3, not on the switch. Contrast [[niches/security-guard-firms/video-analytics-vendors/profile|🔍 Video Analytics & Security Technology Vendors]] (39), where site video is contractually walled per client.
**Verdict:** Logged — below threshold. The insight is a feature of a SaaS fee rather than an invoice, three or four platforms make up the market, and the richest dataset in the industry can be pooled only to improve the platform's own products.
