**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep each customer's alarm eligible for police response under its own city's ordinance (permitted, under the false-alarm limit and verified to the local standard), and whoever keeps the most accounts dispatchable with the fewest fines takes the account.

## The Problem
Dealers register new systems with cities by hand, if at all. Renewals lapse, and customers who move inherit or lose permits. Dealer licences, which some states and cities also require of alarm companies, are tracked separately.

## What Already Exists
Business-licence compliance services (Harbor Compliance, Avalara's licence offering and similar) research requirements by jurisdiction, file registrations and track renewals for multi-location companies.

## The Customization Gap
Alarm permits attach to the end customer's address, not the dealer's location. They're tied to false-alarm counts and fines from the city's billing vendor, and some ordinances require the alarm company to register the system or install verification. The adaptation needs account-level filing at install, ingestion of fine and suspension notices from vendors such as CryWolf and PM AM, verification-rule fields, and an interface to the central station so suspended accounts are flagged at dispatch time.

## Target Customer
Dealers installing thousands of systems a year across many cities, and the compliance teams of wholesale stations serving them.

## Impact If Solved
Permits are filed at install and renewed on time, so customers avoid the non-permit fines that some cities charge on top of false-alarm fines.
