# Small Independent Alarm Dealers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/security-alarm-integrators|Security Alarm Integrators]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to become the wholesale monitoring and platform partner a small dealer can't leave, and whoever makes the dealer's accounts worth the most at sale, through lower attrition and cleaner contracts, takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** $4.50B of 2022 receipts across the 4,980 firms with fewer than 20 employees (Census SUSB 2022)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~15% of 561621 receipts, 86% of firms
**Digital Adoption:** Medium-Low — the panels are connected through Alarm.com or a similar platform, while contracts, billing and service run on QuickBooks, paper files and the owner's memory
**Target Buyer:** Owner-operator, often a former technician
**Automation Potential:** Medium — back-office tasks automate; the owner's relationships and installs don't

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Most alarm companies are small: an owner, a few technicians and an office manager with a book of a few hundred to a few thousand accounts. They don't run a central station. They contract with a wholesale station (Rapid Response, COPS Monitoring, Affiliated Monitoring, Avantguard and others) and resell an interactive platform. The book of accounts is the owner's retirement plan, and they sell it to a dealer programme or rollup at a multiple of RMR. The wholesale stations, platforms and acquirers all compete for these dealers. The dealer's own interest is a book that is worth the most when it's sold, and that depends on attrition, contract quality and documentation the dealer rarely measures.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Wholesale station dealer portals, platform dealer apps, small-business accounting, and alarm billing software sized for larger dealers (SedonaOffice, Bold). The gaps: no view of the book's attrition by cohort, contracts that can't be found or aren't assignable, and no benchmark of what the book would fetch.
