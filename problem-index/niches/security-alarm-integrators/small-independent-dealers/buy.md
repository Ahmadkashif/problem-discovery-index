# Small-Business Subscription Billing Fitted to Alarm Contracts

**Niche:** [[niches/security-alarm-integrators/small-independent-dealers/profile|Small Independent Alarm Dealers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/security-alarm-integrators|Security Alarm Integrators]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Subscription billing for small businesses is cheap and solved; alarm contracts need terms, renewals, wholesale costs and RMR reporting that it doesn't carry.
**Tags:** #feature-engineering #descriptive-statistics #data-integration #revenue-impact #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to become the wholesale monitoring and platform partner a small dealer can't leave, and whoever makes the dealer's accounts worth the most at sale, through lower attrition and cleaner contracts, takes the account.

## The Problem
Small dealers bill monitoring through QuickBooks recurring invoices or a payment processor, with contract terms in a filing cabinet. They can't produce an RMR report, an attrition figure or a list of contracts by renewal date when a buyer or lender asks.

## What Already Exists
Small-business subscription billing and accounting (QuickBooks Online, Stripe Billing, Chargebee) handles recurring charges, dunning and basic revenue reports.

## The Customization Gap
Alarm dealers need contract objects with term, auto-renewal and state cancellation rules. They need monitoring cost per account from the wholesale station and platform fees per account, so margin per account is known. They need RMR and gross and net attrition reporting in the form buyers and lenders request, and links from the account to its central-station number and permit status.

## Target Customer
Dealers below the size where alarm-specific billing suites pay off, typically under 3,000 accounts.

## Impact If Solved
The dealer can answer a buyer's or lender's first questions from its own system, and sees margin and attrition while there's time to act on them.
