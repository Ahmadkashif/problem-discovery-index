# What Is My Book Worth, and Why

**Niche:** [[niches/security-alarm-integrators/small-independent-dealers/profile|Small Independent Alarm Dealers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/security-alarm-integrators|Security Alarm Integrators]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A small dealer's largest asset is its book of accounts, and the owner learns what it's worth only when a buyer's diligence team marks it down.
**Tags:** #survival-analysis #gradient-boosting #large-language-models #feature-engineering #revenue-impact #data-integration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to become the wholesale monitoring and platform partner a small dealer can't leave, and whoever makes the dealer's accounts worth the most at sale, through lower attrition and cleaner contracts, takes the account.

## The Problem
Buyers price books at a multiple of RMR, typically ~30–55× by advisers' estimates, then adjust. They mark down for attrition, for contracts that are missing, expired or unassignable, for accounts on communicators due for replacement, and for customers who haven't paid in months. The owner discovers these adjustments in the letter of intent, years too late to fix them.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Small dealers are a fragmented market with thin software budgets. Valuation know-how sits with a few M&A advisers and acquirers' diligence teams, and wholesale stations hold the signal data without connecting it to valuation.

## What to Build
A dealer-facing book scorecard, offered through a wholesale station or platform. It ingests billing, contracts (scanned and read by an LLM for term, renewal and assignment clauses), signal exports and communicator types. It produces attrition by cohort, an estimated valuation range with the specific markdowns a buyer would take, and a fix list: re-sign these month-to-month accounts, replace these communicators, collect these past-dues.

## Target Customer
Owners of dealers with 500–10,000 accounts planning a sale within five years, reached through their wholesale station or platform as a value-added service.

## Impact If Built
Owners fix the book before they sell, wholesale stations retain dealers by making them more valuable, and buyers receive cleaner portfolios.
