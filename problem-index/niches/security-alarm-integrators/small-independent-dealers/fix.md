# The Contract the Dealer Can't Find at Sale

**Niche:** [[niches/security-alarm-integrators/small-independent-dealers/profile|Small Independent Alarm Dealers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/security-alarm-integrators|Security Alarm Integrators]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** At diligence the buyer asks for every customer contract, and too many of them are missing, expired or signed on a form that can't be assigned.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #quick-win #compliance #revenue-impact #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to become the wholesale monitoring and platform partner a small dealer can't leave, and whoever makes the dealer's accounts worth the most at sale, through lower attrition and cleaner contracts, takes the account.

## The Problem
Over twenty years a dealer uses several contract forms, signs some customers on paper and some electronically, lets terms roll to month-to-month and loses files in an office move. Buyers discount or exclude accounts without a valid, assignable contract, so the owner's book is suddenly worth less than the RMR suggested.

## Why It's Still Broken
Contracts are signed at install and never looked at again. Small dealers have no contract database, and the problem shows only at sale or in a dispute.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Scan and read every contract file with an LLM, extract term, renewal, assignment and cancellation clauses, match each to the billing record, and produce a list of accounts with missing or weak contracts. Fix them through an e-signature re-paper campaign years before sale.

## Who Feels the Pain
Owners selling their life's work at a discount; buyers excluding revenue they wanted; and customers on month-to-month terms nobody re-offered.

## Impact If Fixed
A cheap one-time clean-up protects the sale value of the book and turns month-to-month accounts back into contracted RMR.
