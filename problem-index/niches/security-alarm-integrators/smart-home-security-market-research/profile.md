# Smart Home & Security Market Research Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/security-alarm-integrators|Security Alarm Integrators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Research firms that survey households and track the monitored-security and smart-home market: adoption, churn intentions, DIY versus professional installation, pricing and brand share. Parks Associates is the best-known US specialist, alongside connected-home practices at larger technology research houses and physical-security analysts such as Memoori. Platform vendors, dealers, insurers, telecoms and investors buy the reports, data subscriptions and custom studies.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-30 analysts per firm across connected-home research, security being one practice (estimate)
**Output:** Consumer survey reports, market sizing and forecasts, churn and adoption trackers, custom studies, conference content
**Proprietary data:** Repeated household survey panels on security ownership, satisfaction and switching, kept over many years
**Clock:** Self-set publication calendars and industry conferences; client planning cycles
**Buyer:** Research director or CEO
