# RMR-Secured Lenders to Alarm Dealers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/security-alarm-integrators|Security Alarm Integrators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Banks, specialty finance companies and private-credit funds that lend to alarm dealers against recurring monthly revenue, with borrowing bases and covenants tied to RMR and attrition. Dealers pay interest and fees, and the lender's credit team monitors each borrower's book.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Fewer than 10 analysts on alarm lending per lender (estimate)
**Output:** Credit approvals, borrowing-base certificates, covenant monitoring
**Proprietary data:** Borrowers' monthly RMR and attrition reports, supplied under loan agreements
**Clock:** Monthly borrowing-base and quarterly covenant reporting
**Buyer:** Head of the security-industry lending group
