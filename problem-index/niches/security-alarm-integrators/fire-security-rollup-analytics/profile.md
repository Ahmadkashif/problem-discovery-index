# Fire & Security Rollup Corporate Development

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/security-alarm-integrators|Security Alarm Integrators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Private-equity-backed consolidators buying alarm, fire alarm, sprinkler and integration companies: Pye-Barker Fire & Safety (57 acquisitions in 2025, 9,000 staff in 47 states), Everon, Securitas Technology, and regional platforms. Corporate development and integration teams screen targets, value their RMR and service books, run diligence and migrate acquired accounts onto common billing and monitoring.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 corporate development, integration and FP&A staff per platform (estimate)
**Output:** Target screens, valuation models, diligence reports, integration plans and post-close attrition tracking
**Proprietary data:** Diligence files and post-close performance of dozens of acquired books, including how each seller's RMR actually behaved after purchase
**Clock:** Deal timelines negotiated with sellers; lender and sponsor reporting dates
**Buyer:** Chief development officer or chief financial officer
