# Alarm Account Valuation & M&A Advisers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/security-alarm-integrators|Security Alarm Integrators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Boutique advisers and brokers who value alarm companies and account books, run sale processes and publish attrition and multiple benchmarks for the trade. Owners selling to dealer programmes and rollups, and lenders sizing RMR-secured loans, pay success fees or valuation fees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 2-15 per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Valuations, sale memoranda, attrition and RMR-multiple benchmarks, conference presentations
**Proprietary data:** Deal files and attrition data from past engagements, supplied by clients under confidentiality
**Clock:** Deal timelines the parties set
**Buyer:** Founder or managing partner
