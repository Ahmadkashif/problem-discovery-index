# Identity Governance Extended to the Door

**Niche:** [[niches/security-alarm-integrators/access-control-credential-lifecycle/profile|Access Control & Credential Lifecycle]]
**Industry:** [[industries/security-alarm-integrators|Security Alarm Integrators]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Identity governance platforms already provision and review access to every application; physical doors are the application they handle worst.
**Tags:** #feature-engineering #data-integration #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep the access-control system's cardholders and door permissions exactly in step with the client's HR and IT identity records, and whoever makes every hire, transfer and termination reach the doors the same day takes the account.

## The Problem
The CISO's identity team runs joiner-mover-leaver processes for hundreds of applications through an identity governance platform. The access-control system is usually outside it, fed by a separate HR export or manual tickets that the security desk processes.

## What Already Exists
SailPoint, Saviynt, Okta and Microsoft Entra provide provisioning connectors, role models, certification campaigns and audit trails for logical access.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs physical-specific objects: doors, areas, schedules, readers, visitor and contractor credentials, and mobile and card credentials with their encoding. It needs connectors to each access platform's API and the integrator's change process. And it needs physical policies, such as egress and life-safety constraints and two-person rules for data halls. Someone must also map HR attributes to physical access levels, and that is integrator work.

## Target Customer
Enterprises already running an identity governance platform, and the integrators qualified to build and maintain the physical connector under a service agreement.

## Impact If Solved
One joiner-mover-leaver process covers both screens and doors, and physical access joins the audit evidence the identity team already produces.
