# The Badge That Still Works After the Termination

**Niche:** [[niches/security-alarm-integrators/access-control-credential-lifecycle/profile|Access Control & Credential Lifecycle]]
**Industry:** [[industries/security-alarm-integrators|Security Alarm Integrators]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** HR terminates an employee in the morning, IT disables the laptop by noon, and the badge opens the building for another week because the access-control change waited in someone's inbox.
**Tags:** #feature-engineering #descriptive-statistics #quick-win #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep the access-control system's cardholders and door permissions exactly in step with the client's HR and IT identity records, and whoever makes every hire, transfer and termination reach the doors the same day takes the account.

## The Problem
Badge deactivation depends on a manager or HR coordinator e-mailing the security desk, which keys the change by hand. Contractors' badges outlive their contracts, and transfers keep their old access. The first anyone learns of it is an auditor's sample or an incident.

## Why It's Still Broken
The access platform sits with facilities or security and the HR feed belongs to IT. Building the connector is a project nobody budgets until after an incident. Integrators are paid per change rather than for keeping the systems in step.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A daily reconciliation of active badges against the HR roster and the IT directory, with mismatches deactivated automatically for terminations and queued for review for everything else. A dashboard shows the security director the age of every open mismatch.

## Who Feels the Pain
Corporate security directors facing audit findings; HR and IT teams fielding the same requests twice; and integrators blamed for changes that never reached them.

## Impact If Fixed
Terminated badges stop working the same day, audit findings on physical access disappear, and the reconciliation becomes a recurring service the integrator can price.
