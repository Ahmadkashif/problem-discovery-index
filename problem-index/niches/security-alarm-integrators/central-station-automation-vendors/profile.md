# Central-Station Automation Software Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/security-alarm-integrators|Security Alarm Integrators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The software that receives alarm signals and runs operator workflows in central stations: Bold Technologies' Manitou, Dice, SIMS (Innovative Business Software) and MASterMind (Micro Key), with video monitoring in Immix and similar. Stations pay licences and hosting fees; the vendors integrate ASAP, ECV scripts and, increasingly, AVS-01 scoring.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Fewer than 10 analysts per vendor; the staff are developers and support engineers (estimate)
**Output:** Software releases, integrations, operational reports inside the product
**Proprietary data:** Hosted signal and disposition histories, held for client stations under licence
**Clock:** Release cycles; UL 827 and standards revisions
**Buyer:** Vendor CEO or CTO
