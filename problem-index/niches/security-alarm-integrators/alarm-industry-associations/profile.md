# Alarm Industry Associations & Standards Committees

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/security-alarm-integrators|Security Alarm Integrators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The Monitoring Association (TMA), which runs the ANSI-accredited AVS-01 alarm scoring standard and the ASAP programme with APCO; the Security Industry Association (SIA); the Electronic Security Association (ESA); the Security Industry Alarm Coalition (SIAC), which negotiates false-alarm ordinances with police; and the Partnership for Priority Video Alarm Response (PPVAR). Members pay dues; volunteer committees write the standards.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Fewer than 10 research and standards staff per association, with the technical work done by member committees (estimate)
**Output:** Standards (AVS-01, CS-V-01), model ordinances, market and workforce reports, training and certification
**Proprietary data:** Member surveys and committee records
**Clock:** Standards revision cycles; legislative and ordinance calendars
**Buyer:** Executive director or standards director
