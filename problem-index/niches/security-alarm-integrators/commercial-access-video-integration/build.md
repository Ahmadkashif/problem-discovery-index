Enterprise access control is won by whoever keeps the door system in step with the client's identity systems, and multi-site retail, bank and restaurant programmes are won by whoever proves hundreds of sites healthy at the lowest service cost per site. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
A commercial installation produces as-built drawings, panel programming files, camera lists and a punch list, stored in a project folder. Over five years technicians add readers, swap cameras, re-address panels and leave notes in tickets. By the time the service agreement or the whole system is rebid, the integrator's picture of the site is out of date. A competitor walking the site sees the same thing, so the rebid becomes a price contest.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Integrator software is built around projects and invoices, not assets. Device platforms know their own devices but not the rest of the site, and a multi-vendor site has three or four platforms. Integrators also treat as-builts as deliverables to the client rather than as their own operating record.

## What to Build
A site record assembled automatically from as-built PDFs, controller and VMS exports, network scans and service tickets, with LLM extraction for the documents. It keeps one entry per device with location, model, firmware, age, service history and health status. Kept current by every ticket, it becomes the basis for the service agreement price, lifecycle replacement proposals and the hosted-subscription conversion offer.

## Target Customer
VP of service or COO at integrators with 5,000+ commercial sites under service, and rollups integrating acquired integrators' customer bases.

## Impact If Built
The incumbent enters every rebid with a priced, evidenced lifecycle plan a competitor cannot match without weeks of site surveys, which protects the service and subscription revenue that carries the margin.
