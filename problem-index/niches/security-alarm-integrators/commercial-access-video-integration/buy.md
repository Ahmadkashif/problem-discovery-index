# IT Managed-Service Platforms Adapted to Security Integrators

**Industry:** [[industries/security-alarm-integrators|Security Alarm Integrators]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** IT managed-service providers solved recurring service, remote monitoring and asset tracking a decade ago; security integrators now sell the same shape of contract with tools built for projects.

**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a one-time access-control and video installation into years of service and hosted subscription revenue. Enterprise access control is won by whoever keeps the door system in step with the client's identity systems, and multi-site retail, bank and restaurant programmes are won by whoever proves hundreds of sites healthy at the lowest service cost per site. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## The Problem
Integrators are moving from install-and-leave to recurring service and hosted subscriptions, but their back office was built to bid and bill projects. Service agreements, remote health checks and per-device subscriptions are tracked in spreadsheets bolted onto project software.

## What Already Exists
Professional services automation and remote monitoring and management suites for IT managed-service providers (ConnectWise, Kaseya, N-able) handle contracts, ticketing, asset inventories, remote health checks and recurring billing for networked endpoints.

## The Customization Gap
Security systems need device classes the IT tools lack: door controllers, readers, locks, intrusion panels, cameras and NVR storage. Health checks have to work through each manufacturer's platform API rather than an endpoint agent. Billing has to cover per-door and per-camera hosted subscriptions and monitoring RMR. Service-level terms must reflect life safety, so a failed lock on an egress door is not a printer outage. And it must hold UL and code-required inspection records for intrusion and access systems.

## Target Customer
Integrators with a growing hosted access and video book and an existing service department, especially those already running ConnectWise for their IT services.

## Impact If Solved
Recurring service gets the operating system the IT channel already proved, without integrators building their own.
