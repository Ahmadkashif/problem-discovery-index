# Commercial Access Control & Video Integration

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/security-alarm-integrators|Security Alarm Integrators]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** *Not terminal as stated.* The honest partial sentence is that every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a one-time access-control and video installation into years of service and hosted subscription revenue. Enterprise access control is won by whoever keeps the door system in step with the client's identity systems, and multi-site retail, bank and restaurant programmes are won by whoever proves hundreds of sites healthy at the lowest service cost per site. Decomposed into contested sub-niches below.

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Commercial integrators win projects by bidding against a security consultant's specification or a general contractor's scope, often at thin margins. Securitas Technology, Everon, Convergint, Johnson Controls and hundreds of regional firms compete here. The money is in what follows the install: the service agreement, monitoring, and increasingly hosted access control and video sold per door or per camera per month. Two different contests sit under that one goal. An office campus or hospital buys access control as part of its identity system, and the integrator that keeps cardholders and doors synchronised with HR and IT wins. A national retailer or bank buys a programme across hundreds of sites, and the integrator that keeps every site's devices working and proves it to loss prevention wins. Different buyers, different data and different clocks, so the niche is decomposed.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Access-control platforms (Lenel, Software House, Genetec, Brivo, Verkada), video management systems (Avigilon, Genetec, Milestone), integrator business software (SedonaOffice, ConnectWise adaptations) and project tools. The gaps: no living installed-base record per site, service agreements that lapse silently after warranty, and device health that no one watches between visits.
