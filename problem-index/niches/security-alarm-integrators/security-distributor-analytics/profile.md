# Security Equipment Distributor Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/security-alarm-integrators|Security Alarm Integrators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The specialist distributors that sell panels, sensors, cameras, access hardware and cable to alarm dealers and integrators: ADI Global Distribution (part of Resideo), Wesco's Anixter security business, and regional distributors such as SS&D and Alarmax. Their category, pricing and supplier-analytics teams forecast demand, price quotes and sell sell-through reporting back to manufacturers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 category, pricing and supplier analytics staff at the largest distributor (estimate)
**Output:** Category strategy, quote pricing, demand forecasts, manufacturer sell-through and market-share reports
**Proprietary data:** Purchase histories across tens of thousands of dealer and integrator accounts, an early read on product transitions such as communicator swaps and cloud access control
**Clock:** Quote turnaround set by customers' project bids; manufacturer programme years
**Buyer:** VP of category management or chief analytics officer
