# Fire Marshal Third-Party ITM Reporting Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/security-alarm-integrators|Security Alarm Integrators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Platforms through which fire departments require contractors to file inspection, testing and maintenance reports for fire alarm, sprinkler, backflow and other systems. Brycer's The Compliance Engine is the leading example: free to the jurisdiction, funded by per-report fees paid by contractors, and used by departments such as Raleigh (since 2015), Gastonia and Charleston. Brycer also offers report review on the AHJ's behalf.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Fewer than 10 analysts; report reviewers and support staff are production (estimate)
**Output:** Compliance dashboards for fire marshals, deficiency notices, report-review findings
**Proprietary data:** Submitted ITM reports across many jurisdictions, held for the AHJs
**Clock:** NFPA 72 and local inspection frequencies; AHJ correction deadlines
**Buyer:** Company CEO; on the client side, the fire marshal
