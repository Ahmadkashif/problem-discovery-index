# VMS Analytics Turned Toward the Monitoring Queue

**Niche:** [[niches/security-alarm-integrators/video-verified-remote-guarding/profile|Video-Verified Monitoring & Remote Guarding]]
**Industry:** [[industries/security-alarm-integrators|Security Alarm Integrators]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Enterprise video platforms already run strong detection analytics for on-site security teams; a remote monitoring centre needs them shaped around its queue, its operators and its dispatch rules.
**Tags:** #object-detection #cnns #transfer-learning #evaluation-metrics #workflow-orchestration #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to clear each camera event (real intruder or not) within seconds at a monthly cost per camera below what a guard costs, and whoever delivers the most police-priority verified dispatches per operator hour takes the account.

## The Problem
Remote-guarding providers connect to whatever VMS and cameras each client already has. Analytics configured for an on-site guard's console raise events in a different form for each client, with no common priority or verification evidence.

## What Already Exists
Avigilon, Genetec, Milestone with analytics partners, Verkada and camera-embedded analytics offer person and vehicle detection, line crossing, loitering detection and forensic search for enterprise security operations centres.

## The Customization Gap
The monitoring centre needs normalised events across brands in one queue. It needs priority set by site schedule and recent incidents, and evidence packets (clip, still, track) shaped for the PSAP and for AVS-01 scoring. It needs talk-down audio integration, per-site arming schedules the station controls, and billing per camera per month. And it needs operator workload measures that the enterprise products, built for owners watching their own cameras, don't carry.

## Target Customer
Central stations adding video and remote-guarding providers connecting heterogeneous client systems.

## Impact If Solved
A station can take on any client's cameras without re-engineering, and its operators see a single, prioritised queue.
