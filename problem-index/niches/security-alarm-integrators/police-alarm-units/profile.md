# Police Alarm Units & Verified-Response Policy Offices

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/security-alarm-integrators|Security Alarm Integrators]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
Police departments' alarm coordinators and the 911 centres that receive alarm dispatch requests set and enforce response policy: permits, false-alarm limits, suspension and, in cities such as Seattle (since October 2024), verified response. Seattle's 911 centre fields about 13,000 burglary alarm calls a year, under 4% tied to a confirmed crime in 2023.

## Insight Function
**Size:** One to a handful of staff per department (estimate)
**Output:** Ordinances and policy recommendations, false-alarm statistics, permit and suspension decisions
**Proprietary data:** Dispatch and clearance records for alarm calls
**Clock:** Council and budget cycles; ordinance effective dates
**Buyer:** Police chief or alarm unit supervisor
