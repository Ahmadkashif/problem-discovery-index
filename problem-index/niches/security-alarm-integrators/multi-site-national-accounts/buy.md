# Facilities Work-Order Networks Adapted to Security Programmes

**Niche:** [[niches/security-alarm-integrators/multi-site-national-accounts/profile|Multi-Site National Account Service Programmes]]
**Industry:** [[industries/security-alarm-integrators|Security Alarm Integrators]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Retail facilities teams already dispatch and verify thousands of subcontractor jobs through work-order networks; security service needs the same network with device evidence attached.
**Tags:** #feature-engineering #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #evaluation-metrics #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep hundreds of a retailer's, bank's or restaurant chain's sites with working alarms, cameras and access points at the service level the client's loss-prevention team audits, and whoever proves the most sites healthy at the lowest service cost per site takes the account.

## The Problem
National integrators cover thin markets with local subcontractors. Dispatch, check-in and completion run through phone calls and the integrator's ticketing, and proof of work is a technician's note and an invoice.

## What Already Exists
Facilities work-order networks such as ServiceChannel and Corrigo dispatch, track and pay subcontractors for multi-site retailers, with check-in, SLA tracking and invoice validation.

## The Customization Gap
Security work needs completion verified by device state, not by check-in: the camera back online and recording, the panel test signal received at the central station, the reader reporting to the controller. Subcontractors must carry the credentials and licences each state requires for alarm work, and dispatch needs the life-safety priority of a down fire or egress device. Work also has to be linked to the alarm account and monitoring records the network doesn't hold.

## Target Customer
National integrators running subcontractor networks, and chains that already use a work-order network for facilities and want security on it.

## Impact If Solved
Subcontracted security service is dispatched, verified and paid on evidence, which is the gap between a programme the client trusts and one it audits.
