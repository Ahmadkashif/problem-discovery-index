# Confirmed-Fix Diagnostic Knowledge Databases

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-repair-shops|Auto Repair Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Identifix Direct-Hit, Mitchell 1 SureTrack, Snap-on SureTrack, and Bosch ESI sell what OEM documentation does not contain: what actually fixed this symptom on this vehicle. Master technicians staff diagnostic hotlines, take calls from shops that are stuck, work the problem, and record the confirmed root cause. Millions of those resolutions become a searchable database that shops subscribe to. The product is pure captured tacit knowledge.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-400 master technicians, hotline specialists, and content analysts
**Output:** The confirmed-fix database and hotline resolution — the subscription itself
**Proprietary data:** Millions of symptom-to-confirmed-root-cause records plus decades of hotline call transcripts, which capture diagnostic reasoning that exists in no manual anywhere
**Clock:** None hard — subscribers call when they are stuck
**Buyer:** VP of Content / Director of Diagnostic Services
