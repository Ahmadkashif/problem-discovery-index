# Vehicle Service Contract Claims Adjudication

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-repair-shops|Auto Repair Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The claims operations at vehicle service contract administrators decide, in real time while a car is on a lift, whether a failure is covered, whether the repair is necessary, and what will be paid. Adjudicators — many of them former technicians — interrogate the shop's diagnosis, apply contract language, and authorize or deny.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-600 claims adjudicators, technical reviewers, and analysts
**Output:** Coverage determinations and authorized repair amounts
**Proprietary data:** Failure and claim history by component, vehicle, mileage, and repairing shop, joined to denial and appeal outcomes
**Clock:** The vehicle is on the lift; authorization is measured in minutes
**Buyer:** VP of Claims / Chief Claims Officer
