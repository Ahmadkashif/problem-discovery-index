# Diagnostic Tool Coverage Engineering

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-repair-shops|Auto Repair Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The vehicle coverage groups at Snap-on, Autel, Launch, and Bosch. Engineers acquire vehicles, reverse-engineer manufacturer communication protocols and module behaviour, and build the diagnostic coverage — code definitions, live data, bidirectional controls, calibration routines — that makes a scan tool useful. Coverage breadth is the entire basis of competition in the segment.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-400 coverage engineers, technicians, and validation staff
**Output:** Diagnostic coverage releases and the subscription updates that carry them
**Proprietary data:** Reverse-engineered protocol and module behaviour knowledge across every make, accumulated vehicle by vehicle over decades
**Clock:** Model year introductions and quarterly coverage release commitments
**Buyer:** VP of Engineering / Director of Vehicle Coverage
