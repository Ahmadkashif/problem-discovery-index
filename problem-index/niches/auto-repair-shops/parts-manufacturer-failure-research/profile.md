# Aftermarket Parts Failure Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-repair-shops|Auto Repair Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The engineering groups at Dorman, Standard Motor Products, Denso, and Bosch aftermarket research why OEM components fail, reverse-engineer replacements, and design out the original weakness. Warranty returns are torn down and analyzed, and the findings drive both product design and the technical bulletins shops rely on.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-150 design engineers, failure analysts, and technical service staff
**Output:** Product designs, failure mode analyses, technical service bulletins
**Proprietary data:** Teardown findings across decades of warranty returns, plus technical service call history describing failures as technicians encounter them
**Clock:** Product launch calendars, largely internal
**Buyer:** VP of Engineering / Director of Product Development
