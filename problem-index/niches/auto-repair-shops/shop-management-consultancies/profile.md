# Shop Management Coaching & 20-Groups

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-repair-shops|Auto Repair Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory

## What They Do
Automotive Training Institute, Elite Worldwide, Transformers Institute, and similar firms run coaching programs and peer benchmark groups for independent shop owners, publishing financial benchmarks drawn from member shops' own numbers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 coaches, facilitators, and analysts
**Output:** Benchmark reports, coaching programs, peer group analyses
**Proprietary data:** Financial and operational data contributed by member shops
**Clock:** Peer group meeting cycles, self-set
**Buyer:** President / Director of Coaching
