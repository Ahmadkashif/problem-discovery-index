# Right to Repair Policy Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-repair-shops|Auto Repair Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The CAR Coalition, the Auto Care Association's advocacy arm, and allied groups research manufacturer data access practices, document where independent shops are locked out of telematics and diagnostic information, and produce the analysis behind state and federal right-to-repair legislation.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 3-10
**Output:** Policy analyses, legislative testimony, data access studies
**Proprietary data:** Documented access-denial cases collected from member shops
**Clock:** Legislative sessions and regulatory comment periods
**Buyer:** Executive Director
