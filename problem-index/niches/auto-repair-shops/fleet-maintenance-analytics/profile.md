# Fleet Maintenance Management Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-repair-shops|Auto Repair Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Element Fleet, Holman, Wheels, and ARI manage maintenance for millions of leased and fleet vehicles. Their staff authorize every repair before it happens, negotiate labor and parts rates with independent shops, and publish benchmark analytics on maintenance cost per mile by vehicle, component, and geography back to fleet clients.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-200 analysts, maintenance authorizers, and data staff
**Output:** Maintenance authorization decisions, cost benchmark reports, lifecycle and replacement analyses
**Proprietary data:** Line-item repair history across millions of vehicles by make, model, mileage, and component — the most complete mechanical reliability dataset in existence, and one no manufacturer holds across brands
**Clock:** Client reporting cycles and lease replacement decision windows
**Buyer:** VP of Fleet Analytics / Director of Maintenance Services
