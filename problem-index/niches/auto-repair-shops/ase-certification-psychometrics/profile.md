# Technician Certification & Psychometrics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-repair-shops|Auto Repair Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The National Institute for Automotive Service Excellence defines and tests technician competence across the trade. Staff and subject matter expert panels run job task analyses, write and calibrate exam items, set cut scores, and maintain the certification framework that shops advertise and insurers and fleets require.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 30-80 test development staff, psychometricians, and subject matter coordinators
**Output:** Certification examinations, job task analyses, technical reports on item and cohort performance
**Proprietary data:** Decades of item response data and technician performance across hundreds of thousands of candidates and every specialty area
**Clock:** Test administration windows and five-year recertification cycles
**Buyer:** VP of Test Development / Chief Credentialing Officer
