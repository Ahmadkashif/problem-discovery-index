# OEM Warranty Analytics Groups

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-repair-shops|Auto Repair Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Manufacturer warranty groups analyze claims from franchise dealers to detect emerging failure patterns, reserve for warranty liability, decide on service campaigns and recalls, and feed findings back to engineering. Independent shops sit outside this loop entirely, which is part of why the same failures get re-diagnosed thousands of times in the aftermarket.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 warranty analysts, reliability engineers, and statisticians
**Output:** Emerging issue detection, warranty reserve analyses, campaign and recall recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Complete warranty claim and parts return history for the brand, joined to build records and engineering change history
**Clock:** Financial reporting and reserving cycles, and NHTSA defect reporting obligations
**Buyer:** Director of Warranty Analytics / VP Quality
