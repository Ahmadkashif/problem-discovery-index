# Repair Chain Consolidator Analytics

## What They Do
The corporate development and operations analytics groups at Driven Brands, Mavis, Monro, Sun Auto, and Christian Brothers. They screen acquisitions in a highly fragmented market, model site-level economics, and benchmark technician productivity and ticket composition across hundreds or thousands of shops.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 analysts across corp dev, pricing, and operations
**Output:** Acquisition models, site selection, network performance analytics
**Proprietary data:** Ticket-level repair and productivity data across a large multi-brand shop network
**Clock:** Deal timelines and lender diligence windows
**Buyer:** SVP Corporate Development / VP Business Analytics
