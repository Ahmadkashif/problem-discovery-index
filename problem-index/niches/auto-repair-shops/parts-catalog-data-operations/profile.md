# Aftermarket Parts Catalog Data Operations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-repair-shops|Auto Repair Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Epicor, MOTOR, and the Auto Care Association's data programs maintain the fitment data that answers whether a given part fits a given vehicle. Analysts build and maintain application records across millions of part-to-vehicle combinations under the ACES and PIES standards, and every parts lookup in every shop resolves against that work.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 100-500 catalog analysts, data researchers, and standards staff
**Output:** Fitment and product attribute data — the licensed catalog itself
**Proprietary data:** The accumulated application record, plus returns and mis-fit history showing where fitment data is actually wrong in the field
**Clock:** Model year introductions and supplier product launches
**Buyer:** VP of Data / Director of Catalog Operations
