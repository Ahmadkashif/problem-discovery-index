# Telematics Vehicle Health Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/auto-repair-shops|Auto Repair Shops]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Geotab, Samsara, and the connected-vehicle data platforms stream diagnostic trouble codes, sensor readings, and driving behaviour from millions of vehicles and turn it into predictive maintenance and fleet health products. Repair shops receive the downstream effect: work orders arriving with a fault already identified.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 50-300 data scientists, vehicle engineers, and analysts
**Output:** Predictive maintenance alerts, vehicle health scoring, fleet benchmark analytics
**Proprietary data:** Continuous sensor and fault-code telemetry across millions of vehicles — enormous, and rarely joined to what the repair actually turned out to be
**Clock:** Continuous; no external deadline governs analysis
**Buyer:** VP of Data Science / Head of Vehicle Intelligence
