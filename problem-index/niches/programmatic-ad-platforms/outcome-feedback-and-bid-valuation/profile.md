# Outcome Feedback & Bid Valuation

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/programmatic-ad-platforms|Programmatic Ad Platforms]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to join the sale back to the impression that caused it and price the bid against that rather than against a click — and whoever closes that loop changes what every impression in the market is worth.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$8B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~27% of vendor-layer revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — proxies everywhere, outcomes nowhere
**Target Buyer:** Platform data science leadership and advertiser measurement
**Automation Potential:** Very High — the modelling is the product

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
The bid is priced in ten milliseconds against a click, because the sale it was supposed to cause is observed by the advertiser a month later and never joined back to the impression. This is the category's structural defect and the largest concentration of contested value in it: an entire optimisation industry trained on a proxy it knows is weakly related to what is being bought. The contest is not auction speed or targeting breadth but the label itself — whoever can price against the outcome rather than the click is bidding on a different and better-informed basis than everyone else in the auction.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Click and view-through optimisation, pixel-based conversion tracking, mobile measurement partners, clean rooms, and post-campaign aggregate reporting. The gaps: outcomes that arrive aggregated past the point of joining; no delayed-label handling in bid models; no path for advertisers to return outcome data safely; attribution windows chosen by convention; and optimisation targets that nobody claims to believe in.
