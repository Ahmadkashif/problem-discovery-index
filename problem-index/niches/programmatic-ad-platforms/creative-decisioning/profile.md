# Creative Decisioning

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/programmatic-ad-platforms|Programmatic Ad Platforms]]
**Category:** Highly Automatable
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to build a system that knows why a creative works rather than which variant won — and whoever does that turns the largest untouched lever in the category into an optimisable one.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.5B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~8% of vendor-layer revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — a decade of swapping images
**Target Buyer:** Platform creative teams and advertiser brand leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — generation and understanding are both available

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Dynamic creative optimisation has existed for a decade and still mostly swaps a product image and a headline, because the systems that assemble the ad know nothing about why a creative works. Creative is widely accepted as the largest driver of advertising effectiveness and is the one part of the stack that receives almost no algorithmic attention: the bidder optimises targeting and price against a creative treated as an opaque asset. The contest is over representing the creative itself — what is in it, what it says, how it is constructed — so that the system can reason about it rather than merely permute it.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Dynamic creative optimisation with template slots, A/B and multivariate variant testing, feed-driven product insertion, and creative performance reports by variant identifier. The gaps: no representation of creative content; no transferable learning across campaigns or advertisers; variant testing that needs volume most campaigns lack; no generation beyond slot filling; and no connection between creative attributes and outcomes.
