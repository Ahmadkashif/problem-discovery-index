# The Media Trader

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/programmatic-ad-platforms|Programmatic Ad Platforms]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to take the slider-dragging off the trader so they can do the judgement work they were hired for — and whoever does that changes what a trading desk is worth.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$2.2B US
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~7% of vendor-layer revenue
**Digital Adoption:** Low — manual work in an automated market
**Target Buyer:** Agency and in-house trading leadership
**Automation Potential:** Very High — pacing and delivery are mechanical

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Media traders spend their days dragging budget sliders between line items to hit a delivery number, and the work they were hired for — deciding what the campaign should actually do — happens in whatever time is left. In a category that automates millions of decisions a second, the humans operating it perform a manual optimisation loop that is entirely mechanical and could have been automated a decade ago. This is a distinct niche because the contest is over the trader's working surface, which is separate from what the bidder optimises and separate from what operations reconciles.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Platform campaign managers, pacing reports, spreadsheet trackers, alerting on underdelivery, and manual budget shifting across line items. The gaps: pacing that requires human intervention daily; no cross-platform view; no automation of the repetitive reallocation; alerts that fire after the problem; no support for the strategic decisions; and no record of what a trader's interventions achieved.
