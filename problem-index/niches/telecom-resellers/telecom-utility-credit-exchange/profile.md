# Telecom & Utility Credit Data Exchange

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/telecom-resellers|Telecom Resellers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
The National Consumer Telecom & Utilities Exchange (NCTUE) is a member-owned consumer reporting agency. Telecom, pay-TV, utility and security companies contribute customers' account and payment histories, and members draw reports and scores when deciding whether to require a deposit, offer postpaid service or finance a device. It claims 160+ member contributors and 467M+ active accounts, and Equifax runs the database under contract.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-30 analytics and data staff supporting the exchange (estimate)
**Output:** Consumer reports and risk scores for telecom and utility credit decisions
**Proprietary data:** Pooled payment and account histories contributed by members, including accounts that never appear in mainstream credit files
**Clock:** Point-of-sale credit decisions; no third-party deadline on the exchange's own output
**Buyer:** Head of analytics for the exchange at its database manager
