# Telecom Expense Management & Invoice Audit Firms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/telecom-resellers|Telecom Resellers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Firms that sit on the buyer's side of telecom resale. Telecom expense management vendors such as Tangoe and Calero, and contingency-fee invoice auditors, audit enterprises' carrier and reseller invoices against contracts, find billing errors and unused lines, negotiate credits, and benchmark rates at renewal. Enterprises pay a subscription per line or invoice, or a share of recovered savings.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-100 audit, contract and sourcing analysts per firm (estimate); invoice-processing staff are production and not counted
**Output:** Audit findings and recovered credits, rate benchmarks, inventory clean-up and renewal recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Invoices, contracts and line inventories across many enterprise clients and hundreds of carriers and resellers
**Clock:** Billing-dispute windows in carrier contracts and clients' renewal dates, which are client and contract schedules rather than a regulator's
**Buyer:** Head of audit or sourcing services at the TEM firm
