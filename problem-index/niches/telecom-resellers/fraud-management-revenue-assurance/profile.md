# Telecom Fraud Management & Revenue Assurance Vendors

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/telecom-resellers|Telecom Resellers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Vendors such as Subex and Mobileum, and smaller managed-service specialists, sell fraud detection and revenue assurance to operators and resellers. They detect international revenue share fraud, PBX hacking, Wangiri and subscription fraud, and leakage between network usage and billing. Many run it as a managed service in which their analysts review alerts on the operator's call records. The CFCA put global telecom fraud losses at $38.95B in 2023.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~30-150 fraud and revenue assurance analysts per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Fraud alerts and case reports, leakage findings, high-risk number-range intelligence
**Proprietary data:** Cross-operator fraud number lists and test-call results the vendor collects itself, but mostly the operators' own call detail and billing records processed under contract
**Clock:** Wholesale settlement and dispute windows between carriers; fraud runs over hours and weekends
**Buyer:** Head of fraud managed services at the vendor

**Kill switches:** live — data isn't theirs to use. The core analysis runs on operators' call records, which are customer proprietary network information held under contract and can't be pooled or reused across clients beyond the narrow fraud lists the contracts allow.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. Analyst work scales with traffic and the alerts are the product, but the corpus belongs to the operators.
