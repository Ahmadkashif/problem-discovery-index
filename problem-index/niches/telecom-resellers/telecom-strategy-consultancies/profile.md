# Telecom Strategy & Due-Diligence Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/telecom-resellers|Telecom Resellers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation firms

## What They Do
Strategy consultancies that specialise in telecom and media, such as Altman Solon, Analysys Mason and Cartesian. They advise MVNOs on host negotiations and business plans, help cable operators design mobile offers, and run commercial due diligence for investors buying UCaaS, VoIP and MVNO businesses. Operators and private equity firms pay per project.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-100 telecom consultants per firm in the US (estimate)
**Output:** Market and business-plan studies, wholesale negotiation support, commercial due diligence reports
**Proprietary data:** Benchmarks built from past engagements: wholesale terms, churn and acquisition costs, deal multiples, held under client confidentiality
**Clock:** Clients' deal timetables and negotiation dates, which are client schedules rather than third-party deadlines
**Buyer:** Partner leading the telecom practice
