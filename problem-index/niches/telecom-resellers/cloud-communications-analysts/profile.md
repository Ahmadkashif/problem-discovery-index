# UCaaS & Cloud Communications Industry Analysts

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/telecom-resellers|Telecom Resellers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Analyst and market-research firms such as Synergy Research, Omdia, Metrigy and Frost & Sullivan, along with the unified-communications teams at the large advisory houses, size the UCaaS, CCaaS and CPaaS markets, track vendor share, rank providers and survey buyers. UCaaS providers buy the data for strategy and marketing, investors buy it for diligence, and enterprises buy rankings to shortlist providers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-30 analysts covering enterprise communications per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Market-share trackers, forecasts, vendor rankings, buyer surveys and advisory calls
**Proprietary data:** Survey panels and vendor-briefing history; share estimates built largely from vendor disclosures and briefings
**Clock:** Vendors' quarterly earnings dates, after which share trackers are updated, plus the firms' own publication calendars
**Buyer:** Research director for enterprise communications at the analyst firm
