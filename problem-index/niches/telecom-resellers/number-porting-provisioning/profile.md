# Number Porting & Provisioning Desk

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/telecom-resellers|Telecom Resellers]]
**Category:** Low Digitized
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche (wholesale and CPaaS porting APIs, porting-automation tools and outsourced porting desks) is fighting to get a port order to a firm order commitment on its first submission, and whoever matches the losing carrier's records most reliably before sending takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$600M a year in porting and number-provisioning labour, tooling and expedite fees across resellers (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~1% of a ~$60B resale economy (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low — letters of authorisation as signed PDFs, CSRs requested by email, each losing carrier's portal or fax, rejection codes copied into tickets
**Target Buyer:** Provisioning or Porting Manager at a UCaaS, hosted-voice or MVNO provider
**Automation Potential:** High for validation and tracking; low for disputes with uncooperative carriers

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Every reseller's growth runs through porting: a customer won is a set of numbers to bring over from a carrier that is losing the account and has no reason to hurry. Wireline and VoIP ports are judged against the losing carrier's customer service record, so the work is matching documents to someone else's database, field by field. A trade write-up puts simple ports at two to three weeks and complex ones at six to eight, and says rejections themselves often take two to three weeks to arrive. The porting desk serves every segment (business voice, toll-free, MVNO port-ins) and its counterpart is always another carrier, which sets it apart from the onboarding work around it. Two neighbours handle other ends of the same transaction. The carriers' side, approving port-outs and SIM changes while stopping takeovers, is [[niches/wireless-carriers/porting-sim-account-security/profile|Number Porting, SIM Changes & Account Takeover Defence]], and mobile port-ins done at a dealer's counter are [[niches/wireless-retail-agents/activation-and-port-in-processing/profile|Activation & Port-In Processing]]. This niche is the gaining reseller's back-office desk, mostly wireline, VoIP and toll-free ports judged against a losing carrier's CSR.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Losing carriers' portals and email queues, wholesale and CPaaS porting APIs, the number portability administration center's status, and ticketing systems. The gaps: no pre-submission check against the CSR; carrier-specific rules held in specialists' notes; rejections returned as codes with little explanation; and no record of which losing carriers reject what, and how long they take.
