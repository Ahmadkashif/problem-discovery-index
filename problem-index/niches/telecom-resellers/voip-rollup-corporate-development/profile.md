# VoIP & UCaaS Rollup Corporate Development

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/telecom-resellers|Telecom Resellers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Private-equity-backed and listed consolidators that buy small hosted-voice, UCaaS and reseller businesses and migrate their customers onto one platform. Their corporate development teams screen targets, model customer bases and churn, and run integration.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~2-8 corporate development and integration analysts per platform (estimate)
**Output:** Acquisition models and integration plans for the platform's own use
**Proprietary data:** Acquired companies' customer, billing and churn histories
**Clock:** Deal timetables set by the platform and sellers
**Buyer:** Head of corporate development at the platform
