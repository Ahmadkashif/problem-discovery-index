# Independent Digital MVNOs

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/telecom-resellers|Telecom Resellers]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche (MVNE retention modules, lifecycle-messaging platforms and churn-analytics vendors) is fighting to tell, within a new subscriber's first billing cycles, who will port out before repaying their acquisition cost, and whoever predicts and keeps the most of those subscribers takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$7B a year in service revenue at independent and app-first MVNOs (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~12% of a ~$60B resale economy (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High — sign-up, care and payment are app-based; churn prediction is rare
**Target Buyer:** Founder, Head of Growth or Head of Retention at an independent MVNO
**Automation Potential:** High

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
An independent MVNO buys capacity from a host and sells it online with no stores and often no contracts. It has no network of its own to offload traffic onto, so its cost per line is roughly fixed by the wholesale contract. Its margin is made or lost on acquisition: paid social, referral credits and switching promotions bring a subscriber in, and eSIM makes leaving as easy as joining. A subscriber who ports out in month two has cost money. One who stays two years is the business. Several brands that started independent have been bought by the hosts, so the remaining independents compete against well-funded flanker brands on the same networks.

## Current Tools & Gaps
App analytics and lifecycle messaging platforms, the MVNE's BSS, payment processors and app-store reviews. The gaps: churn models built on app engagement rather than network experience; the clearest exit signal, a transfer-PIN request, handled by self-service with no retention touch; and no join between acquisition channel and early port-out.
