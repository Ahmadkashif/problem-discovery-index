# Wi-Fi Fleet Analytics Pointed at Wholesale Cost

**Niche:** [[niches/telecom-resellers/cable-and-converged-mvnos/profile|Cable & Converged MVNOs]]
**Industry:** [[industries/telecom-resellers|Telecom Resellers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Operators already run analytics over millions of home gateways to cut broadband support calls, and none of it is joined to what each household's phones cost on the host network.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #k-means-clustering #descriptive-statistics #data-integration #revenue-impact #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche (home Wi-Fi and gateway analytics vendors, offload and connection-management suppliers, and the operators' own network teams) is fighting to carry each subscriber's traffic on the operator's own Wi-Fi and small cells instead of the host's macro network without the subscriber noticing the handoff, and whoever cuts the most wholesale gigabytes per line takes the account.

## The Problem
The mobile team knows wholesale gigabytes per line. The broadband team knows each gateway's Wi-Fi health, interference, band steering and dead zones. Nobody connects a household's weak bedroom Wi-Fi to its phones' cellular use in the evening, so the cheapest offload fix, a mesh extender or a channel change, is never targeted at the households where it would save the most.

## What Already Exists
Gateway and home Wi-Fi management platforms, built for broadband operators to diagnose in-home Wi-Fi, reduce support calls and avoid truck rolls, with per-household quality scores.

## The Customization Gap
Add mobile lines to the household model: wholesale usage by line and hour, device connection events from the operator's app, and the hours phones spent on cellular while at home. Score each household on avoidable wholesale cost rather than broadband quality alone. Rank interventions (extender, channel plan, gateway swap) by mobile savings plus broadband retention, and track the result against a control group.

## Target Customer
Mobile finance and network teams at cable operators that already license a home Wi-Fi platform for their broadband business.

## Impact If Solved
An existing broadband tool starts paying back in mobile margin, and offload improvements are targeted at the households where cellular use is avoidable instead of spread evenly.
