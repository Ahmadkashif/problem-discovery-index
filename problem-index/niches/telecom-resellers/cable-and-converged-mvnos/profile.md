# Cable & Converged MVNOs

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/telecom-resellers|Telecom Resellers]]
**Category:** Contested Sub-Niche
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this sub-niche (home Wi-Fi and gateway analytics vendors, offload and connection-management suppliers, and the operators' own network teams) is fighting to carry each subscriber's traffic on the operator's own Wi-Fi and small cells instead of the host's macro network without the subscriber noticing the handoff, and whoever cuts the most wholesale gigabytes per line takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$8B a year in mobile service revenue at cable and converged MVNOs (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~13% of a ~$60B resale economy (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** High — telemetry exists on gateways and devices, but it is rarely joined to the wholesale bill
**Target Buyer:** Head of Mobile Network Strategy at a cable operator
**Automation Potential:** High for steering and attribution; low for small-cell deployment

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A cable operator's MVNO resells a host network's capacity (Spectrum Mobile rides Verizon) but owns millions of home gateways and public hotspots, and in some markets its own small cells. Every gigabyte a phone carries on that infrastructure is a gigabyte not paid for at wholesale. The mobile line is sold inside a broadband bundle, so its margin depends less on price than on how much traffic stays off the host network. Spectrum Mobile reached 11.8M lines at end-2025, adding 428,000 in Q4. The parent operators' broadband business sits in [[industries/wired-broadband-providers|Wired Broadband Providers]]. This sub-niche is their resale of someone else's mobile network.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Gateway management and Wi-Fi analytics, device connection-management settings, the host's usage files, and the operator's own BSS. The gaps: no per-subscriber measure of how much traffic should have been offloaded but wasn't; phones that stay on cellular when home Wi-Fi is weak; and no joined view of gateway quality, device behaviour and wholesale cost.
