# MVNO Enablers' Subscriber Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/telecom-resellers|Telecom Resellers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Mobile virtual network enablers and aggregators run activation, charging, billing, Lifeline handling and host-network integration for many MVNO brands at once. Telgoo5 is one example. They see subscriber and usage data across their brands and offer reporting as part of the platform.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~3-10 analysts per enabler; most staff run platform operations and integrations (estimate)
**Output:** Platform reporting and dashboards bundled with the BSS service
**Proprietary data:** Subscriber, usage and billing records belonging to their MVNO clients
**Clock:** Monthly billing cycles and host settlement dates
**Buyer:** Head of product at the enabler
