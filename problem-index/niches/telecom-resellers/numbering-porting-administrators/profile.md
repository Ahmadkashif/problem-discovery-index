# Numbering & Porting Administrators

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/telecom-resellers|Telecom Resellers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The contractors that run the US numbering databases under FCC oversight. iconectiv operates the number portability administration center, which records every ported number's current carrier, and is the STIR/SHAKEN policy administrator that issues SPC tokens. Somos administers toll-free numbers and the Reassigned Numbers Database. Both also sell number-intelligence and fraud products built near those registries.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-40 data and analytics staff per administrator beyond registry operations (estimate)
**Output:** Registry operations and data access under FCC rules; number-intelligence and anti-fraud data products
**Proprietary data:** The authoritative record of number assignment, porting and reassignment for the whole country
**Clock:** Porting intervals and registry service levels set by FCC rules and contracts
**Buyer:** Head of data products at the administrator
