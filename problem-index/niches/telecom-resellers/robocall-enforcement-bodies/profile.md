# Robocall Enforcement Bodies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/telecom-resellers|Telecom Resellers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The FCC's Enforcement Bureau (with the Wireline Competition Bureau on RMD matters) and state attorneys general's robocall units investigate providers in illegal call paths. They issue show-cause and removal orders and bring cases. They removed more than 1,200 providers from the RMD in August 2025 and 14 more in September 2026, and found SK Teleco in breach of the 24-hour traceback rule in April 2026.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically under 10 analysts and investigators dedicated to robocall matters per body (estimate); the staff exist only when summed across the FCC and many state offices, which isn't one function
**Output:** Investigations, removal and show-cause orders, enforcement actions and settlements
**Proprietary data:** Traceback results, subpoenaed call records and provider filings
**Clock:** Statutes and FCC procedural rules; enforcement priorities
**Buyer:** Chief of the Enforcement Bureau or head of a state robocall unit
