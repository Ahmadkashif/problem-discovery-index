# Telecom Regulatory Counsel & TCPA Call-Record Experts

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/telecom-resellers|Telecom Resellers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation firms

## What They Do
Telecom regulatory law practices that defend resellers and VoIP providers in FCC enforcement, RMD removal and traceback proceedings, and the expert witnesses who analyse call detail records in TCPA class actions and robocall cases: who was called, from which numbers, whether the lines were wireless or reassigned, and who sat in the call path. Providers, callers and insurers pay through counsel.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-40 attorneys and analysts per larger telecom practice or expert firm (estimate)
**Output:** Responses to FCC orders and show-cause notices, expert reports and testimony on call records, compliance opinions
**Proprietary data:** Prior case files and call-record analyses, held under privilege and protective orders
**Clock:** Hard third-party dates: FCC response deadlines in removal and show-cause orders, and court schedules for expert disclosure and trial
**Buyer:** Practice leader at the law firm or expert firm
