# USAC Contribution Audits & Lifeline Program Integrity

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/telecom-resellers|Telecom Resellers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Regulatory & enforcement bodies

## What They Do
The Universal Service Administrative Company administers the Universal Service Fund for the FCC. It collects contributions on providers' Form 499 revenue, sets the projected contribution base each quarter, audits contributors and Lifeline providers, and runs the National Verifier and the duplicate-subscriber database that every Lifeline reseller must pass through. Its work is funded from the fund and directed by the FCC. This pocket covers USAC's contribution side (Form 499 collection, contributor audits) and Lifeline (the National Verifier, the duplicate-subscriber database, Lifeline audits). USAC's high-cost programme (location uploads and deployment verification) sits with the state broadband offices and NTIA in [[niches/wired-broadband-providers/broadband-program-administrators/profile|Broadband Programme Administrators]] (37).

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~30-100 auditors, program-integrity analysts and data staff across contribution and Lifeline functions (estimate)
**Output:** Quarterly contribution-base projections (the proposed Q4 2026 factor rests on $6.910B of projected contribution-base revenue), audit findings and recoveries, eligibility decisions, program data releases
**Proprietary data:** Every contributor's Form 499 filings and every Lifeline subscriber's enrolment and recertification record
**Clock:** Statutory and FCC-set: quarterly filings to the FCC ahead of each contribution factor, 499 due dates, audit cycles
**Buyer:** Head of audit and program integrity at USAC
