# Telecom Regulatory Compliance & USF Filing Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/telecom-resellers|Telecom Resellers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation firms

## What They Do
Compliance firms and telecom-focused accounting practices that prepare small providers' recurring federal and state obligations on retainer: Form 499-Q and 499-A, CPNI certifications, Robocall Mitigation Database filings and recertifications, state registrations, 911 fee and communications tax returns, and the classification of revenue behind them. Resellers and VoIP providers too small for a regulatory department pay per filing or per year.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~10-30 regulatory analysts per larger firm who interpret rules and classify client revenue (estimate); the preparers who key returns are production staff and not counted
**Output:** Filed returns and certifications, revenue classifications, compliance calendars and regulatory alerts
**Proprietary data:** Each client's revenue, product and filing history, held per client under confidentiality; the firm's own tracker of state-by-state obligations
**Clock:** Hard federal dates: Form 499-A each April 1, 499-Q on February 1, May 1, August 1 and November 1, CPNI and RMD recertification each March 1, and RMD updates within 10 business days of a change
**Buyer:** Managing partner or practice leader at the compliance firm
