# Wholesale Network Pricing Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/telecom-resellers|Telecom Resellers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Research firms that publish what wholesale capacity costs, the input every reseller buys. TeleGeography is the clearest example. It sells a Network Pricing Database by module (IP transit, wavelengths, local access, dedicated internet access, SD-WAN, MPLS) and a WAN Cost Benchmark built on more than 2 million price points sourced anonymously from buyers and sellers. Carriers, resellers, enterprises and investors subscribe.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~20-50 analysts at the leading firm, whose work grows with every route, product and market added to the database (estimate)
**Output:** Price databases and benchmarks, market reports, network maps and consulting studies
**Proprietary data:** Years of contributed and researched price quotes by route, product and speed
**Clock:** Buyers' contract-renewal and RFP cycles, when carriers and enterprises price circuits against the benchmark; a business-calendar clock rather than a third-party deadline
**Buyer:** Head of research at the pricing firm
