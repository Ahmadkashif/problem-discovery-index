# Industry Traceback Group

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/telecom-resellers|Telecom Resellers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
The FCC-designated traceback consortium, run by the USTelecom trade association, traces suspected illegal robocalls hop by hop to their source through a secure portal that providers must answer within 24 hours. It ran 3,606 tracebacks in 2024, named 714 providers in call paths and counted 77 as non-responsive, and passes results to the FCC and state attorneys general.

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~5-10 staff running tracebacks through a largely automated portal (estimate)
**Output:** Traceback results and referrals; an annual report to the FCC
**Proprietary data:** The traceback record of which providers carried which campaigns
**Clock:** The 24-hour response rule falls on the providers it queries, not on the group
**Buyer:** Executive leading the group at the association
