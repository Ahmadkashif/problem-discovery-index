# International-Calling & Cash-Paying Prepaid Subscribers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/telecom-resellers|Telecom Resellers]]
**Category:** Underserved Audience
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to be the brand the corner-store clerk recommends and can refill in seconds for cash, with calls to the subscriber's home country that keep working, and whoever wins the retailer's counter wins the subscriber.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$3B a year in prepaid MVNO service and international calling sold to immigrant and cash-paying households (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~5% of a ~$60B resale economy (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Low-Medium — refills are electronic at the till, but the relationship runs through independent shops, paper posters and word of mouth
**Target Buyer:** Head of Prepaid Distribution or Head of International at a prepaid MVNO
**Automation Potential:** Medium — retailer analytics and route monitoring automate; trust at the counter doesn't

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
A large group of subscribers buys mobile service in cash at bodegas, phone shops and grocery stores, refills monthly at the counter, and chooses a brand on its calling and data rates to one or two home countries. Many have no bank card or credit history. Many prefer support in Spanish, Haitian Creole, Bengali, Tagalog or Arabic, and they rely on the shop owner as adviser. The brands serving them are prepaid MVNOs and international-calling resellers that sell through networks of independent retailers and refill distributors. Mainstream tools assume card payments, English and app-first care. The dealers on the other side of the counter are [[industries/wireless-retail-agents|Wireless Retail Agents]]; the independent shops selling several prepaid brands at once are their [[niches/wireless-retail-agents/multi-carrier-prepaid-dealers/profile|Multi-Carrier Prepaid Dealers]] niche, which competes on the owner's commission economics. This niche is the reseller brand and its subscriber.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Prepaid BSS, electronic refill distribution networks, retailer commission programs, and wholesale international voice routes bought on least-cost routing. The gaps: no view of which retailers' subscribers stay; international route quality to specific destinations not monitored from the subscriber's side; and care in the subscriber's language rarely available outside business hours.
