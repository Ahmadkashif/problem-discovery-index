# Retail Execution Tools Pointed at the Refill Counter

**Niche:** [[niches/telecom-resellers/international-prepaid-subscribers/profile|International-Calling & Cash-Paying Prepaid Subscribers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/telecom-resellers|Telecom Resellers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Consumer-goods brands already manage field reps across thousands of independent stores with retail execution software, and prepaid brands still run their store visits from spreadsheets and distributor reports.
**Tags:** #k-means-clustering #descriptive-statistics #feature-engineering #data-integration #worker-facing #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to be the brand the corner-store clerk recommends and can refill in seconds for cash, with calls to the subscriber's home country that keep working, and whoever wins the retailer's counter wins the subscriber.

## The Problem
A prepaid brand's field reps visit shops to restock SIMs, put up posters with current international rates, train clerks and resolve commission disputes. Which shops to visit, what to bring and what to say is decided from distributor sales reports weeks old.

## What Already Exists
Retail execution platforms built for consumer-goods brands selling through independent stores, with visit planning, in-store audits with photos, order capture and rep performance tracking.

## The Customization Gap
Prepaid-specific objects: SIM inventory and activation counts per shop, refill volume and its retention value, commission and chargeback status, competitor rate boards photographed in-store, the languages and destination countries of each shop's customers, and fraud flags for suspicious activations. Visit plans need ranking by retention value, not sales volume, and promotional material needs to be generated in the right languages for each counter.

## Target Customer
Retail channel managers at prepaid MVNOs with field teams covering independent shops in immigrant neighbourhoods.

## Impact If Solved
Field reps spend their visits where the brand's long-term subscribers are won, and the counter always shows the brand's current rates to the right home countries.
