# Holding-Company Content Production Arms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commercial-photographers|Commercial Photographers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The production networks inside advertising holding groups, such as Hogarth (WPP), Publicis Production and Omnicom Production, along with independent content-production groups. They produce and adapt advertising and ecommerce imagery at scale for global brands, commissioning or employing photographers and running in-house studios and retouching hubs. Brands pay production fees under retainers.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-100 content analytics and asset-performance staff across the networks (estimate); thousands in production
**Output:** Produced and adapted assets; some content-performance reporting for clients
**Proprietary data:** Asset-level production and performance records, held for and owned by each client
**Clock:** Campaign launches and media flight dates set by clients
**Buyer:** Chief operating officer / head of data and analytics
