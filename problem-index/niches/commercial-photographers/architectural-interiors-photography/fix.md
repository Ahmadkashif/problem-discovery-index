# Images Passed to Parties Who Never Paid

**Niche:** [[niches/commercial-photographers/architectural-interiors-photography/profile|Architectural & Interiors Photography]]
**Industry:** [[industries/commercial-photographers|Commercial Photographers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The architect shares the shoot with the contractor "as a favour", the contractor posts it everywhere, and the photographer has to choose between invoicing a client's partner and letting it go.
**Tags:** #contrastive-learning #k-nearest-neighbors #compliance #revenue-impact #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to license one shoot to every party on a project (architect, contractor, interior designer, product makers) on terms each can see and none can stretch, and whoever makes the cost-share easy to join and hard to leak takes the account.

## The Problem
Project images move along the project team the same way drawings do: freely. Contractors, consultants and suppliers end up with full-resolution files and use them in proposals, websites and award entries. The photographer finds out by chance and faces an awkward choice, because the infringer is a partner of the client who pays the bills.

## Why It's Still Broken
Licences don't say clearly what the commissioning firm may share, files carry no rights information, and nobody tells the downstream parties that the images are licensed per party. Enforcement threatens the client relationship, so most photographers absorb the loss.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A licence clause and file metadata that state plainly who may use the images. A short, friendly notice sent with every delivery telling all project parties how to license. Periodic image matching against the websites of the project's credited firms, with a pre-priced licence offer rather than a demand letter.

## Who Feels the Pain
Architectural photographers losing a share of every project's value; commissioning firms caught between their photographer and their partners; downstream firms exposed to claims they didn't know they risked.

## Impact If Fixed
Unlicensed use converts into routine licence sales at cost-share prices, without anyone being accused of anything.
