# The Brief That Says "Make It Look Premium"

**Niche:** [[niches/commercial-photographers/small-brand-product-content/profile|Small Brands Commissioning Product Content]]
**Industry:** [[industries/commercial-photographers|Commercial Photographers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A small seller orders a shoot with three adjectives and a competitor's link, gets images that are technically right and commercially wrong, and blames the photographer.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #contrastive-learning #k-nearest-neighbors #quick-win #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to prove that a set of images lifts the listing's conversion on the seller's own marketplace data, and whoever can show the lift per dollar spent takes the seller.

## The Problem
Small sellers don't know the vocabulary of a shot list: angles, scale, props, backgrounds, which images the marketplace requires and which it allows. Their brief is "premium, clean, like this brand", and the studio interprets it as best it can. The result needs a reshoot or a round of AI edits, and the relationship sours.

## Why It's Still Broken
Productised studios compete on low prices, so there's no time for a briefing call, and order forms collect logistics, not intent. Nobody turns the seller's examples into a concrete shot specification before the samples arrive.

## What a Fix Looks Like
A briefing step at order time: the seller pastes reference listings and describes the product. Image retrieval finds similar, well-performing listings in the category, and a language model turns the references into a concrete shot list (angles, backgrounds, props, scale shots, infographic slots) that the seller approves with thumbnails before the shoot.

## Who Feels the Pain
Small sellers paying twice; studio photographers shooting to a guess; productised studios absorbing reshoot costs and bad reviews.

## Impact If Fixed
Fewer reshoots and refunds, and a brief that doubles as the variant plan for the seller's first conversion test.
