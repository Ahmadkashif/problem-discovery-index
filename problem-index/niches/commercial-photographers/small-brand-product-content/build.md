# Image Sets Sold With a Conversion Test

**Niche:** [[niches/commercial-photographers/small-brand-product-content/profile|Small Brands Commissioning Product Content]]
**Industry:** [[industries/commercial-photographers|Commercial Photographers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Small sellers buy product photos on price because no photographer can tell them, with evidence, which images will sell more.
**Tags:** #causal-inference #hypothesis-testing #gradient-boosting #contrastive-learning #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to prove that a set of images lifts the listing's conversion on the seller's own marketplace data, and whoever can show the lift per dollar spent takes the seller.

## The Problem
A seller replaces their phone photos with a professional set and sales rise, or don't, in a month when the price, the ads and the season also changed. They can't tell what the images did, so the next shoot is bought on price, and the cheapest source is increasingly a generator.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Productised studios measure orders, not outcomes. Marketplace split-test tools exist but need traffic, careful setup and patience that small sellers lack. No studio has pooled test results across many products to learn which shot types and compositions win in which categories.

## What to Build
A service sold with every shoot: the studio delivers two or three candidate main images and image orders, sets up the marketplace's own experiment where eligible (or a matched before-and-after design where not), reports the measured conversion lift with confidence intervals, and feeds every result into a pooled model. That model predicts, for a new product, which compositions and shot types are likely to win. Image embeddings make the predictions transfer to unseen products.

## Target Customer
Founders and ecommerce managers at small brands with enough marketplace traffic to test, and the productised studios competing to serve them.

## Impact If Built
Photography is bought as a measured growth input rather than a cost, and the studio with the largest archive of tested results becomes hard to undercut on price.
