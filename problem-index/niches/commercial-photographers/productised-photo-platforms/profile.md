# Productised Studio & On-Demand Photography Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commercial-photographers|Commercial Photographers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
Companies that turn commercial photography into an online product. Soona and Squareshot sell ecommerce images per photo from their own studios (from ~$39 and ~$50 respectively). On-demand marketplaces such as Snappr dispatch freelance photographers to business shoots at fixed prices. Brands and small sellers pay per image or by subscription.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-30 analytics and data staff at the larger platforms (estimate)
**Output:** Images (the product), plus internal pricing, demand and quality analytics
**Proprietary data:** Order, brief, shoot and re-order histories across many thousands of brands and products
**Clock:** Customers' launch dates
**Buyer:** Chief operating officer / head of data
