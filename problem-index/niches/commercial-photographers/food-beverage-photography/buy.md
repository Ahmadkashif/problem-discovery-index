# Online Proofing Tools Adapted to Live Food Sets

**Niche:** [[niches/commercial-photographers/food-beverage-photography/profile|Food & Beverage Photography]]
**Industry:** [[industries/commercial-photographers|Commercial Photographers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Creative proofing tools already route files to reviewers and record approvals; they assume the file will still exist tomorrow, and a food set won't.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #compliance #automation #worker-facing
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get the real product looking its best through brand, legal and retailer approval on a perishable one-day set without crossing truth-in-advertising lines, and whoever gets an approved hero shot of the actual product fastest takes the account.

## The Problem
Remote approvers join food shoots through screen shares of the tether monitor, with unreliable colour, no way to compare frames and no record of who approved what. Afterwards the selects go into a proofing tool for a second round that sometimes reverses the first.

## What Already Exists
Online proofing platforms (Ziflow, Filestage, Frame.io for stills and video) offer review links, annotations, version comparison, staged approval workflows and audit trails.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: a live mode fed directly from the tether, colour-managed on calibrated remote displays where available; a timed decision window per frame with escalation when an approver is silent; side-by-side comparison with the approved layout or the previous pack; stakeholder roles specific to food (brand, regulatory, retailer, nutrition or claims) with required sign-offs before the set is struck; and the approval record joined to the styling log for substantiation.

## Target Customer
Producers of packaging, menu and advertising food shoots, and brand teams at food and beverage companies with distributed approvers.

## Impact If Solved
One approval round on the day instead of two, fewer next-day reshoots, and an approval record legal can rely on.
