# A Set-Day Approval Room With a Real-Product Record

**Niche:** [[niches/commercial-photographers/food-beverage-photography/profile|Food & Beverage Photography]]
**Industry:** [[industries/commercial-photographers|Commercial Photographers]]
**Type:** Build (Greenfield Opportunity)
**One-liner:** A hero shot has a few minutes of life and four approvers in three cities, and when legal asks a year later whether the cheese was real, nobody can prove it.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #object-detection #compliance #workflow-orchestration #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to get the real product looking its best through brand, legal and retailer approval on a perishable one-day set without crossing truth-in-advertising lines, and whoever gets an approved hero shot of the actual product fastest takes the account.

## The Problem
On a packaging shoot the brand manager, the agency art director, a regulatory reviewer and sometimes a retailer buyer all need to approve the hero. Some are on set, some on a video call, some reply to screenshots hours later, by which time the food is gone. Separately, what was actually in the frame (the brand's own product, a stand-in, a styling aid, a retouched element) is recorded nowhere, which leaves the brand exposed if a claim is challenged.

## Why Nobody Has Built This
Food photography is a small specialty, approvals have always happened in the room, and the legal record was nobody's job. Proofing tools were built for asynchronous review of finished files.

## What to Build
A live set room: frames stream from the tether to every approver with a decision window, comments pinned to the image, and a sign-off record per stakeholder. Alongside it, a product-and-styling log captured by the stylist on a tablet (product lot, components used, stand-ins, aids), linked to each approved frame, plus a record of the retouching applied afterwards. Exportable as a substantiation file.

## Target Customer
Food and beverage photographers and their producers, and brand packaging and legal teams at food, beverage and restaurant companies.

## Impact If Built
Hero shots are approved inside the food's life rather than reshot the next day, and every pack or menu image carries proof of what was real.
