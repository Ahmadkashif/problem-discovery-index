# The Model Image Still Live After the Usage Expired

**Niche:** [[niches/commercial-photographers/on-model-lifestyle-ecommerce/profile|On-Model & Lifestyle Ecommerce Content]]
**Industry:** [[industries/commercial-photographers|Commercial Photographers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** A model's agency invoices for a year of overuse after spotting her on a retailer's site, and the brand cannot even list where else the images are running.
**Tags:** #object-detection #large-language-models #compliance #workflow-orchestration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a brand's look identical across thousands of SKUs, models, sets and seasons while every model's usage terms stay inside their contracts, and whoever holds the look without overrunning talent usage takes the account.

## The Problem
Ecommerce images live far longer than their usage terms. Products carry over between seasons, retailers keep cached copies, marketing reuses a strong image in paid social. When the model's agency finds an image past its term, the brand pays an overuse fee and scrambles to find every other placement.

## Why It's Still Broken
Usage terms are agreed by the studio or the brand's production team, while images are published by ecommerce, marketing and retail partners who never see the booking. Expiry dates are not attached to the files, and nobody owns the takedown.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Attach the model's usage terms to every image as rights metadata at delivery. Scan owned and retailer channels monthly for images that show models whose terms are ending. Give production a renewal-or-replace list sixty days ahead, with the cost of renewing compared with reshooting the carryover SKUs.

## Who Feels the Pain
Brand production and legal teams paying overuse claims; studios that negotiated the original terms; models whose images are used without payment.

## Impact If Fixed
Overuse claims are avoided or pre-negotiated, carryover SKUs are reshot on schedule rather than in a rush, and talent relationships stay intact.
