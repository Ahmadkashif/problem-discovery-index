# Contract Management Adapted to Model Usage Terms

**Niche:** [[niches/commercial-photographers/on-model-lifestyle-ecommerce/profile|On-Model & Lifestyle Ecommerce Content]]
**Industry:** [[industries/commercial-photographers|Commercial Photographers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Contract lifecycle software already stores obligations and expiry dates; it has no idea which of a brand's 40,000 live images show the model whose usage ends on Friday.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #object-detection #compliance #data-integration #workflow-orchestration
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to keep a brand's look identical across thousands of SKUs, models, sets and seasons while every model's usage terms stay inside their contracts, and whoever holds the look without overrunning talent usage takes the account.

## The Problem
Model bookings carry usage terms: ecommerce only or also paid social, territories, one year or two, and sometimes exclusivity against competitors. The terms sit in agency confirmations and vouchers. The images are spread across the brand's site, marketplaces, retailers' sites and ads, and nobody connects the two.

## What Already Exists
Contract lifecycle management platforms (Ironclad, DocuSign CLM, Agiloft) extract obligations and track renewals. Digital asset management systems (Bynder, Aprimo, Adobe Experience Manager Assets) can store rights metadata and expiry dates per asset.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: extraction of model usage terms from agency confirmations into structured rights (channel, territory, start, end, exclusivity); linkage of each booking to every image the model appears in, using the shoot record or face matching with consent; placement tracking across owned and retailer channels; expiry alerts with a list of live placements to pull or renew; and consent records for any AI-altered or replica image.

## Target Customer
Creative operations and legal teams at fashion and beauty brands, and studios offering talent management as part of their service.

## Impact If Solved
Expired-usage claims and emergency takedowns become rare, renewals are negotiated before they lapse, and replica consent is documented by default.
