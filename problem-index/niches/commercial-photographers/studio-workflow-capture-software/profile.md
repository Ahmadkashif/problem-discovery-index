# Studio Workflow & Capture Software Platforms

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commercial-photographers|Commercial Photographers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Software vendors whose products run the studio: Capture One for tethered capture and colour, Creative Force for ecommerce studio planning, sample tracking and delivery (from ~$2,900 a month), and automated rig makers such as Orbitvu and PhotoRobot with their control software. Studios and in-house brand teams pay licences and subscriptions.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-80 product research, data and customer-success analytics staff per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Software; benchmark reports and product research for marketing
**Proprietary data:** Production telemetry across many studios: throughput, reshoot rates, capture settings, approval times
**Clock:** None external; release cycles
**Buyer:** Chief product officer

**Kill switches:** data isn't theirs to use. Sessions, images and production records belong to the studios and their brand clients, and any cross-customer benchmark needs consent the contracts don't give.
**Verdict:** Logged — kill switch. The richest operational dataset in the industry, held under customers' terms, and monetised as software rather than research.
