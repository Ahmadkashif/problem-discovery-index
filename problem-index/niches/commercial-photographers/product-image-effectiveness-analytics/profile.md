# Product Image Effectiveness Analytics

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commercial-photographers|Commercial Photographers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Vendors that predict how well a product image will perform with shoppers before it runs. Vizit scores ecommerce images 0–100 for a chosen audience using models trained on online behaviour and publishes category content-effectiveness rankings. Visual-attention vendors such as Neurons and Dragonfly AI predict where the eye lands. CPG and retail brands pay for software subscriptions and category reports, and the scores increasingly shape the brief a photographer receives.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-40 data scientists, researchers and category analysts per vendor (estimate)
**Output:** Image and listing scores, category effectiveness rankings, creative recommendations
**Proprietary data:** Trained preference models and scored archives of product images across categories
**Clock:** None external; brands' launch and range-review calendars
**Buyer:** Chief product officer / head of research
