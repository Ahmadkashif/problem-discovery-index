# Image Copyright Enforcement Services

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commercial-photographers|Commercial Photographers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Pixsy, ImageRights, Copytrack and PicRights crawl the web for photographers' and agencies' images, decide which matches to pursue, and recover retroactive licence fees on contingency, escalating a minority of cases to partner law firms. Pixsy takes 50% of recoveries. PicRights works for news agencies, and Copytrack holds exclusive enforcement rights to archives such as WENN's. Rights holders pay nothing up front; infringers' settlements fund the business.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 20-150 case managers, licensing specialists and legal coordinators per service (estimate)
**Output:** Infringement determinations, retroactive licence demands, settlement negotiations, litigation referrals
**Proprietary data:** Billions of image matches joined to case outcomes: which infringer types, countries and uses settle, at what fee, after how many contacts
**Clock:** Statutes of limitation, Copyright Claims Board response windows and court dates for the litigated minority
**Buyer:** Head of operations / chief executive at the service
