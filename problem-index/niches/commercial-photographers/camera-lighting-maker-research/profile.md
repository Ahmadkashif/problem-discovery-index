# Camera & Lighting Manufacturers' Market Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commercial-photographers|Commercial Photographers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
The market research and professional-user teams inside camera, lens and lighting manufacturers (Canon, Sony, Nikon, Fujifilm, Profoto and others) that study professional photographers' needs, run ambassador and pro-service programmes, and plan products. The research is internal; the company sells equipment.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-50 market research and professional-segment staff per major maker (estimate)
**Output:** Internal product requirements, segment research, pro-programme analytics
**Proprietary data:** Warranty and pro-service registrations, survey panels, repair and usage records
**Clock:** Their own product launch cycles
**Buyer:** Head of professional imaging marketing
