# Commercial Photography Associations

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commercial-photographers|Commercial Photographers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Association research arms

## What They Do
Professional associations for commercial photographers: ASMP (6,500–7,000+ members by its own and third-party counts), APA, the Association of Independent Architectural Photographers, and PPA on the portrait side. They publish business guidance, contract templates and occasional pricing surveys, and lobby on copyright.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Under 10 research or benchmark staff
**Output:** Business guides, templates, surveys, advocacy
**Proprietary data:** Membership surveys
**Clock:** Self-set publication schedules
**Buyer:** Executive director
