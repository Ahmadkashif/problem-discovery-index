# Production Budgeting Software Adapted to Photo Usage Licences

**Niche:** [[niches/commercial-photographers/advertising-campaign-photography/profile|Advertising & Campaign Photography]]
**Industry:** [[industries/commercial-photographers|Commercial Photographers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Film and commercial production budgeting tools already handle crew, vendors, fringes and actualisation; they treat a still photographer's usage licence as a single line of text.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #feature-engineering #workflow-orchestration #automation #revenue-impact
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to win the agency's bid with a treatment and an estimate whose usage terms the art buyer can defend to the client's procurement team and cost consultant, and whoever prices usage most defensibly takes the account.

## The Problem
A campaign estimate has a production half that looks like a small film budget (crew days, studio, casting, stylists, props, catering, insurance, retouching) and a usage half that has no equivalent in film budgeting. Producers rebuild the usage section by hand for every bid and copy it into the licence after the award.

## What Already Exists
Production budgeting software built for film and commercials (Movie Magic Budgeting, Showbiz Budgeting and newer cloud tools such as Saturation) handles chart-of-accounts budgets, vendor rates, actualisation against spend and bid comparison. Photography-specific estimating tools (Blinkbid) handle estimates and invoices on the desktop.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: usage as a structured object with media, placements, territory, term, exclusivity and options, priced separately from production; an estimate format that matches what agency art producers and cost consultants expect to review; options priced up front and invoiced automatically when exercised; licence documents generated from the awarded estimate without re-typing; and photo-specific cost lines (digital tech, capture station, retouching per image, food and prop styling) with typical rates.

## Target Customer
Producers at photo production companies and in-house producers working for advertising photographers who bid several jobs a month.

## Impact If Solved
A bid that takes a producer a day of spreadsheet work takes an hour, and the licence written after the award matches what was estimated, line for line.
