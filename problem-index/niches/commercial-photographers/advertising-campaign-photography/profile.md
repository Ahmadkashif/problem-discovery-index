# Advertising & Campaign Photography

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commercial-photographers|Commercial Photographers]]
**Category:** High Market Share
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to win the agency's bid with a treatment and an estimate whose usage terms the art buyer can defend to the client's procurement team and cost consultant, and whoever prices usage most defensibly takes the account.

## Profile
**Market Size:** ~$1.2B in US commissioned advertising photography fees, including usage licences (estimate)
**Share of Parent Industry:** ~25% of commercial photography revenue (estimate)
**Digital Adoption:** Medium — capture, retouching and delivery are digital; estimates, treatments and licences are spreadsheets and PDFs
**Target Buyer:** Executive producer at a photo production company; agency art producer on the buying side
**Automation Potential:** Medium — estimating and licence administration are highly automatable, the creative treatment is not

## What Makes This a Distinct Niche
Campaign work is commissioned by agencies or brand in-house teams for out-of-home, print, paid social, retail and packaging, and it is the one part of the industry where the licence is worth more than the shoot day. Jobs are won in competitive bids, often three photographers bidding the same brief, with estimates above an agency threshold reviewed by a production cost consultant. The photographer's creative treatment gets them shortlisted. The estimate, and specifically the usage line, decides whether they are awarded at a fee that pays, or win at a usage price they will regret.

## Current Tools & Gaps
Treatments are built in Keynote, InDesign or Canva. Estimates run on Blinkbid, fotoQuote or spreadsheets, sometimes with a production company's or rep's in-house templates. Agencies track bids in their own production systems. The gaps: no structured record of what usage was quoted, won or lost and at what fee, and usage pricing based on a producer's memory and a few published calculators. When the client wants to add a medium after the shoot, the add-on is re-quoted from scratch.
