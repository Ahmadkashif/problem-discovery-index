# The Usage Add-On Re-Quoted by Email

**Niche:** [[niches/commercial-photographers/advertising-campaign-photography/profile|Advertising & Campaign Photography]]
**Industry:** [[industries/commercial-photographers|Commercial Photographers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** Three months after the shoot the client wants Canada and a bus-shelter run, and the photographer improvises a number in an email thread with no reference to the original licence.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #confidence-intervals #revenue-impact #workflow-orchestration #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to win the agency's bid with a treatment and an estimate whose usage terms the art buyer can defend to the client's procurement team and cost consultant, and whoever prices usage most defensibly takes the account.

## The Problem
Campaigns change after the shoot: a new territory, a longer flight, a medium nobody planned for, an image moved from social to packaging. Each change is a licence extension. The photographer gets the request by email, digs out the original estimate, guesses a fee and sends it. The agency, under deadline, pushes back or simply runs the new use and sorts it out later.

## Why It's Still Broken
The original usage was written as prose in a PDF, so there is nothing to price the extension against. Options are rarely priced in the original estimate, which hands the negotiation to the client. Nobody owns licence administration in a small production company, so extensions are handled by whoever answers the email.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Store every awarded licence as structured terms. When an extension request arrives, a language model reads it, maps it to the changed terms and prices the delta against the original licence and the firm's own history. The producer gets a ready-to-send amendment with the original terms quoted. Future estimates price the likely options up front.

## Who Feels the Pain
Photographers and producers negotiating from a weak position after the work is delivered; agency art producers chasing approvals for uses already running; clients exposed to unlicensed use.

## Impact If Fixed
Extensions are priced in minutes and consistently, are invoiced rather than forgotten, and stop being the point at which most of a campaign's usage value is given away.
