# Photography Insurance Programmes

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commercial-photographers|Commercial Photographers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Insurers and brokers that write equipment, general liability and professional liability cover for photographers, often as association-endorsed programmes or small-business packages. Production insurance for larger shoots is written by the entertainment underwriters logged under [[niches/video-production-smb/production-insurance-underwriting/profile|Production Insurance & Completion Underwriting]] and is not re-scored here.

## Insight Function
**Size:** No photography-specific research or underwriting team; photographers are one class within small-business and media lines
**Output:** Policies and claims handling
**Proprietary data:** Claims histories for equipment loss and liability, pooled with other trades
**Clock:** Policy renewals
**Buyer:** Programme manager
