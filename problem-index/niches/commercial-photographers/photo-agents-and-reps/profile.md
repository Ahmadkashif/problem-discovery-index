# Photo Agents & Reps

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commercial-photographers|Commercial Photographers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Payer & intermediary layer

## What They Do
Agents who represent a roster of advertising and commercial photographers. They market the roster to agency art buyers, negotiate estimates and usage, and take a commission, commonly a quarter to a third of the creative fee (estimate).

## Insight Function
**Size:** Typically 1-5 per agency
**Output:** Representation, bids and negotiated licences
**Proprietary data:** Relationships and memory of past bids
**Clock:** Agency bid deadlines
**Buyer:** Agency principal
