# Full-Resolution Files Released Before the Invoice Is Paid

**Niche:** [[niches/commercial-photographers/solo-commercial-photographers/profile|Solo Commercial Photographers]]
**Industry:** [[industries/commercial-photographers|Commercial Photographers]]
**Type:** Fix (Pain Point)
**One-liner:** The client needs the images for a launch on Monday, the photographer sends the full-resolution files on Friday, and the invoice sits unpaid for ninety days.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #workflow-orchestration #automation #revenue-impact #quick-win
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to turn a solo photographer's shoot into a licence that is quoted, signed, attached to the files and paid again on renewal, and whoever makes usage collectable without a producer or rep takes the photographer.

## The Problem
Commercial clients pay on net-30 to net-90 terms through procurement systems, while needing the images immediately. A solo photographer delivers to keep the relationship, and then chases payment with no leverage, because the client is already using the work. The licence technically depends on payment, but nothing enforces it.

## Why It's Still Broken
Delivery and invoicing sit in different tools. Withholding files feels hostile to a small vendor, and contract terms that make the licence conditional on payment are rarely invoked. Some states now protect freelancers on payment (New York's Freelance Isn't Free Act requires written contracts and timely payment), but enforcement is the freelancer's job.

## What a Fix Looks Like
Delivery in two stages tied to the invoice: web-resolution, licence-marked files at once for layout and approvals, and full-resolution files released automatically on payment or deposit. Automated, polite payment reminders reference the licence condition. A one-click statutory claim draft is ready where a state law applies.

## Who Feels the Pain
Solo photographers carrying their clients' payment terms; assistants and stylists the photographer has already paid; clients who would rather pay than lose the files at launch.

## Impact If Fixed
Days sales outstanding fall, write-offs shrink, and the photographer stops financing their clients' campaigns.
