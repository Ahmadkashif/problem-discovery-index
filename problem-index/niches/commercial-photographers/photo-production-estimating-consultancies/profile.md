# Photo Production & Estimating Consultancies

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commercial-photographers|Commercial Photographers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Firms that produce shoots and price them for photographers and clients. Wonderful Machine is the best-known example: its producers build estimates, negotiate usage and run productions, and it publishes pricing case studies. Architectural photo agencies such as Esto play a similar role for their rosters. Photographers pay per estimate or as a share of production; clients pay production fees.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-25 producers and estimating consultants at the larger firms (estimate)
**Output:** Estimates and usage pricing, negotiation support, production management
**Proprietary data:** Archives of thousands of estimates with outcomes across genres and clients
**Clock:** Agency bid deadlines and shoot dates set by clients
**Buyer:** Executive producer / founder
