# Advertising Production Cost Consultants

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commercial-photographers|Commercial Photographers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Specialist advisory & valuation

## What They Do
Independent consultancies hired by advertisers to review and benchmark the production estimates their agencies collect, including photography: the triple-bid estimates for crew, studio, talent, retouching and usage. They recommend which bid to award, challenge line items and usage fees, and report savings to procurement. Some agencies' own policies send estimates above a threshold to a cost consultant. Media-audit firms such as Ebiquity, analysed for their media work under [[niches/media-rep-firms/media-auditing-firms/profile|Media Auditing & Agency Performance Benchmarking]], run related production practices.

## Insight Function
**Size:** 10-60 production cost analysts per firm (estimate)
**Output:** Estimate reviews, rate benchmarks, award recommendations, savings reports
**Proprietary data:** Pooled rate and estimate history across many advertisers' productions, including photography day rates, crew rates and usage fees
**Clock:** Agency bid deadlines and production calendars set by the client
**Buyer:** Head of production consultancy / advertiser procurement lead
