# Reverse Image Search Adapted to Licence Evidence

**Niche:** [[niches/commercial-photographers/image-rights-enforcement/profile|Image Rights Enforcement & Licence Compliance]]
**Industry:** [[industries/commercial-photographers|Commercial Photographers]]
**Type:** Buy & Customize (Vertical Adaptation)
**One-liner:** Reverse image search engines already find copies across the web; they return links, not the dated, archived evidence a demand letter or Board claim needs.
**Tags:** #contrastive-learning #k-nearest-neighbors #large-language-models #compliance #automation
**Contested on:** Every serious competitor in this niche is fighting to prove which online uses of an image are unlicensed (checked against the licences actually granted) and convert them into paid licences without a lawsuit, and whoever keeps claims against licensed users near zero takes the photographer's archive.

## The Problem
A photographer who finds a copy with a reverse search still has to capture it properly: screenshot with date and URL, identify the site owner, establish when the use began, and gather the registration certificate and the original file. By the time they do, the page has often changed, and the evidence is gone.

## What Already Exists
Reverse image search (Google Lens, TinEye, Bing Visual Search) and web archiving services (the Internet Archive, commercial page-capture tools) used separately.

## The Customization Gap
The adaptation needs: scheduled searches over the photographer's portfolio with near-duplicate matching that survives crops and filters; automatic, time-stamped page captures with hashes at first detection; site-owner identification from registration and imprint data; first-seen dating; packaging of the capture, registration details and original file into the evidence format a demand letter or Copyright Claims Board filing needs; and a check against the photographer's licence ledger before anything is sent.

## Target Customer
Self-employed commercial photographers who want to enforce their own rights without handing half the recovery to an agent.

## Impact If Solved
Evidence is preserved at detection rather than reconstructed later, and a solo photographer can file a well-prepared claim in an hour.
