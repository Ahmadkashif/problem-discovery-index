# Retouching & Post-Production Outsourcers

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commercial-photographers|Commercial Photographers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Suppliers selling into the industry

## What They Do
Services that retouch commercial images at volume for studios and brands. Pixelz prices standard ecommerce edits from ~$0.95–1.45 per image, and offshore houses in South and Southeast Asia work overnight for US and European clients. They combine AI background removal with large teams of human editors.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Production headcount only (editors and QA staff, in the hundreds at the larger houses); no research function
**Output:** Retouched images
**Proprietary data:** Before-and-after image pairs and QA histories, owned by the clients
**Clock:** Overnight turnaround commitments
**Buyer:** Head of operations
