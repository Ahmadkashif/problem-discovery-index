# Retailer & Brand In-House Ecommerce Studios

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commercial-photographers|Commercial Photographers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Aggregator / rollup platforms

## What They Do
The in-house photo studios of large retailers and brands (marketplaces, department stores, fashion etailers, big apparel brands) that shoot thousands of SKUs a day with staff photographers, stylists, digital techs and retouchers, on platforms such as Creative Force. They absorb volume that would otherwise go to independent studios.

## Insight Function
**Size:** Production headcount only (photographers, stylists, retouchers, coordinators); no dedicated research function
**Output:** Product images for the company's own listings
**Proprietary data:** Production logs and listing performance, used internally
**Clock:** Launch and drop calendars
**Buyer:** Head of creative production
