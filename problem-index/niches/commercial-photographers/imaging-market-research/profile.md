# Imaging & Camera Market Research

**Parent Industry:** [[industries/commercial-photographers|Commercial Photographers]]
**Category:** Insight Layer
**Value-Chain Position:** Data & benchmark vendors

## What They Do
Research firms and data programmes that track the imaging market: camera, lens and lighting shipments, professional-photographer surveys and demand forecasts. Keypoint Intelligence covers imaging alongside print. The Camera & Imaging Products Association (CIPA) is a manufacturers' association that publishes shipment statistics openly. Camera and lighting makers, retailers and investors are the buyers. The same organisations are logged from the consumer side under [[niches/portrait-photography-studios/photo-imaging-market-research/profile|Photo & Imaging Market Research]].

## Insight Function
**Size:** ~2–8 analysts on photo and imaging topics across the field (estimate)
**Output:** Market sizing and forecasts, shipment tracking, user surveys
**Proprietary data:** Survey panels and multi-year pricing histories; CIPA's aggregate shipment data is public
**Clock:** Manufacturers' product-planning cycles and trade-show calendars
**Buyer:** Head of imaging research
