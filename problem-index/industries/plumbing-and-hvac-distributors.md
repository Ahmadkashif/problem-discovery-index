# Plumbing & HVAC Distributors

## Profile
**Category:** Wholesale & Distribution
**NAICS 2022:** 423720, 423730, 423740 — Plumbing and Heating Equipment and Supplies (Hydronics) Merchant Wholesalers; Warm Air Heating and Air-Conditioning Equipment and Supplies Merchant Wholesalers; Refrigeration Equipment and Supplies Merchant Wholesalers
**Market Size:** ~$192.7B in 2022 receipts across 5,246 firm counts and 14,372 establishments (Census SUSB 2022: 423720 had 2,554 firms and $102.3B; 423730 had 2,072 firms and $80.6B; 423740 had 620 firms and $9.8B); 179 enterprises with 500+ employees took ~$114.7B (~60%), while 3,849 firm counts under 20 employees (~73% of firms) took ~$14.7B. Ferguson, the largest, sold $31.3B in calendar 2025 ($29.8B in the US); Watsco, the largest HVAC distributor, sold $7.24B.
**Tech Maturity:** Medium — the leaders run serious digital channels (Watsco's e-commerce reached $2.7B, 37% of sales, in the 12 months to June 2026, built by close to 300 technologists), but commercial equipment and fixture packages are still selected by hand from engineers' schedules, warranty claims are keyed into each manufacturer's portal, and seasonal buys rest on a branch manager's memory of last summer.
**Workforce:** Counter and will-call staff, inside sales and quotation specialists, applications and hydronics specialists, showroom consultants, outside salespeople calling on mechanical contractors and HVAC dealers, warranty and returns clerks, purchasing agents and inventory planners, warehouse staff and delivery drivers. BLS QCEW counted ~220,600 private employees in ~19,600 establishments in the three codes in 2024, at ~$99,700 average pay (~$111,700 in HVAC, ~$92,400 in plumbing).

## Key Pain Themes
A supply house sells a building's water and air one job at a time. On the commercial side, a mechanical or plumbing contractor bidding a school or a medical office sends the drawings, the Division 22 and 23 specifications and the engineer's schedules: rooftop units listed by basis-of-design model with capacity, airflow, static pressure and electrical data, water heaters and boilers by input and recovery, fixtures by manufacturer and model with an "or approved equal". The distributor sells other brands than the schedule names, so its applications specialist has to re-select every line in the house's own lines at the schedule's design conditions, catch the curb adapter, the low-ambient kit or the ADA carrier the schedule implies, and price it before the bid closes. Whether the engineer accepts that substitution is learned weeks later at submittal, and the specialist's reasons are recorded nowhere.

On the residential side the clock is the weather and the regulator. HVAC dealers replace failed systems on the day the call comes in, and they buy from the distributor that holds their brand's matched system in stock; Watsco's 10-K puts replacement at ~80–90% of residential AC unit sales and serves 130,000 active contractors. The move from R-410A to A2L refrigerants (R-454B and R-32) under the AIM Act touched ~55% of Watsco's products across 650 locations in 2025 and converted more than $1B of its inventory; service cylinders of R-454B ran short in spring 2025 (search summary). Then EPA's May 2026 final rule lifted the 1 January 2026 installation deadline for pre-2025 R-410A equipment, effective 27 July 2026, after distributors had planned their stock around it, and pushed the supermarket system deadline from 2027 to 2032 (search summary). Policy keeps moving demand: the federal 25C credit of up to $2,000 for heat pumps ended for equipment placed in service after 31 December 2025, and DOE's water-heater standard applies to units made after 6 May 2029.

Margin depends on commodities and paper. Copper tube and fittings carry the Section 232 tariff (50% from 1 August 2025, on full value from 6 April 2026), and PVC pipe pricing is the subject of In re: PVC Pipe Antitrust Litigation, which alleges manufacturers coordinated through OPIS's "PVC & Pipe Weekly"; Atkore agreed in April 2026 to pay $72.5M and $64M to two purchaser classes, subject to court approval. Warranty claims, defective-part returns and OEM programme rebates are filed manufacturer by manufacturer. And the channel is two-step and brand-bound: Watsco's Carrier joint ventures were 53% of its 2025 revenue under exclusive territories, Trane, Lennox and Daikin run their own branches (recalled), and independents such as the Johnstone Supply co-op (more than $4B in combined revenue) and Winsupply sit beside them.

## Current Tech Landscape
Epicor Eclipse is the ERP built for plumbing, HVAC and electrical distribution; Infor CSD, Prophet 21 and DDI run elsewhere. Watsco's HVAC Pro+ apps, e-commerce and OnCallAir proposal tool lead the dealer-facing side, and it added more than 10,000 A2L SKUs with "regulatory match-ups" in 2025. Bluon's PartsConnect (April 2026) lets technicians scan a model number against 30M+ model records from 240+ manufacturers to find compatible parts at local distributors, Prokeep runs text ordering, and Rebar raised a $14M Series A in March 2026 for computer-vision quoting of HVAC equipment from blueprints for commercial suppliers, with plumbing next. The AI quoting and order-entry vendors that sell across distribution — Avent, Mercura, Comena, Hexa, Augment, Canals and Proton.ai — are profiled once, under [[niches/industrial-distributors/rfq-and-order-intake-automation/profile|RFQ & Order Intake Automation]]; Augment's Augie went live in Eclipse at Johnstone Supply Heartland Group in July 2026, matching PO lines to SKUs. On 2026-10-24, Rebar had applied-AI, ML-infrastructure and platform engineers open in New York; Avent had founding FDE and engineer roles in San Francisco; Mercura and Comena had FDE and engineering roles in Munich only; Canals had a machine learning engineer open in Bogotá and senior software engineers in San Francisco, Toronto, Santiago, Mexico City, São Paulo, Buenos Aires and Bogotá; Ferguson was hiring a manager for AI platform engineering and data science; Augment's six openings were all go-to-market. Each tool does its job. The record no tool owns is the selection decision: which line the specialist chose against the engineer's schedule, why, and whether it was approved, installed and kept.

## Analysis
The central unmeasured judgement in plumbing and HVAC distribution is selection: turning an engineer's schedule, a dealer's replacement call or a technician's failed part into the one line in the house's own brands that fits, complies and will be accepted. It decides the commercial bid, the residential replacement and the counter sale alike. Three things block fixing it. The knowledge is tacit and sits with a few applications and counter veterans per region. The outcome is scattered: submittal approvals sit in contractors' project platforms, warranty returns in manufacturers' portals and won jobs in later orders nobody joins back to the quote. And the catalogue itself keeps turning over on regulators' dates — the A2L launch, SEER2, the 2029 water-heater standard — so yesterday's matches go stale. The distributors are capable but margin-bound buyers (Ferguson's gross margin was 31.0% and Watsco's 28.0% in 2025), and as insight buyers the operators fail the Pass 2 gate. The strongest commercial opening is the vendor field selling into them: plan-and-spec equipment quoting, where Rebar is moving and hiring, and emailed list and PO intake, which belongs to the cross-distribution field profiled under [[niches/industrial-distributors/rfq-and-order-intake-automation/profile|Industrial Distributors]]. Whoever captures the selection decision together with its submittal and warranty outcomes will hold the dataset this trade has never had.
