# Community Health Centers

## Profile
**Category:** Healthcare & Clinical
**NAICS 2022:** 621498, 621410 — All Other Outpatient Care Centers; Family Planning Centers (federally qualified health centers funded under Section 330, look-alikes, Title X and independent family planning centres, and other outpatient centres that report the Uniform Data System to HRSA and bill PPS rates; physician offices, community mental health centres and dialysis sit in their own industries)
**Market Size:** HRSA-funded health centers took in $55.4B in 2025: Medicaid $25.4B (45.8%), private insurance $8.2B, Medicare $6.7B, Section 330 grants $5.5B (10.0%) and other grants and contracts $5.4B (UDS via KFF). The Census Service Annual Survey puts employer-firm revenue at $69.3B for all of NAICS 621498 in 2022, a wider code that also holds non-FQHC outpatient centres, and $3.9B for family planning centres (621410). Margins are thin: the median health center's operating margin fell from 9.9% in 2021 to 0.3% in 2024, and median days cash fell from 115 to 88 (Capital Link, June 2026)
**Tech Maturity:** Medium — every health center runs a certified EHR and submits a 185-hour annual UDS report (HRSA burden estimate), yet sliding-fee income proof is photocopied at the front desk, Medicaid wraparound payments are reconciled in spreadsheets, and outreach to patients who stop coming is logged by phone and memory.
**Workforce:** 320,826 FTEs at 1,356 HRSA awardees serving 32.7M patients in 2025 (UDS Table 5): 15,649 physician, 13,637 nurse practitioner and 4,596 physician assistant FTEs; 21,629 nurses and 43,895 other medical staff (mostly medical assistants); 22,776 dental and 20,376 mental health FTEs; 30,424 patient support FTEs, including 14,199 case managers, 5,228 eligibility workers, 3,337 community health workers and 2,591 outreach workers; and 4,606 quality improvement staff. HRSA also counts 171 look-alikes that report UDS without a Section 330 grant (Federal Register, December 2025)

## Key Pain Themes
A health center's most consequential judgement happens between visits. Its patients are poor: 67.7% of those with known income are at or below the poverty line, 17.2% are uninsured and 47.9% are on Medicaid (UDS 2025). They change phone numbers, lose coverage, work shifts that clash with clinic hours and stop coming. An experienced community health worker or nurse care manager reads the signs early. A refill isn't picked up, a ride is cancelled twice, or a patient says "I'm fine" in a voice that means otherwise, and the worker calls before the patient disappears. Newer staff work the list in order. The cost shows up in the measures HRSA publishes. Only 69.1% of 5.57M hypertensive patients had blood pressure controlled in 2025, and 26.3% of 3.2M diabetic patients had an HbA1c above 9% or no test at all (UDS Table 7). None of that judgement is recorded, and it leaves with the worker.

The money arrives through rules no commercial practice faces. Medicaid pays health centers a cost-based prospective payment system (PPS) rate per visit. When a managed care plan pays less, the state owes a wraparound payment, reconciled months later. Medicare's FQHC PPS base rate is $207.72 for 2026 (NACHC). Every patient's discount must be traceable to documented household income on a board-approved sliding fee schedule, and HRSA checks this at operational site visits. Section 330 grants are only 10% of revenue, but the Community Health Center Fund that supplies about 70% of federal health center funding expires on 31 December 2026 (NACHC).

Four external clocks land in the next four months. The CY2026 UDS, due in February 2027, adds patient support service lines 35–38, upstream-drivers-of-health lines 39–42 and new diagnosis lines, and drops the wraparound columns from Table 9D (OMB-approved 2026 UDS tables, ICR 202606-0915-002, 3 September 2026). Aligning its quality measures with CMS's 2026 eCQMs was proposed in the December 2025 notice (FR 2025-22443); the final manual was not seen. Medicaid work requirements (80 hours a month, CMS-2454-IFC) and six-month renewals for expansion adults (SMD #26-001) begin on 1 January 2027. So does HRSA's revised 340B rebate pilot, which gives covered entities 45 days from dispense to claim rebates on 25 Medicare-negotiated drugs (Holland & Knight).

## Current Tech Landscape
OCHIN Epic, NextGen, eClinicalWorks, athenahealth and Epic run charts and claims. Azara DRVS, which reports more than 700 health centers and 30+ primary care associations and networks as clients (Azara, search summary), and Relevant, which serves 90+ health centers, compute UDS measures and gap lists on top of the EHR. Fifty-two HRSA-funded health center controlled networks share IT and analytics (search summary). 340B administrators (Sentry, Macro Helix, Verity) qualify claims. Unite Us and findhelp handle social-needs referrals, and Medical Home Network and Community Care Cooperative run FQHC-led value-based contracts. Revenue cycle vendors such as Candid Health and Trellis AI sell to providers broadly rather than to health centers. Each tool covers its own report or claim. None holds the record that joins the care team's read of who is slipping, the patient's coverage status at the next renewal, and the PPS revenue each kept visit brings in.

## Analysis
The industry's central unmeasured judgement is the care team's sense of which patient is about to fall out of care, and what will bring them back. It decides the hypertension and diabetes rates HRSA publishes, every kept visit's PPS payment and each value-based contract's quality pool, yet it lives in community health workers' heads and call logs. Three things block a fix: outcomes happen out of sight (missed visits, lapsed coverage, emergency visits elsewhere), a worker who calls in time prevents the outcome that would have proved her right, and at a median margin of 0.3% most centers cannot fund a data team. The commercial opening is with the vendors hiring engineers to sell into health centers. Relevant had a Lead Engineer role, undated and open on 2026-10-28 (remote US and Canada, $165K–$190K, 25 staff); Azara (Burlington, Massachusetts) listed Implementation Engineer, Senior Software Engineer and Software Engineer Manager roles, undated and open on 2026-10-28; and Medical Home Network had a Staff Software Engineer posted on 1 April 2026 (remote, hybrid near Chicago). In the shared revenue cycle field, Candid Health and Trellis AI had forward deployed engineers open in San Francisco (see the agents note). The two-week MVP is a CY2026 UDS readiness checker on Synthea patients, benchmarked against HRSA's public UDS tables. It targets the revised tables, because HRSA put patient-level UDS+ on hold in April 2025 (search summary) and neither the 2026 notice nor the approved 2026 tables revive it. Under the Pass 2 rubric the centers fail the gate: their insight is spent on care and grant compliance, never invoiced, and nearly all of it is PHI. No pocket qualifies. The nearest is the manufacturer-side 340B claims-data and rebate platforms (42), which sell their findings but work to rebate clocks that bind each claim or their clients, and run on claims contributed under unread terms (see the niche overview).
