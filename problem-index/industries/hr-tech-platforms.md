# HR Tech Platforms

## Profile
**Category:** Horizontal SaaS
**Market Size:** ~$24B US human capital management software
**Tech Maturity:** High as systems of record, thin as systems of insight — Workday, SAP SuccessFactors and Oracle run enterprise HCM; BambooHR, Rippling, Namely and Paylocity serve the mid-market. All of them hold the complete employment history of their customers' workforces and return an org chart and a headcount report.
**Workforce:** Implementation consultants, compliance content teams, benefits operations analysts, integration engineers, people analytics specialists, customer support

## Key Pain Themes
Voluntary attrition is the most expensive recurring event in a company and the platforms holding every input to it produce a turnover percentage after the fact. The structural drivers are visible in the data — pay compression against new hires, managers with unmanageable spans, roles with no internal mobility path, promotion velocity that has stalled for a cohort — and they are visible months before the resignations. Below that sit two content burdens the vendors carry permanently: multi-state and multi-jurisdiction employment compliance, where leave entitlements, pay transparency requirements, sick time accrual and classification rules differ by state and increasingly by city; and benefits administration, where enrolment is configured once and carrier feeds then diverge silently until someone's claim is denied. The HR generalists carrying it spend their days answering the same policy question, and implementation teams rebuild org and history data from spreadsheets on every migration.

## Current Tech Landscape
Workday dominates large enterprise with a long implementation cycle; Rippling has grown quickly by unifying HR, IT and payroll for the mid-market; BambooHR and Paylocity serve smaller employers. Applicant tracking is a distinct adjacent category. People analytics products (Visier, ChartHop, One Model) exist precisely because the HCM systems do not answer analytical questions. Benefits administration is served by both the HCM vendors and specialists, with carrier connectivity remaining a persistent weak point. Compliance content is licensed or maintained in-house.

## Analysis
These platforms hold longitudinal employment histories across millions of workers and thousands of employers: compensation over time, promotion timing, manager changes, internal moves, tenure and exit. That is the empirical basis for every question about how careers and organisations actually work — questions currently answered by consulting benchmarks and survey data. The analytical opportunity is real and the ethical boundary is unusually sharp, because the same data supports both useful organisational diagnosis and individual surveillance, and the category's credibility depends on the distinction being architectural rather than a policy statement.
