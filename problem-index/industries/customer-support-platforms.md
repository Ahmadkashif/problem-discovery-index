# Customer Support Platforms

## Profile
**Category:** Horizontal SaaS
**Market Size:** ~$14B US customer service and support software
**Tech Maturity:** Very high and being repriced — Zendesk, Salesforce Service Cloud, Intercom, Freshdesk and Gorgias run ticketing, routing and knowledge for most of the market. Generative deflection has arrived quickly and is rewriting the category's economics, which were built on seats.
**Workforce:** Support operations analysts, knowledge managers, quality assurance reviewers, workforce management planners, implementation consultants, localisation staff

## Key Pain Themes
The category's central promise is deflection — resolve the customer's problem without an agent — and the mechanism has always been a knowledge base that decays. Articles are written once, the product changes, and the article is subtly wrong in a way nobody notices until an agent contradicts it. Deflection built on a decaying corpus produces confident wrong answers, which is a worse outcome than a queue. Below that, macros and canned responses accumulate into unmaintained libraries, and multilingual coverage is bought at a translation cost that means most companies support two languages and apologise for the rest. The agents are measured on handle time and customer satisfaction, two metrics that are respectively gameable and statistically useless at the individual level, which shapes the job in ways nobody defends. The knowledge manager, meanwhile, is one person maintaining a corpus that the whole company changes.

## Current Tech Landscape
Zendesk remains the volume incumbent; Intercom pivoted hard to generative resolution and repriced on outcomes; Salesforce Service Cloud holds the enterprise; Gorgias and Kustomer serve commerce. Generative answer products from the platforms and from specialists have moved quickly from pilot to production. Workforce management is a mature adjacent category. Quality assurance is largely manual sampling, with automated scoring products emerging. Voice remains a distinct and less-disrupted stack. Knowledge management is universally regarded as the weak link and is universally under-resourced.

## Analysis
Support platforms hold the most direct record of what is wrong with a product that any company possesses: every failure a customer cared enough to report, in their own words, with the resolution attached. That corpus is used to route tickets and to measure agents. It is not used to tell the product organisation what is breaking, which is the most valuable thing it could do — and the reason is organisational, since support reports to a cost centre and product does not read its tickets.
