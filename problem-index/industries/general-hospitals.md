# General Hospitals

## Profile
**Category:** Healthcare & Clinical
**NAICS 2022:** 622110 — General Medical and Surgical Hospitals (acute-care hospitals with inpatient beds, emergency departments and operating rooms, including critical access hospitals; psychiatric and specialty hospitals sit in other codes)
**Market Size:** Hospital care was $1.6T of US health spending in 2024, up 8.9% and 31% of the total (CMS National Health Expenditure), of which general acute-care hospitals take ~$1.3T (estimate). The AHA counts 6,100 hospitals, including 5,121 community hospitals (2,984 not-for-profit, 1,224 investor-owned, 913 state and local government), with 907,216 staffed beds and 35.7M admissions in 2024; 70% of community hospitals belong to a system (AHA Fast Facts 2026)
**Tech Maturity:** Medium — every hospital runs an EHR (Epic, Oracle Health, MEDITECH or TruBridge) and bills electronically, yet beds are placed at whiteboard huddles, open shifts are filled by text message, nursing homes are found by fax, and the median hospital's operating margin was 2.5% for the first half of 2026 (Kaufman Hall Flash Report).
**Workforce:** 5.7M employed in NAICS 622100 (BLS OEWS, May 2023): registered nurses (1.75M), nursing assistants (410,930), physicians (189,800), medical secretaries (173,500), managers (147,250), respiratory therapists, surgical technologists, sterile processing technicians (46,330 medical equipment preparers), case managers and social workers (45,010 healthcare social workers), CDI specialists and coders (47,530 medical records specialists), patient access and billing staff; only 4,430 data scientists and 5,990 software developers across the whole industry

## Key Pain Themes
The hospital's most consequential judgement is made at the bedside by nurses, and almost none of it is recorded as judgement. An experienced med-surg or step-down nurse "just knows" a patient is going downhill hours before the vital signs move. They check on that patient more often, chart more notes, and call the rapid response team on a feeling. The Epic Sepsis Model, the most widely deployed alternative, scored an AUC of 0.63 in Michigan's external validation. It missed 67% of sepsis cases while alerting on 18% of all hospitalisations (JAMA Internal Medicine). The CONCERN trial went the other way. It read the timing and frequency of nurses' own documentation as a signal of their worry, and cut mortality risk by 35.6% across ~60,000 patients (Nature Medicine, 2025). The tacit signal is real, and it walks out when a nurse with fifteen years' experience is replaced by a traveller on a 13-week contract. RN turnover was 17.6% in 2025, travel nurses cost an average $91 an hour, and each bedside RN who leaves costs ~$60,000 (NSI 2026).

Money is decided after the patient leaves. About 15% of claims to private payers are denied first time, and 28.5% of Medicaid managed care claims (Premier survey, 2025). Around 70% of denials are overturned on appeal, at $80 a claim for Medicare Advantage, so providers spend an estimated ~$25.7B a year fighting payments they eventually receive (Premier survey, 2025). The disputes are clinical. Payers downgrade the DRG, deny the inpatient status, or refuse the skilled nursing discharge, and more than 21% of MA SNF discharge requests are denied first time (Premier). The record that wins the appeal is written by physicians and nurses who never see the outcome.

The clocks are federal. The Hospital Readmissions Reduction Program cut payments to 2,334 hospitals (80%) from 1 October 2026, the first year to include Medicare Advantage patients (CMS via Healthcare Dive). Since 1 April 2026 the price transparency file has had to carry median and percentile allowed amounts from remittance data, under an attestation naming the CEO (CMS-1834-FC). 340B purchases passed $100B in 2025, with a manufacturer rebate pilot from 1 January 2027, and MS-DRG weights reset every 1 October; the FY2027 IPPS final rule, issued 31 July 2026, raised operating rates 2.3% (McDermott+ summary).

## Current Tech Landscape
Epic dominates large systems, and its native AI Charting (February 2026) and nursing "Chart with Art" (March 2026) now compete with Abridge, Ambience and Microsoft Dragon Copilot for ambient documentation. Oracle Health, MEDITECH and TruBridge serve the rest, especially rural and critical access hospitals. Revenue cycle runs on Epic Resolute with Waystar, Experian and Availity for claims and eligibility, while R1 RCM, Ensemble and Optum take whole revenue cycles on outsourced contracts. AKASA, Smarter Technologies (SmarterDx), Iodine, Solventum 360 Encompass, Fathom and CodaMetrix compete on CDI, coding and pre-bill review. Qventus, LeanTaaS iQueue and Opmed.ai sell OR, inpatient-flow and capacity optimisation. CarePort (WellSky) and Aidin route post-acute referrals, and UKG, symplr and AMN's ShiftWise run staffing and travel-nurse vendor management. MCG and InterQual criteria govern status decisions on both sides of the payer line. Turquoise Health and Serif Health normalise the price files. Each tool covers its boundary. None holds the record that joins a nurse's concern, a physician's note, a payer's denial and the patient's outcome, and that join is where the industry's judgement could be graded.

## Analysis
The industry's central unmeasured judgement is clinical vigilance: the experienced nurse's sense that a patient is deteriorating, and the physician's matching sense of who is ready to go home. Both drive mortality, length of stay, readmission penalties and the payer disputes that follow. Neither is captured except as side effects in the chart. Three things block a fix. First, labels are confounded, because a nurse who acts on a hunch prevents the deterioration that would have proved it. Second, the cost of a false alarm is paid in fatigue, since the Epic model alerted on 18% of patients. Third, any tool must win a committee of nursing, medical staff, IT, compliance and finance, which is why hospitals buy slowly and pay late. The commercial opening is therefore with the vendors that already sell into hospitals and are hiring engineers to deploy. On 24 October 2026, OpenAI's healthcare FDE team, Hippocratic AI, AKASA, Qventus, Opmed.ai, Commure and Abridge all had such roles open on their own boards (see the agents note). Under the Pass 2 rubric the operators fail: their insight is spent on care and margin, never invoiced, and every byte is PHI. No pocket qualifies. The nearest clean one is the de-identified hospital discharge databases that license benchmarks and real-world evidence (48), which own their corpus but set their own calendars (see the niche overview).
