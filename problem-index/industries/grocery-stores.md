# Grocery Stores

## Profile
**Category:** Retail & E-Commerce
**NAICS 2022:** 445110 — Supermarkets and Other Grocery Retailers (except Convenience Retailers) (chain and independent supermarkets and full-line grocery stores selling fresh and packaged food; specialty food shops, convenience stores, warehouse clubs and food wholesalers sit in other industries)
**Market Size:** Supermarkets and other grocery stores sold $875.0B in 2025, up from $846.8B in 2024 (Census Monthly Retail Trade, summed monthly sales). Statistics of US Businesses counts 38,513 firms running 62,647 stores with $790.6B of receipts in 2022. The 284 firms with 500 or more employees run 22,039 of those stores and take 82% of receipts, while 30,494 firms with fewer than 20 employees take 5% (Census SUSB 2022). Food retail profit margins settled at 1.7% (FMI, 2025)
**Tech Maturity:** Medium — every chain runs scanning POS, a merchandising system, a loyalty programme and some e-commerce, and the largest have serious data science (Kroger's 84.51°, Tesco's dunnhumby). Yet most fresh orders are still written or adjusted by a department manager walking the floor, shrink is recorded as a monthly number rather than a reason, and item eligibility for SNAP and WIC is a file someone uploads by hand.
**Workforce:** 2.80M employees in 445110 (Census SUSB 2022): cashiers and self-checkout attendants, stockers and night crew (about 631,000 stockers work in food and beverage retailers, BLS 2023 via search summary), department managers and clerks in produce, meat, seafood, bakery and deli, meat cutters, receivers, online-order pickers, pharmacists and technicians in in-store pharmacies, store managers, and at headquarters category managers, pricing analysts, replenishment buyers and scan coordinators

## Key Pain Themes
The supermarket's most consequential daily judgement is made by a department manager with a handheld and a clipboard. The produce manager looks at the bananas, the berries on the back dock and next week's ad, and decides how much to order, what to cull, and what to mark down. The meat manager decides how many subprimals to cut into steaks and how much to grind. Fresh foods were 42% of grocery sales in 2024 (FMI). The fresh departments are also where most shrink happens, because the product dies on the shelf. An experienced manager reads ripeness, weather, local events and the ad's lift from years of exposure and cannot write it down. A new manager, in an industry with 48% annual employee turnover in 2024 (FMI), over-orders and throws it away or under-orders and leaves holes. The outcome is visible within days, as sales and as dumpster weight. Almost nothing records why the order was what it was.

Around the fresh floor sits a labour model run on 1.7% margins. Labour is the largest cost a store manager controls, and schedules are written weekly against a forecast of customer counts, truck arrivals and online-order volume. Under Fair Workweek laws they must be posted well ahead: Seattle's Secure Scheduling Ordinance requires retail and food-service employers with 500+ employees worldwide to post schedules at least 14 days in advance and pay for later changes (Seattle Office of Labor Standards). Self-checkout has moved the cashier's job to watching six or eight lanes for missed scans and age checks. Online orders are now mostly picked in the store by employees walking the aisles. Kroger said in November 2025 it would close three Ocado-powered robotic fulfilment centres and take about $2.6B of charges, refocusing e-commerce on its stores (Grocery Dive). Takeoff Technologies, the in-store micro-fulfilment pioneer, filed for Chapter 11 in May 2024 (search summary).

External clocks set the rest. The weekly ad cycle fixes promotional prices and DSD vendor deliveries. SNAP is redeemed mostly at large stores. Supermarkets and super stores together took 79% of about $125B of redemptions in FY2021, and supermarkets alone took 27.0% ($33.9B) (USDA FNS). FNS's super store class includes supercenters, which belong to the queued general merchandise retailers industry. In 2026, 23 states had USDA waivers barring SNAP purchases of soda or candy, each with its own definitions, and a court blocked five of them in June (Avalara). Every chain had to set state-specific eligibility flags by UPC. WIC adds an Authorized Product List that states expect registers to load daily (search summary). The FDA Food Traceability Rule covers supermarkets that receive listed foods and requires records within 24 hours of a request, with enforcement deferred by Congress to 20 July 2028 (FDA).

## Current Tech Landscape
POS and front end come from NCR Voyix, Toshiba, LOC Software and ECRS Catapult, and merchandising and supply chain from Oracle Retail, SAP and Blue Yonder. Forecasting and replenishment come from RELEX, Afresh (which says it is live in over 10% of the US grocery market), Crisp (which bought Shelf Engine), Upshop and Invafresh. Labour scheduling runs on UKG, Legion, Logile and Zebra Workcloud. E-commerce runs on Instacart's marketplace, Storefront and Caper carts, plus Mercatus, Swiftly and in-house builds. Shelf cameras and robots come from Simbe, Focal Systems and Trax, and customer data science from 84.51°, dunnhumby and Eagle Eye. Price optimisation comes from Revionics and Clear Demand, and traceability from ReposiTrak and iFoodDS. Each is competent within its boundary. None of them owns the department manager's decision record: what was ordered and why, what arrived and in what condition, what was culled, marked down, donated or sold, and which of those calls a forecaster would have made differently.

## Analysis
The grocery store's central unmeasured judgement is the fresh department manager's order and cull: thousands of small, perishable bets a week, each with an outcome visible within days, made by people whose pattern recognition is the store's real margin defence and who leave at a high rate. Fixing it is blocked less by modelling than by records. Culls and markdowns are logged as totals or not at all, product condition at receipt is never captured, DSD vendors and wholesalers hold part of the movement data, and chains with forecasting software still let managers override it without a reason. The commercial opening is real but sits with vendors rather than stores. The 284 large firms buy software through long enterprise cycles, and the 30,000 small ones buy through their wholesaler. On 25 October 2026 the desk score of E = 4 held: Afresh had three US forward deployed engineering roles open (remote US and San Francisco). The other FDE titles come with caveats. Vori's forward deployed data engineer role (San Francisco) dates from January–February 2026. 84.51°'s Lead FDE (Cincinnati or Chicago) is internal AI tooling for Kroger developers, not a grocer-facing deployment. Instacart was hiring engineers in US and Canada remote roles. RELEX's FDE was in Barcelona, London or Milan, and Ocado's data and ML roles were in the UK, Poland and Bulgaria. Under the Pass 2 rubric the stores themselves are poor buyers for a research-insight engine. Their analysis is spent on their own margin, and the strongest insight pockets around them are already indexed under other industries.
