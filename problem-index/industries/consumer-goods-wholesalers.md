# Consumer Goods Wholesalers

## Profile
**Category:** Wholesale & Distribution
**NAICS 2022:** 423620, 423410, 423910, 423920, 423990, 424920, 424990 — Household Appliances, Electric Housewares and Consumer Electronics; Photographic Equipment and Supplies; Sporting and Recreational Goods; Toy and Hobby Goods; Other Miscellaneous Durable Goods; Book, Periodical and Newspaper; and Other Miscellaneous Nondurable Goods Merchant Wholesalers
**Market Size:** ~$397B in 2022 receipts across 29,162 firm counts and 32,252 establishments (Census SUSB 2022: 423620 $134.3B, 423910 $72.3B, 423990 $62.4B, 424990 $60.1B, 423920 $47.1B, 424920 $11.4B, 423410 $9.4B). The 355 enterprise firm counts with 500+ employees took ~$197.0B (~50%); in 423620 alone 56 of them took $102.8B (~77%), which is where brand-owned US sales arms and the largest distributors sit. The 25,921 firm counts under 20 employees (~89% of firms) took ~$64.6B (~16%).
**Tech Maturity:** Medium — any wholesaler that sells to big-box retail runs EDI (SPS Commerce alone counted ~46,900 1P recurring revenue customers at the end of 2025), but behind the EDI the work runs through mid-market ERPs, retailer portals, spreadsheets and email, and the import buy is still one merchant's call.
**Workforce:** Merchants and product managers who source in Asia, import and logistics coordinators, inside sales and customer service order desks, EDI coordinators, accounts receivable and deduction analysts, credit managers, costing and pricing analysts, warehouse pickers, packers and shipping leads, returns processors, and the independent rep groups who sell the lines on commission. BLS QCEW counted ~337,400 private employees in ~51,100 establishments in the seven codes in 2024, with average pay from ~$72,600 (books) to ~$127,200 (photographic), and ~$118,600 in appliances and electronics.

## Key Pain Themes
A consumer goods wholesaler lives on the item bet. A housewares, toy, sporting goods or gift importer chooses items at factory showrooms and Asian fairs, commits to a factory minimum four to nine months before the goods reach a shelf (estimate), and finds out only after the season whether the item sold through. Overbuys end as closeouts sold for a fraction of cost; underbuys cannot be re-bought inside the season. The merchant's judgement of which item, at which price point, for which retailer is the craft of the trade, and nothing records it. Since 2025 the cost side has moved under the merchant's feet: IEEPA tariffs from February 2025 were struck down on 20 February 2026 in *Learning Resources, Inc. v. Trump*, brought by an educational toy company; a 10% Section 122 tariff ran from 24 February to 24 July 2026; and Section 301 forced-labour tariffs of 10% or 12.5% on 60 trading partners took effect on 24 July 2026. Nearly 330,000 importers had paid ~$166B of IEEPA duties, and more than 75% had been refunded with interest by 2 October 2026 through CBP's refund process. Hamilton Beach, which buys most of its finished goods from suppliers in China, saw 2025 revenue fall 7.3% as retailers paused buying to assess inventory and tariff price increases.

Selling to big-box retail turns every invoice into a negotiation after the fact. Each account brings a vendor agreement, a routing guide, EDI documents (850 purchase orders, 856 ship notices, 810 invoices, 812 credit and debit adjustments, 820 remittances), on-time and in-full fines (Walmart charges 3% of the cost of goods on cases that miss, search summary) and deductions taken straight from payments: shortages, compliance fines, allowances, defective allowances, markdown money and post-audit claims raised years later by recovery auditors such as PRGX. The wholesaler is the victim on its own invoices and, unlike a maker, must also pass valid charges back to the suppliers that caused them under its own vendor agreements. D&H Distributing told iNymbus it handles thousands of deductions a month from shipping direct to stores. Dispute windows run from days to months depending on the retailer and the charge (search summary), so unresearched claims simply age out.

The independent channel runs on different rails. Gift, toy, outdoor, pet and book wholesalers sell to tens of thousands of small stores through rep groups, regional markets and shows, and now Faire, which lists 100,000+ brands, gives eligible retailers 60-day terms and charges brands 15% plus $10 on a first order. Credit on net terms is extended store by store. Books are returnable (the AAP put the Q1 2026 return rate at 15%, search summary); Baker & Taylor shut down in January 2026 after its sale to ReaderLink collapsed, with Ingram expected to take much of its business. Alliance Entertainment, a public wholesaler of physical media, games and collectibles, sold $1.149B in fiscal 2026 across 340,000+ SKUs to 35,000+ retail locations, with its largest customer at ~21% of sales and supplier price protection and return privileges limited to each programme's window.

## Current Tech Landscape
Operations run on mid-market ERPs (NetSuite, Acumatica, Microsoft Dynamics, SAP Business One, Infor, Sage). EDI goes through networks such as SPS Commerce ($751.5M of 2025 revenue, ~54,600 recurring revenue customers; it bought SupplyPike for $205.8M in 2024 and Carbon6 in 2025 to sell deduction recovery), TrueCommerce, Cleo and Orderful. Deduction platforms (iNymbus, HighRadius, Vividly, SPS Revenue Recovery and the AI entrants Glimpse and Confido) research and dispute claims. Sell-through comes from retailer portals (Walmart Retail Link and Luminate, Target's partner portal) and harmonisers such as Crisp, Alloy.ai and SPS Analytics. Product content goes out through Salsify, Syndigo and 1WorldSync, rep orders through RepSpark, Pepperi, NuORDER and Brandwise, and independent stores order on Faire. Customs brokers and trade-content databases handle entries. AI order-intake vendors read emailed purchase orders into ERPs; that field is profiled once at [[niches/industrial-distributors/rfq-and-order-intake-automation/profile|RFQ & Order Intake Automation]]. Each tool covers its own step. No tool holds the item record end to end: what the merchant bought and how deep, at what landed cost, and what it netted after sell-through, deductions, markdown money and closeout.

## Analysis
The central unmeasured judgement in consumer goods wholesale is the item bet: which items to import, how deep, at what landed cost, and for which retailers. It is made months ahead on a merchant's feel, and its outcome arrives in pieces that are never joined: sell-through in retailer portals under each retailer's supplier terms, deductions and markdown money on remittances months later, and closeout recovery on a separate ledger. Three things block fixing it. The outcome data is largely the retailers', the merchant's reasoning is never recorded, and the operators are thin-margin and mostly small, so as insight buyers they fail the Pass 2 gate. The strongest commercial opening is the vendor field that sells into them, and above all the deduction work. The wholesaler is hit twice there, by retailers on its own invoices and by the need to re-bill its suppliers, and the funded AI vendors are hiring now. On their own boards on 29 October 2026, Glimpse (New York; a16z-led $35M Series A announced on 25 March 2026) listed a Software Engineer, a Senior Software Engineer and a Founding Forward Deployment Engineer, and Confido (New York) listed forward deployed and machine-learning engineers. Among the order-intake vendors, none of them consumer-goods-specific, Endeavor (Chicago; custom AI for distributors and manufacturers broadly) listed a Forward Deployed Engineer, Early Career (posted 2026-07-07) and a Deployment Strategist (2026-07-23), and SalesPatriot (San Francisco; aerospace and defence quoting, an adjacent field) a Forward Deployed Engineer (2026-05-25) and a Lead Forward Deployed Engineer (2026-10-10). Paragon (San Francisco; industrial distributors broadly) listed only an undated Founding Engineer role, not a forward deployed one, and Orderful's board had no openings. A two-week demo that triages X12 812 and 820 deductions and reads 850 or PDF purchase orders into a drafted dispute packet fits this buyer exactly.
