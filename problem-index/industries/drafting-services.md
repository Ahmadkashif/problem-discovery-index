# Drafting Services

## Profile
**Category:** Professional Services
**NAICS 2022:** 541340 — Drafting Services
**Market Size:** ~$1.68B in 2022 receipts at 2,956 US drafting-services firms with 9,178 employees (Census Bureau, Statistics of U.S. Businesses 2022), 85% of them with fewer than five employees; offshore bureaus in India, the Philippines, Vietnam and Eastern Europe serving US clients take US-serving outsourced drafting and detailing spend to an estimated ~$5B (estimate)
**Tech Maturity:** Medium — the authoring tools (AutoCAD, Revit, Tekla Structures, SDS2) are sophisticated, but job intake, quoting, markup interpretation, checking and revision tracking still run on scanned PDFs, email, spreadsheets and the eye of one senior checker.
**Workforce:** CAD drafters and BIM modellers, structural steel and rebar detailers, senior detailers who check every sheet before it leaves (NISD Individual Detailer Certification distinguishes Senior Detailer Class I from Detailer Class II), project coordinators who log client markups and revision rounds, and offshore delivery managers who bridge US clients and overnight production teams. The US has about 180,200 drafters (BLS, 2025), but only a sliver work in bureaus: 49% sit inside architecture and engineering firms, 21% in manufacturing and 13% in construction.

## Key Pain Themes
A drafting bureau sells drawings, not design. It takes someone else's intent — an architect's red-marked set, an engineer's design drawings, a fabricator's model, an inventor's sketch — and turns it into a drawing set or shop drawings at a per-sheet, per-ton or hourly price. It carries no design liability: under AIA A201-2017 §3.12.4 shop drawings are not Contract Documents, the architect's review under §4.2.7 is for design intent only, and the engineer of record owns the structure. It does carry workmanship accountability. A steel detailer whose hole pattern is wrong learns about it when the ironworkers cannot bolt a beam, and the fabricator back-charges the field fix. Per-sheet quotes typically include one or two revision rounds (vendor-published), so every unclear markup and every design change that arrives mid-job comes out of a margin the bureau set before it saw the work.

The external clocks belong to other people. AISC 303 §4.4 lets the owner's side keep steel shop drawings for 14 calendar days and assumes the fabricator's schedule around that; rebar placing drawings under ACI 315 must be approved before bars are bent, release by release, ahead of each pour; trade submittals run on the general contractor's submittal register; residential plans wait in the building department's queue; patent figures must clear 37 CFR 1.84 inside the filing or response period. Offshore delivery adds its own clock: markups sent at the end of the US day come back finished the next morning, which means the drafter in Pune or Manila works through the night on instructions they cannot ask anyone about. The binding control on quality is a person, the checker, who reads every sheet against the design and the client's standards. Contractor pressure for fast submittals pushes fabricators to force detailers to submit drawings before they are checked, and the errors that escape show up weeks later as a rejected submittal, an RFI or a field fix.

The structure explains why so little of this is measured. The US industry is 2,956 firms averaging about three employees, with ~$182,600 of receipts per employee; only 65 firms have twenty or more people. The volume work has moved to offshore enterprises — Pinnacle Infotech self-reports more than 3,400 staff, and fabricators such as Canam run their own detailing teams in Romania, India and the Philippines — which compete at ~$8–35 an hour against ~$75–175 at established US firms (vendor-published ranges). BLS expects CAD and BIM to keep reducing drafter employment, and AI tools now aim at the per-sheet unit itself.

## Current Tech Landscape
AutoCAD and AutoCAD LT remain the default for 2D drafting and permit sets; Revit and Archicad for BIM production; Tekla Structures, SDS2 and Advance Steel for steel detailing, with Qnect automating connection design inside Tekla; aSa Rebar and RebarCAD for placing drawings and bar lists; Autodesk Fabrication and Revit MEP for duct and pipe spools; SolidWorks and Inventor for mechanical part drawings. Bluebeam Revu is the markup surface on both sides of the transaction, and Procore or Autodesk Construction Cloud carries the submittal once it leaves. Quality tooling is thin: Tekla clash checks, AutoCAD's Standards checker, Ideate add-ins for Revit, and new AI checkers such as ClearHandoff, which reviews steel shop-drawing PDFs at about fifty cents a sheet. SWAPP, which raised an $11.5M Series A in 2023, generates construction documents inside Revit and claims up to 80% automation of documentation tasks. Each tool works inside its boundary. The record that links a drafting decision to its consequence — the rejected submittal, the RFI, the field fix, the back-charge — sits in the fabricator's, the general contractor's or the architect's system, and the bureau that drew the sheet rarely sees it.

## Analysis
The industry's central unmeasured judgement is the senior checker's sense of which sheets, connections and dimensions will fail downstream — at the engineer's review, in the fabrication shop or at erection. It is exercised by one or two people per bureau, under deadline, and never graded, because the outcome surfaces in records the bureau does not hold: the fabricator's back-charge log, the general contractor's submittal register, the architect's review stamps. Three things block a fix. Structurally, the feedback loop crosses organisations and the client owns the drawings, often under NDA. Economically, labour arbitrage has always been cheaper than tooling: adding an offshore checker costs less than building a system. Organisationally, the US operator is a two- or three-person shop with no one whose job is quality. The commercial opening is therefore with the buyers and aggregators of detailing at volume — steel and rebar fabricators, MEP subcontractors with prefabrication shops, and the large offshore enterprises — rather than with the small bureaus, which are poor buyers and the most exposed to AI that generates the sheets directly. In the insight layer no pocket clears the Pass 2 bar: the value chain around drafting is production, not research. The nearest miss is delegated steel connection design engineering, and the one strong insight business touching this chain, construction project lead and plan data, is indexed under [[industries/electrical-contractors|Electrical Contractors]]. The boundaries with [[industries/architecture-firms|Architecture Firms]] and [[industries/engineering-consultants|Engineering Consultants]], who own and stamp the design, and with [[industries/metal-fabrication|Metal Fabrication]], whose shops build from the detailer's drawings, hold throughout.
