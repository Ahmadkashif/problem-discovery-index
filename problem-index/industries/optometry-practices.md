# Optometry Practices

## Profile
**Category:** Healthcare & Clinical
**NAICS 2022:** 621320 — Offices of Optometrists (OD practices doing routine eye exams, contact lens fitting and medical eye care, with optical dispensing on the side; optical shops whose main work is selling eyewear, and medical-supply retail, sit in other industries)
**Market Size:** Offices of optometrists took in $18.2B in 2022, up from $16.1B in 2018 (Census Service Annual Survey, employer firms). Statistics of US Businesses counts 18,708 firms and 22,781 establishments with $20.7B of receipts in 2022. 9,076 of those firms have fewer than five employees, and the 45 firms with 500 or more employees run 1,626 offices and take 13% of receipts (Census SUSB 2022). Vision plans steer much of the volume: VSP alone paid $4.8B to private optometry practices on 21.4M claims from 14.4M members in 2024 (VSP Vision)
**Tech Maturity:** Medium — every practice runs an optometry EHR and practice management system (Compulink, Eyefinity OfficeMate and Encompass, RevolutionEHR, Crystal PM, MaximEyes) and a lane of imaging devices, yet benefits are checked one plan portal at a time, the routine-versus-medical billing call is made by hand at checkout, and the optometrist's judgement on a suspicious optic nerve is recorded only as a follow-up interval.
**Workforce:** 151,610 employed in NAICS 621320 (BLS OEWS, May 2023): dispensing opticians (32,000), optometrists (24,950, mean pay $131,590), ophthalmic medical technicians (20,200), receptionists (16,600), medical assistants (11,860), medical secretaries (9,440), office supervisors (5,460), billing and posting clerks (4,960) and ophthalmic lab technicians (2,290). Self-employed owner optometrists are not in that count.

## Key Pain Themes
The optometrist's most consequential judgement is made in a few seconds at the slit lamp, and almost none of it is written down as judgement. An experienced OD looks at an optic nerve, its OCT printout and a visual field and decides whether this is a large but healthy cup, a glaucoma suspect to watch, early glaucoma to treat, or a referral to an ophthalmologist this week. The OCT's normative database flags red on many healthy myopic eyes, and visual fields are noisy, so the device does not make the call. The OD does, from pattern recognition they cannot fully specify. A newer OD with the same printouts over-refers, under-treats or books the wrong interval. The outcome arrives years later, as progression caught late or as a referral the ophthalmologist sends straight back. Owners are selling to private-equity platforms, so this judgement also moves with the doctor. MyEyeDr. announced on 29 September 2026 that it will buy EyeCare Partners' 300-plus optometry offices.

Around the exam sits the payer split that defines the business. A routine exam and refraction are billed to a vision plan (VSP, EyeMed, Davis and Superior under Versant, UnitedHealthcare's Spectera) for a fixed fee, with copays and frame and lens allowances. The same visit becomes a medical visit, billed to Medicare or a medical plan with diagnosis-driven tests, if the patient reports a problem or the doctor finds disease. Medicare excludes routine eye exams, refractions and most eyeglasses by statute (Social Security Act §1862(a)(7); 42 CFR 411.15). Front desks check eligibility on separate portals each morning, and checkout staff decide which plan to bill, often against plan rules on mixing the two. Independent practices kept a median 31% of collected revenue for owner pay and profit in 2025, with cost of goods at 26% and non-OD staff at 24% (IDOC benchmarks).

External clocks are growing. Under the FTC's Contact Lens Rule, practices must release prescriptions and keep confirmations for three years. They have eight business hours to answer a seller's verification request before the prescription counts as verified, and FTC staff sent 37 warning letters to prescribers in June 2025 (penalties up to $53,088 per violation). CMS grades Medicare Advantage plans on the diabetic eye exam Star measure, which became administrative-only in 2025 (search summary), so an exam counts only if its result reaches the plan as a code. Just 66.0% of adults with diabetes reported an annual eye exam in 2023, against a 70.3% target (Healthy People 2030). Scope is expanding too: 14 states now let optometrists perform lasers such as SLT and YAG capsulotomy (Optometric Management, 2026).

## Current Tech Landscape
Compulink, Eyefinity (VSP's OfficeMate and Encompass), RevolutionEHR (now part of PracticeTek), Crystal PM, MaximEyes and Nextech run charts, schedules and claims. Scribing has become a free feature: RevolutionEHR added AI Scribe at no extra cost in April 2026, and Eyefinity ships EncompassScribe. Benefits and claims run through Eyefinity for VSP and through each plan's portal, with Anagram selling a multi-plan layer. Weave and Solutionreach handle reminders and texts. Imaging comes from Zeiss, Topcon (Harmony), Heidelberg, Optos and iCare, each with its own review software. Autonomous diabetic retinopathy AI (Digital Diagnostics' LumineticsCore, Eyenuk's EyeArt, AEYE Health) bills CPT 92229, mostly in primary care. Myopia control arrives as products (CooperVision MiSight, Essilor Stellest, orthokeratology) rather than software. Each tool covers its step. None holds the longitudinal record that joins what the optometrist saw on a nerve or retina, and decided, to whether that eye later progressed. None knows which plan should have paid for the visit.

## Analysis
The industry's central unmeasured judgement is the optometrist's read of an optic nerve or retina: who is a suspect, who is progressing, and who needs a specialist now. It decides whether glaucoma is caught at the first field change or the third. Three things block a fix. Feedback is slow, because progression shows over years and often in an ophthalmologist's chart. Labels are unstable, because experts disagree on cup-to-disc ratios and OCT normative flags mislead on myopic eyes. And practices are small: 9,076 of 18,708 firms have fewer than five staff, so few can buy clinical AI, while the rollups are still integrating offices. The payer side is more tractable and more mechanical. Routing each visit to the right plan, checking benefits before the visit and getting diabetic exams credited by health plans are rule-bound and run by front-desk staff. For the owner's placement goal the evidence is thin. On 25 October 2026 no optometry-specific vendor had a forward deployed engineering role open. VSP Vision, which owns Eyefinity, was hiring a Director, AI Platforms & Engineering (remote US). Weave, which sells to dental, optometry and veterinary offices, had speech and GenAI machine-learning roles (remote US and India). 1-800 Contacts had software engineers in Draper, Utah, Optos a senior software engineer in Dunfermline, Scotland, and AEYE Health engineering and AI research roles in Tel Aviv. Digital Diagnostics, Eyenuk, RevolutionEHR, MaximEyes, Topcon Healthcare and Anagram listed none. The desk score's employer factor of 3 looks generous, and 2 fits. Under the Pass 2 rubric no pocket qualifies. The nearest are the ophthalmic CROs and reading centres (46), which sell trial endpoints but on sponsor-owned data, and the vision plans (42), which hold claims for most insured Americans behind HIPAA (see the niche overview).
