# Colleges & Universities

## Profile
**Category:** Education
**NAICS 2022:** 611310 — Colleges, Universities, and Professional Schools
**Market Size:** ~$329B in receipts at 2,494 private institutions and firms in 2022 (Census SUSB), plus public universities outside that count: state-government-owned establishments employed ~1.76M people in 2024 (BLS QCEW). Total public and private institutional spending is ~$700B a year (estimate). Universities also spent $117.7B on research in FY2024, 55% of it federal (NSF HERD).
**Tech Maturity:** Medium — every institution runs an ERP or student information system (Ellucian Banner or Colleague, Workday Student, Oracle PeopleSoft Campus Solutions), an admissions CRM and a learning management system, but the decisions that set revenue still run on spreadsheets and committees: the aid offer, the class size, the stop-out list and the research budget.
**Workforce:** Admissions counsellors and application readers, financial aid administrators and counsellors, registrars and transfer credit evaluators, academic advisors, institutional researchers, sponsored programs and research administrators, assessment and accreditation staff, enrollment and finance vice presidents, and the faculty and principal investigators they serve

## Key Pain Themes
A four-year college sells one product at hundreds of prices. Private nonprofits now return, on average, a projected 57.1% of first-year gross tuition revenue as institutional grant aid (NACUBO, 2025-26). Average net tuition revenue per undergraduate fell 1.9% after inflation in 2024-25 (NACUBO, via Higher Ed Dive), and private nonprofit undergraduate enrollment fell 1.6% in fall 2025 (National Student Clearinghouse). Every admitted student gets an aid offer built from a leveraging grid and adjusted on appeal by an aid director's judgement. Whether each extra dollar changed the decision is never measured. The pipeline is shrinking: WICHE projects US high school graduates peaked at 3.9 million in 2025 and fall 13% to 3.4 million by 2041. All three rating agencies started 2026 with negative or deteriorating sector outlooks.

The money runs on outside clocks. The FAFSA opened early for 2026-27, after the botched 2024-25 rollout delayed award letters across the country. The One Big Beautiful Bill Act ends Grad PLUS for new borrowers and caps graduate, professional and Parent PLUS loans from 1 July 2026, which reprices every graduate programme. Research universities spent 2025 and 2026 fighting an NIH cap of 15% on indirect costs, enjoined and upheld on appeal in January 2026 (search summary), while also handling grant terminations and new award terms. Accreditation reviews run on multi-year cycles, and six southern public systems have launched a new accreditor (search summary). IPEDS, Common Data Set and rankings surveys each want the same numbers on different dates.

The people in the middle carry it by hand. Aid counsellors chase verification documents and special-circumstance appeals through the spring deposit season. Advisors with several hundred students each get early-alert flags they cannot work through. Transfer evaluators match course descriptions to catalogues one transcript at a time; GAO estimated transfer students lost 43% of their credits. Research administrators re-read award terms after every policy change.

## Current Tech Landscape
Ellucian (about 3,000 customers in 50 countries, by its own count) dominates the ERP and SIS layer and is modernizing Banner and Colleague customers to SaaS. Workday Student and Oracle compete for large migrations. Slate, Salesforce Education Cloud and Element451 run admissions CRM. EAB (Enroll360, Navigate360, Edify), Ruffalo Noel Levitz and Encoura sell enrollment strategy and student success. Watermark and Anthology hold planning, assessment and accreditation workflows. Huron, Kuali and Cayuse run research administration. CollegeVine and Element451 deploy AI agents across admissions, aid and student services. Each tool is competent within its boundary. No tool owns the record that joins an aid offer to whether the student enrolled, stayed and graduated. Nor does any join a research award's terms to the cost the university actually recovered. Neighbouring work already in the vault includes [[industries/k12-private-schools|K-12 Private Schools]] (enrolment and aid at the school level), [[industries/vocational-schools|Vocational Schools]] (Title IV and accreditation for career schools), [[industries/grant-writers|Grant Writers]] (university research development offices) and [[industries/libraries-and-archives|Libraries & Archives]] (academic licensing and bibliometrics).

## Analysis
The industry's central unmeasured judgement is price: what each admitted student must be offered to enroll and stay. The outcome data is complete, because every deposit, melt, stop-out and graduation is recorded, but the counterfactual is missing. Aid directors' adjustments on appeal live in notes and memory, and the consultancies that set leveraging grids hold cross-campus admit and aid records. The blocks are legal and organisational. Applicants and admits who never enroll are not FERPA students, so their records rest on contracts, but the retention and graduation outcomes that grade an aid decision belong to enrolled students, and FERPA lets a vendor use those only for the purpose the college disclosed them for. Committees buy slowly. Enrollment, aid and finance each own a piece of the decision. The operators are poor buyers of an insight engine. Their research is internal, procurement runs through committees, and the data-rich pockets around them (enrolment consultancies for their enrolled-student half, SIS and retention vendors, the Clearinghouse) hold FERPA records under contract. No Pass 2 pocket qualifies. The commercial opening is the vendors selling into colleges. Ellucian, CollegeVine, Element451, Stellic and EAB all listed engineering, forward deployed or data roles on their own boards on 2026-10-24 (CollegeVine's forward deployed role dates from November 2025). A public-data MVP on IPEDS, College Scorecard, NSF HERD and EMMA, or a synthetic-record aid agent, is the fastest proof to put in front of them.
