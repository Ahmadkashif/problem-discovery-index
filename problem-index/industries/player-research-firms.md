# Player Research Firms

## Profile
**Category:** Gaming & Interactive
**Market Size:** ~$600M US in games user research — specialist agencies, playtesting platforms and the in-house research functions at large studios and publishers
**Tech Maturity:** Rigorous method, no outcome loop. Games user research is a genuine discipline with established practice — moderated sessions, think-aloud protocols, task analysis, benchmark studies, telemetry-informed design — and the firms running it deliver findings into development teams and almost never learn whether the finding was acted on or whether the change improved anything.
**Workforce:** User researchers and research leads, session moderators and coordinators, participant recruiters, data analysts working across qualitative and telemetry sources, research operations staff

## Key Pain Themes
The research firm's product is a finding, and the evidence about whether the finding was right lives in the game team's telemetry after a change nobody tells the researcher about. A firm may run hundreds of studies a year across dozens of titles and hold no record of which recommendations were implemented, which were ignored, and what happened either way — which means the accumulated experience of the field is anecdote rather than evidence.

The second theme is the sample. Sessions are expensive, so studies run with small participant counts, and the conclusions are expressed with a confidence the numbers do not support. The discipline knows this and manages it through methodological care; the reporting into development teams is frequently more definite than the evidence, because a hedged finding does not get acted on.

The third is the labour of analysis. A study produces hours of video, think-aloud audio, observer notes and telemetry, and turning that into findings is manual coding performed under deadline. It is the largest cost in a study and the part most compressed when the schedule tightens, which is exactly where the quality of the finding is determined.

## Current Tech Landscape
Playtesting platforms — PlaytestCloud and its peers — provide recruited participants, recorded sessions and basic instrumentation at a scale in-house labs cannot match. Specialist agencies including the games research arms of larger groups supply moderated and expert-led studies. Telemetry platforms from GameAnalytics through to studio-internal stacks supply the quantitative side, usually analysed separately by a different team. Survey and motivation frameworks are established. Video analysis is manual, supported by general-purpose qualitative coding tools that were not built for gameplay footage.

## Analysis
This is the clearest instance in the gaming cluster of the pattern that runs through the whole segment: a firm whose entire product is insight, delivering it across the boundary of an organisation that observes the outcome and does not return it. The consequence is unusually sharp here because the field is methodologically serious — researchers know what evidence would settle their questions and cannot obtain it. Joining findings to subsequent telemetry is technically straightforward, requires only a data-sharing arrangement most clients would grant if asked, and would convert a discipline that accumulates anecdote into one that accumulates evidence about what kinds of finding actually predict what players do.
