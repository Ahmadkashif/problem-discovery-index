# Digital BPO Operations

## Profile
**Category:** Platform Labour & Digital Work
**Market Size:** ~$90B US spend on outsourced customer support and digital back-office operations; Teleperformance, Concentrix, TaskUs, Alorica, TTEC and Foundever operate globally with workforces in the hundreds of thousands
**Tech Maturity:** Highly instrumented workforce, weakly instrumented outcomes. Contact centre platforms measure handle time, adherence, occupancy, after-call work and schedule conformance to the second. What proportion of contacts actually resolved the customer's problem, and what the client's business gained, is measured by a sampled quality review and a satisfaction survey.
**Workforce:** Support agents and back-office processors, predominantly offshore and nearshore; team leaders and quality analysts; workforce management planners; training and operations staff

## Key Pain Themes
The metrics govern the work and measure the wrong thing. Average handle time, adherence and occupancy describe how an agent spends their seconds; resolution describes whether the customer's problem went away. Those pull in opposite directions on precisely the contacts that matter — a genuinely complex issue resolved properly takes longer and prevents a repeat contact that nobody attributes back to the first call.

Generative tooling has changed the composition of the remaining work and not the targets set against it. Straightforward contacts are increasingly deflected or resolved automatically, which means what reaches a human is harder, longer and more emotionally difficult on average — while handle time targets calibrated on the old mix persist. Agents experience this as targets becoming impossible for reasons nobody has acknowledged.

The third theme is that quality is judged on a sampled fraction. A small percentage of contacts are scored against a rubric by a quality analyst, and the score determines coaching, bonuses and sometimes continued employment. Sampling error at that rate is large, the rubric rewards script adherence over problem-solving, and agents know both.

## Current Tech Landscape
Contact centre platforms from Genesys, NICE, Five9, Amazon Connect and Twilio handle routing, recording and measurement. Workforce management from NICE, Verint and Calabrio forecasts volume and builds schedules. Quality management tooling supports sampled review and increasingly automated scoring across the full contact volume. Agent assist and generative summarisation have been deployed widely and quickly. Analytics products measure sentiment and compliance phrase detection. Back-office processing runs on client systems with robotic process automation layered over the gaps.

## Analysis
This industry has the most comprehensively measured workforce in the digital economy and one of the least measured outputs. Every second of an agent's shift is recorded and the question the client is buying — did the customer's problem get solved, and did they come back — is estimated from a two percent sample and a survey with a low and self-selected response rate. The full contact record now supports automated resolution assessment across every interaction, which would replace sampling with measurement and would also expose that handle time targets and resolution pull in opposite directions. That tension is the central fact of the industry and it has been managed rather than confronted, because the commercial contracts are written on the metrics that are easy to measure.
