# IT Distributors & Value-Added Resellers

## Profile
**Category:** Wholesale & Distribution
**NAICS 2022:** 423430 — Computer and Computer Peripheral Equipment and Software Merchant Wholesalers
**Market Size:** ~$416B in 2022 receipts across 5,105 firms and 6,818 establishments (Census SUSB 2022); 126 enterprises with 500+ employees took ~$322B (~77%), while 4,062 firms under 20 employees (~80% of firms) took ~$13.6B (~3%). The leaders run on thin margins: TD SYNNEX reported FY2025 revenue of $62.5B at a ~7.0% gross margin and ~2.3% operating margin, Ingram Micro $52.6B at 6.67%, and CDW, one of the largest US resellers, $22.4B at 21.7%. Many VARs file under computer systems design (541512) instead, so the code undercounts resellers.
**Tech Maturity:** Medium — the distributors run large digital platforms (Ingram Micro says the majority of its net sales now flow through Xvantage) and EDI links to every vendor, while the reseller's own quote is still assembled by hand from vendor configurator exports, distributor price files and spreadsheets, and deal registrations are filed one vendor portal at a time.
**Workforce:** Account managers and inside sales reps, quoting specialists, pre-sales solution architects, licensing and renewals specialists, deal-desk and vendor-programme staff, rebate and MDF analysts, public-sector contracts administrators, integration-centre technicians who image and kit devices, and field engineers delivering attached services. BLS QCEW counted 227,038 private employees in 423430 in 2024 at $196,068 average annual pay, down 3.5% from 2023; Texas and California lead.

## Key Pain Themes
A reseller earns its margin on the quote. A data-centre or networking deal starts as a customer requirement and ends as a bill of materials of dozens to hundreds of lines: chassis, line cards, optics, cables, power cords, rails, software tiers, support contracts and the reseller's own services. The vendor's configurator (Cisco Commerce Workspace, HPE's configurators, Dell's sales tools) checks that the parts are valid together; it does not check that they fit the customer's racks, switches, power and existing licences. That check is done by a senior pre-sales engineer who has seen enough installs to know what goes missing, and when it fails the result is a change order, a return or a field engineer on site without the right optic, absorbed on a deal whose gross margin may be single digits. On the same quote the price depends on the vendor: a registered deal earns extra discount, a special-pricing request to the vendor's deal desk can earn more, and the reseller is guessing what the deal desk will approve and what the competing reseller will bid.

The second clock is the vendors' programme calendar, which changes the economics of deals already in flight. Microsoft announced a global Microsoft 365 price increase effective 1 July 2026, applied at each customer's next renewal, then from 1 October 2026 introduced growth margins whose eligibility is set per customer tenant and locked at the transaction, so it has to be checked before every quote. Broadcom raised VMware's minimum order from 16 to 72 cores from April 2025, according to a notice Arrow sent partners, with a 20% penalty on late renewals, and stopped renewing European cloud-provider partners after 26 January 2026. Cisco replaced its partner programme with Cisco 360 on 25 January 2026, consolidating rebates into one Cisco Partner Incentive. Every change is a renewal to re-quote, a rebate rule to re-learn and a margin assumption to re-check, and the back-office money it governs is large: TD SYNNEX carried $972.7M of receivables from vendors at the end of FY2025.

Public-sector buying runs on contract vehicles and government clocks. NASA's SEWP VI named 2,115 contract winners and opens for orders on 1 November 2026; GSA's OneGov strategy is moving OEMs into the prime-contractor seat that resellers held; and the FCC's E-rate programme has a FY2026 cap of $5.2B, with USAC estimating $1.814B of category-two demand for the switches, wireless and firewalls resellers sell to schools and libraries. The Form 471 window closed on 1 April 2026, and an invoice USAC reduces or denies is the reseller's loss.

## Current Tech Landscape
Distributors' platforms (Ingram Micro Xvantage, TD SYNNEX's StreamOne and ION, Pax8 and AppDirect for cloud subscriptions) publish price, availability and ordering. Resellers quote in channel CPQ tools (ConnectWise CPQ, Kaseya's Quote Manager, QuoteWerks, ScalePad's Quoter, Salesbuildr, Quote.ly) or in a PSA or ERP, after copying lines out of each vendor's configurator. Deal registration and partner programmes run through vendor portals and PRM platforms (Impartner, Zift, ChannelScaler, formerly Allbound and Channel Mechanics); vendors collect partner point-of-sale data through channel data managers such as E2open (which owns Zyme), Model N, Vistex and CMR; licence optimisation is sold by Flexera, CoreView and others. Each tool does its own step. The record no tool owns is the deal's line-level economics end to end: what was configured, what the vendor approved, what shipped and was installed, and what was claimed back.

## Analysis
The central unmeasured judgement in IT resale is the deal call on a complex quote: is this bill of materials complete for this customer's site, and what price will the vendor approve that still wins? It decides whether a thin-margin deal makes money, and it is made by a few senior engineers and account managers who learned it from installs and deal desks. Three things block fixing it. The record is split by design: each vendor holds its own registration and special-pricing history, the distributor holds cost and availability, and the reseller holds quotes without a clean link to what was installed or returned. The rules move monthly, so last quarter's labels go stale. And the large resellers who could fund the work (CDW, SHI, Insight, WWT, Presidio) build in-house, while four in five firms are too small to staff it. As insight buyers the operators fail the Pass 2 gate, and so does the insight layer around them, mostly on whose data it is. For an engineer looking for a placement, the honest finding is that no IT-channel quoting, CPQ or channel-data startup was found hiring engineers on its own page (checked 2026-10-24); the only US engineering roles found are at Flexera (home-based, licence optimisation), Pax8 (remote, a cloud distributor) and Ventura's generic founding engineer for distributor quoting (San Francisco), with AppDirect hiring in Canada only, so the short-term placement filter's desk employer score of 3 for this industry looks generous. None of the distributor AI-quoting vendors profiled under [[industries/industrial-distributors|Industrial Distributors]] mentions IT resellers or distributors on its page.
