## Near-misses and downgrades worth remembering

Pockets that scored 50+ on a builder's first pass and were cut on review, so the next batches calibrate against them:

- **Building & Fire Code Consultants** (`architecture-firms`, 51 → 49): Q4 cut to 4, because the clock is the owner's permit schedule, not a deadline any third party sets on the consultant.
- **Multi-Client Seismic Data Libraries** (`geophysical-survey-firms`, 52 → 48): Q2 cut to 3, because licensing an owned library earns without adding analysts (Pulse Seismic runs on 15 staff), and Q5 cut to 2 for a five-firm market.
- **Research Security & Foreign-Influence Due Diligence** (`contract-research-labs`, 51 → 49): Q5 cut to 3. The buyer is the provider's research head, and the providers are Strider plus a handful.

- **Drug Pipeline & Trial Intelligence** (`biotech-research-firms`, 51 → 49): Q5 cut to 2, because the market is three or four providers (Norstella, Clarivate, GlobalData, BioCentury).
- **PR Measurement & Media Analysis** (`public-relations-agencies`, 51 → 49): Q5 cut to 3, because the pocket is Cision, Onclusive and Meltwater plus a few small firms. Size was restated from whole-company to analyst counts.

- **Invalidity & PTAB Prior-Art Search Houses** (`legal-support-services`, 51 → kill switch at 49): searches commissioned through litigation counsel are client work product. Q5 was cut to 3 for a field of ~10–20 houses.
