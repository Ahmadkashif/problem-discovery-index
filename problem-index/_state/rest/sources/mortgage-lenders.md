# Sources — Mortgage Lenders

## Checked
- State-licensed mortgage companies originated ~$1 trillion in loans in 2024, 59% of US mortgage originations; over 550,000 MLOs maintain licences or registrations through NMLS; NMLS used by nearly 597,000 companies and individuals in 2024 — Virginia SCC report on the operations of State Regulatory Registry LLC (NMLS), 2025 — https://rga.lis.virginia.gov/Published/2025/RD359/PDF (accessed 2026-10-22)
- MBA forecast (October 2026): single-family originations ~$2.1T projected for 2026 (5.3M loans), falling ~5% to just under $2T in 2027 (5.0M loans); purchase $1.42T and refinance $571B in 2027; 30-year mortgage rate forecast to average 7.3% in Q4 2026 and stay above 7% through 2027 — https://themortgagepoint.com/2026/10/14/mortgage-originations-projected-to-decline-in-2027/ and https://www.mba.org/news-and-research/newsroom/news/2026/10/11/mba-forecast--mortgage-originations-to-decrease-5-percent-in-2027-as-mortgage-rates-remain-above-7-percent (accessed 2026-10-22)
- ~4,898 HMDA filers for data year 2024 (banks, credit unions and nonbanks) — https://www.consumerfinance.gov/about-us/newsroom/2024-hmda-data-on-mortgage-lending-now-available/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Independent mortgage companies originated 61.9% of closed-end home purchase loans and 64.3% of closed-end refinance loans in 2023 (CFPB 2023 Mortgage Market Activity and Trends) — https://files.consumerfinance.gov/f/documents/cfpb_2023-mortgage-market-activity-and-trends_2024-12.pdf (accessed 2026-10-22)
- MBA Annual Mortgage Bankers Performance Report (IMBs and mortgage subsidiaries of chartered banks): net production income $785 per loan (21 bps) in 2025, up from $443 in 2024; production revenue $11,879 per loan; production expenses $11,094 per loan; net servicing financial income $89 per loan (from $301); 78% of firms profitable on a pre-tax basis (64% without servicing); 2025 quarterly path included a $28 loss per loan in Q1 and a $1,201 profit in Q3 — https://www.mpamag.com/us/mortgage-industry/market-updates/independent-mortgage-bankers-see-profits-rebound-but-pressure-on-costs-stays-high/572015 and https://x.com/MBAMortgage/status/1990845044388241504 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- FSOC 2024 Report on Nonbank Mortgage Servicing: nonbank mortgage companies originated ~two-thirds of US mortgages in 2022 and owned servicing rights on 54% of mortgage balances; NMC share of Agency servicing rose from 35% to 66% between January 2014 and January 2024; NMCs serviced ~$6T for the Agencies in 2023; ~235 warehouse lenders extended facilities with ~$285B in total limits at Q4 2023; the average NMC had warehouse lines from ~9.5 creditors; warehouse lenders typically finance 90–95% of loan value; MSRs are 10–30% of NMC assets — https://home.treasury.gov/system/files/261/FSOC-2024-Nonbank-Mortgage-Servicing-Report.pdf (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Fannie Mae Selling Guide D1-3-01 (04/01/2026): post-closing QC loans selected at least monthly; random sample of at least 10% of loans originated or acquired (or a statistical sample at 95% confidence and 2% precision) plus discretionary selections; selection, review, rebuttal and reporting completed within 90 days (shortened from 120, mandatory from September 1, 2023) — https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/d1-3-01/lender-post-closing-quality-control-review-process (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Fannie Mae Selling Guide B3-3.2-01: lender must analyse "the stability of the borrower's income", "the ability of the business to continue generating and distributing sufficient income", measure year-to-year trends for gross income, expenses and taxable income, complete Form 1084 or an equivalent cash flow analysis, and give "careful consideration" to borrowers with under two years of self-employment — https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b3-3.2-01/underwriting-factors-and-documentation-self-employed-borrower (accessed 2026-10-22)
- No fixed threshold for an acceptable decline in self-employed income; "it's up to the judgment of the underwriter approving the loan" — https://mymortgageinsider.com/calculating-self-employed-income-for-mortgage/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- ACES Mortgage QC Industry Trends: overall critical defect rate averaged 1.50% in CY2025 (1.52% in 2024) and fell to 1.38% in Q4 2025; income/employment rose to 27.24% of critical defects in Q3 2025, the top category — https://www.housingwire.com/articles/aces-mortgage-defects-q4/ and https://www.acesquality.com/resources/reports/q3-2025-aces-mortgage-qc-industry-trends (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Reggora and STRATMOR study (April 2023–October 2024): average repurchase rate 0.49%; ~$32,288 cost per repurchase request; appraisal and income issues together 57% of requests — https://www.scotsmanguide.com/news/heres-how-much-each-loan-repurchase-request-costs-lenders/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Fannie Mae reintroduced a Notice of Potential Defect process in February 2024 with a 30-calendar-day window to cure or respond; Freddie Mac fee-in-lieu-of-repurchase option for performing loans with minor defects from Q1 2025 — https://nationalmortgageprofessional.com/news/fannie-mae-implements-notice-potential-defect-process-address-loan-repurchase-risks and https://www.mpamag.com/us/news/general/fhfa-unveils-a-series-of-policy-updates-for-fannie-mae-and-freddie-mac/511741 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Urban Institute: GSE repurchase rate 0.24% overall for 2018–23 originations, 0.16% for large nonbank issuers, 0.35% for smaller issuers — https://www.urban.org/sites/default/files/2023-11/GSE%20Repurchase%20Activity%20and%20its%20Chilling%20Effect%20on%20the%20Market.pdf (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Fannie Mae DU validation service / Day 1 Certainty: validated income, employment and assets eligible for enforcement relief from reps and warrants; Fannie says validating all four components reduces repurchase risk by 64% (Fannie's own figure) — https://singlefamily.fanniemae.com/applications-technology/day-1-certainty and https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b3-2-02/du-validation-service (accessed 2026-10-22)
- DU validation service details: validating income or employment from an asset report needs at least 12 months of data from an authorised vendor; close-by dates of 4 months from the report date for income from an asset report and 15 days for employment; income validation is not provided when DU's calculated income does not support the application — https://singlefamily.fanniemae.com/learning-center/originating-and-underwriting/desktop-underwriter-learning-center/du-validation-service-resource-center-frequently-asked-questions (search-result summary, accessed 2026-10-22)
- KBRA publishes non-QM RMBS default studies by credit attribute — https://www.kbra.com/publications/QfQZHmZP/kbra-releases-research-non-qm-rmbs-default-study-credit-attribute-insights?format=web (accessed 2026-10-22)
- MCT's HALO programme stands for Hedging And Loan sales Optimization; MCT and MIAC both publish monthly MSR market updates — https://www.send2press.com/wire/profile/mortgage-capital-trading-inc/ , https://mct-trading.com/blog/msr-market-monthly-update-october-2025/ and https://miacanalytics.com/september-2026-msr-market-update/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
