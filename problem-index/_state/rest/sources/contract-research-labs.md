- SBIR/STTR authority lapsed on 30 September 2025; the Small Business Innovation and Economic Security Act (P.L. 119-83) was signed 13 April 2026 and reauthorises the programmes through 30 September 2031. It requires agencies to assess security risk (foreign ownership, ties, disclosures, intelligence-community coordination) and reject risky applicants; from FY2027 each agency sets a maximum number of Phase I and Phase II proposals a firm may submit; adds strategic breakthrough awards of up to $30M with a 100% match. FY2022 SBIR awards $4.4B and STTR $662.3M; 11 agencies run SBIR and 6 run STTR — https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/IN12705.html (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Six-month lapse barred agencies from issuing new solicitations or committing new SBIR/STTR funds (secondary summaries) — https://www.mondaq.com/unitedstates/government-contracts-procurement-ppp/1774902/sbirsttr-programs-reauthorized-after-six-month-lapse and https://www.aip.org/fyi/congress-passes-small-business-research-reauthorization (accessed 2026-10-22, as reported in search results)
- DoD SBIR: Phase I selection rate 18% in FY2022 (9% Army to 27% other defense agencies), about 20% overall; Phase I averages ~$150,000 over 12 months (1,172 new awards and $185.8M in FY2022; 1,775 in FY2023); Phase II selection 28% in FY2022, 1,334 awards in FY2022 and 1,365 in FY2023, ~$1.3M average; FY2012–2023 34,704 awards to 6,093 small businesses totalling $17.7B; SBA data cannot reliably link Phase I to Phase II awards, so conversion rates cannot be derived (National Academies 2026 review of DoD SBIR/STTR) — https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/29329/chapter/5 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- "SBIR mills": critics say a small set of firms win award after award with little commercialisation; DoD estimated a commercialisation benchmark for firms with more than 50 awards would affect about 152 firms — https://bipartisanpolicy.org/blog/should-sbir-reauthorization-include-limits-on-multiple-award-winners/ and https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10961-024-10144-z (accessed 2026-10-22, as reported in search results)
- SBIR/STTR data rights: since the 2019 SBA Policy Directive a single, non-extendable protection period of at least 20 years from award; the government gets unlimited rights in unmarked SBIR data during the period; the firm has six months to correct marking errors; after the period the government gets government purpose rights rather than unlimited rights; DoD implemented the 20-year period by class deviation effective 17 March 2020 — https://wsgr.com/en/insights/dod-small-business-innovation-research-sbir-contractors-data-rights-protections-extended-to-20-years-government-rights-limited-thereafter.html and https://www.swlaw.com/publication/sba-announces-a-major-change-to-sbirsttr-data-rights-protection/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- CMMC: the DFARS rule (clause 252.204-7021) took effect 10 November 2025, starting Phase 1 (self-assessments at award); Phase 2 (Level 2 third-party C3PAO certification at award) was due 10 November 2026, but the Department of War suspended Phase 2 and later phases by memo dated 10 July 2026 (announced 13 July) pending a 60-day review; Phase 1, DFARS 252.204-7012, NIST SP 800-171 Rev 2 self-assessments and SPRS postings continue. No review outcome found as of 22 October 2026 — https://www.wilmerhale.com/en/insights/client-alerts/20260720-pentagon-suspends-cmmc-phase-2-requirements-and-launches-review-of-cybersecurity-certification-program and https://hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/07/dow-suspends-cmmc-phase-ii-requirements (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Southwest Research Institute: ~$966M research volume in FY2025, 3,200 employees, 10 technical divisions, client-funded, with an internal R&D programme — https://www.swri.org/about-us/southwest-research-institute-frequently-asked-questions (accessed 2026-10-22)
- SRI International: total revenue ~$437.9M in 2024 (~$409.7M in 2023) on Form 990 data; staff ~1,600–2,300 depending on source (third-party 990 summaries, not the filing itself) — https://causeiq.com/organizations/sri-international,941160950 and https://philanthropy.org/990/report/941160950/sri-international (accessed 2026-10-22, as reported)
