# Sources — Media Representative Firms

## Checked
- NAICS 541840 Media Representatives: ~$6.2B in receipts in the 2022 Economic Census; 1,233 establishments and 23,093 employees in County Business Patterns 2023 (third-party page citing Census) — https://vantainsights.com/industry/541840-media-representatives (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Census Service Annual Survey, total revenue for media representatives, employer firms subject to federal income tax: ~$2.9B in 2012, $6.5B in 2013, $14.1B in 2017, $32.5B in 2021, $32.3B in 2022 (series REVEF54184TAXABL, $M) — https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/REVEF54184TAXABL (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Census note (December 2020): revenue and expense estimates for NAICS 541840 were revised upward back to 2012 because of a reclassification of reported business activities; no figures given — https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/sas/newsroom/updates/media-representatives-revision.html (accessed 2026-10-22)
- iHeartMedia FY2025 Form 10-K: Katz Media represents more than 3,400 non-iHeartMedia radio stations and over 400 television stations and their digital platforms; earns revenue via commissions on media sold on behalf of the stations, recognised net when the ads air; media representation contracts typically run up to ten years; Audio & Media Services Group (Katz plus RCS) revenue $272.5M in 2025, $327.1M in 2024, $256.7M in 2023, with the 2025 decline (−16.7%) attributed largely to lower political revenue and nonrecurring contract termination fees earned by Katz in 2024 — https://d18rn0p25nwr6d.cloudfront.net/CIK-0001400891/223e9f79-b858-4bd8-9830-3f41b0b95ee8.pdf (accessed 2026-10-22)
- CoxReps describes itself as four divisions (TeleRep, HRP, MMT and Gamut), giving agencies access to 350+ broadcast TV stations covering more than 90% of US TV households and generating over $3B in national sales (company description on a third-party listing, undated) — https://media-index.kochava.com/buyers/coxreps (accessed 2026-10-22)
- 2024 political ad cycle (AdImpact): record $11.1B; broadcast TV $5.3B; CTV $2.3B (21%); cable $1.37B; digital $1.6B; first cycle in which broadcast's share fell below 50% (published 20 December 2024) — https://www.tvtechnology.com/news/political-ad-spend-for-the-2024-election-cycle-hit-a-record-usd11-1-billion (accessed 2026-10-22)
- 47 CFR 73.1942: lowest unit charge for legally qualified candidates during the 45 days before a primary or primary runoff and the 60 days before a general or special election; all classes and value-enhancing practices must be disclosed; stations have an affirmative duty to disclose rate information (classes, LUC, current selling level for preemptible time, likelihood of preemption) and must negotiate in good faith — https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/47/73.1942 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Nielsen Big Data + Panel became local TV ratings currency in all markets from the first week of January 2025, combining set-top box and smart TV data (Roku, Comcast, Vizio, Dish, DirecTV; 75M+ devices in 45M+ households) with panels; Nielsen advised clients to keep Panel-Only data for post-buys of Q3–Q4 2024 (16 August 2024) — https://www.mrweb.com/drno/news37119.htm (accessed 2026-10-22)
- WideOrbit (24 September 2020): "~95% of local spot TV transactions are still completed using more traditional, and highly manual, workflows" (vendor claim) — https://www.wideorbit.com/blog/automation-local-spot-tv/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- AdExchanger (11 June 2019): local TV buying "still a manual process"; a national buy can repeat the same work across 210 markets; buyers want makegoods resolved in real time "rather than having to wait for an invoice 60 days after a campaign" — https://www.adexchanger.com/ad-exchange-news/hudson-mx-launches-a-platform-and-apis-to-automate-local-tv-buys/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Miller Kaplan Arase media revenue reporting covers radio and TV stations, cable interconnects and out-of-home; Market Revenue Analysis (market share, company and market revenue growth) and Market X-Ray (account-level share by advertiser, agency, category and salesperson); more than 2,900 media outlets rely on it — https://www.millerkaplan.com/industries/media/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Power ratio defined as a station's revenue share divided by its audience share; above 1 means the station takes a larger share of market revenue than of viewing (BIA sample TV market report) — https://www.bia.com/pdf/tv-market-report-sample.pdf (accessed 2026-10-22, via search result text)
- Guideline (30 April 2026) sells SQADCosts Local, "the standard for local pricing transparency in linear", and Standard Media Index among its ad intelligence products; its spend and pricing data covers ~$200B of annual media investment across 65 countries; launched local digital CPM benchmarks for the top 60+ US publishers — https://www.guideline.ai/blog/guideline-launches-new-ad-intelligence-digital-product--bringing-geo-targeted-cpm-pricing-to-ctv-and-audio (accessed 2026-10-22)
- BIA (21 November 2024): local TV advertising projected at $21B in 2025, +3.6% on 2024 excluding political; over-the-air ~$16.5B in 2024 including political — https://www.bia.com/press-releases/local-tv-advertising-poised-for-growth-in-2025-led-by-legal-and-automotive-industries/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
