# Sources — Health Insurance Carriers

## Checked
- 1,156 insurers filed the NAIC health annual statement in 2025 (1,155 in 2024); direct written premium rose 15.2% ($181B) to $1.37T; net earned premium rose 13.2% ($153B); net earnings fell from $9.3B to $6B (profit margin 0.4%); aggregate loss ratio 90.3%; combined ratio 100.6%; administrative expense ratio fell from 11.2% to 10.3%; adjusted operating cash flow −$3.4B; total enrollment ~295.5M (+25.2M) — NAIC, U.S. Health Insurance Industry Analysis Report, 2025 Annual Results — https://content.naic.org/sites/default/files/2025-annual-health-industry-commentary.pdf (accessed 2026-10-23)
- 2025 direct written premium mix: Medicare 37%, Medicaid 26%, group comprehensive 14%, individual comprehensive 11%; premium PMPM $372 vs claims PMPM $337; Medicare premium PMPM $1,458; individual line underwriting loss $6.5B; Part D net earned premium $28.8B (2024) to $65.5B (2025) after the IRA $2,000 out-of-pocket cap; ACA exchange median premiums up ~20% as enhanced premium tax credits expired at end-2025; CMS 2027 MA payment increase 2.48% — same NAIC 2025 report (accessed 2026-10-23)
- 2024 comparison: net earned premium +5.9% (~$64B), net earnings $25B to $9B, loss ratio 89%, operating cash flow −$1.4B; Medicare earned premium $449B, Medicaid $327B — NAIC 2024 annual health industry commentary — https://content.naic.org/sites/default/files/2024-annual-health-industry-commentary.pdf (via search summary, accessed 2026-10-23)
- 430,460 employed in NAICS 524114 (May 2023): customer service representatives 70,860; registered nurses 29,240; insurance claims and policy processing clerks 26,280; claims adjusters, examiners and investigators 19,070; software developers 11,620; compliance officers 6,310; data scientists 5,310; insurance underwriters 3,650; actuaries 2,730; medical records specialists 2,630 — BLS OEWS industry-specific estimates — https://www.bls.gov/oes/2023/may/naics5_524114.htm (accessed 2026-10-23)
- Medicare Advantage 2026: 35.2M enrollees of 64.2M eligible (55%), up ~1.1M (3%); special needs plans 23% of enrollees (8.2M), 85% of net growth; UnitedHealth Group and Humana together 46% of enrollees; 2025: 34.1M (54%) — KFF, Medicare Advantage in 2026: Enrollment Update and Key Trends — https://www.kff.org/medicare/medicare-advantage-in-2026-enrollment-update-and-key-trends/ (via search, accessed 2026-10-23)
- 66.5M Medicaid enrollees (78%) in comprehensive risk-based MCOs as of 1 July 2024; payments to MCOs ~50% of Medicaid spending in FY2024 — KFF, 10 Things to Know About Medicaid Managed Care and Medicaid Managed Care Tracker — https://www.kff.org/medicaid/10-things-to-know-about-medicaid-managed-care/ (via search, accessed 2026-10-23)
- 2026 Marketplace open enrollment: 23.1M plan selections, down ~1.2M (5%) from 2025; bronze 40% of selections (from 30%), silver 43% (from 56%); new consumers down 13% to 3.6M — CMS, Marketplace 2026 Open Enrollment Period Report: National Snapshot — https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/marketplace-2026-open-enrollment-period-report-national-snapshot-2 (via search, accessed 2026-10-23); average pre-subsidy premiums +19.7% — ValuePenguin analysis of CMS data — https://www.valuepenguin.com/aca-marketplace-selections-study (via search, accessed 2026-10-23)
- CMS-0057-F (Interoperability and Prior Authorization final rule): impacted payers (MA organizations, Medicaid and CHIP fee-for-service and managed care, exchange QHP issuers for the API provisions) must meet operational provisions generally from 1 January 2026, including 72-hour expedited and 7-calendar-day standard decision timeframes for items and services (not drugs) for MA and Medicaid/CHIP, specific denial reasons and annual public prior authorization metrics (first due 31 March 2026), and the Prior Authorization, Provider Access, Payer-to-Payer and expanded Patient Access APIs primarily by 1 January 2027 — CMS rule page — https://www.cms.gov/initiatives/burden-reduction/overview/interoperability/policies-regulations/cms-interoperability-prior-authorization-final-rule-cms-0057-f (via search, accessed 2026-10-23)
- MA insurers made 52.8M prior authorization determinations in 2024; 4.1M (7.7%) denied in full or part, up from 6.4% in 2023; 11.5% of denials appealed; 80.7% of appeals overturned the initial denial — KFF, Medicare Advantage Insurers Made Nearly 53 Million Prior Authorization Determinations in 2024 (28 January 2026) — https://www.kff.org/medicare/medicare-advantage-insurers-made-nearly-53-million-prior-authorization-determinations-in-2024/ (via search, accessed 2026-10-23)
- RADV expansion announced 21 May 2025: audit all ~550 eligible MA contracts annually instead of ~60; 35–200 sampled enrollees per contract; CMS medical coders from ~40 to ~2,000; clear the PY2018–2024 backlog by early 2026; a federal court in Texas vacated the RADV extrapolation rule in September 2025, CMS appealed, and a 27 January 2026 memo continued the audits with five months for record submission — Health Care Dive — https://www.healthcaredive.com/news/cms-medicare-advantage-audits-radv-risk-adjustment-update/811320/ ; ASCO Post — https://ascopost.com/issues/july-25-2025/medicare-advantage-audits-are-expanding/ (via search, accessed 2026-10-23)
- 2027 Star Ratings released 8 October 2026: 508 MA-PD contracts rated; 188 (~37%) at four stars or more; ~71% of MA-PD enrollees in four-plus-star contracts (from ~67%); 15 five-star MA-PD contracts (from 22); measure changes include tripled weight on the physical and mental health improvement measures — CMS fact sheet https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/2027-medicare-advantage-part-d-star-ratings ; Fierce Healthcare https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/payers/cms-about-71-mapd-plan-enrollees-coverage-four-or-more-stars-2027 (via search, accessed 2026-10-23)
- States must finalise and post each annual EQR technical report by 30 April; network adequacy validation is a mandatory EQR activity, with quantitative results required in technical reports due from 30 April 2028 — eCFR 42 CFR 438.364 — https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/chapter-IV/subchapter-C/part-438/subpart-E/section-438.364 ; Medicaid.gov EQR page — https://www.medicaid.gov/medicaid/quality-of-care/medicaid-managed-care-quality/quality-of-care-external-quality-review (via search, accessed 2026-10-23)
- Wakely National Risk Adjustment Reporting (WNRAR): ~80 participating ACA issuers including nearly all major multi-market issuers; 35+ states and 19M lives; five Excel reports a year by risk pool and state with year-to-date and projected transfer estimates and issuer-versus-market risk scores, HCC/RXC prevalence and chart-review impact; any issuer meeting the submission requirements and paying an annual fee may join — https://www.wakely.com/products/wnrar/ (accessed 2026-10-23)
- WNRAR participants run Wakely's code on their own enrolment, medical, pharmacy and supplemental diagnosis files and upload only summary output; "No detailed claim data, protected health information (PHI) or member-level data is sent to Wakely as part of this project. Only the summarized aggregate-level output is submitted"; results for a state and market are released only once all participants have submitted; Wakely's own result dates run mid-month after each upload window (e.g. 15 June, 15 September, 15 December 2026) — WNRAR 2026 Participation Guide — https://wramodel.com/wakelyraf/sitecontent/otherfiles/WNRAR_Participation_Guide.pdf (accessed 2026-10-23)
- IPD Analytics sells subscription drug life-cycle intelligence (launch tracking, loss-of-exclusivity timing, pipeline, patent litigation analysis); reports 700+ subscribers, including 220+ in its payer, provider and supply-chain segment (self-reported) — https://www.ipdanalytics.com/ and https://www.ipdanalytics.com/lifecycleinsights (via search, accessed 2026-10-23)
- Health Care Cost Institute: 2022 report relies on de-identified commercial claims contributed by CVS Health/Aetna, Humana and Blue Health Intelligence; dataset described as 1B+ claims a year covering 55M+ individuals (2012–2021); research access through a NORC enclave, non-commercial licences only, with a contributor Data Integrity Committee overseeing use — https://healthcostinstitute.org/wp-content/uploads/images/pdfs/HCCI_2022_Health_Care_Cost_and_Utilization_Report.pdf ; https://www.norc.org/research/projects/health-care-cost-institute-research-database.html (via search, accessed 2026-10-23)
