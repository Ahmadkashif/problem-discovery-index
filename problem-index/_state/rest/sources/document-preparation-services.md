- NAICS 561410 definition: letter and resume writing; document editing and proofreading; typing, word processing and desktop publishing; stenography (except court reporting), transcription and other secretarial services; index entries include medical-adjacent "transcription services", "dictation services", "resume writing services", "editing services", "proofreading services", "technical editing services". - Medical transcriptionists: about 42,000 jobs in 2025; median pay $40,410 (May 2025); employment projected to decline 4% (about 1,900 jobs) 2025-2035; offices of physicians employ 39%, administrative and support services 30%, hospitals 11% (BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook) — https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/medical-transcriptionists.htm (accessed 2026-10-23)
- AHDI "Healthcare Documentation Quality Assessment and Management Best Practices", revised May 2025: AHDI recommends a QA score of 98.0 as the minimum industry standard for documentation created by an HDS or scribe; critical errors carry -3 points (a sample worksheet uses -1 for noncritical); under the "error value from 100" method a single critical error fails a document regardless of length; the alternative "errors divided by lines reviewed" method can let a critical error in a long document pass; samples must be random, and the "5/3 method" (records ending in 5 or 3) is named as a common selection technique; the 2025 toolkit's worked example uses 0.5- and 0.25-point errors over 206 lines — https://ahdionline.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/QABP25-Toolkit-FINAL.pdf (accessed 2026-10-23)
- AQuity Solutions' quality programme adopts AHDI's 98% accuracy goal and adds a minor-error tier weighted 0.25 (vendor document, 2022) — https://aquitysolutions.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/AQuity-Quality-Overview-2022.pdf (accessed 2026-10-23, via search result)
- The 65-character line is the medical transcription billing convention; vendor price guides put transcription at ~$0.09-0.18 per line and speech-recognition editing pay at ~$0.03-0.05 per line (vendor and salary-guide pages, indicative only) — https://www.costowl.com/b2b/emr/medical-transcription-service-cost/ and https://www.scribemed.com/medical-transcription-salary-guide.html (accessed 2026-10-23, via search results)
- For The Record (2018): an unnamed major national vendor cut manual transcription pay from 8 to 7 cents per line and speech-recognition editing pay from 4 to 3 cents per line; the author estimates real editor productivity at 200-240 lines per hour against vendor-reported 250-350, about $8-10 an hour at 4 cents — https://www.fortherecordmag.com/news/021518_exclusive.shtml (accessed 2026-10-23)
