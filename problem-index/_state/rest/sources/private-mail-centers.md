# Sources — Private Mail Centers

## Checked
- NAICS 561431 in 2022: 4,663 employer firms, 7,755 establishments, receipts $5,228,139K (~$5.23B), annual payroll $1,273,648K (~$1.27B), 40,061 employees. Same file: 56143 business service centers 6,555 firms and ~$8.72B receipts; 561439 other business service centers (including copy shops) 1,899 firms and ~$3.50B receipts (2022 Economic Census, sector 56 basic file, US rows) — https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/economic-census/data/2022/sector56/EC2256BASIC.zip (accessed 2026-10-23)
- NAICS 561431 in 2023: 7,614 employer establishments, 39,175 employees, annual payroll $1,249,594K (~$1.25B); by size, 3,789 establishments have fewer than five employees, 3,059 have 5-9, 662 have 10-19, 87 have 20-49, 14 have 50-99 and none is larger (so ~90% have fewer than ten employees) (County Business Patterns 2023, US file) — https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/cbp/datasets/2023/cbp23us.zip (accessed 2026-10-23)
- The UPS Store: 5,603 US and 406 Canadian locations as of September 2026; "more than 6,000" in North America; Mail Boxes Etc. founded 1980, acquired by UPS in 2001, ~3,000 US centers rebranded The UPS Store on 7 April 2003; centers are locally owned and operated, 500-1,800 square feet, typically a full-time staff of two or more including the owner; services include packing, shipping, notarizing, printing, shredding, mailbox and postal services, mail and package receiving, and "Commerce Ready" alternative delivery locations and returns (company profile and fact sheet, updated September 2026) — https://www.theupsstore.com/file%20library/theupsstore/about/the-ups-store-media-kit.pdf (accessed 2026-10-23)
- USPS OIG, Management of Commercial Mail Receiving Agencies (Report 24-146-R25, 24 June 2025): over 1.6 million customers through nearly 12,000 CMRAs registered as of February 2025; 11,684 active CMRA locations in the Facilities Database as of August 2024; of 205 randomly sampled CMRA applications, 14 (7%) were missing, 133 of 191 (69%) were on an outdated form, 94 of 191 (49%) lacked the necessary identification and 75 of 191 (39%) had missing data; 21 of 24 Postal Service staff interviewed were not completing required mail-sample reviews and 14 were not completing data reviews; USPS spent $1.5M in 2022 to build the CMRA Customer Registration Database (CRD) and had uploaded ~39% of the data by February 2025; OIG estimated ~$5.2M of uncollected CMRA-related costs for FY2023-2024; the 2018 OIG report (SAT-AR-18-002) found that people conducting illegal activity frequently rent private mailboxes to disguise identity and that weak oversight left CMRAs vulnerable to drug traffickers; the Inspection Service monitors CMRA data for fraud, identity theft and narcotics — https://www.uspsoig.gov/sites/default/files/reports/2025-06/24-146-r25_0.pdf (accessed 2026-10-23)
- DMM 508.1.8 (Domestic Mail Manual updated 12 July 2026): a separate PS Form 1583 for each PMB addressee, spouses separately, with primary and secondary identification under 608.10.0; the addressee signs in the physical or real-time audio-video presence of the CMRA owner, manager or authorised employee, or acknowledges before a notary commissioned in a US state, territory, possession or DC; mismatched information means the CMRA must deny the application; the CMRA must keep every addressee current in the USPS CMRA Customer Registration Database and certify each quarter (due 15 January, 15 April, 15 July and 15 October) that every Form 1583 is current, terminations are entered and no ID is expired; non-compliant CMRAs get written notice and a 30-day period before suspension; the Chief Postal Inspector may issue emergency mail-withholding orders; CMRAs must remail for at least six months after termination and keep endorsed forms six months; mail must carry "PMB" or "#" — https://pe.usps.com/cpim/ftp/manuals/dmm300/508.pdf (accessed 2026-10-23)
- The CRD upload requirement and quarterly certification took effect 1 July 2023 (Postal Bulletin DMM revision) — https://about.usps.com/postal-bulletin/2023/pb22624/html/updt_002.htm (accessed 2026-10-23); the 2 May 2024 clarification allows notary acknowledgement in person or by real-time audio-video and requires a US-commissioned notary — https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2024/05/02/2024-06989/commercial-mail-receiving-agencies-clarification (accessed 2026-10-23, via search result)
- The UPS Store Pack & Ship Guarantee covers items packed by a participating store with new materials bought there and shipped on the store's account; it reimburses item value (lesser of actual, replacement or repair), packing and shipping charges; without declared value purchased, item reimbursement is capped at $100; customer-packed shipments are excluded — https://www.theupsstore.com/about/pack-ship-guarantee and https://www.ups.com/us/en/the-ups-store/pack-and-ship/pack-ship-guarantee (accessed 2026-10-23, via search results)
- U-PIC parcel insurance excludes losses on parcels packed insufficiently or contrary to carrier requirements, and requires claims within 90 days of shipment; Shipsurance terms require keeping packaging and damaged goods or the claim may be denied for insufficient packing — https://u-pic.com/how-to-avoid-claim-denials and https://u-pic.com/faqs (accessed 2026-10-23, via search results)
- PostalMate (PC Synergy) is a Windows desktop application for retail pack-and-ship centres combining multi-carrier shipping, point of sale and mailbox management; ShipRite NEXT is a point-of-sale product for retail shipping centres in the US, Canada, Puerto Rico and USVI, with Smart Mailboxes and a self-service drop-off kiosk — https://www.postalmate.com/ and https://shipritenext.com/ (accessed 2026-10-23, via search results)
- Retail Shipping Associates describes itself as an "8,000+ strong association" and lists authorised-shipper programmes for FedEx (Authorized ShipCenter), UPS (Authorized Shipping Outlet), DHL (ServicePoint Partner) and USPS (Approved Shipper), plus the Retail Shippers Expo (self-reported) — https://www.rscentral.org/Programs/Authorized-Shipper-Programs/FedEx-Authorized-ShipCenter (accessed 2026-10-23)
- AMBC (Association of Mail & Business Centers), founded 1982 as Associated Mail & Parcel Centers, member-owned not-for-profit since 2014, claims "over 1100 stores" in its network and runs a freight programme, AMBC-U training and the MBC Today newsletter (self-reported) — https://ambc4me.org/ (accessed 2026-10-23)
- FedEx Authorized ShipCenter agreement exhibit (2 September 2025): discounts and terms subject to change on 30 days' notice; FASC stores receive a quarterly incentive for packages dropped off but not billed to the store's account — https://www.rscentral.org/Portals/0/Documents/PBC_Resources/FASC_PBC_ExhibitA_9-2-25.pdf and https://www.fascnet.com/application/about_fasc/drop_incentive.php (accessed 2026-10-23, via search results)
- PostNet: "nearly 700 locations" across North, Central and South America and Africa; joined MBE Worldwide in 2017; MBE Worldwide's Fortidia platform reported 2024 system-wide gross revenue of EUR 1.45bn (US$1.65bn) across all its brands (press release, 16 March 2026) — https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/postnet-reports-strong-franchise-growth-in-2025-with-32-awarded-franchises-and-14-new-locations-302713736.html (accessed 2026-10-23)
- iPostal1 claims virtual mailboxes at 4,250+ locations; a third-party review says the addresses are operated by independent partners; Anytime Mailbox ~2,500 locations per a competitor comparison (marketing claims) — https://ipostal1.com/virtual-mailbox.php and https://ipostal1.com/anytime-mailbox.php (accessed 2026-10-23, via search results)
- UPS agreed to acquire Happy Returns from PayPal in October 2023; UPS said in 2025 the box-free, label-free return network had grown to 10,000 locations including The UPS Store, Staples and Ulta Beauty — https://about.ups.com/us/en/newsroom/press-releases/customer-first/ups-to-acquire-hr.html and https://about.ups.com/us/en/our-stories/customer-first/the-largest-box-free--label-free-return-network-in-the-u-s--is-b.html (accessed 2026-10-23, via search results)
- USPS DPV data flags commercial mail receiving agency addresses; DPV footnotes distinguish a CMRA match with a PMB number from one without; Persona's Address Lookup Report includes a DPV CMRA field and a separate virtual-address detection check; vendors describe the CMRA flag as a fraud and synthetic-identity signal — https://firstlogic.com/insights/cmra and https://help.withpersona.com/articles/79VIhKIprbykNbIDkzkAHt/ and https://accuzip.com/support/tech-notes/cass-ncoalink/?id=137 (accessed 2026-10-23, via search results)
- INFORM Consumers Act, effective 27 June 2023: online marketplaces must collect and verify high-volume third-party sellers' bank, tax ID and contact information within 10 days, and require certification at least annually; high-volume means 200+ sales and $5,000+ in a 12-month period — https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/INFORMAct and https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/what-third-party-sellers-need-know-about-inform-consumers-act (accessed 2026-10-23, via search results)
- Google Business Profile guidelines: a rented mailing address the business does not operate from (a virtual office) is ineligible, as are PO boxes and remote mailboxes (Google text as quoted by local-search sources) — https://whitespark.ca/blog/are-virtual-offices-and-p-o-boxes-eligible-for-a-google-business-profile/ (accessed 2026-10-23, via search result)
- Escapees offers mail-service addresses in Texas, Florida and South Dakota and says full-time RVers often use them in establishing domicile for licences, vehicle registration and voting, subject to state rules; America's Mailbox operates a South Dakota address (provider pages) — https://www.escapees.com/blog/domicile-homebase-mail-forwarding-for-rvers and https://www.mortonsonthemove.com/rv-mail-forwarding-services/ (accessed 2026-10-23, via search results)
