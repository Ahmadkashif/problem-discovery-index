- Active U.S. drug shortages 227 in Q2 2026, a third straight quarterly rise, against an all-time high of 323 in Q1 2024 (ASHP with the University of Utah Drug Information Service); for 2025 shortages 59% of causes were unknown or not given, 16% supply–demand mismatch, 15% manufacturing issues, 6% business decisions, 2% regulatory, 1% shipping delays and 1% raw materials; 48% of new 2026 shortages are sole-source products; ifosfamide in severe shortage after manufacturing quality problems at a key supplier — AJMC, 20 July 2026 — https://www.ajmc.com/view/active-us-drug-shortages-rise-for-third-straight-quarter (accessed 2026-10-24; ASHP's statistics page returned 403)
- Generics were 89% of prescriptions filled in 2025 and 11.6% of prescription drug spending; generic and biosimilar savings $496B in 2025 and $3.6T over ten years — AAM 2026 U.S. Generic & Biosimilar Medicines Savings Report page — https://accessiblemeds.org/?p=5117 (accessed 2026-10-24)
- Section 232 proclamation of 2 April 2026: 100% tariff on patented pharmaceuticals listed in the Orange Book or Purple Book and their APIs and key starting materials; 15% for Japan, EU, Korea and Switzerland; generics and biosimilars "expressly excluded from Section 232 tariffs at this time", with Commerce to advise the President within one year whether to extend the tariffs to generics; effective date reported as 31 July 2026 for most companies and 29 September 2026 for companies listed in an annex (the article itself is inconsistent about which annex) — Crowell & Moring client alert — https://www.crowell.com/en/insights/client-alerts/trump-administration-imposes-section-232-tariffs-on-patented-pharmaceutical-imports-tiered-rate-structure-takes-effect-beginning-july-31-2026 (accessed 2026-10-24)
- BIOSECURE Act signed 18 December 2025 in the FY2026 NDAA: bars federal agencies from contracting with or funding "biotechnology companies of concern" (1260H-listed companies and companies named through an OMB process); OMB's initial list due within one year of enactment; five-year transition for existing contracts, grants and loans; restrictions take effect at most about 970 days after enactment for 1260H companies; no company named in the text — Morrison Foerster, 18 December 2025 — https://www.mofo.com/resources/insights/251218-biosecure-act-update (accessed 2026-10-24). - FDA nitrosamine update of 23 June 2025: instead of submitting changes by 1 August 2025, companies were to submit progress reports (NDSRIs detected, test methods, batch results, root cause, mitigation timeline) as an "NDSRI Update" in the annual report; FDA still encourages confirmatory testing; non-application (monograph) makers keep the update on file — Sidley Austin — https://www.sidley.com/en/insights/newsupdates/2025/06/us-fda-revises-deadline-for-submitting-changes-to-mitigate-nitrosamine-impurities-in-drugs (accessed 2026-10-24). - Paragraph IV: approval of a challenged ANDA is generally postponed if the brand sues within 45 days of notice (the "30-month stay"); the first applicant with a substantially complete ANDA containing a paragraph IV certification is generally eligible for 180 days of generic exclusivity; FDA publishes a Paragraph IV Certifications List — https://www.fda.gov/drugs/abbreviated-new-drug-application-anda/patent-certifications-and-suitability-petitions (accessed 2026-10-24)
- 21 CFR 314.80: serious and unexpected adverse drug experiences reported no later than 15 calendar days from initial receipt ("15-day Alert reports"), with follow-ups within 15 calendar days; other adverse experiences reported at quarterly intervals for 3 years from approval and then annually, each quarterly report within 30 days of quarter close — eCFR versioner API, title 21 part 314 section 314.80 as of 2026-09-30 — https://www.ecfr.gov/api/versioner/v1/full/2026-09-30/title-21.xml?part=314&section=314.80 (accessed 2026-10-24)
