# Sources — Community Banks

## Checked
- 4,238 FDIC-insured institutions at Q2 2026 (down 41 in the quarter); 3,818 community banks with Q2 2026 net income of $8.7B (up 8.2% on the quarter); 4.4% of community banks unprofitable; 47 problem banks — https://bankingjournal.aba.com/2026/08/quarterly-banking-profile-banking-net-income-90-1b-in-q2-2026/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Community banks' full-year 2025 net income $29.9B, up 22.5% on 2024; community bank NIM 3.77% in Q4 2025, highest since 2018; community bank loans up 5.4% year on year — https://www.fdic.gov/news/speeches/2026/fdic-quarterly-banking-profile-fourth-quarter-2025 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Community banks at Q3 2025: 3,953 institutions, total assets ~$2.8T, loans and leases ~$1.9T, domestic deposits ~$2.3T, NIM 3.73% (a correspondent bank's summary of the FDIC community bank section; the assets line is not labelled "community banks" explicitly, but $2.8T fits community banks and not the ~$24T industry) — https://www.pcbb.com/bid/2025-12-10-fdics-q3-data-highlights-positive-trends (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Community banks hold ~15% of all bank loans but ~30% of CRE loans, ~36% of small business loans and ~70% of agricultural loans (year-end 2019 data, FDIC 2020 Community Banking Study) — https://fdic.gov/index.php/news/press-releases/2020/pr20139.html and https://www.fdic.gov/resources/community-banking/report/2020/2020-cbi-study-4.pdf (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Core processing: Fiserv ~42%, Jack Henry ~21%, FIS ~9% of banks surveyed (2022 data); the three together served more than 70% of banks; Jack Henry mainly serves small banks — https://www.kansascityfed.org/research/payments-system-research-briefings/market-structure-of-core-banking-services-providers/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- OEWS May 2023, NAICS 5221 and 5223 combined: 2,039,630 employed; tellers 326,620; loan officers 167,130; customer service representatives 134,890; loan interviewers and clerks 98,670; new accounts clerks 38,130; credit analysts 33,550; financial examiners 24,460; compliance officers 14,050 (all banks and credit unions, not community banks alone) — https://www.bls.gov/oes/2023/may/naics4_5220A1.htm (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Section 1071 revised final rule issued May 1, 2026, effective June 30, 2026, uniform compliance date January 1, 2028; coverage threshold raised to 1,000 covered small business originations in each of the prior two years; small business redefined as gross annual revenue of $1M or less; agricultural credit excluded — https://www.huschblackwell.com/newsandinsights/cfpb-finalizes-revised-section-1071-rule-a-narrower-framework-with-a-2028-compliance-date (accessed 2026-10-22)
- 18-month on-site examination cycle extended in 2018 to well-capitalized, well-managed institutions under $3B in total assets (from $1B) — https://www.fdic.gov/news/financial-institution-letters/2018/fil18045.html and https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/bcreg20180823a.htm (accessed 2026-10-22)
- CSBS compliance-cost work: smaller banks report spending ~11%–15.5% of payroll on compliance against ~6%–10% at the largest institutions — https://www.independentbanker.org/w/csbs-community-banks-pay-more-for-compliance%C2%A0 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Jack Henry deconversion revenue $33.9M in fiscal 2025, mostly from clients acquired by another institution; fiscal 2026 guidance $28M — https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/779152/000077915225000055/jkhy-20250630.htm and https://finder.techleap.nl/news/feed/jack-henry-raises-fiscal-2026-deconversion-revenue-guidance-to-28m-1 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- US bank M&A: 76 deals announced in H1 2025 and 105 in H2 2025, the most since 2021; 52 in Q3 2025 alone; FDIC rescinded its 2024 merger policy statement in 2025 — https://www.spglobal.com/market-intelligence/en/news-insights/articles/2026/1/bank-m-a-in-2026-to-build-on-gains-made-in-2025-96006854 and https://www.spglobal.com/market-intelligence/en/news-insights/articles/2025/10/us-bank-m-a-activity-surges-to-4-year-high-in-q3-93446075 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Cornerstone Advisors says its core-contract pricing database holds more than 15,000 pricing data points from selections, renewals and benchmarking engagements — https://crnrstone.com/home-2/services/contract-negotiations/core-negotiations/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- RMA Annual Statement Studies 2023–2024: more than 182,000 borrower financial statements from member institutions, 627+ industries by NAICS; earlier editions 240,000+ statements from 154 members — https://rmahq.org/product/696944336?gmssopc=1 and https://www.rmahq.org/press-releases/2020/rma-releases-annual-statement-studies-data-for-2020-2021/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- IntraFi network: 3,000+ participating institutions — https://www.intrafi.com/intrafi-faq (accessed 2026-10-22)
- 2006 interagency CRE concentration guidance: construction and land loans at 100% of total risk-based capital, or non-owner-occupied CRE at 300% with 50%+ growth over 36 months, flag a bank for further supervisory analysis; thresholds, not caps — https://comptrollerofthecurrency.gov/news-issuances/bulletins/2006/bulletin-2006-46.html and https://www.federalreserve.gov/bankinforeg/cre/cre-2013-part-1-executive-summary.htm (accessed 2026-10-22)
- SAR deadline: 30 calendar days from initial detection, extendable by 30 days to identify a suspect, never beyond 60 days; an automated alert alone does not start the clock — https://bsaaml.ffiec.gov/docs/manual/regulations/12CFR208-225.htm and https://bankersonline.com/articles/107354 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Abrigo 2020 business lending survey (300+ lenders and credit officers): nearly a third said closing a commercial loan takes five to seven weeks; only ~10% used an automated credit memo; nearly half entered memo data manually — https://www.abrigo.com/news/abrigo-opens-2020-business-lending-readiness-survey (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Trepp's T-ALLR: a consortium of US commercial banks contributing loan-level commercial loan performance data, anonymised and returned as quarterly peer benchmarks — https://nyrej.com/print/41809 (accessed 2026-10-22)
