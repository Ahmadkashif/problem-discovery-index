# Sources — Graphic Design Studios

## Checked
- US graphic design services (NAICS 54143) employer firms subject to federal income tax reported ~$12.6B total revenue in 2022, up from ~$8.6B in 2017 (Census Service Annual Survey, series REVEF54143TAXABL: 2017 8,551; 2018 8,597; 2019 9,342; 2020 9,521; 2021 11,044; 2022 12,610, $M) — https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/REVEF54143TAXABL (accessed 2026-10-22)
- ~$9.8B industry revenue in the 2022 Economic Census; 15,828 establishments and 49,266 employees in County Business Patterns 2023 (third-party page citing Census; 54143 has a single six-digit code, 541430) — https://vantainsights.com/industry/541430-graphic-design-services (accessed 2026-10-22)
- NAICS 541430 definition covers planning, designing and managing the production of visual communication, including printed materials, packaging, advertising, signage and corporate identification (logos), plus commercial artists producing technically accurate or interpretative drawings and illustrations — https://www.worldoftaxonomy.com/system/naics_2022/node/541430 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- BLS: 253,100 graphic designer jobs in 2025; median pay $62,960 (May 2025); 20% self-employed; 9% work in specialized design services, 8% in advertising/PR, 7% in information, 6% in printing; employment projected to decline 2% from 2025 to 2035 with ~16,000 openings a year — https://www.bls.gov/ooh/arts-and-design/graphic-designers.htm (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Adobe renamed Creative Cloud All Apps to Creative Cloud Pro from 17 June 2025, raising the annual-plan monthly price from $59.99 to $69.99, and added a $54.99 Creative Cloud Standard tier with fewer generative credits — https://www.fastcompany.com/91337900/adobe-creative-cloud-pro and https://appleinsider.com/articles/25/05/20/adobe-hikes-creative-cloud-prices-with-a-rebrand-no-one-asked-for (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Canva reported ~265M monthly active users and 31M+ paid users at end-2025, with ~$4B ARR; it relaunched the Affinity design suite as free in late October 2025 — https://sacra.com/c/canva/ and https://www.mobigyaan.com/canva-ai-creative-os-free-affinity-suite (accessed 2026-10-22)
- 99designs was acquired by Vista (Vistaprint's parent) in 2020 and rebranded "99designs by Vista"; logo contests are sold in fixed tiers from roughly $249–$299 to roughly $1,050–$1,499 depending on source, and typically draw 30–100+ submissions — https://www.hireinsouth.com/post/99designs-pricing and https://ddiy.co/99designs-pricing/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- US Copyright Office, *Copyright and Artificial Intelligence, Part 2: Copyrightability* (29 January 2025): prompts alone do not make the user an author of AI output; human selection, arrangement and modification can be protected, decided case by case — https://ipwatchdog.com/2025/01/29/part-two-copyright-office-ai-report-says-creative-prompting-doesnt-constitute-authorship/ and https://copyrightalliance.org/ai-report-part-2-copyrightability/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Font companies audit for unlicensed use and typically send "continue, but pay" letters offering a retroactive licence; Monotype sells audit tooling (Fontwise) and has run audits that found thousands of unlicensed typefaces at a single firm — https://docket.acc.com/node/4266 and https://www.theregister.com/2006/06/27/unlicensed_software_shocker/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- A July 2026 Adobe community post describes a packaging agency whose client received a Monotype licence-violation email over a packshot built with Adobe Fonts (single forum post, outcome unknown; used in the vault only as an illustration) — https://community.adobe.com/questions-438/created-a-packaging-design-for-a-client-using-adobe-fonts-said-client-now-got-an-email-from-monotype-about-license-violation-1632518 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- New York State's Freelance Isn't Free Act requires a written contract for freelance work worth $800 or more (aggregated over 120 days) and payment by the contract date or within 30 days of completion; it took effect in 2024 (sources give different effective dates, so the vault says "2024") — https://www.bsk.com/news-events-videos/new-york-enacts-statewide-ldquo-freelance-isn-rsquo-t-free-rdquo-legislation and https://dwt.com/blogs/employment-labor-and-benefits/2023/11/new-york-freelance-written-agreement-labor-law (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Matthews International agreed to sell SGK Brand Solutions (the packaging prepress agency formerly Schawk) to a new entity formed by affiliates of SGS & Co, for $350M upfront plus a 40% interest; SGK has been described as having 6,500+ employees (undated profile) — https://www.dlpmag.com/?p=79691 and https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/63296/000006329624000063/R8.htm and https://builtin.com/company/sgk (accessed 2026-10-22)
- GlobalVision Verify integrates with Esko WebCenter and Automation Engine for packaging artwork inspection: text compare, spell check in 37 languages, graphics, barcode and braille checks — https://www.globalvision.co/partners/esko/webcenter and https://eskointegration.globalvision.co/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Undeclared allergens drove almost half of US food recalls in 2023 (PIRG), helped by sesame becoming a required label allergen from 1 January 2023; a 2024 summary put undeclared allergens at ~34% of recall incidents — https://pirg.org/media-center/new-report-300-plus-food-recalls-in-2023-highest-since-2020/ and https://www.food-safety.com/articles/9437-thanks-sesame-us-food-recalls-due-to-undeclared-allergens-skyrocketed-in-2023-causing-half-of-all-food-recalls (accessed 2026-10-22)
- 2010 ADA Standards §703: tactile characters 48–60 in. above the floor to baseline; raised characters uppercase, sans serif, 5/8–2 in. high and raised 1/32 in.; tactile signs at the latch side of doors with 18 × 18 in. clear floor space — https://www.access-board.gov/ada/chapter/ch07 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- USPTO design search codes are six-digit codes derived from the Vienna Classification; the USPTO search system does not support reverse image search, so logo clearance depends on coding — https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/design-search-codes (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Corsearch says its trademark clearance combines search tooling with human analyst review, covers 240 jurisdictions and 1,100+ databases, and offers AI image-similarity design search — https://corsearch.com/solution/trademark-brand-clearance/expertsearch and https://corsearch.com/?p=517 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Behaviorally (formerly PRS IN VIVO, founded 1972) runs packaging and shopper research from a database of behavioural pack-design metrics; a pre-rebrand directory listing claims 800+ custom studies a year and a trade article says its eye-tracking method is used in several hundred studies a year — https://www.cbinsights.com/company/behaviorally and https://www.mrweb.com/agencies/spd70326.htm and https://www.bxpmagazine.com/?p=11963 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Brand Finance is accredited to ISO 10668 (brand valuation) and ISO 20671 (brand evaluation), values 6,000+ brands a year by royalty relief, and has offices in 20+ countries — https://brandfinance.com/?p=3896 and https://ivsc.org/professional-insight-analysing-the-value-of-a-brand/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Proofing tool prices: Filestage ~$119–349/month flat with unlimited reviewers; Ziflow entry ~$199/month for 15 users (sources conflict) — https://picflow.com/compare/filestage-vs-ziflow and https://www.g2.com/products/filestage/pricing (accessed 2026-10-22)
- AIGA Design Census 2019 surveyed 9,429 US designers; average salary ~$76,000 — https://www.itsnicethat.com/news/design-census-2019-aiga-google-230919 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Flexographic prepress guidance: minimum positive stroke ~0.5 pt and reverse stroke ~0.7 pt, confirmed with the printer; a converter's standard flexo trap of ~0.009 in.; reversed type tends to fill in — https://flexopedia.net/?p=1048 and https://www.esko.com/en/blog/flexo-prepress-full-guide (accessed 2026-10-22)
- 37 CFR 1.84: patent drawings are normally black ink; colour drawings are allowed only rarely, on petition — https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/37/1.84 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Tailor Brands claimed 3.4M+ businesses as users in 2017 (dated, company claim) — https://legacy.xconomy.com/?p=366683 (accessed 2026-10-22)
