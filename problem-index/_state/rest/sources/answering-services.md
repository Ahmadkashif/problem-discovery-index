# Sources — Telephone Answering Services

## Checked
- NAICS 561421 in 2022: 934 employer firms, 1,043 establishments, receipts $2,592,138K (~$2.59B), annual payroll $1,134,930K (~$1.13B), 31,260 employees. Same file: 561422 telemarketing bureaus and other contact centers 2,380 firms, receipts ~$25.6B, 402,457 employees; 561410 document preparation 3,139 firms (2022 Economic Census, sector 56 basic file, US rows) — https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/economic-census/data/2022/sector56/EC2256BASIC.zip (accessed 2026-10-23)
- NAICS 561421 in 2023: 1,035 employer establishments, 41,582 employees, annual payroll ~$1.61B; by size, 354 establishments (34%) have fewer than five employees, 163 have 5-9, 241 have 10-19, 174 have 20-49, 50 have 50-99, 25 have 100-249, 13 have 250-499, 8 have 500-999 and 7 have 1,000 or more (County Business Patterns 2023, US file) — https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/cbp/datasets/2023/cbp23us.zip (accessed 2026-10-23)
- ATSI (Association of TeleServices International), founded 1942, is the trade association for live answering services in North America; its Award of Excellence uses independent judges who place test calls over about six months and score response time, courtesy, accuracy, knowledge of the account and overall impression; the programme had run 27 years by 2023, and ATSI also runs site and agent certification (member press releases, self-reported) — https://anser.com/2023-atsi-aoe-22-years-and-top-score and https://www.ambscallcenter.com/blog/ambs-call-center-wins-atsi-2024-award-for-outstanding-customer-service and https://answeringservicecare.com/blog/sixth-straight-atsi-award-of-excellence/ (accessed 2026-10-23, via search results; atsi.org returned 403)
- Startel acquired Professional Teledata effective 2 September 2015, and Alston Tascom became a wholly owned business unit of Startel effective 1 September 2017; the combined company sells premise-based and hosted answering-service platforms — https://connectionsmagazine.com/news/startel-merger-with-alston-tascom/ and https://connectionsmagazine.com/news/startel-and-professional-teledata-merge/ (accessed 2026-10-23)
- Amtelco sells the Infinity platform and Genesis, a software-only successor first released in 2013 with the IS 4.1 release, which Amtelco says runs at nearly two hundred call centres; Infinity's Intelligent Messaging scripts branch by call type, time of day and day of week; Telescan sells the Spectrum answering-service platform (vendor and trade-press profiles) — https://connectionsmagazine.com/article/vendor-profile-amtelco/ and https://connectionsmagazine.com/article/spotlight-on-amtelco/ (accessed 2026-10-23, via search results)
- Amtelco Secure Messages (formerly miSecureMessages) is an encrypted, HIPAA-marketed pager-replacement app with delivery and read receipts, used by answering services — https://www.amtelco.com/resources/dexcomm-meeting-hipaa-requirements-using-amtelco-secure-messages/ and https://connectionsmagazine.com/news/amtelco-introduces-misecuremessages-encrypted-messaging/ (accessed 2026-10-23, via search results)
- Schmitt-Thompson telephone triage protocols: used in over 400 medical call centres in the US and Canada and for over 20 million calls a year, used in over 90% of US pediatric offices, in use since 1995 (After Hours version), updated annually from quality-improvement projects at 9 call centres and reviews of high-risk calls; page written by Dr Barton Schmitt in July 2015, so the figures date from 2015 — https://www.cleartriage.com/?p=10931 (accessed 2026-10-23)
- TriageLogic states it partners with the Schmitt-Thompson authors to license the protocols in its software and nurse triage services; its product sheets repeat the ~400 call-centre figure — https://triagelogic.com/?p=18534 and https://triagelogic.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/All-Products.pdf (accessed 2026-10-23, via search results)
- Nurse Licensure Compact: 41 states and 2 territories have enacted it; Connecticut was fully implemented on 1 October 2025; Massachusetts, Guam and the US Virgin Islands are pending implementation (Texas Board of Nursing meeting materials, October 2025) — https://bon.texas.gov/pdfs/board_meetings_pdfs/2025/Oct/6-1.pdf (accessed 2026-10-23, via search result); a nurse in a non-compact state cannot triage a caller located in another state without that state's licence (AAACN member forum) — https://community.aaacn.org/discussion/out-of-state-advice (accessed 2026-10-23, via search result)
- Hospice Conditions of Participation, 42 CFR 418.100(c)(2): nursing services, physician services, and drugs and biologicals must be made routinely available on a 24-hour basis 7 days a week — https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/42/418.100 (accessed 2026-10-23)
- HIPAA conduit exception: HHS limits it to transmission-only services with no storage beyond what is temporary and incidental to transmission; HHS called it narrow in the Omnibus Rule and refused to extend it to entities that store PHI. A service that keeps message logs, recordings or transcripts therefore needs a business associate agreement (HHS FAQ 2077; law-firm and vendor commentary; no HHS document names answering services) — https://www.hhs.gov/guidance/document/faq-2077-can-csp-be-considered-be-conduit-postal-service-and-therefore-not-business and https://www.hollandhart.com/hipaa-business-associates-and-the-conduit-exception (accessed 2026-10-23, via search results)
- A 2017 New York trial-court decision held an after-hours answering service (H-Link) negligent as a matter of law for sending emergent messages to a physician's outdated pager number rather than the cell phone he had designated; Milbert v. Answering Bureau (Louisiana) addressed whether an answering service that failed to relay a patient's messages could be a joint tortfeasor in a malpractice suit — https://www.nycourts.gov/reporter/pdfs/2017/2017_32621.pdf and https://medicalmalpracticeopinions.justia.com/2013/06/29/milbert-v-answering-bureau-inc (accessed 2026-10-23, via search results; the NY PDF returned 403)
- Moneypenny acquired the Atlanta answering service VoiceNation and Ninja Number, announced March 2020, terms undisclosed, expecting US revenue to rise from ~10% to over 25% of the group; Moneypenny is backed by ECI Partners — https://voicenation.com/press/moneypenny-expands-us-services-with-acquisition-of-voicenation-and-ninja-number and https://www.destinationcrm.com/Articles/CRM-News/CRM-Across-the-Wire/Moneypenny-Acquires-VoiceNation-and-Ninja-Number-139539.aspx (accessed 2026-10-23, via search results)
- Spok Holdings reported ~720 thousand wireless (paging) units in service against ~765 thousand a year earlier, a 5.9% decline, in its fourth-quarter and full-year 2024 results — https://www.silicon.co.uk/press-release/spok-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2024-results and https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1289945/000128994525000008/spok-20241231.htm (accessed 2026-10-23, via search results)
- Switchboard operators, including answering service (SOC 43-2011): ~43,830 employed, median annual wage ~$36,750, mean ~$40,020 (BLS OEWS current page; the reference year was not shown in the search result and the page could not be fetched directly) — https://www.bls.gov/OES/CURRENT/oes432011.htm (accessed 2026-10-23, via search result)
- Trades voice-agent vendors named in a 2026 buyer's guide: Sameday, Avoca AI, OnCrew, Trillet, Rosie, alongside AI built into ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro and Jobber; vendors describe paging on-call staff only for genuine emergencies and booking other calls into the field-service calendar (vendor guides) — https://www.stork.ai/blog/ai-receptionist-for-home-services-2026 and https://www.open.cx/voice/for-hvac (accessed 2026-10-23, via search results)
- A nurse triage provider (Conduit Health Partners) cites an industry average handle time of 14.3 minutes for clinician-handled triage calls, plus two minutes when an unlicensed representative takes the call first, and notes per-call versus per-minute pricing models (vendor claim) — https://www.conduithp.com/news/comparing-nurse-triage-pricing-key-questions-to-ask/ (accessed 2026-10-23, via search result)
