# Sources — Directory & List Publishers

## Checked
- Total revenue for directory and mailing list publishers (taxable employer establishments) was $4,582M in 2022, $4,368M in 2021, $18,515M in 2007 and $16,920M in 2002, Census Service Annual Survey (NAICS 51114, the pre-2022 code for 513140) — https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/REVEF51114TAXABL (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Revenue from subscriptions and sales of directories, databases and other collections of information: $1,854M in 2022 ($3,163M in 2013) — https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/REVDSSEF51114ALLEST (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Revenue from rental or sale of mailing lists: $117M in 2022, down from $302M in 2015 — https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/REVRMLEF51114ALLEST (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Revenue from directory advertising space: $1,781M in 2020, the last year the series was published (series updated Nov 2021) — https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/REVDASEF51114ALLEST (accessed 2026-10-22 via search summary)
- NAICS 513140, private sector, 2024 annual averages: 1,375 establishments, 14,671 employees, $129,910 average annual pay; 2023: 1,527 establishments, 16,779 employees; 2022: 1,697 establishments, 19,016 employees — BLS QCEW open data, https://data.bls.gov/cew/data/api/2024/a/industry/513140.csv (and the 2023 and 2022 files at the same path) (accessed 2026-10-22). Under the old code 511140 in 2021: 876 establishments and 12,394 employees — https://data.bls.gov/cew/data/api/2021/a/industry/511140.csv (accessed 2026-10-22); the jump in 2022 is the NAICS 2022 reclassification that moved online-only directory publishing into this code
- NAICS 513140 definition: establishments publishing directories, mailing lists and collections or compilations of fact, typically protected in selection, arrangement and presentation; examples include mailing address lists, telephone directories, business directories, compilations of proprietary drugs or legal case results and public records; print or electronic, including exclusively on the Internet — https://worldoftaxonomy.com/codes/naics_2022/513140 (accessed 2026-10-22; reproduces the Census text, census.gov returned 403)
- Data Axle runs 150+ dedicated data specialists making ~60,000 calls a day, ~25M a year, blending 4,000+ sources, and prioritises calls with a machine-learning tool called Smart-Calling — https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/pp/prodview-shl7ccdi3nly4 (accessed 2026-10-22; vendor listing)
- Data Axle claims 90M+ business profiles with 400+ attributes and 19.5M+ "premium verified" profiles — https://data-axle.com/our-data/business-data/ (accessed 2026-10-22 via search summary; direct fetch returned 404/403; vendor claim)

- California Delete Act DROP: from 1 August 2026 registered data brokers must access DROP and delete matched, non-exempt information on a rolling 45-day basis; DROP reaches over 600 active data brokers — https://privacy.ca.gov/2026/07/drop-data-broker-deletions-how-do-they-work/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
