- Census SUSB 2022, NAICS 522220 Sales Financing, United States: 2,416 firms, 3,637 establishments, 95,912 employees, $10.98B annual payroll, $137.16B receipts; 160 firms with 500+ employees hold 73,180 employees and $123.6B receipts (~90%); 1,982 firms have fewer than 20 employees — https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/susb/tables/2022/us_state_6digitnaics_2022.xlsx (accessed 2026-10-22)
- BLS QCEW, NAICS 522220, private ownership, 2024 annual average: 6,450 establishments, 83,550 employees, average annual pay $126,364 — https://data.bls.gov/cew/data/api/2024/a/industry/522220.csv (accessed 2026-10-22)
- New York Fed Household Debt and Credit, Q2 2026: auto loan balances up $28B to $1.71T; $211B of auto loans originated in the quarter; 3.00% of auto debt transitioning into serious delinquency (2.93% a year earlier) — https://www.newyorkfed.org/newsevents/news/research/2026/20260811 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Experian State of the Automotive Finance Market, Q1 2026 (trade-press write-up): captives 43.4% of new-vehicle loans (47% a year earlier); finance companies and others 9.4% of new-vehicle loans (7.1%); banks 30.7% of all auto loans; credit unions 22.7% — https://www.cutimes.com/amp/2026/06/01/banks-rev-up-auto-lending-while-credit-unions-idle/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- S&P Global Ratings via Auto Remarketing: US auto loan ABS volume ~$127B in 2025, a record for the second year running; subprime annualised net losses 8.88% (8.51% in 2024); subprime 60+ day delinquencies a record 6.18% — https://www.autoremarketing.com/?p=82346 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Tricolor Holdings filed Chapter 7 in September 2025; in December 2025 the SDNY charged its CEO, CFO and COO, alleging falsified auto-loan data and collateral pledged to more than one lender — https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdny/pr/ceo-cfo-coo-charged-connection-billion-dollar-collapse-tricolor-auto and https://news.dealershipguy.com/p/tricolor-the-messy-collapse-of-a-subprime-auto-lender-explained-2025-09-16 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- PrimaLend, a lender providing receivables and floorplan finance to buy-here-pay-here dealers, also filed in 2025 — https://www.coxautoinc.com/insights-hub/auto-loan-health-separating-facts-from-fear/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- CFPB automobile financing larger-participant rule (2015): nonbanks with 10,000+ aggregate annual originations are supervised; an August 2025 ANPR asked about raising the threshold as high as 1.05 million; one practitioner estimate is that coverage would fall from ~63 entities (~94% of the market) to as few as 5 (~42%) — https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-08-08/html/2025-15089.htm and https://www.autoremarketing.com/subprime/cfpb-seeks-input-about-changing-automobile-financing-larger-participant-rule/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Point Predictive 2026 auto lending fraud report (April 2026): estimated fraud exposure a record $10.4B, up from $9.2B; first-party fraud 69% of exposure; lenders push post-funding fraud losses back to dealers through buyback clauses — https://www.autoremarketing.com/?p=82646 and https://www.sovereignmagazine.com/article/auto-lending-fraud-first-party-record-10-4-billion (accessed 2026-10-22)
- ELFA describes equipment leasing and finance as a $1.3 trillion industry; its 2025 SEFA (100 member companies) reports new business volume up 3.1% to $146.9B in 2024 — https://www.elfaonline.org/research/survey-of-equipment-finance-activity/2025-survey-of-equipment-finance-activity-main and https://www.elfaonline.org/communications/magazine/october-2025/2025-sefa-dives-deep-into-equipment-finance-data (accessed 2026-10-22)
- California SB 766, the Combating Auto Retail Scams (CARS) Act, chaptered 2025-10-06 and operative October 1, 2026: total-price disclosure, a ban on valueless add-ons, a 3-day right to cancel on specified used vehicles, prohibitions on misrepresenting finance terms and credit-application information, two-year record retention — https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/file/26olin10-pdf/ and https://calmatters.digitaldemocracy.org/bills/ca_202520260sb766 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Cox Automotive estimated ~1.73 million vehicle repossessions in 2024, the highest since 2009 — https://www.uc.edu/news/articles/2025/11/n21373036.html and https://finance.yahoo.com/news/car-repossessions-expected-hit-highest-150000266.html (accessed 2026-10-22)
- The Recovery Database Network (RDN) captures an estimated ~90% of repossession assignments — https://curepossession.com/rdn-repossession-volume-report-q2-2025/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Regulation AB II: asset-level data filed on Form ABS-EE at offering and with each Form 10-D; 72 data points for auto loans and 66 for auto leases; public offerings only, not Rule 144A — https://www.klgates.com/regulation-ab-ii-second-times-the-charm-9-19-2014 and https://hunton.com/media/legal/2470_user-guide-to-regulation-ab-ii-and-exchange-act-rules-15ga-2-and.pdf (accessed 2026-10-22)
