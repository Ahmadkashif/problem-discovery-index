# Sources — Wholesale Auto Auctions

## Checked
- NAICS 423110 Automobile and Other Motor Vehicle Merchant Wholesalers, 2022: 4,521 firms, 6,047 establishments, 128,083 employees, $758,129,382K receipts; the 122 firms with 500+ employees took $670,463,293K (~88%, mostly new-vehicle distribution); the 3,552 firms under 20 employees took $18,321,116K. Auction houses are a minority of the code, and their receipts are fees, not vehicle values — Census SUSB 2022, US by 6-digit NAICS and enterprise size (released 2025-04-10) — https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/susb/tables/2022/us_state_6digitnaics_2022.xlsx (downloaded and parsed 2026-10-28)
- NAAA 2025 Market Intelligence Report (released 2026-03-23): 12.3 million vehicles offered at NAAA member auctions (+4%, highest since 2021); more than 8 million sold (+3%), 7.2 million in-lane and 820,000 digital-only; half of in-lane sales went to an online bidder; in-lane conversion 58.5%; gross sales value more than $100 billion (+8.5%); average in-lane price $13,800 (+5.3%); commercial vehicles 51.4% of in-lane sales; the average member auction sold 25,000 vehicles and a quarter sold more than 30,000 — Auto Remarketing, 2026-03-23 — https://www.autoremarketing.com/ar/wholesale/vehicles-offered-for-sale-at-auction-reaches-5-year-high/ (accessed 2026-10-28); same totals in Used Car News, 2026-03-24 — https://usedcarnews.com/auction-news/naaa-auction-sales-top-8-million (accessed 2026-10-28)
- NAAA Arbitration Guidelines effective 1 June 2026: the $800 per-defect threshold stays for vehicles under $50,000; at $50,000 and above each defect must cost at least 2% of the purchase price to repair ($1,000 at $50,000, $1,500 at $75,000, $2,000 at $100,000); defects can be filed together but each must clear its own threshold; Manheim retired its $75K High Value Arbitration policy and began moving away from generic announcements on 1 April 2026 — Manheim, "What the Latest NAAA Updates Mean for You", 2026-05-21 — https://site.manheim.com/news/what-the-latest-naaa-updates-mean-for-you (accessed 2026-10-28)
- Same guidelines: generic "No Arb for [defect]" announcements prohibited; announcing only a warning light or diagnostic trouble code no longer relieves the seller of the underlying condition; ADAS components (adaptive cruise, lane departure warning, blind spot monitoring) formally listed in Appendix I, except where in visibly damaged or disclosed parts — NIADA Dashboard, 2026-06-15 — https://niada.com/dashboard/naaa-sharpens-arbitration-rules-on-announcements-high-value-vehicles-and-adas/ (accessed 2026-10-28). The NAAA's own PDFs (guidelines, summary of changes, announcements position statement) returned HTTP 403 to the fetcher.
- Manheim arbitration terms: a 10 calendar day arbitration window for digital transactions (2 calendar days after delivery if transport runs past it); documents, vehicle return and title return each due within 5 calendar days of the case specialist's request; claims otherwise filed within NAAA Appendix I periods — https://site.manheim.com/solutions/arbitration (accessed 2026-10-28)
- ACV Auctions 10-K for 2025 (filed 2026-02-23): total revenue $759.6M ($678.0M marketplace and service, $81.6M customer assurance); 829,276 marketplace units (743,008 in 2024); marketplace GMV $10.4B; 22,062 buyers and 14,905 sellers; Go Green seller assurance against defects missed in the condition report; price guarantee sales; Virtual Lift undercarriage imaging; True360 reports, ACV MAX inventory software, ClearCar trade-in tools, ACV Transportation and ACV Capital floorplan; inspectors meter every paint surface and read and clear trouble codes; titles processed "by a dedicated ACV team or automated through machine learning"; competitors named as Manheim (Cox Enterprises), Adesa ("a subsidiary of Carvana") and OPENLANE — https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1637873/000163787326000011/acva-20251231.htm (accessed 2026-10-28)
- Copart agreed on 2026-09-10 to acquire ACV for $10.50 a share in cash, an implied equity value of ~$1.9B; Copart cited more than 250 locations; the tender offer expired 2026-10-15 and the merger completed 2026-10-16, making ACV a wholly owned Copart subsidiary — ACV 8-K 2026-09-10 with Exhibit 99.1, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1637873/000095010326013780/dp253207_ex9901.htm, and 8-K 2026-10-19, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1637873/000119312526425225/d139534d8k.htm (accessed 2026-10-28)

## Search summaries (figures used in the vault are marked "(search summary)")
- NAAA arbitration policy title framework (2024 and 2025 policy texts as summarised by search; NAAA PDFs returned 403): the seller has an auction-chosen number of days to deliver title; after that the buyer may return the vehicle or wait; the seller can cure by producing title by the close of the next business day after notice; after 90 calendar days the title guarantee lapses; vehicles sold without title should be announced "title attached/absent" (blue light). Local rules seen: 21, 30 and 45 days.
- DealShield (Cox Automotive): launched 2014 as a 21-day return guarantee; 30-day option added 2018; by 2024 a 21-day/360-mile or 30-day/500-mile guarantee applied to eligible purchases by subscription, returnable at any participating location, at over 175 auction locations — Cox Automotive and Auto Remarketing pages surfaced by search (https://www.coxautoinc.com/insights-hub/dealshield-crosses-major-milestone-fueled-by-ongoing-dealer-demand-and-satisfaction/)
- AuctionACCESS: accepted at over 400 marketplaces in the US and Canada; individual membership $103 a year; operated by AuctionACCESS, LLC — https://auctionaccess.com/faqs/index.html (via search results)
- Auction Edge: EDGE Pipeline covers ~206 independent auctions and ~331,000 vehicles (transport-company guides, 2026); the Pipeline Market Report is driven by sale data from more than 175 EDGE Pipeline auctions and was integrated into OPENLANE's autoniq (Auction Edge release, 2024); a 2021 release said more than 240 independent auctions managing more than 4 million vehicles a year used Auction Edge platforms — https://www.auctionedge.com/blog/autoniq-pmr (via search results)
- UVeye and Cox Automotive's vAuto launched an AI service-lane inspection offering in January 2026; UVeye says its systems sit at dealerships, fleet sites and auction lots — https://www.coxautoinc.com/press-releases/cox-automotives-vauto-and-uveye-bring-ai-powered-vehicle-inspections-to-market/ (via search results)
- OPENLANE marketing describes inspections combining professionals with AI damage detection, engine audio analysis and OBD2 diagnostics — https://www.openlane.com/online-auctions/ (via search results)
- Copart UK added a free EV Battery Health Report built with Aviloo (July 2026); Shoreham Vehicle Auctions (UK) ran 1,000 battery checks with Generational, median state of health 95.6% (October 2026) — https://remarketing.copart.co.uk/2026/07/15/ev-battery-health-report/ and https://www.motortrader.com/motor-trader-news/automotive-news/shoreham-accelerates-roll-out-of-ev-battery-testing-at-auction-12-10-2026 (via search results). No US auction battery-grading product was confirmed.
