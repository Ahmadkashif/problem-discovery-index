# Sources — Interior Design Firms

## Checked
- US interior design services (NAICS 54141) employer firms subject to federal income tax reported ~$19.3B total revenue in 2022, up from ~$12.2B in 2017 (Census Service Annual Survey, series REVEF54141TAXABL) — https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/REVEF54141TAXABL (accessed 2026-10-22)
- ~$19.7B industry revenue in the 2022 Economic Census; 16,797 establishments and 55,286 employees in County Business Patterns 2023 (third-party page citing Census; 54141 has a single six-digit code, 541410) — https://vantainsights.com/industry/541410-interior-design-services (accessed 2026-10-22)
- First Research puts the industry at ~13,000 establishments with ~$12B revenue and the 50 largest firms at ~15% of revenue (lower than Census; kept only as the concentration figure) — https://www.firstresearch.com/industry-research/Interior-Design-Services.html (accessed 2026-10-22)
- BLS: 97,100 interior designer jobs in 2025; median pay $67,190 (May 2025); 26% self-employed; 30% work in specialized design services, 14% in architectural and engineering services, 12% in retail trade, 6% in construction; licensing varies by state between practice, title and no regulation — https://www.bls.gov/ooh/arts-and-design/interior-designers.htm (accessed 2026-10-22)
- 29 US states, Washington DC, Puerto Rico and eight Canadian provinces have legislation regulating interior design (CIDQ) — https://www.cidq.org/for-advocates/legislative-map/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Practice-act jurisdictions listed as Florida, Louisiana, Nevada, Oklahoma and DC, with ~22 title-registration states (Harbor Compliance; the sources disagree, so the vault says "a handful of practice-act states") — https://www.harborcompliance.com/interior-design-certification (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Studio Designer acquired Mydoma in July 2024; together they serve nearly 20,000 designers in the US and Canada; Studio Designer earlier bought DesignDocs — https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240725985972/en and https://businessofhome.com/articles/studio-designer-acquires-mydoma (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Studio-software price points: DesignFiles $49–69/month; Ivy by Houzz Pro from $99/month; Programa from $59/month per user; Studio Designer ~$72–129/month per user depending on source — https://blog.designfiles.co/ivy-vs-mydoma-vs-studio-designer/ and https://programa.design/blog/interior-design-project-management-software (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Designer product markups commonly quoted at 10–35%, with 35% cited as an average; a 2012 industry report put product sales at ~60% of a design firm's revenue (dated) — https://blog.designfiles.co/how-should-i-markup-products/ and https://www.homeaccentstoday.com/industry-news/asids-2012-annual-report-provides-in-depth-look-at-us-interior-design-industry (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Material Bank founder said designers spent up to 39% of their day searching for and ordering products (2019 interview); the platform is free to designers and lists 500,000+ products — https://www.workdesign.com/2019/10/material-bank-is-a-game-changer-for-ad-specifiers/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- ThinkLab is SANDOW's research division, created from the acquisition of Contract Consulting Group; it claims 10,000+ annual industry interviews and a network affecting the specification of $263B in product categories — https://sandow.com/brands/thinklab/ and https://officeinsight.com/companies/sandow-acquires-contract-consulting-group-rebrands-as-thinklab/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- HqO acquired the Leesman Index on 14 June 2022; the dataset holds ~1M office respondents and ~300,000 home-based respondents from 6,410 organisations — https://www.hqo.com/resources/releases/hqo-acquires-global-workplace-employee-experience-ex-measurement-leader-leesman-index-bringing-1m-data-points-to-hundreds-of-customers-worldwide/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Configura CET Designer: space planning plus ordering with manufacturer extensions carrying rules, parts and pricing; ~17,000 design professionals use it; dealer designer job ads require CET, accurate BOMs and SIF files for order entry — https://officeinsight.com/?p=29793 and https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/boldbusiness/jobs/4146929009 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- JLL puts average US office fit-out cost at ~$280/sq ft (2025); Cushman & Wakefield's 2025 US guide covers 40 cities, ~$108–219/sq ft, and finds TI allowances not keeping pace with fit-out costs — https://www.jll.com/en-in/insights/global-office-fit-out-costs-guide-2025 and https://www.cushmanwakefield.com/en/united-states/insights/office-fit-out-cost-guide (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Hotel PIPs: triggered by franchise renewal, change of ownership, conversion or standards updates, typically every 7–15 years; select-service change-of-ownership PIPs quoted at ~$10,000–40,000 per key and full-service at $50,000–150,000+; the FF&E reserve (commonly 4% of gross revenue) is separate from the PIP; HVS/Nehmer Hotel Cost Estimating Guide is the paid reference — https://www.peony.ink/blog/hotel-data-room and https://www.matthews.com/things-to-consider-if-your-hotel-pip-is-coming-due/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- PIP-specialist consultancies exist (The Litt Group: PIP negotiation with brands, retaining purchasing and design groups) — https://lodgingmagazine.com/litt-group-continues-focus-pip-renovations/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
