- AMC at December 31, 2025: 855 theatres and 9,640 screens in 11 countries, of which 533 theatres and 7,072 screens in the US; 224 IMAX screens (186 US), 181 Dolby Cinema screens (174 US), 162 in-house PLF screens; ~39M Stubs member households (~78M individuals) and Stubs members ~51% of US attendance; ~73% of US tickets bought online, ~87% of those through AMC's own site and apps; US average revenue per theatre ~$7.0M; films licensed "on a film-by-film and theatre-by-theatre basis", rent usually tied to box office and less often a fixed settlement rate; 33,311 employees of whom 30,380 part-time (~91%), many "at or near the applicable minimum wage"; ADA Title III compliance; the exclusive window has fallen "from approximately four months to approximately one-and-one half months or less" over a decade; 2025 North American box office ~$8.9B, up ~1.5% — AMC Entertainment FY2025 Form 10-K, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1411579/000141157926000016/amc-20251231x10k.htm (accessed 2026-10-22)
