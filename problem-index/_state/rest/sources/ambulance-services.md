- CMS RSNAT prior authorization page: nationwide expansion in waves on 1 December 2021 (AR, CO, LA, MS, NM, OK, TX), 1 February 2022, 1 April 2022, 1 June 2022 and 1 August 2022 (the remaining states, plus Railroad Retirement Board beneficiaries nationwide); from 9 January 2025 the standard review timeframe changed from 10 business days to 7 calendar days and the expedited review option was removed (update dated 14 November 2024); for claims on or after 3 August 2020, prepayment review resumes "if prior authorization has not been requested by the fourth round trip in a 30-day period", and "Claims for the first three round trips are permitted to be billed without prior authorization"; prior authorization is voluntary — https://www.cms.gov/Research-Statistics-Data-and-Systems/Monitoring-Programs/Medicare-FFS-Compliance-Programs/Prior-Authorization-Initiatives/Prior-Authorization-of-Repetitive-Scheduled-Non-Emergent-Ambulance-Transport- (accessed 2026-10-29)
- Coordinator note check: "review stops at the fourth round trip without a request" does not hold as worded. At CMS, claims are stopped for prepayment medical review when no prior authorization has been requested by the fourth round trip in a 30-day period; the first three round trips may be billed without it. The 7-day review holds (7 calendar days since 9 January 2025). The vault uses the CMS wording.
- CMS MLN6805343, "Repetitive, Scheduled Non-Emergent Ambulance Transport Prior Authorization Model" (January 2026): repetitive means three or more round trips in a 10-day period or at least one round trip per week for three weeks; coverage under 42 CFR 410.40(e); a MAC may affirm up to 40 round trips for up to 60 days, more with an added request; the request includes a physician certification statement (PCS) signed by the attending physician, with credentials, dated within 60 days before the requested start date, plus supporting medical documentation "from the patient's clinician and not the ambulance supplier"; review takes up to 7 calendar days; submission by mail, fax, esMD (document type 8.1) or MAC portal; HCPCS A0426 (ALS1 non-emergency) and A0428 (BLS non-emergency) are subject to prior authorization — https://www.cms.gov/files/document/mln6805343-repetitive-scheduled-non-emergent-ambulance-transport-prior-authorization-model.pdf (accessed 2026-10-29)
