# Sources — Music Publishers

## Checked
- NAICS 512230 Music Publishers: 833 establishments and 5,355 employees in County Business Patterns 2023; ~$7.0B "market size" from the 2022 Economic Census (third-party page citing Census) — https://vantainsights.com/industry/512230-music-publishers (accessed 2026-10-22). The vault derives ~6 employees per establishment from these two figures.
- Census Service Annual Survey, total revenue for Music Publishers (512230), all establishments, employer firms: $5,035M (2018), $5,476M (2019), $5,729M (2020), $6,129M (2021), $7,124M (2022) (series REVEF51223ALLEST) — https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/REVEF51223ALLEST (accessed 2026-10-22)
- 512230 definition: establishments primarily engaged in acquiring and registering copyrights for musical compositions, promoting and authorising their use; publishers of music books and sheet music are included (search-result summary of the NAICS definition) — https://www.highergov.com/naics/512230-music-publishers (accessed 2026-10-22, via search result text)
- NMPA (annual meeting, 10 June 2026): US music publishing revenue of $7.29B (~$7.3B) in 2025, up ~3.7% on $7.04B in 2024; publishing outgrew recorded music for the fourth year running; mix of performance 52%, synchronisation 24%, mechanical 19%, other 5% (MBW article returned 403; figures from search-result summaries of MBW and Interspace) — https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/us-music-publishing-revenues-hit-7-3b-in-2025-nmpa-reveals-at-annual-meeting-honoring-pnk-julian-bunetta-and-harvey-mason-jr/ (accessed 2026-10-22, search result only)
- Same meeting (Interspace write-up): $7.29B in 2025 versus $2.17B in 2014; NMPA said Spotify's and Amazon's audiobook bundles cut royalty streams by ~30% and, on Spotify's own estimates, have cost songwriters and publishers "almost $480m" since 2024; NMPA announced licences with AI music companies Udio (signed) and Klay (agreement in principle); Kobalt had struck a similar deal with ElevenLabs — https://interspacemusic.com/blog/nmpa-inks-udio-klay-ai-deals-us-publishing-revenue-reaches-7-29bn/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- The MLC 2024 annual royalty recap: $859.7M distributed in 2024 ($814.7M direct plus $45.0M covered by DSP voluntary licences); average initial match rate 84.1% rising to 89.8% after reprocessing; blanket unmatched royalties held ~$273.8M, 8.4% of royalty pools processed; total blanket royalties pending at year-end $519.8M — https://pages.themlc.com/2024-annual-royalty-recap (accessed 2026-10-22)
- The MLC market share distribution page: the MLC intends to begin distributing remaining unmatched and unclaimed accrued royalties on a market share basis in April 2027, starting with January 2021 usage (the first month DSPs reported under the blanket licence), one usage month per monthly distribution; less than $7M of January 2021 royalties remained in January 2026 and just over $6M in September 2026; the database snapshot for the April 2027 distribution is taken around 10 February 2027; claims and registrations are typically processed within 21 days and match suggestions within about 30 days; allocation is pro rata using actual distribution data — https://www.themlc.com/marketshare (accessed 2026-10-22)
- The MLC announced the April 2027 market share date at its annual membership meeting on 29 September 2026 and reports more than $4.2B distributed since January 2021 (MBW headline and search-result summary) — https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/the-mlc-has-paid-out-more-than-4-2bn-to-songwriters-and-publishers-to-date-and-will-begin-market-share-distributions-of-unmatched-and-unclaimed-royalties-in-april-2027/ (accessed 2026-10-22, search result only)
- The Copyright Office designated Mechanical Licensing Collective, Inc. as the MLC effective 8 July 2019; the blanket licence became available, and the MLC began administering it, on 1 January 2021 — https://copyright.gov/newsnet/2019/update07052019.html and https://www.themlc.com/news/the-mechanical-licensing-collective-begins-full-operations-as-envisioned-by-the-music-modernization-act-of-2018-mechanical-licensing-collective (accessed 2026-10-22, via search result text)
- The Register of Copyrights signed the rule continuing the MLC's designation on 29 May 2026; published in the Federal Register on 3 June 2026, with recommendations on database tools and disclosure (search-result summary) — https://rightstech.com/2026/06/copyright-office-reups-the-mlcs-designation-as-the-statutory-mechanical-licensing-collective/ (accessed 2026-10-22, search result only)
- MLC overclaims: an overclaim is multiple claimants claiming shares totalling over 100% of a work; resolving it is the claimants' responsibility, shown in the MLC's Overclaims Tool; a work can only enter overclaim if shares are added within 90 days of registration — https://help.themlc.com/en/support/what-is-an-overclaim-and-how-do-i-resolve-one-using-the-overclaims-tool (accessed 2026-10-22, via search result text)
- MLC dispute policy covers work overclaims and share-level counterclaims and can hold only the disputed shares in suspense — https://www.themlc.com/dispute-policy (accessed 2026-10-22, via search result text)
- HFA escalates a song-level conflict to the MLC if it remains in conflict after two weeks; a publisher-side help page says disputes typically take several weeks to several months — https://help.harryfox.com/if-i-notify-hfa-of-a-song-level-conflict-and-the-publisher-of-record-does-not-respond-will-hfa-still-update-the-song-to-reflect-my-share and https://help.usemogul.com/en/articles/14759519-what-to-do-if-another-publisher-has-already-claimed-your-royalties (accessed 2026-10-22, via search result text)
- ASCAP calendar 2025: revenue $1.945B (+6.0%); $1.759B made available for distribution (+3.7%), of which $1.304B from US performances and $455M from performances abroad; domestic revenue $1.471B (search-result summaries of MBW, Music Week and Inside Radio) — https://www.musicweek.com/publishing/read/ascap-revenue-collections-up-6-in-2025/093573 (accessed 2026-10-22, search result only)
- BMI fiscal 2022: $1.573B revenue and $1.471B distributed and administered (latest BMI figure found; no fiscal 2025 figure surfaced) — https://www.insideradio.com/free/record-setting-revenue-and-royalty-distributions-at-bmi/article_df34b2fe-3386-11ed-b7b0-cbc970f6aa01.html (accessed 2026-10-22, search result only)
- CISAC Global Collections Report 2025: 2024 creators' royalties €13.97B (+6.6%) across 228 societies in 111 countries; music €12.59B (~90%); digital €5.1B; US music collections ~$3.62B (€3.14B, +10.9%) — https://www.cisac.org/cisac-global-collections-report-2025 and https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2025/11/06/cisac-music-collections-2024-report/ (accessed 2026-10-22, via search result text)
- Copyright Royalty Judges, Phonorecords V (docket 25-CRB-0013-PR, rates for 2028–2032): petitions to participate due 30 January 2026; participants include Amazon, Apple, Google, Pandora, Spotify and the Copyright Owners (NMPA and NSAI); Global Music Rights withdrew — https://app.crb.gov/case/detail/25-CRB-0013-PR%20%282028-2032%29 (accessed 2026-10-22, via search result text)
- Phonorecords V Subpart B (physical, downloads, ringtones) settlement filed 29 June 2026 by the majors, A2IM, NMPA, NSAI and MAC, keeping current rates with inflation adjustment; comments due 10 August 2026; SGA, Word Collections, Eight Mile Style and George Johnson objected — https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2026/07/01/phonorecords-v-proposed-settlement/ (accessed 2026-10-22; DMN 13 July follow-up fetched)
- Phonorecords V streaming rate proposals were filed by participants without a settlement on 5 October 2026 (MBW headline and search-result summary; article not opened) — https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/phono-v-streaming-rate-proposals-filed-with-us-copyright-royalty-board-as-dima-points-to-30-growth-in-publishing-revenues-since-2022/ (accessed 2026-10-22, search result only)
- 17 U.S.C. § 203: termination may be exercised during a five-year window beginning 35 years after publication or 40 years after execution of the grant, whichever is earlier; notice must be served not less than two nor more than ten years before the effective date and recorded with the Copyright Office before the effective date; applies to grants executed by the author on or after 1 January 1978 — https://copyright.gov/recordation/termination.html and https://www.copyright.gov/fedreg/2008/73fr3898.html (accessed 2026-10-22, via search result text)
- EU Directive 2014/26/EU (collective management): Article 13(1) distribution to rightholders within nine months of the end of the financial year of collection, unless objective reasons prevent it; Article 13(4) amounts unpaid three years after the end of the collection year are non-distributable if the society took all necessary identification steps; Article 15(3) same nine-month rule between societies under representation agreements — https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2014/26/article/13 (accessed 2026-10-22, via search result text)
- Citrin Cooperman (published 7 April 2026): its music valuation group priced 566 catalogues worth nearly $13B combined in 2025, up from 557 catalogues worth $10.7B in 2024; average size ~$23M (2025) versus ~$19M (2024); older publishing catalogues around 15x, older masters and mixed catalogues above 17x, younger masters 13.7x; Barry Massarsky heads the practice — https://cm.citrincooperman.com/In-Focus-Resource-Center/2025-Music-Catalog-Valuations-Top-13-Billion (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Citrin Cooperman bought Massarsky Consulting (January 2022) — https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2022/01/19/massarsky-consulting-sale/ (accessed 2026-10-22, search result only)
