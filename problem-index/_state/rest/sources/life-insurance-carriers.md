# Sources — Life Insurance Carriers

## Checked
- 711 US life insurers at year-end 2024 (530 stock, 106 mutual, 65 fraternal, 10 other), down from 830 in 2014 — ACLI Life Insurers Fact Book 2025, "Key U.S. Life Insurers Statistics" and Table 1.1 (ACLI tabulations of NAIC data) — https://www.acli.com/-/media/public/pdf/news-and-analysis/publications-and-research/2025fb/all_acli_fact_book_2025.pdf (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Life insurers hold $9.3 trillion in total assets — same ACLI Fact Book 2025, overview text (accessed 2026-10-22)
- 2024 income of life insurers: life insurance premiums $173.7B, annuity considerations $432.5B, health (mostly disability, supplemental and long-term care) premiums $218.5B, total premiums and considerations $824.7B; investment income $361.9B; aggregate income ~$1.29T — same ACLI Fact Book 2025, "Income of life insurers" (accessed 2026-10-22)
- 2024 payments: $88.5B to beneficiaries, $484.2B in surrender values, $109.8B in annuity payments — same ACLI Fact Book 2025 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- $22.0T of life insurance in force at year-end 2024 ($14.07T individual, $7.85T group) — same ACLI Fact Book 2025 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- 309,300 life insurance home-office personnel in 2024 (BLS Current Employment Statistics, via ACLI Table 1.4) — same ACLI Fact Book 2025 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Individual life voluntary termination rate (by face amount) 5.8% in 2024 (lapse 4.7%, surrender 1.1%); 7.9% by number of policies — ACLI Fact Book 2025, Tables 7.4 and 7.5 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- 65 fraternal life insurers with $209B in assets and $358B of insurance in force (2024) — ACLI Fact Book 2025 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- US retail annuity sales a record $464.1B in 2025 (+7%): FIA $127.9B, RILA $79.5B, fixed-rate deferred $165.3B, traditional VA $63.1B — LIMRA, 2026 news release — https://www.limra.com/en/newsroom/news-releases/2026/limra-final-u.s.-retail-annuity-sales-set-new-sales-high-totaling-$464.1-billion-in-2025/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- VM-22 (principle-based reserving for non-variable annuities) effective January 2026 with a three-year implementation period, mandatory for all new issues January 2029; Valuation Manual operative 1 January 2017, PBR an accreditation standard from 1 January 2020 — NAIC PBR topic page — https://content.naic.org/insurance-topics/principle-based-reserving (accessed 2026-10-22); 2026 effective status also stated by Oliver Wyman — https://www.oliverwyman.com/our-expertise/insights/2026/feb/navigating-vm-22-annuity-strategy.html (search result, accessed 2026-10-22)
- NAIC Generator of Economic Scenarios (Conning's GOES) replaced the Academy Interest Rate Generator in the Valuation Manual from January 2026 — Conning news item "NAIC GOES Go Live Jan 2026" — https://www.conning.com/about-us/news/naic-goes-go-live-jan-2026 (accessed 2026-10-22; year taken from headline and publication date)
- NY DFS Insurance Circular Letter No. 7 (2024) on AI systems and external consumer data in underwriting and pricing, adopted in final form 11 July 2024; insurers may not rely solely on a vendor's claim of non-discrimination — Debevoise summary — https://www.debevoise.com/insights/publications/2024/07/nydfs-adopts-final-circular-on-use-of-ai ; DFS page https://www.dfs.ny.gov/industry-guidance/circular-letters/cl2024-07 (via search, accessed 2026-10-22)
- Colorado life insurer governance regulation (3 CCR 702-10, under SB21-169) effective 14 November 2023; progress report due 1 June 2024; annual compliance attestation from 1 December 2024; ECDIS includes credit scores, purchasing habits, home ownership and insurance risk scores derived from them — Alston & Bird — https://www.alston.com/en/insights/publications/2023/10/new-ai-regulation-from-colorado-dept-of-insurance (accessed 2026-10-22)
- 49 jurisdictions had implemented the 2020 best-interest revisions to the NAIC Suitability in Annuity Transactions Model Regulation (#275) as of 8 August 2025 — NAIC government affairs brief — https://content.naic.org/sites/default/files/government-affairs-brief-annuity-suitability-best-interest-model.pdf (via search, accessed 2026-10-22)
- SOA Product Development newsletter (August 2024): 11 years of random-holdout and post-issue-audit monitoring data on 33,000+ lives across 30 accelerated underwriting programmes; ~81% of audited cases assessed at the same risk class the AU decision gave — https://www.soa.org/sections/product-dev/product-dev-newsletter/2024/august/pm-2024-08-seeman/ (via search, accessed 2026-10-22)
- 2019 SOA AU survey: mortality assumptions where requirements were waived were ~1%–10% higher than fully underwritten — https://www.soa.org/globalassets/assets/files/resources/research-report/2019/accelerated-underwriting-survey.pdf (via search, accessed 2026-10-22)
- MIB member companies account for ~99% of individual life policies issued in the US and Canada (FTC figure as cited by Wikipedia); MIB holds coded flags, not medical records; MIB, Inc. is a consumer reporting company — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIB_Group and CFPB listing https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/credit-reports-and-scores/consumer-reporting-companies/companies-list/mib-inc/ (via search, accessed 2026-10-22)
- Milliman IntelliScript sells prescription history, medical claims (up to seven years of Rx and Dx) and EHR data with risk scores for life, disability, LTC and Medicare supplement underwriting, regulated as a consumer reporting company under FCRA; a WoodmenLife testimonial says medical data cut APS orders nearly in half — https://www.rxhistories.com/irix/ and https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/credit-reports-and-scores/consumer-reporting-companies/companies-list/milliman-intelliscript/ (via search, accessed 2026-10-22)
- Life settlement LE underwriting is concentrated in a few firms (ITM TwentyFirst, AVS, Fasano, LSI in a 2016-era study); underwriters rarely agree on LEs; Fasano and ITM TwentyFirst (both under Longevity Holdings) self-publish actual-to-expected ratios; Texas licenses and Florida registers LE providers — https://lifesettlements.pl/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/DatingDeathAnEmpiricalComparisonofMedicalUnderwritersintheUSLifeSettlementsMarket.pdf ; https://elsa-sls.org/member/longevity-holdings/ ; https://elsa-sls.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/ELSA-Fact-Sheet-Q3-25.pdf (via search, accessed 2026-10-22)
- Ruark Consulting's pooled FIA study (2021): ~4 million policyholders, 16 participating companies, $264B account value; detailed and company-level results sold to participants; 2021 VA study: 21 companies — https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20210302005805/en/Ruark-Consulting-Releases-2021-Fixed-Indexed-Annuity-Studies (via search, accessed 2026-10-22)
- LIMRA/Life Happens 2025 Insurance Barometer: total need gap 40% of adults, ~100 million Americans without adequate coverage; 51% of adults own coverage — https://www.limra.com/en/research/research-series/2025-insurance-barometer-series/ (via search, accessed 2026-10-22)
