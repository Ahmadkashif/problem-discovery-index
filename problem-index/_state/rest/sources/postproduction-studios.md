# Sources — Postproduction Studios

## Checked
- US teleproduction and other postproduction services (NAICS 512191) employer firms subject to federal income tax reported ~$4.88B total revenue in 2022 (Census Service Annual Survey, series REVEF512191TAXABL, $M: 2016 5,161; 2017 5,080; 2018 4,868; 2019 4,671; 2020 4,153; 2021 4,468; 2022 4,882) — https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/REVEF512191TAXABL (accessed 2026-10-22)
- ~3,144 establishments and ~22,670 employees in County Business Patterns 2023, and a ~$6.4B 2022 Economic Census revenue figure for 512191 (third-party page citing Census; the gap to the $4.88B Service Annual Survey figure was not reconciled, so the vault quotes both as approximate) — https://vantainsights.com/industry/512191-teleproduction-and-other-postproduction-services (accessed 2026-10-22)
- NAICS 512191 covers establishments primarily engaged in specialized motion picture or video postproduction services: editing, film/tape transfers, subtitling, credits, closed captioning, and animation and special effects — https://www.worldoftaxonomy.com/system/naics_2022/node/512191 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- BLS: ~72,000 film and video editor and camera operator jobs in 2025 (39,400 editors, 32,600 camera operators); editors' median pay $75,420 (May 2025); 31% of editors work in the motion picture and video industries; 35% self-employed; editor employment projected to grow 4% over 2025–35; assistants track each shot in a database and load footage — https://www.bls.gov/ooh/media-and-communication/film-and-video-editors-and-camera-operators.htm (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Netflix rejects an entire IMF delivery if any track referenced in the CPL fails inspection, and corrections are made by supplemental redelivery; Dolby Vision metadata edit-rate mismatches are a documented failure code; the v5.1 spec requires at minimum L1 Dolby Vision metadata for every frame and does not accept material mastered only for HDR10 — https://backlothelp.netflix.com/hc/en-us/articles/18843271869587 and https://backlothelp.netflix.com/hc/en-us/articles/360035076554-DolbyVisionInspector-MismatchedFrameRate and https://backlothelp.netflix.com/hc/en-us/articles/7262346654995 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Netflix checks IMF packages with its Photon tool at upload; IBC notes ambiguities in delivery specs are a recurring reason for redeliveries (vendor blog and trade article) — https://www.veneratech.com/delivering-to-netflix-qc-requirements-in-a-nutshell-and-how-to-comply-with-them/ and https://ibc.org/publish/looking-ahead-the-ongoing-evolution-of-imf/3202.article (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Netflix sound mix spec: average loudness -27 LKFS ±2 LU dialog-gated (ITU-R BS.1770-1), true peaks generally no higher than -2 dBTP, Atmos loudness measured on a 5.1 re-render; an older QC page cites ±3 LU and a -24 LKFS BS.1770-3 fallback — https://backlothelp.netflix.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001794307-Netflix-Sound-Mix-Specifications-Best-Practices-v1-5 and https://backlothelp.netflix.com/hc/en-us/articles/360050414014-Loudness-and-True-Peaks-How-to-Measure-and-When-to-Flag (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Netflix Post Partner Program (NP3): facilities vetted for onboarding, infrastructure, security, testing and Netflix workflows; productions are not required to use NP3 vendors; current focus includes dubbing and audio description, and "quality control" references were removed from the NP3 section in 2021 — https://backlothelp.netflix.com/hc/en-us/articles/360058892114-What-are-Netflix-s-Partner-Managed-Programs (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Technicolor Group's creative studios collapsed in February 2025: the UK arm entered administration with most of 440+ UK staff made redundant, and US offices of The Mill, MPC Advertising and Mikros Animation closed; the group had employed 10,000+ worldwide — https://britishcinematographer.co.uk/technicolor-goes-into-administration-in-uk/ and https://www.newscaststudio.com/2025/02/24/technicolor-creative-studios-files-for-uk-administration-shutters-u-s-offices/ and https://filmstories.co.uk/news/technicolor-the-storied-firms-collapse-reflects-a-wider-crisis-in-the-vfx-industry/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- VFX vendors bid fixed prices by shot count with no allowance for change hours, eroding thin margins; underbidding to win awards; R&D folded into shot costs (SIGGRAPH and trade commentary, 2013–2018) — https://www.siggraph.org/whats-wrong-with-the-visual-effects-industry and https://postmagazine.com/Publications/Post-Magazine/2017/December-1-2017/Outlook-2018-The-need-for-heightened-VFX-busines.aspx (accessed 2026-10-22)
- IATSE VFX units at Marvel Studios and Walt Disney Pictures ratified first contracts in May 2025 (Avatar unit in February 2025): overtime, pension and health, rest periods; the contracts do not cover the thousands employed by third-party VFX vendors — https://www.thewrap.com/iatse-ratified-vfx-works-marvel-studios-avatar-disney/ and https://aflcio.org/2025/5/21/service-solidarity-spotlight-vfx-workers-ratify-first-three-contracts-major-us-studios (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Shotgun was renamed ShotGrid in 2021 and Autodesk Flow Production Tracking on 26 March 2024 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ShotGrid and https://autodesk.com/support/technical/article/shotgrid-rebrand-frequently-asked-questions (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Streamland Media's companies include Picture Shop, Formosa Group (sound), Picture Head, Ghost VFX, The Farm and Finalé; Streamland bought Technicolor's post business (reported ~$36.5M, January 2021) — https://www.televisual.com/news/the-farms-parent-streamland-merges-its-vfx-companies/ and https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/behind-screen/postproduction-shake-up-technicolors-post-business-to-be-sold-to-formosa-groups-parent-company (accessed 2026-10-22)
- UK Audio-Visual Expenditure Credit: UK VFX costs on film and high-end TV get a 39% gross (29.25% net) rate and are exempt from the 80% cap on qualifying costs, for expenditure from 1 January 2025 with the relief in force from 1 April 2025 — https://www.bfi.org.uk/news/enhanced-vfx-tax-credit-uk-film-high-end-television-productions and https://www.saffery.com/insights/articles/additional-tax-relief-support-for-the-uks-visual-effects-sector/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Australia's PDV Offset is 30% of qualifying Australian production expenditure on post, digital and visual effects; the minimum spend has been $500,000, with government reform papers announcing a rise to $1M (current threshold not confirmed) — https://www.ato.gov.au/forms-and-instructions/film-industry-incentives-2023 and https://www.infrastructure.gov.au/sites/default/files/fact-sheet-screen-production-funding-and-incentives-reforms_2.pdf (accessed 2026-10-22)
- FCC caption quality standards (47 CFR 79.1(j)(2)) cover accuracy, synchronicity, completeness and placement, adopted 2014 and effective from 2015 — https://www.dwt.com/insights/2014/02/fcc-adopts-closed-caption-quality-standards and https://www.3playmedia.com/2018/01/11/accessibility-laws-for-media-and-entertainment/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- FX Research counted ~599–600 adult scripted original series in 2022 (a record) and 516 in 2023 (-14%) — https://thewrap.com/john-landgraf-fx-peak-tv-over and https://www.emarketer.com/content/us-scripted-series-declined-14-2023 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- MPA's Trusted Partner Network (TPN) accredits third-party assessors who evaluate vendors against the MPA Content Security Best Practices (v5.0 added application and cloud security in 2023); TPN+ is the platform where studio members view assessment results; TPN gives no pass/fail grade — https://www.motionpictures.org/press/motion-picture-association-selects-tmt-insights-to-build-trusted-partner-networks-global-platform-for-cloud-based-security-assessment/ and https://www.signiant.com/newroom/signiant-joins-trusted-partner-network-tpn-and-completes-app-and-cloud-gold-shield-assessment and https://broadcastbeat.com/news/tpn-launches-upgraded-program-and-new-tpn-platform-to-advance-entertainment-content-security (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Luminate Film & TV (formerly Variety Insight / Variety Business Intelligence) maintains a production and credits database and a production pipeline from pre-production through completion, updated daily, sold by premium subscription — https://luminatedata.com/faq and https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/pp/prodview-mfkxljpiux2hm (accessed 2026-10-22)
