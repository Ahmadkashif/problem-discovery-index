# Sources — Property & Casualty Insurers

## Checked
- US P&C industry 2025: net premiums written $976.8B (2024: $938.7B), combined ratio 92.9% (2024: 96.9%), underwriting gain $68.7B, net income $150.6B, policyholders' surplus a record $1.27T at 31 Dec 2025, direct premiums written up 4.6% to ~$1.1T, personal lines roughly half of DPW — https://content.naic.org/sites/default/files/2025-annual-property-and-casualty-and-title-insurance-industries-analysis-report.pdf (accessed 2026-10-23)
- Loss and LAE reserves rose 5.1% to $1.03T ($869.2B unpaid losses, $162.9B unpaid LAE); one-year reserve redundancy $20.0B, with personal lines $15.9B favourable; the 2025 development-by-line chart shows other liability occurrence at $7,278M and commercial auto liability at $1,837M on the adverse side; commercial auto liability pure loss ratio 71%; NAIC names social inflation, nuclear verdicts and third-party litigation financing as the drivers of commercial liability reserve pressure — same NAIC report (accessed 2026-10-23)
- CIAB Q4 2025 survey: commercial premiums +1.9% on average across lines, commercial auto +6.6%, commercial property −0.7% — quoted in the same NAIC report (accessed 2026-10-23)
- 2025 countrywide direct premiums written $1,109.8B (98.25% of filings received by 18 Mar 2026); top 10 groups 47.87% and top 25 groups 65.71% of the market; State Farm $115.3B (10.39%), Progressive $84.2B; homeowners multiple peril $188.7B; total private passenger auto $371.0B; total commercial auto $77.1B; other liability $139.5B; workers' compensation $56.1B — https://content.naic.org/sites/default/files/research-actuarial-property-casualty-market-share.pdf (accessed 2026-10-23)
- Employment at P/C direct insurers 699.3K in 2025 (preliminary) and 682.9K in 2024, BLS data compiled by Triple-I — https://www.iii.org/fact-statistic/facts-statistics-industry-overview (accessed 2026-10-23)
- NAIC Property & Casualty Market Intelligence data call (announced 8 Mar 2024): more than 400 property insurers, more than 80% of the US property market by premium, ZIP-code-level premiums, policies, claims, losses, limits, deductibles and non-renewals, 70+ data points, 90 days to submit — https://content.naic.org/article/states-issue-property-casualty-market-intelligence-data-call-covering-over-80-us-market (accessed 2026-10-23)
- P&C Statement of Actuarial Opinion is filed with the annual statement by 1 March; the confidential Actuarial Opinion Summary goes to the domiciliary state by 15 March unless the state sets another date; workpapers kept seven years — https://content.naic.org/sites/default/files/2025-annual-2026-quarterly-financial-statement-filing-deadlines-12-19-2025.pdf and https://www.dfs.ny.gov/apps_and_licensing/insurance_companies/as_prop (accessed 2026-10-23)
- Nuclear verdicts (awards of $10M+) against corporate defendants: nearly 200 in 2025, up 40.7% on 2024, about $25.6B in total, 40 above $100M; product liability 29 nuclear verdicts totalling $12B (Marathon Strategies report) — https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/national/2026/08/25/882676.htm (accessed 2026-10-23)
- US surplus lines direct premium written $143.3B in 2025, up 10.4% from $129.8B; 27.5% of commercial lines DPW and 12.9% of total P&C DPW (AM Best US Surplus Lines Market Segment Report) — https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/us/news/excess-surplus/surplus-lines-hit-143bn-record-as-placement-market-shifts-589475.aspx (accessed 2026-10-23)
- California Department of Insurance completed its review of the Verisk wildfire catastrophe model on 24 Jul 2025, the first model through the Sustainable Insurance Strategy ratemaking review; carriers using cat models in rate filings must commit to writing more policies in wildfire-distressed areas — https://www.claimsjournal.com/news/national/2025/07/24/332013.htm (accessed 2026-10-23)
- FTC finalised its order against GM and OnStar on 14 Jan 2026: a five-year ban on disclosing consumers' geolocation and driver-behaviour data to consumer reporting agencies, with affirmative consent required for collection; GM says it ended its relationships with LexisNexis and Verisk when it ended Smart Driver in 2024 — https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/01/ftc-finalizes-order-settling-allegations-gm-onstar-collected-sold-geolocation-data-without-consumers and https://hintzelaw.com/blog/2026/1/26/ftc-finalizes-order-against-gm-and-onstar-over-driver-data (accessed 2026-10-23)
- NAIC members adopted the Model Bulletin on the Use of Artificial Intelligence Systems by Insurers on 4 Dec 2023; the NAIC tracks state adoption on an implementation map — https://content.naic.org/index.php/article/naic-members-approve-model-bulletin-use-ai-insurers and https://content.naic.org/sites/default/files/legal-adoption-map-ai-model-bulletin.pdf (accessed 2026-10-23)
- US commercial litigation finance: $2.3B of new commitments in 2024 across 42 active funders (Westfleet Insider 2024) — https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/2-3-billion-committed-in-us-commercial-litigation-finance-amid-capital-contraction-in-2024--302411359.html (accessed 2026-10-23)
- NICB: a not-for-profit financed by assessments on member insurers based on premium; nearly 400 employees; membership of more than 1,200 property-casualty companies and other organisations; nine member organisations supplied ~55% of assessment revenue in 2022–2023 — https://www.nicb.org/about-nicb and https://www.nicb.org/annual-reports/annual-report-2023 (accessed 2026-10-23)
- Quadrant Information Services: founded 1991, processes rate filings from state regulators into standardised rate sets, supports more than 20,000 carrier rate sets across 50 states and DC, data updated monthly from filings received daily, licensed to carriers and publishers (NerdWallet's quotes use it) — https://quadinfo.com/about/ (accessed 2026-10-23)

- ZestyAI reports more than 500 regulatory approvals of its property risk models across 317 carrier filings in 44 states (company-reported figure, via trade press) — https://runtimewire.com/article/zestyai-500-regulatory-approvals-property-risk-models (accessed 2026-10-23)
- Demotech was approved by the SEC as an NRSRO in July 2022 (registration 11 Jul 2022) in the class of credit ratings for insurance companies; AM Best and KBRA are also on the SEC's NRSRO list — https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/national/2022/07/19/676526.htm and https://www.sec.gov/ocr/ocr-current-nrsros.html (accessed 2026-10-23)
- Florida HB 837 signed 24 Mar 2023: general negligence limitation period cut from four years to two, modified comparative negligence (a plaintiff more than 50% at fault recovers nothing), one-way attorney fee statute §627.428 repealed — https://www.flgov.com/eog/news/press/2023/governor-ron-desantis-signs-comprehensive-legal-reforms-law and https://www.gtlaw.com/en/insights/2023/4/tort-reform-overhauls-floridas-litigation-landscape (accessed 2026-10-23)
