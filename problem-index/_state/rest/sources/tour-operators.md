# Sources — Tour Operators

## Checked
- NAICS 561520 Tour operators in 2022: 2,513 employer firms, 2,771 establishments, receipts $9,029,899K (~$9.0B), annual payroll $1,533,774K (~$1.5B), 28,158 employees. Same file, for comparison: 561510 travel agencies 7,541 firms and ~$32.3B; 561591 convention and visitors bureaus 863 firms and ~$2.7B; 561599 all other travel arrangement and reservation services 1,661 firms and ~$25.0B. State rows for 561520: California 350 firms and ~$1.47B receipts, Florida 324 firms and ~$1.10B, New York 214 firms and ~$0.52B, Massachusetts 90 firms and ~$1.18B (2022 Economic Census, sector 56 basic file, US and state rows) — https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/economic-census/data/2022/sector56/EC2256BASIC.zip (accessed 2026-10-23)
- NAICS 561520 in 2023: 2,584 employer establishments, 27,113 employees, annual payroll ~$1.66B; by size, 1,711 establishments (~66%) have fewer than five employees, 415 have 5-9, 226 have 10-19, 153 have 20-49, 44 have 50-99, 25 have 100-249, 7 have 250-499, and the 500-999 and 1,000+ classes are suppressed (three establishments between them by subtraction) (County Business Patterns 2023, US file) — https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/cbp/datasets/2023/cbp23us.zip (accessed 2026-10-23)
- Tripadvisor's Experiences segment (Viator) reported gross booking value of ~$4.7B in 2025, ~$4.2B in 2024 and ~$3.7B in 2023; growth came from more experiences booked, partly offset by lower average prices from third-party channels; segment revenue rose $84M in 2025 (Tripadvisor FY2025 Form 10-K, via search result summary) — https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1526520/000119312526051281/trip-20251231.htm (accessed 2026-10-23, via search result). Q3 2025 Viator revenue $294M and GBV $1.3B per an earnings summary — https://quartr.com/events/tripadvisor-inc-trip-q3-2025_3Y1ZMapy (accessed 2026-10-23, via search result)
- GetYourGuide moved some suppliers' commission from 20% to 30% effective 3 July 2025, notified by email during peak season; GetYourGuide called it a targeted adjustment for a small number of partners; a supplier quoted Viator's basic commission at 27% (Arival) — https://arival.travel/article/getyourguide-commission-increasing-for-some-operators (accessed 2026-10-23, via search result)
- USTOA's $1 Million Travelers Assistance Program: each Active Member posts $1M security (bond, letter of credit or Treasury bill) held by the USTOA Tour Depositors Trust; it reimburses consumers' lost deposits and payments on a member's bankruptcy, insolvency or cessation of business, or failure to refund within 120 days of a cancellation; cover is $1M in aggregate per member, which may not cover a large operator's failure; Active Members must have been in business at least three years — https://ustoa.com/travelers-assistance and https://ustoa.com/ustoa-membership (accessed 2026-10-23, via search results)
- Checkfront and Rezdy merged (closed 15 June 2023), with Germany's Regiondo, as RC TopCo; described by Arival as the largest deal in the sector since Booking.com bought FareHarbor in 2018; Checkfront had 5,000+ clients and 100+ employees; Arival estimated such reservation providers serve under 5% of the global market — https://arival.travel/checkfront-and-rezdy-merge/ and https://betakit.com/checkfront-merges-with-australias-rezdy-to-scale-online-tourism-reservations/ (accessed 2026-10-23, via search results)
- Lindblad Expeditions FY2025: Land Experiences segment comprises Natural Habitat, Off the Beaten Path, DuVine, Classic Journeys and Thomson Group; Natural Habitat averages eleven guests per trip, the others ten or fewer (DuVine 14 or fewer); Land Experiences guests book on average nine months before travel, pay a deposit at booking and final payment 60-120 days before travel; travel agents and wholesalers were ~10% of Land Experiences guest ticket revenue; US guests 93%; the company uses forward contracts and/or options to manage a portion of currency risk on charter and tour contracts (Form 10-K) — https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1512499/000143774926005873/lindb20251231c_10k.htm (accessed 2026-10-23). Land Experiences tour revenues of $275.4M in 2025, up 24%, on more guests and higher pricing (Q4 2025 results summary) — https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/02/lindblad-2025-q4-and-full-year-financial-results/ (accessed 2026-10-23, via search result)
- Tour and travel guides (SOC 39-7010): ~46,760 employed nationally in May 2023, median $17.34 an hour (~$36,060 a year) (BLS OEWS, via search result summary; BLS pages return 403 to direct fetches) — https://www.bls.gov/oes/2023/may/oes397010.htm (accessed 2026-10-23, via search result)
- ATTA's annual Adventure Travel Trends & Insights report (formerly Snapshot), in its 18th year, is based on a multilingual operator survey; 81% of operators reported a documented safety and risk management plan (72% the year before); 73% reported higher 2024 revenue; full report limited to Business and Professional members; a separate 2025 Adventure Travel Market Sizing Report surveyed nearly 5,000 travellers in 13 countries — https://learn.adventuretravel.biz/research/2025-adventure-travel-trends-insights-state-of-the-adventure-travel-industry and https://syta.org/blog/atta-releases-2025-annual-adventure-travel-trends-insights-report (accessed 2026-10-23, via search results)
- Arival's Global Operator Landscape (3rd edition) drew on ~7,000 operators worldwide surveyed April-August 2024; its 2025 U.S. Experiences Traveler Outlook surveyed 800 US travellers and sells for $399 to non-members; trade coverage cites an Arival and Phocuswright figure of ~$271B global travel experiences revenue in 2025 — https://arival.travel/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Unlocking-2025-Arival-Civitatis.pdf and https://v2.arival.travel/?p=182066 and https://www.traveldailynews.com/tag/arival/ (accessed 2026-10-23, via search results)
- Globus marks guaranteed departures "GD"; unmarked departures may be cancelled for low numbers, typically up to 45 days before departure for Europe/UK and 30 days for other regions in the cited terms (an older article, ~2015); TourRadar's help pages say operators may cancel when the minimum number of passengers is not met — https://www.intltravelnews.com/node/2386 and https://support.tourradar.com/en/customers/what-happens-if-my-tour-is-cancelled-by-the-tour-operator (accessed 2026-10-23, via search results)
- WorldStrides was reported (Skift, August 2020) to run educational trips for ~550,000 students a year with 7,000 schools and 800 universities; a later company description says 400,000+ students — https://skift.com/2020/08/14/the-state-of-educational-travel-in-a-world-gone-virtual/ and https://all-languages.org.uk/news/corporate-member-news/introducing-worldstrides (accessed 2026-10-23, via search results)
- HBX Group (Hotelbeds) H1 2025: total transaction value €3.4B (+12%), revenue €319M; FY2025 guidance €740-790M revenue; main clients are tour operators, travel agencies and other distributors; a secondary report gives FY2025 TTV of ~€8.2B and revenue ~€720M (not checked against the primary release) — https://www.streetinsider.com/PRNewswire/HBX+GROUP+ANNOUNCES+HALF+YEAR+2025+FINANCIAL+RESULTS/24791671.html (accessed 2026-10-23, via search result)
- The UK Air Travel Trust is funded by ATOL Protection Contributions per protected passenger, paid by ATOL holders; it is no longer insurance-backed after the Thomas Cook payout of 2019; the CAA publishes an open list of ATOL holders — https://www.caa.co.uk/atol-protection/air-travel-trust/about-the-air-travel-trust/ and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Travel_Organisers%27_Licensing (accessed 2026-10-23, via search results)
- Civil Aviation Act 1982, section 23: the CAA, its members and employees must not disclose information relating to a particular person furnished under the Act or an Air Navigation Order, without that person's written consent or a CAA determination after representations, with listed exceptions (Secretary of State, certain international bodies, criminal proceedings); breach is an offence punishable by fine or, for individuals on indictment, up to two years' imprisonment — https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1982/16/section/23 (accessed 2026-10-23)
- The CAA's Aviation & Travel Finance function regulates about 1,600 ATOL holders, gives financial supervision to the 100 leading tour operators, oversees UK airlines and manages the Air Travel Trust; analysts grant, renew and vary licences and carry out year-round financial monitoring (CAA job listing) — https://careers.caa.co.uk/job/London-Senior-Analyst-Airline-&-Travel-Finance-Licensing/1398288233/ (accessed 2026-10-23, via search result). Holders with ATOL limits of £20M or more get an in-depth risk-based assessment (CAA policy 2016/01) — https://caa.co.uk/publication/download/14465 (accessed 2026-10-23, via search result)
- National Park Service Commercial Use Authorizations (Public Law 105-391, section 418) are required for commercial guided services in parks; parks set categories (guided vehicle tours, step-on guides, guided hiking, kayaking) and some cap the number of permits; an online CUA application system was rolled out for the 2024-2025 seasons — https://www.nps.gov/grte/learn/management/upload/2024-CUA-Handbook.pdf and https://www.nps.gov/thro/learn/news/2024-commercial-use-authorization.htm (accessed 2026-10-23, via search results)
- Tourplan is a Christchurch-based enterprise system for inbound tour operators and DMCs (quoting, FIT, groups, MICE), listed from ~$900 per user per month on Capterra; Tourwriter targets FIT multi-day itinerary businesses, listed from ~$149 — https://www.getapp.com/hospitality-travel-software/a/tourplan-is/ and https://www.tourwriter.com/?p=21039 (accessed 2026-10-23, via search results)
- Tour operators generally hedge currency in advance while bedbanks tend to buy at spot (Travolution); an XE guide says packages are often sold 3-12 months before departure while suppliers are paid 30-90 days before travel — https://travolution.com/articles/2114/tour-operators-face-serious-currency-hedging-concerns and https://www.xe.com/da/blog/business/how-travel-agencies-save-on-multi-currency-transactions (accessed 2026-10-23, via search results; vendor content)
- Tour directors are on call around the clock during a tour (Mental Floss; Alberta occupation profile); a Tauck forum poster says base pay is low and the recommended daily tip stayed at $10 for about a decade before rising to $12 — https://www.mentalfloss.com/culture/jobs/secrets-tour-directors and https://alis.alberta.ca/occinfo/occupations-in-alberta/occupation-profiles/tour-director/ and https://forums.tauck.com/bestof/like/p48?Page=p102 (accessed 2026-10-23, via search results)
