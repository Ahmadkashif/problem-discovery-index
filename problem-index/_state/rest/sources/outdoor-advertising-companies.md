- US indoor and outdoor display advertising (NAICS 541850) had 2,166 firms, 2,601 establishments, ~$8.33B in receipts ($8,332,604K), $1.66B annual payroll and 27,361 employees in 2022 — 2022 Economic Census, Sector 54 basic table EC2254BASIC (US row for NAICS 541850, "Indoor and outdoor display advertising"), https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/economic-census/data/2022/sector54/EC2254BASIC.zip (accessed 2026-10-22)

- US out-of-home advertising revenue was a record ~$9.46B in 2025, up 3.6%, the 19th straight quarter of growth; Q4 2025 up 4.8%; digital out-of-home was 36.3% of revenue and grew 10.5%; transit grew 9.2%, the fastest format for a second year — MediaPost on OAAA's full-year release (March 17, 2026), https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/413543/oaaa-reports-ooh-advertising-rose-36-in-2025.html ; https://digitalsignagepulse.com/news/us-out-of-home-advertising-revenue-reaches-record-9-46-billion (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Lamar FY2025: ~159,300 billboard displays in 45 states and Canada (~79,600 bulletins, ~79,700 posters); ~5,500 digital billboards producing ~33% of billboard net revenue; 144,400+ logo displays on ~43,700 structures; ~40,600 transit displays; ~71,500 leased sites and ~11,200 owned parcels; leases run from month-to-month to over ten years; annualised site lease expense ~$348.1M, ~17% of billboard net revenue; local advertising ~79% of outdoor net revenue; 3,500+ employees including ~1,000 local account executives; top categories by billboard net revenue: service 19%, health care 10%, restaurants 9%, retailers 8%, automotive 8%; the 10-K discusses the Highway Beautification Act, permit and condemnation proceedings, amortisation of nonconforming signs and possible new digital restrictions — Lamar Advertising FY2025 Form 10-K, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1090425/000109042526000008/lamr-20251231.htm (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Lamar added ~500 digital billboards in 2025 and billboard revenue was ~$2.01B of ~$2.27B net revenue — secondary summaries of the same 10-K: https://billboardinsider.com/4-things-about-lamar-5/ ; https://bigboards.io/companies/lamar-advertising/ (search-result excerpts, accessed 2026-10-22)
- Outfront FY2024: 39,556 billboard displays and 520,013 transit displays (559,569 in total); 1,935 digital billboards; ~19,600 lease agreements with ~17,900 landlords, terms from one month to multiple years; billboard property lease expense $482.8M in 2024 (34% of billboard revenue), $499.7M in 2023 (35%); impairment charges of $534.7M in 2023 and $17.9M in 2024 on the MTA asset group and transit reporting unit; 2,149 employees; Geopath named as the industry's audience measurement system — OUTFRONT Media FY2024 Form 10-K, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1579877/000157987725000034/out-20241231.htm (accessed 2026-10-22)

- The Highway Beautification Act requires states to maintain effective control of outdoor advertising along federal-aid primary and Interstate routes or lose 10% of their federal-aid highway funds — Arizona DOT, https://azdot.gov/about/historic-and-scenic-roads/about-the-highway-beautification-act ; 23 U.S.C. §131 (accessed 2026-10-22). - City of Austin v. Reagan National Advertising (2022): the Supreme Court held 6–3 that a sign code treating off-premises signs differently from on-premises signs is content-neutral; Austin had refused applications to digitise existing grandfathered off-premises signs — https://www.gibsondunn.com/supreme-court-holds-that-regulation-applying-different-rules-to-off-premises-advertisements-is-content-neutral/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_Austin_v._Reagan_National_Advertising_of_Austin,_LLC (accessed 2026-10-22)
- FHWA's September 2007 memo allowed states to permit commercial electronic variable message signs under acceptable criteria, suggesting display times of four to ten seconds with eight recommended; Tennessee's 2007 Public Chapter 76 requires a static display of at least eight seconds, a change time of at most two seconds, no animation or video, and 2,000-foot spacing on interstates — https://www.digitalsignagetoday.com/news/federal-highway-administration-gives-digital-billboards-the-green-light/ ; https://publications.tnsosfiles.com/acts/105/pub/pc0076.pdf (accessed 2026-10-22)
