# Sources — Electric Distribution Utilities

## Checked
- US revenue from retail electricity sales to ultimate customers was $514,396 million in 2024 (all sectors), up from $491,370 million in 2023; full-service providers $420,861 million, delivery-only providers $42,135 million, energy-only providers $51,400 million — EIA Electric Power Annual Table 2.3 — https://www.eia.gov/electricity/annual/html/epa_02_03.html (accessed 2026-10-24)
- 2024 revenue from sales to ultimate customers by sector: residential $244,367 million, commercial $185,043 million, industrial $84,095 million, transportation $890 million — EIA Electric Power Annual Table 2.6 — https://www.eia.gov/electricity/annual/html/epa_02_06.html (accessed 2026-10-24)
- 1,998 public power utilities serve more than 55 million people (APPA 2026 Public Power Statistical Report); public power is 59% of utilities and 15% of customers, rural cooperatives 26% of utilities and 13% of customers, investor-owned utilities 5% of utilities and 66% of customers — APPA Stats and Facts — https://www.publicpower.org/public-power/stats-and-facts and https://www.publicpower.org/system/files/documents/2026-APPA-About-Public-Power-Fact-Sheet.pdf (accessed 2026-10-24)
- Form EIA-861 is a census of US electric utilities (sales, revenue, customers by sector, SAIDI/SAIFI since 2013, net-metered and small distributed generation, AMR/AMI meter counts, demand response, distribution circuits); 2025 final data released 2026-10-07; next early release August 2027 covering ~3,300 utilities; smaller utilities file the short form EIA-861S — https://www.eia.gov/electricity/data/eia861/ (accessed 2026-10-24)
- US customers averaged 11 hours of interruptions in 2024; major events (Hurricanes Beryl, Helene, Milton) caused 80% of those hours — EIA Today in Energy, 2025-12-01 — https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=66744 (accessed 2026-10-24)
- Investor-owned utilities projected to invest more than $1.1 trillion in 2025–2029 and nearly $208 billion in 2025 (EEI) — Utility Dive, 2025-10-09 — https://www.utilitydive.com/news/investor-owned-utilities-spending-more-than-ever-eei/802315/ (accessed 2026-10-24)
- Data centres used about 4.4% of US electricity in 2023 and are projected at about 6.7%–12% by 2028 (325–580 TWh) — DOE summary of the LBNL 2024 United States Data Center Energy Usage Report — https://www.energy.gov/articles/doe-releases-new-report-evaluating-increase-electricity-demand-data-centers (accessed 2026-10-24)
- Electric and gas utilities requested nearly $31 billion of rate increases in 2025, more than double 2024 (PowerLines analysis of public filings) — E&E News, 2026-01-29 — https://www.eenews.net/articles/utilities-sought-a-record-31b-in-rate-increases-last-year/ (accessed 2026-10-24)
- As of May 2024, 58 utilities and state agencies had published hosting capacity maps in 26 states, DC and Puerto Rico; DOE compiles the list but does not maintain the maps — DOE U.S. Atlas of Electric Distribution System Hosting Capacity Maps — https://www.energy.gov/eere/us-atlas-electric-distribution-system-hosting-capacity-maps (accessed 2026-10-24)
- Power distributors and dispatchers: 9,040 employed nationally, mean wage $105,460; 5,600 of them in electric power generation, transmission and distribution (BLS OEWS May 2023) — https://www.bls.gov/oes/2023/may/oes518012.htm (accessed 2026-10-24)
- Electrical power-line installers and repairers: ~120,170 employed, mean wage $85,900; 57,100 in electric power generation, transmission and distribution and 36,320 in utility system construction (BLS OEWS May 2023) — https://www.bls.gov/oes/2023/may/oes499051.htm (accessed 2026-10-24)
- California's Office of Energy Infrastructure Safety reviews 2026–2028 base Wildfire Mitigation Plans by draft decision, 30-day public comment and final approval or denial (SDG&E approved 2026-02-03; Liberty Utilities draft denial 2026-02-18) — https://energysafety.ca.gov/news/2026/02/03/energy-safety-issues-approval-of-san-diego-gas-and-electrics-2026-2028-wildfire-mitigation-base-plan/ and https://energysafety.ca.gov/news/2026/02/18/energy-safety-issues-draft-denial-of-liberty-utilities-2026-2028-wildfire-mitigation-base-plan-solicits-public-comment/ (accessed 2026-10-24, via search results)
- Power transformer lead times averaged 128 weeks in Q2 2025 — Wood Mackenzie, 15 October 2025 — https://www.woodmac.com/news/opinion/mind-the-gap-tackling-supply-chain-challenges-in-the-electric-td-sector/ ; power and distribution transformers in supply deficits of 30% and 6%, distribution transformer unit costs up 78–95% since 2019 — Wood Mackenzie, 13 August 2025 — https://www.woodmac.com/news/opinion/transformer-troubles-manufacturing-and-policy-constraints-hit-us-transformer-supply/ ; pad-mount distribution transformers ~75 weeks ARO on average — Wood Mackenzie, 27 June 2024 — https://www.woodmac.com/news/opinion/4-years-into-a-difficult-transformers-market-in-the-us-is-there-a-potential-end-in-sight/ (primary pages fetched by the electrical-distributors fix round, 2026-10-24; URLs and quotes in `_state/rest/sources/electrical-distributors.md`). Correction: the first build quoted distribution units at ~30 weeks after a 100+ week 2023 peak and a 10% distribution deficit, from secondary search summaries (EE Power and a Wood Mackenzie press-release mirror). Wood Mackenzie's own pages do not support those figures, and they have been removed from the vault.
