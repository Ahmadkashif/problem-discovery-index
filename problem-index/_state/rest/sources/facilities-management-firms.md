- Total expenses of $24.3B for the same population in 2022 (FRED FSSEAEEF35612, as reported in search results; not opened) — https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/FSSEAEEF35612 (accessed 2026-10-23)

- NAICS 561200 employment of 190,720 (OEWS May 2023), mean annual wage $54,430. Largest occupations: janitors and cleaners 41,700 ($36,840); correctional officers and jailers 14,170 ($44,960); general maintenance and repair workers 12,990 ($55,850); maids and housekeeping cleaners 6,930; hand laborers and material movers 5,160; general and operations managers 3,690 ($116,290); HVAC and refrigeration mechanics 3,500 ($66,030); first-line supervisors of housekeeping and janitorial workers 3,380; groundskeepers 3,020; electricians 2,420 ($77,480); project management specialists 1,950; facilities managers 1,840 ($109,940). - GAO-26-107255 (21 August 2026): an estimated $285 billion DOD maintenance backlog in FY2025; the services aim to fund 90% of maintenance needs and fund about 80%; DOD "doesn't consistently track work orders" and the data are generally unreliable for judging maintenance effectiveness; 13 recommendations, DOD concurred with 7 — https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-26-107255 (accessed 2026-10-23)
- GAO-22-104481: DOD's Sustainment Management System implementation over three years behind schedule, without dedicated funding and inconsistent across components on facility condition assessments — https://www.gao.gov/products/GAO-22-104481 (accessed 2026-10-23, as reported in search results)
- FAR 42.1503: contractors have up to 14 calendar days from notification to comment on a CPARS evaluation; the evaluation becomes available to source selection officials no later than 14 days after notification; disagreements are reviewed at a level above the contracting officer; the comment period was cut from 30 to 14 days in 2014 — https://acquisition.gov/node/31281 and https://www.arnoldporter.com/en/perspectives/advisories/2014/06/new-rule-significantly-decreases-contractor-comm__ (accessed 2026-10-23)
- FAR 52.222-43 (2018 text): the Service Contract Labor Standards wage determination current at each anniversary or option period applies; the price is adjusted only for the increase the new determination requires; the contractor must notify the contracting officer within 30 days of receiving a new wage determination — https://www.acquisition.gov/far/52.222-43 (accessed 2026-10-23)
- EPA Emissions Reduction and Reclamation rule under the AIM Act, published 11 October 2024: appliances with 15 lb or more of an HFC refrigerant with GWP above 53 must repair leaks above 30% (industrial process refrigeration), 20% (commercial refrigeration) or 10% (comfort cooling and others); single compliance date of 1 January 2026; key provisions took effect on that date per a February 2026 trade report — https://www.bdlaw.com/allyn-l-stern/publications/epa-finalizes-hfc-emissions-reduction-and-reclamation-program/ and https://digital.bnpmedia.com/article/OPINION/5111068/860824/article.html (accessed 2026-10-23)
- NYC Local Law 97: most buildings over 25,000 sq ft; penalty of $268 per metric ton CO2e over the limit; first report due 1 May 2025 on calendar-2024 energy use; reports certified by a registered design professional; failure to file $0.50 per sq ft per month — https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/pdf/presentations/2023bsls/ll97.pdf and https://www.cozen.com/news-resources/publications/2025/new-york-city-local-law-97-deadline-may-1-2025 (accessed 2026-10-23)

- NYC Open Data publishes Local Law 87 energy audit data collected on the DOE Asset Score Audit Template, about 2,000 fields including energy conservation measures, HVAC systems and tenant spaces, covering 2019–2024 with a previous dataset for 2012–2018 (Mayor's Office of Climate and Environmental Justice) — https://data.cityofnewyork.us/Environment/LL87-Energy-Audit-Data/au6c-jqvf and https://data.cityofnewyork.us/api/views/au6c-jqvf.json (accessed 2026-10-23)
