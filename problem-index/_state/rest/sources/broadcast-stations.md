# Sources — Broadcast TV & Radio Stations

## Checked
- FCC broadcast station totals as of 30 June 2026 (Public Notice DA 26-652, released 14 July 2026): AM 4,300; FM commercial 6,560; FM educational 4,806; full-power commercial TV 1,390 (UHF 1,041, VHF 349); educational TV 387 (UHF 270, VHF 117); Class A TV 397; LPTV 1,772; low power FM 2,013; FM translators and boosters 8,846; total broadcast stations 33,539 — https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DA-26-652A1.txt (accessed 2026-10-22)
- S&P Global Market Intelligence (reported by MediaPost, 9 January 2025): total US TV station revenue, advertising plus retransmission, ~$40.4B in 2024, projected to fall 7% to ~$37.6B in 2025 on the post-election drop; BIA in the same article: local TV advertising including digital and OTT projected at $21.3B in 2025 (−20% with political), core local TV advertising +3.6% to $21B excluding political; local TV retransmission fees $14.3B in 2024, growing to $15.6B by 2027 — https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/402447/total-tv-station-revenue-to-sink-7-in-2025-sp-g.html (accessed 2026-10-22)
- BIA Advisory Services with the Radio Advertising Bureau (2 April 2025): 2025 US local radio revenue, over-the-air and digital combined, projected at $12.3B, roughly stable on 2024 — https://news.radio-online.com/articles/b18076/2025-Local-Radio-Ad-Revenue-Projected-to-Reach-$123B (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Radio Ink (6 March 2025): BIA's 2025 radio forecast has over-the-air revenue down 6% and combined OTA plus digital down 4.9% including political (−0.8% excluding political) — https://radioink.com/?p=88880 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- BLS OEWS, May 2023, NAICS 516100 Radio and Television Broadcasting Stations: total employment 121,850; producers and directors 19,670; broadcast announcers and radio DJs 15,450; advertising sales agents 12,700; news analysts, reporters and journalists 10,660; broadcast technicians 9,380; general and operations managers 5,280; market research analysts and marketing specialists 2,780; camera operators 1,750; sales managers 1,670 — https://www.bls.gov/oes/2023/may/naics4_516100.htm (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Census Service Annual Survey (via FRED), total revenue for television broadcasting (old 51512 basis, which included networks), employer firms subject to income tax: $74.4B in 2022; not used as the station figure because it includes network revenue — https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/REVEF51512TAXABL (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Pillsbury (10 September 2026): commercial TV stations must elect must-carry or retransmission consent by 1 October 2026 for the cycle 1 January 2027 to 31 December 2029, and place the election statement in the online public inspection file for the three-year cycle; changes are notified to each provider with a copy to the FCC — https://www.pillsburylaw.com/en/news-and-insights/october-1-deadline-approaching-tv-stations-consent-elections.html (accessed 2026-10-22)
- 47 CFR 76.1601: a cable operator must give a broadcast television station written notice at least 30 days before deleting or repositioning it — https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/47/76.1601 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Pillsbury (23 September 2026): quarterly issues/programs lists are due in the public inspection file by the 10th of January, April, July and October; they list the station's "most significant programming treatment of community issues"; stations are generally expected to address at least five issues per quarter; the FCC's base fine for missing or late lists is $10,000; thorough lists reduce risk at licence renewal — https://www.pillsburylaw.com/en/news-and-insights/2026-3rd-quarter-issues-programs-list-advisory-broadcast-stations.html (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Corporation for Public Broadcasting (Missoula Current, 4 August 2025): Congress rescinded ~$1.1B in CPB funding in July 2025; CPB announced an orderly wind-down, with most staff positions ending 30 September 2025 and a small transition team through January 2026; Sen. Patty Murray said more than 1,500 stations would lose funding — https://missoulacurrent.com/corporation-public-broadcasting/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- FCC NextGen TV (ATSC 3.0) Further Notice of Proposed Rulemaking (October 2025): proposes letting each broadcaster decide when to end ATSC 1.0 simulcasting rather than mandating it; NAB had asked for transition deadlines of February 2028 (top 55 markets) and February 2030 (remaining markets); a final order was not confirmed in search results — https://www.newscaststudio.com/2025/10/07/fcc-nextgen-tv-atsc-3-transition-notice-proposed-rulemaking (accessed 2026-10-22, via search result text)
- Broadcast engineer shortage (Current, 2 February 2024, Dave Edwards): SBE surveys show an ageing transmitter workforce with few younger engineers; the US "is projected to require 5,100 broadcast engineers over the next decade due to the retirement of 6,200 existing professionals" (projection source not named); example of a public radio station whose chief engineer is its only engineer — https://current.org/2024/02/shortage-of-engineers-poses-technical-challenge-for-pubmedia-stations/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Nexstar–TEGNA: the FCC approved the combination on 19 March 2026 by granting waivers of its ownership rules, including the 39% national cap; Nexstar already controlled more than 200 stations — https://thedesk.net/2026/03/fcc-says-it-granted-ownership-rule-waivers-to-approve-nexstar-tegna-deal/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- WideOrbit acquired by Constellation Software's Lumine Group, closed 22 February 2023; operates as an autonomous unit; describes itself as system of record for 5,000+ stations and cable networks — https://wsgr.com/en/insights/wilson-sonsini-advises-wideorbit-in-acquisition-by-constellation-software-lumine-group.html (accessed 2026-10-22, via search result text)
- Lumine Group acquired Imagine Communications, including its Landmark Sales advertising product (SVG, 6 July 2026) — https://www.sportsvideo.org/2026/07/06/lumine-group-acquires-imagine-communications-continues-to-build-up-haul-of-media-businesses/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Nielsen Audio measures 250+ US radio metros: 48 PPM markets and 200+ diary markets, plus rural counties — https://www.nielsen.com/solutions/audience-measurement/audio-measurement/methodology/ (accessed 2026-10-22, via search result text)
- AdImpact 2026 midterm projection (The Desk, 26 August 2026): $11.6B total political spending, broadcast TV $5.6B (48%), CTV and streaming $2.7B (23%) — https://thedesk.net/2026/08/political-ad-spending-broadcast-tv-2026-midterm/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
