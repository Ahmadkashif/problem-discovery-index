- 46 CFR Part 541 (demurrage and detention billing): invoices only from ocean common carriers, marine terminal operators or NVOCCs; required elements include B/L and container numbers, the basis for the billed party's liability, free time and its start and end dates, container availability or earliest return date, dates charged, the tariff or contract rate applied, dispute contacts and a URL or QR code, and certifications that the charges comply and that the billing party's own performance did not cause them (§541.6); missing elements remove the obligation to pay (§541.5); carriers and terminals must invoice within 30 calendar days of the last day the charge accrued, or the billed party need not pay (§541.7); billed parties get at least 30 days to request mitigation, refund or waiver, and the billing party must try to resolve within 30 days (§541.8) — https://www.ecfr.gov/api/versioner/v1/full/2026-10-01/title-46.xml?part=541 (accessed 2026-10-24 via the eCFR versioner API).
