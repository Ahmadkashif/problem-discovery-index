# Sources — Libraries & Archives

## Checked
- FY 2022 Public Libraries Survey frame: 9,194 active public libraries (administrative entities) in the 50 states and DC, plus 44 in the outlying territories; 17,329 active outlets (central, branch, bookmobile, books-by-mail) in the 50 states and DC — https://imls.gov/sites/default/files/2024-06/2022_pls_data_file_documentation.pdf (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Same document, Appendix J means and medians for FY 2022 (N ≈ 9,176): mean total operating revenue $1,711,966 and median $288,766; mean operating expenditure $1,541,810; mean 15.4 and median 4.0 total paid employees (FTE); mean 5.6 and median 1.9 librarians (FTE); mean print materials spend $78,275 and electronic materials spend $67,484; mean 199,384 circulation, of which 53,355 electronic; mean 73,136 visits; mean 380 programmes — https://imls.gov/sites/default/files/2024-06/2022_pls_data_file_documentation.pdf (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Same appendix: mean local government revenue $1,472,746 against mean total operating revenue $1,711,966, so ~86% of public library operating revenue is local (derived) — same URL (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Derived from those means × N (so written with "~" in the vault): ~$15.7B public library operating revenue, ~$14.1B operating expenditure, ~141,000 paid FTE, ~51,000 librarian FTE, ~671M visits, ~1.83B circulation of which ~490M (~27%) electronic, ~$718M print and ~$619M electronic materials spend — same URL (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Libraries and archives employer-firm revenue (Census Service Annual Survey, NAICS 51912, which excludes government-operated libraries): $2,380M in 2022, $2,453M in 2021 — https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/REVEF51912ALLEST (accessed 2026-10-22)
- "There are over 120,000 libraries of all kinds in the United States today" (ALA) — https://www.ala.org/tools/atoz/statistics-library (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Librarians and library media specialists: 142,300 jobs, median pay $68,270 (May 2025) — https://www.bls.gov/ooh/education-training-and-library/librarians.htm (accessed 2026-10-22)
- OverDrive 2025: over 820 million digital checkouts through Libby and Sora (+10.9%), 379.4M of them ebooks; more than 200 library systems, consortia and school libraries passed one million digital checkouts; Kanopy 29.9M plays — https://www.infodocket.com/2026/01/21/new-data-from-overdrive-libraries-break-digital-lending-records-in-2025-with-over-820-million-checkouts-and-over-1-billion-minutes-streamed/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- OverDrive 2024: 739.5M digital checkouts (+17%) — https://www.infodocket.com/2025/01/27/new-data-from-overdrive-libraries-break-digital-lending-records-in-2024-with-over-739-million-checkouts/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- HarperCollins capped each library ebook licence at 26 loans in 2011 — https://publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/digital/content-and-e-books/article/46333-librarian-unhappiness-over-new-harper-e-book-lending-policy-grows.html (accessed 2026-10-22)
- 2025 Library Systems Report: Alma implementations at 2,745 libraries; EBSCO signed 159 FOLIO contracts in 2024; Ex Libris and Innovative increasingly merged under Clarivate's Academia and Government division — https://americanlibrariesmagazine.org/2025/05/01/2025-library-systems-report/ (accessed 2026-10-22, via search summary)
- OCLC FY2025: library services revenue $262.7M (FY24 $238.5M); 25.3M new WorldCat records; 25.2B searches; WorldShare Management Services at 850+ libraries; quality of 62% of WorldCat bibliographic records improved in the year; R&D $37.6M of which OCLC Research 8% — https://www.oclc.org/en/annual-report/2025/home.html (accessed 2026-10-22)
- OCLC v. Clarivate (filed June 2022) settled November 2022; Clarivate, Ex Libris and ProQuest ceased MetaDoor for records subject to the WorldCat policy — https://infodocket.com/2022/11/07/oclc-and-clarivate-have-settled-lawsuit (accessed 2026-10-22)
- ALA 2025 censorship data: 4,235 unique titles challenged, 713 attempts to censor library materials and services; 92% of challenges initiated by pressure groups, government officials and decision makers, up from 72% in 2024 — https://www.ala.org/news/2026/04/american-library-association-releases-2025-most-challenged-books-list-national-library (accessed 2026-10-22, via search summary)
- IMLS: March 2025 executive order put nearly all staff on leave and halted data collection and research; a federal court in Rhode Island permanently barred the dismantling on 21 November 2025 — https://ilovelibraries.org/article/federal-judge-halts-elimination-of-institute-of-museum-and-library-services/ and https://www.ila.org/news/6945/legislative-update-november-26-2025 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- OMB/NARA M-23-07: by 30 June 2024 agencies must manage permanent records electronically and transfer them to NARA in electronic format with metadata, with limited exceptions — https://www.archives.gov/files/records-mgmt/pdf/m-23-07.pdf (accessed 2026-10-22)
- NIH Public Access Policy took effect 1 July 2025 (moved up from 31 December 2025), removing the 12-month embargo — https://research.columbia.edu/node/4125 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Baker & Taylor closure announced October 2025 after a ReaderLink acquisition fell through on 26 September; supplier to over 5,000 public libraries; libraries moved mainly to Ingram — https://www.wcpo.com/news/local-news/hamilton-county/cincinnati/major-book-vendor-baker-taylor-shuts-down-after-nearly-200-years and https://www.iowapublicradio.org/ipr-news/2025-10-13/iowa-libraries-baker-and-taylor-shuts-down (accessed 2026-10-22)
- CREW (Continuous Review, Evaluation and Weeding) method from the Texas State Library and Archives Commission, with MUSTIE criteria; the manual leaves the final decision to local judgement — https://www.schoollibraryjournal.com/story/to-weed-or-not-to-weed-criteria-to-ensure-that-your-nonfiction-collection-remains-up-to-date-everyday-librarian and https://www.lili.org/forlibs/ce/able/course4/05criteria.htm (accessed 2026-10-22)
- OCLC Research "Taking Our Pulse" (2010): survey of 275 research libraries; backlogs persist and about half of archival collections had no online presence — https://www.oclc.org/research/publications/2010/taking-our-pulse-research-survey-special-collections.html and https://liberquarterly.eu/article/view/10603 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Hachette v. Internet Archive: Second Circuit affirmed on 4 September 2024 that the Archive's controlled digital lending was not fair use — https://www.authorsalliance.org/2024/09/05/hachette-v-internet-archive-update-second-circuit-court-of-appeals-rules-against-internet-archive/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Web of Science Core Collection selection decisions are made by in-house editors without publisher affiliations; eLife put on hold in October 2024 — https://clarivate.com/academia-government/scientific-and-academic-research/research-discovery-and-referencing/web-of-science/web-of-science-core-collection/editorial-selection-process/journal-evaluation-process-selection-criteria/ and https://retractionwatch.com/2024/10/24/elife-latest-in-string-of-major-journals-put-on-hold-from-web-of-science (accessed 2026-10-22)
- NAICS 519210 definition (collections of documents, archives, film archives, bookmobiles, lending libraries) — https://www.highergov.com/naics/519210-libraries-and-archives (accessed 2026-10-22)

- California Digital Library Shared Collections runs systemwide licensing for UC with an assistant director, licensing analysts and a licensing services manager; no evidence found of a 10+ analysis team at any consortium — https://cdlib.org/cdlinfo/2019/10/02/licensing-positions-available-at-the-california-digital-library and https://cdlib.org/cdlinfo/2018/11/28/cdl-job-openings-licensing-analyst-shared-content-service-coordinator/ (accessed 2026-10-22, via search summary)
- The OverDrive 2025 total counts loans "from libraries and schools" through Libby and Sora, so it includes K-12 school lending (Sora being OverDrive's K-12 app is recalled) — same infodocket URL as above (accessed 2026-10-22)

- COUNTER Release 5.1 usage reports, KBART title lists, MARC 21, RDA, BIBFRAME, LCSH and LCC, DACS and EAD, ArchivesSpace (Lyrasis), FADGI imaging guidelines, Transkribus handwriting recognition, Unsub (OurResearch), GOBI (EBSCO), OASIS (ProQuest), Research Monitor, Lucidea — recalled tool and standard names
