- Queued Up 2026 edition (LBNL with Interconnection.fyi, June 2026, data to end-2025): ~2,061 GW actively seeking interconnection (1,312 GW generation, 749 GW storage); active solar 773 GW (−19%), storage 749 GW (−16%), wind 220 GW (−19%), gas 253 GW (+86%); over 750 GW withdrawn and about 600 GW of new requests in 2025; the median project built in 2025 took 61 months from interconnection request to commercial operation, against 36 months in 2015 and 22 months in 2008; about 19% of projects (13% of capacity) requested 2000–2020 reached commercial operation by end-2025, and more than 70% of requests were withdrawn; 41% of capacity that signed interconnection agreements 2000–2022 had withdrawn by end-2025; 549 GW holds a draft or executed IA but is not online (256 GW solar, 161 GW storage, 76 GW wind, 45 GW gas); only 53 GW of the 2,290 GW active at end-2024 reached operation in 2025 (2% throughput); valid in-service dates exist for only 73% of operational projects and withdrawal years for 63% of withdrawn requests; most 2025 withdrawals happened in the cluster or system impact study phase; data collection led by Interconnection.fyi; some gaps filled with non-public data from transmission providers — https://eta-publications.lbl.gov/sites/default/files/2026-06/queued_up_2026_edition.pdf (PDF downloaded and read 2026-10-28; emp.lbl.gov pages returned 403)
- FERC Order No. 2023 (issued 28 July 2023): first-ready, first-served cluster studies; 45-day cluster request window, 150 days for the initial cluster study and 150 days for restudies; $5,000 application fee and $55,000–$250,000 study deposit by size; 90% site control at request and 100% at facilities study; commercial readiness deposits of 2× the study deposit, then 5%, 10% and 20% of assigned network upgrade costs; withdrawal penalties tied to those deposits; late-study penalties on transmission providers of $1,000 per business day for cluster studies, $2,000 for restudies and affected-system studies, $2,500 for facilities studies — K&L Gates summary, 2023-09-07 — https://www.klgates.com/Order-No-2023-Interconnection-Reform-is-Finally-Here-9-7-2023 (accessed 2026-10-28)
- OBBBA ends 45Y and 48E credits for wind and solar facilities placed in service after 31 December 2027 unless construction began by 4 July 2026; Treasury Notice 2025-42 (15 August 2025) removed the 5% safe harbor for wind and solar except solar of 1.5 MW AC or less, so the physical work test is the main route, with a four-calendar-year continuity safe harbor; it applies to facilities that had not begun construction before 2 September 2025; projects beginning construction by 31 December 2025 avoid the new material assistance (foreign entity) rules — Orrick, August 2025 — https://www.orrick.com/en/Insights/2025/08/IRS-Issues-Guidance-on-Beginning-of-Construction-for-Wind-and-Solar-Facilities-Under-the-OBBBA (accessed 2026-10-28)
- US clean energy tax credit transfers reached $21B in H1 2026 (Q2 2026 $14.9B, a record in Crux's series; H2 2025 $17.7B; H1 down 12.5% year on year); Crux forecasts $47.5–49B of transfers in 2026, up to 18% above 2025 — PV Tech, 2026-08-18, reporting Crux's mid-year report — https://www.pv-tech.org/us-clean-energy-tax-credit-transfers-us21-billion-h1-2026-crux/ (accessed 2026-10-28)
- LevelTen's North American market-averaged solar PPA index fell 4.8% quarter on quarter in Q2 2026 (1.8% excluding CAISO), the first fall in two years, to $61.40/MWh; LevelTen links the outlook to the 4 July 2026 OBBBA cutoff; the index reports prices developers offered on the LevelTen marketplace — LevelTen blog 2026-07-21, https://www.leveltenenergy.com/post/levelten-north-american-ppa-price-index-q2-2026 (percentages, cutoff and method) and PV Tech 2026-07-23, https://www.pv-tech.org/solar-ppa-prices-buck-trends-increasing-europe-falling-north-america-q2-2026/ ($61.40) (accessed 2026-10-28)
