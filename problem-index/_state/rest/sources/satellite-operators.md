- FCC adopted the Space Modernization Report and Order on 22 July 2026, replacing Part 25 with Part 100: GSO keeps the five-year milestone; processing-round NGSO systems keep 50% at six years and 100% at nine, with a $10M declining surety bond posted within 30 days of grant; non-processing-round NGSO systems follow an ITU-aligned schedule (one satellite within 7 years, 10% within 9, 50% within 12, 100% within 14); bonds dropped for GSO and non-round NGSO — https://www.gtlaw.com/de/insights/2026/8/fcc-space-modernization-series-part-1-the-new-bond-and-milestone-framework and https://www.dlapiper.com/en/insights/publications/2026/07/fcc-overhauls-space-and-satellite-licensing-process-key-changes (accessed 2026-10-22)
- ITU BR IFIC (Space Services) is published every two weeks; comments on API/A, CR/C (via CR/D) and certain Appendix 30/30A special sections must be prepared in SpaceCom and submitted by e-Submission within four months of the special section's publication — https://www.itu.int/fr/ITU-R/space/Pages/supportFAQBRIFIC.aspx (accessed 2026-10-22)
- FCC five-year post-mission disposal rule for LEO satellites adopted 29 September 2022 (4-0), replacing the 25-year guideline, with a two-year transition (satellites launched after 29 September 2024) and coverage of foreign satellites seeking US market access — https://www.satellitetoday.com/government-military/2022/09/30/fcc-adopts-5-year-rule-for-deorbiting-satellites/ and https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/FCC-22-74A1_Rcd.pdf (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Starlink satellites performed 207,152 collision-avoidance manoeuvres between December 2025 and May 2026, up from 148,696 in the previous half-year (SpaceX semiannual report to the FCC, as reported by Space.com, July 2026) — https://www.n2yo.com/satellite-news/Every-SpaceX-Starlink-satellite-has-to-dodge-a-collision-almost-weekly-and-experts-fear-the-worst/11012 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Office of Space Commerce: TraCSS continues on FY2026 appropriations of $52.5M, with 62 pilot users and more than 11,000 satellites covered; the FY2027 request of $11M for OSC (~80% cut) does not fund TraCSS (FedScoop, 17 July 2026) — https://fedscoop.com/space-commerce-official-tracss-continues-despite-budget-uncertainty/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- FCC Supplemental Coverage from Space framework (March 2024) kept the −120 dBW/m²/MHz aggregate out-of-band PFD limit; DA 25-197 granted SpaceX a conditional waiver to −110.6 dBW/m²/MHz, conditioned on resolving harmful interference to adjacent terrestrial networks — https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DA-25-197A1.pdf (accessed 2026-10-22)
