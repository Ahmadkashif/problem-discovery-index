# Sources — Drafting Services

## Checked
- 2,956 US drafting-services firms (NAICS 541340) operating 2,985 establishments with 9,178 employees, $614.7M annual payroll and $1,675.5M receipts in 2022; 2,510 firms (85%) have fewer than 5 employees, 2,891 (98%) fewer than 20, and only 65 have enterprise employment of 20 or more — Census Bureau, Statistics of U.S. Businesses 2022, U.S. & states 6-digit NAICS table (released 2025-04-10), https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/susb/tables/2022/us_state_6digitnaics_2022.xlsx via https://www.census.gov/data/tables/2022/econ/susb/2022-susb-annual.html (accessed 2026-10-22). Derived: ~$182,600 receipts and ~$67,000 payroll per employee.
- About 180,200 US drafter jobs in 2025; median pay $68,090 (May 2025); 49% employed in architectural, engineering and related services, 21% manufacturing, 13% construction; employment projected +1% 2025–35; ~14,400 openings a year; BLS expects CAD and BIM to reduce employment for many drafter types — BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook, Drafters, https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/drafters.htm (accessed 2026-10-22). Earlier 2024 edition figures (192,100 jobs; architectural and civil drafters 110,500, mechanical 42,900, electrical 21,600) were seen in search results only and are not used as headline figures.
- AISC Code of Standard Practice §4.4: approval documents (shop and erection drawings) are returned to the fabricator within 14 calendar days unless the contract says otherwise; returned as approved, approved subject to corrections, or not approved and resubmitted — ANSI/AISC 303-16 text, https://www.amiralikhalvati.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/dokumen.tips_ansiaisc-303-16-code-of-standard-practice-for-buildings-ansiaisc-303-are.pdf and AISC 303-05, https://itigalgamuwa.lk/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/15.Code-of-Standard-Practice-for-Steel-Buildings-and-Bridges.pdf; AISC 303-27 public review draft summary, https://www.aisc.org/media/0qzker5f/aisc-303-27_public-review.pdf (accessed 2026-10-22).
- AIA A201-2017 §3.12.4: shop drawings, product data and samples are not Contract Documents; §4.2.7 limits the architect's review to conformance with design intent, leaving dimensions and quantities to the contractor; §3.12.8 approval does not relieve the contractor of errors — A201-2017 copy, https://case.edu/facilities/sites/default/files/2024-10/500_A201-2017%20General%20Conditions%20of%20the%20Contract%20for%20Construction.pdf; commentary https://www.csemag.com/shop-drawing-review-what-does-the-contract-say/ (accessed 2026-10-22).
- National Institute of Steel Detailing founded 1969; members in the US, Canada, Australia, England, India, the Philippines and New Zealand; runs the Individual Detailer Certification (Bridge and Structural/Miscellaneous; Senior Detailer Class I, Detailer Class II; open-book exam up to eight hours) and a Quality Procedures Program — https://www.nisd.org/about/ and https://www.nisd.org/individual-detailer/ (accessed 2026-10-22).
- NISD-linked guidance that contractor pressure for fast submittals pushes fabricators to force detailers to submit unchecked drawings — https://www.structuremag.org/article/the-nisd-difference/ (accessed 2026-10-22, via search summary).
- ACI 315 defines the detailer as the drafter who prepares reinforcing-bar placing drawings and bar lists, and requires placing drawings to be approved before bar fabrication where approval is required — https://regbar.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/ACI-315.pdf (accessed 2026-10-22).
- SWAPP (Tel Aviv and Houston) raised an $11.5M Series A in May 2023 (total $18.5M) for AI that produces construction documents inside Revit and Archicad; claims automation of up to 80% of documentation tasks (vendor claim) — https://swapp.ai/news/swapp-raises-11-5-million-series-a-to-help-architects-with-ai-platform/ and https://archinect.com/news/article/150350727/swapp-raises-11-5-million-for-tool-that-offers-ai-powered-construction-documents-in-minutes (accessed 2026-10-22).
- Qnect automates steel connection design and detailing inside Tekla Structures (QuickQnect); Walters Group led a $2M round in 2015 and Nucor a $3.8M round in 2018 — https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/qnect-continues-to-shape-the-steel-industry-with-investment-from-nucor-300628580.html and https://www.qnect.com/quickqnect (accessed 2026-10-22).
- ClearHandoff sells AI review of steel shop-drawing PDFs exported from Tekla or SDS2 against the client's checklist; Pro plan USD 250 a month for ~500 sheets (~USD 0.50 a sheet) — https://www.clearhandoff.com/en/for/structural-steel/ (accessed 2026-10-22).
- Canam, a steel fabricator, runs its own outsourced detailing and drafting teams in Romania, India and the Philippines — https://www.canam.com/en/engineering-detailing-outsourcing/ (accessed 2026-10-22).
- Pinnacle Infotech (Durgapur, India) self-reports 3,400+ professionals on one page and 5,200+ employees across 15 delivery centres on another (self-reported, inconsistent) — https://pinnacleinfotech.com/about/ (accessed 2026-10-22).
- Vendor-published price ranges: offshore drafting ~$8–35/hour in India and the Philippines against ~$75–175/hour at established US firms; ~$150–400 per sheet for fixed per-sheet work; one or two revision rounds typically included — https://caddrafter.us/how-much-do-cad-drafting-services-cost/ and https://simutecra.com/blogs/cad-drafting-cost and https://drawings.archicgi.com/ (accessed 2026-10-22). Steel detailing ~$90–165 per ton on typical commercial work, ~$25–40 on large repetitive industrial jobs — https://strand-co.com/structural-bim-services/steel-detailing/cost/ (accessed 2026-10-22). These are vendor marketing figures, used only as ranges.
- Offshore redline workflow: markups assigned at the end of the US business day for overnight completion — https://intelassist.com/blog-post/what-a-dedicated-offshore-cad-drafting-team-delivers/ (accessed 2026-10-22).
- MEP fabrication detailing deliverables (LOD 400 models, duct and pipe spool drawings, hanger layouts, SMACNA as the sheet-metal reference) offered to US subcontractors — https://excelize.com/subcontractors/ and https://www.uniquescadd.com/our-services/mep-shop-drawing-services/ (accessed 2026-10-22).
- ADDA credential tiers (Apprentice Drafter, Certified Drafter, Certified Design Drafter and others), pass mark 75, three-year renewal — https://www.adda.org/index.php/professional/professional-certification (accessed 2026-10-22).
- USPTO drawing rules: 37 CFR 1.84 form requirements; MPEP 608.02 says the Office objects to drawings that cannot be reproduced or whose content is unacceptable; corrected sheets are due in the response period — https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s608.html and https://www.uspto.gov/sites/default/files/documents/tc2900_drawing_objections_fy23.pdf (accessed 2026-10-22).

## Estimates (not checked)
- US-serving outsourced drafting and detailing spend ~$5B, onshore and offshore — derived: $1.68B US 541340 receipts plus an assumed ~$3B+ of offshore delivery to US clients; the offshore figure is not measured anywhere public.
- Niche market sizes (steel detailing ~$900M; CD production ~$1.4B split ~$550M overflow / ~$850M dedicated teams; residential permit drafting ~$500M; legacy conversion ~$300M; trade shop drawings ~$600M; patent drawings ~$200M; MEP fabrication modelling ~$700M; rebar detailing ~$400M) and their shares — allocation of the ~$5B estimate by judgement.
- Steel detailing ~$900M — derived from an assumed ~6M tons a year of detailed structural steel (recalled, unverified) at the vendor-published $90–165/ton range.
- Patent drawings ~$200M — derived from a recalled ~600,000 US utility applications a year at a few hundred dollars of figures each; many firms draw in-house.
- Share of first submittals returned "revise and resubmit" (~20–40%), checker time per sheet (~10–30 minutes), revision rounds per sheet, back-charge sizes per field fix (~$500–5,000), offshore turnaround norms (overnight to 48 hours), and offshore drafter pay (~$300–800 a month) — recalled and analogy; no public benchmark found.
- Insight-function headcounts for every Pass 2 pocket — judgement from firm size and structure, not counted.
- Offshore drafting labour pools concentrated in India, the Philippines, Vietnam and Eastern Europe — recalled from vendor sites; not quantified.
- Patent figures must show every feature recited in the claims and use reference numerals consistent with the specification (37 CFR 1.83(a), 1.84(p)(5)) — recalled rule text; only 1.84 and MPEP 608.02 were web-checked.
- Many states let single-family and small residential work be drawn without an architect's seal — recalled; thresholds vary by state and were not checked.
- Residential plan-check correction cycles of ~two to six weeks in busy jurisdictions — recalled analogy, not measured.
- One senior checker typically serving ten or more detailers, and ~an hour a shift lost to deciphering markups offshore — judgement from vendor workflow descriptions, not measured.
- Fire sprinkler layout carries NICET or engineer design responsibility (used to reject the candidate niche) — recalled.
- Millwork submittals referencing AWI Architectural Woodwork Standards quality grades; Trimble SysQue, Autodesk Fabrication (CADmep, ESTmep, CAMduct), aSa Rebar, RebarCAD, Cabinet Vision, Microvellum, Chief Architect, SoftPlan, Scan2CAD, WinTopo, ClaimMaster and PatentOptimizer as named tools — recalled product knowledge, not individually web-checked.
- Joist, deck and metal-building manufacturers running in-house detailing departments of 50-500 — recalled industry structure; headcounts are judgement.
