# Sources — Architecture Firms

## Checked
- US architecture firm gross billings of $104.1B in 2023; firm profitability averaged 13.2% of net billings; average net billings per employee $143,000; one-third of firms of all sizes use AI day to day (61% of large, 42% of midsize, 27% of small firms) (AIA Firm Survey Report 2024 infographic) — https://aia.org/sites/default/files/2024-11/AIA_2024_firm_survey_report_Infographic.pdf (accessed 2026-10-22)
- More than 19,000 architecture firms in the US; ~75% have fewer than 10 employees (28% sole practitioners, 32% with 2–4 employees, 15% with 5–9); institutional work ~53–55% of 2023 billings; residential ~20% of design revenue; single-family homes 8% of billings; new construction 52% and renovation, rehabilitation, retrofit, additions and historic preservation 45% of gross billings; small firms' share of billings fell by half and midsize firms' by 40% between 2015 and 2023 while large firms' rose 40% (AIA, "Latest insights: 2024 Firm Survey Report") — https://www.aia.org/aia-architect/article/latest-insights-2024-firm-survey-report (accessed 2026-10-22)
- 116,005 licensed architects across the 55 US jurisdictions in 2024 (down 4%); more than 39,000 candidates working toward licensure; close to 3,600 newly licensed; average time to licensure 12.9 years; ARE pass rate fell 3 points to 55% (NCARB by the Numbers 2025, as reported) — https://www.archcareersguide.com/ncarb-by-the-numbers-2025/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- US architect count rose 6% in 2025; average time to licensure 12.3 years in 2025 (NCARB by the Numbers 2026, as reported; no absolute total given) — https://www.archcareersguide.com/ncarb-by-the-numbers-2026/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- The Architecture Billings Index is produced monthly by the AIA Economics & Market Research Group from its Work-on-the-Boards survey and now carries Deltek's name; it leads nonresidential construction by ~9–12 months; national readings stayed below 50 through 2025 (e.g. 45.6 in January, 46.8 in June and September) — https://www.calculatedriskblog.com/2025/07/aia-architecture-firm-billings-remain.html and https://www.aia.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/RP25_09-2026-SEP_2025_ABI_infographic.pdf (accessed 2026-10-22)
- CALGreen embodied carbon requirements apply from 1 July 2024 to nonresidential new construction of 100,000 sq ft or more (50,000 sq ft for DSA schools), via one of three paths: a cradle-to-grave whole-building LCA showing a 10% GWP reduction against a reference building, reuse of 45% of an existing structure and enclosure, or product GWP limits evidenced by EPDs; the nonresidential threshold falls to 50,000 sq ft from 1 January 2026 — https://stopwaste.org/sites/default/files/CALGreen%20EC%20Update-May2024.pdf and https://calstate.edu/csu-system/doing-business-with-the-csu/capital-planning-design-construction/Documents/CPDC%20BU%2024-012%20-%20New%20CalGreen%20Embodied%20Carbon%20Reqs.pdf (accessed 2026-10-22)
- RIBA Workplace and Wellbeing Report (UK, 1,450+ respondents, fieldwork June–July 2024, published April 2025): ~90% of practice employees regularly work overtime, two-thirds of them unpaid; contracted 37.5 hours, average 44; 54% say work harms their mental health — https://www.dezeen.com/2025/04/30/riba-workplace-and-wellbeing-report-2025/ and https://workplacejournal.co.uk/2025/04/90-of-architecture-employees-work-overtime-two-thirds-not-compensated-riba (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Monograph's 2021 survey: 67.6% of surveyed architects felt overworked and overloaded — https://monograph.com/blog/state-of-burnout-in-architecture-2021 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- AI drawing-set review tools exist and work mainly on 2D PDF sets: Helonic (coordination, code, missing information, constructability), Structured AI (documents, drawing sets and Revit models with firm-specific rules), InspectMind (YC W24 plan checker); independent accuracy benchmarks were not found — https://app.dealroom.co/companies/helonic and https://www.getapp.co.uk/software/2273285/structured-ai and https://hn.svelte.dev/item/46219386 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Jensen Hughes describes code consulting, fire protection engineering and risk analysis as its core, with a global team cited at 1,500+ (2025 posting) to 1,700 (2024 recruiting flyer) — https://www.brightnetwork.co.uk/graduate-employer-company/jensen-hughes/ and https://fpe.umd.edu/sites/fpe.umd.edu/files/job-files/2024-Jenson%20Hughes%20-%20September.pdf (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Florida CS/CS/CS/HB 267 (2024), Chapter 2024-191, effective 1 January 2025: revises the timeframes in which building officials must act on private-provider permit applications, deems applications approved in certain circumstances, and bars audits of private providers until a published audit procedures manual exists — https://flsenate.gov/Session/Bill/2024/267 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- One Click LCA: team described as 180+ (ISCouncil) to 201–500 (2026 directory); LCA datasets described as 200,000+ to 500,000+ — https://iscouncil.org/?p=13564 and https://www.rankmyai.com/tools/308e792a-a392-431d-9068-0dda63c17d74/one-click-lca (accessed 2026-10-22)
- GBCI standard LEED review is completed within ~20–25 days of submission, expedited ~10–12 days for a fee; GBCI's terms treat timelines as estimates; ~190,000+ LEED-certified projects worldwide cited (article date unclear) — https://gbdmagazine.com/leed-certification-dates/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Deltek acquired Avitru (formerly ARCOM, merged with InterSpec) in January 2019; AIA MasterSpec is powered by Deltek Specpoint, which competes with SpecLink and others — https://www.deltek.com/en/about/media-center/press-releases/2019/deltek-acquires-avitru and https://www.AIA.org/masterspec (accessed 2026-10-22)
- A Canadian design-liability insurer (Encon) reported 55% of claims based on design errors, 8% on inspection errors and 5% on supervision errors (2013, dated, non-US; used only as an order of magnitude) — https://canadianconsultingengineer.com/who-is-most-likely-to-sue-you (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Monograph editorial citing forum polls and surveys describes 60–70-hour weeks during construction-document crunches (less rigorous than the RIBA survey; used as workers' own description) — https://monograph.com/blog/how-many-hours-do-architects-work-each-week (accessed 2026-10-22)
- AIA defines large firms as those with 50+ employees in the 2024 Firm Survey infographic — https://aia.org/sites/default/files/2024-11/AIA_2024_firm_survey_report_Infographic.pdf (accessed 2026-10-22)
