# Sources — Money Service Businesses

## Checked
- 26,276 MSBs registered with FinCEN as of 3 March 2023; FinCEN Form 107 registration renewed every two years; MSBs keep a list of their agents; an average 3,603 initial registrations and 8,429 renewals a year over 2018–2022 — FinCEN, Agency Information Collection Activities notice, Federal Register 2023-07540 (published 11 April 2023), https://public-inspection.federalregister.gov/2023-07540.pdf (accessed 2026-10-22)
- US household transfer payments to the rest of the world: $86.2B in 2024, $81.0B in 2023 (BEA series W396RC1A027NBEA) — https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/W396RC1A027NBEA (accessed 2026-10-22)
- 1% excise tax on cash-funded outbound remittance transfers under new IRC §4475 (One Big Beautiful Bill Act, P.L. 119-21), for transfers on or after 1 January 2026; applies to transfers funded with cash, money orders, cashier's checks or similar physical instruments, with bank-account and US card funding outside it; providers collect, deposit semi-monthly and report quarterly on Form 720; Notice 2025-55 penalty relief for the first three quarters of 2026; proposed regulations 10 April 2026 — https://www.bdo.com/insights/tax/remittance-tax-guidance-implications-for-money-services-and-global-mobility and https://www.cbh.com/insights/articles/excise-tax-on-remittance-transfers-irc-section-4475-explained/ and https://www.nysscpa.org/article-content/irs-offers-temporary-relief-for-remittance-transfer-providers-101325 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Remitly full-year 2025: send volume $74.9B (up 37% from $54.6B), revenue $1.6B (up 29%), first full year of GAAP profit, 9.3 million active customers in Q4 — https://remitly.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/remitly-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025-results-above (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Western Union FY2025: revenue ~$4.05B (from $4.21B in 2024); about 360,000 locations conducted money transfer activity in the prior 12 months, roughly 90% outside the US; services in 200+ countries and territories; ~9,600 employees; agreement of 10 August 2025 to acquire International Money Express (Intermex) for ~$500M in cash, expected to close in Q2 2026 — https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1365135/000119312526061340/wu-20251231.htm and https://www.finextra.com/pressarticle/106679/western-union-to-acquire-intermex (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Western Union 2017 resolution with DOJ, FTC and US Attorneys: $586M forfeiture; admitted wilful failure to maintain an effective AML programme and aiding and abetting wire fraud; 26 Western Union agents in the US and Canada charged and convicted; suspension guidelines would have led to corrective action against more than 2,000 agents worldwide between 2004 and 2013 — https://www.justice.gov/archives/opa/pr/western-union-admits-anti-money-laundering-and-consumer-fraud-violations-forfeits-586-million and https://www.courthousenews.com/?p=214783 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Money Transmission Modernization Act enacted in full or in part by 35 states as of August 2026 (CSBS), with licensees in at least one adopting state accounting for about 99% of reported money transmission activity — https://businesslawtoday.org/?p=36626 ; CSBS enactment tracker (2025 enactments Colorado, Massachusetts, Mississippi, Nebraska, Virginia; 2026 introductions Alaska, Delaware, Louisiana, Michigan, Oklahoma) — https://www.csbs.org/mtma-legislative-update-august-2026 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- NMLS MSB Call Report due within 45 days of each calendar quarter end; four sections (financial condition, transaction activity by state, permissible investments, destination country — the last annual); late filing can block renewal; Georgia fines $1,000 per late report — https://mortgage.nationwidelicensingsystem.org/knowledge/Products/nmls/pubs/msbCR/licensing/msbCR/topics/msbCR_frequency.html and https://dbf.georgia.gov/node/14641 and https://fid.nv.gov/uploadedFiles/fidnvgov/content/Licensing/Money_Transmitter/Nevada%20MT%20Licensees%20-%20%20Financial%20Report%20Submission%20Memo%202026.pdf (accessed 2026-10-22)
- FinCEN Southwest Border Geographic Targeting Order of 11 March 2025: MSBs in 30 ZIP codes in California and Texas to file CTRs at a $200 threshold; later order raised the floor to $1,000; March 2026 order extended coverage to New Mexico; the Ninth Circuit affirmed a preliminary injunction against the original order (July 2026) and an Arizona MSB sued over the March 2026 order — https://fincen.gov/news/news-releases/fincen-issues-southwest-border-geographic-targeting-order and https://infobytes.orrick.com/2026-07-17/ninth-circuit-affirms-preliminary-injunction-of-fincens-reporting-order-for-businesses-along-the-southern-border/ and https://infobytes.orrick.com/2026-04-16/money-services-business-challenges-fincens-southwest-border-reporting-order/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- World Bank Remittance Prices Worldwide: global average cost of sending $200 rose to 6.49% in Q1 2025 from 6.26% in Q4 2024 (later homepage figure 6.36%); 367 country corridors from 48 sending to 105 receiving countries; SDG 10.c target below 3% — https://remittanceprices.worldbank.org/sites/default/files/rpw_main_report_and_annex_q125_1_0.pdf and https://remittanceprices.worldbank.org (accessed 2026-10-22)
- FXC Intelligence pricing data covers 25,000+ corridors and ~700 providers in 110+ countries; supplies data used for the FSB's G20 cross-border payments work and the World Bank's remittance price database; named clients include Western Union, MoneyGram, Remitly, Visa, Mastercard; data partnerships with Remitly and Intermex — https://fxcintel.com/global-indices-cross-border-payments-data and https://investors.intermexonline.com/news-releases/news-release-details/fxc-intelligence-intermex-unveil-strategic-data-partnership (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Treasury's 2023 De-risking Strategy (AMLA §6215) names small and medium MSBs among the customer groups most often de-risked and finds profitability the primary driver of bank de-risking decisions — https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/jy1438 and https://infobytes.orrick.com/2023-05-02/treasury-announces-strategy-address-financial-institution-de-risking/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Pakistan received a record ~$38.3B in workers' remittances in FY2025 (up ~27%), of which ~$3.72B came from the US (State Bank of Pakistan data as reported) — https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/?p=204436 ; SBP discontinued the Telegraphic Transfer Charges Incentive Scheme from 1 July 2026 while the Pakistan Remittance Initiative continues — https://www.dawn.com/news/2012606 and https://propakistani.pk/2026/07/03/sbp-ends-remittance-incentive-schemes-for-banks-after-imf-pressure/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- California AB 1525 (2011) requires money transmission licensees to train their agents annually on recognising and responding to elder financial abuse, with new agents trained within one month of appointment — https://legiscan.com/CA/text/AB1525/2011 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Alessa markets MSB-specific transaction monitoring, watchlist screening and auto-populated CTR/SAR reporting; Hummingbird is a case-management and SAR-filing layer without full monitoring; Unit21 combines monitoring and case management — https://alessa.com/software-solutions/aml-compliance/transaction-monitoring/msbs/ and https://www.flagright.com/post/best-aml-case-management-software (accessed 2026-10-22)
- CFPB international money transfer larger-participant test: more than $1M in annual receipts from international money transfers; CFPB estimated ~28 nonbanks meet it (Q4 2023–Q3 2024 state data) in an August 2025 advance notice — https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-08-08/pdf/2025-15090.pdf and https://www.venable.com/insights/publications/2025/08/cfpb-reviewing-international-money-transfer (accessed 2026-10-22)
- MSB SARs required on transactions of at least $2,000 ($5,000 for money order and traveler's check issuers reviewing clearance records), filed no later than 30 calendar days after initial detection — 31 CFR 1022.320, https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/31/1022.320 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- The World Bank's KNOMAD programme, source of the bilateral remittance flow estimates (latest official corridor data 2021), has closed for lack of funding — https://www.cgdev.org/node/3136422 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Intermex acquired La Nacional and agreed to acquire LAN Holdings / I-Transfer (smaller transmitters) — https://www.privsource.com/acquisitions/deal/international-money-express-intermex-acquires-la-nacional-agrees-to-acquire-lan-holdings-i-transfer-B6SoBG (accessed 2026-10-22)
- NAICS 522390 covers check cashing, money orders, money transmission and loan servicing, excluding loan brokerage (522310) and financial transactions processing (522320) — https://www.ibisworld.com/classifications/naics/522390/other-activities-related-to-credit-intermediation/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
