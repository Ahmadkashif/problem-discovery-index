- Great Lakes Dredge & Dock, FY2025 Form 10-K: domestic dredging bid market averaged ~$586M a year in capital work, ~$605M in coastal protection and ~$1,086M in maintenance over the three years ended 31 December 2025 (~$2.28B a year combined); GLDD's average combined U.S. bid market share was 29% (36% capital, 46% coastal protection, 15% maintenance); "Great Lakes and two other companies comprised approximately 56%" of the domestic bid market; a successful bid must be the lowest responsive bid that does not exceed 125% of the Corps' original estimate; the Foreign Dredge Act of 1906 and Section 27 of the Merchant Marine Act of 1920 (Jones Act) are cited as barriers to foreign competition; dredging backlog fell 36% to ~$0.8B; ~$200.2M of domestic low bids were pending award at year end; the 2026 Corps budget was signed at a record $10.4B; ~380 salaried and ~716 hourly U.S. employees — https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1372020/000119312526064007/gldd-20251231.htm (accessed 2026-10-24, first 100,000 characters read). 2025 revenue $888.3M and net income $73.5M — same filing family, also https://www.barchart.com/story/news/372195/great-lakes-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025-results-and-the-signing-of-two-international-offshore-energy-contracts (accessed 2026-10-24, via search summary).

- USACE dredging data: the Navigation and Civil Works Decision Support Center publishes spreadsheet reports including a dredging advertising schedule, a five-year contracted dredging schedule, current-fiscal-year Dredging Bid Abstracts and Past Dredging Contracts Awarded (field content not verified: the vault assumes bidder totals against the government estimate, not every line item), drawn from the Dredging Information System (DIS), a lifecycle database of in-house and contracted dredging; Independent Government Estimates stay sealed as Controlled Unclassified Information until bid opening, and floating-plant estimates must use the Corps of Engineers Dredging Estimating Program (CEDEP), which "contains proprietary data and is NOT to be released to non-government entities" — https://www.iwr.usace.army.mil/About/Technical-Centers/NDC-Navigation-and-Civil-Works-Decision-Support/NDC-Dredges/ and ER 1110-2-1302 Civil Works Cost Engineering (20 April 2026), https://publibrary.sec.usace.army.mil/api/download?id=60b44ce6-b9c5-451d-8db7-7f1fecb1b3e1&filename=ER+1110-2-1302+Civil+Works+Cost+Engineering_2026+04+20.pdf (accessed 2026-10-24, via search summary; the NDC page returned 403 to fetch). An ASCE paper reports 7,600+ USACE dredging-related contracts worth $12.41B in 2013–2023 and that solicitations go unawarded when bids come in too high against the IGE — https://ascelibrary.com/doi/10.1061/JWPED5.WWENG-2104 (via search summary). The statutory test is not "above the IGE" but more than 25% above the Corps' fair and reasonable estimate (33 U.S.C. 624(a)(2), entry below), so bids between 100% and 125% of the IGE are awardable. GAO-14-290 asked the Corps to populate DIS fields for solicitations with no bids or all bids over 125% — https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-14-290 (via search summary).
- Dredging measurement and payment, UFGS-35 20 23 Dredging (August 2020): pay quantity in cubic yards "by means of volume difference from soundings taken before and after dredging" (or fill-site topographic surveys, or in place); material removed to a stated depth below the minimum required depth is paid at full price as allowable overdepth, with a further unpaid overdepth limit; side slopes measured to a stated ratio; estimated quantities subject to FAR 52.211-18 Variation in Estimated Quantity; monthly estimates from progress soundings or, at the Contracting Officer's option, 85% of scow or barge measurement; the contractor may attend Government soundings and inspect the data used for the final quantity — https://nibs-s3-wbdg3-production.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/FFC/DOD/UFGS/UFGS%2035%2020%2023.pdf (accessed 2026-10-24, text extracted locally).
- Dredging Quality Management (DQM): pipeline dredge monitoring was phased in from 2014, on Corps-owned pipeline dredges in 2015 and on private dredge contracts in 2018, initially for pipeline dredges with discharge over 18 inches; systems send operational data daily to the DQM office at Mobile District; contracts require current DQM certification, a Dredge Plant Instrumentation Plan, sensor repair within two business days, and DQM costs are included in the dredging unit price (UFGS 35 20 23.13 and 35 20 23.33) — https://www.army.mil/article/256563/dredging_quality_management_provides_247_support_to_mobile_district_nation, https://waterwaysjournal.net/?p=14226 and https://legacy.wbdg.org/FFC/DOD/UFGS/UFGS%2035%2020%2023.33.pdf (accessed 2026-10-24, via search summary).
- Project schedules on USACE work, UFGS-01 32 01.00 10 Project Schedule (August 2026): Primavera P6 with .xer export, or another package that creates the Standard Data Exchange Format (SDEF) file defined in ER 1-1-11; non-procurement activity durations not over 20 workdays or 30 calendar days; schedule updated monthly at a minimum; Initial Project Schedule due within 42 calendar days of notice to proceed; justification of delay due "within 10 days of a delay occuring"; prospective time impact analyses per AACE 52R-06 using the last approved schedule before the impact; retrospective analyses per AACE 29R-03 governed by ASCE 67-17; a time extension only when the delay consumes all project float and pushes the "End Project" milestone past the contract completion date; float belongs to the project — https://nibs-s3-wbdg3-production.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/FFC/DOD/UFGS/UFGS%2001%2032%2001.00%2010.pdf (accessed 2026-10-24, text extracted locally).

- Miller Act bonds: performance and payment bonds are required on federal construction contracts exceeding $150,000; alternative payment protections from $35,000 to $150,000 — 48 CFR 28.102-1, https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/48/28.102-1 (accessed 2026-10-24).
