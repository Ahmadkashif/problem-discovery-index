# Sources — Legal Support Services

## Checked
- US "All other legal services" (NAICS 541199) had 4,860 firms, 5,083 establishments, ~$5.99B in receipts ($5,986,226K), ~$1.97B annual payroll and 32,736 employees in 2022 (all taxable; no tax-exempt rows) — 2022 Economic Census, Sector 54 basic table EC2254BASIC (US rows for NAICS 541199), https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/economic-census/data/2022/sector54/EC2254BASIC.zip (accessed 2026-10-22). For scale, the same table gives Offices of lawyers (541110) ~$356B and Title abstract and settlement offices (541191) ~$12.5B.
- 5,776 employer establishments, 33,969 employees and ~$2.52B annual payroll in 2023; 4,733 of the 5,776 establishments (82%) had fewer than five employees, 580 had 5–9, 250 had 10–19, 129 had 20–49, 46 had 50–99, 23 had 100–249, 10 had 250–499 and 3 had 1,000 or more — County Business Patterns 2023, US file, https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/cbp/datasets/2023/cbp23us.zip (accessed 2026-10-22)
- NAICS 541199 comprises "establishments of legal practitioners (except offices of lawyers and attorneys, settlement offices, and title abstract offices)… primarily engaged in providing specialized legal or paralegal services"; illustrative examples are notary public services, patent agent services (patent filing and searching), paralegal services and process serving; index entries add jury consulting and trial consulting services — https://www.naics.com/naics-code-description/?code=541199 (accessed 2026-10-22; census.gov returned 403)
- 54,145 active patent practitioners registered with the USPTO: 14,873 agents, 38,433 attorneys, 6 design and 833 limited recognition — USPTO Office of Enrollment and Discipline practitioner search, https://oedci.uspto.gov/OEDCI/practitionerSearchEntry (accessed 2026-10-22). A March 2015 USPTO dataset gave 10,830 agents and 32,391 attorneys: https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/list-roster-of-active-patent-attorneys-and-agents-with-licenses-to-practice-before-the-usp-9fcc2
- NYC process servers must carry and use a device that records GPS location while serving; licensees must keep records "in an electronic format that cannot be changed"; agencies must submit a monthly written report on each server's recordkeeping; scheduled traverse hearings must be reported to DCWP within 10 days of notice — NYC Business, Process Serving Agency License, https://bn.nyc-business.nyc.gov/nycbusiness/description/process-serving-agency-license/after_apply (accessed 2026-10-22)
- About 17% of the 366,000 consumer credit lawsuits filed in NYC civil courts in 2019–2023 got a response; roughly 152,000 default judgments were entered against consumers in 2019–2024; judges held 656 traverse hearings across 434,000 consumer credit cases filed in 2019–2024; DCWP suspended or declined to renew 242 process server licences between 2019 and fall 2024 and can fine up to $1,000 per violation; the 2010 reforms added a licensing exam, electronic logbooks and GPS; a 2015 class action settled for $59M and vacated nearly 200,000 default judgments — New York Focus, June 10, 2025, https://nysfocus.com/2025/06/10/new-york-sewer-service-debt-collection-fraud (accessed 2026-10-22)
- New York's Attorney General moved in 2010 to vacate more than 100,000 default judgments over sewer service — ABA Journal, https://www.abajournal.com/news/article/new_york_ag_sues_35_law_firms_seek_to_void_100000_default_judgments (search-result excerpt, accessed 2026-10-22)
- California requires anyone making more than ten services of process a year for pay to register with the county clerk (Bus. & Prof. Code §22350 et seq.), with a $2,000 bond or cash deposit for a two-year term and fingerprints under §22351.5 — San Luis Obispo County, https://slocounty.ca.gov/departments/clerk-recorder/all-services/professional-registrations/process-server-registration ; Siskiyou County application, https://www.siskiyoucounty.gov/sites/default/files/fileattachments/clerk/page/15134/clk_20221214_individualprocessserverapplicationaccessible.pdf (search-result excerpts, accessed 2026-10-22)
- California legal document assistants and unlawful detainer assistants register with the county clerk under Bus. & Prof. Code §§6400–6415, post a $25,000 bond (individuals) and need 15 hours of continuing education per two-year renewal — Sacramento County, https://ccr.saccounty.gov/Pages/UDA.aspx ; San Luis Obispo County, https://www.slocounty.ca.gov/departments/clerk-recorder/all-services/professional-registrations/unlawful-detainer-assistant-registration (search-result excerpts, accessed 2026-10-22)
- Arizona requires anyone preparing legal documents without attorney supervision to be certified as a legal document preparer under Rule 31 and ACJA §§7-201 and 7-208, with a written exam that cannot be waived — Arizona Judicial Branch, https://azcourts.gov/cld/Legal-Document-Preparer-Program (search-result excerpt, accessed 2026-10-22)
- Bankruptcy petition preparers are governed by 11 U.S.C. §110; the Central District of California caps BPP fees at $150 including all expenses, the Northern District of Ohio uses a presumptive $125 cap, and courts order disgorgement of excess fees — https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/11/110 ; https://www.canb.uscourts.gov/node/1366 ; https://www.ohnb.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/opinions//op-20140418-re-united-states-trustee-et-al-v-proby-et-al-doc1-maw-13-03051.pdf (search-result excerpts, accessed 2026-10-22)
- FRCP 4(m): a defendant not served within 90 days after the complaint is filed may be dismissed without prejudice; the rule does not apply to service in a foreign country under Rule 4(f), 4(h)(2) or 4(j)(1). Rule 4(d) gives at least 30 days (60 abroad) to return a waiver — https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/frcp/rule_4 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- The Hague Service Convention (1965) has 84 contracting parties; the most recent accessions are the Dominican Republic and El Salvador (21 March 2024) — HCCH status table, https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/status-table/?cid=17 (accessed 2026-10-22). Requests typically take two to four months versus six months to a year by letters rogatory — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hague_Service_Convention (secondary)
- 35 U.S.C. §315(b): an inter partes review may not be instituted if the petition is filed more than one year after the petitioner, real party in interest or privy is served with a complaint alleging infringement; a voluntary dismissal does not reset the bar (Click-to-Call, Fed. Cir. en banc) — https://www.foley.com/insights/publications/2019/10/ptab-precedential-35-usc-315b-service-ipr/ ; https://morganlewis.com/pubs/federal-circuit-estoppel-applies-even-if-infringement-complaint-is-dismissed-without-prejudice (accessed 2026-10-22)
- In FY2025 the PTAB received 1,433 AIA petitions (1,361 IPR, 72 PGR), about two-thirds (967) in electrical/computer technologies — USPTO Trial Statistics FY25 Q4, https://prod-web-alb-tg.uspto.gov/sites/default/files/documents/Trial_StatsFY25_Q4.pdf (search-result excerpt, accessed 2026-10-22)
- PTAB institution rates fell through 2025 from 68% (Q1) to 42% (Q2) and 23% (Q3); Q4 was 41% overall but 14% (27 of 196) for petitions decided under the Director's new process; RPX reports "a dramatic jump in ex parte reexaminations" — RPX, https://www.rpxcorp.com/data-byte/ptab-institution-rates-dropped-in-2025-amid-discretionary-denial-expansion/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- In March 2023 the USPTO awarded two five-year contracts to produce PCT international search reports and written opinions: CPA Global (Clarivate) with a ~$267.4M ceiling and Cardinal Intellectual Property with a ~$271.3M ceiling, ~$538.8M combined — Washington Technology, https://washingtontechnology.com/contracts/2023/03/patent-and-trademark-office-awards-search-support-contracts/384670 ; GovCon Wire, https://govconwire.com/2023/03/uspto-awards-539m-in-patent-cooperation-treaty-support-contracts (search-result excerpts; both pages refused direct fetch, accessed 2026-10-22). The USPTO ran a commercial PCT search pilot with Landon IP and IP Data Miner from 2005 — https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/com/sol/og/2005/week43/patsrch.htm
- Derwent World Patents Index: ~900 editorial staff across eight locations and ~2.5M new records a year (2016 figures); editors with technical backgrounds rewrite abstracts and assign manual codes, revised annually (2025 revision completed) — WIPO CWS workshop presentation, https://wipo.int/edocs/mdocs/cws/en/cws_wk_ge_16/cws_wk_ge_16_dwpi.pdf ; Clarivate, https://clarivate.com/intellectual-property/blog/derwent-world-patents-index-2025-updates-that-redefine-precision-in-patent-analysis/ (search-result excerpts, accessed 2026-10-22)
- Sagacious IP (Gurugram) runs separate engineering and ICT searching teams whose patent analysts perform invalidation, freedom-to-operate and patentability searches, invalidation work being for defendants in infringement suits — https://sagaciousresearch.com/careers/job-opening/engineering-searching-team-patent-analyst/ (search-result excerpt, accessed 2026-10-22)
- Unified Patents runs PATROLL prior-art contests paying cash bounties (e.g., $2,000–$28,000) for art against NPE-asserted patents, funded by member subscriptions by technology "zone" — https://www.unifiedpatents.com/insights/2023/12/4/12000-for-intellectual-ventures-networking-patents-prior-art ; https://unifiedpatents.com/insights/tag/PATROLL (search-result excerpts, accessed 2026-10-22)
- Proof (proofserve.com) closed a $7M Series A on March 8, 2022 led by Blue Heron Capital and The LegalTech Fund, with "over 3,500 law firms and governmental agencies" on its platform; it offers bulk upload, real-time tracking and GPS mapping of every attempt — https://www.proofserve.com/learn/proof-technology-inc-closes-7m-series-a-funding (accessed 2026-10-22)
- ABC Legal runs a national network of vetted process servers with photo and GPS notifications after each attempt — https://www.abclegal.com/value-dsp (search-result excerpt, accessed 2026-10-22)
- ServeManager is process-server job management software (job entry, documents, billing, GPS proof, court filings, client updates); it claimed over 1,000 process serving firms in 2018 — https://capterra.com/p/153037/ServeManager/ ; https://www.servemanager.com/blog/2547/servemanager-approved-as-aba-blueprint-technology-partner (search-result excerpts, accessed 2026-10-22)
- CT Corporation (Wolters Kluwer) receives service of process at each registered office and forwards it, with an online log and e-mail notification; CSC scans SOP within 24 hours with electronic notification and tracking — https://www.wolterskluwer.com/en/solutions/ct-corporation/resources/service-descriptions ; https://www.cscglobal.com/cscglobal/pdfs/CSC_Registered_Agent_Services.pdf (search-result excerpts, accessed 2026-10-22)
- Paralegals and legal assistants: 376,200 jobs in 2024, median pay $61,010 (May 2024), little or no change projected 2024–2034 — BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook, https://www.bls.gov/ooh/legal/paralegals-and-legal-assistants.htm (accessed 2026-10-22)
- At least one party was self-represented in 76% of civil (non-domestic) cases in the NCSC Landscape of Civil Litigation in State Courts (925,344 cases disposed in 10 urban counties, 2012–2013) — https://civilrighttocounsel.org/major_developments/natl-center-for-state-courts-3-4-of-civil-cases-have-one-unrepresented-party/ ; https://www.sji.gov/new-report-on-the-landscape-of-civil-litigation-in-state-courts/ (secondary summaries, accessed 2026-10-22)
- Process-serving guides describe stakeout practice: prior description or photo and vehicle, early-morning, evening and weekend attempts, unmarked vehicles, and reading a sudden change in a subject's routine as a sign of evasion — https://paper-trails-a-louisiana-process-servers-podcast.castos.com/episodes/what-is-a-process-server-stakeout-how-we-serve-evasive-defendants ; https://undisputedlegal.com/process-server-stakeouts-understanding-when-and-why-they-are-used (vendor-run sources, accessed 2026-10-22)

## Estimates (not checked)
- Nonemployer receipts in 541199 (sole-proprietor process servers, freelance paralegals, independent patent searchers and agents) of ~$1–2B on top of the ~$6.0B employer figure — analogy to other professional-service codes with ~80% micro-establishments; the 2022 nonemployer file was not checked at six digits.
- Segment split of the ~$7–8B combined market (process serving ~$2.0B; patent search and agent services ~$1.0B; freelance and contract paralegal ~$1.0B; court filing and attorney services ~$0.6B; document preparers for the public ~$0.4B; international service ~$0.15B; remainder notary, trial and jury consulting and other) — derived, not measured.
- Typical routine serve fees of ~$50–150 per address in metro markets, with rush, stakeout and skip-trace surcharges, and ~1–3 attempts per routine serve — recalled from server price lists and trade press.
- Volume filers (debt buyers' law firms, landlords' attorneys) make up roughly half of process-serving volume — estimate from NYC consumer-credit filing counts and eviction filing totals.
- Freelance paralegal hourly rates of ~$35–75 — recalled from freelance paralegal directories and association surveys.
- Patentability search pricing ~$500–2,500 and invalidity search ~$2,000–10,000+ per search, with India-based houses at the lower end — recalled from vendor pricing pages and trade press; not checked this session.
- Headcounts for Pass 2 pockets (except the Derwent editorial figure and the PCT contract ceilings above) — estimates from public company pages and job listings; no headcount databases were consulted.
- Registered agents receive service of process for hundreds of thousands of entities a year — secondary (process-server vendor blog), not a company figure.
- District courts see ~3,000–4,000 new patent cases a year — recalled from Lex Machina and Docket Navigator annual reports, not re-checked.
- Market sizes for the eight niches and two sub-niches (~$150M to ~$2.0B) — derived from the Census receipts figure and the segment split above.
- Pass 2 scores — judgement against `_direction.md` §4 and the comparable vault pockets named in each profile (forensic-litigation-support, trial-consulting-jury-research, chemistry-bioactivity-databases, trademark-clearance-search-providers, patent-office-drawing-review, identity-skip-trace-data-providers).
