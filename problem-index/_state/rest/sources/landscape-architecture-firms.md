# Sources — Landscape Architecture Firms

## Checked
- Employer firms in landscape architectural services (NAICS 54132) earned ~$5.8B in 2022 ($5,778M), up from $4.6B in 2019 (Census Service Annual Survey, establishments subject to federal income tax, via FRED series REVEF54132TAXABL) — https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/REVEF54132TAXABL (accessed 2026-10-22)
- IBISWorld puts the US landscape design industry (NAICS 54132) at $9.6B revenue and 48,649 businesses in 2026, a count that includes non-employer sole proprietors — https://www.ibisworld.com/united-states/industry/landscape-design/1402/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- BLS: 23,500 landscape architect jobs in 2025; median pay $79,870 (May 2025); 50% employed in architectural, engineering and related services, 20% in landscaping services, 17% self-employed, 8% in government; all states require a licence; ~1,700 openings a year — https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/landscape-architects.htm (accessed 2026-10-22)
- LARE national pass rates (CLARB): Grading, Drainage and Stormwater Management 50% in Spring 2026 (54% Dec 2025, 52% Summer 2025), the lowest of the four sections; Construction Documentation & Administration 80%; Inventory, Analysis and Project Management 63%; Planning and Design 64% — https://architecture.unl.edu/clarb-pass-rates/ (accessed 2026-10-22)
- The LARE is developed by a volunteer Exam Committee of licensed landscape architects in four section sub-committees, led by contracted psychometricians — https://www.clarb.org/committee/exam-committee/ (accessed 2026-10-22, via search summary)
- ASLA 2024 Industry Report (300+ firm leaders, Feb 2024): 61% of firms win new projects through repeat clients; one in four firms is going through an ownership transition; 67% offer hybrid or remote work — https://www.asla.org/news-holding/land/2025/03/the-asla-industry-report-2024-is-here%e2%80%94see-what%e2%80%99s-inside (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Land F/X is an AutoCAD add-on (Planting F/X $695/yr, F/X CAD $775/yr) with plant database, plant placement and labelling, error check and schedule generation; irrigation schedules are exported to Excel for pricing — https://www.landfx.com/plantingfeatures.html and https://www.landfx.com/kb/irrigation-issues/1002-how-do-i-create-an-estimate-or-add-pricing-to-materials-for-irrigation.html (accessed 2026-10-22, via search summary)
- California MWELO revised by the Water Commission on 15 July 2015 after EO B-29-15; the landscape documentation package carries a hydrozone table and MAWA/ETWU water budget, ETAF 0.7 (1.0 for special landscape areas), thresholds of 500 sq ft for new construction in many cities, and some cities require a licensed professional's certification — https://www.losaltosca.gov/sites/default/files/fileattachments/community_development/page/41491/water_efficient_landscape_handout.pdf and https://www.zoneomics.com/code/anderson-CA/chapter_50 (accessed 2026-10-22, via search summary)
- ETC Institute has run household surveys in 400+ communities in 49 states since 1998 and benchmarks parks needs-assessment results against a proprietary national database — https://www.chandleraz.gov/sites/default/files/departments/parks-and-recreation/Chandler-Parks-Strategic-Master-Plan-Appendix-A-Statistically-Valid-Survey-Section-2.pdf (accessed 2026-10-22, via search summary)
- Davey Resource Group runs municipal street-tree inventories and canopy assessments (e.g. StreetsLA's first inventory in decades; Bloomington, IN ~18,000 trees for $125,250 including TreeKeeper software) and is an industry co-operator in USDA Forest Service i-Tree — https://bloomington.in.gov/sites/default/files/2019-09/Bloomington%20Inventory%20and%20Canopy%20Narrative%20091719.pdf and https://www.umass.edu/urbantree/itree%20profile.pdf (accessed 2026-10-22, via search summary)

## Estimates (not checked)
- ~5,000–5,500 employer establishments in NAICS 541320 — third-party summary of County Business Patterns 2023 (Vanta Insights, 5,176); the Census API redirected without a key, so not confirmed against Census tables
- Median employer firm of ~4–8 staff; large design studios (SWA, Sasaki, OLIN, Design Workshop, Mithun, MKSK) of 100–400 staff — recalled, consistent with ~23,500 LAs over ~5,000 establishments plus designers and drafters
- Public-sector clients (parks, municipalities, schools, DOTs) as roughly 40–50% of firm fee revenue — recalled from ASLA business surveys; not checked
- Fee levels: landscape fees of ~6–12% of landscape construction cost on public work, often as a sub-consultant to an architect or civil engineer — recalled
- Plant warranty of one year from substantial completion as the common specification default; ANSI Z60.1 (American Standard for Nursery Stock) as the sizing standard cited in planting specifications — recalled
- Niche and pocket market sizes and headcounts — derived from the employer revenue figure and analogy to the engineering-consultants and landscaping builds; all marked as estimates in the notes
- Design professional liability carriers (CNA, AXA XL, Beazley and others) underwriting landscape architects, with drainage/water intrusion and trip-and-fall claims prominent — recalled; claim-share figures not checked
- Proprietary stormwater and soil-cell manufacturers (Contech, ADS StormTech, Oldcastle Infrastructure, DeepRoot Silva Cell) and irrigation manufacturers (Rain Bird, Hunter, Toro) offering free design assistance to specifiers — recalled from vendor sites, not fetched
