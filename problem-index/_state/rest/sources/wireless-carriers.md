# Sources — Wireless Carriers

## Checked
- Wireless carriers (except satellite) had ~$308B in revenue in 2022 (employer firms, Census Service Annual Survey; $308,153M, up from $295,280M in 2021) — https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/REVEF5172ALLEST (accessed 2026-10-22)
- BLS QCEW, NAICS 517112, private ownership, 2024 annual average: 6,560 establishments, 79,674 employees, average annual pay $116,156 — https://data.bls.gov/cew/data/api/2024/a/industry/517112.csv (accessed 2026-10-22)
- CTIA 2026 Annual Survey: 159.3 trillion MB of mobile data in 2025, up 20% (32% in 2024, 35% in 2023); nearly $30B invested in 2025; more than 600 million connections; nearly 16 million fixed wireless (5G home) subscribers, 3.9 million added in 2025; small cells about 40% of cell sites — https://techblog.comsoc.org/?p=1077187 and https://www.lightreading.com/wireless/americans-used-159-3-trillion-mb-of-mobile-data-in-2025-ctia (accessed 2026-10-22)

- Roughly 2,000 WISPs serve about 9 million US customers (WISPA data, cited November 2025); the three national carriers had 14.7 million FWA customers after Q3 2025 — https://www.lightreading.com/broadband/wisps-clap-back-at-ookla-report-on-fwa-speeds (accessed 2026-10-22)
- FCC BDC mobile coverage parameters: 4G LTE 5/1 Mbps, 5G-NR 7/1 and 35/3 Mbps, each at 90% cell-edge probability and at least 50% cell loading — https://help.bdc.fcc.gov/hc/en-us/articles/6047425308187-Formatting-Mobile-Broadband-Availability-Coverage-Maps (accessed 2026-10-22)
- BDC mobile verification framework (47 CFR 1.7006(c)): providers answer with on-the-ground or infrastructure data; a provider that fails to verify files revised maps within 30 days; providers have 60 days to answer a challenge — https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DA-22-528A1.pdf and https://help.bdc.fcc.gov/hc/articles/9535926296603 (accessed 2026-10-22)
- "The FCC's November 2023 SIM-swap and port-out order (WC Docket 21-341) requires carriers to authenticate customers before a SIM change or port-out and to notify them immediately, but a July 2024 bureau order (DA 24-649) deferred compliance with all of its rules until OMB approval and a Federal Register compliance-date notice, and no such notice had appeared by October 2026." — order: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2023/12/08/2023-26338/protecting-consumers-from-sim-swap-and-port-out-fraud ; waiver: https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DA-24-649A1.pdf and https://www.tlp.law/2024/07/08/fcc-amends-sim-swapping-port-out-fraud-compliance-date/ ; the FCC submitted the information collections to OMB on 2024-10-29 (https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2024/10/29/2024-25103/information-collections-being-submitted-for-review-and-approval-to-office-of-management-and-budget); a Federal Register API search of FCC documents for "Port-Out Fraud", "SIM swap" and "SIM change" from 2024-07-01 found no compliance-date notice, only that OMB submission and the Unified Agendas (https://www.federalregister.gov/api/v1/documents.json?conditions[term]=%22Port-Out+Fraud%22&conditions[agencies][]=federal-communications-commission) (accessed 2026-10-22)
- Q3 2025 postpaid phone churn: AT&T 0.92%, T-Mobile 0.89% — https://www.phonearena.com/news/the-numbers-dont-lie-and-at-t-is-last-among-big-three_id177001 and https://www.benton.org/headlines/t-mobile-releases-third-quarter-2025-earnings (accessed 2026-10-22)
