# Embedded-Research Index

**Second cut across the same 1,530 pockets.** The `_scorecard-index.md` rubric was built around one question — *is the insight the invoice?* — and it rejects, by design, every business where research is a **required step in delivering something else**. This index recovers that population.

The distinction matters commercially. In the primary index the research *is* the product, so improving it is a product decision and the seller is protective of it. Here the research is a cost of delivery: the customer is buying a policy, a certificate, a prescription, a settlement, a formula. Efficiency in the research step goes straight to margin, and there is no fear of commoditising the thing being sold.

---

## Method

Same five scores, read differently.

**Eligibility gate — Q1 = 3 exactly.** Q1 measures how directly the analysis is invoiced. A 5 is a research firm and belongs in the primary index. A 2 or below is an internal cost centre with no external customer for the work. A **3** is the target: the research materially gates a deliverable that is sold to someone else. Q1 is therefore a gate, not a scored term.

**Fit score = Q2×3 + Q3×2 + Q4×2 + Q5×2 — maximum 45.**

| Term | Weight | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Q2 Research labour mass + repeatable | ×3 | The discriminating variable — how many people perform the research step |
| Q3 Proprietary data moat | ×2 | What the delivery step accumulates and discards |
| Q4 External clock | ×2 | Delivery deadlines are usually harder here than in the primary index |
| Q5 Buyer + market | ×2 | Unchanged |

**Kill switches** are applied exactly as before, and cross-referenced duplicates are excluded.

**Threshold: 36/45.**

Scores are read from the existing Pass 2 scorecards. **No pocket has been re-scored** — a pocket that scores 47/60 in the primary index still scores 47/60 there. This is a different question asked of the same evidence.

A phrase recurs across the Pass 2 verdicts often enough to be a finding in itself: *"holds the only record of X … and uses it to price a policy / adjudicate a claim / route a work order."* That sentence is this index's thesis, written down repeatedly by a rubric that was penalising it.

## Worked Cases

Twenty-one pockets have full `build.md` / `buy.md` / `fix.md` analysis written against this thesis. Each entry below states the specific loop that the delivery step leaves open — the thing the organisation observes and then discards.

### Underwriting & risk pricing

- **[[niches/hoa-management/community-association-insurance-underwriting/profile|Community Association Insurance Underwriting]]** (38) — Decades of losses joined to building age, construction, roof and plumbing across thousands of associations: the only empirical failure record for American multifamily housing. It produces a renewal quote.
- **[[niches/tax-prep-firms/refund-transfer-banking/profile|Refund Transfer & Advance Banking]]** (38) — Millions of returns joined to whether the refund arrived, when, short, or offset. The only measured model of tax authority behaviour toward ordinary filers, used to size a loan.
- **[[niches/auto-repair-shops/fleet-maintenance-analytics/profile|Fleet Maintenance Management Analytics]]** (37) — Line-item repair history across millions of vehicles, every brand, delivery to disposal. No manufacturer holds a cross-brand equivalent. It authorises one brake job at a time.

### Claims adjudication & review

- **[[niches/personal-injury-law/mass-tort-claims-administration/profile|Mass Tort & Class Action Claims Administration]]** (37) — Hundreds of settlements, each a live experiment in reaching a dispersed population. Projected notice reach and realised claim rates are never compared, within a matter or across them.
- **[[niches/auto-dealers-independent/auction-condition-report-ops/profile|Auction Condition Report Operations]]** (39) — Arbitration is a free accuracy label on every inspection performed, and it is never added up.

### Verification, inspection & certification

- **[[niches/alterations-tailoring/softlines-testing-labs/profile|Softlines Testing Laboratories]]** (39) — Protocol-driven testing at enormous volume, with pass/fail history by supplier and factory that is never turned into a risk weighting for what to test next.
- **[[niches/auto-repair-shops/state-inspection-program-operators/profile|State Inspection Program Operators]]** (39) — A near-census of vehicle condition, reported as a compliance statistic rather than as evidence about whether the programme works.
- **[[niches/childcare-centers/childcare-background-check-vendors/profile|Childcare Background Check & Screening Vendors]]** (37) — Millions of adjudications against fifty different statutory barrier lists, plus the same workers re-screened on a cycle. Nobody has measured whether any of the rules are calibrated.
- **[[niches/hoa-management/resale-certificate-document-services/profile|Resale Certificate & Association Document Services]]** (39) — A continuously refreshed financial panel of community associations, assembled one closing at a time and filed.
- **[[niches/energy-auditors/utility-program-implementers/profile|Utility Program Implementers]]** (39) — A complete adoption record for efficiency measures, used only to substantiate savings claims to a regulator.

### Technical advisory attached to product

- **[[niches/crop-farming/ag-retail-agronomy-networks/profile|Agricultural Retail Agronomy Networks]]** (39) — Prescriptions written field by field, yields harvested field by field, and the loop between them never closed.
- **[[niches/auto-body-shops/paint-color-formulation-research/profile|Automotive Paint Colour Formulation Research]]** (39) — Colour scientists build the variant libraries; which variant the refinisher actually chose, and whether it matched, is unmodelled.
- **[[niches/greenhouse-horticulture/biological-control-advisory-teams/profile|Biological Control Field Advisory Teams]]** (39) — A private pest and disease surveillance network covering thousands of hectares weekly, used to sell the next shipment of beneficials.
- **[[niches/food-manufacturing/ingredient-applications-labs/profile|Ingredient Applications Laboratories]]** (39) — Thousands of formulations developed for customers, never joined to which of those products succeeded in the market.
- **[[niches/hair-salons-independent/manufacturer-education-technical-teams/profile|Professional Brand Education & Technical Teams]]** (37) — The largest technical education workforce in the industry teaches a formulation system whose real-world results it has never observed, except through the correction queue.
- **[[niches/auto-repair-shops/diagnostic-tool-coverage-engineering/profile|Diagnostic Tool Coverage Engineering]]** (37) — The installed base transmits every diagnosis technicians attempt and fail to complete; the coverage roadmap is still set by sales anecdote.
- **[[niches/accounting-firms-smb/tax-software-content-teams/profile|Tax Software Content & Compliance Teams]]** (39) — Prior-year returns are an unused test oracle for this year's calculation engine.

### Programme & network administration

- **[[niches/towing-companies/roadside-assistance-network-analytics/profile|Roadside Assistance Network Analytics]]** (41) — Highest fit in the index, and already qualified on the primary rubric. Dispatch, arrival and outcome across millions of events, used to route the next truck.
- **[[niches/auto-dealers-independent/marketplace-pricing-analytics/profile|Listing Marketplace Pricing Analytics]]** (39) — The deal rating shown to a shopper is an intervention on the market it describes, and its effect is never measured.
- **[[niches/catering-companies/gpo-category-analytics/profile|Foodservice GPO Category Analytics]]** (37) — Weekly SKU-level purchases across billions of dollars of spend, including what operators switched to when a price moved. Reported as a savings percentage.
- **[[niches/commercial-real-estate/brokerage-research-divisions/profile|Brokerage Research Divisions]]** (36) — The firm's own deal pipeline leads the published statistics by months, and its brokers know the concessions that make published rents wrong. Neither reaches the research division.

### What the twenty-one have in common

In every case the organisation is paid for a delivery — a quote, a determination, a certificate, a prescription, a settlement, a report — and the research that gates it is a cost line. The dataset that accumulates is a by-product nobody owns. That is precisely why the analytical opportunity survives: no one is protecting it, because no one is selling it.

The recurring failure is the same shape throughout. **The outcome is observed by the same organisation that made the prediction, and the two are never joined.** Agronomists see the yield. Insurers see the loss. Inspectors see the arbitration. Educators hear the correction call. The loop is physically closeable inside one company's own records, and in twenty-one distinct industries it has not been closed.
