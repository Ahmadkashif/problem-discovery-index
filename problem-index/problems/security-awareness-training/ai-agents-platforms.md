# AI Agents & Platform Opportunities — Security Awareness Training

**Industry:** [[security-awareness-training|Security Awareness Training]]

---

## 1. Outcome-Linked Awareness Platform
#ai-platform #causal-inference #bayesian-inference #confidence-intervals #survival-analysis #hypothesis-testing #compliance #evaluation-metrics

**Concept:** A platform that makes the category's central number mean something. It rates every simulation template on a calibrated difficulty scale built from measurable properties — contextual plausibility, personalisation, urgency framing, sender legitimacy, presence of the cues training teaches — and reports click rate at fixed difficulty, which removes the incentive to improve the number by making the test easier. It links simulated performance to real-world susceptibility using data every customer already holds: gateway records of real phishing per individual, employee reports, and incident involvement. And it makes reporting rate and speed the primary reported outcome, because a clicked-and-reported message is far less damaging than one that is neither clicked nor reported.

**Inputs:** Templates with rated properties; per-employee simulation outcomes; gateway-recorded real attempts by recipient; reporting events with timing and accuracy; confirmed incidents; role, tenure and communication volume; light survey instruments on trust and willingness to report.

**Outputs / Actions:** Difficulty-controlled resistance, which is the first comparable measure this category would have. An outcome linkage estimate pooled across customers, powered enough to distinguish a weak relationship from none — and a null result reported honestly, since a null would be the most important finding the field could produce. Reporting-first metrics. And a measured trust cost, so a customer can distinguish a programme that builds resistance from one that achieves its numbers by making people afraid to speak.

**Why now:** The current metric improves reliably and the honest one might not, which explains the market's behaviour completely — and is why this is a product for a challenger, an insurer or a customer, rather than for the incumbent whose line is currently going down.

**Market:** Large employers running mature programmes, cyber insurers who treat awareness training as a control with no evidence attached, and the regulators and auditors accepting click rate as assurance.

## 2. Reporting Pipeline Agent
#ai-agent #bert #transformers #dbscan #gradient-boosting #k-nearest-neighbors #automation #worker-facing

**Concept:** An agent that turns the programme's most valuable output from a burden into a detection capability. It clusters reports of the same or similar message into a single campaign object with the recipient list attached, collapsing hundreds of triage tasks into a handful of decisions and surfacing the live attack currently buried in volume. It classifies against the organisation's own normal traffic rather than a generic baseline, because what counts as unusual mail depends on that organisation's vendors, conventions and languages. It auto-resolves reports of the programme's own simulations, which are a meaningful share of the queue for no purpose. And it closes the loop with the reporter.

**Inputs:** Reported messages with full headers, links and attachments; the organisation's normal mail traffic for baselining; simulation send records; mail system records for recipient enumeration; historical triage dispositions.

**Outputs / Actions:** Campaign objects rather than individual reports. Organisation-calibrated classification. Automatic acknowledgement to the reporter with an outcome — simulation, legitimate, malicious and blocked — which is the cheapest thing to build and the thing that sustains reporting behaviour, since an employee who reports and hears nothing concludes it does not matter. And the recipient list for a confirmed campaign, so everyone else who received it can be remediated before they act, which is the response that actually prevents harm.

**Why now:** Programmes that successfully increase reporting create a queue nobody resourced, which then degrades the behaviour they built — a self-defeating loop that clustering and feedback break directly.

**Market:** Security operations teams receiving these reports, awareness platform vendors whose products generate them, and email security vendors positioned to do the triage at the gateway.

## 3. Programme Design and Targeting Agent
#ai-agent #gradient-boosting #bert #transformers #k-nearest-neighbors #confidence-intervals #worker-facing #compliance

**Concept:** An agent that targets training against real exposure and helps design a programme that works without the adversarial dynamic. It assigns against what an individual is actually exposed to — who receives which phishing attempts, who can approve payments or reach production, who has been impersonated externally — rather than by job title, assesses prior knowledge instead of assuming it, and delivers contextually at moments of relevance: a genuinely suspicious message received, a payment change request, an unusual access grant. On design, it flags templates with emotionally significant lures for explicit approval with the harm acknowledged, reports exposure-adjusted results so high-volume communication roles are not mischaracterised, and models what a punitive policy costs in reporting.

**Inputs:** Gateway phishing records by recipient and type; identity and access data; brand monitoring for individual impersonation; adaptive assessment results; behavioural triggers; template emotional content ratings; programme policy configuration; retention follow-up measurements.

**Outputs / Actions:** Individually targeted assignment against measured exposure. Content evaluated on retained behaviour change at 4 and 12 weeks rather than on completion, which will likely show some widely-used modules do nothing. Contextual delivery compared directly against scheduled delivery. Exposure-adjusted results, which change who appears on a remedial list. An estimate of the reporting cost of punitive policy, which is the evidence that changes that policy where a values argument does not. And automated administration — assignment, chasing, reporting, communications — which is most of an awareness manager's week and none of the value.

**Why now:** Training time is a large organisational cost delivered uniformly to a population with wildly different exposure, and every input needed to target it properly is already collected by systems the organisation runs for other reasons.

**Market:** Awareness platform vendors, in-house programme owners, and the security leaders who currently cannot tell whether their programme is building resistance or building silence.
