# The Loan Consultant Working a Priced Lead

**Industry:** [[lending-marketplaces|Lending Marketplaces]]
**Type:** Worker Life Changing
**One-liner:** Licensed consultants call consumers who submitted a form minutes ago, with no idea which lenders will approve them, and are measured on conversion of leads whose quality was decided before they arrived.
**Tags:** #gradient-boosting #large-language-models #bert #k-nearest-neighbors #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #compliance #automation

## The Problem
In the marketplaces that run a call centre, a licensed consultant works a queue of fresh leads. Speed matters enormously — contact rates collapse within minutes of submission — so the queue is a dialler and the pace is set by it.

The consultant has the submitted profile and a set of lender options. What they do not have is any reliable sense of which lenders will actually approve this person. So the conversation is a discovery process conducted live: asking questions that refine the profile, forming a view, and steering toward a lender based on experience and on which partner is currently paying well.

Most calls end in nothing. The consumer does not answer, or answered three forms and has already been called six times, or is not qualified for anything worth having, or was never seriously shopping. The consultant absorbs that rejection rate all day and is compensated substantially on conversion.

Compliance sits on every call. Licensing is state-specific, disclosures are required, what may and may not be said about rates is constrained, and calls are recorded and reviewed.

And the good lead and the bad lead look identical at the moment of dialling. A consultant cannot tell, before the call, whether this is a serious borrower with a clean profile or a co-registration submission from someone who does not remember filling in a form.

## Why It Matters to the Worker
Compensation depends on lead quality, and lead quality is determined by decisions made in marketing and partnerships. A consultant having a bad month may be working badly or may have been given worse traffic, and neither they nor usually their manager can distinguish the two. That is a demoralising structure regardless of how fair the intent behind it is.

The rejection volume is the daily texture. Dozens of calls, most going nowhere, many to people who are irritated at being called, at a pace set by a dialler.

Consumer distress is common. Many people shopping for a personal loan or a small business loan are doing so because something has gone wrong, and the consultant hears a great deal of that while trying to get to a qualification question.

The expertise is real and unrecognised. A good consultant genuinely knows which lender suits which profile, has learned it from thousands of calls, and holds it entirely tacitly. The firm treats it as sales skill rather than as knowledge and does not capture it.

## What a Solution Looks Like
Approval likelihood surfaced before the dial. Which lenders will plausibly approve this profile, at what terms, with what confidence, is exactly what the marketplace's own data can estimate, and it converts the call from discovery into advice.

Lead scoring visible to the consultant rather than only to pricing. Knowing that this lead has a high or low expected conversion changes how the consultant spends their time, and withholding it does not make them work harder — it makes them work uniformly.

Contact history across the marketplace's own network. A consumer already called six times today should not be called a seventh, and the marketplace frequently has that information.

Compliance support in the moment: state licensing and permissible disclosure surfaced in context rather than memorised, and the objection-handling language that has been reviewed and approved.

The tacit expertise captured. Which lenders each consultant steers which profiles toward, and how those steers turn out, is a recoverable dataset that would make the best consultant's judgement available to everyone and would outlive their employment.

Fair measurement. Conversion normalised for lead quality separates the consultant's contribution from the traffic's, which is the single change that would most improve morale in the role.

## Impact If Solved
This role is compensated on an outcome it only partly controls, using information the company holds and does not share. Surfacing approval likelihood, lead quality and network-wide contact history makes the calls shorter, better and less frequent, and normalising measurement for lead quality makes the compensation structure describe what the person actually did.
