# AI Agents & Platform Opportunities — Trust Companies

**Industry:** [[trust-companies|Trust Companies]]

## 1. Discretionary Distribution Desk
#ai-agent #large-language-models #gradient-boosting #tacit-knowledge-ml #compliance #worker-facing

**Concept:** An agent that takes each beneficiary request from email, portal or call note, pulls the governing standard from the abstracted instrument, assembles the beneficiary's distribution history, other resources and the remainder beneficiaries' positions, retrieves precedent decisions under similar language, flags escalation, exploitation and equalisation risks, and drafts the discretionary memorandum and the committee packet. After the committee decides, it drafts the response to the beneficiary and records the decision and reason in a ledger that is later joined to outcomes.
**Inputs:** Beneficiary requests; instrument abstractions; trust accounting distribution and holdings data; CRM notes; committee calendars and prior minutes; complaint and litigation logs.
**Outputs / Actions:** Draft memo with cited instrument language and precedents; risk flags with evidence; committee packet; drafted beneficiary response; structured decision record and consistency reports for the chief fiduciary officer.
**Why now:** Language models can read instruments and correspondence well enough to draft a memo an officer edits rather than writes, and the retirement of the senior officers who carry the reads has made capturing them urgent.
**Market:** Personal trust units at national banks, independent trust companies and the larger bank trust departments; trust accounting vendors are the natural incumbents but sell ledgers, not decision support.

## 2. Account Acceptance Agent
#ai-agent #large-language-models #transformers #compliance #data-integration #automation

**Concept:** An agent that receives a new trust — the instrument and amendments, the asset list and the parties — abstracts the instrument into a fiduciary schema with page citations, runs KYC and beneficial-ownership checks on the grantor, trustees, protector, advisers and beneficiaries, flags acceptance risks (unusual assets, ambiguous standards, hostile co-fiduciaries), drafts the acceptance memo under the pre-acceptance review rule, and, once approved, populates the trust accounting setup and the post-acceptance review.
**Inputs:** Instruments, amendments and court orders; asset statements from the prior trustee or custodian; party identification documents; the firm's acceptance policy and fee schedule.
**Outputs / Actions:** Abstraction with citations; KYC results; acceptance memo; populated system setup; post-acceptance review checklist; transfer-in instructions to the prior custodian.
**Why now:** Long-document extraction with citations is now reliable enough for human-reviewed use, and directed-trust growth has multiplied the number of parties and roles each acceptance must sort out.
**Market:** Every trust company and trust department; directed-trust companies onboarding trusts referred by advisers are the fastest-growing buyers.

## 3. Fiduciary Tax Season Agent
#ai-agent #gradient-boosting #logistic-regression #automation #workflow-orchestration #worker-facing

**Concept:** An agent that plans and runs the 1041 season: it forecasts which holdings will issue corrected or late tax information, sequences returns so unexposed trusts finish first, prepares 65-day election and extension recommendations for officers, ties out each return to the trust ledger, monitors incoming corrected forms, identifies every affected trust and drafts amended K-1s with plain-language explanations.
**Inputs:** Trust ledgers and holdings; tax documents received with dates; prior seasons' correction histories; officer distribution decisions; filing calendars.
**Outputs / Actions:** Season plan and queue; exposure scores per trust; election and extension recommendations; tie-out exceptions; amended K-1 drafts and beneficiary letters.
**Why now:** Cheap inference makes it practical to re-plan the queue daily as documents arrive, and staffing for seasonal fiduciary tax work is harder to find each year.
**Market:** Trust company tax units and the accounting firms that prepare fiduciary returns for smaller trust departments; competes with the fiduciary modules of the tax software vendors.

## 4. Indenture Default Administration Agent
#ai-agent #large-language-models #bert #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation

**Concept:** An agent for corporate trust default teams that monitors trusteeships for payment defaults, covenant breaches and bankruptcy filings, reads the indenture's event-of-default, notice, direction and indemnity provisions, drafts holder notices on the indenture's timetable, tracks holder directions and percentages, and maintains the record of every trustee action and its authority.
**Inputs:** Trusteeship portfolio and payment schedules; issuer notices and filings; court dockets; indentures and supplemental indentures; holder communications and direction letters; counsel's advice.
**Outputs / Actions:** Default alerts; drafted notices to holders; direction and indemnity tracker; deadline calendar; action log for counsel and examiners.
**Why now:** Language models can read indentures and dockets at the speed defaults now unfold, and the volume of defaulted trusteeships — 5,203 issues with ~$310B outstanding at insured trustees at the end of 2025 — makes manual tracking a liability.
**Market:** Corporate trust departments at the large trustees and successor-trustee specialists; a small number of buyers with large exposure per account.
