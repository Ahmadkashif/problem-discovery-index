# AI Agents & Platform Opportunities — Money Service Businesses

**Industry:** [[money-service-businesses|Money Service Businesses]]

---

## 1. Agent Network Risk Platform
#ai-platform #gradient-boosting #survival-analysis #graph-neural-networks #tacit-knowledge-ml #compliance

**Concept:** A platform that builds one longitudinal file for every agent location — transaction features, field visits, complaints, fraud reports, sweep returns, ownership changes and every investigator decision — and ranks the network by risk of adverse outcome. It captures each investigator's closing reason in seconds, learns which signals senior investigators act on, and shows each flag with the features and comparable cases behind it, so suspensions are consistent and defensible to regulators, sponsor banks and monitors.
**Inputs:** Transaction data by agent, field-visit forms and notes, complaints and law-enforcement requests, ACH sweep returns, agent onboarding and ownership records, case management history.
**Outputs / Actions:** Ranked agent risk queue with reasons; field-visit targeting; draft suspension and termination memos; network views of senders and receivers shared across stores; examiner-ready agent oversight reports.
**Why now:** Language models can turn free-text visit notes and case memos into structured features and draft closing reasons cheaply, and graph tooling has made cross-location network features practical at transmitter scale.
**Market:** The large agent-based transmitters (Western Union, MoneyGram, Ria, Intermex) and mid-sized Latin American and South Asian corridor operators with thousands of agents; today served by in-house builds and generic monitoring vendors.

## 2. Licence and Exam Readiness Agent
#ai-agent #large-language-models #transformers #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation

**Concept:** An agent that maintains a transmitter's position in every state: computes net worth and permissible investments against outstanding obligations daily, tracks bond adequacy against volume, prepares and reconciles the quarterly NMLS MSB Call Report, drafts change-of-control and key-individual notices with each state's deadline, and assembles answers to multistate examination request lists from a maintained evidence library.
**Inputs:** General ledger and outstanding-obligations data, transaction totals by state, bond schedules, the state rule model (MTMA and legacy statutes), corporate and personnel changes, prior exam request lists and findings.
**Outputs / Actions:** Daily licence-position dashboard with shortfall alerts; draft call reports with reconciliations; filing calendar and drafted notices; exam response packs.
**Why now:** MTMA adoption (35 states in full or part by August 2026) has made state requirements regular enough to model, and language models can read state bulletins and request lists into structured obligations.
**Market:** Every licensed transmitter, from fintechs holding money transmitter licences to agent networks; competes with licensing counsel and consultancies on maintenance work and with generic GRC platforms.

## 3. Corridor Pricing Agent
#ai-agent #bayesian-inference #causal-inference #revenue-impact #automation

**Concept:** An agent that watches competitors' landed amounts in every corridor, estimates the volume response to the transmitter's own fee and FX spread, proposes daily price moves within guardrails set by the pricing team, runs small randomised tests where elasticity is uncertain, and reports realised margin and volume against the plan.
**Inputs:** Own transfer data by corridor and channel, competitor pricing feeds, FX rates and hedging costs, promotion calendars, payday and holiday calendars.
**Outputs / Actions:** Proposed corridor-day prices with expected volume and margin; test designs; pricing performance reports; alerts when a competitor's move threatens a corridor.
**Why now:** Pricing intelligence now covers tens of thousands of corridors, app-based competition has made landed amount the visible battleground, and the new 1% tax on cash-funded transfers has shifted the price gap between cash and card funding.
**Market:** Corridor pricing teams at app-first and agent-based transmitters; adjacent to pricing intelligence vendors, who could embed it.

## 4. Bank-Ready Compliance Pack for Small MSBs
#ai-platform #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #compliance #data-integration #workflow-orchestration

**Concept:** A platform that turns a small MSB's compliance programme into the evidence package a bank's MSB due-diligence desk asks for — risk assessment by corridor and agent, monitoring coverage and alert statistics, independent review findings and remediation, licence status, sanctions screening coverage — and keeps it current, so a bank can underwrite and keep the relationship rather than exit it. It also prepares the MSB for its independent review and state exam from the same record.
**Inputs:** Transaction data, monitoring and case statistics, agent lists, policies, licence and registration data, independent review reports, sanctions screening logs.
**Outputs / Actions:** A maintained bank due-diligence file with corridor-level risk metrics; quarterly bank reporting; gap alerts; independent review and exam preparation packs.
**Why now:** Treasury's 2023 De-risking Strategy found that profitability drives bank exits from small MSBs; a standard, cheap evidence package lowers the bank's cost of keeping them, and language models can assemble it from records small firms already hold.
**Market:** Small and mid-sized corridor-specialist transmitters and check cashers (thousands of firms), sold directly or through the white-label send platforms and sponsor banks that serve them.
