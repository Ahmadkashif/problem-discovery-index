# The Researcher Whose Report Is Read Once

**Industry:** [[player-research-firms|Player Research Firms]]
**Type:** Worker Life Changing
**One-liner:** The study is rigorous, the findings are clear, the deck is presented to the team, and the researcher never finds out whether any of it changed anything.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #causal-inference #confidence-intervals #bert #gradient-boosting #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #tacit-knowledge-ml

## The Problem
A games user researcher designs a study, runs it, analyses it and presents findings to a development team. The presentation is the last point of contact. What happens afterward — whether the finding was accepted, whether it was scheduled, whether a change shipped, whether it helped — is not communicated back and is frequently not known to anyone.

The reception in the room is its own difficulty. Research findings frequently contradict what a designer believes about their own work, and the messenger is an outsider with twelve participants. Researchers describe the recurring experience of a well-evidenced finding being dismissed because the sample was small, the participants were not the real audience, or the build was early — objections that are sometimes fair and are also the standard way an unwelcome finding is deflected.

The structural position makes it worse. Research is usually commissioned late, against a milestone, when the thing being tested is largely finished and the schedule has no room for substantial change. A finding that identifies a fundamental problem arrives at the point where only cosmetic responses are possible, and everyone knows it.

And the researcher has no evidence that their work matters. Without outcome data, the case for research is made with reference to the field generally rather than to this team's own history, which is a weak argument to bring to a budget conversation.

## Why It Matters to the Worker
This is skilled evidence-producing work whose effect is unobservable to the person producing it. That is demoralising in a specific way — the researcher cannot tell a good study from a bad one by its consequences, only by its execution, which means professional development runs on craft standards rather than on results.

The repeated experience of being dismissed accumulates. Researchers describe learning to soften findings so they are heard, which is a rational adaptation and directly degrades the value of the work. The incentive gradient runs toward findings that are welcome rather than findings that are true.

And the timing problem means the role is frequently set up to fail. Being asked to evaluate something too late to change, then being judged on impact, is a structure that would frustrate anyone.

## What a Solution Looks Like
Close the loop and make it visible. Structured findings tracked through to implementation status and telemetry outcome give the researcher the one thing the role lacks: evidence about their own work. Even partial returns — which findings were implemented — change the picture substantially.

Build the local case. A team's own history of research findings, implementation and outcomes is the argument for research budget and for earlier commissioning, and it is far more persuasive than general appeals to the value of user research.

Support the finding in the room. A finding accompanied by clips from every participant who hit the problem, by an honest statement of what twelve sessions can and cannot support, and by the telemetry pattern consistent with it, is considerably harder to deflect than a slide with a severity rating.

State the evidence honestly and let that be acceptable. Part of the softening problem is that hedged findings are ignored, so researchers overstate. A reporting format that conveys strength of evidence in a way teams can act on — this is near-certain, this is suggestive and worth testing — is a craft problem the field has not solved and would remove the incentive to overstate.

## Impact If Solved
Games user research is a serious discipline whose practitioners cannot see their own effect, which leaves them arguing for their value from first principles and adapting their findings to be heard. Outcome tracking gives them evidence, the local history gives them the budget argument, and better evidential presentation removes the pressure to overstate — which is the mechanism currently degrading the quality of findings across the field.
