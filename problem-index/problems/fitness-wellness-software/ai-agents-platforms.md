## 1. Retention Agent
#ai-agent #survival-analysis #gradient-boosting #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #confidence-intervals #revenue-impact #workflow-orchestration

**Concept:** An agent that watches every member's attendance pattern against their own history and acts while they are still recoverable. It detects the decay — a broken habitual slot, a drop in weekly frequency, a shift away from the classes they used to take — and produces a short daily task list for the studio with the specific reason attached, so a real person can send a real message. Where the studio permits, it sends the outreach itself in the studio's voice. Critically, it runs the interventions as experiments across the platform's studio base, so the question of what actually recovers a lapsing member finally gets an evidence-based answer.

**Inputs:** Check-in events with class, slot and instructor; membership terms and tenure; booking and cancellation behaviour; payment status; instructor departures and schedule changes; prior outreach and its outcomes.

**Outputs / Actions:** A daily prioritised outreach list with the triggering observation named. Optional automated messages within studio-approved templates. Pre-authorised retention offers where evidence supports them. A weekly retention report showing intervention outcomes with honest attribution. It never sends an offer the studio has not authorised, and it never contacts a member who has asked not to be.

**Why now:** The signal has always been there and nobody looked, because these vendors monetise payment processing and retention analytics did not obviously drive volume. The experimentation capability is the genuinely new part and requires the scale these platforms already have.

**Market:** Studios, gyms and wellness businesses on Mindbody, Zen Planner, Glofox, WellnessLiving and their peers — well over a hundred thousand US locations. Retention is the entire economics, and the payback is visible in one quarter of churn numbers the operator already watches.

## 2. Schedule Planning Platform
#ai-platform #time-series-forecasting #gradient-boosting #optimization-fundamentals #confidence-intervals #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact

**Concept:** A platform that treats the class grid as a production plan rather than an inheritance. It forecasts attendance for class configurations the studio has never run, using pooled behaviour from comparable studios, corrects for the demand hidden by full classes and waitlists, and models member-level substitution so an operator can see whether a proposed class adds attendance or just moves it. It proposes grid changes with expected utilisation and revenue, and flags the slots where waitlist depth has been signalling unmet demand for months.

**Inputs:** Class attendance history with type, slot, instructor and capacity; waitlist depth; member-level attendance patterns; studio and market attributes; comparable studios across the platform base; local seasonality and events.

**Outputs / Actions:** Proposed schedule changes with forecast attendance, utilisation and revenue impact, each with a range. Unmet-demand alerts from persistent waitlists. Substitution analysis distinguishing incremental attendance from cannibalisation. Instructor-and-class-type fit recommendations for staffing decisions, framed around scheduling rather than ranking people.

**Why now:** Forecasting a class that has never run requires pooling across studios, which only became possible at the scale these platforms reached. Waitlist censoring — the reason every previous analysis under-measured demand — is a solved statistical problem that nobody in the category applied.

**Market:** Studio and gym operators with fixed rent and fixed instructor cost, where utilisation is the only real margin lever. Also the multi-location and franchise operators, who feel it most and have the analytical appetite to act on it.

## 3. Instructor Coordination Agent
#ai-agent #gradient-boosting #optimization-fundamentals #large-language-models #evaluation-metrics #workflow-orchestration #automation #worker-facing

**Concept:** An agent that serves the instructor rather than the studio. It assembles a single view of the week across every studio they teach at, represents travel time as a hard constraint so double bookings become impossible, publishes availability once to everywhere, and consolidates earnings across studios into a real hourly figure including travel. When a class needs covering it routes the request to instructors who are qualified, available, close enough and historically likely to accept — instead of broadcasting to a group chat and interrupting everyone.

**Inputs:** Teaching schedules across studios; instructor qualifications and class type history; stated availability; travel distances between locations; historical sub offer and acceptance behaviour; pay rates and class attendance.

**Outputs / Actions:** A consolidated weekly schedule with conflicts and travel infeasibility flagged before acceptance. Targeted sub request routing with fill-rate tracking. Availability propagated to every studio. Consolidated earnings with per-hour analysis including unpaid travel. It represents the instructor's interests explicitly, which is what makes it adopted.

**Why now:** Instructor departure is one of the strongest predictors of member churn, and the working conditions that drive it — fragmentation, unpaid coordination, contractor status — have never been anyone's product because every platform serves the studio. The scheduling problem itself is small; the perspective shift is the innovation.

**Market:** Instructors directly as a consumer product, and studios as a retention investment in the people who are their actual product. The strategic argument to a platform vendor is that whoever owns the instructor relationship owns the layer above the studios, which is a position none of them currently hold.
