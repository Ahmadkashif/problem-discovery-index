# AI Agents & Platform Opportunities — Digital Goods Marketplaces

**Industry:** [[digital-goods-marketplaces|Digital Goods Marketplaces]]

---

## 1. Creator Protection Platform
#ai-platform #cnns #contrastive-learning #transformers #dimensionality-reduction #confidence-intervals #compliance #revenue-impact

**Concept:** A platform that moves enforcement from the individual creator to the party with the corpus and the scale. It fingerprints every asset in formats the industry assumed could not be fingerprinted — structural signatures for code and templates, glyph-level for fonts, parameter-level for presets — and matches continuously against public sources, robust to the re-encoding, cropping and repackaging redistributors actually use. It embeds per-purchase watermarks in the delivery path where the format allows, so a leaked copy identifies its origin and the deterrent structure changes. And it generates and tracks notices so a creator confirms rather than composes, with reappearance monitored automatically.

**Inputs:** The full asset corpus by format and category; purchase and delivery records; crawled public sources; confirmed historical redistribution instances; observed transformation patterns.

**Outputs / Actions:** Continuous redistribution detection with match evidence. Per-purchase watermarking at delivery. Automated notice generation and reappearance tracking. Category-level scale measurement — what redistribution actually costs, which nobody currently knows and which is the finding that justifies the investment. Creator-facing reporting that replaces manual searching.

**Why now:** Fingerprinting for functional asset formats is feasible and essentially unexplored, because the industry equated fingerprinting with perceptual hashing and concluded it did not apply. The obstacle was always the economics of enforcement, which is precisely the argument for centralising it.

**Market:** Digital goods marketplaces, font foundries, stock and asset libraries, and game asset stores. It also serves creators directly as a service, since the platforms' incentives are weaker than the creators' — a platform loses only its commission on a substituted sale.

## 2. Licence and Entitlement Agent
#ai-agent #large-language-models #bert #transformers #evaluation-metrics #transfer-learning #compliance #data-integration

**Concept:** An agent that makes licence terms usable by the systems and people who need them. It extracts entitlements from purchase agreements into machine-readable form — permitted uses, seat counts, distribution limits, territory, duration — attaches them to the purchase record, and answers the question a designer actually has: what is my organisation licensed to do with this asset. Where usage is publicly observable, as with fonts on websites or assets in published applications, it identifies use that exceeds the licence and frames it as a compliance prompt rather than an accusation, because most breach is unintentional and originates in the gap between an individual purchase and organisational use.

**Inputs:** Licence agreements and tier definitions; purchase records with buyer, tier and asset; asset fingerprints; publicly observable usage; organisational structure where team purchasing exists; historical breach claims and resolutions.

**Outputs / Actions:** Structured entitlements attached to every purchase. Buyer-facing entitlement lookup across an organisation. Upgrade prompts where intended use exceeds the current licence — which converts a breach into a sale. Creator-facing evidence where breach is established. Team licensing recommendations based on actual usage patterns.

**Why now:** Extraction from these agreements is bounded and reliable, and it is the precondition for every downstream improvement. Font compliance in particular is an expensive enterprise problem precisely because nobody can determine what an organisation is entitled to use.

**Market:** Marketplaces, font foundries with enterprise licensing programmes, and the enterprise buyers who currently carry unquantified compliance exposure. Framing it as helping compliant buyers comply — and as an upgrade path — is both more accurate and commercially stronger than framing it as enforcement.

## 3. Discovery and Review Agent
#ai-agent #contrastive-learning #cnns #transformers #word-embeddings #k-nearest-neighbors #evaluation-metrics #compliance

**Concept:** An agent covering the two functions that determine which creators earn. On discovery it learns a joint embedding between how buyers describe what they want and how assets actually look, inducing a grounded aesthetic vocabulary from millions of query-to-purchase pairs — the only place such a vocabulary could come from — and extracts hard compatibility filters directly from files rather than from creator descriptions, which is where wrong purchases and refunds originate. On review it triages copyright claims: clear matches and demonstrably independent work decided automatically, and the contested middle presented with corpus-wide common-convention analysis showing whether the shared elements are widespread in the category, alongside timestamps, catalogues and comparable prior decisions.

**Inputs:** Search queries with clicks and purchases; asset files and imagery; creator tags as a weak prior; refund reasons; claimed and accused assets with upload history; both creators' catalogues; the full corpus for convention baselines; historical decisions and appeals.

**Outputs / Actions:** Discovery ranked on purchase conversion with new-creator visibility reported separately. Compatibility filters extracted from files. Bundle coherence for assets that work together. Automated claim decisions on clear cases. Evidence packages with convention analysis for the contested middle. Tactical claim pattern detection. Appeal overturn tracking as a first-class metric.

**Why now:** The query-to-purchase corpus encodes the aesthetic vocabulary the field lacks and sits unused inside these marketplaces. On the review side, corpus-wide convention analysis is something no individual reviewer can approximate and is directly relevant to the protectability question they are being asked to answer in minutes.

**Market:** Digital asset marketplaces of every kind. Discovery determines which creators earn and therefore which stay, and claim review determines whether the platform's removal threshold is destroying legitimate creators' income — which appeal tracking is the only way to know.
