## 1. Technical Assessment Platform
#ai-platform #graph-neural-networks #gradient-boosting #change-point-detection #survival-analysis #confidence-intervals #evaluation-metrics #tacit-knowledge-ml

**Concept:** A platform that gives rapid technical assessment an instrument. From repository and delivery history alone — obtainable in days, even under the constrained access typical of diligence — it derives change concentration, co-change coupling, churn and its defect correlation, component stability, ownership concentration and bus-factor exposure, and delivery flow with rework rates. It presents that as the evidence base a practitioner applies judgement to, arriving before the interviews rather than after, so the conversations with the team start from what the system actually does.

**Inputs:** Commit history with authorship and paths; issue and ticket history with estimates, actuals and reopens; incident records; deployment frequency and lead time; contributor tenure; build and coverage history.

**Outputs / Actions:** A measured assessment base within days, with explicit statements of what history cannot see — team dynamics, external constraints and the reasoning behind decisions, which is where judgement remains indispensable and should be said rather than implied. Quantified key-person exposure per critical component, which post-close post-mortems repeatedly identify as decisive and which diligence reports almost never quantify. Delivery capacity tested against the plan's assumptions rather than accepted from management. A retained record so a firm can eventually say which measured patterns preceded the problems that materialised.

**Why now:** The analysis is computationally modest and has existed as a possibility for years; what has changed is that read-only history access is now routinely obtainable in engagements and diligence, and the profession's growth has made the absence of any instrument conspicuous.

**Market:** Fractional and interim CTOs, technical due diligence practices, private equity operating groups running many deals, and the venture firms making the same assessment repeatedly with less structure.

## 2. Selection Evidence Platform
#ai-platform #bert #large-language-models #k-nearest-neighbors #gradient-boosting #confidence-intervals #evaluation-metrics #survival-analysis

**Concept:** A platform that replaces vendor documentation and analyst rankings with operational evidence. It assembles what running a platform is actually like from where that is genuinely recorded — incident write-ups, engineering blogs, conference talks, community threads — weighted by source credibility, since a vendor case study and a postmortem from a team that migrated away are not equivalent inputs. It assesses fit against this client's usage shape, team skills, operational maturity, compliance needs and growth trajectory, models cost against the usage the client will actually produce rather than a list price, and prices the exit.

**Inputs:** Public operational writing with recency and credibility weighting; vendor documentation and pricing models; the client's usage characteristics and constraints; dependency depth and proprietary surface area; realised bills where clients share them.

**Outputs / Actions:** A fit assessment rather than a ranking. Operational reality with attribution, so an advisor can show where a concern comes from. Cost modelled against the client's usage shape, checkable against realised bills. An explicit exit cost estimate as a range with assumptions listed, which prices the lock-in currently accepted implicitly. Recency weighting throughout, because a platform's operational character three years ago may be irrelevant.

**Why now:** The operational evidence has never been more abundant — incident write-ups and engineering blogs are now a substantial public literature — and it is unstructured, scattered and used only by whoever happens to have read it.

**Market:** Fractional CTOs and architecture advisors, in-house platform teams making the same decisions, and the private equity and venture operating partners who see the consequences of these choices repeatedly.

## 3. Engagement Continuity Agent
#ai-agent #large-language-models #bert #graph-neural-networks #gradient-boosting #worker-facing #workflow-orchestration #tacit-knowledge-ml

**Concept:** An agent that addresses the two structural problems of fractional work — context fragmentation while the engagement runs, and the reasoning departing when it ends. On return to a client it assembles what changed since the last visit and ranks it by divergence from what the practitioner expected, surfacing the three things that matter rather than a log. Throughout, it maintains an externalised model of each client — architecture, decisions and their rationale, team structure, open concerns, commitments made. And at the end it produces the handover the profession currently improvises: decision records with rejected alternatives, assumptions with their failure triggers, recorded disagreements and how they were resolved.

**Inputs:** Repository, ticket and incident activity between visits; delivery trends; team communications and meeting records where granted; the practitioner's own notes, recommendations and prior decisions; engagement documents and slides.

**Outputs / Actions:** A short attention-ranked briefing replacing the first two hours of every visit. An externalised client model that reduces reconstruction cost and makes a fractional practice transferable rather than resident in one person's memory. Decision records whose rejected-alternatives and assumptions fields are actually populated — the two fields that make a plan adaptable and that current documentation omits — with honest abstention where no rationale was ever recorded rather than plausible reconstruction. Recorded disagreements, which is what lets a later team revisit a decision as a decision rather than as a challenge to a departed authority.

**Why now:** Fractional engagement volume has grown substantially and the model's two characteristic failures — the practitioner permanently slightly behind, and the abandoned half-executed migration — are both addressable by externalising what currently lives in one person's head.

**Market:** Fractional and interim technology leaders, boutique advisory firms wanting practices rather than individuals, and the client-side engineering leads who inherit these engagements and currently inherit nothing usable.
