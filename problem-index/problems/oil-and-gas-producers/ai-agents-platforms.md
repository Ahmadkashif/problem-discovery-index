## 1. Location Decision Ledger and Inventory Ranking Platform
#ai-platform #gradient-boosting #gaussian-processes #bayesian-inference #tacit-knowledge-ml #revenue-impact

**Concept:** A platform that ranks an operator's undrilled locations on predicted, uncertainty-banded production, accounting for spacing, bench and the age of neighbouring wells. It records every approved location's analog set, haircut and reason as the engineer works, and grades each call against the well's normalised production at 12 and 24 months. A departing engineer's adjustments stay with the acreage, and the next engineer sees how similar calls turned out.
**Inputs:** State production, permit and completion filings; FracFocus completion data; the operator's geology grids, type curves, development plans and planning-tool projects; parent-well production; drilling schedules.
**Outputs / Actions:** Ranked inventory with P10/P50/P90 forecasts; proposed analog sets with the neighbours that drove them; predicted parent-well losses; a decision ledger and quarterly calibration report for the VP of development; inventory books for A&D and lenders.
**Why now:** The remaining locations in the core plays are mostly infill and lower-tier benches where spacing decides the answer. Gradient-boosted and Gaussian-process models now fit well panels of tens of thousands of horizontals cheaply, and LLMs can draft the ledger entry from the engineer's own planning session so capture takes seconds.
**Market:** VPs of development and reservoir engineering at the large operators, plus A&D teams, mineral buyers and investors. Novi Labs, which sells AI production forecasting to "E&P operators, analysts and investors", listed a Director of Platform Engineering and a Director of Software Delivery (Austin or remote), a Senior Product Manager (Calgary, Canada), an Inventory Research Analyst (Calgary or Austin) and a Customer Success Advisor – Reservoir Engineer (Midland) on 25 October 2026, in undated postings. Enverus listed software engineers in Spokane and remote US, a full-stack engineer in Pittsburgh, a site reliability engineer in Calgary or remote Canada, and data and software engineers in Brno, Czechia (undated postings). Corva, which sells drilling and completion analytics, listed solutions engineers in Houston and Midland, and software engineers in Argentina, Spain, Poland, Brazil and Saudi Arabia (undated postings). The vendor layer is profiled as [[niches/oil-gas-field-services/upstream-well-production-data/profile|🔍 Upstream Well & Production Data Providers]] (56, indexed). A two-week MVP is a parent-child loss model on North Dakota or Texas public production and FracFocus completions, scored out of time against realised 12-month volumes.

## 2. Owner Relations and Division Order Agent
#ai-agent #large-language-models #bert #k-nearest-neighbors #compliance #automation

**Concept:** An agent that sits on an operator's revenue and land data and works the owner queue. It answers an owner's question about a check stub line by line from the check detail, the lease's deduction clause and the purchaser statement. It drafts division-order changes from probate orders, deeds and affidavits with the decimals rebalanced, matches suspended interests to likely owners and heirs, and schedules escheat by state before dormancy runs out.
**Inputs:** Owner master file and decimal schedules; check detail; leases and title opinions; recorded instruments; purchaser statements; suspense ledger; owner emails, calls and portal messages; state unclaimed-property rules.
**Outputs / Actions:** Owner replies with the arithmetic shown; draft division-order changes for analyst approval; suspense resolution packets; escheat schedules and filings; a monthly suspense and dispute report for the revenue manager.
**Why now:** LLMs read deeds, probate orders and leases well enough to draft changes for a human to approve, and can explain a deduction in plain words. Mineral ownership keeps fragmenting through inheritance and trading, so owner counts per well rise every year. NARO puts US royalty owners at 8 to 12 million (search summary).
**Market:** Revenue and owner-relations managers at operators, and the accounting vendors that sell to them. Enverus was recruiting an Owner Relations Agent (Austin, Denver or remote US, $43,000–$58,000), a production role that shows the work is staffed rather than automated. Pandell, a Calgary land and accounting software vendor, listed a Senior Cloud Engineer (Calgary, Canada, posted 23 October 2026) and an Industry Specialist for land software (Calgary). Quorum's and PakEnergy's openings could not be read from their sites, and W Energy's applicant-tracking board listed no openings. MineralSoft, an owner-side mineral management platform from YC's W2016 batch, is listed as acquired. Put plainly, no land, royalty, division-order or production-accounting vendor had an engineering or forward deployed role that could be confirmed on its own board on 25 October 2026, apart from Pandell's cloud engineer in Calgary. A two-week MVP answers synthetic owner questions from generated check stubs and public lease forms and drafts decimal changes from sample probate orders, scored on arithmetic exactness.

## 3. Land, Title and Lease-Obligation Agent
#ai-agent #large-language-models #transformers #survival-analysis #compliance #workflow-orchestration

**Concept:** An agent for the land department. It builds a draft runsheet for a drilling unit from county index searches and recorded instruments, extracts lease clauses and amendments, and computes each lease's held-by-production status from production, unit and shut-in data. It warns land and drilling teams when the schedule no longer meets a continuous-development or shut-in deadline, and drafts pooling or spacing applications from the ownership schedule where the state allows them.
**Inputs:** County grantor-grantee indexes and recorded images; title plant data where it exists; lease files; unit designations; monthly production and shut-in status; drilling schedules; commission docket rules.
**Outputs / Actions:** Draft runsheets with every instrument linked; lease abstracts and obligation calendars; HBP status by tract; escalations before deadlines; draft pooling applications and owner notice lists.
**Why now:** Handwriting recognition and LLM extraction on old deed books have become good enough to draft a runsheet for a landman to check. Enverus added a Runsheet Assistant to its Courthouse product in October 2026, claiming up to 75% less analysis time (search summary), which signals the category is opening.
**Market:** Land managers at operators, land brokerages, and mineral buyers doing title diligence. Enverus listed a Product Specialist – Land (Austin, Houston or Denver) and a six-month Contract Energy Examiner (remote US, $58,000–$63,000) who searches title back to patent, both production or product roles rather than engineering. Energy Domain's TitleLab and Rowland, a land AI product from the Paramount brokerage launched with AAPL in 2025, are newer entrants (search summary, hiring not checked). Title plants are profiled as [[niches/land-surveyors/title-plant-search-operations/profile|🔍 Title Plant & Search Operations]] (55, indexed). A two-week MVP builds a runsheet for a few sections in one county from that county's public online index and images and scores it against a landman's hand-built version.

## 4. Field Operations and Methane Records Agent
#ai-agent #cnns #change-point-detection #gradient-boosting #worker-facing #compliance

**Concept:** An agent for the operator's field organisation. For pumpers, it ranks each day's stops by which wells have broken their normal pattern, from gauge readings, SCADA and run tickets, and captures readings by photo or voice. For LDAR crews, it matches OGI clips to sites and components, flags likely plumes for confirmation, drafts survey logs, and chases each repair to closure before its deadline. For the emissions team, it assembles the records for state rules, OOOOb reports and the Greenhouse Gas Reporting Program.
**Inputs:** Gauge sheets and phone readings; SCADA tags; run tickets; downtime notes; OGI video and camera metadata; continuous-monitor and aerial-survey detections; site equipment lists; repair work orders.
**Outputs / Actions:** Exception-ranked routes; production records without re-keying; leak records with clips attached; repair escalations; draft survey logs and annual reports; site risk rankings for the next survey cycle.
**Why now:** An EPA interim final rule published 31 July 2025, and finalised in November 2025, moved several OOOOb compliance dates and the state-plan deadline to 22 January 2027 (EPA), so operators are building programmes now. EPA moved the reporting year 2025 GHGRP deadline to 30 October 2026 and has proposed suspending Subpart W reporting (search summary), which means the record-keeping rules may keep moving. Cheap continuous monitors and aerial surveys now generate far more detections than crews can triage by hand.
**Market:** Production superintendents and emissions managers at operators, and the monitoring vendors. Cognite, an industrial data platform that sells across industries, oil and gas among them, listed a Senior Field Engineer (Pre-Sales/Forward Deployed) in Houston or remote US (updated 9 October 2026) and an Enterprise Field Engineer in Houston. That pre-sales/forward deployed role was the only forward-deployed title found in this check. Project Canary listed a Software Engineer (Rust), an Embedded Test Automation Engineer and Senior Electrical Engineers in Hayward, California and Denver. Validere, a measurement and emissions data platform (YC S2016), listed a Lead Software Engineer in Toronto or Calgary, Canada (undated posting). GHGSat listed a Data Scientist in Montreal, Canada. Highwood's and Bridger Photonics' openings could not be read. Satellite detection is profiled as [[niches/satellite-operators/satellite-methane-analytics/profile|🔍 Satellite Methane & Emissions Analytics]] (40). A two-week MVP is an exception ranker on synthetic gauge-sheet series plus a plume classifier fine-tuned on a public OGI leak video set, scored on recall at a fixed false-alarm rate.
