# AI Agents & Platform Opportunities — Outdoor Advertising Companies

**Industry:** [[outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]

---

## 1. Site Steward Agent
#ai-agent #large-language-models #survival-analysis #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #compliance

**Concept:** An agent that holds one record per structure, covering lease, state permit, local permit, nonconforming status and the face's revenue, and works it continuously. It calculates percentage-rent statements from billing, files permit renewals before state deadlines, drafts renewal and renegotiation letters ahead of option dates, and flags sites at risk: a landlord who has received a buyout offer, a sale of the land, a rezoning hearing, a highway project on the state's plan.
**Inputs:** Scanned leases and amendments, payment ledgers, state DOT permit lists and renewal portals, county ownership records, billing exports, landlord correspondence, transportation improvement plans.
**Outputs / Actions:** Rent statements, permit renewal filings, critical-date queue, landlord letters for approval, ranked site-risk list with recommended action (renegotiate, buy the land, offer an easement purchase).
**Why now:** Language models can read decades of inconsistent lease amendments into structured terms cheaply, which was the obstacle to every earlier attempt. Lease aggregators that pay landlords roughly 6–12× annual rent have made unmonitored leases a direct loss risk.
**Market:** Real estate departments at the large and mid-sized operators, and independents through their billboard software vendor. Lease administration platforms are adjacent sellers built for office tenancies.

## 2. Sign Code and Entitlement Research Agent
#ai-agent #large-language-models #transformers #compliance #workflow-orchestration #revenue-impact

**Concept:** An agent that reads municipal sign codes, state spacing and digital display rules, and council agendas, and keeps a map of what the company may legally build, convert, relocate or must defend. It finds cap-and-replace and relocation opportunities (remove two statics, gain one digital), checks digital conversion eligibility face by face, drafts permit applications and staff-report responses, and alerts when a city proposes a code amendment.
**Inputs:** Municipal codes and amendments, state outdoor advertising rules, the company's structure and permit inventory, GIS layers for spacing and sensitive sites, council and planning commission agendas and minutes.
**Outputs / Actions:** Eligibility map by face and parcel; opportunity list; draft applications and relocation agreement terms; amendment alerts with impact on named faces.
**Why now:** City of Austin v. Reagan National Advertising (2022) confirmed that cities may treat off-premises signs differently without strict scrutiny, which invited code revisions. Language models can now parse thousands of local codes that no legal team could track by hand.
**Market:** Real estate and legal teams at operators; land-use counsel who serve them. There is no existing buyer category, and the work now sits with outside counsel and senior real estate managers.

## 3. Local Proposal and Renewal Agent
#ai-agent #large-language-models #diffusion-models #causal-inference #worker-facing #revenue-impact

**Concept:** An assistant for local account executives that drafts proposals from a short brief, mocks up the advertiser's creative on each proposed face, checks availability and lease and copy restrictions before anything is promised, and before each renewal assembles a plain-language results summary from call tracking, branded search and web lift in the trade area, licensed location data where permitted, and the advertiser's own sales if shared.
**Inputs:** Inventory and availability, face photos and maps, Geopath impressions, rate cards, advertiser brief and creative, response data sources, contract history.
**Outputs / Actions:** Draft proposal and mock-ups; contract-ready package; renewal-risk alerts; results summary that separates measured response from correlation.
**Why now:** Image generation makes convincing face mock-ups instant. Cheap trade-area search and web data finally give a local advertiser something measurable to look at besides an impression estimate.
**Market:** The ~1,000 local account executives at Lamar and their equivalents at every operator; local revenue is ~79% of Lamar's outdoor revenue. CRM vendors and billboard software packages are the incumbents.

## 4. Operating Platform for Independent Operators
#ai-platform #large-language-models #cnns #time-series-forecasting #data-integration #automation

**Concept:** A single system for the ~2,000 small operators covering inventory, availability, contracts, posting work orders with photo verification, leases and permits, billing, a connection to programmatic supply platforms for any digital faces, and audience data. Pooled, anonymised yield data across operators gives each one a location-value model it could not train on its own few hundred faces.
**Inputs:** Each operator's faces, contracts, leases and permits; crew photos; billing; programmatic demand; Geopath data where the operator is a member.
**Outputs / Actions:** Availability and proposals, posting routes and verified proofs, lease and permit alerts, invoices, programmatic listings, pooled benchmarks of rate and occupancy by market and format.
**Why now:** Programmatic digital out-of-home lets a small operator's faces reach national buyers without a sales force, but only if inventory is clean and measured. Geopath's smaller-operator tier ($229 a month) and cloud tools have brought the cost of being "buyable" within reach.
**Market:** Independents now on QuickBooks plus Apparatix, BillboardPlanet, SignDash or HighSign Pro. 64% of the industry's establishments have fewer than five employees, so the price point is small and the count is large.
