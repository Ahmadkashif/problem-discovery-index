# The Local Account Executive at Renewal Time

**Industry:** [[outdoor-advertising-companies|Outdoor Advertising Companies]]
**Type:** Worker Life Changing
**One-liner:** The local account executive gets back the evenings spent assembling proposals by hand, and walks into each renewal with evidence instead of reassurance.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #diffusion-models #causal-inference #gradient-boosting #worker-facing #revenue-impact #automation

## The Problem
Local advertisers pay for most of the industry: about 79% of Lamar's outdoor revenue is local, sold by roughly 1,000 account executives to service businesses, health care providers, restaurants, retailers and car dealers. An account executive carries ~80–150 active advertisers (estimate) and spends much of the week building proposals. They pull availability for the faces that fit, export maps and site photos, attach Geopath impressions the client doesn't understand, price the package, and mock up the client's logo on a board so the owner can picture it. Then they chase creative that arrives late and in the wrong format, and explain a posting delay.

The hardest moment is renewal. A dentist or a personal injury firm that bought a bulletin for twelve months wants to know whether it worked. The account executive has a proof-of-posting photo and an impression estimate, and the client has a feeling. Commission rides on that conversation, and the account executive goes into it with nothing to show.

## Why It Matters to the Worker
Account executives are paid largely on commission, and their book is their income. Hours spent rebuilding the same proposal format are hours not spent prospecting. A renewal lost because the client "didn't see results" is a cut in pay that the account executive could do nothing about. They believe the boards work; they just can't show it to an owner who reads the bank statement, not the impression report.

## What a Solution Looks Like
A proposal assistant that drafts a package from a short brief (business, budget, target area, start date). It picks available faces, writes the plan in plain language with maps and photos, and generates a realistic mock-up of the client's creative on each selected face. For renewal, it assembles a results summary from what can actually be measured for a local business: call-tracking numbers or vanity URLs printed on the board, website and branded search lift in the face's trade area, store visit changes from a licensed location panel where the client allows, and the client's own sales by month if they share them. The summary is honest about what is correlation and what is not, and it flags accounts at renewal risk early enough to fix the creative or the location.

## Impact If Solved
Proposal assembly drops from an hour or more to minutes, freeing several hours a week per account executive for selling. Renewal conversations rest on evidence the owner can follow, which protects the account executive's income and the operator's largest revenue stream.
