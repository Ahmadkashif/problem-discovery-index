# The Associate Rebuilding the Model at Midnight

**Industry:** [[private-equity-firms|Private Equity Firms]]
**Type:** Worker Life Changing
**One-liner:** An associate gets back the nights spent re-spreading the same financials into the same LBO every time the seller posts a new file.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #feature-engineering #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #automation #workflow-orchestration

## The Problem
The associate is the firm's research engine. On a live deal they spread historicals from the CIM into the model, rebuild them when the data room opens with different numbers, rebuild again when the QoE provider moves adjusted EBITDA, maintain a diligence request list across six providers, produce the customer cohort and pricing analyses, and turn all of it into IC pages. Two or three deals run at once, plus screening work on new CIMs. The work is repeatable in shape — the same model architecture, the same analyses, the same pages — and bespoke only in its inputs, which arrive late, in PDF, and in a different layout every time.

## Why It Matters to the Worker
Associates are two- to three-year analysts-turned-investors who joined to learn judgement and spend most of their hours on transcription and reconciliation. The schedule is set by the seller's banker and the exclusivity clock, not by them, so the weekend disappears whenever a data room drop lands on Friday afternoon. The skill they are meant to be building — seeing what matters in a business — is crowded out by the skill they actually practise, which is not breaking the model.

## What a Solution Looks Like
Financial statements and KPI tables extracted from CIMs and data room files into the firm's model template with each figure linked to its source page; a diff when a new file contradicts a prior one ("the revised trial balance changes 2024 gross margin by 140bps; the following model cells depend on it"); diligence request tracking that reads provider responses and marks items closed; and standard analyses — cohort retention, customer concentration, price-volume bridges — generated from the extracted data for the associate to check rather than build.

## Impact If Solved
A meaningful share of an associate's deal hours is transcription, rework and reconciliation. Returning even half of that changes the job from model maintenance to analysis, makes the late nights the exception rather than the rhythm, and lets associates spend their two years learning the part of the work that actually makes them investors.
