# The Fan-Support Agent After a Postponement

**Industry:** [[ticketing-agencies|Ticketing & Reservation Agencies]]
**Type:** Worker Life Changing
**One-liner:** When a tour is postponed or a ticket fails at the gate, a fan-support agent faces the angriest customers in commerce with a policy they cannot change, and gets back clarity and dignity if the system resolves the routine cases before they reach them.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #bert #transformers #logistic-regression #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #automation

## The Problem
Fan-support agents at ticketing companies and resale marketplaces work a queue that moves with the event calendar. On an ordinary weekend it is transfer problems, tickets that will not load in the app, and buyers who cannot find their seats. When a show is cancelled, postponed or moved to another venue, the queue jumps by tens of thousands of contacts in a day (estimate). Every fan wants to know the same things: am I getting a refund or a credit, when, and what about the fees and the hotel? The answer depends on whether the ticket was bought on the primary site or resold, whether the organiser has authorised refunds yet, the marketplace's guarantee, the card used and the buyer's state.

On resale marketplaces the hardest calls are buyers at the gate whose barcode does not scan, because the seat was sold twice or the broker never transferred it, with the show starting in twenty minutes. Vivid Seats reported that event cancellations alone reduced its 2025 marketplace order value by $60.7M. Behind each of those orders sits a contact, a refund and often a chargeback.

## Why It Matters to the Worker
Agents absorb abuse for decisions made elsewhere: by the promoter who has not authorised refunds, the broker who failed to deliver, or the policy that offers a credit instead of cash. They work from scripts that change daily during a postponement, with handle-time targets that punish the careful agent. Many are contractors in outsourced centres with high turnover. The job wears people down because they rarely get to resolve anything.

## What a Solution Looks Like
A support layer that knows each order's full state (primary or resale, organiser's refund status, marketplace guarantee, delivery record, card dispute status) and resolves routine contacts automatically: confirming refund timing, reissuing transfers, offering replacement seats inside policy. The cases that reach the agent come with a one-paragraph summary of what happened and the options the agent is allowed to offer, and policy changes during a postponement are pushed to the agent's screen rather than read out in a morning briefing.

## Impact If Solved
If half of postponement contacts resolved without an agent, the agent's day would shift from repeating the same answer to fixing real failures (estimate). Turnover and chargebacks raised out of frustration would both fall.
