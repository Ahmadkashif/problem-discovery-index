# AI Agents & Platform Opportunities — Executive Search Firms

**Industry:** [[executive-search-firms|Executive Search Firms]]

## 1. Placement Outcome Ledger
#ai-platform #large-language-models #survival-analysis #causal-inference #tacit-knowledge-ml #revenue-impact

**Concept:** A platform that turns a firm's assessments into a graded record. It drafts a structured assessment record from each interview debrief and candidate report, then follows every placed and every presented-but-not-hired candidate through Form 8-K Item 5.02 filings, press releases, sponsor portfolio pages and biography changes, coding departures by likely reason. Partners see their recommendations' survival curves by role, sector and ownership type; the firm sees which assessment signals predicted early failure.
**Inputs:** CRM candidate and placement records, interview notes and debriefs, candidate reports, psychometric results where used, public filings and announcements, guarantee terms.
**Outputs / Actions:** Structured assessment records; 6-, 12-, 18- and 36-month status for every placement; early alerts when a placed executive's company announces a reorganisation or a new CEO; practice-level survival scorecards for pitches; replacement-risk flags inside the guarantee period.
**Why now:** Language models can draft structured records from consultants' free-text notes cheaply enough that capture no longer depends on discipline, and Item 5.02 makes public-company outcomes machine-readable within days. Record CEO turnover (234 exits across Russell Reynolds' tracked indices in 2025) has made boards ask search firms for evidence rather than reputation.
**Market:** The research and knowledge leadership of the large global firms and the 30–60 mid-sized firms with research functions (estimate); search CRMs are natural partners but store candidates, not outcomes.

## 2. Longlist Research Agent
#ai-agent #large-language-models #word-embeddings #k-nearest-neighbors #compliance #automation #workflow-orchestration

**Concept:** An agent that runs the identification stage of a search: proposes a target company list from the brief and the firm's past searches, reconstructs leadership structures at each target, levels titles against the firm's own levelled maps, clears every name against off-limits terms and corporate families, issues GDPR Article 14 privacy notices for European candidates within the one-month window, and drafts first-approach messages for the researcher to send.
**Inputs:** The brief, the firm's historical maps and CRM, company filings and announcements, executive and board data (BoardEx, Equilar), client off-limits terms, privacy policy templates.
**Outputs / Actions:** A levelled longlist with evidence per name; off-limits clearances with the rule applied; privacy notices logged; draft outreach; a refreshed firm-wide market map.
**Why now:** Retrieval over a firm's own past maps plus language-model extraction from filings now does in hours what a researcher did in a week, and the industry is already moving: 68.9% of firms in Clockwork's 2023 survey wanted AI to suggest target company lists.
**Market:** Every retained firm; the large firms' research centres buy for capacity, boutiques buy it instead of a research hire. Competes with AI sourcing tools (Pin, SeekOut, hireEZ) that rank profiles by fit but do not hold off-limits, levelling or the firm's history.

## 3. Offer and Make-Whole Agent
#ai-agent #monte-carlo-methods #large-language-models #revenue-impact #automation

**Concept:** An agent that, from the shortlist stage, builds each finalist's forfeiture picture — vesting schedules, performance-share payout ranges, in-year bonus, deferred pay — from proxies, Form 4 filings and the candidate's own statements; values it with uncertainty; proposes make-whole structures within the client's plan limits; and drafts the term sheet the client's compensation committee will review.
**Inputs:** Candidate grant statements, proxy and Form 4 data, plan documents, the client's equity-plan constraints, peer pay benchmarks.
**Outputs / Actions:** Forfeiture valuation with ranges; make-whole options; affordability flags at shortlist; draft offer term sheet; counteroffer scenarios.
**Why now:** Document extraction from grant statements is reliable enough to trust with review, and the 2022 pay-versus-performance disclosures give five years of realised pay to calibrate payout assumptions.
**Market:** Search firms (to protect fees and timelines) and the hiring companies' total rewards teams; compensation consultants and pay-data vendors are adjacent sellers.

## 4. Board Refreshment Intelligence Platform
#ai-platform #gradient-boosting #survival-analysis #large-language-models #revenue-impact

**Concept:** A platform for board practices that reads every proxy to build each board's skills matrix, director ages against mandatory retirement policies, tenure, committee loads and over-boarding limits; forecasts which boards will open seats in the next proxy cycle; and matches the gaps to a candidate pool screened for interlocks and independence, with the novelty of each candidate relative to the board's existing network shown.
**Inputs:** Proxy statements and 8-K director announcements, governance policies, BoardEx or Equilar board data, the firm's director candidate records.
**Outputs / Actions:** Vacancy forecasts by board and quarter; gap analyses; screened candidate slates; business-development alerts for the board practice.
**Why now:** Seats are scarcer (364 new independent S&P 500 directors in Spencer Stuart's 2026 index, the fewest since 2016), so knowing which boards will open a seat before the nominating committee calls is worth more, and language models can read the proxy narrative sections that structured databases skip.
**Market:** Board practices at search firms; nominating committees and governance counsel; board data vendors are both suppliers and competitors.
