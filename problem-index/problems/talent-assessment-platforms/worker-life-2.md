# The Psychologist Whose Validity Concerns Lose to the Deal

**Industry:** [[talent-assessment-platforms|Talent Assessment Platforms]]
**Type:** Worker Life Changing
**One-liner:** The industrial-organisational psychologist knows the instrument is being applied outside its validated range, says so, and watches the configuration ship because the client asked for it.
**Tags:** #hypothesis-testing #confidence-intervals #bayesian-inference #evaluation-metrics #compliance #worker-facing #cross-validation #causal-inference

## The Problem
Assessment vendors employ industrial-organisational psychologists as the professional guarantee of their products. They design instruments, run validation studies, advise on deployment and are the people who understand what the scores mean and what they do not.

They are frequently overruled. A client wants an instrument used for a role it was not validated against, at a cut score chosen for throughput rather than derived from validity evidence, in a configuration that stacks several assessments in a way nobody has evaluated jointly. The psychologist objects, explains the validity implications and the adverse impact risk, and the deal proceeds — because the client is paying and the commercial team owns the relationship.

The cut score question is the sharpest. Where a threshold is set determines who is excluded, and it should follow from the validity evidence and the selection ratio. In practice it is frequently set to produce a desired pass rate, which is a business decision wearing psychometric clothing, and the psychologist is asked to sign off on it.

Validation studies are similarly squeezed. Doing one properly takes time and sample size that the sales cycle does not allow, so the technical manual is extended with a generalisation argument and the local study never happens.

## Why It Matters to the Worker
This is a professional with genuine expertise and a professional code — the field has established standards through its professional bodies and through the legal framework around selection — working inside a commercial structure that treats those standards as a preference.

The exposure is personal in a specific way. Their name is on the technical documentation, their professional judgement is the product's credibility, and they are aware that a deployment they advised against may exclude people unfairly and may eventually be examined in litigation.

The isolation is real. There is often one such person, or a small team, against a commercial organisation, with no independent standard to appeal to — because the buyer cannot evaluate the argument and no certification body adjudicates it.

And the newer end of the market makes it worse. Vendors selling machine-learned assessment without psychometric grounding compete on claims that a properly-validated product cannot match, which pressures the careful vendors toward the looser standard rather than the reverse.

## What a Solution Looks Like
Make the validity evidence a visible product surface. If the platform reported, per deployment, what the instrument was validated for, what it is being used for, and where those diverge, the psychologist's objection becomes a fact in the system rather than an argument in a meeting.

Derive cut scores from evidence and show the alternative. A threshold presented with its implied selection ratio, expected validity at that point and projected adverse impact makes the trade-off explicit to the person choosing it, which is a much stronger position than a professional objection.

Automate the local validation. If criterion validation runs as a standing report rather than a commissioned study, the sales-cycle argument disappears and the evidence accumulates whether or not anyone asked for it.

Build a real standard. Independent certification distinguishing validated instruments from asserted ones would let careful vendors compete on it, and its absence is why the market currently rewards the confident claim over the substantiated one.

And give the profession an escalation path. A documented internal position, recorded in the system, that a deployment departs from validated use, protects both the psychologist and — when it is eventually examined — the vendor.

## Impact If Solved
The professional standards in this field are real and are enforced by individuals with no institutional backing against commercial pressure they cannot match. Making validity evidence a visible product surface, deriving cut scores with their consequences shown, and automating local validation convert professional objections into system facts — which is the only form in which they reliably survive a sales cycle.
