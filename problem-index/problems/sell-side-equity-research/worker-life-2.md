# The Analyst Who Cannot See What Got Paid For

**Industry:** [[sell-side-equity-research|Sell-Side Equity Research]]
**Type:** Worker Life Changing
**One-liner:** An analyst produces notes, models, calls, conferences and bespoke work all year and learns twice a year, from a vote spreadsheet, roughly how much clients valued all of it together.
**Tags:** #causal-inference #gradient-boosting #linear-regression #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing #revenue-impact

## The Problem
The analyst's output is varied — published notes, models sent to clients, one-to-one calls, analyst marketing days, conference panels, bespoke analysis, management meetings arranged with corporate access. Clients pay through the broker vote: portfolio managers and analysts allocate points to brokers and often to individual analysts, which translates into commission share in a bundled market or into an RPA budget line in an unbundled one. The analyst sees the outcome as a ranking, per client, per vote period, with little indication of which interactions drove it.

Readership data exists — platforms report clicks and downloads — but a click is not a payment, and the conversations that actually move votes are logged inconsistently in CRM by sales.

## Why It Matters to the Worker
The analyst's pay, standing and coverage decisions are set by a number they cannot decompose. Do they write more notes, take more calls, run more marketing trips or spend three months on a deep-dive initiation? The honest answer is unknown, so effort follows anecdote and the loudest client. It is an exhausting way to manage a career, and it pushes analysts toward volume — more notes, more emails — which their clients say they do not want.

## What a Solution Looks Like
A per-analyst, per-client view joining interactions (calls, meetings, notes read, models downloaded, events attended) to vote outcomes over several periods, with an honest model of which interactions are associated with vote movement after controlling for client size and existing relationship. Shown to the analyst as guidance — "your one-to-one calls with these accounts track your vote; your weekly note does not appear to" — not as a quota.

## Impact If Solved
Effort reallocated from undifferentiated volume to the interactions clients actually reward makes the job saner and the research better. For the department it replaces a twice-yearly surprise with a running estimate of where its value is being created.
