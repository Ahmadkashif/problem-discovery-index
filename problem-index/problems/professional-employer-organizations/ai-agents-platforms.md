## 1. Prospect Risk Workbench
#ai-platform #gradient-boosting #bayesian-inference #large-language-models #tacit-knowledge-ml #revenue-impact

**Concept:** A platform for a PEO's new-business underwriting team that assembles each prospect's file automatically and shows comparable admitted groups with their actual outcomes. It recommends an accept, rate-up or decline with the reasons stated in underwriters' own terms, and records every decision, override and approver in an acceptance ledger. Each admitted client is then followed through its health claims, comp losses and termination, so the PEO can grade its underwriting by underwriter, broker, risk flag and override.
**Inputs:** Broker submissions (census spreadsheets, health questionnaires, renewal letters, loss runs, mods), morbidity scores, the PEO's manual rates and guidelines, historical quote files, client-level claims and loss reports, termination records.
**Outputs / Actions:** A normalised prospect file; a risk summary with comparables; a recommended action and price band; override workflow with sign-off; underwriting performance reports by cohort, broker and underwriter.
**Why now:** Language models can turn the spreadsheets and PDFs brokers send into structured files in minutes. After the benefits-cost shocks that hit Insperity's 2025 gross profit and kept TriNet's insurance cost ratio at 91%, PEO boards are asking how new business is selected.
**Market:** The top 30 PEOs with in-house underwriting teams, and the consultants and morbidity-scoring vendors that serve the smaller PEOs. There is a buyer category, health underwriting tools, but no product built around the PEO's combined health and comp decision.

## 2. Multi-State Employer Tax Account Agent
#ai-agent #large-language-models #decision-trees #compliance #automation #workflow-orchestration

**Concept:** An agent that owns the state unemployment and withholding account lifecycle for every client. When a client onboards, or hires in a new state, or leaves, it determines the reporting regime, registers or closes accounts and files the power of attorney or TPA authorisation. It reads every rate notice and agency letter, reconciles rates and wage bases against the payroll system, and drafts responses to notices for an analyst to approve.
**Inputs:** Client and employee work-location data, state reporting-regime rules, account register, rate notices and agency correspondence, quarterly filings, payroll tax ledgers.
**Outputs / Actions:** Account registrations and closures; rate updates queued into payroll; notice responses; a monthly exceptions report of accounts with rate, wage-base or regime mismatches.
**Why now:** Remote work has scattered small clients' employees across many states. Agencies have moved correspondence onto portals an agent can read, and document extraction is now accurate enough to trust with human review.
**Market:** Payroll tax departments at PEOs of every size, and the PEO platforms that could embed it. Competes with generic tax filing services, which compute the tax but do not manage PEO account regimes.

## 3. Client Book Copilot for Service Specialists
#ai-agent #large-language-models #transformers #k-nearest-neighbors #worker-facing #workflow-orchestration

**Concept:** An assistant for the payroll and HR specialists who each carry dozens of client companies. It holds each client's pay rules, history, quirks and open issues. It checks hours files and draft registers before submission, answers worksite employees' pay and benefits questions from the client's own records, and drafts replies to client owners. When a specialist is out, it gives the covering colleague a briefing on the book.
**Inputs:** Payroll registers and history, client setup and pay policies, benefits elections, ticket history, specialist notes, state rule summaries.
**Outputs / Actions:** Pre-submission exception flags with explanations; drafted answers and client messages; per-client briefings; suggested setup fixes when the same error recurs.
**Why now:** Retrieval over a client's own records makes per-client answers reliable enough to show a specialist. Specialists are the PEO's capacity constraint and the main reason clients cite service when they leave.
**Market:** Every PEO's service organisation. Platform vendors such as PrismHR are the natural incumbents. The opening is the specialist's cross-client working memory, which no platform keeps today.

## 4. Injury-to-Light-Duty Agent
#ai-agent #large-language-models #survival-analysis #compliance #workflow-orchestration #worker-facing

**Concept:** An agent that takes over the administrative half of every workers' comp claim at a client worksite. It collects the first report of injury from the client by text or form, pre-fills the state report from payroll records, and keeps every statutory and medical date. It drafts the modified-duty offer from the client's industry and the work restrictions, and nudges owner, worker and adjuster until the worker is back. Claims showing an escalation pattern go to the coordinator.
**Inputs:** Injury reports, payroll and HR records, state forms and deadlines, adjuster updates, work-status notes, client job descriptions.
**Outputs / Actions:** Filed first reports; claim timelines; drafted communications; transitional-duty suggestions; escalation alerts; deductible-layer cost tracking per client.
**Why now:** PEOs retain large deductible layers (Insperity holds the first $1.5M per occurrence), so every lost-time week is their own cost. Messaging-based intake now reaches owners and workers who never log into portals.
**Market:** PEO risk and claims departments, programme administrators writing PEO comp, and claims TPAs serving co-employed clients. The buyer exists; the tools it uses were built for adjusters, not for the employer's coordinator.
