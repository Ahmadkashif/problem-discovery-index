## 1. Research Track Record Platform
#ai-platform #hypothesis-testing #bayesian-inference #confidence-intervals #causal-inference #evaluation-metrics #descriptive-statistics #tacit-knowledge-ml

**Concept:** A platform that gives a research practice the thing it has never had, which is a record of having been right. It captures findings as falsifiable predictions at the time they are made, runs a contracted look-back a quarter or two after release, and matches each prediction to what the behavioural data actually showed. Across many studies and clients it builds the base rates the discipline has argued about for thirty years without evidence — how often a severe finding from an eight-participant study corresponds to a measurable effect, reported separately by finding type rather than pooled into a number that describes neither.

**Inputs:** Findings recorded as predictions with their support characteristics; the product change actually shipped and when; post-release behavioural metrics under a data-access clause; confounding releases in the window; study population and product context.

**Outputs / Actions:** A per-practice accuracy record usable in a budget conversation instead of a methodology lecture. Claims labelled by what the method supports — observed in six of eight, versus inferred, versus requiring quantitative confirmation — carried through into the summary rather than stranded in an appendix. An honest exclusion count for findings whose implementation could not be cleanly identified. Base rates that would settle long-running arguments about sample size on evidence.

**Why now:** Research budgets are cut first in every downturn and the stated reason is always unprovable return; the discipline's answer has been advocacy because the evidence requires a look-back nobody funds. Client-side product analytics are now universal, which makes the look-back a contract clause rather than an instrumentation project.

**Market:** Research agencies and consultancies, in-house research teams facing the same budget argument, and the research platforms who could offer accuracy tracking as a differentiator.

## 2. Participant Integrity Platform
#ai-platform #gradient-boosting #graph-neural-networks #dbscan #k-means-clustering #compliance #evaluation-metrics #worker-facing

**Concept:** A validity layer for the participant supply that every finding rests on. It scores participants and individual responses from behaviour, account relationships and device characteristics rather than from what people say about themselves, fits thresholds per population so that hard-to-recruit groups are not over-filtered by a globally-tuned model, and treats response-level validity in unmoderated studies — assisted or generated open responses, implausible timing, internal contradiction — as its own problem rather than a variant of account screening.

**Inputs:** Screener responses with timing; claimed attributes across a participant's study history and their consistency; device, network and account signals; the account relationship graph; open-response characteristics; confirmed misrepresentation cases from moderator observation.

**Outputs / Actions:** Flags for review rather than silent exclusion, with a route for the participant to contest — because a false positive removes someone's income on the basis of an algorithm they cannot see, and the current practice of an undisclosed internal quality score is not defensible. Per-study validity reporting, so a researcher knows which findings rest on a clean sample. Population-fitted thresholds. Relational detection of coordinated accounts, which per-account heuristics cannot reach.

**Why now:** Unmoderated research has grown into the dominant volume format at exactly the moment that generating a plausible open response became trivial, and the screening model in use was designed for a world where the main risk was an over-eager participant rather than an industry.

**Market:** Participant panels and recruitment platforms, research agencies who currently absorb the cost of bad sessions, and academic and market research buyers with the strongest validity requirements.

## 3. Study Operations and Synthesis Agent
#ai-agent #large-language-models #gradient-boosting #time-series-forecasting #k-means-clustering #compliance #worker-facing #workflow-orchestration

**Concept:** An agent covering the two halves of a study that consume the calendar. On operations it forecasts no-shows and dropouts from population, incentive, session length and lead time so over-recruitment is modelled rather than habitual, predicts which specific bookings will drop while a standby can still be arranged, runs the screener-to-scheduling-to-incentive workflow, and holds consent scope and retention clocks as enforced system properties rather than as something one person remembers. On synthesis it extracts candidate observations, proposes clusters with their evidence, and locates clips — and stops there, because the interpretation is the professional contribution.

**Inputs:** Screener and booking data with historical no-show outcomes; participant populations and incentive structures; payment provider behaviour by country and method; consent records and retention obligations; session recordings and transcripts; the team's own deck structures and prior studies.

**Outputs / Actions:** A modelled recruitment target and early warning on at-risk bookings. Automated scheduling, reminders, rescheduling and incentive disbursement with the known cross-border payment failures routed around rather than discovered. Consent scope and deletion obligations enforced automatically. Proposed themes with evidence attached and dissent easy to register — explicitly tested for semantic collapse, the failure where two quotes using the same words to mean opposite things are grouped into a plausible wrong theme. Deck and highlight reel assembled once the findings are settled.

**Why now:** Transcription and first-pass extraction became reliable recently, which moves the researcher from producing a first pass to interrogating one — the better use of the same hours. The operations half has been a manual interrupt-driven job for a decade with no forecasting applied to a problem that is straightforwardly forecastable.

**Market:** Research agencies, in-house research operations functions, and the repository and panel platforms who already hold most of the required data.
