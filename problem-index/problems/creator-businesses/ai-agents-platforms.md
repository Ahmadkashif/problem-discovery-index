## 1. Creator Evidence Platform
#ai-platform #causal-inference #gradient-boosting #change-point-detection #confidence-intervals #hypothesis-testing #evaluation-metrics #feature-engineering

**Concept:** A platform that treats a creator's back catalogue as the experimental record it actually is. It extracts structured decision variables from every upload — topic, title structure, thumbnail composition, opening structure, length, timing — joins them to outcomes, controls for channel size at publication, seasonality and recent channel performance, and reports effects as intervals rather than as advice. It is deliberately willing to say that a factor cannot be separated at this sample size, which is the finding creators most need and never receive. It pools across consenting channels by niche to reach the statistical power no individual has. And it detects platform ranking changes as simultaneous structural breaks across many channels, so a creator watching their numbers fall learns it was a dated platform event rather than a personal failure.

**Inputs:** Upload-level decision variables extracted from content; platform outcome metrics including retention curves; channel state at publication; pooled data from consenting channels; public metrics at scale; platform announcement dates.

**Outputs / Actions:** Effect estimates with intervals, including explicit "cannot distinguish" findings. Properly powered sequential thumbnail and title experiments, which is the only genuine randomisation available in the workflow. Dated platform change alerts with the affected cohort. Transfer tests showing whether a finding from other channels holds on this one — the claim all creator advice makes and never demonstrates.

**Why now:** An entire profession's expertise is built on reading through a deliberate confound at sample sizes that cannot support it, and the advice ecosystem around it is almost purely anecdotal as a result. Nothing about the analysis is hard; the instrument simply has not been built, and the pooling it needs is a coordination problem rather than a technical one.

**Market:** Full-time creators, multi-channel networks, creator management companies and media businesses operating channel portfolios. The buyer is a creator or their business manager, and the argument is that they are currently making five-figure decisions on evidence they have never examined.

## 2. Production Agent
#ai-agent #cnns #object-detection #large-language-models #bert #transfer-learning #automation #worker-facing

**Concept:** An agent that absorbs the mechanical half of production and learns the creative half. It does the full first pass — syncing, culling unusable takes, cutting filler and silence, transcript-driven rough assembly, colour, audio, captions — and it builds the rough cut in the creator's own rhythm, learned from the paired record of raw footage and final cuts across the back catalogue, which is an unusually clean supervision signal every established creator has and nobody uses. It interprets timestamped feedback into edit operations rather than requiring a human to translate each note. It retrieves b-roll and assets from the creator's own library against the transcript. And it selects clips for each platform from the creator's own clip performance history, generating a restructured opening for each rather than inheriting whatever the cut started with.

**Inputs:** Raw footage and transcripts; paired historical footage and final cuts; feedback threads and the resulting changes; the creator's asset library; historical clip performance by platform; retention curves against editing decisions.

**Outputs / Actions:** A style-matched rough cut. Structured edit operations from prose feedback. Asset suggestions per segment. Platform-assigned clips with generated hooks and genuine adaptation of pacing and opening structure, not merely aspect ratio. Outcome data returned to the editor on the videos they cut — the professional feedback the role currently never receives.

**Why now:** Repurposing has become a second full production line staffed by the same people as the first, and it is the main reason creator teams burn out at the size they do. The mechanical pass is now routine, and the style learning that removes the first feedback round rests on a dataset that already exists in every editor's archive.

**Market:** Creator businesses, podcast networks, media companies with social teams, and the editing and repurposing tool vendors already in this workflow. The metric is videos per editor per week and revision rounds per video, both of which the buyer feels acutely and neither of which current tools move much.

## 3. Creator Business Agent
#ai-agent #bert #large-language-models #gradient-boosting #time-series-forecasting #k-nearest-neighbors #revenue-impact #workflow-orchestration

**Concept:** An agent for everything that is not content. It benchmarks sponsorship rates against comparable channels by size, engagement, niche, format and deliverable scope, which addresses the total and one-directional information asymmetry that currently costs creators real money on every deal. It extracts obligations from partnership contracts — placement, duration, talking points, exclusivity windows, usage rights, disclosure — and tracks them against published content, warning before an exclusivity clause is breached. It qualifies inbound outreach, separating serious offers from gifting requests and automated blasts. It forecasts revenue with honest uncertainty across platform payouts, sponsorship pipeline and seasonality. And it computes the thing every creator fears and nobody has measured: what a break actually costs, estimated from pooled data on channels that paused and recovered.

**Inputs:** Historical deal terms and rates; comparable channel benchmarks where poolable; contract documents; publishing history and platform analytics; inbound correspondence; revenue by source over time; pooled break-and-recovery data across channels; platform concentration by revenue.

**Outputs / Actions:** Rate recommendations with the comparison set shown. Extracted deliverable schedules with pre-breach warnings. Qualified inbound with drafted replies. Revenue forecasts with intervals. An evidence-based estimate of the cost of a pause and the buffer required for a given break length. Platform concentration risk quantified — a number most creators know qualitatively and have never seen.

**Why now:** Sponsorship is the highest-margin revenue in the business and is priced by guesswork against buyers who know the market, while the schedule that governs the creator's life runs on a belief about pausing that has never been tested. Both are answerable from data, and the second is the one that determines whether this profession is survivable.

**Market:** Creators, creator management and talent agencies, multi-channel networks and the creator financial tooling vendors. The rate benchmarking alone pays for the product on a single negotiation, which makes it the natural entry point for everything else.
