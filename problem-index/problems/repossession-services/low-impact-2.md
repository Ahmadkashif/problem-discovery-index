# Lender Updates Re-keyed into Every Portal

**Industry:** [[repossession-services|Repossession Agencies]]
**Type:** Low Impact (Customisation Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Each lender and forwarder wants status updates on open files at its own cadence, in its own words, on its own portal, and an invoice with its own proof — so agency office staff spend their nights retyping the same facts and their mornings fighting denied fees.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #data-integration #workflow-orchestration #automation #compliance #revenue-impact

## The Problem
An open assignment is not just a car to find; it is a reporting obligation. A forwarder may require an update on every skip file every few days, a lender may want a field visit narrative with a photo of the address, and a third may score agencies on how quickly they acknowledge new files and how often they update. Many agencies take assignments from RDN, several forwarders' own systems and a few direct clients by email. The same attempt — truck at the residence at 05:10, vehicle not present, neighbour says borrower moved — is typed into one, two or three places, in each client's required format.

At close-out the agency invoices: the recovery fee, plus any skip, storage, key, transport or personal property fees the client's schedule allows, each with proof — photos, the police notice, the condition report, a mileage log. Fees are denied for a missing photo, a fee code not on that client's schedule, or an update gap that the client says broke its rules. Whether a recovery gets paid in full is decided in the office, not the truck.

## What Already Exists
RDN's update and invoicing screens, forwarders' proprietary portals, towing back-office software such as Towbook for dispatch and invoicing, and generic workflow and robotic process automation tools that can move data between screens.

## The Customisation Gap
The generic tools move text; they do not know the rules. The repossession layer needs: each client's update cadence and content rules stored per file, with a queue of updates due before they lapse; updates drafted from the agent's field record (GPS trace, photos, one-tap reasons) in the client's vocabulary and posted to the right portal; each client's fee schedule and proof requirements checked before an invoice goes out; denial reasons captured and fed back so the same rejection does not recur; and a single file history the agency can show when a client disputes a fee or an update gap.

## Impact If Solved
An office that spends several hours a night on updates and invoices (estimate) gets most of it back, fewer fees are denied for documentation gaps, and agencies stop losing panel position on scorecards that measure typing speed rather than recoveries.
