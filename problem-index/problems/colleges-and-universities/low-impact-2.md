# Accreditation Evidence Assembled by Hand

**Industry:** [[colleges-and-universities|Colleges & Universities]]
**Type:** Low Impact (Customisation Opportunity)
**One-liner:** Every institution rebuilds its accreditation self-study from scratch each review cycle, hunting through committee minutes, assessment reports and policy documents for evidence that each standard is met, when planning and assessment software already holds most of it.
**Tags:** #large-language-models #transformers #k-nearest-neighbors #word-embeddings #compliance #workflow-orchestration #automation

## The Problem
An institution's accreditor (HLC, MSCHE, SACSCOC, WSCUC, NECHE or NWCCU for most four-year colleges) reviews it on a cycle of eight to ten years with interim reports in between. The comprehensive review is a self-study or assurance argument: a narrative for each standard or criterion, backed by an evidence file of policies, minutes, budget documents, programme reviews, learning-outcomes assessment results and data tables. A team of faculty and staff spends 18–24 months on it (estimate). Most of the work is retrieval. Someone has to find the 2022 faculty senate minutes approving the general-education revision, the assessment report showing a programme acted on its results, and the policy proving the complaint process exists and is followed. Then the narrative has to cite each document correctly. Accreditor findings frequently turn on missing or weak evidence rather than missing practice, and a finding means a monitoring report or a follow-up visit.

## What Already Exists
Planning and assessment platforms (Watermark Planning & Self-Study and Outcomes Assessment, Anthology Planning and Accreditation, Weave, HelioCampus) store assessment plans, results and strategic-plan progress and offer self-study templates mapped to each accreditor's standards. Document management (SharePoint, Box, Google Drive) holds the rest. Accreditors publish their standards, guidance and the evidence they expect.

## The Customisation Gap
The templates organise the narrative; they do not find or test the evidence. The accreditation-specific layer is: each accreditor's standards and criteria broken into the specific claims a peer reviewer checks; retrieval over the institution's whole document estate that proposes evidence for each claim, with dates and approving bodies; detection of gaps where a policy exists but no evidence shows it applied; consistency checks between the narrative, IPEDS and the institution's own data tables, which reviewers compare; and change tracking against standards revisions between cycles. Every accreditor words the same expectation differently, and the evidence lives in documents written for other purposes.

## Impact If Solved
An evidence map maintained continuously rather than rebuilt every decade cuts ~3–6 months from an 18–24 month self-study (estimate) and reduces the follow-up reports that come from evidence nobody could find. It matters most at small colleges, where the same three administrators also run institutional research and the budget.
