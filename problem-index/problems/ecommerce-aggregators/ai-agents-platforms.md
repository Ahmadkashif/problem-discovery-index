## 1. Acquisition Underwriting Platform
#ai-platform #survival-analysis #gradient-boosting #causal-inference #confidence-intervals #time-series-forecasting #evaluation-metrics #revenue-impact

**Concept:** A platform that underwrites on realised outcomes rather than on a trailing multiple. It normalises marketplace exports into a standard operational and financial model with fees, returns, storage and advertising allocated properly, enriches with public category data the data room omits — competitor listing growth, category price trends, entry rates over the trailing period — and predicts the post-acquisition revenue trajectory from a model trained on the firm's own completed acquisitions. It scores transferability explicitly: supplier concentration, seller response patterns, review velocity sources, ranking defensibility, and how much of the trailing window sits at an unusual peak relative to the brand's own history.

**Inputs:** Target marketplace exports and financials; public category and competitor data; supplier and contract terms; the firm's historical acquisitions with realised trajectories and migration actions; deal terms.

**Outputs / Actions:** A revenue projection as a distribution with the downside case stated and what would have to be true for it. A transferability score by driver. Peak detection against the brand's own history. Anomaly flags against the portfolio baseline — review velocity inconsistent with sales, margin dependent on an unusual fee treatment. Automated normalisation that removes most of the analyst's mechanical work.

**Why now:** The sector deployed billions on an underwriting model that did not predict persistence, and the supervised training data — pre-acquisition characteristics paired with realised outcomes — accumulated throughout and was never assembled. Anyone re-entering this market with that model has a structural advantage over anyone who does not.

**Market:** Aggregators still acquiring, private equity firms buying marketplace businesses, lenders financing them, and the brokers who intermediate these deals. Also directly useful to sellers preparing to exit, who would benefit from understanding what a buyer should be discounting.

## 2. Portfolio Operations Agent
#ai-agent #change-point-detection #gradient-boosting #time-series-forecasting #confidence-intervals #evaluation-metrics #automation #worker-facing

**Concept:** An agent that watches every listing across every brand so that a manager carrying twelve of them is not relying on noticing. It baselines each listing against its own history, detects material drift in ranking, conversion, buy box share, advertising efficiency and review velocity, and — critically — prioritises by expected revenue impact rather than by how visible the problem is, so a slow decline on a large brand outranks an acute issue on a small one. It diagnoses alongside detecting, naming the competitor price change or the suppressed image, and recognises when several brands move together, which almost always means one marketplace change rather than twelve problems.

**Inputs:** Daily marketplace performance per listing; competitor pricing and listing changes; inventory positions and velocity; advertising performance; review streams; marketplace policy announcements; brand revenue and margin for value weighting.

**Outputs / Actions:** An expected-value-ranked queue with diagnosis attached. Cross-portfolio correlation alerts identifying marketplace-wide causes. Automated routine actions within bounds — bid adjustments, reorder triggers, listing content refresh. Recovery trajectory expectations after a disturbance, so a manager does not panic-spend on advertising. Portfolio health reporting that does not require opening twelve accounts.

**Why now:** Portfolio decline in this sector is accumulated unattended drift rather than a series of disasters, and every input needed to catch it early is already pulled daily for reporting. The operating model — few people, many brands — only works if attention is directed by expected value rather than by urgency.

**Market:** Aggregators, multi-brand marketplace sellers, and agencies managing seller portfolios. The same capability serves any operator running more listings than people, which is now most serious marketplace businesses.

## 3. Migration and Sourcing Agent
#ai-agent #change-point-detection #bert #k-nearest-neighbors #time-series-forecasting #confidence-intervals #optimization-fundamentals #data-integration

**Concept:** An agent covering the two operational disciplines that determine whether the acquisition thesis survives contact with reality. On migration it plans the transition from measured evidence — which steps across the firm's prior migrations actually disturbed ranking, in what sequence, with what recovery time — and forecasts the inventory buffer required to avoid a stockout across the transfer window, which is the most damaging single event available to a marketplace ranking and is currently sized by intuition. On sourcing it resolves products and suppliers across acquired catalogues, identifying which items are substitutable and which supplier records are the same factory under different trading names, then optimises joint orders against differing demand cycles, minimum order quantities and cash constraints.

**Inputs:** Migration timelines and outcomes from prior acquisitions; daily ranking and sales through transitions; inventory positions and transfer durations; acquired product catalogues with images and specifications; supplier documents and purchase history; demand cycles and cash position per brand.

**Outputs / Actions:** A sequenced migration plan with measured risk per step and a stockout-safe inventory buffer. Real-time monitoring through the transition with recovery expectations. Product substitutability groups and canonical supplier identities for sourcing review. Joint order recommendations with the consolidation saving quantified. Freight and packaging aggregation opportunities.

**Why now:** Post-acquisition decline is frequently a transition failure attributed to a diligence failure, and every firm holds dozens of natural experiments it has never analysed. Product and supplier resolution is the enabling layer for the procurement synergy that justified the model in the first place and largely did not materialise.

**Market:** Aggregators and roll-up operators in any fragmented commerce category. The sourcing half generalises well beyond marketplace brands to any acquirer consolidating supply chains across acquired businesses.
